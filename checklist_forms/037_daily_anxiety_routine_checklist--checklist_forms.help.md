# Daily Anxiety Routine Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track and assess your daily anxiety levels, coping strategies, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each page:

### Page 1: Anxiety Routine Checklist
- Select your anxiety level using the dropdown menu. Choose from Anxious, Calm, Very anxious, or Not anxious.

### Page 2: Coping Strategies
- Describe some of the coping strategies you use to manage your anxiety.

### Page 3: Mood and Anxiety Triggers
- Select all the feelings you are experiencing, including Happy, Sad, Angry, Frustrated, and Bored.
- Select all the triggers that contribute to your anxiety, such as Family, School, Work, Finances, Relationships, Health, or Other.

### Page 4: Sleep Quality and Date
- Select your sleep quality using the dropdown menu. Choose from 1-2 hours, 2-3 hours, 3-4 hours, 4-5 hours, 5-6 hours, 6-7 hours, 7-8 hours, More than 8 hours, or Other.
- Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Page 5: Time and Additional Information
- Enter the time in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
- Enter any additional notes or comments, if needed.

### Page 6: Contact Information (Optional)
- Enter your email address, if you wish to provide additional contact information.
- Enter your phone number, if you wish to provide a contact number.

### Page 7: Category and Submission Information
- Select the category that best describes this entry, such as Anxiety, Mental Health, Physical Health, Work, Relationships, or Other.
- Select who submitted this entry, whether you or someone else.

### Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* Use the provided hints to guide your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Please submit the form as soon as possible after completion, so that we can better serve you.
* If you need to submit additional information or updates, please create a new entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Anxiety Routine Checklist**:
	+ (daily_anxiety_routine_checklist): This field is the starting point for your anxiety assessment. Choose your level of anxiety from Anxious, Calm, Very anxious, or Not anxious.
* **Coping Strategies**:
	+ (coping_strategies): Describe some of the strategies you use to cope with anxiety, such as deep breathing, exercise, or talking to a friend.
* **Mood**:
	+ (current_mood): Select all the feelings you are experiencing, like happy, sad, angry, frustrated, or bored.
* **Anxiety Triggers**:
	+ (anxiety_triggers): Choose all the triggers that contribute to your anxiety, such as family issues, school, work, finances, relationships, health concerns, or other.
* **Sleep Quality**:
	+ (sleep_quality): Select your sleep quality from 1-2 hours, 2-3 hours, 3-4 hours, 4-5 hours, 5-6 hours, 6-7 hours, 7-8 hours, More than 8 hours, or Other.
* **Date**:
	+ (date): Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**:
	+ (time): Enter the time in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
* **Note**:
	+ (note): Enter any additional notes or comments, if needed.
* **Email**:
	+ (email): Enter your email address, if you wish to provide additional contact information.
* **Phone**:
	+ (phone): Enter your phone number, if you wish to provide a contact number.
* **Category**:
	+ (category): Choose the category that best describes this entry, such as Anxiety, Mental Health, Physical Health, Work, Relationships, or Other.
* **Submission Information**:
	+ (submitted_by): Select who submitted this entry, whether you or someone else.
