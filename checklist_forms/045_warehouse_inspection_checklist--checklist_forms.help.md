# Warehouse Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Inspection Checklist is used by warehouse staff to inspect and record the status of the warehouse environment, including its location, storage conditions, fire suppression systems, emergency exits, fire prophets, storage areas, aisle width, safety equipment, electrical systems, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your observations during the inspection.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by *required: true* in the field label).
3. If a field has multiple options, select all applicable ones.
4. If a field has a specific format (e.g., number), ensure you enter the correct format (e.g., a numerical value).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **warehouse location** (text): Enter the location of the warehouse being inspected.
* **storage areas** (select multiple): Select all storage areas that need attention, such as Racking system, Shelves, or Floor space.
* **aisle width** (number): Enter the width of the aisle in numerical format (e.g., 10 for 10 feet).
* **fire suppression systems** (select one): Choose the type of fire suppression system present, such as Clean Agent, Water Mist, or Gaseous.
* **emergency exits** (select multiple): Select all emergency exits that are reported as functional or not reported.
* **fire prophets** (select one): Choose the type of fire protection system present, such as Fire alarm system, Sprinkler system, or No system.
* **storage conditions** (select multiple): Select all storage conditions that need attention, such as Dusty, Clean, or Cluttered.
* **safety equipment** (select multiple): Select all safety equipment available, such as First aid kit, Safety glasses, or Fire extinguishers.
* **electrical system** (select one): Choose the type of electrical system present, such as Grounded, Bonded, or No.
* **emergency procedures** (select one): Choose the type of emergency procedures in place, such as Written, Oral, or None.
