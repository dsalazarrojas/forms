# Festival Preparation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help prepare for a festival by checking off tasks and confirming details with vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and fill in the required fields, such as the name of the festival, the event title, and vendor lists 3, 7, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, and 24.
2. Enter the date and time of the event.
3. Add any additional notes or comments.

* Make sure to select all the required fields.
