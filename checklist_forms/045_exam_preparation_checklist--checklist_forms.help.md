# Exam Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps examinees assess their progress in preparing for an exam, providing an opportunity to identify areas of improvement and potential knowledge gaps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select the study materials you have completed from the provided options. This will help you track your progress and identify which materials you still need to work on.
* Enter the dates of your scheduled exams, or leave them blank if you don't have an exam scheduled.
* Enter the deadline for your exams, or leave it blank if you don't have a specific deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **study_materials** (`exam_preparation_checklist`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select each study material you have completed (e.g., textbook, online course, practice test). This will help you track your progress and identify which materials you still need to work on.
* **exam_dates** (`exam_dates`, `date`, required: false): Enter the dates of your upcoming exams. This will help you keep track of exam schedules.
* **exam_deadline** (`exam_deadline`, `date`, required: false): Enter the deadline for your exams, if applicable.
