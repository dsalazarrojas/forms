# Funeral Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Planning Checklist Form is designed to collect information for funeral planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each section.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Funeral Planning Checklist 
* **Funeral Planning Checklist**: This section provides a checklist of funeral planning details.
* **Funeral Service**: This field is for selecting the type of funeral service you wish to have.
* **Burial Site**: This field is for selecting multiple burial site options.
* **Death Certificate**: This field is for entering a number that represents the death certificate.
* **Death Certificate Copy**: This field is for entering a number that represents the death certificate copy.
* **Obituary**: This field is for writing a text for the obituary.
* **Obituary Publication**: This field is for selecting the publication method for the obituary.
* **Funeral Home**: This field is for selecting multiple funeral home options.
* **Burial Site Details**: This field is for entering a text for the burial site details.
* **Death Date**: This field is for entering the date of death.
* **Death Time**: This field is for entering the time of death.
* **Funeral Date**: This field is for entering the date of the funeral.
* **Funeral Time**: This field is for entering the time of the funeral.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for entering the contact information.
* **Contact Information Phone**: This field is for entering the phone number.
* **Contact Information Email**: This field is for entering the email address.
* **Address**: This field is for entering the address.
* **City**: This field is for entering the city.
* **State**: This field is for entering the state.
* **Zip**: This field is for entering the zip code.
* **Country**: This field is for entering the country.
* **Obituary Wishes**: This field is for entering a text for the obituary wishes.
* **Obituary Message**: This field is for entering a text for the obituary message.
* **Obituary Image**: This field is for entering a text for the obituary image.
* **Other**: This field is for entering any other information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the funeral director or administrator.
* This form is designed to be completed by the person planning the funeral or their representative.
