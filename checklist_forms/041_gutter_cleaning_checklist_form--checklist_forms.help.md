# Gutter Cleaning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on the gutter cleaning process of a building's exterior. The form collects data on the condition and frequency of gutter cleaning, as well as any issues that may be present.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Answer the questions based on your knowledge of the building's gutters.
4. If you are unsure about any information, please do not fill out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gutter Section** (`1`, `note`, optional): Describe the condition of the gutters in this section.
* **Roof Section** (`2`, `text`, optional): Provide a short description of the roof's condition.
* **Gutter Condition** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "Yes" if the gutters are in good condition, or "No" if they are not.
* **Gutter Cleaning Frequency** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if the gutters need to be cleaned more than once a year, or "No" if they are cleaned less than once a year.
* **Notes** (`5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the gutters.
* **Photos** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if photos of the gutters are available, or "No" if they are not.
* **Videos** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if videos of the gutters are available, or "No" if they are not.
* **Issues** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if any issues are present in the gutters, or "No" if they are not.
* **Completed Tasks** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if tasks are completed for the gutters, or "No" if they are not.
* **Maintenance Recommendations** (`10`, `note`, optional): Add any maintenance recommendations for the gutters.
* **Completed Tasks Status** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "Completed" if all tasks are completed, or "Partially Completed" if some tasks are not.
