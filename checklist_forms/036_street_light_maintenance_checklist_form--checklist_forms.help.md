# Street Light Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the condition and status of street lights, including their power source, maintenance utilities, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Street Light Maintenance Form** option from the dropdown list, which is the first page of the form.
2. Choose the correct **Power Source** for the street light from the dropdown list.
3. Select the applicable maintenance utilities required for the street light.
4. Evaluate the overall **Condition** of the street light and choose from the dropdown list.
5. Assess the condition of the **Pole** and choose from the dropdown list.
6. Evaluate the condition of the **Foundation** and choose from the dropdown list.
7. Enter the **Date** of the maintenance or inspection.
8. Choose the **Time** of the maintenance or inspection.
9. Enter any **Notes** about the maintenance or inspection.
10. Enter the **Email** contact information of the person performing the maintenance or inspection.
10. Enter the **Phone** number of the person performing the maintenance or inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Light Maintenance Form**: This is the first page of the form, which you must select from the dropdown list.
* **Power Source**: Choose the primary power source for the street light, such as Electric, Gas, or Solar.
* **Street Light Pole Utilities**: Select the maintenance utilities required for the street light, such as Water, Sewer, or Gas.
* **Street Light Condition**: Evaluate the overall condition of the street light, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Street Light Pole Condition**: Assess the condition of the street light pole, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Street Light Foundation Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the foundation of the street light, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the maintenance or inspection.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the maintenance or inspection.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance or inspection.
* **Email**: Enter the email contact information of the person performing the maintenance or inspection.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person performing the maintenance or inspection.
