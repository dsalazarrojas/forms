# Cyber Security Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyber Security Audit Checklist is a tool to help organizations assess their current state of cyber security practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, select the correct options for each field, taking into account your organization's current policies and practices. If a field is not applicable to your organization, simply select "N/A" or "Not Applicable."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is a brief description of the audit checklist and the purpose of the form.
* **Asset Management** (`asset_management`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Assets**: The form is concerned with managing and tracking all company assets, including hardware, software, and data.
	+ **Inventory**: The form is concerned with inventorying and tracking all company assets, including hardware, software, and data.
	+ **Access**: The form is concerned with managing access to company assets, including permissions and restrictions.
	+ **Controls**: The form is concerned with implementing controls to ensure the security and integrity of company assets.
* **Network Security** (`network_security`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all applicable options:
	+ **Firewalls**: The form is concerned with implementing firewalls to block unwanted access to the network.
	+ **Intrusion Detection**: The form is concerned with detecting and preventing unauthorized access to the network.
	+ **Antivirus**: The form is concerned with protecting against malware and viruses.
	+ **Patching**: The form is concerned with ensuring that all software is up to date and patched.
* **Access Control** (`access_control`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Password Policy**: The form is concerned with ensuring that strong passwords are used to protect access to company assets.
	+ **Account Management**: The form is concerned with managing access to company assets, including permissions and restrictions.
	+ **Authentication**: The form is concerned with verifying the identity of users before granting access to company assets.
* **Monitoring** (`monitoring`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is a brief description of the monitoring process used to track and respond to security incidents.
* **Incident Response** (`incident_response`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all applicable options:
	+ **Incident Identification**: The form is concerned with identifying and classifying potential security incidents.
	+ **Incident Classification**: The form is concerned with classifying incidents based on their severity and impact.
	+ **Containment**: The form is concerned with isolating and containing the incident to prevent further damage.
* **Compliance Management** (`compliance_management`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Regulatory Standards**: The form is concerned with adhering to regulatory standards and guidelines.
	+ **Compliance Checklist**: The form is concerned with ensuring compliance with company policies and procedures.
* **Security Training** (`security_training`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is a brief description of the security training program in place within the organization.
* **Patch Management** (`patch_management`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Patch Scheduling**: The form is concerned with scheduling and applying patches to ensure up-to-date software.
	+ **Patch Applicability**: The form is concerned with determining the applicability and priority of patches for software updates.
