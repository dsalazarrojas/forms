# Spiritual Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals share their spiritual practices and experiences. The goal is to gather information on how often they practice, the benefits they derive from it, and the specific practices they engage in.

## How To Complete This Form

* To start, please select "What is your current spiritual practice?" and enter a brief description of your current spiritual practice.
* For "Do you have a spiritual practice?", please select one of the following options:
	+ 1: Yes, I have a spiritual practice.
	+ 2: No, I don't have a spiritual practice.
	+ 3: I'm not sure.
* To answer "How often do you practice?", select one or more of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* In "What are the benefits of your spiritual practice?", please enter the benefits you derive from your practice.
* To answer "Why do you practice?", enter a brief explanation of why you practice spirituality.
* For "How do you practice?", enter a description of your spiritual practice.
* To answer "Where do you practice?", enter the location where you practice your spirituality.
* To answer "What is your practice?", enter a description of your specific spiritual practice.
* For "How often do you practice?", please enter the date and time of day you typically practice.
* To answer "Time of day?", enter the time of day you practice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current spiritual practice?** (`Text`, required/Optional): Enter a brief description of your current spiritual practice.
* **Do you have a spiritual practice?** (`Select One`, Required/Optional): Select one of the following:
	+ 1: Yes, I have a spiritual practice.
	+ 2: No, I don't have a spiritual practice.
	+ 3: I'm not sure.
* **How often do you practice?** (`Select Multiple`, Required/Optional): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **What are the benefits of your spiritual practice?** (`Text`, Required/Optional): Enter the benefits you derive from your practice.
* **Why do you practice?** (`Text`, Required/Optional): Enter a brief explanation of why you practice spirituality.
* **How do you practice?** (`Text`, Required/Optional): Enter a description of your spiritual practice.
* **Where do you practice?** (`Text`, Required/Optional): Enter the location where you practice your spirituality.
* **What is your practice?** (`Text`, Required/Optional): Enter a description of your specific spiritual practice.
* **Spiritual Practices** (`Note`, Required/Optional): This is a note field to collect additional information about your spiritual practices.
* **How often do you practice?** (`Date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date and time of day you typically practice.
* **Time of day?** (`Time`, Required/Optional): Enter the time of day you practice.
