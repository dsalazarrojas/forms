# Weekly Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of tracking and maintaining equipment on a regular basis. It collects information about the equipment, its status, and the details of its maintenance, including the frequency of maintenance, next maintenance date, and cost involved. This form helps ensure that equipment is properly maintained and reduces the risk of equipment failure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Equipment Name" field with the name of the equipment being maintained.
2. Select the maintenance frequency from the options provided.
3. Enter the "Next Maintenance Date" in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. If applicable, mark the status of the equipment as "Completed" in the "Maintenance Completed" field.
5. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
7. Select the personnel responsible for maintaining the equipment in the "Assigned To" field.
8. Choose the location(s) where the equipment is being maintained in the "Location" field.
9. Select the current status of the equipment from the options provided in the "Equipment Status" field.
10. Enter any additional notes about the equipment in the "Notes" field.
11. Select the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By" field.
12. Enter the submitted date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
13. Select the type(s) of equipment from the options provided in the "Equipment Type" field.
14. Enter the asset tag of the equipment if applicable.
15. Enter the equipment model number if applicable.
16. Enter the serial number of the equipment if applicable.
17. Enter a brief description of the equipment if applicable.
18. Select the type of maintenance from the options provided in the "Maintenance Type" field.
19. Enter the cost of maintenance if applicable.
20. Enter the total maintenance hours if applicable.
21. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of maintenance (e.g., 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months).
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next maintenance in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Maintenance Completed** (`maintenance_completed`, select_one, optional): Mark as completed if maintenance is done.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the equipment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person responsible for maintaining the equipment.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location(s) where the equipment is being maintained.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the equipment (e.g., In Service, Out Of Service, Maintenance).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the equipment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the submitted date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of equipment being maintained.
* **Asset Tag** (`asset_tag`, text, optional): Enter the asset tag of the equipment if applicable.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`, text, optional): Enter the equipment model number if applicable.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment if applicable.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment if applicable.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of maintenance from the options provided.
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost of maintenance.
* **Maintenance Hours** (`maintenance_hours`, number, required): Enter the total maintenance hours.
* **Notes 2** (`notes2`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes 3** (`notes3`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes 4** (`notes4`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes 5** (`notes5`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
