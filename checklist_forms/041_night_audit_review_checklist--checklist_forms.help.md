# Night Audit Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Audit Review Checklist is a form used to document and track the progress of night audits, providing a detailed overview of the hotel's performance for a specific night. This form serves as a guide for revenue managers and audit managers to review and verify the accuracy of the night audit data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the hotel name where the night audit was conducted.
2. Select the date of the night audit.
3. Enter the time of the night audit.
4. Provide the revenue manager's name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the audit manager's name, email, and phone number.
6. Enter the room count.
7. Specify the room type.
8. Choose the room status (available, occupied, or vacant).
9. Enter the room rate.
10. Enter the room revenue.
11. Specify the number of room occupants.
12. Enter room service provided.
13. Enter amenities or services offered.
14. Enter any additional comments about the room.
15. If applicable, enter any other comments.
16. Choose the night audit status (complete, in progress, or not complete).
17. Enter the completed by date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hotel_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the hotel where the night audit was conducted.
* **date_of_audit** (date, required): Select the date of the night audit.
* **time_of_audit** (time, required): Enter the time of the night audit.
* **revenue_manager_name** (text, required): Enter the revenue manager's name.
* **revenue_manager_email** (email, required): Enter the revenue manager's email address.
* **revenue_manager_phone** (text, required): Enter the revenue manager's phone number.
* **audit_manager_name** (text, required): Enter the audit manager's name.
* **audit_manager_email** (email, required): Enter the audit manager's email address.
* **audit_manager_phone** (text, required): Enter the audit manager's phone number.
* **room_count** (number, required): Enter the total number of rooms.
* **room_type** (text, required): Enter the type of rooms (e.g., single, double, suite).
* **room_status** (select_one, required): Choose the status of the rooms (available, occupied, or vacant).
* **room_rate** (text, required): Enter the room rate.
* **room_revenue** (number, required): Enter the total revenue.
* **room_occupants** (text, required): Enter the number of room occupants.
* **room_service** (text, required): Enter room service provided.
* **room_amenity** (text, required): Enter amenities or services offered.
* **room_comment** (text, required): Enter any additional comments about the room.
* **other_comment** (text, optional): If applicable, enter any other comments.
* **total_revenue** (number, optional): Enter the total revenue.
* **total_room_nights** (text, optional): Enter the total number of room nights.
* **average_room_rate** (number, optional): Enter the average room rate.
* **night_audit_status** (select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the night audit (complete, in progress, or not complete).
* **completed_by** (text, required): Enter the date and time the night audit was completed.
