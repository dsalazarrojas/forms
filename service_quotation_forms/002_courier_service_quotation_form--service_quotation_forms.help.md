# Courier Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Service Quotation Form is a tool used to create a quotation for courier services. It helps clients or administrators gather information to provide accurate estimates for courier services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the type of service required: Select the type of service you need (Service, Delivery, or Package) in the **service_request** field.
2.  Enter client details: Fill out the **client_details** field with relevant information about the client.
3.  Select additional quotation details: Choose from the **quotation_details** field to provide further information about the quotation.
4.  Estimate the cost: Enter the estimated cost in the **estimated_cost** field.
5.  Estimate the time: Enter the estimated time in the **estimated_time** field.
6.  Specify the service type: Select the type of service from the **service_type** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **service_request**: Select the type of service you need: Service, Delivery, or Package.
    *   This field determines the type of service for which the quotation is being created.
*   **client_details**: Enter information about the client, such as name, address, and contact details.
    *   This field helps the courier service understand who the client is and what their specific needs are.
*   **quotation_details**: Select from the options to provide additional information about the quotation.
    *   This field allows users to select specific details that are relevant to the quotation, such as special instructions or services included.
*   **estimated_cost**: Enter the estimated cost for the service.
    *   This field is required and must be filled out accurately to ensure the quotation is valid.
*   **estimated_time**: Enter the estimated time for the service.
    *   This field is required and must be filled out accurately to ensure the quotation is valid.
*   **service_type**: Select the type of service: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
    *   This field helps categorize the service being quoted.
