# Bottling Services Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bottling Services Quote Request form is designed to gather essential information from businesses seeking a quote for bottling services. This form helps our team understand your needs and requirements, allowing us to provide accurate quotes and tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Company Name and contact information, including Contact Name, Title, Email Address, and Phone Number. These details are essential for us to reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
2. Select the type of product you want to bottle from the Product Type dropdown list.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product, including its characteristics and features.
4. Select the desired Bottle Size and Material for your product.
5. Choose the type of closure or cap you need for your bottles.
6. Select the labeling requirements for your product, such as full wrap labels or individual labels.
7. Choose your estimated monthly volume and initial order quantity.
8. Select the desired production timeline.
9. Choose the packaging requirements, including individual boxes, shrink wrap, or palletizing.
10. Select any required quality certifications for your product.
11. Choose whether you need warehousing or distribution services.
12. Select whether you need distribution services.
13. If applicable, provide any unique specifications or requirements for your product.
14. If you have a budget in mind, select the estimated budget range per bottle.
15. If you know how you heard about us, choose the relevant option.
16. Finally, if you have any additional comments or information to help us better understand your needs, please enter them in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of your company or brand.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter the full name of your primary contact.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, required): Enter your complete business address.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, required): Enter the date you're requesting a quote.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, required): Select the type of product you want to bottle.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, required): Provide a detailed description of your product.
* **Bottle Size** (`bottle_size`, required): Select the desired bottle size for your product.
* **Bottle Material** (`bottle_material`, required): Select the preferred material for your bottles.
* **Closure Type** (`closure_type`, optional): Choose the type of cap or closure you need for your bottles.
* **Labeling Requirements** (`labeling_requirements`, required): Select the labeling requirements for your product.
* **Estimated Monthly Volume** (`estimated_volume`, required): Select the expected number of bottles per month.
* **Initial Order Quantity** (`initial_order_quantity`, required): Enter the number of bottles for the first run.
* **Desired Production Timeline** (`production_timeline`, required): Select the desired production start date.
* **Packaging Requirements** (`packaging_requirements`, required): Select the packaging requirements for your product.
* **Required Certifications** (`quality_certifications`, required): Select any required quality certifications for your product.
* **Storage Requirements** (`storage_needs`, required): Choose whether you need warehousing or storage services.
* **Distribution Requirements** (`distribution_needs`, required): Choose whether you need distribution services.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, optional): If applicable, provide any unique specifications or requirements for your product.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, optional): Select the estimated budget range per bottle.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, optional): If you know, select the source that led you to request a quote.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here.
