# Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Withdrawal Form is used to document and verify student withdrawals from a financial aid program. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently for both the student and the advisor.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, simply follow the instructions in the fields below.
2.  Enter your details accurately and clearly.
3.  Make sure to review your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this withdrawal form.
*   **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your withdrawal from the financial aid program.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student withdrawing from the program.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
*   **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the withdrawal.
*   **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `note`, optional): The student's written acknowledgement of their withdrawal.
*   **Financial Aid** (`financial_aid`, `text`, optional): Enter the current financial aid information.
*   **Financial Aid Signature** (`student_signature_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one of the following options: None, Other.
*   **Advisor Signature** (`advisor_signature`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one of the following options: None, Other.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options: None, Other.
*   **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
*   **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the submitter's name.
*   **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, `note`, optional): The submitter's written acknowledgement of their actions.
*   **Student Acknowledgement** (`student_acknowledgement`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options: None, Other.
*   **Student Acknowledgement Phone** (`student_acknowledgement_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's acknowledgement phone number.
*   **Student Acknowledgement Email** (`student_acknowledgement_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's acknowledgement email address.
*   **Student Acknowledgement Name** (`student_acknowledgement_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's acknowledgement name.
*   **Student Acknowledgement Signature** (`student_acknowledgement_signature`, `note`, optional): The student's written acknowledgement of their withdrawal.
*   **Advisor Acknowledgement** (`advisor_acknowledgement`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options: None, Other.
*   **Advisor Acknowledgement Phone** (`advisor_acknowledgement_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the advisor's acknowledgement phone number.
*   **Advisor Acknowledgement Email** (`advisor_acknowledgement_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the advisor's acknowledgement email address.

Note: This form is optional, and some fields are not required, but it's still important to fill out the form accurately and completely for proper processing.
