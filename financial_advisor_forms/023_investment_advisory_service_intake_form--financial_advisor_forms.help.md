# Investment Advisory Service Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients to provide personalized investment advice and guidance. It helps our team understand your investment goals, risk tolerance, and financial situation, enabling us to offer tailored recommendations and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the most accurate options for each field, taking your time to review and confirm your choices.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your investment goals, risk tolerance, and financial situation.
4. Once you've completed all fields, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Investment Goals (id: 2)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the investment goals that best describe your objectives, such as long-term growth, income generation, or tax efficiency.
* **Risk Tolerance (id: 3)** (`number`, required: false): Choose a number that represents your comfort level with investment risk, with higher numbers indicating a higher risk tolerance.
* **Investment Experience (id: 4)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your level of investment experience, from beginner to expert.
* **Financial Information (id: 5)** (`text`, required: false): Enter any relevant financial information, such as income, assets, or liabilities.
* **Risk Level (id: 6)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose a risk level that best describes your comfort with investment risk, from conservative to aggressive.
* **Time Period (id: 7)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the time frame for your investment goals, such as short-term or long-term.
* **Income Range (id: 8)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your annual income range to help us understand your financial situation.
* **Net Worth (id: 9)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your current net worth to help us assess your financial situation.
* **Risk Free (id: 10)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any risk-free investments you're interested in, such as cash or money market funds.
* **Asset Allocation (id: 11)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the asset classes you're interested in investing in, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Cash Flow (id: 12)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your expected cash flow to help us understand your liquidity needs.
* **Risk Premium (id: 13)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the risk premium you're willing to accept, with higher numbers indicating a higher risk tolerance.
* **Liquidity (id: 14)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your liquidity needs, such as emergency funds or regular withdrawals.
* **Inflation Rate (id: 15)** (`number`, required: false): Enter your expected inflation rate to help us assess your investment horizon.
* **Investment Strategy (id: 16)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose a investment strategy that best fits your goals and risk tolerance, such as active management or passive investing.
* **Investment Style (id: 17)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the investment style that best aligns with your goals and risk tolerance, such as growth or income.
* **Investment Objectives (id: 18)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the investment objectives that best describe your goals, such as capital preservation or growth.

## Tips
- Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and relevance.
- Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
- This form is a starting point for our investment planning process, and we'll work with you to refine your goals and objectives.
