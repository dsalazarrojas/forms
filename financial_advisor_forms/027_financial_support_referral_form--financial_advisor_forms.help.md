# Financial Support Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Support Referral Form is a tool used to refer a client to a financial assistance program or service. The form captures the client's details, the reason for referral, and the type of financial assistance they are eligible for. This information will be used to ensure that the client receives the right assistance at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
4. Describe the reason for referral in the "Referral Reason" field.
5. Select the financial assistance programs the client is interested in or eligible for. You can choose from "Social Services", "Financial Aid", or "Other" (if "Other" is selected, a field will be provided to enter a custom program name).
6. If "Other" is selected in step 5, enter the name of the custom financial assistance program in the "Other Financial Assistance Program" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for referral.
* **Financial Assistance Program** (`financial_assistance_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select the financial assistance programs the client is interested in or eligible for. You can choose from "Social Services", "Financial Aid", or "Other".
* **Other Financial Assistance Program** (`financial_assistance_program_other`, text, optional): If "Other" is selected in the "Financial Assistance Program" field, enter the name of the custom financial assistance program.

## Tips
* Ensure that the client's information is accurate and complete.
* If this form has been completed for a client before, check the client's existing information to avoid duplications.
