# Home Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are applying for a home loan. The lender will use this information to assess the applicant's creditworthiness and determine whether to approve or reject their loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your current address.
3. Provide your employment status, including your job title, company name, and years of employment.
4. If applicable, upload employment status verification documents.
5. Fill in the loan amount and term in years you are applying for.
6. Add any additional details that may be relevant to your loan application.
7. If your loan purpose does not match any of the provided options, select "Loan Purpose Other" and describe your purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (1: Contact Information, text, required: false): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you regarding your loan application.
* **Address** (2: Address, text, required: false): Enter your current address where you reside.
* **Employment Information** (3: Employment Information, text, required: false): Enter your current job title, company name, and years of employment to help us understand your employment status.
* **Employment Status Verification** (4: Employment Status Verification, text, required: false): If applicable, provide employment verification documents such as pay stubs or letters from your employer.
* **Property Information** (5: Property Information, text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the property you are applying for, such as its address and type.
* **Loan Information** (6: Loan Information, text, required: false): Enter the loan amount and term in years you are applying for.
* **Loan Term in Years** (7: Loan Term in Years, text, required: false): Enter the number of years you would like the loan term to be.
* **Additional Details** (8: Additional Details, text, required: false): Add any additional information that may be relevant to your loan application.
* **Employment Status Verification Documents** (9: Employment Status Verification Documents, text, required: false): If you are having trouble verifying your employment status, upload relevant documents such as pay stubs or letters from your employer.
* **Loan Purpose Other** (10: Loan Purpose Other, text, required: false): If your loan purpose does not match any of the provided options, select this option and describe your purpose in the field below.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in processing your loan application.
* If you are having trouble verifying your employment status, please upload relevant documents to support your application.
* If your loan purpose is "Loan Purpose Other", please clearly describe your purpose in the field provided.
* Contact your lender if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
