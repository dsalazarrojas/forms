# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a specific purpose, which is to be determined by the user selecting the category of the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the form.
2. Select the category that best describes the purpose of the form.
3. Provide a brief description of the form.
4. Enter the form's ID number.
5. Enter the filename of the output.
6. Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
7. Select the status of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (form_title)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the form.
* **Category (category)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the category that best describes the purpose of the form.
* **Description (description)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a short description of the form to provide additional context.
* **Form ID (form_id)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the form.
* **Output Filename (output_filename)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the filename of the output of the form.
* **Submitted Date (submitted_date)** (`date`, required: false): Select the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time (submitted_time)** (`time`, required: false): Select the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Status (form_status)** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the form from the available options.

## Tips

* Make sure to select a category that accurately reflects the purpose of the form.
* Enter a description that is brief and to the point.
* Enter the correct form ID to ensure that the form is properly identified.
* Enter a filename that makes sense for the output of the form.
* Select the correct submitted date and time.
* Choose the correct status of the form to reflect its current state.
