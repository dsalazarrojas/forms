# Wealth Management Loan Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Management Loan Inquiry form is a tool used to collect information from clients about their loan preferences and needs. This form helps our team to better understand the client's financial situation and provide personalized guidance on available loan options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Specify the type of loan you are interested in.
3. If applicable, indicate the date of your last review.
4. Schedule a follow-up in 1, 2, or 3 weeks if you'd like us to contact you again.
5. If you have any additional notes or requests regarding your loan inquiry, please include them in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Loan Information (Loan Information)**: Specify the type of loan you are interested in.
* **Review Date (Review Date)**: If you've had a review recently, please indicate the date of your last review.
* **Follow Up (Follow Up)**: Schedule a follow-up in 1, 2, or 3 weeks if you'd like us to contact you again.
* **Loan Request (Loan Request)**: Any additional notes or requests regarding your loan inquiry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure proper processing of your loan inquiry.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward; if you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
