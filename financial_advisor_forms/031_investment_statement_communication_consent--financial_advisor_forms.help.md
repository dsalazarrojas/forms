This help guide will guide users through the process of filling out the "Investment Statement Communication Consent" form.
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients regarding their investment statement communication preferences and consent status. This form will help financial advisors gather necessary contact information and consent to share investment statements with clients.

To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
4. (Optional) Enter the client's address in the "Address" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent Given" field to indicate whether the client has provided their consent.
6. Select the communication preferences in the "Communication Preferences" field (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail, In-person).
7. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about the client in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide the client's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address if necessary.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the client has provided their consent.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select multiple, required): Select how the client would like to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail, In-person).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client.
