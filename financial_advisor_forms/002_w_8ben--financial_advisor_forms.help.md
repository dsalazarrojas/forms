# W 8Ben - Help Guide
## Purpose

The W 8Ben form is a critical document used for identifying individuals who are eligible to be considered US persons for tax purposes. It is used to collect information about an individual's citizenship status, tax identification, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information in the following sections:
	* Personal Information (Pages 1-5)
	* Business Information (Page 6)
	* Foreign Status and Residency (Pages 7-10)
	* Certification and Signature (Pages 11-12)
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Page 1, text, required)
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Page 2, text, required)
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employer Identification Number** (Page 3, text, required)
	+ Enter your EIN if you are an employer.
* **Business Name** (Page 4, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of your business if applicable.
* **Address** (Page 5, text, required)
	+ Enter your physical address.
* **City** (Page 6, text, required)
	+ Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (Page 7, text, required)
	+ Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code** (Page 8, text, required)
	+ Enter your 5-digit zip code.
* **Country** (Page 9, text, required)
	+ Enter your country of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 10, date, required)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employer EIN** (Page 11, text, required)
	+ Enter your EIN if you are an employer.
* **Foreign Status** (Page 12, select_one, required)
	+ Choose your foreign status from the options provided.
* **Foreign Address** (Page 13, text, required)
	+ Enter your foreign address.
* **Foreign Phone** (Page 14, text, required)
	+ Enter your foreign phone number.
* **Email** (Page 15, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Certification** (Page 16, select_multiple, required)
	+ Choose your certification status from the options provided.
* **Signature** (Page 17, text, required)
	+ Sign your name.
* **Date** (Page 18, date, required)
	+ Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (Page 19, time, required)
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Note** (Page 20, note, not required)
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **EIN** (Page 21, text, required)
	+ Enter your EIN if applicable.
* **Foreign Country** (Page 22, text, required)
	+ Enter your foreign country of residence.
* **Foreign Tax ID** (Page 23, text, required)
	+ Enter your foreign tax ID.
* **Foreign Taxpayer Identification** (Page 24, text, required)
	+ Enter your foreign taxpayer ID.
* **Certificate Date** (Page 25, date, required)

Note: This guide is for general use and does not replace official government documentation. If you are unsure about any information or requirements, please consult the official IRS guidelines or a tax professional.
