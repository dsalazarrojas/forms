# Budget Allocation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture budget allocation requests from departments to the financial team. It will help the financial team understand the department's spending priorities and needs for a specific budget period.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Identify your department and select it as the respondent department.
2. Specify the budget period for which you are requesting allocation.
3. List your top priority areas for budget allocation.
4. Provide the total requested budget amount for the period.
5. Explain the reasoning behind your requested allocation (justification).
6. If applicable, describe the expected impact if your allocation is denied.
7. If applicable, specify alternative funding sources.
8. Enter your contact email address so the financial team can follow up on any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Respondent department** (Text, Required)
Enter the name of your department.

### 2. **Fiscal period** (Text, Required)
Enter the budget period for which you are making this request (e.g., Q1, Q2, Q3, etc.).

### 3. **Priority areas** (Text, Required)
List the top priority areas or projects that will benefit from the allocated funds.

### 4. **Requested amount** (Number, Required)
Enter the total amount of budget requested for the specified period.

### 5. **Justification** (Text, Required)
Explain the reasoning behind your requested allocation. Describe how the funds will be used and what benefits it will bring to your department.

### 6. **Impact if denied** (Text, Optional)
If you expect significant consequences or impacts if your request is denied, describe them here.

### 7. **Alternative funding** (Text, Optional)
If you have other funding sources or alternatives, mention them here.

### 8. **Contact email** (Email, Required)
Enter your email address for the financial team to follow up on any questions or concerns.
