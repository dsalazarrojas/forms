# Qualified Charitable Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors regarding their charitable contributions. Financial advisors will use this form to track the frequency and amount of donations made to various charitable organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form and ensure you have all the required information.
* Fill out each field with the relevant information.
* Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Identification** (`donor_identification`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to identify the donor who made the charitable contribution. Enter the donor's unique identifier (ID).
* **Charitable Organization** (`charitable_organization`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select the charitable organization that received the donation. Choose from the available options:
  - '0'
  - '1'
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often the gift was made:
  - Once
  - Reoccurring
* **Gift Date** (`gift_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the gift was made.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the gift made.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your financial advisor.
