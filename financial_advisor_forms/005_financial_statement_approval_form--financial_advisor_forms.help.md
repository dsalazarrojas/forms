# Financial Statement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a financial statement, including the date it was issued and the person or entity that approved it. The form is designed to streamline the approval process and ensure that all necessary information is collected in a standardized format.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Financial Statement Form** field with the relevant details about the financial statement.
2. Enter the **Date of Financial Statement** by selecting the correct date.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Approved By** field to indicate if the financial statement has been approved.
4. Enter the name of the person or entity that approved the financial statement in the **Approved By** field.
5. Enter the email address of the person or entity that approved the financial statement in the **Approved By Email** field.
6. Enter any additional comments about the financial statement in the **Comments** field.
7. Upload any supporting file or document in the **File** field.
8. Enter the attachment of the financial statement in the **Financial Statement Attachment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Statement Form** (`financial_statement_form`, text, required): Enter the details about the financial statement.
* **Date of Financial Statement** (`date_of_financial_statement`, date, required): Enter the date the financial statement was issued.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the financial statement has been approved.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity that approved the financial statement.
* **Approved By Email** (`approved_by_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or entity that approved the financial statement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments about the financial statement.
* **File** (`file`, text, required): Upload any supporting file or document for the financial statement.
* **Financial Statement Attachment** (`financial_statement_attachment`, text, required): Enter the attachment of the financial statement.
