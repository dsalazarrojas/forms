# Financial Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Client Intake Form is a crucial part of the financial planning process. It helps us understand your financial situation, goals, and preferences, which enables us to create a personalized financial plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Use the provided options or type in your answers in the text fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the starting point of the form where you can provide us with your general information, such as your name, address, and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact details, including your email and phone number, so we can reach out to you for any further information or questions.
* **Employment Status** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your current employment status from the options: Active or Inactive.
* **Income Sources** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your income sources, such as salary, investments, or other sources. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Employment Information** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If you are employed, please provide your employment information, such as your job title, employer, and duration of employment.
* **Income History** (`6`, `number`, required: false): If you have any income history, please provide it in numerical format.
* **Financial Information** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant financial information, such as your savings, investments, or debt.
* **Dependents** (`8`, `text`, required: false): If you have dependents, please list their names and ages.
* **Financial Goals** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Briefly describe your financial goals and objectives.
* **Financial History** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant financial history, such as past investments or financial achievements.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your risk tolerance level, choosing between Yes, No, or Other.
* **Investment Goals** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check any investment goals you may have, such as short-term or long-term goals.
* **Financial Assets** (`13`, `text`, required: false): List your financial assets, such as savings, investments, or properties.
* **Debt** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Share any debt you may have, including the amount and type.
* **Tax Status** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your tax status, choosing between Active or Inactive.
* **Tax Filing Status** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your tax filing status, choosing between Active or Inactive.
* **Financial Planner** (`17`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial planner or advisor.
* **Financial Advisor** (`18`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial advisor.
* **Financial Institution** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial institution.
* **Other Investments** (`20`, `text`, required: false): List any other investments you may have.
* **Risk Tolerant** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your risk tolerance level, choosing between Yes, No, or Other.
* **Financial Planner 2** (`22`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial planner or advisor.
* **Financial Advisor 2** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial advisor.
* **Financial Institution 2** (`24`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you have a financial institution.
* **Other Investments 2** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List any other investments you may have.

Note: Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately to ensure we can create a tailored financial plan for you. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
