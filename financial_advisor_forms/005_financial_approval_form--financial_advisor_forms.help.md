# Financial Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Approval Form is a crucial document used to gather and track financial information. It helps financial advisors and accountants make informed decisions regarding budget requests, expense reimbursement, and funding allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked as `true` in the form.
2. Enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Information** (Financial Information, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the financial request.
* **Budget Request** (Budget Request, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed breakdown of the budget request if applicable.
* **Expense Reimbursement** (Expense Reimbursement, `text`, optional): Enter any expense reimbursement information related to the request.
* **Funding Allocation** (Funding Allocation, `text`, optional): Specify the funding allocation for the request.
* **Approval Status** (Approval Status, `select_one`, required): Select the correct approval status from the options provided.
* **Email** (Email, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Financial Approval** (Financial Approval, `number`, optional): Enter a numerical value for the financial approval.
* **Date** (Date, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the financial request.
* **Time** (Time, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the financial request.
* **Financial Approvals** (Financial Approvals, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the financial approvals for the request.
* **Financial Notes** (Financial Notes, `text`, optional): Enter any additional financial notes.
* **Financial Details** (Financial Details, `text`, optional): Provide any additional financial details.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the approval process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the financial advisor or accountant for clarification.
