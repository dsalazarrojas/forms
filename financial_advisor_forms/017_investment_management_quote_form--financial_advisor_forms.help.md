# Investment Management Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Management Quote Form is designed to gather essential information from clients to create a tailored investment strategy that meets their unique needs and goals. This form helps our financial advisors understand the client's investment preferences, risk tolerance, and financial objectives, enabling them to provide the most suitable investment solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's sections and fields carefully.
2.  Select the correct investment type from the dropdown menu.
3.  Choose the appropriate investment timeframe.
4.  Indicate your risk tolerance level.
5.  Input your expected investment return.
6.  Select the investment period.
7.  Indicate your invested amount.
8.  Input your goal amount.
9.  Provide any additional information about yourself as an investor.
10. Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, select_one, **required: false**): This field allows you to select the type of investment you are interested in, such as Equity, Fixed Income, or Alternative Investment. Please choose one or more from the provided options.
* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, number, **required: false**): This field is intended for you to input your asset allocation percentage or amount, but it seems to be optional. If you have a specific asset allocation in mind, please provide it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Investment Goal** (`investment_goal`, text, **required: false**): Describe your investment goal, such as growing your wealth, generating income, or saving for a specific purpose.
* **Investment Goal Value** (`investment_goal_value`, select_one, **required: false**): Choose the term that best fits your investment goal: Short term, Medium term, or Long term.
* **Timeframe** (`timeframe`, time, **required: false**): Select the timeframe for your investment, such as One year or less, or Over one year.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, **required: false**): Indicate your risk tolerance level on a scale, but be aware that the scale is not provided in the form. This field might be intended for a numerical input or a select_one type, but it seems to be a number type. Please clarify this with the form developer or financial advisor.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, number, **required: false**): Input your expected investment return, but be aware that the unit or scale is not provided. Please ensure you understand the expected input format before filling out this field.
* **Investment Period** (`investment_period`, select_one, **required: false**): Choose the investment period from the provided options: One year or less, or Over one year.
* **Invested Amount** (`investment_invested_amount`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Select the invested amount range you are interested in, such as Less than $100,000 or More than $100,000.
* **Investment Goal Amount** (`investment_goal_amount`, number, **required: false**): Input your investment goal amount, but be aware that the unit or scale is not provided. Please ensure you understand the expected input format before filling out this field.
* **Additional Investor Info** (`additional_investor_info`, note, **required: false**): This field is a note for you to provide any additional information about yourself as an investor.
* **Additional Investor Info 2** (`additional_investor_info2`, note, **required: false**): This field is another note for any additional information about yourself as an investor.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Confirm that the provided information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose and expected input. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with a financial advisor or the form developer.
