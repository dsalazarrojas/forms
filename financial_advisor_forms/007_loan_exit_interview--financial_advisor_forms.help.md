Loan Exit Interview - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
The Loan Exit Interview is a form used to collect information from borrowers who are about to exit their loan or have already exited their loan. This form helps our organization to gather insights on the borrowers' experience, satisfaction, and any challenges they faced during the loan process. This information will help us improve our services and provide better support to future borrowers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Loan Exit Interview" link.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
3. Select the repayment option(s) that apply to your situation.
4. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Loan Details
**Loan Details** (`loan_details`, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide any additional information about your loan, such as the loan amount, interest rate, or any other relevant details.

### Contact Info
**Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide any contact information, such as your phone number or email address, in case we need to reach out to you for further clarification or support.

### Repayment Option
**Repayment Option** (`repayment_option`, select_multiple, optional)
This is a multiple choice field where you can select one or more repayment options that apply to your situation. Select all options that are relevant to your loan exit:

*   Option 1
*   Option 2
*   Option 3

Tips:

*   Please answer the questions accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the Loan Details field.
*   If you need any assistance or clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
