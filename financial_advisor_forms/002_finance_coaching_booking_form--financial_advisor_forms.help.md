# Finance Coaching Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Coaching Booking Form is a tool used to gather information and schedule sessions with financial advisors. This form helps users receive personalized coaching and guidance on financial matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (name, phone, and email) in the Session Details section.
2. Select your preferred start and end dates for the session.
3. Choose a session topic that best suits your needs (Budgeting, Investment, Retirement, or Wealth Management).
4. Select the assigned advisor you would like to work with (Jim, John, Jane, Bob, or other).
5. Select additional available advisors you would be interested in meeting with (John, Jane, Bob, or Sarah).
6. Specify the status of your booking (Open, Closed).
7. Review and update any additional information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details (1)**: Enter your name and contact information to help us understand your needs.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name to associate with your booking.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address for communication updates.
* **Start Date (5)**: Choose a date for the session to commence.
* **End Date (6)**: Choose an end date for the session.
* **Time (7)**: Select a time for the session to take place.
* **Session Topic (8)**: Choose a topic that aligns with your financial needs (Budgeting, Investment, Retirement, or Wealth Management).
* **Assigned Advisor (10)**: Select the advisor you would like to work with.
* **Available Advisors (11)**: Choose other advisors you would like to consider for the session.
* **Booking Status (12)**: Indicate the status of your booking (Open, Closed).
* **Created At (13)**: Displays the date the booking was created.
* **Updated At (14)**: Displays the last time the booking was updated.
* **Deleted At (15)**: Displays the date the booking was deleted.
* **Created By (16)**: Displays the user who created the booking.
* **Updated By (17)**: Displays the user who last updated the booking.
* **Deleted By (18)**: Displays the user who deleted the booking.
* **Comments (19)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the advisor.
* **Notes (20)**: Enter any additional notes about your session.
* **Attachments (21)**: Upload any relevant documents or files for the session.
* **Session Status (22)**: Choose the status of your session (Pending, Scheduled, Completed).
* **Next Session (23)**: Displays the date of your next session.
* **Last Session (24)**: Displays the date of your last session.
* **User Notes (25)**: Enter any additional notes about your session.

This form helps ensure accurate and efficient communication between you and your assigned advisor.
