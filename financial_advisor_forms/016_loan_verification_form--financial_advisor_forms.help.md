# Loan Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loan Verification Form is a document used by financial advisors or institutions to gather information about an individual's employment status and financial information. This information is crucial in assessing the applicant's creditworthiness and making informed decisions about loan approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `name` field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your `date_of_birth` in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
3. Enter your `social_security_number` as it appears on your identification documents.
4. Select your current `employment_status` from the list:
	* Employed
	* Unemployed
	* Self-Employed
5. If employed, enter your `income` in the format `###`.
6. If employed, enter your `employment_start_date` in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
7. If employed, enter your `employment_end_date` in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
8. If employed, enter the length of time you have been employed in the `employment_length` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **date_of_birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **social_security_number** (text, optional): Enter your Social Security number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **employment_status** (select_one, required):
	+ Employed: If you are working for an employer.
	+ Unemployed: If you are not working for an employer.
	+ Self-Employed: If you own your own business or work as an independent contractor.
* **income** (number, optional): Enter your monthly income in the format `###`.
* **employment_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you started working in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **employment_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you ended working in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **employment_length** (number, optional): Enter the length of time you have been employed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate information to avoid any issues with loan processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field explanation above.
* If you have any questions or issues with this form, please contact the financial advisor responsible for your case.
