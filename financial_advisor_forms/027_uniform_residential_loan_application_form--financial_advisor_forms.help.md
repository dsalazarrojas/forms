# Uniform Residential Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a residential loan application. It gathers contact, property, employment, income, and credit details from both the borrower and co-borrower. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us better understand your financial situation and facilitate the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you provide all required information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date details about yourself and co-borrower (if applicable).
3. Use the provided fields to input your information, such as contact, property, employment, income, and credit details.
4. Check the form for errors or missing fields before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Contact Info (`borrower_contact_info`):** This field is for the borrower's contact information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Property Info (`property_info`):** Describe the property you're seeking a loan for, including its address, type (e.g., single-family home, condo, townhouse), and estimated value.
* **Credit History (`credit_history`):** Share your credit history, including any past credit accounts, credit scores, and relevant financial events.
* **Employment Info (`employment_info`):** Provide details about your employment status, job title, employer, and length of employment.
* **Income Info (`income_info`):** Report your income details, including your income type, amount, and sources.
* **Co-Borrower Contact Info (`co_borrower_contact_info`):** If you have a co-borrower, fill out this section with their contact information.
* **Co-Borrower Property Info (`co_borrower_property_info`):** If you have a co-borrower, describe the property they're associated with the loan.
* **Co-Borrower Employment Info (`co_borrower_employment_info`):** If you have a co-borrower, provide details about their employment status.
* **Co-Borrower Income Info (`co_borrower_income_info`):** If you have a co-borrower, report their income details.
* **Co-Borrower Credit History (`co_borrower_credit_history`):** If you have a co-borrower, share their credit history.
