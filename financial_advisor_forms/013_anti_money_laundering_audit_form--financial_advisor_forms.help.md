# Anti Money Laundering Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Money Laundering Audit Form is used to guide the audit process and ensure that the institution's anti-money laundering (AML) practices and procedures are properly evaluated. This form is to be filled out during the audit process to help auditors collect and analyze data effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the correct audit reference number.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the form.
3. For each field, enter the required information or select the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Audit Reference Number** (1, text, Required): Unique audit identifier.
* **Financial Institution Name** (2, text, Required): Enter the name of the financial institution being audited.
* **Institution Type** (3, select_one, Required): Select the type of financial institution (e.g., Bank, Credit union, Investment firm).
* **Audit Date** (4, date, Required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Audit Period Start Date** (5, date, Required): Enter the beginning of the review period.
* **Audit Period End Date** (6, date, Required): Enter the end of the review period.
* **Lead Auditor Name** (7, text, Required): Enter the name of the primary auditor conducting the review.
* **Audit Team Size** (8, number, Required): Enter the approximate number of auditors.
* **Audit Scope** (9, select_multiple, Required): Select all applicable areas being reviewed (e.g., Customer due diligence, Transaction monitoring).
* **Institution Risk Rating** (10, select_one, Required): Select the overall risk classification of the institution.
* **Customer Due Diligence Compliance** (11, select_one, Required): Rate the level of customer due diligence compliance.
* **KYP Procedures Assessment** (12, select_one, Required): Assess the adequacy of Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures.
* **Enhanced Due Diligence Implementation** (13, select_one, Required): Determine if enhanced due diligence is implemented for high-risk customers.
* **Transaction Monitoring System** (14, select_one, Required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the transaction monitoring system.
* **Suspicious Activity Report Filing** (15, select_one, Required): Rate the timeliness and accuracy of SAR filings.
* **Number of SARs Filed in Period** (16, number, Optional): Enter the total number of SARs filed during the review period.
* **Record Keeping Compliance** (17, select_one, Required): Assess the institution's record-keeping compliance.
* **AML Training Program** (18, select_one, Required): Evaluate the quality of the anti-money laundering training program.
* **Staff Training Rate** (19, number, Required): Enter the approximate percentage of staff trained.
* **Risk Assessment Program** (20, select_one, Required): Determine if risk assessment is conducted regularly.
* **Sanctions Screening Process** (21, select_one, Required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the sanctions screening process.
* **PEP Monitoring** (22, select_one, Required): Assess the level of politically exposed persons (PEP) monitoring.
* **-- Audit Findings --** (23, note, Optional): Enter any notable findings or comments.
* **Critical Findings** (24, number, Required): Enter the number of critical issues identified.
* **Major Findings** (25, number, Required): Enter the number of major issues identified.
* **Minor Findings** (26, number, Required): Enter the number of minor issues identified.
* **Description of Key Findings** (27, text, Required): Summarize the main issues identified.
* **Recommendations** (28, text, Required): Enter suggested corrective actions for the institution.
* **Recommended Remediation Timeline** (29, text, Required): Enter the timeframe for addressing issues.
* **Follow-up Audit Required** (30, select_one, Required): Determine if additional review is required.
* **Overall Compliance Rating** (31, select_one, Required): Enter the final assessment of overall compliance.
* **Lead Auditor Signature** (32, text, Required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Management Response** (33, text, Optional): Enter the institution's response to findings.
* **Audit Completion Date** (34, date, Required): Enter the date the audit was finalized.

Note: This guide is for the Anti Money Laundering Audit Form, not for the institution's internal use. If you're unsure about any field, contact the audit team or refer to the audit guidelines.
