# Wealth Management Custodian Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a record of authorization and custodianship for an individual or entity's financial assets or property. It captures necessary information from the custodian or authorized party, including their name, contact details, and authorization date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the individual or entity's name.
2. Provide the custodian's or authorized party's name.
3. Enter the account holder's name.
4. Select the account type from the provided options.
5. Choose the authorized party from the options provided.
6. Enter the authorization date.
7. Select the custodian authorized party.
8. Enter the custodian's phone number and address.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, text, Optional)
* Enter the first name of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Last Name (`last_name`, text, Optional)
* Enter the last name of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Email (`email`, email, Optional)
* Enter the email address of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Phone (`phone`, text, Optional)
* Enter the phone number of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Address (`address`, text, Optional)
* Enter the address of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Account Number (`account_number`, number, Optional)
* Enter the account number associated with the individual or entity's financial assets or property.

### Account Type (`account_type`, select_one, Optional)
* Select the type of account being entrusted, such as "Checking", "Savings", or "Investment".

### Custodian Authorization (`custodian_authorization`, select_multiple, Optional)
* Select all applicable options for the custodian's authorization, such as "Full", "Limited", or "Emergency".

### Authorization Date (`authorization_date`, date, Optional)
* Enter the date of the authorization.

### Authorized Party (`authorized_party`, select_one, Optional)
* Choose the party being authorized, such as "Spouse", "Parent", or "Sibling".

### Custodian Name (`custodian_name`, select_one, Optional)
* Select the name of the custodian or authorized party.

### Custodian Address (`custodian_address`, text, Optional)
* Enter the address of the custodian or authorized party.

### Custodian Phone (`custodian_phone`, text, Optional)
* Enter the phone number of the custodian or authorized party.

### Custodian Authorization Date (`custodian_authorization_date`, date, Optional)
* Enter the date of the custodian's authorization.

### Custodian Authorized Party (`custodian_authorized_party`, select_one, Optional)
* Choose the party being authorized by the custodian, such as "Spouse", "Parent", or "Sibling".

### Custodian Notes (`custodian_notes`, select_multiple, Optional)
* Select all applicable options for the custodian's notes, such as "Full", "Limited", or "Emergency".

### Custodian Additional Notes (`custodian_additional_notes`, text, Optional)
* Enter any additional comments or notes for the custodian.

### Custodian Signature (`custodian_signature`, date, Optional)
* Enter the date of the custodian's signature.

### Account Holder Name (`account_holder_name`, text, Optional)
* Enter the name of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Account Holder Address (`account_holder_address`, text, Optional)
* Enter the address of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Account Holder Phone (`account_holder_phone`, text, Optional)
* Enter the phone number of the individual or entity being entrusted.

### Account Holder Relationship (`account_holder_relationship`, select_one, Optional)
* Choose the relationship of the individual or entity being entrusted, such as "Spouse", "Parent", or "Sibling".

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.
* Make sure to attach a copy of this form to the relevant account documents.
* Update the form as necessary when there are changes in the custodianship or authorized party.
