# Income Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income Survey form is used to collect information about an individual's income levels, financial advisor forms, and other relevant details. This form helps in providing insight into the financial situation of the individuals within the target group.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Target Group**: Select the relevant target group that you belong to (Group A, B, C, or D).
2. **Income**: Enter your income level (number).
3. **Level**: Enter your level of income (text).
4. **Population**: Enter the population that you belong to (text).
5. **Population Description**: Provide a description of the population that you belong to (text).
6. **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID of this form (number).
7. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for financial aid (chatjimmy or other).
8. **Financial Aid**: Select whether you receive financial aid or not ('True' or 'False').
9. **Financial Aid Description**: Provide a description of your financial aid situation (text).
10. **Date**: Enter the date when you last received financial aid or the date when you expect to receive it (date).
11. **Time**: Enter the time when you last received financial aid or the time when you expect to receive it (time).
12. **Email**: Enter your email address (email).
13. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (text).
14. **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments (text).
15. **Email Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your email (text).
16. **Phone Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your phone (text).
17. **Financial Aid Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your financial aid (text).
18. **Population Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your population (text).
19. **Target Group Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to your target group (text).
20. **Notes**: Enter any additional general notes or comments (text).
