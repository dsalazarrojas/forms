# Credit Reference Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a credit reference for a specific individual. It is intended to be used by clients or customers to provide essential information about themselves for credit evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about yourself, including your name, company, job title, address, phone number, date of birth, and employment history.
2. Ensure that all the fields are filled out as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company**: (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your current or past employer.
* **Job Title**: (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title or position held at your company.
* **Address**: (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address or the address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment History**: (`employment_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant information about your employment history, such as the duration of employment and your job responsibilities.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the credit reference process goes smoothly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is only to be used for credit evaluation purposes and should not be used for other purposes.
