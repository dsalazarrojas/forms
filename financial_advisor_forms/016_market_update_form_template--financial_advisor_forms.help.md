# Market Update Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from analysts about market-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start filling in the form" button to begin.
2. Enter the title of the market update.
3. Briefly describe the market conditions.
4. Provide any trend data or other relevant information.
5. Share your analyst recommendations.
6. Outline the current market trends.
7. Specify the next steps or follow-up actions.
8. Select a date for the review and assessment.
9. Choose a time for the review and assessment.
10. Enter the name of the reviewer or analyst.
11. Optionally, specify the tools or resources used to gather information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title (`page_1/form_title`, text, required: false)
Enter the title of the market update.

### Form Description (`page_1/form_description`, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the market update.

### Organization (`page_2/organization`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the organization or institution associated with the market update.

### Market Conditions (`page_2/market_conditions`, text, required: false)
Describe the current market conditions.

### Trend Data (`page_2/trend_data`, text, required: false)
Enter any trend data or other relevant information.

### Analyst Recommendations (`page_2/analyst_recommendations`, text, required: false)
Share your analyst recommendations or suggestions.

### Market Trends (`page_2/market_trends`, text, required: false)
Outline the current market trends.

### Next Steps (`page_2/next_steps`, text, required: false)
Specify the next steps or follow-up actions.

### Review Date (`page_3/review_date`, date, required: false)
Select a date for the review and assessment.

### Review Time (`page_3/review_time`, time, required: false)
Choose a time for the review and assessment.

### Reviewer (`page_3/reviewer`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the reviewer or analyst.

### Assigned Tool (`page_4/assigned_tool`, text, required: false)
Optionally, specify the tools or resources used to gather information.

## Tips
This form is for market updates. Please ensure you're filling it out according to your organization's or institution's guidelines. If you're unsure, don't hesitate to reach out for guidance.
