# financial_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial consent form is used to ensure that both the financial practitioner and the patient understand and agree on the financial aspects of their interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each section and its requirements.
2.  Complete each section according to your role as a financial practitioner or a patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Practitioner** (`form_section_1`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their name and description of their practice.
* **Patient** (`form_section_2`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the patient to provide their name and description of their condition.
* **Medical Practitioner Fee** (`form_section_3`, number, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their fee structure.
* **Fees Paid To** (`form_section_4`, select_multiple, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to select the fees paid to, such as Practitioner, Patient, or Other.
* **Fee Payment Date** (`form_section_5`, date, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide the date of payment.
* **Payment Method** (`form_section_6`, select_one, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to select the payment method, such as Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Signature of Practitioner** (`form_section_7`, note, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to sign and provide their name.
* **Signature of Practitioner 2** (`form_section_25`, time, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to sign and provide their name, but this field is not required.
* **Signature of Patient** (`form_section_9`, time, required: false)
	+ This section is for the patient to sign and provide their name, but this field is not required.
* **Financial Practitioner Signature** (`form_section_10`, select_one, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to select the type of financial consent form 2 or 3.
* **Fee Acknowledgement** (`form_section_11`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to acknowledge if the fee is acknowledged, true or false.
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (`form_section_12`, date, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide the date of acknowledgement.
* **Fee Acknowledgement Note** (`form_section_13`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide any additional notes regarding the fee acknowledgement.
* **Medical Practitioner Name** (`form_section_14`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their name.
* **Medical Practitioner Title** (`form_section_15`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their title.
* **Medical Practitioner Address** (`form_section_16`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their address.
* **Medical Practitioner Contact Info** (`form_section_17`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their contact information.
* **Medical Practitioner License Number** (`form_section_18`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their license number.
* **Medical Practitioner Certification Number** (`form_section_19`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their certification number.
* **Medical Practitioner License Date** (`form_section_20`, date, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their license date.
* **Medical Practitioner License Expiration Date** (`form_section_21`, date, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their license expiration date.
* **Medical Practitioner License Status** (`form_section_22`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their license status.
* **Medical Practitioner Fee Schedule** (`form_section_23`, text, required: true)
	+ This section is for the financial practitioner to provide their fee schedule.
* **form_section_24** (`form_section_24`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is not required.
* **form_section_25** (`form_section_25`, time, required: false)
	+ This section is not required.
