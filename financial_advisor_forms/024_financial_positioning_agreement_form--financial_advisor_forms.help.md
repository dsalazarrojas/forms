# Financial Positioning Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Positioning Agreement Form is designed to collect information from participants about their financial positioning, risk assessment, and investment objectives. This form helps users evaluate and manage their investment risks and returns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the text fields (Financial Positioning Terms, Participant Acknowledgement, Financial Positioning Objectives, Participant Commitment, Financial Positioning Statement, Financial Positioning Plan) as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed and clear explanations.
2. Choose the appropriate risk assessment level (High, Medium, Low) for your current financial situation.
3. Select the risk tolerance level (High, Medium, Low) that reflects your comfort level with taking risks.
4. Pick the investment horizon (Short term, Medium term, Long term) that best describes your financial goals.
5. Choose the appropriate investment allocation (100%, 75-99%, 50-74%, 25-49%, Less than 25%) that reflects your current investment strategy.
6. Select the risk level (Aggressive, Moderate, Conservative) that best represents your risk appetite.
7. Choose the investment return (High, Medium, Low) that you expect from your investments.
8. Provide the risk-free return, if applicable.
9. Enter the investment return rate, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Positioning Terms**: This field is where you should describe your current financial situation, including any relevant details about your income, expenses, assets, and liabilities.
* **Participant Acknowledgement**: Please acknowledge that you understand the form's purpose and that you are providing accurate information about your financial positioning.
* **Financial Positioning Objectives**: Here, you should outline your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a specific purpose or achieving a certain financial status.
* **Participant Commitment**: Describe your level of commitment to meeting your financial goals and the steps you plan to take to achieve them.
* **Financial Positioning Statement**: Provide a clear and concise statement of your financial situation, including your income, expenses, assets, and liabilities.
* **Financial Positioning Plan**: Outline your strategy for achieving your financial goals, including any specific investments or actions you plan to take.
* **Risk Assessment**: Choose the risk level that you feel is most relevant to your current financial situation (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Tolerance**: Select the risk level that you are comfortable with (High, Medium, Low).
* **Investment Horizon**: Choose the investment horizon that best represents your financial goals (Short term, Medium term, Long term).
* **Investment Allocation**: Select the investment allocation that best reflects your current strategy (100%, 75-99%, 50-74%, 25-49%, Less than 25%).
* **Risk Level**: Choose the risk level that best represents your risk appetite (Aggressive, Moderate, Conservative).
* **Investment Return**: Select the expected investment return (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Free Return**: If applicable, provide a risk-free return, such as a fixed interest rate or dividend yield.
* **Investment Return Rate**: If applicable, enter the investment return rate, such as a percentage or annual rate of return.
* **Risk Level**: Choose the risk level that best represents your risk appetite.
