# Investment Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Strategy Assessment form is designed to collect information about your investment goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences. This information will help us understand your needs and provide you with tailored investment advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section according to your investment goals and preferences.
2. Select the correct risk tolerance and investment frequency from the provided options.
3. Enter the amount you plan to invest each time.
4. Choose the asset allocation strategy that best suits your needs.
5. Review and confirm your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your risk tolerance level?** (risk_tolerance): This question helps us understand how much risk you're willing to take when investing. Please select from: 
  * Very Conservative: You're very cautious and prefer low-risk investments.
  * Moderate: You're willing to take some risk but still want to be conservative.
  * Aggressive: You're ready to take high risks for potentially high returns.
  * Neutral: You're looking for a balanced approach.
- **What are your short-term and long-term investment goals?** (investment_goals): This question helps us understand what you hope to achieve with your investments. Enter a brief description of your goals.
- **How would you describe your current investment risk level?** (risk_level): This question helps us understand how you've been managing your risk in the past. Please select from: 
  * Very Conservative
  * Moderate
  * Aggressive
  * Neutral
- **Which investments would you like to consider?** (investment_type): Choose all the investment types that appeal to you. Please select from: 
  * Stocks
  * Bonds
  * Real Estate
  * Commodities
  * Cash
  * Other (please specify)
- **Please choose your asset allocation strategy** (asset_allocation): This question helps us understand how you'd like to distribute your assets. Please select from: 
  * Conservative
  * Moderate
  * Aggressive
  * Balanced
- **How often do you plan to invest?** (investment_frequency): This question helps us understand your investment frequency. Please select from: 
  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Monthly
  * Quarterly
  * Annually
- **How much do you plan to invest each time?** (investment_amount): This question helps us understand how much you're willing to invest. Please enter the amount.
- **How often do you plan to invest in the next month?** (investment_frequency_2): This question helps us understand your investment frequency for the next month. Please select from: 
  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Monthly
  * Quarterly
  * Annually
- **How much do you plan to invest in the next month?** (investment_amount_2): This question helps us understand how much you're willing to invest in the next month. Please enter the amount.
- **Are there any other investment types you'd like to consider?** (other_invest_types): If you have any other investment types in mind, please enter them here.
- **How do you plan to allocate your assets for this investment?** (asset_allocation_2): This question helps us understand your asset allocation strategy for this investment. Please select from: 
  * Conservative
  * Moderate
  * Aggressive
  * Balanced
- **How would you describe your risk level for this investment?** (risk_level_2): This question helps us understand your risk level for this investment. Please select from: 
  * Very Conservative
  * Moderate
  * Aggressive
  * Neutral

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible investment advice.
- If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask us for clarification.
- This form will take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.
