# Borrower's Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standardized template for borrowers to authorize access to their financial information and data. It is designed to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and protect borrower data privacy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
2. Provide your complete mailing address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
3. Enter your primary contact phone number.
4. Enter your email address for correspondence.
5. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Enter your Social Security Number (last 4 digits only for security purposes).
7. Enter the name of the financial institution or bank.
8. Enter your loan or account reference number.
9. Select the type of information you authorize to be accessed (e.g., account balance, payment history, credit report).
10. If applicable, list individuals or entities authorized to receive information.
11. Briefly explain the purpose of the authorization.
12. Enter the start date when this authorization becomes effective.
13. If applicable, enter the end date when this authorization expires.
14. Sign with your full legal name as an electronic signature.
15. Date of signing.
16. If desired, have a witness sign as an optional witness.
17. Enter any additional comments or special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents. This is a required field.
* **Borrower Address**: Complete your mailing address including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Borrower Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Borrower Email Address**: Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for security purposes.
* **Financial Institution Name**: Enter the name of the bank or lending institution.
* **Loan Account Number**: Enter your loan or account reference number.
* **Type of Authorization**: Select the type of information you authorize to be accessed (e.g., account balance, payment history, credit report).
* **Authorized Third Party Contacts**: List individuals or entities authorized to receive information (if applicable).
* **Purpose of Authorization**: Briefly explain why this authorization is being granted.
* **Authorization Start Date**: Enter when this authorization becomes effective.
* **Authorization End Date**: Enter when this authorization expires (if applicable).
* **Borrower Signature**: Sign with your full legal name as an electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Date of signing.
* **Witness Name**: Optional witness for the authorization.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or special instructions.
