# Bank Reference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bank Reference Form is used to collect information for a banking reference that a bank employee or officer needs to provide.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name and contact email and phone number.
2.  Enter your mailing address for communication purposes.
3.  Select the purpose of the reference, such as opening a new bank account or applying for a loan.
4.  Choose the type of reference you want to provide, for example, personal banking or business banking.
5.  Enter the bank's name and branch location.
6.  Enter your account information, including account number, account type, average balance, and account standing.
7.  Specify your overdraft and loan history with the bank.
8.  Enter the information of who should receive the reference, including their name, organization, and email.
9.  Confirm that you authorize this reference to be sent.
10.  Enter the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_full_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person requesting the reference.
*   **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **Mailing Address** (`requester_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address for communication purposes.
*   **Purpose of Reference** (`reference_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose why you need this reference, for example, opening a new bank account or applying for a loan.
*   **Reference Type** (`reference_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of reference, such as personal banking or business banking.
*   **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the bank that should provide this reference.
*   **Branch Location** (`bank_branch`, `text`, not required): Enter the branch location of the bank that should provide this reference.
*   **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required): Enter your account number.
*   **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of account, such as checking or savings.
*   **Account Opening Date** (`account_opening_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you opened your account.
*   **Average Balance Range** (`average_balance`, `select_one`, required): Enter the typical balance of your account.
*   **Account Standing** (`account_standing`, `select_one`, required): Specify the status of your account.
*   **Overdraft History** (`overdraft_history`, `select_one`, required): Describe your overdraft history with the bank.
*   **Loan History with Bank** (`loan_history`, `select_one`, not required): Specify your loan history with the bank.
*   **Credit Card History** (`credit_card_history`, `select_one`, not required): Describe your history with credit cards held with the bank.
*   **Reference Recipient** (`reference_recipient`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who should receive the reference.
*   **Recipient Organization** (`recipient_organization`, `text`, required): Enter the organization or institution that should receive the reference.
*   **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the person who should receive the reference.
*   **Authorization** (`authorization`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you authorize this reference.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your request.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any other relevant information for the reference.
