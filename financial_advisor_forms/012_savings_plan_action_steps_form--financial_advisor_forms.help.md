# Savings Plan Action Steps Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help financial advisors and users plan and track their savings goals and action steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Review each field to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Submit the form to review and analyze your savings plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_steps (text)** (`savings_plan_action_steps_1`, text, not required): Enter your initial steps or goals for achieving savings.
* **action_items_1 (select_one)** (`action_items_1`, select one, not required): Choose one of the options to represent an action item you plan to take.
* **timeline_start_date (date)** (`timeline_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date for the timeline or period for which you are planning to save.
* **action_items_1 (select_one)** (`action_items_2`, select one, not required): Choose one of the options to represent another action item.
* **timeline_start_date_2 (date)** (`timeline_start_date_2`, date, not required): Enter the start date for another timeline or period.
* ... (similar fields continue with `action_items_3`, `action_items_4`, etc.)

Please note that some fields have similar names, but they are used to capture different data points or action items. Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure the form is filled out correctly.
