# customer_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers applying for a credit application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the "Credit History" field to choose how you got your credit (e.g., Bank, Credit Card, Other).
2. Enter your income details in the "Income Details" field.
3. Select your employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
4. Enter your credit score in the "Credit Score" field.
5. Enter the amount of credit you are applying for in the "Credit Amount" field.
6. Select the purpose of the loan in the "Purpose Of Loan" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field (optional).
11. Enter your city in the "City" field (optional).
12. Enter your state in the "State" field (optional).
13. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field (optional).
14. Enter your social security number in the "Social Security Number" field.
15. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
16. If employed, enter your employment start date in the "Employment Start" field.
17. If employed, enter your employment end date in the "Employment End" field.
18. If the loan starts and ends on different dates, enter the start date in the "Loan Start" field and the end date in the "Loan End" field.
19. If you have employment income, enter it in the "Employment Income" field.
20. If you have other income, enter it in the "Other Income" field.
21. If you are a business owner, enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
22. If you are a business owner, enter your business address in the "Business Address" field.
23. If you are a business owner, enter your business phone number in the "Business Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Credit History**: Select how you got your credit (e.g., Bank, Credit Card, Other).
* **Income Details**: Enter your income details.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, Retired, Unemployed).
* **Credit Score**: Enter your credit score.
* **Credit Amount**: Enter the amount of credit you are applying for.
* **Purpose Of Loan**: Select the purpose of the loan (e.g., Personal, Business, Other).
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your address (optional).
* **City**: Enter your city (optional).
* **State**: Enter your state (optional).
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code (optional).
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Employment Start**: If employed, enter your employment start date.
* **Employment End**: If employed, enter your employment end date.
* **Loan Start**: If the loan starts and ends on different dates, enter the start date.
* **Loan End**: If the loan starts and ends on different dates, enter the end date.
* **Employment Income**: If you have employment income, enter it.
* **Other Income**: If you have other income, enter it.
* **Business Name**: If you are a business owner, enter your business name.
* **Business Address**: If you are a business owner, enter your business address.
* **Business Phone**: If you are a business owner, enter your business phone number.
