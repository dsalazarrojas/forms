# Loan Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Estimate Form is used to estimate loan information and calculate financial data related to loans. It gathers necessary details about the loan to provide a clear estimate of the loan's terms and amounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the loan type by choosing one or more options from the dropdown list. This will help us understand the nature of the loan.
2. Fill in the loan amount, loan term, down payment, interest rate, and other relevant details about the loan.
3. Provide the loan amount paid per year and monthly payment to further specify the loan terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Details**: This field is a text box where you can enter any additional information about the loan.
* **Loan Type**: Select one or more options from the dropdown list to specify the type of loan you are estimating.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the total amount of the loan.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the loan, usually in years.
* **Down Payment**: Enter the down payment made on the loan.
* **Interest Rate**: Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **Loan Amount Paid Per Year**: Enter the loan amount paid per year.
* **Monthly Payment**: Enter the monthly payment for the loan.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the loan, usually in years.
* **Annual Percentage Rate**: Enter the annual percentage rate of the loan.
* **Total Interest**: This field will display the total interest calculated based on the loan details provided.
* **Total Interest Paid**: This field will display the total interest paid based on the loan details provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to get an accurate estimate of the loan.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the loan officer or financial advisor for guidance.
* Double-check the calculations to ensure they align with your expectations.
