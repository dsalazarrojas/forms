# Investment Performance Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Performance Disclosure Consent Form is designed to gather client consent for the sharing of investment performance data. This form is typically used in financial advisory contexts to ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the sharing of their investment data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select whether you consent to the sharing of your investment performance data. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
2. If you select 'Yes', you will be asked about the sharing of your performance data. Select the options that apply to you.
3. If you select 'No', you will be asked to provide a reason for not consenting to the sharing of your performance data.
4. Please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, to ensure we can reach you if needed.
5. If you have an investment company, please provide its name.
6. If you have an investment manager, please provide their name.
7. If you have an investment advisor, please provide their name.
8. If you have a client email address, please provide it.
9. If you have a client phone number, please provide it.
10. Finally, please enter the date and time of submission of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent**: Please select whether you consent to the sharing of your investment performance data. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Performance Data Sharing**: If you select 'Yes' to client consent, you will be asked about the sharing of your performance data. Select the options that apply to you.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your email address and phone number to ensure we can reach you if needed.
* **Investment Company**: If you have an investment company, please provide its name.
* **Investment Manager**: If you have an investment manager, please provide their name.
* **Investment Advisor**: If you have an investment advisor, please provide their name.
* **Client Email**: If you have a client email address, please provide it.
* **Client Phone**: If you have a client phone number, please provide it.
* **Submission Date**: Please enter the date of submission of this form.
* **Submission Time**: Please enter the time of submission of this form.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended for public use. Please ensure that you are completing this form in a secure and private environment.
