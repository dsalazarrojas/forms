# monthly_spending_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your monthly income, expenses, and savings goals. This information will help you understand your overall financial situation and make informed decisions about your money.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your current monthly income in the "income" field. This can be a salary, investment income, or any other regular income you receive.
2. Select all fixed expenses you pay each month from the "fixed_expenses" dropdown list.
3. Choose your variable expenses from the "variable_expenses" dropdown list. These are expenses that can vary from month to month.
4. Select your primary source of income from the "income_sources" dropdown list.
5. Choose your primary savings goal from the "savings_goals" dropdown list.
6. Select all relevant expense categories from the "expense_categories" dropdown list.
7. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include about your financial situation in the "notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field, which will be used for communication purposes.
9. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field, which will be used for communication purposes.
10. Finally, select the current month and year for which you are reporting your financial data in the "date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input (Text)**: This field is for you to enter any additional information you'd like to share about your financial situation.
* **income (Number)**: This field is for you to report your current monthly income.
* **fixed_expenses (Select Multiple)**: Select all fixed expenses you pay each month, such as rent, utilities, or subscription services.
* **variable_expenses (Select Multiple)**: Choose your variable expenses, like groceries or entertainment expenses, that can vary from month to month.
* **income_sources (Select One)**: Select your primary source of income, such as a salary, investment income, or other.
* **savings_goals (Select One)**: Choose your primary savings goal, like short-term or long-term goals.
* **expense_categories (Select Multiple)**: Select all relevant expense categories, like food, transportation, or housing expenses.
* **notes (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your financial situation.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone_number (Text)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **date (Date)**: Select the current month and year for which you are reporting your financial data.
