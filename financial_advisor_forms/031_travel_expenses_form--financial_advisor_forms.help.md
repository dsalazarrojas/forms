# Travel Expenses Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the reporting and reimbursement of travel expenses for employees.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Employee ID, which is your unique identifier.
2. Enter the Trip Dates, which includes the start and end dates of your trip.
3. Provide the Trip Location, where you traveled to.
4. Select the Mode of Transport used for your trip (e.g., Car, Airplane, Bus).
5. Describe the Travel Reason for your trip.
6. If applicable, enter the Reimbursement Claim, which is the amount you are claiming for reimbursement.
7. Enter the Expense Amount, which is the total expense incurred during your trip.
8. Select the person who submitted this form (Manager, HR, Accounting).
9. Enter the Travel Expense Date, which is the date you incurred the expense.
10. Add any Notes, if required or desired.
11. Submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (1, Required): Enter your unique employee ID to identify yourself.
* **Trip Dates** (2, Not Required): Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Trip Location** (3, Not Required): Enter the location you traveled to.
* **Mode of Transport** (4, Not Required): Select the mode of transport used for your trip (Car, Airplane, Bus).
* **Travel Reason** (5, Required): Describe the purpose or reason for your trip.
* **Reimbursement Claim** (6, Not Required): If you are claiming reimbursement, enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Expense Amount** (7, Required): Enter the total expense incurred during your trip.
* **Submitted By** (8, Required): Select the person who submitted this form (Manager, HR, Accounting).
* **Travel Expense Date** (9, Required): Enter the date you incurred the expense.
* **Notes** (10, Not Required): Add any additional notes or comments about your trip.
* **Submitted Date** (11, Not Required): This field seems to be a duplicate of Travel Expense Date.
* **Assigned Tool** (12, Required): Select the tool used for this form (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
* **Submitted User** (13, Required): Select the user who submitted this form (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
* **Category** (14, Not Required): Enter any additional category or description about your trip.
* **Submit** (15, Required): Confirm your submission of this form.
* **Form ID** (16, Not Required): This field seems to be redundant.
* **Travel Class** (17, Not Required): Select the class of your trip (Business, Personal, Company).
* **Submitted At** (18, Not Required): This field seems to be a duplicate of Submitted Date.
* **Submitted By Name** (19, Not Required): This field seems to be a redundant field.
* **Submitter** (20, Required): Select the user who submitted this form (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
* **Form Id 2** (21, Not Required): This field seems to be a redundant field.
* **Submitted At 2** (22, Required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted User 2** (23, Required): Select the user who submitted this form (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
* **Travel Class 2** (24, Not Required): Select the class of your trip (Business, Personal, Company).
* **Submitted User 3** (25, Required): Select the user who submitted this form (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
