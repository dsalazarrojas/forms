# home_financing_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home financing application form is designed to collect information from a home financing applicant, providing a comprehensive overview of their financial situation to determine the feasibility of a home financing application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2.  Select your employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
3.  Enter your income in the "Income" field.
4.  Choose how often you receive income in the "How often do you receive income?" field.
5.  Provide your employment history in the "Employment History" field (if required).
6.  Select any co-borrowers in the "Co-Borrowers" field (if applicable).
7.  Choose the type of property you are financing in the "Type of property" field.
8.  If the type of property is not listed, provide a description in the "Please describe the type of property" field.
9.  Enter the property value in the "Property Value" field.
10.  Enter the desired financing amount in the "Desired Amount of Financing" field.
11.  Select any co-applicants in the "Co-Applicants" field (if applicable).
12.  Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
13.  Enter the property city and state in the "Property City/State" field.
14.  Enter the property zip code in the "Property Zip" field.
15.  Enter the year the property was built in the "Year Built" field.
16.  Enter the property square footage in the "Square Footage" field.
17.  Enter the property age in the "Property Age" field.
18.  Select your credit score in the "Credit Score" field.
19.  If you have a different credit score, provide a description in the "If other, please describe" field.
20.  If you need to verify your income, please refer to the "Income Verification" field.
21.  If you need to verify your co-borrower's credit score, provide a description in the "If other, please describe" field.
22.  If you need to verify your employment status, please refer to the "Employment Verification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)** (`home_financing_application`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Employment Status (3)** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status from the list: Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled, Other.
* **Income (4)** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your monthly income.
* **How often do you receive income? (5)** (`income_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you receive income from the list: Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Never.
* **Employment History (6)** (`employment_history`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your employment history.
* **Co-Borrowers (7)** (`co_borrowers`, select_multiple, optional): Select any co-borrowers from the list: Spouse, Family, Others.
* **Type of property (8)** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property you are financing from the list: Primary Residence, Second Home, Investment Property, Other.
* **Please describe the type of property (9)** (`property_type_other`, text, optional): If the type of property is not listed, provide a description.
* **Property Value (10)** (`property_value`, number, optional): Enter the property value.
* **Desired Amount of Financing (11)** (`desired_funding`, number, optional): Enter the desired financing amount.
* **Co-Applicants (12)** (`co_applicants`, select_multiple, optional): Select any co-applicants from the list: Spouse, Family, Others.
* **Property Location (13)** (`property_location`, text, optional): Enter the property address.
* **Property Address (14)** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address.
* **Property City/State (15)** (`property_city_state`, text, optional): Enter the property city and state.
* **Property Zip (16)** (`property_zip`, text, optional): Enter the property zip code.
* **Year Built (17)** (`property_year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Square Footage (18)** (`property_sqft`, number, optional): Enter the property square footage.
* **Property Age (19)** (`property_age`, number, optional): Enter the property age.
* **Credit Score (20)** (`credit_score`, select_one, optional): Select your credit score from the list: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, N/A.
* **If other, please describe (21)** (`credit_score_other`, text, optional): If you have a different credit score, provide a description.
* **Income Verification (22)** (`income_verification`, note, optional): If you need to verify your income, please refer to this field.
* **Co-Borrower Credit Score (23)** (`co_borrower_credit_score`, select_one, optional): Select your co-borrower's credit score from the list: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, N/A.
* **If other, please describe (24)** (`co_borrower_credit_score_other`, text, optional): If you need to verify your co-borrower's credit score, provide a description.
* **Employment Verification (25)** (`employment_verification`, note, optional): If you need to verify your employment status, please refer to this field.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.
