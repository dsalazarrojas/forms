# Financial Advisory Service Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback mechanism to help us understand your experience with our financial advisory service. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we use it to identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that you are providing feedback for the correct financial advisory service you received.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, `note`, required: false): Please provide your overall feedback about your experience with our financial advisory service. This is an optional but very helpful field where you can express your satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with the service you received?** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how satisfied you are with the service you received from 1 (yes) to 5 (no). This will help us identify areas where we can improve.
* **Service Advisor Rating** (`service_advisor_rating`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the service advisor who assisted you with their Good, Fair, or Bad. This will help us understand how well they performed and where we can improve.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, `note`, required: false): Please provide specific areas where you believe our service can be improved. This will help us tailor our service to your needs better.
* **How can we improve the service?** (`feedback_question_2`, `note`, required: false): This is a space for you to suggest ideas on how we can improve our service. Your input is very valuable to us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns that are not covered in the previous questions, please use this field to share them with us.
* **Is there anything else you would like to tell us?** (`feedback_question_3`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments that did not fit in the previous questions, please share them with us.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `note`, required: false): If you have any specific goals or outcomes you would like to achieve with our service, please share them with us.
