# Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Statement Form is used to collect and analyze financial information from various sources. This form helps us to better understand individuals' or organizations' financial situations, enabling informed decisions and efficient financial planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form fields to ensure you understand what each field represents.
* Fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each drop-down field.
* Submit the form once you have provided all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Details** (Select one):
  + Option 1: This option may refer to a general financial situation or overview.
* **Account Balance**: Enter your current account balance or the balance you're reporting.
* **Account Type** (Select one):
  + Option 1: This option could represent a specific type of account, such as checking or savings.
  + Option 2: Another type of account.
  + Option 3: This option might refer to a unique account classification.
* **Account Holder**: Enter the name of the account holder or the entity's name.
* **Contact Number**: Provide the phone number of the account holder or a contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the account holder or a contact person.
* **Date Submitted**: Select the date the form is submitted.
* **Note** (Section): This is a free-text field where you may provide any additional information or comments related to the submitted data.
