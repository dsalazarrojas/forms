# Finance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Application Form is designed to collect financial information from applicants to assess their eligibility for funding or other financial support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and options.
2. Select the correct options on each page to provide the most accurate information possible.
3. Ensure you provide all required fields (indicated by a star (*) in the field label).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **finance_application_form_page_1_label** (`text`, not required): This field is the first page of the form where you can enter your basic contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
- **finance_application_form_page_2_label** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the options provided to indicate your primary purpose for applying for financial assistance. This will help us assess your eligibility for the support you are requesting.
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- **finance_application_form_page_3_label** (`text`, not required): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your application.
- **finance_application_form_page_4_label** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable options that describe your financial situation. This will help us understand your financial needs and tailor our support accordingly.
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
