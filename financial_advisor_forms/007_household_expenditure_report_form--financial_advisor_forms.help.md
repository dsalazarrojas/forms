# Household Expenditure Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a household's expenditure. This form is used to record and report a household's spending habits and financial transactions. It is an essential tool for financial advisors to assess a household's financial situation and provide guidance on budgeting and financial planning.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about your household, including income and expenses.
2. Select the date of the expenditure to report.
3. Enter your household's income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Household Expenditure Date
* **household_expenditure_date**: Select the date of the expenditure you want to report.

### Household Income
* **household_income**: Enter your household's monthly income.

### Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your financial advisor.
