# Financial Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Service Appointment Form is designed to book a financial service appointment with a client. Fill out this form to schedule a meeting with a financial advisor or planner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your service type (e.g., "Yes" for a financial planning session or "No" for another type of service).
2. Provide any additional information about the service you are requesting (Service Details).
3. Enter the client's name.
4. Enter the client's phone number.
5. Enter the client's email address.
6. Select the date and time for the appointment.
7. Enter the location of the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (`financial_service_appointment_form_service_type`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a financial planning session, or choose "No" if you are requesting another type of service.
* **Service Details** (`financial_service_appointment_form_service_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the service you are requesting.
* **Client Name** (`financial_service_appointment_form_client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone** (`financial_service_appointment_form_client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`financial_service_appointment_form_client_email`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Appointment Date** (`financial_service_appointment_form_appointment_date`, date, required: false): Select the date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`financial_service_appointment_form_appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select the time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`financial_service_appointment_form_appointment_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the appointment.
