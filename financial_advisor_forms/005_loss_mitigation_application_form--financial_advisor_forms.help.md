# Loss Mitigation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loss Mitigation Application Form is a structured way for borrowers to submit their loan application information to financial advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, borrowers can:

1.  Enter their loan application details on the first page.
2.  Provide additional relevant information on subsequent pages.
3.  Fill out all the required fields, which are denoted by asterisk (*) in the field labels, and leave out any optional fields if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Loan Application**
*  *Required* (`loan_application`, `text`, required): This field is meant to be used for borrowers to enter the details of their loan application. 

### **Borrower Info**
*  *Optional* (`borrower_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for borrowers to provide extra information about themselves.

### **Income Proof**
*  *Optional* (`income_proof`, `text`, optional): Borrowers may need to provide proof of income for their loan application. They can enter the relevant details here.

### **Employment Info**
*  *Optional* (`employment_info`, `text`, optional): Borrowers need to provide details about their employment status for their loan application. They can input relevant information here.

### **Income History**
*  *Optional* (`income_history`, `text`, optional): This field is for borrowers to provide any additional income history or details that may be relevant to their loan application.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If unsure about any field, contact the financial advisor for guidance.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.
- Please note that this form is intended for financial advisors only and should not be used for any other purpose.
