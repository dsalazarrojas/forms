# Credit Card Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The credit card application form is used to collect personal and employment information from applicants to determine their eligibility for a credit card account. This form is designed to gather necessary details to assess the applicant's creditworthiness and provide the best possible credit terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your name in the "name" field.
	* Enter your surname in the "surname" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
	* Enter your address in the "address" field.
2. Provide employment details:
	* Enter your birth date in the "birth-date" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	* Select your employment status in the "employment-status" field.
	* Enter the length of time you have been employed in the "employment-length" field.
	* Enter your income in the "income" field.
	* Select whether you are affiliated with any financial institutions in the "financial-institutions" field.
	* Select whether you work for an institution in the "employment-institution" field.
	* If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, enter the name of your institution in the "employment-institution-other" field.
	* Enter the type of institution you work for in the "employment-institution-type" field.
3. Add optional details:
	* If you have an existing account, enter the account number in the "account-number" field.
	* If you have an existing account type, enter the type of account in the "account-type" field.
	* Enter your current credit score in the "credit-score" field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name.
* **surname**: Enter your surname.
* **email**: Enter your email address. We will use this to contact you with the status of your application.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number. We will use this to contact you with the status of your application.
* **address**: Enter your address for delivery of any physical items or documentation.
* **birth-date**: Enter your birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **employment-status**: Select "Active" if you are currently employed or "Inactive" if you are not.
* **employment-length**: Enter the length of time you have been employed.
* **income**: Enter your annual income.
* **financial-institutions**: Select "Yes" if you are affiliated with any financial institutions.
* **employment-institution**: Select "Yes" if you work for an institution.
* **employment-institution-other**: If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, enter the name of your institution.
* **employment-institution-type**: Enter the type of institution you work for.
* **employment-institution-other-type**: Enter the type of institution you work for.
* **account-number**: If you have an existing account, enter the account number.
* **account-type**: If you have an existing account, enter the type of account.
* **credit-score**: Enter your current credit score.
* **employment-status-frequency**: Select "Active" if you are employed frequently or "Inactive" if you are not.
* **employment-institution-employment-type**: Enter the type of employment you have.
