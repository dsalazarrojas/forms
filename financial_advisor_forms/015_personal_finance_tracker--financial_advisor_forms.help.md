# Personal Finance Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track their personal finances, including income, expenses, and savings data. It is meant to be a general guide and not a substitute for professional financial advice.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section carefully and accurately. If a field is marked as "required," it is necessary for the form to be submitted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income** (number): Enter your monthly income.
* **Expenses** (number): Enter your monthly expenses.
* **Savings** (number): Enter your monthly savings.
* **Income Type** (select_one): Select the type of income you have, such as income or expenses.
* **Income Frequency** (select_multiple): Select how frequently you receive income, such as weekly, biweekly, or monthly.
* **Expenses Type** (select_one): Select the type of expense you have, such as fixed or variable.
* **Income Date** (date): Enter the date of your last income payment. (Note: This field is only for tracking purpose and not for payment processing)
* **Expenses Date** (date): Enter the date of your last expense. (Note: This field is only for tracking purpose and not for payment processing)
* **Savings Type** (select_one): Select the type of savings you have, such as savings or investment.
* **Savings Frequency** (select_multiple): Select how frequently you save, such as monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Income Goal** (number): Enter your income goal for the period. (Note: This field is for tracking purpose and not for actual financial planning)
* **Savings Goal** (number): Enter your savings goal for the period. (Note: This field is for tracking purpose and not for actual financial planning)
* **Expenses Goal** (number): Enter your expenses goal for the period. (Note: This field is for tracking purpose and not for actual financial planning)
* **Income Target** (select_one): Select the target for your income, such as high, medium, or low.
* **Expenses Target** (select_one): Select the target for your expenses, such as high, medium, or low.
* **Savings Target** (select_one): Select the target for your savings, such as high, medium, or low.
* **Income Status** (select_one): Select the status of your income, such as on track or off track.
* **Expenses Status** (select_one): Select the status of your expenses, such as on track or off track.
* **Savings Status** (select_one): Select the status of your savings, such as on track or off track.
* **Income Comment** (note): Enter any additional comments about your income.
* **Expenses Comment** (note): Enter any additional comments about your expenses.
* **Savings Comment** (note): Enter any additional comments about your savings.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so that we can follow up on your progress.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so that we can follow up on your progress.
* **Submitted** (select_one): Select the status of your submission, such as submitted, not submitted, or pending review.
