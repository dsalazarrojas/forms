# Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for loan applications. This information is used to assess the applicant's creditworthiness and determine the suitability of the loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Loan Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name and date of birth.
2. Enter the loan amount and loan terms you are requesting.
3. Share your credit score.
4. Indicate your employment status (Active, Employed, Retired, etc.).
5. Provide your income and income verification details.
6. Share your address information.
7. List your employment references and employment history.
8. Share your education information (if applicable).
9. Confirm your employment status (Active, Self-Employed, Disabled, etc.).
10. Enter the loan amount you are requesting.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check the loan amount and loan terms to ensure they match your financial situation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the lender for clarification.
* This form is for the applicant's information only, do not enter any information about anyone else.
