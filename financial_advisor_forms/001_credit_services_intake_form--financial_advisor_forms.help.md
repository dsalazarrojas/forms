# Credit Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Services Intake Form is a comprehensive tool used to collect information from users about their financial needs and requirements. This form will help the processing team assess the user's eligibility for credit services and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of loan you are applying for from the "Loan Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your credit score, if applicable.
3. Choose your current income level from the options provided.
4. Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
5. Enter the length of your employment (if applicable).
6. Select your income source(s) from the options provided.
7. Briefly describe your employment history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of loan you are applying for.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, optional): Enter your current credit score, if applicable.
* **Income Level** (`income_level`, number, optional): Choose your current income level.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current employment status.
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, number, optional): If employed, enter the length of your employment.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, select_multiple, optional): Select your income source(s).
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, optional): Briefly describe your employment history.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields to ensure the processing team can effectively assess your credit services eligibility.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please describe your situation briefly to help the processing team better understand your needs.
* The form will ask for information that is relevant to your credit services eligibility. Please answer accordingly to facilitate a smooth assessment process.
