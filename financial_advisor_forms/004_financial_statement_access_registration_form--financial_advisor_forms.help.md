# Financial Statement Access Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register access to financial statements for a valid purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of financial information you are requesting access to (e.g. Financial Statements, Tax Returns, Other)
2. Provide a brief description of your request
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your contact phone number
5. Enter your date of birth
6. Enter the date of your access request
7. Select the type of financial information you are requesting access to (e.g. Financial Statements, Tax Returns, Other)
8. Enter the access period in months
9. Enter the access expiration date in months
10. Select the financial institution where the account is held (e.g. Bank, Credit Union, Other)
11. Select the type of account (e.g. Checking, Savings, Other)
12. Enter the account number
13. Enter your social security number
14. Enter your address
15. Enter your city
16. Enter your state
17. Enter your zip code

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (1)**: Select the category of financial information you are requesting access to.
* **Description (2)**: Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address that will be used to contact you.
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number that will be used to contact you.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Access Request (6)**: Enter the date of your access request in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Access Request Type (7)**: Select the type of financial information you are requesting access to (e.g. Financial Statements, Tax Returns, Other).
* **Access Period (8)**: Enter the period in months that you want to have access to your financial information.
* **Access Expiration (9)**: Enter the expiration date in months after the access period.
* **Financial Institution (10)**: Select the financial institution where the account is held (e.g. Bank, Credit Union, Other).
* **Account Type (11)**: Select the type of account (e.g. Checking, Savings, Other).
* **Account Number (12)**: Enter the account number associated with the account you are requesting access to.
* **Social Security Number (13)**: Enter your social security number.
* **Address (14)**: Enter your address.
* **City (15)**: Enter your city.
* **State (16)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (17)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Confirm Email (18)**: Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Contact Phone (19)**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Date of Birth (20)**: Confirm your date of birth.
* **Confirm Date of Access Request (21)**: Confirm the date of your access request.
* **Confirm Access Request Type (22)**: Confirm the type of financial information you are requesting access to (e.g. Financial Statements, Tax Returns, Other).
* **Confirm Access Period (23)**: Confirm the access period in months.
* **Confirm Access Expiration (24)**: Confirm the access expiration date in months.
* **Confirm Financial Institution (25)**: Confirm the financial institution where the account is held (e.g. Bank, Credit Union, Other).
* **Confirm Account Type (26)**: Confirm the type of account (e.g. Checking, Savings, Other).
* **Confirm Social Security Number (27)**: Confirm your social security number.
* **Confirm Address (28)**: Confirm your address.
