# Income And Debt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income And Debt Form is designed to collect financial information from users, specifically focusing on their income and debt status. This form aims to gather comprehensive data to help financial advisors understand a user's financial situation and provide personalized advice.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each field truthfully and accurately.
2. Fill in the numerical fields (income and debt amounts) with your actual numbers.
3. For select fields (debt type and financial status), choose the most applicable options from the provided choices.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income from all sources, including salary, investments, and any other income.
* **Monthly Earnings**: Enter your monthly earnings, which can include a portion of your annual income.
* **Total Debt**: Enter the total amount of your outstanding debt.
* **Debt Interest**: Enter the current interest rate on your outstanding debt.
* **Debt Type**: Choose the type of debt you currently have, such as Credit cards, Loans, or Mortgage.
* **Debt Type 2**: Choose another type of debt you have, such as Home, Car loan, or Personal loan.
* **Debt Type 3 (Select Multiple)**: Choose all debt types that apply to you, including Credit cards and Loans.
* **Debt Type 4**: Choose the type of debt that best describes your current situation, such as Home, Car loan, or Personal loan.
* **Debt Type 5 (Select Multiple)**: Choose all debt types that apply to you, including Credit cards, Cash advance, and Other.
* **Debt Type 6**: Choose the type of debt that best describes your current situation, such as Home, Car loan, or Personal loan.
* **Financial Status 1 (Select One)**: Choose the financial status of your business, such as Business is profitable or Business is loss-making.
* **Income 3**: Enter any other annual income from sources besides salary or investments.
* **Income 4**: Enter any other monthly earnings from sources besides salary or investments.
