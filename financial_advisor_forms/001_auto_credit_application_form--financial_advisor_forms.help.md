# Auto Credit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Auto Credit Application Form is used by financial institutions or auto dealerships to gather information about you to evaluate your creditworthiness for a vehicle loan. This form is designed to assess your ability to repay a loan and make an informed decision about your loan application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth loan application process.
2. Provide the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address we will use to send confirmation details.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**): Include area code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your last 4 digits of your social security number for verification.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, **Required**): Enter the address where you currently reside.
* **City** (`city`, text, **Required**): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, **Required**): Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, **Required**): Enter the zip code of your current address.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, **Required**): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, **Optional**): If employed, enter the name of your employer.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, **Required**): Enter your gross income before taxes.
* **Estimated Credit Score Range** (`credit_score_range`, select_one, **Required**): Select your estimated credit score range from the options provided.
* **Do you have existing auto loans?** (`existing_auto_loans`, select_one, **Required**): Select whether you have existing auto loans.
* **What vehicle type interests you?** (`vehicle_interest`, select_one, **Required**): Select the type of vehicle that interests you from the options provided.
* **Approximate Loan Amount Needed** (`loan_amount_needed`, number, **Required**): Enter the approximate loan amount you require.
* **Down Payment Available** (`down_payment_available`, number, **Required**): Enter the down payment you are willing to pay.
* **Preferred Loan Term** (`preferred_loan_term`, select_one, **Required**): Select the preferred loan term in months.
* **I authorize credit inquiry** (`authorization`, select_one, **Required**): Select yes to authorize a credit inquiry to proceed with your loan application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and required information.
* The required fields indicate that you must provide this information for your loan application to be processed efficiently.
