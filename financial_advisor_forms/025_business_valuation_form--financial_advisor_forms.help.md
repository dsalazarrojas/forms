# Business Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Valuation Form is a financial tool designed to gather essential data about your business, enabling you to determine its value. It's used for financial analysis, investment, or sale purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business name as per its legal name.
2.  Provide your full name and contact information for the primary business owner.
3.  Enter your business contact email address and phone number.
4.  Specify the date for which the valuation is required.
5.  Select the purpose of the valuation from the given options.
6.  Choose your business type from the provided options.
7.  Select the industry sector that best describes your business.
8.  Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
9.  Provide the current number of employees.
10.  Enter the last fiscal year revenue.
11.  Enter your estimated revenue for the current year (if applicable).
12.  Enter the last fiscal year net income.
13.  Enter EBITDA (Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization) for the last fiscal year.
14.  Enter the total value of all business assets.
15.  Enter the total liabilities and debts of the business.
16.  Calculate or provide the owner's equity or net worth.
17.  If applicable, enter the percentage of revenue generated from the top customer.
18.  Enter the historical growth rate of the business (if applicable).
19.  Provide a brief description of the current market conditions and outlook for your industry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full and legal name of your business.
*   **Business Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the primary business owner or representative.
*   **Owner Email Address** (`owner_email`, **email**, **required**): Enter the contact email address for the business owner.
*   **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the contact phone number for the business owner.
*   **Valuation Date** (`valuation_date`, date, **required**): Specify the date for which the valuation is being performed.
*   **Purpose of Valuation** (`valuation_purpose`, **select_one**, **required**): Choose the purpose for which you're requesting the valuation.
*   **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, **required**): Indicate the nature or type of your business.
*   **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, **select_one**, **required**): Select the sector or industry that best describes your business.
*   **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`, number, **required**): Enter the number of years the business has been in operation.
*   **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, **required**): Provide the current number of employees in the business.
*   **Last Fiscal Year Revenue** (`last_fiscal_year_revenue`, number, **required**): Enter the revenue generated by the business during its last fiscal year.
*   **Current Year Revenue Projection** (`current_year_revenue_projection`, number, **optional**): If applicable, enter your estimated revenue for the current year.
*   **Last Fiscal Year Net Income** (`net_income`, number, **required**): Enter the profit after expenses for the last fiscal year.
*   **EBITDA (Last Fiscal Year)** (`ebitda`, number, **optional**): Enter the EBITDA for the last fiscal year.
*   **Total Assets** (`total_assets`, number, **required**): Enter the total value of all business assets.
*   **Total Liabilities** (`total_liabilities`, number, **required**): Enter the total debts and obligations of the business.
*   **Owner Equity or Net Worth** (`owner_equity`, number, **required**): Calculate or provide the owner's equity or net worth.
*   **Customer Concentration Risk** (`customer_concentration`, number, **optional**): If applicable, enter the percentage of revenue generated from the top customer.
*   **Historical Growth Rate** (`growth_rate`, number, **optional**): Enter the average annual growth percentage (if applicable).
*   **Current Market Conditions and Outlook** (`market_conditions`, text, **optional**): Provide a brief description of the current market conditions and outlook for your industry.
