# Financial Reporting Tool Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Reporting Tool Request Form is designed for business users to request access to financial reporting tools. This form helps to track and manage requests for these tools, ensuring that they are properly assigned and delivered to the right users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form ID** (Field 1) - This is a unique identifier for the form submission. You can leave this field blank if you don't have a specific ID.
2. Provide your **Business Name** (Field 2) - Enter the name of your business or organization.
3. Enter your **Contact Person** (Field 3) - This is the person who will be the primary contact for the reporting tool request.
4. Enter your **Contact Email** (Field 4) - This is the email address of the contact person.
5. Enter your **Contact Phone** (Field 5) - This is the phone number of the contact person.
6. Add any **Contact Note** (Field 6) - Provide any additional notes or information about your contact.
7. Select the **Requested Tool** (Field 7) - Choose the financial reporting tool you are requesting access to from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose the **Tool Version** (Field 8) - Select the specific version of the requested tool you need.
9. Enter the **Report Start Date** (Field 9) - The date when you want the reporting tool to start generating reports.
10. Enter the **Report End Date** (Field 10) - The date when you want the reporting tool to stop generating reports.
11. Select the **Report Frequency** (Field 11) - Choose how often you want reports to be generated, from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
12. Select the **Report Format** (Field 12) - Choose the format in which you want reports to be delivered, from CSV, Excel, or PDF.
13. Enter the **Report Delivery Email** (Field 13) - The email address where you want reports to be delivered.
14. Add any **Report Delivery Note** (Field 14) - Provide any additional notes or information about report delivery.
15. Enter the **Assigned Tool** (Field 15) - This is automatically populated with the tool that was assigned to you.
16. Add any **Assigned Tool Comment** (Field 16) - Provide any additional comments about the assigned tool.
17. Select the **Request Status** (Field 17) - This field is automatically updated by the system and indicates the current status of your request.
18. Add any **Request Comment** (Field 18) - Provide any additional comments about your request.
19. Add any **Request Notes** (Field 19) - Provide any additional notes or information about your request.
20. Enter your **Created By** (Field 20) - This field is automatically populated with the user who created the request.
21. Enter your **Updated By** (Field 21) - This field is automatically populated with the user who last updated the request.
22. Enter the **Created On** (Field 22) - This field is automatically populated with the date when the request was created.
23. Enter the **Updated On** (Field 23) - This field is automatically populated with the date when the request was last updated.
24. Enter the **Created At** (Field 24) - This field is automatically populated with the timestamp when the request was created.
25. Enter the **Updated At** (Field 25) - This field is automatically populated with the timestamp when the request was last updated.
