# Loan Repayment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The loan repayment form is a tool to help the institution track and manage student loan repayment information. It is essential to have this information to provide accurate financial aid and support to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Student Info" page and enter your student information, including your name and other relevant details.
2. Proceed to the "Loan Details" page and provide information about your outstanding loan balance.
3. On the "Payment Method" page, select the payment method you will use to repay your loan (e.g., Yes, No).
4. Add any additional notes about your loan repayment situation on the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info (Student Info)** (`student_info`, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you will enter your student information, including your name and other relevant details. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Loan Details (Loan Details)** (`loan_details`, text, optional): In this section, please enter any information about your outstanding loan balance. If you are unsure about your loan balance, you can contact your Financial Aid Office for assistance.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Here, you will select the payment method you will use to repay your loan. Please choose either "Yes" or "No" based on your payment plans.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, note, optional): This is the last section of the form where you can add any additional notes or comments about your loan repayment situation. This can be used to communicate any relevant information or requests to your Financial Aid Office.
