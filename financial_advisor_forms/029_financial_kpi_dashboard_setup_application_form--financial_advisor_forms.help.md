# Financial KPI Dashboard Setup Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial KPI Dashboard Setup Application Form is a setup form to allow users to access their financial performance and progress through a dashboard. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User ID
2. Enter your full name
3. Select your role (Financial Advisor or Other)
4. Enter your email address
5. Enter your phone number

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (User ID, number, required): Enter your unique identifier assigned to you by the system.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, required): Select your role as either Financial Advisor or Other.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your official records.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from an authorized personnel.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure correct setup of your dashboard.
