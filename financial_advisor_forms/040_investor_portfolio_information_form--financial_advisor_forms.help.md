# investor_portfolio_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an investor's investment portfolio and contact details. It is designed to provide a clear and concise summary of the investor's investment status and allow for any necessary follow-up actions or notifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing general investment details on the first page.
2. Confirm whether the investor has any specific type of allocation on the second page.
3. Provide more specific investment details on the second page if necessary.
4. Enter the investor's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investment Details**: Enter a brief description or summary of the investor's current investment portfolio.
* **Portfolio Allocation**: Answer "Yes" if the investor has a specific type of allocation in their portfolio, or "No" if they do not.
* **Investment Details (Page 2)**: Enter any additional, more specific information about the investments, if applicable.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the investor's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
