# W9 Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for collecting and storing client and business information for reference or reporting purposes, possibly for tax or financial contexts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the client's name and address in the respective fields.
2. If applicable, enter the EIN (Employer Identification Number) for the client or business.
3. Select the business type from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the business address, phone number, and business EIN if applicable.
5. Enter the client's phone number.
6. Choose the EIN type from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the dates for client sign, signature sign, and business sign if applicable.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the note fields.
9. Upload any supporting files or documents if necessary.
