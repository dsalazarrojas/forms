# Personal Finance Organizer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track their personal finance by organizing their income, expenses, and savings goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your monthly income in the "Monthly income" field.
2. Enter your monthly expenses in the "Monthly expenses" field.
3. Set your monthly savings target in the "Monthly savings" field.
4. Choose your saving priority level:
	* Always
	* Sometimes
	* Rarely
5. Enter your income amount in the "Income amount" field.
6. Enter your expenses amount in the "Expenses amount" field.
7. Set your savings goal in the "Savings goal" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
9. Choose the importance level of your savings priority:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
10. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Monthly income (`income_earnings`, number, required: false)
Enter your total monthly income.

### Monthly expenses (`expenses_spendings`, number, required: false)
Enter your total monthly expenses.

### Monthly savings (`savings_targets`, number, required: false)
Enter your monthly savings target.

### Saving priority (`save_priority`, select_one, required: false)
Choose how important saving is to you:

* Always
* Sometimes
* Rarely

### Income amount (`income`, number, required: false)
Enter your current income amount.

### Expenses amount (`expenses_amount`, number, required: false)
Enter your current expenses amount.

### Savings goal (`savings_goal`, number, required: false)
Set a specific savings goal for yourself.

### Phone number (`save_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number.

### Savings priority importance (`savings_priority_important`, select_one, required: false)
Choose how important saving is to you:

* High
* Medium
* Low

### Email address (`email_address`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address.
