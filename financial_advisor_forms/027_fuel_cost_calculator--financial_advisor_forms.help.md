# Fuel Cost Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to calculate the fuel cost of a vehicle given various input parameters, including distance traveled, fuel efficiency, vehicle weight, consumption rate, and other factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the distance traveled by the vehicle in the "Distance Traveled" field.
2. Enter the fuel efficiency of the vehicle in the "Fuel Efficiency" field.
3. Enter the weight of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Weight" field.
4. Select the drive frequency of the vehicle from the "Drive Frequency" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the fuel consumption rate of the vehicle in the "Fuel Consumption Rate" field.
6. Select the unit of measurement for fuel consumption from the "Fuel Consumption Unit" dropdown menu.
7. Select the type of fuel consumed by the vehicle from the "Fuel Consumption Type" dropdown menu.
8. Select the type of consumption (directly injected or indirectly injected) from the "Consumption Type" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the estimated cost of fuel (calculated based on the input data) in the "Estimated Cost" field.
10. Enter the price per unit of fuel in the "Fuel Price per Unit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distance Traveled**: Enter the distance traveled by the vehicle. This field is required to calculate the fuel cost.
* **Fuel Efficiency**: Enter the fuel efficiency of the vehicle. This field is required to calculate the fuel cost.
* **Vehicle Weight**: Enter the weight of the vehicle. This field is required to calculate the fuel cost.
* **Drive Frequency**: Select the drive frequency of the vehicle. This field is optional and will affect the fuel cost calculation.
* **Fuel Consumption Rate**: Enter the fuel consumption rate of the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Fuel Consumption Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for fuel consumption. This field is optional.
* **Fuel Consumption Type**: Select the type of fuel consumed by the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Consumption Type**: Select the type of consumption (directly injected or indirectly injected). This field is required.
* **Estimated Cost**: This field is automatically calculated based on the input data and is required.
* **Fuel Cost**: This field is automatically calculated based on the input data and is required.
* **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Fuel Consumption**: Select the level of fuel consumption. This field is required.
* **Fuel Price per Unit**: Enter the price per unit of fuel. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate data for each field to ensure accurate fuel cost calculation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your financial advisor for guidance.
* The calculated fuel cost is based on the input data and is subject to change based on various factors.
