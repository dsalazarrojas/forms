# Financial Forecasting Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial forecasting audit form is a tool used to collect information about a company's financial situation and future projections. This form is designed to help you make informed decisions about the company's financial future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Company Name" field with the name of the company you are providing information for.
2. Select the correct option from the "Financial Forecasting Form 4" field, which will help us understand your financial projections.
3. Enter the date you are providing the information for in the "Date" field.
4. If necessary, enter the year you are referring to in the "Year" field.
5. Select multiple options from the "Financial Forecasting Form 7" field, which will help us understand your financial projections.
6. Enter a numerical value in the "Financial Forecasting Form 8" field, which will help us understand your financial situation.
7. If necessary, enter a time in the "Financial Forecasting Form 9" field, which will help us understand your financial projections.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Financial Forecasting Form 10" field, which will help us understand your financial situation.
9. Finally, enter a valid email address in the "Financial Forecasting Form 11" field, which will help us contact you if needed.
10. Fill out any additional information you feel is relevant to "Financial Forecasting Form 12" and "Financial Forecasting Form 13" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter the name of the company you are providing information for.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required): This field is where you provide information about the company you are providing data for. Please select the correct company from the options provided.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 4** (`Financial Forecasting Form 4`, `select_one`, required): This field is where you select one of the options provided to help us understand your financial projections.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): This field is where you enter the date you are providing the information for.
* **Year** (`Year`, `number`, required): This field is where you enter the year you are referring to, if necessary.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 7** (`Financial Forecasting Form 7`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is where you select multiple options to help us understand your financial projections.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 8** (`Financial Forecasting Form 8`, `number`, required): This field is where you enter a numerical value to help us understand your financial situation.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 9** (`Financial Forecasting Form 9`, `time`, optional): This field is where you enter a time, if necessary.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 10** (`Financial Forecasting Form 10`, `note`, optional): This field is where you enter any additional comments or notes about your financial situation.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 11** (`Financial Forecasting Form 11`, `email`, optional): This field is where you enter a valid email address, which will help us contact you if needed.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 12** (`Financial Forecasting Form 12`, `text`, optional): This field is where you enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your financial situation.
* **Financial Forecasting Form 13** (`Financial Forecasting Form 13`, `text`, optional): This field is where you enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your financial situation.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely, as any missing or incorrect information may lead to incorrect financial projections. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
