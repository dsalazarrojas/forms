# Asset And Liability Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset And Liability Information Form is used to provide a comprehensive overview of a business's financial situation, including its assets, liabilities, and owner equity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name as it is registered with the relevant authorities.
2. Select the reporting year for which you are completing this form.
3. Enter the date of the statement.
4. Provide the owner's first name and last name.
5. Enter the value of each asset category, including cash and equivalents, accounts receivable, inventory, fixed assets (such as property and buildings), equipment and machinery, and other assets.
6. Enter the value of each liability category, including accounts payable, current portion of loans, taxes payable, and other current liabilities.
7. Enter the value of long-term debt.
8. Enter the value of owner's equity.
9. Describe any major changes or significant financial events that occurred during the reporting year.
10. Enter the name of the accountant or preparer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business as it is registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Reporting Year**: Select the year for which you are completing this form.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the statement.
* **Owner First Name**: Enter the first name of the business owner.
* **Owner Last Name**: Enter the last name of the business owner.
* **Cash and Cash Equivalents**: Enter the value of the business's cash and cash equivalents.
* **Accounts Receivable**: Enter the value of money owed to the business by customers.
* **Inventory Value**: Enter the value of goods held in inventory.
* **Current Assets**: Enter the value of other liquid assets.
* **Real Property and Buildings**: Enter the value of owned property.
* **Equipment and Machinery**: Enter the value of equipment and machinery owned by the business.
* **Accumulated Depreciation**: Enter the total depreciation on fixed assets.
* **Intangible Assets**: Enter the value of goodwill, patents, and trademarks.
* **Other Assets**: Enter the value of any other asset categories.
* **Total Assets**: Enter the sum of all assets.
* **Accounts Payable**: Enter the value of money owed to suppliers.
* **Current Portion of Loans**: Enter the value of short-term loan obligations.
* **Taxes Payable**: Enter the value of accrued tax obligations.
* **Other Current Liabilities**: Enter the value of other short-term obligations.
* **Long-Term Debt**: Enter the value of notes and mortgages payable.
* **Total Liabilities**: Enter the sum of all debts and obligations.
* **Owner Equity**: Enter the value of assets minus liabilities.
* **Major Changes**: Describe significant asset or liability changes that occurred during the reporting year.
* **Significant Financial Events**: Describe any major purchases, sales, or events that occurred during the reporting year.
* **Accountant or Preparer Name**: Enter the name of the person preparing this statement.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information on each field.
* Use the same reporting year and date for all entries.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant financial advisor or authority.
