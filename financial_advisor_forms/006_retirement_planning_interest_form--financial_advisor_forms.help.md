# Retirement Planning Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The retirement planning interest form is a questionnaire designed to gather relevant information from individuals seeking financial guidance on their retirement goals and requirements. This form is meant to assist a financial advisor in understanding your needs and provide informed counsel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Contact Information** on page 1.
2. In the **Retirement Goals** field, enter a brief description of your retirement goals (e.g., travel, financial independence, etc.).
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted by checking **Best Contact Method**.
4. In the **Financial Goals** field, provide specific financial goals, such as pension, inheritance, or other sources of income.
5. If you have any **Additional Comments**, use the space provided to explain your situation in detail.
6. Enter your **Phone Number**, **Email**, and choose a **Best Time to Contact**.
7. Indicate your interest in various retirement areas by checking the corresponding boxes under **Retirement Interest Areas**.
8. Specify your interests in **Pensions**, **Real Estate**, **Social Security**, **Insurance**, **Healthcare**, **Employment**, **Education**, and **Legal**.
9. In the **Retirement Goals** field on the last page, provide an updated description of your retirement goals.
10. Finally, provide your **Retirement Date**, the date you are planning to retire.
11. Enter the name and contact information of your preferred **Financial Advisor**.
12. Fill out the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
- **Retirement Goals**: Describe your retirement goals, such as travel or financial independence.
- **Best Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of communication (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
- **Retirement Date**: Enter the date you are planning to retire.
- **Financial Goals**: Describe your financial goals, such as pension or inheritance.
- **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional details about your financial situation.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Best Time to Contact**: Choose a time of day when you are available to be contacted.
- **Retirement Interest Areas**: Indicate your interest in various retirement areas (e.g., pensions, real estate, etc.).
- **Pensions**, **Real Estate**, **Social Security**, **Insurance**, **Healthcare**, **Employment**, **Education**, and **Legal**: Specify your interests in these areas.
- **Retirement Goals**: Provide an updated description of your retirement goals.
- **Financial Advisor**: Enter the name and contact information of your preferred financial advisor.
- **Submission Date** and **Submission Time**: Fill out the submission date and time for reference.

Please note that all fields marked as required are mandatory. Ensure you complete all such fields to provide accurate and detailed information for your financial advisor's guidance.
