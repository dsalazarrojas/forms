Client Perception Of Advisory Value Survey - Help Guide
==============================

### Purpose

The Client Perception Of Advisory Value Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our advisors. This survey helps us understand how well our advisors are performing, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights to enhance our services.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by providing information about your experience with our advisor.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or comments.
* **Advisory Services --** (3): This section is for any general comments or feedback about the advisory services you've received.
* **How valuable are the advisory services you receive?** (4): Rate the value of the services you receive from 1 (Not Valuable) to 5 (Extremely Valuable).
* **My advisor demonstrates strong expertise** (5): Indicate your level of agreement with this statement.
* **How likely are you to recommend this advisor to others?** (6): Rate your likelihood of recommending the advisor on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Communication quality from your advisor** (7): Rate the quality of communication from your advisor.
* **-- Areas for Improvement --** (8): This section is for any suggestions or feedback about areas where our advisor could improve.
* **What could be improved in the advisory services?** (9): Provide any suggestions or feedback about how we could improve our services.
* **Which additional services would you find valuable?** (10): Select all additional services that you believe would be valuable.
* **How quickly does your advisor respond to inquiries?** (11): Rate the speed of response from your advisor.
* **How often do you engage with your advisor?** (12): Rate your frequency of engagement with your advisor.
* **Overall satisfaction with advisory services** (13): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services you receive.
* **How long have you been a client with this advisor?** (14): Rate the length of your relationship with the advisor.
* **Preferred contact method** (15): Select your preferred method of contact with the advisor.
* **Additional comments** (16): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the advisor.

Please note that all fields are required unless marked as optional, and providing feedback is encouraged but not required. Thank you for taking the time to share your experience with us!
