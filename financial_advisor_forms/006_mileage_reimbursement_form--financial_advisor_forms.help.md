# Mileage Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Reimbursement Form is used to capture information for mileage reimbursement requests. It's a crucial document that helps facilitate the reimbursement process for employees who have traveled for work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand what is required and what is optional.
* Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and speedy processing.
* Enter the reimbursed amount in the correct currency (USD, CAD, EUR, or JPY).
* Select the correct travel date and time for your trip.
* Specify the travel location and distance.
* Choose the correct reimbursement requestor (Financial Advisor, Manager, HR Representative, or Travel Manager).
* Indicate the approval status (approved, pending, or rejected).
* Add any additional notes or comments as necessary.
* Provide your contact email and phone number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mileage Reimbursement Form** (Page 1, required): This is the main section where you will enter the mileage reimbursement details.
* **Reimbursed Amount** (Page 1, required): Enter the exact amount of mileage reimbursement you are requesting. Please ensure it's accurate and follows the correct currency.
* **Currency** (Page 1, optional): Select the correct currency for your reimbursement request (USD, CAD, EUR, or JPY).
* **Date of Travel** (Page 1, required): Enter the start and end dates of your trip. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Travel** (Page 1, optional): If necessary, specify the travel time.
* **Travel Location** (Page 1, optional): Enter the location where you traveled.
* **Mileage Distance** (Page 1, optional): Enter the distance you traveled.
* **Reimbursement Requestor** (Page 1, optional): Choose the person or department responsible for your reimbursement request (Financial Advisor, Manager, HR Representative, or Travel Manager).
* **Approval Status** (Page 1, optional): Indicate the status of your reimbursement request (approved, pending, or rejected).
* **Additional Notes** (Page 1, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to your reimbursement request.
* **Email** (Page 1, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 1, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
