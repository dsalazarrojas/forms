## Travel Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to document and submit travel expense claims for reimbursement, ensuring accuracy, completeness, and compliance with company travel policies.

### How To Complete This Form
To ensure that you receive accurate reimbursement for your business travel expenses, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form with accurate information for the date of travel, purpose of trip, destination, and other related details.
2. Estimate or actualize the cost of each expense type: flight cost, transportation expenses, lodging expenses, meals and entertainment expenses, and other expenses.
3. Ensure that the total expenses calculated correctly adds up to the sum of all individual expenses.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`travel_expense_reimbursement_form`): A brief description of the travel expense reimbursement request.
- **Date of Travel** (`date_of_travel`): The date of the travel for which expenses are being claimed.
- **Destination** (`destination`): The location or city visited during the trip.
- **Purpose of Trip** (`purpose_of_trip`): A brief explanation of the business purpose of the trip.
- **Prophets and Suites** (`prophets_and_suites`): (Note: This field appears to be a typographical error. This field may actually be intended to capture "provisions and suites" or "provisions and services", please enter as such.) A brief description of any expenses related to provisions or services, such as meal provisions or transportation services.
- **Flight Cost** (`flight_cost`): The actual cost of the flight(s) taken for the trip.
- **Transportation Expenses** (`transportation_expenses`): Any expenses related to transportation, such as taxi, train, or rental car fees.
- **Lodging Expenses** (`lodging_expenses`): The actual cost of lodging during the trip, including hotel stays or other accommodations.
- **Meals and Entertainment Expenses** (`meals_and_entertainment_expenses`): Any expenses related to meals, entertainment, or other recreational activities.
- **Other Expenses** (`other_expenses`): A brief description of any other expenses not covered by other fields, such as equipment rentals or miscellaneous fees.
- **Total Expenses** (`total_expenses`): The sum of all individual expenses entered in the form, automatically calculated for your convenience.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy and completeness of all entries before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all expenses are properly categorized and estimated or actualized as required.
- If you are unsure about any field or have additional questions, please contact your supervisor or accounting department for clarification.
