# Care Credit Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect all necessary information to process and track payments for medical services rendered under the Care Credit program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Receipt Number**: A unique ID number for the payment receipt. 
    *   Auto-generated or manually entered by the provider.
    *   Required field.
* **Receipt Date**: The date when the payment receipt was issued. 
    *   The date of service or payment processing.
    *   Required field.
* **Payment Time**: The time of payment.
    *   The time when the payment was made.
    *   Required field.
* **Provider Name**: The name of the healthcare provider or business.
    *   Required field.
* **Provider Address**: The full business address of the provider.
    *   Required field.
* **Provider Phone Number**: The phone number of the provider.
    *   Required field.
* **Provider Email**: The email of the provider. 
    *   Optional field, but recommended for easy contact.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the cardholder.
    *   Required field.
* **Patient Address**: The patient's full address.
    *   Required field.
* **Patient Phone Number**: The phone number of the patient. 
    *   Optional field.
*   Only required if on payment plan.
* **Patient Email**: The patient's email. 
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only required for electronic receipt.
* **CareCredit Account Last Four Digits**: The last four digits of the Care Credit account number.
    *   Required field.
* **Transaction ID**: A confirmation number for the transaction.
    *   Required field.
* **Date of Service**: The date of the services rendered.
    *   Required field.
* **Description of Services**: A brief description of the services rendered.
    *   Required field.
* **Service Category**: The category of the services rendered. 
    *   Select one from the options provided.
    *   Required field.
* **Subtotal Amount**: The total amount before any discounts or adjustments.
    *   Required field.
* **Discount Amount**: The amount of discount applied.
    *   Optional field.
*   Only required if applicable.
* **Tax Amount**: The amount of tax applied.
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only required if applicable.
* **Total Amount Paid**: The final amount paid by the patient.
    *   Required field.
* **Payment Plan Selected**: The chosen payment plan.
    *   Select one from the options provided.
    *   Required field.
*   If on payment plan.
* **Payment Status**: The status of the payment.
    *   Select one from the options provided.
    *   Required field.
* **Amount Financed**: The total amount financed (if applicable).
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only required if on a payment plan.
*   Only if applicable.
* **Down Payment**: The down payment (if applicable).
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only required if on a payment plan.
*   Only if applicable.
* **Estimated Monthly Payment**: The estimated monthly payment (if on a payment plan).
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only required if on a payment plan.
*   Only if applicable.
* **Promotional Period End Date**: The end date of the promotional period (if applicable).
    *   Optional field.
    *   Only if applicable.
*   Only if applicable.
* **Patient Acknowledges Payment Terms**: Whether the patient acknowledges the payment terms.
    *   Select one from the options provided.
    *   Required field.
*   True or False.
* **Receipt Delivery Method**: The method of receipt delivery.
    *   Select one from the options provided.
    *   Required field.
*   Options: Email, Print, Both, Patient Portal.
* **Processed By**: The staff member who processed the payment.
    *   Required field.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or notes from the provider.
    *   Optional field.

## Tips

*   Please ensure accurate and complete information to avoid errors in payment processing.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   Contact the provider if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
