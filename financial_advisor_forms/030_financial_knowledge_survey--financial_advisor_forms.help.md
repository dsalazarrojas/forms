# financial_knowledge_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_knowledge_survey form is designed to collect information about your financial situation, goals, and habits. This form will help us understand your financial knowledge and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
4. For open-ended questions, type in your answer in the text box.
5. You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
6. Once you have completed all the questions, click on "Submit" to finish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current income? (current_income)**: This question asks about your current income or annual salary.
* **How do you manage your daily expenses? (expense_management)**: This question is about your methods for tracking and paying your daily expenses.
* **What are your short-term financial goals? (short_term_goals)**: This question is asking about goals you have for the next 1-3 years, such as saving for a down payment on a house or paying off debt.
* **How do you stay informed about financial markets? (financial_markets)**: This question is about your sources of information about financial markets, such as news, books, or online resources.
* **What are your long-term financial goals? (long_term_goals)**: This question is asking about goals you have for 5-10 years or more, such as retirement savings or paying off a mortgage.
* **Who do you consider as your most trusted source for financial advice? (financial_advice)**: This question is about the person or organization you rely on for financial guidance.
* **How much money do you have saved in emergency funds? (emergency_funds)**: This question is about the amount of money you have set aside for unexpected expenses or emergencies.
* **How do you stay up-to-date with tax laws and regulations? (tax_laws)**: This question is about your methods for staying informed about tax laws and regulations.
* **Do you have a budget for your daily expenses? (budgeting)**: This question is about whether you have a plan for tracking and paying your daily expenses.
* **How do you make financial decisions when faced with uncertainty? (financial_decision_making)**: This question is about your thought process when faced with uncertain financial situations.
* **What is your financial knowledge level regarding investments? (investment_knowledge)**: This question is about how knowledgeable you are about investing in stocks, bonds, or other financial instruments.
* **Any additional comments? (additional_comments)**: This question is an open-ended question for you to provide any further information or comments.

## Tips
* Please answer all the required questions.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
* Be honest and accurate with your answers.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
