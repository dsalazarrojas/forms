# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparer Agreement Form is a document used by financial advisers to collect agreement from clients on tax return preparation and authorization. It gathers essential information about the client, including tax return authorization type and signing details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of tax return authorization applicable to you.
2. Enter your name and the date of tax return authorization.
3. Enter the name and date of the person signing the tax return.
4. Provide your signature to confirm your agreement.
5. Enter your client's signature to confirm their agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for the form, if you wish.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, note, required: false): This field is for additional information about the agreement. You can enter any details about the tax return preparation and authorization.
* **Tax Return Authorization** (`tax_return_authorization`, select_one, required: true): Select the type of tax return authorization that applies to you:
	+ Individual
	+ Joint
	+ Joint-Partner
* **Tax Return Signing** (`tax_return_signing`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person signing the tax return.
* **Tax Return Signing Date** (`tax_return_signing_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the tax return signing.
* **Financial Adviser's Name** (`financial_adviser_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the financial adviser.
* **Financial Adviser's Signature** (`financial_adviser_signature`, text, required: false): Provide your signature as the financial adviser.
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about your client.
* **Client's Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required: false): Provide your client's signature.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand the tax return authorization type and terms before providing your signature.
* Review the agreement details and client information carefully before submitting the form.
* Keep a record of the tax return authorization and signing for future reference.
