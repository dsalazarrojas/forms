# Investment Fund Switch Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Fund Switch Request Form is used by financial advisors to request switching investments for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Enter any additional comments or details about the switch request.
4. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Switch Request**: This field is where you can enter a brief description of the reason for the investment switch.
* **Current Investment**: Select 'Yes' if the client currently holds this investment, and 'No' if they do not.
* **Reason for Switch**: Enter a detailed explanation for the investment switch. If you are unsure, you can mention any specific concerns or questions you have about the switch.
* **New Investment**: Select 'Yes' if this is the new investment being switched to, and 'No' if it is not.
* **Investment Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to switch. Please ensure you enter the exact amount to avoid delays in processing.
* **Transfer Date**: Select the date you would like the transfer to take place.
* **Transfer Time**: Enter the time of the transfer. Please note that the transfer time is for the purpose of scheduling and not the time of day the transfer occurs.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about the client or switch request.
* **Email**: Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you about the switch request.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you about the switch request.
* **Advisor**: Enter your name so we can keep a record of who submitted the request.
* **Advisor Email**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you about the switch request.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure you have the client's consent for the investment switch.
* If you're unsure about anything, please reach out to the relevant team for guidance.
