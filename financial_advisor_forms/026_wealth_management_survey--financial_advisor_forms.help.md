# Wealth Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Management Survey is a form designed to help users assess their financial management and investment preferences. It is meant to be filled out once, to provide a comprehensive picture of the user's current situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each section with the best answer that reflects your current situation.
2. Select all that apply to each option in each section.
3. If an option does not apply, simply leave it blank or select "None of the above".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Welcome to the Survey
* **Welcome to the Survey** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before starting to answer the questions.

### Page 2: Investment Experience
* **How would you rate your current investment experience** (`investment_experience`, `number`, not required): This question helps us understand your level of investment experience. Please select the number that best represents your experience.

### Page 3: Risk Tolerance
* **What is your risk tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ **Very aggressive**: You are willing to take on high levels of risk in pursuit of higher returns.
	+ **Moderately aggressive**: You are willing to take on some level of risk, but also want to balance it with more conservative investments.
	+ **Conservative**: You prefer to take on minimal risk and prioritize preservation of your capital.
	+ **Very conservative**: You are extremely risk-averse and prefer to prioritize preservation of your capital above all else.

### Page 4: Investment Objectives
* **What are your top investment objectives** (`investment_objectives`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ **Grow wealth over time**: You want to increase your wealth over the long-term.
	+ **Income generation**: You want to generate income from your investments.
	+ **Income protection**: You want to protect your income and ensure it continues in the future.
	+ **Wealth preservation**: You want to preserve your existing wealth.

### Page 5: Financial Goals
* **What are your financial goals** (`financial_goals`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ **Retirement planning**: You are planning for retirement and want to make sure you have enough money to support yourself in your golden years.
	+ **Education funding**: You are saving for education expenses, such as for your children.
	+ **Major purchase**: You want to buy a big-ticket item, such as a house or a car.
	+ **Other**: You have other financial goals that do not fit in the above categories.

### Page 6: Risk Freezing Point
* **What is your risk freezing point** (`risk_freezing_point`, `number`, not required): This question is not well-defined, but is assumed to be a measure of risk tolerance.

### Page 7: Risk Freezing Point Score
* **What is your risk freezing point score** (`risk_freezing_point_score`, `number`, not required): This question is similar to the previous one, and is likely a more quantitative measure of risk tolerance.

### Page 8: Investment Allocation
* **What is your preferred investment allocation** (`investment_allocation`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ **Stocks**: You prefer to invest in stocks, which can be more volatile.
	+ **Bonds**: You prefer to invest in bonds, which are generally less volatile.
	+ **Cash**: You prefer to keep your money in cash, which is liquid and safe.
	+ **Alternative investments**: You are open to investing in alternative assets, such as real estate or collectibles.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure of an answer, select "None of the above" or leave it blank.
- You can ask for help if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions.
