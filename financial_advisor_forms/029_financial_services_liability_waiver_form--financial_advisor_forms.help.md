# financial_services_liability_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures key information related to liability waiver agreements for financial services provided to clients. The client must acknowledge and understand the terms before commencing any financial planning or advisory engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and details about your engagement with the financial service provider.
2. Indicate your understanding and acknowledgment of the liability waiver terms.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Services Liability Waiver Form** (`financial_services_liability_waiver_form`): Enter your name and a brief description of your engagement with the financial service provider.
* **Advisory Engagement** (`advisory_engagement`): Describe the financial advisory services you are engaging in.
* **Tax Engagement** (`tax_engagement`): Describe the tax services you are engaging in.
* **Planning Engagement** (`planning_engagement`): Describe the financial planning services you are engaging in.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Signature 1** (`signature_signed`): Select one of the available signatures to acknowledge the waiver terms.
* **Acknowledgment 1** (`acknowledgment_signed`): Select one or more of the available acknowledgments to confirm your understanding of the liability waiver terms.
* **Other Information** (`other_information`): Enter any additional information about your engagement with the financial service provider.
* **Other Information 2** (`other_information2`): Enter any additional information about your engagement with the financial service provider.
* **Other Information 3** (`other_information3`): Enter any additional information about your engagement with the financial service provider.
