# Personal Finance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Finance Survey is designed to help individuals assess their financial situation, set financial goals, and make informed decisions about their money management. This form will guide you through a series of questions to gather essential information about your income, expenses, investments, and financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by answering each question carefully and honestly.
2. Please select the correct options for each field from the available choices.
3. If you're unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Income** (`household_income_1`, number, optional): Enter your household's monthly income.
* **Household Expenses** (`household_expenses_2`, number, optional): Enter your household's monthly expenses.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals_3`, text, optional): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals.
* **Current Savings** (`current_savings_4`, number, optional): Enter your current savings amount.
* **Income Source** (`income_source_5`, select_one, optional): Choose your primary income source (Bank, Investment, or Other).
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency_6`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you receive income (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Debt** (`debt_7`, number, optional): Enter your current debt amount.
* **Income Range** (`income_range_8`, select_one, required): Choose your household income range (Less than 10k, $10k-$20k, $20k-$50k, More than $50k).
* **Expenses Range** (`expenses_range_9`, select_one, required): Choose your household expenses range (Less than $1k, $1k-$5k, $5k-$10k, More than $10k).
* **Investment** (`investment_10`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of investments you have (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Other).
* **Savings** (`savings_11`, number, optional): Enter your current savings amount.
* **Savings Frequency** (`savings_frequency_12`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you save (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Income Frequency 13** (`income_frequency_13`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you receive income (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions_14`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of financial institutions you use (Bank, Credit Union, Investment Firm, Other).
* **Income Institution** (`income_institution_15`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of financial institution where you receive income (Bank, Credit Union, Investment Firm, Other).
* **Financial Goals 16** (`financial_goals_16`, text, optional): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals_17`, text, optional): Describe your investment goals.
* **Savings Goals** (`savings_goals_18`, text, optional): Describe your savings goals.
* **Financial Institutions 19** (`financial_institutions_19`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of financial institutions you use (Bank, Credit Union, Investment Firm, Other).
* **Investment Range** (`investment_range_20`, select_one, required): Choose your investment range (Less than 10k, $10k-$20k, $20k-$50k, More than $50k).
* **Assets** (`assets_21`, text, optional): Describe your assets.
* **Liabilities** (`liabilities_22`, text, optional): Describe your liabilities.
* **Financial Advice** (`financial_advice_23`, text, optional): Describe any financial advice you would like to receive.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance_24`, text, optional): Describe your risk tolerance.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history_25`, text, optional): Describe your financial history.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately to ensure a complete assessment.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanation for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
