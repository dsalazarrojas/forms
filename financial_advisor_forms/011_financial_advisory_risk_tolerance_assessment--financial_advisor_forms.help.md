# Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment form is designed to help investors or financial advisors understand your risk tolerance and investment goals. This information will aid in tailoring investment advice or product recommendations that suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the options that best describe your investment goals and preferences.
3. If you are unsure about any option, you can always ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following investment goals:
  + Risk-averse: I want to preserve my capital and avoid losses.
  + Growth: I want to grow my capital over time.
  + Income: I want to generate regular income from my investments.

* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following risk levels:
  + Moderate: I can tolerate some level of risk, but also want some stability.
  + Aggressive: I am willing to take on higher risk for potential higher returns.
  + Very Conservative: I want to minimize risk and preserve capital.

* **Investment Time Frame** (`investment_time_frame`, `date`, required: false): If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank. This field is not essential for this assessment, but it can be helpful for future reference.

* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current income.

* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, `time`, required: false): If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank. This field is not essential for this assessment, but it can be helpful for future reference.

* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of financial institution you are associated with:
  + Bank
  + Credit Union
  + Broker
  + Other

* **Risk Free** (`risk_free`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following risk-free options:
  + False
  + True

* **Investment Objects** (`investment_objects`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following investment objects:
  + Stocks
  + Bonds
  + Real Estate
  + Commodities
  + Other

* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your risk level:
  + High
  + Medium
  + Low

* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your investment frequency:
  + High
  + Medium
  + Low

* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following asset allocation options:
  + Stocks
  + Bonds
  + Real Estate
  + Commodities
  + Other

* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, `number`, required: false): Enter your expected investment return.

* **Investment Period** (`investment_period`, `time`, required: false): If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank. This field is not essential for this assessment, but it can be helpful for future reference.

* **Financial Objectives** (`financial_objectives`, `text`, required: false): Describe your financial objectives.

* **Tax Status** (`tax_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your tax status:
  + Single
  + Joint
  + Divorced
  + Other

* **Tax Filing Status** (`tax_filing_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your tax filing status:
  + Married
  + Single
  + Separated
  + Other

* **Investment Account** (`investment_account`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of investment account you hold:
  + Single
  + Joint
  + Beneficiary

* **Financial Net Worth** (`financial_net_worth`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current financial net worth.

* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your annual income.

* **Annual Income Frequency** (`annual_income_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your annual income frequency:
  + Monthly
  + Quarterly
  + Semiannually
  + Annually
