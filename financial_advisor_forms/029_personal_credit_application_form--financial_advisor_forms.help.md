# Personal Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Credit Application Form is used for personal financial purposes, such as applying for loans or credit applications. It collects personal information to assess your financial history and eligibility for credit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide your employment status, type, and length of employment.
3. Enter your income, credit score, and loan purpose.
4. Select your financial history, education level, income frequency, and employment type.
5. Enter your bank name and loan term (if applicable).
6. Review your form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your current address for verification purposes.
* **Employment Status** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your current employment status: employed, unemployed, etc.
* **Employment Type** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your employment type: part-time, full-time, freelance, self-employed, etc.
* **Income** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income for assessment purposes.
* **Credit Score** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select your credit score: 700, 780, 850, 920, etc.
* **Loan Amount** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the loan amount you are applying for (if applicable).
* **Loan Term** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the loan term: 6-month, 12-month, 24-month, 36-month, etc.
* **Credit History** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your credit history: excellent, good, fair, poor, etc.
* **Employment Length** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your employment length: 0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, etc.
* **Education Level** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your education level: high school, college, some college, bachelor, etc.
* **Income Frequency** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your income frequency: monthly, annual, quarterly, bi-annually, etc.
* **Bank** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter your bank name for verification purposes.
* **Loan Purpose** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the loan purpose (e.g., personal loan, mortgage, etc.).
* **Social Security Number** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter your social security number (last 4 digits only).

**Tips**

* Double-check your form for accuracy before submission.
* Ensure you enter valid and accurate information to avoid delays or rejections.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the financial institution for assistance.
