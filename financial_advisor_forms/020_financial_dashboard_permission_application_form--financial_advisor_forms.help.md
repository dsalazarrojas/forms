# Financial Dashboard Permission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Dashboard Permission Application Form is used to apply for access to the financial dashboard. This form collects the required information to evaluate your request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This is also a required field and should be a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select either **Yes** or **No** in the **Dashboard Access** field, depending on whether you want to be granted access to the financial dashboard.
5. If you have any additional information that you would like to provide, you can enter it in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address that you use for official purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX.XXX.XXXX`.
* **Dashboard Access**: This field is used to indicate whether you want to be granted access to the financial dashboard. Select **Yes** if you want to have access, and **No** if you do not.
* **Additional Information**: This field is optional and is used to provide any additional context or information that you would like to share with the administrators. Please keep it brief and to the point.
