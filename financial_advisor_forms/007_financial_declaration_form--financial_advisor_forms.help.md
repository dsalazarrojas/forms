# Financial Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Declaration Form is designed to collect detailed financial information from individuals or companies for assessment and review purposes. This form is used to understand an individual's or company's financial situation, including income, financial assets, and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window to preserve the original layout.
2. Enter your financial information in the corresponding fields.
3. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Form** (`financial_declaration_form`, text, optional): This is a brief description of the form. You can provide a short introduction to your financial situation.
* **Income Details** (`income_form_data`, text, optional): Enter your income details, including salary, wages, or any other regular income sources.
* **Financial Assets** (`financial_assets_form`, text, optional): List your financial assets, such as cash, savings, investments, or properties.
* **Financial Liabilities** (`financial_liabilities_form`, text, optional): List your financial liabilities, including debts, loans, or other financial obligations.
* **Financial Declaration** (`financial_declaration`, text, optional): This is the main financial declaration where you can describe your current financial situation in detail. Please be as accurate and comprehensive as possible.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
