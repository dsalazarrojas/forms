# Money Order Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a refund for a money order. Fill out this form to provide the necessary details about your money order and the reason for your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Money Order Details page with the details about the money order you want to refund.
	* Ensure you fill out all the required fields with accurate information.
2. On the next page, select the order type from the provided options.
3. Enter the Date of Purchase and Date of Requisition for the money order.
4. Enter the Bank Name, Branch, and Account Number associated with the money order.
5. Enter the Amount you want to refund.
6. Provide a Reason for Refund, stating the reason for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Money Order Details** (money_order_details): Enter the details about the money order you want to refund. This field is not required, but providing accurate information will help us process your refund request quickly and correctly.
* **Order Type** (order_type): Choose the type of order from the options provided. If the order type is not listed, select "Unknown".
* **Date of Purchase** (date_of_purchase): Enter the date you purchased the money order.
* **Date of Requisition** (date_of_requisition): Enter the date you submitted the requisition for the refund.
* **Bank Name** (bank_name): Enter the name of the bank where the money order was purchased.
* **Branch** (branch): Enter the branch where the money order was purchased.
* **Amount** (amount): Enter the amount you want to refund.
* **Account Number** (account_number): Enter the account number associated with the money order.
* **Reason for Refund** (reason_for_refund): Provide a clear and concise reason for your refund request. This field is not required, but it will help us understand the reason for your request and process your refund accordingly.
