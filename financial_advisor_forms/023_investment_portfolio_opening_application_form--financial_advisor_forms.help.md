# Investment Portfolio Opening Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Investment Portfolio Opening Application Form is designed to collect essential information from clients seeking to open an investment portfolio. The form provides a structured approach to gathering details about the client's investment goals, risk profile, and personal information, which is then used to provide tailored investment advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as 'required'.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
4. Enter all relevant details in the text fields.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.
