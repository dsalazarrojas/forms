# invoice_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an invoice from a financial advisor. It is a simple form designed to collect essential information from clients or customers to facilitate the generation of their invoices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields accurately, including the "Order Number," "Order Date," and "Client Name."
2. Ensure that the "Contact Information" field is up-to-date if you are requesting a physical invoice.
3. Leave the "Email Address" and "Phone Number" fields blank if not applicable.
4. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field if necessary.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Invoice Request Form** (`1`, `text`, required): This field should be left blank.
- **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter your current contact information, including any relevant details.
- **Order Number** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter the number assigned to your order, if available.
- **Order Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your order.
- **Client Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client or customer requesting the invoice.
- **Email Address** (`6`, `email`, optional): Leave this field blank unless you are requesting an email invoice.
- **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, optional): Leave this field blank unless you need to provide a phone number for communication.
- **Additional Notes** (`8`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information that is relevant to your invoice request.
- **Submit** (`9`, `text`, not required): Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips
- Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential delays or errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the financial advisor before submitting the request.
- This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it externally.
