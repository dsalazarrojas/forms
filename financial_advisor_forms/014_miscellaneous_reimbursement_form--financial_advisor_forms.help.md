# Miscellaneous Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Miscellaneous Reimbursement Form is used to request reimbursement for miscellaneous expenses incurred outside of your regular work activities. This form will help the finance department process and approve your reimbursement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting the form only once for each expense.
2. Review the form fields below for required information.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name (`user_name`)
* The user name field is where you enter your official work name or username.
* Please enter your correct name as it appears on your employee ID badge or in the company directory.

### Expense Date (`expense_date`)
* The expense date field is where you enter the date of the expense.
* Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Amount Spent (`amount_spent`)
* The amount spent field is where you enter the total amount of the expense.
* Please enter the exact amount spent, including cents if applicable.

### Expense Description (`expense_description`)
* The expense description field is where you briefly describe the expense.
* Please enter a short, concise description of the expense, including the type and location of the expense.
