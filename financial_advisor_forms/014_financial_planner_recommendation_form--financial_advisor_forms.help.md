# financial_planner_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial planner recommendation form is designed to collect personal and financial information from an individual to provide tailored investment advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Select your **Financial Institution** from the provided options.
6. Choose the **Account Type** that best describes your current account type.
7. Select all **Asset Allocation** options that apply to your investment portfolio.
8. Choose your **Risk Tolerance** level based on how comfortable you are with taking risks in your investments.
9. Select the **Investment Horizon** that best matches your investment time frame.
10. Select all **Asset Allocation Target** options that align with your financial goals.
11. Enter your **Financial Goals** in the "Financial Goals" field.
12. Choose all **Investment Objects** that you would like to invest in or consider for your investment portfolio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you will use for communication with the financial advisor.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Financial Institution** (`Financial Institution`, text, required): Select the financial institution you are affiliated with or would like to invest with.
* **Account Type** (`Account Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of account you have or would like to open (Checking, Savings, Credit Card, Investment).
* **Asset Allocation** (`Asset Allocation`, select_multiple, required): Select all asset classes you would like to allocate your funds to (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Mutual Funds).
* **Risk Tolerance** (`Risk Tolerance`, select_one, required): Choose your risk tolerance level (Conservative, Moderately Conservative, Aggressive, Very Aggressive).
* **Investment Horizon** (`Investment Horizon`, select_multiple, required): Select the time frame for your investments (Short, Medium, Long).
* **Asset Allocation Target** (`Asset Allocation Target`, select_multiple, required): Select all asset classes you would like to allocate your funds to (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Mutual Funds).
* **Financial Goals** (`Financial Goals`, text, required): Enter your financial goals and objectives.
* **Investment Objects** (`Investment Objects`, select_multiple, required): Select all investment objects you are interested in or would like to invest in (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Mutual Funds).
