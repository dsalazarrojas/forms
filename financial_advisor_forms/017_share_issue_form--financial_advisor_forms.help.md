The share_issue_form is designed to help financial advisors gather information about a company's share issues, such as company details, share details, and investor information. This form is intended for use by financial advisors to collect data for internal record-keeping and reporting purposes. 

Before proceeding, please confirm that you are authorized to access this form and that the information you are about to input is accurate and up-to-date.

# share_issue_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The share_issue_form is used to collect information about a company's share issues, including company details, share details, and investor information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details, including the company name, share details, and other relevant information.
2. Provide share details, such as the number of shares and share price.
3. Enter the date and time of the share issue.
4. Input the number of shares issued.
5. Enter the share price and issue date.
6. Enter the expiration date (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional details about the investor(s) involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): This field is used to input the name and other relevant information about the company involved in the share issue.
* **Share Details** (`share_details`, text, required): This field is used to input details about the shares being issued, such as the number of shares and share price.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, time, required): This field is used to input the date and time of the share issue.
* **Number of Shares** (`number_of_shares`, number, required): This field is used to input the total number of shares issued.
* **Share Price** (`share_price`, number, required): This field is used to input the price of each share.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, required): This field is used to input the date of the share issue.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): This field is used to input the date by which the share issue expires (if applicable).
* **Investor Details** (`investor_details`, text, optional): This field is used to input any additional details about the investor(s) involved.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Double-check your input for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your financial advisor for guidance.
