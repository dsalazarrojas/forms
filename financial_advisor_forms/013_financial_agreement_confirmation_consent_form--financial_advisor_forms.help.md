# Financial Agreement Confirmation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the client's consent and agreement to a financial arrangement. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the smooth execution of financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide the reference number of the financial agreement being confirmed.
3. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the financial agreement.
4. Confirm your signature, which signifies your agreement to the terms.
5. Enter the date you signed the form.
6. Confirm that the client has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
7. Confirm the financials and ensure they are accurate.
8. Confirm that you have reviewed the terms and conditions.
9. Confirm that the advisor has been identified and agreed upon.
10. Confirm that the address is accurate and up-to-date.
11. Enter the name of the financial institution involved in the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client as it appears on their ID or identification document.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Agreement Reference** (`agreement_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the reference number of the financial agreement being confirmed.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the financial agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign the form to signify your agreement to the terms.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Confirm Consent** (`confirm_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the client has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Confirm Signature** (`confirm_signature`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have signed the form.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Financial Agreement Reference** (`financial_agreement_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the reference number of the financial agreement being confirmed.
* **Advisor Details** (`advisor_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about the advisor involved.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the advisor.
* **Advisor Email** (`advisor_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the advisor.
* **Advisor Phone** (`advisor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the advisor.
* **Advisor License** (`advisor_license`, `text`, required): Enter the license number of the advisor.
* **Advisor Address** (`advisor_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the advisor.
* **Confirm Agreement** (`confirm_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the client has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Confirm Financials** (`confirm_financials`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the financials are accurate.
* **Confirm Signature** (`confirm_signature`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have signed the form.
* **Confirm Terms** (`confirm_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Advisor** (`confirm_advisor`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the advisor has been identified and agreed upon.
* **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the address is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the financial institution involved in the agreement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with the financial agreement.
