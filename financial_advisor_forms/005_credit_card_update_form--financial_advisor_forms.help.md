# Credit Card Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Card Update Form is used to update existing credit card information for customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant credit card type from the options provided.
2. Enter the new credit card number, ensuring it is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Enter the expiry date and expiry month and year for the credit card.
4. Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for the credit card.
5. Enter the card holder's name and phone number.
6. Select the current status of the credit card (Active, Inactive, Suspended, Cancelled, or Pending).
7. Provide a reason for the status change, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the credit card.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of credit card (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter the new credit card number, ensuring it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiry date of the credit card.
* **Expiry Month** (`expiry_month`, number, optional): Enter the expiry month of the credit card.
* **Expiry Year** (`expiry_year`, number, optional): Enter the expiry year of the credit card.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, optional): Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for the credit card.
* **Card Holder** (`card_holder`, text, optional): Enter the name of the card holder.
* **Card Holder Phone** (`card_holder_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the card holder.
* **Card Status** (`card_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the credit card (Active, Inactive, Suspended, Cancelled, or Pending).
* **Reason for Status Change** (`reason_for_status_change`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the status change, if applicable.
