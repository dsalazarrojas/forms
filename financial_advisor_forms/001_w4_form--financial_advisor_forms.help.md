# W4 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W4 Form is a financial worksheet used by employees to claim their tax withholdings. It helps determine the number of allowances an employee can claim on their paycheck.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee's information, including their name, address, and date of birth.
2. Select the correct type of employer identifier (SSN, EIN, ITIN, or ITIN/SSN).
3. Choose the W-4 option (1 or 2) that applies to your situation.
4. Enter the date of signing and employee signature.
5. Upload any necessary files, such as a W-4 certificate.
6. Review and submit the form.

### 1. Employee Name (<name> text, required: false)

### 2. Address (<name> text, required: false)

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting.
- Ensure the correct employer identifier is selected.
- Attach any necessary files for the W-4 form and certificate.
- Double-check the employee and employer signatures.
