# Wealth Preservation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Preservation Assessment is a form designed to collect essential information from clients to help them understand and manage their financial resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your goals and objectives for your financial situation.
2. Indicate your risk tolerance by selecting one of the following options:
	* Low: You are willing to take on some level of risk for potentially higher returns.
	* Medium: You are neutral and willing to balance risk and returns.
	* High: You are risk-averse and prioritize preserving your capital.
3. Provide detailed information about your assets, such as investments, properties, or other significant financial assets.
4. Choose an investment strategy that aligns with your risk tolerance and financial goals:
	* Aggressive: High-risk, high-potential return investments.
	* Moderate: Balanced mix of risk and return.
	* Conservative: Low-risk, lower-potential return investments.
5. Indicate your expected risk-free return (e.g., 3% or 5%).
6. Describe your level of investment experience:
	* Beginner: Little to no experience.
	* Intermediate: Basic understanding and some experience.
	* Advanced: Significant experience and knowledge.
7. Select the types of assets you have in your portfolio:
	* Bonds: Low-risk investments with regular income.
	* Stocks: High-risk investments with potential for high returns.
	* Real Estate: Investments in physical property or property development.
	* Cash and Equivalents: Liquid assets, such as cash, savings, or bonds.
8. Finally, describe your primary financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or retirement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`): Describe your personal and financial objectives, including short-term and long-term goals.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`): Select your risk tolerance level, which will help us understand how much risk you are willing to take on for potential returns.
* **Asset Details** (`asset_details`): Provide detailed information about your assets, such as investments, properties, or other significant financial assets.
* **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`): Choose an investment strategy that aligns with your risk tolerance and financial goals.
* **Risk Free Return** (`risk_free_return`): Indicate your expected risk-free return, which is the return you expect to earn without taking on significant risk.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`): Describe your level of investment experience, from beginner to advanced.
* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`): Select the types of assets you have in your portfolio, which will help us understand your asset mix.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`): Describe your primary financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or retirement.
