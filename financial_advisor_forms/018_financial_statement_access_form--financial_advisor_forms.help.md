# Financial Statement Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request access to financial statements, bank statements, or tax returns on behalf of clients or institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of document being requested from the "Document Type" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the period covered by the request in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
4. State the reason for the request in the "Reason for Requesting" field.
5. Choose the desired level of access from the "Access Type" field.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Document Type** (Financial Statement, Bank Statement, Tax Return, required): Select the type of document being requested.
* **Start Date** (required): Enter the start date of the period covered by the request.
* **End Date** (required): Enter the end date of the period covered by the request.
* **Reason for Requesting** (required): State the reason for the request.
* **Access Type** (Read, Read and Edit, Full Access, required): Choose the desired level of access.
* **Confirm** (required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Signature Date** (required): Date and sign the form to confirm the request.
* **Signature** (optional): A signature from the user or institution.
* **Institution** fields (Institution Name, Institution Address, Institution City, Institution State, Institution Zip, Institution Country, Institution Phone, Institution Fax, optional): Provide additional information about the institution requesting the document.
