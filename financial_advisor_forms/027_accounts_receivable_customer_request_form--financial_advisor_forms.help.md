# Accounts Receivable Customer Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect important information from customers about their accounts receivable, allowing us to process invoices and payments efficiently. Please complete this form accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are completed correctly.
2. Enter your customer's information in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the full name or business name of the customer.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address used for invoicing and payment queries.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number used for payment queries and other inquiries.
* **Billing address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where invoices should be sent to the customer.
* **Invoice amount** (`invoice_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount due from the customer.
* **Invoice due date** (`invoice_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which payment is due.
* **Payment terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment terms (Net 30, Net 60, or Due on receipt).
* **Preferred payment method** (`preferred_payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the preferred method of payment (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Check).
* **Purchase order number** (`purchase_order_number`, text, optional): Enter any applicable purchase order number.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the person responsible for payment processing.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or comments regarding billing.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the payment terms and preferred payment method to guide your communication with the customer.
* The customer information will be used to process invoices and payments efficiently.
