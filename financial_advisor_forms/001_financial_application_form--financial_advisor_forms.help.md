# Financial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a user's employment status, education level, financial income level, income frequency, type of property owned, age of property, value of property, location of property, and any additional information about the property.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the option that best describes your current employment status.
2.  Choose your education level from the options provided.
3.  Select your current financial income level.
4.  Choose how frequently you earn your income.
5.  Select all that apply for the type of property you own.
6.  Enter the age of the property you own.
7.  If the property is owned, enter its value.
8.  If you own the property, enter its location.
9.  Enter the state where the property is located.
10.  Enter the country where the property is located.
11.  Enter the zip code of the property.
12.  If you have any additional information about the property, you can enter it here.
13.  If you have any additional information about the property, you can enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your employment status?** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options to describe your current employment status.
*   **What is your education level?** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options that best describes your education level.
*   **What is your financial income level?** (`income_level`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your current financial income level.
*   **How frequently do you earn your income?** (`income_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options that best describes how frequently you earn your income.
*   **What type of property do you own?** (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply to describe the type(s) of property you own.
*   **How old is the property?** (`property_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the property you own.
*   **What is the value of the property?** (`property_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of the property you own, if known.
*   **Where is the property located?** (`property_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where the property is located.
*   **State?** (`property_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the property is located.
*   **Country?** (`property_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country where the property is located.
*   **Zip?** (`property_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the property.
*   **Additional notes about the property?** (`property_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information about the property, you can enter it here.
*   **Do you have any additional information about the property?** (`additional_property_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information about the property, you can enter it here.
