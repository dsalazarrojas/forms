# Tuition Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tuition Waiver Form is used to request waivers of tuition fees for eligible students. Please fill out the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the "Apply For Tuition Waiver" field with a brief explanation of why you are requesting a tuition waiver.
2. Enter your "Student ID" number to identify yourself as a student.
3. Select the "Program Name" that you are currently enrolled in or have been accepted into.
4. Enter the "Tuition Amount" that you are requesting a waiver for.
5. Choose the "Waiver Type" that applies to your situation.
6. Select your "Student Status" to indicate your current student status.
7. Provide a brief explanation of the "Reason For Waiver" in the space provided.
8. If necessary, attach a "Supporting Document" that explains your situation further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Apply For Tuition Waiver** (`application_info`, text, required): A brief explanation of why you are requesting a tuition waiver. Please enter a short paragraph explaining your situation.
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, select_one, required): Select the program that you are currently enrolled in or have been accepted into.
* **Tuition Amount** (`tuition_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of tuition you are requesting a waiver for.
* **Waiver Type** (`waiver_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of waiver you are requesting.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, required): Select your current student status.
* **Reason For Waiver** (`reason_for_waiver`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your tuition waiver request.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, required): If necessary, attach a supporting document that explains your situation further.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to submit your form.
