# Banking Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order banking supplies for employees. Select the type of supplies and enter the required information for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and ID number in the "Employee Name" and "Employee ID" fields, respectively.
2. If you have a work email address, enter it in the "Email Address" field.
3. If you need to contact you, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select your department from the "Department" field.
5. Enter the branch location where the supplies will be delivered.
6. Specify the date of the order.
7. Select the type of supplies from the "Supplies Category" field.
8. Enter the quantities of each item in the corresponding "Item X Quantity" fields, where X is the item number (e.g., "Item 1 Quantity", "Item 2 Quantity", etc.).
9. Select the delivery method from the "Delivery Method" field.
10. Enter the delivery address.
11. Set the urgency level for the order.
12. If applicable, enter the budget code.
13. Check the "Manager Approval" field, indicating whether the order has been approved by a manager.
14. If you have any special instructions, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your ID number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number (optional).
* **Department**: Select your department (Operations, Customer Service, Teller, Lending, IT, Human Resources, Marketing, Management, or Other).
* **Branch Location**: Enter the branch location where the supplies will be delivered.
* **Order Date**: Specify the date of the order.
* **Supplies Category**: Select the type of supplies you need (Office Supplies, Banking Forms, Marketing Materials, Technology Equipment, Safety Supplies, Customer Service Items, or Other).
* **Item 1**: Enter the name of the first item.
* **Item 1 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the first item.
* **Item 2**: Enter the name of the second item (optional).
* **Item 2 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the second item (optional).
* **Item 3**: Enter the name of the third item (optional).
* **Item 3 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the third item (optional).
* **Item 4**: Enter the name of the fourth item (optional).
* **Item 4 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the fourth item (optional).
* **Item 5**: Enter the name of the fifth item (optional).
* **Item 5 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the fifth item (optional).
* **Delivery Method**: Select the delivery method (Internal Mail, Courier, Pickup, or Standard Shipping).
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the supplies will be delivered.
* **Urgency**: Set the urgency level (Standard, Urgent, or Rush).
* **Budget Code**: Enter the budget code (optional).
* **Manager Approval**: Check whether the order has been approved by a manager.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or notes (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to review all the fields to ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your supervisor or branch manager.
- Double-check the delivery address to ensure it is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about the supplies category or urgency level, consult with your supervisor or branch manager.
