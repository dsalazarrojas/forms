# Financial Services Referral Campaign Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Services Referral Campaign Form is used to collect and track information about referrals for financial services. It is designed to provide a structured and organized way of submitting and tracking referrals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`main_contact`, email, required: false): Please enter the contact information of the person who made the referral.
* **Referral Lead Source** (`main_leadsource`, select_one, required: false): Select the source of the referral lead.
* **Referral Reason** (`main_referralreason`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Comments** (`main_referralcomments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Referral Submitted By** (`main_referralsubmittedby`, select_one, required: false): Select the person who submitted the referral.
* **Referral Submitted Date** (`main_referralsubmitteddate`, date, required: false): Enter the date the referral was submitted.
* **Referral Submitted Time** (`main_referralsubmittedtime`, time, required: false): Enter the time the referral was submitted.
* **Referral Submitted Timezone** (`main_referralsubmittedtimezone`, select_one, required: false): Select the time zone the referral was submitted in.
* **Referral Type** (`main_referralreferraltype`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of referral.
* **Referral Last Updated** (`main_referralreferrallastupdated`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose when the referral was last updated.
* **Referral Domain** (`main_referralreferraldomain`, select_one, required: false): Select the domain of the referral.
* **Referral URL** (`main_referralreferralurl`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the referral.
* **Referral Status** (`main_referralreferralstatus`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the referral.
* **Referral Comments** (`main_referralreferralcomments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Referral Created By** (`main_referralreferralcreatedby`, select_one, required: false): Select the person who created the referral.
* **Referral Created Date** (`main_referralreferralcreateddate`, date, required: false): Enter the date the referral was created.
* **Referral Updated Date** (`main_referralreferralupdateddate`, date, required: false): Enter the date the referral was last updated.
* **Referral Created Time** (`main_referralreferralcreatedtime`, time, required: false): Enter the time the referral was created.
* **Referral Updated Time** (`main_referralreferralupdatedtime`, time, required: false): Enter the time the referral was last updated.
* **Referral Money** (`main_referralreferralmoney`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of money involved in the referral.
* **Referral Currency** (`main_referralreferralcurrency`, select_one, required: false): Select the currency of the referral.
* **Referral Amount** (`main_referralreferralamount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the referral.
* **Referral Percentage** (`main_referralreferralpercentage`, number, required: false): Enter the percentage of the referral.
* **Referral Referral Percentage Incentive** (`main_referralreferralpercentageincentive`, number, required: false): Enter the referral percentage incentive.
