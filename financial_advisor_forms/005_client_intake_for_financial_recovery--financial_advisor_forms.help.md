# Client Intake For Financial Recovery - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to help plan a recovery strategy tailored to their individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "Client Full Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number in the respective fields.
5. Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
6. Estimate your gross monthly income in the "Gross Monthly Income" field.
7. Enter the total amount of outstanding debt.
8. Select all applicable options from the "Primary Goals for Financial Recovery" field.
9. If applicable, check the box indicating if you have received prior financial advice.
10. Choose a preferred time of day and date for an initial consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (Client Full Name, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your preferred email for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter your best phone number to reach you.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status.
	+ Employed full time
	+ Employed part time
	+ Self employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Retired
* **Gross Monthly Income** (Gross Monthly Income, number, optional): Estimate your gross monthly income.
* **Total Debt** (Total Debt, number, required): Enter the total amount of outstanding debt.
* **Primary Goals for Financial Recovery** (Primary Goals for Financial Recovery, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options.
	+ Reduce monthly payments
	+ Debt consolidation
	+ Improve credit score
	+ Avoid bankruptcy
	+ Repayment plan
* **Prior Financial Advice Received** (Prior Financial Advice Received, select_one, optional): Check if you have received prior financial advice.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (Preferred Contact Time, time, optional): Choose your preferred time of day for consultations.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (Preferred Consultation Date, date, optional): Choose a desired appointment date.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the advisor.
