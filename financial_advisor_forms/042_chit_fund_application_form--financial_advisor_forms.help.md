# Chit Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chit Fund Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining a Chit Fund. This form is used to assess an applicant's eligibility and collect necessary details for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). Some fields are marked as optional or not required, but it's essential to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application.
2. For each field, please provide accurate and clear information to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents (\*).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (\*).
* **Age**: Enter your age in years (\*).
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this application (\*).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number without any special characters (\*).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address (\*).
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address (\*).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside (\*).
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside (\*).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code (\*).
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the dropdown menu (\*).
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation or profession (\*).
* **Company or Business Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your company or business (\*).
* **Annual Income Range**: Select your annual income range from the dropdown menu (\*).
* **Desired Chit Fund Amount**: Enter the amount of Chit Fund you are interested in (\*).
* **Chit Fund Tenure**: Select the tenure of your Chit Fund from the dropdown menu (\*).
* **Monthly Contribution Amount**: If you are applying for a Chit Fund, enter the amount you plan to contribute each month (\*).
* **Existing Loans**: Select whether you have existing loans (\*).
* **Purpose of Chit Fund**: Select the purpose of your Chit Fund from the dropdown menu (\*).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact our support team.
* We appreciate your honesty and accuracy in completing this form.
