# Budget Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information about your financial situation, helping you create a tailored budget plan that suits your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately, providing the requested information about your personal and financial details.
2. Select the correct options for each question, ensuring you are providing the most up-to-date and accurate information.
3. Take your time to fill out the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need clarification on any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Full Name
* **Client Full Name** (required, text): Please enter your first and last name as you would like to be identified.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (required, email): Enter your primary contact email address.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (required, text): Provide your best contact number.

### Budget Planning Period
* **Budget Planning Period** (required, select_one): Select the time period for this budget, such as:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-Annually
	+ Annually

### Household Size
* **Household Size** (required, number): Enter the number of people living in your household.

### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (required, select_one): Choose your current employment status:
	+ Employed Full-time
	+ Employed Part-time
	+ Self-employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Retired
	+ Student
	+ Other

### Total Monthly Income
* **Total Monthly Income** (required, number): Enter your combined monthly income after taxes.

### Income Sources
* **Income Sources** (required, select_multiple): Select all the sources of your income:
	+ Salary/Wages
	+ Business Income
	+ Investments
	+ Rental Income
	+ Government Benefits
	+ Pension
	+ Child Support/Alimony
	+ Other

### Monthly Housing Expense
* **Monthly Housing Expense** (required, number): Enter your monthly rent or mortgage payment.

### Monthly Utilities
* **Monthly Utilities** (required, number): Enter your monthly expenses for electricity, water, gas, and internet.

### Monthly Groceries
* **Monthly Groceries** (required, number): Enter your monthly expenses for food and household items.

### Monthly Transportation
* **Monthly Transportation** (required, number): Enter your monthly expenses for car payment, fuel, insurance, and public transit.

### Monthly Insurance
* **Monthly Insurance** (required, number): Enter your monthly expenses for health, life, and other insurance premiums.

### Monthly Debt Payments
* **Monthly Debt Payments** (required, number): Enter your monthly expenses for credit cards, loans, etc.

### Monthly Entertainment
* **Monthly Entertainment** (optional, number): Enter your monthly expenses for dining out, subscriptions, and hobbies.

### Monthly Savings Contribution
* **Monthly Savings Contribution** (required, number): Enter the amount allocated to savings.

### Financial Goals
* **Financial Goals** (required, select_multiple): Select your primary financial goals:
	+ Emergency Fund
	+ Retirement Savings
	+ Home Purchase
	+ Debt Reduction
	+ Education Funding
	+ Investment Growth
	+ Vacation/Travel
	+ Major Purchase

### Budget Challenges
* **Budget Challenges** (optional, select_multiple): Select areas where you struggle the most:
	+ Overspending on Dining
	+ Impulse Purchases
	+ Irregular Income
	+ High Fixed Expenses
	+ Lack of Tracking
	+ Unexpected Expenses
	+ Debt Management
	+ None

### Previous Budgeting Experience
* **Previous Budgeting Experience** (required, select_one): Have you used a budget before?
	+ Yes - Currently Budgeting
	+ Yes - Tried Before
	+ No - First Time

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (optional, text): Enter any other financial information you'd like to share.

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Take your time to complete it, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need clarification on any questions.
* Use this form as a starting point to create a comprehensive budget plan that suits your needs and goals.
