# Investment Research Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Research Log Form is a tool designed to record and document investment research activity in a standardized way. This form helps to provide a clear and consistent structure for collecting and storing information about investment opportunities, status, and associated details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for the investment opportunities, status, and investment type fields.
3. Enter the research date and submitted date using the date picker or text input.
4. Fill in the additional notes fields as necessary.
5. Verify the submitted by information, including email and phone number, as they are required for tracking and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Research Log** (`investment_research_log_form_1`, text, required): This field is the main description of the investment research activity.
* **Investment Opportunities** (`investment_opportunities`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options for the investment opportunities, such as Alpha, Beta, or Gamma.
* **Investment Status** (`investment_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the investment, such as Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
* **Research Date** (`research_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the research was conducted.
* **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes about the research activity.
* **Analyst Name** (`analyst_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the analyst conducting the research.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount invested in the research.
* **Investment Location** (`investment_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the investment, if applicable.
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of investment, such as Equity, Bond, or Other.
* **Investment Currency** (`investment_currency`, select_one, required): Select the currency of the investment, such as USD, EUR, or JPY.
* **Research Time** (`research_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the research was conducted.
* **Research Duration** (`research_duration`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the research duration, if applicable.
* **Additional Research Notes** (`research_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the research activity.
* **Additional Investment Notes** (`investment_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the investment opportunity.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the research was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the research.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the research was submitted.
* **Submitted Location** (`submitted_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the research was submitted, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the research activity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the research.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the research.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person submitting the research.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the research.
* **Submitted By Phone Extension** (`submitted_by_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter the phone extension of the person submitting the research.
* **Submitted By Phone Country** (`submitted_by_phone_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country of the person submitting the research.
