# Senate Finance Committee Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for finance committee members or staff to review and evaluate proposals submitted by users. It helps track and record information about the proposals, committee and staff feedback, and submitter details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are submitting a proposal for a specific financial purpose or reason.
2. Fill out the **User Input** field with a brief explanation of your proposal.
3. Provide details about the **Proposal** in the **Proposal Details** field.
4. Outline the **Budget Proposal** and expected **Expected Outcome** for your project.
5. If you have an actual outcome or update, you can report it in the **Actual Outcome** field.
6. Select the **Finance Officer** and **Finance Committee** responsible for reviewing your proposal.
7. Select the **Staff** members who will be working on your project.
8. Provide your **Submitter Information**, including your name and contact details.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Submitter Note** field.
10. Finally, add your signature in the **Submitter Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text, required): A brief explanation of your proposal.
* **Proposal Details** (text, required): Detailed information about your proposal.
* **Budget Proposal** (text, required): A breakdown of your proposed budget.
* **Expected Outcome** (text, required): A description of the expected results or outcome of your proposal.
* **Actual Outcome** (text, optional): An update on the actual outcome or result of your proposal.
* **Date Submitted** (date, required): The date you submitted your proposal.
* **Submitter Information** (text, required): Your name and title.
* **Submitter Contact** (text, required): Your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Submitter Note** (note, optional): Additional comments or notes from the submitter.
* **Committee Recommendation** (select multiple, required): Select all committee members who reviewed your proposal.
* **Other Committee Recommendation** (select multiple, optional): Select additional committee members who reviewed your proposal.
* **Finance Officer** (select one, required): Select the finance officer who will be working on your proposal.
* **Other Finance Officer** (select one, optional): Select additional finance officers who will be working on your proposal.
* **Staff Recommendation** (select multiple, optional): Select staff members who will be working on your proposal.
* **Other Staff Recommendation** (select multiple, optional): Select additional staff members who will be working on your proposal.
* **Date Reviewed** (date, required): The date your proposal was reviewed.
* **Review Comment** (text, optional): Additional comments from the review committee.
* **Submitter Signature** (note, required): Your signature and name.
* **Finance Committee** (select one, required): Select the finance committee that reviewed your proposal.
* **Other Finance Committee** (select one, optional): Select additional finance committees that reviewed your proposal.
* **Staff** (select one, optional): Select the staff members who reviewed your proposal.
* **Other Staff** (select one, optional): Select additional staff members who reviewed your proposal.
* **Submitter** (select one, required): Select the submitter who submitted your proposal.
* **Other Submitter** (select one, optional): Select additional submitters who submitted your proposal.
