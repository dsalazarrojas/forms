## Personal Budget Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Budget Tracking Form is designed to track your monthly income and expenses, helping you understand your financial situation better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month and year you want to track.
2. Fill in your monthly income.
3. Choose your income source (Salary, Investment, Business, or Other).
4. Select your expense levels for Rent, Food, Transportation, Healthcare, Entertainment, and Miscellaneous.
5. Enter your progress towards your financial goals.
6. Select your total expense level for each category.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your monthly income? (<type: number>)**: Enter your total monthly income.
* **Monthly Expenses (<type: number>)**: Enter your total monthly expenses.
* **Where does your income come from? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Choose from options: Salary, Investment, Business, or Other.
* **How much are your monthly expenses? (<type: select_one>)**: Choose from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your financial goals? (<type: text>)**: Enter your financial goals.
* **How much progress you have made towards your financial goals? (<type: select_one>)**: Choose from options: On Track, Behind Schedule, Ahead of Schedule.
* **What are your expenses for Rent? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your expenses for Food? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your expenses for Transportation? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your expenses for Healthcare? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your expenses for Entertainment? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
* **What are your total expenses for Miscellaneous? (<type: select_multiple>)**: Select from options: High, Medium, Low.
