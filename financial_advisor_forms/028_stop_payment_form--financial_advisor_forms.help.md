# Stop Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stop Payment Form is a financial form used to cancel a payment that has already been initiated. This form is used to stop an existing payment from being processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Account Number** of the account you want to stop payment on.
2. Provide a **Reason for Stopping Payment** to describe why you are stopping the payment.
3. Enter the **Date** when you want the payment to be stopped.
4. Enter the **Amount** of the payment you want to stop.
5. Select the **Account Type** of the account to be stopped (e.g., Checking, Savings, or Credit Card).
6. Enter any **Contact Information** for the client, including their **Email** and **Phone Number**.
7. Confirm that you want to **Confirm Payment Stop**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stop Payment Form**: This is the title of the form, which you should not edit.
* **Account Number**: The account number associated with the payment you want to stop.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Reason for Stopping Payment**: A brief description for the reason of payment stop.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
* **Date**: The date when you want the payment to be stopped.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Amount**: The amount of the payment you want to stop.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Account Type**: The type of account to be stopped (e.g., Checking, Savings, or Credit Card).
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Account Type (2)**: Another field for selecting the account type.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Contact Info**: Contact information for the client, including their email and phone number.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
* **Email**: Email of the client.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: No
* **Phone**: Phone number of the client.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
* **Confirm Payment Stop**: Confirm the payment stop.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Confirm Payment Stop (2)**: Another field to confirm the payment stop.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No

## Tips

* Ensure you are authorized to access this form before submitting it.
* Double-check the account number and reason for stopping payment to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact your administrator or financial advisor for assistance.
