# Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form is used to gather information from individuals or entities requesting a mortgage payoff statement. The form is designed to help the requesting party provide necessary details about their mortgage, loan term, loan type, and other relevant information to facilitate the processing of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal or business details: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
* Enter your contact information: Phone and Address.
* Enter your mortgage details: Account Number, Mortgage Amount, and Loan Term.
* Select the loan type and term that applies to your situation: Loan Type and Loan Term.
* Specify the loan status: Jumbo Loan, Conforming, and Escrow Status.
* Choose the reason for your request: Request Reason.
* Date of the request: Request Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Your last name.
* **Email** (3): Your email address.
* **Phone** (4): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Account Number** (5): The account number associated with your mortgage.
* **Request Date** (6): The date you are requesting the mortgage payoff statement.
* **Mortgage Amount** (7): The original amount of the mortgage.
* **Address** (8): Your address.
* **Loan Number** (9): The loan number associated with your mortgage.
* **Property Value** (10): The value of the property the mortgage is for.
* **Zip Code** (11): The zip code of your address.
* **Request Reason** (12): The reason for your mortgage payoff request.
* **Loan Term** (13): The length of time you want to repay the mortgage (30, 20, 15 years).
* **Loan Type** (14): Select all that apply: 15 years, 20 years, or 30 years.
* **Jumbo Loan** (15): Select all that apply: 1 million, 2 million, 3 million.
* **Conforming** (16): Select all that apply: True, False.
* **Escrow Status** (17): Select all that apply: Open, Closed.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's essential to fill in all relevant information to ensure the efficient processing of your request.
