# Financial Counseling Referral Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their financial situation, goals, and circumstances to provide personalized guidance and support for financial counseling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide contact information that we can use to reach out to you.
3. Share your financial history, including any relevant details about your income, expenses, assets, and liabilities.
4. Describe your financial goals, such as saving for retirement or a down payment on a house.
5. List any financial institutions you are associated with, such as banks or credit cards.
6. Discuss your tax returns, including any relevant tax laws or regulations.
7. Outline your employment status and any relevant employment information.
8. Describe your assets and liabilities, including any investments or debts.
9. Share your risk tolerance, including any concerns or questions you have about investing.
10. Report your income and expenses, including any relevant financial obligations.
11. Describe your investment objects, such as specific investment vehicles or asset classes.
12. Outline your investment strategies, such as risk management or long-term planning.
13. Share any relevant tax laws or regulations.
14. Describe your tax planning goals and objectives.
15. Share your retirement goals and objectives.
16. Discuss your risk management strategies.
17. Describe your estate planning goals and objectives.
18. Share your wills and trusts, including any relevant documents or agreements.
19. Describe your probate and estate planning goals and objectives.
20. Report your tax return status, including any relevant due dates or deadlines.
21. Share your income tax filing status.
22. Outline your tax filing status.
23. Discuss your tax return due date.
24. Report your tax return received status.
25. Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding of the information provided.

* **Client Information**: Enter your name, address, and contact information, so we can reach out to you for further questions or support.

* **Investment Objects**: Describe your investment objects, such as specific investment vehicles or asset classes.
* **Investment Strategies**: Outline your investment strategies, such as risk management or long-term planning.
