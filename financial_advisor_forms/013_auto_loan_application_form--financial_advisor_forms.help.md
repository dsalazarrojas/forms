# Auto Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Loan Application Form is designed to help you apply for an auto loan by providing a structured approach to collecting the necessary information about yourself, your vehicle, and your loan preferences. This guide will guide you through each section of the form to ensure you have a smooth and accurate application process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by carefully reading each section and follow the instructions provided.
*   Ensure you have all required documents and information ready before starting.
*   Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly, taking care to follow the format and instructions for each section.
*   If you are unsure about any section, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`)
    *   This section is a note that explains the purpose of the form and encourages the applicant to provide personal details.
    *   It is a placeholder and not a required field.

### 2. **First Name** (`first_name`)
    *   This field is for your legal first name.
    *   Please enter your true first name.

### 3. **Middle Name** (`middle_name`)
    *   This field is optional, but if you have a middle name, please enter it here.
    *   Enter your middle name, if applicable.

### 4. **Last Name** (`last_name`)
    *   This field is for your legal last name.
    *   Enter your true last name.

### 5. **Suffix** (`suffix`)
    *   This field is for any surname suffixes, such as Jr, Sr, III, etc.
    *   If you have a suffix, enter it here.

### 6. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`)
    *   This field is for your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
    *   Enter your birthdate accurately.

### 7. **Social Security Number** (`ssn`)
    *   This field is for your Social Security Number in XXX-XX-XXXX format.
    *   Enter your Social Security Number as per the format provided.

### 8. **Email Address** (`email`)
    *   This field is for your primary email address.
    *   Enter the email address you use most frequently.

### 9. **Phone Number** (`phone`)
    *   This field is for your best contact number.
    *   Enter the number you prefer to be contacted on.

### 10. **Street Address** (`address`)
    *   This field is for your current street address.
    *   Enter your current address accurately.

### 11. **City** (`city`)
    *   This field is for the city of your residence.
    *   Enter the city where you currently reside.

### 12. **State** (`state`)
    *   This field is for the state of your residence.
    *   Select the state where you currently reside from the provided options.

### 13. **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`)
    *   This field is for your 5-digit ZIP code.
    *   Enter your current ZIP code.

### 14. **Residence Type** (`residence_type`)
    *   This field is for your housing situation.
    *   Select the type of housing you currently reside in from the provided options.

### 15. **Monthly Housing Payment** (`monthly_payment`)
    *   This field is for your monthly rent or mortgage payment.
    *   Enter the amount you pay monthly for your housing.

### 16. **Time at Current Address** (`time_at_address`)

    *   Select the duration of your stay at your current address from the provided options.

### 17. **Previous Address** (`previous_address`)
    *   This field is optional but is for your previous address if you have been at your current address for less than 3 years.
    *   Enter your previous address if applicable.

### 18. **Employment Information** (`employment_info`)

### 20. **Employer Address** (`employer_address`)

### 21. **Job Title** (`job_title`)

### 22. **Employment Phone** (`employment_phone`)
