**Uniform Borrower Assistance Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The **Uniform Borrower Assistance Form** is a document that collects information about the borrower's financial status and mortgage details. This form is used to help borrowers provide accurate and comprehensive information about their financial situation, which is essential for mortgage lenders to assess their creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your financial status and mortgage details.
2. Enter your income and expenses in the correct fields, including income earned, expenses paid, and financial institution information.
3. Select the correct options for your employment status, income source, and loan payment terms.
4. Enter any additional information or comments about your loan application.
5. Review and verify the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Status** (`Financial Status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select your current financial status from the options: Financially Stable, Financially Struggling, Financially Distressed.
* **Income Earnings** (`Income Earnings`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the amount of income you have earned in the past 30 days.
* **Income Source** (`Income Source`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all the sources of income that apply to you: Employment Income, Social Security, Pensions, Other.
* **Expenses Paid** (`Expenses Paid`, `text`, optional):
	+ List all the expenses you have paid in the past 30 days.
* **Income-to-Expenses Ratio** (`Income-to-Expenses Ratio`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter a ratio of your income to expenses (e.g., 10:1, 20:1).
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select your current employment status: Employed, Not Employed, Retiree, Disabled.
* **Income Qualified** (`Income Qualified`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all the income qualifications that apply to you: Income Qualified, Income Ineligible, Not Applicable.
* **Financial Institution** (`Financial Institution`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your financial institution.
* **Mortgage Balance** (`Mortgage Balance`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the outstanding mortgage balance.
* **Total Mortgage** (`Total Mortgage`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the total amount of the mortgage.
* **Monthly Payment** (`Monthly Payment`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the monthly payment.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the type of property you own: Primary Residence, Second Home, Investment Property.
* **Property Value** (`Property Value`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the value of the property.
* **Credit Score** (`Credit Score`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter your credit score.
* **Debt-to-Income** (`Debt-to-Income`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the debt-to-income ratio.
* **Credit History** (`Credit History`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your credit history.
* **Account Opened** (`Account Opened`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the account was opened.
* **Loan Amount** (`Loan Amount`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the loan amount.
* **Loan Interest Rate** (`Loan Interest Rate`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the loan interest rate.
* **Loan Term** (`Loan Term`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the loan term.
* **Loan Payment Terms** (`Loan Payment Terms`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the loan payment terms: Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Quarterly.
* **Loan Security Type** (`Loan Security Type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the type of security for the loan: Collateral, Unsecured, Other.
* **Loan Status** (`Loan Status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the loan status: Active, Inactive, Closed.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `note`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about your loan application.
* **Account Opened** (`Account Opened`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the account was opened.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the type of property you own: Primary Residence, Second Home, Investment Property.
