## Financial Future Assessment - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Financial Future Assessment is a form designed to help users gauge their current financial situation, set achievable financial goals, and identify potential future financial challenges. This form aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of a person's financial health and future plans.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete the Financial Future Assessment form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Start with the **Introduction** section, which is an optional note-taking area for any additional information you'd like to provide.
3. Move on to the **Financial Goals** section, where you'll set your financial goals for a specific duration (e.g., short-term, medium-term, long-term).
4. In the **Financial Status** section, select one of three options that best describes your current financial situation.
5. Enter your current **Financial Income** and **Financial Expenses** in the respective fields.
6. Estimate your current **Financial Assets**.
7. In the **Financial Future Aims** section, describe your short-term, medium-term, and long-term financial goals.
8. Identify any **Financial Future Obstacles** you're currently facing or expect to face in the future (optional).
9. Finally, outline your **Financial Future Plans** to achieve your goals (optional).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (optional): A brief note-taking area for any additional information you'd like to provide about your current financial situation.
* **Financial Goals** (required): Set your financial goals for a specific duration, such as short-term, medium-term, or long-term goals.
* **Financial Status** (required): Select one of three options that best describes your current financial situation: Financially stable, Financially stable, but some challenges, or Not financially stable.
* **Financial Goals Duration** (required): Enter a numerical value representing the duration of your financial goals (e.g., 1 year, 5 years, etc.).
* **Financial Income** (required): Enter your current annual income.
* **Financial Expenses** (required): Enter your current annual expenses.
* **Financial Assets** (required): Enter a numerical value representing your current financial assets (e.g., savings, investments, etc.).
* **Financial Future Aims** (required): Describe your short-term, medium-term, and long-term financial goals.
* **Financial Future Obstacles** (optional): Identify any potential future financial challenges or obstacles you're facing, such as financial difficulties, health issues, relationship problems, or legal issues.
* **Financial Future Plans** (optional): Outline your plans to achieve your financial goals.

### Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Use the provided options for select fields, but feel free to add additional information in the text fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or seek assistance from the form administrator.
* Remember to review and update your answers as your financial situation changes over time.
