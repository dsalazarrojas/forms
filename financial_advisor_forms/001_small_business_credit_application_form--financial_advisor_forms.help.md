# small_business_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The small business credit application form is used by small business owners to apply for credit. This form gathers business and owner details, credit request information, and other relevant data to assess the creditworthiness of the business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your business details: Enter information about your business, such as the nature of your business and its age.
2. Enter owner information: Provide details about yourself, including your education, experience, and contact information.
3. Specify credit request information: Select the loan amount, loan term, and payment frequency for your credit request.
4. Provide additional required information: Select your business type, tax ID (EIN or SSN), and enter your tax ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business details**: Enter a brief description of your business, including its name, address, and type.
* **Business owner details**: Enter information about yourself, including your name, education level, experience, and contact information.
* **Credit request**: Select the loan amount, loan term, and payment frequency for your credit request.
* **Business finances**: Enter information about your business finances, including its financial situation and any relevant financial data.
* **Loan amount**: Enter the amount of credit you are requesting.
* **Loan term**: Select the length of time you are requesting the credit for.
* **Interest rate**: Enter the interest rate you are willing to accept for the credit.
* **Payment frequency**: Select the frequency at which you are willing to make payments on the credit.
* **Credit score**: Enter your credit score (optional).
* **Business type**: Select the type of business you own.
* **Business age**: Enter the age of your business.
* **Business experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Owner experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Owner education**: Select your level of education.
* **Owner address**: Enter your address.
* **Owner email**: Enter your email address.
* **Owner phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Business email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Business phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Tax ID**: Select your tax ID type (EIN or SSN) and enter your tax ID number.
* **Business location**: Enter the location of your business.
* **Owner location**: Enter the location where you live.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our credit team for assistance.
