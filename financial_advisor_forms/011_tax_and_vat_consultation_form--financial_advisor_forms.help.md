# Tax and VAT Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect tax and VAT information from businesses for consultation purposes. It is intended for businesses that need to provide tax-related details to be reviewed by tax experts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tax Information section with your business's tax details.
2. Provide your Vat number and select the Business Details type (e.g., sole proprietorship, company ltd, or other).
3. Enter the contact information (name, email, phone number).
4. Specify the Tax Type (e.g., vat, tax, or other).
5. Enter the Tax Amount and Tax Date.
6. Add any Additional Notes if necessary.
7. If relevant, upload a file and select the Tax Expert type (e.g., chartered accountant, cma, or other).
8. Fill in the Tax Office, Address, Post Code, City, and Country.
9. Enter the Tax Year and Vat Rate.
10. Finally, select the Business Type (e.g., private, public, or both) and provide any optional business details (Company Name, Business Address, Business Phone, Business Email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Information** (Tax Information, id: 1): Enter your business's tax information in this field.
* **Vat number** (Vat number, id: 2): Enter your Vat number.
* **Business Details** (Business Details, id: 3): Select the type of business you have (e.g., sole proprietorship, company ltd, or other).
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, id: 4): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, id: 5): Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, id: 6): Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Tax Type** (Tax Type, id: 7): Specify the type of tax you are dealing with (e.g., vat, tax, or other).
* **Tax Amount** (Tax Amount, id: 8): Enter the tax amount.
* **Tax Date** (Tax Date, id: 9): Enter the date of the tax.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, id: 10): Add any additional information.
* **File Upload** (File Upload, id: 11): Upload a file if necessary (not required).
* **Tax Expert** (Tax Expert, id: 12): Select the type of tax expert (e.g., chartered accountant, cma, or other).
* **Tax Office** (Tax Office, id: 13): Enter the tax office information.
* **Address** (Address, id: 14): Enter the address of your business.
* **Post Code** (Post Code, id: 15): Enter the post code of your business.
* **City** (City, id: 16): Enter the city of your business.
* **Country** (Country, id: 17): Select the country of your business.
* **Tax Year** (Tax Year, id: 18): Enter the tax year.
* **Vat Rate** (Vat Rate, id: 19): Enter the vat rate.
* **Business Type** (Business Type, id: 20): Select the type of business (e.g., private, public, or both).
* **Company Name** (Company Name, id: 21): Enter the name of your company (not required).
* **Business Address** (Business Address, id: 22): Enter the address of your business (not required).
* **Business Phone** (Business Phone, id: 23): Enter the phone number of your business (not required).
* **Business Email** (Business Email, id: 24): Enter the email address of your business (not required).
* **Submit** (Submit, id: 25): Complete and submit the form.
