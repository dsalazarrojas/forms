# Client Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Qualification Form is designed to help us understand your financial situation and needs, allowing us to provide personalized advisory services tailored to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely in each field.
3. Select the most relevant options for each field.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address to receive important updates and communications from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current marital status.
* **Number of Dependents** (`number_of_dependents`, `number`, required): If applicable, enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your primary occupation or profession.
* **Employer Name** (`employer`, `text`, required): If applicable, enter the name of your current or former employer.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Annual Income Range** (`annual_income`, `select_one`, required): Select your annual income range.
* **Sources of Income** (`income_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable sources of income.
* **Estimated Net Worth** (`net_worth`, `select_one`, required): Select your estimated net worth.
* **Liquid Assets Available for Investment** (`liquid_assets`, `select_one`, required): Select the amount of liquid assets you have available for investment.
* **Total Current Debts** (`current_debts`, `select_one`, required): Select your total current debts (excluding mortgage).
* **Types of Debt** (`debt_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable types of debt.
* **Home Ownership Status** (`home_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Select your current home ownership status.
* **Investment Experience Level** (`investment_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of investment experience.
* **Current Investment Holdings** (`investment_holdings`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable current investment holdings.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required): Select your risk tolerance level.
* **Primary Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable primary investment goals.
* **Investment Time Horizon** (`investment_timeframe`, `select_one`, required): Select your investment time horizon.
* **Financial Services Needed** (`financial_services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable financial services needed.
* **Current Advisor** (`current_advisor`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you currently work with a financial advisor.
* **Why Are You Seeking a Financial Advisor Now** (`why_seeking_advisor`, `text`, required): Explain why you are seeking a financial advisor.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, required): Select how you heard about us.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information or questions you have.

Please take your time and fill in the form carefully. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
