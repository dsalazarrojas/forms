# insurance_coverage_needs_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help insurance companies or financial advisors assess your insurance coverage needs. Please provide the required information to help us understand your current insurance coverage and identify potential gaps.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information in the sections below. Make sure to fill out each section carefully, as it will help us provide you with accurate recommendations.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Insurance Coverage Needs** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of insurance coverage you currently have or need. You can select one or multiple options from the list.
	+ Health Insurance
	+ Life Insurance
	+ Disability Insurance
	+ Long-term Care Insurance
* **Contact Details** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your contact details, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): If you have an email address, please provide it for future communication.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your relationship with the emergency contact person. You can select from the list:
	+ Spouse
	+ Child
	+ Parent
	+ Friend
	+ Other
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Next of Kin** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a next of kin, please provide their name and address.

Please note that the required fields are `General Information` and `Insurance Coverage Needs`. The rest of the fields are optional, but providing them will help us better understand your needs and provide more accurate recommendations.
## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as it will help us provide you with relevant recommendations.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
