# Investment Transition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are transitioning their investments, allowing us to understand their current investments, the type of transition they are undergoing, and their satisfaction with the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **transition_details** (text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the transition you are undergoing, including any relevant information about your current investments and the reason for the transition.
* **current_investments** (select multiple, required/optional): Please select all of the investments you currently hold.
* **transition_type** (select one, required/optional): Please select the type of transition you are undergoing (e.g., "Yes" for a transition, "No" otherwise).
* **transition_method** (text, required/optional): Please describe the method you used to transition your investments.
* **satisfaction** (number, required/optional): Please rate your satisfaction with the transition process on a scale of 0-10.
* **comments** (text, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the transition process.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your transition.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your participation in this form.
