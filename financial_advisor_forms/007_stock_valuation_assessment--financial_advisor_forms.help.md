# Stock Valuation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the value and potential of a stock for investment purposes. It helps financial analysts and investors evaluate various aspects of a stock, including its name, risk score, financial metrics, and investment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Company Name** of the stock you are assessing.
2. Express your **Investment Opinion** regarding the stock, e.g., whether it's a buy, sell, or hold.
3. Assign a **Risk Score** to the stock, indicating its potential risk level.
4. Enter the **Stock Price** and **Market Capitalization** of the stock.
5. Select the **Sector** of the stock from the provided options.
6. Enter the **Industry** of the stock.
7. Enter the **Stock Symbol**.
8. Specify the **Investment Horizon**, i.e., the time frame for the investment.
9. Enter the **Return Rate**, **Risk-Free Rate**, and **Stock Dividend Yield** of the stock.
10. Select the **Investment Strategies** applicable to the stock.
11. Enter the remaining fields as requested, providing any additional information about the stock.
