# Asset Activity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Activity Form is designed to collect and document information about company assets, including their type, value, location, and condition. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date records of company assets, facilitating informed decision-making and compliance with financial regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Company Name" field and enter the company's legal business name.
2. Enter the "Fiscal Year" field, which is the year for which the asset report is being filed.
3. Enter the "Reporting Date" field, which is the date the asset activity report was completed.
4. Choose the type of asset from the "Asset Type" dropdown list, such as Cash and Cash Equivalents, Accounts Receivable, or Inventory.
5. Enter a detailed description of the asset in the "Asset Description" field.
6. Indicate the location of the asset in the "Asset Location" field.
7. Enter the "Purchase Date" field, which is the date the asset was acquired.
8. Enter the original cost of the asset in the "Original Cost" field.
9. Enter the accumulated depreciation of the asset, if applicable.
10. Enter the current market value of the asset as of the reporting date.
11. Calculate the "Book Value" by subtracting the accumulated depreciation from the original cost.
12. Choose the current condition of the asset from the "Asset Condition" dropdown list, such as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
13. Optional: Select the depreciation method used from the "Depreciation Method Used" dropdown list, such as Straight-line or Declining Balance.
14. Optional: Enter the estimated useful life of the asset in years.
15. Optional: Enter the estimated salvage value of the asset at the end of its useful life.
16. Optional: Enter the insurance coverage amount, if applicable.
17. Choose the current operational status of the asset from the "Asset Status" dropdown list, such as In Use, Idle, or Sold or Disposed.
18. Optional: Enter any significant changes or activities related to the asset in the "Recent Activity or Changes" field.
19. Optional: Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance for the asset.
20. Optional: Enter any additional notes or comments about the asset.
21. Enter the name of the person preparing the report in the "Prepared By" field.
22. Enter the date the report was prepared in the "Preparation Date" field.
