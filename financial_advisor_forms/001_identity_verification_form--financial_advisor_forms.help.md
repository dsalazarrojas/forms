# IdentityVerificationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and employment information from users, as well as to verify their identity through the upload of identity documents. However, as mentioned in the form description, this is not the primary purpose of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Personal Details** section with your personal information (ID: 1).
2. Provide your **Contact Info** (ID: 2).
3. Enter your **Address** (ID: 3).
4. Enter your **Date Of Birth** (ID: 4) in the specified date format.
5. Select your **Employment Status** (ID: 6) as either Active or Inactive.
6. Enter your **Job Title** (ID: 8) and **Employment Date** (ID: 9) if applicable.
7. If applicable, select the type of **Identity Verification** (ID: 10) you have, e.g., Upload ID Card, Upload Passport, or Upload Driving License.
8. If you selected an **Identity Verification** option, enter the link to the uploaded document (ID: 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (ID: 1, text, required): Enter your personal details as per your identification documents. 
- **Contact Info** (ID: 2, text, required): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email. 
- **Address** (ID: 3, text, required): Enter your address where you can be contacted. 
- **Date Of Birth** (ID: 4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyy (depending on your region). 
- **Employment Info** (ID: 5, text, required): Enter your employment information, such as company name, or job role. 
- **Employment Status** (ID: 6, select_one, required): Select whether you are actively employed or not. 
- **Employer** (ID: 7, text, required): Enter your current or last employer's name. 
- **Job Title** (ID: 8, text, required): Enter your job title or position at your current or last employer. 
- **Employment Date** (ID: 9, date, required): Enter the date you started or ended your employment. 
- **Identity Verification** (ID: 10, select_multiple, required): Select which type of identity document you have, and upload it (if selected). 
- **Document** (ID: 11, text, required): Enter the link or reference to the uploaded document. 

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct identity document type for accurate verification. 
- If you are uploading a document, ensure the link is valid and can be accessed by the intended recipient. 
- Ensure your personal and employment details are accurate to avoid errors or delays in the process. 
- If you have any issues with uploading documents, contact the support team for assistance. 
- This form is for personal and employment information, please do not use it for other purposes unless explicitly mentioned.
