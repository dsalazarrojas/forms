# Financial Investment Strategy Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Investment Strategy Audit Form is designed to help users assess their financial investment strategy by asking a series of questions that cover various aspects of their investment goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply answer the questions truthfully and select the options that best describe your financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences.
- You can select multiple answers for questions that allow for multiple selections, such as Risk Preferences, Asset Classes, and Investment Frequency.
- Make sure to answer all questions, as they all contribute to a comprehensive understanding of your financial investment strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Investment Goals
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, `text`, required: false): This field asks about your financial goals, such as saving for retirement, a down payment on a house, or other specific targets.

### 2. Risk Preferences
* **Risk Preferences** (`risk_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your risk tolerance, with options "Yes" and "No". Select "Yes" if you are willing to take on more risk, and "No" if you are more conservative.

### 3. Return Expectations
* **Return Expectations** (`return_expectations`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your expected return on investment, which is a measure of how much you hope to earn on your investments.

### 4. Risk Tolerance
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your risk tolerance, with options "Yes" and "No". Select "Yes" if you are willing to take on more risk, and "No" if you are more conservative.

### 5. Time Horizon
* **Time Horizon** (`time_horizon`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the time frame for your investment, with options "Yes" and "No". Select "Yes" if you have a longer time horizon, and "No" if you prefer shorter-term investments.

### 6. Investment Strategy
* **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, `note`, required: false): This field provides a text area for you to describe your existing investment strategy.

### 7. Portfolio Assets
* **Portfolio Assets** (`portfolio_assets`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the types of assets in your investment portfolio, with options "Yes" and "No". Select "Yes" if you have a mix of asset classes, and "No" if you prefer a single type.

### 8. Investment Objectives
* **Investment Objectives** (`investment_objectives`, `text`, required: false): This field asks about your investment objectives, such as saving for a specific goal or generating income.

### 9. Risk Level
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your risk level, with options "High" and "Low". Select "High" if you are willing to take on more risk, and "Low" if you prefer lower risk.

### 10. Asset Allocation
* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, `text`, required: false): This field asks about your current asset allocation, such as the proportion of your portfolio invested in different asset classes.

### 11. Asset Classes
* **Asset Classes** (`asset_classes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the types of asset classes in your portfolio, with options "Yes" and "No". Select "Yes" if you have a mix of asset classes, and "No" if you prefer a single type.

### 12. Risk Free Rate
* **Risk Free Rate** (`risk_free_rate`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your expected risk-free rate of return.

### 13. Liquidity Requirement
* **Liquidity Requirement** (`liquidity_requirement`, `number`, required: false): This field asks about your liquidity requirements, or how easily you can access your money when you need it.

### 14. Investment Period
