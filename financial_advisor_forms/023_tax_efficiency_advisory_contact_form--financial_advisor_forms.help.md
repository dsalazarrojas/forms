# Tax Efficiency Advisory Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals seeking tax efficiency advisory services, which includes tax planning and financial guidance to optimize their tax situation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out as they are required fields.
- Please note that the form cannot be saved for later submission. Each time you submit the form, it will be treated as a new request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter your name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`<contact_email>`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address that you check regularly for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`<phone_number>`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Advisory Needs** (`<advisory_needs>`, `text`, required): Describe your tax advisory needs and requirements clearly.
* **Inquiry Type** (`<inquiry_type>`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of inquiry you have, such as Tax Advisory or Financial Planning.
* **Follow-up Preference** (`<follow_up_preference>`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred follow-up method, such as Phone, Email, or In-person.
* **Additional Information** (`<additional_information>`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional details or context that may be helpful to the tax efficiency advisory service provider.
* **Send the form** (`<form_submit>`, `note`, optional): Click this button to submit the form. Please note that submission is final and not reversible.
