# credit_report_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit report authorization form is used to collect information required for a credit report authorization, which is a crucial step in managing client data and reporting. This form ensures that the financial advisor has the necessary authorization to pull a credit report for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Name, email, phone, address, date_of_birth, ssn, employer_name, employer_address, and employer_phone. These fields are marked as "required" and are necessary for verifying the client's identity and employment status.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the authorization fields: authorization_to_pull, credit_bureau, and credit_bureau_agency. These fields are used to indicate whether the client has given consent to pull their credit report and which credit bureaus to use for the report.
3. Use the note fields (note, note_2, note_3, note_4, note_5, note_6, note_7, note_8, note_9, note_10, note_11, note_12) to add any additional information that may be helpful for the financial advisor to provide a comprehensive understanding of the client's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name as it appears on their ID or government-issued ID.
* **email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **address** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the client's physical address.
* **date_of_birth** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the client's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **ssn** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the client's social security number for identification purposes.
* **employer_name** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter the client's employer's name.
* **employer_address** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the client's employer's address.
* **employer_phone** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the client's employer's phone number.
* **authorization_to_pull** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if the client has given consent to pull their credit report, and "No" otherwise.
* **credit_bureau** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" for each credit bureau that the financial advisor has permission to use for the report.
* **credit_bureau_agency** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" for each credit bureau agency that the financial advisor has permission to use for the report.
* **note** (`14`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information that may be helpful for the financial advisor to provide a comprehensive understanding of the client's situation.
* **note_2**, **note_3**, **note_4**, **note_5**, **note_6**, **note_7**, **note_8**, **note_9**, **note_10**, **note_11**, **note_12** (`15-25`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information that may be helpful for the financial advisor to provide a comprehensive understanding of the client's situation.
