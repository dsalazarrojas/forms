# financial_forecast_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for financial advisors to provide information for financial forecast reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the financial forecast period from the options provided.
2. Choose the financial projection currency.
3. Enter a brief description of the financial forecast report.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter your contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **financial_forecast_report** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the financial forecast report.
- **financial_projection_period** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the financial forecast period (Six Months, Three Years, Four Years, Five Years).
- **financial_projection_currency** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the financial projection currency (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY).
- **financial_forecast_report_form_notes** (`5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **financial_forecast_report_form_email** (`6`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the required information ready before filling out the form.
- The form is designed to be filled out by one person at a time.
- The data collected will be used to support financial forecasting.
- All information collected will be kept confidential and secure.
