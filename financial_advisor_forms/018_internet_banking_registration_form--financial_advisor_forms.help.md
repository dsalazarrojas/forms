# internet_banking_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internet banking registration form is used to register a user for internet banking services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name
2. Enter your Email
3. Enter your Phone number
4. Select your Account Type (choose one of the options)
5. Enter your Account Number
6. Enter your Password (a numerical value)
7. Confirm your Password (enter the same numerical value as above)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address that will be used for internet banking communications.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you in case of any issues or queries.
* **Account Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of account you want to register for:
	+ Option 1: Default internet banking registration
	+ Option 2: Alternative internet banking service A
	+ Option 3: Alternative internet banking service B
* **Account Number** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your account number for the selected account type.
* **Password** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a numerical value as your password for internet banking.
* **Confirm Password** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Re-enter your password to confirm it.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the internet banking service you are registering for.
