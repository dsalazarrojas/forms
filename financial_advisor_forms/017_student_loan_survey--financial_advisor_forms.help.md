# Student Loan Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Loan Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather feedback from students about their loan experiences. This information will help us better understand student loan satisfaction and pain points, allowing us to improve our services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully to provide accurate and honest answers.
2. For question 2, select all that apply from the options.
3. For questions 3 and 4, enter your answer in the required format (question 3 is a number, question 4 is a selection from the options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Loan Survey** (`student_loan_survey`, text, required: false): This is a general question to confirm you have reached this survey.
* **Student Loan Questions** (`student_loan_survey_questions`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all that apply to the following questions:
		- Yes: Have you ever taken a student loan?
		- No: Have you not taken a student loan?
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Please enter the total amount borrowed for your student loan.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Very Satisfied: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your student loan experience?
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your student loan experience?
	+ Neutral: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your student loan experience?
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your student loan experience?
	+ Very Dissatisfied: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your student loan experience?

## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much information as possible to help us understand your experience.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your general understanding of the topic.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, we welcome them.
