# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for internal use by financial advisors to collect information from customers. It is not meant to be filled out by the customer directly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are authorized to access this form and that it is not a duplicate entry.
2. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information.
3. Enter the customer's name in the `customer name` field.
4. Provide the customer's address in the `customer address` field.
5. Enter the date of the request in the `request date` field.
6. If applicable, enter the customer's company information, including company name and address.
7. Enter a brief reason for the request in the `reason for request` field.
8. Provide the customer's contact information, including phone number and email.
9. Enter the amount requested by the customer.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the customer.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the request.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the customer's company name.
- **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief reason for the request.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the customer's company address.
- **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the customer's company phone number.
- **Requested Amount** (`requested_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount requested by the customer.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the request.
- **Account Number** (`account_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the customer's account number.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any errors or discrepancies. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant department or contact support for clarification.
