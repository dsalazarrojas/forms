# Inherited IRA Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inherited IRA Distribution Form is used to track and manage the distribution of Inherited Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs). It provides a platform for financial professionals to collect necessary information from beneficiaries, account holders, or other stakeholders involved in the distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and accurate information for the submitter, beneficiary, and other relevant details.
2. Select the correct distribution method and percentage from the available options.
3. Provide the current distribution status of the account.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments related to the distribution.
5. Select the review status to indicate whether the form has been reviewed.
6. Choose the financial services involved in the process.
7. Specify who submitted the form and when it was submitted.
8. Update the form as necessary with the latest information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name (`submitter_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.**
* **Submitter Email (`submitter_email`, `email`, required/optional): Provide the email address of the submitter. This may be the contact information of the person submitting the form.**
* **Beneficiary Name (`beneficiary_name`, `text`, required/optional): Input the name of the beneficiary who is receiving the inheritance.**
* **Beneficiary Phone (`beneficiary_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the beneficiary.**
* **Inherited IRA Account Number (`inherited_ira_account_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the account number of the inherited IRA account.**
* **Inherited IRA Account Type (`inherited_ira_account_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of inherited IRA account, such as IRA or Rollover.**
* **Inherited IRA Value (`inherited_ira_value`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the current value of the inherited IRA account.**
* **Distribution Method (`distribution_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more distribution methods, such as Checklist or Other.**
* **Distribution Percentage (`distribution_percentage`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the percentage of the distribution amount.**
* **Distribution Date (`distribution_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the distribution.**
* **Distribution Status (`distribution_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current status of the distribution, such as Pending, Completed, or Cancelled.**
* **Notes (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the distribution.**
* **Review Date (`review_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was last reviewed.**
* **Review Status (`review_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current review status, such as Pending, Completed, or Cancelled.**
* **Financial Services (`financial_services`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the financial services involved in the process, such as Account Manager, Beneficiary Coordinator, or Financial Planner.**
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who submitted the form, such as Financial Advisor, Beneficiary, or Financial Services Team.**
* **Submitted Date (`submitted_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.**
* **Updated By (`updated_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who last updated the form, such as Financial Advisor or Financial Services Team.**
* **Updated Date (`updated_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was last updated.**
* **Submitted To (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who the form was submitted to, such as Financial Services Team, Beneficiary, or Financial Advisor.**
* **Notes Updated (`notes_updated`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the notes were last updated.**
* **Submitted To Name (`submitted_to_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or entity submitted to.**
* **Submitted To Phone (`submitted_to_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person or entity submitted to.**
* **Submitted To Email (`submitted_to_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person or entity submitted to.**
* **Submitted To Address (`submitted_to_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the person or entity submitted to.**
