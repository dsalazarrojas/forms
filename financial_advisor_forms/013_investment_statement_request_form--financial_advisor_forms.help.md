# Investment Statement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Statement Request Form is a tool used to request investment statements for various investment instruments. This form is designed to collect relevant information from clients to facilitate the process of obtaining investment statements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of investment statement you need by choosing from the "Investment Statement Request" dropdown menu.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email or phone number and address.
3. Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (investment_statement_request_form_client): This field is for you to enter your name and other relevant contact information.
* **Client Name** (client_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Investment Instrument** (investment_instrument): Select one or more investment instruments you are requesting statements for (e.g. Equity, Bond, Mutual Fund).
* **Investment Duration** (investment_duration): Enter the duration of your investment (e.g. years, months).
* **Investment Statement** (investment_statement): Enter a brief description of the investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Instrument 1** (investment_instrument_1): Select the first investment instrument you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 1** (investment_statement_1): Enter a brief description of the investment statement for the selected instrument.
* **Investment Statement 2** (investment_statement_2): Enter a brief description of the second investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 3** (investment_statement_3): Enter a brief description of the third investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 4** (investment_statement_4): Enter a brief description of the fourth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 5** (investment_statement_5): Enter a brief description of the fifth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 6** (investment_statement_6): Enter a brief description of the sixth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 7** (investment_statement_7): Enter a brief description of the seventh investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 8** (investment_statement_8): Enter a brief description of the eighth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 9** (investment_statement_9): Enter a brief description of the ninth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement 10** (investment_statement_10): Enter a brief description of the tenth investment statement you are requesting.
* **Investment Statement Request** (investment_statement_request): Confirm the type of investment statement you are requesting (e.g. for investment statement, account statement, or other).
* **Contact Method** (contact_method): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone).
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address** (contact_address): Enter your address.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Submit** (submit): Review and submit your form.
* **Confirm** (confirm): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your form correctly.
* **Error Message** (error_message): This field will display any error messages if there are any issues with your submission.
