# credit_file_investigation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit file investigation request form is used to gather relevant information related to a credit file investigation. This form is designed to collect contact information, employment status, and other details for the subject of the investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contact title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr., Rev.).
2. Enter the name of the individual being investigated.
3. Provide the email address of the individual being investigated.
4. Enter the phone number of the individual being investigated.
5. Enter the address of the individual being investigated.
6. Select the social security number from the provided options.
7. Enter the date of birth of the individual being investigated.
8. Enter the credit card number of the individual being investigated.
9. Select the employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**: (`contact_info`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This field is used to select the contact title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr., Rev.).
* **Name**: (`name`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the name of the individual being investigated.
* **Email**: (`email`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the email address of the individual being investigated.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the phone number of the individual being investigated.
* **Address**: (`address`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the address of the individual being investigated.
* **Social Security Number**: (`social_security_number`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select the social security number from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false)
  Enter the date of birth of the individual being investigated.
* **Credit Card Number**: (`credit_card_number`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the credit card number of the individual being investigated.
* **Employment Status**: (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false)
  Select the employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student).
