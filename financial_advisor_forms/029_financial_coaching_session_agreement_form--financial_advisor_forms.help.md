# Financial Coaching Session Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Coaching Session Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a financial coaching session between a client and a financial coach. This form is used to ensure a clear understanding of the coaching relationship, client goals, and review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and contact information, including their phone number and email address.
2. Specify the client's coaching goals and desired outcomes.
3. Select the financial coach who will be working with the client.
4. Review and agree on the terms of the coaching session, including the review frequency and date.
5. Specify any additional notes or comments about the coaching session.
6. Confirm the review summary and the financial coaching session agreement.
7. Specify the review status and the client's status.
8. Select the review frequency and version of the financial coaching session agreement form.
