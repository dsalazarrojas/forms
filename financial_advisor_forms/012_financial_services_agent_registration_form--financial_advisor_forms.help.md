# Financial Services Agent Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from financial services agents for the purpose of registering them with the system. It captures contact details, social media links, agent type, license status, and other relevant information for internal processing and reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **agent name** in the field "agent_name". This is the name of the person registering the form.
2. Enter your **agency name** in the field "agency_name". This is the name of the agency or organization you represent.
3. Provide your **contact email** in the field "contact_email". This will be the primary contact email for the agent.
4. Enter your **contact phone** in the field "contact_phone". This is the phone number associated with the agent's contact email.
5. Enter your **agent address** in the field "agent_address". This is the physical address of the agent.
6. Select your **agent type** from the drop-down menu. This indicates the type of agent (e.g., Agent, Broker).
7. Enter your **agency address** in the field "agency_address". This is the physical address of the agency.
8. Enter your **website** in the field "website". This is the URL of the agent's or agency's website.
9. Select your **social media links** from the drop-down menu. This includes your presence on various social media platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube).
10. Enter your **date of birth** in the field "date_of_birth". This is the date of birth of the agent.
11. Enter your **agent since** in the field "agent_since". This is the date and time the agent has been working as an agent.
12. Enter your **agent licensing authority** in the field "agent_licensing_authority". This is the authority that issued the agent's license.
13. Select your **agent licensing status** from the drop-down menu. This indicates whether the agent is actively licensed or not.
14. Enter your **agent license number** in the field "agent_license_number". This is the actual license number associated with the agent.
15. Enter your **agent employer** in the field "agent_employer". This is the name of the employer of the agent.
16. Enter your **agent position** in the field "agent_position". This is the job title or position of the agent.
17. Enter your **agent employer address** in the field "agent_employer_address". This is the address of the agent's employer.
18. Enter your **agent employer phone** in the field "agent_employer_phone". This is the phone number associated with the agent's employer.
19. Enter your **agent license expiration** in the field "agent_license_expiration". This is the date and time the agent's license expires.
20. Enter your **agent termination date** in the field "agent_termination_date". This is the date and time the agent's contract or agreement terminated.
21. Select your **agent termination status** from the drop-down menu. This indicates whether the agent is actively terminated or not.
22. Enter your **agent renewal date** in the field "agent_renewal_date". This is the date and time the agent's license was renewed.
23. Select your **agent renewal status** from the drop-down menu. This indicates whether the agent's license is actively renewed or not.
24. Enter your **submitted by** in the field "submitted_by". This is the person or department submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **agent_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **agency_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the agency or organization you represent.
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Provide your primary contact email for official correspondence.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number associated with your contact email.
* **agent_address** (text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **agent_type** (select_one, optional): Select your type of agent (e.g., Agent, Broker).
* **agency_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the agency or organization you represent.
* **website** (text, optional): Enter your official website URL.
* **social_media** (select_multiple, optional): Select the social media platforms you are present on.
* **date_of_birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **agent_since** (time, optional): Enter the time since you have been working as an agent.
* **agent_licensing_authority** (text, optional): Enter the authority that issued your license.
* **agent_licensing_status** (select_one, required): Select whether you are actively licensed or not.
* **agent_license_number** (number, optional): Enter your actual license number.
* **agent_employer** (text, optional): Enter the name of your employer.
* **agent_position** (text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **agent_employer_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of your employer.
* **agent_employer_phone** (text, optional): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **agent_license_expiration** (time, optional): Enter the date and time your license expires.
* **agent_termination_date** (date, optional): Enter the date and time your contract or agreement terminated.
* **agent_termination_status** (select_one, required): Select whether you are actively terminated or not.
* **agent_renewal_date** (date, optional): Enter the date and time your license was renewed.
* **agent_renewal_status** (select_one, required): Select whether your license is actively renewed or not.
* **submitted_by** (text, optional): Enter the person or department submitting the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the correctness of your contact information.
* Ensure accurate and truthful representation of your agent and agency data.
