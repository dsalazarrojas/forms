# Financial Performance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by financial advisors to assess the financial performance of a business. It is designed to evaluate the financial standing of a company, identify areas of improvement, and provide recommendations for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete the form in the following order:
	* Provide the business name and type
	* Enter the start date of the business
	* Enter the revenue and expenses of the business
	* Calculate the net income
	* Determine the profit margin
	* Assess the financial stance of the business
	* Enter the business goal and financial performance indicators
	* Identify risk factors and financial challenges
	* Enter financial objectives
	* Enter financial risks and indicators
	* Specify the audit period and auditor name and contact

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1): Enter the name of the business. This field is not required.
* **Business Type** (2): Select the type of business, such as Small Business, Large Business, Partnership, or Non-Profit. This field is not required.
* **Start Date** (3): Enter the start date of the business. This field is not required.
* **Revenue** (4): Enter the revenue of the business. This field is not required.
* **Expenses** (5): Enter the expenses of the business. This field is not required.
* **Net Income** (6): Calculate the net income by subtracting expenses from revenue. This field is not required.
* **Profit Margin** (7): Calculate the profit margin as a percentage of revenue. This field is not required.
* **Financial Stance** (8): Select the financial stance of the business, such as Strong, Stable, or Weak. This field is not required.
* **Business Goal** (9): Enter the business goal or objective. This field is not required.
* **Financial Performance Indicators** (10): Enter financial performance indicators that measure the business's financial health. This field is not required.
* **Risk Factors** (11): Enter risk factors that may affect the business's financial performance. This field is not required.
* **Financial Challenges** (12): Enter financial challenges that the business is facing. This field is not required.
* **Financial Objectives** (13): Select the financial objectives of the business, such as Increase Revenue, Reduce Expenses, Improve Profit Margin, or Increase Net Income. This field is not required.
* **Financial Indicators** (14): Enter financial indicators that measure the business's financial performance. This field is not required.
* **Financial Risks** (15): Enter financial risks that the business is facing. This field is not required.
* **Audit Period** (16): Enter the audit period for which the financial performance is being assessed. This field is not required.
* **Auditor Name** (17): Enter the name of the auditor who conducted the financial performance audit. This field is not required.
* **Auditor Contact** (18): Enter the contact information of the auditor who conducted the financial performance audit. This field is not required.
