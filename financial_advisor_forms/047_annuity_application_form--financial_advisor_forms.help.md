# Annuity Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather detailed information from applicants seeking annuity insurance coverage. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields have been completed accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure the completion of this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure all required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) are completed.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (5, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Social Security Number** (6, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Citizenship Status** (7, select_one, required): Select your current citizenship status.
* **Marital Status** (8, select_one, required): Select your current marital status.
* **Spouse Name** (9, text, optional): If applicable, enter your spouse's name.
* **Spouse Date of Birth** (10, date, optional): If applicable, enter your spouse's date of birth.
* **Primary Beneficiary** (11, text, required): Enter the full name of your primary beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary Relationship** (12, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and your primary beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary Percentage** (13, number, required): Enter the percentage allocated to your primary beneficiary.
* **Annuity Type** (14, select_one, required): Select the type of annuity you are applying for.
* **Premium Amount** (15, number, required): Enter the investment amount for the annuity.
* **Payment Frequency** (16, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to receive payments.
* **Payout Option** (17, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive payouts.
* **Payout Start Date** (18, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to begin receiving payouts.
* **Employment Status** (19, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Annual Income** (20, select_one, required): Select your approximate annual income.
* **Net Worth** (21, select_one, required): Select your current net worth (excluding home value).
* **Investment Experience** (22, select_one, required): Select your level of investment experience.
* **Investment Objective** (23, select_one, required): Select your primary investment objective.
* **Risk Tolerance** (24, select_one, required): Select your risk tolerance level.
* **Existing Annuities** (25, select_one, optional): Select if you own other annuities.
* **Like-Kind Exchange** (26, select_one, optional): Select if this is a 1035 exchange.
* **Insurance Producer** (27, text, optional): Enter the name of your insurance producer.
* **Producer License Number** (28, text, optional): Enter the insurance producer's license number.
* **Suitability Review** (29, select_one, required): Select if suitability has been reviewed.
* **Disclosures Read** (30, select_one, required): Select if you have read all disclosures.
* **Terms and Conditions** (31, select_one, required): Select if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Applicant Signature** (32, text, required): Type your full name to sign the form.
* **Signature Date** (33, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Additional Comments** (34, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your insurance producer or agent.
* Review the terms and conditions before accepting them.
* This form is for annuity insurance coverage only.
* Please do not use this form for other purposes.
