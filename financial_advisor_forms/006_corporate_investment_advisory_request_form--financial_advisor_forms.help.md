# Corporate Investment Advisory Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to provide personalized investment advice. It is intended for clients who are seeking investment advisory services from financial advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer accurately.
- Provide as much detail as possible to ensure that your investment needs are met.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your financial advisor for clarification.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Information (1)**: This field is for entering your corporate name and any other relevant corporate details.
* **Company Details (2)**: Please enter your company's details such as address, phone number, and email.
* **Investment Goals (3)**: This field is for describing your investment goals, e.g., retirement, short-term investment, long-term investment.
* **Advisory Needs (4)**: Please describe the investment advisory services you are seeking.
* **Investment Preferences (5)**: This field is for specifying your investment preferences, e.g., risk tolerance, return on investment (ROI).
* **Tax ID (6)**: Please enter your tax ID number for tax purposes.
* **Address (7)**: Please enter your address for contact purposes.
* **City (8)**: Please enter your city.
* **State (9)**: Please enter your state.
* **Zip (10)**: Please enter your zip code.
* **Phone (11)**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (12)**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date (13)**: Please enter a specific date for any specific date-related questions.
* **Time (14)**: Please enter a specific time for any time-related questions.
* **Amount (15)**: Please enter a specific amount for any amount-related questions.
* **Select One (16)**: This field is for selecting one option from a list of options.
* **Select Multiple (17)**: This field is for selecting multiple options from a list of options.
* **Select Three (18)**: This field is for selecting three options from a list of options.
* **Note (19)**: This field is for any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Confirm (20)**: Please select one option to confirm your understanding.
* **Confirm Multiple (21)**: This field is for selecting multiple options to confirm your understanding.
* **Confirm Three (22)**: This field is for selecting three options to confirm your understanding.
* **Confirm Four (23)**: This field is for selecting four options to confirm your understanding.
* **Date Range (24)**: Please select a date range for any date-related questions.
* **Time Range (25)**: Please select a time range for any time-related questions.
