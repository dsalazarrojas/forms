# investment_performance_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help investment managers track and compare the performance of investments. It captures essential details such as start and end dates, initial and final investment values, return rates, duration, and additional comments. By using this form, managers can quickly gauge the efficiency and productivity of investments, make informed decisions, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the start date of the investment in the `investment_start_date` field.
2. Enter the end date of the investment in the `investment_end_date` field.
3. Enter the initial investment value in the `investment_start_value` field.
4. Enter the final investment value in the `investment_end_date` field.
5. Enter the net value of the investment in the `investment_net_value` field.
6. Enter the return rate of the investment in the `investment_return_rate` field.
7. Enter the investment duration in days in the `investment_duration` field.
8. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the `notes` field.
9. (Optional) If you want to compare this investment's performance to others of similar type, select an option in the `investment_compare` field.
10. (Optional) If you know the total return on investment, enter it in the `investment_return` field.
11. (Optional) If you can compare this investment's performance to your expectations, select an option in the `investment_expectation` field.
12. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the `investment_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **investment_start_date**: Enter the date when the investment started.
* **investment_end_date**: Enter the date when the investment ended.
* **investment_start_value**: Enter the initial investment value, e.g., the amount of money you invested.
* **investment_end_value**: Enter the final investment value after the investment period.
* **investment_net_value**: Enter the net value of the investment after deducting any costs or losses.
* **investment_return_rate**: Enter the rate of return, e.g., the percentage increase or decrease in value.
* **investment_duration**: Enter the duration of the investment in days.
* **notes**: Additional comments or concerns about the investment.
* **investment_compare**: Compare this investment's performance to others of similar type.
* **investment_return**: Enter the total return on investment.
* **investment_expectation**: Compare this investment's performance to your expectations.
* **investment_comments**: Additional comments or concerns about the investment.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate start and end dates to ensure correct calculation of the return rate.
* Be as precise as possible when entering the initial and final investment values to ensure accurate net value calculation.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or additional information.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the `notes` or `investment_comments` fields.
