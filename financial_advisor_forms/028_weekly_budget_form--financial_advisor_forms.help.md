# Weekly Budget Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Budget Form is a tool to help users track and manage their weekly finances, setting a financial goal and categorizing expenses to better understand their financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for which you want to set a budget.
2. Enter your total weekly income.
3. Enter your total weekly expenses.
4. Choose a financial goal for the week (Income, Expenses, Savings, or Other).
5. Select the categories of your weekly expenses (e.g., Housing, Utilities, Transportation).
6. Enter the total amount of expenses for each category.
7. Specify the sources of your weekly income (e.g., Paycheck, Interest, Sales).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Weekly expenses**: Enter your total weekly expenses.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: No
- **Weekly income**: Enter your total weekly income.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: No
- **Weekly goal**: Choose a financial goal for the week (Income, Expenses, Savings, or Other).
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: No
- **Weekly expense categories**: Select categories for your weekly expenses (Housing, Utilities, Transportation, Food, Entertainment).
  - Type: Select Multiple
  - Required: No
- **Weekly expense amount**: Enter the total weekly expense amount for each category.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: No
- **Weekly income sources**: Specify the sources of your weekly income (Paycheck, Interest, Sales, Other).
  - Type: Select Multiple
  - Required: No
- **Weekly budget date**: Enter the date for which you want to set a budget.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: No

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when entering your income and expenses.
- Use the goal field to set a specific target for the week, such as saving for a particular expense or saving for a specific amount.
- Use the categories field to group similar expenses for easier tracking and comparison.
- Use the income sources field to specify any additional income, such as interest or sales.
