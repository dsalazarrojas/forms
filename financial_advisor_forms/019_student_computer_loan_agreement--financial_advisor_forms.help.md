# Student Computer Loan Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of a computer loan provided to a student. It captures information about the loan amount, loan terms, computer details, and student and financial aid user information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review and agree to the loan terms and conditions.
2. Provide your student name and details.
3. Enter the loan amount.
4. Choose the type of computer you are loaning (Desktop, Laptop, Mobile, Gaming PC, or Tablet).
5. Provide your signature and signature date.
6. Confirm the loan terms and agreement date.
7. Enter the date of computer return (if applicable).
8. Enter the financial aid user's information (name, email, phone number, and signature).
9. Confirm the financial aid user's agreement.
10. Review and confirm the loan agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your student name.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`): Enter the amount of the loan.
* **Computer Type** (`computer_type`): Choose the type of computer being loaned (Desktop, Laptop, Mobile, Gaming PC, or Tablet).
* **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`): Enter the loan terms and conditions. (Note: This field is not visible in the form, as it is a note field. However, it is intended to capture any additional terms or conditions that need to be understood by both parties.)
* **Loan Terms Details** (`loan_terms_details`): Enter any additional details about the loan terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Loan Terms** (`loan_terms_agree`): Confirm your agreement to the loan terms and conditions by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Agree to Loan Terms Date** (`loan_terms_agree_date`): Enter the date of your agreement to the loan terms and conditions.
* **Computer Details** (`computer_details`): Enter any additional details about the computer being loaned.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`): Sign your name to confirm your understanding of the loan terms and conditions.
* **Student Signature Date** (`student_signature_date`): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the loan agreement.
* **Financial Aid User Information** (`finaid_user_name`, `finaid_user_email`, `finaid_user_phone`, `finaid_user_title`, `finaid_user_signature`, `finaid_user_signature_date`): Enter the financial aid user's name, email, phone number, and signature.
* **Financial Aid User Agreement** (`finaid_agree`): Confirm the financial aid user's agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Financial Aid User Agreement Date** (`finaid_agree_date`): Enter the date of the financial aid user's agreement.
* **Student Loan Agreement Date** (`student_loan_agreement_date`): Enter the date of the loan agreement.
* **Financial Aid User Comments** (`finaid_user_comments`): Enter any additional comments from the financial aid user.
* **Computer Return Date** (`student_computer_return_date`): Enter the date the computer is due to be returned. 

## Tips
- Please review and agree to the loan terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
- Confirm your agreement and signature with the financial aid user.
