# Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Application Form is designed to collect personal and employment information from applicants seeking credit. This information will be used to assess the applicant's creditworthiness and determine the feasibility of extending credit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section (fields 1-3) with your basic information.
2. Provide your employment status (field 4) and income details (fields 5-7).
3. Answer questions about your employment verification, income verification, address verification, and bank verification (fields 8-11).
4. Enter your ID number (field 12) and employment start date (field 13).
5. Select your income frequency (field 14) and income source (field 15).
6. Choose your employment verification level (field 16).
7. Finally, review and confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)**: Enter your personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Loan Details (2)**: Briefly describe the type of loan you are applying for.
* **Contact Info (3)**: Provide your contact information.
* **Employment Status (4)** (`Active` or `Inactive`): Indicate your current employment status.
* **Income Details (5)**: Enter your income details such as amount and frequency.
* **Credit History (6)** (`Yes` or `No`): Answer whether you have a credit history.
* **Social Security Number (7)**: Enter your social security number.
* **Employment Verification (8)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are willing to provide employment verification.
* **Income Verification (9)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are willing to provide income verification.
* **Address Verification (10)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are willing to provide address verification.
* **Bank Verification (11)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are willing to provide bank verification.
* **ID Number (12)**: Enter your ID number.
* **Employment Start Date (13)**: Enter your employment start date.
* **Income Frequency (14)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have an income frequency.
* **Income Source (15)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have an income source.
* **Employment Verification Type (16)** (`High` or `Low`): Choose your employment verification level.
* **Employer Verification (17)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are willing to provide employer verification.
* **Credit Score (18)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have a credit score.
* **Employment Status 2 (19)** (`Active` or `Inactive`): Indicate your current employment status 2.
* **Income Frequency 2 (20)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have an income frequency 2.
* **Employment Start Date 2 (21)**: Enter your employment start date 2.
* **Employment Type (22)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have an employment type.
* **Income Source 2 (23)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you have an income source 2.
* **Employment Verification Level (24)** (`High` or `Low`): Choose your employment verification level.
* **Loan Term (25)** (`Yes` or `No`): Indicate whether you are applying for a loan.
