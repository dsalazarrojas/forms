# Student Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student loan application, including student name, loan type, amount, and other relevant details. It helps to gather accurate and comprehensive data for processing student loan applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the basic information such as student name, student ID number, and date of birth.
2. Select the type of student loan you are applying for (e.g., Private, Federal, Parent, or Other).
3. Enter the school name where you are attending or have attended.
4. Fill out the loan amount and term (e.g., Month, Year, or Month-Year) as required.
5. Enter the loan rate (interest rate) and interest.
6. Provide contact information (phone number) and email address (if applicable).
7. Enter your social security number (for identification purposes).
8. Finally, indicate your current student loan status (e.g., In School, Graduated, In Grace Period, or Default).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID Number** (Student ID Number, number, required/optional): Enter your student ID number issued by your school.
* **Student Loan Type** (Student Loan Type, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of loan you are applying for (e.g., Private, Federal, Parent, or Other).
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student Loan Amount** (Student Loan Amount, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the loan you are applying for.
* **Loan Term** (Loan Term, select_one, required/optional): Select the term of the loan (e.g., Month, Year, Month-Year, or Other).
* **Student Loan Rate** (Student Loan Rate, number, required/optional): Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **Student Loan Interest** (Student Loan Interest, number, required/optional): Enter the interest of the loan.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Social Security Number** (Social Security Number, text, required/optional): Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Student Loan Status** (Student Loan Status, select_one, required/optional): Select your current student loan status (e.g., In School, Graduated, In Grace Period, or Default).
