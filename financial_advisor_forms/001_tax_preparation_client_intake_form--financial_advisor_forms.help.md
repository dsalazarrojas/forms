# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for tax preparation purposes, gathering data to generate financial reports and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
2. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your tax ID number in the "Tax ID" field.
8. Select the correct tax year from the list.
9. Enter your income in the "Income" field (if applicable).
10. Select your financial institution from the list.
11. Select the bank routing numbers corresponding to your financial institution.
12. Enter your account number in the "Account Number" field.
13. Enter your SSN (Social Security Number) in the "SSN" field.
14. Enter your first name in the "Account Holder First Name" field.
15. Enter your last name in the "Account Holder Last Name" field.
16. Select your account type (e.g., Checking, Savings, etc.).
17. Enter the name of your bank in the "Bank Name" field.
18. Enter any additional account numbers as required.
19. Enter your account holder's email address in the "Account Holder Email" field.
20. Enter your account holder's phone number (if applicable).
21. Enter your address 2 (if applicable).
22. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
23. Select your employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, etc.).
24. Select your employment type (e.g., W2, 1099).
25. Fill out any additional account numbers as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to receive communication from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to receive communication from us.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Tax ID**: Enter your tax ID number for identification purposes.
* **Tax Year**: Select the year for which you are preparing taxes.
* **Income**: Enter your annual income.
* **Financial Institution**: Select your bank or financial institution from the list.
* **Bank Routing**: Select the bank routing numbers corresponding to your financial institution.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number for identification purposes.
* **SSN**: Enter your Social Security Number for identification purposes.
* **Account Holder First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your account documents.
* **Account Holder Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your account documents.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of account you hold (e.g., Checking, Savings, etc.).
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank or financial institution.
* **Account Number 2**: Enter any additional account numbers as required.
* **Account Holder Email**: Enter the email address of your account holder.
* **Account Holder Phone**: Enter the phone number of your account holder.
* **Address 2**: Enter your second physical address (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, etc.).
* **Employment Type**: Select your employment type (e.g., W2, 1099).
* **Account Number 3**: Enter any additional account numbers as required.
* **Account Number 4**: Enter any additional account numbers as required.
