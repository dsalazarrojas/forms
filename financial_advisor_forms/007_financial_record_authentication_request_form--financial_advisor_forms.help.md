# Financial Record Authentication Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request authentication of financial records. Please fill it out to provide necessary information to verify financial records. This form is designed to ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Financial Record Authentication Request Form" page.
2. Scroll down and fill out the "Client Information" and "Financial Record Details" fields.
3. Then, provide contact details such as "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Email".
4. Choose the "Financial Institution" you are working with by selecting from the options or type "Other" and provide the name of the financial institution.
5. Choose the "Verification Status" by selecting from the options or type "Other" and provide a description.
6. If you need to attach any financial records, please do so by uploading the files in the respective fields.
7. Once you have filled out all the fields, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Record Authentication Request** (<financial_record_authentication_request>, text, required): Provide a brief description of the financial record you are trying to authenticate.
* **Client Information** (<client_information>, text, required): Enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Financial Record Details** (<financial_record_details>, text, required): Describe the financial record you are trying to authenticate, including the type of record and the date it was last updated.
* **Contact Details** (<contact_details>, text, required): Provide the contact information of the person or organization handling this request.
* **Verification Status** (<verification_status>, text, required): Choose the status of the financial record by selecting from the options or type "Other" and provide a description.
* **Financial Certificate** (<financial_certificate>, text, required): Attach a financial certificate or other relevant document to support your request.
* **Financial Certificate Attachment** (<financial_certificate_attachment>, text, required): Attach any supporting documents or attachments to the financial certificate.
* **Signature** (<signature>, text, required): Sign the request by typing your name.
* **Financial Record Attachment** (<financial_record_attachment>, text, required): Attach any supporting documents or attachments to the financial record.
* **Verification Attachment** (<verification_attachment>, text, required): Attach any supporting documents or attachments to the verification.
* **Financial Certificate Number** (<financial_certificate_number>, text, required): Enter the financial certificate number if available.
* **Certificate Date** (<certificate_date>, date, required): Enter the date of the financial certificate.
* **Verification Date** (<verification_date>, date, required): Enter the date of the verification.
* **Contact Name** (<contact_name>, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (<contact_phone>, text, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (<contact_email>, email, required): Enter the contact email address.
* **Financial Institution** (<financial_institution>, select_one, required): Choose the financial institution from the options or type "Other" and provide the name of the financial institution.
* **Other Financial Institution** (<financial_institution_other>, text, required): If you chose "Other" above, provide the name of the financial institution.
* **Other Financial Institution** (<financial_institution_other>, text, required): This field is not used. Please disregard.
* **Verification Method** (<verification_method>, select_multiple, required): Choose the method of verification from the options or type "Other" and provide a description.
* **Other Verification Method** (<verification_method_other>, text, required): If you chose "Other" above, provide the description of the verification method.
* **Financial Record Status** (<financial_record_status>, select_one, required): Choose the status of the financial record from the options or type "Other" and provide a description.
* **Other Financial Record Status** (<financial_record_status_other>, text, required): If you chose "Other" above, provide the description of the financial record status.
* **Verification Status Description** (<verification_status_description>, text, required): Enter a description of the verification status.
* **Signature Confirmation** (<signature_confirmation>, note, required): Sign the request by typing your name.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out completely and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
