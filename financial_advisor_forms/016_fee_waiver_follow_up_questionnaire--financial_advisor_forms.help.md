# Fee Waiver Follow Up Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to follow up on a fee waiver application to determine the current status and any additional requirements or changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Applicant Name** field.
2. If there have been any updates to the applicant details, please provide them in the **Updated Applicant Details** field.
3. Select the current **Fee Waiver Status** of the application.
4. Upload any required documents related to the fee waiver application in the **Uploaded Documents** field.
5. Provide any relevant comments in the **Review Comments** field.
6. Select the **Review Decision** for the fee waiver application, which can be School, Court, Non-profit, or Service Office.
7. If additional financial support is required, please select **Yes** in the **Financial Support** field.
8. If any other financial support is needed, list it in the **Other Funding Sources** field.
9. If additional comments are necessary, provide them in the **Additional Comments** field.
10. If any other financial support is needed, list it in the **Other Financial Support** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your name to help us keep track of your application.
* **Updated Applicant Details**: Provide any updated information about yourself or your situation that may affect the fee waiver application.
* **Fee Waiver Status**: Select the current status of your application, whether it is active or inactive.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload any documents related to your fee waiver application, such as identification or financial information.
* **Review Comments**: Provide any comments that are relevant to the review process.
* **Review Decision**: Select the decision made by our team regarding your fee waiver application.
* **Other Funding Sources**: Provide any other sources of funding that may be used in conjunction with the fee waiver application.
* **Financial Support**: Select **Yes** if you require additional financial support for your application.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments that are relevant to your application.
* **Other Financial Support**: Provide any other financial support needed for your application.
