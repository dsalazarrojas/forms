# Financial Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Consultation Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals seeking financial consultation. This form helps us understand your financial goals, risk tolerance, and preferences to provide personalized financial guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each question that apply to you.
2. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Consultation Form (Page 1)**
	+ **Financial Consultation Form (Label):** Enter a brief description of your financial goals and objectives (text, maximum 500 characters).
* **Client Info (Page 2)**
	+ **Client Info (Label):** Please select the most accurate description of your current financial situation:
		- Option A: Individual investor
		- Option B: Business owner
		- Option C: Other
