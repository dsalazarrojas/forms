# new_finance_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_finance_client_intake_form is designed to collect essential information from new financial clients during the initial intake process, helping financial advisors provide high-quality services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Fill out the "Financial Info" field with your current financial situation.
3. Select your employment status from the options in the "Employment Status" field.
4. If applicable, provide additional employment-related information in the "Employment Info" field.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail, or None) in the "Contact Method" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
7. Select the assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Add any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the ID of the assigned form in the "Assigned Form ID" field.
10. If needed, add further comments or notes in the "Notes 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required)**: Please provide your name and contact details for our financial advisor to reach out to you.
* **Financial Info (Financial Info, text, required)**: Please fill out this field with your current financial situation, including any relevant details.
* **Employment Status (Employment Status, select_one, required)**: Select your employment status from the provided options.
* **Employment Info (Employment Info, text, optional)**: If you have a unique employment situation, please provide additional details in this field.
* **Contact Method (Contact Method, select_multiple, required)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail, or None).
* **Note (Note, note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes if needed.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool, select_one, required)**: Select the tool assigned to you by the financial advisor.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Add any further comments or notes in this field.
* **Assigned Form ID (Assigned Form ID, number, required)**: Enter the ID of the assigned form.
* **Notes 2 (Notes 2, note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes in this field.
