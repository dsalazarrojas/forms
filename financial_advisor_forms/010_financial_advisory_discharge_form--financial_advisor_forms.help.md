# Financial Advisory Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge of a financial advisor from a client. It captures details about the advisor, the client, and the reason for termination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all fields are filled out with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that you have permission from the client before proceeding with the discharge process.
3. If you need assistance or have questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the financial institution's administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial (1)**: Enter a brief description of the current financial situation or circumstances.
* **Category (2)**: Select the relevant category for the discharge.
* **Discharge Date (3)**: Enter the date of discharge.
* **Discharge Reason (4)**: Enter the reason for the discharge of the financial advisor.
* **Comments (5)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the discharge.
* **Signature (6)**: Enter your signature and date to confirm that you have completed and reviewed the form.
* **Client Name (7)**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Email (8)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (9)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Financial Investment Advisor (10)**: Enter the financial advisor's name.
* **Financial Investment Advisor Company (11)**: Enter the financial advisor's company name.
* **Financial Investment Advisor Phone (12)**: Enter the financial advisor's phone number.
* **Financial Investment Advisor Email (13)**: Enter the financial advisor's email address.
* **Financial Investment Advisor License Number (14)**: Enter the financial advisor's license number.
* **Financial Investment Advisor CFO (15)**: Enter the financial advisor's Chief Financial Officer (CFO) name.
* **Financial Investment Advisor Title (16)**: Enter the financial advisor's title.
* **Termination Date (17)**: Enter the date of termination of the financial advisor.
* **Termination Reason (18)**: Enter the reason for termination of the financial advisor.
* **Termination Reason Code (19)**: Select the relevant reason code for termination.
* **Discharged By (20)**: Enter the person responsible for discharging the financial advisor.
* **Discharge Reason (21)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for discharge.
* **Discharge Date (22)**: Enter the date of discharge.
* **Termination Date (23)**: Enter the date of termination.
* **Termination Reason (24)**: Enter the reason for termination.
* **Termination Reason Code (25)**: Select the relevant reason code for termination.

Note: The fields with "required" set to `false` may not be necessary for every situation, but they should still be filled out if the relevant information is available.
