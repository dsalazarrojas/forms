# Debt Review Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debt Review Cancellation Form is used to collect information from users about a debt review cancellation, which is a process that allows individuals to cancel or dispute a review or inquiry about their debt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Information** in the fields provided. This is where you can add your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide the **Reason for Cancellation**. Explain why you are cancelling the debt review.
3. Enter the **Cancellation Date**. This is the date when the debt review cancellation takes effect.
4. If applicable, describe the **Dispute Resolution**. This is where you can provide details about any dispute resolution that has occurred.
5. Sign the **User Signature** field. This is a required field that confirms your identity.
6. If applicable, enter the **Agency Name** where the debt review was conducted.
7. If you encountered any errors during the review enrollment process, describe them in the **Review Enrollment Error** field.
8. If applicable, enter the **Dispute Resolution Agency** where the dispute was resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your basic contact information and any other details relevant to the debt review cancellation.
  * Please enter your name, phone number, and email address.
  * You can also add any other relevant details about your account or the debt review.
- **Reason for Cancellation** (`review_cancellation_reason`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide the reason why you are cancelling the debt review.
  * Be as specific as possible about the reason for cancellation.
  * You can also attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your reason.
- **Cancellation Date** (`review_cancellation_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the date when the debt review cancellation takes effect.
  * Enter the date in a format that is easy to read (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Dispute Resolution** (`dispute_resolution`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe any dispute resolution that has occurred.
  * If there was a dispute resolution, provide details about what happened.
  * You can also attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your dispute resolution.
- **User Signature** (`user_signature`, note, required/optional): This field is for you to sign and date your identity.
  * Your signature confirms that you are the person requesting the debt review cancellation.
  * Make sure to sign your name legibly.
- **Agency Cancellation** (`agency_cancellation`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to indicate if the debt review was conducted by an agency.
  * If the debt review was conducted by an agency, enter the agency's name.
  * You can also provide any relevant details about the agency's involvement.
- **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the agency where the debt review was conducted.
  * If the debt review was conducted by an agency, enter their name here.
  * You can also provide any other relevant details about the agency.
- **Review Enrollment Error** (`review_enrollment_error`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to report any errors that occurred during the review enrollment process.
  * If you encountered any issues during the enrollment process, describe them in detail.
  * Provide any relevant screenshots or evidence to support your report.
- **Dispute Resolution Agency** (`dispute_resolution_agency`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the agency where the dispute was resolved.
  * If there was a dispute resolution, enter the agency's name here.
  * You can also provide any relevant details about the agency's involvement.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your reason for cancellation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your financial advisor or the agency that conducted the debt review.
