# Financial Optimization Program Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and program interests from individuals who are seeking financial optimization services. The form will be used by our financial advisors to follow up with potential clients, discuss their needs, and provide personalized guidance to achieve their financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, which is a required field.
2. If you have a Last Name, please enter it in the space provided. Note that this field is not required.
3. Enter your Email address, if you have one. This field is not required.
4. Provide your Phone number, which is a required field.
5. Select the financial program that interests you the most from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
6. In the Questions or comments field, please enter any questions or comments you have about our financial optimization services. This field is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): If you would like to include your last name for further contact, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, optional): If you have a valid email address, please enter it here. This will help us keep in touch with you about your financial interests.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your financial needs.
* **What financial program are you interested in?** (`program_interest`, select_one, required): Select the financial program that best describes your interests: 
   + **Retirement**: Planning for a secure future
   + **College Savings**: Saving for your children's education
   + **Investment**: Growing your wealth over time
   + **Tax Planning**: Minimizing tax liability while optimizing investments
   + **Estate Planning**: Planning for the distribution of your assets after your retirement

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your correct contact information so that we can reach out to you in a timely manner.
* If you have any questions or concerns about our financial services, please enter them in the Questions or comments field.
* If you have any difficulty completing this form, please don't hesitate to contact our office for assistance.
