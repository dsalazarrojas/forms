# Financial Advisor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisor Job Application Form is designed to help individuals apply for a financial advisor position. It is intended to collect relevant information about the applicant, including contact details, work experience, education background, financial skills, and other relevant qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Info section with your basic contact information and demographic details.
2. In the Contact Info section, enter your email address and phone number.
3. In the Work Experience section, provide details about your previous work experience, including job title, job type, start and end dates, and company name.
4. In the Financial Skills section, select the financial skills you possess from the available options.
5. Upload your resume in the Resume section.
6. Link to your online portfolio in the Portfolio Link section.
7. In the Personal Statement section, provide a note explaining your qualifications and experience.
8. In the References section, select references from the predefined options.
9. In the Certifications section, select certifications you hold.
10. In the Submit section, review and submit your application.
11. If required, fill out the email, phone, address, birthdate, birth time, job title, work start date, work end date, company, position, department, job type, salary, and job status fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Info**: Enter your name, address, and other demographic details.
- **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number.
- **Work Experience**: Provide details about your previous work experience, including job title, job type, start and end dates, and company name.
- **Financial Skills**: Select the financial skills you possess from the available options.
- **Resume**: Upload your resume.
- **Portfolio Link**: Link to your online portfolio.
- **Personal Statement**: Write a note explaining your qualifications and experience.
- **References**: Select references from the predefined options.
- **Certifications**: Select certifications you hold.
- **Submit**: Review and submit your application.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Address**: Enter your address.
- **Birthdate**: Enter your birthdate.
- **Birth Time**: Enter your birth time.
- **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
- **Work Start Date**: Enter your current work start date.
- **Work End Date**: Enter your current work end date.
- **Company**: Enter your current company name.
- **Position**: Enter your current position title.
- **Department**: Enter your current department.
- **Job Type**: Enter your current job type.
- **Salary**: Enter your current salary.
- **Job Status**: Select your current job status.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application.
- Review your application before submitting it to ensure it is accurate and complete.
- If you have any doubts or need assistance, please contact our support team.
