# Financial Coaching Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive application for financial coaching services. It helps us understand your needs and goals, ensuring we provide personalized guidance.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your basic contact information, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your work experience, financial education, and employment status.
3. Specify your financial background, financial objectives, and challenges.
4. Choose your preferred coaching format and provide any additional information about your financial goals and objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name.
* **Email** (email): Your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number.
* **Address** (text): Your home or office address.
* **What is your work experience level?** (select_one: High, Low): This question helps us understand your current level of work experience.
* **Financial Education** (select_multiple: Yes, No): Indicate whether you have any formal financial education or training.
* **Financial Certifications** (select_multiple: Yes, No): Check if you have any professional financial certifications.
* **Employment Status** (select_one: Active, Inactive): Specify your current employment status.
* **Employment History** (text): A brief description of your employment history.
* **Reason for Seeking Coaching** (text): Explain why you are seeking financial coaching services.
* **Preferred Coaching Format** (select_one: Yes, No): Choose how you would like to receive coaching (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Financial Goals** (text): Describe your primary financial goals.
* **Financial Objectives** (text): List your financial objectives (short-term and long-term).
* **Financial Objectives Details** (text): Provide more details about your financial objectives.
* **Financial Challenges** (text): Describe any significant financial challenges you are facing.
* **Financial Background** (text): Share your financial background and experience.
* **Financial References** (text): Provide any relevant financial references or credentials.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers to help us provide the best guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We value your time and appreciate your participation in the financial coaching process.
