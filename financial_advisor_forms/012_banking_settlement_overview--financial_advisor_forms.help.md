# Banking Settlement Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banking Settlement Overview form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of banking settlements, capturing key details to facilitate effective communication and record-keeping between financial advisors and clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Settlement Date**: Enter the date of the settlement report. This date should accurately reflect the timing of the settlement.
2. **Financial Advisor Name**: Enter the name of the handling financial advisor responsible for this settlement.
3. **Advisor ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the financial advisor.
4. **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the client involved in the settlement.
5. **Client Account Number**: Enter the primary account identifier associated with the client.
6. **Type of Settlement**: Select the category of the settlement from the provided options (Fund Transfer, Investment Settlement, Dividend Distribution, Interest Payment, Fee Collection, Loan Disbursement, or Other).
7. **Settlement Amount**: Enter the total amount being settled in USD.
8. **Currency**: Select the currency of the settlement from the options (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, or CHF). If not applicable, leave this field blank.
9. **Settlement Reference Number**: Enter the unique transaction reference number.
10. **Initiating Bank**: Enter the bank initiating the settlement.
11. **Receiving Bank**: Enter the bank receiving the settlement.
12. **Settlement Method**: Select the method of settlement from the options (Wire Transfer, ACH Transfer, Check, Automated Clearing House, International Wire, or SWIFT Transfer).
13. **Value Date**: Enter the date funds are available.
14. **Settlement Status**: Select the current status of the settlement from the options (Pending, In Progress, Settled, Completed, Failed, or On Hold).
15. **Any Outstanding Items?**: If there are any outstanding items or issues, enter a description in this field.
16. **Fees Charged**: Enter the settlement-related fees, if applicable.
17. **Net Settlement Amount**: Enter the amount after fees, if applicable.
18. **Reconciliation Status**: Select the reconciliation status from the options (Fully Reconciled, Partially Reconciled, Not Reconciled, or Pending Review).
19. **Are There Any Exceptions?**: Select 'Yes' if there are any exceptions or issues, and describe the exceptions in the "Exception Details" field.
20. **Advisor Notes**: Enter any additional observations or comments from the financial advisor.
21. **Follow-Up Required?**: Select 'Yes' if follow-up is required and enter the scheduled follow-up date.
22. **Scheduled Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date follow-up should occur, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Settlement Date**: Date of the settlement report.
* **Financial Advisor Name**: Name of the handling financial advisor.
* **Advisor ID**: Unique advisor identifier.
* **Client Name**: Full name of the client.
* **Client Account Number**: Primary account identifier.

* **Settlement Amount**: Total amount being settled in USD.
* **Currency**: Currency of the settlement (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, or CHF).
* **Settlement Reference Number**: Unique transaction reference number.
* **Initiating Bank**: Bank initiating the settlement.
* **Receiving Bank**: Bank receiving the settlement.
* **Settlement Method**: Method of settlement (Wire Transfer, ACH Transfer, Check, Automated Clearing House, International Wire, or SWIFT Transfer).
* **Value Date**: Date funds are available.
* **Settlement Status**: Current status of the settlement (Pending, In Progress, Settled, Completed, Failed, or On Hold).
* **Any Outstanding Items?**: Description of outstanding items or issues.
* **Fees Charged**: Settlement-related fees.
* **Net Settlement Amount**: Amount after fees.
* **Reconciliation Status**: Reconciliation status (Fully Reconciled, Partially Reconciled, Not Reconciled, or Pending Review).
* **Are There Any Exceptions?**: Indicate if there are any exceptions or issues.
* **Exception Details**: Description of the exceptions or issues.

* **Follow-Up Required?**: Indicate if follow-up is required.
