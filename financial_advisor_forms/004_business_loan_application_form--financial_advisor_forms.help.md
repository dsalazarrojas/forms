# Business Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a business loan application form designed to collect information about your personal and business details to assess your eligibility for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Use a valid email address and phone number.
4. Provide a clear and concise description of your business and project.
5. Be honest and accurate when answering all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your birthdate in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Email Address** (`personal_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone Number** (`personal_phone`, `text`, required): Enter a working phone number where we can reach you.
* **Residential Address** (`residential_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current home address.
* **Years at Current Address** (`years_at_address`, `number`, optional): If you have lived at your current address for more than a year, please enter the number of years.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, `select_one`, required): Select the structure of your business (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, S-Corporation, Other).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): Describe your business and what it does.
* **Project/Loan Purpose** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Describe the project or purpose for which you are applying for a loan.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you expect the project to be completed.
* **Loan Amount Requested** (`loan_amount_requested`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of money you are applying for.
* **Annual Business Revenue** (`annual_business_revenue`, `number`, required): Enter your business's annual revenue from the previous year.
* **Monthly Business Expenses** (`monthly_business_expenses`, `number`, required): Enter your business's average monthly operating costs.
* **Estimated Personal Credit Score** (`personal_credit_score`, `select_one`, required): Select your estimated FICO score range (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unknown).
* **Personal Monthly Income** (`personal_monthly_income`, `number`, required): Enter your personal monthly income from all sources.
* **Total Personal Debt** (`personal_debts`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount of debt you have.
* **Preferred Loan Term** (`preferred_loan_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of months you prefer to repay the loan.
* **Do you have Collateral to Offer?** (`collateral_available`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have any assets you can pledge as collateral (True, False, Unsure).
* **Description of Collateral** (`collateral_description`, `text`, optional): If you have collateral, describe the assets you can offer.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information that might be relevant to your loan application.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any question, contact us for clarification.
* You can use the hints provided with each field for guidance.
