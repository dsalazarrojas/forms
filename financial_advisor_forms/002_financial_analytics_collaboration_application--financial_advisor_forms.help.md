# financial_analytics_collaboration_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_analytics_collaboration_application form is designed to facilitate networking and knowledge sharing among financial professionals. It allows users to identify their areas of expertise and request collaboration from relevant experts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant collaboration requests: Choose the areas where you need collaboration from team members, project leaders, or domain experts.
2. Evaluate the expertise: Choose the areas where you need expertise from financial analysts, data scientists, or IT engineers.
3. Share project ideas: If you have a project idea, share it in the text field.
4. Submit the form: Once you have completed the required fields, submit the form to request collaboration.
