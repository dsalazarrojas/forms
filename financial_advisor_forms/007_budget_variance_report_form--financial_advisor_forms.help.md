# Budget Variance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Budget Variance Report Form is used to report on the variance between the original budgeted amount and the actual amount spent for a specific department and reporting period. This form helps in monitoring and tracking deviations from the budgeted amounts and taking corrective actions to address them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to provide a detailed explanation for significant variances.
3. Select all the primary causes that apply for the variance.
4. Choose the variance direction and threshold status.
5. Fill in the corrective actions planned to address unfavorable variances.
6. Assess the impact of the variance on future periods.
7. Provide recommendations for management.
8. Attach supporting documents if necessary.
9. Get your manager to review and approve the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Preparer Name** (`report_preparer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person preparing the report.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department or division being reported on.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the report preparer.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the report is submitted.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, select_one, required): Select the period covered by this report.
*   - Monthly
*   - Quarterly
*   - Semi-Annual
*   - Annual
*   - Project-Based
* **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`, text, required): Enter the applicable fiscal year.
* **Budget Category** (`budget_category`, select_one, required): Select the category being reported on.
*   - Personnel Costs
*   - Operating Expenses
*   - Capital Expenditures
*   - Marketing Budget
*   - R and D Budget
*   - Administrative Costs
*   - Total Budget
* **Budgeted Amount** (`budgeted_amount`, number, required): Enter the original budgeted amount.
* **Actual Amount Spent** (`actual_amount`, number, required): Enter the actual amount spent.
* **Variance Amount** (`variance_amount`, number, required): Calculate the difference between the budgeted and actual amounts.
* **Variance Percentage** (`variance_percentage`, number, required): Calculate the variance as a percentage of the budget.
*   - Favorable - Under Budget
*   - Unfavorable - Over Budget
*   - On Budget
*   - Not Applicable
* **Variance Direction** (`variance_direction`, select_one, required): Select the direction of the variance.
* **Variance Threshold Status** (`variance_threshold`, select_one, required): Select whether the variance is within an acceptable range or exceeds a threshold.
*   - Within Acceptable Range
*   - Exceeds Threshold - Requires Explanation
*   - Significant Variance - Action Required
* **Primary Causes of Variance** (`primary_causes`, select_multiple, required): Select all the primary causes that apply for the variance.
*   - Volume Changes
*   - Price Changes
*   - Timing Differences
*   - One-time Events
*   - Forecasting Errors
*   - Policy Changes
*   - Market Conditions
*   - Operational Issues
*   - Other
* **Detailed Variance Explanation** (`variance_explanation`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation for significant variances.
* **Corrective Actions Planned** (`corrective_actions`, text, optional): Enter the actions planned to address unfavorable variances.
* **Impact Assessment** (`impact_assessment`, select_one, required): Assess the expected impact on future periods.
*   - No Future Impact Expected
*   - Minor Impact Expected
*   - Moderate Impact Expected
*   - Significant Impact Expected
*   - Impact Uncertain
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Enter recommendations for management.
* **Supporting Documents Attached** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): Select whether supporting documents are attached.
*   - True
*   - False
*   - To Follow
* **Manager Review Status** (`manager_review`, select_one, required): Select whether the manager has reviewed the report.
*   - Reviewed and Approved
*   - Under Review
*   - Pending Review

## Tips

* Make sure to submit accurate and complete information.
* Attach supporting documents whenever possible.
* Get your manager to review and approve your report.
* Follow up on corrective actions and track progress.
* Regularly review and update your reports to reflect changes in the budget and actual expenditures.
