# Withdrawal Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Withdrawal Authorization Form is used by financial advisors to authorize the withdrawal of funds from a student's account when the student is unable to do so themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Parent Name, Relationship to Student, and Contact Information fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the Grade Level of the student from the provided options.
3. Select the School Year of the student from the list (if applicable).
4. Enter the Reason For Withdrawal, explaining the circumstances under which the withdrawal is being authorized.
5. Optionally, add any additional Comments related to the withdrawal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is authorizing the withdrawal.
* **Relationship To Student**: Describe the relationship between the parent/guardian and the student (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the parent/guardian, including any relevant phone number, email, or address.
* **Signature**: This field is for the signature of the parent/guardian, indicating their authorization of the withdrawal.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student whose account is being accessed.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student ID number associated with the student's account.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the student from the options provided (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12, or 9-12 for high school students).
* **School Year**: If applicable, select the school year of the student from the list (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8).
* **Reason For Withdrawal**: Explain the reason for the withdrawal, including any relevant circumstances or details.
* **Comments**: Optionally, add any additional comments or information related to the withdrawal.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential for the form to be considered complete and valid. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
