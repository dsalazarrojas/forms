# Bank Rating Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Rating Form is a tool used by the bank to gather customer feedback on their overall banking experience. By filling out this form, customers will be able to rate and provide feedback on various aspects of their account, from overall satisfaction to specific areas such as digital banking and customer support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your **Customer Full Name** and **Email Address**.
2.  Select the type of account you have with the bank (**Account Type**).
3.  Indicate how long you have been with the bank (**Customer Since**).
4.  Rate the bank's overall performance (**Overall Bank Rating**).
5.  Select how much you trust the bank (**Trust Rating**).
6.  Rate the value you receive from the bank (**Value Rating**).
7.  Rate the convenience of the bank's services (**Convenience Rating**).
8.  Rate the bank's digital banking services (**Digital Banking Rating**).
9.  Rate the bank's customer support (**Customer Support Rating**).
10.  Rate the bank's physical branches (**Branch Rating**).
11.  Rate the bank's ATM network (**ATM Rating**).
12.  Rate the bank's fees (**Fees Rating**).
13.  Rate the bank's interest rates (**Interest Rates Rating**).
14.  Rate the variety of products offered by the bank (**Product Variety Rating**).
15.  Rate the bank's security measures (**Security Rating**).
16.  Indicate whether you would recommend the bank to others (**Recommendation Rating**).
17.  Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have (**Additional Comments**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Full Name** (Text): Your name as it appears on your account.
    *   **Your name**.
*   **Email Address** (Email): Your email address for follow-up communications.
    *   For follow-up.
*   **Account Type** (Select One): Select the type of account you have with the bank.
    *   What type of account do you have (Checking, Savings, Both, Business, Investment, Loan).
*   **Customer Since** (Select One): Indicate how long you have been with the bank.
    *   How long have you been with us (Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, Over 5 years).
*   **Overall Bank Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's overall performance.
    *   Rate our bank overall (5 Stars - Excellent, 4 Stars - Very Good, 3 Stars - Good, 2 Stars - Fair, 1 Star - Poor).
*   **Trust Rating** (Select One): Indicate how much you trust the bank.
    *   How much do you trust us (Completely Trust, Mostly Trust, Somewhat Trust, Slightly Trust, Do Not Trust).
*   **Value Rating** (Select One): Rate the value you receive from the bank.
    *   Rate the value you receive (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
*   **Convenience Rating** (Select One): Rate the convenience of the bank's services.
    *   Rate convenience (Very Convenient, Convenient, Neutral, Inconvenient, Very Inconvenient).
*   **Digital Banking Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's digital banking services.
    *   Rate online and mobile banking (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Used).
*   **Customer Support Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's customer support.
    *   Rate our support (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Branch Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's physical branches.
    *   Rate physical branches (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Never Visited).
*   **ATM Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's ATM network.
    *   Rate ATM network (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Fees Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's fees.
    *   Rate fees (Very Competitive, Competitive, Average, High, Very High).
*   **Interest Rates Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's interest rates.
    *   Rate our rates (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Product Variety Rating** (Select One): Rate the variety of products offered by the bank.
    *   Rate product options (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Security Rating** (Select One): Rate the bank's security measures.
    *   Rate security (Very Secure, Secure, Neutral, Insecure, Very Insecure).
*   **Recommendation Rating** (Select One): Indicate whether you would recommend the bank to others.
    *   Would you recommend us (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, Definitely No).
*   **Bank Strengths** (Text): Identify what the bank does well.
    *   What do we do well.
*   **Bank Weaknesses** (Text): Suggest areas the bank can improve.
    *   What should we improve.
*   **Additional Comments** (Text): Provide any other feedback or comments you may have.
    *   Any other feedback.
## Tips

*   Please fill out the form once per account.
*   Be honest and sincere in your responses.
*   Your feedback is invaluable to us.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
