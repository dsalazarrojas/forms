# Personal Wealth Advisory Recommendations - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about your financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences to provide tailored wealth advisory recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions below to help us understand your financial goals and priorities.
2. You can select one or multiple options for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your main financial goals for the next 12 months?** (`client_goals`, text, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand what you want to achieve in the next 12 months.
* **How would you rate your overall risk tolerance level (1-6)?** (`risk_tolerance`, select_one, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand how comfortable you are with taking risks in your investments.
* **What is your investment horizon (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually)?** (`investment_horizon`, select_one, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand how often you plan to make investments.
* **Which type of investments do you prefer (e.g., Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Alternative Investments)?** (`investment_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand what types of investments you are interested in.
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns about your financial goals or investments?** (`additional_comments`, note, optional)
	+ This is an open field for you to share any thoughts or concerns you may have.
* **What is your risk tolerance level for each type of investment (e.g., Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, Alternative Investments)?** (`risk_tolerance_2`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand how you feel about taking risks in different types of investments.
* **How frequently do you plan to make investments (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually)?** (`investment_frequency`, select_one, optional)
	+ This question helps us understand how often you plan to invest.
