# Margin Trading Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place a margin trading order, which enables users to trade financial instruments with borrowed money. Users can input their details, provide order instructions, select a trading pair, and set margin amounts, leverage, stop loss, take profit, risk management strategy, and confirm their order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the **User Details** field.
2. Provide any additional instructions for your order in the **Order Instructions** field, if necessary.
3. Select the **Trading Pair** you wish to trade from the available options (e.g., BTC/USD, ETH/USD, XRP/USD).
4. Enter the **Margin Amount** you want to trade with.
5. Set your desired **Leverage** level.
6. Define your **Stop Loss** level.
7. Set your **Take Profit** level.
8. Select your **Risk Management** strategy (Aggressive, Medium, or Conservative).
9. Click **Submit** to confirm your order.
10. Review and confirm your order in the **Confirm Order** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (Text): Please fill in your basic contact information.
* **Order Instructions** (Text): Provide any additional notes or instructions for your order (max 1000 characters).
* **Trading Pair** (Select One):
	+ BTC/USD: Trade Bitcoin against the US Dollar.
	+ ETH/USD: Trade Ethereum against the US Dollar.
	+ XRP/USD: Trade Ripple against the US Dollar.
* **Margin Amount** (Number): Enter the amount you want to trade with.
* **Leverage** (Number): Set the level of leverage you want to use (e.g., 1:100, 1:200).
* **Stop Loss** (Number): Set the stop-loss level to limit your potential losses.
* **Take Profit** (Number): Set the take-profit level to limit your potential gains.
* **Risk Management** (Select One):
	+ Aggressive: Take on more risk for potentially higher gains.
	+ Medium: Balance risk and potential gains.
	+ Conservative: Limit risk for potentially lower gains.
* **Submit** (Text): Confirm your order by clicking this button.
* **Confirm Order** (Text): Review and confirm your order before submitting it.
* **Cancel** (Text): Cancel your order, if you wish.
