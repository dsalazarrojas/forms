# Financial Growth Acceleration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Growth Acceleration Application Form is designed to collect information from business owners or financial advisors who want to accelerate the growth of their financial institutions.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* financial_growth_acceleration_application
	* growth_goals
	* financial_details
	* business_goals
	* financial_institution
	* submission_date
	* submission_time
	* submitted_by
	* contact_email
2. Fill out the optional fields:
	* financial_instruments
	* growth_objectives
	* business_details
	* growth_indicators
	* contact_person
	* financial_institution_description
	* growth_indicators_description
	* business_objectives_description
	* notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Growth Acceleration Application Form** (`financial_growth_acceleration_application`, text, required)
	* Enter a brief description of your financial growth acceleration application.
* **Growth Goals** (`growth_goals`, text, required)
	* Describe your short-term and long-term financial growth goals.
* **Financial Details** (`financial_details`, text, required)
	* Provide detailed information about your financial situation.
* **Business Goals** (`business_goals`, select_one, required)
	* Select 'Yes' if you want to accelerate your business growth.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, required)
	* Select 'Yes' if you are a financial institution.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required)
	* Enter the date you are submitting this application in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required)
	* Enter the time you are submitting this application in the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required)
	* Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are submitting this application.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
	* Enter your contact email address.

Please note that the format for submission date and time should be in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" and "HH:MM:SS" respectively. 

Also, please ensure that you are providing accurate and honest information in all the fields. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
