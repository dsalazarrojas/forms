# Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funding Request Form is used to collect and process funding requests from users. This form is completed by users to provide their information and details about the funding they are requesting, which is then reviewed and processed by administrators or decision-makers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to include all required fields (if any).
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure that it is a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide a brief description of your project or initiative in the "Description" field.
7. Enter the amount of funding you are requesting in the "Funding amount" field. Make sure to enter a valid number.
8. Select the type of funding you are requesting from the list provided in the "Funding type" field.
9. Provide a brief reason for your funding request in the "Funding reason" field.
10. Select the current status of your funding request from the list provided in the "Funding status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name.
* **Email** (email): Your email address. Ensure it is valid.
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number.
* **Description** (text): A brief description of your project or initiative.
* **Funding Amount** (number): The amount of funding you are requesting. Enter a valid number.
* **Funding Type** (select_one): The type of funding you are requesting from the list provided.
*   Example: Option 1, Option 2.
* **Funding Reason** (text): The reason for your funding request.
* **Funding Status** (select_multiple): The current status of your funding request from the list provided.
*   Example: In progress, Completed.
