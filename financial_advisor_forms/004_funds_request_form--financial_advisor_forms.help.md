# Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funds Request Form is used to request financial support for a specific purpose or project from the institution. This form helps to gather necessary information to evaluate and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the purpose and guidelines for completing the form.
2. Provide the institution's information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Describe your financial needs and requirements.
4. Specify the sources of funding for your project (e.g., grants, loans, etc.).
5. Enter the total amount of funds requested.
6. Explain the potential financial impact of the funding on your project or institution.
7. Indicate whether you are open to receiving additional funds for other purposes or have any other financial information that needs to be considered.
8. Enter your contact information, including email address, so that we can get back to you with updates and questions.
9. Review all the information you have entered and make sure it is complete and accurate.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Institution Information** (`institution_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the institution you are representing.
- **Financial Needs** (`financial_needs`, text, optional): Describe the financial needs and requirements of your project or institution.
- **Sources of Funding** (`funding_sources`, text, optional): List the sources of funding for your project (e.g., grants, loans, etc.).
- **Total Funds Requested** (`fund_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount of funds requested.
- **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, text, optional): Explain the potential financial impact of the funding on your project or institution.
- **Open to Additional Funds** (`fund_use`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are open to receiving additional funds for other purposes.
- **Additional Financial Information** (`additional_funds_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional financial information that needs to be considered.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with updates and questions.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to send your form.
