# Financial Budgeting Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a client's financial situation for the purpose of providing financial budgeting guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select all applicable income sources and frequencies from the respective fields.
2. Choose the type of financial institution(s) you have accounts with.
3. Select the type of expenses you have (e.g., credit card, rent, loans).
4. Indicate your employment status.
5. Enter your monthly income, expenses, debt total, and emergency fund amounts.
6. Choose your short-term, long-term, or debt payoff financial goal.
7. Enter any additional financial information or goals you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income** (`income`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the ways you earn income (e.g., salary, investment, other).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current employment status (e.g., employed, unemployed, student).
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your monthly income amount.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`monthly_expenses`, `number`, required: false): Enter your monthly expenses amount.
* **Debt Total** (`debt_total`, `number`, required: false): Enter your total debt amount.
* **Debt Goal** (`debt_goal`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your financial goal for paying off debt (e.g., short-term, long-term, pay off entire debt).
* **Emergency Fund** (`emergency_fund`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of money you have set aside for emergencies.
* **Financial Goal** (`financial_goal`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional financial goals or objectives.
* **Income Expenses** (`income_expenses`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all types of income-related expenses (e.g., credit card, rent, loans).
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all types of financial institutions you have accounts with (e.g., bank, credit union, non-profit credit union).
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose where your income comes from (e.g., salary, investment, other).
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you receive income (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
