# Financial Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect comprehensive financial information about a company, including its financial health, compliance status, and contact details. It is intended for auditing and financial analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form section by section, starting from "Financial Health" on Page 1.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Financial Compliance".
3. Enter the company's financial data collection information in the "Financial Data Collection" field.
4. Select a date for "Financial Data Analysis Date" and "Financial Audit Date".
5. Enter the company's contact person's name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Person", "Contact Person Email", and "Contact Person Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the company's name, address, location, and financial institution in the "Company Name", "Company Address", "Company Location", and "Financial Institution" fields, respectively.
7. Select the company's status and financial audit result.
8. Enter the name of the financial advisor if applicable.
9. Finally, review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Financial Health** (`financial_audit_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the company's financial health.
- **Financial Compliance** (`financial_audit_form_2`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the company is in financial compliance, "No" otherwise.
- **Financial Data Collection** (`financial_audit_form_3`, text, optional): Enter the company's financial data collection process and procedures.
- **Financial Analysis** (`financial_audit_form_4`, date, optional): Enter the date of the financial analysis.
- **Auditing Process** (`financial_audit_form_5`, text, optional): Describe the auditing process used by the company.
- **Contact Person** (`financial_audit_form_6`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company's contact person.
- **Contact Person Email** (`financial_audit_form_7`, email, required): Enter the company's contact person's email address.
- **Contact Person Phone** (`financial_audit_form_8`, text, optional): Enter the company's contact person's phone number.
- **Company Name** (`financial_audit_form_9`, text, required): Enter the company's name.
- **Company Address** (`financial_audit_form_10`, text, required): Enter the company's address.
- **Company Location** (`financial_audit_form_11`, text, required): Enter the company's location.
- **Financial Institution** (`financial_audit_form_12`, text, required): Enter the financial institution the company operates with.
- **Financial Data Analysis Date** (`financial_audit_form_13`, date, required): Enter the date of the financial data analysis.
- **Financial Audit Date** (`financial_audit_form_14`, date, required): Enter the date of the financial audit.
- **Non-Compliance Issues** (`financial_audit_form_15`, text, optional): List any non-compliance issues found during the audit.
- **Company Status** (`financial_audit_form_16`, text, required): Select the current company status.
- **Financial Audit Result** (`financial_audit_form_17`, text, required): Enter the result of the financial audit.
- **Financial Advisor** (`financial_audit_form_18`, text, optional): Enter the name of the financial advisor if applicable.
- **Financial Audit Form 19** (`financial_audit_form_19`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the financial audit form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel and may require review and approval before submission. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
