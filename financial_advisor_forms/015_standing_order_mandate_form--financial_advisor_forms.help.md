# Standing Order Mandate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Standing Order Mandate Form is a document used to formalize a standing order, which is an arrangement between a client and their financial institution for recurring payments. This form is used to confirm the details of the standing order and ensure that both parties are in agreement on the terms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the account details on the **Account Details** page, including your account number or client code, bank account number, and any other relevant information.
2.  Enter the standing order details, including the amount, frequency, and any specific instructions for the payment.
3.  Review and confirm the details on the **Confirm** page before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Details** (`account_details`):
	+ This field is used to enter your account details, such as your account number or client code.
	+ Please enter the correct account information to ensure that the standing order is processed correctly.
* **Standing Order Details** (`standing_order_details`):
	+ This field is used to enter the details of the standing order, including the amount and frequency of the payment.
	+ Please enter the specific instructions for the payment, such as the type of payment (e.g., deposit, transfer, etc.).
* **Confirm** (`confirm`):
	+ This field is used to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the details of the standing order.
	+ Please review the form carefully before confirming to ensure that all details are correct.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have entered your account details accurately to avoid any issues with processing the standing order.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your financial institution for clarification.
* Before submitting the form, review all fields carefully to avoid any errors.
