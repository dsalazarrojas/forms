# Income Reduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income Reduction Form is used to collect information from users for some specific financial calculation. Please fill out this form to the best of your ability, ensuring that all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in the following order: 
  1. Enter your First Name.
  2. Enter your Last Name. This is a required field.
  3. Enter your Address. This is a required field.
  4. Enter your Email. This is a required field.
  5. Enter your Phone (optional).
  6. Enter your Date of Birth. This is a required field.
  7. Enter your Employment Start Date. This is a required field.
  8. Enter your Annual Income. This is a required field.
  9. Select the type of Mortgage Modification you are applying for (Mortgage Modification or Credit Reduction). This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it. If not, leave it blank.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, `date`, required): Please enter your employment start date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Annual Income** (`income`, `number`, required): Please enter your annual income.
* **Mortgage Modification Type** (`mortgage_modify_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of mortgage modification you are applying for.
