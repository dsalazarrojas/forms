# Income Certificate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals apply for an income certificate. It is intended for official or tax-related purposes. Please ensure that you are eligible for this specific type of certificate and that you have not already submitted an application for the same purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details:
   * **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
   * **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
   * **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
   * **Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.

2. Enter employment details:
   * **Employer**: Provide the name of your current or previous employer.
   * **Employee Type**: Select whether you are an employee or self-employed.
   * **Employee Number**: Enter your employee identification number (if applicable).
   * **Income Level**: Enter your current income level.

3. Select your certificate type:
   * **Certificate Type**: Select the type of income certificate you are applying for (Form A or Form B).

4. Date and certification status:
   * **Certificate Date**: Enter the date you would like the certificate to be issued for.
   * **Certification Status**: Select the status of your certification (Pending or Certified).
