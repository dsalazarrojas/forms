# KYC Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The KYC Form is designed for customer verification, ensuring that our financial institution meets the necessary anti-money laundering and anti-terrorism financing regulations. This form is used to collect essential information about our customers, helping us assess risks and prevent illicit activities.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Customer Information

Please enter your customer's information accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the customer's date of birth in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the customer's address as it appears on their identification documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, number, required): Enter the customer's account number (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in.
- If you're unsure about any information, please contact our support team for assistance.
