# Debit Card Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debit Card Application Form is used to collect information from users for a debit card application. It is a crucial document that helps financial institutions verify and update user information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your personal details.
2. In the Card Info section, enter your debit card details.
3. Select your Card Holder title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., or Dr.).
4. If you have an account with us, enter your Account Balance.
5. Select the Account Type (6 months or more, 2-6 months, or Less than 2 months).
6. Choose your Account Status (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
7. If you have any account type details, enter them in the Account Type Details section.
8. Enter your User Address.
9. Enter your User Address (2).
10. Enter your User Address (3).
11. In the Date field, enter the date of your birth or application date.
12. In the Time field, enter the time of your application.
13. In the Card Number field, enter your debit card number.
14. In the Card Expiration field, enter the expiration date of your debit card.
15. Enter your Card Expiration Date.
16. In the Card CVV field, enter the Card Verification Value of your debit card.
17. Enter your User Name.
18. Enter your User Phone number.
19. Enter your User Email.
20. Enter any additional User Notes.
21. Finally, attach your User Signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: true): Enter your personal details, including your name and address.
* **Card Info** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: true): Enter your debit card details, including the card number and expiration date.
* **Card Holder** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Select your title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., or Dr.).
* **Account Balance** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, required: false): If you have an account with us, enter your current account balance.
* **Account Type** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Select your account type (6 months or more, 2-6 months, or Less than 2 months).
* **Account Status** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Choose your account status (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
* **Account Type Details** (`id: 7`, `type: note`, required: false): If you have any account type details, enter them here.
* **User Address** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your address details.
* **User-Address-2-Form** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **User-Address-3-Form** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any more address information.
* **User Address** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your address (this field is duplicate, please use User Address).
* **Date** (`id: 13`, `type: date`, required: false): In the Date field, enter the date of your birth or application date.
* **Time** (`id: 14`, `type: time`, required: false): In the Time field, enter the time of your application.
* **Card Number** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your debit card number.
* **Card Expiration** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the expiration date of your debit card.
* **Card Expiration Date** (`id: 17`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter your Card Expiration Date.
* **Card CVV** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the Card Verification Value of your debit card.
* **User Name** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **User Phone** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **User Phone-2-Form** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional phone information.
* **User Email** (`id: 22`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

* **User Note** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
