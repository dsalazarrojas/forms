# Financial Planning Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Planning Consultation Intake Form is a collection of questions designed to help our financial advisors understand your financial planning needs and goals. This form is intended to gather relevant information to provide personalized advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
3. You can skip fields that are not applicable to your situation.
4. If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please reach out to our office for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, Text, Optional): This field is where you can provide your contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, Text, Optional): This field is where you can describe your current financial situation, including any relevant financial events or milestones.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Long-term investing: This goal is for those interested in growing their wealth over an extended period.
	+ Short-term savings: This goal is for those looking to save for a specific purpose, such as a down payment on a house or a emergency fund.
	+ Retirement planning: This goal is for those planning to retire or have a comfortable financial situation in their golden years.
	+ Tax planning: This goal is for those looking to minimize tax liabilities or optimize tax benefits.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Conservative: This option is for those who prioritize stability and security in their investments.
	+ Moderate: This option is for those who balance stability and growth in their investments.
	+ Aggressive: This option is for those who are willing to take on higher risks for potentially higher returns.
* **Financial Net Worth** (`financial_net_worth`, Number, Optional): This field is where you can report your current financial net worth. Please provide the exact amount.
* **Income Info** (`income_info`, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Wages: This option is for those with a steady income from an employer or business.
	+ Investments: This option is for those with income from investments, such as dividends or interest.
	+ Rentals: This option is for those with income from renting properties or other rental properties.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, Select One, Optional):
	+ Full-time employed: This option is for those with a traditional 9-to-5 employment arrangement.
	+ Part-time employed: This option is for those with a reduced work schedule.
	+ Self-employed: This option is for those who own their own business or have a variable income.
	+ Unemployed: This option is for those currently not working.
	+ Student: This option is for those pursuing higher education or other learning endeavors.
	+ Retired: This option is for those who have retired or have chosen not to work.
