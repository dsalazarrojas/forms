# Financial Seminar Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Seminar Referral Form is a tool used to gather and store information about financial seminars, their attendees, and their status. This form helps to ensure accurate and complete tracking of seminar referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Seminar Details section with the required information about the seminar, including date, time, address, and type.
2. Provide the attendee's name and email address in the Attendee Information section.
3. Enter the attendee's phone number in the Phone field.
4. Choose the follow-up status for the seminar.
5. Choose the follow-up method used.
6. Enter any additional follow-up details, if applicable.
7. Provide any follow-up notes, if needed.
8. Confirm the seminar status and invitation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Details (id: 1)**: Enter the seminar's date, time, address, and type.
* **Attendee Name (id: 2)**: Enter the attendee's name.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Seminar Date (id: 5)**: Enter the seminar's date.
* **Seminar Time (id: 6)**: Enter the seminar's time.
* **Seminar Address (id: 7)**: Enter the seminar's address.
* **Attendee Email (id: 8)**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Attendee Phone (id: 9)**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Invitation Details (id: 10)**: Enter any invitation details, if applicable.
* **Notes (id: 11)**: Enter any additional seminar-related notes.
* **Follow-up Status (id: 14)**: Choose the follow-up status for the seminar.
* **Invitation Status (id: 15)**: Choose the invitation status.
* **Seminar Status (id: 14)**: Choose the seminar status.
* **Follow-up Date (id: 16)**: Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time (id: 17)**: Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-up (id: 18)**: Confirm if a follow-up is needed.
* **Follow-up Method (id: 19)**: Choose the follow-up method.
* **Follow-up Details (id: 20)**: Enter any follow-up details.
* **Follow-up Notes (id: 21)**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Seminar Type (id: 22)**: Choose if the seminar is active or inactive.
* **Follow-up Type (id: 23)**: Choose if the follow-up is active or inactive.
* **Follow-up Status (id: 24)**: Choose the follow-up status.
* **Seminar Location (id: 25)**: Enter the seminar's location.
