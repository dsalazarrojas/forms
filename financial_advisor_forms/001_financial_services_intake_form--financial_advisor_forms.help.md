# financial_services_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather financial information from clients, providing an opportunity for them to share their financial details with your organization. The form helps in creating a comprehensive understanding of a client's financial situation, enabling your team to better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: Provide basic details about yourself, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select the financial services you are interested in, such as Checking Account, Investment Account, or Annuity.
3. **Financial History**: Share any relevant financial history, including any previous accounts or experiences you'd like to mention.
4. **Account Information**: If you have existing accounts, please provide details about them, including account numbers or other relevant information.
5. **Contact Methods**: List your preferred contact methods, such as email, phone number, or address.
6. **Next Steps**: Briefly describe your next steps or any expectations you have for the services you've selected.
7. **Uploaded Documents**: If needed, upload scanned or photographed documents to support your information.
