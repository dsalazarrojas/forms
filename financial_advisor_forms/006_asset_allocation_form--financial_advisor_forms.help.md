The Asset Allocation Form is a financial planning instrument used to assess a client's investment strategy and risk tolerance. This form helps identify key financial metrics and goals, enabling advisors to create a customized investment portfolio that suits their needs. Before filling out this form, please confirm that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. Duplicate or outdated information may lead to incorrect investment recommendations and misaligned investment goals.

This guide will walk you through the asset allocation form, field by field, to ensure a smooth and accurate completion process.

## Purpose
The Asset Allocation Form is a comprehensive instrument used to gather essential information about a client's investment needs, preferences, and circumstances. It serves as the foundation for creating a tailored investment strategy that meets their unique requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing basic contact information: enter your first name, last name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2.  Provide your date of birth for age and life stage assessment purposes.
3.  Specify the investment time horizon, or the time frame in which you expect to use these funds.
4.  Enter your combined assets available for investment (total investable assets) and your gross annual income.
5.  Determine your capacity for annual savings and the level of risk you're willing to take.
6.  Choose your primary investment goals, which might include retirement planning, college education, home purchase, or wealth accumulation.
7.  Indicate your target retirement age.
8.  Allocate your current portfolio among stocks, bonds, cash, and alternative investments (such as real estate or commodities).
9.  Indicate your level of experience with investing.
10.  Disclose any significant financial obligations, if applicable.
11.  Indicate the number of months' worth of expenses in your emergency fund.
12.  Choose your preferred communication frequency with your advisor.
13.  Enter the name of your financial advisor assisting with the investment allocation.
14.  Provide today's date as a form completion date.

* **Primary Investment Goals (11)**: Select all relevant goals that apply to you.
* **Target Retirement Age (12)**: Enter the age at which you plan to retire.
