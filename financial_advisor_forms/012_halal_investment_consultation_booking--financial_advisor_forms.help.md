# Halal Investment Consultation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for a halal investment consultation. The form is not intended for users who are not seeking investment consultations or who are not interested in halal investments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your interests from the list of options provided.
3. Choose your preferred time slot for the consultation.
4. Select a date for the consultation from the calendar.
5. Confirm your phone number to ensure accurate contact information.
6. Review and agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant information.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select any or all of the interests that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for halal investments or 'No' for non-halal investments.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred time slot for the consultation, such as 'Yes' for morning or 'No' for afternoon.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the consultation from the calendar.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Choose the time slot for the consultation.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information, such as an address or phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or questions you have about the consultation.
* **Client Interests** (`client_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select your client's interests, such as 'Yes' for halal investments or 'No' for non-halal investments.
* **Financial Advisor** (`financial_advisor`, select_one, optional): Select the financial advisor you would like to consult with, such as 'Advisor 1' or 'Advisor 2'.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number to facilitate communication.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_one, optional): Confirm your phone number by selecting 'Phone 1' or 'Phone 2'.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, optional): Confirm your email address by selecting 'Email 1' or 'Email 2'.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms`, note, optional): Review and agree to the terms of service before proceeding.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure successful communication.
* Select the relevant interests and preferences to receive personalized consultation and investment advice.
* Review and agree to the terms of service before submitting the form.
