# Credit Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Report Request Form is a tool for users to request a credit report. This form helps gather necessary information to process a credit report efficiently and accurately. To avoid duplication, please ensure that you do not submit the same request multiple times. This form is intended for individuals who require a credit report for various reasons, such as applying for loans, obtaining credit, or understanding their credit score.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Credit Report Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, although most fields are not strictly required.
3. Double-check your information before submission to prevent errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Credit Report Request Form** (`form_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form that appears at the top of the page.
* **Name** (`form_2`, text, optional): Provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`form_3`, text, optional): Enter your residential address to help us locate your credit information.
* **Phone Number** (`form_4`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`form_5`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`form_6`, date, optional): Select the date you would like the credit report to be generated for.
* **Time** (`form_7`, time, optional): Select the time you would like the credit report to be generated for.
* **Credit Limit** (`form_8`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your desired credit limit.
* **Credit History** (`form_9`, text, optional): Briefly describe your credit history, if applicable.
* **Employment Status** (`form_10`, select_one, optional): Select your employment status:
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Self-Employed
* **Select Multiple** (`form_11`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
