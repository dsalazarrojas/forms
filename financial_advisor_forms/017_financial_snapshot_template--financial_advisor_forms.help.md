# financial_snapshot_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_snapshot_template form is designed to gather a brief financial snapshot of an individual or business, helping users collect and document their financial data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page, **financial_snapshot_template_page_1**, where you can provide any additional context or information about yourself or your business.
2. Proceed to the second page, **financial_snapshot_template_income**, where you will be asked to input your income details. Ensure that you fill in this required field accurately and completely.
3. On the final page, **financial_snapshot_template_expenses**, you will be asked to input your expenses details. While this field is optional, providing accurate information here will help in getting a comprehensive financial snapshot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **financial_snapshot_template_page_1**: This page is for any additional context or information about yourself or your business. It's an optional field, but filling it in can help provide a more detailed financial snapshot.
* **financial_snapshot_template_income** (`text`, required): This page is where you will be asked to input your income details. Ensure that you fill in this field accurately and completely.
* **financial_snapshot_template_expenses** (`text`, optional): This page is where you will be asked to input your expenses details. While this field is optional, providing accurate information will help in getting a comprehensive financial snapshot.
