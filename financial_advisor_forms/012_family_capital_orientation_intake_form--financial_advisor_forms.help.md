# Family Capital Orientation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Capital Orientation Intake Form is a document that collects information about the client's family capital orientation, including income, employment status, relationship status, and other relevant details. This form is used to gather accurate and comprehensive information about the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential to provide a complete picture of your family's capital orientation.
2. For each field, select the most accurate and relevant information about your family's situation.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`family_capital_orientation_form`): Enter your first name.
* **Annual Income** (`family_capital_orientation_form_income`): Enter your annual income from all sources, including salary, investments, and other sources. 
* **Income Source** (`family_capital_orientation_form_income_source`): Select the source of your income (e.g., Capital Gains, Investments, Salary).
* **Employment Status** (`family_capital_orientation_form_employment_status`): Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled).
* **Relationship Status** (`family_capital_orientation_form_relationship_status`): Select your current relationship status (e.g., Married, Single, Divorced, Common Law).
* **Children** (`family_capital_orientation_form_children`): Enter the number of children under your care (if applicable).
* **Consent** (`family_capital_orientation_form_consent`): Select the type of consent you are providing for this form (e.g., Explicit Consent, Implicit Consent, No Consent).
* **Email** (`family_capital_orientation_form_email`): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`family_capital_orientation_form_phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date and Time** (`family_capital_orientation_form_date` and `family_capital_orientation_form_time`): Enter the current date and time.
* **Questions** (`family_capital_orientation_form_questions`): Enter any questions or concerns you may have.
* **Comments** (`family_capital_orientation_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your family's capital orientation.
* **Agree to Terms** (`family_capital_orientation_form_agree_terms` and `family_capital_orientation_form_agree_terms_2`): Select whether you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of this form.
* **Date 2** (`family_capital_orientation_form_date_2`): Enter the date (if applicable).
* **Time 2** (`family_capital_orientation_form_time_2`): Enter the time (if applicable).
* **Employee Name** (`family_capital_orientation_form_employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee (if applicable).
* **Client Name** (`family_capital_orientation_form_client_name`): Enter the name of the client (if applicable).
* **Client Email** (`family_capital_orientation_form_client_email`): Enter the email address of the client (if applicable).
* **Client Phone** (`family_capital_orientation_form_client_phone`): Enter the phone number of the client (if applicable).
* **Client Address** (`family_capital_orientation_form_client_address`): Enter the address of the client (if applicable).
* **Client Comments** (`family_capital_orientation_form_client_comments`): Enter any comments or information about the client (if applicable).
