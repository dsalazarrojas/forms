# FIA Suitability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Industry Association (FIA) Suitability Assessment Form is used to gather information about a client's financial situation, goals, and preferences. This form helps investment professionals assess the client's suitability for various investment products or services. The goal of this form is to gather essential data to create a comprehensive investment plan tailored to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one of the following options for each question, and answer any text fields as thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to select the most accurate options that reflect your current financial situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please describe your thoughts or circumstances in the text field provided.
4. Complete all the required fields to ensure the form is submitted correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the client's primary financial goals? (Client Goals)** (`client_goals`, `text`, required)
    Please describe the client's main objectives, such as saving for a specific purpose or achieving a particular financial milestone.
* **What is the client's risk tolerance? (Risk Tolerance)** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required)
    Select a risk level that best describes the client:
    + High: Willing to take on higher risks for potential higher returns.
    + Medium: Moderate risk-taking for balanced returns.
    + Low: Cautious and conservative with lower risk.
* **What is the client's time horizon for investment? (Time Horizon)** (`time_horizon`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Select the time period for the investment:
    + 1-5 years
    + 5-10 years
    + 10-20 years
    + 20+ years
* **What type of assets do you currently have that are not invested? (Risk-Free Assets)** (`risk_free_asset`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Please list the types of assets:
    + Bonds
    + Cash
    + Stocks
    + Real Estate
* **What is the client's income type? (Income Type)** (`income_type`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Select the income type:
    + Salary
    + Self-employment
    + Business
* **What is the client's financial status? (Financial Status)** (`financial_status`, `select_one`, required)
    Select the financial status:
    + Retired
    + Inactive
    + Active
* **What is the client's tax rate? (Tax Deficiency)** (`tax_deficiency`, `select_one`, required)
    Select the tax rate:
    + High
    + Medium
    + Low
* **What is the client's asset allocation? (Asset Allocation)** (`asset_allocation`, `text`, required)
    Please describe the asset allocation.
* **What is the client's cash flow? (Cash Flow)** (`cash_flow`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Select the cash flow:
    + Low
    + Medium
    + High
* **What is the client's investment return expectation? (Investment Return)** (`investment_return`, `number`, required)
    Enter the expected investment return as a number.
* **What is the client's investment period? (Investment Period)** (`investment_period`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Select the investment period:
    + 1-5 years
    + 5-10 years
    + 10-20 years
    + 20+ years
* **What type of assets do you currently have invested? (Asset Type)** (`asset_type`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Please list the types of assets:
    + Cash
    + Bonds
    + Stocks
    + Real Estate

## Tips
- Make sure to answer questions thoroughly and honestly to get accurate results.
- If you are unsure about any question, please describe your thoughts or circumstances.
- This form is designed to be completed by the client, but investment professionals may also find it helpful for assessment purposes.
