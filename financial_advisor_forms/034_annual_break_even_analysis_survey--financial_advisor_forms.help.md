# Annual Break Even Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Break Even Analysis Survey is a financial analysis tool used to calculate the break-even point of a business. The survey gathers information on the business's financial performance, including total annual revenue, fixed and variable costs, and break-even point.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name, contact person name, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter the reporting year for which you are performing the analysis.
3. Enter the fiscal year start and end dates, if applicable.
4. Enter the total annual revenue.
5. Enter the revenue growth rate, if applicable.
6. Select the primary revenue sources.
7. Enter the total fixed and variable costs.
8. Enter the break-even point in units and revenue, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted with questions or follow-up information.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person. (Note: This field is optional and not required for the analysis.)
* **Reporting Year** (`reporting_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year for which you are performing the analysis.
* **Fiscal Year Start Date** (`fiscal_year_start`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your fiscal year, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional and not required for the analysis.)
* **Fiscal Year End Date** (`fiscal_year_end`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your fiscal year, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional and not required for the analysis.)
* **Total Annual Revenue** (`total_annual_revenue`, `number`, required): Enter the total revenue for the year.
* **Revenue Growth Rate Percentage** (`revenue_growth_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the growth rate compared to the previous year.
* **Primary Revenue Sources** (`revenue_sources`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the primary sources of revenue for your business.
* **Total Fixed Costs** (`fixed_costs_annual`, `number`, required): Enter the annual fixed costs that do not vary.
* **Total Variable Costs** (`variable_costs_annual`, `number`, required): Enter the annual variable costs that vary with production.
* **Labor and Payroll Costs** (`labor_costs`, `number`, optional): Enter the total labor and payroll costs.
* **Materials and Supplies Costs** (`materials_costs`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of materials and supplies.
* **Overhead and Administrative Costs** (`overhead_costs`, `number`, optional): Enter the overhead and administrative costs.
* **Marketing and Advertising Costs** (`marketing_costs`, `number`, optional): Enter the marketing and advertising costs.
* **Break-Even Point in Units** (`break_even_point`, `number`, optional): Enter the break-even point in units.
* **Break-Even Point in Revenue** (`break_even_revenue`, `number`, optional): Enter the break-even point in revenue.

Note: This form is used to gather information for a financial analysis and the fields marked as required are necessary for the calculation of the break-even point.
