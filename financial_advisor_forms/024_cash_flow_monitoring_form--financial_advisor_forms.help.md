# Cash Flow Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Flow Monitoring Form is used to track and analyze a business's cash flow, including opening and closing balances, cash transactions, and net change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the monitoring date and business name.
2. Enter the period start and end dates.
3. Record the opening balance, including the balance of checking and savings accounts.
4. Record cash transactions, including total receipts, sales or service income, receivables collected, and other receipts.
5. Record cash payments, including total payments, operating expense payments, payroll payments, and vendor or supplier payments.
6. Calculate the closing balance, including net change in cash.
7. Add any monitoring notes and observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monitoring Date**: Enter the date for which you are monitoring the cash flow.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business being monitored.
* **Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the monitoring period.
* **Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the monitoring period.
* **Opening Balance**: Enter the opening balance of the business's checking and savings accounts.
* **Opening Cash Balance**: Enter the opening cash balance of the business.
* **Checking Account Balance**: Enter the balance of the business's checking account.
* **Savings Account Balance**: Enter the balance of the business's savings account.
* **Cash Transactions**:
  * **Total Cash Receipts**: Enter the total cash received during the monitoring period.
  * **Sales or Service Income**: Enter the sales or service income received during the monitoring period.
  * **Receivables Collected**: Enter the amount of receivables collected during the monitoring period.
  * **Other Cash Receipts**: Enter any other cash receipts not included in sales or service income.
* **Cash Disbursements**:
  * **Total Cash Payments**: Enter the total cash paid during the monitoring period.
  * **Operating Expense Payments**: Enter the operating expense payments made during the monitoring period.
  * **Payroll Payments**: Enter the payroll payments made during the monitoring period.
  * **Vendor or Supplier Payments**: Enter the payments made to vendors or suppliers during the monitoring period.
* **Closing Balance**:
  * **Closing Cash Balance**: Enter the closing cash balance of the business.
  * **Net Change in Cash**: Enter the net change in cash during the monitoring period.
* **Monitoring Notes and Observations**: Add any notes or observations related to the cash flow monitoring process.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by financial advisors or accountants to monitor and analyze a business's cash flow.
