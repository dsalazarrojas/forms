# Credit Application For Business Account Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect credit application information for businesses. It's a necessary step in establishing a business account with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies.
3. Review and understand the terms and conditions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Identifier** (`business_identifier`, text, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for your business, if applicable.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person responsible for the account.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person responsible for the account.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email address of the person responsible for the account.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter the business address where the account will be managed.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the business phone number where the account will be managed.
* **Business Fax** (`business_fax`, text, required: false): Enter the business fax number where the account will be managed.
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, date, required: false): Enter the date the business was opened (if applicable).
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required: false): Select the type of business (e.g., corporation, partnership, sole proprietorship).
* **Credit Limit** (`credit_limit`, number, required: false): Set the desired credit limit for the account.
* **Credit Terms** (`credit_terms`, text, required: false): Describe the desired credit terms (e.g., payment terms, due date).
* **Contact Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the contact person responsible for the account.
* **Contact Phone Ext** (`contact_phone_ext`, text, required: false): Enter the phone extension of the contact person responsible for the account.
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, text, required: false): Enter the fax number of the contact person responsible for the account.
* **Business City** (`business_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **Credit Limit Amount** (`credit_limit_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the desired credit limit amount.

## Tips
- Ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays in processing your credit application.
- Review the credit terms and conditions before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
