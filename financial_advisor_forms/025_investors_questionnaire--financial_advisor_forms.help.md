# Investors Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Investors Questionnaire" form is designed to gather information from investors about their financial situations and goals. This data will help in making informed decisions regarding investments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information about your financial situation and goals.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best match your situation.
3. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
4. Enter numerical values for financial amounts (e.g., investment amount).
5. Use the text fields to describe your financial goals, history, and relationships.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Financials** (`company_financials`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant information about your company's financial situation.
* **Investor Interest** (`investor_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are interested in investing or not.
* **Investor Contact** (`investor_contact`, text, optional): Enter your name or contact information.
* **Investor Email** (`investor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **Company Interest** (`company_interest`, number, optional): Enter the level of interest in your company.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, text, optional): Describe your financial goals.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, date, optional): Enter the date of your financial history.
* **Financial Risks** (`financial_risks`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any financial risks.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the investment amount.
* **Investor Relationship** (`investor_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the company.
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current financial status (Active or Inactive).
* **Financial Return** (`financial_return`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you expect a return on investment.
* **Investment Terms** (`investment_terms`, text, optional): Describe the terms of your investment.
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are looking for an investment.
* **Financial Investment** (`financial_investment`, number, optional): Enter the investment amount.
* **Financial Objectives** (`financial_objectives`, text, optional): Describe your financial objectives.
* **Financial Expectation** (`financial_expectation`, text, optional): Describe your financial expectations.
* **Financial Prospectus** (`financial_prospectus`, text, optional): Enter any relevant financial prospectus information.
* **Financial Profits** (`financial_profits`, number, optional): Enter the expected profits.
* **Financial Growth** (`financial_growth`, text, optional): Describe your financial growth goals.
* **Financial Loss** (`financial_loss`, number, optional): Enter the financial loss.
* **Investor Liquidity** (`investor_liquidity`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you expect liquidity.
* **Financial Dividend** (`financial_dividend`, number, optional): Enter the dividend amount.
* **Financial Return** (`financial_return`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you expect a return on investment.
* **Financial Outcome** (`financial_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the financial outcome.
