# Vehicle Maintenance And Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance and repair services for your vehicle. Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information to ensure that your vehicle receives the necessary attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select any maintenance or repair services that are required.
3. Provide your preferred date and time for the service to be performed.
4. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
5. Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for our maintenance and repair team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Maintenance Request (Vehicle Details)** (`vehicle_details`, `text`, not required): This field is where you can provide a brief description of the issue or maintenance request for your vehicle.
* **Problem Description (Problem Description)** (`problem_description`, `text`, not required): In this field, describe the specific problem or maintenance issue you are experiencing with your vehicle.
* **Requested Services (Requested Services)** (`requested_services`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any services from the list that you require for your vehicle. This may include oil changes, tire replacements, or other maintenance tasks.
* **Preferred Date (Preferred Date)** (`preferred_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the service for. This will help our team plan and prepare for the service.
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time)** (`preferred_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time you would like to schedule the service for. This will help our team plan and prepare for the service.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`contact_details`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates or questions about your service request.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with updates or questions about your service request.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information or comments that may be helpful for our maintenance and repair team to understand the situation better.
