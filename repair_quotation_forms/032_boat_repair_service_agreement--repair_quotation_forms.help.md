# Boat Repair Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from customers before performing boat repair services. It captures essential details about the customer, their boat, and the service needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your full name, contact email and phone number.
3. Provide details about your boat, including its name, make, model, year, and hull ID.
4. Select the type of service you need (e.g., engine, hull, electrical, etc.).
5. Describe the issue in detail and when you noticed it.
6. Choose a preferred date and location for the service.
7. Indicate the urgency level and your budget range.
8. Choose your preferred payment terms and authorize the service agreement.
9. Sign and date the agreement, and finally, sign with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Required): Enter your full name as the boat owner.
* **Customer Email** (Required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Address** (Required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Boat Name** (Optional): Enter the name of your vessel.
* **Boat Make** (Required): Enter the manufacturer's name.
* **Boat Model** (Required): Enter the model name of your boat.
* **Boat Year** (Required): Enter the year of manufacture.
* **Boat Hull ID** (Required): Enter the HIN number of your boat.
* **Service Type** (Required): Choose the type of service you need (e.g., engine, hull, electrical, etc.).
* **Problem Description** (Required): Describe the issue in detail.
* **When Problem Started** (Optional): Enter when you noticed the issue.
* **Preferred Service Date** (Required): Choose a date for the service.
* **Service Location** (Required): Select where you want the service to be performed (at our shop, mobile service, etc.).
* **Marina Name** (Optional): If applying for mobile service at a marina, enter the marina name.
* **Slip Number** (Optional): If applying for mobile service at a marina, enter the slip number.
* **Urgency Level** (Required): Choose the urgency level of the service (routine, soon, urgent, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget** (Required): Choose your estimated budget range.
* **Authorization Limit** (Required): Choose your maximum authorization limit.
* **Parts Preference** (Optional): Select your preferred parts type (OEM, aftermarket, no preference).
* **Warranty Status** (Optional): Indicate if your boat is under warranty.
* **Previous Repairs** (Optional): Describe any recent repairs on this issue.
* **Payment Terms** (Required): Choose your payment terms (payment upon completion, 50% deposit, etc.).
* **Service Agreement** (Required): Authorize the terms of service.
* **Customer Signature** (Required): Sign with your full name.
* **Signature Date** (Required): Date of agreement.
* **Service Provider Name** (Optional): Enter company representative name.
* **Agreement Number** (Optional): For internal use.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the form accurately and ensure a smooth repair experience for both you and the service provider.
