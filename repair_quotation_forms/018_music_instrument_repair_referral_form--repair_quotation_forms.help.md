# Music Instrument Repair Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer customers with instrument repair needs to the most suitable technician at Music Shop. It provides essential information for staff to assign the repair work efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following information:
	* **Music Instrument Repair Referral Form** (`music_instrument_repair_referral_form`): Enter a brief description of the issue with your instrument.
	* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name, so staff can address you personally.
	* **Instrument Type** (`instrument_type`): Select the type of instrument that needs repair (Bass, Guitar, or Other).
	* **Repair Description** (`repair_description`): Describe the issue with your instrument in detail.
	* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so staff can contact you for any updates.
	* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
	* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
	* **Route to Repair** (`route_to_repair`): Select how you would like to be contacted for repair updates (Email, Phone, or Text).
	* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Choose whether you want follow-up contact after the repair (True or False).
	* **Service Date** (`service_date`): Enter the date you want your instrument repaired by.
	* **Technician** (`technician`): Select the technician you prefer for the repair (Jimmy, Jane, or John).
	* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`): Enter any other relevant information about your instrument or repair needs.
	* **Instrument Status** (`instrument_status`): Indicate if your instrument is Available for repair or Unavailable.
2. Click on the "Submit" button to send your referral to the staff.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue with your instrument.
* Ensure your email address is up to date, so staff can contact you for updates.
* If you select "False" for follow-up, you will not receive further contact after the repair.
* If your instrument is Unavailable, staff will contact you to discuss alternative options.
