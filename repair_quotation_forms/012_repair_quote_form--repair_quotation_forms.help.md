# Repair Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Repair Quote Form is a form used to collect information about a repair or service quote from a customer. It's used to gather details about the customer's request, repair or service information, and payment status. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and communicated effectively between the customer and the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Make sure to review each section carefully and provide accurate details.
3. If a field is marked as required, ensure it's completed before submitting the form.
4. If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the introductory page where you can add any relevant information about the repair or service quote.
* **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about the customer.
* **Service Info** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the service or repair being requested, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Quote Details** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional details about the quote, such as estimated costs or times.
* **Repair Notes** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the repair or service.
* **Customer Signature** (`6`, `text`, required: false): The customer's signature is required for agreement, but this field can be optional.
* **Technician Signature** (`7`, `text`, required: false): The technician's signature is required for completion, but this field can be optional.
* **Agreement Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'True' if the customer agrees to the quote, or 'False' if they don't.
* **Additional Info** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about the repair or service.
* **Date Submitted** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time the quote was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`11`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the quote was submitted.
* **Repair Quote Status** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the quote: 'pending', 'approved', or 'declined'.
* **Phone Number** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`14`, `email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Service Cost** (`15`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated cost of the service or repair.
* **Service Price** (`16`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price of the service or repair.
* **Payment Status** (`17`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status: 'paid', 'pending payment', or 'not paid'.
* **Technician Comments** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the technician's work.
* **Technician Signature Date** (`19`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the technician signed the quote.
* **Technician Signature Time** (`20`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the technician signed the quote.
* **Technician Signature Status** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the technician has signed the quote: 'True' or 'False'.
* **Customer Comments** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or notes from the customer.
* **Quote Date** (`23`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the quote is valid for.
* **Quote Time** (`24`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the quote is valid for.
* **Customer Signature Date** (`25`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the customer signed the quote.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors or omissions before submitting.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to reach out to our support team.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is collected.
