# Table Extension Slide Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Table Extension Slide Repair Request Form is used to collect information from clients regarding their table extension slide repair needs. This form is designed to gather details about the problem, client information, schedule appointment dates and times, and any additional comments the client may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Table Extension Slide Repair Request Form" title.
2. On the first page, "Table Extension Slide Repair Details", provide a detailed description of the issue with your table extension slide.
3. On the second page, "Client Details", enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your repair request.
4. On the third page, "Schedule Appointments", select the dates and times you are available for the repair.
5. On the fourth page, "Additional Comments", write any further comments or questions you may have about the repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Table Extension Slide Repair Details (Page 1)**: This field is where you can describe the problem with your table extension slide. Please provide a detailed description of what's not working as expected.
* **Client Details (Page 2)**: Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you about your repair request.
* **Schedule Appointments (Page 3)**: Select the dates and times you are available for the repair. Please choose one or more dates and times when it's convenient for you.
* **Additional Comments (Page 4)**: If you have any further comments or questions about the repair, you can write them here. This is also a good place to provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and detailed information about the problem with your table extension slide.
* Double-check that you've entered your contact information correctly.
* If you're not sure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to ask us for clarification.
* We'll do our best to accommodate your schedule, but please select only the dates and times that work for you.
