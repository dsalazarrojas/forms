# Repair Estimation Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Estimation Quote Form is designed to collect information from customers for a repair estimation quote. This form will help you get a clear understanding of the customer's needs and provide an accurate estimation of the repair costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's contact information (page 1).
2. Provide details about your customer (page 1).
3. Describe the repair job (page 1).
4. Enter any relevant repair information (page 1).
5. Estimate the costs involved (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information (page 1)
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, company name, or any other relevant details about you or your company.

### Contact Information (page 1)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact name, email, phone number, or any other way we can reach you.

### Job Details (page 1)
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, optional): Describe the job or issue you need to be fixed.

### Repair Information (page 1)
* **Repair Information** (`repair_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the repair, such as the location, date, or time.

### Cost Estimate (page 2)
* **Cost Estimate** (`cost_estimate`, text, optional): Enter an estimate of the costs involved in the repair. This will help us provide a more accurate quote.

## Tips
* Please provide as much information as possible to ensure an accurate estimation.
* You can add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
