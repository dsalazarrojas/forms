# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees leaving the company to understand their reasons for departure, experiences, and suggestions for improvement. The purpose of this form is to collect constructive feedback that can help the company learn and grow, making it a valuable tool for employee retention and development.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select the main reason for your departure from the provided options.
- For the "Overall Job Satisfaction" question, choose all that apply.
- For questions with text fields, provide as much detail as possible.
- For the "Overall Company Experience" question, select "True" if you would recommend the company to a friend or colleague, and "False" otherwise.
- You are free to provide any additional comments or suggestions in the final comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What was the main reason for your departure?** (Select One, Required)
	* This question helps us understand the underlying reasons for your departure.
	* Choose the reason that best aligns with your experience.
- **How would you rate your overall job satisfaction?** (Select Multiple, Required)
	* This question assesses your overall satisfaction with your role.
	* Choose all options that apply to you.
- **How well did your role meet your career goals and aspirations?** (Select One, Required)
	* This question evaluates how well your role fulfilled your career expectations.
	* Choose the option that best represents your experience.
- **What were the most significant challenges you faced in your role?** (Text, Optional)
	* This question allows you to share any significant challenges you faced in your role.
	* Please provide as much detail as possible.
- **How would you rate your relationship with your supervisor/manager?** (Select One, Required)
	* This question assesses your relationship with your supervisor/manager.
	* Choose the option that best represents your experience.
- **How would you rate your satisfaction with your role on a scale of 1-5?** (Number, Optional)
	* This question is an additional way to gauge your satisfaction with your role.
	* Choose a number between 1 and 5 to represent your experience.
- **What suggestions do you have for improving the company culture?** (Text, Optional)
	* This question invites you to share any suggestions you have for improving the company culture.
	* Please provide as much detail as possible.
- **Would you recommend this company to a friend or colleague?** (Select One, Required)
	* This question assesses your overall experience with the company.
	* Choose "True" if you would recommend the company, and "False" otherwise.
- **Is there anything else you'd like to share with us?** (Text, Optional)
	* This final question allows you to share any additional comments or feedback.
	* Please feel free to provide as much detail as possible.
