# Employee Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from departing employees, including their reason for departure, last day of work, and next steps. It also serves as a platform for the employee to provide feedback, suggestions, and any other relevant details about their experience with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Employee ID number in the designated field. This is an optional field.
2. Provide your name in the space provided.
3. Describe the reason for your departure from the company.
4. Enter the date you will be leaving the company.
5. Enter the last day you worked at the company.
6. Enter the time you left the workplace on your last day.
7. Provide the name of your manager.
8. Rate your overall experience with the company using the exit survey scale.
9. If necessary, explain your reason for rating in the provided field.
10. Choose your next steps, which include internal promotion, new role within the company, external opportunity, retirement, or other (please specify).
11. If you choose "Other" in step 10, please specify your next steps in the text field below.
12. Enter any exit comments or suggestions.
13. Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
14. Enter your email address.
15. Choose the time and person responsible for follow-up actions.
16. If other than HR or manager is selected, please specify.
17. Enter the time of the follow-up action.
18. Enter the date of the follow-up action.
19. Record the last updated date for this form.
20. Record the name of person who last updated this form.
21. Record the time the form was last updated.
22. Record the name of person who last updated this form.
23. Record the person who created this form.
24. Record the time the form was created.
25. Record the date the form was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1): A unique identifier for the employee. Enter your Employee ID number here.
* **Employee Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Reason for Departure** (3): A brief description of why you are leaving the company.
* **Date of Departure** (4): The date you will be leaving the company.
* **Last Day of Work** (5): The last day you worked at the company.
* **Last Day of Work Time** (6): The time you left the workplace on your last day.
* **Manager Name** (7): The name of your direct supervisor.
* **Exit Survey** (8): Rate your overall experience with the company using the scale provided (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very Poor).
*   If you rate anything but "Excellent", please describe your reason for rating in this field.
* **Next Steps** (10): Choose your next steps after leaving the company. Options include internal promotion, new role within the company, external opportunity, retirement, or other (please specify).
*   If you choose "Other" in step 10, specify your next steps in the field below.
* **Next Steps Other (please specify)** (11): A brief description of your plans after leaving the company.
* **Exit Comments** (12): Provide any exit comments or suggestions about your experience with the company.
* **Contact Information** (13): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (14): Enter your email address.
* **Follow-up** (15): Choose the time and person responsible for follow-up actions.
*   If other than HR or manager is selected, specify the person responsible.
* **Follow-up Time** (16): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Date** (17): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
*   This field will be automatically updated when HR or manager is selected.
* **Last Updated** (18): The last updated date of this form.
* **Last Updated by** (19): The name of person who last updated this form.
*   If the form is updated by someone other than HR or manager, specify their name.
* **Last Updated Time** (20): The time the form was last updated.
*   This field will be automatically updated when HR or manager is selected.
* **Last Updated by Other** (21): The name of person who last updated this form.
*   If HR or manager updated the form, this field will be left blank.
*   If the form is updated by someone other than HR or manager, specify their name.
*   **Created by** (22): The person who created this form.
*   This field is typically completed by HR.
* **Created Time** (23): The time the form was created.
*   This field is typically completed by HR.
*   **Created At** (24): The date the form was created.
*   This field is typically completed by HR.
