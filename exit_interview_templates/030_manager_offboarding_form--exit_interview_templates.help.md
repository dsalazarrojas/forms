# Manager Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Offboarding Form is a crucial document that helps ensure a smooth transition for team members when they leave the organization. It captures essential information about the manager's involvement and the reasons for offboarding, which is vital for maintaining accurate records and facilitating future collaborations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct answer for "Manager Information" to confirm your role as the manager of the team member being offboarded.
2. Enter the manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Manager Name" field.
4. Select whether the team member is a "Yes" or "No" for "Team Member Information".
5. Enter the manager's title in the "Manager Title" field.
6. Enter the date of the offboarding in the "Date of Offboarding" field using the calendar selector.
7. Provide a clear and concise reason for offboarding in the "Reason for Offboarding" field.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field (optional).
9. If applicable, describe the knowledge transfer process in the "Knowledge Transfer" field (optional).
10. Lastly, outline the next steps for the team member in the "Next Steps" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Information (Required)**: Select "Yes" if you are the manager of the team member being offboarded. This field ensures that you are completing the form for the correct team member.
* **Manager Email (Required)**: Enter the email address of the manager of the team member being offboarded.
* **Manager Name (Required)**: Enter your name as the manager of the team member being offboarded.
* **Team Member Information (Required)**: Select "Yes" if the team member being offboarded is a member of your team. This field helps us understand the team dynamics.
* **Manager Title (Required)**: Enter your title as the manager of the team member being offboarded.
* **Date of Offboarding (Required)**: Enter the date when the team member started their offboarding process.
* **Reason for Offboarding (Required)**: Provide a clear and concise reason for offboarding the team member.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the offboarding process.
* **Knowledge Transfer (Optional)**: Describe any knowledge transfer process that occurred during offboarding.
* **Next Steps (Optional)**: Outline the next steps for the team member after offboarding.
