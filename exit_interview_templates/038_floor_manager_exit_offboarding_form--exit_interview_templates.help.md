# Floor Manager Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floor Manager Exit Offboarding Form is a document used to collect information from a departing floor manager, including their manager's contact information, department, reason for leaving, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all necessary information is captured.
2. Complete the form accurately and truthfully, using the following guidelines:
* For each field, please provide the most up-to-date and accurate information available.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Info (2)**: Please enter your manager's ID number.
* **Manager Notes (3)**: This field is for your manager to take notes or leave any other comments about your departure.
* **Team Info (4)**: Please provide an overview of your team's current projects and initiatives.
* **Department Info (5)**: Please indicate which department you were working in.
* **Manager Comments (6)**: This field is for your manager to leave comments or feedback about your departure.
* **Exit Comments (7)**: This field is for you to express any thoughts or concerns you may have about your departure.
* **Manager Signature (8)**: Please sign and date this form to indicate that you have reviewed and completed it.
* **Date (9)**: Please enter the date of your departure.
* **Time (10)**: Please enter the time of your departure.
* **Location (11)**: Please indicate where you will be located after your departure.
* **Manager Phone (12)**: Please enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email (13)**: Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Department (14)**: Please indicate which department you were working in.
* **Job Title (15)**: Please indicate your job title.
* **Manager Name (16)**: Please enter your manager's name.
* **Employee ID (17)**: Please enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name (18)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Manager ID (19)**: Please enter your manager's ID number.
* **Manager Department (20)**: Please indicate which department your manager works in.
* **Manager Job Title (21)**: Please indicate your manager's job title.
* **Manager Start Date (22)**: Please enter the date your manager started working.
* **Manager End Date (23)**: Please enter the date your manager will be ending.
* **Manager Reason for Departure (24)**: Please explain why your manager is leaving.
* **Manager Notes (25)**: This field is for your manager to leave any other comments or feedback.
* **Location (11)**: Please indicate where you will be located after your departure.
* **Manager Phone (12)**: Please enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email (13)**: Please enter your manager's email address.
