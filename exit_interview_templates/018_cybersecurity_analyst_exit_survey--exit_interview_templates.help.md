# Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a part of the company's exit interview process. It aims to gather information about your experience, satisfaction, and reasons for leaving the company. Your responses will help us understand your perspective and will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Use the provided options as guidelines, but feel free to elaborate on your responses if needed.
3. If a question does not apply to you, select "No" or "Not Applicable" as your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary job function**: This question is asking about your main responsibilities and tasks in your role. Choose the option that best describes your job function.
* **How long have you worked in your role**: This question is asking about the duration of your employment with the company. Choose the option that best describes your length of service.
* **What tools did you use on a daily basis**: This question is asking about the tools you regularly used in your work. Choose all the tools that apply to your role.
* **What was the most challenging project you worked on**: This question is asking about a specific project that you found difficult or challenging. Please provide more details about the project and what made it challenging for you.
* **What was the most rewarding project you worked on**: This question is asking about a specific project that you found particularly enjoyable or rewarding. Please provide more details about the project and what made it enjoyable for you.
* **What was your favorite tool to use**: This question is asking about a tool that you particularly enjoyed using. Choose all the tools that apply to your role.
* **What was your favorite thing about your work environment**: This question is asking about a positive aspect of your work environment. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **What was your favorite thing about the company**: This question is asking about a positive aspect of the company. Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **What was your least favorite thing about your work environment**: This question is asking about a negative aspect of your work environment. Please provide more details about what made it least favorite.
* **What was your least favorite thing about the company**: This question is asking about a negative aspect of the company. Please provide more details about what made it least favorite.
* **Any other comments**: This question is an open-ended space for you to add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **What is your expected date of departure**: This question is asking about your planned departure date. Enter your expected date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What time do you plan to leave**: This question is asking about your planned departure time. Enter your planned departure time in the format 01:00 PM.
* **Do you plan to stay in contact with any coworkers**: This question is asking about your intention to stay in contact with colleagues. Choose the option that best describes your intentions.
* **Would you recommend this company as a good place to work**: This question is asking about your recommendation for the company. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Why would you not recommend**: This question is asking about reasons for not recommending the company. Please provide more details about your reasons.
* **How will you stay in contact with the company**: This question is asking about your plans for staying in contact with the company. Please provide more details about your intentions.
* **How will you keep in touch with your team**: This question is asking about your plans for staying in touch with your team. Please provide more details about your intentions.
* **Will you be available for an exit interview**: This question is asking about your availability for an exit interview. Choose the option that best reflects your availability.
* **How many years have you worked here**: This question is asking about the duration of your employment with the company. Enter the number of years you have worked.
* **How many projects did you work on in the last year**: This question is asking about the number of projects you completed in the last year. Enter the number of projects.
* **What was the main reason for your decision to leave**: This question is asking about the main reason for your departure. Choose the option that best describes your reason.
* **What is your salary or hourly rate**: This question is asking about your compensation. Enter your current salary or hourly rate.
* **What is your manager's name**: This question is asking about your direct supervisor's name. Enter your manager's name.
* **What is your manager's email**: This question is asking about your manager's email address. Enter your manager's email.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about a question, choose the option that best reflects your intentions or feelings.
- If a question does not apply to you, select "Not Applicable" or "No".
- Please provide more details when answering questions that ask for additional comments.
- Enter dates and times in the specified formats.
