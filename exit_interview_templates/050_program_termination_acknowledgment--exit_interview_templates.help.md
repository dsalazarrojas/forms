# Program Termination Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge the participant's program termination, ensuring that all required information is captured and that the participant is aware of their program status change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details, including program, school, and training provider information.
2. Select the termination date from the calendar picker.
3. Choose the reason for termination from the provided options or select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
4. Confirm that you have received the form and understand the implications of program termination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_details (Required)**: Enter your participant's program name, school name, and training provider name.
* **termination_date (Required)**: Select the date when your program was terminated.
* **reason_for_termination**: Choose a reason for termination from the options provided:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other: Provide a brief explanation if your reason for termination is not listed.
* **confirmations**: Confirm that you have received this form by selecting "Yes", or choose "No" if you disagree.
* **confirmations_other**: If you select "No" as your confirmation, provide a brief explanation.
* **program (Required)**: Enter your participant's program name.
* **school (Required)**: Enter your participant's school name.
* **training_provider (Required)**: Enter the name of the participant's training provider.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your program termination.
