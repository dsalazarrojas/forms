**exit_feedback_form - Help Guide**
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect exit feedback from employees who are leaving the company. It will help us understand the reasons for their departure, their experiences with their current manager and the company, and any areas for improvement. This feedback is crucial in helping us better understand our employees' perspectives and make informed decisions for future growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
--------------------------------

### Introduction
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for your departure from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the name of your current manager.
3. Rate your job performance by selecting a number (e.g., 1-10).
4. Choose the company climate that best describes your experience (e.g., positive, neutral, or negative).
5. If you have any feedback about your communication with your manager, please type it in.
6. If you have any feedback about your communication with your team, please type it in.
7. Describe your experiences and thoughts about your job content.
8. Share any thoughts about work-life balance.

### Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers. Your feedback is valuable to us.
* You can choose 'other' if your reason for leaving is not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
---------------------------

### 1. Why are you leaving
* **Why are you leaving** (select_one, required: false)
	+ Select one of the reasons for leaving: left employer, new opportunity, personal reason, health concerns, or other.

### 2. Current manager
* **Current manager** (text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of your current manager.

### 3. Job performance rating
* **Job performance rating** (number, required: false)
	+ Rate your job performance from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.

### 4. Company climate
* **Company climate** (select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose the company climate that best describes your experience: positive, neutral, or negative.

### 5. Communication with manager
* **Communication with manager** (text, required: false)
	+ Type any feedback or comments about your communication with your manager.

### 6. Communication with team
* **Communication with team** (text, required: false)
	+ Type any feedback or comments about your communication with your team.

### 7. Job content
* **Job content** (text, required: false)
	+ Describe your experiences and thoughts about your job content.

### 8. Work life balance
* **Work life balance** (text, required: false)
	+ Share any thoughts about your work-life balance.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by employees who are leaving the company. If you are not leaving the company, please do not submit this form.
