# Food Service Exit Interview Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees who are leaving the company's food service department. It is used by the HR department to process exit interviews and to provide support to employees who are leaving the company.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions on this form as completely and honestly as possible. The questions are designed to help us understand your reasons for leaving and to gather feedback on your experience. You may need to answer some questions on multiple pages, so please make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
