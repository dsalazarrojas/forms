# Veteran Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veteran Exit Survey is a one-page form used to collect information from veterans who have exited a program. This form is meant to be completed by program staff members to capture the details of the veteran's exit, including their demographic information and reason for departure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the first and last name of the veteran in the corresponding text fields.
* Enter the date of the veteran's death in the "Date of Death" field.
* Enter the time of the veteran's death in the "Time of Death" field.
* Enter the exit date of the veteran in the "Exit Date" field.
* Enter the name of the program that the veteran was participating in in the "Program Name" field.
* Enter the length of time the veteran participated in the program in the "Program Length" field.
* Enter the reason why the veteran exited the program in the "Reason for Exit" field.
* Enter any additional comments or feedback from the veteran in the "Comments" field.
* Enter the veteran's email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter the veteran's phone number in the "Phone" field.
* If the veteran exited another program, enter the name of that program in the "Program Name (Other)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter the first name of the veteran.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter the last name of the veteran.
* **Date of Death (Date of Death)**: Enter the date of the veteran's death.
* **Time of Death (Time of Death)**: Enter the time of the veteran's death.
* **Exit Date (Exit Date)**: Enter the date of the veteran's exit from the program.
* **Program Name (Program Name)**: Enter the name of the program that the veteran was participating in.
* **Program Length (Program Length)**: Enter the length of time the veteran participated in the program.
* **Reason for Exit (Reason for Exit)**: Enter the reason why the veteran exited the program.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the veteran.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the veteran.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the veteran.
* **Program Name (Other) (Program Name (Other))**: If the veteran exited another program, enter the name of that program.
