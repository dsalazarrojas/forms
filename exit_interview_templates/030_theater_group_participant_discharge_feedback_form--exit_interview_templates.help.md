# Theater Group Participant Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Theater Group Participant Discharge Feedback Form" is designed to gather feedback from participants who have participated in theater group activities. This form is meant to be a quick and easy way for participants to share their experiences, opinions, and suggestions with the organizers of the theater group.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Welcome to the form**: This is the introduction to the form and is not required information, so you can skip it if you don't need to.
2. **Participant Details**: This field is required and should be filled with your name, email address, or any other identifying information that you are comfortable sharing.
3. **How was your experience?**: Choose one or multiple options to describe your experience with the theater group from the following options: Very good, Good, Fair, Bad, Very bad.
4. **Additional Comments**: This field is optional, but you are welcome to add any additional comments or suggestions you think would be helpful to the organizers.
5. **Thank you for your feedback**: This is a courtesy message to thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required):
	+ Please enter your name, email address, or any other identifying information that you are comfortable sharing.
* **How was your experience?** (`feedback_questions`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Choose one or multiple options to describe your experience with the theater group. This will help us understand the quality of our service and identify areas of improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional):
	+ This is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have. If you have anything else you'd like to share, please add it here.
* **Welcome to the form** (`form_start`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is just a welcome message and can be skipped.
* **Thank you for your feedback** (`form_end`, `text`, not required):
	+ A courtesy message to thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.
