# Climate Change Specialist Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and insights from departing employees on their experience, reasons for leaving, and suggestions for improvement. It will help the organization understand the reasons behind your departure and identify areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Please fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
3. For multiple-choice questions, select one option that best reflects your experience.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
5. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, \*): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, \*): Enter your current or last position title within the organization.
* **Start Date of Employment** (`employment_dates`, date, \*): Enter the date you started working with the organization.
* **Last Working Day** (`last_working_day`, date, \*): Enter the date you last worked for the organization.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Employment** (`overall_experience`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with your employment experience.
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Rate Your Overall Experience** (`experience_rating`, number, \*): Rate your overall experience from 1 (Poor) to 10 (Excellent).
* **Work Environment and Culture** (`work_environment`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects your experience with the work environment and culture.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Quality of Leadership and Management** (`leadership_quality`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects your experience with the quality of leadership and management.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Career Development Opportunities** (`career_development`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects your experience with career development opportunities.
	+ Very Limited
	+ Limited
	+ Adequate
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Compensation and Benefits** (`compensation_benefits`, select_one, ): Choose one option that best reflects your experience with compensation and benefits.
	+ Below Market
	+ Market Average
	+ Above Market
* **Primary Reason for Leaving** (`primary_reason`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects your primary reason for leaving.
	+ Better Opportunity
	+ Compensation or Benefits
	+ Work-Life Balance
	+ Career Growth
	+ Management or Culture Issues
	+ Location or Relocation
	+ Health or Personal Reasons
	+ Retirement
	+ Other
* **Provide More Details About Your Departure** (`reason_details`, text, \*): Please provide more details about your reasons for leaving.
* **Factors That Could Have Made You Stay** (`improvement_factors`, select_multiple, ): Choose all factors that could have made you stay.
	+ Higher Salary
	+ Better Benefits
	+ Career Growth Path
	+ Better Management
	+ Improved Work Culture
	+ Flexible Schedule
	+ Remote Work Options
	+ Better Equipment or Resources
	+ Other
* **What Are the Organization's Strengths?** (`organizational_strengths`, text, ): Please describe the organization's strengths.
* **Areas for Organizational Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, ): Please suggest areas where the organization could improve.
* **Feedback on Direct Management** (`management_feedback`, text, ): Provide feedback on your direct management experience.
* **How Aligned Was the Work with Your Values?** (`mission_alignment`, select_one, \*): Choose one option that best reflects how aligned you felt the work was with your personal values.
	+ Not Aligned
	+ Somewhat Aligned
	+ Neutral
	+ Well Aligned
	+ Perfectly Aligned
* **Impact on Climate Change Work** (`climate_impact`, text, ): Describe the impact you felt your work had on climate change.
* **Advice for Your Successor** (`successor_advice`, text, ): Provide advice for your successor.
* **Would You Like to Stay Connected with the Organization?** (`stay_in_touch`, select_one, ): Choose one option that best reflects your desire to stay connected.
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
* **Would You Consider Returning to the Organization?** (`return_possibility`, select_one, ): Choose one option that best reflects your likelihood of returning.
	+ Definitely Not
	+ Probably Not
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Definitely Yes
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, ): Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have.

**Tips**:

* Please be as honest as possible when answering the questions.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Your feedback will contribute to making the organization a better place for future employees.
