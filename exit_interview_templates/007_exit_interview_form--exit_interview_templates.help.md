# Exit Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The exit interview form is designed to gather information about your departure from the company, including reasons for leaving, job satisfaction, communication and leadership skills, and other relevant details. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand the reasons behind your decision to leave and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take your time to complete this form carefully, ensuring you answer each question accurately.
2. Select the reasons that best describe your reasons for leaving the company.
3. Enter your job title, supervisor's name, department, and date of your last job evaluation.
4. Provide additional comments if you have any further information you'd like to share.
5. Evaluate your satisfaction with your job, communication skills, leadership skills, time management, and teamwork skills using the rating system provided.
