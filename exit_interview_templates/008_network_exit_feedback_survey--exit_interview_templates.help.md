# Network Exit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from employees who are leaving the network. Your input will help the network management team understand the reasons behind your departure and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have any comments or feedback, please write them in the text fields provided.
3. Select the correct answer for each question, and make sure to choose multiple options if applicable.
4. If you have any final comments or feedback, please use the "final comments" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Why did you leave the network?** (`offboarding_reason`): This question is asking about the reasons behind your decision to leave the network. Please provide as much detail as possible, such as any issues with your role, manager, or department that led to your departure.
* **What could we improve on your experience?** (`feedback`): This question is for suggestions on how we can improve the network experience for future employees. Be specific about what went well and what didn't, and provide concrete examples.
* **How would you rate communication with your manager?** (`communication_with_manager`): This question is assessing the quality of communication with your manager. Please rate your experience from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How often did you contact network team members?** (`frequency_of_contact`): This question is about how often you interacted with the network team. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What factors led to your decision to leave?** (`retention_driver`): This question is asking about the key factors that influenced your decision to leave the network. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Date of your last day of work?** (`exit_date`): This question is for your official last day of work at the network. Please enter the date in the correct format.
* **How many years did you spend at the network?** (`time_spent`): This question is asking about how long you were part of the network. Please enter the number of years you spent at the network.
* **What was your manager's name?** (`manager_name`): This question is for your manager's name at the time of your departure.
* **What feedback would you give to your manager?** (`feedback_to_manager`): This question is for feedback you would like to give to your manager, if any.
* **How would you rate the impact of our network on your work?** (`network_impact`): This question is assessing the effectiveness of the network in supporting your work. Please rate your experience from 1 (very low) to 5 (very high).
* **How would you rate communication with network members?** (`communication_with_members`): This question is about the quality of communication within the network. Please rate your experience from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **What training or development opportunities would you like to see the network offer?** (`training_opportunities`): This question is for suggestions on training or development opportunities you would like to see the network offer.
* **What is your last contact with the network team?** (`last_concept`): This question is about your last interaction with the network team. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What feedback would you give to the network?** (`feedback_to_network`): This question is for feedback you would like to give to the network, if any.
* **What suggestions do you have for the network manager(s)?** (`manager_suggestions`): This question is for suggestions you would like to make to the network manager(s), if any.
* **Overall, how would you rate your experience in the network?** (`overall_experience`): This question is assessing your overall experience in the network. Please rate your experience from 1 (very bad) to 5 (excellent).
* **Do you have any final comments about your experience?** (`final_comments`): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.
* **What was your job title during your last year at the network?** (`job_title`): This question is for your job title at the time of your departure.
* **What was your last role at the network?** (`last_role`): This question is for your role at the network at the time of your departure.
* **What was your last manager's name?** (`last_manager`): This question is for your manager's name at the time of your departure.
* **What was your last department name?** (`last_department`): This question is for your department name at the time of your departure.
* **What was your last manager's manager name?** (`last_manager_manager`): This question is for your manager's manager's name at the time of your departure.
* **What was your last position title?** (`last_position`): This question is for your position title at the time of your departure.
