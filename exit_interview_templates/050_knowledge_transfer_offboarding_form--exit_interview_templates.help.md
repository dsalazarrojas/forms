# knowledge_transfer_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's knowledge, role, and responsibilities during the offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth transition of knowledge and responsibilities.
- Review each field carefully and provide the required information for each section.
- Use the dropdown menus to select the relevant options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information about the employee's role or responsibilities.
- **Role Identifier** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided role identifiers to indicate the employee's role.
- **Name** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's name.
- **Email** (`4`, `email`, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
- **Phone** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
- **Manager ID** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided manager IDs to indicate the employee's manager.
- **Manager Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
- **Team ID** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided team IDs to indicate the team the employee belongs to.
- **Team Lead ID** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided team lead IDs to indicate the team lead.
- **Knowledge Base** (`10`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the employee's knowledge base.
- **Knowledge Owner** (`11`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the knowledge owner.
- **Knowledge Review** (`12`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the knowledge review process.
- **Knowledge Acceptance** (`13`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the knowledge acceptance process.
- **Knowledge Transfer** (`14`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the knowledge transfer process.
- **Knowledge Confirmation** (`15`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the knowledge confirmation process.
- **Next Steps** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided next steps to indicate the next steps in the offboarding process.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
- This form is a critical part of the offboarding process, so please fill it out completely and accurately to ensure a smooth transition of knowledge and responsibilities.
