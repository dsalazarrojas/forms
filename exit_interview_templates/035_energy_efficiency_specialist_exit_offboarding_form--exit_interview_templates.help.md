# energy_efficiency_specialist_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The energy efficiency specialist exit offboarding form is a crucial document that helps maintain accurate records and facilitates a smooth transition process when an employee leaves the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required to fill out the form accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all the required fields.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`): Select your current job title from the provided options (Energy Efficiency Specialist, Senior Energy Efficiency Specialist, Energy Management Specialist, or Energy Efficiency Coordinator).
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`): Choose the reason for your departure from the provided options (Relocation, Career Change, Job Elimination, Retirement, or Resignation).
* **Last Day of Work** (`date_of_last_workday`): Enter the date of your last day of work in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your departure.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number to ensure smooth communication.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Provide the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter the email address of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`): Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the name of your department.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): List the names of your team members.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of the company.
* **Job Performance Rating** (`job_performance_rating`): Rate your job performance from 1 to 5.
* **Job Satisfaction Rating** (`job_satisfaction_rating`): Rate your job satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **Would You Recommend** (`recommendation`): Indicate whether you would recommend the company to others.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Job Duties** (`job_duties`): List the job duties that were part of your role.
* **Job Duties List** (`job_duties_list`): Provide a list of job duties in a bullet-point format.
* **Important Job Duties** (`job_duties_important`): Highlight the most critical job duties.
* **Not Important Job Duties** (`job_duties_not_important`): Identify job duties that were not critical.
* **Additional Comments** (`job_duties_additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments about job duties.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): Describe your job duties in a clear and concise manner.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`): List the requirements for your job position.
* **Exit Date Prophets** (`exit_date_prophets`): Enter the date of your exit from the company in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out accurately to ensure proper processing. If you're unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or the HR department for clarification.
