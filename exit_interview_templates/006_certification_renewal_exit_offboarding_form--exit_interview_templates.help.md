# Certification Renewal Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the sections and fields of the Certification Renewal Exit Offboarding Form.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please confirm that you are eligible to complete this form by ensuring that you are officially leaving the organization and have all necessary information and materials ready. 

1. Begin by selecting the correct department or team from the provided options.
2. Enter your full legal name, followed by your internal employee ID.
3. Select the final day of your employment from the calendar.
4. In the section "Active Certifications Held", list all your professional certifications managed during your tenure with the organization.
5. In the section "Upcoming Renewal Dates", list the dates for all certifications that require renewal within the next 6 months.
6. Select "Yes - All Transferred" if you have provided all login and management access to your supervisor, or choose "No - Not Applicable" if it's not relevant.
7. Choose "True" if all physical company-issued certificates have been returned, or "Digital Only" if only digital copies remain.
8. Select the primary reason for your departure from the provided options.
9. Rate your overall experience with the organization on a scale of 1 to 5.
10. Assess the support provided for training and development during your employment.
11. Provide suggestions on how to improve the offboarding process in the text field.
12. Confirm that you have completed all required exit procedures.
13. Enter the current date.
14. Enter your personal email address for future administrative updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official identification documents.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your internal employee identifier.
- **Department or Team** (`department_team`, `select_one`, required): Select the department or team you are leaving.
- **Last Working Day** (`last_working_day`, `date`, required): Select the final date of your employment.
- **Active Certifications Held** (`certifications_held_active`, `text`, required): List all professional certifications managed during your tenure with the organization.
- **Upcoming Renewal Dates** (`certification_renewal_dates`, `text`, optional): List dates for certifications requiring renewal within the next 6 months.
- **Credential Access Transferred?** (`credential_transfer_complete`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes - All Transferred" if you have provided all login and management access to your supervisor, or choose "No - Not Applicable" if it's not relevant.
- **Physical Certificates Returned?** (`physical_certificates_returned`, `select_one`, required): Choose "True" if all physical company-issued certificates have been returned, or "Digital Only" if only digital copies remain.
- **Primary Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_leaving`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary reason for your departure.
- **Overall Experience with the Organization** (`overall_experience_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the organization on a scale of 1 to 5.
- **Training and Development Adequacy** (`training_development_adequacy`, `select_one`, required): Assess the support provided for training and development during your employment as "Excellent Support", "Adequate Support", "Needs Improvement", or "Poor Support".
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions on how to improve the offboarding process in the text field.
- **Completion of All Offboarding Tasks** (`offboarding_tasks_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have completed all required exit procedures.
- **Personal Email for Future Contact** (`contact_email_post_departure`, `email`, optional): Enter your personal email address for future administrative updates.
- **Today's Date** (`signature_date_offboarding`, `date`, required): Enter the current date.
- **Offboarding Tasks Completed** (`offboarding_tasks_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have completed all required exit procedures. 

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Certification Renewal Exit Offboarding Form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a smooth transition of your responsibilities and certifications, and providing valuable feedback to the organization.
