# Online Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Exit Interview Form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. This form helps to understand their reasons for departure, job satisfaction, and overall experience while working with the company. The data collected will be used to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions for future employee retention and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Submit" from the navigation menu to start the form.
2. Answer the questions in the form fields as accurately and completely as possible.
3. For questions with multiple options, select the response that best describes your experience or reason for leaving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Demographics**: This field is optional, but it's a good idea to provide your employee ID or a brief description of your employment history with the company.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to record the job title you held during your employment with the company.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of your job during your time with the company. Options include:
	+ Active: Currently employed in the company
	+ Inactive: No longer employed in the company
	+ Resigned: Left the company voluntarily
	+ Retired: Left the company due to retirement
* **Reason for Leaving**: Select the reason why you are leaving the company. Options include:
	+ Better job offer: You received a more attractive offer from another company
	+ Job not available: The company offered you a layoff or your position was eliminated
	+ Personal reasons: You had personal reasons for leaving, such as family or health issues
	+ Retired: You have reached retirement age
	+ Terminated: You were terminated due to performance or disciplinary issues
* **Work Experience**: Select how many years you have worked with the company. Options include:
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-6 years
	+ 7-9 years
	+ 10+ years
* **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 being "Very Satisfied".
* **Work-Life Balance**: Rate how well the company met your work-life balance needs on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Poor" and 5 being "Excellent".
* **Communication with Manager**: Rate how often you felt you were informed about important company decisions on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Rarely" and 5 being "Always".
* **Communication with Colleagues**: Rate how often you felt you were able to communicate effectively with your colleagues on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Rarely" and 5 being "Always".
* **Support from Management**: Rate how much support you received from management on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Poor" and 5 being "Excellent".
* **Job Training**: Rate how well you were trained in your job responsibilities on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Poor" and 5 being "Excellent".
* **Job Training Needs**: Rate how much additional training you need on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Little" and 5 being "Much needed".
* **Benefits**: Provide any additional comments about the company's benefits package.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **Company Image**: Provide any additional comments about the company's image or reputation.
