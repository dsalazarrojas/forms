This form is intended to collect information from employees who are leaving a company, including their name, contact information, reason for offboarding, and any other relevant details. The form is typically used during the offboarding process to ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities and to maintain company records. 

Before completing this form, please review the following instructions to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.

## Machine Operator Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Operator Offboarding Form is used to gather essential information from departing employees, facilitating a seamless transfer of tasks and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with the requested information, including:
	* Employee name and contact details (fields 1 and 2)
	* Reason for offboarding (field 3)
	* Machine operator position (field 4)
	* Last date of work (field 5)
	* Notice period (field 6)
	* Information on whether equipment has been returned (field 7)
	* Contact person and their email (fields 8 and 9)
	* Any additional company information (field 10)
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by a red asterisk (*) on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their records.
* **Employee Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address associated with their company account.
* **Reason For Offboarding** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reason the employee is leaving the company.
* **Machine Operator Position** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the employee's role or position as a machine operator.
* **Last Day of Work** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the employee's last day of work was, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Notice Period** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Indicate the number of days or weeks the employee received notice before their last day of work.
* **Equipment Returned** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if the employee has returned any company equipment. If yes, select all that apply.
* **Contact Person** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the employee's offboarding process.
* **Contact Person Email** (`9`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person responsible for the employee's offboarding process.
* **Company Information** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional company information relevant to the offboarding process.
* **Notes** (`11`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the employee's offboarding process.

## Tips
- Always fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- If unsure about a field, contact the designated contact person for assistance.
- Ensure that all equipment has been returned before submitting the form.
