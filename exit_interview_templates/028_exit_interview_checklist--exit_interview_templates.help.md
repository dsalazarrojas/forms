# Exit Interview Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Checklist is a form designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. This form helps to identify reasons for departure, assess company contributions, and determine next steps.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, providing as much detail as you can about your experience and reasons for leaving.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and honestly, as they are intended to help the company improve and learn from your departure.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to ask your HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please take a moment to introduce yourself and briefly explain why you are leaving the company. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and feelings about your time with us.
* **Employee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your current job title or the title you would have been assigned if you were still employed with us.
* **Department**: Please enter the department or team you have been a part of while working with us.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Please share any comments or feedback you have for your supervisor or manager.
* **Reasons for Leaving**: Please select from the list the reasons why you are leaving the company. You can select one or multiple options that apply to your situation.
* **Company Contribution**: Please describe any positive contributions you've made to the company during your time with us.
* **Manager Comments**: Please share any comments or feedback you have for your manager.
* **Feedback**: Is there anything else you'd like to share or any feedback you have for the company?
* **Next Steps**: Are there any next steps you think the company should take to improve?
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth for record-keeping purposes.
* **Date Hired**: Please enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Date Separated**: Please enter the date you last worked with the company.
* **Years of Service**: Please enter the number of years you've been with the company.
* **Hours Worked Last Year**: Please enter the number of hours you worked last year.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Please select from the list the reason why you are leaving the company. This is a duplicate question of 16 and is used for consistency.
* **Job Title (Other)**: If your job title is not listed, please enter it here.
* **Manager Comments (Other)**: If you have any comments or feedback for your manager, please share them here.
* **Company Contribution (Other)**: If you have any other contributions you'd like to mention, please share them here.

Note: Please fill out as much of the form as you can, but don't worry if you can't complete every field. Your honesty and candor are appreciated and will help us learn and grow from your experience.
