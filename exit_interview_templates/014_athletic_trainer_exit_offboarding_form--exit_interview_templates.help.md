# Athletic Trainer Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from departing Athletic Trainers, helping the organization to understand the reasons for their departure, gather feedback, and make necessary improvements. Please complete this form thoroughly to ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your employment information, including your full name, position title, department, and dates of employment.
2.  Choose the primary reason for your departure from the given options.
3.  Share more details about your reason for leaving.
4.  Answer questions about your overall job satisfaction, work environment, and professional development opportunities.
5.  Rate your compensation and benefits package.
6.  Provide suggestions for improvements.
7.  Indicate whether you would recommend our organization to others.
8.  Confirm the completion of all required documentation.
9.  Leave any final comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trainer Name** (`Trainer Name`, `Text`, Required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in company records and for future communications.
*   **Position Title** (`Position Title`, `Text`, Required): Enter your title or position within the organization.
*   **Department** (`Department`, `Text`, Required): Enter the department or team you were a part of.
*   **Employment Start Date** (`Employment Start Date`, `Date`, Required): Enter the date you began employment with the organization.
*   **Last Day of Employment** (`Last Day of Employment`, `Date`, Required): Enter the date you last worked for the organization.
*   **Length of Service** (`Length of Service`, `Text`, Optional): Describe the length of time you were employed by the organization in years and months.
*   **Personal Email Address** (`Personal Email Address`, `Email`, Required): Enter your personal email address for continued communication.
*   **Personal Phone Number** (`Personal Phone Number`, `Text`, Required): Enter your personal phone number for continued communication.
*   **Reason for Departure** (`Reason for Departure`, `Select One`, Required): Choose the primary reason for your departure from the given options.
*   **Departure Details** (`Departure Details`, `Text`, Required): Provide a detailed explanation for your reason for leaving.
*   **Overall Job Satisfaction** (`Overall Job Satisfaction`, `Select One`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your role.
*   **Work Environment Feedback** (`Work Environment Feedback`, `Text`, Optional): Leave any comments about your work environment.
*   **Management and Leadership Feedback** (`Management and Leadership Feedback`, `Text`, Optional): Leave any comments about your direct supervisor or management.
*   **Team Dynamics Feedback** (`Team Dynamics Feedback`, `Text`, Optional): Share your thoughts on your team and colleagues.
*   **Professional Development Opportunities** (`Professional Development Opportunities`, `Select One`, Required): Rate the adequacy of professional development opportunities provided.
*   **Compensation and Benefits** (`Compensation and Benefits`, `Select One`, Optional): Rate your satisfaction with the compensation and benefits package.
*   **Suggested Improvements** (`Suggested Improvements`, `Text`, Optional): Leave any suggestions for improvements.
*   **Would You Recommend This Organization?** (`Would You Recommend This Organization?`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you would recommend our organization to others.
*   **Documentation Status** (`Documentation Status`, `Select One`, Required): Confirm whether you have completed all required documentation.
*   **Final Comments** (`Final Comments`, `Text`, Optional): Leave any final thoughts or feedback.
*   **May We Stay in Contact?** (`May We Stay in Contact?`, `Select One`, Required): Indicate whether you are open to staying in contact with the organization after your departure.

## Tips
- Please answer each question as accurately as possible.
- You can refer to your employment contract or HR documents for accurate dates and job titles.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to contact HR or your supervisor for guidance.
