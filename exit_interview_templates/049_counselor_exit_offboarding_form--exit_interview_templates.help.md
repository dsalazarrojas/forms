# Counselor Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Counselor Exit Offboarding Form is a crucial tool to collect information and feedback from counselors who are leaving the organization. It ensures a smooth transition and helps with understanding reasons for leaving, which can be valuable for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Counselor Information**: This section is for gathering essential details about the counselor.
2.  **Last Day Information**: Provide details about the last day of the counselor's employment.
3.  **Reason for Leaving**: This field is required and should contain a clear and concise statement about why the counselor is leaving.
4.  **Comments on Last Day**: Enter any additional comments or information about the last day of the counselor's employment.
5.  **Comments on Exit Process**: Provide any comments or suggestions on the exit process itself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Counselor Information (text)**: Enter the counselor's name and any other relevant details.
*   **Last Day Information (text)**: Enter the date the counselor worked their last day.
*   **Reason for Leaving (text)**: Clearly state why the counselor is leaving the organization. This is a required field.
*   **Comments on Last Day (text)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the last day of the counselor's employment.
*   **Comments on Exit Process (text)**: Provide any comments or suggestions on the exit process itself.
