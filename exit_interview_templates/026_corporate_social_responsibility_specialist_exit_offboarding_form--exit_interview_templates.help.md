# Corporate Social Responsibility Specialist Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and feedback from Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Specialist employees who are leaving the company. The goal of this form is to collect their thoughts and experiences to improve the CSR program and ensure a smooth transition for future colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Provide feedback on your overall experience as a CSR Specialist in the company.
3. Rate the company's CSR program in the following areas:
	* **Rating 1**: Alignment with the company's values and mission
	* **Rating 2**: Effectiveness of CSR strategy and activities
	* **Rating 3**: Employee engagement and involvement
	* **Rating 4**: Impact of CSR activities on the company's bottom line
	* **Rating 5**: Suggestions for improvement
4. Add any additional comments about your experience and suggestions for the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**:
	+ **Name:** None
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Provide a brief introduction to your feedback and comments. Please be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.
* **feedback**:
	+ **Name:** feedback
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Provide feedback on your overall experience as a CSR Specialist. Please be honest and constructive.
* **comments**:
	+ **Name:** comments
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **rating1**:
	+ **Name:** rating1
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Rate the company's CSR values and mission on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not aligned" and 5 is "Completely aligned".
* **rating2**:
	+ **Name:** rating2
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Rate the company's CSR strategy and activities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not effective" and 5 is "Very effective".
* **rating3**:
	+ **Name:** rating3
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Rate the level of employee engagement and involvement in CSR activities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Low" and 5 is "High".
* **rating4**:
	+ **Name:** rating4
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Rate the impact of CSR activities on the company's bottom line on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Low" and 5 is "High".
* **rating5**:
	+ **Name:** rating5
	+ **Type:** number
	+ **Required:** No
	+ Rate any suggestions you may have for improving the CSR program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "No suggestions" and 5 is "Many suggestions".

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback and ratings.
* Provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
* Remember to rate each area on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "comments" field.
