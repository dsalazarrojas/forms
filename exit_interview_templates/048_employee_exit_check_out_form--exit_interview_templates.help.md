# Employee Exit Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees when they are leaving the company. It helps to gather details about their departure, such as the reason for leaving, last day of work, and manager's contact information. This form is usually filled out by the employee on their last day of work or before their departure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information and details about your departure.
2. Make sure to provide your reason for departure in the "reason for departure" field.
3. Enter your last day of work and the time you left the company.
4. Fill out the manager's name and email address if applicable.
5. If you have any additional comments or information about your departure, you can add them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your manager's name. If you are leaving, please provide your manager's name who you have been working with.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): This field is for your manager's email address. This is an optional field, but it is recommended that you provide this information for future reference.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required/optional): This field is for your email address. This is an optional field, but it is recommended that you provide this information for future reference.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to explain why you are leaving the company. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, required/optional): This field is to note the last day of work for you. Use the calendar to select your last day of work.
* **Last Day of Work Time** (`last_day_of_work_time`, time, required/optional): This field is for you to note the exact time of your last day of work. If possible, use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about your departure. You can use this space to explain anything you want to be noted about your last day of work.
