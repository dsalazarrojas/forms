# Transportation Planner Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from Transportation Planners leaving the company to support a smooth transition and gather feedback for future improvements. Please complete this form to provide essential details about your departure and suggestions for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with your information.
2. Select the relevant options for the `Reason Leaving Company` and `Reason Leaving Job` fields.
3. Enter dates and times as required for `Last Day of Work` and `Last Day of Work Time`.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the `Transportation Planner Comments`, `Exit Survey`, `Feedback to Management`, and `Suggestions for Improvement` fields.
5. Enter your contact details in the `Contact Details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transport Planner Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about your role and position.
* **Reason Leaving Company**: Select the reason why you are leaving the company. You can choose from the following options:
	+ New Job Opportunity
	+ No longer interested in role
	+ Company restructure
	+ Job not challenging enough
	+ Job not matching career goals
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Reason Leaving Job**: Select all that apply from the following options:
	+ New job opportunity
	+ No longer interested in role
	+ Company restructure
	+ Job not challenging enough
	+ Job not matching career goals
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the date you last worked as a Transportation Planner.
* **Last Day of Work Time**: Enter the time you last worked as a Transportation Planner.
* **Transportation Planner Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about your experience as a Transportation Planner.
* **Exit Survey**: Share any thoughts or suggestions about your departure and the company.
* **Feedback to Management**: Suggest any areas for improvement or changes that you think the company should consider.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Offer any suggestions to enhance the Transportation Planner role or the company.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information so that the company can reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and honest as possible when providing your feedback and comments.
- If you are leaving the company for a new job opportunity, please respect the company's policy regarding notice periods and exit procedures.
- If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR representative.
