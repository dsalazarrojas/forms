# Retail Partner Exit Notice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request formal notice of a retail partner's exit from our program. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely, as it will help us understand the reason for their departure and ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the **Retail Partner Exit Notice Request Form** section.
2.  Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you are requesting formal notice.
3.  Provide your **Retail Partner Name** in the text field.
4.  Choose the **Exit Reason** that best describes the situation.
5.  Enter the **Retail Partner Contact** information to ensure we can get in touch with them.
6.  Fill in the **Retail Partner Email** address to enable communication.
7.  Provide the **Retail Partner Phone** number for easy contact.
8.  Enter the **Date of Exit** when they intend to leave the program.
9.  If necessary, fill in the **Last Date of Service**, which is the date of their last activity in the program.
10. In the **Reason for Dispute** field, provide any details regarding conflicts or issues that led to the exit.
11. Fill in the **Resolution Notes** to document any efforts made to resolve the dispute.
12. Select the **Partner Status** that reflects their current condition in the program.
13. Finally, provide your **Retail Partner ID**, which can be any relevant identifier for the partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Retail Partner Exit Notice Request Form**: This is the title of the form that you are being asked to fill out.
*   **Retail Partner Name**: Enter the name of the retail partner that is exiting the program.
*   **Exit Reason**: Select the reason for the partner's exit, such as 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the reason.
*   **Retail Partner Contact**: Enter the contact information of the retail partner to ensure we can get in touch with them.
*   **Retail Partner Email**: Enter the email address of the retail partner to enable communication.
*   **Retail Partner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the retail partner for easy contact.
*   **Date of Exit**: Enter the date when the retail partner intends to leave the program.
*   **Last Date of Service**: If necessary, enter the date of their last activity in the program.
*   **Reason for Dispute**: Enter any details regarding conflicts or issues that led to the exit.
*   **Resolution Notes**: Document any efforts made to resolve the dispute.
*   **Partner Status**: Select the status of the partner in the program, either Active or Inactive.
*   **Retail Partner ID**: Enter any relevant identifier for the partner, such as a customer ID.

## Tips

*   Always use the form for its intended purpose to avoid duplication of efforts.
*   Ensure that the partner's information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
*   If you're unsure about any information, contact your program administrator for guidance.
