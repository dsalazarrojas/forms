# public_utility_worker_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from public utility workers when they are exiting the company. It gathers details about their exit status, reason for leaving, and other relevant information to facilitate a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience and knowledge.
3. Fill in any text fields with your comments or feedback.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Exit Status** (`exit_status`, Select One)
Select the current status of your employment. Choose 'Active' if you are still an active employee or 'Inactive' if you are not.

### 2. **Reason for Exit** (`reason_for_exit`, Text)
Provide a brief description of why you are leaving the company. Be as specific as possible to help your colleagues understand your situation.

### 3. **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, Date)
Enter the date you officially exited the company.

### 4. **What's the next step you'd like to take** (`next_step`, Select Multiple)
Indicate if you would like to be involved in the transition process or not. Choose one or more of the options to express your preferences.

### 5. **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note)
Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the company. This section is optional but helps us improve our processes and services.

### 6. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text)
Enter your contact information, including email address or phone number, so we can reach out to you for any further questions or information.

### 7. **Comments** (`comments`, Text)
Add any comments or observations you'd like to make about your experience with the company.

### 8. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One)
Indicate if an assigned tool is still applicable or if it's not relevant to your exit.

### 9. **Output File** (`output_file`, Select One)
Decide if you need an output file or report after your exit.

### 10. **Category** (`category`, Select One)
Choose the category that best describes your role or department within the company.

Tips:
* Ensure you provide accurate and clear information to help the company understand your situation.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
