# Housing Project Manager Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the necessary information for offboarding a Housing Project Manager.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to enter all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*).
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Id** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the project being offboarded.
* **Manager Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the Project Manager responsible for offboarding the employee.
* **Project Title** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief title for the project being offboarded.
* **Project Description** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the project being offboarded.
* **Project Status** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the project: New, Active, or Closed.
* **Project Manager Note** (`6`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the Project Manager.
* **Offboarding Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the offboarding meeting or meeting to begin offboarding process.
* **Manager Comment** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from the Project Manager.
* **Employee Name** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee being offboarded.
* **Employee Email** (`10`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the employee being offboarded.
* **Employee Phone** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee being offboarded.
* **Exit Reason** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for the employee's exit from the project.
* **Exit Date** (`13`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the employee's last day of work.
* **Last Interaction** (`14`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last interaction with the employee.
* **Next Interaction** (`15`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next interaction with the employee.
* **Notes** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the offboarding process.
* **Employee Signature** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the employee being offboarded.
* **Manager Signature** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the Project Manager.
* **Form Completed** (`19`, `note`, required): Check that you have completed the form accurately and completely.
* **Date Completed** (`20`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time you completed the form.
* **Manager Note** (`21`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes from the Project Manager.
* **Project Manager Name** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the Project Manager.
* **Project Manager Email** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the Project Manager.
* **Project Manager Phone** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the Project Manager.
* **Project Manager Note** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the Project Manager.
* **Company** (`26`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Department** (`27`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the employee being offboarded.
* **Job Title** (`28`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title of the employee being offboarded.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out accurately and completely to ensure that the offboarding process is completed successfully.
