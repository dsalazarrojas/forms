The Hybrid Worker Offboarding Form is a template used to collect necessary information from departing employees, ensuring a smooth transition of knowledge and resources during their exit. Before filling out this form, please confirm that you are the employee who is leaving the company or an authorized representative of the employee. To avoid duplication, ensure that you are not completing this form if the employee's departure is already being processed or if you have previously submitted an offboarding form for the same employee.

# Hybrid Worker Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Worker Offboarding Form is designed to gather essential details from departing employees to facilitate a proper handover and closure of their employment. This template helps in ensuring that all necessary steps are taken during the exit process, including the return of company property and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2.  **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the employee's manager who will be involved in the offboarding process.
3.  **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for the employee's reason for leaving the company.
4.  **Company Property Returns** (`company_property_returns`, text, required/optional): List any company property or assets that the employee is returning to the company before leaving.
5.  **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any feedback or comments about their experience at the company, which will be useful for future improvements.
6.  **Date of Last Day** (`date_of_last_day`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee's last working day was, or the date on which they will leave the company.
7.  **Time of Last Day** (`time_of_last_day`, time, required/optional): If applicable, enter the time of the last working day or the expected departure time.
8.  **Return of Company Property** (`return_of_company_property`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of company property or assets that the employee is returning before leaving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information** (`employee_name`, `employee_number`, etc.): Enter your name as per company records.
*   **Manager Name**: If your manager is not listed, please provide their name for the offboarding process to continue smoothly.
*   **Reason for Leaving**: Briefly describe the circumstances surrounding your departure, if any. This may help in understanding your perspective.
*   **Company Property Returns**: List any items or equipment you are handing over to your representative on your last day.
*   **Feedback**: Your thoughts, suggestions, or feedback on your time at the company can help us grow.
*   **Date of Last Day**: Enter the date when your last working day was, or the date of your expected departure.
*   **Time of Last Day**: Enter the time of your last day, if different from the date.
*   **Return of Company Property**: Count and list the items you are returning to the company.
