# Skilled Labor Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skilled Labor Exit Offboarding Form is used to collect information about an employee's departure from the company. This form is intended to be completed by the employee as part of the offboarding process, and it helps the HR department to track and manage the offboarding process efficiently. This form is typically completed near the employee's exit date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
   - Job title
   - Reason for departure
   - Notice period
   - Last day of work
   - Last shift of work
   - Final salary amount
   - Benefits details
   - Exit date
   - Exit time
   - Signature
   - Employee name
   - Employee title
   - Manager name
   - Manager phone
   - Manager email
   - Company name
   2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
   - Employment status (if applicable)
   - Contact information (to be assigned to a contact person)
   - Next step (to be assigned to a contact person)
   - Assigned contact (to be assigned to a contact person)
   - Next action (to be assigned to a contact person)
   - Next action details
   - Final comments
   - Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their current job title.
* **Reason for departure** (`reason_for_departure`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to explain their reason for leaving the company.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for the employee to choose their employment status, which may include active, inactive, or other options.
* **Notice period** (`notice_period`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the notice period they are giving to the company.
* **Last day of work** (`last_day_of_work`, `date`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the last day they worked at the company.
* **Last shift of work** (`last_shift_of_work`, `time`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the last shift they worked at the company.
* **Final salary amount** (`final_salary_amount`, `number`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their final salary amount, including any accrued leave or vacation pay.
* **Benefits details** (`benefits_details`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to provide details about their benefits, including any remaining benefits or outstanding benefits.
* **Exit date** (`exit_date`, `date`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the date they are actually leaving the company.
* **Exit time** (`exit_time`, `time`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the time they are leaving the company.
* **Contact information** (`contact_information`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for the employee to choose a contact person to be assigned to, in case of future contact.
* **Next step** (`next_step`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for the employee to choose the next step in the offboarding process.
* **Assigned contact** (`assigned_contact`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for the employee to choose a contact person to be assigned to, in case of future contact.
* **Next action** (`next_action`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for the employee to choose the next action in the offboarding process.
* **Next action details** (`next_action_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for the employee to provide details about the next action.
* **Final comments** (`final_comments`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to provide any final comments or feedback about their experience.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to sign the form, indicating their confirmation of the information provided.
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their name.
* **Employee title** (`employee_title`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their job title.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the name of their manager.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their manager's phone number.
* **Manager email** (`manager_email`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in their manager's email address.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the employee to fill in the name of the company they are leaving.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for the employee to provide any additional comments or feedback about their experience.

Note: Please ensure you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
