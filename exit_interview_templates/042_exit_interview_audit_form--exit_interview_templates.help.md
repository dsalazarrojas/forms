# exit-interview-audit-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's experience and reasons for leaving the company. It will help us improve our processes and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Please describe your job title and a brief summary of your role and responsibilities.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional): Please provide a brief overview of your work experience, including relevant job titles, dates, and locations.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): On a scale from 1 to 10, rate your overall job satisfaction.
* **Manager Relationship** (`manager_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your manager, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Company Culture** (`company_culture`, select_multiple, required): Select all the cultural aspects that you think best describe our company culture:
  • Option 1: Option 1
  • Option 2: Option 2
  • Option 3: Option 3
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_multiple, required): Select all the communication styles that best describe our company's communication style:
  • Option 4: Option 4
  • Option 5: Option 5
  • Option 6: Option 6
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, number, optional): On a scale from 1 to 10, rate your overall work environment, including factors such as workspace, resources, and colleagues.
* **Exit Interview Schedule** (`exit_interview_schedule`, time, optional): Please provide the date and time of your exit interview, if scheduled.
* **Exit Interview Location** (`exit_interview_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where you will be conducting your exit interview, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Please indicate which tool was assigned to you during your tenure at the company.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options that best describes your reason for leaving:
  • Option 7: Option 7
  • Option 8: Option 8
  • Option 9: Option 9
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you after you leave the company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your valid phone number to ensure we can contact you after you leave the company.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, note, optional): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience. 

Note: This guide is meant to be filled out before submitting, so please ensure you have all the required fields completed before submitting the form.
