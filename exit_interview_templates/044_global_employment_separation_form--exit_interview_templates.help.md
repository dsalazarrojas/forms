# Global Employment Separation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Employment Separation Form is used to record the reason and details of an employee's separation from the organization. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and provides a standardized process for documenting separations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the employee's name and ID in the respective fields.
2. Select the reason for separation from the available options (Termination, Resignation, or Other).
3. If the reason for separation is "Other", provide a detailed explanation in the "Reason for Separation (Other)" field.
4. Enter the separation date, last day of work, and termination date (if applicable).
5. Select the type of separation (Voluntary, Involuntary, or Other).
6. If the separation is involuntary, select "Involuntary" from the "Separation Type" field.
7. If the separation is involuntary, select "Termination" from the "Termination Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Global Employment Separation Form**: This is the main form name.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their employment records.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID as per the organization's ID system.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager as per the organization's hierarchy.
* **Manager Title**: Enter the title of the employee's manager.
* **Reason for Separation**: Select the reason for separation from the available options (Termination, Resignation, or Other). This field is required to be filled out.
* **Separation Date**: Enter the date of separation.
* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the last day the employee worked.
* **Separation Type**: Select the type of separation (Voluntary, Involuntary, or Other).
* **Reason for Separation (Other)**: If the reason for separation is "Other", provide a detailed explanation in this field.
* **Separation Effective Date**: Enter the date when the separation will be effective.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date when the termination will be effective.
* **Termination Reason**: Select the reason for termination from the available options (Termination, Resignation, or Other).
* **Termination Type**: Select the type of termination (Voluntary, Involuntary, or Other).

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact HR for guidance.
* This form is only for recording employment separations. Do not use for other purposes.
