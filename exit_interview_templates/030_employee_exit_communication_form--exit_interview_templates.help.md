# Employee Exit Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's reasons for leaving the company and their experiences during their employment. It helps the HR department to understand the reasons behind an employee's exit and to gather information that can be used to improve the work environment for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information page with your name, email, and other relevant details.
2. Select the correct exit date from the calendar.
3. Choose the reason for your exit from the provided options.
4. Select all the contributions you made to the company during your employment.
5. Indicate how often you communicated with your manager during your employment.
6. If you have any open-ended comments, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`Employee Exit Communication Form`, text, required): This page is where you will enter your name, email, and other relevant details.
* **Employment Information** (`Employment Information`, text, required): Please enter a brief description of your employment with the company, including your job title, department, and length of service.
* **Exit Date** (`Exit Date`, date, required): Please select the date you will be leaving the company.
* **Reason for Exit** (`Reason for Exit`, select one, required): Choose one of the provided options that best describes your reason for leaving the company.
* **Company Contributions** (`Company Contributions`, select multiple, required): Select all the contributions you made to the company during your employment, such as helping others, improving processes, increasing productivity, or reducing costs.
* **Work Environment** (`Work Environment`, number, required): Indicate how many hours per day you spent in the work environment.
* **Communication with Manager** (`Communication with Manager`, select one, required): Choose how often you communicated with your manager during your employment.
* **Open-Ended Comments** (`Open-Ended Comments`, select multiple, required): If you have any additional comments about your employment with the company, select all that apply.
