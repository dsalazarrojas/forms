# Union Representative Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Union Representative Exit Offboarding Form is a tool used to capture essential information from union representatives as they exit their role. This form helps to document the reason for departure, dates of employment, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question accurately and completely.
2. Enter your reason for departure in the "exit_reason" field.
3. Specify the reason for your departure in the "reason_for_departure" field.
4. Enter the date you started working for the union in the "notice_period" field (if applicable).
5. Enter your last day of work in the "last_day_of_work" field.
6. Enter the date you will leave the union in the "union_offboarding_date" field.
7. Enter your last day of work in the "last_date_of_work" field.
8. Select your employment status (e.g., "Retired", "Resigned", "Terminated", etc.) in the "employment_status" field.
9. Enter your name as it appears on your union ID in the "union_representative_name" field.
10. Enter your title/position within the union in the "union_representative_title" field (if applicable).
11. Enter your union representative ID in the "union_representative_id" field.
12. Enter the department you were a part of while working with the union in the "department" field.
13. Enter your position within the union in the "position" field.
14. Enter the name of your supervisor (if applicable) in the "supervisor" field.
15. Enter the name of the union you represent in the "union" field.
16. Enter the name of your manager in the "manager" field.
17. Enter your contact number (phone or email) in the "contact_number" field.
18. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
19. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field (if applicable).
20. Enter any additional notes for the offboarding process in the "additional_notes" field (if applicable).
21. Enter any next steps for the offboarding process in the "next_steps" field.
22. Enter any comments for the offboarding process in the "comments" field (if applicable).
23. Specify the reason for departing from the union in the "reason_for_departing" field.
24. Enter any other information about the offboarding form in the "union_representative_offboarding_form" field (if applicable).
25. Select all applicable options for the exit interview in the "exit_interview_completed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exit_reason**: The reason for your departure from the union. Please enter the reason accurately and completely.
* **reason_for_departure**: The reason for your departure from the union. Please specify the reason clearly.
* **notice_period**: The date you started working for the union. This field is optional and only required if applicable.
* **last_day_of_work**: Your last day of work with the union. Please enter the date accurately.
* **union_offboarding_date**: The date you will leave the union. Please enter the date accurately.
* **last_date_of_work**: Your last day of work with the union. Please enter the date accurately.
* **employment_status**: Your current employment status (e.g., "Retired", "Resigned", "Terminated", etc.). Please select the status accurately.
* **union_representative_name**: Your name as it appears on your union ID. Please enter your name accurately.
* **union_representative_title**: Your title/position within the union. Please enter your title/position accurately (if applicable).
* **union_representative_id**: Your union representative ID. Please enter your ID accurately.
* **department**: The department you were a part of while working with the union. Please enter the department accurately.
* **position**: Your position within the union. Please enter your position accurately.
* **supervisor**: The name of your supervisor (if applicable). Please enter the supervisor's name accurately.
* **union**: The name of the union you represent. Please enter the union's name accurately.
* **manager**: The name of your manager. Please enter the manager's name accurately.
* **contact_number**: Your contact number (phone or email). Please enter your contact number accurately.
* **email**: Your email address. Please enter your email address accurately.
* **notes**: Any additional comments or notes for the offboarding process. Please enter any additional comments accurately (if applicable).
* **additional_notes**: Any additional notes for the offboarding process. Please enter any additional notes accurately (if applicable).
* **next_steps**: Any next steps for the offboarding process. Please enter any next steps accurately.
* **comments**: Any comments for the offboarding process. Please enter any comments accurately (if applicable).
* **reason_for_departing**: The reason for departing from the union. Please specify the reason accurately.
* **union_representative_offboarding_form**: Any other information about the offboarding form. Please enter any additional information accurately (if applicable).
* **exit_interview_completed**: The exit interview process. Please select all applicable options accurately.
