# Employee Exit Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Exit Interview Form is used to gather information from departing employees about their reasons for leaving the company, their experience, and suggestions for improvement. This form helps the organization understand the employee's perspective and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections below and answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing reasons for leaving and suggestions for improvement.
3. If you have any final comments or concerns, feel free to share them in the "Final Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief note to explain the purpose of the form and the importance of sharing your thoughts.
* **Name**: Please provide your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Job Title**: Describe your job title during your employment.
* **Department**: Specify the department you belonged to during your employment.
* **Reasons for Leaving**: Share your reasons for leaving the company. This is a crucial section, as it helps us understand your decision.
	+ You can select from the provided options or write your own reason.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions on how we can improve the company or your job, please share them here.
* **Next Steps**: If there are any specific actions you would like us to take or any follow-up discussions, please indicate them here.
* **Final Comments**: This is your chance to add any final thoughts or concerns you may have.
* **Submission**: Select the reason that best describes your feelings about your departure from the company.

Note: You can provide additional information if you feel it's necessary but keep in mind that this form is not meant to be a comprehensive document, and you're encouraged to keep it brief and to the point. Remember, your input is valuable to us, and we're here to listen.
