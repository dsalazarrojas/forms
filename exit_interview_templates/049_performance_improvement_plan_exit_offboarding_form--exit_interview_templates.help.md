# performance_improvement_plan_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an employee's exit interview and performance improvement plan during offboarding.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by filling out the "Company Name" field, which should be the name of the company where the employee is leaving.
*   Next, fill out the "Supervisor Name" field with the supervisor's name who is conducting the exit interview and gathering feedback.
*   The "Start Date" and "End Date" fields should be filled out with the dates when the employee started and will end their employment with the company.
*   In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or reasons for the employee's departure.
*   Select a rating (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate the employee's overall performance and satisfaction with their job.
*   In the "Feedback" field, provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
*   The "Action Items" field is for listing specific actions or steps that the employee believes should be taken to address any issues that may have led to their decision to leave.
*   The "Supervisor Comments" field is for the supervisor to provide their feedback and comments on the employee's performance and any issues that may have arisen.
*   In the "Employee Name" field, fill out the name of the employee who is leaving.
*   Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee E-Mail" field.
*   The "Supervisor E-Mail" field should be filled out with the supervisor's email address.
*   In the "Exit Date" field, fill out the date when the employee will be leaving the company.
*   The "Reason" field is for the employee to provide any additional reasons for their departure.
*   The "Action Items List" field is for selecting specific action items that the employee believes should be taken to address any issues that may have led to their decision to leave.
*   The "Action Items Comments" field is for providing any additional comments on the action items selected.
*   Finally, the "Supervisor Signature" field is for the supervisor to sign and date the form, confirming that they have reviewed and discussed the employee's performance and exit interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the company where the employee is leaving.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): This field should be filled out with the supervisor's name who is conducting the exit interview and gathering feedback.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date when the employee started their employment with the company.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): This field should be filled out with the date when the employee will end their employment with the company.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or reasons for the employee's departure.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting a rating (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate the employee's overall performance and satisfaction with their job.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): This field is for providing any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
*   **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required): This field is for listing specific actions or steps that the employee believes should be taken to address any issues that may have led to their decision to leave.
*   **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, required): This field is for the supervisor to provide their feedback and comments on the employee's performance and any issues that may have arisen.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field is for filling out the name of the employee who is leaving.
*   **Employee E-Mail** (`employee_email`, email, required): This field should be filled out with the employee's email address.
*   **Supervisor E-Mail** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): This field should be filled out with the supervisor's email address.
*   **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required): This field should be filled out with the date when the employee will be leaving the company.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): This field is for the employee to provide any additional reasons for their departure.
