# Legal Advocate Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Advocate Exit Offboarding Form is used to collect information about an employee's reasons for leaving the company. This form helps HR and management understand the reasons behind an employee's departure and make data-driven decisions to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to HR for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exit Form Data
* **Exit Form Data** (`exit_form_data`, text, optional): This is a free text field where you can provide any additional information about your exit.

### Exit Date
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, optional): Please enter your last day of work or the date you will be leaving the company.

### Exit Time
* **Exit Time** (`exit_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of your last day of work or the time you will be leaving the company.

### Reasons for Departure
* **Reasons for Departure** (`reasons_for_departure`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Exit Reason
* **Exit Reason** (`exit_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Comments and Feedback
* **Comments and Feedback** (`comments_and_feedback`, note, optional): This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or feedback to HR.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title.
