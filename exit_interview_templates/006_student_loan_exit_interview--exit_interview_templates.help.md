# Student Loan Exit Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Loan Exit Interview form is a crucial tool for students to provide information about their loan experience after graduation. This form helps the institution or lender to understand the student's post-graduation journey, including their employment status, graduation date, and any outstanding payments or arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Answer each field accurately and completely, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Loan Exit Interview Form**: This is the main form that serves as a container for the rest of the fields.
* **Graduation Date**: This field is asking for the date of your graduation. Please enter your graduation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employer Details**: This field is asking for your employer's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Personal References**: This field is asking for your personal references, including their name, phone number, and address.
* **Reminders**: This field is for any personal reminders you'd like to provide, such as any outstanding payments or arrangements.
* **Signature**: This field is for your electronic signature, confirming that you have completed this form accurately and truthfully.
* **Payment Arrangement**: This field is for any payment arrangements or outstanding payments you'd like to discuss.
