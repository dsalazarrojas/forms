# diversity_and_inclusion_specialist_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from employees who are leaving the organization about their experiences and reasons for departure, with a focus on diversity and inclusion matters.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the question "What were your top reasons for leaving?" by selecting the most relevant option from the dropdown list. If none of the options apply, you can type in your own reasons.
2. In the "what_would_you_like_to_tell_us" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the organization's diversity and inclusion programs.
3. In the "exit_comments" field, provide any general comments or feedback about your experience with the organization, its diversity and inclusion programs, or your colleagues/manager.
4. If you have any specific comments about the diversity and inclusion program, you can type them in the "diversity_and_inclusion_program" field.
5. Fill in your name as the diversity and inclusion specialist in the "diversity_and_inclusion_specialist_name" field.
6. If applicable, provide the name of the diversity and inclusion program you were part of in the "diversity_and_inclusion_program_name" field.
7. In the "manager_name" field, type the name of your direct manager.
8. If your manager has an email address, please provide it in the "manager_email" field.
9. If you have a direct phone number for your manager, please provide it in the "manager_phone" field.
10. Finally, select "next_steps" from the dropdown list, indicating what you would like the organization to do next regarding this exit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What were your top reasons for leaving**: This field is where you select your primary reasons for departing the organization. Please choose from the options provided, or type in your own reason if none are listed.
* **top_reasons_for_leaving**: This field allows you to select up to three reasons for leaving from the provided options. You can choose from the list, or type in your own reason if none are listed.
* **what_would_you_like_to_tell_us**: This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the organization's diversity and inclusion programs.
* **exit_comments**: This field is for providing any general comments or feedback about your experience with the organization, its diversity and inclusion programs, or your colleagues/manager.
* **email**: This field is for providing your work email address.
* **phone_number**: This field is for providing your direct phone number.
* **diversity_and_inclusion_program**: This field is for providing any comments or feedback about the diversity and inclusion program you were part of.
* **diversity_and_inclusion_specialist_name**: This field is for providing your name as the diversity and inclusion specialist.
* **diversity_and_inclusion_program_name**: This field is for providing the name of the diversity and inclusion program you were part of.
* **manager_name**: This field is for providing the name of your direct manager.
* **manager_email**: This field is for providing your manager's email address.
* **manager_phone**: This field is for providing your manager's direct phone number.
* **next_steps**: This field is for indicating what you would like the organization to do next regarding this exit. Please select from the provided options.
