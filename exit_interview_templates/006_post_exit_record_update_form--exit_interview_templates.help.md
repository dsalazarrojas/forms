# Post Exit Record Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update post-exit records after an employee has left the company. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the employee's departure, such as their reason for leaving, the date of their departure, and any follow-up actions that were taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review all the fields on this page to ensure you are not duplicating information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Returned Items" field to indicate whether the employee returned any items to the company.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Follow up actions" fields to indicate the follow-up actions taken after the employee's departure.
4. Enter the offboarding record in the "Offboarding Record" field.
5. Enter any comments from the manager in the "Manager Comments" field.
6. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
7. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Enter the date the employee left in the "Date of Leave" field.
9. Enter the date the employee returned to work in the "Date Returned" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Reason for Leaving" field to indicate whether the employee left due to a reason related to the company or not.
11. Select "Active" or "Inactive" for the "Exit Status" field to indicate the current status of the post-exit record.
12. Select "Active" or "Inactive" for the "Follow up Status" field to indicate the current status of the follow-up actions.
13. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow up Date" field.
14. Enter the employee's number in the "Employee Number" field.
15. Enter the manager's number in the "Manager Number" field.
16. Enter any additional reason for the employee's departure in the "Reason Prophets" field.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Follow up Actions 1" to "Follow up Actions 10" fields to indicate the follow-up actions taken for the respective follow-up dates.
