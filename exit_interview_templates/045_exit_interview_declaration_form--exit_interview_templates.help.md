# Exit Interview Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and exit information from employees who are leaving the company. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the reasons behind the employee's departure, their experience with the company, and any suggestions they may have to improve the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible when answering questions.
3. Use the provided fields to provide any supporting documentation or evidence as needed.
4. Save your progress as you go, in case you need to stop and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (exit_interview_declaration_form_1, text, optional): This page is an introduction to the exit interview process. Please review it carefully and make any necessary adjustments before proceeding.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, optional): Please provide the name of your manager, as they will be contacted to confirm your departure and provide any necessary guidance.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional): Please provide your current job title and any additional titles you may have held during your tenure.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name, text, optional): Please provide the name of your supervisor, as they will be contacted to confirm your departure and provide any necessary guidance.
* **Department** (department, text, optional): Please provide the name of the department you were a part of during your tenure.
* **Reason for Departure** (reason_for_departure, text, optional): Please provide a clear and thorough explanation for why you are leaving the company.
* **Job Performance** (job_performance, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of job performance.
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, text, optional): Please provide any comments or feedback you have for your manager.
* **Job Outlook** (job_outlook, text, optional): Please provide any information about your future career plans or outlook.
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions you may have to improve the workplace.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Please provide your email address and phone number for any follow-up contact.
* **Date of Departure** (date_of_departure, date, optional): Please provide the date you plan to leave the company.
* **Date of Last Review** (date_of_last_review, date, optional): Please provide the date of your last performance review.
* **Reason for Leaving** (reason_for_leaving, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your reason for leaving.
* **Company Culture** (company_culture, text, optional): Please provide any feedback on the company culture.
* **Job Satisfaction** (job_satisfaction, number, optional): Please provide a rating of your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager Support** (manager_support, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of manager support.
* **Company Image** (company_image, text, optional): Please provide any feedback on the company image.
* **Work Life Balance** (work_life_balance, text, optional): Please provide any feedback on the balance between work and personal life.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required): Please select one of the provided options to indicate your overall satisfaction.
* **Communication** (communication, text, optional): Please provide any feedback on the communication within the company.
* **Feedback to Manager** (feedback_to_manager, text, optional): Please provide any feedback or suggestions to your manager.
* **Exit Interview Declaration 1, 2, 3** (exit_interview_declaration_1, exit_interview_declaration_2, exit_interview_declaration_3, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback as needed.

Note: Fields marked as required are necessary to complete the form and will be used to generate reports and analytics. Fields marked as optional are for your convenience and can be filled out if you have any additional information to provide.
