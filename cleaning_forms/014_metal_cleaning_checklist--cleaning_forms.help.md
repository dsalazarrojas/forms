# Metal Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document the metal cleaning process, including the condition before and after service, surface areas, and presence of coatings, paint, or clearing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For fields with options, select the most accurate response.
3. Be thorough in your documentation to ensure accurate records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Metal Condition Before Service** (`metal_condition_before_service`): Describe the condition of the metal surface before service.
* **Metal Surface Area** (`metal_surface_area`): Specify the surface area of the metal item.
* **Metal Cleaning Process** (`metal_cleaning_process`): Describe the metal cleaning process used.
* **Inspection Before Service** (`inspection_before_service`): Describe any pre-service inspection findings.
* **Inspection After Service** (`inspection_after_service`): Describe any post-service inspection findings.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Record any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Metal Condition After Service** (`metal_condition_after_service`): Describe the condition of the metal surface after service.
* **Metal Surface Condition** (`metal_surface_condition`): Describe the surface condition of the metal item.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`): Identify the technician performing the service.
* **Metal Surface Area Cleaned** (`metal_surface_area_cleaned`): Record the amount of metal surface area cleaned.
* **Metal Area With Paint** (`metal_area_with_paint`): Select "Yes" if the metal surface has paint.
* **Metal Area Without Paint** (`metal_area_without_paint`): Select "Yes" if there is no paint on the metal surface.
* **Metal Area With Coating** (`metal_area_with_coating`): Select "Yes" if the metal surface has a coating.
* **Metal Area Without Coating** (`metal_area_without_coating`): Select "Yes" if there is no coating on the metal surface.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`): Record any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Metal Surface Condition 2** (`metal_surface_condition_2`): Describe the surface condition of the metal item 2.
* **Metal Surface Area 2** (`metal_surface_area_2`): Specify the surface area of the metal item 2.
* **Metal Area With Clearing** (`metal_area_with_clearing`): Select "Yes" if the metal surface has clearing.
* **Metal Area Without Clearing** (`metal_area_without_clearing`): Select "Yes" if there is no clearing on the metal surface.
* **Metal Condition** (`metal_condition`): Describe the general condition of the metal item.
* **Metal Surface Cleaning Process** (`metal_surface_cleaning_process`): Describe the surface cleaning process used.
* **Metal Condition Before Service 2** (`metal_condition_before_service_2`): Describe the condition of the metal surface before service 2.
* **Metal Condition After Service 2** (`metal_condition_after_service_2`): Describe the condition of the metal surface after service 2.
* **Metal Cleaning Checklist 2** (`metal_cleaning_checklist_2`): Describe the metal cleaning process and condition.
* **Metal Area With Clearing 2** (`metal_area_with_clearing_2`): Select "Yes" if the metal surface has clearing 2.
* **Metal Area Without Clearing 2** (`metal_area_without_clearing_2`): Select "Yes" if there is no clearing on the metal surface 2.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough in your documentation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or manager.
