# Turf Cleaning Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Turf Cleaning Service Request form is used by customers to submit their requests for cleaning and maintenance of grassy areas or lawns.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful service request, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the `name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4.  Choose a date for your preferred appointment in the `preferred_date` field. You can select multiple days if you have multiple preferred dates.
5.  Choose a time for your preferred appointment in the `preferred_time` field. You can select one time slot from the list provided.
6.  Enter any additional details regarding your service request in the `customer_details` field. This is optional but helps us understand your specific needs.
7.  If you have any specific requirements or instructions about your service request, enter them in the `notes` field.
8.  Optionally, enter details about your turf cleaning service request in the `turf_cleaning_service_request_details` field.
9.  Click submit to send your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **name** (`name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name.
*   **email** (`email`, text, required): Please enter your email address to be contacted for further instructions or confirmation.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you regarding your service request.
*   **schedule_appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, date, required): Choose a date for your appointment. If you have multiple preferred dates, select all of them.
*   **preferred_date** (`preferred_date`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple dates for your preferred appointment.
*   **preferred_time** (`preferred_time`, select one, optional): Choose a time slot for your appointment.
*   **customer_details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details regarding your service request.
*   **turf_cleaning_service_request_details** (`turf_cleaning_service_request_details`, text, optional): If you have any specific requirements or instructions about your service request, enter them here.
*   **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your service request.
*   **preferred_time** (`preferred_time`, select one, optional): Select a time slot for your appointment. This field is optional if you have already chosen a date in the `preferred_date` field.
