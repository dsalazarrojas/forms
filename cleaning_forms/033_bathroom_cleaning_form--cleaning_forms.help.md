# Bathroom Cleaning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bathroom Cleaning Form is designed to gather information on bathroom cleaning activities, ensuring that cleaners report their work accurately and efficiently. This helps managers and supervisors to monitor cleaning activities and provide guidance as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Staff Full Name** in the Staff Full Name field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the Email Address field.
3. Select the **Cleaning Date** for the shift you are reporting.
4. Enter the **Shift Start Time** and **Shift End Time** for the shift you are reporting.
5. Select all the **Specific areas cleaned during this shift** that apply (e.g., Public Restrooms (Lobby), Employee Restrooms (Level 1), etc.).
6. List the **Cleaning Supplies Used** that you applied during the shift (e.g., All-purpose Cleaner, Disinfectant Spray, etc.).
7. If needed, list the **Do you need to re-order any supplies?** and describe what is required.
8. If any **safety incidents or slips reported?**, describe the incident in the provided field.
9. Confirm that you have cleaned all areas according to company standards by selecting **I Confirm** in the **I confirm all areas were cleaned according to company standards** field.
10. Finally, provide your **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Full Name**: This field is required to identify you as the cleaner performing the task.
* **Email Address**: This field is required for confirmation and reporting purposes.
* **Cleaning Date**: This field is required to record the date of the cleaning shift.
* **Shift Start Time**: This field is required to record the start time of the cleaning shift.
* **Shift End Time**: This field is required to record the end time of the cleaning shift.
* **Specific areas cleaned during this shift**: Select all areas that you cleaned during this shift (e.g., Public Restrooms (Lobby), Employee Restrooms (Level 1), etc.).
* **Cleaning Supplies Used**: This field is required to record the cleaning supplies you applied during the shift (e.g., All-purpose Cleaner, Disinfectant Spray, etc.).
* **Do you need to re-order any supplies?**: This field is optional, but if you need to reorder supplies, please list them and describe what is required.
* **Any safety incidents or slips reported?**: This field is optional, but if you experienced any safety incidents or slips, please describe them in this field.
* **I confirm all areas were cleaned according to company standards**: This field is required to confirm that you have cleaned all areas according to company standards.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: This field is required to be signed by you, the cleaner, to confirm that you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or refer to the company's cleaning procedures.
* This form is designed to be completed within a reasonable time frame after the cleaning shift.
