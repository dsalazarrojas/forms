# Daily Cleaning Staff Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Cleaning Staff Evaluation form is designed to help supervisors assess and evaluate the performance of cleaning staff members on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to evaluate the staff member's performance, such as Overall Performance Rating, Cleanliness Rating, and other criteria.
2. Provide numerical ratings (1-5) for each of the performance criteria, such as Overall Performance Rating, Cleanliness Rating, etc.
3. If necessary, select multiple values under Recommended Training, Recommended Improvement, and Recommended Resources for specific feedback and guidance.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes under Safety Concerns and Supervisor Comments.
5. Enter the Date Completed and Time Completed to record the completion of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`Overall Performance Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's overall performance during the day.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`Cleanliness Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's cleanliness and organization during the day.
* **Teamwork Rating** (`Teamwork Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's teamwork and collaboration during the day.
* **Communication Rating** (`Communication Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's communication and interaction with colleagues and supervisors during the day.
* **Attitude Rating** (`Attitude Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's attitude and demeanor during the day.
* **Work Ethics Rating** (`Work Ethics Rating`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the staff member's adherence to company policies and procedures during the day.
* **Safety Concerns** (`Safety Concerns`, `note`, required): Enter any safety concerns or issues that arose during the evaluation period.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the staff member.
* **Recommended Training** (`Recommended Training`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from the available options to provide recommendations for training or development needs for the staff member.
* **Recommended Improvement** (`Recommended Improvement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the available options to provide recommendations for improvement or areas of focus for the staff member.
* **Recommended Resources** (`Recommended Resources`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the available options to provide recommendations for resources or support that may be beneficial for the staff member.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`Supervisor Comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the staff member.
* **Date Completed** (`Date Completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the evaluation was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`Time Completed`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which the evaluation was completed.
