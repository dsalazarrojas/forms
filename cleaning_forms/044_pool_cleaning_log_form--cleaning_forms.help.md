# Pool Cleaning Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Cleaning Log Form is designed to record and track pool maintenance activities. It ensures that all necessary tasks are completed and that the pool's status is accurately reflected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the pool cleaning log form.
*   Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
*   Indicate if any maintenance tasks were performed.
*   Add any comments or notes about the activity.
*   Select the person assigned to complete the task.
*   Choose the current pool status (clean, dirty, or overcrowded).
*   Finally, select the status of the form (in progress, complete, or not started).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Date (Field 2)
*   **Date** (`Date`, optional): Enter the date of the maintenance activity.

### Pool Cleaning Log Form (Field 1)
*   **Maintenance Tasks** (`Pool Cleaning Log Form`, optional): List the maintenance tasks performed during this activity.

### Maintenance Tasks (Field 3)
*   **Maintenance Tasks** (`Maintenance Tasks`, optional): This field is used to select from a list of options. Select one or multiple options from the list to indicate the tasks completed during this activity.

### Comments (Field 4)
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, optional): This field is for providing additional information about the activity, such as any issues or concerns that arose during maintenance.

### Assigned To (Field 5)
*   **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, optional): Select the person assigned to complete the task. This will help in tracking who performed the activity.

### Pool Status (Field 6)
*   **Pool Status** (`Pool Status`, optional): This field will be used to reflect the current status of the pool after the maintenance activity. This will help in tracking the pool's status over time.

### Pool Cleaning Log Form Status (Field 7)
*   **Pool Cleaning Log Form Status** (`Pool Cleaning Log Form Status`, optional): Select the status of the form after the maintenance activity. This will help in tracking progress and completion of the form.
