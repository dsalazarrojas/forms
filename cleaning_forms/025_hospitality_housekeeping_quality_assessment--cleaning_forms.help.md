# Hospitality Housekeeping Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the cleanliness and condition of a specific area or room within a hospitality establishment, such as a hotel or resort. This form helps housekeeping staff evaluate the area's cleanliness level, identify areas for improvement, and track the status and completion of the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the area or room being assessed by choosing an option from the "Checked Area" drop-down menu.
2. Evaluate the cleanliness of the area or room by selecting the appropriate cleanliness level from the "Cleanliness Level" drop-down menu.
3. Provide a numerical score for the area's or room's cleanliness out of a maximum of 10 points.
4. Enter any additional comments or observations about the area or room's cleanliness.
5. Select the status of the area or room from the "Status" drop-down menu.
6. Choose the next action to be taken from the "Next Action" drop-down menu.
7. Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the housekeeper completing the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Room Number**: Enter the room number of the area or room being assessed. (Required)
* **Checked Area**: Select the area or room being assessed from the options:
	+ Common Area
	+ Room
	+ Bathroom
	+ Kitchen
	+ Balcony
* **Cleanliness**: Evaluate the cleanliness level of the area or room:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness Level**: Select the area's or room's cleanliness level from the options:
	+ 0-3
	+ 4-6
	+ 7-9
* **Cleanliness Score**: Provide a numerical score for the area's or room's cleanliness out of 10 points. (Required)
* **Cleanliness Note**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the area or room's cleanliness.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the area or room. (Optional)
* **Status**: Select the status of the area or room from the options:
	+ Clean
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Requires Maintenance
* **Next Action**: Choose the next action to be taken from the options:
	+ Send Report
	+ Follow-up
	+ Schedule Maintenance
	+ Done
* **Completed By**: Enter the name of the housekeeper completing the assessment.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date the assessment was completed.
* **Completed Time**: Enter the time the assessment was completed.
* **Completed By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the housekeeper completing the assessment.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the assessment was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time the assessment was submitted.
* **Area**: Enter the specific area or room being assessed.
* **Room**: Enter the room number of the area or room being assessed.
* **Floor**: Enter the floor number of the area or room being assessed.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number of the area or room being assessed. (Required)
* **Housekeeper Name**: Enter the name of the housekeeper completing the assessment.
* **Housekeeper Phone**: Enter the phone number of the housekeeper completing the assessment.
* **Housekeeper Email**: Enter the email address of the housekeeper completing the assessment.

Please complete this form accurately and honestly, and remember that required fields are marked as such for a reason.
