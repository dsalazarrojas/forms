# Deep Cleaning Service Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for processing payment for deep cleaning services. It collects information from the customer to determine the payment method, amount, and other details related to the service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to choose a valid payment method and amount for the service provided.
3. Provide the customer's email address, name, and other relevant details.
4. Specify the service provider and details of the service.
5. Enter the customer's phone number and service address.
6. Select the date and time of the service.
7. Enter the total cost of the service.
8. Check the payment terms.
9. Add any additional notes regarding the service.
10. Select the type of service provided.
11. Choose the status of the payment.
12. Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method of payment for the service. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount to be paid for the service.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`): Enter the details of the service provided.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`): Enter the address where the service was provided.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`): Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`): Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): Enter the total cost of the service.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Check the payment terms for the service.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes regarding the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Enter the type of service provided.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the status of the payment. Options include 'Active' and 'Inactive'.
