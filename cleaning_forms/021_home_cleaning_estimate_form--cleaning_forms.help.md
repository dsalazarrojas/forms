# Home Cleaning Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Cleaning Estimate Form is designed to collect information from clients about the services they require for their homes. This form is used to gather details about the client's contact information, services needed, and estimated costs for the cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
* Select the date you would like to schedule the cleaning service in the "Service Date" field. This should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
* List the services you need in the "Services Needed" field. Please select from the available options:
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3
* Enter the estimated hours required for the cleaning service in the "Estimated Hours" field.
* Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
* Enter the estimated cost for the cleaning service in the "Estimated Cost" field.
* Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's name, which will be used to identify them in the service.
* **Service Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to schedule the cleaning service.
* **Client Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Services Needed** (select_one, required/optional): List the services you need, selecting from the available options.
* **Estimated Hours** (text, required/optional): Enter the estimated hours required for the cleaning service.
* **Client Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Estimated Cost** (number, required/optional): Enter the estimated cost for the cleaning service.
* **Client Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's address, which will be used for service delivery.
