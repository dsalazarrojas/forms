# Deep Cleaning Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deep Cleaning Refusal Form is a document used to record a patient's decision to refuse or decline the deep cleaning services offered by our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the question "Why do you want to refuse the cleaning services?" in the text field.
2.  Choose the option that best represents your reasons for refusing the services in the "Contact Details" field.
3.  If you wish to be contacted for further assistance or alternatives, please provide your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Refuse Reasons** (`refuse_reasons`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter the reasons why you want to refuse the deep cleaning services.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, required: true): This field allows you to choose an option for how you would like to be contacted for further assistance or alternatives.
*   **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter your name in case you want to be contacted.
*   **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for you to enter your email address in case you want to be contacted.
*   **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter your phone number in case you want to be contacted.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `date`, required: false): This field is not applicable in this form as it's not a required field for the form to be completed. 

**Tips**
*   Please fill out this form only if you have not previously accepted or declined the services.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this matter.
*   If you have previously declined or accepted services, your response will not be taken into account unless you contact us directly.
*   Please note that this form is for refusing deep cleaning services and not for other reasons.
