# Cleaning Shift Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Shift Checklist is a form used by cleaning staff to report their activities and feedback during their shifts. It helps ensure that all tasks are completed, and that the facility is maintained to a high standard of cleanliness and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the shift you are completing this form for.
* **Shift Period** (`shift_period`, select_one, required): Select the time period of your shift (e.g. Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All Day).
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person working the shift.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your internal employee number (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor (if applicable).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building or facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required): Enter the address or location identifier of the facility.
* **Floors cleaned and maintained** (`floors_cleaned`, select_one, required): Select whether floors were Clean, In Progress, or Not Done.
* **Restrooms cleaned and sanitized** (`restrooms_cleaned`, select_one, required): Select whether restrooms were Clean, In Progress, or Not Done.
* **Surfaces dusted and wiped** (`surfaces_dusted`, select_one, required): Select whether surfaces were Clean, In Progress, or Not Done.
* **Trash removed and replaced** (`trash_emptied`, select_one, required): Select whether trash was Complete, In Progress, or Not Done.
* **Windows cleaned** (`windows_cleaned`, select_one, required): Select whether windows were Clean, In Progress, or Not Done. (Note: Not Applicable if not applicable).
* **Carpets vacuumed and swept** (`carpets_vacuumed`, select_one, required): Select whether carpets were Clean, In Progress, or Not Done. (Note: Not Applicable if not applicable).
* **Break room supplies restocked** (`break_room_restocked`, select_one, required): Select whether break room supplies were Complete, In Progress, or Not Done.
* **Any Major Issues Encountered** (`major_issues`, text, optional): Describe any problems or issues encountered during the shift.
* **Equipment Problems or Maintenance Needed** (`equipment_problems`, text, optional): Report any broken or malfunctioning equipment.
* **Supplies That Need Restocking** (`supplies_needed`, text, optional): List any supplies that need restocking.
* **Any Safety Hazards Identified** (`hazards_found`, text, optional): Describe any hazards or concerns identified during the shift.
* **Overall Facility Cleanliness Rating** (`overall_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the overall cleanliness of the facility (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Quality of Work Completed** (`work_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of work completed during the shift (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Were All Scheduled Tasks Completed?** (`all_tasks_completed`, select_one, required): Select whether all scheduled tasks were completed.
* **If Not Complete - Reason Why** (`incomplete_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for not completing all tasks.
* **Handoff Notes for Next Shift** (`handoff_notes`, text, optional): Record any important information for the next shift.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_sign_off`, select_one, optional): Obtain supervisor approval for the shift.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Record any feedback or observations from the supervisor.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in your reporting.
* If you are unsure of any field, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or facility management.
