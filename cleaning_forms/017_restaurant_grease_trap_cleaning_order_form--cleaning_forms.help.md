# Restaurant Grease Trap Cleaning Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order services for restaurant grease trap cleaning. Please fill out the form with your restaurant's information and select the specific services and traps you need cleaned.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your restaurant's name and contact information, including email and contact number.
2. Select the date and time you would like the service to take place.
3. Indicate the size of the grease trap(s) you need cleaned (large, medium, or small).
4. Choose the frequency of service for your grease trap(s) (e.g. one time, regular).
5. Select the services you want for your grease trap(s) (e.g. trap cleaning, inspection).
6. Specify which areas of the restaurant you need service for.
7. Choose the status of the grease trap equipment (active or inactive).
8. Optionally, provide a description of the grease trap(s) if they have specific requirements or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Name**: Enter your restaurant's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Email**: Enter your restaurant's contact email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your restaurant's contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your restaurant's physical address.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like the service to take place.
* **Time**: Select the time you would like the service to take place.
* **Trap Size (Large/Small/Medium)**: Indicate the size of the grease trap(s) you need cleaned. This will help us determine the correct equipment and materials needed.
* **Cleaning Frequency**: Indicate how frequently you need the grease trap(s) cleaned. You can select one time or regular cleaning.
* **Service Type**: Choose the services you need for your grease trap(s). This could include trap cleaning, inspection, or other related services.
* **Service Area**: Specify which areas of your restaurant need service. This could include kitchens, bathrooms, or other areas with grease traps.
* **Equipment Status**: Choose the status of your grease trap equipment (active or inactive).
* **Grease Trap Description**: Optionally, provide any specific details or concerns you have about the grease trap(s) you need cleaned. This will help us ensure we properly address your needs.
