# Housekeeping Frequency Adjustment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from residents about their preferred frequency of housekeeping services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information as accurately as possible.
2. Choose your preferred frequency of housekeeping services.
3. Provide your date of consent for the housekeeping frequency adjustment.
4. Enter the time of your consent.
5. If you have any additional notes, please enter them in the provided field.
6. If you would like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address.
7. If you have a phone number, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Details**: This field is used to collect your name and other relevant details. Please enter your name and any other information you feel is necessary.
* **Housekeeping Changes Requested**: Describe the changes you are requesting for housekeeping services.
* **Resident Consent**: Choose one of the three options:
	+ Consent to adjust the frequency of housekeeping: Select this option if you are willing to adjust the frequency of housekeeping services.
	+ Consent to refuse housekeeping: Select this option if you do not want any housekeeping services.
	+ Consent to discuss housekeeping with staff: Select this option if you want to discuss your preferences with staff.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are providing consent for the housekeeping frequency adjustment.
* **Time of Consent**: Enter the time of your consent for the housekeeping frequency adjustment.
* **Additional Notes**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Resident Email**: If you would like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address.
* **Resident Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure that your preferences are taken into account.
