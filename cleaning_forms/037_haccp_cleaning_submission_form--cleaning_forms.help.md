# haccp_cleaning_submission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the cleaning process, including date, frequency, personnel involved, and verification details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Cleaning Date
Enter the date when the cleaning was performed or is scheduled to occur.

### Step 2: Choose Cleaning Frequency
Select the frequency of the cleaning, which can be Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

### Step 3: Select Cleaning Schedule
Choose the schedule for the cleaning, which can be Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.

### Step 4: Choose Person Who Performed Cleaning
Select the person who performed the cleaning, which can be Employee1, Employee2, or Employee3.

### Step 5: Enter Verification Details
Enter any additional verification details for the cleaning.

### Step 6: Provide Contact Information
Enter the name and email of the contact person, as well as their phone number.

### Step 7: Verify with Supervisor
Select whether the supervisor has verified the cleaning or not.

### Step 8: Add Comments
Enter any additional comments or notes about the cleaning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleaning Date (date)**: The date when the cleaning was performed or is scheduled to occur.
* **Cleaning Frequency (select one)**: Select the frequency of the cleaning (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Cleaning Schedule (select multiple)**: Choose the schedule for the cleaning (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Cleaned By (select one)**: Select the person who performed the cleaning (Employee1, Employee2, Employee3).
* **Cleaning Verification Details (note)**: Enter any additional verification details for the cleaning.
* **Contact Name (text)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Supervisor Verification (select one)**: Select whether the supervisor has verified the cleaning (Verified, Not Verified).
* **Comments (note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the cleaning.

## Tips
* Always enter accurate and complete information to ensure proper documentation.
* Ensure the supervisor verifies the cleaning to ensure accuracy and completeness.
