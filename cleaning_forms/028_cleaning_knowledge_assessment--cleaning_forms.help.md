# Cleaning Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an employee's knowledge and skills in the cleaning industry, focusing on safety, cleaning procedures, equipment operation, quality standards, and customer service. The assessment aims to evaluate the employee's overall knowledge and identify areas where additional training is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have filled in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form's instructions.
2. For select-one questions, select the option that best describes your level of knowledge or skill.
3. For text fields, enter the required information according to the provided hints.
4. If you're unsure about any question, ask the assessor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, *): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee identification number, if applicable.
* **Position or Job Title** (`position`, select_one, *): Select the category that best describes your role.
	+ Residential Cleaner: Cleaning staff working with residential clients.
	+ Commercial Cleaner: Cleaning staff working with commercial clients.
	+ Janitorial Staff: Cleaning staff working with general janitorial duties.
	+ Floor Care Specialist: Specialized cleaning staff focusing on floor maintenance.
	+ Carpet Cleaning Technician: Specialized cleaning staff focusing on carpet cleaning.
	+ Window Cleaner: Specialized cleaning staff focusing on window cleaning.
	+ Team Leader or Supervisor: Leadership role within the cleaning team.
	+ Trainee: New or junior staff member.
* **Work Location** (`location`, text, *): Enter the location where you primarily work.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, *): Enter the date of this assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the person conducting this assessment.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of assessment this is.
	+ New Hire Assessment: Initial assessment for new employees.
	+ Post-Training Assessment: Ongoing assessment after training.
	+ Annual Competency Check: Regular assessment to maintain competencies.
	+ Skills Upgrade Assessment: Assessment for upgrading skills.
	+ Remedial Assessment: Assessment to address knowledge gaps.
