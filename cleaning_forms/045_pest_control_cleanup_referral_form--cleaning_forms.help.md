# pest_control_cleanup_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Cleanup Referral Form is used to report pest control issues that need to be addressed. This form collects information about the pest control issue, including the location, priority, and assigned tool or technician. The information collected will be used to assign the task to the right technician and ensure that the pest control issue is resolved efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information so that our team can reach out to you for any follow-up questions.
3. Select the priority level of the issue (High, Low, or Medium).
4. Choose the tool or technician that will be handling the issue (e.g., chatjimmy, another tool).
5. Provide a brief description of the issue, if necessary.
6. Enter the date and time the issue was encountered.
7. Check the route (Yes or No) and whether a technician was assigned to the issue (Yes or No).
8. Select the current status of the issue (Open, In Progress, or Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (required): Enter your first name for reference.
* **Last name** (required): Enter your last name for reference.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your contact phone number so our team can reach out to you.
* **Email** (required): Enter your contact email address so our team can reach out to you.
* **Location** (optional): Provide the location of the issue for reference.
* **Priority** (required): Select the priority level of the issue (High, Low, or Medium).
* **Description** (optional): Provide a brief description of the issue, if necessary.
* **Assigned tool** (required): Choose the tool or technician that will be handling the issue.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes about the issue, if necessary.
* **Date** (required): Enter the date the issue was encountered.
* **Time** (required): Enter the time the issue was encountered.
* **Route** (required): Check if the issue is related to a specific route.
* **Assigned to** (required): Check if a technician was assigned to the issue.
* **Status** (required): Select the current status of the issue (Open, In Progress, or Completed).
