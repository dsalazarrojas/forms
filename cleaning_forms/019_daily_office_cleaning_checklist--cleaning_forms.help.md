# Daily Office Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the office cleaning schedule and tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, as it will help the office maintain a clean and healthy environment.
2. Please make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose the closest one that matches your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How often do you clean the office?**
* **office_cleaning_frequency**: How often do you clean the office? (Select from: Every day, Every week, Every 2 weeks, Monthly)

### 2. **Office Cleaning Tasks**
* **office_cleaning_tasks**: What cleaning tasks do you perform? (Select from: Dust the floors, Vacuum the floors, Disinfect the bathrooms, Disinfect the kitchen)

### 3. **Contact Office Cleaner**
* **office_cleaner**: Enter the name of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (*)
* **office_cleaner_name**: Enter the name of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (*)
* **office_cleaner_email**: Enter the email of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (*)
* **office_cleaner_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (Optional)
* **office_cleaner_department**: Enter the department of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (Optional)
* **office_cleaner_ext**: Enter the extension of the person in charge of cleaning the office. (Optional)

### 4. **Additional Notes**
* **office_cleaning_frequency_2**: How often do you perform the office cleaning tasks? (Select from: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often)
* **assigned_tool**: Enter any tools used for cleaning the office. (Optional)
* **office_cleaning_checklist**: What cleaning tasks do you perform? (Select from: Clean the office, Clean the break room, Clean the office equipment, Disinfect the office)
* **office_cleaning_frequency_3**: How often do you perform the office cleaning tasks? (Select from: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often)
* **assigned_tool_2**: Enter any tools used for cleaning the office. (Optional)
* **office_cleaning_frequency_4**: How often do you clean the office? (Select from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly)
* **office_cleaning_frequency_5**: How often do you perform the office cleaning tasks? (Select from: Yearly, Never, Rarely, Never)

### 5. **Additional Information**
* **office_cleaner_ext**: Enter any additional information about the person in charge of cleaning the office. (Optional)

### Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose the closest one that matches your situation.
* This form is used to track the office cleaning schedule and tasks, so please answer accurately and carefully.
