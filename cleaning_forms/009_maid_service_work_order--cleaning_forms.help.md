# Maid Service Work Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maid Service Work Order form is used to create and manage cleaning jobs for clients. It captures important information about the client, the service required, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Details**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any additional details as required.
2. **Service Details**: Select the type of service needed (e.g., "Cleaning", "Deep Cleaning", etc.).
3. **Schedule**: Enter the desired date and time for the cleaning job.
4. **Instructions**: Enter any specific instructions or notes for the cleaning staff.
5. **Client Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests from the client.
6. **Payment Details**: Enter payment information such as the amount due and payment method.
7. **Signature**: Enter the signature of the person requesting the service (if required).
8. **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions (e.g., "Payment due upon completion", etc.).
9. **Contact Details**: Enter the contact information for the person or company responsible for payment.
10. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of the job (e.g., "Active", "Inactive").
11. **Payment Status Details**: Enter any additional details about the payment status.
12. **Payment Terms Details**: Enter any additional information about payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required): Select the type of service needed (e.g., "Cleaning", "Deep Cleaning", etc.).
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `text`, required): Enter the desired date and time for the cleaning job.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, `text`, required): Enter any specific instructions or notes for the cleaning staff.
* **Client Instructions** (`client_instructions`, `note`, required): Enter any special instructions or requests from the client.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter payment information such as the amount due and payment method.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `time`, required): Enter the signature of the person requesting the service (if required).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Enter any payment terms or conditions (e.g., "Payment due upon completion", etc.).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information for the person or company responsible for payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment status of the job (e.g., "Active", "Inactive").
* **Payment Status Details** (`payment_status_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details about payment status.
* **Payment Terms Details** (`payment_terms_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about payment terms.
