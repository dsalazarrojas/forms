# office_cleaning_service_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about the quality of the office cleaning service provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your information as a client.
2. Rate the cleaning service using the scale provided.
3. Provide any suggestions or comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Page 1)** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant information as a client.
* **Cleaning Service (Page 2)** (`cleaning_service`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the cleaning service as either Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Service Quality (Page 3)** (`service_quality`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of the service using a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Communication (Page 4)** (`communication`, `number`, required: false): Rate the communication with the cleaning staff using a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Page 5)** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the cleaning service using a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Client Suggestions (Page 6)** (`client_suggestions`, `note`, required: false): Provide any suggestions or comments about the service.
* **Staff Performance (Page 7)** (`staff_performance`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the cleaning staff using a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Staff Attendance (Page 8)** (`staff_attendance`, `number`, required: false): Rate the attendance of the cleaning staff using a scale from 1 to 10.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Client Suggestions" field.
* If you want to rate the cleaning service, please use the "Cleaning Service" field.
* If you want to rate the quality, communication, staff performance, and staff attendance, please use the corresponding fields.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing detailed feedback will aid in improving the service.
