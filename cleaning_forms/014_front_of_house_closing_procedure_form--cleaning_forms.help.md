# Front Of House Closing Procedure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the cleaning procedures for front of house areas, which are essential for maintaining a safe and healthy environment for customers and employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the cleaning process.
2. Enter the name of the area to be cleaned, if applicable.
3. Check the types of equipment that require special attention and ensure they are properly cleaned or replaced.
4. Specify the wet areas that need to be cleaned, if applicable.
5. List the dry areas that need to be cleaned, if applicable.
6. Enter any other relevant details or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Area Cleaning (1)** (`Customer Area Cleaning`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the area to be cleaned, if applicable. It can be left blank if the area is the main front of house.

* **Customer Area Equipment (2)** (`Customer Area Equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the types of equipment that require special attention. Select all equipment that needs to be cleaned, such as microwaves, dishwashers, etc.

* **Customer Area Wet Areas (3)** (`Customer Area Wet Areas`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select the wet areas that need to be cleaned, such as bathroom, kitchen, reception, etc. Select one area that needs attention.

* **Customer Area Wet Areas - Bathroom (4)** (`Customer Area Wet Areas - Bathroom`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the bathroom area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Wet Areas - Kitchen (5)** (`Customer Area Wet Areas - Kitchen`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the kitchen area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Wet Areas - Reception (6)** (`Customer Area Wet Areas - Reception`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the reception area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Dry Areas (7)** (`Customer Area Dry Areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the dry areas that need to be cleaned, such as office, reception, stockroom, etc. Select all dry areas that require attention.

* **Customer Area Dry Areas - Office (8)** (`Customer Area Dry Areas - Office`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the office area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Dry Areas - Reception (9)** (`Customer Area Dry Areas - Reception`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the reception area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Dry Areas - Stockroom (10)** (`Customer Area Dry Areas - Stockroom`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the stockroom area, if applicable.

* **Customer Area Dry Areas - Supply Closet (11)** (`Customer Area Dry Areas - Supply Closet`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter specific details about the supply closet area, if applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure that all areas are properly cleaned and maintained to maintain a safe and healthy environment for customers and employees.
* Review the form carefully before starting the cleaning process.
* Use the information gathered in this form to plan and execute the cleaning procedures accurately and efficiently.
