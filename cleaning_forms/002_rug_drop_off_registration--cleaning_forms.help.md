# Rug Drop Off Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rug Drop Off Registration form is a form used by customers to schedule a rug drop off at a designated location. It is intended for customers who have already submitted a rug for processing and need to arrange a time and date for the rug to be picked up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Make sure you have already submitted a rug for processing.
2. Fill in the form with the required information.
3. Provide your preferred drop off date and time.
4. Specify any special handling requirements for your rug.
5. Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you to confirm the drop off appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rug Drop Off Registration** (rug_drop_off_registration, text, required: false): This field is not actually used in this form. Please disregard.
* **Preferred Drop Off Date Time** (preferred_drop_off_date_time, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you would like to drop off your rug. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD for date and HH:MM for time.
* **Special Handling Notes** (special_handling_notes, text, required: false): If you have any special handling instructions for your rug, please enter them here. This could include any specific instructions for handling the rug, such as "handle with care" or "use a dolly".
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm the drop off appointment.
* **Rug ID** (rug_id, select_one, required: false): If your rug has a unique ID provided by us, please select the correct option.
* **Rug Type** (rug_type, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of rug you are dropping off. Please select all that apply.
* **Rug Condition** (rug_condition, select_one, required: false): Select the condition of your rug, if applicable.
* **Preferred Drop Off Time** (preferred_drop_off_time, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to drop off your rug.
* **Rug Weight** (rug_weight, number, required: false): Enter the weight of your rug in pounds.
