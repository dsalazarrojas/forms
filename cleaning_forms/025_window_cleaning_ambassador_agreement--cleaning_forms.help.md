# Window Cleaning Ambassador Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Cleaning Ambassador Agreement is a form used to collect information from potential team members who wish to join the cleaning team as a Window Cleaning Ambassador. The form will help the team determine the applicant's fit for the role and ensure they can fulfill the required duties and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
*   Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This will be used as your contact information.
*   Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This will help us get in touch with you for further communication.
*   Select your **Role** in the team from the provided options (Cleaning Ambassador, Window Cleaning Ambassador, Team Member, or Other).
*   If required, enter your **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip** fields for your location details.
*   Confirm your address by filling out the **Confirm Address** field.
*   Select the days you are **Available Days** of the week.
*   Choose the time slots you are available to work from the **Available Time** field.
*   Finally, sign your **Signature** field with your digital or handwritten signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the team (Cleaning Ambassador, Window Cleaning Ambassador, Team Member, or Other).
*   **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
*   **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
*   **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`, time, required): Confirm your address details between 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM.
*   **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Choose the days you are available to work.
*   **Available Time** (`available_time`, select_multiple, required): Select the time slots you are available to work.
*   **Sign** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your agreement with your digital or handwritten signature.
