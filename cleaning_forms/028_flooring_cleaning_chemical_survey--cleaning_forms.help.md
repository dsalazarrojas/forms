# Flooring Cleaning Chemical Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from facility managers or personnel responsible for managing the flooring cleaning chemicals used in their buildings.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer the questions in the provided fields, making sure to select the correct answers from the available options.
2.  Use the provided explanations to guide your responses.
3.  Be honest in your answers and try to provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company**: Provide the name of the company or organization responsible for managing the flooring in the building.
*   **Flooring Type**: Choose the type of flooring in your building from the available options: Hardwood, Tile, or Carpet.
*   **Chemical Used**: Select the type(s) of chemicals used for cleaning the flooring from the available options: All-purpose cleaner, Glass cleaner, or Soap.
*   **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your satisfaction level with the current cleaning chemicals usage, with options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
*   **Issues Encountered**: Describe any issues you have encountered while using the cleaning chemicals, providing as much detail as possible.
*   **Facility Manager Name**: Provide the name of the facility manager or personnel responsible for managing the flooring cleaning chemicals.
*   **Facility Manager Email**: Provide the email address of the facility manager or personnel responsible for managing the flooring cleaning chemicals.
