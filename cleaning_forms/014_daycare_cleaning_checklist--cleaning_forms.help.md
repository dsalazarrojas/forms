# Daycare Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Cleaning Checklist is designed to help facility maintenance staff ensure the cleanliness of various areas within a daycare setting. It helps staff efficiently and thoroughly clean each area, reducing the risk of accidents and illnesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and mark the corresponding area as "Clean" or "Dirty" accordingly.
2. Make sure to report any issues or concerns you have about the cleanliness of any area.
3. Use the provided sections to document the condition of each area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility** (text, optional): Briefly describe the overall cleanliness status of the facility. If the facility is clean, simply enter 'Clean'. If it needs attention, enter 'Dirty' and provide a short explanation.
* **bathrooms** (select multiple, required): Check all the options that apply to the cleanliness status of the bathrooms:
	+ Clean: The bathrooms are clean and free of debris.
	+ Dirty: The bathrooms require immediate attention.
* **floors** (text, optional): Describe the cleanliness status of the floors, including any issues or concerns.
* **trash** (text, optional): Report the status of the trash bins, including whether they are full or need attention.
* **kitchen** (text, optional): Describe the cleanliness status of the kitchen, including any food, stains, or other issues.
* **play_area** (text, optional): Report the cleanliness status of the play area, including any toys, materials, or other concerns.
* **outdoor** (text, optional): Describe the cleanliness status of the outdoor area, including any debris or other issues.
* **office** (text, optional): Report the cleanliness status of the office, including any paperwork, supplies, or other concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to inspect each area thoroughly before marking it as 'Dirty'.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing any issues or concerns.
* Use this form as a guide to create a routine for regular cleaning and maintenance checks.
