# Disinfection Task Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record the details of disinfection tasks performed in a specific location. It's used by healthcare professionals or cleaning staff to report and monitor disinfection activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of the disinfection task.
2. Enter the time of the disinfection task.
3. Enter the location where the disinfection task was performed.
4. Select the type of disinfection task performed.
5. Select the chemicals used during the task.
6. Provide a brief description of the staff member who performed the task, including their name and position.
7. Enter the contact information of the staff member who performed the task, including their contact number and email.
8. Enter the department responsible for the task.
9. Enter a brief description of the location using the location map.
10. Enter the date and time of the disinfection task (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Disinfection Task Log** (`disinfection_task_log_form`): Enter a brief description of the disinfection task.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the disinfection task.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the disinfection task.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the disinfection task was performed.
* **Disinfection Type** (`disinfection_type`): Select the type of disinfection task performed (Yes/No).
* **Chemicals Used** (`chemicals_used`): Select the chemicals used during the task (Yes/No).
* **Staff Details** (`staff_details`): Enter the number of staff members involved in the task.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the task.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`): Enter any signatures or comments from other staff members.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter the name of the staff member who performed the task.
* **Staff Position** (`staff_position`): Enter the position of the staff member who performed the task.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the staff member who performed the task.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email of the staff member who performed the task.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department responsible for the task.
* **Location Map** (`location_map`): Enter a brief description of the location using the location map.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`): Enter an alternative date of the disinfection task (if not applicable).
* **Time 2** (`time_2`): Enter the alternative time of the disinfection task (if not applicable).

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed. The fields marked as "select_multiple" or "select_one" allow multiple selections.
