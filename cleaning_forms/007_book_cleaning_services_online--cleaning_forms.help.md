# Book Cleaning Services Online - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by customers to book a cleaning service. It collects details about the customer, the property to be cleaned, and the service requirements to provide a comprehensive understanding of the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide the full address of the property where the cleaning service is needed.
3. Select the type of property to be cleaned.
4. Specify the approximate square footage of the property.
5. Enter the total number of rooms and bathrooms in the property.
6. Choose the type of cleaning service required.
7. Select any specific areas that need extra attention.
8. Enter the preferred date and time for the cleaning service.
9. Choose how often you need the cleaning service.
10. Indicate if there are pets on the property and provide any additional pet details if applicable.
11. Enter any special instructions or requirements for the service.
12. Specify how the cleaners will access the property.
13. Choose the payment method.
14. Enter your estimated budget for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): We will send confirmation of your booking here.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): For appointment coordination.
* **Service Address** (4, text, required): Enter the full address where the cleaning is needed.
* **City** (5, text, required): Your city.
* **Postal Code** (6, text, required): Your postal or ZIP code.
* **Property Type** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of property to be cleaned.
* **Property Size** (8, select_one, required): Approximate square footage of the property.
* **Number of Rooms** (9, number, required): Total rooms needing cleaning.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (10, number, required): Total bathrooms to be cleaned.
* **Type of Cleaning Service** (11, select_one, required): Select the cleaning service required.
* **Specific Areas Needing Attention** (12, select_multiple, optional): Choose areas that need extra focus.
* **Preferred Service Date** (13, date, required): When would you like the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Time** (14, time, required): What time works best for the service.
* **Service Frequency** (15, select_one, required): How often do you need the cleaning service.
* **Are there pets on the property?** (16, select_one, required): Indicate if there are pets on the property.
* **Pet Details** (17, text, optional): Describe pets if applicable.
* **Special Instructions** (18, text, optional): Any specific requirements or notes.
* **Property Access Instructions** (19, text, optional): How will cleaners access the property.
* **Payment Method** (20, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay.
* **Estimated Budget** (21, select_one, optional): Enter your expected budget for the service.
* **I agree to the service terms** (22, select_one, required): By selecting yes, you agree to the terms.
