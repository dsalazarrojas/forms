# Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration Form is used to register a sustainable cleaning practice. This form captures essential information about the practice, including its location, contact details, and certification status. Please complete this form to register your sustainable cleaning practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by selecting the page with the label "Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration".
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For the "Eco-friendly Cleaning Practices" field, select "Yes" if your practice adheres to eco-friendly principles.
4. For the "Certification" field, select "Yes" if your practice has been certified.
5. Enter your registration date and time in the "Registration Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Fill in your contact information, including phone number and email address.
7. Upload any relevant files, such as certificates or documents.
8. Finally, review and submit the form.

* **Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration** (\*: Required): Enter a brief description of the sustainable cleaning practice.
