## Mezzanine Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate cleaning area from the "cleaning areas" field.
2. Choose the specific area being cleaned from the "clean area" field.
3. Select the frequency of the cleaning task from the "task frequency" field.
4. Indicate the status of the task from the "task status" field.
5. Assign the task to a user or group from the "assigned to" field.
6. Add any comments or notes about the task in the "comments" field.
7. Specify the frequency of the task due date from the "task frequency due" field.
8. Indicate the status of the task due date from the "task status due" field.

* **Mezzanine Section** (`Mezzanine Section`, `note`, optional): A section header for the mezzanine area.

* **task frequency** (`task frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify how often the cleaning task is performed. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly.
* **task status** (`task status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the current status of the cleaning task. Options: Complete, Incomplete, Not Applicable.
