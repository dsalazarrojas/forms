# Gym Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cleaning Checklist is designed to help gym staff and management ensure a safe and clean environment for gym users. This form collects information on various aspects of gym maintenance, including floor and surface cleaning, bathroom and equipment cleaning, and emergency response procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have accurate and comprehensive information.
2. For each field, provide a detailed description of the current status of the corresponding area or task.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask a supervisor or manager for clarification.
4. Submit the completed form to ensure prompt attention to any cleaning or maintenance issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Floor Cleaning** (1): Provide a description of the condition of the gym floors, including any stains, spills, or other issues that need attention.
* **Bathroom Cleaning** (2): Describe the cleanliness of the bathroom facilities, including sinks, toilets, showers, and mirrors.
* **Equipment Cleaning** (3): Report the condition of exercise equipment, including weights, machines, and other appliances.
* **First Aid Station** (4): Check the availability and condition of first aid kits and equipment.
* **Trash Cans** (5): Report on the state of trash cans, including overflowing or unclean conditions.
* **Locker Room** (6): Evaluate the cleanliness and organization of locker rooms.
* **Restrooms** (7): Assess the overall condition of restrooms, including toilet facilities and hygiene.
* **Gym Equipment** (8): Examine the condition and cleanliness of exercise equipment, including treadmills, bicycles, and free weights.
* **Floors and Surfaces** (9): Inspect the cleanliness and condition of floors, including mats, rubber flooring, or other surfaces.
* **Mirrors and Windows** (10): Check the cleanliness and clarity of mirrors and windows.
* **Electrical Panels** (11): Report on the condition and safety of electrical panels and wiring.
* **Fire Safety** (12): Review the fire safety procedures and equipment in place.
* **Ventilation Systems** (13): Evaluate the effectiveness and maintenance of ventilation systems.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (14): Inspect the location and condition of fire extinguishers.
* **Safety Signs** (15): Check the presence and condition of safety signs, including emergency exit signs.
* **Emergency Exit** (16): Evaluate the safety and accessibility of emergency exits.
* **Gym Entrances** (17): Review the cleanliness and organization of gym entrances.
* **Parking Lot** (18): Assess the condition and maintenance of the parking lot, including parking signs and barriers.
* **First Aid Station** (19): Check the availability and condition of first aid equipment and supplies.
* **Security** (20): Evaluate the effectiveness and maintenance of security measures, including cameras and alarms.
* **Emergency Contact** (21): Provide contact information for emergency situations.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (22): Enter detailed contact information for emergency situations.
* **Incident Report** (23): Report any incidents or near-miss situations that have occurred.
* **Emergency Equipment** (24): Inspect the condition and accessibility of emergency equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location** (25): Map out the location of fire extinguishers.
* **Safety Equipment Location** (26): Identify the location of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.
* **Fire Safety Plan** (27): Review the fire safety plan and procedures in place.
* **Emergency Response** (28): Evaluate the effectiveness and maintenance of emergency response procedures.
* **Incident Response** (29): Report on any incidents or near-miss situations that have occurred.

Note: All fields are required to be filled out for this form. If unsure, please consult with a supervisor or manager for guidance.
