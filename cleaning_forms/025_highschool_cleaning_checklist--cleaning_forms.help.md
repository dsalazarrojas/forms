# High School Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps school administrators and janitorial staff evaluate the cleanliness and safety of school facilities. It is designed to be completed by school personnel to ensure that all necessary areas are maintained and safe for students and staff. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the options for each field by choosing the corresponding answers from the dropdown menus or text fields. 
2.  For fields with multiple selection options, select all that apply. 
3.  Enter text responses in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Facilities** (`school_facilities`): A free text field for any general comments or observations about the school facilities.
- **Bathrooms** (`bathrooms`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school bathrooms. 
- **Floors** (`floors`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school floors. 
- **Trash Receptacles** (`trash_receptacles`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school trash receptacles. 
- **Recycling Bins** (`recycling_bins`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school recycling bins. 
- **Janitorial Closets** (`janitorial_closets`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school janitorial closets. 
- **Utility Rooms** (`utility_rooms`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school utility rooms. 
- **Mechanical Rooms** (`mechanical_rooms`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school mechanical rooms. 
- **Stairwells** (`stairwells`): A free text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school stairwells. 
- **Ceilings** (`ceilings`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school ceilings. 
- **Walls** (`walls`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school walls. 
- **Floors** (`floors`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school floors. 
- **Windows** (`windows`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school windows. 
- **Doors** (`doors`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the cleanliness or safety of school doors. 
- **Locks** (`locks`): A text field for any comments or concerns about the safety of school locks. 
- **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`): A select one field with options for Fire Extinguisher, Fire Alarm, Fire Sprinkler, and Fire Hydrant, indicating the presence of fire safety equipment. 
- **Emergency Exit Lights** (`emergency_exit_lights`): A select multiple field for indicating if exit lights are present in the school. 
- **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`): A select multiple field for indicating if fire drills are conducted regularly. 
- **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): A select multiple field for indicating if emergency phones are present in the school. 
- **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`): A select multiple field for indicating if exit signs are present in the school. 
- **Emergency Exit Signs Color** (`emergency_exit_signs_color`): A select multiple field for choosing the color of the exit signs, with options Red, Blue, and Yellow. 
- **Emergency Exit Lights Color** (`emergency_exit_lights_color`): A select multiple field for choosing the color of the exit lights, with options Red, Blue, and Yellow. 
- **Emergency Exit Signs Color2** (`emergency_exit_signs_color2`): A select multiple field for choosing the color of the second exit sign, with options Red, Blue, and Yellow. 
- **Emergency Exit Signs Color3** (`emergency_exit_signs_color3`): A select multiple field for choosing the color of the third exit sign, with options Red, Blue, and Yellow. 
- **Emergency Exit Signs Color4** (`emergency_exit_signs_color4`): A select multiple field for choosing the color of the fourth exit sign, with options Red, Blue, and Yellow.

## Tips
- Please complete this form thoroughly and honestly to ensure that school facilities are safe and clean for students and staff. 
- This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external publication or sharing. 
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the school administration or the form author.
