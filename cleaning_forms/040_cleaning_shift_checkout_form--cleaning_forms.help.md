# Cleaning Shift Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Shift Checkout Form is used to capture information about the cleaning shift, including the shift timing, staff involved, facility status, and completion summary. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are documented and communicated to the next shift, and for supervisor approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete the shift information section with the date and time of checkout.
2.  Fill out the staff information section with your name, ID, and supervisor's name (if applicable).
3.  Enter the facility information, including the facility name and location.
4.  Review the completion summary section to report the completion status of assigned tasks.
5.  Describe any incomplete tasks and the reason for their incompleteness.
6.  Report the equipment status, including any issues or problems.
7.  Indicate if you have equipment that needs restocking or returning.
8.  Complete the facility status section, including cleanliness and equipment functionality.
9.  Report any incidents or safety issues that occurred during the shift.
10.  Provide details about any property damage found.
11.  Add notes for the next shift.
12.  Obtain supervisor approval by selecting the appropriate option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Checkout Date (id: 2)**: Enter the date you are checking out.
*   **Checkout Time (id: 3)**: Enter the time you are checking out.
*   **Shift Period (id: 4)**: Select the shift period you are checking out from (e.g., Early Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All Day).
*   **Staff Member Name (id: 6)**: Enter your name as the staff member checking out.
*   **Employee ID (id: 7)**: Enter your internal employee ID number (if applicable).
*   **Supervisor Name (id: 8)**: Enter the name of your supervisor on duty (if applicable).
*   **Facility Name (id: 10)**: Enter the name of the facility.
*   **Facility Location (id: 11)**: Enter the address or building identifier of the facility.
*   **All Assigned Tasks Completed (id: 13)**: Select "Yes, All Complete" if all assigned tasks are completed, otherwise choose "Some Incomplete" or "Many Incomplete".
*   **Tasks Not Yet Completed (id: 14)**: If you selected "Some Incomplete" or "Many Incomplete", describe the incomplete tasks.
*   **Reason for Incomplete Tasks (id: 15)**: Provide a brief explanation for why tasks were not completed.
*   **Equipment Status (id: 17)**: Select "All Equipment Functioning" if all equipment is in working order, otherwise choose "Some Equipment Issues" or "Major Equipment Problems".
*   **Equipment Issues or Problems (id: 18)**: If you selected "Some Equipment Issues" or "Major Equipment Problems", describe the issues or problems found.
*   **Supplies Running Low (id: 19)**: Describe any supplies that need restocking.
*   **All Equipment Returned and Stored (id: 20)**: Select "Yes, All Returned" if all equipment is returned and stored, otherwise choose "Missing Items" or "Not Yet Returned".
*   **Missing or Lost Equipment (id: 21)**: If you selected "Missing Items" or "Not Yet Returned", describe the missing or lost equipment.
*   **Overall Facility Cleanliness (id: 23)**: Select the level of cleanliness you observed (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
*   **All Doors Locked and Secured (id: 24)**: Select "True" if all doors are locked and secured, otherwise choose "False".
*   **All Lights Turned Off (id: 25)**: Select "True" if all lights are turned off, otherwise choose "False".
*   **HVAC/Climate Control Off (id: 26)**: Select "True" if HVAC is off, otherwise choose "False" or "Not Applicable".
*   **Security System Activated (id: 27)**: Select "True" if the security system is activated, otherwise choose "False" or "Not Applicable".
*   **Any Incidents or Safety Issues (id: 29)**: Select "No Incidents" if none occurred, otherwise choose "Minor Incident" or "Major Incident".
*   **Incident Description (id: 30)**: If you selected "Minor Incident" or "Major Incident", provide a brief description of what happened.
*   **Damage Found (id: 31)**: Describe any property damage found during the shift.
*   **Notes for Next Shift (id: 33)**: Add any important information to pass on to the next shift.
*   **Supervisor Approval (id: 35)**: Select the appropriate option for supervisor approval (Approved, Approved with Notes, Not Approved, or Pending Review).
