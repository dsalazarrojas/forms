# Home Cleaning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Cleaning Appointment Form is designed to help schedule and manage home cleaning appointments. Users will input necessary information to book a cleaning session with a service provider, including details about the cleaning type, date, time, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the **service type** that best suits your needs from the options provided (General Cleaning, One-off, or Regular Cleaning).
2. Select the **Cleaning Date** and **end Date** for your scheduled cleaning appointment.
3. Choose the **start time** and **end time** for your cleaning session.
4. Enter your contact information, including **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
5. If necessary, provide additional information about your cleaning needs in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Cleaning Appointment**: This field is where you will enter the date and time you'd like to schedule your cleaning appointment.
* **service_type**: This field allows you to choose the type of cleaning service you need. Options include:
	+ **General Cleaning**: For a one-time, general cleaning of your home.
	+ **One-off**: For a one-time cleaning of your home, often used for move-in/move-out or special occasion cleans.
	+ **Regular Cleaning**: For regular, ongoing cleaning services.
* **Cleaning Date**: Enter the date you'd like to schedule your cleaning appointment. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_date**: This field is currently not necessary as the form already has required fields for start and end dates, and is optional and not required.
* **start_time**: Choose the start time for your cleaning session. Please use the format 24-hour time (HH:MM).
* **end_time**: Choose the end time for your cleaning session. Please use the format 24-hour time (HH:MM).
* **Service Time**: This field is not used in the current form design and can be ignored.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as a reference point of contact for your cleaning appointment.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach you with appointment details.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you with appointment details.
* **Home Address**: This field is not required and can be ignored.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any special instructions or requests for your cleaning appointment, enter them here.
