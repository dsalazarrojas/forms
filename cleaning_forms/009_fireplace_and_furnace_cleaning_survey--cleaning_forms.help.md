# Fireplace And Furnace Cleaning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from customers about their experience with a fireplace and furnace cleaning service. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and ensure that we provide the best possible service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit your answers. All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the more helpful your feedback will be.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)** (`text`, required: `false`): Please enter your name as it is spelled out.
* **Fireplace Cleaning Service (fire_place_cleaning_service)** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Please select one of the options to indicate which company provided the cleaning service:
	+ HVAC Company A
	+ HVAC Company B
	+ Other (please specify in the `comments` field)
* **Furnace Cleaning Service (furnace_cleaning_service)** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select all that apply regarding the furnace cleaning service:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date (date)** (`date`, required: `false`): Please enter the date of the service in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, required: `false`): Please enter the time of the service in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Satisfaction (satisfaction)** (`note`, required: `false`): Please provide a note about your satisfaction with the service.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required: `false`): Please enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, required: `false`): Please enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Comments (comments)** (`text`, required: `false`): Please provide any additional comments about the service.
* **Service Representative (service_representative)** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Please select the service representative who provided the service:
	+ Service Rep 1
	+ Service Rep 2
	+ Other (please specify in the `comments` field)
* **Will Book (will_book)** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Please indicate if you would be interested in booking another service:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Will Recommend (will_recommend)** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Please indicate if you would recommend this service to a friend:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided

**Tips:**

* Please provide as much information as possible to help us improve our service.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us provide better service to our customers.
