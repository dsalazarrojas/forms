# window_cleaning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a window cleaning appointment with a cleaning service. Please fill out the form with your contact information, service preferences, appointment date, and desired time slot to book a suitable appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "User Information" page with your contact details.
2. Select your preferred level of service from the "Service Selection" dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar.
4. Select one or more time slots for your appointment from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (text)**: 
This field collects your name and contact information. Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Service Selection (select_one)**:
Choose from the options: Basic Cleaning, Delux Cleaning, or Premium Cleaning. Select the service that suits your cleaning needs.
* **Appointment Date (date)**: 
Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time (select_multiple)**:
Choose one or more time slots for your appointment:
	+ 08:00
	+ 09:00 (not explicitly provided, but inferred as 09:00)
	+ 15:00 (09:00 was inferred from '720', which might be '09:00' in 24h format)
	+ 16:00 

Note: The 24h format is not explicitly supported, but for the sake of this explanation, 09:00 was inferred from '720'.
