# Cleaning Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the client's needs and requirements for cleaning services. The purpose of this form is to provide an accurate quote for the client's requested services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Quote Request Information** section with your name and contact information.
2.  Provide the **Date of Quote Request**, which should be the current date.
3.  Enter your **Quote ID Number**, which can be any unique identifier you prefer.
4.  In the **Client Information** section, fill out the **Client Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** accurately.
5.  For **Client Address**, provide the address where the cleaning services will be performed.
6.  In the **Property Information** section, select the **Property Type** and provide the **Property Address**.
7.  Fill out the **Square Footage** and specify the **Number of Bedrooms** and **Number of Bathrooms** (if applicable).
8.  In the **Service Requirements** section, select the **Type of Cleaning Service** and **Areas to be Cleaned**.
9.  Add any **Special Services or Extras** needed.
10.  In the **Service Frequency** section, select the **Desired Cleaning Frequency**.
11.  Choose the **Contract Duration**.
12.  In the **Special Conditions** section, indicate if there are **Pets in the Property** and specify the **Property Access** method.
13.  Add any **Special Conditions or Requirements**.
14.  In the **Quote Estimate** section, fill out the **Estimated Service Duration**, **Hourly Rate**, and **Cost Per Service**.
15.  Provide the **Estimated Monthly Cost** based on the selected frequency.
16.  Finally, specify the **Quote Validity Period** and add any **Additional Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Date of Quote Request**: This is the date when you requested the quote. Please ensure it's the current date.
*   **Quote ID Number**: A unique identifier for internal reference. You can enter any value you prefer.
*   **Client Name**: The full name of the prospective client.
*   **Client Company**: If the client has a company, provide it here.
*   **Email Address**: The email address of the client for sending the quote.
*   **Phone Number**: The phone number of the client for communication.
*   **Client Address**: The address where the cleaning services will be performed.
*   **Property Type**: The type of property, such as Single Family Home, Apartment, etc.
*   **Property Address**: The address of the property where cleaning will take place.
*   **Square Footage**: The approximate size of the property in square feet.
*   **Number of Bedrooms**: The number of bedrooms in the property (if applicable).
*   **Number of Bathrooms**: The number of bathrooms in the property (if applicable).
*   **Type of Cleaning Service**: The type of cleaning service required, such as Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, etc.
*   **Areas to be Cleaned**: Select all applicable areas to be cleaned, such as Kitchen, Bathrooms, etc.
*   **Special Services or Extras**: Any additional services needed beyond the standard cleaning.
*   **Desired Cleaning Frequency**: The frequency of cleaning required, such as One-Time Service, Weekly, etc.
*   **Contract Duration**: The length of the proposed contract, such as 3 Months, 6 Months, etc.
*   **Pets in the Property**: Indicate if there are pets in the property.
*   **Property Access**: Specify how the cleaning crew will access the property.
*   **Special Conditions or Requirements**: Any specific needs or concerns of the client.
*   **Estimated Service Duration**: The estimated hours required for the service.
*   **Hourly Rate**: The cost per hour for the service.
*   **Cost Per Service**: The total cost for each service.
*   **Estimated Monthly Cost**: The estimated monthly cost based on the selected frequency.
*   **Quote Validity Period**: The length of time the quote is valid, such as 7 Days, 14 Days, etc.
*   **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information about the quote or request.
