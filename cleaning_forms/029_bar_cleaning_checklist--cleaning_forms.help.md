# Bar Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a comprehensive bar cleaning and sanitation check, ensuring that the bar meets cleanliness and safety standards. It covers various aspects of the bar, including the counter, sink, glasses, mixers, and equipment, as well as restrooms and overall cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the date of the check.
2. Select the shift type (Opening, Mid, or Closing).
3. Enter the name of the staff member performing the check.
4. Enter the staff ID or employee number of the staff member (optional).
5. Enter the name of the manager on duty.
6. For each field, select the corresponding status (True, False, or Partial) and provide any additional notes if necessary.
7. Enter any equipment issues in the "Equipment Issues" field.
8. Provide an overall cleanliness status and any action items or issues to address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Date** (`checklist_date`): The date of the bar cleaning and sanitation check.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`): The type of shift (Opening, Mid, or Closing).
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`): The name of the staff member performing the check.
* **Staff ID or Employee Number** (`staff_member_id`): The staff ID or employee number of the staff member (optional).
* **Manager on Duty** (`manager_name`): The name of the supervising manager.
* **Bar Counter Cleaned and Sanitized** (`bar_counter_cleaned`): Whether the bar counter has been cleaned and sanitized.
* **Bar Counter - Notes** (`bar_counter_notes`): Any notes or issues with the bar counter.
* **Glass Washer Checked and Filled** (`glass_washer_checked`): Whether the glass washer has been checked and filled.
* **Bar Sink Cleaned and Stocked** (`bar_sink_cleaned`): Whether the bar sink has been cleaned and stocked.
* **Bottles Restocked and Organized** (`bottles_restocked`): Whether bottles have been restocked and organized.
* **Liquor Bottles - Check for Damage** (`liquor_bottles_checked`): Check for any damage on liquor bottles.
* **Draft Beer Lines Cleaned** (`draft_beer_lines_cleaned`): Whether draft beer lines have been cleaned.
* **Taps and Handles Polished** (`taps_polished`): Whether taps and handles have been polished.
* **Ice Bin Cleaned and Stocked** (`ice_bin_cleaned`): Whether the ice bin has been cleaned and stocked.
* **Garnishes Prepared and Fresh** (`garnish_prepared`): Whether garnishes have been prepared and are fresh.
* **Mixer Bottles Restocked** (`mixer_bottles_restocked`): Whether mixer bottles have been restocked.
* **Floor Swept and Mopped** (`floor_swept_mopped`): Whether the floor has been swept and mopped.
* **Bar Stools Cleaned** (`bar_stools_cleaned`): Whether bar stools have been cleaned.
* **Trash Removed and Disposed** (`trash_removed`): Whether trash has been removed and disposed of.
* **Cutting Boards Sanitized** (`cutting_boards_sanitized`): Whether cutting boards have been sanitized.
* **All Bar Equipment Functioning** (`bar_equipment_check`): Whether all bar equipment is functioning correctly.
* **Equipment Issues** (`equipment_issues`): Any issues with bar equipment.
* **Restroom Checked** (`restroom_checked`): Whether the restroom has been checked and cleaned.
* **Overall Cleanliness Status** (`overall_status`): The overall cleanliness status of the bar (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs Attention, Critical Issues).
* **Action Items or Issues to Address** (`action_items`): Any issues or action items to address.
