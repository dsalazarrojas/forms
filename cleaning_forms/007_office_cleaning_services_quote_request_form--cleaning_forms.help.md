# office_cleaning_services_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office_cleaning_services_quote_request_form is a tool for submitting a quote request or service request for office cleaning services. This form helps our team gather the required information to provide you with an accurate quote or service plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information (contact_person, contact_email, contact_phone) to help us get in touch with you.
2. Choose the type of service you require (cleaning, janitorial, etc.).
3. Specify the areas you need cleaned (kitchen, office, etc.).
4. Select the frequency of the service (daily, weekly, etc.).
5. Provide the start and end dates of the service (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional information or comments you might have.
7. Choose the type of submission (quote or service request).
8. Fill in the form submission date and time (automatically populated).
9. Add any comments or notes about your submission.
10. Provide your email and phone number for follow-up (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Info
* **Contact Person**: <em>Text input for the person responsible for submitting this form.</em>
* **Contact E-mail**: <em>Email address of the person responsible for submitting this form.</em>
* **Contact Phone**: <em>Phone number of the person responsible for submitting this form.</em>

### Service Details
* **Service Type**: <em>Select the type of service you require (cleaning, janitorial, etc.).</em>
* **Service Area**: <em>Select all areas that require cleaning (kitchen, office, etc.).</em>
* **Service Frequency**: <em>Choose the frequency of the service (daily, weekly, etc.).</em>
* **Service Start Date**: <em>Start date of the service (if applicable).</em>
* **Service End Date**: <em>End date of the service (if applicable).</em>
* **Service Duration**: <em>Duration of the service (in minutes or hours, if applicable).</em>

### Submission Details
* **Additional Info**: <em>Any additional comments or information about your request.</em>
* **Comments**: <em>Any other comments or notes about your submission.</em>
* **Form Submission Type**: <em>Type of submission (quote or service request).</em>
* **Form Submission Date**: <em>Date of the submission (automatically populated).</em>
* **Form Submission Time**: <em>Time of the submission (automatically populated).</em>
* **Form Submission Comment**: <em>Any comments about your submission.</em>
* **Form Submission Email**: <em>Email address of the person submitting this form.</em>
* **Form Submission Phone**: <em>Phone number of the person submitting this form.</em>
* **Service Status**: <em>Status of the service (pending, in progress, completed, canceled, or rescheduled).</em>

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately fill out all required fields to ensure our team can efficiently process your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at your contact information provided.
* Our team will be in touch with you shortly after submission with a quote or service plan.
