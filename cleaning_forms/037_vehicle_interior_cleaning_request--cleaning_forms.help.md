# Vehicle Interior Cleaning Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Interior Cleaning Request form is designed to collect information from customers requesting cleaning services for their vehicle interior. This form helps us understand the specific services they require, making it easier to serve them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of service required: Choose between Exterior Cleaning, Interior Cleaning, or Both from the "Service" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the Date and Start Time of the appointment: Select a date and start time that suits you best.
3.  Select the Location of the service: Choose one of the three available options (Service Location 1, Service Location 2, or Service Location 3).
4.  Specify your Service Preference: Select Wet Cleaning, Dry Cleaning, or Special Requests from the options provided.
5.  Add any Comments: If you have any additional information or requests, you can include them in the Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service (E)** (`service_request`, Select One, Optional):
    The "Service" dropdown menu allows you to choose the type of cleaning service you require for your vehicle. Select between Exterior Cleaning, Interior Cleaning, or Both to ensure that our team provides the best service for your needs.
*   **Date of Appointment (E)** (`appointment_date`, Date, Optional):
    Please select a date for the appointment. This helps us schedule the service at your preferred time.
*   **Start Time (E)** (`start_time`, Time, Optional):
    Choose the start time for the appointment. This ensures that our team arrives at the scheduled time.
*   **Location (E)** (`location_id`, Select One, Optional):
    Select the location where the service will be performed. Choose from one of the three available options to ensure that our team knows exactly where to go.
*   **Service Preference (E)** (`service_preference`, Select Multiple, Optional):
    If you have any special requests or preferences for your vehicle cleaning service, select Wet Cleaning, Dry Cleaning, or Special Requests from the options provided.
*   **Comments (E)** (`comments`, Text, Optional):
    If you have any additional information or requests, you can include them in the Comments section. This helps our team provide the best possible service.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the date and time you select are accurate and feasible for your schedule.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them in the Comments section.
*   If you need to make any changes to your appointment, please contact our team directly.
