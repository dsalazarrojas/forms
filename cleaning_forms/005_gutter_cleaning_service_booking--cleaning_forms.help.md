# gutter_cleaning_service_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a gutter cleaning service. Please provide your contact information and service details to schedule a booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section.
2. Select the service type and preferred time for the cleaning service in the Service Information section.
3. Enter the address where the service will be performed.
4. Provide additional service details, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact number to reach you for any further instructions or confirmations.
- **Service Information** (`service_information`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the service you would like to book (e.g., gutter cleaning).
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the service will be performed. This can be your home, office, or any other location.
- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose when you would like the service to be performed. Select 'Yes' if you have a specific time in mind, 'No' if you are flexible.
- **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the service will be performed. This can be your home, office, or any other location.
- **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like the service to be performed.
- **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like the service to be performed.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of service you would like to book. Options are gutter cleaning, gutters and downspouts, and other (please specify).
- **Service Address 2** (`service_address_2`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the service will be performed. This can be your home, office, or any other location.
- **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the service you would like to book (e.g., any special instructions or notes).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check that the address is accurate and correct.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the service details field.
