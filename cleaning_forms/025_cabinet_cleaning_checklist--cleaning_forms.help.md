# Cabinet Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cabinet Cleaning Checklist is a form used by staff members to record the cleaning activities performed on cabinets. This form helps track the cleanliness of cabinets, ensuring they are properly maintained and meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the staff member performing the cleaning task.
2. Enter the date of the cleaning activity.
3. Identify the cabinet location (e.g., room or area).
4. Check if all surfaces were cleaned (e.g., doors, shelves, handles).
5. If applicable, indicate the disinfectant used and its product name or code.
6. Describe any condition after cleaning (e.g., damage or stains).
7. If required, enter the next scheduled cleaning date.
8. Leave the supervisor's signature reference field for their review or verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleaner name**: Enter your name as the staff member completing the cleaning task.
* **Cleaning date**: Enter the date of the cleaning activity using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Cabinet location**: Identify the location of the cleaned cabinet, such as a specific room or area.
* **Surfaces cleaned**: Check the box if all surfaces were cleaned (e.g., doors, shelves, handles).
* **Disinfectant used (if applicable)**: If a disinfectant was used, enter its product name or code.
* **Condition after cleaning**: Describe any damage or stains found after cleaning.
* **Next scheduled cleaning date (if required)**: Enter the next recommended cleaning date.
* **Supervisor signature reference**: Leave a note about the supervisor's signature for review or verification.

## Tips
- Always complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure reliable record keeping.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, consult with your supervisor or colleagues.
- This form is a crucial tool for maintaining a clean and safe environment; be diligent in its use.
