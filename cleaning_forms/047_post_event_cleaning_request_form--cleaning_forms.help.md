# Post Event Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information for post-event cleaning services. It helps us understand your event details, preferred cleaning times, and specific service requirements to ensure that your event area is cleaned efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with a brief description of your event.
2. Select your preferred cleaning time from the Preferred Time dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type(s) of cleaning services needed (Basic, Full, or Deep).
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, contact person's name, email address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Required/Optional)**: Briefly describe your event to help us understand the scope and complexity of the cleaning task.
* **Preferred Time (Optional)**: Choose your preferred time for cleaning. If no time is selected, we will clean at our standard hours.
* **Service Needs (Required/Optional)**: Select the type(s) of cleaning services required (Basic, Full, or Deep).
* **Contact Person (Required/Optional)**: Provide the name of the person in charge or responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email (Required/Optional)**: Enter the email address where you can be reached for updates or questions.
* **Contact Phone (Required/Optional)**: Enter your contact phone number for quick updates or confirmations.
* **Contact Email (Required/Optional)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates or confirmations.
* **Contact Phone (Required/Optional)**: Enter a valid phone number for quick updates or confirmations.
