# Workstation Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in ensuring that workstations are properly cleaned and sanitized to maintain a healthy and safe working environment. By answering this form, you will be able to track the cleaning frequency and methods used for different areas around the workstation, including the equipment, surfaces, and floors.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the areas that need to be cleaned or sanitized on each page.
2. Choose the frequency of cleaning (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly) for each area.
3. Choose the cleaning method (e.g., hand sanitizer, disinfectant, cleaning solution) for each area.
4. Enter the location of the trash can.
5. Select the cleaning supplies needed.
6. Answer questions about the areas around the equipment that need to be cleaned or dusted.
7. Answer questions about the frequency of cleaning or dusting for each area.
8. Select the cleaning method for each area.
9. Answer questions about the cleaning frequency and method for the workstation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Cleaning Checklist** (`workstation_cleaning_checklist`, select_multiple): This field allows you to select which areas of the workstation need to be cleaned or sanitized, such as "Yes" or "No" for each area.
*   **Area to be Cleaned** (`area_to_be_cleaned`, select_one): Select the area to be cleaned from the options "Floor", "Work Surface", or "Equipment".
*   **How often** (`cleaning_frequency`, select_multiple): Choose the frequency of cleaning for the selected area from the options "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
*   **Cleaning Method** (`cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method to be used for the selected area from the options "Hand Sanitizer", "Disinfectant", or "Cleaning Solution".
*   **Where is the trash can located** (`trash_can_location`, text): Enter the location of the trash can.
*   **Cleaning Supplies Needed** (`cleaning_supplies`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning supplies needed from the options "Hand Sanitizer", "Disinfectant", or "Cleaning Solution".
*   **Area Around Equipment** (`clean_area_around_equipment`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for cleaning the area around equipment.
*   **Area Under Equipment** (`clean_area_under_equipment`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for cleaning the area under equipment.
*   **Area Behind Equipment** (`clean_area_behind_equipment`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for cleaning the area behind equipment.
*   **Area In Front of Equipment** (`clean_area_in_front_of_equipment`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for cleaning the area in front of equipment.
*   **Area to be Dusted** (`area_to_be_dusted`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for dusting the area.
*   **Dust Frequency** (`dust_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency of dusting from the options "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
*   **Dust Method** (`dust_method`, select_multiple): Select the dusting method to be used from the options "Dusting Cloth" or "Damp Cloth".
*   **Clean Workstation Frequency** (`clean_workstation_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency of cleaning the workstation from the options "Before each shift", "At the end of shift", or "Daily".
*   **Clean Workstation Method** (`clean_workstation_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the workstation from the options "Cleaning Solution" or "Water".
*   **Clean Workstation Area** (`clean_workstation_area`, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" for cleaning the workstation area.
*   **Clean Workstation Frequency 2** (`clean_workstation_frequency_2`, select_one): Choose the frequency of cleaning the workstation from the options "Every 2 hours", "Every 4 hours", or "Every 6 hours".
*   **Clean Workstation Frequency 3** (`clean_workstation_frequency_3`, select_one): Choose the frequency of cleaning the workstation from the options "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
*   **Work Surface Cleaning Method** (`work_surface_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the work surface from the options "Cleaning Solution" or "Water".
*   **Floor Cleaning Method** (`floor_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the floor from the options "Cleaning Solution" or "Sweeping".
*   **Equipment Cleaning Method** (`equipment_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the equipment from the options "Hand Sanitizer" or "Disinfectant".
*   **Area Around Equipment Cleaning Method** (`area_around_equipment_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the area around equipment from the options "Hand Sanitizer" or "Disinfectant".
*   **Area Under Equipment Cleaning Method** (`area_under_equipment_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the area under equipment from the options "Hand Sanitizer" or "Disinfectant".
*   **Area Behind Equipment Cleaning Method** (`area_behind_equipment_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the area behind equipment from the options "Hand Sanitizer" or "Disinfectant".
*   **Area In Front Equipment Cleaning Method** (`area_in_front_equipment_cleaning_method`, select_multiple): Select the cleaning method for the area in front of equipment from the options "Hand Sanitizer" or "Disinfectant".
## Tips

*   Make sure to select all areas that need to be cleaned or sanitized.
*   Choose the cleaning method that is most effective for each area.
*   Enter the correct location of the trash can.
*   Select the necessary cleaning supplies.
*   Answer questions about the areas around equipment carefully.
*   Choose the correct frequency and method for each area.
*   Follow the recommended frequencies and methods for each area to ensure proper cleaning and sanitation.
