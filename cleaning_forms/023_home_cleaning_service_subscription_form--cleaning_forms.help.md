# Home Cleaning Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Cleaning Service Subscription Form is a form designed to gather information from customers who are interested in subscribing to a home cleaning service. It is intended to collect contact details, schedule preferences, and payment information to facilitate the process of setting up a subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on each page to navigate through the different sections.
*   Fill out the fields with the required and optional information asked.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form.
*   Click on the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer_details (Page 1)**:
    *   This field is optional, but please provide your full name for our records.
*   **contact_details (Page 1)**:
    *   Please provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you about your subscription.
*   **schedule_preferences (Page 2)**:
    *   Choose the frequency you want to receive cleaning services. Please select one of the options: weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
*   **subscription (Page 3)**:
    *   Please select the type of cleaning service you are interested in subscribing to. Currently, we offer standard and premium services.
*   **payment (Page 3)**:
    *   Choose your preferred payment method. Please select one of the available options: credit card, bank transfer, or check.
*   **confirm (Page 4)**:
    *   Verify the information you've entered to ensure it is accurate.
*   **customer_notes (Page 5)**:
    *   Please add any additional comments or requests you may have.
*   **submit (Page 5)**:
    *   This button will send your completed form to us for review.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us using the email address provided at the top of the page.
