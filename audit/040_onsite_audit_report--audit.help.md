# onsite_audit_report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The onsite_audit_report form is a document used to capture information about on-site audits conducted by auditors. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are recorded accurately and consistently, making it easier to track and review the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Provide details about the on-site audit, including the date and status of the audit.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about the audit.
4. Enter the auditor's contact information, including their email and phone number.
5. If applicable, add the client's contact information and the auditor's comments.

* **onsite_audit**: <code>onsite_audit</code> (text, required): Enter the details about the on-site audit, such as the location and purpose of the audit.
* **audit_date**: <code>audit_date</code> (date, required): Select the date when the on-site audit took place.
* **audit_status**: <code>audit_status</code> (text, optional): Provide an update on the status of the audit, if available.
* **audit_notes**: <code>audit_notes</code> (text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the audit.
* **onsite_auditor**: <code>onsite_auditor</code> (text, optional): Enter the contact information of the onsite auditor, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **client**: <code>client</code> (text, optional): Enter the contact information of the client, including their name and phone number.
* **audit_location**: <code>audit_location</code> (text, optional): Enter the location where the on-site audit took place.
* **auditor_comments**: <code>auditor_comments</code> (text, optional): Provide a summary of the auditor's comments and findings about the audit.
* **client_signature**: <code>client_signature</code> (note, optional): If the client needs to sign the report, this field can be used to record their signature.
* **auditor_email**: <code>auditor_email</code> (email, optional): Enter the email address of the onsite auditor.
* **auditor_phone**: <code>auditor_phone</code> (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the onsite auditor.
