# COR Compliance Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COR Compliance Review form is used to evaluate a company's compliance with health and safety standards. The goal is to provide an overall compliance rating and recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have access to all necessary information and documents related to the company's health and safety programs.
2. Complete the form by rating each section's elements, providing comments and signatures as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the company's legal business name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter the company's complete street address.
* **City** (`company_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city location.
* **State or Province** (`company_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province.
* **Postal Code** (`company_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary business activity (e.g., Construction, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, required): Enter the total workforce.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of assessment.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the auditor's name.
* **Reviewer Qualification** (`reviewer_qualification`, `text`, required): Enter the auditor's qualification or certification.
* **Section Header 1: Health and Safety Program Elements** (`section_header_1`, `note`, required): This section assesses the company's health and safety program elements.
* **Written Safety Policy** (`safety_policy`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's written safety policy implementation (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Hazard Identification and Assessment** (`hazard_identification`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's process for identifying hazards (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Hazard Controls** (`hazard_controls`, `select_one`, required): Rate the controls in place for hazards (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Emergency Response Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's emergency response procedures (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Safety Training Program** (`training_program`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's safety training program (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Incident Reporting System** (`incident_reporting`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's incident reporting system (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Incident Investigation Process** (`incident_investigation`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's incident investigation process (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Safety Committee or Representative** (`safety_committee`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's safety committee or representative (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Regular Safety Inspections** (`safety_inspections`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's regular safety inspections (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Personal Protective Equipment Program** (`ppe_program`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's personal protective equipment program (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Health Surveillance Program** (`health_surveillance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's health surveillance program (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Program Evaluation and Review** (`program_evaluation`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's program evaluation and review process (Fully Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented, Not Applicable).
* **Section Header 2: Compliance Summary** (`section_header_2`, `note`, required): This section provides an overall assessment of the company's compliance.
* **Total Elements Reviewed** (`total_elements_reviewed`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of elements assessed.
* **Elements Fully Compliant** (`elements_compliant`, `number`, required): Enter the number of elements meeting standards.
* **Elements Partially Compliant** (`elements_partial`, `number`, required): Enter the number of elements needing improvement.
* **Elements Non-Compliant** (`elements_non_compliant`, `number`, required): Enter the number of elements not meeting standards.
* **Overall Compliance Rating** (`overall_compliance_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the company's overall compliance (Excellent - 90-100%, Good - 75-89%, Fair - 50-74%, Poor - Below 50%).
* **Major Gaps Identified** (`major_gaps`, `text`, required): Enter key areas needing attention.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Enter suggested actions.
* **Corrective Action Deadline** (`corrective_action_deadline`, `date`, required): Enter the deadline for addressing gaps.
* **Management Review Required** (`management_review_required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if senior management needs to review (Yes - Urgent, Yes - Routine, False).
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the reviewer's full name.
* **Management Signature** (`management_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's full name.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have reviewed all necessary information and documents before completing the form.
- For each section, rate the elements based on your assessment, providing comments and suggested actions as required.
- Sign and date the form to validate your assessment.
