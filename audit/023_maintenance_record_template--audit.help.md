# Maintenance Record Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document maintenance activities for company assets, including vehicles, equipment, and properties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Asset ID" field with the unique identifier of the asset being maintained.
2. Enter the "Date" of the maintenance activity in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide a brief description of the maintenance activity in the "Description" field.
4. Select the type of maintenance activity from the "Maintenance Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Repair, Maintenance, Inspection).
5. Enter the cost associated with the maintenance activity in the "Cost" field.
6. Update the "Status" of the maintenance activity from the dropdown menu (e.g., Complete, In Progress, Pending).
7. Select the type of asset being maintained from the "Asset Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Vehicle, Equipment, Property).
8. Add any additional notes related to the maintenance activity in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Asset ID (Number)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the asset being maintained.
* **date (Date)**: Enter the date of the maintenance activity in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **maintenance_type (Select One)**: Select the type of maintenance activity being performed (e.g., Repair, Maintenance, Inspection).
* **cost (Number)**: Enter the cost associated with the maintenance activity.
* **status (Select One)**: Update the status of the maintenance activity from the dropdown menu (e.g., Complete, In Progress, Pending).
* **asset_type (Select One)**: Select the type of asset being maintained from the dropdown menu (e.g., Vehicle, Equipment, Property).
* **notes (Text)**: Add any additional notes related to the maintenance activity.
