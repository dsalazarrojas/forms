# Social Media Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from participants in a social media contest. It is designed to gather demographic information and social media handles for marketing and promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter the required information (email address).
3. Use the provided hints and labels as a guide for each field.

* **Participant Information** (Field 1): This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Name** (Field 2): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the contest.
* **Email** (Field 3): This is a required field where you need to enter your email address. This is an important field that helps us keep in touch with you.
* **Social Media Contest Registration Form** (Field 4, 6, 8): These fields seem to be meant for collecting different types of information about you on social media. However, they are redundant. You can choose one of them to provide your social media handle, if you have one.
* **Additional Info** (Field 9): Use this text field to provide any other information you'd like to share with us.
