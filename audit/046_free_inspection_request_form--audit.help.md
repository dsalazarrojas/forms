# Free Inspection Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting a free inspection for your company. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Company Name** of the entity requesting the inspection.
2. Enter the **Request Date** for the inspection.
3. Select the **Requested By** person who initiated the request.
4. Enter the **Contact Number** of the person we can contact for further information.
5. Enter the **Email** address associated with the request.
6. Enter the **Location** of the inspection site or area.
7. Add any **Notes** regarding the inspection request.
8. If an **Assigned Tool** is required, please select it from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company requesting the inspection.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the inspection is requested for.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, select_one, optional): Select the person who initiated the request.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person we can contact for further information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the request.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the inspection will take place.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the inspection request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool that will be used for the inspection from the dropdown menu.
