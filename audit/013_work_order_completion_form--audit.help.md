# Work Order Completion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture completion status and any relevant customer information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully with the required information.
2. Double-check your email address is correct to ensure proper communication.
3. Enter the correct completion date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the time of completion in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the customer note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Order Completion** (`work_order_completion_form`, text, optional): Enter your work order completion status or any notes about the completion.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your customer account or records.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your work order.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Completion Time** (`completion_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of completion in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your work order completion.
