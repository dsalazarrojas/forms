# Foundation Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Foundation Inspection Form is a tool for reporting and tracking foundation-related issues. It helps identify defects, damage, or concerns that need to be addressed to maintain the integrity and safety of a property's foundation. This form is designed to gather essential information from property owners to facilitate efficient inspection and repair processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and details about the inspection you want to report.
2.  Select the inspection date from the calendar or enter the date manually.
3.  Enter the property owner's name and address in the corresponding fields.
4.  Choose the type of inspection you are requesting (e.g., general inspection, specific issue-related).
5.  Describe the specific issue you would like to report, if applicable.
6.  Add any additional comments or notes, if needed.
7.  Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
8.  Select the user assigned to handle the inspection (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
  - This field is used to record the date the inspection took place.
- **property_owner** (`property_owner`, text, required/optional): The property owner's name.
  - This field is used to capture the name of the property owner.
- **property_address** (`property_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the property address.
  - This field is used to provide the address of the property being inspected.
- **inspection_type** (`inspection_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of inspection.
  - This field allows you to choose the type of inspection you are requesting (e.g., general, specific issue-related).
- **specific_issue** (`specific_issue`, text, required/optional): Describe any specific issues you are requesting inspection for.
  - This field is used to provide detailed information about the specific issue you want inspected.
- **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
  - This field is for any extra information you'd like to provide context or details about the inspection.
- **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  - This field is used to store your email address for future communication.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is used to capture your phone number for contact purposes.
- **assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the user assigned to handle the inspection.
  - This field allows you to choose the user who will handle the inspection (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).
- **form_submission_notes** (`form_submission_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes about the form submission.
  - This field is used to record any notes you'd like to include about the form submission.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the specific issue you are requesting for inspection.
- Make sure to choose the correct type of inspection that best matches your request.
- Ensure you have entered the correct email address and phone number for contact purposes.
