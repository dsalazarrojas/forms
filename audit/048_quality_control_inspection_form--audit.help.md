# Quality Control Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for quality control inspections, gathering information about the items being inspected. Complete this form to record the inspection's details, results, and any necessary corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the inspection date from the calendar.
2. Choose the time of the inspection.
3. Enter the location of the inspection.
4. Input the inspector's name.
5. Identify the item being inspected.
6. Select the status of the item.
7. Describe the item's condition.
8. Record any damage found on the item.
9. List any defects discovered during the inspection.
10. Add any notes about the inspection.
11. Report the inspection results.
12. Specify any corrective actions necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date): This field is for selecting the date when the inspection took place.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Location of Inspection** (`location_of_inspection`, text): Enter the exact location where the inspection occurred.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text): Input the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspection Item** (`inspection_item`, text): Describe the item being inspected.
* **Status** (`item_status`, text): Indicate the status of the inspected item (e.g., pass, fail, pending).
* **Condition** (`item_condition`, text): Report the item's condition, including any notable findings.
* **Damage** (`item_damage`, text): Record any damage found on the item.
* **Defect** (`item_defect`, text): Identify any defects discovered during the inspection.
* **Notes** (`item_notes`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text): Summarize the findings and outcome of the inspection.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text): Specify any necessary corrective actions to address any issues found.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
