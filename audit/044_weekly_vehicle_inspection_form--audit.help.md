# Weekly Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle's details and obtain signatures from the officer and police chief involved in the audit process. It helps to track the vehicle's condition and status on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vehicle's details such as make and year in the "Vehicle Details" section.
2. Enter the vehicle's registration number in the "Vehicle Registration" section.
3. Sign as the officer in the "Officer Signature" section, confirming that you have conducted the weekly vehicle inspection.
4. Sign as the police chief in the "Police Chief Signature" section, confirming that the inspection was conducted as per the protocol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Details** (Vehicle Details, text, required/optional): Fill in the vehicle's make and year.
* **Make And Year** (Make And Year, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make and year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Registration** (Vehicle Registration, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Officer Signature** (Officer Signature, text, required/optional): Sign as the officer, confirming that you have conducted the weekly vehicle inspection.
* **Police Chief Signature** (Police Chief Signature, text, required/optional): Sign as the police chief, confirming that the inspection was conducted as per the protocol.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information in the vehicle details section.
- Ensure that the vehicle's registration number is correct.
- Sign as the officer and police chief only after reviewing the vehicle's condition and status.
- Keep a record of all inspections for future reference.
