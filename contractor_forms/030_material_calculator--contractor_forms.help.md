# Material Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Material Calculator is a tool used to estimate the required materials and costs for a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a project name in the "Project Name" field.
2. Select a building type from the "Building Type" dropdown list.
3. Enter the length, width, and height of the material required (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Name (1)
* **Project Name**: A text field where you can enter a brief name for your project.
	+ Enter a unique and descriptive name for your project.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us to track and reference your project later.

### Building Type (2)
* **Building Type**: A dropdown list of common building types, select one that best fits your project.
	+ Choose from: Concrete, Steel, Wood, or Glass.
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us to provide more accurate material estimates.

### Material Length (3)
* **Material Length**: A numerical field to enter the length of the material required.
	+ Enter the exact length in units (e.g., feet, inches).
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us to provide more accurate material estimates.

### Material Width (4)
* **Material Width**: A numerical field to enter the width of the material required.
	+ Enter the exact width in units (e.g., feet, inches).
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us to provide more accurate material estimates.

### Material Height (5)
* **Material Height**: A numerical field to enter the height of the material required.
	+ Enter the exact height in units (e.g., feet, inches).
	+ This field is not required, but it helps us to provide more accurate material estimates.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and precise measurements to get the best material estimates.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact us for further assistance.
