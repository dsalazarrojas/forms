# spring_pond_cleaning_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spring pond cleaning request form is designed to collect information about a spring pond's characteristics in order to schedule or assign maintenance tasks. This form is used to ensure that the pond is properly cleaned and maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the "spring_pond_cleaning_request_form" field, enter a brief description of your request.
2. In the "pond_size" field, select the size of the pond from 1-10 units (e.g., small, medium, large).
3. In the "pond_depth" field, enter the depth of the pond in feet.
4. In the "pond_type" field, select the type of pond (e.g., natural, man-made).
5. In the "pond_location" field, select all applicable locations where the pond is located (e.g., park, garden, near house).
6. In the "pond_description" field, provide any additional information about the pond that may be helpful for maintenance purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **spring_pond_cleaning_request_form (Text, Required: False)**: Enter a brief description of your request for spring pond cleaning.
* **pond_size (Number, Required: False)**: Select the size of the pond from 1-10 units to help the maintenance team determine the scope of the work.
* **pond_depth (Number, Required: False)**: Enter the depth of the pond in feet to help the maintenance team determine the necessary equipment or resources.
* **pond_type (Select One, Required: False)**: Select the type of pond (e.g., natural, man-made) to help the maintenance team determine the appropriate maintenance procedures.
* **pond_location (Select Multiple, Required: False)**: Select all applicable locations where the pond is located to help the maintenance team schedule the work efficiently.
* **pond_description (Text, Required: False)**: Provide any additional information about the pond that may be helpful for maintenance purposes.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the maintenance team can efficiently schedule and perform the necessary tasks.
* If you have any additional requests or concerns, please provide them in the "pond_description" field.
* This form is intended for recurring maintenance tasks, so feel free to use it to schedule regular cleanings or other maintenance activities.
