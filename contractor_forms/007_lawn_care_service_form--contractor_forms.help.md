# Lawn Care Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for scheduling and planning lawn care services. It helps in collecting and organizing necessary information to provide efficient and reliable service to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the service in the `date_of_service` field.
2. Select the time of the service from the available options in the `time_of_service` field.
3. Enter the service address in the `service_address` field.
4. Provide your name and contact information in the `customer_name` field.
5. Add any relevant notes about the service in the `notes` field.
6. If applicable, select the service type you require in the `service_type` field.
7. If applicable, choose the areas of the lawn that require service in the `lawn_area` field.
8. If applicable, select the equipment used for the service in the `equipment_used` field.
9. If applicable, select any additional services you would like to include in the `additional_services` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Service (date_of_service)**: Enter the date on which the lawn care service is scheduled to take place.
* **Time of Service (time_of_service)**: Select the time of day you would like the service to take place.
* **Service Address (service_address)**: Enter the address where the service will be performed.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Provide your name and contact information so we can reach out to you with any updates or questions.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any notes or comments about the service, such as specific areas of concern or equipment requirements.
* **Service Type (service_type)**: If applicable, select the type of service you require (e.g., mowing, trimming, etc.).
* **Lawn Area (lawn_area)**: If applicable, choose the areas of the lawn that require service (e.g., front yard, back yard, etc.).
* **Equipment Used (equipment_used)**: If applicable, select the equipment used for the service (e.g., lawn mower, trimmer, etc.).
* **Additional Services (additional_services)**: If applicable, select any additional services you would like to include (e.g., fertilization, etc.).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the service is scheduled accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will respond to your request as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
