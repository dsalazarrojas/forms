# Emergency Care Provider Contract - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Care Provider Contract form is used to collect information from providers of emergency care services. This form helps us verify the provider's details and understand their payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
6. Enter the name of the contact person in case we need to reach out to them in the "Contact Person" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Select the payment frequency from the available options in the "Payment Frequency" field. Options include:
	* Every 2 weeks
	* Every 4 weeks
	* Monthly
9. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
10. Select your preferred payment method from the available options in the "Payment Method" field. Options include:
	* Cash
	* Check
	* Online Payment

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter the name that you are known by.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter the surname that is associated with your name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Emergency Contact Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your address so we can send you important information.
* **Prophets (date)**: This field is not clearly labeled, but it's likely meant to be "Contract Date". Enter the date of your contract.
* **Payment Frequency (select_one)**: Select the frequency at which you would like to receive payments.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the amount you would like to receive per payment.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select your preferred method of payment.
* **Note**: The payment amount field may not have a clear hint, but it's likely meant to be filled with the amount you would like to receive per payment.
