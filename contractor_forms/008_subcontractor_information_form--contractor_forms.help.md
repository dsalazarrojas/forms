# subcontractor_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subcontractor information form is used to collect and store data about subcontractors, their business contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the subcontractor's general contact information (name, email, and phone number) in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the subcontractor's business information (company name, address, city, state, and zip code) in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the subcontractor's tax ID number and subcontractor number.
4. Optionally, add any notes about the subcontractor.
5. Select the assigned personnel from the drop-down list.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete and save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. subcontractor_information (1)
This field is for entering the subcontractor's general contact information. Enter the subcontractor's full name.

### 2. name (2)
Enter the subcontractor's name.

### 3. email (3)
Enter the subcontractor's email address.

### 4. phone_number (4)
Enter the subcontractor's phone number.

### 5. company_name (5)
Enter the subcontractor's company name.

### 6. company_address (6)
Enter the subcontractor's company address.

### 7. company_city (7)
Enter the subcontractor's city.

### 8. company_state (8)
Enter the subcontractor's state.

### 9. company_zip (9)
Enter the subcontractor's zip code.

### 10. tax_id_number (10)
Enter the subcontractor's tax ID number.

### 11. subcontractor_number (11)
Enter the subcontractor's subcontractor number.

### 12. date (12)
This field is for entering the date. However, it is unclear what specific date this field refers to.

### 13. notes (13)
Enter any additional notes about the subcontractor.

### 14. assigned_to (14)
Select the assigned personnel from the drop-down list.

### 15. submit (15)
Click this button to complete and save your entry.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) and double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
