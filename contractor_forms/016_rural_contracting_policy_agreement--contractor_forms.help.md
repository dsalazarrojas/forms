# rural_contracting_policy_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a rural contracting policy agreement between the client and the contractor. It captures essential information to establish a formal understanding of the contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with the title of the contract.
2. Select the **Category** of the contract from the provided options.
3. Choose the **Contractor Details** relevant to the contract (Client, Contract, or Payment Details).
4. Describe the **Scope of Work** to be performed under the contract.
5. Select the **Agreement Period** from the options provided (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months).
6. (Optional) Enter the **Agreement Period Date** if the agreement period has already started or will start in the future.
7. Indicate whether there is a **Consent from the Client** (True or False).
8. Select who will be signing the contract (Contractor, Client, or Notary).
9. Enter the **Contract Start Date** when the contract is expected to start.
10. Enter the **Contract End Date** when the contract is expected to end.
11. Enter the **Email of the Contractor** for contact purposes.
12. Enter the **Phone Number of the Contractor** for contact purposes.
13. (Optional) Add any **Drag and Drop** notes or comments relevant to the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, Text, Required): Enter the title of the contract to identify it uniquely.
* **Category** (`form_category`, Text, Required): Select the category of the contract from the provided options.
* **Contractor Details** (`contractor_details`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the relevant details of the contractor (Client, Contract, or Payment Details).
* **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, Text, Required): Describe the work to be performed under the contract.
* **Agreement Period** (`agreement_period`, Select One, Required): Select the agreement period (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months).
* **Agreement Period Date** (`agreement_period_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the agreement period starts or ends (if applicable).
* **Consent** (`consent`, Checkbox, Required): Indicate whether there is a consent from the client (True or False).
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, Select Multiple, Required): Select who will be signing the contract (Contractor, Client, or Notary).
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the contract is expected to start.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the contract is expected to end.
* **Email of the Contractor** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter the email of the contractor for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number of the Contractor** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contractor for contact purposes.
* **Drag and Drop** (`drag_and_drop`, Note, Optional): Add any notes or comments relevant to the contract.
