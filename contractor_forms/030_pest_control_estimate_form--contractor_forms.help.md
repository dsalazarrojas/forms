# Pest Control Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Estimate Form is used to estimate the total cost of pest control services provided by your company. This form helps you to collect information about the services needed and their corresponding costs, making it easier to create accurate estimates for your customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the services required from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the location where the services will be performed.
3. Fill in the number of services needed for each selected service.
4. Enter any additional services that are not listed.
5. Add any notes or comments about the job.
6. Calculate the total cost by summing up the costs of all selected services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pest Control Estimate Form** (`pest_control_estimate_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Service Type** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_type`): Choose the type of service you are providing from the list of options. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Location** (`pest_control_estimate_form_location`): Enter the address or location where the services will be performed.
* **Number of Services** (`pest_control_estimate_form_number_of_services`): Enter the number of services required for each selected service.
* **Service 1** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_1`): Enter the details of the first service required.
* **Service 2** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_2`): Enter the details of the second service required.
* **Service 3** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_3`): Enter the details of the third service required.
* **Service 4** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_4`): Enter the details of the fourth service required.
* **Service 5** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_5`): Enter the details of the fifth service required.
* **Service 6** (`pest_control_estimate_form_service_6`): Enter the details of the sixth service required.
* **Additional Services** (`pest_control_estimate_form_additional_services`): Enter any additional services that are not listed.
* **Notes** (`pest_control_estimate_form_notes`): Add any notes or comments about the job.
* **Total** (`pest_control_estimate_form_total`): The total cost of all services selected will be calculated automatically.
