# Project Estimate And Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a project estimate and service agreement between a client and the service provider. It is not required but helps to capture essential information in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter the project estimate as a numerical value.
- Provide a detailed service agreement, including any terms and conditions.
- Enter the service provider details, including contact information and services provided.
- Enter the estimate details, including any specific costs or expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Estimate** (`project_estimate`, number, required: false): Enter the estimated project cost or value.
* **Service Agreement** (`service_agreement`, text, required: false): Describe the terms and conditions of the service agreement.
* **Service Provider Details** (`service_provider_details`, note, required: false): Provide details about the service provider, including their name, contact information, and services provided.
* **Estimate Details** (`estimate_details`, number, required: false): Enter any specific costs or expenses associated with the project estimate.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
- Make sure to review the completed form with the client and service provider to ensure both parties are in agreement.
