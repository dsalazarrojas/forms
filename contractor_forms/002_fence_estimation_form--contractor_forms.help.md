# Fence Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to estimate the cost and details of a fence installation job for a customer. It is designed to gather information about the customer, their property, and the job details, which will allow the company to provide an accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name and phone number, if available.
2. Provide the property address and any relevant details about the property.
3. Describe the job details, including the type of fence, post size, material type, and any other relevant information.
4. Enter the estimated length and height of the fence.
5. Provide an estimate for labor cost, material cost, equipment cost, and tax rate.
6. Set the date and time the form was submitted.
