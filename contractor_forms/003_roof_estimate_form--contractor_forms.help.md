# Roof Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roof Estimate Form is designed to collect essential information required for a detailed and accurate estimate of a roofing project's cost and feasibility. It is intended for clients or customers to provide project details and contractors to input their expertise and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the "project_info" page, which includes details about the project, such as its location, size, and description.
2.  Proceed to the "contractor_info" page, where you will input details about the contractors or workers involved, including their names, phone numbers, and availability.
3.  Finish by filling out the "estimate_info" page, which includes the estimated cost, materials, and timelines for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **project_info** (`project_info`, text, required: false): This field collects information about the project, such as its location, size, and description.
    *   Enter a brief description of the project, including the type of roof, the number of stories, and any specific features or obstacles.
*   **contractor_info** (`contractor_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing details about the contractors or workers involved in the project.
    *   Enter the name, phone number, and availability of the contractors or workers to be used for the project.
*   **estimate_info** (`estimate_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you provide the estimated cost, materials, and timelines for the project.
    *   Enter a detailed description of the estimated cost, including all the materials and labor costs, and a rough timeline for the project.
