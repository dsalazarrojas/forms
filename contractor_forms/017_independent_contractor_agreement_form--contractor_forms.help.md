# Independent Contractor Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a contract between a client and a contractor, outlining the terms and conditions of the contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your company information, including your name and address.
* Enter your phone number and email address.
* Select the contract start and end dates.
* Enter the contractor's name and rate.
* Choose the payment terms.
* Enter the contractor's responsibilities and work location.
* Select the payment method and payment frequency.
* Enter the payment amount and payment schedule.
* Optional fields include payment method notes, termination clause, termination reasons, and termination notice period.
* Both the client and the contractor must sign the form to confirm their agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of the contract.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the contractor's name.
* **Contract Rate** (`contract_rate`, `text`, required): Enter the contractor's rate.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Enter the payment terms.
* **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, `text`, required): Enter the contractor's responsibilities.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the work will be performed.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method (Credit Card, Cash, or Check).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment frequency (One-Time, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Method Notes** (`payment_method_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes about the payment method.
* **Termination Clause** (`termination_clause`, `text`, required): Enter the termination clause.
* **Termination Reasons** (`termination_reasons`, `text`, required): Enter the reasons for termination.
* **Termination Notice Period** (`termination_notice_period`, `number`, required): Enter the termination notice period.
* **Termination Effective Date** (`termination_effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the termination effective date.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment frequency (One-Time, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Frequency Notes** (`payment_frequency_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes about the payment frequency.
* **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes about the contract.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the client.
* **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the contractor.
