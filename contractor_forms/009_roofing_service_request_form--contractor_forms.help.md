# Roofing Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from customers who are requesting roofing services from the company. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is properly processed and followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name field with your full first name.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Provide your home or business address in the Address field.
6. Select the type of service you are requesting from the Service Type field.
7. Enter the date you would like the service to be performed in the Service Date field.
8. Enter the time you would like the service to be performed in the Service Time field.
9. Enter any additional notes about your service request in the Notes field.
10. If a specific staff member is assigned to your request, please enter their name in the Assigned To field.
11. If the status of your request is different from the default "Pending" status, please select the correct status from the Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name is required to identify you as a customer. Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is required to identify you as a customer. Please enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check frequently so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your request.
* **Address**: Enter your home or business address where the service will be performed.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Service Date**: Enter the date you would like the service to be performed. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Service Time**: Enter the time you would like the service to be performed. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your service request.
* **Assigned To**: If a specific staff member is assigned to your request, please enter their name.
* **Status**: If the status of your request is different from the default "Pending" status, please select the correct status from the following options:
	+ Pending
	+ Completed
	+ In Progress
