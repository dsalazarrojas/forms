# Subcontractor Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by a subcontractor to release a lien on a project, confirming that they have received payment in full for the work completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information. Make sure to check the "Lien Status" field to ensure it is set to "Active" if you are releasing the lien, and "Inactive" if you are not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is where you enter your first name as the subcontractor who is releasing the lien.
* **Last Name**: This is where you enter your last name as the subcontractor who is releasing the lien.
* **Address**: Enter your address as the subcontractor who is releasing the lien.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached as the subcontractor who is releasing the lien.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached as the subcontractor who is releasing the lien.
* **Subcontractor Employer Name**: This is the name of the company or individual who hired you for the project.
*   **Subcontractor Employer Business License Number**: If the subcontractor employer has a business license, enter it here.
*   **Release Date**: Enter the date you are releasing the lien. This should be the date you are submitting this form.
*   **Contract Date**: Enter the date the contract for the project was signed.
*   **Amount Received**: Enter the total amount you have received for the work completed on the project.
*   **Amount Owed**: Enter the amount you are owed for the work completed on the project. Please note this field is only necessary if you are still owed money.
*   **Lien Status**: This field should be set to "Active" if you are releasing the lien, and "Inactive" if you are not.
*   **Reason**: If you are setting the lien to "Inactive", you may enter a brief reason why.
