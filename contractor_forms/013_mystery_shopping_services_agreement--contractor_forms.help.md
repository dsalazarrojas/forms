# Mystery Shopping Services Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Mystery Shopping Services Agreement" is a document used by companies to onboard and guide mystery shoppers in understanding their role and the terms of their engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting.
2. Ensure you understand each section and its requirements before proceeding.
3. Sign the form where indicated to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is the introductory section of the form. It's not required, but it's essential to fill it out for context.
* **Introduction**: This section should contain a brief overview of your role as a mystery shopper. Please provide a summary of your responsibilities.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read this section carefully to understand the terms and expectations of your engagement. These might include confidentiality, data security, and other critical aspects of your role.
* **Assignment Details**: Provide any necessary information regarding your assignment, such as dates, locations, or specific objectives.
* **Confidentiality Expectations**: This is where you acknowledge your understanding of the confidentiality expectations of your role. Please read and agree to these terms carefully.
* **Approvals**: Sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.
* **Date Signed**: Please fill in the date you sign this agreement.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you're working with.
* **Shopper**: Insert your name.
* **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions of your engagement. This is a required field.

Please note that the fields marked as required (Terms and Conditions, Assignment Details, Confidentiality Expectations, Approvals, Date Signed, Company, Shopper, and Signature) must be filled out. The Form Header, Introduction, and Date Signed fields are optional, but it's beneficial to fill them out for context and clarity.
