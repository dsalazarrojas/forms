# Supplier Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback and ratings from suppliers, helping us improve our partnerships and ensure we're working with reliable and valuable business partners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Supplier Name** in the designated text field.
2. Enter the **Email** of the supplier in the email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter the **Phone Number** of the supplier.
4. Select a **Rating** for the supplier from the options below:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
5. If you have any additional comments about the supplier, please use the **Comment** field to provide more detail.
6. Select the **Supplier Category** (e.g., Electronics, Software, Hardware, Service).
7. Enter the **Supplier Address** of the supplier.
8. Enter the **Supplier Contact Person** responsible for the supplier relationship.
9. Select the **Supplier Rating Date** (optional, but recommended for tracking progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier you're evaluating.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the supplier (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Rating**: Select a rating from the options provided to express your overall satisfaction with the supplier.
* **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the supplier. This field is optional but helpful for providing more detail.
* **Supplier Category**: Select the category that best describes the type of service or product the supplier provides (e.g., Electronics, Software, Hardware, Service).
* **Supplier Address**: Enter the physical address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the supplier relationship.
* **Supplier Rating Date**: Select the date of the supplier rating (optional but recommended for tracking progress).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but feel free to provide additional information in the **Comment** field.
* Use the **Supplier Category** field to help us categorize and analyze the ratings across different sectors.
* If you need to make changes or updates to a supplier, you can re-rate them at any time.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
