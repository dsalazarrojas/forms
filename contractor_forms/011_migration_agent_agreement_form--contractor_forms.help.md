# Migration Agent Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture client agreements and information regarding the terms and conditions of a service provided by the form owner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing your client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Select the service terms that apply to your engagement with the client.
3. Choose your onboarding preferences from the available options.
4. Provide any additional onboarding details if necessary.
5. Review and agree to the consent terms.
6. Sign the form digitally to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`): Enter your client's name, email, phone number, and any other relevant information.
* **Service Terms** (`service_terms`): Select all the terms that apply to your engagement with the client. Options include:
	+ Agree to be contacted
	+ Agree to share contact information
	+ Agree to receive promotional emails
* **New Engagement** (`new_engagement`): Enter a brief description of the new engagement with the client.
* **Onboarding** (`onboarding`): Select your preferred onboarding method:
	+ Full Onboarding
	+ Partial Onboarding
	+ No Onboarding
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`): Provide any additional information about onboarding, if needed.
* **Consent Terms** (`consent_terms`): Review and acknowledge your understanding of the consent terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign the form digitally to acknowledge your agreement to the terms and conditions. 

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and truthful information in the "Client Details" field.
* Carefully review and select the service terms that apply to your engagement.
* Choose your onboarding preferences according to your client's needs.
* Make sure to sign the form digitally to acknowledge your agreement.
* Keep a record of the signed form for your reference.
