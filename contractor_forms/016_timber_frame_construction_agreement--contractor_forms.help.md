# Timber Frame Construction Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Timber Frame Construction Agreement form is designed to collect essential information from clients for a timber frame construction project. It helps gather details about the client, project scope, and scope of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the client's details in the `Client Details` page.
*   Provide an overview of the project in the `Project Details` page.
*   Outline the scope of work for the project in the `Scope of Work` page.
*   Sign off on the agreement by entering your name and date on the `Signatures` page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Details (Client Details, Text, Optional)**: This section is for clients to provide their basic details such as name and contact information.
*   **Project Details (Project Details, Text, Optional)**: This field is for clients to provide an overview of the project, including the project name, location, and other relevant details.
*   **Scope of Work (Scope of Work, Text, Optional)**: In this section, clients can outline the scope of work for the project, including the materials, labor, and timeline.
*   **Signatures (Signatures, Text, Optional)**: This field is for clients to sign off on the agreement, indicating their acceptance of the project terms and conditions. Please enter your name and date to sign off. 

## Tips

*   Ensure that the client's information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Consult with legal experts for a more comprehensive and legally binding agreement.
*   Use this form as a starting point and modify it according to your specific project needs.
