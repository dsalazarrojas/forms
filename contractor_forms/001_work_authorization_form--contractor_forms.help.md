# Work Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to authorize work to be performed by employees, ensuring that they are aware of and agree to the work being done. It helps in documenting the work authorization for record-keeping and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information for the employee.
2. Select the correct employment status.
3. Enter the work location and work authorization number (if required).
4. Set the expiration date for the work authorization.
5. Enter the authorized person's name and date of authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info**: Enter the name of the employee who will be performing the work.
* **Work Location**: Enter the location where the work will be performed.
* **Employment Status**: Select the current employment status of the employee performing the work.
* **Work Authorization Number**: Enter the work authorization number (if required for your work).
* **Expiration Date**: Set the date when the work authorization will expire.
* **Authorized By**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the work.
* **Authorized On**: Enter the date and time when the work was authorized.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the employment status of the employee performing the work.
* Ensure the work authorization number is entered correctly (if required).
* Set the expiration date correctly.
* Keep a record of the authorized work for future reference.
