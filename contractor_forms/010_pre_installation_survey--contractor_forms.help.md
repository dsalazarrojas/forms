# pre_installation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential contact information and project details before an installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the **Contact Information** section with the required details.
	*   **Contact Information**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	*   **Project Details**: Enter the name of the project.
2.  In the **Installations** section, fill in the necessary details for the installation.
	*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	*   **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
	*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
3.  Enter the **Company Information** details.
	*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
	*   **Company (2)**: Enter the name of the company if it's different from the first company.
4.  Enter the **Address** details.
	*   **Address**: Enter the address of the installation location.
	*   **Address 2**: Enter the address of another location if different from the first address.
5.  Select the **Installation Date** and **Start Time** and **End Time** for the installation.
6.  If required, enter the additional details in the **Comments** and **Comments 2** fields.
7.  Finally, select the **Installation Type** and **Installation Location** for the installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information (1)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Project Information (2)** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the project.
*   **Contact Person (3)** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Number (4)** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
*   **Email (5)** (`id: 5`, `type: email`, `required: true`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Company (6)** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the company.
*   **Address (7)** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the address of the installation location.
*   **Installation Date (8)** (`id: 8`, `type: date`, `required: true`): Select the date of the installation.
*   **Start Time (9)** (`id: 9`, `type: time`, `required: true`): Select the start time of the installation.
*   **End Time (10)** (`id: 10`, `type: time`, `required: true`): Select the end time of the installation.
*   **Comments (11)** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Enter any additional comments about the installation.
*   **Phone (12)** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Email (2)** (`id: 13`, `type: email`, `required: true`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Company (2)** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the company if it's different from the first company.
*   **Address 2 (15)** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the address of another location if different from the first address.
*   **Installation Type (16)** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the type of installation.
*   **Comments 2 (17)** (`id: 17`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Enter any additional comments about the installation.
*   **Installation Location (18)** (`id: 18`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the location of the installation.
