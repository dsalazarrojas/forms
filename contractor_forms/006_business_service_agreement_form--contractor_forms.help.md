# Business Service Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and clarify the terms of a business service agreement between a service provider and a client. It ensures both parties have a clear understanding of the services to be provided, payment terms, and expectations of each other.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all terms are understood and agreed upon by both parties.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name or company name.
* **Provider Email Address** (`provider_email`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Provider Phone Number** (`provider_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the client receiving services.
* **Client Email Address** (`client_email`, email, **required**): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of service being provided from the options: Consulting, Design and Creative, Technical Services, Maintenance and Repair, Professional Services, Construction, Training, or Other.
* **Detailed Service Description** (`service_description`, text, **required**): Explain the work to be performed in detail.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date when services will begin.
* **Project End Date or Duration** (`project_end_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date or duration when services will be completed. Note: If the duration is selected, leave this field blank.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, select_one, **required**): Choose the service duration from the options: One-time project, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Ongoing.
* **Total Service Cost** (`total_cost`, number, **required**): Enter the total service cost in USD or local currency.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, **required**): Choose the payment terms from the options: Full payment upfront, 50% deposit, 50% upon completion, Monthly installments, Upon completion, or Custom arrangement.
* **Accepted Payment Methods** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all applicable payment methods, including Check, Bank transfer, Credit card, Wire transfer, PayPal, or Cash.
* **Key Deliverables** (`deliverables`, text, **required**): Enter specific items or outcomes to be provided.
* **Revision Rounds** (`revision_rounds`, number, **optional**): Enter the number of revision rounds allowed.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, **optional**): Describe the policy in case of cancellation.
* **Confidentiality Required** (`confidentiality_required`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether confidentiality is required and if an NDA is needed.
* **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether liability insurance is required.
* **Project Terms** (`project_terms`, text, **optional**): Enter any special terms or conditions specific to the project.

Note: This form is a template and should be reviewed and customized according to the specific needs and agreements of each project.
