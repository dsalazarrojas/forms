# RFQ Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFQ Form is a tool used to collect information about clients and contractors. It helps in gathering details such as contact information, client requests, payment terms, business details, and contract terms. This form is used to streamline the process of collecting and organizing the necessary information for client-contractor interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information page (Page 1) with the relevant details of the client or contractor.
2. On the client request page (Page 2), select the services or options that the client is requesting.
3. Enter the payment terms and conditions on the payment terms page (Page 3).
4. Provide business details such as dates and times on the business details page (Page 4).
5. Review and agree to the contract terms on the contract terms page (Page 5).
6. Continue this process for each subsequent page, filling out the relevant information and agreeing to the terms.
