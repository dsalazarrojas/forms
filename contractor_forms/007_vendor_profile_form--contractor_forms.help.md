# Vendor Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Profile Form is designed to collect information from vendors, which will be used to create their profile and display it in a list or map.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as such in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.
2. Enter your First and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter a valid email address for contact.
4. Enter your company's name, address, city, and ZIP in the corresponding fields.
5. Select the type and industry your company belongs to.
6. Optionally, provide a description of your company.
7. If your company has an image or logo, you can upload it.
8. Finally, review your submission and submit it to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (required)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.

### Last Name (required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.

### Email (optional)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact. This will be used to communicate with you.

### Phone (optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number, including the area code (e.g., (123) 456-7890).

### Company Name (required)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.

### Address 1 (required)
* **Address 1**: Enter your company's street address.

### Address 2 (optional)
* **Address 2**: Enter any additional address information, such as suite or apartment number.

### City (required)
* **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.

### ZIP (required)
* **ZIP**: Enter your company's ZIP code.

### State (required)
* **State**: Select the state where your company is located from the list of options.

### Country (required)
* **Country**: Select the country where your company is located from the list of options.

### Website (optional)
* **Website**: Enter your company's website URL.

### Type (required)
* **Type**: Select the type of vendor your company is (e.g., Service, Goods, Other).

### Industry (optional)
* **Industry**: Select the industry your company belongs to from the list of options.

### Description (optional)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your company.

### Image (optional)
* **Image**: Upload an image of your company's logo or profile picture.

### Logo (optional)
* **Logo**: Select the file type of your company's logo (e.g., JPEG, PNG, GIF).

### Language (optional)
* **Language**: Select the languages your company supports (e.g., English, French, Spanish).

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please reach out to your organization's support team.
