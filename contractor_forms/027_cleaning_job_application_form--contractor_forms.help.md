**Cleaning Job Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for cleaning jobs. It is used to assess your qualifications, experience, and availability for work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Read each field carefully and make sure to provide the necessary information.
3. Use the options provided as a guide to help you complete the form.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### Position Information

* **Position Applying For**: Select the position you are interested in applying for from the list provided.
* **Availability**: Select when you are available to work.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are able to start working.

### Work Experience

* **Cleaning Experience Level**: Select the level of cleaning experience you have.
* **Most Recent Employer**: Enter the name of your most recent employer.
* **Previous Job Title**: Enter your job title at this employer.
* **Dates of Employment**: Enter the dates of your employment.
* **Previous Employer**: Enter the name of your previous employer.
* **Previous Job Title**: Enter your job title at this previous employer.
* **Dates of Employment**: Enter the dates of your employment.
* **Reasons for Leaving Previous Positions**: Briefly explain why you left your previous positions.

### Skills and Qualifications

* **Cleaning Skills**: Select all the cleaning skills you have experience with.
* **Equipment Experience**: Select all the equipment you have experience with.
* **Chemical Safety Training**: Select how you have been trained in safe chemical use.
* **Reliable Transportation**: Select how you have transportation to get to work.
* **Valid Driver's License**: Select if you have a valid driver license.
* **Able to Lift 50 Pounds**: Select if you can lift up to 50 pounds.

### Education

* **Highest Level of Education**: Select your highest level of education.
* **Relevant Certifications**: List any cleaning or safety certifications you have.
* **Languages Spoken**: Select all the languages you speak.

### References

* **Reference 1 Name**: Enter the name of your reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship**: Explain how you know this person.
* **Reference 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your reference.
* **Reference 2 Name**: Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Relationship**: Explain how you know this person.
* **Reference 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your second reference.

### Additional Information

* **Have You Ever Been Convicted of a Crime**: Select if you have been convicted of a crime.
* **Explanation**: If yes, explain the circumstances.
* **Eligible to Work**: Select if you are lawfully eligible to work.
* **How Did You Hear About This Position**: Select how you found this job opening.
* **Why Do You Want to Work Here**: Briefly explain why you want to work here.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments you would like to make.
* **Applicant Signature**: Type your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you are applying for the position.

Note: Please ensure that you are providing accurate and truthful information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
