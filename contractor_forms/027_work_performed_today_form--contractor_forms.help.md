# Work Performed Today Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work Performed Today Form" is a simple form for documenting work performed by a contractor or freelancer on a given day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Work Performed Today" field with a brief description of the work accomplished during the day.
2. Provide any additional information that is relevant to the work performed in the "Additional Info" field.
3. Finally, add your signature in the "Signature" field to confirm that you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Performed Today** (`work_performed_today_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the work you completed today.
* **Additional Info** (`work_performed_today_form_additional_info`, text, required): Provide any additional information that is relevant to the work you completed today.
* **Signature** (`work_performed_today_form_signature`, text, required): Add your signature to confirm that you have completed this form accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
- Make sure to be concise and clear in your responses to ensure that your work is accurately reflected in the form.
- If you have any issues or questions about this form, please contact form@gic.mx.
