# Supplier Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a supplier for our organization's records. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper supplier management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted, ensuring all required fields are completed. Please note that some fields are not required, so only fill out the necessary information for your supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Info (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the supplier, such as their name, business name, or any other identifying information.
* **Address (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's address, including street address and city.
* **Contact Phone (id: 3)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's contact phone number.
* **Supplier Type (id: 4)** (`select multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following supplier types:
	+ General
	+ Reseller
	+ Distributor
	+ Service Provider
	+ Manufacturer
* **Contract Term (id: 5)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the contract term (e.g., month, year).
* **Contract Start Date (id: 6)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date (id: 7)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the contract.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will be used to update our supplier records.
* You can save and resume your form at any time using the 'Save' feature.
* If you are a new supplier, please contact us to set up your account before filling out this form.
