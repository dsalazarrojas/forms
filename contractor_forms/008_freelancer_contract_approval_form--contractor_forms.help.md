# Freelancer Contract Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelancer Contract Approval Form is used to review and approve contracts with freelancers, ensuring that all relevant details are properly documented and approved before commencing work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Project Details" page with information about the project.
2. Move to the "Client Details" page and enter the required information about the client.
3. Review and choose the "Payment Terms" options for the contract.
4. Provide an overview of the "Project Scope" on the "Project Scope" page.
5. Add any necessary "Client Contact" information on the "Client Contact" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details (project_details)
* **Project Details**: This is the first page where you will enter general information about the project. This includes details about the project's nature, objectives, and any specific requirements.

### Client Details (client_details)
* **Client Details**: On this page, enter the necessary information about the client. This includes their name, contact details, and any other relevant contact information.

### Contract Details (contract_details)
* **Contract Details**: This page is where you will enter the specifics about the contract, such as its terms and conditions.

### Payment Terms (payment_terms)
* **Payment Terms**: This field allows you to choose whether the freelancer will be paid or not. Please select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether payment terms will be part of the contract.

### Project Scope (project_scope)
* **Project Scope**: On this page, you will provide an overview of the project's objectives, deliverables, and any key milestones.

### Client Contact (client_contact)
* **Client Contact**: If you need to keep in touch with the client for any reason, you can add their contact information here.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review and proofread the information entered in each section before submitting the form.
* If any section is not applicable, you may leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" if required.
* Ensure all necessary fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential misunderstandings.
