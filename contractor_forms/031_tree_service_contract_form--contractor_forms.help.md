# Tree Service Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to tree service contracts, including client approvals, job details, arborist information, and other relevant data. It is intended to be used by administrators or project managers to gather necessary information for tree service contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. List all clients approving this contract under "Client Approvals" (Page 1).
2. Provide job details in the "Job Details" text field (Page 2).
3. Sign with your electronic signature in the "E-Signature" text field (Page 2).
4. Select the arborist responsible for the job (Page 2).
5. Enter any notes for the arborist in the "Arborist Notes" text field (Page 3).
6. Fill in your company information in the "Company Info" text field (Page 3).
7. Select the current job status (Page 4).
8. Enter the date the contract was signed (Page 4).
9. Select the person submitting the form (Page 4).
10. Enter the date the form was submitted (Page 5).
11. Select the person approving the contract (Page 5).
12. Enter the date the contract was approved (Page 5).
