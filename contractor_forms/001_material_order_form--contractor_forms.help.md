# material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material_order_form is used to collect information for material orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Company.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Enter your Location.
7. Select the Material Type.
8. Enter the Quantity of material ordered.
9. Optionally, you can select multiple Options.
10. Choose the Submit or Cancel option.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.

* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date of the order.

* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
