# material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material_order_form is used to collect information for material orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Company.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Enter your Location.
7. Select the Material Type.
8. Enter the Quantity of material ordered.
9. Optionally, you can select multiple Options.
10. Choose the Submit or Cancel option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, text, required): Select the type of material you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of material you want to order.
* **Material Order Id** (`material_order_id`, text, required): Enter the material order ID.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date of the order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the order.
* **Options** (`options`, select multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Submit/Cancel** (`submit`, select one, required): Choose the Submit or Cancel option.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Enter the category of the material order.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter the form ID.
* **User Id** (`user_id`, text, required): Enter the user ID.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required): Enter the assigned user.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of completion.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required): Select the creation date.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required): Select the update date.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, required): Select the deletion date.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the creator of the material order.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required): Enter the updater of the material order.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, required): Enter the deleter of the material order.
