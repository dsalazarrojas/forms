# Daily Construction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the current status of a construction project. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the project's progress, site location, weather conditions, and any other relevant information that may impact the project's timeline or outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the project in the `project_name` field.
2. Provide the location of the site where the project is taking place in the `site_location` field.
3. Select the current status of the project in the `project_status` field (e.g., In Progress, On Hold, or Completed).
4. Enter the date when the project started in the `project_start_date` field.
5. Briefly describe any work that was done on the site in the `work_description` field.
6. Choose the current weather conditions on site in the `weather_conditions` field (e.g., Sunny, Cloudy, or Rainy).
7. Enter the name of the site inspector in the `site_inspector` field.
8. Sign your name as the site inspector in the `inspector_signature` field.
9. Provide any comments or notes about the site in the `inspector_comment` field.
10. Sign your name as the supervisor in the `supervisor_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **project_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the project.
- **site_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the site.
- **project_status** (select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the project (In Progress, On Hold, or Completed).
- **project_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the project started.
- **work_description** (text, optional): Briefly describe any work that was done on the site.
- **weather_conditions** (select_one, optional): Choose the current weather conditions on site (Sunny, Cloudy, or Rainy).
- **site_inspector** (text, optional): Enter the name of the site inspector.
- **inspector_signature** (note, optional): Sign your name as the site inspector.
- **inspector_comment** (text, optional): Provide any comments or notes about the site.
- **supervisor_signature** (note, optional): Sign your name as the supervisor.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* Make sure to sign both the site inspector and supervisor signatures.
* Keep the comments field for any additional notes or observations.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure everything is accurate and complete.
