# Web Application Development Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between the client and developer for a web application development project, outlining project specifics, payment terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your client information and details in the `Client Information` section.
2.  Provide your contact information in the `Client Name`, `Client Email` fields.
3.  Fill out your project specifics, including `Project Description`, `Project Location`, `Project Deadline`, `Project Budget` and `Payment Terms` in the `Project Information` section.
4.  Confirm the payment method in the `Payment Method` field.
5.  In the `Client Signature` and `Developer Signature` fields, confirm that both parties have read and agreed to the terms outlined in the form.
6.  Provide any additional comments or notes in the `Client Comments` and `Developer Comments` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Information**: Enter any identifying details that you want to share with the developer. This is optional.
*   **Client Name**: Enter your name as the client.
*   **Client Email**: Enter your email address so the developer can contact you.
*   **Developer Information**: Enter any identifying details that you want to share with the client. This is optional.
*   **Developer Name**: Enter the name of the developer working on the project.
*   **Developer Email**: Enter the email address of the developer.
*   **Project Information**: Describe the project details, including location and deadline.
*   **Project Description**: Briefly outline the project's objectives and scope.
*   **Project Location**: Specify the location where the project will be delivered.
*   **Project Deadline**: Enter the date when you want the project to be completed by.
*   **Project Budget**: Enter the agreed-upon payment for the project.
*   **Payment Terms**: Specify any payment terms or conditions.
*   **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method, whether it's through PayPal, bank transfer, or credit card.
*   **Terms and Conditions**: This is an optional field for any additional terms or conditions you want to include.
*   **Agreed Date**: Confirm when you both agreed on this agreement.
*   **Sign Date**: Enter the date when you sign this form.
*   **Sign Time**: Enter the time you sign this form.
*   **Client Signature**: Confirm that you have read and agreed on this agreement.
*   **Developer Signature**: Confirm that the developer has read and agreed on this agreement.
*   **Client Note**: Add any comments or notes that you want to share with the developer.
*   **Developer Note**: Add any comments or notes that you want to share with the client.
*   **Client Agreed**: Confirm that you have agreed on this agreement.
*   **Developer Agreed**: Confirm that the developer has agreed on this agreement.
*   **Client Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback that you have.
*   **Developer Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback that you have.
