# Crane Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crane Rental Application Form is a tool used to gather necessary information from customers for the rental process of cranes. Please fill out this form to provide us with the required details to ensure a smooth rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your customer information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select the type of crane you require for your project.
3. Choose the size of the crane you need.
4. Specify the duration of the rental period.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your rental requirements.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`): Enter your name and contact details, so we can reach out to you about your application.
* **Name** (`name`): Fill in your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your rental details.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Provide your phone number for easy communication.
* **Date of Rental** (`date_of_rental`): Select the start date for your crane rental period.
* **Time of Rental** (`time_of_rental`): Choose the start time for your crane rental period.
* **Crane Type** (`crane_type`): Choose the type of crane you require (A-Z or a, b, etc.).
* **Crane Size** (`crane_size`): Select the size of the crane you need (Yes or No).
* **Duration of Rental** (`duration_of_rental`): Specify the length of your rental period.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional details or notes about your rental requirements.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Please sign and date the form to confirm your application.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`): Fill in the contract number if you have one, otherwise, it is not required.
