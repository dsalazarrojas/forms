# Pricing and Commission Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document the terms and conditions related to pricing and commission structures for a contractor. It is intended to be used when working with third-party contractors, freelancers, or independent contractors, where understanding and agreeing on pricing and commission structures is essential for the project's success.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the sections and fields provided.
2. Answer each field truthfully and accurately, ensuring all relevant parties understand the pricing and commission structures.
3. Ensure all necessary sections are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Parties**
	+ This field allows you to indicate if both parties (you and the contractor) are business parties.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you and the contractor both agree to be considered business parties.
	+ You can also select 'No' if only one party or no parties are considered business parties.
* **Pricing Terms**
	+ This field is a note section where you can provide any additional pricing terms or conditions.
	+ Please use this space to clarify any specific pricing terms or conditions that are not covered in the main sections.
* **Commission Structures**
	+ This field allows you to select the type of commission structure you will use.
	+ Please choose 'Yes' if you will be using a commission structure.
	+ You can also select 'No' if no commission structure is applicable.
* **Consent**
	+ This field allows you to indicate if both parties have given their consent for the commission structure.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if both parties have agreed to the commission structure.
	+ You can also select 'No' if only one party or no parties have agreed to the commission structure.
* **Signatures**
	+ This field is for you to sign and date the agreement, confirming your role and agreement with the commission structure.
	+ Please enter your name and date to acknowledge your acceptance of the agreement.
