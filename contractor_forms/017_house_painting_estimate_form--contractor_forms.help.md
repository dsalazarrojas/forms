# House Painting Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a house painting service. It will help the service provider to gather essential details about the client, their property, and the service requirements for a painting job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Client Information**: Fill out the Client Info section with your contact information and details.
2. **Enter Property Information**: Enter the necessary details about the property that needs to be painted.
3. **Select Service Details**: Choose the type of service required (Interior, Exterior, or Both).
4. **Specify Interior and Exterior Services**: Choose the areas that need to be painted (e.g., Living Room, Kitchen, etc.).
5. **Enter Service Price and Labor Cost**: Provide the service price and labor cost for the chosen services.
6. **Specify Material Cost**: Enter the material cost for the painting job.
7. **Enter Total Cost**: Calculate the total cost of the service.
8. **Select Service Type**: Choose the service type (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
9. **Schedule the Painting**: Enter the desired painting schedule.
10. **Confirm Contact Information**: Choose whether you want to be contacted for further information or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: Enter your contact information and details to allow us to reach out to you with any further questions or updates.
* **Property Info**: Provide details about the property that needs to be painted, including its address and any specific requirements.
* **Service Details**: Choose the type of service required (Interior, Exterior, or Both). This will help us determine the scope of work.
* **Minimalist Painting**: Choose whether you need a minimalist painting service or not. If yes, specify the areas that need to be painted.
* **Interior Painting**: Choose the areas that need to be painted on the interior of the property.
* **Service Price**: Enter the price for the chosen services.
* **Exterior Painting**: Choose the areas that need to be painted on the exterior of the property.
* **Labor Cost**: Enter the labor cost for the painting job.
* **Material Cost**: Enter the material cost for the painting job.
* **Total Cost**: Calculate the total cost of the service.
* **Service Type**: Choose the service type (Basic, Standard, or Premium). This will help us determine the level of service.
* **Painting Schedule**: Enter the desired painting schedule.
* **Contact Info**: Choose whether you want to be contacted for further information or not.
