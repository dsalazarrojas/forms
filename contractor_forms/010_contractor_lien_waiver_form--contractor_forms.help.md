# Contractor Lien Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Contractor Lien Waiver Form, ensuring you have all the necessary information and understanding of each field to fill out the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully to ensure all relevant information is provided.
2.  Complete all required fields, indicated by \*.
3.  If a field is not required, you may leave it blank or provide additional comments if necessary.
4.  Ensure all dates are in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5.  Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Full Name** (`contractor_name`, text, \*): Enter the full legal name of the contractor or company performing the work.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`, text, \*): Provide the full business address of the contractor.
* **Contractor Phone** (`contractor_phone`, text, \*): Enter the contact phone number for the contractor.
* **Contractor Email** (`contractor_email`, email, \*): Enter the email address of the contractor.
* **Property Owner Name** (`property_owner_name`, text, \*): Enter the legal name of the property owner.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, \*): Provide the full address of the property.
* **Property Legal Description** (`property_legal_description`, text): Enter the legal description from the property deed if available.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, \*): Describe the work performed on the property.
* **Original Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date): Enter the date when the contract was signed.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, date): Enter the date when work began.
* **Work Completion Date** (`work_completion_date`, date): Enter the date when work was completed.
* **Original Contract Amount** (`original_contract_amount`, number): Enter the total value of the original contract.
* **Payment Amount Being Made** (`payment_amount`, number, \*): Enter the amount of the current payment being made.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select one): Select the type of payment being made:
	+ Progress Payment
	+ Final Payment
	+ Retainage Release
	+ Settlement Payment
* **Check Number** (`check_number`, text): Enter the check or transaction reference number if applicable.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, \*): Enter the date of payment.
* **Type of Lien Waiver** (`waiver_type`, select one): Select the type of waiver:
	+ Conditional Waiver on Progress Payment
	+ Unconditional Waiver on Progress Payment
	+ Conditional Waiver on Final Payment
	+ Unconditional Waiver on Final Payment
* **Waiver Period From** (`waiver_period_from`, date, \*): Enter the start date of the waiver period.
* **Waiver Period Through** (`waiver_period_through`, date, \*): Enter the end date of the waiver period.
* **Disputed Amounts** (`disputed_amounts`, number): Enter any amounts still in dispute.
* **Description of Exceptions** (`exceptions_description`, text): Describe any exceptions to the waiver.
* **Authorized Signer Name** (`authorized_signer_name`, text, \*): Enter the name of the person signing the form.
* **Authorized Signer Title** (`authorized_signer_title`, text, \*): Enter the title or position of the person signing.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, \*): Enter the date of signing.
* **Notary Required** (`notary_required`, select one): Select if this form requires notarization:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text): Enter the name of the witness if applicable.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Enter any other relevant information.
