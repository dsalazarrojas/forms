# petition_for_road_repair_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for citizens to report road repair needs to the local authorities. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the road repair request, including contact information and location details. Your input will help the local authorities to prioritize and schedule road repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form to report road repair needs to the local authorities.
2. Enter your contact information in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your name and title (if any) as the person responsible for this road repair request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Street Name** (`street_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the street that needs repair.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the first line of the address where the road repair is needed.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the second line of the address where the road repair is needed.
* **Street Number** (`street_number`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the house or building number of the location where the road repair is needed.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the city where the road repair is needed.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select your state from the list to ensure the repair request is directed to the correct authority.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the zip code of the location where the road repair is needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and accurate information about the road repair request.
* If you have any additional information or photos about the road repair need, please attach them separately.
* If you have any further questions, feel free to contact the local authorities directly.
