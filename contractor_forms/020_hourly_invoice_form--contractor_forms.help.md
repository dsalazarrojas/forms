# hourly_invoice_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hourly invoice form is a tool used to create a document for tracking hours worked by a contractor or freelancer, ensuring accurate and complete invoicing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the total number of hours worked in the "Work Hours" field.
2. Select the rate type from the "Rate" field - Hourly, Daily, or Weekly.
3. Enter the start time and end time of the work period for which the invoice is being created.
4. Add any relevant goals or notes in the "Goals" field.
5. Enter the date on which the work was performed in the "Date Worked" field.
6. Enter the rate per hour applied to the work in the "Hourly Rate" field.
7. Calculate the total hours worked by the form, which will automatically appear in the "Total Hours" field.
8. Calculate the total amount due by multiplying the total hours by the hourly rate, which will automatically appear in the "Total Amount" field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to generate the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours worked by the contractor or freelancer. 
* **Rate**: Select the rate type - Hourly, Daily, or Weekly - that applies to the work. 
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the work period for which the invoice is being created. 
* **Goals**: Add any additional goals or notes related to the work period. 
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the work period for which the invoice is being created. 
* **Date Worked**: Enter the date on which the work was performed. 
* **Hourly Rate**: Enter the rate per hour applied to the work. 
* **Total Hours**: This field will be automatically calculated based on the start and end times.
* **Total Amount**: This field will be automatically calculated based on the total hours and hourly rate.
* **Submit**: Click this button to generate the invoice, or click the "Reset" button if you need to make changes.
