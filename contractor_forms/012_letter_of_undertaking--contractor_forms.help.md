# Letter Of Undertaking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter of Undertaking form is used to collect essential information about a customer to confirm their undertaking or agreement to perform a specific action or provide certain services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a unique ID for this Letter of Undertaking in the "Form ID" field.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
5. Enter the customer's city in the "Customer City" field.
6. Enter the customer's state in the "Customer State" field.
7. Enter the customer's zip code in the "Customer Zip" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
9. Enter the details of the undertaking or agreement in the "Undertaking Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (text, required): A unique identifier for this Letter of Undertaking.
* **Customer Name** (text, optional): The name of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (text, optional): The physical address of the customer.
* **Customer City** (text, optional): The city of the customer's address.
* **Customer State** (text, optional): The state of the customer's address.
* **Customer Zip** (text, optional): The zip code of the customer's address.
* **Customer Email** (email, optional): The email address of the customer.
* **Undertaking Details** (text, optional): A description of the undertaking or agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields, including the "Form ID".
* Double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy.
* If the customer's address is outside the country, enter their address without a zip code.
* Use a clear and concise description in the "Undertaking Details" field.
