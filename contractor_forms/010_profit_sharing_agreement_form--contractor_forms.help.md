# Profit Sharing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profit Sharing Agreement Form is used to document the terms of profit sharing agreements between partners in a business or project setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each partner involved in the contract (contract_partners field).
2. Describe the profit distribution terms (profit_distribution_terms field) in the designated text field.
3. Confirm your consent to the agreement (consent field) by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Specify the roles of each partner (roles field) by selecting multiple options as needed.
5. Enter the percentage of profit to be shared (profit_percentage field).
6. Enter the total profit to be shared (total_profit field).
7. Set the start date of the agreement (agreement_start_date field) in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Set the end date of the agreement (agreement_end_date field) in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
9. Sign the agreement on the date it starts (signed_on field) in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
10. Sign the agreement with your name and date in the signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **contract_partners (contract_partners)**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the partner is involved in the contract. Select "No" if they are not involved.
  - *Hint*: This field is not necessarily about the number of partners, but rather their level of involvement in the contract.
- **profit_distribution_terms (profit_distribution_terms)**: Describe the terms of profit distribution between partners. This could include details on how profit will be calculated, distributed, and taxed.
  - *Hint*: Keep this field concise and clear, as it will serve as a reference for all parties involved.
- **consent (consent)**: Confirm your consent to the agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
  - *Hint*: This confirms your agreement to the terms established in the form.
- **roles (roles)**: Select multiple roles for each partner involved in the contract. This could include tasks, responsibilities, or accountabilities.
  - *Hint*: This field should reflect the actual roles and responsibilities of each partner.
- **profit_percentage (profit_percentage)**: Enter the percentage of profit to be shared by each partner.
  - *Hint*: This field requires a numerical value in the format 0.x where x is the percentage of profit to be shared.
- **total_profit (total_profit)**: Enter the total profit to be shared among partners.
  - *Hint*: This field requires a numerical value in the format 0.x where x is the total profit to be shared.
- **agreement_start_date (agreement_start_date)**: Set the start date of the agreement in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  - *Hint*: This date marks the beginning of the agreement.
- **agreement_end_date (agreement_end_date)**: Set the end date of the agreement in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  - *Hint*: This date marks the end of the agreement.
- **signed_on (signed_on)**: Sign the agreement on the date it starts in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  - *Hint*: This date indicates when you first sign the agreement.
- **signature (signature)**: Sign the agreement with your name and date in the format "Name: Signature Date: YYYY-MM-DD".
  - *Hint*: This is a formal signature of the agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to clearly describe profit distribution terms to prevent any misunderstandings.
- Ensure that all dates are accurate and correctly formatted.
- Review the agreement carefully before signing it.
- Store the signed agreement in a secure location for future reference.
- Regularly review and update the agreement as needed.
