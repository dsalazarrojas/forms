# Service Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a service invoice for clients who have hired you for specific services. It captures essential information about your company and the client's contact information, as well as details about the service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Company Name**, which represents your company's name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Address** where your company is located or can be reached.
3. Provide your company's **Email Address** and **Telephone Number** for communication purposes.
4. Enter the **Client Name** and **Client Address** for billing and communication purposes.
5. Select the **Invoice Date**, which represents the date the service was provided.
6. Enter a unique **Invoice Number** for identification purposes.
7. Provide a detailed **Description** of the services provided to the client.
8. Enter the total **Fees** involved in the service provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (required): Enter your company's name and contact information for billing and communication purposes.
* **Address** (optional): Enter the address of your company for client reference.
* **Email Address** (optional): Enter your company's email address for communication with clients.
* **Telephone Number** (optional): Enter your company's phone number for communication with clients.
* **Client Name** (optional): Enter the client's name for billing and reference purposes.
* **Client Address** (optional): Enter the client's address for billing and reference purposes.
* **Invoice Date** (required): Select the date the service was provided to the client.
* **Invoice Number** (optional): Enter a unique number for identification purposes.
* **Description** (optional): Provide a detailed description of the services provided to the client.
* **Fees** (optional): Enter the total fees involved in the service provided.
