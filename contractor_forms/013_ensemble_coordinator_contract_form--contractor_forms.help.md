# Ensemble Coordinator Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ensemble Coordinator Contract Form is a crucial document that captures essential information about an ensemble coordinator's contact details, contract dates, and compensation terms. This form ensures accurate and official records are maintained for contract management and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your contact information**: In the first field, enter your full name and any other relevant contact details in the "Coordinator Contact Info" field.
2. **Enter organization details**: Provide your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field, as it is a required field.
3. **Define the contract period**: Enter the start and end dates of your contract in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively. These dates must be in a format that can be understood as actual dates (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
4. **Specify compensation terms**: In the "Compensation Terms" field, describe the terms of your compensation in detail. This could include payment rates, payment methods, or any other relevant details.
5. **Sign and date the form**: Sign the form with your signature in the "Ensemble Coordinator Signature" field and date it in the "Signature Date" field. Note that you can choose from the provided options in this field.
6. **Add organizational signature**: If required or desired, sign the form with your organization's signature in the "Organization Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coordinator Contact Info (text)**: This field is used to store your contact information. Please enter your full name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Organization Name (text)**: This field is required and should contain your organization's name.
* **Start Date (date)**: This field is for the start date of your contract. Please ensure you enter this in a date format (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Date (date)**: This field is for the end date of your contract. Like the "Start Date" field, please enter this in a date format (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Compensation Terms (text)**: This field is where you describe the terms of your compensation. Be detailed and clear so that all parties understand.
* **Signature Date (date)**: This is where you sign the form and date it. It is recommended to use the same date format as the other date fields.
* **Ensemble Coordinator Signature (select_one)**: This field offers a selection of options for your signature. Please select one from the provided options.
* **Organization Signature (select_one)**: This field offers a selection of options for your organization's signature. Select one from the provided options if required or desired.
