# daily-job-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The daily job report form is designed to record an employee's daily work activity. This form helps management track work hours, sites visited, and other relevant details for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that you enter your correct Employee Name and Employee ID in the respective fields.
3. Record your hours worked for the day in the Hours Worked field.
4. Indicate if you visited a job site for the day in the Job Site Visited field.
5. Select the job type that best describes your work activities for the day in the Job Type field.
6. Enter the date of your work hours in the Work Hours Date field.
7. Record your work hours in the Work Hours Time field.
8. Provide any additional notes about your work activities in the Note field.
9. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
10. Indicate if you were assigned to a specific task or project in the Assigned To field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Record your total hours worked for the day.
* **Job Site Visited** (`job_site_visited`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you visited a job site for the day, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job type that best describes your work activities for the day, with options Option 1 and Option 2.
* **Work Hours Date** (`work_hours_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your work hours.
* **Work Hours Time** (`work_hours_time`, time, optional): Record your work hours in this field.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about your work activities.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you were assigned to a specific task or project, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
