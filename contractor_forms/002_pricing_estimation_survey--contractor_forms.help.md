# Pricing Estimation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pricing Estimation Survey is a form designed to help you provide a price estimation for a specific project. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete the survey.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **What's your target price range?**: Please enter your target price range for the project. This can be either "Less than 1000", "1000 - 2000", "2000 - 3000", or "More than 3000".
2. **What's your target price range for this project?**: This is an optional text field where you can provide more details about your target price range. Please use this field to elaborate on your price range if needed.
3. **What type of project is this?**: Please select one or multiple project types from the list (Residential, Commercial, Other). This will help us understand the project's nature.
4. **What's your estimated budget for this project?**: Please enter your estimated budget for the project. This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
5. **What's your actual budget for this project?**: Please enter your actual budget for the project. This is a numerical field where you can enter a value.
6. **What's your pricing strategy?**: Please select one of the following pricing strategies (Fixed, Cost Plus, Time and Materials).
7. **Can you elaborate on your pricing strategy?**: This is an optional text field where you can provide more details about your pricing strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your target price range?** (`price_range_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select a price range for the project. Please choose a price range that suits your project needs.
* **What's your target price range for this project?** (`price_range_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is an optional text field where you can provide more details about your target price range.
* **What type of project is this?** (`project_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select one or multiple project types from the list to help us understand the project's nature.
* **What's your estimated budget for this project?** (`estimated_budget`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to enter a numerical value for your estimated budget.
* **What's your actual budget for this project?** (`actual_budget`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your actual budget for the project.
* **What's your pricing strategy?** (`pricing_strategy`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following pricing strategies: Fixed, Cost Plus, Time and Materials.
* **Can you elaborate on your pricing strategy?** (`pricing_strategy_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is an optional text field where you can provide more details about your pricing strategy.
* **Other**: This field is not currently being used in the form.

Tips:

* Make sure to answer all the questions carefully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask your project manager or team lead for clarification.
* Please enter accurate and realistic values for your estimated and actual budget.
* Please select a pricing strategy that suits your project needs.
* If you have any additional information or details about your project, please use the optional text fields to provide them.
