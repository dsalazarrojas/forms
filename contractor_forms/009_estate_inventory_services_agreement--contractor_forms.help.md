# estate-inventory-services-agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of services provided to a client for a particular property. It captures essential details from both the client and service provider, ensuring both parties are in agreement and have signed off on the terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Go through each page to ensure you're filling in all required fields.
- For the "Service Definition" page, enter a brief description of the services provided.
- On the "Client Approval" page, select 'True' to indicate that the client has approved the services and 'False' otherwise.
- On the "Client Signature" page, write your name to sign the form.
- On the "Service Provider Signature" page, sign and date to confirm your agreement with the client.
- On the "Property Address" page, enter the address of the property for which the services were provided.
- On the "Service Description" page, provide a detailed description of the services (optional but recommended).
- On the "Service Provider Name" page, enter your name and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Definition** (service_definition_page, text, required): Enter a brief description of the services provided to the client.
* **Client Approval** (client_approval_page, select_one, required): Select 'True' if the client has approved the services, 'False' otherwise.
* **Client Signature** (client_signature_page, text, required): Sign and date to indicate your agreement to the services provided.
* **Service Provider Signature** (service_provider_signature_page, time, required): Sign and date to confirm your agreement with the client.
* **Property Address** (property_address_page, text, required): Enter the address of the property for which the services were provided.
* **Service Description** (service_description_page, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the services provided (helpful for future reference). 
* **Service Provider Name** (service_provider_name_page, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the service provider.
