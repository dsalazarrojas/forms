# Work Permit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for work permits, which are internal permissions for employees or contractors to work on specific projects or at certain locations within the company. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that work permits are approved and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of permit you are applying for from the list of options.
3. Enter the location where you will be working.
4. Specify your work hours.
5. Enter the date of submission.
6. Select the individual or individuals who will review and approve your permit application.
7. Select the approver(s) for your permit application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Your company email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Your office or mobile phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Permit Type**: Select the type of work permit you are applying for from the list of options provided.
* **Work Location**: Enter the exact location where you will be working, including the building or floor number if applicable.
* **Work Hours**: Enter your working hours, including the start and end times, in the format `HH:MM - MM:MM`.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted By**: Select the individual or group that will review and approve your permit application. This is typically the person who will approve your work permit.
* **Submitted To**: Select all the individuals or groups that need to be informed about your permit application. This can include managers, team members, or others who need to be aware of your work activities.

## Tips

* Double-check your personal and work details to ensure accuracy.
* Review the submitted-by field to ensure that you are assigning the correct reviewer(s) for your permit application.
* Make sure to submit your form well in advance of your actual work start date to allow for adequate review and approval process.
* If you are experiencing any difficulties or have questions about this form, contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
