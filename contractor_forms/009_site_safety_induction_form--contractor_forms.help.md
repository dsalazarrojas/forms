# Site Safety Induction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Site Safety Induction Form is a crucial document that ensures all contractors and site visitors are adequately informed and prepared for a safe working environment on the site. This form is designed to collect essential information to ensure compliance with site-specific rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
2.  For fields with multiple options (select_one, select_multiple), select all applicable answers.
3.  Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, Required): Enter your name and contact number as per your company's policy.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, Required): Provide the name and contact number of an emergency contact person.
*   **Site Address** (`site_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter the site address if it's different from your company's address.
*   **Site Access** (`site_access`, `select_one`, Required): Select "Yes" if you have access to the site, "No" otherwise.
*   **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all the emergency services available at the site.
*   **Site Safety** (`site_safety`, `text`, Optional): Describe any site-specific safety concerns or issues.
*   **Personal Equipment** (`personal_equipment`, `text`, Optional): List any personal equipment you may need to bring to the site.
*   **Inductions** (`inductions`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all inductions you've received.
*   **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all medical certifications you hold.
*   **Medical Declaration** (`medical_declaration`, `text`, Optional): Describe any medical declaration or condition you may have.
*   **Emergency Plan** (`emergency_plan`, `select_one`, Optional): Select if you're aware of the emergency plan.
*   **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, Optional): Provide the name and contact number of an emergency contact person.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name and contact number of your supervisor or contact person.
*   **Site Safety Supervisor** (`site_safety_supervisor`, `text`, Optional): Name of the site safety supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Contact** (`supervisor_contact`, `text`, Optional): Contact number of your supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, Optional): Phone number of your supervisor.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, Optional): Contact number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Services** (`medical_services`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select all medical services available at the site.
*   **Medical Personnel** (`medical_personnel`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name and contact number of medical personnel.
*   **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `text`, Optional): List any safety equipment you may need to bring to the site.
*   **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, `text`, Optional): List any emergency equipment available at the site.
*   **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, `text`, Optional): List the number and type of fire extinguishers available at the site.
*   **Emergency Lighting** (`emergency_lighting`, `text`, Optional): Describe the emergency lighting available at the site.
*   **Exit Routes** (`exit_routes`, `text`, Optional): Describe the exit routes to the site.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please refer to your company's specific policies and procedures for more detailed guidance.
