# B2B Contract Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The B2B Contract Agreement Form is a document used for businesses to outline and formalize their agreements with other companies. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the partnership, including the scope of work, payment terms, and confidentiality agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the company-specific information:
   - **Company Name**: Enter the legal name of your business.
   - **Company Address**: Provide the full address of your business.
   - **Authorized Representative Name**: Enter the name of the person signing on behalf of the company.
   - **Representative Title**: Enter the job title or position of the representative.
   - **Representative Email**: Enter the company's business email address.
   - **Representative Phone**: Enter the direct contact number of the representative.

2. Select the type of contract your business is entering into:
   - **Type of Contract**: Choose the specific type of agreement (e.g., Service Agreement, Partnership Agreement, etc.).

3. Specify the dates and terms of the contract:
   - **Contract Start Date**: Enter the date when the agreement begins.
   - **Contract End Date**: Enter the date when the agreement ends.
   - **Total Contract Value**: Enter the total contract value in USD.

4. Choose the payment terms for the contract:
   - **Payment Terms**: Select the preferred payment schedule (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, etc.).

5. Outline the scope of work and any special requirements:
   - **Scope of Work Description**: Enter a detailed description of the services or deliverables.
   - **Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm whether you agree to confidentiality terms.
   - **Termination Notice Period**: Choose the required notice period for contract termination.

6. Add any additional terms and conditions:
   - **Additional Terms and Conditions**: Enter any special provisions or amendments.

7. Finalize the contract:
   - **Date of Signature**: Enter the date when signing this agreement.
   - **Agreement Acceptance**: Confirm that you have read and agree to all terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): The legal name of your business entity.
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required): The full business address of your company.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (Authorized Representative Name, text, required): The name of the person signing on behalf of the company.
* **Representative Title** (Representative Title, text, required): The job title or position of the representative.
* **Representative Email** (Representative Email, email, required): The business email address of the company.
* **Representative Phone** (Representative Phone, text, required): The direct contact number of the representative.
* **Type of Contract** (Type of Contract, select_one, required): The specific type of agreement (e.g., Service Agreement, Partnership Agreement, etc.).
* **Contract Start Date** (Contract Start Date, date, required): The date when the agreement begins.
* **Contract End Date** (Contract End Date, date, required): The date when the agreement ends.
* **Total Contract Value** (Total Contract Value, number, required): The total contract value in USD.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, select_one, required): The preferred payment schedule (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, etc.).
* **Scope of Work Description** (Scope of Work Description, text, required): A detailed description of the services or deliverables.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Confidentiality Agreement, select_one, required): Confirm whether you agree to confidentiality terms.
* **Termination Notice Period** (Termination Notice Period, select_one, required): The required notice period for contract termination.
* **Additional Terms and Conditions** (Additional Terms and Conditions, text, optional): Any special provisions or amendments.
* **Date of Signature** (Date of Signature, date, required): The date when signing this agreement.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (Agreement Acceptance, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to all terms.
