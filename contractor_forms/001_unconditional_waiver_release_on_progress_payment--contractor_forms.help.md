# Unconditional Waiver & Release On Progress Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain an unconditional waiver and release from a contractor, acknowledging that they have received progress payment and have agreed to the payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

- Please make sure you have received the progress payment and reviewed the payment terms before filling out this form.
- Fill in the job name and job description, which will help us identify the relevant progress payment and terms.
- Enter the date when you started the job and when you made the payment.
- Enter the amount of the payment made.
- Enter any payment terms that were agreed upon.
- Review and confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unconditional Waiver & Release On Progress Payment** (`unconditional_waiver_and_release_on_progress_payment`): Enter your unconditional waiver and release statement, acknowledging that you have received the progress payment and have agreed to the payment terms.
* **Job Name** (`job_name`): Enter the name of the job you are referring to.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): Enter a brief description of the job, to help us identify the relevant progress payment and terms.
* **Date Job Started** (`date_job_started`): Enter the date when you started the job.
* **Date Payment Made** (`date_payment_made`): Enter the date when you made the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount of the payment made.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any agreed-upon payment terms.

## Tips
* Ensure you have received the progress payment before filling out this form.
* Double-check the payment terms to confirm you have agreed upon them.
* If there are any issues or disputes related to the payment, please contact us before filling out this form.
