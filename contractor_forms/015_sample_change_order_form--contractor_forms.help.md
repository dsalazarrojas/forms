# Sample Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and approve changes to a project or job. It ensures that all relevant information is accurately recorded and that all parties involved are in agreement on the changes made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `Order Number` of the project or job.
2. Enter the `Order Date` of the project or job.
3. Enter the `Client` name and email address.
4. Enter the `Contractor` name and any other relevant information.
5. Select the `Job Type` (Yes or No).
6. Select the `Project Status` (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter the `Reason` for the change.
8. Enter any additional `Comments` about the change.
9. Enter the `Total Change` amount.
10. Enter the name and title of the person who approved the change.
11. Date and sign the `Approved Date` and `Approved By Signature` fields.
12. Enter any other relevant information, such as `Contract Number`, `Job Number`, `Project Number`, `Client Name`, `Contractor Name`, `Job Name`, `Order Number 2`, `Total Change 2`, `Project Status 2`, and `Submitted By`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required): Enter the number of the order or project.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order or project.
* **Client** (`client`, email, required): Enter the name and email address of the client.
* **Contractor** (`contractor`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the contractor.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the project manager.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No to indicate the type of job.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select Active or Inactive to indicate the status of the project.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, optional): Enter a brief explanation of the reason for the change.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the change.
* **Total Change** (`total_change`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the change.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person who approved the change.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the change was approved.
* **Approved By Signature** (`approved_by_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the person who approved the change.
* **Approved By Title** (`approved_by_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person who approved the change.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Enter the client's signature.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, optional): Enter the contract number.
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, optional): Enter the job number.
* **Project Number** (`project_number`, text, optional): Enter the project number.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, optional): Enter the contractor's name.
* **Job Name** (`job_name`, text, optional): Enter the job name.
* **Order Number 2** (`order_number_2`, text, optional): Enter the order number 2.
* **Total Change 2** (`total_change_2`, text, optional): Enter the total change 2.
* **Project Status 2** (`project_status_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select Active or Inactive to indicate the status of the project 2.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No to indicate whether the form was submitted.
