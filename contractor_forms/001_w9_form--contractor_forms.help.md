# W9 Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The W9 Form is used to report income payments to independent contractors or non-employees, ensuring compliance with IRS regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate answers for each field based on the provided options.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information, such as the employee's and client's details.
3. Review and verify all the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. W9 Form Input (`w9_form_input`)
* **Select Multiple**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the form is for reporting income payments.

### 2. Employee Name (`employee_name`)
* **Text**: Enter the full name of the employee or independent contractor.

### 3. Employee Address (`employee_address`)
* **Text**: Enter the address of the employee or independent contractor.

### 4. Employee Phone (`employee_phone`)
* **Text**: Enter the phone number of the employee or independent contractor.

### 5. Employee Email (`employee_email`)
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employee or independent contractor.

### 6. Employee Start Date (`employee_start_date`)
* **Date**: Enter the date the employee or independent contractor started working.

### 7. Employee End Date (`employee_end_date`)
* **Date**: Enter the date the employee or independent contractor ended working.

### 8. Form Filing Date (`form_filing_date`)
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was filed.

### 9. Client Name (`client_name`)
* **Text**: Enter the name of the client or business.

### 10. Client Address (`client_address`)
* **Text**: Enter the address of the client or business.

### 11. Client Phone (`client_phone`)
* **Text**: Enter the phone number of the client or business.

### 12. Client Email (`client_email`)
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the client or business.

### 13. Client Relationship (`client_relationship`)
* **Select One**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the nature of the relationship between the business and client.

### 14. Form Signature (`form_signature`)
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes.

### 15. Employer Name (`employer_name`)
* **Text**: Enter the name of the employer.

### 16. Employer EIN (`employer_ein`)
* **Text**: Enter the Employer Identification Number (EIN) of the employer.

### 17. Employer Address (`employer_address`)
* **Text**: Enter the address of the employer.

### 18. Employer Phone (`employer_phone`)
* **Text**: Enter the phone number of the employer.

### 19. Employer Email (`employer_email`)
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employer.

### 20. Form Notes (`form_notes`)
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes.

### 21. Form Date (`form_date`)
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.

### 22. Form Time (`form_time`)
* **Time**: Enter the time the form was completed.

### 23. Form User (`form_user`)
* **Select One**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate who completed the form.

### 24. Form Status (`form_status`)
* **Select One**: Choose 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the form.
