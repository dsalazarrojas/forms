# Borehole Drilling Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from clients for a borehole drilling service agreement, ensuring a clear understanding of the scope of work, payment terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the Client's Name**: In this field, enter the name of the company or individual requesting the borehole drilling service.
2. **Add the Client's Contact Email**: Enter the preferred email of the client for contact purposes.
3. **Provide Project Site Address**: Enter the full address of the drilling location.
4. **Summary of Scope of Work**: Briefly describe the drilling scope and deliverables. While not required, this field is useful for providing additional context.
5. **Enter Expected Start Date**: Enter the anticipated start date for the project.
6. **Estimated Duration in Days**: Enter the number of calendar days expected for the project. This information helps in scheduling and planning.
7. **Choose Payment Terms**: Select one of the payment options provided:
	- Net 30: Payment is due 30 days after invoice date.
	- Net 14: Payment is due 14 days after invoice date.
	- Due on Receipt: Payment is due upon receipt of the invoice.
8. **Safety Requirements**: If necessary, describe any site safety protocols or PPE required.
9. **Acceptance Signature Email**: Enter the email address for the signature of the acceptance agreement.
10. **Additional Terms**: If necessary, provide any other contractual notes or terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name**: This field is required and is the name of the company or individual requesting the service.
* **Client contact email**: This field is required and is the preferred contact email for the client.
* **Project site address**: This field is required and should include the full address of the drilling location.
* **Scope of work summary**: This field is optional but provides an opportunity to describe the drilling scope and deliverables.
* **Expected start date**: This field is required and is the anticipated start date for the project.
* **Estimated duration days**: This field is optional but helps in scheduling and planning.
* **Payment terms**: This field is required and determines the payment terms for the project.
* **Safety requirements**: This field is optional and is used to describe any site safety protocols or PPE required.
* **Acceptance signature email**: This field is required and is the email address for the acceptance signature.
* **Additional terms**: This field is optional and is used to provide any other contractual notes or terms.

## Tips
* Always ensure that all required fields are filled out for the form to be considered complete.
* Be as detailed as possible in providing information in fields with hints.
* If unsure about any field, consult the form creator or relevant personnel for clarification.
