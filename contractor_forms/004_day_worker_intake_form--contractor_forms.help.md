# day_worker_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The day_worker_intake_form is a form designed to collect essential information from day workers. It is intended to gather details about a day worker's personal and contact information, including their name, address, city, state, zip, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the day_worker_intime_form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is optional.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is optional.
3. Provide your **Address** in the "Address" field. This field is optional.
4. Enter your **City** in the "City" field. This field is optional.
5. Enter your **State** in the "State" field. This field is optional.
6. Enter your **Zip** in the "Zip" field. This field is optional.
7. Enter your email address in the "**Email** field. This field is optional.
8. Enter your phone number in the "**Phone** field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your personal identification document.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your personal identification document.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address, including your street address or post office box.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
