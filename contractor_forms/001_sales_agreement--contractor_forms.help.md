# Sales Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and record the terms of a sales transaction between a seller and a buyer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the contact information** for both the seller and the buyer.
2. **Select the payment terms** that apply to the agreement.
3. **Choose the delivery method** that will be used for the sale.
4. **Enter the payment amount** for the transaction.
5. **Specify the payment date** for the payment.
6. **Select the payment time** for the payment.
7. **Enter any additional payment notes** that are relevant to the agreement.
8. **Enter the seller's notes** and **buyer's notes** if necessary.
9. **Enter any payment terms details** that are relevant to the agreement.
10. **Indicate if payment terms frequency** is applicable to the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (required: false, type: text): Enter the contact information for both the seller and the buyer.
* **payment_terms** (required: true, type: number): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Type** (required: true, type: select_multiple): 
	+ 'Yes': Indicates that the payment term is applicable to the agreement.
	+ 'No': Indicates that the payment term is not applicable to the agreement.
* **delivery_terms** (required: false, type: text): Enter the details of the delivery terms.
* **Delivery Method** (required: true, type: select_one): 
	+ 'Yes': Indicates that the delivery method is applicable to the agreement.
	+ 'No': Indicates that the delivery method is not applicable to the agreement.
* **payment_amount** (required: true, type: number): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (required: true, type: date): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Time** (required: true, type: time): Enter the payment time.
* **Payment Note** (required: false, type: text): Enter any additional notes about the payment.
* **Seller's Notes** (required: false, type: text): Enter any notes from the seller.
* **Buyer's Notes** (required: false, type: text): Enter any notes from the buyer.
* **Payment Terms Details** (required: false, type: text): Enter any additional details about the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (required: false, type: select_one): 
	+ 'Yes': Indicates that the payment terms frequency is applicable to the agreement.
	+ 'No': Indicates that the payment terms frequency is not applicable to the agreement.
