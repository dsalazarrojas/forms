# Subcontractor Warranty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subcontractor Warranty Form is a crucial document that helps ensure the credibility and reliability of subcontractors before they are hired for projects. This form collects essential information about the subcontractor's licensing, experience, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Subcontractor Name.
2. Provide your address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Select the start date of the project.
6. Indicate if you have a subcontractor license.
7. If you have a license, enter the license number.
8. If you have a certificate, enter the certificate number.
9. Enter the issue date of the certificate.
10. Describe your experience as a subcontractor.
11. Briefly describe the work you will be doing.
12. Indicate the payment terms.
13. If necessary, select the payment method.
14. Indicate if you offer a warranty for the project.
15. If you offer a warranty, select the start date of the warranty.
16. If you offer a warranty, select the end date of the warranty.
17. Indicate if the warranty period is applicable.
18. If you work in a different location, enter the address of the work site.
19. Enter the city where you will be working.
20. Enter the state where you will be working.
21. Enter the country where you will be working.
22. Enter the postal code of the work site.
23. Enter the name of the contact person at the work site.
24. Enter the phone number of the work site.
25. Enter the email of the work site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subcontractor Name** (`subcontractor_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the start date of the project.
* **Subcontractor License** (`subcontractor_license`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a subcontractor license.
* **Subcontractor License Number** (`subcontractor_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a license, enter the license number.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, optional): If you have a certificate, enter the certificate number.
* **Certificate Issue Date** (`certificate_issue_date`, date, optional): If you have a certificate, enter the issue date of the certificate.
* **Subcontractor Experience** (`subcontractor_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe your experience as a subcontractor.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the work you will be doing.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Indicate your payment terms.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select if you offer payment by bank transfer or other.
* **Warranty Type** (`warranty_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you offer a warranty for the project.
* **Warranty Start Date** (`warranty_start_date`, date, optional): If you offer a warranty, select the start date.
* **Warranty End Date** (`warranty_end_date`, date, optional): If you offer a warranty, select the end date.
* **Warranty Period** (`warranty_period`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the warranty period is applicable.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the work site if different from your address.
* **Work City** (`work_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you will be working.
* **Work State** (`work_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you will be working.
* **Work Country** (`work_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you will be working.
* **Work Postal Code** (`work_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the work site.
* **Work Contact Person** (`work_contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the work site.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the work site.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the work site.

## Tips
* Make sure to have all necessary documents and information at hand before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
