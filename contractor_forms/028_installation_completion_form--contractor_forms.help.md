# Installation Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Installation Completion Form" is used to track the progress and status of installations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the "customer_feedback" field with any comments or feedback from the customer.
*   Next, fill out the "installation_details" field with any relevant details about the installation.
*   Then, select the "satisfaction_rating" field to rate the customer's satisfaction with the installation.
*   Select the "completion_date" field to enter the date the installation was completed.
*   In the "contact_details" field, select whether contact was made with the customer or not.
*   Select the "installation_completion_status" field to indicate the completion status of the installation.
*   If required, select the "assigned_to" field to choose the person the installation was assigned to.
*   Fill out the "completion_method" field with the method used to complete the installation.
*   In the "installation_status" field, select the current status of the installation.
*   If required, fill out the "completion_date_time" field with the time the installation was completed.
*   In the "notes_2" field, enter any additional notes about the installation.
*   Fill out the "email" field with the customer's email address.
*   Fill out the "phone" field with the customer's phone number.
*   Fill out the "fax" field with the customer's fax number.
*   Fill out the "address" field with the customer's address.
*   Fill out the "zip" field with the customer's zip code.
*   Fill out the "state" field with the customer's state.
*   Fill out the "city" field with the customer's city.
*   Fill out the "contact_person" field with the name of the contact person.
*   Fill out the "email_2" field with the customer's email address (if different from the previous field).
*   Fill out the "phone_2" field with the customer's phone number (if different from the previous field).
*   Finally, fill out the "notes_3" field with any additional notes about the installation.
*   Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer_feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required): Enter any comments or feedback from the customer about the installation.
*   **installation_details** (`installation_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the installation.
*   **satisfaction_rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate the customer's satisfaction with the installation.
*   **completion_date** (`completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date the installation was completed.
*   **contact_details** (`contact_details`, select multiple, required): Select whether contact was made with the customer or not.
*   **installation_completion_status** (`installation_completion_status`, select one, required): Indicate the completion status of the installation.
*   **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select one, optional): Choose the person the installation was assigned to.
*   **completion_method** (`completion_method`, text, optional): Enter the method used to complete the installation.
*   **installation_status** (`installation_status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of the installation.
*   **completion_date_time** (`completion_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the installation was completed.
*   **notes_2** (`notes_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the installation.
*   **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter the customer's fax number.
*   **address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
*   **zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
*   **state** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the customer's state.
*   **city** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the customer's city.
*   **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **email_2** (`email_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's email address (if different from the previous field).
*   **phone_2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if different from the previous field).
*   **notes_3** (`notes_3`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the installation.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Submit the form.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   Enter accurate and complete information in the fields.
*   If you are unsure about a field, refer to your installation documentation or ask the customer for clarification.
*   If you need help with a field or have questions, contact your supervisor or a colleague for assistance.
