# Waiver of Lien Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to release a lien on a property, providing official documentation that protects both parties involved in the project. It is typically used in construction or real estate projects where a contractor or subcontractor has a lien on a property due to unpaid work or materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and documents.
2. Fill out each section completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly before signing.
4. Sign the form, along with the date of signing, to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property**: Enter the property address.
* **Renter Name**: Enter the name of the renter.
* **Sign Date**: Enter the date of signing this waiver.
* **Lender Signature**: Enter the signature of the lender.
* **Lender Name**: Enter the name of the lender.
* **Lien Amount**: Enter the amount of the lien.
* **Waiver of Lien Form Date**: Enter the date this waiver is being filed.
* **Lender Signature 2**: Enter the signature of the lender.
* **Date of Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Notice Date**: Enter the notice date.
* **Form Signed By**: Enter the name of the person signing this form.
* **Form Signed On**: Enter the date this form is being signed.
* **Waiver of Lien Form Version**: Enter the version of this waiver of lien form.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID of this waiver of lien form.
* **Form Date**: Enter the date this form was created.
* **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the contractor or subcontractor.
* **Waiver of Lien Contractor**: Enter the name of the contractor or subcontractor.
* **Waiver of Lien Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the contractor or subcontractor.
* **Lien Contractor**: Enter the name of the contractor or subcontractor.
* **Notice of Lien**: Enter a notice of lien.
* **Waiver of Lien**: Enter a waiver of lien.
* **Contractor**: Enter the name of the contractor or subcontractor.
* **Waiver of Lien Form**: Enter the name of the waiver of lien form.
