# Phase Agreement Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phase Agreement Confirmation form is designed to gather information from participants to confirm their details and provide electronic signatures for project-related activities. It is intended for tracking participant information, agreements, and submission dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the **Project Details** section with relevant information about the project.
* Enter your **Participant Info** in the designated field.
* Confirm your **Participant Consent** by entering a brief statement or agreement.
* Provide your **Participant Signature** by selecting a date for the signature (this will be displayed as a date field).
* Enter your **Contractor Info** if applicable.
* If you have electronically signed the document, select **Yes** for the **Submission** field.
* Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** if you're submitting electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project you are participating in.
* **Participant Info**: Provide your name, contact information, and any relevant details about your participation in the project.
* **Participant Consent**: Confirm your consent by entering a brief statement or agreement, such as "I have read and agree to the project terms and conditions."
* **Participant Signature**: Enter the date you sign the agreement, which will be displayed as a date field.
* **Contractor Info**: If you are a contractor, enter your company information and contact details (this field is optional).
* **Electronic Signature**: If you have signed electronically, select **Yes** to confirm.
* **Submission**: Select **Yes** to confirm that you have submitted the agreement.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the agreement (this will be displayed as a date field).
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted the agreement (this will be displayed as a time field).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are submitting electronically, ensure that you have provided your electronic signature.
* Review your submissions carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
