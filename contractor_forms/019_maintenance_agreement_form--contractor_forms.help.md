# Maintenance Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a maintenance agreement between a client and a service provider or firm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of service you are requesting for maintenance. This can be either a "Maintenance Service Firm" or "Individual Service Provider."
2. Fill in the company name and address of the service provider or firm.
3. Enter the city and state where the service provider or firm is located.
4. Enter the zip code of the service provider or firm.
5. Choose a start date for the maintenance agreement.
6. Choose an end date for the maintenance agreement.
7. Specify the frequency of maintenance (e.g. weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
8. Optionally, include any additional notes for the agreement.
9. Check the status of the agreement.
10. Sign the maintenance agreement with the service provider or firm.
11. Sign the agreement with the client.
12. Enter the date of the agreement.
13. Check the terms and conditions of the agreement.
14. Sign the terms and conditions of the agreement.
15. Enter the contract number.
16. Optionally, include any additional notes for the contract.
17. Check the status of the contract.
18. Sign the contract with a name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_type**: Choose the type of service you are requesting for maintenance, either "Maintenance Service Firm" or "Individual Service Provider."
* **company_name**: Enter the name of the firm or company providing the maintenance service.
* **address**: Enter the address of the service provider or firm.
* **city**: Enter the city where the service provider or firm is located.
* **state**: Optionally, enter the state where the service provider or firm is located.
* **zip**: Enter the zip code of the service provider or firm.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the maintenance agreement.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the maintenance agreement.
* **frequency**: Specify the frequency of maintenance (e.g. weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
* **notice**: Optionally, include any additional notes for the agreement.
* **agreement_status**: Check the status of the agreement.
* **service_agreement**: Sign the maintenance agreement with the service provider or firm.
* **signature_maint**: Sign the maintenance agreement with the service provider or firm's signature.
* **signature_client**: Sign the agreement with the client's signature.
* **agreement_date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
* **terms_conditions**: Check the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **terms_condition_sign**: Sign the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **contract_number**: Enter the contract number.
* **notes**: Optionally, include any additional notes for the contract.
* **contract_terms**: Optionally, include any additional terms for the contract.
* **signature_date**: Enter the date of the signature.
* **contract_status**: Check the status of the contract.
* **contract_signed_by**: Sign the contract with a name.
* **contract_signed_date**: Enter the date of the contract signature.
