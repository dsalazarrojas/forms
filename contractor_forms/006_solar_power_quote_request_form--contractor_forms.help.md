# Solar Power Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Power Quote Request Form is designed to collect information from potential customers who are interested in receiving a quote for a solar power installation. This form helps us understand their requirements and provide a quote based on their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your home address, including street address, city, and state.
2. Enter your home zip code.
3. Provide information about your roof, including its size and pitch.
4. Choose the type of solar panels you are interested in.
5. Specify the number of solar panels you would like to install.
6. Choose the type of roof mounting system you would like to use.
7. Select the type of installation you are interested in.
8. Enter your contact name, email, and phone number.
9. Provide any additional comments or questions you have about your solar power installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission**: Please select whether you would like to submit this form as a quote request or for another purpose.
* **Home Address**: Enter your street address, city, and state where the solar power system will be installed.
* **Home City**: Enter the city where your home is located.
* **Home State**: Enter the state where your home is located.
* **Home Zip**: Enter the zip code of your home address.
* **Roof Size (sqft)**: Enter the size of your roof in square feet.
* **Roof Pitch**: Enter the pitch of your roof, which is the ratio of the roof's height to its base (e.g., 4:12).
* **Solar Panel Type**: Select the type of solar panels you are interested in, such as monocrystalline or polycrystalline.
* **Solar Panel Count**: Enter the number of solar panels you would like to install.
* **Roof Mounting Type**: Choose the type of roof mounting system you would like to use, such as flush, micro, or ballasted.
* **Installation Type**: Select the type of installation you are interested in, such as roof mount or ground mount.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., Mr./Ms./Mrs./Dr.).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Message**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have about your solar power installation.

* **Form Submission Date**: (Not applicable - this field seems to be a redundant field)
* **Form Submit**: (This field seems to be a placeholder - please ignore)

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that we can provide you with an accurate quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form submission and provide you with a quote within a few days.
