# supplier_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data about suppliers and their business practices. It helps our organization understand their performance, payment terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's name in the first field.
2. Enter the contact person's name in the second field.
3. Enter the supplier's email address in the third field.
4. Enter the supplier's phone number in the fourth field.
5. Enter the number of orders you place with the supplier in the fifth field.
6. Enter the average value of your orders with the supplier in the sixth field.
7. Enter the frequency of deliveries from the supplier in the seventh field.
8. Enter the number of days it takes for the supplier to deliver your orders in the eighth field.
9. Enter the total value of your orders with the supplier in the ninth field.
10. Rate the supplier's performance as Excellent, Good, or Bad in the eleventh field.
11. Choose the payment terms (30, 60, or 90 days) in the twelfth field.
12. Choose the payment method (Online or Offline) in the thirteenth field.
13. Enter any additional payment terms or notes in the fourteenth field.
14. Enter any additional payment terms or notes in the fifteenth field.
15. Enter the frequency of orders with the supplier in the seventeenth field.
16. Enter the frequency of deliveries from the supplier in the eighteenth field.
17. Choose the payment method (Online or Offline) for the first additional payment terms in the nineteenth field.
18. Choose the payment method (Online or Offline) for the second additional payment terms in the twentieth field.
19. Enter the name of the contact person for the first additional payment terms in the twenty-first field.
20. Enter the name of the contact person for the second additional payment terms in the twenty-second field.
21. Enter any additional payment terms or notes for the third additional payment terms in the twenty-third field.
