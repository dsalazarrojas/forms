# wholesale_equipment_rental_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a wholesale equipment rental agreement between a company and a customer for the use of equipment or machinery for a specific period. It includes fields for rental details, terms and conditions, rental terms, customer signature, and renter signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the rental details in the "Rental Details" field.
2. Review and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" in this field.
3. Enter the rental terms in the "Rental Terms" field.
4. Sign as the customer in the "Customer Signature" field.
5. Sign as the renter in the "Renter Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Details (Page 1)** (`Rental Details`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter the details of the rental, such as equipment description, rental period, and rental fees.
* **Terms and Conditions (Page 1)** (`Terms and Conditions`, `text`, not required): This field is used to display the terms and conditions of the rental, which the customer must agree to.
* **Rental Terms (Page 1)** (`Rental Terms`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter the rental terms, such as payment terms, usage rules, and any other relevant details.
* **Customer Signature (Page 1)** (`Customer Signature`, `text`, not required): This field is used for the customer to sign and date the rental agreement.
* **Renter Signature (Page 1)** (`Renter Signature`, `text`, not required): This field is used for the renter to sign and date the rental agreement.

## Tips
Make sure to review and agree to all terms and conditions before signing the rental agreement. Ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of the rental terms and conditions.
