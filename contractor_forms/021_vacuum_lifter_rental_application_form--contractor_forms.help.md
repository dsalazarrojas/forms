# Vacuum Lifter Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the rental of vacuum lifters from our company. It includes contact details, rental location, equipment type, and other relevant information to facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the rental location where the equipment will be used.
3. Choose the type of equipment (vacuum lifter, crawler, or genie).
4. Provide the start and end dates for the rental period.
5. Specify the rental duration.
6. Enter the reason for the rental.
7. Select the person to whom the equipment will be assigned.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. Review and agree to the terms of service.
10. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
