# Snow Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Removal Request Form is used to request snow removal services from the city or a contractor. This form is intended to gather necessary information for the contractor to complete the snow removal service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Provide details about the snow removal request, including the location and date for the service.
3. Select the type of snow removal service required (Snowplow, Sanding, or Blowing).
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your snow removal request.
* **Snow Info**: Enter a brief description of the snow removal request, including any specific details about the location and date for the service.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Message) to discuss your snow removal request.
* **Location**: Enter the address or location where snow removal is needed.
* **Date**: Enter the requested date for snow removal.
* **Type of Snow Removal**: Select the type of snow removal service required (Snowplow, Sanding, or Blowing).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or questions about your snow removal request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any updates or questions about your snow removal request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information about your snow removal request to ensure that the contractor can complete the service efficiently and effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating a previous request, as this may cause confusion and delay in processing the request.
