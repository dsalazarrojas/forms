# Load Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document load information.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by providing the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Load Details** (`load_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the load, including its contents and any relevant details.
* **Terms** (`terms`, text, required): Enter the specific terms of the load, such as any relevant laws or regulations that apply.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, text, required): Enter the names and signatures of those involved in the load process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the load.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to access the information in the load before completing this form.
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use a clear and concise format to enter information in each field.
