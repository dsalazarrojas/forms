# Hourly Manager Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an Hourly Manager Contract Agreement, outlining the terms and conditions of a working arrangement between a manager and a contractor.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a successful completion of this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's structure and ensure you understand the purpose and context of each section.
2.  Fill in the job details section with accurate information about the job, including the job start and end dates.
3.  Indicate whether you agree with the payment terms and frequency.
4.  Provide your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
5.  Ensure you provide the required contact information for both the manager and contractor.
6.  Review and confirm the contract's status and type.
7.  Sign the form to acknowledge agreement with the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Details** (id: 1, name: job_details, label: Job Details, type: text, required: false): Fill in the job title, description, and any other relevant details about the job.
* **Contract Duration** (id: 2, name: contract_duration, label: Contract Duration, type: number, required: false): Enter the duration of the contract in days or hours.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 3, name: payment_terms, label: Payment Terms, type: select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you agree to the payment terms, which might include rates, payment frequency, and any other conditions.
* **Payment Frequency** (id: 4, name: payment_frequency, label: Payment Frequency, type: select_one, required: false): Choose how often you want to be paid, whether weekly, biweekly, or monthly.
* **Job Start Date** (id: 5, name: job_start_date, label: Job Start Date, type: date, required: false): Enter the date when the job is expected to start.
* **Job End Date** (id: 6, name: job_end_date, label: Job End Date, type: date, required: false): Enter the date when the job is expected to end.
* **Work Hours** (id: 7, name: work_hours, label: Work Hours, type: select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you will work on specific hours or shifts.
* **Hourly Rate** (id: 8, name: hourly_rate, label: Hourly Rate, type: number, required: false): Enter the rate at which you will be paid per hour.
* **Payment Method** (id: 9, name: payment_method, label: Payment Method, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the method of payment, such as bank transfer, check, or cash.
* **Payment Frequency 2** (id: 10, name: payment_frequency_2, label: Payment Frequency 2, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the payment frequency for additional terms.
* **Notice Period** (id: 11, name: notice_period, label: Notice Period, type: select_one, required: false): Indicate the notice period for any changes or terminations in the contract.
* **Contract Type** (id: 12, name: contract_type, label: Contract Type, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the type of contract, such as hourly, project, or retainer.
* **Contract Status** (id: 13, name: contract_status, label: Contract Status, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the contract, such as active or inactive.
* **Work Place** (id: 14, name: work_place, label: Work Place, type: select_multiple, required: false): Indicate where you will be working, such as at the manager's office or remotely.
* **Job Responsibilities** (id: 15, name: job_responsibilities, label: Job Responsibilities, type: text, required: false): Describe your job responsibilities and expectations.
* **Payment Terms 2** (id: 16, name: payment_terms_2, label: Payment Terms 2, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Manager Name** (id: 17, name: manager_name, label: Manager Name, type: select_one, required: false): Choose whether you agree with the manager's name as listed.
* **Manager Email** (id: 18, name: manager_email, label: Manager Email, type: email, required: false): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (id: 19, name: manager_phone, label: Manager Phone, type: text, required: false): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Address** (id: 20, name: manager_address, label: Manager Address, type: text, required: false): Enter the manager's address.
* **Contractor Name** (id: 21, name: contractor_name, label: Contractor Name, type: text, required: false): Enter your name as the contractor.
* **Contractor Email** (id: 22, name: contractor_email, label: Contractor Email, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contractor Phone** (id: 23, name: contractor_phone, label: Contractor Phone, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Contractor Address** (id: 24, name: contractor_address, label: Contractor Address, type: text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Signature** (id: 25, name: signature, label: Signature, type: select_one, required: false): Choose whether you sign the contract, acknowledging agreement with the terms.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
* Double-check the payment terms and conditions to avoid misunderstandings.
* Ensure you have all necessary contact information for both the manager and contractor.
* Sign the contract to acknowledge agreement with the terms and conditions.
