# Contractor Marketing Discovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your marketing efforts, challenges, and goals as a contractor. We will use this information to provide you with personalized marketing recommendations and support to grow your business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much information as possible.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting, as some fields are required.
3. Use the hints provided to clarify any confusion.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): The name of your contracting business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Full name of the primary contact for your business.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Your best contact number for phone calls.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, required): How long your company has been operating. Choose the option that best represents the number of years your company has been in business.
* **Number of Employees** (`business_size`, `select_one`, required): The size of your team. This will help us understand your company's scale.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, `select_one`, required): The geographic area you serve. This could be local, regional, state-wide, national, or international.
* **Primary Services Offered** (`primary_services`, `select_multiple`, required): The services your company provides. Select all that apply.
* **Current Marketing Methods** (`current_marketing_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): How you currently market your business. Select all that apply.
* **Monthly Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `select_one`, required): Your monthly marketing budget. This will help us understand your resource allocation.
* **Website Status** (`website_status`, `select_one`, required): Do you have a business website? Choose the correct status for your website.
* **Social Media Presence** (`social_media_presence`, `select_multiple`, required): The social media platforms you use. Select all that apply.
* **Biggest Marketing Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, `select_one`, required): What is your primary marketing challenge. This will help us understand your pain points.
* **Primary Lead Source** (`lead_source_current`, `select_one`, required): The primary source of your current leads.
* **Primary Marketing Goals** (`marketing_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): The goals you want to achieve through marketing. Select all that apply.
* **Marketing Services Interested In** (`interested_services`, `select_multiple`, required): The marketing services you are interested in using to achieve your goals.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): When you would like to start marketing efforts. Choose the option that best represents your desired timeline.
* **Decision Maker** (`decision_maker`, `select_one`, required): Are you the decision maker or do you need to consult a partner or corporate approval?
* **Previous Marketing Experience** (`previous_marketing_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Have you worked with marketing agencies before? This will help us understand your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information or questions you would like to share with us.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this will help us provide you with the most accurate and effective recommendations.
* Review each field before submitting, as some are required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
