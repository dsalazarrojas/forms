# Project Price Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Price Estimation Form is a tool used to collect numerical data related to project costs, including general project costs, labor costs, materials costs, and equipment costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the different sections.
2. Fill in the relevant numerical data for each section.
3. Ensure you've answered all required fields (indicated by a star *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Project Costs (1)** (`general_project_costs`, number, optional): Enter an estimate of the overall project costs.
* **Labor Costs (2)** (`labor_costs`, number, optional): Provide an estimate of the labor costs associated with the project.
* **Materials Costs (3)** (`materials_costs`, number, optional): Enter an estimate of the materials costs required for the project.
* **Equipment Costs (4)** (`equipment_costs`, number, optional): Provide an estimate of the equipment costs related to the project.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the project team or project manager.
* Remember to save your form entries for future reference or revisions.
