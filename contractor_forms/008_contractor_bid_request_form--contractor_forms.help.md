# Contractor Bid Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for project owners or managers to collect information from potential bidders. It helps guide contractors through the process of providing a bid by asking for details about the project and the requirements for the work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and company name (if applicable) in the first section.
2. Provide a brief overview of the project in the "Project Overview" field.
3. Fill in the project details, including name, type, location, scope, and size.
4. Choose the desired start and completion dates for the project.
5. Select the project's budget range and preferred payment terms.
6. Specify any licensing, insurance, and bonding requirements.
7. Decide on the project's timeline flexibility and the preferred bid format.
8. Indicate whether a site visit is required before bidding.
9. Specify the deadlines for questions and bid submission.
10. Attach any additional documents or specifications that may be relevant to the project.
11. Finally, add any special notes or requirements that the contractor should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name, the name of the person requesting the bid.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, where bid responses should be sent.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number, where bid responses should be left.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a company, enter your company name.
* **Project Overview** (`section_project_overview`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief overview of the project.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of work for the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required): Enter the full address where the project will be performed.
* **Scope of Work** (`project_scope`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the work needed.
* **Project Size** (`project_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the approximate size or scale of the project.
* **Desired Start Date** (`desired_start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date when work should begin.
* **Desired Completion Date** (`desired_completion_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date when work should be finished.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Choose how flexible the project's timeline is.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose the estimated budget for the project.
* **Preferred Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the preferred payment structure.
* **Licensing Required** (`license_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if a professional license is necessary.
* **Insurance Required** (`insurance_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if liability insurance is necessary.
* **Bonding Required** (`bonding_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if bonding is required.
* **Permits Needed** (`permits_needed`, `select_one`, required): Check if permits are necessary.
* **Bid Submission Deadline** (`bid_deadline`, `date`, required): Choose the last date to submit a bid.
* **Preferred Bid Format** (`bid_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose how bids should be submitted.
* **Site Visit Required** (`site_visit_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if a site visit is necessary before bidding.
* **Questions Deadline** (`questions_deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter the last date to submit questions.
* **Additional Documents** (`additional_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant specifications or drawings.
* **Selection Criteria** (`selection_criteria`, `text`, optional): Specify how the winning bid will be selected.
* **Special Notes or Requirements** (`special_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information contractors should be aware of.
