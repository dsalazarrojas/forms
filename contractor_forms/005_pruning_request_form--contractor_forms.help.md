# Pruning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pruning Request Form is used to collect information from clients who need to request pruning services. This form helps us understand their specific needs and requirements for their pruning requests. Please ensure you only submit one form per request, and avoid duplication or submission of incomplete forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a pruning request, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form, which will serve as a reference for our team (e.g., "Pruning Request for Oak Tree").
2. Provide the client's name, email, and phone number so we can contact them for further discussion.
3. Enter the date and time when the client would like to schedule the pruning service.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pruning Request Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the pruning request, which will help us understand the client's needs.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Provide the full name of the client who requested the pruning service.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's valid email address so we can contact them for further discussion.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/optional): Provide the client's phone number in case we need to contact them for any reason.
* **Pruning Request Date** (`pruning_req_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the desired date for the pruning service.
* **Pruning Request Time** (`pruning_req_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the desired time for the pruning service.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate contact information for the client.
- Only submit one form per pruning request.
- Review and submit your completed form before clicking the submit button.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for assistance.
