# subcontractor_prequalification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subcontractor prequalification form is designed to collect necessary information from subcontractors to prequalify them for a project. This form helps assess the subcontractor's experience, insurance, and bonding status to ensure they are suitable for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the form carefully to understand the required fields and explanations.
2. Answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Provide complete and up-to-date information.
4. Ensure that all sections are filled out accurately and completely.
5. Review the form for errors and inconsistencies before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Subcontractor Info** (`subcontractor_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your company, including your name and contact information.
- **Subcontractor Experience** (`subcontractor_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your experience in the relevant field or industry.
- **Company Info** (`company_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your company, including its name, address, and contact information.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
- **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Please describe your insurance status and policies.
- **Insurance Policy Info** (`insurance_policy_info`, text, optional): Please provide information about your insurance policy, including the start and end dates.
- **Bonding Info** (`bonding_info`, text, optional): Please describe your bonding status and policies.
- **Subcontractor License Info** (`subcontractor_license_info`, text, optional): Please provide information about your subcontractor license.
- **Subcontractor Experience List** (`subcontractor_experience_list`, select multiple, required): Please select all experiences you have in the relevant field or industry.
- **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): Please provide the number of years you have been in business.
- **Years in Business Other** (`years_in_business_other`, text, optional): Please describe any other years of experience or business.
- **Other Years Info** (`other_years_info`, text, optional): Please provide additional information about your years in business.
- **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select one, required): Please select your insurance type.
- **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): Please provide your insurance company name.
- **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, optional): Please provide your insurance number.
- **Insurance Start Date** (`insurance_start_date`, date, required): Please provide the start date of your insurance policy.
- **Insurance End Date** (`insurance_end_date`, date, required): Please provide the end date of your insurance policy.
- **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select one, optional): Please select your insurance status.
- **Bonding Type** (`bonding_type`, select one, optional): Please select your bonding type.
- **Bonding Company** (`bonding_company`, text, optional): Please provide your bonding company name.
- **Bonding Number** (`bonding_number`, text, optional): Please provide your bonding number.
- **Bonding End Date** (`bonding_end_date`, date, optional): Please provide the end date of your bonding policy.
