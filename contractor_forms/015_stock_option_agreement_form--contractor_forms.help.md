# Stock Option Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a participant in a stock option agreement. The participant may be an employee, manager, or other individual involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name.
2. Provide the participant's title.
3. Enter the participant's email address.
4. If necessary, enter the participant's date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant, as it appears on their identification or business card.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, optional): Enter the title or position held by the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, optional): If required, enter the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the participant.
* This form can be printed or saved for future reference.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
