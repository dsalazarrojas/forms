# Landscape Maintenance Site Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscape Maintenance Site Audit Form is used to collect and record site-specific information, including address, customer contact details, and project status. This form helps maintain a clear and organized record of site visits and project progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the site address in the `site_address` field.
2. Fill in the customer's name in the `customer_name` field.
3. Provide a brief description of the site in the `site_description` field.
4. Select the current site status (`site_status`) and choose one or more maintenance status options from `maintenance_status`.
5. Enter any additional notes about the site in the `site_notes` field.
6. Provide contact information for the customer (`customer_contact`).
7. Upload or enter links to relevant photos and videos of the site (`site_photos` and `site_videos`).
8. Enter any maintenance-related notes for the site (`maintenance_notes`).
9. Select the project status (`project_status`).
10. Enter the project manager's name and contact information (`project_manager`).
11. Fill in the date and time of the site survey (`site_survey_date` and `site_survey_time`).
12. Record the date and time of the site inspection (`site_inspection_date` and `site_inspection_time`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **site_address** (Text, Required: False): Enter the address of the site.
* **customer_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the customer's name.
* **site_description** (Text, Required: False): Provide a brief description of the site.
* **site_status** (Select One, Options: Active, Inactive, Pending, Required: False): Select the current status of the site.
* **site_notes** (Note, Required: False): Enter any additional notes about the site.
* **customer_contact** (Text, Required: False): Enter the customer's contact information.
* **site_photos** (Text, Required: False): Upload or enter links to relevant photos of the site.
* **site_videos** (Text, Required: False): Upload or enter links to relevant videos of the site.
* **maintenance_notes** (Text, Required: False): Enter any maintenance-related notes for the site.
* **maintenance_status** (Select Multiple, Options: Not Started, In Progress, Complete, Required: False): Select one or more maintenance status options.
* **customer_email** (Email, Required: False): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **site_map_url** (Text, Required: False): Enter the URL of the site map.
* **site_latitude** (Number, Required: False): Enter the site's latitude.
* **site_longitude** (Number, Required: False): Enter the site's longitude.
* **site_elevation** (Number, Required: False): Enter the site's elevation.
* **project_id** (Text, Required: False): Enter the project ID.
* **project_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the project name.
* **project_status** (Select One, Options: Not Started, In Progress, Complete, Required: False): Select the project status.
* **project_notes** (Note, Required: False): Enter any project-related notes.
* **project_manager** (Text, Required: False): Enter the project manager's name and contact information.
* **site_survey_date** (Date, Required: False): Record the date of the site survey.
* **site_survey_time** (Time, Required: False): Record the time of the site survey.
* **site_inspection_date** (Date, Required: False): Record the date of the site inspection.
* **site_inspection_time** (Time, Required: False): Record the time of the site inspection.
