# Independent Contractor Classification Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to classify independent contractors for business purposes. It helps to determine the status and details of a contractor's contract, including the type of contract, contract dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields. The fields marked as "Required" are essential for processing your data.
2. Select the correct contract type from the dropdown menu in the "Contract Type" field.
3. Enter the name of your business, address, phone number, and email address.
4. Provide your contractor's social security number and tax ID number (if applicable).
5. Select your contractor status as Active or Inactive.
6. If the contract value is relevant to your business relationship, enter the contract value.
7. Answer other fields accordingly, providing all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required: false): This field determines whether the contractor is an individual or a business. Select "Yes" if the contractor is an individual, and "No" if they are a business.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business that hired or works with the contractor.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the business or the contractor.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the business or the contractor.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the business or the contractor.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Classification Type** (`classification_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the classification type of the contract. You can select multiple options.
* **Contractor Status** (`contractor_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the contractor as Active or Inactive.
* **Contract Value** (`contract_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the contract (if applicable).
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, text, required: false): Enter the tax ID number of the contractor (if applicable).
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required: false): Enter the social security number of the contractor (if applicable).
