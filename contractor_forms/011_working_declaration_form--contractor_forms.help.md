# Working Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Working Declaration Form is used to collect essential information about a worker's declaration for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the country of your origin from the dropdown list.
2.  Enter your date of entry if it is required.
3.  Fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
4.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5.  Provide your phone number for communication.
6.  Select the type(s) of work you are performing.
7.  If required, enter the date of your declaration.
8.  Enter the time of your arrival if necessary.
9.  Enter the number of work hours you have performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **country** (Select One): Choose the country where you are working from the list of available options.
*   **date_of_entry** (Date): Enter your date of entry if it is required. Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) or (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **name** (Text): Fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **phone** (Text): Provide your phone number for communication.
*   **work_type** (Select Multiple): Select the type(s) of work you are performing. You can choose one or more options from the list.
*   **declaration_date** (Date): If required, enter the date of your declaration. Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) or (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **time_of_arrival** (Time): Enter the time of your arrival if necessary. Use the standard time format (HH:MM).
*   **work_hours** (Number): Enter the number of work hours you have performed.
