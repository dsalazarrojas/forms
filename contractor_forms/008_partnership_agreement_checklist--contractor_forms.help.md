# Partnership Agreement Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a partnership agreement checklist, which is a crucial document for outlining the terms and conditions of a partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully to understand each section's relevance to your partnership.
- Fill in the relevant fields with accurate information about your partnership.
- Review and revise the form as needed to ensure it accurately reflects the terms of your partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partnership Agreement** (Page 1, id: 1, type: note, required: false): This field is a general note about the partnership agreement. It's a space for you to briefly describe the terms and conditions of your partnership.
* **Contractor Details** (Page 1, id: 2, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting contractor's details. Please fill in the relevant information about your contractor, such as their name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Partner Details** (Page 1, id: 3, type: number, required: false): Similar to the previous field, this is for collecting partner's details. Fill in the necessary information about your partner, such as their name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Partnership Terms** (Page 2, id: 4, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether the partnership is 'Yes' or 'No'. Please answer 'Yes' if the partnership has specific terms that need to be discussed, and 'No' if there are no terms to be discussed.
* **Responsibilities** (Page 2, id: 5, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select multiple responsibilities for both the contractor and partner. Please choose the responsibilities that apply to your partnership.
* **Partnership Duration** (Page 3, id: 6, type: time, required: false): This field is for selecting the duration of the partnership. Please choose a specific time frame for the partnership.
* **Exit Clauses** (Page 3, id: 7, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether there are exit clauses for the partnership. Please answer 'Yes' if there are exit clauses, and 'No' if not.
* **Payment Terms** (Page 4, id: 8, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting payment terms for the partnership. Please fill in the specific payment terms agreed upon by both parties.
* **Communication** (Page 4, id: 9, type: text, required: false): This field is for describing the communication methods and expectations for the partnership. Please write a brief description of how communication will be handled between both parties.
* **Conflict Resolution** (Page 5, id: 10, type: time, required: false): This field is for selecting a time frame for conflict resolution. Please choose a specific time frame for resolving conflicts.
* **Dispute Resolution** (Page 5, id: 11, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether there's a dispute resolution process for the partnership. Please answer 'Yes' if there's a dispute resolution process, and 'No' if not.
* **Termination** (Page 6, id: 12, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether there's a termination process for the partnership. Please answer 'Yes' if there's a termination process, and 'No' if not.
* **Amendment** (Page 6, id: 13, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether the partnership can be amended. Please answer 'Yes' if the partnership can be amended, and 'No' if not.
* **Governing Laws** (Page 7, id: 14, type: text, required: false): This field is for describing the governing laws and regulations that apply to the partnership. Please write a brief description of the relevant laws and regulations.
* **Governing State** (Page 7, id: 15, type: text, required: false): This field is for specifying the governing state for the partnership. Please choose the relevant state or province that applies to your partnership.
* **Governing City** (Page 7, id: 16, type: text, required: false): This field is for specifying the governing city for the partnership. Please choose the relevant city that applies to your partnership.
* **Partnership Type** (Page 8, id: 17, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of partnership. Please choose whether it's a 'Yes' or 'No' type partnership.
* **Ownership Percentage** (Page 8, id: 18, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting the ownership percentage for the partnership. Please fill in the specific percentage of ownership.
* **Ownership Type** (Page 9, id: 19, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of ownership. Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' for the ownership type.
* **Partner Ownership** (Page 9, id: 20, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting the partner's ownership percentage. Please fill in the specific percentage of ownership.
* **Partner Capital** (Page 9, id: 21, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting the partner's capital contribution. Please fill in the specific amount of capital contributed.
* **Partner Contribution** (Page 10, id: 22, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting the partner's contribution. Please fill in the specific amount of contribution.
* **Partner Ownership Type** (Page 10, id: 23, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of partner's ownership. Please choose whether it's a 'Yes' or 'No' type ownership.
* **Exit Procedure** (Page 11, id: 24, type: select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting whether there's an exit procedure for the partnership. Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' for the exit procedure.
* **Exit Fees** (Page 11, id: 25, type: number, required: false): This field is for collecting the exit fees. Please fill in the specific amount of exit fees.

## Tips
- Review and revise this form carefully to ensure it accurately reflects the terms of your partnership.
- Consult with your partner or legal advisor to ensure all terms are covered.
- Keep a copy of this form for future reference.
