# Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's return to work or leave of absence due to an injury or medical reason. It provides a clear and concise way for employees to communicate with their employer about their work status and any necessary medical accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your employer's information**: In the "Employer Name" field, please enter the name of your company or organization.
2. **Enter your employee information**: In the "Employee Name", "Job Title", and "Company Abbreviation" fields, please enter your own information as it appears on your personnel record.
3. **Enter work dates**: In the "Work Start Date" and "Work End Date" fields, please enter the dates of your return to work or leave of absence.
4. **Enter reason for work release**: In the "Reason for Work Release" field, please provide a brief description of the reason for your work release.
5. **Enter medical information (optional)**: If you have a medical condition that is affecting your ability to perform your job duties, please enter the relevant information in the "Medical Condition" field.
6. **Sign and date**: Finally, please sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information contained within.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will be returning to work.
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you left work due to the medical condition.
* **Reason for Work Release** (`reason_for_work_release`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for your work release.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information contained within.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Company Abbreviation** (`company_abbrev`, text, required): Enter the abbreviation or acronym for your company.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have a medical condition that is affecting your ability to perform your job duties, please enter the relevant information here.
* **Doctor's Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Please have your doctor sign and date the form to confirm their involvement in your care.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor's Title** (`doctor_title`, text, required): Enter the title or profession of your healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide all necessary information to facilitate a smooth transition back into work.
* Review and understand the information contained within before signing and dating the form.
