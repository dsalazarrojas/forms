# Voice Over Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Over Service Agreement form is used to document the details of a voice over project, including client information, project specifics, and terms of agreement. This form is meant to capture essential details to ensure clear communication and understanding between the voice over artist and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section as required.
2. Enter client information in the "Project Details" section.
3. Select the desired "Voice Over Artist" from the provided options.
4. Enter the "Client Name", "Client Email", and "Client Phone" for your records.
5. Set the contract start and end dates in the "Contract Start Date" and "Contract End Date" fields.
6. Add a description of the project in the "Project Description" section.
7. Choose applicable project terms from the "Project Terms" options.
8. Select whether or not you accept the client's agreement in the "Client Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details (id: 1)** (`text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the project, including the title, genre, or any other relevant details.
* **Client Name (id: 2)** (`text`, not required): Enter the name of the client for your reference.
* **Client Email (id: 3)** (`email`, not required): Enter the email address of the client for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone (id: 4)** (`text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the client for contact purposes.
* **Voice Over Artist (id: 5)** (`select_one`, not required): Select the voice over artist you would like to work with. This is typically the artist who will be performing the voice over work.
* **Contract Start Date (id: 6)** (`date`, not required): Set the start date of the contract, indicating when the voice over work is expected to begin.
* **Contract End Date (id: 7)** (`date`, not required): Set the end date of the contract, indicating when the voice over work is expected to conclude.
* **Project Description (id: 8)** (`note`, not required): A detailed description of the project, including any specific requirements or instructions.
* **Project Terms (id: 9)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select the terms that apply to this project.
* **Signature (id: 10)** (`select_one`, not required): Indicate your acceptance of the client's agreement.
* **Contract Date (id: 11)** (`date`, not required): Set the date you signed this agreement.
* **Signed By (id: 12)** (`text`, not required): Enter your name as the signatory of this agreement.
* **Client Agreement (id: 13)** (`select_one`, not required): Select whether you agree to the client's terms.

## Tips
* Be sure to clearly enter client contact information for smooth communication.
* Review and understand each field carefully before proceeding.
* Save this form for your records and future reference.
