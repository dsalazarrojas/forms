# Service Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Estimate Form is a tool used by service providers to collect information from clients about their job requirements and provide a detailed estimate of the services needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client name in the Client Name field.
2. Provide details about the job in the Job Type and Service Description fields.
3. Enter the estimated labor and material costs and hours in the Labor Cost, Material Cost, Labor Hours, and Material Hours fields.
4. Set the job status and service estimate status in the Job Status and Service Estimate Status fields.
5. If payment terms are applicable, enter the payment terms, note, date, and time in the Payment Terms fields.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, required): Select the type of job that needs to be done (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Service Description** (service_description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the service required.
* **Labor Cost** (labor_cost, number, required): Enter the estimated labor cost for the job.
* **Material Cost** (material_cost, number, required): Enter the estimated material cost for the job.
* **Labor Hours** (labor_hours, number, optional): Enter the estimated labor hours required for the job.
* **Material Hours** (material_hours, number, optional): Enter the estimated material hours required for the job.
* **Total Cost** (total_cost, number, optional): Enter the total estimated cost for the job.
* **Service Estimate** (service_estimate, number, optional): Enter the service estimate.
* **Labor Rate** (labor_rate, number, optional): Enter the labor rate.
* **Material Rate** (material_rate, number, optional): Enter the material rate.
* **Job Status** (job_status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the job (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Service Estimate Status** (service_estimate_status, select_one, required): Select the status of the service estimate (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms, text, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Note** (payment_terms_note, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Date** (payment_terms_date, date, optional): Enter the date for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Time** (payment_terms_time, time, optional): Enter the time for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Total** (payment_terms_total, number, optional): Enter the total payment terms.
* **Service Estimate Total** (service_estimate_total, number, optional): Enter the total service estimate.
* **Job Comments** (job_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the job.
* **Job Completed** (job_completed, select_one, optional): Select whether the job has been completed (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Terms Status** (payment_terms_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of the payment terms (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Notes** (notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
