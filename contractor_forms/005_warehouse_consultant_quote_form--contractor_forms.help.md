# Warehouse Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Consultant Quote Form is a tool designed to help clients quickly and easily request a quote for a warehouse consultation. This form allows clients to provide necessary information about their request and consultants to provide their services and pricing for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, simply click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. In the **client_info** field, enter your name and contact information so that the consultant can contact you to discuss your quote.
3. In the **consultant_info** field, enter the name and contact information of the consultant you would like to work with.
4. In the **list_services** field, select the services you would like to receive quotes for.
5. In the **service_cost** field, enter the cost of the selected services.
6. In the **payment_terms** field, describe any payment terms or conditions that need to be met.
7. In the **notes** field, enter any additional comments or details about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information so that the consultant can contact you to discuss your quote.
* **consultant_info** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the consultant you would like to work with.
* **list_services** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the services you would like to receive quotes for. Options are:
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3
* **service_cost** (`number`, required: false): Enter the cost of the selected services.
* **payment_terms** (`text`, required: false): Describe any payment terms or conditions that need to be met.
* **notes** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all relevant services to ensure you receive a comprehensive quote.
* Be as detailed as possible in the **payment_terms** field to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the consultant directly.
* The consultant will review and submit a quote to you for your approval.
