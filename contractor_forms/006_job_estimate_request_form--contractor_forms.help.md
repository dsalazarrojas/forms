# Job Estimate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Estimate Request Form is used to collect information about a job from a client. This form helps the contractor to request an estimate for a job, which is a detailed breakdown of the work to be done, the timeframe, and the cost. The form is used to gather contact information, job details, and other relevant notes from the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Provide the client's contact information, including their name and email address.
3. Enter the job description, which should include details about the work to be done.
4. Enter the job start date and duration.
5. Enter the job estimation (optional).
6. Choose the job status (optional).
7. Select the job priority (required).
8. Enter any client notes or job notes (optional).
9. Attach any relevant job files or images (optional).
10. Enter any job comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Page 1)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the client's contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Job Description (Page 2)**: Provide detailed information about the job, including the work to be done, the timeframe, and any other relevant notes.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the expected start date of the job.
* **Job Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the job in days or weeks.
* **Job Estimation**: Enter a rough estimate of the job's duration or cost (optional).
* **Job Status**: Choose the status of the job, which is currently not started, in progress, or finished (optional).
* **Job Priority**: Select the priority level of the job, which can be low, medium, or high (required).
* **Client Notes**: Enter any notes from the client (optional).
* **Job Notes**: Enter any notes about the job (optional).
* **Job Files**: Attach any files related to the job (optional).
* **Job Attachments**: Attach any relevant attachments (optional).
* **Job Images**: Attach any relevant images or screenshots (optional).
* **Job Comments**: Enter any comments about the job (optional).
