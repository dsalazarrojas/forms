# Excavation Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavation Permit Form is used to document site information for sites where excavation work is being or has been performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the site address in the **Site Address** field.
2. Select the **Excavation Date** the work is being or has been performed on.
3. Provide a **Site Description** of the work being performed.
4. Enter the **Site Location** where the work is being or has been performed.
5. Provide the contact information for the **Site Contact**, including their name and phone number.
6. Select the type of **Excavation Type** being performed (A, B, C, D, E).
7. Enter the **Excavation Depth** of the work being performed.
8. Enter the **Site Area** where the work is being or has been performed.
9. Add any additional **Site Notes** about the work.
10. Enter the site contact's **Site Phone** number.
11. Enter the site contact's **Site Email** address.
12. Enter the site contact's name in the **Site Manager** field.
13. Enter the site contact's supervisor's name in the **Site Supervisor** field.
14. Enter the person supervising the excavation in the **Excavation Supervisor** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Address**: Enter the address of the site where the work is being or has been performed.
* **Excavation Date**: Select the date the work is being or has been performed on.
* **Site Description**: Provide a brief description of the work being performed on the site.
* **Site Location**: Enter the location where the work is being or has been performed (e.g. building, room, floor).
* **Site Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the site.
* **Excavation Type**: Select the type of excavation being performed (A, B, C, D, E).
* **Excavation Depth**: Enter the depth of the excavation work.
* **Site Area**: Enter the area where the work is being or has been performed.
* **Site Notes**: Add any additional notes about the site or work.
* **Site Phone**: Enter the site contact's phone number.
* **Site Email**: Enter the site contact's email address.
* **Site Manager**: Enter the name of the site manager.
* **Site Supervisor**: Enter the name of the site supervisor.
* **Excavation Supervisor**: Enter the name of the person supervising the excavation.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help ensure the form is filled out correctly.
