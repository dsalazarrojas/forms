# Carpet Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your carpet cleaning requirements and preferences. It will help our team schedule and prepare for your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding.
3. Use the drop-down menus to select your preferred options for each field.
4. If you need help, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): This is your name for our records and communication.
	+ Please enter your full name, including first and last name.
	+ No need to include title or suffix (e.g., Mr., Mrs., etc.).
* **Email Address** (Required): This is where we will contact you for booking confirmations and updates.
	+ Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (Required): This is your contact number for last-minute updates and scheduling changes.
	+ Please enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Property Address** (Required): This is where the cleaning service will be performed.
	+ Please enter the full address of the property.
* **City** (Required): This is the city where the property is located.
	+ Please enter the correct city name (e.g., New York City).
* **Zip Code** (Required): This is the zip code of the property location.
	+ Please enter the correct zip code.
* **Type of Carpet Cleaning Service** (Required): Select the type of service you require (e.g., Standard, Deep Cleaning, etc.).
	+ Please choose from the given options based on your needs.
* **Area to be Cleaned** (Required): Select the area(s) that needs cleaning.
	+ Please choose from the given options based on the areas you need cleaned.
* **Approximate Square Footage** (Optional): This will help us estimate the cleaning time and cost.
	+ Please enter the approximate square footage of the area to be cleaned.
* **Current Carpet Condition** (Required): Select the current condition of your carpet.
	+ Please choose from the given options based on the actual condition of your carpet.
* **Special Issues or Concerns** (Optional): Describe any specific stains, odors, or damage.
	+ Please provide detailed information to help us understand your requirements.
* **Do you have Pets?** (Required): Yes or No?
	+ Please respond to this question based on your situation.
* **Preferred Service Date** (Required): Select your preferred service date.
	+ Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Preferred Time Window** (Required): Select your preferred service time.
	+ Please choose from the given options based on your availability.
* **Special Access Instructions** (Optional): Provide any special access instructions for our team (e.g., gate codes, etc.).
* **Do you want Furniture Moved?** (Required): Yes, No, or Partly?
	+ Please respond to this question based on your preferences.
* **Eco-Friendly Products** (Required): Yes, No, or No Preference?
	+ Please choose from the given options based on your preferences.
* **Additional Services Needed?** (Optional): Select any additional services you require.
	+ Please choose from the given options based on your needs.
* **Budget Estimate** (Optional): Indicate your budget range.
	+ Please choose from the given options based on your budget.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (Optional): Any additional information or questions you have.
	+ Please provide detailed information to help us serve you better.

## Tips

* Please double-check that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact our support team.
* Our team will review and contact you with any issues or questions.
* We will contact you to schedule your appointment once we receive your form submission.
