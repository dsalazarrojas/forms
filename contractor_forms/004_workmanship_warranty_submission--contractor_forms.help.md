# Workmanship Warranty Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit their workmanship warranty claims, which allows us to track and resolve any potential issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields accurately and honestly.
3.  Provide detailed descriptions where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact.
* **Address 1 Line** (`address_1_line`, text, required): Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address 2 Line** (`address_2_line`, text, required): Enter the second line of your address.
* **Address 3 Line** (`address_3_line`, text, required): Enter the third line of your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Your city or town name.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state from the provided list.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date Received Service** (`date_received_service`, date, required): The date you received the service.
