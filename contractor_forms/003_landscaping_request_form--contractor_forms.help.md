# Landscaping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Request Form is used by users to submit a request to the facilities team for landscaping-related services.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Provide a detailed description of your request in the field labeled "Request Details".
6. Choose a preferred date for the requested work in the field labeled "Preferred Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on official documents. 
* **Last Name**: Enter the surname as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid work email address. This is how the facilities team will contact you to confirm your request and provide updates.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached to discuss your request.
* **Request Details**: Provide a clear and concise description of the landscaping work you require. This will help the facilities team understand your needs accurately.
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date when the work can be conducted. The facilities team will try to schedule the work on or before this date, but please note that exact dates cannot be guaranteed.

## Tips

- Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Be clear and thorough in your description of the required work.
- If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, contact the facilities team via the email or phone number provided.
