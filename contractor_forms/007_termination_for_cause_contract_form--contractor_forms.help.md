# Termination For Cause Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Termination For Cause Contract Form is used to formally document the termination of a contract due to a specific reason or cause. This form is used to provide a clear and concise explanation of the circumstances surrounding the contract's termination. It is an essential document in maintaining accurate records and facilitating communication between parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for "Termination Cause" and "Contract Status" from the provided lists.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the termination, if applicable.
4. Ensure that all dates are in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
5. Sign and date the form by both the contractor and the manager.
