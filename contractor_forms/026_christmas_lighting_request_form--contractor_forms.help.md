# Christmas Lighting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Lighting Request Form is used to gather information from customers who want to request Christmas lighting services for their properties. This form will help us understand their needs and provide a quote for the requested services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number. These details are required to ensure we can contact you to discuss your lighting needs.
2. Enter your property's address, including the city and state or province.
3. Select the type of property you are requesting lighting for (e.g., single family home, commercial building, etc.).
4. Choose the type of installation you require (e.g., roof lights, pathway lights, etc.).
5. Specify your desired installation date.
6. Select your budget range for the lighting installation.
7. If you have any special requests or additional notes, please add them in the "Additional Notes or Special Requirements" field.
8. Indicate if you need our removal service in January.
9. Finally, select your preferred light color and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to input your first name.
* **Last Name**: A field to input your last name.
* **Email Address**: A field to input your email address.
* **Phone Number**: A field to input your phone number.
* **Property Address**: A field to input your property's address.
* **City**: A field to input your property's city.
* **State or Province**: A field to input your property's state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: A field to input your property's ZIP or postal code.
* **Property Type**: A dropdown menu to select the type of property you are requesting lighting for.
* **Type of Installation**: A dropdown menu to select the type of installation you require.
* **Desired Installation Date**: A date field to specify your desired installation date.
* **Budget Range**: A dropdown menu to select your budget range for the lighting installation.
* **Desired Design Style**: A field to input any specific design style you have in mind.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements**: A field to add any additional notes or special requirements.
* **Will you need removal service in January?**: A dropdown menu to indicate if you need our removal service in January.
* **Light Color Preferences**: A dropdown menu to select your preferred light color.
* **Light Type Preference**: A dropdown menu to select your preferred light type.
