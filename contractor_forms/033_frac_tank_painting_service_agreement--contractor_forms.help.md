# Frac Tank Painting Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frac Tank Painting Service Agreement form is designed to collect essential information from clients regarding a painting service for frac tanks. This form will help establish a clear agreement between the client and the service provider, outlining the scope of work, start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Provide your name, company, and contact information as the client.
2. Fill in the **Scope of Work** section with a detailed description of the painting service required.
3. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for the service.
4. In the **Service Details** section, provide any additional information about the service, such as specific paint colors or coatings.
5. Sign and date the **Signatures** section to confirm agreement with the terms of the service.
6. **Comments** (optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in this section.
7. **Assigned User**: If you have a specific individual assigned to oversee the service, provide their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (`text`, `optional`): This section is for you, the client, to provide your name, company name, and contact information. This information will help us understand who to contact and how to reach you regarding the service.
- **Scope of Work** (`text`, `required`): Describe the painting service you require for your frac tank. This could include details about the tank's specifications, the type of paint or coating needed, and any specific requirements or constraints.
- **Start Date** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date when you would like the painting service to begin.
- **End Date** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date when you would like the painting service to conclude.
- **Service Details** (`text`, `required`): Provide any additional information about the service, such as specific paint colors or coatings, that will help us better understand your needs.
- **Signatures** (`text`, `required`): Sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the service.
- **Comments** (`text`, `optional`): If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in this section.
- **Assigned User** (`text`, `optional`): If you have a specific individual assigned to oversee the service, provide their name.
