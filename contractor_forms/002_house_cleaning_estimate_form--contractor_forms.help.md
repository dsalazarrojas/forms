# house_cleaning_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The house_cleaning_estimate_form is designed to gather information from users who are requesting estimates for house cleaning services. This form collects contact information and details about the services needed for a cleaning estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the services needed in the "Estimate Form" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your name, address, and phone number.
3. In the "Services" section, select the type of services you require (e.g., Cleaning, Carpet Cleaning, Window Cleaning).
4. If you have any additional questions or comments, you can provide them in the "Contact Form" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estimate Form** (Estimate Form, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the services you need.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Services** (Services, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific services you need, such as Cleaning, Carpet Cleaning, or Window Cleaning.
* **Contact Form** (Contact Form, text, optional): If you have any additional questions or comments, enter them here.
