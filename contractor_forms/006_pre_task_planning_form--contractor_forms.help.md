# Pre Task Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Task Planning Form is a tool used to gather essential information from clients and project stakeholders before commencing a new project or task. This form helps to ensure that everyone involved is aware of the project's scope, requirements, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and company information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Describe the task or project in the "Task Information" section.
3. Choose the status of the task or project from the dropdown menu in the "Task Status" section.
4. Enter the client's name and contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the task or project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter the client's company name and any other relevant details.
* **Task Information**: Briefly describe the task or project, including its objectives and scope.
* **Task Status**: Choose the status of the task or project from the dropdown menu. This will help track the progress and status of the task.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name who is assigned to this task or project.
* **Company**: Enter the client's company name, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address for easy communication.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for direct contact.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the task or project.
* **Tasks**: Select one or more tasks that apply to the project or task.
