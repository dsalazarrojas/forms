# Service Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend an existing service agreement between a client and a contractor. It helps to update the service agreement by collecting new dates and reasons for the extension.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form. This includes the name of the contractor, the current extension date, the new start date of the service, and the new end date of the service.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Provide a clear reason for the extension in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Agreement Extension Form**: This field allows you to enter a brief description or a unique name for this extension. You can type in anything you like.
- **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the contractor who is extending the service agreement.
- **Extension Date**: Enter the date when the extension is taking place. This is when the service agreement is being extended.
- **New Service Start Date**: Enter the new start date of the service after the extension.
- **New Service End Date**: Enter the new end date of the service after the extension.
- **Reason for Extension**: Provide a clear and concise description of why the service agreement is being extended.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all dates for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact your service agreement manager or the contractor.
- Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form, as this information is crucial for the service agreement extension process.
