# Winterization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for contractors and site owners to submit a winterization request to prepare their site or building for the cold winter months. It helps the site management team to efficiently plan and execute the necessary winterization tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the winterization project in the "Winterization Request Form" field.
2. Provide your name as the contractor who will be performing the winterization work in the "Contractor Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Contractor Phone" field so that we can contact you to confirm the details.
4. Enter your email address in the "Contractor Email" field to ensure we can contact you with any updates or questions.
5. Select the date and time when you would like to start the winterization work in the "Winterization Date" and "Winterization Time" fields.
6. Add any specific notes or comments in the "Winterization Note" field about the site or building that requires winterization.
7. Provide the address of the site or building that needs winterization in the "Contractor Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Winterization Request Form** (`winterization_request_form`, text, required): This field is for the title of the winterization project.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the contractor who will be performing the winterization work.
* **Contractor Phone** (`contractor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm the details.
* **Contractor Email** (`contractor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Winterization Date** (`winterization_date`, date, required): Select the date when you would like to start the winterization work.
* **Winterization Time** (`winterization_time`, time, required): Select the time when you would like to start the winterization work.
* **Winterization Note** (`winterization_note`, note, required): Add any specific notes or comments about the site or building that requires winterization.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the site or building that needs winterization.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing of your winterization request.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We will review and process your request within 24-48 hours after submission.
