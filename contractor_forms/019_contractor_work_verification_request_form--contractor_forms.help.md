# Contractor Work Verification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contractor Work Verification Request Form is a tool used to request verification of work performed by a contractor. This form is used to gather information about the contractor, the work performed, and the verification requirements. It is typically used by organizations to obtain verification of work completed by contractors for employment references, license applications, insurance claims, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Requester Information**: Enter your full name and email address so that we can contact you for further information or clarification if needed.
2. **Contractor Information**: Enter the name of the contractor and their company (if applicable) you are requesting verification for.
3. **Work Details**: Enter the name of the project, location of the work, type of work performed, and provide a detailed description of the work completed.
4. **Verification Requirements**: Select the purpose of the verification and choose what details need to be verified.
5. **Verification Contact**: Enter the name and email of the person who can verify the work completed.
6. **Submission Details**: Enter the deadline for the verification and choose how the verification should be provided.
7. **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have permission to request this verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (1): Your full name.
* **Requester Email** (2): Your email address for communication.
* **Requester Phone** (3): Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (4): The name of the requesting organization.
* **Contractor Name** (6): The name of the contractor to be verified.
* **Contractor Company** (7): The name of the contractor company (if applicable).
* **Contractor Email** (8): Email of the contractor (if known).
* **Contractor Phone** (9): Phone number of the contractor (if known).
* **Project Name** (11): The name of the project the work was performed on.
* **Project Location** (12): The location where the work was performed.
* **Type of Work Performed** (13): The category of work performed (e.g., Construction, Renovation, Electrical).
* **Work Description** (14): A detailed description of the work completed.
* **Work Start Date** (15): The date the work began.
* **Work End Date** (16): The date the work ended.
* **Purpose of Verification** (18): Why do you need this verification (e.g., Employment Reference, License Application).
* **Details to Verify** (19): What details need to be verified (e.g., Work Completed, Quality of Work).
* **Verifier Name** (21): Name of the person who can verify the work.
* **Verifier Title** (22): Position or role of the verifier.
* **Verifier Email** (23): Email of the person who can verify.
* **Verifier Phone** (24): Phone number of the person who can verify.
* **Verification Deadline** (26): Deadline for the verification.
* **Urgency Level** (27): How urgent is this request (e.g., Urgent, High, Normal, Low).
* **Preferred Verification Format** (28): How the verification should be provided (e.g., Email, Printed Letter).
* **Supporting Documents** (29): List any documents attached.
* **Additional Comments** (30): Any other relevant information.
* **Permission to Contact** (31): Confirm that you have permission to request this verification.
