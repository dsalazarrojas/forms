# Get An Estimate For Some Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for an estimate on a specific service. Please provide the required details to help us better understand your service request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as prompted, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Service 
*   **Customer Service**: This field is used to describe the service you need.

### 2. Service to be Estimated
*   **Service to be Estimated**: Enter a detailed description of the service you need estimated.

### 3. Customer Name
*   **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your records.

### 4. Customer Email
*   **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where we can reach you.

### 5. Customer Phone
*   **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.

### 6. Estimated Price
*   **Estimated Price**: Enter an estimate of the price for the service.

### 7. Service Description
*   **Service Description**: Describe the service you need in as much detail as possible.

### 8. Estimated Completion Time
*   **Estimated Completion Time**: Enter the estimated time the service will take to complete.

### 9. Estimated Completion Date
*   **Estimated Completion Date**: Enter the estimated date the service will be completed.

### 10. Assigned Technician
*   **Assigned Technician**: Select 'Yes' if you would like us to assign a technician to your service, or 'No' if you prefer to handle it yourself.

### 11. Estimated Start Date
*   **Estimated Start Date**: Enter the date you would like the service to start.

### 12. Service Status
*   **Service Status**: Select the status of the service (Active or Inactive).

### 13. Customer Comment
*   **Customer Comment**: Any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

### 14. Service Address
*   **Service Address**: Enter the address where the service will be performed.

This help guide will assist customers in filling out the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that we have all the necessary information to provide a comprehensive estimate for their service request.
