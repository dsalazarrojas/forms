# Creative Agency Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend an existing contract with an agency. Please complete the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided to process the contract extension.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please make sure you are completing this form for the correct purpose: you have an existing contract with the agency and are requesting to extend it.
2.  Complete the form carefully, providing all required information in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contractor_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for the contractor's first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): This field is for the contractor's email address. Please enter your agency-provided email address or the one you use for official communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, not required): This field is for the contractor's phone number. Please enter your agency-provided phone number or the one you use for official communications.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, not required): This field is for the contract date. If you are unsure of the contract date, please contact your agency representative.
* **First Page** (`first_page`, `text`, not required): This field is not in use, please do not complete it.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `number`, not required): This field is not used, please do not complete it.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): This field is not used, please do not complete it.
* **Last Page** (`last_page`, `select_one`, not required): This field is not used, please do not complete it. Please select one of the options below:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3

## Tips

* Please make sure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your contract extension.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your agency representative.
* Please note that the agency will review and process the form as soon as possible after submission.
