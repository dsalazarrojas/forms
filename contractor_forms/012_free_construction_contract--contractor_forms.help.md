# free-construction-contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used for free construction contracts, providing a structured template for capturing essential information between a client and a contractor. The form covers various aspects of the contract, including contact details, payment information, and contract status. It serves as a starting point for creating a formal agreement between the parties involved in a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information page with your details as the client or contractor.
2. Enter the contract details, including any necessary descriptions and dates.
3. Provide payment information, including the payment methods and schedule.
4. Specify the payment terms and rates, if applicable.
5. Enter the contract signing date, start date, and end date.
6. Capture the contract signing method and time.
7. Fill in the payment status, if applicable.
8. Complete the contract review date and time, along with the review status and comments.
9. Specify the client name, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Contract Details (Contract Details)**: Describe the construction project, including any necessary details and dates.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information)**: Specify the payment methods and schedule.
* **Contractor Information (Contractor Information)**: Enter the contractor's details, including name and contact information.
* **Contract Status (Contract Status)**: Describe the current status of the contract.
* **Contract Duration (Contract Duration)**: Enter the contract duration, if applicable.
* **Contract Signatures (Contract Signatures)**: Capture the signatures of both parties.
* **Payment Methods (Payment Methods)**: Specify the methods of payment, including any applicable rates.
* **Payment Schedule (Payment Schedule)**: Enter the payment schedule, including any necessary details.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: Describe the payment terms, if applicable.
* **Payment Rates (Payment Rates)**: Enter the payment rates, if applicable.
* **Contract Signing Date (Contract Signing Date)**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract Start Date (Contract Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date (Contract End Date)**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contractor Signature (Contractor Signature)**: Capture the contractor's signature.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature)**: Capture the client's signature.
* **Contract Signing Time (Contract Signing Time)**: Enter the time the contract was signed.
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)**: Describe the current payment status.
* **Contract Signing Method (Contract Signing Method)**: Specify the method used for contract signing.
* **Contract Review Date (Contract Review Date)**: Enter the date the contract was reviewed.
* **Contract Review Time (Contract Review Time)**: Enter the time the contract was reviewed.
* **Contract Review Status (Contract Review Status)**: Describe the review status of the contract.
* **Contract Review Comments (Contract Review Comments)**: Enter any comments or feedback on the contract.
* **Client Name (Client Name)**: Enter the name of the client.
