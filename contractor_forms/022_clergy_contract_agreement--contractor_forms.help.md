# Clergy Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a written contract between a clergy member and a church or congregation, outlining their role, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the contract terms carefully before signing.
3. Ensure you have attached any additional terms or attachments as specified in the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Clergy Member Full Name
**Clergy Member Full Name** (`clergy_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the clergy member to enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.

### 2. Name of Church/Congregation
**Name of Church/Congregation** (`church_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the church or congregation's name as it appears on official documents.

### 3. Assignment Title
**Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, `text`, required): This field is for the title or position held by the clergy member, e.g. Lead Pastor, Associate Rector, or Youth Minister.

### 4. Effective Start Date
**Effective Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): This field is for the start date of the contract term.

### 5. Contract End Date
**Contract End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): If the contract has a fixed term, this field is for the end date. Otherwise, leave blank.

### 6. Is this contract renewable?
**Is this contract renewable?** (`renewable_contract`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if the contract can be renewed, 'False' otherwise. If renewable, indicate the conditions for renewal.

### 7. Liturgical and Worship Duties
**Liturgical and Worship Duties** (`liturgical_duties`, `text`, required): Describe the clergy member's liturgical and worship responsibilities, including weekly services, sacraments, etc.

### 8. Pastoral Care and Counseling
**Pastoral Care and Counseling** (`pastoral_care`, `text`, required): Describe the clergy member's pastoral care and counseling responsibilities, including visitation, counseling hours, etc.

### 9. Administrative and Committee Duties
**Administrative and Committee Duties** (`administrative_duties`, `text`, required): Describe the clergy member's administrative and committee responsibilities, including tasks and expectations.

### 10. Annual Salary/Stipend
**Annual Salary/Stipend ($)** (`annual_salary`, `number`, required): Enter the annual salary or stipend of the clergy member.

### 11. Housing Allowance Provided?
**Housing Allowance Provided?** (`housing_allowance`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if the church provides a housing allowance, 'False' otherwise. If a parsonage is provided, check 'Parsonage provided'.

### 12. Benefits Included
**Benefits Included** (`benefits_included`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the benefits included in the contract, such as Health Insurance, Pension Plan, Continuing Education Allowance, Travel Reimbursement, and Vacation Time.

### 13. I acknowledge and agree to the terms stated in this agreement
**I acknowledge and agree to the terms stated in this agreement** (`signature_acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Check 'I agree' to acknowledge and agree to the contract terms. If not agreeing, select 'Other' and describe your concerns.

### 14. Date of Agreement
**Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement`, `date`, required): Enter the date the contract is signed.

### 15. Date of Board/Vestry Approval
**Date of Board/Vestry Approval** (`board_approval_date`, `date`, optional): If the contract requires approval from the church board or vestry, enter the date of approval. Otherwise, leave blank.

### 16. Name of Authorized Church Official
**Name of Authorized Church Official** (`authorized_official`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the authorized church official who approved the contract.
