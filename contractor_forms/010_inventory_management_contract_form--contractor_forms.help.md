# inventory_management_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for inventory management contract purposes. It captures essential information related to inventory contracts, including inventory details, delivery information, customer and contractor details, and contract terms. The form is designed to gather all necessary data in one place for efficient inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure all fields marked as "required" are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description or title for the contract.
* **Inventory Info** (Text, Required): Provide detailed information about the inventory to be managed.
* **Inventory Location** (Select Multiple, Required): Select relevant locations where the inventory will be used.
*   *   **Note**: This field is intended for selecting multiple locations.
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Delivery Info** (Text, Required): Provide details about the delivery of the contract.
* **Customer Signature** (Note, Required): Include your signature as proof of acceptance of the contract.
* **Customer Info** (Select One, Required): Select the relevant customer information.
*   *   **Note**: This field is intended for selecting a customer from a predefined list.
* **Contractor Signature** (Select One, Required): Select your signature as the contractor.
*   *   **Note**: This field is intended for selecting your signature as the contractor.
* **Delivery Address** (Text, Required): Provide the address where the contract will be delivered.
* **Delivery Method** (Select One, Required): Select the delivery method of the contract.
* **Delivery Info** (Select Multiple, Required): Select relevant information about the delivery.
*   *   **Note**: This field is intended for selecting multiple delivery information.
* **Contract Terms** (Text, Required): Enter or describe the terms of the contract.
* **Contract Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Required): Enter the payment terms related to the contract.
* **Payment Info** (Text, Required): Provide additional payment information.
* **Contract Duration** (Text, Required): Enter the duration of the contract.
* **Contract Status** (Select One, Required): Select the current status of the contract.
*   *   **Note**: This field is intended for selecting the current contract status.
* **Contract Notes** (Text, Required): Enter any additional notes related to the contract.
*   *   **Note**: This field is not limited in length.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (Text, Required): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Contractor Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the contractor.
* **Contractor Info** (Text, Required): Enter any additional information about you as the contractor.
