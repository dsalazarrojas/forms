# Judges Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a judge's agreement to a contract. The purpose of this form is to gather information that confirms a judge's understanding and agreement with the terms of the contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the Event Name in the provided field.
2. Select the Event Date and Event Time from the calendar picker.
3. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
4. If applicable, provide a valid Email address.
5. If applicable, provide a valid Phone number.
6. Read through and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
7. Sign and date the contract with your signature.
8. Review the completed form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (1.1, text, required): Enter the name of the event or case you are being assigned to.
* **Event Date** (1.2, date, required): Select the date of the event or case.
* **Event Time** (1.3, time, required): Select the time of the event or case.
* **First Name** (1.4, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (1.5, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (1.6, email, optional): If you have a professional email address, enter it here. This field is not required but may be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (1.7, text, optional): If you have a phone number that is associated with this account, enter it here. This field is not required but may be used for communication purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (1.8, note, required): Carefully review the terms of the contract and agree to them before signing below.
* **Signature** (1.9, text, required): Sign and date this contract to confirm your agreement to the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and legible information to ensure the form is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your administrative support for assistance.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* Make sure to sign and date the contract before submission.
