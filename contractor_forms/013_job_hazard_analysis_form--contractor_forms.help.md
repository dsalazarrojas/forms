# Job Hazard Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Hazard Analysis Form is used to identify, assess, and control hazards associated with a particular job or task. It helps workers understand and document potential hazards and take steps to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions and the introduction page carefully.
2. Review the hazard identification section and identify any hazards you encounter.
3. Assess each identified hazard using the hazard assessment section.
4. Select the appropriate measures to control or prevent each hazard in the hazard prevention section.
5. Describe any additional safety measures you have taken or plan to take in the safety measures section.
6. Summarize your findings and recommendations in the recommendations section.
7. Review your answers and ensure that you have identified all relevant hazards and taken adequate measures to prevent or control them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction (Note, not required)**: This page provides background information on the job or task and explains the purpose and scope of the form.
- **Hazard Identification (Number, not required)**: Identify potential hazards associated with the job or task. This could include physical hazards like chemicals, noise, or machinery, or environmental hazards like weather conditions or natural disasters.
- **Hazard Assessment (Select Multiple, not required)**: Assess the severity of each hazard identified. Choose the most accurate level of hazard (Hazard Level 1, 2, or 3) based on the information provided.
- **Hazard Prevention (Select One, not required)**: Identify and select one of the measures to control or prevent each hazard.
- **Safety Measures (Text, not required)**: Describe any additional safety measures you have taken or plan to take to prevent or control hazards.
- **Recommendations (Note, not required)**: Summarize your findings and recommendations for future improvements.
- **Review (Select One, not required)**: Review and confirm your findings and recommendations.
- **Signature (Date, required)**: Date and sign to confirm that you have completed and reviewed the form.

## Tips

* Be thorough when identifying and assessing hazards to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use the hazard assessment section to prioritize hazards based on their severity.
* Select only one measure to control or prevent each hazard.
* Describe any additional safety measures taken or planned in the safety measures section.
* Summarize your findings and recommendations in the recommendations section.
* Date and sign the form to confirm completion and review.
