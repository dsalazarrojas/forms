# independent_contractor_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The independent contractor declaration form is designed for independent contractors to declare their working status and provide information about their services, contract terms, and payment details. This form is used by clients, employers, or review committees to assess the independent contractor's legitimacy and verify their working conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by providing your company details:** Select the page called "Company Details" and input your company's information, including its name and identification number (if applicable).
2. Fill out your **contractor details:** Proceed to the next page where you will input your personal information as a contractor. Ensure you fill out all necessary fields.
3. **Describe your contract terms**: In the "Contract Terms" section, type your contract terms, including payment conditions, work location, and other relevant conditions.
4. **Select your services offered**: In the "Services Offered" section, choose the services you provide to your clients or employer.
5. **Set up payment terms**: Fill out your payment terms, including rates and payment schedules.
6. **Add any additional notes:** You can add any additional notes regarding your work location and payment terms if needed.
7. **Provide proof of your work location:** Enter your work location, including the address, which is a required field.
8. **Set up tax withholding and benefits:** Choose your tax withholding and benefits options.
9. **Add your work hours and work location note (optional):** You can add any additional notes regarding your work hours and location if needed.
10. **Review and complete the signature sections:** The last two sections, "Contractor Signature" and "Company Signature", are for you and your company to sign and date, respectively. Ensure both signatures are accurate and complete.
11. **Finalize with review and completion date:** Fill out the "Date Completed" field with the current date. The "Date Reviewed" field is for the reviewer to fill out after reviewing your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (Page 1): Enter your company's name and identification number (if applicable).
* **Contractor Details** (Page 2): Enter your personal details such as your name and email address.
* **Contract Terms** (Page 3): Describe the terms of your contract, including payment conditions and work location.
* **Services Offered** (Page 4): Choose the services you offer to your clients or employer.
* **Contract Start Date** (Page 5): Enter the start date of your contract.
* **Contract End Date** (Page 5): Enter the end date of your contract.
* **Work Location** (Page 6): Enter the address of your work location.
* **Rate and Payment** (Page 7): Enter your payment terms and rates.
* **Tax Withholding** (Page 8): Choose your tax withholding option.
* **Benefits Provided** (Page 9): Choose the benefits you provide to your clients or employer.
* **Work Hours** (Page 10): Enter the hours you work. This field is optional.
* **Work Location Note** (Page 10): Add any additional notes regarding your work location.
* **Work Location Address** (Page 11): Enter the address of your work location. This field is required.
* **Payment Terms** (Page 12): Enter your payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms Note** (Page 12): Add any additional notes regarding your payment terms.
* **Contractor Signature** (Page 13): Sign and date this section, ensuring it accurately reflects your identity.
* **Company Signature** (Page 14): Sign and date this section, ensuring it accurately reflects your company's identity.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Page 15): Sign and date this section, ensuring it accurately reflects the reviewer's identity.
* **Date Completed** (Page 16): Fill out the current date.
* **Date Reviewed** (Page 17): Fill out the date this form was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name** (Page 18): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Company** (Page 18): Enter the reviewer's company.
* **Reviewer Title** (Page 19): Enter the reviewer's title.
* **Reviewer Signature Note** (Page 19): Add any additional notes regarding the reviewer's signature.
* **Review Notes** (Page 20): Add any additional notes regarding the review process.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the contract terms, payment details, and work location before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before signing and completing it.
* Proofread the form for any errors before submitting it.
