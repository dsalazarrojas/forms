# Final Payment Affidavit And Lien Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document final payment details for a project, including payment amount, payment method, and lien release information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the project details in the text fields provided.
2. Enter the final payment date and amount in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose the payment method used to make the final payment.
4. Enter the bank name and check number if applicable.
5. Confirm the payment status and sign the form with your signature and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Final Payment Affidavit And Lien Release** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should remain unchanged.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the project, including its location and any other relevant details.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the project was performed.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project owner.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contractor.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, optional): Enter the contract number, if applicable.
* **Final Payment Date** (`final_payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the final payment.
* **Final Payment Amount** (`final_payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total payment amount made to the contractor or property owner.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, optional): Enter the amount actually paid to the contractor or property owner.
* **Lien Amount** (`lien_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the lien, if applicable.
* **Lien Release Date** (`lien_release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the lien release, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, optional): Choose the method used to make the final payment. Options include Check, Bank Transfer, and Wire Transfer.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the bank that processed the payment, if using a bank transfer.
* **Check Number** (`check_number`, text, optional): Enter the check number, if using a check.
* **Check Date** (`check_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the check was issued.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select one, optional): Confirm the payment status. Options include Paid, Pending, Incomplete, and Partially Paid.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the owner or contractor.
* **Title** (`signature_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Position** (`signature_position`, text, optional): Enter your position or role.
* **Final Payment Affidavit** (`final_payment_affidavit`, text, optional): Enter a statement affirming that the final payment has been made.
* **Lien Release** (`lien_release`, text, optional): Enter a statement confirming the lien release, if applicable.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, text, optional): Enter an acknowledgement of the form completion and understanding of its contents.
