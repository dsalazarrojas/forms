# Auction Trading Post Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for consignors to provide information and agree on terms with a trading post for listing their items on the platform.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, business name, and contact details (email, phone number, and mailing address).
2. Provide your tax ID or SSN number for payment reporting purposes.
3. Choose the contract term length, primary item category, and estimated total value of your items.
4. Select your preferred commission rate and payment method.
5. Set your minimum sale price and pricing flexibility.
6. Choose how unsold items will be handled.
7. Decide if you want your items insured while in trading post custody.
8. Read and agree to the trading post terms and conditions.
9. Sign the electronic agreement with your full name.
10. Finally, sign the contract date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consigndor Full Name**: The legal name of the person or entity listing items on the trading post. This is required and should match the name on your tax documents.
* **Business Name**: If you are listing items as part of a business, enter your business name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Email Address**: The primary contact email for communication between you and the trading post.
* **Phone Number**: A contact phone number for communication and payment purposes.
* **Mailing Address**: The full address for correspondence and payment.
* **Tax ID or SSN**: A tax identification number for payment reporting purposes. This is required for payment processing.
* **Contract Date**: The date of the contract agreement.
* **Contract Term Length**: The duration of this consignment agreement. Choose from 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 180 days, or 1 year, or "Until sold".
* **Item Category**: The primary category of the items being consigned. This will help the trading post understand your items better.
* **Estimated Total Value**: The total estimated value of all items in USD.
* **Commission Rate**: The agreed commission percentage for the trading post.
* **Minimum Sale Price**: The lowest price acceptable for items.
* **Pricing Flexibility**: If the trading post can adjust prices if items do not sell within the contract term. Choose "Yes, may reduce after 30 days", "Yes, may reduce after 60 days", "No, price must remain fixed", or "Contact me before any price change".
* **Payment Method**: How you would like to receive payment proceeds.
* **Payment Threshold**: The minimum amount before payment is issued. Choose from $25, $50, $100, or "No threshold, pay after each sale".
* **Unsold Items Policy**: What should happen to items that do not sell. Choose "Return to consignor", "Donate to charity", "Reduce by 50%", or "Dispose after contract ends".
* **Insurance Coverage**: If you want your items insured while in trading post custody.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Read and agree to the trading post terms and conditions.
* **Consigndor Signature**: Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Date of signing.

Please fill in the form carefully, and do not hesitate to contact the trading post with any questions or concerns.
