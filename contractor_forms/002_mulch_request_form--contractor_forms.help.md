# Mulch Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mulch Request Form" is a tool used to collect information about mulch requests from customers or clients. It helps the business or organization providing the mulch services to understand the needs and requirements of their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Select the type of mulch you require in the "Mulch Type" field.
3. Specify the quantity of mulch you need in the "Quantity" field.
4. Choose the desired mulch color from the options in the "Mulch Color" field. If you don't need to select a color, you can leave this field blank.
5. Enter the date you would like to receive the mulch delivery in the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Choose the time of day you would like to receive the mulch delivery in the "Delivery Time" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field so that we can deliver the mulch to the correct location.
8. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Contact Info" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about your mulch request in the "Comments" field.
10. Finally, review your submission and click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name so that we can address your mulch request personally.
* **Mulch Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of mulch you require, such as Mulch Type 1, Mulch Type 2, or Mulch Type 3.
* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Specify the quantity of mulch you need. Please note that we can only deliver up to 100 units at a time.
* **Mulch Color** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more colors of mulch you would like to receive.
* **Delivery Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the mulch delivery. If left blank, we will use our standard delivery schedule.
* **Delivery Time** (Time, Optional): Choose the time of day you would like to receive the mulch delivery. If left blank, we will use our standard delivery time.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter the address where you would like to receive the mulch delivery.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Required): Provide your email and phone number so that we can contact you about your mulch request.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your mulch request.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your mulch request.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your mulch request.
