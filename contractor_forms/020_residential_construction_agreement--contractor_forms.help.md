# Residential Construction Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the terms and conditions of a residential construction project, ensuring that both parties involved are on the same page and that the terms are agreed upon before commencing work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Project Details**: Enter the project's essential details such as project address, description, and other relevant information.
2. **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms and conditions of the project, including payment schedules, due dates, and any applicable fees.
3. **Owner Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and other relevant details about yourself as the project owner.
4. **Contractor Details**: Enter your contractor's name, contact information, and other relevant details about the person or company handling the construction project.
5. **Project Status**: Update the project's status regularly to reflect any changes or updates.
6. **Project Completion**: Specify the project's completion date and terms of acceptance.
7. **Signatures**: Obtain digital or physical signatures from all parties involved, confirming that they have read and understand the agreement.
8. **Payment Schedule**: Document the payment terms, schedules, and deadlines.
9. **Additional Terms**: Enter any other important terms, conditions, or agreements that are unique to your project.
