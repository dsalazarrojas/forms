# Worker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Registration Form is a tool for HR or personnel departments to register and manage worker information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and authorization to complete this form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check your input for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
Your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)
Your last name.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Your personal email address.

### 4. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
Your current or previous job title.

### 5. **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format.

### 6. **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional)
Your work-related email address.

### 7. **Job Title Worked** (`job_title_worked`, text, optional)
A list of job titles you've held in the past.

### 8. **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, optional)
Enter your employment start date in DD-MM-YYYY format.

### 9. **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional)
Enter your employment end date in DD-MM-YYYY format.

### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Your contact phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Ensure you are authorized to access and process this information.
- This form is for your organization's internal use only.
