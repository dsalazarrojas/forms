# white_label_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for white label agreements with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the agreement details.
2. Enter the partner information, including partner name, address, and contact person.
3. Specify the contract duration and start date.
4. Choose the payment terms and frequency.
5. Enter the total payment amount.
6. Add any additional payment terms details.
7. Enter the client address and contact information.
8. Enter the email address and phone number of the client.
9. Choose the agreement version and contract status.
10. Upload a copy of the signed contract.
11. Record the client signature.
12. Record the contract status and expiration date.
13. Add any agreement version history.
14. Record the contract notes.
15. Choose the form submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of the agreement.
* **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter the partner name and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required):
	+ Enter the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, number, required):
	+ Enter the contract duration in months or years.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required):
	+ Enter the payment terms of the agreement.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the payment frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Total Payment** (`total_payment`, number, required):
	+ Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Terms Details** (`payment_terms_details`, note, required):
	+ Add any additional payment terms details.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter the client name and any other relevant details.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required):
	+ Enter the client address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required):
	+ Enter the client email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter the client phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required):
	+ Enter the contact person for the client.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the agreement version.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required):
	+ Upload a copy of the signed contract.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, note, required):
	+ Add any additional notes about the agreement.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the contract status.
* **Agreement Expiration** (`agreement_expiration`, date, required):
	+ Enter the expiration date of the contract.
* **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, note, required):
	+ Add any additional contract notes.
* **Agreement Version History** (`agreement_version_history`, text, required):
	+ Add any version history for the agreement.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required):
	+ Upload a copy of the client signature.
* **Contract Status History** (`contract_status_history`, text, required):
	+ Add any contract status history.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the form submission status.

Please note that some fields are required to be filled out. Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
