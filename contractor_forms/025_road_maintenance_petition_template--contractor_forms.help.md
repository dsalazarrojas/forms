# Road Maintenance Petition Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request road maintenance for a specific location. This includes reporting issues such as potholes, cracks, or other hazards on the road that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Person 1" and "Contact Person 2" fields. If "Contact Person 2" is not applicable, leave this field blank.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are the contact person for the request.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person for the request.
4. Enter the address of the location where the road maintenance is needed.
5. Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem you are reporting.
6. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
7. Enter the date you are submitting the request.
8. Enter the time you are submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **road_maintenance_petition** (road_maintenance_petition): This is the main title of the petition.
* **contact_person_1** (contact_person_1): Enter your name and title (e.g., Homeowner, Manager, etc.) to identify yourself as the contact person for the request.
* **contact_person_1_email** (contact_person_1_email): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the status of your request.
* **contact_person_2** (contact_person_2): Enter the name and title of the contact person for the request (if applicable).
* **contact_person_2_email** (contact_person_2_email): Enter the email address of the contact person for the request (if applicable).
* **address** (address): Enter the address of the location where the road maintenance is needed.
* **description** (description): Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem you are reporting.
* **signature** (signature): Sign your name to confirm your request.
* **date** (date): Enter the date you are submitting the request.
* **time** (time): Enter the time you are submitting the request.
