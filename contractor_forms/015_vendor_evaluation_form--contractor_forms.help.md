# Vendor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Evaluation Form is used to collect information from vendors on a specific date, which can be used to evaluate and compare different vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are evaluating.
   - **Vendor Contact Information**: Provide the contact information for the vendor.
   - **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of your evaluation.
   - **Rating**: Choose how you would rate your experience with the vendor (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
   - **Email Sent**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
   - **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
   - **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
   - **Submitted On**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
2. Optionally, you can add any additional comments in the **Vendor Evaluation Form Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the vendor you are evaluating.
* **Vendor Contact Information (Required)**: Provide the contact information for the vendor, such as address or phone number.
* **Evaluation Date (Required)**: Enter the date of your evaluation.
* **Rating (Required)**: Choose how you would rate your experience with the vendor (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Email Sent (Required)**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Submitted By (Required)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On (Required)**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Vendor Evaluation Form Comments (Optional)**: Optionally, you can add any additional comments about your experience with the vendor.
