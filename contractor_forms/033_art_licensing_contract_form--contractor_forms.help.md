# Art Licensing Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Art Licensing Contract Form is a critical document that outlines the terms and conditions of licensing an artist's work to a third party. It ensures a clear understanding between the licensor and licensee, preventing disputes and misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Contract Date**: Enter the date of the contract in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2.  **Licensor Name**: Enter the name of the artist or rights holder.
3.  **Licensor Email**: Enter the email address of the artist or rights holder.
4.  **Licensor Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the artist or rights holder.
5.  **Licensor Address**: Enter the full address of the artist or rights holder.
6.  **Licensee Name**: Enter the name of the company or individual taking the license.
7.  **Licensee Email**: Enter the email address of the company or individual taking the license.
8.  **Licensee Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the company or individual taking the license.
9.  **Licensee Address**: Enter the full address of the company or individual taking the license.
10. **Artwork Title**: Enter the title of the artwork being licensed.
11. **Artwork Description**: Enter a brief description of the artwork.
12.  **Artwork ID**: If applicable, enter the identification number of the artwork.
13.  **License Type**: Select the type of license granted:
    - **Exclusive**: Only one party can use the work.
    - **Non-Exclusive**: Multiple parties can use the work.
    - **Sole**: Only one party can use the work in one territory.
    - **Limited**: Specific limitations on who can use the work.
14.  **Licensed Products**: Select the products the artwork may be used on:
    - **Prints and Posters**: Artwork printed on paper or cardboard.
    - **Greeting Cards**: Artwork used on greeting cards.
    - **Calendars**: Artwork used on calendars.
    - **Home Decor**: Artwork used on home decor items.
    - **Apparel**: Artwork used on clothing or accessories.
    - **Stationery**: Artwork used on stationery items.
    - **Digital Media**: Artwork used on digital media.
    - **Packaging**: Artwork used on packaging materials.
    - **Other**: Specify other products.
15.  **Licensed Territory**: Select the geographic territory where the artwork can be used:
    - **Worldwide**: No restrictions on territory.
    - **North America**: Only in North America.
    - **United States Only**: Only in the United States.
    - **Europe**: Only in Europe.
    - **Asia**: Only in Asia.
    - **Other**: Specify other territories.
16.  **License Duration**: Select the length of the license:
    - **1 Year**: The license is valid for 1 year.
    - **2 Years**: The license is valid for 2 years.
    - **3 Years**: The license is valid for 3 years.
    - **5 Years**: The license is valid for 5 years.
    - **Perpetual**: The license is valid forever.
17.  **License Start Date**: Enter the start date of the license in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
18.  **License End Date**: Enter the end date of the license in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` or leave blank for perpetual licenses.
19.  **Royalty Rate**: Enter the percentage royalty rate as a number (e.g., 10 for 10%).
20.  **Advance Payment**: Enter the advance payment amount.
21.  **Minimum Guarantee**: Enter the minimum guaranteed payment.
22.  **Payment Terms**: Select when payments are due:
    - **Quarterly**: Payments are due quarterly.
    - **Semi-Annually**: Payments are due semi-annually.
    - **Annually**: Payments are due annually.
    - **Upon Sale**: Payments are due upon sale.
    - **Other**: Specify other payment terms.
23.  **Approval Required**: Select whether approval is required for all products or key products only:
    - **Yes - All Products**: Approval is required for all products.
    - **Yes - Key Products Only**: Approval is required for key products only.
    - **False**: No approval is required.
24.  **Credit Line**: Enter the required artist credit format.
25.  **Sample Required**: Select whether product samples are required before sale:
    - **True**: Product samples are required.
    - **False**: No product samples are required.
26.  **Termination Clause**: Enter the conditions for termination.
27.  **Exclusivity Terms**: Enter any exclusivity restrictions.
28.  **Sublicensing Allowed**: Select whether sublicensing is allowed:
    - **True**: Sublicensing is allowed.
    - **False**: Sublicensing is not allowed.
29.  **Quality Control**: Enter quality control requirements.
30.  **Additional Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions.
31.  **Licensor Signature**: Type the full name of the licensor as a signature.
32.  **Licensor Signature Date**: Enter the date of the licensor's signature in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
33.  **Licensee Signature**: Type the full name of the licensee as a signature.
34.  **Licensee Signature Date**: Enter the date of the licensee's signature in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
