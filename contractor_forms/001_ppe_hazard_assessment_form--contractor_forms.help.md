# PPE Hazard Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPE Hazard Assessment Form is designed to identify and assess potential hazards related to Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) in the workplace or environment. This form helps gather information on hazards, their severity, and control measures to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the employee information section with your name, job title, and other relevant details.
2.  In the hazard assessment section, describe the potential hazards related to PPE in your area of work or concern.
3.  Provide any additional comments or notes in the notes section if needed.
4.  Sign and date the signature section to confirm that you have taken the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Information (`employee_information`, Text, Optional)
*   This section is for your personal details, including your name and job title. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for proper identification and contact purposes.

### 2. **Hazard Assessment (`hazard_assessment`, Text, Optional)
*   Describe any potential hazards related to PPE in your area of work or concern. Be as detailed as possible, including the severity and any risks involved.

### 3. **Signature (`signature`, Note, Optional)
*   This section is for your digital signature, confirming that you have completed the assessment. Please sign and date this section as an acknowledgment of your responsibility in identifying potential hazards.

### 4. **Notes (`notes`, Text, Optional)
*   Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful in understanding the hazards identified. This section can also be used for future reference or follow-up actions.

## Tips
*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly, as it will help in identifying and mitigating potential hazards.
*   Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the workplace or environment.
*   If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, don't hesitate to reach out to form@gic.mx for support or guidance.
