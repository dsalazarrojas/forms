# Wholesale Partnership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a contract template for a wholesale partnership agreement between two parties. It will gather information about the partnership, including the names, contact information, start and end dates, payment terms, and contribution levels of both partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Title field with a descriptive title for the partnership.
2. Input the name and email of Partner A in the respective fields.
3. Input the name and phone number of Partner A in the respective fields.
4. Input the address of Partner A in the Partner A Address field.
5. Input the name of Partner B in the Partner B Name field.
6. Input the email of Partner B in the Partner B Email field.
7. Input the phone number of Partner B in the Partner B Phone field.
8. Input the address of Partner B in the Partner B Address field.
9. Select the Partnership Start Date and Partnership End Date fields using the calendar tool provided.
10. Fill in the Payment Terms field with any relevant payment details or terms.
11. Fill in the Payment Term 1, Payment Term 2, and Payment Term 3 fields with any relevant payment details or terms.
12. Select the contribution levels of both partners using the number fields Partner A Contribution and Partner B Contribution.
13. Select the responsibilities of both partners using the select_multiple fields Partner A Responsibility and Partner B Responsibility.
14. Fill in the Partnership Terms field with any additional partnership terms or details.
15. Fill in the Partnership Term 1 and Partnership Term 2 fields with any additional partnership terms or details.
16. Fill in the Notes field with any additional context or information relevant to the partnership.
