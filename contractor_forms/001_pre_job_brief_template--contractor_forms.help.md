# pre_job_brief_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre_job_brief_template" is a form designed to create a template for pre-job briefs, which outlines the essential details of a job or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, which is marked as "required".
2. Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any additional details or context that may be relevant to the job or project.
3. Upload attachments, such as documents or images, to support the information provided in the form.
