# Vendor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Request Form is a tool used to collect contact information from potential vendors. It is meant to be filled out by vendors who have been invited to do business with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field. Please use the format of `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your company name in the company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Please enter your first name as it is listed on your business cards or other contact materials.
* **Last Name** (2): Please enter your last name as it is listed on your business cards or other contact materials.
* **Email** (3, required): Please enter a valid email address associated with your company. This will be used to communicate with you about potential business opportunities.
* **Phone** (4, required): Please enter your company phone number. This can be a direct line or the main phone number for your company.
* **Company** (5): Please enter the name of your company as it is listed on your business cards or other contact materials.
* **Tips**: Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
