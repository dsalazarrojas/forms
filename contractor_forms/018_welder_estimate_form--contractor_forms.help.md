# Welder Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Welder Estimate Form is a tool used to gather essential information for welding jobs. It is used by welders to provide accurate estimates for their work and by clients to manage their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Job Number** (required) into the corresponding field. This is a unique identifier for the job.
2.  Select the appropriate **Job Type** from the options provided. This will help determine the type of welding work required.
3.  Enter a brief description of the job in the **Job Description** field. This is a free-text field for providing any relevant details.
4.  Enter the **Customer Information**, including any relevant contact details or notes.
5.  Select the **Job Date** for the scheduled welding work.
6.  Enter the duration of the job in **Job Time** field.
7.  Choose one or more **Job Location(s)** from the provided options.
8.  Enter your **Welder Information**, including any relevant details or certifications.
9.  Enter your estimate for the job in the **Job Estimate** field.
10. Enter any additional **Job Notes** in the free-text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Number** (2): Enter a unique identifier for the job, as assigned by the client or project manager.
- **Job Type** (3): Select the type of welding work required, such as MIG, Arc, Spot, or Orbital.
- **Job Description** (4): Provide a brief description of the job, including any relevant details or instructions.
- **Customer Information** (5): Enter any relevant contact details or notes about the client or project.
- **Job Date** (6): Select the date when the job is scheduled to take place.
- **Job Time** (7): Enter the duration of the job, in hours or minutes.
- **Job Location** (8): Choose one or more locations where the job will take place.
- **Welder Information** (9): Enter your name, contact details, or any relevant certifications.
- **Job Estimate** (10): Enter your estimate for the job, including any relevant details or calculations.
- **Job Notes** (11): Enter any additional notes or comments about the job (optional).
