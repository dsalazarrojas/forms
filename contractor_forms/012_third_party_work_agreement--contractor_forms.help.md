# Third Party Work Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal agreement between [Company Name] and a third party (e.g., a service provider, contractor, or vendor) to outline the terms and conditions of a third-party relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Service Details: Provide a brief description of the services to be performed under this agreement.
	* Contractor Details: Enter your company name and contact information.
	* Terms and Conditions: Outline the terms and conditions of the agreement, including payment terms and termination clause.
	* Contract Duration and Start/End Dates: Specify the duration and start/end dates of the contract.
	* Payment Terms: Define the payment terms, including frequency and amount.
	* Contract Type: Select the type of contract that best describes the agreement.
2. Review and sign the form:
	* Ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.
	* Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
