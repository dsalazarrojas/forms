# Work Change Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Change Order Form is used to document changes made to a work order, ensuring transparency and accuracy in project management and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the necessary details about the change, including the date and description of the change.
2. Specify the location where the change was made.
3. Identify the type of change (e.g., Change in scope, Change in method, or Other).
4. Provide a brief reason for the change.
5. Indicate if the change has been approved or not.
6. Record the date the change was signed off.
7. Enter the date the change was received.
8. Sign off the change by entering your name or initials.
9. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Change Order Form (1)**: This field is used to enter a brief title or description of the change.
* **Date of Order (2)**: Enter the date when the work order was originally issued.
* **Description of Change (3)**: Provide a detailed description of the change made to the work order.
* **Change Location (4)**: Specify the location where the change was made.
* **Work Order ID (5)**: Enter the unique identifier for the original work order.
* **Change Type (6)**: Select from the options to indicate the type of change made to the work order (e.g., Change in scope, Change in method, or Other).
* **Reason for Change (7)**: Explain the reason or motivation behind the change.
* **Approved By (8)**: Indicate whether the change has been approved (Yes or No).
* **Date Signed (9)**: Record the date when the change was signed off.
* **Date Received (10)**: Enter the date when the change was received.
* **Signed By (11)**: Enter your name or initials to sign off on the change.
* **Signature (12)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the change.
