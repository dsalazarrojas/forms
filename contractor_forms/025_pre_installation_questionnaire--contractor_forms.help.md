# Pre Installation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Installation Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information about a site before our team proceeds with the installation work. This form aims to ensure that our team is well-prepared and understands the site specifics to complete the installation efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (denoted by *) and any additional fields you feel are relevant to the installation.
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Site Details
* **Site Details** (id: 1, <type>: text, required: false)
This field is for entering the site's general information, such as its name, location, or other relevant details.

### Client Info
* **Client Info** (id: 2, <type>: text, required: false)
Please enter any relevant information about the client or the project.

### Site Requirements
* **Site Requirements** (id: 3, <type>: text, required: false)
 Describe the specific requirements for the installation, such as equipment or materials needed.

### Schedule
* **Schedule** (id: 4, <type>: time, required: false)
Enter the proposed schedule for the installation, including start and end dates.

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (id: 5, <type>: text, required: false)
Indicate how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (id: 6, <type>: text, required: false)
Enter the name of the contact person responsible for coordinating the installation.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (id: 7, <type>: text, required: false)
Enter the contact number for the site or person responsible for the installation.

### Email
* **Email** (id: 8, <type>: email, required: false)
Enter the email address for the site or person responsible for the installation.

### Contact Address
* **Contact Address** (id: 9, <type>: text, required: false)
Enter the physical address for the site or person responsible for the installation.

### Site Description
* **Site Description** (id: 10, <type>: note, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the site, including any notable features or details.
