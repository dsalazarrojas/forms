# indoor_plant_installation_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The indoor plant installation agreement form is designed to document the terms and conditions of an indoor plant installation project between a client and a contractor. It ensures a clear understanding of the project scope, schedule, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill in the client details, including name, project name, and contact information.
3. Describe the project scope, including the specific services to be provided and the expected outcome.
4. Outline the project schedule, including start and completion dates.
5. Obtain the client's consent to proceed with the project.
6. Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
7. Collect the client's signature and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Provide your name and contact information.
* **Project Scope**: Describe the specific services to be provided and the expected outcome.
* **Schedule**: Outline the start and completion dates of the project.
* **Consent**: Check the box to indicate that you consent to proceed with the project.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your name and contact information.
* **Contractor Signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before signing to avoid any miscommunication or disputes.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
