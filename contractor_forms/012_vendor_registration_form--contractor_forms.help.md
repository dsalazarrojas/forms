# Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Registration Form is used to register vendors for contractor work. Fill out the form with your company details and get listed or approved as a vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields marked as required.
2. Ensure the email and phone fields are in the correct format.
3. Select the correct category and status for your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your company name as registered name.

### 2. **Name** (name, `text`, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

### 3. **Address** (address, `text`, required)
Enter your company's address for correspondence purposes.

### 4. **Email** (email, `email`, required)
Enter a valid company email address.

### 5. **Phone** (phone, `text`, required)
Enter a valid phone number for company contact.

### 6. **Category** (category, `select_one`, required)
Select Yes if you are an internal vendor, No otherwise.

### 7. **Company Name** (company_name, `text`, required)
Enter your company's name as registered name.

### 8. **Contact Person** (contact_person, `text`, required)
Enter the full name of the contact person.

### 9. **Role** (role, `select_one`, required)
Select Yes if you are a contractor, No otherwise.

### 10. **Status** (status, `select_one`, required)
Select Active to register your company as active, Inactive otherwise.

### 11. **Notes** (notes, `text`, optional)
Add any additional notes about your company or contact person.

### 12. **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, `text`, optional)
If applicable, enter the tool(s) assigned to this vendor.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled correctly to avoid errors.
- Review your form before submission.
- Contact us if you need further assistance.
