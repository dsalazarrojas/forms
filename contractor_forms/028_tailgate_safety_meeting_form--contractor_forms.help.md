# Tailgate Safety Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tailgate Safety Meeting Form is a critical document for recording information related to tailgate safety meetings conducted for contractors and workers on site. Completing this form ensures that all essential details are documented and helps maintain compliance with safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information, such as:
   - **Tailgate Safety Meeting Form Title**: Briefly describe the meeting's purpose and content.
   - **Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
   - **Time**: Record the time the meeting was conducted.
   - **Location**: Provide the site or location where the meeting was held.
   - **Attendees List**: Identify the individuals who attended the meeting, choosing from Contractor Foreman, Supervisor, Field Worker, or Apprentice.
   - **Review and Approval**: Confirm if the meeting was reviewed and approved by a designated Reviewer.
   - **Reviewer Name**, **Reviewer Title**, and **Reviewer Date** and **Reviewer Time**: Capture the details of the Reviewer who completed the review.
   - **Signature Date** and **Signature Time**: Record the date and time of the Reviewer's signature.
   - **Signature**: Provide the Reviewer's signature.
   - **Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the Reviewer.
   - **Review Status**: Update the status of the review as Completed, Not Completed, or Pending.
   - **Review Date** and **Review Time**: Record the date and time of the review's completion.
   - **Submitted By**: Identify who submitted the form.
   - **Submitted Date**: Record the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Forms**: This field is not part of the main form content but is a hint suggesting that contractor-related information should be captured in relevant sections.
* **Tailgate Safety Meeting Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the meeting, indicating its purpose and key content.
* **Date**: Record the date when the meeting occurred.
* **Time**: Capture the start and end time of the meeting.
* **Location**: Provide the site or location where the meeting took place.
* **Attendees List**: Select the type of attendees who participated in the meeting, which may include Contractor Foreman, Supervisor, Field Worker, or Apprentice.
* **Attendees Details**: Add any additional details about the attendees, such as their roles or departments.
* **Tailgate Safety Mission**: Describe the overall safety mission or objective of the meeting.
* **Hazards Identification**: Document any identified hazards or risks discussed during the meeting.
* **Controls List**: List the safety controls or measures that were agreed upon or implemented as a result of the meeting.
* **Controls Description**: Elaborate on the safety controls or measures, ensuring they are understood by all attendees.
* **Review and Approval**: Confirm that the meeting was reviewed and approved by a designated Reviewer.
* **Review Status**: Update the status of the review to ensure it is accurately reflected.
* **Reviewer Name** and **Reviewer Title**: Identify and document the details of the Reviewer.
* **Reviewer Date** and **Reviewer Time**: Capture the date and time when the Reviewer completed their assessment.
* **Signature Date** and **Signature Time**: Record the date and time of the Reviewer's signature.
* **Signature**: Provide the Reviewer's signature as a verification of the reviewed items.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Allow the Reviewer to add any comments or suggestions.
* **Review Status**: Update the status of the review to ensure it reflects the accurate completion status.
* **Review Date** and **Review Time**: Record the completion date and time of the review.
* **Submitted By**: Identify who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Record the date the form was submitted. 

**Tips**: Always ensure that each form is reviewed and approved by a designated Reviewer. This helps maintain the accuracy and authenticity of the data captured. If a field is marked as "required" but you are unsure about the relevance, consider consulting with relevant personnel or seeking guidance from the relevant authorities.
