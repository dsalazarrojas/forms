# Supplier Accreditation Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Accreditation Registration Form Template is a document designed to register and verify the credentials of suppliers. This form is intended for use by suppliers to provide information about their business, including their name, address, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
3. Enter your physical street address in the "Street" field.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Select your state from the list of available options in the "State" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
9. Select the type of business (Service, Product, Both, or Other) in the "Business Type" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).
11. Select the current accreditation status (Pending, Accredited, or Denied) in the "Accreditation Status" field (optional).
12. Enter the date and time of the supplier's availability in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
13. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your supplier business.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter a valid business name.
* **Street** (`street`, `text`, required): Enter your physical street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter a valid city name.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select your state from the list of available options.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of business (Service, Product, Both, or Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Accreditation Status** (`acc_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the current accreditation status (Pending, Accredited, or Denied).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the supplier's availability.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the supplier's availability.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the required information before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide or contact the relevant authorities.
* Do not duplicate or alter the form for any reason.
