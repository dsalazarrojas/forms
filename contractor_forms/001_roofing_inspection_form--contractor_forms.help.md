# Roofing Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roofing Inspection Form is used to document the condition of a building's roofing system. It helps assess the quality and safety of the roof and chimney.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the building address where the inspection is taking place.
2. Select the type of roof present (e.g. Flat, Gable, Hip, or Shed).
3. Evaluate the condition of various parts of the roof, such as:
	* Roofing Condition: Check the overall condition of the roof, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
	* Flashing Condition: Check the condition of flashing, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
	* Gutters Condition: Check the condition of gutters, selecting "Satisfactory", "Unsatisfactory", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
	* Gutters Type: Select the type of gutters present (e.g. Gutter Guard, Leaf Filter, or Screen).
4. Assess the condition and safety of the chimney, including:
	* Chimney Condition: Check the overall condition of the chimney, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
	* Chimney Location: Select the location of the chimney (e.g. Front, Back, Left, or Right).
	* Chimney Danger Signs: Check for potential hazards, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
	* Chimney Foundation: Check the condition of the chimney foundation, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
5. Evaluate the age of the roof and its structural integrity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building Address**: Enter the address of the building being inspected.
* **Building Type**: Select the type of building (e.g. Flat Roof, Gable Roof, Hip Roof, or Shed Roof).
* **Roofing Condition**: Check the overall condition of the roof, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Flashing Condition**: Check the condition of flashing, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Gutters Condition**: Check the condition of gutters, selecting "Satisfactory", "Unsatisfactory", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Gutters Type**: Select the type of gutters present (e.g. Gutter Guard, Leaf Filter, or Screen).
* **Chimney Condition**: Check the overall condition of the chimney, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Chimney Location**: Select the location of the chimney (e.g. Front, Back, Left, or Right).
* **Chimney Danger Signs**: Check for potential hazards, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Chimney Foundation**: Check the condition of the chimney foundation, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Roof Condition**: Check the overall condition of the roof, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Roof Structural Integrity**: Check the structural integrity of the roof, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Roof Age**: Enter the age of the roof in years.
* **Roof Type**: Select the type of roof (e.g. Flat, Gable, Hip, or Shed).
* **Chimney Safety**: Check the condition and safety of the chimney, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Flashing Integrity**: Check the condition and safety of flashing, selecting "Satisfactory" or "Unsatisfactory".
* **Gutters**: Check the condition and type of gutters, selecting "Satisfactory", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor", and choosing the type (e.g. Gutter Guard, Leaf Filter, or Screen).

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
* If unsure, consult with colleagues or supervisors for guidance.
* Take notes and photos for reference during the inspection.
* Ensure that the form is completed in the correct format and order.
* Review and verify the form before submitting it.
