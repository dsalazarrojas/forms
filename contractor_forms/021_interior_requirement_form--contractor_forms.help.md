# Interior Requirement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interior Requirement Form is used to gather essential information for a home interior design project. It's designed to be completed by clients to help our team understand their project requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, please fill in the Project Title and Project Details sections. This will give us a good understanding of your project.
2.  Next, select your preferred Design Style from the provided options.
3.  If applicable, provide your budget and deadline for the project.
4.  In the Client Requirements section, list any specific requirements or needs you have for the project.
5.  Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Please describe your project in detail here.
*   **Design Style** (`design_style`, select_one, required): Select your preferred design style for your home interior.
*   **Client Requirements** (`client_requirements`, text, optional): List any specific requirements or needs you have for this project.
*   **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): If you have a budget for the project, please provide it here.
*   **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, optional): If you have a deadline for the project, please specify it here.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your project here.
