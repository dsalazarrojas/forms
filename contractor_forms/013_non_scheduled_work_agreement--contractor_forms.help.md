# Non Scheduled Work Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document non-scheduled work agreements between a client and a contractor. It captures essential details such as work dates, description, hours worked, rates, payment terms, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the work date (1) on which the non-scheduled work was performed.
2. Provide a detailed description (2) of the work performed.
3. Enter the hours (3) worked on the project.
4. Specify the start date (4) and end date (5) of the work.
5. Enter the rate per hour (7) and rate amount (8).
6. Select the payment currency (9) in which the payment will be made.
7. Choose the payment terms (10) that apply to this agreement, such as Payment on receipt of invoice, Payment within 30 days, or Payment within 60 days. If other payment terms apply, please specify in the Payment Details (16) field.
8. Choose the payment method (11) through which payment will be made, such as Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Direct Deposit.
9. Select the payment schedule (12) for this agreement, such as One-time payment, Recurring payment, or Payment on completion.
10. Choose the payment frequency (13) for this agreement, such as Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly.
11. If necessary, enter the payment amount (14) and payment currency (15).
12. Finally, add any additional payment details (16) or contractor notes (17) in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Date (1)** (`date`, required): Enter the date on which the non-scheduled work was performed.
* **Work Description (2)** (`text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the work performed.
* **Hours Worked (3)** (`number`, optional): Enter the total hours worked on the project.
* **Start Date (4)** (`date`, optional): Specify the start date of the work.
* **End Date (5)** (`date`, optional): Specify the end date of the work.
* **Rate/Hour (7)** (`number`, optional): Enter the rate per hour for the work performed.
* **Rate Amount (8)** (`number`, optional): Enter the rate amount for the work performed.
* **Rate Currency (9)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the payment currency in which the payment will be made.
* **Payment Terms (10)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose the payment terms that apply to this agreement.
* **Payment Method (11)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the payment method through which payment will be made.
* **Payment Schedule (12)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the payment schedule for this agreement.
* **Payment Frequency (13)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the payment frequency for this agreement.
* **Payment Amount (14)** (`number`, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Currency (15)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the payment currency for the payment amount.
* **Payment Details (16)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional payment details or notes.
* **Contractor Notes (17)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional contractor notes.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and ensure that all necessary details are captured for a clear and effective non-scheduled work agreement.
