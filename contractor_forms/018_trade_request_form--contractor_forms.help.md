# Trade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Request Form is used to collect information for a trade request, including title, business details, date and time, and any additional notes. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is gathered for the trade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trade Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to give a brief description of the trade request.
2. Select the relevant business details from the options provided for Business A and Business B.
3. Enter the date of the trade request.
4. Enter the time of the trade request.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the trade request.
6. Fill in the Form Title 2 field if needed (this field is optional).
7. Enter the date of the trade request again, if needed (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the trade request.
* **Business A** (business_a, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant business details for Business A from the options provided.
* **Business B** (business_b, text, required: false): Enter the relevant business details for Business B.
* **Date** (date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the trade request in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (time, time, required: false): Enter the time of the trade request in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note** (note, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the trade request.
* **Form Title 2** (form_title_2, text, required: false): Fill in the form title if needed (optional).
* **Date 2** (date_2, date, required: false): Enter the date of the trade request again, if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the trade request is complete.
* Use the correct date and time format (MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM).
* If additional notes are provided, be as detailed as possible to help with the trade request.
* Form Title 2 and Date 2 fields are optional, use only if needed.
* Use the options provided for Business A and Business B to select the relevant details.
