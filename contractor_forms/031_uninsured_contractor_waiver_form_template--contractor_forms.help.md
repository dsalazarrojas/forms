# Uninsured Contractor Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from uninsured contractors for a specific job or project.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. You may skip any fields that are not applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (1, text, required: false): Please enter your name, address, and phone number so we can contact you about the job.
* **Job Description** (2, text, required: false): Describe the job or project that you are working on, including any relevant details or tasks.
* **Contract Terms** (3, select_one, required: false): Select one of the contract options to indicate the terms that you have agreed to. Please choose the option that best matches your situation.
* **Payment Info** (4, number, required: false): Enter your payment information, including any relevant details such as payment method or schedule.
* **Signature** (5, note, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and agreed to the contract terms.
* **Date** (6, date, required: false): Enter the date that you signed this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the contract terms before signing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the form fields, please contact us for clarification.
* Please sign and date this form to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
