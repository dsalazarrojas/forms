# Woodshop Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Woodshop Contact Form is a simple contact form designed to gather basic information from customers or clients interested in inquiring about or making an order at a woodshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the **Contact Form** section.
2. In the **Customer Info** section, provide any necessary information about yourself or your business (e.g. name, title, company name).
3. In the **Contact Details** section, provide your desired contact method (e.g. phone number, email, or a specific contact person).
4. In the **Order Details** section, describe the order you are interested in, including any specific details or questions you may have.
5. In the **Customer Message** section, provide any additional comments or messages you may have for the woodshop.
6. Finally, in the **Contact Person** section, identify the specific person you would like to contact.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure the woodshop can provide the best possible service.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your order, you can attach them to your message in the **Customer Message** section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the woodshop directly.
