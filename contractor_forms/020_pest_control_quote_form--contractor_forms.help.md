# Pest Control Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pest Control Quote Form is a tool used to gather information from customers for pest control services. This form helps pest control professionals provide accurate quotes to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of pest you are experiencing issues with from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the services you need from the list of options.
3. Select the areas where services are needed.
4. Enter your customer name and contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pest Type**: Select the type of pest you are experiencing issues with from the dropdown menu. This will help us provide a more accurate quote for your specific pest problem.
  * Ants
  * Beetles
  * Cockroaches
  * Fleas
  * Flies
  * Moths
  * Rats
  * Spiders
  * Termites
  * Ticks
  * Wasps
  * Worms
  * Other
* **Service Needed**: Choose the services you need from the list of options. You can select one or more services:
  * Inspection
  * Extermination
  * Prevention
* **Service Area**: Select the areas where services are needed:
  * House
  * Yard
  * Pool
  * Attic
  * Basement
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer E-mail**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for faster communication.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure we can provide you with a precise quote. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
