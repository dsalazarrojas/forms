# Smart Renovation Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners or clients who are interested in collaborating with the Smart Renovation Partner. It is meant to be a one-time submission form, so please do not use it for multiple submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Partner Details**: Provide a brief description of your project and your role within the partnership.
2. **Contact Information**: Share your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. **Message**: Write a brief message to the Smart Renovation Partner, introducing yourself and your project.
4. **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are working on (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
5. **Partner Experience**: Choose your level of experience (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
6. **File Upload**: If you need to share any files related to your project, upload them using this field.
7. **Partner Contact Info Form 2**: Provide any additional contact information for yourself or your team.
8. **Partner Form 2**: This is a duplicate field for the Partner Contact Info Form, please do not fill it out unless you really need to.
9. **Message Form**: Write any additional message you may have for the Smart Renovation Partner.
10. **Partner Experience 2**: Choose your level of experience (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
11. **Contact Form**: Provide any other contact information you may have.
12. **Partner Form 3**: This is another duplicate field, please do not fill it out.
