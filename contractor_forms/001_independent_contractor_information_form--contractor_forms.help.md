# Independent Contractor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Contractor Information Form is used to collect details of independent contractors who have been hired for a specific project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the Company Name field.
2. Add your phone number in the Phone Number field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Social Security Number in the Social Security Number field.
5. Enter your EIN (Employer Identification Number) in the EIN Number field.
6. Describe the payment terms and conditions for your work in the Payment Terms 1 and Payment Terms 2 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business cards, website, or other official documents.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, number, required: false): Enter your business phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you about your project.
* **Social Security Number** (`Social Security Number`, number, required: false): Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your social security card or other official documents.
* **EIN Number** (`EIN Number`, number, required: false): Enter your Employer Identification Number (EIN) as assigned by the IRS.
* **Payment Terms 1** (`Payment Terms 1`, text, required: false): Describe how you expect to be paid for your work, including payment schedule, payment method, and any other relevant terms.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`Payment Terms 2`, text, required: false): Enter any other relevant payment terms or conditions that are important for your work.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your project manager or contact person.
* Ensure that you have not filled out this form for the same contractor in the past to prevent duplicated entries.
