# Distribution And Promotion Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution and Promotion Agreement Form is a document used to establish a contract for the distribution and promotion of a product or service. It's a crucial form that ensures both parties understand the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your answer from the options provided in the first field.
2. Enter your business name and any other relevant details in the second field.
3. Provide a brief note with the agreement details in the third field.
4. Attach a contract file, if necessary, in the fourth field.
5. Enter the date of signature in the fifth field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution And Promotion (distribution_and_promotion_agreement_form_label)**:
	+ This field is a select multiple option that asks whether the distribution and promotion are allowed. Please select either 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
* **Business Name (business_name_label)**:
	+ This field is for the name of your business. Enter your business name to help us understand your identity.
* **Agreement Details (agreement_details_label)**:
	+ This field is for a brief note about the agreement details. Please provide a short description of the terms of the agreement.
* **Contract File (contract_file_label)**:
	+ This field is for attaching a contract file, if necessary. You can upload a file related to the agreement.
* **Sign Date (signatures_label)**:
	+ This field is for the date of signature. Please enter the date when you signed the agreement.
* **Contact Email (contact_email_label)**:
	+ This field is for your contact email address. Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you about the agreement.
