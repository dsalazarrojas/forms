# Deck Construction Measurement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect accurate measurements and information about a deck construction project to aid in the planning and execution of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the deck's width in feet in the `deck_width` field.
3. Enter the length of the deck in feet in the `deck_length` field.
4. Provide information about the site where the deck will be constructed in the `site_details` field.
5. List any specific materials that will be used for the deck construction project in the `material_preferences` field.
6. Enter the site address where the deck will be built in the `site_address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **deck_width**: Enter the width of the deck in feet.
	+ This field requires an accurate measurement to ensure correct planning and execution.
* **deck_length**: Enter the length of the deck in feet.
	+ This field is also a crucial measurement for the same reasons as the width field.
* **site_details**: Provide a detailed description of the site where the deck will be constructed.
	+ This could include notes about the terrain, surrounding structures, or other relevant details.
* **material_preferences**: List the specific materials that will be used for the deck construction project.
	+ This field allows you to specify any special requirements or preferences for materials.
* **site_address**: Enter the address of the site where the deck will be built.
	+ This helps in ensuring that the correct location is targeted for the project.
