# Restart Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restart Agreement Form is used to manage or update existing contracts between clients and contractors. This form captures necessary information for the restart or revision process, ensuring all relevant details are captured and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all required information.
2. Fill out the 'Contract ID' field with the relevant contract ID number (if applicable).
3. Enter the 'contractor_name' field with the name of the client or contractor.
4. Provide the 'agreement_start_date' and 'agreement_end_date' with the start and end dates of the agreement.
5. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for 'team_name' and 'team_manager' fields, indicating if the team is involved in the agreement or not.
6. For 'Reason For Restart', provide a brief explanation of the reason for the restart or revision.
7. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for 'restart_approvals' and 'team_lead' fields, indicating if approvals are required or if the team lead is involved.
8. Enter the 'restart_date' if the restart date is different from the agreement end date.
9. Provide your 'Email' and 'Phone' details if applicable.
10. If required, upload the 'Contract File' associated with the agreement.
11. Enter any 'Notes' in the designated field.
12. Review all fields for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract ID (1)**: Enter the ID number for the relevant contract.
* **Contractor Name (2)**: Enter the name of the client or contractor.
* **Agreement Start Date (3)**: Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Date (4)**: Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Team (5)**: Select 'Yes' if the team is involved in the agreement; 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Manager (6)**: Select 'Yes' if the team manager is involved; 'No' otherwise.
* **Reason For Restart (7)**: Provide a brief explanation for the restart or revision reason.
* **Restart Approvals (8)**: Select 'Yes' if approvals are required; 'No' otherwise.
* **Restart Date (9)**: Enter the date for the restart, if different from the agreement end date.
* **Team Lead (10)**: Select 'Yes' if the team lead is involved; 'No' otherwise.
* **Notes (11)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the agreement.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address if necessary.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number if necessary.
* **Contract File Upload (14)**: Upload a file related to the contract if required.
* **Notes (15)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the agreement.
