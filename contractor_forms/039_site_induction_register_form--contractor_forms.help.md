# site_induction_register_form--contractor_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Induction Register Form is a tool used to collect essential information from contractors about their site induction on a given site. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select from the options provided for the site induction status.
    - Choose the correct status for your site induction status: `site_induction_form_1`
    - Indicate whether the status is Yes or No.
2.  For site_induction_form_2, select all that apply:
    - You can select one or more options that best describe your experience status.
3.  Enter any additional comments or remarks about your site induction in `site_induction_form_8`.
4.  Enter your email address in `site_induction_form_9`.
5.  Enter any other details about your site induction in `site_induction_form_10`, `site_induction_form_11`, `site_induction_form_12`, `site_induction_form_13`, `site_induction_form_14`, `site_induction_form_15`, `site_induction_form_16`, `site_induction_form_17`, `site_induction_form_18`, `site_induction_form_19`, `site_induction_form_20`, `site_induction_form_21`, `site_induction_form_22`, `site_induction_form_23`, `site_induction_form_24`, `site_induction_form_25`.
6.  Enter any other details about your site induction in `site_induction_form_3` and `site_induction_form_4`.
7.  Enter the date of your site induction in `site_induction_form_6`.
8.  Enter the time of your site induction in `site_induction_form_7`.
