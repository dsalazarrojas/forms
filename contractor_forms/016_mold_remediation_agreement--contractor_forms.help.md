# Mold Remediation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mold Remediation Agreement form is used to document the terms and conditions of mold remediation services for a property. It's a crucial document that outlines the scope, cost, and timeline of the remediation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contractor's and client's information, including property details.
2. Specify the remediation scope, including what will be done and what won't be included.
3. Determine the remediation status (active or inactive) and agreement status (active or inactive).
4. Sign and date the agreement as the contractor and client.
5. Include any additional notes or remediation certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contractor Info** (`contractor_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the contractor's name and contact information.
- **Property Info** (`property_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the property address, owner's name, and owner's email.
- **Remediation Info** (`remediation_info`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether remediation will be performed or not.
- **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable agreement terms for the client to acknowledge.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, optional): Date the agreement was signed.
- **Date Remediation Completed** (`date_remediation_completed`, `date`, optional): Date the remediation was completed.
- **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the agreement as the contractor.
- **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the agreement as the client.
- **Client Info** (`client_info`, `select_one`, required): Specify the client's response to the remediation process.
- **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the status of the agreement.
- **Remediation Status** (`remediation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the status of the remediation process.
- **Property Manager Signature** (`property_manager_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the agreement as the property manager.
- **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the property manager's name.
- **Property Owner Name** (`property_owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the property owner's name.
- **Property Owner Email** (`property_owner_email`, `text`, required): Enter the property owner's email address.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the property address.
- **Remediation Cost** (`remediation_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated cost of remediation.
- **Remediation Scope** (`remediation_scope`, `text`, optional): Describe the remediation scope.
- **Remediation Notes** (`remediation_notes`, `note`, optional): Include any additional notes about the remediation process.
- **Remediation Certification** (`remediation_certification`, `text`, optional): Enter any certification or license information.
- **Remediation Scope Certification** (`remediation_scope_certification`, `text`, optional): Enter any certification or verification information for the remediation scope.
- **Remediation Methodology** (`remediation_methodology`, `text`, optional): Describe the remediation methodology.
- **Remediation Cost Estimate** (`remediation_cost_estimate`, `text`, optional): Enter an estimated cost for the remediation process.
- **Remediation Agreement Signed Date** (`remediation_agreement_signed_date`, `date`, optional): Date the remediation agreement was signed.
- **Remediation Agreement Signed By** (`remediation_agreement_signed_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.

Note: Some fields may not be required for every form completion, and some fields may not be relevant to your specific use case.
