# it_marketing_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_marketing_agreement_form is a document for marketing teams and clients to agree on project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2.  Describe the scope of work to be performed in the "Scope of Work" field.
3.  Set a project deadline in the "Project Deadline" field.
4.  Select the current project status from the dropdown menu in the "Project Status" field. This will help track the project's progress.
5.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
6.  Select the services you have agreed on with the client in the "Service Agreements" field.
7.  Enter the project's timeline in the "Timeline" field.
8.  Fill in the marketing budget for the project in the "Marketing Budget" field.
9.  Add any notes about the project in the "Notes" field.
10.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: A text field to input the client's details.
* **Scope of Work**: A text field to describe the scope of work to be performed.
* **Project Deadline**: A field to set a project deadline.
* **Project Status**: A dropdown menu to select the current project status.
* **Contact Info**: A text field to provide your contact information.
* **Service Agreements**: A multiple select menu to select the services agreed upon with the client.
* **Timeline**: A field to enter the project's timeline.
* **Marketing Budget**: A numerical field to fill in the marketing budget.
* **Notes**: A field for any additional project notes.
* **Email Address**: A field to enter your email address.
