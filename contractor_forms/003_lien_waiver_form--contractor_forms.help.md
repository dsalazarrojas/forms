# Lien Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lien Waiver Form is used to record the details of a lien waiver, which is a legal document that releases a contractor from their obligation to make further payments on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure that you have all necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
2. Enter the required information into the form fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contractor Name**: contractor_name
	* This field is used to record the name of the contractor who is receiving the lien waiver.
### 2. **Property Address**: property_address
	* This field is used to record the address of the property where the contract was signed.
### 3. **Contract Date**: contract_date
	* This field is used to record the date when the contract was signed.
### 4. **Amount Paid**: amount_paid
	* This field is used to record the amount of money paid to the contractor.
### 5. **Lien Waiver**: lien_waiver
	* This field is used to select whether or not the lien waiver has been issued. Please choose one of the following options:
		+ Lien Waivered
		+ Not Lien Waivered
### 6. **Payment Date**: payment_date
	* This field is used to record the date of the payment made to the contractor.
### 7. **Contractor Address**: contractor_address
	* This field is used to record the address of the contractor.
### 8. **Homeowner Name**: homeowner_name
	* This field is used to record the name of the property owner.
### 9. **Contractor Phone**: contractor_phone
	* This field is used to record the phone number of the contractor.
### 10. **Contractor Email**: contractor_email
	* This field is used to record the email address of the contractor.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek clarification before proceeding.
