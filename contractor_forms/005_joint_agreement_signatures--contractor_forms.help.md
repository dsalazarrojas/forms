# joint_agreement_signatures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The joint agreement signatures form is designed to collect information about a joint agreement between a contractor and a subcontractor. It is intended for use in situations where both parties need to sign off on an agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of Party A (Contractor) in the "Party A" section.
2. Fill in the details of Party B (Subcontractor) in the "Party B" section.
3. Enter the date on which the agreement was signed in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the time on which the agreement was signed in the "Time" field.
5. Fill in your name as the Contractor in the "Contractor" field.
6. Fill in your name as the Subcontractor in the "Subcontractor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Party A (Contractor)
* **Party A** (`party_a`, text, optional): Please fill in the details of Party A, typically the name and contact information of the contractor.

### Party B (Subcontractor)
* **Party B** (`party_b`, text, optional): Please fill in the details of Party B, typically the name and contact information of the subcontractor.

### Date
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the agreement was signed.

### Time
* **Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which the agreement was signed.

### Contractor
* **Contractor** (`contractor_name`, text, optional): Fill in your name as the Contractor.

### Subcontractor
* **Subcontractor** (`subcontractor_name`, text, optional): Fill in your name as the Subcontractor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check that the information you fill in is accurate and complete.
* If you need to make changes to the form or have any questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
