This help guide is intended for users who will be filling out the "Kitchen Cabinet Installation Agreement" form. The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their kitchen cabinet installation needs, including authorization, cabinet details, installation location, installation date and time, and contact information. This guide will walk you through each field in the form, explaining their purpose and expected input.

# Kitchen Cabinet Installation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their kitchen cabinet installation needs. It is used to understand their requirements and preferences for installing cabinets in their kitchens.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as requested, making sure to complete all required fields.
2. Review each field to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 1. customer_authorization</i> **Customer Authorization** (`customer_authorization`, `number`, required: `false`)
* This field asks for your confirmation of authorization to proceed with the installation. Please choose "Authorized" if you are authorized to represent the customer.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 2. customer_name</i> **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: `false`)
* Enter the name of the person authorized to represent the customer.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 3. cabinet_details</i> **Cabinet Details** (`cabinet_details`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
* Choose the type of cabinet installation you require:
	+ Cabinets Installed: Installation of cabinets, including hardware and shelves.
	+ Countertops Installed: Installation of countertops, including material and edges.
	+ Hardware Installed: Installation of cabinet hardware, such as knobs and hinges.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 4. installation_location</i> **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
* Select all locations where you would like cabinets to be installed. You can choose multiple locations, such as:
	+ Bedroom
	+ Living Room
	+ Kitchen
	+ Bathroom

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 5. installation_date</i> **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, `date`, required: `false`)
* Enter the date you would like the installation to take place.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 6. installation_time</i> **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, `time`, required: `false`)
* Enter the time you would like the installation to take place.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 7. authorization</i> **Authorization** (`authorization`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
* Confirm your authorization to proceed with the installation.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 8. notes</i> **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`)
* Enter any additional notes or details about your installation request.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 9. email</i> **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`)
* Enter your contact email address.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"> 10. phone</i> **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`)
* Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is intended to be completed by the person authorized to represent the customer.
