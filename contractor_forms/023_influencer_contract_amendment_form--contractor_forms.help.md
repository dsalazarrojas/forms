# Influencer Contract Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to allow an influencer to propose changes to their existing contract. The form will help facilitate an agreement between the influencer and the business on the new terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The influencer will fill out the first section with the new terms they wish to amend in their contract.
2. In the "Reason for Contract Change" field, the influencer should provide a clear explanation for the proposed changes.
3. The "Effective Date" field should be filled out with the date the contract changes are to take effect.
4. The "Signature of Influencer" and "Signature of Business" fields are optional and should be filled out by the influencer and business representative, respectively, to confirm their agreement on the amended terms.
5. The "Date Signed Influencer" and "Date Signed Business" fields are also optional and should be filled out by the influencer and business representative to record the date of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, `text`, required): Enter the new terms you would like to amend in your contract.
* **New Contract Term** (`new_contract_term`, `text`, required): Enter the new terms of the amended contract.
* **Reason for Contract Change** (`reason_for_contract_change`, `text`, required): Explain the reason for the proposed changes to the contract.
* **Contract Change** (`contract_change`, `text`, required): Describe the specific changes you are proposing to the contract.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the contract changes are to take effect.
* **Signature of Influencer** (`signature_of_influencer`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the agreement to confirm your proposal.
* **Signature of Business** (`signature_of_business`, `text`, optional): Sign and date to confirm the business's agreement on the amended terms.
* **Date Signed Influencer** (`date_signed_influencer`, `date`, optional): Record the date you signed the amended contract.
* **Date Signed Business** (`date_signed_business`, `date`, optional): Record the date the business signed the amended contract.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review the proposed changes before signing.
* Ensure the changes are reasonable and beneficial to both parties.
* This form is a guide, and you should adjust the content and layout according to your specific business needs.
* Keep the form up to date and review it regularly to ensure it continues to meet your needs.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
