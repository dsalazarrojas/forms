# Construction Project Coordination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants in a construction project. The purpose of this form is to understand each team member's role, contact details, and preferred communication method. The collected data will be used for coordination and communication during the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and role on the project.
2. Enter your organization or company name (if applicable).
3. Fill in your contact email address for coordination and updates.
4. Select your preferred meeting days for coordination meetings.
5. Choose your preferred method of communication for updates (Email, Phone, or Project management tool).
6. Briefly describe your responsibilities during the project coordination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (Participant name): Enter your full name as a team member on the project.
* **Role** (Role): Describe your role within the project, such as project manager, site supervisor, or laborer.
* **Organization** (Organization): Enter your company or subcontractor name, if applicable.
* **Contact email** (Contact email): Enter your email address for project coordination and updates.
* **Preferred meeting days** (Preferred meeting days): Select the days you are available for coordination meetings.
* **Communication preference** (Communication preference): Choose how you would like to receive updates (Email, Phone, or Project management tool).
* **Responsibilities summary** (Responsibilities summary): Briefly describe your responsibilities during project coordination.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Participant name, Role, Contact email, and Communication preference).
* If you have any special instructions or requirements, you can include them in the Responsibilities summary field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
