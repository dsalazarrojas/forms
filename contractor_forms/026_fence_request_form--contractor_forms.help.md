# Fence Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fence Request Form is a tool used to collect information from customers who are interested in requesting a fence installation. This form helps us gather essential details about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Company Name (if applicable).
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Choose a Request Date and Request Time for your fence installation.
6. Select the type of fence you're interested in (Residential, Commercial, or Agricultural).
7. Choose the type of property you own (House, Apartment, Land, Condo, Townhouse, Mobile Home, or Commercial Building).
8. Enter the Installation Date and Installation Time for your fence.
9. Add any additional Comments about your fence request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal name as it should appear on our records.
* **Company Name**: If you're a business owner or representative, enter your company's name. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you about your fence request.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your fence request.
* **Request Date**: Choose the date when you'd like to have your fence installed.
* **Request Time**: Choose the time of day when you'd like to have your fence installed.
* **Fence Type**: Select the type of fence you're interested in: Residential, Commercial, or Agricultural.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you own: House, Apartment, Land, Condo, Townhouse, Mobile Home, or Commercial Building.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date when you'd like to have your fence installed.
* **Installation Time**: Enter the time of day when you'd like to have your fence installed.
* **Comments**: Add any additional details or instructions about your fence request.
