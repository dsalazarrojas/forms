# Contractor Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contractor Questionnaire" is a comprehensive form designed to help us assess a contractor's suitability for future collaborations and projects. It collects essential information about their business details, credentials, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the applicant's personal information: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your business details: Business Name, Business Type, Years in Business, and Number of Employees (if applicable).
3. Share your professional credentials: Contractor License Number, License State, License Expiration Date, and Insurance Carrier.
4. Showcase your expertise: Primary Specialty and Secondary Specialties.
5. Select the types of projects you have completed: Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Government, or Institutional.
6. Specify your largest project value handled: Under $50,000, $50,000-$100,000, $100,000-$500,000, $500,000-$1,000,000, or Over $1,000,000.
7. Provide references: 2 references with company name, contact person name, and phone number.
8. Specify your availability for new projects: Immediately, Within 2 weeks, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, or Not available.
9. Define your service area: A geographic area you serve (e.g., Local, Regional, National).
10. List any additional certifications you hold.
11. Leave any additional comments or information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): This is your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your best contact number.
* **Business Address** (`address`, text, required): This is your full business address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This is your business's legal name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business entity you are (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, S-Corporation, Non-Profit).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, select_one, required): Indicate how long you have been operating your business.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): If applicable, specify the total number of employees in your company.
* **Contractor License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Provide your professional license number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, text, required): Indicate the state where your license is issued.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration`, date, required): Specify when your license expires.
* **Insurance Carrier** (`insurance_carrier`, text, required): Name your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, required): Provide your policy number.
* **Liability Coverage Amount** (`liability_coverage`, text, required): Specify your total liability coverage.
* **Workers Compensation Insurance** (`workers_compensation`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have workers comp insurance.
* **Are you Bonded?** (`bonded`, select_one, required): Select if you are bonded.
* **Primary Specialty** (`primary_specialty`, select_one, required): Specify your main area of expertise (e.g., General Contracting, Electrical, Plumbing, etc.).
* **Secondary Specialties** (`secondary_specialties`, select_multiple, optional): List additional areas of expertise you have.
* **Types of Projects Completed** (`project_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of projects you have completed (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Government, Institutional).
* **Largest Project Value** (`largest_project_value`, select_one, optional): Indicate the highest value project you have handled.
* **Reference 1 Company** (`reference_1_company`, text, optional): Provide the name of one of your references.
* **Reference 1 Contact Name** (`reference_1_contact`, text, optional): Specify the contact person's name for this reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, text, optional): Provide the contact number for this reference.
* **Reference 2 Company** (`reference_2_company`, text, optional): Provide the name of another reference.
* **Reference 2 Contact Name** (`reference_2_contact`, text, optional): Specify the contact person's name for this reference.
* **Reference 2 Phone** (`reference_2_phone`, text, optional): Provide the contact number for this reference.
* **Current Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Specify your current availability for new projects.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, text, required): Define the geographic area you serve.
* **Additional Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any additional certifications you hold.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or information about yourself.

Note: All required fields must be filled out accurately and completely to ensure a thorough assessment of your suitability for future collaborations and projects.
