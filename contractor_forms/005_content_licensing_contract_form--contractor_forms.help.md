# Content Licensing Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Licensing Contract Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of licensing content between the Licensor and Licensee. This form ensures that both parties are aware of their rights and responsibilities regarding the content in question.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Licensor Name (Owner)** field with the full name of the content owner.
2. Fill in the **Licensee Name (User)** field with the full name of the entity requesting usage rights.
3. Describe the **Description of Content** field with a clear and concise description of the content being licensed.
4. Select the **Type of License** that suits your needs: Exclusive Rights, Non-Exclusive Rights, One-Time Use Only, or Perpetual Use.
5. Specify where the **Permitted Platforms** where the content can be used: Social Media, Print Media, Television/Broadcast, Website/Digital Ads, or Corporate Presentations.
6. Enter the **Duration of License (Months)**: 0 for perpetual use, or the desired duration in months.
7. Choose the **Geographic Territory** where the content can be used: Worldwide, North America Only, Europe Only, or a specific country mentioned in the notes.
8. Enter the **Total Licensing Fee (USD)** for the content usage.
9. Select the **Payment Terms**: Paid in Full Upfront, 50 percent Deposit - 50 percent on Delivery, or Monthly Royalty Payments.
10. Decide if **Attribution is required**: Yes - Standard Credit Line, Yes - Tagging on Social Media, or No - White Label Use.
11. Enter the **Effective Date of Agreement**: the date when the contract takes effect.
12. Finally, add your **Licensor Digital Signature** and **Licensee Digital Signature** to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contracting Parties**: Information for the Licensor and Licensee.
* **Licensor Name (Owner)**: Full name of the content owner.
* **Licensee Name (User)**: Full name of the entity requesting usage rights.
* **Description of Content**: Clear and concise description of the content being licensed.
* **Type of License**: Select the type of license that suits your needs.
* **Permitted Platforms**: Specify where the content can be used.
* **Duration of License (Months)**: Specify the duration of the license or 0 for perpetual use.
* **Geographic Territory**: Choose the area where the content can be used.
* **Total Licensing Fee (USD)**: Enter the payment for content usage.
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment terms: Paid in Full Upfront, 50 percent Deposit - 50 percent on Delivery, or Monthly Royalty Payments.
* **Is attribution required**: Decide if the licensee needs to credit the creator.
* **Effective Date of Agreement**: Date when the contract takes effect.
* **Licensor Digital Signature**: Type full name to sign as the content owner.
* **Licensee Digital Signature**: Type full name to sign as the entity requesting usage rights.
