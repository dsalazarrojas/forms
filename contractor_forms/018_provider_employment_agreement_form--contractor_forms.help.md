# provider_employment_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms of employment for external providers who will be working on behalf of our organization. It ensures that all parties have a clear understanding of the agreement, including the provider's name, contact information, and compensation terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the appropriate agreement type from the options provided.
3. Enter the start date of the employment agreement.
4. Review and provide any additional compensation terms if necessary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **provider_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the provider, as they will be listed on our records.
* **provider_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the provider, as this will be our primary contact method.
* **provider_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the provider, in case we need to contact them directly.
* **agreement_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of agreement from the options provided, which will determine the terms and conditions of the employment.
* **start_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the employment agreement will start.
* **compensation_terms** (Text, Optional): If any additional compensation terms are necessary, enter them here. This can include any special conditions or requirements for payment.
* **signatures** (Note, Required): This is a text field for both parties to sign and date, indicating their agreement to the terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or contact the relevant team member for assistance.
