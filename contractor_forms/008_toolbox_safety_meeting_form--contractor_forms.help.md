# Toolbox Safety Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the contents of a toolbox safety meeting, including the tools listed, assigned tools, meeting notes, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the fields according to the prompts.
2. List the various tools that are in your toolbox using the options provided (Tools, Equipment, Other).
3. Choose the appropriate category for the form (General, Safety, Operations).
4. Select the form completion status (Complete, Incomplete).
5. Provide any additional notes or observations about the toolbox safety meeting.
6. Enter the date and time of the toolbox safety meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Safety Rules** (`general_safety_rules`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to document any general safety rules or guidelines that were discussed during the meeting.
- **Tools List** (`tools_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the options (Tools, Equipment, Other) to list the various tools that are in your toolbox.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter any tools that were assigned during the meeting.
- **Toolbox Safety Meeting Note** (`toolbox_safety_meeting_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes or observations about the meeting, such as actions items or next steps.
- **Contractor Forms** (`contractor_forms`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to select the form category (General, Safety, Operations).
- **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the form completion status (Complete, Incomplete).
- **Assigned Tool Jim** (`assigned_tool_jim`, `text`, required: false): Enter any tools that were assigned to Jim during the meeting. This field is likely for specific or additional information.
- **Toolbox Safety Meeting Date** (`toolbox_safety_meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the toolbox safety meeting.
- **Toolbox Safety Meeting Time** (`toolbox_safety_meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the toolbox safety meeting.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and update any previous form completion statuses before filling out the current form.
- Ensure the tools listed are accurately reflected in the toolbox and relevant to the meeting.
- Use the date and time fields to record the exact date and time of the meeting.
