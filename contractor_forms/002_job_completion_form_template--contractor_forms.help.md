# Job Completion Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job completion form template is used to collect information about completed jobs from contractors and staff. This form is designed to capture details about the job, including the job title, dates, hours worked, job notes, employer information, and job type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an * symbol.
2. Enter the job title in the *job_title* field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the job in the *start_date* and *end_date* fields, respectively. If the job is still ongoing, you can leave the *end_date* field blank.
4. Enter the hours worked for the job in the *hours_worked* field. This can be left blank if the job is still ongoing.
5. In the *job_notes* field, provide a brief description of the job, including any relevant details.
6. Enter the employer's name and contact information in the *employer* field.
7. Enter the client's name and contact information in the *client* field.
8. Enter the supervisor's name and contact information in the *supervisor* field, if applicable.
9. Enter the job number in the *job_number* field.
10. Select the correct job type from the options provided in the *job_type* field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (*job_title*, *text*, *required*): Enter the name of the job that was completed.
* **Start Date** (*start_date*, *date*, *required*): Enter the date when the job started.
* **End Date** (*end_date*, *text*, *optional*): Enter the date when the job ended. If the job is still ongoing, leave this field blank.
* **Hours Worked** (*hours_worked*, *number*, *optional*): Enter the total number of hours worked on the job. If the job is still ongoing, this field can be left blank.
* **Job Notes** (*job_notes*, *text*, *required*): Provide a brief description of the job, including any relevant details.
* **Employer** (*employer*, *text*, *required*): Enter the employer's name and contact information.
* **Client** (*client*, *text*, *required*): Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Supervisor** (*supervisor*, *text*, *optional*): Enter the supervisor's name and contact information, if applicable.
* **Job Number** (*job_number*, *text*, *required*): Enter the job number for the completed job.
* **Job Type** (*job_type*, *select_one*, *required*): Select the correct job type from the options provided.
    * **Job Type Options:**
        + Job Type 1
        + Job Type 2
        + Job Type 3
        + Job Type 4
        + Job Type 5

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or manager for clarification.
- If you need help with data entry or form submission, please contact our support team.
