# sprint_service_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from contractors regarding the service agreement for a particular sprint, including details about the sprint, client, project, team, and testing status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the **Sprint Details** section, which includes information about the sprint.
2. Proceed to the **Client Details** section and fill in the necessary information.
3. Move on to the **Project Details** section and provide the required information.
4. The **Team Details** section is where you can enter information about your team.
5. In the **Project Manager Details** section, enter the necessary information about the project manager.
6. Move to the **Sprint Scope** section and provide the scope of the sprint.
7. In the **Acceptance Criteria** section, list the acceptance criteria for the project.
8. Fill in the **Testing Status** section, which includes the status of the testing process.
9. Enter the **Testing Status Date** and **Testing Status Time** to mark the date and time of the testing status.
10. In the **Testing Status Note** section, you can add any additional comments or notes about the testing status.
11. Enter the **Testing Status Email** and **Testing Status Phone** to provide contact information.
12. In the **Testing Status Comment** section, add any additional comments about the testing status.
13. Finally, provide the **Testing Status File** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sprint Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the sprint.
* **Client Details** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, company, and contact information.
* **Project Details** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the project's name, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Team Details** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the names and contact information of the team members.
* **Project Manager Details** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the project manager's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Sprint Scope** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the scope of the sprint, including the tasks and deliverables.
* **Acceptance Criteria** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): List the acceptance criteria for the project.
* **Testing Status** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the status of the testing process.
* **Testing Status Date** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date of the testing status.
* **Testing Status Time** (`id: 10`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time of the testing status.
* **Testing Status Note** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the testing status.
* **Testing Status Email** (`id: 12`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the testing status.
* **Testing Status Phone** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the testing status.
* **Testing Status Comment** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the testing status.
* **Testing Status File** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a file or attachment related to the testing status.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form's purpose and the field's label.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project manager or the support team.
