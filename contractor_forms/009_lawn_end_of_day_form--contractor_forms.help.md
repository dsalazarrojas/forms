# Lawn End Of Day Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users report their daily lawn care activities, lawn condition, any issues encountered, and equipment used at the end of the day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions accurately.
2. Select the "Lawn Activities" field to describe any work done on the lawn that day.
3. Provide a brief description of the lawn's condition in the "Lawn Condition" field.
4. List any issues encountered during the day in the "Issues Encountered" field.
5. Mention the equipment used during the day in the "Equipment Used" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lawn Activities** (`lawn_activities`, text, required): Describe any work done on the lawn, such as mowing, pruning, watering, etc.
* **Lawn Condition** (`lawn_condition`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the lawn's condition, such as its appearance, texture, or any noticeable changes.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, text, required): List any issues, problems, or concerns encountered during the day, such as pest control, drainage issues, etc.
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`, text, required): Mention any equipment used during the day, such as lawnmowers, trimmers, hoses, etc.

## Tips
- Be accurate and detailed in your responses to help users understand your lawn care activities, condition, issues, and equipment usage.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed daily, so try to complete it at the end of each day to ensure accurate and consistent reporting.
