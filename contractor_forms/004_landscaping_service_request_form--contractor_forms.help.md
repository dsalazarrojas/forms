# Landscaping Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Service Request Form is designed to gather information from customers about the landscaping services they require. This form will help us provide more accurate and efficient services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Describe the service you require (Landscaping Service Request).
6. Specify your preferred location.
7. List the services you are willing to accept (Services Offered).
8. Specify the services you want (Services Wanted).
9. Enter the date you would like to start the service.
10. Enter the time you would like to start the service.
11. Select the services you are interested in (Services).
12. Enter a brief description of your request.
13. Add any additional comments.
14. Select the tool you would like to use for the service (Assigned Tool).
15. Choose the status of your request (Status).
16. Enter the date you would like to start the service.
17. Enter the date you submitted your request (Submitted Date).
18. Enter the due date for completion.
19. Enter the date the service was completed.
20. Select who submitted the request (Submitted By).
21. Enter the email address of who submitted the request (Submitted By Email).
22. Enter the phone number of who submitted the request (Submitted By Phone).
23. Select the tool used to submit the request (Submitted By Tool).
24. Enter the name of the assigned user.
25. Add any notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Landscaping Service Request** (`service_request`, text, optional): Describe the landscaping service you require.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the service is needed.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): List the services you are willing to accept.
* **Services Wanted** (`services_wanted`, text, optional): Specify the services you want.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start the service.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to start the service.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you are interested in.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool you would like to use for the service.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your request.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start the service.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for completion.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was completed.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the request.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of who submitted the request.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of who submitted the request.
* **Submitted By Tool** (`submitted_by_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool used to submit the request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about your request.
