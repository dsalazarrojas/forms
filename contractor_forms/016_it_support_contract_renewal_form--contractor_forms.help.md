# IT Support Contract Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the IT support contract renewal process. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field to ensure it matches your needs.
2. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
3. If you have any file attachments, please upload them in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, optional): Enter your contract number to help us identify your contract.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your contract, which can be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, note, optional): Provide any relevant contract details for our review.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the contract start date, if known.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the contract end date, if known.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or questions about your contract renewal.
* **File Attachments** (`file_attachments`, text, optional): Upload any relevant file attachments related to your contract renewal.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about a field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Attachments should be in a suitable format (e.g., PDF, JPEG, or DOCX).
