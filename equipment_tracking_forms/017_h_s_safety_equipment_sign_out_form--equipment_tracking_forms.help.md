# H₂S Safety Equipment Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and recording the use of safety equipment by staff members. It helps safety coordinators or managers keep track of who has used the equipment and for how long, which can prevent damage or misuse.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the equipment ID in the `equipment_id` field. This is the unique identifier for the safety equipment being used.
2. Enter your ID in the `user_id` field. This is your unique identifier to be associated with the equipment use.
3. Select the date you signed out the equipment in the `date_signed_out` field. Use the date format MM/DD/YY.
4. Select the time you signed out the equipment in the `time_in_sign_out` field. Use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
5. Enter the total hours the equipment was used in the `time_in_hours_used` field.
6. Enter any comments or notes about the use of the equipment in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment_id**: A unique identifier for the safety equipment being used. Enter the correct ID for the equipment you are signing out.
* **user_id**: Your unique identifier to be associated with the equipment use. Enter your own ID to ensure proper tracking.
* **date_signed_out**: The date you signed out the equipment. Use the MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 01/01/22).
* **time_in_sign_out**: The time you signed out the equipment. Use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:30).
* **time_in_hours_used**: The total hours the equipment was used.
* **comments**: Any additional notes or comments about the use of the equipment. Use this field to report any issues or concerns.
