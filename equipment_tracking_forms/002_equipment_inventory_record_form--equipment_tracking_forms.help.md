# Equipment Inventory Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track equipment inventory. It captures various details about each equipment item, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Choose the correct options for the "Equipment Type", "Location", "Status", and "Maintenance Schedule" fields based on the equipment's specifics.
3. Enter the equipment's "Serial Number", "Model Number", and "Purchase Date" accurately.
4. Indicate the "Purchase Value" of the equipment.
5. Select the "Assigned Employee" and check the "Equipment Owner" field as necessary.
6. Add any relevant "Notes", "Email Address", "Phone Number", and "Comments" for the equipment.
7. Review and submit the form to update the equipment's record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required): Enter the equipment's unique serial number.
* **Model Number** (`model_number`, text, required): Enter the equipment's model number.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of equipment (e.g., Computer, Printer, Other).
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the equipment was purchased (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Purchase Value** (`purchase_value`, number, required): Enter the purchase value of the equipment.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required): Select where the equipment is currently located (e.g., Warehouse, Office, Other).
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required): Select the equipment's status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Lost).
* **Assigned Employee** (`assigned_employee`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the equipment is assigned to an employee.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the equipment.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address for the equipment's owner or manager.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the equipment's owner or manager.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Select the equipment's maintenance schedule (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually).
* **Asset Manager Comments** (`asset_manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments for the asset manager.
* **Equipment Owner** (`equipment_owner`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the equipment is owned by the organization or not.

## Tips

* Always ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the equipment's manual or contact the equipment's owner or manager.
* Review and submit the form to update the equipment's record.
* For any issues or discrepancies, contact the asset manager or supervisor.
