# equipment_maintenance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the `equipment_maintenance_monitoring_form` is to collect data on the maintenance schedule, performance, cost, frequency, and duration of equipment. This form is used to monitor and track the maintenance activities of equipment to ensure they are running efficiently and effectively. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct option from the navigation menu or link to access the `maintenance_schedule` page.
2. Enter the required details for the maintenance schedule, such as the type of maintenance and any other relevant information.
3. Enter the current equipment performance on the `equipment_performance` page, providing accurate numerical values.
4. Enter the cost associated with the maintenance on the `maintenance_cost` page, using numerical values.
5. Select the maintenance frequency on the `maintenance_frequency` page, choosing the correct option from the provided options.
6. Enter the maintenance duration on the `maintenance_duration` page, using a time value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the details of the maintenance schedule, including the type of maintenance and any other relevant information.
* **Equipment Performance** (`equipment_performance`, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the current performance of the equipment, such as efficiency, output, or other metrics.
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the cost associated with the maintenance, including labor, materials, and other expenses.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, text, Optional):
	+ Select the frequency of the maintenance, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
* **Maintenance Duration** (`maintenance_duration`, time, Optional):
	+ Enter the duration of the maintenance, such as the start and end time of the maintenance activity.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Ensure that you are using the correct unit for each field, such as hours, days, or months.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the equipment operator or maintenance personnel for clarification.
