# Ski Equipment Rental Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the status of ski equipment rental orders. The form helps in managing customer information, equipment selection, rental details, and other related data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please indicate if the customer is booking ski equipment**: Check the box to confirm if the customer is booking equipment or not.
2. **Provide customer information**: Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
3. **Specify rental details**: Select from the options to confirm if the customer is renting or not.
4. **Select the equipment to be rented**: Choose the equipment that the customer has selected or not.
5. **Provide delivery details**: Enter any additional information about the delivery of the equipment.
6. **Add any order notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
7. **Calculate the total rental cost**: Enter the total cost of the rental order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ski_equipment_rental_order_form_input**: A checkbox to confirm if the customer is booking ski equipment. Please select 'Yes' if the customer is booking equipment.
* **customer_information**: A field to enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
* **rental_info**: A dropdown menu to confirm if the customer is renting equipment or not.
* **equipment_selected**: A checkbox to confirm if the customer has selected equipment. Please select 'Yes' if the customer has selected equipment.
* **delivery_details**: A field to enter any additional information about the delivery of the equipment.
* **order_notes**: A field to enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **total_rental_cost**: A field to enter the total cost of the rental order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the customer or contact your supervisor for clarification.
* Double-check your calculations for total rental cost to ensure accuracy.
