# Fiberglass Extension Ladder Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fiberglass Extension Ladder Inventory Form is a tool used to monitor and record the status of fiberglass extension ladders within your facility. This form helps ensure that each ladder's information is up-to-date and that inspections are performed regularly.

## How To Complete This Form
### Before starting
- Please note that this form requires you to provide the facility ID to associate the form data with the correct facility.
- The form is split into several sections, each with specific fields for collecting and tracking information.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Form Title**: Enter the title of the form for easy identification and reference.
2. **Facility ID**: Enter the ID of the facility where the ladder is located. This is crucial for tracking and managing the inventory across multiple facilities.
3. **Equipment Type**: Select whether the ladder is available or not.
4. **Equipment Make**: Provide the make of the ladder for accurate documentation.
5. **Equipment Model**: Enter the model number of the ladder for detailed tracking.
6. **Location**: Indicate where the ladder is situated. This will help locate the ladder for maintenance or inspection.
7. **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number for tracking purposes.
8. **Condition**: Indicate the current condition of the ladder. This is used to prioritize maintenance or inspection.
9. **Inspection Due Date**: Enter the date by which the ladder's next inspection is due.
10. **Last Inspection Result**: Record the outcome of the previous inspection.
11. **Next Inspection Date**: Schedule the date for the next inspection.
12. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes specific to this ladder.
13. **User Assigned ID**: Enter the ID of the user assigned to this specific ladder for management and follow-up.
14. **User Comments**: Add any additional comments from the user.
