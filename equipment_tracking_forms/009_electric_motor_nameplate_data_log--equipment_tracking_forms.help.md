# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about equipment details, maintenance, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of the equipment in the "Motor Details" field.
2. Provide any replacement or maintenance information in the "Replacement Info" field.
3. Enter any additional information about the equipment's maintenance in the "Maintenance Info" field.
4. Enter contact information, such as email or phone number, in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Select a category from the list of options in the "Category" field. This will help with organization and filtering.
6. Choose the tags that best describe the equipment from the "Tags" field. You can select multiple tags.
7. Add any custom notes or information in the "Custom Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Motor Details** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the equipment.
* **Replacement Info** (`replacement_info`, `text`, required): Provide any information about replacement or repair history.
* **Maintenance Info** (`maintenance_info`, `text`, required): Enter any maintenance history or schedules.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter contact information for equipment owner or responsible personnel.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select a category for the equipment (e.g. Electrical Motors, Electrical Pumps, etc.).
* **Tags** (`tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose tags that describe the equipment (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Custom Info** (`custom_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any custom notes or information about the equipment.
