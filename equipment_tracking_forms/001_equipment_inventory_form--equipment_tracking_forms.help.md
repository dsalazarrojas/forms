**Equipment Inventory Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose

The Equipment Inventory Form is designed to track and manage equipment within an organization. It ensures that all equipment is accurately documented and easily accessible for maintenance, repairs, or upgrades.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Inventory Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a clear title for the inventory item.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique ID for the equipment item.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the equipment item.
* **Category** (`equipment_category`, text, required: false): Select the category of the equipment from the available options (e.g., IT, Maintenance, etc.).
* **Brand** (`equipment_brand`, text, required: false): Enter the brand of the equipment.
* **Model** (`equipment_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model of the equipment.
* **Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_num`, text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Condition** (`equipment_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the current condition of the equipment.
* **Location** (`equipment_location`, text, required: false): Specify the current location of the equipment.
* **Status** (`equipment_status`, text, required: false): Update the status of the equipment (e.g., operational, in maintenance, etc.).
* **Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required: false): Add any relevant description of the equipment.
* **Notes** (`form_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the equipment.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required: false): Record the person who created the record.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, text, required: false): Record the date and time the record was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required: false): Record the person who last updated the record.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, text, required: false): Record the date and time the record was last updated.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and management of your equipment inventory.
* Use the available options for categories to select the most relevant one for your equipment.
* Keep the condition field up-to-date to reflect any changes in the equipment's status.
* Use the notes field to add any additional information that may be useful for future reference.
* Regularly review and update the equipment inventory to reflect any changes in the equipment's status or location.
