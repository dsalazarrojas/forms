# Robot Equipment Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robot Equipment Safety Inspection Form is used to track and report the safety inspection status of robot equipment. The form helps maintain accurate records and enables timely interventions to prevent accidents and ensure optimal equipment usage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment being inspected (e.g., Robot Arm, Robotic System, or Other).
2. Enter the equipment model (if known or available).
3. Record the date of the last inspection.
4. Schedule the next inspection date.
5. Document any hazardous conditions or notes.
6. Add any comments or observations from the inspection.
7. Select the personnel responsible for the next step in the inspection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **equipment_type**: Choose the type of robot equipment being inspected (e.g., Robot Arm, Robotic System, or Other).
* **equipment_model**: Enter the model of the robot equipment (if known or available).
* **last_inspection**: Record the date of the last inspection.
* **next_inspection**: Schedule the date for the next inspection.
* **hazardous_conditions**: Write any notes about hazardous conditions or observations found during the inspection.
* **comments**: Add any comments or observations from the inspection.
* **assigned_to**: Select the person(s) responsible for the next step in the inspection process (e.g., Jim, John, or Jane).

## Tips

- Always complete the form in a timely manner, ideally after each inspection.
- Update the form regularly to ensure accurate records and prevent potential hazards.
- Review and verify information entered by others to ensure accuracy and maintain a reliable inspection history.
- Use this form as part of a systematic approach to equipment tracking and safety maintenance.
