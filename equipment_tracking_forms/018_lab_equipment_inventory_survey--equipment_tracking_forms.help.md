# Lab Equipment Inventory Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track and document lab equipment details for inventory management. It is essential to ensure that equipment is properly recorded and updated to prevent loss or misplacement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the equipment details, including its name and any additional comments in the "Equipment Details" field.
2. Select the current condition of the equipment from the list provided in the "Condition" field.
3. Choose the location where the equipment is currently stored from the list in the "Location" field.
4. Select the person(s) responsible for the equipment from the list in the "Responsible Staff" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment in the "Notes" field.
6. If applicable, enter the unique Equipment ID in the "Equipment ID" field.
7. Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Serial Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Details (Equipment Details)**: Enter a brief description of the equipment, including its name and any other relevant information.
* **Condition (Condition)**: Select the current state of the equipment, which can be "new", "good", "fair", or "poor".
* **Location (Location)**: Choose where the equipment is currently stored, which can be "main room", "storage", or "other".
* **Responsible Staff (Responsible Staff)**: Select the person(s) responsible for the equipment, which can be "research", "clinical", "industrial", or multiple options.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Equipment ID (Equipment ID)**: If available, enter the unique identifier assigned to the equipment.
* **Serial Number (Serial Number)**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Double-check the "Location" field to ensure the equipment is stored in the correct place.
* Be accurate when selecting the "Condition" of the equipment to reflect its true state.
* Enter the "Responsible Staff" field with the correct personnel assigned to the equipment.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any relevant information about the equipment's maintenance, usage, or issues.
