# Mobile Checklist Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and record information related to mobile devices, such as asset inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the User Input section.
2. Record the asset check results in the Asset Check section.
3. Enter the date and time of submission in the Date and Time Submitted section.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the asset in the Additional Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **MobileDeviceInspection** (User Input): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **AssetCheck** (Asset Check): Record the results of the asset check, including any critical, minor, or normal issues found.
* **DateSubmitted** (Date and Time Submitted): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **SubmittedBy** (User Input): Enter your name.
* **FacilityName** (User Input): Enter the name of the facility or location where the asset is being used.
* **AssetTag** (Asset Details): Enter the asset tag number or ID.
* **AssetModel** (Asset Details): Enter the asset model or type.
* **Condition** (Asset Details): Select the condition of the asset (Critical, Minor, or Normal).
* **AssetStatus** (Asset Details): Select the status of the asset (Active, Inactive, or Offline).
* **Notes** (Additional Comments): Enter any additional comments or notes about the asset.
* **EmailAddress** (User Input): Enter your email address.
* **PhoneNumber** (User Input): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (Additional Comments): Enter any additional comments or notes about the asset.
* **ChecklistStatus** (Asset Details): Select the status of the checklist (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
* **AssetType** (Asset Details): Enter the type of asset.
* **SerialNumber** (Asset Details): Enter the serial number of the asset.
* **AssetLocation** (Asset Details): Enter the location of the asset.
* **AssetDescription** (Asset Details): Enter a brief description of the asset.
* **EquipmentCategory** (Asset Details): Enter the category of the equipment.
* **AssetTagNumber** (Asset Details): Enter the asset tag number or ID.
* **AssetMake** (Asset Details): Enter the asset make or manufacturer.
* **AssetModelYear** (Asset Details): Enter the model year of the asset.
* **AssetModelNumber** (Asset Details): Enter the model number of the asset.
