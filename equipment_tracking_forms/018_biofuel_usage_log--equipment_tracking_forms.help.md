# Biofuel Usage Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track biofuel usage on equipment, providing essential data for monitoring and maintaining accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Operator Information** section:
	* **Operator Name**: Enter the full name of the operator.
	* **Operator ID**: Enter the employee or driver ID.
2. Provide **Department** information:
	* Enter the department or division related to the biofuel usage.
3. Log the **Log Date**:
	* Enter the date of biofuel usage.
4. Add **Equipment Information** (optional):
	* Provide any relevant notes about the equipment.
5. Fill in **Equipment Details**:
	* **Equipment ID**: Enter the vehicle or equipment number.
	* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment used (e.g., Truck, Van, Bus, Generator, etc.).
	* **Make**: Enter the manufacturer name.
	* **Model**: Enter the model name or number.
	* **Year**: Enter the year of manufacture.
6. Provide **Biofuel Details**:
	* **Biofuel Type**: Select the type of biofuel used (e.g., Biodiesel B5, Biodiesel B20, Ethanol E10, etc.).
7. Enter **Fuel Supplier** information:
	* Enter the name of the fuel supplier.
8. Add **Fuel Consumption Data**:
	* **Quantity Used**: Enter the amount of biofuel consumed.
	* **Unit of Measure**: Select the unit of measure for the quantity used (e.g., Gallons, Liters, Barrels, etc.).
9. Add **Operating Conditions**:
	* **Location**: Enter where the equipment was operated (e.g., Highway, City, Off-Road, etc.).
	* **Operating Conditions**: Select the operating conditions (e.g., Highway Driving, City Driving, Off-Road, etc.).
10. Provide **Supervisory Information** (optional):
	* **Supervisor Review**: Select the status of the supervisor review (e.g., Reviewed and Approved, Pending Review, etc.).
	* **Additional Notes** (optional): Add any other comments or notes.

	+ **Operator ID**: Enter the employee or driver ID.

* **Log Date**: Date of biofuel usage.
* **Equipment Information**: Provides additional notes about the equipment.

	+ **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment used.
	+ **Make**: Enter the manufacturer name.
	+ **Model**: Enter the model name or number.
	+ **Year**: Enter the year of manufacture.
* **Biofuel Details**: Select the type of biofuel used.
* **Fuel Supplier**: Enter the name of the fuel supplier.
* **Quantity Used**: Enter the amount of biofuel consumed.
* **Unit of Measure**: Select the unit of measure for the quantity used.
* **Location**: Enter where the equipment was operated.
* **Operating Conditions**: Select the operating conditions.
* **Biofuel Type**: Select the type of biofuel used.
