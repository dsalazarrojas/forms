# Documentary Screening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Documentary Screening Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from users for a documentary screening event. This form will help the organiser to process the orders and manage the event logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information (page 1)
	* Enter your name and other relevant details.
2. Provide your E-mail (page 2)
	* Enter your valid email address.
3. Enter your Phone Number (page 3)
	* Type your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter Number of Tickets (page 4)
	* Type the number of tickets you want to purchase.
5. Select Payment Method (page 5)
	* Choose how you want to make the payment (Card, Cash, or Other).
6. Select Ticket Type (page 6)
	* Choose the type of ticket you want (Standard, VIP, or Premium).
7. Enter Special Requests (page 7)
	* If you have any special requests, type them here.
8. Confirm your order (page 8)
	* Confirm if you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information Form** (page 1)
	* Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **E-mail** (page 2)
	* Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (page 3)
	* Type your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Number of Tickets** (page 4)
	* Type the number of tickets you want to purchase.
* **Payment Method** (page 5)
	* Choose how you want to make the payment (Card, Cash, or Other).
* **Other Payment Method** (page 5)
	* If you selected "Other", describe your payment method.
* **Special Requests** (page 7)
	* If you have any special requests, type them here.
* **Ticket Type** (page 6)
	* Choose the type of ticket you want (Standard, VIP, or Premium).
* **Total Amount Paid** (page 8)
	* Type the total amount you have paid.
* **Confirm** (page 8)
	* Confirm if you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
