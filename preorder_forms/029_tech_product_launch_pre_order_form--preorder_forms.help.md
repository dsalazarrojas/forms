# Tech Product Launch Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tech Product Launch Pre Order Form is designed for customers interested in purchasing a tech product before its official launch. This form helps our team gather essential information about you and your preorder details to confirm your interest and prepare for the launch.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, following the instructions for each field.
2. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form to initiate the preorder process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address associated with your preorder account.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): If you are a business or organization, enter your company name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): If you are a professional, enter your job title here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Shipping Address 1** (`shipping_address_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address line 1.
* **Shipping Address 2** (`shipping_address_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address line 2.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country for your shipping address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required: false): Select a payment method for your preorder (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Card Type** (`payment_card_type`, `text`, required: false): If you selected a credit card, enter the type of card here.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the preorder.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is not a purchase confirmation, and we will contact you to confirm your preorder details.
