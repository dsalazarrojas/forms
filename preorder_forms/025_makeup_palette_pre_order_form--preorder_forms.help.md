# Makeup Palette Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Palette Pre Order Form is a pre-order form for customers to provide their personal and delivery details, select a color and size for their custom-made makeup palette, and confirm their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
2. Provide your delivery details in the "Delivery Details" section.
3. Select the color and size for your custom-made makeup palette in the "Color Selection" and "Size Selection" sections, respectively.
4. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Information" section.
5. Confirm your order in the "Order Confirmation" section.
6. Finally, submit or cancel your order in the "Form Submit" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)** (text, personal_details, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details that you would like to share with us.
* **Delivery Details (2)** (text, delivery_details, required: false): Enter your delivery address, phone number, and any special delivery instructions.
* **Color Selection (3)** (text, color_selection, required: false): Select one of the available color options for your custom-made makeup palette. You can choose from our palette of colors, and we'll work with you to create a customized palette that meets your preferences.
* **Size Selection (4)** (text, size_selection, required: false): Select the size of your custom-made makeup palette from our available sizes.
* **Payment Information (5)** (text, payment_info, required: false): Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card information or PayPal information.
* **Order Confirmation (6)** (text, order_confirmation, required: false): Double-check your order details to ensure everything is correct before submitting your order.
* **Form Submit (7)** (select_one, form_submit, required: false): Click "Submit" to confirm your order, or "Cancel" to start again or exit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details in the "Order Confirmation" section before submitting your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We'll work with you to create a customized palette that meets your preferences and budget.
