# Drone Model Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Model Pre Order Form is designed to help you place a preorder for a specific drone model. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your preorder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the delivery date for your preorder.
6. Select the delivery time for your preorder.
7. Enter the quantity you would like to preorder.
8. Enter your delivery address.
9. Select the drone model you would like to preorder. You can choose from the following options:
	- Drone
	- Drone 2
	- Drone 3
10. Enter your delivery city.
11. Enter your delivery state.
12. Enter your delivery postal code.
13. Select the color of the drone model you would like to preorder. You can choose from the following options:
	- Red
	- Blue
	- Green
	- Yellow
14. Enter the country where you would like to receive your delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (first_name, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery date** (delivery_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your preorder. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery time** (delivery_time, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your preorder.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required: false): Enter the number of units you would like to preorder.
* **Delivery address** (delivery_address, text, required: false): Enter your delivery address.
* **Product model** (product_model, select_one, required: false): Select the drone model you would like to preorder. You can choose from the following options:
	- Drone
	- Drone 2
	- Drone 3
* **Delivery city** (delivery_city, text, required: false): Enter the city where you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery state** (delivery_state, text, required: false): Enter the state where you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery postal code** (delivery_postal_code, text, required: false): Enter the postal code where you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Product color** (product_color, select_multiple, required: false): Select the color of the drone model you would like to preorder. You can choose from the following options:
	- Red
	- Blue
	- Green
	- Yellow
* **Delivery country** (delivery_country, text, required: false): Enter the country where you would like to receive your delivery.
