# Canvas Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, our customer, place a pre-order for a custom canvas print. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a smooth and efficient pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the "Pre-order your custom canvas print today" option to get started.
- Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your accurate and complete information.
- If you have any additional requests or special notes, you can include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
- Finally, review and confirm your agreement to the pre-order terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Canvas Pre-Order Form --**: This is the title of the form and a reminder of what you are pre-ordering.
* **Pre-order your custom canvas print today. Fill out this form with your details and specifications.**: This is a brief introduction to the form and a prompt to fill out the form.
* **First Name**: Your first name is required for contact purposes.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is required for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full address for delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like the canvas to be delivered.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like the canvas to be delivered.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code for delivery purposes.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you would like the canvas to be delivered.
* **-- Canvas Details --**: This is a reminder that the next section is about canvas details.
* **Canvas Size**: Select the size of the canvas you would like to order.
* **Custom Size (if selected)**: If you selected a custom canvas size, enter the dimensions in inches.
* **Canvas Type**: Select the type of canvas you would like to order.
* **Wrap Style**: Select the style of the canvas wrap.
* **Frame or Wrap Color**: Select the color of the frame or wrap (if applicable).
* **-- Image Details --**: This is a reminder that the next section is about image details.
* **Image Source**: Select where the image or design will come from.
* **Describe Your Image or Design**: Provide a brief description of the image or design.
* **Color Tone Preference**: Select the color tone you prefer for the image or design.
* **Finish Type**: Select the finish type for the canvas.
* **Add Text or Personalization?**: Select if you would like to add text or personalization to the canvas.
* **Text Details (if yes above)**: Enter the text or personalization details (if applicable).
* **-- Delivery Information --**: This is a reminder that the next section is about delivery information.
* **Desired Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you would like to receive the canvas.
* **Preferred Shipping Method**: Select how you would like to receive the canvas.
* **Estimated Order Total**: This field will be calculated and displayed after you select your options.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any additional requests or notes you have.
* **I understand and agree to the pre-order terms**: Select to confirm your agreement to the pre-order terms.
