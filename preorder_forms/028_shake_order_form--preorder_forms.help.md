# Shake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shake Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for shake orders. This form is intended for customers who want to place an order for a shake, and the provided fields help gather necessary details to fulfill their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Shake Order Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Select your preferred shake type and flavor in the respective "Shake Type" and "Shake Flavor" fields.
3.  Choose your preferred size from the "Size" options.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you want to be notified about your order.
5.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field if you want to be contacted for further clarification.
6.  Select the date and time of your desired shake delivery in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7.  You can add any additional comments or instructions in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Shake Type** (`shake_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the shake types you would like to have (e.g., "Yes" for all available options).
* **Size** (`size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the size of your shake from the available options (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Shake Flavor** (`shake_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the flavor of your preferred shake.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number if you need further assistance with your order.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you would like to have your shake delivered.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of your desired shake delivery.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or instructions for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly for timely and proper order fulfillment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us via phone or email.
