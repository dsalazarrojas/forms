# Raub's Aromas Scentsy Holiday Collection 2021 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant for users who wish to place preorders for Raub's Aromas' holiday collection. By filling out the form, users can provide information on their preferences for shipping and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below.

1. Select the appropriate options from the 'Preorder Form' dropdown menu.
2. Choose the shipping option that best suits your needs.
3. If desired, add notes for shipping and customer notes.
4. Enter the email address to which we will send updates.
5. Input your phone number for contact.
6. Write customer and shipping notes as needed.
7. Set the shipping cost and preorder cost.
8. Select the shipping date and preorder date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preorder Form (1)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the options you would like to preorder. Please select all applicable options from the dropdown menu.
* **Shipping (2)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the shipping option that best suits your needs for the holiday collection.
* **Release Dates (3)** (`date`, required/optional): If you have a specific date for release, select it here.
* **Notes (4)** (`note`, required/optional): Write any additional information you feel is necessary for the holiday collection.
* **Email (5)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address to which we will send updates for your order.
* **Phone (6)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Notes (7)** (`note`, required/optional): If you need to add notes for the customer, use this field.
* **Shipping Notes (8)** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional shipping notes as needed.
* **Shipping Cost (9)** (`number`, required/optional): Set the shipping cost for your order.
* **Preorder Cost (10)** (`number`, required/optional): Set the preorder cost for your order.
* **Shipping Date (11)** (`date`, required/optional): Select the shipping date for your order.
* **Preorder Date (12)** (`date`, required/optional): Select the preorder date for your order.
