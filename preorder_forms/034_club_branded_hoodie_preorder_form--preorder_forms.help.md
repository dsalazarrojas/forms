# Club Branded Hoodie Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Branded Hoodie Preorder Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to preorder branded hoodies for a club or organization. This form helps to gather essential details such as customer information, hoodie preferences, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Customer Information" section to enter your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and Student ID (if applicable).
3. Choose your desired hoodie size and color preference.
4. Select your fit style and logo placement.
5. If you want to add custom name or text, select the "Add Custom Name or Text" option and enter your name or message.
6. Determine if you want to add additional personalization to your hoodie.
7. Describe the personalization details if you selected "Additional Personalization".
8. Select the quantity of hoodies you want to preorder and the unit price per hoodie.
9. Calculate your total price.
10. Choose your delivery method.
11. Enter your estimated delivery date.
11. Select your payment status (full payment, deposit, payment plan, or TBD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you want to receive order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student ID - Member ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you have a Student ID, enter it here.
* **Hoodie Size** (`hoodie_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your hoodie (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, XXXL).
* **Color Preference** (`color_choice`, select_one, required): Select the color you prefer for your hoodie (Navy blue, Black, Heather gray, White, Red, School colors).
* **Fit Style** (`fit_style`, select_one, required): Choose your fit style (Unisex, Mens fitted, Womens fitted).
* **Logo Placement** (`logo_placement`, select_one, optional): Select where you want your logo placed (Front chest, Back full size, Sleeve embroidered, Custom placement).
* **Add Custom Name or Text** (`custom_name`, text, optional): If you want to add a custom name or text, enter it here.
* **Name Placement Location** (`name_placement`, select_one, optional): Select where you want your custom name or text placed (Back neck, Back lower, Sleeve).
* **Additional Personalization** (`additional_personalization`, select_one, optional): Choose if you want additional personalization (Yes, No).
* **Personalization Details** (`personalization_details`, text, optional): Describe the personalization you want to add if you selected "Additional Personalization".
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of hoodies you want to preorder.
* **Unit Price - USD** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the price per unit of hoodie.
* **Total Price - USD** (`total_price`, number, optional): Calculate your total price by multiplying the quantity by the unit price.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_option`, select_one, required): Choose your delivery method (Club event pickup, Mailed to address, Campus mailroom, Other location).
* **Estimated Delivery Date** (`estimated_delivery`, date, optional): Enter your estimated delivery date.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select your payment status (Full payment enclosed, Deposit due, Payment plan, TBD).
