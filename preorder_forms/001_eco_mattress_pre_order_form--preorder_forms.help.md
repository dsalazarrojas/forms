# Eco Mattress Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eco Mattress Pre Order Form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in pre-ordering an Eco Mattress. This form will help us understand their preferences and requirements for the product, allowing us to provide a more personalized and efficient pre-order experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product that you are interested in pre-ordering.
2. Provide your delivery details, including the date and time you would like to receive your product.
3. Enter your customer details, including name, address, and contact information.
4. Provide any additional customer notes or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Order Details (Page 1)** (`pre_order_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional details about your pre-order.
* **Select A Product (Page 1)** (`product_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the product you are interested in pre-ordering. Please choose the product that best fits your needs.
* **Delivery Details (Page 1)** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional delivery details, such as special instructions or requests.
* **Customer Details (Page 2)** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide your customer details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Customer Updates (Page 2)** (`customer_updates`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional customer updates or information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your pre-order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your pre-order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for selecting the date you would like to receive your product.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for selecting the time you would like to receive your product.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional customer notes or information.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name for our records.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your address for our records.
* **City** (`customer_city`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the city for your address.
* **State** (`customer_state`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the state for your address.
* **Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the postal code for your address.
* **Country** (`customer_country`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the country for your address.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select the color of the product you are interested in.
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select the size of the product you are interested in.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select the type of product you are interested in.
* **Pre-Order ID** (`pre_order_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is automatically generated and assigned to your pre-order.
* **Customer Update** (`customer_update`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional customer updates or information.
* **Customer Update Status** (`customer_update_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the status of your customer update.
* **Product Status** (`product_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the status of your product.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the status of your delivery.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, `select_one`, required: false): This field indicates whether the form has been completed.
