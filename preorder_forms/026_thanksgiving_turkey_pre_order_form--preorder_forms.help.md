# Thanksgiving Turkey Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers place a pre-order for their Thanksgiving turkey. Please provide your contact information and choose the desired turkey order details to ensure timely delivery and optimal customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Address in the relevant fields.
2. Select a valid Date of Birth if you have one. This information helps us ensure that the ordered turkey is delivered to the correct customer.
3. Enter a valid Contact Number and Email for us to reach you for any further information or confirmation.
4. Choose the quantity of turkeys you would like to order.
5. Select the type of turkey (Standard, Large, or Organic) you would like to purchase.
6. Add any additional comments or special requests for your order.
7. Decide on the status of your order submission (True, False, or Maybe).
8. Finally, set the time you are willing to submit your order for processing.
9. Review your information carefully before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Type in your address where you would like the turkey to be delivered.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you have a Date of Birth, please provide it so we can ensure accurate delivery. If unsure, feel free to leave this blank.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for order confirmation or any further questions.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you via email for order updates.
- **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Please indicate how many turkeys you would like to order.
- **Order Type** (`order_type`, select one, optional): Select the size of the turkey you would like to order (Standard, Large, or Organic).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests or special instructions for your order, please mention them here.
- **Submit Status** (`submit_time`, select multiple, optional): Choose the status of your order submission (True, False, or Maybe).
- **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Please set the time you are willing to submit your order for processing.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date for the order delivery, please provide it.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the turkey pre-order process. Please fill it out accurately and completely to avoid any errors or missed information. If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
