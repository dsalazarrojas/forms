# Wireless Earbud Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wireless Earbud Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order wireless earbuds. This form will help us gather necessary details such as shipping information, payment method, and order dates to ensure a smooth pre-ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order information (Order ID, Order Date, and Pre-Order Date) in the first section.
2. Select your preferred shipping option (Ground Shipping or Air Shipping) and provide your shipping details (Country, State, City, and Postal Code) in the next section.
3. Choose your payment method (Pay on Delivery or Pay on Pickup) and confirm your email address in the following sections.
4. Complete your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Phone, Email, and Address details) in the last section.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the final section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information**
  • **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required: false): This is the unique identifier for your order.
  • **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you would like to place your order.
  • **Pre-Order Date** (`preorder_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you would like to pre-order your wireless earbuds.
* **Shipping Details**
  • **Shipping Options** (`shipping_option`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between Ground Shipping and Air Shipping.
  • **Country** (`ship_to_2`, `text`, required: false): The country you would like to ship to.
  • **State** (`ship_to_3`, `text`, required: false): The state or province you would like to ship to.
  • **City** (`ship_to_4`, `text`, required: false): The city you would like to ship to.
  • **Postal Code** (`ship_to_5`, `text`, required: false): The postal or zip code for your shipping address.
* **Payment Information**
  • **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between Pay on Delivery and Pay on Pickup.
  • **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, required: false): Confirm your email address to ensure we have the correct contact information.
* **Contact Information**
  • **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
  • **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
  • **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
  • **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address Details**
  • **Address 1** (`address_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 1.
  • **Address 2** (`address_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 2.
  • **Address 3** (`address_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 3.
  • **Address 4** (`address_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 4.
  • **Address 5** (`address_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 5.
* **Notes**
  • **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure your pre-order is processed smoothly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Our team will review and contact you within 24 hours to confirm your pre-order details.
