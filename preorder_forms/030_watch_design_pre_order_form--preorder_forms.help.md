# Watch Design Pre-Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Watch Design Pre-Order Form" is a form designed to collect information from customers about their pre-order requests for watch designs. This form helps the business or organization to efficiently manage pre-orders and gather essential details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name.
2. Enter your Customer Email address.
3. Provide your Customer Phone number.
4. Specify the Quantity of the watch design you wish to order.
5. Upload or provide the Watch Design Image.
6. Enter the Watch Price.
7. Enter the Watch Deposit amount.
8. Select the Shipping Date.
9. Choose the Shipping Time.
10. Add any Customer Note (optional).
11. Enter your Shipping Address.
12. Enter the Shipping City.
13. Enter the Shipping Postal Code.
14. Select the Shipping Country.
15. Select the Order Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as the customer placing the pre-order.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for easy communication.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Specify the number of watch designs you wish to pre-order.
- **Watch Design Image** (`watch_design_image`, `text`, optional): Upload or provide a link to the image of the watch design you are pre-ordering.
- **Watch Price** (`watch_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the watch design you are pre-ordering.
- **Watch Deposit** (`watch_deposit`, `number`, required): Enter the deposit amount for the pre-order.
- **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, required): Select the desired shipping date for your pre-order.
- **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, `time`, required): Choose the shipping time for your pre-order.
- **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your pre-order (optional).
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
- **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city for your shipping address.
- **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code for your shipping address.
- **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required): Select the country for your shipping address.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your pre-order from the provided options.
