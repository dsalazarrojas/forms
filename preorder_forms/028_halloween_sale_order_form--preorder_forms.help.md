# Halloween Sale Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Halloween Sale Order Form is intended for customers to provide their personal details for a Halloween-themed sale or promotion. Fill out this form to reserve items or participate in the sale.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXXX for best results.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to place your order on (e.g., October 31st, 2023).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to place your order on (e.g., 10:00 PM).
