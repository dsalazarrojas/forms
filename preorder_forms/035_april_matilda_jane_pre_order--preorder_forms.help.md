# April Matilda Jane Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The April Matilda Jane Pre Order form is designed to collect customer information and order details for customers who wish to place a preorder. This form will help us understand your preferences, including size and color options, to ensure we prepare your item correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number for our records.
3. Enter your shipping address where we can deliver your item.
4. Provide details about the item you wish to preorder, including the item name, size, and quantity.
5. Choose your preferred payment method.
6. Enter the expected ship date.
7. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which we will use for order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for order details or updates.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Item Name**: Enter the name of the item you wish to preorder.
* **Size**: Choose the size of the item from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you wish to preorder.
* **Expected Ship Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order by.
* **Payment Information**: If you have any special payment instructions or notes, enter them here.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order from the dropdown menu.
* **Total Order Amount**: Enter the total amount of your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your shipping address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or notes, include them in the payment information field.
* We strive to ship orders on the expected ship date, but may need to adjust this date based on production and shipping availability.
* We will contact you with any updates or changes to your order.
