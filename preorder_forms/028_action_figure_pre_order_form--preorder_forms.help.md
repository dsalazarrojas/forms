# Action Figure Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Action Figure Pre Order Form is designed for customers to pre-order action figures from various franchises. This form collects information about the action figure to be pre-ordered, including shipping details and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once all required fields are filled in.
4. Our customer service team will review and contact you to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name
**Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required)
Full name of the person placing the pre-order.

### 2. Customer Email
**Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required)
Email address for order confirmation and updates.

### 3. Customer Phone
**Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required)
Phone number for order-related communication.

### 4. Shipping Address
**Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required)
Complete address where the action figure should be shipped.

### 5. Action Figure Name
**Name of Action Figure** (`action_figure_name`, `text`, required)
Official name of the action figure being pre-ordered.

### 6. Character Universe
**Character Universe** (`character_universe`, `select_one`, required)
Franchise or universe the character belongs to (e.g. Marvel, DC, Star Wars, etc.).

### 7. Variant Selection
**Variant Selection** (`variant_selection`, `select_one`, optional)
If applicable, select the specific variant of the figure.

### 8. Quantity Ordered
**Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, `number`, required)
How many of this action figure are you ordering.

### 9. Special Requests or Instructions
**Special Requests or Instructions** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional)
Any special packaging or handling requests.

### 10. Estimated Release Date
**Estimated Release Date** (`estimated_release_date`, `text`, required)
When do you expect the figure to be released.

### 11. Payment Method
**Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required)
How will you pay for the pre-order.

### 12. Estimated Order Total
**Estimated Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required)
Approximate total cost of the pre-order.

### 13. Shipping Preference
**Shipping Preference** (`shipping_preference`, `select_one`, required)
How would you like the item shipped.

## Tips
* Please ensure accuracy and completeness in each field to avoid errors or delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer service team for assistance.
