# Seafood Platter Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order a seafood platter, allowing customers to select their preferred pickup option, date and time, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your pickup option from the dropdown menu. If you choose delivery, please enter your delivery address in the next field.
2.  Choose your preferred date and time for pickup or delivery. If you have no preferences, you can leave these fields blank.
3.  Select your payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
4.  Enter the payment amount. Please note that this field is optional.
5.  Provide any special requests or comments for your order. You can also enter additional special requests if needed.
6.  Enter any specific payment terms and conditions if applicable.
7.  Confirm if you will be using online payment or not.
8.  Finally, review your order details, including pickup options, payment method, and special requests before submitting.
