# health-supplement-launch-pre-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order health supplements. It is part of the pre-ordering process for launching new health supplements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Paid Pre-Orders" to confirm you are ready to complete the form.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select the type of order you want to place.
5. Enter the amount and price of the pre-order.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Enter your shipping address.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paid Pre-Orders** (`pre_order_details`, select_multiple, required): Select "Paid Pre-Order" to confirm you are ready to complete the form.
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for us to reference later.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of order you want to place.
* **Pre-Order Amount** (`preorder_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the health supplement you wish to pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Price** (`preorder_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the health supplement you wish to pre-order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit, Debit, or Cash).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if different from your contact address.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any delays or issues with your pre-order.
* Double-check your payment method and shipping address for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
