# App Update Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect interest and pre-orders for upcoming app updates. It is intended to help developers gauge user interest and gather feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2.  Select the "Platform" field to choose your primary device platform (iOS, Android, or Other).
3.  Answer the "Interest level" field by selecting "Very likely", "Somewhat likely", or "Unlikely" to pre-order.
4.  If you are interested in joining the beta program, select "Yes please" in the "Interest in beta program" field.
5.  In the "Additional feedback or feature requests" field, provide any optional feedback or feature requests you would like to see in the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Pre Order Registration** (`section_preorder`, `note`, false): This is a note field that serves as a section header for the form. It is optional and exists to provide context.
*   **Name** (`interested_user_name`, `text`, false): A field for providing your name, optional for exclusive early access.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, true): Required field for the email address used to notify you when pre-order opens. Please enter a valid email address.
*   **Platform** (`platform`, `select_one`, true): Select your primary device platform (iOS, Android, or Other).
*   **Interest level** (`interest_level`, `select_one`, false): How likely are you to pre-order? Select "Very likely", "Somewhat likely", or "Unlikely".
*   **Interest in beta program** (`beta_program_interest`, `select_one`, false): If you are interested in joining the beta program for early access to testing new features, select "Yes please".
*   **Additional feedback or feature requests** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, false): Provide any optional feedback or feature requests you would like to see in the update.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the email address entered in the "Contact email" field is valid and accessible for notifications.
*   This form is intended for anonymous completion, but you are still required to provide a contact email address.
