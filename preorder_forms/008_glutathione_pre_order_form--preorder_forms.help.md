# Glutathione Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glutathione Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from users before they can place an order for Glutathione. This form is intended to be filled out by individuals who want to purchase Glutathione for personal use or for their business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user details page.
2. Enter your delivery information.
3. Provide product details and any relevant information about your order.
4. Select a payment method and enter the payment amount.
5. Choose a shipping date and time.
6. Confirm your order to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### user_info
 * **user_info**: This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant information about yourself. You can enter your name, contact number, email address, or any other details that you want to provide about yourself.

### delivery_details
 * **delivery_details**: This field is also a text field where you can enter your delivery address and any special instructions for the delivery.

### product_info
 * **product_info**: This field is for you to enter any specific details about the product you are ordering. You can include the product name, quantity, and any other relevant information.

### pre_order
 * **pre_order**: This field is for you to enter any pre-order comments or special requests you may have.

### payment_info
 * **payment_info**: This field is for you to enter any payment information, such as the payment method (e.g. credit card, PayPal) and payment amount.

### review_and_comments
 * **review_and_comments**: This field is for you to enter any review or comments about the product or your order.

### confirmation
 * **confirmation**: This field is for you to confirm that you have reviewed your order and that it is correct.

### payment_method
 * **payment_method**: This field is a select option field where you can choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).

### payment_amount
 * **payment_amount**: This field is a numerical field where you can enter the amount you are willing to pay for the product.

### shipping_date
 * **shipping_date**: This field is for you to select the date you would like to receive your order.

### shipping_time
 * **shipping_time**: This field is for you to select the time you would like to receive your order.

Note: Some fields are not required, but they are still important for us to process your order correctly. Please fill out all the necessary fields to ensure that your order is processed smoothly and efficiently.
