## Children's Book Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to pre-order a series of children's books.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your details.
2. Select the book series you want to pre-order.
3. Choose the shipping method for your order.
4. Fill out any additional information or special requests.
5. If you are pre-ordering as a gift, fill out the gift recipient information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we will send order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address where you would like the order shipped.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like the order shipped.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like the order shipped.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you would like the order shipped.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you would like the order shipped.
* **Which book series are you ordering**: Select the book series you want to pre-order.
* **Number of Sets or Books**: Enter the number of sets or books you want to order.
* **Is this order a gift**: If you are pre-ordering as a gift, select "Yes".
* **Gift Recipient Name**: If pre-ordering as a gift, enter the recipient's name.
* **Gift Recipient Age**: If pre-ordering as a gift, enter the recipient's age.
* **Gift Message**: If pre-ordering as a gift, enter an optional message.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method for your order.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any special requests or notes you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order can be processed correctly.
* If you are pre-ordering as a gift, please make sure to include all required gift information.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
