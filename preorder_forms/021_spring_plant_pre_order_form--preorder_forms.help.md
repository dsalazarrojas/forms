# spring_plant_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to pre-order plants for the spring season. It helps us gather details about the type of plant, delivery date and time, pickup location, and any additional comments to facilitate a smooth and efficient delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of plant you are interested in from the "Category" section.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field if you would like to receive updates.
4. Choose the delivery date and time for your pre-ordered plants using the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
5. Specify the location where you would like to pick up your plants in the "Pickup Location" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Spring Flowers, Summer Blooms, Fall Foliage, Winter Greens)**: Select the type of plant you are interested in.
* **Name**: Fill in your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your pre-order.
* **Phone (optional)**: Provide your phone number to receive updates about your pre-ordered plants.
* **Delivery Date (MM/DD/YYYY)**: Choose the date you would like to receive your pre-ordered plants.
* **Delivery Time (HH:MM:SS)**: Specify the time you would like to receive your pre-ordered plants.
* **Pickup Location**: Indicate the location where you would like to pick up your plants.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or requests you may have about your pre-ordered plants.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are not pre-ordering plants for the same category and date multiple times as it may affect our inventory management and lead to double orders. 
- This form is meant for individual use only, so please do not use it for commercial purposes. 
- If you have any questions or need assistance with your pre-order, contact us at your earliest convenience.
