# Paper Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for paper orders. It is intended to be a simple and user-friendly way to gather essential details about the customer, product, and shipping information. Please complete the form below to place your paper order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by entering your customer's name and any other relevant contact information in the <code>customer_info</code> field.
2. In the <code>product_info</code> field, describe the specific paper product you'd like to order, including any special requests or comments.
3. For the <code>shipping_info</code> field, provide your shipping details, including the address and any specific delivery instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information <code>customer_info</code>** (`Customer Info`, text, Optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Product Information <code>product_info</code>** (`Product Info`, text, Optional): Describe the specific paper product you'd like to order, including any special requests or comments.
* **Shipping Information <code>shipping_info</code>** (`Shipping Info`, text, Optional): Provide your shipping details, including the address and any specific delivery instructions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information for each field to help us process your paper order smoothly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can also modify this form to suit your needs, and we appreciate your feedback for improvement.
