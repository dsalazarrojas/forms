# Villains Merchandise Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Villains Merchandise Pre Order Form is used to place pre-orders for merchandise related to villains.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your name and email address to contact you regarding your order.
2.  Provide your shipping address details, including city, state, and zip code.
3.  Choose the merchandise you would like to pre-order, selecting multiple options if available.
4.  Enter the desired price for your pre-order (this will be confirmed with your payment method).
5.  Select a shipping method and provide any additional shipping notes.
6.  Enter any quantity you would like to pre-order.
7.  Provide any additional notes or comments regarding your order.
8.  Choose a payment method from the available options or enter an alternative method and provide additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or as you would like it to appear on your merchandise.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your order.
*   **Shipping**: Enter the address where you would like your merchandise to be shipped.
*   **Product**: Choose one or more merchandise options to pre-order.
*   **Price**: Enter the price you would like to pay for your pre-order (this will be confirmed with your payment method).
*   **Shipping**: Select a shipping method and provide any additional shipping notes.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of merchandise you would like to pre-order.
*   **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes regarding your order.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by a staff member.
*   **Shipping Notes**: Provide any additional shipping instructions or notes.
*   **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding your order.
*   **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method from the available options or enter an alternative method and provide additional comments.
*   **Other Payment Method**: Enter an alternative payment method and any additional comments.
*   **Other Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding your alternative payment method.
*   **Shipping Address 1**: Enter the first line of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping State**: Choose your shipping state from the available options.
*   **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping Country**: Enter the country of your shipping address.
*   **Shipping Rate**: Enter the shipping rate you would like to use (this may vary based on location).
*   **Shipping Date**: Enter the desired shipping date for your merchandise.
*   **Shipping Time**: Select a shipping time for your merchandise.
*   **Product Description**: Enter a description of the merchandise you are pre-ordering.
*   **Shipping Address 1**: Enter the first line of your shipping address.
*   **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding your order.
