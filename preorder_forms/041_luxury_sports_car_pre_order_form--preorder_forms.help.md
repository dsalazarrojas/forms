# Luxury Sports Car Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers interested in pre-ordering a luxury sports car.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form page by page, starting from the first page labeled "Main Page".
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field as prompted.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contactinfo`, `text`, `required`): This is the section where you provide your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Delivery Info** (`deliveryinfo`, `text`, `required`: This field is not used in this version of the form. However, it's likely intended to gather information about delivery preferences, such as address or location.
* **Car Information** (`carinfo`, `select_one`, `required`): Here, you'll choose whether you want a car or not. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in pre-ordering a luxury sports car.
* **Order Information** (`orderinfo`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Indicate your interest in specific features or options for your pre-ordered car. Please select all relevant options from the list.
* **Payment Information** (`paymentinfo`, `text`, `required`): In this field, you'll provide details about your payment method, such as your payment method or account number.
* **Review** (`reviewinfo`, `note`, `required`): Write a short review of your pre-order, including any special requests or concerns you may have.
* **Contact Us** (`contactusinfo`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your pre-order.
* **Phone** (`phoneinfo`, `text`, `required`): Please provide your phone number in this field.
* **Deposit** (`depositinfo`, `number`, `required`): Enter the amount of deposit you are willing to pay for the pre-order.
* **Confirm** (`confirminfo`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Confirm your pre-order by selecting 'Yes' from the list.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the email address provided.
