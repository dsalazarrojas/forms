# Designer Chair Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a pre-order form for designer chairs. It is intended for customers to provide their preferences and details for a future purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review the options for each field.
2. Select the model of chair you prefer.
3. Enter your delivery address.
4. Specify the desired delivery date.
5. Enter the deposit amount.
6. Choose a desired delivery timeframe.
7. Provide your contact information (name, phone number, and email).
8. Add any additional message you'd like to provide.
9. Select how you would like to receive the chair (e.g., Paid in full, Paid in 30 days, etc.).
10. Choose your payment method (e.g., Credit/Debit, Cash/Cheque, etc.).
11. Provide payment details (e.g., Card Number, Card Expiration, etc.) if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Choose a Model** (`model_selection`, `select_one`): Select the model of chair you prefer from the options provided.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`): Enter your delivery address where you would like to receive the chair.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`): Specify the date when you would like to receive the chair.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, `number`): Enter the deposit amount for the chair.
* **Desired Delivery Timeframe** (`delivery_timeframe`, `time`): Choose the timeframe in which you would like to receive the chair.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Additional Message** (`contact_message`, `note`): Add any additional information about your order.
* **Model Notes** (`model_notes`, `note`): Add any notes about the chair model you selected.
* **Deposit Confirmation** (`deposit_confirmation`, `select_one`): Choose how you would like to confirm your deposit (e.g., Paid in full, Paid in 30 days, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): Choose how you would like to pay for the chair (e.g., Credit/Debit, Cash/Cheque, etc.).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `select_multiple`): Provide any necessary payment details (e.g., Card Number, Card Expiration, etc.).

## Tips

* If you have any issues with your order, please contact us.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Our team will follow up with you regarding your order details.
