# new-series-pre-order-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-order information for a book series, including payment details, delivery options, and author/book metadata. It ensures accurate and efficient processing of customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

1. **Payment Information**: Select the desired payment method from the available options (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
2. **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address for the pre-ordered book series.
3. **Name**: Enter your name.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address for accuracy.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
7. **Book Details**: Enter the title, price, author, and series name of the book.
8. **Book Publisher**: Enter the book publisher.
9. **Delivery Options**: Select the desired delivery option (Standard Mail, Priority Mail, Express Mail).
10. **Delivery Date and Time**: Select the desired date and time for delivery.
11. **Delivery Notes**: Enter any additional delivery notes.
12. **Book Store**: Enter the name of the book store.
13. **Book ID**: Enter the ID of the book.
14. **Book Series Name**: Enter the name of the book series.
15. **Book Publish Date and Time**: Select the desired publish date and time.
16. **Author Details**: Enter the author's name, email, and phone number.
17. **Book Details (continued)**: Enter the book title, price, and series name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Information (id: 7)**: Select the payment method to be used for the pre-order.
* **Name (id: 3)**: Enter your full name for the order.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter a valid email address to confirm your identity.
* **Confirm Email (id: 5)**: Repeat your email address for accuracy.
* **Phone (id: 6)**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Book Title (id: 8)**: Enter the title of the book.
* **Book Price (id: 9)**: Enter the price of the book.
* **Book Author (id: 10)**: Enter the author's name.
* **Quantity (id: 11)**: Enter the quantity of the book to be pre-ordered.
* **Delivery Options (id: 12)**: Select the desired delivery option for the book.
* **Delivery Date (id: 13)**: Select the desired date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time (id: 14)**: Select the desired time for delivery.
* **Delivery Notes (id: 15)**: Enter any additional delivery notes.
* **Book Store (id: 16)**: Enter the name of the book store.
* **Book ID (id: 17)**: Enter the ID of the book.
* **Book Series (id: 18)**: Enter the name of the book series.
* **Book Series Name (id: 19)**: Enter the name of the book series.
* **Book Publish Date (id: 20)**: Select the desired publish date for the book.
* **Book Publish Time (id: 21)**: Select the desired publish time for the book.
* **Author Name (id: 22)**: Enter the name of the author.
* **Author Email (id: 23)**: Enter the author's email address.
* **Author Phone (id: 24)**: Enter the author's phone number.
* **Book Publisher (id: 25)**: Enter the name of the book publisher.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out for accurate processing of your order.
* Double-check the delivery options and date for accuracy.
* Review all entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the book store directly.
