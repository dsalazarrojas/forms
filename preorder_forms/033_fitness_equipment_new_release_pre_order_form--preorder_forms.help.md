# fitness_equipment_new_release_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new fitness equipment pre-orders from customers. It helps us prepare for the release and delivery of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your pre-order.
3. Select whether you are a retail store, a gym, or an online store.
4. Choose the type of equipment you are pre-ordering (e.g., elliptical, treadmill, exercise bike).
5. Select the release date you would like to pre-order the equipment for.
6. Choose the payment method you would like to use for this pre-order.
7. Provide your shipping address and city for delivery of the equipment.
8. Select your state and zip code for the shipping address.
9. Enter your phone number for further contact.
10. Add any additional order notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your pre-order.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are a retail store, a gym, or an online store.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have a store name that you would like to associate with this pre-order.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of equipment you are pre-ordering (e.g., elliptical, treadmill, exercise bike).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, required): Select the release date you would like to pre-order the equipment for.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select the delivery date you would like for the equipment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to pay for this pre-order (e.g., cash, credit card, online payment).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Provide your shipping address to ensure delivery of the equipment.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you want to ship the equipment to.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you want to ship the equipment to.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for the shipping address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your pre-order if necessary.
