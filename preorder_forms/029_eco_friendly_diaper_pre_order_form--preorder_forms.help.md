# eco-friendly-diaper-pre-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-order information from customers, allowing them to reserve eco-friendly diapers in advance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose your delivery preference: Pickup, Delivery, or Shipping.
3. Select the type of eco-friendly diaper you would like to pre-order (Baby or Adult).
4. Specify the quantity of diapers you would like to pre-order.
5. Choose a start and end date for your pre-order.
6. Enter your delivery address.
7. Schedule a delivery date and time.
8. Select a payment method (Card, Pay, or Cash).
9. Enter the payment amount.
10. Enter a payment date.
11. Leave any additional notes for the customer.
12. Enter your store ID.
13. Enter your order ID.
14. Enter the date of your order.
15. Enter the name of the store.
16. Enter the address of the store.
17. Enter the website of the store.
