# MB Smells Good Easter Collection Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order the MB Smells Good Easter Collection. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with the following information:
- Make sure to enter your correct phone number and email address.
- Select the correct collection you are interested in (MB Smells Good Easter Collection, MB Smells Good Easter Basket, or MB Smells Good Gift Set).
- Enter the quantity of the collection you want to pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on the order receipt.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your pre-order.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can send you updates about your pre-order.
- **Collection** (`Collection`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the collection you want to pre-order from the options (MB Smells Good Easter Collection, MB Smells Good Easter Basket, or MB Smells Good Gift Set).
- **Quantity** (`Quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of the collection you want to pre-order.
- **Total** (`Total`, `number`, required: false): This field is not currently used, but you can fill it in if you need to.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special requests or questions, please enter them here.
