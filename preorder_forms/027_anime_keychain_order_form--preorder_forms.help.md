# Anime Keychain Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to order anime-themed keychains. It guides the customer through the process of providing their order details, including shipping address, keychain selection, customization options, and shipping method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the "Customer Information" section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Fill in your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" section, including street address, city, state or province, postal code, and country.
3. Select the keychain series or set in the "Keychain Selection" section.
4. Choose the character you want on your keychain in the "Character Selection" section.
5. Decide on the quantity of keychains you want to order in the "Quantity" section.
6. Select the material type, finish style, and size option for your keychain in the "Material and Style Options" section.
7. If you want to add personalization, such as custom name or text, or engraving, in the "Personalization" section.
8. Choose the gift wrapping option in the "Gift Wrapping" section.
9. Select the shipping method in the "Shipping Method" section.
10. Finally, fill in any special instructions or notes in the "Special Instructions or Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for delivery.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal or Zip Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter your country.
* **Keychain Series or Set** (`keychain_series`, select_one, required): Select the keychain series or set you want.
* **Select Your Character** (`character_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose the character on the keychain from the available options.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Decide on the number of keychains you want to order.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the material type for your keychain, such as Soft Rubber PVC, Hard Plastic Acrylic, or Metal with Enamel.
* **Finish Style** (`finish_style`, select_one, optional): Select the finish style for your keychain, such as Matte, Glossy, or Metallic.
* **Size Option** (`size_option`, select_one, optional): Choose the size option for your keychain, such as Small (1 inch), Medium (1.5 inches), or Large (2 inches).
* **Add Custom Name or Text** (`custom_name`, text, optional): Enter any custom name or text you want on your keychain.
* **Add Engraving** (`engraving_option`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want to add engraving to your keychain.
* **Gift Wrapping Option** (`gift_wrapping`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to add gift wrapping to your order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Select the shipping method, such as Standard Shipping (5-7 days), Express Shipping (2-3 days), or Overnight Shipping.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* Choose your options carefully, as they will affect the appearance and quality of your keychain.
* If you have any special instructions or notes, use the "Special Instructions or Notes" section to communicate them to us.
