# Exclusive Tour Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who wish to pre-order tickets for an exclusive tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you would any other form.
2. Make sure to enter your email address correctly for confirmation and communication purposes.
3. Select the option that best suits your tour preferences.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, use the "Note" field to provide more information.
5. Once you've completed all fields, click "Submit" to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (2): Please enter your last name.
- **Email** (3): This field is used for order confirmation and future communication. Please enter your email address correctly.
- **Phone** (4): Please enter your phone number.
- **Message** (5): If you have any additional comments or information, please enter it here.
- **Date** (6): Please select a date for the tour you are interested in.
- **Time** (7): If the tour is time-specific, please select a time.
- **Select One** (8): Choose one option that best suits your preferences.
- **Select Multiple** (9): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Note** (10): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Email 2** (11): This is a secondary email field for backup contact information.
- **Phone 2** (12): This is a secondary phone number field for backup contact information.
- **Date 2** (13): This is a secondary date field for backup tour preferences.
- **Time 2** (14): This is a secondary time field for backup tour preferences.
- **Select One 2** (15): Choose one additional option for your tour preferences.
- **Select Multiple 2** (16): Choose multiple additional options for your tour preferences.
- **Note 2** (17): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Email 3** (18): This is a tertiary email field for backup contact information.
- **Phone 3** (19): This is a tertiary phone number field for backup contact information.
- **Date 3** (20): This is a tertiary date field for backup tour preferences.
- **Time 3** (21): This is a tertiary time field for backup tour preferences.
- **Select One 3** (22): Choose one more option for your tour preferences.
- **Select Multiple 3** (23): Choose multiple more options for your tour preferences.
- **Note 3** (24): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Submit** (25): Click this button to confirm your order and submit it for processing.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields with an asterisk (\*) are completed.
* Double-check your selections to ensure they match your actual preferences.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us using the contact information provided.
