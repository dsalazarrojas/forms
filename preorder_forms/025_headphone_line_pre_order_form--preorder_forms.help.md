# Headphone Line Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headphone Line Pre Order Form is used to place preorders for products that are not yet available for immediate purchase. This form allows customers to provide their details and specify their preferred shipping and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available preorder statuses in the Pre-Order Status field to ensure the product is available for preorder.
2. Complete the User Details section with your name and other required information.
3. Fill out the Shipping Details and Shipping Contact information sections to ensure accurate delivery.
4. Specify the quantity of products you'd like to preorder.
5. Add any Order Notes or Shipping Notes as necessary.
6. Select your preferred Payment Method.
7. Confirm your Pre-Order Status preference.
8. Add any additional Customer Notes.
9. Add any Product Notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (text, required): Enter your name to be associated with the preorder.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for contact and order updates.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Shipping Details** (text, optional): Enter any details about the shipping, such as special instructions or notes.
* **Shipping Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity responsible for shipping.
* **Shipping Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or entity responsible for shipping.
* **Shipping Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or entity responsible for shipping.
* **Quantity** (number, optional): Specify the number of products you'd like to preorder.
* **Order Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes about your order, such as customization requests.
* **Shipping Notes** (note, optional): Add any shipping notes or special instructions.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
* **Pre-Order Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the preorder status you'd like to associate with your order (Pre-Order Available, Pre-Order Sold Out, Pre-Order Canceled, or Pre-Order Not Available).
* **Customer Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Product Notes** (text, optional): Add any notes about the product you're preordering.
