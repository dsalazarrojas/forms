# Resort Opening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Opening Pre Order Form is a pre-reservation request form designed to help guests provide their preferences and requirements for their upcoming stay at our resort. It helps us to better understand their needs and make their stay more enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your guest information and preferred stay dates.
2. Select your preferred package choice(s) and confirm your selection.
3. Provide any special requests or comments you may have.
4. Read and agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details that you would like us to know about you.
* **Preferred Stay Dates** (Preferred Stay Dates, date, required/optional): Select the dates you would like to stay at our resort. Please note that availability may vary depending on the season and room types.
* **Package Choice** (Package Choice, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the available packages that suits your preferences. Our packages include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3. Please review the package details and choose the one that best fits your needs.
* **Preferred Arrival Date** (Preferred Arrival Date, date, required/optional): Select your preferred arrival date for your stay.
* **Preferred Departure Date** (Preferred Departure Date, date, required/optional): Select your preferred departure date for your stay.
* **Number of Guests** (Number of Guests, number, required): Please enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, required): Share any special requests or requirements you may have for your stay. This will help us to provide you with a more personalized experience.
* **Package Choice 2** (Package Choice 2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional packages that you would like to include in your reservation.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required/optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please share them with us.
* **Package Choice 3** (Package Choice 3, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any other additional packages that you would like to include in your reservation.
* **Confirm Package Choice** (Confirm Package Choice, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your package choice(s) selection.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, note, required): Please read and agree to our terms and conditions to ensure that we can process your pre-reservation request.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button to submit your pre-reservation request.
