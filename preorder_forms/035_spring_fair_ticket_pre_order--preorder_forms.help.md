# Spring Fair Ticket Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for pre-ordering tickets for the Spring Fair. It's designed to collect necessary details about your order, ensuring a smooth and efficient ticketing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review your order details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket Details** (`ticket_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the ticket(s) you would like to order.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the event you're ordering tickets for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event you'll be attending.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the event you'll be attending.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the ticket(s) you're ordering.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of tickets you'd like to order.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication and order confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
* **Ticket Total** (`ticket_total`, number, optional): This field will automatically calculate the total cost of your order based on the ticket price and quantity.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to pay for your tickets: Card, Bank, PayPal, or Cash.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests for your order.

Note: Required fields will be indicated by `*` symbol.
