# Panthers Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for custom-made apparel items from the Panthers Apparel company.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the product you want to order, including its name, category, color, and size.
2. Enter your shipping address and select your preferred shipping method.
3. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Enter the order total, tax, and order status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name (`product_name`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the name of the product you want to order.
* **Product Category (`product_category`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the category of the product you want to order.
* **Product Color (`product_color`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the color of the product you want to order.
* **Product Size (`product_size`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the size of the product you want to order.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false):** Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Information (`shipping_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false):** Select your preferred shipping method. Choose from 1-2 business days, 3-4 business days, 6-7 business days, or 8-10 business days.
* **Shipping Cost (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false):** Enter the cost of shipping.
* **Order Total (`product_total`, `number`, required: false):** Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Tax (`tax`, `number`, required: false):** Enter the tax amount.
* **Notes (`notes`, `note`, required: false):** Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false):** Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Phone (`phone`, `text`, required: false):** Enter your phone number.
* **Email (`email`, `email`, required: false):** Enter your email address.
* **Date (`date`, `date`, required: false):** Enter the date you want to receive the order.
* **Time (`time`, `time`, required: false):** Enter the time you want to receive the order.
* **Order ID (`order_id`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the order ID (if applicable).
* **Quantity (`quantity`, `number`, required: false):** Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Status (`status`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the status of the order (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the tool used to assign the order (if applicable).
* **Category (`category`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the category of the product (if applicable).
* **Description (`description`, `text`, required: false):** Enter a brief description of the product (if applicable).
* **Tags (`tags`, `text`, required: false):** Enter any tags or keywords related to the product (if applicable).
* **Form ID (`form_id`, `text`, required: false):** Enter the form ID (if applicable).

Note: This form is not intended for use by customers who are already logged in, as it does not include authentication or authorization features. It is intended for public use, allowing anyone to place an order for custom-made apparel items from the Panthers Apparel company.
