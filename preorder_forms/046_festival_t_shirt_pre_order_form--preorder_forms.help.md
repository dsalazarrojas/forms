# Festival T Shirt Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival T Shirt Pre Order Form is designed to collect information for pre-ordering festival t-shirts. To use this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by entering the following information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.
* **street** (`address_1`, text, required: false): Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
* **zip** (`address_2`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **city** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city for delivery purposes.
* **state** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state for delivery purposes.
* **country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country for delivery purposes.
* **t_shirt_size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required: false): Select the size of the t-shirt you would like to order: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **t_shirt_color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_one, required: false): Select the color of the t-shirt you would like to order: Red, Blue, or Green.
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select how you would like to pay: Online Payment Gateway or Offline Payment Method.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for follow-up actions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays in delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you need to update your order, please use this form to make changes.
* This form is for new orders only. If you have an existing order, please contact us for updates.
