# Christmas Menu Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Menu Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for a Christmas menu pre-order. This form is used to gather details for reservations and orders, allowing the restaurant or catering service to prepare accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field for order confirmation.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for order inquiries.
4. Select your preferred reservation date in the "Reservation Date" field.
5. Choose your preferred reservation time in the "Reservation Time" field.
6. Select the number of guests for your party in the "Party Size" field.
7. Choose the occasion you are celebrating in the "Occasion" field.
8. Select your seating preference in the "Seating Preference" field.
9. Choose your preferred appetizer in the "Appetizer Pre-Order" field.
10. Select your preferred main course in the "Main Course Selection" field.
11. Choose your preferred side dishes in the "Side Dishes" field (select up to 2 options).
12. Select your preferred dessert in the "Dessert Selection" field.
13. If you have any dietary restrictions, select all that apply in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
14. If you have any allergies, provide details in the "Allergy Notes" field.
15. Select your preferred beverage package in the "Beverage Package" field.
16. If you selected wine, choose your preference in the "Wine Preference" field.
17. If you have any special requests, provide details in the "Special Requests" field.
18. Confirm that you understand the deposit policy in the "Deposit Acknowledgment" field.
19. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy in the "Cancellation Policy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name in this field. This is the name you would like to be referred to by the restaurant or catering service.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field for order confirmation. This will help the restaurant or catering service keep you informed about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in this field for order inquiries. This will help the restaurant or catering service contact you if needed.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Select the date of your desired reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): Select your preferred reservation time.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of guests in your party.
* **Occasion** (`occasion`, select_one, required): Select the occasion you are celebrating.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, required): Select where you would like to sit in the restaurant or at your table.
* **Appetizer Pre-Order** (`appetizer_selection`, select_one, required): Select your preferred appetizer from the list.
* **Main Course Selection** (`main_course_selection`, select_one, required): Select your preferred main course from the list.
* **Side Dishes** (`side_dishes`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 2 side dishes that you would like to have with your meal.
* **Dessert Selection** (`dessert_selection`, select_one, required): Select your preferred dessert from the list.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the dietary restrictions that apply to you.
* **Allergy Notes** (`allergies_notes`, text, required): If you have any allergies, provide details in this field.
* **Beverage Package** (`beverage_package`, select_one, required): Select your preferred beverage package.
* **Wine Preference** (`wine_preference`, select_one, required): If you selected wine, choose your preference.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required): If you have any special requests, provide details in this field.
* **Deposit Acknowledgment** (`deposit_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the deposit policy.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the restaurant or catering service directly.
* Review and understand the deposit and cancellation policies before completing the form.
