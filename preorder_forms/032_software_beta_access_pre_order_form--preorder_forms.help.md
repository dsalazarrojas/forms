# Software Beta Access Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users who wish to participate in the beta testing phase of the software. It collects basic information and confirms the user's agreement to the terms of service, allowing them to gain access to the beta software.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **User Input** field, describing your interest in the beta software.
2.  Enter your **Name** as you would like to be known in the beta testing process.
3.  Provide your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you with updates and access information.
4.  If applicable, enter your **Phone** number for additional contact.
5.  If applicable, enter your **Company** name.
6.  Select one or more **Roles** from the provided options (User, Developer, Designer, Tester, Product Owner, or Other) that best describe your involvement in the beta testing process.
7.  In the **Comment** field, provide any additional information you would like to share about your interest and qualifications.
8.  Finally, select **Agree** to confirm your agreement to the terms of service to access the beta software.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input**: Enter a brief description of your interest in the beta software.
    *   **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be known in the beta testing process.
    *   **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and access information.
    *   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
    *   **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
    *   **Roles**: Select one or more roles from the list (User, Developer, Designer, Tester, Product Owner, or Other) that best describe your involvement.
    *   **Comment**: Provide any additional information you would like to share about your interest and qualifications.
    *   **Agree**: Confirm your agreement to the terms of service to access the beta software.
