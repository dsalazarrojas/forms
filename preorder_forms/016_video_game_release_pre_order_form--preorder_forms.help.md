# Video Game Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Video Game Release Pre Order Form is used for customers to pre-order video games in advance. It collects essential information about the game, the customer, and the shipping details to ensure a smooth and efficient ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information for each field. Please make sure to provide accurate and complete details to avoid any issues during the pre-ordering process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Studio**: Select the studio that developed the game you want to pre-order.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the game is expected to be released.
* **Game Price**: Enter the price of the game you would like to pre-order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Customer Address 1**: Enter the first line of your shipping address.
* **Customer Address 2**: Enter the second line of your shipping address.
* **Customer City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Customer State**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Customer Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Shipping Address 1**: Enter the first line of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address 2**: Enter the second line of your shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: Enter your city of shipping.
* **Shipping State**: Enter your state or province of shipping.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter your shipping postal/zip code.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
