# Handbag Exclusive Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place a pre-order for a limited edition handbag. It collects information about the customer, their shipping details, payment method, and product specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
3. For the shipping details, enter the recipient's first name in the "Shipping First Name" field, last name in the "Shipping Last Name" field, address in the "Shipping Address" field, city in the "Shipping City" field, and post office code in the "Shipping Postal Code" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
5. Enter the name on your payment method in the "Name on Payment Method" field.
6. If you want to pay by credit card, select the type of credit card you have (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) from the "Payment Type" dropdown list.
7. Enter the quantity of the handbag you'd like to order in the "Quantity" field.
8. Enter the product ID in the "Product ID" field.
9. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field (this is not required, but it's good to have for your own reference).
10. Enter the price of the product in the "Product Price" field.
11. If you'd like to reserve the handbag on a specific date, you can select it in the "Preorder Date" field.
12. You can also select a shipping date if you'd like to ship the handbag on a different date.
13. If you'd like to add an image of the handbag to your order, select it from the "Product Image" dropdown list.
14. Choose the color of the handbag you'd like to order from the "Product Color" dropdown list.
15. You can add any shipping notes in the "Shipping Note" field.
16. Add any payment notes in the "Payment Note" field.
17. Enter any order notes in the "Order Notes" field. This is a required field.
18. Confirm that you have read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" field.
19. Finally, confirm your order by selecting "True" in the "Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication related to your order.
* **Shipping First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the recipient's first name here.
* **Shipping Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the recipient's last name here.
* **Shipping Address** (Text, Required): Enter the shipping address here.
* **Shipping City** (Text, Required): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Province/State** (Text, Optional): Enter the province or state for the shipping address (if applicable).
* **Shipping Postal Code** (Text, Required): Enter the post office code for the shipping address.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, Money Order, Wire Transfer).
* **Name on Payment Method** (Text, Required): Enter the name on your payment method (e.g. your name, company name, etc.).
* **Payment Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of credit card you have (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Check, Wire Transfer).
* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of handbags you'd like to order.
* **Product ID** (Text, Required): Enter the product ID here.
* **Product Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the product name here (this is not required, but it's good to have for your own reference).
* **Product Price** (Number, Required): Enter the price of the product here.
* **Preorder Date** (Date, Optional): If you'd like to reserve the handbag on a specific date, select it here.
* **Shipping Date** (Date, Optional): If you'd like to ship the handbag on a different date than the preorder date, select it here.
* **Product Image** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more images of the product to add to your order.
* **Product Color** (Select One, Optional): Choose the color of the handbag you'd like to order.
* **Shipping Note** (Note, Optional): Add any shipping notes here.
* **Payment Note** (Note, Optional): Add any payment notes here.
* **Order Notes** (Text, Required): Enter any notes about your order here.
* **Confirmation** (Select One, Required): Confirm your order by selecting "True" here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Text, Optional): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of your order.
* **Custom** (Text, Optional): Add any custom notes here.
