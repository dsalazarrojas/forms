# Color Street SPRING 2021 PRE ORDER - Stylish Nails With Nicole - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order the "Stylish Nails With Nicole" collection for the spring of 2021. It is used to collect customer information, nail set selections, and shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer information section with your name, email, and mailing address.
2. Select the 2021 Spring Solids, 2021 Spring Glitters, and 2021 Spring Designs you would like to preorder.
3. Choose how you will pay for your order (Venmo, PayPal, or Zelle).
4. Enter your payment username.
5. Select your shipping method (Standard, Priority, or Pickup).
6. If applicable, enter the hostess's name for any hostess gifts.
7. Optionally, include any additional notes for Nicole.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`nicole_customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`nicole_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and order confirmation.
* **Mailing Address** (`nicole_address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address for shipping purposes.
* **2021 Spring Solids** (`nicole_spring_solids`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 solids from the 2021 Spring collection.
* **2021 Spring Glitters** (`nicole_spring_glitters`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 glitters from the 2021 Spring collection.
* **2021 Spring Designs** (`nicole_spring_designs`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 designs from the 2021 Spring collection.
* **Are you doing a B3G1 (Buy 3 Get 1 Free) order?** (`nicole_b3g1_status`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you are doing a B3G1 order, otherwise choose "False".
* **Total Number of Sets** (`nicole_total_sets`, number, required): Enter the total number of sets you are preordering.
* **How will you pay?** (`nicole_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for your order (Venmo, PayPal, or Zelle).
* **Payment Username** (`nicole_payment_handle`, text, required): Enter your payment username (e.g., your PayPal email or Venmo ID).
* **Shipping Method** (`nicole_shipping_preference`, select_one, required): Select your shipping method (Standard, Priority, or Pickup).
* **Hostess Name (If applicable)** (`nicole_hostess`, text, optional): Enter the hostess's name for any hostess gifts.
* **Anything else for Nicole?** (`nicole_notes`, text, optional): Include any additional comments or notes for Nicole.
