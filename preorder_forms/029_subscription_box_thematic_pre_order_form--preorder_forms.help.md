# Subscription Box Thematic Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for a subscription box thematic pre-order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Form Title field with your desired form title.
2. Select your preferred Subscription Type: Monthly, One-time, or Other.
3. Choose one or multiple themes that interest you.
4. Enter your Product Name for the subscription.
5. Enter your Customer Name.
6. Enter your Customer Email address.
7. Enter your Customer Phone number.
8. Provide any additional Order Notes about your pre-order.
9. Choose your payment method: Credit Card or PayPal.
10. Select your Card Number, Card Expiry date, and Card CVV.
11. Click Submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title (1)**: Enter a title for your pre-order.
- **Subscription Type (2)**: Select your preferred subscription type: Monthly, One-time, or Other.
  *Note: If you select "Other", please provide further details in the Order Notes field.*
- **Theme (3)**: Choose one or multiple themes that interest you.
- **Product Name (4)**: Enter the name of the product you're pre-ordering.
- **Customer Name (5)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Customer Email (6)**: Enter your valid email address.
- **Customer Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number.
- **Order Notes (8)**: Provide any additional information about your pre-order.
- **Card Type (9)**: Choose your payment method: Credit Card or PayPal.
- **Card Number (10)**: Enter your credit card number without any additional characters.
  *Note: Please ensure your card number is entered correctly to avoid delays in processing your order.*
- **Card Expiry (11)**: Select your card expiry date in MM/YY format (e.g., 12/2024).
- **Card CVV (12)**: Enter your card security code (CVV) without any additional characters.
  *Note: Please ensure your CVV is entered correctly to avoid delays in processing your order.*
