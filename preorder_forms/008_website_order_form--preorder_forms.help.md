# website_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new preorder on the website, which will help us better understand your business and website requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your domain name in the "Enter Domain Name" field.
* Fill in your business name in the "Enter Business Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Enter Email" field.
* Fill in your phone number in the "Enter Phone" field.
* Enter your website in the "Enter Website" field.
* (Optional) Select your business type in the "Select Business Type" field.
* (Optional) Choose domain suggestions in one of the two "Get Domain Suggestions" fields.
* (Optional) Describe the purpose of your website in the "What is the purpose of your website?" field.
* (Optional) Describe your business needs in the "What are your business needs?" field.
* (Optional) Describe your target audience in the "Describe Your Target Audience" field.
* (Optional) Select your target audience in the "Select Target Audience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enter Domain Name (`domain_name`, text, required)
 Enter your domain name in this field.
### Enter Business Name (`business_name`, text, required)
 Enter your business name in this field.
### Enter Email (`email`, email, required)
 Enter your email address in this field.
### Enter Phone (`phone`, text, required)
 Enter your phone number in this field.
### Enter Website (`website`, text, required)
 Enter your website in this field.
### Select Business Type (`business_type`, select_multiple, optional)
 Select one or more business types that apply to your business.
### Get Domain Suggestions (`domain_suggestions`, select_one, optional)
 Select a domain name suggestion from the options provided.
### What is the purpose of your website? (`website_requirements`, text, optional)
 Describe the purpose of your website.
### What are your business needs? (`business_requirements`, text, optional)
 Describe the business needs of your website.
### Describe Your Target Audience (`target_audience`, text, optional)
 Describe the target audience for your business.
### Select Target Audience (`target_audience`, select_multiple, optional)
 Select the target audience for your business.
