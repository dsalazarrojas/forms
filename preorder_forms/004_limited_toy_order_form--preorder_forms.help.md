# limited_toy_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The limited_toy_order_form is designed to collect information from users who want to pre-order limited edition toys. Fill out this form to provide your details and preferences, and we'll get in touch with you to confirm your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the first page, which includes a text field for your name and contact information.
2. In the second page, select your preferred option from the list of choices.
3. On the third page, provide additional details such as the number of items you'd like to order.
4. On the fourth page, select multiple options for your order.
5. On the fifth page, select a date for your order.
6. On the sixth page, select a time for your order.
7. On the seventh page, leave a note for our team if you have any specific requests or questions.
8. On the eighth page, provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
9. On the ninth page, fill in any additional text information you'd like to include.
10. On the tenth page, leave any additional notes for our team.
11. On the eleventh page, select your preferred option from the list.
12. On the twelfth page, select the number of items you'd like to order.
13. On the thirteenth page, select multiple options for your order.
14. On the fourteenth page, select a date for your order.
15. On the fifteenth page, fill out this form to complete your order.
