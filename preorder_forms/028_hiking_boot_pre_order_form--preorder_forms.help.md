# Hiking Boot Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hiking Boot Pre Order Form is designed to collect essential information from customers who want to pre-order hiking boots. This form helps to gather details such as shipping information, payment methods, and order comments to ensure that the order is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Select the relevant options for shipping information, payment methods, and order comments.
3. Enter your email address and phone number accurately.
4. Review your order details and make any necessary changes.
5. Submit the form to proceed with your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate shipping option for your order. This will help us process your order correctly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more payment methods for your order. You can select multiple payment methods if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth accurately to ensure that we can verify your identity.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): If you have any specific comments or requests regarding your order, enter them here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure that we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number accurately to ensure that we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Email Input** (`email_input`, email, required): Enter your email address accurately to receive order updates and notifications.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests regarding your order, select them here.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address to ensure that we can process your payment correctly.
* **Order Submitted** (`order_submitted`, time, required): Select the time you submit your order to ensure that we can process it in a timely manner.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your order to help us track it efficiently.
* **Payment Method Two** (`payment_method_two`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more payment methods for your order, if applicable.
* **Shipping Option** (`shipping_option`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred shipping option for your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, text, optional): If you have any specific questions or requests regarding your order total, enter them here.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter your shipping city to ensure that we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter your shipping state to ensure that we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter your shipping zip code to ensure that we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, text, optional): Enter your billing city to ensure that we can process your payment correctly.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, text, optional): Enter your billing state to ensure that we can process your payment correctly.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, text, optional): Enter your billing zip code to ensure that we can process your payment correctly.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests regarding your order, enter them here.
* **Order Terms** (`order_terms`, select_one, required): Select the terms you agree to for your order.
* **Order Status Two** (`order_status_two`, select_one, required): Select the status of your order to help us track it efficiently.
* **Order Comments Two** (`order_comments_two`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests regarding your order, enter them here.
