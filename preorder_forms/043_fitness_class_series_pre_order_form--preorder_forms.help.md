# Fitness Class Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from students pre-registering for a series of fitness classes. The information collected will be used to create a schedule and send reminders to the students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your title and first name on the "Title and First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name on the "Title and Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address on the "Participant 1's Email" field.
4. Choose your schedule preferences for the first and second session from the available options on the "Schedule Preferences 1" and "Schedule Preferences 2" fields.
5. Fill in your payment details on the "Payment Details" field.
6. Add any additional notes, if needed, on the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title and First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Title and Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Participant 1's Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Schedule Preferences 1** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred schedule for the first session.
* **Schedule Preferences 2** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred schedule for the second session.
* **Payment Details** (Text, Required): Enter your payment details (e.g., credit card information).
* **Additional Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with the instructor.

## Tips

- Please make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Choose your schedule preferences carefully, as they will be used to schedule your classes.
- If you have any issues with your payment, please contact the instructor.
