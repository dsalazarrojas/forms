# nostalgia_merchandise_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place pre-orders for merchandise items. It collects information about the customer and their order details, including their name, email address, product name and description, pre-order cost, shipping cost, shipping details, and shipping date. This form helps us to efficiently process and fulfill customer orders while ensuring accurate and timely shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name**, including your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used for communication about your order.
3. Enter the **Product Name**, which is the name of the item you wish to pre-order.
4. Enter a brief description of the **Product Description**, which will help us identify the product you are ordering.
5. Enter the **Pre-Order Cost**, which is the amount you are willing to pay for the product.
6. Enter the **Pre-Order Quantity**, if applicable, to specify how many items you wish to order. This field is optional.
7. Enter the **Shipping Cost**, which will be added to your total cost.
8. Enter any additional **Message** you would like to include with your order, such as any special instructions or requests.
9. If applicable, upload an image of the **Product Image** to help us identify the product.
10. Enter your **Phone** number, which will help us contact you in case of any issues.
11. Enter your **Address**, including your street address and city, which will be used for shipping.
12. Enter the **Shipping Details**, which should include any specific shipping instructions or requests.
13. Choose a **Shipping Date** when you would like your order to be shipped.
14. Finally, enter your **Customer Shipping**, which should include any specific time of day when you would like your item shipped.
