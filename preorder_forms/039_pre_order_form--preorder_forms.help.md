# Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Order Form is a tool to capture customer's information and preferences for pre-ordering products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email` field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Add any special instructions or comments in the `Preorder Message` field.
6. Select the date and time you would like to pre-order the product in the `Preorder Date` and `Preorder Time` fields.
7. Enter the name of the product you would like to pre-order in the `Product Name` field.
8. Add a brief description of the product in the `Product Description` field.
9. Enter a unique order ID (if you have one) in the `Order ID` field.
10. Add any additional notes about your pre-order in the `Preorder Note` field.
11. Finally, ensure you provide the correct date and time for the pre-order in the `Date` and `Time` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, false): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, false): Enter your email address. Ensure it is valid and up-to-date.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, false): Enter your phone number.
- **Preorder Message** (`preorder_message`, `text`, false): Add any special instructions or comments about your pre-order.
- **Preorder Date** (`preorder_date`, `date`, false): Select the date you would like to pre-order the product.
- **Preorder Time** (`preorder_time`, `time`, false): Select the time you would like to pre-order the product.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, false): Enter the name of the product you would like to pre-order.
- **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, false): Add a brief description of the product.
- **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, false): Enter a unique order ID (if you have one).
- **Preorder Note** (`preorder_note`, `note`, false): Add any additional notes about your pre-order.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, false): Select the date of the pre-order.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, false): Select the time of the pre-order.

This help guide provides a step-by-step explanation of each field to help you complete the Pre Order Form. Please refer to it if you have any doubts or confusion.
