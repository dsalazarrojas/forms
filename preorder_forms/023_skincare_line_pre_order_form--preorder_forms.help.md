# Skincare Line Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer details and product preferences for pre-ordering purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details in the "Customer Details" page.
2. Provide the product details you wish to pre-order on the "Product Details" page.
3. In the "Pre-Order" page, confirm your pre-order request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details, Text, Required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information you'd like to share.
* **Product Details** (Product Details, Text, Required): List the product(s) you're interested in pre-ordering, including any specific variations or quantities.
* **Pre-Order** (Pre-Order, Text, Required): Confirm that you're placing a pre-order request for the selected products.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Ensure you're not duplicating an existing order or pre-order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
