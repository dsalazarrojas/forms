# fundraising_cookbook_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order a cookbook. It will help us gauge the demand and interest for the cookbook before its official release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name
2. Fill in your last name
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your phone number
5. Specify the quantity of cookbooks you would like to pre-order
6. Enter the amount you would like to pay for the pre-order
7. Choose a date and time for your order
8. Add any comments about your order
9. Indicate if you have any interest in supporting the cookbook

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding your pre-order.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number where you can be reached, enter it here.
* **Quantity** (number, required: false): Specify the number of cookbooks you would like to pre-order.
* **Amount** (number, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to pay for the pre-order (note: this will be used for payment processing).
* **Order Date** (date, required: false): Choose the date you would like to order your cookbook (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Order Time** (time, required: false): Choose the time of day you would like to order your cookbook (format: 24-hour clock: 00:00-23:59).
* **Order Comments** (note, required: false): Add any comments or notes about your order, such as any special requests or questions.
* **Supporter Interest** (select multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any interest in supporting the cookbook.
