# Child Warmer Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a limited quantity of a child warmer product with your preferred features.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the model you wish to reserve from the "Child Warmer Model" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the quantity of warmers you wish to preorder, up to 5 units.
3.  Choose your preferred color for the warmer.
4.  Enter any custom engraving text (up to 15 characters) if desired.
5.  Enter your full name as you want it to appear on the order.
6.  Enter your email address for preorder confirmation and updates.
7.  Enter your phone number for shipping updates.
8.  Enter your delivery address, including street, city, state, and zip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child Warmer Model** (`warmer_model`, select_one, required): Select the model you wish to reserve. This will determine the style and features of the warmer.
*   **Quantity to Preorder** (`preorder_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of warmers you wish to reserve, up to 5 units.
*   **Color Choice** (`color_choice`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred color for the warmer.
*   **Custom Engraving - if any** (`custom_engraving_text`, text, optional): Enter any custom engraving text you wish to have on your warmer. Please keep it brief (up to 15 characters).
*   **Customer Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you want it to appear on the order.
*   **Customer Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for preorder confirmation and updates.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for shipping updates.
*   **Delivery Address for Preorder Shipment** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your delivery address, including street, city, state, and zip.

## Tips

*   Please review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.
*   Only a limited quantity of each model is available for preorder; please select the desired model carefully.
*   Ensure you have entered your correct email address for preorder confirmation and updates.
*   Enter a valid phone number for shipping updates.
*   Double-check your delivery address to ensure accurate shipping.
