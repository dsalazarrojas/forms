# Adventures Hiking Trip Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order their hiking trip, providing required information for the trip planning process. It is used to gather details about the customer's group, their preferred hiking trail, trip duration, difficulty level, and other relevant information to ensure a successful and enjoyable trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as requested.
3. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (`customer_full_name`, text, required)**: Please enter your legal full name as the primary customer.
* **Email Address (`email_address`, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address for booking confirmation.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Group Size (`group_size`, number, required)**: Specify the number of people in your hiking group.
* **Trail Name (`trail_name`, select_one, required)**: Select the hiking trail you are interested in.
* **Planned Trip Date (`trip_date`, date, required)**: Enter the date you plan to take this hiking trip.
* **Trip Duration (`trip_duration`, select_one, required)**: Choose the duration of your hiking trip (e.g., day hike, weekend trip, etc.).
* **Difficulty Level (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required)**: Select the difficulty level you are looking for (e.g., easy, moderate, etc.).
* **Group Members Ages (`group_members_ages`, text, required)**: Enter the ages of all group members separated by commas.
* **Group Experience Level (`group_experience_level`, select_one, required)**: Select the overall experience level of your group.
* **Special Requirements (`special_requirements`, text, optional)**: If you have any special requirements or needs, please list them.
* **Equipment Rental Needed (`equipment_rental_needed`, select_multiple, required)**: Select the type of equipment you need to rent.
* **Guide Services (`guide_services`, select_one, required)**: Decide if you want to hire a professional hiking guide.
* **Estimated Budget (`estimated_budget`, number, required)**: Enter your estimated budget for this hiking trip.
* **Pre-Order Commitment (`pre_order_commitment`, select_one, required)**: Confirm that you are interested in pre-ordering this trip and will follow up for booking confirmation.
