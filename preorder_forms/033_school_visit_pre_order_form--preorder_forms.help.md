# School Visit Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from schools to plan and facilitate visits to educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for student attendance.
3.  Enter the number of people attending.
4.  Choose a date for the visit.
5.  Select the time of the visit.
6.  Choose "Yes" or "No" for students attending with a chaperone.
7.  Enter the contact method.
8.  Select the grade level of the students.
9.  (Optional) Describe any medical conditions that may impact the student's ability to participate.
10. (Optional) Provide any additional information.
11. (Optional) Enter the student's birthday.
12. (Optional) Describe any special accommodations needed for the student.
13. Confirm the submission by selecting "Yes" or "No" at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Attendance**: This field asks if students are expected to attend the visit. Choose "Yes" if students will participate, and "No" if they will not.
*   **Number of People Attending**: Enter the total number of people planning to attend the visit.
*   **Date of Visit**: Select the date for the visit in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Time of Visit**: Choose the time for the visit in HH:MM format.
*   **Students Attending with Chaperone**: This field asks if students will attend with a chaperone. Select "Yes" if they will have a chaperone, and "No" otherwise.
*   **Contact Method**: Enter the method of contact, such as phone number or email.
*   **Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the students attending the visit.
*   **Medical Conditions**: (Optional) Describe any medical conditions that may impact the student's ability to participate in the visit.
*   **Additional Information**: (Optional) Provide any other relevant information about the visit.
*   **Student's Birthday**: (Optional) Enter the student's birthday in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Special Accommodations**: (Optional) Describe any special accommodations needed for the student.
*   **Confirm**: Confirm that the submitted information is accurate by selecting "Yes".
