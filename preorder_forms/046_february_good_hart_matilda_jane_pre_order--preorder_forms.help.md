# february_good_hart_matilda_jane_pre_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a pre-order process, likely for a product launch or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field. This field requires a valid email format (e.g., user@example.com).
4. Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "confirm_email" field.
5. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping_address" field.
6. Enter your shipping city in the "shipping_city" field.
7. Enter your shipping state in the "shipping_state" field.
8. Enter your shipping zip code in the "shipping_zip" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "shipping_country" field, depending on whether you're shipping to a country.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "order_comments" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "preorder_type" field, depending on the preorder type you are interested in.
12. Enter the quantity of pre-orders you would like to place in the "preorder_quantity" field.
13. Enter the price of your pre-order in the "preorder_price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **last_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
- **confirm_email** (select_one, required/optional): Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **shipping_address** (text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address.
- **shipping_city** (text, required/optional): Enter your shipping city.
- **shipping_state** (text, required/optional): Enter your shipping state.
- **shipping_zip** (text, required/optional): Enter your shipping zip code.
- **shipping_country** (select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you're shipping to a country.
- **order_comments** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **preorder_type** (select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" depending on the preorder type you are interested in.
- **preorder_quantity** (number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of pre-orders you would like to place.
- **preorder_price** (number, required/optional): Enter the price of your pre-order.
