# february_matilda_jane_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to pre-order products from an online store. It collects essential details such as order details, shipping information, and any additional notes or messages. This form helps ensure that the customer's order is accurate and complete before processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Provide order details**: Enter a brief description of the products or items you wish to pre-order.
2. **Enter pre-order date**: Select the date you would like to pre-order or receive your order by.
3. **Add shipping information**: Provide your shipping address, city, state, zip, phone number, and email address.
4. **Add any additional pre-order message**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your pre-order.
5. **Select pre-order confirmation**: Choose one of the available pre-order confirmation options to acknowledge your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the products or items you wish to pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Date** (`preorder_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to pre-order or receive your order by. This will help us prepare for your delivery accordingly.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address where you want your order delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for delivery updates.
* **Shipping Email** (`shipping_email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can receive updates and notifications about your order.
* **Pre-Order Message** (`preorder_message`, text, required/optional): Enter any special requests or instructions for your pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Message 2** (`preorder_message_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments for your pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Note** (`preorder_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments for your pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Confirmation** (`preorder_confirmation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the available pre-order confirmation options to acknowledge your order.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy, especially shipping details, to avoid any delivery issues. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [your email or contact information].
