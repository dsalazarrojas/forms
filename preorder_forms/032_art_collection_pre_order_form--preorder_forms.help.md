# Art Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Collection Pre Order Form is designed for customers to pre-order art pieces before they are available for regular sale. This allows customers to reserve their desired pieces and ensures timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the artwork you wish to pre-order, including the title, medium, and dimensions.
3. Choose your preferred payment method and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address where the artwork will be shipped.
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Select the title of the artwork you wish to pre-order.
* **Artwork ID or Reference Number** (`artwork_id`, text, optional): If available, enter the artwork ID or reference number.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Medium or Material** (`artwork_medium`, text, optional): Enter the medium or material used to create the artwork (e.g. canvas, metal, wood).
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`artwork_dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the width, height, and depth of the artwork if applicable.
* **Edition Number or Print Number** (`edition_number`, text, optional): If limited edition, enter the edition number or print number.
* **Quantity to Pre-Order** (`preorder_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of artworks you wish to pre-order.
* **Edition or Size Availability** (`available_edition`, select_one, required): Choose the edition or size availability of the artwork.
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery`, date, optional): Enter the expected delivery date of the artwork.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the artwork per unit.
* **Total Pre-Order Price** (`total_price`, number, required): The total price will be calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Deposit Required** (`deposit_percentage`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the deposit percentage or amount required.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g. credit card, debit card, bank transfer).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment schedule (e.g. full payment now, deposit now - balance on delivery).
* **Framing or Mounting Request** (`framing_request`, text, optional): Enter any framing or mounting requests.
* **Special Handling or Delivery Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions.

Please note that this form requires a valid email address and phone number to ensure timely communication and delivery.
