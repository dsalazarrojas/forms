# Sneaker Limited Edition Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sneaker Limited Edition Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers for a limited edition sneaker preorder. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure the preorder process is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Customer Details**: Enter your name and email address in the Customer Details section.
2. **Shipping Address**: Fill in your shipping address in the Shipping Address section. This information will be used for delivery purposes.
3. **Delivery Options**: Choose your preferred delivery method in the Delivery Options section.
4. **Payment Details**: Enter your payment method (Cash, Credit/Debit, or Other) and any additional payment details in the Payment Details section.
5. **Order Details**: Select the product color, size, and quantity you prefer in the Order Details section.
6. **Order Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order in the Order Comments section.
7. **Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method (Yes or No) in the Shipping Method section.
8. **Shipping Option**: Select any additional shipping options you require (Yes or No) in the Shipping Option section.
9. **Product Color**: Select the product color you prefer in the Product Color section.
10. **Product Size**: Select the product size you prefer in the Product Size section.
11. **Product Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order in the Product Quantity section.
12. **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product you are willing to pay in the Product Price section.
13. **Order Status**: Select the status of your order (Active or Inactive) in the Order Status section.
14. **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (Active or Inactive) in the Payment Status section.
15. **Customer Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order in the Customer Comments section.
16. **Customer Signature**: Sign your name in the Customer Signature section.
17. **Customer Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign the form in the Customer Signature Date section.
18. **Customer Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign the form in the Customer Signature Time section.
19. **Other Payment Method**: Enter any other payment method you would like to use in the Other Payment Method section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (1)**: Enter your name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Shipping Address (2)**: Fill in your shipping address so we can deliver your sneaker to the correct address.
* **Delivery Options (3)**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to receive updates about your order status or 'No' if you prefer not to receive any updates.
* **Payment Details (4)**: Choose your preferred payment method and provide any additional details.
* **Billing Address (5)**: Although not required, fill in your billing address if it's different from your shipping address.
* **Payment Method (6)**: Choose from Cash, Credit/Debit, or Other payment methods.
* **Order Date (7)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Date (8)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Time (9)**: Enter the time you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Order Comments (10)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Method (13)**: Choose 'Yes' to receive shipping updates or 'No' if you don't want any updates.
* **Shipping Option (14)**: Select any additional shipping options you require.
* **Product Color (15)**: Select the product color you prefer from the options provided.
* **Product Size (16)**: Select the product size you prefer from the options provided.
* **Product Quantity (17)**: Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Product Price (18)**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
* **Order Status (19)**: Select the status of your order (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Status (20)**: Select the status of your payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Comments (21)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Signature (22)**: Sign your name in this section.
* **Customer Signature Date (23)**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Customer Signature Time (24)**: Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Other Payment Method (25)**: Enter any other payment method you would like to use.
