# Fall Launch Nails Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for Fall-themed nails, likely for events or promotions related to the season. The information collected will be used to fulfill customer orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Specify the product you'd like to order under "Product Name".
4. Indicate the quantity of the product you'd like to order.
5. Enter the date and time you'd like to have the order ready.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your address for delivery or pickup purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documents.
- **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
- **Product Name** (Product Name, text, required): Specify the type of nail design or style you'd like to order (e.g. "Fall-themed Nail Set", "Nail Art Kit", etc.).
- **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Indicate how many sets or kits you'd like to order.
- **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Choose the date you'd like to have the order ready for pickup or delivery.
- **Order Time** (Order Time, time, required): Select the time of day you'd like to have the order ready for pickup or delivery.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you regarding your order.
- **Address** (Address, text, required): Provide your address for delivery or pickup purposes.
- **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or notes, please include them here.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team.
- We'll review your order and contact you to confirm the details.
