# pre_order_the_new_furniture_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre_order_the_new_furniture_collection" form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the new furniture collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer details section with your information.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the customer phone field.
4. Select the product you wish to pre-order from the product selection dropdown menu.
5. Choose the date and time for your order.
6. Enter your address in the address field.
7. Add any additional order notes in the order notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false)
*   A field to enter your name and any other relevant customer details.

### 2. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false)
*   Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.

### 3. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false)
*   Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### 4. **Product Selection** (`product_selection`, select_one, required: false)
*   Select the product you wish to pre-order from the available options: chair, table, or sofa.

### 5. **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false)
*   Choose the date you would like to receive your pre-ordered item.

### 6. **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false)
*   Select the time you would like to receive your pre-ordered item.

### 7. **Address** (`address`, text, required: false)
*   Enter your address where the item will be delivered.

### 8. **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false)
*   Add any additional information about your order that we should know.

### 9. **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required: false)
*   Add any additional information about yourself that we should know.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
*   Review your entries before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
