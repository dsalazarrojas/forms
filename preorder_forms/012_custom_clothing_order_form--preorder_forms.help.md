# Custom Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order custom clothing items. Please fill in the required information to help us process your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide a detailed description of the custom item you wish to order, including its name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Custom item name**: Provide a detailed description of the custom item you wish to order, including its name. For example, "T-shirt with red sleeves and custom text". This will help us understand what you need and process your order accurately.
