# Luxury Handbag Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Luxury Handbag Pre Order Form" is a simple, multi-page form designed to collect essential information from customers who want to pre-order luxury handbags. This information will help us process their order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Customer Info" tab to fill out your personal details.
2. On the "Delivery Details" tab, provide the address where you would like your handbag delivered.
3. On the "Payment Info" tab, enter your payment method and details.
4. In the "Deposit Amount" section, enter the amount you are willing to pay as a deposit for your pre-ordered handbag.
5. On the "Shipping Details" tab, provide additional information about your delivery preferences.
6. In the "Order Notes" section, add any special instructions or requests for your pre-ordered handbag.
7. Finally, enter your "Email" address and "Phone" number for our records and communication purposes.
8. Once you have filled out all the tabs, review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required): This is the first section of the form where you will enter your personal details. Please fill out your name, address, and any other relevant information to help us process your order.
* **Delivery Details** (Delivery Details, text, required): In this section, provide the address where you would like your handbag delivered. Include any specific instructions or requests for delivery.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, required): Here, you will enter your payment method and details. This will help us process your deposit.
* **Deposit Amount** (Deposit Amount, number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay as a deposit for your pre-ordered handbag. Please note that this amount is non-refundable.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Details, text, required): In this section, provide any additional information about your delivery preferences, such as a special delivery date or time.
* **Order Notes** (Order Notes, note, required): Add any special instructions or requests for your pre-ordered handbag here. This is a great place to let us know if you have any specific requests or preferences.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): If you would like to receive updates and notifications about your pre-ordered handbag, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): This is your phone number, which we will use for communication and delivery purposes. Please make sure to enter your correct phone number.

## Tips
- Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will contact you with any updates or changes to your order.
- The deposit amount is non-refundable, and payment will be required in full upon delivery.
