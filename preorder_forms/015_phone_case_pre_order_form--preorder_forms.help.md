# Phone Case Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Phone Case Pre Order Form" is a crucial tool for our business to collect and process customer orders for phone cases. This form helps us gather essential details about the customer and their order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Choose the desired case size (xs, sm, lg).
5. Select the case color (blue, red, black).
6. Select the desired case design (design1, design2, design3).
7. Choose a payment method (credit, debit, paypall).
8. (Optional) Provide any additional notes or messages.
9. Click submit to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with updates.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Case Size** (`case_size`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the size of the case that suits your phone (xs, sm, lg).
* **Case Color** (`case_color`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select one or more colors for your case.
* **Case Design** (`case_design`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the desired design for your case.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, **Optional**): If you know the date of your order completion, you can enter it here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how you would like to pay for your order (credit, debit, paypall).
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, `note`, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or details about your order.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`, **Optional**): If you have any special requests or messages, you can include them here.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your address for delivery.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- You can choose multiple colors for your case.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
