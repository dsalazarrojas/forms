# exclusive_color_phone_preorder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from customers for preorders of an exclusive phone. It includes details about the preorder, shipping, payment, and customer information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely with the required information.
2. Review the form for any errors before submitting.
