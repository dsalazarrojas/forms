# Online Course Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-order information from users who want to purchase a course. It's essential to fill out this form correctly to ensure that your order is processed smoothly and that you receive the right course materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and contact number (if required).
2. Select the course details that interest you from the available options.
3. Enter the date and time of your purchase.
4. Provide your email address to receive updates and confirmation.
5. Enter any additional phone numbers where you can be reached.
6. Leave any additional comments or questions you have about the course.
7. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, Optional): Please fill in your name and contact number to help us reach you with any updates or concerns.
* **Course Information (Page 2)** (`course_info`, text, Optional): Describe the course you're pre-ordering, including its title, date, etc.
* **Course Details (Page 3)** (`course_details`, select_one, Optional): Choose the course details that apply to you, such as Field 1, Field 2, or Field 3.
* **Date of Purchase (Page 4)** (`date_of_purchase`, date, Optional): Enter the date you want to purchase the course.
* **Time of Purchase (Page 5)** (`time_of_purchase`, time, Optional): Enter the time you want to purchase the course.
* **Email (Page 6)** (`email`, email, Optional): Provide your email address to receive updates and confirmation about your order.
* **Phone (Page 7)** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter any additional phone numbers where you can be reached.
* **Notes (Page 8)** (`notes`, note, Optional): Leave any additional comments or questions about the course.
* **User Comments (Page 9)** (`user_comments`, text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you have about the course.
