# pet supplies new range pre order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their pet supplies preferences and contact details for new product ranges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the available Pet Supplies Range options that best describe your needs.
2. Provide a contact number, email, and address to receive updates and information.
3. Optionally, provide more details about your demand and preferred delivery options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Supplies Range** (`pet_supplies_range`, text, not required): Enter a brief description or the name of the pet supplies range you're interested in. This will help us better understand your needs.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_one, not required): Select one of the available options that best describe your delivery preferences, such as "Pet Supplies Range 1", "Pet Supplies Range 2", or "Pet Supplies Range 3".
* **Early Demand** (`early_demand`, date, not required): If you need your supplies early, you can provide a specific date for your delivery. This will help us plan and prioritize our delivery accordingly.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, not required): Please provide more details about your delivery preferences, such as specific dates or times.
* **Fast Data Collection** (`fast_data_collection`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Fast", "Faster", or "Fastest" to indicate your priority level for data collection.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form_pet_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your pet (if applicable) to help us provide more personalized information.
* **Contact Number** (`pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form_contact_number`, text, not required): Enter a valid contact number where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Email** (`pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form_email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates and information.
* **Delivery Address** (`pet_supplies_new_range_pre_order_form_delivery_address`, text, not required): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate and efficient delivery of your supplies.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information to ensure we can contact you and fulfill your needs.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form will be used to gather general information and preferences; no purchase will be processed or charged through this form.
