# Specialty Meat Selection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Meat Selection Pre Order Form is designed to capture customer orders and preferences for specialty meat products, such as specific cuts or types of meat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title (e.g., "John Doe")
2. Provide your contact information (e.g., "customer")
3. Select the type of meat you'd like to order (e.g., "meat selection")
4. Choose between pickup or delivery options
5. Add any additional notes or instructions for your order

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter your name or title to help us identify your order.
* **customer** (`customer`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information or notes about your order.
* **meat selection** (`meat_selection`, text, optional): Select the specific type of meat you'd like to order (e.g., "Ribeye, Sirloin, or Filet Mignon").
* **pickup or delivery** (`pickup_or_delivery`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you'd like to pick up your order or have it delivered.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions about your order, such as special requests or dietary restrictions.
