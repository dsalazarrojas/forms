# Menu Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order their menu items before the actual ordering process. It will help generate a pre-order list for the customer, which will be used for ordering purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (optional) in the text field next to "First Name".
2. Enter your **email** address in the text field next to "email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (First Name, text, optional)
This field is for entering your name. If you'd like to be identified by your name, please enter it here.

### **email** (email, email, optional)
Enter your email address to receive updates about your pre-order and ordering status.
