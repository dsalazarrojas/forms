# Blender Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blender Pre-Order Form is designed to help you reserve your desired Blender model before it's officially launched. This form gathers all the necessary information for the pre-order and payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details: Provide your full name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your shipping address.
3. Choose your preferred Blender model and color.
4. Specify the quantity of units you'd like to pre-order.
5. Select your payment method and confirm your deposit payment.
6. Optionally add any additional accessories or gift message.
7. Select your desired warranty coverage.
8. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Blender Pre-Order Form** (`title`, note, no, no): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, yes, no): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, yes, no): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, yes, no): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, yes, no): Your street address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, no, no): Your apartment or suite number (optional).
* **City** (`city`, text, yes, no): Your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, yes, no): Your state or region.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, yes, no): Your postal code.
* **Blender Model** (`blender_model`, select_one, yes, no): Choose which Blender model you're pre-ordering.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, select_one, yes, no): Select your preferred color.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, yes, no): Specify how many units you'd like to pre-order.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, yes, no): Enter the price per unit.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, yes, no): The total order value.
* **Deposit Paid** (`deposit_paid`, select_one, yes, no): Confirm if you've already paid the deposit.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, yes, no): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Expected Ship Date** (`expected_ship_date`, date, no, no): Optional, but useful for delivery planning.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, no, no): Optionally include a gift message.
* **Warranty Selection** (`warranty_selection`, select_one, yes, no): Select your desired warranty coverage.
* **Additional Accessories** (`accessories_added`, select_multiple, no, no): Select any additional accessories you'd like to add.
* **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`, text, no, no): Enter any promotional code you have.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, yes, no): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, no, no): Include any special instructions or notes.
