# Color Street Spring Launch Preorder - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order Color Street's Spring collection. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. Select the Spring collection items you'd like to pre-order by checking the boxes in the "Spring Solids", "Spring Glitters", and "Spring Designs" sections.
5. Enter the number of sets you'd like to pre-order in the "Total Sets to Pre-Order" field.
6. Choose whether you're eligible for the B3G1 deal by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Applying B3G1 Deal?" field.
7. Select your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your delivery type from the "Delivery Type" dropdown menu.
9. If you have any additional instructions, enter them in the "Additional Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (pre_name)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address (pre_email)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your pre-order and any updates.
* **Shipping Address (pre_address)**: Enter your shipping address where you'd like your pre-ordered items to be delivered.
* **Spring Solids (pre_solids)**: Select the Spring Solids you'd like to pre-order by checking the boxes that correspond to the colors you're interested in (Aspen Sky, Giza Sands, Key West Is Best, and Maine-ly Mums).
* **Spring Glitters (pre_glitters)**: Select the Spring Glitters you'd like to pre-order by checking the boxes that correspond to the designs you're interested in (Glittersweet, Fort Worth It, Chelsea Ya Later, and At the Plaza).
* **Spring Designs (pre_designs)**: Select the Spring Designs you'd like to pre-order by checking the boxes that correspond to the designs you're interested in (Spotted in NYC, Floral Distraction, Polka Dot-com, and Mermaid You Look).
* **Total Sets to Pre-Order (pre_total_sets)**: Enter the total number of sets you'd like to pre-order.
* **Applying B3G1 Deal? (pre_b3g1)**: Select "True" if you're eligible for the B3G1 deal, or "False" if you're not.
* **Preferred Payment (pre_payment_method)**: Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu (Venmo, PayPal, and Facebook Pay).
* **Delivery Type (pre_delivery_method)**: Select your preferred delivery type from the dropdown menu (Standard Shipping, Priority Shipping, and Porch Pick-up).
* **Additional Instructions (pre_notes)**: Enter any additional instructions or messages you'd like to include with your pre-order.
