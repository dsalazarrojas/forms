# scentsy_march_15th_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `scentsy_march_15th_pre_order_form` is a preorder form for reserving a spot on the March 15th event. To secure your spot, please fill out this form with accurate and complete information. This form is mandatory, and incomplete submissions will not be accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields marked as `required`.
2.  Enter your first and last name accurately in the `First Name` and `Last Name` fields respectively.
3.  Enter your email address correctly in the `Email` field.
4.  Enter your phone number correctly in the `Phone` field.
5.  Enter your address accurately in the `Address` field.
6.  Enter your city name accurately in the `City` field.
7.  Enter your state name accurately in the `State` field.
8.  Enter your zip code accurately in the `Zip` field.
9.  Select the correct date for your order in the `Order Date` field.
10. Select the correct shipping date for your order in the `Shipping Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name accurately.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name accurately.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXXX`.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address accurately.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city name accurately.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state name accurately.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code accurately.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to order from the available options.
*   **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to ship from the available options.

## Tips
*   Make sure to check your email address and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that your address and city name match your actual location.
*   If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
