# Guitar Model Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to pre-order a guitar model. It helps us gather the necessary information to process their order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields if they are applicable.
2. Enter your **Email** address to enable us to contact you for more information.
3. Provide your **Phone** number so we can contact you for further details.
4. Enter the **Model Number** of the guitar you want to pre-order.
5. Select the type of **Guitar Type** you are interested in (Acoustic, Electric, or Semi-Acoustic).
6. Choose the **Order Date** you would like to place the order.
7. If you wish, you can provide a **Deposit** to secure your order.
8. Enter the **Delivery Date** you prefer for your order.
9. If you have any additional **Notes**, you can provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Model Number** (Number): Enter the model number of the guitar you want to pre-order.
* **Color** (Text): You can enter any color of your preference.
* **Guitar Type** (Select One):
  * Acoustic: Choose this if you want to pre-order an acoustic guitar.
  * Electric: Choose this if you want to pre-order an electric guitar.
  * Semi-Acoustic: Choose this if you want to pre-order a semi-acoustic guitar.
* **Order Date** (Date): Choose the date you would like to place the order.
* **Deposit** (Number): You can provide a deposit to secure your order.
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Choose the date you prefer for your order delivery.
* **Notes** (Text): Any additional information you want to provide about your order.
* **First Name Error** (Text): This field is for error messages related to the First Name field.
