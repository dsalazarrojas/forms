# Holiday Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-order information for holiday products. It is designed to gather essential details about the product, shipping address, and payment method of the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you wish to pre-order from the options provided.
2. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to pre-order.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Add any additional notes about your order.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Enter your shipping address, including city, state, and zip code.
8. Select your country of origin.
9. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Product** (`preorder_product`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the holiday product you wish to pre-order from the available options.
* **Quantity** (`preorder_quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to pre-order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`preorder_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, `text`, required: false): Select your country of origin.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose "Yes" to submit your pre-order form.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct product and quantity to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Provide a clear note about your order for any special requests.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct payment method and country of origin.
