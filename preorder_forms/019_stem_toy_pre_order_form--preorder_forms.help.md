# STEM Toy Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to pre-order STEM-related products (toys) and collect information about the customer and their order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Review each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form to place your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Fill in this section with your contact information (name and email).
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address (optional).
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional information about your pre-order (optional).
* **Toy Pre-Order**: Select the STEM toy you would like to pre-order from the available options.
* **Pre-Order Date**: Choose a date for your pre-order (optional).
* **Pre-Order Time**: Choose a time for your pre-order (optional).
* **Shipping Details**: Provide any additional shipping information (optional).
* **Shipping Date**: Choose a date for shipping (optional).
* **Shipping Time**: Choose a time for shipping (optional).
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost (optional).
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method you prefer (optional).
* **Customer Note**: Add any notes or comments about your pre-order (optional).
* **Shipping Status**: Select the status of your shipping (optional).
* **Shipping Cost Status**: Select the status of your shipping cost (optional).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (optional).
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (optional).
* **Shipping Note**: Add any additional shipping information (optional).
* **Pre-Order Status**: Select the status of your pre-order (optional).
* **Pre-Order Notes**: Add any additional pre-order information (optional).
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about your pre-order (optional).
* **Form ID**: (Not applicable for customer use).
* **Form Name**: (Not applicable for customer use).
