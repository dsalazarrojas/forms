# School Lunch Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process student lunch requests, allowing schools to prepare and serve individualized meals that cater to each student's specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "School Information" page.
2. Enter your student's school name, grade, and student name as prompted.
3. Move on to the "Student Details" page, where you will enter the student's grade level and student name.
4. Next, provide the parent/guardian's information, which includes their name and contact number.
5. The "Food Menu" page allows you to select your preferred menu options for the student.
6. On the "Payment Information" page, enter the payment method you plan to use.
7. The "Date" field should be filled in with the date you plan to preorder for.
8. The "Special Requests" field is for listing any special requests or dietary allergies the student may have.
9. Finally, select the student's diet type (Vegetarian or Non-Vegetarian) and enter the total cost of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Information (text)**: Enter the student's school name.
* **Student Details (text)**: Enter the student's grade level and name.
* **Parent/Guardian Details (text)**: Enter the parent/guardian's name and contact number.
* **Food Menu (select multiple)**: Select the type of food you want for the student's lunch (Chicken Salad, Grains, Vegetables, Protein, Fruits, Meat, Fats, Sugars).
* **Food Menu (text)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Student Diet (select one)**: Choose whether the student is Vegetarian or Non-Vegetarian.
* **Special Requests (text)**: List any special requests or allergies the student may have.
* **Payment Information (select one)**: Choose how you plan to pay for the order (Cash, Credit Card, Check).
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you plan to preorder for.
* **School Name (text)**: Enter the school name.
* **Grade (text)**: Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Name (text)**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade Level (text)**: Enter the student's grade level.
* **Parent Name (text)**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the parent's email address.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter the parent's contact number.
* **School Address (text)**: Enter the school address.
* **Daily Menu (select multiple)**: Select the type of food you want for the student's lunch (Chicken Salad, Grains, Vegetables, Protein, Fruits, Meat, Fats, Sugars).
* **Total Cost (number)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Student Diet (select one)**: Choose whether the student is Vegetarian or Non-Vegetarian.
* **Special Requests (text)**: List any special requests or allergies the student may have.
* **Payment Method (select one)**: Choose how you plan to pay for the order (Cash, Credit Card, Check).
