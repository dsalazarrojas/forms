# Scentsy Easter 2022 & Dumbo Collection Preorder - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in preordering Scentsy's Easter 2022 and Dumbo collections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in your name and select the collection you are interested in preordering.
2. If you select the Easter 2022 collection, you will also be asked to provide your shipping address and phone number.
3. Enter your email address to be kept informed about preorder status and any updates.
4. Indicate whether you would like to preorder the Easter 2022 collection.
5. Select which items from the Dumbo collection you would like to preorder.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the Easter 2022 preorder question.
7. Confirm your selections by reviewing and submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Easter 2022** (`easter_2022`, text, required): Enter the name of the Easter 2022 collection you are interested in.
* **Collection** (`collection`, text, required): Select the name of the collection you are interested in (e.g. "Dumbo Collection").
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required only if Easter 2022 collection is selected): Enter your shipping address if you are preordering the Easter 2022 collection.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required only if Easter 2022 collection is selected): Enter your phone number if you are preordering the Easter 2022 collection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to be kept informed about your preorder status and any updates.
* **Easter 2022 Preorder** (`easter_2022_preorder`, select one, required): Indicate whether you would like to preorder the Easter 2022 collection.
* **Dumbo Collection Preorder** (`dumbo_collection_preorder`, select multiple, required): Select which items from the Dumbo collection you would like to preorder.
* **Submit** (button): Confirm your selections by reviewing and submitting the form.
