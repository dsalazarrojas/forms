# Harvest And Nightmare Before Christmas With ScentDelishus - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to preorder items from your collection during the holiday season, specifically for the "Harvest And Nightmare Before Christmas With ScentDelishus" event. The form is meant to gather information about the customer's order, including collection selection, shipping details, and order notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your desired collection from the options provided in the "Collection Selection" field.
2. Enter your shipping details in the corresponding fields, including address, city, state, zip, country, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred shipping method from the options provided.
4. Enter any additional order notes or comments in the "Order Notes" field.
5. Enter the preorder date and time for your order.
6. Enter the quantity of the item(s) you wish to preorder.
7. Select your preferred shipping method and rate from the options provided.
8. Enter any additional shipping cost or tax information.
