# holiday_collection_preorder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday collection preorder form is designed to collect preorder information from customers for holiday gifts or collections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Company** name, if applicable.
3. Choose your **Department** and **Position** if applicable.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Select the **Quantity Preordered** and **Date Preordered** for your holiday collection.
6. Choose any applicable **Select Multiple 1** and **Select Multiple 2** options.
7. Enter any additional information in the **Time Preordered** field.
8. Select all that apply from the **Notes** field.
9. Choose one option from the **Select One** field.
10. Enter any additional details in the **Time** field.
11. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name in this text field.
  * Required: No
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this text field.
  * Required: No
- **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.
  * Required: No
- **Company**: Enter your company name, if applicable.
  * Required: No
- **Department**: Enter the department for your company.
  * Required: No
- **Position**: Enter your position in the company.
  * Required: No
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this text field.
  * Required: No
- **Quantity Preordered**: Enter the quantity of the holiday collection you are preordering.
  * Required: No
- **Date Preordered**: Enter the date you preordered the holiday collection.
  * Required: No
- **Select Multiple 1**: Select all applicable options for this field.
  * Required: No
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5
- **Select Multiple 2**: Select all applicable options for this field.
  * Required: No
  * Options: Option 6, Option 7, Option 8
- **Time Preordered**: Enter any additional information about the time you preordered.
  * Required: No
- **Note Preordered**: Enter any additional information about the preorder.
  * Required: No
- **Notes**: Select all that apply from this field.
  * Required: No
  * Options: Option 9, Option 10, Option 11, Option 12, Option 13
- **Select One**: Choose one option from this field.
  * Required: No
  * Options: Option 14, Option 15, Option 16
- **Time**: Enter any additional details in this text field.
  * Required: No
