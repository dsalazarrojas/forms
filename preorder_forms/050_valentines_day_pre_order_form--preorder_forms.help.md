# valentines_day_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The valentines_day_pre_order_form is designed to collect information from customers for pre-ordering items on a specific date, likely for Valentine's Day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Select the date you would like to pre-order items.
7. Choose the items you would like to pre-order.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **last_name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your pre-order.
- **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for our records.
- **date_of_pre_order** (`date_of_pre_order`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to pre-order items.
- **preorder_items** (`preorder_items`, select multiple, optional): Choose the items you would like to pre-order. Please select all applicable items using the options provided.
- **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your pre-order.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) to ensure your pre-order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
