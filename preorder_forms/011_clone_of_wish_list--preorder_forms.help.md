# Clone Of Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users create a duplicate of their wish list entry, which can be shared or ordered from. It collects essential details such as product and contact information to facilitate communication and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the form header** with a brief summary of your wish list entry.
2. **Enter the Requester name** - this is a required field where you should provide your full name as the requester.
3. **Add your Contact email** - this is a required field for us to notify you about availability of your wished item.
4. **Specify the Product name** - provide the name of the item you're interested in ordering.
5. **Choose Product variant** - if available, select the specific size, color, or SKU of the product you're after.
6. **Enter the Quantity desired** - state how many units of the product you need.
7. **Estimate your budget** - indicate the maximum you're willing to pay per unit for the product.
8. **Select your preferred notification method** - choose how you would like to be informed about the product's availability: via Email, SMS, or Phone call.
9. **Provide shipping address** - if the order will be shipped, enter your shipping address to ensure proper delivery.
10. **Add any Additional notes** - include any other preferences or requests you might have related to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Wish List Entry --**: This field is the header for your wish list entry, providing a brief summary of the item you're interested in.
* **Requester name**: Enter your full name as the person requesting the item.
* **Contact email**: A required field for us to notify you about availability of the product. Please ensure this is a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Product name**: Specify the name of the product you wish to preorder.
* **Product variant**: If applicable, indicate the specific size, color, or SKU of the product you're after.
* **Quantity desired**: Enter the number of units of the product you need.
* **Estimated budget**: Indicate the maximum amount you're willing to pay per unit for the product.
* **Preferred notification method**: Choose how you would like to be informed about the product's availability: via Email, SMS, or Phone call.
* **Shipping address**: If the order will be shipped, enter your shipping address for proper delivery. (This field is only required if shipping is an option.)
* **Additional notes**: Include any other preferences or requests you have regarding the order.
