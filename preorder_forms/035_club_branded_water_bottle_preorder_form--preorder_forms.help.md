# Club Branded Water Bottle Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a custom branded water bottle preorder. It is used to ensure accurate and efficient processing of orders and to provide customers with an easy way to customize their water bottle with their club's branding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Information** section with your name and email address.
2. Select the **Bottle Size**, **Color Preference**, and **Bottle Style** that you prefer.
3. Choose the **Logo Placement** and add any **Custom Text** or **Name** you want on your water bottle.
4. Select the **Quantity** of bottles you want to preorder and choose a **Pickup Method** and **Preferred Pickup Date**.
5. Choose your **Payment Status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the water bottle.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you want to receive order confirmations and delivery information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact information.
* **Student ID - Member ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your student ID or member ID.
* **Bottle Size** (`bottle_size`, select_one, required): Choose from 16 oz, 20 oz, 24 oz, 32 oz, or 40 oz.
* **Color Preference** (`bottle_color`, select_one, required): Choose from Black, White, Navy blue, School colors, or Metallic silver.
* **Bottle Style** (`bottle_style`, select_one, required): Choose from Standard cap, Straw lid, Flip cap, or Wide mouth.
* **Material Type** (`material_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose from Stainless steel, Plastic, BPA-free plastic, Insulated, or Other.
* **Logo Placement** (`logo_option`, select_one, required): Choose how the club's logo or name will appear on the water bottle.
* **Add Custom Text or Name** (`custom_text`, text, optional): Add any custom text or name you want on your water bottle.
* **Custom Text Placement** (`text_placement`, select_one, optional): Choose where the custom text will go on the water bottle.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of bottles you want to preorder.
* **Unit Price - USD** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the standard price per bottle (optional).
* **Total Price - USD** (`total_price`, number, optional): The total price calculated from Quantity and Unit Price.
* **Pickup Method** (`pickup_method`, select_one, required): Choose from Club meeting pickup, Campus mailroom, Mailed to address, Campus event, or Other location.
* **Preferred Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when you want to collect your bottles.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery preferences or notes.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose from Paid in full, Deposit paid - balance due, Payment required at pickup, or Fundraiser - free.
