# Produce Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Produce Pre Order Form is a crucial tool for capturing pre-order details from customers. This form helps ensure accurate and efficient ordering processes, reducing errors and misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the designated field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the format "example@example.com".
4. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
5. Select a valid **Pre-Order Date** for your request.
6. If applicable, specify a desired **Pre-Order Time** for your request.
7. If you have any additional information to include with your pre-order, type it in the **Pre-Order Note** field.
8. If you're ordering in bulk, enter the desired **Quantity** of the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the first name of the customer.
* **Last Name**: This is the last name of the customer, which is required for order tracking purposes.
* **Email**: This field is for the customer's email address, which will be used for communication regarding the order.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for efficient communication.
* **Pre-Order Date**: Choose a valid date for your pre-order, taking into consideration production and delivery schedules.
* **Pre-Order Time**: If applicable, select a specific time for your pre-order, which will help with accurate scheduling.
* **Pre-Order Note**: Type any additional information you'd like to include with your pre-order.
* **Quantity**: If you're ordering multiple items, enter the quantity you'd like to order.
