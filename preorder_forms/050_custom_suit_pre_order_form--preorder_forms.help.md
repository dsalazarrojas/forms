# Custom Suit Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers pre-order custom suits with ease, ensuring accurate and complete information for seamless order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field, ensuring it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your desired **Size** from the options provided.
6. Enter your **Measurements** in the "Measurements" field.
7. If necessary, provide any additional **Order Notes** in the "Order Notes" field.
8. Select your preferred **Payment Terms** from the options provided.
9. Choose your **Shipping Terms** from the options provided.
10. Enter your **Delivery Address** in the "Delivery Address" field.
11. Enter your **Billing Address** in the "Billing Address" field.
12. Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for your custom suit order.
13. If applicable, select the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for your custom suit delivery.
14. Enter your **Customer Reference** if desired.
15. Enter the **Tailor's Name** who will be working on your custom suit.
16. Enter the **Tailor's Email** and **Tailor's Phone** for communication purposes.
17. If needed, add any additional **Tailor Notes** about your custom suit order.
18. If desired, add any **Customer Notes** about your custom suit order.
19. Select the **Order Status** from the options provided (e.g., In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).
20. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, PayPal).
21. Select the **Payment Status** from the options provided (e.g., Paid, Pending, Cancelled).
22. Select the **Submission Status** from the options provided (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name to ensure accurate order processing.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name to complete your identity.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for tailor communication and order updates.
* **Size** (`Size`, text, required): Choose your desired suit size from the options provided.
* **Measurements** (`Measurements`, text, required): Enter your measurements for the tailor to create the perfect fit.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your custom suit order, if needed.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, required): Select your preferred payment terms from the options provided.
* **Shipping Terms** (`Shipping Terms`, text, required): Choose your shipping terms from the options provided.
* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, text, required): Enter your delivery address for accurate and efficient delivery.
* **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, text, required): Enter your billing address for order processing and payment purposes.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to have your custom suit ready.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, time, required): Choose the time you'd like to have your custom suit ready.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): If applicable, select a date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, optional): If applicable, choose a time for delivery.
* **Customer Reference** (`Customer Reference`, text, optional): Provide any relevant reference information, if needed.
* **Tailor Name** (`Tailor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tailor working on your custom suit.
* **Tailor Email** (`Tailor Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the tailor for communication purposes.
* **Tailor Phone** (`Tailor Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the tailor for communication.
* **Tailor Notes** (`Tailor Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your custom suit order.
* **Customer Notes** (`Customer Notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about your custom suit order.
* **Order Status** (`Order Status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your custom suit order (e.g., In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method from the options provided (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your payment (e.g., Paid, Pending, Cancelled).
* **Submission Status** (`Submission Status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your submission (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting your pre-order form.
* Use the options provided to ensure you're selecting the correct values for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification from the tailor or our support team.
* Ensure you have all the required information before proceeding with the form submission.
* Review and understand the selected options for **Order Status**, **Payment Method**, **Payment Status**, and **Submission Status** as they will impact your order processing and payment details.
