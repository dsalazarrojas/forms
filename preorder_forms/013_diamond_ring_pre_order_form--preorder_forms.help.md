# Diamond Ring Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for a pre-ordered diamond ring. Please fill out the form to provide us with your details, and we'll be in touch to confirm your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form, following the instructions below.
2. Make sure to review your submission before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
3. Click the submit button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Order Information** (diamond_ring_pre_order_form_page_1):
  * This section is where you can provide information about your pre-order.
  (Note: This field is optional)

* **Enggraving Preferences** (diamond_ring_pre_order_form_page_2):
  * Here, you can specify any custom engravings you'd like for your ring.
  (Note: This field is optional)

* **Delivery Preferences** (diamond_ring_pre_order_form_page_3):
  * In this section, please enter your delivery instructions (e.g., shipping address, delivery date, signature requirements).
  (Note: This field is optional)

* **Payment Options** (diamond_ring_pre_order_form_page_4):
  * This section is not applicable to this form.
  (Note: You can ignore this field, as the form does not have any payment options)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully.
* Review your submission before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
