# Anthology Book Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to pre-order books from an anthology series. To ensure the book is available for you when it's published, please fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer contact information section, including your full name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
2. Select the anthology book you would like to pre-order by choosing the volume from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the number of copies you would like to purchase.
4. Choose your preferred binding type (paperback, hardcover, or digital).
5. If you want a signed copy, select the option.
6. Select your preferred payment method.
7. If you have any special instructions or gift message, enter it in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and tracking updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any additional communication or order updates.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address to ensure your book is delivered to the correct location. Use the format: Street, City, State, ZIP.
* **Country**: Enter your country where you reside.
* **Select Anthology Volume**: Choose the anthology book you would like to pre-order from the dropdown menu.
* **Number of Copies**: Enter the number of copies you would like to purchase.
* **Binding Type**: Choose your preferred binding type: paperback, hardcover, or digital.
* **Would you like a signed copy?**: Select "True" if you want a signed copy of the book, or "False" if not.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method: credit/debit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Gift Message or Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or gift message for the recipient, if applicable.

Note: The more accurate and specific your information is, the smoother the preorder process will be, and the better your experience with us will be.
