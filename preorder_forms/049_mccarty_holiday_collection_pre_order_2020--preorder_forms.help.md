# McCarty Holiday Collection Pre-Order 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to pre-order their items from the McCarty Holiday Collection 2020. It is essential to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, in the format of `example@example.com`.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Select the order date for your pre-order from the calendar.
8. Choose the quantity of items you would like to pre-order.
9. Select the color of your choice from the "Color" dropdown menu.
10. If you would like to include a gift message, you can enter it in the "Gift Message" field.
11. Select the shipping date for your order.
12. If you would like to include a gift card, you can select the gift card type and enter the gift card pin in the "Gift Card" field.
13. Enter any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.
14. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the amount of your order in the "Amount" field.
16. Enter the resource required for your pre-order in the "Resource" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the order date for your pre-order using the calendar.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Choose the number of items you would like to pre-order.
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, required): Select the color of your choice from the dropdown menu.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, optional): If you would like to include a gift message, enter it here.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required): Select the shipping date for your order.
* **Gift Card** (`gift_card`, note, optional): If you would like to include a gift card, select the gift card type and enter the gift card pin.
* **Gift Card Type** (`card_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of gift card you would like to include with your order.
* **Gift Card Pin** (`card_pin`, text, optional): Enter the gift card pin if you are including a gift card.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Gift Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter the gift card number if you are including a gift card.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your order.
* **Resource** (`resource`, text, required): Enter the resource required for your pre-order.
