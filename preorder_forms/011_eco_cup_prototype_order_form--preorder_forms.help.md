# Eco Cup Prototype Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Cup Prototype Order Form is a single-page form designed to collect shipping information for your order. This form must be filled out once for each order you wish to place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "form_1" field with your desired order details.
2. Provide your shipping details in the "shipping_details" field.
3. Confirm your understanding of the terms by filling in the "terms_confirmation" field.
4. If you have additional shipping information, you can add it in the "shipping_address" field.
5. Select your preferred shipping method in the "shipping_method" field.
6. Enter the shipping cost in the "shipping_cost" field, if applicable.
7. If you have any other shipping terms, you can add them in the "shipping_terms" field.
8. Review your order information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_1** (`form_1`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter any additional order details, such as your order quantity or order notes.
* **Shipping details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping details here, including any necessary notes or instructions.
* **Terms confirmation** (`terms_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of your order.
* **form_2** (`form_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently empty and not applicable.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred shipping method, either shipping by post, shipping by courier, or other.
* **Shipping cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the shipping cost of your order, if applicable.
* **Shipping terms** (`shipping_terms`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional shipping terms or instructions.

## Tips
* Be sure to review your order information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the ordering process, contact us for assistance.
* This form is intended to be a one-time submission, so be sure to fill it out correctly before submitting.
