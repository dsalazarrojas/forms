# Streaming Package Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Package Pre Order Form is a tool used to gather customer information and preferences for pre-ordering a streaming package. This form is designed to be filled out only once per customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the **Start Date** for when you want to begin streaming your package.
6. Select your desired **Package Type** from the dropdown menu:
	* **Streaming Video**
	* **Streaming Audio**
	* **Streaming Live**
7. Enter any **Customer Details** about your preferences in the "Customer Details" field (optional).
8. Provide any **Order Notes** or additional information in the "Order Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about your streaming package.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to reach you with any updates or issues.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required): Choose the date you would like to start streaming your package.
* **Package Type** (`package_type`, select_one, not required):
	+ **Streaming Video**: Choose this option for streaming video content.
	+ **Streaming Audio**: Choose this option for streaming audio content.
	+ **Streaming Live**: Choose this option for streaming live content.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, note, not required): Enter any additional details about your preferences or requirements.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, not required): Provide any additional information about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Be sure to enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* Choose the correct package type for your streaming needs.
* Enter any necessary customer details to ensure your streaming package is tailored to your requirements.
