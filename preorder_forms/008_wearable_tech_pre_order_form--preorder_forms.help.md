# Wearable Tech Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wearable Tech Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for pre-orders of wearable tech products. This form helps our sales team prepare for production and fulfillment and may also be used to collect additional details for product personalization or customization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the **Customer** field.
2. Provide the **Product Name** of the item you are interested in pre-ordering.
3. Select a **Shipping Date** for your order.
4. Enter your **Shipping Address**, **Shipping City**, **Shipping State**, and **Shipping Postal Code** for delivery.
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for us to contact you about your order.
6. If desired, add any additional **Order Comments** for our team to know about your specific needs.
7. Enter the **Price** you are willing to pay for your pre-ordered item.
8. If available, upload or share a **Product Image URL** for further product evaluation and customization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your name, title, or any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Product Name** (Text Field, Optional): Type in the name or model number of the wearable tech product you are interested in pre-ordering.
* **Order Date** (Date Field, Optional): Choose the date you would like to receive your pre-ordered item.
* **Shipping Address** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure correct delivery of your pre-ordered item.
* **Shipping City** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your state or province for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Postal Code** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your postal or zip code for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the country for the shipping address.
* **Email** (Email Field, Optional): Enter your contact email for us to reach you about your order.
* **Phone** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your order.
* **Order Comments** (Note Field, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your pre-ordered item.
* **Price** (Text Field, Optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for your pre-ordered item.
* **Product Image URL** (Text Field, Optional): Share a URL of the product image, if available.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields carefully to ensure accurate order processing.
- If you need help with any field, feel free to contact our support team.
- We will contact you about your order and any further steps required for successful product delivery.
