# makana_nails_color_street_wish_list - Help Guide
## Purpose
The makana_nails_color_street_wish_list form is designed to collect information from customers about their nail color wishes for a color street wish list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields with the relevant details:
	* Color: Select your preferred nail color.
	* Nail size: Choose the size of nail you prefer.
	* Date ordered: Enter the date you wish to order your nail color.
3. Optionally, you can add any additional notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Color** (`color`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred nail color (red, blue, or green).
* **Nail size** (`nail_size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the size of nail you prefer (small, medium, or large).
* **Nail type** (`nail_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of nail you prefer (gel, acrylic, or dip).
* **Nail Color Wish List** (`nail_color_wish_list`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional details about your nail color wish list if needed.
* **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you wish to order your nail color.
* **Time Ordered** (`time_ordered`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you wish to order your nail color.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your order.

## Tips
* Ensure you select all required fields carefully.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
