# Holiday Pre Sale Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Pre Sale Registration" form is used to collect information from customers about their interest in purchasing products for a holiday pre sale event. This form helps us understand customer preferences and quantities for the products they wish to purchase, allowing us to better prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields carefully and enter your relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with options, select the correct value from the provided choices.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Pre Sale Registration** (`1`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief title for your registration.
* **Customer Information** (`2`, `text`, not required): Provide your contact information, such as your name, address, and email.
* **Name** (`3`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, not required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, not required): Enter your shipping address to ensure timely delivery.
* **Product** (`7`, `select_one`, not required): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in purchasing a product, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Quantity** (`8`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of products you would like to purchase, if applicable.
* **Price** (`9`, `number`, not required): Enter the total price you are willing to pay, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`10`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional notes or comments about your purchase.
* **Preorder Date** (`11`, `date`, not required): Select the date you would like to receive your preorder, if applicable.
