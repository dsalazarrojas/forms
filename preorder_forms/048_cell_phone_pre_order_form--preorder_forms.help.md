# Cell Phone Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cell Phone Pre Order Form is designed to collect pre-order information from customers before the release of new cell phones. This form helps us to understand the customer's preferences and requirements for the pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information (First Name, Last Name, and Email Address).
2. Select the desired device brand, model, color, and storage capacity.
3. Choose a payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
4. Set the pre-order date and confirm your expected release date.
5. Specify the quantity of devices you want to pre-order.
6. Enter the unit price of the device.
7. Calculate the total pre-order amount (quantity multiplied by unit price).
8. Enter your shipping address information (Street Address, City, Province, and Postal Code).
9. Confirm that you agree to the pre-order terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for any future contact.

### Device Selection
* **Device Brand** (`Device Brand`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the brand of the device you want to pre-order (e.g., Apple, Samsung, Google, OnePlus, Motorola, Other Android).
* **Device Model** (`Device Model`, text, **Required**): Enter the specific model name of the device you want to pre-order.
* **Device Color** (`Device Color`, select_one, **Required**): Select the color of the device you want to pre-order (e.g., Black, White, Silver, Gold, Blue, Red, Purple, Green, Pink, Other).
* **Storage Capacity** (`Storage Capacity`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the storage capacity of the device you want to pre-order (e.g., 128 GB, 256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, Other).

### Pre-Order Details
* **Pre-Order Date** (`Pre-Order Date`, date, **Required**): Set the date you want to pre-order the device.
* **Expected Device Release Date** (`Expected Device Release Date`, date, **Optional**): Enter the expected release date of the device, if known.
* **Quantity to Pre-Order** (`Quantity to Pre-Order`, number, **Required**): Specify the quantity of devices you want to pre-order.
* **Unit Price** (`Unit Price`, number, **Required**): Enter the unit price of the device.
* **Total Pre-Order Amount** (`Total Pre-Order Amount`, number, **Required**): Calculate the total pre-order amount by multiplying the quantity by unit price.

### Shipping Address
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, **Required**): Enter your street address for shipping.
* **City** (`City`, text, **Required**): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **State or Province** (`State or Province`, text, **Required**): Enter the state or province for your shipping address.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, **Required**): Enter the postal code for your shipping address.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Other).
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`Special Requests or Notes`, text, **Optional**): Enter any special requests or notes for the pre-order, such as engraving or accessories.
* **I Agree to the Pre-Order Terms and Conditions** (`I Agree to the Pre-Order Terms and Conditions`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you agree to the pre-order terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete to avoid delays in the pre-order process.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.
* Review the pre-order terms and conditions before confirming your agreement.
