# Nutrition Club Curbside Pick Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for users to pre-order curbside pickup for the Nutrition Club. It is used to collect necessary information for a smooth and efficient curbside pickup experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required: false)**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Last Name (text, required: false)**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive confirmation and any follow-up communication regarding your curbside pickup order.
* **Phone (text, required: false)**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached to confirm or update your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for a successful and efficient curbside pickup experience.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties while completing this form, please contact our support team for assistance.
