# pricing_estimation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide pricing estimates for a product or service. The form collects demographic information about the customer, including their age, income, and frequency of purchasing, as well as their customer segment, to provide a more accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Base" page, which includes the "Residential", "Commercial", or "Other" customer segment selection.
2. Enter the customer's age.
3. Enter the customer's income.
4. Select the customer's frequency of purchasing (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
5. Select the price range (Low, Medium, or High) for the product or service.
6. Enter the base price.
7. Enter the price variation.
8. Enter the margin.
9. Enter the estimated prices for the subsequent 10 fields (estimated_price_1, estimated_price_2, estimated_price_3, estimated_price_4, estimated_price_5, estimated_price_6, estimated_price_7, estimated_price_8, estimated_price_9, estimated_price_10).
10. Enter the total price.
11. Select the customer segment for the subsequent 2 fields (customer_segment_2, customer_age_2, customer_income_2, customer_frequency_2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base** (1, note, required): This page is the starting point for the form. Select "Residential", "Commercial", or "Other" to indicate the customer segment.

* **Estimated Price 3** (13, number, required): Enter the estimated price 3.
