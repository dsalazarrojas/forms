# Request A Free Estimate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request A Free Estimate" form is designed to help you request a free estimate for a construction project. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your project, and our team will be in touch to provide a quote for the work required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the category** that best describes your project (General Construction, Home Improvement, or Other).
2. **Choose the type of service** you need (General Labor, Labor + Materials, or Service Only).
3. **Provide your project location** (type in the address of the project location).
4. **Enter the estimated duration** of the project (optional).
5. **Describe your project** (optional, but recommended).
6. **Enter your contact email** (to receive quotes and updates).
7. **Enter a phone number** (optional, to receive phone calls from our team).
8. **Enter any additional project details** (optional).
9. **Select how you would like to share location** (or Upload Documents, or Other).
10. **Confirm that you have read and understand the form** (to ensure you have reviewed all the necessary information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Category**: Choose a category that best describes your project (e.g., General Construction, Home Improvement, or Other). This helps our team understand the scope of your project.
* **Select Service Type**: Choose the type of service you need (General Labor, Labor + Materials, or Service Only). This ensures that our team can provide the correct quote for your project.
* **Project Location**: Enter the address of the project location. This helps us understand where the work will be done.
* **Job Duration**: If you know the estimated duration of the project, enter it here. This gives us a better understanding of the project timeline.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of your project. This helps our team understand the scope of work required.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email, so we can contact you with quotes and updates about your project.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number, so our team can contact you via phone (optional).
* **Contact Message**: Use this field to enter any additional comments or details about your project.
* **Form Attachments**: Select how you would like to share location, or if you have any documents to upload.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out the form, press the "Submit" button to send your request for a free estimate.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that our team can process your request accurately.
* Use this form to provide detailed information about your project, so we can give you an accurate quote.
* Our team will contact you with any questions or to provide updates about your project.
