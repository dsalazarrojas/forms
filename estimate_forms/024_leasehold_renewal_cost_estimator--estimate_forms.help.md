# Leasehold Renewal Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leasehold Renewal Cost Estimator form is used to gather information about a property to estimate the cost of renewing a leasehold. This form is only intended for users who are seeking to estimate the cost of renewing a leasehold.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of your inquiry from the options provided.
2.  Enter a brief description of your inquiry.
3.  Enter details about the property you are inquiring about.
4.  Provide details about the leasehold.
5.  Enter the date of your enquiry.
6.  Enter the date of the lease renewal.
7.  Choose the type of property you are inquiring about.
8.  Enter the size of the property.
9.  Choose the age of the property.
10. Enter a description of the property's condition.
11.  Choose the status of the property.
12.  Select the type of inquiry you are making.
13.  Enter an estimated cost for the lease renewal.
14.  Set a follow-up time for any further questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Estimate Forms, Other)
	+ Please select the category of your inquiry.
* **Description** (Text)
	+ Enter a brief description of your inquiry.
* **Property Details** (Text)
	+ Enter details about the property you are inquiring about.
* **Leasehold Details** (Text)
	+ Enter details about the leasehold.
* **Enquiry Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of your enquiry.
* **Renewal Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date of the lease renewal.
* **Property Type** (Select One: House, Unit, Other)
	+ Choose the type of property you are inquiring about.
* **Property Size** (Number)
	+ Enter the size of the property.
* **Property Age** (Number)
	+ Enter the age of the property.
* **Property Condition** (Text)
	+ Enter a description of the property's condition.
* **Property Status** (Select Multiple: Under construction, Completed, Under renovation, Sold)
	+ Choose the status of the property.
* **Enquiry Type** (Select Multiple: New, Lease Renewal, Other)
	+ Select the type of inquiry you are making.
* **Estimated Cost** (Number)
	+ Enter an estimated cost for the lease renewal.
* **Follow Up** (Time)
	+ Set a follow-up time for any further questions.

## Tips

* Please enter accurate information to ensure the best possible estimate.
* If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* The estimated cost is subject to change based on the information you provide.
* Please follow up with us to discuss your inquiry further if required.
