# Tour Guide Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for estimating the cost and details of a tour guide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of services offered by the tour guide.
2.  Choose the location of the tour.
3.  Enter the estimated cost of the tour.
4.  Specify the travel time required for the tour.
5.  Enter the duration of the tour.
6.  Select the currency of the estimate (e.g., Dollars, Euros, Pounds).
7.  If the currency is not listed, specify the alternative currency.
8.  Add any additional notes or comments about the tour.
9.  Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
10. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tourGuideForm** (text): This is the title of the form.
* **services** (select multiple): Select all the services that the tour guide will offer. Options include Walking tour, Guided tour, and Other.
* **servicesOther** (text): If the selected services are Other, please specify the services offered.
* **serviceLocation** (select one): Choose the location of the tour. Options include Park, Beach, and Other.
* **locationOther** (text): If the location is Other, please specify the location.
* **estimateCost** (number): Enter the estimated cost of the tour.
* **travelTime** (time): Enter the travel time required for the tour.
* **tourDuration** (number): Enter the duration of the tour.
* **estimateCurrency** (select one): Select the currency of the estimate. Options include Dollars, Euros, Pounds.
* **currencyOther** (text): If the currency is not listed, specify the alternative currency.
* **note** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about the tour.
* **email** (email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information for the estimate.
* Check the location and services selected to ensure they match the information provided.
* Use the estimated currency as the reference for the cost.
