# Dental Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Estimate Form is a document used to collect patient information and procedure details from patients or their caregivers for the dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the patient information section with the patient's basic details such as name, email, and contact number.
2. Proceed to the procedure section and enter the details about the procedure(s) the patient is undergoing.
3. Enter the procedure date and time if applicable.
4. Fill out the procedure cost and fee fields, which are used to estimate the costs associated with the procedure.
5. Provide the patient's name, email, and contact number in the respective fields.
6. Enter the name of the dental clinic and any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Procedure** (`procedure`, text, required): Describe the procedure(s) the patient is undergoing (e.g., filling, extraction, etc.).
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the procedure if applicable.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the procedure if applicable.
* **Procedure Cost** (`procedure_cost`, number, required): Estimate the total cost of the procedure.
* **Procedure Fee** (`procedure_fee`, number, required): Estimate any additional fees associated with the procedure.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the dentist performing the procedure.
* **Dental Clinic** (`dental_clinic`, text, required): Enter the name of the dental clinic where the procedure will take place.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a contact number for the patient or their caregiver.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the dental clinic (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient or procedure.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the dental clinic staff for assistance.
