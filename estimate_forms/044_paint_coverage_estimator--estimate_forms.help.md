# Paint Coverage Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paint Coverage Estimator form is used to estimate the coverage of paint for a given project. It collects information about the project details, paint coverage rate, color type, paint color, and other relevant factors to provide an accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details, including the project area, number of rooms, walls height, and surface area.
2. Choose the paint coverage rate that best suits your project.
3. Select the color type and paint color for your project.
4. Choose the sheen, primer, finish, and paint options as relevant to your project.
5. Select the finish type and paint brand for your project.
6. Indicate if color match is required for your project.
7. Add any additional notes about your project.
8. Enter the submission date and time of the form.
9. Provide the contact name, contact email, and contact phone number for your project.
10. Confirm if the form was submitted by you or someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Area** (`project_area`, number, optional): Enter the area of the project.
* **Rooms** (`rooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of rooms in the project.
* **Walls Height** (`walls_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the walls in the project.
* **Surface Area** (`surface_area`, number, optional): Enter the surface area of the project.
* **Surface Area Units** (`surface_area_units`, select_one, optional): Choose the units of the surface area (Yes/No).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date of the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the submission time of the form.

### Paint Coverage Estimator
* **Paint Coverage Rate** (`paint_coverage_rate`, select_one, optional): Choose the paint coverage rate (Good/Fair).
* **Color Type** (`color_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the color type (Yes/No).
* **Paint Color** (`paint_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the paint color (Yes/No).
* **Sheen** (`sheen`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the sheen options (Yes/No).
* **Primer** (`primer`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the primer options (Yes/No).
* **Finish** (`finish`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the finish options (Yes/No).
* **Paint** (`paint`, select_one, optional): Choose the paint options (Yes/No).
* **Finish Type** (`finish_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the finish type (Yes/No).
* **Paint Brand** (`paint_brand`, select_one, optional): Choose the paint brand (Yes/No).
* **Color Match** (`color_match`, select_one, optional): Indicate if color match is required (Yes/No).

### Contact Information
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, select_one, optional): Enter the contact name (Yes/No).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email (Yes/No).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number (Yes/No).

### Form Submission
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Confirm who submitted the form (Yes/No).
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the form was submitted (Yes/No).
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, select_one, optional): Confirm where the form was submitted from (Yes/No).
