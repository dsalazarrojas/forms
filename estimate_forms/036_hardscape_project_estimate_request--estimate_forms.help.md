# Hardscape Project Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a hardscape project estimate. Please fill out this form to provide your project details, schedule a preferred date, and select the required services for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the project details section with your project's name, location, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the desired services for your project from the list provided.
3. Choose a preferred date for the project.
4. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your project, including its name and location.
* **Site Location** (Site Location, text, required: true): Enter the address or location of your project site.
* **Project Manager** (Project Manager, text, required: false): If you have a project manager assigned, please enter their name.
* **Preferred Date** (Preferred Date, date, required: true): Choose a preferred date for the project.
* **Client Details** (Client Details, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your project or yourself.
* **Services** (Select Multiple, required: true): Select the services you require for your project, including:
	+ Lawn Aeration
	+ Lawn Care
	+ Mulching
	+ Yard Cleanup
	+ Yard Mowing
	+ Yard Mowing & Trimming
	+ Yard Mowing & Trimming & Edging
	+ Landscaping
	+ Hardscape
	+ Lawn Aeration & Lawn Care
	+ Lawn Mowing & Trimming & Edging
	+ Lawn Mowing & Trimming & Mulching
	+ Yard Cleanup & Mulching
	+ Yard Mowing & Trimming & Mulching
* **Site Notes** (Site Notes, text, required: false): Add any notes or comments about your site that may be helpful for our team.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Client Email** (Client Email, email, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required: true): Enter your phone number.
* **Client Phone** (Client Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Project Budget** (Project Budget, number, required: false): If you have a budget for the project, please enter it.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your request correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll contact you within 24-48 hours to discuss your project further.
