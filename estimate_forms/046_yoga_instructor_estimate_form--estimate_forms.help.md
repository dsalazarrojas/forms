# Yoga Instructor Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Instructor Estimate Form is a tool used to provide an estimate of services and rates to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant information in the "Instructor" field.
2. Indicate the number of students in your class in the "Student Count" field.
3. Select the length of your session(s) in the "Session Length" field.
4. Choose the type of pricing for your session(s) in the "Pricing" field.
5. Select the location where your session(s) will take place in the "Location" field.
6. Check all the services you will be offering in the "Services" field.
7. Enter an estimated price for your session(s) in the "Estimated Price" field.
8. If you are offering a package, select the length of the package in the "Package Length" field.
9. If you are offering a package, select the price for the package in the "Package Price" field.
10. If you are offering a group session, select the number of students in your group in the "Group Count" field.
10. If applicable, enter an estimated price for your group session in the "Estimated Price Group" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Info (1)**: Enter a brief description of your form to provide context to the client.
* **Instructor (2)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Student Count (3)**: Enter the number of students in your class.
* **Session Length (4)**: Choose the length of your session(s).
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 45 minutes
	+ 60 minutes
	+ 90 minutes
* **Pricing (5)**: Choose the type of pricing for your session(s).
	+ Private Session
	+ Package (multiple sessions)
	+ Group Class
* **Location (6)**: Choose the location where your session(s) will take place.
	+ Studio
	+ Home
	+ Online
* **Services (7)**: Check all the services you will be offering in addition to yoga.
	+ Meditation
	+ Breathwork
* **Estimated Price (8)**: Enter an estimated price for your session(s).
* **Package Length (9)**: If offering a package, choose the length of the package.
	+ 4 sessions
	+ 8 sessions
	+ 12 sessions
* **Package Price (10)**: If offering a package, enter the price for the package.
	+ $120
	+ $180
	+ $240
* **Group Count (11)**: If offering a group session, choose the number of students in your group.
	+ 1-2 students
	+ 3-4 students
	+ 5-10 students
	+ More than 10 students
* **Estimated Price Group (12)**: If applicable, enter an estimated price for your group session.
* **Submit (13)**: Enter any final comments or instructions for the client.
