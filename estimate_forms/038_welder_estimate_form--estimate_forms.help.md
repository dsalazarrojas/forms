# Welder Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a comprehensive tool for welders to estimate the cost and details of their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your phone number and email address for easy communication.
3. Select the estimate date for the project.
4. Enter the start and end times for the project (if applicable).
5. Briefly describe the project in the "description" field.
6. Enter your name and address for reference.
7. Enter the project name, which is a required field.
8. Enter the total cost, labor cost, equipment rental cost, and deposit required (if applicable) in their respective fields.
9. Select the labor rate, equipment rate, material rate, and payment terms for the project.
10. Finally, enter the labor hours, equipment hours, material hours, and other estimated costs for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Estimate Date**: Select the date for the project estimate.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for the project (if applicable).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time for the project (if applicable).
* **Description**: Briefly describe the project.
* **Welder Name**: Enter your name for reference.
* **Location**: Enter your address for reference.
* **Project Name**: Enter the project name, which is a required field.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the project.
* **Deposit Required**: Enter the deposit required (if applicable).
* **Labor Cost**: Enter the labor cost of the project.
* **Material Cost**: Enter the material cost of the project.
* **Other Cost**: Enter any other estimated costs for the project.
* **Equipment Rental**: Enter the equipment rental cost of the project.
* **Labor Rate**: Select the labor rate for the project.
* **Equipment Rate**: Select the equipment rate for the project.
* **Material Rate**: Select the material rate for the project.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms for the project.
* **Labor Hours**: Enter the labor hours for the project.
* **Equipment Hours**: Enter the equipment hours for the project.
* **Material Hours**: Enter the material hours for the project.
