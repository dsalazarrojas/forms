# ecommercemarketingconsultantestimateform - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ecommercemarketingconsultantestimateform is used to gather information about the services offered by an e-commerce marketing consultant, including estimated costs and hours required for each service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the services offered by the consultant and choose the services that apply to your project.
2. Estimate the cost and hours required for each selected service using the provided fields.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, especially the services offered and estimated hours fields, as they are required for a proper estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What Services Are Offered (`services_offered`)
* **What Services Are Offered** (`services_offered`, `text`, required): List the services offered by the consultant, separated by commas.

### 2. Service Estimate (`service_estimate`)
* **Service Estimate** (`service_estimate`, `number`, required): Estimate the cost of the selected service, in currency units (e.g., dollars).

### 3. Estimated Hours (`estimated_hours`)
* **Estimated Hours** (`estimated_hours`, `number`, required): Estimate the number of hours required to complete the selected service.

### 4. Rate Per Hour (`rate_per_hour`)
* **Rate Per Hour** (`rate_per_hour`, `text`, required): Enter the rate per hour for the selected service.

### 5. Estimated Cost (`estimated_cost`)
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, `number`, required): Calculate the estimated cost by multiplying the estimated hours by the rate per hour.

### 6. Hours Range (`estimated_hours_range`)
* **Hours Range** (`estimated_hours_range`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from two options: Estimated Hours or Other. If Other, please specify the range of hours required.

### 7-10. Service Estimates 2-9 (`service_estimate_2`-`service_estimate_9`)
* **Service Estimate 2** (`service_estimate_2`, `number`, optional): Estimate the cost of the second service, in currency units (e.g., dollars).
* **Service Estimate 3** (`service_estimate_3`, `text`, optional): Estimate the cost of the third service, in currency units (e.g., dollars).
* **Service Estimate 4** (`service_estimate_4`, `text`, optional): Estimate the cost of the fourth service, in currency units (e.g., dollars).
