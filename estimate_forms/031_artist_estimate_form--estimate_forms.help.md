# Artist Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Estimate Form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their artistic project. This form helps artists understand the client's expectations, requirements, and budget constraints, ensuring a smooth collaboration and successful project delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the project, including the title and any relevant context or reference materials.
3. Specify the type of artistic work you want to commission.
4. Describe the project's size or scope.
5. Indicate the complexity level of the project.
6. If specific materials are required, mention them.
7. Select a desired start and completion date for the project.
8. Choose your preferred payment terms and budget range.
9. If required, specify any special requirements or constraints.
10. Finally, provide any additional information or questions you have regarding the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name to ensure accurate communication.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Provide the primary contact email for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's contact number for timely communication.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Describe the name or title of the project.
* **Detailed Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a thorough description of the project, including any relevant context or reference materials.
* **Type of Artistic Work** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the list of artistic work types (e.g., Custom Painting, Portrait or Sculpture, etc.) to help the artist understand the project's nature.
* **Project Size or Scale** (`project_size`, text, required): Describe the project's size or scope to help the artist estimate the project's complexity.
* **Complexity Level** (`complexity_level`, select_one, required): Select from the list of complexity levels (e.g., Simple/Basic, Moderate, Complex, etc.) to help the artist gauge the project's demands.
* **Specific Materials Required** (`materials_specified`, select_one, required): Choose from the options to indicate whether the client wants specific materials or if the artist has free reign.
* **Desired Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Specify the date when you'd like the project to start.
* **Desired Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, required): Indicate the date by which you'd like the project to be completed.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, select_one, optional): Select from the options to indicate how flexible you are with the deadline.
* **Client Budget Range (GBP)** (`budget_range`, text, optional): Provide an estimate of your budget range to help the artist gauge their expectations.
* **Preferred Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Choose from the options to indicate how you'd like to pay for the project (e.g., full payment upfront, 50% deposit, etc.).
* **Number of Revisions Included** (`revision_allowance`, select_one, optional): Indicate the number of revisions you're willing to include in the project.
* **Reference Materials or Inspiration** (`reference_materials`, text, optional): Provide any images, samples, or inspiration materials to help the artist understand your vision.
* **Special Requirements or Constraints** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): List any specific requirements or constraints the artist should be aware of.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any other relevant details or questions you may have regarding the project.
