## Heavy Equipment Operator Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact, project, equipment, pricing, payment, attachments, terms and conditions, and additional information related to heavy equipment operator estimates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form by entering your contact information, project details, equipment specifics, pricing information, payment terms, attach any relevant documents, and agree to the terms and conditions.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields (although some fields are optional, they are still important for a complete understanding of your needs).
*   If you have any additional information or attachments that are not included in the provided fields, please include them in the "Additional Information" field.
*   Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, Optional):
    This field is for your contact information. Please enter your name, title, company, and any other relevant details.
*   **Project Information** (`project_info`, `text`, Optional):
    Describe the project you are interested in, including the type of project, location, and any other relevant details.
*   **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`, `text`, Optional):
    Provide details about the equipment you will be operating, including its type, age, and condition.
*   **Pricing Information** (`pricing_info`, `text`, Optional):
    Include any relevant pricing information, such as costs, rates, or estimates.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, Optional):
    Specify the payment terms and conditions for the project, including payment schedules and methods.
*   **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, Optional):
    Upload or reference any relevant documents or files that support your estimate, such as blueprints or certifications.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, Optional):
    Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the project, including any warranties, liabilities, and obligations.
*   **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, Optional):
    Provide any other relevant details or comments about the project that are not included in the other fields.
*   **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, Optional):
    Include any supporting documents or files for the estimate, such as contracts or permits.
