# Good Faith Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Good Faith Estimate Request form, which is used to provide essential information related to a service request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Good Faith Estimate Request form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form with the required information. Make sure to double-check your details before submitting.
* For fields marked as required, ensure you provide accurate and complete answers.
* If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client whose service request this is related to.
* **Good Faith Estimate** (`good_faith_estimate`, number, required): Enter the good faith estimate amount.
* **Estimate Date** (`estimate_date`, date, required): Select the date of the estimate.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the service requested.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service requested from the options provided (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, optional): Enter the service address, if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the client or the person to be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, text, optional): Provide any relevant service status information.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, optional): Enter the company name if you are representing a company.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Job Number** (`submitter_job_number`, text, optional): Enter your job number.
* **Submitter Message** (`submitter_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback.
* **Submitter Agreed** (`submitter_agreed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have agreed to the terms of the good faith estimate request.
* **Submitter Agreed Date** (`submitter_agreed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you agreed to the terms.
* **Itemized Estimate** (`itemized_estimate`, text, required): Enter an itemized estimate of the good faith estimate request.
* **Service Terms** (`service_terms`, text, optional): Provide any service terms or conditions.
* **Billing Terms** (`billing_terms`, text, optional): Enter any billing terms or conditions.
* **Submitter Terms** (`submitter_terms`, text, optional): Enter any terms or conditions as the submitter.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more complete submission.
