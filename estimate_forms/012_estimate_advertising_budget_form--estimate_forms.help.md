# Estimate Advertising Budget Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect estimates of advertising budgets for various projects. It helps users to provide a rough estimate of the advertising costs for a particular project, including any additional details they might want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the form as per your project requirements**. The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your needs.
2. **Enter a brief description of your project** in the "estimate_advertising_budget_form" field. This will help us understand the context of your estimate.
3. **Check the box to indicate if you have any additional details** about the project in the "wants_details" field. If you do, you can enter them in the text field below.
4. **Provide an estimate of the advertising budget** for your project in the "estimated_budget" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **estimate_advertising_budget_form** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project. This will help us understand the context of your estimate.
* **Wants Details** (text, optional): Check this box if you have any additional details about your project that you'd like to provide. If you checked this box, please enter the details in the field below.
* **estimate_advertising_budget_form_details** (text, optional): If you checked the "Wants Details" box, enter any additional information about your project here.
* **Estimated Budget** (number, optional): Enter an estimate of the advertising budget for your project.
