# Hybrid Battery Maintenance Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and estimate the cost of hybrid battery maintenance for a vehicle. It helps technicians and service managers to efficiently manage and prioritize repair requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Enter the customer's name and vehicle details, such as vehicle year, model, and VIN.
3. Describe the issue with the hybrid battery system.
4. Provide estimated repair dates and details about the technician assigned to the repair.
5. Fill out the cost estimate, labor cost, labor hours, and total cost fields with the estimated values.
6. Set the issue priority, status, and repair status accordingly.
7. Add any additional comments or shop notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account or contact information.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model (if known).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Battery Type** (`battery_type`, text, required): Enter the type of hybrid battery system in the vehicle.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue with the hybrid battery system.
* **Estimated Repair Date** (`estimate_date`, date, required): Enter the estimated date for the repair.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned technician.
* **Shop Number** (`shop_number`, text, required): Enter the shop number or repair shop ID.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the shop or contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the shop or contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the shop or contact person.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Shop Note** (`shop_note`, text, optional): Add any additional shop notes or comments.
* **Shop Phone** (`shop_phone`, text, optional): Enter the shop phone number.
* **Shop Email** (`shop_email`, text, optional): Enter the shop email address.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, text, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High).
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the issue (Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Repair Status** (`repair_status`, select_one, required): Select the current repair status (Pending, In Progress, Completed).
* **Cost Estimate** (`cost_estimate`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the repair or maintenance.
* **Labor Cost** (`labor_cost`, number, required): Enter the labor cost for the repair or maintenance.
* **Labor Hours** (`labor_hours`, number, required): Enter the estimated labor hours required for the repair or maintenance.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the repair or maintenance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid any errors or miscommunication.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended to contact the contact person or shop before proceeding.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties without approval.
