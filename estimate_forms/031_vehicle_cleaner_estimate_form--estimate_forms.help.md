# Vehicle Cleaner Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vehicle Cleaner Estimate Form" is a tool used to estimate the cost and services offered for various vehicle types. It helps vehicle cleaners and owners accurately determine the cleaning needs and costs for their vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of vehicle you are estimating for (e.g., Car, Truck, SUV, Van).
2. Choose the services you would like to include in the estimate (e.g., Basic Wash, Advanced Wash, Full Detail, Interior Clean, Exterior Clean).
3. Estimate the labor hours required for the services selected.
4. Enter the labor rate per hour (e.g., $X.XX per hour).
5. Calculate the total estimated cost by multiplying the labor hours by the labor rate.
6. Choose the payment terms (e.g., Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
7. Provide any special requests or additional information for the estimate.
8. Enter the payment terms details (e.g., payment method, due date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type (id: 2)**: Select the type of vehicle you are estimating for (e.g., Car, Truck, SUV, Van).
* **Services (id: 3)**: Choose the services you would like to include in the estimate (e.g., Basic Wash, Advanced Wash, Full Detail, Interior Clean, Exterior Clean).
* **Labor Hours (id: 4)**: Estimate the labor hours required for the services selected.
* **Labor Rate (id: 5)**: Enter the labor rate per hour (e.g., $X.XX per hour).
* **Total Cost (id: 6)**: The total estimated cost is automatically calculated by multiplying the labor hours by the labor rate.
* **Payment Terms (id: 7)**: Choose the payment terms (e.g., Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Special Requests (id: 8)**: Provide any special requests or additional information for the estimate.
* **Payment Terms Details (id: 9)**: Enter the payment terms details (e.g., payment method, due date).
