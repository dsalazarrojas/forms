# Project Estimate And Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for a project's estimate and service agreement. It's meant to outline the scope, timeline, and costs associated with the project, ensuring both parties are aware of the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Project Estimate And Service Agreement Form" to start entering your project's details.
2. Click on "Estimate Details" to add the project's cost and timeline information.
3. Click on "Service Agreement" to outline the agreement's terms and conditions.
4. Click on "Service Agreement Terms" to add any additional terms and conditions you'd like to include.
5. Review your filled-out form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Estimate And Service Agreement Form** (`Project Estimate And Service Agreement Form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Estimate Details** (`estimate_details`, text, required: false): Enter the estimated cost and timeline for the project.
* **Service Agreement** (`service_agreement`, text, required: false): Describe the service agreement, including any terms and conditions.
* **Service Agreement Terms** (`service_agreement_terms`, text, required: false): Add any additional terms and conditions not covered in the main service agreement.
