# website_maintenance_cost_estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_maintenance_cost_estimator is a form designed to help non-technical users estimate the potential maintenance needs and associated costs of a website. It is intended for users who are responsible for website maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your user information in the "User Information" field.
2.  Provide details about your website in the "Website Information" field.
3.  Select the maintenance needs for your website in the "Maintenance Needs" field.
4.  Enter any relevant budget details in the "Budget Details" field.
5.  Enter an estimated cost for your website's maintenance needs.
6.  Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you to discuss your maintenance needs.
* **Website Information (2)**: Provide a brief description of the website you are responsible for, including its name and any relevant details about its maintenance needs.
* **Maintenance Needs (3)**: Choose the maintenance needs that apply to your website from the provided options (Maintenance, Updates, Fixes).
* **Budget Details (4)**: Enter any additional information that may affect the maintenance cost estimation, such as budget constraints or special requirements.
* **Estimated Cost (5)**: Enter a numerical value for the estimated cost of your website's maintenance needs.
* **Submit (6)**: Click to send the form for review and estimation of the website's maintenance cost.
