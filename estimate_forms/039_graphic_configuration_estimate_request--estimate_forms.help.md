# Graphic Configuration Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Configuration Estimate Request form is a critical tool for clients to provide essential details about their project requirements. It helps us in preparing an accurate and reliable estimate for the project's graphic configuration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the **Graphic Configuration Estimate Request** field.
2. Provide your name in the **Client Name** field.
3. Enter your project name in the **Project Name** field.
4. Add a brief description of your project in the **Description** field.
5. Enter your expected deadline for the project in the **Deadline** field.
6. Enter your budget for the project in the **Budget** field.
7. Select the agency responsible for your project from the **Agency** dropdown menu (if applicable).
8. Select the freelancers or in-house team members involved with your project from the **Freelancer** and **In-house** dropdown menus (if applicable).
9. Enter your contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
10. Enter your contact email in the **Contact Email** field (if applicable).
11. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field (if applicable).
12. Add any additional notes about your project in the **Notes** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graphic Configuration Estimate Request** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your project.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter your project name.
- **Description** (`description`, text, required): Add a brief description of your project.
- **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Enter your expected deadline for the project.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, required): Enter your budget for the project.
- **Agency** (`agency`, select_one, optional): Select one or more agencies responsible for your project.
- **Freelancer** (`freelancer`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more freelancers or in-house team members involved with your project.
- **In-house** (`in_house`, select_one, optional): Select one or more in-house team members involved with your project.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your contact person's name.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your project.
- **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Confirm your submission.
- **Cancel** (`form_cancel`, text, optional): Cancel the form submission.
