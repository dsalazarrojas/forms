# home_value_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home value estimate form is used to collect information from users to estimate the value of a house. This form is designed to provide an estimated value of the house, which can be helpful for various purposes such as determining property taxes, assessing property values for resale or purchase, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Address** where the house is located.
3. Provide an **Estimated Value** for the house.
4. Enter the **Date Viewed Property** if you have viewed the property recently.
5. Add any **Notes** if you have any relevant information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the house you are estimating the value for, including the street number and any additional details.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, required): Provide an estimated value of the house, which can include any relevant details such as the number of bedrooms or square footage.
* **Date Viewed Property** (`date_viewed_property`, `date`, required): Enter the date you viewed the property, which can be helpful for keeping track of when you last viewed the property.
* **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the real estate agent if you worked with one, but this is not required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or information that may be relevant to the estimation of the house value.
