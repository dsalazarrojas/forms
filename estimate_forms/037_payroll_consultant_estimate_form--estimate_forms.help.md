# Payroll Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Consultant Estimate Form is used to provide an estimate to a client of the costs associated with a particular consulting project. This form is intended to be used by consultants to submit their estimates to the payroll department for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the Consultant Information (page 1)
2. Enter the Client Information (page 2)
3. Select the services offered and provide any additional details (page 3)
4. Enter the labor costs, tax rates, and other costs associated with the project (pages 4-5)
5. Select the payment terms and method (pages 6-7)
6. Add any payment terms notes and enter the billing information (pages 8-9)
7. Sign and date the form, both as the consultant and client

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, company, and contact information as the consultant.
* **Client Information (Page 2)**: Enter the client's name, company, and contact information.
* **Services Offered (Page 3)**: Select all the services you will be offering to the client and provide any additional details in the service details field. You can select multiple services.
* **Service Details (Page 4)**: Provide any additional details about the services you selected on the previous page.
* **Labor Costs (Page 5)**: Enter the labor costs associated with the project.
* **Tax Rates (Page 5)**: Enter the tax rates associated with the project.
* **Other Costs (Page 5)**: Enter any other costs associated with the project.
* **Total Estimate (Page 6)**: Enter the total estimate of the project.
* **Payment Terms (Page 7)**: Select how you would like to be paid (Net 10% of Estimate, Net 20% of Estimate, Gross, or Other (Specify)).
* **Payment Method (Page 7)**: Select the method of payment you would like to use (Check, Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT), or Other (Specify)).
* **Payment Terms Notes (Page 8)**: Add any additional notes about the payment terms.
* **Billing Information (Page 8)**: Enter any billing information for the project.
* **Consultant Signature (Page 9)**: Sign and date the form as the consultant.
* **Client Signature (Page 9)**: Sign and date the form as the client.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the payroll department.
