# Animal Nutritionist Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Nutritionist Estimate Form is used to collect information about clients' services and estimated costs. It helps the nutritionist to provide accurate estimates and manage client expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's full name in the **Client Full Name** field.
2. Provide the animal's name and species in the **Animal Name and Species** field.
3. Enter the initial consultation fee in the **Initial Consultation Fee** field.
4. Enter the custom meal plan development fee in the **Custom Meal Plan Development** field.
5. If applicable, enter the recommended supplements or specialty foods fee in the **Recommended Supplements or Specialty Foods** field.
6. Enter the follow-up session rate in the **Follow-up Session Rate - per session** field.
7. Enter the total estimated cost for the services in the **Total Estimated Cost** field.
8. Specify the estimate's validity period in the **Estimate Validity Period** field (e.g., 30 Days).
9. Optionally, add any notes on the proposed services in the **Notes on Proposed Services** field.
10. Select one of the options in the **Decision on Estimate** field to indicate whether to accept or modify the estimate, or decline it.
11. Finally, enter the date of the decision in the **Date of Decision** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the client, as it appears on their records.
* **Animal Name and Species** (`animal_name_species`, text, required): Provide the name and species of the animal (e.g., Bella - Dog).
* **Initial Consultation Fee** (`consultation_fee`, number, required): Enter the initial fee for the consultation.
* **Custom Meal Plan Development** (`meal_plan_fee`, number, required): Enter the fee for creating a customized meal plan for the client's animal.
* **Recommended Supplements or Specialty Foods** (`supplements_fee`, number, optional): If recommended supplements or foods are required, enter the associated fee. Note: This field is optional.
* **Follow-up Session Rate - per session** (`follow_up_rate`, number, required): Enter the rate for follow-up sessions per session.
* **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`, number, required): Enter the total estimated cost for the services.
* **Estimate Validity Period** (`estimate_validity`, text, required): Specify the period for which the estimate is valid (e.g., 30 Days).
* **Notes on Proposed Services** (`services_notes`, text, optional): Optionally, add any notes on the services proposed for the client.
* **Decision on Estimate** (`client_decision`, select_one, required): Select whether to accept, modify, or decline the estimate.
* **Date of Decision** (`decision_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the decision.
