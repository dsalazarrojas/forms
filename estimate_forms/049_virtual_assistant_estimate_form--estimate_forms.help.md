# Virtual Assistant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Estimate Form is designed to help users estimate the time and costs for projects that require virtual assistant services. It is intended to be a quick and efficient tool to reduce the risk of under or over-estimation and enable more accurate project planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the project in the **Project Description** field.
2. Select the type of client you are working with from the **Client Type** dropdown menu.
3. Choose the services you will be offering from the **Services Offered** dropdown menu (select multiple as needed).
4. Enter your hourly rate in the **Hourly Rate** field.
5. Estimate the number of hours you expect to work on the project in the **Estimated Hours** field.
6. Select the project duration from the dropdown menu in the **Project Duration** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Virtual Assistant Agency: A client who provides virtual assistant services to others.
	+ Business Coach or Consultant: A client who provides coaching or consulting services.
	+ Marketing and Design Freelancer: A client who provides marketing and design services.
	+ Virtual Assistant Network or Community: A network or community of virtual assistants.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Virtual Assistant Services: Tasks such as email management, calendar organization, etc.
	+ Marketing and Design Services: Services such as branding, marketing strategy, etc.
	+ Project Management: Services such as project planning, execution, and management.
	+ Consulting: Services such as consulting, coaching, or mentoring.
* **Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`, number, optional): Enter your hourly rate (e.g., $25-$50).
* **Estimated Hours** (`estimated_hours`, number, optional): Estimate the number of hours you expect to work on the project.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, select_one, optional):
	+ Less than 1 hour
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-5 hours
	+ 5-10 hours
	+ More than 10 hours
