# Room Addition Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for a room addition cost estimate. It is used to gather information about your project to provide an accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the fields as follows:
- Start by entering your main contact information, including your name and contact details.
- Provide your main address, which is crucial for the estimation process.
- Enter the room details, such as its type and square feet.
- Indicate the type of addition you are requesting (single story, two story, or two and half story).
- Enter your budget for the project.
- Provide a brief project description.
- Select the project status (not started, in progress, or completed).
- If necessary, enter any notes about the project.
- Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- You can also provide additional contact details such as address and other contact methods.
- Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Address** (ID: 2, required: true): Enter your main address where the room addition will take place.
* **Project Description** (ID: 7, required: true): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Status** (ID: 10, required: true): Select the status of your project from the options provided.
* **Contact Person** (ID: 8, required: true): Enter your name and title to ensure we have the correct contact person for the project.
* **Contact Details** (ID: 9, required: false): Select how you want to be contacted (phone, email, or address).
* **Project ID** (ID: 21, required: true): Select one of the project IDs provided to associate with your request.
* **Submit** (ID: 16, required: false): Review and confirm your form submission.
* **Confirm Submit** (ID: 20, required: false): Double-check your submission before confirming. 

Note: Fields not marked as required are optional but recommended for more accurate project evaluation.
