# Packing Quantity Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Quantity Estimator form is a tool designed to assist internal staff in estimating the quantity of items to be packed for a move. It is intended to simplify the planning process and ensure accurate quantities are provided to relevant teams and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the relevant move details.
2.  Select the applicable inventory lists.
3.  Specify packing preferences.
4.  Enter the desired packing quantity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **move_details**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the move, which can include the type of items being moved, the destination location, and any specific requirements or considerations for the packing process.
*   **inventory_lists**: This field presents a list of available inventory options. Select the specific lists that apply to the move being planned, as these will influence the packing quantity.
*   **packing_preferences**: Enter any specific packing preferences or requirements that need to be considered for the move.
*   **packing_quantity**: Enter the estimated quantity of items to be packed for the move, which will be calculated based on the selected inventory lists and packing preferences.

## Tips
*   Be accurate when entering move details to ensure the form provides the most relevant and useful information.
*   Select all applicable inventory lists to ensure the form takes into account all relevant items.
*   Clearly specify any special packing requirements to help guide the packing process.
*   Adjust the estimated packing quantity based on the selected inventory and preferences.
