# Patient Advocacy Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess patient advocacy training programs to ensure they are meeting specific goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Advocacy Training Course**: Provide a brief description of the patient advocacy training course.
2. **Training Date**: Enter the date when the training took place.
3. **Course Length**: Enter the length of the course in hours.
4. **Learning Objectives**: Describe the learning objectives of the course.
5. **Course Outcome**: Select the outcome of the course (Patient Advocate, Trainer, or Other).
6. **Patient Advocacy Training Evaluation Form**: Select the areas where the training form has been applied (Patient Advocate, Trainer, or Other).
7. **Training Environment**: Choose if the training environment was conducted (Yes or No).
8. **Course Instructor**: Provide the name of the course instructor.
9. **Feedback**: Offer any feedback you may have regarding the training course.
10. **Comments**: Enter any comments about the training course.
11. **Submission**: Provide a submission status for the form.
12. **Email**: Enter your email address.
13. **Form Submissions**: Select the number of submissions (One, Two, Three, Four, or Five).
14. **Form Completion**: Choose if the form is complete (Complete, Incomplete, or Partially Complete).
15. **Form Review**: Select if the form has been reviewed (Reviewed, Not Reviewed).
16. **Form Approval**: Choose the form approval status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
17. **Submission Status**: Select the submission status (Active, Inactive).
18. **Submission Timestamp**: Enter the date of submission.
19. **Submission Datetime**: Enter the date and time of submission.
20. **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
21. **Submission Timezone**: Enter the time zone of submission.
22. **Submission Timezone Offset**: Choose if the submission time zone is offset (Yes or No).
23. **Submission Timezone Offset Minutes**: Enter the time zone offset minutes.
24. **Submission UTC Offset**: Choose if the submission is in UTC (Yes or No).
25. **Submission UTC Offset Hours**: Enter the UTC offset hours.
