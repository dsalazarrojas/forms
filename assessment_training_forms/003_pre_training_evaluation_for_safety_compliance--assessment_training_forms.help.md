# Pre-Training Evaluation For Safety Compliance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on pre-training evaluations for safety compliance. It aims to assess the effectiveness of training programs provided to employees in various settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each field.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for any field where unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (id: 1)
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief note to introduce the training session. Please provide a short description of the training.

### Training Program (id: 2)
* **Training Program** (`Training Program`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of training program that was provided to employees. You can select one of the following:
	+ Safety
	+ Other

### If Other, please specify (id: 3)
* **If Other, please specify** (`If Other, please specify`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" for the training program, please provide a brief description of the training program.

### Training Location (id: 4)
* **Training Location** (`Training Location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose where the training was held:
	+ In-person
	+ Online

### Date (id: 5)
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session.

### Training Hours (id: 6)
* **Training Hours** (`Training Hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours the training session lasted.

### If other, please specify (id: 7)
* **If other, please specify** (`If other, please specify`, `text`, optional): If you want to specify the training hours, please provide a brief explanation.

### Trainer (id: 8)
* **Trainer** (`Trainer`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the name of the trainer:
	+ Jim
	+ Other

### If Other, please specify (id: 9)
* **If Other, please specify** (`If Other, please specify`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" for the trainer, please provide a brief description.

### Trainees (id: 10)
* **Trainees** (`Trainees`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how many trainees attended the training session:
	+ 1
	+ 2-5
	+ 5+

### Training Date (id: 11)
* **Training Date** (`Training Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session.

### Training Duration (id: 12)
* **Training Duration** (`Training Duration`, `time`, optional): Enter the duration of the training session.

### Training Outcome (id: 13)
* **Training Outcome** (`Training Outcome`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how satisfied the trainees are with the training:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ No Opinion

### Evaluation (id: 14)
* **Evaluation** (`Evaluation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how satisfied the trainees are with the evaluation:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ No Opinion

### Evaluation Date (id: 15)
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.

### Evaluation Duration (id: 16)
* **Evaluation Duration** (`Evaluation Duration`, `time`, optional): Enter the duration of the evaluation.

### Safety Equipment Used (id: 17)
* **Safety Equipment Used** (`Safety Equipment Used`, `text`, optional): Enter any safety equipment used during the training session.

### Comments (id: 18)
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the training session.

### If comments are not complete, please specify (id: 19)
* **If comments are not complete, please specify** (`If comments are not complete, please specify`, `text`, optional): If the comments are not complete, please provide a brief explanation.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If unsure about any field, provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the relevant authorities.
* Duplicate evaluations will be discarded and will not affect the data collection.
