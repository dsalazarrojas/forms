# Clairvoyant Training Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Clairvoyant Training Program Application form. Please review each section carefully to ensure you provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: full name, email address, years of relevant experience, areas of interest, weekly time commitment, and motivation statement.
2. If applicable, provide your phone number and date of birth for eligibility screening purposes.
3. List any previous training or certifications you have.
4. Select the areas you are most interested in within the program.
5. Check if you have a professional reference or mentor who can vouch for your skills and character.
6. Finally, confirm that you agree to the program's terms and code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid contact email for communications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): For eligibility screening purposes, but not necessarily required.
* **Years of experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): The number of years you have been practicing mediumship or related fields.
* **Previous training or certifications** (`training_background`, `text`, optional): List any formal training programs or certifications you have completed.
* **Areas of interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three areas of interest within the program (Intuitive development, Energy healing, Ethical practice and boundaries, Business development, Psychic research methods).
* **Weekly time commitment** (`availability_commitment`, `number`, required): Approximate hours per week you are available for coursework.
* **Motivation statement** (`motivation_statement`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your goals and expectations for the program.
* **Professional reference contact** (`reference_contact`, `text`, optional): Name and relationship of a professional reference or mentor.
* **Agreement to terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you will follow the program's guidelines and code of conduct.
