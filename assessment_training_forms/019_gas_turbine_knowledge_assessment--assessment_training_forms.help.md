# Gas Turbine Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess participants' knowledge on gas turbines. It collects information from participants to evaluate their understanding of gas turbine principles and applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select the type of turbine that best describes your work or area of expertise. This field will help us understand your context and provide relevant questions.
3. Choose the type(s) of fuel you typically work with. This will help us understand your specific experience.
4. Indicate your turbine's operating principle. This will help us evaluate your knowledge on axial, radial, or mixed flow turbines.
5. Provide the speed range of your turbine in text format. This field is intended for you to describe your experience with specific speed ranges.
6. Enter the total number of hours you've worked with gas turbines. This helps us understand your level of experience.
7. Input the temperature range of your turbine's operation. Describe your experience with specific temperature ranges.
8. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide to support your answers.
9. Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication, if needed.
10. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
11. Use the "Notes" field to add any extra comments or explanations, if necessary.
12. Finally, select the submission date when you finish filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required/optional)
 Enter your name to identify yourself.

### Turbine Type (`turbine_type`, select_one, optional)
Select the type of turbine that best describes your work or area of expertise. This could be Axial, Radial, or Mixed.

### Fuel Type (`fuel_type`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose the type(s) of fuel you typically work with. This could include Gas, Diesel, or Oil.

### Operating Principle (`operating_principle`, select_one, optional)
Indicate the operating principle of your turbine. This helps us understand your knowledge on axial, radial, or mixed flow turbines.

### Speed Range (`speed_range`, text, optional)
Describe the speed range of your experience with gas turbines. You can provide specific speeds if possible.

### Hours of Operation (`hours_operation`, time, optional)
Enter the total number of hours you've worked with gas turbines. This helps us evaluate your experience level.

### Temperature Range (`temperature_range`, text, optional)
Provide the temperature range of your experience with gas turbines. You can provide specific temperatures if possible.

### Notes (`notes`, note, optional)
Add any extra comments or explanations to support your answers.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication, if needed.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
Provide your phone number for easy contact.

### Comments (`comments`, note, optional)
Use this field to add any extra comments or explanations, if necessary.

### Submission Date (`submission_date`, date, optional)
Select the date when you finish filling out the form.
