# Hospital AI Training Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from users who have participated in the Hospital AI Training Initiative. The purpose of this form is to gauge the user's experience, provide additional comments, and collect contact information for follow-up communication. Please fill out this form to help us improve the Hospital AI Training Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill out the form by typing your answers to each question.
* You can type up to 3 pages of comments.
* Provide your email address so we can contact you for further clarification.
* Enter your phone number (if available).
* Rate your experience with the provided rating scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Form**: Answer to the evaluation form questions on Page 1. Please provide your thoughts and opinions on your experience with the Hospital AI Training Initiative.
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide additional comments about your experience, including any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments 2**: This is another opportunity to provide additional comments about your experience. Please feel free to share any further feedback or suggestions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if available) so we can contact you by phone if needed.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the Hospital AI Training Initiative using the provided rating scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
