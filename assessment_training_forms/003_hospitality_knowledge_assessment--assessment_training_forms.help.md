# Hospitality Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospitality Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of hospitality staff in various areas, including hotel operations, staff skills, emergency protocols, and fire safety procedures. This assessment helps to ensure that staff are competent in their roles and can perform their duties effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the correct answer from the provided options.
3. Ensure that you have completed all required fields and that your answers are accurate.
4. Once completed, submit the form to ensure that your knowledge and skills are up to date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Operations** (`page_1`, text, not required): This section assesses your knowledge of hotel operations. Answer this question in a few sentences, providing examples or details about your experience and knowledge in this area.
* **Staff Skills Assessment** (`staff_skills_assessment`, select_one, not required): Choose the option that best describes your level of proficiency in staff skills:
	+ Good: You are proficient in staff skills and can perform your job duties effectively.
	+ Fair: You have some knowledge, but you need more training or practice to improve.
	+ Needs Improvement: You lack proficiency in staff skills and require significant training or practice.
* **Emergency Protocols** (`emergency_protocols`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last time you reviewed emergency protocols or received training on emergency procedures.
* **First Aid Training** (`first_aid_training`, select_multiple, not required): Check all the boxes that apply to indicate whether you have received training in first aid:
	+ True: You have received training in first aid.
	+ False: You have not received training in first aid.
* **Fire Safety Training** (`fire_safety_training`, text, not required): Enter your thoughts on fire safety training, including any relevant procedures or protocols you have been trained on.
* **Food Safety Training** (`food_safety_training`, text, not required): Enter your thoughts on food safety training, including any relevant procedures or protocols you have been trained on.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, not required): Enter any emergency procedures you have been trained on or have experience with.
* **Evacuation Procedures** (`evacuation_procedures`, text, not required): Enter any evacuation procedures you have been trained on or have experience with.
* **Emergency Evacuation Procedures** (`emergency_evacuation_procedures`, text, not required): Enter any emergency evacuation procedures you have been trained on or have experience with.
* **Emergency Procedure 1** (`emergency_procedure_1`, text, not required): Enter any emergency procedure you have been trained on or have experience with.
* **First Aid Kit Check** (`first_aid_kit_check`, text, not required): Check your first aid kit regularly to ensure it is well-stocked and easily accessible.
* **First Aid Kit Supplies** (`first_aid_kit_supplies`, text, not required): Enter any first aid kit supplies you have on hand.
* **First Aid Kit Contents** (`first_aid_kit_contents`, text, not required): List the contents of your first aid kit.
* **Medical Emergency Procedure** (`medical_emergency_procedure`, text, not required): Enter any medical emergency procedure you have been trained on or have experience with.
* **Injury Reporting** (`injury_reporting`, text, not required): Report any injuries or near misses to your supervisor or HR department.
* **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, text, not required): Report any incidents or near misses to your supervisor or HR department.
* **Emergency Phone Numbers** (`emergency_phone_numbers`, text, not required): Enter any emergency phone numbers you have on hand.
* **Emergency Phone Numbers 2** (`emergency_phone_numbers_2`, text, not required): Enter any additional emergency phone numbers.
* **Fire Extinguisher Locations** (`fire_extinguisher_locations`, text, not required): Locate fire extinguishers throughout the hotel and ensure they are easily accessible.
* **Fire Evacuation Routes** (`fire_evacuation_routes`, text, not required): Ensure that all guests and staff know the emergency evacuation routes.
* **Fire Evacuation Drills** (`fire_evacuation_drills`, text, not required): Participate in regular fire evacuation drills to ensure that you are prepared in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Evacuation Protocols** (`fire_evacuation_protocols`, text, not required): Review and follow fire evacuation protocols in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Evacuation Procedure** (`fire_evacuation_procedure`, text, not required): Enter any fire evacuation procedure you have been trained on or have experience with.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you answer each question honestly and accurately.
* Review your answers with a colleague or supervisor to ensure that you have included all relevant information.
* Complete the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR department.

Note: The required fields are not required, but it's recommended to complete them as they are an essential part of the form.
