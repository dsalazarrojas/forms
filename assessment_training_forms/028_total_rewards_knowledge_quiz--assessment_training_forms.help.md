# Total Rewards Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess knowledge on total rewards, which refer to the various programs and benefits provided by companies to their employees. It is meant to be a self-assessment tool that helps employees understand their own knowledge on this topic. This form covers different aspects of total rewards, including compensation, benefits, and employee recognition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Be accurate and honest in your responses.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.
