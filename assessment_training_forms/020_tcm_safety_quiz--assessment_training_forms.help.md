# TCM Safety Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The TCM (Threat and Crisis Management) Safety Quiz is designed to assess an individual's knowledge and concerns regarding safety procedures and protocols. This quiz helps identify areas where additional training or support is needed to ensure a safe working environment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the TCM Safety Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address (if you have one).
4. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
5. Provide your birth date.
6. Answer the question about your safety knowledge.
7. Select any safety concerns you have (Physical, Energetic, Emotional, or Other).
8. Set the start time of the quiz.
9. Set the date and time you submitted the quiz.
10. Provide the location where you submitted the quiz.
11. Select your results from the quiz (Passed, Failed, Needs Improvement).
12. Answer a follow-up question if necessary.
13. Decide if a follow-up plan is needed.
14. Select if follow-up action is required (Yes, No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (<type:text>): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (<type:text>): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (<type:email>): Enter a valid email address (e.g. example@domain.com).
* **Phone number** (<type:text>): Enter a valid phone number (e.g. 123-456-7890).
* **Birth date** (<type:date>): Enter your birth date in MM/DD/YY format (e.g. 12/25/1990).
* **Safety knowledge** (<type:select_one>): Answer whether you have safety knowledge (Yes or No).
* **Safety concerns** (<type:select_multiple>): Select any safety concerns you have (Physical, Energetic, Emotional, or Other).
* **Quiz start time** (<type:time>): Set the start time of the quiz in 12-hour format (e.g. 02:30).
* **Submit date** (<type:date>): Enter the date you submitted the quiz (in MM/DD/YY format).
* **Submit time** (<type:time>): Set the time you submitted the quiz in 12-hour format.
* **Submit location** (<type:text>): Enter the location where you submitted the quiz (e.g. "Office, Level 3").
* **Safety quiz results** (<type:select_multiple>): Select your results from the quiz (Passed, Failed, Needs Improvement).
* **Follow up question** (<type:text>): Answer a follow-up question if necessary.
* **Follow up plan** (<type:select_multiple>): Decide if follow-up action is needed (Yes or No).
* **Follow up date** (<type:date>): Not applicable (this field is currently not being used).
