# Six Sigma Certification Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Six Sigma Certification Exam.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with the required and optional fields as needed.
- Ensure that the fields with a star (\*) are mandatory.
- Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are a training provider, ensure that you have the permission of the candidate to collect and store their personal data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Candidate Details (optional)
**Candidate Details**: This is an optional text field where you can enter a brief description of the candidate.

### 2. candidate_name
**Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate.

### 3. candidate_email
**Candidate Email**: Enter the email address of the candidate. (Note: This field is a text field but it is intended to be an email address.)

### 4. candidate_phone
**Candidate Phone**: Enter the phone number of the candidate.

### 5. readiness_score
**Readiness Score**: Enter the readiness score of the candidate, if applicable.

### 6. certification_status
**Certification Status**: Select one of the following options:
  - Green Belt
  - Black Belt
  - Master Black Belt
  - Champion

### 7. training_provider
**Training Provider**: Enter the name of the training provider.

### 8. certification_year
**Certification Year**: Enter the year of the candidate's certification.

### 9. training_date
**Training Date**: Enter the date the candidate completed the training.

### 10. assessment_date
**Assessment Date**: Enter the date the candidate was assessed.

### 11. score_details
**Score Details**: This is an optional text field where you can enter additional information about the candidate's score.

### 12. score_1
**Score 1**: Enter the score 1 of the candidate, if applicable.

### 13. score_2
**Score 2**: Enter the score 2 of the candidate, if applicable.

### 14. score_3
**Score 3**: Enter the score 3 of the candidate, if applicable.
