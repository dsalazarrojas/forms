# inservice_training_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect responses from trainees who have completed a training session. It is meant to gauge their understanding and participation in the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your responses to the training quiz questions in the **Training Quiz Responses** field.
2. Confirm your participation by selecting the correct answer in the **Confirm Participation** field. You can choose from the options: True, False, and Partial.
3. If desired, provide additional comments or feedback in the **Review Understanding** field.
4. Select your department from the list in the **Department** field. You can choose from the options: Accounting, Administration, and HR.
5. If you have any additional comments or details about your department, you can provide them in the **Department Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Quiz Responses** (1, text, required/optional): Please answer the training quiz questions as instructed during the training session. This field is optional, but it's recommended that you provide your responses to help evaluate your understanding of the material.
* **Confirm Participation** (2, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your participation in the training session by selecting True, False, or Partial. This field is optional, but it's recommended that you provide a response to indicate your level of participation.
* **Review Understanding** (3, text, required/optional): If you have any questions or need clarification on the material, please provide your review and understanding in this field. This field is optional.
* **Department** (4, select_one, required/optional): Select the department where you are working or related to this training session. You can choose from the options: Accounting, Administration, and HR.
* **Department Details** (5, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or details about your department, please provide them in this field. This field is optional.
