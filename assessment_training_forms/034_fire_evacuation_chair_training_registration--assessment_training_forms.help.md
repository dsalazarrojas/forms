# Fire Evacuation Chair Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for fire evacuation chair training sessions. It is a mandatory requirement for staff to be trained on evacuation procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all fields marked as required (none in this form).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the relevant fields.
3. Enter a valid email address for your Emergency Contact.
4. Select your Shift Hours and Shift Days (if applicable).
5. Choose the type of training you need (Fire Evacuation Chair Training, Fire Evacuation Chair Refresher Training, or Fire Evacuation Chair Recertification Training).
6. Enter any additional notes about your training requirements.
7. Select your Staff Name and Staff Title (if applicable).
8. Enter your Staff Phone Number (if applicable).
9. Confirm that you have read and agreed to all the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter a valid email address for you, or your emergency contact.
* **Department** (Optional): Enter the department you work in.
* **Shift** (Optional): Enter your shift hours e.g. 8, 4.
* **Job Title** (Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Training Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your training in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start Time** (Optional): Enter the start time of your training in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **End Time** (Optional): Enter the end time of your training in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Location** (Optional): Enter the location of your training.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Optional): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Shift Hours** (Optional): Enter the number of hours you work per shift e.g. 8, 4.
* **Shift Days** (Optional): Select the days you work e.g. Monday, Tuesday.
* **Training Type** (Optional): Select the type of training you need e.g. Fire Evacuation Chair Training.
* **Training Location** (Optional): Enter the location of your training.
* **Training Instructor** (Optional): Enter the name of your training instructor.
* **Participant Agreement** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to all the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about your training requirements.
* **Staff Name** (Optional): Enter your staff name.
* **Staff Email** (Optional): Enter your staff email.
* **Staff Title** (Optional): Enter your staff title.
* **Staff Phone** (Optional): Enter your staff phone number.
