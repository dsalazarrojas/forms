# Advocacy Training Completion Certificate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the completion of an advocacy training course and assess participant performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant Information**: Enter your personal details, including your full name and email address. This information is used for certificate delivery and communication purposes.
2. **Training Information**: Enter the name of the training course you've completed, the date you finished, and the location where it was conducted. If applicable, provide the duration of the training program.
3. **Assessment**: Answer questions about your understanding and skills in different areas of advocacy, such as advocacy fundamentals, policy development, stakeholder engagement, communication strategies, and evaluation metrics. Evaluate your performance based on a scale provided.
4. **Overall Evaluation**: Provide an overall assessment of your performance, considering all the areas you've been evaluated on.
5. **Certificate Authority**: Enter the name and title of the person authorizing your certificate and the date it was issued. If applicable, provide the date when the certificate expires.
6. **Participant Signature**: Sign the certificate electronically, acknowledging completion of the training course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Participant Information`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (`Participant Information`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for certificate delivery and communication.
* **Organization** (`Participant Information`, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation name.
* **Job Title** (`Participant Information`, text, optional): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Training Course Name** (`Training Information`, text, required): Enter the name of the training course you've completed.
* **Training Date** (`Training Information`, date, required): Enter the date when the training was completed.
* **Training Location** (`Training Information`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training was conducted.
* **Training Duration** (`Training Information`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the training program.
* **Instructor Name** (`Training Information`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training instructor or facilitator.
* **Advocacy Fundamentals** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your understanding of basic advocacy concepts.
* **Policy Development** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your knowledge of policy development and implementation.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your ability to engage with various stakeholders.
* **Communication Strategies** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your understanding of effective communication in advocacy.
* **Evaluation Metrics** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your knowledge of measuring advocacy effectiveness.
* **Written Exam Score** (`Assessment`, number, optional): Enter your score achieved on the written examination (if applicable).
* **Practical Assessment Score** (`Assessment`, number, optional): Enter your score achieved on the practical assessment (if applicable).
* **Overall Evaluation** (`Assessment`, select_one, required): Evaluate your overall performance, considering all the areas assessed.
* **Authorized By** (`Certificate Authority`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorizing the certificate.
* **Authority Title** (`Certificate Authority`, text, required): Enter the title of the person authorizing the certificate.
* **Certificate Issue Date** (`Certificate Authority`, date, required): Enter the date when the certificate is issued.
* **Certificate Expiry Date** (`Certificate Authority`, date, optional): Enter the date when the certificate expires.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, text, required): Sign the certificate electronically, acknowledging completion of the training course.

## Tips

- Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
- If applicable, provide scores or evaluations for written and practical assessments.
- Review the overall evaluation section carefully, considering all areas assessed.
- Enter the correct name and title of the person authorizing the certificate.
- Sign the certificate electronically, indicating completion of the training course.
