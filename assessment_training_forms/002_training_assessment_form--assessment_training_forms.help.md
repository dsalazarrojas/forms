# Training Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Training Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback and assessment information from participants after they have completed a training session. This form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of the training and gather insights from both the participant and the trainer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined in the internal reasoning section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the training session.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address as a participant in the training session.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date when you attended the training session.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Course Title**: Choose the title of the course you attended.
* **Assessment Notes**: Provide any notes about your experience with the course.
* **Participant Feedback**: Share your feedback about the course.
* **Participant Comments**: Provide any comments about your experience with the course.
* **Trainer Comments**: Enter any comments from the trainer about your participation.
* **Course Outcome**: Select the outcome of the course (e.g., Outcome 1, Outcome 2, Outcome 3).
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant Location**: Enter your location (if applicable).
* **Assessment Time**: Enter the time spent on the course.
* **Participant Status**: Indicate your status as a participant (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Participant Status Notes**: Enter any notes about your status.
* **Form Assigned To**: Indicate whether the form was assigned to someone.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Submission Notes**: Enter any notes about the submission.
* **Participant Status Status**: Indicate the status of the participant (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Form Submitted By**: Indicate whether the form was submitted by you or someone else.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
