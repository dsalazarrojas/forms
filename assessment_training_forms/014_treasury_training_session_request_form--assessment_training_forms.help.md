# treasury_training_session_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treasury training session request form is designed for employees to submit a request for a treasury training session. This form gathers information about the session's specifics, such as date, time, duration, location, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown list.
4. Choose the date for the training session from the "Training Date" field.
5. Enter the start time for the session in the "Start Time" field.
6. Enter the end time for the session in the "End Time" field.
7. Select the duration of the session from the "Duration" field.
8. Choose the location for the session from the "Location" dropdown list.
9. Add any additional notes about the session in the "Notes" field.
10. Optionally, enter a form ID if you have one.
11. Select who submitted the request from the "Submitted by" dropdown list.
12. Finally, enter the date you submitted the request in the "Submit Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (2): Provide your email address for contact.
* **Department** (3): Select your department to help us know which team to direct this request to.
* **Training Date** (4): Choose the date you'd like the training to be held on.
* **Start Time** (5): Enter the time the session should start at.
* **End Time** (6): Enter the time the session should end at.
* **Duration** (7): Enter the length of the session, in minutes or hours.
* **Location** (8): Select from the available locations to hold the session.
* **Notes** (9): Add any additional notes about the session here.
* **Form Id** (10): If you have a form ID, enter it here, but it's optional.
* **Submitted by** (11): Select who submitted the request, or choose a default value.
* **Submit Date** (12): Enter the date you submitted this request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're submitting a new request if you haven't already done so. 
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to contact us. 
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, use your best judgment and make an educated guess. 
* If you need help with completing this form, don't hesitate to ask a colleague.
