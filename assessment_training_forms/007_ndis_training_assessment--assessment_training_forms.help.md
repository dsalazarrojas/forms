# NDIS Training Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess a training session, capturing details such as the training provider, session date, feedback, and outcomes. This form is designed to be completed by the training facilitator or assessor after a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
- Use the options provided for the select_multiple and select_one fields to select the most accurate answer.
- Ensure that you are completing the form for the correct training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Training Provider
**Training Provider** (`training_provider`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in the name of the training provider who delivered the session.

### Session Date
**Session Date** (`training_session_date`, `date`, required: false): Fill in the date of the training session.

### Feedback
**Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments about the training session.

### Learning Outcomes
**Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more of the options provided for the learning outcomes that were achieved during the session.

### Rating
**Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Provide a rating for the training session (e.g. 1-5).

### Training Session Attendance
**Training Session Attendance** (`training_session_attendance`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the attendance level of the trainee during the training session.

### Feedback Frequency
**Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more of the options provided for the feedback frequency.

### Training Materials Provided
**Training Materials Provided** (`training_materials_provided`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of training materials provided during the session.

### Follow Up Plan
**Follow Up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, `text`, required: false): Provide any follow-up plans or actions to be taken after the session.

### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.

### Session Goals
**Session Goals** (`session_goals`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the session goals that were set and achieved during the session.

### Training Content
**Training Content** (`training_content`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the details about the training content delivered during the session.

### Training Outcomes Measured
**Training Outcomes Measured** (`training_outcomes_measured`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the outcomes that were measured during the session.

### Training Outcomes Met
**Training Outcomes Met** (`training_outcomes_met`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more of the options provided for the training outcomes that were met during the session.

### Training Outcomes Missed
**Training Outcomes Missed** (`training_outcomes_missed`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more of the options provided for the training outcomes that were missed during the session.

### Evaluation
**Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `text`, required: false): Provide any evaluation or assessment of the session.

### Next Steps
**Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Provide any next steps or actions to be taken after the session.

### Additional Notes
**Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the session.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form fields are self-explanatory. If a field label is unclear, the explanation will attempt to clarify its intent based on the provided YAML fields.
