# Rapid Sequence Intubation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess a healthcare provider's knowledge and experience with Rapid Sequence Intubation (RSI). It helps to track RSI attempts, successes, and failures, and provides feedback on the RSI process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the number of RSI attempts made in the "RSI knowledge" field.
2. Select the correct RSIs sequence from the "RSI sequence" field.
3. Choose the type of RSI from the "RSI type" field.
4. Indicate whether the RSI was successful or not in the "RSI success" field.
5. Add any additional comments about the RSI in the "RSI comments" field.
6. Check if important information is present in the "RSI important information" field.
7. Select the next steps to be taken in the "RSI next steps" field.
8. Enter the total time taken for the RSI in the "RSI total time" field.
9. Indicate how often the RSI was successful in the "RSI total success" field.
10. Enter the total attempts made in the "RSI total attempts" field.
11. Enter the total failures made in the "RSI total failures" field.
12. Confirm that the form is complete in the "RSI form completed" field.
13. Confirm that the form has been reviewed in the "RSI form reviewed" field.
14. Select the person who completed the form in the "RSI completed by" field.
15. Select the person who reviewed the form in the "RSI reviewed by" field.
16. Select the next steps to be taken in the "RSI next steps 2" field.
17. Select the previous steps taken in the "RSI previous steps" field.
18. Select the previous steps 2 taken in the "RSI previous steps 2" field.
19. Select the person who completed the form in the "RSI completed by who" field.
20. Select the person who reviewed the form in the "RSI reviewed by who" field.
21. Add any additional comments about the RSI in the "RSI additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSI knowledge (id: 2)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how many RSI attempts you've made: I've never performed RSI, Less than 5 attempts, 5-20 attempts, 21-50 attempts, More than 50 attempts.
* **RSI sequence (id: 3)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the correct RSIs sequence: 0 - 5, 6 - 10, 11 - 15, 16 - 20.
* **RSI type (id: 4)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of RSI: Rapid sequence intubation, Non-rapid sequence intubation.
* **RSI success (id: 5)** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the RSI was successful or not: Failed, Success.
* **RSI comments (id: 6)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments about the RSI.
* **RSI important information (id: 7)** (`select_one`, optional): Check if important information is present: True, False.
* **RSI next steps (id: 8)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the next steps to be taken: Complete next step 1, Complete next step 2, Complete next step 3, Complete next step 4, Do not know.
* **RSI total time (id: 10)** (`number`, optional): Enter the total time taken for the RSI.
* **RSI total success (id: 11)** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate how often the RSI was successful: Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Sometimes, Almost always.
* **RSI total attempts (id: 12)** (`number`, optional): Enter the total attempts made for the RSI.
* **RSI total failures (id: 13)** (`number`, optional): Enter the total failures made for the RSI.
* **RSI form completed (id: 16)** (`select_one`, optional): Confirm that the form is complete: True, False.
* **RSI form reviewed (id: 17)** (`select_one`, optional): Confirm that the form has been reviewed: True, False.
* **RSI completed by (id: 18)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the person who completed the form: Name, Role, Date, Time, Comments.
* **RSI reviewed by (id: 19)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the person who reviewed the form: Name, Role, Date, Time, Comments.
* **RSI next steps 2 (id: 20)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the next steps to be taken: Next step 1, Next step 2, Do not know.
* **RSI previous steps (id: 21)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the previous steps taken: Complete next step 1, Complete next step 2, Do not know.
* **RSI previous steps 2 (id: 22)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the previous steps 2 taken: Next step 1, Next step 2, Do not know.
* **RSI completed by who (id: 23)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the person who completed the form: Name, Role, Date, Time, Comments.
* **RSI reviewed by who (id: 24)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the person who reviewed the form: Name, Role, Date, Time, Comments.
* **RSI additional comments (id: 25)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments about the RSI.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the assessment is comprehensive and reliable.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please select the "Do not know" option.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
* The form should be reviewed by another healthcare provider to ensure accuracy and completeness.
