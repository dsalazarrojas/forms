# Dog Training Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Log form is a data collection tool designed to gather information about dog training sessions. It is intended for use by trainers to record and track the details of each session, including its outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the training session using the date field.
2. Enter the location where the training session took place.
3. If applicable, enter the Dog Owner ID.
4. Specify the dog breed participating in the session.
5. Record the training time.
6. Add any relevant notes about the session.
7. Enter the name and contact phone number of the trainer.
8. Weigh and measure the dog's weight.
9. Note the dog's age.
10. Record the duration of the training session.
11. Select the outcome of the training session from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session ID**: A unique identifier for each session. (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Training Date**: Date of the training session. (Required: false, Type: date)
* **Training Location**: Location where the training session was held. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Dog Owner ID**: ID of the dog's owner (optional). (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Dog Breed**: Breed of the dog participating in the session. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Training Time**: Start and end time of the training session. (Required: false, Type: time)
* **Notes**: Any additional information about the session. (Required: false, Type: note)
* **Trainer Name**: Name of the trainer. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Trainer Phone**: Contact phone number of the trainer. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Dog Weight**: Weight of the dog. (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Dog Age**: Age of the dog. (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Training Duration**: Length of the training session. (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Session Status**: Outcome of the session (Success, Failed, In Progress). (Required: false, Type: select_one)

## Tips
* Please ensure the form is completed accurately and consistently.
* Use this form only for dog training sessions.
* Avoid duplicating entries for the same session.
* Keep the form up-to-date to maintain reliable and accurate records.
