# Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz is designed to gauge your knowledge and understanding of fall protection principles and practices. This quiz is meant to be a self-assessment tool for individuals working in industries where fall protection is a critical aspect of their work. It will help you evaluate your knowledge in areas such as fall hazard recognition, prevention, and control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill out each page of the form, which covers different aspects of fall protection knowledge.
3. Provide accurate and honest answers to the questions.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/False): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/False): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/False): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Contact Number** (`phone`, text, required/False): Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required/False): Enter your company or organization name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/False): Enter your job title or position.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/False): Enter the department you work in or are associated with.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/False): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Date of Entry** (`date_of_entry`, date, required/False): Enter the date you started working in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Height** (`height`, number, required/False): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5' 10").
- **Weight** (`weight`, number, required/False): Enter your weight in pounds (e.g., 150 lbs).
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/False): Choose one of the options provided, which may ask you to confirm or deny a fall protection practice or policy.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/False): Select all the options that apply to your knowledge or practice regarding fall protection.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required/False): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information or comments about your fall protection knowledge or experience.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/False): Enter the time it takes for you to complete the quiz, which will help us evaluate your time management skills.

Please note that the form is not mandatory, and you are free to complete it voluntarily for your own learning or review purposes.
