# Worker Skills Validation Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and validate your skills as part of the Worker Skills Validation Program. The program aims to collect information about your skills, experiences, and tools used to perform your job tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields (marked as `required`) to ensure that your registration is successfully submitted.
2. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
3. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field. Please use a valid email address that you can access.
5. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
6. Enter your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
7. Select whether you are enrolled in a skill validation program from the `skill_validation_programs` dropdown menu. If unsure, you can choose "Yes" for now.
8. Select whether you have been assigned a tool from the `assigned_tool` dropdown menu. If unsure, you can choose "Yes" for now.
9. If you have a different tool assigned, enter the name in the `assigned_tool_other` field.
10. You can enter any additional comments in the `comments` field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you can access.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Skill Validation Programs** (`skill_validation_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are enrolled in a skill validation program.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have been assigned a tool.
* **Assigned Tool (Other)** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, optional): Enter the name of a tool assigned to you if it's not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): You can enter any additional comments or information about your skills or experiences.
