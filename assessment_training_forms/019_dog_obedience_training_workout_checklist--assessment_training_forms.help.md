# Dog Obedience Training Workout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Obedience Training Workout Checklist is a form used to create a workout plan for dogs undergoing obedience training. It helps in recording the training sessions and their respective details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the session names for each of the three sessions you have planned. This could be the first, second, and third session, for example.
2. Select the training area where the sessions will take place. Choose from Front yard, Back yard, Local park, or Other.
3. Enter the date for each session.
4. Enter the time for each session.
5. If you want to record the time spent practicing, select the practice_time field.
6. Add any notes or comments for each session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_session** (text, optional): The name of the first session.
* **second_session** (text, optional): The name of the second session.
* **third_session** (text, optional): The name of the third session.
* **training_area** (select_one, required): The location where the training sessions will take place. Choose from Front yard, Back yard, Local park, or Other.
* **date** (date, required): The date of each session.
* **time** (time, required): The time of each session.
* **practice_time** (time, optional): The time spent practicing during each session.
* **note** (note, optional): Any comments or notes for each session.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct training area for each session.
- Enter the session names as accurately as possible.
- Enter the correct date and time for each session.
- If you have any additional notes or comments, use the note field to record them.
