# Organizational Training Programs Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to audit organizational training programs by collecting data on various aspects of these programs. The goal is to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the training programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the training programs that are currently available to your department or unit.
2. Choose the department that the training programs belong to.
3. Evaluate the quality of the training programs.
4. Record the date the training completions were made.
5. Indicate whether your department practices completion of trainings as part of its regular activities.
6. Add any improvement opportunities you have identified during the assessment.
7. Select the frequency at which your department reviews the training programs.
8. Choose the auditor who conducted the assessment.
9. Indicate whether the completions of the training programs are certified.
10. If certified, enter the date of the completion certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Programs**: Select the training programs that are currently available to your department or unit.
* **Department**: Choose the department that the training programs belong to.
* **Training Quality**: Evaluate the quality of the training programs, selecting from the options provided.
* **Training Completions**: Record the date the training completions were made.
* **Completion Practices**: Indicate whether your department practices completion of trainings as part of its regular activities.
* **Improvement Opportunities**: Add any improvement opportunities you have identified during the assessment.
* **Review Frequency**: Select the frequency at which your department reviews the training programs.
* **Auditor**: Choose the auditor who conducted the assessment.
* **Completion Certification**: Indicate whether the completions of the training programs are certified.
* **Certification Date**: If certified, enter the date of the completion certification.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are familiar with the training programs being audited and the relevant departments involved.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is reliable and can be used to make informed decisions about future training programs.
