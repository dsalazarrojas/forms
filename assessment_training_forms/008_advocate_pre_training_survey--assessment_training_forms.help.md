# Advocate Pre-Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Advocate Pre-Training Survey is a tool used by the training organizers to gather information about you and tailor the training content to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a new tab or window, and navigate through the sections using the provided links.
2. Each section has a specific set of questions related to your background, experience, and expectations from the training.
3. Fill out each section thoroughly, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you're unsure about any question.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send it to the training organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This section is for your personal details, including your name, email, and organization.
	+ **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and materials.
	+ **Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation name, if applicable.
* **Experience Level**: Rate your experience in advocacy work.
* **Advocacy Experience Years**: Enter the number of years you have experience in advocacy work.
* **Previous Training Participation**: Have you participated in advocacy training before?
	+ If yes, select 'True'.
* **Details of Previous Training**: If you've participated in advocacy training before, briefly describe the training you've attended.
* **Current Advocacy Activities**: Select all the advocacy activities you are currently involved in.
	+ (Example: Policy research, Community organizing, Public speaking, etc.)
* **Policy Knowledge Level**: Rate your current knowledge of policy development processes.
* **Communication Skills Level**: Rate your confidence in advocacy communication skills.
* **Stakeholder Engagement Level**: Rate your experience with stakeholder engagement.
* **Primary Learning Objective**: What is your primary learning objective for this training?
* **Specific Topics of Interest**: Select all the specific topics you'd like to focus on during the training.
	+ (Example: Policy analysis, Strategic planning, Coalition building, etc.)
* **Preferred Learning Methods**: Select all the learning methods you prefer.
	+ (Example: Interactive workshops, Case studies, Panel discussions, etc.)
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Do you have any accessibility requirements for the training?
	+ If yes, select 'True'.
* **Accessibility Details**: If you have accessibility requirements, specify them.
* **Schedule Availability**: Select the times of day you can participate in the training.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other information about your training needs.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Add any other questions or comments you have about the training.

Note: If you're unsure about any question, don't worry! Just fill out the form to the best of your abilities, and the training organizers will help you with any clarification.
