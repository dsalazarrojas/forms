# Site Hazard Training Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to acknowledge an individual's understanding and completion of site hazard training. It is intended for employees to confirm their attendance and completion of the required training sessions. The form serves as a record of the training received, which may be required for regulatory compliance and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Select the date you completed the training from the calendar.
3. For each identified hazard, select the corresponding training session you received for that hazard. You can select multiple hazards.
4. Ensure you have completed all the training sessions for the hazards listed.
5. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel document.
* **Date of Training**: Select the date you completed the training from the calendar. This is the date you received the training, not the date you are completing this form.
* **Hazards Identified**: Select the hazards you identified during the training. You can select multiple hazards, so be sure to choose all relevant hazards.
	+ For each hazard, select the corresponding training session you received:
		- **Confined Space Training**: This training covers safe entry and exit procedures for confined spaces.
		- **Fall Protection Training**: This training covers safe practices for preventing falls from heights.
		- **Scaffolding Safety Training**: This training covers safe practices when working on scaffolding.
		- **Hazardous Materials Training**: This training covers safe handling and storage of hazardous materials.
		- **Fire Safety Training**: This training covers emergency response and safety procedures in the event of a fire.
		- **Lockout/Tagout Training**: This training covers procedures for safe work stoppages and machine maintenance.
		- **Emergency Response Training**: This training covers response procedures for emergency situations.
		- **Hearing Conservation Training**: This training covers hearing safety and protection.
		- **Bloodborne Pathogens Training**: This training covers bloodborne pathogen safety and handling.
		- **Personal Protective Equipment Training**: This training covers proper use and maintenance of personal protective equipment.
* **Training Received**: Ensure you have completed all the training sessions listed above for each identified hazard.

## Tips
* Be accurate when selecting the hazards you identified.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any of the training sessions, consult with your supervisor or trainer.
