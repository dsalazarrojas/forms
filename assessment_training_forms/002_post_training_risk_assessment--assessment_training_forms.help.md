# Post Training Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the risk level and control measures associated with a training program after it has been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the course ID as provided by the system.
2.  Enter the name and email of the training manager who was responsible for the training program.
3.  Select the risk level associated with the training program (Low, Medium, or High).
4.  Enter the date when the training program was completed.
5.  Enter the name and email of the trainer who delivered the training program.
6.  Enter comments from the training manager regarding the training program.
7.  Enter control measures put in place to mitigate the risk associated with the training program.
8.  Enter additional notes regarding the risk control measures.
9.  Enter the status of the risk control measures (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
10. Enter follow-up actions taken to mitigate the risk associated with the training program.
11. Enter the frequency of the follow-up actions.
