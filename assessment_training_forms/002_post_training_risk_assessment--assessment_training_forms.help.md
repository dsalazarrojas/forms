# Post Training Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the risk level and control measures associated with a training program after it has been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the course ID as provided by the system.
2.  Enter the name and email of the training manager who was responsible for the training program.
3.  Select the risk level associated with the training program (Low, Medium, or High).
4.  Enter the date when the training program was completed.
5.  Enter the name and email of the trainer who delivered the training program.
6.  Enter comments from the training manager regarding the training program.
7.  Enter control measures put in place to mitigate the risk associated with the training program.
8.  Enter additional notes regarding the risk control measures.
9.  Enter the status of the risk control measures (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
10. Enter follow-up actions taken to mitigate the risk associated with the training program.
11. Enter the frequency of the follow-up actions.
12. Enter the status of the follow-up actions (True or False).
13. Enter additional notes regarding the follow-up actions.
14. Enter comments regarding the follow-up actions.
15. Enter recommendations for future training programs.
16. Enter additional notes regarding the risk control measures.
17. Enter additional notes regarding the follow-up actions.
18. Enter the status of the risk control measures (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
19. Enter additional notes regarding the follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **course_id** (1, number, required): A unique identifier for the training program. This field should be entered as provided by the system.
*   **training_manager_name** (3, text, required): The name of the training manager who was responsible for the training program. This field should be entered as it appears in the system.
*   **training_manager_email** (4, email, required): The email of the training manager who was responsible for the training program. This field should be entered as it appears in the system.
*   **risk_level** (5, select\_multiple, required): The risk level associated with the training program. The options are Low, Medium, or High.
*   **training_date** (6, date, required): The date when the training program was completed. This field should be entered in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
*   **trainer_name** (7, text, required): The name of the trainer who delivered the training program. This field should be entered as it appears in the system.
*   **trainer_email** (8, email, required): The email of the trainer who delivered the training program. This field should be entered as it appears in the system.
*   **training_manager_comments** (9, text, required): Comments from the training manager regarding the training program. This field should be entered as relevant comments.
*   **risk_control_measures** (10, text, required): Control measures put in place to mitigate the risk associated with the training program. This field should be entered as relevant measures.
*   **risk_control_measures_notes** (11, text, required): Additional notes regarding the risk control measures. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **risk_control_status** (12, select\_one, required): The status of the risk control measures, which can be Active, Inactive, or Archived.
*   **follow_up_action** (13, text, required): Follow-up actions taken to mitigate the risk associated with the training program. This field should be entered as relevant actions.
*   **follow_up_frequency** (14, text, required): The frequency of the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant frequency.
*   **follow_up_status** (15, select\_one, required): The status of the follow-up actions, which can be True or False.
*   **follow_up_notes** (16, text, required): Additional notes regarding the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **follow_up_comments** (17, text, required): Comments regarding the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant comments.
*   **follow_up_recommendations** (18, text, required): Recommendations for future training programs. This field should be entered as relevant recommendations.
*   **risk_control_measures_notes** (19, text, required): Additional notes regarding the risk control measures. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **follow_up_measures_notes** (20, text, required): Additional notes regarding the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **risk_control_status** (21, select\_one, required): The status of the risk control measures, which can be Active, Inactive, or Archived.
*   **follow_up_notes** (22, text, required): Additional notes regarding the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **follow_up_comments** (23, text, required): Comments regarding the follow-up actions. This field should be entered as relevant comments.
*   **risk_control_plan** (18, text, required): Additional notes regarding the risk control plan. This field should be entered as relevant notes.
*   **follow_up_notes** (24, text, required): Additional notes regarding the follow-up actions.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out this form.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- Enter relevant comments and notes where applicable.
- If any field is unclear or you are unsure what to enter, contact the relevant person or the system administrator.
- This form is a critical part of the training program evaluation process, and your input is essential to ensuring that the process is thorough and effective.
