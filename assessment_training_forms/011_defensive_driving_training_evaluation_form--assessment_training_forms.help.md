# Defensive Driving Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Defensive Driving Training Evaluation Form is a tool for evaluating the effectiveness and quality of the defensive driving training course. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Rate the course rating section on a scale (though not specified, assume a 5-point scale, e.g., 1-5).
3.  Select one of the options for the instructor's performance and course content.
4.  Provide any additional comments about your experience with the course.
5.  Enter your phone number and email address to help us reach out to you for follow-up or further questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Rating** (`Course Rating`, `number`, **required: false**):
    This field asks for your rating of the overall course quality. Rate the course on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not satisfactory and 5 being excellent.
* **Instructor Name** (`Instructor Name`, `text`, **required: false**):
    Please enter the name of the instructor who taught your training session.
* **Instructor Performance** (`Instructor Performance`, `select_one`, **required: false**):
    Evaluate the instructor's performance as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Course Content** (`Course Content`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**):
    Select all the options that best describe the course content. Choose all that apply.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `number`, **required: false**):
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the course. This is another subjective measure, and the scale used here is assumed to be similar to course rating.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, **required: false**):
    Use this text area to provide any additional comments about the course you'd like to share.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, `text`, **required: false**):
    Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, `email`, **required: false**):
    Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communications about the course.
