# Process Improvement Certification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Improvement Certification Application form is used to collect information for the Process Improvement Certification. This form will help you understand your certification status and ensure that it is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Applicant Information.
2. Enter your Contact Information.
3. Provide Certification Information.
4. Select the Certification Type.
5. Enter the Certification Date.
6. Select the Certification Expiration.
7. Choose your Certification Status.
8. Add any Certification Notes.
9. Check if there are any Issues with Certification.
10. Select the action taken for the Certification.
11. Select the outcome of the Certification.
12. Add any Comments about the Certification.
13. Check if you need to Renew your Certification.
14. Enter the Renewal Date.
15. Select the Renewal Status.
16. Add any Renewal Notes.
17. Select the Renewal Action Taken.
18. Select the Renewal Outcome.
19. Add any Renewal Comments.
20. Check if you need to Terminate your Certification.
21. Enter the Termination Date.
22. Enter the Termination Reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (`1`, `text`, required)**: Please provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info (`2`, `text`, required)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Certification Information (`3`, `text`, required)**: Please provide details about your certification.
* **Certification Request (`4`, `number`, required)**: Enter the certification ID.
* **Certification Type (`5`, `select_one`, required)**: Please select the type of certification you are applying for.
* **Certification Date (`6`, `date`, required)**: Enter the date of your certification.
* **Certification Expiration (`7`, `date`, required)**: Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Certification Status (`8`, `select_one`, required)**: Choose the status of your certification.
* **Certification Notes (`9`, `note`, required)**: Add any notes about your certification.
* **Issues with Certification (`10`, `select_multiple`, required)**: Check any issues you have with your certification.
* **Action Taken (`11`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the action taken for your certification.
* **Outcome (`12`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the outcome of your certification.
* **Comments (`13`, `text`, required)**: Add any comments about your certification.
* **Certification Renewal (`14`, `select_one`, required)**: Check if you need to renew your certification.
* **Renewal Date (`15`, `date`, required)**: Enter the renewal date.
* **Renewal Status (`16`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the renewal status.
* **Renewal Notes (`17`, `text`, required)**: Add any renewal notes.
* **Renewal Action Taken (`18`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the action taken for renewal.
* **Renewal Outcome (`19`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the outcome of renewal.
* **Renewal Comments (`20`, `text`, required)**: Add any renewal comments.
* **Termination (`21`, `select_one`, required)**: Check if you need to terminate your certification.
* **Termination Date (`22`, `date`, required)**: Enter the termination date.
* **Termination Reason (`23`, `text`, required)**: Enter the reason for termination.
* **Certification Renewal Reason (`24`, `text`, required)**: Enter the reason for renewal.
* **Certification Renewal (`25`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the status of renewal.
* **Certification Renewal Action Taken (`26`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the action taken for renewal.
* **Certification Renewal Outcome (`27`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the outcome of renewal.
* **Certification Renewal Comments (`28`, `text`, required)**: Add any renewal comments.
* **Certification Termination Reason (`29`, `text`, required)**: Enter the reason for termination.
* **Certification Termination Status (`30`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the status of termination.
* **Certification Termination Action Taken (`31`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the action taken for termination.
* **Certification Termination Outcome (`32`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the outcome of termination.
* **Certification Termination Comments (`33`, `text`, required)**: Add any termination comments.

## Tips
- Make sure to check for any issues with your certification before submitting the form.
- Select the correct certification type and status.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any errors.
- Review your form before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate.
