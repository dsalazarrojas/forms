# Handgun Safety Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have attended a handgun safety training course. It helps us assess the quality and effectiveness of the training program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction and make sure you have understood the purpose of the form.
2. Rate the course and instruction quality, selecting one or multiple options for each question.
3. Answer the remaining questions, providing more specific feedback and comments.
4. Submit the form to help us improve the handgun safety training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the instructor who led the training course.
* **How would you rate the course?** (`course_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the course as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **How would you rate the instruction quality?** (`instruction_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the instruction quality as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Was all safety equipment provided?** (`safety_equipment_provision`, select_one, optional): Select "True" or "False" to indicate if all safety equipment was provided.
* **Were safety procedures explained?** (`safety_procedure`, select_one, optional): Select "True" or "False" to indicate if safety procedures were explained.
* **Was the training environment suitable?** (`training_environment`, select_one, optional): Select "True" or "False" to indicate if the training environment was suitable.
* **How would you rate the instructor's performance?** (`instructor_performance`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the instructor's performance as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions about the course.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing any further feedback or suggestions about the course.
