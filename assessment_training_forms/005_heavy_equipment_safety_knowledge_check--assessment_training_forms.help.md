# Heavy Equipment Safety Knowledge Check - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a safety check for Heavy Equipment. It is designed to ensure the safety of Heavy Equipment in your workplace or facility.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you understand what it represents.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the one that best describes your situation.
3. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
4. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`, `text`, not required): This field is for the safety procedures in place for the heavy equipment.
  * Enter the safety procedures that are followed for the heavy equipment in your workplace or facility.

* **PPE Use** (`ppe_use`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the personal protective equipment (PPE) used for the heavy equipment.
  * This includes items such as hard hats, safety glasses, and earplugs.

* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the safety policies for the heavy equipment.
  * Select "Agree" if you have read and understood the policies.

* **Heavy Equipment PPE** (`heavy_equipment_ppe`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the PPE used for the heavy equipment.
  * This includes items such as hard hats, safety glasses, and earplugs.

* **Safety Trainings** (`safety_trainings`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the safety trainings received for the heavy equipment.
  * This includes trainings such as OSHA, industry-specific, and company-provided trainings.

* **Safety Equipment Inspection** (`safety_equipment_inspection`, `date`, not required): This is for the last inspection date of the heavy equipment.
  * Enter the date of the last inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Maintenance** (`safety_equipment_maintenance`, `time`, not required): This is for the safety equipment maintenance schedule.
  * Enter the schedule for maintenance of the heavy equipment.

* **Safety Equipment Inspection Frequency** (`safety_equipment_inspection_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency of safety equipment inspections.
  * This can be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or never.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Date** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * This is the date of the last inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Next Inspection Date** (`safety_equipment_next_inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * This is the date of the next inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Result** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of the last inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * This can be pass or fail.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Findings** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_findings`, `text`, not required): This is for any findings from the last inspection.
  * Enter any findings or comments from the last inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Comments** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_comments`, `text`, not required): This is for any comments on the last inspection.
  * Enter any comments on the last inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Signature** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_signature`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the last inspection was performed.
  * Select "True" if the last inspection was performed.

* **Safety Equipment Next Inspection Signature** (`safety_equipment_next_inspection_signature`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the next inspection is scheduled.
  * Select "True" if the next inspection is scheduled.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Date Signature** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_date_signature`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the last inspection date was recorded.
  * Select "True" if the last inspection date was recorded.

* **Safety Equipment Last Inspection Date** (`safety_equipment_last_inspection_date`, `date`, not required): This is for the date of the last inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * Enter the date of the last inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Date** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_date`, `date`, not required): This is for the date of the previous inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * Enter the date of the previous inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Result** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of the previous inspection of the heavy equipment.
  * This can be pass or fail.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Findings** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_findings`, `text`, not required): This is for any findings from the previous inspection.
  * Enter any findings or comments from the previous inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Comments** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_comments`, `text`, not required): This is for any comments on the previous inspection.
  * Enter any comments on the previous inspection.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Signature** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_signature`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the previous inspection was performed.
  * Select "True" if the previous inspection was performed.

* **Safety Equipment Previous Inspection Date Signature** (`safety_equipment_previous_inspection_date_signature`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that the previous inspection date was recorded.
  * Select "True" if the previous inspection date was recorded.

* **Safety Equipment Last Three Months Inspection Frequency** (`safety_equipment_last_three_months_inspection_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of inspections in the last three months.
  * This can be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or never.

* **Safety Equipment Last Three Months Inspection Result** (`safety_equipment_last_three_months_inspection_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the result of the inspections in the last three months.
  * This can be pass or fail.

Please note that the following fields should only be filled out if the corresponding inspection has been performed.
