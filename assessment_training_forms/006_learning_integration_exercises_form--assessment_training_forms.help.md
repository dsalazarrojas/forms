# Learning Integration Exercises Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Integration Exercises Form is designed to collect user feedback and ratings about specific learning integration exercises. This form allows users to provide their thoughts on how well the exercises meet their learning needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email, and phone number (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate your experience with the learning integration exercises (1-5).
3. Provide your comments and feedback about the exercises.
4. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title**: Enter a brief title that describes the learning integration exercises you are rating.
- **Name**: Enter your name, which will be associated with your feedback.
- **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed (if you have a valid email).
- **Phone**: If applicable, enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you if needed.
- **Rating (1-5)**: Rate your experience with the learning integration exercises on a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Comments**: Share your thoughts about the exercises, including what you liked or disliked about them.
- **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the exercises.
- **Rating (Duplicate)**: Another opportunity to rate your experience with the learning integration exercises on a scale from 1 to 5.

Please note that the second "Rating (1-5)" field is a duplicate, intended to allow users to confirm their experience or to ensure accuracy, but it's not a required field.
