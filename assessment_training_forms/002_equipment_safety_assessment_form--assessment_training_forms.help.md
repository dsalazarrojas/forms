# Equipment Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Equipment Safety Assessment Form is designed to document and assess the safety of equipment in a workplace. It is an essential tool for ensuring that equipment is safe for use and helps identify potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Be honest when answering questions, as this information will be used for reference.
3.  Use the available space for additional comments and information.
4.  Ensure all required fields are filled in.
5.  Review the form before submission for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **UserInfo** (`user_info`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Please enter your name and other relevant information in the space provided.
*   **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Enter the details of the equipment being assessed, including its make and model.
*   **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
    Indicate whether the inspection found the equipment to be safe or not.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
    Select the level of risk associated with the equipment.
*   **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    Check all the safety measures that have been taken or recommended for the equipment.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`):
    Add any additional comments or information about the assessment.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `date`, required: `false`):
    Date and sign to confirm you have completed the assessment.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: `false`):
    Enter the date of the assessment.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: `false`):
    Enter the time of the assessment.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`):
    Enter your email address.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Enter your work address.
*   **Status** (`equipment_status`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    Check the status of the equipment.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Add any additional comments about the assessment.
