# Wheelchair_Assistance_Training_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheelchair Assistance Training Survey is designed to gather feedback from users who have attended a wheelchair assistance training session. The survey asks questions about your confidence level with the tools, satisfaction with the session, and interest in attending future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page to understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Answer the questions about your confidence level, satisfaction with the session, and interest in attending future sessions. You can select from the provided options.
3. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, not required): This is a brief overview of the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Confidence Level** (`2`, `select_one`, not required): How confident do you feel about using the wheelchair assistance tools? Choose from:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Training Quality** (`3`, `select_multiple`, not required): How would you rate the quality of the training session? Select all that apply:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Satisfaction Level** (`4`, `select_one`, not required): How satisfied are you with the training session? Choose from:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Future Sessions** (`5`, `select_multiple`, not required): How many more sessions would you like to attend in the next six months? Select all that apply:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5 or more
* **Additional Comments** (`6`, `note`, not required): Do you have any additional comments or feedback about the training session? Enter your thoughts here.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive more information).
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive more information).

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any technical difficulties or need assistance with the survey, please contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
