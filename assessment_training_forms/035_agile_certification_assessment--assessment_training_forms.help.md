# Agile Certification Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agile Certification Assessment form is designed to evaluate your knowledge, skills, and experiences related to Agile methodologies. This form helps assessors determine your suitability for an Agile certification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. For text fields, provide detailed examples or descriptions.
4. For rating scales, use the numbers that best describe your level of understanding, skill, or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name** (1, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Candidate Email** (2, Required): Enter your email address for communication with assessors.
* **Candidate Phone** (3, Required): Enter your phone number for assessors to contact you.
* **Assessment Date** (4, Required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Certification Level** (5, Required): Select the level of certification being assessed. This will help us guide your preparation for the certification process.
* **-- Professional Background --** (6, Optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional information about your professional background, if needed.
* **Years of Experience in Agile Methodologies** (7, Required): Rate your years of experience with Agile methodologies. This will help us understand your level of expertise.
* **Agile Roles Previously Held** (8, Required): Select all Agile roles you have previously held. This will help us understand your experience in different roles.
* **Agile Frameworks Experience** (9, Required): Select all Agile frameworks you have experience with. This will help us understand your familiarity with different frameworks.
* **-- Knowledge Assessment --** (10, Optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional information about your knowledge, if needed.
* **Understanding of Agile Principles** (11, Required): Rate your understanding of the Agile Manifesto and its principles. This will help us determine your foundation for Agile knowledge.
* **Scrum Process Knowledge** (12, Required): Rate your knowledge of Scrum processes and ceremonies. This will help us understand your ability to apply Scrum principles.
* **Agile Tool Proficiency** (13, Required): Rate your proficiency with Agile project management tools. This will help us understand your ability to use tools effectively.
* **Sprint Planning Skill** (14, Required): Rate your ability to facilitate effective sprint planning. This will help us understand your facilitation skills.
* **Stakeholder Communication Skill** (15, Required): Rate your ability to communicate with stakeholders in Agile environments. This will help us understand your interpersonal skills.
* **-- Practical Application --** (16, Optional): This is a note field for you to provide any additional information about your practical application of Agile methodologies, if needed.
* **Agile Project Examples** (17, Required): Describe 1-2 Agile projects you have worked on. This will help us understand your experience in applying Agile principles.
* **Challenges Overcome** (18, Required): Describe challenges you have overcome in Agile implementations. This will help us understand your problem-solving skills.
* **Continuous Improvement Initiatives** (19, Optional): Describe initiatives you have led or contributed to for continuous improvement. This will help us understand your ability to improve processes.
* **Certification Motivation** (20, Required): Explain why you are pursuing this Agile certification. This will help us understand your motivation for pursuing the certification.
* **Future Agile Goals** (21, Required): Describe your goals for applying Agile methodologies in the future. This will help us understand your vision for applying Agile principles.
* **Assessor Evaluation** (22, Optional): This is a note field for the assessor to provide their evaluation of you.
* **Certification Recommendation** (23, Required): The assessor will recommend certification based on your assessment. This is a final step in the assessment process.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* For text fields, provide detailed examples or descriptions.
* For rating scales, use the numbers that best describe your level of understanding, skill, or experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the assessors for clarification.
