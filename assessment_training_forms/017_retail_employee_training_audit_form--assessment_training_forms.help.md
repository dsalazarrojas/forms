# retail_employee_training_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the training audit details of retail employees. The form captures the key information about the employee's training, including the name of the retail manager, the date of the training, the type of training, and the outcome. This form is used to track and evaluate the performance of retail employees after receiving training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, first, select the "Retail Manager" field and enter the name of the retail manager who conducted the training.
2. Enter the "Training Date" in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Select the type of training from the dropdown menu. The options are "Retail Management", "Product Knowledge", and "Technical Skills".
4. Select the outcome of the training by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu. The options are "Satisfactory", "Needs Improvement", and "Unsatisfactory".
5. Enter the "Location" where the training was conducted.
6. If applicable, enter the "Evaluation Date" in the format MM/DD/YY.
7. Enter the "Evaluation Score" as a numerical value.
8. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Note" field.
9. Enter the employee's "Email" address.
10. Enter the employee's "Phone" number.
11. Enter the "Category" of the training.
12. Select the "Assigned User" who is responsible for the training.
13. Select the "Assigned Group" to which the training belongs.
14. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
15. Enter the "Form ID" (this field is not required and is usually auto-generated).
16. Enter the date and time when the form was created ("Created Time" and "Created Date" fields).
17. Enter the date and time when the form was last modified ("Modified Time" and "Modified Date" fields).
18. Enter the date when the form was deleted ("Deleted Date" field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Manager**: Enter the name of the retail manager who conducted the training.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date when the training was conducted in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the training.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training from the dropdown menu.
* **Training Outcome**: Select the outcome of the training by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the training was conducted.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when the evaluation was conducted (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter the numerical value for the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the training (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the training.
* **Assigned User**: Select the user responsible for the training.
* **Assigned Group**: Select the group to which the training belongs.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the training (if applicable).
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID of the form (not required, usually auto-generated).
* **Created Time**: Enter the time when the form was created.
* **Created Date**: Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Modified Time**: Enter the time when the form was last modified.
* **Modified Date**: Enter the date when the form was last modified.
* **Deleted Date**: Enter the date when the form was deleted.
* **Deleted Time**: Enter the time when the form was deleted.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the user who created the form.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the user who last updated the form.
* **Deleted By**: Enter the name of the user who deleted the form.
