# sales_onboarding_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the onboarding readiness of sales personnel, evaluating their understanding of various aspects of the sales process and other essential skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply review the sections below and answer the questions as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is a brief overview of the form. You can read through it and then begin answering the questions.
* **Sales Process** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have a good understanding of our sales process. Choose "Yes" if you are confident in your knowledge.
* **Product Knowledge** (`3`, `number`, optional): Estimate the amount of time you have spent learning about our products. This will help us gauge your product knowledge level.
* **Communication Skills** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you possess strong communication skills, such as writing, speaking, and listening. Choose "Yes" for each skill you are confident in.
* **Time Management** (`5`, `time`, optional): Estimate how you manage your time, such as how you prioritize tasks and meet deadlines. Choose the time range that best describes your current time management skills.
* **Presentation Skills** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have strong presentation skills, such as creating and delivering presentations. Choose "Yes" if you are confident in this skill.
* **Problem Solving** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you possess strong problem-solving skills, such as analyzing data and finding solutions. Choose "Yes" for each skill you are confident in.
* **Team Collaboration** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have experience working in teams and collaborating with others. Choose "Yes" for each skill you are confident in.
* **Sales Skills** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have a good understanding of sales skills, such as negotiating and closing deals. Choose "Yes" if you are confident in this skill.
* **Adaptability** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you are able to adapt to changing situations and priorities. Choose "Yes" if you are confident in this skill.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately as possible to ensure the assessment is fair and effective.
* If you are unsure about any question, try your best to estimate or provide a reason for your uncertainty.
* This form is intended to be a helpful tool in the onboarding process, not a test or a punishment.
