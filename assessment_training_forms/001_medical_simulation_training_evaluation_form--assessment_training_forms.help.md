# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data from trainees after a medical simulation training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into four pages, each with specific questions or fields to fill out. Please complete each page according to the instructions provided.
2. If you are unsure about any question or field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Training Session Information
* **Training Session Information** (`Training Session Information`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the training session, such as the date, time, and location of the session.

### Page 2: Simulation Details
* **Simulation Details** (`Simulation Details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the simulation details that apply to your training session. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Page 3: Trainer Information
* **Trainer Information** (`Trainer Information`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the trainer who led your training session. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Page 4: Evaluation Form
* **Evaluation Form** (`Evaluation Form`, `select_one`, required/optional): This form contains the evaluation criteria for your training session. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
	+ Option 6

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the Training Session Information field.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation above or contact your training administrator for assistance.
