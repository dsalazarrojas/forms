# Property Safety Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Property Safety Training Effectiveness Survey form, which is used to assess the effectiveness of property safety training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, click on the first page of the survey, "Property Safety Training Effectiveness Survey".
2. Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* **Training program name**: Enter the name of the training program being evaluated.
	* **Training location**: Enter the location where the training was conducted.
	* **Total trainees**: Enter the number of trainees who participated in the training program.
	* **Survey type**: Select the type of survey being conducted, whether it's on-site or off-site.
	* **Survey frequency**: Select how often the training program surveys are conducted.
	* **Training quality**: Select the trainees' opinion on the quality of the training program.
	* **Relevance**: Select the relevance of the training program, whether it's highly relevant, somewhat relevant, or not relevant.
	* **Confidence level**: Select the trainees' confidence level in the training program.
	* **Overall confidence**: Select the trainees' overall confidence in the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date survey conducted**: This field asks for the date when the survey was conducted. You can use the calendar icon to select the date.
* **Training program manager**: This field asks for the name of the person responsible for the training program.
* **Survey type**: This field asks for the type of survey being conducted, whether it's on-site or off-site. Please select the correct option from the list.
* **Survey frequency**: This field asks for how often the training program surveys are conducted. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Training quality**: This field asks for the trainees' opinion on the quality of the training program. Select from the options: excellent, good, or fair.
* **Relevance**: This field asks for the relevance of the training program, whether it's highly relevant, somewhat relevant, or not relevant. Select from the options.
* **Confidence level**: This field asks for the trainees' confidence level in the training program, whether it's high, medium, or low. Select from the options.
* **Overall confidence**: This field asks for the trainees' overall confidence in the training program, whether it's very confident, somewhat confident, or not confident. Select from the options.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the training program manager or administrator for clarification.
* The survey is designed to be completed by trainees who have participated in the training program.
* Please select the correct options for each field based on your experience and knowledge.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
