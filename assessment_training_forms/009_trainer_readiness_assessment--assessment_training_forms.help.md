# trainer_readiness_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trainer_readiness_assessment form is designed to evaluate the qualifications and readiness of trainers to conduct training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understood the instructions provided above.
2. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring you provide all required fields.
3. For non-required fields, use your best judgment to provide the most relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Trainer Phone Number** (`trainer_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Departmental Email** (`departmental_email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter your departmental email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Training Venue** (`training_venue`, text, optional): Provide the name of the training venue.
* **Date of Last Training** (`date_of_last_training`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last training session.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have received training in the past (`No`) or not (`Yes`).
* **Training Experience Years** (`training_experience_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have experience in training.
* **Training Experience Months** (`training_experience_months`, number, optional): Enter the number of months you have experience in training.
* **Previous Training Hours** (`previous_training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have completed training in the past.
* **Trainer Skills Self-Assessment** (`trainer_skills_self_assessment`, note, optional): Briefly describe your trainer skills and qualifications.
* **Training Delivery Experience Self-Assessment** (`training_delivery_experience_self_assessment`, note, optional): Briefly describe your training delivery experience and qualifications.
* **Trainer Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, select_one, optional): Rate your trainer feedback as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Trainer Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more feedback options for your trainer feedback.
* **Trainer Qualifications** (`trainer_qualifications`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have received qualifications in training (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Training Topics** (`training_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have received training on various topics (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Feedback 3** (`feedback_3`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have received feedback on your feedback (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your trainer experience.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you belong to a department (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional): Provide the name of the training program you have completed.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have received training in a specific location (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
