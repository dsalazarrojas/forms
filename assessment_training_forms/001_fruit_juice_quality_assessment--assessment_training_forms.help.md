# Fruit Juice Quality Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fruit Juice Quality Assessment form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on fruit juice quality from end-users.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the name of the fruit juice you are assessing.
2. Evaluate the color of the fruit juice (e.g., light, dark, intense).
3. Describe the aroma of the fruit juice (e.g., strong, weak, none).
4. Assess the appearance of the fruit juice (e.g., clear, cloudy, opaque).
5. Evaluate the taste of the fruit juice (e.g., sweet, sour, bland).
6. Describe the texture of the fruit juice (e.g., smooth, rough, pulpy).
7. Assess the mouthfeel of the fruit juice (e.g., refreshing, astringent, dry).
8. Evaluate the juiciness of the fruit juice (e.g., high, low, neutral).
9. Describe the color intensity of the fruit juice (e.g., light, dark, intense).
10. Assess the clarity of the fruit juice (e.g., clear, hazy, opaque).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fruit Juice Name**: Enter the name of the fruit juice you are assessing.
* **Color**: Evaluate the color of the fruit juice (e.g., light, dark, intense).
* **Aroma**: Describe the aroma of the fruit juice (e.g., strong, weak, none).
* **Appearance**: Assess the appearance of the fruit juice (e.g., clear, cloudy, opaque).
* **Taste**: Evaluate the taste of the fruit juice (e.g., sweet, sour, bland).
* **Texture**: Describe the texture of the fruit juice (e.g., smooth, rough, pulpy).
* **Mouthfeel**: Assess the mouthfeel of the fruit juice (e.g., refreshing, astringent, dry).
* **Juiciness**: Evaluate the juiciness of the fruit juice (e.g., high, low, neutral).
* **Color Intensity**: Describe the color intensity of the fruit juice (e.g., light, dark, intense).
* **Clarity**: Assess the clarity of the fruit juice (e.g., clear, hazy, opaque).
* **Description**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the fruit juice.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when evaluating each aspect of the fruit juice.
* Use the provided options as a guideline, but feel free to add your own comments and descriptions.
* If a particular aspect of the fruit juice does not apply, you can leave the field blank.
