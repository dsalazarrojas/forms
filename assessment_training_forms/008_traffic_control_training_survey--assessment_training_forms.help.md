# Traffic Control Training Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users who have undergone training on Traffic Control procedures. Your input is valuable in helping us improve and refine our training process to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Course Name**, if you've attended any.
3. Enter the **Session Length** (in hours or minutes) if you're able to recall it.
4. Enter the **Training Date** when you attended the training session.
5. Choose a specific **Training Time** slot when you attended the session.
6. In the **Feedback** field, type in any comments or suggestions you might have about the training process.
7. If you have an **Email Address**, enter it so we can get in touch with you for follow-up.
8. Finally, enter your **Phone Number** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Course Name**: If you've attended a course, enter its name for us to know which training you're referring to.
* **Session Length**: If you can recall it, enter the length of your training session.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Training Time**: Select the specific time slot when you attended the session.
* **Feedback**: This is where you can provide any comments, suggestions, or concerns you might have about the training process. Be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number is optional, but it helps us know how to reach you if needed.

Note: You can always refer back to this guide if you have any questions while filling out the form. Good luck, and thanks for taking the time to provide feedback!
