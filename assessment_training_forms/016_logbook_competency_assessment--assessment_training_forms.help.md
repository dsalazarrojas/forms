# Logbook Competency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and evaluate competencies or skills possessed by candidates in a specific context. It captures the assessor's name, their position, and the candidate's information, as well as their rating and comments on the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for the assessment in the "Form Title" field.
2. Enter the Assessor's name and position in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the candidate's number and name in the "Candidate Number" and "Candidate Name" fields.
4. Select the assessment date using the "Assessor Date" field.
5. Enter your rating using the "Rating" field.
6. Optionally, provide comments for the assessment using the "Comments" and "Assessor Comments" fields.
7. Optionally, provide additional feedback for the assessor using the "Assessor Feedback" field.
8. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (1)**: A title for the assessment, which can be used for reference or documentation purposes.
* **Assessor Name (2)**: The name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Position (3)**: The position or title of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Candidate Number (4)**: The unique identifier of the candidate being assessed.
* **Candidate Name (5)**: The name of the candidate being assessed.
* **Assessor Date (6)**: The date the assessment was conducted.
* **Rating (7)**: A numeric rating or score for the candidate's performance.
* **Comments (8)**: Optional space for assessor to provide additional comments on the candidate's performance.
* **Assessor Comments (9)**: Optional space for assessors to record their personal thoughts or reflections on the assessment process.
* **Assessor Feedback (10)**: Optional space for assessors to provide feedback for the candidate or for future reference.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Be as specific as possible when providing ratings and comments.
- Review and verify the information before submission to ensure accuracy.
