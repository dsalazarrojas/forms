# Worker Training Module Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Training Module Request Form is a tool to help us identify your training needs and schedule the training sessions accordingly. Please fill out this form to let us know which modules you need training for, your preferred date and time, and other necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the training modules you need by choosing the options from the "Module 1", "Module 2", and "Module 3" dropdown menus. You can select one or multiple modules, as needed.
2. Choose the preferred date and time for the training session by clicking on the calendar and selecting the desired date and time.
3. Add any comments or additional information about your training needs in the "Comments" field.
4. If you are an employee of a company, please provide your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
5. Select your employee status (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract) and the status of your training modules (Complete, Not Complete, or In Progress).
6. Finally, select your type (Manager or Employee) and the team you are assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Module 1**: Choose the "Module 1" option if you need training for the first module.
* **Module 2**: Choose the "Module 2" option if you need training for the second module.
* **Module 3**: Choose the "Module 3" option if you need training for the third module.
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date you would like to have the training session.
* **Preferred Time**: Select the time you would like to have the training session.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your training needs.
* **Employee ID**: If you are an employee of a company, please provide your employee ID.
* **Employee Status**: Select your employee status (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract).
* **Employee Type**: Select your type (Manager or Employee).
* **Module Status**: Select the status of your training modules (Complete, Not Complete, or In Progress).
* **Assigned Group**: If you have a specific team you are assigned to, please select it.
* **Assigned Team**: If you have a specific team you are assigned to, please select it.
* **Status**: Select the status you would like to display on your profile (Active, Inactive).
* **Module 1**: This field is not currently used, please ignore it.
* **Module 2**: This field is not currently used, please ignore it.
* **Module 3**: This field is not currently used, please ignore it.
* **Module Requirement**: If you have any specific requirements for the training session, please add them here.
* **Module Requirement 2**: This field is not currently used, please ignore it.
