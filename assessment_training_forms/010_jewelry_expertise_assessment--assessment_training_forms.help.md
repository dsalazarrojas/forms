# Jewelry Expertise Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Expertise Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge, skills, and experience in the jewelry industry. This assessment is used to determine an individual's level of expertise in various aspects of jewelry, including their knowledge of different types of jewelry, their required skills, and their level of experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Provide a brief statement about your background and experience in the jewelry industry. This is not a mandatory field, but it will help us understand your context.
2. Select your **Assess Knowledge** level:
	* A: Novice
	* B: Intermediate
	* C: Advanced
3. Check all the **Jewelry Knowledge** options that apply to you:
	* Diamond
	* Gold
	* Silver
4. Describe the **Skills Required** for your specific role or position in the jewelry industry.
5. Enter your **Experience** in the jewelry industry, measured in years.
6. (Optional) List your **Certifications**:
	* GIA (Gemological Institute of America)
	* WJA (Women's Jewelry Association)
	* NAMP (National Association of Master Jewelers)
7. Select your **Company**:
	* JOTFORM
	* OTHER
8. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assess Knowledge**: This question determines your level of expertise in assessing and evaluating jewelry knowledge.
* **Jewelry Knowledge**: This question is used to evaluate your knowledge in different types of jewelry, such as diamond, gold, and silver.
* **Skills Required**: Describe the skills and qualifications you possess that are relevant to the jewelry industry.
* **Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the jewelry industry.
* **Certification**: List any certifications you have obtained in the jewelry industry, such as GIA, WJA, or NAMP.
* **Company**: Select the company you are associated with, either JOTFORM or another company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Conclusion**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your experience in the jewelry industry.
