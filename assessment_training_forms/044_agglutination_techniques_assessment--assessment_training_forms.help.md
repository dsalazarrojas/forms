# Agglutination Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agglutination Techniques Assessment form is used to evaluate the skills and knowledge of candidates undergoing assessment. It gathers information about the candidate, their performance during the assessment, and the assessor's comments and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's full name in the "Candidate name" field.
2. Enter the candidate's ID, if applicable.
3. Select the date the assessment was administered.
4. Enter the score for the candidate's theoretical knowledge out of 100.
5. Enter the score for the candidate's practical skills out of 100.
6. Rate the candidate's protocol adherence as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
7. Enter any common errors observed during the assessment.
8. Select Pass or Fail as the final result of the assessment.
9. Provide any assessor comments and recommendations for the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate name** (Candidate name, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the trainee.
* **Candidate ID** (Candidate ID, Text, Optional): Enter the candidate's ID, if applicable.
* **Assessment date** (Assessment date, Date, Required): Enter the date the assessment was administered.
* **Theoretical knowledge score** (Theoretical knowledge score, Number, Required): Enter the score for the candidate's theoretical knowledge out of 100.
* **Practical skills score** (Practical skills score, Number, Required): Enter the score for the candidate's practical skills out of 100.
* **Protocol adherence** (Protocol adherence, Select One, Required): Rate the candidate's protocol adherence as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Common errors observed** (Common errors observed, Text, Optional): Enter any common errors observed during the assessment.
* **Pass or fail assessment** (Pass or fail assessment, Select One, Required): Select Pass or Fail as the final result of the assessment.
* **Assessor comments** (Assessor comments, Text, Optional): Provide any comments and recommendations for the candidate.
