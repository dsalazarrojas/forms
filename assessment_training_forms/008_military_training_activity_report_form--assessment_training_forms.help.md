# Military Training Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and report on military training activities. It's designed to be completed by training activity coordinators or instructors to provide a record of drills, exercises, and other activities conducted during a unit's training session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the unit identifier in the `unit_identifier` field.
2. Select the date of the training activity in the `date` field.
3. Enter the location of the training activity in the `location` field.
4. In the `drills_and_exercises` field, provide a brief description of the drills and exercises conducted during the training session.
5. In the `outcomes` field, select all the outcomes that apply to the training activity. You can choose more than one outcome.
6. In the `notes` field, provide any additional comments or information about the training activity.
7. In the `follow_up_recommendations` field, provide any recommendations for future training activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Identifier** (`unit_identifier`): Enter the unique identifier for the unit that conducted the training activity.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date on which the training activity took place.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the training activity took place.
* **Drills and Exercises** (`drills_and_exercises`): Provide a brief description of the drills and exercises conducted during the training session.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`): Select all the outcomes that apply to the training activity. You can choose more than one outcome.
	+ 'Yes' - The training activity was successful and achieved the desired outcomes.
	+ 'No' - The training activity was not successful and did not achieve the desired outcomes.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or information about the training activity.
* **Follow-up Recommendations** (`follow_up_recommendations`): Provide any recommendations for future training activities.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have a legitimate reason to complete this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
* Do not duplicate efforts by not submitting multiple reports for the same training activity.
* If you have already submitted a report for the same training activity, do not submit another report.
