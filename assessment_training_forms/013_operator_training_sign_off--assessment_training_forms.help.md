# Operator Training Sign Off - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to track the completion status of operator training sessions. It captures information from both the operator and their supervisor, ensuring both parties are on the same page regarding training progress and completion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the training status of the operator (In Progress or Complete).
2. Enter the training completion date.
3. Select the operator who completed the training.
4. Select the supervisor who signed off the training.
5. Enter the training hours completed.
6. Select the status of the training (In Progress or Complete).
7. Enter any comments regarding the training completion.
8. Enter the operator's training hours.
9. Enter the supervisor's sign off date.
10. Enter the supervisor's comments.
11. Select the operator's sign off status (In Progress or Complete).
12. Select the supervisor's sign off status (In Progress or Complete).
13. Enter the review date for the operator's completion status.
14. Enter the review date for the supervisor's sign off status.
15. Select the operator's review complete status (In Progress or Complete).
