# Supervisor Training Certification Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor Training Certification Quiz is a form designed to evaluate a supervisor's understanding and completion of the required training. It consists of three fields: "Quiz", "Confirm Completion", and "Assess Readiness".

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the "Quiz" field by selecting either "Yes" or "No" to confirm your completion of the training.
2. Select either "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Completion" field to verify your completion of the training.
3. In the "Assess Readiness" field, select either "Yes" or "No" to evaluate your readiness to take on supervisory tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz**: Select "Yes" if you have completed the required training, or "No" otherwise.
* **Confirm Completion**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have finished the training, or "No" if you have not.
* **Assess Readiness**: Choose "Yes" if you feel you are ready to take on supervisory tasks, or "No" otherwise.
