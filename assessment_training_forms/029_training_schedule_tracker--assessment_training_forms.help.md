# Training Schedule Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Schedule Tracker" form is a tool for tracking and managing training sessions. It's essential to understand the purpose of this form to use it effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Click on the "Training Schedule Tracker" form to open it.
*   Select the date of the training session using the date picker.
*   Enter the duration of the training session.
*   Type the location of the training.
*   Enter the name of the training session.
*   Enter the name of the trainer.
*   Select the current status of the training session (In Progress, Complete, or Canceled).
*   If necessary, provide feedback or suggestions for future improvements.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session using the date picker.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Type the location of the training session.
* **Training Name** (`training_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training session.
* **Trainer** (`trainer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the current status of the training session (In Progress, Complete, or Canceled).
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide feedback or suggestions for future improvements.
* **Training Suggestions** (`training_suggestions`, note, required: false): Enter any suggestions for improvement in this free text field.

Note: The status selection is required to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of training sessions. Please select one of the options provided.
