# guest_registration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests arriving at an event or location, allowing us to manage their registration efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Make sure to include your ID copy in the provided field.
3. Indicate your agreement status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Agreement" field.
4. Enter your contact email and contact phone number in the corresponding fields.
5. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should be spelled on your ID.
* **ID copy** (`id_copy`, `text`, required): Enter your ID number as it appears on your identification document.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose "Yes" to confirm you have read and understand the terms, or "No" to indicate otherwise.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): The date of your arrival (optional).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): The time of your arrival (optional).
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): If you are staying at an address other than the event location, provide it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Leave any additional comments or information about your visit.
* **Guest number** (`guest_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests in your party (optional).

Please ensure to submit the form accurately and completely to avoid any errors or issues during your stay.
