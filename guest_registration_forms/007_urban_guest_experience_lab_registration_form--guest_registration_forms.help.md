# urban-guest-experience-lab-registration-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to register their information for a lab visit. It is used to gather essential details about the guest and their company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your company name in the **Company** field.
4. Enter your position in the **Position** field.
5. Enter your department in the **Department** field.
6. Enter your date of registration in the **Date Registered** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
8. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
9. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
10. Enter any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
11. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Position** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your position.
* **Department** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Date Registered** (`6`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you registered for the lab visit.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Notes** (`10`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your visit.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above.
