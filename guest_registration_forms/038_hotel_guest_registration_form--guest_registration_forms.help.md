# hotel_guest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for hotel guests to register their information and provide details about their stay.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by entering your Guest Name in the first field, "Guest Name".
2. If you have information about the room number, you can enter it in the "room_number" field. This is optional.
3. Enter your arrival and departure dates in the "arrival_date" and "departure_date" fields, respectively.
4. If you have any reservation notes, you can add them in the "reservation_notes" field. This is optional.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be used for communication purposes.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This will be used to contact you during your stay.
7. If you have any specific room type requests, select "Yes" or "No" in the "room_type" field.
8. If you have a preferred room rate, enter it in the "room_rate" field. This is optional.
9. If you have any special requests during your stay, select "Yes" or "No" in the "special_requests" field.
10. If you want to specify the source of your reservation, enter the relevant information in the "reservation_source" field. This is optional.
11. For your check-in time, select the time from the available options in the "checkin" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
* **room_number** (number, optional): Enter the room number you have been assigned or would like to be assigned.
* **arrival_date** (date, optional): Enter your arrival date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **departure_date** (date, optional): Enter your departure date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **reservation_notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your reservation, such as any special requests or requirements.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for staff to contact you during your stay.
* **room_type** (select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any room type preferences, such as a view or a specific type of room.
* **room_rate** (number, optional): Enter your preferred room rate, if any.
* **special_requests** (select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any special requests during your stay, such as extra towels or a late check-out.
* **reservation_source** (text, optional): Enter the source of your reservation, such as an online booking platform or a travel agent.
* **checkin** (time, optional): Select your check-in time from the available options.
