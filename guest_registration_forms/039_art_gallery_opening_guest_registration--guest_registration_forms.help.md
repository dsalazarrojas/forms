# Art Gallery Opening Guest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Opening Guest Registration form is a vital tool for us to manage attendance and guest information. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure we can provide you with the best experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by selecting the exhibition you plan to attend**. Choose from the given options to ensure we're aware of your attendance for that specific event.
2.  **Provide your full name and email address**. We use this information for contact purposes and to keep you informed about upcoming events.
3.  **Enter your phone number** (optional) to allow us to contact you if needed.
4.  **Select your guest category** from the provided options (Artist - Collaborator, Member, Media - Press, VIP Guest, General Public, or Guest of Staff).
5.  **Choose the exhibition you're attending**. This is crucial for us to manage attendance and allocate resources accordingly.
6.  **Enter the number of guests** (including yourself) you're attending with. This helps us plan for the correct number of attendees.
7.  **Select your preferred arrival time slot** to help us manage gallery capacity and plan accordingly.
8.  **Indicate your consent to being photographed or filmed** for promotional purposes. This may be used on social media and our website.
9.  **Choose whether you'd like to be added to our VIP opening invitation list**. This ensures you receive priority invitations to future events.
10. **Select the types of art you'd like to hear about**. This helps us tailor our communication to your interests.
11.  **Enter the date and time you registered**. This helps us track registration and manage guest information accurately.
12.  **Provide any accessibility or special requirements** you may have. This allows us to make necessary arrangements for your comfort and safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
*   **Email Address**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number**: Your phone number (optional, but recommended for easier contact).
*   **Guest Category**: Your affiliation with the gallery or exhibition (Artist - Collaborator, Member, Media - Press, VIP Guest, General Public, or Guest of Staff).
*   **Exhibition Attending**: The exhibition you plan to attend (Urban Echoes - Photography Exhibition, Infinite Canvas - Abstract Series, or Sculpting the Future - Group Show).
*   **Number of Guests**: The number of guests attending with you (including yourself).
*   **Preferred Arrival Time Slot**: Your preferred time to arrive at the gallery (6:00 PM - 6:45 PM, 6:45 PM - 7:30 PM, 7:30 PM - 8:15 PM, or 8:15 PM - 9:00 PM).
*   **Photo Consent Agreement**: Your consent to being photographed or filmed for promotional purposes.
*   **Stay Informed**: Your agreement to be added to our VIP opening invitation list.
*   **Interest Areas**: The types of art you'd like to hear about (Painting, Sculpture, Digital Art, Performance Art, Installations, or any other).
*   **Registration Timestamp**: The date and time you registered.
*   **Special Requirements**: Any special needs or requirements you may have during your visit.

By following these steps and understanding each field's purpose, you'll be able to complete the form accurately and truthfully, ensuring a smooth experience for both you and the gallery.
