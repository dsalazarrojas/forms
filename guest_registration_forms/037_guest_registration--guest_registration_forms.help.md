## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time (time)**: Optional field for you to provide additional time information. If not required, leave blank.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note (note)**: Optional field for you to provide additional notes. If not required, leave blank.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your physical address.
* **Interests (interests)**: Select your interests from the provided options.
* **Language (language)**: Select the languages you speak.
* **Occupation (occupation)**: Select your occupation.
* **Job (company)**: Enter your job title and company.
* **Position (position)**: Select your position within the company.
* **Department (department)**: Select your department.
* **Subdepartment (subdepartment)**: Select your subdepartment.
* **Manager (manager)**: Select your manager.
* **Team (team)**: Select your team(s).
* **Role (role)**: Select your role within the team.
* **Job 2 (job2)**: Optional field for you to provide additional job 2 information. If not required, leave blank.
* **Job 3 (job3)**: Optional field for you to provide additional job 3 information. If not required, leave blank.

**Tips**: Ensure that you fill out each field carefully and correctly, as any mistakes may cause issues with your registration.
