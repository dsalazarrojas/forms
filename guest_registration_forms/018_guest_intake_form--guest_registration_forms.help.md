# Guest Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Guest Intake Form is used to collect registration information for guests attending an event. This form helps us gather essential details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your basic contact information, including:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Provide the date and time of the event you are attending.
3. Enter the name of the event.
4. Select the type of event you will be attending from the list of options.
5. Add any additional details about the event, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Optional**):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **Optional**):
	+ Enter the date of the event you will be attending in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **Optional**):
	+ Enter the time of the event you will be attending in the format `HH:MM:SS AM/PM`.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, **Optional**):
	+ Select the type of event from the list of options provided.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, **Optional**):
	+ Enter any additional details or comments about the event.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at [contact email/phone number].
* If you have already registered for this event, do not fill out this form again.
