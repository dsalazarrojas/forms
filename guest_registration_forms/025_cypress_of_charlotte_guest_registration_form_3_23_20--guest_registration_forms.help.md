# Cypress Of Charlotte Guest Registration Form 3.23.20 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cypress Of Charlotte Guest Registration Form 3.23.20 is designed to collect information from guests for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as "Required: true" in the form).
3. Enter accurate and complete information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Please enter the guest's name.
* **Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`guest_dob`, date, optional): Select the guest's date of birth.
* **Temperature** (`guest_temperature`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the guest has a temperature.
* **Temperature Unit** (`temperature_unit`, select_multiple, optional): If the guest has a temperature, select the unit used (e.g., Celsius, Fahrenheit).
* **Note** (`guest_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional note or comment for the guest.
* **Do you have any health concerns?** (`guest_question`, select_one, optional): Answer yes or no to this question.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If unsure about any field, ask your event organizer or contact person for clarification.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
