# tourist_id_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tourist_id_registration_form is designed for guests to register their tourist ID information for a smooth and secure stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and truthful information.
2. Review and verify each field to ensure accuracy before submission.
3. Complete all fields marked as "required" to avoid delays or rejections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (1)**: Enter your general information, including your name and any relevant details. (text, required: false)
* **ID Number (2)**: Enter the ID number issued by the relevant authorities. (text, required: false)
* **Nationality (3)**: Select your nationality from the list provided. (select_one, required: false)
* **Birth Date (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD). (date, required: false)
* **Arrival Date (5)**: Enter the date you are scheduled to arrive. (date, required: false)
* **Email (6)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. (email, required: false)
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (text, required: false)
* **Passport Copy (8)**: Upload a copy of your passport (if applicable). (text, required: false)
* **Photo ID Back (9)**: Attach a photo of your ID back (if applicable). (text, required: false)
