# safari_villa_guest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary guest information for booking and stay purposes. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields (if applicable).
2. Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and completely.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: This field is where you enter your name. Please enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **User Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **User Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **User Address**: This field is for your address. Please enter your full address where you would like to be delivered or contacted.
Each field is optional, but filling out all fields will help us better serve you during your stay.
