# qrcode_scan_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a guest by scanning a QR code and providing some basic information such as event ID, name, phone number, and email. This form is typically used in events, meetups, or conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scan the provided QR code to begin the registration process.
2. Fill out the required fields with your information:
	* Event ID: Enter the unique ID of the event you are attending.
	* Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* Notes: (Optional) Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
3. Select the QR code corresponding to your registration type.
4. Click 'Submit' to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event ID** (1): A unique ID of the event you are attending. This is required to associate your registration with the correct event.
* **Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification. This is required to verify your identity.
* **First Name** (3): Enter your first name. This field is required and should match the first name on your identification.
* **Last Name** (4): Enter your last name. This field is required and should match the last name on your identification.
* **Email** (5): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This is required and will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is required and will be used for emergency or follow-up communication.
* **Notes** (7): (Optional) Enter any additional comments or information about your registration. This field is not required but can be helpful for event organizers.
* **QR Code** (8): Select the QR code corresponding to your registration type. This field is required and will direct the registration process accordingly.
* **Submit** (9): Click 'Submit' to complete the registration process. You can also click 'Cancel' to exit without submitting your registration.
