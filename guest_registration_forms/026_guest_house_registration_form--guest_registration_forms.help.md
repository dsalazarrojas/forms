# guest-house-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a guest house owner with a way to register their property to be listed on a local online platform for rent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about your guest house.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest House Name** (`guest_house_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your guest house.
* **Owner's Username** (`owner_username`, `text`, optional): Enter your username (this will be used to identify you as the owner).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for communication).
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the location of your property.
* **Owner's Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of property you are registering (Apartment, Villa, House, or Other).
* **Number of Rooms** (`rooms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the number of rooms in your property.
* **Price per Night** (`price_per_night`, `number`, required): Enter the price per night for your property.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date guests can check in.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date guests can check out.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your property.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use accurate and relevant information to avoid any issues with listing your property.
* Review your submission before submitting the form.
