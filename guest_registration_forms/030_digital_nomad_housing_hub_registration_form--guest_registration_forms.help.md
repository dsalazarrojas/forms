# Digital Nomad Housing Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Nomad Housing Hub Registration Form" is designed to gather information from remote workers on their work and accommodation preferences. This information will help the platform match their needs with the best possible housing options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your remote work address, city, and country.
2. Select your preferred location(s) from the options provided.
3. Choose your accommodation type (e.g., Shared Room, Private Room, Entire place).
4. Enter your maximum number of guests (if applicable).
5. Select your checkin and checkout dates (if applicable).
6. Provide your remote work phone number, email, and website (if applicable).
7. Enter your remote work description, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Youtube, permit, visa, passport, tax number, license, insurance, vehicles, and other information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Work Address** (`id: 1`): Enter your remote work address.
* **Remote Work City** (`id: 2`): Enter your remote work city.
* **Remote Work Country** (`id: 3`): Enter your remote work country.
* **Preferred Location** (`id: 4`): Select your preferred location(s) from the options provided (e.g., Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines).
* **Accommodation Type** (`id: 5`): Choose your accommodation type (e.g., Shared Room, Private Room, Entire place).
* **Max Guests** (`id: 6`): Enter your maximum number of guests (if applicable).
* **Checkin Date** (`id: 7`): Select your checkin date (if applicable).
* **Checkout Date** (`id: 8`): Select your checkout date (if applicable).
* **Remote Work Phone** (`id: 9`): Enter your remote work phone number.
* **Remote Work Email** (`id: 10`): Enter your remote work email.
* **Remote Work Website** (`id: 11`): Enter your remote work website.
* **Remote Work Description** (`id: 12`): Enter a brief description of your remote work.
* **Remote Work Facebook** (`id: 13`): Enter your remote work Facebook URL.
* **Remote Work LinkedIn** (`id: 14`): Enter your remote work LinkedIn URL.
* **Remote Work Twitter** (`id: 15`): Enter your remote work Twitter URL.
* **Remote Work Instagram** (`id: 16`): Enter your remote work Instagram URL.
* **Remote Work Youtube** (`id: 17`): Enter your remote work Youtube URL.
* **Remote Work Permit** (`id: 18`): Enter your remote work permit information.
* **Remote Work Visa** (`id: 19`): Enter your remote work visa information.
* **Remote Work Passport** (`id: 20`): Enter your remote work passport information.
* **Remote Work Tax Number** (`id: 21`): Enter your remote work tax number.
* **Remote Work License** (`id: 22`): Enter your remote work license information.
* **Remote Work Insurance** (`id: 23`): Enter your remote work insurance information.
* **Remote Work Vehicles** (`id: 24`): Enter your remote work vehicles information.
* **Remote Work Other** (`id: 25`): Enter any other relevant information about your remote work.
