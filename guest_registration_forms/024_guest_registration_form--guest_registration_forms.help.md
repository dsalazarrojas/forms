# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is designed to collect basic information from guests for the purpose of registering their stay at the hotel or resort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name that will be associated with your account.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is the surname of the person registering.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This will be used for communication and emergency contact.
5. Select the **Checkin Date** for your arrival from the calendar. This is the date you will be checking in.
6. Choose the **Room Type** that suits your needs from the options provided. You can select between Single Room, Double Room, Suite Room, or Other.
7. Enter the **Check Out Date** for your departure from the calendar. This is the date you will be checking out.
8. Enter the **Room Number** of your assigned room. This will help with room assignments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the surname of the person registering.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Checkin Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of your arrival.
* **Room Type** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of room you require.
* **Check Out Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of your departure.
* **Room Number** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of your assigned room.
