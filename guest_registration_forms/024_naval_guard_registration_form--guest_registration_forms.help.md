# Naval Guard Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Naval Guard Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register as naval guards. This form is used to verify an individual's identity and confirm their affiliation with the naval guard.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have read the purpose section and understand that this form is for new registrations only.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Once completed, submit the form and a confirmation will be sent to your email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address for contact and verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Registration** (time, not required): Enter the time you are registering.
* **Unit ID** (text, not required): Enter your unit ID or other identifying information.
* **Branch** (text, not required): Enter the branch you are affiliated with.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be accurate and truthful in your responses.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
