# Simple Guest List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests attending an event. It helps to verify the presence of individuals at the event and provides an opportunity to add additional notes and details about their attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. Double-check that you have provided accurate information for each field.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have filled in all relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address as it is currently registered on your official identification.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number as it is registered on your official identification.

### Date of Attendance
**Date of Attendance** (`date_of_attendance`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date you plan to attend the event.

### Time of Attendance
**Time of Attendance** (`time_of_attendance`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.

### Note
**Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false)
Add any additional notes about your attendance or any special requirements.
