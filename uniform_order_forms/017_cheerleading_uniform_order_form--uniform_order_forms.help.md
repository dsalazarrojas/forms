# Cheerleading Uniform Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for cheerleading uniforms, providing details about the team, order date, delivery deadline, uniform style, colors, quantity, and customization requirements. It also allows customers to select additional accessories and shipping options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
2. **School or Organization**: Enter the name of the school or organization associated with your team.
3. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order.
4. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive order communications.
5. **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for order inquiries.
6. **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to place this order.
7. **Delivery Deadline**: Enter the date you need the uniforms delivered by.
8. **Uniform Style**: Select the style of uniform you would like to order.
9. **Primary Color**: Choose the main color of the uniform.
10. **Secondary Color**: Choose the accent color of the uniform.
11. **Quantity of Tops**: Enter the number of uniform tops you need.
12. **Quantity of Bottoms**: Enter the number of uniform bottoms you need.
13. **Quantity of Full Dresses**: Enter the number of full dresses you need (if applicable).
14. **Youth Small Quantity**: Enter the number of youth small uniforms you need.
15. **Youth Medium Quantity**: Enter the number of youth medium uniforms you need.
16. **Youth Large Quantity**: Enter the number of youth large uniforms you need.
17. **Adult Small Quantity**: Enter the number of adult small uniforms you need.
18. **Adult Medium Quantity**: Enter the number of adult medium uniforms you need.
19. **Adult Large Quantity**: Enter the number of adult large uniforms you need.
20. **Adult XL Quantity**: Enter the number of adult XL uniforms you need.
21. **Adult 2XL Quantity**: Enter the number of adult 2XL uniforms you need.
22. **Adult 3XL Quantity**: Enter the number of adult 3XL uniforms you need.
23. **Customization Required**: Select whether you need custom customization, such as team name or logo, on your uniforms.
24. **Customization Details**: If you selected customization, enter the details of your requirements.
25. **Additional Accessories**: Select any additional accessories you need.
26. **Shipping Address**: Enter your full shipping address.
27. **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping option.
28. **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for this order.
29. **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requirements or instructions for this order.
30. **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
