# Uniform Allocation Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Allocation Record Form is used to collect and track information about uniforms being allocated to staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields on each page.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (Page 1)**: Provide a brief description of the uniform being allocated.
* **Uniform Info (Page 2)**: Enter detailed information about the uniform, including its type, size, color, etc.
* **Submit Info (Page 3)**: Enter any additional comments or instructions related to the submission of the uniform.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information entered in each field for accuracy.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the form owner for assistance.
