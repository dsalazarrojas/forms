# sublimated_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sublimated_uniform_order_form is a form designed for sports teams to order uniforms. It helps teams collect necessary information from team members or customers to process and fulfill uniform orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information in the `contact_details` field.
2. Provide team details in the `team_details` field.
3. List the players in the `player_list` field.
4. Enter order details in the `order_details` field.
5. Specify shipping instructions in the `shipping` field.
6. Add any design or customization requests in the `design_details` field.
7. If required, upload any additional files in the `file_upload` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This field is where you can enter your contact information, such as name, phone number, and email.
* **Team Details**: Here, provide information about your team, including team name, league, or other relevant details.
* **Player List**: List the names and numbers of the players in your team.
* **Order Details**: Enter the type and quantity of uniforms you need, along with any special requests.
* **Shipping**: Specify the shipping address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Design Details**: Add any custom design requests, such as logo, colors, or font styles.
* **File Upload**: If required, upload any files necessary for the design process, like logos or images.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team administrator or manager for assistance.
