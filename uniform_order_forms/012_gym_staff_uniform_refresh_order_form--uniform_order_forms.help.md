# Gym Staff Uniform Refresh Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for staff members to submit uniform refresh orders, including sizes and quantities of each size.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your staff ID in the **staff id** field. This is a unique identifier for your profile.
2. Choose the sizes you need for your uniform refresh order in the **sizes** field. Select all sizes you want to refresh.
3. Choose the quantities you want for each size. You should select the quantity of each size you need.
4. Choose the delivery option you prefer in the **delivery** field. Select 'Yes' if you want us to deliver the uniforms and 'No' if you want to pick up the uniforms yourself.
5. If you selected 'Yes' in the previous step, choose the delivery location in the **delivery location** field. Enter the location where you want your uniforms delivered.
6. If you selected 'Yes' in the previous step, choose the delivery date in the **delivery date** field. Enter the date you would like your uniforms delivered on.
7. If you selected 'Yes' in the previous step, choose the delivery time in the **delivery time** field. Enter the time you would like your uniforms delivered on.
8. Choose the delivery method you prefer in the **delivery method** field. Select 'Yes' if you want us to handle the delivery and 'No' if you want to arrange for another method.
9. Enter the total number of uniforms you need in the **total** field. This will ensure that we process your order accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **staff id** (`staff_id`, number, required): Enter your unique staff ID to associate your order with your profile.
- **sizes** (`sizes`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the sizes you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **delivery** (`delivery`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' for delivery or 'No' for pickup.
- **delivery location** (`delivery_location`, note, required if delivery is selected): Enter the location where you want your uniforms delivered if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field.
- **delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, required if delivery is selected): Enter the date you would like your uniforms delivered on if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field.
- **delivery time** (`delivery_time`, time, required if delivery is selected): Enter the time you would like your uniforms delivered on if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field.
- **delivery method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you want us to handle the delivery and 'No' if you want to arrange for another method.
- **size 0** (`sizes_0`, number, required): Enter the quantity of size 0 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **size 1** (`sizes_1`, number, required): Enter the quantity of size 1 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **size 2** (`sizes_2`, number, required if size 1 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 2 you need if you selected size 1 in the previous field.
- **size 3** (`sizes_3`, number, required if size 2 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 3 you need if you selected size 2 in the previous field.
- **size 4** (`sizes_4`, number, required if size 3 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 4 you need if you selected size 3 in the previous field.
- **size 5** (`sizes_5`, number, required if size 4 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 5 you need if you selected size 4 in the previous field.
- **quantity 0** (`quantities_0`, number, required if size 0 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 0 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **quantity 1** (`quantities_1`, number, required if size 1 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 1 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **quantity 2** (`quantities_2`, number, required if size 2 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 2 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **quantity 3** (`quantities_3`, number, required if size 3 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 3 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **quantity 4** (`quantities_4`, number, required if size 4 is selected): Enter the quantity of size 4 you need for your uniform refresh order.
- **delivery 1** (`delivery_1`, number, required if delivery is selected): Enter the number of uniforms you want delivered on the first delivery.
- **total** (`total`, number, required): Enter the total number of uniforms you need for your uniform refresh order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately to ensure correct processing of your order.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
- We will process your order as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
