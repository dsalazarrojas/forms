# Uniform Fitting Appointment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students or team members for a uniform fitting session. It collects necessary information for scheduling and coordinating the fitting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that you fill in your school and team information accurately.
3. Select a preferred date and time for your fitting appointment.
4. Provide your measurements and any specific fitting details.
5. Review and confirm your booking details before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your team or squad.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `date`, required/optional): Select a preferred date for the fitting appointment.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `time`, required/optional): Select a preferred time for the fitting appointment.
* **Measurements** (`measurements`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your measurements for the fitting session.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any specific preferences you may have for the fitting.
* **Fitting Details** (`fitting_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide additional details about the fitting session.
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your booking details.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for your booking.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time for your booking.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of your booking (Active or Inactive).
* **Status Label** (`status_label`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description for your status (optional). 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the person managing the form for guidance.
* Review your submission carefully before confirming it. 

Note: The fields and labels might need further clarification or refinement based on the specific use case and context. This help guide serves as a general outline for users to understand the form's purpose and use.
