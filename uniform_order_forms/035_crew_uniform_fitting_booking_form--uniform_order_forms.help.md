# Crew Uniform Fitting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for crew members to book a fitting session for their uniforms. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your fitting preferences are met.

## How To Complete This Form

- Review the fields below and ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification from the person responsible for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Booking Info** (`Booking Info`, text, not required): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your fitting session. You can include any relevant details about your fitting preferences.
- **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, not required): Please provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details. This helps the fitting team reach out to you with questions or updates about your fitting.
- **Fitting Info** (`Fitting Info`, text, not required): Use this field to provide any additional information about your fitting, such as specific items you need fitted or special requirements.
- **Submit Info** (`Submit Info`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the fitting team, please include them here.
- **Fitting Type** (`Fitting Type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of fitting you require. Please choose from the available options: Fitting 1, Fitting 2, or Fitting 3.
- **Select Multiple Fittings** (`Select Multiple Fittings`, select_multiple, not required): If you need multiple fittings, please select all the relevant options here.
- **Date Fitting** (`Date Fitting`, date, not required): Please select a date for your fitting session. You can choose from the available dates or enter a custom date.
- **Time Fitting** (`Time Fitting`, time, not required): Select a time slot for your fitting session. Please choose from the available options or enter a custom time.
- **Submitter** (`Submitter`, select_one, not required): Please select the person submitting this form. You can choose from the available options: John, Mary, or Jane.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, not required): Provide your email address so the fitting team can reach out to you with updates or questions about your fitting session.
- **Fitting 1, Fitting 2, Fitting 3** (`Fitting 1, Fitting 2, Fitting 3`, select_multiple, not required): These fields are for selecting specific fittings you need during your session. Please choose from the available options.

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification from the person responsible for the form or the fitting team.
- Double-check your selections and information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact [insert contact email or phone number].
