# University Sports Uniforms Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for new sports uniforms for your team. Please use it to provide all necessary information about your team's uniform needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your team's information in the "Team Info" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Select the types of uniforms you need in the "Uniform Requests" section.
4. Specify if you need a specific size in the "Size" section.
5. Choose the type of uniform you require in the "Type" section.
6. Select the color of the uniform in the "Color" section.
7. Enter the quantity of uniforms you need in the "Quantity" section.
8. Briefly describe the style of uniform you are looking for in the "Style" section (optional).
9. Add any additional information about your uniform request in the "Description" section (optional).
10. Select the date you need the uniforms by in the "Date" section.
11. Select the time of day you need the uniforms by in the "Time" section.
12. Add any additional comments or notes about your uniform request in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Info (Text)**:
  This section is where you provide information about your team. Please enter your team's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (Text)**:
  This section is where you enter your contact information. This will help us get in touch with you to confirm your uniform request.
* **Uniform Requests (Select Multiple)**:
  Select the types of uniforms you need from the provided options. Make sure to choose only what is necessary for your team.
* **Size (Select Multiple)**:
  If you need a specific size, select it from the options provided. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
* **Type (Select Multiple)**:
  Choose the type of uniform you require from the provided options.
* **Color (Select Multiple)**:
  Select the color you need for your uniform. If no color is required, leave this field empty.
* **Quantity (Number)**:
  Enter the number of uniforms you need.
* **Style (Text)**:
  Briefly describe the style of uniform you are looking for. This is an optional field.
* **Description (Text)**:
  Add any additional information about your uniform request in this section. This is also an optional field.
* **Date (Date)**:
  Select the date you need the uniforms by.
* **Time (Time)**:
  Select the time of day you need the uniforms by.
* **Notes (Text)**:
  Add any additional comments or notes about your uniform request here. This field is mandatory to ensure we have all necessary information to process your request.
