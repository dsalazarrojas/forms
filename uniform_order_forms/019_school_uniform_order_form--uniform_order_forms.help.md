# School Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Uniform Order Form is designed to collect information about students' uniform orders, including order details, student and parent information, and specific order items. This form helps school administrators and staff to efficiently manage and fulfill uniform orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the order details, including any specific requirements or requests you have for your uniform order.
2. Provide student and parent information, including their names and contact numbers.
3. Specify the order items you wish to purchase, by selecting the relevant options from the list.
4. Enter the date you wish to order the uniform on.
5. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (order_details): Enter a brief description of your uniform order.
* **Student Information** (student_info): Enter the student's name and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Information** (parent_info): Enter the parent's name and any other relevant details.
* **Order Items** (order_items): Select the specific items you wish to order from the provided list.
* **Student Name** (student_name): Enter the student's name.
* **School Year** (school_year): Enter the student's school year.
* **Parent Name** (parent_name): Enter the parent's name.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): Enter the parent's contact number.
* **Email** (email): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **School ID** (school_id): Enter a unique ID for the school (if required).
* **Order Date** (order_date): Enter the date you wish to order the uniform on.
* **Submit** (submit): Review and submit your order for processing.
