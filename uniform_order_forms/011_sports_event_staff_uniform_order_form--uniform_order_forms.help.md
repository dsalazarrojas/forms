# sports_event_staff_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sports event staff members to order uniforms for the event. You will need to provide your details for uniform selection, delivery preferences, and other relevant information to ensure timely and accurate uniform distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your staff details on **staff_details**.
2. Select your staff role(s) on **staff_roles**.
3. Choose your uniform size on **uniform_size**.
4. Select your preferred delivery method on **delivery_preferences**.
5. Provide your delivery address on **delivery_address**.
6. Specify the delivery date and time on **delivery_date** and **delivery_time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **staff_details**: Enter your full name and any other relevant details.
* **staff_roles**: Choose all roles that apply to you (Admin, Operations Manager, Volunteer).
* **uniform_size**: Select the size of your uniform (Small, Medium, Large, X-Large).
* **delivery_preferences**: Select how you want to receive your uniform (Email, Phone, Post).
* **delivery_address**: Enter the address where you want your uniform to be delivered.
* **delivery_date** and **delivery_time**: Specify the date and time you want to receive your uniform.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting your order.
- Ensure your delivery address is accurate and complete.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, please contact us.
