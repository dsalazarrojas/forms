# custom_dress_measurement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_dress_measurement_form is a form designed to collect measurements and specifications for custom-made dresses. This form is intended for users who want to order custom-made dresses that fit their unique body shape and style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your body type from the drop-down menu.
2. Provide your measurements for each of the following:
	* Bust measurement
	* Chest measurement
	* Waist measurement
	* Hip measurement
	* Height measurement
	* Weight measurement
	* Sleeve length
	* Dress length
	* Top length
	* Bottom length
	* Arm sleeve length
	* Shoulder width
	* Sleeve width
	* Collar depth
	* Neck depth
	* Collar width
	* Waist circumference
	* Hip circumference
	* Dress sleeve length
	* Dress width
	* Fabric length
