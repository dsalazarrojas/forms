# Exhibit Team Apparel Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended for requesting team apparel for your team. This form will collect information regarding your team's apparel preferences, including their preferred sizes, colors, and quantities of shirts, pants, and jacket. Please ensure that your team's information is complete and accurate, and that this form is not duplicated.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form and ensure that all required fields are completed. The fields that are marked as required must be filled out to proceed with the request.
*   Select your team's preferred apparel type (shirts, pants, or jacket) and choose the corresponding sizes and colors.
*   Specify the quantity of each item you would like to request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Team Name

*   **Team Name**: `team_name`
    - This field is used to identify your team's name.

### Shirts

*   **Shirt Size**: `shirt_size`
    - Select the size of the shirt you would like to request.
    - Options: Small, Medium, Large

### Shirts - Colors

*   **Shirt Color**: `shirt_color`
    - Select the color of the shirt you would like to request.
    - Options: Blue, Red, Green

### Shirts - Quantity

*   **Shirt Quantity**: `shirt_quantity`
    - Specify the quantity of shirts you would like to request.

### Pants

*   **Pants Size**: `pants_size`
    - Select the size of the pants you would like to request.
    - Options: Small, Medium, Large

### Pants - Colors

*   **Pants Color**: `pants_color`
    - Select the color of the pants you would like to request.
    - Options: Blue, Red, Green

### Pants - Quantity

*   **Pants Quantity**: `pants_quantity`
    - Specify the quantity of pants you would like to request.

### Jacket

*   **Jacket Size**: `jacket_size`
    - Select the size of the jacket you would like to request.
    - Options: Small, Medium, Large

### Jacket - Colors

*   **Jacket Color**: `jacket_color`
    - Select the color of the jacket you would like to request.
    - Options: Blue, Red, Green

### Jacket - Quantity

*   **Jacket Quantity**: `jacket_quantity`
    - Specify the quantity of jacket you would like to request.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Review your team's information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator for assistance.
