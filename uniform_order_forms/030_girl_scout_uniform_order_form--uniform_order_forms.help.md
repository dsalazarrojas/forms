# Girl Scout Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to purchase Girl Scout uniforms for their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Troop Number in the "Troop Number" field.
2. Enter the first name of the child for whom you are ordering the uniform in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter the last name of the child for whom you are ordering the uniform in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter the email address of the parent or guardian in the "Parent Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian in the "Parent Phone" field.
6. Select the payment method you would like to use for the order from the "Payment Method" options.
7. Enter the quantity of the uniform item you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
8. Select the item you would like to order from the "Item Name" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Troop Number** (`troop_number`, number, required: false): Enter your troop number to associate your order with your troop.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the child for whom you are ordering the uniform.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the child for whom you are ordering the uniform.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select from one of the following payment methods: Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the uniform item you would like to order.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the following items: Shirt, Pants, Hat, or Scarf.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* We will contact you with any issues or questions regarding your order.
