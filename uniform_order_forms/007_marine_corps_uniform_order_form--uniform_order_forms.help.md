# Marine Corps Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Corps Uniform Order Form is a simple form designed to collect important information from customers who need to place an order for uniforms. This form gathers details about the customer, their shipping preferences, and payment methods, as well as any additional notes they may have. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the order is processed smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Marine Corps Uniform Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Customer Information by filling in the fields on the "Customer Information" page.
2. Enter your shipping information on the "Shipping Information" page.
3. Select the product you would like to order on the "Product Information" page.
4. Enter your payment information on the "Payment Information" page.
5. Add any additional customer notes on the "Customer Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Information**: Provide the shipping address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Product Information**: Choose the specific uniform you would like to order, and include any customization options if necessary.
* **Payment Information**: Enter your payment method, including the credit card number, expiration date, and security code.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions you have regarding your order.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to include all required fields, as indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
- If you have any special requests or questions, don't hesitate to contact us using the contact information provided.
- We strive to process orders efficiently, but please allow 3-5 business days for order processing.
