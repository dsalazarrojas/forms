# Team Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Uniform Order Form is a tool to collect information about team uniform preferences, allowing teams to order uniforms with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your team's uniform type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as the customer representative.
3. Enter your email address associated with your order.
4. Enter your phone number for further contact and communication.
5. Specify the quantity of uniforms you would like to order.
6. Add any additional comments or instructions for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Uniform Type**: Select the type of uniform your team is interested in ordering.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the representative for your team or organization.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order and any updates.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact and communication.
* **Order Quantity**: Specify the number of uniforms you would like to order.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order, such as specific sizes or special requests.

## Tips
- Ensure your team uniform type selection accurately reflects your team's requirements.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure smooth communication.
- Clearly specify your order quantity and any additional comments to avoid delays or errors.
