# Soccer Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Uniform Order Form is designed to collect information for soccer uniform orders. This form will help the team or organization to process and fulfill orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `first_name` field with your name.
2. Enter your `email` address in the `email` field.
3. Select the `jersey_size` that you need for your uniform from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose the team you would like to order from the `team` dropdown menu.
5. Select the payment method you would like to use for the order from the `payment_method` dropdown menu.
6. Enter the `payment_amount` for the order.
7. If you would like to specify a date and time for the order, you can enter it in the `date_ordered` and `time_ordered` fields.
8. Add any additional notes about your order in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive order updates.
* **Jersey Size (`jersey_size`, select_one, required): Select the size you need for your uniform from the dropdown menu.
* **Team (`team`, select_multiple, required): Choose the team you would like to order from. 
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you would like to use for the order.
* **Payment Amount (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you would like to pay for the order.
* **Date Ordered (`date_ordered`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to have the order fulfilled if different from the current date. 
* **Time Ordered (`time_ordered`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to have the order fulfilled if different from the current time. 
* **Notes (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions about your order.
