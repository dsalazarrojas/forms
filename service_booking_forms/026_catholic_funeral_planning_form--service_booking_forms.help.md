# Catholic Funeral Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catholic Funeral Planning Form is designed to help funeral directors, clergy, and family members collect necessary information for planning a deceased person's funeral service. This form will guide you through the process of gathering details about the deceased, their family contact information, service preferences, and burial details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand its structure and sections.
2. Start by providing the deceased's information, including their full name, date of birth, and date of death.
3. Then, provide the primary contact person's name and their relationship to the deceased.
4. Enter the preferred service type, date, and time (if required).
5. Select the church where the service will take place.
6. Choose the burial type and cemetery details (if applicable).
7. Optionally, select a casket type preference.
8. Consider providing music and flower preferences.
9. Add any special requests or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deceased Full Name** (`deceased_full_name`, **text**, required): Enter the deceased person's full name, including any middle names or nicknames.
* **Date of Birth** (`deceased_date_of_birth`, **date**, required): Provide the deceased person's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date of Death** (`deceased_date_of_death`, **date**, required): Enter the deceased person's date of death in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person, typically a family member or close relative.
* **Relationship to Deceased** (`primary_contact_relationship`, **text**, required): Describe the relationship between the primary contact person and the deceased.
* **Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, **text**, required): Enter the primary contact person's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`primary_contact_email`, **email**, optional): Provide the primary contact person's email address (if applicable).
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, **select_one**, required): Choose the type of service that best suits the deceased's wishes, such as Traditional Wake and Funeral Mass or Memorial Service.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`service_date`, **date**, required): Enter the preferred date for the service in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Preferred Service Time** (`service_time`, **time**, optional): Specify the preferred time for the service (if applicable).
* **Church Name** (`church_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the parish or church where the service will take place.
* **Burial Type** (`burial_type`, **select_one**, required): Select the type of burial that suits the deceased's wishes, such as Ground Burial or Cremation with Burial.
* **Cemetery Name** (`cemetery_name`, **text**, optional): Provide the name of the cemetery where the burial will take place.
* **Casket Type Preference** (`casket_preference`, **select_one**, optional): Select the preferred type of casket, such as wood or metal.
* **Cremation Preference** (`cremation_preference`, **text**, optional): If cremation is selected, provide any additional preference or instructions.
* **Music Selections** (`music_selections`, **text**, optional): Enter the preferred hymns or music that the deceased would like to be played during the service.
* **Flowers Preference** (`flowers_preference`, **text**, optional): Specify the preferred flowers or floral arrangements that the deceased would like to have.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, **text**, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions, such as readings or memorial details.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the deceased and their wishes.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to prevent errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the funeral director or clergy for guidance.
