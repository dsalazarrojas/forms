# Pest Control Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Appointment Form is designed to help customers book a pest control service with our company. This form collects essential information to schedule an appointment tailored to the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the Service Booking Form (Page 1).
2. Provide your customer name in the Customer Name field.
3. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
4. Select the type of service you require (Pest Control, Pest Control Service, or Pest Control Treatment).
5. Choose a preferred date and time for the appointment.
6. Select the type of pest control service you need (Pest Control, Pest Control Service, or Pest Control Treatment).
7. Enter your address where the service will be performed.
8. Add any additional information about your appointment.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Booking Form (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form where you will start by providing your service booking information.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require (Pest Control, Pest Control Service, or Pest Control Treatment).
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date for your appointment. The format is mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Preferred Time**: Select a time for your appointment. The format is hh:mm.
* **Pest Control Service**: Select the type of pest control service you need (Pest Control, Pest Control Service, or Pest Control Treatment).
* **Address**: Provide the address where the service will be performed.
* **Additional Information**: Add any extra information about your appointment, such as specific pest concerns or allergies.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.

## Tips
- Please ensure the address is accurate, as this is where our technicians will visit you.
- If you have any allergies or specific requests, mention them in the Additional Information field.
- Our team will contact you to confirm your appointment and provide further instructions.
