# Window Wash Service Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for the window wash service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the "Service Date" when you would like to receive the window wash service.
3. Confirm if you need the service for a window type by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Provide your address to help us deliver the service efficiently.
5. Let us know your payment terms by selecting "Yes", "No", and "Payment Details" if required.
6. You can add any additional notes in the "Notes" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Service Date**: This is the date on which you would like to receive the window wash service. Please select a date from the calendar.
* **Window Type**: Confirm if you need the service for a window type or not.
* **Address**: Enter your full address, including house number and postal code.
* **Payment Terms**: Let us know your payment terms, including if you need to pay on the spot or not.
* **Payment Details**: If you are paying on the spot, enter your payment details.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or information regarding the service.
