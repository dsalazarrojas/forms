# Cleaning Service Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are requesting cleaning services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information section:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email Address: Enter your contact email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Fill out the Service Location section:
	* Service Address: Enter the full street address of the location that needs cleaning.
	* City: Enter the city or town of the location.
	* State: Enter the state or province of the location.
	* ZIP or Postal Code: Enter the postal code of the location.
3. Choose your Service Request Details:
	* Type of Cleaning Service: Select the type of cleaning service you need.
	* Urgency Level: Select how soon you need the service.
	* Preferred Service Date: Enter the date you would like the service to be performed.
	* Preferred Time: Select the time of day you would like the service to be performed.
4. Specify Areas and Tasks:
	* Areas That Need Cleaning: Select all the areas that need cleaning.
	* Additional Services Requested: Select any additional services you need.
5. Provide Household or Business Details:
	* Do You Have Pets: If you have pets, select "Yes".
	* Pet Instructions: If you have pets, enter special instructions about them.
	* Allergies or Chemical Sensitivities: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, enter them here.
6. Choose your Payment and Billing Information:
	* Preferred Payment Method: Select how you would like to pay.
7. Additional Information:
	* Special Requests or Notes: Enter any additional information or requests you have.
