# Makeup Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and specific details related to a makeup service. It will help the service provider understand the client's needs and preferences for a custom makeup service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields to ensure your information is submitted correctly.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing event details to help the service provider offer the best possible experience.
3. If you have any allergies or special requests, please include them in the "Allergies Information" field.
4. Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your name and contact details so the service provider can get in touch with you to finalize your appointment.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Here, describe the event or occasion you're looking to have a makeup service for. This could be a wedding, prom, or any other special occasion.
* **Makeup Preferences** (`makeup_preferences`, text, required: false): In this field, let the service provider know about your favorite makeup styles, colors, or any specific services you're interested in (e.g., bridal, special occasion, everyday makeup).
* **Allergies Information** (`allergies`, text, required: false): If you have any allergies, please list them here so the service provider can take necessary precautions during the service.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): This is the button to click after filling out the form.
