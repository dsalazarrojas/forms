# Towing Job Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information required for booking a towing service. Please fill in the form with your details and submit it to proceed with the booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job details in the first field.
2. Provide the vehicle details in the second field.
3. Enter your contact information in the third field.
4. Specify the towing location in the fourth field.
5. Choose the towing time in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of the job or the reason for the towing service.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the make, model, year, and any other relevant details about the vehicle that is in need of towing.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name, phone number, and any other contact information to help us reach you for further instructions or updates.
* **Towing Location** (`towing_location`, text, optional):
	+ Specify the exact location where the vehicle is currently situated.
* **Towing Time** (`towing_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose the desired time for the towing service to take place.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth processing of your booking request.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the job details field.
* Our team will contact you shortly after submitting this form to confirm your booking and provide further instructions.
