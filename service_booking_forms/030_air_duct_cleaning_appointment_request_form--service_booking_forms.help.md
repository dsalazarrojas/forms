# Air Duct Cleaning Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an air duct cleaning service appointment with our company. Fill out the form with your information to schedule a suitable time for our team to visit your location.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your contact phone number for us to get in touch with you about the appointment.
3. Enter your email address for our records, if you have one.
4. Enter the address of the location where the service will take place.
5. Choose a date for the appointment.
6. Select a time for the appointment.
7. Specify whether the service is for a residential or commercial property.
8. Enter the approximate area to be serviced in square meters.
9. Provide any special instructions or access notes for our team.
10. Read and agree to our service terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number we can use to contact you about your appointment.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for our records, if you have one.
* **Service address** (`service_address`): Enter the exact address of the location where the service will take place.
* **Preferred appointment date** (`preferred_date`): Choose a date that works best for you. Our team will review and confirm the appointment with you.
* **Preferred appointment time** (`preferred_time`): Select a time of day for your appointment (morning, afternoon, or evening). This will help us estimate the job duration.
* **Property type** (`property_type`): Choose between "Residential" and "Commercial" to specify the type of property.
* **Approximate area to service** (`approximate_area`): Enter the approximate area in square meters of the property that needs cleaning. This will help our team estimate the job duration.
* **Special instructions or access notes** (`special_instructions`): Provide any special instructions or access notes for our team to know before the service, such as gate codes or parking instructions.
* **Consent to service terms** (`consent_terms`): Read and agree to our service terms by typing "Yes" in the text field. This includes consent to our scheduling and service terms.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information, as this will help us prepare for your appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
* Our team will review and confirm your appointment after receiving your form submission.
