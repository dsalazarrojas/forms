# Tattoo Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Consultation Form is a tool used to gather information from potential clients about their tattoo consultation requirements. This form helps to streamline the consultation process and provides a convenient way for clients to provide detailed information about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Customize the form by selecting your business information in the "Customize the form for your business" field.
2. Provide a link to your website and Facebook book now button in the "Link form to your website and Facebook book now button" field.
3. If you would like to create a free Zapier account, enter the required information in the "Create Zapier free forever account" field.
4. If you would like to create a Trello board, enter the required information in the "Create Trello free forever account" field.
5. Enter the details for creating a board in Trello in the "Create a board in Trello called" field.
6. Add a card to your Trello board in the "Add a card to board click it open and" field.
7. Go back to Zapier and create a zap in the "Go back to Zapier and create a zap" field.
8. Set up your Trello board with 4 steps in the "Create 4 steps in your board New/Consulting/Appointments/Done" field.
9. Take it a step further by zapping your Trello board in the "Take it a step further by zapping your" field.
10. Review and confirm that the form is complete in the "Done" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customize the form for your business**: Enter your business name, address, and contact information to associate with your consultation form.
* **Link form to your website and Facebook book now button**: Enter the URL of your website and Facebook book now button to allow clients to easily access your website and book now.
* **Create Zapier free forever account**: Enter the information required to create a free Zapier account.
* **Create Trello free forever account**: Enter the information required to create a free Trello account.
* **Create a board in Trello called**: Enter the name of the board you would like to create in Trello.
* **Add a card to board click it open and**: Enter the card title and description to add to your Trello board.
* **Go back to Zapier and create a zap**: Enter the trigger and action for the Zapier zap.
* **Create 4 steps in your board New/Consulting/Appointments/Done**: Enter the title and description for each of the 4 steps in your Trello board.
* **Take it a step further by zapping your**: Enter the trigger and action for the Zapier zap.
* **Done**: Review and confirm that the form is complete.
