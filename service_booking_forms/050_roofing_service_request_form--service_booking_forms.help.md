# Roofing Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roofing Service Request Form is used to collect information from clients about their roofing service requests. This form is designed to be filled out by clients who need assistance with their roofing needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the relevant services you are requesting from the available options.
3. Provide any additional details or notes in the "Description" field.
4. Submit the form to send your request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name
* **Client Name**: (`client_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your name as the client or customer making the request.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further contact or discussion.

### Service Requested
* **Service Requested**: (`service_requested`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the specific service you are requesting, such as "Lawn care" or "Roof Repair".

### Start Date
* **Start Date**: (`start_date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date when you would like the service to start. This can be a specific date or a range of dates.

### End Date
* **End Date**: (`end_date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date when you would like the service to end. This can be a specific date or a range of dates.

### Description
* **Description**: (`description`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional details or notes about your request.

### Address
* **Address**: (`address`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the address where the service is needed.

### Email
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for further contact or discussion.

### Preferred Contact Time
* **Preferred Contact Time**: (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Select the time of day when you prefer to be contacted for further discussion or updates.

### Service Request
* **Service Request**: (`service_request`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional details or notes about your service request.

### Notes
* **Notes**: (`notes`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional notes or details that may be helpful for our team to know.

### Additional Services
* **Additional Services**: (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select any additional services you may require from our team, such as "Lawn care", "Plumbing", etc.

### Service Request 2
* **Service Request 2**: (`service_request_2`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the second service request from the available options.

### Roofing Service Area
* **Roofing Service Area**: (`roofing_service_area`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the area of the roofing service needed, such as "Front of home", "Back of home", etc.

### Other Services
* **Other Services**: (`other_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select any additional services not listed in the "Additional Services" field.

### Special Instructions
* **Special Instructions**: (`special_instructions`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Provide any special instructions or requirements for our team to follow.

### Assigned
* **Assigned**: (`assigned`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the representative or technician assigned to your request.

### Assigned Time
* **Assigned Time**: (`assigned_time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Select the time when the representative or technician is assigned to your request.

### Client Type
* **Client Type**: (`client_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of client you are (e.g. residential, commercial, government agency).

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method**: (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the method of contact you prefer, such as phone, email, or online form.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person**: (`contact_person`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the contact person responsible for your request.
