# Equipment Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Repair Request Form is used to submit a request for equipment repair to the maintenance team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
2. Enter your name and contact information to associate with the repair request.
3. Enter the equipment ID to specify the equipment requiring repair.
4. Select the repair type to choose between "repair now" or "schedule repair".
5. Provide a specific date for the repair, if scheduled.
6. Describe the issue with the equipment to help the maintenance team identify the problem.
7. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information to associate with the repair request.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment requiring repair.
* **Repair Type** (`repair_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "repair now" and "schedule repair" to determine the urgency and timing of the repair.
* **Repair Date** (`repair_date`, date, optional): Enter a specific date for the scheduled repair, if applicable.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe the issue with the equipment to help the maintenance team identify the problem and provide effective assistance.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about the equipment ID, please contact your supervisor or the equipment department for assistance.
* Ensure accuracy in entering your name and contact information to ensure proper communication.
* Be as detailed as possible in describing the equipment issue to help the maintenance team provide efficient service.
* Only submit the form once you have reviewed all the information to avoid duplication and ensure timely service.
