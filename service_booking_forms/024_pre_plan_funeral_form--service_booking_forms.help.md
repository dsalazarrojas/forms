# Pre Plan Funeral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Plan Funeral Form is designed to help clients or family members gather information and make necessary arrangements for the funeral of a loved one. This form collects details such as name, date and time, location, time zone, service type, and contact information to facilitate planning and preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of the deceased.
2. Enter the date of the funeral.
3. Select the time of the funeral.
4. Specify the location of the funeral.
5. Choose the time zone for the funeral.
6. Indicate whether a service will be held.
7. Fill in your name.
8. Provide your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`funeral_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the deceased.
* **Date** (`funeral_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the funeral in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`funeral_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the funeral in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Location** (`funeral_location`, text, required/optional): Specify the location of the funeral (e.g., church, crematorium, etc.).
* **Time Zone** (`funeral_time_zone`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the time zone for the funeral from the available options (e.g., Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific).
* **Service Type** (`funeral_service_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether a service will be held ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to choose the correct time zone for the funeral.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact us for assistance.
