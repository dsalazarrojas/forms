# Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultation Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about you and your requirements for a consultation with our expert advisors. By completing this form, you will be helping us understand your needs and preferences, which will enable us to tailor our advice to suit your specific goals and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Consultation Questionnaire, follow these steps:

* Please fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
* Select a preferred date and time for your consultation.
* Choose the service you are interested in (Business Strategy, Financial Planning, Marketing Audit, Operational Efficiency, or Other).
* Specify your primary goal for the consultation.
* If you have any current challenges, please briefly describe them.
* If you have an estimated budget range, select one that applies to you.
* Let us know how you heard about us.
* Finally, add any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): We will send a confirmation email to this address after you submit the form.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Include your area code to ensure correct dialing.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_consultation_date`, required): Select a date that works best for you.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, required): Choose your preferred time of day (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Service of Interest** (`service_of_interest`, required): Select the service you are inquiring about (e.g., Business Strategy, Financial Planning, etc.).
* **Primary Goal for Consultation** (`primary_goal`, required): Briefly describe your main objective for the consultation.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, optional): Briefly describe any obstacles you are facing.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, optional): Select a budget range that applies to you.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, optional): Referral, social media, or other means.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any other information you would like to share with us.
