# Auto Service Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers for an auto service appointment. It helps our team understand your needs and schedule the appointment efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. These are essential details to ensure we can contact you for appointment confirmation and any updates.
2. Enter your **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year**. This helps us identify your vehicle and provide the correct service.
3. Select the **Primary Service Reason** for bringing your vehicle in. This could be an **Oil Change**, **Brake Inspection**, **Tire Rotation**, **Engine Light On**, or **Other**.
4. Describe the **Detailed Symptoms** you are experiencing with your vehicle.
5. Choose a **Preferred Date** and **Time Slot** that works best for you.
6. Select how you would like to **Drop-off** the vehicle. You can choose **In-person**, **Key Drop-box**, or **Valet Pick-up**.
7. Decide if you require a **Loaner Vehicle**. Please note that availability is not guaranteed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year**: Enter your vehicle's details, e.g., 2020 Jeep Wrangler.
* **Primary Service Reason**: Select the reason for bringing your vehicle in. This will help us prepare for the service.
* **Detailed Symptoms**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with your vehicle in detail.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a date that works best for your appointment.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Choose a time that suits you for the appointment.
* **Drop-off Method**: Select how you want to drop off your vehicle.
* **Require Loaner Vehicle?**: Decide if you need a loaner vehicle. Please note that availability is not guaranteed.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that our team can schedule the appointment according to your needs.
