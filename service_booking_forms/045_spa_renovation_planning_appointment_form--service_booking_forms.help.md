# spa_renovation_planning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide spa owners and managers with information about user's spa renovation needs and preferences. It helps to tailor the renovation process to individual requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the User Input page with the required and optional information.
2. Provide detailed information about the project in the Project Details page.
3. Submit your appointment request in the Appointment Request page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information in this field. This will help us to get in touch with you to discuss your renovation needs.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your project requirements, including the scope of work, timelines, and any specific needs or concerns you may have.
* **Appointment Request** (`appointment_request`, `text`, required: false): In this field, specify the specific services or consultations you are interested in booking. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide clear and concise information in each field to ensure that our team can efficiently process your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* We will follow up with you to confirm your appointment and discuss your project details.
