# Service Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to provide a structured way to document the terms of a service agreement between a service provider and a client. It ensures that all necessary information is captured, making it a useful tool for both the service provider and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have a clear understanding of the service agreement terms. Here is a suggested step-by-step guide on how to complete this form:

1. **Start by completing the mandatory fields**: The fields marked as "required" should be completed first. These include:
	* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the client, as agreed upon in the service agreement.
	* **Service Agreement Template**: Enter the title and details of the service agreement.
	* **Service Details**: Describe the services offered by the service provider to the client.
	* **Contract Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the service contract.
2. **Optional fields for additional information**: If relevant to the service agreement:
	* **Service Type**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the service has any specific type or category.
	* **Service Location**: If the service will be provided in a specific location, enter the details here.
	* **Client Contact**: Include any contact information for the client.
	* **Service Provider**: Include any contact information for the service provider.
	* **Termination Date**: If applicable, enter the date for termination of the service contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the client, as agreed upon in the service agreement.
* **Service Agreement Template** (`service_agreement_form`, text, required): This is the title and description of the service agreement. Enter a brief overview of what the agreement covers.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if the service has a specific type or category. This will help in categorizing the services.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the services offered by the service provider to the client.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the service will be provided.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, optional): Include any contact information for the client.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, optional): Include any contact information for the service provider.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the service contract.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date for termination of the service contract.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Include any terms and conditions of the service agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure all mandatory fields are completed before finalizing the form.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
* This template serves as a basic structure for service agreements. Adjust it according to the specific needs of your business or organization.
