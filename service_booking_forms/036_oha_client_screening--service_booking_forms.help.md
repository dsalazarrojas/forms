# oha_client_screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The oha_client_screening form is used to collect information about clients for services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's appointment date and time fields.
2. Enter the client's phone number and email address if applicable.
3. Indicate whether the client is a staff member of the organization.
4. Select the services that the client is receiving.
5. Add any additional notes about the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Screening (Text)** (`client_screening`, required: false): This is the main field for collecting client information.
* **Appointment (Date)** (`appointment`, required: false): Enter the client's appointment date.
* **Client Phone Number (Text)** (`client_phone_number`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **oha_staff_member (Yes/No)** (`oha_staff_member`, required: false): Indicate whether the client is a staff member of the organization.
* **oha_date (Date)** (`oha_date`, required: false): Enter the client's date of birth or other date relevant to their service.
* **oha_time (Time)** (`oha_time`, required: false): Select a specific time for the client's appointment.
* **oha_service (Yes/No/Multiple)** (`oha_service`, required: false): Choose the services the client is receiving.
* **Client Note (Note)** (`oha_client_note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the client.
* **Client Email (Email)** (`oha_client_email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address.

## Tips
- Only fill in the required fields that are applicable to your client's situation.
- Use this form only for its intended purpose to avoid duplication and errors.
- If you're unsure about a field, contact the organization for clarification.
