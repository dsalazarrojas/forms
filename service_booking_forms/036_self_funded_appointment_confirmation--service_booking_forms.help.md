# Self Funded Appointment Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Funded Appointment Confirmation form is designed to gather patient information and appointment details for self-funded appointments. It is intended to be used by patients or their representatives to confirm their appointment schedules and payment arrangements. This form helps to streamline the communication process and ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the contact number of the patient or their representative in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Select a payment date from the calendar in the "Payment Date" field.
4. Choose the payment method (Online or Offline) in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Enter the patient's ID (if applicable) in the "Patient ID" field.
6. Select the appointment date from the calendar in the "Appointment Date" field.
7. Choose the appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Patient Name (patient_name)
* **Patient Name** (text): Enter the name of the patient who is scheduled for the appointment.

### 2. Contact Number (contact_number)
* **Contact Number** (text): Provide the phone number or other contact information for the patient or their representative.

### 3. Payment Date (payment_date)
* **Payment Date** (date): Select the date when the payment for the appointment will be made.

### 4. Payment Method (payment_method)
* **Payment Method** (select_one): Choose how the payment will be made (Online or Offline).

### 5. Patient ID (patient_id)
* **Patient ID** (text): If applicable, enter the patient's ID or reference number.

### 6. Appointment Date (appointment_date)
* **Appointment Date** (date): Select the date of the appointment.

### 7. Appointment Time (appointment_time)
* **Appointment Time** (time): Choose the time of the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth communication and scheduling.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please review your completed form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is correct.
