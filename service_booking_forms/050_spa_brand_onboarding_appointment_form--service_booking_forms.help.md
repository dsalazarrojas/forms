# spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and request meetings or consultations with a spa brand representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, including your name, phone number, and email.
2.  Select a preferred date and time for the appointment.
3.  Choose your service interest by selecting "Spa Onboarding" or "Other" from the drop-down menu.
4.  If applicable, select multiple services you are interested in.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment in the "Note" field.
6.  Click "Submit" to send in your appointment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_first_page** (`text`, required/optional): Please enter a brief message or introduction for your appointment request.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name to associate with this appointment request.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive communication about your appointment.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_date** (`date`, required/optional): Select a preferred date for the appointment.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_time** (`time`, required/optional): Select a preferred time for the appointment.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_select_one_1** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you are interested in "Spa Onboarding" or "Other" services.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_select_multiple** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple services you are interested in, if available.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_note** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
- **spa_brand_onboarding_appointment_form_submit** (`text`, required/optional): Confirm your appointment request by clicking "Submit".
