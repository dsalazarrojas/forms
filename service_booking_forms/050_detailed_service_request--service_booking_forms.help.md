# Detailed Service Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Detailed Service Request form is used to submit detailed service requests to the service team. This form is not intended for urgent or time-sensitive requests. If your request has a deadline or requires immediate attention, please contact the service team directly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are submitting a new service request.
2. Choose the correct answer for "First Page" to indicate if this is a new service request.
3. Select the service you are requesting and provide a clear description in the "Service Requested" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the service required, if applicable, in the "Description" field.
5. If you have a preferred date for the service, select it in the "Date Service Required" field.
6. If you have a preferred time for the service, select it in the "Preferred Time" field.
7. Enter your contact information, including email, to allow the service team to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page (id: 1)
* **First Page** (`select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to confirm that this is a new service request. Select "Yes" to indicate that this is a new service request.

### Service Requested (id: 2)
* **Service Requested** (`text`, required: false): Enter a clear and brief description of the service you are requesting.

### Description (id: 3)
* **Description** (`text`, required: false): If you have any additional details or context to share about your service request, enter them here.

### Date Service Required (id: 4)
* **Date Service Required** (`date`, required: false): Enter a date when you need the service. Please format the date as "YYYY-MM-DD" to avoid errors.

### Preferred Time (id: 5)
* **Preferred Time** (`time`, required: false): If you have a preferred time for the service, select it from the available options. This field is not guaranteed to be honored.

### Contact Info (id: 6)
* **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact information so that the service team can reach out to you regarding your service request.

### Email (id: 7)
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that the service team can contact you regarding your service request. Please ensure it is a valid email address.

### Additional Info (id: 8)
* **Additional Info** (`text`, required: false): If you have any additional information or context about your service request, enter it here.

### Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you are submitting a new service request by selecting "Yes" in the "First Page" field.
- Be clear and concise in your service request description and description fields to ensure accurate processing.
- Use the provided format for dates and times to avoid errors.
