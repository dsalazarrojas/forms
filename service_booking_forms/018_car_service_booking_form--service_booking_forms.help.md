# Car Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Service Booking Form is used to book a service for your vehicle. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details (name, email, and phone number) in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your vehicle information (year, make, model, and registration number).
3. Choose the preferred service date and time for your vehicle.
4. Select the type of service needed for your vehicle.
5. Describe the problem or concern with your vehicle.
6. Choose your preferred payment method for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: A required field to identify yourself.
* **Email address**: A required field to ensure we can contact you about your service booking.
* **Phone number**: A required field for us to contact you about your service booking.
* **VEHICLE INFORMATION**:
	+ **Vehicle year**: A required field to identify the year of your vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle make**: A required field to identify the make of your vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle model**: A required field to identify the model of your vehicle.
* **Registration number**: An optional field, but it's helpful to have it for our records.
* **Current mileage**: An optional field, but it helps us to know the vehicle's condition.
* **SERVICE BOOKING DETAILS**:
	+ **Preferred service date**: A required field to schedule your service booking.
	+ **Preferred service time**: A required field to schedule your service booking.
* **Type of service needed**: A required field to identify the type of service required.
* **Describe the service you need**: A required field to provide us with more details about the issue with your vehicle.
* **Service urgency**: An optional field to help us prioritize your service booking.
* **Preferred mechanic or service center**: An optional field to choose your preferred service provider.
* **Warranty coverage**: An optional field to check if your vehicle has warranty coverage.
* **Do you need a loaner vehicle?**: An optional field to check if you need a loaner vehicle.
* **Drop-off or mobile service?**: A required field to choose between drop-off at our service center or mobile service at your location.
* **Service address (if not at center)**: An optional field if you're opting for mobile service.
* **Has your vehicle been serviced here before?**: An optional field to help us know your vehicle's history.
* **Special requests or requirements**: An optional field to provide us with any special requests or requirements.
* **Are you aware of the estimated service cost?**: An optional field to check if you're aware of the estimated cost.
* **Preferred payment method**: An optional field to choose your preferred payment method.
* **Additional notes or comments**: An optional field to provide us with any additional information about your vehicle or service booking.
