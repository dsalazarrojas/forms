# it_service_request_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_service_request_form_2" form is a general service request form used to collect information from users about their service needs or inquiries. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your service request.
2. Enter your name and contact information (name, contact, and email).
3. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
4. Describe the problem you are experiencing.
5. Add any further explanation or details about your issue.
6. Optionally, add any comments if you have additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (service_request_form_2_page_1)**: Select the category that best describes your service request.
* **Name**: Enter your name so we can address you appropriately.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can respond to you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Problem Description (problem_description)**: Describe the problem you are experiencing in detail.
* **Further Explanation (further_explanation)**: If necessary, provide further details or explanations about your issue.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or information about your issue, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your service request is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to add them in the "comments" field.
* If you have further questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us directly.
