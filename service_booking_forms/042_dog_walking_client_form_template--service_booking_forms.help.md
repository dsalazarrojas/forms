# Dog Walking Client Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for dog owners or their representatives to provide information about their dog walking needs. The collected data will be used to schedule and tailor a dog walking experience that suits the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`Client Info`, text, optional): Enter the client's name, address, and contact information.
* **Appointment** (`Appointment`, text, optional): Enter the date, time, and duration for the dog walking appointment. Be sure to include the start and end time for the walk.
* **Services** (`Services`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of walk service that suits the client's needs. Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Morning Walk
	+ Afternoon Walk
	+ Evening Walk
	+ Special Walk
