# Garden Pond Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Pond Cleaning Request Form is designed for customers to request service and maintenance for their garden ponds. It helps service providers collect necessary information to schedule and deliver efficient services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide below:

1.  Fill in the "Schedule an appointment" field to specify your preferred appointment date and time.
2.  In the "Pond details" field, provide a brief description of your pond, including its location, size, and any relevant details.
3.  Enter your "Contact info" to ensure we can get in touch with you about your appointment.
4.  Select the type of service you require in the "Service type" field. Options include Algae treatment, General cleaning, and Pond maintenance.
5.  Specify your preferred date in the "Preferred date" field.
6.  Choose your preferred time in the "Preferred time" field.
7.  In the "Service location" field, select the location of your pond (Garden pond, Indoor pond, or Outdoor pond).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Schedule an appointment** (`schedule_an_appointment`, text, optional): 
    Specify your preferred appointment date and time. This will help us schedule your appointment efficiently.
*   **Pond details** (`pond_details`, text, optional): 
    Provide a brief description of your pond, including its location, size, and any relevant details. This will help us understand your pond's specific needs.
*   **Contact info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): 
    Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your appointment.
*   **Service type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): 
    Choose the type of service you require for your pond. Select Algae treatment for issues related to algae growth, General cleaning for routine maintenance, or Pond maintenance for more comprehensive services.
*   **Preferred date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): 
    Specify your preferred date for the appointment. We will do our best to accommodate your request.
*   **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): 
    Choose your preferred time for the appointment. Please note that our availability might vary.
*   **Service location** (`service_location`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select the location of your pond (Garden pond, Indoor pond, or Outdoor pond). This will help us know where to send our team.

## Tips

- Ensure you provide accurate information to help us schedule the appointment efficiently.
- Please be flexible with your preferred date and time as our availability may vary.
- If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them in the "Pond details" field.
- A member of our team will contact you to confirm your appointment and discuss any further details.
