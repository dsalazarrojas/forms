# piercing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients who have requested a piercing service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Client ID.
2. Provide your First name.
3. Provide your Last name.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Select a Preferred date for the service.
7. Select a Preferred time for the service.
8. Specify the Desired piercing you would like to receive.
9. Choose your preferred Artist from the list.
10. Write a Message to the service provider if you have any special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client ID** (`Client ID`, `number`, required): This is a unique identifier for the client.
* **First name** (`First name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred date** (`Preferred date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the service.
* **Preferred time** (`Preferred time`, `time`, required): Select a time for the service.
* **Desired piercing** (`Desired piercing`, `text`, required): Specify the type of piercing you would like to receive.
* **Preferred artist** (`Preferred artist`, `select_one`, required): Choose the artist who will perform the service.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, required): Write any additional information or special requests to the service provider.
