# Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking backstage services for events such as birthday parties, weddings, or photoshoots. It allows you to easily collect necessary information from customers, streamlining the booking process and reducing administrative tasks for service providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service you require (Hair, Makeup, or both) in the "Service Type" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the type of event (optional) in the "Event Type" field.
6. Select the number of services you require in the "Number of Services" field.
7. Enter the start time of the service in the "Service Start Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Details (Yes/No)** (`backstage_booking_form`, Select Multiple, required: False): This field allows you to select if you require any additional services such as hair styling or makeup applications.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, Select One, required: False): Select the type of service you require, whether it's Hair, Makeup, or both.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, Time, required: False): Enter the time you wish for the service to start, in the format "HH:MM".
* **Name** (`customer_name`, Text, required: False): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, Email, required: False): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Select One, required: False): Choose the type of event you are attending, such as a birthday party or wedding.
* **Number of Services** (`num_services`, Number, required: False): Select the number of services you require.
* **Service Start Time** (`service_start_time`, Time, required: False): Enter the start time of the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as this will be used for communication purposes.
* Enter your phone number correctly, so service providers can contact you.
* If you're unsure about the type of event, you can select "Other" and provide more details in the notes section.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the notes section (not available in this form).
