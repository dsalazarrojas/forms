# Snow Removal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Snow Removal Request Form is designed to collect necessary information for snow removal requests. This form will aid in the efficient management and assignment of tasks to ensure timely and effective snow removal services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is not a required field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is not a required field.
4. Specify your location in the "Location" field. This is not a required field.
5. Select the type of snow removal service you require in the "Type of Snow Removal" field. Choose from "Residential", "Commercial", or "Other".
6. Enter the date you would like the snow removal service to start in the "Date" field. This is not a required field.
7. Enter the start time for the snow removal service in the "Start Time" field. This is not a required field.
8. Enter the end time for the snow removal service in the "End Time" field. This is not a required field.
9. Add any additional notes about the snow removal service in the "Note" field. This is not a required field.
10. Select the staff member you would like to be assigned to your snow removal request in the "Assigned To" field. Choose from "Jimmy", "Jane", or "Other".
11. Enter any additional categories relevant to your snow removal request in the "Category" field. This is not a required field.
12. Provide any additional notes for the staff member assigned to your snow removal request in the "Additional Notes" field. This is not a required field.
13. Enter any additional comments for the snow removal request in the "Additional Comments" field. This is not a required field.
14. Enter the date you submitted the snow removal request in the "Submitted By Date" field. This is not a required field.
15. Enter the time you submitted the snow removal request in the "Submitted By Time" field. This is not a required field.
16. Enter your email address in the "Submitted By Email" field. This is not a required field.
17. Enter your phone number in the "Submitted By Phone" field. This is not a required field.
18. Enter your location in the "Submitted By Location" field. This is not a required field.
19. Provide a description of the type of snow removal service you require in the "Type of Snow Removal Description" field. This is not a required field.
20. Enter any notes for the submitter of the snow removal request in the "Note For Submitter" field. This is not a required field.
21. Enter any additional categories relevant to your snow removal request in the "Category 2", "Category 3", "Category 4", or "Category 5" fields. These are not required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you as the submitter of this snow removal request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Specify the location of the snow removal service you require.
* **Type of Snow Removal** (`snow_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the type of snow removal service you need, whether "Residential", "Commercial", or "Other".
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you would like the snow removal service to start.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the start time for the snow removal service.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the end time for the snow removal service.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes or information about your snow removal request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, not required): Select the staff member you would like to be assigned to your snow removal request.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional categories relevant to your snow removal request.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional notes for the staff member assigned to your snow removal request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments for the snow removal request.
* **Submitted By Date** (`submitted_by_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you submitted the snow removal request.
* **Submitted By Time** (`submitted_by_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time you submitted the snow removal request.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitted_by_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location of where you submitted the snow removal request.
* **Type of Snow Removal Description** (`snow_type_description`, `text`, not required): Provide a description of the type of snow removal service you require.
* **Note For Submitter** (`note_for_submitter`, `text`, not required): Enter any notes for the submitter of the snow removal request.
* **Category 2**, **Category 3**, **Category 4**, **Category 5** (`category_2`, `category_3`, `category_4`, `category_5`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional categories relevant to your snow removal request.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate assignment and scheduling of tasks.
* Ensure that you do not duplicate requests for the same location or time.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the snow removal request form, contact the relevant personnel.
