# SEO Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to book SEO services, providing essential details to ensure a smooth and efficient service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Choose your service options from the dropdown menu.
3. Select your preferred services for this booking.
4. Provide a date and time for your booking.
5. Specify your communication preference.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your booking.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact.
8. Confirm your booking by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the last two fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`seo_service_booking_form`): Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Service Options** (`service_booking_form`): Select the type of service you are interested in booking.
* **Service Preferences** (`seo_service_booking_form`): Choose the services you are interested in for this booking.
* **Booking Date** (`seo_service_booking_form_date`): Select a date for your booking. Please use the date format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/2023).
* **Booking Time** (`seo_service_booking_form_time`): Select a time for your booking. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Communication Preference** (`seo_service_booking_form_communication_preference`): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or no preference).
* **Note** (`seo_service_booking_form_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.
* **Email** (`seo_service_booking_form_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`seo_service_booking_form_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirmed** (`seo_service_booking_form_confirmed`): Confirm that you have submitted this form by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' (we recommend selecting 'Yes' to avoid errors).
* **Not Confirmed** (`seo_service_booking_form_not_confirmed`): Confirm that you have not submitted this form (we recommend selecting 'No' for internal tracking purposes).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- Be clear in your communication preference to ensure timely and efficient contact.
- Use the booking date and time format specified above for consistency.
