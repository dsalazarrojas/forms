# Daycare Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Service Booking Form is used to book services in a daycare setting for children or adults with disabilities. Please complete this form to provide your child's details and preferences for the services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the service option that best suits your needs from the "Booking Service" dropdown menu.
2. Provide your child's details, including their age and any relevant health information.
3. Choose the start and end dates for the service period.
4. Specify the start and end times for the service period (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your child's needs or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Service** (`service_booking_form`, select_one, **required**): Select the service option that best suits your needs.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, number, **required**): Please provide your child's age and any relevant health information.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, **optional**): Choose the start date for the service period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, **optional**): Choose the end date for the service period.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, **optional**): Specify the start time for the service period (if applicable).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, **optional**): Specify the end time for the service period (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or preferences about your child's needs.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary information and consent to book the service before completing this form.
- If you need help with any of the fields, please contact the service provider for assistance.
