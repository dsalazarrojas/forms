# Digital Artist Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect booking information for a digital artist's services, including artist details, service scheduling, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
2.  Enter your email address in the Email field.
3.  If you have a Platform ID, enter it in the Platform ID field.
4.  In the Phone field, enter your phone number.
5.  Type a message in the Message field if you have any additional information to provide.
6.  Select the service type from the Service Type dropdown menu.
7.  Enter the date for the service in the Date field.
8.  Choose the time for the service from the Time dropdown menu.
9.  Select the time type from the Time Type dropdown menu.
10. Enter the location for the service in the Location field.
11. Provide more details about the platform you are using in the Platform Details field.
12. Select the services you are interested in from the Service dropdown menu.
13. Enter the price for the service in the Price field.
14. Choose the currency for the service from the Currency dropdown menu.
15. Provide any additional notes about the service in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (text, required)**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Platform ID (number, optional)**: If you have a platform ID, enter it here.
*   **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Message (text, optional)**: Type any additional information you have about the service.
*   **Service Type (select_one, required)**: Choose the type of service you are booking.
*   **Date (date, required)**: Enter the date for the service.
*   **Time (time, required)**: Choose the time for the service.
*   **Time Type (select_one, required)**: Select the type of time for the service.
*   **Location (text, required)**: Enter the location for the service.
*   **Platform Details (text, required)**: Provide more details about the platform you are using.
*   **Service (select_one, required)**: Choose the service you are interested in.
*   **Price (number, required)**: Enter the price for the service.
*   **Currency (select_one, required)**: Choose the currency for the service.
*   **Notes (note, optional)**: Provide any additional notes about the service.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid any issues with submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
*   This form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient. If you encounter any issues, please report them to us so we can make improvements.
