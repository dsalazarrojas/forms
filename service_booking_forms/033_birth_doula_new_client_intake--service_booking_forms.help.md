# Birth Doula New Client Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new clients who are seeking doula services for their childbirth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the form carefully and understanding the purpose of each section.
2. Enter your full name, phone number, and email address in the "Client Details" section.
3. Enter the due date and number of weeks pregnant in the "Maternity Information" section.
4. Answer questions about your medical history, birth preferences, and logistics for the doula's support in the subsequent sections.
5. Be as detailed and honest as possible in answering the questions, as this information will help the doula understand your needs and provide personalized support.
6. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `text`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Partner Name and Phone** (`partner_info`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name and phone number of your partner.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected date of birth.
* **Weeks of Gestation** (`weeks_gestation`, `number`, required): Enter how many weeks pregnant you are.
* **Pregnancy Number** (`pregnancy_count`, `number`, required): Enter if this is your first, second, etc. pregnancy.
* **Number of Previous Births** (`birth_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of previous births.
* **Obstetrician or Midwife Name** (`obstetrician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Facility or Hospital** (`healthcare_facility`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital or healthcare facility where you will give birth.
* **Pregnancy Risk Status** (`pregnancy_risk_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your pregnancy is low-risk, moderate-risk, high-risk, or undetermined.
* **Brief Medical History** (`medical_history_brief`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Birth Philosophy** (`birth_philosophy`, `text`, required): Describe your ideal birth experience.
* **Pain Coping Methods** (`pain_coping_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the natural or medical pain relief methods you prefer.
* **Comfort Level with Medical Interventions** (`intervention_comfort_level`, `select_one`, required): Select how open you are to medical interventions.
* **Who Will Be in Your Birth Team** (`birth_team_composition`, `text`, required): Enter who will be present during the birth.
* **Cultural or Spiritual Needs** (`cultural_or_spiritual_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any special cultural or spiritual needs.
* **Important Considerations for Doula** (`dola_certification_importance`, `text`, required): Enter what matters most to you.
* **First Time Doula Client** (`first_time_doula_client`, `select_one`, required): Select whether this is your first time working with a doula.
* **Expectations for Doula** (`dola_expectations`, `text`, required): Enter your expectations for the doula.
* **Desired Support Duration** (`support_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select when the doula should arrive and leave.
* **Preferred Date to Begin Doula Services** (`start_date_preference`, `date`, optional): Enter when you would like to start the doula services.
* **Interest in Postpartum Support** (`postpartum_support_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you are interested in postpartum support.
* **Postpartum Support Duration** (`postpartum_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter how long you are interested in postpartum support.
* **Scheduling Flexibility** (`scheduling_flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Select how flexible you are with scheduling.
* **Questions and Concerns** (`questions_for_doula`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you have for the doula.
* **Special Considerations or Concerns** (`special_considerations`, `text`, optional): Enter any special considerations or concerns you have.
* **Permission to Follow-up and Discuss Details** (`consent_follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are okay with the doula following up with you to discuss details.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers, as this information will help the doula provide personalized support.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* The doula will use this information to tailor their support to your needs and preferences.
* It is okay to change your answers or provide additional information as your needs and preferences evolve throughout your pregnancy, labor, and postpartum period.
