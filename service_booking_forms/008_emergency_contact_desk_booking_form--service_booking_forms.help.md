# Emergency Contact Desk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for emergency contact desk services, such as requesting emergency assistance or reporting a non-urgent issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location you wish to visit.
2. Choose the type(s) of emergency you are experiencing.
3. Choose a start time for your visit.
4. Choose an end time for your visit.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you.
6. Enter a contact phone number.
7. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location** (`Location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location where you will be visiting.
* **Emergency Type** (`Emergency Type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more emergency types that apply to your situation.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, select_one, optional): Choose the start time for your booked slot.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, select_one, optional): Choose the end time for your booked slot.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
