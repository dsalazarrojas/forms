# Voice Actor Recording Time Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help voice actors book sessions for recording voiceovers, live recordings, or audiobooks. It will guide you through the process of selecting the best time and location for your session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your session details, such as the project name and any specific requirements.
2. Choose your desired session time and duration.
3. Select the project details, including any scripts or references.
4. Indicate whether you will need voice sessions during your recording session.
5. Select the recording location (studio, home studio, or mobile recording).
6. Choose the recording type (live, voiceover, or audiobook).
7. Enter the date and time you would like to book your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details**: Provide a brief description of your project or session.
* **Session Time**: Choose a time slot that suits you best. Please format your answer using the 24-hour clock (HH:MM) e.g., 14:00 or 15:00.
* **Session Duration**: Indicate how long you expect your recording session to take.
* **Project Details**: Describe your project or session in more detail, if needed.
* **Script Reference**: If you have any relevant scripts or documents related to your project, attach them here.
* **Voice Sessions**: Select any voice sessions you will need during your recording. 
* **Recording Location**: Choose the location of your recording session. This will determine the equipment and resources available.
* **Recording Equipment**: Select the equipment you require for your recording session. Please select one of the following options: Studios, Home Studio, or Mobile Recording.
* **Recording Type**: Select the type of recording you will be doing. Choose from Live Recording, Voiceover Recording, or Audiobook Recording.
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date you would like to book your session.
* **Booking Time**: Enter the time you would like to book your session. Please format your answer using the 24-hour clock (HH:MM) e.g., 14:00 or 15:00.

## Tips

* Please double-check your session details and dates before submitting the form.
* If you need any additional equipment or resources, make sure to mention it when filling out the "Recording Location" field.
* The form is not a commitment to a specific project or service, but rather a way to gather essential information for booking a session.
