# Remote Writers' Hub Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a service from a remote writer or freelancer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in the necessary information in the fields provided, starting from the top of the form.
2.  If you are booking on behalf of someone else, make sure to provide your own information in the "Submitter" field at the end.
3.  Select the service(s) required from the "Service Requested" field.
4.  Enter any additional details or comments in the "Project Brief" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1

*   **Booking Form** (`booking_form`, text, not required): This is the main form section where you can enter any information you want to provide.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name or the name of the person booking the service.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number or email address where the writer can contact you.
*   **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, not required): Enter the deadline for the project.
*   **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, select_multiple, not required): Select the service(s) you are booking.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person booking the service.
*   **Message** (`message`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or information about the project.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): This is a placeholder field and should not be filled in.
*   **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, not required): This is a placeholder field and should not be filled in.
*   **Page 2** (`page_2_section`, text, not required): This is the second page of the form.
*   **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional details about the project.
*   **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, not required): Enter the deadline for the project.
*   **Writer Name** (`writer_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Writer Email** (`writer_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Writer Phone** (`writer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Project Brief** (`project_brief`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the project.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, not required): Enter any payment terms or details.
*   **Service Terms** (`service_terms`, text, not required): Enter any service terms or details.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of submission.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of submission.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the submission.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the booking request.

### Page 2

*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of submission.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of submission.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the submission.
*   **Writer Name** (`writer_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Writer Email** (`writer_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Writer Phone** (`writer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the writer or freelancer.
*   **Project Brief** (`project_brief`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the project.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your booking request is complete.
*   Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the remote writers' hub administration.
*   Please note that this form is intended for booking services from remote writers or freelancers, and the fields may not apply to other types of services or bookings.
