# Braiding Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Braiding Service Registration Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from clients interested in booking braiding services. It helps the stylist understand the client's preferences, hair characteristics, and needs, ensuring an accurate consultation and appointment planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you would like it to be addressed.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you would like it to be addressed.
3. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for communication and appointment reminders.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
5. **Address**: Enter your complete home address.
6. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. **Requested Appointment Date**: Select your preferred appointment date.
8. **Requested Appointment Time**: Select your preferred appointment time.
9. **Service Type**: Choose the type of braiding service you prefer.
10. **Current Hair Length**: Select your natural hair length.
11. **Desired Braid Length**: Select the length you prefer for your braids.
12. **Braid Size**: Choose the preferred thickness of the braids.
13. **Hair Type**: Select your natural hair texture.
14. **Hair Condition**: Assess your current hair condition.
15. **Scalp Sensitivity**: Indicate your scalp sensitivity level.
16. **Allergies**: If you have any allergies to hair products, provide details.
17. **Previous Braiding Experience**: Indicate your braiding experience.
18. **Last Braiding Date**: If you have had braids before, provide the date of your last braiding experience.
19. **Add-on Services**: Select any additional services you may want.
20. **Hair Extensions**: Indicate if you will need extensions, bring your own, or need no extensions.
21. **Extension Color**: If you need extensions, enter the preferred color.
22. **Style Reference**: Choose how you will provide your style reference.
23. **Estimated Duration**: Confirm your understanding of the time commitment for your appointment.
24. **Price Estimate**: Confirm your understanding of the estimated cost for your appointment.
25. **Deposit Paid**: Indicate if you have paid the deposit.
26. **Cancellation Policy**: Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy.
27. **Aftercare Instructions**: Confirm your agreement to follow aftercare instructions.
28. **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests or notes.
29. **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose how you heard about our service.
30. **Client Signature**: Type your full legal name as a signature.
31. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of registration.
