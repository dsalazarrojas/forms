# Roadside Assistance Service Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their roadside assistance service needs and preferences. The goal is to recommend the best service provider and type to meet their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service provider that best matches your needs from the list.
2. Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Change, Battery Replacement).
3. Decide on the contact method you prefer (e.g., Phone, Email, In Person).
4. Enter your appointment date and time.
5. Provide detailed information about yourself, including your name, vehicle details, and contact information.
6. If applicable, enter your vehicle's year and make/model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, required): 
Select the service provider that you trust and prefer. This can be AAA, AA Plus, or AAA Plus, depending on your specific needs and preferences.

*Note: This field is required to ensure that the most suitable service provider is recommended for your needs.*

**Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required): 
Choose the type of service you require, such as Oil Change, Tire Change, or Battery Replacement. Select all that apply to ensure you get the best service recommendation.

*Note: This field is required to ensure that the most suitable service type is recommended for your needs.*

**Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): 
Select how you would like to be contacted for your appointment (e.g., Phone, Email, In Person).

*Note: This field is required to ensure that you receive timely and effective communication about your appointment.*

**Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): 
Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment. This is a required field to ensure that we have enough time to prepare for your visit.

**Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): 
Enter the time you would like to schedule your appointment. This is a required field to ensure that we have enough time to prepare for your visit.

**Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): 
Provide detailed information about yourself, including your name, vehicle details, and contact information. This is a required field to ensure that we have all necessary details to provide you with the best service recommendation.

**Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): 
If you know the year of your vehicle, enter it here. This information helps us provide more accurate service recommendations.

**Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): 
Enter your vehicle's make and model. This information helps us understand your vehicle's specific needs and requirements.

**Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): 
If you know your vehicle's registration number, enter it here. This information can be helpful if there are any issues with your vehicle's registration.

**Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text, optional): 
If you have a preferred phone number to contact you, enter it here. This information ensures that we can reach you effectively.

**Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): 
If you have a preferred email to contact you, enter it here. This information ensures that we can communicate with you efficiently.

**Service Description** (`service_description`, note, optional): 
If you have any specific requirements or requests for your service, enter them here. This information helps us tailor our services to your needs.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Select service provider and type that best match your needs.
* Provide detailed information about yourself and your vehicle to ensure the best service recommendation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
