# Moving Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details for a moving service registration. It helps the service provider to understand the customer's needs and preferences for the move, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Select your preferred date for the move from the "Preferred Move Date" field.
3. Choose your preferred time for the move from the "Move In Time" and "Move Out Time" fields.
4. Enter address details in the "Address Details" field.
5. Select your contact preferences (phone, email, or text message) in the "Contact Method" field.
6. If you have any special requests, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Service Registration Form
* **Service Registration Form (text):** A text box to input any general comments or information related to your service registration.

### Customer Details
* **Customer Details (text):** Enter your name and other relevant information about the customer requesting the service.

### Move Details
* **Move Details (text):** Enter any specific details about the move, such as packing instructions or special handling requirements.

### Preferred Move Date
* **Preferred Move Date (date):** Select a date for the move. This will help the service provider schedule the move accordingly.

### Move In Time
* **Move In Time (time):** Enter the time you would like to move into your new location.

### Move Out Time
* **Move Out Time (time):** Enter the time you would like to move out of your old location.

### Move Details
* **Move Details (text):** Enter any additional details about the move, such as specific instructions or requirements.

### Address Details
* **Address Details (text):** Enter the address of the location you are moving from and to.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details (text):** Enter your name and other relevant contact information for the service provider to reach you.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests (text):** Enter any special requests or requirements you have for the service.

### Preferred Move Time
* **Preferred Move Time (time):** Enter the time you would prefer for the move.

### Schedule Move
* **Schedule Move (select one):** Select "Yes" to schedule the move, or "No" to indicate that you would like to schedule it later.

### Move Type
* **Move Type (select one):** Select whether the move is for a residential or commercial purpose.

### Move Frequency
* **Move Frequency (select multiple):** Select whether the move is a one-time, regular, or recurring move.

### Move Size
* **Move Size (number):** Enter the estimated size of the move.

### Move Weight
* **Move Weight (number):** Enter the estimated weight of the move.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number (text):** Enter your phone number for contact.

### Email
* **Email (email):** Enter your email address for contact.

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method (select one):** Select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or text message).

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person (text):** Enter the name and title of the person you would like to contact.

### Notes
* **Notes (note):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the service registration.

### Move Status
* **Move Status (select one):** Select whether the move is active or inactive.

### Status Change Date
* **Status Change Date (date):** Enter the date the move status was changed.

### Move Schedule
* **Move Schedule (time):** Enter the schedule for the move.

### Confirm Move
* **Confirm Move (select one):** Select "Yes" to confirm the move, or "No" to indicate that you would like to reschedule or cancel the move.

## Tips

- Be sure to fill out all required fields.
- Enter specific and accurate information to ensure efficient processing of your service request.
- If you have any special requests, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
- Contact the service provider if you have any questions or need further assistance.
