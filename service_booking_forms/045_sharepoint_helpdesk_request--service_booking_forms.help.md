# sharepoint_helpdesk_request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit a request to the helpdesk for assistance with SharePoint site updates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with the required information.
2. Enter a brief **Subject** that describes the nature of your request.
3. Provide a detailed **Description** of your issue or problem.
4. Select the relevant **Sharepoint Site Update** option that best describes your issue.
5. If applicable, select the personnel involved in the issue using the **Add Personnel** and **Remove Personnel** fields.
6. Finally, click the submit button to send your request to the helpdesk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to allow the helpdesk to address you correctly.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that the helpdesk can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that the helpdesk can contact you in case of further questions.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Describe the nature of your request in a few words.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your issue or problem.
* **Sharepoint Site Update** (`sharepoint_site_update`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your issue, such as adding a user to a group.
* **Add Personnel** (`sharepoint_site_update_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the personnel involved in the issue, such as IT Department or Sharepoint Admin.
* **Remove Personnel** (`remove_personnel`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the personnel to be removed from the issue.
* **Sharepoint Site Update Details** (`sharepoint_site_update_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about the issue.
