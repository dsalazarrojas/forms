# Pruning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pruning Request Form is a form designed to collect information from clients for pruning requests. This form helps the service provider to efficiently process and manage client requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pruning Request Form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3.  Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field.
4.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is an optional field.
5.  Select the **Type of Service** that you require from the options provided in the "Type of Service" field. This is an optional field.
6.  Enter the **Service Location** where the pruning service is required in the "Service Location" field. This is an optional field.
7.  Enter any additional **Message** or comments about your pruning request in the "Message" field. This is an optional field.
8.  Select the **Assigned Tool** that will be used for the service from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your service request.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone about your service request.
*   **Type of Service** (`service_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the options to choose the type of service you require.
	+   Pruning Service
	+   Tree Trimming
	+   Branch Pruning
	+   Other
*   **Service Location** (`service_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the service is required.
*   **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your pruning request.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the options to choose the tool that will be used for the service.
	+   chatjimmy
	+   Other
