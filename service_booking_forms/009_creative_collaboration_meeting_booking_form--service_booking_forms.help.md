# Creative Collaboration Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and organize creative collaboration meetings. It is intended for employees to provide information about meeting details, attendees, and other relevant data to facilitate efficient and organized meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting details (name, location, start and end times, date, and duration) as required.
2. Select the attendees for the meeting from the list of options.
3. Choose the meeting type (Brainstorming, Planning, or Strategy).
4. Optionally, select the person assigned to, and the person who assigned, the meeting.
5. Indicate the status of the meeting (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
6. If relevant, add any comments or description about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **meeting_details** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **meeting_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the meeting.
* **location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **start_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the meeting in "HH:MM" format.
* **end_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time of the meeting in "HH:MM" format.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the meeting in "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **duration_minutes** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **attendees** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the attendees for the meeting from the list of options.
* **description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **meeting_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the meeting type (Brainstorming, Planning, or Strategy).
* **booking_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the booking in "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **booking_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the booking time in "HH:MM" format.
* **booking_status** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the status of the booking (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **assigned_to** (Select One, Optional): Select the person assigned to the meeting.
* **assigned_by** (Select One, Optional): Select the person who assigned the meeting.
* **submitted** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the booking has been submitted.
* **submit_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of submission in "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **submit_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the submission time in "HH:MM" format.
* **submit_status** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the status of the submission (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **review_status** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the status of the review (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **review_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the review date in "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **review_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the review time in "HH:MM" format.
* **review_by** (Select One, Optional): Select the person who reviewed the booking.
* **form_completed** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the form has been completed.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments about the meeting.
* **submission_count** (Number, Optional): Enter the submission count.
* **total_pages** (Number, Optional): Enter the total number of pages.
* **form_id** (Text, Optional): Enter the form ID.
* **created_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the created time in "HH:MM" format.
* **created_by** (Select One, Optional): Select the person who created the form.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, and you only need to fill in the required information for the form to be submitted successfully.
