# Remote Work Accommodation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book remote work accommodations for work-related purposes. This form helps managers and administrators process requests for remote work arrangements such as booking office space or hotel rooms for work projects or meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the details of your booking request.
2.  Provide information about yourself as a professional.
3.  Select the accommodation you need for your work.
4.  Choose a room or space that suits your needs.
5.  Approve your booking and terms.
6.  Add any additional information about your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Details (page 1)** (`booking_details`, text, required): Enter the date and time of your booking request.
* **Professional Information (page 2)** (`remote_professional_info`, text, required): Enter your employee ID or other relevant professional information.
* **Accommodation Selection (page 2)** (`accommodation_selection`, text, required): Choose the accommodation you need for your work.
* **Room Selection (page 3)** (`room_selection`, text, required): Select the room or space that suits your needs.
* **Approval (page 4)** (`approval`, text, required): Approve your booking request.
* **Terms (page 4)** (`terms`, text, required): Review and acknowledge the terms and conditions of your booking.
* **Additional Information (page 5)** (`additional_information`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your booking.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or issues with your booking, contact your manager or HR department.
