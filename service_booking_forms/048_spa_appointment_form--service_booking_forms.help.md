# Spa Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients book and manage spa appointments. It collects essential information about the client, appointment details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date for your appointment from the calendar.
2.  Choose the time slot that suits you best.
3.  Select the services you would like to book.
4.  Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
5.  Choose the payment method for your appointment (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
6.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
7.  Select a staff member to assist you during your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, **Required**): Select the date for your appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, **Required**): Choose the time slot that suits you best.
*   **Service Selected** (`service_selected`, `select_multiple`, **Not Required**): Select the services you would like to book.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, **Not Required**): Enter your email address to receive updates about your appointment.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, **Not Required**): Enter any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
*   **Service Name** (`service_name`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter the name of the service you are booking.
*   **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter a brief description of the service you are booking.
*   **Staff** (`staff`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Select a staff member to assist you during your appointment.
*   **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Select the status of your appointment (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, or No Show).
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **Not Required**): Choose the payment method for your appointment (Credit Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter any additional payment information.
*   **Appointment Booking Status** (`appointment_booking_status`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter the status of your appointment booking.
*   **Client Message** (`client_message`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter any message or feedback for the client.
*   **Staff Comment** (`staff_comment`, `text`, **Not Required**): Enter any comments or feedback for the staff.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and clear when filling out the form.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the spa staff for assistance.
