# Music Mixing Review Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Mixing Review Booking Form is a tool to book a music mixing review session. This form collects information to schedule a review of a mix, including the date and time of the review, details about the session, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you would like the review to take place.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the mix engineer.
3. Enter the name of the studio where the mix was created.
4. Provide details about the mix, including any notable features or issues.
5. Choose the duration of the session, if applicable.
6. Enter any mix details that need to be reviewed.
7. List the tracks that need to be reviewed.
8. Enter the estimated review time for the session.
9. Enter the location where the review will take place.
10. If applicable, enter the name of the mix coach or person responsible for the mix.
11. Provide any additional notes about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like the review to take place.
* **Mix Engineer** (`mix_engineer`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the mix engineer who created the mix.
* **Studio Name** (`studio_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the studio where the mix was created.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, text, required: false): Provide any notable features or issues with the mix.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required: false): Choose the duration of the session, if applicable.
* **Mix Details** (`mix_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional mix details that need to be reviewed.
* **Tracks** (`tracks`, text, required: false): List the tracks that need to be reviewed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required: false): Enter the estimated review time for the session.
* **Review Location** (`review_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the review will take place.
* **Mix Coach** (`mix_coach`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the mix coach or person responsible for the mix.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the session.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate information will help us better understand your needs and provide the best possible service.
