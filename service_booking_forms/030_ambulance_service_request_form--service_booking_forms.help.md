# Ambulance Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request ambulance services for events, such as concerts, festivals, or sports events. It collects information about the event, including the type of service requested, event details, and payment information. The form is used by the ambulance service to prepare for the event and ensure that all necessary resources are available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service requested from the options provided.
2. Enter the event details, including name, type, date, start and end times, and expected attendance.
3. Provide the venue address and contact information.
4. Specify the number of ambulances and personnel needed.
5. Select the equipment and personnel required for the event.
6. Provide any additional information, such as medical history at the venue or previous incidents.
7. Select a payment method and provide any necessary payment information.
8. Review and sign the terms and conditions, cancellation policy, and provide your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type of Service Requested**: Select the type of service requested, such as Event Standby, Private Transport, or Inter-Facility Transfer.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event, such as Concert/Festival, Sports Event, or Corporate Event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Expected Attendance**: Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue.
* **Venue Address**: Enter the full street address of the venue.
* **Venue City**: Enter the city of the venue.
* **Venue State**: Enter the state of the venue.
* **Venue ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of the venue.
* **Venue Contact Person**: Enter the name and phone number of the venue contact person.
* **Parking Instructions**: Enter instructions for parking, such as where to park and any specific instructions.
* **Site Access Instructions**: Enter instructions for accessing the site, such as gate codes or entry points.
* **Age Demographics**: Select the age demographics of the attendees, such as all ages or mostly adults.
* **High-Risk Activities**: Select if there will be high-risk activities, such as stunts or water activities.
* **High-Risk Description**: Enter a description of the high-risk activities, if applicable.
* **Number of Ambulances Needed**: Enter the number of ambulances required for the event.
* **Ambulance Type Required**: Select the type of ambulance required, such as Basic Life Support or Advanced Life Support.
* **Personnel Requirements**: Select the personnel required, such as EMT-Basic or Paramedic.
* **Special Equipment Needed**: Select any special equipment required, such as a cardiac monitor or ventilator.
* **Specify Other Equipment**: Enter any other equipment required, if applicable.
* **Medical History at Venue**: Enter any medical history at the venue, such as previous incidents.
* **Previous Events**: Select if the event has been held before, and if so, how many times.
* **Previous Incidents**: Select if there were previous medical incidents, and if so, describe them.
* **Describe Previous Incidents**: Enter a description of the previous incidents, if applicable.
* **Nearest Hospital**: Enter the name and distance from the venue of the nearest hospital.
* **Hospital Directions**: Enter directions to the hospital, if known.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method, such as invoice or credit card.
* **Purchase Order Number**: Enter the purchase order number, if applicable.
* **Billing Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of the billing contact, if different from the event contact.
* **Billing Email**: Enter the email of the billing contact, for invoices.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or considerations for the event.
* **Weather Contingency Plan**: Enter a plan in case of bad weather.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the event.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Service**: Select how you heard about the ambulance service.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Select if you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Requester Signature**: Enter your full name as a signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of signature.
