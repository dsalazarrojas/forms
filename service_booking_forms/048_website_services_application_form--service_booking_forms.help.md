# Website Services Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in our website services. It will help our team process your request and contact you with further instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submit your application**: Click on the "Submit" button to send your application to our team.
2. **Fill out the form**: Provide the required and optional information in the fields below.
3. **Review and edit**: Review your application before submitting it to ensure it's accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Services Application**: This is the main field where you can describe your website services request.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so our team can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so our team can contact you for further instructions.
* **Website**: If you have a website, provide the URL.
* **Services**: Select the services you're interested in from the list. (Yes/No)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your website services request.
* **Date**: Enter the date you'd like to start the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a prompt response.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your email when responding to our team.
* Our team will review your application and contact you within 3-5 business days.
