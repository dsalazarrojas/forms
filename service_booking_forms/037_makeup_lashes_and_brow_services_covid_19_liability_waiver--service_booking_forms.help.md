# Makeup, Lashes And Brow Services COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Makeup, Lashes And Brow Services COVID 19 Liability Waiver" form is designed to gather essential information from clients before providing makeup, lashes, and brow services. This form aims to inform clients of the risks associated with the services and to obtain their consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide a brief description of the services you'd like to receive.
3. Enter your address, phone number, and email for contact purposes.
4. If applicable, enter the date and time of your scheduled appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **COVID 19 Liability Waiver** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for this waiver, if desired.
* **Waiver Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the services and risks associated with them. This is an opportunity to inform clients about any necessary precautions or requirements.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your full address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for communication and appointment reminders.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): Enter the time of your scheduled appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth service delivery and to maintain contact with the service provider.
* If you're unsure about any information, please don't hesitate to ask the service provider for clarification.
* Review the waiver description carefully to understand the risks associated with the services and the precautions you need to take.
