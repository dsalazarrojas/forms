# Roller Shutter Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers to request a service visit for their roller shutter systems. Please complete this form to submit a new service request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Service Request" page.
2. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Choose an appointment date for the service visit in the "Appointment Date" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the type of service you require (e.g. repair, maintenance, installation) in the "Service Type" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the issue or service needed in the "Description" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the person to contact regarding the service request in the "Contact Person" field.
10. Enter any additional notes regarding the service visit in the "Notes" field.
11. Select the staff member or team member you would like to be assigned to handle the service visit in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of the service request.
* **Customer Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name to be associated with the service request.
* **Customer Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Appointment Date** (4, date, required): Choose a date for the service visit.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Enter your address for the service visit.
* **Service Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you require (e.g. repair, maintenance, installation).
* **Description** (7, text, required): Enter a brief description of the issue or service needed.
* **Customer Email** (8, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Person** (9, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person to contact regarding the service request.
* **Notes** (10, note, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the service visit.
* **Assigned To** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select the staff member or team member you would like to be assigned to handle the service visit.
