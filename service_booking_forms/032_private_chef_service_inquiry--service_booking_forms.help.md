# Private Chef Service Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Chef Service Inquiry form is designed to collect information from clients who are interested in booking a private chef service. The form is used to gather details about the client's preferences, including their name, contact information, service requirements, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and contact information in the provided fields.
*   Provide details about the services you are seeking, including the number of guests and services required.
*   Choose your preferred cuisine type, service type, and payment method.
*   Enter any special requests or notes for the chef.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the client.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief message for the chef.
*   **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required/optional): Enter details about the services you require.
*   **Client Diet** (`client_diet`, text, required/optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or preferences.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Enter any special requests or notes for the chef.
*   **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the service.
*   **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the service.
*   **Cuisine Type** (`cuisine_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred cuisine type (Vegetarian, Vegan, Non-Vegetarian).
*   **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests for the service.
*   **Number of Services** (`number_of_services`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of services you are booking.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred service type (Private Chef, Private Caterer, Private Chef & Caterer).
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit/Debit Card, Online Payment).
*   **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location for the service.
*   **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the service address.
*   **Service Zip** (`service_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the service zip code.
*   **Chef Name** (`chef_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the chef's name.
*   **Chef Number** (`chef_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the chef's contact number.
*   **Chef Email** (`chef_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the chef's email address.
*   **Chef Phone** (`chef_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the chef's phone number.
*   **Chef Notes** (`chef_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes for the chef.
*   **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes for the client.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Submit the form.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
*   Proofread your responses for accuracy and completeness.
*   Be clear and specific in your responses to help the private chef service provider understand your needs.
*   Use the provided fields to attach any relevant supporting documents or files.
*   Review and confirm your submission before submitting the form.
