# Product Demo Technician Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Demo Technician Booking Form is designed to enable customers to book a product demo session with a technician. This form collects relevant information about the demo session, including the customer's name, email, phone number, and demo details like start and end time, date, location, and product demo ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the product demo ID provided by the sales team or customer service representative.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
3. Enter your email address where you can be reached.
4. Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
5. Select the location where the demo session will take place.
6. Enter the start and end time of the demo session.
7. Enter the date of the demo session.
8. Select the technician you want to conduct the demo session with.
9. Enter a brief description of the product demo to be conducted.
10. Click on the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter technician ID**: This field is used to identify the specific technician assigned to conduct the product demo session. Please enter the ID provided by the sales team or customer service representative.
* **Customer name**: This field is used to collect your name as it appears on your business card or identification. Please enter your full name as it should be spelled correctly.
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date of the product demo session. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Start time**: This field is used to select the start time of the product demo session. Please use the "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **End time**: This field is used to select the end time of the product demo session. Please use the "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Location**: This field is used to select the location where the product demo session will take place.
* **Description**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the product demo to be conducted.
* **Product demo ID**: This field is used to enter the product demo ID provided by the sales team or customer service representative.
* **Customer email**: This field is used to enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Technician Scheduling**: This field is used to select the technician who will conduct the product demo session. Please select one or more of the available options.
