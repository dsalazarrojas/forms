# Actor Coaching Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Actor Coaching Session Booking Form is designed to gather information from the user to book a personalized coaching session with a professional coach. This form will help the coach understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that you get the most out of your coaching session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and correct.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your professional stage name or your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This is the email address where we will send confirmation and possibly additional information about your coaching session.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you to confirm your session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching you require:
	+ Audition Prep
	+ Monologue Work
	+ Scene Study
	+ Voice and Diction
	+ Career Consultation
* **Experience Level** (`coaching_level`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Professional
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your coaching session.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose a time for your coaching session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your coaching session:
	+ 30 Minutes
	+ 60 Minutes
	+ 90 Minutes
	+ 2 Hours
* **Coaching Goals** (`goals_and_focus`, text, optional): If you have specific areas you'd like to focus on during this session, let us know.
* **Audition Material Link** (`audition_material`, text, optional): If prepping for a specific audition, provide a link to the sides or script.
* **Additional Comments or Requirements** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Let us know if you have any additional comments or requirements for your coaching session.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when choosing your session type, date, and time to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you to confirm your session details after receiving your form submission.
