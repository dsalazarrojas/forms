# rug_drop_off_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rug_drop_off_registration form is designed to collect important information from customers about their rug drop-off needs. This form helps the service provider understand the customer's requirements and ensure that their rug is properly handled and serviced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information** (Page 1):
	* Enter your name and contact information in the text field provided.
2. **Rug Information** (Page 1):
	* Enter a description of your rug, including its type and condition, to help the service provider understand its needs.
3. **Schedule Pick-up** (Page 1):
	* Choose a preferred date for the pick-up of your rug.
4. **Drop-off Information** (Page 2):
	* Enter the address where your rug will be dropped off.
5. **Service Type** (Page 2):
	* Select the type of service you require for your rug (Rug Repair, Rug Cleaning, or Both).
6. **Rug Condition** (Page 2):
	* Indicate the condition of your rug (New, Used, or Damaged).
7. **Additional Notes** (Page 2-3):
	* Add any additional notes or comments about your rug or service requirements.
8. **Signature** (Page 3):
	* Select whether you want to sign the form or not.
9. **Service Provider Information** (Page 3):
	* Enter the assigned staff member's name.
10. **Additional Notes (2)** (Page 3-4):
	* Add any additional notes or comments about your service requirements.
