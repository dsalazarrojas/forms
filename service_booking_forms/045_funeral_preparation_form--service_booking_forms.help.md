# Funeral Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Preparation Form is a tool used to gather essential information from the bereaved family to prepare for a funeral service. This form helps the service provider to understand the family's needs and preferences, ensuring a respectful and personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name, contact information, and any additional personal details on the first page, "Personal Info".
2.  On the "Contact Info" page, provide your contact details, including phone number and email address.
3.  On the "Funeral Info" page, enter the details of the deceased, including the name and date of birth.
4.  On the "Select a relative" page, choose a family member to be the main point of contact.
5.  On the "Select Services" page, choose the services you would like to be performed during the funeral service.
6.  On the "Comments" page, write any additional information or requests you have.
7.  Finally, review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info (1)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any additional personal details.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **Funeral Info (3)**: Enter the details of the deceased, including name and date of birth.
* **Select a relative (4)**: Choose a family member to be the main point of contact. Select from the options: Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, or Other.
* **Select Services (5)**: Choose the services you would like to be performed during the funeral service. Select from the options: Burial, Cremation, Memorial.
* **Comments (6)**: Write any additional information or requests you have.
* **Submit (7)**: Review your selections and submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a funeral representative for assistance.
* The funeral service provider will contact you to discuss your selections and preferences.
