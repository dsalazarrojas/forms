# Pool Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Party Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from guests attending a pool party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the date of your reservation in the "Reservation Date" field.
5. Select the time of your reservation in the "Reservation Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Your name is the first field on the form. Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about the party.
* **Pool Party Details** (`pool_party_registration_form`, text, required): This is not a field on the form, but it is the name of the form page, and is here to indicate this as a heading for the field-by-field explanation section. 
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the pool party.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): Select the time you plan to arrive at the pool party.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are experiencing difficulties with your reservations, contact us at the contact information below.
* You can also contact us with any additional information or changes.
