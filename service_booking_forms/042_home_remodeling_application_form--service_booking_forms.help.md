# Home Remodeling Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Remodeling Application Form is designed to gather information from clients about their home remodeling requests. This form helps our team understand the client's needs and preferences for their home remodeling project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form and fill in the required information for each field.
2. Enter your name in the **Client Name** field (Field 1) to identify yourself.
3. Enter your email in the **Client Email** field (Field 2) to help us contact you for further information or updates.
4. Provide a brief description of your home remodeling request in the **Request Description** field (Field 3).
5. Describe your home in the **Home Details** field (Field 4) so that our team can better understand your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Client Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Request Description (Text, Optional)**: Provide a brief description of your home remodeling request, including any specific services or features you're interested in.
* **Home Details (Text, Optional)**: Describe your home, including its size, type (e.g., single-family, condo), and any other relevant information that will help us understand your project.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your request and home.
* If you have any supporting documents or photos, you can attach them separately and provide their URLs in the **Request Description** field.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you for further information or clarification if needed.

Note that the "Client Name" and "Client Email" fields are required to ensure that we can contact you for further information or updates. The other fields are optional but highly recommended to provide our team with the best possible understanding of your project.
