# Piano Tuning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piano Tuning Appointment Form is designed for customers to book a piano tuning service with a technician. To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Piano Tuning Appointment Form** (Page 1, Required): This is the title of the form, indicating that it is the Piano Tuning Appointment Form.
* **Customer Name** (Page 1, Required): Please enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Page 1, Required): Enter your email address, as we may need to contact you for further details or updates.
* **Piano Type** (Page 1, Required): Describe the type of piano you would like the technician to tune (e.g., grand, upright, or baby grand).
* **Service Details** (Page 1, Required): Provide any additional notes or details about the service required (e.g., tuning, adjustment, or repair).
* **Service Date** (Page 2, Required): Choose the date for the service appointment.
* **Service Time** (Page 2, Required): Select the time slot for the service appointment.
* **Service Location** (Page 2, Required): Specify the location where the service will take place (e.g., your home, a specific studio, or workshop).
* **Contact Phone** (Page 2, Required): Enter the phone number where we can contact you for further communication.
* **Contact Note** (Page 2, Required): Provide any special notes or instructions for the technician (e.g., parking information or access details).
* **Technician Name** (Page 2, Required): Choose the technician who will be performing the service.
* **Technician Notes** (Page 2, Required): Leave any additional notes or instructions for the selected technician.
* **Customer Signature** (Page 2, Required): Select the signature that best represents your agreement and acceptance of the service terms.
* **Technician Signature** (Page 2, Required): Choose the signature that represents the technician's agreement and acceptance of the service terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the service details and location to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you need further assistance or clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
