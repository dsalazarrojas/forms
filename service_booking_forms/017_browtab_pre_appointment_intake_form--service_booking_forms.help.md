# BrowTab Pre Appointment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients before their appointment for microblading, cosmetic tattoos, brow lamination, or correction services. This form helps us understand your needs and requirements, ensuring that we provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to help us understand your needs.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, and if you're unsure about any question, choose the 'Not sure' option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Full Name**
* (Client Full Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
### 2. **Date of Birth**
* (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 3. **Phone Number**
* (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any important information.
### 4. **Email Address**
* (Email Address, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
### 5. **Appointment Date**
* (Appointment Date, date, required): Select the date you've scheduled for your appointment.
### 6. **Service Type**
* (Service Type, select one, required): Choose the type of service you're interested in:
	+ Microblading
	+ Cosmetic Tattoo
	+ Brow Lamination
	+ Correction
### 7. **Have you had cosmetic tattoos before?**
* (Have you had cosmetic tattoos before, select one, required): Choose one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
### 8. **Current Brow Type**
* (Current Brow Type, select one, required): Choose the current state of your brows:
	+ Natural Brows
	+ Previous Microblading
	+ Previous Tattoo
	+ Sparse
	+ Fully Shaved
### 9. **Skin Type**
* (Skin Type, select one, required): Choose the type of skin you have:
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
	+ Normal
### 10. **Current skin conditions**
* (Current skin conditions, text, not required): If you have any skin conditions such as acne, rashes, eczema, or psoriasis, please describe them briefly.
### 11. **Known allergies to ink or numbing agents**
* (Known allergies to ink or numbing agents, text, not required): If you have any allergies, please list them.
### 12. **Current medications**
* (Current medications, text, not required): If you're currently taking any medications, especially blood thinners or antibiotics, please list them.
### 13. **Any history of poor wound healing?**
* (Any history of poor wound healing, select one, required): Choose one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not sure
### 14. **Are you pregnant or nursing?**
* (Are you pregnant or nursing, select one, required): Choose one of the options:
	+ Yes, pregnant
	+ Yes, nursing
	+ False
### 15. **Your desired brow look and shape**
* (Your desired brow look and shape, text, required): Describe your desired brow shape and style, and include reference photos if possible.
### 16. **Preferred brow color**
* (Preferred brow color, select one, required): Choose the color of your preferred brow:
	+ Ash Brown
	+ Medium Brown
	+ Dark Brown
	+ Black
	+ Custom Blend
### 17. **Pain tolerance level**
* (Pain tolerance level, select one, required): Choose your pain tolerance level:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
