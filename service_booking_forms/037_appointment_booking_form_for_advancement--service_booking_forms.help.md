# Appointment Booking Form For Advancement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your advancement goals and preferences for a consultation or coaching session. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your needs are accurately represented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. **Be truthful and accurate**: Provide clear and concise information about your current role, career goals, and advancement areas.
3. **Double-check your information**: Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure that all fields are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (\*):** Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address (\*):** Enter your valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (\*):** Enter your contact phone number for scheduling and follow-up purposes.
* **Company or Organization (Optional):** If you are affiliated with a company or organization, please enter the name.
* **Type of Advancement Consultation (\*):** Select the type of consultation that best suits your needs.
	+ Career advancement: General guidance on career growth.
	+ Skill development: Development of new skills for career advancement.
	+ Leadership coaching: Personalized coaching for leadership development.
	+ Professional development: Career development and planning.
	+ Other: Please specify the type of consultation required.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (\*):** Select a date for your appointment from the available dates.
* **Preferred Time Slot (\*):** Select a time slot for your appointment from the available options.
* **Preferred Session Duration (Optional):** Select the duration of your appointment from the available options.
* **Meeting Format (\*):** Select the format of your appointment from the available options.
	+ In-person: Meet in person.
	+ Video call: Meet via video call.
	+ Phone call: Meet via phone call.
	+ Hybrid - flexible: Meet in a combination of formats.
* **Current Role or Position (\*):** Enter your current role or position.
* **Career Goals (\*):** Describe your career goals and what you want to achieve.
* **Areas for Advancement (Optional):** Select all areas for advancement that apply.
	+ Technical skills: Acquire new skills for career growth.
	+ Leadership skills: Develop leadership abilities.
	+ Communication: Improve communication skills.
	+ Networking: Expand your professional network.
	+ Education and credentials: Obtain relevant education or credentials.
	+ Career planning: Plan your career path.
* **Current Challenges or Concerns (Optional):** Describe any current challenges or concerns you are experiencing.
* **Have you had coaching or mentoring before? (Optional):** Check if you have had coaching or mentoring before.
* **Additional information about your advancement goals (Optional):** Provide any additional information about your advancement goals.

**Tips:** Please ensure that all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are filled out accurately and completely. Optional fields can be filled out if relevant to your needs.
