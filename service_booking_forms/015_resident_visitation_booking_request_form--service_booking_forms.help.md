# Resident Visitation Booking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Resident Visitation Booking Request Form is designed to allow residents to book visits with visitors for specific dates and times. This form helps to track and manage resident visitation requests, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the resident's name** (Residents Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the resident requesting the visit.
2. **Enter the resident's date of birth** (Residents DOB, date, optional): Enter the resident's date of birth.
3. **Select the visitation date** (Visitation Date, date, optional): Choose the date of the visit you would like to book.
4. **Select the visitation time** (Visitation Time, time, optional): Choose the time of the visit you would like to book.
5. **Enter the visitation duration** (Visitation Duration, number, optional): Enter the length of the visit (e.g., 15 minutes, 1 hour).
6. **Enter the visitor's name** (Visitors Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the visitor(s) coming to see the resident.
7. **Enter the number of visitors** (Visitors Number, number, optional): Enter the number of visitors coming to see the resident.
8. **Describe the purpose of the visit** (Purpose of Visitation, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the visit (e.g., social visit, medical appointment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residents Name (Residents Name)**: Enter the name of the resident requesting the visit.
* **Residents DOB (Residents DOB)**: Enter the date of birth of the resident, if applicable.
* **Visitation Date (Visitation Date)**: Choose the date you would like to book the visit for.
* **Visitation Time (Visitation Time)**: Choose the time you would like to book the visit for.
* **Visitation Duration (Visitation Duration)**: Enter the length of the visit in minutes or hours.
* **Visitors Name (Visitors Name)**: Enter the name(s) of the visitor(s) coming to see the resident.
* **Visitors Number (Visitors Number)**: Enter the number of visitors coming to see the resident.
* **Purpose of Visitation (Purpose of Visitation)**: Briefly describe the reason for the visit.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure efficient processing of your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
