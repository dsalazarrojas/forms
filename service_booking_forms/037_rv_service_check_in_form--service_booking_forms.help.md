# RV Service Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Service Check In Form is used to collect information from customers for service requests and check-in purposes. It helps the service center understand the owner's requirements and preferences for service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the "Owner Details" section.
2. Provide vehicle information in the "Vehicle Info" section.
3. Select the services you require from the "Service Requests" section.
4. Enter your check-in time in the "Check In Time" section.
5. Sign the form digitally using the "Owner Signature" section.
6. Enter your phone number and email in the "Owner Phone" and "Owner Email" sections respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details**: 
  - <label> Owner Details </label> (`owner_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your details such as name, address, and contact information. This is the primary section where you can provide your basic information.
* **Vehicle Info**: 
  - <label> Vehicle Info </label> (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about your vehicle, including make, model, and year. Please enter vehicle details to ensure accurate service processing.
* **Service Requests**: 
  - <label> Service Requests </label> (`service_requests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the services you require, such as oil change, tire rotation, or maintenance check. You can select multiple services by checking the boxes next to the services.
* **Check In Time**: 
  - <label> Check In Time </label> (`check_in_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your estimated check-in time to help the service center schedule services efficiently.
* **Owner Signature**: 
  - <label> Owner Signature </label> (`owner_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Sign the form digitally to confirm that you have provided accurate information.
* **Owner Phone**: 
  - <label> Owner Phone </label> (`owner_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so the service center can contact you for any updates or information.
* **Owner Email**: 
  - <label> Owner Email </label> (`owner_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so the service center can contact you for any updates or information about your service.
