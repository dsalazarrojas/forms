# Sorter Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a sorter service for a specific date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields:
	* Sorter ID
	* Date of Visit
	* Time of Visit (if applicable)
2. Fill out the optional fields:
	* Sorter Booking Form Comments (if you have any additional comments)
	* Email (if you want to provide an email for communication)
	* Phone (if you want to provide a phone number for communication)
	* Sorter Booking Form Comments (if you have any additional comments)
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sorter ID** (required): Enter the ID of the sorter you want to book.
* **Date of Visit** (required): Select the date you want to visit the sorter. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit** (optional): Select the time you want to visit the sorter (if applicable). Please use the format HH:MM.
* **Sorter Booking Form Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your booking.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address if you want to provide a contact email.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number if you want to provide a contact phone number.
* **Submit** (required): Click on this button to send your booking request.
