This form is for users to book a consulting service with a consultant. Its purpose is to collect booking details from clients who require consulting services. The form will be used by internal teams to track and confirm bookings.

## Consultant Booking System - Help Guide

## Purpose
The consultant booking system is designed to collect and organize client information for consulting services. It will help the consultant to better serve their clients and provide a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the person booking the service.
2. Select the consulting service that you require from the dropdown list.
3. Choose a date and time that suits you best for the booking.
4. Select whether you prefer to meet in person or virtually.
5. Add any additional notes or special requests that might be relevant for the booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as the person booking the service.
* **Service required** (`service_required`, select_one, **Required**): Select the consulting service you require from the dropdown list. Options include:
	+ Strategy session: A one-on-one session to discuss and plan a strategy.
	+ Implementation support: Ongoing support for implementing the strategy.
	+ Advisory call: A call to provide guidance and advice.
	+ Training: A session to learn new skills or processes.
* **Preferred date** (`preferred_date`, date, **Required**): Select a date for the booking that suits you best.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, **Required**): Select a time for the booking that suits you best.
* **Meeting format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether you prefer to meet in person or virtually.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, **Optional**): Add any additional notes or background information that might be relevant for the booking.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your preferred date and time as they may be subject to change.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please add them in the additional notes section.
- If you have any conflicts or changes, please contact us directly.
- You will receive a confirmation email once your booking is confirmed.
- If you have any questions or need to make changes to your booking, please contact us directly.
