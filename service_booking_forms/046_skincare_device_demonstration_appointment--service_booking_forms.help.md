# Skincare Device Demonstration Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Device Demonstration Appointment form is designed to collect information from clients interested in demonstrating a skincare device. The form helps the client schedule a demo appointment and provides relevant details to ensure a smooth and successful demonstration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of demo you are interested in: **Facial, Body Treatment, Lashes, Makeup, or Skincare**.
2.  Provide your contact details, including **First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone**.
3.  Select a date and time for the demo appointment.
4.  Add any additional **notes** about the client's requirements or preferences.
5.  Indicate whether you would like to schedule a **follow-up** appointment and select a time if necessary.
6.  Confirm your appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Make a demo request**: This field allows you to select the type of demo you are interested in. Please choose from the available options: **Facial, Body Treatment, Lashes, Makeup, or Skincare**.
*   **Contact Information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone)**: This field is used to gather your contact details. Please ensure you enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number accurately. This information will be used to communicate with you about your demo appointment.
*   **Demo Date and Time**: This field is used to select a date and time for your demo appointment. Please choose a date and time that suits you best.
*   **Demo Time**: This field allows you to select a specific time for your demo appointment.
*   **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requirements for the demo, please add them in this field.
*   **Follow Up**: If you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment, select **Yes** in this field. If you select **Yes**, please specify a time for the follow-up in the next field.
*   **Follow Up Time**: If you selected **Yes** for follow-up, this field will be visible to select a specific time for the follow-up appointment.
*   **Confirm**: Please review your appointment details carefully and confirm your submission.
