# Seaside Creative Studio Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a studio session at Seaside Creative Studio. It collects relevant information about the project, booking details, and contact information for communication and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields to understand what information is required and optional.
2. Fill in the relevant fields as accurately as possible.
3. Submit the form to book your studio session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled with the studio name.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for your studio session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time for your studio session.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: false): Describe your project briefly.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Enter your project name.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false): Enter your project budget.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your project (Active or Inactive).
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant details about the studio.
* **Studio Location** (`studio_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the studio.
* **Studio Contact** (`studio_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the studio.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your reservation.
* **Reservation Duration** (`reservation_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your reservation.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your booking (Active or Inactive).
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your session.
* **Session Date 2** (`session_date_2`, date, required: false): Enter the date for your second session.
* **Session Time 2** (`session_time_2`, time, required: false): Enter the time for your second session.
* **Session Duration 2** (`session_duration_2`, number, required: false): Enter the duration for your second session.
* **Session Status 2** (`session_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your second session (Active or Inactive).
* **Session Location 2** (`session_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether your session will be held in a specific location (Yes or No).
* **Session Note** (`session_note`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your session.
* **Reservation Message** (`reservation_message`, text, required: false): Enter any message with your reservation.
* **Studio Message** (`studio_message`, text, required: false): Enter any message for the studio.
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your booking status (Yes or No).
