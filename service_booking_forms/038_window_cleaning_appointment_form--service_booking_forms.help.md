# Window Cleaning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to book an appointment for window cleaning services. It gathers essential details from the client to schedule the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Client Name, Email, and Contact Number).
2. Specify the type of service you need (Service Request).
3. Choose a suitable schedule for the visit (Schedule Visit).
4. Mention the preferred visit time (Preferred Visit Time).
5. Indicate the length of the service (Service Duration).
6. Select the type of service (Service Type).
7. Update the appointment status (Appointment Status).
8. Add any additional comments or notes (Notes).
9. Confirm your appointment details (Appointment Time, Appointment Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Provide your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your contact number for further communication.
* **Client Contact Number**: Provide your contact number for further communication.
* **Service Request**: Type of service you need (e.g., window cleaning, window treatment, etc.).
* **Schedule Visit**: Select a suitable date and time for the visit.
* **Preferred Visit Time**: Choose your preferred visit time.
* **Service Duration**: Specify the length of the service you need (e.g., 1 hour, half-day, etc.).
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you need (e.g., full, partial, etc.).
* **Appointment Status**: Update the status of your appointment (e.g., pending, confirmed, rescheduled, cancelled).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Appointment Time**: Confirm the appointment time.
* **Appointment Date**: Confirm the appointment date.
* **Status**: Update the status of your appointment.
* **Service Provider**: Mention the name of your service provider.
* **Client Signature**: Sign to confirm your appointment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Form Subscribed**: Confirm if you have subscribed to the form.
* **Submitted by**: Mention the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date**: Confirm the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time**: Confirm the time you submitted the form.
* **Submission IP**: Mention the IP address of the device you used to submit the form.
* **Submission Device**: Mention the device you used to submit the form.
* **Submission Country**: Mention the country where you submitted the form.
* **Submission City**: Mention the city where you submitted the form.
* **Submission State**: Mention the state where you submitted the form.
