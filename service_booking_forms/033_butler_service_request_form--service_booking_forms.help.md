# Butler Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Butler Service Request Form is a tool used to gather information about the services requested by guests. This form helps us understand their needs, preferences, and requirements to provide exceptional service. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we can cater to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest name, contact email, and contact phone number in the respective fields.
2. Select the type of service you require from the options provided.
3. Provide a detailed description of the service you need.
4. Choose the urgency level of your request.
5. Select a preferred date and time for the service.
6. If applicable, mention any special requests, dietary restrictions, or guest preferences.
7. Specify the number of people involved in the service.
8. If needed, list any specific items or materials required.
9. Choose a budget range for the service (if applicable).
10. If you have a specific staff preference, mention it.
11. Choose your preferred contact method for confirmation.
12. Acknowledge our cancellation policy.
13. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Your full name, as we will address you by this.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email, for us to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number, for us to call you back.
* **Room Number or Suite**: Your accommodation details, including room or suite number.
* **Type of Service Requested**: Select one of the options provided, depending on your need.
* **Detailed Description of Service Needed**: Explain specifically what you need, and we'll do our best to provide it.
* **Urgency Level**: Choose how soon you need the service, from immediate to flexible.
* **Preferred Service Date**: Select a date for the service, if you have a specific preference.
* **Preferred Service Time**: Choose a time for the service, if you have a specific preference.
* **Special Requests or Preferences**: Mention any specific requirements or preferences we should know.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have any food-related requirements, mention them here.
* **Guest Preferences or Habits**: If you have any specific preferences or requirements we should know, mention them here.
* **Number of People Involved**: Specify the number of people involved in the service.
* **Specific Items or Materials Needed**: If you need any specific items or materials, list them here.
* **Budget Range (if applicable)**: Choose a budget range for the service, if you have one.
* **Staff Preference**: If you have a specific staff member you'd like to assist you, mention it here.
* **Privacy or Discretion Level**: Choose your level of privacy preference.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for confirmation.
* **Acknowledge Cancellation Policy**: Review and agree to our cancellation terms.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Add any additional information we should know about your service request.

By following these steps and filling out this form accurately, you'll help us provide the best possible service for your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us!
