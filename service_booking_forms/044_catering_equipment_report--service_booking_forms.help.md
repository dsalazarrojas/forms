# Catering Equipment Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a service call for a catering equipment maintenance visit. It is intended to collect information about the equipment's condition, the work done, and the outcome of the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for the service engineer's information, including the date and time of the visit.
2. Provide the contact details of the site and contact person.
3. Describe the equipment's manufacturer and model, its serial number, and any asset tag number (if applicable).
4. Report the problem that occurred with the equipment.
5. Document the work done to repair the equipment, including any parts replaced.
6. Determine the final status of the equipment.
7. Add any further actions or recommendations.
8. Sign-off the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Engineer Name (`engineer_name`)**: Enter the name of the service engineer performing the visit.
* **Date of Visit (`service_date`)**: Enter the date of the visit in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Time of Arrival (`arrival_time`)**: Enter the arrival time in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Site/Business Name (`site_name`)**: Enter the name of the business or site where the equipment is located.
* **Site Address (`site_address`)**: Enter the address of the site.
* **Contact Name at Site (`site_contact_person`)**: Enter the name of the contact person at the site.
* **Equipment Manufacturer and Model (`equipment_make_model`)**: Enter the manufacturer and model of the equipment.
* **Serial Number (`serial_number`)**: Enter the serial number of the equipment (if applicable).
* **Asset Tag Number (if applicable) (`asset_tag_number`)**: Enter the asset tag number (if applicable).
* **Problem Description (as reported) (`fault_reported`)**: Describe the problem that occurred with the equipment.
* **Description of Work Undertaken (`jobs_undertaken`)**: Describe the work done to repair the equipment, including any parts replaced.
* **Parts Replaced (Include Part Numbers) (`parts_replaced_list`)**: List the parts replaced during the visit (including part numbers).
* **Final Equipment Status (`final_equipment_status`)**: Determine the final status of the equipment:
	+ Functional - Fully Repaired
	+ Functional - Temporary Fix Applied
	+ Non-Functional - Awaiting Parts
	+ Non-Functional - Requires Replacement
	+ Decommissioned - Uneconomical to Repair
* **Further Actions or Recommendations (`further_actions_needed`)**: Add any further actions or recommendations for the customer.
* **Customer/Site Rep Signature (Type Name) (`customer_signature_name`)**: Sign off to confirm that the work was completed.
* **Engineer Signature (Type Name) (`engineer_signature_name`)**: Sign off to confirm completion of the form.
* **Time of Departure (`departure_time`)**: Enter the departure time in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
