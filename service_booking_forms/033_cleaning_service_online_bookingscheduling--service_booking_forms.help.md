# Cleaning Service Online Booking and Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cleaning Service Online Booking and Scheduling" form is designed to collect information from customers to provide them with a personalized cleaning service. It helps the service provider understand the customer's needs and preferences, including their contact information, service requirements, and property details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field. This is used for booking confirmation and future communication.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is used for scheduling and last-minute updates.
4. Choose the type of cleaning service you need from the "Type of Cleaning Service" dropdown menu.
5. Select the type of property that needs cleaning from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, enter the approximate square footage of the property in the "Property Size" field.
7. Choose how often you need the service from the "How Often Do You Need Service?" dropdown menu.
8. Select the preferred start date for the service from the calendar.
9. Choose the preferred day of the week for the service from the "Preferred Day of Week" dropdown menu.
10. Choose the preferred time of day for the service from the "Preferred Time of Day" dropdown menu.
11. In the "Areas to Clean" section, select all the areas you want cleaned.
12. If you have any special requests or preferences, type them in the "Special Requests or Preferences" field.
13. In the "Additional Services" section, specify any extra services you might need.
14. Enter the estimated duration of the service in the "Estimated Service Duration" field.
15. In the "Property Access" section, choose how you will be granting access to the property.
16. Provide any access instructions if needed.
17. Indicate if there are pets in the home in the "Pets in the Home" field.
18. Finally, confirm that the provided information is accurate and agree to the service terms and conditions.

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.

* **Phone Number**: Contact number for scheduling and last-minute updates.

	+ Monthly
	+ As Needed
* **Preferred Start Date**: When would you like service to begin?
* **Preferred Day of Week**: Which days work best for you?
