# microbeauty_studio_lash_lift_service_booking_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a lash lift service with microbeauty studio. It is divided into several sections to gather essential information from the client.
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Select the Client Info Section
- Fill out the Client Info section with your basic details.

### Step 2: Choose the Services
- In the Services section, select the specific service you wish to book (e.g., lash lift).
- Ensure that you select the correct service to avoid any confusion.

### Step 3: Choose the Service
- In the Service section, select the specific option for the service you chose in Step 2.

### Step 4: Schedule the Appointment
- In the Date and Time section, select the date and time that suits you best.
- Ensure that you check the availability of the date and time with the microbeauty studio staff.

### Step 5: Additional Information
- In the Notes section, add any additional comments or instructions if needed.
- This is optional but can help the microbeauty studio staff with your appointment.

### Step 6: Payment Method
- In the Payment Method section, select the method of payment that you prefer.
- Ensure that you check with the microbeauty studio staff if they have any specific payment instructions.

* **Client Info (client_info)**: 
  * (Text input, optional) 
  This is where you can enter your personal details.
* **Services (services)**:

  Select the lash lift service you wish to book.
* **Service (service)**:

  Select the date that suits you best for the appointment.
* **Time (time)**: 
  * (Time input) 
  Select the time for the appointment that suits you best.
* **Notes (notes)**: 
  * (Text input, optional) 
  Add any additional comments or instructions if needed.
* **Service Info (service_info)**: 
  * (Text input, optional)

  Please note that this form is only for lash lift services with microbeauty studio, and some fields may have specific requirements or restrictions. Please ensure that you are providing accurate information to avoid any issues with your booking.
