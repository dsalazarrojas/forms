# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book services for various tasks such as landscaping, lawn mowing, tree service, and yard cleanup. Please provide your details so that our team can assist you with your service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Check if you have already submitted the form**. If you have, please make sure to review your submission to ensure everything is filled out correctly.
2. Fill in all the required fields (if any) and any additional details you'd like to share with us.
3. For the **Service** field, select the type of service you need from the provided options.
4. For the **Date** field, enter the date you'd like to book the service for in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. For the **Time** field, enter the time you'd like to book the service for in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
6. You can add any additional **Notes** about your service request in the text field provided.
7. If you have any further instructions or details, you can add them in the **Submission** text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landscaping Request** (`landscaping_request`, text, optional): Enter the specific request or details about your landscaping needs.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your service request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your service request.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address so we can locate your property for the service.
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you need from the provided options (Landscaping, Lawn Mowing, Tree Service, Yard Cleanup, Other).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to book the service for in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you'd like to book the service for in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional details or instructions about your service request.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or instructions, please add them here.
