# HVAC Service Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HVAC Service Scheduling Request Form is a form designed to collect information for scheduling a service visit related to heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
2. Provide your contact information so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
3. Select the type of service you are interested in.
4. Choose a date and time for the service visit.
5. Enter the location where the service will take place.
6. Leave any additional comments or messages in the "Contact Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Scheduling Request** (`service_scheduling_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, text, required): Enter the type of service you are interested in (e.g. maintenance, repair, replacement).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you are requesting (e.g. air filter replacement, thermostat adjustment).
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the service visit.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required): Choose a time for the service visit.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the service will take place.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or messages about your appointment.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled to ensure accurate and prompt service scheduling.
- Be specific when selecting the service type to ensure we understand your needs exactly.
- Provide a reliable contact phone number and email address to avoid any confusion or missed calls.
- Include any relevant comments or messages to give us a better understanding of your needs.
