# VIP Security Escort Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VIP Security Escort Booking Form is used to request or confirm a security escort service for high-profile individuals or events. You will need to provide personal and contact information to submit a request for a security escort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Company (if applicable).
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select a Date for the escort service.
6. Choose a Time for the escort service.
7. Enter the Address where the escort service will take place.
8. Select the required Services for the escort.
9. Choose the Category for the escort service.
10. Provide additional Services Details if needed.
11. Select the Assigned User responsible for the escort.
12. Enter the Assigned User's Comment if needed.
13. Confirm your name as the Submitter.
14. Provide your Email address as the Submitter.
15. Enter your Phone number as the Submitter.
16. Enter your Company as the Submitter (if applicable).
17. Enter any additional Message if needed.
18. Enter any additional Notes if needed.
19. Enter your IP Address (if required by the organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Company**: Enter your company name if you represent a business or organization.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date**: Select the date for the escort service.
* **Time**: Select the time for the escort service.
* **Address**: Enter the location where the escort service will take place.
* **Services**: Select the services you require for the escort.
* **Category**: Select the category for the escort service.
* **Services Details**: Provide any additional information about the services you require.
* **Assigned User**: Select the user responsible for handling your request.
* **Assigned User Comment**: Enter any additional comment for the assigned user.
* **Submitter Name**: Confirm your name as the submittter.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Submitter Company**: Enter your company name if you represent a business or organization.
* **Submitter Message**: Enter any additional message if needed.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes if needed.
* **Submitter IP Address**: Enter your IP address (if required by the organization).
