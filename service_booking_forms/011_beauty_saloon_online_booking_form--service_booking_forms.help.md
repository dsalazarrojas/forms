# Beauty Saloon Online Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows clients to book an appointment at the Beauty Saloon. To book an appointment, please provide the required information, including your contact details and preferred date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in its entirety to book a successful appointment.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Use the options provided for services and preferred stylist to choose your preferred options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, **required**): Enter your full name for booking purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **required**): Provide your phone number for the stylist to contact you.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, **required**): Enter your email address for booking confirmation and updates.
* **Service Requested** (Service Requested, select_one, **required**): Choose one of the available services offered at the Beauty Saloon.
* **Preferred Stylist** (Preferred Stylist, select_one, **required**): Select your preferred stylist for the appointment.
* **Preferred Date** (Preferred Date, date, **required**): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, **required**): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Is this your first time with us?** (Is this your first time with us?, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether it's your first time at the Beauty Saloon.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (Special Requests or Notes, text, **optional**): Provide any special requests or notes for your appointment.
* **I agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy** (I agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.
