# Train Ticket Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a train ticket for passengers who wish to reserve a seat on a train.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and surname in the corresponding text fields.
* Enter your email address in the email field.
* Select your date of birth using the date field.
* Choose your passenger type (Adult, Senior, or Child) from the dropdown menu.
* Enter the seat number you wish to book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (name, text, required): This field is where you enter your name.
* **Surname** (surname, text, required): This field is where you enter your surname.
* **Email** (email, email, required): This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): This field is where you enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Passenger Type** (passenger_type, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select whether you are an Adult, Senior, or Child passenger.
* **Seat Number** (seat_number, text, optional): This field is where you enter the seat number you wish to book.
