# Photography Service Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Service Extension Form is used to collect and manage information for photography service extension requests. This form helps gather essential details to process and track such requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Category** option that best describes the service request. This helps identify the service type and ensures accurate processing.
2. Enter the **Photography Service Name** and **Description** to provide a clear understanding of the request.
3. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Additional Services** if required.
4. Enter the **Client Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** to contact the client effectively.
5. Provide the **Address** (if necessary) and **Service Booking Notes** for additional context.
6. Enter the **Service Booking Form ID** to uniquely identify the form.
7. Optionally, select the **Assigned Tool** for the service extension request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: <label>Category</label> (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the service category to identify the type of request.
* **Photography Service Name**: <label>Photography Service Name</label> (`photography_service_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the photography service requested.
* **Description**: <label>Description</label> (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the service request.
* **Additional Services**: <label>Additional Services</label> (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select **Yes** if additional services are required.
* **Client Name**: <label>Client Name</label> (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Email**: <label>Email</label> (`email`, `text`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: <label>Phone</label> (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address**: <label>Address</label> (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's address (if necessary).
* **Service Booking Notes**: <label>Service Booking Notes</label> (`service_booking_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide additional notes for the service booking.
* **Assigned Tool**: <label>Assigned Tool</label> (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Select or enter the tool assigned for the service extension request.
* **Service Booking Form ID**: <label>Service Booking Form ID</label> (`service_booking_form_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID for the form.
* **Created By**: <label>Created By</label> (`created_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person creating the form.
* **Created At**: <label>Created At</label> (`created_at`, `date`, required): Record the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated By**: <label>Updated By</label> (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person updating the form.
* **Updated At**: <label>Updated At</label> (`updated_at`, `date`, optional): Record the date and time the form was updated.
* **Form ID**: <label>Form ID</label> (`form_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the form's unique ID.
* **Form Title**: <label>Form Title</label> (`form_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Form Description**: <label>Form Description</label> (`form_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of the form.
* **Category 2**: <label>Category 2</label> (`category_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category 2 for the service request.
* **Form Assigned Tool**: <label>Form Assigned Tool</label> (`form_assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Select or enter the tool assigned for the form.
* **Form Output File**: <label>Form Output File</label> (`form_output_file`, `text`, optional): Enter the form output file.
* **Service Booking Form ID 2**: <label>Service Booking Form ID 2</label> (`service_booking_form_id_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the service booking form ID 2.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: <label>Assigned Tool 2</label> (`assigned_tool_2`, `text`, optional): Select or enter the tool 2 assigned for the service extension request.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Select **Yes/No** options for **Additional Services** and **Category** and **Category 2** carefully.
- Provide a clear description for **Photography Service Name** and **Form Description**.
- Use a format for dates that is readable and consistent.
- Be precise when entering client **Email** and **Phone** details.
- Review the form for any errors before submission.
