# pest_control_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request pest control services. Customers can fill out this form to provide the required information for the pest control service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary details before starting to complete the form.
2. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's contact email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the address where the pest control service is required.
* **Pest Type** (`pest_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Ants
	+ Bed Bugs
	+ Cockroaches
	+ Fleas
	+ Rats
	+ Spiders
* Choose the type of pest that is being targeted for service from the list above.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the pest control service is needed.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Choose a time slot for the pest control service.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the service required.

## Tips
- Please double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.
- The information provided in this form will be used to schedule and provide effective pest control services.
