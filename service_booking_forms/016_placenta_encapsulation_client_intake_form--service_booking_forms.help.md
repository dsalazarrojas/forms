# Placenta Encapsulation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for placentas encapsulation services. It aims to gather medical and birth-related details to provide safe and proper service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
4. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Main Page (1)**
* **Name** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or medical records.
* **Phone Number** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.

### **Medical Info (2)**
* **Medical Conditions** (`title`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Medications** (`title`, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Surgeries or Hospitalizations** (`title`, text, required/optional): List any past surgeries or hospitalizations.

### **Birth Info (3)**
* **Date of Birth** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Birth Weight** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your birth weight.

### **Payment Info (4)**
* **Payment Method** (`name`, text, required/optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your services.
* **Payment Amount** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to pay for your services.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your information for errors before submission.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Ensure to follow the submission guidelines for proper service delivery.
