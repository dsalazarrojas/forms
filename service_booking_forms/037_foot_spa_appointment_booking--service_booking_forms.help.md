# foot_spa_appointment_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for a spa appointment booking system. It aims to gather information from clients to schedule a suitable time slot and service provider for their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you require, such as a foot spa, nail spa, massage, pedicure, or hair removal.
2. Enter your name in the `client_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `contact_email` field.
4. Input your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
5. Select a date for your appointment from the `date` field.
6. Choose a time slot for your appointment from the `appointment_time` field.
7. Choose a preferred spa location from the `preferred_spa` field.
8. Select preferred language from the `preferred_language` field.
9. Enter your preferred contact number in the `preferred_contact_number` field.
10. Select the length of your appointment in the `appointment_duration` field.
11. Indicate your gender from the `client_gender` field.
12. Select a language in which you would like to receive communication from the `preferred_language` field.
13. Enter your age group in the `client_age` field (optional).
14. Enter any additional notes in the `client_notes` field.
15. Confirm if you have a preferred spa location or not from the `preferred_spa` field.
16. Select your preferred contact method from the `preferred_service_provider` field.
17. Enter the price of the service you are willing to pay in the `service_price` field.
18. Select the currency of the service price from the `service_price_currency` field.
19. Set the status of your appointment as confirmed or pending in the `appointment_status` field.
20. Enter a brief description of the service you require in the `service_description` field.
21. Select additional options if you have a preferred spa location in the `preferred_spa` field.
22. Select additional options if you have a preferred service provider in the `preferred_service_provider` field.
