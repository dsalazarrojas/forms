# Dishwasher Installation Site Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a customer's site where a dishwasher will be installed. It is necessary for our team to understand the site's requirements, address, and contact details to ensure a smooth installation process.

## How To Complete This Form
Please read each field carefully and provide accurate information to the best of your ability. If you are unsure about any field, you can contact our team for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, required: false): Please enter the site's physical address where the dishwasher will be installed.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person we can contact for any further questions or concerns.
- **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, please provide the date of the installation.
- **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Site Phone** (`site_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the site's phone number.
- **Site Phone Type** (`site_phone_type`, select_one, required: false): Specify whether the site phone is a Home, Work, or Other number.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): If applicable, please provide the appointment time for the installation.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide the appointment date for the installation.
- **Contact Phone Type** (`contact_phone_type`, select_one, required: false): Specify whether the contact phone is a Home, Work, or Other number.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Site Notes** (`site_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any relevant notes about the site.
- **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the appointment.
- **Contact Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Specify whether you prefer to be contacted by Phone, E-Mail, SMS, or Other.
- **Contact Preferred Contact Time** (`contact_preferred_contact_time`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the preferred contact time, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime.
- **Contact Language** (`contact_language`, select_one, required: false): Specify the language you prefer to be contacted in, such as English, Spanish, Mandarin, or Other.
- **Appointment Language** (`appointment_language`, select_one, required: false): Specify the language of the appointment, such as English, Spanish, Mandarin, or Other.
- **Site Access** (`site_access`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether our team has access to the site, True or False.
- **Contact Access** (`contact_access`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether we have access to the contact person, True or False.

## Tips
- Please complete the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth installation process.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact our team for further assistance.
- Ensure that this form is only completed once per site location.
- If any fields are marked as required, these fields are essential for our team to complete the installation efficiently.
