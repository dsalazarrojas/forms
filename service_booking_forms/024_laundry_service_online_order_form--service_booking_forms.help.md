# Laundry Service Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the general public to place online orders for the laundry service. It guides you through selecting relevant information to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a pickup date**: Choose a date for your order to be picked up. This is the date you want the laundry service to collect your dirty laundry.
2. **Select a pickup time**: Choose a time for your order to be picked up. This will help the laundry service know when to collect your items.
3. **Enter pickup location**: Provide the address or location where the laundry service can collect your dirty laundry.
4. **Enter delivery address**: Provide the address or location where you want your clean laundry delivered.
5. **Choose order type**: Select the type of service you need, such as Regular or Priority.
6. **Select laundry items**: Choose the types of items you want to be laundered, such as Tops, Bottoms, Dress, or Upholstered items.
7. **Add special instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, enter them here.
8. **Enter order notes**: Provide any additional information about your order, such as a reference number or order details.
9. **Enter contact number**: Provide your contact number in case the laundry service needs to contact you.
10. **Enter email**: Provide your email address in case the laundry service needs to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **pickup_date**: Enter the date you want your laundry to be picked up.
  • This is the date the laundry service will collect your dirty laundry.
  • Use the format `yyyy-mm-dd` (e.g., 2023-07-15).
  
- **pickup_time**: Enter the time you want your laundry to be picked up.
  • This is the time the laundry service will collect your dirty laundry.
  • Use the format `hh:mm` (e.g., 13:30).
  
- **delivery_date**: Enter the date you want your clean laundry delivered.
  • This is the date the laundry service will deliver your clean laundry.
  • Use the format `yyyy-mm-dd` (e.g., 2023-07-17).
  
- **delivery_address**: Enter the address where you want your clean laundry delivered.
  • This is the address where the laundry service will deliver your clean laundry.
  • Enter the full address, including house number and street name.
  
- **order_type**: Select the type of service you need.
  • `Regular`: Standard laundry service.
  • `Priority`: Expedited laundry service.
  
- **laundry_items**: Select the types of items you want to be laundered.
  • `Tops`: T-shirts, tops, and other upper-body items.
  • `Bottoms`: Pants, shorts, and other lower-body items.
  • `Dress`: Formal dresses and special occasion dresses.
  • `Upholstered items`: Upholstered items, such as sofas, chairs, and curtains.
  
- **special_instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests for your order.
  • This is a free-text field where you can enter any notes or comments.
  
- **order_notes**: Enter any additional information about your order.
  • This is a free-text field where you can enter any notes or comments.
  
- **contact_number**: Enter your contact number.
  • This is the number the laundry service can contact you at.
  
- **email**: Enter your email address.
  • This is the email the laundry service can contact you at.
