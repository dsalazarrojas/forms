# Bartender Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from event planners to provide tailored bartending services for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Enter your client full name.
	* Provide your email address.
	* Enter your phone number.
2. Enter event details:
	* Select the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate event, Private party, etc.).
	* Choose the event date.
	* Select the start and end times of the event.
3. Provide event specifics:
	* Enter the event location (including address).
	* Estimate the number of guests attending.
	* Specify the number of bartenders needed.
4. Choose your services:
	* Select the type of bar service you need (full bar, beer and wine, etc.).
	* Decide who will provide the alcohol (you or the service provider).
	* Indicate if you need mixers and garnishes.
	* Specify if you need glassware rental or have your own.
	* Choose if you need a full bar setup or a portable bar.
5. Customize your experience:
	* If you have a custom cocktail, provide its name.
	* Specify how early you need the bartenders to arrive.
6. Finalize your booking:
	* Select the post-event cleanup option (yes or no).
	* Decide if you need liability insurance.
	* Provide an estimated budget range for the service.
	* Confirm if you're ready to pay a deposit.
