# Voiceover Studio Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to book a voiceover studio session with a voice actor. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required information, including the booking date, start time, voice actor, and other necessary details.
2.  Ensure you have selected the correct voice actor for your session.
3.  Enter your contact information for communication with the studio.
4.  Review and submit your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **booking_info** (`booking_info`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter any additional information about your booking request.
*   **client_name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): 
    Please enter your name as the person booking the session.
*   **studio_address** (`studio_address`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the address of the studio where the session will take place.
*   **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): 
    Select the date you would like the voiceover session to start on.
*   **start_time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): 
    Choose the time you would like the voiceover session to start at.
*   **voice_actor** (`voice_actor`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    Select the voice actor you would like to work with for your voiceover session.
*   **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): 
    Select the end date for your voiceover session.
*   **end_time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): 
    Choose the end time for your voiceover session.
*   **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): 
    Review and submit your booking request.
*   **client_email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): 
    Enter your email address for communication with the studio.
*   **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter your phone number for communication with the studio.
*   **studio_email** (`studio_email`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the email address of the studio.
*   **booking_status** (`booking_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Select the status of your booking request.
*   **studio_contact** (`studio_contact`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the contact information for the studio.
*   **additional_info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter any additional information about your session.
*   **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): 
    Enter any notes or comments about your session.
*   **assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the name of the user assigned to your session.
*   **studio_name** (`studio_name`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the name of the studio.
*   **studio_address** (`studio_address`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the address of the studio.
*   **studio_phone** (`studio_phone`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the phone number of the studio.
*   **studio_email** (`studio_email`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter the email address of the studio.
