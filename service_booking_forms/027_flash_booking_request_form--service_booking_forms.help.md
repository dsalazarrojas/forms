# Flash Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flash Booking Request Form is used by service staff to request a booking for a specific service, allowing the service team to efficiently manage the booking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your required information.
2. Ensure you select the correct service type from the drop-down options.
3. Enter your preferred booking time and any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (<input id="service_request">): Please describe your service request. This will help us understand the scope of the booking.
* **Preferred Booking Times** (<input id="preferred_booking_times">): Specify your preferred booking times for the service.
* **Contact Email** (<input id="contact_email">): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (<input id="contact_phone">): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about the booking.
* **Service Type** (<input id="service_type">): Select the type of service you require from the available options.
* **Service Details** (<input id="service_details">): Provide any additional comments or details about the service.
* **Booking Time** (<input id="booking_time">): Specify your preferred time for the booking.
* **Assigned Tool** (<input id="assigned_tool">): Select the tools required for the service.
* **Notes** (<input id="notes">): Add any additional comments or notes about the booking.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have a valid reason for the booking before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Make sure to select the correct service type and tools.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the 'Notes' field.
