# Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form is designed to help you book and confirm rehearsal studio lighting services. This form ensures that you can efficiently provide the necessary details for a smooth and successful rehearsal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Step 1: Select the Date of Your Rehearsal**
	* Choose the date you would like to book the rehearsal studio lighting services.
2. **Step 2: Choose the Time of Your Rehearsal**
	* Select the time you would like to book the rehearsal studio lighting services.
3. **Step 3: Provide Rehearsal Details**
	* In the "Rehearsal Details" field, enter a brief description of your rehearsal requirements.
4. **Step 4: Specify Number of Lights**
	* Enter the total number of lights needed for your rehearsal.
5. **Step 5: Identify Your Lighting Designer**
	* If you have a preferred lighting designer, enter their name in the "Lighting Designer" field.
6. **Step 6: Share Contact Information**
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and confirmations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form** (`note`): This is the title of the form and not a field you need to fill.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you would like to book the rehearsal studio lighting services.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time you would like to book the rehearsal studio lighting services.
* **Rehearsal Details** (`text`): Provide a brief description of your rehearsal requirements.
* **Number of Lights** (`number`): Enter the total number of lights needed for your rehearsal.
* **Lighting Designer** (`text`): If you have a preferred lighting designer, enter their name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and confirmations.
