# Internet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for internet service providers to register their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name (page 1).
2. Enter your Last Name (page 1).
3. Enter your email address (page 2).
4. Enter your Phone number (page 2).
5. Enter your Address (page 2).
6. Enter the City where your internet service is located (page 3).
7. Enter your Zip code (page 3).
8. Select the type of service you offer (page 4, if you have multiple services, you can select one at a time).
9. Choose the speed of your internet service (page 4, select one or more options that apply).
10. Enter the start date of your service (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your service.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter the physical address of your service location.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your service is located.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `number`, required): Enter the zip code of your service location.
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of internet service you offer, such as DSL, Cable, or Fiber.
* **Speed** (`Speed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more internet speeds you offer, such as 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, 2000, or 3000.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your service started.
