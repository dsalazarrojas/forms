# Marketing Quest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect marketing-related information from customers to help us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you fill out all required and optional fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
3. Check our database to avoid submitting duplicate forms.
4. If you're unsure about any field, ask your team member for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Quest Form 1** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your marketing quest.
* **Marketing Quest Form 2** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the number of people involved in your marketing quest.
* **Marketing Quest Form 3** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following options for your marketing quest:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3
* **Marketing Quest Form 4** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple options for your marketing quest:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
* **Marketing Quest Form 5** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your marketing quest.
* **Marketing Quest Form 6** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your marketing quest.
* **Marketing Quest Form 7** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your marketing quest.
* **Marketing Quest Form 8** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Marketing Quest Form 9-23** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your marketing quest for each of these fields.

Note: Fields 9-23 are repeated fields for collecting more information about your marketing quest. Please provide as much detail as possible for each of these fields.
