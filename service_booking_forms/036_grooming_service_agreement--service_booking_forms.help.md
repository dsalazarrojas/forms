# Grooming Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by pet owners to schedule and book a grooming service for their pets. The service provider will use this form to collect relevant information from the client and their pet, and to prepare for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Client Details**
	* Enter your name and contact information in this section.
2. **Service Selections**
	* Select the grooming service you would like to book (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
3. **Appointment Preferences**
	* Select your preferred appointment date and time.
4. **Payment Method**
	* Select your preferred payment method (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
5. **Signed Consent**
	* Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the service.
6. **Pet Details**
	* Enter your pet's name, age, and breed.
7. **Service Date**
	* Select the date you would like to schedule the service.
8. **Service Start Time**
	* Select the start time of the service.
9. **Submit**
	* Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.
