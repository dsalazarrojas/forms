## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Service Type (Facial, Massage, Manicure, or Pedicare) that you would like to book.
2. Enter the Client Name and Client Email in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose the date and time of the service from the available options.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Note field (optional).
5. If applicable, assign the service to a specific technician from the list (Jim, Jenny, Bob, or Sue) and select any tools that will be used during the service.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the service booking.
