# smc_hairdressers_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for booking a service with a hairdresser.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date for your service in the Date field. You can select a date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
6. Choose a time for your service in the Time field. You can select a time in the format hh:MM.
7. Select the type of service you want to book in the Service Type field. You can choose from: Plumber, Electrician, Brician, or Beautician.
8. Write a message or note about your service in the Message field.
9. If applicable, enter the assigned tool for the service in the Assigned Tool field.
10. If you want to categorize your service, enter it in the Category field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: A text field where you can enter your first name.
* **Last name**: A text field where you can enter your last name.
* **Email**: An email field where you can enter your email address. Please ensure it is in the correct format: name@domain.com.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Date**: A date field where you can select a date for your service. You can select a date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time**: A time field where you can select a time for your service. You can select a time in the format hh:MM.
* **Service Type**: A select field where you can choose from: Plumber, Electrician, Brician, or Beautician.
* **Message**: A text field where you can enter any additional notes or comments about your service.
* **Assigned Tool**: A text field where you can enter the assigned tool for your service.
* **Category**: A text field where you can categorize your service.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth booking process.
* Double-check the service type, date, and time before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us.
