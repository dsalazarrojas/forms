# Boat Service Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Service Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information about your boat and its maintenance needs. This information will help us provide you with the best possible service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for guidance.
3. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information about your boat and its history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required): Please provide the full name of the boat owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the boat owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): Provide the contact phone number of the boat owner.
* **Boat Name** (`boat_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the boat (e.g., "My Yacht", "Sailboat XYZ").
* **Boat Make** (`boat_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of the boat (e.g., "Fiberglass", "Steel").
* **Boat Model** (`boat_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model name of the boat.
* **Boat Year** (`boat_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year the boat was manufactured.
* **Boat Hull ID** (`boat_hull_id`, `text`, required): Provide the HIN (Hull Identification Number) of the boat.
* **Engine Make** (`engine_make`, `text`, optional): Enter the make of the engine (e.g., "Mercedes", "BMW").
* **Engine Model** (`engine_model`, `text`, optional): Enter the model name of the engine.
* **Engine Hours** (`engine_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours on the engine.
* **Last Service Date** (`last_service_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last service.
* **Last Service Type** (`last_service_type`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of service performed.
* **Previous Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the previous service provider.
* **Current Issues** (`current_issues`, `text`, optional): Describe any current issues with the boat.
* **Service Needed** (`service_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service required (e.g., "Routine Maintenance", "Oil Change").
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the preferred date for the service.
* **Boat Location** (`boat_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the boat is stored.
* **Storage Type** (`storage_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the storage type (e.g., "In Water - Marina Slip", "Dry Stack").
* **Haul-Out Needed** (`haul_out_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the boat needs to be hauled out.
* **Parts Replacement Expected** (`parts_replacement`, `text`, optional): Provide any known parts needed for replacement.
* **Warranty Work** (`warranty_work`, `select_one`, required): Check if this is a warranty service.
* **Insurance Claim** (`insurance_claim`, `select_one`, required): Check if this is an insurance claim.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the expected cost range.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the service.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about us (e.g., "Website", "Social Media").
