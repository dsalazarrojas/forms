# gutter_cleaning_service_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gutter Cleaning Service Booking form is used to gather information for booking gutter cleaning services. It helps us understand your preferences, requirements, and schedule an appointment for our service professionals to visit your location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure we have all the necessary information to process your booking.
2. Double-check your details before submission to avoid any mistakes.
3. Click the submit button to send your request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: This field is for the name of your business or property.
* **Service Address**: Provide the address where the gutter cleaning service is required.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter the time of day you prefer for the service appointment.
* **Start Date** and **End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the service period.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for us to reach you.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the service.
* **Services Offered**: Select the services you require (Gutter Cleaning, Power Washing, or Gutter Inspections).
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the service technician assigned to your job (ChatJimmy, JohnDoe, or JaneDoe).
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service required (Full Service, Maintenance, or Inspection).
* **Gutter Size**: Enter the size of your gutters.
* **Gutter Type**: Enter the type of gutters you have.
* **Gutter Material**: Enter the material of your gutters.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the preferred time for the appointment.
* **Confirmation**: Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Service Area**: Select the areas to be serviced (Front of the house, Back of the house, or Basement).
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Service Status**: Select the current status of your service (Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Completed).
* **Cancellation Reason**: Provide the reason for cancellation (if applicable).
* **Cancellation Status**: Select the status of your cancellation (Pending, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Customer Note**: Add any notes for the service provider.
* **Staff Note**: Add any internal notes for the service team.
* **Service Note**: Add any additional information for the service provider.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (Pending, Completed, or Refunded).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you used (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).

This help guide is to ensure that you have a clear understanding of the form and its fields. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
