# Plumbing Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers for a plumbing service request. It will help the plumbing company understand the client's needs and schedule the necessary service visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including your contact name and details.
2. Select the type(s) of service you require (e.g., drain cleaning, leak detection, or plumbing repair).
3. Provide your contact details, including phone number and email.
4. Select a date and time for the service visit.
5. Optionally, you can provide additional service information.
6. Enter a message if you have any specific requests or concerns.
7. Submit the form to initiate the service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This section is for you to enter your name and details. Please provide accurate information so that we can serve you better.
* **Contact Information**: This is an important section where you need to provide your phone number and email address. We will use this to contact you to confirm the service visit and provide updates.
* **Select a Service**: Choose the type(s) of service you need. You can select multiple services if required.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide any additional contact information you have, such as your address or other relevant details.
* **Service Information**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information about your service request.
* **Schedule**: Select a date and time for the service visit. We will do our best to accommodate your preferred time slot.
* **Payment Terms**: If you have any specific payment terms or requests, please enter them here.
* **Select Multiple Services**: Similar to the previous service selection, but for multiple services.
* **Service Info 2**: Another optional section for any additional information you may want to provide.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the service visit.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Message**: If you have any specific requests or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Submission**: This is not a required field and should only be filled by our staff when they confirm the service visit and send it to you.
