# Utility Service Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Service Application Form is used to collect information from customers who are requesting utility services such as electricity, water, or gas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Information** (page 1) - Please fill in the required fields with your contact details.
2. Select the **Service Request** (page 1) - Choose the type of service you are requesting (Electricity, Water, or Gas).
3. Enter your **Contact Information** (page 1) - Provide your contact details.
4. Provide any **Additional Information** (page 2) - If you have any additional comments or details about your service request, please add them here.
5. Select the **Service Request Date** (page 2) - Choose the date when you would like the service to begin.
6. Select the **Service Request Time** (page 2) - Choose the time when you would like the service to begin.
7. Sign with your **Digital Signature** (page 3) - This may be required for service agreement or verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: This field is required and should be filled in with your name, address, and contact details.
* **Service Request**: Please select the type of service you are requesting (Electricity, Water, or Gas).
* **Contact Information**: This field is required and should be filled in with your contact details.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional comments or details about your service request, please add them here.
* **Service Request Date**: Please select the date when you would like the service to begin.
* **Service Request Time**: Please select the time when you would like the service to begin.
* **Digital Signature**: A digital signature is required to complete the form. It may be used for service agreement or verification purposes.
* **Submit**: Please review your information before submitting the form. Once submitted, the information will be reviewed and processed.
* **Signature**: This field is not currently used, but is included as part of the form structure.

Note: The **Submit Button** field is not actually used in the form submission process and can be ignored.
