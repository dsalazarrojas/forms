# Maid Service Work Order - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a work order for a maid service. It gathers information about the service type, date, start and end times, client details, and any additional services required. This form is essential for scheduling and preparing the necessary resources for the service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the type of service you need (Maid Service, Deep Cleaning, Window Cleaning, or Other - specify in the text field).
2.  Choose a service date for your appointment.
3.  Provide the start and end times for the service.
4.  Enter your client's name, contact number, and email address.
5.  If applicable, specify any additional services you require.
6.  Add any service details or requirements you'd like to mention to the maids.
7.  Schedule the appointment by providing the date in the Schedule Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you need, such as Maid Service, Deep Cleaning, Window Cleaning, or specify other details in the text field.
*   **Service Date** (`Service Date`, date, required): Select the date for your appointment.
*   **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required): Enter the start time for your service.
*   **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required): Enter the end time for your service.
*   **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person you're booking the service for.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact number or the contact number of the person you're booking the service for.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person you're booking the service for.
*   **Service Details 1/2** (`Service Details 1/2`, text, optional): Add any additional details or requirements for the service.
*   **Additional Services** (`Additional Services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you'd like to include in the service, such as Vacuuming, Mopping, Dusting, Polishing, Cleaning, or specify other options.
*   **Schedule Date** (`Schedule Date`, date, required): Schedule the appointment by providing the date in the Schedule Date field.
