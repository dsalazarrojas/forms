# idg_meeting_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The idg_meeting_template form is designed to help authorized personnel schedule meetings with facilities. It collects relevant information for meeting scheduling, goal definition, and action items.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name in the "facility_name" field.
2. Provide the meeting date in the "meeting_date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the meeting time in the "meeting_time" field in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Enter your name in the "name" field.
5. Select your role in the "role" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the international format (XXX-XXX-XXXX) in the "contact_number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Define the goal of the meeting in the "goal_of_meeting" field.
9. Select the action item from the options provided in the "action_item" field.
10. Provide a brief note about the selected action item in the "action_item_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility_name**: Enter the name of the facility you would like to schedule a meeting with.
* **meeting_date**: Provide the date of the meeting in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **meeting_time**: Enter the meeting time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **name**: Enter your name.
* **role**: Select your role in the meeting.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number in international format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **goal_of_meeting**: Define the purpose or goal of the meeting.
* **action_item**: Select the action item related to the meeting goal.
* **action_item_note**: Provide a brief note about the selected action item.
