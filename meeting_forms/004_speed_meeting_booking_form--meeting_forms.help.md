# Speed Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed Meeting Booking Form is a tool used to create meeting bookings with attendees. It allows you to collect meeting details, attendees, and other relevant information in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter meeting details such as start time, end time, meeting duration, and meeting type.
2.  Select the venue and room number for the meeting.
3.  Add attendees to the meeting by selecting 'Yes' for each attendee.
4.  Enter meeting notes and any additional meeting attachments.
5.  Add meeting document links or files as needed.
6.  Set the meeting status to 'Confirmed', 'Tentative', 'Cancelled', 'Rescheduled', or 'In Progress'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
- **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the meeting.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of the meeting.
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number): Enter the duration of the meeting.
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple): Select each attendee's participation by choosing 'Yes' for each one.
- **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one): Choose the type of meeting (e.g., Meeting, Conference, Seminar).
- **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, text): Enter a brief description of the venue.
- **Venue** (`venue`, text): Enter the name of the venue.
- **Room Number** (`room_number`, number): Enter the room number for the meeting.
- **Location** (`location`, text): Enter the location of the meeting.
- **Meeting Room Details** (`meeting_room_details`, text): Enter any additional details about the meeting room.
- **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, text): Enter any notes about the meeting.
- **Meeting Status** (`meeting_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the meeting.
- **Meeting Sponsor** (`meeting_sponsor`, select_multiple): Select if the meeting is sponsored by choosing 'Yes' for each sponsor.
- **Meeting Presentation** (`meeting_presentation`, text): Enter a brief description of the presentation.
- **Meeting Document Link** (`meeting_document_link`, text): Enter a link to any meeting documents.
- **Meeting File** (`meeting_file`, text): Enter a file related to the meeting.
- **Meeting Attachment** (`meeting_attachment`, text): Upload any additional meeting attachments.
- **Meeting Note** (`meeting_note`, text): Enter any final notes about the meeting.
- **Meeting Attachment 2** (`meeting_attachment_2`, text): Upload any additional meeting attachments.
- **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text): Enter the name of the attendee.
- **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email): Enter the email of the attendee.
- **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
- **Attendee Organization** (`attendee_organization`, text): Enter the organization of the attendee.
- **Attendee Job Title** (`attendee_job_title`, text): Enter the job title of the attendee.
