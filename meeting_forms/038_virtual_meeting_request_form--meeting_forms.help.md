# Virtual Meeting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for virtual meetings, allowing team members to request meetings with clients or colleagues for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in the correct form and have all required permissions.
2. Select the meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field.
3. Choose the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field.
4. Enter the expected meeting duration in the "Meeting Duration" field.
5. Provide the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field.
6. List the attendees in the "Attendees" field.
7. Select the meeting type from the options provided in the "Meeting Type" field.
8. If applicable, enter a brief description of the meeting's purpose in the "Meeting Purpose" field.
9. Optionally, select the user(s) you would like to be assigned to the meeting in the "Assigned User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date (meeting_date)**: Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time (meeting_time)**: Choose the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (meeting_duration)**: Enter the length of time the meeting will last.
* **Meeting Location (meeting_location)**: Provide the location of the meeting.
* **Attendees (attendees)**: List all the individuals attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Type (meeting_type)**: Select one of the provided options for the meeting type.
* **Meeting Purpose (meeting_purpose)**: A brief description of the meeting's purpose (if applicable).
* **Assigned User (assigned_user)**: Optionally select the user(s) assigned to the meeting (if applicable).
