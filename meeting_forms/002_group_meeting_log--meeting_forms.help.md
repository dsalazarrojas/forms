# Group Meeting Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Meeting Log form is a document used to record information about group meetings. It aims to gather details about the meeting, including the participants, the notes taken during the meeting, and the status of the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the General Info section with a brief title or description of the meeting.
* In the Members section, select the members who attended the meeting.
* In the Notes section, type any important or relevant notes taken during the meeting.
* In the Meeting Status section, select the current status of the meeting (Finished, Ongoing, or Upcoming).
* In the Meeting Date, Meeting Time, and Meeting Start/End Time sections, enter the relevant dates and times for the meeting.
* In the Task and Assignee sections, enter any tasks assigned to specific individuals and their names.
* In the Assigned Time and Finished By sections, enter the time assigned and time finished for tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (text)
	+ This is the title or brief description of the meeting.
* **Members** (select_multiple)
	+ Select the members who attended the meeting.
* **Notes** (note)
	+ Type any important or relevant notes taken during the meeting.
* **Meeting Status** (select_one)
	+ Select the current status of the meeting (Finished, Ongoing, or Upcoming).
* **Meeting Date** (date)
	+ Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (time)
	+ Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Start Time** (time)
	+ Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting End Time** (time)
	+ Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **Task** (text)
	+ Enter any tasks assigned to specific individuals during the meeting.
* **Assignee** (text)
	+ Enter the name of the person to whom a task is assigned.
* **Assigned Time** (time)
	+ Enter the time assigned to complete the task.
* **Finished By** (date)
	+ Enter the date a task is expected to be finished.
* **Finished Time** (time)
	+ Enter the time a task is expected to be finished.
