# Meeting Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Room Reservation Form is used to reserve a meeting room in the organization. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the meeting room is reserved correctly and to prevent conflicts with other meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the meeting room you would like to reserve.
2. Provide a brief information about your meeting topic.
3. Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Room**: Select the meeting room you would like to reserve. You can select multiple rooms if you need multiple rooms for your meeting.
* **Meeting Topic**: Provide a brief description of your meeting topic. This will help the meeting room management to prepare the necessary equipment and materials.
* **Reserve Meeting**: Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes" or "No". If you select "Yes", the meeting room will be reserved for you. If you select "No", your meeting will not be reserved.
