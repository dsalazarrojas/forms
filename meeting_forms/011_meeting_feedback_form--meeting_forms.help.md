# Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from meeting attendees, helping organizers understand their experience, suggestions, and areas for improvement. Your input will contribute to making future meetings more engaging, productive, and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Meeting Feedback Form" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to receive a copy of your feedback or be notified when it's published.
3. Share your thoughts and suggestions in the "Comment" field. This is an opportunity to express your opinions and help us improve future meetings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Feedback Form (Page 1)
* **Meeting Feedback Form** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your meeting attendance list. This will help the meeting organizers identify your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like to receive a copy of your feedback or be notified when it's published. If you're not comfortable sharing your email, you can leave this field empty.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the meeting. Be as detailed or brief as you like, and let us know how we can improve for future events. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your openness.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular point, feel free to ask.
