# one_on_one_meeting_focus_sheet_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The One-on-One Meeting Focus Sheet is used to document and track important details about individual meetings, such as the name and email of the person meeting with, the date of the meeting, and any relevant notes or discussions. This form is designed to help ensure that all necessary information is captured and stored for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the meeting minutes and notes taken during the meeting.
2.  Fill in the name and email of the person you met with.
3.  Enter the date of the meeting.
4.  Take a few minutes to review the meeting notes and add any additional comments or notes.
5.  Submit the form to update the meeting record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Focus Sheet**: This is where you can add any additional notes or comments from the meeting. You can type in as much information as needed.
* **Name**: Please enter the name of the person you met with.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person you met with.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number for the person you met with, you can enter it here.
* **Meeting Date**: Please select the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Date**: This field will automatically populate with the selected date.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the meeting minutes and notes before filling out this form to ensure accuracy.
* If the person you met with does not have an email or phone number, you can leave those fields blank.
* You can add additional comments or notes in the Meeting Focus Sheet field to supplement the meeting record.
