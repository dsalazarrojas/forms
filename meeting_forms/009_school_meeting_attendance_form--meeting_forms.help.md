# School Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Meeting Attendance Form is used to record the attendance status of individuals at school meetings, ensuring accurate and timely tracking for administrative, academic, and logistical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the submitter.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Add any relevant notes about your attendance.
5. Select your attendance status (Present, Absent, or Late).
6. Choose the date of the meeting you attended.
7. If applicable, select the start and end times of the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the submitter of this form.
- **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will help us contact you if needed.
- **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed.
- **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about your attendance, such as the meeting location or any specific details about your attendance.
- **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Select whether you were Present, Absent, or Late to the meeting.
  - Select **Present** if you attended the meeting as expected.
  - Select **Absent** if you were not present at the meeting.
  - Select **Late** if you arrived late to the meeting.
- **Date of Attendance** (`attendance_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the meeting you attended.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): If applicable, enter the start time of the meeting.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): If applicable, enter the end time of the meeting.
