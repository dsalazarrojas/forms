# Company Meeting Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness and quality of company meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Review the meeting you are evaluating. Consider its objectives, agenda, and any action items discussed during the meeting.
2.  Answer each question honestly, providing detailed and specific feedback on your experience during the meeting.
3.  Ensure that you have provided accurate and relevant information for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
*   **Email Address** (`employee_email`, email, optional): This is your work email address. Enter your official company email address.
*   **Department** (`department`, select\_one, required): Please choose the department you belong to.
*   **Meeting Name/Type** (`meeting_name`, select\_one, required): Please select the type of meeting from the given options.
*   **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): This is the date of the meeting you are reviewing. Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, select\_one, required): Rate the duration of the meeting. Choose the option that best describes how long the meeting lasted.
*   **Meeting Organizer** (`meeting_organizer`, text, optional): If you know who organized the meeting, enter their name here.
*   **Meeting Effectiveness** (`section_effectiveness`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about the meeting's effectiveness.
*   **Clarity of Purpose and Objective** (`clear_objective`, select\_one, required): Rate how clearly the meeting's purpose and objective were established.
*   **Success of Objective** (`objective_achieved`, select\_one, required): Rate how successful the meeting's objective was in achieving its purpose.
*   **Agenda Provided** (`agenda_provided`, select\_one, required): Rate whether an agenda was provided before the meeting.
*   **Agenda Adherence** (`agenda_followed`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether the agenda was followed during the meeting.
*   **Time Management** (`section_time`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about time management during the meeting.
*   **Meeting Start Time** (`started_on_time`, select\_one, required): Rate whether the meeting started on time.
*   **Meeting End Time** (`ended_on_time`, select\_one, required): Rate whether the meeting ended on time.
*   **Time Spent** (`time_well_spent`, select\_one, required): Rate whether the time spent during the meeting was worthwhile.
*   **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length_appropriate`, select\_one, required): Rate whether the meeting length was appropriate for the content.
*   **Participation** (`section_participation`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about participation during the meeting.
*   **Encouraged Participation** (`encouraged_participation`, select\_one, required): Rate how encouraged participants were to contribute during the meeting.
*   **Comfortable Sharing** (`comfortable_speaking`, select\_one, required): Rate how comfortable you felt sharing your thoughts during the meeting.
*   **All Voices Heard** (`all_voices_heard`, select\_one, required): Rate whether all participants had an opportunity to share their thoughts.
*   **Productive Discussions** (`discussion_productive`, select\_one, required): Rate the quality of discussions during the meeting.
*   **Facilitation** (`section_leadership`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about the meeting's facilitation.
*   **Well-Facilitated** (`well_facilitated`, select\_one, required): Rate the quality of the meeting's facilitation.
*   **Ground Rules** (`ground_rules_followed`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether the meeting ground rules were established and followed.
*   **Conflicts Managed** (`conflicts_managed`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether conflicts were managed constructively during the meeting.
*   **Outcomes** (`section_outcomes`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about the meeting's outcomes.
*   **Clear Action Items** (`clear_action_items`, select\_one, required): Rate how clearly action items were defined during the meeting.
*   **Action Item Owners** (`owners_assigned`, select\_one, optional): Rate how well action items were assigned to participants.
*   **Deadlines Set** (`deadlines_set`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether deadlines were set for action items.
*   **Follow-Up Planned** (`follow_up_planned`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether follow-up was planned for action items.
*   **Materials and Resources** (`section_materials`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about the meeting's materials and resources.
*   **Prepared Materials** (`materials_prepared`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether necessary materials were prepared before the meeting.
*   **Technology Functionality** (`technology_worked`, select\_one, optional): Rate whether technology was used during the meeting was functional.
*   **Overall Assessment** (`section_overall`, note, optional): This section is for additional notes or observations about the meeting's overall assessment.
*   **Meeting Effectiveness Rating** (`meeting_effectiveness_rating`, select\_one, required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the meeting. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
*   **Future Attendance** (`would_attend_again`, select\_one, required): Rate your intention to attend similar meetings in the future.
*   **Best Aspect** (`best_aspect`, text, optional): If there was a standout moment or aspect of the meeting, enter it here.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): If there's anything that could be improved for future meetings, enter it here.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or feedback, enter it here.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): This is the current date.

Please fill out this form honestly and thoroughly, providing clear and specific feedback on your experience during the meeting. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our company meetings.
