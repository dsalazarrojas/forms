# Matrimonial Meet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals attending a meeting. Please fill out the form with your details to provide the meeting organizer with necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "user_info" page.
2. On this page, provide your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
3. Continue to the "event_info" and "event_name" pages to provide information about the meeting.
4. On the "availability" page, select your availability for the meeting.
5. Finally, enter your email address and phone number to ensure you can be reached before and after the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This page is where you can provide any additional details about yourself that you'd like to share with the meeting organizer.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name to associate with your information.
* **email** (`email`, text, optional): Provide your email address so the meeting organizer can reach you before and after the meeting.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure the meeting organizer can contact you.
* **availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select your availability for the meeting from the options: "1", "2", or "3" - please note that "1" and "2" and "3" are likely to be labels for different days or time slots.
* **event_info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Describe the meeting in a few words so that the organizer can get a clear picture.
* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the meeting.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the meeting.
