# Meeting Attendance Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Meeting Attendance Record Form is used to record information about meetings, including meeting date, time, location, attendees, and other relevant details. This form is designed to help you keep track of your meetings in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting date and start/end times in the respective date and time fields.
2. Enter the meeting location in the "meeting location" field.
3. Indicate whether you attended the meeting ("Yes" or "No") in the "meeting attendee" field.
4. If you were the meeting organizer, select "Yes" in the "Meeting Organizer" field.
5. If you used a specific platform to connect with meeting attendees, select "Yes" from the list of options in the "meeting connect platforms" field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the meeting in the "meeting notes" field.
7. If you used a specific email or phone number to connect with meeting attendees, enter it in the "meeting connect email" and "meeting connect phone" fields respectively.
8. Enter the number of meeting attendees in the "meeting attendance count" field.
9. Enter the percentage of meeting attendees in the "meeting attendance percent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **meeting_attendance_form** (Text, Not Required): This is the main section of the form where you can enter information about the meeting.
* **meeting_date** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **meeting_start** (Time, Not Required): Enter the start time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **meeting_end** (Time, Not Required): Enter the end time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **meeting_location** (Text, Not Required): Enter the location where the meeting took place.
* **meeting_attendant** (Select One, Not Required): Indicate whether you attended the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **meeting_organizer** (Select One, Not Required): If you were the meeting organizer, select "Yes".
* **meeting_notes** (Note, Not Required): Enter any additional notes about the meeting.
* **meeting_platform** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select from the list of options if you used a specific platform to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_connect** (Text, Not Required): This field is not used in this form.
* **meeting_connect_email** (Email, Not Required): Enter the email used to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_connect_phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter the phone number used to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_connect_platforms** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select from the list of options if you used a specific platform to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_connect_url** (Text, Not Required): Enter the URL used to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_platforms** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select from the list of options if you used a specific platform to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_connect_platforms_2** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select from the list of options if you used a specific platform to connect with meeting attendees.
* **meeting_attendance_count** (Number, Not Required): Enter the number of meeting attendees.
* **meeting_attendance_percent** (Number, Not Required): Enter the percentage of meeting attendees.
