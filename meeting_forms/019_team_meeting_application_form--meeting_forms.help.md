# Team Meeting Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for team members to request a meeting with their team. Please note that non-team related requests will not be approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Review the fields below for additional guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member** (`applicant_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name.
* **ID** (`applicant_id`, number, **required**): Enter your ID number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, **required**): Select a meeting date from the calendar.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, **required**): Choose a meeting time that works for you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select a meeting date and time that works for your team.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Non-team related requests will be rejected. If you need to discuss a non-team related topic, please reach out to your team leader directly.
