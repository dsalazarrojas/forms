# Educational Meeting Minutes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the details and outcomes of educational meetings. It helps capture relevant information, such as the meeting title, date, time, attendees, and any decisions made or actions items assigned. This information will help to ensure accurate and efficient communication among team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the "Meeting Title" field, which should concisely describe the purpose and topic of the meeting.
2. Enter the "Date" of the meeting in the date field.
3. Enter the "Meeting Time" to specify the exact time the meeting took place.
4. Select "Meeting Attendees" by choosing the individuals who attended the meeting.
5. In the "Purpose" field, briefly describe the main objective or topic discussed during the meeting.
6. Enter the "Location" where the meeting was held.
7. Take a few minutes to write down the "Meeting Notes" summarizing the key points and discussions.
8. Indicate whether "Next Steps" were assigned to any attendees by selecting the corresponding options.
9. Select "Decisions Made" to indicate whether any decisions were reached during the meeting.
10. Finally, mark "Action Items" that were assigned to any attendees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Title (1)**: The title of the meeting, e.g., "Weekly Team Meeting".
* **Date (2)**: The date the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Time (3)**: The exact time the meeting occurred.
* **Meeting Attendees (4)**: Select all attendees that participated in the meeting.
* **Purpose (5)**: A brief description of the meeting's main objective or topic.
* **Location (6)**: The physical location where the meeting was held.
* **Meeting Notes (7)**: Write down a summary of the key points and discussions during the meeting.
* **Next Steps (8)**: Indicate if next steps were assigned to any attendees.
* **Decisions Made (9)**: Check if any decisions were made during the meeting.
* **Action Items (10)**: Mark action items assigned to any attendees.
