# Meeting Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants and meeting details for meeting management and administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information: First name, last name, and participant email.
2. Choose the participant's status and type from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional meeting details in the "Meeting Details" text box.
4. Select whether the participant is actively participating or not from the "Participant List" dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, add any notes about the participant's status or meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant** (`participant`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required): Choose the participant's type (Participant, Host, Presenter, or No Answer).
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's first name.
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's last name.
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, required): Choose the participant's status (Active or Inactive).
  - This field is required to be filled in.
- **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional meeting details.
  - This field is optional.
- **Participant List** (`participant_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the participant is actively participating or not.
  - This field is optional.
- **Participant Status 2** (`participant_status_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the participant's status (Active or Inactive).
  - This field is optional.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the participant's status or meeting.
  - This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the participant's details before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your meeting management guidelines for clarification.
