# Online Meeting Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after an online meeting. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for future online meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the meeting details: title, date, time, and location.
* Provide your name and position.
* Rate the presenter's performance (if applicable).
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting.
* Rate the relevance of the meeting and its content.
* Provide any comments or justifications for your ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_form`): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`): Enter the title of the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Objectives** (`meeting_objectives`): Enter the objectives of the meeting.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name.
* **Participant Position** (`participant_position`): Enter your position.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`): Enter your department.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting (1-5).
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`): Provide any comments or feedback about the meeting.
* **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`): Enter the name of the presenter.
* **Presenter Position** (`presenter_position`): Enter the position of the presenter.
* **Presenter Department** (`presenter_department`): Enter the department of the presenter.
* **Presenter Rating** (`presenter_rating`): Rate the presenter's performance (1-5).
* **Presenter Comments** (`presenter_comments`): Provide any comments or feedback about the presenter.
* **Meeting Outcome** (`meeting_outcome`): Select the outcome of the meeting (Good, Satisfactory, Fair).
* **Meeting Outcome Justification** (`meeting_outcome_justification`): Provide a justification for the meeting outcome.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Satisfaction Justification** (`overall_satisfaction_justification`): Provide a justification for your overall satisfaction.
* **Participant Relevance** (`participant_relevance`): Rate the relevance of the meeting content to you (Highly Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant).
* **Participant Relevance Justification** (`participant_relevance_justification`): Provide a justification for the participant relevance.
* **Presenter Relevance** (`presenter_relevance`): Rate the presenter's relevance (Highly Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant).
* **Presenter Relevance Justification** (`presenter_relevance_justification`): Provide a justification for the presenter's relevance.
* **Overall Relevance** (`overall_relevance`): Rate the overall relevance of the meeting (Highly Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant).
