# Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form is used to schedule and organize parent-teacher meetings between parents, teachers, and students. This form helps to facilitate communication and coordination among stakeholders, ensuring that everyone is aware of meeting times, dates, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the `parent_name` field.
2. Choose a date for the meeting in the `meeting_date` field.
3. Enter the student's name in the `student_name` field.
4. Enter the teacher's name in the `teacher_name` field.
5. Select a time for the meeting in the `meeting_time` field.
6. Enter the meeting location in the `meeting_location` field.
7. Enter the expected duration of the meeting in the `meeting_duration` field.
8. Enter the parent's phone number in the `parent_phone` field.
9. Enter the student's phone number in the `student_phone` field.
10. Enter the teacher's phone number in the `teacher_phone` field.
11. Add any additional meeting notes in the `meeting_notes` field.
12. Enter the exact date and time of the meeting in the `meeting_date_time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's name to identify them in the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the meeting.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name to associate them with the meeting.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's name to identify them for the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the meeting to fit everyone's schedules.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false): Enter the physical location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the expected length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's phone number for communication.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number for communication.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's phone number for communication.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or details about the meeting.
* **Meeting Date Time** (`meeting_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the exact date and time of the meeting for record-keeping purposes.
