# Meeting Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Report Template is a form used for reporting on meetings. It is designed to capture relevant information about the meeting, including the meeting summary, date, location, attendees, meeting objectives, and discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Meeting Summary** field. This is a text box where you can provide a brief summary of the meeting.
2. Next, select the **Date** of the meeting using the date picker or enter it manually.
3. If the meeting took place in a specific location, type that location in the **Location** field.
4. In the **Attendees** field, select all the people who attended the meeting.
5. Describe the **Meeting Objectives** that were discussed during the meeting.
6. Summarize the main points from the **Discussion**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Summary** (Meeting Summary, text, required: false): 
    A brief summary of the meeting's key takeaways, purpose, and outcome.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): 
    The date on which the meeting took place.
* **Location** (Location, text, required: false): 
    The physical address or location of the meeting.
* **Attendees** (Attendees, select_multiple, required: false): 
    List all the people who attended the meeting.
* **Meeting Objectives** (Meeting Objectives, text, required: false): 
    Briefly describe the meeting's objectives and goals.
* **Discussion** (Discussion, text, required: false): 
    Summarize the main points discussed during the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the information captured is reliable and useful.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for guidance.
