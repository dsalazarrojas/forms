# General Meeting Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user opinions on meeting agenda items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each agenda item.
2. For each agenda item, type your opinion or decision.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your responses.

## Tips
* Please answer each agenda item truthfully and accurately.
* You can type a brief description or a simple "yes" or "no" for each agenda item.
* This form is intended to be anonymous, so you do not need to provide any personal information.
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
