# Virtual City Hall Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registered participants, employees, or authorized users to register for a virtual meeting at the city hall. It helps to gather essential information about attendees, their company, job titles, and other relevant details to facilitate the meeting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the meeting you are attending by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Which meeting are you attending?" field.
5. Provide the **Date of meeting** and **Time of meeting**.
6. If needed, enter any **Comments** about your attendance.
7. Fill in your **Company**, **Job Title**, and **Department**.
8. Enter your **Office Location**.
9. Select your role in the meeting by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Role" field.
10. Enter the **Number of participants** attending with you.
11. Enter your **Zip**, **City**, and **Country**.
12. If necessary, fill in your **Fax** and **Website**.
13. Enter your **Email 2** and **Phone 2** (if applicable).
14. Select which of the provided options apply to you in the "Which of the following apply?" field.
15. Enter any additional **Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Which meeting are you attending?**: Select "Yes" if you are attending the meeting, "No" otherwise.
* **Date of meeting**: Enter the date of the meeting, in a standard date format (e.g., month/day/year).
* **Time of meeting**: Enter the time of the meeting, in a standard 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00 or 9:00 AM/PM).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your attendance.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Office Location**: Enter your office location (e.g., building, floor, room).
* **Role**: Select "Yes" if you have a specific role in the meeting, "No" otherwise.
* **Number of participants**: Enter the total number of people attending with you.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you are located.
* **Country**: Enter the name of your country.
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number (optional).
* **Website**: Enter your company website (optional).
* **Email 2**: Enter a second email address (if necessary).
* **Phone 2**: Enter an alternative phone number (if necessary).
* **Which of the following apply?**: Select all applicable options that apply to you.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you don't reuse the same information across multiple forms.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your meeting organizer for assistance.
