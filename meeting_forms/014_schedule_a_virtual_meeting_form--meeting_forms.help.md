# Schedule A Virtual Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule A Virtual Meeting Form is designed to gather information about virtual meetings, allowing you to easily organize and coordinate with colleagues or clients across different time zones.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or email address in the "Meeting Requester" field to identify yourself as the person requesting the meeting.
2. Provide a clear title for the meeting in the "Meeting Title" field.
3. Choose the start and end dates for the meeting using the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
4. Select the start and end times for the meeting using the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
5. Enter the duration of the meeting in the "Duration" field.
6. Choose the type of meeting (Virtual, In-person, Phone) in the "Meeting Type" field.
7. Indicate whether attendees will be present in the "Attendees" field.
8. Enter the location of the meeting in the "Location" field.
9. Provide a brief description of the meeting in the "Description" field.
10. Add any additional information or links to the meeting in the "Meeting Link" field.
11. Specify whether a meeting room is required in the "Meeting Room" field.
12. Choose whether a specific tool is assigned to the meeting in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Set the meeting status to "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Meeting Status" field.
14. Select the time zone in which the meeting will take place using the "Meeting Link" field.
15. Enter the time zone's offset from UTC using the "Start Time (UTC)" field.
16. Set the duration of the meeting in the specified time zone using the "Duration (UTC)" field.
17. Indicate whether attendees will be present in the specified time zone using the "Attendees (UTC)" field.
18. Enter any additional information or links to the meeting in the "Meeting Link (UTC)" field.
19. Specify whether a meeting room is required in the specified time zone using the "Meeting Room (UTC)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Requester** (`email`, required): Enter the email address of the person requesting the meeting.

- **Start Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the start date of the meeting.
- **Start Time** (`time`, optional): Select the start time of the meeting.
- **End Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the end date of the meeting.
- **End Time** (`time`, optional): Select the end time of the meeting.
- **Duration** (`number`, optional): Enter the duration of the meeting.
- **Meeting Type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of meeting (Virtual, In-person, Phone).
- **Attendees** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether attendees will be present.
- **Location** (`text`, optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
- **Description** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the meeting.
- **Meeting Link** (`text`, optional): Add any additional information or links to the meeting.
- **Meeting Room** (`select_one`, optional): Specify whether a meeting room is required.
- **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, optional): Choose whether a specific tool is assigned to the meeting.
- **Meeting Status** (`select_one`, optional): Set the meeting status to "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Created At** (`date`, required): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
- **Updated At** (`date`, required): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
- **Submitted At** (`date`, required): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
- **Scheduled At** (`date`, required): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
- **Start Time (UTC)** (`time`, optional): Select the start time of the meeting in UTC time zone.
- **End Time (UTC)** (`time`, optional): Select the end time of the meeting in UTC time zone.
- **Duration (UTC)** (`number`, optional): Enter the duration of the meeting in UTC time zone.
