# Community Sports Project Funding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Sports Project Funding Application form is designed to collect information about your community sports project, its goals, budget, and sustainability. This form helps us understand your project's details, ensuring that we can make informed decisions about funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section with your name, title, organization, and email address.
2. Provide information about your project, including its name, description, goals, and timeline.
3. Specify the number of participants, target audience, and expected impact on the community.
4. Outline your project's budget, including the total budget, amount requested, and how funds will be used.
5. Indicate other funding sources and any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Your last name.
* **Title or Position** (`id: 4`): Your title or position within your organization.
* **Organization Name** (`id: 5`): The name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`id: 6`): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Youth Sports League, Community Sports Club, School Team, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`id: 7`): Your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 8`): Your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Street Address** (`id: 9`): Your physical address.
* **City** (`id: 10`): The city where your organization is located.
* **State** (`id: 11`): The state where your organization is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`id: 12`): The zip code of your organization's address.
* **Project Information** (`id: 13`): Briefly describe your project's details.
* **Project Name** (`id: 14`): Enter your project's name.
* **Project Description** (`id: 15`): Describe your project in detail, explaining its goals and objectives.
* **Sport or Activity** (`id: 16`): Select the sport or activity related to your project (e.g., basketball, soccer, etc.).
* **Project Goals** (`id: 17`): Describe the goals you aim to achieve with your project.
* **Target Audience** (`id: 18`): Specify the audience that will benefit from your project.
* **Expected Number of Participants** (`id: 19`): Enter the number of people expected to participate in your project.
* **Age Groups Served** (`id: 20`): Select the age groups that your project will serve (e.g., Under 6, 6-10, 11-14, etc.).
* **Project Timeline** (`id: 21`): Briefly describe your project's timeline, including start and end dates.
* **Project Start Date** (`id: 22`): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`id: 23`): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Duration** (`id: 24`): Select the duration of your project (e.g., one-time event, less than 1 month, etc.).
* **Total Project Budget** (`id: 26`): Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Amount Requested** (`id: 27`): Enter the amount you are requesting for funding.
* **Budget Breakdown** (`id: 28`): Describe how you plan to use the funds.
* **Other Funding Sources** (`id: 29`): Indicate if you have other funding sources (e.g., organization funds, participant fees, etc.).
* **Amount from Other Sources** (`id: 30`): Enter the amount from other sources.
* **Major Expense Categories** (`id: 31`): Select the major expense categories related to your project (e.g., equipment, facilities, uniforms, etc.).
* **How Will the Project Be Sustained** (`id: 32`): Describe how your project will be sustained after funding ends.
* **Expected Community Impact** (`id: 33`): Describe the impact your project will have on the community.
* **How Will You Measure Success** (`id: 34`): Outline your key performance indicators to measure success.
* **Organization History** (`id: 35`): Briefly describe how long your organization has been operating.
* **Previous Funding** (`id: 36`): Indicate if you have received funding before (e.g., yes from this source, yes from other sources, no first time).
* **Previous Funding Details** (`id: 37`): If applicable, provide details about previous funding.
* **Supporting Documents** (`id: 38`): Indicate which supporting documents you are including (e.g., budget spreadsheet, project plan, etc.).
* **Professional References** (`id: 39`): Provide names and contact information for professional references.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 40`): Include any additional comments or information about your project.
* **Certify Information Is Accurate** (`id: 41`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Applicant Signature** (`id: 42`): Sign your name with your full name.
* **Date** (`id: 43`): Enter the date.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Double-check your information for any errors before submitting the form.
* Provide supporting documents and references when necessary.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and explanations.
