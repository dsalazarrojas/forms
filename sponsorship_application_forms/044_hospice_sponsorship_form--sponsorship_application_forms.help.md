# Hospice Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from organizations interested in becoming a sponsor for our hospice. The form will help us understand your goals and needs, as well as other relevant details about your organization. This form is intended for organizations that want to support our mission by providing financial sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact details, including First Name, Goals, Address, Contact Number, Email, and Contact Number 2.
* Choose the type of sponsorship you are interested in (e.g., Community, Grant, Corporate, Individual, or Other).
* If you are interested in becoming a sponsor, enter the Sponsorship Amount.
* If you are a financial institution, enter your Bank Name, Account Number, and Branch.
* Provide the name of the contact person responsible for this sponsorship.
* Enter the email address of the contact person.
* If you have a website, enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your name in plain text.
* **Goals**: Describe your goals for this sponsorship, as this will help us understand your needs.
* **Name of organisation**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
* **Website**: If you have a website, enter it (optional).
* **Sponsorship Type**: Select the type of sponsorship you are interested in (e.g., Community, Grant, Corporate, Individual, or Other).
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount of sponsorship you are offering.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the financial institution providing the sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number of the financial institution providing the sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Branch**: Enter the branch of the financial institution providing the sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
* **Contact Number 2**: Enter the contact number of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
* **Website 2**: If you have a website, enter it (optional).

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields. This will help us process your application efficiently. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
