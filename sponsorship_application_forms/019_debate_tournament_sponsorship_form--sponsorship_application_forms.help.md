# Debate Tournament Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Debate Tournament Sponsorship Form is a tool for organizations and individuals interested in sponsoring a debate tournament. This form gathers necessary information to evaluate potential sponsorships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Event Details section with the relevant information about the debate tournament.
2. Provide the Sponsor Details, including the name and contact information of the potential sponsor.
3. Review and agree to the Sponsorship Terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): This section is for providing details about the debate tournament, such as the date, location, and topic of the event. Please fill in this section with accurate and up-to-date information about the tournament.

### Sponsor Details
* **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`, text, required): This section is for providing contact information of the potential sponsor, including their name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that this information is accurate and complete.

### Sponsorship Terms
* **Sponsorship Terms** (`sponsorship_terms`, text, required): This section is for reviewing and agreeing to the terms of the sponsorship. Please carefully read the terms before making a decision. You must submit the form by selecting "Yes" to indicate your agreement.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): To complete the form, please select "Yes" to submit your application for sponsorship. If you do not want to be a sponsor, please select "No".
