# sponsored_rider_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sponsored rider application form is designed to collect information about the rider's details, contact information, and sponsorship preferences. This form is used to facilitate the process of sponsoring a rider for a specific program.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form in order, starting from the first section.
3. Be sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Details** (`rider_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing general information about the rider. Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This section is for providing your contact information, including your email address and phone number. This will help us reach you for any follow-up questions or to confirm your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address. This will be used as your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number. This will be used as your contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select a date for the sponsorship. This will help us track the sponsorship timeline.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please select a time for the sponsorship. This will help us track the sponsorship schedule.
