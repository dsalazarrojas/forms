# event_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event sponsorship form is used to collect information from potential sponsors of an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your event title and description in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your contact information (name, email, and phone number) to enable communication.
3. Enter your business name and address to help us understand your location.
4. Fill in your business city and state for additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event you are sponsoring.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The title of your event.
- **Event Description**: Describe the event you are sponsoring.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: A brief description of your event.
- **Contact Name**: Enter your name for communication purposes.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The name of the person responsible for your sponsorship application.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false
  - Hint: A valid email address for our team to contact you.
- **Contact Phone**: Your phone number for easy contact.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: A phone number for our team to get in touch with you.
- **Business Name**: Enter your company's name for reference.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The name of your business or organization.
- **Business Address**: Enter your company's address.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The address of your business or organization.
- **Business City**: Enter the city of your business.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The city where your business is located.
- **Business State**: Enter the state of your business location.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The state where your business is located.
- **Business Zip**: Enter your business zip code for postal purposes.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Hint: The zip code of your business location.
