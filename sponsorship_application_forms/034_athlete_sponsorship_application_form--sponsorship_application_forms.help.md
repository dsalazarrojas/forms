# Athlete Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Athlete Sponsorship Application Form is designed to collect information from athletes seeking sponsorship for their sports career. The form helps sponsors review and make informed decisions on potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your basic information, including your name, date of birth, and primary sport.
2.  List your current team or organization.
3.  Specify your current level of competition.
4.  Indicate the categories of support you are requesting, such as equipment, travel, and medical expenses.
5.  Mention any previous achievements, world rankings, or notable events.
6.  List any other relevant social media handles you have.
7.  Describe any traditional media coverage you have received.
8.  Specify what you are willing to offer in return for sponsorship, such as event appearances or social media posts.
9.  Declare any current sponsorship or branding conflicts.
10. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
11. Sign the application with your electronic signature.
12. Date the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as the athlete.
*   **Date of Birth** (`athlete_dob`, `date`, required): Type your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, `text`, required): Enter the sport you compete in.
*   **Current Team or Organization** (`team_org`, `text`, required): List the team or organization you currently represent.
*   **Current Level of Competition** (`competition_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current level of competition from the options provided.
*   **Sponsorship Request Details**
    +   **Sponsorship Funding Amount Requested** (`requested_amount`, `number`, optional): Provide the amount of sponsorship funding you are requesting in USD.
    +   **Categories of Support Requested** (`support_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all categories of support you are requesting, such as equipment, travel, and medical expenses.
    +   **Requested Duration of Sponsorship** (`sponsorship_duration`, `text`, required): Specify the duration of the sponsorship you are requesting, e.g., 1 year, 2 seasons, or one-time event.
*   **Achievements and Athletic Visibility**
    +   **Major Career Highlights and Milestones** (`career_highlights`, `text`, required): List your major career achievements and milestones.
    +   **Current World or National Ranking** (`athlete_ranking`, `text`, optional): Enter your current world or national ranking.
    +   **Projected Competition Schedule for Next 12 Months** (`competition_schedule`, `text`, required): List your major competition schedule for the next 12 months.
*   **Social Media and Visibility Metrics**
    +   **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, `text`, optional): Enter your Instagram handle.
    +   **Estimated Average Reach per Post** (`instagram_reach`, `number`, optional): Approximate the number of views or impressions per post.
    +   **Other Relevant Social Media Handles** (`other_social_handles`, `text`, optional): List any other social media handles you have.
    +   **Summary of Traditional Media Coverage** (`traditional_media`, `text`, optional): Describe any traditional media coverage you have received.
*   **Proposed Sponsor Deliverables**
    +   **What will the Sponsor receive in return for their support?** (`sponsor_benefits`, `select_multiple`, required): Select what you are willing to offer in return for sponsorship, such as event appearances or social media posts.
*   **Contact and Application Review**
    +   **Email Address** (`athlete_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
    +   **Phone Number** (`athlete_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
    +   **Applicant Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as the applicant.
    +   **Date of Application** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Date the application.

Note: This guide assumes the form is filled out in an online environment, and does not include any specific instructions for offline use. Always follow the guidelines and regulations of the sponsoring organization and local laws when filling out this form.
