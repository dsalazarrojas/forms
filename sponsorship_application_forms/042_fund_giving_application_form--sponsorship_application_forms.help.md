# fund-giving-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fund-giving-application-form is a tool to help non-profit organizations apply for funding in a systematic and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your non-profit organization's information, including its name and address.
2. Provide a brief description of the purpose of the funding.
3. Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
4. If applicable, provide the contact information and email of the funder.
5. If applicable, provide the non-profit tax ID, registration number, and license details.
6. Specify the start and end dates of the funding period.
7. Enter the funding amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Profit Organization** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name and description of your non-profit organization.
* **Name Of Organization** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your non-profit organization.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your non-profit organization's physical address.
* **Purpose Of Funding** (`4`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the funding you are seeking.
* **Amount Requested** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **Funders Contact Info** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you are applying on behalf of a funder, provide their contact information.
* **Funder Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): If you are applying on behalf of a funder, enter their email address.
* **Funders Name** (`8`, `text`, optional): If you are applying on behalf of a funder, enter their name.
* **Non Profit Tax Id** (`9`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your non-profit organization's tax ID.
* **Non Profit Registration Number** (`10`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your non-profit organization's registration number.
* **Non Profit License** (`11`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your non-profit organization's license details.
* **Non Profit Status** (`12`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your non-profit organization's status.
* **Date Funding Start** (`13`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the funding period.
* **Date Funding End** (`14`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the funding period.
* **Funding Amount** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the funding amount (optional, but recommended).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application.
* If you are having trouble with any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* This form is intended for internal use by authorized personnel, please do not share with unauthorized individuals.
