# Therapy Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors who are interested in providing support to therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us for help.
3. Once you submit the form, we will review it and get back to you regarding your sponsorship request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name** (Sponsor Name, text, required): Please provide your name as the sponsor.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Please specify your organization or company name.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your business or home address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Please provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Services Needed** (Services Needed, select_multiple, required): Please select the specific services you are interested in sponsoring from the list below:
	+ Services 1
	+ Services 2
	+ Services 3
	+ Services 4
	+ Services 5
	+ Services 6
	+ Services 7
	+ Services 8
* **Services Description** (Services Description, text, required): Briefly describe the specific services you are interested in sponsoring.
Note: You can select multiple services if you are interested in multiple areas of therapy support. Please ensure that your selection is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
