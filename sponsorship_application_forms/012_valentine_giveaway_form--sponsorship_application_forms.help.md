# Valentine Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to participate in a Valentine's themed contest or event and have not already applied.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have a valid email address and a working phone number, as these will be used for communication with the organizers of the contest or event.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
3.  Double-check your answers for consistency and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address. This is the primary means of communication for contest organizers and winners will be notified via this address.
*   **Contact Phone**: If you have a working phone number, please enter it. This is an optional field but can be used for additional contact if needed.
*   **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Time of Birth**: Please enter your time of birth in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 AM).
*   **Winner Announcement Date**: This field is not required for participants, but if you would like to be notified when the winners are announced, please enter the date when you would like to receive the announcement. 

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and consistency.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the contest organizers for clarification.
- If you've already applied to this or similar contests with the same organizers, do not fill out this form again.
