# Individual Sponsorship Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Individual Sponsorship Request form is used to collect information from sponsors who are interested in supporting a project or cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the Sponsor Name field.
2. Enter the date and time of the proposal in the Proposal Date and Proposal Time fields. 
3. Enter your organization's name, if applicable, in the Organization Name field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the Additional Comments field.
7. Enter your postal address in the Postal Address field.
8. Enter your zip/postal code in the Zip/Postal Code field.
9. Enter your city in the City field.
10. Enter your state in the State field.
11. Enter your country in the Country field.
12. Select any proposal files that you would like to attach to your request.
13. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name as the sponsor or representative of the organization (if applicable).
* **Proposal Date**: Enter the date you would like to propose your support.
* **Proposal Time**: Enter the time of your proposal.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name, if you are representing an organization (optional).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your proposal.
* **Postal Address**: Enter your mailing address.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **City**: Enter the city where you are based.
* **State**: Enter the state where you are based.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you are based.
* **Proposal Files**: Select any proposal files that you would like to attach to your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you attach all required proposal files to support your request.
* Please be accurate and clear in your entry of contact information (email, phone, address).
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

Note: The form fields and options are subject to change based on the project and organization needs.
