# Internship Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Sponsor Application Form is a tool used to gather information from internship sponsors to support internship programs. This form helps our team assess sponsor interest and eligibility for sponsoring an intern. Please ensure you are the correct sponsor or have permission to submit on behalf of the sponsor before completing this form. If you have already submitted this form for the same sponsor, you may not need to complete this form again. If you are not sure or have questions, please reach out to your internal team for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sponsor's name in the "Sponsor Name" field.
2. Add the sponsor's address in the "Sponsor Address" field.
3. Enter the sponsor's phone number in the "Sponsor Phone" field.
4. Enter the sponsor's email address in the "Sponsor Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: This field is required for identifying the sponsor and is used to ensure we are aware of who is representing the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the company or individual sponsoring the intern. This is the name we will use to communicate with the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Address**: Enter the physical address of the sponsor's organization or location. This will be used to send any necessary documents or information to the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the sponsor's phone number for easy communication. Please format the number in the standard international format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address where we can reach them for questions or updates. Please ensure the email is correct and up-to-date.
