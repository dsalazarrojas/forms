# Eco Friendly Supplier Recognition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers that wish to be recognized for their eco-friendly practices. Please fill in this form with accurate and up-to-date information to demonstrate your company's commitment to sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide detailed and truthful information for each field.
3. If you need help or clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Provide a brief introduction to your company and its sustainability efforts.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your company's contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Company Information**: Enter your company's name, URL, and address.
* **Sustainability Initiatives**: Describe your company's sustainability initiatives and practices.
* **Certifications**: List any relevant certifications earned by your company.
* **Awards and Recognition**: List any awards and recognition received for your sustainability efforts.
* **Additional Info 1** and **Additional Info 2**: Provide any additional information that demonstrates your company's commitment to sustainability.
* **Submission**: Please proofread your form before submitting.

Note that some fields may have specific requirements or guidelines. For example, the "Company Name" field should be the exact name of your company as it appears on your business card or website. The "Company URL" field should be the URL of your company's website. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date to ensure a successful evaluation of your eco-friendly practices.
