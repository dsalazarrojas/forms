# Artist Promotion Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from artists and creative individuals to evaluate their eligibility for sponsorship. It collects details about the artist's background, project requirements, and expected outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your professional name or project title in the "Artist or Project Name" field (section 1).
2.  Provide your primary contact email address in the "Email Address" field (section 3).
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (section 4).
4.  Enter the URL of your website or portfolio in the "Website or Portfolio URL" field (section 5), if applicable.
5.  Select your primary artistic discipline from the list in the "Artistic Discipline" field (section 7).
6.  Write a brief professional bio in the "Artist Biography" field (section 8).
7.  Enter the number of years you have been active in your field in the "Years Active in This Field" field (section 9).
8.  Describe the specific project you are applying for in the "Project Name" field (section 11).
9.  Provide a detailed overview of the project in the "Project Description" field (section 12).
10.  Specify what you hope to achieve through the project in the "Promotion or Project Goals" field (section 13).
11.  Describe your target audience for the project in the "Target Audience" field (section 14).
12.  Specify the amount of funding you are requesting in the "Sponsorship Amount Requested (GBP)" field (section 16).
13.  Provide a detailed breakdown of how the funds will be used in the "How Will Funds Be Used?" field (section 17).
14.  Specify the deadline for the project in the "Deadline or Timeline" field (section 18).
15.  Estimate the number of people you expect to impact through your project in the "Expected Reach or Audience Size" field (section 20).
16.  Specify what outcomes or deliverables you expect from the project in the "Expected Outcomes or Deliverables" field (section 21).
17.  Describe how sponsors will be recognized in the "Sponsor Exposure and Recognition" field (section 22).
18.  Specify what supporting materials you are attaching, if any, in the "Supporting Materials Attached" field (section 24).
19.  Finally, confirm that your application is complete and accurate in the "I Confirm This Application is Complete and Accurate" field (section 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Project Name** (Section 1): Enter your professional name or project title.
* **Email Address** (Section 3): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Section 4): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (Section 5): The URL of your website or portfolio, if applicable.
* **Artistic Discipline** (Section 7): Select your primary artistic discipline, which is the area of your artistic work.
* **Artist Biography** (Section 8): Write a brief professional bio, approximately 100-150 words in length.
* **Years Active in This Field** (Section 9): Enter the number of years you have been active in your field.
* **Project Name** (Section 11): Enter the specific project you are applying for.
* **Project Description** (Section 12): Provide a detailed overview of the project.
* **Promotion or Project Goals** (Section 13): Specify what you hope to achieve through the project.
* **Target Audience** (Section 14): Describe your target audience for the project.
* **Sponsorship Amount Requested (GBP)** (Section 16): Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **How Will Funds Be Used?** (Section 17): Provide a detailed breakdown of how the funds will be used.
* **Deadline or Timeline** (Section 18): Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Expected Reach or Audience Size** (Section 20): Estimate the number of people you expect to impact through your project.
* **Expected Outcomes or Deliverables** (Section 21): Specify what outcomes or deliverables you expect from the project.
* **Sponsor Exposure and Recognition** (Section 22): Describe how sponsors will be recognized.
* **Supporting Materials Attached** (Section 24): Specify what supporting materials you are attaching, if any.
* **I Confirm This Application is Complete and Accurate** (Section 25): Confirm that your application is complete and accurate.

## Tips
- Ensure that your application is thorough and accurate.
- Double-check your email and phone number for errors.
- Attach any relevant supporting materials for review.
- Clarify any doubts or questions you may have with the sponsor organization.
