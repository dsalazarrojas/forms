# Nail Salon Event Sponsorship Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for organizations or individuals interested in becoming a sponsor for a nail salon event. It collects essential information about the event, the sponsor's details, and the sponsorship request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to complete all required fields. If you need help with any of the fields or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
2. Start by providing Event Details, including the Event Date, Event Time, Event Location, Event Name, and a brief Event Description.
3. Select the Sponsor Details that best fit your organization or individual's profile.
4. Specify the type of Sponsorship you are requesting (e.g., monetary support, in-kind contribution, etc.).
5. Enter a detailed request for sponsorship, including any specific needs or requirements.
6. Choose the terms of agreement that apply to your sponsorship.
7. Specify the sponsorship status (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
8. Enter any sponsor notes or comments.
9. If applicable, provide the Event Manager's and Sponsor Manager's contact information.
10. Enter the Sponsor Email and Sponsor Phone number.
11. Enter the Sponsor's Address.
12. Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
13. Finally, review your submission carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**
  * **Event Date**: The date of the event.
  * **Event Time**: The start and end times of the event.
  * **Event Location**: The venue or location of the event.
  * **Event Name**: A brief name for the event.
  * **Event Description**: A short description of the event.
* **Sponsor Details**
  * **Sponsor Details**: Choose one of the options provided.
* **Sponsorship Type**: The type of sponsorship you are requesting (e.g., monetary support, in-kind contribution, etc.).
* **Sponsorship Request**: A detailed request for sponsorship, including any specific needs or requirements.
* **Agreement**: Choose the terms of agreement that apply to your sponsorship.
* **Event Requirements**: Any specific requirements for the event.
* **Sponsor Requirement**: Any specific requirements for the sponsor.
* **Terms Of Agreement**: Review and agree to the terms of agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: The date the agreement was made.
* **Sponsorship Status**: The status of the sponsorship (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
* **Sponsor Note**: Any sponsor notes or comments.
* **Event Manager**: If applicable, enter the Event Manager's contact information.
* **Sponsor Manager**: If applicable, enter the Sponsor Manager's contact information.
* **Sponsor Email**: The sponsor's email address.
* **Sponsor Phone**: The sponsor's phone number.
* **Sponsor Address**: The sponsor's address.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions URL**: If applicable, enter the URL of the terms and conditions.
* **Terms of Service**: Review and agree to the terms of service.
* **Submission**: The time of submission of the sponsorship application.
