# Gaming Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Sponsorship Application Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals or groups interested in obtaining sponsorship for gaming-related activities from our organization. This form helps us to assess your eligibility and understand your gaming background, objectives, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your personal data, including your email address and phone number.
2. Describe your gaming experience and objectives, explaining why you are interested in our sponsorship.
3. Provide details about your gaming activities, including game name, URL, and any relevant information.
4. If you have social media presence, provide your social media links.
5. Confirm that you have read and accepted our terms of service and user agreement.
6. Select your gamer tag(s) that you wish to represent.
7. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Data**: This field is used to collect your personal contact information, including email address and phone number.
* **Objectives**: Please describe why you are applying for sponsorship, including your goals and motivations.
* **Game Details**: Provide details about the game(s) you are interested in, including game name, URL, and any other relevant information.
* **Gaming Experience**: Describe your gaming experience, including your skill level, game type, and any notable achievements.
* **Social Media**: If you have social media presence, provide links to your social media profiles.
* **Terms Accepted**: Confirm that you have read and accepted our terms of service and user agreement.
* **Consent**: Please acknowledge that you have read and understood our terms of service and user agreement.
* **User Agreement**: This field is currently empty, but please review our terms of service and user agreement.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number.
* **Address**: Provide your address.
* **Game Name**: Provide the name of the game(s) you are interested in.
* **Game URL**: Provide the URL of the game(s) you are interested in.
* **Gamer Name**: Provide your name as a gamer.
* **Gamer URL**: Provide a link to your gamer profile.
* **Gamer Tag**: Provide your gamer tag(s).
* **Game ID**: Provide the ID of the game(s) you are interested in.
* **Gamer ID**: Provide the ID of your gamer profile.
* **Gamer Tag ID**: Provide the ID of your gamer tag.
* **Game Tag ID**: Provide the ID of the game tag you are interested in.
* **Terms of Service**: Please review our terms of service.
* **Agreement**: Please review our user agreement.
* **Game Name ID**: Provide the ID of the game name.
* **Gamer Tag Count**: Please select the number of gamer tags you wish to represent.
* **Gamer Tag List**: Select the gamer tag(s) you wish to represent from the list of options provided.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure that your application is considered for sponsorship.
