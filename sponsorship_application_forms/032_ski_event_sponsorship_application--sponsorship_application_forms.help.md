# Ski Event Sponsorship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Event Sponsorship Application is designed to gather relevant information for potential sponsors interested in supporting our ski events. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Event Details** of your sponsored event in the designated field.
2. Provide **Organizer Details** if applicable.
3. Enter your **Contact Information** so we can get in touch with you.
4. Select the **Event Type** of your event from the available options.
5. Enter the date and time of your event, or the date and time you expect it to take place.
6. Provide the **Event Venue**, **Event Address**, **Event City**, and **Event Postal** details of your event location.
7. Enter your **Event Phone**, **Event Email**, and **Event Notes** for further communication.
8. If applicable, provide the **Sponsor Details**, including **Sponsor Name**, **Sponsor Website**, **Sponsor Type**, **Sponsor Address**, **Sponsor City**, **Sponsor Postal**, and **Sponsor Phone**, **Sponsor Email**, and **Sponsor Notes**.
9. Finally, select the **Sponsorship Status** of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter the title and details of your event. This could be a short text that describes the event, e.g. "Alpine Skiing Competition" or "Freeride Festival".
* **Organizer Details (`organizer_details`): Enter your name, address, phone, and email if you're the organizer of the event.
* **Contact Info (`contact_info`): Enter your name, address, phone, and email so we can get in touch with you.
* **Event Type (`event_type`): Select the type of event from the options: Alpine, Nordic Skiing, Cross Country Skiing, Snowboarding, Cross Country, Downhill, Telemark, Freeride, or All.
* **Event Date (`event_date`): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time (`event_time`): Enter the time of your event, or the time when you expect it to take place.
* **Event Venue (`event_venue`): Enter the venue name of your event location.
* **Event Address (`event_address`): Enter the address of your event location.
* **Event City (`event_city`): Enter the city of your event location.
* **Event Postal (`event_postal`): Enter the postal code (zip/postal code) of your event location.
* **Event Phone (`event_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Email (`event_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Event Notes (`event_notes`): Enter any additional notes about your event.
* **Sponsor Details (`sponsor_details`): Enter a short description of the sponsor, e.g. "Skiing Federation" or "Local Ski Shop".
* **Sponsor Name (`sponsor_name`): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Website (`sponsor_website`): Enter the URL of the sponsor's website.
* **Sponsor Type (`sponsor_type`): Select the type of sponsor from the options: Individual, Organization, or Team.
* **Sponsor Address (`sponsor_address`): Enter the address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor City (`sponsor_city`): Enter the city of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Postal (`sponsor_postal`): Enter the postal code (zip/postal code) of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone (`sponsor_phone`): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Sponsor Email (`sponsor_email`): Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Sponsor Notes (`sponsor_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Status (`sponsorship_status`): Select the status of your sponsorship application: Accepted, Rejected, or Pending.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* Use clear and concise language.
* Double-check your information before submitting the application. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <your contact email>.
