# Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sponsor Application Form is used to collect information from potential sponsors about their event and application details. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your event.
2. Make sure to select the correct sponsorship type that aligns with your event.
3. Provide a brief message about your event, including any additional details or requirements.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are applying for sponsorship.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Sponsor Type** (`sponsor_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of sponsorship you are applying for, such as "Team Sponsor," "Event Sponsor," or "Non-Event Sponsor."
* **Sponsor Logo** (`sponsor_logo`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, provide a link to your company logo.
* **Sponsor Link** (`sponsor_link`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, provide a link to your company website or social media page.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of event (e.g., conference, festival, workshop).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start and end time of the event (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Message 1** (`sponsor_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief message about your event and why you are interested in sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Message 2** (`sponsor_message2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details or requirements about your event (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Message 3** (`sponsor_message3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any further information about your event or sponsorship application (if applicable).
