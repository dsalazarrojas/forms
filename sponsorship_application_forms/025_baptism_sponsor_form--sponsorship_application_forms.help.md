# Baptism Sponsor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who want to sponsor a candidate for the sacrament of Baptism. The sponsor is responsible for guiding the candidate in their faith and supporting them in their spiritual journey.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the application date at the top of the form.
- Enter your first name in the first name field.
- Enter your last name in the last name field.
- Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field.
- Enter your email address in the email field.
- Enter your phone number in the phone number field.
- Enter your street address in the street address field.
- Enter your city of residence in the city field.
- Enter your state or province in the state or province field.
- Enter your ZIP or postal code in the ZIP or postal code field.
- Enter the name of the candidate you are sponsoring in the name of Baptism candidate field.
- Select the relationship you have with the candidate in the relationship field.
- Select your current faith status in the faith status field.
- Enter your parish or church membership in the parish or church membership field.
- Select how active you are in your faith community in the active participation field.
- Select any sacraments you have completed in the sacraments completed field (optional).
- Check the moral character box if you attest to good moral character.
- Confirm that you understand the sponsor responsibilities in the understand sponsor responsibilities field.
- Write a brief statement of your commitment to the sponsor role in the statement of commitment field.
- If you have an alternate sponsor, enter their name in the alternate sponsor name field.
- Confirm that you agree to the sponsor responsibilities and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Date** (Application Date, required): Date of sponsor application.
- **Sponsor First Name** (Sponsor First Name, required): Your first name.
- **Sponsor Last Name** (Sponsor Last Name, required): Your last name.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, required): Your date of birth.
- **Email Address** (Email Address, required): Your email contact.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, required): Your contact phone.
- **Street Address** (Street Address, required): Your residential address.
- **City** (City, required): City of residence.
- **State or Province** (State or Province, required): State or province.
- **ZIP or Postal Code** (ZIP or Postal Code, required): Postal code.
- **Name of Baptism Candidate** (Name of Baptism Candidate, required): Who are you sponsoring?
- **Relationship to Candidate** (Relationship to Candidate, required): How are you related?
- **Current Faith Status** (Current Faith Status, required): Your religious affiliation.
- **Parish or Church Membership** (Parish or Church Membership, required): Your current parish affiliation.
- **Active Church Participation** (Active Church Participation, required): How active are you in your faith community?
- **Sacraments Completed** (Sacraments Completed, optional): Select all that apply.
- **Moral Character** (Moral Character, required): Do you attest to good moral character?
- **Do You Understand Sponsor Responsibilities?** (Do You Understand Sponsor Responsibilities?, required): Confirm that you understand the sponsor responsibilities.
- **Statement of Commitment** (Statement of Commitment, optional): Brief statement of your commitment to sponsor role.
- **Is There an Alternate Sponsor?** (Is There an Alternate Sponsor?, optional): If you cannot fulfill the role.
- **Alternate Sponsor Name** (Alternate Sponsor Name, optional): Name of backup sponsor.
- **I Agree to the Sponsor Responsibilities and Requirements** (I Agree to the Sponsor Responsibilities and Requirements, required): Confirm that you agree to the sponsor responsibilities and requirements.
