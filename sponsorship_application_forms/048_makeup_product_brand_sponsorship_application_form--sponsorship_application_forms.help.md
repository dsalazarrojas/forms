# Makeup Product Brand Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Product Brand Sponsorship Application Form is designed to collect detailed information from makeup product brands interested in sponsorship opportunities with our organization. This form helps us evaluate potential partners and tailor our sponsorship offers to their specific needs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to start the application process.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your brand and product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Base Information** (`base_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief introduction to your brand, including its name and a short description.
* **Product Details (Page 1)**:
  * **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required: false): In this field, please enter a detailed description of your product, including its key features, benefits, and target audience.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)**:
  * **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Here, provide us with your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any attachments or supporting materials to showcase your brand and product, please attach them separately.
* Our team will review your application and respond with further instructions or questions.
