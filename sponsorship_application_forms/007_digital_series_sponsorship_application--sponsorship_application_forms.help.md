# digital_series_sponsorship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital series sponsorship application form is a crucial step in the process of securing a sponsorship for your digital series. The information gathered from this form will be reviewed and evaluated by our team to determine whether your series meets the necessary criteria for sponsorship. Please answer each question thoroughly and accurately to increase your chances of being selected.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please make sure that you have read and understood the sponsorship eligibility criteria before proceeding.
- Review each section carefully to ensure that you provide accurate and complete answers.
- You can select multiple options for the event details field, but be sure to only choose options that apply to your series.
- Make sure to provide all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk symbol (*).
- If you have any attachments or materials that support your application, you can upload them using the file upload field.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (id: 1, text, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction to your digital series, including a summary of its concept, genre, and target audience.
* **Event Details** (id: 2, select_multiple, required: false): This section allows you to specify the type of events that this digital series will cover. Select all options that apply to your series.
*  - **Yes**: The event will feature a physical presence.
  - **No**: The event will not feature a physical presence.
* **Sponsorships** (id: 3, select_one, required: false): If your series requires sponsorships, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Media Assets** (id: 4, text, required: false): Please provide details about the media assets you will use in your series, such as images, videos, or other visual materials.
* **Partner Details** (id: 5, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information about your partners or collaborators that you think would be relevant to your application.
* **File Upload** (id: 6, text, required: false): If you have any supporting materials that you'd like to attach to your application, such as a press kit or a series description document, you can upload them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your answers carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your patience and understanding as we review your application.
- We'll get back to you with a decision as soon as possible.
