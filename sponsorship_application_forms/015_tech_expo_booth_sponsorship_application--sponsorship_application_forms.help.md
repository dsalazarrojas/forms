# Tech Expo Booth Sponsorship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for companies to submit a sponsorship application for Tech Expo. It's meant to collect information about the company and their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the General Information page with your company's basic details.
2. Choose the package that suits your company's needs.
3. Upload your company's logo image and text.
4. Provide payment details.
5. Enter your company's email, phone number, and other required details.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Preview and edit your application as needed.
8. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, required): This page is where you can provide basic information about your company. Please fill out your company's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Choose A Package** (`Choose A Package`, select_one, required): This is where you can choose the package that suits your company's needs. Please select one of the available options: Basic, Premium, or Platinum.
* **Logo Image** (`Logo Image`, text, required): This is where you can upload your company's logo image.
* **Logo Text** (`Logo Text`, text, required): This is where you can enter your company's name.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required): This is where you can provide payment details for your sponsorship.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): This is where you can choose the payment method: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cheque.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This is where you can enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): This is where you can enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): This is where you can enter your company's name.
* **Company Representative** (`Company Representative`, text, required): This is where you can enter your company representative's name.
* **Contact Address** (`Contact Address`, text, required): This is where you can enter your company's contact address.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, required): This is where you can provide any additional information about your company.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, select_multiple, required): This is where you can indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, note, required): This is where you can read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submission** (`Submission`, text, required): This is where you can preview and submit your application.
* **Back** (`Back`, text, required): This is where you can go back to the previous page.
* **Next** (`Next`, text, required): This is where you can proceed to the next page.
* **Save Draft** (`Save Draft`, text, required): This is where you can save your draft application.
* **Save** (`Save`, text, required): This is where you can save your completed application.
* **Clear Draft** (`Clear Draft`, text, required): This is where you can clear your draft application.
* **Delete Draft** (`Delete Draft`, text, required): This is where you can delete your draft application.
* **Edit Draft** (`Edit Draft`, text, required): This is where you can edit your draft application.
* **Preview** (`Preview`, text, required): This is where you can preview your application.
* **Save Changes** (`Save Changes`, text, required): This is where you can save changes to your application.
* **Submit Application** (`Submit Application`, text, required): This is where you can submit your completed application.

Please note that all fields are required except for "General Information" which is not required but it is a good practice to fill it out.
