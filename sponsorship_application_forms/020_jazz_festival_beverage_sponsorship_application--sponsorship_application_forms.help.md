# Jazz Festival Beverage Sponsorship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a sponsorship request for your beverage company to be featured at the Jazz Festival. Please provide your contact information, details about your agreement, and a brief description of your beverage product. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
2. Provide a brief description of your agreement with the festival in the "Agreement Info" page.
3. Enter details about your beverage product in the "Beverage Info" page.
4. Indicate your current agreement status with the festival in the "Agreement Status" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): This page is where you should enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This is how the festival organizers will get in touch with you to discuss your sponsorship request.

### Agreement Info
* **Agreement Info** (`Agreement Info`, `text`, required/optional): This page is where you provide a brief description of your agreement with the festival. Please include details about the duration, scope, and any specific conditions of your agreement. This information will help the festival organizers understand your partnership and review your sponsorship request.

### Beverage Info
* **Beverage Info** (`Beverage Info`, `text`, required/optional): In this page, provide a brief description of your beverage product. Please include details about the type of beverage (e.g. beer, wine, spirits, soda), its origin, and any other relevant characteristics. This information will help the festival organizers understand your product and review your sponsorship request.

### Agreement Status
* **Agreement Status** (`Agreement Status`, `text`, required/optional): On this page, indicate your current agreement status with the festival. Please select one of the following options: "Applied", "Approved", "Rejected", or "Awaiting Response". This will give the festival organizers an update on the status of your sponsorship request.
