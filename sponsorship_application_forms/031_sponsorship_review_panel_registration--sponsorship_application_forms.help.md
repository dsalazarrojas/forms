# Sponsorship Review Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Review Panel Registration form is designed to collect information from potential panelists to help determine their fit and suitability for participating in a sponsorship review panel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the options that best match your availability, qualifications, and preferences.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing information.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number. This is optional but helps us contact you if needed.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, not required): Choose when you are available to serve on the panel (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekends, Other).
* **Expertise** (`expertise`, select_one, not required): Choose the area where you have expertise (Art, Cultural, Financial, Health, Legal, Medical, Technology, Other).
* **Qualifications** (`qualification`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the qualifications you have (Art, Cultural, Financial, Health, Legal, Medical, Technology, Other).
* **Panelist** (`panelist`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of organization you represent (Nonprofit, Foundation, Organization).
* **Apply Date** (`apply_date`, date, not required): Choose the date you are applying to join the panel (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Apply Time** (`apply_time`, time, not required): Choose the time you are available to serve on the panel (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekends, Other).
* **Apply Note** (`apply_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Panelist Availability 2** (`panelist_availability_2`, select_one, not required): Choose when you are available to serve on the panel 2 (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekends, Other).
* **Review Period 2** (`review_period_2`, select_one, not required): Choose the period you are willing to review (Immediate, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year, 2 years, Other).
* **Apply Frequency 2** (`apply_frequency_2`, select_one, not required): Choose how often you are willing to apply (As needed, Bi-monthly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually).
* **Review Frequency 2** (`review_frequency_2`, select_one, not required): Choose how often you are willing to review (As needed, Bi-monthly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually).
* **Confirm Apply** (`confirm_apply`, select_one, not required): Confirm that you are applying to join the panel (Yes, No).
* **Confirm Review** (`confirm_review`, select_one, not required): Confirm that you are willing to review (Yes, No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Apply Date & Time** (`apply_date_time`, date, not required): Enter the date and time you are applying to join the panel (format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM).
* **Apply Note 2** (`apply_note_2`, note, not required): Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Panelist Availability 2** (`panelist_availability_2`, select_one, not required): (same as **Panelist Availability**).
* **Review Period 2** (`review_period_2`, select_one, not required): (same as **Review Period**).
* **Apply Frequency 2** (`apply_frequency_2`, select_one, not required): (same as **Apply Frequency**).
* **Review Frequency 2** (`review_frequency_2`, select_one, not required): (same as **Review Frequency**).
* **Confirm Review 2** (`confirm_review_2`, select_one, not required): (same as **Confirm Review**).

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need help, feel free to contact us.
* You can only submit the form once per individual. If you are already registered as a panelist, do not complete this form again.
