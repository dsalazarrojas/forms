# Hair Event Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event organizers to provide information about their upcoming events to potential sponsors, aiming to secure sponsorship for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out this form for a single event only. If you have multiple events, please submit separate forms for each event.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays or rejection of your sponsorship request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Information** (salon_info): Enter a brief description of your salon or organization.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address. This will help us get in touch with you to discuss your sponsorship request.
* **Event Information** (event_info): Enter a brief description of your event, including its purpose and target audience.
* **Sponsorship Information** (sponsorship_info): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting, including the amount and type of sponsorship you seek.
* **Event Sponsorship** (event_sponsorship): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Event Date** (event_date): Enter the date of your event in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time** (event_time): Enter the start time of your event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Duration** (event_duration): Enter the duration of your event in hours.
* **Event Location** (event_location): Enter the location of your event.
* **Sponsorship Request** (sponsorship_request): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting, including any specific requests or requirements.
* **Contact First Name** (contact_first_name): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (contact_last_name): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Event Sponsorship Request Amount** (event_sponsorship_request_amount): Enter the amount you are requesting for sponsorship.
* **Event Sponsorship Request Type** (event_sponsorship_request_type): Select the type of sponsorship you are requesting (Monetary or Non-Monetary).
* **Event Sponsorship Request Description** (event_sponsorship_request_description): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting.
* **Event Sponsorship Request Frequency** (event_sponsorship_request_frequency): Enter the frequency of the sponsorship request.
* **Event Sponsorship Request** (event_sponsorship_request): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Salon Sponsorship Request** (salon_sponsorship_request): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting for your salon.
* **Brand Sponsorship Request** (brand_sponsorship_request): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting for your brand.
* **Show Class Sponsorship Request** (show_class_sponsorship_request): Enter a brief description of the sponsorship you are requesting for your show class.
* **Form Submit** (form_submit): Click this button to submit your form.

## Tips
Please make sure to review your form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. This will help us process your sponsorship request efficiently and effectively.
