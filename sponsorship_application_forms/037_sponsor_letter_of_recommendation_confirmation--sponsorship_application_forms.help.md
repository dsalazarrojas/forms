# Sponsor Letter Of Recommendation Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Letter Of Recommendation Confirmation form is used to verify the sponsorship of an individual or organization. It helps to confirm the identity and contact information of the sponsor, and their willingness to sponsor the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct sponsor from the "Confirm Sponsor" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the sponsor's name in the "Sponsor Name" field.
3. If the sponsor has multiple names, select all applicable names from the "Sponsor Name 2" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the sponsor's email address in the "Sponsor Email" field.
5. Enter the sponsor's phone number in the "Sponsor Phone" field.
6. Confirm the sponsor's name and email address in the "Confirm Sponsor Name" and "Confirm Sponsor Email" fields.
7. Check the "Confirm Sponsor" checkbox to indicate whether the sponsor has agreed to sponsor the applicant.
8. Enter any additional comments about the sponsorship in the "Confirm Sponsor Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Confirm Sponsor**: A dropdown menu to select whether the sponsor has agreed to sponsor the applicant. Options: "True" or "False".
* **Sponsor Name**: The name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Name 2**: A dropdown menu to select multiple names of the sponsor (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Email**: The email address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone**: The phone number of the sponsor.
* **Confirm Sponsor Name**: A field to confirm the sponsor's name.
* **Confirm Sponsor Email**: A field to confirm the sponsor's email address.
* **Confirm Sponsor Phone**: A field to confirm the sponsor's phone number.
* **Confirm Sponsor**: A checkbox to indicate whether the sponsor has agreed to sponsor the applicant.
* **Confirm Sponsor Comments**: A text field for any additional comments about the sponsorship.
