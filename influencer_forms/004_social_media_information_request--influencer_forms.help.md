# Social Media Information Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is to assist you in completing the "Social Media Information Request" form. The form is designed to collect information from you about your social media presence for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

*   Please answer all the required fields. Required fields are marked as "required" in the form.
*   If you have any social media profiles on the platforms listed, please provide the corresponding information.
*   Please enter your information accurately and completely.
*   You can fill out the form in multiple pages, each corresponding to a specific social media platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Email Address
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your social media profile.

### Phone Number
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number associated with your social media profile.

### LinkedIn Profile
*   **LinkedIn Profile**: If you have a LinkedIn profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### Twitter Profile
*   **Twitter Profile**: If you have a Twitter profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### Instagram Profile
*   **Instagram Profile**: If you have an Instagram profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### Facebook Profile
*   **Facebook Profile**: If you have a Facebook profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### YouTube Profile
*   **YouTube Profile**: If you have a YouTube profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### TikTok Profile
*   **TikTok Profile**: If you have a TikTok profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### Discord Username
*   **Discord username**: Enter your Discord username.

### Snapchat Profile
*   **Snapchat Profile**: Enter your Snapchat profile URL if you have one.

### Twitch Profile
*   **Twitch Profile**: If you have a Twitch profile, enter the URL of your profile.

### GitHub Profile
*   **GitHub Profile**: If you have a GitHub profile, enter the URL of your profile.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you only fill out the fields that are relevant to your social media presence.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
*   This form is for internal purposes only, and the information collected will not be shared or disclosed to any third party.
