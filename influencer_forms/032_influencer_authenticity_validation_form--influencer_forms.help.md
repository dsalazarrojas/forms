# Influencer Authenticity Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influencer Authenticity Validation Form is used to collect information about an influencer's audience demographics and engagement metrics to validate their authenticity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your brand name in the "Brand Name" field.
2. Select the category that best fits your audience from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the gender and age range of your audience from the "Audience Gender", "Audience Age Range", and "Audience Type" fields.
4. Enter your average number of viewers in the "Average Viewers" field.
5. Select the level of audience engagement you've achieved from the "Audience Engagement" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the type of audience you represent from the "Audience Type" field.
7. Enter the average engagement rate you've achieved in the "Average Engagement Rate" field.
8. (Optional) Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your influence in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your last engagement activity in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
