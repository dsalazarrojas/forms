# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather budget and targeting information for marketing campaigns. It is crucial to fill it out accurately to ensure that your campaigns reach the right audience and are budgeted correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked as "Required" in the field-by-field explanation section.
2.  Ensure that you fill out all fields accurately and correctly according to the provided instructions.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Name** (`brand_info`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of your brand. This is a crucial field as it will be used to identify your brand throughout the campaign.
* **Brand Info 2** (`brand_info_2`, `text`, Optional): This field is meant to provide additional information about your brand. If you have any specific details about your brand, enter them here.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, Optional): Enter the budget for your campaign. This can be a one-time payment or a recurring amount.
* **Currency** (`budget_currency`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the currency for your budget. This will help ensure that your budget is accurate and applicable to your campaign.
* **Duration** (`budget_duration`, `date`, Optional): Enter the duration of your campaign. This will help in planning and scheduling your campaign activities.
* **Location** (`budget_location`, `text`, Optional): Enter the location where your campaign will be targeting. This can be a specific city, region, or country.
* **Rate Card** (`budget_rate_card`, `number`, Optional): Enter the rate card for your campaign. This is the price you are willing to pay per unit of your product or service.
* **Budget Rate Card 2** (`budget_rate_card_2`, `number`, Optional): This field is similar to the Rate Card field above. Enter the rate card for your campaign, if applicable.
* **Frequency** (`budget_frequency`, `text`, Optional): Enter the frequency of your campaign activities. This can be daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Rate Card Currency** (`budget_rate_card_currency`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the currency for your rate card. This will ensure that your rate card is accurate and applicable to your campaign.
* **Start Date** (`budget_start_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the start date for your campaign. This will help in planning and scheduling your campaign activities.
* **End Date** (`budget_end_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the end date for your campaign. This will help in evaluating the campaign's performance.
* **Budget Type** (`budget_type`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the type of budget for your campaign. This can be fixed, hourly, or daily.
* **Note** (`budget_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your budget. This can be used to provide context or clarification.
* **Budget Note 2** (`budget_note_2`, `note`, Optional): Similar to the Note field above, enter any additional notes about your budget.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, Optional): Enter your campaign goals. This can be used to track and measure the campaign's success.
* **Amplifier** (`amplifier`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional information about your amplifier. This can be used to provide context or clarification.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the target audience for your campaign. This can be demographic, interest group, or niche.
* **Target Audience Location** (`target_audience_location`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the location of your target audience. This can be country, region, or city.
* **Target Audience Gender** (`target_audience_gender`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the gender of your target audience. This can be male, female, or other.
* **Target Audience Gender 2** (`target_audience_gender_2`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Similar to the Target Audience Gender field above, select the gender of your target audience.
* **Campaign Media** (`campaign_media`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the media for your campaign. This can be social media, video, or image.
* **Rate** (`budget_rate_card_rate`, `number`, Optional): Enter the rate for your campaign. This is the price you are willing to pay per unit of your product or service.
* **Min Budget** (`budget_rate_card_min_budget`, `number`, Optional): Enter the minimum budget for your campaign. This is the lowest amount you are willing to pay per unit of your product or service.
* **Chart** (`chart`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the type of chart for your campaign. This can be bar, line, or pie.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you can fill them out according to your specific needs. Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with your campaign.
