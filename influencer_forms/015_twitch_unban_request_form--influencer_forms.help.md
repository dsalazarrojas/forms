# Twitch Unban Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who have been banned on their Twitch account and are seeking to reinstate their account. Please fill out this form if you have been banned and are looking to appeal or request removal of the ban.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the **general-information** page.
	* Enter any relevant information about your account and the reason for your ban.
2. Proceed to the **user-info** page.
	* Enter your user information, including your username and any other relevant details.
3. Go to the **request-info** page.
	* Provide a detailed explanation of why you believe your account should be unbanned.

* **General-information (Page 1)**:
	+ **(no label)** (`general-information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your situation.
* **User-info (Page 2)**:
	+ **(no label)** (`user-info`, text, optional): Enter your username.
* **Request-info (Page 3)**:
	+ **(no label)** (`request-info`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of your request.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Be patient, as the review process may take time.
* If you have already submitted this form in the past, do not resubmit.
