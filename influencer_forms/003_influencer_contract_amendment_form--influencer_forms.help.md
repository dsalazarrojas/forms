# Influencer Contract Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to make changes to an existing influencer contract. It allows you to record the details of the amendment, including the reason for the amendment, the new terms, and the current status of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Please note that some fields are required and some are optional. Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a complete and accurate record of the contract amendment.

- **Step 1:** Select the type of amendment (Amended, Canceled, or Terminated) from the dropdown menu.
- **Step 2:** Enter the contract ID, business name (or alias), and date of the original contract.
- **Step 3:** Specify the reason for the amendment in the "Reason for Amendment" field.
- **Step 4:** Enter any new terms or conditions in the "New Terms" field.
- **Step 5:** Enter your signature as the person signing the amendment, and enter the signature of the business representative.
- **Step 6:** Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
- **Step 7:** Enter any additional notes or comments from the business side.
- **Step 8:** Select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the original contract.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business associated with the contract.
* **Business Name Alias**: Enter an alias or nickname for the business (if applicable).
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date of the original contract.
* **Amendment Date**: Enter the date of the amendment.
* **Reason for Amendment**: Specify the reason for making changes to the original contract.
* **New Terms**: Enter any new terms or conditions that are being added or modified.
* **Signed By**: Enter your signature as the person signing the amendment.
* **Business Signature**: Enter the signature of the business representative.
* **Influencer Signature**: Enter the signature of the influencer.
* **Amendment Type**: Select the type of amendment (Amended, Canceled, or Terminated).
* **Agreement Amendment**: Enter any additional details or notes related to the amendment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the amendment.
* **Business Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the business side.
* **Agreement Status**: Select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate record of the contract amendment.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
