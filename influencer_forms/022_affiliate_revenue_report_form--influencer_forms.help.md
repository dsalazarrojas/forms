# Affiliate Revenue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affiliate Revenue Report Form is used to collect revenue information from affiliates who participate in the program. It provides a detailed overview of an affiliate's performance during a specified period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the reporting period for which you are submitting this report.
2. Enter your affiliate name and ID.
3. Select your preferred payment method and provide your email associated with it (if applicable).
4. Indicate your tax classification and provide your tax ID (if applicable).
5. Report any expenses and deductions you are claiming.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your performance.
7. Finally, sign to confirm the accuracy of this report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Affiliate Information
* **Affiliate Name**: Enter your full name or business name.
*   **Affiliate ID**: Enter your unique affiliate identifier.

### Reporting Information
* **Reporting Period**: Specify the time period for which you are submitting this report.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date when this report is submitted.

### Sales and Revenue
* **Gross Revenue**: Enter the total revenue generated before commissions.
* **Commission Earned**: Enter the total commission earned from affiliate sales.
* **Referral Fees**: Enter any referral fees earned (if applicable).
* **Bonuses and Incentives**: Enter any bonuses or incentives earned (if applicable).
* **Total Earnings**: This field will be automatically calculated based on your inputs.

### Sales Performance
* **Total Clicks**: Enter the number of clicks generated to the merchant site.
* **Total Leads Generated**: Enter the number of leads generated from your referrals.
* **Total Sales**: Enter the number of sales attributed to your referrals.
* **Conversion Rate (%)**: Enter the percentage of clicks that resulted in sales.
* **Average Order Value**: Enter the average value of orders from your referrals.

### Tax and Expenses Information
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
*   **Payment Email**: If applicable, enter your email associated with the payment method.
*   **Tax Classification**: Select how you would like your earnings to be reported for tax purposes (Independent Contractor, Business Entity, or Other).
*   **Tax ID**: If applicable, enter your tax identification number.
*   **Marketing Expenses**: Enter expenses related to promoting the affiliate program.
*   **Other Deductions**: Enter any other deductible expenses.
*   **Net Earnings**: This field will be automatically calculated based on your inputs.

### Additional Information
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your performance during this period.
*   **Additional Performance Notes**: Provide any additional performance notes or comments.
*   **Electronic Signature**: Sign to confirm the accuracy of this report.

### Payment Information (Conditional)
*   This section appears only if your preferred payment method is Direct Deposit or PayPal. 
*   **Payment Email**: Enter your email associated with your payment method.
