# Influencer Outreach Software Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Influencer Outreach Software Quote Request Form is designed to gather information from potential customers for a software quote request. This form helps the company understand the user's company and project requirements to provide a tailored quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required contact information fields: **User Information**, **Contact Person**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
2. Choose the relevant services you need from the **Services Needed** field.
3. Provide a brief description of your project in the **Project Details** field.
4. If applicable, choose your company's **Company Size**, **Industry**, and **Job Function**.
5. Choose your preferred contact method: **Email**, **Phone**, or **Other**.
6. Finally, add any additional **Notes** about your project, if necessary.
