# Twitch Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects feedback and rating from users about the content they watched on Twitch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of stream you are surveying:
	+ Streamer: Feedback about a specific streamer's stream.
	+ Viewers: Feedback about the content you've watched on Twitch.
	+ Followers: Feedback about a community or a particular type of content.
	+ Subscribers: Feedback about the subscription-based content on Twitch.
* Provide your feedback in the text field.
* Rate your experience by selecting a number rating (e.g., 1-5).
* Enter your contact email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **stream_info** (`stream_info`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose the type of stream you are surveying. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can provide your feedback about the content you watched on Twitch. Be as descriptive as you can about your experience.
* **rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5. This will help us understand your perception of the content.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): If you have any further questions or would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address here. This is optional, but it helps us to get back to you if we need more information.
* **Note:** Some fields are optional, but we still need your honest feedback to help us improve the platform. Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form!
