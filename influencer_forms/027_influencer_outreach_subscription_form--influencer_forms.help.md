# influencer_outreach_subscription_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect subscription information from influencers to tailor outreach efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the subscription service(s) you are subscribed to from the "subscription_service" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date of your subscription in the "subscription_date" field.
6. Enter the time of your subscription in the "subscription_time" field.
7. If applicable, add any additional notes to the "note" field.
8. Select all relevant services you are subscribed to from the "influencer_form" dropdown menu.
9. Choose a category for your subscription from the "category" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required: false): Provide a valid email address.
* **phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **subscription_service** (select_one, required: false): Select the service you are subscribed to.
* **subscription_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of your subscription.
* **subscription_time** (time, required: false): Enter the time of your subscription.
* **note** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your subscription.
* **influencer_form** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant services you are subscribed to.
* **category** (select_one, required: false): Choose a category for your subscription.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure relevant communication.
