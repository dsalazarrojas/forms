# Content Creator Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for content creation positions within our organization. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your application is properly reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that you have provided a valid email address and phone number.
3. Choose the correct options for each select field (e.g., employment type, position applied for, etc.).
4. Attach a cover letter explaining why you are the best candidate for the position.
5. Click submit to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): This is your legal first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your best contact number for us to reach you.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Your street address where we can send you mail.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Your postal code.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, select_one, required): Choose which content creator role you are applying for (e.g., Social Media Content Creator, Video Content Creator, etc.).
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): The date you can start working.
* **Employment Type Preference** (`employment_type_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your employment type preference (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, etc.).
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text, required): Expected annual salary or hourly rate.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required): Choose how many years of content creation experience you have.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, required): Link to your portfolio or work samples.
* **Social Media Profiles** (`social_media_profiles`, text, required): Links to your social media accounts.
* **Relevant Skills** (`relevant_skills`, text, required): List your content creation skills.
* **Software Proficiency** (`software_proficiency`, select_multiple, required): Check what content creation software you know (e.g., Adobe Creative Suite, etc.).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education completed (e.g., High School, Bachelor's Degree, etc.).
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): The field of study you completed your degree in.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, optional): Name of institution where you attended.
* **Most Recent Employer** (`previous_employer`, text, optional): Name of your last employer.
* **Previous Role** (`previous_role`, text, optional): Your job title at previous employer.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): Why you left your previous position.
* **Notable Achievements** (`notable_achievements`, text, required): Describe your proudest content creation achievements.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Choose if you can provide professional references.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required): Explain why you are the best candidate for the position.
* **How Did You Hear About This Position?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required): Where did you find this job posting.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date of application.
