# ugc_review_collaboration_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for creators to collaborate with brand representatives on the review process for Ungoverned Content (UGC) campaigns. The goal is to ensure a smooth and transparent collaboration between the creator and the brand, while maintaining the highest standards of quality and integrity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide campaign details in the first field.
2. Select the review deliverables that apply to your campaign.
3. Specify the review timeline.
4. Enter your contact details as the creator.
5. Enter the brand details.
6. Provide agency details (if applicable).
7. Describe the review process.
8. Specify the submission status.
9. Add any submission notes.
10. Sign off with your creator signature.
11. Sign off with the brand signature.
12. Add any review notes.
13. Enter the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details (Campaign Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the campaign, including its title, objectives, and target audience.
* **Review Deliverables (Review Deliverables)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the review deliverables that apply to your campaign, such as content creation, editing, or content management.
* **Review Timeline (Review Timeline)** (`date`, required/optional): Specify the review timeline, including the start and end dates for the review process.
* **Creator Details (Creator Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Brand Details (Brand Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the brand's name, website, and any other relevant information.
* **Agency Details (Agency Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the agency's name, contact information, and any other relevant details (if applicable).
* **Review Process (Review Process)** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the review process, including any specific requirements or guidelines for the review.
* **Submission Status (Submission Status)** (`select_one`, required): Select the current submission status, such as "Pending", "Approved", or "Denied".
* **Submission Notes (Submission Notes)** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **Creator Signature (Creator Signature)** (`text`, required/optional): Sign off with your creator's signature.
* **Brand Signature (Brand Signature)** (`text`, required/optional): Sign off with the brand's signature.
* **Review Notes (Review Notes)** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional review notes.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth collaboration process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the brand representative.
