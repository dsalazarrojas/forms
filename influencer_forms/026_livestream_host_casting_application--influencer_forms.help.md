# Livestream Host Casting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential Livestream hosts to apply for hosting duties on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section carefully and accurately. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible, especially for fields marked as required.
2. If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, please contact us for assistance.
3. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Experience** (`experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select your years of experience in the relevant field (0-2, 2-5, 5-10, or More than 10 years).
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select the category you're applying for (Lifestyle, Beauty, Fashion, Music, Food, Gaming, Sports, Travel, Technology, or Other).
* **Demo Reel 1** (`demo_reel`, `text`, required: false): Upload your first demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 2** (`demo_reel`, `text`, required: false): Upload your second demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 3** (`demo_reel`, `text`, required: false): Upload your third demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 4** (`demo_reel`, `text`, required: false): Upload your fourth demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 5** (`demo_reel`, `text`, required: false): Upload your fifth demo reel.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach your resume or bio.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your location (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Philadelphia, San Antonio, San Diego, Dallas, San Jose, Jacksonville).
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the days you're available to host (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, `time`, required: false): 
	+ Enter your availability time in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **Availability Time 24h** (`availability_time_24`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your availability time in 24-hour format (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Anytime).
* **Availability Time 12** (`availability_time_12`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your availability time in 12-hour format (AM, PM).
* **Availability Time 16** (`availability_time_16`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your availability time in 16-hour format (AM, PM).
* **Availability Time 24.2** (`availability_time_24_2`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your availability time in 24.2-hour format (AM, PM).
* **Availability Time 16.2** (`availability_16_2`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your availability time in 16.2-hour format (AM, PM).
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your availability.
* **Demo Reel 1** (`demo_reels_1`, `text`, required: false): Upload your first demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 2** (`demo_reels_2`, `text`, required: false): Upload your second demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 3** (`demo_reels_3`, `text`, required: false): Upload your third demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 4** (`demo_reels_4`, `text`, required: false): Upload your fourth demo reel.
* **Demo Reel 5** (`demo_reels_5`, `text`, required: false): Upload your fifth demo reel.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select "Submit" to send your application for review.
