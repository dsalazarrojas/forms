# Makeup Product Brand Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a mandatory step for all makeup product brands interested in applying for our sponsorship opportunities. Please complete it accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your first and last name correctly.
2. Enter your active email address for us to contact you for further information.
3. Describe your product and why you would like to be considered for sponsorship.
4. Specify your brand and company website.
5. Confirm your social media presence by checking "Yes" for the platforms you have an account on.
6. Enter your social media handles for the platforms you have confirmed.
7. Tell us why you are interested in being a sponsored brand.
8. Confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
9. Set a submission deadline for your application.
10. Confirm that you are willing to submit your application for review.
11. Add any additional comments or information you think we should know.
12. Attach any supporting materials for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): This is the active email address we can use to contact you for further information about your application.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you in case we need more information.
* **Product Name** (5, text, required): Describe your product, including its name and a brief description.
* **Product Description** (6, text, optional): Provide more details about your product, its features, and benefits.
* **Brand** (7, text, required): Enter the name of your brand.
* **Website** (8, text, optional): Enter your brand's website address.
* **Social Media** (9, select multiple, optional): Confirm which social media platforms you are present on.
	+ Select all platforms where you have an account.
* **Instagram** (10, text, optional): Enter your Instagram handle (e.g., @yourhandle).
* **Twitter** (11, text, optional): Enter your Twitter handle (e.g., @yourhandle).
* **TikTok** (12, text, optional): Enter your TikTok handle (e.g., @yourhandle).
* **YouTube** (13, text, optional): Enter your YouTube channel name or handle (e.g., @yourchannel).
* **LinkedIn** (14, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile address or handle (e.g., @yourprofile).
* **Reddit** (15, text, optional): Enter your Reddit handle (e.g., u/yourhandle).
* **Snapchat** (16, text, optional): Enter your Snapchat handle (e.g., @yourhandle).
* **Facebook** (17, text, optional): Enter your Facebook profile address or handle (e.g., @yourprofile).
* **Other** (18, text, optional): If you have other social media platforms not listed here, enter their names and handles.
* **Why Sponsor** (19, text, required): Explain why you are interested in being sponsored by us.
* **Terms and Conditions** (20, select one, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
* **Submission Deadline** (21, time, required): Set a specific date for us to receive your application.
* **Submission Agreement** (22, select one, required): Confirm that you are willing to submit your application for review.
* **Additional Comments** (23, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your application.
* **Attachment** (24, text, optional): If you have supporting materials or documents to attach, upload them here.
* **Signature** (25, text, optional): Your signature, printed or handwritten, for identification purposes.

Note: The required fields are marked with "required" in the field explanation. Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
