# which_elements_are_you_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "which_elements_are_you_quiz" form is designed to help you identify your connection to various fantasy elements. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to share your affinity with others in the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: Read the brief introduction to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Fill out each page with your chosen options for each question.
3. Select your preferred response from the provided options for each field.
4. If you don't identify with any of the given options, select "Other" as a default response.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
