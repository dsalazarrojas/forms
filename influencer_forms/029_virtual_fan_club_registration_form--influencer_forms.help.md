# Virtual Fan Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Fan Club Registration Form is designed to be used by individuals who want to join the Virtual Fan Club, a platform for fans to connect and engage with each other. This form helps us understand your preferences and provide a personalized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Please note that fields marked as "required" must be filled out in order to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select multiple, optional): Select all your favorite foods from the list.
* **How did you find us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, text, optional): Tell us how you found out about the Virtual Fan Club.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, select one, optional): Select one of your favorite colors.
* **Favorite Animal** (`favorite_animal`, select multiple, optional): Select all your favorite animals.
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, select one, optional): Select one of your favorite books.
* **How did you join the fan club** (`how_did_you_join_fan_club`, select one, optional): Tell us how you joined the Virtual Fan Club.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, select one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Favorite Food2** (`favorite_food2`, text, optional): This field is not clear in its purpose, but it seems to be another question about favorite foods.
* **Favorite Food2** (`favorite_food2`, text, optional): We will ignore any subsequent submissions, so please do not fill out this field again. 

Note: The field "Favorite Food2" is likely a duplicate field and should be removed or merged with "Favorite Food".
