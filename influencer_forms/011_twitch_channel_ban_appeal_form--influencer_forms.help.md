# Twitch Channel Ban Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Twitch Channel Ban Appeal Form is used to appeal against a ban on a Twitch channel. If your channel has been banned, please use this form to provide more information about your case and help our team review your appeal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your channel's ID, found in your channel settings.
2. Enter your channel's name.
3. Explain the reason for the appeal in the "reason" field.
4. If applicable, provide additional context or information in the "ban_message" field.
5. In the "proof" field, provide any relevant links or evidence that support your appeal.
6. In the "appeal_message" field, write a detailed message explaining why you believe the ban was unjustified.
7. You may also want to specify if your channel has been targeted by a spam filter in the "spam_filter" field.
8. If you have any additional information or links that support your appeal, you can provide them in the "proof_link" and "discord_link" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Channel ID** (channel_id): Enter the unique ID of your channel.
* **Channel Name** (channel_name): Enter the name of your channel.
* **Reason** (reason): Explain the reason for the appeal.
* **Ban Message** (ban_message): Provide any additional context or information about the ban.
* **Proof** (proof): Provide any relevant links or evidence that support your appeal.
* **Appeal Message** (appeal_message): Write a detailed message explaining why you believe the ban was unjustified.
* **Spam Filter** (spam_filter): If your channel has been targeted by a spam filter, select "Yes" to indicate this.
* **Proof Link** (proof_link): If you have any additional information or links that support your appeal, provide them here.
* **Discord Link** (discord_link): If you have any additional information or links that support your appeal, provide them here.
* **Submit** (submit): Click this button to submit your appeal.
