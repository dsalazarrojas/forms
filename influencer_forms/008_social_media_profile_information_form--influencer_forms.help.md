# social_media_profile_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect social media profile information from influencers or content creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Go through each page of the form and select the social media platforms you are present on.
*   Enter your profile username or username associated with the selected platforms.
*   Enter the URL of your profile on the selected platforms (optional).
*   Enter any additional information you want to include in your profile description.
*   Enter your email address (optional).
*   Enter your phone number (optional).
*   For platforms where required, enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
*   For platforms where required, enter your Facebook profile URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Social Media Platforms
*   **Select the social media platforms where you are present** (`social_media_platforms`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more of the following social media platforms: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitch, Twitch Stream, Discord, Twitch Chat, Patreon, and Twitch Donate.

### Platform URL
*   **Enter the URL of your profile** (`platform_url`, `text`): Enter the URL of your profile on the selected social media platforms. This field is optional.

### Profile Username
*   **Enter your profile username** (`profile_username`, `text`): Enter the username associated with your profile on the selected social media platforms.

### Profile Description
*   **Enter a description of your profile** (`profile_description`, `note`): Enter a brief description of your profile.

### Profile Email
*   **Enter your email address** (`profile_email`, `email`): Enter your email address. This field is optional.

### Profile Phone
*   **Enter your phone number** (`profile_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number. This field is optional.

### LinkedIn
*   **Enter your LinkedIn profile URL** (`profile_linkedin`, `text`): Enter the URL of your LinkedIn profile.

### Facebook
*   **Enter your Facebook profile URL** (`profile_facebook`, `text`): Enter the URL of your Facebook profile.

Note: The fields for LinkedIn and Facebook are only required for the platforms you have selected in the social media platforms page.
