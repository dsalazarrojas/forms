# Playlist Promotion Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists and music promoters to contact us about promoting their music on our playlists. We collect information about you and your music to get in touch with you and potentially feature it on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as accurately as possible. You can submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address associated with you or your artist manager.
* **Your Email** (`email_label`, text, required: false): Enter your email address associated with you or your artist manager. Please note that this field is meant to confirm your email address.
* **Email Type** (`email_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Personal**: I want to be contacted personally.
	+ **Promotional**: I want to be contacted for promotional purposes.
	+ **Both**: I want to be contacted both personally and for promotional purposes.
* **Artist/Artist Manager** (`artist_name`, text, required: false): Enter your artist name or artist manager's name.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, email, required: false): Enter the artist's email address.
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **Indie**: Indie music style.
	+ **Pop**: Pop music style.
	+ **Rock**: Rock music style.
	+ **Hip-Hop**: Hip-Hop music style.
	+ **Electronic**: Electronic music style.
	+ **Classical**: Classical music style.
	+ **Jazz**: Jazz music style.
	+ **Folk**: Folk music style.
	+ **Metal**: Metal music style.
	+ **Country**: Country music style.
	+ **R&B**: R&B music style.
	+ **Reggae**: Reggae music style.
	+ **Blues**: Blues music style.
	+ **Country-Rock**: Country-Rock music style.
	+ **Electronic-Dance**: Electronic-Dance music style.
* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **Hip-Hop**: Hip-Hop music genre.
	+ **R&B**: R&B music genre.
	+ **Country**: Country music genre.
	+ **Electronic**: Electronic music genre.
	+ **Pop**: Pop music genre.
	+ **Rock**: Rock music genre.
	+ **Jazz**: Jazz music genre.
	+ **Classical**: Classical music genre.
	+ **Reggae**: Reggae music genre.
	+ **Blues**: Blues music genre.
	+ **Folk**: Folk music genre.
	+ **Rap**: Rap music genre.
	+ **Electronic-Dance**: Electronic-Dance music genre.
	+ **R&B/Pop**: R&B/Pop music genre.
	+ **Rap/Hip-Hop**: Rap/Hip-Hop music genre.
	+ **Country-Rock**: Country-Rock music genre.
* **Why should we feature your music?** (`contact_request`, text, required: false): Briefly describe why you want us to feature your music.
* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, text, required: false): Provide any further information about your campaign.
* **Playlist Name** (`playlist_name`, text, required: false): Enter the playlist name.
* **Playlist Type** (`playlist_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **New Release**: New release playlist.
	+ **Popular**: Popular playlist.
	+ **Up and Coming**: Up and coming playlist.
	+ **Editor's Pick**: Editor's pick playlist.
	+ **User Created**: User created playlist.
	+ **Featured**: Featured playlist.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required: false): Enter the release date.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, required: false): Enter the release time.
* **Playlist Image** (`playlist_image`, text, required: false): Enter the playlist image URL.
* **Release Info** (`release_info`, note, required: false): Enter any release information.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false):
	+ **Yes**: We want to follow up.
	+ **No**: We do not want to follow up.
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`, email, required: false): Enter the follow-up email address.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible in the optional fields.
- If you have any further questions, contact us at [your contact email].
