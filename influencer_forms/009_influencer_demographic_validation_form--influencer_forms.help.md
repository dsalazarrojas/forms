# Influencer Demographic Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects demographic information from influencers to validate their audience data and identify potential issues with misrepresentation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the field explanation below).
2. Ensure that the email and first name fields match the account information associated with your online presence.
3. Select the correct options for your website and location.
4. Answer the category question truthfully, as it helps to identify whether you have a specific niche or topic focus.
5. Fill in the correct platform information (if applicable).
6. Answer the language question truthfully.
7. Select the correct timezone.
8. For the engagement metrics, select the options that best represent your average engagement rate on your platform(s).
9. Answer the demographic overlap question truthfully.
10. Answer the average age question truthfully.
11. Answer the average gender question truthfully.
12. Answer the average interest question truthfully.
13. Answer the average occupation question truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your public profile.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your public profile.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address associated with your online presence.
* **website** (text, required): Enter your website URL.
* **location** (text, required): Enter your location or city as it appears on your public profile.
* **category** (select_one, required): Select whether your account has a specific niche or focus.
* **platform** (select_multiple, required): Select the platform(s) where you have an online presence.
* **language** (select_one, required): Select the primary language of your audience.
* **timezone** (time, required): Select your timezone.
* **followers** (number, required): Enter the number of followers on your platform(s).
* **views** (number, required): Enter the number of views on your platform(s).
* **engagement** (number, required): Enter the engagement rate on your platform(s).
* **audience_overlap** (select_multiple, required): Select whether you have an overlapping audience with other influencers.
* **average_engagement** (select_one, required): Select whether your average engagement rate is good or fair.
* **average_views** (number, required): Enter the average number of views on your platform(s).
* **average_engagement_rate** (select_one, required): Select whether your average engagement rate is good or fair.
* **audience_overlap_rate** (select_one, required): Select whether your audience overlap is good or fair.
* **engagement_rate** (select_one, required): Select whether your average engagement rate is good or fair.
* **views_rate** (select_one, required): Select whether your average views rate is good or fair.
* **demographic_overlap** (select_multiple, required): Select whether your audience has an overlapping demographic.
* **average_age** (select_one, required): Select whether your average age is yes or no.
* **average_gender** (select_one, required): Select whether your average gender is yes or no.
* **average_interest** (select_one, required): Select whether your average interest is yes or no.
* **average_occupation** (select_one, required): Select whether your average occupation is yes or no.

**Tips**: Take your time to answer the questions truthfully, as this information will help identify potential issues with your demographic data. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
