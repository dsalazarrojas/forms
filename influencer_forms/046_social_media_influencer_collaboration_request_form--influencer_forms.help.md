# social_media_influencer_collaboration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by brands to gather information from potential social media influencers for collaboration requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your brand name and contact email address.
2. Provide a brief proposal for the collaboration, including any supporting documents or media.
3. Choose the media format you would like to use for the collaboration (Image, Video, Audio, or Text).
4. Select all categories the brand is interested in (Fashion, Beauty, Food, Sports, Gaming, or Travel).
5. Specify your expected collaboration duration (optional).
6. Choose your preferred communication channel (Email, Phone, or In-app).
7. Add any additional information about the collaboration.
8. Set a proposal deadline and the date you would like to review proposals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Name** (`brand_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your brand name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Proposal** (`proposal`, `text`, required/optional): A brief proposal from the brand, including any supporting documents or media.
* **Media** (`media`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the media format for the collaboration (Image, Video, Audio, or Text).
* **Categories** (`categories`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all categories the brand is interested in (Fashion, Beauty, Food, Sports, Gaming, or Travel).
* **Expected Collaboration** (`expected_collaboration`, `time`, required/optional): Specify your expected collaboration duration (optional).
* **Preferred Communication** (`preferred_communication`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred communication channel (Email, Phone, or In-app).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information about the collaboration.
* **Proposal Deadline** (`proposal_deadline`, `date`, required/optional): Set a proposal deadline.
* **Proposal Review Date** (`proposal_review_date`, `date`, required/optional): Set the date you would like to review proposals.
