# social_media_influencer_collaboration_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of the collaboration with the social media influencer. Your feedback will be used to improve future collaborations and provide better experiences for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
3. Make sure to select all applicable options for the collaboration recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **engagement** (`engagement`, `number`, required/optional): Please rate your level of engagement with the collaboration. This will help us assess the effectiveness of the communication.
* **audience reach** (`audience_reach`, `number`, required/optional): Please estimate the reach of the collaboration (e.g., number of people who saw or interacted with the content).
* **overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the collaboration.
* **collaboration goals** (`collaboration_goals`, `text`, optional/optional): Please describe your collaboration goals and expectations.
* **effective communication** (`effective_communication`, `text`, optional/optional): Please describe the communication process and how it affected the collaboration.
* **collaboration process** (`collaboration_process`, `text`, optional/optional): Please describe your experience with the collaboration process.
* **collaboration outcomes** (`collaboration_outcomes`, `text`, optional/optional): Please describe the outcomes of the collaboration.
* **collaboration recommendation** (`collaboration_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all applicable options that describe your collaboration experience.
	+ Option 1: The collaboration met or exceeded my expectations.
	+ Option 2: The collaboration met some of my expectations.
	+ Option 3: The collaboration did not meet my expectations.
	+ Option 4: I would not recommend this collaboration.
	+ Option 5: This collaboration was not applicable to me.
* **comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional/optional): Please leave any comments or suggestions on how to improve future collaborations.

**Tips**

* Please answer the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the comments section.
* If you need more information or clarification on any question, please ask us and we will be happy to assist.
