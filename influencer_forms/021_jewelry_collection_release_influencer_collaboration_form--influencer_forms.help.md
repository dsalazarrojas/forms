# jewelry_collection_release_influencer_collaboration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from influencers interested in collaborating with the company on a jewelry collection release. This form will help the marketing team evaluate the suitability of the influencer for the collaboration and ensure alignment with the brand's objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "User Input" field. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide contact information for follow-up communication.
2. Select your preferred social media platforms where you would like to collaborate with us. You can choose one or multiple platforms from the list.
3. Provide your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up calls or messages.
5. Write a brief note about your interest in collaborating with us and any additional information you'd like to share.
6. Choose your type of influence (Social Media Influencer, Content Creator, or Brand Ambassador).
7. Indicate your collaboration frequency (e.g., one-time, weekly, monthly).
8. Select the length of your desired collaboration (e.g., short-term, long-term).
9. Provide links to your portfolio on the social media platforms you selected.
10. Enter a brief bio about yourself.
11. Provide your website URL.
12. Choose your content specialties (e.g., Jewelry Photography, Jewelry Modeling, Jewelry Design).
13. Enter the name of the person you would like to be the primary contact for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): Enter your name and email address to provide contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Social Media Platforms** (Social Media Platforms, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred social media platforms where you would like to collaborate with us.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up calls or messages.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Write a brief note about your interest in collaborating with us and any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Influencer Type** (Influencer Type, select_one, optional): Choose your type of influence (Social Media Influencer, Content Creator, or Brand Ambassador).
* **Collaboration Frequency** (Collaboration Frequency, number, optional): Indicate your collaboration frequency (e.g., one-time, weekly, monthly).
* **Collaboration Length** (Collaboration Length, number, optional): Select the length of your desired collaboration (e.g., short-term, long-term).
* **Portfolio Links** (Portfolio Links, select_multiple, optional): Provide links to your portfolio on the social media platforms you selected.
* **Bio** (Bio, text, optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Website** (Website, text, optional): Provide your website URL.
* **Content Specialties** (Content Specialties, select_multiple, optional): Choose your content specialties (e.g., Jewelry Photography, Jewelry Modeling, Jewelry Design).
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be the primary contact for communication.
