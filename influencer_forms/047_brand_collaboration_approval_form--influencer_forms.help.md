# Brand Collaboration Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and track brand collaborations with social media influencers. It helps in ensuring that all necessary information is collected and approved before proceeding with a collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the unique collaboration ID, which will help track the collaboration process.
*   Select the request date when the collaboration was proposed.
*   Enter the name of the social media influencer.
*   Enter the social media handle of the influencer.
*   Select the primary social media platform that the influencer is primarily active on.
*   Enter the total follower count of the influencer.
*   Choose the type of collaboration (e.g., sponsored content, product endorsement, etc.).
*   Enter the name of the campaign.
*   Select the duration of the collaboration (e.g., one-time, 1 week, 6 months, etc.).
*   Specify the content deliverables required from the influencer.
*   Assess whether the influencer has a good fit for the brand.
*   Determine if the influencer reaches the target audience.
*   Select the approval status of the collaboration (e.g., approved, approved with conditions, etc.).
*   Enter any approval notes or conditions.
*   Finally, enter the name of the person who approved the collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Collaboration ID**: This field is used to uniquely identify the collaboration and help track the collaboration process.
*   **Request Date**: This field is used to record when the collaboration was proposed.
*   **Influencer Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the social media influencer.
*   **Influencer Handle**: This field is used to enter the social media handle of the influencer.
*   **Primary Platform**: This field is used to select the primary social media platform that the influencer is active on.
*   **Follower Count**: This field is used to enter the total number of followers of the influencer.
*   **Type of Collaboration**: This field is used to select the type of collaboration (e.g., sponsored content, product endorsement, etc.).
*   **Campaign Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the campaign.
*   **Collaboration Duration**: This field is used to select the duration of the collaboration.
*   **Content Deliverables**: This field is used to specify what content needs to be created.
*   **Brand Fit**: This field is used to assess whether the influencer has a good fit for the brand.
*   **Audience Alignment**: This field is used to determine if the influencer reaches the target audience.
*   **Approval Status**: This field is used to select the approval status of the collaboration.
*   **Approval Notes**: This field is used to enter any approval notes or conditions.
*   **Approved by**: This field is used to enter the name of the person who approved the collaboration.
