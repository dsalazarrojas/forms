# Influencer Authenticity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us verify the authenticity of your account and ensure that it aligns with our platform's standards. By filling out this form, we will assess your account details, profile picture, and other necessary information to validate your identity and ensure you meet our requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your account details, including your account type (e.g., type a, b, or c).
2. Upload a profile picture that accurately represents you.
3. Select the correct profile completion status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Provide your account name and description.
5. Enter a link to your account (if applicable).
6. Enter your email address (if applicable).
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. Select your social media links (e.g., linkedin, facebook, or instagram).
9. Share some content samples from your account.
10. Add any additional information about your account that you think is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Details (Account Type)**: Select the category that best represents your account type (type a, b, or c).
* **Profile Picture** (Profile Picture): Upload a clear and high-quality image of yourself or your brand's logo.
* **Profile Completion** (Profile Completion): Select the status that describes how complete your profile is (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Account Name** (Account Name): Enter your account name, as it appears on your social media platform.
* **Account Description** (Account Description): Provide a brief description of your account, including a summary of your services or products.
* **Account Link** (Account Link): Enter a link to your account, if applicable.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, if you have one associated with your account.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Social Media Links** (Social Media Links): Select the social media platforms where your account is present (e.g., linkedin, facebook, or instagram).
* **Content Samples** (Content Samples): Share some examples of your content, such as blog posts, images, or videos.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info): Add any additional information about your account that you think is relevant, such as your expertise or services offered.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your account.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact us for help.
* This form is a one-time submission and is not required to be filled out again unless specified by our team.
* By filling out this form, you're helping us to verify your account and ensure it meets our platform's standards.
