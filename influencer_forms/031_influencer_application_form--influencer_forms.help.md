# Influencer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential influencers who wish to collaborate with the brand. The form is intended for influencers who have an established presence on social media and are looking to partner with the brand for sponsored content or other collaborative opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with a `*` asterisk. These fields are crucial in helping us assess your suitability for collaborations.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, and do not enter custom options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, `*`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, `*`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, `*`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text,): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, ): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, ): Enter your time zone (e.g., `EST`, `PST`, `GMT`, etc.).
* **Profile Picture URL** (`profile_picture_url`, text, ): Enter the URL of your profile picture on social media.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, ): Enter a brief description of yourself and your expertise.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, select_multiple, `*`): Select the social media platforms where you are active.
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_one, `*`): Select one category that best describes the type of content you create (e.g., `Beauty`, `Fashion`, `Lifestyle`, etc.).
* **Subcategories** (`subcategories`, select_multiple, ): Select all relevant subcategories for your chosen category.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, `*`): Select your level of experience as an influencer (e.g., `1-2 years`, `5-10 years`, etc.).
* **Followers Count** (`followers_count`, number, ): Enter the number of followers on your most popular social media platform.
* **Engagement Rate** (`engagement_rate`, number, ): Enter your average engagement rate (e.g., `2%`, `5%`, etc.).
* **Content Types** (`content_types`, select_multiple, ): Select all types of content you create (e.g., `Photo`, `Video`, `Live`, etc.).
* **Content Topics** (`content_topics`, text, ): Enter all topics you cover in your content (e.g., `Beauty`, `Fashion`, etc.).
* **Collaborations** (`collaborations`, select_multiple, ): Select the number of collaborations you have done with other influencers.
* **Average Engagement Rate** (`average_engagement_rate`, number, ): Enter your average engagement rate for your content.
* **Average Follower Growth** (`average_follower_growth`, number, ): Enter your average follower growth per month.
* **Average Reach** (`average_reach`, number, ): Enter your average reach per post.
* **Average Impressions** (`average_impressions`, number, ): Enter your average impressions per post.
* **Average Engagement Impression** (`average_engagement_impressions`, number, ): Enter your average engagement impressions per post.
* **Average Engagement Rate Impression** (`average_engagement_rate_impression`, number, ): Enter your average engagement rate impression per post.
* **Average Views** (`average_views`, number, ): Enter your average views per post.
* **Average Clicks** (`average_clicks`, number, ): Enter your average clicks per post.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Use the provided options for select fields, and do not enter custom options.
* Do not enter duplicate or fake information. Our system may flag suspicious activities and reject your application.
