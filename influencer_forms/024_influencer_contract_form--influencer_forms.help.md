# Influencer Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for influencer and advertiser partnerships, ensuring both parties have a clear understanding of the collaboration. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Influencer Details** page with the required information about the influencer.
2.  Move on to the **Advertiser Details** page and provide the necessary information about the advertiser.
3.  Next, fill out the **Social Media Accounts** page with the influencer's social media information.
4.  Then, move on to the **Marketing Campaign** page and input details about the marketing campaign.
5.  After that, sign your **E-Signature** on the **E-Signature** page.
6.  Finally, agree to the **Terms and Conditions** on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Influencer Details
*   **Influencer Details**: Enter the name, email, and a brief description of the influencer to be partnered with.
*   **Advertiser Details**: Enter the name, email, and a brief description of the advertiser to be partnered with.
*   **Social Media Accounts**: List the social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.) and provide a brief description or link to the influencer's account.
*   **Marketing Campaign**: Describe the marketing campaign, including its objectives and scope.
*   **E-Signature**: Sign with your digital signature (a typed name and date).
*   **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the partnership.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth partnership experience.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to form@gic.mx.
