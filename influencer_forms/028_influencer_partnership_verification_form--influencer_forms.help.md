Influencer Partnership Verification Form - Help Guide
=====================================================

This guide is designed to help users understand the purpose and requirements of the Influencer Partnership Verification Form. The form is used to verify information about an influencer's partnership proposal, including contact information, social media presence, and streaming credentials. 

To complete the form, users should start with the **Partnership Details** field, which requires a brief description of their proposal or goals. They should then select the type of partnership they are interested in, either as a brand or a content creator, in the **Brand Partnership Type** field. 

The **Audience Characteristics** field is where users describe their target audience, including demographics and interests. The **Contact Info** field requires users to enter their contact information, including email and phone number. 

Users must select the social media platforms where they have an official presence in the **Verification Documents** field. They should also provide any additional notes in the **Additional Notes** field if needed. 

To complete the **Email**, **Phone Number**, and **Social Media** fields, users should enter their email address, phone number, and social media username or handle, respectively. 

Users should also indicate the number of streams they've created on platforms like Twitch or YouTube in the **Number of Streams** field. They must enter the URLs for their live streams in the **Stream URL** field. 

In the **Discord Server**, **Discord Username**, **Pinterest Channel**, **Pinterest Profile URL**, **Twitch/Twitch Streams**, **Snapchat Channel**, **Snapchat Stream URL**, **YouTube Channel**, **YouTube Stream URL**, and **YouTube Streams** fields, users enter their Discord server URL and username, Pinterest channel URL and name, Twitch/Twitch username and URL, Snapchat channel URL and username, and YouTube channel URL and stream URL, respectively.

To avoid any issues with the verification process, users should fill out the form completely and accurately. Be honest and authentic in your responses, and provide any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your partnership proposal. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
