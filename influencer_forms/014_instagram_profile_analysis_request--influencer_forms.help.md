# Instagram Profile Analysis Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from your Instagram profile for analysis purposes. The goal is to gather data on your profile, including names, contact information, and account details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, please provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. Note that this is an optional field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which is a required field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which is a required field.
5. If you have an account, enter your Instagram handle in the "Instagram Handle" field, which is a required field.
6. Select the category that best describes your account from the "Category" dropdown menu.
7. Enter a brief description of yourself in the "Bio" field.
8. If you have a public or private account, select the appropriate option from the "Account Type" dropdown menu.
9. Upload a profile picture from your local device or enter the URL of the picture in the "Profile Picture" field.
10. Select the status of your account from the "Account Status" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the number of followers you have in the "Followers Count" field.
12. Enter your engagement score in the "Engagement Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, `text`, required): Enter your Instagram handle.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes your account from the following options:
	+ Gaming
	+ Beauty
	+ Sports
	+ Music
	+ Food
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of account you have from the following options:
	+ Public
	+ Private
	+ Business
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, `text`, required): Upload a profile picture from your local device or enter the URL of the picture.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of your account from the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Pending
	+ Restricted
* **Followers Count** (`followers_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of followers you have.
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, `number`, optional): Enter your engagement score.
* **Account Type 2** (`account_type_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your account type.
