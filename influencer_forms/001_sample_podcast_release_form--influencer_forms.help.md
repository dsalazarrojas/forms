# sample_podcast_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect relevant information about a podcast release, including guest and podcast details, release permission, and other supporting details. This form is likely used by content creators, producers, or administrators to manage podcast releases, such as scheduling interviews, recording and publishing episodes, or coordinating with guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the guest and podcast.
2. Fill in the guest information section with the guest's name, title, and any other relevant details.
3. Provide the podcast information, including its title, and any other relevant details.
4. Specify the release permission for the podcast.
5. Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the podcast.
6. Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the podcast.
7. Select the date and time of the podcast release.
8. Add any additional notes about the release.
9. Choose the person assigned to manage the podcast release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information (Guest Info)** (`guest_info`, text, optional):
    This field is where you will enter the details of the guest for the podcast. This can be the guest's name, title, and any other relevant information.
* **Podcast Information (Podcast Info)** (`podcast_info`, text, optional):
    This field is where you will enter the details of the podcast, including its title and any other relevant information.
* **Release Permission (Release Permission)** (`release_permission`, number, optional):
    This field is not clearly defined, but it's assumed to be related to permission or agreement status for the podcast release. Enter a number corresponding to the relevant status.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional):
    Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the podcast.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional):
    Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the podcast.
* **Date of Release (Date of Release)** (`date_of_release`, date, optional):
    Select the date on which the podcast is to be released.
* **Time of Release (Time of Release)** (`time_of_release`, time, optional):
    Select the time at which the podcast is to be released.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, note, optional):
    Enter any additional notes or comments about the podcast release.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To)** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional):
    Choose the person or team assigned to manage the podcast release.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields, as the more information you provide, the better the release process will be managed.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the relevant team or contact them for clarification.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
