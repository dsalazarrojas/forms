# Fitness Influencer Collaboration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Influencer Collaboration Survey" is a form designed to collect information from fitness influencers about their collaboration interest, audience characteristics, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your user profile details in the "User Profile" section.
2. Select your niche in the "Niche" section.
3. Provide details about your audience in the "Audience Details" section.
4. In the "Social Media Channels" section, select the platforms where you have a presence.
5. In the "Work Experience" section, describe your relevant work experience in the fitness industry.
6. Indicate your collaboration interest in the "Collaboration Interest" section.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the "Note" section.
8. Finally, provide your email address in the "Email" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Profile** (`user_profile`, text, required: false)
	+ This section is where you can provide your basic user profile information.
* **Niche** (`user_niche`, text, required: false)
	+ Please select the category that best describes your area of expertise in the fitness industry.
* **Audience Details** (`audience_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Share information about your target audience, such as their age, fitness level, or interests.
* **Profile** (`audience_details_profile`, text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional details about your audience, such as their demographics or psychographics.
* **Niche** (`audience_details_niche`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all the areas within the fitness industry that you have expertise in (e.g., Fitness, Health, Wellness, Sports).
* **Audience** (`audience_details_audience`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your audience in more detail, using 255 characters or less.
* **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all the social media platforms where you have a presence.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your relevant work experience in the fitness industry, using 255 characters or less.
* **Collaboration Interest** (`collaboration_interest`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Indicate whether you are interested or not interested in collaboration opportunities.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your collaboration interest.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Provide your email address for communication purposes.
