# Gaming Streaming Platform Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about gaming streaming platform referrals. This includes details about games, creators, and their associated accounts on various platforms. By filling out this form, you'll be helping our team keep track and manage referrals effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, go through each page and answer the required fields. Here's a list of the pages with their corresponding fields:

*   Game Details:
    *   **Game Details** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        This page asks for general information about the game you're referring. Please provide a brief description of the game.
*   Creator Info:
    *   **Creator Info** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        This page is for providing information about the creator you're referring. Please enter their name or username.
*   Referral Source:
    *   **Referral Source** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        Here, you can enter the source of the referral, such as where you met the creator.
*   Platform Name:
    *   **Platform Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        If the referral source is associated with a specific platform, please enter it here.
*   Streamer Type:
    *   **Streamer Type** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
        Select the type of streamer you're referring, which can be YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok.
*   Platform Account:
    *   **Platform Account** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        Please enter the streamer's account name or ID on the selected platform.
*   Streamer ID:
    *   **Streamer ID** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the streamer's ID or username.
*   Creator Referral Status:
    *   **Creator Referral Status** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
        Select the status of the referral, which can be New, Existing, Referred, Inactive, or Pending.
*   Follow up Date:
    *   **Follow up Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required: false)
        Enter the date for the follow-up.
*   Follow up Time:
    *   **Follow up Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required: false)
        Enter the time for the follow-up.
*   Notes:
    *   **Notes** (`<name>`, `note`, required: false)
        Provide any additional comments or information about the referral.
*   Email:
    *   **Email** (`<name>`, `email`, required: false)
        Enter the creator's email address.
*   Phone:
    *   **Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
        Enter the creator's phone number.
*   Email Type:
    *   **Email Type** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
        Select the type of email address, which can be Business or Personal.
*   Follow up Frequency:
    *   **Follow up Frequency** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
        Select the frequency of follow-ups, which can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never.
*   Follow up Channel:
    *   **Follow up Channel** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
        Select the channel to use for follow-ups, which can be YouTube, Twitch, Phone, Email, or In-App.
*   Referral Source:
    *   **Referral Source** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
        Select the source of the referral, which can be Social Media, Email, Referral, Streamer, or In-App.

Please note that most fields are not required and can be filled based on your needs and preferences.
You can refer to the field labels for a better understanding of each field's purpose and usage.
