# Physical Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the informed consent of patients for physical therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Indicate your willingness to receive physical therapy services by answering "Yes" to the "Patient Identification" field.
3. Provide your current medical condition in the "Patient Condition" field.
4. Confirm that you have reviewed and understand your treatment plan by answering "Yes" to the "Informed Consent" field.
5. Sign the form to confirm your understanding of the treatment plan.
6. Date the form with the date you signed it.
7. Have a witness sign the form, if possible.
8. Sign the form as the therapist to confirm that you have reviewed and will implement the treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the physical therapy form.
* **Patient Identification**: Indicate that you are the patient and are willing to receive treatment.
* **Therapist**: Indicate that the healthcare provider is a physical therapist.
* **Patient Condition**: Describe your current medical condition.
* **Treatment Plan**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand your treatment plan.
* **Informed Consent**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand your treatment plan and are willing to receive treatment.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand your treatment plan.
* **Date Signed**: Date the form with the date you signed it.
* **Patient Signature**: Indicate that the patient has reviewed and understands the treatment plan and is willing to receive treatment.
* **Witness Signature**: Have a witness sign the form, if possible.
* **Therapist Signature**: Sign the form as the physical therapist to confirm that you have reviewed and will implement the treatment plan.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the treatment plan or your condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing it.
* Ensure that you have understood and agreed to the treatment plan.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
