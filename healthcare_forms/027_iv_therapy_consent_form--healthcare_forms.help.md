# IV Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IV Therapy Consent Form is a document that requires the patient's informed consent before undergoing IV therapy. IV therapy involves the administration of medications or fluids through an intravenous line, which can be a sensitive topic. It is essential to ensure that the patient understands the risks and benefits of the treatment and agrees to it.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, as it is a legal document.
- Ensure that you have understood the risks and benefits of IV therapy and are willing to undergo it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Consent to IV Therapy** (`consent_to_iv_therapy`, text, optional): Check "Yes" if you consent to IV therapy, otherwise, explain your concerns or reasons for refusal.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions that may affect your ability to undergo IV therapy.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medications you are currently taking from the provided options, or select "Other" if your medication is not listed.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a detailed medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions that may impact your treatment.
* **Signature** (`consent_signature`, text, required): Sign and date your name, indicating that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the consent form.

Please note that the medical history and signature fields are required to be completed before submitting the form.
