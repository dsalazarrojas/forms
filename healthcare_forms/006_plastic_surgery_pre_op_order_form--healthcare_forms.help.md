# Plastic Surgery Pre Op Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather and document essential information about patients undergoing plastic surgery procedures. It will help medical staff prepare for the surgery and ensure that all necessary data is collected and reviewed before the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that all required fields are filled in.
2. Review each field for completeness and relevance before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you have selected the correct options for laboratory tests, surgery procedure, and pre-operative medications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, providing the name exactly as it appears on their identification.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Data**: Enter any relevant medical details or conditions that may affect the patient's surgery.
*   **Laboratory Tests**: Select the laboratory tests that will be performed before the surgery. This can include Blood Test A, Blood Test B, or Blood Test C.
*   **Diagnosis**: Enter the diagnosis related to the patient's condition.
*   **Surgery Procedure**: Select the specific procedure that the patient will undergo. This can be Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.
*   **Pre Operative Medications**: Select any medications that the patient needs to take before the surgery. This can include Medication 1, Medication 2, or Medication 3.
*   **Surgeon Information**: Enter any relevant details about the surgeon performing the surgery.
*   **Date Of Surgery**: Enter the date of the scheduled surgery.
*   **Time Of Surgery**: Enter the time of the scheduled surgery.
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient exactly as it appears on their identification.

## Tips
*   Double-check that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Update the form only when there are changes in the patient's medical history or surgery details.
*   Ensure that the form is submitted with the required fields filled in and reviewed by medical staff before the surgery.
