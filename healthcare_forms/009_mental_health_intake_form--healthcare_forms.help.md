# Mental Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect patient information for mental health services.
It is designed to gather essential details to provide effective treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your personal information in the "Patient Information" page.
3. Provide your medical history in the "Medical History" page.
4. If applicable, upload supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" page.
5. In the "Fee Payment" page, you will find information on payment options and instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
* **Label (Page 2)** (no description available): This field is currently empty.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share your medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any relevant documents, such as medical records or identification documents.
* **Fee Payment** (`fee_payment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is currently empty.

Please note that some pages are optional, and you may skip them if not applicable to your situation. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
