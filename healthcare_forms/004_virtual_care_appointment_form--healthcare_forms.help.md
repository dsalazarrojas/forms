# Virtual Care Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Care Appointment Form is used to collect essential information for virtual care appointments, ensuring a smooth experience for both patients and care providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, navigate through the different pages or sections, and fill in the required information for each page. You can start by filling in the patient's info, then provide the medical record ID, and continue with the appointment details such as date and time.
2.  Ensure you fill in all required fields, and use the available options to provide more information if necessary. If you're unsure about any field, contact your healthcare provider or the relevant authorities for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info)** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This is the initial page where you provide information about the patient, such as their name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Record ID (Medical Record ID)** (`medical_record_id`, text, optional): If the patient has a medical record ID, please enter it here to allow care providers to access relevant medical history.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date)** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the scheduled virtual care appointment. Make sure to select a specific date in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Start Time (Start Time)** (`start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time of the virtual care appointment, including the time of day in the format (HH:MM).
* **End Time (End Time)** (`end_time`, time, optional): Pick the end time of the virtual care appointment, again using the (HH:MM) format.
* **Appointment Duration (Appointment Duration)** (`appointment_duration`, number, optional): If known, enter the expected duration of the virtual care appointment, in the format of a number of minutes.

Note: Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and with the necessary permissions, and that you're aware of the virtual care appointment schedule and any necessary preparations for the session.

## Tips

*   If you're unsure or have questions about any field, reach out to the care provider or relevant authority for guidance.
*   Double-check that all fields are completed accurately before submitting the form.
*   Ensure you have all necessary permissions and information to fill in the form accurately.
