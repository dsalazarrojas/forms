# Medicines Fridge Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check the temperature range of the medicines fridge and record the action taken. It's essential to ensure that the fridge is within a safe temperature range to store medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Medicine Name" field with the name of the medicine stored in the fridge.
2. Record the temperature readings for morning, afternoon, and evening in the respective fields.
3. Select the temperature range that best describes the current temperature of the medicine from the "Temperature Range" field.
4. If necessary, select one or more actions taken from "Action Taken" field. You can also add a brief description of any other action taken in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the name of the person or tool assigned to check the fridge temperature in the "Assigned to" field.
6. Enter your name as the person submitting the form in the "Submitted by" field.
7. Date the form by selecting the date you are submitting it from the "Date Submitted" field.
8. Record the date when the form will be reviewed by entering the date in the "Date Reviewed" field.
9. Enter the name of the person reviewing the form in the "Reviewed by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medicine Name** (text): Enter the name of the medicine stored in the fridge.
* **Morning Temperature** (number): Record the temperature reading for the morning.
* **Afternoon Temperature** (number): Record the temperature reading for the afternoon.
* **Evening Temperature** (number): Record the temperature reading for the evening.
* **Temperature Range** (select_one): Select the temperature range that best describes the current temperature of the medicine.
	+ Low: Below the ideal storage temperature.
	+ Average: Within the ideal storage temperature range.
	+ High: Above the ideal storage temperature.
* **Action Taken** (select_multiple): Select one or more actions taken to adjust the temperature.
	+ Checked the temperature: Verify that the temperature is within the safe range.
	+ Adjusted the temperature: Adjusted the storage temperature to a safe range.
	+ Called maintenance: Notified maintenance personnel for assistance.
	+ Other (please specify): Add a brief description of any other actions taken.
* **Comments** (note): Add a brief description of any other notes or comments about the fridge condition.
* **Assigned to** (text): Enter the name of the person or tool assigned to check the fridge temperature.
* **Submitted by** (text): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Date Submitted** (date): Select the date you are submitting the form.
* **Date Reviewed** (date): Enter the date when the form will be reviewed.
* **Reviewed by** (text): Enter the name of the person reviewing the form.
