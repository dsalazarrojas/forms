# Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather informed consent for therapy sessions, ensuring that patients understand and agree to the terms of their treatment. It is a critical aspect of the healthcare process and requires accurate and thorough completion.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete all the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the label. If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.
*   Enter your information in the corresponding fields and ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Therapy Informed Consent Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, and it should be filled in to clearly identify the form.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the form from the options provided.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about the therapy sessions or treatment plans.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient, as it will be used for identification purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's address for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's emergency contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's emergency contact email address.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact treatment.
*   **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Describe the treatment plan and any relevant information that the patient should know.
*   **Therapy Goals** (`therapy_goals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the goals of therapy from the options provided.
*   **Therapist** (`therapist`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's therapist.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required: false): Sign and date the form to indicate informed consent.
*   **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, select_one, required: false): Sign and date the form to indicate agreement with the patient's information.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the consent in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the consent in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the therapy sessions will take place.
*   **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the therapy process.
*   **Patient Signature 2** (`patient_signature_2`, select_one, required: false): Sign and date the form to indicate informed consent, if applicable.
