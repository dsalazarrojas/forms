# CT Scan Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and clinical indications for a CT scan from referring physicians. It helps to gather essential details, such as patient demographics, medical history, and scheduling preferences, to facilitate efficient and effective patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referring physician's name and contact information in the "Referring physician name" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient date of birth" field.
4. Provide the clinical indication for the CT scan in the "Clinical indication for CT" field.
5. List any known contrast allergies or contraindications for the patient in the "Known contrast allergies or contraindications" field (if applicable).
6. Select the urgency level for the scan from the available options: "Routine", "Urgent", or "Stat".
7. If applicable, enter the patient's preferred appointment date in the "Preferred appointment date" field.
8. Provide any additional instructions or mobility needs in the "Additional instructions" field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referring physician name**: Enter the full name and contact information of the referring physician.
* **Patient name**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Patient date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Clinical indication for CT**: Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the imaging study and suspected diagnosis.
* **Known contrast allergies or contraindications**: List any known allergies or contraindications related to contrast agents.
* **Urgency level**: Select the urgency level of the scan: "Routine", "Urgent", or "Stat".
* **Preferred appointment date**: Enter a preferred date and time for the appointment.
* **Additional instructions**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the patient's care.
