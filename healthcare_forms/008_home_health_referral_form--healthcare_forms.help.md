# Home Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Referral Form is designed to collect and document patient information and referral details for home health services. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential information to ensure a smooth referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section with the required details.
2. Select the referring provider and the referral reason.
3. Enter the referral date and time.
4. Choose the referral type (e.g., Home Health Aide, Home Health Nurse, or both).
5. Enter any additional contact information for the patient.
6. Fill out the patient's address and any relevant notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as recorded on their medical record.
* **Patient DOB (Date of Birth)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's home address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number, if available.
* **Referring Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider referring the patient.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time**: Enter the time of the referral.
* **Referral Type**: Select the type of home health service required (e.g., Home Health Aide, Home Health Nurse, or both).
* **Patient Information**: Enter any additional information about the patient.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Referral Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the referral.

## Tips

* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Use the referral reason and type to ensure that the referral is processed correctly.
