# Voiding Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voiding Diary Form is used to collect and document information about an individual's experience with voiding events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the General Information section with your personal details.
2.  Enter the Date and Time of the last voiding event.
3.  Enter the Frequency and Volume of the last voiding event.
4.  Fill out the Post-operative Information section if you have undergone surgery.
5.  Enter any additional post-operative details, such as the volume and frequency of voiding after surgery.
6.  Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery.
7.  Finally, click the Submit button to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`General Information`,  text, required): Please enter your full name, date of birth, and any other personal details that you feel are relevant to your voiding experience.
*   **Date of Last Voiding Event** (`voiding_date`,  date, required): Enter the date of the last time you voided.
*   **Time of Last Voiding Event** (`voiding_time`,  time, required): Enter the time of the last voiding event.
*   **Frequency of Voiding** (`voiding_frequency`,  text, required): Enter how often you void, e.g., once a day, twice a day, etc.
*   **Volume of Voiding** (`voiding_volume`,  number, required): Enter the volume of the last voiding event.
*   **Post-operative Information** (`postop_info`,  text, optional): If you have undergone surgery, provide any additional information about your post-operative experience.
*   **Post-operative Volumes** (`postop_volumes`,  number, optional): If you have undergone surgery, enter the volume of voiding after surgery.
*   **Post-operative Voiding Frequency** (`voiding_frequency_postop`,  text, required): If you have undergone surgery, enter how often you void after surgery.
*   **Post-operative Voiding Duration** (`voiding_duration_postop`,  text, optional): If you have undergone surgery, enter the duration of voiding after surgery.
*   **Post-operative Times** (`voiding_times_postop`,  text, optional): If you have undergone surgery, enter the times you void after surgery.
*   **Surgeon Information** (`surgeon_info`,  text, optional): Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery.
*   **Submit** (`submit`,  text, required): Click this button to submit the completed form.

## Tips
Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, and make sure to enter all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
