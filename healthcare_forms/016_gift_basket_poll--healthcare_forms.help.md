# Gift Basket Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Basket Poll form is used to collect information about teams participating in a gift basket poll. This form helps to gather data on team names, descriptions, and images to be used for charity events or fundraising campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the Team Name field.
2. Select a theme for the gift basket from the options provided in the basket theme field.
3. Write a description of the gift basket in the basket description field.
4. Enter the amount of money raised for the gift basket.
5. Choose an image for the team's gift basket from the provided options.
6. Select the team members who will be assigned to the gift basket.
7. Choose whether or not the gift basket has been assigned.
8. Enter the date and time of submission for the gift basket.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team participating in the gift basket poll.
* **Basket Theme** (`basket_theme`, text, required): Select a theme for the gift basket.
* **Basket Description** (`basket_description`, text, required): Write a description of the gift basket.
* **Money Raised** (`money_raised`, number, required): Enter the amount of money raised for the gift basket.
* **Team Basket Image** (`team_basket_image`, select_one, required): Choose an image for the team's gift basket.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the assigned tool.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required): Select the team members who will be assigned to the gift basket.
* **Gift Basket** (`gift_basket`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not the gift basket has been assigned.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission for the gift basket.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission for the gift basket.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the gift basket.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the team or organization.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the team or organization.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the assigned date for the gift basket.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, optional): Enter the assigned time for the gift basket.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team participating in the gift basket poll.
* **Team Members 1** (`team_members_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members for the first team.
* **Team Members 2** (`team_members_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members for the second team.
* **Team Members 3** (`team_members_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members for the third team.
* **Team Basket Image 1** (`team_basket_image_1`, text, optional): Choose an image for the first team's gift basket.
* **Team Basket Image 2** (`team_basket_image_2`, text, optional): Choose an image for the second team's gift basket.
* **Team Basket Image 3** (`team_basket_image_3`, text, optional): Choose an image for the third team's gift basket.
* **Gift Basket Image 1** (`gift_basket_image_1`, text, optional): Choose an image for the gift basket.
* **Gift Basket Image 2** (`gift_basket_image_2`, text, optional): Choose an image for the gift basket.
* **Gift Basket Image 3** (`gift_basket_image_3`, text, optional): Choose an image for the gift basket.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the options for each field to ensure you are choosing the correct information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the purpose of the form or ask for clarification.
* Fill out the form with accurate and meaningful information.
* Review your submissions before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
