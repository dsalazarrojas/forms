# Immunization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect immunization consent information from patients, including their personal details, medical provider information, and consent dates. It is essential to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to maintain patient confidentiality and comply with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Enter the patient's contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
4. Select the medical provider who administered the vaccination from the **Medical Provider** dropdown list.
5. Enter the date of vaccination in the **Date of Vaccination** field.
6. Select the type of vaccination administered from the **Type of Vaccination** dropdown list.
7. Enter the dose number in the **Dose Number** field.
8. Select who administered the vaccine from the **Vaccine Administered By** dropdown list.
9. Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field.
10. Enter the consent date in the **Consent Date** field.
11. Enter the signature date for each of the signatories in the corresponding **Signature of** fields.
12. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Medical Provider**: Select the medical provider who administered the vaccination from the dropdown list.
* **Date of Vaccination**: Enter the date the vaccination was administered in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Type of Vaccination**: Select the type of vaccination administered from the dropdown list.
* **Dose Number**: Enter the dose number for the vaccination.
* **Vaccine Administered By**: Select who administered the vaccine from the dropdown list.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the consent was given.
* **Signature of 1-12**: Enter the signature of each signatory for the form.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and save the form.
