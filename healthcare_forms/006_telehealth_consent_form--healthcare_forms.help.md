# Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from patients for telehealth services, ensuring they understand and agree to participate in online consultations and other remote healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Telehealth Consent**: Select 'Yes' to consent to telehealth services. This confirms you are willing to receive online consultations and other remote care services.
2.  **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3.  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/25/1990).
4.  **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted for appointments and updates.
5.  **Phone Number**: Enter your active phone number where you can be contacted.
6.  **Signature**: Sign your name in the provided space, confirming you have read and understood this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Telehealth Consent**: This field is used to confirm your consent to receive telehealth services. Please select 'Yes' to consent.
*   **Patient Name**: This field is used to capture your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: This field is used to record your date of birth, which is an essential piece of information for your medical records.
*   **Email**: This field is used to collect a valid email address where you can be contacted for appointments and updates.
*   **Phone Number**: This field is used to collect an active phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Signature**: This field is used to confirm that you have read and understood this form. Please sign your name in the provided space.

## Tips

*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to avoid any delays or errors in your care.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about telehealth services or this form, please ask a healthcare professional for clarification.
*   Your consent and information collected in this form will be kept confidential and secure.
