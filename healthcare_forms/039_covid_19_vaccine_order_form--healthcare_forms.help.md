# COVID 19 Vaccine Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from facilities or organizations that need to order COVID-19 vaccines. It helps us track the number of doses needed, the type of vaccine, and the delivery requirements for each order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your facility or organization name in the "Facility or Organization Name" field.
2. Select the type of facility or organization from the drop-down list in the "Type of Facility" field.
3. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person Name" field.
4. Enter the title of the contact person in the "Contact Person Title" field.
5. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter the facility information, including the facility name, address, city, state, and zip code.
8. Select the type of vaccine needed from the drop-down list in the "Vaccine Type Needed" field.
9. Enter the number of doses needed in the "Number of Doses Needed" field.
10. If necessary, enter any additional vaccine types needed in the "Additional Vaccine Types" field.
11. If necessary, enter any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests or Comments" field.
12. Enter the name of the authorized person who can approve the order in the "Authorized Person Name" field.
13. Enter the date of the order in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your facility or organization.
* **Type of Facility**: Select the type of facility or organization from the drop-down list.
	+ Hospital
	+ Clinic
	+ Pharmacy
	+ Healthcare provider office
	+ Nursing home
	+ Other
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **City**: Enter the city of the facility.
* **State**: Enter the state of the facility.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the facility.
* **Vaccine Type Needed**: Select the type of vaccine needed from the drop-down list.
	+ Pfizer
	+ Moderna
	+ Johnson and Johnson
	+ All types needed
	+ Other
* **Number of Doses Needed**: Enter the number of doses needed.
* **Additional Vaccine Types**: If necessary, enter any additional vaccine types needed.
* **Authorized Person Name**: Enter the name of the authorized person who can approve the order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
* If you have any special requests or comments, enter them in the "Special Requests or Comments" field.
