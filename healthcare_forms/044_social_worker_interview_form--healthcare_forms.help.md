# social-worker-interview-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients in the healthcare setting for social workers to better understand their needs and provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
• **Client Info** (`Client Info`, `text`, optional): Please provide any relevant personal information about the client, such as their name, birthdate, or address.

### Contact Methods
• **Contact Methods** (`Contact Methods`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable contact methods that the client has, such as phone or email.

### Medical History
• **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional): Describe any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or allergies.

### Medication List
• **Medication List** (`Medication List`, `select_one`, optional): Select any medications the client is currently taking, such as Aspirin or Ibuprofen.

### Allergies
• **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any allergies the client has, such as Penicillin or Ibuprofen.

### Social Workers
• **Social Workers** (`Social Workers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any social workers the client has been in contact with, such as Social Worker 1 or Social Worker 2.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Use the provided options for the Contact Methods, Medication List, and Allergies fields to select the most accurate responses.
- If a field is not applicable to the client, you can leave it blank.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
