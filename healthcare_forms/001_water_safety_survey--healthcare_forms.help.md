# Water Safety Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Safety Survey form is designed to gather information about your experience and qualifications in water safety, lifesaving skills, and water conditions/hazards. This form will help us understand your background and assess your suitability for specific roles or activities related to water safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant options from the list.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. For date fields, enter the date in the required format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your job?** (intro_page, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job or role related to water safety.
- **Watercraft experience** (watercraft_page, select_one, required): Please select your experience in watercraft, such as:
  - Lifeguard: Experience in lifeguarding, swimming, or water rescue.
  - Scuba Diver: Experience as a scuba diving instructor or diver.
  - Boat Operator: Experience operating a boat in water safety contexts.
  - Teacher: Teaching water safety or related courses.
- **Lifesaving skills** (lifesaving_page, select_multiple, required): Please select all the lifesaving skills you have experience with:
  - CPR: Basic Life Support training.
  - First aid: Providing basic medical care in emergencies.
  - Lifesaving: Advanced lifesaving techniques, such as using rescue tubes or other equipment.
- **Water conditions** (waterconditions_page, text, optional): Describe your experience with different water conditions, such as currents, depths, or temperatures.
- **Water hazards** (waterhazard_page, select_one, required): Please select all the water hazards you have experience with:
  - Strong current: Experience navigating or working in strong currents.
  - Steep drop: Experience working near or in steep water drops.
  - Underwater hazard: Experience working underwater or near underwater hazards.
- **Rescue techniques** (rescue_page, select_multiple, required): Please select all the rescue techniques you have experience with:
  - Floating: Experience using flotation devices or swimming to rescue people.
  - Swimming rescue: Experience swimming to rescue people or using swimming techniques.
  - Boating rescue: Experience using boats to rescue people.
- **Waterborne illness** (waterhealth_page, text, optional): Describe your experience with waterborne illnesses, such as cholera or other water-borne diseases.
- **Survey completion** (surveycompletion_page, date, optional): The date you completed this form (not required for submission, but for our records).
