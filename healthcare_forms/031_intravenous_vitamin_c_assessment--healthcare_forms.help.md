# intravenous_vitamin_c_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intravenous vitamin C assessment form is used to collect information from patients that will be used to determine their suitability for intravenous vitamin C treatment. This form is not intended to replace a medical professional's evaluation, but rather to aid in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient details page (page 1) with the patient's relevant information, such as their name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2. On the appointment preferences page (page 2), select the patient's preferred appointment time from the available options.
3. Leave any additional notes on the notes page (page 3) if you have any specific comments or information to add.
4. On the email page (page 4), enter the patient's email address.
5. On the phone page (page 5), enter the patient's phone number.
6. On the date page (page 6), select the date of the appointment.
7. On the time page (page 7), select the time of the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (Page 1):
	+ Enter the patient's name, contact email, and contact phone number.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (Page 2):
	+ Select the preferred appointment time for the intravenous vitamin C treatment. Options include morning, afternoon, and evening.
* **Additional Notes** (Page 3):
	+ Provide any additional comments or information about the patient's medical history or treatment needs.
* **Email** (Page 4):
	+ Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Page 5):
	+ Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (Page 6):
	+ Select the date of the appointment for intravenous vitamin C treatment.
* **Time** (Page 7):
	+ Select the time of the appointment for intravenous vitamin C treatment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient is aware of the specific requirements and procedures involved in intravenous vitamin C treatment before filling out this form.
* This form is not intended to diagnose or treat any medical condition. If you are not a medical professional, please do not fill out this form without first consulting a qualified healthcare expert.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the intravenous vitamin C assessment form, contact your healthcare provider or medical team for guidance.
