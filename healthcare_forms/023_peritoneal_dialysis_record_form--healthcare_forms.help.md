# Peritoneal Dialysis Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and document patient data during peritoneal dialysis treatments. It helps healthcare professionals gather and track relevant information for patient care and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select the modality of peritoneal dialysis (Automated or Manual).
2. Enter the date of treatment.
3. Record the duration of the dialysis session.
4. Record the total dose administered during the session.
5. Record the residual volume after the session.
6. Record the ultrafiltration rate.
7. Record the patient's weight.
8. Record the blood flow rate.
9. Select the peritoneal dialysis machine setting used (Machine A, Machine B, or Machine C).
10. Record the blood flow rate frequency.
11. Record the blood flow rate unit (optional).
12. Record any notes about the blood flow rate (optional).
13. Record any additional comments about the blood flow rate (optional).
14. Select any relevant peritoneal dialysis machine settings.
15. Enter any additional information about the blood flow rate frequency.
16. Record any additional notes about the patient's treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Peritoneal Dialysis Modality** (Automated or Manual): Select the type of peritoneal dialysis used (Automated or Manual).
* **Date Of Treatment**: Record the date on which the patient underwent peritoneal dialysis.
* **Dialysis Session Duration**: Record the duration of the peritoneal dialysis session in hours or minutes.
* **Total Dose**: Record the total dose administered during the session.
* **Residual Volume**: Record the residual volume after the dialysis session.
* **Ultrafiltration Rate**: Record the ultrafiltration rate during the session.
* **Patient Weight**: Record the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Flow Rate**: Record the blood flow rate during the session.
* **Blood Flow Rate Unit**: Record the unit of measurement for the blood flow rate (e.g., mL/minute).
* **Blood Flow Rate Note**: Record any additional notes about the blood flow rate.
* **Blood Flow Rate Comment**: Record any additional comments about the blood flow rate.
* **Peritoneal Dialysis Machine Settings**: Select the machine setting used for the treatment (Machine A, Machine B, or Machine C).
* **Blood Flow Rate Frequency**: Record the frequency of the blood flow rate.
* **Blood Flow Rate Frequency Unit**: Record the unit of measurement for the blood flow rate frequency.
* **Patient Email**: Record the patient's email address (if available).
* **Patient Phone**: Record the patient's phone number (if available).
* **Patient Name**: Record the patient's name.
* **Blood Pressure**: Record the patient's blood pressure (if available).
* **Pulse Rate**: Record the patient's pulse rate.
* **Patient Temperature**: Record the patient's temperature (if available).
* **Blood Urea**: Record the patient's blood urea level.
* **Blood Urea Unit**: Record the unit of measurement for the blood urea level (e.g., mmol/L).
* **Hematocrit**: Record the patient's hematocrit level.
* **Hematocrit Unit**: Record the unit of measurement for the hematocrit level (e.g., %).
* **Notes**: Record any additional notes about the patient's treatment.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and update the form for each patient visit.
* Store the form in the patient's medical record for future reference.
