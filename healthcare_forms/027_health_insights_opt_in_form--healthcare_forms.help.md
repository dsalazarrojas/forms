# health_insights_opt_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_insights_opt_in_form is designed to collect medical information from patients to provide healthcare professionals with valuable insights into their health and medical background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out the form in its entirety, as all fields are not required but it's best to provide as much information as possible for better patient care.
2.  Ensure that you are a valid patient or authorized to fill out this form on behalf of the patient.
3.  If you need to add a medical history, please use the text area provided and describe your medical history in as much detail as possible.
4.  If you need to add an emergency contact, please fill in the required fields with the relevant information.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents, for accurate record-keeping purposes.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Enter your current contact number for communication purposes.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **medical_history** (text, required/optional): Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, surgeries, etc.
* **emergency_contact_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_relation** (text, required/optional): If applicable, enter your relationship to the emergency contact, e.g. "Mother", "Father", "Spouse", etc.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant health professionals.
