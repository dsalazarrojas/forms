# Pre-Competition Medical Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pre-Competition Medical Assessment Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about an individual's health status and medical history. The form is used to identify potential health risks and ensure that necessary precautions are taken to prevent injuries or health complications.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Fill in your personal details and medical history in the designated fields.
3. Select the correct answer for each question.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the questions and answers provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
• **General Information**: This field is for providing your personal details such as your name, age, and contact information.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Medical History
• **Medical History**: Describe your past medical history, including any illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions you have experienced.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Injury History
• **Injury History**: Report any injuries you have experienced, including the nature and severity of the injury.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Health Clearance
• **Health Clearance**: Provide any medical clearance or documentation you may have, such as a doctor's note or medical certificate.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Emergency Contact
• **Emergency Contact**: Provide contact information for a family member or friend who can be reached in case of an emergency.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Medication Use
• **Medication Use**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Performance Concerns
• **Performance Concerns**: If you have any concerns or issues that may affect your performance in the activity or event, describe them here.
• (Type: text, required: false)

### Signature
• **Signature**: Select one of the three options:
	+ **Physically fit**: I am physically fit and cleared to participate in the activity or event.
	+ **Not physically fit**: I am not physically fit and may need further medical evaluation before participating.
	+ **Unable to participate**: I am unable to participate in the activity or event due to health reasons.
• (Type: select_one, required: false)

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with a medical professional before completing the form.
* This form is a pre-competition medical assessment tool, and your health and safety are the top priority.
