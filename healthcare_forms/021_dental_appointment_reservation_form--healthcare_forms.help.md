# Dental Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients or their representatives schedule dental appointments with available doctors. It is used to provide necessary information about the appointment, including patient details, appointment time and date, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required and where to find the available options.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, including all necessary fields.
3. Review the form for errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Name** (text): Enter the name of the patient, including any relevant titles or initials.
* **Patient Email** (email): Enter the patient's email address so that we can contact them with appointment reminders and other important information.
* **Patient Phone** (text): Enter the patient's phone number so that we can contact them with appointment reminders and other important information.

### Appointment Details (page 2)
* **Available Doctor** (select_one): Select the doctor you would like to schedule an appointment with from the list of available doctors.
* **Appointment Time** (date): Select the date and time of the appointment from the available options.
* **Appointment Duration** (number): Enter the expected length of the appointment in minutes.

### Payment (page 3)
* **Payment Method** (select_one): Select the payment method you would like to use for the appointment, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Insurance.
* **Payment Status** (select_multiple): Select the status of the payment, such as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.

### Additional Information (page 4)
* **Doctor Name** (text): Enter the name of the doctor you are scheduling an appointment with, if different from the available doctor selected on the previous page.
* **Appointment Date** (date): Enter the date of the appointment, if different from the previously selected date.
* **Doctor Shift** (select_multiple): Select the shift of the doctor you are scheduling an appointment with, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Doctor Specialty** (select_multiple): Select the specialty of the doctor you are scheduling an appointment with, such as General Dentistry, Orthodontics, or Oral Surgery.
* **Appointment Status** (select_one): Select the status of the appointment, such as Scheduled, Confirmed, or Cancelled.

### Confirmation (page 5)
* **Confirm Appointment** (button): Review and confirm your appointment details before submitting the form.
