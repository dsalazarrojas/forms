# Daily Health Monitoring Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Monitoring Sheet is a form designed to help you track and monitor your health metrics and habits on a daily basis. This includes vital signs, medical conditions, medications, exercise, sleep, nutrition, and water intake. Completing this form regularly will help you stay on top of your health and wellness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User ID (ID number) to identify your profile.
2. Enter your Date Of Birth for age verification purposes.
3. Measure and enter your height and weight.
4. Record your blood pressure, temperature, blood oxygen level, and blood glucose level.
5. Describe any medical conditions you have or are currently experiencing.
6. List your current medications.
7. Mention any physical exercise you did today, including duration and intensity.
8. Record your sleep duration and quality.
9. Note your current water intake for the day.
10. Add any additional notes or observations about your health.
11. Use the "Note 2" field for any additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID**: A unique identifier for your profile.
* **Date Of Birth**: Your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Height**: Measure and enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Weight**: Record your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Blood Pressure**: Measure and enter your blood pressure in millimeters of Mercury (mmHg).
* **Temperature**: Record your body temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
* **Blood Oxygen Level**: Measure and enter your blood oxygen level as a percentage (%).
* **Blood Glucose Level**: Record your blood glucose level in millimeters of glucose per deciliter (mmol/L).
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions you have or are currently experiencing.
* **Medications**: List your current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Exercise**: Mention any physical exercise you did today, including duration and intensity.
* **Sleep**: Record your sleep duration and quality.
* **Water Intake**: Record your total water intake for the day in milliliters (mL).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or observations about your health.
* **Note 2**: Use this field for any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to ensure your health data is accurate.
* Use the form regularly to track your health metrics and habits over time.
* Do not rely on this form as a substitute for professional medical advice.
* If you have concerns or issues with your health, consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.

This help guide will enable end-users to understand the purpose and usage of the "Daily Health Monitoring Sheet" form, ensuring they complete it correctly and get the most out of the tool.
