# Homeless Outreach Data Protection Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Homeless Outreach Data Protection Consent Form is a document designed to obtain informed consent from homeless clients, ensuring their sensitive information is protected and used responsibly. This form is a critical component of outreach services, as it helps establish trust and maintains confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the **General Information** page, which includes essential details about the client.
2. On the **Consent** page, select the appropriate response to indicate your willingness to participate in the service.
3. Move on to the **Contact Details** page, where you will input your contact information.
4. If applicable, complete the **Follow-Up** page to specify how you would like to be contacted and when you would like to be followed up.
5. Finally, review and complete the **Review and Sign** page with the necessary details and signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: This page includes details about you, such as your name, address, and other personal information. Ensure you input accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Consent (2)**: This field is where you express your consent to participate in the outreach service. Select 'Yes' to indicate your agreement.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Here, you will provide your contact information, including your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Follow-Up (4)**: If you want to be contacted after the initial meeting, use this page to specify the method (Email, Phone, or Other) and frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly) and duration (if applicable).
* **Contact Method (5)**: Confirm your preferred contact method, which you selected in the previous page.
* **Preferred Contact Method (6)**: Select one or multiple methods for which you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Consent Date (7)**: This field automatically fills in the current date, marking the day you provided consent.
* **Follow-Up Date (8)**: If you selected to be contacted in the future, this field will be filled with the expected follow-up date.
* **Follow-Up Method (9)**: Confirm the method (Email, Phone, or Other) for the follow-up.
* **Follow-Up Frequency (10)**: Choose the frequency for the follow-up (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Follow-Up Duration (11)**: If applicable, specify the duration of the follow-up.
* **Note (12)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, you can input them here.
* **Email (13)**: Input your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (14)**: Provide your phone number for contact.
* **Address (15)**: Input your address for contact purposes.
* **City (16)**: Input the city where you reside.
* **State (17)**: Input the state where you reside.
* **Zip (18)**: Input your zip code.
* **Consent Version (19)**: This field is typically for internal use and will be updated automatically.
* **Consent Signed By (20)**: Input the name of the person who signed the consent form.
* **Consent Date Signed (21)**: This field is automatically filled in when the form is signed.
* **Assigned User (22)**: The staff member assigned to your case.
* **Assigned Date (23)**: This field indicates when you were assigned to the staff member.
* **Review User (24)**: The staff member who reviewed your form.
* **Review Date (25)**: This field indicates when your form was reviewed by the staff member.

Please note that some fields may appear or disappear depending on your responses to other fields, ensuring that you complete the form accurately and efficiently.
