# Flu Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flu Checklist form is designed to gather information on the current state of flu season in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account the specifics of your healthcare facility's situation.
2. Be accurate and thorough in your answers, as this form will be used to inform decision-making and resource allocation.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preventive Measures**: This field is where you can describe the preventive measures your healthcare facility has in place to mitigate the spread of flu. This might include vaccination programs, hand hygiene practices, and other infection control measures.
* **Health Monitoring**: In this field, describe the health monitoring practices currently being used in your facility. This could include temperature checks, symptom tracking, and other monitoring methods used to detect flu cases.
* **Workspace Adaptations**: Here, you can outline any adaptations made to your workspace to reduce the risk of flu transmission. This might include adjusting ventilation systems, increasing cleaning frequency, or rearranging workspaces to maintain social distancing.
* **Community Engagement**: In this field, describe any community engagement efforts your facility is undertaking to educate staff and visitors about flu prevention and control.
* **Emergency Preparedness**: If your facility has an emergency preparedness plan in place, describe it here. This should include procedures for responding to flu outbreaks, including protocols for isolating affected individuals and communicating with local health authorities.
* **Flu Outbreak Reporting**: Choose whether a flu outbreak has been reported in your facility. This is a crucial field, as it will help determine the next steps in managing the flu season.
* **Flu Season Impact**: Estimate the current impact of the flu season on your facility's operations. Choose from Low, Moderate, or High to indicate the severity of the impact.
* **Flu Outbreak Status**: If your facility has experienced a flu outbreak, enter the date it began. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Flu Season Start** and **Flu Season End**: Enter the start and end dates of the current flu season, as reported by local health authorities.
* **Emergency Status**: Provide a brief description of your facility's emergency status, if applicable.
* **Flu Outbreak Reporting Date**: If a flu outbreak has been reported, enter the date it was reported.

Note: Please be accurate and thorough in your answers, as this form will be used to inform decision-making and resource allocation.
