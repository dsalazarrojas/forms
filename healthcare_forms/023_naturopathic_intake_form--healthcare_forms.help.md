# Naturopathic Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering essential information about patients seeking naturopathic medical services. It ensures that we have a comprehensive understanding of their medical history, contact details, and any relevant medical specialties or professionals involved in their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any relevant medical history, including any existing medical conditions.
2. Select any applicable medical specialties or medical conditions that you have been diagnosed with or experience.
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email.
4. If applicable, enter your physician's name, license number, and license expiration date.
5. Enter your medical record number and date.
6. Fill out the patient's address and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your existing medical history, including any relevant conditions or allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any medical conditions you've been diagnosed with or experience, such as Arthritis, Back Pain, Cancer, etc.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your medical history, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Info Phone** (`contact_info_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if different from your patient phone number).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if different from your patient phone number).
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your medical history, if applicable.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, select_one, optional): Select the medical specialty of the professional treating you, such as Naturopathic Doctor, Homeopath, etc.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter any medical license information, if applicable.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary care physician.
* **Physician License Number** (`physician_license_number`, text, required): Enter your physician's license number.
* **Physician License Expiration Date** (`physician_license_expiration`, date, optional): Enter your physician's license expiration date, if applicable.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your naturopathic doctor.
* **Doctor License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, text, required): Enter your doctor's license number.
* **Doctor License Expiration Date** (`doctor_license_expiration`, date, required): Enter your doctor's license expiration date.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter your medical record number.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your medical record.
* **Medical Record Time** (`medical_record_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your medical record.
* **Medical License Status** (`medical_license_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of your medical license, if applicable.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
