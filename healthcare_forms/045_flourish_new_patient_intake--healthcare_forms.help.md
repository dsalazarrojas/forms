# FLOURISH_New_Patient_Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FLOURISH_New_Patient_Intake form is used to collect new patient intake information, including personal and medical history, relationships, medications, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Contact" field with the patient's name and relevant contact information.
2. Answer the "Payment Method" question by selecting one of the available options (Online or Walk-in).
3. Enter the "Insurance Information" and "Billing Date" in the respective fields.
4. Provide any necessary details in the "Notes" field.
5. Add the patient's email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
6. Specify the patient's emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field (if applicable).
7. Record the date of the medical visit or appointment in the "Date" field (if applicable).
8. Indicate the patient's medical conditions using the "Medical Conditions" field.
9. Select the relevant medications using the "Medications" field.
10. Provide any further medical information in the "Medical Info" field.
11. Enter the next steps or follow-up appointments in the "Next Steps" field.
12. Enter the follow-up appointment dates and notes in the "Follow-up Appointments" and "Follow-up Notes" fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information (1)**: Enter the patient's personal information, such as name and address.
* **Medical History (2)**: Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medical History Details (3)**: Provide more detailed information about the patient's medical history, if required.
* **Patient Relationships (4)**: Specify if the patient has any relationships with other patients or practitioners.
* **Medications (5)**: Select the medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions (6)**: Indicate if the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medical History Notes (7)**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's medical history.
* **Patient Contact (8)**: Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Practitioner (9)**: Enter the patient's practitioner's contact information.
* **Next Steps (10)**: Record the next steps or follow-up appointments.
* **Follow-up Appointments (11)**: Enter the follow-up appointment dates.
* **Follow-up Notes (12)**: Enter any follow-up appointment notes.
* **Insurance Information (13)**: Enter the patient's insurance information.
* **Payment Method (14)**: Select the payment method (Online or Walk-in).
* **Billing Information (15)**: Enter any billing information.
* **Billing Date (16)**: Enter the billing date.
* **Notes (17)**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Email (18)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Emergency Contact (19)**: Enter the patient's emergency contact information.
* **Medical Info (20)**: Enter any further medical information.
* **Medical Condition 1 (21)**: Indicate if the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition 2 (22)**: Indicate if the patient has any other medical conditions.
* **Practitioner Notes (23)**: Enter any practitioner notes.
* **Date (24)**: Record the date of the medical visit or appointment.
* **Billing Date (25)**: Enter the billing date.

Please note that the required fields are marked as such, and the user should fill in the required fields first.
