# Personal Support Worker Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Support Worker Quiz is a form designed to gather information from personal support workers, including their contact details, role, experience, and training. This information will help us better understand their qualifications and provide them with more tailored support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your role from the "Role" field: Support Worker, Agency Staff, or Manager.
5. Enter your years of experience in the "Experience" field.
6. Select your level of training: Basic, Advanced, or Refresher.
7. Answer the quiz question in the "Quiz Question" field.
8. Select your options for the first quiz question from the "Option 1" field.
9. Select your options for the second quiz question from the "Option 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Role** (`Role`, select_one, required): Choose your role as a Support Worker, Agency Staff, or Manager.
  - Select Support Worker for those who provide direct support to individuals.
  - Select Agency Staff for those who work in an agency setting.
  - Select Manager for those who manage support workers.
- **Experience** (`Experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working as a support worker.
- **Training** (`Training`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of training: Basic, Advanced, or Refresher.
  - Select Basic for those who have completed basic training.
  - Select Advanced for those who have completed advanced training.
  - Select Refresher for those who have completed refresher training.
- **Quiz Question** (`Quiz Question`, text, required): Answer the quiz question as it is presented.
- **Option 1** (`Option 1`, select_multiple, required): Select your options for the first quiz question.
- **Option 2** (`Option 2`, select_multiple, required): Select your options for the second quiz question.
