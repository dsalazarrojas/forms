# tb_screening_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and assess information on patients who are being screened for tuberculosis (TB) exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before completing it.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant identifying information.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous health issues or allergies.
- **TB Exposure** (`tuberculosis_exposure`, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" if the patient has been exposed to TB, "No" otherwise.
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all symptoms that apply to the patient, such as coughing, fever, etc.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email address.
- **Screening Results** (`screening_results`, text, required: false): Enter the results of any screenings or tests performed, including any abnormal results.
- **Patient Info Date** (`patient_info_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the patient's last visit or the date they were screened.
- **Contact Info Date** (`contact_info_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the patient's contact information was last updated.
- **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes from the healthcare provider.
- **Physician Notes** (`physician_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or information for the healthcare provider's reference.
- **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or questions from the patient.
- **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical certification or credentials.
- **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the healthcare provider's name and title.
- **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the patient's last medical history update.
- **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, time, required: false): Enter the date and time the healthcare provider signed the form. 

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely to avoid any potential delays or issues.
