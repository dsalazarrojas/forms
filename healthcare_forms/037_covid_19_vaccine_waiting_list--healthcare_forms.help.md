# Covid 19 Vaccine Waiting List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or customers about their availability and preferences for receiving the Covid-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Customer Information" section with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Contact Information" section, enter your email address and/or phone number, so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
3. In the "Vaccine Information" section, provide any additional information about your vaccine preferences.
4. Select your availability for receiving the vaccine from the options in the "Availability" section.
5. If you have a preferred date, enter it in the "Date" section.
6. If you have any further comments or questions, use the "Message" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text)**: This field is where you enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text)**: Enter your email address and/or phone number so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
* **Vaccine Information (Vaccine Information, text)**: Provide any additional information about your vaccine preferences.
* **Availability (Availability, select_one)**: Select your availability for receiving the vaccine. Options are:
	+ Available to receive vaccine immediately
	+ Available to receive vaccine after 1 week
	+ Available to receive vaccine after 2 weeks
	+ Not available
* **Date (Date, date)**: If you have a preferred date, enter it here.
* **Email (Email, email)**: Enter your email address so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
* **Phone (Phone, text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
* **Message (Message, text)**: Use this field to add any further comments or questions.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple, select_multiple)**: Select all that apply. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Text Entry (Text Entry, text)**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Integer (Integer, number)**: Enter a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Decimal (Decimal, number)**: Enter a decimal number (e.g., 1.5, 2.3).
* **Date 2 (Date 2, date)**: If you have a preferred date, enter it here.
* **Time (Time, time)**: Enter a time of day (e.g., 10:00 AM, 3:00 PM).
* **Email 2 (Email 2, email)**: Enter your email address so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
* **Phone 2 (Phone 2, text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you about your vaccine availability.
* **Select One (Select One, select_one)**: Select one of the options. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Text Entry 2 (Text Entry 2, text)**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Integer 2 (Integer 2, number)**: Enter a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Decimal 2 (Decimal 2, number)**: Enter a decimal number (e.g., 1.5, 2.3).
* **Date 2 (Date 2, date)**: If you have a preferred date, enter it here.

Please take a moment to review each field carefully and provide accurate information to help us better serve you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to use the "Message" field to ask us.
