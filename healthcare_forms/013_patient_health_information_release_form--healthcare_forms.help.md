# Patient Health Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and authorization from a healthcare provider for accessing patient health records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Health Information**:
	* Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and address. This information is required to identify the patient's healthcare records.
2. **Healthcare Provider**:
	* Select the healthcare provider that has access to the patient's health records. You can choose from the following options: 
	* Name: The name of the healthcare provider
	* Role: The role of the healthcare provider
	* Contact Number: The contact number of the healthcare provider
3. **Authorization Date**:
	* Enter the date on which the authorization was given by the patient or their guardian.
4. **Expiration Date**:
	* Enter the date on which the authorization expires.
5. **Signature**:
	* Provide a signature from the patient or their guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Health Information** (Patient Health Information, Text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
* **Healthcare Provider** (Healthcare Provider, Select One, not required): Select the healthcare provider that has access to the patient's health records.
* **Authorization Date** (Authorization Date, Date, not required): Enter the date on which the authorization was given by the patient or their guardian.
* **Expiration Date** (Expiration Date, Date, not required): Enter the date on which the authorization expires.
* **Signature** (Signature, Text, not required): Provide a signature from the patient or their guardian.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays or complications in the healthcare process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form should be signed by the patient or their guardian to ensure that the patient's health information is being accessed with their consent.
