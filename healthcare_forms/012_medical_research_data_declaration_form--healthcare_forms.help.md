# Medical Research Data Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect participant's personal and medical information for medical research studies. It is used to ensure the accuracy and completeness of the data and to understand participant's medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- Fill out the form in sections, from top to bottom.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask your research study coordinator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information**: This section is used to collect your personal information, such as your contact details.
  - <label>Participant Information</label> (required): This field is required if it's marked as so in the form.
  - <label>Contact Information</label> (optional): This field is used to enter your contact details for follow-up.

- **Medical History**: This section is used to describe your medical history.
  - <label>Medical History</label> (optional): This field is used to describe your medical history.

- **Medical Conditions**: This section is used to list all medical conditions you have experienced or have a history of.
  - <label>Medical Conditions</label> (optional): This field is used to select all medical conditions you have experienced or have a history of.

- **Medication Usage**: This section is used to list all medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past 5 years.
  - <label>Medication Usage</label> (optional): This field is used to select all medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past 5 years.

- **Medical Procedure**: This section is used to describe any past medical procedures you have had.
  - <label>Medical Procedure</label> (optional): This field is used to describe any past medical procedures you have had.

- **Allergies**: This section is used to enter your allergies.
  - <label>Allergies</label> (optional): This field is used to enter your allergies.

- **Medical Research History**: This section is used to list any medical research participation you have already participated in.
  - <label>Medical Research History</label> (optional): This field is used to select all medical research participation you have already participated in.

- **Medical Research History Detail**: This section is used to provide details of your previous medical research participation.
  - <label>Medical Research History Detail</label> (optional): This field is used to provide details of your previous medical research participation.

- **Previous Study**: This section is used to enter details of the previous study you have participated in.
  - <label>Previous Study</label> (required): This field is used to select the previous study you have participated in.

- **Previous Study Date**: This section is used to enter the date of your previous study.
  - <label>Previous Study Date</label> (optional): This field is used to enter the date of your previous study.

- **Previous Study Location**: This section is used to enter the location of your previous study.
  - <label>Previous Study Location</label> (optional): This field is used to select the location of your previous study.

- **Previous Study Institution**: This section is used to enter the institution where you participated in your previous study.
  - <label>Previous Study Institution</label> (optional): This field is used to enter the institution where you participated in your previous study.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask your research study coordinator for assistance.
- The fields marked as 'required' are necessary to fill out.
- The fields marked as 'optional' are not necessary to fill out, but can be filled out if you want to provide more information.
