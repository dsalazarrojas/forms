# Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) Survey is a self-assessment tool for healthcare professionals to gauge their knowledge and skills in managing cardiac emergencies. The survey is designed to identify areas where additional training or support is needed to enhance team performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, based on your current level of knowledge and skills.
2. Choose the options that best describe your confidence or proficiency level in each question.
3. If you are unsure or do not know the answer to a question, select the "Not confident" or "Not familiar" option.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving ACLS training in the "Training Improvement Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Professional Name** (ID 2): Your full name as a healthcare professional.
* **Professional License Number** (ID 3): Your medical license or certification number.
* **Job Role** (ID 4): Your current position in healthcare (e.g. Physician, Nurse, Paramedic, etc.).
* **Years in Practice** (ID 5): How many years you have been practicing in healthcare.
* **ACLS Certified** (ID 7): Are you currently certified in ACLS?
* **Last Certification Date** (ID 8): When was your last ACLS certification (optional).
* **Certification Expiry Date** (ID 9): When does your ACLS certification expire (optional).
* **Cardiac Arrest Protocol Knowledge** (ID 11): How confident are you in managing cardiac arrest protocols.
* **Medication Administration Knowledge** (ID 12): How familiar are you with ACLS medications and dosages.
* **Rhythm Recognition Ability** (ID 13): How proficient are you in recognizing cardiac rhythms.
* **Airway Management Skills** (ID 14): How skilled are you in advanced airway management.
* **Cardiac Emergency Response Confidence** (ID 16): How confident are you in responding to cardiac emergencies.
* **Team Leadership Ability** (ID 17): How comfortable are you leading a resuscitation team.
* **Training Update Frequency** (ID 19): How often do you participate in ACLS training updates.
* **Training Improvement Suggestions** (ID 20): What would improve ACLS training effectiveness.
