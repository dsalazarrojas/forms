# NICU Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NICU Discharge Form is designed to collect essential information and instructions for discharging patients from the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and complete information, follow these steps:

1.  **Patient Information Page**: Enter the patient's personal details, such as their name, date of birth, and contact information.
2.  **Patient Medical History Page**: Record any significant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures that may impact the patient's care.
3.  **Discharge Details Page**: Enter the date and time of discharge, the reason for discharge, and any post-discharge instructions or medication information.
4.  **Follow Up Instructions Page**: Provide instructions for follow-up care, including any scheduled appointments or medication regimens.
5.  **Follow Up Info Page**: Enter any additional follow-up information, such as contact details for family or caregivers.
6.  **Hospital Info Page**: Record any hospital-specific information, such as the room number or nurse's notes.

*   **Patient Information Page (patient_information_page)**:
    *  <b>**Patient Name**</b>: This field is for entering the patient's name as listed on their medical records.
    *  <b>**Date of Birth**</b>: Enter the patient's date of birth for accurate and up-to-date information.
    *  <b>**Contact Information**</b>: Include the patient's contact details, such as phone number or email, for easy communication.
*   **Patient Medical History Page (patient_medical_history_page)**:
    *  <b>**Medical Conditions**</b>: This field is for detailing any significant medical conditions or allergies that may affect the patient's care.

*   **Discharge Details Page (discharge_details_page)**:
    *  <b>**Date and Time of Discharge**</b>: Enter the exact date and time of the patient's discharge from the NICU.
