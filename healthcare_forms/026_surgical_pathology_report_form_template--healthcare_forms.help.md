# Surgical Pathology Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Pathology Report Form Template is designed to document the details of a surgical pathology report. This form is used by pathologists, lab technicians, and other healthcare professionals to gather and record information about a specimen, including specimen details, pathologist details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide specimen details such as specimen ID, description, and collection date/time.
2. Enter pathologist details such as name, institution, and comments.
3. Select the specimen condition from the available options (normal, benign, or malignant).
4. Enter a brief description of the specimen.
5. Select the diagnosis from the available options.
6. Enter hospital details such as name and department.
7. Enter diagnostic lab details such as name.
8. Enter the diagnostic date.
9. Select the specimen collection time.
10. Select the collection date.
11. Enter pathologist's name and institution.
12. Enter diagnosis date.
13. Select the specimen condition (normal, benign, or malignant).
14. Enter any additional comments or notes from the pathologist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Specimen Details**: Enter the unique identifier of the specimen, a brief description, and the date and time it was collected.
* **Pathologist Details**: Enter the name of the pathologist who examined the specimen, their institution, and any comments they may have.
* **Specimen Condition**: Select the condition of the specimen (normal, benign, or malignant).
* **Specimen Description**: Enter a brief description of the specimen.
* **Diagnosis**: Select the diagnosis from the available options.
* **Hospital Details**: Enter the name of the hospital where the specimen was examined and the department it was from.
* **Diagnostic Lab**: Enter the name of the diagnostic lab where the examination was performed.
* **Diagnostic Date**: Enter the date the examination was performed.
* **Specimen Collection Time**: Enter the time the specimen was collected.
* **Specimen Collection Date**: Enter the date the specimen was collected.
* **Pathologist Name**: Enter the name of the pathologist who examined the specimen.
* **Pathologist Institution**: Enter the institution of the pathologist who examined the specimen.
* **Diagnosis Date**: Enter the date the diagnosis was made.
* **Specimen Type**: Enter the type of specimen (e.g., tissue, fluid, etc.).
* **Specimen Submitted**: Enter any additional information about the specimen.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the pathologist.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the pathologist.
