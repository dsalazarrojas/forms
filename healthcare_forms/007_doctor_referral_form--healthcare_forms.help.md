# Doctor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Doctor Referral Form. This form is designed to facilitate referrals between doctors to ensure that patients receive the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Patient DOB** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the doctor's name in the **Doctor Name** field.
4. Select the doctor's specialty in the **Doctor Specialty** field.
5. Enter the patient's medical condition in the **Patient Condition** field.
6. Specify the referral reason in the **Referral Reason** field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
8. Enter the doctor's phone number in the **Doctor Phone** field.
9. Enter the hospital name where the referral will take place in the **Hospital Name** field.
10. Enter the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.
11. Enter the doctor's address in the **Doctor Address** field.
12. Enter the patient's email address in the **Patient Email** field.
13. Enter the doctor's email address in the **Doctor Email** field.
14. Select the referral date in the **Referral Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
15. Enter a brief note about the patient in the **Patient Note** field.
16. Enter a brief note for the doctor in the **Doctor Note** field.
17. Select the patient's medical group in the **Patient Medical Group** field.
18. Select the doctor's medical group in the **Doctor Medical Group** field.
19. Enter the emergency contact's name in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
20. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
21. Select the emergency contact's relationship with the patient in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
22. Enter the emergency contact's address in the **Emergency Contact Address** field.
23. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.
24. Enter a brief note about the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Note** field.
25. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient DOB** (Date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Doctor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the doctor's name who is referring the patient.
* **Doctor Specialty** (Text, Optional): Select the doctor's specialty, e.g., cardiology, oncology, etc.
* **Patient Condition** (Text, Required): Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Referral Reason** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief reason for the referral.
* **Patient Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Doctor Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Hospital Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the hospital's name where the referral will take place.
* **Patient Address** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Doctor Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the doctor's address.
* **Patient Email** (Email, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Referral Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of referral in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional note about the patient.
* **Doctor Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional note for the doctor.
* **Patient Medical Group** (Text, Optional): Select the patient's medical group, e.g., general practitioner, specialist, etc.
* **Doctor Medical Group** (Text, Optional): Select the doctor's medical group, e.g., general practitioner, specialist, etc.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact with the patient, e.g., son, daughter, etc.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional note about the emergency contact.
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Click to submit the form.
