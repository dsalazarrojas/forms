# Immunization Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Appointment Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect and record patient information, schedule appointments, and track vaccination progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information**: This is the first step in completing the form. Please enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant medical history.
2. **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and any relevant email or address.
3. **Vaccination Date**: Choose the date of the vaccination appointment. This can be a single date or a range of dates for follow-up appointments.
4. **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the appointment, if applicable.
5. **Vaccination Type**: Select the type of vaccination being administered (e.g., Flu, COVID-19, or Other).
6. **Dose 1 Date**: Enter the date of the first dose of the vaccination, if applicable.
7. **Dose 2 Date**: Enter the date of the second dose of the vaccination, if applicable.
8. **Follow-up Date**: Choose the date for any follow-up appointments.
9. **Contact Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number, if applicable.
10. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions for the healthcare team.
