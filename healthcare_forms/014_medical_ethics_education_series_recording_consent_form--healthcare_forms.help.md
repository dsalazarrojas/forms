# Medical Ethics Education Series Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the recording of medical ethics education series. It is meant to be completed by participants and providers to provide information and consent for the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the form carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Enter the participant's name and email address (if applicable).
3. Choose a date and time for the recording.
4. Sign and date your participant's signature (if they have one).
5. Sign and date your provider's signature (if they have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): Enter the participant's name, email address (if applicable), and other relevant details.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): This is a space for the participant to sign their name to confirm their consent. Please have the participant sign and date their name.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`): Choose a date and time for the recording. This should be accurate and precise.
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`): Enter the provider's name, title, and contact information.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`): This is a space for the provider to sign their name to confirm their consent. Please have the provider sign and date their name.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their ID or government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter the participant's email address (if applicable).
* **Provider Signature**: Enter the provider's signature to confirm their consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the form carefully before completing it.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Have the participant and provider sign and date their respective signatures.
* Keep a record of the completed form for future reference.
