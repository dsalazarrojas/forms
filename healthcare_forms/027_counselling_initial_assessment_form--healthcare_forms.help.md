# counselling_initial_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counselling_initial_assessment_form is used to collect information from patients for initial assessments, helping healthcare professionals develop an effective treatment plan tailored to the patient's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field carefully, ensuring all required information is provided.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and medical knowledge.
3. Be thorough in providing details about your medical and mental health history, medication usage, and other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional):
  Provide your personal details, such as name, contact information, and other identifying information.

* **Medical History (Page 1)** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional):
  Share your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or treatments.

* **Mental Health Issues (Page 1)** (`mental_health_issues`, `text`, optional):
  Describe any mental health issues you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

* **Medication Usage (Page 2)** (`medication_usage`, `select_one`, optional):
  Indicate whether you are currently using medications, using them partially, or not using them at all.

* **Treatment Goals (Page 2)** (`treatment_goals`, `text`, optional):
  Outline your goals for treatment and therapy.

* **Medical Contacts (Page 2)** (`medical_contacts`, `text`, optional):
  Provide contact information for your medical contacts.

* **Mental Health Contacts (Page 3)** (`mental_health_contacts`, `text`, optional):
  Share contact information for your mental health contacts.

* **Family History (Page 3)** (`family_history`, `text`, optional):
  Share any relevant family medical and mental health history.

* **Social History (Page 4)** (`social_history`, `text`, optional):
  Describe your social situation, including relationships and support systems.

* **Personal History (Page 4)** (`personal_history`, `text`, optional):
  Share any relevant personal medical and mental health history.

* **Medical History Notes (Page 4)** (`medical_history_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your medical history.

* **Mental Status (Page 5)** (`mental_status`, `text`, optional):
  Share your current mental state and any relevant issues.

* **Mental Status Notes (Page 5)** (`mental_status_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your mental status.

* **Treatment Plan (Page 5)** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, optional):
  Outline your treatment goals and plan.

* **Mental Status Goals (Page 6)** (`mental_status_goals`, `text`, optional):
  Specify your mental status goals.

* **Medication History (Page 6)** (`medication_history`, `text`, optional):
  Share your medication history.

* **Medication Usage Notes (Page 7)** (`medication_usage_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your medication usage.

* **Family Dynamics (Page 7)** (`family_dynamics`, `text`, optional):
  Share information about your family dynamics.

* **Family Dynamics Notes (Page 8)** (`family_dynamics_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your family dynamics.

* **Social Support System (Page 8)** (`social_support_system`, `text`, optional):
  Describe your social support system.

* **Social Support System Notes (Page 9)** (`support_system_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your social support system.

* **Mental Status Assessment (Page 9)** (`mental_status_assessment`, `text`, optional):
  Share your mental status assessment.

* **Mental Status Assessment Notes (Page 10)** (`mental_status_assessment_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your mental status assessment.

* **Discharge Summary (Page 10)** (`discharge_summary`, `text`, optional):
  Summarize your discharge.

* **Discharge Notes (Page 11)** (`discharge_notes`, `text`, optional):
  Add any additional notes about your discharge.
