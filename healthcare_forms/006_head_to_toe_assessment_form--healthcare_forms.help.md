# Head To Toe Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Head To Toe Assessment Form is a standardized tool used in healthcare settings to evaluate the status of a patient's head, torso, abdomen, back, arms, and legs. It's designed for healthcare professionals to collect detailed information about a patient's condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by completing the form page by page, working from top to bottom.
2. Please refer to the page's specific instructions and hints provided for each field.
3. Answer all required fields accurately and completely.
4. Use the provided options to select the most suitable response.
5. If an option is not listed, choose "Other (please specify)" or enter a brief description.

* **Page One (Page One)** (`page_one`, text, optional): This is the first page of the assessment. Please enter any comments or concerns regarding the patient's overall condition.
* **Page Two (Page Two)** (`page_two`, email, required): Please enter the patient's email address (or alternative contact information) so the healthcare provider can reach out to you for further clarification or updates. This field is essential for communication purposes.

* **Page Four (Page Four)** (`page_four`, text, optional): Enter comments or concerns about the patient's torso area, such as pain or discomfort.
* **Page Five (Page Five)** (`page_five`, time, optional): Record the time spent on this activity or task. If you're unsure, enter a rough estimate.

* **Page Seven (Page Seven)** (`page_seven`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options regarding the patient's condition in this area.
* **Page Eight (Page Eight)** (`page_eight`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list regarding the patient's condition.
* **Page Nine (Page Nine)** (`page_nine`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition, particularly regarding the back area.
* **Page Ten (Page Ten)** (`page_ten`, text, optional): This is the final page for entering any comments or concerns regarding the patient's overall condition.
