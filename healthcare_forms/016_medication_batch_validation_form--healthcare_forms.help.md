# Medication Batch Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medication Batch Validation Form is used to verify the quality and safety of medication batches before release or distribution. This form helps ensure that the medication is safe for consumption and meets quality standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the batch number to identify the specific batch being validated.
2. Enter the product name and product code to accurately identify the medication product.
3. Enter the expiration date to ensure it matches the product's actual expiration date.
4. Optional: Select the verification signature and verification date to confirm the validation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the medication batch being validated.
* **Batch Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the batch being validated.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the medication product being validated.
* **Product Code**: Enter the code assigned to the medication product being validated.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the actual expiration date of the medication product. Ensure it matches the product's packaging or labeling.
* **Verification Signature**: Select one of the following options to confirm the validation process:
	+ Dr. John
	+ Dr. Jane
	+ Pharmacist
* **Verification Date**: Enter the date you verified the medication batch.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields to validate the medication batch.
- Double-check the expiration date against the product's packaging or labeling.
- Select the correct verification signature to confirm the validation process.
- This form is for internal use only; please do not share it with unauthorized personnel.
