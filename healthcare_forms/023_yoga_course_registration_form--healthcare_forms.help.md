# Yoga Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students to register for a yoga course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field to identify yourself.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Student Phone" field to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field to allow us to communicate with you.
4. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field to verify your age requirement for the course.
5. Choose the name of the course you want to register for in the "Course Name" field.
6. Enter the name of the instructor who is leading the course in the "Instructor Name" field.
7. Enter the email address of the instructor in the "Instructor Email" field to facilitate communication.
8. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click "Submit" to register for the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding course updates and reminders.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can send course materials and notifications.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth to verify that you meet the age requirement for the course. Please use the date format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Choose the name of the course you want to register for from the available options.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): If known, please enter the name of the instructor who is leading the course.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the instructor if you have it, to facilitate communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
- We will review and verify your registration before confirming your spot in the course.
