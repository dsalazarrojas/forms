# Medical Record Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Audit Form is a tool used to collect and review patient medical information. This form is designed to ensure that patient records are accurate and up-to-date, and to help healthcare providers identify and address any issues that may arise during patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field (Field 11).
2. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field (Field 15).
3. Choose the clinical status of the patient in the "Clinical Status" field (Field 17).
4. Select the medical provider's ID and specialty in the "Medical Provider ID" and "Medical Specialty" fields (Fields 18 and 19, respectively).
5. Enter the medical provider's name in the "Medical Provider Name" field (Field 20).
6. Enter the medical provider's email and phone number in the "Medical Email" and "Medical Phone" fields (Fields 22 and 23, respectively).
7. Enter the patient's address in the "Medical Address" field (Field 24).
8. Review and verify the accuracy of the patient's medical information.
9. Add any additional medical notes or comments in the "Medical Note" field (Field 21).
10. Finally, enter the date and time of the medical record audit in the "Date" and "Time" fields (Fields 12 and 13, respectively), and enter the date and time of the audit in the "Date Time" field (Field 14).

* **Clinical Status** (Field 17): Select the current clinical status of the patient.

* **Medical Specialty** (Field 19): Select the medical specialty of the provider who last saw the patient.
* **Medical Provider Name** (Field 20): Enter the name of the medical provider who last saw the patient.
* **Medical Email** (Field 22): Enter the email address of the medical provider who last saw the patient.
* **Medical Phone** (Field 23): Enter the phone number of the medical provider who last saw the patient.
