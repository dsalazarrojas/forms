# Patient Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for patients' appointments. It includes fields for collecting the patient's name, appointment date, and appointment time. Please fill it out with the required information for your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select a date for your appointment using the "Appointment Date" field. This field accepts dates in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Select a time for your appointment using the "Appointment Time" field. This field accepts times in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your scheduled appointment in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
