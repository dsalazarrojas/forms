# Biotics Research Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotics Research Assessment Form is a survey designed to gather information about your health and lifestyle habits. The data collected will aid researchers in understanding the factors influencing human health and microbiome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by reviewing each section of the form carefully.
2. For each field, read the hint provided and select the most accurate response based on your personal experience and knowledge.
3. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, and do not duplicate or omit any information.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask the research team for clarification.
