# Patient Advocacy Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Patient Advocacy Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the program goals and objectives to ensure you understand what needs to be evaluated.
2. Complete the form by providing feedback on the training's effectiveness in achieving the program goals.
3. Enter any comments or suggestions for improvement in the Comments field.
4. Enter a score for the overall effectiveness of the training in the Score field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Comments** (`patient_advocacy_training_form_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions for improvement in this field. This field is optional, but providing feedback is highly encouraged.
* **Score** (`patient_advocacy_training_evaluation_form_score`, `number`, optional): Enter a score for the overall effectiveness of the training. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide a numerical value to evaluate the training's success. The score should reflect how well the training met the program goals and objectives.
* **First page** (`First page`, `note`, optional): This is a placeholder page to accommodate additional fields that may not fit on the standard pages. Please do not fill out this field as it is not intended for user input.
