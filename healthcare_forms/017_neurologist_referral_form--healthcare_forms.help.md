# neurologist_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurologist Referral Form is designed to facilitate communication between healthcare providers and specialists in the field of neurology. This form enables efficient and accurate referrals, ensuring that patients receive the necessary attention from the right medical professionals. By completing this form, you will be able to provide essential information about the patient, including their name, birth date, medical history, and any relevant contact details. The form also helps in assessing the patient's medical specialty, ensuring that the referral is made to the appropriate specialist.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Neurologist Referral Form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is a required field, and it is essential to provide the patient's full name (including their surname and any middle names) for accurate record-keeping and communication.
2. Next, enter the patient's birth date in the "Birth Date" field. This is also a required field and helps in understanding the patient's medical history.
3. In the "Medical History" field, provide a brief description of the patient's medical history. This is a required field and should include any relevant medical information, such as allergies, previous treatments, or medical conditions.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the referring provider in the "Referring Provider" field. This field is not required, but it provides valuable information about the patient's current medical care and facilitates communication between healthcare providers.
5. If necessary, enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This field is not required, but it helps in establishing communication with the patient or their family members.
6. Finally, indicate the patient's medical specialty in the "Medical Specialty" field. This is a required field and helps in ensuring that the referral is made to the appropriate specialist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): This is the field where you enter the patient's full name (including their surname and any middle names) for accurate record-keeping and communication.
* **Birth Date** (date, required): This field requires the patient's birth date to help understand their medical history.
* **Medical History** (note, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any allergies, previous treatments, or medical conditions.
* **Referring Provider** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the provider who referred the patient for further medical attention.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): If necessary, enter the patient's contact number to facilitate communication with the patient or their family members.
* **Medical Specialty** (select one, required): Select the medical specialty that best suits the patient's needs, such as "Yes" for a neurologist.
