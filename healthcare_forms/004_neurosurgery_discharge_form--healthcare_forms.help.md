# # Neurosurgery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurosurgery Discharge Form is designed to provide essential information about a patient's neurosurgery procedure and post-discharge instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing the patient's details in the **Patient Details** page.
- Document the **Surgical Procedure** performed on the patient.
- List any **Complications** that occurred during the procedure.
- Select the **Discharge Status** (e.g., status 1 or status 2).
- Enter the **Discharge Date** and **Discharge Time** (if applicable).
- Enter your name as the **Surgeon** who performed the procedure.
- Specify the **Surgical Unit** where the procedure was performed (if applicable).
- Choose the **Anaesthesia Level** and **Anaesthesia Type** used during the procedure.
- Enter the **Anaesthesia Dose** administered.
- Sign the form with your **Patient Signature** to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
- Sign the form with your **Doctor Signature** to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information to identify the patient.
* **Surgical Procedure (2)**: Describe the surgical procedure performed on the patient, including any complications that occurred during the procedure.
* **Complications (3)**: Select any complications that occurred during the procedure. This can include but not limited to: 
    * Complications 1
    * Complications 2
    * Complications 3
* **Discharge Status (4)**: Select the status of the discharge, for example: 
    * Status 1
    * Status 2
* **Discharge Date (5)**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Surgeon (6)**: Enter your name as the surgeon who performed the procedure.
* **Surgical Unit (7)**: If applicable, specify the name of the surgical unit where the procedure was performed.
* **Anaesthesia Level (8)**: Choose the level of anaesthesia used during the procedure. For example: 
    * Level 1
    * Level 2
    * Level 3
* **Anaesthesia Type (9)**: Enter the type of anaesthesia used during the procedure.
* **Anaesthesia Dose (10)**: Enter the amount of anaesthesia used during the procedure.
* **Patient Signature (11)**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Doctor Signature (12)**: Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Facility Licence (13)**: If applicable, choose the facility licence.
* **Facility Address (14)**: Enter the facility's address.
* **Contact Number (15)**: Enter the contact number for the facility.
* **Fax Number (16)**: Enter the fax number for the facility (if applicable).
* **Email (17)**: Enter the facility's email address.
* **Emergency Contact (18)**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Number (19)**: Enter the emergency contact number.
* **Patient Consent (20)**: If applicable, select the patient's consent. For example: 
    * Consent 1
    * Consent 2
    * Consent 3
* **Patient Inform (21)**: Enter any additional information about the patient's condition.
* **Follow Up Instructions (22)**: Select any follow-up instructions for the patient, for example:
    * Follow up 1
    * Follow up 2
    * Follow up 3
* **Follow Up Contact (23)**: Enter the contact information for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Date (24)**: Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Time (25)**: Enter the follow-up time.
