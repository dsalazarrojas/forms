# Healthcare Professional Effectiveness Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture feedback from patients about their experience with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the unique identifier of the patient.
2. Enter the name of the healthcare professional providing the services.
3. Rate your experience with the healthcare professional (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Provide additional feedback notes about the experience.
5. Enter the date of the visit.
6. Enter the time of the visit.
7. Select the medical specialty(ies) of the healthcare professional.
8. If applicable, enter the date when feedback was given.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Id** (`patient_identifier`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier of the patient.
* **Healthcare Professional** (`healthcare_professional`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare professional providing the services.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with the healthcare professional as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback Notes** (`feedback_notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the experience.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the medical specialty(ies) of the healthcare professional (e.g., General Practice, Cardiology, etc.).
* **Feedback Given** (`feedback_given`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the date when feedback was given.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure reliable results.
* If you need to fill out the form for multiple patients or healthcare professionals, repeat the form for each individual case.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your healthcare provider or administrative staff for assistance.
