# music_therapy_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering patients for music therapy sessions. It is used to collect the patient's information and schedule music therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter your contact phone number in the correct format (###-####-#####).
5. Enter the name of your music therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification card or other official documents.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification card or other official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for music therapy session reminders and updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format ###-####-##### (e.g., 555-123-4567).
* **Therapist's Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of your music therapist.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure correct music therapy scheduling and communication.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your music therapist for clarification.
