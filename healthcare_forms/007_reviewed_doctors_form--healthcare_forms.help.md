# reviewed_doctors_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "reviewed_doctors_form" is used to collect contact information from doctors who have reviewed a medical case. This form helps ensure that the reviews are valid and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Name" field with the doctor's name.
2. Enter the doctor's email address in the "email" field.
3. Optionally, provide the doctor's phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name** (text, required: false)
* This field is where you enter the doctor's name.

### **email** (email, required: true)
* This field is for the doctor's email address.
* Please enter a valid email address to ensure that the review is sent to the correct email.

### **phone** (text, required: false)
* This field is optional, but can be used to enter the doctor's phone number.
* Please enter the doctor's phone number if you have it.
* If you don't have the doctor's phone number, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (marked with ***) to ensure that the review is valid.
* If you have any issues while filling the form, feel free to contact our support team.
