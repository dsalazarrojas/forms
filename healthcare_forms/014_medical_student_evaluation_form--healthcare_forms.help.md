# Medical Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Student Evaluation Form is a document used by professors to evaluate medical students' performance, attendance, and conduct. The form aims to provide a comprehensive assessment of the student's progress and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form for each medical student, using the required information provided.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing specific examples or details when possible.
3. Make sure to save your completed form after submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **medical_student_evaluation_form**:
    - A text box for the title of the evaluation, which should reflect the student's performance and any specific areas of focus.
- **student_name**: 
    - The name of the student being evaluated.
- **professor_name**:
    - The name of the professor conducting the evaluation.
- **student_performance**:
    - A text field for the student's performance evaluation, covering topics such as clinical skills, communication, and problem-solving abilities.
- **student_attendance**:
    - A text field for the student's attendance evaluation, including any notable absences or tardiness.
- **student_conduct**:
    - A text field for the student's conduct evaluation, covering any instances of unprofessional behavior or conflict resolution.
- **evaluation_comments**:
    - A text field for additional comments or feedback from the professor.
- **recommendation**:
    - A select-one field asking the professor to recommend the student for further studies or future positions.
    - Please select 'Yes' if you recommend the student, and 'No' otherwise.
- **evaluation_date**:
    - The date the evaluation was completed.
- **evaluation_time**:
    - The time the evaluation was completed.
- **evaluation_note**:
    - A free-text field for any additional comments or feedback from the professor. 

## Tips

- Please ensure that each field is filled out thoroughly and accurately, taking into account the student's performance and conduct.
- If you need to refer back to the student in the future, make sure to save the completed form.
- If you're unsure about any field or have further questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
