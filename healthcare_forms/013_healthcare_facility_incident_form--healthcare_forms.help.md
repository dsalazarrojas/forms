# Healthcare Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur in healthcare facilities. It helps to ensure that incidents are reported accurately and consistently, enabling the relevant authorities to take necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient information section with details about the patient involved in the incident.
2. Enter the facility information in the facility information section.
3. Provide information about the incident in the incident information section.
4. Enter details about the staff member involved in the incident in the staff information section.
5. Fill in the location and time sections with the details about where and when the incident occurred.
6. Choose the incident type and outcome from the available options.
7. Enter the incident description, location, and any other relevant details.
8. Fill in the staff contact information section with details about the staff member involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (Required)**: Enter the patient's name, age, gender, and race.
* **Facility Info (Required)**: Enter the facility's name and address.
* **Incident Info (Required)**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Staff Info (Required)**: Enter the staff member's name, title, role, and contact information.
* **Location Info (Required)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Date Occurred (Required)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time Occurred (Required)**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Facility Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the incident occurred.
* **Facility Address (Required)**: Enter the address of the healthcare facility where the incident occurred.
* **Facility Phone (Required)**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Email (Required)**: Enter the email address of the healthcare facility.
* **Incident Date (Required)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time (Required)**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type (Required)**: Choose the type of incident that occurred.
* **Incident Description (Required)**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location (Required)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Staff Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the staff member involved.
* **Staff Title (Required)**: Enter the title of the staff member involved.
* **Staff Role (Required)**: Enter the role of the staff member involved.
* **Staff Phone (Required)**: Enter the phone number of the staff member involved.
* **Staff Email (Required)**: Enter the email address of the staff member involved.
* **Patient Gender (Required)**: Choose the gender of the patient involved.
* **Patient Age (Required)**: Enter the age of the patient involved.
* **Patient Race (Required)**: Choose the race of the patient involved.
* **Incident Outcome (Required)**: Choose the outcome of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location of the incident (e.g. room number).
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Staff Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the staff member involved.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as it is used to report incidents that may require further investigation or action.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Make sure to save the form after completing it, so you can reference it later if needed.
