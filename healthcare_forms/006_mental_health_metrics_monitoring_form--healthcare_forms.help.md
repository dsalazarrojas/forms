# mental_health_metrics_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track mental health metrics and data from patients or individuals. It is intended to monitor and analyze mental health status, symptoms, and medical history to inform treatment decisions and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page, "Client Data", where you will be asked to provide basic information about the patient or individual.
2. Proceed to the next page, "Mental Health Metrics", where you will be asked to select from a list of options regarding their mental health status.
3. On the "Symptoms" page, list any symptoms the patient or individual is experiencing.
4. On the "Medications" page, list any medications they are currently taking.
5. On the "Medical History" page, record any relevant medical history.
6. Finally, on the "Mental Status" page(s), select the mental status that best describes the patient or individual's current condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Data** (`client_data`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient or individual's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Mental Health Metrics** (`mental_health_metrics`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the list of options that describe the patient or individual's mental health status, such as "Optimistic" or "Sad".
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any symptoms the patient or individual is experiencing, such as "Fatigue" or "Anxiety".
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications the patient or individual is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Record any relevant medical history.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the list of options that best describe the patient or individual's mental status, such as "Good" or "Poor".
* **Mental Status 2** (`mental_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the list of options that best describe the patient or individual's mental status (this field appears to be a duplicate of the previous "Mental Status" field and may not be necessary; please review and confirm its purpose and relevance).

## Tips
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when completing the form.
* If unsure about any of the fields, refer to the patient or individual's records or consult with a healthcare professional.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains accurate and relevant.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare team or form administrator.
