# Research Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to inform participants about the purpose, benefits, and risks of the study. It ensures that participants understand their rights and safety are protected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review the form and ensure that you understand each section before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, making sure to provide all required information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Provide your name, contact information, and any other identifying information as required.
* **Research Study** (`research_study`, select_one, optional): Check one of the two options: 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the study is active or inactive.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_information`, text, optional): Provide contact information for researchers involved in the study.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the date of your consent.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, optional): Provide the address or location of the study.
* **Participant Statement** (`participant_statement`, text, optional): A statement about your rights as a participant.
* **Researcher Statement** (`researcher_statement`, text, optional): A statement about the researcher's rights.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional study details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide contact information for future reference.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, optional): Provide the research institution's information.
* **Research Institutional Review** (`research_institutional_review`, text, optional): A statement about the research institution's review.
* **Other Institutional Review** (`other_institutional_review`, text, optional): A statement about the other institution's review.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Your signature (your name or initials).
* **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, text, optional): Researcher signature (researcher's name or initials).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Date of signing.
* **Study ID** (`study_id`, text, optional): Study ID number.
* **Research Study Name** (`research_study_name`, text, optional): Research Study Name.
* **Study Status** (`study_status`, select_one, optional): Check one of the two options: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Institutional Review Date** (`institutional_review_date`, date, optional): Date of Institutional Review.
* **Institutional Review Version** (`institutional_review_version`, text, optional): Version of Institutional Review.
* **Other Institutional Review Version** (`other_institutional_review_version`, text, optional): Version of Other Institutional Review.
* **Participant Version** (`participant_version`, text, optional): Participant version of Institutional Review.
* **Institutional Review Status** (`institutional_review_status`, select_one, optional): Check one of the two options: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Participant Review** (`participant_review`, text, optional): Participant review (yes/no).
* **Participant Review Status** (`participant_review_status`, select_one, optional): Check one of the two options: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
