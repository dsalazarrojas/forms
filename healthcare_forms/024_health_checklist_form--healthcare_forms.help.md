# Health Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Checklist Form is a tool to collect and assess information on various health-related topics to support healthcare providers in making informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question or field carefully and ensure you understand the intended use of the information being requested.
2. Enter relevant and accurate information in each field, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. If you are unsure or have questions about a particular field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.
4. Once completed, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Health
* **General Health**: Describe your overall health status, including any current medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, including past illnesses, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or substances.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Family Medications**: List any medications taken by family members.
* **Family Medical Conditions**: Describe any family medical conditions, such as heart disease or cancer.
* **Social Habits**: Discuss your social habits, including smoking, exercise, or substance use.
* **Lifestyle**: Describe your daily habits and routines.
* **Health Goals**: State your health goals, such as losing weight or reducing stress.
* **Physical Activity**: Report on your physical activity level.
* **Mental Health**: Describe your mental health status.
* **Vaccinations**: List any vaccinations you have received.
* **Past Medical History**: Provide any relevant past medical history, including illnesses or treatments.
* **Social Support**: Describe your social support network.
* **Health Care Provider**: Identify your primary health care provider.
* **Emergency Services**: List any emergency services you use.
* **Transportation**: Describe your transportation options.
* **Housing**: Describe your living situation.
* **Income**: Report on your income level.
* **Education**: Provide any relevant educational background.
* **Employment**: Describe your employment status.
* **Insurance**: List any insurance coverage you have.
* **Emergency Plan**: Describe your emergency plan, including any emergency contact information.
* **Next Steps**: Outline any next steps or recommendations you would like to take for your health.
