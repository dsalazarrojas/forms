# Release And Waiver Of Liability - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a release or waiver from users, typically for medical procedures or treatments. The user is required to provide their information, confirm a release date, and sign a waiver or agreement. This form is essential for ensuring the user's understanding and consent for any medical treatment or procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your user information, including any necessary details.
2. Enter the release date for the waiver or agreement.
3. Provide a detailed waiver or agreement statement in the text field.
4. Specify any medical conditions relevant to your treatment or procedure.
5. Choose the correct signature options to confirm the release or waiver.
6. Enter your phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us understand your needs.
* **Release Date (date)**: Enter the date you are releasing or waiving your rights for any medical treatment or procedure.
* **Waiver (text)**: Please provide a detailed description of the waiver or agreement you are entering into. This is a critical part of the release process.
* **Medical Condition (text)**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment or procedure, please provide details in this field.
* **Signature (select multiple)**: Choose one or more of the signature options to confirm your release or waiver of liability. Please select carefully, as this confirms your agreement.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for any further communication or contact regarding your release or waiver.
