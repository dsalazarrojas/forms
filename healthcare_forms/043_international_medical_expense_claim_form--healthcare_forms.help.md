# international_medical_expense_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The international medical expense claim form is used to report medical expenses incurred while traveling abroad. This form is required for processing claims for medical expenses related to international travel.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure that you have all necessary documents and information before filling out this form.
- Complete all mandatory fields as they are marked with an asterisk (*).
- If you are unsure about any field, please seek assistance from the healthcare representative or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Details (<name>, <text>, optional):** Enter the claimant's name as per their identification documents.
* **Medical Provider (<name>, <text>, optional):** Enter the name of the medical provider who treated the claimant.
* **Medical Expense (<name>, <number, optional):** Enter the total amount of medical expense incurred.
* **Claim Date (<name>, <date, optional):** Enter the date of the medical expense (if any).
* **Medical Certificate (<name>, <select_one, required):** Select 'Yes' if a medical certificate is attached with the form.
* **Medical Receipt (<name>, <select_multiple, required):** Select 'Yes' if a medical receipt is attached with the form.
* **Medical Report (<name>, <text, required):** Enter a brief description of the medical condition.
* **Medical Note (<name>, <text, optional):** Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Medical Expense Claim (<name>, <select_multiple, required):** Select 'Yes' if this expense is part of a claim.
* **Submit Claim (<name>, <select_one, required):** Select 'Yes' to submit the claim.
* **Medical Certificate Attachment (<name>, <text, optional):** Attach any relevant medical certificate documents.
* **Medical Report Attachment (<name>, <text, optional):** Attach any relevant medical report documents.

## Tips
- Ensure that all attached documents are legible and in the correct format.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please seek assistance from the healthcare representative or support team.
