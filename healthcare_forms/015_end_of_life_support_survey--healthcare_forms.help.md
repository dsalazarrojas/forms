# End Of Life Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patients who are nearing the end of their life, enabling healthcare providers to offer appropriate support and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the survey.
2.  Fill out the survey questions (page 1) with the patient's current medical history and condition.
3.  Enter the patient's ID number (page 2) to identify the patient.
4.  Enter the date of death (page 3) and the date death was reported (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions (page 1)** (`survey_questions`, text, required: false): Answer the patient's current medical history and condition in a free-text format.
* **Patient ID Number (page 2)** (`patient_id_number`, number, required: true): Enter the patient's identification number to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Date Of Death (page 3)** (`date_of_decease`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the patient's passing.
* **Date Of Death Reported (page 4)** (`date_of_death_reported`, date, required: true): Enter the date the patient's death was reported.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure the form is valid.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to provide the best possible care for the patient.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with medical staff for guidance.
