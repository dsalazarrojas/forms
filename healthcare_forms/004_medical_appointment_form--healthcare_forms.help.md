# Medical Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient and appointment information for medical scheduling and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on the first page, including any relevant details.
2. Select a date and time for the appointment on the second page.
3. Provide the doctor's information on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, text, required: false): This field is for entering the patient's details, such as their name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
* **Date/Time** (Date/Time, date, required: false): Choose the date and time of the appointment. You can select a specific date from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
* **Doctor Info** (Doctor Info, text, required: false): Enter the doctor's name, specialty, or any other relevant details about the medical professional attending the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete patient information.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to make any changes or corrections, you can modify the form accordingly.
