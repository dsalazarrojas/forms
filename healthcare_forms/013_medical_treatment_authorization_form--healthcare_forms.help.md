# Medical Treatment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide authorization for medical treatment. It is intended to be completed by the person receiving treatment, and should be filled out by them accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your medical condition, treatment name, start and end dates, frequency, treatment status, and consent information.
2. Be sure to answer all the fields marked as required, as they are necessary for us to process your treatment authorization.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe your current medical condition, including any relevant details.
* **Treatment Name** (`treatment_name`, text, optional): Name the specific medical treatment you are undergoing.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your treatment started.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your treatment will end.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of your treatment (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Treatment Status** (`treatment_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of your treatment (Active, Not Active, or Completed).
* **Medical Staff** (`medical_staff`, select_multiple, optional): Select the medical staff members involved in your treatment (Doctor, Nurse, Other).
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have given consent for your treatment (True or False).

## Tips
* Double-check that you have answered all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about your treatment, please reach out to your medical staff directly.
* If you need to update your treatment information, please resubmit the form with the new information.
