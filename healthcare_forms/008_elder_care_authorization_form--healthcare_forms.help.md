# elder_care_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elder_care_authorization_form is designed for healthcare professionals to authorize care and medical treatment for elderly patients. It ensures that the necessary information is gathered to provide effective and safe care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Specify the relationship between the individual filling out the form and the patient (e.g., Primary Caregiver, Alternate Caregiver, Power of Attorney).
3. Enter the care giver's name if applicable.
4. Provide the patient's date of birth.
5. Enter the patient's residence.
6. Fill in the address of the patient.
7. Select the role(s) of the healthcare agent (e.g., Primary Caregiver, Alternate Caregiver, Power of Attorney).
8. Provide the start date for the authorization.
9. Provide the end date for the authorization (if applicable).
10. Enter the name of the emergency contact.
11. Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
12. Enter the relationship of the emergency contact.
13. If applicable, enter the signature of the healthcare agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Relationship to Patient**: Specify the relationship between the individual filling out the form and the patient (e.g., Primary Caregiver, Alternate Caregiver, Power of Attorney).
* **Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the care giver if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Residence**: Enter the patient's residence.
* **Address**: Fill in the address of the patient.
* **Healthcare Agent**: Select the role(s) of the healthcare agent (e.g., Primary Caregiver, Alternate Caregiver, Power of Attorney).
* **Role**: Select the role(s) of the healthcare agent (e.g., Financial, Medical, Legal).
* **Authorization**: This field is a note for any additional information about the authorization.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date for the authorization.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date for the authorization (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
* **Signature**: If applicable, enter the signature of the healthcare agent.
