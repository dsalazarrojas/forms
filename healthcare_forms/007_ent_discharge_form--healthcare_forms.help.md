# ENT Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ENT Discharge Form is used to document the details of a patient's discharge from the hospital, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded and communicated effectively between healthcare providers and patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the patient's ID**: Enter the patient's ID number in the field labeled "Patient Id." This is a unique identifier for each patient and is used to track their medical history.
2. **Enter the patient's name**: Type the patient's full name in the field labeled "Patient Name."
3. **Enter the doctor's name**: Type the doctor's name in the field labeled "Doctor Name."
4. **Enter the discharge date**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. **Enter the discharge time**: Enter the time the patient was discharged from the hospital in the format 12:00 PM.
6. **Enter the discharge location**: Type the location where the patient was discharged from the hospital.
7. **Choose the discharge status**: Select either "Discharged" or "Not Discharged" to indicate the patient's status.
8. **Enter the patient's acknowledgement**: Type the patient's acknowledgement or statement of understanding in the field labeled "Patient Acknowledgement."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id** (`Patient Id`, number, required): Enter the unique patient ID number to ensure accurate tracking of the patient's medical history.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Type the patient's full name as it appears on their identification card or medical record.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, required): Type the name of the doctor who is responsible for the patient's care.
* **Discharge Date** (`Discharge Date`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Discharge Time** (`Discharge Time`, time, required): Enter the time the patient was discharged from the hospital in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Discharge Location** (`Discharge Location`, text, required): Type the location where the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Discharge Status** (`Discharge Status`, select_one, required): Select "Discharged" if the patient has been discharged from the hospital, otherwise select "Not Discharged."
* **Patient Acknowledgement** (`Patient Acknowledgement`, text, required): Type the patient's acknowledgement or statement of understanding in this field.

## Tips

* Ensure that all necessary information is entered accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any missing or incorrect information before submitting.
* Verify the patient's identity and discharge status with the doctor and patient before completing the form.
