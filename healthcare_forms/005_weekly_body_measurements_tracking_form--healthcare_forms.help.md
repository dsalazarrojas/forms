# weekly_body_measurements_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track weekly body measurements for individuals, collecting data on weight, height, and various body circumferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the week you are tracking measurements for (Week 1).
2. Enter the measurements taken for that week.
3. Enter the date you took the measurements.
4. Enter your weight in kilograms.
5. Enter your height in centimeters.
6. Enter the circumferences for each of the following body parts:
	* Waist Circumference (cm)
	* Hip Circumference (cm)
	* Shoulder Circumference (cm)
	* Chest Circumference (cm)
	* Waist Circumference 2 (cm)
	* Shoulder Circumference 2 (cm)
	* Chest Circumference 2 (cm)
	* Hip Circumference 3 (cm)
7. Add any additional comments about your measurements.
8. (Optional) Attach a photo of the measurements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week** (Week 1): Enter the week you are tracking measurements for.
* **Measurements Taken**: Enter the measurements taken for that week.
* **Date Measurements Taken**: Enter the date you took the measurements.
* **Weight (kg)**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Height (cm)**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Waist Circumference (cm)**: Enter the circumference of your waist in centimeters.
* **Hip Circumference (cm)**: Enter the circumference of your hip in centimeters.
* **Shoulder Circumference (cm)**: Enter the circumference of your shoulder in centimeters.
* **Chest Circumference (cm)**: Enter the circumference of your chest in centimeters.
* **Waist Circumference 2 (cm)**: Enter the second waist circumference measurement.
* **Shoulder Circumference 2 (cm)**: Enter the second shoulder circumference measurement.
* **Chest Circumference 2 (cm)**: Enter the second chest circumference measurement.
* **Hip Circumference 3 (cm)**: Enter the third hip circumference measurement.
* **Measurements Comment**: Enter any additional comments about your measurements.
* **Measurements Photo**: Attach a photo of your measurements (optional).
