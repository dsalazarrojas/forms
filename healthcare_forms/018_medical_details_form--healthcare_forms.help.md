# Medical Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Details Form is designed to collect essential medical information for patient care. This form will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical status, allergies, current medications, contact information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your medical history and needs.
3. Enter all required fields. While not all fields are required, providing as much information as possible will help healthcare professionals provide better care.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Allergies
**allergies** (`patient_allergies`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check all the medical allergies or sensitivities you have.

### Medications
**medications** (`patient_medications`, Select Multiple, Optional): List all your current or past medications, including dosages and frequencies.

### Medical History
**medical_history** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any chronic conditions, surgeries, or previous illnesses.

### Contact Information
**contact information** (`patient_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information so healthcare professionals can reach out to you as needed.

### Phone
**phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for quick contact.

### Email
**email** (`contact_email`, Text, Optional): Enter your email address for secure and fast contact.

### Emergency Contact Name
**emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.

### Emergency Phone
**emergency phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

### Relationship
**relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.

## Tips

- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure healthcare professionals have all necessary information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
- Review and update your entries as needed to reflect changes in your medical status.
