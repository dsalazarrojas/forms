# Therapy Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support individuals seeking therapy sponsorship. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the contact method you prefer:
	* Mail
	* Email
	* Phone
	* None (if you do not wish to be contacted)
2. Enter your information and details as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Preferences**: Select how you prefer to be contacted for further discussion or updates. You can choose multiple options, separated by commas.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address for communication purposes.
* **Sponsor**: Enter the name and affiliation (if applicable) of the person sponsoring your therapy.
* **Therapy Details**: Describe the type and scope of the therapy you are requesting.
* **Financial Support**: If applicable, indicate if you require financial support for your therapy.
* **Financial Support Amount**: If you require financial support, enter the specific amount you need.
* **Financial Support Frequency**: If you require financial support, select the frequency of support you need: One-time or Ongoing.
* **Payment Terms**: If applicable, describe the payment terms you agree to.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your application.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
- We will review your application and respond accordingly.
