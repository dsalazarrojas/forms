# Diagnostic Angiography Event Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diagnostic Angiography Event Log is a form used to document diagnostic angiography events. This form helps healthcare professionals log patient information, procedure details, and outcomes to ensure accurate and comprehensive records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on Page 1 (Patient Info).
2. Log the procedure details on Page 2 (Procedure Info).
3. Document the imaging information on Page 3 (Imaging Info).
4. Record catheterization details on Page 4 (Catheterization Info).
5. Provide post-procedure information on Page 5 (Post Procedure Info).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Info
• **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, medical record number (MRN), and date of birth.
### Page 2: Procedure Info
• **Procedure Info**: Enter the type of diagnostic angiography procedure performed, the location of the procedure, and the name of the radiologist or operator involved.
### Page 3: Imaging Info
• **Imaging Info**: Enter the type and number of angiograms performed, the location and orientation of the angiogram, and any notable findings or diagnoses.
### Page 4: Catheterization Info
• **Catheterization Info**: Enter the location and size of the catheter used, the type of catheterization (e.g. femoral, radial), and any complications or issues encountered during catheterization.
### Page 5: Post Procedure Info
• **Post Procedure Info**: Enter the patient's post-procedure status, including any complications, allergic reactions, or other notable outcomes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in the patient's condition or procedure details.
- Maintain accurate and consistent formatting and terminology to facilitate easy searching and data analysis.
