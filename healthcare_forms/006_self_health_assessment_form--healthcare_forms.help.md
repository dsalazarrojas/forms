# Self Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This self-assessment form is designed to collect information about your health and wellness. Please fill out this form to provide a clear and accurate picture of your health status, including any medical history, mental health concerns, and physical health habits. This data will help healthcare providers better understand your needs and make informed decisions about your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, being as accurate and thorough as possible.
3. Review your answers for completeness and clarity before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (`text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant personal details.
* **medical_history** (`text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or health concerns that you have or have had in the past.
* **health_goals** (`text`, optional): Outline your short- and long-term health goals, including any specific objectives or priorities you have for your well-being.
* **wellness_routines** (`text`, optional): List your regular habits and practices that contribute to your overall well-being, such as exercise routines, meditation, or sleep schedules.
* **mental_health** (`text`, optional): Describe any mental health concerns or issues you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **physical_health** (`text`, optional): Report any physical health issues or concerns you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **nutrition** (`text`, optional): Outline your typical diet, including any food preferences, allergies, or restrictions.
* **social_support** (`text`, optional): Indicate any support systems you have, such as family, friends, or support groups, that aid in your overall well-being.
* **health_care** (`text`, optional): Describe your current health care plan or any medical appointments scheduled.
* **health_data** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant health data, such as test results or medical records.

**Tips**

* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for your personal and confidential use, and the data collected will be kept secure and private.
