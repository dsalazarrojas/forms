# substance_usage_waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The substance_usage_waiver form is used to obtain a waiver or consent from an individual or their legal representative for the use of a certain substance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the substance usage waiver form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

* **Substance Name** (substance_name, text, required): Enter the name of the substance being waived.
* **Dosage Instructions** (dosage_instructions, text, required): Provide instructions for the dosage of the substance.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition, text, required): Describe the medical condition(s) that require the waiver.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Provide any relevant medical history related to the substance.
* **Signature** (signature, text, required): Sign your name to indicate your consent.
* **Date Filled Out** (date_filled_out, date, required): Enter the date you filled out the form.
* **Date Completed** (date_completed, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Signed By** (signed_by, text, required): Sign your name to indicate your completion of the form.
* **Printed Name** (printed_name, text, required): Print your name.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Time** (time, time, required): Enter the current time.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, required): Provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Phone Number** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Address** (emergency_contact_address, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Other** (emergency_contact_other, text, required): Provide any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Physician Signature** (physician_signature, text, required): The healthcare provider's signature to confirm their approval.
