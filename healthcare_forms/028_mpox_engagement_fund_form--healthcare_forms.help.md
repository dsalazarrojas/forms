# Mpox Engagement Fund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Engagement Fund Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking support for Mpox prevention and response efforts. This form will help assess the need for funding, resources, and services related to Mpox.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information on the first page.
2. Select your organization type from the options provided on the second page.
3. Choose the prevention services you require on the third page.
4. Add any additional education or public health response information on the fourth page.
5. Enter your funding request on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (1)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Provide your name and contact details to enable us to get in touch with you regarding your funding request.

### Organization (2)
* **Organization (organization)**: Select the type of organization you represent, which can be Local Health Department, State Health Department, or Other.

### Prevention Services (3)
* **Prevention Services (prevention_services)**: Choose the prevention services you require, which can be Vaccination, Testing, Education, Contact Tracing, or Other.

### Education (4)
* **Education (education)**: Add any additional information about education or public health response efforts you'd like to provide.

### Public Health Response (4)
* **Public Health Response (public_health_response)**: Provide any further information about your public health response efforts.

### Funding Request (5)
* **Funding Request (funding_request)**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting for your Mpox prevention and response efforts.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all required fields to ensure your funding request is considered.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Funding requests will be reviewed based on your provided information.
