# Physical Activity Readiness - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals understand an individual's physical activity level, pain or discomfort experienced during exercise, and any visits to a doctor regarding their pain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question in the corresponding section on the form.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. For number fields, enter a numerical value.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **i am active participant** (`physical_activity_readiness_1`, number, optional): Indicate if you participate in physical activity on a regular basis. Enter 1 if you do, or 0 if not.
- **How often do you participate in physical activity** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you engage in physical activity from 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, 5-6 times per week, or almost daily.
- **How long do you participate in physical activity** (`exercise_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of your physical activity from less than 15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, or more than 45 minutes.
- **Intensity Frequency** (`intensity_frequency`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value indicating your intensity frequency. (Note: This field is currently empty and may not be applicable.)
- **Physical Activity Readiness 5** (`physical_activity_readiness_5`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would rate your physical activity readiness from low, medium, or high.
- **Physical Activity Readiness 2** (`exercise_frequency_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would rate your physical activity readiness from low, medium, or high.
- **Have you experienced any pain or discomfort when engaging in physical activity?** (`pain_or_discomfort_1`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have experienced any pain or discomfort during physical activity. Select 'yes' or 'no'.
- **Have you experienced any pain or discomfort when engaging in physical activity 2?** (`pain_or_discomfort_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have experienced any pain or discomfort during physical activity 2. Select 'yes' or 'no'.
- **Have you seen a doctor about your pain?** (`doctor_visit_1`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have visited a doctor about your pain. Select 'yes' or 'no'.
- **Have you seen a doctor about your pain 2?** (`doctor_visit_1`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have visited a doctor about your pain 2. Select 'yes' or 'no'.

## Tips
- Answer each question honestly and accurately to provide reliable information for healthcare professionals.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, discuss with your healthcare provider for clarification.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for medical advice.
