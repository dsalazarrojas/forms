# Daily Food Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Food Intake Form is designed to help users track their daily food intake. It is used to capture information about the food consumed on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the first page with a brief description of the food intake for the day.
* On the second page, provide a brief description of the food intake for the day.
* On the third page, list the food items consumed on the day. For each food item, type in the name of the food.
* On the fourth page, enter the quantity of each food item consumed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **daily_food_intake_form_page_1 (Page 1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the food intake for the day. This is the first page of the form where you can describe your food intake in a few sentences.
* **food_intake_form_page_2 (Page 2)** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the food intake for the day. This page is similar to the first page, but you can add more details if needed.
* **food_intake_form_page_3 (Page 3)** (`text`, required/optional): List the food items consumed on the day. Type in the name of each food item, one item per line.
* **food_intake_form_page_4 (Page 4)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of each food item consumed. Match the food items listed on the previous page with their corresponding quantity.
