# Orthodontic Records Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect and store patient data for orthodontic treatment records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the form's purpose and fields.
2. Enter the required information, including patient details.
3. Select the relevant treatment options and progress details.
4. Add any necessary notes or comments.
5. Verify the accuracy and completeness of the submitted information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, ID, or any other identifying details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant health issues or allergies.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, select_one, optional): Select the type of treatment the patient is undergoing (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Treatment Progress** (`treatment_progress`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current treatment progress (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's treatment.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, select_one, optional): Select any notes or comments specific to the patient (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the treatment or appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the treatment or appointment.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the patient's medical history.

## Tips
- Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and completely.
- Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.
- Verify that the submitted information is up-to-date and relevant to the patient's treatment.
