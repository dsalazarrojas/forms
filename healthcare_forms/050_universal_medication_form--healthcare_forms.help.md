# Universal Medication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record essential information about a patient's medications, allergies, and medical history for healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are in a healthcare setting or context where this form can be used for its intended purpose.
2. Select the "Patient Info" page to input the patient's basic information.
3. On the "Medications" page, list all the patient's current and past medications, including dosage and frequency.
4. In the "Allergies" page, list any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
5. Under "Medical History", provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant health conditions or previous treatments.
6. Finally, on the "Other Medical Info" page, include any additional medical information not covered elsewhere.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect basic patient information such as name, date of birth, or any other identifying details.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): Please list all current and past medications, including the dose and frequency of each medication.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, required: false): If the patient has any allergies, please list them here, along with any known symptoms or reactions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required: false): In brief, describe the patient's medical history, including previous treatments or health conditions.
* **Other Medical Info** (`other_medical_info`, note, required: false): Include any other relevant medical information not covered elsewhere, such as medical procedures or test results.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields (none in this form) are completed to ensure accurate and complete patient data.
* If a field is left blank, please provide a clear explanation or reason for its absence.
* Always handle patient information with confidentiality and respect.
