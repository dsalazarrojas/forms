# Vascular Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the patient's vascular health, gathering essential information on their medical history, current health conditions, and potential cardiovascular risk factors. It helps healthcare providers evaluate and address the patient's overall health status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
2. **Birth Date**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's home address.
4. **Primary Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the patient's primary caregiver (if applicable).
5. **Primary Caregiver Relationship**: Select the relationship between the patient and their primary caregiver (e.g., spouse, child, parent, etc.).
6. **Medical Record ID**: Enter the patient's medical record ID (if applicable).
7. **Patient Health Conditions**: List the patient's current health conditions, such as hypertension, diabetes, etc.
8. **Surgical History**: List any previous surgeries or medical procedures undergone by the patient.
9. **Medication List**: List the patient's current medications and dosages.
10. **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
11. **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
12. **Vital Signs**: Enter the patient's vital signs (not applicable for this field type).
13. **Cardiovascular Risk Factors**: Select the cardiovascular risk factors that apply to the patient (e.g., hypertension, diabetes, etc.).
14. **Vascular Disease Assessment**: Select the types of vascular disease that have been assessed (e.g., atrial fibrillation, peripheral artery disease, etc.).
15. **Patient Counseling**: Provide any additional information or advice given to the patient during the assessment.
16. **Patient Counseling Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from patient counseling.
17. **Provider Comments**: Enter any comments from the healthcare provider.
18. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's health status.
