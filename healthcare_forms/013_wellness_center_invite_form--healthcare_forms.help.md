# wellness_center_invite_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wellness center invite form is designed for inviting patients or contacts to visit a wellness center. It is used to gather essential information for scheduling and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information in the **User Input** section.
2.  Select the **Contact Method** to reach out to you (Email or Phone).
3.  Indicate whether **Follow-up** is required.
4.  Provide the **Expected Visit Date** and **Expected Visit Time** for the invitee's visit.
5.  Choose the **Visit Purpose** (Yes/No).
6.  Add any **Additional Comments**.
7.  Select the **Follow-up** method (Yes/No).
8.  Provide **Contact Method 3** (Email or Phone).

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that the contact method selected is the most suitable for reaching out to the invitee.
*   If **Follow-up** is selected, be prepared to provide regular updates or reminders.
*   Clearly state the **Visit Purpose** to avoid any confusion.
*   Add any **Additional Comments** that may be relevant to the visit.
*   Select the correct **Follow-up** method to ensure efficient communication.
*   Be precise with the **Expected Visit Date and Time** to avoid conflicts or misunderstandings.
