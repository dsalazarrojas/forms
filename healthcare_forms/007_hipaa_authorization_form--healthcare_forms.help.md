# hipaa-authorization-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hipaa-authorization-form is used to obtain permission from patients to access their protected health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name**, **Provider Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Date of Service**, and **Reason** fields as required.
2. Select your **Authorization Type** and **Authorized Provider** options from the provided dropdown menus.
3. Sign and date the **Signature** field with your authentic digital or physical signature.
4. Enter optional dates in **Date of Birth**, **Date of Service**, **Effective Date**, and **Expiration Date** fields if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name**
* (text, required, **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
### 2. **Provider Name**
* (text, required, **Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
### 3. **Date of Birth**
* (date, optional, **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format if applicable. (If you are unsure, please contact your healthcare provider.)
### 4. **Date of Service**
* (date, optional, **Date of Service**: Enter the date of service if applicable. (If you are unsure, please contact your healthcare provider.)
### 5. **Signature**
* (note, required, **Signature**: Sign and date your authorization with your authentic digital or physical signature.
### 6. **Authorization Type**
* (select_one, required, **Authorization Type**: Select one of the provided options: Electronic, Written.
### 7. **Authorized Provider**
* (select_multiple, required, **Authorized Provider**: Select one or more of the provided options: Dr. John Smith, Dr. Jane Doe.
### 8. **Effective Date**
* (date, optional, **Effective Date**: Enter the effective date of authorization if applicable. (If you are unsure, please contact your healthcare provider.)
### 9. **Expiration Date**
* (date, optional, **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of authorization if applicable. (If you are unsure, please contact your healthcare provider.)
### 10. **Reason**
* (text, required, **Reason**: Provide a reason for authorizing access to your protected health information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your healthcare provider or the form administrator for assistance.
* Remember to save a copy of your completed form for your records.
