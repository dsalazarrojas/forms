# parent_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent authorization form is used to collect information from parents or guardians of a child patient, ensuring that the child's treatment and care are managed accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring all fields are accurate and complete.
2. Start by providing the Patient Name.
3. Enter the names of both Parents Names.
4. Provide the Address.
5. Enter the Phone number.
6. Enter the Email address.
7. Enter the Date of Birth of the child.
8. Select the appropriate Treatment Type.
9. Provide any additional Treatment Details.
10. List all types of consents given for the treatment.
11. If necessary, enter an Emergency Contact name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the child patient.
* **Parents Names** (`parents_names`, `text`, Required): List the names of both parents or guardians.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Required): Enter the address of the child's residence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, Required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of treatment you will be providing.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, `note`, Required): Provide any additional information about the treatment.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, Required): List all types of consents given for the treatment.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, Optional): List the medical history of the child patient.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, Optional): List any allergies or medical conditions of the child patient.
