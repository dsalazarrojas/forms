# homemade_soap_survey_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect feedback and information from users about their purchasing experience with homemade soap products. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the type of soap purchased, purchasing location, and other relevant details to help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as prompted.
3. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name, email address, and phone number. If you want to be contacted for any follow-up questions or to receive updates, please fill in this section.
* **Soap Type** (`soap_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of soap you purchased, such as Apple, Pear, or Grapes.
* **Purchasing Location** (`purchasing_location`, select_one, optional): Choose where you purchased the soap, such as Local Store, Online Store, or Market.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our homemade soap products.
* **Soap Brand** (`soap_brand`, select_one, optional): Select the brand of soap you purchased.
* **Purchasing Date** (`soap_purchasing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the soap.
* **Purchasing Time** (`soap_purchasing_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you purchased the soap.
* **Purchased Soap Weight** (`purchased_soap_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the soap you purchased.
* **Purchasing Price** (`soap_purchasing_price`, number, optional): Enter the price you paid for the soap.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Street** (`contact_street`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Country** (`contact_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Comments** (`contact_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our homemade soap products.
* **Rating** (`contact_rating`, select_one, optional): Select a rating for your experience with our soap products, from 1 to 5.
* **Age** (`soap_purchaser_age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`soap_purchaser_gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Income** (`contact_purchaser_income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Occupation** (`soap_purchaser_occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation.
* **Relationship** (`soap_purchaser_relationship`, text, optional): Select your relationship to the soap purchaser, such as family member or friend.
