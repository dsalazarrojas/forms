# TattooMicrobladingConsentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TattooMicrobladingConsentForm is used to collect client information and consent for microblading services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Client Information" section with the client's name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the client's contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Provide the client's contact number in the "Contact Number" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (client_information, text, required):** Please enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant details for their microblading procedure.
* **Contact Info (contact_info, text, required):** Enter the client's contact information, including email address or phone number, to ensure we can reach them before, during, and after the microblading service.
* **Contact Number (contact_number, text, required):** Enter the client's contact number, which will help us reach them in case of any emergency or appointment reminders.
