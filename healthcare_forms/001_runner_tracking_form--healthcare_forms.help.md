# Runner Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Runner Tracking Form is designed to collect and store information about runners for the organization's records and progress tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the runner's name in the "Runner Name" field.
2. Provide the runner's email address in the "Runner Email" field.
3. Enter the runner's phone number in the "Runner Phone" field.
4. Enter the distance covered by the runner in the "Distance" field.
5. Select the date of the run from the calendar in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time taken by the runner to complete the distance in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the runner's performance or progress in the "Runner Notes" field.
8. Provide any comments or feedback about the runner in the "Runner Comments" fields (there are multiple fields for this purpose).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Runner Name** (runner_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the runner.
* **Runner Email** (runner_email, email, required: false): Enter the runner's email address.
* **Runner Phone** (runner_phone, text, required: false): Enter the runner's phone number.
* **Distance** (distance, number, required: false): Enter the distance covered by the runner.
* **Date** (date, date, required: false): Select the date of the run from the calendar.
* **Time** (time, time, required: false): Enter the time taken by the runner to complete the distance.
* **Runner Notes** (runner_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the runner's performance or progress.
* **Runner Comments** (runner_comments, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about the runner.
* **Runner Comments 2** (runner_comments_2, text, required: false): Repeat the same field as above for additional comments.
* **Runner Comments 3** (runner_comments_3, text, required: false): Same as above.
* **Runner Comments 4** (runner_comments_4, text, required: false): Same as above.
* **Runner Comments 5** (runner_comments_5, text, required: false): Same as above.
