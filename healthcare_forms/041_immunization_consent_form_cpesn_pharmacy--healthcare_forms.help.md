# Immunization Consent Form CPESN Pharmacy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record patient information, immunization preferences, payment preferences, schedule appointments, and medical details for immunization-related services provided by the CPESN Pharmacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: This is the first section of the form where you can input the patient's details.
2. **Select Immunization Preferences**: You will be asked about the patient's preference regarding immunization.
3. **Select Payment Preferences**: Here, you can choose the payment methods or options that apply to the patient.
4. **Schedule Appointments**: You can select whether you want to schedule appointments with the CPESN Pharmacy.
5. **Enter Medical Details**: This section is for any additional medical information relevant to the patient's immunization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **immunization_preferences**: Choose whether the patient is willing to receive immunizations or not.
* **payment_preferences**: Select the payment method(s) you wish to use for the immunization services.
* **schedule_appointments**: Choose whether you want to schedule appointments for the immunization services.
* **medical_details**: Enter any additional medical information relevant to the patient's treatment.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field or option, please contact the CPESN Pharmacy staff for clarification.
- This form is for immunization-related services only; do not fill it out for other medical purposes.
- Ensure that you have the patient's consent for their information before filling out this form.
