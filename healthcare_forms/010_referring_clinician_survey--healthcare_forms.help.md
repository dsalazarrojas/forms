# Referring clinician survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from referring clinicians for patients being referred to a medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's information and ensure that you have the authority to complete this form.
2. Select the correct contact method for communication (phone, email, or mail).
3. Provide the patient's contact details.
4. Enter the patient's phone number.
5. Enter the patient's email address.
6. Enter the patient's fax number (if applicable).
7. Enter the patient's address.
8. Enter the patient's medical record number or other relevant identifier.
9. Select the patient's current status (active, inactive, or unknown).
10. Select the patient's category (new or existing).
11. Choose the type of visit (new, follow-up, or urgent).
12. Enter the date and time of the visit.
13. Enter the referring clinician's name and any relevant information.
14. Enter the visit duration (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient Info** (`new_patient_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the patient's new patient information. Please provide any relevant details that may be useful for the receiving facility.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information that may be relevant.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's fax number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number or other relevant identifier.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's current status (active, inactive, or unknown).
* **Patient Category** (`patient_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the patient's category (new or existing).
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of visit (new, follow-up, or urgent).
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Referring Clinician** (`referring_clinician`, text, optional): Enter the referring clinician's name and any relevant information.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Enter the estimated visit duration (if applicable).
