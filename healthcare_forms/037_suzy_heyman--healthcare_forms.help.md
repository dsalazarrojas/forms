# suzy_heyman_healthcare_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information about a health insurance provider. You will enter the provider's details, including their contact information and any other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the health insurance provider's name in the "Health Insurance Provider" field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person at the insurance provider in the "Contact Person At Insur" field.
3. Enter the contact email of the insurance provider in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the contact number of the insurance provider in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter the address of the insurance provider in the "Address Of Insur" field.
6. Enter the date of birth of the insured individual in the "Date Of Birth" field.
7. Enter the medical record number of the insured individual in the "Medical Record Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the health insurance provider you wish to add or update.
* **Name Of Insurance Provider** (`name_of_insurance_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Contact Person At Insur** (`contact_person_at_insp`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the insurance provider.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address Of Insur** (`address_of_insp`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the insurance provider.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the insured individual.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the medical record number of the insured individual.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, double-check the insurance provider's documentation or contact them directly.
* This form is for administrative purposes only, and all information collected will be used to update the system accordingly.
