# emergency_department_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency department assessment form is used to collect patient information and medical history for assessment and treatment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field to indicate if the patient has the corresponding condition or history.
2. If "Yes" is selected for any of the conditions, provide additional information in the "Assessment Result" field.
3. Enter any relevant medical details in the "Chief Complaint" field.
4. Enter the patient's medical record number.
5. Indicate the patient's status as Active or Inactive.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the assessment result.
7. Select the assessment score.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for each medication and medical condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Contact Information** (`Patient Contact Information`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient's contact information is available and relevant.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history about the patient.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient has any allergies.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient is currently taking any medications.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedure** (`Medical Procedure`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient has undergone any medical procedures.
* **Chief Complaint** (`Chief Complaint`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter any emergency contact information for the patient.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Patient Status** (`Patient Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Active" to indicate the patient is currently being treated or "Inactive" to indicate the patient has completed treatment.
* **Assessment Result** (`Assessment Result`, `text`, required: false): Enter the result of the assessment or any relevant medical details.
* **Assessment Score** (`Assessment Score`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate the assessment was completed successfully.
* **Medical Procedure List** (`Medical Procedure List`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient has undergone any medical procedures.
* **Medical Condition List** (`Medical Condition List`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medication List** (`Medication List`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate if the patient is currently taking any medications.

## Tips
* Be sure to thoroughly review each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use the "Patient Contact Information" field to gather relevant information about the patient.
* If the patient has allergies, select "Yes" and enter relevant allergy information.
* If the patient has medical conditions or procedures, select "Yes" and provide additional information in the "Assessment Result" field.
* If the patient has taken medications, select "Yes" and provide additional information in the "Assessment Result" field.
* If the patient's status is "Active", the medical staff will continue to treat the patient.
* If the patient's status is "Inactive", the medical staff will not continue to treat the patient.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for each field to ensure accurate and efficient completion of the form.
