# Coronavirus Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about patients' awareness and behavior during the COVID-19 pandemic. It will help healthcare professionals track patients' demographics, prevention methods, symptoms, and contact tracing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable options for "Demographics" on the "Demographics" page.
2. Choose the prevention methods you have been following on the "Prevention Methods" page.
3. Indicate whether you have visited the hospital on the "Hospital Visits" page.
4. Select all symptoms you are experiencing on the "Symptoms" page.
5. Provide details about your contact history on the "Contact Tracing" page.
6. If you have visited the hospital, confirm your contact history on the "Contact Tracing Confirmation" page.
7. Confirm your healthcare worker status on the "Healthcare Worker Info Confirmation" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Survey Page**: This page is a text input field where you can provide any information about your recent medical history.
* **Demographics**: Select your racial or ethnic background to help us understand the diversity of our community.
	+ **Asian**: If you identify as Asian.
	+ **Black or African American**: If you identify as Black or African American.
	+ **Hispanic or Latino**: If you identify as Hispanic or Latino.
	+ **Native American or Alaskan Native**: If you identify as Native American or Alaskan Native.
	+ **White**: If you identify as White.
	+ **Other**: If you identify as something else.
* **Prevention Methods**: Choose the methods you have been using to prevent the spread of the virus.
	+ **Hand washing**: Washing your hands regularly with soap and water.
	+ **Social distancing**: Keeping a safe distance from others to reduce the risk of transmission.
	+ **Masking**: Wearing a mask to cover your mouth and nose.
	+ **Other**: Other methods you have been using.
* **Hospital Visits**: Indicate whether you have visited the hospital in the past week.
* **Symptoms**: Select all symptoms you are experiencing.
	+ **Fever**: A high temperature, usually above 100.4°F (38°C).
	+ **Cough**: A cough that is dry or productive (with mucus).
	+ **Shortness of breath**: Difficulty breathing or feeling like you can't catch your breath.
	+ **Headache**: A headache or pressure in the face.
	+ **Sore throat**: A scratchy or sore feeling in the throat.
	+ **Runny nose or stuffy nose**: A runny or stuffy nose that is not caused by allergies.
	+ **Body aches or pains**: Muscle or joint pain.
	+ **Diarrhea**: Loose, watery stools.
	+ **Nausea or vomiting**: Feeling nauseous or vomiting.
	+ **Fatigue**: Feeling extremely tired or lacking energy.
	+ **Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath**: Trouble breathing or feeling like you can't catch your breath.
	+ **Congestion or stuffy nose**: A runny or stuffy nose that is not caused by allergies.
	+ **Coughing**: A persistent or violent cough.
	+ **Sneezing**: A sudden, explosive cough.
	+ **Loss of taste or smell**: A loss or decrease in your sense of smell or taste.
	+ **Loss of appetite**: Not feeling hungry or interested in food.
	+ **Loss of smell**: A loss or decrease in your sense of smell.
	+ **Muscle aches or pains**: Muscle or joint pain.
	+ **Abnormal heart rate or rhythm**: A change in your heart rate or rhythm.
	+ **Chest pain**: Pain or pressure in the chest.
	+ **Loss of taste**: A loss or decrease in your sense of taste.
* **Contact Tracing**: Provide details about who you have recently visited, such as family members, colleagues, or friends.
* **Contact Tracing Confirmation**: Confirm whether you have visited the hospital recently.
* **Survey Completed**: This is a text input field where you can provide any final comments or thoughts.
* **Healthcare Worker Info**: Select your healthcare worker status.
	+ **Doctor**: If you are a doctor.
	+ **Nurse**: If you are a nurse.
	+ **Healthcare assistant**: If you are a healthcare assistant.
	+ **Other**: If you identify as something else.
* **Healthcare Worker Info Confirmation**: Confirm your healthcare worker status.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form. The information you provide will help us understand and manage the spread of the virus.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
