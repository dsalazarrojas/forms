# Emergency Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Visit Registration Form is for patients to provide essential information about their condition, contact details, and medical history during emergency visits to a medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your medical condition in the Medical Condition field.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
4. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
5. Enter your emergency contact's name in the Emergency Contact field.
6. Enter your emergency contact's number in the Emergency Contact Number field.
7. Select the medical facilities you are visiting from the Medical Facility field (select one or more options).
8. Select the reason for your visit from the Visit Reason field (select one option).
9. Enter any allergies you may have in the Allergies field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your medical condition.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `1995-02-12`).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number or other contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more medical facilities you are visiting.
* **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for your emergency visit.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Enter any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment. 

# Tips
- Please fill out this form completely and accurately to help our staff provide the best care possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, don't hesitate to ask. 
- Please make sure to update this form upon any changes to your medical condition or contact information. 
- If you are unsure about any field, try to provide the most accurate information available. 

Please note: This form is for emergency visits. If you are scheduled for a regular appointment, you will receive a separate form with additional instructions.
