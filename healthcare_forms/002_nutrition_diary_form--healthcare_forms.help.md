# Nutrition Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Diary Form is designed to collect information about your daily diet and habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, simply fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Enter your food intake in the "Food Intake" field.
3. Check your daily habits in the "Daily Habits" field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or any other relevant information that you'd like to provide.
* **Food Intake** (`food_intake`, text, optional): A description of your food intake, e.g., "breakfast: 2 eggs, toast, coffee".
* **Daily Habits** (`daily_habits`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Habit 1: Check this box if you regularly exercise.
	+ Habit 2: Check this box if you smoke or use any tobacco products.
	+ Habit 3: Check this box if you consume any recreational drugs.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to include with your entry.
