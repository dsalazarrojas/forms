# Nursing Home Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are inquiring about nursing homes. This form is designed to gather relevant details to facilitate a smooth and efficient interaction between the inquirer and the nursing home staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. If you are under 18, please enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
4. Specify your Best Time to Contact in the Best Time to Contact field.
5. Select the Type of Inquiry from the dropdown list (Nursing Home Enquiry, Admissions, or General Enquiry).
6. Enter your message in the Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): If you are under 18, enter your date of birth to ensure the correct services are provided to you.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`Best Time to Contact`, time, optional): Specify the time of day when it is best to contact you, e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`Type of Inquiry`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for your inquiry, which can be:
	+ Nursing Home Enquiry
	+ Admissions
	+ General Enquiry
* **Message** (`Message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the nursing home staff.

## Tips
- Please be as clear and concise as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the nursing home staff directly.
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible experience.
