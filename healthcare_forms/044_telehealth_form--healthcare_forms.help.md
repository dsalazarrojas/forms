# telehealth_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **telehealth_form** is a form designed to collect information from patients for telehealth services. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential details from patients for remote consultations, appointments, and medical histories. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information, starting from the first page.
2. Enter your name, contact number, and email address in the provided fields.
3. Provide your medical history in the **Medical History** field, describing your health concerns or previous medical conditions.
4. Enter the date of your medical history in the **Medical History Date** field, following the YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Select your appointment time and date in the **Appointment Time** and **Appointment Date** fields, respectively, using 24-hour format.
6. If you have any additional medical histories, fill in the **Medical History 1**, **Medical History 2**, **Medical History 3**, and **Medical History 4** fields with relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or identification document. 
- **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes during the appointment or consultation.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for secure communication.
- **Medical History**: Describe any existing medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you have.
- **Medical History Date**: Enter the date of your medical history in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Appointment Time**: Select your appointment time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- **Appointment Date**: Select your appointment date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Medical History 1**, **Medical History 2**, **Medical History 3**, and **Medical History 4**: If needed, provide additional medical histories or concerns in these fields.
  - **Medical History 1, 2, 3, and 4** are optional fields for providing further medical details if required.
  - Use these fields to describe any ongoing treatments, previous surgeries, or other relevant health matters.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact number and email address for accuracy.
- If you have any allergies, medications, or existing medical conditions, provide detailed explanations in the **Medical History** field.
- Ensure that you select the correct appointment date and time for your scheduled consultation or appointment.
- Use 24-hour format for time fields (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- Fill in the **Medical History 1, 2, 3, and 4** fields only if necessary, as they are optional.
