# Substance Abuse Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substance Abuse Counseling Consent Form is a document that ensures clients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the confidentiality of their personal and medical information. This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before they begin counseling sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your name, date of birth, contact information, and address.
2. Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person.
3. If applicable, provide your social security number.
4. Check the box to indicate if you have provided consent to counseling.
5. Provide the name of your healthcare provider.
6. Provide the name of your counselor.
7. Specify the frequency and length of your counseling sessions.
8. Specify the start and end dates of your counseling sessions.
9. Read and agree to the confidentiality agreement.
10. Sign the consent form to indicate your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your full name as it should appear on your records and in any documents or correspondence related to your counseling. (text)
* **Birthdate**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format. (date)
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached for contact or emergencies. (text)
* **Contact Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for contact or emergencies. (email)
* **Address**: Your current address where you can be reached for visits or mail. (text)
* **Emergency Contact**: The name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency. (text)
* **Social Security Number**: Your social security number (if applicable). (number)
* **Consent Date**: The date you are providing consent for counseling. (date)
* **Consent Status**: Check "Yes" if you are providing consent for counseling, "No" otherwise. (select_one)
* **Provider Name**: The name of your healthcare provider. (text)
* **Counselor Name**: The name of your counselor. (text)
* **Session Frequency**: The number of sessions you are committing to attend per week. (number)
* **Session Length**: The length of each counseling session. (number)
* **Session Start Date**: The date when your counseling sessions will begin. (date)
* **Session End Date**: The date when your counseling sessions will end. (date)
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Read and agree to the confidentiality agreement. This agreement ensures your personal and medical information is kept private. (note)
* **Consent Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms of the consent form. (note)

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask your counselor for clarification.
* Make sure to review and understand the confidentiality agreement before signing the form.
* Keep a copy of the signed consent form for your records.
