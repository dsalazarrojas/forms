# Patient Consent Documentation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document patient consent for treatment and emergency contact information. It collects the patient's personal details, medical history, and contact information to ensure the healthcare provider has the necessary information to provide quality care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
3. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
4. Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
5. Enter the patient's medical history and any known allergies.
6. Provide any additional contact information for the emergency contact.
7. Check the box to indicate consent to treatment. Note that the available options are 'Yes' and 'No', which indicates the patient's consent or refusal to the treatment.
8. Optionally, add any additional consent or description regarding the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name (text)**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number (text)**: Enter the emergency contact's contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (text)**: Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, child).
* **Patient Medical History (text)**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Allergy List (text)**: List any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Emergency Contact Info (text)**: Enter any additional contact information for the emergency contact.
* **Consent to Treatment (select multiple)**: Check the box to indicate consent to treatment. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Consent to Treatment Description (text)**: Optionally, add any additional consent or description regarding the treatment.
