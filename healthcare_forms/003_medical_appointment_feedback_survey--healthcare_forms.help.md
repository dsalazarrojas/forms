# Medical Appointment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Medical Appointment Feedback Survey, which is designed to collect your feedback on your recent medical appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information, which includes the date of your appointment.
* Select the type of appointment you had, such as Doctor, Dietitian, or Nurse.
* Rate your satisfaction with various aspects of your appointment, using a scale from 1-5.
* Rate the doctor's or staff member's bedside manner, timeliness, and overall satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Appointment Feedback Survey** (1):
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Appointment Date** (2):
	+ Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (3):
	+ Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time Slot** (4):
	+ If you had an appointment at a specific time slot (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.), enter it here.
* **Doctor's Name** (5):
	+ Enter the name of the doctor who saw you.
* **Staff Name** (6):
	+ Enter the name of the staff member who assisted you.
* **Appointment Type** (7):
	+ Select the type of appointment you had, such as Doctor, Dietitian, or Nurse.
* **Communication Level** (8):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the doctor's communication skills, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (9):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with your appointment, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Appointment Satisfaction** (10):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the appointment itself, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Wait Time** (11):
	+ Rate the wait time before your appointment, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Clarity** (12):
	+ Rate how clear your doctor or staff member explained things to you, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Empathy** (13):
	+ Rate how much you felt understood by your doctor or staff member, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Flexibility** (14):
	+ Rate your doctor or staff member's flexibility, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Efficiency** (15):
	+ Rate how efficiently your doctor or staff member managed their time, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Confidentiality** (16):
	+ Rate how confidential your conversation or files were kept, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Timeliness** (17):
	+ Rate how timely your appointment started and ended, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Staff Friendliness** (18):
	+ Rate how friendly and welcoming your staff member was, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Staff Knowledge** (19):
	+ Rate how knowledgeable your staff member was about your case, using a scale from 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction Score** (20):
	+ Enter your overall satisfaction score, which is calculated based on your ratings.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form as accurately and honestly as possible.
* Ensure that you are providing feedback for the correct appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.

Note: The "Overall Satisfaction Score" field is a calculated score based on your ratings, so please don't worry about filling it out if you're unsure. The system will calculate it for you.
