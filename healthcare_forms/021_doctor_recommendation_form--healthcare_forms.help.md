# doctor_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a doctor's recommendation for a patient's care, including their medical history, reason for referral, and any necessary next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **doctor_recommender** field with your name and title.
2.  In the **patient_name** field, enter the patient's name.
3.  In the **condition** field, briefly describe the patient's medical condition.
4.  In the **reason_for_referral** field, describe the reason for referring the patient to another medical professional.
5.  In the **medical_history** field, record the patient's medical history, including any relevant past illnesses or medical procedures.
6.  In the **patient_needs** field, describe any specific needs or concerns the patient has.
7.  In the **referring_doctor_details** field, provide any additional information about the referring doctor, if applicable.
8.  In the **date_of_last_contact** field, enter the date of the last contact with the patient.
9.  In the **date_of_next_follow_up** field, enter the date of the next follow-up appointment or check-in with the patient.
10. In the **next_follow_up_frequency** field, describe the frequency of the next follow-up appointment or check-in with the patient.
11. Select the **medical_specialty** field to specify the medical specialty of the referring doctor or medical professional.
12. In the **doctor_recommendation** field, describe your recommendation for the patient's care, including any necessary treatments or medications.
13. In the **patient_status** field, select the current status of the patient's care (e.g. Not started, In Progress, Resolved, Discontinued, or Waiting list).
14. In the **doctor_signature** field, sign your name as the doctor or medical professional.
15. In the **patient_signature** field, have the patient sign the form, if applicable.
