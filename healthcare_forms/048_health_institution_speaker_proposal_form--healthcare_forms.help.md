# health_institution_speaker_proposal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for speakers or individuals who would like to propose a new event to be hosted at a health institution. The form will help to collect relevant information about the proposed event, including the speaker's qualifications and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out this form, please make sure to have all the necessary information ready, including the event details, speaker's contact information, and any relevant attachments. The form will guide you through the necessary steps to submit your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution** (`id: 1`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the institution where you would like to host the event.
* **Speaker Name** (`id: 2`, `required: true`): Enter your name as the speaker or event proposer.
* **Speaker Title** (`id: 3`, `required: true`): Enter your title or professional position.
* **Bio** (`id: 4`, `required: true`): Briefly describe your background and qualifications as a speaker.
* **Speaker Experience** (`id: 5`, `required: true`): Describe your relevant experience as a speaker.
* **Speaker Qualification** (`id: 6`, `required: false`): If applicable, describe any relevant qualifications or certifications you hold.
* **Proposed Event** (`id: 7`, `required: true`): Briefly describe the proposed event, including its topic or theme.
* **Proposed Date** (`id: 8`, `required: true`): Enter the date you propose for the event to take place.
* **Proposed Time** (`id: 9`, `required: true`): Enter the time you propose for the event to take place.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 10`, `required: true`): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 11`, `required: true`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Message** (`id: 12`, `required: true`): Briefly describe any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Proposed Location** (`id: 13`, `required: true`): Select the location where you would like to host the event from the options provided.
* **Proposed Duration** (`id: 14`, `required: true`): Select the duration of the event from the options provided.
* **Additional Requirements** (`id: 15`, `required: false`): If you have any additional requirements, please describe them here.
* **Files** (`id: 16`, `required: false`): You can attach any relevant files or supporting documents to your proposal.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`id: 17`, `required: false`): Indicate whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`id: 18`, `required: true`): Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your proposal.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your proposal before submitting it to ensure that all the necessary information is included.
* If you have any supporting materials or documents, please attach them to the "Files" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Once you have submitted your proposal, it will be reviewed and assessed by the event organizers before being accepted or rejected.
