# prettyampgold-body-contouring-amp-spa-yoni-therapys-vaginal-steam-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pretty Amp Gold Body Contouring & Spa Yoni Therapy Vaginal Steam Intake Form is used to collect information about a client's body contouring and vaginal steam therapy experiences. This form is designed to ensure that clients can provide their preferences and concerns before undergoing the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose all that apply for each question by selecting the corresponding options for "Yes" and/or "No".
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you have selected all relevant options.
3. Once you have completed all sections, submit your response to proceed with the next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Body Contouring Questions** (`body_contouring_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please answer the following questions about your body contouring experiences:
  • What areas of your body do you want to address with body contouring? (Select all that apply)
  • Have you had any previous body contouring treatments or services? (Select all that apply)
  • What are your goals for your body contouring experience? (Select all that apply)
  • Are there any medical conditions or health concerns you'd like to disclose? (Select all that apply)

- **Steam Therapy Questions** (`steam_theraphy_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please answer the following questions about your steam therapy experiences:
  • Have you ever tried steam therapy? (Select all that apply)
  • Are you aware of any health concerns that may impact your steam therapy experience? (Select all that apply)

- **Yoni Questions** (`yoni_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please answer the following questions about your yoni experiences:
  • Have you had any previous yoni experiences? (Select all that apply)
  • Are you aware of any health concerns that may impact your yoni experience? (Select all that apply)

## Tips
