# Vaccine Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccine Waiver Form is a document used by patients to provide necessary information and consent for vaccine-related procedures. This form will guide you through the process of completing the form, explaining each field and its purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the Patient Information section**. This is where you'll provide general information about yourself as a patient.
2. **Enter your Patient Name**. Please type your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Enter your Date of Birth**. This field is for your date of birth, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Review and sign the form**. Carefully review the form's contents, then sign the **Doctor Signature** and **Patient Signature** fields to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This section captures general information about you as a patient. Please ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Patient Name**: This is your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Doctor Signature**: This is a reserved field for your healthcare provider to sign, indicating their approval of the vaccine waiver.
* **Patient Signature**: This is where you, as the patient, will sign to indicate your consent for the vaccine procedure.

## Tips
* Please ensure all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before signing to understand the terms and conditions of the vaccine waiver.
