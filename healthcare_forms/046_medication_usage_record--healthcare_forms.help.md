# Medication usage record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medication usage information from patients, allowing healthcare professionals to monitor and manage medication usage effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User information" section with your details.
2. Provide the "Medication information" in the following format: [Name of medication], [Dosage].
3. If applicable, fill in the "Dosage Information" section with details about the dosage.
4. Enter your "Usage Information" about the medication, such as how often it's taken, how it affects you, etc.
5. If necessary, describe your "Medical history" in the relevant section.
6. List your medical providers (doctors/clinics/hospitals) in the "Medical Providers" section.
7. Add any "Medical Notes" that you'd like to include.
8. Finally, sign the "Signature" section to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User information** (text, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Medication information** (text, required): Enter the name of the medication, followed by its dosage.
- **Dosage Information** (text, optional): If you're taking the medication, describe your dosage instructions, including how often it's taken.
- **Usage Information** (text, optional): Describe your usage of the medication, such as how it affects you.
- **Medical history** (text, optional): If you have any medical history that's relevant to the medication, enter it here.
- **Medical Providers** (text, optional): List the names and contact information of your medical providers.
- **Medical Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns about your medical treatment or history.
- **Signature** (text, required): Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

Please note that the "Dosage Information" and "Usage Information" fields are optional but are crucial for healthcare professionals to understand your treatment details. The "Medical Providers" and "Medical Notes" fields are also optional but can provide valuable context for your medical treatment. The "Signature" field is required to ensure that the information provided is accurate and authentic.
