# doctors_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **doctors_appointment_form** is a form used to capture information about a patient's appointment with a healthcare provider. It collects patient demographics, appointment schedule, and medical history to ensure a smooth and efficient appointment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Info** section.
3. Choose the appointment date in the **Appointment Date** section.
4. Select the appointment time in the **Appointment Time** section.
5. Indicate whether the doctor is aware of the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** section.
6. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** section.
7. Select the type of appointment in the **Appointment Type** section.
8. Enter any additional details about the appointment in the **Appointment Details** section.
9. Select the doctor who will be attending the appointment in the **Doctor** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info**: This section is used to collect the patient's demographic information.
  - **Name**: This is the patient's name.
  - **Appointment Date**: This is the date of the appointment.
  - **Appointment Time**: This is the time of the appointment.
  - **Doctor**: This is the healthcare provider who will be attending to the patient.
  - **Medical History**: A note field for the patient's medical history.
- **Appointment Type**: This is a select field to choose the type of appointment.
  - **Yes**: This option is for a routine appointment.
  - **No**: This option is not used.
- **Doctor**: This section is used to select the healthcare provider who will be attending the appointment.
  - **Yes**: This option indicates that the doctor is available.
  - **No**: This option indicates that the doctor is not available.
- **Patient Name**: This field is used to enter the patient's name.
- **Appointment Details**: This field is used to enter any additional details about the appointment.
- **Medical History**: This field is for taking notes about the patient's medical history.
- **Appointment Time**: This field is used to select the time of the appointment.
  - Note: The time format is not specified, but it should be in 24-hour format.
