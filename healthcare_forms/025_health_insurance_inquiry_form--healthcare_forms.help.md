# Health Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's health insurance inquiry. It is used to gather details about the policy holder's health, insurance policy, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the correct "Policy Holder Relationship" from the provided options.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have any health issues in the "Health Issue" field.
5. If you have health issues, provide a brief explanation in the "Health Issue" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to whether you have insurance inquiries in the "Insurance Inquiries" field.
7. If you have insurance inquiries, provide a brief explanation in the "Insurance Inquiries" field.
8. Enter the name of the policy holder in the "Policy Holder Name" field.
9. Enter the policy holder's date of birth in the "Policy Holder DOB" field.
10. Enter the policy holder's phone number in the "Policy Holder Phone" field.
11. Enter the policy holder's email address in the "Policy Holder Email" field.
12. If applicable, provide the policy holder's address in the "Policy Holder Address" field.
13. If applicable, provide the policy holder's notes in the "Policy Holder Notes" field.
14. If applicable, provide the policy holder's relationship details in the "Policy Holder Relationship Details" field.
15. Enter any additional medical history or reports in the "Medical History" field.
16. Enter any medical reports in the "Medical Report" field.
17. Select the health insurance provider from the provided options.
18. Enter the policy number if applicable.
19. Enter the health insurance provider's address if applicable.
20. Enter any agent notes if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
* **Health Issue** (text, required/optional): If you have any health issues, provide a brief explanation.
* **Health Insurance** (text, required/optional): Enter details about the policy holder's health insurance.
* **Policy Holder Information** (note, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder DOB** (date, required/optional): Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
* **Policy Holder Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the policy holder's phone number.
* **Policy Holder Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the policy holder's email address.
* **Policy Holder Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the policy holder's address.
* **Policy Holder Notes** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Relationship Details** (text, required/optional): Enter any relationship details about the policy holder.
* **Insurance Inquiries** (select multiple, required/optional): Select whether you have any insurance inquiries. If you do, provide a brief explanation in the "Insurance Inquiries" field.
* **Policy Holder Relationship** (select one, required/optional): Select the policy holder's relationship.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (text, required/optional): Enter the health insurance provider's name.
* **Policy Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the policy number if applicable.
* **Health Insurance Provider Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the health insurance provider's address if applicable.
