# Summer Camp Medical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document important medical information about each individual's health conditions, allergies, and vaccination status. This information will be used to ensure that any potential medical emergencies or treatments are handled properly during their time at the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields. You may skip any optional fields if not applicable to you. Start by providing general health information, followed by specific allergy and vaccination details. Then, provide emergency contact information, including the relationship between the emergency contact and the individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Information** (`health_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any health conditions that apply to you.
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Similar to the previous field, this section allows you to select any health conditions that you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you take any medications. Please note that 'Yes' indicates you are currently taking the medication and 'No' indicates you are not.
* **Vaccination Information** (`vaccination_information`, select_one, required): Select whether you are vaccinated or not. If not vaccinated, please describe the circumstances.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`, select_one, required): Select 'None' if you do not have any allergies, or 'Other' if you have allergies that are not listed.
* **First Medication** (`first_medication`, select_multiple, required): If applicable, select whether you take any medications. Please note that 'Yes' indicates you are currently taking the medication and 'No' indicates you are not.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your medication use. For example, 'Daily', 'Weekly', 'As needed', etc.
* **Second Medication** (`second_medication`, select_multiple, required): If applicable, select whether you take any other medications. Please note that 'Yes' indicates you are currently taking the medication and 'No' indicates you are not.
* **Other Medication** (`other_medication`, select_multiple, optional): If you take other medications not listed, select 'Yes' to provide more information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): Describe any medical history or conditions not listed in previous fields.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have an emergency contact and select their relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and you.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_multiple, optional): If you are under 18, indicate if your parents/guardians have given consent for treatment.
* **Medical History Other** (`medical_history_other`, text, optional): Provide any additional medical history or comments.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information or comments for the emergency contact.
* **Date Last Visit** (`date_last_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical visit.
* **Date Last Vaccination** (`date_last_vaccination`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last vaccination.
* **Date Next Vaccination** (`date_next_vaccination`, date, optional): Enter the date of your next vaccination.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Enter any medical record or comments.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Upload a photo of any medical device or equipment you use.
* **Medical History Additional** (`medical_history_additional`, note, optional): Provide any additional medical history or comments.
* **Allergies Medication** (`allergies_medication`, note, optional): Provide any information about allergies or medications that may affect your health.
* **Medical History Comments** (`medical_history_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or information about your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact Comments** (`emergency_contact_comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments or information about your emergency contact.
* **Medical History Other Comments** (`medical_history_other_comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments about your medical history.
