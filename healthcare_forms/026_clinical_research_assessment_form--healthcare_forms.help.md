# Clinical Research Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Research Assessment Form is used to collect patient eligibility and medical history data for clinical trials. This form is completed by medical professionals or researchers to determine a patient's suitability for participating in a clinical trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above in the "Step-by-Step Instructions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study ID or Protocol Number** (`study_id`, required): Enter the patient's study ID or protocol number.
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, required): Enter the study title.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, required): Enter your name.
* **Assessor Credentials** (`assessor_credentials`, optional): Enter your professional credentials (e.g., MD, RN, etc.).
* **Patient ID or MRN** (`patient_id`, required): Enter the patient's ID or medical record number.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, optional): Select the patient's ethnicity.
* **Race** (`race`, optional): Select the patient's race.
* **Height in cm** (`height_cm`, optional): Enter the patient's height in cm.
* **Weight in kg** (`weight_kg`, optional): Enter the patient's weight in kg.
* **BMI** (`bmi`, optional): The patient's BMI is calculated based on the height and weight entered.
* **Blood Pressure Systolic** (`vital_signs_bp_systolic`, optional): Enter the patient's blood pressure systolic in mmHg.
* **Blood Pressure Diastolic** (`vital_signs_bp_diastolic`, optional): Enter the patient's blood pressure diastolic in mmHg.
* **Heart Rate** (`vital_signs_heart_rate`, optional): Enter the patient's heart rate in bpm.
* **Temperature** (`vital_signs_temperature`, optional): Enter the patient's body temperature in Celsius.
* **Respiratory Rate** (`vital_signs_respiratory_rate`, optional): Enter the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Primary Diagnosis** (`primary_diagnosis`, required): Enter the patient's primary diagnosis.
* **Date of Diagnosis** (`diagnosis_date`, optional): Enter the date of the primary diagnosis.
* **Disease Stage or Severity** (`disease_stage`, optional): Enter the disease stage or severity.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, required): List the patient's current medications, including dosages.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, required): List the patient's medication allergies.
* **Other Allergies** (`other_allergies`, optional): List the patient's other allergies (e.g., food, latex, etc.).
* **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`, required): Enter the patient's relevant medical history.
* **Family Medical History** (`family_medical_history`, optional): Enter the patient's family medical history.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, optional): Enter the patient's social history (e.g., smoking, alcohol, drug use).
* **Recent Laboratory Results** (`lab_results_recent`, optional): Enter the patient's recent laboratory results, including dates and values.
* **Inclusion Criteria Met** (`inclusion_criteria_met`, required): Select whether the inclusion criteria have been met.
* **Any Exclusion Criteria Present** (`exclusion_criteria_present`, required): Select whether any exclusion criteria are present.
* **Exclusion Criteria Details** (`exclusion_criteria_details`, optional): Provide any additional exclusion criteria details.
* **Eligibility Status** (`eligibility_status`, required): Select the patient's eligibility status.
* **Informed Consent Status** (`consent_status`, required): Select the informed consent status.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, optional): Select the date of informed consent.
* **Baseline Assessments Completed** (`baseline_assessments_completed`, required): Select whether the baseline assessments are completed.
* **Eligible for Randomization** (`randomization_eligible`, optional): Select whether the patient is eligible for randomization.
* **Assessor Recommendation** (`assessor_recommendation`, required): Select the assessor's recommendation for the patient's enrollment.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's eligibility and medical history.
