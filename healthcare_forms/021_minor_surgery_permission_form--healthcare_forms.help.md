# Minor Surgery Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather necessary information for minor surgery procedures. It helps healthcare professionals to obtain consent from patients, document medical history, and ensure that the patient's contact information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the patient's name in the `Patient Name` field (field 12).
2. Provide the name of the procedure in the `Procedure Name` field (field 3).
3. Enter any relevant medical history in the `Medical History` field (field 4).
4. List any allergies the patient has in the `Allergies` field (field 5).
5. Enter the name of the surgical team in the `Surgical Team` field (field 6).
6. Enter the date of the surgery in the `Date of Surgery` field (field 7).
7. Enter the time of the surgery in the `Time of Surgery` field (field 8).
8. Enter the date of consent in the `Consent Date` field (field 9).
9. Enter the date of the patient's signature in the `Signature Date` field (field 10).
10. Enter your signature as the physician in the `Physician Signature` field (field 11).
11. Enter your name as the physician in the `Physician Name` field (field 12).
12. Enter your title (e.g., MD, DO, etc.) as the physician in the `Physician Title` field (field 13).
13. Enter the name of the facility where the surgery will take place in the `Facility Name` field (field 14).
14. Enter the address of the facility where the surgery will take place in the `Facility Address` field (field 15).
15. Enter the contact phone number of the facility in the `Contact Phone` field (field 16).
16. Enter the email of the contact person at the facility in the `Contact Email` field (field 17).
17. Enter the name of the contact person at the facility in the `Contact Name` field (field 18).
18. Enter the contact information of the facility in the `Facility Contact` field (field 19).
19. Enter any additional address information for the facility in the `Facility Address 2` field (field 20).
20. Enter your signature as the patient in the `Patient Signature` field (field 21).

**Note:** Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any). If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for assistance.
