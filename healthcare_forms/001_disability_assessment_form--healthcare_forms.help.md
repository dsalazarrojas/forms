# Disability Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, titled "Disability Assessment Form," is designed for healthcare providers to collect information about a patient's disabilities. The goal is to gather detailed data on the patient's conditions, symptoms, functional difficulties, and other needs to create a comprehensive care plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  **Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient has the conditions mentioned.**

2.  **For conditions that are selected as "Yes," please provide more details in the "Comments" field.**

3.  **Choose "Yes" or "No" for each listed symptom to help assess the patient's overall health status.**

4.  **Select "Yes" or "No" for each functional difficulty listed to identify any challenges the patient faces.**

5.  **For "Other Needs," select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires additional support.**

6.  **In the "Comments" field, provide any other relevant information not covered in this form.**

7.  **Enter the date and time of submission.**

8.  **The patient and healthcare provider must sign the form to confirm its accuracy.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Disability Assessment Form** (`title`, `note`, required): This is the main heading of the form.
*   **Student Patient** (`student_patient`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if the patient is a student or patient.
*   **Conditions** (`conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the conditions you have, with "Yes" or "No" answers.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the symptoms you have, with "Yes" or "No" answers.
*   **Functional Difficulties** (`functional_difficulties`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the functional difficulties you face, with "Yes" or "No" answers.
*   **Impact** (`impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the overall impact on your health, with "Yes" or "No" answers.
*   **Other Needs** (`other_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you have other needs, with "Yes" or "No" answers.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information not covered in this form.
*   **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical details.
*   **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, required: true): Enter your healthcare provider's name.
*   **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
*   **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this form.
*   **Student Patient Signature** (`student_patient_signature`, `note`, required: true): Sign this form to confirm your submission.
*   **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, `note`, required: true): Sign this form to confirm your assessment and recommendations.
