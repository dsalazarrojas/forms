# need-assistance-at-a-distance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Need Assistance at a Distance" form is designed to collect information from patients about their requirements for remote medical care, enabling healthcare providers to tailor their services accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select all the options that apply to your needs for assistance at a distance.
2. Enter your location and a detailed description of the issues you are facing.
3. Choose your preferred language for communication.
4. Select your time zone.
5. Provide your phone number and email address.
6. Choose whether you have identified the best doctors for your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Get In Touch With Doctors** (`get_in_touch_with_doctors`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the options that you need assistance from the doctors listed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location so that healthcare providers can better understand your needs.
* **Description Of Issues** (`description_of_issues`, text, required): Describe in detail the medical issues you are facing, so that healthcare providers can provide accurate assistance.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Choose the language you would like to communicate with your healthcare providers.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, required): Select your time zone to ensure that healthcare providers can accommodate your time requirements.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by healthcare providers.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates from healthcare providers.
* **Best Doctors** (`best_doctors`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have identified the best doctors for your needs.
