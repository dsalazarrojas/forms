# Pregnancy Journal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect detailed information about a new pregnancy, allowing healthcare professionals to track and evaluate the mother's health and the development of the baby. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as much as possible.
2. Be as detailed as you can in the text fields, providing as much information as possible.
3. Select the correct options for multiple-choice fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Pregnancy**: Enter a brief description of the new pregnancy.
* **Baby Birthdate**: Enter the expected date of the baby's birth.
* **Baby Weight**: Enter the weight of the baby (if known).
* **Baby Length**: Enter the length of the baby (if known).
* **Mother's Height**: Enter the height of the mother.
* **Mother's Weight**: Enter the weight of the mother.
* **Due Date**: Enter the expected date of the baby's birth.
* **Prophets and Predictions**: Enter any relevant medical notes about the pregnancy.
* **Baby Name**: Enter the name of the baby (if known).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the pregnancy.
* **Medical History**: Enter any medical conditions or concerns the mother has.
* **Meditations**: Enter any medications the mother is taking.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any medical notes about the pregnancy or birth.
* **Test Results**: Enter any test results relevant to the pregnancy.
* **Baby Test Results**: Enter any test results relevant to the baby.
* **Complications**: Indicate if there were any complications during the pregnancy.
* **Birth Location**: Indicate if the baby was born at home, in the hospital, or in another location.
* **Birth Method**: Indicate the method of birth (vaginal, C-section, etc.).
