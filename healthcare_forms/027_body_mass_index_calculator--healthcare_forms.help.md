# Body Mass Index Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Body Mass Index Calculator is a simple tool designed to estimate a user's Body Mass Index (BMI) based on their height and weight. The BMI is a widely used measurement to categorize individuals as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name.
2. Select the date on which you would like to perform the calculation.
3. Choose your preferred measurement system (Metric or Imperial).
4. Enter your weight in kilograms (if using Metric system) or pounds (if using Imperial system).
5. Enter your height in centimeters (if using Metric system) or feet and inches (if using Imperial system).
6. Select your gender.
7. Optionally, select your age group.
8. Optionally, select your relevant health conditions.
9. Optionally, select your current medications.
10. Select your exercise frequency.
11. Select your diet type.
12. Select your weight goals.
13. Indicate if you were aware of your BMI before.
14. Plan to discuss the results with a healthcare provider.
15. Accept the disclaimer that BMI is a screening tool only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (user_name, text, required/optional): Enter your name to be referenced for any follow-up discussions.
* **Date of Calculation** (calculation_date, date, required): Select the date on which you would like to perform the calculation.
* **Measurement System** (measurement_system, select_one, required): Choose your preferred measurement system, either Metric (kg and cm) or Imperial (lbs and inches).
* **Weight in Kilograms** (weight_kilograms, number, optional): Enter your weight in kilograms if you selected Metric system.
* **Weight in Pounds** (weight_pounds, number, optional): Enter your weight in pounds if you selected Imperial system.
* **Height in Centimeters** (height_centimeters, number, optional): Enter your height in centimeters if you selected Metric system.
* **Height - Feet** (height_feet, number, optional): Enter your height in feet if you selected Imperial system.
* **Height - Inches** (height_inches, number, optional): Enter your height in inches if you selected Imperial system.
* **Gender** (gender, select_one, optional): Select your gender for reference only.
* **Age Group** (age_group, select_one, optional): Optionally select your age group.
* **Relevant Health Conditions** (health_conditions, text, optional): Provide any relevant health conditions for context.
* **Current Medications** (medications, text, optional): List any current medications for context.
* **Exercise Frequency** (exercise_frequency, select_one, optional): Select how often you exercise.
* **Diet Type** (diet_type, select_one, optional): Choose your diet type.
* **Weight Goals** (weight_goals, select_one, optional): Select your weight goals.
* **Prior BMI Knowledge** (bmi_awareness, select_one, optional): Indicate if you were aware of your BMI before.
* **Plan to Discuss with Health Provider** (health_provider_consultation, select_one, optional): Plan to discuss the results with a healthcare provider.
* **I Understand BMI is a Screening Tool Only** (disclaimer_acceptance, select_one, required): Accept the disclaimer that BMI is a screening tool only.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct measurement system to ensure accurate calculations.
* Enter your weight and height values correctly in the selected measurement system.
* Select your gender for reference only, as it's not required for the calculation.
* Age group and health conditions are optional fields for additional context.
* Select your exercise frequency and diet type truthfully to get a more accurate calculation.
* Understand that BMI is not a diagnostic tool and should not be used to make medical decisions.
