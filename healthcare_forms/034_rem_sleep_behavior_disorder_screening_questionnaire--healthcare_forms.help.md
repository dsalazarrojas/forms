# rem_sleep_behavior_disorder_screening_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to assess an individual's sleep behavior disorders. This form is intended to be used by medical professionals to gather information about a patient's sleep-related experiences and behaviors, which can help in identifying potential sleep disorders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. For questions that require a numerical answer, please enter a whole number.
3. For questions that ask for a text description, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. If a question does not apply to you, you can skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can briefly describe your sleep behavior and any concerns you may have about your sleep.
* **Sleepiness**: Enter a number indicating how sleepy you feel most of the time, with higher numbers indicating greater sleepiness.
* **Vivid Hands Acts**: Describe any vivid hand actions you experience during sleep, such as talking, writing, or acting out.
* **Dreams**: Provide a text description of your dreams, including any recurring themes or nightmares.
* **Sleep Talking**: Describe any sleep talking you experience, such as talking to yourself or others in your sleep.
* **Sleepwalking**: Describe any sleepwalking episodes you've experienced, including any potential harm to yourself or others.
* **Sleep Drinking**: Describe any sleep drinking episodes you've experienced, including any potential harm to yourself or others.
* **Sleep Eating**: Describe any sleep eating episodes you've experienced, including any potential harm to yourself or others.
* **Sleep Parasitism**: Describe any sleep parasitism episodes you've experienced, including any potential harm to yourself or others.
* **Sleep Harm to Self**: Describe any harm you've experienced as a result of your sleep, including any injuries or accidents.
* **Sleep Harm to Other**: Describe any harm you've caused to others as a result of your sleep, including any injuries or accidents.
* **Sleep Harm to Other Details**: Provide more details about any harm you've caused to others as a result of your sleep.
* **Sleep Disorder**: If you believe you have a sleep disorder, provide a text description of your symptoms.
* **Sleep Disorder Duration**: Enter the duration of your sleep disorder, in weeks or months.
* **Rem Sleep B D**: Select one of the following options: Yes, No, or Sometimes, indicating whether you have ever experienced a sleep behavior disorder.
