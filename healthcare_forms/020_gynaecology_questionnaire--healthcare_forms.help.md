# Gynaecology Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gynaecology Questionnaire form is designed to collect information about patients' medical history, contact information, and appointment details to improve patient care and medical record accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Use the select options to choose relevant information about your medical history, medical specialist, and hospital admission.
4. Enter accurate contact information, including your phone number and email address.
5. Fill out the appointment date and time fields with your scheduled appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (Page 1): This page is for you to provide general information about yourself, such as your name, medical condition, and appointment date. Please fill out all the fields that apply to your situation.
* **Medical History** (Page 2): This page is for you to disclose your medical history, including any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with or have treated in the past. Select all the options that apply to your situation.
* **Contact Info** (Page 3): This page is for you to provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address. Please fill out all the fields that apply to your situation.

### Contact and Appointment Information
* **Phone Number** (Page 4): Please enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you with appointment reminders and updates.
* **Email Address** (Page 5): Enter your email address to receive appointment notifications and updates.
* **Appointment Date** (Page 6): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Page 7): Enter the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Medical Notes** (Page 8): This is a text field for you to add any important medical notes or information you'd like to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Specialist** (Page 9): Choose the medical specialist you are seeing, such as a gynaecologist or obstetrician.
* **Hospital Admission** (Page 10): If you have been admitted to a hospital, select the option that applies to your situation.
* **Discharge Date** (Page 11): Enter the date of your hospital discharge.
* **Medical Team** (Page 12): This is a multi-select field for you to choose any additional medical professionals involved in your care.
* **Gynaecology Questionnaire** (Page 13): This is an optional field for you to enter any additional information or comments.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully to ensure you have filled out all required information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* If you are unsure about any medical terms or conditions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for help.
* If you have any medical images or documents, you may attach them as supporting files.
* If you need assistance with completing the form, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
