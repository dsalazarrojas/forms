# Massage Salons Coronavirus Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's health status, travel history, and medical conditions before providing massage services. It is used to ensure the safety and well-being of both the client and the massage therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you are providing accurate and truthful information.
2. Enter your personal and contact information in the **Client Information** field.
3. Describe your current **COVID-19 Symptoms** in the corresponding field.
4. Share your **Medical History** and any existing medical conditions.
5. Report your **Travel History**, including recent travel destinations and dates.
6. Provide information about your current **Health Status**.
7. Answer questions about your **COVID-19 Health Status**.
8. Enter any **Test Results**, including COVID-19 test results.
9. Disclose any **Medical Condition** you may have.
10. Share information about your **COVID-19 Medical Condition**.
10. Enter your **Medication History**, including any medications you are currently taking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms** (`covid_symptoms`, text, required):
	+ Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required):
	+ Share any existing medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required):
	+ Report any recent travel destinations and dates.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, text, required):
	+ Please describe your current health status, including any health concerns or conditions.
* **COVID-19 Health Status** (`health_status_covid`, text, required):
	+ Answer questions about your current COVID-19 health status.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, required):
	+ Enter any COVID-19 test results, including positive or negative test outcomes.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required):
	+ Disclose any medical conditions you may have.
* **COVID-19 Medical Condition** (`medical_condition_covid`, text, required):
	+ Share information about any COVID-19 medical conditions you may have.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, text, required):
	+ Enter any medications you are currently taking.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate when completing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your massage therapist.
