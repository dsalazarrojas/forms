# outpatient_psychiatric_progress_note_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the outpatient psychiatric progress note template. The template is used to document the progress of patients receiving outpatient psychiatric care. It helps healthcare providers track and record important information about the patient's treatment, diagnosis, and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources before starting.
2. Select the relevant patient's identification number from the patient ID field.
3. Enter the date of the visit in the date of visit field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the name of the provider in the provider name field.
5. For the patient encounter type, select the appropriate option:
	* Initial: First time the patient is visiting the provider.
	* Follow-up: Subsequent visits after the initial visit.
6. For the patient caregiver relationship, select all applicable options:
	* Father
	* Mother
	* Son
	* Daughter
	* Other
7. In the chief complaint field, describe the reason for the patient's visit.
8. In the history of present illness field, provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history.
9. In the medication list field, select all applicable medications the patient is currently taking.
10. In the plan for treatment field, describe the treatment plan for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id**: A unique identifier for the patient.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the provider who is visiting the patient.
* **Patient Encounter Type**: Select the type of visit this is (Initial or Follow-up).
* **Patient Caregiver Relationship**: Select all applicable relationships between the patient and their caregiver.
* **Chief Complaint**: Describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **History of Present Illness**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history.
* **Medication List**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Plan for Treatment**: Describe the treatment plan for the patient.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the patient's care plan.
* Store the form securely and according to your organization's guidelines.
