# Public Health Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and authorization for releasing their health records. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date health information to healthcare providers for proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your personal details in the Patient Information section (page 1).
2. Provide your medical history in the Medical History section (page 2).
3. Specify whether you authorize the release of your health records to your healthcare provider (page 3).
4. Enter your date of birth, phone number, and email address (pages 4-6).
5. Enter your address, select your healthcare provider, and specify authorized parties for accessing your records (pages 7-9).
6. Sign the Authorized Signature section (page 10).
7. Date and time your signature in the Date of Signature and Time of Signature sections (pages 11-12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and address to identify yourself as the patient.
- **Medical History (Page 2)**: Describe any medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have undergone.
- **Authorization (Page 3)**: Specify whether you give permission to release your health records to your healthcare provider.
  - **True**: You grant permission to release your health records.
  - **False**: You decline to release your health records.
- **Date of Birth (Page 4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Phone Number (Page 5)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Email (Page 6)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Address (Page 7)**: Enter your current address for records delivery.
- **Healthcare Provider (Page 8)**: Select your healthcare provider from the list (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Jones, Dr. Doe).
- **Authorized Parties (Page 9)**: Specify who you authorize to access your health records (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse).
- **Authorized Signature (Page 10)**: Sign your name to confirm you are the patient.
- **Date of Signature (Page 11)**: Date and sign your name to confirm you are the patient.
- **Time of Signature (Page 12)**: Enter the time of your signature.
- **Tips**: Make sure to keep your form up-to-date and accurate to ensure your healthcare provider has the most recent information.
