# Oral Treatment Acceptance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Oral Treatment Acceptance Form is used to record patient acceptance or declination of a treatment. This form helps to document the patient's consent and understanding of the treatment, ensuring that all parties involved are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
* Enter the treatment details in the "Treatment Terms" field.
* Enter the treatment price in the "Treatments Price" field (if required).
* Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Patient Acceptance" field to indicate acceptance or declination of the treatment.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Oral Treatment Acceptance Form** (`oral_treatment_acceptance_form`, text, required: false): This is the main title of the form. Please do not change it.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's residential or mailing address.
* **Treatment Terms** (`treatment_terms`, text, required: false): Describe the treatment in detail, including any relevant medical history or allergy information.
* **Treatment Price** (`treatment_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the treatment if it applies.
* **Patient Acceptance** (`patient_acceptance`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you accept the treatment, or "No" if you decline. 
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required: false): Please sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance of the treatment.
