# health_insurance_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify health insurance information from patients or providers. It helps in ensuring accurate and up-to-date insurance details for medical billing and claims purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to enter accurate and complete details.
2. Start with the top fields that are marked as "required".
3. Move down the page, filling in the remaining fields based on your specific insurance needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **healthcare_provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated or referred the patient. This is a required field.
  - **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their insurance card or documentation. This is a required field.
  - **date_of_service**: Enter the date the patient received medical services. This field is not required but helps in tracking dates of service.
  - **policy_information**: Select the type of policy or plan that the patient has. Choose one of the options: "policy 1", "policy 2", or "policy 3".
  - **policy_number**: Enter the policy number associated with the selected policy. This is a required field.
  - **claim_information**: Select all the claims related to the patient's visit. Choose one or more of the options: "claim 1", "claim 2". This field is required.
  - **claim_number**: Enter the claim number associated with the selected claim. This is a required field.
  - **billing_information**: Enter the billing details for the patient's visit. This field is optional.
  - **insurance_provider**: Select the name of the insurance provider that covers the patient. Choose one of the options: "provider 1", "provider 2". This is a required field.
  - **insurance_plan**: Select all the insurance plans that the patient is covered under. Choose one or more of the options: "plan 1", "plan 2". This field is required.
  - **policy_holder**: Enter the name of the policy holder or policy owner. This is a required field.
  - **claim_status**: Select the status of the claim, such as "status 1", "status 2", or "status 3". This is a required field.
