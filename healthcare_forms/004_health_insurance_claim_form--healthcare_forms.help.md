# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers submit claims for patients who require medical attention or have incurred medical expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the patient, including their ID, provider name, claim date, claim amount, claim type, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the correct claim type from the options provided (Health, Dental, Vision, or Medical).
3. Enter the patient's ID, provider name, claim date, claim amount, address, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's treatment in the "Note" field.
5. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient ID
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's unique ID number.
### Provider Name
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated the patient.
### Claim Date
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date on which the patient received treatment.
### Claim Amount
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the total amount of money claimed for the patient's treatment.
### Claim Type
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim being made (Health, Dental, Vision, or Medical).
### Address
* **Address**: Enter the patient's mailing address.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
### Note
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's treatment.
### Submit
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information entered to avoid errors.
* If you need to submit multiple claims, you can save the form and re-open it later to continue working on it.
