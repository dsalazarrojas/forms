# Self Report Measures Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Introduction
Start by reading the introduction page and taking a few moments to understand the purpose and instructions of the form. This page is meant to provide a brief overview of what the form is about and what you can expect.

### Step 2: Symptoms
Choose the options that best describe your symptoms from the list on the next page. This page is designed to help us understand your current health status.

### Step 3: Physical Activity
If you have engaged in any physical activity today, enter the number of minutes you have spent on that activity.

### Step 4: Social and Mental Health
Select all the options that apply to your current situation on this page. This will help us assess your mental and social well-being.

### Step 5: Mental Health
Choose one of the options that best describes your current mental health status.

### Step 6: Self-Care and Support
Select one of the options that best describes your self-care and support situation.

### Step 7: Support Network
Select all the options that apply to your current support network on this page. This will help us understand who you rely on for emotional support.

### Step 8: Additional Information
Use this page to provide any additional information about your health status or situation that you think is important for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Symptoms 
* **Symptoms** (Symptoms, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describes your current symptoms.

### Physical Activity 
* **Physical Activity** (Physical Activity, number, optional): If you have engaged in any physical activity today, enter the number of minutes you have spent on that activity.

### Social and Mental Health 
* **Social and Mental Health** (Social and Mental Health, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your current situation.

### Mental Health 
* **Mental Health** (Mental Health, radio, required): Choose one of the options that best describes your current mental health status.

### Self-Care and Support 
* **Self-Care and Support** (Self-Care and Support, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describes your self-care and support situation.

### Support Network 
* **Support Network** (Support Network, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your current support network.

### Additional Information 
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, note, optional): Use this page to provide any additional information about your health status or situation that you think is important for us to know.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about what a question or option means, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* You can ask for help or clarification if you need it.
