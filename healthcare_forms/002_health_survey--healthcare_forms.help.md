# health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health-related information from patients, which will be used for medical research and analysis. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Answer each question carefully and truthfully**, providing as much detail as possible about your health history and current symptoms.
2. **Use the provided text fields** to enter your answers, and make sure to fill out every question on each page.
3. **Submit the form** once you have completed all the questions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Questions** (text, required: false): This section asks for general information about your health history. Please provide any relevant details about your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Medical History** (text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional medical history or information that may be relevant to your health.
* **Symptoms** (text, required: false): Describe your current symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or unusual sensations you are experiencing.

## Tips
* Please answer every question carefully and thoroughly, as the information collected will be used for research and analysis.
* If you are unsure about any question or feel that the information requested is not relevant, please ask for clarification.
* Ensure that your answers are accurate and up-to-date, as incorrect or outdated information may affect the validity of the research results.
