# Dental Treatment Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver from a patient for dental treatment. A waiver is a written statement that acknowledges a patient's understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions of their dental treatment. This form helps to ensure that the patient is aware of their rights and responsibilities and provides a clear understanding of the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **dental_treatment_waiver** title in the first field.
2. Provide your **patient_name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **medical_practice_name** in the third field (if required).
4. Select the **treatment_date** using the date picker or enter it manually.
5. Enter your **contact_number** in the eighth field.
6. Provide your **patient_email** (if required).
7. Enter your **medical_practice_license** (if required).
8. Read and agree to the **waiver_terms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dental_treatment_waiver** (1, text, required: false):
  A brief title to identify the waiver.

* **patient_name** (2, text, required: true):
  The patient's name who is receiving the treatment.

* **medical_practice_name** (3, text, required: false):
  The name of the medical practice providing the treatment (optional).

* **treatment_date** (4, date, required: true):
  The date when the treatment will take place.

* **doctor_signature** (5, text, required: false):
  The signature of the doctor providing the treatment.

* **patient_signature** (6, text, required: false):
  The signature of the patient receiving the treatment.

* **contact_number** (7, text, required: true):
  A contact number for the medical practice.

* **patient_email** (8, email, required: false):
  The email address of the patient (if required).

* **medical_practice_license** (9, text, required: false):
  The license number of the medical practice (if required).

* **waiver_terms** (10, text, required: false):
  A text field to display the waiver terms, which the patient must read and agree to.
