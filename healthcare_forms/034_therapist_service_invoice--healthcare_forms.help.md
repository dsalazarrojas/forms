# Therapist Service Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a service invoice for a therapist's services. It is designed to collect session details, payment information, and billing data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the session details section, including the session date and start and end times.
2. Enter the therapist's details in the Therapist Details field.
3. Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, or bank transfer).
4. Enter the amount paid and any additional notes.
5. Provide the billing information, including the email and phone number of the bill-to person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (Session Details, text, required): This section requires you to enter the details of the session, including the date and start and end times.
* **Bill To** (Bill To, text, required): This field asks for the name or company the bill is being sent to.
* **Therapist Details** (Therapist Details, text, required): This field is for entering the name and other relevant details of the therapist.
* **Session Date** (Session Date, date, required): Enter the date of the session in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required): Enter the start time of the session in the 12:00 AM/PM format.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required): Enter the end time of the session in the 12:00 AM/PM format.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, required): Describe the services provided by the therapist.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select multiple, optional): Select the payment method used for the session (e.g., credit card, debit card, or bank transfer).
* **Amount Paid** (Amount Paid, number, required): Enter the total amount paid for the session.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the session.
* **Bill To Email** (Bill To Email, email, optional): Enter the email of the person being billed.
* **Bill To Phone** (Bill To Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person being billed.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Double-check the accuracy of the session details and billing information.
* Use the correct payment method and format for the amount paid.
* Keep the notes field for any additional information that may be relevant to the session.
