# Locate Medical Professionals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Locate Medical Professionals Form is designed to gather information about medical professionals, including their preferences, location, and medical history. This form is intended to be used by healthcare providers to locate medical professionals who match specific criteria, such as location, specialization, and medical status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as needed.
2. Select relevant options for the "Specialization", "Insurance Provider", "Medical History", "Patient Status", and "Patient Condition" fields.
3. Enter the "Phone Number" and "Email Address" of the medical professional or patient.
4. Select relevant options for the "Patient Relationship", "Patient Medication", and "Medical Record" fields.
5. Fill in the "Patient Age", "Patient Gender", "Medical Profession", "Patient DOB", "Patient Address", and "Medical Specialty" fields.
6. Select the "Medical License" and "Medical Employer" fields.
7. Select any additional relevant medical notes or comments in the "Medical Note" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Preferences** (id: 1, label: User Preferences, type: text, required: false): This field is for entering any additional information or preferences that may be relevant to the medical professional or patient.
* **Location** (id: 2, label: Location, type: number, required: false): Enter the location where the medical professional or patient is based.
* **Specialization** (id: 3, label: Specialization, type: select_one, required: false): Select whether the medical professional specializes in a specific area.
* **Insurance Provider** (id: 4, label: Insurance Provider, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select which insurance providers are associated with the medical professional or patient.
* **Patient ID** (id: 5, label: Patient ID, type: number, required: false): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Medical History** (id: 6, label: Medical History, type: note, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
* **Contact Info** (id: 7, label: Contact Info, type: select_one, required: false): Select whether the patient or medical professional has contact information available.
* **Phone Number** (id: 8, label: Phone Number, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the medical professional or patient.
* **Email Address** (id: 9, label: Email Address, type: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the medical professional or patient.
* **Patient Relationship** (id: 10, label: Patient Relationship, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the relationship between the medical professional and the patient.
* **Patient Age** (id: 11, label: Patient Age, type: number, required: false): Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Profile** (id: 12, label: Medical Profile, type: text, required: false): Enter a brief medical profile of the patient.
* **Patient Gender** (id: 13, label: Patient Gender, type: select_one, required: false): Select the gender of the patient.
* **Medical Profession** (id: 14, label: Medical Profession, type: select_one, required: false): Select the medical profession of the medical professional.
* **Patient DOB** (id: 15, label: Patient DOB, type: date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (id: 16, label: Patient Address, type: text, required: false): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Specialty** (id: 17, label: Medical Specialty, type: select_one, required: false): Select the medical specialty of the patient.
* **Medical License** (id: 18, label: Medical License, type: select_one, required: false): Select whether the medical professional has a medical license.
* **Medical Employer** (id: 19, label: Medical Employer, type: text, required: false): Enter the medical professional's employer.
* **Patient Status** (id: 20, label: Patient Status, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the patient's status, such as active or inactive.
* **Patient Condition** (id: 21, label: Patient Condition, type: select_one, required: false): Select the patient's condition.
* **Patient Medication** (id: 22, label: Patient Medication, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the patient's medication.
* **Medical Record** (id: 23, label: Medical Record, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select whether the patient has a medical record.
* **Medical Note** (id: 24, label: Medical Note, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Submit** (id: 25, label: Submit, type: text, required: false): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

* Use the "Patient Relationship" field to select the relationship between the medical professional and the patient.
* Use the "Patient Status" field to select the patient's status.
* Use the "Patient Condition" field to select the patient's condition.
* Use the "Patient Medication" field to select the patient's medication.
