# Treatment Plan Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for healthcare professionals to collect and document essential information for developing a treatment plan for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the first page, "Patient Info", where you will provide basic patient information such as name, medical record number, and other identifying details.
* Move on to the second page, "Treatment Plan", where you will outline the planned treatment, including diagnosis, treatment goals, and potential medication or therapy options.
* Continue to the third page, "Medical History", where you will collect information about the patient's medical history, including past illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* Finally, review and edit the information on the fourth page, "Review and Edit", to ensure accuracy and completeness before finalizing the treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (Page 1)** (`Patient Name`, `Text`, required):
	+ Enter the patient's full name, medical record number, and other identifying details as prompted.
* **Treatment Plan (Page 2)** (`Treatment Plan`, `Text`, required):
	+ Describe the planned treatment, including the diagnosis, treatment goals, and potential medication or therapy options.
* **Medical History (Page 3)** (`Medical History`, `Text`, required):
	+ Provide information about the patient's past illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Review and Edit (Page 4)** (`Review and Edit`, `Text`, required):
	+ Review the information gathered on the previous pages and make any necessary edits or additions before finalizing the treatment plan.
