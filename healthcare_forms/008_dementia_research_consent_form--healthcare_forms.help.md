# Dementia Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to elicit informed consent from participants in research studies related to dementia. Please complete the form carefully to ensure you understand the terms of your involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review each field carefully before submitting.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Title** (study_title, text, required: false)
  • This field asks for a brief description of the study.
  • Please provide a short title of the study, e.g., "Exploring Cognitive Impairment in Dementia Patients".
- **Participant Information** (participant_info, text, required: false)
  • This field requests personal details about you, the participant.
  • Please provide your full name, date of birth, and contact information.
- **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required: false)
  • This field is for your contact details.
  • Please enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
- **Research Team** (research_team, text, required: false)
  • This field is for the names and roles of the research team members.
  • Please list the names and positions of the people involved in the study.
- **Informed Consent** (informed_consent, text, required: false)
  • This field requires you to confirm your understanding and agreement to participate in the study.
  • Please provide a brief statement of your consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will get back to you to confirm your consent.
