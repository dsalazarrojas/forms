# nicole_pass_1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **nicole_pass_1** form is used to collect personal and medical information from patients or individuals for healthcare-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. If applicable, enter your **Date Of Birth** in the third field. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
4. Provide your **Contact Number** in the fourth field, as this will be used for communication.
5. Enter your **Email** (if applicable) in the fifth field.
6. If you have any **Medical History**, describe it in the sixth field.
7. Indicate if you have any **Medical Condition** in the seventh field. Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to respond to this question.
8. Specify any **Medical Procedure** you have or are undergoing, by selecting multiple options if applicable.
9. If you have seen a doctor, enter their **Doctor Name** in the ninth field.
10. If you have seen a doctor, specify their **Doctor Specialty** in the tenth field.
11. If you have seen a doctor, enter their **Doctor License Number** in the eleventh field.
12. If you have seen a doctor, provide their **Doctor Contact** information in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): Describe any medical history you have.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have any medical conditions. Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify any medical procedures you have undergone or are undergoing.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): If you have seen a doctor, enter their name.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, `text`, optional): If you have seen a doctor, specify their specialty.
* **Doctor License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, `text`, optional): If you have seen a doctor, enter their license number.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have seen a doctor, provide their contact information.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or the form administrators.
