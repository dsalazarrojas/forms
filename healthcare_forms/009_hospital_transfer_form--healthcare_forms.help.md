# Hospital Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Transfer Form is designed to collect information for hospital transfers, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data collection and streamlining the process for healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Medical Record Number** in the "Medical Record Number" field.
4. Select the date of transfer in the "Date of Transfer" field using the calendar icon to select the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for accurate identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for accurate identification.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Provide your hospital or healthcare provider's unique identifier for medical record management.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, date, optional): Select the date of the hospital transfer using the calendar icon.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate smooth hospital transfers.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact form@gic.mx for clarification.
* This form is designed to be used for hospital transfers only; do not use it for other purposes.
