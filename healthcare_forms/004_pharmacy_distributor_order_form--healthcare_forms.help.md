# pharmacy_distributor_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and order information from pharmacy distributors for accurate order processing and reporting in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the contact information fields with the relevant details for your pharmacy distribution business.
2.  Provide order details, including any specific products being ordered.
3.  Enter your contact email address so that we can communicate with you about your order.
4.  Enter your contact phone number for easy phone communication about your order.
5.  Enter your contact fax number for any faxed documents or confirmation.
6.  Enter your contact address for accurate shipping and reporting purposes.
7.  Enter any order notes or special instructions for our team.
8.  Select whether you have been assigned a tool for this order.
9.  Select whether you have been assigned a user for this order.
10. Select whether this order was created by our system or by an external user.
11.  Select whether this order was submitted by your organization or by an external entity.
12.  Enter the date the order was submitted.
13.  Select whether you or your organization submitted this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the contact information for your pharmacy distribution business.
* **Order Details**: (`order_details`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter specific details about the order being placed.
* **Products Ordered**: (`products_ordered`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the products being ordered.
* **Contact Email**: (`contact_email`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the email address to contact your pharmacy distribution business.
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the phone number to contact your pharmacy distribution business.
* **Contact Fax**: (`contact_fax`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the fax number to contact your pharmacy distribution business.
* **Contact Address**: (`contact_address`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the address to contact your pharmacy distribution business for accurate shipping and reporting purposes.
* **Order Notes**: (`order_notes`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you have been assigned a tool for this order.
* **Assigned User**: (`assigned_user`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you have been assigned a user for this order.
* **Created By**: (`created_by`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether this order was created by our system or by an external user.
* **Submitted On**: (`submitted_on`, date, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the date the order was submitted.
