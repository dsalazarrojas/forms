# Sleep Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients' sleep consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "general_info" field with any relevant information about the patient.
2. Select one or multiple options from the "questions" field to indicate what specific questions the patient has about their sleep.
3. Choose one option from the "treatment_options" field to indicate what treatment options the patient is interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **general_info** (`general_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient.
* **questions** (`questions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or multiple options to indicate what specific questions the patient has about their sleep. Options are: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **treatment_options** (`treatment_options`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option to indicate what treatment options the patient is interested in. Options are: 'Yes', 'No'.
