# healthcare_design_thinking_lab_filming_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The healthcare_design_thinking_lab_filming_consent_form is used to collect participant information and consent for filming in a healthcare setting. This form helps to ensure that participants are aware of the purposes of recording and provide their consent for the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields.
2. Enter participant information in the Participant Information section.
3. Select the recording consents for your activity.
4. Choose the type of recording media to be used.
5. Enter your consent to record, along with any additional comments.
6. Enter the participant's photo or video, if applicable.
7. Enter contact information for the participant.
8. Enter any additional comments or information from the participant.
9. Enter the healthcare organization's information.
10. Enter the filming consent date.
11. Select who signed the recording consent.
11. Enter the participant's signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant Information
**Participant Information** (text)
 Enter the participant's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.

### 2. Recording Consents
**Recording Consents** (select multiple)
Select all applicable reasons for recording, such as:
	* Recording for education and research purposes
	* Recording for promotional use by our organization
	* Recording for internal project team meetings

### 3. Recording Media
**Recording Media** (select one)
Choose the type of recording media to be used:
	* Video
	* Audio
	* Both

### 4. Consent to Record
**Consent to Record** (select one)
Select one of the following options:
	* 'True'
	* 'False'

### 5. Participant's Photo or Video
**Participant's Photo or Video** (select one)
Select whether a photo or video of the participant is to be recorded:
	* 'True'
	* 'False'

### 6. Contact Information
**Contact Information** (select multiple)
Select all applicable contact methods:
	* Phone Number
	* Email
	* Both

### 7. Participant Comments
**Participant Comments** (text)
Enter any additional comments or information from the participant.

### 8. Healthcare Organization
**Healthcare Organization** (text)
Enter the name of the healthcare organization.

### 9. Filming Consent Date
**Filming Consent Date** (date)
Enter the date the consent was given.

### 10. Recording Consent Signed By
**Recording Consent Signed By** (select one)
Select who signed the consent:
	* Participant
	* Representative

### 11. Participant's Signature
**Participant's Signature** (text)
Enter the participant's signature, if required.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is intended for authorized personnel only. If you are not authorized to complete this form, please do not proceed.
