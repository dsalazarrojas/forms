# surgery_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing surgery. It gathers information about the patient, the surgery details, and the consent process to ensure the patient understands their rights, responsibilities, and the potential risks and benefits of the surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Patient Details" section.
2. Provide information about the surgery, including the date and time it will take place.
3. Sign the form to indicate your understanding and consent for the surgery.
4. Have your doctor sign the form as well to confirm their agreement to perform the surgery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant information about yourself to help us get in touch with you.
* **Surgery Details** (`surgery_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about the surgery, including the reason for the procedure, the expected outcome, and any specific instructions or precautions you should follow.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign your name below to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of the surgery.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required: false): This is the date you are completing this form.
* **Date Surgery** (`date_surgery`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the surgery will take place.
* **Time Surgery** (`time_surgery`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the surgery will take place.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign your name below to confirm your agreement to perform the surgery.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand all the information on this form before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your doctor or medical staff.
* Please sign this form before the surgery takes place to ensure you are aware of the risks and benefits of the procedure.
