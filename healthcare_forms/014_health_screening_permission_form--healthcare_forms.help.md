# Health Screening Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Permission Form is used to obtain health screening permissions from patients or clients in a healthcare setting. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential information and consent from individuals before performing certain medical screenings, procedures, or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Provider Name** (1) to identify the healthcare provider administering the screening.
2. Select **Screening Type** (2) to indicate the type of screening being performed, such as 'Yes' for an annual checkup or 'No' for a specific procedure.
3. Enter the **Screening Date** (3) when the screening was performed or is scheduled to take place.
4. Provide the **Consent Date** (4) when the patient or client gave their consent for the screening.
5. Describe the **Reason for Screening** (5) to help healthcare providers understand the context and purpose of the screening.
6. Enter the **Screening Location** (6) where the screening was performed or is scheduled to take place.
7. Specify the **Screening Method** (7) used during the screening, such as blood draw or X-ray.
8. Enter the **Screening Result** (8) of the test or procedure, if applicable.
9. Provide the **Screening Frequency** (9) of the screening, such as how often the screening is performed.
10. Select the **Screening Status** (10) of the screening, whether it's **Active** or **Inactive**.
11. Enter any **Screening Comments** (11) for additional information or remarks.
12. Confirm whether **Consent Was Given** (12) by selecting 'True' or 'False'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title or credentials.
* **Screening Type** (`screening_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of screening being performed.
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the screening or when it is scheduled to occur.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the patient or client gave their consent.
* **Reason for Screening** (`reason_for_screening`, `text`, required): Describe the purpose or reason for the screening.
* **Screening Location** (`screening_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the screening or where it will take place.
* **Screening Method** (`screening_method`, `text`, required): Describe the method or technique used during the screening.
* **Screening Result** (`screening_result`, `text`, required): Enter the result of the screening or test.
* **Screening Frequency** (`screening_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify how often the screening is performed.
* **Screening Status** (`screening_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the screening is **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Screening Comments** (`screening_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the screening.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether consent was given, by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
