# Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mental Health Journal Template is designed to help individuals track their mental health and wellness over time. This template includes several sections to rate and report on daily mood, mental health status, emotions, and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your daily mood by selecting a number between 1 and 10.
2. Indicate your current mental health status by selecting from the options provided.
3. Record any progress you've made towards your mental health goals.
4. List any emotions you've experienced today.
5. List any emotions you've experienced over the last 2 days.
6. Indicate whether you have a mental health goal or not.
7. Select the status of your mental health goal.
8. Rate your mental health on a scale of 1-10.
9. Rate your mental health again on a scale of 1-10 (this is optional).
10. Rate how your mental health is perceived by 1-8 raters (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Mood Rating (1-10)** (<code>1, number</code>): Rate your current mood on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Mental Health Status** (<code>Mental Health Status</code>, select_one): Select your current mental health status from the options provided.
* **Progress Notes** (<code>Progress Notes</code>, text): Describe any progress you've made towards your mental health goals.
* **Emotions Today** (<code>Emotions Today</code>, select_multiple): List any emotions you've experienced today.
* **Emotions Over Last 2 Days** (<code>Emotions Over Last 2 Days</code>, select_multiple): List any emotions you've experienced over the last 2 days.
* **Mental Health Goal** (<code>Mental Health Goal</code>, select_one): Indicate whether you have a mental health goal or not.
* **Goal Status** (<code>Goal Status</code>, select_one): Select the status of your mental health goal.
* **Mental Health Rating (1-10)** (<code>Mental Health Rating (1-10)</code>, number): Rate your mental health on a scale of 1-10.
* **Mental Health Rating 2 (1-10)** (<code>Mental Health Rating 2</code>, number): This is an optional field to rate your mental health again on a scale of 1-10.
* **Mental Health Rater 1 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 1 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 1.
* **Mental Health Rater 2 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 2 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 2.
* **Mental Health Rater 3 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 3 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): This is an optional field to rate your mental health as perceived by rater 3.
* **Mental Health Rater 4 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 4 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 4.
* **Mental Health Rater 5 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 5 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 5.
* **Mental Health Rater 6 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 6 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 6.
* **Mental Health Rater 7 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 7 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 7.

* **Mental Health Rater 9 (Good/Fair)** (<code>Mental Health Rater 9 (Good/Fair)</code>, select_one): Rate your mental health as perceived by rater 9.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when rating your mental health and emotions.
* Use this template regularly to track your progress and identify patterns.
* Review and reflect on your entries to make adjustments to your mental health goals and strategies.
* Consider sharing your entries with a mental health professional for guidance and support.
