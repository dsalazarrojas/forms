# Care Provider Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Provider Application Form is a form designed to collect information from applicants who wish to provide care services to our organization. This form is not applicable to existing employees and contractors of our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your applicant information on page 1.
2. Fill out the Full name field with your legal name.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your phone number in the Phone number field.
5. Enter your current residential address in the Address field.
6. Describe your professional qualifications in the Professional qualifications field.
7. Enter the number of years of experience you have in caregiving in the Years of experience field.
8. Provide at least two professional references in the Reference contacts field.
9. Answer the Consent for background check field with either 'True' or 'False'.
10. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the Additional notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your current residential address.
* **Professional qualifications** (Professional qualifications, text, required): Describe your certifications, licenses, and relevant skills.
* **Years of experience** (Years of experience, number, optional): Enter the total number of years you have spent in caregiving.
* **Reference contacts** (Reference contacts, text, required): Provide at least two professional references, including their name, job title, and contact information.
* **Consent for background check** (Consent for background check, select_one, required): Answer with either 'True' or 'False' to indicate your consent for background check.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): Enter any other information you wish to provide about yourself.

# Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure proper processing of your application.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* Provide complete and accurate information about your professional qualifications.
* Make sure to provide at least two professional references.
