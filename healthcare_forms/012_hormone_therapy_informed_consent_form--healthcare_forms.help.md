# Hormone Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from patients before undergoing hormone therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields:
	* **patient_id**: Enter your unique patient ID.
	* **patient_name**: Enter your full name.
	* **patient_dob**: Enter your date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD).
	* **patient_gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
	* **patient_contact_info**: Enter your contact information (phone number and/or email).
	* **doctor_name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
	* **doctor_title**: Enter the title of your healthcare provider (MD, DO, NP, etc.).
	* **therapy_prophets**: Select the dosage of hormone therapy you will be receiving (1mg, 1.25mg, 1.5mg, 2mg, or 4mg).
	* **start_date**: Enter the date you will start hormone therapy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This section serves as the title of the form.
* **patient_id**: A unique identifier assigned to you for medical records.
* **patient_name**: Your full name for medical records.
* **patient_dob**: The date of your birth (important for medical history).
* **patient_gender**: A selection of your gender for medical records.
* **patient_contact_info**: Your contact information (phone number and/or email).
* **doctor_name**: The name of your healthcare provider.
* **doctor_title**: The title of your healthcare provider (MD, DO, NP, etc.).
* **therapy_prophets**: The dosage of hormone therapy you will be receiving.
* **start_date**: The date you will start hormone therapy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to ensure accurate medical records.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form should not be used as a substitute for medical guidance, but rather as a tool to inform and document your consent.
* Please review and sign the form to confirm your informed consent.
