# patient_belongings_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient belongings inventory form is used to track and manage patient's belongings, including their status, location, and value.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the patient identifier.
2. Choose the type of belonging (e.g., Phone, Wallet, Watch, etc.).
3. Provide a detailed description of the belonging.
4. Select the status of the belonging (e.g., Available, Missing, Damaged, etc.).
5. Choose the location of the belonging (e.g., Room, Ward, Closet, etc.).
6. Choose the security measures applied to the belonging (e.g., Locked, Tagged, Identified, etc.).
7. Enter the value of the belonging.
8. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information (if necessary).
9. Enter the returning date (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional remarks regarding the belonging.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier**: This is a unique identification number assigned to the patient. This field is required.
* **Belonging_type**: Choose the type of belonging from the available options (e.g., Phone, Wallet, Watch, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the belonging. This field is optional.
* **Status**: Select the status of the belonging from the available options (e.g., Available, Missing, Damaged, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Location**: Choose the location of the belonging from the available options (e.g., Room, Ward, Closet, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Security_measure**: Select the security measures applied to the belonging from the available options (e.g., Locked, Tagged, Identified, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Value**: Enter the value of the belonging. This field is optional.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name. This field is optional.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth. This field is optional.
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information. This field is optional.
* **Returning Date**: Enter the date the belonging will be returned. This field is optional.
* **Remarks**: Provide any additional information regarding the belonging. This field is optional.
