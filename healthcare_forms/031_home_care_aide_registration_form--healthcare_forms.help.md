# Home Care Aide Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual as a home care aide. It is necessary for us to have your personal details and to verify your status as a home care aide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you are associated with a medical practice, enter the name in the "Medical Practice" field.
6. If you are a caretaker or companion, select "A". If you are neither, select "C".
7. If you are a healthcare worker, select "P". If you are not, select "V".
8. If you are a volunteer, select "Y". If you are not, select "N".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Practice**: Enter the name of your associated medical practice.
* **Are you a caretaker or companion**: Select "A" if you are a caretaker or companion, "C" otherwise.
* **Healthcare worker**: Select "P" if you are a healthcare worker, "V" otherwise.
* **Volunteer**: Select "Y" if you are a volunteer, "N" otherwise.
