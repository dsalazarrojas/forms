# Psychology Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Checklist form is designed to collect relevant information from patients or clinicians for the assessment, planning, and monitoring of mental health services. This form may be used by mental health professionals for diagnosis, treatment planning, and research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for the form.
2. Fill in the **Patient Identifier** field with the patient's unique identifier or reference number.
3. Enter the **Assessment Date** when the patient's mental health was assessed.
4. Provide the patient's **Name** in the **Patient Name** field.
5. Select the primary diagnostic from the drop-down menu in the **Primary Diagnostic** field.
6. Select any additional diagnostcis that apply for the **Secondary Diagnostic** field.
7. Add any relevant information for **Treatment Planning** field.
8. Provide any relevant educational or training information in the **Education** field.
9. Record any relevant research or study data in the **Research** field.
10. Enter any quality assurance comments in the **Quality Assurance** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Identifier
• **Patient Identifier** (`patient_ident`, `text`, required: false): A unique identifier or reference number for the patient.

### Assessment Date
• **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required: false): The date when the patient's mental health was assessed.

### Patient Name
• **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): The patient's full name.

### Primary Diagnostic
• **Primary Diagnostic** (`primary_diagnostic`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the primary diagnostic from the drop-down menu.

### Secondary Diagnostic
• **Secondary Diagnostic** (`secondary_diagnostic`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional diagnostcis that apply.

### Treatment Planning
• **Treatment Planning** (`treatment_planning`, `text`, required: false): Add any relevant information for treatment planning.

### Education
• **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant educational or training information.

### Research
• **Research** (`research`, `text`, required: false): Record any relevant research or study data.

### Quality Assurance
• **Quality Assurance** (`quality_assurance`, `text`, required: false): Enter any quality assurance comments.
