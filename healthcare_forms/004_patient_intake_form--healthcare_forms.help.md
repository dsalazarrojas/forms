# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's medical history, contact details, and other relevant information for healthcare professionals to better understand their needs and provide effective care. This form is intended for patients, but it can also be used by healthcare providers to gather necessary information prior to appointments or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. List any medical conditions you are experiencing, including heart issues, diabetes, or hypertension, in the "Health Problems" section.
3. Select any relevant medical conditions or allergies from the list provided in the "Medical Conditions" section.
4. Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions of this form.
5. If applicable, provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
6. Sign and date the form.
7. Submit the completed form to the healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Information)** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and date of birth in this field.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, optional): Enter any contact information you have, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`note`, optional): Use this field to provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Health Problems (Health Problems)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the list of health problems provided.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the list of medical conditions provided.
* **Consent (Consent)** (`select_one`, optional): Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions of this form.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`text`, optional): Enter emergency contact information, if applicable.
* **Signature (Signature)** (`text`, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your identity.
* **Date Created (Date Created)** (`date`, optional): This field displays the date and time this form was created.
* **Time Created (Time Created)** (`time`, optional): This field displays the date and time this form was created.
* **Date Modified (Date Modified)** (`date`, optional): This field displays the date and time this form was last modified.
* **Time Modified (Time Modified)** (`time`, optional): This field displays the date and time this form was last modified.
* **Created By (Created By)** (`text`, optional): This field displays the name of the person who created this form.

Tips:
* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your healthcare provider before submitting the form.
* This form is for your protection and benefit; it helps healthcare providers understand your medical needs and provide better care.
