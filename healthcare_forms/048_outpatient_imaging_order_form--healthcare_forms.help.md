# outpatient_imaging_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outpatient_imaging_order_form is a crucial document used to gather essential information for outpatient imaging orders in a healthcare setting. It's designed to facilitate accurate and efficient processing of these orders, ensuring that patients receive the necessary care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1.  Ensure you have the required information and authority to fill out this form.
2.  Enter the patient's information (patient's name and medical record number) in the designated fields.
3.  Provide the date and time of the service.
4.  Specify if an imaging is needed.
5.  Select if the service has been authorized by a provider.
6.  Enter the date and time the order was placed.
7.  Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name and medical record number for proper identification and record-keeping.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): Enter the patient's unique medical record number for accurate tracking and billing purposes.
*   **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`): Enter the date on which the imaging service is to be performed.
*   **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`): Enter the time at which the imaging service is to be performed.
*   **Imaging Type** (`imaging_type`): Select 'Yes' if imaging is required or 'No' if not.
*   **Reason for Imaging** (`reason_for_imaging`): Enter the reason for the imaging service (if applicable).
*   **Authorized Provider** (`authorized_provider`): Select 'Yes' if the imaging service has been authorized by a provider or 'No' if not.
*   **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`): Enter the date on which the order was placed.
*   **Time Ordered** (`time_ordered`): Enter the time at which the order was placed.

## Tips

*   Ensure you have all required information before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
*   Review the form regularly to ensure it's up-to-date and relevant to your needs.
