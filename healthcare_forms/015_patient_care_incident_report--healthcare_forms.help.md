# Patient Care Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient care incident that has occurred, allowing healthcare professionals to document and follow up on incidents in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible, providing all relevant details about the incident.
* Ensure that you have the patient's consent to report their information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id** (Text): Enter the patient's ID number or other unique identifier.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for patient tracking purposes.
* **Date of Incident** (Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for accurate reporting.
* **Time of Incident** (Time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for accurate reporting.
* **Location of Incident** (Text): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for context.
* **Incident Description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
	+ This field is required for incident tracking and follow-up.
* **Patient Name** (Text): Enter the patient's name.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for patient tracking purposes.
* **Reporting Clerk Name** (Text): Enter your name as the reporting clerk.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up tracking.
* **Date Reported** (Date): Enter the date the incident was reported.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for accurate follow-up.
* **Time Reported** (Time): Enter the time the incident was reported.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for accurate follow-up.
* **Incident Type** (Select One): Select the type of incident that occurred.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Incident Status** (Select One): Select the status of the incident.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Follow-up Status** (Select One): Select the follow-up status of the incident.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Follow-up Details** (Text): Provide any follow-up details regarding the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up tracking.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date): Enter the date the follow-up occurred.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up tracking.
* **Follow-up Time** (Time): Enter the time the follow-up occurred.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up tracking.
* **Incident Outcome** (Text): Describe the outcome of the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for incident review.
* **Patient Outcome** (Text): Describe the patient's outcome after the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for patient tracking.
* **Incident Cause** (Text): Describe the cause of the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for incident review.
* **Action Taken** (Text): Describe any actions taken regarding the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for incident review.
* **Follow-up Action Taken** (Text): Describe any follow-up actions taken.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up tracking.
* **Follow-up Comments** (Text): Add any follow-up comments regarding the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for follow-up review.
* **Incident Reported By** (Text): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for tracking.
* **Follow-up Reported By** (Text): Enter your name as the person who followed up on the incident.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for tracking.
