# Patient Health History - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record a patient's health history, allowing healthcare professionals to assess and provide appropriate care and services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Patient Information page): Enter the patient's first and last name, date of birth, and relevant contact information.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions page): Select all applicable medical conditions from the options provided.
* **Allergies** (Allergies page): Indicate if the patient has any allergies.
* **Medical Tests** (Medical Tests page): List any medical tests performed, including dates and results.
* **Medications** (Medications page): Select all current medications.
* **Medical History** (Medical History page): Describe any previous medical conditions, treatments, or hospitalizations.
* **Additional Medical History** (Additional Medical History page): Provide any additional medical history, if necessary.
* **Family History** (Family History page): Record any relevant family medical history, including conditions and ages affected.
* **Additional Family History** (Additional Family History page): Add any further family medical history details.
* **Personal History** (Personal History page): Enter any relevant personal details, such as lifestyle habits and social history.
* **Additional Personal History** (Additional Personal History page): Add any additional personal history information.
