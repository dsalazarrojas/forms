# Psychotherapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for psychotherapy intake purposes. It is meant to be used by patients to provide their personal and medical history, which will be used by healthcare professionals to better understand their mental health needs and provide effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Use the available options for select fields to make the right choice.
3. If unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First Name (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* Last Name (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* Email (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Phone
* Phone (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Address
* Address (`address`, text, optional): If you'd like to share your address with us, please fill it in.

### Date of Birth
* Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### Date of Last Visit
* Date of Last Visit (`date_of_last_visit`, date, optional): If you have any previous medical history, please enter the date of your last visit.

### Reason for Visit
* Reason for Visit (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Briefly explain why you are visiting us.

### Medical Notes
* Medical Notes (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or concerns with us.

### Medication
* Medication (`medication`, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please list them.

### Emergency Contact
* Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Relation
* Emergency Contact Relation (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Relationship with the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email
* Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Email of the emergency contact person.

### Insurance Provider
* Insurance Provider (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Name of your insurance provider.

### Group Number
* Group Number (`insurance_group_number`, text, optional): Your insurance group number.

### Plan
* Plan (`insurance_plan`, select_one, optional): Select your insurance plan from the options provided.

### Effective Date
* Effective Date (`insurance_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter your insurance effective date.

### End Date
* End Date (`insurance_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your insurance end date.

### Group Name
* Group Name (`insurance_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select your insurance group.

### Medical Condition
* Medical Condition (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current medical conditions.

### Medical Notes 2
* Medical Notes 2 (`medical_notes_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional medical notes or conditions.

### Allergies
* Allergies (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them.

### Medical Procedure
* Medical Procedure (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): If you have any medical procedures, please list them.

### Additional Comments
* Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them.
 

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check your email and phone numbers for accuracy.
* Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
* Complete all required fields to ensure that your information is complete and accurate.
