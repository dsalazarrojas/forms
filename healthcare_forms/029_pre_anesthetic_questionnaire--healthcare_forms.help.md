# Pre Anesthetic Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients about their medical history, allergies, and other relevant details before undergoing an anesthetic procedure. This information will help medical professionals make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. List any contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Specify any allergies in the "Allergies" section (select multiple as needed).
4. Describe any medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" section.
5. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" section.
6. List all medications the patient is taking in the "Medication" section.
7. Describe the upcoming medical procedure in the "Medical Procedure" section.
8. Select the anesthetic plan (e.g., Local, General, IV Sedation) in the "Anesthesia Plan" section.
