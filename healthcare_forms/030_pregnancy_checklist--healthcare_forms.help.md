# Pregnancy Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy Checklist form is a healthcare tool for capturing essential information about an expectant parent and their pregnancy status. This form helps healthcare providers gather accurate and consistent data to inform decisions regarding prenatal care and postpartum support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Expectant Parent Info" section with the expectant parent's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the current "Pregnancy Status" in the designated field, which may include details such as "pregnant", "miscarried", "terminated", or other pregnancy-related conditions.
3. For "Pregnancy Duration", enter the exact number of weeks or months since the last menstrual period.
4. Provide the expected "Due Date" in the date field, following the standard date format (MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expectant Parent Info** (text): Enter the expectant parent's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Pregnancy Status** (text): Describe the current pregnancy status, using terms such as "pregnant", "miscarried", "terminated", or other relevant conditions.
* **Pregnancy Duration** (number): Enter the exact number of weeks or months since the last menstrual period.
* **Due Date** (date): Enter the expected date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD).
