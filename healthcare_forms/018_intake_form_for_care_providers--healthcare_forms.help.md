# intake_form_for_care_providers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for healthcare providers. It consists of multiple sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the patient's medical history and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each section of the form, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Use the available options to select the most relevant answers for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (patient_info, text, optional): This field is for the patient's basic details, such as their name, contact information, and demographics.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history in this field.
* **Allergies** (allergies, select_multiple, optional): Check off any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medications** (medications, select_multiple, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, select_multiple, optional): Note any pre-existing medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Patient Agreement** (patient_agreement, select_one, required): Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Patient Signature** (patient_signature, note, optional): Provide a note or comment if needed.
* **Healthcare Provider Info** (healthcare_provider_info, text, optional): Enter your information as a healthcare provider.
* **Facility Info** (facility_info, text, optional): Enter facility information.
* **Facility Address** (facility_address, text, optional): Enter facility address.
* **Date Of Admission** (date_of_admission, date, optional): Provide the date of admission.
* **Time Of Admission** (time_of_admission, time, optional): Provide the time of admission.
* **Date Of Discharge** (date_of_discharge, date, optional): Provide the date of discharge.
* **Time Of Discharge** (time_of_discharge, time, optional): Provide the time of discharge.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, text, optional): Add any relevant medical notes.
* **Phone** (patient_phone, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (patient_email, text, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Insurance Provider** (insurance_provider, text, optional): Enter insurance provider information.
* **Insurance Number** (insurance_number, text, optional): Enter insurance number.
* **Facility Type** (facility_type, text, optional): Select facility type.
* **Facility Number** (facility_number, text, optional): Enter facility number.
* **Medical Department** (medical_department, text, optional): Enter medical department information.
* **Date Of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, optional): Provide the date of visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (time_of_visit, time, optional): Provide the time of visit.
* **Medical Notes 2** (medical_notes_2, text, optional): Add any additional medical notes.

Note: The required fields are marked with 'required: true'.
