# Medical Equipment Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the medical equipment used in healthcare settings, ensuring it is in good working condition and functioning safely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Select the correct equipment model from the options provided.
3. Choose the relevant equipment type from the options listed.
4. Provide the manufacturer's name and contact information if available.
5. Enter the serial number of the equipment if applicable.
6. Record the date of the last inspection or evaluation.
7. Note any safety precautions or concerns.
8. Specify the maintenance schedule for the equipment.
9. Enter the user's comments or notes.
10. Select the status of the equipment from the options provided.
11. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
12. Specify the location where the equipment is stored or used.
13. Note the height and width of the equipment if necessary.
14. Enter the results of the evaluation and any comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_model** (`equipment_model`, text, not required): Enter the model number or identifier of the medical equipment.
* **equipment_type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of equipment from the options listed.
* **manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, not required): Enter the name of the equipment manufacturer if available.
* **serial_number** (`serial_number`, text, not required): Enter the serial number of the equipment if applicable.
* **last_inspection_date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, not required): Record the date of the last inspection or evaluation.
* **safety_precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, not required): Note any safety concerns or precautions for the equipment.
* **maintenance_schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, text, not required): Specify the maintenance schedule for the equipment.
* **user_comments** (`user_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or notes from the user.
* **equipment_status** (`equipment_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select the status of the equipment from the options provided.
* **last_calibration_date** (`last_calibration_date`, date, not required): Record the date of the last calibration or evaluation.
* **calibration_interval** (`calibration_interval`, number, not required): Enter the interval for calibration or evaluation if applicable.
* **manufacturer_contact** (`manufacturer_contact`, text, not required): Enter the contact information for the manufacturer if available.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number for the manufacturer if applicable.
* **user_notes** (`user_notes`, note, not required): Enter any comments or notes from the user.
* **completed_status** (`completed_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of completion from the options provided.
* **completed_by** (`completed_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **evaluation_date** (`evaluation_date`, date, not required): Record the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_time** (`evaluation_time`, time, not required): Record the time of the evaluation.
* **equipment_location** (`equipment_location`, text, not required): Specify the location where the equipment is stored or used.
* **equipment_area** (`equipment_area`, text, not required): Enter the area where the equipment is used or stored.
* **equipment_height** (`equipment_height`, text, not required): Enter the height of the equipment if necessary.
* **equipment_width** (`equipment_width`, text, not required): Enter the width of the equipment if necessary.
* **evaluation_result** (`evaluation_result`, text, not required): Enter the results of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments or remarks from the evaluation.
* **evaluation_remarks** (`evaluation_remarks`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or remarks.
