# Roxy's Velvet Aesthetics Consent & Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients seeking cosmetic treatments that involve injectable products to provide medical history, allergies, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question.
2. Fill out the form honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Be sure to provide all necessary information in each field, especially if it is marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Yes/No** (1):
	+ This field asks whether you are willing to sign the consent for the treatments you are seeking.
* **Patient Info** (2):
	+ This field is for you to provide any relevant personal details or information about yourself.
* **Date of Birth** (3):
	+ Please provide your date of birth to help us understand your medical history and age.
* **Phone** (4):
	+ Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Email** (5):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your treatments and any follow-ups.
* **Medical History** (6):
	+ Describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
* **Medication Allergies** (7):
	+ List any medications you are allergic to or have had adverse reactions with in the past.
* **Surgical History** (8):
	+ Describe any previous surgeries you have undergone.
* **Medical Conditions** (9):
	+ List any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medical History 2** (10):
	+ This field is a second section for you to provide further medical history details if needed.

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to us for clarification.
- Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting.
- Be honest and accurate in your responses to provide the best possible care.
