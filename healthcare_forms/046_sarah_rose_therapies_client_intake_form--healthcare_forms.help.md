# sarah_rose_therapies_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for clients to provide essential information about themselves to the healthcare providers at Sarah Rose Therapies. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately to ensure the best possible care and attention.

## How to Complete This Form

- Carefully read the instructions and explanations for each section.
- Fill out the form in its entirety, or you can choose to leave fields that are marked as optional.
- Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Info
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required: false): This section is for your basic contact information and identification. Please provide your full name, email, and phone number to ensure we can reach you if needed.

### 2. Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, required: false): If you have any allergies, please list them here. This includes medications, foods, or environmental factors that may trigger a reaction.

### 3. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any significant medical conditions, injuries, or surgeries you've experienced.

### 4. Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any of the listed medical conditions.

### 5. Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required: false): Report any current or past medications you're taking.

### 6. Emergency Contacts
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): Provide information about your emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, and relationships.

### 7. Mental Health
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have any mental health concerns or conditions.

### 8. Medical History 2
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required: false): Add any additional medical history information that's not captured in the first field.

### 9. Medical History 3
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, required: false): Add any further details about your medical history.

### 10. Medications 2
* **Medications 2** (`medications_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Report any further medications you're taking.

Please submit this form carefully, and remember that it's always best to be accurate. This form is a crucial step in the healthcare process.
