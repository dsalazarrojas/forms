# counseling_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counseling informed consent form is used to collect contact information from clients or patients in a healthcare setting, including contact and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, email, address, city, state, and zip code.
* If you have an emergency contact, please provide their contact information, including name, phone number, email address, address, city, state, and zip code.
* Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact, if applicable.
* Provide any additional relationship notes if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required:false): This is the name of the client or patient.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required:false): This is the phone number of the client or patient.
* **Email** (Email, email, required:false): This is the email address of the client or patient.
* **Contact Address** (Contact Address, text, required:false): This is the address of the client or patient.
* **Contact City** (Contact City, text, required:false): This is the city of the client or patient's address.
* **State** (State, text, required:false): This is the state of the client or patient's address.
* **Contact Zip** (Contact Zip, text, required:false): This is the zip code of the client or patient's address.
* **Contact Date** (Contact Date, date, required:false): This is the date the client or patient is available for contact.
* **Contact Time** (Contact Time, time, required:false): This is the time the client or patient is available for contact.
* **Emergency Name** (Emergency Name, text, required:false): This is the name of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required:false): This is the phone number of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, email, required:false): This is the email address of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship** (Emergency Relationship, text, required:false): This is the relationship between the client and their emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Emergency Contact Address, text, required:false): This is the address of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City** (Emergency Contact City, text, required:false): This is the city of the client's emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact State** (Emergency Contact State, text, required:false): This is the state of the client's emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (Emergency Contact Zip, text, required:false): This is the zip code of the client's emergency contact's address.
* **Relationship Other** (Relationship, text, required:false): This is the relationship between the client and their emergency contact if other than family or friend.
* **Relationship Notes** (Relationship Notes, text, required:false): This is any additional notes about the client's emergency contact relationship.
