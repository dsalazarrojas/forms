# Skin Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Skin Surgery Consent Form" is a document that requires clients to provide information about themselves and their medical history in order to obtain their consent for the skin surgery.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, make sure you have all the necessary information and documents ready. Here's a step-by-step guide on how to complete the form:

1. **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the client's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): If the client has a medical record number, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
4. **Surgical Procedure** (`surgical_procedure`): Select the type of surgical procedure the client is undergoing.
5. **Consent Date** (`consent_date`): Enter the date you are obtaining the client's consent for the skin surgery.
6. **Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name as the person obtaining the client's consent.
7. **Consent Form** (`i_agree`): Check the box to indicate that you agree to the terms of the skin surgery consent form.
8. **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe the client's medical history, including any past illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
9. **Medications Taken** (`medications_taken`): List any medications the client is currently taking.
10. **Additional Comments** (implicit): If you have any additional comments or concerns about the client's medical history or the skin surgery, include them here.
