# Medication List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients or healthcare professionals to record and manage medication information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Name** (Field 1: "patient_name") in the text box provided. Please ensure the name is spelled correctly.
2. For patients with a medical record, enter your **Medical Record Number** (Field 2: "medical_record_number") if known. If not available, leave this field blank.
3. If you have **Dosage Instructions** (Field 3: "dosage_instructions"), enter them in the text box provided.
4. Enter your **Frequency** (Field 4: "frequency") of medication administration.
5. If applicable, enter the **Duration of Use** (Field 5: "duration_of_use") of the medication.
6. Enter the **Last Updated** (Field 6: "last_updated") date if the medication information has been updated recently.
7. Select the **Medication List** (Field 7: "medication_list") as 'Yes' if the medication is currently being taken, and 'No' otherwise.
8. Select the **Medication List Item** (Field 8: "medication_list_item") as 'Yes' if this is the patient's current medication list, and 'No' otherwise.
9. If applicable, enter the **Medication Name** (Field 9: "medication_name") of the medication.
10. If applicable, enter the **Start Date** (Field 10: "start_date") when the medication was started.
11. If applicable, enter the **End Date** (Field 11: "end_date") when the medication was discontinued.
12. If applicable, enter the **Dosage Form** (Field 12: "dosage_form") of the medication.
13. If applicable, enter the **Route of Administration** (Field 13: "route_of_administration") of the medication.
14. If applicable, enter the **Frequency of Administration** (Field 14: "frequency_of_administration") of the medication.
15. If applicable, enter any **Instructions** (Field 15: "instructions") regarding the medication.
16. If applicable, enter the **Medication Strength** (Field 16: "medication_strength") of the medication.
17. If applicable, enter the **Dosage** (Field 17: "dosage") of the medication.
18. If applicable, enter the **Medication Form** (Field 18: "medication_form") of the medication.
19. If applicable, enter the **Start Dose** (Field 19: "start_dose") of the medication.
20. If applicable, enter any **Note** (Field 20: "note") regarding the medication.
21. If applicable, enter the **Patient ID** (Field 21: "patient_id") of the patient.
22. If applicable, enter the **Medication ID** (Field 22: "medication_id") of the medication.
23. If applicable, enter the **Medical Staff ID** (Field 23: "medical_staff_id") of the healthcare provider.
24. If applicable, enter the **Facility ID** (Field 24: "facility_id") where the medication is administered.
25. If applicable, enter the **Updated By** (Field 25: "updated_by") healthcare provider who updated the record.

* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, **date**, optional): Enter the date the medication information was last updated.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select 'Yes' if the medication is currently being taken, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Medication List Item** (`medication_list_item`, **select_one**, optional): Select 'Yes' if this is the patient's current medication list, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, **text**, optional): If applicable, enter the medication's name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, **date**, optional): Enter the date the medication was started.
