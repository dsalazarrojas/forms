# Covid Questionnaire For Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Questionnaire for Patients is a form designed to collect information from patients about their exposure to COVID-19 and their interactions with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply select the appropriate options for each question and provide clear and concise answers to the text-based questions. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you been exposed to Covid 19?** 
    (select_one: True, False, unsure)
    This question helps healthcare professionals understand if you have been exposed to COVID-19, which is crucial for contact tracing and quarantine purposes.
* **Describe your symptoms** 
    (text)
    Please provide a clear description of your symptoms, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Contact a doctor** 
    (select_multiple: call, email, visit)
    Indicate how you plan to contact a healthcare professional, which will help healthcare providers understand your preferences and provide guidance on next steps.
* **Medical Record ID** 
    (number)
    If you have a medical record ID, please enter it here.
* **Medical Record Number** 
    (text)
    If you have a medical record number, please enter it here.
* **Doctor Name** 
    (text)
    If you have a preferred doctor or healthcare professional, please enter their name here.
* **Patient Name** 
    (text)
    Enter your name for our records.
* **Date of Birth** 
    (date)
    Enter your date of birth, which will help healthcare professionals understand your age group and medical history.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please answer based on your best understanding of the situation.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your healthcare provider directly.
