# Doctor's Notes Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doctor's Notes Release Form is used to capture a patient's consent for the release of their medical information. This form ensures that the patient's privacy and confidentiality are respected, as specified in the healthcare regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Sign the form below to indicate your consent for the release of your medical information.
2. Enter your name, which will be used to identify you.
3. Select the healthcare provider(s) you want to release your information to.
4. Choose the date you would like the information to be released.
5. Select the authorized personnel you want to release your information to (choose one or more options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Signature**: This is the signature box where you, as the patient, confirm your consent for the release of your medical information. Please sign your name to indicate your agreement.
* **Healthcare Provider**: This field is where you enter the name of the healthcare provider you want to release your medical information to. Please ensure to enter the name exactly as it appears on their ID or badge.
* **Date Of Release**: This field is where you select the date on which you would like your information to be released. Please use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Authorized Release**: This field allows you to select the authorized personnel who can release your medical information. Please choose one or more options to indicate who you trust with your confidential data.
