# Hospital Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Visitation Form is a document used to record and manage visits to the hospital, ensuring that patient care and visitor interactions are accurately tracked and authorized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info (id: 1)**: Enter the patient's name and any relevant details.
	* This field is optional.
2. **Authorized Visitors (id: 2)**: Select all authorized visitors to the patient's room.
	* Choose one or multiple options from the list.
3. **Visit Frequency (id: 3)**: Enter the date and time of the visit.
	* Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. **Visit Duration (id: 4)**: Enter the length of the visit in hours.
	* Enter a whole number between 0 and 24.
5. **Visit Comments (id: 5)**: Enter any comments or notes about the visit.
	* This field is a free-text area for additional information.
6. **Contact Info (id: 6)**: Enter any contact information for the visitor.
	* Enter a name, phone number, or email address.
7. **Medical History (id: 7)**: Enter any relevant medical history information.
	* Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
8. **Medical Condition (id: 8)**: Select the patient's current medical condition.
	* Choose from the list: healthy, sick, or critical.
9. **Visit Status (id: 9)**: Select the status of the visit.
	* Choose from the list: approved, denied, or pending.
10. **Additional Info (id: 10)**: Enter any additional information not covered by other fields.
	* Enter a free-text description.
11. **Notes (id: 11)**: Enter any additional notes about the visit.
	* This field is for recording observations or comments.
12. **Signature Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date of signature.
	* Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
13. **Signature (id: 13)**: Sign your name as the responsible healthcare professional.
	* Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
14. **Medical Certificate (id: 14)**: Select whether a medical certificate is issued for the visit.
	* Choose from the list: 'Yes' or 'No'.
15. **Medical License (id: 15)**: Select whether the visitor has a medical license.
	* Choose from the list: 'Yes' or 'No'.
16. **Visit Type (id: 16)**: Select the type of visit.
	* Choose from the list: 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Ensure that authorized visitors are correctly selected.
* Enter dates and numbers in the correct format.
* Sign and date your signature field.

This help guide provides a detailed explanation of each field to ensure you can complete the Hospital Visitation Form with ease.
