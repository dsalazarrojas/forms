# Healthcare Professional Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare professional application form is designed for healthcare professionals to apply for a specific position or opportunity within a healthcare organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Describe your relevant work experience in the experience field.
6. Attach any relevant medical credentials in the credentials field (this field is for upload or note-taking purposes).
7. Select your preferred category from the category field (Healthcare or Other).
8. Select your department of specialty from the department field (Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, General Practice, Neurology, Orthopedic Surgery, Pathology, Physical Medicine, Radiology).
9. Enter your specialty in the specialty field.
10. Finally, click the submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required: False)**: Please enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (Text, Required: False)**: Please enter your last name here.
* **Email (Email, Required: False)**: Please enter a valid email address here.
* **Phone (Text, Required: False)**: Please enter your phone number here.
* **Experience (Text, Required: False)**: Describe your relevant work experience here.
* **Credentials (Note, Required: False)**: Attach any relevant medical credentials (e.g. certification, license, etc.) or take notes about your experience.
* **Category (Select One, Required: False)**: Select either Healthcare or Other as your category.
* **Department (Select Multiple, Required: False)**: Select one or more departments you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Specialty (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your area of specialty here.
* **Submit (Text, Required: False)**: Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are only filling out this form if you are indeed a healthcare professional with the necessary qualifications and experience.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the relevant department or organization.
