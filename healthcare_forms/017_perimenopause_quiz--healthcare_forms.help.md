# Perimenopause Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Perimenopause Quiz form is designed to collect information on menopause symptoms, experiences, and other related details from users. This form is intended for a specific medical context, possibly for women who are approaching or are in the perimenopause stage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your age in the "What is your age?" field.
*   For the "Menses" field, select one or more of the options that best describe your menstruation cycle experiences.
*   In the "Menopause Symptoms" field, select all that apply to describe your symptoms.
*   In the "Menopause Benefits" field, select all that apply to describe any benefits you've experienced.
*   Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
*   If applicable, provide your menopause date in the "Menopause Date" field.
*   Enter your menopause age in the "Menopause Age" field.
*   Indicate if you are using hormone replacement therapy in the "Hormone Replacement Therapy" field.
*   Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Perimenopause Quiz Form Additional Comments 2" field.
*   Provide any family history in the "Family History" field.
*   Describe the duration of your symptoms in the "Duration of Symptoms" field.
*   Describe the frequency of your symptoms in the "Frequency of Symptoms" field.
*   Describe the severity of your symptoms in the "Severity of Symptoms" field.
*   Describe the timing of your symptoms in the "Timing of Symptoms" field.
*   Describe the onset of your symptoms in the "Onset of Symptoms" field.
*   Enter any other symptoms you're experiencing in the "Perimenopause Quiz Form Menopause Symptoms Duration 2" field.
*   Enter any other information you have in the "Perimenopause Quiz Form Menopause Symptoms Frequency 2" field.

*   Describe the timing of your symptoms in the "Perimenopause Quiz Form Menopause Symptoms Timing 2" field.
