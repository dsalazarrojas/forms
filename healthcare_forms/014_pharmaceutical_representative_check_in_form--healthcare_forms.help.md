# Pharmaceutical Representative Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Representative Check In Form is used to record the details of your visit to a healthcare setting. This includes the date and time of your visit, your contact information, and any additional notes or comments you may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, click on the "Check In Date" field and select the date of your visit.
2. In the "Check In Time" field, select the time you arrived at the healthcare setting.
3. Enter your "Check In Location" where you are visiting.
4. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional information you may have about your visit.
5. Enter your "Contact Number" and "Contact Email" as a way for us to reach out to you with any important information.
6. Enter your "Contact Name" and "Company Name" for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your visit to a healthcare setting.
* **Check In Time** (`checkin_time`, time, optional): Select the time you arrived at the healthcare setting.
* **Check In Location** (`checkin_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are visiting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information you may have about your visit.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number as a way for us to reach out to you with any important information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address as a way for us to reach out to you with any important information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and title or affiliation.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
