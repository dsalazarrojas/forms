# Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fat Cavitation Client Intake Form is designed to collect patient information and medical history for preparation and care planning. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that you receive the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your contact number where you can be reached for updates and follow-up.
3. Enter your email address for communication and appointment reminders.
4. Enter your date of birth for accurate record-keeping.
5. Provide a brief description of your medical history for your healthcare provider.
6. Describe your procedure to ensure accurate preparation and planning.
7. Enter your next appointment schedule for follow-up and reminders.
8. List any medical conditions that require attention.
9. List any medications you are currently taking.
10. List any allergies or sensitivities.
11. Provide your emergency contact information for critical situations.
12. Enter your next visit schedule for follow-up care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number where you can be reached for updates and follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and appointment reminders.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for accurate record-keeping.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history for your healthcare provider.
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, text, optional): Describe your procedure to ensure accurate preparation and planning.
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, optional): Enter your next appointment schedule for follow-up and reminders.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions that require attention.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): List any medical procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, text, optional): Enter your next visit schedule for follow-up care.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide your emergency contact information for critical situations.
