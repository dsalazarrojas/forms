# Pediatric Medical Daycare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and document the compliance of pediatric medical facilities with established medical standards. It helps to identify areas of improvement and ensure the safety and well-being of children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter the daycare's address in the `daycare_address` field.
*   Enter the daycare's name in the `daycare_name` field.
*   Enter the daycare's phone number in the `daycare_phone` field.
*   Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection in the `daycare_inspector` field.
*   Select the date of the inspection in the `date_inspection` field.
*   Select the time of the inspection in the `time_inspection` field (optional).
*   Choose the correct medical certification from the drop-down menu in the `medical_cert` field.
*   Choose the relevant medical staff certification(s) from the drop-down menu in the `staff_medical_cert` field (optional).
*   Select the relevant medical staff from the drop-down menu in the `medical_staff` field.
*   Enter the name of the medical facility in the `medical_facility` field (optional).
*   Enter the rating of the facility in the `facility_rating` field.
*   Enter any additional comments about the facility in the `facility_comments` field (optional).
*   Select the current license status of the facility from the drop-down menu in the `facility_license` field (optional).
*   Enter the expiration date of the facility's license in the `license_exp_date` field (optional).
*   Select the outcome of the inspection in the `inspection_result` field.
*   Enter any additional comments about the inspection in the `comments` field (optional).
*   Enter any additional recommendations for improvement in the `recommendations` field (optional).
*   Enter your signature in the `signature` field.
*   Select the correct type of daycare license from the drop-down menu in the `daycare_license` field.
*   Enter your name as the inspector in the `inspector_name` field.
*   Enter your phone number in the `inspector_phone` field (optional).
*   Enter your email address in the `inspector_email` field.
*   Select the date the form was submitted in the `date_submitted` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **daycare_address**: Enter the daycare's address where the inspection took place.
*   **daycare_name**: Enter the name of the daycare facility being inspected.
*   **daycare_phone**: Enter the phone number of the daycare facility.
*   **daycare_inspector**: Enter your name as the inspector performing the inspection.
*   **date_inspection**: Select the date of the inspection using the calendar tool.
*   **time_inspection**: Select the time of the inspection (optional).
*   **medical_cert**: Choose the correct medical certification for the facility.
*   **staff_medical_cert**: Choose the relevant medical staff certification(s) (optional).
*   **medical_staff**: Select the relevant medical staff for the facility.
*   **medical_facility**: Enter the name of the medical facility (optional).
*   **facility_rating**: Rate the facility based on the inspection (1-5).
*   **facility_comments**: Enter any additional comments about the facility (optional).
*   **facility_license**: Select the current license status of the facility.
*   **license_exp_date**: Enter the expiration date of the facility's license (optional).
*   **inspection_result**: Select the outcome of the inspection.
*   **comments**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection (optional).
*   **recommendations**: Enter any additional recommendations for improvement (optional).
*   **signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector.
*   **daycare_license**: Select the correct type of daycare license.
*   **inspector_signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector.
*   **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
*   **inspector_phone**: Enter your phone number as the inspector (optional).
*   **inspector_email**: Enter your email address as the inspector.
*   **date_submitted**: Select the date the form was submitted.
