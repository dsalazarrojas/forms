# patient_history_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient history questionnaire is used to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient identification section (page 1) with the patient's basic information.
2. In the medical history section, provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
3. Answer the medication and allergy questions (pages 2-3) to help the healthcare provider understand the patient's medication usage and allergies.
4. In the chief complaint section (page 4), describe the main reason for the patient's visit or the reason for their current health concern.
5. Complete the additional medical history details section (pages 5-25) with any other relevant medical information not covered in the previous sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History (Page 2)**: Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
* **Medical History 2 (Page 3)**: This field is used for additional medical history information not covered in the previous page.
* **Medication (Page 4)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient is currently taking any medications.
* **Medication 2 (Page 4)**: If the patient is taking medications, enter a brief description of the medication(s).
* **Allergies (Page 5)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has any allergies.

Tips:

* Be thorough and complete in your responses to ensure accurate medical history documentation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you're unsure about any information, provide as much detail as possible and mark the "Unknown" or "Not applicable" option.
* Review and update your responses as necessary based on your healthcare provider's feedback.
