# Electronic Communication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain electronic communication consent from patients for healthcare providers to communicate with them via electronic means (e.g., email, phone, or text message). 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address and/or phone number in the provided fields to ensure that the healthcare provider can communicate with you electronically.
2. Review the form to make sure you understand what electronic communication means and what types of messages you will be receiving from the healthcare provider.
3. Submit the form once you have provided the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email Address (1)**: Enter your primary email address that you would like to use for electronic communication with your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Email** (2): Enter the email address of your healthcare provider or organization that you would like to use for electronic communication.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your phone number that you would like to use for electronic communication with your healthcare provider (optional).

## Tips
* Please make sure to update your contact information if any of your email addresses or phone numbers change.
* If you have any concerns or questions, you can reach out to your healthcare provider directly.
