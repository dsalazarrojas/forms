# Neoplasia Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neoplasia Assessment Form is a comprehensive document used to collect information about patients with neoplasia. This form helps healthcare professionals track patient outcomes, treatments, and other relevant medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, or as much as required.
2. Enter the patient's information, medical history, tumor details, treatment specifics, and other relevant data.
3. Select options for questions marked as select_multiple or select_one type fields.
4. Enter dates for the Follow Up field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Medical History (page 2)**: Answer the questions about the patient's medical history, including whether or not they have a history of cancer.
* **Tumor Info (page 3)**: Describe the patient's tumor, including its characteristics and any related symptoms.
* **Treatment Info (page 4)**: Enter details about the patient's treatment, including medications or therapies used.
* **Medication Info (page 5)**: Answer the questions about the patient's medication use, including whether or not they take any medications.
* **Medical Team (page 6)**: Select the medical professionals involved in the patient's care.
* **Patient Outcome (page 7)**: Indicate the patient's current outcome, whether they have improved, deteriorated, or are stable.
* **Follow Up (page 8)**: Enter the date for the patient's next scheduled follow-up appointment.
* **Neoplasm Info (page 9)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's neoplasm.
