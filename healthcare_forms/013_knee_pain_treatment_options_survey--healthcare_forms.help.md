# Knee Pain Treatment Options Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their knee pain treatment options, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Select all relevant answer options for each field.
* Enter any additional information or comments in the "Additional comments" field.
* If you have any follow-up appointments scheduled, enter the date and time in the "Follow-up date" and "Follow-up time" fields.
* If you have any additional comments or information to share, use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Treatment options**: Select all the treatments you are considering or have considered for your knee pain. Options include:
	+ Physical therapy
	+ Medications
	+ Surgeries
* **Pain level**: Please rate your current pain level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Current treatment**: Are you currently receiving any treatment for your knee pain? (Yes/No)
* **Patient experience**: Describe your experience with your current treatment. (Text field)
* **Preferred treatment**: Which treatment option do you prefer? (Select one)
	+ Physical therapy
	+ Medications
	+ Surgery
* **Treatment outcome**: How effective do you think your current treatment has been? (Select one)
	+ Effective
	+ Ineffective
	+ Unknown
* **Follow-up**: If you have any follow-up appointments scheduled, enter the date and time in the "Follow-up date" and "Follow-up time" fields.
* **Follow-up doctor**: Who will be following up with you after surgery? (Select one)
	+ Primary care physician
	+ Specialist
* **Additional comments**: Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience or treatment? (Text field)
* **Medication side effects**: If you are taking medications for your knee pain, please list any side effects you are experiencing. (Text field)
* **Surgery risks**: If you are considering surgery, please list any risks you are aware of. (Text field)
* **Surgery success rate**: If you have had surgery, please enter a success rate of 1-10.
* **Patient satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with your current treatment? (Select one)
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Follow-up nurse**: Who will be following up with you after surgery? (Select one)
	+ Nurse Practitioner
	+ Physician Assistant
* **Follow-up location**: Where will your follow-up appointment take place after surgery? (Select one)
	+ Clinic
	+ Hospital
* **Treatment duration**: How many treatment sessions have you had for your knee pain?
* **Medication dosage**: What is the dosage of your medication?
* **Medication frequency**: How often do you take your medication? (Select one)
	+ Once a day
	+ Twice a day
* **Surgery type**: What type of surgery are you considering or have undergone? (Select one)
	+ Arthroscopic
	+ Open surgical
* **Follow-up duration**: How long will your follow-up appointment last?
* **Follow-up timeframe**: When will your follow-up appointment take place?
* **Follow-up provider**: Who will be providing your follow-up care after surgery? (Select one)
	+ Physician
	+ Nurse Practitioner
* **Additional comments**: Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience or treatment? (Text field)

Note: This form is intended to gather feedback from patients and is not a diagnostic tool. Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
