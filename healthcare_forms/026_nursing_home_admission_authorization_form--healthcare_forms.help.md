# Nursing Home Admission Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and medical information from individuals who require admission to a nursing home. It's essential to fill it out accurately and truthfully to ensure the patient receives the necessary care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form in the following order:

1. Personal Info: Provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
2. Medical History: Describe your medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
3. Insurance Info: If applicable, provide your insurance information, including policy numbers and dates.
4. Emergency Contact: Identify an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5. Next of Kin: List the name and contact information of your next of kin or family member.
6. Medical Facility Info: Provide information about your current medical facility or healthcare provider.
7. Authorization: Sign and date the form to authorize the admission process.
8. Review and Sign: Review the form carefully and sign it to confirm you've filled it out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info**: 
    *   **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. 
    *   **Address**: Provide your current address, including your city, state, and zip code. 
    *   **Contact Info**: Enter your phone number and email address where you can be reached.
*   **Medical History**: 
    *   Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical treatments you have received. This section is essential for the nursing home staff to understand your health needs.
*   **Insurance Info**: 
    *   If you have health insurance, please provide your policy number, provider name, and dates of coverage. This will help us understand your insurance coverage and billing information.
*   **Emergency Contact**: 
    *   Identify a person who can be reached in case of an emergency, providing their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
*   **Next of Kin**: 
    *   List the name and contact information of your next of kin or family member who can be notified in case of an emergency or during your stay at the nursing home.
*   **Medical Facility Info**: 
    *   Provide information about your current medical facility or healthcare provider, including their name and contact information.
*   **Authorization**: 
    *   Sign and date the form to confirm you've read and understood the information provided. This is a required field for the admission process.
*   **Review and Sign**: 
    *   Carefully review the form for accuracy and completeness. If you need help or clarification, ask the nursing home staff. Once you're confident, sign and date the form to authorize the admission process.
