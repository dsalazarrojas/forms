# Mucus Less Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mucus Less Tracking Form is designed to collect and track patient information for healthcare providers. It includes fields for mucus collection date, collection method, amount collected, symptoms, medication taken, and other relevant details. This form helps healthcare providers keep accurate records and track patient progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of mucus collection from the calendar input field.
2. Choose the method of collection from the dropdown menu (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
3. Enter the amount of mucus collected.
4. Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient.
5. Select any medications taken by the patient.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the collection and patient's condition.
7. Enter the name, phone number, and email of the healthcare provider.
8. Optionally, specify the location where the form was completed.
9. Choose a tracking status from the dropdown menu (Active, Disabled, Closed).
10. Enter any additional comments about the tracking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mucus Collection Date**: Enter the date of the mucus collection.
* **Collection Method**: Choose how the mucus was collected (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Amount Collected**: Enter the amount of mucus collected.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient.
* **Medications Takes**: Select any medications taken by the patient (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the collection and patient's condition.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Email**: Enter the email of the healthcare provider.
* **Tracking Location**: Optionally, specify the location where the form was completed.
* **Tracking Method**: Enter any information about the tracking method used.
* **Tracking Comments**: Add any additional comments about the tracking process.
* **User ID**: Enter the ID of the user who completed the form.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the ID of the patient.
* **Tracking Status**: Choose the status of the tracking (Active, Disabled, Closed).

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and honestly to provide valuable information to the healthcare providers.
