## Postpartum Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postpartum Discharge Form is a crucial document used to record the patient's postpartum information, enabling healthcare providers to accurately monitor progress and adjust care as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for the mother and baby, including last name, first name, date of birth, and contact phone number.
2. Enter the discharge date, which is the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital.
3. Record the baby's first and last name, date of birth, and weight.
4. Schedule the next follow-up appointment by indicating the date and time for the follow-up visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother Last Name** (`mother_last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the last name of the mother.
* **Mother First Name** (`mother_first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the first name of the mother.
* **Mother Date of Birth** (`mother_date_of_birth`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the mother's date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Mother Phone Number** (`mother_phone_number`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the mother's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Baby First Name** (`baby_first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the first name of the baby.
* **Baby Last Name** (`baby_last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the last name of the baby.
* **Baby Date of Birth** (`baby_date_of_birth`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the baby's date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Baby Weight** (`baby_weight`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the baby's weight in kilograms.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date for the next follow-up appointment in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Next Follow-up Time** (`next_follow_up_time`, `time`, **Optional**): Enter the time for the next follow-up appointment.
