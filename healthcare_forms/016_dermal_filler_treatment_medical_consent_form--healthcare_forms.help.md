# Dermal Filler Treatment Medical Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential medical information and consents from patients undergoing dermal filler treatment. It collects general information, patient details, medical history, allergies, and consents for treatment. Completing this form helps ensure that the treatment is conducted safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information. Ensure that you provide the correct date and time of birth, medical conditions, consents, and contact information. Be sure to sign the medical consent and patient signature fields after reviewing and agreeing to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture any additional information that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any existing medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, optional): Provide further information on any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities that you have.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, required): Describe the treatment you are undergoing.
* **Medical Treatment 2** (`medical_treatment_2`, text, optional): Provide further information on the treatment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any existing medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, text, optional): Provide further information on any medical conditions.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, required): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Medical Consents** (`medical_consents`, select_multiple, required): Choose the consents that apply to your treatment.
* **Medical Consents 2** (`medical_consents_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose the consents that apply to your treatment.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Medical Consent Date** (`medical_consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your medical consent.
* **Medical Consent Time** (`medical_consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your medical consent.
* **Medical Condition Info** (`medical_condition_info`, text, optional): Provide further information on any medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition 2 Info** (`medical_condition_2_info`, text, optional): Provide further information on any medical conditions.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, note, required): Sign below to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required): Sign below to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your treatment.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, optional): Provide further information on your medical history.
