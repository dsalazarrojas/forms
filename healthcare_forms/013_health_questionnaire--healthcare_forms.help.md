# Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Questionnaire is a form designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health, medical history, and test results. This information will be used by healthcare professionals to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information about the patient.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Enter your answers in the corresponding fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (patient_info, select_one, Required): Select 'Yes' if the patient has provided their information and consent for data collection. Select 'No' if the patient has not provided their information or has withdrawn consent.
* **Health Issues** (health_issues, select_multiple, Required): Check all applicable health issues from the options provided. This field is crucial for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, date, Required): Enter the date of the patient's last medical check-up or appointment.
* **Symptoms** (symptoms, select_multiple, Required): Check all the symptoms the patient is experiencing. This information will help healthcare professionals identify potential underlying causes.
* **Medications** (medications, select_multiple, Required): Check all the medications the patient is currently taking. This information will help healthcare professionals identify potential interactions or allergies.
* **Test Results** (test_results, text, Required): Enter any relevant test results or lab reports from recent medical tests.
* **Test Comments** (test_comments, text, Required): Provide any additional comments or results from tests that are not reflected in the 'Test Results' field.
* **Medical History Comments** (medical_history_comments, text, Required): Provide any additional information about the patient's medical history that is not reflected in the 'Medical History' field.
