# patient_psychological_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_psychological_health_questionnaire is designed to gather comprehensive data on a patient's mental health status, medical history, and social support systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing patient demographics on page 1.
2. Select multiple options for the patient's current mental health status on page 2.
3. Choose the type of medication (if any) being taken by the patient on page 3.
4. Provide information about the patient's health insurance on page 4.
5. Enter the patient's medical history on page 5.
6. Choose the types of mental health treatments the patient has undergone or plans to undergo on page 6.
7. Select the type of social support the patient receives on page 7.
8. Identify the patient's mental health care giver on page 8.
9. Provide information on any patient counseling the patient has received on page 9.
10. Select multiple mental health services the patient has used on page 10.
11. Enter the patient's emergency contact information on page 11.
12. Choose the types of family history relevant to the patient's mental health on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (page 1, `name: patient_demographics`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **What is the patient's current mental health status?** (page 2, `name: mental_health_status`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the mental health issues that currently affect the patient, such as Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar Disorder, or Substance Abuse.
* **Medication Use** (page 3, `name: medication_use`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of medication (if any) the patient is currently taking for mental health.
* **Health Insurance** (page 4, `name: health_insurance`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the patient's health insurance information, including the provider and policy number.
* **Medical History** (page 5, `name: medical_history`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide information about the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or procedures undergone.
* **Mental Health Treatment** (page 6, `name: mental_health_treatment`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the types of mental health treatments the patient has received or plans to receive, such as Inpatient treatment, Outpatient treatment, Individual therapy, or Group therapy.
* **Social Support** (page 7, `name: social_support`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of social support the patient receives, such as Family and friends, Support group, Professional therapist, or None.
* **Mental Health Caregiver** (page 8, `name: mental_health_caregiver`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Identify the patient's mental health care giver, such as Primary care physician, Mental health provider, Social worker, or Other.
* **Patient Counseling** (page 9, `name: patient_counseling`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide information about any patient counseling the patient has received.
* **Mental Health Services** (page 10, `name: mental_health_services`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the types of mental health services the patient has used, such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Group therapy, or Individual therapy.
* **Patient Emergency Contact** (page 11, `name: patient_emergency_contact`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the patient's emergency contact information, including name and contact details.
* **Patient Family History** (page 12, `name: patient_family_history`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the types of family history that may be relevant to the patient's mental health, such as Family history of mental health issues, Family history of addiction, Family history of physical health issues, or Family history of medical conditions.
