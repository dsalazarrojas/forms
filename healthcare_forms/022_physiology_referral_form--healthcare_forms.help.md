# Physiology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiology Referral Form is a tool for healthcare providers to refer a patient to a test or procedure. It collects essential information for communication between healthcare providers and patients, and for tracking the referral status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
3. Select the type of test requested from the "Test Type" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the frequency of the test from the "Test Frequency" dropdown menu.
5. Select the location where the test will be conducted from the "Test Location" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date of the referral in the "Referral Date" field.
7. If required, enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
8. If required, enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
9. Select the type of phone number from the "Patient Phone Type" dropdown menu.
10. Select the status of the test from the "Test Status" dropdown menu.
11. Enter any relevant comments or notes in the "Patient Notes" and "Physician Notes" fields.
12. Enter the test results in the "Test Results" field, if available.
13. Enter the clinical diagnosis in the "Clinical Diagnosis" field, if applicable.
14. Enter any additional comments or explanations in the "Test Request Comments" and "Laboratory Result Comments" fields.
15. Sign the form with the "Physician Signature" field.

  * A text field to enter any comments or requests related to the test.
* **Test Type**:
  * A dropdown menu to select the type of test or procedure.
* **Patient Gender**:
  * A dropdown menu to select the patient's gender.
* **Test Frequency**:

* **Test Location**:
  * A dropdown menu to select where the test will be conducted.

  * A date field to enter the date of the referral.

  * A text field to enter the patient's phone number.

  * A text field to enter the patient's address.
