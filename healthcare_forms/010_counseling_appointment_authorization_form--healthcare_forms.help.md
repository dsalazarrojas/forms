# counseling_appointment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counseling appointment authorization form is used to collect information and authorization for counseling sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Information** section with the relevant details about the session.
2. Enter the **Client Information** in the designated field.
3. Choose whether or not the client has given **Authorization** for the session.
4. Select the client's **Consent** to participate in the session.
5. Indicate the client's **Contact Preferences** (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
6. Provide information on the client's **Emergency Contact**, if applicable.
7. Select the client's **Contact Preferences 1**, if necessary.
8. Enter any additional information in the **Session Details** field, which is a date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Information (Text)**: Enter the details about the session you are scheduling.
* **Client Information (Text)**: Enter any relevant information about the client.
* **Authorization (Number)**: Choose "1" if the client has given authorization for the session, and "0" otherwise.
* **Consent (Select One)**: Choose "Yes" if the client has given consent to participate in the session.
* **Contact Preferences (Select Multiple)**: Select all applicable contact preferences for the client (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Emergency Contact (Text)**: Enter any emergency contact information for the client.
* **Contact Preferences 1 (Select Multiple)**: Select all applicable contact preferences for the client, similar to **Contact Preferences**.
* **Session Details (Date)**: Enter any additional details about the session, preferably in the format of a date.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the client's representative for clarification.
