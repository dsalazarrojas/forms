# patient Own Frame Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient own frame waiver form is used to document a patient's ownership and consent for a specific frame, typically for medical purposes. This form ensures that the patient's personal information and frame details are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's own frame waiver in the "Frame Owner Consent" field, indicating whether the patient agrees to share their information.
2. Fill in the "Patient Own Frame" field with a brief description of the frame.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
4. Record the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Provide the contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Describe the details of the frame in the "Frame Details" field.
8. Sign and date the form by leaving a note in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Frame Owner Consent**: A checkbox indicating whether the patient consents to share their frame information.
* **Patient Own Frame**: A text field where the patient can describe their own frame.
* **Patient Name**: A field where the patient's name is entered.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: A date field where the patient's date of birth is recorded.
* **Contact Email**: A field where the patient's email address is entered.
* **Contact Phone**: A text field where the patient's phone number is entered.
* **Frame Details**: A field where the patient can describe the details of their frame.
* **Signature**: A field where the patient can leave their signature.
* **Signature**: The patient leaves their signature, confirming their agreement to share their information.
