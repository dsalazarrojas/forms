# Calculate Body Mass Index And Body Fat Percentage - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users calculate their body mass index (BMI) and body fat percentage based on their personal information. It includes fields for personal details such as age, gender, measurements, and health goals, allowing users to provide accurate input for the calculation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your name**: Type in your name in the provided text field.
2. **Enter your age**: Type your age in the numeric field.
3. **Select your gender**: Choose your gender from the dropdown list.
4. **Choose a measurement unit**: Select the unit you'd like to use for your height and weight measurements, either Metric (kg, cm) or Imperial (lbs, inches).
5. **Enter your height**: Type your height in the corresponding unit selected above (e.g., kg or lbs for Metric/Imperial).
6. **Enter your weight**: Type your weight in the corresponding unit selected above (e.g., kg or lbs for Metric/Imperial).
7. **Measure your waist circumference**: Measure the circumference of your belly button level and enter the value in the corresponding unit selected above.
8. **Measure your neck circumference**: Measure the circumference just below your larynx and enter the value in the corresponding unit selected above.
9. **Measure your hip circumference (for females)**: Measure the circumference at the widest point of your hips and enter the value in the corresponding unit selected above (only for females).
10. **Optional: Enter triceps skinfold**: Measure the skinfold at the back of your arm, just above the elbow, and enter the value in millimeters.
11. **Optional: Enter biceps skinfold**: Measure the skinfold on the front of your arm, above the elbow, and enter the value in millimeters.
12. **Optional: Enter subscapular skinfold**: Measure the skinfold on the back of your body, just below your armpit, and enter the value in millimeters.
13. **Optional: Enter suprailiac skinfold**: Measure the skinfold on the back of your body, at the base of your spine, and enter the value in millimeters.
14. **Select your activity level**: Choose how active you are from the dropdown list (Sedentary, Lightly active, Moderately active, Very active, Extremely active).
15. **Select your health goal**: Choose your health goal from the dropdown list (Weight loss, Weight gain, Maintain weight, Build muscle, General fitness).
16. **Optional: Enter any relevant medical conditions**: If you have any health conditions such as diabetes or heart disease, enter them in the text field.
17. **Optional: Enter any current medications**: If you are taking any medications, enter them in the text field.
