# Mpox Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mpox Checklist is a form designed to help individuals and organizations prepare for, prevent, and respond to Mpox, a viral disease. This form guides you through the critical steps and considerations necessary to ensure a safe and effective response to Mpox.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the different sections and fields.
2. For each section, fill in the relevant information as prompted.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure the form can be submitted successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prepare for Mpox** (`preparing_for_mpox`, text, required: false): This field is a general text input where you can provide any relevant information about your preparation for Mpox, such as your current protocols, procedures, or policies in place.
* **Prevent Mpox** (`prevent_mpox`, text, required: false): This field is designed to capture any measures you take to prevent Mpox, like vaccination strategies, sanitation protocols, or education initiatives.
* **Respond to Mpox** (`responding_to_mpox`, text, required: false): This field is for reporting any responses you've taken or are planning to take in case of a Mpox outbreak, including emergency contact information, evacuation procedures, or medical protocols.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be flexible and adaptive, so please don't hesitate to add any additional information that you feel is relevant to the Mpox response.
* Remember to review and submit your completed form carefully to ensure it accurately reflects your response to Mpox.
