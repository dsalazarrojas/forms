# Personal Training Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Client Intake Form is designed to gather information from clients who are seeking personal training services. It helps personal trainers and fitness coaches understand the client's goals, availability, and medical information to create a workout plan tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information**: Provide your name and contact details so that we can get in touch with you.
2. **Client Information**: Please provide your basic information such as age, height, and weight.
3. **Goals**: Select one or more of the goals you want to achieve through personal training: Weight Loss, Endurance, or Strength.
4. **Availability**: Enter the days and times you are available for training.
5. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, phone number, and email of an emergency contact person.
6. **Health Information**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
7. **Emergency Medical**: If you have any special medical needs or requirements, please include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information**: Your contact details, including name, phone number, and email.
- **Client Information**: Your basic information, such as age, height, and weight.
- **Goals**: Select one or more of the goals you want to achieve through personal training: Weight Loss, Endurance, or Strength.
- **Availability**: Enter the days and times you are available for training.
- **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, phone number, and email of an emergency contact person.
  - **Name**: The name of the emergency contact person.
  - **Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact person.
  - **Email**: The email of the emergency contact person.
- **Health Information**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
- **Emergency Medical**: If you have any special medical needs or requirements, please include them here.
  - **Note**: This is a text area for you to provide any special medical information.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when providing your information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
- Our personal trainers will use this information to create a workout plan tailored to your needs and goals.
