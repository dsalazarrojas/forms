# Nasal Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients or their authorized representatives for the administration of the Nasal Flu Vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): The date the patient or their representative signs this form.
2.  **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
3.  **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birthday`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
4.  **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact number.
5.  **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
6.  **Allergy** (`allergy`, select_multiple, required): Select any allergies the patient has from the options provided.
7.  **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If the patient has any medical conditions, enter a brief description.
8.  **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's insurance ID from the options provided.
9.  **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's medical group from the options provided.
10. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
11. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): A space for the patient or their representative to sign and date their name.
12. **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider who will be administering the vaccine.
13.  **Vaccine Administered By** (`vaccine_administered_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will administer the vaccine.
14.  **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the vaccine will be administered.
15.  **Vaccine Doses** (`vaccine_doses`, text, optional): Enter any additional vaccine doses the patient will receive.
