# CAHPS Health Plan Survey - Adult Medicaid 5.0 - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CAHPS Health Plan Survey - Adult Medicaid 5.0 is a questionnaire designed to gather feedback on the quality of health plans from adult Medicaid beneficiaries. It is used to evaluate and improve the performance of health plans in providing care and services to their members. This survey is essential for health plans to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance member satisfaction and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your personal experience with your health plan.
2. Select the best answer for each question based on your understanding of your health plan's performance.
3. Provide accurate and honest feedback to help improve the health plan's services and care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of your primary health plan** (`primary_medicaid_plan`): This field asks for the name of your primary health plan. Please enter the name of the health plan you are currently enrolled in.
* **How often was it easy to get the care, tests, or treatment you needed?** (`easy_to_get_care`): This question evaluates the ease of access to care and services provided by your health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Never: You never experienced easy access to care and services.
	+ Sometimes: You sometimes experienced easy access to care and services.
	+ Usually: You usually experienced easy access to care and services.
	+ Always: You always experienced easy access to care and services.
* **How often were the health plan's customer service staff helpful?** (`customer_service_clarity`): This question measures the helpfulness of your health plan's customer service staff. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Never: You never experienced helpful customer service staff.
	+ Sometimes: You sometimes experienced helpful customer service staff.
	+ Usually: You usually experienced helpful customer service staff.
	+ Always: You always experienced helpful customer service staff.
* **How often was it easy to find a doctor in your health plan?** (`easy_to_find_doctor`): This question assesses the ease of finding a doctor within your health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Never: You never experienced easy access to a doctor within your plan.
	+ Sometimes: You sometimes experienced easy access to a doctor within your plan.
	+ Usually: You usually experienced easy access to a doctor within your plan.
	+ Always: You always experienced easy access to a doctor within your plan.
* **How clear is the information about your out-of-pocket costs?** (`cost_transparency_rating`): This question evaluates the clarity of cost information provided by your health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Very Unclear: The cost information was very difficult to understand.
	+ Unclear: The cost information was difficult to understand.
	+ Clear: The cost information was easy to understand.
	+ Very Clear: The cost information was very easy to understand.
* **Did your health plan remind you to get preventative care (like flu shots)?** (`preventure_care_reminder`): This question checks if your health plan provided reminders for preventative care. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ True: You received reminders for preventative care.
	+ False: You did not receive reminders for preventative care.
	+ Unsure: You are unsure if you received reminders for preventative care.
* **How easy was it to get an appointment with a specialist through your plan?** (`specialist_appointment_ease`): This question evaluates the ease of accessing specialist care. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Very Difficult: It was very difficult to get a specialist appointment.
	+ Difficult: It was difficult to get a specialist appointment.
	+ Easy: It was easy to get a specialist appointment.
	+ Very Easy: It was very easy to get a specialist appointment.
* **How often were the plan materials (handbooks, website) easy to understand?** (`plan_material_clarity`): This question assesses the clarity of plan materials. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Never: The plan materials were never easy to understand.
	+ Sometimes: The plan materials were sometimes easy to understand.
	+ Usually: The plan materials were usually easy to understand.
	+ Always: The plan materials were always easy to understand.
* **Satisfaction with your plan’s list of covered drugs (formulary)** (`drug_coverage_satisfaction`): This question evaluates your satisfaction with your plan's formulary. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You were very dissatisfied with your plan's formulary.
	+ Dissatisfied: You were dissatisfied with your plan's formulary.
	+ Satisfied: You were satisfied with your plan's formulary.
	+ Very Satisfied: You were very satisfied with your plan's formulary.
* **Overall rating of your health plan** (`overall_medicaid_rating`): This question asks for your overall rating of your health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ 0: This is the lowest rating.
	+ 1: This is a poor rating.
	+ 2: This is a fair rating.
	+ 3: This is a good rating.
	+ 4: This is a very good rating.
	+ 5: This is an excellent rating.
	+ 6: This is a very good rating.
	+ 7: This is a good rating.
	+ 8: This is a fair rating.
	+ 9: This is a poor rating.
	+ 10: This is the highest rating.
* **How likely are you to stay with this health plan next year?** (`plan_switching_intent`): This question evaluates your intent to stay with your current health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Very Unlikely: You are very unlikely to stay with the plan.
	+ Unlikely: You are unlikely to stay with the plan.
	+ Likely: You are likely to stay with the plan.
	+ Very Likely: You are very likely to stay with the plan.
* **In general, how would you rate your overall health?** (`general_health_status_adult`): This question asks for your overall health status. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Poor: Your health is poor.
	+ Fair: Your health is fair.
	+ Good: Your health is good.
	+ Very Good: Your health is very good.
	+ Excellent: Your health is excellent.
* **What is one thing your health plan could do better?** (`specific_plan_improvement`): This question provides an opportunity for you to suggest improvements to your health plan. Enter a suggestion or comment that you think would help improve your plan.
* **I confirm I am the primary member or authorized representative** (`survey_validation_check`): This question confirms that you are the primary member or authorized representative of your health plan. Select the answer that best reflects your experience:
	+ Confirmed: You are the primary member or authorized representative.
	+ Other: You are not the primary member or authorized representative.

Please answer these questions according to your personal experience with your health plan. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact your health plan or the CAHPS team.
