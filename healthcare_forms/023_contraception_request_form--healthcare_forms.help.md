# Contraception Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's request for contraception, including demographic data, medical history, and preferences for contact and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full name.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number.
4. Enter the patient's contact email address.
5. Enter the patient's phone number.
6. Select the date the patient is requesting contraception.
7. Choose the type of contraception the patient is requesting.
8. If the patient has used contraception before, select 'Yes, currently using' or 'Yes, used in the past'.
9. If the patient has used contraception before, select the type of contraception they used.
10. Select the reason for changing or starting contraception.
11. List any relevant medical conditions the patient may have.
12. List any current medications the patient is taking.
13. List any known allergies the patient may have.
14. Select the patient's smoking status.
15. Select the patient's pregnancy status.
16. If applicable, select whether the patient is currently breastfeeding.
17. If applicable, enter any side effects the patient may have experienced.
18. Enter the name and location of the preferred pharmacy.
19. Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
20. Enter the name of the provider requesting contraception.
21. Select the preferred method of contact.
22. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient ID** (3, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter the patient's contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Request Date** (6, date, required): Select the date the patient is requesting contraception.
* **Contraceptive Type Requested** (7, select_one, required): Choose the type of contraception the patient is requesting.
* **Previous Contraceptive Use** (8, select_one, required): If the patient has used contraception before, select 'Yes, currently using' or 'Yes, used in the past'.
* **Previous Method** (9, text, optional): If the patient has used contraception before, select the type of contraception they used.
* **Reason for Change** (10, select_one, required): Select the reason for changing or starting contraception.
* **Medical Conditions** (11, select_multiple, required): List any relevant health conditions the patient may have.
* **Current Medications** (12, text, required): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (13, text, required): List any known allergies the patient may have.
* **Smoking Status** (14, select_one, required): Select the patient's smoking status.
* **Pregnancy Status** (15, select_one, required): Select the patient's pregnancy status.
* **Breastfeeding** (16, select_one, optional): If applicable, select whether the patient is currently breastfeeding.
* **Side Effects Experienced** (17, text, optional): If applicable, enter any side effects the patient may have experienced.
* **Preferred Pharmacy** (18, text, optional): Enter the name and location of the preferred pharmacy.
* **Insurance Provider** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
* **Provider Name** (20, text, optional): Enter the name of the provider requesting contraception.
* **Follow-up Preference** (21, select_one, required): Select the preferred method of contact.
* **Additional Comments** (22, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate dates of birth to prevent issues with the patient's medical history.
- If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field's label for a hint or ask for clarification.
- If you select 'Yes, used in the past' for Previous Contraceptive Use, make sure to fill out Previous Method.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label for a hint or ask for clarification.
