# aekmlant_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The aekmlant_consent_form is designed to collect patient information for consent purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have a valid reason for collecting this information.
2. Enter your name in the space provided.
3. Enter your address in the space provided.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (name)**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their official documents.
* **Address (address)**: Enter the patient's current address.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that you use to contact the patient.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper identification and communication with the patient.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's recommended to consult with the patient directly to confirm the details.
