# Adult Chiropractic Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from adults who are seeking chiropractic treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, contact information, and emergency contact details.
2. Provide your primary reason for visiting the chiropractor and rate your pain intensity level.
3. Specify the location of your discomfort or pain.
4. Indicate any symptoms you are experiencing.
5. If applicable, provide details about previous chiropractic care you have received.
6. Select your daily activity level.
7. Choose how you sleep and whether you wish to receive treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (2)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Contact Number (4)**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for any further information or appointment reminders.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information or appointment reminders.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone (6)**: Enter the name and phone number of a person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Primary Reason for Visit (8)**: Select the main reason you would like the chiropractor to address.
* **Pain Intensity Level (9)**: Rate your pain intensity from 1 to 10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Location of Discomfort (10)**: Specify the location of your discomfort or pain.
* **Symptoms Experienced (11)**: Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list provided.
* **Date Symptoms Started (12)**: Enter the approximate date when your symptoms started.
* **Previous Chiropractic Care History (13)**: If you have received chiropractic care before, please list the clinics or dates of your previous visits.
* **Sleep Position (15)**: Select how you usually sleep, whether it is on your back, side, stomach, or varies.
* **Daily Activity Level (16)**: Select your typical level of physical activity from the options provided.
* **Treatment Consent (18)**: Select whether you authorize or do not authorize treatment, understanding that results are not guaranteed.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure you receive the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or options, please ask a healthcare professional for clarification.
* Please fill in the form carefully and thoroughly, as this will help us understand your needs and preferences better.
