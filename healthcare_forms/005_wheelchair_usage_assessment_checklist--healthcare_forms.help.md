# Wheelchair Usage Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheelchair Usage Assessment Checklist is a form used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's mobility level, wheelchair type, and overall condition of their wheelchairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth in the designated fields.
2. Choose the patient's mobility level from the options provided.
3. Select the patient's mobility status, which describes their current level of mobility.
4. If the patient requires assistance with transfers, select the frequency and type of transfers they need.
5. Specify the patient's wheelchair type and condition.
6. Enter the weight of the patient and their wheelchair.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's wheelchair usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Mobility Level (3)**: Choose from the options:
	+ Low: The patient requires assistance with daily activities, but can perform them.
	+ Moderate: The patient has some difficulty performing daily activities.
	+ Severe: The patient is unable to perform daily activities without assistance.
* **Mobility Status (4)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Independent: The patient is able to perform daily activities without assistance.
	+ Assisted: The patient requires assistance with daily activities.
	+ Requires Assistance: The patient requires full assistance with daily activities.
* **Transfers (5)**: If the patient requires assistance with transfers, select the number of transfers they need per day. If not applicable, select "No Transfers".
* **Transfers Frequency (6)**: Enter the frequency of transfers, if applicable.
* **Transfers Type (7)**: Choose from the options:
	+ Manual: Transfers are performed manually.
	+ Power: Transfers are performed using a power chair or device.
	+ No Transfers: The patient does not require transfers.
* **Wheelchair Type (8)**: Choose from the options:
	+ Manual: The patient uses a manual wheelchair.
	+ Power: The patient uses a power wheelchair.
	+ Tilt: The patient uses a tilt-in-space wheelchair.
* **Wheelchair Usage (9)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's wheelchair usage.
* **Wheelchair Condition (10)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Good: The wheelchair is in good condition.
	+ Fair: The wheelchair requires some repair or maintenance.
	+ Poor: The wheelchair is in poor condition and requires replacement.
* **Wheelchair Weight (11)**: Enter the weight of the patient's wheelchair.
* **User Weight (12)**: Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
