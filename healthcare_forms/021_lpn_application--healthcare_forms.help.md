# LPN Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic demographic information from LPNs applying for a specific program or service. The information collected will help us assess your eligibility and provide a more tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you provide a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your birth date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your license number and issued date in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current address, including street address, apartment number, and city, state, or province.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the correct format (###-#####-##### or (###) ###-####).
* **Birth Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **License Number** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your LPN license number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **License Issued Date** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your LPN license was issued in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information for spelling and formatting errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
