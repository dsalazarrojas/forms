# video consultation informed consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients prior to video consultations. It collects patient information, consent to communicate, medical history, allergies, medications, medical conditions, emergency contacts, and signatures from both the patient and clinician. The form ensures that both parties are on the same page and aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on page 1, under "Patient Information".
2. Provide contact information for the patient on page 2.
3. Consent to communicate with the patient and select either "None" or "Other" as the method of communication.
4. Consent to treatment on page 3, selecting either "None" or "Other".
5. Provide medical history, allergies, medications, and medical conditions on page 4.
6. List emergency contacts on page 5.
7. Sign and date the form on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
	+ **Contact Information (Page 2)**
		- **Name**: Enter the patient's contact name.
		- **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
		- **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
	+ **Consent to Communicate (Page 3)**
		- **Consent to Communicate**: Select either "None" or "Other" as the method of communication.
	+ **Consent to Treat (Page 3)**
		- **Consent to Treat**: Select either "None" or "Other".
	+ **Medical History (Page 4)**
		- **Medical History**: Describe any past medical history that may be relevant to the consultation.
	+ **Allergies (Page 4)**
		- **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
	+ **Medications (Page 4)**
		- **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
	+ **Medical Conditions (Page 4)**
		- **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions the patient has.
	+ **Emergency Contacts (Page 5)**
		- **Emergency Contact 1**: List the name and contact information of the first emergency contact.
		- **Emergency Contact 2**: List the name and contact information of the second emergency contact.
		- **Emergency Contact 3**: List the name and contact information of the third emergency contact.
	+ **Signatures (Page 6)**
		- **Signature of Patient**: Sign and date the form as the patient.
		- **Signature of Clinician**: Sign and date the form as the clinician.
		- **Date of Consent**: Sign and date the form with the date of consent.
		- **Time of Consent**: Sign and date the form with the time of consent.
		- **Signature of Patient 2**: Sign and date the form as the patient (second signature line).
		- **Signature of Clinician 2**: Sign and date the form as the clinician (second signature line).
		- **Date of Consent 2**: Sign and date the form with the date of consent (second signature line).
		- **Time of Consent 2**: Sign and date the form with the time of consent (second signature line).

Note: This form is designed to ensure that both the patient and clinician are aware of their roles and responsibilities prior to the video consultation. Please ensure that the form is completed thoroughly and accurately.
