# Laboratory Result Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Result Form is designed to collect information about test results and specimen analysis from clinical laboratories. This form is used to streamline the process of reporting test results and improve communication between healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*). If a field is not required, feel free to skip it.
2. Enter the necessary information for each field, following the provided instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to the test result or specimen analysis. This field is optional and should only be used for supplementary comments or context.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, as recorded on the medical record or laboratory report.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, optional): Report the results of the test or analysis, including any significant findings or abnormalities.
* **Clinical Laboratory** (`clinical_laboratory`, text, optional): Enter the name of the clinical laboratory that performed the test or analysis.
* **Specimen Analysis** (`specimen_analysis`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the specimen used for the test or analysis.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the form has been submitted to, such as Primary Care Physician, Clinical Laboratory, or Pathologist.
* **Medical Official** (`medical_official`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical professional who authorized the test or analysis.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed the test results carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and complete information to avoid errors or miscommunication.
