# Daily Support Carer Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Support Carer Notes" form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to document important information about patients under their care. It serves as a daily record of the patient's status, including their medical history, vital signs, medications, and next steps for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the patient's ID and name.
2.  Record the date and carer's name.
3.  Indicate if the patient has any medical notes.
4.  Report if the patient is on any medications.
5.  Check the patient's vital signs.
6.  Report any allergies the patient has.
7.  Document the patient's medical history.
8.  Add any carer notes for reference.
9.  Choose the next action for the patient.
10. Schedule the next visit date (if applicable).
11.  Choose the contact method for the patient.
12.  Sign the form with your carer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **patient_id** (Number): Enter the patient's ID number for reference.
*   **date** (Date): Record the date of the entry.
*   **carer_name** (Text): Type in your name as the carer for identification purposes.
*   **carer_id** (Number): Enter your ID number as the carer.
*   **patient_name** (Text): Record the patient's name.
*   **medical_notes** (Note): Enter any important medical notes for the patient.
*   **medications** (Select Multiple): Check if the patient is on any medications.
*   **vital_signs** (Select Multiple): Check the patient's vital signs.
*   **allergies** (Select Multiple): Report any allergies the patient has.
*   **medical_history** (Note): Document the patient's medical history.
*   **carer_note** (Text): Add any carer notes for reference.
*   **next_action** (Select One): Choose the next action for the patient.
*   **next_visit_date** (Date): Schedule the next visit date.
*   **contact_method** (Select Multiple): Choose how you will contact the patient.
*   **carer_signature** (Text): Sign the form with your carer's signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly for the patient's best care.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form for the patient's benefit.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the patient's chart or consult with other healthcare professionals.
- This form is designed to aid in documenting crucial patient information, so it's crucial to complete it as a routine part of your workday.
