# Patient Portal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for sharing their personal and medical information with healthcare providers and/or insurance companies. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your medical information is shared correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please review the form carefully**: Before starting, take a moment to review the form and its sections to ensure you understand what is required.
2. **Provide personal information**: On page 1, please fill out your personal details such as name, date of birth, and address.
3. **Share your medical history**: On page 2, describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
4. **Choose your consent options**: On page 3, select the types of information you're willing to share with healthcare providers, insurance companies, or family members.
5. **Add any additional notes**: On page 5, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	+ **Address** (Text): Provide your address to ensure we can contact you with any important updates.
* **Medical History (Page 2)**:
	+ **Medical History** (Text): Describe any previous surgeries, illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Consent (Page 3)**:
	+ **Consent to share information with healthcare provider**:
		- Select this option to allow sharing your medical information with your healthcare providers.
	+ **Consent to share information with insurance company**:
		- Select this option to allow sharing your medical information with your insurance company.
	+ **Consent to share information with family member**:
		- Select this option to allow sharing your medical information with your family member.
* **Contact Information (Page 4)**:
	+ **Phone Number** (Text): Provide your phone number to ensure we can contact you with any urgent matters.
	+ **Email** (Text): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Additional Notes (Page 5)**:
	+ **Additional Notes** (Text): This is an optional field for you to add any relevant details or comments about your medical history or consent.
