# Virtual Doula Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for clients to provide consent for virtual doula services, which includes online support and guidance during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and any other requested information on the form.
2. Check the box to agree to the terms of service.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (`client_name`):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`):** Provide your phone number and any other contact details where we can reach you.
* **Birth Location (`birth_location`):** The city, state, or province where you plan to give birth.
* **Doulas Information (`doula_info`):** A brief description of your doula experience and any relevant qualifications.
* **Consent (`i_agree`):** Check the box to agree to our terms of service, including confidentiality, communication, and cancellation policies.
* **Birth Information (`birth_info`):** Provide any additional information about your birth, such as any medical conditions or complications.
* **Notes (`notes`):** Any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with your doula.
* **Email (`email`):** Your email address where we can send you updates and information.
* **Phone (`phone`):** Your phone number where we can reach you for any urgent matters.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`):** The date of your birthday.
* **Time of Birth (`time_of_birth`):** The time of your birth.
* **Location of Birth (`location_of_birth`):** The city, state, or province where you were born.
* **Birth Place (`birth_place`):** The location where you were born.
* **Birth Name (`birth_name`):** The name of your birth location (e.g. hospital, home, etc.).
