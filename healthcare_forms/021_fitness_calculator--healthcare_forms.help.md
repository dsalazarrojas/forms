# Fitness Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users to estimate their fitness level based on their demographics and physical activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your age in years.
2. Enter your height in centimeters.
3. Enter your weight in kilograms.
4. Select your gender.
5. Select your exercise frequency (e.g., High, Low, Moderate, Rarely).
6. Select your exercise duration (e.g., Long, Medium, Short, None).
7. Select your water intake (e.g., 2-3 cups, 1 cup, More than 3 cups, None).
8. Select your exercise type (e.g., Running, Swimming, Cycling, Strength training, Flexibility exercises, Other).
9. Enter your sleep duration in hours.
10. Enter your maximum heart rate (beats per minute).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Exercise Frequency**: Select your exercise frequency (High, Low, Moderate, Rarely).
* **Exercise Duration**: Select your exercise duration (Long, Medium, Short, None).
* **Water Intake**: Select your water intake (2-3 cups, 1 cup, More than 3 cups, None).
* **Exercise Type**: Select your exercise type (Running, Swimming, Cycling, Strength training, Flexibility exercises, Other).
* **Sleep Duration**: Enter your sleep duration in hours.
* **Max Heart Rate**: Enter your maximum heart rate in beats per minute.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please consult a healthcare professional before using this form.
* This form is for estimation purposes only and should not be relied upon as a medical diagnosis.
* If you need personalized advice or recommendations, please consult a fitness professional.
