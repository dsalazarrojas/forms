## Urine Drug Screening Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the purpose of collecting and screening urine samples from patients for drug testing and analysis. This includes selecting the type of sample collection (urine, blood, or hair), specifying the ordered tests (e.g., urine drug screening, comprehensive urine drug screen, or toxicology panel), and recording the results of the tests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of sample collection (urine, blood, or hair) from the "Sample Collection" dropdown menu.
2.  Choose the test to be ordered (e.g., Urine Drug Screening Test, Comprehensive Urine Drug Screen, or Toxicology Panel Test) from the "Tests Ordered" dropdown menu.
3.  Record the patient's information, which may include name, address, phone number, and contact information, in the "Lab Information" field.
4.  Enter the date the test was ordered in the "Test Date" field.
5.  Select the time the specimen was received by the laboratory in the "Specimen Received" field.
6.  Specify who ordered the test in the "Ordered By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sample Collection**: Select the type of sample to be collected (Urine, Blood, or Hair) to indicate the type of test to be performed.
*   **Tests Ordered**: Choose the specific test to be performed, such as Urine Drug Screening Test, Comprehensive Urine Drug Screen, or Toxicology Panel Test, to ensure accurate testing.
*   **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's information, including name, address, phone number, and contact information, for accurate identification and contact.
*   **Test Date**: Enter the date the test was ordered to keep records accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Specimen Received**: Indicate the time the specimen was received by the laboratory for accurate tracking.
*   **Ordered By**: Specify who ordered the test (e.g., Assigned Tool or Other) to identify responsible parties.

## Tips

*   Ensure to clearly select the test ordered and sample collection to avoid errors.
*   Double-check the patient's information for accuracy to prevent misunderstandings.
*   Use the correct format for the "Test Date" to keep records consistent.
*   Indicate the exact time the specimen was received to ensure accurate tracking and processing.
*   Verify who ordered the test for accurate record-keeping.
