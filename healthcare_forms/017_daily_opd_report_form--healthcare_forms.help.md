# Daily OPD Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily OPD (Outpatient Department) Report Form is used to capture information about patient visits that occurred on a specific date. This form helps healthcare professionals track and report on patient visits, allowing for accurate and efficient management of patient care and data analysis. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the patient's visit details, including their name, department visited, date, and time of visit.
- Select the correct department from the list of available options.
- Enter the date of the visit using the date field.
- Enter the time of the visit using the time field.
- If necessary, provide a brief description of the visit type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (`patient_name`, Text, Required):** Enter the name of the patient who visited the OPD on the specified date.
* **Department (`department`, Select One, Optional):** Choose the department that the patient visited from the list of available options. This field is useful for tracking patient visits across different departments.
* **Date (`date`, Date, Required):** Enter the date on which the patient visited the OPD.
* **Time (`time`, Time, Required):** Enter the time of the visit.
* **Visit Type (`visit_type`, Text, Optional):** Provide a brief description of the visit type (e.g., new patient, follow-up, emergency) if necessary.
* **Total Visits (`total_visits`, Number, Required):** Enter the total number of visits for the patient on that date.

## Tips
- Ensure that the date and time fields are entered accurately to reflect the actual visit schedule.
- Double-check the department field to ensure it matches the patient's visit record.
- If the patient had multiple visits on the same date, enter the total number of visits in the "Total Visits" field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult the patient's visit record or contact the relevant department for clarification.
