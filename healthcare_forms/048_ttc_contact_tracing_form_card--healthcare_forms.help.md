# TTC Contact Tracing Form Card - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about individuals who have been exposed to a contagious disease. It helps healthcare professionals track the spread of the disease and identify potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name and contact information of the person you had contact with in the "Exposed Contact" field.
2. Describe the location of the exposure in the "Exposure Location" field.
3. Select the date range during which the exposure occurred in the "Exposure Date Range" field.
4. Enter the length of time you were exposed to the disease in the "Exposure Duration" field.
5. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing in the "Exposure Symptoms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exposed Contact**: This field is where you enter the name and contact information of the person you had contact with.
* **Exposure Location**: This field is for describing the location where the exposure occurred.
* **Exposure Date Range**: This field is for selecting the date range during which the exposure occurred.
* **Exposure Duration**: This field is for entering the length of time you were exposed to the disease.
* **Exposure Symptoms**: This field is for describing any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Tips**: Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to help healthcare professionals track the spread of the disease effectively.
