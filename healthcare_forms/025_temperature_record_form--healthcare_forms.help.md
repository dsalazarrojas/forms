# temperature_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The temperature_record_form is a medical form used to record temperature readings in multiple areas of the body, such as body, oral, and rectal, for accurate and consistent patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on the User Info page.
2. Record the body temperature on the Body Temp page.
3. Record the oral temperature on the Oral Temp page.
4. Record the rectal temperature on the Rectal Temp page.
5. Record the pulse on the Pulse page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (user_info, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or any other relevant medical information.
* **Body Temp** (body_temp, number, required: false): Record the body temperature reading.
* **Oral Temp** (oral_temp, number, required: false): Record the oral temperature reading.
* **Rectal Temp** (rectal_temp, number, required: false): Record the rectal temperature reading.
* **Pulse** (pulse, number, required: false): Record the pulse reading.
