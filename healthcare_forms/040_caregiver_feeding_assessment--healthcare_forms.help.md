# Caregiver Feeding Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Caregiver Feeding Assessment is a form designed to gather information about a child's feeding habits, concerns, and history. This form helps healthcare providers and caregivers understand the child's eating patterns, appetite, and overall feeding environment to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the caregiver's name and contact information.
2.  Provide information about the child, including their name, age, date of birth, and healthcare provider or clinic name (if applicable).
3.  Describe the reason for the feeding assessment.
4.  Answer questions about the child's feeding history, including their main concerns, dietary habits, and any allergies or sensitivities.
5.  Report on the child's current diet and any feeding utensils used.
6.  Describe the child's eating environment and behavior during meals.
7.  Provide information about the child's appetite level, recent changes in appetite, and any digestion concerns.
8.  Answer questions about the child's weight and growth concerns, developmental delays, and medical conditions affecting feeding.
9.  Finally, report on any medications the child is taking that may impact feeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the caregiver providing the information.
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child being assessed.
* **Child's Age** (`child_age`, text, required): Enter the child's age in years and months.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date this assessment was completed.
* **Healthcare Provider or Clinic Name** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the healthcare provider or clinic conducting the assessment.
* **Reason for Feeding Assessment** (`reason_for_assessment`, text, required): Briefly explain why this assessment is being conducted.
* **Primary Feeding Concerns** (`feeding_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select the child's primary feeding concerns from the list below:
	+ Difficulty swallowing or choking
	+ Refuses to eat or poor appetite
	+ Selective eating or limited foods
	+ Difficulty chewing
	+ Drooling or dribbling
	+ Food aversion
	+ Weight gain or growth concerns
	+ Allergies or sensitivities
	+ Gastrointestinal issues
	+ Behavioral feeding problems
* **Feeding History** (`feeding_history`, text, optional): Describe the child's feeding background.
* **Current Diet and Foods Consumed** (`current_diet`, text, required): Describe the child's current diet and the foods they consume.
* **Bottle Feeding and Formula (if applicable)** (`bottle_formula`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the child's bottle feeding and formula usage.
* **Breastfeeding Status (if applicable)** (`breast_feeding`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the child's breastfeeding status.
* **Food Preferences and Favorites** (`food_preferences`, text, optional): Describe the child's food preferences and favorites.
* **Food Dislikes or Refused Foods** (`food_dislikes`, text, optional): Describe the child's food dislikes or refused foods.
* **Texture Preferences** (`texture_preferences`, text, optional): Describe the child's texture preferences.
* **Feeding Utensils and Independence** (`feeding_utensils`, text, optional): Describe the child's feeding utensils and independence level.
* **Number of Meals and Snacks Per Day** (`meals_per_day`, number, required): Enter the number of meals and snacks the child typically has per day.
* **Typical Feeding Time Duration** (`feeding_time`, text, optional): Describe the length of a typical meal or snack.
* **Typical Feeding Environment** (`feeding_environment`, text, optional): Describe the environment where the child typically eats.
* **Child's Behavior During Meals** (`behavior_during_meals`, text, optional): Describe the child's behavior during meals.
* **Gagging, Coughing, or Choking Episodes** (`gagging_choking`, text, optional): Report any episodes of gagging or choking.
* **Known Food Allergies or Sensitivities** (`allergies_sensitivities`, text, required): List any known food allergies or sensitivities.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Special Diets** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any dietary restrictions or special diets.
* **Current Appetite Level** (`current_appetite`, select_one, required): Select the child's current appetite level from the list below:
	+ Excellent - eats well and enthusiastically
	+ Good - adequate appetite
	+ Fair - inconsistent appetite
	+ Poor - minimal appetite
	+ Refuses food
* **Recent Changes in Appetite** (`recent_appetite_changes`, text, optional): Report any recent changes in appetite.
* **Digestion or Gastrointestinal Concerns** (`digestion_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any digestion concerns, such as constipation, diarrhea, or reflux.
* **Weight and Growth Concerns** (`weight_growth`, text, optional): Report any concerns about the child's weight or growth.
* **Any Developmental Delays or Special Needs?** (`developmental_delays`, text, optional): Report any developmental delays or special needs related to feeding.
* **Medical Conditions Affecting Feeding** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions affecting the child's feeding.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Report any medications the child is taking that may impact feeding.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly to ensure accurate information.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the child's feeding habits and concerns.
* If unsure about any questions, ask for guidance from a healthcare provider or caregiver.
* This form is intended to be completed by a caregiver or parent, but healthcare providers may also use it for their own assessment.
