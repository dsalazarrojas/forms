# hospital_innovation_roundtable_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital innovation roundtable registration form is designed to register hospital staff members for the hospital innovation roundtable event. It helps the event organizers to gather information about each attendee and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your event date.
2. Enter your name as a required field.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select your category of work or affiliation with the hospital.
6. Choose one or more categories that describe your role as a healthcare professional.
7. Add any additional note or comment about your attendance or interest in the roundtable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Date (1)
Event Date is the date of the event you are registering for. Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Attendee Name (2)
Enter your name to help us identify you as a registered attendee.

### Email (3)
Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event details and updates.

### Phone (4)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Category (5)
Select your category of work or affiliation with the hospital. Choose from the options healthcare or other.

### Attendee Category (6)
Select one or more categories that describe your role as a healthcare professional. Choose from the options physician, nurse, or other.

### Note (7)
Add any additional note or comment about your attendance or interest in the roundtable. 

### Hospital Name (8)
This field is optional and you can leave it blank. It is meant for hospitals to register multiple staff members at once. 

### Tips
- Please ensure that you enter the correct event date.
- Make sure to provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
- Select the correct category of work or affiliation with the hospital.
- You can select multiple attendee categories if applicable.
