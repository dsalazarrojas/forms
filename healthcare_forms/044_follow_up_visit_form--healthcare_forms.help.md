# follow_up_visit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "follow_up_visit_form" is designed to record information about a patient's progress during a follow-up visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the patient's ID.
2. Enter the date of the current visit.
3. Provide a brief medical history.
4. Mention any medication taken.
5. List any allergies.
6. State the patient's chief complaint.
7. Describe the treatment plan.
8. Set the date for the next follow-up visit.
9. Record the patient's progress.
10. Document any medication side effects.
11. Include any comments from the patient.
12. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Id** (`Patient Id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier of the patient.
* **Date Of Visit** (`Date Of Visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the current visit.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Medication Taken** (`Medication Taken`, text, required): List any medication the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required): List any allergies the patient may have.
* **Chief Complaint** (`Chief Complaint`, text, required): Describe the patient's chief complaint.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, text, required): Outline the treatment plan for the patient.
* **Follow Up Visit Date** (`Follow Up Visit Date`, date, required): Enter the date for the next follow-up visit.
* **Next Appointment** (`Next Appointment`, date, required): Set the date for the next appointment.
* **Patient Progress** (`Patient Progress`, text, required): Record the patient's progress since the last visit.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`Medication Side Effects`, text, required): Document any side effects experienced by the patient due to medication.
* **Patient Comments** (`Patient Comments`, text, required): Allow the patient to provide any comments or concerns. 

Note: Ensure all fields are filled in as necessary, as indicated by the "required" label for each field.
