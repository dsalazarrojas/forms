# Physical Therapy Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a patient's physical therapy records, allowing the patient to authorize the sharing of their medical information with healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Choose whether you are authorized to share your medical information in the "Authorized to Share" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter the name of the healthcare provider you are authorizing to access your records in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.
5. Select the date you are authorizing the release of your records in the "Authorization Date" field.
6. Explain why you are releasing your records in the "Reason for Release" field.
7. Enter the date your authorization will expire in the "Authorization Expires" field.
8. You can add any additional information you want to include in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): This field allows you to input your name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): This field allows you to input your date of birth.
* **Medical Record ID** (`medical_record_id`, text, required: true): This is your unique identifier in the healthcare system.
* **Authorized to Share** (`authorized_to_share`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose whether you are authorized to share your medical information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: true): This is your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): This is your email address.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required: true): This is the name of the healthcare provider you are authorizing to access your records.
* **Healthcare Organization** (`healthcare_organization`, text, required: false): This is the name of the healthcare organization you are affiliated with.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: true): This is the date you are authorizing the release of your records.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required: false): This is your signature to confirm you authorized the release.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required: true): This is a brief explanation of why you are releasing your records.
* **Authorization Expires** (`authorization_expires`, date, required: false): This is the date your authorization will expire.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, note, required: false): This is the healthcare provider's signature to confirm they received the authorization.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): This is a field for any additional information you want to include.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before signing and submitting it.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
- Contact the healthcare provider if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
