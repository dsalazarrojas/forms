# sickness_monitoring_invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their health status, including their sicknesses, symptoms, and follow-up details. This information is used to monitor and manage the patient's health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
2. Check the boxes for the **Sicknesses** you are experiencing (e.g. Sore Throat, Headache, Flu, Cough).
3. Describe your **Symptoms** in the text field.
4. Enter your **Contact Number**.
5. If applicable, choose the **Follow Up Date** and **Follow Up Time** for your next appointment.
6. Select the method you would like to use for your next **Follow Up Contact** (e.g. Self, Medical Team, Family Member, Other).
7. Provide details on your **Follow Up Method**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Sicknesses** (`sicknesses`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes for the sicknesses you are experiencing (e.g. Sore Throat, Headache, Flu, Cough).
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe your symptoms in the text field.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the method you would like to use for your next follow-up contact (e.g. Self, Medical Team, Family Member, Other).
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, text, optional): Provide details on your follow-up method.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Check for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* The information collected will be used to monitor and manage your health status.
