# Acupuncturist Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used by patients to provide consent for telehealth services with an acupuncturist. It is essential to understand and agree to the terms of the session, including technology requirements, limitations, and confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
3. Sign the form electronically when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Provide your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter your current residential address.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Provide a contact phone number for telehealth connection details.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for telehealth connection details.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the licensed acupuncturist providing the session.
* **Practitioner License Number** (`practitioner_license`, text, required): Enter the practitioner's professional license number.
* **Telehealth Session Date** (`telehealth_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled telehealth appointment.
* **Telehealth Session Time** (`telehealth_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the scheduled telehealth appointment.
* **Technology Requirements Acknowledged** (`technology_requirements_acknowledged`, select_one, required): If you have the necessary technology for the telehealth session, select "Yes", otherwise, select "No" or "Need assistance".
* **Internet Connection Quality** (`internet_connection_quality`, select_one, required): Rate your internet connection quality as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Device Used for Session** (`device_used`, select_one, required): Select the device you will use for the telehealth session, such as "Computer", "Tablet", "Smartphone", or "Other".
* **Limitations of Telehealth Acknowledged** (`limitations_acknowledged`, select_one, required): If you understand the limitations of telehealth compared to in-person visits, select "Yes, I understand", otherwise, select "No, I need clarification".
* **Privacy Measures** (`privacy_measures`, text, required): Describe how you will ensure privacy during the session.
* **Emergency Procedures Acknowledged** (`emergency_procedures_acknowledged`, select_one, required): If you understand what to do in case of an emergency during the session, select "Yes", otherwise, select "No".
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): If you agree to maintain confidentiality of the session, select "Yes", otherwise, select "No".
* **Consent to Telehealth Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): If you consent to receive acupuncture consultation via telehealth, select "Yes, I consent", otherwise, select "No, I do not consent".
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, optional): If you consent to the session being recorded for clinical purposes, select "Yes", otherwise, select "No" or "Only certain portions".
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing this consent form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Provide your electronic signature.
* **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, text, required): The practitioner's signature acknowledging the consent discussion.
