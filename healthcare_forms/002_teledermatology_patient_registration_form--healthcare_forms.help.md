# Teledermatology Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register patients for teledermatology services, which enables healthcare providers to access and manage patient data for remote consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have permission from the patient to share their data and that you have the necessary permissions to access it within your institution.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Patient Registration (page 1)
	* Medical Practitioner (page 2)
	* Patient Name (page 3)
	* Date of Birth (page 4)
	* Medical Condition (page 5)
	* Phone Number (page 6)
	* Email (page 7)
	* Medical Practitioner's Email (page 8)
	* Medical Practitioner's Phone Number (page 9)
	* Medical Record Number (page 10)
	* Patient Address (page 11)
	* Medical Practitioner Name (page 12)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Registration (page 1)**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll enter the patient's information.
* **Medical Practitioner (page 2)**: Enter the contact information of the healthcare practitioner who will be handling the patient's care.
* **Patient Name (page 3)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID or medical record.
* **Date of Birth (page 4)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical Condition (page 5)**: Select all applicable medical conditions the patient has, such as Acne, Psoriasis, Eczema, Melanoma, or Other (if not listed).
* **Phone Number (page 6)**: Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email (page 7)**: Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **Medical Practitioner's Email (page 8)**: Enter the email address of the healthcare practitioner who will be handling the patient's care.
* **Medical Practitioner's Phone Number (page 9)**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare practitioner who will be handling the patient's care.
* **Medical Record Number (page 10)**: Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
* **Patient Address (page 11)**: Enter the patient's address for billing and communication purposes.
* **Medical Practitioner Name (page 12)**: Enter the name of the healthcare practitioner who will be handling the patient's care.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or select "Other" if specified.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with the patient or healthcare practitioner before proceeding.
