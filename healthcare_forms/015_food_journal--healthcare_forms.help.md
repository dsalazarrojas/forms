# food journal - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Journal form is a daily tracking tool designed to help you document your eating habits and progress over a 7-day period. This form collects information on the days you consume food and allows you to record your experiences, observations, and any notable events related to your eating habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Food Journal, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting each day of your food intake using the "Day One" through "Day Seven" fields. Select "Yes" for the days you do consume food and "No" for the days you do not.
*   Fill in any relevant details in the "Notes" field, providing context and explanations for your food intake and experiences.
*   In the second "Notes" field, you can write any additional notes or comments about your food consumption and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Day One** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day One, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Two** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Two, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Three** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Three, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Four** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Four, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Five** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Five, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Six** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Six, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Day Seven** (`Yes/No`, required): Select "Yes" if you consumed food on Day Seven, and "No" if you did not.
*   **Notes** (`Text`, optional): Add any relevant details or explanations for your food intake and experiences.
*   **Note** (`Text`, optional): Write any additional notes or comments about your food consumption and experiences.
