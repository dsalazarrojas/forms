# Meal Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Planning Form is designed to help healthcare professionals create personalized meal plans that cater to your dietary needs, habits, and preferences. This information will aid in improving your health outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Meal Planning Info**:
	* Enter your personal details and meal planning preferences.
2. **User Phone**:
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. **User Email**:
	* Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Meal Planning Info (text)**:
	* Enter a brief description of your meal planning needs, habits, and preferences. This will help healthcare professionals better understand your dietary requirements.
* **User Phone (text)**:
	* Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **User Email (email)**:
	* Enter your email address where you can receive updates and notifications regarding your meal plans.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help healthcare professionals create effective meal plans for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare team.
* You will receive personalized meal plans based on your submitted information.
* If you have any updates to your information, please notify your healthcare team promptly.
