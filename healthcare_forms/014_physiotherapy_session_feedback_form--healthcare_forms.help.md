# Physiotherapy Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiotherapy Session Feedback Form is designed to collect your feedback and evaluation of the physiotherapy session you've just attended. Your feedback will help the physiotherapist assess the session's effectiveness and make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a moment to review each page of the form carefully.
- Fill in the information requested on each page, taking care to fill in all required fields.
- Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each question.
- Take your time, and don't rush through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`patient_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the session overall, from 1 to 5.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the physiotherapy session.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required/optional): Write the name of the physiotherapist who conducted the session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required/optional): Rate the session length.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable ratings (High, Medium, Low).
* **Comments** (`rating_comment`, note, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you'd like to share.

### Page 2: Additional Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Write your feedback about the session.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you'd like a follow-up appointment (True, False, Maybe).
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comment`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the ratings you selected.
