# Health Nutrition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect data from patients for the purpose of evaluating their nutritional health. It is divided into 24 sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6.  Enter your height in feet in the "Height in Feet" field.
7.  Enter your weight in kilograms in the "Weight in KG" field.
8.  Select your blood type from the list of options in the "Blood Type" field.
9.  Describe any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Condition" field.
10.  Select the diets you follow from the list of options in the "Diet" field.
11.  Describe any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
12.  Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
13.  Select the medications you take from the list of options in the "Medications" field.
14.  Enter your height in centimeters in the "Height in CM" field.
15.  Enter your weight in pounds in the "Weight in LBS" field.
16.  Select your body mass index from the list of options in the "BMI" field.
17.  Enter your blood pressure in the "Blood Pressure" field.
18.  Describe any medical tests you have undergone in the "Medical Tests" field.
19.  Describe your family medical history in the "Family History" field.
20.  Describe your sleep pattern in the "Sleep Pattern" field.
21.  Enter your daily water intake in the "Daily Water Intake" field.
22.  Describe how frequently you exercise in the "Exercise Frequency" field.
23.  Describe how long you exercise in the "Exercise Duration" field.
24.  Describe how often you eat in the "Diet Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Height in Feet** (`height_in_feet`, number, required): Enter your height in feet.
* **Weight in KG** (`weight_in_kg`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, select_one, required): Select your blood type from the list of options.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Diet** (`diet`, select_multiple, required): Select the diets you follow from the list of options.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Describe any allergies you have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): Select the medications you take from the list of options.
* **Height in CM** (`height_in_cm`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight in LBS** (`weight_in_lbs`, number, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **BMI** (`body_mass_index`, select_one, required): Select your body mass index from the list of options.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, required): Enter your blood pressure.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, required): Describe any medical tests you have undergone.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required): Describe your family medical history.
* **Sleep Pattern** (`sleep_pattern`, text, required): Describe your sleep pattern.
* **Daily Water Intake** (`daily_water_intake`, number, required): Enter your daily water intake.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, text, required): Describe how frequently you exercise.
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, text, required): Describe how long you exercise.
* **Diet Frequency** (`diet_frequency`, text, required): Describe how often you eat.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, text, required): Set your health goals.
