# Dental Lab Work Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and obtain consent for dental lab work. It ensures that patients are aware of how their medical information will be handled and shared.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Provide the patient's contact information in the "contact_info" field.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "address" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone" field.
5. Indicate whether the patient consents to disclose their medical information by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "consent_to_disclose" field.
6. Indicate whether the patient consents to share their medical information by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "consent_to_share" field.
7. Enter the patient's email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
9. Enter the date of service for the dental lab work in the "date_of_service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **contact_info** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including any relevant details such as phone numbers and email addresses.
* **address** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **patient_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **consent_to_disclose** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient consents to disclose their medical information.
* **consent_to_share** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient consents to share their medical information.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **date_of_service** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of service for the dental lab work.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient has provided their consent before proceeding with the dental lab work.
* If the patient has not provided consent, please do not proceed with the dental lab work.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with third parties without patient consent.
