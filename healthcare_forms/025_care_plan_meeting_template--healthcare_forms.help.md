# Care Plan Meeting Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Plan Meeting Template is a comprehensive form designed to facilitate care planning meetings in healthcare settings. It guides healthcare providers in capturing essential information to create effective care plans for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the care plan meeting in the "Meeting date" field.
2. Enter the patient's full name in the "Patient name" field.
3. List the participants and their roles in the "Attendees" field (if applicable).
4. Describe the short-term and long-term goals of care in the "Goals of care" field.
5. Outline the interventions and their responsibilities in the "Interventions planned" field.
6. Assign a responsible person for follow-up and accountability in the "Responsible person for follow-up" field.
7. Set a timeline for review of the care plan in the "Timeline for review" field (if applicable).
8. List any additional resources required, such as equipment or referrals, in the "Additional resources needed" field (if applicable).
9. Summarize the meeting decisions and next steps in the "Meeting summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, date): Enter the date of the care plan meeting to record the occasion.
- **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name to identify the individual receiving care.
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, optional): List participants and their roles to establish accountability.
- **Goals of care** (`goals_of_care`, text, required): Describe the patient's short-term and long-term objectives.
- **Interventions planned** (`interventions_planned`, text, required): Outline the interventions and their respective responsibilities.
- **Responsible person for follow-up** (`responsible_person`, text, required): Assign a person accountable for follow-up and review of the care plan.
- **Timeline for review** (`timeline_for_review`, date, optional): Set a date for reassessment of the care plan, if necessary.
- **Additional resources needed** (`additional_resources_needed`, text, optional): List any extra resources, such as equipment or referrals.
- **Meeting summary** (`meeting_summary`, text, required): Summarize the meeting decisions and next steps for future reference.
