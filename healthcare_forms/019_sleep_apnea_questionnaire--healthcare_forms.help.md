# sleep_apnea_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sleep apnea questionnaire is designed to collect data about sleep apnea symptoms and severity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on each page (Page1, Page2, Page3) and fill out the corresponding questions or fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully, taking care to provide as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page1
* **Name** (`page1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the questionnaire. Please make sure to fill out your name accurately.
* **Page 2** (`page2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the second page of the questionnaire. Please make sure to fill out this page completely.
* **Page 3** (`page3`, `text`, required/optional): This is the third page of the questionnaire. Please make sure to fill out this page thoroughly.

### Page2
* **Age** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide your age to help us better understand your demographic information.
* **Sex** (`text`, required/optional): Please select your sex to help us better understand your demographic information.
* **Height** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide your height to help us better understand your physical characteristics.
* **Weight** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide your weight to help us better understand your physical characteristics.

### Page3
* **Symptoms of Sleep Apnea** (`text`, required/optional): Describe your sleep apnea symptoms, such as loud snoring, morning headaches, or difficulty staying awake during the day.
* **Duration of Symptoms** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide the length of time you have been experiencing sleep apnea symptoms.
* **Frequency of Symptoms** (`text`, required/optional): Please describe how often you experience sleep apnea symptoms, such as every night or only occasionally.
* **Impact on Daily Life** (`text`, required/optional): Please describe how sleep apnea has affected your daily life, such as difficulty concentrating or feeling tired easily.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This questionnaire is for information purposes only and is not intended to be used as a medical diagnosis. If you are experiencing sleep apnea symptoms, please consult a healthcare professional for proper diagnosis and treatment.
