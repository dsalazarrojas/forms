# Cardiology Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, symptoms, and treatment goals for a cardiology consultation. It helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and provide appropriate recommendations for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's details:
	* Patient Name: Enter the patient's full legal name.
	* Date of Birth: Enter the patient's date of birth.
	* Consultation Date: Enter the date of the consultation.
2. Describe your symptoms:
	* Chief Complaint: Explain why you are seeking cardiology consultation.
	* Symptoms: Select all current symptoms you are experiencing (e.g., Chest pain or discomfort, Shortness of breath, etc.).
3. Provide medical history:
	* Relevant Medical History: Select any relevant medical conditions you have (e.g., hypertension, diabetes, etc.).
	* Family History of Heart Disease: Confirm if any close relatives have cardiac disease.
4. List your current medications:
	* Current Medications: List all prescription and over-the-counter medications you are taking.
	* Medication Allergies: List any known allergies.
5. Lifestyle information:
	* Smoking Status: Indicate if you are a smoker, former smoker, or never smoked.
	* Alcohol Use: Report how many drinks you consume per week.
	* Exercise Frequency: Describe how often you exercise.
	* Dietary Habits: Describe your typical diet.
6. Additional information:
	* Recent Cardiac Tests or Procedures: List any tests or procedures performed in the past year.
	* Previous Cardiac Treatments: Describe any previous treatments or surgeries.
	* Quality of Life: Describe how your symptoms affect your daily activities.
	* Treatment Goals: Specify what you hope to achieve through treatment.
7. Confirm consent:
	* I consent to cardiac evaluation and treatment recommendations: Confirm you are willing to receive recommendations for treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's ID or medical record number (optional).
* **Consultation Date**: Enter the date of the consultation.
* **Chief Complaint**: Explain why you are seeking cardiology consultation.
* **How long have you experienced these symptoms?**: Describe the duration of your symptoms.
* **Current Symptoms**: Select all current symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Chest Pain Severity (if applicable)**: Rate the severity of your chest pain on a scale of 1-10.
* **What triggers your symptoms?**: Explain what triggers your symptoms (e.g., physical activity, stress, etc.).
* **What relieves your symptoms?**: Explain what relieves your symptoms (e.g., rest, medication, change in position, etc.).
* **Relevant Medical History**: Select any relevant medical conditions you have.
* **Family History of Heart Disease**: Confirm if any close relatives have cardiac disease.
* **Current Medications**: List all prescription and over-the-counter medications you are taking.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any known allergies.
* **Smoking Status**: Indicate if you are a smoker, former smoker, or never smoked.
* **Alcohol Use**: Report how many drinks you consume per week.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Describe how often you exercise.
* **Dietary Habits**: Describe your typical diet.
* **Recent Cardiac Tests or Procedures**: List any tests or procedures performed in the past year.
* **Previous Cardiac Treatments**: Describe any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Quality of Life**: Describe how your symptoms affect your daily activities.
* **Treatment Goals**: Specify what you hope to achieve through treatment.
* **Primary Insurance Provider**: Enter your primary insurance provider.
* **I consent to cardiac evaluation and treatment recommendations**: Confirm you are willing to receive recommendations for treatment.

Note: Some fields may not be required for every patient, as indicated by the "required" field in the form YAML. However, it's essential to fill in all relevant information to provide a complete and accurate picture of your condition.
