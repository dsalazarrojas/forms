**Form Purpose:** This form is designed to gather information about an individual's healthy habits, including their physical activity, water intake, sleep duration, eating habits, smoking status, alcohol consumption, exercise frequency, daily stress, overall health, and mental health. This data will be used to provide personalized insights and recommendations for maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

**How To Complete This Form:**

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your current habits.
3. Be honest when answering questions about your health and habits.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Physical activity**: Select one or more options that describe your level of physical activity. This includes exercise, sports, or other physical activities that you engage in regularly or occasionally.
	+ Exercise regularly: You engage in physical activity at least 3 times a week.
	+ Exercise occasionally: You engage in physical activity less than 3 times a week.
	+ Rarely exercise: You hardly ever engage in physical activity.
* **Water intake**: Enter the amount of water you drink per day. This is a measure of your daily water consumption.
* **Sleep duration**: Enter the duration of your average sleep time per night. This is a measure of your sleep quality and duration.
* **Healthy eating**: Select one option that best describes your eating habits. This includes your preference for fruits and vegetables, processed foods, or unhealthy snacks.
	+ Fruits and vegetables: You eat a balanced diet with plenty of fruits and vegetables.
	+ Processed foods: You eat a diet with a lot of processed foods.
	+ Unhealthy snacks: You eat a lot of unhealthy snacks.
	+ No preference: You don't have a specific preference for a certain type of food.
* **Smoking status**: Select one option that best describes your smoking status.
	+ True: You are a smoker.
	+ False: You are a non-smoker.
	+ Do not know: You are unsure about your smoking status.
* **Alcohol consumption**: Select one option that best describes your alcohol consumption frequency.
	+ Less than once a week: You drink alcohol less than once a week.
	+ 2-3 times a week: You drink alcohol 2-3 times a week.
	+ Once a week: You drink alcohol once a week.
	+ Every 2-3 days: You drink alcohol every 2-3 days.
	+ Every 1-2 days: You drink alcohol every 1-2 days.
	+ Every day: You drink alcohol daily.
* **Exercise frequency**: Select one or more options that describe how often you exercise.
	+ 1-2 times per week: You exercise 1-2 times per week.
	+ 3-4 times per week: You exercise 3-4 times per week.
	+ 5-6 times per week: You exercise 5-6 times per week.
	+ 7 or more times per week: You exercise very frequently.
* **Daily stress**: Select one or more options that describe your level of daily stress.
	+ Not at all: You experience little to no stress.
	+ 1-2 days per week: You experience stress 1-2 days per week.
	+ 3-4 days per week: You experience stress 3-4 days per week.
	+ Almost every day: You experience stress almost daily.
* **Overall health**: Select one option that best describes your overall health.
	+ Excellent: You are in excellent health.
	+ Very good: You are in very good health.
	+ Good: You are in good health.
	+ Fair: You are in fair health.
	+ Poor: You are in poor health.
* **Mental health**: Select one or more options that describe your mental health.
	+ Excellent: You are in excellent mental health.
	+ Very good: You are in very good mental health.
	+ Good: You are in good mental health.
	+ Fair: You are in fair mental health.
	+ Poor: You are in poor mental health.

**Tips:** Be honest when answering questions about your health and habits. This form is designed to provide you with personalized insights and recommendations for maintaining a healthy lifestyle.
