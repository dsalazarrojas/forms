# doctor information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and medical information from doctors to be stored in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Information section with your name, address, and date of birth.
2. In the Medical Information section, select your specialties, medical license status, and board certifications.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Page One, 6: `name`): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the system.
* **Address** (Page One, 7: `address`): Enter your full address, including street, city, and state.
* **Date of Birth** (Page One, 8: `date of birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number** (Page One, 5: `page_two_4`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Page One, 5: `page_two_4`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Information** (Page Two, 1: `page_one`): This field is not applicable.
* **Medical School** (Page Three, 9: `page_three`): Enter the name of your medical school.
* **Years Practicing** (Page Three, 10: `page_three_2`): Enter the number of years you have been practicing as a doctor.
* **Medical License Status** (Page Three, 11: `page_three_3`): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your medical license status.
* **Specialties** (Page Three, 13: `page_three_5`): Select all your specialties.
* **Board Certifications** (Page Three, 14: `page_three_6`): Select all your board certifications.
* **Medical Group** (Page Three, 15: `page_three_7`): Enter the name of your medical group. This field is not mandatory.
* **Phone/Contact Info** (Page Two, 4: `page_two_4`): Enter your contact information in the format 'Yes' or 'No', this field is optional and is for internal purpose.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and correctly. This information will be used to maintain accurate and up-to-date records for the doctor's contact and medical information.
