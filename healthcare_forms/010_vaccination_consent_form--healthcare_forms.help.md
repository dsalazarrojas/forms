# Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Consent Form is a document that captures the necessary patient information and vaccination details for accurate and efficient healthcare service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the patient information section.
2. Enter the patient's vaccination details, including the type of vaccination, location, and any other relevant information.
3. Provide the patient's contact information and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (`text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **patient_information** (`text`, optional): A brief description of the patient's medical condition or relevant health history.
* **vaccination_details** (`text`, optional): A detailed description of the vaccination, including the type, dosage, and frequency.
* **consent** (`select_multiple`, optional): Check the consents provided to the patient, such as Consent 1, Consent 2, and Consent 3.
* **vaccination_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the vaccination in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **vaccination_time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the vaccination in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **vaccination_location** (`select_one`, optional): Select the location where the vaccination was administered from the options, such as Location 1, Location 2, and Location 3.
* **provider_name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccination.
* **patient_age** (`number`, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **patient_gender** (`select_one`, optional): Select the patient's gender from the options, such as Gender 1, Gender 2, and Gender 3.
* **patient_race** (`select_one`, optional): Select the patient's race from the options, such as Race 1, Race 2, and Race 3.
* **patient_ethnicity** (`select_one`, optional): Select the patient's ethnicity from the options, such as Ethnicity 1, Ethnicity 2, and Ethnicity 3.
* **patient_primary_language** (`select_one`, optional): Select the patient's primary language from the options, such as Language 1, Language 2, and Language 3.
* **patient_medications** (`text`, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **patient_allergies** (`text`, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **vaccination_provider** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccination.
* **patient_contact_person** (`text`, optional): Enter the name and relationship of the contact person, such as parent or guardian.
* **patient_relationship** (`text`, optional): Enter the relationship between the patient and the contact person.
* **patient_other_health_conditions** (`text`, optional): Enter any other relevant health conditions the patient has.
* **patient_vaccination_history** (`text`, optional): Enter any relevant vaccination history the patient has.
* **vaccination_dosage** (`number`, optional): Enter the dosage of the vaccination.
* **patient_vaccination_frequency** (`number`, optional): Enter the frequency of the vaccination.
* **vaccination_administered_by** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccination.
* **vaccination_administered_by_position** (`text`, optional): Enter the position of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccination.
* **vaccination_administered_by_provider** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccination.
