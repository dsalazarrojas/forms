# morrison_clinic_new_patient_consent_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Morrison Clinic New Patient Consent Forms are designed to collect essential information from new patients, including their identification, contact details, and emergency contact information. This form helps us provide personalized care and ensures we can contact you or your emergency contacts in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient identification details, including your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email.
3. List your emergency contact details, including the name, phone number, and relationship with the contact person.
4. Specify the purpose of your contact with the clinic (e.g., appointment, treatment, or consultation).
5. Review and confirm the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Consent Date (2)**: Date you are consenting to provide your information.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Select the methods by which you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Address).
* **Emergency Contact Information (4)**: List the name, phone number, and relationship with your emergency contact person.
	+ **Emergency Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
	+ **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
	+ **Emergency Contact Relationship (8)**: Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, or friend).
* **Contact Purpose (5)**: Briefly describe the reason you are visiting the clinic or need to be contacted.
* **Emergency Contact Address (9)**: Enter your emergency contact person's address (if applicable).
