# Hospice Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect patient feedback and satisfaction data from hospice care services, allowing us to evaluate the quality of care provided and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "What is your contact number" field.
3. Provide the name of your care provider in the "Care provider name" field.
4. Rate your overall care experience in the "Overall rating" field by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
5. Select your satisfaction level from 1 to 5 in the "Satisfaction level" field.
6. Add any comments about your care experience in the "Comments" field.
7. Provide the date of your contact with us in the "Contact date" field.
8. Select the time of your contact with us in the "Time of contact" field.
9. Enter the name of the staff nurse who provided your care in the "Staff nurse name" field.
10. Enter the contact information for the staff nurse in the "Staff nurse contact" field.
11. Select whether a physician visited you in the "Visited physician" field.
12. If a physician visited you, enter the date of the visit in the "Visit date" field.
13. If a physician visited you, select the time of the visit in the "Visit time" field.
14. Enter the name of the care coordinator who provided your care in the "Care coordinator name" field.
15. Enter the contact information for the care coordinator in the "Care coordinator contact" field.
16. Select whether a care coordinator visited you in the "Visited care coordinator" field.
17. If a care coordinator visited you, enter the date of the visit in the "Care Coordinator Visit Date" field.
18. If a care coordinator visited you, select the time of the visit in the "Care Coordinator Visit Time" field.
19. Enter the name of the social worker who provided your care in the "Social worker name" field.
20. Enter the contact information for the social worker in the "Social worker contact" field.
21. Select whether a social worker visited you in the "Visited social worker" field.
22. If a social worker visited you, enter the date of the visit in the "Social Worker Visit Date" field.
23. If a social worker visited you, select the time of the visit in the "Social Worker Visit Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (text, required): Please enter your name as a patient to identify yourself.
* **What is your contact number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Care provider name** (text, optional): Please enter the name of your care provider who provided your care.
* **Overall rating** (number, optional): Rate your care experience from 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction level** (select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with your care experience from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any comments about your care experience.
* **Contact date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your contact with us.
* **Time of contact** (time, optional): Select the time of your contact with us.
* **Staff nurse name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the staff nurse who provided your care.
* **Staff nurse contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the staff nurse.
* **Visited physician** (select_one, optional): Select if a physician visited you.
* **Visit date** (date, optional): If a physician visited you, enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit time** (time, optional): If a physician visited you, select the time of the visit.
* **Physician name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the physician who visited you.
* **Physician contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the physician.
* **Visited care coordinator** (select_one, optional): Select if a care coordinator visited you.
* **Care Coordinator Visit Date** (date, optional): If a care coordinator visited you, enter the date of the visit.
* **Care Coordinator Visit Time** (time, optional): If a care coordinator visited you, select the time of the visit.
* **Care coordinator name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the care coordinator who provided your care.
* **Care coordinator contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the care coordinator.
* **Visited social worker** (select_one, optional): Select if a social worker visited you.
* **Social Worker Visit Date** (date, optional): If a social worker visited you, enter the date of the visit.
* **Social Worker Visit Time** (time, optional): If a social worker visited you, select the time of the visit.
* **Social worker name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the social worker who provided your care.
* **Social worker contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the social worker.
