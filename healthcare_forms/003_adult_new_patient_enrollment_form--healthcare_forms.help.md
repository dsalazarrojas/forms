# Adult New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult New Patient Enrollment Form is a crucial part of the patient onboarding process. It collects demographic data, contact information, and medical history to create a comprehensive patient profile. This information is vital for our healthcare professionals to provide personalized care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Patient Full Name** in the first field. Please enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date field. Please ensure the date format is in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3.  Select your **Gender Identity** from the drop-down menu provided. Please choose the option that best aligns with your identity.
4.  Enter your **Home Address** in the text field provided. Please include your current residential address.
5.  Enter your **City, State, ZIP Code** in the text field provided. Please include your city, state, and ZIP code.
6.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field provided. Please ensure it is your primary contact number.
7.  Enter your **Email Address** in the email field provided. Please use a valid email that we can use for communication.
8.  Enter the **Name of Your Emergency Contact** in the text field provided.
9.  Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone Number** in the text field provided.
10.  If you have a **Insurance Provider**, please enter the name of your health insurance company in the text field.
11.  If you have a **Policy Number**, please enter it in the text field provided.
12.  Enter the **Name of Your Current Primary Physician** in the text field provided.
13.  If you have any **Known Medical Conditions**, please list them in the text field provided.
14.  If you are currently taking any **Medications**, please list them in the text field provided.
15.  If you have any **Allergies**, please list them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Please ensure the date format is in MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (3, select_one, required): Choose your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Home Address** (4, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (5, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address** (7, email, required): Use a valid email that we can use for communication.
* **Emergency Contact** (8, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (9, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider** (10, text, optional): If you have a health insurance company, please enter its name.
* **Policy Number** (11, text, optional): If you have a policy number, please enter it.
* **Primary Physician** (12, text, optional): If you have a current primary care physician, please enter their name.
* **Known Medical Conditions** (13, text, optional): If you have any chronic conditions or diseases, please list them.
* **Medications Currently Taking** (14, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please list them.
* **Allergies** (15, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them.

Tips:

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Please use a valid email address for our communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
