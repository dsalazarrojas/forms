# New Patient Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information about new patients, including demographic data and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go to the designated section of the application and select the "New Patient Questionnaire" form. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form. Enter the required information in each field as prompted and click "Submit" once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name <small>(Text)</small>
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.

### Last Name <small>(Text)</small>
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.

### Date of Birth <small>(Date)</small>
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### Address <small>(Text)</small>
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.

### Phone <small>(Text)</small>
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.

### Email <small>(Email)</small>
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.

### Emergency Contact Name <small>(Text)</small>
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the patient's emergency contact.

### Relationship <small>(Select One)</small>
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the patient and their emergency contact (e.g. Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Other).

### Medical History <small>(Text)</small>
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history or allergies.

### Medication List <small>(Text)</small>
* **Medication List**: Enter a list of any medications the patient is currently taking, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid errors.
* If unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or medical professional for guidance.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Be aware of patient confidentiality and HIPAA guidelines when collecting and sharing patient data.
