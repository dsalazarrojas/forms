# Dental Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect patient information and exam details for a dental clinic or office.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the practice name and patient information (name, address, and age).
2. If necessary, enter the doctor's name and the exam date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Name** (1): The name of the dental practice or clinic where the patient visit takes place.
* **Patient Name** (2): The name of the patient being examined.
* **Patient Address** (3): The patient's address, which may be required for billing or correspondence purposes.
* **Doctor Name** (4): The name of the doctor or dentist performing the exam (optional).
* **Exam Date** (5): The date of the examination (optional).
* **Exam Time** (6): The time of the examination (optional).
* **Patient Age** (7): The age of the patient, which may be required for medical records.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If a field is optional, only fill it in if necessary.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the practice staff or patient records.
