# SAA Dermal Filler Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather the necessary information for a dermal filler treatment. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the treatment is completed successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page of the form to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.
2.  Complete the form in its entirety before submitting it for review.
3.  Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form on behalf of the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Address** (`address`, text): Enter the patient's address as they would like it to be recorded.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Provide the patient's contact phone number for follow-up care.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one): Select the medical condition that the dermal filler is intended to address.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, select_multiple): Select the medical procedures that will be performed during the treatment.
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date): Enter the date on which the patient provided their consent for the treatment.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Select the tool that will be used for the treatment (e.g., chatjimmy).
*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one): Select whether the treatment will be performed by a user or an automated system.
*   **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date): Enter the date on which the treatment will be performed.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the treatment.
*   **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text): Enter the name of the medical facility where the treatment will be performed.
*   **Medical Specialist** (`medical_specialist`, text): Enter the name of the medical specialist performing the treatment.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text): Sign the form with your name and date as the patient.
*   **Medical Signature** (`medical_signature`, text): Sign the form with your name and date as the medical professional performing the treatment.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date): Enter the date on which you signed the form as the patient.
*   **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time): Enter the time on which you signed the form as the patient.
*   **Medical Cert** (`medical_cert`, text): Enter your medical certification number or license number.
*   **Cert Date** (`cert_date`, date): Enter the date on which your medical certification was granted or renewed.
*   **Cert Time** (`cert_time`, time): Enter the time on which your medical certification was granted or renewed.
*   **Consent Version** (`consent_version`, select_one): Select the version of the consent form that was used.
*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text): Enter your name as the medical professional performing the treatment.
*   **User Title** (`user_title`, text): Enter your title as the medical professional performing the treatment.
*   **User Organization** (`user_organization`, text): Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.

Please note that this guide assumes that the user has the necessary permissions to complete this form on behalf of the patient. If there are any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the medical facility or healthcare professional for further guidance.

Please ensure that you have reviewed this guide carefully before proceeding with the form completion.
