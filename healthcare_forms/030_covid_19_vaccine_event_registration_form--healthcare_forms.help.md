# COVID 19 Vaccine Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from attendees at a COVID-19 vaccine event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. In the "Event Information" section, enter the name of the event you are attending.
5. Select the date of the event from the calendar.
6. Choose the time slot you prefer for the event.
7. Enter the location of the event.
8. In the "Vaccination Status" section, select the reason for attending from the drop-down menu.
9. Choose the type of vaccine you are interested in from the drop-down menu.
10. In the "Demographics" section, select your age group from the drop-down menu.
11. Select your race or ethnicity from the drop-down menu.
12. Choose your language preference from the drop-down menu.
13. In the "Health Information" section, select "True" if you have any chronic health conditions, and select "False" otherwise.
14. If you are currently pregnant, select "True" if you are, "False" otherwise, or select "Not applicable" if you are not applicable.
15. In the "Accessibility" section, select "True" if you need any special accommodations, and select "False" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive event confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Reason for Attending**: Select the reason for attending from the drop-down menu.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Choose the type of vaccine you are interested in from the drop-down menu.
* **Number of People in Your Group**: Enter the number of people in your group attending the event.
* **Additional Attendee Names**: Enter the names of anyone else attending with you.
* **Reason for Attending**: Select the reason for attending from the drop-down menu.
* **Any Chronic Health Conditions**: Select "True" if you have any chronic health conditions, and select "False" otherwise.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the drop-down menu.
* **Race or Ethnicity**: Select your race or ethnicity from the drop-down menu.
* **Language Preference**: Choose your language preference for materials from the drop-down menu.
* **Chronic Health Conditions**: Select "True" if you have any chronic health conditions, and select "False" otherwise.
* **Pregnancy Status**: Select "True" if you are currently pregnant, "False" otherwise, or select "Not applicable" if you are not applicable.
* **Vaccine Allergies or Sensitivities**: Enter any vaccine allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Transportation Needs**: Select "True" if you need transportation assistance, and select "False" otherwise.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Consent**: Select "I agree" if you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine at this event, and select "I do not agree" otherwise.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the individual who is attending the event. If you are registering for multiple people, please fill in their information below as well.
