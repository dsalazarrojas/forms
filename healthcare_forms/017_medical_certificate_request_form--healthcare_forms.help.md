# Medical Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Certificate Request Form is used to request medical certificates for patients. This form will help healthcare providers collect and document necessary information for medical certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Medical Certificate Request" page.
2. Enter the healthcare provider's name in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
3. Choose the type of medical certificate needed in the "Certificate Type" field.
4. Explain the reason for the request in the "Reason for Request" field.
5. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, contact number, and email address.
6. Select the type of medical certification form needed in the "Medical Certification Form" field.
7. Specify the medical specialist's name in the "Medical Specialist" field.
8. Enter the medical certification number.
9. Upload any relevant files or documents.
10. Review and submit or save the form for later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Certificate Request**:
	+ This is the main page of the form.
	+ Enter a brief description of the certificate request.
- **Healthcare Provider**:
	+ Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
- **Certificate Type**:
	+ Select one of the available certificate types (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Reason for Request**:
	+ Explain why the certificate is being requested.
- **Patient Name**:
	+ Enter the patient's full name.
- **Date of Birth**:
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth.
- **Contact Number**:
	+ Enter the patient's contact number.
- **Email**:
	+ Enter the patient's email address.
- **Medical Certification Form**:
	+ Select one of the available certification forms (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Medical Specialist**:
	+ Enter the name of the medical specialist providing the certification.
- **Medical Certification Number**:
	+ Enter the certification number.
- **Upload Certificate**:
	+ Upload any relevant files or documents.
- **Signature of Doctor**:
	+ Enter the signature of the doctor providing the certification.
- **Certificate Number**:
	+ Enter the certificate number.
- **Submit**:
	+ Review and submit the form for processing.
- **Cancel**:
	+ Cancel the form and start over.
- **Save and Send**:
	+ Save the form and send it for processing.
- **Save**:
	+ Save the form for later.
- **Back**:
	+ Return to the previous page.
- **Reset**:
	+ Reset the form to its original state.
- **Upload File**:
	+ Upload any relevant files or documents.
- **Uploaded File**:
	+ View any uploaded files.
- **Medical Doctor**:
	+ Enter the name of the medical doctor providing the certification.
