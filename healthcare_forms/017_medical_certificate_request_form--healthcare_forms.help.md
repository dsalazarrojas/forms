# Medical Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Certificate Request Form is used to request medical certificates for patients. This form will help healthcare providers collect and document necessary information for medical certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Medical Certificate Request" page.
2. Enter the healthcare provider's name in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
3. Choose the type of medical certificate needed in the "Certificate Type" field.
4. Explain the reason for the request in the "Reason for Request" field.
5. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, contact number, and email address.
6. Select the type of medical certification form needed in the "Medical Certification Form" field.
7. Specify the medical specialist's name in the "Medical Specialist" field.
8. Enter the medical certification number.
9. Upload any relevant files or documents.
10. Review and submit or save the form for later.
