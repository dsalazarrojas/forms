# New Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Intake Form is designed to collect comprehensive information from new clients to ensure they receive proper care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including name, contact information, and medical history.
2. Select your insurance provider and doctor's name.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including the contact person's name, phone number, and email.
4. Indicate your medical supervision status and reason, if applicable.
5. Select your preferred language and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client Name** (`new_client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Client Contact Information** (`client_contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **New Client Phone** (`new_client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **New Client Email** (`new_client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **New Client Address** (`new_client_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any health conditions or allergies.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, required): Select your insurance provider from the available options.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, select_multiple, not required): Select the doctor's name from the available options.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe your medical condition or reason for seeking care.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, note, not required): Enter a message to the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Supervision** (`medical_supervision`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you require medical supervision.
* **Medical Supervision Reason** (`medical_supervision_reason`, text, not required): Provide a reason for medical supervision if applicable.
* **Medical Supervision Notes** (`medical_supervision_notes`, text, not required): Enter additional comments about medical supervision.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Medical Supervision Agreement** (`medical_supervision_agreement`, text, required): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the medical supervision agreement.
* **New Client Agreement** (`new_client_agreement`, text, required): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the new client agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact our staff for assistance.
