# Certified Phlebotomy Training - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certified Phlebotomy Training form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to enroll in the Certified Phlebotomy Technician program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your personal details (applicant name, date of birth, contact email, and phone).
3. Select your highest level of completed education (high school, associate degree, bachelor degree, or other).
4. Check your health clearance status (provided, pending, or not required).
5. Indicate your availability for hands-on sessions.
6. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number (optional).
7. Read and agree to the program policies and exam inclusion terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): This field is your full legal name.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This field is needed for certification records.
* **contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is your main communication channel for the program.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for urgent notifications.
* **education level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest completed education level from the options provided.
* **health clearance** (`health_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Check your health clearance status from the options provided.
* **availability for hands-on sessions** (`availability`, `text`, required): Indicate your days or times available for practical labs.
* **emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **program acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree to the program policies and exam inclusion terms.
* **program acknowledgement note** (`section_overview`, `note`, not required): This is a general note about the program.
