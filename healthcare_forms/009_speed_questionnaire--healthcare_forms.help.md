# Speed Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed Questionnaire is designed to collect information about a patient's visual symptoms and their impact on daily life.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's basic information, including their name and ID number (if applicable).
2.  **Visual Symptoms** (`visual_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing, such as dry eye, blurred vision, flashes, and eye pain.
3.  **Visual Acuity** (`visual_acuity`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the level of visual acuity the patient is experiencing, with options ranging from 20/20 to 20/100.
4.  **Tear Reflex** (`tear_reflex`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the patient's tear reflex, which can be Normal, Diminished, or Absent.
5.  **Tear Breakup** (`tear_breakup`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the presence or absence of tear breakup, with options None, Minimal, or Present.
6.  **Photocephalos** (`photocephalos`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the presence or absence of photocephalos, which can be None, Minimal, or Present.
7.  **Tear Distribution** (`tear_distribution`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the distribution of the patient's tears, which can be None, Minimal, or Present.
8.  **Tear Quality** (`tear_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the quality of the patient's tears, which can be None, Good, or Poor.
9.  **Tear Stability** (`tear_stability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the stability of the patient's tears, which can be None, Stable, or Unstable.
10. **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the patient's outcome, which can be None, Favorable, Favorable with limitations, or Unfavorable.
