# Immunization Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather and document immunization details for patients, facilitating accurate and up-to-date health records.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering the patient's basic information, including their name and date of birth (patient_name and patient_dob fields).
2.  Select the type of immunization administered (vaccination_type field).
3.  Record the date the vaccination was given (vaccination_date field).
4.  Specify the doctor who administered the immunization (doctor_name and doctor_contact fields).
5.  Enter any relevant notes about the vaccination procedure or the patient's response (vaccination_notes field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name.
*   **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date the vaccination was administered.
*   **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of immunization given. Options include Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, MMR, MMR2, MMRV, Pneumococcal, Rota, and Varicella.
*   **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate the status of the vaccination. Choose from Not Completed, Completed, and Partially Completed.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
*   **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter the healthcare provider's contact information.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Re-enter the patient's name for confirmation.
*   **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, Date, Optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address.
*   **Vaccination Notes** (`vaccination_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any relevant notes about the vaccination process or patient response.

## Tips

*   Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and record this patient's information.
*   If unsure about any field, consult with the healthcare team or patient's medical record.
*   This form is for internal use and should not be shared with the patient without consent.
*   Regularly review and update the patient's immunization record to reflect any changes.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact [contact email or phone number].
