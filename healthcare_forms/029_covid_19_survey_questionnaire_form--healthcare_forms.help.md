# Covid 19 Survey Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid-19 Survey Questionnaire Form is designed to collect information about your current health status, recent activities, and contact information during the pandemic.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the multiple-choice questions in each section.
2. Provide more information when prompted.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your current concerns?** (`covid_concerns`, `text`, `optional`): This field is used to collect information about your current concerns related to Covid-19, such as symptoms, health risks, or other pandemic-related concerns.
* **Do you have any of the following symptoms?** (`covid_symptoms`, `select_one`, `true`): This field is used to select one or more symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or sore throat.
* **Did you visit any public places recently?** (`contact_location`, `select_multiple`, `false`): This field is used to select one or more public places you have visited recently, such as restaurants, grocery stores, or schools.
* **Who you have been in contact with recently?** (`contact_people`, `select_multiple`, `true`): This field is used to select one or more people you have been in contact with recently, such as family members, friends, coworkers, or classmates.
* **Have you visited a healthcare provider recently?** (`healthcare_provider`, `select_one`, `false`): This field is used to select whether you have visited a healthcare provider recently.
* **What type of health care provider did you visit?** (`healthcare_type`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect more information about the healthcare provider you visited.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, `true`): This field is used to collect the date of your visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Time of Visit** (`visit_time`, `time`, `true`): This field is used to collect the time of your visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Please provide more information about contact** (`contact_other`, `text`, `false`): This field is used to collect more information about your contact.
* **Do you have any underlying medical conditions?** (`medical_condition`, `select_multiple`, `false`): This field is used to select one or more underlying medical conditions you have.
* **Are you currently taking any medication?** (`medication`, `select_one`, `false`): This field is used to select whether you are currently taking any medication.
* **Have you received any vaccines?** (`vaccine_status`, `select_one`, `true`): This field is used to select whether you have received any vaccines.
* **Have you traveled outside of the country recently?** (`travel_history`, `select_one`, `false`): This field is used to select whether you have traveled outside of the country recently.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect your country of residence.
* **State/Province** (`state`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect your state or province of residence.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect your city of residence.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, `true`): This field is used to collect your age.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `true`): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Name of Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the name of your medical facility.
* **Address of Medical Facility** (`medical_facility_address`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the address of your medical facility.
* **Phone number of Medical Facility** (`medical_facility_phone`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the phone number of your medical facility.
* **Email of Medical Facility** (`medical_facility_email`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the email of your medical facility.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, `true`): This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact person.
 
**Tips:**

* Please fill out all required fields carefully.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email].
