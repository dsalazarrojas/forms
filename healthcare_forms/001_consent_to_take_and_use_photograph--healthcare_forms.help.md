# Consent To Take And Use Photograph - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture consent from individuals to take and use photographs for specific purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name in the "Full name" field.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3.  Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
4.  Enter your contact phone number, including your country code, in the "Contact phone" field.
5.  Describe how the photographs will be used in the "Photo usage purpose" field.
6.  Select the duration for which the photographs may be used from the "Usage duration" options.
7.  Confirm your consent to the photograph usage by selecting "True" in the "Consent given" field.
8.  Enter the name of the authorized photographer or organization in the "Photographer name" field.
9.  Type your signature name in the "Participant signature name" field.
10. Enter the date when consent was provided in the "Consent date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Fill in your full legal name.
*   **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required): Use YYYY-MM-DD format to enter your date of birth.
*   **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up communication.
*   **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, required): Include your country code if you're outside your local area.
*   **Photo usage purpose** (Photo usage purpose, text, required): Briefly describe how the photographs will be used.
*   **Usage duration** (Usage duration, select one, required): Select the duration for which the photographs may be used.
    +   Options: 1 year, 2 years, Indefinite
*   **Consent given** (Consent given, select one, required): Confirm your consent to the photograph usage.
    +   Options: True, False
*   **Photographer name** (Photographer name, text, required): Enter the name of the authorized photographer or organization.
*   **Participant signature name** (Participant signature name, text, required): Type your signature name for confirmation.
*   **Consent date** (Consent date, date, required): Enter the date when consent was provided.
