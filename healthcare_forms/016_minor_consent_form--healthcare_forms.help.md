## Minor Consent Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to collect information regarding a minor's consent and their parent's consent to participate in medical treatments or procedures. It helps ensure that both the minor and their parent are aware and agree to the terms of the treatment.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the minor's consent is required for the treatment.
2. Provide a brief description of the massage or treatment in the "Describe Massage" field.
3. Select the type of consent given by the minor in the "Minor Consent" field.
4. Enter the minor's name and parent's name in their respective fields.
5. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the parent's consent is required for the treatment, or "No" if not.
* **Describe Massage** (`massage_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the massage or treatment, including any necessary details.
* **Minor Consent** (`minor_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" for each type of consent given by the minor, as applicable.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Minor Name** (`minor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the minor's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address.

### Tips

* Make sure to select "Yes" for all types of consent if the minor is able to give all consent.
* Ensure that the description of the massage is clear and concise.
* Enter the parent's email address correctly to ensure they receive the necessary information.
* If the minor is unable to give consent, select "No" for all types of consent.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the healthcare team for guidance.
