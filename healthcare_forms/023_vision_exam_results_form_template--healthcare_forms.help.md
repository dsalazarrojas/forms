# Vision Exam Results Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on vision exam results for patients undergoing eye exams. It captures essential information such as acuity scores, prescription details, and other relevant test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
4. Enter the patient's right eye acuity score in the "Right Eye Acuity Score" field.
5. Enter the patient's left eye acuity score in the "Left Eye Acuity Score" field.
6. Enter the patient's field of vision score in the "Field of Vision Score" field.
7. Enter the patient's prescription details in the "Prescription" field.
8. Enter any test results comments in the "Test Results Comments" field (optional).
9. Select the type of prescription from the "Prescription Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Spectacles, Contact Lenses, or Surgery).
10. Enter the prescription date and time in the "Prescription Date" and "Prescription Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (patient_first_name)**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name (patient_last_name)**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Age (patient_age)**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Right Eye Acuity Score (right_eye_acuity_score)**: Enter the patient's right eye acuity score.
* **Left Eye Acuity Score (left_eye_acuity_score)**: Enter the patient's left eye acuity score.
* **Field of Vision Score (field_of_vision_score)**: Enter the patient's field of vision score.
* **Prescription (prescription)**: Enter the patient's prescription details, such as glasses or contact lenses.
* **Test Results Comments (test_results_comments)**: Enter any additional comments about the test results (optional).
* **Prescription Type (prescription_type)**: Select the type of prescription from the dropdown menu (e.g., Spectacles, Contact Lenses, or Surgery).
* **Prescription Date (prescription_date)**: Enter the date of the prescription.
* **Prescription Time (prescription_time)**: Enter the time of the prescription.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Ensure that you have permission to collect and record this information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or consult with the patient's doctor or healthcare professional.
