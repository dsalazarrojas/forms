# 120th Annual Convention Attestation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 120th Annual Convention Attestation Form is a required document for attendees to confirm their attendance and provide information necessary for the event organizers to ensure a safe and healthy environment. This form is used to gather essential data on attendees' health, exposure, and vaccination status, as well as their contact information and convention attendance details.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidelines

*   Complete the form in full, ensuring all required fields are accurately filled.
*   For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
*   If you have any known allergies, medical history, or exposure, provide a brief summary.
*   Sign the attestation by typing your full name.

### Tips

*   Ensure you have the most recent COVID-19 test result before filling this form.
*   If you have any known exposure to confirmed cases, report it truthfully.
*   Provide accurate contact information for on-site contact persons.
*   Complete the form before the convention starts to ensure you can attend without any issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Affiliation / Employer**: Enter your current or former affiliation with a hospital, clinic, or organization.
*   **Date of birth**: Enter the date you were born (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Age**: Enter your current age.
*   **Phone number**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
*   **Email address**: Enter your work or personal email address (optional).
*   **State license / DO number**: Enter your state-issued license or DO number (if applicable).
*   **Current symptoms**: Select all symptoms you are currently experiencing (e.g., Fever, Headache, Fatigue, etc.).
*   **Pain level**: Rate your current pain level from 0 to 10.
*   **Current medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages.
*   **Known allergies**: List any known allergies you have.
*   **Relevant medical history**: Provide a brief summary of any chronic conditions or recent hospitalizations.
*   **COVID-19 vaccination status**: Select your current vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to say).
*   **Date of most recent COVID-19 test**: Leave blank if you have not been tested.
*   **Result of most recent COVID-19 test**: Select the result of your most recent test (Negative, Positive, Pending).
*   **Recent exposure**: Report any known exposure to confirmed cases in the last 14 days.
*   **Accommodation needs**: Select any accommodations required for the event (e.g., Wheelchair accessible seating, Dietary restrictions, etc.).
*   **Convention days attending**: Select the days you will attend the convention.
*   **On-site contact person**: Provide the name and phone number of your on-site contact person (if available).
*   **I confirm**: Select "Confirm" to attest that the information provided is accurate and you have no known exposures or active symptoms inconsistent with event policy.
*   **Typed signature (Full name)**: Type your full name to agree to the attestation.
*   **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any additional comments or information you wish to share.
