# COVID 19 Immunization Screening And Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Immunization Screening And Consent Form is designed to collect critical information about a patient's medical history, current health status, and consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine. This information is essential for healthcare professionals to assess the patient's suitability for vaccination and ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's medical record number (MRN) in the "Medical Record Number" field (if applicable).
4. Enter the patient's contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Answer the screening questions, including:
	* Allergic reaction to vaccine components
	* Current fever
	* Acute illness or infection
	* Describe any illness or infection (if applicable)
7. Provide information about the patient's medication and medical history:
	* Taking antiviral or immunosuppressive medications
	* List any medications being taken
	* Bleeding or blood clotting disorder
8. Answer questions about the patient's pregnancy status:
	* Pregnant status
	* Expected delivery date (if applicable)
	* Nursing status
9. Provide consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine:
	* Understand the benefits of the vaccine
	* Understand potential side effects and risks
	* Consent to receive the vaccine

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Full Name
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name, as it will be used throughout their medical history.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Medical Record Number
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's MRN if applicable. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's contact phone number for follow-up and communication.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address for follow-up and communication.

### Allergic Reaction to Vaccine Components
* **Allergic reaction to vaccine components**: Answer "True" if the patient has an allergic reaction to vaccine components, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Type of Allergic Reaction
* **Type of allergic reaction**: If the patient has an allergic reaction, describe the type of reaction in this field.

### Current Fever
* **Do you currently have a fever**: Answer "True" if the patient has a fever, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Temperature if Applicable
* **Temperature if applicable**: If the patient has a fever, enter the temperature reading.

### Acute Illness or Infection
* **Currently have acute illness or infection**: Answer "True" if the patient has an acute illness or infection, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Describe Illness if Applicable
* **Describe illness if applicable**: If the patient has an acute illness or infection, describe the illness or infection in this field.

### Taking Antiviral or Immunosuppressive Medications
* **Taking antiviral or immunosuppressive medications**: Answer "True" if the patient is taking antiviral or immunosuppressive medications, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### List Medications
* **List medications**: If the patient is taking antiviral or immunosuppressive medications, list the medications in this field.

### Bleeding or Blood Clotting Disorder
* **Have bleeding or blood clotting disorder**: Answer "True" if the patient has a bleeding or blood clotting disorder, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Immunocompromised
* **Are you immunocompromised**: Answer "True" if the patient is immunocompromised, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Prior COVID-19 Infection Confirmed
* **Prior COVID-19 infection confirmed**: Answer "True" if the patient has a confirmed prior COVID-19 infection, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if uncertain.

### Date of Prior Infection if Applicable
* **Date of prior infection if applicable**: If the patient has a confirmed prior COVID-19 infection, enter the date of the infection.

### Pregnancy Status
* **Are you pregnant**: Answer "True" if the patient is pregnant, "False" if not, "Unsure" if uncertain, or "N/A" if not applicable.

### Expected Delivery Date if Applicable
* **Expected delivery date if applicable**: If the patient is pregnant, enter the expected delivery date.

### Nursing
* **Are you nursing**: Answer "True" if the patient is nursing, "False" if not.

### Consent to Vaccine
* **I understand benefits of COVID-19 vaccine**: Answer "True" if the patient understands the benefits of the vaccine, "False" if not.
* **I understand potential side effects and risks**: Answer "True" if the patient understands potential side effects and risks, "False" if not.
* **I consent to receive COVID-19 vaccine**: Answer "I Consent" if the patient consents to receive the vaccine, "I Do Not Consent" if not.
* **Date of Consent**: If the patient consents to receive the vaccine, enter the date of consent.

Note: This form is designed for healthcare professionals to assess a patient's suitability for vaccination and ensure their safety.
