# stress_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stress survey is designed to help healthcare providers and researchers understand stress patterns and coping strategies among individuals. The survey collects information on stress levels, triggers, and coping mechanisms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply for "Stress Level" question.
2. Choose a frequency for "How often do you feel stressed?"
3. Choose a duration for "How long do you feel stressed?"
4. Rate your stress level on a scale of 1-10.
5. Select all that apply for "What triggers your stress?"
6. Choose a single coping mechanism for "What do you do to cope with stress?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stress Level** (Very stressed, Somewhat stressed, Not stressed): This question helps us understand your current stress level. Please select all that apply.
* **How often do you feel stressed?** (Always, Mostly, Rarely): This question asks you to describe how often you feel stressed. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
* **How long do you feel stressed?** (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-8 hours, More than 8 hours): This question measures the duration of your stress episodes. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
* **Stress Level (1-10)**: This question asks you to rate your stress level on a scale of 1-10. Be honest, and rate your stress level as it feels to you at the moment.
* **What triggers your stress?** (Work/School, Relationships, Health, Money, Hobbies/Leisure): This question helps us understand what causes your stress. Select all that apply.
* **What do you do to cope with stress?** (Exercise, Talk to a friend, Listen to music, Meditate, Watch a movie, Read a book, Play a game, Other): This question asks you to describe your coping mechanisms. Choose a single option that best describes how you handle stress.
