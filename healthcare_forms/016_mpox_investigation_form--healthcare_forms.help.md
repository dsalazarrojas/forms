# Mpox Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Investigation Form is designed to collect and track information about Mpox cases and contacts to aid in the investigation and management of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the Case Id, which is the unique identifier for the case.
3. Enter the Case Date, which is the date the case was reported.
4. Enter the Case Gender and Case Age, which are demographic details about the case.
5. Enter the Patient Gender and Patient Age, which are demographic details about the patient.
6. Enter the Patient Phone number, which is the patient's contact information.
7. Select the Case Status, which is the current status of the case.
8. Enter the Investigation Date, which is the date the investigation was started.
9. Enter the Investigation Location, which is where the investigation took place.
10. Enter the Investigator Name, which is the name of the person conducting the investigation.
11. Select whether Contact Tracing is Complete.
12. Enter any additional Case Notes or Case Photo information.
13. Enter any additional Contact Notes or Contact Photo information.
14. Select the Exposure Risk, which is the risk of exposure to the disease.
15. Select the Case Symptoms and Case Diagnosis.
16. Select the Case Treatment.
17. Select the Case Outcome.
18. Enter any additional Contact Symptoms information.
19. Enter any additional Contact Notes information.
20. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Id** (1, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the case, which is used to track and manage the case information.
* **Case Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date the case was reported.
* **Case Reported** (3, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the case being reported.
* **Case Gender** (4, select_one, required): Select the gender of the case.
* **Case Age** (5, number, optional): Enter the age of the case.
* **Case Contact Location** (6, text, optional): Enter the location where the case was in contact with others.
* **Patient Gender** (7, select_one, required): Select the gender of the patient.
* **Patient Age** (8, number, optional): Enter the age of the patient.
* **Patient Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Case Status** (10, select_one, required): Select the current status of the case.
* **Investigation Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date the investigation was started.
* **Investigation Location** (12, text, optional): Enter the location where the investigation took place.
* **Investigator Name** (13, text, optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the investigation.
* **Contact Tracing Complete** (14, select_one, required): Select whether contact tracing is complete.
* **Case Note** (15, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the case.
* **Case Photo** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select whether a photo of the case is available.
* **Contact Photo** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select whether a photo of the contact is available.
* **Exposure Risk** (18, select_one, required): Select the risk of exposure to the disease.
* **Case Symptoms** (19, text, optional): Enter any additional symptoms of the case.
* **Case Diagnosis** (20, select_one, required): Select the diagnosis of the case.
* **Case Treatment** (21, select_one, optional): Select whether the case received treatment.
* **Case Outcome** (22, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the case.
* **Contact Symptoms** (23, text, optional): Enter any additional symptoms of the contact.
* **Contact Note** (24, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the contact.
* **Submitted By** (25, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

Note that the required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) and must be completed before submitting the form.
