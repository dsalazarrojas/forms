# Responsive Life Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and data necessary to provide life insurance quotes. It is intended for users who wish to obtain life insurance quotes and is an optional, non-mandatory form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your relevant information and click the 'Submit' button at the end. You can choose not to fill out any fields you are not comfortable providing, as all fields except the 'Submit' button are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information**: This section is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.

### Life Insurance
* **Life Insurance**: In this field, you can enter any additional information about your life insurance needs or requirements.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This field is for you to provide your contact details, such as phone number or email.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please enter them here.

### Policy Details
* **Policy Details**: Describe your policy needs and requirements in this field.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Information**: If you have any payment preferences or details, enter them here.

### Submit
* **Submit**: This is the final step to complete the form. You must select 'Yes' to submit your quote request.

### Quote Information
* **Quote Information**: This field is not currently used in the form and is for internal purposes only.

### User Agreement
* **User Agreement**: This field is not used in the form and is for internal purposes only.

### Quote Amount
* **Quote Amount**: This field is not used in the form and is for internal purposes only.

Note: The form fields marked as 'for internal purposes only' are not actually used in the form, so they can be removed from the help guide if you'd like.
