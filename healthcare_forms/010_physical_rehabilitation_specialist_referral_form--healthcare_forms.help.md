# Physical Rehabilitation Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to a physical rehabilitation specialist for further treatment. It provides a clear and concise referral that includes patient contact information, medical history, and rehabilitation goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Provide the patient's contact phone number and healthcare provider's contact information.
3. Describe the patient's condition and rehabilitation goal.
4. Select the type of physical rehabilitation specialist the patient should be referred to (Physical Medicine Specialist, Occupational Therapist, or Physical Therapist).
5. Add any additional notes about the patient's treatment plan or progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is referring the patient.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical condition that requires physical rehabilitation.
* **Rehabilitation Goal** (`rehabilitation_goal`, text, required): Specify the patient's rehabilitation goal, such as regaining mobility or strength.
* **Physical Rehabilitation Specialist Referred** (`physical_rehabilitation_specialist_referred`, select_one, optional): Select the type of physical rehabilitation specialist the patient should be referred to (Physical Medicine Specialist, Occupational Therapist, or Physical Therapist).
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, such as their email or address.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact** (`healthcare_provider_contact`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's contact information, such as their email or address.
* **Date Of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's last visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Physical Rehabilitation Specialist Note** (`physical_rehabilitation_specialist_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the patient's treatment plan or progress.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Be as specific as possible when describing the patient's condition and rehabilitation goal.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the patient's healthcare provider or the physical rehabilitation specialist.
