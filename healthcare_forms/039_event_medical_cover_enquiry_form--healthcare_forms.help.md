# event_medical_cover_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_medical_cover_enquiry_form is designed to collect information about an event's medical cover needs. This form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to record or update event-specific medical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information about your event.
2. Provide details about the event's medical cover needs, such as whether general or specific cover is required.
3. Specify the event's cover type (Basic, Comprehensive, or Enhanced).
4. Enter the event's name and date.
5. Choose the time of the event.
6. Provide the location of the event.
7. List the event's participants.
8. Choose the type of medical cover request (General or Specific).
9. Update the status of the medical cover request (New, In-progress, or Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event details** (Event details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event medical cover details** (Event medical cover details, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether general or specific medical cover is required for the event.
* **Event medical cover type** (Event medical cover type, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical cover required for the event (Basic, Comprehensive, or Enhanced).
* **Event name** (Event name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event date** (Event date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time** (Event time, time, optional): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event location** (Event location, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event organizer** (Event organizer, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event participants** (Event participants, text, optional): Enter a list of participants attending the event.
* **Event medical cover request type** (Event medical cover request type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of medical cover request (General or Specific).
* **Event medical cover request status** (Event medical cover request status, select_one, optional): Update the status of the medical cover request (New, In-progress, or Completed).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate information to ensure proper medical cover arrangements can be made.
* If you're unsure about any field, please seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in your event's medical cover needs.
