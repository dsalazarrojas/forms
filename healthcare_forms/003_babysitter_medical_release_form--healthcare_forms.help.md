# Babysitter Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect crucial medical information and authorization from parents or guardians for the care of their child while they are in the care of a babysitter. The form is meant to ensure that the babysitter has the necessary medical clearance and emergency contact information to provide proper care for the child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
2. **Provide the correct contact information** for the parent or guardian, as this will be used in case of an emergency.
3. **Answer the medical questions honestly and thoroughly**, as this will help the babysitter understand any potential medical needs of the child.
4. **Sign and date the form** to confirm authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, text, **required**): Please enter the child's full name, including any middle name or surname.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Known Allergies** (`child_allergies`, text, **optional**): List any known allergies the child may have, such as food, medication, or environmental allergies.
* **Medical Conditions or Special Needs** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): List any medical conditions or special needs the child may have, such as asthma or diabetes.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, **optional**): List the names and dosages of any current medications the child is taking.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Primary Contact Number** (`parent_phone`, phone, **required**): Enter the primary contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Secondary Contact Number** (`secondary_contact_phone`, phone, **optional**): Enter a secondary contact number for the parent or guardian (e.g. a secondary caregiver or another trusted contact).
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Child's Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the child's physician's name.
* **Physician Phone Number** (`physician_phone`, phone, **optional**): Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, **optional**): Enter the child's insurance provider (e.g. Blue Cross, Medicaid, etc.).
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, **optional**): Enter the child's insurance policy number.
* **Special Emergency Procedures or Treatments** (`emergency_procedures`, text, **optional**): List any special emergency procedures or treatments that may be needed for the child.
* **Preferred Hospital or Medical Facility** (`hospital_preference`, text, **optional**): List the preferred hospital or medical facility in case of an emergency.
* **Do You Authorize Emergency Medical Treatment?** (`emergency_medical_auth`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" to authorize emergency medical treatment for the child.
* **May the Babysitter Administer Medications?** (`babysitter_medication_auth`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" if the babysitter is authorized to administer medications to the child.
* **Date of Authorization** (`parent_signature_date`, date, **required**): Sign and date the form to confirm authorization.
