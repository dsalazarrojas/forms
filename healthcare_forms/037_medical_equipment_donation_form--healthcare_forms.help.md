# Medical Equipment Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Equipment Donation Form is used to collect details about donated medical equipment for processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Donation Item** field with the name of the medical equipment being donated.
2. Enter the **Quantity** of items being donated.
3. Indicate the **Condition** of the donated item (New, Used, or Refurbished).
4. Enter the **Serial Number** of the donated item, if applicable.
5. Enter the **Model Year** of the donated item.
6. Enter the **Manufacturer**'s name and other relevant details.
7. Provide a detailed **Description** of the donated item and its current condition.
8. Enter your **Contact** information so that the donation can be verified and processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Item** (`Donation Item`, text, required): This field is meant to describe the item being donated.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of items being donated.
* **Condition** (`Condition`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the donated item is new, used, or refurbished.
* **Serial Number** (`Serial Number`, text, required): If the item has a serial number, please enter it here.
* **Model Year** (`Model Year`, number, required): Enter the model year of the donated item.
* **Manufacturer** (`Manufacturer`, text, required): Enter the name of the item's manufacturer.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the donated item and its current condition.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information so that the donation can be verified and processed correctly.
