# First Aid Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Aid Quiz form is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of first aid techniques and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a response for each question based on your knowledge and experience.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
3. Review your responses to ensure you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Training to Become** (First Aid Responder, Healthcare Professional, Other)
	* Select the category that best describes your training to become a first aid responder.
### 2. **Healthcare Professional** (text)
	* Enter your professional title or role in healthcare.
### 3. **Provide First Aid** (note)
	* Briefly describe a situation where you provided first aid.
### 4. **First Aids to Provide** (CPR, Bleeding, Burns, Other)
	* Select all first aid procedures you are trained to provide.
### 5. **CPR Training** (number)
	* Enter the number of hours or days of CPR training you have completed.
### 6. **Bleeding Training** (number)
	* Enter the number of hours or days of bleeding training you have completed.
### 7. **Burn Training** (number)
	* Enter the number of hours or days of burn training you have completed.
### 8. **First Aid Certification** (Certified First Aid Responder, In Progress, None)
	* Select your first aid certification status.
### 9. **Quiz Yourself** (True, False)
	* Select whether you have completed a previous first aid quiz or training session.
### 10. **Provide to Others** (note)
	* Briefly describe how you provide first aid to others.
### 11. **First Aids to Provide Others** (CPR, Bleeding, Burns, Other)
	* Select all first aid procedures you provide to others.
### 12. **Other First Aids** (text)
	* Enter any additional first aid procedures you have learned or are trained in.
