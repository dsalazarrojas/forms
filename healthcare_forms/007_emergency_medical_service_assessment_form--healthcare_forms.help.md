# emergency-medical-service-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency-medical-service-assessment-form is a tool for healthcare providers to quickly and accurately assess patient conditions in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the patient's information and any relevant details about their medical condition.
2. Answer the select_multiple questions for Medical Conditions, Allergies, and Medications.
3. Enter any relevant information in the text fields for Medical History, Vital Signs, and Emergency Contact Info.
4. Select one option for Medical Service Provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (Patient Info): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions): Check the boxes for any medical conditions the patient has, such as Yes or No.
* **Allergies** (Allergies): Check the boxes for any allergies the patient has, such as Yes or No.
* **Medications** (Medications): Check the boxes for any medications the patient is taking, such as Yes or No.
* **Medical History** (Medical History): Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical history, such as previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Vital Signs** (Vital Signs): Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.
* **Medical Service Provided** (Medical Service Provided): Select one option for the type of medical service provided, such as Yes or No.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info): Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact, including name, relationship, and phone number.
* **Patient Assessment Form** (Patient Assessment Form): Enter any additional information or comments about the patient's assessment.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling out the form, as this information will be used to inform treatment decisions.
* If the patient has multiple medical conditions, check all relevant boxes.
* If the patient has multiple allergies or medications, check all relevant boxes.
* If the patient's condition is critical, please contact the emergency contact immediately.
* If you are unsure about any information, please enter "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" in the relevant field.
