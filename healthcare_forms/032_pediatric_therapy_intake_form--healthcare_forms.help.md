# Pediatric Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect important information from a child's family or caregiver to ensure effective and personalized care in a pediatric therapy setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information.
3. Double-check the form for any errors or missing fields before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <label> Child Information (<name> child_info, <type> text, required: <false>)

Enter basic information about the child, such as their name, date of birth, and contact information.

### 2. <label> Medical History (<name> medical_history, <type> text, required: <false>)

Describe the child's medical history, including any past illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions that may impact their therapy.

### 3. <label> Family Information (<name> family_info, <type> text, required: <false>)

Provide information about the child's family, including their parents' or caregivers' names, contact numbers, and any other relevant details.

### 4. <label> Medical Conditions (<name> medical_conditions, <type> text, required: <false>)

List any known medical conditions that the child has, such as ADHD, autism, or other developmental delays.

### 5. <label> Therapy Goals (<name> therapy_goals, <type> text, required: <false>)

Describe the child's goals and expectations for therapy, including any specific skills or areas they want to improve.

### 6. <label> Emergency Contact (<name> emergency_contact, <type> text, required: <false>)

Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in case of an urgent situation.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when providing information about the child's medical history and conditions.
- Include any relevant medical records or documentation if possible.
- If unsure about any information, consult with the healthcare professional before submitting the form.
