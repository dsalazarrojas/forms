# mental_health_support_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental_health_support_check_out_form is a feedback form used to measure the satisfaction of patients with mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select your rating for the overall experience with the mental health services.
3. Answer the questions about your satisfaction with the services and what you liked most about them.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
5. Indicate if you would like to be contacted for a follow-up.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (`rating_scale`, Select One, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the mental health services on a scale of 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How satisfied are you with the therapist?** (`question_1`, Select Multiple, required): Choose one or more options that apply to your satisfaction with the therapist, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Question 1** (`question_1_value`, Text, optional): Provide a detailed answer to how you feel about the therapist.
* **What do you like most about our services?** (`question_2`, Text, optional): Share your positive feedback about the mental health services you received.
* **Is there anything we could have done differently?** (`question_3`, Text, optional): Share any constructive criticism or areas for improvement you think could benefit the services.
* **Would you like to be contacted for a follow-up?** (`question_4`, Select One, required): Indicate if you would like to be contacted for further support or discussion.
* **How can we improve our services?** (`question_5`, Text, optional): Provide suggestions on how the mental health services can be improved.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the mental health services.
