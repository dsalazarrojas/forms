# dandelionz_intake_form_2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a client's training history, contact information, medical conditions, and other relevant details for a holistic practitioner's session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the provided fields with the requested information. Each section is designed to gather specific details to help the practitioner better understand the client's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Info**: This field is for you to provide information about your past training and experience in holistic practices.
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you to schedule a session or follow up on any questions or concerns.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you may be experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Medical History**: Share your medical history, including any surgeries, hospitalizations, or significant medical events.
* **Emotional States**: Describe your current emotional states, such as anxiety, depression, or stress levels.
* **Spiritual States**: Discuss your spiritual states, including any beliefs or practices that influence your well-being.
* **Session Goals**: Define your goals for the upcoming session, including what you hope to achieve or work through.
* **Medical Emo-Spiritual Background**: Share any relevant medical or spiritual background information that may impact your session.
* **Holistic Practitioner Background**: Provide information about your experience and training as a holistic practitioner.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form to ensure the practitioner has a clear understanding of your needs.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for in-person consultations or professional medical advice. If you have any urgent medical concerns, please seek help from a qualified medical professional.
