# Medical Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients and healthcare providers for referral purposes. It helps in streamlining the process by ensuring accurate and comprehensive data is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
2. **Select relevant options**: When prompted, select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus.
3. **Enter relevant details**: Type in the required information for fields like email, phone, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter the patient's full name.

### 2. **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, number, required)
Enter the patient's unique ID number.

### 3. **Referring Doctor** (`referring_doctor`, select_one, required)
Select the name of the doctor referring the patient.

### 4. **Specialist** (`specialist`, select_one, required)
Choose the specialist the patient needs to see.

### 5. **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date of the referral.

### 6. **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, optional)
Enter the time of the referral.

### 7. **Referral Note** (`referral_note`, note, optional)
Add any additional notes about the referral.

### 8. **Email** (`email`, email, required)
Enter the patient's email address.

### 9. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
Enter the patient's phone number.

### 10. **Address** (`address`, text, required)
Enter the patient's address.

### 11. **City** (`city`, text, required)
Enter the patient's city.

### 12. **State** (`state`, text, required)
Enter the patient's state.

### 13. **Zip** (`zip`, number, required)
Enter the patient's zip code.

### 14. **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional)
Enter the patient's fax number (if applicable).

### 15. **Specialty** (`specialty`, text, required)
Enter the specialist the patient needs to see (if not already selected).

### 16. **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required)
Enter the patient's age.

### 17. **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required)
Select the patient's gender.

### 18. **Other (Specify)** (`patient_gender_other`, text, optional)
Enter any other gender option not listed.

### 19. **Patient Birthdate** (`patient_birthdate`, date, required)
Enter the patient's birthdate.

### 20. **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required)
Enter the patient's medical condition.

### 21. **Patient Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, text, required)
Enter the patient's medical history.

### 22. **Specialist Comments** (`specialist_comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the specialist.

### 23. **Doctor Comments** (`doctor_comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments from the doctor.

### 24. **Referral Status** (`referral_status`, select_one, required)
Select the status of the referral.

### 25. **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the person who created this form.

By following this guide, users will be able to accurately and efficiently complete the "Medical Referral Form", ensuring that the necessary information is captured for smooth referral processing.
