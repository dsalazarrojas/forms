# Corona Virus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corona Virus Self Declaration Form is a simple, text-based form designed to collect information about an individual's personal details, travel history, and potential exposure risk related to the corona virus. This form is intended for data collection and is not a substitute for medical consultations or expert evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Person Information" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. If you are experiencing any symptoms related to the corona virus, describe them in the "Symptoms" section.
4. List your recent travel history in the "Travel History" section.
5. Describe your potential exposure risk in the "Exposure Risk" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Person Information
• **Person Information** (optional): Enter your name, age, and any other relevant personal details.

### Contact Information
• **Contact Information** (optional): Enter your address, phone number, or any other contact information you're comfortable sharing.

### Symptoms
• **Symptoms** (optional): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or difficulty breathing. If you're not experiencing any symptoms, simply leave this field blank.

### Travel History
• **Travel History** (optional): List your recent travel destinations, dates, and places you've visited.

### Exposure Risk
• **Exposure Risk** (optional): Describe any potential exposure risk you may have had, such as close contact with an infected person or visiting a high-risk area. 

# Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing information about your symptoms, travel history, and exposure risk.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of this form, do not hesitate to contact the form's administrator for clarification.
* Remember that the information you provide will be treated as anonymous and non-sensitive, and it will be used for research, public health purposes, or other legitimate uses as specified by the form's administrator.
