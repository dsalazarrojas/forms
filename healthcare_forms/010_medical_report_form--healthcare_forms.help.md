# Medical Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Report Form is designed to collect important medical information for patients. This form ensures that patients receive accurate and timely medical care by providing healthcare professionals with a comprehensive overview of a patient's medical history, diagnosis, and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2.  Provide the patient's date of birth (DOB) in the **Patient DOB** field.
3.  Select the **Patient Contact Info** field's options that apply to the patient.
4.  Choose the **Medical Specialty** field's option that best describes the patient's medical concern.
5.  Enter the patient's medical **Diagnosis** and **Treatment Plan** in their respective fields.
6.  Provide any additional **Medical Comments** if needed.
7.  Select the **Medical Prescription** field's options that apply to the patient's treatment.
8.  Sign the **Physician Signature** field to confirm that you have reviewed the patient's medical report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
*   **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Patient Contact Info**: Select all contact methods that apply to the patient, such as phone, email, or address.
*   **Medical Specialty**: Choose the medical specialty that best describes the patient's concern, such as cardiologist or dermatologist.
*   **Medical Diagnosis**: Enter the patient's medical diagnosis.
*   **Treatment Plan**: Outline the patient's treatment plan.
*   **Medical Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the patient's medical history.
*   **Medical Prescription**: Select all medications or treatments that apply to the patient's treatment.
*   **Physician Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed the patient's medical report.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you complete all required fields.
*   Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
