# Flu Vaccine Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flu Vaccine Screening Form is designed to collect and track patient information, including vaccination status, medical history, and consent for treatment. This form is intended to aid healthcare providers in making informed decisions about patient care and to provide a systematic way to collect and store patient data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
2.  Use the provided hints and guidance to ensure accurate and complete answers.
3.  Double-check your work before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, *): Enter your name as it is spelled on your identification documents. This field is required to identify you as a patient.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, ): This field should include your phone number or other contact method where you can be reached.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Note, ): This field should include any relevant information about your medical history, including any chronic conditions or allergies.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, ): This field should be your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Flu Shot Status** (`flu_syrup_status`, Select One, ): Choose Active or Inactive to indicate your current vaccination status.
* **Flu Vaccine Status** (`flu_vaccine_status`, Select Multiple, ): Choose Active or Inactive to indicate your current vaccination status. Please select all that apply.
* **Vaccination History** (`vaccination_history`, Text, ): This field should include any vaccination records or history you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, ): This field should include any medical conditions you have or are being treated for.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, ): This field should include any allergies you have or are being treated for.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, Text, ): This field should include any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, Note, ): This field should include any additional notes or information about your medical history.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, Select One, ): Choose Yes or No to indicate your consent for treatment.
* **Flu Shot Vaccination Status** (`flu_shot_vaccination_status`, Select Multiple, ): Choose Active or Inactive to indicate your current vaccination status.
* **Flu Shot Vaccination Date** (`flu_shot_vaccination_date`, Date, ): This field should be the date you were vaccinated.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, Date, ): This field should be the date you were vaccinated.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select One, ): Choose Active or Inactive to indicate your current vaccination status.
* **Flu Vaccine Screening Form Comments** (`flu_vaccine_screening_form_comments`, Text, ): This field should include any comments or notes about your screening form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Text, ): This field should include your signature, indicating that you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, Text, ): This field should include your healthcare provider's signature, indicating that they have reviewed and verified the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, Date, ): This field should be the date the form was submitted.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, ): This field should be the date the form was signed by your healthcare provider.
* **Time Submitter** (`time_submitted`, Time, ): This field should be the time the form was submitted.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, Time, ): This field should be the time the form was signed by your healthcare provider.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Text, ): This field should include the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, Text, ): This field should include the name of your healthcare provider.

# Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Double-check your work before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
