# Virtual Critical Incident Response Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for collecting information related to virtual critical incidents. It is designed to gather details about the incident, including the employee's information, incident details, and coping mechanisms used.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page, "Employee Information".
2. On this page, you will find fields for collecting basic contact information about the employee who experienced the virtual critical incident.
3. Please fill out all the required fields (if any) and select the correct options from the dropdown menus (if any).
4. Move to the next page, "Incident Information", where you will find fields for collecting details about the incident.
5. Continue filling out the required fields and selecting options as needed.
6. Repeat this process for each page, "Coping Information", "Trauma Information", and "Services Information".
7. When you have filled out all the pages, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information
* **Employee Name** (`employee_info`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_info`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number or other unique identifier.
* **Contact Information** (`employee_info`, text, required): Provide a contact method, such as email or phone number, where you can be reached for further questions or support.

### Incident Information
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred, including online platforms or systems involved.
* **Incident Date/Time** (`incident_date_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time Period** (`incident_time_period`, time, required): Enter the duration of the incident.

### Coping Information
* **Coping Mechanisms Used** (`coping_info`, text, required): Describe any coping mechanisms or strategies you used to deal with the incident.

### Trauma Information
* **Trauma Experienced** (`trauma_info`, text, required): Describe any trauma experienced as a result of the incident.

### Services Information
* **Services Used** (`services_info`, text, required): Describe any services used to address the incident, such as counseling or support groups.
* **Services Information 2** (`services_info_2`, text, required): Describe any additional services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 3** (`services_info_3`, text, required): Describe any further services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 4** (`services_info_4`, text, optional): Describe any other services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 5** (`services_info_5`, text, optional): Describe any additional services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 6** (`services_info_6`, text, optional): Describe further services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 7** (`services_info_7`, text, required): Describe any services used to address the incident.
* **Services Information 8** (`services_info_8`, text, required): Describe any further services used to address the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location of the services used to address the incident.

Note: This is a very long form, and some fields might not be necessary for your specific use case. Please only fill out the required fields.
