# gswo_health_information_and_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "gswo_health_information_and_release_form" is used to collect and document essential health information for the individual, including their parent/guardian's contact details, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully review the form and ensure you don't duplicate any previously collected information.
2. Complete the form for each individual, providing the necessary contact information and medical details.
3. If you're unsure about any information, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: <parent_name> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the individual's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone**: <parent_phone> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the phone number of the individual's parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Consent**: <guardian_consent> (<type: select_one, required: false)
    Indicate whether the individual's parent or guardian has given consent for treatment.
* **Medical Condition**: <medical_condition> (<type: text, required: false)
    Describe the individual's medical condition, if applicable.
* **Medication Allergies**: <medication_allergies> (<type: text, required: false)
    List any known medication allergies.
* **Medical History**: <medical_history> (<type: text, required: false)
    Describe the individual's medical history, if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions List**: <medical_conditions_list> (<type: select_multiple, required: false)
    Indicate whether the individual has any of the following medical conditions: Yes, No, Other, please specify, Not Applicable.
* **Other Medical Conditions**: <other> (<type: text, required: false)
    If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief description.
* **Medical Provider**: <medical_provider> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the individual's medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone**: <medical_provider_phone> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the phone number of the individual's medical provider.
* **Health Insurance Info**: <health_insurance_info> (<type: text, required: false)
    Describe the individual's health insurance information.
* **Health Insurance Number**: <health_insurance_number> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the individual's health insurance number.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: <health_insurance_provider> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the individual's health insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: <emergency_contact_name> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the individual's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: <emergency_contact_phone> (<type: text, required: false)
    Enter the phone number of the individual's emergency contact.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- If unsure about any information, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- Ensure to provide up-to-date information.
