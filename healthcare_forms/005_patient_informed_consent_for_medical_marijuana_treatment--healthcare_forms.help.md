# Patient Informed Consent For Medical Marijuana Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for medical marijuana treatment. It collects patient identification information, medical condition, risks, and benefits of use, as well as other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including name and date of birth.
2. Select your medical condition that you are seeking treatment for.
3. List any risks associated with the use of medical marijuana.
4. List any benefits you expect to receive from the treatment.
5. Provide your signature and date.
6. Enter any additional contact information.
7. Enter the date and time of signature.
8. Confirm your consent type (informed or authorization to treat).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (<id= 1>)**: Please enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Name (<id= 2>)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **Date of Birth (<id= 3>)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information (<id= 4>)**: Enter any additional contact information you would like to provide, such as phone number or email.
* **Medical Condition (<id= 5>)**: Select the medical condition you are seeking treatment for (e.g. Chronic Pain, Anxiety, Depression, etc.).
* **Doctor Recommendation (<id= 6>)**: Enter your doctor's contact information, if applicable.
* **Risks of Use (<id= 7>)**: Select any risks you are aware of associated with the use of medical marijuana (e.g. Addiction, Cognitive Impairment, Cardiovascular Impairment, etc.).
* **Benefits of Use (<id= 8>)**: Select any benefits you expect to receive from the treatment (e.g. Reduced Inflammation, Improved Mood, Pain Relief, etc.).
* **Signature (<id= 9>)**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understand the form.
* **Date (<id= 10>)**: Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Time (<id= 11>)**: Enter the time you are signing the form.
* **Printed By (<id= 12>)**: Enter the name of the person printing this form.
* **Date Signed (<id= 13>)**: Enter the date this form was signed.
* **Goals (<id= 14>)**: Enter any specific goals or expectations you have for the treatment.
* **Signer (<id= 15>)**: Enter your name as it appears on the form.
* **Signature Date (<id= 16>)**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Printed By 2 (<id= 17>)**: Enter the name of the person printing this form (if different from above).
* **Date Signed 2 (<id= 18>)**: Enter the date this form was printed (if different from above).
* **Consent Type (<id= 19>)**: Select the type of consent you are providing (informed or authorization to treat).
* **ID (<id= 20>)**: Enter a unique identifier for your records.
* **Name 2 (<id= 21>)**: Enter your name as it appears on the form (if different from above).
* **Date Of Birth 2 (<id= 22>)**: Enter your date of birth (if different from above).
* **Contact Information 2 (<id= 23>)**: Enter any additional contact information (if different from above).
* **Medical Condition 2 (<id= 24>)**: Select your medical condition (if different from above).
* **Risks Of Use 2 (<id= 25>)**: Select any risks you are aware of (if different from above).
