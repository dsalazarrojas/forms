# Daily Clinical Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily Clinical Evaluation Form is a medical form designed to collect information from healthcare professionals on the clinical status of patients. This form is essential for evaluating the patients' conditions, medication administration, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each section.
2.  Ensure you fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Condition** (`personal_condition`, select_one, *required*): Please select the current patient's medical condition using the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, select_multiple, *optional*): Choose the relevant patient information such as Patient Name, Patient Age, Patient Gender, or Patient Weight.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, *optional*): Select the current medical condition of the patient using the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, select_one, *optional*): Indicate if medication was administered to the patient by selecting True or False.
*   **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, *optional*): If medication was administered, provide the name of the medication.
*   **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, number, *optional*): If medication was administered, specify the frequency of administration (e.g., number of times per day).
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, *optional*): Provide any relevant medical history information about the patient.
*   **Pre-Existing Medical History** (`medical_history_pre`, text, *optional*): Record any pre-existing medical history related to the patient's current condition.
*   **Post-Medication History** (`medical_history_post`, text, *optional*): Document any post-medication history related to the patient's current condition.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all necessary sections accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check the options selected for each field to ensure they are correct.
*   If unsure about any section, please seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
*   Keep in mind that this form is for daily clinical evaluation, so it's essential to be as detailed and accurate as possible.
