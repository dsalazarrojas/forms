# Chiropractic Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Exam Form is designed to gather patient information for examination and diagnosis purposes. It helps healthcare professionals like chiropractors to collect necessary details in a standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the patient's information. Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:

1. **Patient Information**: Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, age, and other essential details.
2. **Medical History**: Answer questions about previous medical conditions, medications, and treatments received.
3. **Chief Complaint**: Describe the reason for the visit and your primary complaint.
4. **Symptoms and Findings**: Provide information about your symptoms, pain levels, and any factors that make them worse or better.
5. **Additional Information**: Answer questions about your lifestyle, habits, and previous treatments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`id: 2`, `label: Patient First Name`): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`id: 3`, `label: Patient Last Name`): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 4`, `label: Date of Birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`id: 5`, `label: Age`): Enter the patient's age in numerical format.
* **Gender** (`id: 6`, `label: Gender`): Select the patient's gender from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`id: 7`, `label: Occupation`): Enter the patient's current occupation.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 8`, `label: Phone Number`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`id: 9`, `label: Email Address`): Enter the patient's email address (optional).
* **Address** (`id: 10`, `label: Address`): Enter the patient's address, including street, city, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`id: 11`, `label: Emergency Contact Name and Phone`): Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Chief Complaint** (`id: 12`, `label: Chief Complaint`): Describe the main reason for the visit.
* **What is your primary complaint** (`id: 13`, `label: What is your primary complaint`): Describe your main issue in detail.
* **Pain Location** (`id: 14`, `label: Where is your pain or discomfort`): Select all the areas where you experience pain or discomfort.
* **Pain Level** (`id: 15`, `label: Current Pain Level`): Rate your current pain level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Pain Scale Reference** (`id: 16`, `label: Pain Scale Reference`): This is a reference for understanding the pain level scale.
* **When did symptoms begin** (`id: 17`, `label: When did symptoms begin`): Describe when your symptoms started.
* **What caused your symptoms** (`id: 18`, `label: What caused your symptoms`): Select the cause of your symptoms.
* **Describe Your Symptoms** (`id: 19`, `label: Describe Your Symptoms`): Describe your symptoms in detail.
* **What makes symptoms worse** (`id: 20`, `label: What makes symptoms worse`): List any factors that make your symptoms worse.
* **What makes symptoms better** (`id: 21`, `label: What makes symptoms better`): List any factors that make your symptoms better.
* **Have you received treatment for this before** (`id: 22`, `label: Have you received treatment for this before`): Select the type of treatment you have received.
* **Medical History** (`id: 23`, `label: Medical History`): Provide any relevant medical background.
* **Current Medications** (`id: 24`, `label: Current Medications`): List all your current medications.
* **Do you have any of the following conditions** (`id: 25`, `label: Do you have any of the following conditions`): Select all relevant medical conditions.
* **Previous Surgeries** (`id: 26`, `label: Previous Surgeries`): List any previous surgeries and dates.
* **Relevant Family Medical History** (`id: 27`, `label: Relevant Family Medical History`): List any relevant family medical history.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (`id: 28`, `label: Lifestyle Factors`): Provide any lifestyle information.
* **How often do you exercise** (`id: 29`, `label: How often do you exercise`): Select your exercise frequency.
* **Is your occupation physically demanding** (`id: 30`, `label: Is your occupation physically demanding`): Select the level of physical demand in your occupation.
* **How is your sleep quality** (`id: 31`, `label: How is your sleep quality`): Select the quality of your sleep.
* **Current Stress Level** (`id: 32`, `label: Current Stress Level`): Select your current stress level.
* **Do you smoke or use tobacco** (`id: 33`, `label: Do you smoke or use tobacco`): Select your smoking status.

* **Examination Findings** (`id: 35`, `label: Examination Findings`): This is for clinical use.
* **Posture Assessment** (`id: 36`, `label: Posture Assessment`): Select the assessment of your posture.
* **Range of Motion** (`id: 37`, `label: Range of Motion`): Select the range of motion in your joints.
* **Neurological Findings** (`id: 38`, `label: Neurological Findings`): List any neurological findings.
* **Orthopedic Test Results** (`id: 39`, `label: Orthopedic Test Results`): List any orthopedic test results.

* **Recommended Visit Frequency** (`id: 44`, `label: Recommended Visit Frequency`): Select the recommended visit frequency.

* **Doctor Signature** (`id: 46`, `label: Doctor Signature`): Sign the form with your official signature.
* **Date of Examination** (`id: 47`, `label: Date of Examination`): Enter the date of the examination in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
