# Travel Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Risk Assessment Form is used by healthcare professionals to evaluate a traveller's risk level when embarking on a journey. This form is designed to collect relevant information about the traveller's medical history, travel history, and other factors that may impact their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by providing your name and contact information if you are a healthcare professional.
* Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, taking into account your specific travel plans and medical history.
* If you are unsure about any question or have a medical condition that may not be listed, please contact your healthcare provider or travel agent for guidance.
* Once you have completed the form, review it for accuracy and completeness before submitting it to your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Risk Assessment Form** (`id: 1`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should not be completed by the traveller. It is for reference only.
* **Traveller Medical History** (`id: 2`, text, optional): Please describe your medical history, including any pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns that may impact your travel.
* **Travel History** (`id: 3`, text, optional): Describe your recent and upcoming travel plans, including destinations, modes of transportation, and accommodation arrangements.
* **Medical Condition** (`id: 4`, text, optional): If you have a pre-existing medical condition that may impact your travel, please describe it in detail.
* **Medication** (`id: 5`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them and their dosage amounts.
* **Travel Documents** (`id: 6`, text, optional): Provide copies of any relevant travel documents, such as vaccination records or travel insurance policies.
* **Travel Insurance** (`id: 7`, text, optional): If you have travel insurance, please provide information about your policy, including policy numbers and expiration dates.
* **Medical Accommodations** (`id: 8`, select_one): Choose one of the following options to indicate your medical accommodation needs:
	+ No Special Accommodations
	+ Accommodations at destination
	+ Accommodations at source
* **Client Consent** (`id: 9`, note, optional): This field is for the healthcare professional's use only, and is intended to confirm that the traveller has provided informed consent for the assessment. Please do not complete this field unless instructed to do so by the healthcare professional.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and thorough when completing this form, as the data collected will be used to assess your risk level when traveling.
* If you are unsure about any question or have a medical condition that may not be listed, please contact your healthcare provider or travel agent for guidance.
* This form is not a substitute for medical advice or a medical consultation. If you are concerned about your health or travel risks, please speak with a healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
