# Patient Ownership Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Ownership Assessment Form is used to assess the ownership and access rights of health records. This form helps medical staff to determine who has control over patient records and to document any medical decisions made regarding the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the patient records are owned by the patient or under the control of a healthcare provider.
2. Answer the medical decision questions to document any decisions made regarding the patient.
3. List all health records you have access to.
4. Indicate whether you have access to these records.
5. Specify where the records are stored.
6. Confirm ownership of the records.
7. Enter the patient's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Ownership Form** (`patient_ownership_form`, Note, Optional): This field is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the ownership of the patient records.
* **Medical Decision** (`medical_decision_form`, Select One, Optional): This field asks if you have made any medical decisions regarding the patient. Choose 'Yes' if you have made a decision.
* **Medical Decisions** (`medical_decision_list`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to list all medical decisions you have made regarding the patient. Choose all applicable options.
* **Health Records** (`health_records_form`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field asks you to list all health records you have access to. Choose all applicable options.
* **Records Access** (`records_access_form`, Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you have access to the listed health records. Choose 'Yes' if you have access.
* **Record Location** (`record_location_form`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the patient records are stored.
* **Ownership** (`ownership_form`, Note, Optional): This field is for you to confirm the ownership of the patient records.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider_form`, Select One, Optional): This field asks if you are a healthcare provider. Choose 'Yes' if you are a healthcare provider.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name_form`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name.
