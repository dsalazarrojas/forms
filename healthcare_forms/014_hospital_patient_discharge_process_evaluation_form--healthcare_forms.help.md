# Hospital Patient Discharge Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hospital Patient Discharge Process Evaluation Form is a tool to gather feedback from patients about their experience during their hospital stay, focusing on the discharge process and various aspects of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, providing specific examples or incidents that highlight your experiences.
2. Use the options provided for each question, or type in your own words if none of the options fit your experience.
3. Be thorough in your answers, providing detailed explanations for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discharge Process Evaluation** (`discharge_process_evaluation`): Describe your overall experience with the discharge process, including any challenges or difficulties you faced.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`): Rate your overall experience with our hospital, using the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication with Staff** (`communication_with_staff`): Describe the effectiveness of communication between staff members, such as clarity, responsiveness, and timeliness.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`): Share any difficulties or confusion you experienced with discharge instructions, including any misunderstandings or miscommunications.
* **Hospital Environment** (`hospital_environment`): Describe the cleanliness, comfort, and amenities of the hospital environment.
* **Nurse to Patient Ratio** (`nurse_to_patient_ratio`): Rate the nurse-to-patient ratio during your stay, using the number input field.
* **Patient Friendly Hours** (`patient_friendly_hours`): Rate the patient-friendly hours offered by the hospital, using the options: 8-12 hours, 0-7 hours, 7-16 hours, 16-24 hours.
* **Pain Management** (`pain_management`): Share any concerns or difficulties you experienced with pain management, including any medication side effects or ineffectiveness.
* **Discharge Location** (`discharge_location`): Describe the location where you were discharged, including any challenges or difficulties you faced.
* **Patient Care Team** (`patient_care_team`): Rate the effectiveness of the patient care team, using the options: Too Small, Just Right, Too Large.
* **Hospital Cleanliness** (`hospital_cleanliness`): Describe the cleanliness and maintenance of the hospital environment.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`): This field is not editable, it is displayed for reference only.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`): Share any concerns or difficulties you experienced with pain management, including any medication side effects or ineffectiveness.
* **Staff Attentiveness** (`staff_attentiveness`): Rate the attentiveness of the hospital staff, using the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Patient Flow** (`patient_flow`): Share any challenges or difficulties you experienced with the flow of care, including any delays or miscommunications.
* **Nurse Punctuality** (`nurse_punctuality`): Rate the punctuality of the nursing staff, using the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Hospital Clarity** (`hospital_clarity`): Rate the clarity of hospital information and instructions, using the options: Very Clear, Good, Fair, Very Confused, Confused.
