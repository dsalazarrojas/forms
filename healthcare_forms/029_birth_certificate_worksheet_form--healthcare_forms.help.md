# Birth Certificate Worksheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Birth Certificate Worksheet Form is a legal document that gathers information about a newborn's birth details. This form is used to create an official birth certificate, which is essential for various purposes, such as government records, insurance, and identification documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the infant's first name, middle name (if applicable), and last name.
2. Provide the infant's date and time of birth, including the hour and minute.
3. Choose the infant's sex.
4. Enter the infant's weight and length at birth.
5. If applicable, enter the Apgar score.
6. Fill in the mother's first name, middle name (if applicable), last name, date of birth, age at birth, social security number (last 4 digits), and address.
7. If applicable, fill in the father's information, including name, date of birth, age at birth, and social security number (last 4 digits).
8. Provide the name and address of the birth facility.
9. Choose the method of delivery.
10. Enter the name and title of the attending provider.
11. Enter the name and title of the certifying official.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Infant First Name** (id: 4, label: Infant First Name): Enter the infant's first name.
* **Infant Middle Name** (id: 5, label: Infant Middle Name): Enter the infant's middle name (if applicable).
* **Infant Last Name** (id: 6, label: Infant Last Name): Enter the infant's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 7, label: Date of Birth): Enter the infant's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (id: 8, label: Time of Birth): Enter the hour and minute of the infant's birth.
* **Sex** (id: 9, label: Sex): Choose the sex of the infant (Male or Female).
* **Weight at Birth** (id: 10, label: Weight at Birth): Enter the infant's weight at birth in pounds and ounces or grams.
* **Length at Birth** (id: 11, label: Length at Birth): Enter the infant's length at birth in inches or centimeters.
* **Apgar Score** (id: 12, label: Apgar Score): If recorded, enter the Apgar score.
* **Mother First Name** (id: 14, label: Mother First Name): Enter the mother's first name.
* **Mother Middle Name** (id: 15, label: Mother Middle Name): Enter the mother's middle name (if applicable).
* **Mother Last Name** (id: 16, label: Mother Last Name): Enter the mother's last name.
* **Mother Date of Birth** (id: 18, label: Mother Date of Birth): Enter the mother's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Mother Age at Birth** (id: 19, label: Mother Age at Birth): Enter the mother's age at birth.
* **Mother Social Security Number** (id: 20, label: Mother Social Security Number): Enter the last 4 digits of the mother's social security number.
* **Mother Address** (id: 21, label: Mother Address): Enter the mother's current residence.
* **Mother City** (id: 22, label: Mother City): Enter the city of the mother's residence.
* **Mother State** (id: 23, label: Mother State): Enter the state of the mother's residence.
* **Is Father's Name Being Included?** (id: 25, label: Is Father's Name Being Included): Choose if the father's name is included in the birth certificate.
* **Father First Name** (id: 26, label: Father First Name): Enter the father's first name (if applicable).
* **Father Middle Name** (id: 27, label: Father Middle Name): Enter the father's middle name (if applicable).
* **Father Last Name** (id: 28, label: Father Last Name): Enter the father's last name (if applicable).
* **Father Date of Birth** (id: 29, label: Father Date of Birth): Enter the father's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Father Age at Birth** (id: 30, label: Father Age at Birth): Enter the father's age at birth (if applicable).
* **Father Social Security Number** (id: 31, label: Father Social Security Number): Enter the last 4 digits of the father's social security number (if applicable).
* **Father Address** (id: 32, label: Father Address): Enter the father's current residence (if applicable).
* **Facility Name or Hospital** (id: 34, label: Facility Name or Hospital): Enter the name of the birthplace.
* **Facility City** (id: 35, label: Facility City): Enter the city of the birthplace.
* **Facility State** (id: 36, label: Facility State): Enter the state of the birthplace.
* **Method of Delivery** (id: 37, label: Method of Delivery): Choose the method of delivery (Vaginal, Cesarean Section, Vacuum Assisted, Forceps Assisted, or Other).
* **Attending Provider Name** (id: 38, label: Attending Provider Name): Enter the name of the attending provider.
* **Certifying Official Name** (id: 40, label: Certifying Official Name): Enter the name of the certifying official.
* **Certifying Official Title** (id: 41, label: Certifying Official Title): Enter the title of the certifying official.
* **Date of Certification** (id: 42, label: Date of Certification): Enter the date the form is certified.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If applicable, enter all necessary information about the father and mother.
* Double-check the fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or guidance.
