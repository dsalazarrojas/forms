# medication_disposal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication_disposal_form is designed to help healthcare professionals safely and accurately dispose of medications in a way that follows regulatory requirements and organizational policies. This form provides a structured format for recording key information about medication disposal events, ensuring consistency and completeness of data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out **medication_disposal_form_part_1** (text): Enter the date of the medication disposal event.
2. Provide the **medication_disposal_form_part_2** (number): Enter the quantity of medication disposed of.
3. Answer **medication_disposal_form_part_3** (select_multiple): Indicate whether the medication was hazardous, non-hazardous, or unknown.
4. Enter **medication_disposal_form_part_4** (time): Record the time the medication disposal occurred.
5. Answer **medication_disposal_form_part_5** (select_one): Confirm that the medication was disposed of properly.
6. Enter **medication_disposal_form_part_6** (text): Note any additional comments or information about the disposal event.
7. Enter **medication_disposal_form_part_7** (email): Provide a contact email for follow-up or additional information.
8. Select **medication_disposal_form_part_8** (date): Record the date of the medication disposal event.
9. Enter **medication_disposal_form_part_9** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about the disposal event.
10. Fill in the remaining text fields with any additional information relevant to the disposal event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **medication_disposal_form_part_1** (text): Enter the date of the medication disposal event.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_2** (number): Enter the quantity of medication disposed of.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_3** (select_multiple): Indicate whether the medication was hazardous, non-hazardous, or unknown.
  - Choose from the following options: Yes, No, Unknown.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_4** (time): Record the time the medication disposal occurred.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_5** (select_one): Confirm that the medication was disposed of properly.
  - Choose from the following options: Yes, No.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_6** (text): Note any additional comments or information about the disposal event.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_7** (email): Provide a contact email for follow-up or additional information.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_8** (date): Record the date of the medication disposal event.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_9** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about the disposal event.
* **medication_disposal_form_part_10** to **medication_disposal_form_part_23** (text): Fill in with any additional information relevant to the disposal event.

Please note that this form has multiple pages with 23 fields, and the provided instructions are for the first page. You can refer to the fields listed above for further instructions on each field.
