# Hospital Discharge Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Discharge Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback and insights from patients about their experience during their hospital stay and post-discharge care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Complete one page at a time, if necessary.
4. Submit the completed form to the relevant hospital staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): Provide a brief statement about your overall experience during your hospital stay. This could include any positive or negative comments, suggestions, or concerns.
* **Experience Of Care** (`experience_of_care`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the care provided by hospital staff, including any interactions with doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals.
* **Discharge Procedure** (`discharge_procedure`, text, optional): Share any details about the discharge process, including any concerns or issues you had during the process.
* **Hospital Staff** (`hospital_staff`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the hospital staff's attitude, responsiveness, and quality of care provided.
* **Discharge Process** (`discharge_process`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the discharge process, including any challenges or difficulties you faced.
* **Care Plan** (`care_plan`, text, optional): Share any information about your care plan, including any instructions or advice provided by hospital staff.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, text, optional): Describe any discharge instructions you received, including any questions or concerns you had about them.
* **Follow Up Care** (`follow_up_care`, text, optional): Share any information about your follow-up care, including any appointments or medication reminders.
* **Hospital Environment** (`hospital_environment`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the hospital environment, including cleanliness, comfort, and amenities.
* **Discharge Location** (`discharge_location`, text, optional): Share the location where you were discharged from (e.g., hospital room, emergency department, etc.).
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your discharge, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask hospital staff.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the care we provide.
