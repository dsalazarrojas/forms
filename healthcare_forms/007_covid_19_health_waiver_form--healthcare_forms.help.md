# COVID 19 Health Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals to express their interest in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine and provide necessary health information for vaccine administration. It is a required form for anyone seeking to receive the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the instructions carefully.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Patient Name): Enter your full name, including any middle names or suffixes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-01-01).
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: If you have an ID or medical record number, enter it here. This helps us to identify you in our system.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for vaccine appointment reminders or updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for vaccine appointment reminders or updates.
* **Are you interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine?** (Select one of the options):
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, maybe
	+ No, not at this time
	+ Unsure
* **What is the primary reason for your decision?**: Enter a brief explanation for your decision.
* **Do you have allergies to vaccine components?** (Select one of the options):
	+ No known allergies
	+ Allergies to eggs
	+ Allergies to gelatin
	+ Other allergies
* **Please describe your allergies**: Enter a brief description of any allergies you have.
* **Do you have any chronic health conditions?** (Select all that apply):
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart disease
	+ Lung disease
	+ Kidney disease
	+ Liver disease
	+ Cancer
	+ Immunocompromised
	+ None
* **Current medications**: Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?** (Select one of the options):
	+ Not applicable
	+ False
	+ Yes, pregnant
	+ Yes, breastfeeding
* **I have read and received information about the COVID-19 vaccine**: Select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **I understand the potential benefits and risks**: Select one of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **I waive any liability and accept responsibility for my health decision**: Select one of the options:
	+ Accept
	+ Do not accept
* **Date of Waiver**: Enter the date you are signing this waiver.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should be reviewed and approved by a healthcare professional before use.
