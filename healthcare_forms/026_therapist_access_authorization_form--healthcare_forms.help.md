# Therapist Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Access Authorization Form is a document used to grant access to a client's medical information or treatment team by an authorized individual. It's an essential tool to ensure the client's information is secure and only accessible to those with legitimate access rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
2.  Provide the client's birth date in the `Birth Date` field.
3.  Enter the authorized individual's name in the `Therapist Name` field.
4.  Enter the authorized individual's email address in the `Email` field.
5.  Input the authorized individual's phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
6.  Select the authorization type (e.g., "Authorization for treatment team", "Authorization for billing", or "Authorization for insurance") from the `Authorization Type` dropdown.
7.  Choose a date for the consent to be valid from the `Consent Date` field.
8.  Enter any additional comments or reasons for the request in the `Reason For Request` field (optional).
9.  Add any additional comments or information in the `Additional Comments` field (optional).
10. Click "Submit" to complete and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name (Client Name)**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Birth Date (Birth Date)**: Enter the client's birthdate in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
*   **Therapist Name (Therapist Name)**: Enter the name of the authorized individual who needs access to the client's information.
*   **Email (Email)**: Enter the authorized individual's email address.
*   **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter the authorized individual's phone number.
*   **Consent Date (Consent Date)**: Choose a date when the consent is valid, which is recommended to be the same as the date the form is completed.
*   **Authorization Type (Authorization Type)**: Select from the list of available options (e.g., "Authorization for treatment team", "Authorization for billing", or "Authorization for insurance") to specify the purpose of the authorization.
*   **Authorization Expires On (Authorization Expires On)**: Enter the date when the authorization expires, which is recommended to be the same as the consent date.
*   **Reason For Request (Reason For Request)**: Enter a brief explanation for the request, if necessary.
*   **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Provide any additional information or comments that may be helpful for the treatment team or billing purposes.
*   **Form ID (Form ID)**: This field is not editable and is automatically generated for internal use.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you're only authorizing access to the necessary personnel or services to maintain the client's information security.
*   Be cautious with the authorization types, as they may have different implications for the client's care and confidentiality.
*   Keep the consent date and authorization expires on the same or close to the same date to avoid confusion.
