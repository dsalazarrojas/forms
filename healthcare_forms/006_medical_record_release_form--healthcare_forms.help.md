# Medical Record Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Release Form is a document used to request access to a patient's medical records from a healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Carefully enter all required and optional information as directed by the instructions below.
2. **Submit the form**: Once completed, submit the form to the healthcare provider for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Record Release Form**: This is the title of the form, which explains its purpose.
* **patient_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter the date of your birth in the standard date format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **medical_history**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or treatments you are currently receiving.
* **patient_signature**: Sign the form with your signature, which will be used as proof of your identity and agreement to the release of your medical records.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays in processing.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the healthcare provider or the contact person listed at the top of the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required and optional information is included.
