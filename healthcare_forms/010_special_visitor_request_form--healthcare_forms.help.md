# Special Visitor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Visitor Request Form is used to collect information from visitors who are visiting a healthcare facility for a specific reason. This form helps us understand who is visiting and why, ensuring that their visit is handled properly and they receive the necessary care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Visitor Name" field.
2. Enter your organization or company in the "Organization" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, making sure to use the format: `username@domain.com`.
5. Check the "Type of Visit" field and select "Yes" if this is a scheduled visit, or "No" if it's an unscheduled visit.
6. Describe the reason for your visit in the "Description of Event" field.
7. Enter the date of the event or visit in the "Event Date" field.
8. If applicable, enter the number of people attending in the "Number of People Attending" field.
9. Optionally, add any additional comments about your visit in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Visitor Name
* **Visitor Name** (`text`, required): Enter your name in this field.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address in the format `username@domain.com`.

### Type of Visit
* **Type of Visit** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if this is a scheduled visit, or "No" if it's an unscheduled visit.

### Description of Event
* **Description of Event** (`text`, required): Describe the reason for your visit in this field.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the event or visit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Number of People Attending
* **Number of People Attending** (`number`, optional): If applicable, enter the number of people attending the event or visit.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your visit in this field.
