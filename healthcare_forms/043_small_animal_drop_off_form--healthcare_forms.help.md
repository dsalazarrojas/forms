# small_animal_drop_off_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The small_animal_drop_off_form is a form designed to collect information about small animals being dropped off at a veterinary clinic. This form helps clinic staff to quickly and accurately record essential details about each animal, ensuring that they can provide the best care and attention to the animal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of small animal you are dropping off by choosing one or multiple options in the "Animal Type" field.
2. Enter the name of the animal in the "Animal Name" field.
3. Enter the drop-off date and time in the "Drop Off Date" and "Drop Off Time" fields, respectively.
4. Specify the drop-off location and contact person in the "Drop Off Location" and "Drop Off Contact Person" fields.
5. Provide any relevant veterinary clinic information in the "Veterinary Clinic Information" field.
6. If the animal has a medical condition, enter it in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Enter any special instructions or notes for the care of the animal in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Enter the animal's weight in the "Animal Weight" field.
9. Select the species of the animal from the "Species" field.
10. Enter the animal's size and color in the "Size" and "Color" fields, respectively.
11. Select the sex of the animal from the "Sex" field.
12. Enter the animal's age in the "Age" field.
13. Enter the breed of the animal in the "Breed" field.
14. Select whether the animal is within a weight range by choosing an option in the "Weight Range" field.
15. Enter any microchip information, including the microchip number, owner, and serial number in the "Microchip Number", "Microchip Owner", and "Microchip Serial Number" fields, respectively.
16. Enter the microchip date and expiration date in the "Microchip Date" and "Microchip Expiration Date" fields, respectively.
17. Finally, have the owner and vet sign the form to confirm the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal Type**: Select the type of small animal you are dropping off.
* **Animal Name**: Enter the name of the animal.
*   **Drop Off Date**: Enter the date on which you are dropping off the animal.
*   **Drop Off Time**: Enter the time at which you are dropping off the animal.
*   **Drop Off Location**: Enter the location where you are dropping off the animal.
*   **Drop Off Contact Person**: Enter the contact person responsible for the animal's care.
*   **Veterinary Clinic Information**: Enter any relevant information about the veterinary clinic.
*   **Medical Condition**: If the animal has a medical condition, enter it here.
*   **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or notes for the animal's care.
*   **Animal Weight**: Enter the weight of the animal.
*   **Species**: Select the species of the animal.
*   **Size**: Enter the size of the animal.
*   **Color**: Enter the color of the animal.
*   **Sex**: Select the sex of the animal.
*   **Age**: Enter the age of the animal.
*   **Breed**: Enter the breed of the animal.
*   **Weight Range**: Select whether the animal is within a weight range.
*   **Species ID**: Enter any Species ID associated with the animal.
*   **Microchip Number**: Enter the microchip number associated with the animal.
*   **Microchip Owner**: Enter the owner of the microchip.
*   **Microchip Serial Number**: Enter the microchip serial number associated with the animal.
*   **Microchip Date**: Enter the date the microchip was implanted.
*   **Microchip Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of the microchip.
*   **Owner Signature**: Have the owner sign to confirm the information provided.
*   **Vet Signature**: Have the vet sign to confirm the information provided.
