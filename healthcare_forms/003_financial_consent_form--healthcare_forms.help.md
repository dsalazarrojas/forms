# Financial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Consent Form is a document used to record financial information from patients and medical practitioners to ensure a clear understanding of any financial obligations or expectations related to medical services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Form Header** section with the relevant details.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the **Medical Practitioner** name in the corresponding field.
4. Enter the **Medical Practitioner Contact** information in the corresponding field.
5. If applicable, enter the **Fees** associated with medical services.
6. Select the **Payment Terms** that apply.
7. Enter the date the form was signed in the **Date Signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
• **Form Header** (1, text, not required): This section provides a summary of the form's purpose and content.

### Patient Name
• **patient_name** (2, text, not required): Enter the name of the patient.

### Medical Practitioner
• **medical_practitioner** (3, text, not required): Enter the name of the medical practitioner.

### Medical Practitioner Contact
• **medical_practitioner_contact** (4, text, not required): Enter the contact information of the medical practitioner.

### Fees
• **fees** (5, number, not required): Enter any fees associated with medical services.

### Payment Terms
• **payment_terms** (6, select multiple, not required): Select the payment terms that apply (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

### Date Signed
• **date_signed** (7, date, not required): Enter the date the form was signed.

## Tips
• Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
• Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
• This form is for recording financial information only; do not use for billing or payment processing.
