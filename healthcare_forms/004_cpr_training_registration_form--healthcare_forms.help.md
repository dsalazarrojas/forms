# CPR Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for CPR training registration. It includes contact details, medical considerations, and preferences for training format and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address to receive registration confirmation.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for updates and reminders.
3. Provide your organization or school name if applicable.
4. Choose the appropriate CPR course level based on your needs.
5. Select the preferred training format.
6. Choose the preferred training date, subject to availability.
7. Specify how you heard about us.
8. If you have any medical considerations, describe them briefly.
9. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Participant Information --
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive registration confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Organization or School Name** (text, optional): If you are representing an organization or school, enter the name here.

### -- Course Selection --
* **CPR Course Level** (select one, required): Select the level of CPR training you require:
	+ General Public - Basic
	+ Healthcare Provider - BLS
	+ Professional Rescuer
	+ Instructor Level

### -- Training Details --
* **Preferred Training Format** (select one, required): Choose your preferred training format:
	+ In-Person Classroom
	+ Blended Learning - Online plus Skills Session
	+ Group On-site Training

### -- Additional Information --
* **Preferred Training Date** (date, required): Select the preferred date for your training session, subject to availability.
* **How did you hear about us** (select one, optional): Specify how you heard about our training services:
	+ Employer
	+ Social Media
	+ Search Engine
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Other

### -- Contact Information --
* **Do you have any physical limitations that might affect your ability to perform chest compressions** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform chest compressions, describe them briefly.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I understand that certification is valid for 2 years** (select one, required): Confirm your understanding of certification validity.
