# phq-9-and-gad-7 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 form is a screening tool used to assess symptoms of Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) and depression. This form helps evaluate the frequency and intensity of feelings related to anxiety, worry, and other emotional states.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. For each question, choose the answer that best describes how you have been feeling over the past two weeks.
2. Select all that apply for questions that ask about multiple feelings or symptoms.
3. Answer each question independently, and do not skip any sections.
4. Fill in the form carefully, as the answers will be used to assess your symptoms.
