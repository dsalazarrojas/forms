# Postoperative Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Postoperative Discharge Form is used to document a patient's postoperative experience, including details about their surgery, discharge instructions, and follow-up appointments. This form helps healthcare professionals standardize the collection of postoperative data and improve patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the surgery in the "Date of Surgery" field.
3. Enter the date of discharge in the "Date of Discharge" field.
4. Provide postoperative instructions in the "Postoperative Instructions" field.
5. Outline the patient's postoperative pain management plan in the "Postoperative Pain Management" field.
6. List any medications the patient is taking in the "Medication" field.
7. Schedule follow-up appointments with the patient in the "Follow-up Appointments" field.
8. Record the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact Info" field.
9. Enter the name of the physician who signed the form in the "Physician Name" field.
10. Sign and date the form in the "Signature" field to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Surgery** (`date_of_surgery`, date, required): Enter the date of the surgery.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Postoperative Instructions** (`postoperative_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any postoperative instructions or advice for the patient.
* **Postoperative Pain Management** (`postoperative_pain_management`, text, required): Outline the patient's postoperative pain management plan, including any medication or treatment options.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is taking.
* **Follow-up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, text, optional): Schedule follow-up appointments with the patient.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, text, required): Record the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the physician who signed the form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
