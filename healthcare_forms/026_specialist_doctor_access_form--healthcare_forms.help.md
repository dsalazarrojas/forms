# Specialist Doctor Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request an appointment or share medical history with a specialist doctor. Fill out this form to provide essential information for your specialist doctor to assess your needs and provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Request Appointment" field and enter your request. You can provide a brief description of your appointment request.
2. If you have medical history to share, click on the "Share Medical History" field and enter your medical history.
3. In the "Medical History" field, enter any relevant medical information about your condition or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Appointment** (`request_appointment`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your appointment request.
* **Share Medical History** (`share_medical_history`, text, optional): If you have medical history to share, enter your medical history.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical information about your condition or treatment.

## Tips
* Please be concise and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our support team at form@gic.mx.
