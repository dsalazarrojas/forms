# healthcare_employee_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from healthcare employees on various aspects of their work environment, including workplace environment, work experience, relationship with manager, communication, benefits and compensation, work-life balance, team environment, job security, and feedback and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience and perception.
3. Make sure to provide specific examples or explanations to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workplace Environment** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): This section is a free-text field where you can write any comments or suggestions about your workplace environment.
- **Work Experience** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): How do you like working here? This field is for you to express your feelings about your work experience.
- **Relationship with Manager** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): How would you describe your relationship with your manager? Please select one option that best fits your experience.
  - Excellent
  - Neutral
  - Poor
  - Don't Know
- **Communication** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): How would you rate the communication within your team and department? Please select all that apply.
  - Excellent
  - Needs Improvement
  - Poor
- **Benefits and Compensation** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for you to express any comments or suggestions about benefits and compensation.
- **Work Life Balance** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): How many hours do you work per week? (Please answer with a number)
- **Team Environment** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): How would you describe your team environment? Please select one option that best fits your experience.
  - Excellent
  - Needs Improvement
  - Poor
- **Job Security** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): How would you rate the job security within your department? Please select all that apply.
  - Excellent
  - Needs Improvement
  - Poor
  - Very Poor
- **Feedback** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Is there anything you'd like to share about your experience that isn't covered in the above questions? Please use this field to provide any additional feedback.
- **Suggestions** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving the work environment or work experience? Please use this field to provide any ideas or suggestions.
