# Nutrition And Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information from patients about their medical history, allergies, nutrition, and wellness goals to provide personalized care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Provide detailed information about your medical history, allergies, and nutrition habits.
3. Share your wellness goals and any relevant social information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**:
	+ This field is used to collect basic contact information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
	+ Please provide your full name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Medical History (Page 2)**:
	+ Describe your past medical conditions, surgeries, and medical procedures.
	+ Include any chronic conditions, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Medication List (Page 3)**:
	+ List all your current medications, including vitamins and supplements.
	+ Include the dosage and frequency of each medication.
* **Allergies (Page 4)**:
	+ List all known allergies, including food, medication, and environmental allergens.
* **Nutrition Information (Page 5)**:
	+ Describe your current diet, including any food restrictions or preferences.
	+ Share your eating habits, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Wellness Goals (Page 6)**:
	+ Share your short-term and long-term wellness goals.
	+ Include any specific health outcomes you aim to achieve.
* **Social Info (Page 7)**:
	+ Provide any relevant social information, such as occupation or hobbies.
* **Contact Info (Page 8)**:
	+ Confirm your contact information for follow-up purposes.
