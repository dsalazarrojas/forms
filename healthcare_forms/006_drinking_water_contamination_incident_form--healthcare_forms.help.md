# Drinking Water Contamination Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of drinking water contamination to aid public health officials in identifying and addressing potential risks to the public.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Click on the "Start form" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Provide the date and location of the incident in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (1, date): \* This field requires the date the incident occurred.
* **Location Name** (2, text): This is the name of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Water Source** (3, select\_multiple): Select all sources of drinking water that may be related to the incident (e.g., Ground water, Well, Spring, Tap water, Reservoir).
* **Contamination Type** (4, select\_one): Choose the type of contamination (e.g., Bacteria, Viral, Chemical, Radioactive, Heavy Metal, Other).
* **Reporting Party** (5, select\_one): Identify the person or organization responsible for reporting the incident (e.g., Water Company, Public Health Official, Community Leader, Other).
* **Contact Information** (6, text): Provide contact details for follow-up and further information.
* **Incident Details** (7, text): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Action Taken** (8, text): Describe any actions taken or planned to address the issue.
* **Follow-up** (9, text): Describe any follow-up activities or plans.
* **Outcome** (10, text): Describe the outcome of the incident.
* **Note** (11, note): Add any additional information or notes about the incident.
* **Email** (12, email): Provide an email address for further contact.
* **Phone** (13, text): Provide a phone number for further contact.
* **Submitter** (14, text): Identify the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Contact Information** (15, text): Provide contact information for the person who submitted the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (16, select\_one): Select the tool or equipment used to investigate and mitigate the incident (e.g., Chatjimmy, Other).
* **Form Version** (17, text): Provide the version of the form used.
* **Date Modified** (18, date): The date the form was last modified.
* **Modified By** (19, text): Identify the person who last modified the form.

This guide is provided to ensure that users can complete the form accurately and effectively, and to provide a comprehensive understanding of the incident.
