# Psychological Trauma Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's experiences with psychological trauma. It aims to assess the presence and severity of trauma-related symptoms and provide a comprehensive understanding of the individual's situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your experiences.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for assistance or clarification.
