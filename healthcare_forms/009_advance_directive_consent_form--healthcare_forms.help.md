# Advance Directive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advance Directive Consent Form is a legal document used to outline a patient's preferences for medical treatment in situations where they are unable to communicate their wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information Section**: Provide your legal full name, date of birth, and current residential address.
2. Select your preferences for life-sustaining treatment, cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), mechanical ventilation, artificial nutrition and hydration, and organ donation.
3. **Healthcare Proxy Section**: Designate a healthcare proxy to make decisions on your behalf, provide their name, relationship, and contact information.
4. **Specific Instructions Section**: Provide any religious or spiritual preferences and any other specific instructions for healthcare providers.
5. **Signature Section**: Date and sign the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the advance directive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Full Name**
*   **Patient Full Name** (`Patient Full Name`, `text`, required): This is the patient's legal full name.

### 2. **Date of Birth**
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): This is the patient's date of birth.

### 3. **Address**
*   **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): This is the patient's current residential address.

### 4. **Life-Sustaining Treatment Preference**
*   **Life-Sustaining Treatment Preference** (`Select One`, required): Choose your preference for life-sustaining treatment if you are terminally ill:
	+   **Yes, all possible measures**
	+   **Only measures that offer hope of recovery**
	+   **Comfort care only**
	+   **No life-sustaining treatment**

### 5. **Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) Preference**
*   **Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) Preference** (`Select One`, required): Choose your preference for cardiopulmonary resuscitation:
	+   **Yes, perform CPR**
	+   **No, do not perform CPR**
	+   **Depends on circumstances**

### 6. **Mechanical Ventilation Preference**
*   **Mechanical Ventilation Preference** (`Select One`, required): Choose your preference for mechanical ventilation:
	+   **Yes, if needed**
	+   **No, do not use mechanical ventilation**
	+   **Only for temporary periods**

### 7. **Artificial Nutrition and Hydration Preference**
*   **Artificial Nutrition and Hydration Preference** (`Select One`, required): Choose your preference for artificial nutrition and hydration:
	+   **Yes, provide nutrition and hydration**
	+   **No, do not provide artificially**
	+   **Only temporarily while recovering**

### 8. **Organ Donation Decision**
*   **Organ Donation Decision** (`Select One`, required): Choose your preference for organ donation:
	+   **Yes, I consent to organ donation**
	+   **No, I do not wish to donate**
	+   **Family decision**
	+   **Don't know**

### 9. **Healthcare Proxy**
*   **Healthcare Proxy Name** (`Healthcare Proxy Name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person designated to make healthcare decisions.
*   **Healthcare Proxy Relationship** (`Healthcare Proxy Relationship`, `text`, required): This is the relationship to the patient.
*   **Healthcare Proxy Contact** (`Healthcare Proxy Contact`, `text`, required): This is the phone number and/or address of the healthcare proxy.

### 10. **Alternate Healthcare Proxy**
*   **Alternate Healthcare Proxy** (`Alternate Healthcare Proxy`, `text`, required): This is the name of an alternate person to make healthcare decisions if the primary proxy is unavailable.

### 11. **Religious or Spiritual Preferences**
*   **Religious or Spiritual Preferences** (`Religious or Spiritual Preferences`, `text`, required): This is any religious or spiritual consideration for care.

### 12. **Additional Instructions**
*   **Additional Instructions** (`Additional Instructions`, `text`, required): This is any other specific instructions for healthcare providers.

### 13. **Signature Date**
*   **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, `date`, required): This is the date of signing this advance directive.

### 14. **Witness Signature Required**
*   **Witness Signature Required** (`Select One`, required): Choose whether this form requires witness signatures according to state law:
	+   **Yes**
	+   **No**
	+   **Unsure**

This guide is designed to be as helpful as possible, but keep in mind that specific regulations regarding advance directives vary across jurisdictions. Please refer to relevant local laws and regulations for more information.
