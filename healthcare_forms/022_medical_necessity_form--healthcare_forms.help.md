# Medical Necessity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's medical necessity, which is essential for determining if a particular medical treatment or service is required.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:

1. Fill in the patient's name and age on pages 1 and 2 of the form.
2. Select the reason for the medical necessity on page 3.
3. Select all applicable reasons for the medication or service on page 4.
4. Sign and date the form on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (Page 1, Text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card.
* **patient_age** (Page 2, Number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **reason_for_request** (Page 3, Text, required): Enter a brief explanation of the reason for the medical necessity.
* **reasons_for_medication_or_service** (Page 4, Select Multiple, optional): Select all applicable reasons for the medical necessity, such as Cardiac Arrhythmia, Chronic Kidney Disease, or others.
* **physician_signature** (Page 5, Note, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all applicable reasons for the medical necessity to ensure the form is complete.
* Double-check the patient's name and age for accuracy.
* Be concise in the reason for the medical necessity to avoid unnecessary details.
* Ensure the form is signed and dated by a licensed healthcare professional.
