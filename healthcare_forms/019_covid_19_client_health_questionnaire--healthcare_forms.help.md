# COVID 19 Client Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their health status regarding COVID-19. It is essential to ensure that the client's personal, medical, and vaccination history is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. For date fields, use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. For select_one fields, choose the most accurate answer from the options provided.
4. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable chronic health conditions.
5. For text fields, enter a brief description of your symptoms or other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Personal Information --
### 2. **Full Name**
* Label: Full Name
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.

### 3. **Date of Birth**
* Label: Date of Birth
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 4. **Client ID**
* Label: Client ID
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your client ID number, if applicable.

### 5. **Phone Number**
* Label: Phone Number
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### 6. **Email Address**
* Label: Email Address
* Type: Email
* Description: Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### 7. **Date of Visit**
* Label: Date of Visit
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date you visited the healthcare provider.

### -- COVID-19 Specific Questions --
### 9. **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?**
* Label: Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose yes if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, no if not, or unsure if you are not sure.

### 10. **If yes, when were you diagnosed?**
* Label: If yes, when were you diagnosed?
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date you were diagnosed with COVID-19, if applicable.

### 11. **Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms?**
* Label: Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms?
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose yes if you currently have COVID-19 symptoms, no if not, or unsure if you are not sure.

### 12. **If yes, describe your symptoms**
* Label: If yes, describe your symptoms
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter a brief description of your current COVID-19 symptoms.

### 13. **How long have you had symptoms?**
* Label: How long have you had symptoms?
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter a brief description of how long you have been experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.

### 14. **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19?**
* Label: Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19?
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose yes if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19, no if not, or unsure if you are not sure.

### 15. **When was the exposure?**
* Label: When was the exposure?
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date of the exposure, if applicable.

### -- Vaccination History --
### 17. **Vaccination Status**
* Label: Vaccination Status
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose your vaccination status: Not vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Fully vaccinated, or Fully vaccinated with booster.

### 18. **Vaccine Type Received**
* Label: Vaccine Type Received
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose the type of vaccine you received: Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, AstraZeneca, or Other (please specify).

### 19. **Date of First Dose**
* Label: Date of First Dose
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date of your first dose, if applicable.

### 20. **Date of Second Dose**
* Label: Date of Second Dose
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date of your second dose, if applicable.

### 21. **Date of Booster Dose**
* Label: Date of Booster Dose
* Type: Date
* Description: Enter the date of your booster dose, if applicable.

### 22. **Any side effects from vaccination?**
* Label: Any side effects from vaccination?
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose yes if you experienced any side effects from vaccination, no if not.

### -- Medical History --
### 24. **Chronic Health Conditions**
* Label: Chronic Health Conditions
* Type: Select Multiple
* Description: Choose all chronic health conditions that apply, such as Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart Disease, Lung Disease, Kidney Disease, or Immunocompromised.

### 25. **Current Medications**
* Label: Current Medications
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter a list of your current medications, if applicable.
