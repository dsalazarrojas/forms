> **Patient Daily Log Form Template - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
----------

This form is designed to track patient information and activities on a daily basis. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to monitor and record patient vital signs, symptoms, treatments, and other relevant medical information. This form is typically used in clinical settings to ensure accurate and efficient documentation of patient care.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient Id" field.
2. Select the date of the log entry using the date picker.
3. Enter the time of the log entry using the time picker.
4. Record the patient's symptoms, treatments, and activities in the respective fields.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition in the "Notes" field.
6. (Optional) If additional patient information is available, you can enter it in the "Additional Patient Id", "Additional Date", "Additional Time", "Additional Symptoms", "Additional Treatments", "Additional Patient Activities", and "Additional Notes" fields.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
-------------------------

* **Patient Id** (`patient_id`, number, required): Enter the patient's ID number. This is a unique identifier for the patient and is used to track their medical records.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the log entry using the date picker. This is the date on which the patient's information was recorded.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the log entry using the time picker. This is the time at which the patient's information was recorded.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Record the patient's symptoms, such as pain, fever, or other medical concerns.
* **Treatments** (`treatments`, text, required): Record any treatments or medications administered to the patient.
* **Patient Activities** (`patient_activities`, text, required): Record any activities or events that occurred with the patient, such as hospital visits or test results.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
* **Additional Patient Id** (`patient_id_2`, number, optional): If additional patient information is available, enter it here.
* **Additional Date** (`date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the additional log entry.
* **Additional Time** (`time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time of the additional log entry.
* **Additional Symptoms** (`symptoms_2`, text, optional): Record any additional symptoms or medical concerns.
* **Additional Treatments** (`treatments_2`, text, optional): Record any additional treatments or medications administered to the patient.
* **Additional Patient Activities** (`patient_activities_2`, text, optional): Record any additional activities or events that occurred with the patient.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.

**Tips**
--------

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Patient Id, Date, Time, Symptoms, Treatments, Patient Activities, and Notes) for accurate and efficient documentation.
* Use the additional fields only when necessary to provide extra information.
* Review and edit the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
