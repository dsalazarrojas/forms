# Treatment Interruption Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Treatment Interruption Assessment Form" is used to assess and document the interruption of treatment for a patient, gathering relevant medical history, reason for interruption, treatment status, medication side effects, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
2. Specify the reason for treatment interruption.
3. Select the current treatment status.
4. Document any medication side effects or allergic reactions.
5. Describe the patient's outcomes and any relevant details.
6. Write any additional notes or comments from the physician.
7. Summarize the treatment plan and patient care plan.
8. Specify any next steps, follow-up dates, and referrals.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Reason For Interruption** (`Reason For Interruption`, text, optional): Please specify the reason for treatment interruption.
* **Treatment Status** (`Treatment Status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Active: The treatment is currently active.
	+ Completed: The treatment has been completed.
	+ Discontinued: The treatment has been discontinued.
	+ Pending: The treatment status is pending.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`Medication Side Effects`, text, optional): Please document any medication side effects experienced by the patient.
* **Medication Allergic Reactions** (`Medication Allergic Reactions`, text, optional): Please document any medication allergic reactions experienced by the patient.
* **Patient Outcomes** (`Patient Outcomes`, text, optional): Please describe the patient's outcomes and any relevant details.
* **Physician Notes** (`Physician Notes`, text, optional): Please add any additional notes or comments from the physician.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, text, optional): Please summarize the treatment plan and patient care plan.
* **Patient Care Plan** (`Patient Care Plan`, text, optional): Please describe the patient care plan.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Follow-up: The patient needs follow-up care.
	+ Referral: The patient needs a referral.
	+ Other: Other next steps.
* **Next Steps Description** (`Next Steps Description`, text, optional): Please describe the next steps.
* **Physician Recommendations** (`Physician Recommendations`, text, optional): Please add any physician recommendations.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, date, optional): Please enter the follow-up date.
* **Referral** (`Referral`, text, optional): Please specify the referral information.
* **Other Referral Notes** (`Other Referral Notes`, text, optional): Please add any additional referral notes.
* **Other** (`Other`, text, optional): Please add any other relevant information.
* **Other Notes** (`Other Notes`, text, optional): Please add any other notes or comments.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's name.
* **Patient DOB** (`Patient DOB`, date, optional): Please enter the patient's date of birth.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
