# medical_history_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical history verification form is used to collect and verify the medical history of a patient. This form is part of the "healthcare_forms" category and is intended to be completed by medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information (page 1)
	* Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Provide medical history (page 2)
	* Enter a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
3. Select medical conditions (page 3)
	* Choose any medical conditions that the patient may have from the list provided.
4. Select medications (page 4)
	* Choose any medications the patient is currently taking from the list provided.
5. List allergies (page 5)
	* Choose any allergies the patient has from the list provided.
6. Verify medical history (page 6)
	* Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history that could impact current treatment.
7. (Additional fields for verification and verification dates)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information. Please fill in the patient's details accurately.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect a detailed account of the patient's medical history. Please provide as much information as possible.
* **Medical History Verification** (`medical_history_verification`, text, required/optional): This field is used to verify the patient's medical history and ensure accuracy.
* **Medical Condition List** (`medical_condition_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select any medical conditions the patient may have from the list provided. Choose all that apply.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select any medications the patient is currently taking from the list provided. Choose all that apply.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select any allergies the patient has from the list provided.
* **Medical History Verification 2** (`medical_history_verification_2`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the patient's medical history that could impact current treatment.
* **Medical History Verification 3** (`medical_history_verification_3`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Verification 4** (`medical_history_verification_4`, time, required/optional): This field is used to record the date and time of the last medical update.
* **Medical History Verification 5** (`medical_history_verification_5`, date, required/optional): This field is used to record the start date of the medical update.
* **Medical History Verification 6** (`medical_history_verification_6`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Verification 7** (`medical_history_verification_7`, email, required/optional): This field is used to record the patient's email address.
* **Medical History Verification 8** (`medical_history_verification_8`, note, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the patient's medical history that could impact current treatment.
* **Medical History Verification 9** (`medical_history_verification_9`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Verification 10** (`medical_history_verification_10`, number, required/optional): This field is used to record a numerical value related to the patient's medical history.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their medical professional.
* The information collected in this form will be kept confidential and will only be used for the patient's benefit.
* Please do not share this form with unauthorized personnel.
