# Liver Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Liver Quiz is a form designed to gather and evaluate information about a patient's liver health, including potential causes of liver dysfunction and test results. It's not intended to be a comprehensive medical assessment but rather a quick quiz to aid in initial evaluation or follow-up consultations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: no): This page provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Liver Function** (`liver_function`, `text`, required: no): Answer this question to describe any issues or concerns you've experienced with your liver function.
* **Liver Dysfunction** (`liver_dysfunction`, `select_multiple`, required: no): Select all potential causes of liver dysfunction you've experienced, such as Fatty Liver Disease, Viral Hepatitis, or Autoimmune Hepatitis.
* **Liver Test Results** (`liver_test`, `date`, required: no): Enter the date of your latest liver test results.
* **Liver Result Score** (`liver_result`, `number`, required: no): Enter your liver function test results, if available.
* **Liver Treatment** (`liver_treatment`, `select_one`, required: no): Choose the type of treatment you're currently undergoing or have undergone for your liver condition, such as Surgery, Medicine, or Dietary Changes.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions to ensure accurate assessment.
* If you're unsure about any question, please consult with a medical professional for guidance.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any additional information or concerns not addressed in this form, please include it in the form's comments or description section.
