# Flu Shot Proof Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flu Shot Proof Form is designed to collect information from patients who have received a flu vaccination. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to accurately record and verify patient data, ensuring timely and efficient follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name and date of vaccination.
2. Select the type of vaccine administered (Influenza A, B, or A+B).
3. Indicate the dose of vaccine administered (0.25 mL, 0.5 mL, 0.75 mL, or 1 mL).
4. Identify the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine (Nurse, Physician, or Other).
5. Optionally, include patient and healthcare provider signatures as proof of vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
- **Date of Vaccination**: Enter the date the flu shot was administered using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of flu vaccine administered from the options provided.
- **Dose Administered**: Select the amount of vaccine administered from the options provided.
- **Vaccine Administered By**: Select the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine from the options provided.
- **Patient Signature**: This field is optional and may be used as a patient's acknowledgement of receiving the vaccine.
- **Healthcare Provider Signature**: This field is optional and may be used as a healthcare provider's verification of vaccine administration.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Sign the form with your institution's stamp or verification, as needed.
* Store this form in the patient's medical record for future reference.

Note: The fields marked as optional may be removed or added to according to your institution's policies and guidelines. Always refer to your institution's policies and guidelines for form completion.
