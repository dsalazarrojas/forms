# respiratory_infection_screening_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to screen for respiratory infections in patients. It collects information about the patient's symptoms, medical history, and other relevant details. This form is meant to help healthcare professionals quickly assess the patient's situation and determine the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their contact details and demographic data.
2. Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for visiting the healthcare professional.
3. Provide a detailed account of the patient's history of pain, including when it started, how long it has lasted, and any associated factors.
4. Report on the patient's respiratory symptoms, including duration and characteristics.
5. Answer the questions about fever duration, cough duration, and any other relevant symptoms.
6. Describe the patient's sputum characteristics, if applicable.
7. Rate the patient's chest tightness, difficulty breathing, and shortness of breath.
8. Report on sputum production, color, smell, and texture, if applicable.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information**
* `patient_information`: Enter the patient's personal details, such as name, contact information, and demographic data.
### 2. **Chief Complaint**
* `chief_complaint`: Describe the patient's main reason for visiting the healthcare professional.
### 3. **History of Pain**
* `history_of_pain`: Provide a detailed account of the patient's pain history, including when it started, how long it has lasted, and any associated factors.
### 4. **Respiratory Symptoms**
* `respiratory_symptoms`: Report on the patient's respiratory symptoms, including duration and characteristics.
### 5. **Fever Duration**
* `fever_duration`: Answer the question about the duration of the patient's fever.
### 6. **Cough Duration**
* `cough_duration`: Report on the duration of the patient's cough.
### 7. **Sputum Characteristics**
* `sputum_characteristics`: Describe the patient's sputum characteristics, if applicable.
### 8. **Chest Tightness**
* `chest_tightness`: Rate the patient's chest tightness.
### 9. **Difficulty Breathing**
* `difficulty_breathing`: Rate the patient's difficulty breathing.
### 10. **Shortness of Breath**
* `shortness_of_breath`: Rate the patient's shortness of breath.
### 11. **Sputum Production**
* `sputum_production`: Report on sputum production.
### 12. **Sputum Color**
* `sputum_color`: Report on sputum color.
### 13. **Sputum Smell**
* `sputum_smell`: Report on sputum smell.
### 14. **Sputum Texture**
* `sputum_texture`: Report on sputum texture.
