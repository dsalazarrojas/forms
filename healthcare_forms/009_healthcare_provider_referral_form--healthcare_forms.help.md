# Healthcare Provider Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate healthcare referrals between healthcare providers. It collects information about the healthcare provider being referred, including their contact details and specialty. This form is intended to be completed by healthcare professionals to help streamline the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the healthcare provider's first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide the healthcare provider's date of birth.
3. Enter their medical record number (if applicable).
4. Specify the healthcare provider's specialty.
5. Enter their contact details, including phone number, email, and fax (if applicable).
6. Enter the facility's contact information, including name, address, phone number, email, and fax (if applicable).
7. Enter any additional notes about the referral.

* **Facility**: Enter the facility's name.

* **Facility city**: Enter the facility's city.
* **Facility state**: Select the facility's state.
