# personal_hygiene_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal hygiene survey is a form designed to assess an individual's personal hygiene habits. It is used in a healthcare setting to gather information on the frequency and practice of various personal hygiene habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best describe your personal hygiene habits for each question. You may select only one answer for each question, even if you practice a habit multiple times a day or week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do You Practice Good Hygiene Habits** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice good hygiene habits in general.
* **How Often Do You Wash Your Hands** (After Every Use, After Using Bathroom, Before Eating, After Sneezing, After Coughing, After Touching Raw Meat, Other): Please select how often you wash your hands.
* **How Often Do You Shower** (Every Day, 2-3 Times a Week, 1 Time a Week, Less Than 1 Time a Week): Please select how often you shower.
* **Do You Use Deodorant** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select if you use deodorant daily.
* **Do You Floss Your Teeth** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you floss your teeth.
* **Do You Wear Contacts** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select if you wear contact lenses.
* **How Much Water Do You Drink Per Day** (0-100 mL, 101-200 mL, 201-300 mL, 301-600 mL, 601-1200 mL, More Than 1200 mL, Less Than 0 mL): Please select the amount of water you drink per day.
* **Do You Exercise Daily** (True, False): Please select if you exercise daily.
* **Do You Eat Fruits And Vegetables** (Often, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you eat fruits and vegetables.
* **Do You Sleep 7 Hours** (True, False): Please select if you sleep 7 hours per day.
* **Do You Wear Separaters** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select if you wear separaters.
* **Do You Use Clothes** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you wear clothes.
* **How Much Time Do You Spend Outdoors** (0-1 Hour, 1-2 Hours, 2-3 Hours, 3-4 Hours, More Than 4 Hours, Never): Please select how much time you spend outdoors.
* **Do You Wear Mask** (True, False): Please select if you wear a mask.
* **Do You Practice Hair Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice hair hygiene.
* **Do You Practice Skin Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice skin hygiene.
* **Do You Practice Foot Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice foot hygiene.
* **Do You Practice Nail Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice nail hygiene.
* **Do You Practice Ear Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice ear hygiene.
* **Do You Wash Your Hair** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you wash your hair.
* **Do You Wash Your Face** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you wash your face.
* **Do You Floss Your Tongue** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you floss your tongue.
* **Do You Practice Proper Toilet Hygiene** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you practice proper toilet hygiene.
* **Do You Haircutting** (Always, Frequently, Rarely, Never): Please select how often you haircutting.
* **Do You Wash Your Hair Frequency** (Daily, 2-3 Times a Week, 1 Time a Week, 2-4 Times a Month, 1 Time a Month, Never): Please select how often you wash your hair.

## Tips
Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your abilities.
