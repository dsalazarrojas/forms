# Health Screening Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to health screenings, including personal details and reason for absence.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start filling out the form, click on the "Health Screening Absence Form" button or link.
2.  Fill out the required fields, marked with \* (asterisk), and leave the optional ones blank.
3.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
4.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
5.  Click on the calendar icon next to "Date of Absence" to select the date of your absence.
6.  Enter a brief description of your **Reason for Absence** in the text field.
7.  Select your **Department** from the drop-down list. This will help us to direct your absence request to the right team or manager.
8.  Enter the **Doctor's Name** if you are required to provide this information.
9.  Enter the **Medical Certification** number or reference if applicable.
10. Add any additional **Comments** about your absence in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help us quickly identify you in our records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to help us confirm your identity.
* **Date of Absence**: Select the date of your absence from the calendar. This will help us track your absence schedule.
* **Reason for Absence**: Describe the reason for your absence, whether medical, personal, or family-related.
* **Department**: Choose your department from the list to direct your absence request to the right team or manager.
* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of your doctor, if applicable, for further reference.
* **Medical Certification**: Enter the medical certification number or reference, if required.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your absence, such as the duration or any special requirements.

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting your form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy, especially dates and names.
* If you are uncertain about any field, consult with your HR representative for guidance.
* This form is for health-related absences only; for other types of absences, please use the corresponding form.
