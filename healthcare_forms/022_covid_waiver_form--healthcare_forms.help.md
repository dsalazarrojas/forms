## Covid Waiver Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Covid Waiver Form is a document used to obtain patient consent and medical information for healthcare providers. This form is essential for healthcare providers to collect necessary information about a patient's medical history, conditions, and waiver status.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Please fill in the patient's details, including their name, date of birth, phone number, and medical record number (if applicable).
2. Select any medical insurance provider information (if applicable).
3. Provide details about medical insurance plans (if applicable).
4. Record dates of vaccination doses (if applicable).
5. Document any known medical contraindications.
6. List any known allergies.
7. Provide a brief description of the patient's medical conditions.
8. Record any medical terminations (if applicable).
9. Document the patient's medical conditions history.
10. Provide a signature from both the patient and the healthcare provider to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (`name` , `text`, required): Fill in the patient's name.
- **Patient Information** (`date_of_birth` , `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
- **Phone** (`patient_phone` , `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number` , `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
- **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider` , `text`, required): Select the patient's medical insurance provider (if applicable).
- **Medical Insurance Group Number** (`medical_insurance_group_number` , `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical insurance group number (if applicable).
- **Medical Insurance Plan ID** (`medical_insurance_plan_id` , `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical insurance plan ID (if applicable).
- **Medical Insurance Plan Name** (`medical_insurance_plan_name` , `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical insurance plan name (if applicable).
- **Vaccine Dose 1 Date** (`vaccine_dose_1_date` , `date`, required): Record the date of the first vaccine dose (if applicable).
- **Vaccine Dose 2 Date** (`vaccine_dose_2_date` , `date`, required): Record the date of the second vaccine dose (if applicable).
- **Vaccine Dose 3 Date** (`vaccine_dose_3_date` , `date`, required): Record the date of the third vaccine dose (if applicable).
- **Vaccine Dose 4 Date** (`vaccine_dose_4_date` , `date`, required): Record the date of the fourth vaccine dose (if applicable).
- **Medical Contraindication** (`medical_contraindication` , `text`, required): Document any known medical contraindications.
- **Medical Contraindication Details** (`medical_contraindication_details` , `text`, required): Enter details about any known medical contraindications.
- **Medical Contraindication Date** (`medical_contraindication_date` , `date`, required): Record the date of the medical contraindication (if applicable).
- **Allergies** (`allergies` , `text`, required): List any known allergies.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions` , `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical conditions.
- **Medical Conditions History** (`medical_conditions_history` , `text`, required): Document the patient's medical conditions history.
- **Medical Termination** (`medical_terminations` , `text`, required): Record any medical terminations (if applicable).
- **Medical Termination Details** (`medical_terminations_details` , `text`, required): Enter details about any medical terminations (if applicable).
- **Medical Conditions Details** (`medical_conditions_details` , `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical conditions.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history` , `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature` , `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
- **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature` , `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
