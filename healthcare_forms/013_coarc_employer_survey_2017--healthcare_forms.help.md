# Coarc Employer Survey 2017 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from employers about their experiences with graduates from a specific program. The information collected will help the program understand its strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate answers for each field, making sure to provide accurate and honest feedback.
2. Answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. For optional fields, provide any additional information you feel is relevant or necessary.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
