# Behavioral Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Health Referral Form is used to gather crucial information for referring providers and patients seeking behavioral health services. It is designed to streamline the referral process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for efficient and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the referral information sections, ensuring that you, as the referring provider, are accurately representing your practice or organization.
2. Enter the patient's information, including date of birth, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
3. Select the primary behavioral health concern for the patient.
4. Describe the clinical presentation of the patient's symptoms.
5. Identify the patient's risk level and suicidal or homicidal ideation status.
6. Specify the type of service requested for the patient.
7. Choose the preferred frequency of sessions or interventions.
8. Acknowledge your authorization for the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Referral Information -- (Note)**: This section is a general note field for referring providers to add any additional context or comments.
* **Date of Referral** (`referral_date`, required): Enter the date the referral is being submitted.
* **Referring Provider Name** (`referring_provider`, required): Enter your name and credentials as the referring provider.
* **Referring Provider Phone** (`provider_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Referring Facility Name** (`facility_name`, optional): If you are referring from a specific facility, enter its name.
* **-- Patient Information -- (Note)**: This section is a general note field for patient information.
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`patient_email`, optional): If the patient has an email address, enter it.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, optional): If the patient has an insurance provider, enter its name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, optional): If the patient has a policy number, enter it.
* **-- Reason for Referral -- (Note)**: This section is a general note field for the reason for referral.
* **Primary Behavioral Health Concern** (`primary_concern`, required): Select the primary behavioral health concern for the patient from the provided options.
* **Clinical Presentation** (`clinical_presentation`, required): Describe the patient's symptoms in detail.
* **Duration of Symptoms** (`duration_of_symptoms`, optional): Select how long the patient has experienced their symptoms.
* **Prior Behavioral Health Treatment** (`prior_treatment`, optional): If the patient has received previous behavioral health treatment, describe it.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, optional): List any current psychiatric medications the patient is taking.
* **Safety Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, required): Select the patient's risk level.
* **Suicidal or Homicidal Ideation** (`suicidal_ideation`, required): Select the patient's suicidal or homicidal ideation status.
* **-- Requested Services -- (Note)**: This section is a general note field for requested services.
* **Type of Service Requested** (`service_type_requested`, required): Select the type of service requested for the patient.
* **Preferred Frequency** (`frequency_preference`, optional): Select the patient's preferred frequency of sessions or interventions.
* **Authorization for Referral** (`authorization`, required): Acknowledge your authorization for the referral.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to facilitate efficient processing of the referral.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, refer to your organization's policies and guidelines for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by the referring provider and the patient. However, if you are unable to gather information from the patient, please provide the best available information based on your assessment.
