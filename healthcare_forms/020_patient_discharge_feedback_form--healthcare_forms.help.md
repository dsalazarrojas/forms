**Patient Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information about a patient's discharge from healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate "Patient Discharge" option from the list.
2. Rate your overall experience (if applicable).
3. Provide a brief reason for admission.
4. Indicate your discharge status.
5. Enter the discharge date.
6. Provide information about the care team involved.
7. Enter any comments or feedback you have about your care.
8. Enter the discharge location.
9. Enter any discharge instructions.
10. Enter any comments or feedback you have about your care.
11. Sign and date the form.
12. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Discharge**: Select one of the provided options to indicate whether the patient has been discharged or not.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with a number (if applicable).
* **Reason for Admission**: Briefly explain why you were admitted to the healthcare service.
* **Discharge Status**: Indicate whether you are active or inactive.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of your discharge.
* **Care Team**: Enter the name and role of the care team members involved in your care.
* **Care Coordinator**: Enter the name and role of the care coordinator.
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about your care.
* **Discharge Location**: Enter the location where you were discharged.
* **Discharge Instructions**: Enter any instructions or advice given to you during discharge.
* **Patient Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your care.
* **Provider Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your care provider.
* **Provider Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your feedback.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your feedback.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your feedback.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the phone number of the patient.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the email of the patient.
* **Care Team Name**: Enter the name of the care team.
* **Care Team Role**: Enter the role of the care team.
* **Discharge Diagnosis**: Enter any discharge diagnosis.
* **Patient Condition**: Enter your current condition.
* **Follow Up Appointment**: Enter any follow-up appointment details (date and time).
