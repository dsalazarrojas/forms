# elder_care_program_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elder care program admission form is used to collect information for the admission process of patients into the elder care program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the facility where the patient will be admitted.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Enter the date and time of admission.
4. Enter the patient's age.
5. Provide the patient's contact phone number.
6. Provide the patient's email address.
7. Enter any relevant medical notes.
8. Select the level of care required for the patient (basic, extended, or prophetic).
9. Enter the address of the facility where the patient will be admitted.
10. Select the relationship between the patient and the contact person (family, friend, or other).
11. Review and submit the form for admission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **facility_name** (Text): Enter the name of the facility where the patient will be admitted.
* **patient_name** (Text): Enter the name of the patient.
* **date_of_admission** (Date): Enter the date of the patient's admission.
* **time_of_admission** (Time): Enter the time of the patient's admission.
* **age** (Number): Enter the age of the patient.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **medical_notes** (Text): Enter any relevant medical notes or history.
* **care_level** (Select One):
	+ **basic**: Select this option if the patient requires basic care.
	+ **extended**: Select this option if the patient requires extended care.
	+ **prophets**: Select this option if the patient requires prophetic care (Note: This option might be unclear or redundant, please confirm its purpose with the form's author or stakeholders.)
* **facility_address** (Text): Enter the address of the facility where the patient will be admitted.
* **contact_relationship** (Select Multiple):
	+ **family**: Select this option if the patient's contact is a family member.
	+ **friend**: Select this option if the patient's contact is a friend.
	+ **other**: Select this option if the patient's contact is someone else (e.g., social worker, caregiver).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
* Contact the facility or program administrators if you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form.
