# Bladder Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bladder Diary Form is a patient-completed form used to track and record daily bladder habits, providing healthcare providers with valuable information for patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your patient name and date of birth to identify yourself as the patient.
2.  Specify the start and end dates of the diary, as these will help healthcare providers track your progress over time.
3.  Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as you can about your experiences.
4.  For each day, fill out the sections on voiding times, volume estimates, leakage episodes, fluid intake, urgency level, pad usage, activities, symptoms, sleep quality, medications, caffeine intake, alcohol intake, bowel movements, exercise routine, and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Bladder Health Diary**: This is the main section where you will record your experiences and data for each day.
*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Your full name as the patient.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Your birth date for identification purposes.
*   **Diary Start Date** (`Diary Start Date`, `date`, required): The date you begin tracking your bladder habits with this diary.
*   **Diary End Date** (`Diary End Date`, `date`, required): The date you finish tracking your bladder habits.
*   **Day 1 Wake Time** (`Day 1 Wake Time`, `time`, optional): Record the time you wake up each day.
*   **Day 1 Void Times** (`Day 1 Void Times`, `text`, optional): List all times you urinated each day.
*   **Day 1 Volume Estimates** (`Day 1 Volume Estimates`, `text`, optional): Estimate the volume of each urination as small, medium, or large.
*   **Day 1 Leakage Episodes** (`Day 1 Leakage Episodes`, `text`, optional): Note any incidents of leakage or accidents.
*   **Day 1 Fluid Intake** (`Day 1 Fluid Intake`, `text`, optional): List all beverages consumed, including times and amounts.
*   **Day 1 Urgency Level** (`Day 1 Urgency Level`, `text`, optional): Rate the urgency of each urination on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Day 1 Pad Usage** (`Day 1 Pad Usage`, `number`, optional): Record the number of pads or protective garments used each day.
*   **Day 1 Activities** (`Day 1 Activities`, `text`, optional): Note any activities that may affect your bladder.
*   **Day 1 Symptoms** (`Day 1 Symptoms`, `text`, optional): Note any pain, burning, or discomfort experienced during urination.
*   **Day 1 Sleep Quality** (`Day 1 Sleep Quality`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your sleep quality as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Medications Taken** (`Medications Taken`, `text`, optional): List any bladder-related medications taken each day.
*   **Caffeine Intake** (`Caffeine Intake`, `select_one`, optional): Report caffeine intake as None, 1-2 servings, 3-4 servings, or 5 or more servings.
*   **Alcohol Intake** (`Alcohol Intake`, `select_one`, optional): Report alcohol intake as None, 1 drink, 2-3 drinks, or 4 or more drinks.
*   **Bowel Movement Regularity** (`Bowel Movement Regularity`, `select_one`, optional): Report bowel regularity as Regular Daily, Every Other Day, Irregular, or Constipated Often.
*   **Exercise Routine** (`Exercise Routine`, `text`, optional): Describe your exercise routine each day.
*   **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, optional): Add any other observations or comments relevant to your bladder health.

Note: The required fields are marked with `required: true` in the form schema and cannot be left blank. The optional fields can be completed at your discretion, providing valuable but non-mandatory information for your healthcare provider.
