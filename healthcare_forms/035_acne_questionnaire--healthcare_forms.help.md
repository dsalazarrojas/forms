# Acne Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acne Questionnaire is a tool for healthcare professionals to gather information from patients about their acne condition. It is a detailed form used to gather medical history, potential triggers, and preferences for treatment, helping healthcare providers offer personalized guidance and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in (marked with *).
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient name** (`Patient name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your full name, which will be included in your medical records.
- **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth, which helps the healthcare provider understand your medical history.
- **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, text, optional): This is a contact number for appointment reminders and updates.
- **Email address** (`Email address`, email, optional): Provide an email address to receive any necessary information or updates from your healthcare provider.
- **Age when acne began** (`Age when acne began`, number, optional): If you know the approximate age you started experiencing acne, please enter that here.
- **Acne severity** (`Acne severity`, select_one, required): Select the level of severity that best describes your acne: Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
- **Areas affected** (`Areas affected`, select_multiple, required): Choose all areas where you experience acne: Face, Back, Chest, or Shoulders.
- **Current treatments** (`Current treatments`, text, optional): List any topical or oral medications you currently use for acne treatment.
- **Past treatments tried** (`Past treatments tried`, text, optional): Describe any treatments that were ineffective or discontinued.
- **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies, especially to drugs or skin products, please mention them here.
- **Hormonal symptoms** (`Hormonal symptoms`, text, optional): If you experience irregular periods, hirsutism, or other hormone-related issues, describe them here.
- **Family history of acne** (`Family history of acne`, select_one, optional): If any family members have severe acne, indicate that here.
- **Lifestyle factors** (`Lifestyle factors`, text, optional): Share any relevant lifestyle factors that might be contributing to your acne, such as diet, sleep, or exercise habits.
- **Treatment goals** (`Treatment goals`, text, optional): Specify the outcomes you hope to achieve with treatment.
- **Consent to treatment** (`Consent to treatment`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to the proposed treatments.
- **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Provide any further information or thoughts you feel are important for your healthcare provider.

Please remember, the information you provide will help your healthcare provider understand your condition better and provide personalized care.
