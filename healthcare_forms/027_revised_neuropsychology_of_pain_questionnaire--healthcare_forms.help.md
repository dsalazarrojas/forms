# Revised Neuropsychology Of Pain Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Revised Neuropsychology Of Pain Questionnaire is designed to help healthcare professionals understand your pain experience, including its location, intensity, frequency, and duration. This information will aid in tailoring a personalized treatment plan to address your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your best understanding.
2. Enter your age in the "How old are you?" field.
3. Select the correct answer for your occupation from the provided options.
4. Enter your height and weight in their respective fields.
5. Enter the date of your last visit to a healthcare professional, if applicable.
6. Choose your marital status from the provided options.
7. Write a brief description of your pain symptoms in the "What are your pain symptoms?" field.
8. Select the correct location of your pain from the provided options.
9. Choose the frequency with which you experience pain from the provided options.
10. Select the intensity of your pain from the provided options.
11. Select the duration of your pain from the provided options.
12. Enter the date of your last pain episode.
13. Enter the time of your last pain episode.
14. Select the reason for your current visit from the provided options.
15. Choose your medical history from the provided options.
16. Select your smoking habits from the provided options.
17. Enter your age.
18. Enter the date of your last visit to a healthcare professional, if applicable.
19. Enter the time of your last visit to a healthcare professional, if applicable.
20. Enter the time you spend outside, if applicable.
21. Enter the time you spend outside, if applicable.
22. Describe your education level.
23. Describe your income brackets.
24. Describe your family history.
25. Answer any additional questions as instructed by a healthcare professional, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How old are you?** (`question_1`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter your age in years.
* **What is your occupation?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select the description of your job or occupation from the provided options.
* **What is your height?** (`question_3`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **What is your weight?** (`question_4`, `number`, required/optional)
	+ Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **What is your marital status?** (`question_6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)
	+ Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **What are your pain symptoms?** (`question_7`, `note`, required/optional)
	+ Write a brief description of your pain symptoms, including their duration, intensity, and any other relevant details.
* **What is your pain location?** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select where you feel pain from the provided options.
* **What is your pain frequency?** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Choose how often you experience pain from the provided options.
* **What is your pain intensity?** (`question_10`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Select how intense your pain is from the provided options.
* **What is your pain duration?** (`question_11`, `select_one`, required/optional)
	+ Choose how long you experience pain from the provided options.
* **Date of last visit** (`question_12`, `date`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the date of your last visit to a healthcare professional, if applicable.
* **Time of last visit** (`question_13`, `time`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the time of your last visit to a healthcare professional, if applicable.
* **How much time do you spend outside?** (`question_14`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the amount of time you spend outside, if applicable.
* **How much time do you spend outside?** (`question_15`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Enter the amount of time you spend outside, if applicable.
* **What are your education levels?** (`question_16`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Describe your educational background.
* **What are your income brackets?** (`question_17`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Describe your income level.
* **What is your family history?** (`question_18`, `text`, required/optional)
	+ Describe your family history, including any relevant medical conditions.
* **What do you smoke?** (`question_6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional)
	+ Select your smoking habits from the provided options.
