# Mental Health Counseling Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to schedule a mental health counseling session. It gathers details such as user contact information, preferred session length and frequency, and appointment dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **User ID** (a unique identifier for the user).
2. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** (a valid phone number).
3. Enter your **Email** (a valid email address).
4. Enter your **Address** (a physical address where the session will be conducted).
5. Select your **Session Length** (e.g., 30, 60, or 90 minutes).
6. Choose your desired **Session Frequency** (e.g., once a week, every two weeks, or as needed).
7. Select your preferred **Session Date** for the appointment.
8. Choose your preferred **Session Time** for the appointment.
9. If necessary, enter additional **Session Notes** for the mental health professional to review.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
