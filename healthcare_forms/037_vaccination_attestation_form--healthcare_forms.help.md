# Vaccination Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help healthcare practitioners collect information from patients about their vaccination history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare practitioner for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): This is the patient's full name, including any middle names or initials.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required/optional): This is the patient's date of birth.
* **Vaccine Name** (`vaccine_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the vaccine the patient has received, if any.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required/optional): The patient's email address, if they have one.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the healthcare practitioner who administered the vaccination, if any.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, required/optional): The date the patient was vaccinated, if any.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any information, please contact your healthcare practitioner for guidance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to fill out this form multiple times for the same patient, please ensure that the information is updated and accurate each time.
