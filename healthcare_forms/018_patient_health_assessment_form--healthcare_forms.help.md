# patient_health_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information about a patient's medical status, including their medical history, current medications, and contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your medical history in the **Medical History** field. This section is used to collect information about your past health experiences.
2. List your current **Medications** in this field. Include the names and dosages of any medications you are currently taking.
3. Select all **Medical Conditions** that apply to you from the provided options.
4. Enter your **Contact Information** in the format "Name", "Phone Number", and "Address". This information is used for emergency purposes.
5. If you have any **Allergies**, list them in this field. Include any reactions or symptoms you have experienced due to these allergens.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of your medical history. Try to be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise and relevant to your case.
### Current Medications
* **Current Medications** (`text`, required: false): List the names and dosages of any medications you are currently taking. Include the prescribed dosage and frequency for each medication.
### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all medical conditions that apply to you. You can select multiple options if you have multiple conditions.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name, phone number, and address for emergency contact purposes. Use the format "Name", "Phone Number", and "Address".
### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`text`, required: false): List any allergies you have, including any reactions or symptoms you have experienced due to these allergens.
