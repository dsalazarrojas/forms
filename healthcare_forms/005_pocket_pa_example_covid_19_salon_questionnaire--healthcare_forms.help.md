# pocket_pa_example_covid_19_salon_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a salon's COVID-19 related data. It is used to record details about the salon's address, contact person, and other relevant health information. This form will likely be used for contact tracing and reporting COVID-19 cases in a salon setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the salon's address in the **salon_address** field.
2. Provide the salon's phone number in the **salon_phone** field.
3. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the salon in the **email** field.
4. Enter the name of the owner of the salon in the **owner_name** field.
5. Describe the relationship between the owner and other employees in the **owner_relationship** field.
6. List the names of all employees in the **employee_names** field.
7. Describe the relationships between employees in the **employee_relationships** field.
8. Identify the contact person for the salon in the **contact_person** field.
9. Select the date of the incident in the **date** field.
10. Choose the time of the incident in the **time** field.
11. Describe any symptoms the salon staff or patrons have experienced in the **symptoms** field.
12. Enter any relevant medical notes about the incident in the **medical_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **salon_address** (text, required: false): Enter the address of the salon where the COVID-19 incident occurred.
* **salon_phone** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the salon where the incident occurred.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the salon.
* **owner_name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the owner of the salon.
* **owner_relationship** (text, required: false): Describe the relationship between the owner and other employees.
* **employee_names** (text, required: false): List the names of all employees at the salon.
* **employee_relationships** (text, required: false): Describe the relationships between employees.
* **contact_person** (text, required: false): Identify the contact person for the salon.
* **date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the incident.
* **time** (time, required: false): Choose the time of the incident.
* **symptoms** (text, required: false): Describe any symptoms experienced by the salon staff or patrons.
* **medical_notes** (text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical notes about the incident.
