# f45 Greatwood Par Q Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The f45 Greatwood Par Q Form is used to gather information from patients or individuals who have been referred to the Greatwood healthcare facility. This form helps our medical team understand your personal details, medical history, and age to provide you with the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
2. If you wish to provide your age, enter it in the corresponding field. Note that this field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, **required**): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Age** (`Age`, number, **optional**): Enter your age in years.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, **optional**): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may affect your care.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the First Name field.
* If you are unsure about your age, you can consult your official documents or ask a family member for assistance.
* If you have any medical concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to ask our medical team for guidance.
