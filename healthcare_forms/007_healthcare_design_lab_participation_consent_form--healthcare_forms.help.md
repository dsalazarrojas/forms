# Healthcare Design Lab Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in the healthcare design lab. It collects essential information, including contact details, medical history, and signature for legal validation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Complete each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Sign and date the form when indicated to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant participant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact details to facilitate communication and follow-up.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional): Describe the study you are participating in, including its purpose and objectives.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Confirm your understanding of the study's scope, risks, and benefits.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, optional): Define your relationship with the study's contact person (e.g., primary investigator, sponsor, or collaborator).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Document your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Choose a communication method for the study team to contact you (e.g., email, phone, or mail).
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your participation and understanding of the study.
* **Study Coordinator Signature** (`study_coordinator_signature`, text, optional): Sign your title and name as the study coordinator.
* **Study Coordinator Title** (`study_coordinator_title`, text, optional): Enter your title and name as the study coordinator.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Record the date you signed the form.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's essential to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and valid data.
