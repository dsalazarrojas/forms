# CNA Unit Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CNA Unit Completion Form is used to document your completion of a shift as a Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA). This form helps healthcare organizations gather information on your performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by entering your CNA Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or employee records.
2. **Enter your Employee ID**: Provide your unique employee ID assigned by the healthcare organization.
3. **Select Shift Date**: Enter the date of the shift you completed.
4. **Select Shift Time**: Choose the shift time you worked, using the options provided (e.g., Day Shift - 7am to 3pm, Evening Shift - 3pm to 11pm, Night Shift - 11pm to 7am).
5. **Enter Unit Name**: Type the name of the healthcare unit or department you worked in.
6. **Enter Supervisor's Name**: Provide the name of the RN supervisor on duty.
7. **Report Number of Patients Assigned**: If applicable, enter the number of patients you were assigned to care for during the shift. (Note: This field is only required for certain shifts.)
8. **Document Vital Signs Monitoring**: Indicate whether vital signs were monitored for all patients during the shift (Yes, No, Partially, or N/A).
9. **Complete Bathing and Personal Hygiene Tasks**: Indicate whether all patient hygiene tasks were completed (Completed, Partially Completed, Deferred, or N/A).
10. **Complete Toileting and Incontinence Care**: Indicate whether all toileting needs were addressed (Completed, Partially Completed, Deferred, or N/A).
11. **Provide Ambulation and Movement Assistance**: Indicate whether all ambulation and movement assistance needs were met (Completed, Partially Completed, Deferred, or N/A).
12. **Offer Meal Assistance and Feeding**: Indicate whether all meal assistance and feeding needs were met (Completed, Partially Completed, Deferred, or N/A).
13. **Provide Comfort Measures**: Indicate whether patients were made comfortable (Yes, Partially, No, or N/A).
14. **Report Incidents**: If any incidents occurred during the shift, select the type of incident (No incidents, Minor incident, Significant incident, or Serious incident). If yes, describe the incident in the next field.
15. **Describe Incidents (if applicable)**: Provide a brief description of any incidents that occurred during the shift.
16. **Report Equipment or Supply Issues**: If any equipment or supply issues arose during the shift, describe them in this field.
17. **Supervisor Comments or Observations**: Provide feedback or comments from your supervisor.
18. **Shift Completion Time**: Enter the time you completed your shift.
19. **Handoff Communication**: Indicate whether you communicated with the next shift staff (Yes, No, or Partial).
20. **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date this form is being completed.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If unsure about any field, check with your supervisor or healthcare organization's policies.
* Double-check for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you experience any issues while filling out this form, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
