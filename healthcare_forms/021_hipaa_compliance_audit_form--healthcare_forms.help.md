# HIPAA Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Compliance Audit Form is designed for healthcare professionals to collect and document information for auditing HIPAA compliance. This form helps ensure that all relevant patient and audit information is accurately and comprehensively recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions 1-4.
3. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and address in the respective fields.
4. Enter the medical record number.
5. Select or enter the audit date and time.
6. Provide the name, email, and phone number of the compliance officer performing the audit.
7. Provide the name and phone number of the auditor conducting the review.
8. Enter any relevant audit results or notes in the "Audit Results" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (Select Multiple): Please select "Yes" if the patient's information is up-to-date, and "No" otherwise.
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
- **Patient Date of Birth (DOB)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address as it appears on their medical record.
- **Medical Record Number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the patient's medical record.
- **Audit Date**: Select the date of the audit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Audit Time**: Select the time of the audit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Compliance Officer Name**: Enter the name of the compliance officer performing the audit.
- **Compliance Officer Email**: Enter the email address of the compliance officer.
- **Compliance Officer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the compliance officer.
- **Auditor Name**: Enter the name of the auditor conducting the review.
- **Auditor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the auditor.
- **Audit Results**: Enter any relevant results or notes from the audit process.
