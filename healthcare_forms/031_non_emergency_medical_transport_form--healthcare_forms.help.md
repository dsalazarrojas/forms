# non-emergency-medical-transport-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for non-emergency medical transport to collect relevant information about the patient, transporters, and healthcare providers. It is essential to ensure accurate and efficient transportations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Be accurate in filling out the patient's medical condition.
3. Enter the correct contact phone number for the medical transporter and healthcare provider.
4. Double-check the address, city, state, and zip code for both the patient and healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, \*): Enter the name of the patient being transported.
* **Medical Transporter Name** (`medical_transporter_name`, Text, \*): Enter the name of the medical transporter responsible for the transport.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, \*): Enter the phone number of the medical transporter or person in charge.
* **Transport Date** (`transport_date`, Date): Enter the date of the transport.
*   **Transport Time** (`transport_time`, Time): Enter the time of the transport.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, \*): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
*   **Transport Vehicle** (`transport_vehicle`, Text, \*): Enter the type of vehicle used for transport.
*   **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, Text, \*): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, Text, \*): Enter the patient's medical record number.
*   **Address** (`address`, Text, \*): Enter the address of the patient's location.
*   **City** (`city`, Text, \*): Enter the city where the patient's location is.
*   **State** (`state`, Text, \*): Enter the state where the patient's location is.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, Text, \*): Enter the zip code of the patient's location.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, Text): Enter any additional comments related to the transport.
*   **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, Text, \*): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
*   **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, Text): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider. 
*   **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, Text, \*): Enter the address of the healthcare provider.
*   **Medical Provider State** (`medical_provider_state`, Text, \*): Enter the state of the healthcare provider.
*   **Medical Provider City** (`medical_provider_city`, Text, \*): Enter the city of the healthcare provider.
*   **Medical Provider Zip** (`medical_provider_zip`, Text, \*): Enter the zip code of the healthcare provider.
*   **Medical Transporter Address** (`medical_transporter_address`, Text, \*): Enter the address of the medical transporters.
*   **Medical Transporter State** (`medical_transporter_state`, Text, \*): Enter the state of the medical transporters.
*   **Medical Transporter City** (`medical_transporter_city`, Text, \*): Enter the city of the medical transporters.
*   **Medical Transporter Zip** (`medical_transporter_zip`, Text, \*): Enter the zip code of the medical transporters.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, Text): Enter any additional notes related to the transport. 

Note: Ensure all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are filled in accurately to ensure the form is completed correctly.
