# Childhood Development Screening Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to evaluate a child's development skills, specifically in the areas of gross motor, fine motor, communication and language, social-emotional development, and overall health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the required fields**: Child Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Age at Screening, and Examiner Name.
2. **Answer the questions about the child's abilities**: In the sections for Gross Motor, Fine Motor, Communication and Language, and Social and Emotional Development, answer each question based on the child's performance.
3. **Provide your observations and recommendations**: In the Screeners Observations and Recommendations section, write down any notable observations and recommendations you have for the child's development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Child Development Screening --**:
	+ This section serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Child Name** (`Child Name`, text, **required**):
	+ Enter the child's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, **required**):
	+ Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Select the child's gender (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
* **Age at Screening - months** (`Age at Screening - months`, number, **required**):
	+ Enter the child's age in months.
* **-- Gross Motor Skills --**:
	+ This section assesses the child's physical abilities.
* **Walks independently and steadily** (`Walks independently and steadily`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child can walk independently.
* **Climbs stairs with assistance** (`Climbs stairs with assistance`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child can climb stairs with assistance.
* **-- Fine Motor Skills --**:
	+ This section assesses the child's fine motor abilities.
* **Uses a spoon or fork with some success** (`Uses a spoon or fork with some success`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child can use utensils with some success.
* **Stacks two or more blocks** (`Stacks two or more blocks`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child can stack two or more blocks.
* **-- Communication and Language --**:
	+ This section assesses the child's verbal and non-verbal communication skills.
* **Approximately how many single words does the child use?** (`Approximately how many single words does the child use?`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Select the number of single words the child uses (0-5, 6-20, 21-50, More than 50).
* **Follows simple one-step instructions** (`Follows simple one-step instructions`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child can follow simple one-step instructions.
* **-- Social and Emotional Development --**:
	+ This section assesses the child's social and emotional abilities.
* **Shows affection to familiar people** (`Shows affection to familiar people`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child shows affection to familiar people.
* **Plays near other children - parallel play** (`Plays near other children - parallel play`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if the child plays near other children.
* **Screeners Observations and Recommendations** (`Screeners Observations and Recommendations`, text, **required**):
	+ Write down any notable observations and recommendations.
* **Is a follow-up evaluation recommended?** (`Is a follow-up evaluation recommended?`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Check if a follow-up evaluation is recommended.
* **Examiner Name** (`Examiner Name`, text, **required**):
	+ Enter the examiner's name.
* **Screening Date** (`Screening Date`, date, **required**):
	+ Enter the screening date.

Note: The required fields are marked as **required**. Please fill out all required fields to complete the form.
