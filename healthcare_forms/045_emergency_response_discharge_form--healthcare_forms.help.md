# emergency_response_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency response discharge form is used to document the details of an emergency response event in a hospital or healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date and time of discharge for the patient.
2. Enter the reason for discharge, including any relevant medical information.
3. Record any relevant diagnosis or treatment notes.
4. Enter any comments or additional information about the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_response_discharge_form_section_1** (`emergency_response_discharge_form_section_1`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the emergency response event.
* **discharge_date** (`discharge_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of discharge.
* **discharge_time** (`discharge_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of discharge.
* **time_hhmm** (`time_hhmm`, Time, Optional): Enter the exact time of discharge in 24-hour format (e.g. 14:30).
* **discharge_reason** (`discharge_reason`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason for discharge, including any relevant medical information.
* **discharge_diagnosis** (`discharge_diagnosis`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant diagnosis or treatment notes.
* **discharge_treatment** (`discharge_treatment`, Text, Optional): Enter any treatment or medications administered during the emergency response.
* **discharge_comments** (`discharge_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's care.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and detailed information about the emergency response event.
* Use clear and concise language when completing the form.
* If unsure about any field, leave it blank and consult with a healthcare professional.
