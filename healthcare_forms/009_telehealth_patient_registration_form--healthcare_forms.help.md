# Telehealth Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Patient Registration Form is a necessary step to complete before your telehealth consultation. It helps our healthcare provider to gather essential information about you and your medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information.
- Use the hints provided as a guide to help you complete each field.
- If you are unsure about any information, please consult your medical records or speak with your primary care provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, optional): If you have medical insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Primary Care Provider** (`primary_care_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a primary care provider.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and relationship to the next of kin.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Share any relevant medical history.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are allergic to.
* **Telehealth Agreement** (`telehealth_agreement`, `note`, optional): Read and sign this section to confirm you understand the terms of telehealth services.
* **Patient Agreement** (`patient_agreement`, `note`, optional): Read and sign this section to confirm you understand your rights and responsibilities as a patient.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you consent to the terms of the consent form.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your condition or treatment.
* **Primary Care Provider Notes** (`primary_care_provider_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your primary care provider.
* **Next of Kin Notes** (`next_of_kin_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your next of kin.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Medication Allergies Notes** (`medication_allergies_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your medication allergies.
* **Telehealth Agreement Notes** (`telehealth_agreement_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the telehealth agreement.
* **Patient Agreement Notes** (`patient_agreement_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your patient agreement.
* **Consent Form Notes** (`consent_form_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the consent form.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML structure and is intended to be a general help guide. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please contact our staff for assistance.
