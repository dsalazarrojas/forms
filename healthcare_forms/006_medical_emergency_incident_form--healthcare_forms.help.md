# medical-emergency-incident-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Medical-Emergency-Incident-Form" is a tool designed to collect vital information about medical emergencies or incidents. It is crucial to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure timely and effective medical response.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering the basic emergency information on page 1, providing a brief description of the incident.
- Move on to page 2, "patient info" and enter the patient's details such as name, age, and any relevant medical history.
- Proceed to page 3, "incident details" and document the incident's specifics, including the location, date, and time.
- Continue to page 4, "medical history" and document any previous medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
- On page 5, "treatment procedure" describe the actions taken to treat the patient.
- On page 6, "patient response" ask the patient how they are feeling and what symptoms they are experiencing.
- Finally, on page 7, "witness info" provide any information about witnesses or bystanders that may be relevant to the incident.
- On the last page 8, "medical records" enter any relevant medical records, such as medications or test results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Info** (`emergency_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, age, and any relevant medical history.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required/optional): Document the incident's specifics, including location, date, and time.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Document any previous medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
* **Treatment** (`treatment_procedure`, text, required/optional): Describe the actions taken to treat the patient.
* **Patient Response** (`patient_response`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient how they are feeling and what symptoms they are experiencing.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any information about witnesses or bystanders that may be relevant to the incident.
* **Medical Records** (`medical_records`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical records, such as medications or test results.

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as possible in the form fields.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
- If unsure about any information, please seek medical guidance or advice.
- This form is a general tool and may not cover every specific situation.
