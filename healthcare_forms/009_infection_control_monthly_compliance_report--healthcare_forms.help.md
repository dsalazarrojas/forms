# Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report is a document used to monitor and control infections in healthcare settings. This report tracks incidents, including their location, status, and follow-up dates, ensuring that patients receive the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the incident reporting page.
- Complete the required fields, including Date of Incident, Facility Identifier, Description of Incident, Incident Location, and Reviewer Name.
- For the Reviewer Comments field, provide a detailed explanation of the incident and any necessary actions or follow-up.
- Select the appropriate incident status and follow-up dates.
- Finally, sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Reporting** (Text)
	+ A field to report incidents of infections, including their location and description.
* **Reviewer Oversight** (Text, Optional)
	+ A field for reviewers to provide additional comments or oversight on reported incidents.
* **Date of Incident** (Date)
	+ The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Facility Identifier** (Select Multiple)
	+ The facility where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (Text)
	+ A detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (Select One)
	+ The location where the incident occurred.
* **Reviewer Name** (Text)
	+ The name of the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (Date)
	+ The date on which the reviewer reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Note)
	+ Additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Form Submitted By** (Select One)
	+ The person who submitted the form.
* **Facility Name** (Text)
	+ The name of the facility.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Select Multiple)
	+ The signature of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (Select One)
	+ The title of the reviewer.
* **Date Completed** (Date)
	+ The date on which the form was completed.
* **Reviewer Name 2** (Text)
	+ The name of the second reviewer.
* **Date Reviewed** (Date)
	+ The date on which the reviewer reviewed the incident.
* **Incident Status** (Select Multiple)
	+ The status of the incident.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date)
	+ The date on which the follow-up was performed.
* **Follow-up Status** (Select One)
	+ The status of the follow-up.
* **Incident Report** (Select Multiple)
	+ A field to select the incident report.
* **Reviewer Note** (Note)
	+ Additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Note 2** (Note)
	+ Additional comments from the second reviewer.
* **Date Submit** (Date)
	+ The date on which the form was submitted.
* **Follow-up Note** (Note, Optional)
	+ Additional comments from the follow-up reviewer.
* **Submitter Name** (Text)
	+ The name of the person who submitted the form.
