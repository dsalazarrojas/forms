# Initial Psychotherapy Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Psychotherapy Consultation Form is used to gather information about the client's medical history, chief complaints, and other relevant details during the initial consultation with a mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and category.
2. Provide your name as the client would like to be addressed.
3. Enter your contact number as the client would like to be contacted.
4. Enter your email address (if applicable).
5. Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, tests, or procedures you have undergone.
6. Specify your chief complaints that brought you here today.
7. List any medications you are currently taking.
8. List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
9. Select any medical conditions you have or have had.
10. Select any medical tests you have undergone or are scheduled to undergo.
11. Select any medical procedures you have undergone or are scheduled to undergo.
12. Provide comments for medical history, chief complaints, medications, medical conditions, medical tests, and medical procedures (if applicable).
13. Sign the form with your signature.
14. Enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Initial Psychotherapy Consultation Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **category**: Select the category of this form (e.g., "Healthcare Forms").
* **description**: Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, tests, or procedures you have undergone.
* **client name**: Provide your name as the client would like to be addressed.
* **contact number**: Enter your contact number as the client would like to be contacted.
* **email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **client_dob**: Enter your date of birth.
* **client_signature**: Sign the form with your signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your medical history and chief complaints.
* If you have any medical conditions, tests, or procedures, please list them accurately.
* If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them accurately.
* If you have any comments about your medical history, chief complaints, medications, medical conditions, medical tests, or medical procedures, please provide them in the comments section.
* Please sign the form with your signature to confirm your identity.
