# Pre Op Anesthetic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Op Anesthetic Assessment Form is designed to assess the patient's medical history, allergies, and potential risks associated with anesthesia before undergoing surgical procedures. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential information to ensure a safe and successful operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. For each field, select the most accurate option from the available choices (for select fields) or enter a brief description (for text fields).
3. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's condition.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information** (`patient_information`)
*   **Patient Information (Text Field)**: Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition, relevant allergies, or any other relevant medical history.

### 2. **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`)
*   **Have the patient undergone any medical condition that may affect the anesthesia?** (`Select One` field)
*   Options: Yes / No

### 3. **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`)
*   **List the patient's allergies that may affect the anesthesia** (`Select Multiple` field)
*   Options: Yes / No

### 4. **Anesthetic History** (`anesthetic_history`)
*   **Provide a description of the patient's anesthetic history, including previous surgeries and medical conditions that may affect anesthesia.**

### 5. **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`)
*   **List any previous surgeries or medical conditions that may impact the upcoming procedure.** (`Select Multiple` field)
*   Options: Yes / No

### 6. **Medication Information** (`medication_info`)
*   **Describe any medications currently being taken by the patient.**

### 7. **Lab Results** (`lab_results`)
*   **Enter any relevant lab results that may impact the patient's condition.**

### 8. **Anesthesia Risks** (`anesthesia_risks`)
*   **Describe any potential risks associated with anesthesia.**

### 9. **Pre-op Order** (`pre_op_order`)
*   **Has the patient undergone any pre-operative order?** (`Select One` field)
*   Options: Yes / No

### 10. **Other Risks** (`other_risks`)
*   **Describe any other potential risks or concerns.**
