# Patient Consent Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate patient consent for medical procedures. The form is designed to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the procedures they are being offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate answer for each question.
2. Make sure to answer all fields that are marked as "required".
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Consent Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Consent** (`select_one`, **patient_consent_validation_form_page_1** ,Required/Optional: Optional):
	+ Please choose whether you consent to the medical procedure by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Procedures** (`select_multiple`, **patient_consent_validation_form_page_2** ,Required/Optional: Optional):
	+ Select all procedures you consent to. You can select multiple procedures if applicable.
* **Consent Date** (`date`, **patient_consent_validation_form_page_3** ,Required/Optional: Optional):
	+ Enter the date you consented to the medical procedures.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields truthfully to ensure the validation process is completed successfully.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
