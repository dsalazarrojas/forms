# mental_health_treatment_plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health treatment plan form is a tool used to create a tailored treatment plan for patients dealing with mental health issues. It helps healthcare professionals gather essential information about the patient's goals, strategies, and progress tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

* Review the form's purpose and ensure you have the necessary information and authorization to fill it out.
* Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Use the provided options for select fields to make informed choices.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, `note`, not required): This section is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about the patient's situation.
* **Therapeutic Goals** (`Therapeutic Goals`, `select_one`, not required): Select one or more goals for the treatment, such as improving mental health, managing symptoms, or increasing coping skills. Choose the option that best describes the focus of the treatment.
* **Strategies** (`Strategies`, `text`, not required): Describe the strategies and approaches you plan to use to achieve the selected goals. Be specific and provide detailed information.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the treatment options that apply to the patient's situation, such as medication, therapy, or lifestyle changes. Select all relevant options.
* **Progress Tracking** (`Progress Tracking`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you'll use to track the patient's progress and monitor their treatment's effectiveness.
* **Mental Status** (`Mental Status`, `number`, not required): This field is intended for numerical data entry, such as scores or ratings, to assess the patient's mental health status. Please enter a number as required.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`Treatment Start Date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the treatment started or is planned to start.
* **Treatment End Date** (`Treatment End Date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the treatment is expected to end or has ended.
