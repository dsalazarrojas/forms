# Patient Discharge Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Discharge Experience Questionnaire is a healthcare form used to gather important information about patients at the time of discharge from a healthcare facility. This form will help healthcare providers evaluate and document the patient's discharge experience, ensuring accurate records and future care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and access to complete this form.
2. Complete the form accurately and consistently, filling out all required fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction**: This page serves as a placeholder for any necessary background information or context for the form. Please add any relevant details that will aid in understanding the patient's discharge experience.

### Page 2: Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required/optional): Record the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Hospital ID** (`Hospital ID`, number, required/optional): Enter the hospital's identifying number for the patient.
* **Discharge Date** (`Discharge Date`, date, required/optional): Record the date the patient was discharged from the healthcare facility.
* **Discharge Location** (`Location of Discharge`, text, required/optional): Specify the location where the patient was discharged from, such as a room number or department.
* **Discharge Time** (`Discharge Time`, time, required/optional): Record the time the patient was discharged from the healthcare facility.
* **Discharge Status** (`Discharge Status`, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the patient's discharge, using one of the following options: Complete, Incomplete, or Pending.
* **Outcome Prophets** (`Outcome prophets`, note, required/optional): Record any significant events or outcomes experienced by the patient during their stay or at the time of discharge.

### Discharge Status Explanation
When selecting the discharge status, consider the following:
*   Complete: The patient was fully discharged with no outstanding medical conditions or treatments.
*   Incomplete: The patient's discharge was incomplete due to remaining medical conditions or further treatment needs.
*   Pending: The patient's discharge was temporarily halted due to administrative or logistical reasons, pending further action or evaluation.

### Tips
*   Always verify the patient's identity before filling out this form.
*   Review the patient's medical history for any relevant details that may impact their discharge experience.
*   Ensure accurate and consistent information is entered for each field.
*   Consult with healthcare providers if you're unsure about any aspect of the form.
*   Keep the form accurate and up-to-date for future reference and planning.
