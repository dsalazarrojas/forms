# Suicide Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suicide Risk Assessment Form is designed to help identify individuals who may be at risk of suicide. Please complete the form if you are experiencing suicidal thoughts, feelings, or engaging in risk-taking behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read all instructions carefully** before starting.
- Answer all questions as accurately as possible.
- **Do not skip any questions**.
- **Use the text fields to provide detailed answers**.
- **Submit the form only once you have completed it in its entirety**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suicidal Thoughts** (`suicidal_thoughts`, text, optional): This field is designed to gather information about any thoughts you may be having about hurting yourself or others. Please answer this question in detail, providing as much information as possible.
* **Feelings** (`feelings`, text, optional): This field is meant to help us understand your current emotional state. Please describe any feelings you are experiencing, such as sadness, hopelessness, or irritability.
* **Behaviors** (`behaviors`, text, optional): This field is intended to collect information about any behaviors you may be engaging in that could potentially increase your risk of suicide, such as substance abuse or self-harming activities. Please provide specific details about your behaviors.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, text, optional): This field is designed to gather any risk factors you may have that could contribute to your suicide risk, such as financial difficulties or social isolation. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Protective Factors** (`protective_factors`, text, optional): This field is meant to understand any protective factors you have that may help mitigate your suicide risk, such as support from loved ones or a positive mental health history. Please describe these factors in detail.

## Tips
- **Please answer honestly and thoroughly**.
- **If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to a mental health professional**.
- **This form is for your personal assessment and is not intended for public sharing**.
- **Please do not fill out this form if you are under 18 or if you are in crisis**.
