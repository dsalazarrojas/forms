# emergency_veterinary_referral_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about pets that require emergency veterinary care. It helps ensure that the pet receives prompt and appropriate attention from a veterinarian.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the pet that requires emergency care.
2. Provide the owner's name and contact information (phone number and email).
3. Specify the species of the pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
4. Describe the reason for the referral (e.g., injury, illness, etc.).
5. Provide any relevant medical history or medication information.
6. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who referred the pet.
7. Enter the address of the clinic where the pet will be receiving care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet that requires emergency care.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Pet Species** (`pet_species`, text, required): Specify the species of the pet (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, required): Describe the reason for the referral (e.g., injury, illness, etc.).
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Veterinarian Information** (`veterinarian_info`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian who referred the pet.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Provide any relevant medical history or medication information.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name of the veterinarian who referred the pet.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the clinic where the pet will be receiving care.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the pet receives the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the veterinarian who referred the pet for guidance.
* This form should only be used for emergency veterinary referrals, and not for routine or follow-up care.
