# Medical Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Request Form is used to request access to a patient's medical records from a healthcare provider or facility. This form is typically used when a patient needs to access their medical records for personal reasons, such as for insurance purposes or to share medical information with a new healthcare provider. The form requires patient and provider/facility information, as well as the reason for the request. The form will be sent to the relevant parties and they will sign and date it to acknowledge the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record in the `Patient Name` field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field.
3. Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility in the `Name of Provider/Facility` field.
4. Enter the name of the facility where the patient received treatment in the `Name of Facility` field.
5. Enter the reason for requesting the form in the `Reason for Request` field.
6. If applicable, enter the patient's email address in the `Patient Email` field.
7. If applicable, enter the patient's phone number in the `Patient Phone` field.
8. If applicable, enter the provider's email address in the `Provider Email` field.
9. If applicable, enter the provider's phone number in the `Provider Phone` field.
10. If applicable, enter the facility's email address in the `Facility Email` field.
11. If applicable, enter the facility's phone number in the `Facility Phone` field.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the relevant parties for signature and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD format.
* **Name of Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility treating the patient.
* **Name of Facility**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the patient received treatment.
* **Reason for Request**: Enter a brief description of the reason for requesting the form.
* **Patient Signature**: A signature from the patient is required to authenticate the request.
* **Date Completed**: The date the form was completed by the patient.
* **Provider Signature**: A signature from the healthcare provider or facility is required to authenticate the request.
* **Date Sent**: The date the form was sent to the healthcare provider or facility.
* **Facility Signature**: A signature from the healthcare facility is required to authenticate the request.
* **Patient Email**: If the patient has an email address, enter it here.
* **Provider Email**: If the healthcare provider has an email address, enter it here.
* **Facility Email**: If the healthcare facility has an email address, enter it here.
* **Patient Phone**: If the patient has a phone number, enter it here.
* **Provider Phone**: If the healthcare provider has a phone number, enter it here.
* **Facility Phone**: If the healthcare facility has a phone number, enter it here.

Please note that some fields are not required for this form, and you may skip them if they are not applicable.
