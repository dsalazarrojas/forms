# Health Insurance 2020 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health insurance information for a patient. It is used to gather essential details about the patient's name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email. This information is crucial for health insurance claims and billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the patient's email in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their official documents, such as ID cards or passports.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address in the format "Street Number, City, State ZIP Code" (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, NY 12345).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address in the format "example@example.com".
