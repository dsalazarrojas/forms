# Personal Care Assistant Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Care Assistant Authorization Form is used to gather information from families and caregivers about their loved ones' medical conditions, care requirements, and emergency contacts. This information enables healthcare providers to offer personalized care services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your family name in the `Family Name` field (ID: 1).
2. Enter the name of the care provider (ID: 2).
3. Specify the caregiver's title (ID: 3) from the provided options (Adminstrator, Caregiver, or Nurse).
4. Fill in the date of birth (ID: 4).
5. Enter the start and end dates of service (ID: 5 and ID: 6, respectively).
6. Provide details about the frequency of service (ID: 7).
7. Enter the service details (ID: 8).
8. Fill in the name and relationship of the emergency contact (ID: 9 and ID: 10).
9. Enter the emergency contact's phone number (ID: 11).
10. Provide an alternative emergency contact phone number (ID: 12).
11. Describe the medical condition (ID: 13).
12. Enter the medical history (ID: 14).
13. Select the authorization date and expiration date (ID: 15 and ID: 16, respectively).
14. Sign the authorized signature field (ID: 17).
15. Sign the caregiver's signature field (ID: 18).
16. Sign the care provider's signature field (ID: 19).
17. Fill in your contact phone number (ID: 20).
18. Upload a relevant document (ID: 21).

Note that fields marked as `required: false` can be left blank or filled in if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Name** (ID: 1): Enter the name of the family member or relative.
* **Care Provider Name** (ID: 2): Enter the name of the care provider.
* **Care Giver Title** (ID: 3): Select the title of the caregiver from the provided options (Adminstrator, Caregiver, or Nurse).
* **Date of Birth** (ID: 4): Enter the date of birth of the family member or relative.
* **Start Date of Service** (ID: 5): Enter the start date of the service provided.
* **End Date of Service** (ID: 6): Enter the end date of the service provided.
* **Frequency of Service** (ID: 7): Describe the frequency of service provided.
* **Service Details** (ID: 8): Enter the details of the service provided.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (ID: 9): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (ID: 10): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the family member or relative.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (ID: 11): Enter the primary emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Alt)** (ID: 12): Enter an alternative emergency contact phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (ID: 13): Describe the medical condition of the family member or relative.
* **Medical History** (ID: 14): Enter the medical history of the family member or relative.
* **Authorization Date** (ID: 15): Enter the date of authorization.
* **Authorization Expiration Date** (ID: 16): Enter the date of authorization expiration.
* **Authorized Signature** (ID: 17): Sign and date the authorized signature field.
* **Caregiver Signature** (ID: 18): Sign and date the caregiver's signature field.
* **Family Signature** (ID: 19): Sign and date the family member's signature field.
* **Care Provider Signature** (ID: 20): Sign and date the care provider's signature field.
* **Email** (ID: 21): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (ID: 22): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (ID: 23): Enter your fax number.
* **Upload Document** (ID: 24): Upload a relevant document.
* **Note** (ID: 25): Add any additional notes or comments.
