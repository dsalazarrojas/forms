# Medication Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Incident Report Form is designed to report medication incidents that occurred during patient care. This form helps healthcare professionals document and review medication incidents to ensure patient safety and quality of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient-name" field.
2. Choose a date and time for the incident using the calendar icon in the "date-of-event" field.
3. Enter the name of the medication involved in the "medication-name" field.
4. Type in the dose of the medication given in the "medication-dose" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "event-description" field.
6. List any contributing factors that led to the incident in the "contributing-factors" field.
7. Report who reported the incident in the "reported-by" field.
8. Describe the actions taken in response to the incident in the "action-taken" field.
9. List any additional actions taken to resolve the incident in the "additional-actions-taken" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient-name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name as recorded in the hospital's patient database.
* **date-of-event** (date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred using the calendar icon.
* **time-of-event** (time, required): Choose the time the incident occurred using the clock icon.
* **medication-name** (text, required): Enter the name of the medication involved in the incident.
* **medication-dose** (text, optional): Type in the dose of the medication given if known.
* **event-description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **contributing-factors** (text, optional): List any factors that contributed to the incident.
* **reported-by** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **action-taken** (text, optional): Describe the actions taken to address the incident.
* **additional-actions-taken** (text, optional): List any additional actions taken to resolve the incident.
