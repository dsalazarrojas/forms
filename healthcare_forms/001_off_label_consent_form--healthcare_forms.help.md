# off_label_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The off_label_consent_form is used to document the off-label use of medication in a clinical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are:
	* consent_form_signed_by (id: 20)
	* medication_prescribed_by (id: 21)
2. Provide the necessary information for the following fields:
	* patient_name (id: 1)
	* date_conducted (id: 2)
	* patient_signed_date (id: 3)
	* physician_signed_date (id: 4)
	* physician_signed_name (id: 5)
	* medication_name (id: 6)
	* medication_dose (id: 7)
	* medication_frequency (id: 8)
	* medication_duration (id: 9)
	* medication_reason_for_use (id: 10)
	* patient_consent (id: 11)
	* physician_consent (id: 12)
	* patient_signature (id: 13)
	* physician_signature (id: 14)
	* date_of_last_administration (id: 15)
	* last_dose_administered (id: 16)
	* next_dose_scheduled (id: 17)
	* patient_informed (id: 18)
	* physician_informed (id: 19)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (id: 1)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Date Conducted (id: 2)**: Enter the date the medication was administered.
* **Patient Signed Date (id: 3)**: Enter the date the patient signed the consent form.
* **Physician Signed Date (id: 4)**: Enter the date the physician signed the consent form.
* **Physician's Name (id: 5)**: Enter the physician's name who prescribed the medication.
* **Medication Name (id: 6)**: Enter the name of the medication being administered.
* **Medication Dose (id: 7)**: Enter the dose of the medication administered.
* **Medication Frequency (id: 8)**: Enter the frequency of medication administration.
* **Medication Duration (id: 9)**: Enter the duration of medication administration.
* **Medication Reason for Use (id: 10)**: Enter the reason for off-label use of the medication.
* **Patient Consent (id: 11)**: A field for the patient's consent statement.
* **Physician Consent (id: 12)**: A field for the physician's consent statement.
* **Patient Signature (id: 13)**: A field for the patient's signature.
* **Physician Signature (id: 14)**: A field for the physician's signature.
* **Date of Last Administration (id: 15)**: Enter the date of the last medication administration.
* **Last Dose Administered (id: 16)**: Enter the date of the last medication dose administered.
* **Next Dose Scheduled (id: 17)**: Enter the next scheduled dose date.
* **Patient Informed (id: 18)**: A field indicating if the patient was informed of the risks and benefits.
* **Physician Informed (id: 19)**: A field indicating if the physician was informed of the risks and benefits.
* **Consent Form Signed By (id: 20)**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the consent form was signed by the patient.
* **Medication Prescribed By (id: 21)**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the medication was prescribed by the physician.
* **Patient Understood (id: 22)**: Select all applicable options to indicate if the patient understands the risks and benefits of the medication.
* **Medication Administered By (id: 23)**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the medication was administered by the physician.
* **Physician Comments (id: 24)**: A field for the physician's comments.
* **Patient Comments (id: 25)**: A field for the patient's comments.
