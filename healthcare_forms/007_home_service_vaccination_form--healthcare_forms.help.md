# home_service_vaccination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home_service_vaccination_form is designed to collect patient information and details related to vaccinations provided at home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's information in the `Patient Information` page, including any relevant details that may impact vaccine administration.
2. Enter the healthcare provider's name or contact information in the `Healthcare Provider` page.
3. Enter the details of the vaccination, including type, date, and dosage, in the `Vaccination Details` page.
4. List any allergies or medical conditions that the patient may have, in the `Allergies` page.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the medical practice or clinic providing the vaccination service, in the `Medical Practice` page.
6. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the vaccination, such as medication history or medical equipment used, in the `Additional Information` page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name, birthdate, and contact information for billing or follow-up purposes.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the healthcare provider's name or contact information for billing or follow-up purposes.
* **Vaccination Details** (`vaccination_details`, text, required: false): Enter the type of vaccine administered, date and time, and dosage details, as well as any relevant medical notes.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List any known allergies or medical conditions that may impact vaccine administration.
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the medical practice or clinic providing the vaccination service.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information relevant to the vaccination, such as medication history or medical equipment used.
