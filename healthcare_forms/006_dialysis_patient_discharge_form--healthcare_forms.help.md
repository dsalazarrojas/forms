# dialysis_patient_discharge_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **dialysis patient discharge form** is used to gather information about a patient's treatment and medication during the discharge process. This form helps healthcare professionals ensure that the patient's medical needs are documented and communicated effectively to the patient and their caregivers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the patient information fields, providing details about the patient's condition, treatment, and medication.
3. Enter the medication information, including any prescribed medications and their dosages.
4. Document the treatment information, including any procedures or therapies undergone during the patient's stay.
5. Review and submit the form to ensure accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to capture essential details about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and contact information. Please ensure that this section is filled out accurately and completely.
* **Medication Info (Medication Info, text, required/optional)**: Enter the patient's current medication list, including the name, dosage, and any other relevant information about the medication.
* **Treatment Info (Treatment Info, text, required/optional)**: Describe the patient's treatment plan, including any surgeries, therapies, or procedures performed during their stay.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the patient's healthcare provider or other relevant medical staff.
