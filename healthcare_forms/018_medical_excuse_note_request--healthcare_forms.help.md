# Medical Excuse Note Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Excuse Note Request form is used to request medical notes from healthcare professionals for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please provide the reason for your request**: Fill out the "What is the reason for the request" field to specify the purpose of your request.
2. **Provide your date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. **Enter your phone number**: Fill out the "Phone Number" field with your contact information.
4. **Select the type of doctor**: Choose the type of doctor who will provide the note from the "What kind of doctor will provide the excuse note" dropdown menu.
5. **Select applicable conditions**: Choose the conditions that apply to your request from the "Which of the following conditions apply" dropdown menu.
6. **Provide a note**: Enter a brief note in the "Please provide a note" field to explain your request.
7. **Confirm authorization**: Check the "Is this request authorized" field to confirm if the request is legitimate.
8. **Enter email address**: Fill out the "Email" field with your email address.
9. **Response details**: Fill out the "Response" field with any relevant response details.
10. **To**: Enter the intended recipient of the response.
11. **Date and Time**: Fill out the "Date" and "Time" fields to schedule the response.
12. **Confirm Date and Time**: Repeat the date and time of the response in the "Confirm Date" and "Confirm Time" fields.
13. **Assign to**: Choose the user responsible for responding to your request in the "Assigned To" field.
14. **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to your request.
15. **Status**: Enter the status of your request, such as "In Progress", "Completed", or "Cancelled".
16. **Reason for Request**: Enter a brief explanation for your request in the "Reason for Request" field.
17. **Confirm Status**: Choose the current status of your request from the "Confirm Status" dropdown menu.
18. **Confirm Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your request in the "Confirm Note" field.
19. **Multiple options**: Select any additional conditions or options that apply to your request from the "Another multiple option" dropdown menu.
20. **Another option for select one**: Choose an additional option from the "Another option for select one" dropdown menu.
21. **Another text field**: Fill out the "Another text field" with any additional information.
22. **Another date field**: Enter another date in the "Another date field".
23. **Another time field**: Enter another time in the "Another time field".
