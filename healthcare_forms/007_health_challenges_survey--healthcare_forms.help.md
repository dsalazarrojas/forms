# health_challenges_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals collect information about the overall health of patients or clients. It aims to gather their health status and any existing health challenges or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in the "section one" field, which is a text field for providing additional information.
*   Next, fill in the "section two" field, another text field for more details.
*   Then, move on to the "section three" field, where you can provide further information.
*   Finally, fill in the "section four" field with any additional comments or notes.
*   After that, select your "health status" by choosing between "Active" and "Inactive".
*   If you have any health challenges, select all that apply from the "health challenges" field.
*   Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the "submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Section one** (1, text, required: false): A text field for providing additional information.
*   **Section two** (2, text, required: false): A text field for more details.
*   **Section three** (3, text, required: false): A text field for further information.
*   **Section four** (4, text, required: false): A text field for additional comments or notes.
*   **health_status** (6, select_one, required: false): A select field where you can choose between "Active" and "Inactive" status.
*   **health_status_label** (7, text, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
*   **health_challenges** (8, select_multiple, required: false): A multi-select field where you can choose multiple challenges from the list of options.
*   **health_challenges_label** (9, text, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
*   **Submit** (10, note, required: false): A note that indicates the form is ready to be submitted.
