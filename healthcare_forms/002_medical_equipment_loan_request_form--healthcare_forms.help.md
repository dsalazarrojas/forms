# Medical Equipment Loan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Equipment Loan Request Form is a standardized process to request medical equipment for a specific period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the "prophets" field, enter your name and any other relevant information.
2.  In the "loan_dates" field, select the dates when you need the equipment. You can choose multiple dates if necessary.
3.  In the "reason" field, describe the reason you need the equipment. Be as specific as possible to help the equipment manager understand your request.
4.  In the "details" field, provide additional information about your loan request, such as the quantity of equipment you need.
5.  In the "equipment_status" field, select the current status of the equipment you are requesting. This will help the equipment manager determine the availability of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **prophets**: Your name and any other relevant information to help the equipment manager understand your request.
- **loan_dates**: Select the dates when you need the equipment. You can choose multiple dates by separating them with commas.
- **reason**: Describe the reason you need the equipment. Be as specific as possible.
- **details**: Provide additional information about your loan request, such as the quantity of equipment you need.
- **equipment_status**: Select the current status of the equipment you are requesting, which will help the equipment manager determine the availability of the equipment.
