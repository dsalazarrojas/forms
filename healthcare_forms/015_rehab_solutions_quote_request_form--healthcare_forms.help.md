## # rehab_solutions_quote_request_form - Help Guide

## ## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from healthcare professionals and patients to provide personalized rehabilitation solutions. It is a multi-page form that gathers essential details such as patient and insurance information, contact details, and specific rehabilitation preferences. The form is designed to ensure that patients receive the best possible care and support for their recovery.

## ## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Patient Information** (page 1) to confirm the patient's status.
2. Fill in the required fields for **Insurance Information** (page 2) with the patient's insurance details.
3. Enter the contact person's details in **Contact Info** (page 3).
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Rehab Information** (page 4) to confirm the patient's rehabilitation needs.
5. Enter the patient's **Date of Birth** and **Date of Service** (page 5) in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Contact Method** (page 6) to choose how the patient would like to be contacted.
7. Enter the contact person's details for **Contact Person** (page 7).
8. Fill in the **Email** (page 8), **Phone** (page 9), and **Fax** (page 10) fields if applicable.
9. Enter the **Address** (page 11) of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This page confirms whether the patient is currently in the system.
* **Insurance Information**: Enter the patient's insurance details such as provider and policy number.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact person's name, title, and phone number.
* **Rehab Information**: Confirm whether the patient requires rehabilitation services.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date the patient requires rehabilitation services in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how the patient would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Fax**: Enter the contact person's fax number.
* **Address**: Enter the contact person's address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the patient's **Date of Birth** and **Date of Service** for correct formatting.
* Be aware that some fields are required, and failing to fill them in may affect the submission process.
