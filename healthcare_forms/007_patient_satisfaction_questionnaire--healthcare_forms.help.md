# Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire is a form designed to measure patient satisfaction with the healthcare services provided. This form is typically used to collect feedback from patients after they have received medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focused on a different aspect of patient satisfaction. Please complete all sections thoroughly to provide comprehensive feedback.
2. For each section, select one of the provided options that best matches your experience with the healthcare services.
3. If you have any additional comments or descriptions about your experience, please provide them in the free-text fields (e.g., "patient_satisfaction_description").
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Satisfaction** (2)
* (`patient_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to gauge how satisfied you are with your experience with the healthcare services.
	+ Select one of the following options that best describes your level of satisfaction:
		- Highly Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Highly Dissatisfied

### **Healthcare Services** (3)
* (`healthcare_services`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is a free-text field for you to provide any comments or descriptions about your experience with the healthcare services.

### **Healthcare Services Description** (4)
* (`healthcare_services_description`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is a free-text field for you to provide any additional comments or descriptions about your experience with the healthcare services.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
