# Patient Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information and medical history from patients. Please complete this form to provide us with the necessary details to better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
5. **Address**: Enter your address where you can be contacted.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
7. **Medical Condition**: Describe your medical condition.
8. **Emergency Contact Name**: If someone is available to contact in case of an emergency, enter their name and phone number.
9. **Date of Visit**: If applicable, enter the date of your visit.
10. **Time of Visit**: If applicable, enter the time of your visit.
11. **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional information about your medical history or concerns.
