# Emergency Medicine Residency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by evaluating officers to assess the performance and competencies of medical residents during their emergency medicine residency program. It helps identify areas of strength and weakness for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the evaluation criteria, which is the first field in the form.
2. Provide feedback on the resident's technical skills and competencies, using the text field labeled "Resident Competency".
3. Evaluate the resident's performance using the text field labeled "Resident Evaluation".
4. Provide any comments on the evaluation criteria in the text field labeled "Evaluation Criteria Comments".
5. Write a brief summary of your feedback in the text field labeled "Resident Feedback".
6. Fill in the form title, category, and description fields to provide context for the form.
7. Finally, provide the form ID and assigned tool information, which will help track the form's history and usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Criteria** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is where you can specify the evaluation criteria used to assess the resident's performance. Please provide a brief description of the criteria being evaluated.
* **Resident Competency** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to evaluate the resident's technical skills and competencies. Please provide a detailed description of the resident's performance based on the evaluation criteria.
* **Resident Evaluation** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide a summary of the resident's performance and any areas of improvement.
* **Evaluation Criteria Comments** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide any additional comments on the evaluation criteria.
* **Resident Feedback** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide a brief summary of your feedback and impressions of the resident's performance.
* **Category** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to categorize the form under the healthcare forms category.
* **Description** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide a brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide the title of the form and its heading.
* **Form ID** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to track the form's ID and version information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to specify the tool used to create and manage the form.
* **Output File** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to specify the output file for the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your feedback is clear and concise.
* Be specific and provide concrete examples whenever possible.
* Use a professional tone and language.
* Review the form's fields carefully to ensure that you are providing all necessary information.
* If you need help or clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
