# Health Examination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Examination Form is designed to collect information from patients for medical examination purposes. This form is used to capture personal details, medical history, medications, allergies, lab results, medical procedures, diagnosis, treatment, and other relevant medical information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Health Examination Form, follow these steps:

1. **Personal Info (Page 1)**: Please provide your personal details such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
2. **Patient History (Page 2)**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions you may have.
3. **Medical Info (Page 3)**: List any current medications, medical procedures, or treatments you are undergoing.
4. **Allergies (Page 4)**: If you have any known allergies, please list them here.
5. **Lab Results (Page 5)**: If you have any lab results or test results, please provide them here.
6. **Medical History (Page 6)**: Provide any additional medical history or relevant information.
7. **Medical Procedure (Page 7)**: Describe any medical procedures you are undergoing or have undergone.
8. **Diagnosis (Page 8)**: If you have been diagnosed with a medical condition, please select "Yes" or "No" here.
9. **Treatment (Page 9)**: If you are currently undergoing treatment, please provide details here.
10. **Medical History 2 (Page 10)**: Provide any additional medical history or relevant information.
11. **Vital Signs (Page 11)**: Report your vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, or pulse.
12. **Doctor's Notes (Page 12)**: If you have any medical notes from your doctor, please provide them here.
13. **Medical Record ID (Page 13)**: If you have a medical record ID, please provide it here.
14. **Doctor Signature (Page 14)**: Please sign this page to confirm you have reviewed the form.
15. **Medical Image (Page 15)**: If you have any medical images, please provide them here.
16. **Medical Video (Page 16)**: If you have any medical videos, please provide them here.
17. **Patient Statement (Page 17)**: Please provide any additional information about your medical condition or treatment.
18. **Doctor Statement (Page 18)**: Please provide a statement from your doctor if applicable.
19. **Medical Certificate (Page 19)**: If you have any medical certificates, please provide them here.
20. **Medical Certificate 2 (Page 20)**: If you have any additional medical certificates, please provide them here.
21. **Doctor Info (Page 21)**: Please provide any additional information about your doctor.
22. **Other Info (Page 23)**: Please provide any additional information not captured elsewhere in the form.
23. **Medical Info 2 (Page 24)**: Provide any additional medical information here.
24. **Medical Info 3 (Page 25)**: Provide any final medical information here.
