# Telehealth Psychotherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Psychotherapy Consent Form is a document that patients must complete before starting telehealth therapy sessions. This form helps the therapist understand the patient's demographic information, preferences, and consent for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have answered all required questions.
2. Fill in your patient's demographic information, including name and email address.
3. Choose the appropriate time and date for your therapy sessions.
4. Sign the telehealth agreement and report section to indicate your consent for telehealth services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report** (`1`, `note`, optional): This section is a free text field where you can provide any additional information about your therapy sessions.
* **Patient** (`2`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering your patient's demographic information, such as their identity or any other relevant details.
* **Patient Name** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your patient's full name.
* **Date** (`4`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for your therapy sessions.
* **Time** (`5`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for your therapy sessions.
* **Therapist** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or title.
* **Patient Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your patient's phone number.
* **Telehealth Agreement and Report** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you agree to have your therapy sessions conducted via telehealth.
* **Telehealth Agreement** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you agree to the telehealth services and terms.
* **Note** (`11`, `note`, optional): This section is for entering any additional comments or notes about your therapy sessions.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and sign the telehealth agreement and report section carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields and provided accurate information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or the administration office.
