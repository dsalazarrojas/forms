# Rehab Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehab Application Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to document a patient's rehabilitation plan and progress. This form helps track a patient's treatment goals, services received, and discharge status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you are filling it out for a patient who is currently undergoing or has recently completed a rehabilitation program.
2. Fill in the required fields, including:
	* **patient_name**: Enter the name of the patient.
	* **patient_phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
	* **patient_email**: Enter the patient's email address.
3. For the **rehab_services** field, select the type of rehab service the patient is currently receiving or has received.
4. Fill in the **date_of_admission** field with the date the patient started their rehabilitation program.
5. Fill in the **date_of_discharge** field with the date the patient completed their rehabilitation program (if applicable).
6. Enter the patient's **rehab_goals** in the designated field.
7. Describe the patient's **rehab_plan** in the designated field.
8. If applicable, enter the patient's **medical_clearance**.
9. For the **discharge_status** field, select the current status of the patient's discharge.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **application_form**: This field is the main form where you will enter the patient's rehabilitation information.
* **patient_info**: This field is used to enter additional information about the patient.
* **rehab_services**: This field allows you to select the type of rehab service the patient is currently receiving or has received.
* **date_of_admission**: This field is used to enter the date the patient started their rehabilitation program.
* **date_of_discharge**: This field is used to enter the date the patient completed their rehabilitation program (if applicable).
* **rehab_goals**: This field is used to enter the patient's rehabilitation goals.
* **rehab_plan**: This field is used to enter the patient's rehabilitation plan.
* **medical_clearance**: This field is used to enter any medical clearance information related to the patient's rehabilitation.
* **discharge_status**: This field allows you to select the current status of the patient's discharge.
* **patient_name**: This field is used to enter the patient's name.
* **patient_phone**: This field is used to enter the patient's phone number.
* **patient_email**: This field is used to enter the patient's email address.
