# Therapy Collaboration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Collaboration Consent Form is used to gather essential information from clients and clinicians for effective therapy collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user information section with your details.
2. Select the information you are willing to share with your clinician and specify the scope of sharing.
3. Provide contact information for yourself and your emergency contact (if necessary).
4. Sign the client and clinician sections to confirm your understanding and agreement.
5. Include any additional information in the notes section if necessary.
6. Select a date and time for the next contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: 
  * **Name, Email, Phone (text)** (`user_information`): 
    * Please enter your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Share Information**: 
  * **Sharing Preferences (Select Multiple)** (`sharing_information`): 
    * Select the information you are willing to share with your clinician (e.g., medical history, personal details).
  * **Sharing Scope**: 
    * Specify the scope of sharing, such as medical history, personal details, or progress reports.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * **Contact Information (Text)** (`contact_information`): 
    * Enter your contact information, such as your email address or phone number.
  * **Client Signature**: 
    * Sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement with the sharing scope.
* **Therapist Information**: 
  * **Clinician Signature**: 
    * Sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement with the sharing scope.
* **Session Details**: 
  * **Date**: 
    * Enter the date of the last contact.
  * **Next Contact**: 
    * Specify the date and time of the next contact.
  * **Notes**: 
    * Enter any additional information or notes about the session.
* **Emergency Contact**: 
  * **Emergency Contact (Text)** (`emergency_contact`): 
    * Enter the name of the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Emergency Contact Name (Text)** (`emergency_contact_name`): 
    * Enter the name of the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Emergency Contact Relationship**: 
    * Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Emergency Contact Phone (Text)** (`emergency_contact_phone`): 
    * Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Emergency Contact Email (Email)** (`emergency_contact_email`): 
    * Enter the email address of the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Emergency Contact Address (Text)** (`emergency_contact_address`): 
    * Enter the address of the emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact (Text)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`): 
    * Enter the name of the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact Name (Text)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_name`): 
    * Enter the name of the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship (Text)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_relationship`): 
    * Specify your relationship with the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone (Text)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`): 
    * Enter the phone number of the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact Email (Email)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`): 
    * Enter the email address of the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
  * **Alternate Emergency Contact Address (Text)** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address`): 
    * Enter the address of the alternate emergency contact person or organization.
* **Client Agreement**: 
  * **Number of Days** (`client_agreement`): 
    * Specify the number of days for therapy collaboration.

Note: The client and clinician sections have separate signature fields for consent and agreement.
