# public_health_initiative_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_health_initiative_consent_form is designed to collect participant information and contact details for public health initiatives. It ensures data confidentiality and integrity while allowing for accurate contact and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the purpose of the form and its implications.
2. Ensure you are not filling out this form for an existing participant.
3. Fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `note`, required: false): This section is for providing a brief description of the participant, which will help identify and verify their details. Please provide a concise but relevant description.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `number`, required: false): This is where you can provide any contact information for the participant, such as address or other details that could be useful for future communications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address to allow for easy and secure communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Add the participant's phone number for further communication and updates.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
- Review your submissions before proceeding.
- If you have any doubts, contact relevant authorities or support personnel for guidance.
