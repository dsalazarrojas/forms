# social-medical-history-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, including their allergies, medications, and medical conditions. This data will be used to support the diagnosis and treatment of the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on each page and read the label carefully.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field.
3. Select the correct options for each field where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (Text, Optional): Please provide a brief history of your medical conditions, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Allergies** (Select One, Optional): Check "Allergies" if you have any known allergies. If not, select "Other".
* **Medications** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all medications you are currently taking, including any prescription and non-prescription medications. Options include:
	+ Aspirin
	+ Ibuprofen
	+ Acetaminophen
* **Medical Conditions** (Select One, Optional): Check "True" if you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes, heart disease, etc. If not, select "False".
* **Family History** (Text, Optional): Provide information about your family medical history, including any known medical conditions or allergies in your immediate family members.

Note: Please fill in all fields that apply to you, and do not worry about leaving any fields blank if they are not relevant to you.
