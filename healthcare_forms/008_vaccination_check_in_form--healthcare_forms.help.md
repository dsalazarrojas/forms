# Vaccination Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to check in patients for vaccinations. It helps gather essential information about the patient, including their name, contact details, medical history, and vaccination details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Provide the patient's contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Enter the patient's email address in the **Email** field.
5. Describe the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** field.
6. Select the relevant vaccination details from the options provided in the **Vaccination Details** field. This field is required.
7. Enter the name of the doctor who administered the vaccination in the **Doctor Name** field.
8. Provide the patient's identifier (e.g., ID number) in the **Patient Identifier** field.
9. Enter the date the vaccination was administered in the **Vaccination Date** field.
10. Select the follow-up date for the patient in the **Follow-up Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number where they can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address where they can be contacted.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Vaccination Details**: Select all relevant vaccination details that apply to the patient.
