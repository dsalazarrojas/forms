# Patient Demographic Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Demographic Information Form is a healthcare form designed to gather essential details about patients. It collects information on patients' identification, contact details, and vital signs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please enter your First name in the first text field.
* Enter your Last name in the second text field.
* Select your Date of birth from the calendar or enter it manually in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Choose your Gender from the dropdown menu (Male or Female).
* Enter your Pulse (if applicable).
* Enter your Artery pressure (if applicable).
* Enter your Heart rate (if applicable).
* Enter your Email address, following the format: prophets@domain.com.
* Enter your Phone number in the format: XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* Enter your Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Select your date of birth from the calendar or enter it manually in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose your gender from the dropdown menu (Male or Female).
* **Pulse** (`pulse`, number, required: false): Enter your pulse (if applicable).
* **Artery pressure** (`artery_pressure`, number, required: false): Enter your artery pressure (if applicable).
* **Heart rate** (`heart_rate`, number, required: false): Enter your heart rate (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format: prophets@domain.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format: XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.

## Tips

* Ensure that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Avoid duplicate entries to prevent errors and inconsistencies in patient records.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your healthcare provider or medical professional for guidance.
