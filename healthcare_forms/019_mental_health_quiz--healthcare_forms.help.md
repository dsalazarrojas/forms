# Mental Health Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Quiz is a tool designed to help individuals assess their mental health status by providing a series of questions and options to choose from.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the mental health quiz, follow these steps:
1.  Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2.  Choose the best answer that applies to your situation.
3.  Move on to the next question when you are ready.
