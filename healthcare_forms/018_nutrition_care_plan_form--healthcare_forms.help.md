# Nutrition Care Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Care Plan Form is designed to capture essential information about a patient's health and nutritional needs. This form helps healthcare providers and caregivers create personalized care plans for patients with medical conditions that require tailored nutrition recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the patient's basic information: name and date of birth.
2. Describe the patient's medical condition, which helps healthcare providers understand their nutritional requirements.
3. List any dietary restrictions the patient has, such as food allergies or preferences.
4. Set nutritional goals for the patient's care plan, which can include weight loss, weight gain, or maintaining a stable weight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: A field to enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth**: A field to enter the patient's date of birth, which helps healthcare providers understand their age and medical history.
* **Medical Condition**: A field to describe the patient's medical condition, such as diabetes, hypertension, or obesity. This helps healthcare providers understand their nutritional needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: A field to list any food allergies, intolerances, or preferences, such as vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free.
* **Nutritional Goals**: A field to set goals for the patient's care plan, such as weight loss, weight gain, or maintaining a stable weight.
