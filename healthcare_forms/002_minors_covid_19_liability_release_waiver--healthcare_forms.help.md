# Minors COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minors COVID-19 Liability Release Waiver form is used to gather information and obtain consent from parents or guardians for medical procedures or services, particularly for administering COVID-19 vaccines to minors.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the form (field 1: form_title)
2. Select the category (field 2: category) - choose "Healthcare"
3. Enter a brief description of the minor's condition or procedure (field 3: description)
4. Choose your relationship with the minor (field 4: consent) - select "I am the legal parent/guardian"
5. Enter the minor's date of birth (field 5: date_of_birth)
6. Select the type of vaccine being administered (field 6: vaccine_name) - choose "Pfizer", "Moderna", or "Janssen"
7. Enter the dosage of the vaccine (field 7: dosage)
8. Enter any known medical conditions (field 8: medical_condition)
9. List any allergies or medical conditions (field 9: allergies)
10. Enter the minor's emergency contact information (field 10: emergency_contact)
11. Enter the minor's contact information (field 11: contact_information)
12. Sign and date the form (field 12: signature)
13. Sign as the parent/guardian (field 13: parent_signature)
14. Sign as the emergency contact (field 14: emergency_contact_signature)
15. Enter the date and time of administration (field 16: date) and (field 17: time)
16. Enter the location (field 18: location)
17. Enter the medical facility's name (field 19: medical_facility)
18. Enter the doctor's name (field 20: doctor_name)
19. Enter the contact number (field 21: contact_number)
20. Enter the medical license number (field 22: medical_license_number)
21. Enter the medical board (field 23: medical_board)
22. Sign as the doctor (field 24: doctor_signature)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1): Enter a brief title for the form.
* **Category** (2): Select "Healthcare" to indicate that the form is related to medical procedures or services.
* **Description** (3): Enter a brief description of the minor's condition or procedure.
* **Consent** (4): Choose "I am the legal parent/guardian" to indicate your relationship with the minor.
* **Date of Birth** (5): Enter the minor's date of birth.
* **Vaccine Name** (6): Select the type of vaccine being administered - choose "Pfizer", "Moderna", or "Janssen".
* **Dosage** (7): Enter the dosage of the vaccine.
* **Medical Condition** (8): Enter any known medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (9): List any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (10): Enter the minor's emergency contact information.
* **Contact Information** (11): Enter the minor's contact information.
* **Signature** (12): Sign and date the form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the procedures.
* **Parent Signature** (13): Sign as the parent/guardian to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the procedures.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (14): Sign as the emergency contact to confirm that they have reviewed and agreed to the procedures.
* **Date** (16): Enter the date of administration.
* **Time** (17): Enter the time of administration.
* **Location** (18): Enter the location of administration.
* **Medical Facility** (19): Enter the medical facility's name.
* **Doctor Name** (20): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Contact Number** (21): Enter the contact number.
* **Medical License Number** (22): Enter the medical license number.
* **Medical Board** (23): Enter the medical board.
* **Doctor Signature** (24): Sign as the doctor to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the procedures.
* **Doctor** (25): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Date of Administration** (26): Enter the date of administration.
* **Vaccine Site** (27): Enter the location of the vaccine administration.
