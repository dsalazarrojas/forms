# Case Report Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a study or case report. It protects participant privacy and confidentiality while ensuring they understand the study's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Read each section carefully, and answer the questions honestly.
3. Make sure you understand the study's purpose, data collection methods, and confidentiality agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Study or Case Report Title** (`research_study_title`, Text, Required): Provide a clear and concise title for the study or case report.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which you provided consent to participate in the study.
* **Research Institution or Healthcare Facility** (`research_institution`, Text, Required): Indicate the institution or facility where the study will take place.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it should be recorded in the study's records.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Record Number** (`participant_mrn`, Text, Optional): If you have a medical record number, provide it. This is not required but may be necessary for study purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`participant_contact`, Text, Required): Provide a phone number where you can be reached for study-related purposes.
* **Purpose of the Study or Case Report** (`study_purpose`, Text, Required): Explain the study's purpose in simple terms.
* **Principal Investigator Name** (`principal_investigator`, Text, Required): Identify the principal investigator or study lead.
* **Investigator Contact Information** (`investigator_contact`, Text, Required): Provide the contact information of the principal investigator.
* **Expected Duration of Study** (`study_duration`, Text, Optional): Estimate the length of time the study will take.
* **Types of Information to be Collected** (`data_types`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the types of data that will be collected (e.g., Medical History, Laboratory Results, Imaging Studies, etc.).
* **How Will Your Data Be Used?** (`data_usage`, Text, Required): Explain how the data will be used in the study.
* **Will Results Be Published?** (`publication_intent`, Select One, Required): Choose whether study results will be published in medical journals, conference presentations, or other formats.
* **How Will Privacy Be Protected?** (`privacy_protection`, Text, Required): Describe the measures taken to protect participant privacy.
* **Will Personal Identifiers Be Removed?** (`identifier_removal`, Select One, Required): Choose how personal identifiers will be handled (completely de-identified, coded identifier only, or identifiable information retained).
* **How Will Data Be Stored and Protected?** (`data_storage`, Text, Required): Explain the data storage and protection methods.
* **I Have Read and Understood This Consent Form** (`understood_information`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **I Voluntarily Agree to Participate** (`voluntary_participation`, Select One, Required): Confirm your willingness to participate in the study.

Note: Ensure you understand each section and answer the questions honestly to provide informed consent.
