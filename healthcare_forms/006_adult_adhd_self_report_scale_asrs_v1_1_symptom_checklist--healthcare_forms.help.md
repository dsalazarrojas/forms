# Adult ADHD Self Report Scale ASRS V1 1 Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult ADHD Self Report Scale ASRS V1 1 Symptom Checklist is a questionnaire designed to help adults self-report on their symptoms of ADHD. This form is used to collect data on inattention and hyperactivity symptoms, their impact on daily life, and relevant medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each item carefully and select the frequency that best describes your experience.
2. For inattention and hyperactivity items (questions 4 and 5), select the frequency that best describes your experience over the past 6 months.
3. If you have a prior diagnosis of ADHD or related conditions (question 7), please provide the diagnosis and year of diagnosis.
4. If you are currently taking any medications (question 8), list the name, dosage, and any other relevant information.
5. Check "True" on question 9 if you agree to allow your provider to follow up with you about your responses.
6. Finally, enter the date you completed this form (question 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`respondent_name`, optional): This is your full name. It's optional, but including it can be helpful for follow-up contact.
* **Contact email** (`email`, optional): This is your email address. If you would like to be contacted by your healthcare provider about your responses, please enter it here.
* **Inattention symptoms frequency** (`inattention_items`, required): This question asks about the frequency of inattention symptoms you've experienced over the past 6 months. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Hyperactivity and impulsivity symptoms frequency** (`hyperactivity_items`, required): This question asks about the frequency of hyperactivity symptoms you've experienced over the past 6 months. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Impact of symptoms on work and relationships** (`symptoms_impact`, optional): Describe any functional impairments or difficulties you've experienced due to your symptoms.
* **Prior diagnosis of ADHD or related conditions** (`prior_diagnosis`, optional): If you've been diagnosed with ADHD or a related condition, please provide the diagnosis and year of diagnosis.
* **Current medications** (`medications_currently`, optional): List any medications you're currently taking, including dosage and any other relevant information.
* **Agree to provider follow up** (`agree_follow_up`, optional): Check "True" to allow your healthcare provider to follow up with you about your responses.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
