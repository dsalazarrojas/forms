# medical_device_follow_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical device follow-up form is designed to gather information about the status of a medical device, including its condition, feedback, and follow-up details. This form is used to track the progress of medical devices that have been returned or require ongoing monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Start" page to begin the form.
2. Enter the patient ID, which is a unique identifier for the patient.
3. Enter the name of the medical device that needs follow-up.
4. Select the "Feedback Date" using the calendar tool.
5. Provide a brief description of the feedback you have for the device.
6. Rate the device's status using the provided options (Active, Non-Active, or Pending).
7. Select the follow-up date for the next checkup.
8. Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
9. Choose the follow-up status (Active, Non-Active, or Pending).
10. Enter any follow-up comments or notes.
11. Enter the name of the doctor who will be performing the follow-up.
12. Enter the follow-up date and time.
13. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Start** (`1`, `number`, required): This page marks the beginning of the form.
* **Patient Id** (`2`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's unique identifier.
* **Device Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the medical device that requires follow-up.
* **Feedback Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Select the date of the feedback using the calendar tool.
* **Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required): Describe your feedback for the device.
* **Rating** (`6`, `number`, required): Rate the device's status using the provided options (Active, Non-Active, or Pending).
* **Follow Up Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Select the date for the next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Notes** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
* **Medical Device Status** (`9`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the current status of the device.
* **Device Returned** (`10`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select whether the device has been returned.
* **Additional Comments** (`11`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Patient Comments** (`12`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments from the patient.
* **Doctor Comments** (`13`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments from the doctor.
* **Follow Up Status** (`14`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the follow-up status.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`15`, `text`, not required): Enter any follow-up comments.
* **Follow Up Doctor** (`16`, `text`, not required): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Follow Up Time** (`17`, `time`, required): Enter the follow-up time.
* **Follow Up Date** (`18`, `date`, required): Select the date for the next follow-up.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals to track the progress of medical devices. If you are not authorized to complete this form, please do not attempt to do so.
