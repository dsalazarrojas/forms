# Health Proxy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Proxy Consent Form is a document that grants permission to a designated healthcare professional to act on behalf of a patient in medical situations. This form is used to establish a legal agreement between the patient and their chosen proxy, ensuring that the healthcare provider can make decisions and take actions on behalf of the patient as per their wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth of the patient.
*   Select the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
*   Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
*   Specify the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
*   Choose the healthcare provider's relationship to the patient.
*   Enter the healthcare provider's name and contact information (if applicable).
*   Sign and date the form as the patient and their proxy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (`first_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the first name of the patient.
*   **Last Name (`last_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the last name of the patient.
*   **Address (`address`, `text`, required):** Enter the patient's address.
*   **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required):** Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Email (`email`, `email`, required):** Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Phone (`phone`, `text`, required):** Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Proxy Designation Date (`proxy_designation_date`, `date`, required):** Enter the date when the proxy designation was made.
*   **Relationship to Patient (`relationship_to_patient`, `select_one`, required):** Select the relationship between the patient and their proxy, such as Spouse, Parent, Sibling, etc.
*   **Proxy Signature Date (`proxy_signature_date`, `date`, required):** Enter the date when the proxy signature was made.
*   **Proxy Signature (`proxy_signature`, `text`, required):** Enter the proxy's signature.
*   **Patient Signature Date (`patient_signature_date`, `date`, required):** Enter the date when the patient's signature was made.
*   **Patient Signature (`patient_signature`, `text`, required):** Enter the patient's signature.
*   **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required):** Enter the emergency contact's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required):** Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required):** Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
*   **Emergency Contact Address (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required):** Enter the emergency contact's address.
*   **Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required):** Enter the emergency contact's email address.
*   **Relationship to Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact_relationship_to_patient`, `select_one`, required):** Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
*   **Conversation with Patient (`patient_conversation`, `text`, required):** Enter a description of the conversation had with the patient.
*   **Conversation Date (`patient_conversation_date`, `date`, required):** Enter the date of the conversation with the patient.
*   **Healthcare Provider Name (`healthcare_provider_name`, `text`, required):** Enter the healthcare provider's name.
*   **Healthcare Provider Phone (`healthcare_provider_phone`, `text`, required):** Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
*   **Relationship to Healthcare Provider (`healthcare_provider_relationship`, `select_one`, required):** Select the relationship between the healthcare provider and the patient.
*   **Healthcare Provider Address (`healthcare_provider_address`, `text`, required):** Enter the healthcare provider's address.
*   **Healthcare Provider Email (`healthcare_provider_email`, `email`, required):** Enter the healthcare provider's email address.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Ensure that the patient's signature is valid (e.g., not a scan or printout of an electronic signature).
*   Keep a copy of the signed form for reference.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   Make sure the healthcare provider's relationship and contact information are accurate.
*   Enter the date of the conversation with the patient and the healthcare provider's name correctly.
