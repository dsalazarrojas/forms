# slaa prophets recovery questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to help you assess your recovery progress and identify areas that need improvement. It will also help your healthcare provider understand your situation and develop effective recovery plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. For select multiple options questions, choose all that apply.
3. For text boxes, provide as much information as you feel comfortable sharing.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recovery History**: Describe your recovery journey, including any successes and challenges you have faced.
	+ **Recovery Date**: If you have a specific date when you started your recovery, please provide it.
* **Sex Addict Status**: Choose if you are a sex addict or not.
* **Current Status**: Choose how you currently see your recovery progress.
* **Therapist Info**: If you are seeing a therapist, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Support Group**: If you are attending a support group, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Recovery Goal**: Describe your recovery goals and objectives.
* **Relapse Info**: If you have relapsed, please describe the situation.
* **Relapse Frequency**: Choose how often you have relapsed.
* **Current Circumstances**: Describe your current living situation.
* **Recovery Supports**: Describe the people and resources that support your recovery.
* **Recovery Barriers**: Choose the obstacles that hinder your recovery.
* **Recovery Strategies**: Describe the strategies you use to maintain your recovery.
* **Recovery Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term recovery goals.
* **Relapse Risk Factors**: Describe the situations that trigger your relapse.
* **Recovery Plans**: Describe your recovery plans and strategies.
* **Progress Barriers**: Describe the obstacles you face in your recovery progress.
* **Relapse Triggers**: Describe the situations that trigger your relapse.
* **Relapse Situations**: Choose the situations in which you are more likely to relapse.
* **Relapse Situations Context**: Describe the context in which you relapse.
* **Relapse Situations Description**: Describe your relapse situations.
* **Recovery Plans Context**: Describe the context of your recovery plans.
* **Relapse Triggers Context**: Describe the context in which you relapse.
