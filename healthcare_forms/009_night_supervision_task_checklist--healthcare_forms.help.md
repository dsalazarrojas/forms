# Night Supervision Task Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Supervision Task Checklist helps supervisors ensure that required tasks are completed and monitor incidents that may have occurred during the night shift, promoting a safe and secure environment for those under supervision.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Night Supervision Details** (`night_supervision_details`): Enter a brief description of the night supervision period, including the date and duration. This field is required.
2. **Completed Tasks** (`completed_tasks`): Select all tasks that were completed during the night supervision period from the dropdown list. Multiple selections are allowed.
	* Tasks are likely related to regular checks and checks on equipment, patients, or facilities.
3. **Incidents** (`incidents`): Record any incidents that occurred during the night supervision period in this field. This field is not required.
4. **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Date and sign to verify that you completed the required tasks and reviewed this form. This field is not required.
5. **Task Comments** (`task_comments`): Add any comments or notes about the tasks finished or incidents that occurred. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Night Supervision Details** (`night_supervision_details`): A text field to provide a brief description of the night supervision period.
*   **Completed Tasks** (`completed_tasks`): A dropdown list of tasks to select from, indicating completed tasks during the night supervision period.
*   **Incidents** (`incidents`): A text field for recording any incidents that occurred during the night supervision period.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): A date field to record the date and signature of the supervisor, indicating verification of completed tasks.
*   **Task Comments** (`task_comments`): A text field for adding comments or notes about completed tasks and incidents.

## Tips

*   Make sure to carefully review and complete all required fields for accurate reporting.
*   Use the Task Comments field for any additional information or explanations.
*   The date field for Supervisor Signature is a date picker, make sure to select the correct date.
