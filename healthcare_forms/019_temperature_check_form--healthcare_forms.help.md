# temperature_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The temperature_check_form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's temperature check. Please fill out this form with accurate and truthful information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Record the patient's body temperature in the "Temperature" field. Use numeric values only.
3. If applicable, provide the duration of the patient's temperature check in the "Duration" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the temperature check in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
5. If you have any additional comments or observations related to the temperature check, enter them in the "Note" field.
6. If the temperature check was assigned to someone, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text)**: This field is the patient's name. Enter the name as it appears on the patient's medical record.
* **Temperature (number)**: This field is used to record the patient's body temperature. Enter the temperature reading as a numeric value.
* **Duration (text)**: This field is optional, but if the patient's temperature was checked for more than one point in time, enter the duration in a format that makes sense (e.g., "30 minutes" or "3 hours").
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the temperature check using the standard date format (e.g., "MM/DD/YYYY").
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the temperature check using the standard time format (e.g., "HH:MM AM/PM").
* **Note (text)**: This field is for any additional comments or observations related to the temperature check.
* **Assigned To (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" if the temperature check was assigned to someone, and "No" otherwise.
