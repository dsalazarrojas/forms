# Nutritional Protocol Recommendations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Protocol Recommendations Form is designed to collect patient information that helps healthcare providers make informed decisions about their nutritional care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's demographic information:
	* Enter your patient's **Patient Name**.
	* Select your patient's **Gender** from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide medical history:
	* List any **Medications** your patient is currently taking.
	* Describe any **Medical Conditions** (such as Diabetes, Hypertension, or Obesity).
3. Enter laboratory data:
	* Enter your patient's **Blood Sugar Level** (if known).
	* Enter your patient's **Blood Pressure (mmHg)**.
4. Provide other relevant information:
	* Enter your patient's **Age**.
	* Enter your patient's **Weight (lbs)** and **Height (inches)**.
5. Answer the remaining questions:
	* Select your patient's **Nutritional Status** and **Medical Status**.
	* Enter any **Additional Comments**.
