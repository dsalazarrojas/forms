# Medical Research Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Research Release Form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from healthcare research participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the formats and guidelines provided for date fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask your research coordinator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Study Information** (`study_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the research study you are participating in.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for your next of kin or other authorized contact.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the relationship between you and the contact person (e.g. Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend).
* **Other (specify)** (`contact_relationship_other`, text, required: false): If the above relationship options do not apply, please specify the contact relationship.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license`, number, required: false): Enter your medical license number if applicable.
* **Research Institution Affiliation** (`research_institution`, text, required: false): Specify your affiliation with the research institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required: true" and must be completed before submitting the form.
