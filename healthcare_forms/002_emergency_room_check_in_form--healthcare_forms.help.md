# emergency_room_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patients' information for emergency room check-in. It is designed to ensure that the medical team is aware of any allergies, medical conditions, and other important information before treating the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including their name, contact details, and medical history.
3. Make sure to check the box for allergies and medical conditions that apply.
4. If the patient has any medical allergies, list them in the provided field.
5. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the contact details of the person responsible for the patient's care or the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Room Details** (`emergency_room_details`, text, required): Describe the reason for the patient's visit and any other important information about their condition.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, required): List any medications that the patient is allergic to or has a sensitivity towards.
* **Signature Verification** (`signature_verification`, text, required): This field is for signature verification purposes only. Please do not fill out this field unless instructed otherwise.
* **Date Entered** (`date_entered2`, text, required): The date and time the patient entered the emergency room.
* **Additional Information** (`emergency_room_check_in_form_page_14`, text, required): Any additional information about the patient's condition or needs.
* **Signature** (`emergency_room_check_in_form_page_15`, text, required): This is where the patient's signature is to be verified.

Note that some fields may not be required or displayed, please review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all necessary fields are filled out accurately.
