# Psychology Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from patients about their mental health, medical history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please ensure you have read and understood the content of this form before proceeding.
* Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your clinician for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`): This field is for collecting basic patient information such as name, contact details, and other relevant identification.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select all the symptoms that you are currently experiencing from the given options (Depression, Anxiety, Mood Disorders, Sleep Problems, Substance Abuse).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any relevant diagnoses, treatments, or conditions.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Social Info** (`social_info`): Share any relevant social information that may impact your mental health or treatment, such as relationships or social support networks.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Report any known medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Family History** (`family_history`): Disclose any relevant family medical history that may impact your mental health or treatment.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone in the past.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`): Share your mental health status and any concerns you may have.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment`): Outline your current treatment plan, including any therapies, medications, or counseling.
* **Follow-up** (`followup`): Schedule follow-up appointments or meetings with your clinician.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your treatment.
* **Clinician Comments** (`clinician_comments`): Record any comments or feedback from your clinician about your treatment.
* **Clinician Recommendations** (`clinician_recommendations`): List any recommendations or guidance provided by your clinician.
* **Patient Agreement** (`patient_agreement`): Confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms of your treatment plan.
* **Clinician Agreement** (`clinician_agreement`): Confirm that your clinician has reviewed and agreed to the terms of your treatment plan.
* **Clinician Signature** (`clinician_signature`): Confirm that your clinician has signed off on your treatment plan.
* **Patient Agreement Signature** (`patient_agreement_signature`): Confirm that you have signed off on your treatment plan.
