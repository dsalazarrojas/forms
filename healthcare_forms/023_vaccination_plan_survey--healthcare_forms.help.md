# Vaccination Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Plan Survey is designed to gather information about your vaccination status, medical history, and other relevant details to help healthcare providers make informed decisions about your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification from the healthcare provider.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccination Plan Survey** (`vaccination_plan_survey`, text, optional): This is a brief description of the purpose of the form, but it's not a required field. You can leave it blank if you don't want to provide additional context.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant illnesses, allergies, or conditions you may have.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Vaccinated
	+ Not Vaccinated
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, number, optional): If you have received a vaccination, please enter the number of doses you have had.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): If you have received a vaccination, please enter the date of your vaccination.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): If you have received a vaccination, please enter the time of your vaccination.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Please provide the name and contact information of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Covid-19
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart condition
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please provide them in this field.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medication, please provide the name and dosage information in this field.
* **Vaccination Side Effects** (`vaccination_side_effects`, text, optional): If you have experienced any side effects from a vaccination, please describe them in this field.
