# Fitness Logger Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Logger Form is designed to track and monitor a client's fitness progress and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Fill in the client's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the client's current fitness status and goals.
3. Record the client's progress photos, workouts completed, and exercises completed.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the client's visit.
* **Height** (`height`, `text`, required): Enter the client's height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `text`, required): Enter the client's weight.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the client's current fitness goals.
* **Workouts Completed** (`workouts_completed`, `text`, required): Record the number of workouts completed.
* **Progress Photos** (`progress_photos`, `text`, required): Upload or link to progress photos.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's progress.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the client's workout category (e.g., Cardio, Strength Training, etc.).
* **Exercises Completed** (`exercises_completed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the exercises completed by the client.
* **Weight Lifting Frequencies** (`weight_lifting_frequencies`, `text`, required): Record the frequency of weight lifting exercises.
* **Cardio Frequencies** (`cardio_frequencies`, `number`, required): Record the frequency of cardio exercises.
* **Flexibility Frequencies** (`flexibility_frequencies`, `text`, required): Record the frequency of flexibility exercises.
* **Progress Photos** (`progress_photos`, `text`, required): Upload or link to progress photos.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments about the client.
* **Exercise Completed** (`exercise_completed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the exercises completed by the client.
* **Workout Completed** (`workout_completed`, `text`, required): Record the workout completed by the client.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the client's training level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Client ID** (`client_id`, `text`, required): Enter the client's ID.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the client's current fitness level.
* **Client Status** (`client_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the client's status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Date Last Update** (`date_last_update`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Time Last Update** (`time_last_update`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the last update.
