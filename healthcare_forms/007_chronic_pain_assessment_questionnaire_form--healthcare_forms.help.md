# Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form is designed to gather patient information for the assessment of chronic pain. This form will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's experiences and details about their chronic pain, which will aid in treatment planning and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your medical records.
2. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Describe the primary area of pain in the "Primary pain location" field.
4. Indicate how long you have experienced this pain in the "Pain duration" field.
5. Rate your typical pain intensity from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain) in the "Pain intensity" field.
6. Describe the pain quality in the "Pain character" field, if applicable.
7. Identify any aggravating factors that worsen your pain in the "Aggravating factors" field, if applicable.
8. Identify any factors that relieve your pain in the "Relieving factors" field, if applicable.
9. List any current treatments or interventions you are using in the "Current treatments" field, if applicable.
10. Describe how your pain affects your daily life in the "Impact on daily life" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your medical records.
  • This field is required and should be completed with your name.
  
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  • This field is required and should be completed with your birthdate.
  
- **Primary pain location** (`pain_location`, text, required): Describe the area of your chronic pain.
  • This field is required and should be completed with a description of your pain location.
  
- **Pain duration** (`pain_duration`, text, required): Indicate how long you have experienced this pain.
  • This field is required and should be completed with a duration of time (e.g., "6 months").
  
- **Pain intensity** (`pain_intensity`, select_one, required): Rate your typical pain intensity from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
  • This field is required and should be completed with a rating from 0 to 10.
  
- **Pain character** (`pain_character`, text, optional): Describe the quality of your pain, for example, burning, sharp, aching.
  • This field is optional and should be completed with a description of your pain quality if you have a specific experience.
  
- **Aggravating factors** (`aggravating_factors`, text, optional): Identify any activities or situations that worsen your pain, for example, stress, movement.
  • This field is optional and should be completed with a list or description of factors that worsen your pain.
  
- **Relieving factors** (`relieving_factors`, text, optional): Identify any factors that relieve your pain, for example, rest, exercise.
  • This field is optional and should be completed with a list or description of factors that relieve your pain.
  
- **Current treatments** (`current_treatments`, text, optional): List any current medications, therapies, or interventions you are using to manage your pain.
  • This field is optional and should be completed with a list or description of your current treatments.
  
- **Impact on daily life** (`impact_daily_life`, text, required): Describe how your pain affects your daily life, for example, sleep, work, relationships.
  • This field is required and should be completed with a description of how your pain affects your daily life.
