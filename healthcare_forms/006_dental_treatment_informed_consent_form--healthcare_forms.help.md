# dental_treatment_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental treatment informed consent form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients prior to dental treatment. This form helps ensure that patients understand the proposed treatment and their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the patient's personal details in the "Patient Information" field.
3. In the "Procedure Details" field, describe the proposed treatment, including the type of procedure, the risks involved, and any alternative options.
4. In the "Dental Clinic Information" field, enter the details about the dental clinic, such as the address, contact number, and hours of operation.
5. In the "Patient Signature" field, sign and date your consent, indicating that you understand the proposed treatment and its risks.
6. In the "Dentist Signature" field, sign and date your consent as the dentist, indicating that you have explained the treatment to the patient.
7. In the "Informed Consent" field, indicate whether you consent to the proposed treatment.
8. If you do not consent to the proposed treatment, select "No, I do not consent" in the "Waiver" field.
9. If you have insurance, enter your insurance details in the "Insurance Information" field.
10. In the "Medical History" field, enter any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your treatment.
11. In the "Allergies" field, list any allergies you have.
12. In the "Meds" field, select the type of medication you are currently taking.
13. Finally, review the form carefully before submitting it to the dentist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the proposed treatment, including the type of procedure, risks involved, and alternative options.
* **Dental Clinic Information** (`dental_clinic_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the details about the dental clinic, such as address, contact number, and hours of operation.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign and date your consent, indicating that you understand the proposed treatment and its risks.
* **Dentist Signature** (`dentist_signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign and date your consent as the dentist, indicating that you have explained the treatment to the patient.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, required: false): Indicate whether you consent to the proposed treatment.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you consent or do not consent to the proposed treatment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as address, phone number, and email.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your insurance details, such as insurance provider, policy number, and coverage dates.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Enter any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your treatment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Meds** (`meds`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of medication you are currently taking.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, required: false): Review and sign the informed consent section.
 
## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as it will be used to ensure your informed consent.
* Make sure to review the form with your dentist before signing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your dentist.
* If you do not consent to the proposed treatment, do not sign the form.
* Please note that this form is for your own protection and the protection of your oral health.
