# Pregnancy Therapies Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy Therapies Consultation Form is designed for clients to provide personal and pregnancy-related information to healthcare professionals for medical consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (Page 1).
2. Provide your contact information (Page 2).
3. Enter your pregnancy information (Page 3).
4. Share any relevant health information (Page 4).
5. Read and select your consent to treatment terms and conditions (Page 5).
6. Specify the week of your pregnancy (Page 6).
7. Choose your current state (Page 7).
8. Add any further details about your treatment (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Personal Info** (Page 1, text, required: false): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other personal details as requested.
* **Contact Info** (Page 2, text, required: false): Provide your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you for further consultation.
* **Pregnancy Info** (Page 3, number, required: false): Enter the number of your pregnancy weeks.
* **Health Info** (Page 4, text, required: false): Share any relevant health information that may be important for your treatment.
* **Consent** (Page 5, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your consent to treatment terms and conditions.
* **Week of Pregnancy** (Page 6, number, required: false): Enter the week of your pregnancy.
* **Current State** (Page 7, select_one, required: false): Choose your current state of health.
* **Further Details** (Page 8, text, required: false): Add any further details about your treatment that you think is important to share with your healthcare professional.
