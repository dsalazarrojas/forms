# Soul Wound Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for the Soul Wound Clinic. It is not a mandatory form and can be completed by patients or healthcare professionals for reference purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's personal details on the **Patient Intake** page.
* Provide the patient's medical history on the **Medical History** page.
* Enter the patient's contact information on the **Contact Info** page.
* Note any medical notes or concerns on the **Medical Notes** page.
* Specify the patient's billing information on the **Billing Info** page.
* Answer questions about the patient's insurance status, allergies, and medical procedures on the **Insurance**, **Allergies**, and **Medical Procedure** pages.
* Select the patient's medical condition, medical test results, medical procedures, medications, and medical specialties on the **Medical Condition**, **Medical Test Results**, **Medical Procedures**, **Medications**, and **Medical Specialties** pages.
* Finally, provide any additional medical test results on the **Medical Test Results 2** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Intake (1)**: Enter the patient's name and other personal details.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Contact Info (3)**: Provide the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Notes (4)**: Note any important medical information or concerns for the clinician.
* **Billing Info (5)**: Enter the patient's billing information, including insurance provider and policy number.
* **Insurance (6)**: Answer whether the patient has insurance coverage.
* **Allergies (7)**: Specify the patient's allergies.
* **Medical Procedure (8)**: Answer whether the patient has undergone a specific medical procedure.
* **Medical Condition (9)**: Select the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical Test Results (10)**: Select the patient's medical test results.
* **Medical Procedures (11)**: Select the patient's medical procedures.
* **Medications (12)**: Specify the patient's medications.
* **Medical Specialties (13)**: Select the patient's medical specialties.
* **Medical Test Results 2 (14)**: Provide additional medical test results.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
* If you are unsure about a field, please contact the Soul Wound Clinic for assistance.
