# healthcare_access_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to declare patient healthcare access needs, medical history, and other relevant details to healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic patient information in the **Patient Info** section.
2. List your access needs in the **Access Needs** section.
3. Enter your insurance details in the **Insurance Details** section.
4. List any emergency contacts in the **Emergency Contacts** section.
5. Describe your medical history in the **Medical History** section.
6. List any allergies or sensitivities in the **Allergies** section.
7. List any current medications in the **Medications** section.
8. List any medical devices you use in the **Medical Devices** section.
9. Repeat the medical conditions, history, and devices sections for up to 4 iterations (sections 10-23).
10. Repeat the medications and allergies sections for up to 4 iterations (sections 24-28).

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your medical history, conditions, and devices.
