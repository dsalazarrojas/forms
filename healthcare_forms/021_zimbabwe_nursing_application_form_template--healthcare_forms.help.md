# zimbabwe_nursing_application_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zimbabwe_nursing_application_form_template is a healthcare form designed for nursing applications in Zimbabwe. The form helps healthcare organizations collect relevant information from applicants to evaluate their qualifications and suitability for nursing positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documents before starting the form.
2. Fill in your demographic information (personal details) in the "personal_details" field.
3. Provide your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
4. Share your educational background in the "education_background" field.
5. Describe your work experience in the healthcare industry in the "work_experience" field.
6. List any relevant professional certifications you hold in the "professional_certifications" field.
7. Provide emergency contact information in the "emergency_contacts" field.
8. Share your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
9. Sign the form digitally in the "e_signature" field.
10. Upload any supporting documents in the "upload_documents" field (if required).
11. Finally, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required: false): This is the main section where you can provide a brief introduction or cover letter explaining your interest in the nursing position.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required: false): Here, you will provide your demographic information such as name, date of birth, nationality, etc.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required: false): Describe your educational qualifications and background in nursing.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Share your work experience in the healthcare industry, including job positions, dates of employment, and job responsibilities.
* **Professional Certifications** (`professional_certifications`, text, required: false): List any relevant professional certifications you hold, such as First Aid or CPR certification.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): Provide contact information for emergency contacts, such as family members or next of kin.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical information that may impact your work as a nurse.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This is a field for you to sign the form digitally.
* **Electronic Signature** (`e_signature`, text, required: false): Enter your electronic signature to complete the form.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required: false): Attach any supporting documents, such as your ID, certificates, or diplomas.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documents before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when answering each field.
- Proofread your form before submission to catch any errors or omissions.
