# Weight Loss Program Participant Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and program participation information from individuals interested in joining a weight loss program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name of the weight loss program you are interested in joining.
2. Enter the location where you plan to participate in the program.
3. Provide your age.
4. Enter your height in inches (e.g., 68 for 5'8").
5. Enter your current weight in pounds (e.g., 150).
6. Choose your weight loss goal (e.g., 10% weight loss, 15% weight loss, 20% weight loss, etc.).
7. If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in the text box.
8. List any medications you are currently taking.
9. Enter your contact number for follow-up and support.
10. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
11. Enter your email address (e.g., [example@email.com](mailto:example@email.com)).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the weight loss program you are interested in joining.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location where you plan to participate in the program.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Height**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Weight Loss Goal**: Choose your weight loss goal (10%, 15%, 20%, etc.).
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, describe them in the text box.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up and support.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for program updates and support.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help the program team provide effective support and guidance throughout your journey.
* Please do not duplicate your application.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to ask the program team for assistance.
