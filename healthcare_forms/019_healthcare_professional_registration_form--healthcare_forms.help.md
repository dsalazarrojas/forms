# Healthcare Professional Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Professional Registration Form is designed to collect information from medical professionals to register them for various healthcare-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`3`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that you use for professional purposes.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., `+1 555 123 4567`).
* **License** (`6`, `text`, required): Please enter your medical license number or other relevant identification.
* **Specialty** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your area of specialization (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology, etc.).
* **Medical school** (`8`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your medical school.
* **Graduation date** (`9`, `date`, required): Please enter the date of your graduation in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Practice since** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Select how long you have been practicing medicine (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, etc.).
* **Hospital affiliation** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your hospital affiliation (e.g., Private hospital, Public hospital, etc.).
* **Medical group** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your medical group (e.g., Large, Small, etc.).
* **Address** (`13`, `text`, required): Please enter your current address.
* **Medical license number** (`14`, `text`, required): Please enter your medical license number or other relevant identification.
* **Medical license state** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select the state where your medical license is issued (e.g., Alabama, Alaska, etc.).
* **Medical boards certification** (`16`, `text`, optional): Please enter any medical boards certification you hold.
* **Emergency contact name** (`17`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relation** (`18`, `text`, optional): Please specify your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`19`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`20`, `text`, optional): Please specify your relationship with your secondary contact person.
* **Emergency contact address** (`21`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact person's address.
* **Secondary contact phone** (`22`, `text`, optional): Please enter your secondary contact person's phone number.
* **Medical license status** (`23`, `text`, optional): Please enter your current medical license status.
* **License expiration date** (`24`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date your medical license expires.
* **Medical license number 2** (`25`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional medical license number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any difficulties filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is intended for one-time submission; do not fill out the form multiple times unless specified by our team.
