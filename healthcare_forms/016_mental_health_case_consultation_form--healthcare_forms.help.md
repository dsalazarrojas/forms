# Mental Health Case Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about mental health cases from healthcare providers. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly for better analysis and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please start by filling in the **client_info** field with any relevant details about the client, such as their name, age, and contact information. This will help the case manager to quickly identify and access the client's information.
2.  Next, fill in the **clinical_info** field with any relevant clinical information about the client's case, such as diagnosis, treatment, and progress.
3.  In the **medication** field, enter the medication(s) the client is currently taking, if any.
4.  Select all applicable assessment tools used in evaluating the client's mental health case from the **assessment_tools** field. This is important for tracking the client's progress and evaluating their condition.
5.  In the **notes** field, enter any additional comments or observations about the client's case.
6.  Enter the **email** address of the client or their caregiver in the **email** field. This is important for keeping them informed about their case.
7.  Enter the client's **phone** number in the **phone** field. This is important for keeping in touch with the client or their caregiver.
8.  In the **case_summary** field, enter a brief summary of the client's case. This should include all relevant details about their condition and progress.
9.  If there is any additional information about the client that is not captured in the other fields, use the **additional_info** field to enter it. This can include any relevant medical history, social situation, or other factors that may impact their mental health case.
10.  Finally, click on the **submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **client_info** (text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the client, such as their name, age, and contact details.
*   **clinical_info** (text, required: false): This field is used to gather any relevant clinical information about the client's case, such as diagnosis and treatment.
*   **medication** (text, required: true): This field is used to enter any medication the client is currently taking.
*   **assessment_tools** (select multiple, required: false): Select all applicable assessment tools used to evaluate the client's mental health case.
*   **notes** (note, required: true): Enter any additional comments or observations about the client's case.
*   **email** (email, required: true): Enter the client's email address or their caregiver's email address.
*   **phone** (text, required: true): Enter the client's phone number or their caregiver's phone number.
*   **case_summary** (note, required: true): Enter a brief summary of the client's case, including all relevant details about their condition and progress.
*   **additional_info** (note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the client that is not captured in the other fields.
*   **submit** (text, required: true): Click this button to submit the form.
