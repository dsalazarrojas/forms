# Pediatric Intubation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather critical information about a patient's medical history, anesthesia, and other relevant details to ensure a safe and effective intubation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History** (1): This field is for recording the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
* **Patient Information** (2): Select all applicable medical conditions that the patient has or may have, such as Anesthesia, Asthma, or Bronchitis.
* **Medications** (3): Enter any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken recently.
* **Anesthesia** (4): Select the type of anesthesia used for the intubation process, such as Etomidate or Fentanyl.
* **Vital Signs** (5): Record the patient's vital signs, including their blood pressure, pulse, and other relevant measurements.
* **Patient Status** (6): Indicate the patient's current status, such as Stable or Other.
* **Note** (7): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Date** (8): Enter the date of the intubation process.
* **Time** (9): Record the time of the intubation process.
* **Medical Team** (10): Enter the names of the medical team members involved in the process.
* **Patient Name** (11): Enter the patient's name.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before using this form.
* If you're unsure about any field or process, consult with your medical team or institution's guidelines.
