# COVID 19 Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for COVID-19-related activities. This form helps us understand their personal details, background, and availability for volunteering, ensuring we can match them with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Personal Information** section:
	* Enter your first name and last name.
	* Provide your date of birth (MM-DD-YYYY).
	* Enter your email address and phone number.
	* Provide your current address.
	* Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
2. In the **Background Information** section:
	* Select your highest level of education completed.
	* Indicate whether you have a medical background or relevant training.
	* Mention any relevant skills or certifications you possess.
3. In the **Volunteer Opportunity** section:
	* Choose the type of volunteer work you are interested in (patient care, administrative support, etc.).
	* Select how many hours per week you can volunteer.
	* Specify when you can start volunteering and for how long.
4. In the **Scheduling** section:
	* Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
	* Select your preferred shift times.
5. In the **Health Information** section:
	* Indicate your COVID-19 vaccination status.
	* If applicable, mention any health conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer.
6. Finally, confirm that you agree to the Volunteer Code of Conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact.
* **Current Address**: Your street address where you live.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Your location (city, state, and ZIP code).
* **Education Level**: Your highest level of education completed.
* **Medical Background**: Indicate whether you have a medical background or relevant training.
* **Relevant Skills or Certifications**: Mention any relevant skills or certifications you possess.
* **Languages You Speak Fluently**: List any languages you speak besides English.
* **Position or Role You Are Interested In**: Choose the type of volunteer work you are interested in (patient care, administrative support, etc.).
* **Time Commitment Available**: Select how many hours per week you can volunteer.
* **Preferred Start Date**: Specify when you can start volunteering.
* **Commitment Duration**: Choose how long you can volunteer (1 month, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Available Days**: Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Available Times**: Select your preferred shift times.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Indicate your vaccination status.
* **Any Health Conditions That May Affect Volunteering**: Mention any health conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer.
* **I Agree to the Volunteer Code of Conduct**: Confirm that you agree to the Volunteer Code of Conduct.

# Note:
This form is designed to ensure that volunteer information is accurate and consistent with our organization's policies. Please provide honest and detailed information to help us match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.
