# CAHPS Cancer Care Drug Therapy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with drug therapy care in the cancer treatment process. Your input will help our facility improve the quality of care provided to patients like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

*   Please answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
*   Select the appropriate option for each question, or type your answer if a text field is available.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - Cancer Care - Drug Therapy Experience

*   **-- Cancer Care - Drug Therapy Experience --** (`drug_therapy_intro`, note, not required):
    This is an introduction to the drug therapy care experience section of the form.
    Please read through the instructions and prepare to answer the questions that follow.
