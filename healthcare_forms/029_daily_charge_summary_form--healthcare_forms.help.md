# Daily Charge Summary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Charge Summary Form is used to record and summarize daily charges, which may be related to billing or financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the charges you want to record.
2. Enter the total number of charges for the selected date.
3. Enter the total payments received for the selected date.
4. Calculate the net charges by subtracting total payments from total charges.
5. Select the charge type (e.g., Cash, Credit, or Other).
6. Enter the charge amount.
7. Select the payment method(s) used (e.g., Cash, Credit, or Other).
8. Enter a brief description of the charges.
9. Indicate the payment status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
10. If necessary, provide more details about the payment method.
11. Add any additional notes or comments.
