# health_appraisal_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_appraisal_questionnaire is a form designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health status, medical history, lifestyle, and insurance details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Answer each question according to your personal medical history. This includes providing details about your overall health status, medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
3. Provide information about your medical history, including any medical procedures you've undergone.
4. Choose the best description for your lifestyle and health insurance details.
5. Add any emergency contacts and medical history records.
