# psychiatric_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The psychiatric referral form is designed to be completed by a mental health practitioner when referring a patient to another healthcare professional or service. It helps to gather relevant information about the patient's mental health status, medical history, and other important details that can aid in the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have obtained the patient's consent to share their information.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the "medical history" field to describe the patient's relevant medical history.
4. Clearly state the reason for referral in the "reason_for_referral" field.
5. Provide a diagnosis and treatment plan in the "diagnosis" and "treatment_plan" fields, respectively.
6. Outline the next steps for the patient's care in the "next_steps" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional information in the "additional_info" field.
8. Finally, specify the follow-up date for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Practitioner**: Enter the name and contact information of the mental health practitioner referring the patient.
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **medical_history**: Describe the patient's relevant medical history, including any past mental health issues, medical conditions, or treatments.
* **reason_for_referral**: State the reason why the patient is being referred to another healthcare professional or service.
* **diagnosis**: Provide a diagnosis for the patient's mental health condition.
* **treatment_plan**: Outline the proposed treatment plan for the patient's care.
* **next_steps**: Outline the next steps for the patient's care, including any recommended appointments, treatments, or follow-ups.
* **additional_info**: Add any additional information about the patient that is relevant to their care.
* **follow_up**: Specify the date for the patient's follow-up appointment or contact.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field, as this information will be used to support the patient's care.
* Ensure that you have the patient's consent to share their information before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any field or question, please consult with your team or seek guidance from a relevant expert.
