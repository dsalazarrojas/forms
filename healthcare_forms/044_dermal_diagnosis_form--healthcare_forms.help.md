# dermal_diagnosis_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dermal_diagnosis_form is used to collect patient information for dermal diagnosis and treatment. It is a comprehensive form that includes fields for patient demographics, medical history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's name and date of birth (DOB) accurately.
2. Provide a brief medical history, if applicable.
3. Indicate whether or not a skin condition is present.
4. List any relevant medical procedures that have been performed.
5. List any medication allergies.
6. Enter any relevant medical notes.
7. Sign as the doctor and obtain a signature from the patient, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient DOB** (date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical History** (text, optional): If the patient has a medical history, enter the details here.
* **Diagnosis** (text, required): Enter the dermal diagnosis.
* **Skin Condition** (select multiple, required): Check all boxes that apply to the patient's skin condition.

  • No
* **Medical Procedure** (text, optional): If a medical procedure has been performed, list the details here.
* **Medication Allergies** (text, optional): If the patient has any medication allergies, list them here.
* **Medical Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Doctor Signature** (select one, required): Sign as the doctor to confirm the diagnosis.

  • No
* **Client Signature** (select one, required): If the patient is new, sign as the patient; otherwise, leave blank.
