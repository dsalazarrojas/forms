# Medical Center Online Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and medical information from patients or visitors to a medical center. It will be used to gather a comprehensive medical history to ensure proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
3. Provide your **Contact Number** for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Address** for contact and visitation purposes.
5. Fill in the **Medical History** fields with any relevant medical information, such as medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any important information.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for visitation and contact purposes.
* **Medical History 1** to **Medical History 18**: These fields are for providing any relevant medical history or information. Please enter any medical conditions, allergies, previous surgeries, or other relevant medical information.
