# medical_assistant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical assistant application form is used to gather necessary information about your medical background and qualifications. This information will be used for administrative purposes to ensure the highest quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields with your accurate and true information.
2. Select the correct options for each field, especially for fields with multiple options.
3. Double-check your inputs before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This is the first name of the person filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This is the last name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is the phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **Medical Assistant Application Form - Experience** (`medical_assistant_application_form_experience`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to describe your experience as a medical assistant.
* **Medical School Attended** (`medical_school_attended`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to describe the medical school you attended.
* **Medical Assistant Application Form - Education** (`medical_assistant_application_form_education`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to describe your education level, such as associate or bachelor's degree.
* **Medical License Issued** (`medical_license_issued`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to enter the date of when your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Issued Date** (`medical_license_issued_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to enter the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical Language Proficiency** (`medical_language_proficiency`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for you to select the languages you are proficient in, such as English or Spanish.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for you to select the certifications you have earned, such as BLS or ACLS.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for you to select the medical specialties you have expertise in, such as general medicine or surgery.
* **Medical Experience** (`medical_experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for you to select your level of experience, such as less than 1 year or 3-5 years.
* **Medical Languages Spoken** (`medical_languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for you to select the languages you can speak, such as English or Spanish.
* **Medical Worked At** (`medical_worked_at`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for you to select if you have worked as a medical assistant, and where you worked.
* **Medical Certifications Earned** (`medical_certifications_earned`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for you to select if you have earned any medical certifications.
* **Medical Specialties Certified** (`medical_specialties_certified`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for you to select if you are certified in any medical specialties.
* **Medical Certifications Date** (`medical_certifications_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to enter the date you earned your medical certifications.
* **Medical School Attended** (`medical_school_attended`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the medical school you attended.
* **Medical Degree** (`medical_degree`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your medical degree.
* **Medical School Graduation Date** (`medical_school_graduation_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to enter the date you graduated from medical school.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your medical license number.
* **Medical Assistant Application Form - Education Level** (`medical_assistant_application_form_education_level`, `select_one`, required): This field is for you to select your education level, such as associate or bachelor's degree.
