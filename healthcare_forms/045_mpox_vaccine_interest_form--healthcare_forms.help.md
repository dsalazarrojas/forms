# Mpox Vaccine Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for individuals interested in receiving the Mpox vaccine. It will help healthcare providers gather necessary information about you, including your contact details, medical history, and vaccination eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it to a healthcare provider for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: 
    Enter your name and any other information you feel is necessary for healthcare providers to know about you.
* **Health History**: 
    Enter any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures that may affect your health and eligibility for the vaccine.
* **Exposure Risk**: 
    Enter your risk level for exposure to the Mpox virus.
* **Vaccine Eligibility**: 
    Enter your eligibility for the vaccine based on your previous answers.
* **Contact Info**: 
    Enter your contact information so healthcare providers can reach out to you with further questions or updates.
* **Medical Conditions**: 
    Enter any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Allergies**: 
    Enter any allergies you have that may affect your health and eligibility for the vaccine.
* **Medical History**: 
    Enter any previous medical procedures or treatments you have undergone.
* **Next Steps**: 
    Enter any next steps you would like to take to receive the vaccine.
* **Patient Name**: 
    Enter the name of the patient (if it is not you).
* **Age**: 
    Enter your age.
* **Date Of Birth**: 
    Enter your date of birth.
* **Email**: 
    Enter your email address so healthcare providers can contact you.
* **Phone**: 
    Enter your phone number so healthcare providers can reach out to you.
* **Address**: 
    Enter your address so healthcare providers can contact you.
* **Exposure Risk Description**: 
    Enter a description of your exposure risk.
* **Vaccine Doses**: 
    Choose the type of vaccine you are interested in receiving.
* **Vaccine Type**: 
    Choose the type of vaccine you are interested in receiving.
* **Vaccination Date**: 
    Enter the date you would like to receive the vaccine.
* **Healthcare Provider**: 
    Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **Health Insurance**: 
    Enter whether you have health insurance.
* **Medical Record Number**: 
    Enter your medical record number.
* **Medical Record Provider**: 
    Enter whether your healthcare provider has your medical record.
* **Contact Person**: 
    Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: 
    Enter the phone number of the contact person.

Note: This guide is for general information purposes only and not intended to be a comprehensive medical guide. Always follow the guidance of a healthcare professional for medical advice and treatment.
