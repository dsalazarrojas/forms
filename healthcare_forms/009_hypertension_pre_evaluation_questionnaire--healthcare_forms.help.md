# Hypertension Pre Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypertension Pre Evaluation Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, lifestyle, and other relevant factors related to hypertension. The form is intended to help healthcare professionals evaluate the risk of hypertension in patients and provide personalized guidance for prevention and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
3.  Please fill out the form in a quiet and private area, free from distractions.
4.  If you need to leave the form, please save your progress and return to it when you are ready to continue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`):
	+ This field is for entering your personal information, such as your name, contact details, and medical ID number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`):
	+ This field is for listing any pre-existing medical conditions, such as heart disease, diabetes, or asthma.
* **Medications** (`medications`):
	+ This field is for listing any medications you are currently taking.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`):
	+ This field is for recording your blood pressure reading.
* **Family History** (`family_history`):
	+ This field is for listing any family members with a history of hypertension.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`):
	+ This field is for listing any risk factors you may have for developing hypertension, such as smoking or a family history of heart disease.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`):
	+ This field is for listing any medical conditions you may have, such as high cholesterol or kidney disease.
* **Lifestyle** (`lifestyle`):
	+ This field is for describing your lifestyle habits, such as your diet, exercise routine, and stress levels.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`):
	+ This field is for listing any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`):
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- High: You are a smoker.
		- Low: You are a light smoker (less than 1 cigarette per day).
		- None: You do not smoke.
* **Hypertension** (`hypertension`):
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- High: You have high blood pressure.
		- Medium: You have moderately high blood pressure.
		- Low: You have low blood pressure.
* **Blood Pressure Averages** (`blood_pressure_averages`):
	+ This field is for recording your average blood pressure readings.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`):
	+ This field is for listing any medications you may be taking.
* **Other Conditions** (`other_conditions`):
	+ This field is for listing any other medical conditions you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Please fill out the form in a quiet and private area, free from distractions.
* If you need to leave the form, please save your progress and return to it when you are ready to continue.
