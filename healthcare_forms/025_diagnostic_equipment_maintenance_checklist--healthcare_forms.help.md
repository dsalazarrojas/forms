# Diagnostic Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track maintenance information for diagnostic equipment, ensuring that critical devices are properly maintained to prevent malfunctions and ensure patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of equipment being maintained from the options provided in the "Equipment Type" field.
2. Enter the frequency of maintenance required for the selected equipment in the "Maintenance Frequency" field.
3. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed in the "Last Maintenance Date" field.
4. Enter the date for the next scheduled maintenance in the "Next Maintenance Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of diagnostic equipment being maintained from the available options to ensure accurate maintenance tracking.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`Maintenance Frequency`, text, optional): Enter the recommended frequency of maintenance for the selected equipment to prevent malfunctions and ensure optimal performance.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`Last Maintenance Date`, date, optional): Record the date of the last maintenance performed to track the equipment's maintenance history.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`Next Maintenance Date`, date, optional): Enter the scheduled date for the next maintenance to stay on track and prevent missed maintenance.
