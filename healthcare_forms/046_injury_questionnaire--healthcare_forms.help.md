# Injury Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injury Questionnaire form is designed to collect information about a patient's injury, including medical history, symptoms, and treatment plans. This information will help healthcare professionals provide the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about your injury.
2.  Fill out each field with the requested information, making sure to answer all required fields (denoted by a asterisk \*).
3.  Use the provided options for select_multiple and time fields.
4.  If you're unsure about any field, contact the healthcare professional responsible for your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Happened** (1: introduction): Describe the circumstances surrounding your injury, including the date and time it occurred.
* **Medical History** (2: medical_history): Share any relevant medical history, including previous injuries or conditions that may impact your current injury.
* **Pain Level** (3: pain_level): Report your current pain level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
* **Symptoms** (4: symptoms): List any symptoms you're experiencing, such as pain, numbness, or limited range of motion. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Treatment Plan** (5: treatment_plan): Outline your current treatment plan, including any medications, therapies, or appointments.
* **Daily Activities** (6: daily_activities): Describe any changes or limitations you've experienced in your daily activities due to your injury.
* **Follow Up** (7: follow_up): Schedule a follow-up appointment with your healthcare professional if necessary.
* **Doctor's Info** (8: doctor_info): Leave notes or comments for your healthcare professional about your treatment.
* **Contact Details** (9: contact_details): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Patient Info** (10: patient_info): Enter your email address for secure correspondence with your healthcare professional.

Please submit your completed form to the designated healthcare professional.
