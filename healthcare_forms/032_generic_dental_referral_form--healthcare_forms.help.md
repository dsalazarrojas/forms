# Generic Dental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Generic Dental Referral Form is a tool used to facilitate communication between healthcare professionals in referring patients to dental specialists. This form is meant to be completed by the referring healthcare provider, providing crucial information about the patient's condition, treatment plans, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred, either from the provided options or type "Other" and enter the specialist's name in the "Other Specialist" field.
3. Choose the reason for referral from the options provided in the "Referral Reason" field.
4. Enter any relevant medical history or treatment plans in the "Medical History" and "Treatment Plans" fields.
5. Enter the patient's contact information, including their contact address, phone number, and any other relevant details in the "Contact Info" field.
6. If applicable, select the specialist's availability from the options provided in the "Specialist Info" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the patient's condition or referral in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (2): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Specialist Name** (3): Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred. Options include:
	+ Dentist
	+ Orthodontist
	+ Periodontist
	+ Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon
	+ Pediatric Dentist
	+ Endodontist
	+ Oral Pathologist
	+ Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Specialist
	+ Orthodontic Specialist
* **Other Specialist** (4): Enter the name of a specialist not listed above.
* **Referral Reason** (5): Select the reason for referral from the options provided:
	+ Urgent care
	+ Specialist referral
	+ Other
* **Medical History** (6): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions affecting the patient.
* **Treatment Plans** (7): Enter any treatment plans or recommendations for the patient.
* **Contact Info** (8): Enter the patient's contact information, including address and phone number.
* **Follow up Schedules** (9): Enter the patient's next appointment schedule in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Next Appointment** (10): Enter the date of the next scheduled appointment.
* **Patient Relationship** (11): Enter the relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider.
* **Specialist Info** (12): Select the specialist's availability from the options provided:
	+ Available 9am-5pm
	+ Available 10am-4pm
	+ By appointment only
* **Notes** (13): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition or referral.
