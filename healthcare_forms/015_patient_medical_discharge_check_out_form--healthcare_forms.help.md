# Patient Medical Discharge Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record patient information and discharge instructions when a patient is being checked out of a healthcare facility. The goal is to ensure that the patient's medical history is up-to-date and that they have a clear understanding of their post-discharge care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history and ensure that all necessary information is recorded.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter the date of discharge in the "discharge_date" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the patient's next appointment in the "next_appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **patient_name** (Text)
patient_name is the field for the patient's name.

### 2. **discharge_date** (Date)
discharge_date is the field for the date the patient is being discharged from the healthcare facility.

### 3. **next_appointment** (Date)
next_appointment is the field for the date and time of the patient's next scheduled appointment.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form with the patient to ensure they understand their post-discharge care instructions.
- Update the form as necessary to reflect any changes in the patient's medical history or care instructions.
