# Lifemark Informed Consent Telehealth Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent and relevant patient information for telehealth treatments. It ensures that patients understand and agree to the terms of the treatment and provides a platform to collect contact and medical history details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please review the patient information page and fill in the required details such as name, contact number, and any other information requested.
2. On the patient consent page, select "I understand and agree to the terms" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.
3. If you have any medical history or health concerns, please fill in the medical history field.
4. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and relationship with you.
5. Confirm your HIPAA consent by selecting "I have reviewed and understand the HIPAA Consent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This page is used to collect basic patient information such as name, contact number, and any other details requested. Please fill in the required fields carefully and accurately.

### Patient Consent
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the telehealth treatment. Select "I understand and agree to the terms" to complete this field.

### Telehealth Treatment
* **Telehealth Treatment** (`telehealth_treatment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect more details about the telehealth treatment. Please provide a clear and concise description of the treatment.

### HIPAA Consent
* **HIPAA Consent** (`hipaa_consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your understanding of the HIPAA Consent. Select both "I have reviewed and understand the HIPAA Consent" to complete this field.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information in case the healthcare provider needs to reach you. Please provide a valid contact number and email address.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and relationship with you. This is crucial for the healthcare provider to contact them in case of an emergency.

### Contact Relationship
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact. Please select "parent", "spouse", "friend", etc.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any medical history or health concerns you may have. Please provide detailed and accurate information to help the healthcare provider prepare for your treatment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* The information provided will be kept confidential and secure under the HIPAA guidelines.
