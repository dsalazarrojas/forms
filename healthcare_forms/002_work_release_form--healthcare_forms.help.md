# Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Release Form is a document used to communicate information about an employee's work status, reason for release, and other relevant details between healthcare providers and employers.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the employee's name, ID, and employer's name and address.
2.  Specify the doctor's name and date of release.
3.  Enter the reason for release and time of release.
4.  Have both the employee and employer sign the form to confirm its completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being released.
*   **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee being released.
*   **Employer Name**: Enter the name of the employer providing the employee's work release.
*   **Employer Address**: Enter the address of the employer providing the employee's work release.
*   **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider releasing the employee.
*   **Date of Release**: Enter the date the employee is being released to return to work.
*   **Time of Release**: Enter the time of the employee's release.
*   **Reason for Release**: Enter a detailed explanation for the employee's release.
*   **Employer Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the employer's understanding and agreement with the release.
*   **Employee Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the employee's understanding and agreement with the release.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately before submitting it.
*   Review the form for completeness and ensure all required fields are filled before submission.
*   Store the completed form in a secure location for future reference.
