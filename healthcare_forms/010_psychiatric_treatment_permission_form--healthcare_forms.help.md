# Psychiatric Treatment Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain necessary information from patients for psychiatric treatment. It helps healthcare providers to understand the patient's medical condition, treatment plan, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including name, date of birth, contact number, and email.
2. Select the treatment plan that suits the patient's needs (medication, therapy, or hospitalization).
3. Confirm your consent for the treatment plan.
4. Provide the healthcare provider's name and contact information.
5. Specify the treatment location (home, hospital, or clinic).
6. Confirm your emergency contact information.
7. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
8. Provide any additional medical conditions that may be relevant to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Information** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's gender (male, female, or other).
* **Patient Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the treatment frequency.
* **Treatment Location** (`treatment_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the treatment location (home, hospital, or clinic).
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `select_one`, required): Select the healthcare provider (Dr. Smith or Dr. Johnson).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your consent for the treatment plan (medication, hospitalization, or therapy).
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your consent for any additional treatment plan.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact** (`healthcare_provider_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's contact information.
* **Healthcare Provider 2** (`healthcare_provider_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the healthcare provider 2 (Dr. Smith or Dr. Johnson).
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number 2.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify the relationship with the healthcare provider.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date signed.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signature.
* **Healthcare Organization** (`healthcare_organization`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare organization.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `number`, required): Enter your signature (this field is not clearly defined; please clarify with the healthcare team).
* **Healthcare Provider 2 Contact** (`healthcare_provider_2_contact`, `text`, required): This field is not clearly defined; please clarify with the healthcare team.
* **Emergency Contact Number 2** (`emergency_contact_number_2`, `text`, required): This field is not clearly defined; please clarify with the healthcare team.
