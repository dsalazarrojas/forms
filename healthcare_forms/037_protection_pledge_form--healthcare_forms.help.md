# Protection Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protection Pledge Form is a crucial document that allows students or employees to express their commitment to protecting themselves and others from workplace hazards. This form is designed to ensure that individuals understand their responsibilities and obligations in maintaining a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in the following fields:
   - **Employee ID** (`employee`): Enter your ID number.
   - **Date of First Pledge** (`date_of_first_pledge`): Enter the date when you took your protection pledge.
   - **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person or department you will contact in case of an emergency.
   - **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person or department.
   - **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person or department.
   - **Department Head** (`department_head`): Select whether you are the department head.
   - **Manager** (`manager`): Select whether you have a manager.
2. Choose the correct options for the following fields:
   - **Student or Employee** (`student_or_employee`): Select whether you are a student or an employee.
   - **Location** (`location`): Select where you work (Remote or On-Site).
   - **Department** (`department`): Select your department (HR, Operations, or Finance).
   - **Shift** (`shift`): Select your working shift (Morning, Afternoon, or Night).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`): Enter your ID number to identify you as an employee.
* **Date of First Pledge** (`date_of_first_pledge`): Record the date when you took your protection pledge, indicating your commitment to workplace safety.
* **Student or Employee** (`student_or_employee`): Choose whether you are a student or an employee. This helps us understand your specific needs and responsibilities.
* **Location** (`location`): Select whether you work remotely or on-site, as this affects your safety procedures.
* **Department** (`department`): Choose your department to ensure we have the right contact information and safety protocols for you.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Indicate your working shift to help us schedule safety training and resources accordingly.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person or department you will contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`) & **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact information so we can reach you quickly in an emergency.
* **Department Head** (`department_head`): If you are the department head, indicate this to receive priority attention and support.
* **Manager** (`manager`): If you have a manager, select this option to ensure we have the right contact information.
* **Role** (`role`): Choose your role within the organization to ensure we provide the right support and resources for your position.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
