# medical_record_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical record amendment form is designed to allow patients or their authorized representatives to request corrections or updates to a patient's medical record. This form is used to collect the necessary information for the amendment process, ensuring that the record is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's information, including any necessary contact details and demographics.
2. Describe the changes you are requesting to be made to the patient's medical record.
3. Provide your signature as a patient or representative, confirming that you are the authorized person submitting the request.
4. Enter the date of your request.
5. Provide the name and signature of the healthcare provider who will process this request (if applicable).
6. Confirm your medical record amendment request by submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info Form** (`patient_info_form`, text, required): This is the initial page of the form where you will enter the patient's information, including any necessary contact details and demographics.
- **Medical Record Changes** (`medical_record_changes`, text, required): Describe the changes you are requesting to be made to the patient's medical record. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): This is your digital signature as a patient or representative, confirming that you are the authorized person submitting the request.
- **Healthcare Provider Info** (`healthcare_provider_info`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name and information of the healthcare provider who will process this request.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request for the medical record amendment.
- **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required): If applicable, enter the signature of the healthcare provider who will process this request.
- **Medical Record Amendment Request** (`medical_record_amendment_request`, text, required): This is an optional field for any additional information you would like to provide about your request.
