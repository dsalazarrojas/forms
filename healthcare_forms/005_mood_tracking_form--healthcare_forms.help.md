# mood_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mood tracking form is used to capture information on a patient's mood, anxiety levels, energy levels, and other health indicators. This helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's mental state and develop targeted treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information, marked by an asterisk (\*), for each field.
* Enter your name and contact information to associate with the form submission.
* Answer the mood tracking questions truthfully and accurately.
* Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Mood Rating** (`mood_rating`): Rate your current mood on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is extremely low and 10 is extremely high.
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`): Rate your current anxiety level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is extremely high and 10 is extremely low.
* **Energy Level** (`energy_level`): Rate your current energy level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is extremely low and 10 is extremely high.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Appetite** (`appetite`): Rate your current appetite on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
* **Social Interaction** (`social_interaction`): Check if you have interacted with others today.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`): Select all the emotional states that apply to you, such as happy, sad, frustrated, anxious, angry, bored, or confused.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or remarks about your mood or condition.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the current date.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the current time.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`): Enter the person or organization submitting to.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`): Select the time of submission.
* **Timestamp Date** (`timestamp_date`): Select the date of submission.
* **Mood Scale** (`mood_scale`): Indicate if you have been able to track your mood over time.
* **Anxiety Scale** (`anxiety_scale`): Indicate if you have been able to track your anxiety levels over time.
* **Energy Scale** (`energy_scale`): Indicate if you have been able to track your energy levels over time.
* **Sleep Scale** (`sleep_scale`): Indicate if you have been able to track your sleep quality over time.
