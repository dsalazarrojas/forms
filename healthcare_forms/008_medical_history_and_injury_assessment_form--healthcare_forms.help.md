# Medical History And Injury Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information about a patient's medical history and injury assessment. It includes sections for patient information, medical history, injury information, treatment plans, and follow-up appointments. This form is intended to be completed by medical professionals to gather relevant data for patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the patient information section, which includes the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Move on to the medical history section, where you can describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or medical procedures.
3. In the injury information section, provide details about any injuries or accidents that have occurred.
4. If applicable, answer the medication use question with "Yes" or "No".
5. Provide any relevant medical allergies or sensitivities in the medical allergies section.
6. Describe any medical procedures in the medical procedures section.
7. In the medical history and injuries section, you can add any additional medical history or injury information not already covered.
8. If necessary, select the urgency level (Low, Medium, or High) for the medical procedure in the medical procedure urgency section.
9. Choose the priority level (Low, Medium, or High) for the medical procedure in the medical procedure priority section.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the medical procedure in the medical procedure notes section.
11. In the patient injury info section, provide details about any injuries or accidents that have occurred.
12. Create a treatment plan in the treatment plan section.
13. Update the treatment status in the treatment status section.
14. Schedule follow-up appointments with the patient, including the date and time in the follow-up appointments section.
15. Select the urgency level (Low, Medium, or High) for the follow-up appointment in the follow-up appointments urgency section.
16. Choose the priority level (Low, Medium, or High) for the follow-up appointment in the follow-up appointments priority section.
17. Add any notes or comments about the follow-up appointment in the follow-up appointments notes section.
18. Update the follow-up appointment status in the follow-up appointments status section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info (id: 1)**: A text field to enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **medical_history (id: 2)**: A text field to describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or medical procedures.
* **injury_info (id: 3)**: A text field to provide details about any injuries or accidents that have occurred.
* **medication_use (id: 4)**: A select multiple field to answer "Yes" or "No" about medication use.
* **medical_allergies (id: 5)**: A text field to list any medical allergies or sensitivities.
* **medical_procedures (id: 6)**: A text field to describe any medical procedures.
* **medical_history_and_injuries (id: 7)**: A text field to add any additional medical history or injury information not already covered.
* **medical_procedure_date (id: 9)**: A date field to schedule a medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_time (id: 10)**: A time field to schedule a medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_details (id: 11)**: A text field to add any additional notes or comments about the medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_urgency (id: 12)**: A select one field to choose the urgency level (Low, Medium, or High) for the medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_priority (id: 13)**: A select one field to choose the priority level (Low, Medium, or High) for the medical procedure.
* **medical_procedure_notes (id: 14)**: A text field to add any additional notes or comments about the medical procedure.
* **patient_injury_info (id: 15)**: A text field to provide details about any injuries or accidents that have occurred.
* **treatment_plan (id: 16)**: A text field to create a treatment plan.
* **treatment_status (id: 17)**: A text field to update the treatment status.
* **follow_up_appointments (id: 18)**: A text field to schedule follow-up appointments with the patient.
* **follow_up_appointments_date (id: 19)**: A date field to schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_time (id: 20)**: A time field to schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_details (id: 21)**: A text field to add any notes or comments about the follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_urgency (id: 22)**: A select one field to choose the urgency level (Low, Medium, or High) for the follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_priority (id: 23)**: A select one field to choose the priority level (Low, Medium, or High) for the follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_notes (id: 24)**: A text field to add any notes or comments about the follow-up appointment.
* **follow_up_appointments_status (id: 25)**: A text field to update the follow-up appointment status.

## Tips

* Ensure that you accurately fill out each section to provide complete and accurate patient information.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar mistakes.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, consult with a medical professional.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the patient.
