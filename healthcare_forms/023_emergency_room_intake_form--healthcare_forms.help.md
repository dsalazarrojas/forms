# Emergency Room Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Room Intake Form is a collection of questions designed to gather vital information about patients visiting the emergency room. This information helps healthcare professionals provide the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the form carefully and accurately**: Please provide clear and concise answers to all questions.
2. **Provide detailed information**: Share as much information as possible, but do not include any personal medical records or documents.
3. **Check your answers for errors**: Review your form for any mistakes or inconsistencies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Patient Information**
**Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): 
This field is where you can enter your name, date of birth, or any other relevant identifying information.

*Please note that this field is optional, but providing your correct name and date of birth will help us better identify you and your medical records.*

### **2. Contact Details**
**Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): 
This field is for entering your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.

*You can include any relevant contact information you feel comfortable sharing, but this is not required.*

### **3. Emergency Contact**
**Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): 
If you have an emergency contact, such as a family member or friend, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.

*This information will only be used in case of an emergency. You can leave this field blank if there is no emergency contact.*

### **4. Medical History**
**Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): 
Describe any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking. This information will help healthcare professionals better understand your medical history.

*Please be as detailed as possible, but do not include any confidential medical records or documents.*

## Tips
*Be honest and accurate when completing the form.*
*If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.*
*Please remember that your information will only be used to provide you with the best possible care and treatment.*
