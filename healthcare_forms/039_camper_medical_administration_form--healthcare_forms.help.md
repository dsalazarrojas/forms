# Camper Medical Administration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Medical Administration Form is used to collect information about a camper's personal and medical history to ensure proper medical care and safety at camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the camper's personal information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, and age.
2. Select their gender from the provided options.
3. Enter their parent or guardian's name, phone number, and email address.
4. Provide their home address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Select the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the camper.
6. Enter the name and contact information of their primary physician.
7. List any known medical conditions, allergies, and current medications.
8. Provide information about any recent hospitalizations, surgeries, and immunization status.
9. Authorize medical treatment and medication administration.
10. Provide the camper's blood type, if known.
11. Describe any allergies and medical conditions.
12. Indicate if an EpiPen is required.
13. List any behavioral concerns or mental health issues.
14. Authorize medical treatment and medication administration.
15. Sign and date the form with the parent or guardian's signature.
16. Sign and date the form with the camper's physician's signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the camper's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter the camper's current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the camper's gender from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's full name, including first and last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's home address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city or town where the camper lives.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where the camper lives.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the camper's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the camper.
* **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the primary physician's name.
* **Primary Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the primary physician's phone number.
* **List of Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List all known medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List all known allergies.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, required): List all current medications, including dosages and schedule.
* **Is EpiPen Required** (`epipen_required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if an EpiPen is required.
* **Immunization Status** (`immunization_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the immunization status from the provided options.
* **Date of Last Tetanus Shot** (`tetanus_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last tetanus shot, if known.
* **Mental Health Concerns** (`mental_health_concerns`, `text`, required): Describe any mental health concerns.
* **Behavioral Supports Needed** (`behavioral_supports`, `text`, required): Describe any behavioral concerns or supports needed.
* **I Authorize Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Indicate authorization for medical treatment.
* **I Authorize Medication Administration** (`medication_administration_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Indicate authorization for medication administration.
* **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the blood type from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the form with the parent or guardian's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date signed.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the form with the physician's signature, if required.
* **Physician Signature Date** (`physician_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date signed, if required. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* This form is not a substitute for proper medical care. Camp staff will use the information to provide additional support and services, but medical emergencies may still arise.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the camp administration before filling out this form.
