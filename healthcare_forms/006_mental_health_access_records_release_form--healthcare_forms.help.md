# mental-health-access-records-release-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mental health access records release form is a document that grants authorized providers access to a patient's mental health records. It is a crucial step in the process of ensuring that providers have the necessary information to provide care to the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the provider to whom you are granting access in the "Authorized Provider" field.
3. Select the "Authorized Provider Type" from the provided options (Mental Health Professional or Primary Care Physician).
4. Enter the reason for granting access in the "Authorization Reason" field, if required.
5. If necessary, enter the duration of authorization in the "Authorization Duration" field.
6. Enter the signature of the person authorizing access in the "Signed By" field, if required.
7. Select the "Authorization Status" from the provided options (Active, Denied, or In Review).
8. Enter the date the form was signed in the "Authorization Date" field, if required.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the authorization in the "Comments" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, as it appears on their medical records.
* **Authorized Provider** (`authorized_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the provider to whom you are granting access.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the authorization was granted or revoked.
* **Authorization Start Date** (`authorization_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the authorization started.
* **Authorization End Date** (`authorization_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the authorization ends.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the provider who will be accessing the patient's records.
* **Authorized Provider Type** (`authorized_provider_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of provider (Mental Health Professional or Primary Care Physician).
* **Authorization Reason** (`authorization_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for granting access, if required.
* **Authorization Duration** (`authorization_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the authorization, if necessary.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person authorizing access, if required.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the signature of the person authorizing access, if required.
* **Authorization Status** (`authorization_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the authorization (Active, Denied, or In Review).
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the authorization was granted or revoked, if required.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the authorization, if required.
