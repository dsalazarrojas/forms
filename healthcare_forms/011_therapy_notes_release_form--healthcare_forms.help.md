# Therapy Notes Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Notes Release Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients and healthcare providers to ensure the release of accurate and secure therapy notes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with the client's contact information, including their name and any other relevant details. 
2. Record the date and time of your last contact with the client in the "Date Of Last Contact" field. 
3. In the "Client Consent" field, confirm that you have obtained the client's consent for the release of their therapy notes. 
4. Enter your own information as the healthcare provider in the "Provider Information" field. 
5. Provide your own signature in the "Signature" field to confirm the accuracy of the information provided. 
6. In the "Client Name" and "Provider Name" fields, enter the names of the client and provider, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, Text, Optional): Enter the client's contact information, such as their name, address, and phone number.
* **Date Of Last Contact** (Date Of Last Contact, Date, Optional): Record the date and time of your last contact with the client.
* **Client Consent** (Client Consent, Text, Optional): Confirm that you have obtained the client's consent for the release of their therapy notes.
* **Provider Information** (Provider Information, Text, Optional): Enter your own information as the healthcare provider.
* **Release Form** (Release Form, Text, Optional): This field is intended for notes about the release of therapy notes, but it is not clearly defined, so it's best-effort intention is to provide any additional information about the release process.
* **Signature** (Signature, Text, Optional): Provide your own signature to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Optional): Record the date of the current action.
* **Time** (Time, Time, Optional): Record the time of the current action.
* **Client Name** (Client Name, Text, Optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Provider Name** (Provider Name, Text, Optional): Enter your own name as the healthcare provider.

## Tips
* Make sure to have the client sign the release form to ensure their consent. 
* Verify the accuracy of the information you enter in the "Client Information" and "Provider Information" fields. 
* Use a clear and legible font when signing the "Signature" field. 
* Ensure the date and time of last contact are recorded accurately in the "Date Of Last Contact" and "Time" fields. 

Note: The form's fields are not clearly defined, so the explanations are based on best-effort intentions and may require further clarification from a healthcare professional.
