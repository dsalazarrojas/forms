# health_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information, contact details, and medical history for healthcare-related purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the personal information and contact details sections accurately and completely.
3.  Provide a detailed medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (`personal_info`, `text`, **required**):
    This section collects the patient's basic identification data.
* **Contact Details (`contact_info`, `text`, **optional**):
    Enter the patient's contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Medical History (`health_details`, `text`, **optional**):
    Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or treatments.

## Tips

*   Be thorough when providing medical history, as this information is crucial for healthcare professionals to make informed decisions.
*   Use clear and accurate language when filling in the form.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
