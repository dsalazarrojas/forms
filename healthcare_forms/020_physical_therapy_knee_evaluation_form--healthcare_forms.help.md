# Physical Therapy Knee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's knee evaluation, which is a crucial process in the physical therapy field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and contact information in the **Patient Information** section.
2. Describe your knee history in the **Knee History** section, including any recent or past injuries, surgeries, or conditions affecting your knee.
3. Share your medical history in the **Medical History** section, highlighting any pre-existing medical conditions, surgeries, or medications that may impact your knee health.
4. Explain your physical therapy needs and goals in the **Physical Therapy** section.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the **Review** section to share them with your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information, so your healthcare provider can efficiently communicate with you about your knee evaluation.
* **Knee History**: Describe your knee history, including any recent or past injuries, surgeries, or conditions that may be affecting your knee.
* **Medical History**: Share your medical history, including any pre-existing medical conditions, surgeries, or medications that may impact your knee health.
* **Physical Therapy**: Explain your physical therapy needs and goals, including any specific objectives you want to achieve through treatment.
* **Review**: Use this section to share any additional comments or questions you have about your knee evaluation or physical therapy.
