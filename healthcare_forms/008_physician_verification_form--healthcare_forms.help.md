# Physician Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Verification Form is used to gather detailed information about a physician for verification purposes. This information helps ensure the accuracy of the verification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the First Name field with the physician's first name.
2.  Fill in the Last Name field with the physician's last name.
3.  For the Specialties field, select all applicable specialties that the physician has.
4.  Enter the physician's address in the Address field.
5.  Enter the physician's phone number in the Phone field.
6.  Enter the physician's email address in the Email field.
7.  Fill in the License field with the physician's license number.
8.  Enter the state where the physician's license is issued in the License State field.
9.  Enter the date the physician's license was issued in the License Issued field.
10. Select Yes or No for whether the physician has been issued with other licenses.
11. Select Yes or No for whether the physician has worked with other physicians.
12. Enter any other treatment details in the Treatment field.
13. Enter any other procedure details in the Procedure field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`physician_verification_form_input`, text, required: false): Enter the physician's first name.
* **Last Name** (`physician_verification_form_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the physician's last name.
* **Specialties** (`physician_verification_form_specialties`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable specialties that the physician has.
* **Address** (`physician_verification_form_address`, text, required: false): Enter the physician's address.
* **Phone** (`physician_verification_form_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Email** (`physician_verification_form_email`, email, required: false): Enter the physician's email address.
* **License** (`physician_verification_form_license`, text, required: false): Enter the physician's license number.
* **License State** (`physician_verification_form_license_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the physician's license is issued.
* **License Issued** (`physician_verification_form_license_issued`, date, required: false): Enter the date the physician's license was issued.
* **License Exp** (`physician_verification_form_license_exp`, date, required: false): Enter the expiration date of the physician's license.
* **Issued Licenses** (`physician_verification_form_issued_licenses`, select_multiple, required: false): Select Yes or No for whether the physician has been issued with other licenses.
* **Worked With** (`physician_verification_form_worked_with`, select_multiple, required: false): Select Yes or No for whether the physician has worked with other physicians.
* **Treatment** (`physician_verification_form_treatment`, text, required: false): Enter any other treatment details.
* **Procedure** (`physician_verification_form_procedure`, text, required: false): Enter any other procedure details.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a successful verification process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for support.
* Please note that some fields may be mandatory, but it's always a good idea to fill out as much information as possible to aid the verification process.
