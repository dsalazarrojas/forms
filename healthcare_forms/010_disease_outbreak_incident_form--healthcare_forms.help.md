# Disease Outbreak Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Disease Outbreak Incident Form" is a tool used by healthcare professionals to report and document incidents of diseases such as common cold, influenza, and pneumonia. This form helps in tracking and managing outbreaks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct disease type from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct incident status from the options provided.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if needed.
5. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name and contact information.
* **Disease Info** (`disease_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe the disease or condition reported.
* **Outbreak Location** (`outbreak_location`, `text`, required: false): Provide the location where the outbreak occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time Note** (`incident_time_note`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information about the incident time.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_trace`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the cases related to the incident from the options provided.
* **Disease Type** (`disease_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the type of disease from the options provided.
* **Reporting Provider** (`reporting_provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Provider Phone** (`reporting_provider_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Reporting Provider Email** (`reporting_provider_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Reporting Provider Address** (`reporting_provider_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the healthcare provider.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the current status of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Status Note** (`incident_status_note`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information about the incident status.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Describe the next steps to be taken regarding the incident.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the form is being submitted.
* **Incident Location Note** (`incident_location_note`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information about the incident location.
* **Outbreak** (`incident_outbreak`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outbreak incident in more detail.
* **Outbreak Note** (`incident_outbreak_note`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about the outbreak incident.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals and is not intended for public access.
