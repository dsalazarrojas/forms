# supplemental_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **supplemental_health_questionnaire** form is designed to gather information about a patient's health and travel history, contact with infected individuals, symptoms, and risk factors related to their medical condition. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's situation and provide personalized guidance and care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as these are essential for accurate assessment.
3. Select all relevant options for each field, even if it's a single option.
4. Provide detailed and accurate answers to the text fields.
5. Submit the completed form to proceed with the assessment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen Questions** (`screen_questions`, text, optional):
	+ Answer any questions that may be relevant to your current health status.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any recent travel or exposure to potentially infectious environments.
* **Contact with Infected** (`contact_with_infected`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if you've had any contact with someone infected with a disease.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional):
	+ List any symptoms you're currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or headache.
* **Exposure History** (`exposure_history`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any recent exposure to potential health risks.
* **Travel History 2** (`travel_history_2`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all travel-related options that apply to you.
* **Travel Restrictions** (`travel_restrictions`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of travel restrictions you're currently following.
* **Symptoms 2** (`symptoms_2`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if you've experienced any of the listed symptoms.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, text, optional):
	+ Mention any factors that may affect your health, such as smoking or obesity.
* **Contact with Infected 2** (`contact_with_infected_2`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if you've had any contact with someone infected with a disease.
## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this information will help healthcare professionals provide accurate guidance.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional for clarification.
* The form is designed to be completed by the patient or their representative.
