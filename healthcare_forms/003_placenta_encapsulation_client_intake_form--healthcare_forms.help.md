# Placenta Encapsulation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Placenta Encapsulation Client Intake Form is a health-related form used by healthcare professionals to gather information about clients' personal data and medical history for placenta encapsulation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (page 1).
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (page 1).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (page 1).
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (page 1).
6. Enter your address (optional) in the "Address" field (page 1).
7. Select the desired encapsulation services in the "Encapsulation Services" field, which is a dropdown menu with three options (page 2).
8. List any medical conditions you have or are concerned about in the "Medical Conditions" field (page 2).
9. If you have any comments or additional information, please use the "Comments" field (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This is the email address you want us to contact you at.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): If you would like us to contact you at your home or office address, please provide it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, required): Please enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Encapsulation Services** (`Encapsulation Services`, select one, required): Choose the services you are interested in from the available options.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have or are concerned about.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or questions, please let us know here.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
* It is essential to provide your accurate medical history to ensure proper care and services.
