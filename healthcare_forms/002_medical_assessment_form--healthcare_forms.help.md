# Medical Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Assessment Form is used to gather and document a patient's medical history, conditions, allergies, and contact information. This form helps healthcare providers to quickly and accurately gather essential information for patient consultations and ongoing care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and legibly, using the options provided in the dropdown fields.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields for medical history and next steps.
3. If unsure about any field, refer to the provided explanations for guidance.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This page is for providing basic patient contact information, such as their name, contact number, and email address.
	+ **Contact Number** (text): A field for entering the patient's phone number.
	+ **Email Address** (email): A field for entering the patient's email address.
* **Medical Information**: This section collects information about the patient's medical history and conditions.
	+ **Medical Conditions** (select multiple): Check all that apply for any known medical conditions.
	+ **Medical Conditions** (select one): Select the primary medical condition being assessed.
	+ **Medical History** (note): Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant details.
* **Allergies and Contact Information**: This section collects information about the patient's allergies and contact details.
	+ **Allergies** (select multiple): Check all that apply for any known allergies.
	+ **Allergies** (select one): Select the primary allergy to note.
	+ **Date of Last Contact** (date): Record the date of the patient's last contact with the healthcare provider.
	+ **Date of Last Visit** (date): Record the date of the patient's last visit with the healthcare provider.
* **Additional Information**: This section provides space for any further notes or details.
	+ **Medical Notes** (note): Add any relevant medical notes or observations.
	+ **Next Steps** (text): Outline any next steps or follow-up actions for the patient.
	+ **Medical Professional** (note): Record the name of the medical professional who is treating the patient.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and relevance.
* If unsure about any field or requirement, refer to the healthcare provider or relevant medical resources for guidance.
* Keep the form in a secure and accessible location for easy reference.
