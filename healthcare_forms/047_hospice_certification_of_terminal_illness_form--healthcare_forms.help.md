# Hospice Certification Of Terminal Illness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a written statement from a medical provider confirming a patient's terminal illness. This document is used for various administrative processes, including insurance claims and legal documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each field to understand the information required.
- Answer each question truthfully and accurately based on the patient's condition.
- Make sure to sign the medical provider signature field, as it confirms the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (patient_info): This field is for the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details. Please provide the patient's full name and any relevant contact information.
* **medical_provider_info** (medical_provider_info): In this field, please provide your medical provider information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant credentials.
* **diagnosis** (diagnosis): Describe the patient's terminal illness diagnosis, including the relevant medical conditions and any supporting documentation (e.g., lab results, medical records).
* **medical_team_members** (medical_team_members): List the names of any medical team members involved in the patient's care, including doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals.
* **medical_history** (medical_history): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, treatments, or surgeries.
* **hospice_info** (hospice_info): In this field, provide information about the patient's hospice care, including the type of care required (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, home care).
* **signature** (signature): This field is for your medical provider signature, confirming that you have reviewed and verified the information provided.
* **patient_signature** (patient_signature): This field is for the patient's signature, indicating that they have reviewed and agreed with the information provided.
* **medical_provider_signature** (medical_provider_signature): This field is for your signature as the medical provider, confirming that the information provided is accurate and complete.
