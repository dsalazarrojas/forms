# Employee Pre-Return Work Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Pre-Return Work Health Questionnaire is a form used to gather crucial information from employees before they return to work after a medical episode or quarantine. This information helps ensure that the employee's health status and work readiness are assessed accurately, which contributes to maintaining a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information about your health status and work readiness.
* Enter your Employee ID, which is your unique identifier in our system.
* Provide your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* Enter your email address (if available).
* Enter your phone number.
* Indicate any symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* Answer whether you have a medical condition that needs attention.
* Choose your expected return date.
* Enter the number of work hours you are able to work when you return.
* Enter the number of work days you will be able to work when you return.
* Provide any additional comments or notes about your work status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee ID (`employee_id`, number, **Required**)
* This field is your unique identifier in our system, and it's essential to enter your correct ID number.

### 2. Name (`name`, text, **Required**)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 3. Email (`email`, email, **Optional**)
* If you have an email address that is available, please enter it here.

### 4. Phone (`phone`, text, **Required**)
* Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with any necessary information.

### 5. Symptoms (`symptoms`, select_multiple, **Required**)
* Indicate any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as Fever, Fatigue, Cough, or Sore Throat.

### 6. Medical Condition (`medical_condition`, select_one, **Optional**)
* If you have a medical condition that requires attention, please answer **Yes**.

### 7. Return Date (`return_date`, date, **Required**)
* Choose your expected return date from our calendar.

### 8. Work Hours (`work_hours`, time, **Required**)
* Enter the number of hours you will be able to work when you return.

### 9. Work Days (`work_days`, number, **Required**)
* Enter the number of days you will be able to work when you return.

### 10. Notes (`notes`, note, **Optional**)
* Provide any additional comments or notes about your work status.

### 11. Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **Optional**)
* If you have been assigned a tool for your return to work, please answer **Yes**.
