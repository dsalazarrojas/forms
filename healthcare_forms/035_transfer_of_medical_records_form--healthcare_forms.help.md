# Transfer of Medical Records Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the transfer of medical records between healthcare providers. This form will help ensure that patients receive seamless and continuous care by collecting the necessary information from both the transferring and receiving healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the Patient Name field with the name of the patient whose medical records are being transferred.
2. In the Healthcare Provider field, enter the name of the healthcare provider that is transferring the medical records.
3. Enter the Medical Records Request Date in the corresponding field, which is the date when the medical records are requested to be transferred.
4. In the Medical Records field, provide a detailed explanation of the medical records being transferred.
5. Select the Reason for Transfer from the provided options (Emergency Medical Condition or Non-Emergency).
6. If applicable, include the Patient Signature in the corresponding field.
7. Enter the Healthcare Provider Signature of the transferring provider.
8. Select Yes or No in the Medical Record Type field if medical records are to be transferred (or not).
9. In the Patient Contact Information field, enter the contact information of the patient.
10. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of any healthcare providers involved in the patient's care.
11. Enter the Medical Record Transfer Date when the medical records are transferred.
12. Enter the Medical Record Return Date when the medical records are expected to be returned.
13. Select the Medical Record Transfer Status (Active or Inactive).
14. Select Yes or No in the Transfer Method field if the transfer is to be done electronically or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter the name of the patient whose medical records are being transferred.
* **Healthcare Provider (text)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider that is transferring the medical records.
* **Medical Records Request Date (date)**: Enter the date when the medical records are requested to be transferred.
* **Medical Records (note)**: Provide a detailed explanation of the medical records being transferred.
* **Reason for Transfer (select one)**: Select the reason for the transfer, which can be either Emergency Medical Condition or Non-Emergency.
* **Patient Signature (text)**: If the patient has signed the form, enter their signature in this field.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature (text)**: Enter the signature of the healthcare provider that is transferring the medical records.
* **Medical Record Type (select multiple)**: Select Yes if medical records are to be transferred and No if they are not.
* **Patient Contact Information (text)**: Enter the contact information of the patient, if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider 1 Name (text)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider involved in the patient's care, if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider 1 Contact Information (text)**: Enter the contact information of the healthcare provider involved, if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider 2 Name (text)**: Enter the name of the other healthcare provider involved in the patient's care, if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider 2 Contact Information (text)**: Enter the contact information of the other healthcare provider involved, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Transfer Date (date)**: Enter the date when the medical records are transferred.
* **Medical Record Return Date (date)**: Enter the date when the medical records are expected to be returned.
* **Medical Record Transfer Status (select one)**: Select the status of the medical record transfer, which can be Active or Inactive.
* **Transfer Method (select multiple)**: Select Yes if the transfer is done electronically and No if it is not.
