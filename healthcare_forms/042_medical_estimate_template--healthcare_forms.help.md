# Medical Estimate Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Estimate Template form is used to collect information for a patient's medical estimate. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential details for accurate and efficient medical records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please type the patient's name in this field.
2. Select the **Estimate Date** (`estimate_date`): Choose the date of the estimated medical procedure or treatment.
3. Enter the **Provider Name** (`provider_name`): Enter your name and credentials as the healthcare provider.
4. Enter the **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`): Enter an estimated cost for the medical procedure or treatment.
5. Choose the **Estimated Range** (`estimated_range`): Select a low, medium, or high estimated range for the procedure or treatment.
6. Add a **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe the procedure or treatment.
7. Enter the **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Estimate Template** (`medical_estimate_template`): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): This field is used to record the patient's full name.
* **Estimate Date** (`estimate_date`): Enter the date on which the estimate is being made.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`): Enter your name and credentials as the healthcare provider.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`): Enter the estimated cost for the procedure or treatment.
* **Estimated Range** (`estimated_range`): This field is used to indicate the estimated range of the procedure or treatment. Select one of the following options: 
  - Low
  - Medium
  - High
* **Description** (`description`): Add a brief description of the procedure or treatment.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up communication.

## Tips

- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided instructions.
