# wellness_and_nutrition_club_bio_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wellness and nutrition club bio form is designed to collect and store personal information about the user's health and wellness history, medical history, and contact details. This information will be used by healthcare professionals to provide personalized care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (\*) on each field.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask a healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`name`, `string`, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Medical History** (`string`, optional): Describe any medical history or conditions you have.
* **Medications** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any medications you are currently taking.
*   - item1
*   - item2
*   - item3
* **Contact Info** (`string`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`string`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Next of Contact** (`string`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your next of contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`string`, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`string`, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Insurance Info** (`string`, optional): Enter your insurance information, including your policy number and provider.
* **Payment Info** (`string`, optional): Enter your payment information, including your payment method and account number.
* **Medical Professional Info** (`string`, optional): Enter any relevant medical professional information, such as your primary care physician's name and contact information.
* **Medical History 2** (`string`, optional): Describe any additional medical history or conditions you have.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`string`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Be sure to review and update your information regularly to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you experience any issues or have questions while filling out this form, contact a healthcare professional for assistance.
