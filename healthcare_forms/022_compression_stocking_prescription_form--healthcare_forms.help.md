# Compression Stocking Prescription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Compression Stocking Prescription Form is used to collect patient information and details required for compression stocking prescriptions. This includes demographic data, medical history, and clinician information to ensure accurate and valid prescriptions are made.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (marked as **required**) and provide accurate information.
2. Please fill out the form for the intended patient.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name**: Provide the full name of the patient as per the hospital's identification protocols.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth to verify their age and medical history.
* **Medical record number**: This is an optional field for tracking patient medical records internally. If you're unsure, leave it blank or fill it out as required by your institution.
* **Diagnosis**: Describe the clinical reason for the compression stocking prescription.
* **Stocking type**: Choose the recommended stocking class from the options provided to ensure correct sizing and fitting.
* **Measurement instructions**: Provide clear circumference measurements and fitting notes for accurate prescription.
* **Quantity needed**: Enter the number of pairs of compression stockings required.
* **Prescribing clinician**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional prescribing the compression stockings.
* **Clinician contact**: Provide an email or phone number for any queries or follow-ups.
* **Patient Information**: This section is a general area for any additional patient information.

**Tips**:
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or clinician for guidance.
- Ensure the form is filled out correctly to avoid errors and delays in processing.
- If you're filling out the form for a specific patient, please ensure you've reviewed their medical records and are aware of any contraindications or special considerations.
