# new_patient_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new patients in the system, collecting essential information about the patient, their medical practice, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "new_patient_registration" field (Page 1).
2.  Provide a brief description of the patient in the "patient_info" field (Page 2).
3.  Select the patient's medical practice from the list provided (Page 3).
4.  Enter the patient's contact number (Page 4).
5.  Enter the patient's email (Page 5).
6.  Describe the patient's medical condition (Page 6).
7.  Enter the patient's date of birth (Page 7).
8.  Enter the patient's address (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **new_patient_registration** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the form, which is "new_patient_registration". This field is optional.
*   **patient_info** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the patient. This field is optional.
*   **medical_practice** (`select_one`, required: true): Select the patient's medical practice from the options provided. This field is required.
*   **contact_number** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact number. This field is optional.
*   **email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the patient's email address. This field is optional.
*   **medical_condition** (`text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical condition. This field is optional.
*   **date_of_birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth. This field is optional.
*   **address** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's address. This field is optional.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
*   This form is intended for new patient registration and should not be used for duplicate entries.
