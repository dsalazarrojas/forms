# COVID 19 Intake Form For Massage Therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients before their massage therapy session to ensure their safety and well-being. This includes capturing details about their health status, medical history, and allergies/sensitivities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields in each section, and don't forget to save your progress after each step.
2.  Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (text, required): This field is used to capture your full name.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): This field is used to capture your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Email Address** (email, required): This field is used to capture your email address for communication.
- **Appointment Date** (date, required): This field is used to capture the date of your scheduled massage therapy session.
- **Appointment Time** (time, required): This field is used to capture the time of your scheduled massage therapy session.
- **Type of Massage Requested** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture the type of massage you are requesting (e.g., Swedish, Deep Tissue, Relaxation, Sports, etc.).
- **Any COVID-19 symptoms in past 14 days** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have had any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
- **Current COVID-19 symptoms** (select_multiple, optional): This field is used to capture any current COVID-19 symptoms.
- **Current temperature** (number, optional): This field is used to capture your current temperature.
- **Exposed to confirmed COVID-19 case** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have been exposed to a confirmed COVID-19 case.
- **Tested positive for COVID-19 recently** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have recently tested positive for COVID-19.
- **Date of positive test if applicable** (date, optional): This field is used to capture the date of your positive test if applicable.
- **Vaccination Status** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture your vaccination status.
- **Pre-existing medical conditions** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have any pre-existing medical conditions.
- **List conditions** (text, optional): This field is used to capture a list of your pre-existing medical conditions.
- **Currently taking medications** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you are currently taking any medications.
- **List medications** (text, optional): This field is used to capture a list of your current medications.
- **Immunocompromised or on immunosuppressant** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you are immunocompromised or on immunosuppressants.
- **Any allergies** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have any allergies.
- **Describe allergies** (text, optional): This field is used to capture a description of your allergies.
- **Sensitivities to oils or lotions** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture if you have any sensitivities to oils or lotions.
- **I am healthy enough for massage therapy** (select_one, required): This field is used to capture your health status before receiving a massage therapy session.
