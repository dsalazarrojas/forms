# Medical Weight Loss Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Weight Loss Consent Form is used to gather patient information and obtain consent for weight loss programs. It is essential to understand that this form is required for patients seeking weight loss services.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your patient information, including your name, contact information (address, phone number, and email), and any other relevant details.
2. Describe your medical practice or the healthcare provider administering the weight loss program.
3. Clearly state your weight loss goals, whether it is for a specific reason or general weight health.
4. Provide consent to contact you by any method (phone, email, or mail), and indicate your preferred contact method.
5. Confirm your emergency contact person and their contact information.
6. Finally, review and confirm your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you effectively.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, such as your address, phone number, and email address.
- **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact details of your healthcare provider.
- **Weight Loss Goals** (`weight_loss_goals`, text, required: false): Clearly describe your weight loss goals and reasons for seeking weight loss services.
- **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you grant permission to be contacted regarding your weight loss program. Select "Yes" to grant consent.
- **Contact Preferred** (`contact_preferred`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or mail).
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Confirm the method by which you are willing to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
- **Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how frequently you are willing to be contacted regarding your weight loss program.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an urgent situation.
