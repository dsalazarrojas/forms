# Purpose

This form is used to gather information about your health status, medical history, and medical treatment preferences. By filling out this form, you will be providing accurate and up-to-date information about your health to your healthcare provider. This will help them better understand your medical situation and provide the best possible care for you.

# How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately, using the spaces provided for each field.
2. If you need help or have any questions, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
3. Ensure that you sign and date the form to declare that the information you provide is accurate.

# Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is used to capture your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact person from the options provided.
* **Primary Care Physician** (`primary_care_physician`, text, optional): If you have a primary care physician, enter their name.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, optional): If you have a primary care physician, enter their contact number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have an insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, optional): If you have an insurance provider, enter their policy number.
* **Current Symptoms** (`current_symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing from the options provided.
* **Symptom Severity** (`symptom_severity`, select_one, required): Select how severe your symptoms are from the options provided.
* **Symptom Onset Date** (`symptom_onset`, date, optional): If you have symptoms, enter the date when they started.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the chronic medical conditions you have from the options provided.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): List all the medications you are currently taking.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any medication allergies, enter them in this field.
* **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any food allergies, enter them in this field.
* **Other Allergies** (`other_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any other allergies, such as environmental or other, enter them in this field.
* **Recent Travel History** (`recent_travel`, select_one, required): Select whether you have recently traveled domestically or internationally.
* **Travel Locations** (`travel_locations`, text, optional): If you have recently traveled, enter the locations you visited.
* **COVID-19 Exposure** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Select whether you have had confirmed or possible exposure to COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`covid_vaccination`, select_one, required): Select your COVID-19 vaccination status from the options provided.
* **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`, select_one, required): Declare that the information you provide is accurate.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to treatment.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment** (`hipaa_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether you understand your HIPAA rights.
* **Date of Declaration** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign this form to declare that the information you provide is accurate.

# Tips

* Please fill out this form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Ensure that you sign and date the form to declare that the information you provide is accurate.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
