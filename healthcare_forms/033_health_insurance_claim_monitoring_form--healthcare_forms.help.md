# Health Insurance Claim Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Monitoring Form is designed to collect relevant information about patient claims to ensure accurate and efficient handling of insurance claims. This form helps healthcare providers monitor and track patient claims in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info (page 1)**: Fill out the patient's basic information, including any relevant details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. **Claim Details (page 2)**: Enter the details of the claim, including the type of claim (e.g., doctor visit, prescription, etc.), date of service, and any other relevant information.
3. **Provider Info (page 3)**: Enter the provider's information, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
4. **Submit Claim (page 4)**: Once you have filled out the above sections, click the "Submit Claim" button to finalize the form and submit the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email address (if applicable).

### Claim Details
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim being made (e.g., doctor visit, prescription, etc.).
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date the service was performed.
* **Any Other Relevant Information**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the claim.

### Provider Info
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider (e.g., doctor, nurse practitioner, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Enter the healthcare provider's contact information, including phone number and email address (if applicable).

### Submit Claim
* **Submit Claim**: Click this button to finalize the form and submit the claim for processing.
