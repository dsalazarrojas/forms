# Health Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Client Information Form is designed to collect and store client-specific information for efficient and effective healthcare management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
* Ensure all fields marked as required are filled out before proceeding.
* You may leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
* Review and verify the accuracy of the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their official documents, such as ID cards or passport.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 1990-02-15.
* **Address**: Enter the client's current home address, including house number and street name.
* **Contact Number**: If applicable, enter the client's contact number, which can be a phone number or email.
* **Health Conditions**: Select all medical conditions the client has been diagnosed with, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history, including past illnesses or treatments.
* **Family History**: Indicate if the client has a family history of any medical conditions, such as heart disease or cancer.
* **Medications**: Select all medications the client is currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies the client has, such as latex or penicillin.
* **Medical Providers**: Select all medical providers the client sees, such as a primary care physician or specialist.
* **Emergency Contact**: Indicate if the client has an emergency contact, such as a family member or friend.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Indicate the relationship of the emergency contact, such as spouse or sibling.
* **Emergency Notes**: Provide any relevant notes about the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear in providing information, as this data will be used to inform client care.
* If unsure about a particular field, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you need to update client information, please use the same form to ensure consistency in client data.
