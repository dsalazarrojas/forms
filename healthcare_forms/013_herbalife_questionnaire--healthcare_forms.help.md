# Herbalife Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Herbalife Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about your health and wellness goals, and how you would like to use Herbalife products or services to achieve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select one of the options for "overall health goal" to indicate your main objective.
3. Choose all relevant options for "fitness goals" to indicate your specific goals.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback you have in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address if you would like to receive further guidance or support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form data** (`form_data`, text, required: false): This is where you can enter any additional information about yourself, such as your current health status, medical conditions, or other relevant details.
* **Overall fitness level** (`overall_fitness_level`, number, required: false): Indicate your current level of physical fitness, with higher numbers indicating better fitness.
* **Overall health goal** (`overall_health_goal`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options to indicate your main objective, such as "weight loss" or "health gain".
* **Fitness goals** (`fitness_goals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant options to indicate your specific goals, such as "weight loss" and "muscle gain".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your health and wellness journey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to receive further guidance or support.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Remember to enter your email address only if you are sure you would like to receive further guidance or support.
