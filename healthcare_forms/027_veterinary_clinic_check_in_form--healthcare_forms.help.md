# veterinary_clinic_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from pet owners when their pets are being checked in at a veterinary clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are completing the required fields.
2. Enter the pet's information in the Pet Information field.
3. Provide the owner's information in the Owner Information field.
4. Enter the appointment details in the Appointment Details field.
5. Enter the contact information in the Contact Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Information** (`pet_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the pet's name, species, age, and any other relevant information.
- **Owner Information** (`owner_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the owner's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the appointment, as well as any other relevant details.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of pet (Cat, Dog, or Other).
- **Owner Type** (`owner_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of owner (Individual Owner, Veterinary Clinic, or Other).
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the clinic's address or location.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the appointment date.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the appointment time.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
- **Veterinary Clinic Check In Form 5** (`veterinary_clinic_checkin_form_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether or not to receive any follow-up calls or emails from the clinic.
