# Acupuncture Basic New Client Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Acupuncture Basic New Client Form is designed to gather essential information about you, our new client, to provide personalized and effective treatment. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form carefully, and we'll use the information to tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form online or print it out and write your answers by hand.
2. Answer each question carefully, and make sure to provide detailed information where required.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name, as we want to get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name, as we need to ensure accurate identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please input your date of birth in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): This is how we'll send appointment confirmations and updates. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Include your area code for easy contact.
* **What is your primary health concern?** (`primary_complaint`, text, required): Describe your main reason for seeking acupuncture treatment.
* **How long have you experienced these symptoms?** (`symptom_duration`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Less than 1 week, 1 to 4 weeks, 1 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, or More than 6 months.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any medications or supplements you're currently taking.
* **Have you received acupuncture treatment before?** (`previous_acupuncture`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Yes, recently, Yes, years ago, or No, first time.
* **Do you have any chronic health conditions?** (`health_history`, text, optional): Include conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, etc.
* **Have you had any surgeries?** (`surgical_history`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: True or False.
* **Do you have any allergies?** (`allergies`, text, optional): Include medication and environmental allergies.
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing?** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Yes, pregnant, Yes, nursing, or No, neither.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referred_by`, select_one, optional): Select from the options: Friend or family recommendation, Online search, Insurance provider directory, Healthcare provider referral, or Other.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, note, optional): If applicable, provide your insurance details.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your policy number.
* **I consent to acupuncture treatment** (`consent_treatment`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: I agree, or Other.
* **I acknowledge the risks and benefits of acupuncture have been explained to me** (`acknowledge_risks`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: I agree, or Other.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will review your form carefully and contact you within 24-48 hours to discuss your treatment options and schedule an appointment.
