# Antigen Appointment Form Differx - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Antigen Appointment Form Differx is used to book a rapid antigen test appointment at the clinic. Please fill it out carefully and thoroughly to ensure your appointment request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your personal details in the designated fields. 
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information.
- If you've recently been exposed to a confirmed case or are currently symptomatic, please check the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient name**: Enter the full name of the person being tested (e.g., "John Doe").
  - Required field.
- **Contact phone number**: Enter the phone number for appointment reminders and other clinic communication.
  - Required field.
- **Preferred appointment date**: Select a date for the antigen test appointment.
  - Required field. Please choose a date from the calendar provided.
- **Preferred time slot**: Estimate the preferred time of day for the appointment.
  - Optional field. You can choose from available time slots.
- **Are you currently symptomatic**: Indicate if you are experiencing symptoms within the last 14 days.
  - Required field.
- **Recent exposure to confirmed case**: Indicate if you have had recent exposure to a confirmed case.
  - Optional field. Please choose from the options provided.
- **Insurance information**: If applicable, provide your insurance information for billing purposes.
  - Optional field.
- **Additional notes**: Provide any other requests or comments for the clinic.
  - Optional field.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Double-check your appointment details before submission.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the clinic for assistance.
