# health_monitoring_device_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_monitoring_device_order_form is used to order health monitoring devices for patients. This form requires accurate and complete information to ensure that the ordered devices are delivered promptly and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "user name" and "contact phone" fields.
2. Select the relevant medical group from the "medical group" dropdown list.
3. Choose the required monitoring device types from the "monitoring device type" dropdown list.
4. Enter the date and time of the order in the "order_date" and "order_time" fields.
5. Provide a brief description of the order in the "order_details" field.
6. Enter the quantity of devices ordered.
7. Select the status of the order from the "order_status" dropdown list.
8. Enter any additional contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **medical_group** (select_one, optional): Select the relevant medical group for the order.
* **monitoring_device_type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the monitoring device types you wish to order. 
* **order_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you wish to receive the order.
* **order_time** (time, optional): Enter the time you wish to receive the order.
* **order_details** (note, optional): Provide a brief description of the order.
* **order_quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of devices ordered.
* **order_status** (select_one, required): Select the status of the order.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **patient name** (text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **doctor name** (text, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
* **hospital name** (text, optional): Enter the hospital name.
* **department name** (text, optional): Enter the department name.
* **order_note** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the order.
* **order_comment** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **order_comment** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the order.
