# orthotic_device_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather patient consent and medical history for the use of orthotic devices in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Enter your general information on page 1.
3. Indicate whether you consent to the use of an orthotic device on page 2.
4. Select the type of orthotic device you will be using on page 3.
5. Provide any medical history that may be relevant to your use of an orthotic device on page 4.
6. List any medications you are currently taking on page 5.
7. Describe the device you will be using on page 6.
8. Add any additional notes or comments on page 7.
9. Provide a signature indicating that you have read and understand the form on page 8.
10. Have your provider sign and date the form on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant general information.
* **Patient Consent (Page 2)**: Choose "True" to indicate that you consent to the use of an orthotic device.
* **Device Information (Page 3)**: Select the type of orthotic device you will be using (e.g., walker, cane, other).
* **Medical History (Page 4)**: Please describe any medical conditions that may affect your use of an orthotic device.
* **Medication (Page 5)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Device Information (Page 6)**: Describe the specific device you will be using.
* **Notes (Page 7)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your use of the orthotic device.
* **Signature (Page 8)**: Sign and date to indicate that you have read and understand the form.
* **Provider Signature (Page 9)**: Have your provider sign and date the form to confirm their agreement with your use of the orthotic device.
* **Date Signed (Page 9)**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed (Page 9)**: Enter the time you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review the form and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the information.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understand all the questions before signing the form.
* Have your provider review the form before signing it.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
