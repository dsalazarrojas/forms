# Prescription Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Authorization Form is used to authorize a prescription for a patient. It is a crucial document that ensures the patient's healthcare provider has verified the prescribed medication and the patient's pickup details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on page 1.
2. Select the authorized person for the prescription on page 2.
3. Enter the medication name, quantity, and dosage instructions on page 3.
4. Enter the pickup date and time on page 4.
5. Enter the pickup location on page 5.
6. Sign the prescriber's signature on page 6.
7. Sign the authorized person's signature on page 7.
8. Sign the patient's signature on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, select_one, optional): Select the person authorized to pick up the prescription (e.g., family member, caregiver, etc.).
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the prescribed medication.
* **Medication Quantity** (`medication_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the medication prescribed.
* **Dosage Instructions** (`dosage_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any specific dosage instructions for the medication.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the medication is to be picked up.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the medication is to be picked up.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the medication is to be picked up.
* **Prescriber's Signature** (`prescribers_signature`, note, optional): Sign to indicate that the prescriber has reviewed and authorized the prescription.
* **Prescriber's Name** (`prescribers_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the prescriber.
* **Authorized Sign Date** (`authorized_sign_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the authorized person signed the form.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, note, optional): Sign to indicate that the authorized person has received and acknowledged the prescription.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Sign to indicate that the patient has received and acknowledged the prescription.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
