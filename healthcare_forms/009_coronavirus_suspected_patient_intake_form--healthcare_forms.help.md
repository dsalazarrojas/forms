## Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form is designed to collect information from patients suspected of having COVID-19 or other related health concerns. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to gather essential details about the patient's symptoms, medical history, and contact information, which will help in assessing and treating the patient's condition appropriately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list provided in the Symptom Check page.
2.  If you have any medical history, please describe it in the Medical History text field.
3.  If applicable, provide a detailed description of your symptoms in the Symptom Description text field.
4.  Indicate whether you have seen a doctor recently by checking 'yes' or 'no' in the Contact Information page.
5.  If 'yes', enter the date of your last visit to the doctor.
6.  Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
7.  Submit the form to complete your intake.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Symptom Check (select multiple):**
    *   This page lists the common symptoms associated with COVID-19 and other similar health concerns. Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
*   **Contact Information (select one):**
    *   Indicate whether you have seen a doctor recently. Select 'yes' if you have visited a doctor within the past week, and 'no' otherwise.
*   **Medical History (text):**
    *   If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies, please provide a brief description in this text field.
*   **Symptom Description (text):**
    *   Describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible, including how long you have been experiencing them and any factors that may have triggered or relieved them.
*   **Date Last Seen Doctor (date):**
    *   If you have visited a doctor recently, enter the date of your last visit.
*   **Phone Number (text):**
    *   Enter your phone number for follow-up communication from our medical team.
