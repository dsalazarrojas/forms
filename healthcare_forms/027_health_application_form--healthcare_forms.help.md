# Health Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect information about an individual's health condition, medical history, and contact details for a medical-related purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information (name, contact details, etc.)
2. Share your medical information, including any health conditions, medical history, and previous physical exams.
3. Enter your contact details so we can reach out to you with any further questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This section is for you to provide your personal details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact details so we can reach out to you with any further questions or updates.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required: false): Share your medical history, including any existing health conditions, medical treatments, or previous physical exams.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, required: false): Describe your overall health history, including any relevant medical events or conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This field is for you to sign and confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Date Of Last Physical Examination** (`date_of_last_physical_exam`, date, required: false): If you have had a recent physical exam, enter the date it was performed.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, including previous treatments or hospitalizations.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's last name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, text, required: false): Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have a medical license.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have a medical specialty.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Date** (`medical_certification_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your medical certification.
* **Medical Board Certification** (`medical_board_certification`, select_multiple, required: false): Check which medical board certifications you hold.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, number, required: false): Enter your medical license number if applicable.
* **Medical License Issued Date** (`medical_license_issued_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Expiration Date** (`medical_license_expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your medical license expires.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Contact** (`medical_provider_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your medical provider.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone without permission.
