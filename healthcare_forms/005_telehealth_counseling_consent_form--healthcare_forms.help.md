## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Provider Info** (`provider_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information as the healthcare provider.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): This is a text area for any additional information or notes about the session.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This is a field for the provider to sign and date the form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): This is a field for the patient to sign and date the form, acknowledging that they have read and understand the consent.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, optional): This is a field for the provider to sign and date the form, acknowledging that they have obtained consent from the patient.
* **Form Complete** (`form_complete`, text, optional): This field is meant for the provider to verify that the form is complete and signed by both parties.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the session was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the session was completed.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date the session was reviewed.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required information for the form to be valid.
* If you need help, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
