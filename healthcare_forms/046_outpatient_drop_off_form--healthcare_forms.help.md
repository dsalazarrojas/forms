# Outpatient (Drop Off) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to out-patient care, including patient and pet details, medical history, and scheduling information. It is intended to be used in a healthcare setting, possibly by a medical professional or a medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each field, please provide as much information as you can, even if it's not complete or up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and other relevant identification details.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, required): Enter the pet's name, species, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the patient or their representative.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Medical History 1** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history for the patient, including previous conditions or treatments.

* **Medical Notes 1** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or instructions.
* **Medical Notes 2** (`medical_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any further medical notes or instructions.
* **Medical Notes 3** (`medical_notes_3`, text, optional): Enter any more medical notes or instructions.
* **Medical Notes 4** (`medical_notes_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or instructions.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the healthcare professional or facility administering the form.
- Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing.
