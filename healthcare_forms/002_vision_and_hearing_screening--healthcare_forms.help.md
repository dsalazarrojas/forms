# Vision And Hearing Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vision and Hearing Screening form is used to document the results of vision and hearing tests performed on students in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
2. Enter your name, grade, and any comments in the corresponding fields on pages 1-3.
3. Enter the date of the vision and hearing tests on pages 4 and 7 respectively.
4. Record the results of the vision and hearing tests on pages 6 and 8.
5. Enter any additional comments on page 9.
6. Have a nurse sign off on page 10 to confirm the results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the teacher conducting the test.
* **Grade** (`grade`, text, required): Enter your grade level or class.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or remarks about the test.
* **Date of Vision Test** (`date_of_vision_test`, date, required): Enter the date the vision test was conducted.
* **Results of Vision Test** (`results_of_vision_test`, text, required): Record the results of the vision test.
* **Date of Hearing Test** (`date_of_hearing_test`, date, required): Enter the date the hearing test was conducted.
* **Results of Hearing Test** (`results_of_hearing_test`, text, required): Record the results of the hearing test.
* **Comments (2)** (`comments_2`, text, required): Enter any additional comments on the test.
* **Nurse's Signature** (`nurses_signature`, text, required): Sign off on the form to confirm the results.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool used for the test (e.g., otoscope).

Please note that the fields are not marked with asterisks, but rather, they are required or not.
