# health-services-availability-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare organizations to gather information about their health services availability and provider details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the services offered by your healthcare organization from the "Services List" page.
2. Indicate the availability of services on the "Availability" page.
3. Select the schedule you are available on the "Availability Schedule" page.
4. If necessary, enter the provider's name and credentials on the "Provider Info" page.
5. Enter the provider's contact information on the "Provider Contact" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Services List** (`services_list_page`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select all the services provided by your healthcare organization. 
* **Availability** (`availability_page`, Date, required: false): Indicate the availability of services, i.e., whether the services are available or not.
* **Service Availability** (`service_availability_page`, Select One, required: false): Choose whether the services are available or not.
* **Availability Schedule** (`availability_schedule_page`, Time, required: false): Select the schedule you are available on.
* **Patient Inquiries** (`patient_inquiries_page`, Note, required: false): Enter information about handling patient inquiries on a daily basis.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact_page`, Email, required: false): Enter the contact information of the patient.
* **Provider Info** (`provider_info_page`, Select One, required: false): Enter the provider's name and credentials.
* **Provider Contact** (`provider_contact_page`, Text, required: false): Enter the provider's contact information.
