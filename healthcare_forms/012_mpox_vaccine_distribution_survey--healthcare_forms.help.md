# mpox_vaccine_distribution_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data about the distribution of the mpox vaccine to survey recipients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Survey Recipient" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the survey recipient has received the vaccine.
2. Enter the survey recipient's "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email" in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the "Date Of Birth" of the survey recipient.
4. Enter the survey recipient's "Phone Number" and "Location" (city, state/province, and country) where the vaccine was administered.
5. Enter the "Survey Date" when the vaccination was administered.
6. Select the "Vaccine Dose" administered to the survey recipient.
7. If the survey recipient has been vaccinated, answer "Yes" or "No" to "Has the survey recipient been vaccinated?" and provide the "Vaccination Date" and "Vaccination Location" if applicable.
8. If applicable, select the "Vaccination Dose" administered to the survey recipient.
9. Finally, answer "Completed", "Partial", "Not Completed", or "Unknown" to "Vaccination Status" to indicate the status of the vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Recipient** (Select One): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the survey recipient has received the vaccine.
* **First Name** (Text): Enter the survey recipient's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter the survey recipient's last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the survey recipient's email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date): Enter the survey recipient's date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter the survey recipient's phone number.
* **Location** (Select Multiple): Select the city, state/province, and country where the vaccine was administered.
* **Survey Date** (Date): Enter the date when the vaccination was administered.
* **Vaccine Dose** (Select One): Select the dose of the vaccine administered to the survey recipient.
* **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the survey recipient's vaccination status.
* **Has the survey recipient been vaccinated?** (Select One): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the survey recipient has been vaccinated.
* **If yes, date of vaccination** (Date): If the survey recipient has been vaccinated, enter the date of vaccination.
* **Vaccination Location** (Select Multiple): Select the location where the vaccine was administered (Clinic, Hospital, Community Clinic, or Pharmacy).
* **Vaccination Dose** (Select One): Select the dose of the vaccine administered to the survey recipient.
* **Vaccination Status** (Select One): Select the status of the vaccination (Completed, Partial, Not Completed, or Unknown).
