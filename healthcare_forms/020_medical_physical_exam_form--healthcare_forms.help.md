# medical_physical_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical physical examination form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to collect and document patient information in a structured format. It helps healthcare providers gather essential details about a patient's medical history, chief complaint, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Medical History**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medications they are currently taking.
2. **Chief Complaint**: Describe the patient's current chief complaint, including symptoms and any related medical conditions.
3. **Chief Complaint Duration**: Record the duration of the patient's chief complaint in date format.
4. **Medical History (Symptoms)**: List any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
5. **Medical History (Family)**: Enter any relevant family medical history, including any inherited conditions or diseases.
6. **Medical History (Social)**: Record any relevant social history, such as lifestyle habits or recent travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: This field is for recording the patient's past medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medications they are currently taking. Please provide a brief but comprehensive account of the patient's medical history.
* **Chief Complaint**: Describe the patient's current chief complaint, including symptoms and any related medical conditions. Be as specific as possible when describing the complaint.
* **Chief Complaint Duration**: Record the duration of the patient's chief complaint in date format (e.g., "2 weeks" or "3 months").
* **Medical History (Medication)**: Select any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical History (Allergies)**: If the patient has any known allergies, list them in this field.
* **Medical History (Symptoms)**: List any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Medical History (Family)**: Enter any relevant family medical history, including any inherited conditions or diseases.
* **Medical History (Social)**: Record any relevant social history, such as lifestyle habits or recent travel.
