# golds_gym_dc_metro_par_q - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather personal and medical information from users for efficient and effective medical care in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
4. Enter the **Date of Visit** when you visited the doctor or healthcare provider.
5. Enter your **Email** address.
6. Enter your **Phone** number.
7. Provide your **Address** (optional).
8. If applicable, enter your **Medical Condition**.
9. If applicable, enter the name of your **Insurance Provider**.
10. If applicable, enter your doctor's **Name**.
11. If applicable, enter your doctor's **Phone** number.
12. If applicable, enter your doctor's **Specialty**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required): Enter the date you visited the doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home or work address if applicable.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have a medical condition, enter a brief description.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, optional): If you have a medical insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): If you are visiting a doctor, enter their name.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, optional): If you are visiting a doctor, enter their phone number.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, `text`, optional): If you are visiting a doctor, enter their specialty.

**Tips**
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended for efficient and effective medical care.
