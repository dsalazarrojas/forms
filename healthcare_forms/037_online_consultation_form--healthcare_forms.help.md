# Online Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for online consultations with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the contact information sections with your name and address.
2. Answer the medical conditions questions, selecting all relevant conditions.
3. Describe your symptoms in detail.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (`contact_info_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address where you can be reached for consultation.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_info_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number in case the healthcare professional needs to reach you directly.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all additional medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe your symptoms in detail.
* **Symptoms 2** (`symptoms_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe your additional symptoms in detail.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the healthcare professional for support.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the healthcare professional for clarification or support.
