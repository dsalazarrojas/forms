# Patient Data Sharing Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients or their authorized representatives to provide contact details and express their consent for sharing their health-related information between healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Patient Consent.
2. Choose your preferred method for sharing your data (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
3. Select the reason for sharing your data (e.g., appointment scheduling, lab results, or treatment plans).
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can provide them in the Additional Comments field.
5. Review your form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your personal email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Patient Consent** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best reflects your consent for sharing your data:
  - Option 1: Share data with healthcare providers only.
  - Option 2: Share data with family members or caregivers.
  - Option 3: Do not share data with anyone.
- **Sharing Method** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the method(s) you prefer for sharing your data:
  - Option 1: Phone call.
  - Option 2: Email.
  - Option 3: In-person meeting.
- **Sharing Reason** (`7`, `text`, required for data sharing): Select the reason for sharing your data:
  - Option 1: Appointment scheduling.
  - Option 2: Lab results.
  - Option 3: Treatment plans.
  - Option 4: Other (please specify in Additional Comments).
- **Additional Comments** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests regarding data sharing.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submission.
- Provide a valid email address and phone number to ensure prompt contact.
- Selecting a consent option will guide the sharing process accordingly.
- Sharing methods and reasons will help healthcare providers tailor their communication with you.
- If you have any further questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
