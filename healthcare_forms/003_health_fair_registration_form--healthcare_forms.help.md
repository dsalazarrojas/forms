# Health Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Fair Registration Form is designed to collect personal and contact information from participants, as well as details about their medical insurance status and birth information. This form will help us better understand your needs and ensure a smooth experience during the Health Fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Start by entering your personal information on the "Personal Information" page.
3. On the "Contact Details" page, enter your contact information.
4. On the "Medical Insurance" page, select whether you have medical insurance or not.
5. On the "Birth Details" page, enter your birth information.
6. Finally, review and confirm your medical insurance and birth information on the respective "Confirm" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, text, required: false): Enter your personal details, such as your name and ID number.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have medical insurance or not. Options are "True" or "False".
* **Birth Details** (`birth_details`, text, required: false): Enter your birth information, including date of birth and place of birth.
* **Confirm Medical Insurance** (`confirm_medical_insurance`, text, required: false): Confirm your medical insurance status.
* **Confirm Birth Details** (`confirm_birth_details`, text, required: false): Confirm your birth information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information on all pages.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
