This form is designed for healthcare professionals to provide information about their health and training status. It is intended to collect data on student names, program sites, cohort start dates, certifications, health clearance status, immunizations, emergency contacts, clinical availability, instructor notes, and submission dates. This form is meant for healthcare professionals to report their own data, and the data will be used for administrative purposes. The form's purpose is to streamline the process of collecting and tracking health and training data, making it easier for healthcare professionals to manage their information efficiently.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student name" field.
2. Select your program site from the list (if applicable).
3. Enter your cohort start date if applicable.
4. List any current certifications you hold.
5. Select your health clearance status.
6. Record any required immunizations you have.
7. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
8. Specify your clinical availability.
9. Add any internal notes for program staff in the "Instructor notes" field.
10. Finally, enter the date you completed the form.

Here is a field-by-field explanation of each section:

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as registered.
* **Program site** (`program_site`, text, optional): Select the campus or clinical site you are associated with.
* **Cohort start date** (`cohort_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your training program.
* **Certifications held** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any current certifications you hold.
* **Health clearance status** (`health_clearance`, select_one, required): Select your clearance status from the options: Cleared, Pending, or Not cleared.
* **Immunizations** (`immunizations`, text, optional): Record of required immunizations you have.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Clinical availability** (`clinical_availability`, text, optional): Specify your availability for clinical placement.
* **Instructor notes** (`instructor_notes`, text, optional): Add any internal notes for program staff.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unclear about any field, consult with your program staff.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
