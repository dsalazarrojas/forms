# Hippotherapy Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hippotherapy Interest Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in hippotherapy. The form helps us understand your interest and gather relevant information for potential patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section below with the required information. Make sure to provide accurate and complete responses for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your interest in hippotherapy.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you about your interest in hippotherapy.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all the times of day you are available for therapy sessions. Choose from Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend.
* **Therapy Type** (`therapy_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of therapy you are interested in from Equine-Assisted Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, or Physical Therapy.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, Optional): If you were referred to us by someone, please enter their name and contact information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your interest in hippotherapy.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, Optional): We require a signature of consent for our records.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, Optional): If you have an insurance provider, please enter the name of your insurance company or policy number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Be specific and clear when providing information in the "Referral Source" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to us for assistance.
