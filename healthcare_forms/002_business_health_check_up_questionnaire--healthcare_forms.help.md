# Business Health Check Up Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Business Health Check Up Questionnaire, a comprehensive tool designed to help us understand your company's overall performance and identify areas for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2.  Enter the requested information accurately and thoroughly.
3.  If you're unsure about a field, please provide a best-effort answer or explain the circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, **required**): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the primary contact person for this company.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the primary phone number for contact.
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, select_one, **required**): Select the number of employees in your organization:
	+ 1-10 employees
	+ 11-50 employees
	+ 51-200 employees
	+ 201-500 employees
	+ 500+ employees
* **Fiscal Year End Month** (`fiscal_year_end`, select_one, **required**): Select your fiscal year end month:
	+ January
	+ February
	+ March
	+ April
	+ May
	+ June
	+ July
	+ August
	+ September
	+ October
	+ November
	+ December
* **Revenue Trend - Past 12 Months** (`revenue_trend`, select_one, **required**): Indicate how your revenue has changed over the past year:
	+ Significant increase
	+ Moderate increase
	+ Stable
	+ Moderate decrease
	+ Significant decrease
* **Current Profit Margin Range** (`profit_margin_range`, select_one, **required**): Select your approximate profit margin range:
	+ Less than 5 percent
	+ 5-10 percent
	+ 11-20 percent
	+ 21-30 percent
	+ Over 30 percent
* **Cash Flow Status** (`cash_flow_status`, select_one, **required**): Indicate your current cash flow status:
	+ Excellent - always positive
	+ Good - mostly positive
	+ Adequate - manageable
	+ Challenging - frequent concerns
	+ Critical - immediate attention needed
* **Debt to Equity Ratio** (`debt_to_equity_ratio`, number, **optional**): Enter your approximate debt to equity ratio percentage. If unsure, enter 0.
* **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, number, **required**): Rate customer satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Customer Retention Rate** (`customer_retention_rate`, number, **optional**): Enter your customer retention rate percentage for the past year.
* **Market Position** (`market_position`, select_one, **required**): Indicate your market position:
	+ Market leader
	+ Strong competitor
	+ Average performer
	+ Emerging player
	+ Struggling
* **Operational Efficiency Rating** (`operational_efficiency_rating`, select_one, **required**): Rate your operational efficiency:
	+ Highly efficient
	+ Above average
	+ Average
	+ Below average
	+ Inefficient
* **Technology Adoption Level** (`technology_adoption_level`, select_one, **required**): Rate your technology adoption level:
	+ Industry leader
	+ Early adopter
	+ Average adopter
	+ Late adopter
	+ Resistant to change
* **Employee Engagement Level** (`employee_engagement_level`, select_one, **required**): Rate overall employee engagement:
	+ Very high
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ Very low
* **Key Performance Indicators Tracked** (`key_performance_indicators`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all KPIs your business currently tracks:
	+ Revenue growth
	+ Profit margins
	+ Customer acquisition cost
	+ Customer lifetime value
	+ Employee productivity
	+ Cash flow
	+ Inventory turnover
	+ None currently
* **Strategic Planning Status** (`strategic_planning_status`, select_one, **required**): Indicate your current strategic planning status:
	+ Yes - comprehensive 3-5 year plan
	+ Yes - basic annual plan
	+ In progress
	+ No - planning to create
	+ No - not prioritized
* **Risk Management Practices** (`risk_management_practices`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all risk management practices in place:
	+ Insurance coverage
	+ Emergency fund
	+ Diversified revenue streams
	+ Succession planning
	+ Regular audits
	+ Compliance monitoring
	+ None currently
* **Primary Growth Objectives** (`growth_objectives`, select_multiple, **required**): Select your top growth priorities for the next year:
	+ Revenue increase
	+ Market expansion
	+ Product development
	+ Team expansion
	+ Process improvement
	+ Technology upgrade
	+ Customer base growth
* **Biggest Business Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, text, **optional**): Describe your current biggest business challenges.
* **Preferred Consultation Method** (`consultation_preferences`, select_one, **optional**): Indicate your preferred consultation method:
	+ In-person meeting
	+ Video conference
	+ Phone call
	+ Email consultation
	+ Written report only

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately, as this information will help us better understand your business and provide tailored guidance.
* If unsure about any field, provide the best-effort answer or explain the circumstances.
* Ensure that the data entered is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
