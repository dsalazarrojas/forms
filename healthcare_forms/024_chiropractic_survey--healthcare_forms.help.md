# Chiropractic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with a chiropractic office. The form is used to identify areas of satisfaction and areas for improvement within the office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer questions that best fit your experience.
2.  Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
3.  For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best fits your experience.
4.  For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which is used to identify you as a patient. Your name will not be shared with anyone.
*   **Date of Last Visit** (`visit_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date of your last visit to the office. If you've never been to the office, please answer with the date of your first visit.
*   **How many times have you visited our office?** (`number_of_visits`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the number of times you've visited the office. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, note, optional): This field is for your general impression of your experience with the office. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
*   **Open Feedback** (`open_feedback`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share. Please keep it as detailed as possible.

### Office Environment

*   **Office Environment** (`office_environment`, note, optional): This field is for your impressions of the office facilities and atmosphere. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Office Cleanliness** (`office_cleanliness`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the cleanliness of the office. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Office Comfort and Atmosphere** (`office_comfort`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the comfort and atmosphere of the office. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Waiting Area Comfort** (`waiting_area`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the comfort of the waiting area. Choose the option that best fits your experience.

### Staff

*   **Front Desk Staff Friendliness** (`front_desk_staff`, select_one, required): This field asks about the friendliness of the front desk staff. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Front Desk Staff Helpfulness** (`front_desk_helpfulness`, select_one, required): This field asks about the helpfulness of the front desk staff. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Assistant Staff Professionalism** (`assistant_staff`, select_one, required): This field asks about the professionalism of the assistant staff. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Billing Staff Helpfulness** (`billing_staff`, select_one, required): This field asks about the helpfulness of the billing staff. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Doctor's Ability to Listen** (`doctor_listening`, select_one, required): This field asks about the ability of the doctor to listen to you. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Doctor's Explanation of Treatment** (`doctor_explanation`, select_one, required): This field asks about the doctor's explanation of your treatment. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Doctor's Knowledge and Expertise** (`doctor_knowledge`, select_one, required): This field asks about the doctor's knowledge and expertise. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Doctor's Bedside Manner** (`doctor_bedside_manner`, select_one, required): This field asks about the doctor's bedside manner. Choose the option that best fits your experience.

### Treatment

*   **Treatment Effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`, note, optional): This field is for your impressions of the effectiveness of your treatment. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Improvement in Symptoms** (`symptom_improvement`, select_one, required): This field asks about the improvement in your symptoms. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Did treatment meet your expectations?** (`treatment_met_expectations`, select_one, required): This field asks about whether the treatment met your expectations. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Level of Pain Relief** (`pain_relief`, select_one, required): This field asks about the level of pain relief you experienced. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Improvement in Daily Function** (`functional_improvement`, select_one, required): This field asks about the improvement in your daily function. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Scheduling and Access** (`scheduling_access`, note, optional): This field is for your impressions of the scheduling process. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Ease of Scheduling Appointments** (`scheduling_ease`, select_one, required): This field asks about the ease of scheduling appointments. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Typical Wait Time** (`wait_time`, select_one, required): This field asks about the typical wait time in the office. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Availability of Preferred Appointment Times** (`appointment_availability`, select_one, required): This field asks about the availability of preferred appointment times. Choose the option that best fits your experience.

### Additional Information

*   **Value Assessment** (`value_assessment`, note, optional): This field is for your impressions of the value you received from your experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): This field asks about the value for money you experienced. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Insurance Claims Handling** (`insurance_handling`, select_one, required): This field asks about the insurance claims handling process. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
*   **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, note, optional): This field is for any additional services you may be interested in. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Interested Services** (`interested_services`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about the additional services that interest you. Choose the options that best fit your experience.
*   **May we contact you about your feedback?** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, optional): This field asks about whether you'd like to be contacted about your feedback. Choose the option that best fits your experience.

### Open-Ended Questions

*   **What did you like most about our office?** (`what_liked_most`, text, optional): This field is for your comments on what you liked most about the office.
*   **What areas need improvement?** (`what_needs_improvement`, text, optional): This field is for your comments on areas that need improvement.
*   **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
