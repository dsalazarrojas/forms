# Pediatric Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information about pediatric patients for medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Name** field with the patient's full name.
2. Enter the **Date Of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
3. (Optional) Provide **Contact Information** if available. This includes the patient's contact phone number and any other relevant contact information.
4. (Optional) Fill in the **Family Info** field with any relevant family member contact information or medical history.
5. (Optional) Provide any relevant **Medical History** in the space provided.
6. (Optional) List any existing **Allergies** if applicable.
7. (Optional) Provide any existing **Medical Conditions** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Fill in the patient's full name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide the patient's contact phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Family Info** (`family_info`, text, optional): Fill in any relevant family member contact information or medical history.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any existing allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Provide any existing medical conditions.
