# senior-housing-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The senior-housing-request-form is designed to collect information from senior citizens and their caregivers to facilitate the process of finding suitable housing solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic user information in the "User Info" section.
2. Enter your contact information, including your preferred contact method (Email or Phone), in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Describe your current housing situation in the "Housing Needs" section.
4. Select your preferred type of housing in the "Housing Preference" section.
5. Set a follow-up schedule and provide any additional comments if necessary in the "Follow Up" section.
6. Enter the property manager's information in the "Property Manager" section.
7. Finalize your application by agreeing to the user agreement terms and submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (Required: False)
    * This section is for providing your personal information to us. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (Required: False)
    * You can choose to provide either your Email or Phone number for us to contact you.
* **Housing Needs** (Required: True)
    * Describe your current housing situation to help us better understand your needs.
* **Housing Preference** (Required: True)
    * Select your preferred type of housing, whether Urban, Suburban, or Rural.
* **Follow Up** (Required: False)
    * Set a follow-up schedule and provide any additional comments if necessary.
* **Property Name** (Required: True)
    * Enter the name of the property where you reside or would like to reside.
* **Property Manager Name** (Required: True)
    * Provide the name of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Email** (Required: True)
    * Enter the email of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Phone** (Required: True)
    * Enter the phone number of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Address** (Required: True)
    * Enter the address of the property where you reside or would like to reside.
* **Property Manager Email** (Required: True)
    * Enter the email of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Manager Phone** (Required: True)
    * Enter the phone number of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Manager Notes** (Required: False)
    * Add any additional comments or information for the property manager.
* **Property Visit Date** (Required: False)
    * Set a date for a potential property visit.
* **Follow Up Plan** (Required: True)
    * Select when you would like to be followed up on, whether True or False.
* **User Availability** (Required: True)
    * Select a time slot when you are available for follow-up, whether Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Follow Up Status** (Required: True)
    * Select the status of your follow-up, whether Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Missed.
* **Next Follow Up Date** (Required: False)
    * Set a date for the next follow-up.
* **Next Follow Up Time** (Required: False)
    * Set a time for the next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Comments** (Required: False)
    * Add any additional comments for the follow-up.
* **Notes to User** (Required: False)
    * Leave any additional notes for the user.
* **User Agreement** (Required: False)
    * Read and agree to the terms of the user agreement.
