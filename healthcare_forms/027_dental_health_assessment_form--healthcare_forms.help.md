# Dental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a patient's dental health status, medical history, and other relevant details. The information collected will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about the patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Make sure you're answering questions in the context of the patient's current health status.
3. If you're unsure about any question, consult with the patient or healthcare professional responsible for their care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief overview of the form. Please read and understand the form's purpose and instructions before proceeding.

### Patient Information (Page 2)
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `select_one`, not required): This section asks if the patient has a specific condition or characteristic. Please select one of the options provided.

### Medical History (Page 3)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, not required): This section is for recording any relevant medical information about the patient. Please provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.

### Medical History 2 (Page 4)
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `note`, not required): This section is a continuation of the medical history section. Please provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.

### Allergies (Page 5)
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, not required): This section lists potential allergens that may impact the patient's dental health. Please select any allergies the patient has.

### Dental History (Page 6)
* **Dental History** (`dental_history`, `select_multiple`, not required): This section asks if the patient has any previous dental procedures or conditions. Please select any relevant options.

### Dental History 2 (Page 7)
* **Dental History 2** (`dental_history_2`, `select_multiple`, not required): This is a continuation of the dental history section. Please select any additional relevant options.

### Dental Procedure (Page 8)
* **Dental Procedure** (`dental_procedure`, `select_multiple`, not required): This section asks if the patient has undergone any dental procedures. Please select any relevant options.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with the patient or healthcare professional responsible for their care.
