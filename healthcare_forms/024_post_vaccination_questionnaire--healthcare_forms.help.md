# Post Vaccination Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Post Vaccination Questionnaire form to ensure accurate and efficient completion of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Post Vaccination Questionnaire, follow the instructions below:

- **Start by identifying the patient**: Enter the patient's ID number.
- **Select the vaccine type**: Choose the type of vaccine administered (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca).
- **Report any side effects**: Describe any side effects experienced after the vaccination.
- **Document symptoms**: If any symptoms are present, provide a brief description in the text area.
- **Schedule follow-up appointments**: Enter the date for the next follow-up appointment.
- **Enter contact information**: Provide the doctor's name, patient's name, email, phone number, and any medical history relevant to the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient ID**: A unique identifier for the patient. 
  • Type: Number
  • Required
  • Hint: A unique identifier for the patient.
- **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine administered.
  • Type: Select Multiple
  • Required
  • Options: Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca
- **Side Effect 1**: Describe any side effects experienced after vaccination.
  • Type: Text
  • Required
  • Hint: Describe the side effect in detail.
- **Side Effect 2**: Describe any additional side effects experienced after vaccination.
  • Type: Text
  • Required
  • Hint: Describe the side effect in detail.
- **Side Effect 3 (Optional)**: If further side effects are experienced after vaccination, describe them here.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional
  • Hint: Only enter if additional side effects occur.
- **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms present after vaccination.
  • Type: Note
  • Optional
  • Hint: Describe the symptoms in detail.
- **Follow-up**: Schedule the next follow-up appointment.
  • Type: Date
  • Required
  • Hint: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
- **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who administered the vaccination.
  • Type: Text
  • Required
  • Hint: Enter the doctor's name.
- **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
  • Type: Text
  • Required
  • Hint: Enter the patient's name.
- **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
  • Type: Email
  • Required
  • Hint: Enter the patient's email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
  • Type: Text
  • Required
  • Hint: Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical history for the patient.
  • Type: Text
  • Optional
  • Hint: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
