# Post Vaccination Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Post Vaccination Questionnaire form to ensure accurate and efficient completion of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Post Vaccination Questionnaire, follow the instructions below:

- **Start by identifying the patient**: Enter the patient's ID number.
- **Select the vaccine type**: Choose the type of vaccine administered (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca).
- **Report any side effects**: Describe any side effects experienced after the vaccination.
- **Document symptoms**: If any symptoms are present, provide a brief description in the text area.
- **Schedule follow-up appointments**: Enter the date for the next follow-up appointment.
- **Enter contact information**: Provide the doctor's name, patient's name, email, phone number, and any medical history relevant to the patient.
