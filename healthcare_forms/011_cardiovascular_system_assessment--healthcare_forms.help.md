# Cardiovascular System Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiovascular System Assessment form is used to collect information about a patient's cardiovascular system, including their symptoms, test results, and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the Assessment Header page, which is a brief introduction to the assessment.
2. Enter the Patient ID if available, or leave it blank if not provided.
3. Describe the Presenting Complaint, which is the main reason for the assessment.
4. Enter the Most Recent Measured Heart Rate (in bpm).
5. Enter the Blood Pressure Reading (systolic over diastolic).
6. Check if Peripheral Edema is present.
7. Describe any Auscultation Findings (e.g., murmurs or gallops).
8. List any Recommended Diagnostics (e.g., ECG, echocardiogram, blood tests).
9. Enter a Provisional Diagnosis, based on your clinical impression.
10. Outline a Plan and Follow-up, including treatment and follow-up arrangements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Header (-- Assessment Header --)**: A brief introduction to the assessment.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the internal identifier of the patient, if available. If not, leave it blank.
* **Presenting Complaint**: Describe the main reason for the assessment, such as chest pain or shortness of breath.
* **Heart Rate (bpm)**: Enter the most recent measured heart rate.
* **Blood Pressure Reading (systolic over diastolic)**: Enter the measured blood pressure in a format such as "120/80 mmHg."
* **Peripheral Edema Present**: Check yes or no to indicate if swelling is present in the legs or ankles.
* **Auscultation Findings**: Describe any unusual sounds or findings during auscultation (e.g., murmurs or gallops).
* **Recommended Diagnostics**: List any recommended tests or procedures (e.g., ECG, echocardiogram, blood tests).
* **Provisional Diagnosis**: Enter a clinical impression based on the collected information.
* **Plan and Follow-up**: Outline a plan for treatment and follow-up, including any necessary tests or procedures.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language to describe the presenting complaint and auscultation findings.
* Be thorough when listing recommended diagnostics and outlining the plan and follow-up.
* Review and update the provisional diagnosis and plan as necessary based on new information or test results.
