# covid_19_attendance_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "COVID-19 Attendance Record Form" is a document used to record and track patient attendance in medical settings. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that patients receive necessary care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip any fields that do not apply to you.
*   Enter your information accurately and truthfully.
*   Review your form for completeness and error-free submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **patient (id: 1)**
*   **Label: patient**
*   **Type: number**
*   **Purpose: This field is intended to identify the patient, and is likely a unique patient ID.**

### **visit_date (id: 2)**
*   **Label: visit_date**
*   **Type: date**
*   **Purpose: This field is used to record the date of the patient's visit. Enter the date of the visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.**

### **visit_time (id: 3)**
*   **Label: visit_time**
*   **Type: time**
*   **Purpose: This field is used to record the time of the patient's visit. Enter the time in the format HH:MM:SS.**

### **medical_practice (id: 4)**
*   **Label: medical_practice**
*   **Type: text**
*   **Purpose: This field is for the name of the medical practice or clinic where the patient was seen.**

### **patient_name (id: 5)**
*   **Label: patient_name**
*   **Type: text**
*   **Purpose: This field is used to record the patient's name. Enter their full name, including their first and last name.**

### **patient_relationship (id: 6)**
*   **Label: patient_relationship**
*   **Type: text**
*   **Purpose: This field is intended to record the patient's relationship to the medical practice or clinic.**

### **medical_practice_phone (id: 7)**
*   **Label: medical_practice_phone**
*   **Type: text**
*   **Purpose: This field is for the phone number of the medical practice or clinic.**

### **patient_phone (id: 8)**
*   **Label: patient_phone**
*   **Type: text**
*   **Purpose: This field is used to record the patient's phone number. Enter their phone number in the format (XXX)-XXX-XXXX.**
