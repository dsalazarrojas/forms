# Skin And Hair Care Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your skin and hair conditions, treatment history, and other medical conditions. This data will help our medical professionals make informed decisions about your skin and hair care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of skin you have (Normal, Oily, Sensitive, Dry, or Combination).
2. Choose the condition of your hair (Normal, Dull, Curly, Dry, Damaged, or Colorful).
3. If you have any previous skin or hair treatments, describe them in the text field.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your skin or hair, describe them in the text field.
5. If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, describe them in the text field.
6. List any skin care products you currently use.
7. List any hair care products you currently use.
8. Provide any additional medical notes or concerns.
9. Schedule a follow-up appointment by selecting a date and time.
10. Choose the date and time of your next visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Type** (skin_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of skin you have (Normal, Oily, Sensitive, Dry, or Combination) to help us understand your skin condition.
* **Hair Condition** (hair_condition, select_one, required: false): Choose the condition of your hair (Normal, Dull, Curly, Dry, Damaged, or Colorful) to help us understand your hair condition.
* **Skin Treatment History** (skin_treatment_history, text, required: false): If you have any previous skin treatments, describe them here (e.g., acne treatments, surgery, etc.).
* **Hair Treatment History** (hair_treatment_history, text, required: false): If you have any previous hair treatments, describe them here (e.g., hair dye, perms, etc.).
* **Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, required: false): List any other medical conditions you have that may affect your skin or hair (e.g., diabetes, thyroid issues, etc.).
* **Other Conditions** (other_conditions, text, required: false): If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, describe them here.
* **Skin Care Products** (skin_care_products, note, required: false): List any skin care products you currently use (e.g., soaps, creams, etc.).
* **Hair Care Products** (hair_care_products, note, required: false): List any hair care products you currently use (e.g., shampoos, conditioners, etc.).
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, note, required: false): Provide any additional medical notes or concerns you have.
* **Follow-up** (follow_up, time, required: false): Schedule a follow-up appointment by selecting a date and time.
* **Next Visit** (next_visit, date, required: false): Choose the date and time of your next visit.
