# Patient Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Engagement Survey is a tool to measure patient satisfaction and engagement. By understanding your experience, we can refine our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information in the first section.
2. Rate your experience with our service in the second section.
3. Share your thoughts on what we did well in the third section.
4. Suggest areas where we can improve.
5. Add any additional feedback you'd like to share.
6. Confirm your submission.
7. Click "Thank You" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **About You** (`patient_information`, required): Provide your name, email address, and contact number.
* **Service Quality** (`service_experience`, required): Rate your overall experience with our service (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **What We Did Well** (`service_satisfaction`, required): Share one or two things that we did well.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, required): Suggest areas where we can do better.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_comments`, required): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, required): Confirm that you submitted the form.
* **Submission** (`submission`, required): Click to submit your completed form.
* **Thank You** (`thankyou`, not required): A friendly message to thank you for your feedback.
