# Dietary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information about their dietary allergies, medical conditions, and other relevant details. This information will be used by healthcare providers to inform treatment decisions and patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the relevant sections.
2. Select all the food allergies that apply to the patient from the list provided.
3. Choose the medical conditions that affect the patient from the list provided.
4. Select the dietary preferences of the patient from the list provided.
5. Enter the date and time the patient needs to take their medication.
6. Add any additional medical notes about the patient's condition.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including a phone number and email address.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for emergency contact, indicating if the patient has an emergency contact.
9. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient's Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`):
  A list of common food allergies that the patient has. Select all the allergies that apply.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`):
  A list of medical conditions that affect the patient. Select all the conditions that apply.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`):
  A list of dietary preferences that the patient has. Select all that apply.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`):
  Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact, such as their name and relationship to the patient.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`):
  Enter the date and time the patient needs to take their medication.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`):
  Additional notes about the patient's condition, such as recent medical history or relevant medical tests.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (`emergency_phone`):
  Enter the phone number of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`):
  Enter the email address of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Additional Medical Notes** (`emergency_notes`):
  More detailed notes about the patient's condition, such as treatment plans or medications.
* **Date** (`date`):
  Enter the date the patient needs to take their medication.
* **Time** (`time`):
  Enter the time the patient needs to take their medication.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_2`):
  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has an emergency contact.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Remember to submit the form only once, unless you need to make changes to your information.
