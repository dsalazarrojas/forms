# Psychology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's medical history, mental health, and treatment goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill in the required information for each page.
2. The form is divided into several pages with different sections for various topics.
3. Each section has a clear label and a text box where you can enter your information.
4. Please make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by a (*) symbol.
5. If a field is marked as optional, feel free to fill it in or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or surgeries.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, optional): Share any concerns or issues you have regarding your mental health.
* **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`, text, optional): List any past treatments or therapies you have received.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant family medical or mental health history.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional): Describe your social situation, including your occupation and social relationships.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional medical information you want to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, select_one, optional): Rate your current mental status as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Medication** (`medication`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medications you are currently taking, including None, Prescription, or Non-prescription.
* **Treatment Goal** (`treatment_goal`, text, optional): Describe your treatment goals and expectations.
* **Mental Status 2** (`mental_status_2`, select_one, optional): Rate your current mental status as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Family Member** (`family_member`, text, optional): If you have any family members with medical conditions or mental health concerns, list them here.
* **Family Member 2** (`family_member_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional family members with medical conditions or mental health concerns, list them here.
* **Mental Status 3** (`mental_status_3`, select_one, optional): Rate your current mental status as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Medical Notes 2** (`medical_notes_2`, text, optional): Add any additional medical information you want to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`emergency_contact_name_2`, text, optional): Provide the name of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Medical Notes 3** (`medical_notes_3`, text, optional): Add any additional medical information you want to share with your healthcare provider.
