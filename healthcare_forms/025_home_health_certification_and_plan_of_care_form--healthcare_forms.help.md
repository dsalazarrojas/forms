# Home Health Certification And Plan Of Care Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Home Health Certification And Plan Of Care Form is used to document the certification and planning of care for home health services. This form captures essential information from the patient and healthcare provider to ensure that necessary details are recorded for certification and care planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Patient Name** field with the full name of the patient, as recorded in their identification documents.
2. Enter the **Patient Address** field with the patient's current residence address.
3. For the **Date Of Visit**, select the date of the visit or the start date of care.
4. Enter the **Start Date** of the care planning process.
5. Enter the **End Date** of the care planning process.
6. In the **Healthcare Provider** field, enter the name of the healthcare provider who is treating the patient.
7. Enter the **Healthcare Provider Address** field with the healthcare provider's address.
8. Select the **Certification Status** as **Certified**, **Not Certified**, or **Partially Certified** from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the **Signature Date** of the certifying provider.
10. Enter the **Signature Name** of the certifying provider.
11. Enter the **Signature** field with the signature of the certifying provider.
12. Enter the **Patient Signature** field with the patient's signature.
13. In the **Certifying Provider Name** field, enter the name of the certifying provider.
14. Enter the **Certifying Provider Title** field with the title of the certifying provider.
15. Enter the **Patient Name Signature** field with the patient's signature.
16. In the **Certifying Provider Telephone** field, enter the contact number of the certifying provider.
17. Enter the **Patient Telephone** field with the patient's contact number.
18. In the **Certification Number** field, enter a unique certification number for the patient.
19. Enter the **Certification Date** field with the date of the certification.
20. Enter the **Certifying Provider Fax** field with the fax number of the certifying provider.
21. Enter the **Certifying Provider Address** field with the address of the certifying provider.
22. In the **Certifying Provider Email** field, enter the email of the certifying provider.
