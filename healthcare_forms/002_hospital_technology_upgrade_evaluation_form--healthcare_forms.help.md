# Hospital Technology Upgrade Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Technology Upgrade Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and data from hospital staff on the current status of hospital technology upgrades. This form helps hospital administrators or IT personnel evaluate the effectiveness and usability of various technologies in the hospital setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in Section 1: Hospital Information**
	* Provide the name of the hospital and a brief description of the technology being evaluated.
2. **Fill in Section 2: Contact Information**
	* Provide the name, job title, and contact information (email and phone number) of the person submitting the form.
3. **Provide Feedback in Section 3: Feedback**
	* Describe any issues or concerns you have with the technology, including any technical difficulties or user experience problems.
4. **Submit the Form**
	* Select the submission status of the form (New submission, Updated submission, or In progress).
5. **Make a Technology Upgrade Decision**
	* Decide whether to proceed with the upgrade or not, based on your evaluation of the technology.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Information** (Section 1):
	+ The hospital name and technology description will help us understand the context of the evaluation.
* **Contact Information** (Section 2):
	+ This section helps us know who submitted the form and how to contact them for further questions.
* **Feedback** (Section 3):
	+ Please provide detailed feedback on any issues or concerns you have with the technology, including technical difficulties or user experience problems.
* **Submission Status** (Section 4):
	+ Select the current status of the submission to help us track the evaluation process.
* **Technology Upgrade Decision** (Section 5):
	+ Choose whether to proceed with the upgrade or not, based on your evaluation of the technology.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and technology description.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
