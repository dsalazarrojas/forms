# Patient Weight And Height Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect weight and height measurements from patients, enabling healthcare professionals to track and analyze the data for better patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's weight in kilograms (kg) in the "weight_in_kg" field.
2. Enter the patient's height in centimeters (cm) in the "height" field.
3. Select the date of the measurement using the calendar picker in the "date_of_measurement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weight in kg**: Enter the patient's current weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Height**: Enter the patient's height in centimeters (cm).
* **Date of measurement**: Select the date of the measurement using the calendar picker.
