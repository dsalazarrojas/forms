# Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flu Vaccine Consent Form is a document used to obtain and record patients' consents for flu vaccination. It captures essential information about the patient's status, vaccine type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's basic contact information (Patient Name, Phone, Email).
2. Provide details about the patient's flu vaccination status (FluVaccineType, FluVaccineDose, FluVaccineDoseGiven).
3. Record the patient's and doctor's information (PatientInfo1, PatientInfo2, DoctorName, DoctorInfo, DoctorLicense).
4. Capture the consent status and date/time of consent (ConsentStatus, DateConsentGiven, TimeConsentGiven).
5. Sign and date the consent form to indicate the doctor's acceptance of the patient's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): A text field for capturing the patient's personal contact information.
* **Flu Info** (`flu_info`): A text field for providing additional details about the patient's flu vaccination status.
* **Consent** (`consent`): A text field for capturing the patient's consent statement.
* **Signature** (`signature`): A text field for the doctor to sign the consent form.
* **Date** (`date`): A date field for recording the date of consent.
* **Time** (`time`): A time field for recording the time of consent.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): A text field for providing the patient's contact information.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): A text field for capturing the patient's name.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): A text field for capturing the doctor's name.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): A text field for capturing the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`): A text field for capturing the patient's email address.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`): A select field for recording the patient's consent status.
* **Date Consent Given** (`date_consent_given`): A date field for recording the date of consent.
* **Time Consent Given** (`time_consent_given`): A time field for recording the time of consent.
* **Flu Vaccine Type** (`flu_vaccine_type`): A select field for selecting the type of flu vaccine administered.
* **Flu Vaccine Dose** (`flu_vaccine_dose`): A select field for selecting the flu vaccine dose administered.
* **Flu Vaccine Dose Given** (`flu_vaccine_dose_given`): A select field for recording the status of the flu vaccine dose administered.
* **Consent Form Status** (`consent_form_status`): A select field for recording the status of the consent form.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`): A text field for the doctor to sign the consent form.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`): A text field for capturing the doctor's medical license number.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`): A text field for providing additional details about the doctor.
* **Patient Info 1** (`patient_info_1`): A text field for capturing additional patient information.
* **Patient Info 2** (`patient_info_2`): A text field for capturing additional patient information.
* **Patient Info 3** (`patient_info_3`): A text field for capturing additional patient information.
