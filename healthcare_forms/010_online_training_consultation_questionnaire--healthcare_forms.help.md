# online_training_consultation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of conducting an online training consultation. It will help assess users' goals, exercise frequency and duration, health details, progress, medical history, and other relevant information to provide personalized guidance and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being asked and why.
2. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field description.
3. For non-required fields, choose the most accurate or relevant option.
4. Enter your responses accurately and honestly.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your goals** (training_goals, text, optional): This field asks for your training goals, such as improving cardiovascular health, increasing muscle mass, or enhancing flexibility. Please be specific about what you want to achieve through our consultation.
* **Exercise frequency** (exercise_frequency, select_one, optional): Please select the frequency with which you exercise, such as daily, 3-4 times a week, or less than once a week.
* **Exercise duration** (exercise_duration, number, optional): If you selected "daily" or "3-4 times a week" from the previous field, please enter the average duration of your workouts in minutes. For example, if you work out for 30 minutes a day, enter 30. If you work out for 45-60 minutes 3-4 times a week, enter 45.
* **Health details** (health_details, text, optional): Please describe any health details or concerns you have, such as medical conditions, allergies, or current medications.
* **Progress** (progress, select_multiple, optional): Please select the areas where you have made progress, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or improved mood.
* **Medical history** (medical_history, text, optional): Please describe your medical history, including any past surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
* **Height** (height, number, optional): If you want to provide your height for the consultation, please enter it in centimeters or inches.
* **Weight** (weight, number, optional): If you want to provide your weight for the consultation, please enter it in kilograms or pounds.
* **Age** (age, number, optional): If you want to provide your age for the consultation, please enter it.
* **Phone number** (phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or guidance.

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is not intended to replace professional medical advice and guidance, and any decisions made based on this form should be made with a qualified professional.
