# Credentialing Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify information about medical professionals or healthcare workers' credentials, including their background, education, professional experience, and any other relevant details. This information is crucial for credentialing and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the form's sections and identify the relevant questions that apply to the individual being credentialled.
2. For each section, carefully read the questions and answer them accurately and thoroughly.
3. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such. Some sections may not be required, but please make sure you have answered all necessary questions before submitting.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Background Information** (background_info, text, required: false): This field is meant to provide a brief overview of the individual's background, including their professional history, medical specialties, or any relevant experience.
- **Education** (education, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all applicable options that describe the individual's educational background:
	+ 'Yes': The individual has completed relevant education and training.
	+ 'No': The individual does not have relevant education and training.
- **Professional Experience** (professional_experience, number, required: false): This field is for you to enter the individual's professional experience in years.
- **Credentialing Details** (credentialing_details, text, required: false): Please provide any additional information that supports or describes the individual's credentials.
- **License and Certifications** (license_certifications, select_one, required: false): Check all applicable options:
	+ 'Yes': The individual holds a license or certification.
	+ 'No': The individual does not hold a license or certification.
- **Verification Details** (verification_details, select_multiple, required: false): Check all applicable options:
	+ 'Yes': The individual has undergone verification or background checks.
	+ 'No': The individual has not undergone verification or background checks.
- **Background Check** (background_check, date, required: false): Enter the date of the individual's last background check.
- **Verification Status** (verification_status, select_one, required: false): Choose the current verification status:
	+ Active: The individual's credentials are active and verified.
	+ Inactive: The individual's credentials are inactive or not verified.
- **Verification Notes** (verification_notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the individual's verification status.
- **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required: false): Enter the individual's contact information, such as name and address.
- **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the individual's email address.
- **Assigned User** (assigned_user, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual assigned to the credentialing process.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers before submitting to avoid errors.
* This form may need to be completed multiple times for each individual being credentialled.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please consult with a qualified healthcare professional or the relevant authority for guidance.
