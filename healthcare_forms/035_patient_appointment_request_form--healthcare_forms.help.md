# Patient Appointment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients request appointments with healthcare professionals or medical staff. It collects essential information for scheduling and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it should be spelled by our staff.
2.  **Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method for appointment scheduling and follow-up: Phone, Email, or In-person.
3.  **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information in the required format (123-456-1234).
4.  **Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment in (YYYY-MM-DD) format.
5.  **Appointment Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for your appointment in (HH:MM) format.
6.  **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact for your appointment.
7.  **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
8.  **Appointment Reason**: Specify the reason for your appointment.
9.  **Additional Notes**: Share any additional information that might be helpful for your appointment.
10. **Preferred Contact Method**: If you have a preferred contact method for follow-up or notification, select it from the options provided.
11.  **Patient Relationship**: If you have any existing relationship with our healthcare provider, please describe it briefly. (This field is optional.)
