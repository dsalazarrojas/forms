# COVID 19 Testing Registration And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Testing Registration And Consent Form is used to collect demographic and medical information from patients for COVID-19 testing purposes. This form is designed to be completed by patients before undergoing a COVID-19 test. It collects information on patient demographics, medical history, testing preferences, and consent for testing and data sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible. For fields with options, select the most relevant option. For date fields, use MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
