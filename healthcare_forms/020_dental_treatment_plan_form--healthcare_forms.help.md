# Dental Treatment Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Treatment Plan Form is a crucial document used to outline a patient's dental treatment plan, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded and communicated among healthcare providers. This form is used to gather information about the patient, their medical condition, and the treatment plan, facilitating effective care and coordination among healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "true" in the form.
2. For fields like "Insurance Provider" and "Insurance Plan", you can choose from the available options or select "No" if the patient doesn't have an insurance provider.
3. For fields like "Medical Record Status" and "Medical Record Note", use the available options to select the correct status or note.
4. Use the "Note" field for any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical condition.
5. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (id: 1, required: true): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Contact Email** (id: 2, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Medical Record Number** (id: 3, required: true): Enter the patient's medical record number for identification.
* **Insurance Provider** (id: 4, required: false): Select "Yes" if the patient has an insurance provider; otherwise, select "No".
* **Insurance Plan** (id: 5, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance plan details if they have an insurance provider.
* **Treatment Plan** (id: 6, required: true): Describe the patient's treatment plan in detail, including any necessary procedures or medications.
* **Treatment Date** (id: 7, required: true): Enter the date the treatment plan was created or updated.
* **Doctor Name** (id: 8, required: true): Enter the name of the healthcare provider creating the treatment plan.
* **Hospital Name** (id: 9, required: true): Enter the name of the hospital or healthcare facility where the treatment plan is being implemented.
* **Patient Address** (id: 10, required: true): Enter the patient's address for correspondence purposes.
* **Patient Phone** (id: 11, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Doctor Phone** (id: 12, required: true): Enter the doctor's phone number for emergencies or follow-ups.
* **Doctor Specialty** (id: 13, required: true): Select the doctor's specialty for the treatment plan.
* **Doctor License** (id: 14, required: true): Enter the doctor's license number for verification purposes.
* **Patient DOB** (id: 15, required: true): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Record Status** (id: 16, required: true): Select the patient's medical record status (Active or Inactive).
* **Medical Record Note** (id: 17, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the patient's medical record.
* **Insurance Company** (id: 18, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance company details if they have an insurance provider.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (id: 19, required: false): Describe the patient's medical condition, if any.
* **Patient Medical History** (id: 20, required: false): Enter the patient's medical history, if any.
* **Doctor Signature** (id: 21, required: true): Sign the treatment plan with the doctor's signature.
* **Patient Signature** (id: 22, required: true): Sign the treatment plan with the patient's signature.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (id: 23, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance policy number if they have an insurance provider.
* **Medical Record Notes** (id: 24, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the patient's medical record.
* **Doctor Address** (id: 25, required: true): Enter the doctor's address for verification purposes.
