# Home Care Support Worker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Support Worker Intake Form is a tool for healthcare organizations to collect and manage information about home care support workers. It helps to streamline the onboarding process, ensuring that all necessary details are gathered and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. Do not repeat or copy information from other sources without proper verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Worker Details (<worker_details>**, Text, Required: False)**: Enter the worker's basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
- **Qualifications (<qualifications>**, Select Multiple, Required: False)**: List the worker's relevant qualifications and certifications.
- **Experience (<experience>**, Number, Required: False)**: Record the number of years of experience the worker has in the field.
- **Onboarding Date (<onboarding_date>**, Date, Required: False)**: Enter the date the worker was on-boarded or started working with the organization.
- **Onboarding Time (<onboarding_time>** , Time, Required: False)**: Record the time of day the worker on-boarded or started working.
- **Notes (<notes>**, Text, Required: False)**: Add any important notes or comments about the worker.
- **Phone Numbers (<phone_numbers>** , Text, Required: False)**: List the worker's contact numbers.
- **Email (<email>** , Email, Required: False)**: Enter the worker's email address.
- **Onboarding Status (<onboarding_status>** , Select One, Required: False)**: Choose the status of the worker's onboarding.
- **Worker Notes (<worker_notes>** , Text, Required: False)**: Add any other notes or comments about the worker.
- **Additional Details (<additional_details>** , Text, Required: False)**: Include any additional information about the worker.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each field carefully before submission.
* Contact the relevant personnel if you are unsure about any field or requirement.
