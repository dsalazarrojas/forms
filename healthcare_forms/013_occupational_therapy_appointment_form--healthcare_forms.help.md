# Occupational Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupational Therapy Appointment Form is used for scheduling and tracking occupational therapy appointments. It collects information about the appointment schedule, payment details, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the schedule date for the appointment.
2. Enter the schedule time for the appointment.
3. Enter the name of the therapist responsible for the appointment.
4. Enter the patient's name.
5. Enter the patient's email address.
6. Enter the patient's phone number.
7. Enter the appointment duration.
8. Select the appointment status (Active, Rescheduled, or Canceled).
9. Enter the reason for the appointment.
10. Select any pre-existing medical conditions that are relevant to the appointment.
11. Select the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Insurance, or Other).
12. Enter the payment amount.
13. Enter any additional comments about the appointment.
14. Enter the patient's email address again (this field is likely intended for the patient's email address, but it's not clear why it's repeated).
15. Enter the patient's phone number again (this field is also not clear why it's repeated).
16. (Optional) Enter the schedule date and time for any additional appointments.
17. (Optional) Select the payment method and enter the payment amount for any additional appointments.
