# FMLA Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FMLA Policy Form is designed to collect essential information about an employee's leave of absence request, including details about the leave reason, dates, and required approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, fill in the required fields marked as mandatory (*):
	- Employee ID: Enter your unique identifier issued by the company.
	- Supervisor Confirmation: Confirm or reject the leave request.
	- Employee Confirmation: Confirm or reject the leave request on behalf of the employee.
	- Approval Status: Choose whether the leave request is approved, rejected, or pending approval.
2. Fill in the optional fields if necessary:
	- Employee Name: Enter your full name.
	- Job Title: Enter your job title.
	- Department: Enter your department.
	- Reason for Leave: Explain the reason for your leave request.
	- Date Submitted: Enter the date you submitted the leave request.
	- Date Approved/Rejected: Record the date of approval or rejection.
	- Comments: Leave any additional comments about the leave request.
3. Review and submit the form for approval or rejection. Ensure all mandatory fields are complete before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique employee ID issued by the company.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `note`, optional): Explain the reason for your leave request.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`supervisor_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm or reject the leave request as your supervisor.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or reject the leave request on behalf of the employee.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the leave request is approved, rejected, or pending approval.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted the leave request.
* **Date Approved/Rejected** (`approval_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of approval or rejection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional comments about the leave request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's department.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, `number`, optional): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Employee ID 2** (`employee_id2`, `number`, optional): Enter your employee ID 2 (if applicable).
* **Employee ID 2** is not a field that is typically used in the FMLA Policy Form. Please do not fill this field unless instructed otherwise by your supervisor.

## Tips
- Ensure all mandatory fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Review and understand the FMLA Policy before submitting your leave request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, consult with your supervisor or HR department.
