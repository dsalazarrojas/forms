# roc_performance_therapy_client_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "roc_performance_therapy_client_consent_form" is a form designed to collect essential information from patients who are interested in participating in ROC (Respiratory and Occupational Consultation) therapy. This form is used to obtain the patient's consent to proceed with the therapy and gather relevant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information, which is marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Complete the form carefully, as the information collected is essential for the success of the ROC therapy.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clients Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **contact_information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you regarding your therapy plan and progress.
* **Client Consent** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options below:
	+ Option 1: I consent to participate in ROC therapy.
	+ Option 2: I do not consent to participate in ROC therapy.
* **ROC Therapy** (`roc_therapy`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the therapies that interest you:
	+ Option 1: Respiratory therapy
	+ Option 2: Occupational therapy
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
	+ A note box is provided for you to sign your name.

Note: The form must be completed carefully, as the information collected is essential for the success of the ROC therapy. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
