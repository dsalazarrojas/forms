# Perinatal And Postnatal Mental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Perinatal And Postnatal Mental Health Questionnaire is designed to collect information about a mother's mental health status during the perinatal and postnatal period. This form is used to assess their exposure to trauma, symptoms of anxiety and depression, and their experiences with mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Mother Name" field with your name.
2. Select the "Contact Details" field to provide your contact information (phone number or email).
3. In the "Mental Health History" field, provide a brief description of your mental health history.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the questions about depression symptoms, anxiety symptoms, and trauma exposure.
5. For the "Anxiety Symptoms Duration" and "Depression Symptoms Duration" fields, enter the date from which you have been experiencing symptoms.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about mental health professional help.
7. If you have a mental health professional, provide their name in the "Mental Health Professional Name" field.
8. Provide the contact information of your mental health professional in the "Mental Health Professional Contact" field.
9. In the "Mental Health Professional Notes" field, provide any additional information about your mental health professional.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about mental health services used.
11. If you have used mental health services, provide the name of the service in the "Mental Health Service Name" field.
12. Provide the contact information of the mental health service in the "Mental Health Service Contact" field.
13. In the "Mental Health Service Notes" field, provide any additional information about the mental health service.
14. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about other services used.
15. If you have used other services, provide the name of the service in the "Other Services Used" field.
16. In the "Mental Health Service Comments" field, provide any additional comments about the mental health service.
17. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about the referral source for mental health services.
18. If you have referred to mental health services, provide the date of referral in the "Mental Health Service Referral Date" field.
19. Provide the time of referral in the "Mental Health Service Referral Time" field.
20. In the "Mental Health Service Referral Comments" field, provide any additional comments about the referral.
