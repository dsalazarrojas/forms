# Telehealth Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Release Form is used to document the discharge of a patient from a healthcare facility, ensuring a smooth and secure transition of care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
5. Select the reason for release from the provided options in the "Reason for Release" field.
6. Enter the date of release in the "Date of Release" field.
7. Confirm the date of your signature as the doctor in the "Date of Doctor Signature" field.
8. Indicate if you have signed the form in the "Doctor Signature" field.
9. Enter the date of your signature as the doctor in the "Date of Doctor Sign Date" field.
10. Confirm if you have signed the form in the "Doctor Signature 2" field.
11. Indicate if you have signed the form as the patient in the "Patient Signature 2" field.
12. Enter the date of your signature as the patient in the "Date of Patient Signature" field.
13. Confirm that the form has been completed in the "Form Completed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the patient being released.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address (Note)**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Reason for Release (Select One)**: Select the reason for the patient's release from the provided options:
	+ Discharged
	+ Moved
	+ Other
* **Date of Release (Date)**: Enter the date of the patient's release.
* **Doctor Sign Date (Date)**: Confirm the date of your signature as the doctor.
* **Doctor Name (Text)**: This field is intended for the doctor to enter their name, although it is not currently required.
* **Patient Signature (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you have signed the form as the patient.
* **Doctor Signature (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you have signed the form as the doctor.
* **Date of Patient Signature (Date)**: Enter the date of your signature as the patient.
* **Release Date (Date)**: This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **Doctor Sign Date 2 (Date)**: Confirm the date of your signature as the doctor.
* **Doctor Signature 2 (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you have signed the form as the doctor.
* **Patient Signature 2 (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you have signed the form as the patient.
* **Form Completed (Select One)**: Confirm that the form has been completed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
* Sign the form when complete.
