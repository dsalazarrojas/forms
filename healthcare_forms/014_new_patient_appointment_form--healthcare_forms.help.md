# New Patient Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for new patients to submit necessary details for their appointment with our healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient information (page 1).
2. Enter the appointment details, including time, doctor specialty, and any other relevant information (page 2).
3. Provide any necessary additional notes or comments (page 3).
4. Confirm payment method and insurance information (page 4).
5. Enter any additional details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name to help our staff identify them.
* **Patient ID (if applicable)**: If you already have a patient ID, enter it here.
* **Other**: Enter any additional details about the patient.

### Appointment Details (Page 2)
* **Doctor Name/ID**: Enter the name and/or ID of the healthcare professional you'd like to see.
* **Doctor Specialty**: Select the specialty of the doctor you've chosen.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose a suitable time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Notes**: Add any additional information to make your appointment more efficient.

### Doctor Information (Page 3)
* **Doctor Name/ID**: Enter the name and/or ID of the healthcare professional.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information about your appointment.

### Payment Method (Page 4)
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for your appointment (e.g., insurance, cash, etc.).

### Insurance Information (Page 4)
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider information.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **Patient Number**: Enter your patient number if you have one.
* **Doctor Number**: Enter your doctor's number if you have one.
* **Payment Method**: Confirm your payment method.
* **Specialty**: Select the doctor's specialty.

### Appointment Confirmation (Page 5)
* **Time**: Confirm the appointment time.
* **Appointment Notes**: Confirm any additional notes from Page 3.

### Tips
* Double-check for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your appointment.
