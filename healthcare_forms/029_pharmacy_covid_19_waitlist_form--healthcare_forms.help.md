# pharmacy_covid_19_waitlist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pharmacy_covid_19_waitlist_form is designed to collect patient information for tracking and managing waitlists during the COVID-19 pandemic. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Waitlist Info** (`waitlist_info`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information regarding the patient's waitlist status.
2. **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter the patient's email address.
3. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: true): Enter the patient's phone number.
4. **Patient Name** (`name`, text, required: true): Enter the patient's name.
5. **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, required: true): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: true): Enter the patient's phone number.
7. **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose how the patient would like to be contacted (in-person or online).
8. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: true): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
9. **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: true): Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
