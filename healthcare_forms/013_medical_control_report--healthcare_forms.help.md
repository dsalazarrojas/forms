# medical_control_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_control_report form is designed to track and document patient information, medical history, and treatment details. It is used to monitor and manage patient progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Complete the fields for patient name, medical record number, and visit date.
2. **Document Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
3. **Specify Medical Condition**: Enter the current medical condition of the patient.
4. **Record Medical Treatment**: Enter any medical treatment provided to the patient.
5. **Update Medical Control**: Enter any updates to the patient's medical control status.
6. **Update Form**: Enter any updates to the form, including the date and time of the update, and the user who made the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (1)**: Enter the patient's name and medical record number to identify the patient.
  * **patient_name** (Text): Enter the patient's name.
  * **medical_record_number** (Text): Enter the patient's medical record number.
  * **visit_date** (Date): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
- **Medical Status** (10): Select the current medical status of the patient (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
- **Medical History (6)**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
- **Medical Condition (7)**: Enter the current medical condition of the patient.
- **Medical Treatment (8)**: Enter any medical treatment provided to the patient.
- **Medical Control (9)**: Enter any updates to the patient's medical control status.
- **Medical Status Updates (15)**: Enter the date of the update and the user who made the update.
  * **medical_control_update_date** (Date): Enter the date of the medical control update.
  * **medical_control_update_time** (Time): Enter the time of the medical control update.
  * **medical_control_update_by** (Text): Enter the user who made the medical control update.
- **Additional Fields**: Complete any other fields as necessary, such as medical record ID, assigned by, medical record version, created by, updated by, created at, updated at, and any other relevant fields.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and update the form regularly to maintain up-to-date patient information.
