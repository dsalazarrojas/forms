# 48 Hour Opt Out Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 48 Hour Opt Out Agreement form is designed to collect information from patients about their medical history, contact details, and visit information while ensuring they understand the implications of opting out of treatment for 48 hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and date of birth in the "Full name" and "Date of birth" fields respectively.
2. Provide your contact information by entering your phone number and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number.
4. Select the date and time of your visit.
5. Briefly describe the reason for your visit and select all applicable symptoms you are experiencing.
6. If you are taking any medications, list them in the "Current medications" field.
7. If you have any allergies or medical conditions, include them in the "Known allergies" and "Existing medical conditions" fields respectively.
8. Declare your understanding of the implications of opting out of treatment for 48 hours and provide a reason for opting out.
9. Indicate whether you consent to follow-up contact within 48 hours.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.
10. Finally, sign the acknowledgement section by selecting "I agree" to confirm you have read and understood the 48-hour opt-out agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Date of visit**: Enter the date of your scheduled or planned visit.
* **Time of visit**: Enter the time of your scheduled or planned visit.
* **Reason for visit**: Briefly describe why you are seeking care or treatment.
* **Symptoms**: Check all symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Pain level**: Indicate your pain level, from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain imaginable).
* **Current medications**: If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Known allergies**: If you have any known allergies, include them here.
* **Existing medical conditions**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, list them here.
* **Do you understand the implications of opting out of treatment for 48 hours?**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm your understanding.
* **Reason for opting out of treatment**: If you are opting out of treatment, provide a reason for doing so.
* **Do you consent to follow-up contact within 48 hours?**: Select "True" to indicate you are open to follow-up contact.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share with our team.
* **I acknowledge that I have read and understood the 48-hour opt-out agreement**: Select "I agree" to confirm your understanding and agreement.
