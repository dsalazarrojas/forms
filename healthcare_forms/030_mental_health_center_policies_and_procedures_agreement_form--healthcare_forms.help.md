# mental_health_center_policies_and_procedures_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Center Policies and Procedures Agreement Form is a template used to ensure that patients are aware of and agree to the mental health center's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the "Policies And Procedures" section to familiarize yourself with the mental health center's policies.
2. Select the policies to which you agree, using the "Policies List" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your response to the statement "Client has read, understands and agrees to the Mental Health Center Policies and Procedures" using the "Client Signature" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your response to the statement "I, [Center Representative], confirm that I have read, understand and agree to the Mental Health Center Policies and Procedures" using the "Center Signature" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date and time you are submitting this form using the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent (2)** (number, required): This field requires a patient to indicate their level of consent regarding the mental health center's policies and procedures.
* **Policies And Procedures (3)** (note, required): This field is a text area where the mental health center's policies and procedures are explained to the patient.
* **Policies List (4)** (select multiple, required): This field allows the patient to select which policies they agree to, including Client Rights and Responsibilities, Client Information and Confidentiality, and Code of Conduct.
* **Client Signature (5)** (select one, required): This field requires the patient to select a statement indicating their understanding and agreement to the mental health center's policies and procedures.
* **Center Signature (6)** (select one, required): This field requires the center representative to select a statement indicating their understanding and agreement to the mental health center's policies and procedures.
* **Date (7)** (date, required): This field requires the date the form is being submitted.
* **Time (8)** (time, required): This field requires the time the form is being submitted.
* **Contact Info (9)** (email, required): This field requires the patient's contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Client Signature (5)** (select one, required): This field is actually redundant and will be removed from the guide, as it is not actually part of the form.

Note: The "Client Signature" field with id 5 is actually not part of the form, but was included in the yaml. It was removed from the guide above.
