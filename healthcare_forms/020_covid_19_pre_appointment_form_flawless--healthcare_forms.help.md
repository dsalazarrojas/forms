# covid_19_pre_appointment_form_flawless - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to provide information necessary for a pre-appointment consultation with a healthcare professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient name and email address.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Provide any medical history.
5. Indicate if you have any medical conditions.
6. Select the appointment details (type, time, duration, and location).
7. Enter the doctor's name.
8. Enter the appointment time.
9. Enter the appointment duration.
10. Enter the appointment date.
11. Choose a payment method (credit card, check, or cash).
12. (Optional) You can also enter payment terms and medical certificate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): Enter any medical conditions or history that you think might be relevant to your consultation.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have any of the listed medical conditions (allergic to shellfish, nuts, cats, or dogs).
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the appointment type, time, duration, and location.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the doctor you will be seeing.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will be paying for your appointment (credit card, check, or cash).
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you give your consent for the appointment.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, optional): Enter your medical insurance information.
* **Doctor Specialization** (`doctor_specialization`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor's specialization.
* **Hospital Affiliation** (`hospital_affiliation`, `text`, optional): Enter the hospital affiliation.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the clinic.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes about your appointment.

Note: Ensure you fill out all required fields to complete the form correctly.
