# Ambulance Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ambulance Refusal Form is used to document the refusal of ambulance transport to a healthcare facility by a patient or their representative. This form ensures that all necessary information is recorded to facilitate proper patient care and future medical actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all the necessary fields, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2.  Review the form carefully to ensure that all relevant information is included.
3.  Check for any inconsistencies or missing values.
4.  Ensure that all sections are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Call/Run Number**: Enter the call number assigned by the emergency medical services (EMS) system.
*   **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Location**: Enter the full address of the incident location.
*   **Patient First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
*   **Patient Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
*   **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's age in years.
*   **Patient Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
*   **Patient Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Patient Alert and Oriented**: Select the patient's level of alertness and orientation.
*   **Patient Understands Medical Condition**: Select if the patient understands their medical condition.
*   **Patient Understands Risks of Refusal**: Select if the patient understands the risks associated with refusal of transport.
*   **Is Patient Under the Influence**: Select if the patient is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
*   **Mental Status**: Select the patient's mental status.
*   **Pain Level**: Enter the patient's pain level on a scale of 0-10.
*   **Blood Pressure**: Enter the patient's blood pressure in mmHg.
*   **Heart Rate**: Enter the patient's heart rate in BPM.
*   **Respiratory Rate**: Enter the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
*   **Oxygen Saturation**: Enter the patient's oxygen saturation percentage.
*   **Blood Glucose**: Enter the patient's blood glucose level in mg/dL.
*   **Temperature**: Enter the patient's temperature.
*   **Chief Complaint**: Enter the patient's chief complaint.
*   **History of Present Illness**: Enter a brief description of the patient's history of present illness.
*   **Past Medical History**: Enter the patient's past medical history.
*   **Current Medications**: Enter the patient's current medications.
*   **Known Allergies**: Enter the patient's known allergies.
*   **Assessment Offered**: Select if a full assessment was offered to the patient.
*   **Treatment Offered**: Select if treatment was offered to the patient.
*   **Transport to Hospital Offered**: Select if transport to a hospital was offered.
*   **Risks Explained**: Select all risks that were explained to the patient.
*   **Did Patient Demonstrate Understanding of Risks**: Select if the patient demonstrated understanding of risks.
*   **Alternatives Offered**: Enter any alternative care options offered to the patient.
*   **Patient's Stated Reason for Refusal**: Select the patient's stated reason for refusal.
*   **Additional Details About Refusal**: Enter any additional details about the patient's refusal.
*   **Were Witnesses Present**: Select if witnesses were present.
*   **Witness Names**: Enter the names of witnesses present.
*   **Did Witnesses Understand Risks**: Select if witnesses understood the risks associated with refusal.
*   **Family Member Present**: Select if a family member was present.
*   **Family Member's Name**: Enter the name of the family member present.
*   **Family Member's Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the family member present.
*   **Does Family Agree with Refusal**: Select if the family member agrees with the patient's refusal.
*   **Discharge Instructions Given**: Select all instructions given to the patient.
*   **Did Patient Understand Instructions**: Select if the patient understands the instructions.
*   **Follow-Up Recommendations**: Enter any follow-up recommendations made to the patient.
*   **Patient Signature**: Enter the patient's signature.
*   **Patient Signature Witnessed**: Enter the name of the witness who witnessed the patient's signature.
*   **Time of Refusal**: Enter the time of the refusal.
*   **EMT/Paramedic Name**: Enter the name of the EMT or paramedic who provided care.
*   **EMT/Paramedic Certification Level**: Select the certification level of the EMT or paramedic.
*   **Partner Name**: Enter the name of the partner or other EMT/Paramedic who provided care.
*   **EMT/Paramedic Signature**: Enter the signature of the EMT or paramedic.
*   **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's refusal.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   Use the form to document the refusal of ambulance transport to a healthcare facility.
*   Use the form to communicate effectively with the patient, family, and healthcare providers.
