# reflexology_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reflexology consultation form is used to gather essential information from clients about their health issues, appointment details, and other relevant information. This form helps healthcare professionals to better understand the client's needs and provide effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Client Name**, which is required to identify you.
2.  Enter your **Contact Number** so we can get in touch with you.
3.  Select your **Appointment Date** for scheduling purposes.
4.  Describe your **Health Problem** to help us understand your concerns.
5.  Indicate if you have any **Treatment Plans** in place.
6.  If relevant, provide your **Client Address** for our records.
7.  Share your **Medical History** if it's essential to your health concerns.
8.  For field 8, please note that this field is optional and doesn't have a specific label yet. However, we will use this field for additional information in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name (Client Name)** (`Client Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to help us identify you correctly.
*   **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
*   **Appointment Date (Appointment Date)** (`date`, required): Choose a date for your scheduled appointment.
*   **Health Problem (Health Problem)** (`text`, required): Describe your health concerns or issues.
*   **Treatment Plans (Treatment Plans)** (`select multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have any treatment plans, and 'No' if you don't.
*   **Client Address (Client Address)** (`text`, optional): If relevant, provide your address for our records.
*   **Medical History (Medical History)** (`text`, optional): Share your medical history if it's essential to your health concerns.
*   **Field 8 (Field 8)** (`text`, optional): This field is currently not labeled. Please provide any additional information you feel is necessary, but note that this field is not yet assigned a label or a clear purpose.
