# Three Factor Eating Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Three Factor Eating Questionnaire is a form designed to assess an individual's eating habits and frequency, as well as their goals and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name and contact information in the top section of the form.
2. Select your eating habits, eating frequency, and food group preferences from the corresponding fields.
3. Provide additional healthcare provider information, including their name, email, and phone number.
4. Review and submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Eating Habits** (`eating_habits`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you have eating habits that are True, False, or Rarely.
* **Eating Frequency** (`eating_frequency`, `text`, required): Enter your eating frequency.
* **Eating Frequency Scale** (`eating_frequency_scale`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your eating frequency is Low, Medium, or High.
* **Eating Frequency Scale Type** (`eating_frequency_scale_type`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your eating frequency scale type is Low, Medium, or High.
* **Eating Frequency Scale Description** (`eating_frequency_scale_description`, `text`, required): Describe your eating frequency scale.
* **Food Groups** (`food_groups`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your food groups (Carbohydrates, Fats, Sugary Foods, Protein).
* **Food Groups Description** (`food_groups_description`, `text`, optional): Describe your food group preferences.
* **Food Groups Scale** (`food_groups_scale`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your food group scale (Low, Medium, High).
* **Food Groups Scale Type** (`food_groups_scale_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your food group scale type (Carbohydrates, Fats, Sugary Foods, Protein).
* **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter your patient's goals.
* **Patient Goals Description** (`patient_goals_description`, `text`, optional): Describe your patient's goals.
* **Patient Goals Scale** (`patient_goals_scale`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your patient's goals scale (Low, Medium, High).
* **Patient Goals Scale Type** (`patient_goals_scale_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your patient's goals scale type (Carbohydrates, Fats, Sugary Foods, Protein).
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **Healthcare Provider Email** (`healthcare_provider_email`, `email`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's email.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name (second instance).
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Name 2** (`healthcare_provider_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's name (second instance).
* **Healthcare Provider Email 2** (`healthcare_provider_email_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's email (second instance).
* **Healthcare Provider Phone 2** (`healthcare_provider_phone_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number (second instance).
* **Patient Name 2** (`patient_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name (second instance).
* **Patient Email 2** (`patient_email_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's email (second instance).
* **Patient Phone 2** (`patient_phone_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number (second instance).
