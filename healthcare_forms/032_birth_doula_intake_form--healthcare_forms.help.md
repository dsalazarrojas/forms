# Birth Doula Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birth Doula Intake Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about your pregnancy, birth plans, and preferences. This form will help your doula understand your needs and provide the best possible support during your pregnancy journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly. Make sure to answer all required questions and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_contact_time`, `text`, optional): Preferred contact times for the doula to reach you.
* **Expected Due Date** (`expected_due_date`, `date`, required): The date of your expected delivery.
* **Current Age** (`current_age`, `number`, required): Your age.
* **Number of Pregnancies Including This One** (`gravidity`, `number`, required): Total number of pregnancies, including this one.
* **Number of Previous Births** (`parity`, `number`, required): Number of times you have given birth before.
* **Number of Miscarriages or Losses** (`miscarriage_loss`, `number`, optional): Number of miscarriages or losses you have experienced.
* **Type of Previous Deliveries** (`previous_deliveries`, `text`, optional): Describe past births.
* **Previous Cesarean Sections** (`cesarean_history`, `select_one`, optional): Have you had any previous C-sections? (Yes, No)
* **Interested in VBAC if Previous C-Section** (`vbac_interested`, `select_one`, optional): Are you interested in VBAC if you have had a previous C-section? (Yes, No, Undecided)
* **Complications in Previous Pregnancies** (`complications_previous`, `text`, optional): Have you experienced any complications in previous pregnancies.
* **Current Pregnancy Health Status** (`current_pregnancy_health`, `select_one`, required): How is your current pregnancy going? (Healthy - No Complications, Minor Concerns, Significant Concerns, High Risk)
* **Current Pregnancy Details or Concerns** (`current_pregnancy_details`, `text`, optional): Any specific issues or concerns with your current pregnancy.
* **Planned Birth Location** (`planned_birth_location`, `select_one`, required): Where do you plan to give birth? (Hospital, Birth Center, Home, Undecided)
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, optional): Name of your healthcare provider (e.g. doctor, midwife).
* **Who Will Be Present at Birth** (`birth_companions`, `text`, required): Who will be present at the birth.
* **Birth Goals** (`birth_goals`, `text`, required): What do you hope for during your birth experience.
* **Pain Management Plan** (`pain_management_plan`, `text`, optional): How do you plan to manage pain during labor and birth.
* **Labor Preparation or Classes** (`labor_preparation`, `select_one`, optional): Have you taken any labor preparation classes? (Yes, No, Planning To)
* **Have You Worked with a Doula Before** (`doula_experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you worked with a doula before? (Yes, No)
* **What Do You Expect from Your Doula** (`support_expectations`, `text`, required): What do you expect from your doula during your pregnancy and birth experience.
* **Specific Requests or Preferences** (`specific_requests`, `text`, optional): Any specific requests or preferences you have for your doula.
* **Preference for Length of Labor Support** (`labor_duration_preference`, `select_one`, required): How long do you want your doula to support you during labor? (Entire Labor and Birth, Active Labor Only, Flexible, Undecided)
* **Interest in Postpartum Support Services** (`postpartum_care_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Are you interested in postpartum support services? (Yes, No, Maybe)
* **Type of Postpartum Support Desired** (`postpartum_support_type`, `text`, optional): What kind of postpartum support do you desire.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Any additional information or comments you have for your doula.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required questions carefully.
* Provide as much detail as possible for each section.
* If you have any specific requests or concerns, please make sure to mention them in the "Specific Requests or Preferences" section.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any section, do not hesitate to reach out to your doula.
* The completed form will help your doula provide the best possible support during your pregnancy and birth experience.
