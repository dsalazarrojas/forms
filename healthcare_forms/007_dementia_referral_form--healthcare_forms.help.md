# Dementia Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients with dementia to a specialist unit for further evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct diagnosis for the patient from the "Diagnosis" field.
2. Choose the appropriate treatment plan for the patient from the "Treatment Plan" field.
3. Select the specialist unit where the patient will be referred to from the "Specialist Unit" field.
4. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information from the "Patient Info" fields.
5. Choose the healthcare provider who will be referring the patient from the "Healthcare Provider" field.
6. Select the specialist doctor who will be treating the patient from the "Specialist Doctor" field.
7. Determine the patient's status and select the corresponding option from the "Patient Status" field.
8. Confirm whether the patient has family members who can be contacted from the "Patient Family" field.
9. Enter the treatment location and specialist doctor's notes if necessary.
10. Schedule a follow-up date and status for the patient from the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Status" fields.
11. Enter any additional follow-up details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Diagnosis** (`Dementia`, `Alzheimer's`, `Other`): Select the correct diagnosis for the patient.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Medication`, `Therapy`, `Other`): Choose the treatment plan that best suits the patient's needs.
* **Specialist Unit** (`Memory Clinic`, `Geriatric Unit`, `Other`): Select the specialist unit where the patient will be referred to.
* **Patient Info** (`Patient Name`, `Patient DOB`, `Patient Address`, `Patient Phone`, `Patient Email`): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email (if available).
* **Healthcare Provider** (`Dr. John Doe`, `Dr. Jane Smith`): Choose the healthcare provider who will be referring the patient.
* **Specialist Doctor** (`Dr. John Doe`, `Dr. Jane Smith`): Select the specialist doctor who will be treating the patient.
* **Patient Status** (`Active`, `Active - In Progress`, `Other`): Determine the patient's status and select the corresponding option.
* **Patient Family** (`True`, `False`): Confirm whether the patient has family members who can be contacted.
* **Treatment Location** (`Hospital`, `Clinic`): Select the treatment location.
* **Specialist Doctor Notes** (`Text`): Enter any necessary notes for the specialist doctor.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Date`): Schedule a follow-up date for the patient.
* **Follow-up Status** (`Scheduled`, `Completed`, `Other`): Determine the follow-up status and select the corresponding option.
* **Follow-up Details** (`Text`): Enter any additional follow-up details if necessary.
* **Patient Notes** (`Text`): Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Referral Reason** (`Text`): Enter any additional information about the referral.
* **Patient ID** (`Text`): Enter the patient's ID number (if available).
* **Patient Contact** (`Text`): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Patient Address** (`Text`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Referral Date** (`Date`): Enter the date of referral.
* **Specialist Unit Details** (`Text`): Enter any additional information about the specialist unit.
* **Patient Phone** (`Text`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`Email`): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
