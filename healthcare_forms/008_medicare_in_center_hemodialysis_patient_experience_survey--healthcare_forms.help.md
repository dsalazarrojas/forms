# medicaid_in_center_hemodialysis_patient_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the center's hemodialysis services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that you answer all questions marked as "required".
3. If a question does not apply to you, please select "N/A" or provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Hemodialysis Services
**Hemodialysis Services**: Describe your experience with the hemodialysis services provided by the center. Please provide any comments or suggestions you may have.

### 2. Staff
**Staff**: Select the staff member you interacted with during your hemodialysis services.

* Option 1: Nurse
* Option 2: Doctor
* Option 3: Other (please specify)

### 3. Education
**Education**: If you received any education or training from the center during your hemodialysis services, please describe it.

### 4. Dialysis Access
**Dialysis Access**: Please select the type of dialysis access you used during your treatment.

### 5. Patient Experience
**Patient Experience**: Describe your overall experience with the center's hemodialysis services.

### 6. Comments
**Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your experience, please provide them here.

### 7. Email
**Email**: Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

### 8. Phone
**Phone**: Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

### 9. Follow up
**Follow up**: Select the follow-up option that best describes your preferences.

* Option 1: Contact me via email
* Option 2: Contact me via phone
* Option 3: Do not contact me
