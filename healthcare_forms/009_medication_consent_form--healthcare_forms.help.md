# medication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication_consent_form is designed to collect patient information and medication details for healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your personal and demographic information in the **Patient Information** section (page 1).
2. Enter your medication details in the **Medication Details** section (page 2).
3. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** section (page 3).
4. Sign below to confirm your understanding (page 4).
5. If necessary, include additional information about your medication in the **Medication Information** section (page 5).
6. Finally, please sign the form to confirm your consent (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is for collecting your basic information, such as your name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medication Details (Page 2)** (`medication_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the medication you are currently taking, including the dosage and any relevant medical condition.
* **Terms and Conditions (Page 3)** (`terms_conditions`, text, required: false):
	+ Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the healthcare provider.
* **Consent (Page 4)** (`consent`, text, required: false):
	+ Please sign here to confirm your understanding of the healthcare provider's terms and conditions.
* **Signature (Page 5)** (`signature`, text, required: false):
	+ This is where you will sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Medication Information (Page 6)** (`medication_info`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have any additional information about your medication, please include it here.
* **Date (Page 7)** (`date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of this form.
* **Time (Page 8)** (`time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of this form.
* **Patient Signature (Page 9)** (`patient_signature`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the final signature of the patient to confirm their consent and understanding.
