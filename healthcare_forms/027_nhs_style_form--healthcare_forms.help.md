# NHS Style Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NHS Style Form is designed to collect patient information and medical history in a comprehensive and structured manner. This helps healthcare professionals to better understand the patient's medical condition and provide more efficient and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your Phone Number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. If applicable, enter your Medical Record Number and Medical Record Provider.
5. Select your Gender.
6. Provide any relevant Medical History, Medical Condition, and Medical Specialty information.
7. Enter your Address and Postcode.
8. If required, indicate the status of your medical record and add any relevant notes.
9. If necessary, confirm the provider for your medical record and enter notes for any additional information.
10. Repeat the same steps for Medical Record Provider 2 and Medical Record Provider 3 if applicable.
