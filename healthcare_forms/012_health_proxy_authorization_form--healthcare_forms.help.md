# Health Proxy Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Proxy Authorization Form is used to appoint a healthcare proxy in a healthcare setting. The form requires the patient to provide information about the proxy's name, relationship, and other personal details to ensure that the patient's wishes are respected and their healthcare is managed accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the patient's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter the patient's address in the **Address** field.
6. Enter the patient's zip code in the **Zip Code** field.
7. Enter the name of the authorized person in the **Name of Authorized Person** field.
8. Select the relationship to the authorized person in the **Relationship to Authorized Person** field.
9. Enter the name of the authorized person's doctor in the **Name of Authorized Person's Doctor** field.
10. Select the relationship to the authorized person's doctor in the **Relationship to Authorized Person's Doctor** field.
11. Enter the authorized person's signature in the **Signature** field.
12. Enter the authorized person's printed name in the **Authorized Person's Name (Print or Type)** field.
13. Enter the date of authorization in the **Date of Authorization** field.
14. Select the time of authorization in the **Time of Authorization** field.
15. Enter the authorized person's printed name in the **Authorized Person's Name (Print or Type) Time** field.
16. Enter the date of the second authorization (if necessary) in the **Date 2** field.
17. Enter the time of the second authorization (if necessary) in the **Time of Authorization 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address** (Required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Zip Code** (Required): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Name of Authorized Person** (Required): Enter the name of the authorized person.
* **Relationship to Authorized Person** (Required): Select the relationship to the authorized person.
* **Name of Authorized Person's Doctor** (Required): Enter the name of the authorized person's doctor.
* **Relationship to Authorized Person's Doctor** (Required): Select the relationship to the authorized person's doctor.
* **Signature** (Required): Enter the authorized person's signature.
* **Authorized Person's Name (Print or Type)** (Required): Enter the authorized person's printed name.
* **Authorized Person's Name (Print or Type) Relationship** (Required): Enter the authorized person's printed name and relationship.
* **Date of Authorization** (Required): Enter the date of authorization.
* **Time of Authorization** (Required): Select the time of authorization.
* **Authorized Person's Name (Print or Type) Time** (Required): Enter the authorized person's printed name and time of authorization.
* **Date 2** (Required): Enter the date of the second authorization (if necessary).
* **Time of Authorization 2** (Required): Select the time of the second authorization (if necessary).

## Tips

* Ensure that the patient's name and authorized person's name are spelled correctly.
* Double-check the email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter the date and time of authorization correctly.
* Sign and print your name as required.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
