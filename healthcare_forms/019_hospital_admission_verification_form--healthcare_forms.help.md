# hospital_admission_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital admission verification form is used to verify patient information and ensure accurate hospital admission records. This form helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date patient data, which is crucial for providing efficient and effective medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate patient information.
2. Ensure that the patient's medical record number and phone number are correctly entered.
3. If the patient has a medical record number, please enter it in the "Medical Record Number" field.
4. If the patient has a phone number, please enter it in the "Patient Phone" field.
5. If the patient has an email address, please enter it in the "Patient Email" field.
6. Please indicate whether the patient is being admitted by a doctor or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's basic information.
  This field should contain the patient's name, medical record number, and other relevant details.
- **Patient Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name.
  This field is used to ensure that the patient's name is accurately recorded.
- **Birth Date** (3, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
  This field helps in tracking the patient's age and medical history.
- **Medical Record Number** (4, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
  This field is a unique identifier for the patient's medical records.
- **Patient Phone** (5, text, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
  This field ensures that the patient can be contacted for medical updates and emergencies.
- **Patient Email** (6, email, required: false): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
  This field is used to contact the patient for medical information and updates.
- **Medical Record Number** (7, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
  This is a duplicate field to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- **Admitting Doctor** (8, select_one, required: false): Indicate if the patient is being admitted by a doctor.
  This field helps in tracking the patient's admitting doctor and medical history.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If a patient has multiple medical record numbers, please enter all of them in the corresponding fields.
- Please contact the patient or their representative if you need any additional information.
