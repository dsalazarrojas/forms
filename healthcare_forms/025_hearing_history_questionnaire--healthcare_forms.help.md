# Hearing History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the patient's hearing history, including their symptoms, treatment history, and other relevant details. This information will help medical professionals understand the patient's condition and provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records or identification documents.
2. **Hearing Health**: Describe the patient's overall hearing health. For example, "Good" or "Bad."
3. **Ear Infection**: Select one of the following options:
	* Ear infection
	* No Ear infection
	* Not specified
4. **Hearing Loss**: Choose all that apply:
	* Left
	* Right
	* Both
5. **Hearing Impairment**: Select one of the following options:
	* None
	* Mild
	* Moderate
	* Severe
6. **Treatment History**: Describe any previous treatments or medications the patient has undergone for hearing-related issues.
7. **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of the patient's primary care physician or audiologist.
8. **Medical Condition**: Describe any underlying medical conditions that may be contributing to the patient's hearing loss.
9. **Other Health Issues**: List any other health issues the patient is currently experiencing.
10. **Hearing Test Results**: Describe the results of any hearing tests or exams the patient has undergone.
11. **Hearing Test Date**: Enter the date of the hearing test or exam.
12. **Hearing Test Result Date**: Enter the date the hearing test results were received.
13. **Hearing Test Location**: Describe where the hearing test was conducted.
14. **Doctor's Name Hearing Test**: Enter the name of the doctor who conducted the hearing test.
15. **Hearing Testing Method**: Select one of the following options:
	* Pure Tone Threshold
	* Recruitment
	* Speech Recognition
	* Bone Conduction
	* Other
16. **Type of Hearing Loss**: Choose all that apply:
	* Sensorineural
	* Conductive
	* Mixed
17. **Cause of Hearing Loss**: Choose all that apply:
	* Genetic
	* Injury
	* Age-related
	* Noise exposure
	* Unknown
18. **Ear Wax**: Select one of the following options:
	* Ear Wax Buildup
	* No Ear Wax Buildup
	* Not Specified
19. **Hearing Assistance Device**: Select one of the following options:
	* Hearing Aid
	* None
	* Other
20. **Hearing Aids Use**: Choose all that apply:
	* Yes
	* No
21. **Impact of Hearing Loss**: Choose all that apply:
	* Emotional
	* Social
	* Professional
	* Financial
22. **Hearing Loss Symptoms**: Choose all that apply:
	* Difficulty understanding speech
	* Difficulty understanding phone calls
	* Difficulty understanding TV
	* Difficulty with phone
	* Difficulty hearing in background noise
	* Difficulty hearing conversation
	* Difficulty hearing in quiet
23. **Duration of Hearing Loss**: Describe the duration of the patient's hearing loss.
24. **Date of Hearing Loss**: Enter the date the patient's hearing loss started.
25. **Other Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the patient's hearing history.
