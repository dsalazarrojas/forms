# Nutrition Coaching Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for clients to agree to the terms and conditions of the Nutrition Coaching Service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your client details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Specify your goals for the coaching program.
3. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
4. Acknowledge the coaching terms and version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (client_details)**:
	+ Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Goals (goals)**:
	+ Specify your goals for the coaching program.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms)**:
	+ Read and agree to these terms and conditions.
* **E-Signature (e_signature)**:
	+ This field is for digital acknowledgement.
* **Address (client_details_address)**:
	+ Enter your address.
* **Goals (client_goals)**:
	+ Specify the number of your goals.
* **Coaching Terms (coaching_terms)**:
	+ Read and understand the coaching terms.
* **E-Signature (e_signature)**:
	+ This is another field for digital signature.
* **Phone (client_details_phone)**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email (client_details_email)**:
	+ Enter your email.
* **Terms Acknowledgement (terms_acknowledgement)**:
	+ Acknowledge whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Coaching Terms Acknowledgement (coaching_terms_acknowledgement)**:
	+ Acknowledge whether you have read and agreed to the coaching terms.
* **Coaching Terms Version (coaching_terms_version)**:
	+ Specify whether you have agreed to the coaching terms version.
