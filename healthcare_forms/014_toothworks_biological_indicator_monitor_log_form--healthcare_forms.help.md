# toothworks_biological_indicator_monitor_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect biological indicator monitor log data for patients in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Info** section with the patient's name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Enter the date and time of the patient's treatment.
3. Choose the method of sterilization used for the patient.
4. Select the outcome of the patient's test result.
5. Input the value of the test result, if applicable.
6. Select whether or not the patient received treatment.
7. Enter any relevant notes from the patient and doctor.
8. Record the status of the sterilization process.
9. Select the status of the test result.
10. Enter any necessary contact information for the patient and doctor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name.
- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's address.
- **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time of the patient's treatment.
- **Sterilization Method** (`sterilization_method`, `text`, optional): Select the method of sterilization used for the patient.
- **Test Result** (`test_result`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the outcome of the patient's test result.
- **Test Result Value** (`test_result_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of the test result, if applicable.
- **Treatment ID** (`treatment_id`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether or not the patient received treatment.
- **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information from the doctor.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name.
- **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
- **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
- **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes from the patient.
- **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes from the doctor.
- **Sterilization Status** (`sterilization_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the sterilization process.
- **Test Result Status** (`test_result_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the test result.
- **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the next visit.
- **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the doctor's ID.
- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's address.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out as much information as possible will help ensure accurate and complete patient data.
