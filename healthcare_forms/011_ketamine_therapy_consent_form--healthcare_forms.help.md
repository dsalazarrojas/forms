# Ketamine Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ketamine Therapy Consent Form is a medical document that outlines the patient's consent for treatment with Ketamine therapy. This form is used to ensure the patient's understanding and agreement to the treatment plan and any potential risks associated with it.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including their name, address, and contact details.
2. Answer questions about the patient's medical history and any current medications they are taking.
3. Describe the treatment plan and any medications that may be prescribed.
4. Indicate whether the patient has any allergies or sensitivities to medications.
5. Provide emergency contact information in case of an emergency.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (`patient_info`, text, required): Please enter the patient's name, address, and contact details.
* **Medical History (2)** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Treatment Details (3)** (`treatment_details`, text, required): Outline the treatment plan, including any medications that may be prescribed.
* **Medications (4)** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, required): Check all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medication Dosage (5)** (`medication_dosage`, number, required): Enter the dosage of any medications prescribed.
* **Follow-up Info (6)** (`follow_up_info`, text, required): Provide information about the patient's follow-up appointments and any specific instructions for follow-up care.
* **Emergency Contact (7)** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Patient Declaration (8)** (`patient_declaration`, text, required): Write a statement indicating that the patient understands and agrees to the treatment plan.
* **Clinic Signature (9)** (`clinic_signature`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm the clinic's acceptance of the patient's consent.
* **Patient Signature (10)** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm the patient's agreement to the treatment plan.
* **Date (11)** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the form is being completed.
* **Time (12)** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time the form is being completed.
* **Note (13)** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email (14)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the clinic's email address for correspondence.
* **Phone (15)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the clinic's phone number for contact.
* **Clinic Note (16)** (`clinic_note`, note, optional): Add any additional clinic notes or comments.
* **Patient Phone (17)** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for contact.
* **Clinic Email (18)** (`clinic_email`, email, optional): Enter the clinic's email address for correspondence.
* **Clinic Address (19)** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter the clinic's address.
* **Patient Address (20)** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Clinic Phone (21)** (`clinic_phone`, text, optional): Enter the clinic's phone number.
* **Clinic Email 2 (22)** (`clinic_email2`, email, optional): Enter the clinic's secondary email address.
* **Clinic Note 2 (23)** (`clinic_note2`, note, optional): Add any additional clinic notes or comments.
* **Clinic Note 3 (24)** (`clinic_note3`, note, optional): Add any additional clinic notes or comments.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed. Fields marked as "optional" can be completed if desired.
