# Physician Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Physician Application Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your professional qualifications, background, and availability to provide quality patient care. It's essential to complete this form accurately and honestly, as it will be reviewed and used for evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your name and contact information in the "Physician Name" and "Contact Phone" fields.
2. Indicate your availability to work by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Availability" field.
3. Select your relevant medical specialty from the "Job Category" field.
4. Enter the number of years you have been practicing medicine in the "Years of Experience" field.
5. List your educational background, such as Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral degrees.
6. Specify the languages you are fluent in from the "Languages Spoken" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Note" field.
8. If applicable, upload your resume and medical license number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your medical license.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Job Category (select multiple)**: Select all relevant medical specialties you hold or have experience in.
* **License Number (number)**: Enter your medical license number, if applicable.
* **Availability (select one)**: Indicate whether you are available to work or not.
* **Years of Experience (number)**: Enter the number of years you have been practicing medicine.
* **Education Background (select multiple)**: List your relevant educational background, such as Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral degrees.
* **Languages Spoken (select multiple)**: Specify the languages you are fluent in.
* **Additional Note (note)**: Provide any additional comments or explanations.
* **Upload Resume (text)**: Attach your resume for review.
* **Upload License (text)**: Attach your medical license number, if applicable.

Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask. Thank you for your application!
