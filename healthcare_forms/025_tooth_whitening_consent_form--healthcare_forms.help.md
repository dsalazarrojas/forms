# Tooth Whitening Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tooth Whitening Consent Form is used to collect essential information from patients before undergoing tooth whitening treatment. It helps healthcare providers gather medical and dental history, allergies, and consent to proceed with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Information section with your name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Answer the Questionnaire section with your medical and dental history, including any allergies or medical conditions you may have.
3. Indicate your medical conditions, medications, and medical tests you've undergone.
4. Answer the Dental History section with your dental history, including any previous dental treatments or procedures.
5. Check the Allergies section to report any allergies you have.
6. Sign the Signature section to indicate your consent to proceed with the tooth whitening treatment.
7. Review and complete the Medical Conditions, Medications, and Medical Tests sections to report any medical conditions, medications, or medical tests you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Questionnaire (2)** (`Questionnaire`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the questions regarding your medical and dental history, including any allergies or medical conditions you may have.
* **Waiver And Consent (3)** (`Waiver And Consent`, `text`, required/optional): This section should remain empty, as it's a placeholder for the healthcare provider to document their consent.
* **Contact Information (4)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **Dental History (5)** (`Dental History`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the questions regarding your dental history, including any previous dental treatments or procedures.
* **Medical History (6)** (`Medical History`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the questions regarding your medical history, including any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Allergies (7)** (`Allergies`, `text`, required/optional): Check all the allergies you have.
* **Signature (8)** (`Signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date this section to indicate your consent to proceed with the tooth whitening treatment.
* **Medical Conditions (9)** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' if you have any medical conditions.
* **Medications (10)** (`Medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' if you take any medications.
* **Medical Tests (11)** (`Medical Tests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' if you've undergone any medical tests.
* **Dental Tests (12)** (`Dental Tests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' if you've undergone any dental tests.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all the questions carefully and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
- Please review the form carefully before proceeding with the treatment.
