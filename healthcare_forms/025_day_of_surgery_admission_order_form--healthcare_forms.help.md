# Day Of Surgery Admission Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Of Surgery Admission Order Form is used to collect patient contact and medical information prior to their admission for surgery. This information helps the healthcare team to better understand the patient's needs and prepare for a smooth and safe surgical experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information.
* Enter the patient's contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* Provide information about the patient's medical history, including any cardiac problems, previous surgeries, and allergies.
* Enter the patient's medical information, including any current medication and medical conditions.
* Review and verify the contact information for any contact persons (e.g., family members or friends).
* Enter the patient's marital status and other relevant information.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Person 1 Information** (`contact_person_1`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Person 2 Information** (`contact_person_2`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Has the patient got heart problems?** (`medical_history_question_1`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options (Cardiac Problems or None).
* **Has the patient ever had major surgeries?** (`medical_history_question_2`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options (Major Surgeries or None).
* **Has the patient got allergies?** (`medical_history_question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options (Allergies or None).
* **Patient Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant medical information.
* **Patient Medical Info Text** (`medical_info_text`, `text`, required): Enter any additional medical information.
* **Patient Medication** (`patient_medication`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's current medication.
* **Patient Medication 2** (`patient_medication_2`, `text`, required): Enter any additional medical information.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options (Married, Single, Divorced, or Widowed).
* **Other Marital Status** (`marital_status_other`, `text`, required): Enter any other marital status information.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's address.
