# Headspace Self Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to refer themselves for mental health services. It is used to gather information about the patient's identity, reason for visit, and contact details in order to facilitate an efficient and effective referral process. Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and honestly, as the information provided will be used to assess the patient's needs and allocate necessary resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, including all applicable fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Enter accurate and honest information in each field, as this will help us understand your needs and provide the best possible care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Name (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Referral Date (<name>, date, required: false)
Indicate the date you would like to be seen for an appointment.

### Reason For Visit (<name>, text, required: false)
Explain your reason for visiting the mental health service. This will help us understand your needs and allocate the right resources for you.

### Contact Details (<name>, note, required: false)
Provide any additional contact information that may be helpful for us to reach you about your appointment or to discuss your care.

### Phone Number (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone about your appointment or to discuss your care.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly, as this will help us provide the best possible care for you. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
