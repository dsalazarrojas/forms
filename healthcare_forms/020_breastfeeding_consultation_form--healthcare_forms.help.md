# Breastfeeding Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from breastfeeding mothers to help healthcare professionals assess their breastfeeding journey and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all questions carefully, as they will help healthcare professionals understand your unique situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please describe your concerns in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother's Name** (`mother_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if available).
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this consultation.
* **Infant's Name** (`infant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your baby's name.
* **Infant Age** (`infant_age`, `select_one`, required): Select the age of your baby from the options provided.
	+ Newborn - 0 to 1 week
	+ 1 to 2 weeks
	+ 2 to 4 weeks
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ Over 12 months
* **Infant Birth Weight** (`birth_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter your baby's birth weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter your baby's current weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Current Breastfeeding Status** (`breastfeeding_status`, `select_one`, required): Select how you are currently breastfeeding.
	+ Exclusively breastfeeding
	+ Breastfeeding with supplements
	+ Exclusively formula feeding
	+ Previously breastfed
	+ Pumping exclusively
* **How long have you been Breastfeeding** (`breastfeeding_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Select the duration of your breastfeeding from the options provided.
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1 to 2 weeks
	+ 2 to 4 weeks
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ Over 6 months
* **Primary Concern or Reason for Consultation** (`chief_concern`, `text`, required): Describe your main concern or reason for consulting.
* **Feeding Frequency** (`feeding_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often your baby feeds.
	+ Every 1-2 hours
	+ Every 2-3 hours
	+ Every 3-4 hours
	+ Every 4-5 hours
	+ Irregular
* **Duration Per Feeding** (`feeding_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Select the duration of each feeding session.
	+ 5-10 minutes
	+ 10-15 minutes
	+ 15-20 minutes
	+ 20-30 minutes
	+ Variable duration
* **Breast Symptoms or Issues** (`breast_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any issues you are experiencing with your breast.
	+ Pain or soreness
	+ Engorgement
	+ Plugged ducts
	+ Nipple damage
	+ Inverted or flat nipples
	+ Low milk supply concerns
	+ Oversupply
	+ Mastitis
	+ No issues
* **Infant Feeding Issues** (`infant_feeding_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any feeding problems you are experiencing with your baby.
	+ Latch problems
	+ Tongue tie
	+ Difficulty swallowing
	+ Reflux or spitting up
	+ Fussiness during feeding
	+ Sleepiness while feeding
	+ Weight concerns
	+ No issues
* **Milk Production Assessment** (`milk_production`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you would assess your milk supply.
	+ Very low supply
	+ Low supply
	+ Adequate supply
	+ Oversupply
	+ Unsure
* **Experience with Pumping** (`pumping_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Select your experience with pumping.
	+ No pumping
	+ Occasional pumping
	+ Regular pumping
	+ Exclusively pumping
* **Returning to Work or School** (`return_to_work`, `select_one`, optional): Select when you plan to return to work or school.
	+ Not returning soon
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ Over 6 months
* **Support System** (`support_system`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select who is supporting you with your breastfeeding journey.
	+ Spouse or partner
	+ Family members
	+ Friends
	+ Lactation consultant
	+ Breastfeeding group
	+ No support
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, optional): Describe what you would like to achieve with your breastfeeding journey.
