# Surgery Scheduling Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgery Scheduling Information Form is a crucial document that helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and prepare for the surgery. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful surgical process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the options carefully for each field and select the most appropriate choice that applies to your situation.
2. Enter your name and date of birth accurately as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Provide a contact person's name and relationship to you in case of emergencies.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any medical procedures or allergies.
5. Enter any relevant medical history and details about your health conditions.
6. Enter your height and weight accurately for proper surgical planning.
7. Enter the date of your birth for accurate patient records.
8. Enter the name and contact details of your emergency contact person.
9. Confirm if any surgical procedures will be performed (yes/no).
10. Select the type of anesthesia required for the surgery (yes/no).
11. Enter any additional procedure steps that need to be taken before, during, or after the surgery.
12. Enter the date of the scheduled surgery.
13. Confirm if a surgeon will be present during the surgery (yes/no).
14. Enter any other medical history or conditions that are not listed above.
15. Enter any notes or details about your medical conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Status**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your current medical status.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect the surgery.
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies or sensitivities that may impact the surgery.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical History Date**: Enter the date of your last medical check-up or relevant medical event.
* **Height**: Enter your accurate height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight**: Enter your accurate weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person and their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number and email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the contact person (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Procedure**: Confirm if any surgical procedures will be performed (yes/no).
* **Type of Anesthesia**: Select the type of anesthesia required for the surgery (yes/no).
* **Procedure Steps**: Enter any additional procedure steps that need to be taken before, during, or after the surgery.
* **Surgical Date**: Enter the date of the scheduled surgery.
* **Surgeon**: Confirm if a surgeon will be present during the surgery (yes/no).
* **Other Medical History**: Enter any other medical history or conditions that are not listed above.
* **Other Medical History Notes**: Enter any notes or details about your medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any current medical conditions or conditions that may affect the surgery.
* **Medical Condition Notes**: Enter any notes or details about your medical conditions.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by patients and is a crucial part of the surgical process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
