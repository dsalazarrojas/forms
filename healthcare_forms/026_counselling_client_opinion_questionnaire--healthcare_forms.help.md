# Counselling Client Opinion Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their counselling experience. The form has several questions that ask clients to rate their satisfaction or rating on a scale, and some questions that allow for free-form text responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Provide your name, email, phone number, client organization (if applicable), and therapist name (if applicable).
4. Enter the date and time of the session, and the number of hours and minutes spent in the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Satisfaction
* **Client Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the counselling services you received.
### Therapy Effectiveness
* **Therapy Effectiveness**: Rate how effective you found the therapy sessions to be.
### Areas for Improvement
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe any areas where you think the therapy could be improved.
### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills**: Rate the communication skills of your therapist.
### Time Management
* **Time Management**: Rate how well your therapist managed their time during the session.
### Scheduling Convenience
* **Scheduling Convenience**: Rate how convenient the scheduling process was for you.
### Client Service
* **Client Service**: Rate the service provided by your therapist.
### Session Goals
* **Session Goals**: Describe the goals for your therapy sessions.
### Client Recommendation
* **Client Recommendation**: Rate whether you would highly recommend the therapy services to others.
### Assigned Tools
* **Assigned Tools**: Select the tools assigned to you during the session.
### Therapist Skills
* **Therapist Skills**: Rate the skills of your therapist.
### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the counselling services.
### Comments
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
### Client Name
* **Client Name**: Enter your name.
### Client Organization
* **Client Organization**: If you represent an organization, enter the name of the organization.
### Therapist Name
* **Therapist Name**: If you received therapy from a specific therapist, enter their name.
### Session Date
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session.
### Session Time
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session.
### Session Hours
* **Session Hours**: Enter the number of hours spent in the session.
### Session Minutes
* **Session Minutes**: Enter the number of minutes spent in the session.
### Session Days
* **Session Days**: Rate whether the session was during the week or weekend.
### Client Location
* **Client Location**: Select where you received the session (online, in-person, or both).
### Assigned Form
* **Assigned Form**: Select the form that was assigned to you.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure of what a question is asking, contact us for clarification.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the "Comments" field to provide them.
