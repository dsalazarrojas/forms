# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information, consent, and signature for telemedicine services. It's a basic form for healthcare providers to obtain necessary details from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand the sections and fields.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely in each section.
3. Use the provided fields to ensure your answers are clear and concise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text): This section is for you to provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
  • Please fill in your name, address, and any additional contact information as required.
  • Ensure your contact number and email are accurate to enable smooth communication.
  • If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them briefly.
- **Consent** (`consent`, text): This is your statement of consent for telemedicine services.
  • Clearly indicate your agreement to telemedicine services, including video or phone consultations.
  • Be specific about your understanding of telemedicine services and any limitations you're aware of.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text): This is the space for your digital or handwritten signature.
  • Please sign your name to confirm your consent and agreement with the form.
  • Ensure your signature matches your name on your identification documents.
- **Date** (`date`, date): This is the date you're agreeing to receive telemedicine services.
  • Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2023) for accuracy.

## Tips
- Ensure you've read and understood each field before filling it out.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you need help or clarification, please contact the healthcare provider.
- This form is for your safety and convenience; it helps healthcare providers understand your needs and preferences.
