# Newborn First Doctor Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare providers to collect information for a newborn's first doctor appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form's text fields with relevant details about the newborn and their mother or parent.
2. Enter the date and time of the appointment in the date field.
3. Enter the start and end time of the appointment in the time field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new-born-first-doctor-appointment-1-label (Text)** (`new_born_first_doctor_appointment_1`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the appointment.
* **new-born-first-doctor-appointment-2-label (Text)** (`new_born_first_doctor_appointment_2`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the newborn's medical history.
* **new-born-first-doctor-appointment-3-label (Date)** (`new_born_first_doctor_appointment_3`, date, not required): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **new-born-first-doctor-appointment-4-label (Time)** (`new_born_first_doctor_appointment_4`, time, not required): Enter the start and end times of the appointment in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **new-born-first-doctor-appointment-5-label (Text)** (`new_born_first_doctor_appointment_5`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with healthcare staff.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
