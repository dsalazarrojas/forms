# Healthcare Professional Time Tracking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track healthcare professionals' shifts, roles, and other relevant information to support staff scheduling and resource allocation within healthcare organizations.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has two parts: **Section 1 - Shift Information** and **Section 2 - Additional Details**.

### Section 1 - Shift Information

- **Shift**: Select the shift you worked during the reporting period. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" options.
- **Role**: Select all the roles you were assigned to during the shift. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" options for each role.
- **Unit**: Select the unit you were working on during the shift. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" options.
- **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift. You can use the date picker to select a date.
- **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift. You can use the date picker to select a date.
- **Staff Member Name**: Enter your name as a staff member. This is a required field.
- **Role 2**: Enter your role in the organization (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
- **Unit 2**: Enter the unit you worked on (e.g., hospital department, clinic, etc.).
- **Shift 2**: Enter any additional information about your shift (e.g., notes, comments, etc.).

### Section 2 - Additional Details

- **Key Activities**: Enter any key activities you performed during the shift. This is a text field where you can provide additional comments or notes about your work.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of your entries to ensure the data is reliable.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the Key Activities field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form. The specific requirements and expectations may vary depending on the organization's policies and procedures.
