# Substance Abuse Treatment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substance Abuse Treatment Intake Form is designed to collect patient information for substance abuse treatment. Its primary goal is to provide healthcare providers with a comprehensive understanding of the patient's medical history, mental health, family history, and substance use patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required questions truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
2. Provide detailed responses to the text fields.
3. Select all applicable options for the substance use field.
4. Ensure you understand the implications of your responses on your treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing your basic contact information and demographic details such as name, date of birth, address, and contact numbers.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): This field is for describing any significant medical conditions, illnesses, or allergies you have. Please provide as much detail as possible to help healthcare providers understand your medical background.
* **Substance Use** (`substance_use`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for indicating the types of substances you have used. Please select all applicable options from the list provided:
	+ Heroin
	+ Cocaine
	+ Cannabis
	+ Amphetamines
	+ Alcohol
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, optional): This field is for describing any mental health concerns or conditions you may have, such as depression, anxiety, or other relevant issues.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, note, optional): This field is for providing information about any significant family medical or mental health concerns that may affect your treatment.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, note, optional): This field is for describing any additional medical information that may be relevant to your treatment.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure the most accurate and effective treatment plan.
- If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare provider.
- Please note that this form is not a substitute for medical advice or treatment. Consult your healthcare provider for personalized guidance.
