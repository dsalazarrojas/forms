# occupational_health_specialist_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The occupational health specialist referral form is used to gather and document patient information, referral details, and healthcare provider information for further treatment and referral processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" page.
2. Provide the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" page.
3. Specify the referral reason and details in the "Referral Reason" and "Referral Details" pages.
4. Select the assigned doctor and healthcare providers for the referral.
5. Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.
6. Confirm the referral date and time.
7. Update the referral status and select the required healthcare services.
8. Obtain signatures from both the patient and the healthcare provider.
9. Record the date and time of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (1, text, optional): Enter the patient's basic information, including name and address.
- **Medical History** (2, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous treatments.
- **Referral Reason** (3, text, optional): Specify the reason for the referral.
- **Referral Details** (4, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the referral.
- **Healthcare Providers** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select the healthcare providers involved in the referral.
- **Patient Contact** (8, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information.
- **Patient Phone Number** (9, number, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Patient Email** (11, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Assigned Doctor** (12, select_one, optional): Select the assigned doctor for the referral.
- **Referral Date** (13, date, optional): Record the date of the referral.
- **Referral Time** (14, time, optional): Record the time of the referral.
- **Referral Status** (15, select_one, optional): Update the status of the referral (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
- **Healthcare Services** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select the healthcare services required for the referral.
- **Patient Signature** (17, text, optional): Obtain the patient's signature.
- **Healthcare Provider Signature** (18, text, optional): Obtain the healthcare provider's signature.
- **Date of Service** (19, date, optional): Record the date of service.
- **Time of Service** (20, time, optional): Record the time of service.
- **Service Provider** (21, select_one, optional): Select the service provider.
- **Referral Number** (22, text, optional): Record the referral number.
- **Healthcare Provider Phone** (23, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
- **Patient Notes** (24, text, optional): Record any additional patient notes.
- **Healthcare Provider Notes** (25, text, optional): Record any additional healthcare provider notes.
