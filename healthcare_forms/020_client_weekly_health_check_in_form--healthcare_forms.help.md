# Client Weekly Health Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals monitor the health status of their clients on a weekly basis. It collects information on the client's overall health, wellness, and potential health concerns. This information helps healthcare providers to provide accurate and timely care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee ID (if available) in the "Employee ID" field. This is optional.
3. Select your department from the "Department" field.
4. Enter the date of the current week in the "Check-in Date" field.
5. Rate your overall health status for the week in the "Overall Health Status" field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Any COVID-19 symptoms?" and provide a detailed description if necessary.
7. Report any acute illnesses or infections in the "Any Acute Illness or Infections?" field.
8. Indicate if there are any changes in chronic conditions in the "Any Changes in Chronic Conditions?" field.
9. Report any changes in medications in the "Any Changes in Medications?" field.
10. Rate your sleep quality for the week in the "Sleep Quality" field.
11. Rate your stress level for the week in the "Stress Level" field.
12. Report the number of days you have exercised this week in the "Days Exercised This Week" field.
13. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Close Contact with Anyone with Symptoms?" and provide a detailed description if necessary.
14. Report your status for coming to work in the "Status for Coming to Work" field.
15. Add any additional health notes or concerns in the "Additional Health Notes or Concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Employee ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your employee ID if you have one.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Select your department from the list.
* **Check-in Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the current week.
* **Overall Health Status** (Select One, Required):
	+ Rate your overall health status for the week as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Any COVID-19 Symptoms?** (Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have had any COVID-19 symptoms this week.
* **If Yes, Describe Symptoms** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your symptoms.
* **Any Acute Illness or Infections?** (Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have had any acute illnesses or infections this week.
* **Any Changes in Chronic Conditions?** (Select One, Required):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if there are any changes in your chronic conditions this week.
* **Any Changes in Medications?** (Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have made any changes to your medications this week.
* **Sleep Quality** (Select One, Required):
	+ Rate your sleep quality for the week as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Stress Level** (Select One, Required):
	+ Rate your stress level as Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Days Exercised This Week** (Number, Optional): Report the number of days you have exercised this week.
* **Close Contact with Anyone with Symptoms?** (Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have had close contact with anyone who has symptoms this week.
* **Status for Coming to Work** (Select One, Required):
	+ Select your status for coming to work as Ready to work, Work from home preferred, Medical leave needed, or Other.
* **Additional Health Notes or Concerns** (Text, Optional): Add any additional health notes or concerns you may have.
