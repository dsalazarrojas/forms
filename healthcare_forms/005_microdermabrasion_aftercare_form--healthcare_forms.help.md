# Microdermabrasion Aftercare Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential contact and aftercare information from clients who have undergone microdermabrasion treatments. Completing this form helps medical professionals provide personalized aftercare advice and communicate effectively with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the first section.
2. Provide your name and any relevant details about your treatment.
3. Enter your phone number and email address to allow us to contact you.
4. Enter your address to ensure we can communicate with you effectively.
5. Share any relevant information about your treatment and aftercare needs.
6. Indicate if you are authorized to receive aftercare instructions and advice.
7. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your contact information?** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_questions`, text, required: false): This section is where you can enter your contact details to help us communicate with you effectively. Please provide your name, phone number, and email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Client Name** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_client_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name, which will help us personalize our communication with you.
* **Phone** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any urgent aftercare advice.
* **Email** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to send you personalized aftercare advice and recommendations.
* **Address** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_address`, text, required: false): This field is for your address, which will help us ensure we can communicate with you effectively.
* **Dermabrasion Treatment** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_dermabrasion_treatment`, text, required: false): Share any relevant information about your treatment, such as the type of treatment you underwent.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_aftercare_instructions`, text, required: false): Provide any specific aftercare instructions you need or have been advised to follow.
* **Downtime** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_downtime`, text, required: false): Indicate the length of downtime you need to follow after the treatment.
* **Downtime Length** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_downtime_length`, text, required: false): This field is not explicitly required but provides more information about the downtime period.
* **Aftercare Product Recommendations** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_aftercare_product_recommendations`, text, required: false): Share any aftercare product recommendations you have been advised to follow.
* **Additional Comments** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_additional_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Authorized** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form_authorized`, select_one, required: false): Check the box if you are authorized to receive aftercare instructions and advice.
