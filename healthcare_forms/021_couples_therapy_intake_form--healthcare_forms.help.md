# Couples Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Couples Therapy Intake Form, which helps the couples therapist understand your relationship dynamics, communication patterns, and treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as **required** in the explanations below.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in the non-required fields, as this information will help the couples therapist tailor the treatment to your needs.
3. For select fields, choose one or multiple options that apply to your situation.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Therapist Information (`couples_therapist_info`)**:
	+ This field is optional and allows you to provide any information about your therapist or therapist's office.
* **Couple Information (`couple_info`)**:
	+ This field is optional and is used to provide background information about you and your partner.
* **Problem Area (`problem_area`)**:
	+ This field is optional and is used to describe the issue you want to address in therapy.
* **Relationship Length (`relationship_length`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks how long you've been in your relationship.
* **Frequency of Conflict (`frequency_of_conflict`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks how often conflicts arise in your relationship.
* **Couple Counseling Experiences (`couple_counseling_experiences`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks if you or your partner have had any couple counseling experiences.
* **Communication Skills (`communication_skills`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks about your communication patterns with your partner.
	+ Choose one or multiple options:
		- **Good**: You and your partner usually communicate effectively.
		- **Fair**: You and your partner sometimes communicate effectively, but also have misunderstandings.
		- **Poor**: You and your partner rarely communicate effectively.
* **Conflict Resolution Skills (`conflict_resolution_skills`)**:
	+ This field is required and asks if you and your partner have the ability to resolve conflicts effectively.
	+ Choose one option: 
		- **Yes**: You and your partner can usually resolve conflicts.
		- **No**: You and your partner usually cannot resolve conflicts.
* **Couple Counseling Goal (`couple_counseling_goal`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks about your goals for couple counseling.
	+ Choose one or multiple options:
		- **Relationship Improvement**: You want to improve your relationship.
		- **Relationship Dissolution**: You want to end your relationship.
* **Treatment Plan (`treatment_plan`)**:
	+ This field is optional and is used to describe any specific treatment you would like to have.
* **Treatment Duration (`treatment_duration`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks how many sessions you expect the treatment to last.
* **Payment Information (`payment`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks if you are able to pay for the therapy sessions.
* **Couple Counseling Session Goal (`couple_counseling_session_goal`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks about your goals for the couple counseling sessions.
	+ Choose one or multiple options:
		- **Relationship Repair**: You want to repair your relationship.
		- **Relationship Improvement**: You want to improve your relationship.
		- **Relationship Dissolution**: You want to end your relationship.
* **Couple Counseling Session Frequency (`couple_counseling_session_frequency`)**:
	+ This field is required and asks if you are able to attend regular therapy sessions.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`)**:
	+ This field is optional and asks how you would like to pay for the therapy sessions.
	+ Choose one or multiple options:
		- **Card**: You want to pay with a credit card.
		- **Pay Now**: You want to pay immediately.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate assessment of your relationship dynamics.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your relationship issues and goals.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your therapist for guidance.
- This form is for your benefit, and it will help the couples therapist tailor the treatment to your unique needs.
