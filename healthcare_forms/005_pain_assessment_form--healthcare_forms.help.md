# Pain Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and track patient's pain levels, medical history, and follow-up information for further analysis and treatment planning in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the body area where you feel pain (head, neck, back, or shoulder).
2. Indicate the level of pain you are experiencing (number).
3. Specify the duration of your pain (number).
4. Select how often your pain occurs (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5. List any current medications you are taking (if any).
6. Provide a brief medical history.
7. Describe your pain in detail.
8. Indicate if you have experienced any medication side effects.
9. Choose the level of pain reduction you are experiencing (Significant, Moderate, Minimal).
10. Select the healthcare professionals you have seen for pain management (Doctor, Nurse, Physiotherapist).
11. Choose the methods you are using for pain reduction (Medication, Exercise, Rest, Other).
12. Schedule a follow-up appointment (date and time).
13. Specify how often you want to be followed up (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
14. Choose the healthcare provider you will follow up with (Doctor, Nurse, Physiotherapist).
15. Enter the date and time of your pain assessment.
16. Enter the name of the user who submitted this form.
17. Enter your name (optional).
18. Choose how you submitted this form (User, Admin).
19. Select the location where you submitted this form (Clinic, Home).
20. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pain Assessment Form** (page 1): This is the title of the form.
* **Body Area** (2): Choose the area where you feel pain (head, neck, back, or shoulder).
* **Pain Level** (3): Enter the level of pain you are experiencing (e.g., 1-10).
* **Pain Duration** (4): Enter the duration of your pain (e.g., number of days).
* **Pain Frequency** (5): Choose how often your pain occurs (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Current Medications** (6): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Medical History** (7): Provide a brief medical history.
* **Pain Description** (8): Describe your pain in detail.
* **Medication Side Effects** (9): Indicate if you have experienced any medication side effects.
* **Pain Reduction** (10): Choose the level of pain reduction you are experiencing (Significant, Moderate, Minimal).
* **Medical Team** (11): Select the healthcare professionals you have seen for pain management (Doctor, Nurse, Physiotherapist).
* **Pain Reduction Methods** (12): Choose the methods you are using for pain reduction (Medication, Exercise, Rest, Other).
* **Follow Up** (13): Schedule a follow-up appointment (date and time).
* **Follow Up Frequency** (14): Specify how often you want to be followed up (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Follow Up Provider** (15): Choose the healthcare provider you will follow up with (Doctor, Nurse, Physiotherapist).
* **Pain Assessment Date** (16): Enter the date of your pain assessment.
* **Pain Assessment Time** (17): Enter the time of your pain assessment.
* **Submitted By** (18): Enter the name of the user who submitted this form.
* **Submitted Date** (19): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time** (20): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By User** (21): Enter your name (optional).
* **Submitted By Role** (22): Choose how you submitted this form (User, Admin).
* **Submitted Location** (23): Select the location where you submitted this form (Clinic, Home).
* **Submitter Name** (24): Enter any additional comments or notes.
