# Physiotherapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Physiotherapy Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from patients to support the development of an effective treatment plan. This form is used to collect medical and demographic data that will help our physiotherapists understand your medical history, needs, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your basic contact information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and phone number.
2.  Provide your email address to ensure we can reach you with any important updates or instructions.
3.  Share your medical history, including any relevant health concerns or previous medical procedures you've had.
4.  Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you've had any recent medical procedures. If you've had a procedure, select the type from the list.
5.  Complete the form by reviewing your answers carefully and submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: A text field to record your first name. This will help our physiotherapist get to know you better.
*   **Last Name**: A text field to record your last name. This will help us understand your family background and medical history.
*   **Date of Birth**: A date field to record your date of birth. This will help us understand your age and medical history.
*   **Phone**: A text field to record your phone number. This will ensure we can reach you with any important updates or instructions.
*   **Email**: An email field to record your email address. This will help us communicate with you effectively.
*   **Medical History**: A note field to record your medical history, including any health concerns or previous medical procedures you've had.
*   **Medical Procedure**: A select-one field to indicate if you've had any recent medical procedures. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to proceed.
*   **Medical Procedure Type**: A select-one field to specify the type of medical procedure you've had, if applicable. Choose from the list of available options.
*   **Other**: If you've had a medical procedure not listed, select 'Other' to provide more information.

## Tips

*   Take your time to fill in the form accurately and carefully.
*   If you're unsure about any field, ask your physiotherapist for guidance.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
