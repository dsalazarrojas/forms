# veterinary_care_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary care records release form is a document that allows an owner to transfer their pet's medical records to another clinic. This form is a crucial step in ensuring a smooth transfer process and maintaining continuity of care for the pet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the receiving clinic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Veterinary Care Records Release Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as the pet's owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that the receiving clinic can contact you if needed.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the receiving clinic can communicate with you about the transfer.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet Birthday** (`pet_birthday`, date, required): Fill in your pet's birthday.
* **Veterinary Clinic Name** (`veterinary_clinic_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the clinic where your pet is currently being treated.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If your pet has any medical conditions, please describe them briefly.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional notes about your pet's medical history, please add them here.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to give consent for the transfer, or 'No' if you do not consent.
* **Transfer To** (`transfer_to`, select_multiple, required): Select the destination clinic for the transfer.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, required): Fill in the date you want to transfer the records.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, time, required): Choose the time you want the transfer to take place.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): If you have a specific reason for the transfer, please describe it briefly.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them here.

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Ensure that the owner's consent and contact information are up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the receiving clinic.
