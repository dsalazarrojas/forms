# strength_empire_consult_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal and medical history, habits, and preferences. This information will be used to create a personalized fitness and nutrition plan tailored to your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional information in each field. Please note that some fields may not be required, but are optional for providing more detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Optional): Please enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (Optional): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age.
* **Medical Insurance** (Optional): Please indicate if you have any medical insurance.
* **Medical Condition** (Optional): Please describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Goals** (Optional): What are your fitness goals? (e.g., lose weight, gain muscle, improve endurance).
* **Diet** (Optional): Describe your current diet, including any restrictions or preferences.
* **Exercise** (Optional): Describe your current exercise plan, including frequency and type.
* **Supplements** (Optional): Are you currently taking any supplements? (e.g., vitamins, minerals, proteins).
* **Medications** (Optional): Are you currently taking any medications? (e.g., blood thinners, painkillers).
* **Allergies** (Optional): Do you have any allergies or sensitivities?
* **Medical History** (Optional): Do you have any medical history that we should know about? (e.g., surgeries, illnesses).
* **Family History** (Optional): Do you have any family history that we should know about? (e.g., genetic predispositions).
* **Previous Injuries** (Optional): Have you had any previous injuries or surgeries?
* **Sleep** (Optional): What is your sleep schedule like? (e.g., regular, irregular, insomnia).
* **Stress Levels** (Optional): What is your stress level like? (e.g., high, low, moderate).
* **Social Media** (Optional): Which social media platforms do you have accounts on? (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Favorite Activities** (Optional): What are your favorite activities or hobbies?
* **Work** (Optional): What kind of work do you do? (e.g., office job, physical labor, entrepreneur).
* **Travel** (Optional): When was your last date of travel, or when are you planning to travel?
* **Medical Expenses** (Optional): Estimate your current medical expenses.
* **Medical Expenses Currency** (Optional): What is the currency of the medical expenses? (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR).
* **Medical Expenses Amount** (Optional): Enter the amount of medical expenses.
* **Medical Expenses Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the medical expenses.
