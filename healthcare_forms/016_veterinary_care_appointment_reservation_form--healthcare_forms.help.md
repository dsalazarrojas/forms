# Veterinary Care Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help pet owners book appointments at a veterinary clinic or hospital. It requires inputting the appointment date, time, pet owner's name, contact number, email, pet type, service type, and owner's comments. Additionally, it allows selection of a veterinary clinic, assigned provider, appointment status, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date for the appointment using the date picker provided.
2. Choose the time for the appointment using the time picker provided.
3. Enter the name of the pet owner.
4. Provide a contact number for the pet owner.
5. Enter the pet owner's email address.
6. Choose the type of pet by selecting from the options (Small, Medium, Large, Other).
7. Select the type of service required (Examination, Vaccination, Consultation, Other).
8. Add any additional comments or notes for the assigned provider.
9. Choose the veterinary clinic where the appointment will take place (Pet Clinic, Animal Hospital, Other).
10. Select the assigned provider for the appointment (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Other).
11. Choose the status of the appointment (Confirmed, Rescheduled, Cancelled, Pending).
12. Select the payment method (Credit Card, Cash, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Appointment Date (appointment_date)**: Choose the date for the appointment by selecting from the available dates in the calendar.
* **Select Time (selected_time)**: Choose the time for the appointment by selecting from the available times in the clock.
* **Pet Owner Name (owner_name)**: Enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter the contact number for the pet owner.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the pet owner's email address.
* **Pet Type (pet_type)**: Choose the type of pet by selecting from the options (Small, Medium, Large, Other).
* **Service Type (service_type)**: Select the type of service required by choosing from the options (Examination, Vaccination, Consultation, Other).
* **Owner Comment (owner_comment)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the assigned provider.
* **Veterinary Clinic (veterinary_clinic)**: Choose the veterinary clinic where the appointment will take place by selecting from the options (Pet Clinic, Animal Hospital, Other).
* **Assigned Provider (assigned_provider)**: Choose the assigned provider for the appointment by selecting from the options (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Other).
* **Appointment Status (appointment_status)**: Choose the status of the appointment by selecting from the options (Confirmed, Rescheduled, Cancelled, Pending).
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose the payment method by selecting from the options (Credit Card, Cash, Other).
* **Owner Signature (owner_signature)**: Please sign this form to confirm that the appointment details are accurate.
