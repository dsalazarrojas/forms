# Chronic Pain Management Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients or participants about their chronic pain management. The form aims to gather details about their current pain level, medication effectiveness, and other relevant factors that impact their treatment and care. The collected data will be used to better understand the needs and experiences of chronic pain patients, enabling healthcare professionals to provide more personalized and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields. Your email address will only be used for follow-up purposes if you have given consent.
2. Select your current pain level from the scale provided (0-10).
3. Choose how effective your current medications are in managing your pain.
4. Indicate if you have tried physical therapy in the past 12 months.
5. Rate how often pain disturbs your sleep.
6. Answer whether pain impacts your mood or mental wellbeing.
7. If applicable, describe any barriers to receiving care that you are currently experiencing.
8. Choose any additional treatments you are interested in exploring (such as counseling, acupuncture, or injectables).
9. Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address, which will only be used for follow-up purposes if you have given consent. This is an optional field.
* **Current pain level**: Select a number from 0 to 10, where 0 indicates no pain and 10 indicates the highest level of pain you are experiencing.
* **Medication effectiveness**: Choose how effective your current medications are in managing your pain.
* **Use of physical therapy**: Indicate if you have tried physical therapy in the past 12 months.
* **Sleep disturbance**: Rate how often pain disturbs your sleep (Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always).
* **Mood impact**: Answer whether pain impacts your mood or mental wellbeing (Not at all, A little, Moderately, Significantly).
* **Barriers to care**: Describe any obstacles you are experiencing that may hinder your treatment or care. This is an optional field.
* **Additional treatments interested**: Choose any additional treatments you are interested in exploring, such as counseling, acupuncture, or injectables.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any further comments or concerns about your care that you would like to share with your healthcare team.

## Tips

* Please answer questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
* You can choose multiple options for "Additional treatments interested".
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare team.
