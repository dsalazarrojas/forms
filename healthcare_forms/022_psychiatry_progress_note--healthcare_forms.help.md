# Psychiatry Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatry Progress Note form is designed to record and track the progress of patients with mental health conditions. It helps healthcare professionals to document the patient's current mental state, treatment plan, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Session Notes** in the text field, describing the current mental state and any notable events or concerns discussed during the session.
2. Select the **Mental Health Condition** that best describes the patient's condition from the dropdown menu. This will help you to identify the specific condition being addressed.
3. Enter the **Patient Name** in the text field, ensuring you use the patient's full name and any relevant initials or IDs.
4. Enter the **Provider Name** in the text field, ensuring you use your own name or the name of the clinic/organization.
5. Enter the **Chief Complaint** in the text field, describing the primary reason for the patient's visit or current concerns.
6. If the patient's chief complaint is not listed in the dropdown menu, use the **Chief Complaint Other** field to describe it in more detail.
7. Enter the **Social History** in the text field, including any relevant background information about the patient's life, medical history, or family dynamics.
8. Enter the **Mental Status** in the text field, describing the patient's current mental state, including any notable changes or concerns.
9. Enter the **Plan** in the text field, outlining the treatment plan and any specific goals or objectives.
10. Enter the **Next Session** date, indicating the date of the next scheduled appointment.
11. Enter the **Follow-up Plan** in the text field, describing the follow-up plan, including any specific actions or appointments.
12. Enter the **Follow-up Frequency**, specifying the frequency of follow-up appointments.
13. Enter the **Follow-up Contact**, specifying the contact method (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
14. Enter the **Follow-up Contact Other** field if the follow-up contact method is not listed in the dropdown menu.
