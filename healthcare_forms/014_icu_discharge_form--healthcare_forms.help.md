# ICU Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICU Discharge Form is a template used to document the details of a patient's discharge from the Intensive Care Unit (ICU). This form is essential in ensuring that the patient's care transitions smoothly from the hospital to their home or other care settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review the patient's information**: Ensure that you have access to the patient's medical history, current status, and any relevant medical notes.
2. **Complete the patient information page**: Fill in the patient's basic details, such as their name, email, and phone number.
3. **Provide a discharge summary**: Describe the patient's condition and treatment in a concise manner, highlighting any important details that need to be shared with the patient or their caregivers.
4. **Enter the discharge date and time**: Select the date and time of the patient's discharge from the ICU.
5. **Sign the form**: Verify your role (physician or nurse) and provide your name as a sign of approval.
