# X Ray Refusal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The X Ray Refusal Form is used to document a patient's informed refusal of an X-ray procedure. This form ensures that patients are aware of the risks and benefits of the X-ray and are able to provide informed consent before undergoing the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Informed Refusal". If you choose "Yes", provide a reason for refusal, if necessary.
3. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that the patient has been informed and agrees to refuse the X-ray procedure.
4. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (text)**: A field to enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information. This information is used to identify the patient and ensure that the refusal is documented for future reference.
* **Informed Refusal (select multiple)**: A field to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient has provided informed consent. If "Yes" is selected, you may need to provide a reason for refusal, as prompted.
* **Signature (text)**: A field for the healthcare provider to sign and date, acknowledging that the patient has been informed and has refused the X-ray procedure.
* **Date (date)**: A field to enter the date on which the refusal was documented. This ensures that the refusal is accurately dated and timestamped.

## Tips

* Always ensure that the patient has received and understood the risks and benefits of the X-ray procedure before completing this form.
* If the patient has a valid medical reason for refusing the X-ray, be sure to document this reason in the "Informed Refusal" field.
