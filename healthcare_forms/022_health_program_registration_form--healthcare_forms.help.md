# health_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form designed for participants of healthcare-related programs. It is meant to gather participant information, program details, and other relevant data to facilitate communication between program administrators and participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant information section with your details, including your name, program status (new or existing), and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your email address in the email address section so that program administrators can contact you.
3. Specify the dates of the program in the program dates section.
4. Choose your status as a new or existing participant in the participant status section.
5. Enter the location of the program in the program location section.
6. If applicable, list any relationships you have with the program or its participants in the participant relationships section.
7. Specify the services you are interested in or currently receiving in the program services section.
8. Provide your consent to participate in the program by signing in the participant consent section.
9. Add any additional notes about your participation in the participant notes section.
10. Finally, sign the participant signature section to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Page 1)** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your personal details, including your name, program status (new or existing), and any other relevant information.
* **Program Information (Page 1)** (`program_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the program you are interested in or currently enrolled in.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Email Address (Page 2)** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so that program administrators can contact you.
* **Program Dates (Page 2)** (`program_dates`, `text`, required: false): Please specify the dates of the program you are interested in or currently enrolled in.
* **Participant Status (Page 2)** (`participant_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether you are a new or existing participant in the program.
* **Program Location (Page 3)** (`program_location`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the location of the program.
* **Participant Relationships (Page 3)** (`participant_relationships`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, list any relationships you have with the program or its participants.
* **Program Services (Page 4)** (`program_services`, `text`, required: false): Please specify the services you are interested in or currently receiving in the program.
* **Participant Consent (Page 5)** (`participant_consent`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your consent to participate in the program by signing in this section.
* **Participant Notes (Page 6)** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required: false): Please add any additional notes about your participation in the program.
* **Participant Signature (Page 7)** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign this section to confirm your registration.
