# Patient Progress Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture patient progress information, including medical history, treatments, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to gather comprehensive data about a patient's health status, medical history, and progress, which can be used to inform treatment decisions and monitor patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully, and ensure you are answering all required fields.
2. Enter the patient's basic information on the "Patient Info" page, including any relevant medical history, allergies, and contact details.
3. On the "Medical Notes" page, provide any additional medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.
4. On the "Treatment Info" page, record any medications, treatments, or interventions the patient is currently undergoing.
5. For the "Medical History" section, select "Yes" if the patient has the listed condition, and "No" otherwise.
6. For the "Medications" and "Lab Results" sections, select all applicable options.
7. In the "Patient Outcomes" section, select all applicable options.
8. In the "Medical Team" section, select all applicable team members.
9. Enter the date for "Follow Up" if applicable.
10. Select the patient's current "Medical Status" (Active or Inactive).
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Patient Progress".
12. Select all applicable "Medical Certifications".
13. Enter any relevant "Insurance" information.
14. Provide "Patient Contact" details.
15. Enter "Patient Address".
16. Enter "Patient Email".
17. Enter any relevant "Physician Info".
18. Provide any "Medical History Allergies".
19. Enter any "Medical History Surgeries".
20. Enter any "Medical History Medical Condition".
21. Enter any "Medical History Injuries".
22. Enter any "Medical History Family History".
23. Enter any "Medical History Social History".
24. Enter any "Medical History Illnesses".
25. Enter any "Medical History Vaccinations".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's basic information, including name, contact details, and medical history.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Treatment Info** (`treatment_info`, `text`, optional): Record any medications, treatments, or interventions the patient is currently undergoing.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if the patient has the listed condition, and "No" otherwise.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable medications.
* **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, `select_one`, optional): Select all applicable lab results.
* **Patient Outcomes** (`patient_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable patient outcomes.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable team members.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for follow up.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the patient's current medical status (Active or Inactive).
* **Patient Progress** (`patient_progress`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for patient progress.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable medical certifications.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide patient contact details.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email.
* **Physician Info** (`physician_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant physician information.
* **Medical History Allergies** (`medical_history_allergies`, `text`, optional): Provide any medical history allergies.
* **Medical History Surgeries** (`medical_history_surgeries`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history surgeries.
* **Medical History Medical Condition** (`medical_history_medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history medical condition.
* **Medical History Injuries** (`medical_history_injuries`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history injuries.
* **Medical History Family History** (`medical_history_family_history`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history family history.
* **Medical History Social History** (`medical_history_social_history`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history social history.
* **Medical History Illnesses** (`medical_history_illnesses`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history illnesses.
* **Medical History Vaccinations** (`medical_history_vaccinations`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical history vaccinations.
