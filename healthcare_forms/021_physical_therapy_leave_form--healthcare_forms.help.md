# Physical Therapy Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees or students report their need for physical therapy, providing information about their availability, employer/educational institution, and reason for absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Request Form" field with any relevant information.
2. Indicate your availability using the "Availability" and "Availability 2" fields. You can select multiple options as needed.
3. If you are an employee or student of an employer/educational institution, indicate it in the "Employer/Educational Institution" field.
4. Provide comments or any additional information in the "Comments" and "Comments 2" fields.
5. If you are requesting a physical therapy leave, indicate your reason for absence in the "Reason for Absence" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your request for physical therapy.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your availability, choosing from "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if not listed, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation).
* **Employer/Educational Institution** (`employer_educational_institution`, select_multiple, required): If you are an employee or student, indicate whether your institution is an employer or educational provider.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Add any comments or explanations related to your request.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_multiple, optional): Provide additional information about your availability, if not provided in the previous field.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your absence, selecting from "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if not listed, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Submit your completed form to request physical therapy.
