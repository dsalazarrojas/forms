# BetterNOW Healthcare Confidential Patient Intake Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect confidential patient intake information for healthcare services. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the patient's identity, medical history, and lifestyle factors that might affect their health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to you.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Full Name**
`Full Name (required)`: This is your complete name, including your first and last name.

### **2. Date of Birth**
`Date of Birth (required)`: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **3. Gender**
`Gender (required)`: Please select your gender from the options:
	* Male
	* Female
	* Non-binary
	* Prefer not to say

### **4. Contact Phone Number**
`Contact Phone Number (required)`: This is the phone number of the contact person or the patient's preferred contact method.

### **5. Email Address**
`Email Address (required)`: This is the email address of the contact person or the patient's preferred email address.

### **6. Primary Reason for Visit**
`Primary Reason for Visit (required)`: Briefly describe your main health concern.

### **7. Current Medications**
`Are you currently taking any medications? (optional)`: If you are taking any medications, please list them, including prescriptions, over-the-counter, and supplements.

### **8. Allergies**
`Do you have any known allergies? (optional)`: If you have any known allergies, please list them.

### **9. Current Pain Level**
`Current Pain Level (required)`: Please rate your current pain level from 0 to 10.

### **10. Chronic Conditions**
`Have you been diagnosed with any of the following? (optional)`: Select all that apply:
	* Diabetes
	* Hypertension
	* Heart Disease
	* Asthma
	* Arthritis
	* Depression/Anxiety

### **11. Recent Hospitalization**
`Have you been hospitalized in the last 12 months? (required)`: Please select true or false.

### **12. Family Medical History**
`Significant Family Medical History (optional)`: Please describe significant medical history of immediate family members.

### **13. Lifestyle Factors**
`Select all that apply (optional)`: Please select all that apply:
	* Regular Exercise
	* Smoker
	* Alcohol Consumption
	* High Stress Environment

### **14. Emergency Contact Name**
`Emergency Contact Name (required)`: Please enter the name of the emergency contact.

### **15. Emergency Contact Phone Number**
`Emergency Contact Phone Number (required)`: Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the patient or authorized representative. If you need assistance or clarification, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
