# Vaccination Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and vaccination details for the healthcare provider. It is intended for patients who require vaccinations and need to provide relevant health data to ensure proper vaccination delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Enter patient name" field (Field 1).
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (Field 2).
3. Specify the preferred vaccination date in the "Preferred vaccination date" field (Field 3).
4. Enter the healthcare provider's information in the "Healthcare provider" field (Field 4).
5. Provide the patient's contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field (Field 5).
6. Enter the patient's contact email in the "Contact email" field (Field 6).
7. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient address" field (Field 7).
8. Enter the city where the patient lives in the "City" field (Field 8).
9. Enter the state where the patient lives in the "State" field (Field 9).
10. Enter the patient's zip code in the "Zip" field (Field 10).
11. Enter the date of the vaccination in the "Vaccination date" field (Field 11).
12. Enter the time of the vaccination in the "Vaccination time" field (Field 12).
13. Specify the type of vaccine in the "Vaccine type" field (Field 13).
14. Enter the patient's dose information, including dose number and dose interval, in the "Dose", "Dose number", and "Dose interval" fields (Fields 14, 15, and 16).
15. Enter the total dose of the vaccine administered in the "Total dose" field (Field 17).
16. Specify the contact relationship with the patient in the "Contact relationship" field (Field 18).
17. If the contact relationship is not listed, specify it in the "Other (please specify)" field (Field 19).
18. Enter any additional patient notes in the "Patient notes" field (Field 20).
19. Enter any provider notes in the "Provider notes" field (Field 21).
20. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical history" field (Field 22).
21. List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field (Field 23).
22. Enter any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medication" field (Field 24).
23. Finally, enter your provider signature in the "Provider signature" field (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter patient name** (Field 1, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Field 2, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Preferred vaccination date** (Field 3, date, required: false): Specify the preferred date for the vaccination.
* **Healthcare provider** (Field 4, text, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Contact phone** (Field 5, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Contact email** (Field 6, email, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Patient address** (Field 7, text, required: false): Enter the patient's home address.
* **City** (Field 8, text, required: false): Enter the city where the patient lives.
* **State** (Field 9, text, required: false): Enter the state where the patient lives.
* **Zip** (Field 10, text, required: false): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Vaccination date** (Field 11, date, required: false): Enter the date of the vaccination.
* **Vaccination time** (Field 12, time, required: false): Enter the time of the vaccination.
* **Vaccine type** (Field 13, text, required: false): Specify the type of vaccine administered.
* **Dose** (Field 14, number, required: false): Enter the patient's dose information.
* **Dose number** (Field 15, number, required: false): Enter the dose number.
* **Dose interval** (Field 16, number, required: false): Enter the dose interval.
* **Total dose** (Field 17, number, required: false): Enter the total dose of the vaccine administered.
* **Contact relationship** (Field 18, text, required: false): Specify the contact relationship with the patient.
* **Other (please specify)** (Field 19, text, required: false): If the contact relationship is not listed, specify it here.
* **Patient notes** (Field 20, text, required: false): Enter any additional patient notes.
* **Provider notes** (Field 21, text, required: false): Enter any provider notes.
* **Medical history** (Field 22, text, required: false): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (Field 23, text, required: false): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medication** (Field 24, text, required: false): Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Provider signature** (Field 25, text, required: false): Enter your provider signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended to be used for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for medical professional advice.
