# Hormonal Health Cycle Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track their hormonal health cycles, including menstrual phases, symptoms, and lifestyle factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one of the following options for the "Daily Cycle Phases" field:
	* Menstruation
	* Follicular
	* Ovulation
	* Menopause
	* Postmenopause
2. Check off any symptoms you are experiencing in the "Symptoms" field:
	* Bloating
	* Cramps
	* Moodiness
	* Breast Tenderness
	* Fatigue
3. Select one option for the "Moods" field:
	* Happy
	* Sad
	* Frustrated
	* Angry
	* Tired
4. Enter your hours of sleep in the "Sleep" field. This will help you keep track of your sleep habits.
5. Indicate whether you engaged in exercise in the "Exercise" field:
	* True
	* False
6. If you took any medication, enter its name in the "Medication" field.
7. Log your water intake in the "Water Intake" field. You can enter the amount in cups.
8. Describe your diet in the "Diet" field, listing any food you consumed today.
9. Enter the date of your last menstruation in the "Last Period Date" field.
10. Enter the date of your next period in the "Next Period Date" field.
11. Enter the length of your current menstrual cycle in days in the "Menstruation Cycle Length" field.
12. Select the frequency of your symptoms:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
13. Check off any lifestyle factors that may be affecting your health:
	* Stressful
	* Busy
	* Fatigued

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Cycle Phases** (Select One): Select one of the following phases of your menstrual cycle:
	* Menstruation: The phase when you are bleeding or experiencing heavy flow.
	* Follicular: The phase when your body is producing follicles for ovulation.
	* Ovulation: The phase when your body releases an egg for potential fertilization.
	* Menopause: The phase when you are no longer experiencing menstruation.
	* Postmenopause: The phase after menopause.
* **Symptoms** (Check off any of the following): Select any symptoms you are experiencing:
	* Bloating: Feeling uncomfortably full or swollen.
	* Cramps: Muscle aches or spasms.
	* Moodiness: Feeling irritable or emotional.
	* Breast Tenderness: Feeling sensitive or sore in your breasts.
	* Fatigue: Feeling tired or lacking energy.
* **Moods** (Select One): Select one of the following moods:
	* Happy: Feeling content and positive.
	* Sad: Feeling depressed or sad.
	* Frustrated: Feeling trapped or stuck.
	* Angry: Feeling angry or resentful.
	* Tired: Feeling tired or lacking energy.
* **Sleep** (Hours): Enter the number of hours you slept.
* **Exercise** (Select One): Indicate whether you engaged in exercise:
	* True: You did exercise.
	* False: You did not exercise.
* **Medication** (Text): If you took any medication, enter its name.
* **Water Intake** (Text): Log your water intake in cups.
* **Diet** (Text): Describe your diet, listing any food you consumed today.
* **Menstruation Date** (Date): Enter the date of your last menstruation.
* **Last Period Date** (Date): Enter the date of your last period.
* **Next Period Date** (Date): Enter the date of your next period.
* **Menstruation Cycle Length** (Number): Enter the length of your current cycle in days.
* **Symptoms Frequency** (Select Multiple): Select the frequency of your symptoms:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
* **Lifestyle Factors** (Select Multiple): Select any lifestyle factors that may be affecting your health:
	* Stressful
	* Busy
	* Fatigued
