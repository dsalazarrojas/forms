# COVID 19 Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect medical history and health information from patients, including demographics, medical conditions, vaccination status, and lifestyle habits. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to assess a patient's medical history and develop an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Patient Information" section, including your full name, date of birth, age, and contact information.
2. In the "Medical History" section, answer questions about any chronic health conditions you may have, such as diabetes, heart disease, or cancer.
3. Provide details about any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
4. If you have any allergies, describe any severe allergic reactions you have experienced.
5. Answer questions about your COVID-19 history, including whether you have been diagnosed with COVID-19 and if so, the date of diagnosis.
6. In the "Vaccination Status" section, indicate whether you have been vaccinated against COVID-19 and if so, the type of vaccine you received.
7. In the "Lifestyle and Wellness" section, answer questions about your exercise frequency and smoking status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Patient Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (Age, Number, Optional): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (Gender, Select One, Optional): Select your gender from the options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to answer.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your email address as it appears on your email account.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your phone bill or ID.
* **Do you have any chronic health conditions?** (Chronic Conditions, Select Multiple, Required): Select any chronic health conditions you have from the options: Diabetes, Heart disease, Hypertension, Lung disease, Kidney disease, Cancer, Obesity, None, Other.
* **Details of chronic conditions** (Details of chronic conditions, Text, Optional): Provide any additional details about your chronic conditions.
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, Text, Optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Known Allergies** (Known Allergies, Text, Optional): List any known allergies you have.
* **Describe any severe allergic reactions** (Describe any severe allergic reactions, Text, Optional): Describe any severe allergic reactions you have experienced.
* **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?** (Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?, Select One, Required): Select whether you have been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Date of COVID-19 diagnosis** (Date of COVID-19 diagnosis, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your COVID-19 diagnosis if applicable.
* **Were you hospitalized for COVID-19?** (Were you hospitalized for COVID-19?, Select One, Optional): Select whether you were hospitalized for COVID-19.
* **Duration of hospitalization** (Duration of hospitalization, Text, Optional): Enter the duration of your hospitalization if applicable.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (COVID-19 Vaccination Status, Select One, Required): Select your COVID-19 vaccination status from the options: Not vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Fully vaccinated, Prefer not to answer.
* **Vaccine Type (if vaccinated)** (Vaccine Type (if vaccinated), Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of vaccine you received if vaccinated.
* **Number of booster doses** (Number of booster doses, Number, Optional): Enter the number of booster doses you received if vaccinated.
* **How often do you exercise?** (How often do you exercise?, Select One, Optional): Select how often you exercise from the options: Rarely, 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, 5+ times per week.
* **Smoking Status** (Smoking Status, Select One, Optional): Select your smoking status from the options: Never smoked, Former smoker, Current smoker.
