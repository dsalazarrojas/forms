# Medical Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to medical expenses, including services and costs associated with healthcare services. It helps to streamline the process of tracking and managing medical expenses within the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details as the submitter (fields: `user_input`, `submitter`, `submitter_name`, `submitter_email`, `submitter_phone`)
2. Enter the details of the medical expense, including the `medical_expense`, `medical_service`, `healthcare_provider`, and `medical_expense_amount_captured` fields
3. Specify the type and date of the medical expense using the `medical_expense_type_captured` and `medical_expense_date_captured` fields
4. Provide a description of the expense if necessary using the `medical_expense_description_captured` field

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field where you can enter any additional information about your medical expense.
### Medical Expense
* **Medical Expense** (`medical_expense`, text, optional): Describe the medical expense or service you are claiming.
### Medical Service
* **Medical Service** (`medical_service`, text, optional): Specify the medical service you received, such as hospitalization, doctor visit, etc.
### Submitter
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
### Submitter Info
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter.
### Healthcare Provider
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or doctor who provided the service.
### Medical Expense Amount
* **Medical Expense Amount** (`medical_expense_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the medical expense.
### Medical Expense Date
* **Medical Expense Date** (`medical_expense_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the medical expense.
### Medical Expense Type
* **Medical Expense Type** (`medical_expense_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of medical expense (e.g. Hospitalization, Doctor Visit, Lab Test, etc.)
### Medical Expense Description
* **Medical Expense Description** (`medical_expense_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the medical expense if necessary.
### Submitter Name
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
### Submitter E-mail
* **Submitter E-mail** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
### Submitter Phone
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
### Healthcare Provider Name
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or doctor who provided the service.
### Healthcare Provider Phone
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider or doctor.
### Healthcare Provider E-mail
* **Healthcare Provider E-mail** (`healthcare_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider or doctor.
### Medical Expense Amount Captured
* **Medical Expense Amount Captured** (`medical_expense_amount_captured`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the medical expense captured by the system.
### Medical Expense Date Captured
* **Medical Expense Date Captured** (`medical_expense_date_captured`, date, optional): Enter the date of the medical expense captured by the system.
### Medical Expense Type Captured
* **Medical Expense Type Captured** (`medical_expense_type_captured`, select one, optional): Choose the type of medical expense (e.g. Hospitalization, Doctor Visit, Lab Test, etc.)
### Medical Expense Description Captured
* **Medical Expense Description Captured** (`medical_expense_description_captured`, text, optional): Enter the description of the medical expense captured by the system.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your [healthcare provider/claims administrator] for assistance.
