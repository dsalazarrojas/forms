# Hospital Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Check In Form is a general form designed to collect patient information and medical history to ensure that our staff provides you with the best possible care during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, providing accurate information.
2. Select "Emergency" if you are visiting the hospital for an emergency medical condition.
3. Enter your name and date of birth as requested.
4. Provide your contact information and emergency contact information.
5. List any medical history, medications, or allergies you have.
6. If applicable, provide insurance information.
7. Take a photo of yourself for identification purposes (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (page 1)**: This section is where you can provide general information about yourself.
* **Medical Condition (page 2)**: Select "Emergency" if you are visiting the hospital for an emergency medical condition.
* **Name (page 3)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (page 4)**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information (page 5)**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact (page 6)**: List the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical History (page 7)**: List any medical history, medications, or allergies you have.
* **Medication (page 8)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Insurance Information (page 9)**: If you have insurance, provide the policy number and details of your coverage.
* **Payment Information (page 10)**: This section is not applicable for most patients. If you have any questions, please ask our staff.
* **Patient Photo (page 11)**: Take a clear photo of yourself for identification purposes (if required).
