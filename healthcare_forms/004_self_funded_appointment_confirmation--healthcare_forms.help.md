# Self Funded Appointment Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Self Funded Appointment Confirmation form is used to gather information from patients about their self-funded appointments, including payment terms, provider details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate to the form and select "Start Form" to begin.
2.  Fill in the required fields to complete the form.
3.  Review and adjust the fields as needed to suit your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* ****appointment_details** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the details of the appointment, including date, time, and any other relevant information.
* ****payment_terms** (`Select One`, Optional): Select the payment terms for the appointment, such as "Pay in full today" or "Pay over 3 months".
* ****provider_name** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the service.
* ****patient_name** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the name of the patient receiving the service.
* ****contact_number** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the contact number of the patient or their representative.
* ****date_of_service** (`Date`, Optional): Enter the date of the appointment or service.
* ****time_of_service** (`Time`, Optional): Enter the time of the appointment or service.
* ****service_description** (`Text`, Optional): Briefly describe the service or treatment being provided.
* ****payment_method** (`Select One`, Optional): Select the payment method used for the appointment, such as "Cash" or "Credit Card".
* ****note** (`Note`, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* ****email** (`Email`, Optional): Enter the email address of the patient or their representative.
* ****self_funded** (`Select One`, Optional): Select whether the appointment is self-funded or not.
* ****` (Optional): Note: There is no field labeled "**`, please disregard this line.

Tips:
* Make sure to adjust the form according to your specific needs and preferences.
* Use this form for both self-funded and non-self-funded appointments.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
