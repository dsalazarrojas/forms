# Health Certificate Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Certificate Form Template is a tool for healthcare professionals to document essential information about a patient's health status and work capacity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the fields on each page, moving from top to bottom and left to right.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate and clear information for each field.
3.  If a field is marked as "required," you must fill it out.
4.  Use the options for select fields to select the correct answer.
5.  If a field has a hint, use it as a guide to provide more detailed information.
6.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
7.  Sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Medical Institution
*   **Medical Institution**: Enter the name and address of the medical institution where you received your medical treatment.
*   **Patient Info**: Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant patient information.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Select the medical conditions you are experiencing (True or False).
*   **Sick Leave**: Provide a brief description of your medical condition (optional, but recommended for a clear understanding of your situation).
*   **Fitness for Duty**: Indicate whether you are fit for duty (True or False).
*   **Insurance**: Enter your insurance details (if applicable).
*   **Pre-Employment**: Select whether you are pre-employment (True or False).

### Page 2
*   **Occupational Compliance**: Select whether your injury or condition is occupational (True or False) - select multiple if applicable.
*   **Return to Work Certification**: Indicate whether you have a valid return to work certification (True or False).
*   **Medical Certification**: Select whether you have a valid medical certification (True or False).
*   **Signature**: Sign your name, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
*   **Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of signing the form (optional).
*   **Medical Condition Description**: Provide a detailed description of your medical condition (optional).

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions.
*   Proofread your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
