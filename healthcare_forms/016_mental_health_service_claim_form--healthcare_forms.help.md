# mental_health_service_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information related to mental health service claims. It is used to collect data on patients, claimants, and healthcare providers, helping to streamline the claim processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding.
3. Review your entries for accuracy and correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Claim Form** (`claim_form`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you will enter your mental health service claim details.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the patient's details here.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a description of the mental health services you are claiming.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, `text`, required: false): Enter the current status of your claim.
* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your information as the claimant.
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information.
* **Claimant Relationship** (`claimant_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with the claimant.
* **Claimant Claim Date** (`claimant_claim_date`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claimant Claim Type** (`claimant_claim_type`, `text`, required: false): Choose the type of claim you are making.
* **Claimant Claims** (`claimant_claims`, `text`, required: false): List the services or treatments you are claiming.
* **Claimant Total Claim Value** (`claimant_total_claim_value`, `text`, required: false): Enter the total value of your claim.
* **Claimant Claimant Signature** (`claimant_claimant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm your claim.
* **Healthcare Provider Information** (`healthcare_provider_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter details of the healthcare provider who provided the services.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm the patient's information.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm the healthcare provider's information.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Claimant Phone Number** (`claimant_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email.
* **Healthcare Provider License** (`healthcare_provider_license`, `text`, required: false): Enter the healthcare provider's license number.
* **Claimant Date of Birth** (`claimant_dob`, `text`, required: false): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
* **Claimant Claimant Date** (`claimant_claimant_date`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claimant Claimant ID** (`claimant_claimant_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the claimant's ID number.
* **Claimant Claimant Name** (`claimant_claimant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the claimant's name.
* **Claimant Claimant Gender** (`claimant_claimant_gender`, `text`, required: false): Choose the claimant's gender.
* **Tips**

Note: This form is intended to be filled out accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant documentation or seek assistance from a qualified professional.
