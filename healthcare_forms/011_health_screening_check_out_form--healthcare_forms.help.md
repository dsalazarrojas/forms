# Health Screening Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Check Out Form is used to collect patient contact information and ensure a smooth transition out of the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name as it is currently spelled.
2. Enter the date you were discharged from the hospital.
3. Enter your home phone number.
4. Enter your email address (if applicable).
5. Enter your current address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it is currently spelled.
* **Date of Discharge**: Enter the date you were discharged from the hospital.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your home phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Address**: Enter your current home address.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your name as it is currently spelled.
- If you have an email, please enter it to stay in touch with us.
- If you need assistance with filling out this form, please don't hesitate to ask.
