# Employee Daily Health Screening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees on their daily health status and other relevant contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Temperature** in the first field, entering your current body temperature.
2. Describe any **Symptoms** you may be experiencing in the second field.
3. Select your current **Health Status** in the third field:
	* **Fever**
	* **Normal**
	* **Sick**
4. For **Medication**, select all medications you are currently taking from the fourth field:
	* **Aspirin**
	* **Ibuprofen**
	* **None**
5. Enter your **Contact with Doctor** date if you have been in contact with a doctor in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Contact with Nurse** date if you have been in contact with a nurse in the sixth field.
7. Enter your **Last Health Screening** date if you have completed a previous health screening in the seventh field.
8. Enter your **Employee ID** in the eighth field.
9. Enter your name as your **Employee Name** in the ninth field.
10. Enter your department as your **Department** in the tenth field.
11. Select your shift as your **Shift** in the eleventh field.
12. Enter your location as your **Location** in the twelfth field.
13. Select your preferred **Contact Information** method in the thirteenth field:
	* **Mobile**
	* **Email**
	* **Both**
14. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourteenth field.
15. Enter your **Email** address in the fifteenth field.
16. Enter your **Emergency Contact** person's name in the sixteenth field.
17. Enter your **Emergency Contact Number** in the seventeenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Temperature** (1): Enter your current body temperature.
* **Symptoms** (2): Describe any symptoms you may be experiencing.
* **Health Status** (3): Select your current health status.
	+ **Fever**: You have a fever or are experiencing flu-like symptoms.
	+ **Normal**: You are healthy with no symptoms.
	+ **Sick**: You are experiencing symptoms of being sick.
* **Medication** (4): Select all medications you are currently taking.
	+ **Aspirin**: You are taking Aspirin.
	+ **Ibuprofen**: You are taking Ibuprofen.
	+ **None**: You are not taking any medications.
* **Contact with Doctor** (5): Enter the date you contacted a doctor.
* **Contact with Nurse** (6): Enter the date you contacted a nurse.
* **Last Health Screening** (7): Enter the date of your last health screening.
* **Employee ID** (8): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (9): Enter your name as your employee name.
* **Department** (10): Enter your department name.
* **Shift** (11): Select your shift schedule.
* **Location** (12): Enter your location.
* **Contact Information** (13): Select your preferred contact method.
	+ **Mobile**: You prefer to be contacted by mobile phone.
	+ **Email**: You prefer to be contacted by email.
	+ **Both**: You are open to being contacted by either mobile or email.
* **Phone Number** (14): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (15): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (16): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (17): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
