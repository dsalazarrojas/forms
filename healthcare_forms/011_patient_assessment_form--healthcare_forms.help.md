# Patient Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient data for assessment and management in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
6.  Describe the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
7.  List the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
8.  Select the healthcare providers who have seen the patient in the "Doctors" field (Select Multiple).
9.  Enter the length of hospitalization in the "Hospitalization Length" field.
10.  Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown) in the "Patient Status" field (Select Multiple).
11.  Describe the patient's diagnosis in the "Diagnosis" field.
12.  List the patient's medication in the "Medication" field.
13.  List the patient's allergies in the "Allergies" field.
14.  Describe the patient's medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
15.  Describe the patient's treatment history in the "Treatment History" field.
16.  Describe the patient's treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
17.  Enter the date of the next appointment in the "Next Appointment" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
18.  Enter any additional notes about the patient in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's first name.
*   **Last Name (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's last name.
*   **Date of Birth (date, optional)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Email (email, optional)**: Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Medical History (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's medical history.
*   **Symptoms (text, optional)**: List the patient's symptoms.
*   **Doctors (Select Multiple, optional)**: Select the healthcare providers who have seen the patient.
*   **Hospitalization Length (number, optional)**: Enter the length of hospitalization.
*   **Patient Status (Select Multiple, optional)**: Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
*   **Diagnosis (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's diagnosis.
*   **Medication (text, optional)**: List the patient's medication.
*   **Allergies (text, optional)**: List the patient's allergies.
*   **Medical Conditions (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's medical conditions.
*   **Treatment History (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's treatment history.
*   **Treatment Plan (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's treatment plan.
*   **Next Appointment (date, optional)**: Enter the date of the next appointment in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Notes (note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.
*   **Doctors (Select One, optional)**: Select the healthcare provider who is currently treating the patient.
