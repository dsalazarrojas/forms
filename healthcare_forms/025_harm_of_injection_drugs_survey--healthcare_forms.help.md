# Harm Of Injection Drugs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Harm Of Injection Drugs Survey" form is designed to collect information about the harm caused by injection drug use. This form is intended for healthcare professionals or individuals with a background in social sciences to gather data for research or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about injection drug use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can provide any background information or context about your experience with injection drug use.
* **Do you know someone who uses injection drugs**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your situation.
* **How do you feel about the risks of injection drug use**: This question is for you to describe your feelings about the risks associated with injection drug use.
* **How likely is it that someone you know will use injection drugs**: This question is a number field where you can select a number between 0 and 10 to indicate how likely you think someone you know will use injection drugs.
* **What do you think are the most common ways people get injection drugs**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **How often is it used in the general public**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **How do you think injection drugs impact the community**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **How do you think injection drugs impact the individual**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **What do you think are the biggest risks associated with injection drug use**: This question is a text field where you can provide any information about the risks associated with injection drug use.
* **Do you feel that injection drug use is a serious health risk**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **How likely is it that you will report someone you know who uses injection drugs**: This question is a multiple-choice question with three options: Yes, No, and Other. Please select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **Do you have any other comments about injection drug use**: This question is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about injection drug use.

This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals or individuals with a background in social sciences. If you're not sure about any of the questions or options, please refer to the help guide for further clarification.
