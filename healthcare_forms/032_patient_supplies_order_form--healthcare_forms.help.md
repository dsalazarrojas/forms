# Patient Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers order patient supplies for medical treatment. It collects patient information and supplies ordering details for efficient and accurate billing and inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's general information in the "Patient Information" section.
2.  Order the required supplies for the patient in the "Patient Supplies" section. Select all relevant options from the dropdown list.
3.  Enter the costs associated with the service and material supplies in the respective fields.
4.  Review and update the form as needed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (Page 1)
*   **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and ID number.
*   **Patient Information 1**: Enter any additional patient information as needed.

### Patient Supplies (Page 2)
*   **Patient Supplies**: Select the required supplies for the patient from the dropdown list. The options are Syrup, Saline, and Painkiller.

### Costs (Page 3)
*   **Cost**: Enter the cost associated with the service supplies.
*   **Service Cost 1**: Enter the cost associated with the first service supply.
*   **Service 2**: Enter the cost associated with the second service supply.
*   **Service 3**: Enter the cost associated with the third service supply.
*   **Service 4**: Enter the cost associated with the fourth service supply.
*   **Material 1 Cost**: Enter the cost associated with the first material supply.
*   **Material 2 Cost**: Enter the cost associated with the second material supply.
*   **Material 3 Cost**: Enter the cost associated with the third material supply.
