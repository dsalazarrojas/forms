# Nursing Home Care Giver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect job application information from individuals interested in working as a nurse in a nursing home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If applicable, enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your employment start date in the "Employment Start Date" field.
6. Enter your degree earned in the "Degree" field.
7. Enter the name of the institution where you earned your degree in the "Education Institution" field.
8. Enter the name of the course or program you completed in the "Course Name" field.
9. Enter the name of a professional reference in the "Reference Name" field.
10. Select the relationship between you and your reference in the "Reference Relationship" field (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Colleague).
11. Enter your reference's contact information in the "Reference Contact Information" field.
12. Enter your print name in the "Print Name" field.
13. Enter your print email address in the "Print Email" field.
14. Enter your print phone number in the "Print Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your personal or professional email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your personal or mobile phone number.
* **Job Title** (Text, optional): If you have a specific job title, enter it here (e.g., Registered Nurse, CNA, etc.).
* **Employer** (Text, required): Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Employment Start Date** (Date, required): Enter the date you started your employment in your current or previous position.
* **Degree** (Text, required): Enter the degree you have earned (e.g., Associate's, Bachelor's, Master's, etc.).
* **Education Institution** (Text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Course Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the course or program you completed.
* **Reference Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of your professional reference.
* **Reference Relationship** (Select One, required): Select the relationship between you and your reference (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Colleague).
* **Reference Contact Information** (Text, required): Enter your reference's contact information (e.g., email, phone number, address, etc.).
* **Print Name** (Text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **Print Email** (Email, required): Enter your print email address.
* **Print Phone** (Text, required): Enter your print phone number.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
