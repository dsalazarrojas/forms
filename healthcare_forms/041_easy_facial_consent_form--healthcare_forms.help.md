# Easy Facials Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Easy Facials Consent Form" is a user-facing form designed to collect information about a user's experiences with various beauty services. This form aims to gather consent from users for these services and provide a space for notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions regarding the services you have visited or will visit.
2. Select the services you have used or would like to use from the provided options.
3. Enter any additional notes you would like to share about your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (user_input, text, optional): This field is intended for users to provide any additional information they would like to share about themselves.
  - 
- **Salons Visited** (salons_visited, select_multiple, optional): This field allows users to select which salons they have visited. Please choose 'Yes' to indicate you have visited the salon, or 'No' if you haven't.
  - 
- **Salons Visited 2** (salons_visited_2, select_multiple, optional): This field is similar to "Salons Visited", but is used to gather information about visiting a second salon.
  - 
- **Services** (services, select_one, optional): This field allows users to select which beauty services they have used or would like to use. Please choose 'Yes' to indicate you have used the service, or 'No' if you haven't.
  - 
- **Hair Cut** (hair_cut, select_one, optional): Similar to "Services", this field is used to gather information about hair cuts.
  - 
- **Hair Coloring** (hair_coloring, select_one, optional): This field is used to gather information about hair coloring services.
  - 
- **Make Up** (makeup, select_one, optional): This field is used to gather information about makeup services.
  - 
- **Nails** (nails, select_one, optional): This field is used to gather information about nail services.
  - 
- **Esthetics** (esthetics, select_one, optional): This field is used to gather information about esthetic services.
  - 
- **Other Services** (other_services, select_one, optional): This field is used to gather information about other beauty services not listed above.
  - 
- **Consent** (consent, text, optional): This field is for users to enter their consent for the services they have selected.
  - 
- **Notes** (notes, note, optional): This field is a space for users to add any additional notes or comments about their experiences.
  - 
- **Salons Visited 3** (salons_visited_3, select_multiple, optional): This field is similar to "Salons Visited" and "Salons Visited 2", used to gather information about visiting a third salon.
