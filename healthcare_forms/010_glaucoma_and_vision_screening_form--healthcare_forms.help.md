# Glaucoma And Vision Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Glaucoma And Vision Screening Form is a tool used to capture essential information related to glaucoma and vision screening. This form is designed to guide healthcare professionals in the process of documenting and tracking patients' data after a glaucoma test is conducted. It helps in maintaining accurate and standardized records, facilitating better patient care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the "Screening Details" page (Page 1), which requires minimal information and serves as an introduction to the form.
2. Move on to the "Glaucoma Test Results" page (Page 2), where you will be presented with multiple options. Select all applicable results that apply to the patient.
3. On the "Follow Up Planning" page (Page 3), take a moment to note any necessary actions or recommendations for the patient's follow-up care. This field is a note-taking section for any specific next steps or advice given to the patient.
4. The "Patient Contact" and "Doctor Contact" pages (Pages 4 and 5, respectively) are for capturing additional details about the patient's contact information, which may be useful in the screening process or follow-up.
5. "Screening Outcome" on Page 6 is to determine the outcome of the glaucoma test; you may choose from the options to indicate the status.
6. "Next Steps" on Page 7 is another note-taking section similar to "Follow Up Planning," where any further recommendations or actions can be noted.
7. "Other Comments" on Page 8 is for any additional comments or information that might not fit into the other categories.
8. Finally, "Assigned Tool" on Page 9, select the tool or equipment used during the screening process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screening Details** (`screening_details`, text, optional): This page serves as an introduction and is a good place to start your form completion. It's essentially a text field for capturing any relevant details about the screening process.
* **Glaucoma Test Results** (`glaucoma_test_results`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the results that apply from the given options for the patient.
* **Follow Up Planning** (`follow_up_planning`, note, optional): Note down any necessary follow-up actions or recommendations for the patient's care.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): If relevant, add the patient’s contact information here.
* **Doctor Contact** (`doctor_contact`, text, optional): Add the doctor’s contact information here, if applicable.
* **Screening Outcome** (`screening_outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose the outcome of the glaucoma test from the given options.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, optional): Another note field for any further recommendations or actions needed.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments that might be useful for further reference or discussion.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool or equipment used during the screening process.

## Tips

- Be sure to fill out all required fields, especially the "Screening Details" and "Glaucoma Test Results" sections, as they play a crucial role in accurately capturing the screening data and outcomes.
- For fields like "Follow Up Planning," "Next Steps," and "Other Comments," use clear and concise language to ensure that the information is easily understandable by others reviewing the form.
- If you are unsure about any field or option, refer to the provided instructions or consult a medical professional for clarification.
- Ensure that the information you input is accurate and relevant to the patient's care.
