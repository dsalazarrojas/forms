# medical_record_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical record communication form is a tool for healthcare providers to collect and record relevant information about a patient's medical record. This information is used to ensure effective communication between the provider and the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth (Dob) in the "Patient Dob" field.
3. Enter the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact Info" field.
4. Select the method of communication you prefer with the patient in the "Communication Method" field. Options include Phone, Email, Fax, or Other.
5. Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility in the "Provider Name" field.
6. Enter the name of the department or location where the patient is receiving care in the "Provider Department" field.
7. Enter the ID number of the patient's medical record in the "Medical Record ID" field.
8. If applicable, enter the patient's health insurance information in the "Patient Health Insurance" field.
9. Enter any relevant medical notes or comments in the "Medical Notes" field.
10. Sign and date the form as the healthcare provider in the "Provider Signature" field.
11. If the patient is present, have them sign and date the form in the "Patient Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is a note field for any additional information or instructions.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient being referred to.
* **Patient Dob**: Enter the patient's date of birth (MM/DD/YY).
* **Patient Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or facility.
* **Communication Method**: Select the method you prefer to communicate with the patient (Phone, Email, Fax, or Other).
* **Provider Facility**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider's facility or location.
* **Communication Frequency**: Enter the frequency of communication you plan with the patient (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Provider Department**: Enter the department or location where the patient is receiving care.
* **Medical Record ID**: Enter the ID number of the patient's medical record.
* **Patient Health Insurance**: If applicable, enter the patient's health insurance information.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Provider Signature**: Sign and date as the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Signature**: If the patient is present, sign and date as the patient.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Use the same communication method and frequency selected in the "Communication Method" field.
* Ensure the patient's contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Review and update the form as necessary to ensure it accurately reflects the patient's medical record.
* Store the completed form according to your organization's policies and procedures.
