# Dental Clearance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dental Clearance Form is designed to collect essential information from patients for dental clearance purposes. This form is meant to be used in a healthcare setting, such as a dental clinic or office, to gather necessary details for patient safety and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with the patient's name and contact information.
2.  Provide a brief medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
3.  Specify the patient's dentist and dentist's information (if applicable).
4.  Choose the type of dental clearance form that is relevant to the patient's visit.
5.  Identify the patient's insurance provider (if applicable).
6.  Provide a time for the patient's visit (if applicable).
