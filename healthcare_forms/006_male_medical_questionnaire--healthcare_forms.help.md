# Male Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information from male patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the personal info section with your basic demographic information.
2. Select your gender from the options provided.
3. If you have any medical history, please describe it in the text area provided.
4. If you have any allergies, select all that apply from the options provided.
5. Enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
6. If you have any existing medical conditions, select all that apply from the options provided.
7. Enter any medication you are currently taking.
8. Enter the date of your last medical check-up or health assessment.
9. If you have a medical condition, select it from the options provided.
10. Enter your blood pressure reading (if applicable).
11. Enter your height measurement.
12. Enter your weight measurement.
13. Enter your body mass index (BMI) measurement.
14. Enter your head circumference measurement.
15. Enter your neck circumference measurement.
16. Enter your waist circumference measurement.
17. Enter your hip circumference measurement.
18. Enter your body length measurement (in the selected units).
19. Enter your body mass measurement (in the selected units).
20. Enter your blood pressure reading (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **personal_info** (text, optional): Enter your basic demographic information.
- **gender** (select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
- **medical_history** (text, optional): If you have any medical history, please describe it in this text area.
- **medical_history_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your last medical check-up or health assessment.
- **allergies** (select_multiple, optional): Select all allergies that apply from the options provided.
- **contact_info** (text, optional): Enter your contact information for emergency purposes.
- **medical_conditions** (select_multiple, optional): Select all existing medical conditions that apply from the options provided.
- **medication** (text, optional): Enter any medication you are currently taking.
- **emergency_contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
- **blood_pressure** (number, optional): Enter your blood pressure reading (if applicable).
- **body_mass_index** (number, optional): Enter your body mass index (BMI) measurement.
- **height** (number, optional): Enter your height measurement.
- **weight** (number, optional): Enter your weight measurement.
- **head_circumference** (number, optional): Enter your head circumference measurement.
- **waist_circumference** (number, optional): Enter your waist circumference measurement.
- **hip_circumference** (number, optional): Enter your hip circumference measurement.
- **body_length** (number, optional): Enter your body length measurement.
- **body_mass** (number, optional): Enter your body mass measurement.
- **blood_pressure_units** (select_one, optional): Select the units for your blood pressure reading.
- **body_mass_units** (select_one, optional): Select the units for your body mass measurement.
- **neck_circumference** (number, optional): Enter your neck circumference measurement.
- **hip_circumference** (number, optional): Enter your hip circumference measurement.
- **height_units** (select_one, optional): Select the units for your height measurement.
- **blood_pressure** (number, optional): Enter your blood pressure reading.
- **body_length_units** (select_one, optional): Select the units for your body length measurement.
