# COVID 19 Vaccination Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather essential information from patients before their visit to the COVID 19 vaccination clinic. It helps the clinic to understand the patient's medical history, preferences, and contact details for a smooth and safe vaccination experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Page 1: Registration Date, Patient Information**
	* Fill out the **Registration Date** field with the date of your registration.
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**, as per your legal name.
	* Provide your **Date of Birth** in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
2. **Page 2: Demographics**
	* Select your **Gender** from the options provided.
	* Choose your **Race** and **Ethnicity** from the options for demographic reporting.
3. **Page 3: Contact Information**
	* Enter your **Phone Number**, which is your contact number for any updates or changes.
	* Provide your **Email Address**, which will be used for clinic communications.
	* Enter your **Home Address**, including street address.
4. **Page 4: Insurance and Healthcare Worker Information**
	* Select if you have **Health Insurance** and provide the **Insurance Provider** and **Insurance Policy Number** if applicable.
	* Check if you are a **Healthcare Worker** or not.
5. **Page 5: Occupation and Health Status**
	* Enter your **Occupation** if you wish to share this information.
	* Check if you have any **Underlying Medical Conditions** that may impact vaccination; choose "None" if you don't have any.
6. **Page 6: Infection History and Vaccination Preferences**
	* Confirm if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19 and when.
	* Select your preferred **Vaccine Brand** if you have a preference.
7. **Page 7: Additional Information**
	* List your **Current Medications** if you are taking any.
	* Check if you have any **Medication Allergies**.
8. **Page 8: Clinic Preferences**
	* Choose your preferred **Clinic Date** and **Clinic Time** if available.
	* Enter your alternative **Clinic Date** and **Clinic Time** if you prefer not to attend the first choice.
9. **Page 9: Accessibility and Transportation**
	* Check if you need transportation assistance or any **Accessibility Accommodations**.
10. **Page 10: Emergency Contact and Consent**
	* Provide your **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone Number**.
	* Confirm that you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine and share your information with public health authorities.
	* Acknowledge receipt of the HIPAA Privacy Notice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (1): Date of registration for the clinic.
* **First Name** (2): Legal first name of the patient.
* **Last Name** (3): Legal last name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (5): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Race** (6): Select your race for demographic reporting.
* **Ethnicity** (7): Select your ethnicity for demographic reporting.
* **Phone Number** (8): Contact number for updates and changes.
* **Email Address** (9): Contact email for clinic communications.
* **Home Address** (10): Street address for clinic records.
* **City** (11): City of residence.
* **State** (12): State of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (13): Postal code.
* **Preferred Language** (14): Language for clinic communications.
* **Interpreter Needed** (15): If you need an interpreter for communication.
* **Health Insurance** (16): If you have health insurance coverage.
* **Insurance Provider** (17): If you have health insurance, provide the name of your provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (18): If you have health insurance, provide your policy ID number.
* **Healthcare Worker** (19): Confirm if you are a healthcare worker.
* **Essential Worker** (20): Confirm if you are an essential worker.
* **Occupation** (21): Job or profession for your work.
* **Underlying Conditions** (22): Confirm if you have any underlying medical conditions.
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection** (23): Confirm if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Previous Infection Date** (24): Date you tested positive if previously infected.
* **Current Medications** (25): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medication Allergies** (26): If you have any medication allergies.
* **Preferred Clinic Date** (27): First-choice clinic date for vaccination.
* **Preferred Clinic Time** (28): First-choice clinic time for vaccination.
* **Alternative Clinic Date** (29): Second-choice clinic date for vaccination.
* **Alternative Clinic Time** (30): Second-choice clinic time for vaccination.
* **Vaccine Brand Preference** (31): If you have a preference for the vaccine brand.
* **Transportation Needs** (32): If you need transportation assistance for your visit.
* **Accessibility Needs** (33): If you need any accessibility accommodations.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (34): Name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (35): Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Consent to Vaccination** (36): Confirm your consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Data Sharing Consent** (37): Confirm your consent to share your information with public health authorities.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment** (38): Confirm receipt of the HIPAA Privacy Notice.
