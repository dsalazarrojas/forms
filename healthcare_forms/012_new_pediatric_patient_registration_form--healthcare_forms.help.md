# new_pediatric_patient_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "new_pediatric_patient_registration_form" collects demographic and medical information for new pediatric patients. This form is optional and supplementary in nature, ensuring that patients' or their families can provide additional information beyond the mandatory registration requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "New Patient" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient** (`new_pediatric_patient_registration_form_page_1`, page, optional): This field is the title for the form.
* **New Patient Name** (`new_pediatric_patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **New Patient Birthdate** (`new_pediatric_patient_birthdate`, date, optional): Enter the patient's birthdate.
* **New Patient Address** (`new_pediatric_patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's home or contact address.
* **New Patient Phone** (`new_pediatric_patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **New Patient Email** (`new_pediatric_patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **New Primary Diagnosis** (`new_primary_diagnosis`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's primary diagnosis from the options provided. This field is optional as it might be already captured in other medical records.
* **New Allergies** (`new_pediatric_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any allergies the patient has from the options provided.
* **New Medication** (`new_pediatric_medication`, text, optional): Enter any current or ongoing medications the patient is taking.
* **New Vaccinations** (`new_pediatric_vaccinations`, text, optional): Enter any recent vaccinations the patient has received.

## Tips

* If you are unsure of any field, contact the relevant healthcare team for guidance.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you need to make multiple submissions for a single patient, please ensure each submission is complete and accurate to avoid data duplication.
