# Ambulance Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ambulance Booking Form is a comprehensive tool designed to gather essential information for medical transportation services. It ensures that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently to provide the best care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of booking (Emergency, Urgent, Scheduled/Non-Emergency, Inter-Facility Transfer, Hospital Discharge, Event Standby)
2. Enter the patient's first and last name
3. Enter the patient's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format)
4. Select the patient's gender
5. Enter the patient's weight and height (in pounds and inches, respectively)
6. Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address
7. Select the relationship between the contact person and the patient
8. Select the pickup date and time
9. Enter the pickup location name and address
10. Enter the destination location name and address
11. Select the level of care required
12. Indicate whether the patient's vital signs are stable
13. Indicate whether oxygen is required during transport
14. Select any medical equipment needed
15. List any medications to be administered during transport
16. Enter the number of attendants accompanying the patient
17. List the names of the attendants
18. Select the payment type
19. Enter the insurance company, policy number, and authorization number (if applicable)
20. Enter any special requirements or instructions
21. Select the language preference
22. Enter any additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Type (1)**: Select the type of booking (e.g., Emergency, Urgent, Scheduled/Non-Emergency).
* **Patient First Name** (4): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (5): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (6): Enter the patient's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Patient Gender** (7): Select the patient's gender.
* **Patient Weight** (8): Enter the patient's weight (in pounds).
* **Patient Height** (9): Enter the patient's height (in inches).
* **Requester Name** (11): Enter the name of the person making the booking.
* **Requester Relationship** (12): Select the relationship between the requester and the patient.
* **Requester Phone** (13): Enter the requester's phone number.
* **Requester Email** (14): Enter the requester's email address.
* **Pickup Date** (16): Enter the date of pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (17): Enter the time of pickup.
* **Pickup Location Name** (18): Enter the name of the pickup location.
* **Pickup Address** (19): Enter the address of the pickup location.
* **Pickup City** (20): Enter the city of the pickup location.
* **Pickup State** (21): Enter the state of the pickup location.
* **Pickup ZIP Code** (22): Enter the ZIP code of the pickup location.
* **Destination Location Name** (26): Enter the name of the destination location.
* **Destination Address** (27): Enter the address of the destination location.
* **Destination City** (28): Enter the city of the destination location.
* **Destination State** (29): Enter the state of the destination location.
* **Destination ZIP Code** (30): Enter the ZIP code of the destination location.
* **Patient Mobility Status** (34): Select the patient's mobility status.
* **Level of Care Required** (35): Select the level of care required.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (36): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
* **Vital Signs Stable** (37): Indicate whether the patient's vital signs are stable.
* **Oxygen Required** (38): Indicate whether oxygen is required during transport.
* **Equipment Needed** (39): Select any medical equipment needed.
* **Medications to be Administered** (40): List any medications to be administered during transport.
* **Attendants Count** (42): Enter the number of attendants accompanying the patient.
* **Attendant Names** (43): List the names of the attendants.
* **Payment Type** (45): Select the payment type.
* **Insurance Company** (46): Enter the insurance company (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Number** (47): Enter the insurance policy number (if applicable).
* **Special Requirements or Instructions** (50): Enter any special requirements or instructions.
* **Language Preference** (51): Select the language preference.
* **Additional Comments** (53): Enter any additional comments.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requirements or instructions, please provide a clear and concise description.
