# Anger Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anger Management Evaluation Form is designed to provide healthcare professionals with a comprehensive understanding of a client's anger management issues. This form assesses the client's anger frequency, triggers, and prior treatment history to aid in effective evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Provide Client Information**
    - Enter the client's full name.
    - Describe the reason for referral.

2.  **Evaluate Anger Issues**
    - Choose the frequency of anger episodes (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
    - Identify common triggers (List typical triggers).
    - Provide any prior treatment history (e.g., counseling or therapy).
    - Assess the risk level of the anger issues (Low, Medium, High).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

###  **Client Name** 
**Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter the client's full name.

###  **Reason for Referral** 
**Reason for referral** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Provide a brief description of why the client was referred to this form.

###  **Frequency of Anger Episodes** 
**Frequency of anger episodes** (`frequency_of_anger`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
    *   Daily
    *   Weekly
    *   Monthly
    *   Rarely

###  **Common Triggers** 
**Common triggers** (`triggers`, text, optional): List typical triggers or factors that cause the client's anger episodes.

###  **Prior Treatment History** 
**Prior treatment history** (`prior_treatment`, text, optional): Describe any prior counseling or therapy the client has received.

###  **Risk Assessment Result** 
**Risk assessment result** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High

###  **Evaluator Notes** 
**Evaluator notes** (`evaluator_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for the clinician's use.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when providing client information and evaluating anger issues.
*   Ensure to select the most accurate frequency of anger episodes and risk assessment.
*   If the client has a prior treatment history, describe it in as much detail as possible.
*   Provide clear and concise evaluator notes for the clinician's reference.
