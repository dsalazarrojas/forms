# Suturing Lab Skill Check Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and evaluate a healthcare worker's suturing lab skills. It helps assess a patient's procedure and the worker's skill level and confidence in their abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Name**: Please provide the name of the patient who underwent the suturing procedure.
2. **Enter Procedure**: Briefly describe the suturing procedure completed.
3. **Select Skill Level**: Choose the level of skill demonstrated during the procedure. You can select from "Intermediate," "Advanced," or "Proficient."
4. **Select Skill Confidence**: Mark the level of confidence you have in your skills. You can choose from "Confident," "Inconclusive," or "None."
5. **Date Completed**: Enter the date the procedure was completed.
6. **Time Completed**: Enter the time the procedure was completed.
7. **Location**: Briefly describe the location where the procedure was completed.
8. **Completed By**: Please enter the name of the healthcare worker who completed the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient who underwent the procedure.
* **Procedure** (`procedure`, text, optional): Briefly describe the suturing procedure completed.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, optional): Choose from "Intermediate," "Advanced," or "Proficient" to indicate the level of skill demonstrated during the procedure.
* **Skill Confidence** (`skill_confident`, select_multiple, optional): Mark your confidence level in your skills from "Confident," "Inconclusive," or "None."
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the procedure was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the procedure was completed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Briefly describe the location where the procedure was completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the healthcare worker who completed the procedure.
