# Smile Assessment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information about a patient's smile assessment and treatment plan, which includes their dental and medical history, contact details, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: Fill out the basic details of the patient, including their name, contact info, and address.
2. **Patient Contact Info**: Fill out the patient's contact information, such as their phone number and email address.
3. **Patient Medical Info**: Provide any relevant medical information about the patient.
4. **Smile Assessment**: Answer the questions about the patient's smile assessment.
5. **Dental History**: Answer the questions about the patient's dental history.
6. **Medical History**: Answer the questions about the patient's medical history.
7. **Dental History Questions**: Answer any additional dental history questions.
8. **Smile Assessment Questions**: Answer any additional smile assessment questions.
9. **Smile Assessment Results**: Record the results of the smile assessment.
10. **Treatment Plan**: Record the patient's treatment plan.
11. **Treatment Plan Questions**: Answer any additional treatment questions.
12. **Dental Record**: Record the patient's dental record.
13. **Patient Notes**: Record any notes about the patient.
14. **Dentist Signature**: The dentist must sign this field to confirm they have read and reviewed the form.
15. **Dentist Comments**: Record any comments or notes about the patient.
16. **Patient Signature**: The patient must sign this field to confirm they have read and reviewed the form.
17. **Patient Comments**: Record any comments or notes from the patient.
18. **Insurance Info**: Record the patient's insurance information.
19. **Billing Info**: Record the billing information for the patient.
20. **Billing Address**: Record the billing address for the patient.
21. **Patient Address**: Record the patient's address.
22. **Patient Phone**: Record the patient's phone number.
23. **Patient Email**: Record the patient's email address.
24. **Dental Clinics**: Record the dental clinics information.
25. **Patient Name**: Record the patient's name.
