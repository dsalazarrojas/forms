# Physical Rehabilitation Center Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare professionals to assess a patient's physical rehabilitation needs and create a personalized plan for their recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's general information, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide any necessary contact information about the patient, such as their address and phone number.
3. Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
4. Select any applicable medical conditions from the provided options.
5. List any medications the patient is currently taking.
6. Enter the details of the patient's rehabilitation plan, including any specific goals or targets.
7. Select a follow-up date for the patient's progress to be reviewed.
8. Provide any insurance information, such as policy numbers or claims addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the patient's general information, such as their name, address, and contact details.

### Patient Contact
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter any necessary contact information about the patient, such as their phone number or email address.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any applicable medical conditions from the provided options that apply to the patient.

### Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking.

### Rehabilitation Plan
* **Rehabilitation Plan** (`rehabilitation_plan`, `number`, required: false): Enter the details of the patient's rehabilitation plan, including any specific goals or targets.

### Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required: false): Select a follow-up date for the patient's progress to be reviewed.

### Insurance
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, required: false): Enter any insurance information, such as policy numbers or claims addresses.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare team for assistance.
* Ensure that each patient's information is collected only once.
* Review and update the form as necessary to reflect any changes in the patient's status.
