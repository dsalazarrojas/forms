# Sapphire Brow Clinic PMU - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for scheduling and tracking PMU (Permanent Makeup Usher) appointments at Sapphire Brow Clinic. It collects patient contact information and details for PMU services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your name and contact email and phone number.
3. Select the PMU technician who will perform the service.
4. Select the date and time for the PMU appointment.
5. Add any additional treatment details in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **PMU Technician** (`pmu_technician`, select_one, required): Select the technician who will perform your PMU service.
- **PMU Date** (`pmu_date`, date, required): Select the date for your PMU appointment.
- **PMU Time** (`pmu_time`, time, required): Select the time for your PMU appointment.
- **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your PMU treatment.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
- If you need to schedule multiple appointments, repeat the form for each appointment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the clinic directly.
