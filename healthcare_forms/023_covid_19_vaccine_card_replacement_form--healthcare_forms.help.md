# COVID 19 Vaccine Card Replacement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a replacement for your COVID-19 vaccination card. Please fill out the form below to provide information about your vaccination history and preferences for receiving your replacement card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Enter your full name, date of birth, and email address (for replacement card delivery).
* Provide your mailing address for the replacement card to be sent to.
* Select your reason for requesting a replacement card (e.g. lost, damaged, etc.).
* Enter the approximate date you lost or damaged your original card.
* Choose the type of vaccine you received.
* Enter the dates of your first and second doses (if applicable).
* If you received a booster dose, enter its date.
* Enter the location where you received each dose (if known).
* Provide any additional information about your vaccination history (if necessary).
* Select your status regarding full or partial vaccination.
* If you were vaccinated at a provider or clinic, enter their information.
* Select your preferred method of receiving the replacement card (e.g. mail, email, pickup).
* Check the box indicating this is an urgent request (if necessary).
* Verify that the vaccination information provided is accurate.
* Confirm your consent to release your vaccination records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1): As it appears on vaccination record
	+ Enter your full name as it is listed on your vaccination record.
* **Date of Birth** (2): 
	+ Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (3): For delivery of replacement card
	+ Enter the email address where you want the replacement card to be sent.
* **Phone Number** (4): 
	+ Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Mailing Address** (5): 
	+ Enter your home address where you want the replacement card to be mailed.
* **Street Address** (6): 
	+ Enter the street address associated with your home address.
* **City** (7): 
	+ Enter the city associated with your home address.
* **State** (8): 
	+ Enter the state associated with your home address.
* **Zip Code** (9): 
	+ Enter the zip code associated with your home address.
* **Original Vaccination Card Information** (10): 
	+ This section is for additional information about your original vaccination card.
* **Reason for Card Replacement** (11): 
	+ Select the reason you are requesting a replacement card (e.g. lost, damaged, etc.).
* **Approximate Date Card Was Lost or Damaged** (12): 
	+ Enter the approximate date you lost or damaged your original card.
* **Vaccine Type Received** (13): 
	+ Select the type of COVID-19 vaccine you received (e.g. Pfizer, Moderna, etc.).
* **Date of First Dose** (14): 
	+ Enter the date of your first dose of the vaccine.
* **Location of First Dose** (15): 
	+ Enter the location where you received your first dose.
* **Date of Second Dose** (16): 
	+ Enter the date of your second dose.
* **Location of Second Dose** (17): 
	+ Enter the location where you received your second dose.
* **Date of Booster Dose** (18): 
	+ Enter the date of your booster dose.
* **Location of Booster Dose** (19): 
	+ Enter the location where you received your booster dose.
* **Batch Numbers - If you remember them** (20): 
	+ If you remember your batch numbers, enter them here.
* **Vaccination History Summary** (21): 
	+ Provide any additional information about your vaccination history (if necessary).
* **Status** (22): 
	+ Select your status regarding full or partial vaccination.
* **Primary Vaccinating Provider or Clinic** (23): 
	+ Enter the information of your primary vaccinating provider or clinic.
* **Provider Contact Information** (24): 
	+ Enter any contact information for your provider (if known).
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (26): 
	+ Select how you want to receive your replacement card (e.g. mail, email, pickup).
* **This is an urgent request** (27): 
	+ Check this box if this is an urgent request.
* **I verify the vaccination information is accurate** (29): 
	+ Verify that the vaccination information provided is accurate.
* **I consent to release my vaccination records** (30): 
	+ Confirm your consent to release your vaccination records.
