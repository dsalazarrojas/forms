# Gain Servicing Outbound Lead - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about gain servicing outbounds, which occur when an out-of-network provider or facility offers care or services to a member of another health plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the lead name, the name of the first contact, and the name of the person in charge.
3. Enter the phone number and email of the lead if available.
4. Enter the name of the person served, their first name, and last name.
5. Enter the date and time of the service.
6. Add any additional notes about the interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead name**: Enter the name of the out-of-network provider or facility that offered services to the member.
* **Name of first contact**: Enter the name of the person who first contacted you about the lead.
* **Name of person in charge**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the out-of-network provider or facility.
* **Phone number**: Enter the phone number of the lead if available.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the lead if available.
* **Name of person served**: Enter the name of the person who received services from the out-of-network provider or facility.
* **First name of person served**: Enter the first name of the person served.
* **Last name of person served**: Enter the last name of the person served.
* **Date of service**: Enter the date of the service.
* **Time of service**: Enter the time of the service.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the interaction, such as the details of the services provided and the outcome of the interaction.
