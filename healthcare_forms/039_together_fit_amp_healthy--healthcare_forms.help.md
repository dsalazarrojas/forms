# TOGETHER FIT & HEALTHY - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "TOGETHER FIT & HEALTHY" form is designed to gather information about your fitness goals, health status, and medical conditions. This form will help our healthcare professionals to create a personalized plan tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the goals you want to achieve through this program.
3. Answer the questions about your fitness level, current weight, and available time for exercise.
4. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. If applicable, report any medical conditions, emergency contacts, and medications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What brings you here?** (`what_brings_you_here`, Text, Required)
	+ This is the title of the form, and you can enter any information that describes why you are seeking a personalized fitness plan.
* **What are your goals?** (`what_are_your_goals`, Select Multiple, Required)
	+ Choose one or more goals you want to achieve through this program. Options are:
		- Lose weight
		- Increase fitness
		- Reduce stress
		- Improve health
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your current fitness level, with 1 being the lowest level and 7 being the highest.
* **Current Weight (kg)** (`current_weight_kg`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your current weight in kilograms.
* **Target Weight (kg)** (`target_weight_kg`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your desired weight in kilograms.
* **Available Time (minutes)** (`how_many_minutes_can_you_devote`, Time, Required)
	+ Enter the number of minutes you can dedicate to exercise per session.
* **Available Days per Week** (`how_many_days_a_week_can_you_commit`, Select One, Required)
	+ Choose how many days per week you can commit to exercise.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Any Medical Conditions?** (`any_medical_conditions`, Note, Optional)
	+ If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, Note, Optional)
	+ If you have any emergency contacts, please list them here.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor`, Note, Optional)
	+ If you have a doctor, please provide their name.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, Note, Optional)
	+ If you have an insurance provider, please provide their name.
* **Pharmacy** (`pharmacy`, Note, Optional)
	+ If you have a pharmacy, please provide their name.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, Select Multiple, Required)
	+ Choose how often you can exercise per week. Options are:
		- 1
		- 2
		- 3
		- 4
		- 5
		- 6
* **Diet Frequency** (`diet_frequency`, Select Multiple, Required)
	+ Choose how often you can follow a diet per week. Options are:
		- 1
		- 2
		- 3
		- 4
		- 5
		- 6
* **Water Consumption** (`water_consumption`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your daily water consumption in milliliters.
* **Height** (`height`, Number, Optional)
	+ If you want to provide your height, please enter it here.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Body Fat** (`body_fat`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your body fat percentage.
* **Blood Cholesterol** (`blood_cholesterol`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your blood cholesterol level.
* **Blood Glucose** (`blood_glucose`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your blood glucose level.
* **Blood Lipids** (`blood_lipids`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your blood lipids level.
* **Blood Sugar** (`blood_sugar`, Number, Required)
	+ Enter your blood sugar level.
* **Medications** (`medications`, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ If you take any medications, please list them here. Options are:
		- Aspirin
		- Ibuprofen
		- Paracetamol

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate when filling this form. The information you provide will help our healthcare professionals to create a personalized plan that suits your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
