# dental_insurance_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental insurance verification form is used to collect and verify a patient's insurance information for dental services. This includes checking the coverage status of their insurance plans, ensuring accurate billing, and assessing the patient's eligibility for services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Check the "insurance_provider" field and select "Yes" if the patient is covered under an insurance plan.
4. Enter the patient's policy number in the "policy_number" field.
5. Enter the patient's address in the "patient_address" field.
6. Enter the patient's phone number in the "phone_number" field.
7. Enter the patient's email address in the "email_address" field.
8. Check the "policy_dental" field and select "Yes" if the patient's policy covers dental services.
9. Check the "policy_medical" field and select "Yes" if the patient's policy covers medical services.
10. Check the "policy_vision" field and select "Yes" if the patient's policy covers vision services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID or medical record.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their ID or medical record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/12/1999).
* **Insurance Provider**: Check "Yes" if the patient is covered under an insurance plan.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the patient's insurance policy number.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's current address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's current phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's current email address.
* **Policy Covers Dental**: Check "Yes" if the patient's policy covers dental services.
* **Policy Covers Medical**: Check "Yes" if the patient's policy covers medical services.
* **Policy Covers Vision**: Check "Yes" if the patient's policy covers vision services.
