# Mpox Vaccine Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and medical details from patients who are interested in receiving the Mpox vaccine. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that you are eligible for the vaccine and that your contact and medical information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
5. Select your preferred vaccine type in the "Vaccination Type" field.
6. Select your preferred vaccination frequency in the "Vaccination Frequency" field.
7. Enter your vaccination time in the "Vaccination Time" field.
8. Enter any additional medical history that may be relevant to your vaccination in the "Medical History" field.
9. Read and sign the "Consent Form" field to confirm that you have read and understood the provided information.
10. Enter your signature in the "Signature" field.
11. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your vaccination in the "Additional Info" fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure that you are eligible for the vaccine before filling out this form.
