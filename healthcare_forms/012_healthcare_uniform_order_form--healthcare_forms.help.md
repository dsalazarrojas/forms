# healthcare_uniform_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare uniform order form is used to collect preferences and details for ordering healthcare worker uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred uniform size from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred uniform style from the options provided.
3. Select your preferred uniform color from the options provided.
4. Enter the date when you would like to receive your uniform (if applicable).
5. Choose your preferred delivery time (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes about your order.
7. Enter your email address for follow-up communication (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
9. Select your preference for the first option (if applicable).
10. Select your preference for the multiple options (if applicable).
11. Select your preferred size for the second option (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preferred uniform size** (Small, Medium, Large): Select your preferred uniform size from the options provided.
* **Preferred uniform style** (Button-Down, Polo, Scrubs): Choose your preferred uniform style from the options provided.
* **Preferred uniform color** (Blue, White, Black): Select your preferred uniform color from the options provided.
* **Date required**: Enter the date when you would like to receive your uniform (if applicable).
* **Preferred delivery time**: Enter your preferred delivery time (if applicable).
* **Additional notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email for follow-up**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication (if applicable).
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Select one**: Select your preference for this option (if applicable).
* **Select multiple**: Select your preference for this option (if applicable).
* **Select Size 2**: Select your preferred size for this option (if applicable).
