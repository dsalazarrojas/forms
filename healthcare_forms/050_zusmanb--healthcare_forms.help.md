# Zusmanb - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive health information from patients for the purpose of assessment and treatment.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general contact information in the "Main Information" section.
2. Provide your height and weight in the respective fields.
3. Specify your height unit in the drop-down menu.
4. Answer the questions about your diet plan and exercise frequency.
5. Select your blood type from the list.
6. List your current and previous medical medications.
7. Specify any allergies you have.
8. List your medical conditions, including any current, chronic, or acute conditions.
9. Provide additional information about your medical history.
10. Specify your emergency contact person and contact information.
11. Add any additional information you think is relevant to your health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information** (text, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Height** (number, required): Enter your current height in centimeters or meters.
* **Weight** (number, required): Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Height Unit** (select_one, required): Choose the unit for your height (meter, inches, feet, or yard).
* **Diet Plan** (text, optional): Describe your current diet plan, including any specific foods you eat or avoid.
* **Exercise Frequency** (select_one, optional): Choose how frequently you exercise (frequently, rarely, seldom, or never).
* **Blood Type** (select_one, optional): Select your blood type from A, B, AB, or O.
* **Medications** (text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (text, optional): Specify any allergies you have, including any severe or life-threatening allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): List any current, chronic, or acute medical conditions you have.
* **Medical History** (text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any significant events or surgeries.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Additional Info** (text, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your health status.
