# Medication Release Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for healthcare staff to collect feedback on the release of medication to patients. It helps gather information on patient satisfaction, incident occurrence, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, provide the required information as specified.
3. If a field is not relevant, leave it blank or enter "N/A" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the medication was released.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the clinic where the medication was released.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who released the medication.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who prescribed the medication.
* **Nurse Name** (`nurse_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the nurse who administered the medication.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the department where the medication was released.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the medication was released.
* **Facility** (`facility`, `text`, required): Enter the facility where the medication was released.
* **Ward** (`ward`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the ward where the medication was released.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the medication that was released.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the medication was released.
* **Patient Name 2** (`patient_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the second patient, if applicable.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the patient was satisfied or dissatisfied with the service.
* **Incident Occurred** (`incident_occurred`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if an incident occurred, "No" otherwise.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Enter any suggestions or comments you may have.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact phone number, if necessary.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a contact email address, if necessary.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time when the medication was released.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Enter any final comments or information.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `email`, optional): Enter a contact email address, if necessary.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* For optional fields, enter only relevant information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or staff member for guidance.
