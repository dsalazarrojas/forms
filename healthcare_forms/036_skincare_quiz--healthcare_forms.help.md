# Skincare Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skincare Quiz form helps users provide information about their skin type, concerns, and current products used. This information is used to provide personalized product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all that apply for **What is your skin type**. You can choose one or more options.
   - Dry skin: You have dry, flaky skin that tends to feel tight or rough.
   - Oily skin: You have oily skin that tends to feel greasy or shiny.
   - Combination skin: You have skin that is a mix of dry and oily.
2. Select all that apply for **What are your skin concerns**. You can choose one or more options.
   - Acne: You are concerned about acne or pimples on your skin.
   - Aging: You are concerned about aging skin or fine lines on your skin.
   - Sensitivity: You are concerned about skin sensitivity or irritation.
3. Select one option for **Which products do you currently use on your skin**.
   - Cleanser: A cleanser is a product used to clean and clear your skin.
   - Moisturizer: A moisturizer is a product used to hydrate and protect your skin.
   - Sunscreen: A sunscreen is a product used to protect your skin from the sun's UV rays.
4. Select all that apply for **How important are these concerns to you**. You can choose one or more options.
   - Acne: This is a concern for you.
   - Aging: This is a concern for you.
   - Sensitivity: This is a concern for you.
5. If you have a skin type that is not listed, please enter it in the text box for **Is there anything else you would like to add about your skin type**.
6. If you have any other skin concerns not listed, please enter them in the text box for **Are there any other skin concerns you have**.
7. If you have any other products or concerns not listed, please enter them in the text box for **What products should we recommend you**.
8. For **How likely are you to purchase a recommended product**, select one option.
   - Definitely: You are likely to purchase a recommended product.
   - Probably: You are somewhat likely to purchase a recommended product.
   - Not Likely: You are not likely to purchase a recommended product.
9. Enter your email address in the text box for **Email**.
10. Enter your phone number in the text box for **Phone**.
11. Click the **Submit** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your skin type**
  • A select multiple field with options for dry, oily, and combination skin types.
* **What are your skin concerns**
  • A select multiple field with options for acne, aging, and sensitivity concerns.
* **Which products do you currently use on your skin**
  • A select one field with options for cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.
* **How important are these concerns to you**
  • A select multiple field with options for acne, aging, and sensitivity concerns.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add about your skin type**
  • A text field where you can enter additional information about your skin type.
* **Are there any other skin concerns you have**
  • A text field where you can enter additional skin concerns.
* **What products should we recommend you**
  • A text field where you can enter other products or concerns not listed.
* **How likely are you to purchase a recommended product**
  • A select one field with options for definitely, probably, and not likely.
* **Email**
  • A text field for entering your email address.
* **Phone**
  • A text field for entering your phone number.
* **Submit**
  • A button to submit your form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Select all that apply for fields with multiple options.
- Be honest about your concerns and skin type.
- Enter your contact information in the text fields.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.
