# Covid 19 Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help patients and medical professionals acknowledge and document important details about a patient's COVID-19 status, medical history, and interactions with healthcare providers. By completing this form, you will be able to collect and record essential information to provide better care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please provide the required information in the corresponding fields.
* Each field is labeled with its purpose to help guide you through the process.
* If you are unable to complete a field, please reach out to a healthcare provider for assistance.
