# waiting_room_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients about their experience in the waiting room.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please choose one of the available options for each question based on your experience.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the questions that have a "Yes/No" format.
3. Select one or multiple options for the question that has a "Select Multiple" format.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **waiting_room_experience** (Select One, 1):
	+ This question asks about your overall experience in the waiting room.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you were satisfied with your experience, "No" otherwise.
* **overall_wait_time** (Number, 2):
	+ This question asks about the length of time you waited before being called to the exam room.
	+ Please enter the actual time you waited in minutes.
* **staff_interaction** (Select One, 3):
	+ This question asks about the interaction with staff in the waiting room.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the staff's interaction, "No" otherwise.
* **services_offered** (Select One, 4):
	+ This question asks about the services available in the waiting room.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you were offered services that met your needs, "No" otherwise.
* **amenities_available** (Select Multiple, 5):
	+ This question asks about the amenities available in the waiting room.
	+ Please select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, you can include them in the space provided below.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
