# Patient Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity of a patient, ensuring accuracy and up-to-date information in the healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete patient information.
2. Double-check that all fields are correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/01/1999).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Type the patient's full address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
