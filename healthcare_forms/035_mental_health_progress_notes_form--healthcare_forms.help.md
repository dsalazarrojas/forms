# mental health progress notes form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health progress notes form is designed to capture essential information on a patient's mental health journey, including their progress, goals, and challenges. This form helps healthcare professionals track and record vital information about patient care, ensuring continuity and accuracy in treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the mental health progress notes form, follow these steps:

1.  Start with the **Progress Notes** section (Page 1).
2.  Ensure to fill in the **Category** field with relevant details about the patient's condition or treatment category.
3.  Enter the **Date** of the progress notes, which can be done using a standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4.  Fill out the **Time** field for accurate scheduling and planning purposes.
5.  In the **Provider** field, enter the name of the healthcare provider who is currently treating or has treated the patient.
6.  Use the **Notes** section for detailed descriptions of the patient's progress, goals, and any challenges they faced.
7.  The **Patient ID** field is for entering the patient's ID number for proper identification.
8.  If referring another healthcare professional for further treatment, mark **Referring Provider** as "Yes" and fill out the **Referring Provider Name** field with the name of the provider.
9.  Enter the contact phone number for the referring provider in the **Referring Provider Phone** field.
10.  Select the **Referring Provider Phone Type** to indicate whether the phone is landline or mobile.
11.  Finally, confirm whether the referring provider was notified or not by marking **Referring Provider Notified**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
**Progress Notes (`mental_health_progress_notes_form`, type: note)**: Enter a brief description of the patient's current mental state, including any notable developments or issues. This section should provide a concise overview of the patient's progress.
*   **Category (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_category`, type: text)**: This field is for specifying the category of the patient's mental health treatment or condition. Choose a relevant term from your organization's standard classification system.
*   **Date (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_date`, type: date)**: Record the date of the progress notes entry using a standard date format (e.g., 02/15/2022).
*   **Time (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_time`, type: time)**: Enter the time of the progress notes entry for scheduling and planning purposes.
*   **Provider (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_provider`, type: text)**: Identify the healthcare provider who is currently treating or has treated the patient.
*   **Notes (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_notes`, type: note)**: Provide detailed descriptions of the patient's progress, goals, and any challenges they faced.
*   **Patient ID (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_patient_id`, type: number)**: Enter the patient's ID number for proper identification and tracking.
*   **Referring Provider (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_referring_provider`, type: select_one)**: Mark "Yes" if another healthcare professional is referring the patient for further treatment.
*   **Referring Provider Name (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_referring_provider_name`, type: text)**: Enter the name of the referring provider.
*   **Referring Provider Phone (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_referring_provider_phone`, type: text)**: Enter the contact phone number of the referring provider.
*   **Referring Provider Phone Ext (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_referring_provider_phone_ext`, type: text)**: If necessary, enter any extensions for the referring provider's phone number.
*   **Referring Provider Phone Type (`mental_health_progress_notes_form_referring_provider_phone_type`, type: select_multiple)**: Choose whether the referring provider's phone number is a landline or mobile number.

## Tips
- When filling the **Notes** field, keep the language clear, concise, and respectful of the patient's privacy.
- Use standard formatting for dates and times to avoid confusion.
- Ensure that the **Referring Provider Notified** field is accurately filled out to reflect the referring process.
- Always refer to the relevant healthcare guidelines and regulations for accurate documentation and reporting.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be used for external sharing without proper clearance.
