# Virtual Clinic Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Clinic Appointment form is designed to collect necessary information for a virtual clinic appointment. This form is used to gather details about the patient, appointment type, date, time, physician name, medical record, insurance provider, appointment duration, and follow-up needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Form" page.
2. Enter the patient's details in the "Patient Form" fields.
3. Choose the appointment type (Consultation, Check up, or Scheduling).
4. Enter the appointment date and time.
5. Enter the physician's name who will be attending the appointment.
6. Provide a medical record of the patient (if necessary).
7. Identify the insurance provider (if applicable).
8. Specify the appointment duration.
9. Confirm if follow-up is required (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Form (<name>)**: Enter the patient's details.
* **Appointment Type (<label>)**: Select the type of appointment (Consultation, Check up, or Scheduling).
* **Date (<name>)**: Enter the appointment date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time (<name>)**: Enter the appointment time.
* **Physician Name (<label>)**: Enter the physician's name who will be attending the appointment.
* **Medical Record (<label>)**: Provide a medical record of the patient (if necessary).
* **Insurance Provider (<label>)**: Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Appointment Duration (<label>)**: Specify the duration of the appointment.
* **Follow Up (<label>)**: Confirm if follow-up is required (Yes or No).
