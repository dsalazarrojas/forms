# Informed Consent For Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Informed Consent For Counseling Form is a crucial document that clients must complete before starting counseling sessions. It serves as a record of their understanding and agreement to the counseling process and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in the client details on the first page.
3. On the second page, select the correct answer to the question "Consent to Counseling".
4. In the "Therapy Details" field, provide a brief description of your current situation or concerns.
5. In the "Client Rights" field, list any rights you would like to assert or express during the counseling process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Details** (`client_details`, Text, Required): Please fill in your name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details that you would like to share with your counselor.

- **Consent to Counseling** (`consent_to_counseling`, Select One, Required): Please select "True" if you are willing to participate in counseling and "False" if you are not. This is a mandatory field to ensure you have given your informed consent.

- **Therapy Details** (`therapy_details`, Note, Optional): This field is for you to describe your current situation or concerns that brought you to counseling. Please write a brief but detailed account to help your counselor understand your needs.

- **Client Rights** (`client_rights`, Text, Optional): In this field, list any rights you would like to assert or express during the counseling process. For example, you may wish to have a specific level of confidentiality or communication preferences.
