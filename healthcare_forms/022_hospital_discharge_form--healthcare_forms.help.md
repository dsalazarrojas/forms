# Hospital Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document patient discharges from a hospital setting, providing a standardized way to capture necessary information about each patient's departure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the patient's **Patient Name** in the text field provided. This is the name of the person being discharged.
2. Enter the **Discharge Date** in the date field, which is the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
3. Enter the **Discharge Time** in the time field, which is the time the patient was discharged from the hospital.
4. Enter the **Doctor's Name** in the text field, which is the name of the doctor who attended to the patient.
5. Enter the **Hospital Name** in the text field, which is the name of the hospital where the patient was discharged.
6. Describe the **Reason for Discharge** in the text field, providing a clear and concise explanation for the patient's discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the doctor who attended to the patient.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was discharged.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, Text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the patient's discharge.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear understanding of the patient's discharge.
* If any field is left incomplete, please fill it in as soon as possible to maintain the integrity of the patient's record.
* This form is intended for internal use by healthcare professionals.
