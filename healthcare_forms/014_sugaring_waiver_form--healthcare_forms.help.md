# Sugaring Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a patient's informed consent for sugaring treatments and to record the signature of the patient or their authorized representative on file.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:
    1.  Enter your name in the **Customer Name** field, as this is the required field for identification purposes.
    2.  If you have a contact phone number, please enter it in the **Contact Phone** field so that we can reach you if needed.
    3.  In the **Sugaring Waiver Form** field, please enter your informed consent for the sugaring treatment, including any relevant medical history, allergies, or other important details.
    4.  Finally, sign the **Signature** field after you have read and understood the waiver form. Please print your name next to your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as this field is required for identification purposes.
*   **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: If you have a contact phone number, enter it so we can reach you if needed.
*   **Sugaring Waiver Form (waiver_text)**: Enter your informed consent for the sugaring treatment, including any relevant medical history, allergies, or other important details.
*   **Signature (waiver_signed)**: Please sign this field after you have read and understood the waiver form. Print your name next to your signature.
