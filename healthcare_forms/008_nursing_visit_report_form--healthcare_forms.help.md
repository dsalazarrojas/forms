# Nursing Visit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Visit Report Form is a standardized form used to document and record information about a patient's nursing visit. It is designed to gather information on medication administration, medical conditions, patient assessment, and demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the patient's personal details, including their name, date of birth, and monthly and yearly dates of birth.
2. Select the correct option for the "Nursing Visit Report" field, indicating whether the condition was managed or not.
3. Provide a brief text description of the medication administered, if any.
4. List any medical conditions managed or not managed during the visit.
5. Select any relevant symptoms or concerns from the list provided in the "Patient Assessment" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or details about the patient's condition in the "Nursing Visit Report Form Section 4" field.
7. Finally, submit the completed form to ensure accurate and up-to-date patient records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: A text field for the patient's full name.
	+ Please enter the patient's full name, first and last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: A date field for the patient's date of birth.
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient DOB (Monthly)**: A number field representing the patient's age in months.
	+ Enter the number of months old the patient is.
* **Patient DOB (Yearly)**: A number field representing the patient's age in years.
	+ Enter the number of years old the patient is.
* **Medication Administered**: A text field for a brief description of any medication administered.
	+ If medication was administered, provide a brief description of the dosage, frequency, and route of administration.
* **Medical Condition**: A text field for listing any medical conditions managed or not managed during the visit.
	+ Provide a brief description of the condition and its management.
* **Nursing Visit Report**: A select field indicating whether the condition was managed or not.
	+ Select "Condition Managed" if the condition was successfully managed during the visit, or "Condition Not Managed" if it was not.
* **Patient Assessment**: A select field for listing relevant symptoms or concerns.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to the patient's condition.
* **Nursing Visit Report Form Section 2**: A select multiple field for listing relevant symptoms or concerns.
	+ Select one or more options that apply to the patient's condition.
* **Nursing Visit Report Form Section 3**: A text field for providing additional comments or details.
	+ Provide any additional information that may be helpful in understanding the patient's condition.
* **Nursing Visit Report Form Section 4**: A text field for providing additional comments or details.
	+ Provide any additional information that may be helpful in understanding the patient's condition.
