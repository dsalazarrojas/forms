# Speech Therapy Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between a client and a speech therapy service provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the client's name and date in sections 1 and 8, respectively.
2. In section 3, select "Yes" if the service agreement has been signed, and "No" otherwise.
3. In section 4, sign your name as the client to confirm agreement to the terms of the service.
4. Fill in the service provider's name in section 5.
5. In section 6, sign your name as the service provider to confirm agreement to the terms of the service.
6. In section 7, select "Yes" if the service agreement has been reviewed, and "No" otherwise.
7. Fill in the dates in sections 8 and 9, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as the client.
* **Date Service Starts (2)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date the service will start.
* **Service Agreement Signed (3)** (`select_one`, required: false): Confirm if the service agreement has been signed.
* **Client Signature (4)** (`note`, required: false): Sign your name as the client to confirm agreement.
* **Service Provider Name (5)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Signature (6)** (`note`, required: false): Sign your name as the service provider to confirm agreement.
* **Service Agreement Reviewed (7)** (`select_one`, required: false): Confirm if the service agreement has been reviewed.
* **Client Date (8)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date.
* **Service Provider Date (9)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have carefully reviewed the service agreement before signing.
* If you are unsure about any part of the agreement, please contact your service provider.
