# Dental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication and referrals between healthcare providers, ensuring that patients receive optimal care. It collects and shares essential information about patients' dental needs, medical insurance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate information.
3.  Use the hint provided for each field as a guide to enter the correct information.
4.  If you're unsure about a field or its purpose, ask for clarification or seek guidance from the healthcare team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dentist 1 Name** (`dentist_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dentist who referred the patient.
* **Dentist 2 Address** (`dentist_2_address`, text, optional): Type the address of the dentist who referred the patient (if applicable).
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical insurance the patient has, if any (e.g., Medicare, Medicaid, Private, or Other).
* **Other Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance_provider_other`, text, optional): If the patient has "Other" medical insurance, please specify the type in this field.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, optional): Record the date of the patient's last visit to the dentist (if applicable).
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, select_one, optional): Choose the reason for the referral (e.g., Emergency, Routine, Cosmetic, or Other).
* **Dentist 1 Address** (`dentist_1_address`, text, optional): Type the address of the dentist who is referring the patient (if applicable).
* **Dentist 2 Phone Number** (`dentist_2_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the dentist who is referring the patient (if applicable).
* **Dentist 2 Name** (`dentist_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dentist who is referring the patient.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, optional): Record the date of the referral.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional relevant notes about the patient's referral.
* **Assigned Doctor** (`assigned_doctor`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of doctor who will be handling the referral (e.g., Dentist, Doctor, Specialist, or Other).
