# Doctor Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's medical history, diagnosis, and treatment plan. It is intended for healthcare professionals to use for accurate and efficient record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email.
2. Select the patient's gender and age.
3. Choose the doctor's specialty from the provided options.
4. Enter the patient's medical history, including any allergies and medical conditions.
5. Provide the doctor's signature status and approval status.
6. Select the patient's signature status and approval status.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes from the doctor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or allergies.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`): Enter the diagnosis or condition being treated.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`): Outline the treatment plan for the patient's condition.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`): Select the doctor's specialty from the provided options (e.g., Cardiologist, Dermatologist, etc.).
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Choose the patient's gender from the provided options (e.g., Male, Female, Non-Binary).
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`): Enter the patient's age.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`): Select the doctor's signature status from the provided options (e.g., Signed, Unsighed, Not Signed).
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Select the patient's signature status from the provided options (e.g., Signed, Unsighed, Not Signed).
* **Doctor Approval** (`doctor_approval`): Select the doctor's approval status from the provided options (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Patient Approval** (`patient_approval`): Select the patient's approval status from the provided options (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Date of Diagnosis** (`date_of_diagnosis`): Enter the date of the diagnosis.
* **Time of Diagnosis** (`time_of_diagnosis`): Enter the time of the diagnosis.
* **Doctor Comment** (`doctor_comment`): Enter any additional comments or notes from the doctor.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* Ensure the doctor's signature is signed before submitting the form.
* Review the patient's approval status before submitting the form.
* Enter any additional comments or notes from the doctor for future reference.
