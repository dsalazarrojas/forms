# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is intended for patients to provide a detailed account of their health background, which will be used by healthcare professionals to inform diagnosis, treatment, and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form carefully and answering every question honestly.
2. Select the relevant information for each field, based on your personal experiences and medical history.
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
- **Weight**: If applicable, enter your weight in kilograms.
- **Allergies**: Indicate if you have any known allergies, such as food or medication allergies.
  - If you answer "Yes", you can specify the type of allergy in the "Additional Information" field.
- **Illnesses**: Indicate if you have any pre-existing medical conditions, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
  - If you answer "Yes", you can describe the condition and its impact on your daily life.
- **Operations**: Indicate if you have undergone any surgical procedures or operations in the past.
  - If you answer "Yes", you can specify the type of operation and the date it was performed.
- **Healthy Habits**: Select "Yes" if you engage in regular exercise, eat a balanced diet, or practice stress-reducing activities.
- **Unhealthy Habits**: Select "Yes" if you have any habits that are detrimental to your health, such as smoking or excessive drinking.
  - If you answer "Yes", you can describe the habits and their impact on your health.
- **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant information about your medical history or health status, such as medication use or recent medical events.
