# Oral Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your oral surgery, including your medical history, surgery details, and consent for anesthesia. It's essential for your healthcare provider to have accurate and complete information before proceeding with the surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Please fill in all the required fields (marked with \*).
* For each field, provide as much detail as possible to ensure your healthcare provider has a complete understanding of your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please provide your full name, date of birth, and contact information (phone number and email or address).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.
* **Surgery Details** (`surgery_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the oral surgery you are planning to undergo, including the type of surgery, expected duration, and any specific instructions or concerns.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your consent for anesthesia:
	+ General Anesthesia
	+ IV Sedation
	+ Local Anesthesia
	+ Twilight Sedation
	+ Deep Sedation
	+ No Anesthesia
* **Anesthesia Type** (`anesthesia_type`, select one, required): Select the type of anesthesia you will be using for the surgery:
	+ General Anesthesia
	+ IV Sedation
	+ Local Anesthesia
	+ Twilight Sedation
	+ Deep Sedation
	+ No Anesthesia
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your surgery or anesthesia, please describe them here.
* **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): If you have insurance coverage, please provide your insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): In case of an emergency, please provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the person to contact.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure your healthcare provider has a complete understanding of your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
