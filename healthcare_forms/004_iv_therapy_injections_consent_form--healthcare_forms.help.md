# IV Therapy Injections Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain patient consent and authorization for IV therapy injections, ensuring the patient is aware of the potential benefits and risks involved in the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Fill in your name and any other required fields as directed on each page.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_consent**: A text field where you can write your consent for the IV therapy injections. This is a required field.
* **patient_understanding**: A text field where you can provide details about your understanding of the treatment. This field is optional, but it's recommended for your own understanding.
* **medical_condition**: A text field where you can describe the medical condition being treated with IV therapy injections. This field is optional.
* **therapy_understanding**: A text field where you can write about your understanding of the therapy. This field is optional.
* **injection_details**: A text field where you can provide details about the injection, such as the dosage and administration method. This field is optional.
* **patient_authorization**: A text field where you can select your level of authorization for the injection. This field is not required, but it's recommended for verification purposes.
* **injection_site**: A text field where you can specify the site of the injection. This field is optional.
* **consent_signature**: A multiple selection field where you can sign and date the consent form. This field is a required field.
* **healthcare_provider**: A text field where you can enter the name of the healthcare provider administering the injection. This field is optional.
* **injection_date**: A date field where you can select the date the injection was administered. This field is optional.
* **injection_time**: A time field where you can select the time the injection was administered. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before filling it out.
- Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
