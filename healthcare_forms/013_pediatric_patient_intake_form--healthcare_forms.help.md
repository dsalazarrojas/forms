# Pediatric Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive medical intake form designed for pediatric patients. It helps healthcare providers gather information about the patient's medical history, family history, and other relevant details to ensure accurate diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are the parent or guardian of the patient.
2. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about the patient.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all applicable answers.
4. For fields with text input, provide a clear and concise description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page**: This is the beginning of the form, introducing the patient's basic information.
	+ **First_page**: This is the title of the form page.
	+ **First_page_section**: This section is for general information about the patient.
* **Parent Info**: Please provide your contact information as the parent or guardian of the patient.
* **Medical History**: This section is for any medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
	+ **Medical Conditions**: Please select all medical conditions that apply from the list.
	+ **Other Medical Conditions**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed, please provide a description.
* **Family History**: This section is for any medical conditions or genetic risks in the patient's family history.
	+ **Allergies**: Please select all allergies that apply from the list.
	+ **Other Allergies**: If the patient has any other allergies not listed, please provide a description.
* **Medical History**: This section is for any medical events or treatments the patient has undergone.
	+ **Surgical History**: Please list any surgeries the patient has had.
	+ **Medsications**: Please list any medications the patient is currently taking.
	+ **Injuries**: Please describe any injuries the patient has experienced.
	+ **Other Surgeries**: If the patient has had any other surgeries not listed, please provide a description.
	+ **Other Medications**: If the patient is taking any other medications not listed, please provide a description.
	+ **Other Injuries**: If the patient has experienced any other injuries not listed, please provide a description.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please select all medical conditions that apply from the list.
	+ **Other Medical Conditions**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed, please provide a description.
* **Family History**: This section is for any medical conditions or genetic risks in the patient's family history.
	+ **Other Family History**: If there is any other family history not listed, please provide a description.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise answers for each field.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
