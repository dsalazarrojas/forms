# Hearing And Eyecare Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hearing and Eyecare Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a patient's hearing and eye care. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to gather relevant data to support patient care and referrals. Please ensure that you only complete this form for patients who require a hearing and eye assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter patient information on page 1.
3. Complete the hearing assessment on page 2.
4. Enter visual acuity information on page 3.
5. Select hearing test results on page 4.
6. Indicate if any eyecare referrals are required on pages 5 and 6.
7. Enter any additional comments on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
**Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information, as required.

### Page 2: Hearing Assessment
**Hearing Assessment** (`hearing_assessment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
* Yes
* No

This field is used to assess the patient's hearing ability.

### Page 3: Visual Acuity
**Visual Acuity** (`visual_acuity`, number, optional): Enter the patient's visual acuity measurement, usually expressed as a decimal value.

### Page 4: Hearing Test Results
**Hearing Test Results** (`hearing_test_results`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
* Normal
* Abnormal

This field is used to record the results of the patient's hearing test.

### Page 5: Eyecare Referrals
**Eyecare Referrals** (`eyecare_referrals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
* Yes
* No

This field is used to indicate if any eyecare referrals are required.

### Page 6: Other Eyecare Referrals
**Other Eyecare Referrals** (`other_eyecare_referrals`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
* None
* Family Doctor
* Specialist

This field is used to specify any additional eyecare referrals required.

### Page 7: Hearing and Eyecare Recommendations
**Hearing and Eyecare Recommendations** (`hearing_and_eyecare_recommendations`, text, optional): Enter any additional recommendations or notes for the patient's hearing and eye care.

### Page 8: Additional Comments
**Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's hearing and eye care.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to consult with a healthcare professional.
* This form is designed to support patient care; if you are unsure about a patient's condition or require further guidance, please consult a healthcare professional.
