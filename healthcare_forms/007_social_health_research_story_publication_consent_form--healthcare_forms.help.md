# social_health_research_story_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant consent and demographic information for social research publications. It is a required form for participants to provide their details and status of publication.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your information is up-to-date and complete.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields (participant_id, first_name, last_name, email, publication_date, consent_status).
*   If a field is optional, you can choose to leave it blank if it's not applicable to you.
*   Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **participant_id**: A unique identifier for the participant, usually provided by the researcher. Please enter this number if you have it.
*   **first_name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
*   **last_name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
*   **email**: Your email address where the researcher can contact you for more information.
*   **phone**: Your phone number where the researcher can contact you for more information.
*   **publication_date**: The date of the publication you are submitting your story to. This should match the date the story was actually published.
*   **consent_status**: Select 'Published' if the story has been published already or 'Not Published' if it hasn't. You can select multiple statuses if you have published and not-published stories.
*   **story_title**: The title of your publication. This is an optional field but helps researchers understand the context of your story.
