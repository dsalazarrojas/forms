# Precautionary Coronavirus Liability Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to provide a precautionary measure to release liability from the Service Provider for any potential consequences or damages resulting from the services provided. It's essential to complete this form to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the terms and conditions associated with the services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the information required.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer ID, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Liability Release (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false)**: This section is for you to select the type of liability release you want to apply. You can choose one or more of the options provided. Please select the options that best describe your situation.
* **Service Terms and Conditions (Service Terms, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to acknowledge that you have read and understand the service terms and conditions. Please type out your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Signature (Waiver Signature, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to sign and date the waiver. This indicates that you have read and understand the terms and conditions and release liability for any potential consequences.
* **Service Provider (Service Provider, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to identify the Service Provider who will be providing the services.
* **Signature Date (Signature Date, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to date and sign the waiver, indicating that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Signed (Waiver Signed, Type, required: false)**: This section is for you to confirm whether you have signed the waiver or not. Please choose the option that accurately reflects your situation.

Note: Please make sure to review each field carefully and ensure you understand the information required before submitting the form.
