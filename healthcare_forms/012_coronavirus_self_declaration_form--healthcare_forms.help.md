# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users regarding their health status during the COVID-19 pandemic. It aims to gather essential details about the user's symptoms, if any, and their signature for confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "User Info" page and fill in the required information, if any.
2. Proceed to the "Symptoms" page and provide any symptoms you are experiencing.
3. Finally, on the "Signature" page, sign the form by selecting the date on which you are completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (User Info, text, optional)**: This field is for providing your basic contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Symptoms (Symptoms, text, optional)**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Signature (Signature, date, optional)**: Sign the form by selecting the date on which you are completing this form, indicating your acknowledgement of the provided information.

## Tips
* Please fill in your symptoms accurately to the best of your ability.
* This form is for your health and safety, and your cooperation is greatly appreciated.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
