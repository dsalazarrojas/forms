# Personal Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general personal training informed consent form used in a healthcare setting to collect user information and ensure they understand the training process and the institution's policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Answer all required fields as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask the institution's staff for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This is the main text field where you can enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your contact details or personal details.
* **Medical History**: This field is for providing any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation in the training.
* **Emergency Contacts**: This field is for listing the people you want to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Next Steps**: This field is for indicating any next steps you'd like to take after completing the training.
* **Medical Conditions**: This field is for providing additional information about your medical conditions, if not already mentioned in the Medical History field.
* **Allergies**: This field is for listing any allergies you may have.
* **Medications**: This field is for providing information about any medications you are currently taking.
* **Other Medical History**: This field is for providing any other relevant medical history that is not listed in the Medical History field.
* **Personal Data**: This field is for entering any other relevant personal details, such as your contact information.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for providing your contact information, such as phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Consent**: This field is for indicating that you have read and understood the information provided in this form. Please select the option that best represents your understanding.
