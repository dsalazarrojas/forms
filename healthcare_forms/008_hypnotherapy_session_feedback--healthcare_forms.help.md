# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their hypnotherapy sessions. The collected data will help improve the hypnotherapist's service.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out all the required fields. If a field is not filled out, you will not be able to submit the form. The form has two sections: General Information and Feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **Hypnotherapist** (`Hypnotherapist`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the hypnotherapist who conducted the session.
* **Date & Time** (`Date & Time`, `date`, required): Please select the date and time of the session.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with the hypnotherapist on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the session (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Would Recommend** (`Would Recommend`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would recommend this hypnotherapist to someone else (True or False).
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates or notifications about your feedback.

**Note:** Please fill out all required fields to submit your feedback.
