# health_screening_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health screening communication form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to document and track patient health screening and communication history. This form helps ensure that patients receive the necessary care and attention they require, and that healthcare providers have accurate and up-to-date information about each patient's health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing a title for the form, which is typically the name of the health screening or communication process being documented.
2. Select the relevant category of the health screening or communication.
3. Provide a brief description of the health screening or communication, if necessary.
4. Enter the version number of the form.
5. Specify the person who submitted the form.
6. Enter the date the form was submitted.
7. Specify the completion status of the form.
8. Enter the patient's first name.
9. Enter the patient's last name.
10. Enter the patient's date of birth.
11. Enter the patient's address.
12. Enter the patient's city.
13. Enter the patient's state.
14. Enter the patient's zip code.
15. Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
16. Enter the patient's email (optional).
17. List any patient health conditions.
18. List any patient allergies.
19. Specify the medical provider responsible for the patient's care.
20. Enter the result of the health screening.
21. Specify the vaccination status of the patient.
22. Provide any follow-up recommendations for the patient.
23. Specify the follow-up status of the patient's care.
24. Enter any comments or notes related to the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for the form that describes the health screening or communication process being documented.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the health screening or communication from the provided options (e.g., healthcare forms, other).
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the health screening or communication, if necessary.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Specify the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Specify the completion date of the form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the form from the provided options (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City** (`patient_city`, text, required): Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State** (`patient_state`, text, required): Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, required): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email (optional).
* **Patient Health Conditions** (`patient_health_conditions`, text, optional): List any health conditions the patient has.
* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Specify the medical provider responsible for the patient's care.
* **Health Screening Result** (`health_screening_result`, text, optional): Enter the result of the health screening.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Select the vaccination status of the patient from the provided options (e.g., True, False, unsure).
* **Follow Up Recommendation** (`follow_up_recommendation`, text, optional): Provide any follow-up recommendations for the patient's care.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Select the follow-up status of the patient's care from the provided options (e.g., completed, pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes related to the patient's care.
