# Palliative_Care_Monitoring_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Monitoring Form is a critical tool used to track and assess the health status of patients undergoing palliative care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical record and other relevant documentation to ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on the patient's current condition.
3. Be sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Comfort** (2: patient_comfort): This field assesses the patient's comfort level, which can be Very High, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Pain Management** (3: pain_management): This field indicates the level of pain management needed, which can be Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
* **Medication** (4: medication): Enter the number of medications currently prescribed to the patient.
* **Medical History** (5: medical_history): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact the patient's care.
* **Allergies** (6: allergies): Indicate whether the patient has any known allergies.
* **Vital Signs** (7: vital_signs): Record the patient's current vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, etc.
* **Medical Notes** (8: medical_notes): Enter any additional medical notes or observations.
* **Medication Side Effects** (9: medication_side_effects): Indicate whether the patient is experiencing any side effects from their medications.
* **Patient Progress** (10: patient_progress): Select the patient's current progress, which can be Improved, Stable, or Decreased.
* **Discharge Status** (11: discharge_status): Indicate whether the patient has been discharged or not.
* **Care Team** (12: care_team): Select the members of the care team, which can include Nurse, Doctor, or Therapist.
* **Patient Status** (13: patient_status): Indicate the patient's current status, which can be Admitted, Discharged, or Unknown.
* **Patient Condition** (14: patient_condition): Enter any relevant information about the patient's current condition.
* **Hospital Length of Stay** (15: hospital_length_of_stay): Enter the patient's expected length of stay in the hospital.
* **Discharge Date** (16: discharge_date): Record the date of the patient's discharge (if applicable).
* **Follow-up** (17: follow_up): Indicate whether follow-up appointments or check-ups are needed.
* **Medical Care** (18: medical_care): Select any relevant medical specialties, such as Cardiology, Oncology, or Pulmonology.
* **Care Plan** (19: care_plan): Select the patient's current care plan, which can be Palliative Care or Hospice Care.
* **Medical Specialties** (20: medical_specialties): Select any relevant medical specialties, such as Dermatology, Cardiology, or Neurology.
* **Treatment Plan** (21: treatment_plan): Enter any relevant information about the patient's treatment plan.
* **Patient Status Update** (22: patient_status_update): Select the patient's current status update, which can be Improved, Stable, or Decreased.
* **Care Team Update** (23: care_team_update): Select the updated care team members.
* **Notes** (24: notes): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.

## Tips
* Be sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure that it reflects the patient's changing condition.
* Use the form in conjunction with other relevant medical records and documentation to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult with other healthcare professionals or the patient's primary care physician.
