# Healthcare Plan Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to access the Healthcare Plan Portal, which provides users with a personalized health plan and medical group information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your health insurance provider from the "Health Insurance Provider" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your healthcare plan name in the "Healthcare Plan Name" field.
7. Select your medical group from the "Medical Group" multi-select menu.
8. Enter your physical address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (Health Insurance Provider, select_one, required): Select your health insurance provider from the dropdown menu.
* **Healthcare Plan Name** (Healthcare Plan Name, text, required): Enter your healthcare plan name.
* **Medical Group** (Medical Group, select_multiple, required): Select your medical group from the multi-select menu.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your physical address.
