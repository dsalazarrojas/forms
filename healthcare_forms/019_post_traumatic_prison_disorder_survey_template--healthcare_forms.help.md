# Post-Traumatic Prison Disorder Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the experiences of individuals with post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) in the context of their incarceration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. You can skip questions that you do not feel comfortable answering.
3. If you have any questions or need help, please contact the form administrators at <form@gic.mx>.
4. The form is divided into sections, each addressing different aspects of your experiences with PTSD.
5. Please answer the questions in the order they appear, and use the provided options for each question.
6. If you need to add any additional information or clarify your answers, please use the "Other" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This question welcomes you to the survey and asks you to take a few minutes to answer the following questions about your experiences with post-traumatic stress disorder.
* **Name** (`id: 2`): Please provide your name for the purpose of this survey.
* **Duration of Symptoms** (`id: 3`): This question asks you to report the length of time you have been experiencing symptoms of PTSD.
* **Trauma History** (`id: 4`): This question asks if you have a history of trauma in your life. Please select "True" if you have experienced trauma and "False" if you have not.
* **Type of Trauma** (`id: 5`): If you selected "True" in the previous question, please select all the types of trauma you have experienced.
* **Severity of Symptoms** (`id: 6`): This question asks you to rate the severity of your PTSD symptoms on a scale.
* **Symptoms** (`id: 7`): Please select all the symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Symptoms while Incarcerated** (`id: 8`): This question asks if you have experienced any of the symptoms while incarcerated. Please select all that apply.
* **Duration of Incarceration** (`id: 9`): This question asks you to report the length of time you have been experiencing symptoms of PTSD while incarcerated.
* **Symptoms Incarcerated** (`id: 10`): Please select all the symptoms you are experiencing while incarcerated.
* **Severity of Symptoms while Incarcerated** (`id: 11`): This question asks you to rate the severity of your symptoms while incarcerated on a scale.
* **Stressors** (`id: 12`): This question asks you to describe what has made you feel more anxious or stressed while incarcerated.
* **Trauma History while Incarcerated** (`id: 13`): This question asks if you have experienced trauma while incarcerated.
* **Comfort with Disclosure** (`id: 14`): Please select all that apply regarding your comfort with disclosing certain information.
* **Other** (`id: 15`): If you need to add any additional information or clarify your answers, please use this field.

## Tips

* If you are experiencing distress or need immediate support, please seek help from a qualified mental health professional or crisis hotline.
* You can skip any question that you do not feel comfortable answering.
* If you need help with this form, please contact the form administrators at <form@gic.mx>.
