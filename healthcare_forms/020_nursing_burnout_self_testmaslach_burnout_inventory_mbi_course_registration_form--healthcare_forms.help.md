# Nursing Burnout Self Test Maslach Burnout Inventory MBI Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect demographic and course-related information from nurses who are registering for a course on burnout prevention. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure successful course registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form in English, as no other languages are supported.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your course registration and other important updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further contact and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, required): Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, number, required): Enter your medical license number as a numeric value.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical school you attended.
* **Graduate Year** (`graduate_year`, number, required): Enter the year you graduated from medical school.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the course in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the course in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Course Status** (`course_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your course from the options provided.
* **Course Status Description** (`course_status_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your course status.
* **Clinical Site** (`clinical_site`, text, optional): Enter the name of the clinical site where you will be working.
* **Clinical Site Type** (`clinical_site_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the clinical site is a real site, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Clinical Site Name** (`clinical_site_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Address** (`clinical_site_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Phone** (`clinical_site_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Fax** (`clinical_site_fax`, text, required): Enter the fax number of the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Email** (`clinical_site_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Website** (`clinical_site_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the clinical site (if available).
* **Clinical Site Map Url** (`clinical_site_map_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the clinical site's map (if available).
* **Clinical Site Comments** (`clinical_site_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the clinical site.
