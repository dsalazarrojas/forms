# Patient Encounter Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Encounter Form Template is a document used to record essential details about a patient's visit to a healthcare provider. It is designed to capture demographic, clinical, and treatment information to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit in the "Visit Date" field.
3. Choose the attending physician's name from the "Physician" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the patient's medical ID number in the "Medical ID" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the chief complaint in the "Chief Complaint" field.
6. Describe the history of the present illness in the "History of Present Illness" field.
7. Document the results of any physical exams in the "Physical Exam" field.
8. Select any relevant allergies from the "Allergies" dropdown menu.
9. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" dropdown menu.
10. Select any relevant test results from the "Test Results" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the diagnosis in the "Diagnosis" field.
12. Select any relevant treatments from the "Treatment" dropdown menu.
13. Provide any additional notes about the visit in the "Note" field.
14. Schedule follow-up appointments by entering the next visit date in the "Next Visit" field and time in the "Next Visit Time" field.
15. Document any lab results in the "Lab Results" field.
16. Document any radiology results in the "Radiology Results" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Visit Date** (`Visit Date`, date, required): Record the date of the patient's visit.
* **Physician** (`Physician`, select_one, required): Choose the attending physician's name from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical ID** (`Medical ID`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical ID number.
* **Chief Complaint** (`Chief Complaint`, text, required): Describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **History of Present Illness** (`History of Present Illness`, text, required): Record the patient's history of present illness.
* **Physical Exam** (`Physical Exam`, text, required): Document the results of any physical exams.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, select_multiple, required): Select any allergies the patient has.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, select_multiple, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Test Results** (`Test Results`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant test results.
* **Diagnosis** (`Diagnosis`, select_multiple, required): Enter the diagnosis.
* **Treatment** (`Treatment`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant treatments.
* **Note** (`Note`, text, required): Provide any additional notes about the visit.
* **Next Visit** (`Next Visit`, date, required): Schedule the next visit date.
* **Next Visit Time** (`Next Visit Time`, time, required): Schedule the next visit time.
* **Lab Results** (`Lab Results`, select_multiple, required): Document any lab results.
* **Radiology Results** (`Radiology Results`, select_multiple, required): Document any radiology results.
* **Allergies 2** (`Allergies 2`, text, optional): (Note: This field is redundant and not required)
* **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, text, optional): (Note: This field is redundant and not required)
* **Lab Results 2** (`Lab Results 2`, text, optional): (Note: This field is redundant and not required)
* **Radiology Results 2** (`Radiology Results 2`, text, optional): (Note: This field is redundant and not required)
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): This field may be used for recording the patient's medical history, but it is not required.
* **Family History** (`Family History`, text, optional): This field may be used for recording the patient's family history, but it is not required.
* **Social History** (`Social History`, text, optional): This field may be used for recording the patient's social history, but it is not required.)
