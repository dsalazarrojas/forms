# Nutrition Reform Consultation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Reform Consultation Registration Form is a tool for individuals to register their interest in consulting with a healthcare professional about nutrition-related matters. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential information from clients, ensuring a smooth and efficient consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Information section with your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address, in the Contact Details section.
3. If applicable, enter your appointment details in the Appointment Details section. Please note that this section is not required.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Registration Information (Registration Information)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: true)
This section asks for your basic contact information, such as your name, address, and other relevant details.

*   **Name (Registration Information)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Address (Registration Information)**: Provide your current address for contact and billing purposes.
*   **Phone (Registration Information)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email (Registration Information)**: Provide your email address for correspondence and appointment reminders.

### **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: true)
Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so we can reach you for any questions or appointment updates.

*   **Phone (Contact Details)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email (Contact Details)**: Provide your email address for correspondence and appointment reminders.

### **Appointment Details (Appointment Details)** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false)
If you have a specific appointment or date in mind, enter it here. Please note that this section is not required.

*   **Date (Appointment Details)**: Enter the date you would like to have your consultation.
*   **Time (Appointment Details)**: Enter the time you would prefer for your consultation.
*   **Reason (Appointment Details)**: Briefly describe the reason for your consultation.

### **Notes (Notes)** (`id: 4`, `type: note`, required: false)
Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with your healthcare provider.

*   **Notes (Notes)**: Enter any relevant comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
