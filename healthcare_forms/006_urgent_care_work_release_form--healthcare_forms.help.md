# Urgent Care Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urgent Care Work Release Form is a document used to facilitate the process of work release for patients visiting urgent care centers. It collects information from both the patient and their employer to provide an efficient and smooth work release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are visiting an urgent care center for your appointment.
* Have your supervisor or HR representative complete Section 1: Patient Information.
* Fill in Section 2: Employer's Information with your workplace details.
* In Section 3: Work Release Request, describe your reason for visiting the urgent care center and the expected duration of your absence from work.
* In Section 4: Patient Contact Information, provide any contact details you would like to share with your employer.
* Finally, sign and date Section 5: Signature, as your employer's representative and yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required):
	+ Provide your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Employer's Information** (`employer_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your company name, address, and contact details.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required):
	+ Provide your workplace address for your employer to contact you.
* **Work Release Request** (`work_release_request`, text, required):
	+ Describe the reason for your visit and the expected duration of your absence from work.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, required):
	+ Share any contact details you'd like to provide with your employer.
* **Supervisor's Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, required):
	+ Your supervisor or HR representative must sign this section to confirm the work release.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required):
	+ You, the patient, must sign this section to confirm you have provided accurate information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date the form is completed.
