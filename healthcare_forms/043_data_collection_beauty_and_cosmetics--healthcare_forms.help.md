# data_collection_beauty_and_cosmetics - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user, product, and contact information in the beauty and cosmetics industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form and fill in the required information.
2. The form consists of 3 pages: User Information, Product Information, and Contact Details.
3. Fill in the relevant details for each page, and proceed to the next page when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide your user information. You can fill in any relevant details about yourself, such as your name, email, or phone number.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, `text`, required: false): This page is for providing information about the product you are interested in or have experience with. You can fill in any details about the product, such as its name, description, or price.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This final page is for providing any additional contact details, such as your address or other relevant contact information.
