# Clinical Supervision Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Clinical Supervision Form is used to document and evaluate clinical supervision sessions between supervisors and supervisees. This form helps both parties track progress, identify areas of improvement, and set goals for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the necessary information for the session, including the supervisor and supervisee names, date of the supervision session, and type of session (individual, group, or peer).
2.  Enter a brief session overview to capture the main focus and discussion points of the session.
3.  Assess the supervisee's clinical skills, professionalism, and documentation and record keeping skills using the provided rating system (1-5).
4.  Identify key points discussed during the session.
5.  Outline action items for the next session.
6.  Record the supervisee's reflections on their learning and growth.
7.  Sign and date both the supervisor and supervisee's copies of the form to indicate completion and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your name as the supervisor.
*   **Supervisee Name** (`supervisee_name`): Enter the name of the supervisee participating in the session.
*   **Date of Supervision Session** (`session_date`): Enter the date of the supervision session.
*   **Type of Supervision Session** (`supervision_type`): Choose the type of session (individual, group, or peer).
*   **Case Reference or Patient ID** (`case_reference`): Enter a case reference or patient ID associated with the session.
*   **Clinical Competencies** (`section_competencies`): This field is a placeholder for additional notes on the session's focus areas.
*   **Assessment of Clinical Skills** (`clinical_skills_assessment`): Rate the supervisee's clinical skills using the provided rating system (1-5).
*   **Assessment of Professionalism** (`professionalism_assessment`): Rate the supervisee's professionalism using the provided rating system (1-5).
*   **Assessment of Documentation and Record Keeping** (`documentation_assessment`): Rate the supervisee's documentation and record keeping skills using the provided rating system (1-5).
*   **Key Points Discussed** (`discussion_points`): Enter key points discussed during the session.
*   **Action Items for Next Session** (`next_action_items`): Outline action items identified for the next session.
*   **Supervisee Reflections** (`supervisee_reflections`): Record the supervisee's reflections on their learning and growth.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Enter your digital signature to indicate completion and agreement.
*   **Supervisee Signature** (`supervisee_signature`): Enter the supervisee's digital signature to indicate completion and agreement.
