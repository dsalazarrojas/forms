# orthopedic_treatment_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for orthopedic treatment assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information about the patient.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (patient_name)** (<name>, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth (patient_dob)** (<name>, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Doctor's Name (doctor_name)** (<name>, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional filling out the form.
* **Patient Condition (patient_condition)** (<name>, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's condition being assessed.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the healthcare professional or the patient's medical history.
