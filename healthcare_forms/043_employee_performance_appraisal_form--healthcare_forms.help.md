# Employee Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Performance Appraisal Form is a tool used to evaluate an employee's performance over a specific period. It is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of an employee's goals, achievements, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name and job title.
2. Assess their performance score and provide comments.
3. Identify goals met and areas for improvement.
4. Select the review type (Performance, Development, or Career Development).
5. Evaluate their performance level (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Provide comments on their performance level.
7. Set the date and time of the last review.
8. Set the date and time of the next review.
9. Enter the supervisor's name and date.
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`, number, optional): Assess the employee's performance score out of 100.
* **Performance Comments** (`performance_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments on the employee's performance.
* **Goals Met** (`goals_met`, select_one, optional): Select which goals the employee has met.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Allow the employee to provide comments.
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`, date, optional): Set the date of the last review.
* **Time of Last Review** (`time_of_last_review`, time, optional): Set the time of the last review.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the review type (Performance, Development, or Career Development).
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the evaluation form was submitted.
* **Employee Performance Rating** (`employee_performance_rating`, number, optional): Assess the employee's performance rating.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, number, optional): Assess the overall performance rating.
* **Performance Rating Scale** (`performance_rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the performance rating scale.
* **Performance Rating Comments** (`performance_rating_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments on the performance rating.
* **Date of Next Review** (`date_of_next_review`, date, optional): Set the date of the next review.
* **Time of Next Review** (`time_of_next_review`, time, optional): Set the time of the next review.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Set the date the form was submitted.
* **Last Reviewed By** (`last_reviewed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last reviewed the form.
* **Last Reviewed On** (`last_reviewed_on`, date, optional): Set the date the form was last reviewed.
* **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, select_one, optional): Select the performance level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Performance Level Comments** (`performance_level_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments on the performance level.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are filled out.
- Use the form as a starting point for regular performance appraisals.
- Consider having multiple reviewers for large organizations.
