# Stem Cell Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Cell Intake Form is a comprehensive form used to collect patient information and medical history for stem cell procedures. This form is intended for patients who are undergoing stem cell therapy or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
3. Enter your date of birth and stem cell type (yes or no).
4. Specify your stem cell dose and concentration.
5. Indicate any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you have.
6. Enter your blood pressure, pulse, temperature, height, and weight.
7. Specify whether you are undergoing stem cell procedure (yes or no).
8. Identify the location of the stem cell.
9. Enter any additional stem cell notes.
10. Check whether you have insurance coverage (yes or no).
11. Enter the name and specialty of your doctor.
12. Sign the form with your signature as the patient.
13. Sign the form with your doctor's signature.
14. Provide your medical license number (not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Stem Cell Type**: Specify whether you are undergoing stem cell therapy or treatment (yes or no).
* **Stem Cell Dose**: Enter the dose of stem cells you are receiving.
* **Stem Cell Concentration**: Enter the concentration of stem cells you are receiving.
* **Medical Conditions**: Indicate any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Blood Pressure**: Enter your blood pressure measurement.
* **Pulse**: Enter your pulse measurement.
* **Temperature**: Enter your body temperature measurement in degree Fahrenheit.
* **Height**: Enter your height measurement in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight measurement in pounds or kilograms.
* **Stem Cell Procedure**: Specify whether you are undergoing stem cell procedure (yes or no).
* **Stem Cell Location**: Identify the location of the stem cell.
* **Stem Cell Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the stem cell.
* **Insurance**: Check whether you have insurance coverage (yes or no).
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Specialty**: Enter the specialty of your doctor.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form with your signature as the patient.
