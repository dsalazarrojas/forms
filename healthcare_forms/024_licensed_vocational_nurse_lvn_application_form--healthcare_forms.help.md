# Licensed Vocational Nurse (LVN) Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for a Licensed Vocational Nurse (LVN) position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your name and contact information in the **contact_info** field.
2.  Provide your personal details in the **personal_info** field.
3.  Share your work history in the **work_history** field.
4.  Describe your education background in the **education_background** field.
5.  Enter your LVN license information in the **lvn_license_info** field.
6.  List your LVN training certifications in the **lvn_training_cert** field.
7.  Indicate your current status as a licensed vocational nurse in the **lvn_status** field.
8.  Share your experience as a licensed vocational nurse in the **lvn_experience** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **contact_info**: Enter your name and contact information, such as phone number and email address.
*   **personal_info**: This field is for providing your personal details, including your date of birth and social security number.
*   **work_history**: Describe your previous work experience, including the names of employers and job titles.
*   **education_background**: Share your educational background, including institutions you have attended and degrees earned.
*   **lvn_license_info**: Enter your LVN license information, including license number and expiration date.
*   **lvn_training_cert**: List your LVN training certifications, such as ACLS or PDS certification.
*   **lvn_status**: Choose your current status as a licensed vocational nurse from the options: Active, Active (Expired), Active (Inactive), Active (Retired), Inactive, Inactive (Expired), Inactive (Retired).
*   **lvn_experience**: Share your experience as a licensed vocational nurse, including years of experience and job titles.
