# Elective Procedure Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to acknowledge and take responsibility for elective procedures performed outside of emergency situations. It is essential to fill this form to ensure that the patient understands the risks and consequences associated with the procedure and to provide an opportunity for the patient to express their consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the patient.
2. Enter your contact email address.
3. Provide a brief description of the procedure.
4. List any complications that may occur during or after the procedure.
5. Sign your name to acknowledge that you understand the risks and have taken responsibility for the procedure.
6. Enter the date and time of the procedure.
7. Enter your physician's name and title.
8. Enter the name of the hospital or facility where the procedure will be performed.
9. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are aware that the procedure may have risks associated with it.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Enter your address.
12. List any medical conditions you have that may affect the procedure.
13. Enter your age.
14. Enter the medical specialty of your physician.
15. Enter the medical group of your physician.
16. Enter the date you gave your consent for the procedure.
17. Enter the duration of the procedure.
18. Enter the time of the procedure.
19. Enter the location of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
20. Enter the phone number of the facility.
21. Provide any additional notes from the patient.
22. Provide any notes from your physician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **elective_procedure_liability_waiver_form**: This field is the title of the form. Please fill in your name as the patient to acknowledge that you are aware of the risks and consequences associated with the elective procedure.
• **patient_name**: Enter your full name as the patient.
• **contact_email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
• **procedure_description**: Provide a brief description of the elective procedure you are seeking.
• **complications**: List any complications that may occur during or after the procedure.
• **patient_signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you understand the risks and have taken responsibility for the procedure.
• **procedure_date**: Enter the date of the elective procedure.
• **physician_name**: Enter the name of your physician.
• **physician_title**: Enter the title of your physician.
• **hospital_name**: Enter the name of the hospital or facility where the procedure will be performed.
• **facility_name**: Enter the name of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
• **procedure_type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are aware that the procedure may have risks associated with it.
• **patient_phone**: Enter your phone number.
• **patient_address**: Enter your address.
• **medical_condition**: List any medical conditions you have that may affect the procedure.
• **patient_age**: Enter your age.
• **medical_specialty**: Enter the medical specialty of your physician.
• **medical_group**: Enter the medical group of your physician.
• **consent_date**: Enter the date you gave your consent for the procedure.
• **procedure_duration**: Enter the duration of the procedure.
• **procedure_time**: Enter the time of the procedure.
• **facility_location**: Enter the location of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
• **facility_phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
• **patient_notes**: Provide any additional notes from the patient.
• **physician_notes**: Provide any notes from your physician.
