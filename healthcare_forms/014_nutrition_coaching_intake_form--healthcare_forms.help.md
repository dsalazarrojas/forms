# Nutrition Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's health and wellness goals, medical history, and other relevant details to create a personalized nutrition plan.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to provide the nutrition coach with a clear understanding of your health and wellness goals.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult your primary healthcare provider for guidance.
- This form is intended for general information purposes only and should not be considered a comprehensive medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): 
  This field is intended to collect general information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and contact details. Please enter your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): 
  This field is for you to describe any pre-existing medical conditions or health issues that may affect your nutrition plan. Please be as detailed as possible, but do not worry about providing an exhaustive list.

* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Check all the foods, ingredients, or substances that you are allergic to or have sensitivities to. This information will help the nutrition coach avoid recommending foods that may cause adverse reactions.

* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): 
  Describe any dietary restrictions or preferences you have, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or dairy-free diets. This will help the nutrition coach create a personalized plan that meets your needs.

* **Nutrition Plan** (`nutrition_plan`, text, optional): 
  Describe your short-term and long-term nutrition goals, including any specific health outcomes you aim to achieve. This information will help the nutrition coach create a targeted plan for you.

* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): 
  List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, heart disease, or obesity. This information will help the nutrition coach create a plan that takes into account your unique health needs.

* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): 
  List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies. This information will help the nutrition coach identify potential interactions or sensitivities.

* **Medical Devices** (`medical_devices`, text, optional): 
  List any medical devices you use, such as pacemakers, insulin pumps, or hearing aids. This information will help the nutrition coach avoid advising against any devices that may be impacted by your diet.

## Tips

- Be honest and thorough when filling out this form to ensure the nutrition coach has a clear understanding of your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask the nutrition coach.
- This form is intended to be a starting point for your nutrition coaching relationship. The nutrition coach will work with you to create a personalized plan that meets your unique needs and goals.
