# Acupuncture Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture Consultation Form is used to gather essential information about a patient's health status, medical history, and reasons for seeking acupuncture treatment. This form helps our acupuncturists to prepare for and provide personalized care during the initial consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the personal details section.
2.  Provide your full name, date of birth, and biological sex.
3.  If applicable, describe your occupation and any relevant lifestyle factors.
4.  In the health profile section, explain your primary reason for seeking acupuncture treatment and any secondary health concerns or symptoms you are experiencing.
5.  Rate your general energy levels and sleep quality.
6.  Indicate your level of stress.
7.  If you have any issues with your digestive health or circulation, describe them in detail.
8.  List any other therapies or treatments you are currently receiving.
9.  Optionally, choose a preferred date for your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Biological Sex** (`sex`, `select_one`, required): Choose your biological sex for TCM diagnostic purposes.
*   **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): Describe your occupation or any relevant lifestyle factors that may affect your health.
*   **What is your primary reason for seeking acupuncture?** (`primary_reason`, `text`, required): Explain why you want to try acupuncture, e.g., pain management, stress relief, etc.
*   **Secondary health concerns or symptoms** (`secondary_concerns`, `text`, optional): Describe any secondary health issues or symptoms you are experiencing.
*   **General Energy Levels (1-10)** (`energy_levels`, `number`, required): Rate your general energy levels from 1 (very low) to 10 (high).
*   **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose your sleep quality from the options provided.
*   **General Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of stress from the options provided.
*   **Digestive Health** (`digestive_health`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues with your digestive health.
*   **Do you experience cold hands or feet?** (`circulation_issues`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you experience cold hands or feet, or never experience this symptom.
*   **Other therapies or treatments currently receiving** (`current_treatments`, `text`, optional): List any other therapies or treatments you are currently receiving.
*   **Preferred Date for Consultation** (`preferred_consult_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a preferred date and time for your consultation, if applicable.
*   **How did you hear about our acupuncture services?** (`discovery_source`, `text`, optional): Tell us how you became aware of our acupuncture services.

**Tips**

*   Please be as detailed as possible in your answers, as this will help us provide you with personalized care during your consultation.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
*   Remember to choose a date and time for your consultation that works best for you.
