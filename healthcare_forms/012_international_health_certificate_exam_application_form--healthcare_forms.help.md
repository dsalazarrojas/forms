International Health Certificate Exam Application Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
This form is intended for applicants who are applying for an International Health Certificate Exam. It collects information required for the exam application process. Please ensure you have all necessary documents and information before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Check the box on the first page if you have the required medical certificates or other documents.
2.  Fill in your applicant information on the second page, including any relevant medical history.
3.  Choose a date for your medical examination.
4.  Indicate whether the exam requirements are met.
5.  Specify the location of the exam.
6.  Upload any required medical certificates.
7.  Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **International Health Certificate Exam Application Form**: This page is for applicants to acknowledge that they have all the necessary documents and information for the exam.
### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information**: Please provide your name, address, phone number and email address.
### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Please provide detailed medical history. This information will be used for the exam evaluation process.
### Date
* **Date**: Please select a date for your medical examination.
### Exam Requirements
* **Exam Requirements**: Please indicate whether the exam requirements are met. If you're unsure, please consult with your healthcare provider.
### Exam Location
* **Exam Location**: Please specify the location of the exam. This could be your office or home.
### Medical Certificates
* **Medical Certificates**: Please upload required medical certificates or other documents. This could include vaccination records, medical records, or other relevant documents.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide the name, phone number and relationship to the applicant for an emergency contact person.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to upload required documents and provide clear information in the fields.
* Review your information carefully before submission.
* Ensure you meet all the exam requirements before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts, please consult with your healthcare provider.
