# telemedicine_consultant_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telemedicine consultant evaluation form is used to collect feedback on the performance of telemedicine consultants. The form helps evaluate the consultant's communication skills, overall experience, and likelihood of recommendation. It also captures information on the time spent on calls and follow-ups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Telemedicine Consultant**: Choose the telemedicine consultant whose performance you are evaluating.
2. **Provide a Rating**: Rate the consultant's performance on a scale of 1-5.
3. **Provide Feedback**: Enter your comments or feedback on the consultant's performance.
4. **Enter Telemedicine Consultant ID**: Enter the ID of the telemedicine consultant.
5. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
6. **Time Spent on Call**: If applicable, enter the time spent on the call.
7. **Time Spent on Follow-ups**: If applicable, enter the time spent on follow-ups.
8. **Communication Skills**: Select the consultant's communication skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. **Overall Experience**: Rate the overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
10. **Likely to Recommend**: Indicate if you would likely recommend the consultant (True, False, Undecided).
11. **Email**: Enter the consultant's email address.
12. **Phone Number**: Enter the consultant's phone number.
13. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.
14. **Follow-up Frequency**: Select the frequency of follow-ups (Less than once a month, Once a month, Multiple times a month, More than once a week).
15. **Assigned to**: Select the type of healthcare professional assigned to (Primary Care Physician, Specialist, Other, Multiple).
