# x_ray_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for billing purposes to collect information about X-ray services provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Info** field.
2. Provide the relevant insurance information in the **Insurance** field, if applicable.
3. Specify the service details in the **Service Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (Patient Info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or any other relevant information about the patient.
* **Insurance (Insurance)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance information, including policy number, provider name, or any other relevant details.
* **Service Details (Service Details)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the X-ray service provided, including the date and time of the service, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure efficient and accurate billing processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
