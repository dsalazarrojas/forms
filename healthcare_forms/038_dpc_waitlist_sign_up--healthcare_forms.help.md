# Dpc Waitlist Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dpc Waitlist Sign Up" form is a registration process for patients or healthcare providers to sign up for or register on a waitlist for various medical services, appointments, or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all the required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, which includes:
	* Patient Information (page 1)
	* Contact Information (page 2)
	* Medical Information (page 3)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to collect the patient's name and contact information. 
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): This is a field to describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have medical insurance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Primary Care Provider** (`primary_care_provider`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a primary care provider, 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are part of a medical group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are part of a medical team, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to be contacted through this method, 'No' otherwise.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred language.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional medical history you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority for assistance.
