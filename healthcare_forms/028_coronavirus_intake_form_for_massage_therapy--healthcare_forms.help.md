# coronavirus_intake_form_for_massage_therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus_intake_form_for_massage_therapy is designed to collect information about a patient's health status, medical history, and treatment preferences for massage therapy. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential details to provide safe and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and select "I have read and understand the information provided" under the "massage_therapy_agreement" field.
2. Fill out the "massage_therapy_date_of_last_massage" field with the date of your last massage therapy session.
3. In the "massage_therapy_number_of_massage_session" field, enter the number of massage sessions you have had in the past.
4. Select your symptoms from the list in the "massage_therapy_symptoms" field. 
5. Choose the body parts that were massaged in the "massage_therapy_body_part_massaged" field.
6. Select your temperature from the list in the "massage_therapy_temperature" field.
7. Provide your medical conditions, if any, in the "massage_therapy_medical_conditions" field.
8. Enter any medications you are taking in the "massage_therapy_medication" field.
9. List any previous medical conditions in the "massage_therapy_previous_medical_conditions" field.
10. Describe any previous medical procedures in the "massage_therapy_previous_medical_procedure" field.
11. Enter details about previous massage sessions, including date, number of sessions, and any notable events or issues that occurred during these sessions.
12. Provide your massage treatment plan and goals in the "massage_therapy_massage_treatment_plan" field.
13. Enter the outcome of your previous massage sessions in the "massage_therapy_massage_treatment_outcome" field.
14. Specify your medical supervision preferences, including any medical conditions that require supervision.
15. Enter details about your medical supervision needs, if applicable.
16. Provide emergency contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
17. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact, if applicable.
