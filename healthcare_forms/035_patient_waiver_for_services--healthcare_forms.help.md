# Patient Waiver For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to acknowledge and agree to the risks associated with a treatment or service. It is a consent form that requires the patient to provide their medical history, declare any allergies or risks they are aware of, and consent to treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the patient service agreement carefully and acknowledge the risks associated with the procedure.
2.  Check the "Risk Associated with Procedure" field to declare any risks you are aware of.
3.  In the "Consent to Treatment" field, select "yes" if you agree to receive treatment.
4.  In the "Medical History" field, provide a brief description of your medical history.
5.  In the "Allergies" field, select any allergies you are aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Service Agreement** (`patient_service_agreement`, note, optional)
    This is a note field where you can read and acknowledge the risks associated with the procedure. Please read this carefully before proceeding.
*   **Risk Associated with Procedure** (`risk_associated_with_procedure`, text, optional)
    This field is for you to declare any risks you are aware of associated with the procedure. Please be specific and detailed in your response.
*   **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, optional)
    Select "yes" if you agree to receive treatment. This is a required field.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
    Provide a brief description of your medical history. This will help the healthcare provider understand your medical background.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select any allergies you are aware of. Please be specific and detailed in your response.

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any question or have any questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
