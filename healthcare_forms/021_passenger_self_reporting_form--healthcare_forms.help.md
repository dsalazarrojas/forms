# Passenger Self Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers about their travel experiences, including their travel agency, flight details, and health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your travel agency from the list of options provided.
2. Choose all the airports you visited during your travel.
3. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing or experienced during your travel.
4. Enter a valid email address.
5. Enter your contact phone number.
6. Select a travel date using the date picker.
7. Enter the travel time using the time picker.
8. If you want to provide additional information about your travel location, you can enter it in the text field.
9. If you want to provide additional information about your airline, you can enter it in the text field.
10. If you want to provide additional information about your travel duration, you can enter it in the text field.
11. If you want to provide additional information about your travel agency type, you can select an option from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your travel agency?**: Select your travel agency from the list of options.
* **What airports did you travel from?**: Choose all the airports you visited during your travel.
* **Describe your symptoms**: Enter your symptoms, e.g. fever, cough, sore throat, etc.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Travel date**: Select a travel date using the date picker.
* **Travel time**: Enter the travel time using the time picker.
* **Travel location**: If you want to provide additional information about your travel location, enter it in the text field. (Optional)
* **Airline**: If you want to provide additional information about your airline, enter it in the text field. (Optional)
* **Flight number**: If you want to provide additional information about your flight number, enter it in the text field. (Optional)
* **Travel duration**: If you want to provide additional information about your travel duration, enter it in the text field. (Optional)
* **Travel agency type**: If you want to provide additional information about your travel agency type, select an option from the list. (Optional)
