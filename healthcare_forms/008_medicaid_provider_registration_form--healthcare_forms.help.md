# medicaid_provider_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for healthcare providers to register their information for Medicaid purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "provider_details" field with your provider's details.
2. Provide your "provider_credentials" in the second field.
3. Enter your "provider_info" in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **provider_details**: Enter your provider's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **provider_credentials**: Enter your provider's credentials, such as NPI or EIN numbers.
* **provider_info**: Enter any additional information about your provider that you feel is relevant to your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your Medicaid administrator or supervisor for guidance.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
