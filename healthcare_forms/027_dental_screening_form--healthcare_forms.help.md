# Dental Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Screening Form is a patient information collection tool used by healthcare professionals to gather essential details about a patient's medical history, allergies, and previous procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please ask for assistance from a healthcare professional.
4. Submit the form to the designated healthcare professional.
