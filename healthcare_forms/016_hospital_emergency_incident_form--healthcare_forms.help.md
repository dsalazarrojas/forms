# Hospital Emergency Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to document emergency incidents that occur within a hospital setting. It captures essential information about the patient's general information, incident details, treatment procedures, and medical staff involved in the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the incident.
2.  Ensure that you provide the necessary details about the patient's general information, incident details, treatment procedures, and medical staff involved in the incident.
3.  If a field is not applicable or not relevant to the incident, leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" (Not Applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information**: This field is used to capture basic information about the patient, such as name, age, and medical record number. Please provide the patient's name, age, and medical record number if applicable.
*   **Patient Information**: This field is used to gather more detailed information about the patient, including address, contact details, and medical history. Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
*   **Incident Details**: This field is used to describe the incident that occurred, including the date, time, location, and nature of the incident. Please provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
*   **Treatment Procedure**: This field is used to document any medical treatment or procedures performed on the patient, including medications, tests, or surgeries. Please include all relevant information about the treatment or procedures.
*   **Outcome**: This field is used to report the patient's outcome, including their condition, recovery, or any complications. Please ensure that you provide an accurate and detailed description of the patient's outcome.
*   **Medical Staff**: This field is used to capture information about the medical staff involved in the incident, including their name, title, and contact details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the medical staff.
*   **Medication Administered**: This field is used to document any medications given to the patient, including dosage, time, and frequency. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the medication administered.
*   **Incident Reporter**: This field is used to capture the name and contact information of the person reporting the incident. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the incident reporter.
*   **Medical History**: This field is used to gather information about the patient's medical history, including previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the patient's medical history.
*   **Medical Condition**: This field is used to document the patient's medical condition at the time of the incident, including any pre-existing medical conditions. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's medical condition.
*   **Patient Consent**: This field is used to record the patient's consent to treatment or procedures, including any relevant documentation or signatures. Please ensure that you obtain and document the patient's consent before providing treatment or procedures.
*   **Emergency Services**: This field is used to report any emergency services provided to the patient, including CPR, defibrillator, or other emergency interventions. Please provide accurate and complete information about the emergency services provided.
*   **Facility Response**: This field is used to document the facility's response to the incident, including any relevant protocols, procedures, or resources used. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the facility's response.
*   **Follow-up**: This field is used to schedule or document follow-up appointments, treatments, or procedures for the patient. Please ensure that you schedule or document follow-up appointments, treatments, or procedures as necessary.
*   **Reporting Doctor**: This field is used to capture the name and contact information of the doctor reporting the incident. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the reporting doctor.
*   **Reporting Doctor Name**: This field is used to capture the name of the doctor reporting the incident. Please ensure that you provide the correct name of the doctor.
*   **Reporting Date**: This field is used to capture the date the incident was reported. Please ensure that you provide the correct date of the incident report.
*   **Reporting Time**: This field is used to capture the time the incident was reported. Please ensure that you provide the correct time of the incident report.
*   **Reporting Doctor Specialty**: This field is used to capture the specialty of the doctor reporting the incident. Please ensure that you provide the correct specialty of the doctor.
*   **Reporting Doctor Phone**: This field is used to capture the phone number of the doctor reporting the incident. Please ensure that you provide the correct phone number of the doctor.
*   **Reporting Doctor Email**: This field is used to capture the email of the doctor reporting the incident. Please ensure that you provide the correct email of the doctor.
*   **Reporting Doctor Facility**: This field is used to capture the facility where the incident was reported. Please ensure that you provide the correct facility of the incident report.
*   **Reporting Doctor Department**: This field is used to capture the department where the incident was reported. Please ensure that you provide the correct department of the incident report.
*   **Reporting Doctor Room**: This field is used to capture the room where the incident was reported. Please ensure that you provide the correct room of the incident report.
*   **Reporting Doctor Note**: This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments from the doctor reporting the incident. Please ensure that you provide any relevant and accurate notes or comments.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is intended to provide clear and practical instructions for filling out the form. If you are unsure about any field or requirement, please consult with the relevant medical staff or administration before completing the form.
