# Health Screening Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Inquiry Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect important health screening information about patients prior to treatment or medical procedures. This form ensures that the patient's health and safety are considered in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections and questions carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2.  Answer all questions in a clear and concise manner.
3.  Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., "Yes" or "No") unless otherwise indicated.
4.  Ensure all required fields are filled out before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (1): 
	+ Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition or reason for visit.
* **Screening Reasons** (2): 
	+ Select "Yes" if the patient has any screening reason(s) for their visit, such as allergies, medications, or medical conditions.
* **Medical History** (3): 
	+ Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Allergies** (4): 
	+ Select "Yes" if the patient has any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications** (5): 
	+ List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Contact Info** (6): 
	+ Select "Yes" if the patient has any contact information (e.g., phone number, email) that may be relevant to their health.
* **Medical Conditions** (7): 
	+ Select "Yes" if the patient has any medical conditions (e.g., diabetes, high blood pressure).
* **Family History** (8): 
	+ Select "Yes" if there are any family medical histories that may be relevant to the patient's health.
* **Patient History** (9): 
	+ Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical procedures or treatments.
* **Next Visits** (10): 
	+ Enter the date of the next scheduled visit or appointment.
* **Follow-up Info** (11): 
	+ Provide any follow-up information or instructions for the patient.
* **Medical Team** (12): 
	+ Select "Yes" if the patient has a specific medical team assigned to their care.
* **Medical Facility** (13): 
	+ Enter the name of the medical facility where the patient will be treated.
* **Medical Notes** (14): 
	+ Provide any additional medical notes or comments about the patient.

## Tips
* Always ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all relevant information is included.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure the information is easy to read and understand.
* If unsure about any questions or fields, consult with the patient or a healthcare professional.
* Ensure patient consent is obtained for the completion of this form.
