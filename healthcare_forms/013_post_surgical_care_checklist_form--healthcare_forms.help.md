# Post Surgical Care Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Surgical Care Checklist Form is designed to gather essential information from patients after a surgical procedure. This form helps healthcare professionals ensure that patients receive proper care and instructions following their surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal information on page 1.
2. Record the patient's vital signs on page 2.
3. Check the post-op care checklist on page 3.
4. List any medications the patient is currently taking on page 4.
5. Identify any allergies the patient has on page 5.
6. Provide post-operative care instructions on page 6.
7. Enter the patient's discharge information on page 7.
8. Schedule a follow-up visit on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This section is for collecting the patient's personal details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs, such as temperature, blood pressure, and pulse.
* **Post Op Care Checklist** (`post_op_care_checklist`, text, optional): Check off the post-operative care instructions that have been provided to the patient.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select multiple, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including pain medication, antibiotics, blood thinners, and antihistamines.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select one, optional): Identify any allergies the patient has, such as peanuts, shellfish, soy, or pollen.
* **Post-Op Care Instructions** (`post_op_care_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any additional post-operative care instructions that the patient needs to follow.
* **Patient Discharge Info** (`patient_discharge_info`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the patient's discharge from the hospital.
* **Follow-Up Visit Info** (`follow_up_visit_info`, select one, optional): Schedule a follow-up visit for the patient, either as a routine or emergency visit.
