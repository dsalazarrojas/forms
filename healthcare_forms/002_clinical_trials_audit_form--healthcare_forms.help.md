# Clinical Trials Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the results of clinical trials audits, providing a structured format for reporting key findings and compliance status. The audit is conducted by a lead auditor, and its purpose is to evaluate the conduct of clinical trials in accordance with regulatory requirements and institutional review board (IRB) guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start with the audit details section, where you will enter the study name, protocol number, and audit date.
2.  In the "Study name" field, enter the full protocol or study title.
3.  In the "Sponsor protocol identifier" field, enter the sponsor's protocol identifier.
4.  In the "Audit date" field, select the date of the audit.
5.  In the "Lead auditor's name" field, enter the full name of the lead auditor.
6.  In the "Site location" field, enter the name of the clinic or facility where the audit was conducted.
7.  In the "Findings summary" field, provide a brief summary of the key observations made during the audit.
8.  In the "Compliance status" field, select the overall compliance status (e.g., compliant, minor non-compliance, major non-compliance, not applicable).
9.  In the "Recommended corrective actions" field, enter any recommended corrective actions and deadlines.
10. Attach any relevant documents or photos to support your findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Audit Details -- (id: 1)**: This section is a general note about the audit details.
*   **Study name (id: 2)**: Enter the full protocol or study title for the clinical trial.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Protocol number (id: 3)**: Enter the sponsor's protocol identifier.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: No.
*   **Audit date (id: 4)**: Select the date of the audit.
    *   Type: Date field.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Auditor name (id: 5)**: Enter the full name of the lead auditor.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Site location (id: 6)**: Enter the name of the clinic or facility where the audit was conducted.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Findings summary (id: 7)**: Provide a brief summary of the key observations made during the audit.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Compliance rating (id: 8)**: Select the overall compliance status.
    *   Type: Select One field.
    *   Options: Compliant, Minor non-compliance, Major non-compliance, Not applicable.
    *   Required: Yes.
*   **Recommended corrective actions (id: 9)**: Enter any recommended corrective actions and deadlines.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: No.
*   **Attachments or evidence (id: 10)**: Reference any attached documents or photos to support your findings.
    *   Type: Text field.
    *   Required: No.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Attach relevant documents or photos to support your findings.
- Use clear and concise language in the "Findings summary" field.
- Ensure the "Compliance rating" field is selected from the provided options.
