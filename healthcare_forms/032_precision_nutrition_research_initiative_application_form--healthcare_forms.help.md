# precision_nutrition_research_initiative_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Precision Nutrition Research Initiative application form is designed to gather information from researchers applying to the Precision Nutrition Research Initiative. This form helps in understanding the focus, funding, and status of various research projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the project information (fields 1-3).
2. Select the research team members (field 4).
3. Provide project details (field 5).
4. Specify the principal investigator (field 6).
5. Enter your email address (field 7).
6. Provide your institution's name (field 8).
7. Enter your contact phone number (field 9).
8. Choose the research area (field 10).
9. Select the research focus (field 11-13).
10. Specify any other research focus (field 14).
11. Select the funding source (field 15-18).
12. Enter any other funding source (field 19-21).
13. Choose the project status (field 22).
14. Select the project status (field 23-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Information (1)** (project_info, text, required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Research Team (2)** (research_team, text, required): List the names and titles of team members.
* **Project Details (3)** (project_details, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Principal Investigator (4)** (principal_investigator, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Email (5)** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Institution (6)** (institution, text, required): Enter your institution's name.
* **Contact Phone (7)** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Research Area (8)** (research_area, select_one, required): Choose the research area that best describes your project.
* **Research Focus (9)** (research_focus, select_one, required): Select the primary research focus of your project.
	+ **Human Nutrition**: Research on human nutrition, including dietary habits and nutritional science.
	+ **Nutrigenomics**: Research on the genetic basis of human nutrition.
	+ **Other**: If your research does not fit into the above categories, select "Other" and provide a detailed description in field 10.
* **Other Research Focus (10)** (research_focus_other, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in field 9, provide a detailed description of your research focus.
* **Funding Source (11)** (funding_source, select_one, required): Select the funding source of your project.
	+ **Yes**: If you are funded by the selected source.
	+ **No**: If you are not funded by the selected source.
* **Funding Source Other (12)** (funding_source_other, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" in field 11, enter the name of the funding source.
* **Funding Source (13)** (funding_source_grant, select_one, required): Select whether your project has received a grant.
	+ **Yes**: If your project has received a grant.
	+ **No**: If your project has not received a grant.
* **Funding Source (14)** (funding_source_industry, select_one, required): Select whether your project has industry funding.
	+ **Yes**: If your project has industry funding.
	+ **No**: If your project has no industry funding.
* **Project Status (15)** (project_status, select_one, required): Choose the current project status.
	+ **Active**: If your project is active and ongoing.
	+ **Inactive**: If your project is inactive and not ongoing.
* **Project Status (16)** (project_status_active, select_one, optional): Select whether your project is active.
	+ **Yes**: If your project is active.
	+ **No**: If your project is not active.
* **Project Status (17)** (project_status_inactive, select_one, optional): Select whether your project is inactive.
	+ **Yes**: If your project is inactive.
	+ **No**: If your project is active.

Note: The optional fields in the form are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field-by-field explanation.
