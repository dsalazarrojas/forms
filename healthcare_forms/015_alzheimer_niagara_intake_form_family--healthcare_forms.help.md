# Alzheimer Niagara Intake Form Family - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alzheimer Niagara Intake Form Family is a comprehensive form designed for family members or caregivers to gather information about a loved one affected by Alzheimer's disease or other dementias. This form helps healthcare providers understand the client's needs and provide personalized care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Read each section carefully**: Review each section and its corresponding fields to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. **Provide accurate and complete information**: Enter accurate and complete information about the client, including their personal details, contact information, and medical history.
3. **Select relevant services**: If applicable, select all support services that the client may need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name** (required): Enter the full legal name of the person affected by Alzheimer's disease or other dementias.
* **Date of birth** (required): Enter the client's date of birth in the Day month and year format (e.g., 12 1990).
* **Primary contact name** (required): Enter the name of the family member or caregiver responsible for providing information about the client.
* **Primary contact phone** (required): Enter the best number to reach the caregiver or family member.
* **Diagnosis date** (optional): If known, enter the approximate date of diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease or other dementias.
* **Support services needed** (optional): Select all support services that may be required for the client, such as:
	+ Day programs
	+ Respite care
	+ Counseling
	+ Home support
* **Medical notes** (optional): Enter any relevant medical information or medications about the client.
* **Consent to contact** (required): Select **True** to allow healthcare providers to contact the caregiver or family member for further information or support.

## Tips
* Ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible care and support for the client.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the healthcare provider for assistance.
* This form is confidential and will be stored securely to ensure the client's information remains private.
