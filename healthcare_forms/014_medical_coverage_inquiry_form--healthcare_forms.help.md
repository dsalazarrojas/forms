# medical_coverage_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and medical coverage details for potential clients or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Information page with your details.
2. Proceed to the Medical Information page and fill out the required information.
3. On the Insurance Info page, provide your insurance details if applicable.
4. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, required):
	+ Fill in your personal details, including any relevant identifying information.
* **Medical Information** (Medical Information, text, required):
	+ Provide any relevant medical history or current conditions that may affect your medical coverage or insurance requirements.
* **Insurance Info** (Insurance Info, text, required):
	+ If applicable, enter your insurance provider details, such as your insurance card number, policy number, or other relevant information.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, not required):
	+ Click this button to submit your completed form for review and further action.
