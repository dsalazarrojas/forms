# Food Insecurity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a household's food security level, which is essential for identifying areas where support is needed to ensure access to affordable and nutritious food.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the pages presented to you.
2. Please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. The form consists of multiple pages, so please follow the instructions to ensure you complete it in the correct order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Household Income Range
* **Household Income Range** (`household_income_range`, number, optional): This field is used to determine the current income range of the household. Please provide a rough estimate of the household's monthly income.

### Page 2: Household Size
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, text, optional): This field is used to determine the number of people living in the household. Please provide the actual number of members in the household.

### Page 3: Food Security Level
* **Food Security Level** (`food_security_level`, select_one, optional): This field is used to determine the level of food security for the household. Please select the option that best represents your household's current situation:
	+ 1 - Very Low
	+ 2 - Low
	+ 3 - Moderate
	+ 4 - High
	+ 5 - Very High

### Page 4: Household Location
* **Household Location** (`household_location`, text, optional): This field is used to determine the location of the household. Please provide a brief description of the household's address or location.

### Page 5: Food Insecurity Level
* **Household Food Insecurity Level** (`food_insecurity_level`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to determine the level of food insecurity for the household. Please select all options that apply to your household:
	+ 1 - Food Insecure
	+ 2 - Food Secure
	+ 3 - Somewhat Food Insecure

## Tips
* Please answer the questions carefully and honestly to provide accurate insights.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide the best answer you can.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
