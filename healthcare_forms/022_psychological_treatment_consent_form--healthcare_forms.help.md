# Psychological Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from a client for psychological treatment. It gathers essential information about the client, including their name, contact details, and medical history, as well as the treatment plan and provider's information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2.  **Client Signature Date**: The date the client signed the form.
3.  **Informed Consent**: Check 'Yes' if the client has given informed consent for treatment.
4.  **Provider Signature**: The date and time you sign the form, indicating your agreement to provide care.
5.  **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the client's emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the client.
6.  **Treatmetn Duration**: The number of sessions or days planned for the treatment.
7.  **Session Schedule**: Enter any scheduled sessions or appointments.
8.  **Contact Info**: Enter any contact details for the client or client's contact person.
9.  **Medical History**: Describe the client's medical history, if applicable.
10.  **Allergies**: List any allergies the client may have.
11.  **Medication**: List any medication the client is currently taking.
12.  **Insurance**: Enter any insurance information for the client.
13.  **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the client or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
*  **Treatmetn Duration** (`treatment_duration`, number, required): The number of sessions or days planned for the treatment.
*  **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select\_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if the client has given informed consent for treatment.
*  **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, note, required): The date and time you sign the form, indicating your agreement to provide care.
*  **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the client's emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the client.
*  **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
*  **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Describe the emergency contact's relationship to the client.
*  **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Describe the client's medical history, if applicable.
*  **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the client may have.
*  **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medication the client is currently taking.
*  **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, optional): Enter any insurance information for the client.
*  **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes about the client.
*  **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the client or treatment.
