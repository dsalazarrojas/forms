# Doctor Bio Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doctor Bio Collection Form collects basic information about a doctor's bio, including first name, last name, email, phone number, and bio.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the doctor's first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter the doctor's last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter the doctor's email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the doctor's phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the doctor's bio in the Bio field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the doctor's bio.

## Tips

- Double-check the entered information for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure the doctor's permission is obtained before collecting and storing their information.
- Use this form for new doctor additions or updates only to avoid duplicate entries.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the form owner or administrator for assistance.
