# Coronavirus 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about individuals who have been exposed to or infected with COVID-19. The form helps healthcare professionals assess the risk level of exposure and gather vital information to provide proper treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the medical organization from the list provided.
2. Choose all the symptoms you have experienced, if any.
3. Enter the date and location of exposure to COVID-19.
4. Enter your travel history, including travel destinations and dates.
5. Enter any previous medical conditions you may have.
6. Provide contact information for an emergency contact.
7. Choose the relationship of the emergency contact.
8. Enter the contact's phone number and address.
9. Enter your name, phone number, and address.
10. Enter your birth date.
11. Select your gender.
12. Enter your age.
13. Enter your height and weight.
14. Select the exposure risk level of COVID-19.
15. If applicable, choose the country and airport you traveled to.
16. Finally, provide any other symptoms you are experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Organization** (`medical_organization`): Select the medical organization where you were exposed to or infected with COVID-19. This could be a hospital, clinic, or private practice.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Choose all the symptoms you have experienced, such as fever, cough, fatigue, sore throat, runny nose, or headache.
* **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`): Enter the date you were exposed to COVID-19.
* **Exposure Location** (`exposure_location`): Enter the location where you were exposed to COVID-19.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`): Enter any travel history you have, including destinations and dates.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`): If you traveled recently, select the destination city from the list provided.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`): Enter the date of your last travel.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Enter any previous medical conditions you may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Provide the contact information for an emergency contact, such as a friend or family member.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`): Choose the relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your name.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter your address.
* **Patient Birth Date** (`patient_birth_date`): Enter your birth date.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select your gender from the list provided.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`): Enter your age.
* **Patient Height** (`patient_height`): Enter your height.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`): Enter your weight.
* **Exposure Risk Level** (`exposure_risk_level`): Select the risk level of exposure to COVID-19 from the list provided.
* **Travel Country** (`travel_country`): If you traveled recently, select the country of travel from the list provided.
* **Travel Airport** (`travel_airport`): If you traveled recently, select the airport of travel from the list provided.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`): Enter any other symptoms you are experiencing that are not listed.
