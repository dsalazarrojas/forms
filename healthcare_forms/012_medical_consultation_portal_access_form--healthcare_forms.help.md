# Medical Consultation Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients access their medical consultation portal account. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First name.
2. Enter your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Medical record number.
6. Enter your Date of birth (dd/mm/yyyy).
7. Enter the Doctor's name associated with your medical record.
8. Enter any relevant Medical history.
9. Enter any relevant Medical condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Medical record number** (text): Enter your unique medical record number.
* **Date of birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Doctor name** (text): Enter the name of the doctor associated with your medical record.
* **Medical history** (note): Enter any relevant medical history that may impact your treatment.
* **Medical condition** (text): Enter any relevant medical condition that may impact your treatment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently to avoid any issues with accessing your medical records.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit.
