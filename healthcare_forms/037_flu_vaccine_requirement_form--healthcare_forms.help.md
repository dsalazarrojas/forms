# Flu Vaccine Requirement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information from patients about their flu vaccine requirements, including the type of vaccine administered, healthcare provider, and more. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your healthcare provider has the necessary information to provide optimal care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Flu Vaccine Type**: Select the type of flu vaccine that was administered to the patient from the dropdown menu.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
3. **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who administered the flu vaccine from the dropdown menu.
5. **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
6. **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
7. **Flu Vaccine Status**: Indicate the flu vaccine status by selecting from the following options:
	* True: The patient has received the flu vaccine.
	* False: The patient has not received the flu vaccine.
	* Not Required: The patient is not required to have the flu vaccine.
	* Requires Referral: The patient requires referral for the flu vaccine.
8. **Note**: Provide any additional information or notes about the patient's flu vaccine status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flu Vaccine Type**: This field captures the type of flu vaccine administered to the patient.
* **Patient Name**: This field requires the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: This field captures the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered**: This field is not required, but if completed, it will capture the date the flu vaccine was administered.
* **Flu Vaccine Next Administered**: This field is not required, but if completed, it will capture the date the next flu vaccine was administered.
* **Healthcare Provider**: This field captures the name of the healthcare provider who administered the flu vaccine.
* **Patient Phone**: This field captures the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: This field captures the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Flu Vaccine Status**: This field requires the selection of the flu vaccine status from the provided options.
* **Note**: This field provides an opportunity to add any additional information or notes about the patient's flu vaccine status. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If the patient has not received the flu vaccine, select the appropriate status in the "Flu Vaccine Status" field.
* If the patient requires referral for the flu vaccine, select the "Requires Referral" option in the "Flu Vaccine Status" field.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the healthcare provider for clarification.
