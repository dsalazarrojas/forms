# shelter_resident_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Resident Participation Consent Form is a document used by shelters to gather essential information from residents regarding their medical conditions, emergency contacts, and participation in activities. This form is essential for the shelter staff to understand the resident's needs, provide appropriate care, and ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first section, "Resident Details," with your personal information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Emergency Contacts" section, list the names and contact information of any emergency contacts you have, such as family members or friends.
3. In the "Signed Consent" section, indicate whether you give your consent to participate in activities and programs offered by the shelter.
4. In the "Activity Programs" section, select which activities you are interested in participating in.
5. In the "Signed Consent Activities" section, indicate which activities you have signed up for.
6. In the "Emergency Contacts Relationships" section, describe your relationship with each emergency contact.
7. In the "Medical Conditions" section, list any medical conditions you have that may affect your stay or treatment at the shelter.
8. In the "Medical History" section, provide any relevant medical history that may impact your stay at the shelter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Details** (text): Fill in your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contacts** (text): List the names and contact information of any emergency contacts you have, such as family members or friends.
* **Signed Consent** (text): Indicate whether you give your consent to participate in activities and programs offered by the shelter.
* **Activity Programs** (select multiple): Select the activities you are interested in participating in from the options provided.
* **Signed Consent Activities** (text): Indicate which activities you have signed up for.
* **Emergency Contacts Relationships** (text): Describe your relationship with each emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Medical Conditions** (text): List any medical conditions you have that may affect your stay or treatment at the shelter.
* **Medical History** (text): Provide any relevant medical history that may impact your stay at the shelter.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can fill out as much or as little information as you need to. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the shelter staff for assistance.
