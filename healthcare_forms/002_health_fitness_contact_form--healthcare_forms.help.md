# Health & Fitness Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from individuals who need health and fitness advice or guidance from healthcare professionals. It is meant to be a starting point for healthcare providers to offer personalized advice and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. If applicable, indicate if you have an advisor in the "Advisor" field.
6. If necessary, describe any problematic areas you would like to address in the "Problematic Area" field.
7. Share any additional details about your health and fitness goals or concerns in the "Details" field.
8. Click on the "Form Submit" field to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.

### Email
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address to ensure healthcare professionals can contact you.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help healthcare professionals understand your age and medical history.

### Advisor
* **Advisor**: If you have a healthcare advisor or professional, indicate 'Yes' here. This will help healthcare professionals know who to contact for further guidance.

### Problematic Area
* **Problematic Area**: If you have any specific health or fitness concerns, describe them in this field. This will help healthcare professionals understand your needs better.

### Details
* **Details**: Share any additional information about your health and fitness goals or concerns. This will help healthcare professionals provide more tailored advice.

### Form Submit
* **Form Submit**: Click this field to submit your information and allow healthcare professionals to review and respond to your concerns.
