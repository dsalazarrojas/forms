## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clinics about dengue fever cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Clinic Information (Clinic Information)** (`Clinic_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the clinic's name and contact information.
*   **Patient Information (Patient Information)** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for inputting the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
*   **Medical Certificate (Medical Certificate)** (`medical_certificate`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the patient's medical information, including their symptoms and medical history.
*   **Dengue Symptoms (Dengue Symptoms)** (`dengue_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required/true): This field is for selecting the patient's dengue symptoms, such as fever, severe headache, and joint pain.
*   **Doctor Information (Doctor Information)** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the doctor's name and contact information.
*   **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for inputting the patient's contact information, such as their phone number and email.
*   **Medical History (Medical History)** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the patient's medical history.
*   **Allergies (Allergies)** (`allergies`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for listing any allergies the patient has.
*   **Medical Tests (Medical Tests)** (`medical_tests`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering any medical tests the patient has undergone.
*   **Treatment (Treatment)** (`treatment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for documenting the patient's treatment.
*   **Signature (Signature)** (`signature`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for signing and dating the form.
*   **Date (Date)** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the medical certificate.
*   **Doctor's Signature (Doctor's Signature)** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for signing and dating the form.

## Tips

*   Always use the exact name of the dengue symptom in the "Dengue Symptoms" field, if possible.
*   If you are unsure about any of the symptoms, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
*   Make sure to enter accurate contact information for the patient and doctor.
*   If the patient has any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" field.
*   If the patient has undergone any medical tests, please list them in the "Medical Tests" field.
*   Please document the patient's treatment in the "Treatment" field.
*   Finally, sign and date the form in the "Doctor's Signature" field.
