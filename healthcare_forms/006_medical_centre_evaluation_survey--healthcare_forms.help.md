# Medical Centre Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their overall experience at the medical centre, including their satisfaction, visit details, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with our medical centre on a scale of 1-5.
2. Select the department you visited (e.g. Emergency, General Practitioner, Specialist, etc.).
3. Choose the time of day you visited the medical centre (e.g. Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
4. Select the length of time you waited for services.
5. Share any comments or suggestions you have about your visit.
6. Provide your contact information (email or phone number) so we can follow up with you.
7. If you'd like to be contacted again, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your overall satisfaction with our medical centre?** (Page 1)
	+ This question asks for your rating on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Which department did you visit?** (Page 2)
	+ Select the department you visited from the provided options (e.g. Emergency, General Practitioner, Specialist, etc.).
* **What time of day did you visit the medical centre?** (Page 3)
	+ Choose the time of day you visited the medical centre from the provided options (e.g. Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **How long have you waited for services?** (Page 4)
	+ Select the length of time you waited for services from the provided options (e.g. Less than 5 minutes, 5-15 minutes, 16-30 minutes, etc.).
* **Comments about your visit?** (Page 5)
	+ Share any feedback or suggestions you have about your visit to help us improve our services.
* **Contact information?** (Page 6)
	+ Please provide your email or phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Will you be visiting us again?** (Page 7)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted again, please let us know.
