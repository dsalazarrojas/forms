# Lifemark_consent_form_and_questionnaires - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifemark consent form and questionnaires are used to gather essential information from patients about their health and medical history, contact information, and consent for treatment. This form is designed to be completed by patients and healthcare workers, and it helps ensure that all necessary information is recorded and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Complete each field accurately and honestly.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Sign and date the form when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Section** (`form_section`, `text`, not required): This section is a text field for any additional comments or information about the patient's form.
- **Questionnaires** (`questionnaires`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all questionnaires that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has been presented with the questionnaire.
  * 'No' indicates the patient has not been presented with the questionnaire.
- **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, not required): A text field for the patient to provide informed consent for treatment.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has contact information available.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has contact information.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have contact information.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, not required): A text field for the patient or healthcare worker to provide medical history information.
- **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all medications that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient is taking the medication.
  * 'No' indicates the patient is not taking the medication.
- **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `text`, not required): A text field for the patient or healthcare worker to provide health insurance information.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has an emergency contact person.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has an emergency contact person.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have an emergency contact person.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all allergies that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has the allergy.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have the allergy.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all medical conditions that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has the condition.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have the condition.
- **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, `text`, not required): A text field for the patient or healthcare worker to provide health insurance provider information.
- **Health Insurance Plan** (`health_insurance_plan`, `text`, not required): A text field for the patient or healthcare worker to provide health insurance plan information.
- **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has a medical specialty.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has a medical specialty.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have a medical specialty.
- **Primary Care Provider** (`primary_care_provider`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has a primary care provider.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has a primary care provider.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have a primary care provider.
- **Healthcare Worker** (`healthcare_worker`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has a healthcare worker assigned to them.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has a healthcare worker assigned to them.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have a healthcare worker assigned to them.
- **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has a medical facility assigned to them.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has a medical facility assigned to them.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have a medical facility assigned to them.
- **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all patient relationships that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has the relationship.
  * 'No' indicates the patient does not have the relationship.
- **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all patient statuses that apply to the patient's condition.
  * 'Active' indicates the patient is active.
  * 'Inactive' indicates the patient is inactive.
- **Medical Team Member** (`medical_team_member`, `text`, not required): A text field for the patient or healthcare worker to provide medical team member information.
- **Healthcare Worker Signature** (`healthcare_worker_signature`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the healthcare worker has signed the form.
  * 'Yes' indicates the healthcare worker has signed the form.
  * 'No' indicates the healthcare worker has not signed the form.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether the patient has signed the form.
  * 'Yes' indicates the patient has signed the form.
  * 'No' indicates the patient has not signed the form.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): A date field for the date of the form.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): A time field for the time of the form.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): A text field for any additional notes.
- **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, not required): A text field for any additional notes.
