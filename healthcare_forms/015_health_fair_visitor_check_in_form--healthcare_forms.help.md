# health_fair_visitor_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from visitors for health services check-in.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
- Make sure to provide your first and last names, email address, phone number, check-in time, and select your service interest and on-site service preferences.
- Optional fields include service interest and on-site service preferences; feel free to provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your first name for check-in purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name for check-in purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Interest** (`service_interest`, Select One, Optional): Choose the service you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, Time, Required): Select the time you plan to check in for your service.
* **On-Site Service** (`on_site_service`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the on-site services you are interested in.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.
