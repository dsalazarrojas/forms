# healthcare_research_records_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect healthcare research records release information from patients. The form gathers personal and contact information, medical record release options, and other relevant details to ensure the safe and secure release of healthcare records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (page 1).
2. Provide contact information for the patient (page 2).
3. Answer the medical record release questions, specifying the level of access you wish to grant (page 3).
4. Enter the contact person's name and email address (page 4).
5. Enter the patient's phone number (page 5).
6. Sign and date the form as a witness (page 6).
7. Select the type of medical record information you wish to release (page 7).
8. Specify whether you wish to release test results, images, or both (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_information (Text, Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
* **contact_information (Text, Page 2)**: Provide the patient's or their family member's contact information, such as phone number and email address.
* **prophets_additional (Number, Page 3)**: This field is not clearly defined in the YAML file, but it appears to be a placeholder or a typo. Please disregard this field.
* **date_of_birth (Date, Page 4)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **medical_record_release (Select One, Page 5)**: Choose how much access you wish to grant to the healthcare records, from "No, not at this time" to "Yes, fully".
* **contact_person (Text, Page 6)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for receiving the healthcare records.
* **email (Email, Page 7)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **phone_number (Text, Page 8)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **signature (Note, Page 9)**: Sign and date the form as a witness, indicating that you have read and understood the terms of the form.
* **medical_record_information (Select Multiple, Page 10)**: Choose the type of medical record information you wish to release, from "Medical records only" to "Medical records and images".
* **test_results_release (Select One, Page 11)**: Specify whether you wish to release test results, and if so, under what conditions (e.g., "Yes, with restrictions").
