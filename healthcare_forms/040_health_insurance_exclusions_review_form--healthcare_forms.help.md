# health_insurance_exclusions_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_insurance_exclusions_review_form is a tool used to review and manage patient health insurance exclusions. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential information about a patient's medical conditions, insurance policy, and provider details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's basic information, including their name, medical condition, and policy number.
2. Select the exclusions from the policy that apply to the patient's medical condition.
3. Enter the effective date of the policy and any additional comments about the patient's case.
4. Provide details about the provider information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the patient's basic information.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical condition that requires review.
- **Policy Exclusions** (`policy_exclusions`, select_one, optional): Choose from the list of policy exclusions that apply to the patient's medical condition.
- **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the effective date of the policy.
- **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the patient's insurance policy.
- **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the policy number for easy reference.
- **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the healthcare provider involved in the patient's care.
- **Exclusion List** (`exclusion_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple policy exclusions that apply to the patient's medical condition.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's case.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the patient's information is properly reviewed.
- Double-check the policy exclusions and select the correct options that apply to the patient's medical condition.
- Use the provider information section to record details about the healthcare provider involved in the patient's care.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare team for assistance.
