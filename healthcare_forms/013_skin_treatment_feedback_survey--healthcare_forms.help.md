# skin_treatment_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our skin treatment service. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure we provide the best possible care for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options or enter your text answers. Please provide honest and specific feedback where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the outcome of your skin treatment?** (`treatment_outcome`, Select one): Please select one of the following options to describe the outcome of your treatment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Rate your overall experience** (`overall_experience`, 1-5): Rate your overall experience with our skin treatment service, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How satisfied are you with the service?** (`satisfaction`, 1-5): Rate your satisfaction with the service, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Were there any issues with your treatment?** (`treatment_issues`, Text): If you encountered any problems or issues during your treatment, please describe them in this field.
* **Do you have any feedback about your treatment?** (`feedback_treatment`, Text): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about your treatment experience.
* **How can we improve our service?** (`improvement_service`, Text): Offer any suggestions on how we can improve our skin treatment service.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, Text): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Do you have any recommendations for us?** (`treatment_recommendation`, Text): Share any suggestions or recommendations you have for improving our service.
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_info`, Text): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact method** (`contact_method`, Select multiple): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone).
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions?** (`follow_up_contact`, Checkbox): Check if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`follow_up_method`, Select multiple): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone).
* **When would you like to be contacted?** (`follow_up_date`, Date): Select a date for follow-up contact.
* **What time would you like to be contacted?** (`follow_up_time`, Time): Select a time for follow-up contact.
* **Additional information** (`follow_up_note`, Note): Provide any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Follow-up email address** (`follow_up_email`, Text): Enter your follow-up email address.
* **Follow-up phone number** (`follow_up_phone`, Text): Enter your follow-up phone number.
* **Follow-up message** (`follow_up_message`, Text): Enter a message for our follow-up contact.
