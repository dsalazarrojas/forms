# permanent makeup treatment record form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document a patient's permanent makeup treatment session. It captures key details about the treatment, including the client's information, treatment date and time, notes, and technician's certification. The form ensures accurate and thorough documentation to maintain client and technician records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields as follows:
	* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
	* **Treatment Date**: Select the date of the treatment.
	* **Treatment Time**: Choose the time of the treatment.
	* **Treatment Notes**: Enter a brief description of the treatment.
	* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician who performed the treatment.
	* **Technician Contact Info**: Provide contact information of the technician, such as their name and phone number.
	* **Technician Certification**: Select the technician's certification level (B, C, or D).
	* **Client Signature**: Have the client sign to acknowledge they have received the treatment.
	* **Client Signature Date**: Record the date the client signed.
	* **Technician Signature**: Have the technician sign to confirm they performed the treatment.
	* **Treatment Location**: Enter the location where the treatment took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Permanent Makeup Client Info): Enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Treatment Date**: The date the treatment was performed.
* **Treatment Time**: The time the treatment was performed.
* **Treatment Notes**: A brief description of the treatment, including any notable details or issues.
* **Technician Name**: The name of the technician who performed the treatment.
* **Technician Contact Info**: The contact information of the technician, such as their name and phone number.
* **Technician Certification**: The technician's certification level (B, C, or D).
* **Client Signature**: A confirmation from the client that they received the treatment.
* **Client Signature Date**: The date the client signed to confirm they received the treatment.
* **Technician Signature**: A confirmation from the technician that they performed the treatment.
* **Treatment Location**: The location where the treatment was performed.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the technician's certification level to ensure it is correct.
* Make sure the client and technician sign the form to confirm they received and performed the treatment, respectively.
* Keep the form up-to-date and stored securely for future reference.
