# COVID 19 Case Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Case Investigation Form is used to collect information on COVID-19 cases, including demographic data, medical history, contact information, and exposure details. This form is used for surveillance, outbreak investigation, and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by entering the patient's full legal name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in MM-DD-YYYY format.
3. Select the patient's gender from the "Gender" field.
4. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for follow-up contact.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Enter the patient's street address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter the city of residence in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state or province of residence in the "State" field.
9. Enter the patient's ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
10. If applicable, select the patient's race and ethnicity from the "Race and Ethnicity" field.
11. Enter the date of the case investigation in the "Investigation Date" field.
12. Enter the name of the investigator or healthcare staff member in the "Investigator Name" field.
13. Enter the health department case ID number in the "Case ID Number" field.
14. Select the type of diagnostic test received by the patient from the "Test Type" field.
15. Enter the date the test was performed in the "Test Date" field.
16. Select the result of the test from the "Test Result" field.
17. Select whether the patient is symptomatic or asymptomatic from the "Symptom Status" field.
18. If applicable, enter the date symptoms started in the "Symptom Onset Date" field.
19. Select all relevant symptoms the patient has experienced from the "Symptoms Experienced" field.
20. Select whether the patient was hospitalized, and if so, enter the date of hospitalization in the "Hospitalization Date" field.
21. Select all underlying medical conditions from the "Underlying Medical Conditions" field.
22. Select the patient's occupation from the "Occupation" field.
23. Select whether the patient works in healthcare from the "Healthcare Worker" field.
24. Select whether the patient is an essential worker with a critical infrastructure role from the "Essential Worker" field.
25. Select the type of residence from the "Residence Type" field.
26. Enter the number of people living in the household from the "Household Size" field.
27. Enter the number of close contacts identified from the "Close Contacts Identified" field.
28. Select the likely source of exposure from the "Likely Exposure Source" field.
29. Select whether the patient has traveled within the past 14 days from the "Travel in Past 14 Days" field.
30. Select the patient's vaccination status from the "Vaccination Status" field.
31. Select whether the patient is currently isolated and if they need any assistance from the "Isolation Status" field.
32. Select whether the patient needs isolation support from the "Isolation Support Needed" field.
33. Select the case outcome from the "Case Outcome" field.
34. If applicable, enter additional investigator notes in the "Investigator Notes" field.
35. Select whether additional follow-up is required from the "Follow-up Required" field.
36. If applicable, select the follow-up date from the "Follow-up Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the patient.
*   **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Gender** (gender, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender from Male, Female, Other, or Prefer Not to Say.
*   **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Enter the patient's email address for follow-up contact.
*   **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Street Address** (address, text, required): Enter the patient's complete street address.
*   **City** (city, text, required): Enter the city of residence.
*   **State** (state, text, required): Enter the state or province of residence.
*   **ZIP Code** (zip_code, text, required): Enter the patient's ZIP code.
*   **Race and Ethnicity** (race_ethnicity, select_one, optional): Select the patient's race and ethnicity from the provided options.
*   **Investigation Date** (investigation_date, date, required): Enter the date of the case investigation.
*   **Investigator Name** (investigator_name, text, required): Enter the name of the investigator or healthcare staff member.
*   **Case ID Number** (case_id, text, required): Enter the health department case ID number.
*   **Test Type** (test_type, select_one, required): Select the type of diagnostic test received by the patient.
*   **Test Date** (test_date, date, required): Enter the date the test was performed.
*   **Test Result** (test_result, select_one, required): Select the result of the test from Positive, Negative, Inconclusive, or Pending.
*   **Symptom Status** (symptom_status, select_one, required): Select whether the patient is symptomatic or asymptomatic.
*   **Symptom Onset Date** (symptom_onset_date, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date symptoms started.
*   **Symptoms Experienced** (symptoms_experienced, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant symptoms the patient has experienced.
*   **Hospitalization Required** (hospitalization, select_one, required): Select whether the patient was hospitalized.
*   **Hospitalization Date** (hospitalization_date, date, optional): If hospitalized, enter the date of hospitalization.
*   **Underlying Medical Conditions** (underlying_conditions, select_multiple, required): Select all underlying medical conditions.
*   **Occupation** (occupation, text, required): Select the patient's occupation.
*   **Healthcare Worker** (healthcare_worker, select_one, required): Select whether the patient works in healthcare.
*   **Essential Worker** (essential_worker, select_one, required): Select whether the patient is an essential worker with a critical infrastructure role.
*   **Residence Type** (residence_type, select_one, required): Select the type of residence.
*   **Household Size** (household_size, number, required): Enter the number of people living in the household.
*   **Close Contacts Identified** (close_contacts_identified, number, required): Enter the number of close contacts identified.
*   **Likely Exposure Source** (exposure_source, select_one, required): Select the likely source of exposure.
*   **Travel in Past 14 Days** (travel_history, select_one, required): Select whether the patient has traveled within the past 14 days.
*   **Vaccination Status** (vaccination_status, select_one, required): Select the patient's vaccination status.
*   **Isolation Status** (isolation_status, select_one, required): Select whether the patient is currently isolated.
*   **Isolation Support Needed** (isolation_support_needed, select_one, required): Select whether the patient needs isolation support.
*   **Case Outcome** (outcome, select_one, optional): Select the case outcome from Recovered, Still ill, Hospitalized, Deceased, or Unknown.
*   **Investigator Notes** (investigator_notes, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
*   **Follow-up Required** (follow_up_required, select_one, required): Select whether additional follow-up is required.
*   **Follow-up Date** (follow_up_date, date, optional): If follow-up is required, enter the follow-up date.

## Tips

*   Always enter accurate and up-to-date information.
*   If applicable, select all relevant symptoms.
*   Select all underlying medical conditions.
*   Verify the patient's vaccination status and provide any necessary documentation.
*   Ensure the patient's contact information is accurate for follow-up.
