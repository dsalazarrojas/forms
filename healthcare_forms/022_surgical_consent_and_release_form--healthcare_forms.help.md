# Surgical Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain surgical consent and release from patients before undergoing a surgical procedure. It's a crucial document that outlines the patient's rights, responsibilities, and the risks and benefits of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all fields are completed as required.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure that you understand the risks and benefits of the procedure as outlined in the form.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Surgical Information** (`surgical_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the surgical procedure you are undergoing, including the date and time of the procedure.
* **Anesthesia Information** (`anesthesia_info`, text, required/optional): If you are receiving anesthesia, please provide information about the type and dosage used.
* **Consent Signed** (`consent_signed`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the correct option to indicate that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Release Signed** (`release_signed`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the correct option to indicate that you release the healthcare provider to perform the procedure.
* **Anesthesia Provider** (`anesthesia_provider`, text, required/optional): If you are receiving anesthesia, please provide the name and contact information of the anesthesia provider.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your surgeon.
* **Surgeon Signature** (`surgeon_signature`, text, required/optional): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required/optional): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of the procedure.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time of the procedure.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): Please enter your address.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the hospital or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required/optional): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`, text, required/optional): Please enter the address of the hospital or healthcare provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required/optional): Please enter the fax number of the hospital or healthcare provider.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you understand the consent and release form before proceeding with the procedure.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider before signing the form.
