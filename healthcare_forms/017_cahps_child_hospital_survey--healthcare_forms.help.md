# CAHPS Child Hospital Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CAHPS Child Hospital Survey is designed to assess the quality of care provided to children in hospitals. This survey helps hospitals understand their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance patient experience and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For each question, select the most accurate response based on your personal experience with the hospital.
3. Provide your feedback in the text box at the end of the survey, if you have any comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Hospital and Unit (e.g., PICU)** (ID: 3, Required): Enter the name of the hospital and the specific unit where your child received care (e.g., Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU)).
* **Child Name (Optional)** (ID: 2, Optional): Enter your child's name for reference purposes only.
* **How often did nurses explain things about your child's care in a way you could understand?** (ID: 4, Required): Answer how often the nurses communicated effectively with you about your child's care. Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Sometimes
	+ Usually
	+ Always
* **How often did doctors treat your child with courtesy and respect?** (ID: 5, Required): Answer how often the doctors treated your child with courtesy and respect. Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Sometimes
	+ Usually
	+ Always
* **Did the hospital staff do everything they could to help your child feel comfortable?** (ID: 6, Required): Answer whether the hospital staff made efforts to ensure your child's comfort. Choose from:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, somewhat
	+ False
* **Were you involved as much as you wanted in decisions about your child's care?** (ID: 7, Required): Answer how much you were involved in decisions about your child's care. Choose from:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, somewhat
	+ False
* **Before giving your child a new medicine, did staff tell you what it was for and its side effects?** (ID: 8, Required): Answer how often the hospital staff informed you about new medicines. Choose from:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, somewhat
	+ False
* **How often was the area around your child's room quiet at night?** (ID: 9, Required): Answer how quiet the area around your child's room was at night. Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Sometimes
	+ Usually
	+ Always
* **How often were the bathroom and child's room kept clean?** (ID: 10, Required): Answer how clean the bathroom and your child's room were. Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Sometimes
	+ Usually
	+ Always
* **Did you get information in writing about what health problems to look for after your child left the hospital?** (ID: 11, Required): Answer whether you received written information about potential health problems to watch for after discharge. Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Overall rating of this hospital for your child's care** (ID: 12, Required): Rate the hospital's care for your child on a scale of 0 to 10, with 0 being the worst and 10 being the best.
* **Would you recommend this hospital to friends and family?** (ID: 13, Required): Answer whether you would recommend the hospital to others. Choose from:
	+ Definitely No
	+ Probably No
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Definitely Yes
* **Number of nights your child stayed in the hospital** (ID: 14, Required): Enter the number of nights your child stayed in the hospital.
* **Please share any other comments about your child's stay** (ID: 15, Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your child's hospital experience.
