# Medical Marijuana Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consents from patients regarding their medical marijuana use. It is intended for medical professionals to assess the patient's eligibility for medical marijuana treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's basic information on the first page.
2. On the second page, indicate whether the patient has a medical condition that may be related to medical marijuana use.
3. On the third page, select the conditions that apply to you regarding medical marijuana use.
4. On the fourth page, select the treatments that apply to you regarding medical marijuana use.
5. On the fifth page, enter any relevant information provided by your doctor.
6. On the sixth page, enter any consents or agreements you have with your doctor.
7. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (1, text, required): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information in this field.
- **Medical Condition** (2, select_one, optional): If you have a medical condition that may be related to medical marijuana use, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
- **Medical Marijuana Use** (3, select_multiple, optional): Select all the conditions that apply to you regarding medical marijuana use from the dropdown menu.
- **Medical Marijuana Treatment** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select all the treatments that apply to you regarding medical marijuana use from the dropdown menu.
- **Doctor Information** (5, text, optional): Enter any relevant information provided by your doctor, such as medical history or treatment plan.
- **Patient Consents** (6, text, optional): Enter any consents or agreements you have with your doctor, such as understanding of treatment risks or responsibilities.
- **Patient Signature** (7, note, optional): Sign and date this page to confirm your understanding and agreement.
- **Doctor Signature** (8, note, optional): Sign and date this page to confirm your doctor's understanding and agreement.

## Tips
Remember to review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor for clarification.
