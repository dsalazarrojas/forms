# Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form is a crucial document that ensures the accuracy and completeness of the child's medical information. This form is used to collect personal details, medical history, and contact information from the parent or guardian of the child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title and your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter the child's date of birth.
5. Enter the date of entry into the course.
6. Enter the name and phone number of the parent or guardian.
7. Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact.
8. Specify any medical conditions or allergies the child may have.
9. List any medications the child is taking.
10. Specify the relationship of the emergency contact to the child.
11. Provide the emergency contact's address.
12. Specify the type of alternate contact phone (e.g., home, work, cell).
13. Specify the child's medical procedure and facility.
14. Sign the consent and signature of the parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form**: The title of the form that you are filling out. Enter the name of the form, which should be the same as the one displayed.
* **Name**: Enter your name, which is the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used as a contact point.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Date of Entry**: Enter the date the child entered the course.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any known medical conditions or allergies the child may have.
* **Medications**: List any medications the child is taking.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone Type**: Specify the type of alternate contact phone (e.g., home, work, cell).
* **Emergency Contact Permission**: Specify the level of permission the child's parent or guardian has given to the emergency contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Permission**: Specify the level of permission the parent or guardian has for the child's care.
* **Medical Procedure**: Enter any medical procedure the child may undergo.
* **Consent**: Sign the consent form, agreeing to the terms and conditions of the child's care.
* **Signature**: Sign the form as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign the form as the parent or guardian.
* **Medical Facility**: Specify the medical facility where the child will be treated.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are authorized to fill out this form before proceeding.
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible care for the child.
* Please do not duplicate or share this form with anyone else.
