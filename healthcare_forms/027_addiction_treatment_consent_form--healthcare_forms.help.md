# Addiction Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Addiction Treatment Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for addiction treatment. It ensures that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment and are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient name" field.
2. Describe the treatment approach in the "Treatment description" field.
3. Acknowledge understanding of the risks and benefits in the "Risks and benefits explained" field.
4. If applicable, describe alternative treatment options in the "Treatment alternatives discussed" field.
5. Select the "Consent to treatment" field with "I consent" to provide informed consent.
6. Describe the limits of confidentiality in the "Limits of confidentiality" field.
7. Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field.
8. Enter your typed name in the "Signature name" field.
9. Enter the date in the "Date signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient name**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Treatment description**: Describe the proposed treatment approach, including the type of treatment and any relevant details.
* **Risks and benefits explained**: Acknowledge that you have explained the risks and benefits of the treatment to the patient.
* **Treatment alternatives discussed**: If applicable, describe alternative treatment options that were discussed with the patient.
* **Consent to treatment**: Select "I consent" to provide informed consent.
* **Limits of confidentiality**: Describe the limits of confidentiality, including mandatory reporting and legal exceptions.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Signature name**: Enter your typed name as the healthcare professional providing consent.
* **Date signed**: Enter the date the consent was signed.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have explained the treatment risks and benefits to the patient before filling out this form.
* Review the form with the patient to ensure they understand their rights and responsibilities.
* Store the completed form in the patient's medical record.
* Update the form as necessary to reflect changes in the patient's treatment plan.
