# Mental Health Evaluation Form For Supervisors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for supervisors to assess an employee's current mental health status, job performance, and any potential mental health issues they may be experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the employee's name as it appears on their identification or personnel records.
2. Select the employee's job title from the options provided.
3. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
4. Choose whether the employee's performance is satisfactory, unsatisfactory, or not applicable.
5. Provide any mental health issues you have observed or are aware of.
6. Add any comments or feedback you have for the employee.
7. Sign and date the form to indicate that you have completed the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification or personnel records. This is the employee's name as they are currently listed in our HR system.
* **Job Title**: Select the employee's current job title from the options provided. This will help us track performance data by job type.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This will help us track when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation. This will help us track the duration of the evaluation.
* **Employee Performance**: Choose whether the employee's performance is satisfactory, unsatisfactory, or not applicable. This will help us assess their current performance level.
* **Mental Health Issues**: Provide any mental health issues you have observed or are aware of. This can include any concerns you have about the employee's mental health status or any issues they have reported to you.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have for the employee. This can include any suggestions or recommendations for supporting their mental health or improving their job performance.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate that you have completed the evaluation. This will serve as evidence that the evaluation was conducted.

## Tips
* Please be respectful and sensitive when conducting mental health evaluations. Avoid using language that could be perceived as judgmental or confrontational.
* If you are unsure about how to fill out this form, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended for supervisors to use as a tool to support employee mental health. If you are concerned about an employee's mental health, please reach out to our Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for support.
* Remember to keep the evaluation confidential and secure.
