# health_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information for healthcare assessment purposes, including medical history, allergies, medications, and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in the patient's basic information (Page 1).
2. Answer the medical history questions (Pages 2-8).
3. Answer the medications and allergies questions (Pages 3 and 4).
4. Answer the medical procedures questions (Pages 5-10).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient information** (Page 1, required): Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Medical history** (Page 2, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
* **Allergies** (Page 3, required): List any known allergies the patient has, such as medications, foods, or substances.
* **Medications** (Page 4, required): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical procedure** (Page 5, required): Enter the date of the last medical procedure the patient underwent.
* **Medical condition** (Page 6, required): Describe the patient's current medical condition or condition of interest.
* **Additional medical history** (Page 7, optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Past medical history** (Page 8, optional): Describe any past medical history that is relevant to the patient's current condition.
* **Procedure 2** (Page 9, optional): Enter the date of the second medical procedure the patient underwent.
