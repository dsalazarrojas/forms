# NICU Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NICU Monitoring Checklist is a form used to collect data on newborn patients in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). It is designed to track vital signs, patient conditions, and responses to treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Select the appropriate responses for each field based on the patient's condition.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Info
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, medical record number, and other identifying information.
### Page 2
* **Blood pressure** (`monitor_1`, select_one, required): Record the patient's blood pressure as either Hypertension, Normal, Bradycardia, or Tachycardia.
* **Temperature** (`monitor_2`, number, required): Record the patient's temperature in degrees Celsius.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant vital signs, including Heart Rate, Respiratory Rate, Blood Oxygen, and Blood Pressure.
### Page 3
* **Monitor 3** (`monitor_3`, select_one, required): Record the patient's condition as either Hypothermia or Hyperthermia.
* **Vital Signs** (`monitor_4`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant vital signs.
### Page 4
* **Monitor 4** (`monitor_4`, select_multiple, required): Record the patient's condition as either Bradycardia or Tachycardia.
* **Date** (`monitor_5`, date, required): Record the date of the monitor.
* **Time** (`monitor_6`, time, required): Record the time of the monitor.
