# Missed Appointment Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missed Appointment Policy Acknowledgement Form is used to obtain a patient's acknowledgement of the policy that will be carried out in case of a missed appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the policy statement above.
2. Select one of the two options to acknowledge your understanding of the policy.
3. Enter your signature date and time in the appropriate fields.
4. Sign the form to confirm your acknowledgement.
5. Enter your name as the patient.
6. Enter the name of the healthcare provider (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter your name, as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **policy** (`policy`, text, optional): This is the policy that will be carried out in case of a missed appointment. Please read and understand it carefully before proceeding.
* **acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the two options to acknowledge your understanding of the policy:
	+ Patient understands and acknowledges that the policy will be carried out in case of an appointment miss.
	+ Other
* **signature_date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **signature_time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you sign the form.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name in this field to confirm your acknowledgement.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the patient.
* **patient_signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the patient to confirm your acknowledgement.
* **provider_name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider (if applicable).
* **provider_signature** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Sign the name of the healthcare provider (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the policy carefully before proceeding.
* Select the correct option to acknowledge your understanding of the policy.
* Enter your signature date and time accurately.
* Sign your name as the patient and healthcare provider (if applicable) to confirm your acknowledgement.
