# Safe Sleep Post Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safe Sleep Post Survey form is designed to collect information about safe sleep habits and related factors for parents, aiming to improve understanding and promote better sleep safety practices. This form gathers demographic information and specific questions about sleep practices and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the survey as it appears on this page.
2. Fill in the parent's name.
3. Provide the parent's age.
4. Answer the question about whether the child is in the home.
5. Indicate whether the respondent (breastfeeding mom) is also the mother of the child.
6. Optionally, elaborate on any safe sleep message or comment in the "Safe Sleep Message" field.
7. Click the "Form Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safe Sleep Post Survey** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title as it appears on this page for reference purposes.
* **Parent Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of the parent or the individual answering this survey.
* **Parent Age** (`parent_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter the age of the parent or the individual.
* **Is Child In Home** (`is_child_in_home`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the child is in the home, "No" otherwise.
* **Is Breastfeeding Mom** (`is_breastfeeding_mom`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the respondent is the breastfeeding mom, "No" otherwise.
* **Safe Sleep Message** (`safe_sleep_message`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or messages related to safe sleep practices.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, `note`, required: false): Click this button to submit your completed form.
