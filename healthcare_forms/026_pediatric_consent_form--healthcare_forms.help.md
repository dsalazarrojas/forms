# Pediatric Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for procedures or treatments performed on their child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): The full name of the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`): The contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
	* **Child Name** (`child_name`): The full name of the child.
	* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): The date of birth of the child.
	* **Address** (`address`): The address of the child's residence.
	* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): The name of the emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): The contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
	* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`): The name of the medical provider performing the procedure or treatment.
	* **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`): The name of the procedure or treatment being performed.
	* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`): A brief description of the procedure or treatment.
2. Review and sign the form in the presence of a medical provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): A text field for the parent or guardian to enter their full name.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`): A text field for the parent or guardian to enter their contact phone number.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`): A text field for the parent or guardian to enter the child's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): A date field for the parent or guardian to enter the child's date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`): A text field for the parent or guardian to enter the child's residence address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): A text field for the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): A text field for the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`): A text field for the medical provider's name.
* **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`): A text field for the procedure or treatment's name.
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`): A text field for a brief description of the procedure or treatment.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): A field for the parent or guardian to sign, indicating their consent for the procedure or treatment. 

Note: This form requires signatures from parents or guardians for consent.
