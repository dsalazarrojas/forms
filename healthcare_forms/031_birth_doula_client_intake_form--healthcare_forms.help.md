# Birth Doula Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about you and your birth preferences for our doula services. It is a crucial document that helps us understand your needs, preferences, and medical history to provide the best possible care and support during your pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum period.

## How to Complete This Form
Please fill out this form thoroughly and honestly to ensure we have a clear understanding of your needs and preferences. You can save your progress as you go and come back later to complete any sections you are not ready to fill out yet. Each section is explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (3): Your first name, required for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (5): Your last name, required for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (6): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (7): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for any communication.
* **Phone Number** (8): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Partner Name** (9): If you have a birth partner, please enter their name.
* **Partner Email** (10): If you have a birth partner, please enter their email address.
* **Due Date** (12): Enter the expected due date of your baby's birth.
* **Weeks Pregnant** (13): Enter how many weeks along you are in your pregnancy.
* **Is This Your First Pregnancy** (14): Select "Yes" if this is your first pregnancy, or "No" if you have had previous pregnancies.
* **Number of Previous Children** (15): Enter the number of children you have had before.
* **Tell Us About Previous Births** (16): Describe any previous birth experiences, such as type of delivery and any complications.
* **Pregnancy Complications** (17): Enter any health issues or concerns you are experiencing during your pregnancy.
* **Preferred Birth Setting** (19): Choose where you would like to give birth, such as hospital, birthing center, or home birth.
* **Healthcare Provider** (20): Enter the name of your healthcare provider, such as OB-GYN or midwife.
* **Birth Philosophy** (21): Describe your ideal birth experience, including any specific preferences or expectations.
* **Pain Management Preferences** (22): Select any pain relief options you are interested in, such as natural techniques, epidural, or other medical interventions.
* **Intervention Preferences** (23): Describe your comfort level with medical interventions, such as fetal monitoring or cesarean section.
* **Who Do You Want Present at Birth** (24): List anyone you would like or do not want to have present during labor and delivery.
* **Areas Where You Want Doula Support** (26): Select which areas of support you would like from our doula services, such as emotional support or physical comfort measures.
* **Specific Concerns or Fears** (27): Enter any worries or concerns you may have during your pregnancy, labor, or postpartum period.
* **History of Trauma Affecting Birth** (28): Select "Yes" if you have a history of trauma that may affect your birth experience.
* **Please Describe** (29): Enter any details about your trauma history that you would like us to know.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (31): Enter any current health issues or medical conditions you are experiencing.
* **Medications** (32): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (33): Enter any food, medication, or other allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Hours of Doula Support Desired** (35): Enter how many hours of doula support you would like, either continuous or when labor starts.
* **Postpartum Support Desired** (36): Select whether you would like postpartum visits from our doula services.
* **Questions or Additional Comments** (37): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have for us.
