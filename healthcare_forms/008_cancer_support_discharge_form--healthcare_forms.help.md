# Cancer Support Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge process of patients from support programs, ensuring a smooth transition of care and providing necessary information for ongoing support. It is crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to guarantee the best possible outcomes for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed before moving on to the next one.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the form.
3. Be aware that some fields are required and must be completed for accurate record-keeping and continuity of care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name (1)**: Enter the patient's full, legal name.
* **Patient ID Number (2)**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Patient Date of Birth (3)**: Enter the patient's birth date.
* **Patient Phone Number (4)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Patient Email Address (5)**: Enter the patient's email address for follow-up (optional).
* **Emergency Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of the primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number (7)**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Support Program Name (8)**: Select the type of support program the patient is being discharged from (e.g., Emotional Support Counseling, Peer Support Group, etc.).
* **Program Start Date (9)**: Enter the date the support program began.
* **Discharge Date (10)**: Enter the date of discharge from the program.
* **Reason for Discharge (11)**: Select the reason for discharge (e.g., Treatment Completed Successfully, Patient Requested Discharge, etc.).
* **Discharge Details (12)**: Enter any additional information regarding the discharge process.
* **Were Support Goals Achieved (13)**: Select whether all, most, some, or few goals were achieved during the program.
* **Summary of Goals Achieved (14)**: Enter a brief description of the progress towards goals.
* **Patient Current Status (15)**: Select the patient's current health status (e.g., In Remission, Active Treatment Ongoing, etc.).
* **Follow-up Care Needed (16)**: Select whether follow-up care is still required (Yes - Ongoing Support Required, Yes - Periodic Check-ins, etc.).
* **Recommended Follow-up Services (17)**: Select the services the patient should continue to receive (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Oncology Follow-up, etc.).
* **Next Follow-up Appointment Date (18)**: Enter the date and time of the next scheduled appointment (optional).
* **Current Medications (19)**: List all current medications the patient is taking.
* **Special Instructions for Patient (20)**: Enter any special instructions for the patient's care.
* **Resources Provided at Discharge (21)**: Select the materials given to the patient during discharge (e.g., Written Care Plan, Medication List, etc.).
* **Patient Satisfaction (22)**: Select the patient's level of satisfaction with the program (e.g., Very Satisfied, Dissatisfied, etc.).
* **Caregiver Involvement Level (23)**: Select the level of caregiver participation (e.g., Highly Involved, Minimally Involved, etc.).
* **Was Discharge Plan Reviewed with Patient (24)**: Select whether the discharge plan was reviewed with the patient.
* **Does Patient Understand Discharge Plan (25)**: Select whether the patient understands the discharge plan.
* **Care Coordinator Name (26)**: Enter the name of the care coordinator.
* **Coordinator Signature Date (27)**: Enter the date of signature.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth transition of care for your patients. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare team for guidance.
