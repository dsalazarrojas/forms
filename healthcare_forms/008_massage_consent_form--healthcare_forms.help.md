# massage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain client information and consent for a massage therapy session, which is a legal and regulatory requirement in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, users must fill out all required fields. Follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name, contact information, and emergency contact details.
2. Indicate if you have any medical conditions or allergies.
3. Select the type of massage you are interested in.
4. Confirm the date and time of the massage session.
5. Enter the expected duration of the massage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Text, Required):** Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Contact Info (Text, Required):** Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Client Contact Info (Text, Required):** Enter the client's contact phone number. (Note: This field seems to be duplicated; please ensure that the correct field is filled out.)
* **Medical Conditions (Select Multiple, Optional):** Check any known medical conditions or diagnoses.
* **Allergies (Text, Optional):** List any allergies the client is aware of.
* **Massage Type (Select One, Required):** Choose the type of massage you are interested in.
* **I hereby consent to (Date, Required):** Confirm the date of the massage session.
* **Emergency Contact (Text, Required):** Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Therapist License (Text, Optional):** Indicate if the therapist has a license to practice (this field is optional).
* **Massage Location (Select Multiple, Optional):** Choose the location of the massage.
* **Massage Time (Time, Required):** Confirm the time of the massage session.
* **Massage Duration (Text, Optional):** Enter the expected duration of the massage.
* **Additional Notes (Note, Optional):** Provide any additional notes or comments about the massage session.
