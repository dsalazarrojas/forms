# Medical Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect informed consent from patients before medical procedures. This form is typically used to obtain patient consent for treatment, tests, or other medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
2. Enter your patient's name and address in the respective fields.
3. Fill in the healthcare provider's name and the consent date and time.
4. Sign the patient's signature and the healthcare provider's signature in the respective fields.
5. Provide any additional medical information in the "Medical Informations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Name
• **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Patient Address
• **Patient Address**: Enter your home address.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Healthcare Provider Name
• **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter your healthcare provider's name.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Consent Date
• **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which you gave consent for treatment.
• **Type:** Date field
• **Required:** No

### Consent Time
• **Consent Time**: Enter the time at which you gave consent for treatment.
• **Type:** Time field
• **Required:** No

### Patient Signature
• **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to indicate that you understand and agree to the treatment plan.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Healthcare Provider Signature
• **Healthcare Provider Signature**: The healthcare provider will sign their name to confirm they discussed the treatment options with you.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Medical Record Number
• **Medical Record Number**: Enter your medical record number if applicable.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Patient Contact Information
• **Patient Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Emergency Contact Person
• **Emergency Contact Person**: Identify the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
• **Type:** Select one of the options (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Brother, Sister, Grandparent, Parent, Other)
• **Required:** No

### Emergency Contact Number
• **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Emergency Contact Email
• **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
• **Type:** Email field
• **Required:** No

### Medical Informations
• **Medical Informations**: Enter any additional medical information that is relevant to your treatment.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

### Medical Informations 2
• **Medical Informations 2**: Enter any additional medical information that is relevant to your treatment.
• **Type:** Text field
• **Required:** No

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you have filled in all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
