# Clinical Trials Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Trials Referral Form is designed to collect relevant information for referring patients to clinical trials. This form helps healthcare providers and researchers to gather essential details about the patient's condition, medical history, and interest in participating in clinical trials. By using this form, healthcare providers can efficiently identify potential candidates for clinical trials and ensure that patients are properly matched with trials that suit their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2.  Provide all necessary information about the patient, including their referral date, medical history, and interest in clinical trials.
3.  Be clear and concise in your entries, using specific details whenever possible.

*   **Clinical Trials Referral Form** (Header)
