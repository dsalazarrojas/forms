## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from attendees and speakers for a health conference. It will help the conference organizers gather the necessary details to manage the event effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, such as your name, address, and contact details.
2. Confirm your area of specialty (yes or no).
3. Provide details about the speaker you are registering (if applicable).
4. Enter the conference details, including dates, venue, and a brief description.
