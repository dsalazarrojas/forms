# Medical Board Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Board Discharge Form is used by healthcare providers to document patient discharges from medical boards. This form helps gather information related to patient discharges, ensuring accurate and complete records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth in the "patient_dob" field.
3. Provide your patient's address in the "patient_address" field.
4. Enter your patient's healthcare provider's name in the "healthcare_provider_name" field.
5. Provide your patient's healthcare provider's contact information in the "healthcare_provider_contact" field.
6. Select the date of your patient's discharge in the "patient_discharge_date" field.
7. Enter any relevant discharge instructions in the "discharge_instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **patient_dob** (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter your patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **patient_address** (Text, Optional):
	+ Provide your patient's home or contact address for further communication.
* **healthcare_provider_name** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your name or the name of your institution/organization.
* **healthcare_provider_contact** (Text, Optional):
	+ Provide your contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **patient_discharge_date** (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date of your patient's discharge from the medical board.
* **discharge_instructions** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any necessary instructions or follow-up care information for your patient.
