# dbs_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record patient data in the healthcare setting. It captures information about the patient's identification, medical history, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have the patient's medical records and consultations available for reference. Review the form carefully and ensure that you've asked the patient to verify the accuracy of the information provided.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. Select multiple medical conditions that apply from the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Enter any allergies in the "Allergies" field (note: this field is for free-form text entry).
8. Enter the patient's email (if applicable) in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the emergency contact's information in the "Emergency Contact" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field (if applicable).
12. Select the insurance provider from the "Insurance Provider" field (if applicable).
13. Select the medical facility from the "Medical Facility" field (if applicable).
14. Select the medical specialist from the "Medical Specialist" field (if applicable).
15. Enter any additional medical test results in the "Medical Test Results" field.
16. Enter the date of the test in the "Test Date" field.
17. Enter the test time in the "Test Time" field.
18. Enter the name of the medical provider in the "Medical Provider" field (if applicable).
19. Enter any additional medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
20. Enter any additional medical test results in the "Medical Test Results 2" field (if applicable).
21. Enter any additional medical conditions that apply in the "Medical Condition 2" field (if applicable).
22. Enter the name of the medical provider in the "Medical Provider 2" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Phone** (Phone, Number, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Required): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, Select Multiple, Required): Select multiple medical conditions that apply from the dropdown list.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, Note, Required): Enter any allergies in this field (free-form text entry).
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's information (if applicable).
* **Address** (Address, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address (if applicable).
* **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
* **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider, Select One, Optional): Select the insurance provider from the dropdown list (if applicable).
* **Medical Facility** (Medical Facility, Select One, Optional): Select the medical facility from the dropdown list (if applicable).
* **Medical Specialist** (Medical Specialist, Select One, Optional): Select the medical specialist from the dropdown list (if applicable).
* **Medical Test Results** (Medical Test Results, Text, Optional): Enter any additional medical test results.
* **Test Date** (Test Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the test.
* **Test Time** (Test Time, Time, Optional): Enter the test time.
* **Medical Provider** (Medical Provider, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the medical provider (if applicable).
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, Text, Optional): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Medical Test Results 2** (Medical Test Results 2, Text, Optional): Enter any additional medical test results (if applicable).
* **Medical Condition 2** (Medical Condition 2, Select Multiple, Optional): Select additional medical conditions that apply (if applicable).
* **Medical Provider 2** (Medical Provider 2, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the medical provider (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to review the patient's previous records and consultations before starting.
* Ensure that you've asked the patient to verify the accuracy of the information provided.
* Complete all required fields.
* Double-check for any optional fields that you've selected.
* Save the completed form to update the patient's records.
