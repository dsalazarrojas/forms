# Ambulance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare providers to request ambulance services for their patients or events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of applicant (individual or organization).
2. Fill out the relevant fields for the selected applicant type, including name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Choose the type of service requested (e.g., emergency transport, non-emergency transport, etc.).
4. Select the type of ambulance needed (e.g., basic life support, advanced life support, etc.).
5. Specify the number of ambulances required.
6. Choose the requested service date and time.
7. Select the pickup and destination locations.
8. Provide patient information (optional).
9. Choose the payment method (e.g., insurance, self-pay, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Type**: Select the type of applicant (individual or organization).
	+ Select "Individual" if the service is for a personal request.
	+ Select "Organization" if the service is for a healthcare organization or event.
* **First Name**: Fill out the first name of the applicant.
* **Last Name**: Fill out the last name of the applicant.
* **Organization Name**: Fill out the name of the healthcare organization or event.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the applicant or organization.
* **Phone Primary**: Enter the primary phone number of the applicant or organization.
* **Phone Secondary**: Enter the secondary phone number of the applicant or organization (optional).
* **Street Address**: Enter the full street address of the pickup location.
* **City**: Enter the city of the pickup location.
* **State**: Enter the state of the pickup location.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the pickup location.
* **Type of Service Requested**: Choose the type of service requested (e.g., emergency transport, non-emergency transport, etc.).
* **Ambulance Type Needed**: Choose the type of ambulance needed (e.g., basic life support, advanced life support, etc.).
* **Number of Ambulances Needed**: Specify the number of ambulances required.
* **Request Service Date**: Enter the date of the requested service.
* **Request Service Time**: Enter the time of the requested service.
* **Expected Duration (Hours)**: Enter the expected duration of the service in hours (optional).
* **Recurring Service**: Select if this is a recurring service (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Recurring Schedule**: Enter the recurring schedule details (if applicable).
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the full address of the pickup location.
* **Pickup Location Contact**: Enter the contact name and phone number of the pickup location.
* **Destination Location**: Enter the full address of the destination location.
* **Destination Contact**: Enter the contact name and phone number of the destination location.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient (if applicable).
* **Patient Age**: Enter the age of the patient (if applicable).
* **Patient Weight**: Enter the weight of the patient in pounds (if applicable).
* **Patient Condition**: Enter a brief description of the patient's condition (if applicable).
* **Patient Mobility Level**: Choose the patient's mobility level (e.g., ambulatory, wheelchair bound, etc.).
* **Medical Equipment Needed**: Select all medical equipment needed for the patient (e.g., oxygen, cardiac monitor, etc.).
* **Medical Personnel Required**: Select all medical personnel required for the patient (e.g., EMT-Basic, EMT-Intermediate, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (e.g., insurance, self-pay, etc.).
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the insurance policy number (if applicable).
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address (if different from the pickup location).
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special needs or considerations.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **How did you hear about our service**: Choose how the applicant heard about the service (e.g., physician referral, internet search, etc.).
* **Applicant Signature**: Sign the form with full name as signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of the signature.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure efficient processing of your application.
