# Emotional Wellness Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's emotional wellness, medical history, and contact information. The form will help healthcare professionals assess the client's needs and provide personalized support and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and any emergency contact.
3. Provide medical and mental health history, including any medications and their dosages.
4. Answer questions about your emotional wellness history and goals.
5. Select your mental health providers and specialties.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (`client_information`, text, optional)**: Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, optional)**: Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emotional Wellness History (`emotional_wellness_history`, text, optional)**: Describe your emotional wellness history, including any stressors or triggers.
* **Goals and Objectives (`goals_and_objectives`, text, optional)**: List your personal goals and objectives related to emotional wellness.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`, text, optional)**: Describe your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Mental Health History (`mental_health_history`, text, optional)**: Describe your mental health history, including any mental health conditions or treatments.
* **Substance Use (`substance_use`, text, optional)**: Describe your substance use history.
* **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)**: Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Status (`emergency_contact_relationship_status`, select_one, optional)**: Select the relationship status of your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Mental Health Providers (`mental_health_providers`, select_one, optional)**: Select your mental health provider(s).
* **Mental Health Specialties (`mental_health_specialties`, select_one, optional)**: Select your mental health specialty(ies).
* **Mental Health Providers List (`mental_health_providers_list`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select multiple mental health provider(s) if applicable.
* **Mental Health Specialties List (`mental_health_specialties_list`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select multiple mental health specialty(ies) if applicable.
* **Medication (`medication`, text, optional)**: Enter any medications you are taking.
* **Medication List (`medication_list`, text, optional)**: List any medications you are taking.
* **Medication Dosage Last Administered (`dosage_last_medication_administered`, number, optional)**: Enter the dosage of the last medication you took.
* **Medication Dosage Next Administered (`dosage_next_medication_administered`, number, optional)**: Enter the expected dosage of the next medication to be taken.
* **Medication Effectiveness (`medication_effectiveness`, select_one, optional)**: Select the effectiveness of your medication(s).
* **Medication Effectiveness List (`medication_effectiveness_list`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select multiple effectiveness of your medication(s) if applicable.
* **Date Last Medication Administered (`date_last_medication_administered`, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the last medication was administered.
* **Time Last Medication Administered (`time_last_medication_administered`, time, optional)**: Enter the time of the last medication was administered.
* **Date Next Medication Administered (`date_next_medication_administered`, date, optional)**: Enter the expected date of the next medication to be taken.
* **Time Next Medication Administered (`time_next_medication_administered`, time, optional)**: Enter the expected time of the next medication to be taken.
* **Note (`note`, note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date Last Contact (`date_last_contact`, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your last contact with us.
* **Time Last Contact (`time_last_contact`, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your last contact with us.
* **Date Next Contact (`date_next_contact`, date, optional)**: Enter the expected date of your next contact with us.
* **Time Next Contact (`time_next_contact`, time, optional)**: Enter the expected time of your next contact with us.
* **Note Last Contact (`note_last_contact`, note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your last contact.
