# COVID 19 Booster Shot Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients who are seeking a COVID-19 booster shot. It is intended to gather relevant data about the patient's medical history, vaccination history, and current health status to ensure a safe and effective vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the patient's basic information, including their name, date of birth, and contact phone number and email address.
2. Provide the date of the appointment for the booster shot.
3. Select the type of vaccine that was received as the primary vaccination.
4. Enter the dates of the first and second doses of the primary vaccination, if applicable.
5. If you have any chronic health conditions or allergies, please list them.
6. Answer questions about your COVID-19 history and recovery date, if applicable.
7. Confirm your consent to receive the booster shot.
8. Finally, submit the form by providing the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter the date of your birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient ID** (3, text, required): Enter your patient ID or medical record number, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Appointment Date for Booster** (6, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled booster shot appointment.
* **Primary Vaccine Type Received** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of vaccine you received as your primary vaccination.
* **Date of First Dose** (8, date, required): Enter the date of your first dose of the vaccine.
* **Date of Second Dose** (9, date, optional): Enter the date of your second dose of the vaccine, if applicable.
* **Where were you vaccinated** (10, text, optional): Provide the location where you received your primary vaccination.
* **Side effects from primary vaccination** (11, text, optional): Describe any side effects you experienced from the primary vaccination.
* **Do you want to receive a booster shot?** (12, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in receiving a booster shot.
* **Reasons for hesitation if applicable** (13, text, optional): Provide any reasons you may have for not wanting a booster shot.
* **Current Health Status** (14, select_one, required): Select your current health status.
* **Chronic Health Conditions** (15, select_multiple, required): Select any chronic health conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (16, text, optional): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Known Allergies** (17, text, optional): List any known allergies, especially to vaccine components.
* **Have you had COVID-19 since vaccination?** (18, select_one, required): Select whether you have had COVID-19 since your vaccination.
* **If yes, when were you infected?** (19, date, optional): Enter the date you were infected with COVID-19.
* **I consent to receive the booster shot** (20, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to receive the booster shot.
* **Form Submission Date** (21, date, required): The current date when the form is submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is intended to help healthcare providers assess your eligibility for a booster shot.
