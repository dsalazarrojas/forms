# Dental Extraction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather patient information, consent, and other relevant details before a dental extraction procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details in the **Patient Identity** section.
2.  Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3.  Describe the dental extraction procedure in the **Procedure Description** section.
4.  List any potential risks or complications in the **Risks and Complications** section.
5.  Sign the **Consent Form** section to confirm that you have read and understood the procedure's details.
6.  Sign the **Signature** section to confirm your consent.
7.  Fill in your personal details in the **Patient Information** section.
8.  Date and time of your consent in the **Consent Date** section.
9.  Enter your dentist's contact information in the **Dentist Information** section.
10. State the cost of the procedure in the **Procedure Cost** section.
11. Describe any post-procedure care instructions in the **After Effects** section.
12. Provide your emergency contact's information in the **Emergency Contact** section.
13. List any allergies or medical conditions you may have in the **Allergy Information** section.
14. Describe your medical history in the **Medical History** section.
15. Sign the **Dentist Signature** section to confirm that you have read and understood the procedure's details.
16. Sign the **Patient Signature** section to confirm your consent.
17. Date and time of the procedure in the **Date** and **Time** sections.
18. Select the facility where the procedure will take place in the **Facility** section.
19. If applicable, enter the name of the alternative facility in the **Other Facility** section.
20. Enter your date of birth in the **Patient DOB** section.
21. Enter your contact phone number in the **Phone Number** section.
22. Enter your email address in the **Email** section.
23. Enter your address in the **Address** section.
24. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Contact Number** section.
