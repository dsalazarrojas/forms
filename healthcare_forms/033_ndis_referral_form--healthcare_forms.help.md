# NDIS Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDIS Referral Form is a tool designed to collect information from individuals seeking referrals for the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS). This form helps in gathering personal details and referral request information, as well as contact details for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Personal Details (Form 1).
2. Choose the type of referral you are requesting (Form 2).
3. Provide your Contact Details (Form 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form 1: Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant personal details.

### Form 2: Referral Request
* **Referral Request** (`Referral Request`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of referral you are requesting:
	+ NDIS Referral for Assessment
	+ NDIS Referral for Planning
	+ Other

### Form 3: Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required): Enter your email address, phone number, or other contact information so that we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are only using this form for NDIS referrals.
* Do not submit duplicate referrals.
* Fill in the form clearly and accurately to ensure that your referral request is processed efficiently.
