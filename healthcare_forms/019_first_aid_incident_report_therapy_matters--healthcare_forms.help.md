## First Aid Incident Report Therapy Matters - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that have occurred during therapy sessions. The purpose of this form is to provide a systematic way to document and track incidents, which can include injuries, illnesses, or other unforeseen events that may have happened during therapy sessions. By filling out this form, you will be able to report incidents in a clear and concise manner, helping to ensure that necessary actions are taken to address the incident and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of incident that occurred (Injury, Illness, or Other).
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Enter the patient's name and date of birth (if available).
5. Choose the reporting physician's name (Dr. Smith, Dr. John, or Dr. Doe).
6. Select the outcome of the incident (Yes, No, or Other).
7. If the outcome is Other, provide a detailed description of the outcome.
8. Enter the date of the outcome.
9. Select the current status of the incident (Active, Resolved, or Pending).
10. If the status is Other, provide a detailed description of the status.
11. Enter the date of the status.
12. Select the follow-up method (Phone, Email, In-person, Video, or Other).
13. If the follow-up method is Other, provide a detailed description of the method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of incident that occurred (Injury, Illness, or Other).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth (if available).
* **Reporting Physician** (`reporting_physician_name`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the reporting physician's name (Dr. Smith, Dr. John, or Dr. Doe).
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required/optional): Select the outcome of the incident (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Other** (`incident_outcome_other`, text, required/optional): If the outcome is Other, provide a detailed description of the outcome.
* **Outcome Date** (`incident_outcome_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the outcome.
* **Outcome Status** (`incident_outcome_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the incident (Active, Resolved, or Pending).
* **Other Status** (`incident_outcome_status_other`, text, required/optional): If the status is Other, provide a detailed description of the status.
* **Status Date** (`incident_outcome_status_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the status.
* **Status Details** (`incident_outcome_status_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the status.
* **Status Comments** (`incident_outcome_status_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments about the status.
* **Follow-up Date** (`incident_outcome_status_follow_up_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`incident_outcome_status_follow_up_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency of the follow-up (Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Other Frequency** (`incident_outcome_status_follow_up_frequency_other`, text, required/optional): If the frequency is Other, provide a detailed description of the frequency.
* **Follow-up Method** (`incident_outcome_status_follow_up_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the follow-up method (Phone, Email, In-person, Video, or Other).
* **Other Method** (`incident_outcome_status_follow_up_method_other`, text, required/optional): If the follow-up method is Other, provide a detailed description of the method.
