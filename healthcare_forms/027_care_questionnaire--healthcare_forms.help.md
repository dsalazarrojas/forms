# Care Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on a person's healthcare needs and experiences to provide comprehensive care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your details in the provided fields, which are:
	- **Respondent name**: Please enter your name, which will help us identify you as the person completing this form.
	* **Relationship to patient**: Select one of the options: Self, Family, Caregiver, or Other.
	* **Physical health concerns**: Describe your current physical health concerns.
	* **Mental health concerns**: If you have any emotional or cognitive issues.
	* **Mobility status**: Select one of the options: Independent, Assisted, or Dependent.
	* **Medication list**: List your current medications and dosages.
	* **Care goals**: Priorities for your care.
	* **Additional comments**: Any other helpful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: Please enter your name, which will help us identify you as the person completing this form.
* **Relationship to patient**: This field is used to determine the connection between you and the patient. Select one of the options: Self, Family, Caregiver, or Other.
* **Physical health concerns**: Describe any current physical health concerns you may have. This is an opportunity to let us know about any physical limitations or issues affecting you.
* **Mental health concerns**: If you have any emotional or cognitive issues, please describe them here.
* **Mobility status**: This field is used to identify your level of independence. Select one of the options: Independent, Assisted, or Dependent.
* **Medication list**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Care goals**: Priorities for your care should be listed here.
* **Social support available**: Describe any available social support you may have, such as family or friends.
* **Mobility status**: Please select one of the options: Independent, Assisted, or Dependent.
* **Medication list**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
*   **Care goals**: Priorities for your care should be listed here.
* **Additional comments**: Any other helpful information you may have can be described here.
