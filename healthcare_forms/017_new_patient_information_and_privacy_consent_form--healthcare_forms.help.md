# new-patient-information-and-privacy-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from new patients and obtain their consent for data collection and privacy policies in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Name" field with your full name.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
4. If you have a contact email address, enter it in the "Contact E-mail" field.
5. Enter your contact address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address if you have one.
