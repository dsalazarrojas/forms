# Nutrition Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Referral Form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions regarding their nutrition needs and referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section (page 1) with the patient's details, including name, contact information, and other relevant personal details.
2. Provide the referral reason for the patient's nutrition referral in the "Referral Reason" section.
3. Enter the name of the dietitian/registered dietitian responsible for the patient's care.
4. Select the date requested for the referral and the date the patient is available to attend.
5. List any dietary restrictions the patient may have in the "Dietary Restrictions" section.
6. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" section.
7. List any allergies or contraindications in the "Allergy/Contraindications" section.
8. Make any special requests in the "Special Requests" section.
9. Finally, sign the form with your patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for the referral.
- **Dietitian/Registered Dietitian Name** (`dietician_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the dietitian/registered dietitian responsible for the patient's care.
- **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, `date`, required: false): Select the date the referral is requested.
- **Date Available** (`date_available`, `date`, required: false): Select the date the patient is available to attend.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required: false): List any dietary restrictions the patient may have.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history.
- **Allergy/Contraindications** (`allergy_contraindications`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or contraindications.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): Make any special requests.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign the form with your patient's signature.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please consult with a healthcare professional.
- This form is not a substitute for professional medical consultations or assessments.
- The information gathered will be used to support the patient's healthcare needs.
