# disaster_relief_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The disaster_relief_registration_form is a crucial tool used to gather vital information about individuals affected by disasters, such as natural catastrophes, accidents, or other emergencies. This form helps emergency services and medical professionals assess the severity of the situation and provide the necessary aid and care to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in a safe and stable environment before accessing this form.
2. Carefully review each section of the form and provide accurate and honest information.
3. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Emergency Contact Information (id: 1)
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, optional): This section is designed to capture essential contact information from a trusted individual, such as a family member or close friend. This information will be used by emergency services to reach out to you if needed.
