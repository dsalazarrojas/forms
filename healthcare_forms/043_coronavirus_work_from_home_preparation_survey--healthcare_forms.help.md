# Coronavirus Work From Home Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess and prepare for working from home during the coronavirus pandemic. It collects information about home preparation, work-from-home experiences, and precautions to ensure a safe and effective work-from-home setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review each section below and provide the requested information about your home preparation, work-from-home experiences, and precautions taken.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each section.
3. Scroll down to the next section if you need to provide information for multiple topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label Home Preparation (1): 
**Home Preparations**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
This field is for you to describe the preparations you have made at your home to work effectively. This may include setting up a dedicated workspace, acquiring necessary equipment, or other relevant details.

### <label Work From Home (2): 
**Work From Home**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
In this field, please describe your previous experience with working from home, including any challenges you've faced and how you've addressed them.

### <label Work From Home Location (3): 
**Work From Home Location**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
Please enter the location where you plan to work from home, including the address or a description of your home office space.

### <label Family Preparation (4): 
**Family Preparation**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
Here, you can describe any preparations made by your family members to work from home, such as setting up a separate workspace or adjusting schedules.

### <label Emergency Plan (5): 
**Emergency Plan**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
Describe the emergency plan in place in case of a coronavirus outbreak, including any protocols for isolating oneself or seeking medical attention.

### <label Equipment Preparation (6): 
**Equipment Preparation**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
List any equipment or software needed to perform your work-from-home tasks, such as a computer, internet connection, or specific software requirements.

### <label Remote Work From Home Experience (7): 
**Remote Work From Home Experience**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
 Share your experience with remote work-from-home arrangements, including any benefits or challenges you've faced.

### <label Coronavirus Precautions (8): 
**Coronavirus Precautions**
**type:** text
**required/optional:** false**
List any precautions you take to prevent the spread of the virus, such as frequent handwashing, social distancing, or wearing protective gear.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please explain your thought process or concerns.
* If you need to provide additional information not covered in the form, use the comments field or contact the administrator.

This form is designed to provide insights into the preparedness and experiences of individuals working from home during the coronavirus pandemic. Please answer these questions to help improve the working-from-home setup and reduce the risk of coronavirus transmission.
