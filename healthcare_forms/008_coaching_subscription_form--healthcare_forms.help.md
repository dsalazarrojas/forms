# Coaching Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Subscription Form is designed to collect necessary information from potential coaching clients to determine their coaching subscription preferences. This form helps to identify their goals, coaching type, and delivery methods to provide an accurate coaching experience. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure we can tailor our coaching programs to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the coaching type you're interested in from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred coaching subscription duration and frequency.
3. Choose your preferred session duration in minutes.
4. Select your preferred delivery method for coaching sessions (in-person, online, phone, or hybrid).
5. Choose your preferred session times.
6. Provide your personal and contact information (first name, last name, email address, and phone number).
7. Select if you have any medical clearance for coaching (if applicable).
8. Choose your payment method for the coaching subscription.
9. Accept the terms and conditions of the coaching agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your preferred contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Type of Coaching** (`coaching_type`, select_one, required): Choose the coaching program you're interested in (Personal Training, Nutrition Coaching, Wellness Coaching, Performance Coaching, Life Coaching, or Group Classes).
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required): Select your preferred coaching subscription duration (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annual, or Pay-per-session).
* **Preferred Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like coaching sessions (once per week, twice per week, three times per week, four times per week, or custom).
* **Session Duration - minutes** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of each coaching session in minutes (30, 45, 60, 90, or custom).
* **Coach Preference** (`coach_preference`, text, optional): If you have a preferred coach, enter their name.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive coaching (in-person, online, phone, or hybrid).
* **Your Timezone** (`timezone`, text, required): Enter your timezone for scheduling purposes.
* **Preferred Session Times** (`preferred_time`, select_multiple, required): Select the times that work best for your coaching sessions (early morning, late morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible).
* **Primary Goal** (`primary_goal`, text, required): Describe your main coaching objective.
* **Specific Challenges or Concerns** (`specific_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any particular issues you want to address.
* **Do you have medical clearance for coaching?** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): If you have medical clearance for coaching, select 'Yes'; otherwise, select 'No' or 'Not Applicable'.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the coaching subscription (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Digital Wallet).
* **I accept the terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Select 'I accept' to complete the subscription.
