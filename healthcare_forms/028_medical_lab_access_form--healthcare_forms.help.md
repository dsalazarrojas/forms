# medical lab access form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Lab Access Form is used to request access to a medical lab for a specific user.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the user who needs access to the lab from the "User" dropdown menu.
2.  Choose the date of access using the date picker.
3.  Choose the time of access using the time picker.
4.  Provide a brief reason for requesting access in the "Reason" field.
5.  Select the level of access required from the "Access Level" dropdown menu.
6.  Choose the duration of access in hours.
7.  Select the authorized staff members who will be granting access.
8.  Sign the form to confirm your request.
9.  Enter any additional comments.
