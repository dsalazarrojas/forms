# Health Insurance Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare clients for insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and fields to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your general information in the "General Information" page.
3. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Information" page.
4. Select your insurance policies from the "Insurance Policies" page.
5. Fill in your policy application details in the "Policy Application" page.
6. Provide your provider information in the "Provider Information" page.
7. Select your additional information from the "Additional Info" page.
8. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
9. Select your policy status from the "Policy Status" page.
10. Add any policy notes or comments in the "Policy Notes" page.
11. Finally, provide any client comments or feedback in the "Client Comments" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1, Text): Fill in your general information, including your name, title, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1, Text): Fill in your contact information, including your address, email, and phone number.
* **Insurance Policies** (Page 2, Select Multiple): Select all the insurance policies you have, such as health insurance, other insurance, or no insurance.
* **Policy Application** (Page 2, Text): Fill in your policy application details, including your policy number and any other relevant information.
* **Provider Information** (Page 2, Text): Fill in your provider information, including your provider's name and contact details.
* **Additional Info** (Page 3, Select One): Select the correct information type, such as broker, agent, other, or none.
* **Contact Details** (Page 3, Text): Fill in your contact details, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Policy Status** (Page 4, Select One): Select your policy status, such as active, inactive, or cancelled.
* **Policy Notes** (Page 4, Text): Add any policy notes or comments.
* **Client Comments** (Page 5, Text): Fill in your client comments or feedback.
