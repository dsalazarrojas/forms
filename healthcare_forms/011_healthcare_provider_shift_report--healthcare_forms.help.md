# Healthcare Provider Shift Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a healthcare provider's shift and provide updates and comments for other staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Start of Shift" field with the time you started your shift.
2. Fill in the "End of Shift" field with the time you finished your shift.
3. Select the staff members present during your shift from the "Staff Present" field.
4. Provide any incidents that occurred during your shift in the "Incidents" field.
5. Enter any patient updates in the "Patient Updates" field.
6. Provide handover notes in the "Handover Notes" field.
7. Enter any medication administered during your shift in the "Medication Administered" field.
8. Identify any important incidents that occurred during your shift from the "Important Incidents" field.
9. Provide comments or additional notes in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter any staff comments or feedback in the "Staff Comments" field.
11. Enter any medication administered comments in the "Medication Administered Comments" field.
12. Sign the "Handover Notes" field with your signature.
13. Add your signature to the "Staff Signature" field.
14. If applicable, add the patient's signature to the "Patient Signature" field.
15. Enter the date you completed the form in the "Date" field.
14. Enter the person completing the form in the "Completed By" field.
15. Enter the completion date in the "Completed On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start of Shift** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you started your shift.
* **End of Shift** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you finished your shift.
* **Staff Present** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the staff members present during your shift.
* **Incidents** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any incidents that occurred during your shift.
* **Patient Updates** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any patient updates.
* **Handover Notes** (`text`, required/optional): Provide handover notes for other staff members.
* **Medication Administered** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any medication administered during your shift.
* **Important Incidents** (`select_one`, required/optional): Identify any important incidents that occurred during your shift.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Provide comments or additional notes.
* **Staff Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any staff comments or feedback.
* **Medication Administered Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any comments about medication administered.
* **Handover Notes Signature** (`text`, required/optional): Sign the handover notes with your signature.
* **Staff Signature** (`text`, required/optional): Add your signature to the form.
* **Patient Signature** (`text`, required/optional): Add the patient's signature to the form if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Completed By** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the person completing the form.
* **Completed On** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the completion date.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's essential to fill in any relevant information to ensure accurate reporting and communication.
