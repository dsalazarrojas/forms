# Dental Office Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and report incidents that occur in the dental office, including incidents involving patients, staff, or third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Select the appropriate incident type**: Choose the category that best describes the incident (Patient Incident, Staff Incident, or Third Party Incident).
2. **Enter incident date**: Provide the date when the incident occurred.
3. **Location**: Enter the location where the incident took place in the office.
4. **Incident description**: Provide a brief summary of what happened during the incident.
5. **Patient involvement**: Indicate if the patient was involved in the incident.
6. **Staff involvement**: Indicate if staff members were involved in the incident.
7. **Third party involvement**: Indicate if a third party was involved in the incident.
8. **Incident outcome**: Describe the outcome of the incident.
9. **Root cause**: Enter the reason behind the incident.
10. **Action taken**: Briefly describe any actions taken to address the incident.
11. **Review status**: Choose the status of the incident review (Open, Closed, or Pending).
12. **Review date**: Enter the date when the review was completed.
13. **Review comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative before submitting the form.
