# Dental Clinic Management Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about dental clinics, including their services, location, operating hours, and other relevant details. This form helps in formalizing the owner-management relationship and ensures accurate records for management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill in the relevant sections with accurate and up-to-date information about the clinic.
3. Select the services and equipment provided by the clinic that apply to you.
4. Enter the clinic's operating hours and other relevant details.
5. Proofread the form carefully to ensure everything is correct before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Formalizing Clinic Owner-Management Relationship (1)**: A brief overview of the clinic's owner-management relationship, providing a context for the rest of the form.
* **Clinic Owner Information (2)**: Enter the information about the owner(s) of the clinic, including their name(s), contact details, etc.
* **Management Contact (3)**: Enter the management contact person's email address.
* **Clinic Services (4)**: Select all services provided by the clinic from the dropdown list (Dental Cleaning, Dental X-rays, Dental Fillings, etc.).
* **Clinic Location (5)**: Choose the clinic's location from the dropdown list (Main Street, Sub Street, Clinic Avenue, etc.).
* **Clinic Hours (6)**: Enter the clinic's operating hours, including the start and end time.
* **Clinic Contact (7)**: Enter the clinic's contact person's name and contact details.
* **Clinic Notes (8)**: Add any additional notes about the clinic.
* **Clinic Employees (9)**: Enter the number of employees working at the clinic.
* **Clinic Equipment (10)**: Select all equipment used by the clinic from the dropdown list (Dental Chair, X-Ray Machine, Syringe, etc.).
* **Clinic Fees (11)**: Enter the clinic's fees for services.
* **Clinic Insurance (12)**: Enter the clinic's insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact (13)**: Enter the emergency contact person's name and contact details.
* **Other Services Provided (14)**: Select all other services provided by the clinic from the dropdown list (Surgery, Orthodontics, Endoscopy, etc.).
* **Other Clinic Notes (15)**: Add any additional notes about the clinic's other services.
* **Other Clinic Equipment (16)**: Select all other equipment used by the clinic from the dropdown list (Syringe, Dental Chair, X-Ray Machine, etc.).
* **Other Clinic Employees (17)**: Enter the number of other clinic employees.
* **Other Clinic Fees (18)**: Enter the other clinic's fees for services.
* **Other Clinic Insurance (19)**: Enter the other clinic's insurance information.
* **Other Clinic Insurance Contact (20)**: Enter the other clinic's insurance contact person's name and contact details.
* **Other Clinic Notes (21)**: Add any additional notes about the other clinic.
* **Other Clinic Location (22)**: Choose the other clinic's location from the dropdown list (Main Street, Sub Street, Clinic Avenue, etc.).
* **Other Clinic Hours (23)**: Enter the other clinic's operating hours.
* **Other Clinic Fees Notes (24)**: Add any additional notes about the other clinic's fees.
* **Other Clinic Equipment Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes about the other clinic's equipment.
