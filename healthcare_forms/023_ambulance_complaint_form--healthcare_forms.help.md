This is the Ambulance Complaint Form help guide. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and process of completing the form.

## Purpose
The Ambulance Complaint Form is designed to collect information about incidents or complaints related to ambulance services provided by our organization. This form helps us to identify areas of improvement and ensure that our services meet the highest standards of quality and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of complaint you are submitting.
2. Provide detailed information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your personal contact information so we can get back to you.
4. Provide information about the patient involved, if applicable.
5. If you have any supporting documentation, please describe it.
6. Confirm that the information you provide is accurate.
7. Specify the resolution you are seeking.
8. Choose how you would like us to respond to your complaint.
9. Review and sign the form to indicate your consent to investigate the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type of Complaint**: Select the primary category of your complaint from the list below:
	+ Response time
	+ Staff behavior
	+ Quality of care
	+ Billing issue
	+ Equipment concern
	+ Vehicle condition
	+ Communication issue
	+ Other
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the approximate time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the pickup location or address of the incident.
* **Ambulance Unit Number**: If known, enter the ambulance unit number.
* **Destination Facility**: Enter the name of the destination facility.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Relationship to Patient**: Select the relationship you have to the patient from the list below:
	+ Self - I was the patient
	+ Spouse/Partner
	+ Parent
	+ Child
	+ Sibling
	+ Other family member
	+ Friend
	+ Witness
	+ Other
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Patient First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth, if known.
* **Can You Identify the Staff Involved**: Select yes if you can identify the staff involved.
* **Staff Names**: If applicable, enter the names of the staff involved.
* **Staff Descriptions**: If applicable, enter a physical description or role of the staff involved.
* **Approximate Number of Staff Involved**: If applicable, enter the approximate number of staff involved.
* **Detailed Description of Complaint**: Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Response Time Concerns**: If applicable, describe concerns related to response time.
* **Quality of Care Concerns**: If applicable, describe concerns related to quality of care.
* **Staff Behavior Concerns**: If applicable, describe specific behaviors of the staff that caused concern.
* **Were There Any Witnesses**: Select yes if there were witnesses.
* **Witness Names and Contact Information**: If applicable, enter the names and contact information of witnesses.
* **How Did This Affect the Patient**: Describe the impact on the patient.
* **What Was the Outcome**: Describe the outcome of the incident.
* **Have You Contacted Us Before About This**: Select yes if you have contacted us before about this incident.
* **Date of Previous Contact**: If applicable, enter the date of previous contact.
* **Outcome of Previous Contact**: If applicable, enter the outcome of previous contact.
* **What Resolution Are You Seeking**: Describe the resolution you are seeking.
* **How Would You Like Us to Respond**: Select how you would like us to respond to your complaint.
* **Do You Have Supporting Documentation**: Select yes if you have supporting documentation.
* **Describe Documentation**: If applicable, describe the documentation you have.
* **Consent to Investigate**: Select yes to authorize an investigation of this complaint.
* **Information Accuracy**: Select yes to confirm that the information you provide is accurate.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date of submission in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
