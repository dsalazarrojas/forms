# skincare_facial_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain client consent and gather information about their upcoming visit to the healthcare provider. It is designed to ensure that the client understands their expectations and agrees to the skincare treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form

### To begin, select "Upcoming visit" page and provide the date of your next visit.
- **Upcoming visit** (`upcoming_visit`, text, required/optional): Enter the date of your next visit to schedule the treatment.

### On the "Expectations" page, briefly describe your expectations from the treatment.
- **Expectations** (`expectations`, text, required/optional): Describe your expectations from the treatment, such as what you hope to achieve or what you are hoping to get out of it.

### Next, select the skincare treatment options you are interested in.
- **Skincare treatment** (`skincare_treatment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more of the available options for your treatment.

### Finally, provide your client consent for the treatment.
- **Client consent** (`client_consent`, note, required/optional): Please read and sign this form to indicate that you have understood and agreed to the treatment.

## Tips

- Please make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure that your visit and treatment are properly recorded.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or options, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Remember to review and understand each field before submitting the form to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
