# Business Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses assess their overall health and wellness within the organization. It covers various aspects such as organizational structure, employee satisfaction, health and safety concerns, employee programs, and technology infrastructure.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please complete this survey honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3.  If you're unsure about any question, please select "Don't know" or "Not applicable" as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Organization Name (`organization_name`)
*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.

### 2. Industry Type (`industry_type`)
*   **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary industry your organization operates in.

### 3. Number of Employees (`number_of_employees`)
*   **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of full-time and part-time employees.

### 4. Years in Operation (`years_in_operation`)
*   **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`, `number`, required): Enter how many years your organization has been operating.

### 5. Annual Revenue Range (`annual_revenue_range`)
*   **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your organization's annual revenue bracket.

### 6. Organizational Structure (`organizational_structure`)
*   **Organizational Structure** (`organizational_structure`, `select_one`, required): How would you describe your current organizational structure.

### 7. Employee Satisfaction Rating (`employee_satisfaction_rating`)
*   **Employee Satisfaction Rating** (`employee_satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required): Rate overall employee satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 10.

### 8. Turnover Rate (`turnover_rate`)
*   **Turnover Rate** (`turnover_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate percentage of employees who left in the past year.

### 9. Primary Health and Safety Concerns (`health_safety_concerns`)
*   **Primary Health and Safety Concerns** (`health_safety_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas of concern regarding employee health and safety.

### 10. Current Employee Health Programs (`current_health_programs`)
*   **Current Employee Health Programs** (`current_health_programs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all health programs currently offered.

### 11. Average Monthly Absenteeism Rate (`absenteeism_rate`)
*   **Average Monthly Absenteeism Rate** (`absenteeism_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the percentage of workdays missed due to illness or other reasons.

### 12. Main Productivity Challenges (`productivity_challenges`)
*   **Main Productivity Challenges** (`productivity_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top challenges affecting productivity.

### 13. Training Budget Percentage (`training_budget_percentage`)
*   **Training Budget Percentage** (`training_budget_percentage`, `number`, optional): What percentage of payroll is allocated to training and development?

### 14. Leadership Development Programs Available (`leadership_development_programs`)
*   **Leadership Development Programs Available** (`leadership_development_programs`, `select_one`, required): Does your organization offer leadership development programs?

### 15. Work Environment Rating (`work_environment_rating`)
*   **Work Environment Rating** (`work_environment_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall work environment quality.

### 16. Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives (`diversity_inclusion_initiatives`)
*   **Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives** (`diversity_inclusion_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all initiatives currently in place.

### 17. Technology Infrastructure Rating (`technology_infrastructure_rating`)
*   **Technology Infrastructure Rating** (`technology_infrastructure_rating`, `select_one`, required): How would you rate the effectiveness of your current technology systems?

### 18. Last Compliance Audit Date (`compliance_audit_date`)
*   **Last Compliance Audit Date** (`compliance_audit_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last regulatory compliance audit.

### 19. Top Improvement Priorities (`improvement_priorities`)
*   **Top Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top three areas needing immediate improvement.

### 20. Additional Comments (`additional_comments`)
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Please share any additional information about your organizational health.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
*   If unsure, select "Don't know" or "Not applicable" as necessary.
*   Complete this survey honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
*   If you have any additional comments or insights, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
