# Medical Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Reimbursement Claim Form is used to submit a claim for medical expenses to an insurance provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the claimant's information:
	* Provide the claimant's name.
	* Enter the claimant's contact information.
	* Enter the claimant's medical provider's name.
	* Enter the claimant's address.
	* Enter the claimant's city.
	* Enter the claimant's state.
	* Enter the claimant's zip code.
	* Enter the claimant's phone number.
	* Enter the claimant's email (optional).
2. Enter claimant's date of birth.
3. Select insurance provider.
4. Enter insurance plan.
5. Enter policy number.
6. Enter group plan.
7. Enter the date of service.
8. Enter the date of service end.
9. Enter the amount claimed.
10. Select the currency of the amount claimed.
11. Enter a description of the medical services rendered.
12. Enter the claimant's signature and the claimant's medical provider signature.
13. Select the claimant's relationship with the provider.
14. Enter any other information (optional).
14. Select the submit option from the options below.
	* Claimant Name
	* Claimant Contact Information
	* Claimant Medical Provider
	* Claimant Policy Number
	* Claimant Group Plan
	* Claimant Date Of Service
	* Claimant Date Of Service End
	* Claimant Amount
	* Claimant Description
15. Click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name**: Enter the claimant's full name.
* **Claimant Contact Information**: Enter the claimant's phone number and email.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the medical provider who rendered the services.
* **Claimant Address**: Enter the claimant's home or office address.
* **Claimant City**: Enter the claimant's city of residence.
* **Claimant State**: Enter the claimant's state or province.
* **Claimant Zip**: Enter the claimant's zip code.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter the claimant's email (if available).
* **Claimant Date Of Birth**: Enter the claimant's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider from the options.
* **Insurance Plan**: Select the insurance plan from the options.
* **Claimant Policy Number**: Enter the claimant's policy number.
* **Claimant Group Plan**: Enter the claimant's group plan (if applicable).
* **Claimant Date Of Service**: Enter the date the services were rendered in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Claimant Date Of Service End**: Enter the date the services ended in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Claimant Amount**: Enter the amount claimed in the selected currency.
* **Claimant Currency**: Select the currency of the amount claimed.
* **Claimant Description**: Enter a description of the medical services rendered.
* **Claimant Signature**: Enter the claimant's signature.
* **Claimant Provider Signature**: Enter the medical provider's signature.
* **Insurance Provider Signature**: Enter the insurance provider's signature.
* **Claimant Relationship**: Select the claimant's relationship with the provider.
* **Claimant Other Information**: Enter any other relevant information (optional).
* **Submit**: Select the option to submit the form.
