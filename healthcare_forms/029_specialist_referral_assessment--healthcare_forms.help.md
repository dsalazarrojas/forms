# Specialist Referral Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a specialist referral assessment, which is a process where a healthcare provider refers a patient to a specialist for further evaluation or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the specialist you are referring the patient to in the **Specialists Name** field.
2. Select the **Medical Specialist Department** that the specialist works in.
3. Enter the reason for referral in the **Referral Reason** field.
4. If necessary, provide additional **Clinical Details** about the patient in the notes field.
5. Enter the date of the specialist referral in the **Specialist Referral Date** field.
6. If applicable, enter the time of the specialist referral in the **Specialist Referral Time** field.
7. Select the time zone of the specialist referral from the dropdown menu.
8. If you wish to include additional information about the patient, enter it in the **Patient Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Specialists Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the specialist you are referring the patient to.
* **Medical Specialist Department (select_one, required)**: Select the department the specialist works in.
* **Referral Reason (text, required)**: Enter the reason for referral.
* **Clinical Details (note, optional)**: Provide any additional notes about the patient's clinical status.
* **Specialist Note (text, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the specialist referral.
* **Specialist Referral Date (date, required)**: Enter the date of the specialist referral in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Specialist Referral Time (time, optional)**: Enter the time of the specialist referral in 12-hour format (e.g., 02:00 PM).
* **Specialist Referral Time Zone (select_one, required)**: Select the time zone of the specialist referral.
* **Patient Information (text, optional)**: Enter any additional information about the patient, if necessary.
