# Cervical Cancer Awareness Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cervical Cancer Awareness Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge about cervical cancer and its prevention. It is essential to answer the questions carefully and honestly to help the health team provide you with more targeted education and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidelines

- Please answer the questions honestly and carefully.
- Select all the options that apply for the multiple-choice questions.
- If you have any questions or comments, you can provide them in the "Any questions or comments for our health team?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Awareness Quiz Information (Section)

*   **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your official identification document.
*   **Participant Email Address** (`participant_email_address`, email, required): The email address where you would like to receive your quiz results and educational resources.
*   **Quiz Questions (Section)**

    *   **Is the Human Papillomavirus (HPV) a common cause of cervical cancer?** (`quiz_hpv_cause`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - True
        - False
        - Unsure
    *   **What is the primary screening test for cervical cancer?** (`quiz_screening_method`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - Mammogram
        - Pap Smear (Pap Test)
        - Colonoscopy
        - Blood Pressure Check
    *   **Is there a vaccine available to help prevent cervical cancer?** (`quiz_prevention_vaccine`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - Yes, the HPV vaccine
        - False
        - Only for other types of cancer
    *   **Does cervical cancer always show symptoms in its early stages?** (`quiz_symptoms_early_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - Yes, always
        - No, it is often asymptomatic early on
        - Only in older adults
    *   **Which of the following are known risk factors for cervical cancer?** (`quiz_risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that apply.
        - Smoking
        - Weakened immune system
        - Long-term use of oral contraceptives
        - Family history
        - Lack of regular screening
    *   **At what age should regular cervical cancer screening typically begin?** (`quiz_screening_age`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - Age 13
        - Age 21
        - Age 35
        - Age 50
    *   **How often is a Pap test generally recommended for most individuals?** (`quiz_frequency_pap`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
        - Every Year
        - Every 3 Years
        - Every 5 Years
        - Every 10 Years

*   **Knowledge Self-Assessment (Section)**

    *   **How confident are you in your knowledge of cervical cancer prevention?** (`self_confidence_rating`, number, required): Rate your confidence level from 1 (Not confident) to 5 (Very confident)
*   **Would you like to receive more educational materials?** (`interest_in_resources`, select_one, required): Choose the correct option from the list.
    - Yes, please send via email
    - No, thank you

### Tips

- If you are unsure or have any questions, please provide them in the "Any questions or comments for our health team?" field.
- Please answer all the questions to the best of your knowledge and ability.
- If you have any concerns or need further clarification, please contact your healthcare provider or a trusted medical professional.
