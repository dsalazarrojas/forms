# mental_health_counselor_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health counseling reservation form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal and session details. This form helps mental health counselors gather essential data to provide accurate and effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Enter your personal contact information and demographics on this page.
2. **Session Details**: Provide information about the session you are requesting, including the date, time, and duration.
3. **Preferences**: Share any personal preferences or requirements you have for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Details
* **Name** (`input`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`input`, `email`, required): Provide your email address where you can be reached by the counselor.
* **Phone Number** (`input`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Birth Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select your date of birth.
* **Gender** (`select`, `list`, required): Choose your gender identity.
* **Language** (`select`, `list`, optional): Select your preferred language for the session.

### Session Details
* **Session Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for your session.
* **Session Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select a time slot for your session.
* **Session Duration** (`select`, `list`, required): Choose the length of your session.
* **Session Type** (`select`, `list`, required): Select the type of session you need.

### Preferences
* **Special Requests** (`text`, `text`, optional): Share any special requests or needs you have for the session.

### Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to ask the counselor.
