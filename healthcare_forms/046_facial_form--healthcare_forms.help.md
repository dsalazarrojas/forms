# Facial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Facial Form" is designed for medical professionals to collect and document patient information, especially for patients with facial issues or concerns. It captures crucial data for diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with the patient's identifying information.
2. Enter the patient's Email and Phone Number for contact purposes.
3. Describe the Medical History, including any relevant health issues or concerns.
4. Provide the Birthdate, Time of Infection, and Date of Infection for a clear understanding of the patient's condition.
5. Enter the Test Results and Test Date for further evaluation.
6. Select the relevant Treatment Options for the patient's care.
7. Sign and date the Doctor Signature and Patient Signature fields to confirm consent and agreement.
8. Enter the Medical Record Number and Medical Provider's Name (if applicable) for record keeping.
9. Finally, ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's first name as per their identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's last name as per their identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any health issues or concerns.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's birthdate for age-related purposes.
* **Time of Infection** (`time_of_infection`, `time`, required): Enter the time of infection for time-related purposes.
* **Date of Infection** (`date_of_infection`, `date`, required): Enter the date of infection for record keeping.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's symptoms if applicable.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, `text`, required): Enter the test results for evaluation purposes.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the test for record keeping.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider, if applicable.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant treatment options for the patient's care.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date to confirm treatment agreement.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date to confirm consent.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the medical professional, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of completion for record keeping.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of completion for record keeping.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the medical provider.
* **Test Date/Time** (`test_date_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the date and time of the test.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required): Enter the medical record number for record keeping.
* **Medical Record Number 2** (`medical_record_number2`, `text`, optional): Enter the secondary medical record number, if applicable.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history2`, `note`, optional): Describe the secondary medical history, if applicable.
* **Doctor Name 2** (`doctor_name2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the secondary medical provider, if applicable.
* **Medical Provider 2** (`medical_provider2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the secondary medical provider, if applicable.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If any field is unclear, please ask your medical provider for clarification.
