# Foot And Ankle Care Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Foot and Ankle Care Center Registration Form" is a comprehensive tool used to collect patient information, appointment details, and medical records for proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Input the patient's date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required/optional): Provide the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's insurance number, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Provide the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): List any allergies or medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's name and number.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Schedule an appointment by entering the date.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the appointment time.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Select the doctor's name from the provided list.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, text, required/optional): Choose the doctor's specialty from the options.
* **Patient Type** (`patient_type`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient is a new or existing patient.
* **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's ID number.
* **Clinic ID** (`clinic_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the clinic ID number.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the patient's status (active, inactive, or pending) from the options.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the appointment status (pending, confirmed, or cancelled) from the options.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the follow-up status (pending, confirmed, or cancelled) from the options.
* **Doctor Availability** (`doctor_availability`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the doctor's availability (available or unavailable) from the options.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate the payment status (paid, unpaid, or pending) from the options.
