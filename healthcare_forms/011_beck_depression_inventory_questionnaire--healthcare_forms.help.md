# Beck Depression Inventory Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Beck Depression Inventory (BDI) Questionnaire is a standardized self-report instrument used to measure the presence and severity of depression in patients. The questionnaire is designed to assess various aspects of depressive symptoms, including mood, motivation, and overall functioning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and honestly, indicating how you have been feeling over the past two weeks.
2.  Be sure to answer based on your experiences over the past two weeks, rather than how you typically feel or how you feel today.
3.  Select one of the options for each question that best describes your current feelings or experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Patient ID or Date of Birth** (2, text, required): Enter your identification number or date of birth for identification purposes.
*   **Assessment Date** (3, date, required): Enter the current date.
*   **Age** (4, number, required): Enter your age in years.
*   **Gender** (5, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options:
    *   Male
    *   Female
    *   Non-binary
    *   Prefer not to say
    *   Other
*   **Sadness** (6, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes your sadness over the past two weeks:
    *   I do not feel sad
    *   I feel sad much of the time
    *   I am sad all the time and cannot snap out of it
    *   I am so sad or unhappy that I cannot stand it
*   **Pessimism** (7, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about your future:
    *   I am not discouraged about my future
    *   I feel more discouraged than usual
    *   I do not expect things to work out for me
    *   I feel my future is hopeless and will only get worse
*   **Past Failure** (8, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you view your past:
    *   I do not feel like a failure
    *   I have failed more than I should have
    *   As I look back, I see a lot of failures
    *   I feel I am a total failure as a person
*   **Loss of Pleasure** (9, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes your ability to experience pleasure:
    *   I get as much satisfaction as ever
    *   I do not enjoy things as I used to
    *   I do not get real satisfaction from anything anymore
    *   I am dissatisfied or bored with everything
*   **Guilty Feelings** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how often you feel guilty:
    *   I do not feel particularly guilty
    *   I feel guilty over many things I have done
    *   I feel quite guilty and feel bad most of the time
    *   I feel as if I am being punished
*   **Punishment Feelings** (11, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about being punished:
    *   I do not feel I am being punished
    *   I feel I may be punished
    *   I expect to be punished
    *   I feel I am being punished
*   **Self-Dislike** (12, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about yourself:
    *   I feel the same about myself as ever
    *   I have lost confidence in myself
    *   I am disappointed in myself
    *   I dislike myself
*   **Self-Criticalness** (13, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how critical you are of yourself:
    *   I do not criticize or blame myself more than usual
    *   I am more critical of myself than usual
    *   I criticize myself for all of my faults
    *   I blame myself for everything bad that happens
*   **Suicidal Thoughts or Wishes** (14, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes whether you have had thoughts of self-harm:
    *   I do not have any thoughts of killing myself
    *   I have thoughts of killing myself but would not carry them out
    *   I would like to kill myself
    *   I would kill myself if I had the chance
*   **Crying** (15, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how often you cry:
    *   I do not cry any more than usual
    *   I cry more now than I used to
    *   I cry all the time now
    *   I used to be able to cry but now I cannot cry even though I want to
*   **Agitation** (16, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how restless you feel:
    *   I am no more restless than usual
    *   I feel more restless than usual
    *   I am so restless I have to keep moving
    *   I am so restless I cannot sit still
*   **Loss of Interest** (17, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about other people:
    *   I have not lost interest in other people
    *   I am less interested in other people than I used to be
    *   I have lost most of my interest in other people
    *   I have lost all of my interest in other people
*   **Indecisiveness** (18, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes your ability to make decisions:
    *   I make decisions about as well as ever
    *   I find it more difficult to make decisions
    *   I have much greater difficulty making decisions
    *   I have trouble making any decisions
*   **Worthlessness** (19, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about your worth as a person:
    *   I do not feel I am worthless
    *   I do not consider myself as worthwhile as I used to be
    *   I feel more worthless compared to others
    *   I feel utterly worthless
*   **Loss of Energy** (20, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes your energy level:
    *   I have as much energy as ever
    *   I have less energy than I used to have
    *   I do not have enough energy to do anything
*   **Changes in Sleeping Pattern** (21, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how your sleep has changed:
    *   I sleep as well as I used to
    *   I sleep somewhat more than usual
    *   I sleep somewhat less than usual
    *   I sleep a lot more or a lot less than usual
*   **Irritability** (22, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how irritable you feel:
    *   I am no more irritable than usual
    *   I am more irritable than usual
    *   I am much more irritable than usual
    *   I am irritable all the time
*   **Changes in Appetite** (23, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how your appetite has changed:
    *   I have not experienced any change in appetite
    *   My appetite is somewhat less than usual
    *   My appetite is much less than before
    *   I have no appetite at all
*   **Concentration Difficulty** (24, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes your ability to concentrate:
    *   I can concentrate as well as ever
    *   I cannot concentrate as well as usual
    *   It is hard to keep my mind on anything for very long
    *   I find I cannot concentrate on anything
*   **Tiredness or Fatigue** (25, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how tired you feel:
    *   I am no more tired than usual
    *   I get more tired than usual
    *   I am too tired to do a lot of the things I used to do
    *   I am too tired to do most of the things I used to do
*   **Loss of Interest in Sex** (26, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes how you feel about sex:
    *   I have not noticed any recent change in interest in sex
    *   I am less interested in sex than I used to be
    *   I have almost no interest in sex
    *   I have lost interest in sex completely
*   **Clinician Notes** (27, text, optional): Enter any additional observations or comments from your clinician.
*   **Follow-Up Recommended** (28, select_one, required): Select one of the following that describes whether follow-up is recommended:
    *   Yes urgent
    *   Yes routine
    *   No follow-up needed
