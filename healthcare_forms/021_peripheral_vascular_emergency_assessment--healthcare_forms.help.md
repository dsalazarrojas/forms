# Peripheral Vascular Emergency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess patients with peripheral vascular emergencies. It collects information on patient's vital signs, medical history, allergies, current medications, and other relevant information to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the patient's date of birth using the "Patient Dob" field.
3. Describe the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
4. Record the patient's vital signs in the "Vital Signs" field.
5. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
7. Enter the current medications in the "Current Medications" field.
8. Select the affected vascular system (Carotid Artery, Aorta, Peripheral Vessels, Other) in the "Vascular System" field.
9. Conduct a peripheral examination and note the findings in the "Peripheral Examination" field.
10. Check if the patient has undergone any vascular surgeries and select the relevant option in the "Vascular Surgeries" field.
11. Determine if the patient has any peripheral diseases and select the relevant option in the "Peripheral Diseases" field.
12. Check if the patient has any vascular diseases and select the relevant option in the "Vascular Diseases" field.
13. Determine if the patient has undergone any peripheral procedures and select the relevant option in the "Peripheral Procedures" field.
14. Check if the patient has any peripheral injuries and select the relevant option in the "Peripheral Injuries" field.
15. Describe any other symptoms not listed in the "Other Symptoms" field.
16. Select the medical image type (Chest X-ray, MRI, CT, Ultrasound, Other) in the "Medical Image" field.
17. Check the clinical findings (None, True, Other) in the "Clinical Findings" field.
18. Select the peripheral diagnosis (None, Vascular Disease, Peripheral Vascular Disease, Other) in the "Peripheral Diagnosis" field.
19. Determine if the patient requires any peripheral treatment and select the relevant option in the "Peripheral Treatment" field.
20. Schedule a follow-up date in the "Peripheral Follow Up" field.
21. Check the peripheral status (None, True, False, Other) in the "Peripheral Status" field.
22. Check the peripheral outcome (None, True, False, Other) in the "Peripheral Outcome" field.
23. Enter any comments or notes in the "Peripheral Comments" field.
24. Select the peripheral diagnosis disease (None, True, False, Other) in the "Peripheral Diagnosis Disease" field.
25. Enter the surgeon's name in the "Peripheral Surgeon Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Dob** (`Patient Dob`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or unusual sensations.
* **Vital Signs** (`Vital Signs`, `note`, required): Record the patient's vital signs, including blood pressure, pulse, respiratory rate, and other relevant metrics.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the patient has, including medications or substances that cause adverse reactions.
* **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, `text`, required): Enter any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Vascular System** (`Vascular System`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the affected vascular system (Carotid Artery, Aorta, Peripheral Vessels, Other).
* **Peripheral Examination** (`Peripheral Examination`, `note`, required): Conduct a peripheral examination and note any findings.
* **Vascular Surgeries** (`Vascular Surgeries`, `select_one`, required): Check if the patient has undergone any vascular surgeries and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Diseases** (`Peripheral Diseases`, `select_one`, required): Determine if the patient has any peripheral diseases and select the relevant option.
* **Vascular Diseases** (`Vascular Diseases`, `select_one`, required): Check if the patient has any vascular diseases and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Procedures** (`Peripheral Procedures`, `select_one`, required): Determine if the patient has undergone any peripheral procedures and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Injuries** (`Peripheral Injuries`, `select_one`, required): Check if the patient has any peripheral injuries and select the relevant option.
* **Other Symptoms** (`Other Symptoms`, `text`, required): Describe any other symptoms not listed.
* **Medical Image** (`Medical Image`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the medical image type (Chest X-ray, MRI, CT, Ultrasound, Other).
* **Clinical Findings** (`Clinical Findings`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the clinical findings (None, True, Other) and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Diagnosis** (`Peripheral Diagnosis`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the peripheral diagnosis (None, Vascular Disease, Peripheral Vascular Disease, Other).
* **Peripheral Treatment** (`Peripheral Treatment`, `select_multiple`, required): Determine if the patient requires any peripheral treatment and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Follow Up** (`Peripheral Follow Up`, `date`, required): Schedule a follow-up date.
* **Peripheral Status** (`Peripheral Status`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the peripheral status (None, True, False, Other) and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Outcome** (`Peripheral Outcome`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the peripheral outcome (None, True, False, Other) and select the relevant option.
* **Peripheral Comments** (`Peripheral Comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes about the patient's assessment.
* **Peripheral Diagnosis Disease** (`Peripheral Diagnosis Disease`, `select_one`, required): Select the peripheral diagnosis disease (None, True, False, Other).
* **Peripheral Surgeon Name** (`Peripheral Surgeon Name`, `text`, required): Enter the surgeon's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Review and confirm the patient's information before submitting the form.
* Use the form to make informed decisions about patient care.
* Keep the form up-to-date and current.
* Use the form to track patient progress.
* Review and refine the form regularly for improvement.
* Consider using the form as part of a larger assessment process.
