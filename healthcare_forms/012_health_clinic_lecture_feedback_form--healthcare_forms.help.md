# Health Clinic Lecture Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients who have attended a health clinic lecture. The purpose of this form is to gather insights and suggestions from patients to improve the quality of the clinic's lectures and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the quality of the lecture by selecting one of the four options on page 1.
2. Share your overall experience on page 2 by selecting one of the four options.
3. If you have any feedback or recommendations, type them into the text field on page 3.
4. If you wish to provide more information about yourself, please enter your name, phone number, and email address on pages 4-6.
5. Finally, if you have a message for us, type it into the text field on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How would you rate the quality of the lecture?** (`lecture_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of the lecture by selecting Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor from the dropdown menu.
- **What was your overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Share your overall experience by selecting Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor from the dropdown menu.
- **Do you have any feedback or recommendations?** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): If you have any feedback or recommendations, type them in the text field.
- **Your Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): If you wish to provide more information about yourself, enter your name here.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required: false): If you have an email address, enter it here.
- **Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, required: false): If you have any message for us, type it in here.

## Tips
* Make sure to rate the quality of the lecture honestly.
* Be as detailed as possible when sharing your overall experience.
* If you have any feedback or recommendations, try to be specific and constructive.
* Please note that all fields are optional, but providing more information will help us better understand your needs and concerns.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
