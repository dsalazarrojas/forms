# Medical Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals seeking medical assistance. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any specific healthcare provider or platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your contact information on page 1, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe your symptoms on page 2.
3. Provide your medical history on page 3.
4. List any current medications you are taking on page 4.
5. Share any allergies you have on page 5.
6. Specify your medical assistant request on page 6.
7. Provide additional contact information on page 7.
8. If necessary, provide further medical history information on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you about your medical concerns.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible to help us better understand your medical needs.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses or medical conditions that may impact your current health.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required/optional): Share any allergies you have, including the severity and any medications you are allergic to.
* **Medical Assistant Request** (`medical_assistant_request`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the specific medical assistance you are requesting, such as a prescription, appointment, or medical consultation.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, in case you need to provide further information.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required/optional): If necessary, provide further medical history information on page 8.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is intended to be a general-purpose tool and should not replace a visit to a healthcare professional. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call emergency services.
