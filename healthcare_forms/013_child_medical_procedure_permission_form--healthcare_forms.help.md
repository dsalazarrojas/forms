# Child Medical Procedure Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Medical Procedure Permission Form is used to obtain legal consent for a medical procedure to be performed on a child. The form ensures that the parent or guardian of the child understands the nature and risks of the procedure, as well as their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely**. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
2. **Fill out the form**:
	* Enter the **Permission Date**: Date of permission form submission.
	* Enter the **Parent or Guardian First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**: Legal name, email, and phone number of the parent or guardian.
	* Enter the **Parent or Guardian Address**: Full address of the parent or guardian.
	* Enter the **Child First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth**: Legal name and birth date of the child.
	* Enter the **Child Weight**: Current weight of the child in pounds or kilograms.
	* Enter the **Medical Procedure Name**: Name or type of the medical procedure.
	* Enter the **Scheduled Procedure Date** and **Time**: Date and time when the procedure is scheduled.
	* Enter the **Medical Facility Name**, **Address**, and **Phone**: Name, address, and phone number of the medical facility.
	* Enter the **Performing Physician Name**, **Phone**, and **Address**: Name, phone number, and address of the physician performing the procedure.
3. **Select the following options**:
	* **Do you understand the nature and purpose of this procedure?**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
	* **Do you understand the potential risks and complications?**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
	* **Do you understand the expected benefits of this procedure?**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
	* **Will anesthesia be used for this procedure?**: Select the correct type of anesthesia if applicable.
	* **Have the risks of anesthesia been discussed with you?**: Select "Yes, thoroughly" or "Yes, briefly" if applicable.
	* **Have alternative treatments been explained to you?**: Select "Yes, alternatives were discussed" if applicable.
	* **Have all your questions been answered?**: Select "Yes, all questions answered" if applicable.
	* **Do you grant permission for this medical procedure?**: Select "Yes, I grant permission" if you consent.
4. **Sign and date the form**: Type your full name as your electronic signature and the date of the electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Permission Date**: Date of permission form submission.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Primary phone number for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Address**: Full street address.
* **Child First Name**: Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Please provide the child's birth date.
* **Child Weight**: Current weight of the child in pounds or kilograms.
* **Medical Procedure Name**: Name or type of the medical procedure.
* **Scheduled Procedure Date**: Date when the procedure is scheduled.
* **Scheduled Procedure Time**: Time when the procedure is scheduled (optional).
* **Medical Facility Name**: Name of the hospital or medical facility.
* **Medical Facility Address**: Address of the hospital or medical facility.
* **Performing Physician Name**: Name of the physician performing the procedure.
* **Physician Phone**: Contact number for the physician.
* **Procedure Explanation Understanding**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
* **Procedure Risks Understanding**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
* **Procedure Benefits Understanding**: Select "Yes, completely" if you have a complete understanding.
* **Anesthesia Required**: Select the correct type of anesthesia if applicable.
* **Anesthesia Risks Discussed**: Select "Yes, thoroughly" or "Yes, briefly" if applicable.
* **Alternative Treatments**: Select "Yes, alternatives were discussed" if applicable.
* **Questions Addressed**: Select "Yes, all questions answered" if applicable.
* **Permission Granted**: Select "Yes, I grant permission" if you consent.
* **Blood Transfusion Consent**: Select "Yes, I consent" if applicable.
* **Additional Permissions or Restrictions**: Enter any additional permissions or restrictions (optional).
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Date of electronic signature.
* **Witness Name**: Name of witness to signature (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
* This form is a legal document and your signature is your consent.
* Please note that this is not a contract, but a form to obtain your consent.
* If you need to make any changes to the form, please notify your healthcare provider.
* Please make sure to read and understand all the fields before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider.
* Please sign and date the form accurately and completely.
* If you need any assistance with the form, please contact your healthcare provider.
