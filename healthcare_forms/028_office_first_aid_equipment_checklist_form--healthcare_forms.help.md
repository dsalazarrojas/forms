# Office First Aid Equipment Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Office First Aid Equipment Checklist Form is designed to help you ensure that your office is well-equipped to provide basic first aid in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Scroll through each section of the form, selecting the relevant options for your office.
2.  For each section, enter a description of the equipment or supplies in that category.
3.  List items that are available in the corresponding category.
4.  Indicate whether the tool is assigned for use.
5.  Repeat the process for each section of the form.
