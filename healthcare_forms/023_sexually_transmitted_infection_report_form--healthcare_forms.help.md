# Sexually Transmitted Infection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to gather information and document sexually transmitted infections (STI) in patients. It is used to collect and report patient data, symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, test results, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to collect and share patient information.
2. Review the patient's medical history and gather relevant information.
3. Complete the patient details section with accurate information.
4. Report the patient's STI symptoms, diagnosis, and test results.
5. Document any treatment plans or recommendations.
6. Update the patient's data and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Details
* **Patient Details**: This section is used to collect basic patient information, including patient name, date of birth, and contact details.

### Sti Symptoms
* **Sti Symptoms**: Please select all the symptoms that apply to the patient. This will help healthcare professionals quickly identify the presence of STI.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Please provide any relevant medical history that may affect the patient's condition.

### Diagnosis
* **Diagnosis**: Select "Yes" if the patient has been diagnosed with an STI, or "No" if not.

### Treatment
* **Treatment**: Select "Yes" if the patient has received treatment for an STI, or "No" if not.

### Test Results
* **Test Results**: This field is used to document any test results related to the patient's STI diagnosis.

### Patient Data
* **Patient Data**: Please select the date of the patient's latest test or visit.

### Doctor Notes
* **Doctor Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments that may be helpful for future reference.

### Email
* **Email**: Please enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
