# Eye Center Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and medical information from patients visiting the eye center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and date of birth.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
3. Enter your address.
4. Provide your medical history.
5. If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance information.
6. Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
7. If you have a doctor, enter their contact information (name, specialty, phone, and email).
8. If you have medical conditions or allergies, enter them here.
9. Enter your patient ID and doctor ID.
10. Sign and date the form to confirm you have reviewed and understand the information collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Address** (address, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous eye conditions or surgeries.
* **Medical Insurance** (medical_insurance, text, optional): If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance information here.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, optional): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Contact Relationship** (contact_relationship, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Doctor Note** (doctor_note, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments from your doctor.
* **Eye Center ID** (eye_center_id, text, required): Enter the ID number for this eye center visit.
* **Date of Last Visit** (date_of_last_visit, date, required): Enter the date of your last visit to this eye center.
* **Date of Next Visit** (date_of_next_visit, date, required): Enter the date of your next visit to this eye center.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medication** (medication, text, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, optional): Enter any allergies you are currently experiencing.
* **Doctor Name** (doctor_name, text, optional): Enter your doctor's name, if applicable.
* **Doctor Specialty** (doctor_specialty, text, optional): Enter your doctor's specialty, if applicable.
* **Doctor Phone** (doctor_phone, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number, if applicable.
* **Doctor Email** (doctor_email, text, optional): Enter your doctor's email address, if applicable.
* **Doctor Address** (doctor_address, text, optional): Enter your doctor's address, if applicable.
* **Patient ID** (patient_id, text, required): Enter your unique patient ID number.
* **Doctor ID** (doctor_id, text, required): Enter your doctor's ID number.
* **Patient Signature** (patient_signature, text, required): Sign and date this form to confirm you have reviewed and understand the information collected.
* **Eye Center Signature** (eye_center_signature, text, required): Sign and date this form to confirm you have reviewed and understand the information collected.
