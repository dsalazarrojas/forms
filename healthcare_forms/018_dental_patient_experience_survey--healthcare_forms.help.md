# Dental Patient Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience at the dental clinic. It includes ratings and comments on various aspects of their visit, such as staff helpfulness, schedule convenience, facilities, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the patient.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the date of your visit.
5. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
6. If you would recommend this clinic, select "Yes".
7. If you would not recommend this clinic, enter a brief reason why.
8. Provide a brief comment on your overall experience.
9. Rate the helpfulness of the clinic staff.
10. Rate the convenience of the clinic's schedule.
11. Rate the friendliness of the clinic staff.
12. Rate the cleanliness of the facilities.
13. Rate the condition of the facilities.
14. Rate the availability of staff at the facilities.
15. Rate the availability of parking at the facilities.
16. Rate the wait time at the facilities.
17. Rate the price of the facilities.
18. Rate the safety of the facilities.
19. Rate the cleanliness of the facilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as the patient.
* **Patient E-mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Experience Rating**: Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Dentist Name**: Enter the name of the dentist who performed the procedure.
* **Assistant Name**: Enter the name of the dental assistant who helped you.
* **Procedure Name**: Enter the name of the procedure performed.
* **Procedure Description**: Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Procedure Fee**: Enter the fee for the procedure.
* **Overall Experience**: Enter a brief comment on your overall experience.
* **Would Recommend**: If you would recommend this clinic, select "Yes".
* **Reasons for Not Recommending**: If you would not recommend this clinic, enter a brief reason why.
* **Improvement Area**: Suggest an area for improvement for the clinic.
* **Staff Helpfulness**: Rate the helpfulness of the clinic staff.
* **Schedule Convenience**: Rate the convenience of the clinic's schedule.
* **Staff Friendly**: Rate the friendliness of the clinic staff.
* **Facilities Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the facilities.
* **Facilities Condition**: Rate the condition of the facilities.
* **Facilities Staff Availability**: Rate the availability of staff at the facilities.
* **Facilities Parking**: Rate the availability of parking at the facilities.
* **Facilities Wait Time**: Rate the wait time at the facilities.
* **Facilities Price**: Rate the price of the facilities.
* **Facilities Safety**: Rate the safety of the facilities.
* **Facilities Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the facilities.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Improvement Area" field.
* If you would not recommend this clinic, please explain why in the "Reasons for Not Recommending" field.
* If you have any other comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Overall Experience" field.
