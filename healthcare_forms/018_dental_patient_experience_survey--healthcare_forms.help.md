# Dental Patient Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience at the dental clinic. It includes ratings and comments on various aspects of their visit, such as staff helpfulness, schedule convenience, facilities, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the patient.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the date of your visit.
5. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
6. If you would recommend this clinic, select "Yes".
7. If you would not recommend this clinic, enter a brief reason why.
8. Provide a brief comment on your overall experience.
9. Rate the helpfulness of the clinic staff.
