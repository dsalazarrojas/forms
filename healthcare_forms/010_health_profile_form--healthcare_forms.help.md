# Health Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Profile Form is designed to collect information from patients about their personal details, medical history, allergies, and medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure you have filled in all required fields (marked as required).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Information (patient_info)** (`patient_info`, text, required): This field is for entering your personal details such as your name, date of birth, or ID number. Please fill in your real name, date of birth, and ID number accurately.

### Medical History (page 2)
* **Medical History (medical_history)** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for describing any relevant medical history you have. This could include previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.

### Allergies (page 3)
* **Allergies (allergies)** (`allergies`, text, optional): This field is for listing any allergies or sensitivities you have to medications, foods, or substances.

### Medications (page 4)
* **Medications (medications)** (`medications`, text, optional): This field is for listing any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when filling in your personal details.
* If unsure about any medical information, consult your healthcare provider or contact your health insurance provider.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
