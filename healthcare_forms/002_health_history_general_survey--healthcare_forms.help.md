# health_history_general_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "health_history_general_survey", is used to gather general information about a patient's health status. It is intended to collect data on their medical conditions, medication lists, doctor's name, contact details, and medical record numbers. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's overall health situation, enabling them to provide effective care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
3.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient is taking any medications in the "Medication List" field.
4.  If the patient is taking medications, provide the dosage information in the "Dosage" field.
5.  Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
6.  Enter the patient's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
7.  Enter the patient's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
8.  Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (`Patient Name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name, including their first and last name.
* **Medical Condition (2)** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's current medical condition, e.g., diabetes, hypertension, etc.
* **Medication List (3)** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if the patient is taking any medications. Select "Yes" if they are, and "No" if they are not.
* **Dosage (4)** (`medication_dosage`, text, required: false): If the patient is taking medications, provide the dosage information, e.g., 2 pills per day.
* **Doctor Name (5)** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Contact Phone (6)** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact phone number where they can be reached.
* **Contact Email (7)** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email address where they can be reached.
* **Medical Record Number (8)** (`medical_record_number`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's medical record number for their previous medical records.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's health status.
* If the patient is taking medications, ensure to include dosage information to provide proper care.
* Double-check contact details to ensure accurate communication with the patient.
* Enter the correct medical record number to access previous medical records.
* This form is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation. If you are unsure about the patient's health status, consult a healthcare professional for further assessment.
