# Biopsychosocial Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biopsychosocial Assessment Form is used to gather information on a patient's demographics, medical history, mental state, and environmental factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient's demographic information (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Age) in section Patient Demographics.
2. Provide information on the patient's current medical conditions, medications, and allergies in section Biological Factors.
3. Answer questions on the patient's sleep quality, exercise frequency, and other psychological factors in section Psychological Factors.
4. Report on the patient's substance use, including alcohol and recreational drug use in section Substance Use.
5. Answer questions on the patient's social support network and living situation in section Social and Environmental Factors.
6. Provide any additional information on recent stressful life events and financial concerns in section Social and Environmental Factors.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's current age in years.
* **Current Medical Conditions**: List any active medical diagnoses.
* **Medications**: List all medications and dosages.
* **Allergies or Adverse Reactions**: List any allergies or reactions to medications.
* **Sleep Quality**: Choose how you rate your sleep quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Hours of Sleep per Night**: Enter the average hours of sleep per night.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Choose how often you exercise (Daily, 3-5 Times per Week, 1-2 Times per Week, Rarely, Never).
* **Current Mood**: Choose how you rate your current mood (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Anxiety Symptoms**: Choose if you experience anxiety symptoms (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Depression Symptoms**: Choose if you experience depressive symptoms (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Healthy Coping Strategies**: Choose coping strategies you use (Exercise, Meditation or Mindfulness, Talking with Friends, Creative Activities, Professional Counseling, Other).
* **Alcohol Consumption**: Choose how often you drink alcohol (Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Regularly, Daily).
* **Tobacco Use**: Choose if you use tobacco products (Never, Former User, Current User).
* **Recreational Drug Use**: Choose if you use recreational drugs (Never, Former User, Current User).
* **Living Situation**: Choose who you live with (Alone, With Partner, With Family, With Friends, Other).
* **Social Support Network**: Choose your level of social support (Strong Support, Moderate Support, Limited Support, No Support).
* **Recent Stressful Life Events**: Describe any major life changes.
* **Financial Concerns**: Choose if you have financial stress (None, Minor, Moderate, Significant).
