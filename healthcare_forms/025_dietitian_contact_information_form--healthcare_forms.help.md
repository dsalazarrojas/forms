# dietitian-contact-information-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from dietitians, which will be used for administrative tasks such as appointment scheduling and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This is the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This is the client's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the client's email address, where we will send any important information or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is the client's phone number, which we will use to reach out to you for any urgent matters.
