# Lymphatic Recovery Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lymphatic Recovery Intake Form is a document that helps healthcare professionals gather essential information from clients about their lymphatic recovery journey. It is designed to collect data that aids in providing personalized care and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please follow these steps:
  1. Fill in the "General Info" section with your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal data.
  2. In the "Client Health History" section, provide information about your past medical experiences, if any.
  3. For "Goals and Preferences," describe your short-term and long-term goals for your lymphatic recovery and any specific preferences you have for your treatment.
  4. In "Appointment Preferences," specify your schedule and availability for appointments.
  5. Provide your contact details in "Contact Details."
  6. In "Emergency Contact," list the person(s) you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  7. In "Medical History," share any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your treatment.
  8. In "Allergies and Concerns," list any allergies or concerns you may have that could affect your treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info (text)**: This is the first section of the form where you'll provide your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal data.
- **Client Health History (text)**: This section is for providing information about your past medical experiences, if any. This will help the healthcare professionals understand your medical background.
- **Goals and Preferences (text)**: In this section, describe your short-term and long-term goals for your lymphatic recovery and any specific preferences you have for your treatment.
- **Appointment Preferences (text)**: Specify your schedule and availability for appointments.
- **Contact Details (text)**: Provide your contact details so the healthcare professionals can reach you.
- **Emergency Contact (text)**: List the person(s) you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Medical History (text)**: Share any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your treatment.
- **Allergies and Concerns (text)**: List any allergies or concerns you may have that could affect your treatment.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and truthfully to ensure a comprehensive understanding of your needs and preferences.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare professional handling your case for guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or your treatment, feel free to ask the healthcare team for clarification.
