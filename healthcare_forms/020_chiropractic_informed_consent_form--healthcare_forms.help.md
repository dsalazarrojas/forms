### Chiropractic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This guide helps you complete the Chiropractic Informed Consent Form, which is a document that outlines the patient's rights, risks, and expectations of chiropractic care. The form is designed to provide a clear understanding of the treatment process, potential risks, and benefits, and to ensure that the patient has provided voluntary consent for the treatment.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each field carefully**: Read each section and question carefully before answering.
2. **Answer questions truthfully**: Answer each question honestly, and if you're unsure about any information, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
3. **Sign and date each page**: Make sure to sign and date each page of the form, as required.
4. **Keep a copy for yourself**: Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, **required**): Enter the date you are signing this consent form.
* **Nature of Treatment** (`nature_of_treatment`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about the treatment process.
* **I understand that chiropractic care involves spinal adjustments** (`understand_chiropractic`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand the nature of chiropractic care.
* **I understand the purpose of chiropractic treatment is to improve function** (`purpose_of_treatment`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand the purpose of chiropractic treatment.
* **Treatment Methods** (`treatment_methods`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about the treatment methods.
* **I consent to spinal adjustments and manual manipulations** (`consent_adjustments`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I consent" if you consent to spinal adjustments and manual manipulations.
* **I consent to additional therapies as recommended** (`consent_therapies`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I consent" if you consent to additional therapies.
* **Limitations on Therapies** (`therapy_limitations`, text, **optional**): Enter any limitations on therapies.
* **I consent to X-rays if deemed necessary** (`consent_xray`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I consent" if you consent to X-rays.
* **Risks and Complications** (`risks_and_complications`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about risks and complications.
* **I understand that all healthcare treatments carry some risk** (`understand_risks`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand that all healthcare treatments carry some risk.
* **The risks have been explained to me** (`risks_explained`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand that the risks have been explained to you.
* **I understand common risks may include temporary soreness, stiffness or discomfort after adjustment** (`common_risks`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand common risks.
* **I understand rare but serious risks exist** (`rare_risks`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand rare but serious risks.
* **Specific Risks Discussed** (`specific_risks_discussed`, text, **optional**): Enter any specific risks discussed.
* **Benefits of Treatment** (`benefits_of_treatment`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about benefits of treatment.
* **The potential benefits have been explained to me** (`benefits_explained`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand that the potential benefits have been explained to you.
* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, text, **optional**): Enter your expected benefits.
* **I understand that no guarantee has been made about results** (`no_guarantee`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand that no guarantee has been made about results.
* **Alternatives to Treatment** (`alternatives_to_treatment`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about alternatives to treatment.
* **Alternative treatments have been explained to me** (`alternatives_explained`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, completely" if you understand alternative treatments.
* **Alternatives Discussed** (`alternatives_list`, text, **optional**): Enter any alternative treatments discussed.
* **Right to Refuse Treatment** (`right_to_refuse`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about your right to refuse treatment.
* **I understand I have the right to refuse treatment at any time** (`understand_right_to_refuse`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand your right to refuse treatment.
* **I understand I can stop treatment at any time** (`understand_right_to_stop`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand that you can stop treatment at any time.
* **Opportunity for Questions** (`questions_opportunity`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about your opportunity to ask questions.
* **I have had the opportunity to ask questions** (`had_opportunity`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I asked questions" if you have had the opportunity to ask questions.
* **All my questions have been answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, all answered" if all your questions have been answered.
* **Do you have any remaining questions** (`remaining_questions`, select_one, **required**): Choose "No, all answered" if you do not have any remaining questions.
* **List Remaining Questions** (`questions_list`, text, **optional**): Enter any remaining questions.
* **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, note, **optional**): This field is for notes about your voluntary consent.
* **My consent is given voluntarily without coercion** (`consent_voluntary`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, voluntary" if your consent is given voluntarily.
* **I have read this consent form** (`read_document`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I read it" if you have read this form.
* **I understand the contents of this form** (`understand_document`, select_one, **required**): Choose "Yes, I understand" if you understand the contents of this form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, **required**): Enter your digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`patient_signature_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, **optional**): Enter the witness' digital signature, if required.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, **required**): Enter the doctor's digital signature.
* **Doctor Signature Date** (`doctor_signature_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the doctor's signature.
