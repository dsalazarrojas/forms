# Nutritionist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare professionals, specifically nutritionists, for various administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your contact details, including your address and phone number, so that we can reach out to you.
3. Share your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies that may impact your work as a nutritionist.
4. Provide dietary guidance that you follow or recommend, including any specific diets or meal plans you use.
5. List your client goals, including what you hope to achieve with your clients and how you approach client relationships.
6. Enter emergency contact information for yourself and any other relevant contacts.
7. List any medical conditions that you have or have had in the past.
8. Describe any medical tests that you have undergone, including relevant test results.
9. List any allergies or sensitivities that you have or have had in the past.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)**: This field is where you can enter your name and any other information about yourself that is relevant to your work as a nutritionist.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: This field is where you can enter your address and phone number, so that we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Medical History (Medical History)**: This field is where you can enter any previous medical conditions or allergies that may impact your work as a nutritionist.
* **Dietary Guidance (Dietary Guidance)**: This field is where you can enter any specific diets or meal plans that you follow or recommend.
* **Client Goals (Client Goals)**: This field is where you can list your client goals, including what you hope to achieve with your clients and how you approach client relationships.
* **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts)**: This field is where you can enter emergency contact information for yourself and any other relevant contacts.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)**: This field is where you can list any medical conditions that you have or have had in the past.
* **Medical Tests (Medical Tests)**: This field is where you can describe any medical tests that you have undergone, including relevant test results.
* **Allergies (Allergies)**: This field is where you can list any allergies or sensitivities that you have or have had in the past.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form, as the information will be used for administrative purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
