## Training Request For Nursing Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect information about training requests for nursing education. The purpose of this form is to provide necessary details about a training session, including the title, category, description, assigned tool, date, number of attendees, and any additional information that may be relevant. This form is used by authorized personnel to review and manage training requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title for the training session in the "Title" field.
2. Select the category of the training request from the dropdown options.
3. Provide a brief description of the training session in the "Description" field.
4. Choose the assigned tool from the dropdown options.
5. Enter the date of the training session using the date picker or manually in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Enter the expected number of nurses attending the training session.
7. If there is any other information about the training session, select "Option A" or "Option B" for "Additional Information".
8. If you would like to provide feedback for quality improvement, select "Option A" or "Option B" for "Feedback".
9. If there is any other training session scheduled during the same period, select "Option A" or "Option B" for "Other Sessions".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the title of the training request?** (`1`, `text`, **required**): Enter the title of the training session in a clear and concise manner.
* **Category** (`2`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the category of the training request from the provided options.
* **Briefly describe the training session** (`3`, `text`, **required**): Provide a brief description of the training session, including any relevant details.
* **Assigned Tool** (`4`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the assigned tool from the provided options.
* **Date of the training session** (`5`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the training session using the date picker or manually in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **How many nurses are expected to attend this training session?** (`6`, `number`, **required**): Enter the expected number of nurses attending the training session.
* **Is there any other information about this training session?** (`7`, `checkbox`, **optional**): If there is any other information about the training session, select "Option A" or "Option B" to provide details.
* **Can we use your feedback for quality improvement?** (`8`, `checkbox`, **optional**): If you would like to provide feedback for quality improvement, select "Option A" or "Option B".
* **Is there any other training session scheduled during the same period?** (`9`, `checkbox`, **optional**): If there is any other training session scheduled during the same period, select "Option A" or "Option B".
