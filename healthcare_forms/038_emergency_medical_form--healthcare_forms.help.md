# emergency-medical-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for recording the details of patients who are in need of immediate medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Answer each question completely and accurately.
* Use the options provided for fields that have them.
* If a question does not have an option provided, please enter a clear and concise answer.
* Ensure that the fields marked as "required" have an answer provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, including first and last name.
* **Date and Time of Incident** (`date_and_time_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Nature of Injuries** (`nature_of_injuries`, text, required): Describe the nature of the patient's injuries.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, required): Enter a contact number or name for the patient's next of kin.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any pre-existing medical conditions that may be relevant to the patient's current condition.
* **Allergies and Contrainications** (`allergies_contraindications`, text, optional): List any allergies or contraindications that may be relevant to the patient's treatment.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the patient's current vital signs, which can be Normal, Abnormal, or Critical.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter a contact number or name for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Medical Attention** (`medical_attention`, select_one, required): Check if the patient received medical attention.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, optional): Enter the patient's status, which can be any free-form description.
* **Patient Injuries** (`patient_injuries`, text, optional): Describe the injuries sustained by the patient.
* **Medical Transport** (`medical_transport`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient requires medical transport.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the emergency services that were required, which can be Ambulance, Fire, or Police.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current condition.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Sex** (`patient_sex`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's sex, which can be Male, Female, or Other.
* **Patient Birthdate** (`patient_birthdate`, date, optional): Enter the patient's birthdate.
* **Patient Height** (`patient_height`, number, optional): Enter the patient's height.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`, number, optional): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical Provider Notes** (`medical_provider_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, text, required): Enter the medical provider's signature.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure of any field, please ask for help or clarification.
* Ensure that the form is filled out legibly and accurately to ensure the patient receives the best care available.
