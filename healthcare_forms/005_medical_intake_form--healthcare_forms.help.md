# Medical Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Intake Form is a document used to gather information about a patient's medical history, current symptoms, and past surgeries. This form is intended to help healthcare providers understand the patient's medical background and provide accurate and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each section of the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each section, select the appropriate response from the dropdown menu or text field.
3. If you are unsure about a particular question or section, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing a brief overview of the patient's medical history. Please enter any relevant information about the patient's medical history, including any significant events or diagnoses.

### Past Surgeries
* **Past Surgeries** (`Past Surgeries`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for listing any past surgeries or medical procedures the patient has undergone. Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
