# Nutrition Counseling Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about you, the patient, to create a personalized nutrition plan tailored to your unique health and nutrition needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information:
	* Provide your name, age, and any other identifying information about yourself.
2. Enter your Contact Information:
	* Fill in your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Enter your Nutrition Information:
	* Share as much detail as possible about your diet, eating habits, and any health concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)** (`text`, optional): Provide your name, age, and any other identifying information about yourself.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`text`, optional): Fill in your contact details, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Nutrition Information (Nutrition Information)** (`text`, optional): Share as much detail as possible about your diet, eating habits, and any health concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible with your answers, as this information will help us create a personalized nutrition plan for you.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* If you have any medical documents or test results, please attach them to the form or provide them separately to your healthcare provider.
