# Behavioral Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to behavioral therapy services for mental health treatment. It collects information about the patient's referral status, personal details, clinical diagnosis, treatment history, and requested services.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete the Referral Section
1. Enter the referring provider's name, title, and facility/clinic information in the "Referring Provider" section.
2. Select the date of referral.

### Step 2: Provide Patient Information
1. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and phone number in the "Patient" section.
2. Enter the patient's email address (if available).
3. Select the patient's insurance company and policy/member ID number (if applicable).

### Step 3: Describe the Clinical Diagnosis and Presenting Concerns
1. Select the primary diagnosis from the provided list (e.g., Major Depressive Disorder, Generalized Anxiety Disorder, etc.).
2. If applicable, enter the secondary diagnosis.
3. Describe the patient's current symptoms and their duration.
4. Select the level of functional impairment caused by the symptoms.

### Step 4: Provide Treatment History and Services Needed
1. Indicate whether the patient has received prior mental health treatment.
2. Provide details about any prior treatment (if applicable).
3. List the patient's current psychiatric medications (if any).
4. Select the type of behavioral therapy requested (e.g., CBT, DBT, etc.).
5. Choose the recommended session frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
6. Select the urgency level for the referral (e.g., routine, urgent, emergency).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Date**: Enter the date when the patient was referred to behavioral therapy services.
* **Referring Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider referring the patient.
* **Referring Provider Title**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider (e.g., MD, DO, NP, etc.).
* **Referring Facility/Clinic**: Enter the name of the facility or clinic where the patient was referred from.
* **Referring Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address (if available).
* **Insurance Company Name**: Enter the patient's insurance company name (if applicable).
* **Policy/Member ID Number**: Enter the patient's insurance policy or member ID number (if applicable).
* **Primary Diagnosis**: Select the primary diagnosis from the provided list.
* **Secondary Diagnosis**: Enter any secondary diagnosis (if applicable).
* **Description of Presenting Symptoms**: Describe the patient's current symptoms and their duration.
* **Duration of Current Symptoms**: Select the duration of the patient's symptoms.
* **Functional Impairment Level**: Select the level of functional impairment caused by the symptoms.
* **Prior Mental Health Treatment**: Indicate whether the patient has received prior mental health treatment.
* **Details of Prior Treatment**: Provide details about any prior treatment received (if applicable).
* **Current Psychiatric Medications**: List any current psychiatric medications and their dosages.
* **Type of Behavioral Therapy Requested**: Select the type of behavioral therapy requested (e.g., CBT, DBT, etc.).
* **Recommended Session Frequency**: Choose the recommended session frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level for the referral (e.g., routine, urgent, emergency).

Note: This form is designed to help you gather the necessary information for a referral to behavioral therapy services. Please ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
