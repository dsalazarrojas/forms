# Psychological Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychological Program Evaluation Form is designed to collect and evaluate the progress and outcome of psychological programs. This form will guide you through a series of questions to gather information on program details, client information, assessment tools, and evaluation data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "Required" label.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program being evaluated.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client participating in the program.
* **Evaluation Start Date** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation End Date** (`evaluation_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Assessment Tool** (`assessment_tool`, text, optional): Describe any assessment tools used during the evaluation.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, optional): Record any feedback or comments from the client.
* **Practitioner Notes** (`practitioner_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments from the practitioner.
* **Evaluation Form Type** (`evaluation_form_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of evaluation form (Evaluation Form, Client Report, or Progress Report).
* **Evaluation Form Submitted** (`evaluation_form_submitted`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the form has been submitted or not (True, False, or Pending).
* **Evaluation Form Status** (`evaluation_form_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the evaluation form (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Assessment Frequency** (`assessment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the assessment (Initial, Ongoing, or Quarterly).
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location where the program takes place (In-person, Online, or Phone).
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the practitioner or administrator responsible for the program.
* **Practitioner Title** (`practitioner_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the practitioner or administrator.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, text, optional): Record the client's contact information.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the evaluations (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
* **Practitioner Notes 2** (`practitioner_notes_2`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the practitioner.
* **Evaluation Form Notes** (`evaluation_form_notes`, text, optional): Record any additional comments or observations about the evaluation form.
* **Program Outcome** (`program_outcome`, text, optional): Enter the outcome or result of the program.
* **Next Evaluation Date** (`next_evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the next evaluation date.
* **Program Outcome 2** (`program_outcome_2`, text, optional): Record the outcome or result of the program (if applicable).
* **Program Goal** (`program_goal`, text, optional): Enter the goal of the program.
* **Program Goal 2** (`program_goal_2`, text, optional): Enter the second goal of the program.
* **Assessment Tool 2** (`assessment_tool_2`, text, optional): Describe any assessment tools used during the evaluation.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from experienced professionals in the field.
