# dental_clinic_performance_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the performance of a dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the sections that apply to you.
2. Fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Leave blank any sections that are not applicable to your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Services Evaluated** (text, required: false):
This field is where you can list the services that were evaluated during your assessment. For example, you could list the types of dental services provided, such as cleanings, fillings, or extractions.

* **Patient Feedback** (text, required: false):
In this field, you can gather comments or feedback from patients about their experience at the dental clinic. This could include their satisfaction with the services provided, their experience with the staff, or any other comments they may have.

* **Services Gathered** (text, required: false):
This field is where you can list the services that were gathered or collected during your assessment. This could include any relevant information or materials gathered from the patients, such as medical records or billing information.

* **Comments** (text, required: false):
Use this field to provide any additional comments or notes about your assessment.

* **Rating** (number, required: false):
In this field, you can rate the performance of the dental clinic on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

* **Rating Scale** (select_one, required: false):
Select a rating from the provided scale of 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 to rate the performance of the dental clinic.

* **Rating Scale Label** (text, required: false):
This field provides an explanation of what each rating scale number means. For example, "1: Poor" could mean that the clinic's performance was subpar, while "5: Excellent" could mean that the clinic's performance was outstanding.

* **Submitted By** (email, required: false):
This field is where you can enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your assessment.

## Tips

* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to leave blank any sections that are not applicable to your assessment.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or your supervisor for guidance.
