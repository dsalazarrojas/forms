# Daily Health Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Attestation Form is a tool used to collect essential information from patients and clinicians for health-related purposes. This form helps to ensure that patients and clinicians are able to communicate effectively and provide accurate details about their health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **Patient Info** section (page 1).
    -   In the **Patient Info** section, you will be asked to provide basic demographic information about the patient. This includes their name, email, and other contact details. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Move on to the **Session Details** section (page 2).
    -   In this section, you will be asked to provide details about the current session, including the duration of the session. Make sure to enter the correct information accurately.
3.  Next, fill out the **Clinician Notes** section (page 3).
    -   In this section, you will be asked to enter any notes or comments from the clinician about the patient's health.
4.  In the **Patient Signature** section (page 4), select either **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether the patient has signed the form.
5.  In the **E-Signature** section (page 5), select whether the clinician has electronically signed the form (Yes or No).
6.  Fill out the **Clinician Sign** section (page 6).
    -   In this section, select whether the clinician has signed the form (Yes or No).
7.  Enter the patient's **Email** and **Clinician Email** in the respective sections (pages 7 and 8).
    -   Ensure you enter the correct email addresses accurately.
8.  In the **Phone** sections (pages 9 and 10), enter the patient's and clinician's phone numbers.
9.  The **Note** sections (pages 11 and 12) are for any additional comments or observations from the clinician.
10. Enter the **Patient Phone**, **Clinician Phone**, **Patient Address**, **Clinician Address**, **Patient IP**, **Clinician IP**, **Patient Country**, **Clinician Country**, **Patient State**, **Clinician State**, and **Patient City**, **Clinician City** sections (pages 13-20) if they are relevant to the patient's or clinician's location.
11.  In the **Session Duration** section (page 21), enter the time of the session.
