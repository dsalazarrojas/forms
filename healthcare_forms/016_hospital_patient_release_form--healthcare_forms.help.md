# Hospital Patient Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of a patient from a medical facility. It collects information about the patient's name, condition, and treatment details to ensure a smooth and safe transfer or discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter any contact information for yourself or your representative.
4. Enter the name of the medical facility that treated you.
5. Describe your current medical condition.
6. Enter the date your condition began.
7. Provide a detailed description of your treatment.
8. Enter the date you started your treatment.
9. Enter the date you completed your treatment (if applicable).
10. Sign your name to acknowledge this form as a release statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY, depending on your region.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any relevant contact information for yourself or your representative, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Medical Facility Name**: Enter the name of the medical facility that treated you.
* **Patient Condition**: Describe your current medical condition in as much detail as possible.
* **Patient Condition Date**: Enter the date your condition began.
* **Treatment Description**: Provide a detailed description of your treatment.
* **Treatment Start Date**: Enter the date you started your treatment.
* **Treatment End Date**: Enter the date you completed your treatment (if applicable).
* **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge this form as a release statement.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out the form accurately and completely before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Please return this form to your healthcare provider before leaving the medical facility.
