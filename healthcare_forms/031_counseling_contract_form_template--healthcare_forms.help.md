# Counseling Contract Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by counselors or therapists for client intake and session planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This is a general input field for clients to provide any additional information they want to share with the counselor.
2.  **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information, including their name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
3.  **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Describe the specific services you are offering to the client, including the number of sessions, frequency, and duration.
4.  **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Outline the payment terms and conditions, including payment frequency, method, and amount.
5.  **Counseling Plan** (`counseling_plan`, text, optional): Create a comprehensive counseling plan, including goals, objectives, and steps to achieve them.
6.  **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Include any additional agreements or contracts that need to be signed by the client.
7.  **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Include the counselor's signature and date to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract.
8.  **Contact Signature** (`contact_signature`, text, optional): Include the client's signature and date to confirm they have read and agreed to the contract.
9.  **Billing** (`billing`, text, optional): Enter the billing contact information, including name, address, and phone number.
10. **Payment Methods** (`payment_methods`, text, optional): List the payment methods accepted by the client, such as credit card, bank transfer, etc.
11. **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, text, optional): Enter the payment amount for each session or package.
12. **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the payment frequency, such as weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
13. **Payment Method 1** (`payment_method_1`, text, optional): List the primary payment method.
14.  **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, text, optional): List secondary payment methods.
15.  **Payment Method 3** (`payment_method_3`, text, optional): List tertiary payment methods.
16.  **Additional Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_2`, text, optional): Include any additional payment terms or conditions.
17.  **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, text, optional): Obtain client consent for certain aspects of the counseling contract.
18.  **Counselor Consent** (`counselor_consents`, text, optional): Confirm the counselor's consent and agreement with the client contract.
19.  **Termination** (`termination`, text, optional): Specify the conditions under which the counseling sessions can be terminated.
20.  **Cancellation** (`cancellation`, text, optional): Outline the steps and procedures for canceling the sessions.
21.  **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, text, optional): Document the reasons for cancellation.
22.  **Cancellation Consequences** (`cancellation_consequences`, text, optional): Specify the consequences of canceling the sessions.
23.  **Billing Contact** (`billing_contact`, text, optional): Confirm the billing contact information.
24.  **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address.
25.  **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, optional): Confirm the billing phone number.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to review and understand each field before completing this form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the counselor.
*   The client should sign and date each section after it has been reviewed and agreed upon with the counselor.
*   The counselor should sign and date each section after reviewing and agreeing with the client.
*   This form is a template and may need to be adapted to fit the specific needs and requirements of each client and counselor.
