# Medical Report Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request clarification on a medical report. Please use this form to ask for additional information or clarification on a medical report.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date the report you are requesting clarification on.
2. Choose the medical professional who generated the report.
3. Indicate the type of report or document you are requesting clarification on.
4. Specify the type of information you are requesting clarification on.
5. (Optional) Attach any supporting documentation or files to your request.
6. Leave any additional comments or questions you have about the report.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requested date of report** (`request_medical_report_clarification`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the date of the report you are requesting clarification on.
* **Requested medical professional** (`medical_professional`, select_one, **required**): Select the medical professional who generated the report.
* **Type of report or document** (`type_of_report`, select_multiple, **required**): Indicate the type of report or document you are requesting clarification on.
* **Type of information requested** (`type_of_info_requested`, select_multiple, **required**): Specify the type of information you are requesting clarification on.
* **Medical report attachment** (`medical_report_attachment`, text, **optional**): Attach any supporting documentation or files to your request.
* **Comments** (`comments_section`, note, **optional**): Leave any additional comments or questions you have about the report.
* **Patient signature** (`patient_signature`, text, **optional**): Sign and date the form to confirm your request.
* **Medical sign off** (`medical_sign_off`, text, **optional**): Not used in this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when requesting clarification.
* If you have any additional information or supporting documentation, attach it to the form.
* If you are unsure about what information you need or how to fill out the form, contact the healthcare provider for assistance.
