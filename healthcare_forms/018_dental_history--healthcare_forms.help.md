# dental_history - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dental_history" form is designed to collect patient information and dental history for healthcare providers. It helps gather essential details about a patient's medical and dental conditions, allergies, and insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Patient Information section, which includes basic details such as phone and address.
2. Move on to the Medical History section, where you can describe your medical conditions, medications, and any allergies you have.
3. In the Dental History section, select any relevant procedures you have undergone, such as fillings, root canals, or extractions.
4. For your dental status, choose from the options "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor".
5. If you have any other dental problems or procedures not listed, you can describe them in the "Dental Problems" or "Other Procedures" fields.
6. In the Emergency Contact section, provide information about who to contact in case of an emergency and their contact details.
7. Finally, fill in the Medical History Date and Medical History Time fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe any medical conditions you have, such as hypertension or diabetes.
* **Medical Conditions (3)**: Select any medical conditions you have, including hypertension, diabetes, and asthma.
* **Medications (4)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies (5)**: Select any allergies you have, including penicillin, sulfa, or erythromycin.
* **Dental History (6)**: Describe your dental history.
* **Dental Procedures (7)**: Select any dental procedures you have undergone, such as root canals, fillings, or extractions.
* **Dental Procedures Other (8)**: Describe any other dental procedures not listed above.
* **Dental Status (9)**: Choose how you perceive your current dental status, as "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Dental Problems (10)**: Select any dental problems you have, including toothache, gum disease, or tooth decay.
* **Dental Problems Other (11)**: Describe any other dental problems not listed above.
* **Dental Awareness (12)**: Describe your dental awareness.
* **Dental Insurance (13)**: Fill in your dental insurance information.
* **Dental Insurance Other (14)**: Describe any other dental insurance not listed above.
* **Medical Insurance (15)**: Fill in your medical insurance information.
* **Medical Insurance Other (16)**: Describe any other medical insurance not listed above.
* **Emergency Contact (17)**: Provide information about who to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Other (18)**: Describe any other emergency contact not listed above.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (19)**: Fill in the contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (20)**: Describe your relation to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address (21)**: Fill in the contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Note (22)**: Provide any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Medical History Date (23)**: Fill in the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Time (24)**: Fill in the time of your medical history.
