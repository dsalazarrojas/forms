# Chestnut Hill - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chestnut Hill form is designed to gather essential information from patients to provide personalized and safe vaccination services. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Fill in your full name, date of birth, phone number, email address, and address.
3. Select your preferred appointment date and time (if applicable).
4. Choose the type of vaccine you need.
5. Confirm vaccine availability and previous vaccinations.
6. List any medical conditions, allergies, or current medications.
7. Indicate your pregnancy status and whether you are immunocompromised.
8. Report any recent illness or previous vaccine reactions.
9. Select your preferred pharmacy location and appointment reminder method.
10. Confirm your consent to vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (`patient_name`, `text`, required)
* Provide your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
### 2. Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required)
* Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
### 3. Phone Number (`phone_number`, `text`, required)
* Enter your contact phone number.
### 4. Email Address (`email_address`, `email`, required)
* Enter your email address (if you have one).
### 5. Address (`address`, `text`, required)
* Enter your full address.
### 6. Insurance Provider (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required)
* Enter the name of your insurance provider.
### 7. Insurance ID Number (`insurance_id`, `text`, required)
* Enter your insurance member ID number.
### 8. Preferred Appointment Date (`appointment_date`, `date`, required)
* Select a date for your appointment. Choose a date that works best for you.
### 9. Preferred Appointment Time (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional)
* If available, select a time for your appointment that suits you best.
### 10. Vaccine Type Needed (`vaccine_type`, `select_one`, required)
* Choose the type of vaccine you need (e.g., Flu, COVID-19, Tdap, etc.).
### 11. Check Vaccine Availability (`vaccine_availability_check`, `select_one`, required)
* Confirm if you've checked the vaccine availability.
### 12. Previous Vaccinations at This Location (`previous_vaccinations`, `select_one`, required)
* Indicate if you've received vaccines here before.
### 13. Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional)
* List any medical conditions that might affect your vaccination process.
### 14. Allergies (`allergies`, `text`, required)
* List any allergies, especially to medications or vaccines.
### 15. Current Medications (`current_medications`, `text`, optional)
* List medications you are currently taking.
### 16. Pregnancy Status (`pregnancy_status`, `select_one`, optional)
* Indicate your pregnancy status (if applicable).
### 17. Are You Immunocompromised (`immune_compromised`, `select_one`, required)
* Confirm if you're immunocompromised.
### 18. Recent Illness (`recent_illness`, `select_one`, required)
* Report if you've been ill in the past 2 weeks.
### 19. Previous Vaccine Reactions (`previous_vaccine_reactions`, `select_one`, required)
* Confirm if you've had reactions to vaccines before.
### 20. Reaction Details (`reaction_details`, `text`, optional)
* Describe any previous reactions to vaccines.
### 21. Pharmacy Preference (`pharmacy_preference`, `select_one`, required)
* Select your preferred pharmacy location.
### 22. Appointment Reminder Preference (`appointment_reminder`, `select_one`, required)
* Choose how you'd like to be reminded of your appointment.
### 23. Consent to Vaccination (`consent_to_vaccinate`, `select_one`, required)
* Confirm your consent to receive the vaccine.
### 24. Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
* Provide any other information that might be helpful for your appointment.

## Tips
* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing complete and honest information to ensure a safe and successful vaccination process.
