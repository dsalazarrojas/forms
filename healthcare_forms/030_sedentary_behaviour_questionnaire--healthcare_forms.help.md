# Sedentary Behaviour Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sedentary Behaviour Questionnaire is designed to collect information on daily habits and activities that can help individuals understand their sedentary behavior. The form is for personal use only and aims to provide insights on potential physical inactivity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, answering each question honestly and thoroughly.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you are unsure about any question, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is meant to collect any additional information that may be helpful for the analysis.
* **Sedentary Behaviours**: Please list your sedentary behaviors (e.g., watching TV, playing video games, etc.).
* **Sedentary Behaviours Frequency**: For each sedentary behaviour listed, please indicate how frequently you engage in it on a daily basis.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Frequency Questions**: This field is meant to collect more specific information about your sedentary behavior frequency.
* **Physical Activities**: List any physical activities that you engage in.
* **Sedentary Behaviours Duration**: For each sedentary behaviour, please indicate how long you typically spend engaging in it.
* **Sedentary Behaviours Duration Questions**: This field is meant to collect more specific information about the duration of your sedentary behavior.
* **Sedentary Behaviors**: Similar to the 'Sedentary Behaviours' field, this field is meant to collect more information on your sedentary behaviors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Questions**: This field is meant to collect more specific information on your sedentary behavior.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors**: This field is meant to collect information on your sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors Frequency**: For each sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors, please indicate how frequently you engage in it on a daily basis.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors Questions**: This field is meant to collect more specific information on your sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors 2**: This field is a duplicate of 'Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors', please refer to that field for explanation.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Duration Outdoors**: For each sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors, please indicate how long you typically spend engaging in it.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Physical Activity**: List any physical activities that you engage in while outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Physical Activity Duration**: For each physical activity that you engage in while outdoors, please indicate how long you typically spend engaging in it.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Time Outdoors**: This field is meant to collect information on how much time you spend outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Questions Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors**: This field is meant to collect more specific information on your sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors Questions**: This field is meant to collect more specific information on your sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors 2**: This field is a duplicate of 'Sedentary Behaviour Sedentary Behaviour Outdoors', please refer to that field for explanation.
* **Sedentary Behaviour Sedentary Behaviour Duration Outdoors**: This field is meant to collect more specific information on the duration of your sedentary behavior that occurs outdoors.

Please note that some fields have similar labels, but they are meant to collect different types of information. If you are unsure about any question, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.
