# vet_clinic_patient_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to check in at the vet clinic, providing essential information for the healthcare staff to attend to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (format must be valid).
5. If applicable, enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select the type of pet visiting the clinic in the "Pet Type" field. Options are: Dog, Cat, or Other (please select the correct option).
7. Enter the name of your pet in the "Pet Name" field.
8. If necessary, enter any relevant medical notes about your pet in the "Medical Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Phone Number** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address, format must be valid.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): If you're unsure, don't worry! This field is optional.
- **Pet Type** (select_one, options: Dog, Cat, Other, required: false): Select the type of pet visiting the clinic.
- **Pet Name** (text, required: false): Enter your pet's name.
- **Medical Notes** (note, required: false): Share any important medical history or notes about your pet.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email format is correct to allow us to send you notifications efficiently.
- For "Date of Birth", please enter your date of birth if you wish to provide it.
- If unsure about any field, feel free to contact us, and we'll be happy to assist!
