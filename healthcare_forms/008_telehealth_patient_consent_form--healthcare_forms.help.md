# Telehealth Patient Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients for participation in telehealth services. This form ensures that patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of telehealth consultations, including confidentiality, data security, and communication methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of telehealth services.
2. Select the telehealth services you wish to participate in from the list of available options.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent** (`telehealth_patient_consent_form`, text, required): This field is where you should indicate your understanding and agreement to participate in telehealth services. Please type "yes" to confirm.
* **Telehealth Services** (`telehealth_services`, select_one, not required): Select the telehealth services you wish to participate in. Options include:
	+ Telehealth services 1
	+ Telehealth services 2
* **Telehealth Services 1-4** (`telehealth_services_1` to `telehealth_services_4`, select_multiple, not required): Select the specific telehealth services you wish to participate in for each category:
	+ Telehealth services 2
	+ Telehealth services 3
	+ Telehealth services 4
	+ Telehealth services 5
* **Telehealth Services 4** (`telehealth_services_4`, select_multiple, not required): Select the specific telehealth services you wish to participate in, or select "Other" if none of the provided options apply:
	+ Telehealth services 5
	+ Other
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, not required): This field is for your digital signature, which confirms that you have read and understood the form. Please print your name and date.

## Tips

* Please read the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of telehealth services.
* Select the specific services you are interested in participating in, but be aware that this list is not exhaustive.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Your digital signature on this form is a confirmation that you have read and understood the form.
