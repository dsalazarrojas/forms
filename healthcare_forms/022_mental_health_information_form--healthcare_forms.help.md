# mental_health_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's mental health. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of their mental health status, medical history, social support, and treatment plans. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and detailed information about your mental health.
2. For "Mental Health Services", select one or multiple options that best describe the services you are receiving or have received.
3. For "Mental Health Goal" and "Mental Health Service", select one or multiple options that best describe your goals and services.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about your mental health in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (ID: 1): Fill in your personal contact information, such as name and address.
* **Mental Health Info** (ID: 2): Provide a detailed description of your mental health, including any diagnoses, symptoms, or concerns.
* **Social Support** (ID: 3): Describe the social support you have in place, such as family, friends, or community resources.
* **Medical Info** (ID: 4): Enter any relevant medical information, such as hospitalizations, medication, or medical conditions.
* **Treatment Info** (ID: 5): Describe your current treatment plans, including therapy, counseling, or medication.
* **Contact Info** (ID: 6): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Mental Health Services** (ID: 7): Select one or multiple options that best describe the services you are receiving or have received, such as Mental Health Professional, Psychiatric Nurse, or Support Group.
* **Mental Health Goals** (ID: 8): Select one or multiple options that best describe your goals, such as Goal 1 or Goal 2.
* **Mental Health Status** (ID: 9): Enter a description of your current mental health status.
* **Health Care Team** (ID: 10): Enter any relevant information about your healthcare team, such as doctors or therapists.
* **Mental Health Plan** (ID: 11): Enter a description of your mental health plan.
* **Mental Health Progress** (ID: 12): Enter a description of your mental health progress.
* **Mental Health Outcome** (ID: 13): Enter a description of your desired mental health outcome.
* **Notes** (ID: 14): Enter any additional comments or notes about your mental health.
* **Mental Health Risk** (ID: 15): Enter a description of any mental health risks you are experiencing.
* **Mental Health Crisis** (ID: 16): Enter a description of any mental health crises you have experienced.
* **Mental Health Caregiver** (ID: 17): Enter a description of any caregivers you have for your mental health.
* **Mental Health Self-Help** (ID: 18): Enter a description of any self-help strategies you use.
* **Mental Health Support** (ID: 19): Enter a description of any support you receive.
* **Mental Health Goal** (ID: 20): Select one or multiple options that best describe your goals.
* **Mental Health Goal Frequency** (ID: 21): Enter a description of how often you work towards your mental health goals.
* **Mental Health Progress Status** (ID: 22): Enter a description of your progress towards your mental health goals.
* **Mental Health Service** (ID: 23): Select one or multiple options that best describe the services you receive.
* **Mental Health Service Frequency** (ID: 24): Enter a description of how often you receive mental health services.
* **Mental Health Service Outcome** (ID: 25): Enter a description of the outcome of the mental health services you receive.
