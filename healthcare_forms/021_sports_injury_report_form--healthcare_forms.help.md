# sports_injury_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports injury report form is designed to collect information from athletes who have experienced an injury during their sports-related activities. This form will help in documenting and understanding the injury details, medical treatment, and recovery status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the athlete's name in the **Athlete Name** field.
2. Enter the date the athlete was injured in the **Date Injured** field in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Provide a brief description of the injury in the **Description of Injury** field.
4. Select the type of injury from the **Type of Injury** field.
5. Indicate the severity of the injury in the **Severity of Injury** field.
6. Check if any treatment is required in the **Treatment Required** field.
7. Enter the trainer's assessment of the injury in the **Trainer Assessment** field.
8. Enter any doctor's diagnosis in the **Doctor Diagnosis** field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
10. Select the athlete's current training status in the **Training Status** field.
11. Indicate the athlete's competition status in the **Competition Status** field.
12. Select the sport(s) the athlete participates in from the **Sports** field.
13. Specify the location of the injury in the **Injury Location** field.
14. Enter the name of the trainer who assessed the injury in the **Trainer Name** field.
14. Enter the name of the doctor who diagnosed the injury in the **Doctor Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name**: Enter the name of the athlete who was injured.
* **Date Injured**: Enter the date the athlete was injured in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Description of Injury**: Provide a brief description of the injury, including details about the circumstances surrounding it.
* **Type of Injury**: Select the type of injury from the dropdown list.
* **Severity of Injury**: Select the severity of the injury from the dropdown list.
* **Treatment Required**: Indicate if any treatment is required for the injury.
* **Trainer Assessment**: Enter the trainer's assessment of the injury.
* **Doctor Diagnosis**: Enter any doctor's diagnosis related to the injury.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the injury.
* **Training Status**: Select the athlete's current training status.
* **Competition Status**: Indicate the athlete's competition status.
* **Sports**: Select the sports the athlete participates in.
* **Injury Location**: Specify the location of the injury.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who assessed the injury.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who diagnosed the injury.
