# The Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT) is a screening tool used to identify people who may have an alcohol use disorder. The test assesses the severity of alcohol-related problems and is used to determine the level of treatment needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer for each question by choosing the option that best describes your behavior.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider or mental health professional for guidance.
