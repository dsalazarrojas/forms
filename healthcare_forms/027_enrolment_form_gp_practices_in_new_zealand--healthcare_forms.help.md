# Enrolment Form GP Practices In New Zealand - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect general information from patients about their health and medical history, as well as details about the healthcare practice they are enrolling in.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide accurate and honest information about your general health and medical history.
2. Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
3. Fill out the patient identification section with your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
4. Provide details about your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, and medical test results.
5. List any medications you are currently taking.
6. If you have any medical photos, please upload them here.
7. Share your medical records with us, if applicable.
8. Tell us about your healthcare practice's personnel, equipment, and location.
9. Provide us with your practice's social media links.
10. Share any comments or feedback about your experience with your healthcare practice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for general information about yourself.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional)
	+ Please enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Practice Details** (`practice_details`, text, optional)
	+ Provide details about your healthcare practice, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your address for billing and other administrative purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
	+ Share your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, and medical test results.
* **Allergies And Medical Conditions** (`allergies_medical_conditions`, text, optional)
	+ List any allergies or medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Test Results** (`medical_test_results`, text, optional)
	+ Share any relevant medical test results.
* **Medications** (`medication`, text, optional)
	+ List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Patient Photos** (`patient_photos`, text, optional)
	+ Upload any relevant medical photos.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional)
	+ Share any relevant medical records.
* **Practice Personnel** (`practice_personnel`, text, optional)
	+ Tell us about your healthcare practice's personnel, including doctors and nurses.
* **Practice Equipment** (`practice_equipment`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the equipment used at your healthcare practice.
* **Practice Location** (`practice_location`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your practice's address.
* **Practice Administrator** (`practice_administrator`, text, optional)
	+ Share the name and contact information of your practice's administrator.
* **Practice Specialties** (`practice_specialties`, text, optional)
	+ List any specialties offered by your healthcare practice.
* **Practice Hours** (`practice_hours`, text, optional)
	+ Share your practice's hours of operation.
* **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your practice's phone number.
* **Practice Email** (`practice_email`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your practice's email address.
* **Practice Website** (`practice_website`, text, optional)
	+ Share your practice's website URL.
* **Practice Facebook** (`practice_facebook`, text, optional)
	+ Share your practice's Facebook link.
* **Practice Instagram** (`practice_instagram`, text, optional)
	+ Share your practice's Instagram link.

	+ Share your practice's Twitter link.

	+ Share any reviews or ratings about your practice.
* **Practice Comments** (`practice_comments`, text, optional)
	+ Share any comments or feedback about your practice.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* Make sure to upload any required documents or photos.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
