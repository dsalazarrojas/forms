# professional_counseling_informed_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inform clients about the services we offer in counseling, the risks and limitations involved, and to obtain their agreement to proceed with the counseling process. It's a crucial document that outlines the client's understanding and consent to the counseling plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your client information on page 1.
2.  Read and understand the informed consent on page 2.
3.  Indicate your counseling goals on page 3.
4.  Acknowledge the risks and limitations of counseling on page 4.
5.  Indicate your emergency procedures on page 5.
6.  Sign the confidentiality agreement on page 6.
7.  Provide an emergency contact on page 7.
8.  Sign the form with your signature on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Page 1): This page is where you'll enter your client's basic information. Please fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate identification.
* **Informed Consent** (Page 2): This page is where you'll read and understand the counseling services we offer and the potential risks and limitations. Make sure to ask questions or clarify any doubts before proceeding.
* **Counseling Goals** (Page 3): Choose the goals you wish to achieve through counseling.
* **Client Agreement** (Page 4): Select all the statements that you understand and agree with regarding the counselor's limitations and confidentiality.
* **Risk and Limitations** (Page 5): This is a list of potential risks and limitations that may occur during the counseling process. Please read and understand these carefully.
* **Emergency Procedures** (Page 6): Indicate what you want to happen in case of a crisis.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Page 7): This is where you'll provide an emergency contact for the counselor to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 8): Provide the contact details of an emergency contact.
* **Signature** (Page 9): Sign this form to indicate your understanding and agreement to the counseling process.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand each page before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or doubts, don't hesitate to ask your counselor.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
