# Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypnotherapy Informed Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing hypnotherapy sessions. It ensures patients are aware of the risks, benefits, and procedures involved in the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Fill in the required information about the patient, such as their name and date.
2. **Consent Given**: Select the type of consent given by the patient's family member, adult of the patient, or a practitioner.
3. **Date Started and Ended**: Record the date the hypnotherapy sessions began and ended, if applicable.
4. **Date Ended Reason**: If the sessions ended prematurely, provide a reason.
5. **Practitioner Information**: Fill in the practitioner's name and title.
6. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the patient or session.
7. **Patient Consent**: If you are a healthcare professional, ensure you have a patient's consent recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Fill in the patient's name and date to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Select the type of consent given, which could be by a family member, an adult of the patient, or the patient themselves.
* **Date Started** (`date_start`, date, optional): Record the date the hypnotherapy sessions began, if applicable.
* **Date Ended** (`date_end`, date, optional): Record the date the hypnotherapy sessions ended, if applicable.
* **Date Ended Reason** (`date_end_reason`, text, optional): If the sessions ended prematurely, provide a reason.
* **Practitioner Information** (`practitioner_info`, text, optional): Record any relevant information about the practitioner.
* **Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, optional): Record the practitioner's name.
* **Title** (`practitioner_title`, text, optional): Record the practitioner's title.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about the patient or session.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, text, optional): This field is intended for healthcare professionals to record a patient's consent, but is not explicitly used in this form.
