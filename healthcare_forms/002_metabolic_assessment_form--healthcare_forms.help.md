# metabolic_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Assessment Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to collect vital information about a patient's general information, physical characteristics, and follow-up details. This information is essential for creating a comprehensive patient record and making informed decisions about their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's general information, including their name, address, and contact details.
2. Record the patient's height, weight, and age.
3. Select the unit of measurement for their height and weight.
4. Enter the patient's metabolic rate.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about the patient's condition.
6. Enter the patient's email address.
7. Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
8. Choose the follow-up details, such as follow-up appointments or phone calls.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`text`, Required): Enter the patient's name, address, and contact details.
* **Height (2)** (`number`, Required): Record the patient's height.
* **Weight (3)** (`number`, Required): Record the patient's weight.
* **Age (4)** (`number`, Required): Record the patient's age.
* **Height Unit (5)** (`select_one`, Required): Select the unit of measurement for the patient's height (e.g., cm, in, ft, mm).
* **Weight Unit (6)** (`select_one`, Required): Select the unit of measurement for the patient's weight (e.g., lb, kg, g, oz).
* **Metabolic Rate (7)** (`number`, Required): Enter the patient's metabolic rate.
* **Note (8)** (`note`, Optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone (10)** (`text`, Optional): Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
* **Follow-up (11)** (`select_multiple`, Required): Choose the follow-up details, such as follow-up appointments or phone calls.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct unit of measurement for the patient's height and weight.
* Enter the patient's email address accurately to ensure they receive follow-up information.
* Choose the correct follow-up details to keep the patient informed about their treatment progress.
* Use the "Note" field to add any relevant comments or observations about the patient's condition.
* If the patient has a phone number, enter it to enable follow-up calls or messages.
