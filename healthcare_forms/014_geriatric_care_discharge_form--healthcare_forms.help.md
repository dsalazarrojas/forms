# Geriatric Care Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Geriatric Care Discharge Form is designed to collect and document essential information about a patient's geriatric care during the discharge process. This form ensures that healthcare professionals gather all necessary details about the patient's medical condition, treatment, and post-discharge care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, medical condition, and medical history.
2. Select the medication(s) the patient is currently taking.
3. Choose the care team members involved in the patient's care.
4. Provide the patient's family contact information.
5. Select any social services the patient requires.
6. Enter the discharge date and location.
7. Provide any discharge instructions for the patient.
8. Specify the follow-up care required.
9. List any discrepancies that occurred during the discharge process.
10. Enter the patient's concerns or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (patient_information): Enter the patient's full name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition): Select all the patient's current medical conditions (e.g., Alzheimer's Disease, Arthritis, Cancer, etc.).
* **Medication** (medication): Enter the name of any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Medication List** (medication_list): Select all the medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): Enter any relevant medical history or allergies the patient may have.
* **Care Team** (care_team): Select all the healthcare professionals involved in the patient's care (e.g., Dr. John, Dr. Jane, etc.).
* **Patient Family Contact** (patient_family_contact): Enter the patient's family contact information (e.g., name, phone number, address, etc.).
* **Social Services** (social_services): Select any social services the patient requires (e.g., Adult Protective Services, Home Health Aide, etc.).
* **Discharge Date** (discharge_date): Enter the date the patient is being discharged.
* **Discharge Location** (discharge_location): Enter the location where the patient is being discharged.
* **Discharge Instructions** (discharge_instructions): Provide any instructions for the patient's care after discharge.
* **Follow-up Care** (follow_up_care): Specify any follow-up care required after discharge.
* **Discrepancies** (discharge_discrepancies): Enter any discrepancies that occurred during the discharge process.
* **Patient Concerns** (patient_concerns): Enter any concerns or worries the patient or their family may have.
* **Patient Comments** (patient_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback the patient or their family may have.
