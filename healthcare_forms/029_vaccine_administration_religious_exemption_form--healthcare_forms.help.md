# Vaccine Administration Religious Exemption Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's religious beliefs and medical conditions that may impact their vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input** page (Page 1). This section is not required but will help you provide more context to your healthcare provider.
2. On the **User Input** page, you may enter any relevant information about your vaccination concerns or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Religious Belief**: Please describe your religious belief that may prevent you from receiving a vaccine. This could include any specific scripture, teachings, or practices that guide your decision.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have a medical condition that may impact your ability to receive a vaccine, please describe it in this field.
* **Doctor Signature**: This field is for your healthcare provider to sign and date, indicating they have reviewed and accepted your information.
* **Date**: Your healthcare provider will sign and date this field to confirm they have reviewed your information.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when filling out this form. Accurate information is crucial for your healthcare provider to make informed decisions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your healthcare provider before completing this form.
* Ensure that your healthcare provider is aware of this form and its purpose to avoid duplication of effort.
