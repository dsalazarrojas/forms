# Healthcare Team Workload Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare team workload feedback form is designed to gather your opinions and experiences on your workload, job satisfaction, and work environment. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your needs and challenges, which will inform decisions to support the team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all the options that apply to you from the following questions.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
4. Finally, please enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further feedback or discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Team**: Select all the teams you belong to or have worked with in the recent past.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your level of job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Workload**: Indicate how much time you spend on administrative tasks (High, Medium, Low).
* **Job Support**: Select how you perceive your level of support from your colleagues and superiors (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Time Management**: Indicate if you agree or disagree with the statement "The time I have for tasks is sufficient" (Agree, Disagree).
* **Communication**: Select how you feel about communication within the team (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither, Strongly Disagree).
* **Teamwork**: Select all the teams you work with or collaborate with regularly (Yes, No).
* **Work Environment**: Select how you rate your work environment (Excellent, Poor).
* **Job Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or discussion.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date**: Select the date you completed this form.
* **Time**: Select the time you completed this form.
