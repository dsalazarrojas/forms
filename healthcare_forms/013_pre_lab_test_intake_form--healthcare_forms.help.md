# Pre Lab Test Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Lab Test Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from patients before they undergo lab tests. This form helps healthcare professionals gather crucial details about the patient's medical history, contact information, and test-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the information is complete and up-to-date.
2. Make sure to provide all required and optional information, depending on the field labels and instructions.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare professional or the form's creator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`patient-info`, `text`, required/optional: false): 
Please provide your name and other identifying information so we can identify you properly.

### **Medical History** (`medical-history`, `text`, required/optional: false): 
Describe any medical conditions or allergies we need to know about for your lab test.

### **Contact Information** (`contact-info`, `text`, required/optional: false): 
Provide your address, phone number, and email so we can contact you with test results or any concerns.

### **Test Information** (`test-info`, `text`, required/optional: false): 
Describe the type of test you're undergoing, and any relevant information about it.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the form before submission to ensure all required and optional information is included.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
