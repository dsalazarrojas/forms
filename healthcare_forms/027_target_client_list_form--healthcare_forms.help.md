# Target Client List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a list of target clients for a healthcare-related project or initiative. The form collects basic contact details, job title, department, and job description of the clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the First Name field with the client's first name.
- Proceed to fill out the Last Name field with the client's last name.
- Enter the client's Email address in the Email field, following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
- Provide the client's Phone number in the Phone field.
- Enter the client's Address in the Address field.
- If applicable, enter the client's Company name in the Company field.
- Enter the client's Job Title and Job Description in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the client's company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the client's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the client's department.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the client's job role or responsibilities.

## Tips
- Double-check the email address format before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the project lead before filling out the form.
- Make sure to fill out the form for each client only once to avoid duplication.
