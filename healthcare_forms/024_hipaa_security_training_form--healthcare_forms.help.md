# HIPAA Security Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The HIPAA Security Training Form is used to track employee participation in HIPAA security training sessions. This form is required for all employees who have received HIPAA training.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2.  Provide the date of your training in the "Training Date" field.
3.  Enter the number of hours you completed in the "Hours Completed" field.
4.  Indicate if you have completed the training on the topics covered by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Topics Covered" field.
5.  Add your Employee ID.
6.  Select your Department, if applicable.
7.  Sign the form with your employee signature.
8.  Enter your supervisor's name.
9.  Sign the form with your supervisor's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): This is the name of the employee who is completing the form.
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, optional): Enter the date the employee attended the HIPAA training session.
*   **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, optional): This field is not used in the provided form but is typically used to record the name of the trainer who provided the training.
*   **Hours Completed** (`training_hours_completed`, optional): If the training session included hours of instruction, enter the number of hours completed.
*   **Topics Covered** (`training_topics`, optional): Select whether you have completed the training on the topics covered. Select "Yes" if you have completed the training, and "No" if you have not.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, optional): Sign the form with your employee signature to confirm completion.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, optional): Enter your employee ID.
*   **Department** (`department`, optional): Select your department from the provided options.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, optional): Sign the form with your supervisor's signature to confirm completion.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
