# Student Disability Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Disability Verification Form is used to collect information about a student's disability status, type, and accommodation needs. This form helps the student's institution to understand their requirements and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. Select the type of disability the student has from the "Disability Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date the disability was identified or became apparent in the "Disability Date" field.
6. Enter the name of the institution where the student is currently enrolled or has enrolled in the past in the "Institution Name" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the student's disability in the "Disability Description" field.
8. Select the certifying officer's name from the "Certifying Officer" dropdown menu.
9. Date the signature of both the student and the certifying officer in the "Signature Date" field.
10. Have the student sign their name in the "Student Signature" field.
11. Have the certifying officer sign their name in the "Certifying Officer Signature" field.
12. Have the institution representative sign their name in the "Institution Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's institutional email address or personal email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Disability Type**: Select the type of disability the student has, such as physical, mental, or learning disability.
* **Disability Date**: Enter the date the student's disability was identified or became apparent.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution where the student is currently enrolled or has enrolled in the past.
* **Disability Description**: Provide a brief description of the student's disability, such as the nature and impact of their condition.
* **Certifying Officer**: Select the name of the certifying officer who verified the student's disability status.
* **Signature Date**: Date the signature of both the student and the certifying officer.
* **Student Signature**: Sign your name as the student, affirming that the information provided is accurate.
* **Certifying Officer Signature**: Sign your name as the certifying officer, confirming that the student's disability status is accurate.
* **Institution Signature**: Sign your name as the institution representative, confirming that the student's information is accurate and authentic.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and completely to avoid any errors or delays.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your institution's disability support staff for guidance.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records, as it may be required for future reference.
* Verify the accuracy and authenticity of the information provided before submission.
