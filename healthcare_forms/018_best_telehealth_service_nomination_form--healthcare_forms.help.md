# Best Telehealth Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and nominations for telehealth services from individuals with direct experience. Your input helps us identify exceptional services that deserve recognition and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address to ensure we can contact you for confirmation and further discussion.
2. Select your role as a patient, family member, healthcare provider, administrator, or other.
3. Provide the name of the telehealth service or platform you are nominating.
4. Select the type of service they provide (e.g., primary care, mental health, specialist consultations, etc.).
5. Indicate how long you have used the service and your frequency of use.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, its ease of use, and the quality of healthcare providers.
7. Provide your feedback on the average wait time, technical quality, and privacy and security measures of the service.
8. Rate the cost and value of the service.
9. Identify standout features that make this service exceptional.
10. Explain why this service deserves recognition.
11. If you have additional comments or suggestions for improvement, please include them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator's Name (Page 1)**: Enter your full name as a patient or healthcare provider.
* **Email Address (Page 1)**: We will use this email to contact you for confirmation and further discussion.
* **Role (Page 1)**: Select your role as a patient, family member, healthcare provider, administrator, or other.
* **Telehealth Service Name (Page 1)**: Enter the name of the telehealth service or platform you are nominating.
* **Type of Service (Page 1)**: Select the type of service they provide (e.g., primary care, mental health, specialist consultations, etc.).
* **Duration of Use (Page 1)**: Indicate how long you have used the service.
* **Usage Frequency (Page 1)**: Select how often you use the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Page 1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Ease of Use (Page 1)**: Rate the ease of use of the platform.
* **Healthcare Provider Quality (Page 1)**: Rate the quality of healthcare providers on the platform.
* **Average Wait Time (Page 1)**: Provide feedback on the average wait time for appointments.
* **Technical Quality (Page 1)**: Rate the technical quality of consultations (video and audio).
* **Privacy and Security Confidence (Page 1)**: Rate your confidence in the service's privacy and security measures.
* **Cost and Value Rating (Page 1)**: Rate the value of the service in relation to its cost.
* **Standout Features (Page 1)**: Select features that make this service exceptional.
* **Nomination Reason (Page 1)**: Explain why this service deserves recognition.
* **Improvement Suggestions (Page 2)**: Provide suggestions for improving the service (optional).
* **Would You Recommend (Page 2)**: Indicate whether you would recommend this service to others.
* **Additional Comments (Page 2)**: Provide any other feedback or comments about the service.

## Tips
- Please be specific and honest in your feedback to help us identify exceptional services.
- If you have used the service multiple times, please provide feedback on your entire experience.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
- We will review and verify your nomination internally to ensure it aligns with our criteria.
