# Hospital Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Attendance Form is a tool designed to gather essential information from patients visiting a hospital or healthcare facility. This information helps healthcare professionals provide personalized care and attention to each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Patient Name field with the patient's full name.
2. Provide a brief description of the Reason For Visit.
3. Share any relevant Medical History.
4. Enter the patient's Treatment Details.
5. Add the patient's Address.
6. Enter the patient's Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, optional): Fill in the patient's full name.
* **Reason For Visit** (reason_for_visit, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or concerns.
* **Treatment Details** (treatment_details, text, optional): Enter the details of the treatment or care the patient is receiving.
* **Patient Address** (patient_address, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient Phone** (patient_phone, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to aid healthcare professionals in providing the best possible care.
* If a patient has a complex medical history or condition, consider adding additional information or attaching relevant documentation to provide a clear understanding of their needs.
* Remember that this form is intended for internal use within healthcare facilities, and sensitive information should be treated with confidentiality and care.
