# Mental Health Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information for mental health services, therapy, or treatment in a healthcare setting. It may be used for billing, insurance claims, or other administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal information, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the patient's insurance coverage, including the insurance name and provider.
3. Enter the patient's therapist information, which may include the therapist's name and contact details.
4. Review and fill in any additional information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details. (Text, optional)
* **Insurance Information**: Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider and any other relevant details. (Text, optional)
* **Insurance Name**: Enter the name of the patient's insurance policy. (Text, optional)
* **Policy Holder**: Enter the name of the policy holder, which may be the patient or their guardian. (Text, optional)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY". (Date, optional)
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter any additional information about the patient's medical insurance, such as policy numbers or plan types. (Text, optional)
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the patient's medical insurance provider. (Text, optional)
* **Therapist Information**: Enter the name and contact details of the patient's therapist. (Text, optional)

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with unauthorized parties.
