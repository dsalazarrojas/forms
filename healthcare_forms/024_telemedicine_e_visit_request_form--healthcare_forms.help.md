# Telemedicine E Visit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine E Visit Request Form is designed for patients to request virtual medical consultations with healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please select the type of visit you are requesting: "Telemedicine Visit", "In-person Visit", or "Phone Call".
- Choose the type of healthcare professional you would like to meet with: "Doctor", "Specialist", or "Nurse".
- Select a preferred date and time for the visit.
- Enter any additional comments or questions in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Telemedicine E Visit Request Form Page 1 Label**: The first page is a note field where you can provide any additional information or context about your visit request.
* **Telemedicine Visit/In-person Visit/Phone Call**: This field allows you to choose the type of visit you are requesting. Please select one of the available options: "Telemedicine Visit", "In-person Visit", or "Phone Call".
* **Doctor/Specialist/Nurse**: This field lets you choose the type of healthcare professional you would like to meet with. Select one or multiple options that fit your needs.
* **Preferred Date and Time**: Enter your preferred date and time for the visit. Please format the date as MM/DD/YY and the time as HH:MM.
* **Additional Comments/Questions**: This text field is where you can provide any further comments or questions related to your visit request.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you will receive confirmation of your visit request and any further instructions.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional comments or information that will help us process your visit request.

## Tips

- Please ensure to provide accurate and complete information to help us process your visit request efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will do our best to accommodate your preferred date and time, but please be flexible and understand that availability may be subject to change.
