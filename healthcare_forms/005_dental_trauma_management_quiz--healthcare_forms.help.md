# Dental Trauma Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Trauma Management Quiz is a form designed to help healthcare professionals assess the level of trauma in patients with dental injuries. It consists of several sections that guide the evaluation and treatment planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's information, including their age and any relevant medical history.
2. Evaluate the trauma score and Trauma Score 2 to determine the level of trauma.
3. Create a treatment plan based on the trauma assessment and patient's needs.
4. Indicate your level of confidence in the assessment and treatment plan.
5. Document your clinical decision-making process, including any relevant medical considerations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the patient's age category to provide context for the trauma assessment.
	+ Choose the patient's age from the provided options: 1-5 years, 6-12 years, 13-17 years, or 18+ years.
* **Trauma Score** (`trauma_score`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value to represent the level of trauma.
	+ The higher the value, the more severe the trauma.
* **Trauma Score 2** (`trauma_score_2`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the level of trauma using the provided options: mild, moderate, or severe.
	+ Choose the most accurate description of the trauma.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Describe the recommended treatment for the patient.
	+ Provide a concise and clear plan tailored to the patient's needs.
* **Treatment Plan 2** (`treatment_plan_2`, text, required: false): Provide an additional treatment plan or note if necessary.
	+ This field can be used to record additional information or concerns.
* **Trauma Assessment** (`trauma_assessment`, time, required: false): Estimate the time required for the trauma assessment.
	+ Enter the time it took to complete the assessment.
* **Trauma Assessment 2** (`trauma_assessment_2`, time, required: false): Estimate the time required for the trauma assessment.
	+ Enter the time it took to complete the second assessment.
* **Confidence** (`confidence`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your level of confidence in the assessment and treatment plan.
	+ Choose from the options: 0-25%, 26-50%, 51-75%, or 76-100%.
* **Clinical Decision Making** (`clinical_decision_making`, select_multiple, required: false): Record your clinical decision-making process.
	+ Indicate the factors that influenced your decision, such as patient medical history or other relevant considerations.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Only fill in fields that are relevant to the patient's condition.
* If a field is marked as optional, use your professional judgment to determine whether it is necessary.
* Consult the patient's medical record or seek additional information if unsure about any field or section.
