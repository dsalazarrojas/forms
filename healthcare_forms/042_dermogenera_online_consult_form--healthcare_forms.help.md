# Dermogenera Online Consult Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dermogenera Online Consult Form is designed to gather information about a patient's skin condition, treatment, and follow-up appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Provide clear and concise answers to each field.
3.  Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Skin Condition** (1, text, required): Describe your skin condition in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your skin condition.
*   **Medical History** (2, text, required): Provide your medical history in this field.
    Please provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your skin condition.
*   **Skin Care Routine** (3, select one, required): Choose how often you perform your skin care routine.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often you perform your skin care routine.
*   **Skin Care Products** (4, select multiple, required): Choose your current skin care products.
    Select all the products that you currently use for your skin care routine.
*   **Skin Symptoms** (5, text, required): Describe your skin symptoms in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your skin symptoms.
*   **Medical Treatment** (6, text, required): Describe your medical treatment in this field.
    Please provide any medical treatment that you are currently receiving.
*   **Skin Care Doctor** (7, text, required): Describe your skin care doctor in this field.
    Please provide the name and contact information of your skin care doctor.
*   **Follow Up** (8, date, required): Schedule your follow-up appointment in this field.
    Please choose a date for your follow-up appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (9, time, required): Choose your appointment time in this field.
    Select a time for your follow-up appointment.
*   **Contact Information** (10, email, required): Provide your contact information in this field.
    Please provide your email address or phone number for contact.
*   **Contact Number** (11, text, required): Provide your contact number in this field.
    Please enter your contact number.
*   **Other Information** (12, text, required): Provide any other relevant information in this field.
    Please provide any other information that may be helpful for your skin condition.
*   **Skin Condition Frequency** (13, select one, required): Choose how often your skin condition occurs.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often your skin condition occurs.
*   **Skin Condition Description** (14, text, required): Describe your skin condition in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your skin condition.
*   **Skin Care Doctors** (15, select multiple, required): Choose your skin care doctor.
    Select all the skin care doctors that you see for your skin condition.
*   **Specialist** (16, select multiple, required): Choose your specialist.
    Select all the specialists that you see for your skin condition.
*   **Skin Care Doctor Specialist** (17, select one, required): Choose whether you have a specialist.
    Select Yes or No to indicate whether you have a specialist.
*   **Skin Care Routine Frequency** (18, select one, required): Choose how often you perform your skin care routine.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often you perform your skin care routine.
*   **Skin Care Routine Description** (19, text, required): Describe your skin care routine in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your skin care routine.
*   **Medical Treatment Frequency** (20, select one, required): Choose how often you receive medical treatment.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often you receive medical treatment.
*   **Medical Treatment Description** (21, text, required): Describe your medical treatment in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your medical treatment.
*   **Skin Care Doctor Frequencies** (22, select one, required): Choose how often you see your skin care doctor.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often you see your skin care doctor.
*   **Skin Care Doctor Frequencies Description** (23, text, required): Describe your skin care doctor frequencies in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your skin care doctor frequencies.
*   **Follow Up Frequencies** (24, select one, required): Choose how often you have follow-ups.
    Select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely to indicate how often you have follow-ups.
*   **Follow Up Frequencies Description** (25, text, required): Describe your follow-up frequencies in this field.
    Please provide a clear and concise description of your follow-up frequencies.
