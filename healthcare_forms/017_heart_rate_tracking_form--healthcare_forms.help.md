# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prophets" form is used to record a client's workout information, including their resting and maximum heart rate, exercise name, and workout date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client ID, which is a unique identifier for the client.
2. Select the workout date for the client's workout session.
3. Enter the exercise name that was performed during the workout session.
4. Enter the client's resting heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).
5. Enter the client's maximum heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).
6. Enter the client's minimum heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client ID (1, number, required: false):** Enter the unique identifier for the client. This field is optional but recommended for accurate tracking.
* **Workout Date (2, date, required: false):** Select the date of the client's workout session.
* **Exercise Name (3, text, required: false):** Enter the name of the exercise performed during the workout session.
* **Resting Heart Rate (4, number, required: false):** Enter the client's resting heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Maximum Heart Rate (5, number, required: false):** Enter the client's maximum heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Minimum Heart Rate (6, number, required: false):** Enter the client's minimum heart rate, measured in beats per minute (bpm).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the client's workout session.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
