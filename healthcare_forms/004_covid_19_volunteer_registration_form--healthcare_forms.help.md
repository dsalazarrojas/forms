# Covid 19 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who want to register as volunteers during the COVID-19 pandemic. The information collected in this form will help us match volunteers with the right opportunities in our healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
* Ensure you have permission from your manager or HR department before registering as a volunteer if you are an employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Volunteer role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role in the volunteer program. Options are:
	+ Volunteer: General volunteer duties.
	+ Staff: Medical or administrative staff role.
	+ Other: Specify your role in the "Other" field.
* **Volunteer location** (`volunteer_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose where you are willing to volunteer. Options are:
	+ Hospital
	+ Clinic
	+ Other
* **Availability days** (`availability_days`, date, optional): Enter the date when you can start volunteering.
* **Availability time** (`availability_time`, time, optional): Enter your availability time slot in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 or 14:30).

## Tips
* Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your volunteering, please let us know in the "Other" field under Volunteer role.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
