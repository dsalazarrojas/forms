# patient-preparation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient-preparation-survey form is a comprehensive tool for collecting patient information in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. Ensure that you answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or advice from a healthcare professional.
4. Complete all required fields to ensure that your response is submitted correctly.
5. Review your submission to ensure that all information is accurate and complete before submitting.
