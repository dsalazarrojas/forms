# Covid 19 Screening Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Screening Questionnaire Form is a medical form used to gather information about patients, particularly concerning their health status, vaccination history, and symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your Medical Facility Name, which is the name of your medical facility.
2.  Enter the Patient Name to identify the patient being screened.
3.  Enter the Contact Information to provide the contact details for follow-up or further communication.
4.  For Medical Card Number, please enter the patient's unique medical ID number.
5.  If applicable, select the Vaccine Dose 1 Date and Time for the first vaccination dose administered.
6.  Similarly, if necessary, enter the Vaccine Dose 2 Date and Time for the second dose.
7.  Indicate whether the patient has experienced any Cough symptoms.
8.  Check if the patient has a Runny Nose.
9.  Check if the patient has a Sore Throat.
10. Check if the patient is experiencing Shortness of Breath.
11. If applicable, select the Vaccine Dose 3 Date and enter the corresponding Time for the third vaccination dose.
12. Add any additional Note about the patient's case, if required.
13. Enter the Email address of the Medical Facility for communication purposes.
14. Enter the Phone number of the medical facility.
15. Enter the Medical Facility Address where the patient was vaccinated or treated.
16. Provide the Medical Facility City.
17. Enter the state of the Medical Facility.
18. Enter the zip code of the Medical Facility.
19. Enter the Country where the Medical Facility is located.
20. Enter the Fax number of the Medical Facility.
21. Enter the Medical Facility's Website for reference.
22. Enter the Phone number of the Medical Facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical Facility Name** (`medical_facility_name`, Text, Required): The name of the medical facility providing healthcare services to the patient.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): The name of the patient being screened.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, Text, Required): Contact details for follow-up or further communication with the patient.
*   **Medical Card Number** (`medical_card_number`, Text, Required): The patient's unique medical ID number.
*   **Vaccine Dose 1 Date** (`vaccine_dose1_date`, Date, Optional): The date of the first vaccination dose administered to the patient.
*   **Vaccine Dose 1 Time** (`vaccine_dose1_time`, Time, Optional): The time of the first vaccination dose administered to the patient.
*   **Vaccine Dose 2 Date** (`vaccine_dose2_date`, Date, Optional): The date of the second vaccination dose administered to the patient.
*   **Vaccine Dose 2 Time** (`vaccine_dose2_time`, Time, Optional): The time of the second vaccination dose administered to the patient.
*   **Cough** (`symptoms_cough`, Select One, Optional): Indicates whether the patient has experienced a Cough.
*   **Runny Nose** (`symptoms_runny_nose`, Select One, Optional): Indicates whether the patient has a Runny Nose.
*   **Sore Throat** (`symptoms_sore_throat`, Select One, Optional): Indicates whether the patient has a Sore Throat.
*   **Shortness of Breath** (`symptoms_shortness_of_breath`, Select One, Optional): Indicates whether the patient is experiencing Shortness of Breath.
*   **Vaccine Dose 3 Date** (`vaccine_dose3_date`, Date, Optional): The date of the third vaccination dose administered.
*   **Vaccine Dose 3 Time** (`vaccine_dose3_time`, Time, Optional): The time of the third vaccination dose administered.
*   **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): A field for any additional comments or notes about the patient's case.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): The email address of the Medical Facility.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): The phone number of the medical facility.
*   **Medical Facility Address** (`medical_facility_address`, Text, Optional): The address where the patient was vaccinated or treated.
*   **Medical Facility City** (`medical_facility_city`, Text, Optional): The city where the medical facility is located.
*   **Medical Facility State** (`medical_facility_state`, Text, Optional): The state where the medical facility is located.
*   **Medical Facility Zip** (`medical_facility_zip`, Text, Optional): The zip code of the Medical Facility.
*   **Medical Facility Country** (`medical_facility_country`, Text, Optional): The Country where the medical facility is located.
*   **Medical Facility Fax** (`medical_facility_fax`, Text, Optional): The fax number of the medical facility.
*   **Medical Facility Website** (`medical_facility_website`, Text, Optional): The website of the medical facility.
*   **Medical Facility Phone** (`medical_facility_phone`, Text, Optional): The phone number of the medical facility.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have a legitimate reason to use this form and do not duplicate patient screenings or forms if the information is already available or redundant.
*   Double-check all provided information to avoid errors or inconsistencies.
*   Follow the form's requirements and guidelines for accurate and thorough completion.
