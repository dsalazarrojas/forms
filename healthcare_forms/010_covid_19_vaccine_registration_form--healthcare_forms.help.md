# COVID 19 Vaccine Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather personal and vaccination details from patients. It is used to collect and store data for healthcare systems, allowing for efficient and secure vaccine administration.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  For each field, provide the required information.
3.  Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your active and valid email address for vaccine registration confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, **required**): Enter your full street address for vaccination services.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, **required**): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, **required**): Enter your valid zip code.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, **optional**): If you have any known allergies, list them here.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): List any chronic medical conditions you may have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, **optional**): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Current Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, **required**): Please select your current vaccination status from the options provided:
	+ Unvaccinated
	+ One dose received
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Boosted
* **Which Dose Are You Seeking** (`dose_seeking`, select_one, **required**): Please select which dose of the vaccine you are seeking:
	+ First dose
	+ Second dose
	+ Booster dose
	+ Second booster
* **Vaccine Type Preference** (`vaccine_preference`, select_one, **optional**): If you have a preference for the vaccine type, select one from the options provided:
	+ Pfizer-BioNTech
	+ Moderna
	+ Johnson & Johnson
	+ No preference
* **Consent & Acknowledgment** (`consent`, select_one, **required**): Please select your consent for receiving the COVID-19 vaccine:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ No, I do not consent

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or the healthcare system.
