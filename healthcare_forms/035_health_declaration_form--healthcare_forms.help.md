# Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect health information from individuals for specific purposes, such as traveling, attending events, or visiting healthcare facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your traveler type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name and email address (if applicable).
3. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
4. Provide your date of birth.
5. Choose your contact preference (email, phone, or mail).
6. List any medical conditions you may have.
7. Select any allergies you may have (multiple selections allowed).
8. Describe your travel history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler Type**: Select one of the available options to indicate your type of traveler. This field is not required.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you have one. This field is not required but recommended for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you have one. This field is not required but recommended for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted in case of any issues or emergencies.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have, such as medication, food, or insect stings. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Travel History**: Describe your recent travel history, including any relevant dates, locations, and activities.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Ensure you are completing the form for the correct purpose and purpose.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult a medical professional.
* If you have completed this form previously, please ensure you are updating it with the latest information.
