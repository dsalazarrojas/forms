# CME Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from healthcare professionals about their experience with CME programs. Your input is valuable in helping providers improve the quality of their programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the name of the CME event you attended in the "CME Event Name" field.
2. Select the date you attended the event from the calendar.
3. Enter your name and select your medical professional credential.
4. If applicable, enter your medical specialty.
5. Rate the relevance, quality, and effectiveness of the content, instructors, and overall program using the provided scales.
6. Describe any new knowledge you gained from the program and how you plan to apply it in your practice.
7. Finally, provide any suggestions for improvement and topics of interest for future programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **CME Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the CME event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date you attended the event.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a healthcare professional.
* **Professional Credential** (`professional_credential`, select_one, required): Select your medical license type (e.g., MD, DO, RN, etc.).
* **Medical Specialty** (`specialty`, text, optional): Enter your primary medical specialty (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for future programs).
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the content on a scale of 1-5.
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the content on a scale of 1-5.
* **Instructor Effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the instructors on a scale of 1-5.
* **Presentation Clarity** (`presentation_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity and organization of the presentation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Learning Objectives Met** (`learning_objectives_met`, select_one, required): Rate whether the learning objectives were met on a scale of 1-5.
* **New Knowledge Gained** (`knowledge_gained`, text, optional): Describe any new knowledge you gained from the program.
* **Applicability** (`applicability`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to apply the knowledge gained in your practice.
* **Venue Quality** (`venue_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the venue and facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Materials Quality** (`materials_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the educational materials provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement for future programs.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, text, optional): Suggest any topics you'd like to see in future programs.
* **Attend Future Programs** (`attend_future`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would attend future programs from this provider.
* **Venue Quality** is removed as it seems like a duplicate field with a different label.
