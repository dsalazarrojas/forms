# Nova Fitness ( Body Information Form ) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nova Fitness ( Body Information Form ) is used to gather essential data about your physical characteristics and preferences. This information will help us to better understand and support your fitness journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and ensure you are answering the questions as accurately as possible.
* For fields with options, select the one that best describes your situation.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask.
* Submit your form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to collect your basic contact information.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: true): Please enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: true): Select your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Indicate any medical conditions that may affect your fitness journey.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history that may impact your fitness activities.
* **Workout Goal** (`workout_goal`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your primary workout goal.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose how often you plan to train.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter emergency contact information for your safety and support.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
