# Preliminary Health Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preliminary Health Insurance Intake Form is used to collect initial information from users regarding their health insurance coverage. This form helps gather essential demographic information, such as name, contact details, and insurance-related information, to facilitate further processing and registration in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each field with the corresponding information.
2.  Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Required): Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your active email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your active phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, Required): Select the relation of your emergency contact person (Self, Spouse, Other).
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, Required): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Insurance Group Number** (`insurance_group_number`, text, Not Required): If applicable, enter your insurance group or policy number.
* **Policy Holder Name** (`policy_holder_name`, text, Not Required): Enter the name of the policy holder, if different from yourself.
* **Policy Holder DOB** (`policy_holder_dob`, date, Not Required): Enter the date of birth of the policy holder, if different from yourself.
* **Policy Holder Phone** (`policy_holder_phone`, text, Not Required): Enter the phone number of the policy holder, if different from yourself.
* **Policy Holder Email** (`policy_holder_email`, email, Not Required): Enter the email address of the policy holder, if different from yourself.
* **Policy Holder Address** (`policy_holder_address`, text, Not Required): Enter the address of the policy holder, if different from yourself.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your insurance coverage.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your insurance provider or contact our support team for assistance.
- This form is a one-time form, and the information will be used for the sole purpose of initial registration and processing.
