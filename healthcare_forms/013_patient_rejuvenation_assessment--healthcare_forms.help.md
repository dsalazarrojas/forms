# Patient Rejuvenation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history, treatment goals, and other relevant health-related details to aid healthcare professionals in providing personalized care and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Answer all questions and fields that apply to the patient's current condition.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing medical history, concerns, and treatment goals.
3. If unsure about any question or field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification or consult with the healthcare professional filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Goals**
• **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, text, optional): This field is intended for the patient to list their personal goals and objectives related to their health and treatment.

### 2. **Patient History**
• **Patient History** (`patient_history`, text, optional): Please describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical procedures they have undergone.

### 3. **Concerns**
• **Concerns** (`concerns`, text, optional): List any concerns or issues the patient is currently experiencing related to their health or treatment.

### 4. **Medical History**
• **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any chronic conditions, allergies, or medical conditions that require ongoing treatment.

### 5. **Treatment History**
• **Treatment History** (`treatment_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's treatment history, including any medications, therapies, or medical procedures they have undergone.

### 6. **Medications**
• **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.

### 7. **Allergies**
• **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has to medications or substances.

### 8. **Medical Conditions**
• **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or illnesses the patient is currently experiencing.

### 9. **Medical History Additional**
• **Medical History Additional** (`medical_history_additional`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's medical history that is not covered in previous fields.

### 10. **Treatment Goals**
• **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, text, optional): List any treatment goals or objectives the patient has for their health and treatment.

### 11. **Patient Concerns**
• **Patient Concerns** (`patient_concerns`, text, optional): List any concerns or issues the patient is currently experiencing related to their health or treatment.

### 12. **Medical Professionals**
• **Medical Professionals** (`medical_professionals`, text, optional): List any medical professionals involved in the patient's care, such as doctors, nurses, or therapists.

### 13. **Medical Procedure**
• **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures the patient has undergone or is scheduled to undergo.

### 14. **Medical Specialties**
• **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, text, optional): List any medical specialties or areas of expertise the patient requires, such as cardiology or oncology.

# Tips

* Please be honest and detailed when providing information in this form.
* If unsure about any question or field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification or consult with the healthcare professional filling out the form.
* This form is intended to aid healthcare professionals in providing personalized care and treatment plans for the patient.
