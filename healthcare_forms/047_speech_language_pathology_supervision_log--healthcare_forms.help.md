# Speech Language Pathology Supervision Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track supervision activities in speech language pathology. It captures essential details about the supervisee, session, and comments from both the supervisor and supervisee. This information helps maintain accountability and ensures quality control in clinical or educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the "Supervisee Details" section with the supervisee's name, ID number, and any relevant contact information.
* In the "Session Details" section, provide a brief description of the session, including date, time, and location.
* In the "Session Notes" section, write a detailed description of the session, including any notable events, challenges, or outcomes.
* Both the supervisor and supervisee are required to sign and date the form to confirm they reviewed the information and provide comments.
* Select the "Status" field to indicate the current status of the session (Approved, In Progress, or Completed).
* Complete any additional comments or notes fields as needed.
* Finally, save the form to ensure it is recorded and easily accessible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisee Details** (`supervisee_details`, text, required): Enter the supervisee's name, ID number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required): Describe the session, including date, time, and location.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, required): Write a detailed description of the session, including any notable events, challenges, or outcomes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Write any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm you reviewed the information.
* **Supervisee Signature** (`supervisee_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm you reviewed the information.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter your name or ID number.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Enter the name or ID number of the person or organization responsible for supervising.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the session (Approved, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name or ID number (this field may be redundant as the supervisor's signature is already present).
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, required): Write any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Supervisee Comments** (`supervisee_comments`, text, required): Write any additional comments or feedback from the supervisee.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Comments 1** (`comments_1`, text, optional): Write any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): Write any additional comments or feedback about the session.

Note: Fields 11 and 16 can be considered duplicate or redundant and can be removed or merged.
