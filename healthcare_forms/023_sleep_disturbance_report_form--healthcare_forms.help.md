# Sleep Disturbance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Disturbance Report Form is designed to collect detailed information about a patient's sleep patterns, quality, and environment. This form is typically used by healthcare professionals to assess and manage sleep disturbances in patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information section (field 1).
2. Describe your sleep patterns and any notable sleep-related issues (field 2).
3. Evaluate your sleep quality and any factors affecting it (field 3).
4. Report any sleep-related disturbances or issues (field 4).
5. Share your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments (field 5).
6. Describe your sleep environment and any factors that might impact your sleep (field 6).
7. Record your daily sleep schedule (field 7).
8. List any medications you're currently taking (field 8).
9. Report your sleep duration and any notable sleep-related metrics (fields 9-15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`field 1`, text, required): Enter your basic contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`field 2`, text, optional): Describe your typical sleep patterns, including times you go to bed and wake up.
* **Sleep Quality** (`field 3`, text, optional): Assess your sleep quality using your own evaluation or any notable factors that affect it.
* **Sleep Disturbances** (`field 4`, text, optional): Report any sleep-related issues or disturbances you've experienced.
* **Medical History** (`field 5`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
* **Sleep Environment** (`field 6`, text, optional): Describe any factors in your sleep environment that might be affecting your sleep, such as noise or temperature.
* **Sleep Schedule** (`field 7`, text, optional): Record your daily sleep schedule, including the times you go to bed and wake up.
* **Medication** (`field 8`, select_one, optional): List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Sleep Duration** (`field 9`, text, optional): Report your average or typical sleep duration.
* **Bedtime** (`field 10`, time, optional): Record the time you go to bed.
* **Wake Time** (`field 11`, time, optional): Record the time you wake up.
* **Sleep Quality Score** (`field 12`, number, optional): Evaluate your sleep quality using a score.
* **Sleep Latency** (`field 13`, number, optional): Record your sleep latency.
* **Wake Latency** (`field 14`, number, optional): Record your wake latency.
* **Sleep Efficiency** (`field 15`, number, optional): Assess your sleep efficiency.
* **Total Sleep Time** (`field 16`, number, optional): Report your total sleep time.
* **Latency To Sleep** (`field 17`, number, optional): Record your latency to sleep.
* **Total Wake Time** (`field 18`, number, optional): Report your total wake time.
* **Average Wake Time** (`field 19`, number, optional): Calculate your average wake time.
* **Sleep Efficiency Percentage** (`field 20`, number, optional): Evaluate your sleep efficiency percentage.
* **Total Latency** (`field 21`, number, optional): Record your total latency.
* **Wake Time Per Day** (`field 22`, number, optional): Report your average wake time per day.
* **Average Latency To Sleep** (`field 23`, number, optional): Record your average latency to sleep.
* **Sleep Latency Per Day** (`field 24`, number, optional): Report your sleep latency per day.
* **Medication List** (`field 25`, select_multiple, optional): List any other medications you're taking.

## Tips
To ensure accurate and helpful data, please provide as much detail as possible for each field. If you have trouble understanding a field or feel unsure about how to fill it out, please don't hesitate to contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
