# Patient-Centered-Care-Model-Training-Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register staff members who have completed patient-centered care model training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the staff members who have completed training from the list.
2. Choose the type of training completed.
3. Enter the duration of the training.
4. Enter the date and time of the training.
5. Choose the status of the enrollment.
6. Enter any comments about the training.
7. Choose the status of the submission.
8. Enter the email and phone number of the form owner.
9. Choose the status of the reviewer 1.
10. Choose the status of the reviewer 2.
11. Choose the status of the submission for the form owner.
