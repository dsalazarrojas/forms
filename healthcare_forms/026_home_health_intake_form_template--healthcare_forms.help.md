**Home Health Intake Form Template - Help Guide**
=====================================================

## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information for home health services. It is designed to gather essential details about the patient, including their contact information, medical history, and careplan plan. This information will be used to provide personalized care and services to the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
--------------------------------

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's street address in the "Street" field.
4. Enter the patient's city in the "City" field.
5. Enter the patient's state in the "State" field.
6. Enter the patient's zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
8. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter any medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing in the "Medical Condition" field (optional).
10. If applicable, enter additional details about the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition Details" field.
11. If applicable, enter the patient's relationship with the caregiver in the "Caregiver Relationship" field.
12. If applicable, enter any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
13. If applicable, enter the patient's current medications in the "Current Medications" field.
14. If applicable, enter the patient's medical provider's name and contact information in the "Medical Provider" field.
15. If applicable, enter the name and phone number of the patient's primary caregiver in the "Primary Caregiver" field.
16. If applicable, enter the name and phone number of the patient's emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
17. If applicable, enter the emergency contact's relationship with the patient in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
18. If applicable, enter the patient's medical insurance information in the "Medical Insurance" field.
19. If applicable, enter the patient's medical insurance group information in the "Medical Insurance Group" field.
20. If applicable, enter the source of the patient's referral in the "Referral Source" field.
21. If applicable, enter the patient's careplan plan in the "Careplan Plan" field.
22. If applicable, enter any notes from the caregiver in the "Caregiver Notes" field.
23. Enter the patient's discharge date in the "Discharge Date" field.
24. If applicable, enter the patient's discharge diagnosis in the "Discharge Diagnosis" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Street** (`patient_street`, text, optional): Enter the patient's street address.
* **City** (`patient_city`, text, required): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`patient_state`, text, required): Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, required): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Condition** (`patient_medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`patient_medical_condition_details`, text, optional): If applicable, enter additional details about the patient's medical condition.
* **Caregiver Relationship** (`patient_caregiver_relationship`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's relationship with the caregiver.
* **Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any allergies the patient has.
* **Current Medications** (`patient_current_medications`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's current medications.
* **Medical Provider** (`patient_medical_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's medical provider's name and contact information.
* **Primary Caregiver** (`patient_primary_caregiver`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and phone number of the patient's primary caregiver.
* **Emergency Contact** (`patient_emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and phone number of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`patient_emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the emergency contact's relationship with the patient.
* **Medical Insurance** (`patient_medical_insurance`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's medical insurance information.
* **Medical Insurance Group** (`patient_medical_insurance_group`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's medical insurance group information.
* **Referral Source** (`patient_referral_source`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the source of the patient's referral.
* **Careplan Plan** (`patient_careplan_plan`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's careplan plan.
* **Caregiver Notes** (`patient_caregiver_notes`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any notes from the caregiver.
* **Discharge Date** (`patient_discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the patient's discharge date.
* **Discharge Diagnosis** (`patient_discharge_diagnosis`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's discharge diagnosis.

Note: This form is intended to be completed by the patient or their caregiver.
