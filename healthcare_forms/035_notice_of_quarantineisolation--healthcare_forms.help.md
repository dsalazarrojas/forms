# Notice Of QuarantineIsolation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's release from quarantine or isolation. It's essential for healthcare providers to fill this out accurately, as it helps track and manage patients' health statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a date of release (1) or specify if the patient is still under quarantine or isolation.
2. Enter the date the patient started quarantine or isolation (2).
3. Enter the date the patient should start or has started isolation (3).
4. Enter the date the patient's quarantine or isolation will end (4).
5. Provide the patient's name (5) as it appears in their medical records.
6. Enter the patient's address (6) as it appears in their medical records.
7. Enter the patient's contact number (7) as it appears in their medical records.
8. Enter the name of the physician who is responsible for the patient's care (8).
9. Specify the medical facility where the patient received care (9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date (1)** (`release_date`, optional): Enter the date you are releasing the patient from quarantine or isolation.
* **Quarantine Start Date (2)** (`quarantine_start_date`, optional): Enter the date when the patient entered quarantine or isolation.
* **Isolation Start Date (3)** (`isolation_start_date`, optional): Enter the date when the patient's isolation period began.
* **Quarantine End Date (4)** (`quarantine_end_date`, optional): Enter the date when the patient's quarantine or isolation will end.
* **Patient Name (5)** (`patient_name`, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears in their medical records.
* **Patient Address (6)** (`patient_address`, optional): Enter the patient's address as it appears in their medical records.
* **Contact Number (7)** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter the patient's contact number as it appears in their medical records.
* **Physician Name (8)** (`physician_name`, optional): Enter the name of the physician who is responsible for the patient's care.
* **Medical Facility (9)** (`medical_facility`, optional): Specify the medical facility where the patient received care.

## Tips
- Make sure to use accurate dates for each field.
- Double-check that the patient's information is up-to-date.
- Complete all fields that apply to the patient's situation.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
