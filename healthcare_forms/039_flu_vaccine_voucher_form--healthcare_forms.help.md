# Flu Vaccine Voucher Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about flu vaccine administration, including patient details, vaccination status, and provider information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the patient and their flu vaccine administration.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Flu Vaccine Type**: Select the type of flu vaccine administered to the patient, such as "Flu vaccine for children aged 6 months to 17 years" or "Flu vaccine for adults aged 18-64".
* **Medical Insurance Status**: Indicate whether the patient has medical insurance or not.
* **Flu Vaccine Dosage**: Enter the number of vaccines administered to the patient.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By**: Select the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine, such as "Clinic" or "Doctor".
* **Date Flu Vaccine Administered**: Enter the date the vaccine was administered in the format of `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Flu Vaccine Received Location**: Enter the location where the vaccine was received, such as a clinic or hospital.
* **Flu Vaccine Voucher Number**: Enter a unique voucher number for the vaccine administration.
* **Flu Vaccine Provider**: Enter the name and title of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your organization's relevant department or team for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only and the information collected will be used for public health monitoring and research purposes.
