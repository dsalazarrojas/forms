# Medical Biller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants applying for a medical billing position. It will be reviewed by our team to assess your qualifications and schedule for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the required information on each page to the best of your ability. Each field should be filled out carefully to avoid missing any important details.
2.  Review each page to ensure you have completed all required and optional fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (Page 1)** (`medical_biller_application_form`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, where you can provide any additional information about your application.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have worked in a similar medical billing role.
* **Experience Description** (`experience_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your experience in medical billing, including any relevant job duties and accomplishments.
* **Availability Days Per Week** (`availability_days_per_week`, number, optional): Indicate how many days you are available to work per week.
* **Availability Hours Per Week** (`availability_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_one, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Work Days Per Week** (`work_days_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you are available to work per week.
* **Work Hours Per Day** (`work_hours_per_day`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per day.
* **Break Duration** (`break_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of your typical break, if applicable.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you take breaks.
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you hold any relevant certifications.
* **Medical School Attended** (`medical_school_attended`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have attended medical school.
* **Medical Degree** (`medical_degree`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have a medical degree.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you hold a medical license.
* **Malpractice Insurance** (`malpractice_insurance`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have malpractice insurance.
* **Malpractice Coverage** (`malpractice_coverage`, select_multiple, optional): Select your malpractice coverage type.
* **Malpractice Limit** (`malpractice_limit`, number, optional): Enter your malpractice limit, if applicable.
* **Malpractice Coverage Limit** (`malpractice_coverage_limit`, number, optional): Enter your malpractice coverage limit, if applicable.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee** (`medical_biller_application_fee`, number, optional): Enter the application fee for the medical billing position.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee Currency** (`medical_biller_application_fee_currency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the currency of the application fee.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee Amount** (`medical_biller_application_fee_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the application fee.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee Payment Method** (`medical_biller_application_fee_payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method for the application fee.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee Includes** (`medical_biller_application_fee_includes`, select_multiple, optional): Select what the application fee includes.
* **Medical Biller Application Fee Refund Policy** (`medical_biller_application_fee_refund_policy`, select_multiple, optional): Select the refund policy for the application fee.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents or certificates for the application.
