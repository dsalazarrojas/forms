# Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Consent Form is a document that collects patient information and consent for a specific procedure. This form is meant to provide a clear and concise way to gather all the necessary details about the patient, the procedure, and the patient's understanding of the risks and benefits involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "patient_info" field, which is a text field where you can enter the patient's personal details.
2.  Next, move on to the "procedure_info" field, where you can provide information about the procedure being performed.
3.  After that, fill out the "risks_and_benefits" field, where you can discuss the potential risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
4.  Finally, read and sign the "informed_consent" field, where you will be able to provide your consent to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (patient_info, text, optional): Enter any necessary information about the patient, including their name, contact information, and medical history.
* **procedure_info** (procedure_info, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the procedure, including its purpose, duration, and any specific instructions or requirements.
* **risks_and_benefits** (risks_and_benefits, text, optional): Discuss the potential risks and benefits associated with the procedure, including any side effects, complications, or alternative options.
* **informed_consent** (informed_consent, text, required): Read and sign this section to provide your consent to the procedure. This section should be signed by the patient or their representative.
