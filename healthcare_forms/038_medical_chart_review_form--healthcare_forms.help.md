# Medical Chart Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Chart Review Form is used for healthcare providers to review a patient's medical chart. It is a critical tool for documenting patient information, tracking medical history, and monitoring treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Patient Name** in the designated field. This information will help you identify the patient's identity and locate their chart.
2. Record the **Date of Visit** in the date field. This is the date of the patient's most recent visit.
3. Document the **Medical History** in the text field. This section is for noting any significant medical issues or allergies.
4. Select the **Medication Allergies** by choosing one or more of the available options. This is for recording any known allergies.
5. Select the **Medications** by choosing one or more of the available options. This is for documenting any medications the patient is currently taking.
6. Enter the **Lab Results** in the text field. This is for recording any lab results or test results.
7. Record the **Diagnosis** by selecting one or more of the available options. This is for documenting the patient's diagnosis.
8. Enter the **Treatment Plan** in the text field. This is for outlining the patient's treatment plan.
9. Enter the **Provider Name** in the designated field. This will help identify the healthcare provider responsible for the patient's care.
10. Obtain the **Provider Signature** from the healthcare provider. This is for verifying that the provider has reviewed the chart.
11. Record the **Date** in the date field. This is the date the form was completed.
12. Record the **Time** in the time field. This is the time the form was completed.
13. Enter the **Medication Dosage** in the numeric field. This is for recording the dosage of the medication.
14. Select the **Medication Frequency** by choosing one or more of the available options. This is for recording how frequently the medication is taken.
15. Enter the **Medical Record** in the text field. This is for adding any additional information to the patient's medical record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Record the date of the patient's most recent visit.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Document any significant medical issues or allergies.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any known allergies the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_one, optional): Select any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, text, optional): Record any lab results or test results.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, select_multiple, optional): Select any diagnosis the patient has been given.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline the patient's treatment plan.
* **Provider** (`provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Obtain the healthcare provider's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Record the date the form was completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Record the time the form was completed.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, number, optional): Enter the medication dosage.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently the medication is taken.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Add any additional information to the patient's medical record.
