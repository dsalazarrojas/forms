# pre_visit_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-visit planning form is designed to collect essential patient information before an upcoming visit. This form helps our medical team gather relevant details to provide the best possible care and service during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your personal details in the `patient_info` section.
2. Select the reason for your visit in the `visit_reason` section.
3. Provide your contact information in the `patient_phone` section.
4. Answer the questions regarding your medical history, family history, allergies, and other medical conditions in the `medical_history`, `family_history`, `allergies`, `medications`, `medical_conditions`, and `medical_procedures` sections.
5. Describe your social history in the `social_history` section.
6. Enter your address in the `patient_address` section.
7. Provide your email address in the `patient_email` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (Text, Required: No): Enter your personal details, such as your name and date of birth.
* **appointment_info** (Number, Required: No): Enter the appointment date, time, and other relevant details about your visit.
* **visit_reason** (Text, Required: No): Select the reason for your visit from the available options.
* **patient_phone** (Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your visit.
* **medical_history** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your medical history.
* **family_history** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your family history.
* **allergies** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your allergies.
* **medications** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your medications.
* **medical_conditions** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your medical conditions.
* **medical_procedures** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your medical procedures.
* **medical_tests** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your medical tests.
* **social_history** (Text, Required: No): Describe your social history in detail.
* **patient_address** (Text, Required: No): Enter your address to ensure we can contact you at the right location.
* **patient_email** (Email, Required: No): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about your visit.
