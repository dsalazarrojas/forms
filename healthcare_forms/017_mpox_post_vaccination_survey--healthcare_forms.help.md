# Mpox Post-Vaccination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Post-Vaccination Survey is a tool used to collect information from patients after they have received the Mpox vaccination. This form helps healthcare professionals gather data to monitor and assess the effects and outcomes of the vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (Patient Info, text, optional): Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other identifying information to help us better understand your health profile.
* **vaccination_details** (Vaccination Details, text, optional): Describe your experience with the Mpox vaccination, including any side effects you may have experienced.
* **symptoms** (Symptoms, text, optional): List any symptoms you may be experiencing that are related to the vaccination.
* **contact_info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Provide any contact information you feel comfortable sharing with us, such as your email or phone number.
* **medical_history** (Medical History, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history that may impact your vaccination experience.
* **test_results** (Test Results, text, optional): If you have undergone any tests related to the vaccination, enter the results here.
* **vaccination_schedule** (Vaccination Schedule, text, optional): Describe your vaccination schedule, including the dates and times of your vaccination appointments.
* **vaccine_doses** (Vaccine Doses, text, optional): Report how many doses of the Mpox vaccination you have received.
* **side_effects** (Side Effects, text, optional): List any side effects you may be experiencing after receiving the vaccination.
* **follow_up** (Follow-Up, text, optional): Provide any follow-up information, such as appointment dates or contact details.

Please note that this form is intended for patients who have received the Mpox vaccination. If you have not received the vaccination, you will not be able to access this form. Authorized healthcare professionals will be able to access the form and use the collected data to monitor and assess the effects and outcomes of the vaccination.
