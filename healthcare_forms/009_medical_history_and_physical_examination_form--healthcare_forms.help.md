# Medical History And Physical Examination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect comprehensive data on a patient's medical history, including personal details, medical history, physical examination, allergies and medications, social history, and family medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Information** (<type>note)
	* This field is used to note any important information about the patient's visit.

### 2. **Medical History**
	* This field is used to record the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions.

### 3. **Physical Examination**
	* This field is used to record the results of a physical examination, including any notable observations or findings.

### 4. **Allergies And Meds** (Aspirin, Asthma, Blood Thinners)
	* This field is used to select any allergies or medications the patient is currently taking. Please select all that apply.

### 5. **Social History** (Male, Female)
	* This field is used to determine the patient's sex. Please select the correct option.

### 6. **Family History** (text)
	* This field is used to record any medical conditions that run in the patient's family. Please provide any relevant information.

### 7. **Medical Conditions** (Diabetes, Hypertension, Asthma)
	* This field is used to select any medical conditions the patient has or is currently experiencing. Please select all that apply.

### 8. **Medications** (Painkiller, Blood Thinners, Insulins)
	* This field is used to select any medications the patient is currently taking. Please select all that apply.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* This form is for general use and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical judgment.
* If any field is unclear or not applicable, please contact the relevant medical professional for guidance.
