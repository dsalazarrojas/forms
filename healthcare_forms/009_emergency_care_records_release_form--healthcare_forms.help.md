# Emergency Care Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Care Records Release Form is a document used to grant permission for healthcare providers to share or disclose medical records, lab results, or other healthcare information with authorized parties. This form is typically used to facilitate the access of sensitive patient information in emergency or urgent situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required information, which includes your name, the name of the healthcare provider, record access date, authorization type, authorized party, and expiration date (if applicable).
* Select the type of record you want to access (Medical Record, Surgical Record, Lab Results, or Prescription Information).
* Choose the type of authorization you are granting (Authorization to View, Authorization to Share, or Authorization to Copy).
* Select the authorized party (Patient, Parent or Guardian, or Authorized Representative).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as the patient or authorized party requesting access to your medical records.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the name of the healthcare provider who will be accessing your records. You may leave this field blank.
* **Record Access Date** (`record_access_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you want to access your medical records.
* **Record Type** (`record_type`, select multiple, optional): Select all the types of records you want to access (Medical Record, Surgical Record, Lab Results, or Prescription Information).
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of authorization you are granting (Authorization to View, Authorization to Share, or Authorization to Copy).
* **Authorized Party** (`authorized_party`, select one, optional): Select the person or party authorized to access your records (Patient, Parent or Guardian, or Authorized Representative).
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the authorization expires (if applicable).
* **Record Access Status** (`record_access_status`, select multiple, optional): Select the status of your record access (Active, Inactive, Denied).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are authorized to complete this form and have the necessary permissions to access the requested records.
* If you need to access your records for travel, please provide your travel itinerary or reason for travel in the form's optional fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about completing this form, contact your healthcare provider or authorized representative for assistance.
