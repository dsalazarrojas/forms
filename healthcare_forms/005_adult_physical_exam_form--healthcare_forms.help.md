# Adult Physical Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Adult Physical Exam Form is designed to collect vital signs, physical appearance, and examination findings for adult patients. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to conduct thorough physical examinations and document their findings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and examination date.
2.  Record the patient's vital signs, including blood pressure, heart rate, body temperature, and respiratory rate.
3.  Measure and record the patient's height and weight.
4.  Document the patient's general appearance and any notable findings from the head and neck, chest and lungs, cardiovascular, abdominal, and neurological examinations.
5.  Provide a final impression and recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Examination Date**: Enter the date when the physical exam is conducted.
* **Examining Physician**: Enter the name of the physician conducting the exam.
* **Blood Pressure**: Record the patient's systolic and diastolic blood pressure measurements.
* **Heart Rate**: Record the patient's heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Temperature**: Record the patient's body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Respiratory Rate**: Record the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Height**: Record the patient's height in inches.
* **Weight**: Record the patient's weight in pounds.
* **General Appearance Notes**: Document any notable observations about the patient's general appearance.
* **Head and Neck Exam**: Document findings from the head and neck examination.
* **Chest and Lungs Exam**: Document findings from the chest and lungs examination.
* **Cardiovascular Exam**: Document findings from the cardiovascular examination.
* **Abdomen Exam**: Document findings from the abdominal examination.
* **Neurological Exam**: Document findings from the neurological examination.
* **Final Impression**: Provide an overall assessment and recommendations for the patient's care.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled out. If a field is not required, it is optional and can be skipped.

## Tips

* Make sure to record measurements in the correct units (e.g., blood pressure: mmHg, heart rate: beats per minute, temperature: F).
* Be accurate and thorough when documenting examination findings.
* Use standardized language and formatting for consistency.
* Review and verify your entries before submitting the form.
