# Pre Appointment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Appointment Declaration Form is a mandatory form that patients must complete before their appointment with a healthcare provider. This form is designed to gather essential information about the patient, including their name, contact details, and appointment specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "patient_name" field. This is the name of the person who will be attending the appointment.
2. Enter your phone number in the "patient_phone" field. This will help us contact you if we need to discuss any additional information or schedule changes.
3. Fill in the doctor's name in the "doctor_name" field. If you don't know the doctor's name, leave this field blank.
4. Enter the doctor's phone number in the "doctor_phone" field, if known.
5. Choose the date of your appointment in the "appointment_date" field. Use the date format "YYYY-MM-DD".
6. Select the time of your appointment in the "appointment_time" field. Use the time format "HH:MM".
7. Choose the doctor's department from the list provided in the "doctor_department" field. This will help us direct you to the right healthcare provider.
8. If you have any symptoms or concerns that you'd like to share with the doctor, please write them down in the "patient_symptoms" field.
