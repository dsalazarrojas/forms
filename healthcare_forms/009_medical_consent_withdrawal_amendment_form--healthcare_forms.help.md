# prophets_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "prophets_form" is a healthcare registration form used to collect patient contact information for documentation and record-keeping purposes. It appears to be an optional form, allowing users to provide their details if they wish.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide your patient's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text)** (`patient_name`, **required: false**): Enter your patient's full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Email (email)** (`patient_email`, **required: false**): Enter your patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (text)** (`patient_phone`, **required: false**): Enter your patient's phone number for contact purposes.
