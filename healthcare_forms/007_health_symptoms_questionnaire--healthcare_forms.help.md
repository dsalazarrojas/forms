# Health Symptoms Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on various symptoms experienced by patients, helping healthcare professionals to better understand their conditions and provide accurate diagnoses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form categories and sections, which include General, Cardiovascular, Neurological, Musculoskeletal, Respiratory, Genito-Urinary, and Abdominal symptoms.
2. Select the category that best matches your symptoms.
3. For each selected category, type a brief description of the symptom(s) you are experiencing.
4. Provide detailed information as needed for each symptom.
5. Once you have completed all sections, review your answers to ensure you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Symptoms**: A place to describe any general symptoms you are experiencing, such as fatigue, fever, or pain.
* **Cardiovascular Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms related to your heart, such as chest pain, irregular heartbeat, or shortness of breath.
* **Neurological Symptoms**: Report any symptoms affecting your nervous system, including headaches, numbness, or seizures.
* **Musculoskeletal Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms affecting your muscles, bones, or joints, like muscle cramps, joint pain, or limited mobility.
* **Respiratory Symptoms**: Document any breathing difficulties, shortness of breath, or other respiratory issues.
* **Genito-Urinary Symptoms**: Describe any genitourinary symptoms, such as urinary tract infections, kidney stones, or menstrual cramps.
* **Abdominal Symptoms**: Report any gastrointestinal issues, including abdominal pain, nausea, or constipation.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when describing your symptoms.
* If you are unsure about a particular symptom, try to provide more context or details to help healthcare professionals better understand your condition.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the contact provided for further assistance.
