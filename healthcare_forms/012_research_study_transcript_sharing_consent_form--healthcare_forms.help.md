# research_study_transcript_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from participants to share their research study transcripts. The purpose of this form is to ensure that participants understand how their transcripts will be used, stored, and shared. It's crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure that all necessary permissions are obtained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Select the applicable category that best describes the research study.
3. Provide a brief description of the research study, explaining its purpose and scope.
4. Choose the tools that will be used to store and share the transcript.
5. Enter the title of the research study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that best describes the research study. This will help in organizing and categorizing the transcript.
	+ Choose the option that best matches the nature of the research study.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the research study, explaining its purpose and scope.
	+ Keep the description concise and focused on the key aspects of the study.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the tools that will be used to store and share the transcript.
	+ Select all the tools that apply to the research study.
* **Title** (`Title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the research study.
	+ Make sure the title accurately reflects the study's purpose and scope.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with the research study's principal investigator or the data manager.
