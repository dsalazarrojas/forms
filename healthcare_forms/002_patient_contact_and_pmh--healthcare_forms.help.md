# patient_contact_and_pmh - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to collect patient information, track medical history, and store contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Provide Patient Information**
	* Fill in the "Patient Info" field with the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact details.
2. **Track Medical History**
	* In the "Medical History" field, provide any significant medical events or conditions that the patient has experienced.
3. **Enter Physical Rehabilitation Information**
	* In the "Physical Rehab Info" field, provide any relevant information about the patient's physical rehabilitation process.
4. **Enter Contact Information**
	* In the "Contact Info" field, provide the patient's contact details, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact details.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): Enter any significant medical events or conditions that the patient has experienced.

### Physical Rehab Info
* **Physical Rehab Info** (`physical_rehab_info`, text, not required): Enter any relevant information about the patient's physical rehabilitation process.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter the patient's contact details, including phone number and email.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the healthcare team for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, and all data collected will be stored securely and confidentially.
