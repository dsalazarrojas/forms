# Medication Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and record information about a patient's medication regimen.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the patient's birthday using the calendar provided in the "Patient Birthday" field.
3. Enter the name of the medication the patient is taking in the "Medication Name" field.
4. Enter the dose of the medication in the "Dose" field.
5. Enter the frequency of the medication in the "Frequency" field.
6. Select the start date of the medication using the calendar provided in the "Medication Start Date" field.
7. Select the end date of the medication using the calendar provided in the "Medication End Date" field.
8. Enter the refill frequency of the medication in the "Refill Frequency" field.
9. Select the status of the medication (Active, Inactive, or Discontinued) in the "Medication Status" field.
10. Enter any specific instructions for the medication dosage in the "Medication Dose Instructions" field.
11. Enter the medical condition the medication is treating in the "Medical Condition" field.
12. Enter the name of the prescribing doctor in the "Prescribing Doctor Name" field.
12. Enter any known side effects of the medication in the "Medication Side Effects" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false)
The patient's name is a required field for tracking the patient's identity.

### **Patient Birthday** (`patient_birthday`, date, required: false)
Enter the patient's birthday using the calendar provided.

### **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the medication the patient is taking.

### **Dose** (`dose`, text, required: false)
Enter the dose of the medication.

### **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, required: false)
Enter the frequency of the medication.

### **Medication Start Date** (`medication_start_date`, date, required: false)
Select the start date of the medication using the calendar provided.

### **Medication End Date** (`medication_end_date`, date, required: false)
Select the end date of the medication using the calendar provided.

### **Refill Frequency** (`refill_frequency`, text, required: false)
Enter the frequency at which the medication needs to be refilled.

### **Medication Status** (`medication_status`, select_one, required: false)
Select the status of the medication (Active, Inactive, or Discontinued).

### **Medication Dose Instructions** (`medication_dose_instructions`, text, required: false)
Enter any specific instructions for the medication dosage.

### **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false)
Enter the medical condition the medication is treating.

### **Prescribing Doctor Name** (`prescribing_doctor_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the doctor who prescribed the medication.

### **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, required: false)
Enter any known side effects of the medication.
