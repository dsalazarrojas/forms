# mental_health_service_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to assess a patient's mental health service needs. It will help healthcare providers evaluate and provide the necessary information to refer patients to mental health services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Mental Health Service Goal" field and enter the primary reason for seeking mental health services.
2. Choose the "Mental Health Service Referral Source" field and select the source of the referral (e.g., Primary care physician, Community health worker, etc.).
3. Select the "Mental Health Service Goal Frequency" field and choose how often the patient would like to receive services (e.g., Once, Ongoing, etc.).
4. Enter the "Mental Health Service Referral Source Others" field if the referral source is not listed.
5. Enter the "Mental Health Service Goal Frequency Others" field if the patient's desired frequency is not listed.
6. Enter the "Mental Health Service Provider" field, which is the name of the mental health service provider.
7. Enter the "Mental Health Service Provider Others" field if the provider is not listed.
8. Enter the "Mental Health Service Agreement" field if you have an agreement with the service provider.
9. Enter the "Mental Health Service Agreement Signed By" field, which is the signature of the agreement.
10. Enter the "Mental Health Service Agreement Signed On" field, which is the date the agreement was signed.
11. Enter the "Mental Health Service Agreement Signed At" field, which is the time the agreement was signed.
11. Enter the "Mental Health Service Location" field, which is the location where the services will be provided (e.g., Inpatient, Outpatient, etc.).
12. Enter the "Mental Health Service Date" field, which is the date the services will start.
13. Enter the "Mental Health Service Time" field, which is the time the services will start.
14. Enter the "Mental Health Service Provider Other Details" field if you have additional information about the provider.
15. Enter the "Mental Health Service Address" field, which is the address of the service provider.
15. Enter the "Mental Health Service Email" field, which is the email of the service provider.
16. Enter the "Mental Health Service Phone" field, which is the phone number of the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Service Assessment**: Enter the reason why you are seeking mental health services.
* **Mental Health Service Goal**: The primary reason for seeking mental health services.
* **Mental Health Service Referral Source**: Select the source of the referral (e.g., Primary care physician, Community health worker, etc.).
* **Mental Health Service Referral Source Others**: If the referral source is not listed, enter the name of the source.
* **Mental Health Service Goal Frequency**: Select how often you would like to receive services (e.g., Once, Ongoing, etc.).
* **Mental Health Service Goal Frequency Others**: If the desired frequency is not listed, enter it.
* **Mental Health Service Referral Source Other Details**: Enter any additional details about the referral source.
* **Mental Health Service Type**: Select the type of mental health service (e.g., Inpatient, Outpatient, Online, etc.).
* **Mental Health Service Location**: Select the location where the services will be provided (e.g., Inpatient, Outpatient, etc.).
* **Mental Health Service Date**: Enter the date the services will start.
* **Mental Health Service Time**: Enter the time the services will start.
* **Mental Health Service Note**: Enter any notes or comments about the services.
* **Mental Health Service Email**: Enter the email of the service provider.
* **Mental Health Service Phone**: Enter the phone number of the service provider.
* **Mental Health Service Address**: Enter the address of the service provider.
* **Mental Health Service Provider**: Enter the name of the mental health service provider.
* **Mental Health Service Provider Others**: If the provider is not listed, enter their name.
* **Mental Health Service Provider Other Details**: Enter any additional details about the provider.
* **Mental Health Service Agreement**: Enter the text of the agreement.
* **Mental Health Service Agreement Signed By**: Enter the name of who signed the agreement.
* **Mental Health Service Agreement Signed On**: Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Mental Health Service Agreement Signed At**: Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Mental Health Service Agreement Signed By Details**: Enter any additional details about who signed the agreement.
