# Contrast Agent Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather informed consent from patients before administering contrast agents during medical procedures. It collects information about the patient's health history, medical conditions, and consent for the administration of contrast agents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full legal name and patient ID number on page 1.
2. Provide your date of birth and select your gender on page 1.
3. Select the type of procedure (e.g., CT scan, MRI, X-ray, or ultrasound) on page 2.
4. Choose the type of contrast agent to be used on page 2.
5. Answer questions regarding your medical history, including kidney disease, diabetes, high blood pressure, and asthma on page 3.
6. List any known allergies, including medications you are allergic to, on page 4.
7. List your current medications and dosages on page 4.
8. Answer questions about your medical history and consent on page 5.
9. Sign and date the form on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (page 1): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Patient ID Number** (page 1): Provide your patient ID number, if available.
* **Date of Birth** (page 1): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (page 1): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Procedure Information** (page 2): This section provides a brief overview of the procedure and the contrast agent used.
* **Type of Procedure** (page 2): Select the type of imaging procedure you will undergo (e.g., CT scan, MRI, X-ray, or ultrasound).
* **Contrast Agent Type** (page 2): Choose the type of contrast agent to be used (e.g., Iodine-Based, Gadolinium-Based, or Barium-Based).
* **Scheduled Procedure Date** (page 2): Enter the date of the scheduled procedure in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical History** (page 3): Answer questions regarding your kidney disease, diabetes, high blood pressure, and asthma.
* **Do You Have Kidney Disease or Problems** (page 3): Select 'True' if you have kidney disease or problems.
* **Do You Have Diabetes** (page 3): Select 'True' if you have diabetes.
* **Do You Have High Blood Pressure** (page 3): Select 'True' if you have high blood pressure.
* **Do You Have Asthma or Allergies** (page 3): Select 'True' if you have asthma or allergies.
* **Do You Have a Previous Contrast Reaction** (page 3): Select 'True' if you have had a reaction to contrast in the past.
* **Describe the Previous Reaction** (page 3): Provide details about your previous reaction to contrast.
* **Allergies** (page 4): List any known allergies, including medications you are allergic to.
* **Do You Have Any Medication Allergies** (page 4): Select 'True' if you have allergies to medications.
* **List Your Allergies** (page 4): Describe your known allergies.
* **Current Medications** (page 4): List your current medications and dosages.
* **Medication Allergies** (page 4): Select 'True' if you have allergies to medications.
* **Are You Taking Metformin for Diabetes** (page 4): Select 'True' if you are taking metformin for diabetes.
* **Risks and Benefits** (page 5): This section provides an overview of the potential risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
* **Have the Risks Been Explained to You** (page 5): Select 'True' if the risks have been explained to you.
* **Have the Benefits Been Explained to You** (page 5): Select 'True' if the benefits have been explained to you.
* **Have Alternative Options Been Explained** (page 5): Select 'True' if alternative options have been explained to you.
* **Consent** (page 6): Sign and date the form to indicate your consent to the administration of contrast agents.
* **Signature Date** (page 6): Enter the date of your signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Witness Name** (page 6): Enter the name of the healthcare provider witness.
* **Witness Signature Date** (page 6): Enter the date of the witness's signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Additional Notes or Concerns** (page 6): Provide any additional information or concerns.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
