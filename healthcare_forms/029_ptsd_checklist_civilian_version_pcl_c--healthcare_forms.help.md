# ptsd_checklist_civilian_version_pcl_c - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ptsd_checklist_civilian_version_pcl_c form is designed to assess the presence and severity of symptoms related to Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) in civilians.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that this form is not a comprehensive diagnostic tool and should not be used as a substitute for a thorough medical evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional.
3. The questions are intended to be self-explanatory, so take your time to answer them carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traumatic events** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This question is designed to understand the type of traumatic events you may have experienced. Answer this question truthfully, even if the events seem insignificant or unrelated to your current situation.
* **Physical symptoms** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please describe any physical symptoms you are experiencing, such as headaches, nausea, or fatigue.
* **Anxiety symptoms** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any anxiety-related symptoms you are experiencing, such as racing thoughts, difficulty concentrating, or feeling on edge.
* **Sleep disturbances** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Report any sleep-related issues you are experiencing, such as insomnia, nightmares, or excessive daytime sleepiness.
* **Anger aggression** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any anger or aggressive feelings you are experiencing, such as irritability, aggression, or difficulty controlling your emotions.
* **Guilt/sense of guilt** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Report any feelings of guilt or a sense of regret that you are experiencing.
* **Hypervigilance** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any situations or stimuli that trigger a heightened state of alertness or vigilance in you.
* **Emotional reactions** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): This question is designed to understand any strong emotional reactions you may be experiencing, such as feelings of fear, anger, or sadness.
* **Dissociation** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any feelings of detachment or disconnection from your surroundings or self.
* **Nightmares** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Report any nightmares or recurring disturbing dreams you are experiencing.

## Tips

* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Please do not skip any questions, as this form is designed to gather comprehensive information.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the form administrator at `form@gic.mx` for guidance.
