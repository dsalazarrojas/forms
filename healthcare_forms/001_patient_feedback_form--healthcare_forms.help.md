# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences at the clinic. The information gathered will help us understand our patients' opinions and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with accurate and honest information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **How would you rate your overall experience at the clinic?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one of the options: 1 - Very Satisfied, 2 - Somewhat Satisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat Dissatisfied, 5 - Very Dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate your doctor's communication skills?** (`question2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one of the options: 1 - Excellent, 2 - Good, 3 - Fair, 4 - Poor.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the clinic?** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select one or more of the options: 1 - Very Clean, 2 - Somewhat Clean, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat Dirty, 5 - Very Dirty.
* **Would you recommend this clinic to a friend or family member?** (`question4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select one or more of the options: True, False.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience at the clinic.
* **When did you visit the clinic?** (`question5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What time did you visit the clinic?** (`question6`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **What is your age?** (`question7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age in years.
* **What is your gender?** (`question8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one of the options: Male, Female, Other.
