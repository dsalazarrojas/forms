# Health Coach Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their health goals, medical history, and other relevant details to help health coaches understand the client's needs and provide personalized guidance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (1): Enter your basic contact information, including name and date of birth.
* **Health Goals** (2): Describe your current health goals, including what you hope to achieve through the coaching process.
* **Medical History** (3): Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any significant events, conditions, or surgeries.
* **Medication List** (4): Select all medications you are currently taking, including vitamins and supplements.
* **Medical Conditions** (5): Select the medical conditions that apply to you, if any.
* **Medical Procedures**: (6): Note any medical procedures you have had in the past or are currently undergoing.
* **Emergency Contact** (7): Provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Professionals** (8): Select the names of any medical professionals you currently see, such as doctors or dentists.
* **Allergies** (9): Select any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or environmental allergies.
* **Medical Questions** (10): If you have any questions or concerns about your health, list them here.
* **Emergency Info** (11): Provide any relevant emergency information, such as a list of emergency contact numbers or locations of nearby hospitals.
* **Signature** (12): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood each section.
* **Phone** (13): Provide your contact information, including phone number and any other relevant contact details.
