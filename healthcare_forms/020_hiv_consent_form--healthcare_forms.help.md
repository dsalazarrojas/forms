# Hiv Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain patient consent for an HIV test, allowing healthcare professionals to collect essential information and ensure that patients understand the testing process. It is a crucial document that outlines the patient's rights, responsibilities, and expectations before undergoing the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and instructions provided.
2. Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
3. Select the consent option that applies to you.
4. Choose the testing result (if applicable).
5. Sign the form to indicate your agreement and understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (page 1, text): This field asks for your personal details such as name, date of birth, and contact information. Please provide accurate information to ensure that we can contact you with your test results.
* **Medical History** (page 1, text): Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
* **Testing Info** (page 1, text): In this field, please provide any additional information about your medical history, symptoms, or concerns related to your HIV test.
* **Consent** (page 2, select multiple): This is where you indicate your consent to have the HIV test. Please select one of the two options:
	+ "I consent to have the HIV test."
	+ "I decline the HIV test."
* **Testing Results** (page 2, select one): If you've had the test, select one of the three options for your test result:
	+ "Normal"
	+ "Abnormal"
	+ "Inconclusive"
* **Doctor Signature** (page 3, note): This field is for the doctor to sign and date, indicating that they have read and discussed the test results with you.
* **Patient Signature** (page 3, note): This field is for you to sign, indicating that you have read and understood the test results and consented to the test.
* **Date** (page 3, date): Please provide the date you signed the form.
* **Time** (page 3, time): Please provide the time you signed the form.
* **Note** (page 3, note): If you have any further comments or additional information, please enter it here.

## Tips
Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your healthcare provider.
