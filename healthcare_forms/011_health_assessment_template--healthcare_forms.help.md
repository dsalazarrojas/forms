# Health Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Assessment Template is a form used to gather information about a patient's medical history, current symptoms, and treatment plan. This form is intended to be completed by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's health status and identify potential areas of concern. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
* Select the correct options for each field based on the patient's medical history, current symptoms, and treatment plan.
* For each field, select the most accurate option or enter the relevant information.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical history**: Enter the patient's medical history in the space provided.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies the patient has, such as medications or conditions.
* **Current Symptoms**: Select the current symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as pain, fatigue, or nausea.
* **Medical History Conditions**: Select all conditions the patient has, such as hypertension, diabetes, or cancer.
* **Family History**: Select all family members with a history of medical conditions.
* **Medications**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Social History**: Select all social factors that may impact the patient's health.
* **Medical Tests**: Select all medical tests the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Procedures**: Select all medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Prognosis**: Enter the patient's medical prognosis in the space provided.
* **Medical Procedure Status**: Select the status of the medical procedure, such as not started, completed, or pending.
* **Patient Status**: Select the current status of the patient, such as active, resuscitated, or deceased.
* **Patient Status Other**: If the patient's status is not listed, enter the correct status in the text field.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter the patient's treatment plan in the space provided.
* **Treatment Plan Status**: Select the status of the treatment plan, such as not started, completed, or pending.
* **Patient Outcome**: Select the outcome of the treatment, such as not completed, completed, or pending.
* **Date of Last Contact**: Enter the date and time of the last contact with the patient.
* **Date of Last Medication**: Enter the date and time of the last medication.
* **Date of Next Contact**: Enter the date and time of the next contact with the patient.
* **Date of Last Follow-up**: Enter the date and time of the last follow-up.
* **Date of Next Follow-up**: Enter the date and time of the next follow-up.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the patient's health, please consult with a healthcare professional.
