# Holistic Therapies Covid 19 Pre Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Therapies Covid 19 Pre Consultation Form is designed to collect information from patients interested in pursuing holistic therapies, particularly for COVID-19. This form helps the medical center understand the patient's needs and preferences, enabling them to provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your surname in the "surname" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select the date of your visit in the "date" field.
6. Select the time of your visit in the "time" field.
7. Enter your address in the "address" field.
8. Specify the medical center you are interested in visiting in the "medical_center" field.
9. Indicate your interest in the "interested" field by selecting COVID-19 Vaccine, Booster Dose, or Other.
10. If you are interested in something else, please specify in the "other" field.
11. Select the method you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, SMS, or Other) in the "contact_method" field.
12. Select your preferred language (English, French, or Spanish) in the "preferred_language" field.
13. If you have any follow-up requirements, specify the date in the "follow_up" field.
14. Leave any additional comments in the "comments" field.
15. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "phone_number" field.
16. If you have an alternative email address, enter it in the "email_address" field.
17. Select any medical specialties you are interested in (Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, or Other) in the "medical_specialties" field.
18. If you have other medical specialties in mind, specify them in the "other_specialties" field.
19. If you are available at a specific time, select it in the "available_at" field.
20. If you are available to be contacted on a specific date, select it in the "available_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **surname**: Enter your surname.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **date**: Select the date of your visit.
* **time**: Select the time of your visit.
* **address**: Enter your address.
* **medical_center**: Specify the medical center you are interested in visiting.
* **interested**: Indicate your interest in COVID-19 Vaccine, Booster Dose, or Other.
* **other**: If you are interested in something else, please specify.
* **contact_method**: Select your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, SMS, or Other).
* **preferred_language**: Select your preferred language (English, French, or Spanish).
* **follow_up**: If you have any follow-up requirements, specify the date.
* **comments**: Leave any additional comments.
* **phone_number**: If you have a phone number, enter it.
* **email_address**: If you have an alternative email address, enter it.
* **medical_specialties**: Select any medical specialties you are interested in (Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, or Other).
* **other_specialties**: If you have other medical specialties in mind, specify them.
* **available_at**: If you are available at a specific time, select it.
* **available_to**: If you are available to be contacted on a specific date, select it.

This form will help the medical center provide you with personalized services and better meet your needs. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
