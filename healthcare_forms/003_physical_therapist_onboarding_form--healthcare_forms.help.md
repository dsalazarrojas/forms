# Physical Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to onboard physical therapists to a healthcare system, capturing their clinic and personal details to enable seamless integration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your clinic name.
2. Enter your name as a physical therapist.
3. Select your preferred schedule type (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Choose your available schedule time.
5. Select your credential type (PT License, Physical Therapist, Doctor, or Other).
6. Indicate the status of your credential verification (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
7. Select your schedule frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Biweekly).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cinic Name**: Enter the name of your clinic where you will be working.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter your name as a physical therapist.
* **Schedule Type**: Select the time of day you are available to work (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Schedule Time**: Enter the specific time you are available to work.
* **Credential Type**: Select your professional credential type (PT License, Physical Therapist, Doctor, or Other).
* **Credential Verification**: Indicate the status of your credential verification (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
* **Schedule Frequency**: Select how often you are available to work (Daily, Weekly, or Biweekly).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
