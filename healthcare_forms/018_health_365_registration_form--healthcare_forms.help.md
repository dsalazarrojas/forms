# Health 365 Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health 365 Registration Form is a basic registration form designed to collect essential information from patients. It contains the following sections to gather details about the patient, medical conditions, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding text fields.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
3. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact verification.
5. If applicable, specify your **Medical Condition** from the dropdown list provided.
6. Enter any relevant **Medication** information in the text field.
7. List any **Allergies** you may have.
8. Indicate whether you have **Medical Insurance**, if so. If not, select "False" from the dropdown list.
9. Enter any **Doctor Referral** information, if applicable.
10. Provide a brief summary of your **Medical History** in the text field.
11. Enter the **Emergency Contact** information in case of emergencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for verification.
* **Medical Condition**: Select a medical condition that best describes your condition from the options provided.
* **Medication**: Enter any relevant medication information, if applicable.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether you have medical insurance or not. Select "True" if you have insurance, "False" otherwise.
* **Doctor Referral**: Enter any information provided by your doctor, if applicable.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of your emergency contact.
