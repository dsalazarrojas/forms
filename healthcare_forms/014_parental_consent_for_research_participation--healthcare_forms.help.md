## Parental Consent For Research Participation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the parent's details, the child's details, and the parent's consent to participate in research studies. It helps researchers to verify the identity of the parent and ensure that the child has given their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Parental Consent Form**: This page is where you will be asked to provide your consent for your child's participation in research studies. Please read the form carefully and fill in all the required fields.
2.  **Name of Parent**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification document (e.g., driver's license, passport).
3.  **Relationship to Child**: Describe your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
4.  **Consent to Participate**: Select one of the options to indicate your consent for your child's participation in research studies:
    *   Option 1: I grant my child's consent for participation in research studies.
    *   Option 2: I do not grant my child's consent for participation in research studies.
    *   Option 3: I need more information about the research study before making a decision.
5.  **Name of Child**: Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification document (e.g., birth certificate, passport).
6.  **Date of Birth of Child**: Enter your child's date of birth.
7.  **School Name**: If your child is currently in school, enter the name of their school.
8.  **Email of Parent**: Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for any updates or further information).
9.  **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for any updates or further information).
10. **Address**: Enter your address (physical or mailing).
11.  **Additional Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional information that you believe is relevant to your child's participation in research studies.
12.  **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher or institution conducting the research study.
13.  **Researcher Institution**: Enter the name of the institution conducting the research study.
