# COVID 19 Vaccine Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from patients to facilitate the COVID 19 vaccination process. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure you can receive the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in your personal details in sections marked -- Patient Information --.
2.  Provide your medical history and vaccination history in sections marked -- Vaccination History -- and -- Health Status --.
3.  Confirm your eligibility for priority vaccination in the section marked -- Eligibility Information --.
4.  Ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`reg_first_name`, required): Please enter your first name as you would like it to appear on your vaccination records.
*   **Last Name** (`reg_last_name`, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on your vaccination records.
*   **Middle Initial** (`reg_middle_initial`, optional): If you have a middle name, please enter your middle initial. If not, leave this field empty.
*   **Date of Birth** (`reg_date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-01-1999).
*   **Gender** (`reg_gender`, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
*   **Email Address** (`reg_email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`reg_phone`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Street Address** (`reg_address`, required): Enter your street address for record purposes.
*   **City** (`reg_city`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State** (`reg_state`, required): Enter the state where you reside.
*   **ZIP Code** (`reg_zip_code`, required): Enter your ZIP code for mailing purposes.
*   **Number of COVID-19 Vaccine Doses** (`reg_previous_doses`, required): Report how many doses of the vaccine you have received so far.
*   **Vaccine Types Received** (`reg_vaccine_type_received`, required): Select the type(s) of vaccine you have received.
*   **Date of Most Recent Dose** (`reg_last_dose_date`, optional): If you have received the vaccine, enter the date of your last dose.
*   **Age Group** (`reg_age_eligibility`, required): Select your age category.
*   **Do You Qualify for Priority Vaccination** (`reg_priority_eligibility`, required): Select if you qualify for priority vaccination based on the options provided.
*   **Underlying Medical Condition** (`reg_medical_condition`, optional): If you have an underlying medical condition, describe it in this field.
*   **Current Symptoms** (`reg_current_symptoms`, required): Report if you are currently experiencing COVID symptoms.
*   **Previous COVID-19 Infection** (`reg_had_covid`, required): Report if you have had COVID-19 before.
*   **I Confirm All Information is Accurate** (`reg_confirmation`, required): Confirm that all provided information is correct before proceeding.
*   **Registration Date** (`reg_registration_date`, required): This field will be automatically populated with the current date and time.
