# Hospice Care Patient Demographics Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient demographics in a hospice care setting, gathering essential information for accurate documentation and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that you have permission from the patient or their representative to collect and process their personal and medical data.
- Review each field explanation below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional)
Please fill out the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and any other details relevant to their identification.

### 2. **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional)
Fill out the patient's medical history, including past diagnoses, current conditions, and any ongoing treatments.

### 3. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
Provide contact details for the patient, including phone numbers and email addresses.

### 4. **Address Info** (`address_info`, text, optional)
Fill out the patient's address and any other relevant contact details.

### 5. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)
Specify the emergency contact's name and contact information.

### 6. **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional)
List the patient's medical insurance details, including the provider and policy number.

### 7. **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional)
Record the payment information, including billing and insurance details.

### 8. **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, optional)
Fill out any other relevant information about the patient that may be important for their care.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- Use this form as a sample to create a customized version for your specific use case.
- Adapt this form according to your organization's needs and ensure it complies with relevant laws and regulations.
