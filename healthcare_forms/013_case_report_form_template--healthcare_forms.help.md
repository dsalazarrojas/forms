## Case Report Form Template - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Case Report Form Template is a standardized form used to collect and document patient information, medical history, clinical presentation, diagnostic findings, and treatment outcomes in a systematic and comprehensive manner. This template is designed to facilitate accurate and efficient data collection, ensuring that healthcare providers have a clear and concise understanding of each patient's case.

### How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the relevant sections that apply to the patient's case.
2.  Fill out the required fields in each section, using the provided fields as a guide.
3.  For fields with dropdown options, select the most accurate description that reflects the patient's condition.
4.  Be sure to include all relevant information for each field, and avoid leaving any sections blank unless explicitly marked as optional.
5.  Once completed, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

**Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics_section`): This section collects basic patient information, including medical record number, which is essential for tracking and referencing patient data.

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Age (years)** (`age_years`): Calculate the patient's age in years from their date of birth.
*   **Sex** (`sex`): Select the patient's sex as male, female, or other.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): Enter the patient's unique medical record number.

**Clinical Presentation** (`clinical_presentation_section`): This section outlines the patient's chief complaint, symptoms, and their duration.

*   **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`): Describe the primary reason for the patient's visit or admission.
*   **Date of Symptom Onset** (`date_of_onset`): Enter the date the symptoms began in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Presenting Symptoms** (`presenting_symptoms`): Select all symptoms that apply to the patient's condition.
*   **Duration of Symptoms** (`symptom_duration`): Describe the duration of the symptoms.
*   **Symptom Severity** (`severity_scale`): Assess the severity of the symptoms as mild, moderate, severe, or critical.

**Past Medical History** (`past_medical_history_section`): This section collects information on the patient's medical history.

*   **Significant Past Medical History** (`significant_medical_history`): List any relevant medical conditions or history.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`): List all medications the patient is currently taking.
*   **Known Allergies or Adverse Reactions** (`allergies`): Document any known allergies or adverse reactions.

**Diagnostic Findings** (`diagnostic_findings_section`): This section outlines the results of any lab tests, imaging studies, or physical examinations.

*   **Physical Examination Findings** (`physical_examination_findings`): Describe any relevant clinical findings.
*   **Laboratory Test Results** (`laboratory_tests`): List key lab findings with values.
*   **Imaging Studies Performed** (`imaging_studies`): Describe any imaging studies performed.

**Diagnosis and Treatment** (`diagnosis_section`): This section documents the final diagnosis or case description and any treatments provided.

*   **Final Diagnosis or Case Description** (`diagnosis`): Describe the final diagnosis or case outcome.
*   **Treatment Provided** (`treatment_provided`): Document any treatments or interventions administered.
*   **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`): Select the patient's outcome as recovered fully, improved, stable, worsened, deceased, or unknown.
