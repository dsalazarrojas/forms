# CAHPS Cancer Care Surgery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CAHPS Cancer Care Surgery Survey is a patient feedback form designed to measure the quality of care provided during a cancer surgery experience. The survey aims to gather information about the patient's experience with the care team, hospital facilities, and overall satisfaction with the surgery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide detailed answers in a respectful and professional manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name of the Surgeon who performed your operation**
   **Name of the Surgeon who performed your operation**: This field is a required text field where you should enter the name of the surgeon who performed your surgery.

### 2. **Hospital or Surgical Center Name**
   **Hospital or Surgical Center Name**: This field is also required and should be filled with the name of the hospital or surgical center where your surgery took place.

### 3. **Before surgery, did the care team explain what would happen during the operation?**
   **Before surgery, did the care team explain what would happen during the operation?**: This question has three options:
   - Yes, definitely: If the care team explained the procedure clearly before the surgery.
   - Yes, somewhat: If the care team provided some explanation but not thoroughly.
   - 'False': If the care team did not provide any explanation before the surgery.

### 4. **Did you have the opportunity to ask all your questions before the surgery?**
   **Did you have the opportunity to ask all your questions before the surgery?**: This question has three options:
   - Yes, all questions answered: If you were able to ask all your questions before the surgery and received clear answers.
   - Some questions answered: If you were able to ask some questions but not all of them.
   - No opportunity given: If you were not given the chance to ask questions before the surgery.

### 5. **In the recovery room, how often did nurses explain things in a way you could understand?**
   **In the recovery room, how often did nurses explain things in a way you could understand?**: This question has four options:
   - Never: If the nurses did not explain anything in a way you could understand.
   - Sometimes: If the nurses sometimes explained in a way you could understand.
   - Usually: If the nurses usually explained in a way you could understand.
   - Always: If the nurses always explained in a way you could understand.

### 6. **How often was your pain well-controlled after surgery?**
   **How often was your pain well-controlled after surgery?**: This question has four options:
   - Never: If your pain was not well-controlled at all.
   - Sometimes: If your pain was occasionally not well-controlled.
   - Usually: If your pain was usually well-controlled.
   - Always: If your pain was always well-controlled.

### 7. **Before you left the facility, did you get clear instructions about what you could and could not do?**
   **Before you left the facility, did you get clear instructions about what you could and could not do?**: This question has three options:
   - Yes, definitely: If you received clear instructions before leaving the facility.
   - Yes, somewhat: If you received some clear instructions but not all of them.
   - 'False': If you did not receive any clear instructions.

### 8. **Did the care team keep your family or friends informed during your surgery?**
   **Did the care team keep your family or friends informed during your surgery?**: This question has four options:
   - Yes, definitely: If the care team kept your family or friends informed throughout the process.
   - Yes, somewhat: If the care team kept some family or friends informed.
   - 'False': If the care team did not keep any family or friends informed.
   - Not Applicable: If you did not have any family or friends to keep informed.

### 9. **How often did the surgeon treat you with courtesy and respect?**
   **How often did the surgeon treat you with courtesy and respect?**: This question has four options:
   - Never: If the surgeon did not treat you with courtesy and respect.
   - Sometimes: If the surgeon sometimes treated you with courtesy and respect.
   - Usually: If the surgeon usually treated you with courtesy and respect.
   - Always: If the surgeon always treated you with courtesy and respect.

### 10. **How well did the surgical team and the oncology team work together?**
   **How well did the surgical team and the oncology team work together?**: This question has five options:
   - Poor: If the teams worked very poorly together.
   - Fair: If the teams worked somewhat poorly together.
   - Good: If the teams worked fairly well together.
   - Very Good: If the teams worked very well together.
   - Excellent: If the teams worked perfectly together.

### 11. **Overall rating of your cancer surgery experience**
   **Overall rating of your cancer surgery experience**: This question has eleven options:
   - 0: Worst experience.
   - 1: Very poor experience.
   - 2: Poor experience.
   - 3: Fair experience.
   - 4: Good experience.
   - 5: Very good experience.
   - 6: Excellent experience.
   - 7: Very good experience.
   - 8: Good experience.
   - 9: Fair experience.
   - 10: Excellent experience.

### 12. **Was a follow-up visit scheduled before you left the facility?**
   **Was a follow-up visit scheduled before you left the facility?**: This question has two options:
   - 'True': If a follow-up visit was scheduled before you left.
   - 'False': If no follow-up visit was scheduled before you left.

### 13. **Please share any details about your recovery process**
   **Please share any details about your recovery process**: This field is a text field where you can provide any additional details about your recovery process.

### 14. **May we contact you for more details about your feedback?**
   **May we contact you for more details about your feedback?**: This question has two options:
   - 'True': If you are willing to be contacted for further discussion.
   - 'False': If you do not want to be contacted.
