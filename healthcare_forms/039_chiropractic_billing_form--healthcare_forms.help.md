# Chiropractic Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect billing information for chiropractic services, including patient name, billing address, insurance provider (if applicable), policy number, services rendered, total amount due, payment method, billing contact phone, and billing notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full legal name as the patient.
2. Enter your billing address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
3. If applicable, provide the name of your insurance provider.
4. Enter your insurance policy number or member ID (if applicable).
5. Describe the services rendered, including any treatments provided.
6. Enter the total amount due for the services.
7. Select the payment method (Credit Card, Insurance, Check, or Cash).
8. Provide your billing contact phone number for any inquiries.
9. Enter any special billing instructions or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter the street, city, state, and zip code of the billing address.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have an insurance policy, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your insurance policy number or member ID (if applicable).
* **Services Rendered** (`services_rendered`, text, required): Describe the services rendered, including any treatments provided.
* **Total Amount Due** (`total_amount_due`, number, required): Enter the total amount due for the services.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay for the services (Credit Card, Insurance, Check, or Cash).
* **Billing Contact Phone** (`billing_contact`, text, optional): Provide your billing contact phone number for any inquiries.
* **Billing Notes** (`billing_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special billing instructions or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your insurance provider and policy number if applicable.
- Ensure accurate total amount due to avoid any errors.
- Select a valid payment method.
- Keep your billing contact phone number up-to-date.
- Enter any special billing instructions or notes for clarity.
