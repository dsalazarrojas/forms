# Zoo Animal Health Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoo Animal Health Monitoring Form is designed to track the health status and care of animals in the zoo. This form is used to collect data on animal vital signs, health status, and veterinary notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the animal's **animal_name** and select its **animal_type** from the options provided.
2. Enter the **animal_status**, **animal_species**, and **animal_breed** of the animal.
3. Enter the animal's **animal_sex** and **animal_weight**, **animal_length**, and **animal_height** (if applicable).
4. Enter the **vital_signs_temperature** and **vital_signs_blood_pressure** (if applicable).
5. Enter the **health_status** of the animal, selecting from the options of Healthy, Sick, or Unknown.
6. Provide any additional **health_notes** about the animal.
7. Choose the **next_action** to take for the animal, selecting from Check-in again in 1 hour, Contact Zoo Director, or Contact VET.
8. If necessary, schedule a **followup_date** and **followup_time** for the next check-up.
9. Select the **followup_method** for the next check-up, choosing from Phone, Email, or In-person visit.
10. Enter any **veterinary_contact** information (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional **animal_diet** information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **animal_id**: A unique identifier for the animal. This field is required.
* **health_notes**: Any additional notes about the animal's health. This field is optional.
* **health_status**: The current health status of the animal (Healthy, Sick, or Unknown). This field is required.
* **vital_signs_temperature**: The animal's current body temperature. This field is optional.
* **vital_signs_blood_pressure**: The animal's current blood pressure. This field is optional.
* **vital_signs_respiratory_rate**: The animal's current respiratory rate. This field is optional.
* **veterinary_notes**: Any additional veterinary notes about the animal. This field is optional.
* **next_action**: The next step to take for the animal's care. This field is optional.
* **followup_date**: A schedule for the next follow-up check-up. This field is optional.
* **followup_time**: The time scheduled for the next follow-up check-up. This field is optional.
* **followup_method**: The method to use for the next follow-up check-up. This field is optional.
* **veterinary_contact**: Any additional veterinary contact information. This field is optional.
* **animal_name**: The name of the animal. This field is required.
* **animal_type**: The type of animal (Mammals, Reptiles, or Birds). This field is required.
* **enclosure_number**: The enclosure number for the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_status**: The current status of the animal (Active, Retired, or In transit). This field is optional.
* **animal_species**: The species of the animal. This field is required.
* **animal_breed**: The breed of the animal. This field is optional.
* **animal_sex**: The sex of the animal (Male or Female). This field is required.
* **animal_weight**: The weight of the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_length**: The length of the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_height**: The height of the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_color**: The color of the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_diet**: The diet of the animal (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **animal_housing_location**: The location where the animal is housed. This field is required.
* **veterinary_contact**: Any additional veterinary contact information. This field is optional.
