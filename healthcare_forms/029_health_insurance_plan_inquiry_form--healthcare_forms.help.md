# Health Insurance Plan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from new customers inquiring about health insurance plans. Please complete the form accurately to ensure that the insurance agency or representative can best assist with your plan needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to begin filling out the form
- Select the relevant options for each field as applicable
- Fill out as much of the form as possible before proceeding to the next page
- Submit the completed form to initiate contact from the insurance agency or representative

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: Enter your general information as requested, which may include any relevant details about your current situation.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Coverage Details**: Provide details about your coverage needs or preferences.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Premium Rates**: Enter any information regarding premium rates or costs associated with your potential plan.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details to facilitate communication with the insurance agency or representative.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Health Insurance Type**: Select the type of health insurance plan you are interested in (Individual, Family, or Group).
  - **Type:** Select one option
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Age**: Enter your age to help determine plan options.
  - **Type:** Number field
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Coverage Start Date**: Select or enter the desired start date for your coverage.
  - **Type:** Date field
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Coverage End Date**: Select or enter the desired end date for your coverage.
  - **Type:** Date field
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Premium Frequency**: Choose how often you want to pay your premium (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
  - **Type:** Select one option
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Premium Amount**: Enter your expected premium amount.
  - **Type:** Number field
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Name**: Enter your name.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  - **Type:** Email field (e.g., @ symbol required)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Message**: Briefly describe your message or any questions you have.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional
- **Contact Agreement**: Indicate whether you accept the terms of service.
  - **Type:** Select one option
  - **Required:** Optional
  - **Note:** Please check the box to confirm your agreement.
  - **Hint:** (Please see contact_agreement_hint field for further clarification)
- **Contact Agreement Hint**: This field should be used to clarify any questions or concerns regarding the terms of service. If you do not understand or have questions, please let us know.
  - **Type:** Text field (e.g., free form)
  - **Required:** Optional

Note: The required status of each field is indicated as "Optional" because it is not explicitly required, but it is implied that users should fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate and efficient handling of their inquiry.
