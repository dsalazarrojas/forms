This form is designed to collect information about incidents of substance abuse, which can be critical in providing timely and effective support to patients struggling with addiction. ## Purpose

The Substance Abuse Incident Form is a crucial tool in the management and reporting of incidents related to substance abuse. Its primary purpose is to collect and document information about incidents, including the patient's details, incident type, and any related treatment facilities or investigations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a clear and legible manner, using the provided fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Provide the patient's name and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the correct incident type from the available options.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant circumstances or events leading to the incident.
6. Provide the name and address of the treatment facility (if applicable).
7. Select the incident status and enter any relevant notes.
8. Provide follow-up information, including the follow-up date, time, and method (if applicable).
9. Enter the follow-up notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident from the available options (Overdose, Non-overdose, Other).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the incident.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant circumstances or events leading to the incident.
* **Treatment Facility Name** (`treatment_facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the treatment facility, if applicable.
* **Treatment Facility Address** (`treatment_facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the treatment facility, if applicable.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Investigation** (`incident_investigation`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether an investigation was conducted (Yes, No, Unknown).
* **Incident Investigation Notes** (`incident_investigation_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes related to the incident investigation.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the incident (Active, Completed, Closed).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Incident Status Notes** (`incident_status_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes related to the incident status.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether follow-up is required (Yes, No, Unknown).
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reporting officer (if applicable).
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (`reporting_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reporting officer (if applicable).
* **Reporting Officer Email** (`reporting_officer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reporting officer (if applicable).
* **Reporting Officer Notes** (`reporting_officer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the reporting officer.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the follow-up frequency (Once, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, optional): Select the follow-up method (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Follow-up Provider** (`follow_up_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the follow-up provider (if applicable).
