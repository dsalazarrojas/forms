# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mental Health Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your mental health. This form is used to assess your current emotional state, sleep patterns, and areas of body where you experience pain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Choose one or more options that best describe your current state for each question.
3. Enter any additional details or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ **Note**: This is a text field to provide any background information about your current situation.
	+ **Required**: No, you can skip this field if you don't have anything to add.
* **Mood Assessment**:
	+ **Select one or more options that best describe your current mood**:
		- Depressed
		- Anxious
		- Angry
		- Sad
		- Frustrated
		- Bodily pain
		- Fatigue
		- Confused
		- Ashen
	+ **Required**: No, you can skip this field if you're feeling fine.
* **Sleep Assessment**:
	+ **Enter a number of hours you've slept in the last 24 hours**:
	+ **Required**: No, but it will help us understand your sleep patterns.
* **Pain Location**:
	+ **Select the location where you experience pain**:
		- Head
		- Neck
		- Back
		- Abdomen
		- Legs
		- Arms
		- Joints
		- Other (add specific location in the comment field)
	+ **Required**: No, but it will help us understand where you feel pain.
