# Midwifery Client Intake & Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new clients and their medical history to ensure that our healthcare team can provide the best possible care and attention to each client.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your basic client information (Page 1: Client Info).
2.  Enter your contact information and emergency contact information (Pages 2 and 22).
3.  Report your medical history, including any allergies you may have (Pages 3, 20, and 21).
4.  Provide information on any medical conditions, surgeries, or medications you have undergone or are taking (Pages 4-17).
5.  Indicate your insurance information (Page 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (Page 1): Please enter your name and any relevant contact information. This helps our staff know who we are interacting with.
* **Contact Preferences** (Page 21): If you would like us to communicate with you in a specific way (e.g., email, phone, mail), please indicate it here.
* **Contact Info** (Page 2): Enter your address or contact information so that our staff can reach you when necessary.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 22): Please provide the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Birth Plan** (Page 23): If you have any specific birth plan preferences, let us know here.
* **Medical History** (Page 3): Describe your medical history in this space.
* **Allergies** (Page 20): List any allergies you have or may have had in the past.
* **Medical Condition** (Page 4): Indicate whether you have any known medical conditions.
* **Surgies** (Page 6): Check if you've had any surgeries in the past.
* **Family History** (Page 7): Provide information on your family medical history.
* **Medications** (Page 8): List any medications you take, but this is not required.
* **Immune System** (Page 9): Check if you have any conditions that affect your immune system.
* **Hematological** (Page 10): Indicate whether you have any hematological conditions.
* **Cardiovascular** (Page 11): Check if you have any cardiovascular conditions.
* **Respiratory** (Page 12): Check if you have any respiratory conditions.
* **Gastrointestinal** (Page 13): Check if you have any gastrointestinal conditions.
* **Endocrine** (Page 14): Check if you have any endocrine conditions.
* **Neurological** (Page 15): Check if you have any neurological conditions.
* **Musculoskeletal** (Page 16): Check if you have any musculoskeletal conditions.
* **Genitourinary** (Page 17): Check if you have any genitourinary conditions.
* **Therapeutic** (Page 18): Check if you have any therapeutic conditions.
* **Other** (Page 19): If you have any medical history that does not fit in any of the other categories, please list it here.
* **Insurance** (Page 25): Enter your insurance information so that we can ensure we have the right coverage for your care.
