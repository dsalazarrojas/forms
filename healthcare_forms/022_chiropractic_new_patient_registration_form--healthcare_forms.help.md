# Chiropractic New Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic New Patient Registration Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from new patients. This form helps us understand your medical history, preferences, and insurance details to provide you with the best possible care during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. Incomplete or inaccurate information may delay your treatment or diagnosis.
2. For each field, ensure you fill in the required and optional information as specified.
3. If you encounter any difficulties or questions while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **First Name**: Provide your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Middle Initial**: Your middle name's initial, if applicable.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Suffix**: Any suffix (e.g., Jr, Sr, III) associated with your name.
* **Preferred Name**: If different from your given name, please specify the name you prefer to be called.

### Personal Details
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age**: Your age in years.
* **Gender**: Select the correct gender for you.
* **Social Security Number**: Your Social Security Number, if applicable.
* **Contact Information**: Provide a brief explanation of how you can be reached.
* **Street Address**: Your current street address.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **State**: The state where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Your zip code.
* **Home Phone**: Your home phone number.
* **Mobile Phone**: Your mobile phone number.
* **Work Phone**: Your work phone number, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.

### Employment and Family Details
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Occupation**: Your job title or occupation.
* **Employer Name**: The name of your employer, if applicable.
* **Work Address**: Your work address, if different from your home address.
* **Is your job physically demanding**: Select the level of physical demand in your job.
* **Family Information**: Provide a brief explanation of your family details.
* **Marital Status**: Select your marital status.
* **Number of Dependents**: The number of dependents you have.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: The relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact.

### Healthcare Preferences
* **Healthcare Preferences**: Provide any healthcare preferences or concerns you may have.

### Medications and Allergies
* **Are you currently taking any medications**: Select yes or no.
* **List Your Current Medications**: Provide a list of your current medications and dosages.
* **Do you have any allergies**: Select yes or no.
* **List Your Allergies**: Provide a list of your allergies.

### Referral and Visit Details
* **Referral Source**: How you found our office.
* **How did you hear about our office**: How you heard about our office.
* **Referred By**: If someone referred you, provide their name.
* **Reason for Visit**: A brief explanation of why you are visiting our office.
* **Primary Complaint**: The main reason for your visit.
* **Current Pain Level**: Your current pain level on a scale of 1-10.

### Insurance and Billing
* **Do you have health insurance**: Select yes or no.
* **Insurance Company**: The name of your insurance company, if applicable.
* **Policy Number**: Your policy number or member ID.
* **Group Number**: Your group number.
* **Policy Holder Name**: The name of the policy holder, if different from you.
* **Policy Holder Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the policy holder.

### Signature and Confirmation
* **Patient Signature**: A digital signature to confirm your details.
* **Date of Signature**: The date you sign this form.
* **Policy Holder Date of Birth**: A digital signature to confirm your details, if different from you.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure we provide you with the best possible care.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our team for support.

Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient registration process. If you encounter any difficulties or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team for support.
