# Healthcare Cybersecurity Awareness Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and obtain consent from participants in the healthcare cybersecurity awareness video screening, online training, or training session. It is a mandatory form that must be completed accurately to ensure a smooth and secure participant experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, making sure it matches your professional or personal email used for official purposes.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Specify the "Date of Video Screening" if you are participating in the video screening.
6. Choose the "Type of Consent" that best aligns with your participation in the video screening, online training, or training session.
7. If you are participating in a training session, provide the "Video Title", "Course Title", "Training Session ID", "Project Number", and "Project Name" if required.
8. If you are uploading files, provide the necessary information in the "Upload files", "Video Title", "Video Description", and "Video Tags" fields.
9. Confirm your consent to participate in the video screening, online training, or training session by selecting all relevant options in the "Consent" field.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes about your participation in the "Comments" field (optional).
11. Sign the form by providing your signature in the "Signature" field.
12. Attach the video file and any additional files required for the training session in the "Video File" and "Uploaded Files" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address in this field.
* **Contact Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number in this field.
* **Date of Video Screening** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the video screening if you are participating.
* **Type of Consent** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of consent that aligns with your participation (Video Screening, Online Training, or Training Session).
* **Video Title** (`7`, `text`, optional): Provide the title of the video if required for the training session.
* **Course Title** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter the course title if required for the training session.
* **Training Session ID** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the training session ID if required.
* **Project Number** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the project number if required for the training session.
* **Project Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the project name if required for the training session.
* **Location** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide the location of the training session if required.
* **Date** (`13`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session if required.
* **Time** (`14`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the training session if required.
* **Instructor** (`15`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the instructor for the training session if required.
* **Consent** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your consent to participate in the video screening, online training, or training session.
* **Comments** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your participation in the training session.
* **Signature** (`19`, `text`, required): Sign the form by providing your signature.
* **Video File** (`20`, `text`, required): Attach the video file for the training session.
* **Upload files** (`21`, `text`, required): Upload any additional files required for the training session.
* **Video Title** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the uploaded video.
* **Video Description** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the uploaded video.
* **Video Tags** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter tags for the uploaded video.
* **Submitted Date** (`25`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you submitted the form.

Please ensure to review each field carefully and provide accurate information to complete this form successfully.
