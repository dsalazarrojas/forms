# Therapist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Interview Form is a comprehensive guide for collecting client information during an interview. This form is designed to help therapists gather essential details about the client's personal and medical history, mental status, support system, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
• **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page of the form, where you will provide general information about the client. Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant details about the client.

### Contact Details
• **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): On this page, please provide the client's contact information, including their name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

### Medical History
• **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): This page is for recording the client's medical history, including any chronic conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.

### Mental Status
• **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the client's mental status, including any mental health conditions, treatment plans, or current emotional state.

### Support System
• **Support System** (`support_system`, `text`, required: false): List the client's support system, including family members, caregivers, or friends who can provide emotional support.

### Emergency Contact
• **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Identify the person to contact in case of an emergency, including their name, phone number, and relationship to the client.

### Client Status
• **Client Status** (`client_status`, `text`, required: false): Provide an overview of the client's current situation, including their motivation for seeking therapy, goals, and any challenges they are facing.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the form's instructions or contact the relevant authorities.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical or therapeutic advice.
