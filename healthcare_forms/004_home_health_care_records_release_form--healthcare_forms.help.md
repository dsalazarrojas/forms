# Home Health Care Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to transfer a patient's health care records from one healthcare provider to another. It ensures that the patient's medical history, records, and other relevant information are accurately and securely transferred between healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Complete the patient's name, address, and any other relevant demographic details in the "patient_info" field.
2. **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date when the transfer of records is planned or completed in the "date_of_transfer" field.
3. **Signature of Healthcare Provider**: Sign your name in the "healthcare provider signature" field, indicating your approval for the transfer.
4. **Patient Signature**: Have the patient sign their name in the "patient signature" field, indicating their consent for the transfer.
5. **Reason for Transfer**: Briefly describe the reason for the transfer in the "reason for transfer" field, if necessary.
6. **Patient Concurrence**: Select the type of concurrence required for the transfer from the available options (Patient, Patient with family member, Patient and family member).
7. **Patient Relationship**: Choose the relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider (Spouse, Parent, Other, None).
8. **Medical Provider**: Select the type of medical provider involved in the transfer (Doctor, Nurse, Other, Unknown).
9. **Patient Demographics**: Enter any additional patient information or medical history in the "patient demographics" field.
10. **Authorization**: Enter the level of authorization required for the transfer.
11. **Transfer Date**: Enter the date and time of the transfer in the "transfer date" field.
12. **Medical Record**: Enter the medical record number or any other identifying information in the "medical record" field.
13. **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient in the "medical history" field.
14. **Transfer Method**: Choose the method of transfer (Electronic, Fax, Mail, Other, None).
15. **Transfer Location**: Enter the location where the records will be transferred.
16. **Transfer Method Details**: Enter any additional details about the transfer method.
17. **Transfer Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the transfer (if different from the previous field).
18. **Medical Record Number**: Enter any medical record number or identifier.
19. **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider involved in the transfer.
20. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address for transfer purposes.
21. **Transfer Date and Time 2**: Enter any additional transfer date and time information.
