# Global Preventive Medicine Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Preventive Medicine Fellowship Application Form is designed for individuals to apply for a Preventive Medicine Fellowship position. This form is used to gather information about the applicant's personal and professional background, medical training, experience, and qualifications to assess their eligibility for the fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal details.
2. Provide details about your medical training, including any relevant courses, certifications, or degrees.
3. Share your experience in the field, including any relevant work or research experience.
4. List your research background, including any studies, projects, or publications.
5. Provide information about your education, including any schools or institutions you've attended.
6. List your professional licenses, certifications, or board certifications.
7. Specify your medical specialties, if applicable.
8. Describe your practice settings, if applicable.
9. Share your work experience, including any relevant positions or roles.
10. List your availability for the fellowship program.
11. List any languages you speak or are proficient in.
12. Specify any languages you're familiar with, but not necessarily proficient in.
13. Describe any languages skills you possess, if applicable.
14. List any medical certifications or board certifications you hold.
15. List any medical schools you've attended.
16. Provide details about any medical residency programs you've participated in.
17. List any medical fellowship programs you've participated in.
18. Describe any medical conferences you've attended.
19. List any publications or papers you've authored.
20. List any presentations you've given.
21. Provide details about your professional references.
