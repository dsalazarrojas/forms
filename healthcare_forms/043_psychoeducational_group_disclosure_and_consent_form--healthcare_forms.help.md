# Psychoeducational Group Disclosure And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to ensure you understand the group's purpose, boundaries, and expectations, and to obtain your consent for the duration and frequency of sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Provide your Address.
3. Enter your Date Of Birth.
4. List your Contact Phone.
5. Enter your Email.
6. Provide the name of your Emergency Contact.
7. Specify your Emergency Contact Relationship.
8. Provide the phone number and email of your Emergency Contact.
9. Describe Group Disclosure Information.
10. Sign the Informed Consent.
11. Specify Group Leadership.
12. Select whether you have an Emergency Contact.
13. Specify whether you are bound by the rules of confidentiality or not.
14. Explain your understanding of the rules of confidentiality.
15. Sign the Confidentiality Agreement.
16. Specify the duration of group sessions in hours.
17. Specify the time of the group sessions.
18. Specify the day of the group sessions.
19. Specify the month of the group sessions.
20. Specify the year of the group sessions.
21. Specify the location of the group sessions.
22. Specify the duration of group sessions in hours.
23. This field is a duplicate, please only fill in one of the two.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (User Name, text, optional): Your name as it should be displayed in the group.
*   **Address** (Address, text, optional): Your address for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth, date, optional): Your date of birth for demographic purposes.
*   **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Email** (Email, email, optional): Your email address for communication with group members.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, optional): The name of the person you designate as your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, optional): Your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, optional): The phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, email, optional): The email address of your emergency contact.
*   **Group Disclosure Information** (Group Disclosure Information, text, optional): Additional information regarding your group disclosure.
*   **Informed Consent** (Informed Consent, number, optional): Sign to indicate you understand the group's rules and expectations.
*   **Group Leadership** (Group Leadership, text, optional): Information about the group's leadership.
*   **Emergency Contact Permission** (Emergency Contact Permission, select_one, optional): Whether you have an emergency contact or not.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement** (Confidentiality Agreement, select_multiple, optional): Whether you are bound by the rules of confidentiality or not.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement Explained** (Confidentiality Agreement Explained, select_multiple, optional): Your understanding of the rules of confidentiality.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement Signed** (Confidentiality Agreement Signed, select_one, optional): Sign to indicate you understand the confidentiality agreement.
*   **Group Disclosure Date** (Group Disclosure Date, time, optional): (Duplicates Field 23, please only fill in one of the two.)
*   **Group Disclosure Time** (Group Disclosure Time, time, optional): The time of the group sessions.
*   **Group Disclosure Day** (Group Disclosure Day, date, optional): The day of the group sessions.
*   **Group Disclosure Month** (Group Disclosure Month, date, optional): The month of the group sessions.
*   **Group Disclosure Year** (Group Disclosure Year, date, optional): The year of the group sessions.
*   **Group Disclosure Location** (Group Disclosure Location, text, optional): The location of the group sessions.
*   **Group Disclosure Duration** (Group Disclosure Duration, time, optional): (Duplicates Field 21, please only fill in one of the two.)
*   **Group Disclosure Date** (Group Disclosure Date, time, optional): (Duplicates Field 23, please only fill in one of the two.)
