# Health History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health History Form is a tool used to collect relevant information about a patient's medical history, including their personal details, medical conditions, and relationships. This form is designed to gather accurate and comprehensive data to aid healthcare professionals in providing better care and treatment for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it out accurately.
2. Enter the patient's birth date in the `patient_birth_date` field. This date should be entered in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Select the relationship between the patient and the family member in the `family_member_relationship` field. Choose from the available options or select "Other" if the relationship is not listed.
4. Enter the family member's name in the `family_member_name` field.
5. Enter the patient's email address in the `patient_email` field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
6. Enter the patient's phone number in the `patient_phone` field. This can be a phone number with an area code.
7. For the `sufferings` field, select all the areas where the patient experiences pain. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's pain points.
8. In the `draw_pain_area` field, select the specific area where the patient is experiencing pain. This field is optional but helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's pain points.
9. Enter any additional medical history or information about the patient in the `medical_history` field. This field is optional but provides valuable context.
10. Finally, enter any information about the patient's family history or medical history that is not covered in the previous fields in the `family_info` and `habits` fields. These fields are optional but can provide further context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the patient.
- **patient_birth_date** (date, required): Enter the birth date of the patient.
- **family_member_relationship** (select_one, required): Select the relationship between the patient and the family member (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend, Other).
- **family_member_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the family member.
- **patient_email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
- **patient_phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **sufferings** (select_multiple, optional): Select all areas where the patient experiences pain (Pain in head, Pain in neck, Pain in shoulders, Pain in back, Pain in arms, Pain in hands, Pain in legs, Pain in hips, Pain in knees, Pain in feet).
- **draw_pain_area** (select_one, optional): Select the specific area where the patient is experiencing pain (Chest, Back, Abdomen, Head, Neck, Shoulders, Hips, Hands, Legs, Knees, Feet, Other).
- **medical_history** (text, optional): Enter any additional medical history or information about the patient.
- **family_info** (text, optional): Enter any information about the patient's family history or medical history that is not covered in the previous fields.
- **habits** (text, optional): Enter any information about the patient's habits that may be relevant to their medical history.
- **patient_info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (patient_name, patient_birth_date, family_member_relationship, patient_email, patient_phone).
- Select all relevant areas of pain in the `sufferings` field to provide a comprehensive pain profile.
- Use the `draw_pain_area` field to provide further context to the patient's pain points.
- Keep the `medical_history`, `family_info`, and `habits` fields concise and focused on relevant information.
- If unsure about any field, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
