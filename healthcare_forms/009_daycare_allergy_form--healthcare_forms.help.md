# Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Allergy Form is designed to collect information about a patient's known and suspected allergies for the purpose of providing safe and effective care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and title (Form Administrator or Caregiver) to sign the form.
2.  Provide the name of the known allergy.
3.  Provide a description of the known allergy.
4.  Enter the name of the suspected allergy.
5.  Enter a description of the suspected allergy.
6.  Choose the status of the allergy (Known or Suspected).
7.  Enter the date the allergy was last recorded.
8.  Enter the time the allergy was last recorded.
9.  Enter a phone number for follow-up contact.
10. Enter an email address for follow-up contact.
11. Enter any additional notes regarding the allergy.
12. Choose the name of the allergist(s) who diagnosed the allergy.
13. Enter the date the form was signed.
14. Enter the time the form was signed.

* **Daycare Allergy Form (form_title)** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the form.
* **Known Allergy 1 (all_01_allergy_1_name)** (`all_01_allergy_1_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the known allergy.
* **Known Allergy 1 Description (all_03_allergy_1_description)** (`all_03_allergy_1_description`, text, required: false): Enter a description of the known allergy.
* **Suspected Allergy 1 (all_05_suspected_allergy_1_name)** (`all_05_suspected_allergy_1_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the suspected allergy.
* **Suspected Allergy 1 Description (all_07_suspected_allergy_1_description)** (`all_07_suspected_allergy_1_description`, text, required: false): Enter a description of the suspected allergy.
* **Allergy Status (allergy_09_allergy_status)** (`allergy_09_allergy_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the allergy (Known or Suspected).
* **Allergy Date Last Recorded (allergy_10_allergy_date_last_recorded)** (`allergy_10_allergy_date_last_recorded`, date, required: false): Enter the date the allergy was last recorded.
* **Allergy Time Last Recorded (allergy_11_allergy_time_last_recorded)** (`allergy_11_allergy_time_last_recorded`, time, required: false): Enter the time the allergy was last recorded.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible about the allergy.
* If the allergy is suspected, please provide any relevant information.
* If the allergy is known, please provide the most up-to-date information.
* If the patient has multiple allergies, please list them all.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the allergist or medical team.
