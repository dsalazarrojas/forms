# Medical First Point Of Contact Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about patients or their next of kin, including demographic information, medical history, and emergency contact details. This information is essential for providing proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's information in the fields provided.
2. **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
3. **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
4. **Email**: Enter the patient's email.
5. **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
6. **Medical Record**: Enter any existing medical records.
7. **Next Of Kin**: Select the next of kin's contact information from the provided options.
8. **Medical History**: Select all relevant medical history items that apply to the patient.
9. **Medical History Other**: Enter any other medical history information not listed.
10. **Medical History Other Note**: Enter a note about the patient's medical history.
11. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's information.
12. **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the emergency contact's contact number.
13. **Medical History Other 2**: Enter any other medical history information not listed.
14. **Emergency Medical Condition**: Select the emergency medical condition.
15. **Emergency Medical History**: Select all relevant emergency medical history items that apply to the patient.
16. **Allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient may have.
17. **Medical Procedure**: Enter any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
18. **Medication**: Enter any medications the patient is taking.
19. **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the patient.
20. **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (1): Enter the patient's name, last name, and any other identifying information.
* **Address** (2): Enter the patient's complete address including street address and postal code.
* **Contact Number** (3): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (4): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (5): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical Record** (6): Enter any existing medical records, including doctor's notes, test results, etc.
* **Next Of Kin** (7): Select the next of kin's contact information, including their name, relationship, contact number, and email.
	+ **Name**: Enter the next of kin's name.
	+ **Relationship**: Select the next of kin's relationship to the patient (e.g., mother, father, sister, brother).
	+ **Contact Number**: Enter the next of kin's contact number.
	+ **Email**: Enter the next of kin's email address.
* **Medical History** (8): Select all relevant medical history items that apply to the patient, including allergies, medical conditions, medications, and medical procedures.
* **Medical History Other** (9): Enter any other medical history information not listed.
* **Medical History Other Note** (10): Enter a note about the patient's medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (11): Enter the emergency contact's information, including their name and contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (12): Enter the emergency contact's contact number.
* **Medical History Other 2** (13): Enter any other medical history information not listed.
* **Emergency Medical Condition** (14): Select the emergency medical condition.
* **Emergency Medical History** (15): Select all relevant emergency medical history items that apply to the patient.
* **Allergies** (16): Enter any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical Procedure** (17): Enter any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medication** (18): Enter any medications the patient is taking.
* **Additional Information** (19): Enter any additional information about the patient.
* **Signature** (20): Sign and date the form to confirm completeness and accuracy.

## Tips
1. Please ensure you have reviewed and understood each field carefully before completing the form.
2. If you are unsure about a field, please ask for clarification or consult with the healthcare provider.
3. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper care and treatment.
