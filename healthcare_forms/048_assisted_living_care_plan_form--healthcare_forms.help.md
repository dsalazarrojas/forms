# Assisted Living Care Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Assisted Living Care Plan Form is a comprehensive document used to create a personalized care plan for residents in assisted living facilities. This form helps care managers and healthcare professionals assess the resident's medical, social, and emotional needs, and identify areas where they require support and intervention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the resident's name, ID, and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the admission date and care plan date, if applicable.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the care manager who created the plan.
4. List the current medical conditions, allergies, and medications of the resident.
5. Assess the resident's mobility status, fall risk level, and activities of daily living (ADL) capabilities.
6. Evaluate the resident's cognitive status and identify any dietary needs or restrictions.
7. Record the resident's social preferences and any family involvement level.
8. Specify the care goals and interventions required.
9. Enter any special instructions or comments.
10. Confirm the family involvement level and resident consent for the care plan.
11. Set the next review date for the care plan.
12. Sign and date the form, ensuring the resident and care manager have acknowledged and agreed to the care plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name**: The full name of the resident, as identified on their identification documents.
* **Resident ID**: The room or unit number of the resident.
* **Date of Birth**: The resident's birthdate.
* **Admission Date**: The date the resident was admitted to the facility.
* **Care Plan Date**: The date the care plan was created.
* **Care Manager Name**: The name of the care manager who created the care plan.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the resident's primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: The relationship between the resident and emergency contact (e.g., spouse, family member, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Primary Physician Name**: The name of the resident's primary physician.
* **Physician Phone**: The phone number of the primary physician.
* **Current Medical Conditions**: A list of the resident's current medical conditions or diagnoses.
* **Allergies**: A list of any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Current Medications**: A list of all current medications.
* **Mobility Status**: The level of mobility and independence of the resident (e.g., Independent, Requires Cane, Requires Walker).
* **Fall Risk Level**: The level of risk assessment for falls.
* **Activities of Daily Living (ADL) Assessment**: An assessment of the resident's abilities with bathing, dressing, eating, toileting, transferring, and other daily activities.
* **Cognitive Status**: An evaluation of the resident's mental function and cognitive impairment (e.g., Alert and Oriented, Mild Impairment, Severe Impairment).
* **Dietary Needs**: A list of the resident's dietary requirements or restrictions.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Any food or food products that the resident cannot or should not consume.
* **Social Preferences**: Information about the resident's social interests or preferences.
* **Family Involvement Level**: The level of involvement of the family member or support system (e.g., Very Involved, Moderately Involved, Minimally Involved).
* **Care Goals**: The objectives and goals of the care plan.
* **Interventions Required**: A list of any special interventions required for the resident.
* **Special Instructions**: Any important notes or comments.
* **Family Involvement**: The level of family involvement in the care plan.
* **Resident Consent**: Confirmation of the resident's consent for the care plan.
* **Resident Signature**: The resident's signature, if applicable.
* **Guardian Signature**: The guardian's signature, if applicable.
* **Care Manager Signature**: The care manager's signature, confirming their agreement to the care plan.
* **Physician Review Date**: The date the primary physician reviewed the care plan.
* **Next Review Date**: The date for the next review of the care plan.
