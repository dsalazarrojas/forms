# Surgery Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients for surgery reservation purposes. It is intended to be completed by the patient or their representative to provide details about the procedure, including name, contact information, and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

- Read the form carefully and fill out all the required fields (indicated by *required*).
- Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (page 1).
- Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (page 1).
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field (page 3), ensuring it is a valid email format (e.g., `patient@example.com`).
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (page 4).
- Select the date of your surgery in the "Surgery Date" field (page 5).
- Choose the time of your surgery in the "Time" field (page 6).
- Provide any additional details about your surgery in the "Details" field (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Page 1)** (`First Name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Page 1)** (`Last Name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Page 3)** (`patient_email`, `email`, *required*): A valid email address is required for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Page 4)** (`phone`, `text`, *optional*): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Surgery Date (Page 5)** (`surgery_date`, `date`, *required*): Select the date of your scheduled surgery.
* **Time (Page 6)** (`time`, `time`, *required*): Choose the time of your scheduled surgery.
* **Details (Page 7)** (`details`, `text`, *optional*): Add any additional information about your surgery.
