# Falls Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Falls Risk Assessment Survey is a tool used to evaluate the risk of falls for patients in a healthcare setting. It aims to identify patients who are at a higher risk of falls, enabling healthcare professionals to create a plan to prevent them.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Falls Risk Assessment Survey, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing patient information in the patient_info field. This field is optional but helpful for contextualizing the risk assessment.
2. Select your answer to each risk question. Each question asks you to choose a level of risk (High, Moderate, or Low).
3. If you have time, enter any relevant medical notes or falls prevention plan details in the medical_notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_info** (optional text): Enter any relevant information about the patient that you think might be useful for assessing their risk of falls.
- **risk_questions_1** (select_one, High, Moderate, Low): Assess the patient's mobility risk level. Select High, Moderate, or Low to indicate their risk of falls.
- **risk_questions_2** (select_multiple, High, Low, Moderate): Evaluate the patient's risk of falls due to medications.
- **risk_questions_3** (select_one, High, Moderate, Low): Assess the patient's risk of falls due to medical conditions.
- **risk_questions_4** (select_multiple, High, Low, Moderate): Evaluate the patient's risk of falls due to mobility issues.
- **risk_questions_5** (select_one, High, Moderate, Low): Assess the patient's risk of falls due to cognitive impairment.
- **risk_questions_6** (select_multiple, High, Low, Moderate): Evaluate the patient's risk of falls due to environmental factors.
- **risk_questions_7** (select_one, High, Moderate, Low): Assess the patient's risk of falls due to other factors.
- **risk_questions_8** (select_multiple, High, Low, Moderate): Evaluate the patient's risk of falls due to balance issues.
- **patient_info_2** (optional text): Enter any additional information about the patient that you think is relevant.
- **falls_prevention_plan** (optional text): Describe any falls prevention plan you have for the patient.
- **medical_notes** (optional text): Enter any medical notes or relevant information about the patient's condition.
- **risk_score** (optional text): Enter the calculated risk score for the patient (usually provided by the system).
- **falls_risk_assessment** (optional text): Enter a narrative assessment of the patient's risk of falls.
- **falls_risk_level** (select_one, High, Moderate, Low): Evaluate the overall risk of falls for the patient, based on their answers to the previous questions.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Use any additional information fields to provide context and relevant details.
* Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by a star *).
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they make sense and are consistent.
