# Specimen Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specimen Collection Form is used by medical professionals to gather patient information for future reference. This form is essential in ensuring that patient data is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask a medical professional for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Name** (`patient_info`, text, required): Please enter the patient's full name.
* **Date** (`collection_date`, date, optional): Record the date the specimen was collected.
* **Time** (`collection_time`, time, optional): Record the time the specimen was collected.

### Specimen Information
* **Specimen Type** (`specimen_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from Blood, Tissue, Urine, or Other.
* **Specimen Description** (`specimen_description`, text, optional): Describe the specimen in detail.
* **Specimen ID** (`specimen_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the specimen.
* **Weight** (`patient_weight`, number, optional): Record the patient's weight.
* **Height** (`patient_height`, number, optional): Record the patient's height.
* **Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Record the patient's age.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the specimen.

### Additional Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult a medical professional for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.
