# Aesthetics Supplementary Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a client's aesthetics concerns and lifestyle habits to create a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name as you would like it to appear on your treatment plan and in any resulting records.
2. Select your main aesthetics concern that bothers you the most.
3. Indicate your average time spent outdoors and your sunscreen usage habits.
4. Describe your current skincare routine, including any products you use.
5. Rate your diet and hydration level.
6. Select your stress levels, as it can impact skin health.
7. Choose areas of concern related to skin issues you would like to improve.
8. Indicate your pain tolerance for numbing during treatments.
9. Choose a budget range for treatment plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your treatment plan and records.
* **Main Aesthetic Concern** (`main_concern`, required): Select the primary issue you'd like to address.
* **Daily Sun Exposure** (`sun_exposure`, required): Choose how much time you spend outdoors daily.
* **Sunscreen Usage** (`spf_usage`, required): Select how often you use sunscreen.
* **Current Skincare Routine** (`skincare_routine`, optional): Describe your current skincare products and habits.
* **Diet and Hydration** (`diet_water`, optional): Rate your diet and hydration level.
* **Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, optional): Choose how you'd rate your current stress levels.
* **Areas of Concern** (`concern_areas`, required): Select all areas where you'd like to see improvement.
* **Pain Tolerance** (`pain_tolerance`, required): Indicate your pain tolerance for numbing during treatment.
* **Budget Range for Treatment Plan** (`budget_expectations`, optional): Choose a budget range for the treatment plan.
