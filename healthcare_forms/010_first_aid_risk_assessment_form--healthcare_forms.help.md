# First Aid Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Aid Risk Assessment Form is used to assess the risk level of first aid equipment and supplies in a facility. This information helps identify potential risks and enables the facility to take necessary measures to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of facility (Facility, Company, or School) from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the necessary first aid equipment and supplies required for the facility from the list.
3. Select the type of first aid training provided to employees (First Aid and CPR, Basic Life Support, or Emergency Response).
4. Assess the risk level of the facility (Low, Medium, or High) and provide the date of the last first aid inspection.
5. Enter the emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
6. Provide any additional facility characteristics that may be relevant to the risk assessment.
7. If applicable, specify the frequency of first aid supplies reordering, training, and review.
8. Select the status of first aid supplies (Available, Available but Expired, or Not Available).
9. Provide any comments regarding first aid supplies status.
10. Enter the status of first aid training (if any).
11. Specify the frequency of first aid inspection.
12. Enter the expiration date of first aid supplies (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Type** (Facility, Company, School): Select the type of facility from the dropdown menu.
* **First Aid Equipment** (First Aid Kit, Bandages, Gloves, CPR Mask, CPR Bag): Choose the necessary first aid equipment required for the facility.
* **First Aid Training** (First Aid and CPR, Basic Life Support, Emergency Response): Select the type of first aid training provided to employees.
* **Risk Rating** (Low, Medium, High): Assess the risk level of the facility.
* **First Aid Inspection** (Date): Enter the date of the last first aid inspection.
* **Emergency Contact** (Name, Phone): Enter the emergency contact name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Phone): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Facility Characteristics** (Text): Provide any additional facility characteristics that may be relevant to the risk assessment.
* **First Aid Risk Factors** (Text): Specify any first aid risk factors.
* **Site Characteristics** (Text): Enter any site-specific characteristics that may be relevant to the risk assessment.
* **First Aid Supplies** (First Aid Kit, Bandages, Gloves, CPR Mask, CPR Bag): Choose the necessary first aid equipment required for the facility.
* **First Aid Supplies Quantity** (Text): Enter the quantity of first aid supplies on hand.
* **First Aid Equipment Location** (Text): Enter the location of first aid equipment.
* **First Aid Equipment Location Details** (Text): Enter any additional details about first aid equipment location.
* **First Aid Supplies Reorder Frequency** (Text): Specify the frequency of first aid supplies reordering.
* **First Aid Training Frequency** (Text): Specify the frequency of first aid training.
* **First Aid Review Frequency** (Text): Specify the frequency of first aid review.
* **First Aid Reporting** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly): Select the frequency of first aid reporting.
* **First Aid Supplies Status** (Available, Available but Expired, Not Available): Select the status of first aid supplies.
* **First Aid Supplies Status Comments** (Text): Enter any comments regarding first aid supplies status.
* **First Aid Training Status** (Text): Enter the status of first aid training.
* **First Aid Inspection Frequency** (Text): Specify the frequency of first aid inspection.
* **First Aid Supplies Expiration** (Text): Enter the expiration date of first aid supplies (if known).
* **First Aid Risk Categorization** (Text): Enter any risk factors.
* **Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes relevant to the risk assessment.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all necessary information has been entered.
- If any of the fields are not applicable, leave them blank.
- Use the "Notes" field for any additional information that does not fit in other categories.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the facility's administration.
