# COVID 19 Screening Form For Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to screen patients for COVID-19 symptoms and exposure risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the date you visited the healthcare facility or were consulted through telehealth.
4. Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit.
5. Select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing from the list provided.
6. Indicate whether you have had a positive COVID-19 test in the last 14 days.
7. Confirm if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the last 14 days.
8. Indicate if anyone in your household is currently positive or awaiting test results.
9. Select whether you have traveled outside the region in the last 14 days.
10. Provide your signature with your full name as proof of your identity.
11. Confirm that you consent to a telehealth consultation if deemed necessary.
12. Certify that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Screening**: Enter the date you are visiting the healthcare facility or consulting through telehealth.
* **Temperature (F)**: Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Are you currently experiencing any of the following?**: Select all the symptoms that apply to you. These include:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Loss of taste/smell
	+ Sore throat
	+ Fatigue
* **Have you had a positive COVID-19 test in the last 14 days?**: Select 'True' if you have had a positive test, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in 14 days?**: Select 'True' if you have had close contact, 'False' otherwise.
* **Is anyone in your household currently positive or awaiting test results?**: Select 'True' if anyone in your household is positive or awaiting test results, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you traveled outside the region in the last 14 days?**: Select 'True' if you have traveled, 'False' otherwise.
* **Patient Signature (Type Full Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **I consent to telehealth consultation if deemed necessary**: Select 'True' if you consent, 'I consent' otherwise.
* **I certify the above information is accurate**: Select 'True' if the information is accurate, 'False' otherwise.
