# New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and contact information for a new patient, as well as emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal information page (page 1).
2. Enter the patient's contact information (page 2).
3. Provide emergency contact information (page 3).
4. Add any relevant medical history (page 4).
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information (Personal Information)** (`personal_information`, text, optional)
	+ This page is for collecting basic patient details.
	+ Enter the patient's name, address, date of birth, and other essential details.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_information`, text, optional)
	+ This page is for collecting the patient's contact details.
	+ Enter the patient's phone number, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)
	+ This page is for listing any emergency contacts.
	+ Enter the names and contact information of the patient's emergency contacts.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
	+ This page is for listing any relevant medical history.
	+ Describe any past medical conditions, treatments, or allergies.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`submit`, text, optional)
	+ This is the final button to submit the form.
	+ Review and verify the information before submitting.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check all entered information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or doubts, contact the healthcare provider for guidance.
* Remember to keep patient data secure and confidential.
* This form is intended for personal use and not for mass collection or automated submission.
