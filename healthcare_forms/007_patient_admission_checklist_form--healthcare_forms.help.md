# patient_admission_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient admission checklist form is used to collect information from patients to help healthcare professionals understand their medical history and needs during the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Provide the patient's address in the `patient_address` field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of emergencies in the `contact_name`, `contact_phone`, and `contact_email` fields.
5. Describe the patient's current medical condition in the `medical_condition` field.
6. List any current medications the patient is taking in the `current_medications` field.
7. List any allergies the patient has in the `allergies` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **patient_address** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's home address.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **contact_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of emergencies.
* **contact_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of emergencies.
* **contact_email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact in case of emergencies.
* **medical_condition** (text, required/optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **current_medications** (text, required/optional): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **allergies** (text, required/optional): List any allergies the patient has.

## Tips
* Please double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the patient for clarification or seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
* If the patient has any sensitive or confidential information, please handle it according to local data protection laws and regulations.
