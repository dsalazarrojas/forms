# Mental Health First Aid Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mental Health First Aid Course Registration Form is used to gather information from individuals interested in registering for the mental health first aid course. This form is designed to be completed by potential attendees to provide essential details for the course administration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the instructions and form carefully before starting.
2.  Fill in the required fields (marked as "required").
3.  Enter your details accurately and completely.
4.  Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Form**:
    +  **<label>** (Registration Form): Enter your name in this field.
*   **Course Details**:
    +  **<label>** (Course Details): Enter the name and any other relevant details about the course you are registering for.
*   **Personal Details**:
    +  **<label>** (Personal Details): Enter your personal details such as your address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
*   **Contact Info**:
    +  **<label>** (Contact Info): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for your care or emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact**:
    +  **<label>** (Emergency Contact): Enter the contact information of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Info**:
    +  **<label>** (Medical Info): Enter any medical information you think is relevant to your registration.
*   **Medical History**:
    +  **<label>** (Medical History): Enter any medical history you have, including any ongoing medical conditions or previous treatments.
*   **Emergency Medical Info**:
    +  **<label>** (Emergency Medical Info): Enter any emergency medical information you have, including your medical insurance number or any other relevant medical history.
*   **Registration Date**:
    +  **<label>** (Registration Date): Enter the date you are planning to register for the course.
*   **Course Attendance**:
    +  **<label>** (Course Attendance): Select 'Yes' if you plan to attend the course, 'No' if you do not plan to attend.
*   **Course Fee**:
    +  **<label>** (Course Fee): Enter the course fee you are willing to pay.

# Tips

*   Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review your form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or omissions.
*   If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
