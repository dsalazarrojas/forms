# daily_symptoms_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's daily symptoms and medical history to help healthcare professionals assess their current health status and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields:
    *   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name.
    *   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the healthcare professional's name who is treating the patient.
    *   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select any of the symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing.
2.  Fill in the optional fields:
    *   **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide any relevant medical history or previous conditions.
    *   **Current Conditions** (`current_conditions`): Briefly describe any current medical conditions or treatments.
    *   **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the assessment or evaluation (optional).
    *   **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the assessment or evaluation (optional).
    *   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the patient's contact phone number (optional).
    *   **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's contact email (optional).
    *   **Address** (`address`): Enter the patient's contact address (optional).
    *   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): Enter the patient's medical record number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`): This field is for providing any relevant medical history or previous conditions.
*   **Current Conditions** (`current_conditions`): This field is for briefly describing any current medical conditions or treatments.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select any of the listed symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): This field is for entering the patient's full name.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): This field is for entering the healthcare professional's name who is treating the patient.
*   **Date** (`date`): This field is for entering the date of the assessment or evaluation (optional).
*   **Time** (`time`): This field is for entering the time of the assessment or evaluation (optional).
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): This field is for entering the patient's contact phone number (optional).
*   **Email** (`email`): This field is for entering the patient's contact email (optional).
*   **Address** (`address`): This field is for entering the patient's contact address (optional).
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): This field is for entering the patient's medical record number (optional).

## Tips

*   Make sure to only select symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing.
*   Keep the medical history brief and focused on relevant information.
*   Use correct formats for date and time (e.g., MM/DD/YY or HH:MM AM/PM).
*   If you're unsure about any information, consult with the healthcare professional treating the patient.
*   This form is meant to be a starting point for a more comprehensive assessment. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the healthcare professional treating the patient.
