# Physician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Referral Form is a tool used to collect information about patients who require specialized medical care. It is essential to gather relevant details to facilitate effective communication with the referring physician and ensure a smooth transition of care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the referring physician's name, email, and phone number in the "Physician Name," "Physician Email," and "Physician Phone" fields, respectively.
3. Select the referring physician's specialty from the drop-down menu in the "Physician Specialty" field.
4. Enter the date of referral in the "Date of Referral" field.
5. Provide a brief explanation for the referral in the "Referring Reason" field.
6. Enter the name of the referring doctor in the "Referring Doctor's Name" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Name
* **Patient Name**: Text input field to enter the patient's name.
### 2. Physician Name
* **Physician Name**: Text input field to enter the referring physician's name.
### 3. Physician Email
* **Physician Email**: Text input field to enter the referring physician's email address.
### 4. Physician Phone
* **Physician Phone**: Text input field to enter the referring physician's phone number.
### 5. Physician Specialty
* **Physician Specialty**: Select a specialty from the drop-down menu to indicate the referring physician's specialty.
### 6. Date of Referral
* **Date of Referral**: Date input field to record the date of referral.
### 7. Referring Reason
* **Referring Reason**: Text input field to provide a brief explanation for the referral.
### 8. Referring Doctor's Name
* **Referring Doctor's Name**: Text input field to enter the name of the referring doctor.
### 9. Note
* **Note**: Text area field for adding any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your internal records or reach out to your team for clarification.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to prevent duplicate submissions.
* If you have any issues while completing the form, please contact your administrator or the technical support team for assistance.
