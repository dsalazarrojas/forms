# medical_company_employee_reference_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_company_employee_reference_request_form is a tool used to collect information from employees and their managers to verify the status and details of an employee's medical license.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, carefully read through each field and ensure you have all required information readily available.
- Fill out each field clearly and accurately, following the instructions provided for each section.
- If you are unsure about a particular field, please do not hesitate to consult with your HR representative or supervisor for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your current job title here.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter your current department here.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required: false): Enter your current job location here.
* **Manager First Name** (`manager_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's first name here.
* **Manager Last Name** (`manager_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's last name here.
* **Job Title Start Date** (`job_title_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your current job title here.
* **Job Title End Date** (`job_title_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your current job title here.
* **Job Title Reason** (`job_title_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for your current job title here.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter your medical license number here.
* **Medical License Status** (`medical_license_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your medical license from the dropdown list.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current status as an employee from the dropdown list.
* **Job Location 2** (`job_location_2`, text, required: false): Enter your current job location here.
* **Employee Reference Question** (`employee_reference_question`, note, required: false): This field is for a note or reference question from the employee.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's phone number here.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter your manager's email address here.
* **Medical Company Employment Status** (`medical_company_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current employment status with the medical company from the dropdown list.
* **Employee Reference Answer** (`employee_reference_answer`, text, required: false): Enter the answer to the employee reference question here.
* **Manager Reference Question** (`manager_reference_question`, note, required: false): This field is for a note or reference question from the manager.
* **Manager Reference Answer** (`manager_reference_answer`, text, required: false): Enter the answer to the manager reference question here.
* **Medical License Number 2** (`medical_license_number_2`, text, required: false): Enter your medical license number here.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's signature here.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's name here.
