# Patient Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to collect detailed information about a patient's health history and medical conditions. This form is intended to be used for general healthcare purposes, such as patient intake, medical histories, and health assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of a patient's health information.
2.  Please fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
3.  If a section is not relevant to the patient, you can leave it blank.
4.  Review and update the form as necessary to reflect any changes in the patient's health status.

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and contact information.

    *   Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email address.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
