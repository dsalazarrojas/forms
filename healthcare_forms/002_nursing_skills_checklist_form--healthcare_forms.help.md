# Nursing Skills Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on nurses' skills and expertise in various areas of healthcare. It helps in evaluating a nurse's qualifications for a specific role or assessing their skill level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the medical assessment area(s) that you are knowledgeable about or skilled in, such as Cardiopulmonary or Medication Management.
2. Choose your skill level from Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced, depending on your expertise.
3. If you'd like, add a note or comment about your qualifications or experiences.
4. Provide your contact email address for follow-up or additional information.
5. Enter your contact phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Assessment** (`Cardiopulmonary`, `Medication Management`, `Wound Care`): 
Select the areas of medical assessment that you are knowledgeable about or skilled in. Choose all that apply.
* **Skills Level** (`Basic`, `Intermediate`, `Advanced`): 
Choose your skill level, indicating your expertise in the selected medical assessment areas. This will help in assigning tasks or responsibilities accordingly.
* **Note** (`Note`): 
Add any additional information or comments about your qualifications or experiences. This field is optional.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`): 
Enter your email address for follow-up or additional information. This field is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`Contact Phone Number`): 
Provide your phone number for communication purposes. This field is optional, but recommended for follow-up or urgent matters.
