# Speech Pathology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speech Pathology Referral Form is designed to collect essential information for referrals to a speech pathology service. This form helps healthcare professionals gather accurate and detailed information to facilitate a smooth referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct referral details for the patient.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Enter the date of birth.
4. Provide the referral reason.
5. Enter the name of the doctor referring the patient.
6. Enter the patient's contact details.
7. Enter the speech pathology details.
8. Enter the date of referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral details**:
  * Referral details (Select one or more options): 
    Select "Child" or "Adult" to indicate the type of patient being referred.
* **Name**:
  * Name:
    Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of birth**:
  * Date of birth:
    Enter the patient's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Referral reason**:
  * Referral reason:
    Provide a brief description of the reason for the referral, e.g., "Speech therapy for stuttering".
* **Referring doctor**:
  * Referring doctor:
    Enter the name of the doctor referring the patient.
* **Patient contact details**:
  * Patient contact details:
    Enter any relevant contact information for the patient, e.g., phone number or email.
* **Speech pathology details**:
  * Speech pathology details:
    Enter any relevant information about the patient's speech pathology history or needs.
* **Date of referral**:
  * Date of referral:
    Enter the date when the referral was made.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information for each field.
* Check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant healthcare professional for assistance.
