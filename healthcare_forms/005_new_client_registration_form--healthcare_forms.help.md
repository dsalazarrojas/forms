# New Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Registration Form is used to collect and store client information for the healthcare system. This form helps to verify that each client's data is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your correct information.
2. Double-check that your email address and phone number are not already registered in our system to avoid duplication.
3. Select your preferred coaching service from the options provided.
4. Consent to the terms of the service.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is recorded on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is recorded on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you about your care.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service (1-5).
* **Expectations from Coaching Service** (`expectations_from_coaching_service`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching service you are interested in (Coaching or Non-Coaching).
* **Consent Terms** (`consent_terms`, select_multiple, required): Accept or decline the terms of the service by selecting one or both options.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you experience any errors while filling out the form, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We will review and verify your registration once you submit the form.
