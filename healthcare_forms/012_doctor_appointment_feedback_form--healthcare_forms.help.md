# Doctor Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients on their experiences with a doctor during an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide the name of the doctor who treated you.
4. If applicable, enter the appointment date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the time of the appointment.
6. Select the service quality of your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Enter the name of the nurse who treated you.
8. Enter any comments about your experience.
9. Select whether you agreed on the recommended treatments or not (True, False, or Not Sure).
10. Enter any recommendations for improvement.
11. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_doctor_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the doctor who treated you.
* **Appointment Date** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the appointment in the format YYYY-MM-DD. This field is optional.
* **Appointment Time** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_appointment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Service Quality** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_service_quality`, select_one, optional): Select the service quality of your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Feedback for Nurse** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_nurse_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nurse who treated you. This field is optional.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_doctor_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who treated you. This field is optional.
* **Comments** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about your experience.
* **Agreed on Recommendations** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_agreed_on_recommendations`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you agreed on the recommended treatments or not (True, False, or Not Sure).
* **Recommendations** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_recommendations`, text, required): Enter any recommended treatments or actions.
* **Recommendations for** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_recommendations_for`, select_one, optional): Select whether you recommend this doctor or not (Yes or No).
* **Suggestions** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_suggestions`, text, required): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`doctor_appointment_feedback_form_additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments.
