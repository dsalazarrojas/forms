# Hospice Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hospice Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from patients or their caregivers to provide hospice care services. This form is typically used to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the terms and conditions of care, including the services that will be provided and the responsibilities of the caregiver.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's name and address.
2.  Enter the patient's phone number and the caregiver's name and phone number.
3.  List the doctor's name and phone number (if applicable).
4.  Select the care team members who will be involved in the patient's care (if applicable).
5.  Choose the date of entry into hospice care and the date of discharge from hospice care (if applicable).
6.  Sign and date the form to indicate consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the name of the patient for whom care will be provided.
*   **Patient Address** (patient_address, text, optional): Enter the patient's address, including their city, state, and zip code.
*   **Patient Phone** (patient_phone, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Caregiver Name** (caregiver_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the caregiver who will be responsible for the patient's care.
*   **Caregiver Phone** (caregiver_phone, text, optional): Enter the caregiver's phone number.
*   **Doctor Name** (doctor_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who will be providing care to the patient.
*   **Doctor Phone** (doctor_phone, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
*   **Hospice Name** (hospice_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospice care provider.
*   **Care Team** (care_team, select_multiple, optional): Select the care team members who will be involved in the patient's care.
*   **Other (Specify)** (care_team_other, text, optional): If the patient's care team is not listed, enter the names of the team members here.
*   **Date Of Entry** (date_of_entry, date, optional): Enter the date the patient entered hospice care.
*   **Date Of Discharge** (date_of_discharge, date, optional): Enter the date the patient will be discharged from hospice care.
*   **Signature** (signature, text, optional): Sign and date the form to indicate consent.
*   **Date** (date, text, optional): Enter the date you sign and date the form.
*   **Printed Name** (printed_name, text, optional): Enter your printed name.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's best to fill them out for accurate record-keeping and to ensure that all necessary information is captured.
