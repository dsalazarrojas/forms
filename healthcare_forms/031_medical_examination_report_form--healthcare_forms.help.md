# Medical Examination Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document medical examinations and assessments performed on patients, providing a comprehensive record of the examination results, findings, and certificates issued.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a date and time for the medical examination from the **Date** and **Time** fields.
2. Enter the **Doctor Information**, including the doctor's name, department, and license number.
3. Select the doctor's department from the **Doctor Department** field.
4. Enter the patient's **Name**, **Address**, **City**, and **State** and **Zip**.
5. Record the **Medical Record Number**.
6. Specify the **Medical Examination Type** (e.g., General Physical, Pregnancy Test, Blood Test, etc.).
7. Enter the examination results in the **Medical Examination Results** field.
8. Enter any additional comments in the **Medical Examination Comments** field.
9. If applicable, issue a **Medical Certificate** with details in the corresponding fields.
10. Sign the **Signature** of the doctor and the **Patient Signature** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Job Application**: This field is likely not needed for medical examinations and can be ignored.
*   **Medical Information**: Enter any relevant medical information about the patient.
*   **Medical History**: Enter a detailed history of the patient's medical conditions and treatments.
*   **Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have examined the patient.
*   **Doctor Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the medical examination.
*   **Patient Signature**: Ask the patient to sign the form to acknowledge they understand their medical condition.
*   **Doctor Information**: Enter your name, department, and license number for the doctor's details.
*   **Doctor Department**: Select your department from the options provided.
*   **Doctor License**: Enter your license number.
*   **Date**: Record the date of the medical examination.
*   **Time**: Record the time of the medical examination.
*   **Name**: Enter the patient's name.
*   **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
*   **City**: Enter the patient's city.
*   **State**: Enter the patient's state.
*   **Zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
*   **Medical Record Number**: Record the patient's medical record number.
*   **Medical Examination Type**: Select the type of medical examination performed (e.g., General Physical, Pregnancy Test, etc.).
*   **Medical Examination Results**: Enter the results of the medical examination.
*   **Medical Examination Comments**: Enter any additional comments or findings from the examination.
*   **Medical Certificate Issue**: Issue a medical certificate with details (e.g., issuer, number, and date).
*   **Issued By**: Enter the issuer of the medical certificate.
*   **Number**: Enter the number of the medical certificate.
*   **Medical Certificate Date**: Record the date the medical certificate was issued.
*   **Status**: Set the status of the medical certificate (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
