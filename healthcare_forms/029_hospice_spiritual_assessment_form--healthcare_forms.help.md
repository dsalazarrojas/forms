# Hospice Spiritual Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's spiritual needs and practices to provide targeted spiritual care and support in a hospice care setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and the questions being asked.
2. Complete each section according to the patient's responses.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the patient or their family members for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro (1)**: This section is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance in providing spiritual care.
* **Patient Info (2)**: Please enter a brief description of the patient's spiritual background, including their religious or spiritual practices, and any other relevant information.
* **Spiritual Clinical (3)**: Please describe the patient's spiritual clinical practices, such as their prayer habits, spiritual leaders, or spiritual growth.
* **Patient Spirituality (4)**: Please describe the patient's spiritual beliefs and practices, including their sense of purpose, meaning, and hope.
* **Spiritual Clinical Score (5)**: Please select a score that reflects the patient's spiritual clinical practices, using the options provided.
* **Patient Spirituality Score (6)**: Please select a score that reflects the patient's spiritual beliefs and practices, using the options provided.
* **Religious Practice (7)**: Please select all the religious practices that apply to the patient, such as "Spiritual or religious group", "Baptist", "Catholic", etc.
* **Spiritual Leadership (8)**: Please select the level of spiritual leadership that the patient is actively seeking or involved with.
* **Spiritual Growth (9)**: Please select how frequently the patient practices their spiritual growth, using the options provided.
* **Patient Awareness (10)**: Please select the level of the patient's awareness of their spiritual beliefs and practices.
