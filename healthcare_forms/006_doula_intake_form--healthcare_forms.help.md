# Doula Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Intake Form is designed to collect information from potential clients about their experiences and preferences for doula services. The form helps doulas understand the client's needs and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide potential client information (page 1)
	* Enter the client's name and contact information.
2. Answer questions about prenatal history (page 2)
	* Share any relevant medical or health concerns during pregnancy.
3. Share medical history (page 3)
	* Provide any relevant medical history, including allergies or medical conditions.
4. Provide pregnancy details (page 4)
	* Share information about the pregnancy, including estimated due date and any relevant medical conditions.
5. Discuss doula service details (page 5)
	* Describe the type of doula services needed.
6. Share contact information (page 6)
	* Enter the client's contact information for further communication.
7. Specify birth preferences (page 7)
	* Share any preferences for birth location, birth position, and pain management.
8. Identify support system (page 8)
	* List any support team members, such as partner or birth partners.
9. Choose a birth place (page 9)
	* Select a preferred birth location (e.g., home, hospital, birth center).
10. Create a birth plan (page 10)
	* Describe any specific birth plans or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Potential Client Information**: Enter the client's name, email, and phone number for further communication and coordination.
* **Prenatal History**: Share any relevant medical or health concerns during pregnancy, such as gestational diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, including allergies or medical conditions that may impact pregnancy or birth.
* **Pregnancy Details**: Share information about the pregnancy, including estimated due date and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Doulas Service Details**: Describe the type of doula services needed, such as support during labor, birth, or postpartum care.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the client's contact information for further communication.
* **Birth Preferences**: Share any preferences for birth location, birth position, and pain management.
* **Support System**: List any support team members, such as partner or birth partners.
* **Birth Place**: Select a preferred birth location (e.g., home, hospital, birth center).
* **Birth Plan**: Describe any specific birth plans or preferences.
