# Lab Test Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Lab Test Referral Form is a tool for patients or medical staff to request and schedule lab tests. This form collects essential information to ensure lab tests are ordered correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and completely.
2. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
3. Enter the date of the referral in the **Referral Date** field.
4. Enter the lab order number in the **Lab Order Number** field.
5. Select the lab tests required from the list of options.
6. If necessary, enter any lab results in the **Lab Results** field.
7. Enter the name of the medical provider referring the patient in the **Medical Provider Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient to be referred for lab tests.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date the lab tests are requested.
* **Lab Order Number**: Enter the unique lab order number assigned to the patient.
* **Lab Tests**: Select one or more lab tests required for the patient from the list of options.
* **Lab Results**: Enter any lab results related to the referral, if applicable.
* **Medical Provider Name**: Enter the name of the medical provider referring the patient.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing the referral.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the lab technician or medical staff for guidance.
* This form is for lab test referrals only; do not use it for other purposes, such as patient intake or medical history.
