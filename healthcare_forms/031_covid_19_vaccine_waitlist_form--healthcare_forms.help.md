# COVID 19 Vaccine Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine Waitlist Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine. This form helps to:

*   Assess an individual's priority status for vaccination
*   Gather necessary contact information for follow-up
*   Provide important health information to aid in vaccine distribution and allocation

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter your contact details**: Provide your first name, last name, and date of birth.
2.  **Provide your contact information**: Enter your email address and phone number.
3.  **Enter your address**: Provide your current street address and city, state, and ZIP code.
4.  **Choose your priority status**: Select your correct priority status based on the options provided.
5.  **Report any medical conditions**: If applicable, list any ongoing health conditions or allergies.
6.  **Provide vaccination readiness information**: Let healthcare professionals know your readiness for vaccination.
7.  **Agree to be on the waitlist**: Confirm that you consent to be on the waitlist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Your first name is required and will be used for contact and identification purposes.
*   **Last Name**: Your last name is required and will be used for contact and identification purposes.
*   **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Gender**: Select your correct gender from the available options.
*   **Email Address**: Your email address is required and will be used for communication and follow-up.
*   **Phone Number**: Your phone number is required and will be used for communication and follow-up.
*   **Street Address**: Provide your current street address.
*   **City**: Enter your city.
*   **State**: Enter your state.
*   **ZIP Code**: Provide your ZIP code.
*   **Priority Status for Vaccination**: Select your correct priority status based on the provided options.
*   **Occupation or Employment Status**: If applicable, provide your current occupation or employment status.
*   **Immunocompromised**: Indicate whether you have any immune system issues.
*   **Chronic Medical Conditions**: If applicable, list any ongoing health conditions.
*   **Severe Allergies**: If applicable, list any severe allergies.
*   **Current Medications**: If applicable, list any medications you are taking.
*   **Vaccination Readiness**: Let healthcare professionals know your readiness for vaccination.
*   **Preferred Vaccine Type**: Select your preferred vaccine type if available.
*   **Need Transportation Assistance**: Indicate whether you need help getting to the vaccination site.
*   **Confirm Contact Information**: Confirm that your contact information is current and accurate.
*   **Agree to Be on the Waitlist**: Confirm that you consent to be on the COVID-19 vaccine waitlist.
