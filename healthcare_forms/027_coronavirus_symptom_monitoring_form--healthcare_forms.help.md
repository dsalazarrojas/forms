# coronavirus_symptom_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus_symptom_monitoring_form is used to collect and record symptoms of COVID-19 patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, please select the symptoms that you or the patient are experiencing from the list provided.
2.  If you are filling out the form on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to do so.
3.  Please ensure that the submitted information is accurate and complete.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, you can leave it blank and submit the form.

    *   Enter the patient's information here.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false):

        *   Cough
