# Health Intake Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from patients or individuals seeking healthcare services. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Personal Info" section with your name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
2. Provide details about your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
3. List your emergency contacts, including their names, relationships, and contact information.
4. Add any additional information or medical history that may be relevant to your healthcare needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, text, required): Fill out your name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Describe any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions that may be relevant to your healthcare needs.
* **Medical History 2** (`Medical History 2`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your medical history, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): List the name of an emergency contact person, such as a family member or friend.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`Emergency Contact Info`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for your emergency contact, including phone number and address.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`Emergency Contact 2`, text, optional): List another emergency contact person, if necessary.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Fill out your contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Contact Info 2** (`Contact Info 2`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information.
* **Emergency Contact 3** (`Emergency Contact 3`, text, optional): List another emergency contact person, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (`Emergency Contact Info 2`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Medical History 3** (`Medical History 3`, text, optional): Describe any additional medical history that may be relevant.
* **Medical History 4** (`Medical History 4`, text, optional): Provide any more details about your medical history.
