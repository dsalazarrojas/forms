# Coronavirus Business Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess a business's readiness to reopen after the pandemic. It helps gather information on the business's status, type, and other necessary details for a successful reopening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the select_one fields by choosing one of the provided options that best fit your business's status.
2. Fill out the text fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Enter a valid email address in the email field.
4. Fill out the select_multiple fields by selecting all applicable options.
5. Fill out the date and time fields in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM respectively).
6. Enter a number value in the number fields.
7. Use the note field to add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Status** (`select_one`): Choose the status of your business (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Business Type** (`select_one`): Choose the type of your business (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Number** (`text`): Enter your business's contact number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Business Email** (`email`): Enter your business's email address in the format XXXXX@XXXX.com.
* **Additional Requirements** (`select_multiple`): Select all the additional requirements that apply to your business.
* **Date of Reopening** (`date`): Enter the date of your business's reopening in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reopening Time** (`time`): Enter the time of your business's reopening in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Number of Employees** (`number`): Enter the number of employees that will be working at your business after reopening.
* **Number of Customers** (`number`): Enter the expected number of customers that will be visiting your business after reopening.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`): Add any additional comments or information about your business's reopening.
