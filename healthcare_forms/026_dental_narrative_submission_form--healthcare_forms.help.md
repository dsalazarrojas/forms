# dental_narrative_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_narrative_submission_form is a comprehensive document that collects detailed information from patients about their dental health and history. This form should be filled out by patients to provide their healthcare providers with a clear understanding of their current dental status, medical history, and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For fields with multiple forms, use the provided space to describe each form in detail.
3. Do not leave any fields blank without an explanation.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions you have, including allergies, medications, or previous medical procedures.

* **Patient Medication** (`patient_medication`, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, required/optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including medications, foods, or environmental factors.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required/optional): Describe any treatment plans you are currently following, including diets, exercises, or therapies.
* **Dental Procedure** (`dental_procedure`, text, required/optional): Describe any dental procedures you have undergone, including surgeries, fillings, or extractions.
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, required/optional): Describe your desired outcome for your dental health, including goals or expectations.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, required/optional): Provide information about your current healthcare provider, including their name and contact information.
* **Dental History** (`dental_history`, text, required/optional): Describe your dental history, including any significant events or procedures.

## Tips
