# ufea_welcome_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for registration and identification.

## How to Complete This Form
This guide will walk you through each field of the form. Follow the instructions for each field to complete it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: A required field that asks for the patient's full name.
* **Email (2)**: An optional field that requests the patient's email address.
* **Contact Number (3)**: An optional field for the patient's contact number.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: An optional field for the patient's date of birth.
* **Sign In Time (5)**: A non-essential field for the patient's sign-in time.
* **Note (6)**: An optional field for any additional notes about the patient.
* **Patient Address (7)**: An optional field for the patient's address.
* **Contact Person (8)**: An optional field for the contact person's name.
* **Department (9)**: An optional field for the patient's department.
* **Assigned To (10)**: A select-one field where you can choose who the patient is assigned to.
* **Patient Category (11)**: A select-multiple field where you can choose one or more categories for the patient.
* **Sign In Time (12)**: A non-essential field for the patient's sign-in time.

## Tips
- Ensure the patient's name is accurate and complete.
- Double-check the patient's email address and contact number.
- Verify the patient's date of birth and address if required.
- Choose the correct department and assigned to correctly.
- Select the correct patient category.
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately before submission.

Please note that this guide is for informational and educational purposes only. The fields and instructions are subject to change based on the form's version and requirements.
