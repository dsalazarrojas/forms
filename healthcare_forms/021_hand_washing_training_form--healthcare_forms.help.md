# Hand Washing Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information for hand washing training sessions. It captures details about the training form, questions, submitter information, and training completion status. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to provide information about the hand washing training they have conducted or attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the "Training Form" page and enter the title of the training session.
* Click on the "Questions" page and enter any questions or concerns raised during the training.
* On the "Submitter Info" page, enter the submitters name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
* On the "Training Completion" page, select the status of the training (e.g., completed, pending, etc.).
* On the "Training Status" page, select the status of the training application (e.g., approved, declined, etc.).
* On the "Additional Info" page, enter any additional information about the training.
* On the "Submit" page, review all the information entered and ensure it is accurate.
* Finally, select the "Agree" option to confirm that the information is correct and you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Form** (`training_form`, text, optional): Enter the title of the training session.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): Enter any questions or concerns raised during the training.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Enter the submitters name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
* **Training Completion** (`training_completion`, text, optional): Select the status of the training (e.g., completed, pending, etc.).
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, text, optional): Select the status of the training application (e.g., approved, declined, etc.).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the training.
* **Training Application** (`training_application`, text, optional): Select the type of training application (e.g., webinar, onsite, etc.).
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the submitters email address.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the submitters phone number.
* **Address** (`submitter_address`, text, optional): Enter the submitters physical address.
* **Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Attendance** (`training_attendance`, select multiple, optional): Select whether the training was attended by the submitter (e.g., yes, no, Other).
* **Attendance Notes** (`training_attendance_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the submitters attendance.
* **Training Application Type** (`training_application_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of training application (e.g., webinar, onsite, etc.).
* **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the training.
* **Training Application Status** (`training_application_status`, select one, optional): Select the status of the training application (e.g., pending, approved, declined, etc.).
* **Comments** (`training_application_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the training application.
* **Training Application Date** (`training_application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training application.
* **Training Application Time** (`training_application_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the training application.
* **Status Comments** (`training_status_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the training status.
* **Additional Training Info** (`additional_training_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the training.
* **Agree** (`submitter_agreement`, select one, optional): Select to confirm that the information is correct.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter the submitters email address.
* **Submit** (button): Click to submit the form.
* **Yes** (`submitter_agreement`, select one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
* **No** (`submitter_agreement`, select one, required): Disagree with the terms of service.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you are not required to complete them. Ensure you only enter information that is relevant to the training session.
