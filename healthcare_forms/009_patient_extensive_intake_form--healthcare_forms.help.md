# patient_extensive_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_extensive_intake_form is a comprehensive form designed to gather personal and medical information from patients for the purpose of providing comprehensive care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to your healthcare professional.
3. Ensure that you provide complete and accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Middle Name** (`2`, `text`, Optional): Enter your middle name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date`, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Date of Death** (`5`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of death for deceased patients.
* **Date of Death Certified** (`6`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of certification of death for deceased patients.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`7`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Number** (`8`, `text`, Optional): Enter a contact number for you or your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`10`, `number`, Optional): Enter your medical record number.
* **Blood Pressure** (`11`, `number`, Optional): Enter your blood pressure reading.
