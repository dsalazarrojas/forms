# COVID 19 Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Patient Screening Questionnaire is a standardized form designed to identify patients at risk for COVID-19 and guide healthcare professionals in determining the appropriate course of action for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by gathering the patient's full name, date of birth, contact phone number (if available), and any relevant screening information (e.g., symptoms, exposure history, travel history).
2. Review the patient's symptoms and determine if they have any of the listed symptoms (fever, cough, shortness of breath, sore throat, loss of taste or smell, or no symptoms).
3. Inquire about the patient's exposure history to confirmed cases in the last 14 days and select the relevant option.
4. Ask the patient about their recent travel history outside the local area in the last 14 days (if applicable).
5. List any chronic health conditions the patient may have.
6. Determine the outcome of the screening, choosing one of the three options: Proceed with appointment, Defer and test, or Refer to clinician.
7. Finally, enter the name of the healthcare professional performing the screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter the patient's full name as provided by the patient or guardian.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number where the patient can be reached for follow-up.
* **Symptoms**: Select all relevant symptoms the patient is experiencing.
*   *Note: Multiple symptoms can be selected.*
* **Exposure History**: Indicate if the patient has had contact with a confirmed case in the last 14 days.
* **Travel History**: Record any travel outside the local area in the last 14 days.
* **Chronic Conditions**: List any ongoing health conditions the patient may have.
* **Screening Outcome**: Choose the recommended action based on the screening results.
*   *Note: Please refer to the form's guidelines for specific criteria for each option.*
* **Screener Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional performing the screening.
* **Tips**:
  * Ensure accurate completion of the form for accurate patient care.
  * Refer to the form's guidelines for specific criteria and protocols.
  * Review the patient's chart for any existing medical conditions or previous test results before making decisions.
  * Consult with colleagues or supervisors for guidance when unsure.
