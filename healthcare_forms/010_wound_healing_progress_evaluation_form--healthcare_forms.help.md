# Wound Healing Progress Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess the healing progress of a patient's wound and provide guidance on necessary interventions and follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question according to your best knowledge and professional judgment.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, using the options provided or describing your observations in the space provided.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please do your best to answer based on available data and clinical experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the patient's relevant medical history and any notable medical conditions.
* **Wound Details** (`wound_info`, text, optional): Describe the wound's size, location, and any notable features.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which the evaluation was performed.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Record the time of day when the evaluation was performed.
* **Wound Size** (`wound_size`, number, optional): Estimate the wound size (e.g., 2 cm x 3 cm).
* **Wound Depth** (`wound_depth`, number, optional): Estimate the wound depth (e.g., 1 cm).
* **Tissue Type** (`tissue_type`, select_one, optional): Describe the type of tissue affected by the wound (e.g., skin, muscle, bone).
* **Bleeding** (`bleeding`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the wound is experiencing active bleeding.
* **Edema** (`edema`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the patient is experiencing swelling or edema in the affected area.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the patient's pain level related to the wound (e.g., mild, moderate, severe).
* **Redness** (`redness`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the wound is experiencing redness or erythema.
* **Swelling** (`swelling`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the patient is experiencing swelling or edema in the affected area.
* **Odor** (`odor`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the odor of the wound (e.g., mild, strong).
* **Redness Intensity** (`redness_intensity`, number, optional): Estimate the intensity of redness or erythema (e.g., 1-10).
* **Pain Intensity** (`pain_intensity`, number, optional): Estimate the patient's pain intensity related to the wound (e.g., 1-10).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the patient's condition.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, note, optional): Provide guidance on necessary follow-up care or interventions.
* **Wound Location** (`wound_location`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the location of the wound (e.g., head and neck, upper back, lower back).
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which the assessment was performed.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Record the time of day when the assessment was performed.
* **Wound Stage** (`wound_stage`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the current stage of wound healing (e.g., unhealed, healing, closed).
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the patient's overall medical condition (e.g., stable, improving, deteriorating).
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if follow-up care is required.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which follow-up care is scheduled.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Record the time of day when follow-up care is scheduled.
* **Follow-up Status Intensity** (`follow_up_status`, optional): Not included in the original form fields, this field was added to ensure follow-up instructions are clearly visible to healthcare staff.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" should have a default value set to "None" or an empty string to ensure that the form doesn't crash when submitting.
