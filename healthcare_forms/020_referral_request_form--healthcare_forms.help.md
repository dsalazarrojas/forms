# Referral Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for patient referrals from referring physicians to other healthcare providers. The form gathers relevant details about the patient, the referring doctor, and the referral reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the user's name in the **User Name** field.
2. Enter the user's phone number in the **User Phone** field.
3. Enter the user's email address in the **User Email** field.
4. Enter the name of the referring doctor in the **Referring Doctor Name** field.
5. Enter the email address of the referring doctor in the **Referring Doctor Email** field.
6. Enter the phone number of the referring doctor in the **Referring Doctor Phone** field.
7. Enter the reason for the referral in the **Referral Reason** field.
8. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
9. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
10. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Patient Date of Birth** field. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
11. Enter the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** field.
12. Enter the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the person who is requesting the referral.
* **User Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who is requesting the referral.
* **User Email**: Enter the email address of the person who is requesting the referral.
* **Referring Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who is referring the patient.
* **Referring Doctor Email**: Enter the email address of the doctor who is referring the patient.
* **Referring Doctor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the doctor who is referring the patient.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason why the doctor is referring the patient.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who is being referred.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the phone number of the patient who is being referred.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history about the patient.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the address of the patient who is being referred.

Please note that all fields are optional unless specified as required. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the referring doctor or the patient's healthcare provider.
