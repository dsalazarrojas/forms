# Treatment Site Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treatment Site Verification Form is a form designed to verify treatment sites for medical purposes. This form is used to gather information from patients about their treatment site, including their name, medical information, and treatment site preferences. The form is intended to be filled out by patients to provide accurate and up-to-date information for healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your first name and last name in the "First Name" field.
*   Select one or more treatment options from the "Treatment Site" field.
*   Provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Info" field.
*   If applicable, sign and date your signature in the "Doctor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`text`, required)
    This field is used to capture your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official ID or medical records.
*   **Treatment Site** (`select_multiple`, optional)
    Select one or more treatment options that apply to you. This field is used to ensure that you are receiving the correct treatment for your specific needs.
*   **Medical Info** (`text`, optional)
    This field is for any additional medical information that you think might be relevant to your treatment. Please provide any pertinent medical history or allergies.
*   **Doctor Signature** (`date`, optional)
    If you are being treated by a doctor, please have them sign and date this field. This is a confirmation of your treatment plan.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
*   This form is intended to be filled out by patients, not healthcare professionals. If you are a healthcare professional, please do not fill out this form on behalf of a patient.
