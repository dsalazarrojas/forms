# health_coaching_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users on their demographics and health-related metrics for health coaching purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select your gender (male, female, or other) on the "Demographics" page.
3. Enter your age, height, and weight on the respective pages.
4. Choose the unit of measurement for your height (ft, in, cm, or meters).
5. Enter the start and end of your age and height and weight ranges (in years, inches, cm, or meters).
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Demographics** (select_one, required: false): Select your gender to provide basic demographic information.
  * Please choose one of the following options: male, female, or other.

- **Age** (number, required: false): Enter your age in years.
  * This field is optional but helps us understand your age-related metrics.

- **Height** (number, required: false): Enter your height in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your height-related metrics.

- **Weight** (number, required: false): Enter your weight in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your weight-related metrics.

- **Height unit** (select_one, required: false): Choose the unit of measurement for your height.
  * Please select one of the following options: ft, in, cm, meters, or other.

- **Age range start** (number, required: false): Enter the start of your age range in years.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your age range.

- **Age range end** (number, required: false): Enter the end of your age range in years.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your age range.

- **Height range start** (number, required: false): Enter the start of your height range in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your height range.

- **Height range end** (number, required: false): Enter the end of your height range in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your height range.

- **Weight range start** (number, required: false): Enter the start of your weight range in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your weight range.

- **Weight range end** (number, required: false): Enter the end of your weight range in the selected unit of measurement.
  * This field is optional and helps us understand your weight range.
