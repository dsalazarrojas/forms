# Speech Therapy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a patient's speech and language skills, communication abilities, and medical history. It is a crucial tool for speech therapists to evaluate the patient's current situation and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's information on page 1, including their name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. On page 2, evaluate the patient's speech skills by answering the questions about their ability to speak clearly and articulately.
3. On page 3, assess the patient's language skills by selecting the relevant options for their language abilities.
4. On page 4, evaluate the patient's communication skills by answering the questions about their ability to communicate effectively.
5. On page 5, review the patient's medical history, including any medical procedures they have undergone and any medications they are taking.
6. On page 6, provide recommendations for the patient's treatment plan based on their assessment.
7. Finally, review and sign the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Provide the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
* **Speech Skills** (`speech_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's speech skills, such as "Yes" for clear and articulate speech or "No" for difficulties with speech.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's language skills, such as "Yes" for fluent language abilities or "No" for difficulties with language.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required): Describe the patient's ability to communicate effectively, including their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Cognitive Skills** (`cognitive_skills`, text, required): Describe the patient's cognitive skills, including their ability to think critically and problem-solve.
* **Behavioral Observations** (`behavioral_observations`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's behavioral observations, such as "Yes" for cooperative behavior or "No" for difficulties with cooperation.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that best describe the patient's treatment plan, such as "Yes" for ongoing therapy or "No" for no further treatment needed.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Provide the date of the assessment.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the speech therapist who completed the form.
* **Review Completed Date** (`review_completed_date`, date, required): Provide the date the form was reviewed.
* **Speech Therapist** (`speech_therapist`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the speech therapist who completed the form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Have the patient sign the form to confirm they have reviewed and understood the assessment.
* **Speech Therapist Signature** (`speech_therapist_signature`, text, required): Have the speech therapist sign the form to confirm they have completed the assessment accurately.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or procedures they have undergone.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's medications, such as "Yes" for taking medications or "No" for no medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's allergies, such as "Yes" for allergies or "No" for no allergies.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's medical condition, such as "Yes" for a medical condition or "No" for no medical condition.
* **Medical Procedure Result** (`medical_procedure_result`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the results of any medical procedures, such as "Yes" for successful outcome or "No" for unsuccessful outcome.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's treatment plan, such as "Yes" for ongoing therapy or "No" for no further treatment needed.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best describe the patient's outcome, such as "Yes" for improvement or "No" for no improvement.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe the next steps for the patient's treatment plan.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Provide the date the form was reviewed.

**Tips**

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the patient's information carefully before completing the assessment.
* Provide clear and concise recommendations for the patient's treatment plan.
* Sign the form to confirm it has been completed accurately.
* Review the form regularly to ensure the patient's treatment plan is on track.
