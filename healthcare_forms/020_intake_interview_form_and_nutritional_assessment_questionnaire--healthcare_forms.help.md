**intake_interview_form_and_nutritional_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide**
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history, contact details, and insurance coverage. It will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions and provide proper care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the intake form (page 1) with the patient's basic information, including their name, date of birth, height, and weight.
2. Next, select the patient's contact details, such as phone and email options.
3. Then, select the patient's blood types, medical conditions, allergies, and medications that apply.
4. After that, select the patient's insurance provider, insurance plan, and insurance effective and end dates.
5. Finally, fill out any additional medical notes, address, emergency contact information, and medical group details as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Intake Form

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date of birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height in Centimeters** (`height in centimeters`, number, required): Enter the patient's height in centimeters.
* **Weight in Kilograms** (`weight in kilograms`, number, required): Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.

### Page 2: Contact Details

* **Contact Details** (`contact details`, select_one, required): Select the patient's contact details by choosing either phone or email.
* **Blood Types** (`blood types`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's blood types by choosing from options A, B, and C.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's medical conditions by choosing from options disease and condition.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's allergies by choosing from options allergy1, allergy2, and allergy3.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's medications by choosing from options medication1, medication2, and medication3.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance provider`, select_one, required): Select the patient's insurance provider by choosing from options provider1, provider2, and provider3.
* **Family History** (`family history`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's family history by choosing from options family history1, family history2, and family history3.
* **Social History** (`social history`, select_multiple, required): Select the patient's social history by choosing from options social history1, social history2, and social history3.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical notes`, text, required): Enter any additional medical notes or comments about the patient.
* **Next of Contact** (`next of contact`, text, required): Enter the next of contact person's name and relationship.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency contact name`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency contact phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency contact relationship`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's relationship.
* **Insurance Payer** (`insurance payer`, select_one, required): Select the patient's insurance payer by choosing from options payer1, payer2, and payer3.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance plan`, select_one, required): Select the patient's insurance plan by choosing from options plan1, plan2, and plan3.
* **Insurance Effective Date** (`insurance effective`, date, required): Enter the insurance effective date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Insurance End Date** (`insurance end`, date, required): Enter the insurance end date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Group** (`medical group`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical group.
* **Other Medical Group** (`other medical group`, text, required): Enter any other medical group details.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent from the patient to collect and use their personal and medical information.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information has been collected.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please consult with the patient or a supervisor for clarification.
