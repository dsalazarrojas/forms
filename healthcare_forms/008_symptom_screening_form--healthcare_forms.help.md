# Symptom Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Symptom Screening Form is a tool used to assess and record a patient's medical history, symptoms, and vital signs. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential information to make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions accurately and honestly, as they will help our healthcare team provide the best possible care for you.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an `*` symbol, as they are crucial for your assessment.
3. If you're unsure about any questions, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom Screening Form** (`symptom_screening_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can describe your symptoms and any concerns you have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Share any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`): Provide more detailed information about your medical history, if necessary.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`): Indicate if you have any existing medical conditions or specialties, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Medical Specialty List** (`medical_specialty_list`): Select any medical specialties you have, like diabetes, high blood pressure, or asthma.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Answer if you have any medical conditions that require attention.
* **Patient Info List** (`patient_info_list`): List any medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Patient Info Details** (`patient_info_details`): Provide more information about your patient info, if necessary.
* **Patient Info Phone** (`patient_info_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Info Email** (`patient_info_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Patient Info Address** (`patient_info_address`): Provide your current address.
* **Patient Info City** (`patient_info_city`): Enter the city where you live.
* **Patient Info State** (`patient_info_state`): Enter the state where you live.
* **Patient Info Zip** (`patient_info_zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Patient Info Country** (`patient_info_country`): Enter your country of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_info_date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Visit** (`patient_info_time_of_visit`): Indicate the time of your visit.
* **Height** (`patient_info_height`): Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`patient_info_weight`): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Body Temperature** (`patient_info_body_temperature`): Enter your current body temperature in degrees Fahrenheit or Celsius.
* **Heart Rate** (`patient_info_heart_rate`): Enter your current heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Respiratory Rate** (`patient_info_respiratory_rate`): Enter your current respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Blood Pressure** (`patient_info_blood_pressure`): Enter your current blood pressure readings.
* **Allergies** (`patient_info_allergies`): List any allergies you have.
* **Medications** (`patient_info_medications`): List any medications you're currently taking.
