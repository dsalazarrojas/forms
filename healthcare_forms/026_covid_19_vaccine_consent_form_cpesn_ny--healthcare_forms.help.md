# COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form CPESN NY - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before administering the COVID-19 vaccine. It ensures that patients understand the benefits and risks of the vaccine and acknowledge their consent to receive it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read and understand the form**: Before completing the form, read through each section carefully to ensure you understand the information being asked.
2. **Complete each section**: Each section is designed to collect specific information from you. Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. **Sign and date the form**: When you have completed the form, sign and date it to confirm your consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth to determine your eligibility for the vaccine.
* **Patient ID or Social Security Number** (`patient_id`, text, optional): If you have a patient ID number or Social Security Number, please enter it. This is not required.
* **Pharmacy or Healthcare Facility Name** (`pharmacy_name`, text, required): Please enter the name and contact information of the pharmacy or healthcare facility where you will receive the vaccine.
* **Pharmacist or Healthcare Provider Name** (`pharmacist_name`, text, required): Please enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider administering the vaccine.
* **Patient Education** (`education_section`, note, required): This section provides information about the COVID-19 vaccine, its benefits, and potential risks. Please read and understand this section carefully.
* **Received COVID-19 Vaccine Information Fact Sheet** (`received_fact_sheet`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have received and read the vaccine information fact sheet.
* **Understood Information** (`understood_information`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you have read and understood the vaccine information provided.
* **Given Opportunity to Ask Questions** (`questions_allowed`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you were given the opportunity to ask questions and had the chance to do so.
* **Medical History Screening** (`medical_history_section`, note, required): This section screens for potential medical history that may impact your eligibility for the vaccine.
* **Allergies to Vaccine Components** (`allergies_vaccine_components`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have allergies to any vaccine components.
* **Describe Allergies (if yes)** (`allergies_describe`, text, optional): If you have allergies, please describe them briefly.
* **History of Severe Allergic Reaction or Anaphylaxis** (`severe_reaction_prior`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have a history of severe allergic reaction or anaphylaxis.
* **Currently Ill with Fever or Acute Illness** (`current_illness`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are currently ill with a fever or acute illness.
* **Immunocompromised or on Immunosuppressive Therapy** (`immunocompromised`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are pregnant or planning to become pregnant.
* **Breastfeeding Status** (`breastfeeding_status`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you are breastfeeding.
* **Prior Vaccination History** (`prior_vaccine_section`, note, required): This section records your prior vaccination history, including vaccine types and dates.
* **Previously Received COVID-19 Vaccine** (`prior_covid_vaccine`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you have previously received the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Prior Vaccine Type (if yes)** (`prior_vaccine_type`, text, optional): If you have received the vaccine, please specify the type.
* **Date of Prior Dose** (`prior_vaccine_date`, date, optional): If you have received the vaccine, please enter the date of your prior dose.
* **Adverse Reaction to Prior COVID Vaccine** (`prior_adverse_reaction`, select_one, required): Please select "True" if you experienced an adverse reaction to the prior COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Describe Reaction (if yes)** (`adverse_reaction_describe`, text, optional): If you experienced a reaction, please describe it briefly.
* **Consent Agreement** (`consent_agreement_section`, note, required): This section outlines the terms and conditions of the consent.
* **Understand Benefits and Risks** (`understand_benefits_risks`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you understand the benefits and risks of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Acknowledge Known and Unknown Risks** (`acknowledge_risks_known`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you acknowledge the known and potential risks of the vaccine.
* **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Please select "I consent" to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Right to Refuse** (`right_to_refuse`, select_one, required): Please select "I understand" if you understand that you can refuse vaccination without penalty.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Please select "I agree" if you release the pharmacist and pharmacy from liability.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of your consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by patients before receiving the COVID-19 vaccine. The fields and sections are intended to inform patients about the vaccine and obtain their consent, as well as their medical history and prior vaccination history.
