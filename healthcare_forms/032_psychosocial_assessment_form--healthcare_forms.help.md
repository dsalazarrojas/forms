# Psychosocial Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's psychosocial well-being, including their emotional state, sleep patterns, and experience with various mental health conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you are unsure about any question, you may leave it blank or describe your situation in the notes section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **How Do You Feel?** (text)
* **how_do_you_feel** (`how_do_you_feel`, text, optional): Describe your current emotional state. This could be a brief explanation of how you feel today, including any emotional state you have experienced recently.
*   Note: This field is optional, so feel free to skip it if you prefer not to describe your current emotional state.

### **Assessment Frequency** (number)
* **assessment_frequency** (assessment_frequency, number, required): Answer how often you have experienced the following conditions in the past. Use the following scale:
    * 0: Never
    * 1: Rarely (1-2 times)
    * 2: Sometimes (3-5 times)
    * 3: Often (6-10 times)
    * 4: Always (more than 10 times)
*   Note: This question is required to assess how often you have experienced the following conditions.

### **What Domain?** (select_one)
* **what_domain** (what_domain, select_one, required): Choose the domain that best describes your current condition:
    * Anxiety
    * Depression
    * Trauma
    * Mood Disorder
    * Psychosis
    * Addiction
    * ADHD
    * Other
*   Note: This question is required to help us understand which domain you are inquiring about.

### **Emotional State** (date)
* **emotional_state** (emotional_state, date, optional): Note the date when your current emotional state changed or began to be problematic for you.
*   Note: This field is optional, so feel free to skip it if you don't have a specific date in mind.

### **Sleep Patterns** (text)
* **sleep_patterns** (sleep_patterns, text, optional): Describe your sleep patterns in the past week. This could include any changes you have noticed in your sleep schedule or quality.
*   Note: This field is optional, so feel free to skip it if you prefer not to describe your sleep patterns.

### **Anxiety** (time)

    * 0: Never
    * 1: Rarely (1-2 times)

    * 3: Often (6-10 times)
    * 4: Always (more than 10 times)
