# medical_supplies_usage_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_supplies_usage_report_form is designed to collect information on the usage and availability of medical supplies. This form helps with inventory management and restocking decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Review the options for the "Products Used" and "Products to Restock" fields, selecting all applicable products.
3. Enter the correct truck number in the "Truck Number" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
5. If you have any additional comments or information to provide, enter them in the "Submitter Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Usage Report**: A text field where you can enter a brief report on the usage of medical supplies.
* **Products Used**: A multiple-choice field where you can select all the products that have been used. Select 'Yes' for each product that has been used.
* **Products to Restock**: A multiple-choice field where you can select all the products that need to be restocked. Select 'Yes' for each product that is low in stock.
* **Truck Number**: A text field where you can enter the number of the truck or shipment that the supplies were delivered on.
* **Submitter Name**: A text field where you can enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Notes**: A text field where you can enter any additional comments or information about the report.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the options for the "Products Used" and "Products to Restock" fields to ensure all relevant products are selected.
* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to the relevant staff member for clarification.
