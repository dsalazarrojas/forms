# Summer Camp Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Health Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential health-related information about campers attending our summer camp program. This data helps us to ensure the health, safety, and well-being of all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please provide all requested information for each section.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Info (Camper Information)** (`camper_info`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the camper, including their name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the camper's emergency contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Allergies (Allergies)** (`allergy_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any allergies or food sensitivities the camper has, such as Peanut, Tree, Shellfish, Milk, Eggs, Wheat, Soy, Fish, Crustacean, or Sulfites.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions or health concerns the camper has, such as epilepsy, diabetes, or other chronic conditions.
* **Meds (Medications)** (`medication_list`, text, required/optional): List any regular medications the camper is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Vaccination History (Vaccination History)** (`vaccination_history`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last vaccination the camper received, or check the "No vaccinations required" box if applicable.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the camper's medical history, including any relevant medical procedures or allergies.
* **Doctor Info (Doctor Information)** (`doctor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the camper's attending physician, if applicable.
