# Level Of Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Level Of Care Assessment Form is a crucial tool used to gather information about a patient's medical status, history, and needs. This form helps healthcare providers assess the patient's level of care requirements, ensuring that they receive the necessary care and attention to maintain their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all necessary patient information, including their medical history, medications, allergies, and any other relevant details.
2. Fill in the patient's information in the corresponding fields, making sure to include any relevant medical history, current medications, and allergies.
3. Select the relevant medical specialty and provider from the options provided.
4. Enter the patient's email and phone number for easy contact.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Patient Information** (1)
* (patient_info, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant demographic information.

### **Medical History** (2)
* (medical_history, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.

### **Nursing Level** (3)
* (nursing_level, select_one, optional): Select the appropriate nursing level based on the patient's needs:
	+ Value 1: Low
	+ Value 2: Moderate
	+ Value 3: High

### **Rehabilitation Programs** (4)
* (rehab_programs, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant rehabilitation programs or services the patient requires:
	+ Value 1: Value 2
	+ Value 3

### **Signature** (5)
* (signature, text, optional): Enter your signature as the healthcare provider completing this form.

### **Patient Signature** (6)
* (patient_signature, text, optional): Enter the patient's signature, confirming they have reviewed and understand the information provided.

### **Date** (7)
* (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.

### **Time** (8)
* (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.

### **Medical Notes** (9)
* (medical_notes, text, optional): Provide any additional medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.

### **Medical Notes 2** (10)
* (medical_notes_2, text, optional): Enter any further medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.

### **Medical Notes 3** (11)
* (medical_notes_3, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.

### **Email** (12)

### **Phone** (13)
