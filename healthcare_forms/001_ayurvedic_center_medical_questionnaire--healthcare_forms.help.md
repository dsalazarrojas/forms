# Ayurvedic Center Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients seeking Ayurvedic treatment at our center. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that our medical staff can provide the best possible care for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2.  Select your **Gender** from the options provided.
3.  Provide the **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** in case we need to contact you during treatment.
4.  List your **Primary Health Concern**, **Current Symptoms**, **Medical History**, **Current Medications**, and **Allergies**.
5.  Answer **Yes/No** questions regarding your **Ayurvedic Experience** and **Consent to Treatment**.
6.  Rate your **Stress Level** on a scale of 1 to 10.
7.  Describe your **Treatment Goals** and **Additional Comments or Questions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender identity from the options provided.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): We will use this to contact you, so ensure it is accurate.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Include your area code in the phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): In case of emergency during treatment, please provide us with a contact person and their phone number.
*   **Primary Health Concern** (`primary_concern`, text, required): Describe the main reason you are seeking Ayurvedic treatment.
*   **Current Symptoms** (`current_symptoms`, text, required): List any symptoms you are experiencing now.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Share any chronic conditions or past surgeries.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): Include dosage and frequency of your current medications.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List food, medication, and environmental allergies.
*   **Previous Ayurvedic Treatment Experience** (`ayurvedic_experience`, select_one, required): Answer **Yes** if you have received Ayurvedic treatment before, **No** if it is your first time, or **Not sure** if you are unsure.
*   **Lifestyle Habits** (`lifestyle_habits`, text, optional): Describe your diet, exercise patterns, and sleep routines.
*   **Stress Level** (`stress_level`, number, required): Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, text, required): Define what you hope to achieve with Ayurvedic treatment.
*   **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Answer **Yes** if you consent to Ayurvedic treatment.
*   **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips

*   Please be accurate and honest while filling this form, as this information will help our medical staff to tailor the best treatment plan for you.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask our staff for clarification.
*   This form is designed to be completed once per patient visit. If you have any updates to your information, please inform our staff.
