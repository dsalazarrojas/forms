# Health Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients' medical history, symptoms, and treatment plans before their medical consultation appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your personal details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter your medical record number, date, and time if applicable.
3. Select the type of medical record from the provided options.
4. Describe your symptoms and medical conditions.
5. List any medications you are currently taking.
6. Specify any allergies you have.
7. Note any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
8. Enter any additional medical consultation notes.
9. Schedule follow-up appointments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for follow-up appointments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up appointments.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `number`, required): Enter your medical record number, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your most recent medical record, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Time** (`medical_record_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your most recent medical record, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Type** (`medical_record_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of medical record from the provided options.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, optional): Describe your current symptoms, if any.
* **Medical Records** (`medical_records`, `text`, optional): List any previous medical records, if applicable.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies you have, if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Note any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medical Consultation Notes** (`medical_consultation_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your medical consultation.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Schedule a follow-up appointment if necessary.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, optional): Schedule a follow-up appointment if necessary.
* **Follow-up Remarks** (`follow_up_remarks`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional follow-up remarks.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Schedule the next follow-up appointment if necessary.
* **Next Follow-up Time** (`next_follow_up_time`, `time`, optional): Schedule the next follow-up appointment if necessary.
* **Next Follow-up Remarks** (`next_follow_up_remarks`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional next follow-up remarks.
* **Medical Consultation Type** (`medical_consultation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of medical consultation from the provided options.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your current medical provider, if applicable.
* **Medical Staff** (`medical_staff`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your medical staff, if applicable.
* **Health Coach** (`health_coach`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your health coach, if applicable.
* **Medical Record Status** (`medical_record_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your medical record from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for patients only, please do not complete this form if you are a healthcare provider.
