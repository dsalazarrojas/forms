# mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health survey is a tool used to gather information about an individual's mental health. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals to assess an individual's mental health status, identify potential areas of concern, and provide personalized recommendations for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by completing the pages in the order they appear.
2. For each page, read the questions carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
3. Use the provided options to select the most appropriate response for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: mental_health_survey_1_page
* **mental_health_survey_1_label (Note)**: Please provide any additional comments or information about your mental health that you feel is relevant to this survey. This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional information you feel is important.

### Page 2: mental_health_survey_2_page
* **mental_health_survey_2_label (Select Multiple)**: How often do you experience anxiety? Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes' 
	+ 'No'

### Page 3: mental_health_survey_3_page
* **mental_health_survey_3_label (Text)**: Can you describe your typical stressors? Please provide as much detail as possible.

### Page 4: mental_health_survey_4_page
* **mental_health_survey_4_label (Date)**: When did you first start noticing your current mental health concerns? Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Page 5: mental_health_survey_5_page
* **mental_health_survey_5_label (Time)**: What time of day do you usually feel your best? Please enter the time of day in the format 12:00 AM, 1:00 AM, etc.

### Page 6: mental_health_survey_6_page
* **mental_health_survey_6_label (Email)**: Do you have a healthcare provider? If so, what is their name and contact information? Please enter your healthcare provider's name and email or phone number for follow-up.

### Page 7: mental_health_survey_7_page
* **mental_health_survey_7_label (Text)**: Are there any specific mental health concerns or issues you are currently experiencing? Please provide as much detail as possible.

### Page 8: mental_health_survey_8_page
* **mental_health_survey_8_label (Note)**: Is there anything else you would like to add or discuss about your mental health? Please use this field to provide any additional thoughts or concerns you have.
