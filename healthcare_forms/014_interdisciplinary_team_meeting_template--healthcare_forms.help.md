# interdisciplinary_team_meeting_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interdisciplinary_team_meeting_template form is designed to help teams schedule and document their meetings. It provides a structured format for capturing essential information about each team meeting, including date, start and end times, location, topic, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the meeting date from the calendar input field.
2. Enter the start and end times of the meeting.
3. Enter the location of the meeting.
4. Choose who will be participating in the meeting (Team Members) and what tool will be used (Assigned Tool) if applicable.
5. Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.
6. Enter any additional meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Date (1)
* Meeting Date (text, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the meeting. This field is optional, but it's recommended to fill it for reference and organization purposes.

### Meeting Start Time (2)
* Meeting Start Time (time, required/optional: false): Enter the start time of the meeting.

### Meeting End Time (3)
* Meeting End Time (time, required/optional: false): Enter the end time of the meeting.

### Meeting Location (4)
* Meeting Location (text, required/optional: false): Enter the physical or virtual location of the meeting.

### Team Members (5)
* Team Members (select_multiple, required/optional: false): Choose who will be participating in the meeting. This field is optional, but it's useful for tracking attendance and coordination.

### Meeting Topic (6)
* Meeting Topic (text, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.

### Meeting Notes (7)
* Meeting Notes (text, required/optional: false): Enter any additional notes or information about the meeting that you'd like to capture.

### Assigned Tool (8)
* Assigned Tool (text, required/optional: false): If applicable, enter the tool or resource assigned to the team for this meeting.
