> # Home Health Aide Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Aide Skills Checklist is used to assess the skills and abilities of home health aides in the field of healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the appropriate category from the "category" field.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter a brief description of yourself in the "description" field.
4. Enter your address in the "address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`form_2_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your role as a home health aide. This will help us understand your expertise and experience.
* **Name** (`form_4_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Description** (`form_3_name`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your qualifications, experience, and skills related to home health care.
* **Address** (`form_7_name`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_8_name`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`form_9_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly, as the information will be used to evaluate your suitability for providing care.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or administrator for guidance.
* If you need help with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
