# Hospice Nursing Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Nursing Assessment Form is a tool used in a hospice care setting to collect information about a patient's medical history, condition, medication, and social support needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field to understand what information is required.
2. Provide answers to each question based on the patient's situation.
3. Enter the answers in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to indicate whether the patient has a terminal condition (`Yes`) or not (`No`).
* **Pain Management** (`pain_management`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select all applicable pain management options for the patient (`Yes`, `No`).
* **Social Support** (`social_support`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select all applicable social support options for the patient (`Yes`, `No`).
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a list of the patient's current medication.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Hospice ID** (`hospice_id`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the patient's Hospice ID number.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the patient's ID number.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure accurate assessment of the patient's situation.
* If a field is not applicable, select "None" or leave it blank.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
