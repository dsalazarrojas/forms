# SBAR Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SBAR Form is a medical form designed to collect detailed information from patients about their symptoms, medical history, and treatment plans. It helps medical professionals gather essential data for patient care and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the patient's name**: Enter the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
2. **Provide symptom start date**: Enter the date when the patient's symptoms first started in the "symptom start date" field.
3. **Specify body area affected**: Describe the area of the body affected by the symptoms in the "body area affected" field.
4. **Name the medical provider**: Enter the name of the medical professional who provided care or is providing care in the "medical provider" field.
5. **Describe symptoms**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms in the "description of symptoms" field.
6. **Bar**: Leave this field blank, as it's not intended for patient input.
7. **Concerns**: If there are any concerns or questions, enter them in this field.
8. **Patient medical history**: Share any relevant medical history in the "patient medical history" field.
9. **Allergy information**: Enter any relevant allergy information, if applicable.
10. **Medication list**: List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "medication list" field.
11. **Medical test ordered**: Enter any medical tests ordered by the medical provider in this field.
12. **Diagnosis**: Enter the diagnosis made by the medical provider.
13. **Treatment plan**: Describe the treatment plan recommended by the medical provider in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient name**: Enter the patient's name, as recorded on their identification documents or as known to you.
* **symptom start date**: Enter the date when the patient's symptoms first began, to track the progression of the condition.
* **body_area_affected**: Describe the specific area of the body affected by the symptoms, e.g., head, neck, back, etc.
* **medical_provider**: Enter the name of the medical professional who provided care or is providing care to the patient.
* **description_of_symptoms**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's symptoms, including severity and duration.
* **bar**: This field is not intended for patient input; please leave it blank.
* **concerns**: Share any concerns or questions the patient may have, such as fears, expectations, or requests.
* **patient_medical_history**: Share any relevant medical history, including previous conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **allergy_info**: Enter any relevant allergy information, including allergens and severity.
* **medication_list**: List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **medical_test_ordered**: Enter any medical tests ordered by the medical provider.
* **diagnosis**: Enter the diagnosis made by the medical provider.
* **treatment_plan**: Describe the treatment plan recommended by the medical provider, including any specific instructions or recommendations.
