# Patient Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient referral form is a tool for healthcare providers to refer patients to other healthcare services, ensuring that all relevant information is shared with the receiving provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Provide the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
4.  If applicable, provide the patient's referral reason in the "Referring Physician" field.
5.  If applicable, enter the patient's medical diagnosis in the "Patient Diagnosis" field.
6.  List any known allergies the patient has in the "Patient Allergies" field.
7.  Enter any current medications the patient is taking in the "Patient Medications" field.
8.  Record any contact information for the patient's contacts, such as family members or guardians, in the "Patient Contacts" field.
9.  Finally, enter the name and address of the referring healthcare provider in the "Doctor Name" and "Doctor Address" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth to ensure accurate record-keeping.
*   **Patient Address** (`Patient Address`, text, required): Enter the patient's home address.
*   **Referring Physician** (`Referring Physician`, text, optional): If you're referring the patient from your practice, enter your own name.
*   **Patient Diagnosis** (`Patient Diagnosis`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical diagnosis information.
*   **Patient Allergies** (`Patient Allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies the patient has.
*   **Patient Medications** (`Patient Medications`, text, optional): Enter any current medications the patient is taking.
*   **Patient Contacts** (`Patient Contacts`, text, optional): Record any contact information for the patient's contacts, such as family members or guardians.
*   **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other healthcare provider names involved in the patient's care.
*   **Doctor Address** (`Doctor Address`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare practice's address, or the location where you'll be providing care for the patient.
