# Covid 19 Vaccine Exemption Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals and patients declare a Covid-19 vaccine exemption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Provide your reason for exemption in the "Reason For Exemption" section.
3. Select your exemption status from the options provided in the "Exemption Status" section.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" section.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Patient Phone" section.
6. Sign and date the form as the "Doctor Signature", "Assessor Signature", and "Medical Practitioner Signature" sections.
7. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" sections.
8. Enter your signature as the "Patient Signature" and "Assessor Signature" sections.
9. Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare professional who completed the form in the "Doctor", "Doctor Phone", "Medical Practitioner", and "Medical Practitioner Phone" sections.
10. Finally, review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information in this section.
- **Reason For Exemption** (`reason_for_exemption`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your exemption from the Covid-19 vaccine.
- **Exemption Status** (`exemption_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to indicate your exemption status.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your exemption.
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this section as the healthcare professional.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
- **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
- **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, required): Enter the time the form was completed.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, date, required): Sign and date this section as the patient.
- **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this section as the assessor.
- **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who completed the form.
- **Date Completed By** (`date_completed_by`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was completed by the healthcare professional.
- **Time Completed By** (`time_completed_by`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was completed by the healthcare professional.
- **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who completed the form.
- **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the healthcare professional who completed the form.
- **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this section as the doctor.
- **Assessor** (`assessor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assessor who completed the form.
- **Assessor Phone** (`assessor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the assessor who completed the form.
- **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this section as the assessor.
- **Medical Practitioner** (`medical_practitioner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical practitioner who completed the form.
- **Medical Practitioner Phone** (`medical_practitioner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the medical practitioner who completed the form.
- **Medical Practitioner Signature** (`medical_practitioner_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this section as the medical practitioner.
- **Patient** (`patient`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient who completed the form.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and review all sections before submission. If you need assistance or have questions, please contact your healthcare professional.
