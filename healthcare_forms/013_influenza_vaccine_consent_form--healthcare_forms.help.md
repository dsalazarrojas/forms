# Influenza Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influenza Vaccine Consent Form is a vital document that allows healthcare providers to obtain informed consent from patients before administering the influenza vaccine. This form ensures that patients are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the vaccine and provides a legal basis for the healthcare provider to administer the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and read the instructions provided.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Select the appropriate options for each field.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent To Treatment** (`Consent To Treatment`, select_multiple): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent for the influenza vaccine treatment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter your date of birth in the format 'MM/DD/YY'.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your primary contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text): Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Vaccination** (`date_of_vaccination`, date): Enter the date you will be receiving the influenza vaccine.
* **Time Of Vaccination** (`time_of_vaccination`, time): Enter the time you will be receiving the influenza vaccine.
* **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, text): Please describe the type of vaccination you will receive.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous illnesses you may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text): List any allergies you may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): If you selected 'Yes' for emergency contact, please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note): Please sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, note): Please sign and date the form to indicate your consent for the treatment.
* **Date Of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date): Please enter the date you sign the form.
* **Date Of Signature Timestamp** (`date_of_signature_timestamp`, date): Enter the timestamp of when you sign the form.
* **Consent To Treatment 2** (`Consent To Treatment 2`, select_multiple): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent for the treatment.

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before completing it.
- Answer each question accurately and honestly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider before signing the form.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
