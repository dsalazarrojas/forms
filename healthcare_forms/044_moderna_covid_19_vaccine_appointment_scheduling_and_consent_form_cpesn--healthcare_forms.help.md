# Moderna COVID 19 Vaccine Appointment Scheduling And Consent Form CPESN - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and consent for COVID-19 vaccination scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, address, and date of birth.
2. Provide your occupation and phone number for communication purposes.
3. Indicate if you have had the vaccine before and specify your health condition, if any.
4. If applicable, fill in your insurance details, including name, number, and provider.
5. Specify the dose number and type of vaccine you will receive.
6. Select the date and time of your vaccination appointment.
7. Sign the consent form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for communication and treatment purposes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Occupation**: Enter your current occupation or job title.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for appointment reminders and updates.
* **Dose Number**: Enter the number of doses you will receive.
* **Number Of Doses**: Enter the total number of doses you are scheduled to receive.
* **Date Of Vaccination**: Enter the date of your scheduled vaccination appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Vaccination**: Enter the time of your scheduled vaccination appointment.
* **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine you will receive (Moderna COVID-19 vaccine, for example).
* **Have You Had Vaccine Before**: Check if you have had the vaccine before. If yes, provide details if applicable.
* **Health Condition**: If you have any health conditions, please specify.
* **Insurance Name**: Enter your insurance name (if you have insurance).
* **Insurance Number**: Enter your insurance number (if you have insurance).
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Term 1**: Enter term 1 as per the healthcare provider's requirement.
* **Term 2**: Enter term 2 as per the healthcare provider's requirement.
* **Term 3**: Enter term 3 as per the healthcare provider's requirement.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

Note: Please fill in the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
