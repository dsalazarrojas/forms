# Vividness Of Visual Imaginary Questionnaire (VVIQ) - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vividness Of Visual Imaginary Questionnaire (VVIQ) is a series of questions designed to assess an individual's experiences with visual imagery, including their frequency, intensity, and impact on daily life.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question truthfully, based on your personal experiences.
2.  For questions with options, select the response that best describes your experiences.
3.  For questions that ask for specific events or memories, provide detailed descriptions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How often do you experience vivid dreams about visual scenes?** (`vividness_score_1`, `number`, required): This question asks about the frequency of your vivid dreams about visual scenes. Please answer with a number that best describes how often you experience such dreams.
*   **Can you recall a visual image that you had in childhood?** (`question_2`, `text`, required): Describe a visual image that you recall from your childhood.
*   **Do you experience visual imagery when falling asleep?** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that best describes your experiences with visual imagery when falling asleep.
*   **Do you experience visual imagery when falling asleep?** (`question_4`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is similar to the previous one, but it asks about the frequency of your experiences.
*   **Have you ever had a vivid visual hallucination during sleep?** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your experiences with visual hallucinations during sleep.
*   **Do you experience visual imagery when listening to music?** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that best describes your experiences with visual imagery when listening to music.
*   **Can you recall a vivid visual memory?** (`question_7`, `date`, required): Provide the date of a vivid visual memory that you can recall.
*   **How often do you see mental images when thinking about a specific task or problem?** (`question_8`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the frequency of your visual imagery when thinking about specific tasks or problems.
*   **Have you had a vivid mental image that interfered with your ability to fall asleep?** (`question_9`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your experiences with vivid mental images that interfere with falling asleep.
*   **Have you experienced a change in your visual imagination during periods of stress or anxiety?** (`question_10`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that best describes your experiences with changes in your visual imagination during stress or anxiety.
*   **Do you experience mental images when your eyes are closed?** (`question_11`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your experiences with mental images when your eyes are closed.
*   **Can you recall a vivid mental image that was very intense?** (`question_12`, `text`, required): Describe a vivid mental image that you can recall and was very intense.
