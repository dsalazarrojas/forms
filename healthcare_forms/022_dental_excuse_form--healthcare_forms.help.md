# Dental Excuse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a student's absence from school due to a dental appointment or procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's name and ID number in the respective fields. Make sure to enter the student's name as it appears on their school record.
2. Enter the reason for the student's absence in the "Reason for Absence" field. Please be as specific as possible about the reason for the absence.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the student's absence in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields. Make sure to enter the dates accurately and in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. If applicable, enter the name of the doctor or medical professional who treated the student in the "Doctor Name" field.
5. If applicable, enter the name of the clinic or medical facility where the treatment took place in the "Clinic Name" field.
6. Enter any relevant notes or comments from the doctor in the "Doctor Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the student as it appears on their school record.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required):
	+ This field is used to enter the student's ID number, which is used to identify them in the school's system.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the reason for the student's absence. Please be as specific as possible about the reason for the absence.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
	+ This field is used to enter the start date of the student's absence. Make sure to enter the date accurately and in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required):
	+ This field is used to enter the end date of the student's absence. Make sure to enter the date accurately and in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the doctor or medical professional who treated the student.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the clinic or medical facility where the treatment took place.
* **Doctor Note** (`doctor_note`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter any relevant notes or comments from the doctor.
