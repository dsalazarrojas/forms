# Sleep Deprivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Deprivation Survey is designed to collect information about a patient's sleep habits and patterns, helping healthcare professionals understand their sleep disorders and provide personalized recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, as your responses will help healthcare professionals understand your sleep habits and provide guidance for improvement.
2. Select all applicable answers for each question, as multiple responses are allowed for some questions.
3. Enter numerical values accurately, such as the number of hours you sleep.
4. Enter specific times according to the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
5. Complete all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you sleep?** (`sleep_duration`, `select_multiple`):
	+ This question asks you to select all applicable times of the day when you usually sleep.
	+ Examples: Morning, Afternoon, Late
* **How long do you sleep?** (`sleep_length`, `select_one`):
	+ Choose the category that best describes your usual sleep duration.
	+ Examples: 0-4 hours, 4-6 hours, 6-8 hours, More than 8 hours
* **Do you have tiredness?** (`tiredness`, `select_multiple`):
	+ Check all the symptoms that you experience due to sleep deprivation.
	+ Examples: Fatigue, Difficulty concentrating, Memory problems, Mood changes
* **How much do you sleep?** (`sleep_quantity`, `number`):
	+ Enter the number of hours you usually sleep per day.
	+ Ensure accurate entry, as this will help healthcare professionals understand your sleep habits.
* **Do you usually sleep?** (`sleep_timing`, `select_one`):
	+ Choose the frequency of your sleepiness.
	+ Examples: Always, Sometimes, Rarely, Never
* **What time do you wake up?** (`wake_up_time`, `time`):
	+ Enter the time you usually wake up, using the 24-hour clock format.
	+ Example: 08:00 (8:00 AM)
* **What time do you go to bed?** (`bedtime`, `time`):
	+ Enter the time you usually go to bed, using the 24-hour clock format.
	+ Example: 22:00 (10:00 PM)
