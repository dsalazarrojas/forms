# hipaa_notice_of_privacy_practices_and_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and have them acknowledge that they have received the HIPAA notice of privacy practices. It is likely to be used in healthcare settings to ensure patients understand their rights and the practices in place to protect their personal information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the form in the **Form Title** field.
2. Select one or more categories that apply to this form. Choose "Healthcare Forms" if it is related to medical information or procedures.
3. Provide a short description of the form in the **Description** field, if necessary.
4. Enter a brief description of the form in the **Form Description** field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the form, e.g., "HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices and Acknowledgement".
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more categories that apply to this form, such as "Healthcare Forms" for medical information or procedures.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, required/optional): This field is not required and can be left blank. If you want to provide additional information about the form, enter a brief description here.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required/optional): This field is also not required and can be left blank. If you need to provide more details about the form, enter a brief description here.
