# Hometown Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hometown Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist form is used to collect and manage contact information and position on the waitlist for patients seeking COVID-19 vaccines or treatment. This form is a digital representation of the manual process, allowing healthcare staff to efficiently track and contact patients when their turn is reached.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

## Tips

* Ensure the email and phone fields are up-to-date and active to receive timely notifications.
* If you experience issues while completing the form, contact your healthcare staff for assistance.
