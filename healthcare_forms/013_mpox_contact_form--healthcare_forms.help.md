# Mpox Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Contact Form is used to collect information from individuals who may have been exposed to mpox. This form helps healthcare professionals identify potential cases and take necessary actions to prevent the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Date of Birth.
3. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or other).
4. If you selected 'other' as your contact method, provide a brief description in the 'Other' field.
5. Enter your address.
6. Provide the date of exposure to mpox.
7. Enter the duration of exposure to mpox.
8. Describe any symptoms you're experiencing.
9. Share your travel history.
10. Provide any relevant contact history.
11. (Optional) If you'd like to be contacted at a different address or by a different method, specify in the 'Contact Details' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, or other).
* **Other** (`contact_method_other`, text, optional): If you selected 'other' as your contact method, provide a brief description of your preferred contact method.
* **Email** (`contact_method_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address if you selected 'email' as your contact method.
* **Phone** (`contact_method_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you selected 'phone' as your contact method.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where you can be contacted.
* **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`, date, required): Enter the date of exposure to mpox.
* **Exposure Duration** (`exposure_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of exposure to mpox.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe any symptoms you're experiencing.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): Share your travel history.
* **Contact History** (`contact_history`, text, required): Provide any relevant contact history.
* **Exposure Location** (`exposure_location`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the location of exposure to mpox.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted at a different address or by a different method, specify in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is for public health purposes only, and the information collected will be used to help prevent the spread of mpox.
