# Surgical Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients or potential patients undergoing surgery in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information for each field.
* Enter the applicant's contact information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* Provide a brief description of the applicant's medical history.
* Share information about the applicant's family.
* Describe the surgical procedure in detail.
* Write a review of the applicant's case.
* Add any additional details about the applicant's review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (`applicant_info`)**: Enter the applicant's name, address, and other contact details.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`)**: Provide the applicant's contact information, including phone number, email, and other relevant details.
* **Medical History (`medical_info`)**: Briefly describe the applicant's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Family Information (`family_info`)**: Share information about the applicant's family, including any significant relationships or concerns.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`)**: Describe the applicant's medical history in more detail, including any previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or significant medical events.
* **Surgical Information (`surgical_info`)**: Describe the surgical procedure in detail, including the type of surgery, expected risks, and any other relevant information.
* **Review (`review`)**: Write a brief review of the applicant's case, including any concerns or recommendations.
* **Review Details (`review_details`)**: Add any additional details about the applicant's review, including any supporting evidence or research.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and truthfully.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the applicant's medical history and surgical procedure.
* Use a clear and concise writing style when writing the review and review details.
