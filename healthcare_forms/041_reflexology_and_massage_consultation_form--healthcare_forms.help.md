# Reflexology And Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reflexology And Massage Consultation Form is used to gather information about your health, massage preferences, and medical history to ensure a safe and effective massage experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information section with your name, contact details, and any relevant information about your visit.
2. In the Medical History section, describe your medical conditions, allergies, or any previous medical experiences that might impact your massage.
3. Indicate your massage preferences in the Massage Preferences section.
4. Answer the Allergies question truthfully, specifying any allergies or sensitivities you have.
5. In the Medical Conditions section, list any medical conditions that might affect your massage experience.
6. Add any notes for the massage therapist in the Massage Therapist Notes section.
7. Finally, sign and date the Client Signature section to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required): This section collects your name, contact details, and any relevant information about your visit.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Please provide us with your contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): This section is for describing any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical experiences that might impact your massage experience.
* **Massage Preferences** (Massage Preferences, text, optional): Please let us know about your massage preferences, such as pressure, duration, or areas to focus on.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have, such as fragrances, essential oils, or other substances.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions that might affect your massage experience, such as recent injuries, surgeries, or medical procedures.
* **Massage Therapist Notes** (Massage Therapist Notes, text, optional): Add any notes for the massage therapist, such as specific areas to focus on or any medical equipment needed.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, note, optional): Sign and date this section to confirm the accuracy of your submitted information.
* **Massage Services** (Massage Services, select multiple, optional): Choose the massage services you would like to receive, such as aromatherapy or hot stone massage.
