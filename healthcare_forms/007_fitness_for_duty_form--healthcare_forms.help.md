# fitness_for_duty_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's fitness for duty, which means their ability to perform their job safely and without any physical or mental limitations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information about the employee's current status and any related medical conditions or injuries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **employee_name** (employee_name, text, optional)
Enter the name of the employee being assessed for fitness for duty.

### 2. **employee_id** (employee_id, text, optional)
Enter the ID number of the employee being assessed for fitness for duty.

### 3. **supervisor_name** (supervisor_name, text, optional)
Enter the name of the supervisor or person responsible for assessing the employee's fitness for duty.

### 4. **date_of_injury** (date_of_injury, date, optional)
Enter the date of the injury or medical condition that is affecting the employee's fitness for duty.

### 5. **work_status** (work_status, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
* available
* not available
* not at all

### 6. **work_status_date** (work_status_date, date, optional)
Enter the date on which the employee is expected to return to work, if applicable.

### 7. **medical_provider** (medical_provider, text, optional)
Enter the name and title of the medical provider who assessed the employee's fitness for duty.

### 8. **medical_certification** (medical_certification, text, optional)
Enter the medical certification or documentation number issued by the medical provider.

### 9. **fitness_for_duty** (fitness_for_duty, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
* fit
* not fit
* unable to determine

### 10. **reason_for_unfitness** (reason_for_unfitness, text, optional)
Enter the reason why the employee is unfit for duty.

### 11. **return_to_work_date** (return_to_work_date, date, optional)
Enter the date on which the employee is expected to return to work, if applicable.

### 12. **comments** (comments, note, optional)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the employee's fitness for duty.

### 13. **supervisor_signature** (supervisor_signature, text, optional)
Enter the signature of the supervisor or person responsible for assessing the employee's fitness for duty.

### 14. **supervisor_name** (supervisor_name, text, optional)
Enter the name of the supervisor or person responsible for assessing the employee's fitness for duty.

### 15. **employee_signature** (employee_signature, text, optional)
Enter the signature of the employee being assessed for fitness for duty.

### 16. **date** (date, date, optional)
Enter the date on which this form is being completed.

### 17. **time** (time, time, optional)
Enter the time on which this form is being completed.

### 18. **supervisor_title** (supervisor_title, text, optional)
Enter the title or position of the supervisor or person responsible for assessing the employee's fitness for duty.

### 19. **employee_title** (employee_title, text, optional)
Enter the title or position of the employee being assessed for fitness for duty.

### 20. **employee_id** (employee_id, text, optional)
Enter the ID number of the employee being assessed for fitness for duty.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct option for "work_status" to reflect the employee's current status.
* If the employee is unfit for duty, be sure to enter the reason for unfitness.
* Enter the correct signature of the supervisor and employee to indicate their agreement and understanding of the employee's fitness for duty.
