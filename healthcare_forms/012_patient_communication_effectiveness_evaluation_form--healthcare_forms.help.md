# Patient Communication Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of patient communication. It captures patient feedback, communication quality, and satisfaction with healthcare providers in different categories. The form is designed to provide an objective measure of patient communication and help healthcare providers identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form (Field 1: Form Title).
2. Select the category of the form (Field 2: Category).
3. Enter a brief description of the form (Field 3: Description).
4. Select the category (Field 4: Category 1) that you want to evaluate (e.g., Healthcare, Medical, Other).
5. Provide feedback on your experience with the healthcare provider (Field 5: Feedback 1).
6. Indicate the quality of communication during your visit (Field 6: Quality 1).
7. Rate your satisfaction with the healthcare provider (Field 7: Satisfaction 1).
8. Enter the name of the healthcare provider you saw (Field 8: Healthcare Provider 1).
9. Enter any additional information about the healthcare provider (Field 9: Healthcare Provider 2).
10. Select the date of the visit (Field 10: Date 1).
11. Select the time of the visit (Field 11: Time 1).
12. Provide any additional notes about the visit (Field 12: Note 1).
13. Enter your email address (Field 13: Email 1).
14. Enter your phone number (Field 14: Phone 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (Field 1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title of the form to identify the purpose of the evaluation.
* **Category (Field 2)** (`text`, required/optional): Select the category of the form to categorize the evaluation (e.g., Healthcare, Medical, Other).
* **Description (Field 3)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the form to provide context.
* **Category 1 (Field 4)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more categories to evaluate (e.g., Healthcare, Medical, Other).
* **Feedback 1 (Field 5)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Provide feedback on your experience with the healthcare provider (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Quality 1 (Field 6)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate the quality of communication during your visit (e.g., Communication, Information, Explanation, Support, Education, Other).
* **Satisfaction 1 (Field 7)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the healthcare provider (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Healthcare Provider 1 (Field 8)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider you saw.
* **Healthcare Provider 2 (Field 9)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the healthcare provider.
* **Date 1 (Field 10)** (`date`, required/optional): Select the date of the visit.
* **Time 1 (Field 11)** (`time`, required/optional): Select the time of the visit.
* **Note 1 (Field 12)** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes about the visit.
* **Email 1 (Field 13)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone 1 (Field 14)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
