# Physician Release To Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record a physician's statement regarding a patient's work status after a medical evaluation. It should only be used by healthcare providers to document their professional opinion and should not be used for other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the three options for the "Work Status" field: Physically fit, Physically limited, or Permanently disabled.
2. If you are reporting that the patient is Physically limited, enter a brief description of the symptoms in the text field below.
3. Enter the name of the physician who is completing the form.
4. Sign the form with your signature, which serves as an electronic signature.
5. Enter the date the form was signed.
6. If you have a medical license number, enter it in the corresponding field.
7. Enter the date of the last medical examination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Status**: Select one of the three options provided. If you select "Physically limited", you will be prompted to enter a brief description of the symptoms in the text field below.
* **Symptoms**: This field is only required if you have selected "Physically limited" for the "Work Status" field. Enter a brief description of the symptoms that prevent the patient from returning to work.
* **Physician Name**: Enter your name as a healthcare provider.
* **Physician Signature**: This field is where you sign the form, serving as an electronic signature.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date on which you signed this form.
* **Physician License Number**: If you have a medical license number, enter it in this field.
* **Date of Last Examination**: Enter the date of the last medical examination.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose and not for any other reason.
* Complete the form accurately and honestly.
* This form is not a substitute for a detailed medical evaluation or a medical record.
