# ivf patient information form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVF Patient Information Form is designed to collect essential data about you, our patient, to ensure accurate and personalized IVF treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out this form, please note that this is a crucial document for your treatment process. You are advised to answer all questions accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required/optional): If you have a medical record number, please enter it here.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your last medical visit.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, required/optional): If you have any allergies, please describe them here.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply:
	+ I have read and understood the consent form
	+ Option 2
	+ I have not read and understood the consent form
* **Medical Specialist** (`medical_specialist`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your primary medical specialist:
	+ Gynecologist
	+ Urologist
	+ Cardiologist
	+ Nephrologist
	+ Gastroenterologist
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply:
	+ Private Insurance
	+ Public Insurance
	+ No Insurance
* **Medical Test** (`medical_test`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply:
	+ Ultrasound
	+ Blood test
	+ X-ray
* **Blood Group** (`blood_group`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your blood group:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ AB
	+ O
* **Height** (`height`, number, required/optional): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required/optional): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, required/optional): Enter your blood pressure reading.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required/optional): Describe any additional medical history.
* **Doctor Specialist 2** (`doctor_specialist_2`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Specialist 2** (`medical_specialist_2`, select_one, required/optional): Please select your primary medical specialist:
	+ Gynecologist 2
	+ Urologist 2
	+ Cardiologist 2
	+ Nephrologist 2
	+ Gastroenterologist 2
* **Medical Test 2** (`medical_test_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply:
	+ Ultrasound 2
	+ Blood test 2
	+ X-ray 2
* **Medical Specialist 3** (`medical_specialist_3`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, required/optional): Describe any additional medical history.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or doubts, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* Ensure that your answers are complete and up-to-date.
* If you have any medical records or documents, please attach them to this form.
