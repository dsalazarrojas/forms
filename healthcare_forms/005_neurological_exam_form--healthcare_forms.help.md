# Neurological Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's neurological examination. It is designed to be used in a healthcare setting to aid healthcare professionals in their assessment and diagnosis of neurological conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
3. Enter the date of the examination in the "Date of Exam" field.
4. Provide details about the exam location in the "Exam Location" field.
5. Describe the exam procedure in the "Exam Procedure" field.
6. Enter the results of the neurological examination in the "Neurological Examination" field.
7. Provide a description of the cranial exam in the "Cranial Exam" field.
8. Enter the results of the neurological system exam in the "Neurological System Exam" field.
9. Enter the results of the motor exam in the "Motor Exam" field.
10. Enter the results of the sensory exam in the "Sensory Exam" field.
11. Provide a description of the cranial caverns exam in the "Cranial Caverns Exam" field.
12. Enter the results of the cerebellar exam in the "Cerebellar Exam" field.
13. Enter the results of the brainstem exam in the "Brainstem Exam" field.
14. Provide a description of muscle strengthening exercises in the "Muscle Strengthening" field.
15. Enter any muscle tension observed during the examination in the "Muscle Tension" field.
16. Enter any muscle spasticity observed during the examination in the "Muscle Spasticity" field.
17. Enter any muscle weakness observed during the examination in the "Muscle Weakness" field.
18. Enter the results of the motor function assessment in the "Motor Function" field.
19. Enter the results of the sensory function assessment in the "Sensory Function" field.
20. Provide a description of the mental status in the "Mental Status" field.
21. Enter any comments about the mental status in the "Mental Status Comments" field.
22. Enter any cranial injuries observed during the examination in the "Cranial Injuries" field.
23. Enter any cranial surgery performed on the patient in the "Cranial Surgery" field.
24. Enter any trauma observed during the examination in the "Trauma" field.
25. Enter any other relevant information in the "Other" field.
