# Teletherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for teletherapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the information provided.
2. Fill in your personal details in the "Patient Information" section.
3. Provide information about your therapy sessions and treatment goals in the "Therapy Information" section.
4. Answer the questions about your treatment details in the "Treatment Details" section.
5. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the form in the "Informed Consent" section.
6. If applicable, provide information about an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact" section.
7. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the contents.
8. If you are a healthcare provider, sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the contents.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**:
	+ This section collects your personal details, including your name, contact information, and other relevant information.
	+ Please fill in your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Therapy Information (page 1)**:
	+ This section is used to describe the therapy sessions you will be attending, including the frequency, duration, and goals of the sessions.
	+ Please provide details about your therapy sessions, including the type, frequency, and duration of the sessions.
* **Treatment Details (page 2)**:
	+ This section provides information about the treatment you will receive, including the type, risks, and benefits.
	+ Please read and understand this section carefully before signing the form.
* **Informed Consent (page 2)**:
	+ This section is used to check that you have read and understand the form's contents.
	+ Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the form.
* **Emergency Contact (page 2)**:
	+ If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name, contact information, and relationship to you.
	+ This information will only be used in case of an emergency.
* **Patient Signature (page 3)**:
	+ This section is used to sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the contents.
	+ Please sign the form in the provided space.
* **Health Care Provider (page 3)**:
	+ If you are a healthcare provider, please review and sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the contents.
* **Health Care Provider Signature (page 3)**:
	+ This section is used to sign the form as a healthcare provider.
* **Submit (page 4)**:
	+ Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please read the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
