# Simple Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Prescription Form is a basic form used to collect information for prescriptions that do not require special attention or follow-up actions. It is designed for everyday prescriptions and helps healthcare providers gather essential information efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the **Medication Name** and **Dosage** for the prescribed medication.
3. Indicate the **Frequency** of the medication (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
4. Provide the **Prescription Date**, which is the date the prescription was written.
5. Enter a **Prescription Note** for any additional information about the medication or patient.
6. Choose the **Prescription Status** (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter the **Prescriber Name**, who is the healthcare provider issuing the prescription.
8. Enter the **Patient Phone** number for easy communication.
9. Enter the **Provider Phone** number for follow-up actions.
10. If applicable, enter the **Date of Last Refill** and **Refill Count** for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Medication Name (medication_name)**: Type the name of the prescribed medication.
* **Dosage (dosage)**: Enter the dosage instructions for the medication, e.g., "2 pills per day".
* **Frequency (frequency)**: Indicate how often the medication should be taken, e.g., "daily", "weekly", etc.
* **Prescription Date (prescription_date)**: Enter the date the prescription was written in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Prescription Note (prescription_note)**: Add any additional information about the medication or patient.
* **Prescription Status (prescription_status)**: Choose whether the prescription is Active or Inactive.
* **Prescriber Name (prescriber_name)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider issuing the prescription.
* **Patient Phone (patient_phone)**: Enter the patient's phone number for easy communication.
* **Provider Phone (provider_phone)**: Enter the healthcare provider's phone number for follow-up actions.
* **Date of Last Refill (date_of_last_refill)**: If applicable, enter the date of the last refill for the medication.
* **Refill Count (refill_count)**: If applicable, enter the total number of refills remaining.
