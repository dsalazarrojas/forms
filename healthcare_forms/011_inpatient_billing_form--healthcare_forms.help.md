# Inpatient Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for inpatient billing purposes. It is intended for healthcare providers to submit billing information for services provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the billing provider in the "Billing Provider" field.
3. Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of the service in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
5. Enter the length of the service in the "Service Length" field.
6. Enter the diagnosis in the "Diagnosis" field.
7. Enter the charges for the room, physician, nurse, lab, pharmacy, and radiology services in the respective fields.
8. Enter any additional fees for "Other" services, if applicable.
9. Select the payment method for the service from the "Payment Method" dropdown.
10. Enter the payment status as "Paid" or "Outstanding".
11. If the payment method is "Card" or "Insurance", enter the payment date.
12. Finally, please sign the "Patient Signature", "Doctor Signature", and "Billing Provider Signature" fields to confirm that the information is accurate and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Billing Provider** (2): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is submitting this form.
* **Date of Service** (3): Enter the date on which the service was provided.
* **Start Time** (4): Enter the start time of the service.
* **End Time** (5): Enter the end time of the service.
* **Service Length** (6): Enter the length of time the service was provided.
* **Diagnosis** (7): Enter the diagnosis of the patient's condition.
* **Room Charge** (9): Enter the charge for the room service.
* **Physician Fee** (10): Enter the fee charged by the physician.
* **Nurse Fee** (11): Enter the fee charged by the nurse.
* **Lab Fee** (12): Enter the fee charged by the lab.
* **Pharmacy Fee** (13): Enter the fee charged by the pharmacy.
* **Radiology Fee** (14): Enter the fee charged by the radiology department.
* **Other Fee** (15): Enter any additional fees, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (20): Select the payment method from the dropdown options (Card, Insurance, or Cash).
* **Payment Status** (21): Enter the payment status as "Paid" or "Outstanding".
* **Payment Date** (22): Enter the date of payment, if applicable.
* **Patient Signature** (23): Sign and date the patient's signature to confirm that the information is accurate and correct.
* **Doctor Signature** (24): Sign and date the doctor's signature to confirm that the information is accurate and correct.
* **Billing Provider Signature** (25): Sign and date the billing provider's signature to confirm that the information is accurate and correct.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly to avoid any errors or discrepancies during the billing process. If you are unsure about any of the information required, please consult with your healthcare team or contact our billing department for guidance.
