# healthcare_provider_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare provider recommendation form is used to capture the healthcare provider's assessment of the patient's needs and make informed recommendations for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the **Name** field.
2. Select the relevant specialty(ies) from the **specialty** field.
3. Choose the department that best aligns with the patient's needs from the **department** field.
4. Select the medical group that will be providing care from the **medical_group** field.
5. Enter the healthcare provider's contact information in the **contact_number** and **contact_email** fields.
6. Enter the patient's address in the **address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **specialty**: Select all specialties that apply to the patient's needs. For example, if the patient is a cardiac patient, select both 'Yes' and 'cardiac' to indicate that the patient requires both cardiology and other specialties.
* **department**: Select the department that best aligns with the patient's needs. For example, if the patient is requiring general care, select 'General' and if it's more specific, select a specialized department like 'Cardiovascular'.
* **medical_group**: Select the medical group that will be providing care to the patient. For example, if the patient is requiring primary care, select 'Yes' for primary care and other groups that will be involved in the patient's care.
* **contact_number**: Enter the healthcare provider's contact number. This will be used to reach out for further information or clarification.
* **contact_email**: Enter the healthcare provider's email address. This will be used for further communication and clarification.
* **address**: Enter the patient's address. This will be used to send important information or communicate with the patient.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before completing the form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the patient receives the best care.
* If you are unsure of any information, contact the patient's primary contact for clarification.
