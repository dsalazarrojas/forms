# Massage Therapy Session Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about massage therapy sessions, including client details, session specifics, and billing and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Massage Therapist's name in the "Massage Therapist" field.
2. Enter the client's name and details in the "Client Name" field.
3. Select the session date using the date picker.
4. Enter the session time.
5. Select the massage modality.
6. Enter the session location.
7. Enter the duration of the massage session.
8. Record any session notes, if necessary.
9. Enter whether the client has insurance coverage and select the insurance company.
10. Enter the insurance card number.
11. Enter the expiration date of the insurance card.
12. Indicate whether the client has given their consent.
13. Enter the billing amount.
14. Enter any billing notes, if necessary.
15. Enter the client's signature.
16. Enter the massage therapist's signature.
17. Enter the therapist's name.
18. Enter the therapist's phone number.
19. Enter the therapist's email address.
20. Enter the therapist's address.
21. Enter the therapist's license number.
22. Enter any additional therapist notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Massage Therapist**: Enter the name of the massage therapist who conducted the session.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who received the massage therapy session.
* **Session Date**: Select the date on which the massage therapy session was conducted.
* **Session Time**: Enter the start and end time of the massage therapy session.
* **Massage Modality**: Select the type of massage modality used during the session (e.g. "Yes" for a specific type of massage or "No" for no modality used).
* **Session Location**: Enter the location where the massage therapy session was conducted.
* **Massage Duration**: Enter the length of the massage session in minutes or hours.
* **Session Notes**: Record any notes about the session, if necessary.
* **Billing Insurance**: Indicate whether the client has insurance coverage.
* **Billing Method**: Select the method used for billing (e.g. "Yes" for electronic billing or "No" for manual billing).
* **Payment Status**: Indicate the payment status of the session (e.g. "Active" for paid or "Inactive" for unpaid).
* **Billing Amount**: Enter the total amount to be billed for the session.
* **Billing Notes**: Record any billing notes, if necessary.
* **Client Signature**: Obtain the client's signature, if applicable.
* **Massage Therapist Signature**: Obtain the massage therapist's signature, if applicable.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of the massage therapist who conducted the session.
* **Therapist Phone**: Enter the phone number of the massage therapist.
* **Therapist Email**: Enter the email address of the massage therapist.
* **Therapist Address**: Enter the address of the massage therapist.
* **Therapist License Number**: Enter the license number of the massage therapist.
* **Therapist Notes**: Record any notes from the massage therapist, if necessary.
* **Insurance Company**: Enter the insurance company the client is covered by.
* **Insurance Card Number**: Enter the number of the client's insurance card.
* **Insurance Card Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the client's insurance card.
* **Client Consent**: Indicate whether the client gave their consent for the session.
