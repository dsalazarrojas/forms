# Physician Supervision Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Supervision Agreement form is used to document the supervision arrangements between physicians, including supervision start date, frequency, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the practice address in the "Practice Address" field.
2. Enter the physician's name in the "Physician Name" field.
3. Select the supervision start date from the calendar.
4. Choose the supervision frequency from the options (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5. Enter the supervision duration.
6. The "Physician Signature" field is intended for the physician to sign and date the agreement.
7. Enter the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
8. Enter the supervisor's title in the "Supervisor Title" field.
9. Sign and date the "Supervisor Signature" field.
10. Enter the practice name in the "Practice Name" field.
11. Select the date for the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Address**: Enter the address where the practice is located.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician who will be under supervision.
* **Supervision Start Date**: Select the date when supervision will begin.
* **Supervision Frequency**: Choose how often the physician will be supervised (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Supervision Duration**: Enter the duration of the supervision arrangement.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign and date the agreement for the physician.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person supervising the physician.
* **Supervisor Title**: Enter the title of the person supervising the physician.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date the agreement for the supervisor.
* **Practice Name**: Enter the name of the practice where the physician is working.
* **Date**: Select the date for the agreement.
