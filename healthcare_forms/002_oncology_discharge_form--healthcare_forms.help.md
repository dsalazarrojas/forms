# Oncology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oncology Discharge Form is used to document the discharge of a patient from the oncology department. It gathers information about the patient's name, medical history, treatment details, medication list, vital signs, allergies, family history, social support, mental status, lab tests, imaging studies, patient care goals, discharge date, follow-up care, discharge status, next appointment, next appointment time, patient phone, patient email, healthcare provider details, and discharge summary. This form is used to ensure that all relevant information is documented before discharging the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's name on page 1.
* Provide the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
* List the patient's current medications in the "Medication List" field.
* Enter the patient's vital signs in the "Vital Signs" field.
* Indicate any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
* Provide information about the patient's family history in the "Patient Family History" field.
* Describe any social support the patient has in the "Social Support" field.
* Select the patient's mental status in the "Mental Status" field.
* Indicate whether lab tests were performed in the "Lab Tests" field.
* Indicate whether imaging studies were performed in the "Imaging Studies" field.
* Specify the patient's care goals in the "Patient Care Goals" field.
* Enter the discharge date in the "Discharge Date" field.
* Indicate whether the patient will need follow-up care in the "Follow-up Care" field.
* Select the discharge status in the "Discharge Status" field.
* Enter the next appointment date in the "Next Appointment" field (if applicable).
* Enter the next appointment time in the "Next Appointment Time" field (if applicable).
* Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
* Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
* Enter the healthcare provider's name in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
* Enter the healthcare provider's phone number in the "Healthcare Provider Phone" field.
* Enter the healthcare provider's email address in the "Healthcare Provider Email" field.
* Enter the name of the medical group in the "Medical Group" field (if applicable).
* Enter a discharge summary in the "Discharge Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
### 2. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required)
Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or surgeries.
### 3. **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, required)
List any treatment details, such as medication regimens or therapy sessions.
### 4. **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, required)
Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
### 5. **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, optional)
Report any side effects of the patient's medications.
### 6. **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, number, required)
Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.
### 7. **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional)
Select any allergies the patient has.
### 8. **Patient Family History** (`patient_family_history`, text, optional)
Provide information about the patient's family history.
### 9. **Social Support** (`social_support`, text, optional)
Describe the patient's social support system.
### 10. **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, select_one, optional)
Select the patient's mental status from the provided options.
### 11. **Lab Tests** (`lab_tests`, select_one, optional)
Select whether lab tests were performed.
### 12. **Imaging Studies** (`imaging_studies`, select_one, optional)
Select whether imaging studies were performed.
### 13. **Patient Care Goals** (`patient_care_goals`, text, required)
Specify the patient's care goals.
### 14. **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
### 15. **Follow-up Care** (`follow_up_care`, select_multiple, optional)
Select whether the patient will need follow-up care.
### 16. **Discharge Status** (`discharge_status`, select_one, required)
Select the patient's discharge status from the provided options.
### 17. **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, optional)
Enter the date of the next appointment (if applicable).
### 18. **Next Appointment Time** (`next_appointment_time`, time, optional)
Enter the time of the next appointment (if applicable).
### 19. **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required)
Enter the patient's phone number.

Enter the patient's email address.
### 21. **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
### 22. **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
### 23. **Healthcare Provider Email** (`healthcare_provider_email`, email, optional)
Enter the healthcare provider's email address.
### 24. **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the medical group.
### 25. **Discharge Summary** (`discharge_summary`, text, required)
Enter a summary of the patient's discharge.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the patient's details to ensure accuracy and up-to-date information.
* Enter the discharge date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Select the correct options for lab tests, imaging studies, mental status, and discharge status.
* Enter the correct contact information for the patient and healthcare provider.
