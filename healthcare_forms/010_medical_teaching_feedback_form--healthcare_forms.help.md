# Medical Teaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Teaching Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from students about their medical teaching experience. This feedback is used to help instructors improve their teaching methods and provide better learning experiences for their students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with your full name.
2. Enter your Student Email address if you'd like to receive a copy of your submission.
3. Select the Faculty who led your medical teaching experience from the Faculty dropdown menu.
4. Enter the Course Name where the medical teaching experience took place.
5. Provide detailed feedback about your experience in the Feedback field.
6. Optionally, select the date of submission.
7. Submit your feedback by clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive a copy of your submission. (optional)
* **Faculty**: Select the Faculty member who led your medical teaching experience. Please choose one of the options listed.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the Course where the medical teaching experience took place.
* **Feedback**: Provide specific, detailed, and constructive feedback about your experience. This will help the instructor understand your concerns and areas for improvement.
* **Faculty**: Unfortunately, this label is a repeat of a previous field, likely an error in the form design. Please disregard and submit your feedback.
* **Submission**: This field is for the instructor's use in date-stamping your feedback. Please do not worry about completing this field.
