# patient_health_questionnaire_phq9 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "patient_health_questionnaire_phq9," is a health assessment tool designed to gather information from patients about their health status, mental health, and social determinants. It's essential to fill it out accurately to help healthcare professionals understand the patient's health better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **demographic information** page (page 1) with your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Proceed to the **general health questionnaire** page (page 2) and answer questions related to your health status, such as overall health, medical conditions, and healthcare utilization.
3. Move on to the **mental health questionnaire** page (page 3) and respond to questions about anxiety, depression, and other mental health aspects.
4. Answer the **social problems** page (page 4) questions regarding social determinants that might affect your health.
5. Continue to the **pain** page (page 5) and provide information about any pain you're experiencing.
6. The **anxiety** page (page 6) is a continuation of the mental health questionnaire, focusing on anxiety-related topics.
7. The **depression** page (page 7) also continues the mental health questionnaire, focusing on depression-related topics.
8. The **sleep** page (page 8) is a separate section to assess your sleep patterns and quality.
9. The **phq9 total score** page (page 9) will calculate your total score based on previous answers.
10. The **mental health profiling** page (page 10) is a general assessment of your mental health.
11. The **social determinants** page (page 11) is an evaluation of social factors that impact your health.
12. Finally, the **phq9 score** page (page 12) will display your overall phq9 total score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Demographic Information**: Please provide your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information. This section is crucial for healthcare professionals to reach out to you for any follow-up questions or care.
* **General Health Questionnaire**: Answer questions about your health status, medical conditions, and healthcare utilization. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* **Mental Health Questionnaire**: Respond to questions related to anxiety, depression, and other mental health aspects. Please be honest about your feelings and experiences.
* **Social Problems**: Answer questions about social determinants that might affect your health, such as relationships, finances, and environment.
* **Pain**: Provide information about any pain you're experiencing, including location, intensity, and impact on daily activities.
* **Anxiety**: Answer questions about anxiety-related topics, such as frequency, intensity, and impact on daily life.
* **Depression**: Respond to questions about depression-related topics, such as mood, interest, and energy levels.
* **Sleep**: Assess your sleep patterns and quality, including duration, difficulty falling asleep, and restlessness.
* **phq9 Total Score**: This section will calculate your total phq9 score based on previous answers.
* **Mental Health Profiling**: This is a general assessment of your mental health, based on your previous answers.
* **Social Determinants**: Evaluate social factors that impact your health, such as relationships, finances, and environment.
* **phq9 Score**: This will display your overall phq9 total score.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
* Be sure to keep your answers private and secure.
