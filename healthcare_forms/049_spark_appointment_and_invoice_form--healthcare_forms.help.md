# Spark Appointment And Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information for appointments and invoices in the context of healthcare services, allowing for streamlined and standardized data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "appointment-details" field to enter a brief description of the appointment.
2. Choose the "appointment-rate" from the available options.
3. Enter the "therapist-name" in the text field.
4. Provide the "appointment-duration" in numerical format.
5. Enter the "costing-amount" in numerical format.
6. Select the "invoice-date" using the date picker.
7. Enter any additional "notes" about the appointment in the text field.
8. Select the "invoice-status" from the available options.
9. Enter the "invoice-email" address of the client.
10. Enter the "phone-number" of the client.
11. Select the "appointment-type" from the available options.
12. Select the "payment-method" from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appointment Details (appointment-details)** (`appointment-details`, `text`, `false`): A field for entering a brief description of the appointment.
* **Appointment Rate (appointment-rate)** (`appointment-rate`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the rate of the appointment from the available options.
* **Therapist Name (therapist-name)** (`therapist-name`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of the person performing the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration (appointment-duration)** (`appointment-duration`, `number`, `false`): Enter the duration of the appointment in numerical format.
* **Costing Amount (costing-amount)** (`costing-amount`, `number`, `false`): Enter the amount charged for the appointment in numerical format.
* **Invoice Date (invoice-date)** (`invoice-date`, `date`, `false`): Select the date of the invoice using the date picker.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, `note`, `false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
* **Invoice Status (invoice-status)** (`invoice-status`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the status of the invoice from the available options.
* **Invoice Email (invoice-email)** (`invoice-email`, `email`, `false`): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Phone Number (phone-number)** (`phone-number`, `text`, `false`): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Appointment Type (appointment-type)** (`appointment-type`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the type of appointment from the available options.
* **Payment Method (payment-method)** (`payment-method`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the payment method from the available options.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth processing of appointments and invoices.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to prevent errors.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please consult your organization's policies and procedures or seek assistance from a qualified personnel.
