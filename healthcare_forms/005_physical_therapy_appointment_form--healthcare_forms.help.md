# Physical Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects and processes patient data for physical therapy appointments, enabling healthcare professionals to manage patient records and appointments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the appointment time and date for your physical therapy sessions.
3. Confirm your appointment details, including start and end dates.
4. Provide your therapist's ID and your patient ID (if applicable).
5. Choose your preferred appointment date and time.
6. Select the payment method and amount (if applicable).
7. Update your payment status and add any additional notes (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: Enter your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Appointment Time (appointment_time)**: Select the time of your physical therapy appointment.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Choose the start date of your appointment.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Choose the end date of your appointment.
* **Therapist ID (therapist_id)**: Enter your therapist's ID number.
* **Patient ID (patient_id)**: Enter your patient ID number (if assigned by the practice).
* **Appointment Date (appointment_date)**: Select the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time (appointment_time)**: Select the time of your appointment.
* **Booking ID (booking_id)**: Enter your booking ID (if applicable).
* **Status (status)**: Select the status of your appointment from the options: Pending, Confirmed, Rescheduled, or Cancelled.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select one or more payment methods (if applicable).
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount)**: Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* **Payment Status (payment_status)**: Select the payment status from the options: Paid, Pending, or Refunded.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment (if necessary).
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have already been verified as a legitimate patient or client of the physical therapy practice before filling out this form.
* If you have previously filled out this form, please reference your existing data to avoid duplicating your information.
* Please select the correct payment method and status for your appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your appointment, please contact your healthcare professional.
