# E Visits Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the E Visits Informed Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information to complete it accurately.
2.  Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the consent statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **E Visits Informed Consent Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form that you are about to complete.
    *   This field is the title of the form and should be completed as is.
*   **E-visits Methods** (`e_visits_method`, text, required): This field asks you to choose the method of E-visits you are about to undergo.
    *   Please select the method that best describes your situation. This could be virtual, in-person, or another method.
*   **E Visits Privacy** (`e_visits_privacy`, text, required): This field asks about your understanding of the privacy of your personal and medical information.
    *   Please select the option that best describes your understanding of your privacy.
*   **E Visits Protection** (`e_visits_protection`, text, required): This field asks about your understanding of the protection of your personal and medical information.
    *   Please select the option that best describes your understanding of your protection.
*   **E-Visits Informed Consent Statement** (`e_visits_consent_statement`, text, required): This is a statement that you will read and understand before proceeding with the E-Visits.
    *   Please read this statement carefully and acknowledge that you have understood it.
*   **E-Visits Informed Consent Statement Part 1**, **E-Visits Informed Consent Statement Part 2**, **E-Visits Informed Consent Statement Part 3** (`e_visits_consent_statement_part_1`, `e_visits_consent_statement_part_2`, `e_visits_consent_statement_part_3`, text, required): These three parts are continuations of the statement that you need to understand before proceeding with the E-Visits.
    *   Please read these parts carefully and acknowledge that you have understood them.
*   **E Visits Informed Consent Form Footer** (`e_visits_informed_consent_form_footer`, text, required): This is the footer of the form where you can add any additional comments or questions.
    *   If you have any questions or concerns, please add them here.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
*   Review the form carefully before signing and dating it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or medical staff.
