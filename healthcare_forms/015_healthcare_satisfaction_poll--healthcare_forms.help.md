# Healthcare Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Healthcare Satisfaction Poll" form, which is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with our healthcare services. This form is meant to help us understand what we've done well and what we can improve, so we can provide better care in the future. Please answer these questions to help us understand your thoughts and feelings about your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each page of questions to the best of your ability. You will be asked a series of questions, and each page will have a specific set of questions related to your experience. Be as thorough as possible and provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: What Was Your Last Visit Like?
* **what_was_your_last_visit_like** (`text`, Optional): This field is asking you to describe your last visit to the healthcare facility. Please provide a brief account of what happened during your visit, including any notable events or interactions that stood out to you.

### Page 2: Rate Your Hospital Experience
* **rate_your_hospital_experience** (`select_one`, Required): Here, you will be asked to rate your overall experience at the hospital on a scale of Very Good, Good, Bad, or Extremely Bad. Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings about your visit.

### Page 3: What Did We Do Differently?
* **what_did_we_do_differently** (`text`, Optional): In this field, we are asking for any suggestions or ideas you have for how we can improve our services. This could be anything from changing our procedures, offering additional amenities, or even just a simple tip on how to make your experience more enjoyable.

### Page 4: What Did We Do Differently 2
* **what_did_we_do_differently_2** (`text`, Optional): This is another field for any additional suggestions or ideas you have on how we can improve our services. If you didn't think of anything for the previous field, feel free to share anything here as well.

### Page 5: How Would You Rate Your Overall Experience
* **how_would_you_rate_your_overall_experience** (`number`, Optional): Here, you will be asked to rate your overall experience with a number from 1 to 5. Please rate your experience based on your feelings about your visit, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### Page 6: What Can We Improve
* **what_we_can_improve** (`text`, Optional): This field is asking for any areas where you think we can improve. Please be as specific as possible and provide any details that will help us understand how to better serve you in the future.

### Page 7: Do You Have Any Suggestions
* **do_you_have_any_suggestions** (`text`, Optional): This is the final field for any additional comments or suggestions you have about how we can improve our services. If you have any thoughts, no matter how big or small, please share them with us.

### Page 8: 
* **page-8** (`text`, Optional): This field is left blank, as it seems there is no specific question or field to explain for this page. Please feel free to share any further comments or concerns you may have about your visit.
