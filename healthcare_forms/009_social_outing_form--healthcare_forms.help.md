# Social Outing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Social Outing Form" is used to document a patient's social outing or interaction with an outside environment, aiding healthcare professionals in understanding the patient's social recovery process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the patient's outing.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
3. Enter the date of the outing in the "outing date" field.
4. Enter the time of the outing in the "outing time" field.
5. Enter the location of the outing in the "outing location" field.
6. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient phone" field.
7. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient email" field.
8. Enter any relevant medical condition in the "medical condition" field.
9. Enter the doctor's name if involved with the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient name**: A text input field to record the patient's name.
- **outing date**: A date input field to record the date of the outing.
- **outing time**: A time input field to record the time of the outing.
- **outing location**: A text input field to record the location of the outing.
- **patient phone**: A text input field to record the patient's phone number.
- **patient email**: An email input field to record the patient's email address.
- **medical condition**: A text input field to record any relevant medical conditions.
- **doctor name**: A text input field to record the doctor's name if involved with the patient's care.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- Use clear and concise language when entering information.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or contact your supervisor for guidance.
