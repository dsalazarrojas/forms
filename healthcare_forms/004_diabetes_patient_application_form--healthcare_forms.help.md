# diabetes_patient_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diabetes Patient Application Form is used to collect medical information from patients with diabetes. This form is designed to help healthcare providers understand a patient's medical history, current health status, and treatment plan. Completing this form will aid in the assessment and management of your diabetes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all required questions.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask a healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (Text): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **medical_history** (Text): Report any medical conditions or health issues you are currently experiencing.
* **patient_info** (Text): Enter any relevant information about your patient status.
* **medical_conditions** (Select Multiple): Indicate if you have any of the following medical conditions:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **medications** (Select Multiple): Indicate if you are currently taking any of the following medications:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **medical_test_results** (Select Multiple): Indicate if you have had any of the following test results:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **diabetes_control** (Select One): Indicate your level of control over your diabetes:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **medical_history_family** (Select Multiple): Indicate if any of the following medical conditions affect your family:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **medications_family** (Select Multiple): Indicate if any of your family members are taking the following medications:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **medical_test_results_family** (Select Multiple): Indicate if any of your family members have had any of the following test results:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **diabetes_control_family** (Select One): Indicate your family's level of control over their diabetes:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **contact_info** (Text): Enter any relevant contact information for follow-up.
* **medical_info_family** (Text): Enter any relevant medical information about your family members.
* **medical_history_other** (Text): Report any other medical information that is relevant to your health.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you need help, ask a healthcare provider for assistance.
- This form will help healthcare providers better understand your medical status and develop a plan for your care.
