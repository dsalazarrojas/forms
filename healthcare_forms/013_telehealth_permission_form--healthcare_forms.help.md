# Telehealth Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain patient consent and healthcare provider permission for telehealth consultations or sharing patient records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date in the **Patient Name** and **Date** fields, respectively.
2. Enter the patient's email address and phone number in the **Email Address** and **Phone Number** fields, respectively.
3. Sign the **Patient Signature** field with the patient's signature.
4. Choose the healthcare provider's signature option in the **Healthcare Provider Signature** field, ensuring the selected option indicates the provider's licensure status.
5. Select the desired option for **Consent to Share** field, indicating whether to share records with the healthcare team or not.
6. Enter a brief reason for the telehealth call in the **Reason for Call** field.
7. Enter the healthcare provider's name and title in the **Healthcare Provider Name** and **Healthcare Provider Title** fields, respectively.
8. Read and agree to the **Consent Statement**, ensuring you understand the terms of the telehealth consent form.
9. Read and agree to the **Terms of Service**, ensuring you understand the terms governing the telehealth services.
10. Enter any relevant comments or notes for the patient's signature in the **Patient Signature Note** field, if necessary.
11. Enter any relevant comments or notes for the healthcare provider's signature in the **Healthcare Provider Signature Note** field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the patient's signature.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`Healthcare Provider Signature`, `select_one`, required): Choose the healthcare provider's signature option, ensuring the selected option indicates licensure status.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the telehealth call.
* **Reason for Call** (`Reason for Call`, `text`, required): Enter a brief reason for the telehealth call.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`Healthcare Provider Name`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **Healthcare Provider Title** (`Healthcare Provider Title`, `text`, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's title.
* **Telehealth Permission** (`Telehealth Permission`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired option for telehealth permission.
* **Consent Statement** (`Consent Statement`, `text`, optional): Read and agree to the consent statement.
* **Terms of Service** (`Terms of Service`, `text`, optional): Read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Patient Signature Note** (`Patient Signature Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes for the patient's signature.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature Note** (`Healthcare Provider Signature Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes for the healthcare provider's signature.
* **Consent to Share** (`Consent to Share`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired option for consent to share records.
