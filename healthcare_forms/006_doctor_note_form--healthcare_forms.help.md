# Doctor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare professionals about patient visits, focusing on essential details such as patient name, doctor name, date of service, and reason for absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the patient.
2. Enter your name as the doctor or healthcare professional.
3. Enter the date of the patient visit (using the date format, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Provide a brief explanation for the patient's absence (e.g., sick leave, vacation, or other valid reasons).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name
* **patient_name** (text): Enter the patient's full name.
* **doctor_name** (text): Enter your full name as the doctor or healthcare professional.
* **date_of_service** (date): Enter the date of the patient visit using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **reason_for_absence** (text): Provide a brief explanation for the patient's absence.

## Tips
* Always use the correct format for the date of service to ensure accurate data.
* Be concise in providing the reason for absence to avoid unnecessary details.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
