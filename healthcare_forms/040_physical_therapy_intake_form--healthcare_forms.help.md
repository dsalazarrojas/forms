# Physical Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Intake Form is a general intake form used to gather information for patients requiring physical therapy. The form is designed to collect various types of information from the patient, including demographics, medical history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal information on Page 1, including their name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
2. On Page 2, provide medical history, including any past injuries, medical conditions, and medications.
3. On Page 3, enter any necessary notes from the doctor and patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, age, and contact details. This information will help the physical therapist understand who the patient is and how to contact them.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant information about the patient's past injuries, medical conditions, or previous treatments. This information will help the doctor understand the patient's medical context.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the patient has, including any chronic conditions or allergies.
* **Medication**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Phone Numbers**: Enter any phone numbers for the patient or emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, relationship, and contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Insurance**: Enter the patient's insurance information, including the policy number and provider.
* **Work Status**: Indicate the patient's current work status.
* **Medical Provider**: Identify the patient's current medical provider (if applicable).
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the patient.
* **Doctor Notes**: Enter any notes from the doctor.
* **Medical History 2-6**: These fields are for additional medical history information.
* **Allergies 2**: If the patient has any allergies, list them here.
* **Medication 2**: List any additional medications the patient is taking.
* **Work 2**: If the patient's work status has changed, indicate it here.
* **Emergency Contact 2**: If the patient's emergency contact has changed, provide the new information.

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as possible for each field.
- If unsure about any information, please consult the patient or their medical provider.
- This form is for general information gathering and may be subject to revision based on additional information received during the consultation.
