# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about workplace safety and concerns from employees. It is intended for employees who have reported or are experiencing symptoms or concerns related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
2. Select the method through which you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In Person).
3. Provide the location of your workplace, if applicable.
4. Describe your current symptoms, if any.
5. Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing (Fever, Cough, Shortness of Breath).
6. If you have had a test, select the result (Positive, Negative, Pending).
7. Indicate if you are currently under quarantine or isolation.
8. Provide a brief description of any travel history, if applicable.
9. Indicate if you have been exposed to anyone with a confirmed case of COVID-19.
10. Provide any information on workplace safety measures taken to minimize exposure.
11. Follow up with any further instructions or details as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Workplace Location** (`Workplace Location`, `text`, optional): Provide the location of your workplace, if applicable.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, optional): Describe your current symptoms or concerns, if any.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing (Fever, Cough, Shortness of Breath).
* **Test Results** (`Test Results`, `select_one`, optional): If you have had a test, select the result (Positive, Negative, Pending).
* **Quarantine Status** (`Quarantine Status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are currently under quarantine or isolation.
* **Travel History** (`Travel History`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of any travel history, if applicable.
* **Exposure Status** (`Exposure Status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have been exposed to anyone with a confirmed case of COVID-19.
* **Workplace Safety Measures** (`Workplace Safety Measures`, `text`, optional): Provide any information on workplace safety measures taken to minimize exposure.
* **Follow Up Instructions** (`Follow Up Instructions`, `text`, optional): Follow up with any further instructions or details as requested.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm that you are providing accurate and true information.
