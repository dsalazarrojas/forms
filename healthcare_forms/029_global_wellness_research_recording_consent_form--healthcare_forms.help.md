# global_wellness_research_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consents for global wellness research recording in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the purpose and the information it collects.
2. Enter your participant information, including their contact method (email or phone).
3. Check the consent boxes indicating your willingness to be contacted (Yes/No) and your understanding of participant consent.
4. Provide your contact information for the form submitter, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**:
	+ (Participant Information, none, optional)
		- This section is a free-text field where you can enter participant information.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ (Contact Information, Select, optional)
		- Choose how you wish to be contacted (Yes/No).
* **Contact Method**:
	+ (Contact Method, Select, optional)
		- Select how you wish to be contacted (email or phone).
* **Participant Consent**:
	+ (Participant Consent, none, optional)
		- We value your privacy and confidentiality, indicating your understanding of this principle.
* **Contact Consent**:
	+ (Contact Consent, Select, optional)
		- Check the box if you are willing to be contacted (Yes/No).
* **Participant Consent Date**:
	+ (Participant Consent Date, Date, optional)
		- Enter the date on which you gave or will give participant consent.
* **Form Submitter**:
	+ (Form Submitter, Text, optional)
		- Enter your name or title, who submitted this form.
* **Form Submitter Phone**:
	+ (Form Submitter Phone, Text, optional)
		- Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this form.
* **Form Submitter Email**:
	+ (Form Submitter Email, Email, optional)
		- Enter the email address of the person who submitted this form.

## Tips
- Double-check your information and consents before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the form submitter's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please contact the relevant authorities or your healthcare provider.
