# Mpox Vaccine Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your eligibility for the MPOX vaccine. Please complete this form to help us assess your eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each question based on your situation.
2. Provide any additional information as required.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vaccine Eligibility Form** (Form 1, required: false)
	+ This field is for your convenience to describe your situation.
* **Do you have any of the following risk factors?** (Form 2, required: false)
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have any of the listed risk factors, or "No" if you do not.
* **What is your current health status?** (Form 3, required: false)
	+ Select "Good health" if you are healthy, "Poor health" if you have a medical condition, or leave blank if you are unsure.
* **Have you had any vaccinations in the past 5 years?** (Form 4, required: false)
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have had the MPOX vaccine or a similar vaccine, or "No" if you have not.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may affect your eligibility?** (Form 5, required: false)
	+ Select all that apply if you have any medical conditions listed, or add "Other" if you have a condition not listed.
* **Have you had the MPOX vaccine previously?** (Form 6, required: false)
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have had the MPOX vaccine, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you provided your medical documentation?** (Form 7, required: false)
	+ Answer "Yes" if you have uploaded or provided your medical documentation, or "No" if you have not.
* **Contact Information** (Form 8, required: false)
	+ Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Name** (Form 9, required: false)
	+ Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (Form 10, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Form 11, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Next Steps** (Form 12, required: false)
	+ This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Date of Birth** (Form 13, required: false)
	+ Enter your date of birth for reference purposes.
* **Medical Documentation File** (Form 14, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional information about your medical documentation.
* **Upload Medical Documentation** (Form 15, required: false)
	+ Upload your medical documentation as a file.
* **Address** (Form 16, required: false)
	+ Enter your address for follow-up purposes.
* **City** (Form 17, required: false)
	+ Enter your city for follow-up purposes.
* **State** (Form 18, required: false)
	+ Enter your state for follow-up purposes.
* **Zip Code** (Form 19, required: false)
	+ Enter your zip code for follow-up purposes.
