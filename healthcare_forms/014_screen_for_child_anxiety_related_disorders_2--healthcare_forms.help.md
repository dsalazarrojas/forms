# Screen For Child Anxiety Related Disorders 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Screen For Child Anxiety Related Disorders 2" form is designed to gather information about a child's mental health and identify potential anxiety-related disorders. This form is used to collect data for research and clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the child's information:
	* Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
	* Enter the child's age in the "Child Age" field.
	* Select the level of anxiety your child experiences on a scale of 1-3 in the "How Anxious" field.
	* Choose any fears your child may have from the "What Fears Do You Have?" field.
	* Select any sleep, eating, or social difficulties your child experiences from the "Sleep Difficulties", "Eating Difficulties", and "Social Difficulties" fields.
	* Indicate if you suspect your child has an anxiety disorder in the "Do You Suspect An Anxiety Disorder?" field.
2. Fill out your own contact information:
	* Enter your name in the "Parent Name" field.
	* Enter your contact information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name (Child Name, text, required)**: Enter the child's name to identify the child's information.
* **Parent Name (Parent Name, text, required)**: Enter your name to provide contact information.
* **Child Age (Child Age, number, required)**: Enter the child's age in years.
* **How Anxious (Anxiety Level, number, required)**: Select 1, 2, or 3 to indicate the level of anxiety your child experiences on a scale of 1-3.
* **What Fears Do You Have? (Fears, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose any fears your child may have from the available options.
* **Sleep Difficulties (Sleep Difficulties, note, optional)**: Describe any sleep difficulties your child experiences.
* **Eating Difficulties (Eating Difficulties, note, optional)**: Describe any eating difficulties your child experiences.
* **Social Difficulties (Social Difficulties, note, optional)**: Describe any social difficulties your child experiences.
* **Concentration Difficulties (Concentration Difficulties, note, optional)**: Describe any concentration difficulties your child experiences.
* **Do You Suspect An Anxiety Disorder? (Anxiety Disorder, select_one, optional)**: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" if you suspect your child has an anxiety disorder.
* **Anxiety Disorder Diagnosis (Anxiety Disorder Diagnosis, select_one, optional)**: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" for the type of anxiety disorder you suspect.
* **Anxiety Disorder Treatment (Anxiety Disorder Treatment, select_one, optional)**: Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" for the treatment option you prefer.
* **Anxiety Disorder Symptoms (Anxiety Disorder Symptoms, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose any symptoms you suspect your child experiences from the available options.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions truthfully to provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure of any answer, choose the option that best fits your child's situation.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical evaluation, so consult a healthcare professional if you suspect an anxiety disorder.
* You will be asked to fill out the form twice, once with your child's information and once with your own contact information.
