# OHS (Occupational Health And Safety) Work Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the safety protocols and medical requirements for workers in hazardous environments. It helps ensure that workers are adequately prepared and equipped to perform their tasks safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Complete all required fields, marked as "required" in this guide.
3. For each field, provide clear and concise answers to the best of your ability.
4. If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Work Permit Information** (`ohs_header`, text, required: false)
Enter your work permit information, including your name, job title, and work location.

### **2. Work Site Details** (`ohs_worksite_details`, text, required: false)
Describe the work site where you will be performing your tasks, including the location and any specific hazards present.

### **3. Site Safety Risks** (`ohs_site_safety_risks`, note, required: false)
List any hazards present on the site that may impact your safety, such as chemicals, electrical hazards, or physical dangers.

### **4. Hazard Preventions** (`ohs_hazard_prevention`, note, required: false)
List any measures that can be taken to reduce the risks identified in the previous field, such as personal protective equipment (PPE) or safety procedures.

### **5. Safety Equipment** (`ohs_safety_equipment`, select_one, required: true)
Select the safety equipment you will be using while performing your tasks, such as:

* Hard Hat
* Safety Vest
* Steel Toed Boots
* Safety Glasses
* Earplugs
* Respirator
* Safety Harness
* Fire Extinguisher

### **6. Safety Training** (`ohs_safety_training`, select_multiple, required: true)
Select the safety training you have received, such as:

* Basic First Aid
* CPR
* Rescue
* Firefighting
* Hazmat
* Forklift

### **7. Medical Conditions** (`ohs_medical_condition`, select_one, required: true)
Select any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform your tasks, such as:

* Allergies
* Back Injury
* Headache
* Muscle Strain
* Eye Strain
* Other

### **8. Medical Certificate** (`ohs_medical_certificate`, note, required: false)
Attach a medical certificate, if required, to confirm your medical fitness to work in a hazardous environment.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have completed all required fields.
