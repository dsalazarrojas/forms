# Telehealth Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Waiver Form is designed to collect information about you, the patient, and your emergency contact. This includes your name, birthdate, address, contact information, and relationship with your emergency contact. This data helps ensure that we can contact your emergency contact in case of an emergency and meet insurance and billing requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Patient Name** in the field provided. Please ensure that your name is spelled correctly and includes any relevant title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr.).
2. Enter your **Birthdate** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your **Address** where you currently reside.
4. Enter your **Contact Info** (e.g., phone number, email).
5. Enter your **Patient Email** address.
6. Enter the **Emergency Contact** person's name and information in the field provided.
7. Select the **Relationship** between you and your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, child).
8. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number for your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birthdate**: Enter your birthdate in mm/dd/yyyy format to help us identify you accurately.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached for care coordination.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any contact information we may use to reach you, such as a phone number or email.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your primary email address that we may use for communication.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, child).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any information, please don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
