# Initial Visit Patient Forms (MDR) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Visit Patient Forms (MDR) are used to collect essential patient information during the first visit to a medical provider. This form helps the provider to understand the patient's medical history, conditions, and needs, enabling them to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently:

1. Start by filling in your **Patient Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** (phone or other contact method) in the second field, as this is a required field.
3. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the fourth field, using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. For the **Medical Conditions** field, list any existing medical conditions you are currently being treated for.
5. Provide your **Medication List** to inform the medical provider about your current medications.
6. Enter the **Reason For Visits** field to help the medical provider understand why you are visiting.
7. List any **Allergies** you have, if applicable.
8. If relevant, include your **Medical Procedure List** for any previous procedures.
9. Enter your **Next Visit** date, if applicable.
10. Add any **Medical Procedure Notes** for the medical provider's reference.
11. Sign the **Patient Signature** field, indicating your understanding of the form.
12. Sign the **Medical Provider Signature** field, confirming the medical provider's agreement with the form.
13. Finally, the medical provider will fill in their information, including **Medical Provider Name**, **Medical Provider Title**, **Medical Provider Address**, **Medical Provider Phone**, **Medical Provider Email**, **Medical Provider Fax**, **Medical Provider License**, **Medical Provider License Number**, and **Medical Provider Last Visit Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your contact information, which may be a phone number or other contact method.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Use MM/DD/YYYY format to enter your date of birth.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any existing medical conditions you are currently being treated for.
- **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): Provide your current medication list to inform the medical provider.
- **Reason For Visits** (`visit_reason`, text, optional): Explain why you are visiting the medical provider.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, if applicable.
- **Medical Procedure List** (`medical_procedure_list`, text, optional): Include any previous medical procedures you have undergone.
- **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, date, optional): Enter your next visit date, if applicable.
- **Medical Procedure Notes** (`medical_procedure_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about previous medical procedures.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, required): Sign this field to indicate your understanding of the form.
- **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, note, required): The medical provider will sign this field to confirm their agreement with the form.
- **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's name.
- **Medical Provider Title** (`medical_provider_title`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's title or profession.
- **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's address.
- **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's phone number.
- **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter your medical provider's email address.
- **Medical Provider Fax** (`medical_provider_fax`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's fax number.
- **Medical Provider License** (`medical_provider_license`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's license information.
- **Medical Provider License Number** (`medical_provider_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider's license number.
- **Medical Provider Last Visit Date** (`medical_provider_last_visit_date`, date, optional): Enter your medical provider's last visit date.
- **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about your medical conditions or treatments.
