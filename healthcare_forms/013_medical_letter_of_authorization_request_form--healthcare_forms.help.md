# Medical Letter Of Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request medical treatment authorization from a doctor. It is intended to be filled out by a patient and signed by their doctor, providing necessary information for medical treatment authorization processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Enter patient and doctor contact information (if available).
- If applicable, enter treatment start and end dates.
- Sign the form with your authorized doctor's signature.
- Ensure that the form is complete before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): The patient's name as it appears on their identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport). This field is used to identify the patient.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): The name of the patient's doctor. This field should be filled out with the doctor's name as it appears on their medical license.
* **Treatment Name** (`treatment_name`, text, required: false): The name of the treatment or service being requested. This field should be filled out as it appears on the treatment or service documentation.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, text, required: false): The type of treatment or service being requested. For example, surgery, medication, etc.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, date, required: false): The date the treatment or service is scheduled to begin.
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, date, required: false): The date the treatment or service is scheduled to end.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required: false): The doctor's phone number where they can be reached for questions or clarification.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, required: false): The doctor's email address for communication purposes.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required: false): The patient's phone number where they can be reached for questions or clarification.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required: false): The patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): The doctor's signature, indicating their authorization for the treatment or service.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): The date the form was completed, for reference purposes.
