# End Of Life Care Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals document their wishes and preferences for end-of-life care, including their medical care, hospice care, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the options that apply to your situation. You can choose multiple options for some fields.
2.  Make sure to review each field carefully and select the options that best reflect your wishes and preferences.
3.  If you need assistance, you can ask for help from a healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **end_of_life_care_plan** (`end_of_life_care_plan`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the options that apply to your end-of-life care plan.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a plan in place.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a plan in place.
*   **end_of_life_care_preferences** (`end_of_life_care_preferences`, text, required/optional): Describe your preferences for end-of-life care.
*   **end_of_life_care_instructions** (`end_of_life_care_instructions`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive end-of-life care instructions.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you would like to receive instructions.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want to receive instructions.
*   **end_of_life_care_medical_history** (`end_of_life_care_medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe your medical history.
*   **hospice_care** (`hospice_care`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose if you would like to receive hospice care.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you would like to receive hospice care.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want to receive hospice care.
*   **advance_directive** (`advance_directive`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you have an advance directive.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have an advance directive.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have an advance directive.
*   **medical_care_preferences** (`medical_care_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your medical care preferences.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a preference.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a preference.
*   **end_of_life_care_wishes** (`end_of_life_care_wishes`, text, required/optional): Describe your wishes for end-of-life care.
*   **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be contacted.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be contacted.
*   **caregiver** (`caregiver`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to be your caregiver.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be your caregiver.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be your caregiver.
*   **physician** (`physician`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to be your physician.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be your physician.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be your physician.
*   **emergency_contact_number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the number of your emergency contact.
*   **end_of_life_care_preferences_hospice** (`end_of_life_care_preferences_hospice`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your hospice care preferences.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a preference.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a preference.
*   **end_of_life_care_instructions_hospice** (`end_of_life_care_instructions_hospice`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive hospice care instructions.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you would like to receive instructions.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want to receive instructions.
*   **advance_directive_hospice** (`advance_directive_hospice`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you have an advance directive for hospice care.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have an advance directive.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have an advance directive.
*   **medical_care_preferences_hospice** (`medical_care_preferences_hospice`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your medical care preferences for hospice care.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a preference.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a preference.
*   **end_of_life_care_wishes_hospice** (`end_of_life_care_wishes_hospice`, text, required/optional): Describe your wishes for end-of-life care in hospice.
*   **emergency_contact_hospice** (`emergency_contact_hospice`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to contact in case of an emergency in hospice.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be contacted.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be contacted.
*   **caregiver_hospice** (`caregiver_hospice`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to be your caregiver in hospice.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be your caregiver.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be your caregiver.
*   **physician_hospice** (`physician_hospice`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you would like to be your physician in hospice.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you want this person to be your physician.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want this person to be your physician.
*   **emergency_contact_number_hospice** (`emergency_contact_number_hospice`, text, required/optional): Enter the number of your emergency contact in hospice.
*   **end_of_life_care_preferences_other** (`end_of_life_care_preferences_other`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your other end-of-life care preferences.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a preference.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a preference.
*   **end_of_life_care_instructions_other** (`end_of_life_care_instructions_other`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive end-of-life care instructions in other settings.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you would like to receive instructions.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not want to receive instructions.
*   **advance_directive_other** (`advance_directive_other`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you have an advance directive in other settings.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have an advance directive.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have an advance directive.
*   **medical_care_preferences_other** (`medical_care_preferences_other`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your medical care preferences in other settings.
    *   **Yes** : Indicates that you have a preference.
    *   **No** : Indicates that you do not have a preference.

## Tips

*   Review each field carefully and choose the options that best fit your situation.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask for help from a healthcare provider.
*   Update your form as needed to reflect any changes in your wishes or preferences.
