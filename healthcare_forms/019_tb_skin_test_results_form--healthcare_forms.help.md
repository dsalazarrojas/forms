# tb_skin_test_results_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tb_skin_test_results_form is designed to collect and document the results of skin tests for patients. It is used to record the test results, test dates, test sites, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the test results in the "Test Results" field.
2. Enter the date of the test in the "test_date" field.
3. Select the test site from the options provided in the "test_site" field.
4. If the test site is "Other", enter the specific site in the "other_test_site" field.
5. Choose the result interpretation from the options provided in the "result_interpretation" field.
6. Select the healthcare provider who performed the test in the "healthcare_provider" field.
7. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Results**: A field to enter the results of the skin test.
* **test_date**: A field to enter the date when the skin test was conducted.
* **test_site**: A field to select the location of the test (either "Right Forearm", "Other", or leave blank if it's not applicable).
* **other_test_site**: A field to enter the specific site if the test site is "Other" (only if test_site is selected as "Other").
* **result_interpretation**: A field to select the interpretation of the test results (either "Positive", "Negative", or "Indeterminate").
* **healthcare_provider**: A field to select the healthcare provider who performed the test.
* **patient_name**: A field to enter the patient's name.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct test results and date to ensure accurate patient records.
* If the test site is "Other", be specific and clear when entering the location.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant healthcare professional for clarification.
* Please ensure that the patient name is spelled correctly and accurately represents the patient's name.
