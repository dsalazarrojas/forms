# Hip Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for referrals from patients to specialists. It ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently, enabling effective and safe care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Info** section, including their name, contact details, and any additional notes.
2. Select the specialist's information in the **Specialist Info** section, including their name, contact details, and any additional notes.
3. Choose the reason for referral in the **Referral Reason** section.
4. Select the specialist's facility and location in the **Specialist Facility** section.
5. Enter the specialist's and patient's dates and times in the **Specialist Date**, **Specialist Time**, **Patient Date**, and **Patient Time** sections.
6. Select the specialist's and patient's status in the **Specialist Status** and **Patient Status** sections.
7. Choose the submitted-by option to specify who is submitting the referral in the **Submitted By** section.
8. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (id: 1)**: Enter the patient's name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Specialist Info (id: 2)**: Enter the specialist's name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Referral Reason (id: 3)**: Describe the reason for referral.
* **Specialist Name (id: 4)**: Select the specialist to whom you are referring the patient.
* **Specialist Phone (id: 5)**: Enter the specialist's phone number.
* **Patient Phone (id: 6)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Specialist Email (id: 7)**: Enter the specialist's email address.
* **Patient Email (id: 8)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Specialist Address (id: 9)**: Optional; enter the specialist's address.
* **Patient Address (id: 10)**: Optional; enter the patient's address.
* **Specialist Fax (id: 11)**: Optional; enter the specialist's fax number.
* **Specialist License No (id: 12)**: Optional; enter the specialist's license number.
* **Specialist Notes (id: 13)**: Optional; enter any additional notes about the specialist.
* **Patient Notes (id: 14)**: Optional; enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Specialist Facility (id: 15)**: Select the specialist's facility.
* **Patient Facility (id: 16)**: Select the patient's facility.
* **Specialist Date (id: 17)**: Enter the specialist's availability date.
* **Specialist Time (id: 18)**: Enter the specialist's availability time.
* **Patient Date (id: 19)**: Enter the patient's date.
* **Patient Time (id: 20)**: Enter the patient's time.
* **Specialist Status (id: 21)**: Select the specialist's status.
* **Specialist Status Note (id: 22)**: Optional; enter any additional notes about the specialist's status.
* **Patient Status (id: 23)**: Select the patient's status.
* **Patient Status Note (id: 24)**: Optional; enter any additional notes about the patient's status.
* **Submitted By (id: 25)**: Choose who is submitting the referral.
