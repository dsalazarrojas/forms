# mental_health_counseling_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The mental health counseling waiver form is a document that healthcare providers use to document a client's consent and understanding of their treatment. The form collects information about the client, their contact person, and their relationship to the client.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Provide the client's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Select the client's relationship to the contact person from the dropdown menu in the "Relationship" field.
4.  Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
5.  Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6.  Select the client's consent to treatment from the dropdown menu in the "Consent to Treatment" field.
7.  In the "Read and Understand" field, please enter "Yes, I understand" or "No, I do not understand".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter the client's name.
    *   The client's name is a required field and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth.
    *   This field is a required field and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
*   **Relationship** (Relationship, select_one, required): Select the client's relationship to the contact person from the dropdown menu.
    *   This field is a required dropdown menu that allows the user to select a relationship type from a list of options.
*   **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
    *   This field is a required field and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
*   **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
    *   This field is a required field and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
*   **Consent to Treatment** (Consent to Treatment, select_multiple, required): Select whether the client consents to treatment.
    *   This field is a required dropdown menu that allows the user to select whether the client consents to treatment.
*   **Read and Understand** (Read and Understand, text, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the treatment process.
    *   This field is a required text field where the client confirms they have read and understood the treatment process.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your healthcare provider.
