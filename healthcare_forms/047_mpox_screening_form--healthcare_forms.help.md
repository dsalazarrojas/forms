# Mpox Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Screening Form is designed to help healthcare professionals quickly collect information from patients or caregivers to assess the risk factors and symptoms of Mpox.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the patient information section with the patient's details.
2. Select the symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
3. Choose the risk factors that apply to the patient.
4. Provide any relevant travel history.
5. Record the patient's medical history.
6. Indicate the patient's exposure risk.
7. Select the expected medical response.
8. Choose the contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, age, and contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information for follow-up.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the symptoms the patient is experiencing from the available options.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the risk factors that apply to the patient.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `text`, required: false): Record the patient's travel history, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Exposure Risk** (`exposure_risk`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the patient's exposure risk.
* **Medical Response** (`medical_response`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the expected medical response.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the contact information for follow-up.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Record any additional notes or comments about the patient.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the patient's medical records or consult a healthcare professional.
* If you need to add or modify any of the fields, please contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
