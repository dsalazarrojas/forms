# Medical Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Liability Waiver Form is used to document and obtain consent from individuals who are participating in medical procedures. It is intended for use in healthcare settings to ensure that participants understand their rights and responsibilities and to provide a clear record of the consent process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant's information, including their name, date of birth, and address.
2. Provide your guardian's information, including their name and relationship to the participant.
3. Indicate whether you acknowledge the risks associated with the medical procedure.
4. Provide any additional medical procedure details, if necessary.
5. Identify your emergency contact information, if applicable.
6. Sign the waiver to indicate your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, Required/Optional): Enter the participant's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the guardian or legal representative.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, text, Required/Optional): Describe the relationship between the participant and the guardian (e.g., parent, guardian, power of attorney).
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, Required/Optional): Indicate whether you acknowledge the risks associated with the medical procedure.
	+ Select 'Acknowledged' to confirm that you understand the risks.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, text, Required/Optional): Provide any additional information about the medical procedure.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Liability Waiver** (`medical_liability_waiver`, select_one, Required/Optional): Indicate whether you agree to the terms of the waiver.
	+ Select 'True' to indicate agreement.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, Required/Optional): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, Required/Optional): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms as the guardian or legal representative.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, ask the medical professional for clarification.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
