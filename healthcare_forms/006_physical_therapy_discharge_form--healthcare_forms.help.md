# Physical Therapy Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Discharge Form is used to document a patient's progress and readiness for discharge from physical therapy services. It captures essential information to ensure a smooth transition of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Visit (Date)**: Enter the date of your first physical therapy visit.
2. **Insurance Information**: Select your insurance type from the options provided: Medicaid, Medicare, Private Insurance, or Self-Pay.
3. **Physical Therapy Goals**: Select all the goals you wish to achieve through physical therapy: Improve mobility, Reduce pain, or Increase strength.
4. **Physical Therapy Status**: Indicate your current status: Active Care, Inactive care, or No PT.
5. **Treatment Plan**: Enter a brief note describing your treatment plan.
6. **Readiness for Discharge**: Indicate your readiness for discharge by selecting True or False.
7. **Reason for Discharge**: Enter a brief explanation for your discharge.
8. **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your understanding of your treatment plan and readiness for discharge.
9. **Provider Signature**: Sign your name as the treating provider to confirm your agreement with the patient's treatment plan and readiness for discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Visit (Date)**: Enter the date of your first physical therapy visit.
* **Insurance Information**: Your insurance type, which is used to determine your coverage and payment.
* **Physical Therapy Goals**: Select all goals you wish to achieve through physical therapy.
* **Physical Therapy Status**: Indicate your current status in physical therapy.
* **Treatment Plan**: Enter a brief description of your treatment plan.
* **Readiness for Discharge**: Indicate your willingness to be discharged from physical therapy services.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Provide a brief explanation for your discharge.
* **Patient Signature**: Confirm your understanding of your treatment plan and readiness for discharge.
* **Provider Signature**: Confirm your agreement with the patient's treatment plan and readiness for discharge.
* **Prophets Provider Signature**: This field is for the treating provider to sign, not the patient.
*   **Patient Signature**: Confirm your understanding of your treatment plan and readiness for discharge.
*   **Prophets Provider Signature**: This field should be renamed to **Provider Signature** for clarity.
*   **Physical Therapy Status**: Indicate your current status in physical therapy.
