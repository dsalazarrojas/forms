# Coronavirus Molecular Testing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for molecular testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the necessary details for the requested fields. Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately. If you're unsure about any information, you can contact the relevant authorities or seek clarification before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request a Test** (`coronavirus_testing_request`, `text`, optional): This field is for a brief description of the testing request. Please provide a clear and concise statement of the purpose of the test.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact details of the person requesting the test. This will help our team get in touch with you to discuss further information or clarify any doubts.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Add the contact phone number of the person requesting the test. This will enable us to contact you quickly in case of any updates or queries.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Provide the email address of the person requesting the test. We will use this to send you updates and notifications regarding your test results.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of test you require (PCR, Rapid Antigen, or Rapid Antibody). This will help us understand your testing needs.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Specify the location where the test will be conducted. This can be a hospital, clinic, or any other relevant site.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, optional): If you're experiencing any symptoms, please describe them here. This will help us assess your condition and proceed with the test.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share about your testing request.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm whether you consent to the testing process by selecting 'True'. This is an essential field to ensure we can proceed with the test.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the test results.
* **Test Result Time** (`test_result_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the time when the test results were obtained.
* **Test Result Comments** (`test_result_comments`, `text`, optional): Describe any comments or observations about the test results.
* **Test Result Status** (`test_result_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the test result (Completed, Pending, or Canceled).
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or information you'd like to share about the test.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the test.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the time of the test.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `text`, optional): Suggest any follow-up details or actions needed after the test.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the time for the follow-up.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Share any relevant medical history or information related to your condition.
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the medical history was last updated or assessed.
* **Medical History Time** (`medical_history_time`, `time`, optional): Specify the time when the medical history was last updated or assessed.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant billing information or payment details.
* **Billing Contact** (`billing_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information of the person handling billing or payment details.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Specify the billing address or payment location.

Note: Some fields are optional, but ensuring the completion of all required fields will ensure a swift and accurate processing of your testing request.
