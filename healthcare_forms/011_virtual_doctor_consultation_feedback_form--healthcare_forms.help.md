# Virtual Doctor Consultation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their recent virtual doctor consultation. Your input will help us improve the quality of care we provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all required questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback Rating
* **Feedback Rating (1)** (`number`, **required**):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with your overall experience.
* **Quality of Care (2)** (`text`, **required**):
	+ Please describe your experience with the quality of care provided by your doctor.
* **Communication (3)** (`text`, **required**):
	+ Describe how well your doctor communicated with you during the consultation.
* **Understanding (4)** (`text`, **required**):
	+ How well did your doctor understand your concerns and questions.
* **Doctor Availability (5)** (`select_one`, **required**):
	+ How well was your appointment scheduled at the correct time?
* **Doctor Communication (6)** (`select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ How would you rate the doctor's communication style? 
* **Doctor Knowledge (7)** (`select_one`, **required**):
	+ How knowledgeable was your doctor during the consultation?
* **Doctor Empathy (8)** (`select_one`, **required**):
	+ How empathetic was your doctor during the consultation?
* **Patient Satisfaction (9)** (`select_one`, **required**):
	+ How satisfied are you with your overall experience?
* **Appointment Length (10)** (`time`, **required**):
	+ How long did your appointment last?

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, and do not skip any questions. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided.
