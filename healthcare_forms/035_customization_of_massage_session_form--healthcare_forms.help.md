# Customization Of Massage Session Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to customize a massage session for a client. Clients can choose their preferred massage style and session length.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you and proceed to the next page. You can choose to complete as much or as little information as you like, as most fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client** (Select multiple): Check the box if you are a client.
* **Session Length** (Number): Choose a session length that suits your needs.
* **Massage Style** (Select one): Select your preferred massage style. 
* **Session Date** (Date): Select a date for your massage session (if you select "Client" on Page 1).

## Tips
If you select "Client" on page 1, you will be asked to provide a date for your massage session on page 2. You can choose any date that suits your needs. If you answer "No" to "Client", you will be asked to provide other information.
