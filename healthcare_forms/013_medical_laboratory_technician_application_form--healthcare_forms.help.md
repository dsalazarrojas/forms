*   **General Information** (Page 1, `text`): Please fill in your general information, including your name and any other relevant details.
*   **Contact Information** (Page 2, `text`): Enter your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
*   **Education Background** (Page 3, `text`): Describe your educational background, including any relevant courses or degrees.
*   **Work Experience** (Page 4, `text`): Share your work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
*   **Medical Certifications** (Page 5, `select_multiple`): Select all applicable medical certifications from the options provided.
*   **Personal Statement** (Page 6, `text`): Write a brief statement explaining why you're a good fit for the position.
*   **Resume Upload** (Page 7, `text`): Attach your resume for review.
*   **References** (Page 8, `text`): List your professional references, including names and contact information.
*   **Submit** (Page 9, `text`): Click this button to submit your application.
*   **Medical License** (Page 10, `text`): Attach your medical license for review.
