# Denture Treatment Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather necessary information from the patient or their representative to provide an accurate cost estimate for denture treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required for this form.
2. Select the relevant fields and fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Upload any required images of the patient's face and upper and lower jaws.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to prevent any delays or inaccuracies in the cost estimate process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, required: false): Please provide your name and any other relevant patient details.
* **Treatment Needs** (`treatment_needs`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of denture treatment you require, such as Denture Repair, Partial Denture, Full Denture, or Dental Implants.
* **Images** (`images`, number, required: false): Upload up to 10 images of the patient's face and upper and lower jaws. These images will help the dentist or treatment center assess the condition of your denture and provide a more accurate cost estimate.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant information about your doctor or treatment center.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required: false): If you have any medical history that may impact your treatment, please select 'True'.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information so that the treatment center can get back to you with any updates or questions.
* **Estimated Costs** (`estimated_costs`, number, required: false): If you have any estimated costs in mind, please provide them in this field.
