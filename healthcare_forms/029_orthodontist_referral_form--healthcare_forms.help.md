# Orthodontist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare providers to refer a patient to an orthodontist for further treatment and evaluation. Please complete this form with accurate and relevant information to facilitate the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's First Name.
2. Enter the patient's Last Name.
3. Enter the patient's Date of Birth.
4. Enter the patient's Email (if applicable).
5. Enter the patient's Phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter the patient's Address (if applicable).
7. Select the Referring Provider from the list of options.
8. Choose whether the patient has a Medical Record with the option "Existing" or "Non-Existing".
9. Enter the Referral Date.
10. Enter the Referral Time (if applicable).
11. Select the Referring Facility from the list of options.
12. Enter the Referral Reason.
13. Enter any Medical History (if applicable).
14. Enter any Allergies (if applicable).
15. Enter any Medical Conditions (if applicable).
16. Enter any Medications (if applicable).
17. Enter any additional Medical History information (if applicable).
18. Enter any Referral Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name. (Required)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address (if applicable).
* **Referring Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who is referring the patient to the orthodontist.
* **Medical Record**: Choose whether the patient has an existing medical record or not.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time**: Enter the time of the referral (if applicable).
* **Referring Facility**: Select the facility where the referral is being made from the list of options.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history information (if applicable).
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient has (if applicable).
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions the patient has (if applicable).
* **Medications**: Enter any medications the patient is taking (if applicable).
* **Medical History Other**: Enter any additional medical history information (if applicable).
* **Referral Note**: Enter any additional referral information or notes.
* **Follow Up**: Enter any follow-up information (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up (if applicable).
* **Referral Facility**: Select the facility where the patient will be referred to from the list of options.
* **Referral Doctor**: Select the doctor who the patient will be referred to from the list of options.
* **Referral Status**: Select the status of the referral as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Referral Notes**: Enter any additional referral notes.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful referral process.
