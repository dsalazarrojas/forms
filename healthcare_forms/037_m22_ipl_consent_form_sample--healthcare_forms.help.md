# M22 IPL Consent Form Sample - Help Guide

## Purpose
The M22 IPL Consent Form Sample is used to obtain informed consent from patients for specific medical procedures or tests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter your user information, including your name and contact number.
3. Provide your date of birth.
4. Select any relevant medical conditions that apply to you (such as Diabetes or Hypertension).
5. Choose a date to sign and date your consent.
6. Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**:
	+ This field is used to collect your basic information, such as name and contact number.
* **Date Of Birth**: 
	+ Enter the date of your birth in MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 02/12/99).
* **Contact Number**: 
	+ This is your phone number or any other contact information that we may need to reach you.
* **Medical Conditions**:
	+ Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the provided options (e.g., Diabetes or Hypertension).
* **Consent Date**:
	+ Choose a date to sign and date your consent.
* **Consent Date** (optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes if needed, but please make sure to date it.

## Tips

* Always fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
* Review this form carefully before signing.
* Ask questions or clarification if you're unsure about any field or process.
