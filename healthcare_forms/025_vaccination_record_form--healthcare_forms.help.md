# Vaccination Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record the vaccination details of patients, including their personal information and the vaccination administration details. It helps healthcare providers keep accurate and up-to-date records of vaccinations, ensuring that patients receive the necessary vaccinations and follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.
3. Enter the date of vaccination in the "Date of Vaccination" field.
4. Select whether the vaccine was administered ("Yes" or "No") in the "Vaccine Administered" field.
5. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor's Name" field.
6. Enter the doctor's license number in the "Doctor's License Number" field.
7. Enter the facility name in the "Facility Name" field.
8. Enter the facility address in the "Facility Address" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient's Phone Number" field.
10. (Optional) Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor's Phone Number" field.
11. (Optional) Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient's Email" field.
12. (Optional) Enter the doctor's email address in the "Doctor's Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Address** (patient_address, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Date of Vaccination** (vaccination_date, date, required): Enter the date the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Administered** (vaccine_administered, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the vaccine was administered, "No" otherwise.
* **Doctor's Name** (doctor_name, text, required): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor's License Number** (doctor_license_number, text, required): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Facility Name** (facility_name, text, required): Enter the facility name where the vaccination was administered.
* **Facility Address** (facility_address, text, required): Enter the facility's address.
* **Patient's Phone Number** (patient_phone, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Doctor's Phone Number** (doctor_phone, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Patient's Email** (patient_email, email, optional): (Optional) Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor's Email** (doctor_email, email, optional): (Optional) Enter the doctor's email address.
