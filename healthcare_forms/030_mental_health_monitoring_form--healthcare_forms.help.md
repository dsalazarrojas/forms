# Mental Health Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help monitor mental health status, covering various indicators of stress, anxiety, and depression. It provides a comprehensive assessment tool for individuals or organizations to track and evaluate mental health progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions (identified by a *).
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or multiple options that best reflect your current mental health status.
3. For free-text fields, provide detailed and honest responses.
4. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form.
