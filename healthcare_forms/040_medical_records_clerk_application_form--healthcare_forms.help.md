# Medical Records Clerk Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Records Clerk Application Form is designed to collect information from Medical Records Clerks who are applying for a position at a healthcare facility. The form is used to gather personal and professional information, including full name, date of birth, contact information, work experience, medical certifications, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all necessary information ready for completion.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Provide your contact information, including address, city, state, and zip, in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
6. List your medical certifications, including BLS, ACLS, and PALS, in the "Medical Certifications" field.
7. Select your availability for work in the "Availability" field.
8. Choose the frequency of your availability in the "Availability Frequency" field.
9. Enter your medical license and certification information, including license type and expiration date, in the "Medical License" field.
10. Enter your address, city, state, and zip in the "Address" field.
11. Enter your phone numbers, including work and home phone, in the "Work Phone" and "Home Phone" fields.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
13. Provide emergency contact information, including name and relationship, in the "Emergency Contact Information" field.
14. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your address, city, state, and zip for contact purposes.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe your previous work experience in the healthcare field.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): List any medical certifications you hold, such as BLS, ACLS, and PALS.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your availability for work, such as full-time or part-time.
* **Availability Frequency** (`availability_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of your availability, such as daily or weekly.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter your medical license information, including license type and expiration date.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): Enter any additional certifications or qualifications you hold.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of medical license you hold.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of certification you hold.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date of your medical license.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, text, optional): Enter your home phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide emergency contact information, including name and relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Indicate the relationship of the emergency contact.
 
# Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the healthcare facility.
