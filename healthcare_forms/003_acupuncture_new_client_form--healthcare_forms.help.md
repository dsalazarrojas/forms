# Acupuncture New Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture New Client Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information about you, the client, before starting treatment. This information will help us tailor your treatment plan to your specific needs and ensure your comfort and safety during and after treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in all the required fields carefully and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, you can ask your acupuncturist for assistance. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter your legal name.

- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format <code>MM/DD/YYYY</code>.
  * Type: <code>date</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Your birth date.

- **Gender**: Select your gender identity from the options provided.
  * Type: <code>select_one</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Select your gender identity.

- **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter your current address.

- **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter your primary contact number.

- **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for appointment confirmations.
  * Type: <code>email</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter a valid email.

- **Health Insurance Provider**: If you have health insurance, enter the name of your insurance company.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: If you have insurance, enter the name of your provider.

- **Policy Number**: If you have health insurance, enter your policy number.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: If you have insurance, enter your policy number.

- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter the name of the person to contact.

- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter the contact number.

- **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Describe your relationship.

- **Medical History Header**: This section is informational only and not editable.
  * Type: <code>note</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: This section is not editable.

- **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: List your current medications.

- **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies to medications, foods, or substances.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: List your known allergies.

- **Chronic Conditions**: List any ongoing medical conditions.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: List your chronic conditions.

- **Previous Surgeries**: List any past surgical procedures.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: List your previous surgeries.

- **Reason for Seeking Acupuncture**: Describe why you are seeking acupuncture treatment.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Describe your reason.

- **Preferred Treatment Areas**: Specify which areas of the body you want to be treated.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>false</code>
  * Hint: Specify your treatment areas.

- **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm that you agree to the terms of treatment.
  * Type: <code>select_one</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Confirm your agreement.

- **Privacy Policy Acknowledged**: Confirm that you have read and understood our privacy policy.
  * Type: <code>select_one</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Confirm that you have read.

- **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing this form.
  * Type: <code>date</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Enter the date of signing.

- **Client Signature**: Sign this form electronically.
  * Type: <code>text</code>
  * Required: <code>true</code>
  * Hint: Sign the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your acupuncturist for assistance.
- Please review and sign the privacy policy acknowledgement before starting treatment.
