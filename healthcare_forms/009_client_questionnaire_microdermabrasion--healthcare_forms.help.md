# CLIENT QUESTIONNAIRE Microdermabrasion - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients before undergoing microdermabrasion treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. For the "Client name" field, enter the client's full legal name.
3. For the "Date of birth" field, use the format YYYY-MM-DD as specified in the hint.
4. For the "Contact number" field, enter the best number to reach the client.
5. For the "Skin type" field, select one of the provided options that best describes the client's skin type.
6. For the "Known allergies" field, list any known allergies, including medication and topical allergies.
7. For the "Current medications" field, list any prescription or over the counter medications the client is currently taking.
8. For the "Prior procedures" field, list any relevant past skin treatments the client has undergone.
9. For the "Expected outcome" field, clearly state what the client hopes to achieve from the treatment.
10. For the "Consent to treatment" field, select "True" if the client is willing to proceed with the treatment, and "False" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, *): Enter the client's full legal name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, ): Use the format YYYY-MM-DD to enter the client's date of birth.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, ): Enter the best number to reach the client for follow up or communication.
* **Skin type** (`skin_type`, select_one, ): Select one of the following options that best describes the client's skin type:
	+ Normal
	+ Dry
	+ Oily
	+ Combination
* **Known allergies** (`allergies`, text, ): List any known allergies, including medication and topical allergies.
* **Current medications** (`current_medications`, text, ): List any prescription or over the counter medications the client is currently taking.
* **Prior skin procedures** (`prior_procedures`, text, ): List any relevant past skin treatments the client has undergone.
* **Expected outcome** (`expected_outcome`, text, *): Clearly state what the client hopes to achieve from the treatment.
* **Consent to treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, *): Select "True" if the client is willing to proceed with the treatment, and "False" otherwise.
