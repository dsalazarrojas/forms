# Behavioral Health Integration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Health Integration Form is used to gather information about a patient's behavioral health needs and preferences. This form is used to support the integration of behavioral health services with medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, phone number, and email address. This information will be used to identify the patient and communicate with them regarding their behavioral health services.
2. **Provide Family Member/Caretaker Information**: Enter the family member/caretaker's name, phone number, and email address. This information will be used to notify the family member/caretaker about the patient's behavioral health services.
3. **Specify Behavioral Health Services**: Choose the type of behavioral health service recommended for the patient, and provide the name or facility of the recommended provider. Also, select the urgency level of the referral, such as Routine, Soon, Urgent, or Emergency.
4. **Consent and Commitment**: Indicate your consent to share behavioral health information between the medical provider and behavioral health provider. Also, specify whether you agree or disagree to notify the family member/caretaker about the patient's services.
5. **Commitment to Care**: Confirm your commitment to attend the recommended services and whether the family will support the patient's participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter the patient's name.
### 2. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Enter the patient's date of birth.
### 3. **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required)
Enter the patient's phone number.
### 4. **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, optional)
Enter the patient's email address.
### 5. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional)
This field is not required, but it's used to identify the patient's medical record number.
### 6. **Family Member/Caretaker Name** (`family_member_name`, text, required)
Enter the family member/caretaker's name.
### 7. **Relationship to Patient** (`relationship`, select_one, required)
Select how the family member/caretaker is related to the patient, such as Spouse, Adult Child, Parent, Sibling, Other Family, or Caretaker.
### 8. **Phone Number** (`family_phone`, text, required)
Enter the family member/caretaker's phone number.
### 9. **Email Address** (`family_email`, email, optional)
Enter the family member/caretaker's email address.
### 10. **Type of Behavioral Health Service Recommended** (`service_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of behavioral health service recommended for the patient, such as Counseling, Therapy, Psychiatric Evaluation, Substance Abuse Treatment, Mental Health Crisis Support, Wellness Program, or Other.
### 11. **Recommended Provider/Facility** (`service_provider`, text, required)
Enter the name or facility of the recommended provider.
### 12. **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required)
Enter the referral date for the behavioral health services.
### 13. **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required)
Select the urgency level of the referral, such as Routine, Soon, Urgent, or Emergency.
### 14. **Consent to Share Behavioral Health Information** (`consent_sharing`, select_one, required)
Indicate whether you consent to share behavioral health information between the medical provider and behavioral health provider.
### 15. **Family Member/Caretaker May Be Notified** (`consent_family_notification`, select_one, required)
Select whether the family member/caretaker may be notified about the patient's services.
### 16. **Commit to Attend Recommended Services** (`patient_commitment`, select_one, required)
Confirm your commitment to attend the recommended services.
### 17. **Family Will Support Patient Participation** (`family_support`, select_one, required)
Specify whether the family will support the patient's participation in the services.
### 18. **Date of Form Completion** (`form_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of form completion.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for assistance.
