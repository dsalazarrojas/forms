# Medical Records Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from a patient to access and share their medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's **Patient Information**. This includes basic details such as name, date of birth, or patient ID.
2.  Select the **Authorization Type** (View, Edit, or Share) to specify how you wish to access their medical records.
3.  Enter the **Healthcare Provider** who will be accessing the patient's medical records.
4.  Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** when the authorization is valid from and to.
5.  Provide **Authorization Details** explaining how you will use the patient's medical records.
6.  Indicate if you have **Medical Record Access** (Yes/No) and **Medical Record Sharing** (Yes/No) permissions.
7.  Sign the **Patient Signature** as a confirmation that you have read and understand this form.
8.  Sign the **Healthcare Provider Signature** to confirm that you have received the patient's authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's personal details, such as name, date of birth, or patient ID.
* **Authorization Type** (Required):
	+ **View**: To access the patient's medical records for review purposes.
	+ **Edit**: To modify the patient's medical records.
	+ **Share**: To share the patient's medical records with other healthcare providers or staff.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter your name and contact information as the healthcare provider accessing the patient's records.
* **Start Date**: The date when the authorization is valid from.
* **End Date**: The date when the authorization is valid to.
* **Authorization Details**: Explain how you will use the patient's medical records, including what you will do with the records and who you will share them with.
* **Medical Record Access** (Required): Check "Yes" if you are accessing the patient's medical records; "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Record Sharing** (Required): Check "Yes" if you are sharing the patient's medical records; "No" otherwise.
* **Patient Signature** (Required): Sign your name as a confirmation that you have read and understand this form.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (Required): Sign your name as a confirmation that you have received the patient's authorization.
* **Date Completed** (Optional): The date when you completed this form.
* **Patient ID** (Required): Enter your patient ID or other unique identifier.
