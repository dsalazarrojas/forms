# Flu Vaccine Declination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Flu Vaccine Declination Form" is used to document patients who decline or have declined a flu vaccine. This form ensures that patients are properly tracked and followed up with, as per medical protocol.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's **Name** in the space provided.
2. Enter the patient's **Date of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the type of vaccine that was declined (**Flu Vaccine** or **Other**).
4. If applicable, enter the **Dose Number** of the vaccine that was declined.
5. Enter the **Reason for Decline** in the space provided (optional).
6. Sign your name in the **Signature** section to confirm that the information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine that was declined (Flu Vaccine or Other).
* **Dose Number**: Enter the number of doses of the vaccine that was declined (optional).
* **Reason for Decline**: Enter the reason why the patient declined the flu vaccine (optional).
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that the information is correct.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the healthcare provider.
- This form is for documentation and follow-up purposes only.
