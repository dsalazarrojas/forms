# Dermatology Virtual Visits Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from patients before conducting virtual or in-person visits with a healthcare provider. It ensures that the patient understands and agrees to the terms of the visit, including the location and type of visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's information, including name, date of visit, phone number, and email (if applicable).
2. Choose the type of visit you are having (Virtual Visits or In-person Visits).
3. Select the location of the visit (Home Visit, Clinic, or Hospital).
4. Acknowledge your understanding of HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. If you have any emergency contact information, please provide it.
6. Sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Visit** (Date): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Patient Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Provider Information** (Text): This field is for the healthcare provider's information.
* **Visit Type** (Select One): Choose between Virtual Visits or In-person Visits.
* **Visit Location** (Select Multiple): Select the location of your visit (Home Visit, Clinic, or Hospital).
* **Electronic Signature** (Text): This field is for your electronic signature.
* **Date Signed** (Date): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgement** (Select Multiple): Acknowledge your understanding of HIPAA by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Patient Email** (Email): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Visit Notes** (Text): This field is for any additional visit notes.
* **Patient Signature** (Text): This field is for your electronic signature.
* **Provider Signature** (Text): This field is for the healthcare provider's electronic signature.
* **Provider Name** (Text): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Title** (Text): Enter your healthcare provider's title.
* **Clinic Name** (Text): Enter the name of the clinic.
* **Clinic Address** (Text): Enter the address of the clinic.
* **Clinic Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the clinic.
* **Clinic Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the clinic.
* **Clinic Fax** (Text): Enter the fax number of the clinic.
* **Clinic License** (Text): Enter the clinic's license number.
* **Medical Condition** (Text): If applicable, enter your current medical condition.
* **Medical History** (Text): If applicable, enter your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text): Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified.
