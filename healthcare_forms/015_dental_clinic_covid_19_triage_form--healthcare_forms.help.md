# Dental Clinic COVID 19 Triage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for triage and initial assessment during the COVID-19 pandemic. It helps healthcare professionals quickly and efficiently gather necessary data to provide the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's personal information in the **patient info** section.
2. Provide the contact number of the patient or their guardian/parent for emergency contact.
3. List the patient's symptoms in the **symptoms** section.
4. If applicable, enter the patient's medical history.
5. If applicable, enter the patient's allergies.
6. Indicate if the patient requires medical attention and provide the reason in the **reason medical attention required** field.
7. Enter the patient's current medical condition.
8. Report the patient's COVID-19 test results in the **covid 19 test results** section.
9. Indicate the patient's vaccine status.
10. Schedule appointments for the patient by entering the appointment date and time in the **appointment date** and **appointment time** fields.
11. Provide comments for follow-up visits in the **follow up comments** field.
12. Enter the details of the next appointment, including date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient info**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant personal details.
* **contact number**: Enter the patient's or their guardian/parent's contact phone number.
* **symptoms**: Select all the symptoms the patient is experiencing (e.g. Cough, Fever, Shortness of breath).
* **medical history**: Enter any relevant medical history the patient has, such as previous medical conditions or surgeries.
* **allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient has.
* **emergency contact**: Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **medical attention required**: Check if the patient requires medical attention.
* **reason medical attention required**: If the patient requires medical attention, explain the reason in this field.
* **medical condition**: Enter the patient's current medical condition.
* **covid 19 test results**: Report the patient's COVID-19 test results (Positive, Negative, Not tested).
* **vaccine status**: Indicate if the patient is vaccinated or not.
* **appointment date**: Schedule the appointment date.
* **appointment time**: Schedule the appointment time.
* **follow up date**: Schedule the follow-up visit date.
* **follow up time**: Schedule the follow-up visit time.
* **follow up comments**: Add any comments for the follow-up visit.
* **next appointment**: Enter the details of the next appointment, including date and time.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* Ensure you provide all necessary information for the patient's healthcare needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the healthcare provider.
* This form is for the sole purpose of collecting information for the patient's care and treatment.
