# Telehealth Services Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to outline the terms and conditions of providing telehealth services. It's a required document that ensures both the patient and the healthcare provider are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Please select "Patient Information" from the options to provide your name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2.  Choose the healthcare provider from the options to select the provider you'll be working with.
3.  Enter the date and time of service.
4.  Confirm your electronic consent to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Information**
(Required: Yes)
This field is used to collect your personal details, such as your name and date of birth. Please select the correct information to ensure accurate record-keeping.

### 2. **Telehealth Services Agreement**
(Required: Yes)
This field is where you'll confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions of telehealth services. Please read and agree to the terms carefully.

### 3. **Electronic Consent**
(Required: No)
This field is a note where you'll confirm your electronic consent to the terms of service. Please review the terms carefully before confirming.

### 4. **Healthcare Provider**
(Required: Yes)
This field is used to select the healthcare provider you'll be working with. Please choose the correct provider from the options.

### 5. **Provider's Phone**
(Required: No)
This field is used to enter the healthcare provider's phone number. Please ensure you have the correct number before completing this field.

### 6. **Date Of Service**
(Required: Yes)
This field is used to enter the date of service. Please ensure the date is accurate and complete.

### 7. **Time Of Service**
(Required: Yes)
This field is used to enter the time of service. Please ensure the time is accurate and complete.

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
- The information provided will be used for administrative and billing purposes only.
