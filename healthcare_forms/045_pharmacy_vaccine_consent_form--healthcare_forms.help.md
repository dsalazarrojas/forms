# Pharmacy Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmacy Vaccine Consent Form is a document used to gather patient consent for vaccine administration. It helps healthcare professionals ensure that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with vaccines and provides a formal record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient consents or refuses the vaccine.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Provide the patient's contact information.
4. Record the name of the medical practice or pharmacy.
5. Enter the patient's date of birth.
6. Enter the Medical Practice ID.
7. Log the date the patient provided consent.
8. Select reasons why the vaccine was refused.
9. Enter details about the refusal reason.
10. Include any additional comments or notes about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Consent Form** (select_one):
  Select whether the patient consents or refuses the vaccine. This is a critical field that indicates whether the patient is willing to receive the vaccine or not.
* **Patient Name** (text):
  Enter the patient's name. This field is required to identify the patient.
* **Contact Number** (text):
  Enter the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email.
  This helps healthcare professionals reach the patient for further information or follow-up.
* **Medical Practice Name** (text):
  Record the name of the medical practice or pharmacy where the vaccine is being administered.
* **Date of Birth** (date):
  Enter the patient's date of birth. This helps verify the patient's age and eligibility for vaccines.
* **Medical Practice ID** (text):
  Enter the Medical Practice ID. This is a unique identifier for the medical practice or pharmacy.
* **Date of Consent** (date):
  Log the date the patient provided consent for the vaccine.
* **Reason for Refusal** (text):
  Enter the reason why the vaccine was refused. This is an important field for patient understanding and communication.
* **Vaccine Refused** (select_multiple):
  Select reasons why the vaccine was refused. This helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's concerns.
* **Reason for Refusal Details** (text):
  Enter additional details about the refusal reason. This provides more context for healthcare professionals.
* **Note** (note):
  Include any additional comments or notes about the patient. This is an optional field for further information.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before completing it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that patients understand the form and its purpose before completing it.
- Store the form securely and confidentially to protect patient information.
