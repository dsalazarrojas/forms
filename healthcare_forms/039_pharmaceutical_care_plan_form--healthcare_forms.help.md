# Pharmaceutical Care Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from healthcare professionals to ensure patient safety and effective medication management. It includes sections for patient information, medication status, follow-up appointments, and notes. This form is typically used by healthcare providers to document care plans for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the patient's medical history and any previous care plans.
2. For each section, provide accurate and relevant information about the patient.
3. Use the options provided for select fields to choose the most suitable answer.
4. Fill out the form for every patient interaction to ensure up-to-date records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (`patient_info`, Text, Not required): This field is for entering the patient's demographic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medications (2)** (`medications`, Select Multiple, Not required): Select all current medications prescribed for the patient. This will help monitor medication adherence and potential interactions.
* **Assessment (3)** (`assessment`, Text, Not required): Briefly describe the patient's medical status and any concerns.
* **Follow-up (4)** (`follow_up`, Select One, Not required): Choose the most suitable follow-up action required for the patient.
* **Medication Status (5)** (`medication_status`, Select Multiple, Not required): Indicate the patient's current medication status, such as adherence or non-adherence.
* **Notes (6)** (`notes`, Note, Not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's care plan.
* **Follow-up Status (7)** (`follow_up_status`, Select One, Not required): Indicate the follow-up status, such as scheduled or completed.
* **Next Appointment (8)** (`next_appointment`, Date, Not required): Record the date and time of the next scheduled appointment.
* **Medication Last Administered (9)** (`medication_last_administered`, Number, Not required): Enter the number of days since the last medication was administered.

## Tips

- Ensure this form is filled out for every patient interaction to maintain accurate records.
- Review the patient's medical history and previous care plans before completing this form.
- Use the select options provided for select fields to ensure accuracy.
- Update the 'Last Updated' field to track recent changes.
- Save the form regularly to prevent loss of data.
