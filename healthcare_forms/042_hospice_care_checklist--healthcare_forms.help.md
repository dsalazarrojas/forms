# Hospice Care Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Care Checklist is a tool used to evaluate and document the needs and status of patients requiring end-of-life care. It helps healthcare professionals assess the necessity and availability of hospice care services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form with the relevant information.
2. Answer the questions accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you're unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional or patient's family member if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, Required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, Optional): List the patient's current symptoms.

### Page 2: Care Needs
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, number, Optional): Rate the patient's pain level on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's medical history.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, Optional): List the patient's current medications.

### Page 3: Care Status
* **Hospice Care Necessary** (`hospice_care_necessary`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if the patient requires hospice care (Yes/No).
* **Hospice Care Status** (`hospice_care_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all that apply for the patient's current status (Active/Inactive).
* **Patient Family** (`patient_family`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's family situation.
* **Social Services** (`social_services`, text, Optional): List any social services the patient is currently receiving.
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's expected outcome.

### Page 4: Care Team
* **Caregiver Support** (`caregiver_support`, text, Optional): Describe the support provided by caregivers.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's medical team.
* **Discharge Plans** (`discharge_plans`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's discharge plans.
* **Pain Management** (`pain_management`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's pain management plan.
* **Emotional Support** (`emotional_support`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's emotional support.

### Page 5: Additional Information
* **Emotional Support** (`emotional_support`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's emotional support.
* **Spiritual Support** (`spiritual_support`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's spiritual support.
* **Cultural Sensitivity** (`cultural_sensitivity`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's cultural sensitivity needs.
* **Hospice Team** (`hospice_team`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's care team.

### Page 6: Administrative Information
* **Discharge Status** (`discharge_status`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's discharge status.
* **Hospice Care Cost** (`hospice_care_cost`, number, Optional): Estimate the cost of hospice care for the patient.
* **Hospice Care Location** (`hospice_care_location`, text, Optional): Describe the patient's care location.
* **Hospice Care Providers** (`hospice_care_providers`, text, Optional): List the patient's care providers.
* **Hospice Care Services** (`hospice_care_services`, text, Optional): List the patient's care services.
