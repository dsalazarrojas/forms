# westview-dental - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture patient information for the Westview Dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Select a date for the appointment in the "Appointment Date" field.
* Choose the time of the appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.
* Write any notes from the doctor in the "Doctor Notes" field.
* Add any personal notes from the patient in the "Patient Notes" field.
* Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
* Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
* Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, `text`, optional): Write any medical notes from the doctor.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any personal notes from the patient.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if optional fields can be skipped.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the clinic staff for assistance.
