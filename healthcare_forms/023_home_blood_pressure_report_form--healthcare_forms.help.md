# Home Blood Pressure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Blood Pressure Report Form is designed to collect data from patients regarding their home blood pressure measurements. This form helps healthcare professionals track and record vital signs, enabling them to monitor patients' conditions and provide accurate medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Systolic Pressure (1)
	* Diastolic Pressure (2)
	* Heart Rate (3)
	* Date Taken (4)
	* Time Taken (5)
2. Optional fields include:
	* Pulse (6)
	* Medication (7)
	* Notes (8)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Systolic Pressure (1)** (number, required): Enter the patient's systolic blood pressure reading. Please ensure you have measured it correctly.
* **Diastolic Pressure (2)** (number, required): Enter the patient's diastolic blood pressure reading. Please ensure you have measured it correctly.
* **Heart Rate (3)** (number, required): Enter the patient's heart rate reading. Please ensure you have measured it correctly.
* **Date Taken (4)** (date, required): Select the date on which you took the blood pressure reading.
* **Time Taken (5)** (time, required): Select the time on which you took the blood pressure reading.
* **Pulse (6)** (number, optional): Enter the patient's pulse reading if applicable.
* **Medication (7)** (note, optional): If the patient is taking any medication, enter a brief description of it.
* **Notes (8)** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the patient's condition.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate measurements and recording of data for better medical care.
* If a patient is taking medication, make sure to note it for future reference.
* Keep the form up to date and accurate for the patient's benefit.
