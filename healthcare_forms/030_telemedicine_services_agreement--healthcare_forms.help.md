# Telemedicine Services Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about telemedicine services offered by a healthcare provider, including physician and patient information, services provided, frequency and duration of services, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Telemedicine Services Agreement Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of the telemedicine services being offered.
2.  **Physician Information**: Enter the name, address, and contact number of the physician providing the telemedicine services. This information will help patients contact the physician for any questions or concerns.
3.  **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's information, including their name, address, and contact number. This information will help the physician provide personalized care and services.
4.  **Services Provided**: Select the telemedicine services offered, such as in-office visits, virtual visits, phone consultations, and messaging.
5.  **Services Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the services provided, such as 1-2 visits per week, 3-4 visits per week, or daily visits.
6.  **Services Duration**: Select the duration of each service, such as 30 minutes, 15-30 minutes, or less than 15 minutes.
7.  **Services Agreement**: Choose how the agreement will be handled, such as signed and dated or unsigned agreement sent via email.
8.  **Agreement Signed**: This field is not used as it is not a required field.
9.  **Agreement Signed Date**: This field is not used as it is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Telemedicine Services Agreement Form**: This field is for a brief description of the telemedicine services being offered.
* **Physician Information**: This field is used to collect the physician's name, address, and contact number.
* **Patient Information**: This field is used to collect the patient's name, address, and contact number.
* **Services Provided**: This field allows you to select the telemedicine services offered, such as in-office visits, virtual visits, phone consultations, and messaging.
* **Services Frequency**: This field enables you to choose the frequency of the services provided.
* **Services Duration**: This field allows you to select the duration of each service.
* **Services Agreement**: This field is used to select how the agreement will be handled.
* **Agreement Signed**: This field is not used as it is not a required field.
* **Agreement Signed Date**: This field is not used as it is not a required field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the telemedicine services being offered.
* Patients should be informed about the services provided, frequency, and duration to ensure clear communication and understanding.
* The agreement terms should be reviewed and signed by both parties to avoid any confusion or disputes.
