# Orthodontic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to provide informed consent for orthodontic treatments or consultations. It collects essential information about the patient, emergency contacts, and other relevant details for treatment planning and administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Provide your signature as the patient to acknowledge you have read and understand the consent terms.
3. Enter your date of birth, if requested.
4. Enter the date and time you are giving your consent.
5. Fill in the emergency contact's information, including their name, phone number, and email.
6. Specify the relationship with the emergency contact.
7. Provide an alternative phone number, email, and address for the emergency contact, if applicable.
8. Specify the emergency contact's relationship with you.
9. Review and sign the consent terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, and email for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are giving your consent to the orthodontic treatment or consultation.
* **Time of Consent** (`consent_date_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you are giving your consent.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Provide your signature as the patient to acknowledge you have read and understand the consent terms.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Provide your signature as the healthcare provider.
* **Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your patient's name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe the patient's medical condition or reason for treatment.
* **Procedure** (`procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the planned procedure for the orthodontic treatment or consultation.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the treatment plan or recommended treatment options.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, email, and address.
* **Consent Terms** (`consent_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Review and sign the consent terms, which will be provided by the healthcare provider.
* **Date of Birth** (`consent_date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth, if requested.
* **Consent Phone** (`consent_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Alternative Phone** (`emergency_phone_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter an alternative phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Alternative Email** (`emergency_email_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter an alternative email address for the emergency contact.
* **Alternative Address** (`emergency_address_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter an alternative address for the emergency contact.
* **Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper care and treatment.
* Review and sign the consent terms to acknowledge your understanding of the treatment and risks involved.
* If you have any questions or concerns, discuss them with your healthcare provider before signing the form.
