## Purpose
The daily health questionnaire fill-up form is designed to gather information about your daily health status.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review all pages of the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately, as the data collected will be used to monitor your health trends.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about your health, please consult a medical professional.
