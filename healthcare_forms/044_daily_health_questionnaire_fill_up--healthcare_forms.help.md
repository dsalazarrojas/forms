# daily-health-questionnaire-fill-up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily health questionnaire fill-up form is designed to gather information about your daily health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review all pages of the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately, as the data collected will be used to monitor your health trends.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about your health, please consult a medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Health Questionnaire Page 1** (Required: False)
	+ This page is a note field where you can provide any comments or information about your current health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 2** (Required: False)
	+ This page is a text field where you can provide more detailed information about your health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 3** (Required: False)
	+ On this page, select one of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that best describes your current health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 4** (Required: False)
	+ In this page, select all applicable options (Normal, Fever, Cough) that describe your current health issues or concerns.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 5** (Required: False)
	+ On this page, select the date of your last health check-up or when you last felt unwell.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 6** (Required: False)
	+ On this page, select the time of your last health check-up or when you last felt unwell.
* **Health Status 7** (Required: False)
	+ Select one of the provided options (Active, Inactive) that best describes your current health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 8** (Required: False)
	+ In this page, select all applicable options (Yes, No) that describe your current health issues or concerns.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 9** (Required: False)
	+ This page is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 10** (Required: False)
	+ On this page, select one of the provided options (Yes, No) that best describes your current health status.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 11** (Required: False)
	+ In this page, select all applicable options (Yes, No) that describe your current health issues or concerns.
* **Health Questionnaire Page 12** (Required: False)
	+ This page is a text field where you can provide more detailed information about your health status.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your health, please consult a medical professional.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information.
