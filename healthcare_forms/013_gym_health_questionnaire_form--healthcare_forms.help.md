# Gym Health Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Health Questionnaire Form is designed to gather information about your health and medical conditions to ensure your safety and well-being while participating in gym activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Use your best judgment to answer each question based on the information provided.
4. If you are unsure or have any concerns, please do not hesitate to consult with a medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please provide your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please provide your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Not Required): Please provide your contact phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Not Required): Please provide your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, Not Required): Please list any pre-existing medical conditions or health concerns that may impact your ability to participate in gym activities.
* **Medication** (`medication`, Text, Not Required): Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, Not Required): Please list any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Not Required): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the type of relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Option 1** (`contact_relationship_option1`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the specific option that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Option 2** (`contact_relationship_option2`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the specific option that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, Text, Not Required): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the type of relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Option 1** (`emergency_contact_relationship_option1`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the specific option that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Option 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_option2`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the specific option that best describes your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Not Required): Please provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the month of your birth (e.g., January, February, etc.).
* **Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the day of your birth (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, Select One, Not Required): Please select the year of your birth (e.g., 1990, 1991, etc.).
* **Time Of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, Time, Not Required): Please provide your time of birth in the 12-hour format (e.g., 12:00 AM, 02:00 PM, etc.).
* **Height** (`height`, Select One, Not Required): Please select your height in the format X feet Y inches (e.g., 5' 8", 6' 2", etc.).
* **Weight** (`weight`, Select One, Not Required): Please select your weight in the format X lbs Y oz (e.g., 150 lbs, 5' 8", etc.).
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, Select One, Not Required): Please select your blood pressure reading.
* **Resting Heart Rate** (`resting_heart_rate`, Select One, Not Required): Please select your resting heart rate reading.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, Note, Not Required): Please provide any additional medical notes or comments.
