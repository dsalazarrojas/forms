# Patient Education Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's education level, evaluation method, and other relevant details for patient education platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **User Information** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Select the **Education Platform** you are using for patient education.
3.  Choose your **Evaluation Method** for patient education.
4.  Select the **Education Channels** you are using for patient education.
5.  Choose your **Submission Status** for patient education.
6.  Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** of the evaluation.
7.  Select the **Education Level**, **Patient Outcome**, **Education Outcome**, and **Evaluation Results** for patient education.
8.  Enter your **Comments** for patient education.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **1. User Information** (`user_info`, text, required):
    *   Enter your name, email, and phone number.
*   **2. Education Platform** (`education_platform`, select_one, required):
    *   Select the platform you are using for patient education.
*   **3. Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, note, required):
    *   Enter any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation form.
*   **4. Submission** (`submission`, date, optional):
    *   Enter the date of the submission.
*   **5. Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required):
    *   Enter the time of the submission.
*   **6. Education Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your education level.
*   **7. Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required):
    *   Enter your patient information.
*   **8. Email** (`email`, email, required):
    *   Enter your email address.
*   **9. Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
    *   Enter your phone number.
*   **10. Education Platforms** (`education_platforms`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select the platforms you are using for patient education.
*   **11. Education Levels** (`education_levels`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your education levels.
*   **12. Evaluation Methods** (`evaluation_methods`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your evaluation methods.
*   **13. Education Channels** (`education_channels`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select the channels you are using for patient education.
*   **14. Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your submission status.
*   **15. Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required):
    *   Enter the submission date.
*   **16. Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional):
    *   Enter the submission time.
*   **17. Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, required):
    *   Enter your comments as a patient.
*   **18. Education Planning** (`education_planning`, date, required):
    *   Enter the date of the education planning.
*   **19. Education Goals** (`education_goals`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your education goals.
*   **20. Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select the evaluation scale.
*   **21. Education Planning Status** (`education_planning_status`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select the status of your education planning.
*   **22. Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your patient outcome.
*   **23. Education Outcome** (`education_outcome`, select_multiple, required):
    *   Select your education outcome.
*   **24. Evaluation Results** (`evaluation_results`, note, required):
    *   Enter your evaluation results.
*   **25. Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
    *   Enter any additional comments.
