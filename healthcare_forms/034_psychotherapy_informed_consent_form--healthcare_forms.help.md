# Psychotherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be completed by patients before their first therapy session with a mental health professional. It's essential to understand the contents and provide accurate information to ensure a successful therapeutic relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each page carefully**: This form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific topic. Please take your time to read and understand each question before answering.
2. **Fill in your personal details**: Provide accurate and up-to-date information in the General Information section, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. **Be honest about your medical history**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may impact your therapy sessions in the Medical History section.
4. **Provide your mental health history**: Share any mental health conditions, concerns, or experiences in the Mental Health History section.
5. **List your current medications**: In the Medication section, list any medications you're currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
6. **Read and review the consent carefully**: In the Consent section, carefully read and understand the documents provided, and acknowledge that you've reviewed them.
7. **Sign and date the form**: In the Signature section, sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms.
8. **Enter your appointment details**: In the Time section, enter your scheduled appointment time and date.
9. **Enter your therapist's name**: In the Therapist section, enter your therapist's name, who will be working with you throughout your therapy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Medical History (5)**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may impact your therapy sessions.
* **Mental Health History (6)**: Share any mental health conditions, concerns, or experiences that may impact your therapy sessions.
* **Medication (7)**: List any medications you're currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
* **Consent (8)**: Carefully read and review the documents provided. Check the box to acknowledge that you've read and understand the documents.
* **Signature (9)**: Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms.
* **Date (10)**: Enter the date you're signing this form.
* **Time (11)**: Enter the time and date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Therapist (12)**: Enter your therapist's name, who will be working with you throughout your therapy.

Please keep in mind that this form is only a guide. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your therapist for clarification.
