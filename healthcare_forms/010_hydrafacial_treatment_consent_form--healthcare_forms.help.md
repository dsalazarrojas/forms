# Hydrafacial Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrafacial Treatment Consent Form is designed to capture a patient's informed consent for hydrafacial treatment. This form is used to provide clear and concise information about the patient's treatment options and to obtain their agreement to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (text, required)
Enter your full name to help us identify you properly.

### 2. **Date Of Birth** (date, required)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 3. **Contact Information** (text, optional)
This field is for your convenience, providing us with a way to contact you. Please include your phone number and email address if you'd like us to reach out to you.

### 4. **Medical History** (text, optional)
Please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may be relevant to your care.

### 5. **Treatment Options** (select_multiple, required)
Choose one or more of the following options: AHA, BHA, or OHA.

### 6. **Signature** (note, required)
Type your signature to confirm your consent.

### 7. **Contact Phone** (text, optional)
Include your phone number for us to reach out to you about your treatment.

### 8. **Contact Email** (email, optional)
Enter your email address for us to contact you about your treatment.

## Tips
Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure you've provided all necessary details.
