# Visitation Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitation Attestation Form is designed to verify the details of patient visits, ensuring that accurate records are kept for patient care and billing purposes. This form captures essential information about patients, including their name, visit date, location, and interaction with staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, marked with a `*` symbol next to the field label.
2. Enter the Patient Name in the text field provided.
3. Select the Visit Date from the calendar or enter the date manually.
4. Choose whether the visit took place at the Location Visit or not.
5. Enter the Patient ID, if applicable.
6. Enter the Staff Member Name who interacted with the patient.
7. If applicable, select the Visit Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient, as provided to you or on file.
* **Visit Date**: Select the date on which the patient visit took place. If you are unsure, you can check the patient's chart or consult with the patient.
* **Location Visit**: Confirm whether the visit occurred at the location where you are currently working. If not, please indicate that the visit took place at a different location.
* **Patient ID**: If the patient has a unique ID assigned to them, enter it here. This is often used for billing and insurance purposes.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who interacted with the patient. This is important for patient care and follow-up.
* **Visit Time**: If the visit took place at a specific time, select it from the dropdown options. If not, leave this field blank.
 
## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered, as this data may be used for billing and patient follow-up purposes.
