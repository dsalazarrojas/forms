# Men's Hormonal Health Lab Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Men's Hormonal Health Lab Review form is used to document and review lab test results for male patients. This form helps healthcare professionals gather essential information about the patient's hormonal health and review the results of lab tests to ensure accurate and thorough diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's personal details (first name, last name, email, phone number, date of birth).
2. Enter the date of visit and date of last lab test.
3. Describe the patient's symptoms.
4. Choose the type of lab test performed (Blood Test, Urine Test, or Saliva Test).
5. Enter the lab test results.
6. Select the review status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
7. Add any additional comments from the doctor.
8. Enter the patient's signature.
9. Enter the lab report.
10. Enter the medical record number.
11. Enter the doctor's signature and date.
12. Enter any doctor's special instructions.
13. Enter the date and time of the lab test.
14. Enter the lab test duration and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter the patient's last name. (Required)
* **Email** (3): Enter the patient's email address. (Required)
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter the patient's phone number. (Required)
* **Date of Birth** (5): Enter the patient's date of birth. (Required)
* **Date of Visit** (6): Enter the date of the patient's visit. (Required)
* **Date of Last Lab Test** (7): Enter the date of the last lab test. (Required)
* **Symptoms Description** (8): Describe the patient's symptoms. (Required)
* **Lab Results** (9): Choose the type of lab test performed (Blood Test, Urine Test, or Saliva Test). (Optional)
* **Review Status** (10): Select the review status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending). (Optional)
* **Additional Comments** (11): Enter any additional comments from the doctor. (Optional)
* **Patient Signature** (12): Enter the patient's signature. (Required)
* **Lab Report** (13): Enter the lab report. (Required)
* **Medical Record Number** (14): Enter the patient's medical record number. (Required)
* **Doctor Comments** (15): Enter any additional comments from the doctor. (Optional)
* **Doctor Signature** (16): Enter the doctor's signature. (Required)
* **Lab Ordering Information** (17): Enter any lab ordering information. (Optional)
* **Patient History** (18): Enter the patient's medical history. (Optional)
* **Date of Doctor’s Signature** (19): Enter the doctor's signature date. (Required)
* **Doctor’s Special Instructions** (20): Enter any special instructions from the doctor. (Required)
* **Lab Test Date** (21): Enter the date of the lab test. (Required)
* **Lab Test Time** (22): Enter the time of the lab test. (Required)
* **Lab Test Duration** (23): Enter the duration of the lab test. (Required)
* **Lab Test Frequency** (24): Enter the lab test frequency. (Required)
* **Lab Ordering Frequency** (25): Enter the frequency of lab ordering. (Required)

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid any issues with the lab review process.
- Enter the patient's signature and doctor's signature in the required format (e.g., signature, date).
