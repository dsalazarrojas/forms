# Vital Signs Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vital Signs Tracking Form is a form used to track vital signs for patient health monitoring. This form is intended to collect body temperature, pulse rate, respiration rate, and blood pressure readings for patients. This information is used to monitor patient health and provide necessary medical attention if required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options from the provided form pages.
2. Fill in the required fields for each page, which are marked as "Required" in the field explanation.
3. Enter your input for each field, using the appropriate units (e.g., °C for body temperature, mmHg for blood pressure).
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Body Temperature** (`body_temperature`, `number`, Required): Enter the body temperature of the patient in degrees Celsius (°C). This field is used to monitor the patient's body temperature.
* **Pulse Rate** (`pulse_rate`, `number`, Required): Enter the pulse rate of the patient in beats per minute (bpm). This field is used to monitor the patient's heart rate.
* **Respiration Rate** (`respiration_rate`, `number`, Required): Enter the respiration rate of the patient in breaths per minute (bpm). This field is used to monitor the patient's breathing rate.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `number`, Required): Enter the blood pressure of the patient in millimeters of mercury (mmHg). This field is used to monitor the patient's blood pressure.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any values, you can consult with medical professionals for guidance.
* Please review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
