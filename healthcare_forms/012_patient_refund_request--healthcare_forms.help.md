# Patient Refund Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow patients to request a refund for services they have paid for but not received or are disputing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Patient Refund Request**
	* Select "Yes" to confirm that the patient is requesting a refund.
2. **Patient Name**
	* Enter the patient's name as it appears on their records.
3. **Service Date**
	* Enter the date of the service they are requesting a refund for.
4. **Reason for Refund**
	* Provide a brief description of why the patient is requesting a refund.
5. **Amount Refund Request**
	* Enter the amount they are requesting as a refund.
6. **Patient Email**
	* Enter the patient's email address as it appears on their records.
7. **Patient Phone**
	* Enter the patient's phone number as it appears on their records.
8. **Description**
	* This is an optional field for any additional comments or explanations.
9. **Signature**
	* This is the staff member's signature to confirm they have received the request.
10. **Patient Signature**
	* The patient must sign here to confirm they are requesting a refund.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Refund Request** (`Yes, No`): This field indicates if the patient is requesting a refund.
* **Patient Name** (`text`): This is the patient's name as it appears on their records.
* **Service Date** (`date`): This field indicates the date of the service the patient is requesting a refund for.
* **Reason for Refund** (`text`): This is a brief description of why the patient is requesting a refund.
* **Amount Refund Request** (`number`): This field indicates the amount the patient is requesting as a refund.
* **Patient Email** (`email`): This is the patient's email address as it appears on their records.
* **Patient Phone** (`text`): This is the patient's phone number as it appears on their records.
* **Description** (`note`): This field is optional and can be used for any additional comments or explanations.
* **Signature** (`note`): This is the staff member's signature to confirm they have received the request.
* **Patient Signature** (`note`): The patient must sign here to confirm they are requesting a refund.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the patient's email and phone number to ensure they are up-to-date.
* If the patient is requesting a refund for a service that has not been provided yet, please ensure that the service has not been rescheduled or postponed.
* If the patient has not received the service and is requesting a refund, please ensure that the service date is accurate and corresponds with the patient's records.
* Please keep a record of the refund request and the patient's response for future reference.
