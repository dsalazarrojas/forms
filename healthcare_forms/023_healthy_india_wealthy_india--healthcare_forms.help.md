# healthy india wealthy india - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general survey designed to help people who are affected by various diseases in India. It is meant to collect information from individuals who are not able to fight with disease problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions on each page of the form.
* For each question, provide a response in the space provided.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **This Survey is Only to Help Peoples which are not able to fight with his disease problems* (`form_healthy_india`, text, optional): This question is a general introduction to the survey.
* **Which disease is more effective in your nearby areas* (`form_effective_disease`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Cancer
	+ Malaria
	+ Tuberculosis
* **Tell us about any person which is discriminated with any kind of disease* (`form_discriminated_person`, text, optional): Describe a person who has been discriminated against due to a disease.
* **We were try to help him her out with financially manner* (`form_financial_help`, text, optional): Explain how you would like to be helped financially.
* **Our motive is to make an healthy and wealthy India* (`form_motive`, text, optional): Describe the form's purpose in making India healthy and wealthy.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Please note that this form is for general information gathering purposes only and not for any medical or financial advice.
