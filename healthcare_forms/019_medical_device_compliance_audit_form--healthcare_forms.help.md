# Medical Device Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from facilities regarding the compliance status of medical devices. It will help in the process of conducting audits and ensuring that medical devices meet regulatory standards. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Please note that this form is divided into several sections. Follow the instructions carefully to ensure a smooth completion process.

1. Start by filling out the user information section.
2. Enter the necessary details in the "User Name" field.
3. Enter the "Organization Name" of the relevant facility or company.
4. Choose the type of medical device from the "Medical Device Type" dropdown list.
5. Fill in the "Compliance Date" field, which refers to the date of the latest compliance audit or submission.
6. If applicable, enter the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" details of the person responsible for the audit.
7. Provide a detailed description of the medical device in the "Medical Device Description" field.
8. Enter the name and address of the facility where the medical device is used or manufactured.
9. Enter the "Facility Address", "Facility City", "Facility State", "Facility Postal Code", and "Facility Country" details.
10. Enter the "Facility Phone" and "Facility Fax" numbers.
11. Enter the facility's "Facility Email".
12. Choose the type of audit from the "Audit Type" dropdown list.
13. Fill in the "Audit Date" field for the scheduled or completed audit.
14. Enter the name and contact details of the auditor in the respective fields.
15. Enter any additional comments or notes from the auditor in the "Auditor Notes" field.
