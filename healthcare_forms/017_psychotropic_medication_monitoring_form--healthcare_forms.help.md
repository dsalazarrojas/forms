# psychotropic_medication_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to monitor the mental health status of patients undergoing psychotropic medication treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's information in the **patient_info** field. This field is not required but can be useful for quick reference.
2. List the patient's current medications in the **medication_list** field. This field is also not required but can help track medication changes.
3. Add any relevant notes about the patient's mental status and treatment progress in the **notes** field. This field is not required but can be helpful for future reference.
4. Describe the patient's current mental status in the **patient_status** field. This field is not required but can aid in tracking progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, ID number, or any other relevant details.
* **medication_list** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List all the medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Record any notes about the patient's mental status and treatment progress, such as changes in mood, behavior, or treatment response.
* **patient_status** (`patient_status`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current mental status, including any changes or concerns.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* This form is intended to supplement, not replace, comprehensive medical records.
* Review and update the form regularly to track patients' progress.
