# Vaccine Distribution Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the efficiency of vaccine distribution processes in various settings, such as hospitals, clinics, and community centers.
It helps assess the effectiveness of vaccination teams, vaccination sites, and vaccine types.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the evaluation period from the list of options.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the size of the vaccination team.
4. Select the location where the vaccine was distributed (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Community Center, Home, or Other).
5. Choose the type of vaccine distributed (e.g., COVID-19, Flu, MMR).
6. Enter a score out of 10 to evaluate the efficiency of the vaccine distribution process.
7. Leave comments or feedback about the evaluation.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluating Period** (`evaluation_period`, select one): Choose the period of time being evaluated (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date): Enter the date of the evaluation (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Vaccination Team Size** (`vaccination_team_size`, text): Enter the size of the vaccination team.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, select multiple): Select all the locations where the vaccine was distributed (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Community Center, Home, Other).
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select one): Choose the type of vaccine distributed (e.g., COVID-19, Flu, MMR).
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number): Enter a score out of 10 to evaluate the efficiency of the vaccine distribution process.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Leave any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select one): Choose a rating out of 5 to evaluate the overall experience.
