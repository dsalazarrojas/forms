# Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Progress Note form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of a patient's medical progress. This form is used to document patient information, visit details, and physician observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date of Visit" field.
3. Enter the time of the visit in the "Time of Visit" field.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the physician who attended the visit in the "Physician Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit, either by selecting it from the calendar or typing it manually.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the visit, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Physician Name**: If the visit was conducted by a physician, enter their name here.
