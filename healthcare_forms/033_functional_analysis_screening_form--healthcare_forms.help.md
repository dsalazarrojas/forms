# Functional Analysis Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Analysis Screening Form is a tool used to collect information about a patient's functional abilities in various aspects, helping healthcare professionals to assess and provide appropriate care or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Functional Analysis Screening Form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have a genuine reason for filling out the form, such as preparing for a surgery or assessing mobility issues.
*   Read through each section carefully and answer the questions according to the patient's condition.
*   Fill in the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
*   Select all relevant medical conditions that apply to the patient in the "Medical Conditions" section.
*   Answer the questions related to functional abilities in the "Functional Ability" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
• **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will enter the patient's details, such as their name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.

### Medical Conditions
• **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): In this section, select all medical conditions that apply to the patient from the available options.

### Functional Ability Bath
• **Functional Ability Bath** (`functional_ability_bath`, `number`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to bathe themselves. Please respond with a numerical value indicating the level of difficulty or ease of this activity for the patient. A lower value indicates a greater level of difficulty.

### Functional Ability Dress
• **Functional Ability Dress** (`functional_ability_dress`, `number`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to dress themselves. Please respond with a numerical value indicating the level of difficulty or ease of this activity for the patient. A lower value indicates a greater level of difficulty.

### Functional Ability Ironing
• **Functional Ability Ironing** (`functional_ability_ironing`, `number`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to iron clothing. Please respond with a numerical value indicating the level of difficulty or ease of this activity for the patient. A lower value indicates a greater level of difficulty.

### Functional Ability Stairs
• **Functional Ability Stairs** (`functional_ability_stairs`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to use stairs. Please select "Yes" if the patient can use stairs without difficulty, and "No" if they cannot.

### Functional Ability Walk
• **Functional Ability Walk** (`functional_ability_walk`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to walk. Please select all applicable options that describe the patient's walking ability, such as "Yes" for easy walking and "No" for difficulty or inability to walk.

### Functional Ability Kneel
• **Functional Ability Kneel** (`functional_ability_kneel`, `text`, required: false): This question is about the patient's ability to kneel. Please respond with a brief description of the patient's ability to kneel, such as "yes" for easy kneel, "no" for difficulty or inability to kneel, or other descriptive text.
