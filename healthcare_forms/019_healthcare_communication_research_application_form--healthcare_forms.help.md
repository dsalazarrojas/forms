# Healthcare Communication Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

### Tips

To complete this form efficiently, please note the following:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
*   For each field, provide accurate and relevant data according to your research application.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Research Application** (`research_application`): Select the type of research application you are submitting (Applicant Name, Contact Person, Application Date). This field helps us track the nature of your research application.
    
    *   **Applicant Name**: Enter your name as the applicant or researcher.
    *   **Contact Person**: If your institution or research center has a contact person for this application, enter their name.
    *   **Application Date**: Enter the date of your research application submission.
*   **Review Proposal** (`review_proposal`): This field is for reviewers or editors to leave notes and comments on your research proposal. Please note that this field is required.
*   **Organize Data** (`organize_data`): Select where your research data is organized (University, Hospital, Research Team). This helps in understanding your data management practices.
*   **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your research application and review process.
*   **Research Details** (`research_details`): Provide details about your research project or study. This includes numbers (e.g., 1-10).
*   **Application Status** (`application_status`): Select the current status of your research application (In Progress, Completed, Rejected). This helps in tracking the application's progress.
*   **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`): Select the channels you used for communication (Email, Phone, Mail). This helps in understanding how we can contact you.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted your research application.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Enter the time you submitted your research application.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of your research application review.
*   **Review Time** (`review_time`): Enter the time of your research application review.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the status of your research application review (In Progress, Completed, Rejected).
*   **Applicant Feedback** (`applicant_feedback`): Provide any additional feedback you have regarding your application or our review process. This is optional.
*   **Reviewer Feedback** (`reviewer_feedback`): Provide feedback for the reviewers who reviewed your application. This is optional.
*   **Research Team** (`research_team`): Select the team(s) involved in your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Research Group** (`research_group`): Select the group(s) associated with your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Research Institute** (`research_institute`): Select the institute(s) involved with your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Department** (`department`): Select the department(s) associated with your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the person in charge of this research application.
*   **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact email of the person in charge of this research application. This is required.
*   **Research Area** (`research_area`): Select the research area of your project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Research Topic** (`research_topic`): Select the topic of your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Research Project** (`research_project`): Select the research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Research Funding** (`research_funding`): Select the funding source(s) for your research project or study (University, Hospital, Research Team).
*   **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`): Provide additional comments or explanations about your research application submission. This is optional.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, refer to this help guide or contact our support team.

Note: This help guide is designed to be concise and practical, ensuring that you can quickly and efficiently complete the form. If you require further clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out.
