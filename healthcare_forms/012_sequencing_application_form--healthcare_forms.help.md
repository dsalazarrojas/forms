# sequencing_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sequencing application form is designed to gather information from applicants to determine their eligibility and suitability for a sequencing program. This form helps us understand their background, contact details, and specific requirements for the sequencing program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter a unique Form ID to identify your application.
2. Provide your name as an applicant.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you.
4. Enter your phone number for communication.
5. Provide your address for contact and logistics purposes.
6. Indicate if you are a researcher or not.
7. Select the sequencing area(s) you are interested in.
8. Provide detailed information about your sequencing needs and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies your application. This will help us track and manage your submission.
* **Applicant Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further communication. We will use this email to send you information about your application status.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your application and any additional information you might need.
* **Address**: Provide your address so we can contact you for any logistics or delivery-related purposes.
* **Researcher**: Check 'Yes' if you are a researcher, and 'No' if you are not. This helps us understand your background and requirements.
* **Sequencing Area**: Select the area(s) where you are interested in sequencing. You can choose more than one area.
* **Sequencing Details**: Provide a detailed description of your sequencing requirements and needs. This will help us better understand your request and tailor our assistance.
