# Wellnees Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Wellnees Profile" form is used to collect information about healthcare professionals, specifically their well-being and medical profile. This form is designed to help us better understand your background and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use the correct email format.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Select your medical license from the "Medical license" dropdown menu.
6. Check any applicable specialties from the "Specialty" multi-select menu. You must select at least one specialty.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact number" field.
8. Select your medical school from the "Medical school" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical license**: Select your valid medical license.
* **Specialty**: Select one or more of your specialties. You must check at least one specialty.
	+ Note: This field is required.
* **Contact number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Medical school**: Select your medical school.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to use the correct formats for date of birth and email.
- If you have any issues with your medical license, contact us for assistance.
