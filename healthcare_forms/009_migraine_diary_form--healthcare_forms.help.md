# Migraine Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migraine Diary Form is a tool for tracking and analyzing individual migraines, helping healthcare professionals make informed treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Headache Description** (`headache_description`, text, optional): Describe the characteristics of your current or recent headache, including any associated symptoms, such as pain location, severity, and duration.
* **Aura Description** (`aura_description`, text, optional): If you experienced an aura (a warning sign of an impending migraine), describe it in detail, including the characteristics and duration.
* **Headache Frequency** (`headache_frequency`, text, optional): Estimate the frequency of your migraines per week or month.
* **Severity** (`severity`, number, optional): Rate the severity of your current or recent headache on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the least severe and 10 being the most severe.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Report the length of your current or recent headache in hours or minutes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Record the date and time of when your current or recent headache started.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Record the date and time of when your current or recent headache ended.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications you took for your current or recent headache, including dosage and frequency.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, optional): Describe any treatments or therapies you received for your current or recent headache.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, text, optional): Report any side effects you experienced from medication or treatment, including duration and severity.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and accurate as possible when completing each field.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Complete the form regularly, ideally after each migraine attack, to track and analyze your migraines effectively.
- Review and update your previous entries regularly to monitor changes in your migraine patterns.
