# hospice_license_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospice license application form is a document used to apply for a hospice license. This form collects necessary information from applicants to process and evaluate their application for a hospice license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
5. Enter your address in the "address_line_1", "address_line_2", "city", "state", and "zip" fields.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Enter your medical license number in the "medical_license_number" field.
9. Select the type of medical license you hold from the "license_type" field.
10. Enter the name of the facility you are applying for a license in the "facility_name" field.
11. Enter the start and end dates of your facility's operation in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively.
12. Enter the hours of operation for your facility in the "hours_of_operation" field.
13. Enter the number of staff members working at your facility in the "staff_count" field.
14. Enter the address of your facility in the "facility_address" field.
15. Enter the city of your facility in the "facility_city" field.
16. Enter the state of your facility in the "facility_state" field.
17. Enter the zip code of your facility in the "facility_zip" field.
18. Select the services offered by your facility from the "facility_services" field.
19. Enter a description of your facility in the "facility_description" field.
20. Select the license status of your facility from the "facility_license_status" field.
21. Add any additional notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter your address, line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter your address, line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical license you hold (Medicare, Medicaid, or Private).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility you are applying for a license.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your facility's operation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your facility's operation.
* **Hours of Operation** (`hours_of_operation`, text, optional): Enter the hours of operation for your facility.
* **Staff Count** (`staff_count`, text, optional): Enter the number of staff members working at your facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your facility.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your facility.
* **Facility State** (`facility_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your facility.
* **Facility Zip** (`facility_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your facility.
* **Facility Services** (`facility_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services offered by your facility (Home Health Services, Hospice Care Services, or General Medical Services).
* **Facility Description** (`facility_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your facility.
* **Facility License Status** (`facility_license_status`, select_one, optional): Select the license status of your facility (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your application.
