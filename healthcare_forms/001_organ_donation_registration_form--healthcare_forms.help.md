# Organ Donation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from individuals who wish to register for organ donation. The form is intended to be a straightforward and efficient way to gather necessary contact and identification details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it follows the standard email format (e.g., `example@email.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, Required): Enter your personal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, Required): Enter your personal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for updates or further information.
* **Date of Birth** (date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, as found on your identification documents.
