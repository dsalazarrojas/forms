# Health and Travel History Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an individual's health status, travel history, and potential exposure to COVID-19. The form helps healthcare professionals accurately assess the individual's medical history and identify potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Ensure that you have the most up-to-date information about your health status and travel history.
* Review each question carefully to understand the type of information being requested.
* If unsure about any question, contact a healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **2. Current Health Status** (Required)
Please answer all questions truthfully about your current health status, including any symptoms, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **3. Countries Visited in Last 2 Weeks** (Required)
List all countries you have visited in the last 2 weeks. If none, select "None".
* **4. Reasons for Visit** (Required)
State the reasons for your visit, such as business, vacation, or medical treatment.
* **5. Symptoms** (Required)
List all the symptoms you are experiencing, including any new or unusual symptoms.
* **6. Countries to Visit in Next 2 Weeks** (Required)
List all countries you plan to visit in the next 2 weeks. If none, select "None".
* **7. Contacted Areas** (Required)
List all areas you have contacted in the last 2 weeks, such as hospitals, clinics, or public areas.
* **8. Contact with COVID-19** (Required)
Answer "True" if you have been in contact with anyone who has COVID-19 in the last 2 weeks, or "False" if you have not.
