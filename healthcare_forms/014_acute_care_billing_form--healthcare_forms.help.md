# Acute Care Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acute Care Billing Form is a document designed for healthcare providers to record and manage billing information for patients in acute care situations. It captures essential details such as patient contact and insurance information, services rendered, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and required fields.
2. Review the form carefully, filling in all required fields.
3. Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their insurance.

### 2. **Account or medical record number** (`account_number`, text, optional)
Enter the hospital or clinic record ID if applicable.

### 3. **Insurance provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required)
Enter the primary insurer's name.

### 4. **Insurance policy number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, required)
Enter the subscriber or policy ID.

### 5. **Billing contact name and phone** (`billing_contact`, text, required)
Enter the account payor contact details.

### 6. **Services rendered description** (`services_rendered`, text, required)
Enter a brief list of services billed.

### 7. **Total amount due** (`total_amount_due`, number, required)
Enter the full numeric amount due.

### 8. **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required)
Select one of the following payment methods:
	* Insurance
	* Self pay
	* Payment plan

### 9. **Additional billing notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional)
Enter any special billing instructions or additional information.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Verify all information with patients and their insurance providers before finalizing the billing process.
