# Daily Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Plan form helps individuals prioritize and organize their daily activities, focusing on different aspects of their lives such as productive, physical, social, entertaining, meaningful, and relaxing activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the activities you plan to engage in on a daily basis from the respective categories: Productive, Physical, Social, Entertaining, Meaningful, and Relaxing.
2. For each category, choose all the activities that apply to you.
3. If you want to add any additional comments or reminders, you can use the "Notes" field.
4. If you want to receive email reminders, input your email addresses in the "Email Reminders" field.
5. Submit the form to finalize your daily plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Productive** (`productive_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the productive activities you plan to do today, such as Reading, Writing, Work, Errands, or Chores.
* **Physical** (`physical_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the physical activities you plan to do today, such as Exercise, Yoga, Walking, or Sports.
* **Social** (`social_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the social activities you plan to do today, such as Phone call, Video call, Meet with friends, or In-person meetup.
* **Entertaining** (`entertaining_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the entertaining activities you plan to do today, such as Movie night, Game night, TV show, or Dinner out.
* **Meaningful** (`meaningful_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the meaningful activities you plan to do today, such as Personal project, Volunteer work, Learning something new, or Meditate.
* **Relaxing** (`relaxing_activities`, Select Multiple): Select all the relaxing activities you plan to do today, such as Nature walk, Meditate, Daydreaming, or Reading a book.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note): Add any additional comments or reminders you have.
* **Email Reminders** (`email_reminders`, Email): Input your email addresses to receive reminders or notifications.
