# Medical Insurance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical insurance application information from applicants. It is used to gather essential details about the applicant's medical history, employment, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the applicant information section, which includes basic details about the applicant.
2. Proceed to the medical history section, where you will provide details about your medical history.
3. Next, provide your employment information, including your work experience and employment status.
4. You will then be asked to provide contact information, including your address and phone number.
5. If necessary, upload any supporting documents.
6. Finally, select your medical insurance information, which will determine the type of insurance coverage you are eligible for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (text, required): Enter your full name, date of birth, and other basic details about yourself.

### 2. Insurance Information
* **Insurance Information** (text, not required): Enter any relevant information about your current medical insurance, if applicable.

### 3. Medical History
* **Medical History** (text, not required): Describe any significant medical events or conditions you have experienced.

### 4. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (text, not required): Enter your address and phone number for communication purposes.

### 5. Upload Documents
* **Upload Documents** (text, not required): Upload any supporting documents, such as medical records or identification documents.

### 6. Employment Information
* **Employment Information 1** (number, not required): Enter your employment status (e.g., employed, self-employed, etc.).
* **Employment Information 2** (text, not required): Enter any other relevant employment details.

### 7. Medical Insurance Information
* **Medical Insurance Information** (select_one, not required): Select the type of medical insurance coverage you are eligible for.

### 8-9. Medical History
* **Medical History 1** (date, not required): Enter a date related to your medical history.
* **Medical History 2** (select_multiple, not required): Select any medical conditions you have experienced.

### 10-11. Contact Information
* **Contact Information 1** (text, not required): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Contact Information 2** (select_one, not required): Select any other relevant contact information.

### 12-13. Upload Documents
* **Upload Documents 1** (text, not required): Upload any relevant documents, such as identification documents.
* **Upload Documents 2** (text, not required): Upload any other relevant documents.

### 14-15. Employment Information
* **Employment Information 2** (number, not required): Enter any additional employment information.
* **Upload Documents 3** (select_multiple, not required): Select any other relevant documents to upload.

### 16-17. Medical Insurance Information
* **Medical Insurance Information 2** (select_one, not required): Select any additional medical insurance information.

### 18-19. Upload Documents
* **Upload Documents 4** (text, not required): Upload any additional documents.
* **Upload Documents 5** (text, not required): Upload any other relevant documents.

### 20. Employment Information
* **Employment Information 3** (text, not required): Enter any other relevant employment details.

Note: Fields marked with "required" are essential for form submission, while fields marked as "not required" are optional and can be completed according to your needs.
