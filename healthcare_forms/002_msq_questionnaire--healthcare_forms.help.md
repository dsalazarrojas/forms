# msq_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The msq_questionnaire form is designed to collect information from users about their migraines. This information will help healthcare professionals understand the user's experiences with migraines and provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the page by typing your response in the provided text field.
2. Select your answer from the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help healthcare professionals provide better care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_a_migraine**: This is the first question on the form, where you will be asked to describe what you think migraines are.
* **how_migraines_affect_lifestyle**: Please type an answer to this question, describing how migraines affect your lifestyle.
* **how_migraines_affect_physical_health**: Type an answer to this question, describing how migraines affect your physical health.
* **what_is_the_frequency_of_your_migraines**: This question asks about the frequency of your migraines. Please respond with a numerical value, e.g., 1-7, where 1 is "daily" and 7 is "rarely".
* **how_severe_are_your_migraines**: This question asks how severe your migraines are. Type your answer to this question, describing the level of pain or discomfort you experience.
* **which_days_of_week_do_you_experience_migraines**: Please select all the days of the week you experience migraines. You can select multiple options.
* **what_time_of_day_do_you_experience_migraines**: This question is not required, so you can choose to skip it.
* **what_type_of_migraine_do_you_experience**: Select the type of migraine you experience from the options provided.
* **how_migraines_affect_mood**: Type an answer to this question, describing how migraines affect your mood.
* **how_migraines_affect_cognition**: Type an answer to this question, describing how migraines affect your cognitive function.
