# Acupuncture Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture Appointment Form is a tool for patients to book an appointment with an acupuncturist. The form is used to gather important information about the patient, including their contact details, appointment preferences, and a brief health summary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of appointment you would like to schedule.
3. Choose a preferred practitioner from the list.
4. Specify a preferred date and time for your appointment.
5. Briefly describe your primary reason for visiting and the duration of your symptoms.
6. Indicate your current pain level and pregnancy status.
7. Accept the 24-hour cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Full Name (Required)
* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Email Address (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to receive important updates and reminders about your appointment.

### 3. Phone Number (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.

### 4. Type of Appointment (Required)
* **Type of Appointment**: Select the type of appointment that best suits your needs:
	+ Initial Assessment and Treatment
	+ Follow-up Treatment
	+ Express Treatment (30 mins)
	+ Cupping Only Session

### 5. Preferred Practitioner (Optional)
* **Preferred Practitioner**: Choose a practitioner from the list:
	+ No Preference
	+ Dr. Chen
	+ Dr. Smith
	+ Dr. Lee

### 6. Preferred Date (Required)
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date for your appointment.

### 7. Preferred Time (Required)
* **Preferred Time**: Select the preferred time for your appointment.

### 8. -- Brief Health Summary --
* **Brief Health Summary (Optional)**: This is a note field for patients to provide a brief description of their health status. This field is not required but is helpful for our team to understand your needs.

### 9. Primary Reason for Visit (Required)
* **Primary Reason for Visit**: Briefly describe the reason you are visiting the acupuncturist.

### 10. Duration of Symptoms (Required)
* **Duration of Symptoms**: Select how long you have been experiencing symptoms:
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1-4 weeks
	+ 1-6 months
	+ Chronic (Over 6 months)

### 11. Current Pain Level (Optional)
* **Current Pain Level**: Indicate your current pain level on a scale of 0-10:
	+ 0 = No pain
	+ 10 = Severe pain

### 12. Pregnancy Status (Required)
* **Pregnancy Status**: Indicate whether you are currently pregnant:
	+ False
	+ True
	+ Unsure

### 13. I acknowledge the 24-hour cancellation policy (Required)
* **I acknowledge the 24-hour cancellation policy**: Select that you accept the policy to ensure timely scheduling and respect for our staff's time.
