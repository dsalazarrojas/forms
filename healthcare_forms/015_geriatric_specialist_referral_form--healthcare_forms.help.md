# Geriatric Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Geriatric Specialist Referral Form is designed to collect relevant information from patients for referral to a geriatric specialist. This information will aid in determining the best course of action for the patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient demographic information (Page 1: Patient Info).
2. Specify the reason for the referral (Page 2: Referral Reason).
3. Enter any relevant medical history, specialist information, medication allergies, social history, and physical exam findings (Pages 3-7: Specialist Info, Medical History, Medication Allergy, Social History, and Physical Exam).
4. Report any information regarding the patient's cognitive impact (Page 8: Cognitive Impact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (Page 1)
	+ This page collects demographic information from the patient.
* **Referral Reason** (Page 2)
	+ Please specify the reason for the referral.
* **Specialist Info** (Page 3)
	+ Provide any relevant information about the specialist the patient will be referred to.
* **Medical History** (Page 4)
	+ Enter any relevant medical history that may impact the patient's care.
* **Medication Allergy** (Page 5)
	+ List any medications the patient is allergic to.
* **Social History** (Page 6)
	+ Enter any information that may impact the patient's social situation or living conditions.
* **Physical Exam** (Page 7)
	+ Report any relevant physical exam findings.
* **Cognitive Impact** (Page 8)
	+ Provide any information related to the patient's cognitive impact or functional status.

## Tips
- Complete the form accurately and thoroughly for each patient referral.
- Review the form for completeness before submitting it for referral.
- Ensure that the form is filled out by a healthcare professional.
