# TEMPLATE COVID 19 Health Screening Daily - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended to be a daily self-assessment tool for employees to report their health status and potential COVID-19 symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID in the "employee_id" field.
2. Provide your name in the "employee_name" field.
3. Select your current health status from the options: "good", "bad", or "critical".
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field (optional).
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
6. Report any fever by entering a number (in degrees Fahrenheit) in the "fever" field.
7. Indicate if you are experiencing a cough by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "cough" field.
8. Describe any shortness of breath in the "shortness_of_breath" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id**: Enter your unique employee ID as assigned by HR. This is a required field.
* **employee_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or in our records. This is a required field.
* **health_status**: Select your current health status from the options: "good", "bad", or "critical". This helps us assess any potential risks.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes (optional). This field is not required.
* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number for emergency purposes (optional). This field is not required.
* **fever**: If you are experiencing a fever, enter the number of degrees Fahrenheit in the box. Please be honest about any symptoms.
* **cough**: Select "Yes" if you are experiencing a cough, and "No" otherwise. This helps us monitor potential respiratory issues.
* **shortness_of_breath**: Describe any shortness of breath you are experiencing in this field.
