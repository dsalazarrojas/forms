# Hospital Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Admission Form is designed to collect information from patients during the admission process in a hospital setting. This form helps hospital staff gather essential details about the patient's medical history, allergies, and current medications, which are crucial for providing proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the correct answers for each field based on the patient's condition.
3. Provide accurate and detailed answers for all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information. This field helps the hospital staff identify the patient and contact them if needed.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions. Provide a detailed account of any relevant medical events, such as surgeries, hospitalizations, or significant health issues.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the medications or substances the patient is allergic to. This includes penicillin, amoxicillin, and lipitor. Be sure to check all the relevant boxes to ensure the patient's safety.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the current medications the patient is taking, such as aspirin, ibuprofen, or prednisone. Select only the medications the patient is currently taking, as this information is crucial for their treatment and medication management.
