# Patient Experience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Experience Interview Consent Form is designed to collect information from patients about their experiences within this healthcare organization. This form helps healthcare providers understand patient needs and preferences, enabling them to improve patient satisfaction and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields.
3. Submit the form to initiate the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Experience Interview Consent Form**
This is the main title of the form, which is required to be filled out.
### 2. **I am a patient at this healthcare organization**
Please select 'True' if you are currently a patient here.
### 3. **Are you currently a patient at this healthcare organization?**
Please select 'True' if you are currently a patient here.
### 4. **Do you have any concerns or questions about this interview?**
Please provide any questions or concerns you may have regarding the interview.
### 5. **How long have you been a patient at this healthcare organization?**
Please provide the length of time you have been a patient here.
### 6. **What is your relationship to the healthcare provider?**
Please describe your relationship to the healthcare provider.
### 7. **What are your expectations for this interview?**
Please provide your expectations for this interview.
### 8. **How do you prefer to be contacted?**
Select all preferred contact methods:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Mail
	* Other (please specify)
### 9. **Other (please specify)**
If "Other" is selected in question 8, please provide your preferred contact method here.
### 10. **Patient Experience Interview Consent Form (not used in this form)**
This field is not part of the form and has been removed.
