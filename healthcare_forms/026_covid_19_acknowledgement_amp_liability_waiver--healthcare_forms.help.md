# COVID 19 Acknowledgement and Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Acknowledgement and Liability Waiver form is a crucial document that helps ensure participants understand and acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19, as well as their rights and responsibilities while participating in activities or services offered by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your personal information, including your name, date of birth, email address, phone number, street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
2.  Confirm your participation in the activity or service by selecting "Yes I Participate" in the "Voluntary Participation" field.
3.  Acknowledge your understanding of the risks associated with COVID-19 by selecting "Yes I Understand" in the "COVID-19 Risk Acknowledgment" field.
4.  Confirm that you have completed a health screening by selecting "Yes - No symptoms" or "Yes - With symptoms disclosed" in the "Health Screening Confirmation" field.
5.  Agree to follow safety protocols by selecting "Yes I Agree" in the "Agreement to Follow Safety Protocols" field.
6.  Release the organization from liability by selecting "Yes I Release" in the "Liability Release" field.
7.  Assume all risks related to COVID-19 by selecting "Yes I Assume" in the "Assumption of Risk" field.
8.  Acknowledge that you understand if you are high risk by selecting "I am not high risk" or "I am high risk and accept" in the "High Risk Acknowledgment" field.
9.  Confirm that you are responsible for your medical costs by selecting "Yes I Understand" in the "Insurance Responsibility" field.
10.  Agree to report any symptoms by selecting "Yes I Agree" in the "Symptom Reporting Agreement" field.
11.  Cooperate with contact tracing by selecting "Yes I Agree" in the "Contact Tracing Cooperation" field.
12.  Understand that policies may change without notice by selecting "Yes I Understand" in the "Policy Changes Acknowledgment" field.
13.  Confirm if you are a minor by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Is Participant a Minor" field.
14.  If you are a minor, fill in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field with the name of your parent or guardian.
15.  Sign the "Participant Signature" field with your full name.
16.  If you are a minor, sign the "Parent or Guardian Signature" field with the signature of your parent or guardian.
17.  If you have an optional witness, fill in their name in the "Witness Name" field.
18.  If you have an optional witness, sign their name in the "Witness Signature" field.
19.  Sign the "Date Signed" field with the current date.
