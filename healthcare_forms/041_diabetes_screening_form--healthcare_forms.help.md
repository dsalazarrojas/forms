# Diabetes Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information for patients undergoing a diabetes screening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have answered all required questions.
2. Enter your blood glucose levels in the "blood_glucose_levels" field.
3. Enter your body mass index (BMI) in the "body_mass_index_bmi" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **blood_glucose_levels** (`blood_glucose_levels`, text, required): Enter your current blood glucose levels, as measured by a blood test or other approved method.
* **body_mass_index_bmi** (`body_mass_index_bmi`, number, required): Enter your body mass index (BMI), calculated as your weight in kilograms divided by the square root of your height in meters.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required") to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, consult your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Ensure that you are completing the form accurately and honestly, as the information collected will be used for medical purposes.
