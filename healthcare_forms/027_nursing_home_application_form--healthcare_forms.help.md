# Nursing Home Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are looking to be admitted to a nursing home due to their own medical needs or preferences. It is used to gather information on personal details, medical conditions, contact information, and preferred language.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, Phone, Street, City, and ZIP.
2. Indicate your Medical Conditions by selecting from the provided options.
3. Provide contact information for your Emergency Contact.
4. If applicable, select your Medical Insurance and Healthcare Provider.
5. Enter your Nursing Home Name (if applicable).
6. Briefly describe your Reason for Application.
7. Fill in your Social Security Number.
8. Indicate your Preferred Language and Nursing Home Rating (if applicable).
9. Select your Care Level and Other Care Needs (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Date of birth. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email**: Your email address. Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Your phone number. Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Street**: Your street address. Enter your street address in the format "123 Main St".
* **City**: Your city. Enter the name of your city or town.
* **State**: If you have a medical condition that requires special care, please select "Yes".
* **ZIP**: Your postal code. Enter your 5-digit or 6-digit ZIP code.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you are experiencing. Select from the provided options.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of your emergency contact. Enter the name of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of your emergency contact. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relation**: Relationship with your emergency contact. Enter the relationship (e.g. Spouse, Child, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Address of your emergency contact. Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Physician Name**: Name of your physician. Enter the name of your treating physician.
* **Physician Phone**: Phone number of your physician. Enter the phone number of your physician.
* **Medical Insurance**: If you have medical insurance, please select "Yes".
* **Healthcare Provider**: If you have a healthcare provider, please select "Yes".
* **Nursing Home Name**: If you are currently in a nursing home, please enter the name of the nursing home.
* **Reason for Application**: Briefly describe your reason for applying to a nursing home.
* **Social Security Number**: Your social security number. Enter your social security number.
* **Preferred Language**: Indicate your preferred language.
* **Nursing Home Rating**: If applicable, select the rating of the nursing home you are applying to.
* **Care Level**: Select your care level (High, Low).
* **Other Care Needs**: List any other care needs you require. Select from the provided options.
