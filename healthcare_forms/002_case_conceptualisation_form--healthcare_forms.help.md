# Case Conceptualisation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Conceptualisation Form is designed to help healthcare professionals gather comprehensive information about a patient's case, including their identification details, medical history, and treatment plan. This form is meant to aid in developing an appropriate treatment strategy and identifying potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Read each question carefully**, and ensure you answer all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
- **Use internal reference codes** for Patient ID or Case Number when filling out the patient's identifier field.
- **Describe each answer** in the space provided for text fields.
- **Choose the correct risk level** for the patient's risk assessment.
- **Select 'Yes' or 'No'** for the clinical review required field.
- **Schedule the follow-up review date**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID or Case Number**: Enter the patient's internal reference code or case number. This will help us identify and track the patient's case.
* **Clinician Name**: Enter the name of the clinician or healthcare professional conducting the assessment.
* **Date of Conceptualization**: Enter the date when the case was conceptualized and the assessment began.
* **Primary Presenting Problems**: Describe the core challenges reported by the client, including any physical or emotional symptoms.
* **Biological Factors**: Describe the patient's physical health, genetic, neurobiological, and medical history.
* **Psychological Factors**: Describe the patient's cognitive patterns, emotional regulation, and personality traits.
* **Social Factors**: Describe the patient's relationships, work/school environment, and cultural influences.
* **Family Mental Health History**: Describe any history of relevant conditions in biological relatives.
* **Precipitating Stressors**: Describe any recent events that triggered the current symptoms.
* **Strengths and Protective Factors**: Describe the patient's social support, coping skills, or internal resources.
* **Provisional Diagnosis**: Enter a provisional diagnosis based on DSM-5 or ICD-10 criteria.
* **Proposed Intervention Strategy**: Describe the core therapeutic approach and goals for the patient.
* **Risk Level Assessment**: Choose the level of risk to the patient or others:
	+ Low Risk
	+ Moderate Risk
	+ High Risk
	+ Emergency - Immediate Intervention
* **Clinical Review Required**: Check if this case needs supervision or peer review.
* **Scheduled Review Date**: If required, schedule the follow-up review date.

## Tips

- **Save your work** and **submit the form** once complete.
