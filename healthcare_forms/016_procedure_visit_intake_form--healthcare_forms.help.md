# procedure_visit_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic and medical information from patients for both patient intake and procedure tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the patient's basic information, such as their name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth and medical record number (if applicable).
3. Record the procedure date and time for the patient's visit.
4. Enter the start and end times of the visit.
5. Document any allergies or medical conditions the patient has.
6. Provide the patient's medical history and any relevant medical conditions.
7. Specify the type of procedure being performed (e.g. new patient visit, follow-up visit, etc.).
8. Identify the medical specialist performing the procedure (if applicable).
9. Choose the procedure status (e.g. active, complete, canceled, pending).
10. Optionally, provide a reason for the procedure status.
11. Add any additional notes about the procedure.
12. Enter the patient's gender.
13. Record the duration of the visit.
14. Select the type of visit (e.g. new patient visit, follow-up visit, etc.).
