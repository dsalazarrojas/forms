# Care Home Health Aide Log - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to record the daily activities, incidents, and concerns of a healthcare aide working in a care home setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select the correct shift date**: Ensure you are logging the correct shift date in the "Date" field.
2.  **Enter your name**: Type your name in the "Aide name" field as the staff member responsible for completing the log.
3.  **Enter resident's name**: Enter the name of the resident you served in the "Resident name" field.
4.  **Enter shift start and end times**: Enter the start and end times of your shift in the "Shift start time" and "Shift end time" fields, respectively.
5.  **Log activities completed**: Provide a brief summary of the activities you completed in the "Activities completed" field.
6.  **Log vital signs (if applicable)**: Enter any vital signs recorded during your shift in the "Vital signs recorded" field.
7.  **Log incidents or concerns**: Note any incidents or concerns that arose during your shift in the "Incidents or concerns" field.
8.  **Add supervisor review notes (if necessary)**: Use the "Supervisor review notes" field for any additional comments or guidance for your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Aide name** (`aide_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the healthcare aide completing the log.
*   **Resident name** (`resident_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the resident you served during your shift.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your shift in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Shift start time** (`shift_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of your shift in the format `hh:MM AM/PM`.
*   **Shift end time** (`shift_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your shift in the format `hh:MM AM/PM`.
*   **Activities completed** (`activities_completed`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of the activities you completed during your shift.
*   **Vital signs recorded** (`vital_signs_recorded`, `text`, optional): Enter any vital signs recorded during your shift, if applicable.
*   **Incidents or concerns** (`incidents_or_concerns`, `text`, optional): Note any incidents or concerns that arose during your shift.
*   **Supervisor review notes** (`supervisor_review`, `text`, optional): Use this field for any additional comments or guidance for your supervisor.
