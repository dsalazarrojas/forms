# Medication Error Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to report medication errors that have occurred during patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure that all required fields are filled out. The form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** 
Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.

### 2. **Date Of Error** 
Date Of Error (`date_of_error`, date, required): Enter the date of the error in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 3. **Error** 
Error (`medication_error`, text, required): Describe the error that occurred.

### 4. **Time Of Error** 
Time Of Error (`time_of_error`, time, required): Enter the time of the error in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

### 5. **Reason For Error** 
Reason For Error (`reason_for_error`, text, required): Explain the reason for the error that occurred.

### 6. **Description Of Error** 
Description Of Error (`description_of_error`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the error.

### 7. **Action Taken** 
Action Taken (`action_taken`, text, required): Describe the action taken to address the error.

### 8. **Reported By** 
Reported By (`reported_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who reported the error.

### 9. **Contact Information** 
Contact Information (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the healthcare professional who reported the error.

### 10. **Medical Condition** 
Medical Condition (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical condition at the time of the error.

### 11. **Medication Administered** 
Medication Administered (`medication_administered`, text, required): Enter the name of the medication that was administered.

### 12. **Dose Administered** 
Dose Administered (`dose_administered`, text, required): Enter the dose of the medication that was administered.

### 13. **Time Administered** 
Time Administered (`time_administered`, text, required): Enter the time of administration of the medication in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

### 14. **Route Of Administration** 
Route Of Administration (`route_of_administration`, text, required): Describe the route of administration of the medication.

### 15. **Error Type** 
Error Type (`error_type`, text, required): Describe the type of error that occurred.

### 16. **Patient Condition At Error** 
Patient Condition At Error (`patient_condition_at_error`, text, required): Describe the patient's condition at the time of the error.

### 17. **Treatment Taken** 
Treatment Taken (`treatment_taken`, text, required): Describe the treatment taken to address the error.

### 18. **Outcome** 
Outcome (`outcome`, text, required): Enter the outcome of the error.

### 19. **Follow-Up Recommendations** 
Follow-Up Recommendations (`follow_up_recommendations`, text, required): Provide any follow-up recommendations for the patient's care.

### 20. **Reporting Reason** 
Reporting Reason (`reporting_reason`, text, required): Explain the reason why the error was reported.

### 21. **Reporting Date** 
Reporting Date (`reporting_date`, date, required): Enter the date the error was reported in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 22. **Reporting Time** 
Reporting Time (`reporting_time`, time, required): Enter the time the error was reported in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

### 23. **Reported By Name** 
Reported By Name (`reported_by_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who reported the error.

### 24. **Department** 
Department (`department`, text, required): Enter the department where the error occurred.

### 25. **Contact Person** 
Contact Person (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare professional who reported the error.

## Tips

* Please use this form only for reporting medication errors during patient care.
* Fill out all required fields.
* Ensure that all dates and times are entered in the correct format.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
