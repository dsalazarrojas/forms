# healthcare_insurance_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect healthcare insurance subscription information from clients. It includes fields for client information, insurance enrollment, billing details, contact information, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Client Information" section to provide your personal details.
2. Select your insurance provider and insurance plan in the "Insurance Provider" and "Insurance Plan" sections.
3. Choose your billing frequency, billing amount, billing method, and payment method in the "Billing Frequency", "Billing Amount", "Billing Method", and "Payment Method" sections.
4. Enter your subscription date, start date, and end date in the "Subscription Date", "Subscription Start Date", and "Subscription End Date" sections.
5. Fill in your contact information, including email and phone numbers, in the "Contact Information" section.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This field is used to collect your personal details.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider you wish to subscribe to.
* **Insurance Plan**: Select the insurance plan you wish to subscribe to.
* **Subscription Date**: Enter the date you wish to subscribe to the insurance plan.
* **Subscription Start Date**: Enter the date your subscription starts.
* **Subscription End Date**: Enter the date your subscription ends.
* **Billing Frequency**: Select how frequently you wish to be billed (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Billing Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to be billed.
* **Billing Method**: Select how you wish to be billed (e.g., email, mail).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your subscription.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number.
