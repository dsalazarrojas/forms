# healthcare_coverage_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The healthcare coverage verification form is used to gather and verify patient information related to their healthcare coverage. This information is crucial for medical professionals to provide accurate diagnoses, treatments, and care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information for the patient.
2. Review and correct any inaccuracies or missing details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (text, required: false)**: Enter the patient's basic details, such as name, address, and contact information. This field helps us get a clear understanding of who the patient is.
* **Insurance Coverage (select_one, required: false)**: Select the type of insurance coverage the patient has, such as option 1, option 2, or option 3. This field ensures we understand the patient's coverage type.
* **Medical History (text, required: false)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or conditions. This field helps us assess the patient's overall health status.
* **Date of Birth (date, required: false)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy). This field is essential for age-related assessments and calculations.
* **Phone (text, required: false)**: Provide the patient's phone number for easy contact and communication. This field is optional but recommended for follow-up appointments and test results.

## Tips

* Please double-check the information provided for accuracy, as it will impact the patient's care and treatment.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask a medical professional for guidance.
* Complete the form with the patient's permission and involvement to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
