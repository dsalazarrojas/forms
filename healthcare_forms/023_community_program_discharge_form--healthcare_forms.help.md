# Community Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Program Discharge Form is used to document the discharge process of participants in the community program. This form helps ensure a smooth transition for the participant and for the program itself by capturing key information about the participant's involvement and the discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant Information**: Complete the participant's personal details, including their first name, last name, date of birth, participant ID (if applicable), and phone number.
2. **Program Information**: Enter the program's name, admission date, and discharge date.
3. **Discharge Reason**: Select the reason for discharge from the provided options, and provide additional details if necessary.
4. **Program Duration**: Choose the duration of the participant's program involvement.
5. **Goals and Outcomes**: Assess whether the participant's goals were met during their program involvement.
6. **Key Achievements**: List the participant's key achievements and accomplishments.
7. **Follow-Up Plan**: Plan any post-discharge support or follow-up actions required for the participant.
8. **Referrals Made**: Select any referrals made for the participant, and specify the details of these referrals.
9. **Participant Feedback**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the participant's experience.
10. **Staff Information**: Enter the case manager and discharge coordinator's names for internal use.
11. **Discharge Status**: Select the discharge status from the provided options.
12. **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the participant's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the participant's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the participant's ID number (if applicable).
* **Email Address**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Current Address**: Enter the participant's current address.
* **City**: Enter the participant's city.
* **State**: Enter the participant's state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the participant's ZIP code.
* **Program Name**: Enter the program's name.
* **Admission Date**: Enter the date the participant was admitted into the program.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date the participant was discharged from the program.
* **Program Duration**: Select the duration of the participant's program involvement from the provided options.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Select the reason for discharge from the provided options, and provide additional details if necessary.
* **Discharge Reason Details**: Provide additional details about the reason for discharge (if other reason was selected).
* **Progress Assessment**: Assess whether the participant's goals were met during their program involvement.
* **Key Achievements**: List the participant's key achievements and accomplishments.
* **Follow-Up Plan**: Plan any post-discharge support or follow-up actions required for the participant.
* **Referrals Made**: Select any referrals made for the participant, and specify the details of these referrals.
* **Referral Details**: Specify the details of the referrals.
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the participant's experience.
* **Participant Recommendations**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Case Manager Name**: Enter the case manager's name.
* **Discharge Coordinator**: Enter the discharge coordinator's name.
* **Discharge Status**: Select the discharge status from the provided options.
* **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Be clear and specific when providing details about the participant's involvement in the program.
* If unsure about any field or option, consult with the program staff or supervisor.
