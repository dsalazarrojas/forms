# Release Of Medical Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of a patient's medical information, including their medical history, diagnosis, and treatment. The form is designed to ensure that the patient's medical team has the necessary permissions to share their health information with other healthcare professionals or third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form on behalf of the patient.
3. Select the correct answers for the questions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Of Medical Information** (1): This field is the first step in the form, where you will indicate whether or not you want to release the patient's medical information. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Medical Information To Release** (2): This field is where you will specify the type of medical information you want to release. Please select "Yes" if you want to release this information, and "No" otherwise.
* **Authorization Form** (3): This field is used to indicate whether or not you have been authorized by the patient or their representative to release their medical information. Please select "Yes" if you have been authorized, and "No" otherwise.
* **Release By** (4): This field is used to indicate whether or not the patient wants their medical information released. Please select "Yes" if the patient wants their information released, and "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Records** (5): This field is used to indicate whether or not you want to release the patient's medical records. Please select "Yes" if you want to release this information, and "No" otherwise.
* **Other Health Information** (6): This field is used to indicate whether or not you want to release other health information that is not specified in the medical records. Please select "Yes" if you want to release this information, and "No" otherwise.
* **Date Of Release** (7): This field is used to specify the date when you want the medical information to be released. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date Of Signature** (8): This field is used to specify the date when you signed this form. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature** (9): This field is where you will sign your name to confirm that you have completed this form truthfully.
* **Reason For Signature** (10): This field is where you will specify the reason why you are signing this form. Please enter a brief explanation.
* **Release Of Medical Information Form** (11): This field is used to confirm whether or not you want to release the patient's medical information. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have obtained the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form on behalf of the patient.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification before completing the form.
* This form is intended to be completed in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
