# Therapist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Information Form is used to collect and record information about a therapist. This form is typically used in conjunction with other forms, such as patient records, to provide a comprehensive view of the therapist's contact and contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field marked "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field marked "Phone".
5. Enter your address in the field marked "Address".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to be recorded for the therapist.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to be recorded for the therapist.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the therapist. This must be a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the therapist. This can be their primary or secondary phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the therapist's address to be recorded for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check that you have entered all required information correctly and completely.
- Ensure that your supervisor or the person who initiated this form has reviewed and approved your submission.
- If you're unsure about any field or information, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the person who initiated this form.
