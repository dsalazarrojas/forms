# Reiki Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reiki Intake Form is designed for healthcare professionals to collect information from clients about their Reiki sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Name, Email, and Phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide your E-Signature in the E-Signature field to confirm your understanding and agreement to the session terms.
3. Specify the date and time of the Reiki session, including the duration of the session.
4. Select the Reiki certification level you are interested in and choose your preferred payment method.
5. Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`): This field requires your electronic signature to confirm your agreement and commitment to the session terms.
* **Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of your e-signature.
* **Provider Name** (`reiki_provider_name`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Session Duration** (`reiki_session_duration`): Enter the duration of the Reiki session in hours.
* **Session Date** (`reiki_session_date`): Enter the date of the Reiki session.
* **Session Time** (`reiki_session_time`): Enter the start time of the Reiki session.
* **Session Notes** (`reiki_session_notes`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`): Select all that apply to confirm your understanding and agreement to the session terms.
* **Reiki Certification** (`reiki_certification`): Select the level of Reiki certification you are interested in.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the total payment amount for the session.
* **Session Price** (`reiki_session_price`): Enter the price of the Reiki session.
* **Price Type** (`reiki_session_price_type`): Enter the type of price (e.g., per session).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., Cash, Credit Card).
* **Session Comments** (`reiki_session_comments`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Session Status** (`reiki_session_status`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Provider Comments** (`reiki_provider_comments`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Session Outcome** (`reiki_session_outcome`): Select the outcome of the session (e.g., Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Neutral).
* **Session Outcome Comments** (`reiki_session_outcome_comments`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Provider Signature** (`reiki_provider_signature`): This field is not applicable to clients.
* **Signature Date** (`reiki_session_signature_date`): Enter the date of the provider's signature.
* **Signature Time** (`reiki_session_signature_time`): Enter the time of the provider's signature.
* **Signature Location** (`reiki_session_signature_location`): Enter the location of the provider's signature.
