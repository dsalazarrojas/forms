# Disability Support Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release disability support records, allowing users to provide information about their disability type, authorization, and other relevant details. The form is used to inform the recipient about the contents of the records being released and to obtain their agreement to receive the records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you are filling out the correct form for releasing disability support records.
* Enter the required information, which includes:
	+ User information (page 1)
	+ Disability type (page 2)
	+ Authorization (page 3)
	+ Date and time of release (page 4)
	+ Type of records to be released (page 5)
	+ Recipient's information (page 6)
	+ Reason for release (page 7)
* Review and confirm your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (Page 1)
* **User Information**: Enter your full name and any other identifying information as required by your organization.
* **User Information** is used to identify the person filling out the form.

### Disability Type (Page 2)
* **Disability Type**: Select the type of disability you are experiencing from the provided options (Disability 1, Disability 2, Disability 3).
* This field is used to specify the type of disability you are experiencing.

### Authorization (Page 3)
* **Authorization**: Select all applicable options to indicate who is authorized to receive the records (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* This field is used to specify who is authorized to receive the records.

### Date of Release (Page 4)
* **Date of Release**: Enter the date when you want to release the records.
* This field is used to specify the date when you want to release the records.

### Time of Release (Page 5)
* **Time of Release**: Enter the time when you want to release the records.
* This field is used to specify the time when you want to release the records.

### Type of Records (Page 6)
* **Type of Records**: Select the type of records you want to release (Records 1, Records 2).
* This field is used to specify the type of records you want to release.

### Recipient (Page 7)
* **Recipient**: Enter the recipient's full name, email address, and phone number.
* This field is used to specify who will receive the records.

### Reason for Release (Page 8)
* **Reason for Release**: Enter the reason why you are releasing the records.
* This field is used to provide a brief explanation for releasing the records.

### Signature (Page 9)
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm you have reviewed and agreed to the release of the records.
* This field is used to sign and confirm the release of the records.

### Email (Page 10)
* **Email**: Enter the recipient's email address.
* This field is used to provide the recipient's email address.

### Phone (Page 11)
* **Phone**: Enter the recipient's phone number.
* This field is used to provide the recipient's phone number.

### Notes (Page 12)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about the release.
* This field is used to provide any additional information or comments about the release.

### Confirm Signature (Page 13)
* **Confirm Signature**: Confirm that you have signed the form (True or False).
* This field is used to confirm that you have signed the form.

### Confirm Recipient (Page 14)
* **Confirm Recipient**: Confirm that the recipient's information is correct (True or False).
* This field is used to confirm that the recipient's information is accurate.

### Confirm Email (Page 15)
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address is correct (True or False).
* This field is used to confirm that the email address is accurate.

### Confirm Phone (Page 16)
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm that the phone number is correct (True or False).
* This field is used to confirm that the phone number is accurate.

### Confirm Recipient Email (Page 17)
* **Confirm Recipient Email**: Confirm that the recipient's email address is correct (True or False).
* This field is used to confirm that the recipient's email address is accurate.
