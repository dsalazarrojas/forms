# patient_clinic_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_clinic_registration_form is designed to collect patient information for clinic registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:

1. Enter your name in the Patient Name field.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Number and Email Address fields.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
4. Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the Medical Condition field.
5. List any medications you are currently taking in the Medications field.
6. Provide emergency contact information in the Emergency Contact, Emergency Contact 2, and Emergency Contact 3 fields.
7. Enter your medical specialist's name and phone number in the Medical Specialist and Medical Specialist Phone fields.
8. Enter your medical facility's name and phone number in the Medical Facility and Medical Facility Phone fields.
9. Enter your patient address in the Patient Address field.
10. Enter your doctor's name and phone number in the Doctor Name and Doctor Phone fields.
11. Enter any medical licenses you may have in the Medical License fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address (text)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical Condition (select_one)**: Select any medical condition that applies to you from the list.
* **Medications (select_multiple)**: Select multiple medications you are currently taking from the list.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Contact Relationship (select_one)**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Child, Parent, Spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Number (text)**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 2 (text)**: Enter an additional emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact 3 (text)**: Enter an additional emergency contact person's name.
* **Medical Specialist (text)**: Enter the name of your medical specialist.
* **Medical Specialist Phone (text)**: Enter your medical specialist's phone number.
* **Medical Facility (text)**: Enter the name of your medical facility.
* **Medical Facility Phone (text)**: Enter your medical facility's phone number.
* **Patient Address (text)**: Enter your patient address.
* **Doctor Name (text)**: Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone (text)**: Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Medical License (text)**: Enter any medical license numbers you may have.
