# Sugar Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients' sugar intake habits. Please answer the questions honestly to help healthcare providers understand your sugar consumption patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your general information, including any additional comments you may have.
2. Answer the questions about your sugar intake habits, including how much sugar you consume, how frequently, and what type of sugar you consume.
3. If you have any contact information, please provide your email and phone number.
4. Finally, select a date to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Enter any additional comments or details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **How much sugar do you consume**: Please select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 10 grams
	+ 10 to 20 grams
	+ 20 to 30 grams
	+ More than 30 grams
* **How frequently do you consume sugar**: Please select one of the following options:
	+ Never
	+ Daily
	+ Occasionally
	+ Often
* **What type of sugar do you consume**: Please select all that apply:
	+ White sugar
	+ Brown sugar
	+ Honey
	+ Artificial sweeteners
	+ Fruit
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share about your sugar intake habits.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone number**: Please enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Date**: Select a date to complete this form.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose.
