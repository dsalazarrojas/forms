# health-care-outreach-material-delivery-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for delivering healthcare outreach materials. Please fill out the form with the requested information to facilitate the delivery of materials to the right recipient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the recipient (patient or caregiver). Please enter your email address or phone number so that we can contact you about the material delivery.
2. Choose the method you would like to use for the material delivery (Email or Postal Mail).
3. Enter the name of the material you would like to receive.
4. Enter a brief description of the material.
5. If the material is available online, provide the URL where it can be accessed.
6. Enter the name of the patient receiving the material.
7. Enter the patient's age, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **user-info** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information so that we can contact you about the material delivery.
- **material-delivery-method** (`material_delivery_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to receive the material (Email or Postal Mail).
- **email-address** (`email_address`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the material delivery.
- **phone-number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
- **date-of-delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the material (if applicable).
- **time-of-delivery** (`time_of_delivery`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the material (if applicable).
- **material-name** (`material_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the material you would like to receive.
- **material-description** (`material_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the material you are requesting.
- **material-file** (`material_file`, Text, Optional): If the material is attached, provide the file name or a link to access the material.
- **material-url** (`material_url`, Text, Optional): If the material is available online, provide the URL where it can be accessed.
- **patient-name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the patient receiving the material.
- **patient-age** (`patient_age`, Number, Optional): Enter the age of the patient receiving the material (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form or material delivery, contact our support team.
* We will contact you with more information about the material delivery after you submit this form.
