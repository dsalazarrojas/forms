# Health Insurance Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Health Insurance Plan Assessment Form is a tool used to assess and collect information about health insurance plans. This form is used to gather data on various aspects of health insurance plans, including plan name, provider details, coverage levels, premium costs, and policy holder information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Plan Name** in the corresponding field. This field is required only if you select an option to enter a plan name.
2. Enter the **Provider Name** in the corresponding field. This field is required only if you select an option to enter a provider name.
3. Select one of the following **Coverage Levels**:
	* Individual: For individual plans that cover only one person.
	* Family: For family plans that cover two or more people.
	* Group: For plans that cover groups of people, such as companies or organizations.
4. Enter the **Premium Cost** as a numerical value.
5. Select one or more of the following **Coverage Types**:
	* Health Insurance: For plans that cover medical expenses.
	* Prescription: For plans that cover prescription medications.
	* Both: For plans that cover both medical and prescription expenses.
6. Select the **Coverage Area**:
	* Local: For plans that cover a specific geographic area.
	* Nationwide: For plans that cover the entire country.
	* Regional: For plans that cover a specific region.
7. Enter the **Deductible** as a numerical value.
8. Enter the **Copay** as a numerical value.
9. Enter the **Coinsurance** as a numerical value.
10. Enter the **Max Out of Pocket** as a numerical value.
11. Select the **Effective Date** and **Expiration Date** as dates.
12. Select the **Policy Status**:
	* Active: For plans that are currently active.
	* Cancelled: For plans that have been cancelled.
	* Expired: For plans that have expired.
13. Select the **Policy Type**:
	* Individual: For individual plans.
	* Group: For group plans.
	* Family: For family plans.
14. Enter the **Employee Name** and **Employer Name** in the corresponding fields.
15. Select the **Coverage Start Date** and **Coverage End Date** as dates.
16. Enter the **Policy Holder Name**.
17. Select the **Premium Frequency**:
	* Monthly: For plans that have a monthly premium payment schedule.
	* Quarterly: For plans that have a quarterly premium payment schedule.
	* Annually: For plans that have an annual premium payment schedule.
18. Enter the **Coverage Type Description** and **Coverage Area Description** in the corresponding fields.
19. Enter the **Coverage Start Date Description** and **Coverage End Date Description** in the corresponding fields.
20. Enter the **Policy Holder Name**.
21. Select the **Premium Frequency**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plan Name** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter the name of the health insurance plan you are assessing.
* **Provider Name** (id: 2, text, optional): Enter the name of the provider of the health insurance plan.
* **Coverage Level** (id: 3, select_one, optional): Select the coverage level of the plan.
* **Premium Cost** (id: 4, number, optional): Enter the premium cost of the plan as a numerical value.
* **Coverage Type** (id: 5, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more types of coverage offered by the plan.
* **Coverage Area** (id: 6, select_one, optional): Select the geographic area covered by the plan.
* **Deductible** (id: 7, number, optional): Enter the deductible of the plan as a numerical value.
* **Copay** (id: 8, number, optional): Enter the copay of the plan as a numerical value.
* **Coinsurance** (id: 9, number, optional): Enter the coinsurance of the plan as a numerical value.
* **Max Out of Pocket** (id: 10, number, optional): Enter the max out of pocket of the plan as a numerical value.
* **Effective Date** (id: 11, date, optional): Select the effective date of the plan.
* **Expiration Date** (id: 12, date, optional): Select the expiration date of the plan.
* **Policy Status** (id: 13, select_one, optional): Select the status of the policy.
* **Policy Type** (id: 14, select_one, optional): Select the type of policy.
* **Employee Name** (id: 15, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee or policy holder.
* **Employer Name** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter the name of the employer.
* **Coverage Start Date** (id: 17, date, optional): Select the start date of the coverage.
* **Coverage End Date** (id: 18, date, optional): Select the end date of the coverage.
* **Policy Holder** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Premium Frequency** (id: 20, select_one, optional): Select the premium payment schedule frequency.
* **Coverage Type Description** (id: 21, text, optional): Enter a description of the coverage type.
* **Coverage Area Description** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter a description of the coverage area.
* **Coverage Start Date Description** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter a description of the start date of the coverage.
* **Coverage End Date Description** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter a description of the end date of the coverage.
* **Policy Holder Name** (id: 25, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Premium Frequency** (id: 26, select_one, optional): Select the premium payment schedule frequency.
