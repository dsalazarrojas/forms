# Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide their consent for telehealth services. This form is used to ensure that patients are aware of their rights and responsibilities, as well as the benefits and risks associated with telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and instructions.
2. Answer all the questions in the form honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Please enter your name and other identifying information as required.
* **General Consent** (`general_consent`, select_multiple, required): Please select one of the two options provided. If you are aware that by signing this form, you grant permission for telehealth services, select "I am aware that by signing this form, I grant permission for telehealth services". If you are not aware or do not grant permission, select "I am not aware and do not grant permission for this statement".
* **Telehealth Details** (`telehealth_details`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information you would like to share regarding your telehealth services.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g. family member, friend, etc.).
* **Provider** (`provider`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Contact** (`provider_contact`, text, optional): Please enter the contact information of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant medical history you would like to share.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, date, required): Please sign and date the electronic signature line to indicate your consent.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, date, required): Please sign and date the patient signature line to confirm your identity.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, date, required): Please sign and date the provider signature line to confirm your healthcare provider's identity.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date of signing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please enter the time of signing the form.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter the location where the telehealth services were provided.
* **Telehealth Consent** (`telehealth_consent`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments or concerns regarding telehealth services.
* **Patient Acknowledgement** (`patient_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required): Please select one of the two options provided. If you are aware that you will not receive the same level of care in person, select "I am aware that I will not receive the same level of care in person". If you are not aware or do not agree with this statement, select "I am not aware and do not agree with this statement".
* **Provider Acknowledgement** (`provider_acknowledgement`, text, required): Please enter your acknowledgement of the provided statement.

Please note that the required fields are marked with a star (*) in the form.
