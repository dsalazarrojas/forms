# Senior Citizen Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Citizen Program Discharge Form is used to document the discharge of a senior citizen from a program. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected and documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the senior citizen's name in the **name** field.
2. Enter the senior citizen's contact information, including their phone number in the **contact** field.
3. Enter the senior citizen's address in the **address** field.
4. Select the discharge date and time from the calendar or clock.
5. Enter the reason for discharge in the **reason_for_discharge** field.
6. Select the next contact option from the list.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior citizen's name.
* **contact** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior citizen's contact phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the senior citizen's address.
* **discharge_date** (`date_discharge`, date, required/optional): Select the discharge date from the calendar.
* **discharge_time** (`time_discharge`, time, required/optional): Select the discharge time from the clock.
* **reason_for_discharge** (`reason_discharge`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for discharge.
* **next_contact** (`next_contact`, select_one, required/optional): Select the next contact option from the list.
  - Discharge
  - Follow-up
  - Other
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the discharge.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields.
- Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
- Save the form once completed to ensure that the information is saved.
