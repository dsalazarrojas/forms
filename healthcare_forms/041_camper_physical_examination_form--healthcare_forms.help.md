# Camper Physical Examination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Physical Examination Form is designed to collect information for the physical examination of a camper, ensuring their safety and well-being during camp activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Answer all required fields truthfully.
3. Provide relevant details for optional fields.
4. Review each field carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the camper's birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the camper's age in years.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the camper's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Date of Physical Examination** (`examination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the physical examination.
* **Examining Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the examining physician's name.
* **Physician License Number** (`physician_license_number`, text, required): Enter the examining physician's license number.
* **Clinic or Practice Name** (`physician_clinic`, text, required): Enter the examining physician's clinic or practice name.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the examining physician's phone number.
* **Height** (`height`, text, required): Enter the camper's height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter the camper's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Vision Right Eye** (`vision_right_eye`, text, required): Enter the camper's vision in the right eye.
* **Vision Left Eye** (`vision_left_eye`, text, required): Enter the camper's vision in the left eye.
* **Hearing Status** (`hearing_status`, select_one, required): Select the camper's hearing status.
* **General Health Status** (`general_health`, select_one, required): Select the camper's general health status.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List the camper's medical conditions.
* **Activity Restrictions** (`activity_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select whether there are any activity restrictions.
* **Activity Restriction Details** (`activity_restriction_details`, text, required): Enter any activity restriction details.
* **Cardiac History** (`cardiac_history`, select_one, required): Select the camper's cardiac history.
* **Respiratory Conditions** (`respiratory_conditions`, text, optional): List the camper's respiratory conditions.
* **Neurological Conditions** (`neurological_conditions`, text, optional): List the camper's neurological conditions.
* **Musculoskeletal Conditions** (`musculoskeletal_conditions`, text, optional): List the camper's musculoskeletal conditions.
* **Skin Conditions** (`skin_conditions`, text, optional): List the camper's skin conditions.
* **Mental Health Conditions** (`mental_health_conditions`, text, optional): List the camper's mental health conditions.
* **Immunization Status** (`immunization_status`, select_one, required): Select the camper's immunization status.
* **Date of Last Tetanus Shot** (`tetanus_date`, text, optional): Enter the date of the last tetanus shot.
* **Date of Last TB Test** (`tb_test_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last TB test.
* **Is Camper Cleared for Camp Participation** (`cleared_for_camp`, select_one, required): Select whether the camper is cleared for camp participation.
* **Clearance Restrictions** (`clearance_restrictions`, text, optional): Describe any clearance restrictions.
* **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the examining physician.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, required): Sign with the examining physician's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date signed.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully before submitting.
* If unsure about any field, consult the accompanying guidelines or ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
