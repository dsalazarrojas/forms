# employee_physical_examination_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an employee's medical history, current medical conditions, and allergies. It is intended for medical professionals to review and use as part of an employee's physical examination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Scroll through each page of the form to review the sections.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and truthfully.
3. If you are unsure about any question, consult with a medical professional.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History** (<type:text>): Enter a brief description of your past medical history.
* **Medical Condition** (<type:text>): List any current or chronic medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (<type:text>): Describe any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical History 2** (<type:text>): Provide any additional medical history that was not covered in the first section.
 
## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when answering each question.
* If you have any concerns or questions, consult with a medical professional.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all sections.
 
Note: This form is designed to be used by medical professionals to review and use as part of an employee's physical examination. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical evaluation.
