# Vaccine Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture information about a patient's refusal to receive a vaccine. It will help healthcare providers gather necessary details about the patient's medical history, reasons for refusal, and contact information for follow-up. Completing this form will help ensure that the patient's concerns are documented and can inform future interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Each field is described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name and any relevant details you have about them. (text)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name, including their first and last name. (text, required)
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format. (date, optional)
* **Patient Medical History**: Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any previous health conditions or allergies. (text, optional)
* **Reason for Refusal**: Specify the reason why the patient is refusing the vaccine. Be as detailed as possible. (text, optional)
* **Refusal of Vaccine**: Provide a brief description of the vaccine being refused. (text, optional)
* **Date**: Enter the date when the refusal was recorded. (date, optional)
* **Time**: Enter the time when the refusal was recorded. (time, optional)
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including any relevant phone numbers or email addresses. (text, optional)
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address, including their street address or postal code. (text, optional)

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form with accurate and clear information.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask the patient or their representative for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary fields are completed.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by healthcare providers, so it's essential to have a basic understanding of the patient's medical history and vaccination process.
