# influenza_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 'influenza_questionnaire' form is a tool for collecting information about a patient's flu-like symptoms, recent travel, and vaccination status. This information can be used for further assessment or treatment by healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Provide accurate and detailed information about your symptoms, recent travel, and vaccination status.
3.  If you have any concerns or queries, it's always best to consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information**: This section is for providing basic contact information about yourself.
    *   **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
    *   **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Symptoms**: This section is for reporting your flu-like symptoms.
    *   **Fever Onset**: Report the number of days you have had a fever.
    *   **Fever Days**: Report the number of days you have had a fever.
    *   **Symptoms Severity**: Report the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Symptoms Frequency**: Report how often you experience your symptoms.
*   **Potential Exposure**: This section is for reporting potential exposure risks.
    *   **Travel**: Report any recent travel history.
    *   **Close Contact**: Report any close contact with someone who has flu-like symptoms.
    *   **Food Handler**: Report any exposure to people handling food.
*   **Recent Travel**: This section is for reporting recent travel details.
    *   **Recent Travel**: Report any recent travel history.
    *   **Recent Travel Duration**: Report the duration of your recent travel.
    *   **Recent Travel Country**: Report the country you recently visited.
    *   **Recent Travel City**: Report the city you recently visited.
    *   **Other Recent Travel Country**: Report any other country you recently visited.
*   **Vaccination Status**: This section is for reporting your vaccination status.
    *   **Vaccination Type**: Report the type of vaccination you received.
    *   **Vaccination Date**: Report the date you received your vaccination.
    *   **Vaccination Provider**: Report the name of your vaccination provider.
    *   **Vaccination Dose**: Report the number of doses of the vaccination you received.
*   **Recent Vaccination**: Report any recent vaccination history.
*   **Other Contact**: Report any other contact information that may be relevant.
*   **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for providing any additional comments or information about your health status.
