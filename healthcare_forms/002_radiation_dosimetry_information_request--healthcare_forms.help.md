# Radiation Dosimetry Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to radiation dosimetry, which is essential for healthcare professionals to assess and manage radiation exposure in patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Patient ID of the individual who received radiation exposure.
2. Provide the Patient's Name as recorded in their medical records.
3. Enter the date of the radiation exposure.
4. Enter the time of the radiation exposure.
5. Enter the radiation dose received by the patient.
6. Select the source of the radiation (Source 1, Source 2, or Source 3).
7. Choose the type(s) of radiation exposure (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
8. Enter the name of the facility where the radiation exposure occurred.
9. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the radiation exposure.
10. Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the radiation exposure.
11. Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the radiation exposure.
12. Enter the location of the radiation exposure.
13. Enter the direction of the radiation exposure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the patient who received radiation exposure.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient as recorded in their medical records.
* **Radiation Exposure Date** (`radiation_exposure_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the radiation exposure.
* **Radiation Exposure Time** (`radiation_exposure_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the radiation exposure.
* **Radiation Dose** (`radiation_dose`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of radiation received by the patient.
* **Radiation Source** (`radiation_source`, select_one, required: false): Select the source of the radiation (Source 1, Source 2, or Source 3).
* **Radiation Type** (`radiation_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type(s) of radiation exposure (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility where the radiation exposure occurred.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the radiation exposure.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the person responsible for the radiation exposure.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the radiation exposure.
* **Radiation Exposure Location** (`radiation_exposure_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the radiation exposure.
* **Radiation Exposure Direction** (`radiation_exposure_direction`, text, required: false): Enter the direction of the radiation exposure.
