# medical_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_consent_form is a standardized form used to obtain medical consent from patients. It collects patient information, treatment details, and other relevant medical notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" field.
2. Select the medical provider's information in the "Medical Provider" field.
3. Enter the treatment information in the "Treatment Info" field.
4. Select the appropriate medical treatment options in the "Medical Treatment" field.
5. Select the relevant medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6. Select the medical procedures in the "Medical Procedures" field.
7. Enter any additional medical treatment information in the "Medical Treatment Info" field.
8. Enter the medical provider's information in the "Medical Provider Info" field.
9. Enter the date and time of the medical procedure in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10. Enter any medical notes in the "Medical Note" field.
11. Enter the medical provider's email address in the "Medical Email" field.
12. Enter the medical provider's phone number in the "Medical Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, address, and other identifying information.
- **Medical Provider** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the medical provider.
- **Treatment Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the treatment.
- **Signature** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the signature type from the options provided.
- **Patient Contact** (`text`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and address.
- **Medical Treatment** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant medical treatments from the options provided.
- **Medical Conditions** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the medical conditions applicable to the patient.
- **Medical Procedures** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant medical procedures.
- **Medical Treatment Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional medical treatment information.
- **Medical Provider Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter the medical provider's information, including specialty and qualifications.
- **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the medical procedure.
- **Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the medical procedure.
- **Medical Note** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes.
- **Medical Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the medical provider's email address.
- **Medical Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the medical provider's phone number.
