# Teletherapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teletherapy Consent Form is used to collect patient information and consent for teletherapy sessions in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, email, and phone number.
2. If applicable, provide the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
3. Select the type of medical service being provided (e.g., HIPAA Compliant, Non-HIPAA Compliant, or Other).
4. Click on the "Electronic Signature" field to sign and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Type of Medical Service**: Select the type of medical service being provided (e.g., HIPAA Compliant, Non-HIPAA Compliant, or Other).
* **Electronic Signature**: This field is used to sign and save the form electronically. Clicking on this field will prompt you to sign and confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Keep the form up to date and secure.
* Use this form in a healthcare setting only and in compliance with HIPAA regulations.
