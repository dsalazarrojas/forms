# Health Management Training Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to healthcare management training applications. The goal is to gather relevant details about employee training activities, including applicant information, job titles, and training status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the training application in the "Health Management Training Application" field.
2. Fill in the applicant's information, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Answer the question about whether the applicant has job applications. If they do, provide the job title(s).
4. Provide the contact number and email of the applicant, if applicable.
5. Enter the date of birth of the applicant, including day, month, and year.
6. Select the training programs the applicant is interested in or has completed.
7. Enter the details of the training provider, including their name and location.
8. Enter the start and end dates of the training, if applicable.
9. Enter the hours of training completed, if applicable.
10. Select the status of the training, including Active or Inactive.
11. Enter any additional details about the training status.
12. Fill in the supervisor's contact information, if applicable.
13. Enter the manager's contact information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Management Training Application** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the training application.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required): Fill in the applicant's name and any other relevant details.
* **Job Applications?** (`job_applications`, select_one, required): Answer whether the applicant has job applications. If they do, provide the job title(s).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the applicant.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the applicant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the applicant.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the applicant.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the applicant, including day, month, and year.
* **Date of Birth - Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, text, required): Enter the day of the applicant's birth.
* **Date of Birth - Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, select_multiple, required): Select the month of the applicant's birth.
* **Date of Birth - Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, text, required): Enter the year of the applicant's birth.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required): Select the training programs the applicant is interested in or has completed.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required): Enter the details of the training provider, including their name.
* **Training Provider Location** (`training_provider_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training provider.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_date_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training, if applicable.
* **Training End Date** (`training_date_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training, if applicable.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, text, optional): Enter the hours of training completed.
* **Training Hours/Week** (`training_hours_per_week`, text, optional): Enter the number of training hours per week.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the training, including Active or Inactive.
* **Training Status - Other** (`training_status_other`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the training status.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the supervisor's email.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
