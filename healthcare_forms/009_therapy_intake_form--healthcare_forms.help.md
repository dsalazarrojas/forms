# Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapy Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients for therapy sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone numbers in the Email and Phone fields.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
4. Select a Date and Time for the therapy session.
5. Provide your Medical History, Medical Conditions, and Allergies in the Medical History, Medical Conditions, and Allergies fields.
6. Enter your Medical Insurance Provider and Medical Insurance ID in the Medical Insurance Provider and Medical Insurance ID fields.
7. Provide your Medical History 1-3 and Therapy Goals fields if necessary.
8. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Contact Phone fields.
9. Enter your relationship with the emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Relationship field.
10. Confirm your therapist's signature and your own signature after the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (text): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your birthdate (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of Therapy Session** (date): Enter the date of your upcoming therapy session.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of your therapy session.
* **Medical History** (text): Provide a brief summary of your medical history.
* **Medication** (text): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (text): Enter the email of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (text): Enter your medical insurance provider's name.
* **Medical Insurance ID** (text): Enter your medical insurance ID number.
* **Medical Conditions** (text): List any medical conditions you are aware of.
* **Medical History 1-3** (text): Provide more detailed information about your medical history.
* **Therapy Goals** (text): Enter your goals for therapy sessions.
* **Therapist Notes** (text): Allow your therapist to take notes about your session.
* **Therapist Signature** (text): The therapist's digital signature confirming session completion.
* **Client Signature** (text): Your digital signature confirming session completion.
* **Submission Date** (date): The date the form was submitted.
