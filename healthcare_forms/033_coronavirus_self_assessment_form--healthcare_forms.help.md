# coronavirus_self_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The coronavirus self-assessment form is designed to collect information from patients about their health status, travel history, and other relevant details. This form is used to assess the patient's risk level for coronavirus and provide guidance on next steps.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For select fields, choose the correct option that best describes your situation.
4. Enter your date of birth and time of symptoms in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Survey** (`1`, `text`): This field is a general survey about your overall health status. Please provide your current health status as described on the scale provided.
* **Symptoms** (`2`, `select_multiple`, \*): Check all that apply to your current symptoms:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Travel History** (`3`, `select_one`): Please select how often you travel:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Contact Exposure** (`4`, `select_multiple`, ): Check all that apply to your contact exposure:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Medical History** (`5`, `text`): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this field.
* **Medication Allergies** (`6`, `select_multiple`, ): If you have any medication allergies, check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Emergency Contact** (`7`, `text`): Please provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`8`, `select_one`, \*): Please agree to our privacy policy:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submit** (`9`, `text`): Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`): If you have a valid email address, please enter it.
* **Phone** (`11`, `text`): If you have a valid phone number, please enter it.
* **Date of Birth** (`12`, `date`): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Symptoms** (`13`, `time`): If you have been experiencing symptoms, please enter the time you started feeling unwell (HH:MM).
* **Date of Symptoms** (`14`, `date`): If you have been experiencing symptoms, please enter the date you started feeling unwell (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Conditions** (`15`, `text`): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this field.

## Tips

- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to enter your date of birth and time of symptoms in the correct format.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them clearly.
- Click submit to submit your completed form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
