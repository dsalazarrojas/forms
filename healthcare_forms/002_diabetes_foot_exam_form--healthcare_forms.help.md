# diabetes_foot_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The diabetes foot examination form is designed to collect detailed medical information related to a patient's foot condition, helping healthcare professionals make a more accurate diagnosis and effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "patient_id" field.
2. Enter the medical record number in the "medical_record_number" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
4. Record the date of the last examination in the "date_of_last_exam" field.
5. Record the date of the last foot examination in the "date_of_last_foot_exam" field.
6. Enter the blood pressure reading in the "blood_pressure" field.
7. Enter the pulse reading in the "pulse" field.
8. Select the level of pain the patient is experiencing from the "pain_level" options.
9. Select whether the patient has any deformity of the toes from the "deformity_of_toes" options.
10. Select whether the patient has any ulcers from the "ulcers" options.
11. Select whether the patient is experiencing any swelling from the "swelling" options.
12. Select whether the patient is experiencing any redness from the "redness" options.
13. Select whether the patient is experiencing any pain from the "pain" options.
14. Select whether the patient is experiencing any warmth from the "warmth" options.
15. Select whether the patient is experiencing any sensation in their foot from the "sensation" options.
16. Select whether the patient is experiencing any sensation in their toes from the "sensation_in_toes" options.
17. Select whether the patient is experiencing any numbness from the "numbness" options.
18. Select whether the patient is experiencing any tingling in their foot from the "tingling" options.
19. Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with weight-bearing from the "pain_with_weightbearing" options.
20. Select whether the patient is experiencing pain at rest from the "pain_at_rest" options.
21. Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with ankle movement from the "pain_with_ankle_movement" options.
22. Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with dorsal tilt from the "pain_with_dorsal_tilt" options.
23. Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with plantar flexion from the "pain_with_plantar_flexion" options.
24. Record the patient's signature in the "patient_signature" field.
25. Record the physician's signature in the "physician_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_id** (1, number, required): Enter the unique ID number assigned to the patient.
* **medical_record_number** (2, text, not required): Enter the medical record number for the patient.
* **medical_history** (3, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **date_of_last_exam** (4, date, not required): Record the date of the last examination.
* **date_of_last_foot_exam** (5, date, not required): Record the date of the last foot examination.
* **blood_pressure** (6, number, not required): Enter the patient's blood pressure reading.
* **pulse** (7, number, not required): Enter the patient's pulse reading.
* **pain_level** (8, select_multiple, not required): Select the level of pain the patient is experiencing.
* **deformity_of_toes** (9, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient has any deformity of the toes.
* **ulcers** (10, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient has any ulcers.
* **swelling** (11, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any swelling.
* **redness** (12, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any redness.
* **pain** (13, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any pain.
* **warmth** (14, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any warmth.
* **sensation** (15, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any sensation in their foot.
* **sensation_in_toes** (16, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any sensation in their toes.
* **numbness** (17, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any numbness.
* **tingling** (18, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing any tingling.
* **pain_with_weightbearing** (19, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with weight-bearing.
* **pain_at_rest** (20, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing pain at rest.
* **pain_with_ankle_movement** (21, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with ankle movement.
* **pain_with_dorsal_tilt** (22, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with dorsal tilt.
* **pain_with_plantar_flexion** (23, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the patient is experiencing pain with plantar flexion.
* **patient_signature** (24, text, not required): Record the patient's signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and consistently by authorized healthcare professionals.
* The collected data will help healthcare professionals make a more accurate diagnosis and effective treatment plan.
* If any of the fields are not relevant to the patient's condition, it is acceptable to leave them blank.
* Always record the patient's and physician's signatures in the "patient_signature" and "physician_signature" fields, respectively.
