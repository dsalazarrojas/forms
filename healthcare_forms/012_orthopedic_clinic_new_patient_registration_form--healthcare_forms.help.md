# Orthopedic Clinic New Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather basic contact and personal information from new patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your Contact Number in the Contact Number field.
5. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure it is in the format 'username@domain.com'.
6. If you have a Medical Record Number, enter it in the Medical Record Number field.
7. If you have seen a doctor at the clinic before, enter the Doctor's Name.
8. Enter your current Address in the Address field.
9. If you have any relevant Medical History, enter it in the Medical History field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will be used to verify your age for treatment eligibility.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your primary contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your contact number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required/optional): If you have a medical record number from a previous visit, enter it here.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): If you have seen a doctor at this clinic before, enter their name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address where you can be contacted or treated.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your treatment.
