# Psychologist Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychologist Information Form is designed to collect demographic, contact, and medical information from clients. This data will help psychologists tailor their services to each client's needs and provide accurate treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Start by filling out the demographic fields:**
    *   Provide your name and date of birth to ensure we can identify you correctly.
    *   Enter your address to help us get in touch with you.
    *   Provide your email address for communication purposes.
2.  **Enter your contact information:**
    *   Enter your phone number so we can reach you in case of emergencies.
    *   Provide your emergency contact's name and relationship with them.
    *   Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
3.  **Share your medical history:**
    *   Describe any medical conditions you have or are prone to.
    *   List any medications you're currently taking.
    *   Mention any allergies you have.
4.  **Share your treatment goals:**
    *   Clearly state your reasons for seeking therapy.
    *   Provide any specific goals you want to work on during your sessions.
5.  **Share any relevant notes or information:**
    *   Share any previous mental health history or medical conditions.
    *   Mention any medical procedures you've had.
    *   Provide any other relevant information.
6.  **Confirm your consent:**
    *   Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you've been informed of services provided.
    *   Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you've given consent for treatment.
