# Patient Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Review Form is used to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history, treatment details, and contact information. This form is essential for healthcare professionals to gather accurate data for patient care and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the patient's basic information.
2.  Fill in the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
3.  Provide the patient's contact information, such as phone number and email address.
4.  Enter the date of birth and medical notes.
5.  Fill in the medical details, including the medical specialty, medical provider, medical department, and medical visit date, time, and duration.
6.  Provide the medical purpose and medical insurance information.
7.  Enter any additional medical notes and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical History** (Text, Optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Medication** (Text, Optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Patient Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address for contact and billing purposes.
* **Medical Specialty** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical specialty for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Record** (Text, Optional): Summarize the patient's medical record.
* **Medical Record Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional medical notes about the patient.
* **Medical Specialist** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical specialist for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Department** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical department for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Provider** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical provider for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Visit Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the medical visit.
* **Medical Visit Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the medical visit.
* **Medical Visit Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the medical visit.
* **Medical Bill** (Text, Optional): Enter any medical bill or payment details.
* **Medical Insurance** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical insurance information.
* **Medical Purpose** (Text, Optional): Describe the medical purpose of the visit.
* **Medical Notes 2** (Note, Optional): Add any additional medical notes.
* **Medical Specialty 2** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical specialty for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Provider 2** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical provider for the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Department 2** (Text, Optional): Choose the medical department for the patient's treatment.
