# Medical Outreach Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Outreach Vaccination Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from patients before administering vaccinations. This form helps healthcare providers ensure the patient's safety and well-being by gathering details about their medical history, contact information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the patient's details, including their age and contact number.
2.  Provide information about any medical history, allergies, and medications.
3.  Indicate whether you consent to the vaccination and provide an emergency contact name and number.
4.  Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to provide their personal details, including their age.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any medical conditions or concerns you have, such as chronic illnesses or previous surgeries.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Confirm your consent to receive the vaccination. Please select 'Yes' to proceed.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach you with any updates or questions.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for further communication.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for easy identification and follow-up.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age for the healthcare provider's reference.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have or are concerned about.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Inform us of any allergies you have.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Share any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, number, optional): Enter the contact number for the emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Other).
*   **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Name of your healthcare provider for reference.

## Tips

*   Ensure you answer all questions truthfully to ensure your safety and the success of the vaccination process.
*   Please double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask your healthcare provider.

This guide is meant to be a helpful resource for patients and healthcare providers to understand the form's purpose, completion process, and each field's significance in the vaccination process.
