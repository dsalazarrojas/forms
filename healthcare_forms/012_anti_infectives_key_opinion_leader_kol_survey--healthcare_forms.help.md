# Anti Infectives Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Infectives Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Survey is designed to gather information from healthcare professionals on various aspects of anti-infective treatments. This form aims to understand your opinions, insights, and experiences in the field of anti-infective treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the survey administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): This field is for your full name.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, text, required): This field is for your current position or title.
* **Institution or Organization** (`institution`, text, required): This field is for the institution or organization where you work or practice.
* **Medical Specialty** (`specialty_area`, select_one, required): Please select your area of expertise from the list provided.
* **Years of Clinical Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required): Please select how long you have been practicing.
* **Practice Setting** (`practice_setting`, select_one, required): Please select where you primarily work from the list provided.
* **Geographic Region** (`geographic_region`, select_one, required): Please select your practice location from the list provided.
* **Areas of Anti-Infective Focus** (`anti_infective_focus`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the areas you are interested in.
* **Level of Concern About Antibiotic Resistance** (`antibiotic_resistance_concern`, select_one, required): Please select how concerned you are about antibiotic resistance.
* **Involvement in Stewardship Programs** (`stewardship_involvement`, select_one, required): Please select your level of involvement in stewardship programs.
* **Primary Factors Influencing Prescribing** (`prescribing_patterns`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the factors that guide your prescribing decisions.
* **Awareness of New Anti-Infective Therapies** (`new_therapy_awareness`, select_multiple, required): Please select how you stay informed about new anti-infective therapies.
* **Biggest Challenges in Anti-Infective Treatment** (`treatment_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the challenges you face in anti-infective treatment.
* **Satisfaction with Current Diagnostic Tools** (`diagnostic_tools`, select_one, required): Please select how satisfied you are with available diagnostic tools.
* **Interest in Clinical Research Participation** (`research_interest`, select_one, required): Please select your level of interest in clinical research participation.
* **Educational Needs** (`educational_needs`, select_multiple, required): Please select the topics you would like to learn more about.
* **Frequency of ID Consultations Requested** (`consultation_frequency`, select_one, required): Please select how often you request consultations from specialists.
* **Role in Formulary Decisions** (`formulary_decisions`, select_one, required): Please select your level of involvement in formulary decisions.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Please select your preferred method of communication.
* **Interest in Speaking Engagements** (`speaking_engagements`, select_one, required): Please select if you would be interested in speaking engagements.
* **Interest in Co-Authoring Publications** (`publication_interest`, select_one, required): Please select if you would be interested in co-authoring publications.
* **Additional Comments or Insights** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please share any additional thoughts or insights you have.
* **May we Contact you for Follow-up** (`follow_up_consent`, select_one, required): Please select if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up research questions.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you completed the survey.

Please note that some fields are required, and you must complete them before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the survey administrator.
