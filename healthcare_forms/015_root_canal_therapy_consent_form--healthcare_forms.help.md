# Root Canal Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Root Canal Therapy Consent Form is a legal document that must be completed by a patient before undergoing root canal therapy. It is a crucial step in ensuring that the patient understands and agrees to the terms and conditions of the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the treatment terms carefully.
2. Check the box or select "I agree to the treatment terms" if you are willing to undergo the treatment.
3. Fill out your patient name, contact information (phone number, email address, and emergency contact), and medical history.
4. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`root_canal_therapy_form`, text, required): This field is the main form where you will enter your consent to undergo root canal therapy.
* **Treatment Terms** (`root_canal_therapy_treatment_terms`, text, required): Here you will find a brief summary of the treatment terms and conditions. Please read and understand these terms before signing the form.
* **Do you consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box or select "I agree to the treatment terms" if you are willing to undergo the treatment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): This field is not required but please enter the name of your attending doctor if you wish to do so.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled visit for root canal therapy.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of your scheduled visit for root canal therapy.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your contact number where you can be reached during the visit.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached during the visit.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please provide a list of them in this field.
