# KC New Patient Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and medical information from new patients. The information collected will be used to create or update the patient's medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by filling out each field as much as possible. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the medical staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
* **First Name**: This is the patient's first name, which will be used to identify them in their medical record.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name**: This is the patient's last name, which will be used to identify them in their medical record.

### Address 1 Line (3)
* **Address 1 Line**: This is the patient's address, which will be used to identify their location and contact information.

### Address 2 Line (4)
* **Address 2 Line**: This is the continuation of the patient's address, which will be used to identify their location and contact information.

### Address 3 Line (5)
* **Address 3 Line**: This is the additional information about the patient's address, which will be used to identify their location and contact information.

### Date of Birth (6)
* **Date of Birth**: This is the patient's date of birth, which will be used to identify them in their medical record and for age-related medical history.

### Contact Phone (7)
* **Contact Phone**: This is the patient's contact phone number, which will be used by the medical staff to contact them.

### Medical Record Number (8)
* **Medical Record Number**: This is the patient's unique identifier in our medical record system, which will be used to access their medical history.

### Medical Record Provider (9)
* **Medical Record Provider**: This is the healthcare provider who has access to the patient's medical record, which will be used to identify who has access to their medical history.

### Date of Last Visit (10)
* **Date of Last Visit**: This is the date of the patient's last visit to our medical center or clinic, which will be used to track their medical history.

### Medical Condition (11)
* **Medical Condition**: This is a description of the patient's current medical condition, which will be used to better understand their needs and provide the best possible care.
