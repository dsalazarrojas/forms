# Telehealth Agreement And Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare providers to obtain agreement and consent from patients. It ensures that patients understand and agree to healthcare-related policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all the sections and questions.
2. Enter your name and any other required information in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Select the options for "Medical Group", "Medical Provision", and "Contact Preference" as applicable.
4. Record the date and time of the consent in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields.
5. Sign the form in the "Signature" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" fields.
9. Add any additional contact notes in the "Contact Note" field.
10. Select the type of consent you are giving in the "Consent Type" field.
11. Check the "Agreement Status" field to ensure you have read and agreed to all the terms.
12. Record the date and time of the agreement in the "Agreement Date" and "Agreement Time" fields.
13. Sign the agreement in the "Agreement Signature" field.
14. Enter your patient's ID in the "Patient ID" field.
15. Enter your provider ID in the "Provider ID" field.
16. Enter a unique ID for this consent in the "Consent ID" field.
17. Enter a unique ID for this form in the "Form ID" field.
18. Enter the URL of this form in the "Agreement URL" field.
19. Enter the IP address of the patient's device in the "Patient IP" field.
20. Enter the IP address of the provider's device in the "Provider IP" field.
21. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who submitted this form in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name, and any other required information.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided.
* **Medical Provision** (`medical_provision`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Record the date you gave consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Record the time you gave consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional contact notes.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of consent you gave.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select_one, optional): Check to ensure you have read and agreed to all terms.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, optional): Record the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`, note, optional): Sign the agreement to confirm your understanding.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): Enter your patient's ID.
* **Provider ID** (`provider_id`, text, optional): Enter your provider ID.
* **Consent ID** (`consent_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for this consent.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Agreement URL** (`agreement_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of this form.
* **Patient IP** (`patient_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the patient's device.
* **Provider IP** (`provider_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the provider's device.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who submitted this form.
