# Soap Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Soap Note Form, explaining its purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by entering the patient's information on the "Patient Info" page. This page is not required but is helpful if you want to add some basic patient details such as name, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
2. Move on to the "Medical History" page, where you should provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
3. The "Medication" page is where you should list any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken recently.
4. On the "Allergies" page, you can indicate if the patient has any allergies by selecting the relevant option ("Yes" or "No").
5. The "Social History" page is where you can take note of any social factors that might impact the patient's health, such as lifestyle choices or habits.
6. The "Family History" page is where you can document any relevant family medical history that may be pertinent to the patient's skin condition.
7. Finally, on the "Medical Notes" page, you can add any additional medical information or notes that are essential for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info (Patient Info)**: A page to enter some basic patient details such as name, date of birth, or other relevant information if needed.
  - **Medical History (Medical History)**: A brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
  - **Medication (Medication)**: A list of medications the patient is currently taking or has taken recently.
  - **Allergies (Allergies)**: Indicate if the patient has any allergies by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
  - **Social History (Social History)**: Note any social factors that might impact the patient's health, such as lifestyle choices or habits.
  - **Family History (Family History)**: Document any relevant family medical history that may be pertinent to the patient's skin condition.
  - **Medical Notes (Medical Notes)**: Add any additional medical information or notes that are essential for the patient's care.
