# Program Participant Intake And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from program participants for administrative or research purposes. It will help us understand your background, consent to participate, and provide a brief description of your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Select the category of the form (Healthcare or Non-Healthcare).
	* Provide a brief description of your experience.
	* Optionally, add any additional details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Participant Intake And Consent Form** (text): This is a free-text field where you can provide any information you feel is relevant to your participation in the program.
- **Description** (text): Please provide a brief description of your experience in the program or how you fit into this category. 
- **Category** (select one): Select "Healthcare" or "Non-Healthcare" to help us understand the type of program you are participating in.

## Tips
- Please be as concise as possible while providing a clear and accurate description.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
