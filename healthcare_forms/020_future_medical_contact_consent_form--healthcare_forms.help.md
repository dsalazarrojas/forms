# Future Medical Contact Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future Medical Contact Consent Form is used to collect information about a patient's future medical contact, which is essential for emergency medical situations or ongoing care coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2.  Select the contact relationship between the patient and the contact person from the **contact_relationship** dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the contact person's name in the **contact_name** field.
4.  Enter the contact person's phone number in the **contact_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *patient_name* (`patient_name`, text, **required**): This field is where you should enter the patient's name for whom you are filling out the form.
*   *contact_relationship* (`contact_relationship`, select_one, **required**): This field lets you select the type of relationship between the patient and the contact person. Options are: **Spouse**, **Family Member**, or **Emergency Contact**.
*   *contact_name* (`contact_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person whom the patient is in contact with for medical emergencies.
*   *contact_phone* (`contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the contact phone number of the person the patient is in contact with for medical emergencies.

## Tips
*   Make sure the **patient_name** field is filled in accurately and correctly.
*   In the **contact_relationship** field, select the most suitable relationship from the provided options.
*   Ensure the **contact_name** field is filled with the correct name of the person in contact with the patient.
*   Enter the correct **contact_phone** number of the contact person.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the patient or their authorized representative for guidance.
