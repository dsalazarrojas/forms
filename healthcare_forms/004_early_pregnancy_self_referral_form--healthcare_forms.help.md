# early_pregnancy_self_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's medical history, pregnancy information, and contact details from patients themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information section with your personal details.
2. Provide details about your medical history, including any conditions you are being treated for.
3. Fill in your pregnancy information, including any relevant details.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
5. Add any additional pregnancy history information, if necessary.
6. Provide information about your healthcare provider and medical office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe your medical history, including any conditions you are being treated for.
* **What medical condition(s) are you being treated for? (3)**: Select all the medical conditions you are being treated for.
* **Condition (4)**: Select the specific condition you are being treated for from the list.
* **Pregnancy Information (5)**: Enter details about your pregnancy, including the expected due date and any other relevant information.
* **Pregnancy History (6)**: Enter any pregnancy history, including previous births or miscarriages.
* **Additional Pregnancy History (7)**: Provide any additional information about your pregnancy history.
* **Obstetric History (8)**: Enter any relevant obstetric history.
* **Contact Information (9)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
* **Name (10)**: Enter your name.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Address (13)**: Enter your address.
* **Insurance (14)**: Select your insurance type, if applicable.
* **Healthcare Provider (15)**: Enter details about your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Information (16)**: Enter any additional information about your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Name (17)**: Enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **Healthcare Provider Address (18)**: Enter your healthcare provider's address.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone (19)**: Enter your healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Email (20)**: Enter your healthcare provider's email address.
* **Medical Office (21)**: Enter details about your medical office.
* **Medical Office Information (22)**: Enter any additional information about your medical office.
* **Medical Office Address (23)**: Enter your medical office's address.
* **Medical Office Phone (24)**: Enter your medical office's phone number.
* **Medical Office Email (25)**: Enter your medical office's email address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to your healthcare provider for clarification.
- This form is intended to support your healthcare journey, and your input is essential for your care.
