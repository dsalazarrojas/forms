# Addiction Recovery Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Addiction Recovery Discharge Form is designed to provide a comprehensive documentation of a patient's treatment and recovery progress, facilitating smooth transition from in-patient treatment to post-treatment care. It's used by treatment centers and healthcare professionals to track and document the patient's journey, ensuring that they receive the necessary care and support after discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and context of each field.
2. Gather all necessary information and documents before starting to fill out the form.
3. Complete each field carefully, using the provided hints as a guide.
4. Review and verify the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's legal name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's birth date, as recorded on their identification documents.
* **Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's unique medical record number or identification number.
* **Admission Date** (`admission_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the patient was admitted to the treatment center.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Enter the date the patient is discharging from the treatment center.
* **Primary Substance of Abuse** (`primary_substance`, select one, required): Select the primary substance the patient is recovering from (e.g., alcohol, opioids, etc.).
* **Length of Stay** (`length_of_stay`, number, required): Enter the total number of days the patient stayed in treatment.
* **Treatment Program Completed** (`treatment_program`, select one, required): Select the treatment program the patient completed (e.g., detoxification, in-patient rehabilitation, etc.).
* **Discharge Status** (`discharge_status`, select one, required): Indicate how the patient is discharging (e.g., completed treatment, transferred, left against medical advice, etc.).
* **Days of Continuous Sobriety** (`sobriety_days`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of days the patient maintained sobriety during their stay.
* **Treatment Goals Met** (`treatment_goals_met`, number, optional): Enter the percentage of treatment goals achieved during the patient's stay.
* **Relapse Prevention Plan Created** (`relapse_prevention_plan`, select one, required): Indicate if a relapse prevention plan was created for the patient.
* **Aftercare Plan in Place** (`aftercare_plan`, select one, required): Indicate if an aftercare plan is in place for the patient's continued recovery.
* **Recommended Aftercare Services** (`aftercare_services`, select multiple, required): Select all recommended aftercare services for the patient's continued recovery (e.g., outpatient counseling, support groups, etc.).
* **Available Support System** (`support_system`, text, optional): Describe the patient's available support system, including family, friends, or support groups.
* **Scheduled Follow-Up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, text, optional): List any scheduled follow-up appointments for the patient after discharge.
* **Medications Prescribed at Discharge** (`medication_on_discharge`, text, optional): List any medications prescribed to the patient upon discharge.
* **Discharge Instructions Provided** (`discharge_instructions`, select one, required): Indicate if discharge instructions were given to the patient.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): List the patient's emergency contacts and their relationship to the patient.
* **Discharge Summary** (`discharge_summary`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the patient's progress and condition at discharge.
* **Counselor Signature** (`counselor_signature`, text, required): The treating counselor's signature, confirming that they have reviewed the form and verified the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date of the counselor's signature, confirming their agreement with the information provided.
