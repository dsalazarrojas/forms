# healthcare-access-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare-access-assessment-form is designed to collect information about a patient's healthcare needs and access to services in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Barriers Identified** (`barriers_identified`, text, optional): Describe any barriers the patient may be facing.
* **Next Visit Scheduled** (`next_visit_scheduled`, date, optional): Schedule the next visit date (if applicable).
* **Follow up Visit Scheduled** (`follow_up_visit_scheduled`, date, optional): Schedule the follow-up visit date (if applicable).

## Tips
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is best to consult with the healthcare provider.
* This form is meant to be used in a healthcare setting to assess the patient's needs.
