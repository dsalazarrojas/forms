# Patient Treatment Plan Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by healthcare professionals to collect additional information about a patient's treatment plan, including type, start and end dates, medical history, and other relevant details. It should only be used when there are updates or modifications to a patient's treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary medical expertise and authorization to complete this form.
2. Select the relevant fields to update or modify the patient's treatment plan.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, using the options provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, as recorded in their medical records.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of treatment the patient is currently undergoing (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the treatment began.
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the treatment is expected to end.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required: false): A free-text field for recording any relevant medical history for the patient.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all relevant medical conditions the patient is experiencing (e.g. Option 4, Option 5, Option 6).
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the doctor currently treating the patient.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email address.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `note`, required: false): A free-text field for recording any additional notes on the patient's treatment plan.

Note: The optional fields allow for flexibility and accuracy when documenting a patient's treatment plan. However, they must be accurately filled out to ensure continuity of care.
