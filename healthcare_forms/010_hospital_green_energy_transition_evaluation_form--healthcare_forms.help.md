# Hospital Green Energy Transition Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Green Energy Transition Evaluation Form is designed to help hospitals assess their current energy usage and identify opportunities for green energy adoption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions on each page carefully, ensuring the required fields are filled.
2. Review each question to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance to your hospital's energy transition goals.
3. Select the correct options from the provided lists.
4. Enter numerical values where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Sustainability Goals
* **Sustainability Goals** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_1`, `text`, required): Describe your hospital's sustainability goals and objectives.

### Page 2:
* **Current Energy Usage** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_2`, `number`, required): Enter your hospital's current total energy usage in a numerical value.
* **energy_consumption_by_department** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the departments within your hospital that consume the most energy.
* **energy_consumption_by_department** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the department with the highest energy consumption.
* **energy_consumption_by_department** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_3`, `select_multiple`, required): This field should be ignored as it's a duplicate.

### Page 3:
* **Renewable Energy Options** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select renewable energy options your hospital is considering or has already implemented.

### Page 4:
* **Energy Savings** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_4`, `select_one`, required): Select the estimated energy savings your hospital can achieve through green energy adoption.

### Page 5:
* **energy_savings_in_dollars** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_5`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated energy savings in dollars.
* **energy_savings_in_dollars** (`hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form_page_6`, `number`, required): This field should be ignored as it's a duplicate.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field, as some are required.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If unsure about any field, consult your hospital's energy transition team or relevant documentation before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submission.
