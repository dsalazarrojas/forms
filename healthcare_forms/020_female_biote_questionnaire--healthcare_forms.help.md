# Female BioTe Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and health-related information from female patients. Please complete the form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page of the form, which is labeled "Patient Information".
2. Provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Proceed to the "Contact Information" page and provide any relevant contact details.
4. On the "Medical Information" page, answer the questions about your medical history, medications, and medical tests.
5. On the "Reproductive History" page, provide information about your menstrual cycle, pregnancy, and breastfeeding history.
6. On the "Health Status" page, report any medical conditions, allergies, and procedures you have undergone.
7. On the "Family History" page, provide information about your family medical history.
8. Finally, review all the information you have provided and ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
