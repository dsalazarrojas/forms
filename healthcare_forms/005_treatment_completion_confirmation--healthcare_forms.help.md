# Treatment Completion Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm the completion of a treatment plan or procedure by a healthcare provider. It ensures that the provider has documented their signature and completion date to maintain accurate patient records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **treatment_completion_confirmation** field with the confirmation of treatment completion.
2.  Provide your **provider_signature** in the designated field.
3.  Enter the **date_completed** in the designated field.
4.  Enter the **date_treatment_started**, if applicable.
5.  Add any additional comments in the **provider_comments** field.
6.  If required, provide your signature in the **patient_signature** field.
7.  If required, date and sign in the **signature_dated** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **treatment_completion_confirmation**:
    *   Label: treatment_completion_confirmation
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
    *   A text field to confirm the completion of a treatment.
*   **provider_signature**:
    *   Label: provider_signature
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
    *   A text field for the healthcare provider's signature.
*   **date_completed**:
    *   Label: date_completed
    *   Type: date
    *   Required: false
    *   A date field to record the completion date of the treatment.
*   **date_treatment_started**:
    *   Label: date_treatment_started
    *   Type: date
    *   Required: false
    *   A date field to record the start date of the treatment.
*   **provider_comments**:
    *   Label: provider_comments
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
    *   A text field for additional comments from the healthcare provider.
*   **patient_signature**:
    *   Label: patient_signature
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
    *   A text field for the patient's signature (if required).
*   **signature_dated**:
    *   Label: signature_dated
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
    *   A text field to record the date and signature of the patient (if required).

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure completeness and accuracy.
*   Save and print the form for future reference, if necessary.
*   Use the form in accordance with your healthcare facility's policies and procedures.
