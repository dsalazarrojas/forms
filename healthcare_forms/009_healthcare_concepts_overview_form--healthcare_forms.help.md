# Healthcare Concepts Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Healthcare Concepts Overview Form" is designed to collect and track information about healthcare concepts, including titles, descriptions, categories, providers, and ratings. This form helps to provide a comprehensive overview of healthcare concepts, enabling better understanding and management of healthcare-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Healthcare Concept Title" field with a brief title of the healthcare concept.
2. Provide a detailed description of the concept in the "Concept Description" field.
3. Choose a category for the concept from the options "Healthcare", "Non-Healthcare", and "Wellness".
4. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
5. Enter the name of the patient associated with the concept in the "Patient" field.
6. Share any feedback related to the healthcare concept in the "Feedback" field.
7. Enter the date and time the data was collected using the "Date Collected" and "Time Collected" fields.
8. Enter the name of the person who collected the data in the "Collected By" field.
9. Add any comments about the healthcare concept in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the email address of the healthcare provider in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider in the "Phone Number" field.
12. Provide the name of the healthcare service associated with the concept in the "Healthcare Service" field.
13. Enter the patient's concept in the "Patient Concept" field.
14. Enter the title of the person who collected the data in the "Collected By Title" field.
15. Enter the healthcare concept in the "Healthcare Concept" field.
16. Enter any patient feedback in the "Patient Feedback" field.
17. Rate the healthcare concept using the "Concept Rating" field.
18. Enter a label for the rating in the "Concept Rating Label" field.
19. Choose the rating scale from the options provided in the "Rating Scale" field.
20. Enter the rating value in the "Rating Value" field.
21. Select the rating range from the options provided in the "Rating Range" field.
22. Enter the patient's rating of the healthcare concept using the "Patient Rating" field.
23. Enter the healthcare rating of the concept using the "Healthcare Rating" field.
24. Enter the provider's concept in the "Provider Concept" field.
