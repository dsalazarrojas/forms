# mental_health_care_release_of_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health care release of information form is used to release confidential mental health care information from a patient's medical record. This form is typically used when a patient has given permission for someone else to access their medical records.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Information** section with the patient's details.
2. In the **Authorized Person** section, enter the contact information for the person who will be accessing the medical records.
3. Choose the **Authorization Type** (e.g., Doctor, Nurse, Family Member) from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Authorization Date** for when the patient granted authorization.
5. If necessary, provide **Release Information** to include any additional details about the release of information.
6. Choose whether the records will be released **Yes** or **No**.
7. Finally, fill in the **Facility Name**, **Facility Address**, **Facility City**, **Facility State**, and **Facility Zip** and **Facility Phone** and **Facility Fax** of the healthcare provider who will be handling the records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Page 1, patient_info):
	+ **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Authorized Person** (Page 1, authorized_person):
	+ **Authorized Person**: Enter the name, contact information, and relationship to the patient for the person who will be accessing the medical records.
* **Authorization Type** (Page 2, authorization_type):
	+ **Authorization Type**: Choose the type of person who will be accessing the medical records (e.g., Doctor, Nurse, Family Member).
* **Authorization Date** (Page 2, authorization_date):
	+ **Authorization Date**: Enter the date when the patient granted authorization.
* **Release Information** (Page 3, release_info):
	+ **Release Information**: Provide any additional details about the release of information, if necessary.
* **Release Type** (Page 3, release_type):
	+ **Release Type**: Choose **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether the medical records should be released.
* **Facility Name** (Page 4, facility_name):
	+ **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility Address** (Page 4, facility_address):
	+ **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility City** (Page 4, facility_city):
	+ **Facility City**: Enter the city of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility State** (Page 5, facility_state):
	+ **Facility State**: Enter the state of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility Zip** (Page 5, facility_zip):
	+ **Facility Zip**: Enter the zip code of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility Phone** (Page 5, facility_phone):
	+ **Facility Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the healthcare provider's facility.
* **Facility Fax** (Page 5, facility_fax):
	+ **Facility Fax**: Enter the fax number of the healthcare provider's facility.
