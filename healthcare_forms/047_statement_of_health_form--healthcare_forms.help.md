# statement_of_health_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's general health, medical history, current medications, and any medical conditions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. If you have multiple medical conditions, please select all relevant options under Medical Conditions.
3. If you have other medical conditions not listed under Medical Conditions, please use the Other Medical Conditions field to describe them.
4. Be as detailed as possible when describing your current medications and medical conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Health
* **General Health**: Describe your general health status, including any current health issues or concerns. (`text`, required: false)

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any significant medical events or procedures. (`text`, required: false)

### Medication
* **Medication**: List your current medications, including dosages and frequencies. (`text`, required: false)
