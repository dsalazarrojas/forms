# Radiology Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiology Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing any radiological procedures. It helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and potential risks and benefits associated with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Patient Info
Complete the "Patient Info" section by providing the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

*   **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name.

### Step 2: Select the Consent Type

*   **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, `select_one`, required): Select from the options: "Radiological Procedure", "Diagnostic Imaging", or "Other".

### Step 3: Describe the Risks and Benefits of the Procedure

*   **Risks and Benefits** (`risks_benefits`, `note`, required): Describe the potential risks and benefits associated with the procedure in detail.

### Step 4: Provide Medical and Emergency Contact Information

*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the patient's medical history.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the patient has.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the contact information for the emergency contact.

### Step 5: Insurance and Procedure Details

*   **Insurance** (`insurance`, `text`, required): Provide the patient's insurance information.
*   **Procedure Info** (`procedure_info`, `text`, required): Describe the specific procedure the patient is undergoing.
*   **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the procedure.

### Step 6: Informed Consent

*   **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, required): Obtain the patient's informed consent by having them sign and date this section.

## Tips

*   Make sure to carefully review the form with the patient and address any questions or concerns.
*   Ensure that the patient understands the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
*   Keep the form on file for future reference and medical history purposes.
