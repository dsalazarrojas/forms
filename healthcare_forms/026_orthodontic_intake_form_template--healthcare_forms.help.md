# Orthodontic Intake Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary patient information for an orthodontic consultation. Please complete this form to provide essential details about your medical history, dental history, and contact information. The information collected will help our orthodontic team better understand your needs and provide the best possible care for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field to ensure it applies to you.
3. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it out.
4. If you're unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification from our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID. This will help our team know who you are.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or illnesses that may affect your treatment. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Dental History (dental_history)**: Share any previous dental procedures, conditions, or problems you've experienced. This will help our team understand your dental background.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Provide any relevant contact information such as phone numbers or email addresses where we can reach you.
* **Allergies (allergies)**: Select all applicable allergies from the list. Please be aware that not selecting an option may not mean you don't have any allergies, but rather that you're unsure or have no allergies.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: If you have any medical conditions not listed, please describe them here.
* **Medications (medications)**: List any medications you're currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Providers (medical_providers)**: If you have a medical provider, such as a doctor or specialist, that has treated you for a condition related to your orthodontic needs, please list them here.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when filling out the form to ensure you receive the best possible care.
- If you're unsure about any information, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
- Please review and submit the form before your appointment to save time on the day of your visit.
