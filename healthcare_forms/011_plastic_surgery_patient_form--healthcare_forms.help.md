# Plastic Surgery Patient Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects essential information from plastic surgery patients for pre-screening and preparation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Medical History** field with a concise summary of your past medical experiences and any relevant medical conditions.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** in the provided field, including your name, address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
3. Describe your **Medical Condition** in the space provided.
4. Provide details about the **Surgery Details**, including the type of surgery you are undergoing.
5. Enter your **Patient Information**, including any relevant medical information or background.
6. Enter your **Doctor's Information**, including their name and contact details.
7. Answer the **Allergies** question by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any allergies.
8. In the **Medical History Additional** field, provide any additional comments or information about your medical history.
9. In the **Medical History Addendum 1**, **Medical History Addendum 2**, and **Medical History Addendum 3** fields, provide any further details or information related to your medical history (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: This field is for you to provide a brief summary of your past medical experiences and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including name, address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
* **Medical Condition**: Describe your current medical condition in this field.
* **Surgery Details**: Provide details about the surgery you are undergoing, including the type of surgery.
* **Patient Information**: Enter any additional medical information or background that might be relevant to your care.
* **Doctor's Information**: Enter your doctor's name and contact details.
* **Allergies**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any allergies.
* **Medical History Additional**: This field is for providing any additional comments or information about your medical history.
* **Medical History Addendum 1**, **Medical History Addendum 2**, and **Medical History Addendum 3**: These fields are optional and provide further space to add any additional medical history details.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly, as the information will be used to tailor your care.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare team.
- It's essential to provide as much information as possible to ensure the best possible outcome for your surgery and recovery.
