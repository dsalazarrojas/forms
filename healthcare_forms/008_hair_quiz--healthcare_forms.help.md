# Hair Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Quiz form is a part of a larger health survey aimed at collecting information about patients' hair health, preferences, and demographic details for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your basic contact information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Answer the questions about your hair type and condition in the "Hair Type" and "Hair Condition" section.
3. Identify any hair problems you are experiencing in the "Hair Problem" section.
4. Enter your hair length, face wash frequency, scalp condition, hair color, and hair problems in the "Hair Length," "Face Wash," "Scalp Condition," "Hair Color," and "Hair Problems" sections.
5. Specify any hair procedures you have undergone or plan to undergo in the "Hair Procedure" section.
6. List any hair products you use in the "Hair Product" section.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
8. Fill in your contact information in the "Email," "Phone," and "Address" sections.
9. Select your location and time zone in the "Location" and "Time" sections.
10. Enter your age in the "Age" section.
11. Choose your gender, marital status, and ethnicity in the "Gender," "Marital Status," and "Ethnicity" sections.
12. Select your occupation in the "Occupation" section.
13. Specify your income in the "Income" section.
14. Enter your height in the "Height" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Text)** (1, Text, Optional): Enter your basic contact and personal details, including your email address.
* **Hair Type (Select One)** (2, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of hair you have.
* **Hair Condition (Select One)** (3, Select One, Optional): Describe the condition of your hair.
* **Hair Problem (Select Multiple)** (4, Select Multiple, Optional): Identify any hair-related issues you are experiencing.
* **Hair Length (Number)** (5, Number, Optional): Enter the length of your hair.
* **Face Wash (Number)** (6, Number, Optional): Specify how often you wash your face.
* **Scalp Condition (Select One)** (7, Select One, Optional): Report the condition of your scalp.
* **Hair Color (Select One)** (8, Select One, Optional): Choose the color of your hair.
* **Hair Problems (Select Multiple)** (9, Select Multiple, Optional): List any hair problems you are facing.
* **Hair Procedure (Select Multiple)** (10, Select Multiple, Optional): Specify any hair procedures you have undergone or plan to undergo.
* **Hair Product (Select Multiple)** (11, Select Multiple, Optional): List any hair products you use.
* **Notes (Note)** (12, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Email (Email)** (13, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)** (14, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address (Select One)** (15, Select One, Optional): Specify your address.
* **Location (Select One)** (16, Select One, Optional): Choose your location.
* **Time (Time)** (17, Time, Optional): Enter your time zone.
* **Date (Date)** (18, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Age (Number)** (19, Number, Optional): Enter your age.
* **Gender (Select One)** (20, Select One, Optional): Choose your gender.
* **Marital Status (Select One)** (21, Select One, Optional): Choose your marital status.
* **Ethnicity (Select One)** (22, Select One, Optional): Choose your ethnicity.
* **Occupation (Select One)** (23, Select One, Optional): Choose your occupation.
* **Income (Number)** (24, Number, Optional): Enter your income.
* **Height (Number)** (25, Number, Optional): Enter your height.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all the required questions accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please refer to the study's instructions or contact a research coordinator for assistance.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Notes" section to provide further information.
* If you need to correct or update any information, please contact the research coordinator with your new information.
