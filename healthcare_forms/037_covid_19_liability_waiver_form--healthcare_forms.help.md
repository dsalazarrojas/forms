# COVID 19 Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants who are at risk of COVID-19 exposure. It gathers essential details about their health status, recent exposure history, and understanding of the risks involved. The form also serves as a waiver of liability for the organization providing the service, releasing them from any potential claims or lawsuits related to COVID-19 exposure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Answer questions about your health status, such as whether you have been recently tested positive for COVID-19, if you have had close contact with someone with the virus, and if you have any current symptoms.
3. Answer questions about your participation in the service or activity, including the type of service or activity and the date of service.
4. Acknowledge your understanding of the risks involved and your agreement to assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure.
5. Confirm that you release the organization from liability for any related claims.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **Organization or Facility Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization or facility providing the service.
* **Type of Service or Activity** (`service_type`, `text`, required): Describe the type of service or activity you will be participating in.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the service or activity.
* **COVID-19 symptoms currently present** (`current_symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you are currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Describe symptoms if applicable** (`symptom_details`, `text`, required): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, describe your symptoms in this field.
* **Positive COVID-19 test recently** (`recent_positive`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you have recently been tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Date of positive test if applicable** (`positive_test_date`, `date`, required): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, enter the date of your positive test.
* **Known COVID-19 exposure in past 14 days** (`exposure_recent`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you have had close contact with someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, required): Answer your vaccination status.
* **I assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure** (`assume_risk`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge your understanding and agreement to assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure.
* **I release the organization from liability** (`release_liability`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you release the organization from liability for any related claims.
* **I hold the organization harmless from claims** (`hold_harmless`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you hold the organization harmless from claims related to COVID-19 exposure.
* **I indemnify the organization from liability** (`indemnify`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you indemnify the organization from liability for any related claims.
* **I understand the risks and consequences** (`understanding_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the risks and consequences of your participation.
* **Date of Waiver** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are signing the waiver.
