# Nutrition Program Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Program Customer Registration Form is a form used to collect customer information and physical characteristics for the nutrition program. This form is intended for customers registering for the nutrition program and ensures the program's team has accurate and up-to-date contact information and physical characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address where you can be reached.
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Enter your address where we can deliver information and services.
5. Enter your zip code.
6. Provide your date of birth.
7. Enter your height in feet.
8. Enter your height in inches.
9. Enter your weight.
10. Select the unit of measurement for your height.
11. Select the unit of measurement for your weight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address where we can deliver information and services.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth.
* **Height (ft)**: Enter your height in feet.
* **Height (in)**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight.
* **Height Units**: Select the unit of measurement for your height. Options are feet, centimeters, or meters.
* **Weight Units**: Select the unit of measurement for your weight. Options are pounds or kilograms.
 

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields (marked with *) to complete the form correctly.
* If you are having trouble with any field, please ask for help from our support team.
