# Client Consent For Therapy Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Consent For Therapy Services form is used to obtain explicit consent from clients before initiating therapy sessions. It ensures that clients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the boundaries of the therapeutic relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full legal name.
2. Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
3. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
4. Enter the name of your assigned clinician.
5. Briefly describe the therapy services you wish to receive.
6. Outline the limits of confidentiality that may be broken during treatment.
7. Summarize the expected risks and benefits of the therapy services.
8. Check the box indicating you consent to receive treatment.
9. Check the box indicating you consent to share information with other providers, if applicable.
10. Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Provide a contact name and phone number that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Therapist or clinician name** (`therapist_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your assigned clinician.
* **Description of therapy services** (`treatment_description`, note, not required): Briefly describe the specific therapy services you wish to receive.
* **Limits of confidentiality** (`confidentiality_limits`, note, not required): Describe the situations where confidentiality may be broken during treatment.
* **Risks and benefits** (`risks_and_benefits`, note, not required): Summarize the expected benefits and possible risks associated with the therapy services.
* **Consent to receive treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Check the box indicating you consent to receive treatment.
* **Consent to share information with other providers** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, not required): Check the box indicating you consent to share information with other providers, if applicable.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your clinician for clarification.
* Complete this form before the start of therapy sessions.
