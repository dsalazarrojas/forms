# New Patient Intake Form_Bayview COVID - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Intake Form_Bayview COVID is designed to collect essential information about new patients at Bayview COVID. This form helps gather information such as patient demographics, contact details, and medical history to provide personalized care to the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the **New Patient Info** page.
2.  Provide your **Patient History** in the **Patient History** page.
3.  Fill in your **Contact Info** in the **Contact Info** page.
4.  Describe your **Medical History** in the **Medical History** page.
5.  Answer any additional questions in the **New Patient Form Additional Info** pages.
6.  Add any **Medical Notes** if applicable.
7.  Ensure to fill out **Email** and **Phone** fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Patient Info** (`text`, `false`): This is the initial section of the form where you will fill out your basic information such as name, email, and phone number.
*   **Patient History** (`text`, `false`): This section is for providing a brief description of your medical history.
*   **Contact Info** (`text`, `false`): Fill in your contact information such as email and phone number.
*   **Medical History** (`text`, `false`): Describe your medical history in this section.
*   **New Patient Form Additional Info** (`text`, `false`): This section has multiple fields where you can provide additional information as needed.
*   **Medical Notes** (`text`, `false`): Enter any relevant medical notes.
*   **Email** (`email`, `false`): Fill in your email address.
*   **Phone** (`text`, `false`): Fill in your phone number.
*   **New Patient Form Additional Info 1-17** (`text`, `false`): These fields are for providing additional information as needed.
