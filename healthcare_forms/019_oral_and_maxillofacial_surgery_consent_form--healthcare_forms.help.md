# oral_and_maxillofacial_surgery_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The oral and maxillofacial surgery consent form is used to gather information from patients before undergoing oral and maxillofacial surgery. This form ensures that the patient's medical history, allergies, and other relevant information are documented and taken into account before proceeding with the surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional: false): This is the patient's name. Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the patient's address, including their street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or medications they are taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional: false): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including any medications they are allergic to.
* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, text, required/optional: false): Describe any previous surgical history, including the type of surgery and the date it was performed.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* This form should be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that it continues to meet the needs of the patient and the healthcare team.
