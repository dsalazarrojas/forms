# Healthcare Policy Literacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields required to fill out the healthcare policy literacy survey, designed to gather information on policy understanding and implementation from patients and their contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the survey with accurate information.
2. Ensure you fill out all the fields marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false)
    * This is a basic field for collecting any information about the patient.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false)
    * This field is for any additional contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false)
    * Provide any relevant medical history you want to share.
* **Health Plan** (`healthplan`, `text`, required: false)
    * This field is for any information about the patient's health plan.
* **Policy Understanding** (`policy_understanding`, `text`, required: false)
    * Explain your understanding of the healthcare policy.
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, `text`, required: false)
    * Answer any questions related to healthcare policy.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false)
    * Sign your name as the patient.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, required: false)
    * Sign your name as the doctor.
* **Contact Doctor** (`contact_doctor`, `text`, required: false)
    * Contact information for the doctor.
* **Additional Medical Info** (`medical_info_additional`, `text`, required: false)
    * Any additional medical information not covered by other fields.
* **Additional Contact Info** (`contact_info_additional`, `text`, required: false)
    * Any additional contact information not covered by other fields.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `text`, required: false)
    * Information about the patient's health insurance.
* **Medical History (2)** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
    * This field is a duplicate of the previous medical history field.
* **Policy Info** (`policy_info`, `text`, required: false)
    * More information about the healthcare policy.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required: false)
    * Additional contact information.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false)
    * The patient's address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false)
    * The contact's address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false)
    * The medical condition of the patient.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required: false)
    * The date of birth of the patient.
* **Contact DOB** (`contact_dob`, `date`, required: false)
    * The date of birth of the contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required: false)
    * More information about the patient's medical insurance.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required: false)
    * Information about the doctor.
* **Additional Policy Info** (`policy_info_additional`, `text`, required: false)
    * Additional information about the healthcare policy.
* **Contact Info Additional 2** (`contact_info_additional_2`, `text`, required: false)
    * Additional contact information not covered by other fields.

## Tips
* Answer all the questions and fill out all the fields that are marked as required.
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible while filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to your doctor or the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Please sign both your name and your contact's name where required.
