# Medical Facility Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Facility Compliance Form is a tool used to assess the compliance status of a medical facility with various regulatory requirements. This form is designed to collect information on the facility's risk assessments, audit reports, and medical certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure that you understand the questions and fields presented.
2. For each field, provide accurate and truthful information about the facility's compliance status.
3. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it out completely.
4. Use the drop-down menu to select the correct options for each field.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Medical Facility Compliance Form** (`medical_facility_compliance_form_first_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, which asks for a brief description of the medical facility's compliance status.
- **Risk Assessments** (`medical_facility_compliance_form_second_page`, select_multiple, optional): On this page, select all the risk assessments that apply to the facility. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. Please ensure that you select all relevant risk assessments.
- **Audit Reports** (`medical_facility_compliance_form_third_page`, text, optional): This field is for providing a description of the audit reports conducted at the facility.
- **Medical Certifications** (`medical_facility_compliance_form_fourth_page`, select_one, optional): On this page, select the medical certifications that apply to the facility. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. Please ensure that you select all relevant medical certifications.
- **Compliance Procedures** (`medical_facility_compliance_form_fifth_page`, text, optional): This field is for describing the compliance procedures in place at the facility.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out each field truthfully and accurately.
* If a field is unclear or you need further guidance, please reach out to the facility's compliance officer for support.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* This form is for internal use only; please do not share it with external parties.
