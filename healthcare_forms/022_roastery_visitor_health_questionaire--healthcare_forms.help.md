# Roastery Visitor Health Questionaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your health status and help us assess potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
2. You can select one of the options for each question.
3. You can leave blank any question that you are not comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you have a cough** (has_cough, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are experiencing any respiratory symptoms, such as a cough.
* **Fever scale 1-10** (fever_scale, number, required: false): Please indicate on a scale of 1-10 how severe your fever is, with 1 being mild and 10 being severe.
* **Have you had a fever in the last 7 days** (has_fever, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have had a fever in the past 7 days.
* **Have you had chills** (has_chills, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are experiencing chills or feeling cold to the touch.
* **Do you have a runny nose** (has_runny_nose, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are experiencing any nasal congestion or discharge.
* **Do you have a runny eyes** (has_runny_eyes, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are experiencing any symptoms of conjunctivitis or allergies.
* **Do you have a sore throat** (has_sore_throat, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are experiencing any throat pain or discomfort.
* **Have you traveled in the last 2 weeks** (have_you_traveled, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have traveled anywhere in the past 2 weeks.
* **Have you traveled out of state** (have_you_traveled_out_of_state, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have traveled to another state in the past 2 weeks.
* **Have you traveled out of country** (have_you_traveled_out_of_country, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have traveled to another country in the past 2 weeks.
* **Have you traveled with anyone who is ill** (have_you_traveled_with, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have traveled with anyone who is experiencing symptoms or illness.
* **Do you live with anyone who is ill** (do_you_live_with, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you live with anyone who is experiencing symptoms or illness.
* **Have you been in contact with a sick person** (have_you_been_in_contact_with, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have come into contact with anyone who is sick.
* **Have you received any vaccination** (have_you_received_vaccination, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have received any vaccinations or immunizations.
* **Have you had any medical condition** (have_you_had_medical_condition, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have any pre-existing medical conditions.
* **Have you taken any medication** (have_you_taken_medication, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you are currently taking any medication.
* **Have you been in contact with another person** (have_you_been_in_contact_with_another, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have come into contact with another person who is sick.
* **Have you had contact with another person** (have_you_had_contact_with_another, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have had contact with another person who is sick.
* **Have you traveled by public transport** (have_you_traveled_by_public, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have traveled by public transportation.
* **Have you worked with** (have_you_worked_with, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have come into contact with someone who is sick while working.
* **Have you attended an event** (have_you_attended_event, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have attended any events or gatherings.
* **Have you eaten** (have_you_eaten, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have eaten anything in the past 24 hours.
* **Have you had any contact** (have_you_had_any_contact, select_one, required: false): This question is asking if you have had any contact with anyone or anything that may pose a risk.
