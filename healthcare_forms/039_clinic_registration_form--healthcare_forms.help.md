# Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient registration information for the clinic. The information collected will be used for both administrative and clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each section of the form, starting with the "Patient Information" section.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each required field, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information
* **Full Name** (id: 2, text, \*): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 4, date, \*): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (id: 5, email, \*): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6, text, \*): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Gender Identity** (id: 7, select_one): Select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Pronouns** (id: 8, text): Enter your preferred pronouns for communication.
* **Primary Language** (id: 10, text, \*): Select your primary language.
* **Need Interpreter Services?** (id: 11, select_one): If you need interpreter services, select "Yes".
* **Accessibility Needs or Accommodations** (id: 12, text): Enter any accessibility needs or accommodations you require.

### Language and Accessibility
* **Language Section** (id: 9, note): This section is for additional language information.
* **Language and Accessibility needs** (id: 13, note): This section is for additional language information.

### Address Information
* **Street Address** (id: 14, text, \*): Enter your street address.
* **City** (id: 15, text, \*): Enter your city.
* **State** (id: 16, text, \*): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 17, text, \*): Enter your zip code.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 19, text, \*): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 20, text, \*): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (id: 21, text, \*): Select your relationship with the emergency contact.

### Insurance Information
* **Do you have health insurance?** (id: 23, select_one, \*): If you have health insurance, select "Yes".
* **Insurance Company Name** (id: 24, text): If you have health insurance, enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Member ID** (id: 25, text): If you have health insurance, enter your member ID number.
