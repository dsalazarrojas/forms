# hospital_administrator_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for hospital administrators to provide recommendations for candidates applying for hospital positions. It helps to document their relationship with the candidate, job duties, and any other relevant details that can support the candidate's application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Candidate Name, Job Title, Relationship with the candidate, and Job duties and accomplishments.
2. Provide any additional comments or details about the candidate in the "Any other comments" field.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the candidate you are recommending.
* **Job Title** (2, text, required): Enter the job title of the candidate's position.
* **Supervisor Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name as the supervisor or administrator recommending the candidate.
* **Supervisor Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your work phone number so the candidate can contact you.
* **Supervisor Email** (5, email, required): Enter your work email address so the candidate can contact you.
* **Alternate Supervisor Contact** (6, text, optional): Enter an additional contact method for you, such as a secondary email or phone number.
* **What is the applicant's relationship to you?** (7, select_one, required): Choose your relationship to the candidate, such as Primary, Secondary, or Other.
* **What is your relationship to the candidate?** (8, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your relationship to the candidate, such as "Supervisor," "Mentor," or "Colleague."
* **Date you started working with candidate** (9, date, optional): Enter the date you started working with the candidate.
* **Date you ended working with candidate** (10, date, optional): Enter the date you ended working with the candidate.
* **Length of employment** (11, number, optional): Enter the number of months or years you worked with the candidate.
* **Job duties and accomplishments** (12, select_multiple, required): Choose from the options what job duties and accomplishments the candidate demonstrated during their tenure.
* **Any other comments** (13, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details that support the candidate's application.
