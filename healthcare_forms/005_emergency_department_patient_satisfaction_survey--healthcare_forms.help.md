# emergency_department_patient_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency department patient satisfaction survey is a tool used to gather feedback from patients about their experience in the emergency department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Enter the required information accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Length** (`visit_length`, number, required: false): This question asks you to estimate how long you had to wait in the emergency department. Please respond with a numerical value.
* **Providers And Staff** (`prophets`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the boxes that apply to the doctors and nurses who provided care for you during your visit. If none of the options apply, please type in "None".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care you received in the emergency department.
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, time, required: false): This question asks you to estimate how long you had to wait in the emergency department, including waiting time in the waiting room.
* **Pain Management** (`pain_management`, select_one, required: false): Describe how much pain you experienced during your visit.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of communication from the hospital staff during your visit. Check all the boxes that apply.
* **Hospital Staff** (`hospital_staff`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of care provided by the hospital staff.
* **Discharge Process** (`discharge_process`, select_one, required: false): Describe how smooth the discharge process was for you.
* **Billing Clarity** (`billing_clearness`, select_one, required: false): Rate how clear the billing process was for you.
* **Recommendations For Future Care** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for how the emergency department could improve patient care.
