# Healthcare Innovation Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Healthcare Innovation Records Release Form" is a document used to gather information from patients about their medical records and verify certain aspects of their records. This form helps healthcare providers to update and verify patient information, facilitate communication between providers, and ensure accuracy and consistency in patient records.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Patient Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
2.  **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  **Address (3)**: Enter your current address or the address where you would like to receive updates about your medical records.
4.  **Medical Record Number (4)**: Enter your unique medical record number assigned to you by your healthcare provider.
5.  **Consent Given (5)**: Select the option that best describes your consent for the sharing of your medical information (e.g., "Yes, I give consent" or "No, I do not give consent").
6.  **Signature Date (6)**: Enter the date and time you signed this form.
7.  **Signature (7)**: Sign your name above the date.
8.  **Date of Signature (8)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
9.  **Provider Name (9)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be receiving or using your medical information.
10. **Date of Signature Confirmation (10)**: Enter the date and time the provider verified your signature.
11. **Patient Email (11)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates about your medical records.
12. **Provider Email (12)**: Enter the email address of the healthcare provider who will be receiving or using your medical information.
13. **Medical Record Number Confirmation (13)**: Enter your unique medical record number again for verification purposes.
14.  **Patient Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates about your medical records.
15.  **Provider Phone (15)**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider who will be receiving or using your medical information.
16.  **Date of Signature Verification (16)**: Enter the date and time the healthcare provider verified your signature.
17.  **Signature Verification (17)**: Select the option that best describes how you verified your signature (e.g., "In person" or "By phone").
18.  **Date of Last Submission (18)**: Enter the date and time your information was last submitted.
19.  **Medical Record Number Verification (19)**: Select the option that best describes your medical record number verification (e.g., "Verified" or "Not Verified").
20.  **Date of Last Verification (20)**: Enter the date and time your medical record number was last verified.
21.  **Provider Signature Verification (21)**: Select the option that best describes how the healthcare provider verified their signature (e.g., "In person" or "By phone").
22.  **Date of Last Verification Confirmation (22)**: Enter the date and time the healthcare provider verified their signature.
23.  **Date of Last Update (23)**: Enter the date and time your medical records were last updated.
24.  **Date of Last Revision (24)**: Enter the date and time your medical records were last revised.
25.  **Date of Last Audit (25)**: Enter the date and time your medical records were last audited.
