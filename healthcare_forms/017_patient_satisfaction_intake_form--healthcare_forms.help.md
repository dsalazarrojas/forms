# Patient Satisfaction Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Satisfaction Intake Form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience at our healthcare organization. This form helps us understand areas where we excel and areas for improvement, allowing us to enhance the overall patient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient ID (ID)
2. Enter your name (Patient Name)
3. Answer questions 1-4 using the provided options or free-form text
4. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `number`, *required*): Enter your unique patient ID assigned by our organization.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.
* **How was the staff interaction?** (`question1`, `number`, *required*): Rate the interaction with our staff as 1 (poor), 2 (fair), 3 (good), 4 (very good), or 5 (excellent).
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`question2`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2

	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **How would you rate the staff?** (`question3`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **How would you rate the environment?** (`question4`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, `note`, *optional*): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *optional*): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *optional*): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you need assistance or clarification on any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask.
* Some fields have specific formatting requirements, so please enter your information carefully.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not repeat your comments.
