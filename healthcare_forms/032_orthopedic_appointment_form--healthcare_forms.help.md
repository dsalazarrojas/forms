# orthopedic_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "orthopedic_appointment_form" is designed to collect essential information from patients for an orthopedic consultation. It is used to schedule appointments, record medical history, and provide necessary details for orthopedic treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information in the "Patient Information" section (page 1).
2. Select a suitable time slot for your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field on page 2.
3. Choose a date for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" field on page 3.
4. Provide your medical history in the "Medical History" field on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, contact details, and any relevant medical ID numbers. This information will be used for identification and to communicate with you effectively.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Select a suitable time for your appointment, taking into account your schedule and availability. Please note that this field is used to determine the best time for the orthopedic consultation.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your appointment, ensuring it is within a reasonable timeframe for the orthopedic treatment plan. This field is used to schedule the appointment and plan accordingly.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any previous injuries, conditions, or treatments. This information will help the healthcare professionals assess your situation and provide effective treatment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with appointment scheduling or treatment planning.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact form@gic.mx for support and clarification.
- Remember to review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or omissions.
