# Eye Clinic Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for new patients to register their information for an eye clinic visit. It includes fields for basic contact information, medical conditions, and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with the required information.
- Make sure to provide your contact information accurately.
- Select all applicable medical conditions to help our medical staff better understand your needs.
- Choose a time slot that is most convenient for you.
- Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with our staff.
- Once you've completed the form, click on the submit button to send the information to our clinic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
- **Surname** (`surname`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of your appointment.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of your appointment.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant medical conditions you have.
- **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any additional medical conditions you have.
- **Medical Conditions 3** (`medical_conditions3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select further medical conditions you have.
- **Medical Conditions 4** (`medical_conditions4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any more medical conditions you have.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with our staff.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our staff for assistance.
- Our staff will review your form and contact you with further instructions or questions if needed.
