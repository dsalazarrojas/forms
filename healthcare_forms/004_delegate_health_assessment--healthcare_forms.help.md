# delegate_health_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The delegate_health_assessment form is used to gather essential information about a patient's health status, aiding healthcare professionals in their clinical decision-making and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's "Patient Name" field with the patient's full name.
* Enter the patient's "Date of Birth" in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Record the patient's "Medical Record Number" in the format provided.
* If applicable, list any "Allergies" the patient has, including the type and any related reactions or conditions.
* If applicable, list any "Medications" the patient is currently taking, including the dosage and any relevant instructions.
* If applicable, describe any "Surgical Procedure" the patient is undergoing or has undergone.
* Select the appropriate level of health "Delegate Health Assessment Scale" based on your clinical judgment.
* Enter the "Delegate Health Assessment Score" as a numerical value.
* Provide any additional "Delegate Health Assessment Note" comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (Required): Enter the patient's full name as recorded in their medical record.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Record Number** (Required): Record the patient's unique medical record number.
* **Allergies** (Optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including the type and any related reactions or conditions.
* **Medications** (Optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including the dosage and any relevant instructions.
* **Surgical Procedure** (Optional): Describe any surgical procedure the patient is undergoing or has undergone.
* **Delegate Health Assessment Scale** (Optional): Select the appropriate level of health assessment based on your clinical judgment.
* **Delegate Health Assessment Score** (Required): Enter the delegate health assessment score as a numerical value.
* **Delegate Health Assessment Note** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the patient's condition.

## Tips
* Ensure the form is completed accurately and consistently to maintain patient confidentiality and protect their rights.
* Refer to clinical standards and best practices, such as the Glasgow Coma Scale, for guidance on assessing patient health.
* Use standardized formats for dates and medical record numbers to ensure clarity and consistency.
* Be mindful of patient safety and well-being when completing this form, and take necessary precautions to protect their data.
