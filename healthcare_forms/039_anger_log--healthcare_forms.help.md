# Anger Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anger Log is a form designed to help individuals track and record their daily experiences with anger triggers, intensity ratings, coping strategies, and outcomes. By completing this form, users can gain insights into their anger patterns and develop strategies to manage their emotions more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant date for the entry you are logging.
2. Describe the trigger that led to the anger episode in the "Trigger description" field.
3. Rate the intensity of your anger on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Intensity rating" field.
4. Describe the coping strategy you used to manage your anger in the "Coping strategy used" field.
5. Share any additional information about what happened after the incident in the "Outcome" field.
6. If you'd like to record any other remarks, use the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Daily Entry --** (`section_daily_entry`, `note`, `required: false`): This section is the title of the daily entry.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `required: true`): Record the date of the entry.
* **Trigger description** (`trigger_description`, `text`, `required: true`): Describe the trigger that led to the anger episode.
* **Intensity rating** (`intensity_rating`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Rate the intensity of your anger on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Coping strategy used** (`coping_strategy_used`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe the coping strategy you used to manage your anger.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, `text`, `required: false`): Share any additional information about what happened after the incident.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, `text`, `required: false`): Record any other remarks or observations about your experience.

## Tips
* Be sure to log each entry at the end of each day to maintain a consistent and accurate record.
* Review previous entries to identify patterns and trends in your anger episodes.
* Use this data to develop effective coping strategies and seek support when needed.
* Consider discussing your entries with a mental health professional for personalized guidance.
