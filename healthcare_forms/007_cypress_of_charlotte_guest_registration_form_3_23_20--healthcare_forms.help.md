# Cypress Of Charlotte Guest Registration Form 3.23.20 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information, medical history, and other relevant details from guests visiting the Cypress Of Charlotte healthcare facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read through each section carefully to understand what information is required.
- Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
- If you have any medical history, select the relevant medical conditions that apply to you.
- Select the medications you are currently taking, if any.
- Choose your medical specialist doctor or nurse from the list.
- Enter any relevant medical records or hospitalization information.
- Select your emergency contact and their contact information.
- Enter your medical visit date and time.
- Review your information to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information**:
  - **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Medical History**:
  - **Medical Condition**: Select the medical condition that applies to you, if any.
  - **Meds**: Select the medications you are currently taking, if any.
- **Allergy Information**:
  - **Allergy**: Select the allergies you have, if any.
- **Medical Procedure**:
  - **Procedure**: Select the medical procedure you are undergoing, if any.
- **Medical Temperature**:
  - **Temperature**: Enter your current body temperature, if required.
- **Emergency Contact**:
  - **Emergency Contact**: Select the emergency contact that applies to you.
  - **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  - **Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Attending Doctor**:
  - **Doctor**: Select the attending doctor for your medical visit.
- **Medical Surgeon**:
  - **Surgeon**: Select the surgeon for your medical visit.
- **Medical Specialist Doctor**:
  - **Specialist Doctor**: Select the specialist doctor for your medical visit.
- **Medical Nurse**:
  - **Nurse**: Select the nurse for your medical visit.
- **Medical Doctor Specialty**:
  - **Specialty Doctor**: Select the specialty doctor for your medical visit.
- **Medical Hospitalization**:
  - **Hospitalization**: Select the hospitalization information relevant to you.
- **Medical Physician**:
  - **Physician**: Select the physician for your medical visit.
- **Medical Specialist**:
  - **Specialist**: Select the specialist for your medical visit.
- **Medical Surgeon Specialty**:
  - **Surgeon Specialty**: Select the surgeon specialty for your medical visit.
- **Medical Doctor Visit Notes**:
  - **Visit Notes**: Enter any relevant visit notes, if required.
- **Medical Hospital Bill**:
  - **Hospital Bill**: Enter the hospital bill amount, if required.
- **Medical Surgery Dates**:
  - **Surgery Dates**: Enter the dates of your surgery, if required.
- **Medical Visit Date**:
  - **Visit Date**: Select the date of your medical visit.
- **Medical Time Of Visit**:
  - **Visit Time**: Select the time of your medical visit.
