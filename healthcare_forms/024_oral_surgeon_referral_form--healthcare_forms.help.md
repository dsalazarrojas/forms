# Oral Surgeon Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to refer patients to an oral surgeon for treatment. It helps collect essential information about the patient's medical history, current condition, and other relevant details for the oral surgeon to provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information such as name and other relevant details on the "Patient Info" page.
2. Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, and current symptoms on the "Medical History" page.
3. List the patient's current medications on the "Medications" page.
4. Provide details about the surgical procedure the patient requires on the "Surgical Procedure" page.
5. List any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient has on the "Medical Conditions" and "Allergies" pages.
6. Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant contact information. This information will help the oral surgeon to communicate with the patient effectively.
- **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical conditions, or current symptoms. This will help the oral surgeon understand the patient's current condition.
- **Medications**: List all the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequencies. This information is crucial for the oral surgeon to ensure safe treatment.
- **Surgical Info**: Enter the details of the surgical procedure required for the patient. This will help the oral surgeon prepare for the procedure.
- **Surgical Procedure**: Select "Yes" if the procedure is surgical, "No" if it's not. This helps the oral surgeon to determine the type of procedure required.
- **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the patient has that may impact the surgical procedure. This information will aid the oral surgeon in providing proper care.
- **Allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient has that may be relevant to the procedure. This will help the oral surgeon to take necessary precautions.
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name to associate with the referral information.
