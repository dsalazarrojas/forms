# Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for telemedicine services, which allows healthcare providers to offer remote medical care and treatment. By completing this form, you are giving permission for your healthcare provider to communicate with you via electronic means and to access your medical information and records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Fill in your patient information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information required.
2. Choose your consent option:
	* Select **Consent** to allow your healthcare provider to offer telemedicine services.
	* Select **Decline** if you do not wish to participate in telemedicine services.
3. Review and confirm your consent by selecting the **Telemedicine Terms** option:
	* Choose **Yes** to agree to the terms and conditions of telemedicine services.
	* Choose **No** if you do not agree to the terms and conditions.
4. **Patient Privacy**: Please read and acknowledge our patient privacy statement.

* **Patient Information (page 1)**:
	+ **Patient Information**: A field to input your patient information (name, contact details, etc.)
* **Consent to Telemedicine Services (page 2)**:
	+ **Consent to Telemedicine Services**: Choose to grant or decline consent for telemedicine services.
* **Telemedicine Terms (page 3)**:
	+ **Telemedicine Terms**: Choose to agree or decline to the terms and conditions of telemedicine services.
* **Patient Privacy (page 4)**:
	+ **Patient Privacy**: Acknowledge our patient privacy statement.
