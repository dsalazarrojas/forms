**Sample Health Safety Facility Protocols Agreement Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The "Sample Health Safety Facility Protocols Agreement Form" is designed to collect business and safety information for healthcare facilities. This information will help facilities maintain a safe and healthy environment by adhering to industry standards and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business details, including the facility's name and location.
2. Provide an address for the facility where we can reach you if needed.
3. Give a brief description of the facility to help us understand the context.
4. Choose the facility contacts we can reach out to.
5. Select the health safety protocols your facility currently follows.
6. Identify your emergency contact person, in case we need to reach them.
7. Fill in the date and time of the form completion.
8. Add any additional comments or notes that might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Details** (`Business Details`, text, required/optional): Fill in your facility's name and location.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required/optional): Provide the address of your facility so we can reach you if needed.
* **Facility Description** (`facility_description`, text, required/optional): Give a brief description of your facility to help us understand the context.
* **Facility Contacts** (`Facility Contacts`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the contacts we can reach out to for further information.
* **Health Safety Protocols** (`Health Safety Protocols`, select one, required/optional): Select the health safety protocols your facility currently follows.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, select one, required/optional): Identify your emergency contact person.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Fill in the date of the form completion.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Fill in the time of the form completion.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes that might be helpful.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to ensure the safety and compliance of your facility.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by facility staff or management.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
