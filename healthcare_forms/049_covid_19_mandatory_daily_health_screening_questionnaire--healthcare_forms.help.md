This guide will help you complete the Covid 19 Mandatory Daily Health Screening Questionnaire form. Before starting, please ensure you have not already completed a similar form for the day, as this form is intended for daily screenings.

# Covid 19 Mandatory Daily Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your current health status, especially regarding symptoms related to COVID-19. Completing this form will help healthcare professionals assess your well-being and ensure your safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your contact email.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. If applicable, enter your address.
6. Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing from the list.
7. Answer if you have traveled outside of the home recently.
8. Answer if you have had contact with a person who tested positive for COVID-19.
9. Answer if you have any other health issues not related to today.
10. If not applicable, leave the medical condition, medication, vaccination, and emergency contact fields blank.
11. If not applicable, leave the emergency contact phone number and email blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that can be used for contact and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that can be reached at.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you are staying at a different address, provide it here.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_yes`, select_multiple, optional): Select any symptoms you are experiencing today. These include:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Runny nose/congestion
	+ Muscle aches
	+ Headache
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Nausea
	+ Vomiting
	+ Confusion
	+ Fatigue
	+ Runny eyes
	+ Coughing up blood
	+ Chest pain
	+ Difficulty breathing
* **Have you traveled outside of the home recently?** (`travel_history`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have traveled outside of your home recently, or 'False' if you have not.
* **Have you had contact with a person who tested positive for COVID-19?** (`contact_history`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have had contact with someone who tested positive, or 'False' if not.
* **Do you have any other health issues that you are not experiencing today?** (`symptoms_2`, select_one, optional): Answer 'True' if you have other health issues, or 'False' if not.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have a pre-existing medical condition, provide details here.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medication, provide details here.
* **Vaccination** (`vaccination`, text, optional): If you have received any vaccinations, provide details here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Provide the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you have any concerns or issues while completing this form, please contact the designated authorities for assistance.
* This form is intended for daily use; if you have already completed a similar form, you can disregard this one.
* Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date to guarantee your health safety and the safety of others.
