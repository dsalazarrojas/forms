# Disability Support Service Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals who require disability support services to provide essential information about themselves and their support needs. It ensures that those who require assistance can access the right support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide the patient's information, including name, age, and contact details.
2. Answer the referral questions, which will help us understand the reason for the referral.
3. Select the types of support services required (e.g., counseling, home visits, etc.).
4. Add any additional information that may be helpful for the support services team to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, text, required): This section is where you provide the basic information about the patient. Please enter the patient's name, age, and contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Referral Info** (Referral Info, text, required): Answer the referral questions truthfully, providing a clear explanation of why you require disability support services. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Social Worker Info** (Social Worker Info, text, required): If you have a social worker assigned to you, please provide their name, contact information, and any relevant details about your interactions with them.
* **Support Services** (Support Services, select multiple, optional): Select the types of support services you require, such as counseling, home visits, or respite care. You can choose multiple options if needed.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, optional): Use this section to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the support services team to know. This could include medical conditions, personal circumstances, or any other relevant details.
