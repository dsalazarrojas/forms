# Medical Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from medical volunteers, including their personal details, medical credentials, and availability for volunteer work. The collected data will be used to determine the applicant's qualifications and suitability for medical volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or confirmation.
3. Enter your address to help us verify your identity and location.
4. Answer the medical volunteer role question to indicate your interest in volunteering in a medical setting.
5. Select the dates for your availability and preferred shift start and end times.
6. If applicable, provide your medical experience, hospital affiliation, and volunteer hours completed.
7. Confirm your medical certification status for various areas.
8. Enter your medical license details, including the type, state, and expiration date.
9. Finally, confirm the relationship of the emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further discussion or confirmation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or confirmation.
* **Address**: Enter your address to help us verify your identity and location.
* **Medical license**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Emergency contact**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical volunteer role**: Choose "Yes" if you are interested in volunteering in a medical setting.
* **Shift start**: Select the start date and time for your available shifts.
* **Shift end**: Select the end date and time for your available shifts.
* **Availability**: Choose the dates you are available to volunteer.
* **Medical experience**: Provide any relevant medical experience you have.
* **Hospital affiliation**: If you are affiliated with a hospital, please enter the name.
* **Volunteer hours**: Enter the number of volunteer hours you have completed.
* **Medical volunteer certified**: Confirm your certification status for various areas.
* **Medical license date**: Enter the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical license number**: Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical license state**: Enter the state where your medical license was issued.
* **Medical license type**: Enter the type of medical license you hold.
* **Medical license expiration**: Enter the date your medical license expires.
* **Emergency contact relationship**: Confirm the relationship with the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your personal and medical details for accuracy.
* Be honest and clear when answering the medical certification questions.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* We will review your application and contact you shortly for further discussion or confirmation.
