# Antibiotic Selection Form For Surgery - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and guide the selection of an appropriate antibiotic for a scheduled surgical procedure. It helps the prescriber (surgeon or clinician) assess the patient's risk level and choose the best antibiotic option based on the provided information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Collect the patient's basic demographics and contact details under "Patient Information".
2. Enter the patient's full legal name under "Patient name".
3. Provide the date of birth under "Date of birth".
4. List any known drug allergies under "Known drug allergies", including the nature of the reaction.
5. List any current medications under "Current medications", as relevant to the antibiotic selection.
6. Enter a brief description of the scheduled surgical procedure under "Scheduled surgical procedure".
7. Classify the infection risk level as "Low", "Medium", or "High" under "Assessed infection risk level".
8. Enter the recommended antibiotic option under "Recommended antibiotic option" including the agent name, dose, and route of administration.
9. Optionally provide the clinical reasons or protocol reference for the antibiotic selection under "Rationale for antibiotic selection".
10. Enter the name of the prescriber under "Prescriber name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Date of birth** (Date, Required): Provide the patient's date of birth to help with dosing and record keeping.
* **Known drug allergies** (Text, Required): List any known drug allergies the patient has experienced, including the nature of the reaction.
* **Current medications** (Text, Optional): List any current medications the patient is taking, as relevant to the antibiotic selection.
* **Scheduled surgical procedure** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the scheduled surgical procedure.
* **Assessed infection risk level** (Select One, Required): Classify the infection risk level as "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **Recommended antibiotic** (Text, Required): Enter the recommended antibiotic option, including the agent name, dose, and route of administration.
* **Rationale for choice** (Text, Optional): Optionally provide the clinical reasons or protocol reference for the antibiotic selection.
* **Prescriber name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the prescriber (surgeon or clinician).
