# diagnostic_imaging_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for authorizing diagnostic imaging procedures for patients. It requires information about the patient, the procedure, and the personnel involved in the authorization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name.
2. Select the date prosthesis required (if any).
3. Enter the date of the service.
4. Select the date procedures required.
5. Choose the type of imaging authorization required.
6. Enter the patient's medical history.
7. Select the imaging procedure.
8. Choose whether the patient has given consent for the procedure.
9. Select who authorized the procedure.
10. Enter the date and time authorized.
11. Enter the date and time of the procedure.
12. Select the medical nurse, doctor, and specialist involved in the procedure.
13. (Optional) Enter the date of the next procedure.
14. (Optional) Enter the date of the last procedure provided.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to fill them in to ensure accurate record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date Prophets Required** (Date Prophets Required, date, required): Select the date prosthesis required.
* **Date Of Service** (Date Of Service, date, required): Enter the date of the service.
* **Date Procedures Required** (Date Procedures Required, date, required): Select the date procedures required.
* **Authorization Type** (Authorization Type, select one, required): Choose the type of imaging authorization required (e.g., MRI, CT, Ultrasound).
* **Medical History** (Medical History, note, required): Enter the patient's medical history.
* **Goals** (Goals, text, optional): Enter any additional goals or notes for the procedure.
* **Imaging Procedure** (Imaging Procedure, select multiple, required): Select the imaging procedure (e.g., Chest, Abdomen, Head and Neck).
* **Patient Consent** (Patient Consent, select one, required): Choose whether the patient has given consent for the procedure.
* **Authorized By** (Authorized By, select one, required): Select who authorized the procedure (e.g., Doctor, Nurse, Other).
* **Date Authorized** (Date Authorized, date, required): Enter the date authorized.
* **Time Authorized** (Time Authorized, time, required): Enter the time authorized.
* **Time Of Service** (Time Of Service, time, required): Enter the time of the service.
* **Medical Nurse** (Medical Nurse, select multiple, required): Select the medical nurse involved in the procedure.
* **Medical Doctor** (Medical Doctor, select multiple, required): Select the medical doctor involved in the procedure.
* **Medical Specialist** (Medical Specialist, select multiple, required): Select the medical specialist involved in the procedure.
* **Other Healthcare Provider** (Other Healthcare Provider, select multiple, optional): Select any other healthcare providers involved in the procedure.
* **Date Of Next Procedure** (Date Of Next Procedure, date, optional): Enter the date of the next procedure.
* **Time Of Next Procedure** (Time Of Next Procedure, time, optional): Enter the time of the next procedure.
* **Date Of Last Procedure** (Date Of Last Procedure, date, optional): Enter the date of the last procedure.
* **Date Of Last Procedure Provided** (Date Of Last Procedure Provided, date, required): Enter the date of the last procedure provided.
* **Time Of Last Procedure** (Time Of Last Procedure, time, required): Enter the time of the last procedure.
* **Date Of Last Procedure Provided By** (Date Of Last Procedure Provided By, date, required): Enter the date of the last procedure provided by.
* **Date Of Last Procedure Provided By Doctor** (Date Of Last Procedure Provided By Doctor, date, optional): Enter the date of the last procedure provided by the doctor.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* If unsure about any field, ask for clarification from the relevant personnel.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Keep the form up-to-date to reflect any changes in the patient's information.
