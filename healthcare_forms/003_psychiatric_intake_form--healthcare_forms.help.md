# Psychiatric Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatric Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's psychiatric and medical history, as well as their current mental status and family history. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and inform treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's name and contact information on the first page.
2. Provide a brief psychiatric and medical history in the respective text fields.
3. Select the current mental status and medical status from the provided options.
4. Indicate any medications being used.
5. List any relevant family history information.
6. Select any substance use history that may be relevant.
7. Provide any additional mental status information as a date.
8. Select any relevant mental status options.
9. Select any family history options.
10. Finally, enter any email addresses related to substance use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This field is where you enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for the patient's contact information, including any relevant details that might be useful for follow-up.
* **Psychiatric History**: This is a text field for you to enter the patient's psychiatric history, including any relevant details or events that might be helpful for their treatment.
* **Medical History**: This is another text field for you to enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant details or events that might be helpful for their treatment.
* **Chief Complaint**: This field is for you to enter the patient's chief complaint or the reason they are seeking treatment.
* **Mental Status**: This field is for you to select the patient's current mental status from the provided options.
* **Medical Status**: This field is for you to select the patient's current medical status from the provided options.
* **Medication Use**: This field is for you to enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Family History**: This field is for you to select any relevant family history information from the provided options.
* **Substance Use**: This field is for you to enter any relevant substance use history.
* **Mental Status (Date)**: This field is for you to enter a date related to the patient's mental status.
* **Medical Status (Date)**: This field is for you to enter a date related to the patient's medical status.
* **Mental Status 1**: This field is for you to select another relevant mental status option.
* **Medical Status 1**: This field is for you to select another relevant medical status option.
* **Family History 1**: This field is for you to select multiple relevant family history options.
* **Substance Prophets**: This field is for you to enter any email addresses related to substance use.
* **Mental Status 2**: This field is for you to select multiple relevant mental status options.

Please note that some fields may not be required for every patient, and some fields may have additional information or instructions that are not explicitly mentioned here. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to a healthcare professional for guidance.
