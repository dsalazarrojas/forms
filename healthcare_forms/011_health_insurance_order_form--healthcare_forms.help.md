# Health Insurance Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients or healthcare providers about their health insurance orders. It will help healthcare providers, administrators, or other authorized personnel to track and manage insurance orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last name** field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the **date of birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your address in the **address** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **phone number** field.
7. Select your insurance provider from the **insurance provider** dropdown list.
8. Select your insurance plan from the **insurance plan** dropdown list.
9. Enter your medical group name in the **medical group** field.
10. If required, add your signature in the **signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Select your insurance provider (e.g., Aetna, Blue Cross, Blue Shield).
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Select your insurance plan (e.g., Basic, Premium, Gold).
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, text, optional): Enter your medical group name.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Add your signature if required.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the form (e.g., Client, Provider, Agent).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the form (e.g., New, Pending, Active, Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the form.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_multiple, optional): Select if the form is submitted or not.
* **Reviewed** (`reviewed`, select_multiple, optional): Select if the form is reviewed or not.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, select_one, optional): Select who reviewed the form (e.g., Client, Provider, Agent).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any errors or issues.
* If you encounter any difficulties while filling out this form, please contact your healthcare provider or the form administrator.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.
