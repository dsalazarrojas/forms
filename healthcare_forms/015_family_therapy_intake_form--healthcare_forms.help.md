# Family Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a family's background and treatment goals for family therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Therapy Intake Form** (`family_therapy_intake_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, text, required): Please enter the family name as it appears on official documents.
* **Family Relationship Status** (`family_relationship_status`, select_one, required): Choose your family relationship status from the options Single, Married, Other, Divorced, Separated, or Widowed.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please enter the contact information for the family member filling out the form.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose the relationship of the emergency contact from the options Spouse, Parent, Friend, or Other.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, text, required): List all family members, including children, spouses, and parents.
* **Family Member Ages** (`family_member_ages`, text, required): Please enter the ages of all family members.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide a brief medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications being taken by family members.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies of family members.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Group** (`insurance_group`, text, optional): Enter the insurance group.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, text, optional): Enter the insurance plan.
* **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`, text, required): Please enter the insurance ID.
* **Insurance Provider Type** (`insurance_provider_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the insurance provider type from the options Private, Public, or Both.
* **Insurance Plan Type** (`insurance_plan_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the insurance plan type from the options Single, Family, or Both.
* **Insurance Deductible** (`insurance_deductible`, text, optional): Please enter the deductible.
* **Co-Pay** (`insurance_co_pay`, text, optional): Please enter co-pay.
* **Co-Insurance** (`insurance_co_ins`, text, optional): Please enter co-insurance.
* **Family Therapy Goals** (`family_therapy_goals`, text, required): Please list the family's therapy goals.
* **Family Therapy Frequency** (`family_therapy_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the family therapy frequency from the options Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Family Therapy Duration** (`family_therapy_duration`, text, required): Please enter the expected duration of family therapy.
* **Family Therapy Cost** (`family_therapy_cost`, text, optional): Please enter the estimated cost of family therapy (if applicable).
