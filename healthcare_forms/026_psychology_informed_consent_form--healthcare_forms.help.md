# Psychology Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Psychology Informed Consent Form" is a crucial document used in psychological studies, research, or treatments to obtain informed consent from participants. It ensures that they understand the study's goals, methods, and potential risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information (Researcher Name, Study Title, and Study Description).
2. Provide a detailed treatment plan for the study or treatment.
3. Check the boxes indicating your understanding of the study's voluntary nature, confidentiality, and right to withdraw at any time.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information as the researcher or treating professional.
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the study or treatment you are participating in.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the study or treatment.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required/optional): Describe the treatment plan and methods you will undergo as part of the study or treatment.
* **Research Informed Consent** (`research_informed_consent`, text, required/optional): Check the box to indicate that you understand the study's voluntary nature.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all the boxes indicating you understand:
	+ You understand that participation in this study is voluntary.
	+ You understand that my participation will be kept confidential.
	+ You understand that I can withdraw from the study at any time.
* **Confidentiality** (`confidentiality`, select_one, required/optional): Check the box indicating that your participation will be kept confidential.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your informed consent.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand each field's purpose before filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your researcher or treating professional for clarification.
