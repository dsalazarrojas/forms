# medicare_opt_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Opt-out Form is used to document a healthcare provider's or practice's opt-out status and related details. This form is required for providers or practices that wish to opt-out of Medicare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Provider name**, which is the name of the provider or practice opting out.
2. Enter the **Practice name**, which is the name of the practice associated with the provider.
3. Enter the **Date of birth**, which is the date of birth of the provider or practice owner.
4. Enter the **Date of opt out**, which is the date the provider or practice is opting out of Medicare services.
5. Provide the **Reason for opt out**, which is the reason the provider or practice is opting out.
6. Enter the **Signature**, which is the provider's or practice's signature.
7. Enter the **Provider ID**, which is the unique identifier of the provider.
8. Enter the **Practice address 1**, which is the address of the practice.
9. Enter the **Practice city**, which is the city of the practice.
10. Enter the **Practice state**, which is the state of the practice.
11. Enter the **Practice zip**, which is the zip code of the practice.
12. Enter the **Provider phone**, which is the contact phone number of the provider.
13. Enter the **Practice email**, which is the email of the practice.
14. Enter the **Practice fax**, which is not required but can be used for contact purposes.
15. Enter the **Practice license**, which is the practice's license number.
16. Enter the **Practice NPI**, which is the National Provider Identifier (NPI) of the practice.
17. Enter the **Provider NPI**, which is the NPI of the provider.
18. Select the **Opt out status**, which is the status of the opt-out (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
19. Enter the **Opt out date**, which is the date of the opt-out.
20. Enter the **Signature date**, which is the date of the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Provider name** (1): Enter the name of the provider or practice opting out.
- **Practice name** (2): Enter the name of the practice associated with the provider.
- **Date of birth** (3): Enter the date of birth of the provider or practice owner.
- **Date of opt out** (4): Enter the date the provider or practice is opting out of Medicare services.
- **Reason for opt out** (5): Provide a clear and concise reason for opting out.
- **Signature** (6): Sign and date the signature to confirm the opt-out.
- **Provider ID** (7): Enter the unique identifier of the provider.
- **Practice address 1** (8): Enter the address of the practice.
- **Practice city** (10): Enter the city of the practice.
- **Practice state** (11): Enter the state of the practice.
- **Practice zip** (12): Enter the zip code of the practice.
- **Provider phone** (13): Enter the contact phone number of the provider.
- **Practice email** (14): Enter the email of the practice.
- **Practice fax** (15): Enter the fax number of the practice (optional).
- **Practice license** (16): Enter the practice's license number.
- **Practice NPI** (17): Enter the National Provider Identifier (NPI) of the practice.
- **Provider NPI** (18): Enter the NPI of the provider.
- **Opt out status** (23): Select the status of the opt-out (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Opt out date** (24): Enter the date of the opt-out.
- **Signature date** (25): Enter the date of the signature.

Note: Fields marked as required must be completed before submitting the form.
