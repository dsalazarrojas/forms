# Pre Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Screening Form is designed to collect contact and medical information from patients before screening. This information will be used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's eligibility for certain medical procedures or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field.
2. Enter any relevant medical history information.
3. Provide the required date for medical history.

* **Contact Information (2)**: Select one or more of the following options to provide contact information:

* **Medical History (3)**: Enter any relevant medical history information.
* **Medical History Date (4)**: Enter the date of your medical history.
