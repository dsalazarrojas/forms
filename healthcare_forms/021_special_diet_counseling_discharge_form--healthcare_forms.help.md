# Special Diet Counseling Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge status of patients, including their dietary restrictions, medical conditions, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the discharge date of the patient.
2. Select any dietary restrictions that apply to the patient.
3. Record the patient's weight at discharge.
4. Note any patient medical conditions that need attention.
5. Provide dietary guidance to the patient.
6. Schedule follow-up appointments as necessary.
7. List any healthcare providers involved in the patient's care.
8. Record any patient allergies.
9. List the patient's medication.
10. Determine if the patient has a diet counseling form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Discharge Date
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
### Dietary Restrictions
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions that apply to the patient, such as "Yes" or "No".

* **Discharge Weight** (`discharge_weight`, `number`, optional): Record the patient's weight at discharge.

* **Patient Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" if the patient has any medical conditions.
