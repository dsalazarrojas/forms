# medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Medical History Form" is used to collect detailed information about a patient's medical history, including allergies, medical procedures, and other relevant health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are entering the correct information.
2. For each field, choose the relevant options from the dropdown menus or enter your answer in the text fields.
3. Ensure you have answered all required fields (indicated by).
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.
