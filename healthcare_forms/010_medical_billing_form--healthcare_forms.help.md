# Medical Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Billing Form is designed for healthcare providers to document and process patient billing information, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of medical services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
2. **Date of Birth**: Input the patient's date of birth in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
3. **Address**: Enter the patient's address for billing purposes.
4. **Insurance Name**: Enter the patient's insurance provider name.
5. **Insurance ID**: Enter the patient's insurance ID number (if applicable).
6. **Medical Group**: Select the medical group under which the services were provided (A, B, or C).
7. **Medical Specialty**: Select all medical specialties that were applied during the visit (Dermatology, Cardiology, Rheumatology).
8. **Medical Service**: Select all services provided to the patient (Consultation, Treatment, Follow-up).
9. **Billing Date**: Input the date of service for billing purposes (yyyy-mm-dd).
10. **Billing Time**: Input the time of service for billing purposes (hh:mm).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Input the patient's date of birth in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter the patient's address for billing purposes.
* **Insurance Name** (text, optional): Enter the patient's insurance provider name.
* **Insurance ID** (text, optional): Enter the patient's insurance ID number (if applicable).
* **Medical Group** (select_one, optional): Select the medical group under which the services were provided (A, B, or C).
* **Medical Specialty** (select_multiple, optional): Select all medical specialties that were applied during the visit (Dermatology, Cardiology, Rheumatology).
* **Medical Service** (select_multiple, optional): Select all services provided to the patient (Consultation, Treatment, Follow-up).
* **Billing Date** (date, required): Input the date of service for billing purposes (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Billing Time** (time, required): Input the time of service for billing purposes (hh:mm).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the patient's details for accuracy.
* Select the correct medical group, specialties, and services provided.
* Verify insurance information if applicable.
* Enter the correct billing date and time.
