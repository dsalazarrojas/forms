# Doctor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and usage of the Doctor Complaint Form, a tool designed to help healthcare services gather feedback from patients and doctors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate medical service you received during your visit.
2. Choose the healthcare provider who assisted you during your visit.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your complaint or feedback.
4. Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, use the note field to include them.
6. Finally, choose how you would rate your overall experience: positive, negative, or neutral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide your feedback about your experience at the healthcare service.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): Describe your complaint or issue with the service.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can get back to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Medical Service** (`medical_service`, select_one, optional): Select the medical service you received during your visit.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the healthcare provider who assisted you during your visit.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience as positive, negative, or neutral.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback to help us improve our service.
* Make sure to select the correct medical service and healthcare provider for your visit.
* If you have any complaints, please describe them clearly and concisely.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the note field to include them.
* Finally, choose your rating honestly to help us understand your overall experience.
