# Pain Psych Patient Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pain Psych Patient Questionnaire is designed to collect information from patients about their pain experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your current pain experience for each question.
2. If you have any medical conditions or notes about your history, please provide them in the text fields.
3. Ensure that you answer all required questions and optional questions that are marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: 

* **Pain Type** (`pain_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you experience any kind of pain or discomfort. If you don't experience pain, select "No".
* **Pain Characteristics** (`pain_characteristics`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all characteristics that describe your pain (e.g., sharp, dull, burning, stabbing, etc.).
* **Pain Location** (`pain_location`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the location where you feel the pain (e.g., head, back, abdomen, etc.).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any medical history, such as surgeries, injuries, or chronic conditions.
* **Psychological History** (`psychological_history`, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you have any psychological history, such as mental health conditions, trauma, etc.
* **Pain Impact** (`pain_impact`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the impact of your pain on your daily life (e.g., affects daily activities, interferes with sleep, etc.).
* **Pain Frequency** (`pain_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Please select how often you experience pain (e.g., daily, occasionally, rarely, etc.).
* **Pain Duration** (`pain_duration`, select_one, required/optional): Please select how long you have been experiencing pain.
* **Pain Intensity** (`pain_intensity`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the intensity of your pain (e.g., mild, moderate, severe, etc.).
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, text, required/optional): Please provide any additional medical history or conditions that are not listed in the previous questions.
* **Psychological History Notes** (`psychological_history_notes`, text, required/optional): Please provide any psychological history or notes that are not listed in the previous questions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): Please list any medical conditions you have that may be related to your pain experience.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by patients, but if you are a healthcare professional, please use your professional judgment when interpreting the results.
