# Pharmacy Prescription Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pharmacy Prescription Communication Form is designed to gather and communicate essential information between patients and healthcare providers regarding their prescribed medication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully** before starting to fill it out.
2. **Make sure to fill out all required fields**, even though the form is designed for patients, it is also important for healthcare providers to have accurate and up-to-date information.
3. **Be thorough** when filling out the form, as the information collected will be used to ensure accurate medication management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**:
	+ <name> (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and any other identifying information.
* **Medication Information (page 2)**:
	+ <label> (`medication_info`, text, required): Describe the prescribed medication, including its name, dosage, and any relevant instructions.
* **Medical History (page 3)**:
	+ <label> (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous injuries.
* **Submit (page 4)**:
	+ <label> (`submit_button`, text, required): This field is not meant to be filled out by the user, it is a submit button used to finalize the form submission.

Note that fields are not explicitly labeled as "required" or "optional" but based on the form structure, some fields might be more important than others.
