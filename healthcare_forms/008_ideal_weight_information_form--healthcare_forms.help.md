# Ideal Weight Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ideal Weight Information Form is designed to collect patient information for determining their ideal weight. This form is used to gather essential details such as gender, age, height, and current weight to calculate a patient's ideal weight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your gender from the provided options.
2. Enter your age.
3. Enter your height.
4. Enter your current weight.
5. Provide any additional information or comments in the "Instructions" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other). This field is optional.
- **Ideal Weight** (`ideal_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your ideal weight (no specific unit is required, just the numerical value). This field is optional.
- **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age to help calculate your ideal weight.
- **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: false): Enter your height to help calculate your ideal weight.
- **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current weight (no specific unit is required, just the numerical value). This field is optional.
- **Instructions** (`instructions`, `note`, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional information or comments about your ideal weight.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate information for precise calculations.
- If you're unsure about any information, please consult with a healthcare professional.
- The form is not intended for medical diagnosis, but rather to provide a general estimate of ideal weight.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
