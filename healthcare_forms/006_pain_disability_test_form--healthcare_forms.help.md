# Pain Disability Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pain Disability Test Form is a comprehensive assessment tool designed to evaluate the level of pain disability in patients. This form is intended to help healthcare professionals gather valuable information about pain experience and its impact on patients' daily lives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question accurately and honestly, as it will help healthcare professionals to better understand your pain experience.
2. Fill out each field carefully, ensuring that you've selected the correct option or entered the correct information.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or contact your healthcare provider.
4. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

* **Pain Intensity** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`):
