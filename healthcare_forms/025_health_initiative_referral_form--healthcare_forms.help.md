# health_initiative_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_initiative_referral_form" is designed for healthcare professionals to refer patients to various health initiatives, gathering necessary information for proper patient care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Describe the patient's medical conditions in the "medical_conditions" field.
3. Provide a brief medical history for the patient in the "medical_history" field.
4. Select the referral reason from the "referral_reason" field.
5. Enter the healthcare provider's name and contact information in the "healthcare_provider" and "patient_contact_info" fields.
6. Choose the follow-up method (e.g., phone, email, or mail) and frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly) in the "follow_up_method" and "follow_up_frequency" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the follow-up location in the "follow_up_location" field.
8. Provide any additional follow-up comments or method details in the "follow_up_comments" and "follow_up_method_details" fields.
9. Select the health initiatives the patient will be referred to, providing details in the "healthcare_initiatives_details" field.
10. Confirm the follow-up date and any further instructions or comments in the "follow_up_date" and "follow_up_comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (text): Enter the patient's name.
* **medical_conditions** (text): Describe the patient's medical conditions.
* **medical_history** (text): Provide a brief medical history for the patient.
* **referral_reason** (select_one): Select the reason for referral from the provided options (e.g., Health Initiative).
* **healthcare_provider** (text): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **patient_contact_info** (text): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **referral_date** (date): Enter the referral date.
* **follow_up_info** (text): Provide follow-up information or comments.
* **next_steps** (note): Enter any next steps or instructions.
* **referral_comments** (note): Enter any referral comments or notes.
* **healthcare_initiatives** (select_multiple): Select the health initiatives the patient will be referred to.
* **healthcare_initiatives_details** (text): Provide details about the selected health initiatives.
* **follow_up_contact** (text): Enter the follow-up contact information.
* **referral_source** (text): Enter the referral source or origin.
* **follow_up_method** (select_one): Select the follow-up method (e.g., phone, email, or mail).
* **follow_up_frequency** (select_one): Select the follow-up frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **follow_up_location** (text): Enter the follow-up location.
* **follow_up_method_details** (text): Provide follow-up method details.
* **follow_up_frequency_details** (text): Provide follow-up frequency details.
* **follow_up_contact_method** (select_one): Select the follow-up contact method.
* **follow_up_contact_location** (text): Enter the follow-up contact location.
* **follow_up_comments** (note): Enter any follow-up comments or notes.
* **follow_up_date** (date): Enter the follow-up date.
* **follow_up_method_details** (text): Provide follow-up method details.
* **follow_up_location** (text): Enter the follow-up location.

Tips:

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of entered information to avoid errors.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
