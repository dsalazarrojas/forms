# health_initiative_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_initiative_referral_form" is designed for healthcare professionals to refer patients to various health initiatives, gathering necessary information for proper patient care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Describe the patient's medical conditions in the "medical_conditions" field.
3. Provide a brief medical history for the patient in the "medical_history" field.
4. Select the referral reason from the "referral_reason" field.
5. Enter the healthcare provider's name and contact information in the "healthcare_provider" and "patient_contact_info" fields.
6. Choose the follow-up method (e.g., phone, email, or mail) and frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly) in the "follow_up_method" and "follow_up_frequency" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the follow-up location in the "follow_up_location" field.
8. Provide any additional follow-up comments or method details in the "follow_up_comments" and "follow_up_method_details" fields.
9. Select the health initiatives the patient will be referred to, providing details in the "healthcare_initiatives_details" field.
10. Confirm the follow-up date and any further instructions or comments in the "follow_up_date" and "follow_up_comments" fields.
