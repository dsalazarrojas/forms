# Medical Information And Emergency Contacts Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information and emergency contacts for patient care and administration. It is intended for use in healthcare settings to gather essential details about patients' medical history, contact information, and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill in the Medical Information Form with your medical history, allergies, and any relevant health conditions.
3. Enter your name and date of birth in the Patient Name and Date of Birth fields.
4. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, contact address, phone number, email, and relationship to you.
5. Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
6. Enter any additional contact information for your emergency contact, such as alternate phone numbers and email addresses.
7. Add any notes or comments about your contact information in the Contact Note field.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Information Form** (`medical_information_form`): A text field to provide your medical history, allergies, and any relevant health conditions.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Patient** (`relationship_to_patient`): Indicate your relationship to your emergency contact (e.g., Other, Spouse, Child, Parent).
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Describe any medical conditions or health issues you are dealing with.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Indicate the relationship between your emergency contact and you (e.g., Other, Spouse, Child, Parent).
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_alternate`): Enter an alternate phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email_alternate`): Enter an alternate email address for your emergency contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_alternate`): Enter an alternate phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address for your emergency contact.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`): Provide any additional notes or comments about your contact information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed and provided accurate information.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`): Sign the healthcare provider's name to confirm that they have reviewed and verified the information.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you complete this form.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you complete this form.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`): Enter the name of your medical group or facility.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`): Enter the name of your physician or healthcare provider.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`): Enter any relevant medical license information, if applicable.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable to your specific situation, and you should fill out only the relevant fields.
