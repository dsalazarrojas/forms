# healthcare_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare_conference_registration_form is designed to collect essential information from individuals registering for a conference. This form will help the organizers in planning and managing the event by collecting details about the conference attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Conference Details" section with the required information about the conference you want to register for.
2. In the "Submitter Details" section, provide your personal details so that we can contact you in case of any updates or changes.
3. In the "Contact Info" section, please enter your preferred contact information in case we need to reach out to you.
4. Finally, in the "Attendance Details" section, let us know about your attendance preferences (e.g., dietary needs, accommodation requirements, or any special requests).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Details** (`conference_details`): Enter the title of the conference you're interested in, including the date and location, to help us get a better understanding of the event you're registering for.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`): This section is for your personal details. Please fill in your name and email address so that we can get in touch with you regarding any updates or changes to the event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This is where you can specify your preferred contact method for reaching out to you, such as phone number, email, or postal address.
* **Attendance Details** (`attendance_details`): Let us know about your preferences, including any dietary requirements, accommodation needs, or special requests related to your attendance.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
