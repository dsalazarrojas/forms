# Trauma Bag Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trauma Bag Checklist is a form designed to document the contents of a trauma bag, a critical medical supply kit used in emergency situations. The form helps ensure that the bag is fully stocked and ready for use in case of a medical emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Trauma Bag" page to start filling out the form.
2. Select the contents of the trauma bag from the list of options for each relevant area (e.g. Airway, Breathing, Chest, etc.). You can choose multiple options by clicking on each item in the list.
3. Fill in the "Review and update the Trauma Bag contents before the next inspection" field with the date you completed the review.
4. Fill in the "Assigned to", "Checked by", and "Checked date" fields with the relevant information.
5. Add any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
6. Select the "Assigned to" personnel from the dropdown list.
7. Enter the next inspection date in the "Next inspection" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trauma Bag**: This is the header page for the form.
* **Airway**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for airway management, such as Oxygen, Bag Valve Mask, and End Tidal CO2 Monitor.
* **Breathing**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for breathing management, such as Suctioning Equipment, Nasal Cautery Tube, and Laryngoscopy.
* **Chest**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for chest management, such as Chest Tube, End Tidal CO2 Monitor, and Chest Seal.
* **Abdominal**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for abdominal management, such as Abdominal Ultrasound, Pelvic Packing, and Suctioning Equipment.
* **Extremities**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for extremities management, such as Tourniquet, Suctioning Equipment, and Nasal Cautery Tube.
* **Head**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for head management, such as Headache, Suctioning Equipment, and Nasal Cautery Tube.
* **Neck**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for neck management, such as Cervical Collar, Cervical Traction, and Suctioning Equipment.
* **Thorax**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for thorax management, such as End Tidal CO2 Monitor, Chest Tube, and Suctioning Equipment.
* **Abdomen**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for abdomen management, such as Abdominal Ultrasound, Pelvic Packing, and Suctioning Equipment.
* **Extremities**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for extremities management, such as Tourniquet, Suctioning Equipment, and Nasal Cautery Tube.
* **Neuro**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for neuro management, such as Headache, Suctioning Equipment, and Nasal Cautery Tube.
* **Spine**: Select the equipment and supplies needed for spine management, such as Cervical Collar, Cervical Traction, and Suctioning Equipment.
* **Other**: Add any other equipment or supplies not listed in the other fields.
* **Review and update the Trauma Bag contents before the next inspection**: Enter the date you completed the review.
* **Assigned to**: Select the personnel assigned to the trauma bag.
* **Checked by**: Enter the name of the person who checked the form.
* **Checked date**: Enter the date the form was checked.
* **Checked time**: Enter the time the form was checked.
* **Checked by signature**: Enter the signature of the person who checked the form.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the form.
* **Assigned to**: Select the personnel assigned to the trauma bag (this field is a duplicate of the "Assigned to" field).
* **Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the form.
* **Next inspection**: Enter the date of the next inspection (if applicable).
* **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Review and update the Trauma Bag contents before the next inspection**: This field is not a field you need to fill, but rather a reminder to review and update the contents of the trauma bag before the next inspection.
