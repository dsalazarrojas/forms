# Chiropractic Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a consent document used to ensure you, as a patient, understand your rights, responsibilities, and the treatment plan before undergoing chiropractic care. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully.
2.  Ensure you understand each question and the implications of your answers.
3.  Discuss any questions or concerns with your chiropractor before submitting the form.
4.  Sign and date the form to confirm your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name (Patient Name)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (Patient DOB)**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are consenting to the treatment plan.
* **Chiropractor Name**: Enter the name of the chiropractor performing the treatment.
* **-- Treatment Authorization --**: This is a note section to acknowledge you are providing authorization for treatment.
* **I authorize chiropractic treatment**: Select "Yes" if you understand and agree with the treatment plan.
* **I understand my treatment plan**: Select "Yes" to indicate you have a clear understanding of the treatment procedure.
* **All my questions have been answered**: Select "Yes" to confirm you have asked and received answers to all questions.
* **Alternative options were explained**: Select "Yes" to indicate you have been informed of alternative treatment options.
* **-- Risks and Benefits --**: This is a note section to acknowledge the risks and benefits of the treatment.
* **I understand the potential risks of treatment**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the potential risks.
* **I understand the potential benefits**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the benefits of the treatment.
* **I acknowledge there is no guarantee of outcome**: Select "Yes" to indicate you understand that treatment outcomes are not guaranteed.
* **I understand the treatment fees**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the fees associated with the treatment.
* **I understand the payment terms**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the payment terms and conditions.
* **I understand insurance coverage and my responsibility**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the insurance coverage and your responsibilities.
* **-- Cancellation and Attendance --**: This is a note section to acknowledge the cancellation policy.
* **I understand the cancellation policy**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the policy.
* **I understand the no-show policy**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the policy.
* **-- Medical Disclosure --**: This is a note section to acknowledge the disclosure of medical information.
* **I have disclosed all relevant medical information**: Select "Yes" to confirm you have provided all necessary medical information.
* **I release the chiropractor from liability**: Select "I agree" to confirm you release the chiropractor from liability.
* **By signing, I give informed consent**: Select "I consent" to confirm you give informed consent.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully before submitting it to your chiropractor.
* Discuss any questions or concerns with your chiropractor before signing the form.
* Ensure you understand each section before confirming informed consent.
