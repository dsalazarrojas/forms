# Pre Vaccination Checklist For COVID 19 Vaccines - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Vaccination Checklist For COVID 19 Vaccines form is designed to collect essential information from healthcare professionals about patients before administering COVID-19 vaccines. This form helps ensure a safe and efficient vaccination process by gathering crucial data on patient medical conditions, allergies, and other important factors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary patient information and medical records available.
2. Review the form carefully and select the relevant fields that apply to each patient.
3. Fill in the form fields with accurate and complete information.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding with the vaccination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: This field collects basic patient information (e.g., name, phone number). Please fill in the patient's name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that apply to the patient from the list of options. This will help healthcare professionals identify potential risks and take necessary precautions.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies that apply to the patient from the list of options. This ensures that the patient's allergies are accounted for during vaccination administration.
* **Medication**: Fill in the name and dose of any medications the patient is currently taking. This helps healthcare professionals identify potential interactions with the vaccine.
* **Vital Signs**: Select the patient's vital signs (yes/no) as applicable.
* **Vaccination Site**: Choose the specific area where the vaccine will be administered (e.g., left arm, right leg).
* **Patient Status**: Select the patient's current status (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the vaccination appointment.
* **Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes or observations about the patient.
* **Phone Number**: Provide the patient's phone number for follow-up or emergency contact.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address for communication.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address for follow-up or contact purposes.
* **Medical History**: Select any relevant medical history items that apply to the patient.
* **Laboratory**: Select any laboratory tests or screenings that have been performed on the patient.
* **Blood Tire**: Select the patient's blood tire status.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding with the vaccination process.
* If a patient has multiple medical conditions or allergies, select all applicable options.
* If a patient has taken any medications, provide the name and dose.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
