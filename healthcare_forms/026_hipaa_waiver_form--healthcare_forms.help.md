The HIPAA Waiver Form is a document that requires patient consent to disclose personal and medical information to authorized parties. This form helps to ensure that patients are aware of and agree to the sharing of their medical information in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA). The form typically requires the patient's name, medical record number, and contact information to be completed, along with other relevant details such as the date of birth and authorization dates. The form is used to grant permission for healthcare providers to share medical information with authorized individuals or organizations.

# HIPAA Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Waiver Form is a document that requires patient consent to disclose personal and medical information to authorized parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, medical record number, and date of birth.
2. Provide the contact phone number and address of the patient.
3. Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
4. Sign and date the signature line to indicate that you have agreed to the terms of the form.
5. Choose the authorized person or organization that will receive the medical information.
6. Date and sign the authorization line to confirm that you have granted permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required)
* This is the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or medical record.

### 2. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, Text, Required)
* This is a unique identifier assigned to the patient by the healthcare provider.

### 3. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional)
* This is the patient's date of birth.

### 4. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional)
* This is the patient's contact phone number.

### 5. **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional)
* This is the patient's address.

### 6. **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
* This is the patient's email address (if applicable).

### 7. **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, Date, Required)
* This is the date the patient signed the waiver form.

### 8. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Text, Required)
* This is the patient's signature indicating they have read and agreed to the terms of the form.

### 9. **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, Select One, Required)
* This is the authorized person or organization that will receive the medical information.

### 10. **Authorized Date** (`authorized_date`, Date, Required)
* This is the date the authorized person agreed to receive the medical information.

### 11. **Waiver** (`waiver`, Note, Required)
* This is a note indicating the patient's waiver to share medical information.

### 12. **Authorization** (`authorization`, Note, Required)
* This is a note indicating the authorized person's authorization to receive the medical information.

### 13. **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, Text, Optional)
* This is the name of the medical group or organization that will receive the medical information.

### 14. **Physician** (`physician`, Text, Optional)
* This is the name of the physician or healthcare provider.

### 15. **Medical Fax** (`medical_fax`, Text, Optional)
* This is the fax number of the medical group or organization.

### 16. **Fax** (`fax`, Text, Optional)
* This is the patient's fax number.

### 17. **Email Provider** (`email_provider`, Text, Optional)
* This is the email address of the medical group or organization.

### 18. **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, Text, Optional)
* This is the patient's medical record number (or other identifying information).

### 19. **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, Date, Optional)
* This is the date the patient authorized the sharing of medical information.

### 20. **Medical Group Name** (`medical_group_name`, Text, Optional)
* This is the name of the medical group or organization.

### 21. **Patient Signature 2** (`patient_signature2`, Text, Required)
* This is the patient's second signature indicating they have read and agreed to the terms of the form.

### 22. **Authorized Person 2** (`authorized_person2`, Select One, Required)
* This is the authorized person or organization that will receive the medical information.

### 23. **Authorized Date 2** (`authorized_date2`, Date, Required)
* This is the date the authorized person agreed to receive the medical information.

### 24. **Authorization 2** (`authorization2`, Note, Required)
* This is a note indicating the authorized person's authorization to receive the medical information.

### 25. **Waiver 2** (`waiver2`, Note, Required)
* This is a note indicating the patient's waiver to share medical information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the terms of the form before signing.
* Make sure to date and sign all required fields to confirm your agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
