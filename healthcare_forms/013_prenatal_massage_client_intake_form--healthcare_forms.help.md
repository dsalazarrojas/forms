# Prenatal Massage Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Prenatal Massage Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before a prenatal massage session. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the client's medical history, consent, and any additional comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Client Information" page with the client's personal details.
2. Answer the questions in the "Medical History" section truthfully and completely.
3. Read and sign the "Consent" section, indicating your understanding and agreement.
4. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (<name>)** (`client_information`, text, **required**): Please enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History (<name>)** (`medical_history`, text, **required**): Share your medical history, including any previous pregnancies, allergies, and health conditions.
* **Consent (<name>)** (`consent`, text, **required**): Check the box to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms of the massage therapy session.
* **Additional Comments (<name>)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the healthcare professional, enter it here.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when answering the "Medical History" questions. This will help the healthcare professional tailor the massage session to your needs.
* Make sure to read the "Consent" section carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about any aspect of the session.
