# Rehabilitation Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rehabilitation Participation Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from a participant for their involvement in a rehabilitation program. This form is essential for ensuring that the participant understands their role, responsibilities, and potential risks involved in the program. It also serves as a record of the participant's consent, which is crucial for the healthcare provider to take necessary actions and ensure the participant's rights are respected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, date of birth, and address.
2. Provide your phone number and medical record number (if available).
3. Describe your medical condition and the expected outcome of the rehabilitation program.
4. Sign the form, date it, and provide a declaration of consent.
5. If applicable, include an emergency contact and comments from the healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: 
  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: 
  Enter your date of birth in the standard format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**:
  Enter your current address where you can be reached for any updates or concerns.
* **Phone Number**:
  Enter your phone number where we can contact you for updates or concerns.
* **Medical Record Number**:
  If you have a medical record number, enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact**:
  Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition**:
  Describe your medical condition that requires rehabilitation.
* **Rehabilitation Goal**:
  Describe the expected outcome or goal of the rehabilitation program.
* **Expected Outcome**:
  Describe what you expect to happen or achieve through the rehabilitation program.
* **Actual Outcome**:
  This field will be filled in later, once the rehabilitation program has started.
* **Date Of Consent**:
  The date when you provided your consent for the rehabilitation program.
* **Signature**:
  Sign the form to indicate your consent to participate in the rehabilitation program.
* **Date Of Signature**:
  The date when you signed the form.
* **Participant Declaration**:
  Declare that you understand the risks and benefits of the rehabilitation program and that you have read and understood the provided information.
* **Healthcare Provider Declaration**:
  Declare that you are a healthcare provider who has informed the participant of the risks and benefits of the rehabilitation program.
* **Date Of Signature Hc**:
  The date when the healthcare provider signed the form.
* **Date Of Signature Pp**:
  The date when the participant signed the form.
* **Date Of Signature Pp2**:
  Another date for the participant's signature.
* **Date Of Signature Pp3**:
  Another date for the participant's signature, not required but for record keeping.
* **Healthcare Provider Comments**:
  If you have any comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Participant Comments**:
  If you have any comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Date Of Participation**:
  The date when the participant starts the rehabilitation program.
* **Date Of End Participation**:
  The date when the participant ends the rehabilitation program.
* **Participant Status**:
  Describe your status in the rehabilitation program (e.g., active, inactive, completed).
* **Date Of Rehabilitation Start**:
  The date when the rehabilitation program started.
* **Emergency Contact**:
  The name and contact information of the person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition**:
  Describe your medical condition that requires rehabilitation.
* **Rehabilitation Goal**:
  Describe the expected outcome or goal of the rehabilitation program.
* **Expected Outcome**:
  Describe what you expect to happen or achieve through the rehabilitation program.
* **Actual Outcome**:
  This field will be filled in later, once the rehabilitation program has started.
