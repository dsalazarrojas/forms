# Breast and Cervical Cancer Screening Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information to assess a patient's eligibility for breast and cervical cancer screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with your demographic information, medical history, and preferred screening type.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name** (1, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Gender at Birth** (3, select_one, required): Select your gender as assigned at birth.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Provide your email address for appointment communications.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Home Address** (6, text, required): Enter your full address.
* **Insurance Status** (7, select_one, required): Indicate your insurance status.
* **Family History of Breast Cancer** (8, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a family history of breast cancer.
* **Family History of Cervical Cancer** (9, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a family history of cervical cancer.
* **Personal History of Cancer** (10, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a personal history of cancer.
* **Previous Mammogram** (11, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had a mammogram in the past.
* **Previous Pap Smear** (12, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had a Pap smear in the past.
* **Current Symptoms** (13, select_multiple, required): Select any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Symptom Description** (14, text, required): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **HPV Vaccination Status** (15, select_one, required): Indicate if you have received HPV vaccination.
* **Smoking History** (16, select_one, required): Indicate your smoking history.
* **Consent for Eligibility Assessment** (17, select_one, required): Consent to the assessment of your eligibility.
* **Preferred Screening Type** (18, select_one, required): Select your preferred screening type.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (19, date, required): Enter your preferred appointment date.
* **Additional Health Conditions** (20, text, required): Provide any additional health conditions you have.
* **Patient Signature** (21, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Signature Date** (22, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult a healthcare professional for guidance.
