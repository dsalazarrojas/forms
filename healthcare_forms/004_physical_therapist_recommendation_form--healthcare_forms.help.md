# Physical Therapist Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's physical therapy needs and provide essential details to their healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your name (first and last) in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field, if applicable.
3. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field, if necessary.
4. Specify your treatment goals in the "Treatment Goals" field.
5. Select the name of your referring therapist from the "Therapist Referral" dropdown menu.
6. Describe your treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
7. Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment in the "Follow Up Date" field, if applicable.
8. Record any additional notes or comments from your therapist in the "Therapist Notes" field.
9. Sign your name in the "Patient Signature" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
10. Sign your therapist's name in the "Therapist Signature" field to confirm that they have reviewed the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Info** (Optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Medical History** (Optional): Describe any relevant medical history that may impact your treatment.
* **Treatment Goals** (Optional): Describe your goals for physical therapy.
* **Therapist Referral** (Optional): Select the name of your referring therapist.
* **Treatment Plan** (Optional): Describe your treatment plan.
* **Follow Up Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Therapist Notes** (Required): Enter any additional notes or comments from your therapist.
* **Patient Signature** (Required): Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Therapist Signature** (Required): Sign your therapist's name to confirm that they have reviewed the information.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Insurance Provider** (Optional): Select your insurance provider (e.g., Medicare, Medicaid, Private Insurance).
* **Insurance Number** (Optional): Enter your insurance number.
* **Address** (Optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (Optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (Optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (Optional): Enter your zip code.
