# NonProfit Donation Consent Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NonProfit Donation Consent Template is a form used by non-profit organizations to collect donations from individuals, ensuring they consent to their information being shared with the organization for donation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide **General Info**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Choose your **Donation Amount**: Select the amount you'd like to donate, if you're donating a specific amount.
3. Specify **Frequency**: Select how often you'd like to donate, if applicable. Options include Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.
4. Choose **Causes**: Select the specific causes you'd like to support.
5. Select your **Contact Method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted, if applicable. Options include Email, Phone, and Mail.
6. Provide **Donor Info**: Select which of your information you'd like to share with the non-profit, if any. Options include Name, Address, Email, Phone.
7. Leave a **Signature**: Add any additional comments or signature if needed.
8. If you're donating in the future, select the **Date** and **Time** of your donation in the future.
9. Enter your **Email Address** for communication purposes.
10. Provide your **Phone Number** for additional communication.
11. Enter any **Additional Info**: Provide any further details regarding your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other necessary contact information.
* **Donation Amount** (`2`, `number`, required: false)
	+ If you're donating a specific amount, enter it here.
* **Frequency** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select how often you'd like to donate (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, etc.).
* **Causes** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose which causes you'd like to support.
* **Contact Method** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail).
* **Donor Info** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select which of your information you'd like to share with the non-profit (Name, Address, Email, Phone).
* **Signature** (`7`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Add any comments or signature if needed.
* **Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false)
	+ If you're donating in the future, select the date of your donation.
* **Time** (`9`, `time`, required: false)
	+ If you're donating in the future, select the time of your donation.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`11`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for additional communication.
* **Additional Info** (`12`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any further details regarding your donation.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the correct options and provide accurate information for each field.
* If any field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

This guide will help you navigate and understand the NonProfit Donation Consent Template form, ensuring you can complete it accurately and efficiently.
