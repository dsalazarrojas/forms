# physical_therapy_session_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to bill patients for physical therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Session Date** (id: 1) - This is the date of the physical therapy session being billed for.
   *   Type the date in the date format.

2. Enter the **Patient Name** (id: 2) - This is the name of the patient being billed.
   *   Type the patient's name as it appears on their records.

3. Enter the **Patient ID** (id: 3) - This is the unique identifier of the patient being billed.
   *   Type the patient's ID number as it appears on their records.

4. Enter the **Practitioner ID** (id: 4) - This is the unique identifier of the practitioner who provided the service.
   *   Type the practitioner's ID number as it appears on their records.

5. Select the **Payment Method** (id: 5) - This is the method of payment used for the session.
   *   Choose from the options: Credit, Debit, or Cash.

6. Enter the **Payment Amount** (id: 6) - This is the amount paid for the session.
   *   Type the amount paid in the currency specified by the healthcare system.

7. Select the **Payment Status** (id: 7) - This is the status of the payment for the session.
   *   Choose from the options: Paid, Pending, or Declined.

8. Enter any additional **Notes** (id: 8) - This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about the session.
   *   Type any relevant notes about the session.

9. Enter the **Email** (id: 9) - This is the contact email of the patient or practitioner.
   *   Type the email address in the correct format (username@domain.com).

10. Enter the **Phone Number** (id: 10) - This is the contact phone number of the patient or practitioner.
    *   Type the phone number in the E.123 format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).

11. Enter the **Patient Address** (id: 11) - This is the address of the patient.
    *   Type the address in the correct format (123 Main St, Anytown, ST, ZIP).

12. Enter the **Practitioner Name** (id: 12) - This is the name of the practitioner who provided the service.
    *   Type the practitioner's name as it appears on their records.
