# Consulting Client Referral Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consulting Client Referral Form Template is a tool used by consultants to refer clients to other specialists for additional care or services. This form helps gather necessary information about the client being referred and the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information about the consultant making the referral:
   * Referring Consultant Name
   * Referring Consultant Email
   * Referring Consultant Phone
2. Fill in the client's personal information:
   * Client First Name
   * Client Last Name
   * Client Date of Birth (optional)
   * Client Email
   * Client Phone
3. Fill in the client's contact information:
   * Client Address
   * Client City
   * Client State
   * Client ZIP Code
4. Select the reason for referral:
   * Specialized Consultation
   * Second Opinion
   * Additional Services Needed
   * Geographic Relocation
   * Insurance Requirements
   * Client Request
   * Other
5. Provide a detailed description of the reason for referral:
   * Referral Details
6. If applicable, list any current services or treatments the client is receiving:
   * Current Services Being Received
7. Summarize any relevant medical history:
   * Relevant Medical History
8. List any current medications:
   * Current Medications
9. Indicate the urgency level of the referral:
   * Routine
   * Semi-Urgent
   * Urgent
   * Emergency
10. If desired, specify a preferred consultant:
   * Preferred Consultant
11. Confirm that the client has consented to the referral:
   * Client Consent for Referral
12. Provide any additional information:
   * Additional Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referring Consultant Name** (`referring_consultant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the consultant making the referral.
* **Referring Consultant Email** (`referring_consultant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the consultant making the referral.
* **Referring Consultant Phone** (`referring_consultant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the consultant making the referral.
* **Referring Organization** (`referring_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization making the referral.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this referral.
* **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the client being referred.
* **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the client being referred.
* **Client Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the client being referred.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the client being referred.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter the street address of the client being referred.
* **Client City** (`client_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of residence of the client.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, text, optional): Enter the state or province of the client's residence.
* **Client ZIP Code** (`client_zip`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the client's residence.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance company if applicable.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the policy or member ID number if applicable.
* **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, select_one, required): Select the reason for referral from the provided options.
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the reason for referral.
* **Current Services Being Received** (`current_services`, text, optional): List any current services or treatments the client is receiving.
* **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Summarize any relevant medical history.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any current medications.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of the referral from the provided options.
* **Preferred Consultant** (`preferred_consultant`, text, optional): Specify a preferred consultant if desired.
* **Client Consent for Referral** (`client_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that the client has consented to the referral.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If a field is optional, be mindful of the level of detail needed to provide useful information.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language in the referral details field to ensure that the information is easily understood.
* Ensure that the client has consented to the referral before submitting the form.
* Consider including additional information or attachments that may be relevant to the referral.
