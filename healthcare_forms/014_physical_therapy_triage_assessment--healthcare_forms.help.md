# Physical Therapy Triage Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Triage Assessment form is designed to be used for assessing patients in a physical therapy setting. It collects information on the patient's medical history, condition, diagnosis, treatment plan, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the General Info field to collect any additional information about the patient that is not covered by the other fields.
3. In the Medical History field, provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
4. In the Assessment field, describe the patient's condition in detail.
5. In the Diagnosis field, provide a diagnosis or suspected diagnosis of the patient's condition.
6. In the Treatment Plan field, outline the treatment plan for the patient.
7. In the Follow Up field, schedule follow-up appointments for the patient.
8. In the Medical Team field, list the names of the medical team members involved in the patient's care.
9. In the Patient Info field, collect any additional information about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info
* **General Info (General Info, text, optional)**: This field is used to collect any additional information about the patient that is not covered by the other fields.
 
### Medical History
* **Medical History (Medical History, text, optional)**: This field is used to gather a detailed account of the patient's medical history.
 
### Assessment
* **Assessment (Assessment, text, optional)**: This field is used to describe the patient's condition in detail.
 
### Diagnosis
* **Diagnosis (Diagnosis, text, optional)**: This field is used to provide a diagnosis or suspected diagnosis of the patient's condition.
 
### Treatment Plan
* **Treatment Plan (Treatment Plan, text, optional)**: This field is used to outline the treatment plan for the patient.
 
### Follow Up
* **Follow Up (Follow Up, date, optional)**: This field is used to schedule follow-up appointments for the patient.
 
### Medical Team
* **Medical Team (Medical Team, select multiple, optional)**: This field is used to list the names of the medical team members involved in the patient's care.
 
### Patient Info
* **Patient Info (Patient Info, text, optional)**: This field is used to collect any additional information about the patient.
 
## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the patient's condition and treatment plan.
* Make sure to schedule follow-up appointments as necessary.
* List all relevant medical team members involved in the patient's care.
* Keep the form up to date and accurate.
 
This help guide is designed to assist users in understanding the purpose and usage of the Physical Therapy Triage Assessment form. By following these guidelines, medical professionals can efficiently gather the necessary information to provide quality care for their patients.
