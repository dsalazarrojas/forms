# Mydoctells - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mydoctells" form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare professionals provide accurate and personalized care. This form includes questions about daily life activities, sexual life, medical history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by asterisks) and provide as much detail as possible.
3. For select fields, choose the correct option from the list.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Daily Life Activity
* **Daily life activity** (`daily_life_activity`, text, required): Describe your daily activities, including your work or studies, social interactions, and any other relevant information.
* **Daily life activity details** (`daily_life_activity_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your daily activities, such as any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Sexual life**
* *Sexual life* (`sexual_life`, text, required): Discuss your sexual life and any concerns you may have.
* *Sexual life details* (`sexual_life_details`, text, required): Share more information about your sexual life, including any medical history or concerns.
* **Medical history**
* *Medical history* (`medical_history`, text, required): Share your medical history, including any previous illnesses or allergies.
* *Medical history details* (`medical_history_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your medical history, including any ongoing medical conditions.
* **Contact information**
* *Contact person* (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the name and title of the contact person for your medical care.
* *Contact person relation* (`contact_person_relation`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the contact person.
* *Contact information* (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number.
* *Contact information details* (`contact_info_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your contact information.
* *Contact information phone* (`contact_info_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* *Contact information email* (`contact_info_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* *Contact information address* (`contact_info_address`, text, optional): Provide your address (if you wish to share it).
* *Contact information postal code* (`contact_info_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code (if you wish to share it).
* *Contact information country* (`contact_info_country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* *Contact information city* (`contact_info_city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* *Contact information zip* (`contact_info_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code (if you wish to share it).
* **Medical Review Form**
* *Medical review form language* (`medical_review_form_language`, select_one, required): Select the language in which you would prefer to fill out the medical review form.
* *Medical review form id* (`medical_review_form_form_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the medical review form.
* *Medical review form version* (`medical_review_form_form_version`, text, required): Enter the version of the medical review form you are using.
* *Medical review form date* (`medical_review_form_form_date`, date, required): Enter the date you filled out the medical review form.
* *Medical review form time* (`medical_review_form_form_time`, time, required): Enter the time you filled out the medical review form.
* *Medical review form notes* (`medical_review_form_form_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about the medical review form.
* *Medical review form uploads* (`medical_review_form_form_uploads`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you wish to upload any files or attachments with the medical review form.
* *Medical review form comments* (`medical_review_form_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the medical review form (if you wish to share).
