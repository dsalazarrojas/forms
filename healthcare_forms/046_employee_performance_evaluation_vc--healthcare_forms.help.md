# Employee Performance Evaluation VC - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for evaluating an employee's performance across different aspects. It is intended for periodic performance reviews to assess an employee's growth and progress over a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the "Employee Performance" field, which is the main subject of this evaluation.
2.  In the "Evaluation Criteria" field, describe the specific criteria used to evaluate the employee's performance. This can include specific goals, objectives, or areas of improvement.
3.  Select the "Performance Level" from the dropdown options to indicate the employee's overall performance level, which can be "High" or "Low".
4.  Enter the "Evaluation Period" start date to specify the beginning of the evaluation period.
5.  Enter the "Evaluation Period End" date to specify the end of the evaluation period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Performance** (employee_performance_evaluation_1, text, not required): This field is for describing the employee's performance. You can enter any relevant details about their work habits, skills, or areas of improvement.
*   **Evaluation Criteria** (employee_performance_evaluation_2, text, not required): This field is for outlining the specific criteria used to evaluate the employee's performance. Be sure to include relevant details about the specific goals, objectives, or areas of improvement.
*   **Performance Level** (employee_performance_evaluation_3, select_one, not required): This dropdown field allows you to select the overall performance level of the employee, which can be either "High" or "Low".
*   **Evaluation Period** (employee_performance_evaluation_4, date, not required): Enter the start date of the evaluation period. This is when the employee's performance began being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Period End** (employee_performance_evaluation_5, date, not required): Enter the end date of the evaluation period. This is when the evaluation period ended.

## Tips
*   Use specific details and examples to support your evaluation when completing the "Employee Performance" and "Evaluation Criteria" fields.
*   Ensure the "Performance Level" accurately reflects the employee's performance level.
*   Be thorough when entering the evaluation period dates to maintain accurate records.
