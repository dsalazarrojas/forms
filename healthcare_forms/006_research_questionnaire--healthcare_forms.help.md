# Research Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and health-related information from respondents. The purpose of this form is to inform public health policy, research, and education initiatives.
## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing true and accurate information.
2.  Please select an option that best describes your condition or behavior.
3.  If a question is not relevant to you, please answer with 'N/A' (Not Applicable) or skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your age?** (Required: false)
    Age is a numerical representation of your current age.
*   **Do you exercise regularly?** (Required: false)
    Exercise is a regular physical activity or sport that you participate in for health benefits.
*   **What is your occupation?** (Required: false)
    Occupation refers to your work or professional role.
*   **Do you have any medical conditions?** (Required: false)
    Medical conditions are any health issues or illnesses that you have.
*   **What is your height?** (Required: false)
    Height is a measurement of your overall height in centimeters or meters.
*   **Have you had a medical test recently?** (Required: false)
    A medical test is any examination or evaluation done for health reasons.
*   **What is your weight?** (Required: false)
    Weight is a measurement of your overall weight in kilograms or pounds.
*   **Do you have any allergies?** (Required: false)
    Allergies are any adverse reactions to specific substances or environmental factors.
*   **What is your blood pressure?** (Required: false)
    Blood pressure is the measurement of the pressure of blood circulating inside your body.
*   **Do you have any physical disabilities?** (Required: false)
    Physical disabilities refer to any condition that limits your physical ability.
*   **When did you last exercise?** (Required: false)
    The last time you exercised is the date and time you last engaged in any form of physical activity.
*   **How long do you usually exercise for?** (Required: false)
    This question is concerned with the duration of time you usually spend exercising.
*   **Do you eat a balanced diet?** (Required: false)
    A balanced diet is one that contains all the necessary nutrients for healthy living.
*   **Do you have any chronic diseases?** (Required: false)
    Chronic diseases are health conditions that persist over time.
*   **How often do you smoke?** (Required: false)
    Smoking frequency is the regularity of your smoking habit.
*   **Do you drink alcohol?** (Required: false)
    Alcohol consumption is the frequency and amount of drinking you do.
*   **Do you take any medications?** (Required: false)
    Medications are any prescribed or over-the-counter drugs that you take for health reasons.
*   **Do you use any assistive technologies?** (Required: false)
    Assistive technologies are tools or devices that aid in your daily living activities.
*   **When do you usually go to bed?** (Required: false)
    The time you go to bed is related to your sleep patterns and schedules.
*   **For how long do you sleep at night?** (Required: false)
    This question is concerned with the length of time you usually sleep at night.
*   **Do you have any sleep disorders?** (Required: false)
    Sleep disorders are any disruptions to your sleep patterns.
*   **Do you have any mental health issues?** (Required: false)
    Mental health issues are any psychological or emotional concerns.
*   **Do you have any social support systems?** (Required: false)
    Social support systems are networks of people who help you in your life.
*   **What is the name of your health insurance provider?** (Required: false)
    A health insurance provider is your current health insurance company or organization.
*   **How many years do you usually exercise per month?** (Required: false)
    This question is concerned with the number of years you usually exercise per month.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
*   If a question is not relevant to you, please answer with 'N/A' (Not Applicable) or skip it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.
*   This form is designed to gather information for public health purposes, and your participation is greatly appreciated.
