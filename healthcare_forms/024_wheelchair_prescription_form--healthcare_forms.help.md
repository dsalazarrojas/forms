# Wheelchair Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheelchair Prescription Form is used to create a personalized prescription for a patient's wheelchair, taking into account their personal details and wheelchair specifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's Date of Birth in the **Date of Birth** field. This date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the patient's contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Enter the patient's diagnosis in the **Diagnosis** field.
5. Select the type of wheelchair needed from the **Wheelchair Type** dropdown menu (Standard, Tilt-in-space, or Tilt-in-space 2).
6. Enter the desired height and width of the wheelchair in the **Height and Width** field.
7. Select the required **Wheelchair Size** from the dropdown menu (16, 18, 20, or 22 inches).
8. Enter the weight of the wheelchair in the **Wheelchair Weight** field (optional).
9. Enter the seat depth of the wheelchair in the **Seat Depth** field (optional).
10. Select the frame type of the wheelchair from the **Frame Type** dropdown menu (Standard, Tilt-in-space, or Tilt-in-space 2).
11. Finally, enter the wheelchair model name in the **Wheelchair Model** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient for whom the prescription is being created.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number for further communication.
* **Diagnosis**: Enter the patient's diagnosis for accurate prescription.
* **Wheelchair Type**: Select the type of wheelchair needed from the dropdown menu (Standard, Tilt-in-space, or Tilt-in-space 2).
* **Height and Width**: Enter the desired height and width of the wheelchair in inches.
* **Wheelchair Size**: Select the size of the wheelchair from the dropdown menu (16, 18, 20, or 22 inches).
* **Wheelchair Weight**: (Optional) Enter the weight of the wheelchair.
* **Seat Depth**: (Optional) Enter the seat depth of the wheelchair in inches.
* **Frame Type**: Select the frame type of the wheelchair from the dropdown menu (Standard, Tilt-in-space, or Tilt-in-space 2).
* **Wheelchair Model**: Enter the name of the wheelchair model being prescribed.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the patient's record or the healthcare management system for more information.
* This form should only be completed by authorized healthcare professionals.
