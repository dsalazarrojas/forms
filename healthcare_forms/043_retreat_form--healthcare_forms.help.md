# Retreat Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retreat Form is used to collect information for planning and organizing retreats. This form is meant to gather all the necessary details about the retreat to ensure that it runs smoothly and successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Retreat Title" field with the name of the retreat.
2. Provide the "Retreat Date" in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter the "Event Time" in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Select one of the options for the "Speakers" and "Retreat Theme" fields.
5. Choose multiple options for the "Speakers Details" field.
6. Enter the "Venue", "Location", "City", and "Postal Code" fields with the relevant information.
7. Select the correct "Country" option.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
