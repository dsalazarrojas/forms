# Health History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health History Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about a patient's contact details and medical history. This information is essential for healthcare providers to make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Contact details" page.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the required fields.
3. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the "Medical history" page.
4. Provide a detailed description of your medical history in the free-text field.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact details** (`1`, `contact_details`, required): Enter your name and contact information, including your address and phone number. This information is crucial for the healthcare provider to contact you for further discussions or appointments.
* **Medical history** (`2`, `medical_history`, optional): Provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions. This information will help the healthcare provider understand your overall health situation.
* **Contact details 2** (`3`, `contact_details_2`, optional): Enter your email address. This information will help the healthcare provider contact you for further discussions or appointments.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and detailed information about your contact details and medical history.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, be sure to include them in the medical history field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, be sure to contact the healthcare provider after completing this form.
