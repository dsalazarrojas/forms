# Patient Health Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Health Report Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health status, medical history, and treatment needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
2. For each field, select the most appropriate option from the available choices or enter the required information.
3. If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.
