# ADHD Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ADHD Consultation Form is used to collect information for a consultation with a patient suspected of having Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). This form helps gather essential details about the patient, including contact information and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's **Patient full name** in the "Patient full name" field.
2. Enter the patient's **Date of birth** in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Provide the patient's **Contact email address** in the "Contact email address" field.
4. Enter the patient's **Contact phone number** in the "Contact phone number" field.
5. Describe the patient's **Primary concerns** in the "Primary concerns" field.
6. Specify the **Duration of symptoms** if known.
7. List any **Current medications** the patient is taking, including dose and frequency.
8. If known, indicate if there is a **Family history of ADHD**.
9. Summarize any **Previous evaluations or diagnoses**.
10. Enter any **Screening scale score** related to the condition.
11. Indicate **Consent for consultation and record sharing**.
12. Enter any **Clinician notes and initial impressions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient full name** (text, required): The patient's full name, including any middle names.
* **Date of birth** (date, required): The patient's date of birth, used to determine age-related assessments.
* **Contact email address** (email, required): The preferred email address for appointment notices and communication.
* **Contact phone number** (text, required): The best phone number to reach the patient or guardian.
* **Primary concerns** (text, required): A description of the main symptoms or difficulties prompting the consultation.
* **Duration of symptoms** (text, optional): How long the symptoms have been present.
* **Current medications** (text, optional): Any medications the patient is taking, including dose and frequency.
* **Family history of ADHD** (select_one, optional): Select 'True' if a family history of ADHD is known, 'False' otherwise, or 'Unknown' if not known.
* **Previous evaluations or diagnoses** (text, optional): A summary of any prior testing or diagnoses.
* **Screening scale score** (number, optional): Enter any standardized screening or rating scores.
* **Consent for consultation and record sharing** (select_one, required): Select 'Consent given' if the patient or guardian consents to the consultation and record sharing.
* **Clinician notes and initial impressions** (note, optional): Observations and recommended next steps from the clinician.
* **Clinician notes** (note, optional): Observations and recommended next steps from the clinician.
 
## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to facilitate a thorough and effective consultation. If you are unsure about any information or questions, please ask for help from a healthcare professional.
