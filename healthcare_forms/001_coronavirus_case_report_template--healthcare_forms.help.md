# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant to collect and report information about suspected or confirmed cases of coronavirus disease, to inform public health responses and prevent further transmission of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the instructions carefully and complete each field as it pertains to the specific case.
2. Use this template to guide you through the process, but adapt it if needed to suit your specific context.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. Review and submit the completed form once finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)

* **General Information**: A brief description of the case, including location and date of onset of symptoms.
* **Hint:** None

### Workplace Info (Page 2)

* **Workplace Info**: Information about the workplace or environment where the case was detected, including any relevant context.
* **Hint:** None

### Personal Info (Page 3)

* **Personal Info**: Details about the individual affected, including their contact information and any relevant medical history.
* **Hint:** None

### Symptoms (Page 4)

* **Symptoms**: A detailed description of the symptoms exhibited by the individual, including any fever, cough, or other relevant symptoms.
* **Hint:** None

### Contact (Page 5)

* **Contact**: Information about how the case was detected, including any relevant contact tracing information.
* **Hint:** None

### Workplace Safety Precautions (Page 6)

* **Workplace Safety Precautions**: Any safety measures or protocols implemented in the workplace to mitigate the spread of the disease.
* **Hint:** None

### Community Safety Precautions (Page 7)

* **Community Safety Precautions**: Any safety measures or protocols implemented in the community to mitigate the spread of the disease.
* **Hint:** None

### Healthcare Provider Info (Page 8)

* **Healthcare Provider Info**: Information about the healthcare provider or facility involved in the case.
* **Hint:** None

### Contact Tracing (Page 9)

* **Contact Tracing**: Any relevant contact tracing information, including dates and times of exposure.
* **Hint:** None

### Exposure (Page 10)

* **Exposure**: Any known or suspected sources of exposure to the disease.
* **Hint:** None
