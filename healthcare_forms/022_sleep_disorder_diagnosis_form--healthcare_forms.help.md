# Sleep Disorder Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's sleep patterns, habits, and experiences. It will help healthcare professionals diagnose potential sleep disorders and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options for select fields.
* If a field does not apply to you, select "None" or "Not at all".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Routine** (`morning_routine`, text, optional): Describe your usual morning routine, including what time you wake up and any activities you engage in.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the times of day when you experience sleep or drowsiness.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Night
* **Quality of Sleep** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of your sleep, where:
	+ Very good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Terrible
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, number, optional): Enter the average number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Wake Time** (`wake_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you usually wake up.
* **Bedtime** (`bedtime`, time, optional): Enter the time you go to bed.
* **Do you feel sleepy?** (`sleepiness`, select_one, optional): Rate how much you feel sleepy:
	+ Not at all
	+ Somewhat
	+ Very much
	+ Extremely much
* **Do you snore?** (`snore`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you snore:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Sleep turbulence** (`sleep_turbulence`, note, optional): Provide any notes about your sleep experiences, such as frequency of sleep disruptions.
* **Medication** (`medication`, select_one, optional): Select if you take any medication for sleep:
	+ None
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ At night
* **Medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions that may be related to your sleep:
	+ Insomnia
	+ Sleep disorder
	+ Narcolepsy
	+ Sleep apnea
	+ Other
* **Medical treatments** (`medical_treatments`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you receive any medical treatments for sleep:
	+ True
	+ None

Note: If you have any further questions or concerns, please consult with a healthcare professional. This form is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
