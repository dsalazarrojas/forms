# medical_abstract_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit medical abstracts and presentation equipment needs for review and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the contact information section with your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the personal information section, provide your first and last name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
3. If you are submitting an abstract, provide the title, description, and any relevant equipment needs in the presentation and equipment necessities section.
4. Select up to 6 featured abstract topics that best describe your submission.
5. Provide information about your institution, department, and address if applicable.
6. Describe your study design, type, population, and methodology, including inclusion and exclusion criteria, data collection methods, and data analysis.
7. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you about your submission.
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, number, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional personal details not covered in the contact information section.
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Presentation and Equipment Necessities** (`Presentation and Equipment Necessities`, text, required/optional): Describe the presentation and equipment needs associated with your submission.
- **Equipment** (`Equipment`, text, required/optional): Describe the specific equipment needed for your presentation.
- **Goals** (`Goals`, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your submission.
- **Presentation Title** (`Presentation Title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title that reflects the main theme of your presentation.
- **Presentation Description** (`Presentation Description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your presentation.
- **Featured Abstract Topics** (`Featured Abstract Topics`, select multiple, required/optional): Select up to 6 topics that best describe your submission.
- **Institution** (`Institution`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your institution.
- **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your institution.
- **Department** (`Department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department name of your institution.
- **Study Design** (`Study Design`, text, required/optional): Describe the design of your study.
- **Type** (`Type`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of study.
- **Population** (`Population`, number, required/optional): Enter the population size.
- **Methodology** (`Methodology`, text, required/optional): Describe your methodology.
- **Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria** (`Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria`, text, required/optional): Describe your inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- **Data Collection** (`Data Collection`, text, required/optional): Describe your data collection methods.
- **Data Analysis** (`Data Analysis`, text, required/optional): Describe your data analysis methods.

Note that some fields may be optional, but it is still important to fill out any required fields to ensure that your submission is complete. Review each field carefully and only enter information that is accurate and relevant to your submission.
