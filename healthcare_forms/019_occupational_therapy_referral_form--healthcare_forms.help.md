# occupational_therapy_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The occupational therapy referral form is designed to gather essential information from healthcare providers and patients to facilitate the referral process. This form helps in collecting relevant details about the patient's medical history, therapy goals, and other necessary information for a successful referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
2. Describe the referral reason and goals for the occupational therapy treatment.
3. Enter medical information, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
4. Specify the therapy goals and objectives for the patient.
5. Provide the patient's contact information and healthcare provider details.
6. Select the assigned tool or equipment for the therapy.
7. Record the submission date and time.
8. Indicate the submitted by healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (1, `patient_info`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
* **Referral Reason** (2, `referral_reason`, required: false): Describe the reason for the referral, including any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medical Info** (3, `medical_info`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical information, including conditions, treatments, or medications.
* **Therapy Goals** (4, `therapy_goals`, required: false): Specify the therapy goals and objectives for the patient.
* **Patient History** (5, `patient_history`, required: false): Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.
* **Treatment Plan** (6, `treatment_plan`, required: false): Outline the treatment plan for the patient.
* **Healthcare Provider** (7, `healthcare_provider`, required: false): Enter the healthcare provider's name and contact details.
* **Contact Info** (8, `contact_info`, required: false): Provide the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Assigned Tool** (9, `assigned_tool`, required: false): Select the assigned tool or equipment for the therapy from the options provided.
* **Submission Date** (10, `submission_date`, required: false): Record the date of submission for the referral.
* **Submission Time** (11, `submission_time`, required: false): Record the time of submission for the referral.
* **Submitted By** (12, `submitted_by`, required: false): Indicate the healthcare provider who submitted the referral.
* **Submission Status** (13, `submission_status`, required: false): Select the status of the submission from the options provided.
