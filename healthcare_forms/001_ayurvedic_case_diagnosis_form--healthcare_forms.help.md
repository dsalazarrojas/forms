# Ayurvedic Case Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ayurvedic Case Diagnosis Form is a tool used by healthcare practitioners to gather information about patients for an effective diagnosis and treatment plan. This form helps practitioners to understand the patient's medical history, lifestyle, and chief complaint, which enables them to provide personalized recommendations for Ayurvedic therapies, detox, diet, and herbal remedies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in the patient's full legal name as shown on their ID.
* Enter the patient's date of birth.
* If applicable, provide the patient's contact phone number and email address for appointment confirmations.
* Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for visiting the practitioner.
* Provide any relevant medical history, past diagnoses, surgeries, medications, and allergies.
* List the patient's current medications and supplements, including dose and frequency.
* Indicate the patient's lifestyle habits, such as diet, sleep, exercise, tobacco use, and alcohol use.
* Describe the practitioner's recommended treatment plan, including suggested therapies, detox, diet, and herbal remedies, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient full name** (`patient_name`, text, required): The full name of the patient as shown on their ID.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): The patient's birth date.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): The patient's phone number, including country code if outside local area. (Optional)
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): The patient's email address, for appointment confirmations and further communication. (Optional)
* **Chief complaint or presenting concern** (`chief_complaint`, text, required): A brief description of the patient's symptoms or reason for visiting the practitioner.
* **Pulse and dosha observations** (`pulse_and_dosha_notes`, note, optional): Practitioner's notes on pulse and dosha assessment.
* **Relevant medical history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): The patient's past diagnoses, surgeries, medications, and allergies.
* **Current medications and supplements** (`current_medications`, text, optional): A list of the patient's current medications and supplements, including dose and frequency.
* **Lifestyle habits** (`lifestyle_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the following:
	+ Diet
	+ Sleep
	+ Exercise
	+ Tobacco use
	+ Alcohol use
* **Recommended treatment plan** (`practitioner_plan`, text, optional): The practitioner's suggested therapies, detox, diet, and herbal remedies for the patient.
