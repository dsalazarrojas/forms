# NDIS Support Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to be used by participants to document their support goals and outcomes, current support, and the review and approval process for their NDIS support plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the participant's information, including their goals and outcomes.
2. Describe the current support the participant is receiving.
3. Review and approve each section of the form, as relevant.
4. Return the completed form to the relevant parties for further processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (page 1)** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Goals (page 2)** (`support_plan_goals`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the goals and outcomes that the participant is aiming to achieve as part of their NDIS support plan.
* **Current Support (page 3)** (`current_support`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the current support the participant is receiving, including any services or programs they are involved in.
* **Participant Details (page 4)** (`participant_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the participant, as required.
* **Goals and Outcomes (page 5)** (`goals_and_outcomes`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how the participant's goals and outcomes will be achieved.
* **Support Plan (page 6)** (`support_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the support plan in place for the participant, including any services or programs they are involved in.
* **Review and Approval (page 7-12)** (`review_and_approval`, `text`, required/optional): Review and approve each section of the form, as relevant. This may involve signing off on the participant's goals and outcomes, current support, and support plan.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and approve each section of the form carefully to ensure it accurately reflects the participant's needs and circumstances.
* Enter any additional information that is relevant to the participant's support plan.
* Return the completed form to the relevant parties for further processing.
