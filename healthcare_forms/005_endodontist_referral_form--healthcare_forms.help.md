# endodontist_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about patients referred to an endodontist for specialized care. This form gathers essential details about the patient's medical history, insurance information, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the patient's information section (fields 1-5) with the patient's personal details.
2. Enter the patient's contact information (fields 6 and 7).
3. Specify the type of endodontic care the patient needs (fields 8-10).
4. Provide the patient's insurance information (fields 11 and 12).
5. Select the relevant endodontic procedure codes (fields 13 and 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient info** (patient_info, text, required: false): Enter the patient's general information, such as their name, address, and any relevant medical history.
* **patient name** (patient_name, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **patient address** (patient_address, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full address.
* **patient contact info** (contact_info, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **patient prosthesis** (patient_prosthetic, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the patient's prosthetic devices or implants.
* **referring dentist** (referring_dentist, text, required: false): Enter the name of the referring dentist.
* **referring dentist contact info** (referring_dentist_contact_info, text, required: false): Enter the referring dentist's contact information.
* **endodontic care needed** (endodontic_care_needed, text, required: false): Specify the type of endodontic care the patient needs.
* **endodontic care reason** (endodontic_care_reason, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the referral.
* **endodontic care recommendation** (endodontic_care_recommendation, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or recommendations from the referring dentist.
* **patient insurance** (patient_insurance, text, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance information.
* **patient insurance id** (patient_insurance_id, text, required: false): Enter the patient's insurance ID number.
* **endodontic procedure code** (endodontic_procedure_code, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant endodontic procedure code from the options provided.
* **endodontic procedure code 2** (endodontic_procedure_code_2, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple endodontic procedure codes from the options provided.
* **patient pittsburgh id** (patient_prosht_id, text, required: false): Enter the patient's Pittsburgh ID number.

Note: This form is not intended to be used for actual medical purposes, but rather as an example of a form generated by an AI.
