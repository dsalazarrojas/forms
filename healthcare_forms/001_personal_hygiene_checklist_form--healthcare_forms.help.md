# Personal Hygiene Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you track and evaluate your personal hygiene routines. It is a simple and straightforward form that guides you through three sections: Morning Routine, Daily Hygiene, and Additional Hygiene Routines. By completing this form, you can identify areas where you may need improvement and develop healthy habits for better personal hygiene.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form sections: Morning Routine, Daily Hygiene, and Additional Hygiene Routines.
2.  For each section, answer the questions or select options that best describe your personal hygiene practices.
3.  Be honest and thorough when providing information.
4.  You can skip sections that do not apply to you or your situation.
5.  Submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Hygiene Checklist Form** (`text`, required/optional): This section has no label, but it is the form's title and does not contain a field to fill in. Please ignore this section.
* **Morning Routine** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): This section is about your morning routine, including activities you do every day in the morning.
	+ Answer with details about your morning activities, such as brushing your teeth, taking a shower, or enjoying a cup of coffee.
* **Daily Hygiene** (`section_one`, text, required/optional): This section is about your daily hygiene practices, including activities you do regularly throughout the day.
	+ Answer with details about your daily activities, such as washing your hands, using deodorant, or washing your clothes.
* **Additional Hygiene Routines** (`section_three`, text, required/optional): This section is for any additional hygiene practices you have that are not covered in the previous sections.
	+ Answer with details about any unique or specialized hygiene activities you do.
**Tips**

* Be honest and detailed in your responses.
* If you have any medical conditions that affect your personal hygiene, please consult with your doctor or healthcare provider before filling out this form.
* Remember, this form is for your personal use and to help you maintain good hygiene practices.
