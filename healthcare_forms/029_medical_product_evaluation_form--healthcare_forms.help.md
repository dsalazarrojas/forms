# medical_product_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and evaluation of medical products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the medical product you are evaluating.
2. Provide a brief description of the product.
3. Rate the product's effectiveness on a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Select the medical conditions that this product is intended to treat.
5. Enter a score for your overall evaluation of the product.
6. Enter the date and time you evaluated the product.
7. Enter the name and title of the medical specialist who recommended or prescribed the product.
8. Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the product.
9. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
10. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the medical product you are evaluating.
* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product Rating**: Rate the product's effectiveness on a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rating Scale**: Select the medical conditions that this product is intended to treat.
* **Medical Condition**: Select all relevant medical conditions that this product is intended to treat. 
  - This field is meant to be filled out with multiple selections if the product treats multiple conditions.
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter a score for your overall evaluation of the product.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date you evaluated the product.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time you evaluated the product.
* **Medical Specialist**: Enter the name and title of the medical specialist who recommended or prescribed the product.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the product.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.

Note: This form has been designed to be easy to use and understand. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
