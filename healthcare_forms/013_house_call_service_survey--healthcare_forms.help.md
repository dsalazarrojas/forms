# house_call_service_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and feedback from patients during house calls. It is designed for healthcare providers to gather clinical and non-clinical data to better understand patient needs and provide quality care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the patient's information in the **patient_info** field.
2. Provide the patient's medical history in the **medical_history** field.
3. Enter the date of the visit in the **date_of_visit** field.
4. For questions 1, 2, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 16, select the most applicable option from the list.
5. For questions 3, 5, 9, 13, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, and 22, type in your answer in the text field.
6. For questions 7 and 14, provide notes about the patient's condition.
7. Finally, submit the form to ensure that your feedback is submitted correctly.
