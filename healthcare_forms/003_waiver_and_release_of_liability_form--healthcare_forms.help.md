# Waiver And Release Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver And Release Of Liability Form is used to obtain legal releases and agreements from participants to ensure they understand and acknowledge their responsibilities and liabilities related to activities, events, or services provided by the organization. This form helps to protect both the participant and the organization by outlining the terms and conditions of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the participant's information, including their name, contact details (email and phone number), and address.
2. Specify the activity or event information, including the dates, times, and locations involved.
3. Include the participant's agreement and signature to acknowledge their understanding of the terms and conditions.
4. Sign the waiver and release sections to confirm the participant's release of liability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide the participant's name, age, and any other relevant information.
- **Participant Contact** (`Participant Contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's email and phone number.
- **Activity Information** (`Activity Information`, `text`, required: false): Specify the activity or event name, date, time, and location.
- **Waiver** (`Waiver`, `text`, required: false): Write a clear and specific waiver statement that includes all necessary details.
- **Release** (`Release`, `text`, required: false): Write a clear and specific release statement that includes all necessary details.
- **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign the waiver and release sections to confirm the participant's understanding and agreement.
- **Witness Name** (`Witness Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the witness's name if a witness is present.
- **Witness Signature** (`Witness Signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign the witness's name to confirm their presence.
- **Witness Title** (`Witness Title`, `text`, required: false): Specify the witness's title or position.
- **Witness Phone** (`Witness Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the witness's phone number.
- **Participant Agreement** (`Participant Agreement`, `text`, required: false): Write a clear and specific agreement statement that includes all necessary details.
- **Participant Agreement Date** (`Participant Agreement Date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date the participant agrees to the terms and conditions.
- **Participant Agreement Time** (`Participant Agreement Time`, `time`, required: false): Specify the time the participant agrees to the terms and conditions.
- **Participant Agreement Location** (`Participant Agreement Location`, `text`, required: false): Specify the location where the participant agrees to the terms and conditions.
- **Participant Agreement Notes** (`Participant Agreement Notes`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments from the participant.
- **Waiver Date** (`Waiver Date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date the waiver is effective.
- **Waiver Time** (`Waiver Time`, `time`, required: false): Specify the time the waiver is effective.
- **Release Date** (`Release Date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date the release is effective.
- **Release Time** (`Release Time`, `time`, required: false): Specify the time the release is effective.
- **Release Agreement** (`Release Agreement`, `text`, required: false): Write a clear and specific release statement that includes all necessary details.

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields and sign all sections that require signatures. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and clarity.
