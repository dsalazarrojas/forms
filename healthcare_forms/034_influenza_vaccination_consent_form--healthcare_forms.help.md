# influenza_vaccination_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information and obtain consent for influenza vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Patient Information

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, and email.
### 2. Medical Conditions

* **Has the patient got any of the following medical conditions**: Check the box if the patient has any of the listed medical conditions.
### 3. Flu Vaccination

* **Flu Vaccination**: Select the type of flu vaccination the patient is receiving.
### 4. Emergency Contact Information

* **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
### 5. Patient Consent

* **Patient Consent**: Sign the form to indicate that the patient has given their consent for the vaccination.
### 6. Physician Signature

* **Physician Signature**: Sign the form as a physician to confirm that the form has been completed accurately.
### 7. Date and Time

* **Date**: Enter the date of the vaccination.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the vaccination.
### 8. Physician Contact Information

* **Physician Name**: Enter the physician's name.
* **Physician Title**: Enter the physician's title.
* **Physician Office**: Enter the physician's office information.
### 9. Patient Information (continued)

* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **City**: Enter the patient's city.
* **State**: Enter the patient's state.
* **Country**: Enter the patient's country.
* **Zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
### 10. Additional Information

* **Medical Supplies**: Enter any additional medical supplies the patient may need.
* **Vaccination Site**: Enter the site where the vaccination will take place.
* **Flu Type**: Enter the type of flu vaccine the patient will receive.
* **Other Allergies**: Enter any other allergies the patient may have.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* Sign the form as a physician to confirm that the form has been completed accurately.
* Keep the form for record-keeping purposes.
