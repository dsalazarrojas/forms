# Pre Exercise Health Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your exercise habits and medical history to ensure your safety and well-being during physical activity. Please answer the questions honestly to help your healthcare provider assess your risks and provide guidance on safe exercise routines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the frequency of your exercise routine from the options provided.
2. Choose the types of exercise you regularly engage in from the list.
3. Indicate the intensity of your exercise routine on a scale of 1-10.
4. Select the duration of your exercise sessions from the options.
5. Answer the question about past injuries, selecting all that apply.
6. Report any past surgeries, selecting all that apply.
7. Identify any medical conditions you have, selecting all that apply.
8. Disclose your family medical history, selecting all that apply.
9. Answer the question about your medical history, selecting all that apply.
10. Indicate how often you visit the doctor.
11. Report any medical tests you have undergone, selecting all that apply.
12. Answer the question about abnormal medical test results, selecting all that apply.
13. Report any medical test results, selecting all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you exercise?** (`exercise_frequency`): This question helps us understand your regular exercise routine. Please select the frequency that best describes your exercise habits.
* **What type of exercise do you do?** (`exercise_type`): This question is about the types of physical activities you engage in. Choose all that apply from the list.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how intense is your exercise routine?** (`exercise_intensity`): Indicate the intensity of your exercise sessions, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **How long do you exercise?** (`exercise_duration`): This question helps us understand the duration of your exercise sessions.
* **Have you ever had a past injury?** (`past_injuries`): Please select all that apply if you have experienced any past injuries.
* **Have you ever had a surgery?** (`past_surgeries`): Choose all that apply if you have undergone any surgeries.
* **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`medical_conditions`): Select all that apply if you have any medical conditions.
* **Family medical history?** (`family_history`): Disclose any medical history from your family members, selecting all that apply.
* **Have you had medical history?** (`medical_history`): Answer this question by selecting all that apply.
* **How often do you visit the doctor?** (`doctor_visits`): Indicate how frequently you visit the doctor for check-ups.
* **Have you had medical tests?** (`medical_tests`): Choose all that apply if you have undergone medical tests.
* **Have you ever had abnormal medical test results?** (`medical_test_results`): Select all that apply if you have received any abnormal test results.
* **Medical Test Results 2** (`medical_test_results_2`): Report any additional medical test results, selecting all that apply.
