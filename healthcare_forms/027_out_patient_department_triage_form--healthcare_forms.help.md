# Out Patient Department Triage Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Out Patient Department Triage Form is used to gather essential information from patients in the out-patient department for triage and assessment. This form helps healthcare professionals quickly assess the patient's medical condition, determine the urgency of their situation, and provide appropriate treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand each field.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Enter your information in the relevant fields.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please consult a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: This field is used to capture the patient's personal details, including their name, contact information, and next of kin's details.
* **Medical History (2)**: Check the box if the patient has any medical history, including chronic conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries. Select all applicable options.
* **Chief Complaint (3)**: Describe the primary reason for the patient's visit. Be as specific as possible.
* **Triage Information (4)**: Enter any relevant triage information, such as the patient's vital signs or any other relevant medical details.
* **Allergies (5)**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, including medications or substances.
* **Medications (6)**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Condition (7)**: Describe the patient's current medical condition, including symptoms or concerns.
* **Vital Signs (8)**: Enter the patient's vital signs, such as temperature, pulse, blood pressure, etc.
* **Diagnosis (9)**: This field is not applicable for this form. You can skip it.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and truthful as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult a healthcare professional.
* If you need help or have any concerns, please do not hesitate to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
