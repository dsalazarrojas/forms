# Guided Meditation Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guided Meditation Intake Form is designed to gather information from clients about their meditation goals, needs, and objectives. This form will help the meditation coach understand the client's preferences and requirements for an effective meditation session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2.  Select the options that best fit your needs and goals.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4.  If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Additional Session Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, `text`, `false`): Describe your personal goals that you would like to achieve through meditation.
* **Client Needs** (`client_needs`, `text`, `false`): Share any specific needs or concerns you have that the meditation coach should be aware of.
* **Meditation Coach Name** (`meditation_coach_name`, `text`, `false`): If you have a preferred meditation coach, please provide their name.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, `false`): Select the length of the meditation session you prefer (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose how often you would like to have a meditation session (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Meditation Focus** (`meditation_focus`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the areas where you would like to focus on during meditation (e.g., Mindfulness, Self-Compassion, Stress Reduction).
* **Meditation Goal Type** (`meditation_goal_type`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose the type of meditation goals you have (e.g., Goal Achievement, Self-Discovery, Stress Management).
* **Client Meditation Goals and Objectives** (`client_meditation_goals_and_objectives`, `text`, `false`): Describe your meditation goals and objectives in more detail.
* **Preferred Meditation Method** (`preferred_meditation_method`, `select_one`, `false`): Select your preferred meditation method (e.g., Guided Meditation, Mindfulness, Body Scan).
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Choose the topic you would like to focus on during the meditation session (e.g., Anxiety, Depression, Focus, Relationship Issues, Self-Esteem).
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_one`, `false`): Select how you would like to communicate with the meditation coach (e.g., Text-Based, Phone-Based, In-Person).
* **Client Meditation Goals and Objectives Note** (`client_meditation_goals_and_objectives_note`, `text`, `false`): Add any additional notes or comments about your meditation goals and objectives.
* **Client Meditation Goals and Objectives Description** (`client_meditation_goals_and_objectives_description`, `text`, `false`): Provide a detailed description of your meditation goals and objectives.
* **Meditation Coach Experience** (`meditation_coach_experience`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the level of meditation coach experience you would like (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Meditation Coach Certification** (`meditation_coach_certification`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose the meditation coach certification (e.g., Certified, Non-Certified).
* **Meditation Coach Qualifications** (`meditation_coach_qualifications`, `text`, `false`): List any relevant qualifications or skills the meditation coach should have.
* **Additional Session Notes** (`additional_session_notes`, `text`, `false`): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.

Note: The fields marked as `false` are not required, but please fill them out if applicable.
