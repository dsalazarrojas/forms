# CAHPS Clinician & Group Survey 3.0 (Child) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CAHPS Clinician & Group Survey 3.0 (Child) is a form designed to collect feedback from parents/guardians about their child's experience in a pediatric clinic. This feedback will help improve the quality of care provided by the clinic and its staff.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read each question carefully and answer them based on your recent visit to the clinic.
- For questions like "How often did the clinician...?", choose the answer that best reflects your experience.
- If a question does not apply to your situation, please select "Not Applicable" for the answer.
- You can add any additional feedback in the "Any additional feedback about the pediatric clinic?" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name of the Pediatrician or Clinician**: Enter the name of the pediatrician or clinician your child visited.
- **Pediatric Practice Name**: Enter the name of the clinic where your child received care.
- **How often did you get an appointment for your child as soon as you needed?**: This question asks about the timeliness of appointments. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **When your child needed care right away, how often did you get it?**: This question is similar to the previous one, but it asks about the immediacy of care. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **How often did the clinician explain things about your child's health in a way you understood?**: This question assesses the clinician's communication skills. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **How often did the clinician explain things in a way that was easy for your child to understand?**: This question is also about the clinician's communication skills, but focused on the child's understanding. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", "Always", or "Not Applicable".
- **How often did the clinician listen carefully to you as the parent/guardian?**: This question asks about the clinician's listening skills. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **How often did the clinician treat your child with courtesy and respect?**: This question is about the clinician's attitude and behavior. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **How often did the clinician seem informed about care your child received from specialists?**: This question checks the clinician's awareness of the child's specialist care. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", "Always", or "No specialists seen".
- **How well did the clinician help your child feel comfortable during the examination?**: This question is about the child's comfort level during the examination. Select one of the options: "Poor", "Fair", "Good", "Very Good", or "Excellent".
- **How often did office staff treat you and your child with respect?**: This question asks about the staff's attitude and behavior. Select one of the options: "Never", "Sometimes", "Usually", or "Always".
- **Overall rating of this pediatrician/clinician**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the clinician. Select a number from 0 (worst) to 10 (best).
- **Would you recommend this clinician to other parents?**: Share your recommendation. Select one of the options: "Definitely No", "Probably No", "Probably Yes", or "Definitely Yes".
- **Any additional feedback about the pediatric clinic?**: You can add any comments or suggestions you have about the clinic.
