# radiology_report_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "radiology_report_access_form" is a tool used to access, view, edit, or delete radiology reports in a healthcare setting.


## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field if you have access rights.
2. If you know the patient's date of birth, enter it in the "Date Of Birth" field for more accurate search results.
3. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
4. Select the type of report you wish to access from the "Report Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date the report was generated in the "Report Date" field.
6. Choose the status of the report you wish to access from the "Report Status" dropdown menu.
7. Select whether you wish to view, edit, or delete the report in the "Report Access" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the name of the physician who generated or is associated with the report in the "Physician Name" field.
9. Enter the name of the hospital where the report was generated in the "Hospital Name" field.
10. Enter the department where the report was generated in the "Department" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient to whom the report belongs. 
* **Date Of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth for more accurate search results. 
* **Medical Record Number** (text, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number if you have access to it. 
* **Report Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of radiology report you wish to access, such as "Radiology Report". 
* **Report Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the report was generated. 
* **Report Status** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the report, such as "Available", "Pending", or "Not Available". 
* **Report Access** (select_one, optional): Select whether you wish to "View", "Edit", or "Delete" the report. 
* **Physician Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the physician who generated or is associated with the report. 
* **Hospital Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the report was generated. 
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter the department where the report was generated.
