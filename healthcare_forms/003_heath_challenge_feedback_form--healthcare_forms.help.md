# Heath Challenge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heath Challenge Feedback Form is a collection of questions to gather insights from participants who have undergone the challenge. This information will help the team to identify areas of improvement and make adjustments for future challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the challenge you participated in.
3. Provide your feedback about the challenge in the "Feedback" field.
4. Enter the start date of the challenge you participated in.
5. Enter the end date of the challenge you participated in.
6. Enter your email address (if available) in the "Participant Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number (if available) in the "Participant Phone" field.
8. Enter the website of the challenge (if available) in the "Challenge Website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): This is the field to enter your name, which will help us to identify you as a participant.
* **Challenge Name** (Challenge Name, text, required): Enter the name of the challenge you participated in.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, note, required): Describe your experience and provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Challenge Start Date** (Challenge Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the challenge.
* **Challenge End Date** (Challenge End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the challenge.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, email, optional): If you have an email address, enter it here.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Challenge Website** (Challenge Website, text, optional): If you know the website of the challenge, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.
* If you are unable to find the website of the challenge, you can leave the "Challenge Website" field empty.
* If you are unable to find your email or phone number, you can also leave the respective fields empty.
