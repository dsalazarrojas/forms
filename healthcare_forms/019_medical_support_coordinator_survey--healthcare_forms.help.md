# Medical Support Coordinator Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and feedback from patients after medical visits, helping medical institutions and professionals evaluate and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Select the date of your visit in the "date of visit" field.
4. Enter the time of your visit in the "time of visit" field.
5. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the "provider name" field.
6. Rate your overall experience with the provider using the "rating score" field.
7. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, first name, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (text, last name, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of visit** (date, date of visit, required): Select the date you visited your healthcare provider.
* **Time of visit** (time, time of visit, required): Select the time you visited your healthcare provider.
* **Provider name** (text, provider name, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Rating score** (number, rating score, required): Rate your overall experience with your healthcare provider using a number from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (text, feedback, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with your healthcare provider.
