# Congress Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of congresses or events. Your input will help event organizers understand what they did well and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. You can save your progress and come back to the form later, or submit it when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1

* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, text, Optional): Enter your name for the purpose of this feedback, if you wish.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, Optional): Provide your email address for follow-up if needed.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, Optional): Enter your institution or company name.
* **Profession** (`Profession`, select_one, True): Choose your profession from the given options.
* **Congress or Event Name** (`Congress or Event Name`, text, True): Enter the name of the congress or event you attended.
* **Congress Date** (`Congress Date`, date, True): Enter the date of the event.

### Page 2

* **Attendance Type** (`Attendance Type`, select_one, True): Choose how you attended the event.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, True): Rate your overall experience.
* **Quality of Scientific Content** (`Quality of Scientific Content`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of presented content.
* **Quality of Speakers** (`Quality of Speakers`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of presenters and faculty.
* **Relevance to Your Practice** (`Relevance to Your Practice`, select_one, True): Rate how applicable the content was to your work.
* **Favorite Sessions or Presentations** (`Favorite Sessions or Presentations`, text, Optional): Mention which sessions or presentations you enjoyed most.

### Page 3

* **Quality of Venue** (`Quality of Venue`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of the venue.
* **Organization and Logistics** (`Organization and Logistics`, select_one, True): Rate the event organization.
* **Registration Process** (`Registration Process`, select_one, True): Rate how smooth the registration process was.
* **Networking Opportunities** (`Networking Opportunities`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of networking chances.
* **Exhibition or Poster Quality** (`Exhibition or Poster Quality`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of exhibits or posters.
* **Quality of Conference Materials** (`Quality of Conference Materials`, select_one, True): Rate the quality of handouts and resources.
* **Value for Registration Fee** (`Value for Registration Fee`, select_one, True): Rate if the event was worth the cost.
* **Would You Recommend This Congress** (`Would You Recommend This Congress`, select_one, True): Rate the likelihood of recommending the event.
* **Would You Attend Again** (`Would You Attend Again`, select_one, True): Rate your willingness to attend future events.
* **Topics for Future Congresses** (`Topics for Future Congresses`, text, Optional): Suggest topics for future congresses.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`Suggested Improvements`, text, Optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, Optional): Add any other feedback or comments.

## Tips

* Be honest in your feedback, as it will help event organizers improve the congress.
* You can save your progress and return later, or submit the form when finished.
* The form will not be shared or used for any purpose other than improvement, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
