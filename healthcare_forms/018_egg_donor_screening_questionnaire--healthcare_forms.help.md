# Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire is designed to gather essential medical, family, and contact information from individuals who want to become egg donors. This information helps ensure the health and well-being of both the donor and the recipients of the donated eggs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the medical history questions (Pages 1-5) by providing a brief description of any medical conditions or allergies you have.
2. Select your health insurance type (Page 6) and any medical testing you have undergone recently (Page 7).
3. Provide emergency contact information (Page 8).
4. Answer any additional medical history questions (Pages 9-12).
5. Select your preferred contact method (Page 13) and provide any additional contact information (Page 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions or allergies you have, including any treatments or medications you are currently taking.
* **Family Medical History** (`family_history`, text, required): Describe any family medical history, including any conditions that may be relevant to egg donation.
* **Personal History and Habits** (`personal_history`, text, required): Describe your personal habits, lifestyle, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Allergies and Medications** (`medical_history_2`, text, required): Describe any allergies or medications you are currently taking.
* **Recent Medical Testing** (`medical_testing`, select_one, required): Select whether you have undergone recent medical testing (True) or not (False).
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select_multiple, required): Select your health insurance type (Private, Government, Self-Pay).
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Previous Surgical History** (`medical_history_3`, text, required): Describe any previous surgical history, including any complications or concerns.
* **Family History 2** (`family_history_2`, text, required): Describe any family medical history, including any conditions that may be relevant to egg donation.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Chronic Conditions** (`medical_history_4`, select_multiple, required): Select any chronic conditions you have (Hypertension, Diabetes, Heart Disease, Lung Disease).
* **Medical History 5** (`medical_history_5`, text, required): Describe any other medical history or concerns.
* **Family History 3** (`family_history_3`, text, required): Describe any family medical history, including any conditions that may be relevant to egg donation.
* **Additional Contact Information** (`contact_info_2`, text, required): Provide any additional contact information.
* **Medical History 6** (`medical_history_6`, text, required): Describe any other medical history or concerns.
* **Family History 4** (`family_history_4`, text, required): Describe any family medical history, including any conditions that may be relevant to egg donation.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_info_3`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Medical History 7** (`medical_history_7`, text, required): Describe any other medical history or concerns.

This help guide is designed to be concise and easy to understand, with clear explanations of each field. By following these steps and providing accurate information, you will be able to complete the Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire successfully.
