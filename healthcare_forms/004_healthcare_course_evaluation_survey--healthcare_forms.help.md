# Healthcare Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from course attendees on their experiences with the course, helping to identify areas of improvement and assess the overall effectiveness of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and in the space provided.
2. Use the provided options for selection-based questions, such as "How would you rate this course?".
3. For open-ended questions, such as "Additional comments", provide as much detail as possible.
4. Fill in the contact information to enable us to get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall satisfaction with the course?** (`course_overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the course, using the numerical value.
* **Were the course materials helpful?** (`course_materials`, text, optional): Describe how helpful you found the course materials.
* **How would you rate the course format?** (`course_format`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options: Very Engaging, Somewhat Engaging, Not Engaging, Not Sure.
* **Were the course instructions clear?** (`course_instructions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply: True, False.
* **How many weeks would you recommend the course be?** (`course_length`, number, optional): Provide the number of weeks you think the course should be.
* **Rate this course** (`course_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Additional comments** (`course_comments`, note, optional): Write any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, email, optional): Enter your name for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Institution** (`contact_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your institution for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter your department name for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Role** (`contact_role`, text, optional): Enter your role or position for follow-up communication.
* **Course Title** (`course_attended`, text, optional): Enter the title of the course you attended for follow-up communication.
