# Food Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Allergy Form is a tool used to collect information from patients about their food allergies, helping healthcare professionals provide personalized care and ensure patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the food allergen that best describes the patient's allergy.
2. List any other foods that trigger an allergic reaction.
3. Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient due to the allergy.
4. Add any relevant medical notes or information.
5. Provide the patient's medical provider's contact information.
6. Establish the relationship with the patient's medical provider (e.g., doctor, nurse).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **food-allergy-form** (`Peanut`, `Tree nut`, `Fish`, `Shellfish`, `Crustacean`, `Milk`, `Egg`, `Soy`, `Wheat`, `Sesame`, `Other (specify)`)
Select the food that best describes the patient's allergy. This field is optional but crucial for healthcare professionals to understand the specific allergy type.

### 2. **allergy-list** (`Peanut`, `Tree nut`, `Fish`, `Shellfish`, `Crustacean`, `Milk`, `Egg`, `Soy`, `Wheat`, `Sesame`, `Other (specify)`)
List any other foods that trigger an allergic reaction. Select multiple options if applicable.

### 3. **symptoms** (text)
Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient due to the allergy. This field allows you to provide specific details about the patient's symptoms.

### 4. **medical-note** (text)
Add any relevant medical notes or information that healthcare professionals should be aware of.

### 5. **medical-provider** (text)
Provide the patient's medical provider's contact information, including their name and contact number.

### 6. **medical-relationship** (text)
Establish the relationship with the patient's medical provider (e.g., doctor, nurse, or other healthcare professional). This field is required as it helps healthcare providers understand their role in the patient's care.

### 7. **emergency-contact** (text)
Provide the patient's emergency contact information, including their name and contact number. While not required, this field is useful for healthcare providers to have an alternative contact in case of emergencies.
