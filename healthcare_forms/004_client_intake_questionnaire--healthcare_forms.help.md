# Client Intake Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Intake Questionnaire is a standardized form used to collect essential information from patients. This information helps healthcare providers understand the patient's background, medical history, and contact details, enabling effective care and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and fill in the information requested.
- Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- Be truthful and accurate when completing the form.
- If unsure about any question or field, ask for assistance from the healthcare provider.
- Once completed, submit the form to the healthcare provider for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information --** (Section 1)
  **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification documents or driver's license.
  Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Gender**: Select the option that best describes your identity.
* **Address**: Enter your current address including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number for appointment and billing purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment and billing communication.
* **Primary Care Physician**: Enter the name and contact information of your primary care physician or clinic.
* **Insurance Provider**: If applicable, enter your insurance provider's name and policy number.
* **Current Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions you currently have or have had in the past.
* **Current Medications**: List all your current prescription and over-the-counter medications.
* **Allergies**: List all known allergies, including medication and environmental allergies.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact with a relationship.
* **Consent for Care and Release of Information**: Select your preference for consent and information sharing.
