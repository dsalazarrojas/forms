# Family Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form captures the family medical history of a patient, providing essential information for healthcare providers to understand genetic predispositions and potential health risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the appropriate pages. The first page contains text fields where you can provide a brief description of your family medical history.
2. On the second page, select all the conditions that apply to your family history from the provided options.
3. If you have any additional medical history questions that are not listed, you can provide them on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Medical History Form** (`family_medical_history_form`, text, optional): This is a description field where you can provide a brief overview of your family medical history.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have a family history of a specific medical condition.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have a family history of that condition.
* **Medical History Questions** (`medical_history_questions`, text, optional): If you have any additional medical history questions that are not listed, you can provide them here.
