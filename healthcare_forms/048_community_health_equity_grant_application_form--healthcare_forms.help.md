# Community Health Equity Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Equity Grant Application Form is designed to support projects or initiatives that promote health equity and address disparities in health outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your organization's information:
	* Provide your organization's name, type, and tax identification number (EIN).
	* Enter your organization's address and city, state, and ZIP code.
	* Add your contact information: first name, last name, title, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your project:
	* Enter your project's title and a brief summary.
	* Identify the primary focus area of your project (e.g., Access to Healthcare, etc.).
	* Specify the target population and the number of people you aim to serve.
	* Enter the duration of your project (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, etc.).
3. Financial information:
	* Specify the grant amount you are requesting.
	* Enter specific, measurable objectives for your project.
4. Project execution and evaluation:
	* Describe your implementation plan, including how you will execute the project.
	* Explain how you will evaluate the success of your project.
5. Additional information:
	* If applicable, list any community partners you are working with.
	* Describe your sustainability plan, ensuring the project's continuation after funding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization (e.g., Nonprofit 501(c)(3), Community Organization, etc.).
* **EIN Number**: Enter your organization's tax identification number.
* **Organization Address**: Enter your organization's street address.
* **City, State, ZIP**: Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Contact Title**: Enter your position or role.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Project Title**: Enter your project's name.
* **Project Summary**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Health Equity Focus Area**: Select the primary focus area of your project (e.g., Access to Healthcare, etc.).
* **Target Population**: Enter the population you will be serving with your project.
* **Number of People to Serve**: Enter the estimated number of people you aim to serve.
* **Project Duration**: Select the duration of your project (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, etc.).
* **Grant Amount Requested**: Select the grant amount you are requesting.
* **Project Goals**: Enter the specific objectives you aim to achieve.
* **Measurable Objectives**: Enter specific, measurable outcomes.
* **Implementation Plan**: Describe how you will execute the project.
* **Evaluation Plan**: Explain how you will measure the success of your project.
* **Organizational Capacity**: Explain why your organization is qualified to execute the project.
* **Community Partners**: If applicable, list any community partners you are working with.
* **Sustainability Plan**: Describe how you will continue the project after funding.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other relevant information about your project.
