# Acupuncture Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing acupuncture treatment. It gathers essential information about the patient's identity, medical history, and understanding of the treatment process and potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details as required.
2. Carefully read and acknowledge any risks and side effects associated with the treatment.
3. Provide a clear understanding of your medical history and current medications.
4. If applicable, acknowledge the risks and provide a clear signature indicating your consent for the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Your birth date, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Your current residential address.
* **Phone Number**: A contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address**: A valid email for contact purposes.
* **Treatment Date**: The date of the scheduled acupuncture session.
* **Practitioner Name**: The name of the licensed acupuncturist performing the treatment.
* **Description of Treatment**: A brief description of the acupuncture treatment you are receiving.
* **Understanding of Risks**: Select "Yes, I understand" to confirm you understand the potential risks of acupuncture.
* **Common Side Effects Acknowledged**: Check all side effects you are aware of, such as temporary soreness, minor bleeding, bruising, fatigue, dizziness, and nausea.
* **Serious Risks Acknowledged**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand and acknowledge rare but serious risks.
* **Existing Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
* **Current Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies to medications or materials.
* **Pregnancy Status**: Select your current pregnancy status, if applicable.
* **Consent Given**: Select "Yes, I consent" to indicate your agreement to the treatment.
* **Signature Date**: The date of signing this consent form.
* **Patient Signature**: Your electronic signature, confirming your consent to the treatment.
* **Practitioner Signature**: The practitioner's signature, confirming discussion of consent.

Please fill in all required fields accurately and sign the form after reading and understanding the information provided. This will ensure a smooth and safe treatment experience for all parties involved.
