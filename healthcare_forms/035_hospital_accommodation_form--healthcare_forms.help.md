# Hospital Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Hospital Accommodation Form** is used to collect information from patients and their family members about their accommodation needs while staying in the hospital. This form helps hospital staff to prepare for their visit and provide a smooth stay experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
- Enter your personal details in the designated fields.
- Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): If you have a personal email address, enter it here. This is not required.
- **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of accommodation you require: Private Room, Shared Room, or Ward.
- **Accommodation Duration** (`accommodation_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you plan to stay in the hospital.
- **Accommodation Start Date** (`accommodation_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to start your accommodation.
- **Accommodation End Date** (`accommodation_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to end your accommodation.
- **Accommodation Additional Notes** (`accommodation_additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your accommodation needs.
- **Patient Family Name** (`patient_family_name`, text, optional): If you are accompanied by family members, enter their names here.
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your personal phone number for communication purposes.

**Tips**
- Please double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask hospital staff for assistance.
- This form is for your convenience, and you can also contact hospital staff directly if you need help with your accommodation.
