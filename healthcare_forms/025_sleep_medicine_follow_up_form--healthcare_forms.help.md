# reported_sleep_problems - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help healthcare providers gather information on a patient's reported sleep problems. It is designed to collect data on the duration, symptoms, current medications, and treatment plan progress related to the patient's sleep issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Indicate the length of time the patient has been experiencing sleep problems by entering a number in the "How long has the problem been going on" field.
3. Describe the symptoms the patient is experiencing in the "What are some of the symptoms?" field.
4. List the current medications the patient is taking in the "Current medications" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has reported sleep problems in the "Reported Sleep Problems" field.
6. Provide a treatment plan for the patient in the "Treatment Plan" field.
7. Indicate the progress of the treatment plan by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Treatment Plan Progress" field.
8. Enter the date of the next appointment in the "Next Appointment Date" field.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, as recorded in their medical chart.
* **How long has the problem been going on** (number, required: false): Report the number of days, weeks, or months the patient has been experiencing sleep problems.
* **What are some of the symptoms?** (text, required: false): Describe the symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as insomnia, sleep apnea, or restless leg syndrome.
* **Current medications** (text, required: false): List the medications the patient is currently taking for their sleep problems.
* **Reported Sleep Problems** (select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has reported sleep problems.
* **Treatment Plan** (text, required: false): Outline a treatment plan for the patient's sleep problems, including any recommended medication, therapy, or lifestyle changes.
* **Treatment Plan Progress** (select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the progress of the treatment plan by selecting "Yes" or "No" to show whether the patient has started or completed the plan.
* **Next Appointment Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the next appointment scheduled for the patient.
* **Comments** (text, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the patient's condition or treatment progress.
