# Medical Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Eligibility Form is a document used to determine whether patients are eligible for certain medical services or programs. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Gather the necessary information about the patient, including their basic contact information and medical history.
2.  Choose the relevant medical services they are applying for.
3.  Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Terminated).
4.  Enter any additional comments or notes from the physician.
5.  Provide your insurance information (if applicable).
6.  Enter your contact phone number and email (if applicable).
7.  Enter the patient's address and other address information (if applicable).
8.  Sign and date the form (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info**: Enter the patient's basic contact information, including their name and any other details that may be necessary for communication.
* **medical_history**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **medical_conditions**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to the patient, such as Hypertension, Diabetes, or Heart Disease.
* **contact_info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email.
* **medical_services**: Select the medical services the patient is applying for, such as Cardiology, Dermatology, or Gastroenterology.
* **patient_status**: Choose the patient's status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
* **physician_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the physician.
* **insurance_info**: Enter the patient's insurance information, if applicable.
* **insurance_plan**: Select the patient's insurance plan, such as Blue Cross, Blue Shield, or Medicare.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email, if applicable.
* **patient_address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **address_1**: Enter additional address information, if necessary.
* **address_2**: Enter further address information, if necessary.
* **city**: Enter the city where the patient is located.
* **state**: Enter the state where the patient is located.
* **zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **physician_signature**: Date and sign the form, if required.
* **medical_eligibility_form**: This field is for additional comments, if necessary.
* **medical_eligibility_determination**: Choose the determination of the patient's medical eligibility, which can be Eligible, Ineligible, or Pending. 

Note: The fields marked as optional can still be important for the patient's medical eligibility, so please be sure to fill them out if applicable.
