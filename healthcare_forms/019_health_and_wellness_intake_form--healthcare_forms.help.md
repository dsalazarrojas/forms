# Health And Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health And Wellness Intake Form is designed to collect information about your health and wellness goals, medical history, and other relevant details. This data will be used to better understand your needs and provide you with personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the appropriate page for the section you wish to fill out.
2. For each field, enter the requested information in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your name and any other identifying information as instructed.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide any relevant medical history, including past illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Health Goals** (`Health Goals`, `text`, optional)
	+ Describe your health goals, including any specific objectives or concerns you may have.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide information about your emergency contact, including name and contact number.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter payment information, including payment method and payment amount (if applicable).
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, optional)
	+ List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, optional)
	+ Describe any medical conditions you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any section, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* Once completed, please submit the form to the designated recipient.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
