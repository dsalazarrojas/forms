# Spa Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consultation Form is designed to gather necessary information from clients for a personalized spa experience. This form helps spa staff to understand client preferences, schedule appointments, and ensure that clients receive the best possible services tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Client Information (Page 1)**: Provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
2. **Services (Page 2)**: Select all the services you are interested in, such as massage, facials, haircuts, make-up, nail care, or others. This will help the spa staff understand what you are looking for.
3. **Appointment (Page 3)**: Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time that suits you.
4. **Assigned Staff (Page 4)**: Select the spa staff member assigned to take care of you.
5. **Consultation Time (Page 5)**: If desired, provide the expected consultation time.
6. **Notes (Page 6)**: Add any additional notes or concerns that you want the spa staff to be aware of.
7. **Client Comment (Page 7)**: If needed, provide any further comments or questions you have.
8. **Staff Comment (Page 8)**: If desired, provide any comments or feedback you want to share with the spa staff.
9. **Client Signature (Page 9)**: Provide your signature to confirm that you have completed the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, Text, Required)**: This field is for your personal information such as name, contact number, and address.
* **Services (Services, Select Multiple, Required)**: Select all the services you are interested in, such as massage, facials, haircuts, make-up, nail care, or others.
* **Appointment (Appointment, Date, Required)**: Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time that suits you.
* **Assigned Staff (Assigned Staff, Select One, Required)**: Select the spa staff member assigned to take care of you.
* **Consultation Time (Consultation Time, Time, Optional)**: If desired, provide the expected consultation time.
* **Notes (Notes, Note, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or concerns that you want the spa staff to be aware of.
* **Client Comment (Client Comment, Text, Optional)**: If needed, provide any further comments or questions you have.
* **Staff Comment (Staff Comment, Text, Optional)**: If desired, provide any comments or feedback you want to share with the spa staff.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature, Text, Optional)**: Provide your signature to confirm that you have completed the form accurately.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you need to make any changes, please contact the spa staff to discuss further.
* This form is for your convenience, and we appreciate your understanding in helping us provide you with a personalized spa experience.
