# dental_health_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental health history form is designed to collect patient information and medical history for dental health purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
	* Enter the patient's name, age, and contact information in the "patient_info" field.
2. **Medical History (Page 2)**:
	* Briefly describe the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
3. **Allergies (Page 3)**:
	* Select the type of allergies the patient has from the dropdown menu.
4. **Dental History (Page 4)**:
	* Enter a brief description of the patient's dental history.
5. **Dental Problems (Page 5)**:
	* Select multiple dental problems the patient is experiencing from the dropdown menu.
6. **Current Medications (Page 6)**:
	* List the patient's current medications.
7. **Additional Information (Page 7)**:
	* Enter any additional medical information that may be relevant to the patient's dental health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (patient_info, text, required):** Enter the patient's name, age, and contact information.
* **Medical History (medical_history, text, required):** Briefly describe the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies (allergies, select_one, required):** Select the type of allergies the patient has.
* **Dental History (dental_history, text, required):** Enter a brief description of the patient's dental history.
* **Dental Problems (dental_problems, select_multiple, required):** Select multiple dental problems the patient is experiencing.
* **Current Medications (current_medications, text, required):** List the patient's current medications.
* **Additional Information (medical_info, text, optional):** Enter any additional medical information that may be relevant to the patient's dental health.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
* This form is designed to be used in a healthcare setting, so please use it within that context.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
