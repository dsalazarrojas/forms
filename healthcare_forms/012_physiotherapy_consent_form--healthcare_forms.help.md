# physiotherapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiotherapy Consent Form is used to collect patient information, medical history, and consent for physiotherapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their contact details and medical history.
2. Select the physiotherapy goals that apply to the patient.
3. Provide your emergency contact information and details.
4. Specify your contact preference (phone, email, or none).
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter the medical organization and date of last visit.
7. Record the date of last contact and contact frequency.
8. Enter the physiotherapist's name, license number, email, and phone number.
9. Add any medical notes.
10. Sign and date the form as the patient and physiotherapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section asks for the patient's basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History**: This field is for any relevant medical history that the patient would like to share with the physiotherapist.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact details of someone the patient would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Physiotherapy Goals**: Choose the physiotherapy goals that apply to the patient's treatment plan.
* **Patient Consent**: This is a text field for the patient to provide their consent for treatment.
* **Emergency Contact Details**: Provide more detailed information about the emergency contact, such as their relationship with the patient.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how the patient would like to be contacted (phone, email, or none).
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or notes from the patient or physiotherapist can be entered here.
* **Medical Organization**: Enter the name of the medical organization providing the physiotherapy services.
* **Date of Last Visit**: Record the date of the patient's last visit to the physiotherapist.
* **Date of Last Contact**: Record the date of the last contact with the patient.
* **Contact Frequency**: Specify how often the patient would like to be contacted (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
* **Physiotherapist Name**: Enter the name of the physiotherapist providing the treatment.
* **Physiotherapist License Number**: Enter the license number of the physiotherapist.
* **Physiotherapist Email**: Enter the email address of the physiotherapist.
* **Physiotherapist Phone**: Enter the phone number of the physiotherapist.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes or observations about the patient's treatment.
* **Medical Notes 2**: Enter any additional medical notes or observations about the patient's treatment.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the form as the patient to indicate consent for treatment.
* **Physiotherapist Signature**: Sign and date the form as the physiotherapist to indicate completion of treatment.
* **Date of Last Update**: Record the date of the last update to the patient's record.
* **Last Update Reason**: Enter the reason for the last update to the patient's record.
* **Update Reason Details**: Enter any additional details about the last update.
* **Last Update By**: Enter the name of the person who made the last update.
* **Last Update Time**: Record the time of the last update to the patient's record.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Keep in mind that some fields may have specific formatting requirements (e.g., date and time fields should be in a specific format).
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the physiotherapist or medical organization providing the treatment.
