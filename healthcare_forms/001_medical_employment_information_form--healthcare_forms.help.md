# medical_employment_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Employment Information Form is used to collect essential details from healthcare employees. This form helps in verifying employment-related information, such as contact information and insurance details, to facilitate smooth communication and administrative tasks. Please fill in the form accurately and honestly, as the information will be used for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary details ready.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This is the name used for official records and communication.
* **Employee Email**: Type in your work email address. This will be used for internal and external communication.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your work phone number. This is how we may contact you for important updates and information.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency or for any other critical matters.
* **Medical Insurance**: If you have medical insurance, please specify the name of your insurance provider and policy number. This is optional but helps us keep accurate records.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your input in each field.
- Ensure your emergency contact is aware of their responsibility in case of an emergency.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
