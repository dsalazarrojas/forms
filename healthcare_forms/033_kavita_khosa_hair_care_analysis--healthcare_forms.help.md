# Kavita_Khosa_Hair_Care_Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kavita_Khosa_Hair_Care_Analysis form is designed to collect personal and medical information from patients to provide hair care recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the personal details form fields (page 1).
2. Provide any medical details that may be relevant to your hair care analysis (page 2).
3. Enter the date of your visit to ensure accurate tracking and recommendation (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): This section is where you can enter your name, age, contact information, or any other details you'd like to share with us.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your hair care, please list them here.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Please enter the date of your visit so we can track your progress and provide accurate recommendations.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider or contact us for clarification.
- This form is designed to be completed by patients in a legitimate healthcare setting.
- Please do not use this form for marketing or other non-qualified uses.
