# Thought Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thought Record is a personal reflection tool designed to help individuals gain insight into their thoughts, feelings, and behaviors in response to a specific upsetting situation. By filling out this form, you will be able to identify patterns and triggers that may contribute to your distress, and gain a better understanding of yourself.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please choose the section that best describes your current emotional state:
	+ If you are in an upsetting situation, go to the next step.
	+ If you are feeling neutral or relaxed, you can skip to the "Tips" section.
2. Answer the questions in each section honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Please be as detailed as possible when describing your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro Page**: This is the first page of the form where you can briefly describe the situation that's upsetting you.
* **Upsetting Situation**: Describe the specific situation that's causing you distress.
* **Feelings**: Describe your feelings in relation to the upsetting situation (e.g., anxious, sad, angry, etc.).
* **Thoughts**: Describe your thoughts and inner dialogue related to the upsetting situation.
* **Behaviors**: Describe any behaviors or actions you've taken or considered taking in response to the situation.
* **Balanced Outcomes**: Describe any positive outcomes or insights you've gained from the situation.
* **Mental Exercises**: Select the mental exercises that you've found helpful in managing your emotions and thoughts (e.g., deep breathing, journaling, etc.).
* **Emotional State**: Describe your current emotional state after completing the form.

## Tips

* Be patient and take your time filling out the form. It's okay to take breaks if needed.
* Remember that this form is for your personal growth and self-awareness, not for anyone else to judge you.
* Take the insights and observations you gain from this form and use them to create positive changes in your life.
