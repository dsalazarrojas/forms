# Health Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients or their representatives for the purpose of enrolling them in a health insurance program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of insurance you are interested in from the "Insurance" field.
2. If you have a medical history, provide a brief description of your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
3. List any current treatments you are receiving in the "Treatments" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select your medical group(s) from the "Medical Group" field.
8. Finally, enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Insurance** (Insurance, select_one, required): Select the type of insurance you are interested in.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, note, required): Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Treatments** (Treatments, text, required): List the treatments you are currently receiving.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Group** (Medical Group, select_multiple, required): Select your medical group(s) from the available options.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the best possible outcomes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose than health insurance intake.
