# Patient Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health history, including medical conditions, treatments, and test results. The data collected in this form will be used to inform healthcare decisions and ensure continuity of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please provide basic contact information for the patient, including name, address, and phone number.
2. **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition(s).
3. **Past Treatments** (`past_treatments`, text, optional): List any previous medical treatments or surgeries the patient has had.
4. **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): Check all medications the patient is currently taking, including over-the-counter (OTC) and prescription medications.
5. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Record any significant medical history, including hospitalizations or emergency room visits.
6. **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Describe any significant medical history or conditions in the patient's family members.
7. **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient is currently receiving medical treatment for their condition.
8. **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
9. **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient has undergone or is undergoing any significant medical procedures.
10. **Test Results** (`test_result`, text, optional): Record any recent test results, including lab results or radiology reports.
11. **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Attach any relevant medical records or test results, if available.
12. **Medical Test Result** (`medical_test_result`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient has recently received any medical test results.
13. **Medical Test Result 2** (`medical_test_result_2`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient has recently received any additional medical test results.
14. **Medical Test Result 3** (`medical_test_result_3`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient has recently received any other medical test results.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields, if applicable.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information or need help, please contact your healthcare team.
