# Telemedicine Patient Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect essential information about patients to ensure accurate medical evaluation and treatment. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to help healthcare professionals provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form by following each field's label and instructions.
* Use the space provided for each field to enter your answers.
* If a field is marked as required, please ensure that you fill it out completely.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask for assistance from our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history in brief, including any relevant information about your past medical conditions, surgeries, and medications.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): List your current symptoms, including any pain or discomfort you are experiencing.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any allergies to medications or 'No' if you do not.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_history_conditions`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions (e.g., diabetes, heart disease) or 'No' if you do not.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, text, optional): Describe your treatment goals and expectations.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, optional): Report any side effects you are experiencing from your medications.
* **Medical History-Surgical** (`medical_history_surgical`, text, optional): Describe any past surgeries you have had.
* **Medical History-Medical History** (`medical_history_meds`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have a history of medical conditions or 'No' if you do not.
* **Medical History-Other** (`medical_history_others`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant medical information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Medical History-Family** (`medical_history_family`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history about your family members.

Please fill out this form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
