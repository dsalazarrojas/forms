# food_hygiene_course_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your knowledge and skills in food hygiene and safety practices. It's used to evaluate your understanding and application of food safety and hygiene principles and practices in a training or course setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them according to your knowledge and experience.
2. Select the correct answers for each question, considering the available options.
3. If you have any comments or observations, use the "Participant Comments" field to share them.
4. Finally, review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **TrainersName** (`Trainers Name`, select_one, not required): This field is used to record the name or ID of the trainer who conducted the training or course.
* **ParticipantsName** (`Participants Name`, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to record the names of participants who took the training or course.
* **FoodSafetyPrinciples** (`FoodSafetyPrinciples`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if you have a good understanding of food safety principles, or "No" if you're unsure.
* **FoodHygienePractices** (`FoodHygienePractices`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the hygiene practices you're familiar with, such as "Yes" for each option.
* **TrainingGaps** (`TrainingGaps`, select_multiple, not required): Select the areas where you feel you need additional training or improvement, such as "Yes" for each option.
* **FoodSafetyProcedures** (`FoodSafetyProcedures`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the food safety procedures you're familiar with, such as "Yes" for each option.
* **AssessmentDate** (`AssessmentDate`, date, not required): Record the date when the assessment was conducted, if applicable.
* **Evaluation** (`Evaluation`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" to indicate that the assessment was helpful, or "No" if you have any concerns.
* **ParticipantComments** (`ParticipantComments`, text, not required): Use this field to provide any comments or feedback on the assessment process or any areas for improvement.

## Tips

* Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your knowledge and experience.
* If you're unsure about any question, select the option that best reflects your understanding.
* Use the available space to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

Please note that the "required" status of fields may vary depending on the context in which this form is used. Always refer to the relevant guidelines or instructions provided by the trainer or course administrator for any specific requirements.
