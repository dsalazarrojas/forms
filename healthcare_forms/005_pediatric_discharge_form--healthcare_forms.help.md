# Pediatric Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Discharge Form is used to document patient information before discharge from a healthcare facility, particularly in a pediatric setting. This form is intended for use by medical professionals to gather essential information for the patient's care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the Medical History section to gather information about the patient's medical history.
2. In the Allergies section, check all the allergies that apply to the patient.
3. In the Medications section, list the patient's current medications and dosages.
4. In the Treatment Plan section, describe the treatment plan for the patient.
5. Schedule follow-up care in the Follow-Up Care section.
6. Record the date of discharge in the Discharge Date section.
7. In the Medications Dosage section, list the dosages for each medication.
8. In the Follow-Up Contact section, schedule a follow-up contact with the patient or family.
