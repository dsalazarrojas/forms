## Healthcare Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for healthcare consulting quotations. It is intended for healthcare professionals to gather essential details about services offered, consultant information, patient data, and quotation requests. This form helps healthcare consultants provide accurate quotes and serve their clients better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review and understand the purpose of this form**: Before filling out the form, make sure you are aware of its purpose and how it will be used.
2. **Fill out the form carefully**: Take your time to accurately enter the necessary information in each field.
3. **Submit your completed form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click the submit button to send your information for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Details (Page 1)
* **Service Details**: This field is where you will provide a brief description of the services you are seeking quotes for. Enter a clear and concise description of the services you need.

### Consultant Information (Page 1)
* **Consultant Information**: Here, you will enter details about yourself or your organization. Include your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that will help us reach you.

### Patient Information (Page 2)
* **Patient Information**: If this consultation is related to a patient, please provide their name, age, and any other relevant medical history or context.

### Quotation Request (Page 2)
* **Quotation Request**: In this field, describe the specific quotation you are requesting. Provide details about what you need to be quoted on (e.g., consultation, treatment, or procedure).

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field to ensure accurate quotes and better service.
* Double-check your information before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
