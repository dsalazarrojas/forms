# Home Care Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Home Care Feedback Form is a tool used by healthcare providers and caregivers to collect feedback from patients about their care experiences. This form is designed to gather information on the quality of care and communication with care providers, as well as any other feedback or comments patients may have. The data collected from this form will help caregivers improve their services and ensure better care for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) symbol.
* Fill in your Patient Name and Date of Visit accurately.
* Rate your experience with the care provider(s) you met with using the "How would you rate the care you received from your care provider?" question (5 options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* Rate your experience with the communication from your care provider(s) using the "How would you rate the communication with your care provider?" question (5 options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* Select the response time to your visit from the options provided (30, 60, 90, 120).
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please type it in the "Additional Feedback" section.
* Fill in your Contact Number if you wish to be contacted by our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name as the patient.
* **Date of Visit**: Please enter the date of your visit to our care center.
* **Care Provider**: Please list the care provider(s) you met with during your visit.
* **How would you rate the care you received from your care provider?**: Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **How would you rate the communication with your care provider?**: Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **What was the response time to your visit?**: Select the response time from the options provided.
* **Additional Feedback**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number if you wish to be contacted by our team.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues with your care experience, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
