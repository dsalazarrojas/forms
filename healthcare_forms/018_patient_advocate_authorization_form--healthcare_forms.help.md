# Patient Advocate Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize a patient's advocate to act on their behalf in medical decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out all required fields.
- Enter the patient's name, the name of the person who will be acting as the advocate, and their relationship to the patient.
- Provide the advocate's phone number and email.
- Select the healthcare provider who will be providing care to the patient.
- Date and sign the form with both the patient and the advocate.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be authorizing the advocate.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Relationship to Patient**: Describe the relationship of the advocate to the patient (e.g., mother, father, spouse, etc.).
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Patient Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Advocate Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be acting as the advocate.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Advocate Phone**: Enter the advocate's phone number.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Advocate Email**: Enter the advocate's email address.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: No
- **Relationship to Patient**: Describe the relationship of the advocate to the patient (e.g., mother, father, spouse, etc.).
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Date Signed**: Date the form is signed by the patient.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
- **Patient Signature**: Date and sign the form with the patient's signature.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: No
- **Advocate Signature**: Date and sign the form with the advocate's signature.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: No
- **Date Signed - Advocate**: Date the form is signed by the advocate.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
- **Date Signed By**: Date the form is signed by the healthcare provider.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
- **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who will be providing care to the patient.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
- **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  Please note that some fields are not required, but it is still important to fill them out for accurate record-keeping and communication.
