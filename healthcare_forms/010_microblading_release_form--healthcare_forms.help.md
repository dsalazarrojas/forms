# Microblading Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the release process for microblading procedures. It is intended to be used by patients to provide their consent and release information after undergoing microblading treatment. This form is a standard template and can be adapted or modified as needed to suit specific business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter your personal details in the `Microblading Release Form` field.
3. Select a valid date for `Release Date`.
4. Sign and date the `Signature` field to confirm your release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Microblading Release Form** (`microblading_realease_form`, text, optional): This is the main field where you can enter your personal details. Please provide any relevant information you want to include about your microblading procedure, such as your name, contact information, or any special instructions.
* **Release Date** (`microblading_date`, date, optional): Select a valid date for when you want to be released from the microblading procedure. Make sure to enter a date that is within a reasonable timeframe after your procedure, as this will determine when your release is effective.
* **Signature** (`microblading_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date this field to confirm your release. Your signature will serve as evidence of your consent to be released from the microblading procedure. Please ensure that your signature is legible and clear.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand what you are releasing.
- Enter accurate and truthful information in all fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or medical professionals.
