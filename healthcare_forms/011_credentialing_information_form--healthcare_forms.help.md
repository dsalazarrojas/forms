# credentialing_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credentialing_information_form is designed to collect necessary information about healthcare providers' credentials to ensure they meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "provider_education" field with the name of the institution where you received your education and training as a healthcare provider.
2. Select the type of medical license you hold from the options provided in the "medical_license" field.
3. If applicable, enter the name of the hospital with which you are affiliated in the "hospital_affiliation" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your training in the "provider_training" field.
5. For the "malpractice_suites" field, select the current status of your malpractice suits from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **provider_education** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution where you received your education and training as a healthcare provider.
* **medical_license** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of medical license you hold from the options provided. Options include USMLE and ABMS.
* **hospital_affiliation** (text, required/optional): If you are affiliated with a hospital, enter the name of the hospital.
* **provider_training** (text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your training or certifications.
* **malpractice_suites** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your malpractice suits from the options provided. Options include None, Active, and Pending.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your selections and entries for correctness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
