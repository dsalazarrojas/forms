# Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a patient's insurance policy, including their contact information, medical history, policy details, and provider information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider or supervisor for guidance.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: This field is used to collect general patient information, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Medical History (2)**: This field is used to collect any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact your care.
* **Contact Info (3)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Family Info (4)**: This field is used to collect family member information, such as name, relationship, and contact details.
* **Policy Info (5)**: Enter information about your insurance policy, such as policy type and premium details.
* **Policy Details (6)**: This field is used to collect additional policy details, such as policy start and end dates.
* **Patient Risk Questions (7)**: This field is used to ask questions about any risks or concerns related to your patient care.
* **Policy Premium (8)**: Enter the premium details of your policy.
* **Premium Deduction (9)**: If applicable, enter any premium deductions or discounts.
* **Policy Type (10)**: Select the type of policy you have (e.g., type1, type2, type3).
* **Insurance Provider (11)**: Select the insurance provider from the list of options.
* **Policy Number (12)**: Enter your policy number.
* **Policy Start Date (13)**: Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date (14)**: Enter the end date of your policy.
* **Patient Address (15)**: Enter your patient address.
* **Patient City (16)**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Patient State (17)**: Enter your state of residence.
* **Patient Country (18)**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Patient Zipcode (19)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Insurance Provider Address (20)**: Enter the address of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider City (21)**: Enter the city of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider State (22)**: Enter the state of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Country (23)**: Enter the country of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Zipcode (24)**: Enter the zip code of your insurance provider.
