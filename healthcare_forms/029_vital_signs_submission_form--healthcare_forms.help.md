# vital_signs_submission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "vital_signs_submission_form" is designed to collect essential data from patients, including demographics, medical history, and clinical status. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to ensure accurate diagnosis, treatment, and care planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's existing records, if available, to confirm that you're not duplicating efforts.
2. Ensure you're using the latest version of the form.
3. Enter the patient's information accurately and completely, following the instructions for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false)
* Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and ID number (if available) in this field.

### 2. **Height (inches)** (`height`, number, required: false)
* Measure and enter the patient's height in inches, without units.

### 3. **Weight (pounds)** (`weight`, number, required: false)
* Measure and enter the patient's weight in pounds, without units.

### 4. **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, required: false)
* Record the patient's blood pressure reading in mmHg, including systolic and diastolic values.

### 5. **Temperature (F)** (`temperature`, number, required: false)
* Record the patient's temperature reading in Fahrenheit.

### 6. **Respiration Rate** (`respiration_rate`, number, required: false)
* Record the patient's respiration rate in breaths per minute.

### 7. **Oxygen Saturation** (`oxygen_saturation`, number, required: false)
* Record the patient's oxygen saturation level as a percentage.

### 8. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false)
* Describe the patient's medical history, including any significant medical conditions or surgeries.

### 9. **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false)
* List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### 10. **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false)
* Document any allergies the patient has, including any relevant reactions or symptoms.

### 11. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false)
* Identify the patient's emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship.

### 12. **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required: false)
* Record the patient's current medical condition, including any recent changes or updates.

### 13. **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, required: false)
* Document the patient's current status, such as admitted, discharged, or deceased.
