# Medical History Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical history information from patients. It is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation or diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or allergies.
3. List any medications you are currently taking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, **optional**): Describe any previous illnesses, injuries, or allergies you have experienced.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, **optional**): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for further assessment and care. It is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation or diagnosis.
