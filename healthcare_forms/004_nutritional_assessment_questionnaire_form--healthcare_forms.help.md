# Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form is a tool used to collect information on a patient's nutritional status, providing a comprehensive assessment of their health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate answer for each question from the provided options or enter a numerical value as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Blood Sugar Levels** (Select One): This question asks about your current blood sugar levels. Please select one of the following options:
  - 1 - Normal
  - 2 - High
  - 3 - Low

- **Fatty Acid Levels** (Select Multiple): This question asks about your current fatty acid levels. Please select all that apply:
  - 1 - Normal
  - 2 - High
  - 3 - Low

- **Inflammation Levels** (Select One): This question asks about your current inflammation levels. Please select one of the following options:
  - 1 - Normal
  - 2 - High
  - 3 - Low

- **Toxicity** (Select One): This question asks about your current toxicity levels. Please select one of the following options:
  - 1 - Normal
  - 2 - High
  - 3 - Low

- **Eating Habits** (Select One): This question asks about your eating habits. Please select one of the following options:
  - 1 - 1-2 meals per day
  - 2 - 3-5 meals per day
  - 3 - Less than 1 meal per day

- **Food Intake** (Text): This question asks about your food intake. Please enter a brief description of your current food intake.

- **Blood Pressure** (Number): This question asks about your current blood pressure. Please enter a numerical value.

- **Body Mass Index** (Number): This question asks about your current body mass index. Please enter a numerical value.

- **Cholesterol** (Number): This question asks about your current cholesterol levels. Please enter a numerical value.

- **Blood Glucose** (Number): This question asks about your current blood glucose levels. Please enter a numerical value.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice.
