# 10 Panel DOA Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The 10 Panel DOA Form is a standardized document for collecting and recording patient data and specimen information for drug testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the patient's full name as it appears on their official identification.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Select the patient's gender.
4. Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
5. Provide the patient's email address for follow-up or result notification (optional).
6. If applicable, enter the patient's Medical Record Number (MRN).
7. Fill in the collection date and time.
8. Provide the collector's full name.
9. Enter the collection site name.
10. Select the specimen type (e.g., urine, oral fluid, hair follicle, or blood).
11. Indicate whether the collection process was observed or not.
12. Confirm the specimen temperature strip reading for urine samples.
13. Select the primary reason for the test (e.g., pre-employment, random, or post-accident).
14. If 'Other' reason is selected, specify the reason in the text field.
15. Indicate if the patient is currently taking any prescription or over-the-counter medications.
16. If yes, list all medications.
17. Indicate if the patient has consumed any substances in the last 72 hours.
18. Select if any signs of specimen tampering or adulteration were observed.
19. Provide any additional comments or observations by the collector.
20. Review and complete all required fields before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient's Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, phone, required): Enter the patient's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Patient Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Provide the patient's email address for follow-up or result notification (if applicable).
* **Medical Record Number (MRN)** (`patient_mrn`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the patient's existing MRN.
* **Date of Collection** (`collection_date`, date, required): Fill in the collection date.
* **Time of Collection** (`collection_time`, time, required): Enter the collection time.
* **Collector's Full Name** (`collector_name`, text, required): Provide the collector's full name.
* **Collection Site Name** (`collection_site_name`, text, required): Enter the collection site name.
* **Specimen Type** (`specimen_type`, select_one, required): Select the specimen type (e.g., urine, oral fluid, hair follicle, or blood).
* **Collection Observation Status** (`observed_collection`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the collection process was observed or not.
* **Specimen Temperature Valid?** (`temperature_strip_valid`, select_one, required): Confirm the specimen temperature strip reading for urine samples.
* **Primary Reason for Test** (`reason_for_test`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for the test (e.g., pre-employment, random, or post-accident).
* **If 'Other' Reason Selected, Specify** (`other_reason_details`, text, optional): If 'Other' reason is selected, provide a detailed explanation.
* **Are you currently taking Medications?** (`currently_taking_medications`, select_one, required): Indicate if the patient is currently taking any medications.
* **List All Medications** (`list_current_medications`, text, optional): If yes, list all medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Have you consumed any Substances?** (`consumed_substances_72h`, select_one, required): Indicate if the patient has consumed any substances in the last 72 hours.
* **Signs of Tampering** (`signs_of_tampering`, select_one, required): Select if any signs of specimen tampering or adulteration were observed.
* **Collector's Notes** (`collector_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations by the collector.
