# Dog Health Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a medical record used to certify the health status of a dog. It's designed to gather essential information from the dog's owner and a veterinarian to maintain accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Owner Info" section with your contact information.
2. Provide information about your dog in the "Dog Info" section, including their identification number (if applicable).
3. Answer the "Vaccination History" question with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your dog is vaccinated.
4. If you're a veterinarian, please sign the form with your name and date.
5. If you're a vet, sign the form as your representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Info (owner_info)** (`text`, **Required**): Please fill out your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email (if available).
* **Dog Info (dog_info)** (`text`, **Required**): Provide your dog's identification number (if applicable) and a brief description of your dog (e.g., breed, age, weight, color).
* **Vaccination History (vaccination_history)** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): Answer "Yes" if your dog is vaccinated, "No" if they are not. Please note that this question is not about the type of vaccine, but rather the overall vaccination status.
* **Veterinary Info (veterinary_info)** (`text`, **Optional**): If you're a veterinarian, please provide any additional information about your dog's health status, medical history, or treatment recommendations.
* **Signature (signature)** (`text`, **Optional**): If you're the owner, please sign this section to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Veterinarian Signature (vet_signature)** (`text`, **Required**): If you're a veterinarian, please sign this section to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
