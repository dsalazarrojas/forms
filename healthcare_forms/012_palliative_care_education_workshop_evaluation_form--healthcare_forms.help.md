# Palliative Care Education Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions for improvement from participants after a palliative care education workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
*   Be sure to provide specific examples or details whenever possible.
*   If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
