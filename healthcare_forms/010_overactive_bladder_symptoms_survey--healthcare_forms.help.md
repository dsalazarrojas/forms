# Overactive Bladder Symptoms Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Overactive Bladder Symptoms Survey" form is designed to collect information about patients' experiences with overactive bladder symptoms. This form will help healthcare providers understand the severity and frequency of these symptoms to provide better patient care.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and its purpose.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your experience.
4. For free-text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your symptoms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (text): Please enter your name and any other relevant patient information.
* **symptoms** (select multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ Overactive bladder (OB)
	+ Nocturia
	+ Nocturnal enuresis
	+ Urinary incontinence
	+ Urgency
	+ Other (please specify)
* **urinary_frequency** (number): How many times do you urinate per day?
* **voiding_volume** (number): Please enter the volume of fluid you void at each urination.
* **voiding_time** (time): Please enter the average time it takes you to void.
* **voiding_frequency** (number): Please enter the number of times you void per day.
* **voiding_volume_urgency** (number): Please enter the volume of fluid you void when experiencing urgency.
* **urinary_frequency_night** (number): How many times do you urinate at night?
* **urinary_frequency_night_necessity** (time): When do you usually urinate at night? (e.g., during sleep, after waking up)
* **voiding_pain** (select multiple):
	+ None
	+ Mild
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
* **urinary_frequency_day_night** (select multiple):
	+ None
	+ Less than 2 times
	+ 2-3 times
	+ More than 3 times
* **overactive_bladder_symptoms** (select multiple):
	+ Overactive bladder (OB)
	+ Nocturia
	+ Nocturnal enuresis
	+ Urinary incontinence
	+ Urgency
	+ Other (please specify)
* **other_symptom** (text): If you have other symptoms not listed above, please specify.
* **patient_notes** (text): Please provide any additional information about your symptoms or experiences.
* **healthcare_provider_notes** (text): Please provide any notes or comments for your healthcare provider.
* **urgency_severity** (select one):
	+ 0 - None
	+ 1 - Mild
	+ 2 - Moderate
	+ 3 - Severe
* **urgency_frequency** (select multiple):
	+ None
	+ Less than 1 time
	+ 1-2 times
	+ More than 2 times
