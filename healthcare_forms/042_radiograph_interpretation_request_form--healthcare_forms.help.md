# Radiograph Interpretation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiograph Interpretation Request Form is a tool used to gather information from patients and radiologists for radiograph interpretation. It serves as a comprehensive record of the patient's details and request for radiologist services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2.  Enter the patient's age in the "patient_age" field.
3.  Select the patient's gender in the "patient_gender" field.
4.  Enter the patient's birthdate in the "patient_birthdate" field.
5.  Enter the request date in the "request_date" field.
6.  Select the "request_service" option that applies to your request.
7.  Enter the radiologist's name in the "radiologist_name" field.
8.  Enter the request reason in the "request_reason" field.
9.  Enter the radiologist's comment in the "radiologist_comment" field.
10. Enter the radiologist's signature in the "radiologist_signature" field.
11. Enter the radiologist's initials in the "radiologist_initials" field.
12. Enter the radiologist's license number in the "radiologist_license_number" field.
13. Enter the request status in the "request_status" field.
14. Select the patient's status in the "patient_status" field.
15. Enter any additional request notes in the "request_notes" field.
16. Enter the patient's notes in the "patient_notes" field.
17. Enter the review date in the "review_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
*   **Patient Age** (patient_age, text, optional): Enter the patient's age.
*   **Patient Gender** (patient_gender, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender.
*   **Patient Birthdate** (patient_birthdate, date, optional): Enter the patient's birthdate.
*   **Request Date** (request_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the request.
*   **Request Service** (request_service, select_multiple, optional): Select the service requested.
*   **Radiologist Name** (radiologist_name, text, optional): Enter the radiologist's name.
*   **Request Reason** (request_reason, text, optional): Enter the reason for the request.
*   **Radiologist Comment** (radiologist_comment, text, optional): Enter the radiologist's comment.
*   **Radiologist Signature** (radiologist_signature, text, optional): Enter the radiologist's signature.
*   **Radiologist Initials** (radiologist_initials, text, optional): Enter the radiologist's initials.
*   **Radiologist License Number** (radiologist_license_number, text, optional): Enter the radiologist's license number.
*   **Request Status** (request_status, select_one, optional): Select the request status.
*   **Patient Status** (patient_status, select_one, optional): Select the patient's status.
*   **Request Notes** (request_notes, text, optional): Enter additional request notes.
*   **Patient Notes** (patient_notes, text, optional): Enter the patient's notes.
*   **Review Date** (review_date, date, optional): Enter the review date.
