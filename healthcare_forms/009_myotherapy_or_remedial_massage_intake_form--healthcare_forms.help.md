# Myotherapy Or Remedial Massage Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about clients before their massage therapy sessions. The information collected helps us provide the best possible experience and ensure your safety and well-being during treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately, answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide as much detail as possible.
3. Select the relevant medical professions that apply to you.
4. Choose the massage techniques and style that interest you the most.
5. Provide your goals and objectives for the massage therapy sessions.
6. Answer the client assessment questions to help us understand your needs and expectations.
7. Fill out the treatment plan section with any relevant information about your current medical record.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): This is where you can provide any general information about yourself, such as your name, address, and contact details.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, optional): Please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical experiences that may impact your massage therapy sessions.
* **Contact Methods** (`contact_methods`, `text`, optional): List your contact methods, including email and phone numbers.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions, illnesses, or injuries that may affect your massage therapy experience.
* **Massage Availability** (`massage_availability`, `text`, optional): Indicate your availability for massage therapy sessions, including days and times that work best for you.
* **Medical Professions** (`medical_professions`, `select_one`, optional): Choose any medical professions that apply to you, such as doctor, nurse, or physical therapist.
* **Massage Techniques** (`massage_techniques`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the massage techniques you are interested in, such as Swedish, deep tissue, or sports massage.
* **Massage Style** (`massage_style`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the massage style that appeals to you the most, such as gentle, firm, or light.
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, `text`, optional): Describe your goals and objectives for the massage therapy sessions.
* **Client Objectives** (`client_objectives`, `text`, optional): Outline any specific objectives you hope to achieve through massage therapy.
* **Client Assessment** (`client_assessment`, `text`, optional): Answer the assessment questions to help us understand your needs and expectations.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant information about your current medical record, including dates, times, and providers.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your medical record.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your medical record.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for medical record purposes.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, optional): Provide your medical record number.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your medical record.
* **Medical Record Time** (`medical_record_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your medical record.
* **Medical Record Provider** (`medical_record_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the provider of your medical record.
* **Medical Record Date Provider** (`medical_record_date_provider`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your medical record provider.
* **Medical Record Time Provider** (`medical_record_time_provider`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your medical record provider.
* **Medical Record Date Provided By** (`medical_record_date_provided_by`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your medical record was provided.
