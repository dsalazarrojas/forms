# CI Daily Health Check (v4) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CI Daily Health Check (v4) form is designed to gather essential information from students, staff, or visitors about their health status, recent exposure, and travel history. This data helps healthcare professionals monitor and respond to potential health risks on campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role (Student, Teacher, Staff, or Visitor) in the "Role" field.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
3. Select the date of your last health screening in the "Date of check" field.
4. If applicable, enter your temperature in the "Temperature" field (in Celsius).
5. Choose any symptoms you are experiencing from the list in the "Symptoms present" field.
6. Confirm your recent exposure to a confirmed case by selecting "True", "False", or "Unsure".
7. Provide any recent travel history outside the local area in the "Recent travel history" field.
8. Select the action taken by the screener, such as "Allowed on campus", "Sent home to isolate", "Referred to clinician", or "Other".
9. Enter your name as the staff member who completed the screening in the "Screener name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name as a student, teacher, staff, or visitor.
* **Role**: Select your role on campus (Student, Teacher, Staff, or Visitor).
* **Date of check**: Select the date of your last health screening.
* **Temperature**: If you have a fever, please enter your temperature in Celsius.
* **Symptoms present**: Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the list (Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, Sore throat, Loss of taste or smell).
* **Recent exposure**: Confirm your recent exposure to a confirmed case by selecting "True", "False", or "Unsure".
* **Recent travel history**: Provide any recent travel history outside the local area.
* **Action taken by screener**: Select the action taken by the screener, such as "Allowed on campus", "Sent home to isolate", "Referred to clinician", or "Other".
* **Screener name**: Enter your name as the staff member who completed the screening.
