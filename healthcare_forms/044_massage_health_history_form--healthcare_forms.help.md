# Massage Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive health history information from clients, ensuring that the massage therapist has the necessary details to provide personalized and safe massage therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Complete each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Use the provided space for notes and comments if necessary.
4. Submit the completed form to the massage therapist before your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide your contact details, including your address and phone number.
* **Medical Conditions (3)**: List all medical conditions you have or have had in the past, including any ongoing health issues or previous injuries.
* **Medication Information (4)**: Report any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies (5)**: List any allergies you have, including any allergies you have to medications or substances.
* **Surgical History (6)**: Report any surgical procedures you have undergone or are planning to undergo.
* **Current Health Status (7)**: Describe your current health status, including any current symptoms or conditions that may affect your massage therapy session.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the massage therapist has the best understanding of your health history.
* Update your health information as needed to reflect any changes in your conditions or medications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the massage therapist for clarification or assistance.
