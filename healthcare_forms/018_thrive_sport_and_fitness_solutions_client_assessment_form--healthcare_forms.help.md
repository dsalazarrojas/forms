# thrive_sport_and_fitness_solutions_client_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather information about a client's sport and fitness goals, medical conditions, and medical procedures, which will help the client provide their details and feedback used to assess their fitness needs and provide personalized advice.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
1. **Client Info**: Fill out the "Client Info" section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. **Sport Info**: Provide information about your sport preferences, such as your favorite sports or activities.
3. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you want to be assessed for fitness needs.
4. **Time of Day**: Select the time of day you prefer to be assessed, which will be helpful for scheduling.
5. **Fitness Goals**: Choose the fitness goals you want to achieve, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or muscle loss.
6. **Medical Conditions**: If applicable, indicate any medical conditions you may have, such as high blood pressure or high cholesterol.
7. **Medical Procedures**: If applicable, list any medical procedures you have undergone, such as MRI or blood tests.
8. **Client Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your sport and fitness needs.
