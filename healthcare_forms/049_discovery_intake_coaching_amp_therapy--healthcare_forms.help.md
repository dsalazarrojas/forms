# Discovery Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your goals and preferences for treatment, coaching, and therapy. The data you provide will help us understand your needs and tailor care plans to suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required fields. You will need to select multiple goals for Treatment Goals and Therapy Goals, but the other fields are optional. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Life Domains**: Please enter a brief description of the areas you would like to focus on for your care plan. 
* **Treatment Goals**: Select all the goals that apply to you. 
  - Goal 1
  - Goal 2
  - Goal 3
* **Coaching Goals**: Enter a brief description of your coaching goals. 
* **Therapy Goals**: Select all the goals that apply to you. 
  - Goal 4
  - Goal 5
* **Self-Selected Goals**: Enter a brief description of your self-selected goals. 
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person or organization creating this form. 
* **Domain Expertise**: Select the area of domain expertise you think you would benefit most from. 
  - Sara Zelda
  - Other
* **Coaching Approach**: Enter a brief description of your preferred coaching approach. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Select all relevant goals for Treatment Goals and Therapy Goals.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
