# Provider Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Provider Survey is designed to collect information about healthcare providers to improve the quality of care and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure that all required fields (marked as "True" in the "required" field) are filled in accurately.
2.  Fill out the form fields as prompted, ensuring that the correct type of input (text, email, date, etc.) is used for each field.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider First Name** (`provider_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Last Name** (`provider_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the healthcare provider.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the healthcare provider if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the healthcare provider if applicable.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the healthcare provider has a medical license, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Board** (`medical_board`, text, optional): Enter the medical board information of the healthcare provider if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately before submission.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
- If a field has a specific type (like email or date), use the correct input format to ensure accurate submission.
