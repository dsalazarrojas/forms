# Patient Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare providers to obtain authorization from patients to access, view, and manage their medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your basic information**: Fill in the "Patient Information" page with your name, contact details, and any other required information.
2. **Select your healthcare provider**: Choose the type of healthcare provider you want to grant authorization to (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Specialist, etc.).
3. **Authorize the release**: Enter the date you want the records to be released.
4. **State your reason for release**: Explain why you're requesting to authorize the release of your medical records.
5. **Add your contact number**: Enter your contact number so the healthcare provider can reach out to you for any questions or concerns.
6. **Choose your signature method**: Select whether you'd like to provide a hand-written or digital signature.
7. **Add your signature**: Sign the form with your chosen method.
8. **Choose your healthcare provider's signature**: Select the healthcare provider who will be handling your records (e.g., Provider 1, Provider 2, etc.).
9. **Select which records to release**: Choose which types of records you're authorizing the healthcare provider to access (e.g., Medical Records, Medications, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**: Please provide your basic information, including your name, contact details, and any other required information.
  - **type**: text
  - **required**: false
- **Healthcare Provider**: Choose the type of healthcare provider you want to grant authorization to (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Specialist, etc.).
  - **type**: select_one
  - **required**: true
- **Authorization Date**: Enter the date you want the records to be released.
  - **type**: date
  - **required**: true
- **Release Reason**: Explain why you're requesting to authorize the release of your medical records.
  - **type**: text
  - **required**: true
- **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so the healthcare provider can reach out to you for any questions or concerns.
  - **type**: text
  - **required**: true
- **Signature**: Choose whether you'd like to provide a hand-written or digital signature.
  - **type**: select_one
  - **required**: true
- **Patient Signature**: Sign the form with your chosen method.
  - **type**: text
  - **required**: true
- **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Select the healthcare provider who will be handling your records (e.g., Provider 1, Provider 2, etc.).
  - **type**: select_one
  - **required**: true
- **Patient Records**: Choose which types of records you're authorizing the healthcare provider to access (e.g., Medical Records, Medications, etc.).
  - **type**: select_multiple
  - **required**: true

## Tips
- Make sure to sign the form accurately and clearly, as this is a legal document.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure all information is correct and complete.
- If you're unsure about any information, please consult with your healthcare provider or contact our support team.
