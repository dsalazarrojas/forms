# online_coaching_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for coaching sessions. It's intended for coaches to document client progress, schedule check-ins, and provide a structured way to communicate with clients.

## How to Complete This Form

This form is divided into two pages with fields for collecting information from your clients. Please complete each page according to the following steps:

1. **Page 1**
	* Enter the Client Name in the "Client Name" field. Please provide the full name of your client.
	* Enter the Client Email in the "Client Email" field. Please enter a valid email address for your client.
	* Provide the Progress Notes for the client in the "Progress Notes" field. Briefly describe the client's progress during the previous meeting or session.
	* Enter your Coach Name in the "Coach Name" field. Please provide your name as the coach.
2. **Page 2**
	* Enter the date of the next check-in in the "Check In Date" field. Please format the date as per your preference (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
	* Enter the Check In Time in the "Check In Time" field. Please provide the time of the next check-in.
	* Enter the location of the next check-in in the "Check In Location" field. Please provide the location where the client will be checking in.
	* Provide any additional Notes for the client in the "Notes" field. This is an optional field for any additional comments or information.
