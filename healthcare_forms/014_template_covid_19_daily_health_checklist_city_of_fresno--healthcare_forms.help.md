# template_covid_19_daily_health_checklist_city_of_fresno - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a patient's health status, contact history, travel history, and test results to aid in the assessment and surveillance of COVID-19 cases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on the patient's information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields to aid in a comprehensive assessment.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptoms** (1): Select all symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing from the provided options. This includes Fever, Chills, Cough, Shortness of breath, Sore Throat, Runny nose, Fatigue, Muscle or body aches, Diarrhea, Headache, New loss of taste or smell, Numbness or tingling, Confusion, Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath, Chest pain or discomfort, Constriction or chest tightness, Repeated shaking or trembling, Dizziness or feeling faint, Nausea or vomiting, New loss of smell.
* **Temperature** (2): Enter the patient's current temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Contact Tracing** (3): Select True if the patient has had contact with an infected individual, False otherwise.
* **Travel History** (4): Select True if the patient has traveled recently, False otherwise.
* **Recent Travel** (5): Select True if the patient has recently traveled, False otherwise.
* **Travel Destination** (6): Enter the location of the patient's recent travel (optional).
* **Travel Date** (7): Enter the date of the patient's recent travel (optional).
* **Contact Person** (8): Select True if the patient has had contact with an infected individual, False otherwise.
* **Contact Number** (9): Select True if the patient has had contact with an infected individual, False otherwise.
* **Symptoms Onset** (10): Describe the onset of the patient's symptoms (optional).
* **Symptoms Description** (11): Describe the patient's symptoms in more detail (optional).
* **Test Results** (12): Enter any relevant test results (optional).
* **Medical History** (13): Enter any relevant medical history (optional).
* **Medication** (14): Enter any current medication the patient is taking (optional).
* **Allergies** (15): Enter any allergies the patient has (optional).
* **Previous Conditions** (16): Enter any previous health conditions the patient has (optional).
* **Current Conditions** (17): Enter any current health conditions the patient has (optional).
* **Previous Vaccinations** (18): Enter any previous vaccinations the patient has received (optional).
* **Current Vaccinations** (19): Enter any current vaccinations the patient has received (optional).
* **Previous Tests** (20): Enter any previous tests the patient has undergone (optional).
* **Contact Type** (21): Describe the type of contact the patient had with an infected individual (optional).
* **Contact Source** (22): Describe the source of the patient's contact with an infected individual (optional).
* **Travel Type** (23): Describe the type of travel the patient has undergone (optional).
* **Travel Location** (24): Describe the location of the patient's travel (optional).
* **Travel Mode** (25): Describe the mode of travel the patient used (optional).
