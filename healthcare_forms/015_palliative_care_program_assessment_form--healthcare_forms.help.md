# palliative-care-program-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the palliative care program's appropriateness and effectiveness for the patient. It collects information on patients' medical conditions, pain management, and social support needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions that apply to the patient's situation.
2. Select multiple options when appropriate (e.g., for multiple medical conditions).
3. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (1, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional information or context about the patient.
- **Patient Information** (2, select_multiple, optional): Choose all conditions that apply to the patient.
  • Heart failure
  • Lung disease
  • Cancer
  • Diabetes
  • Hypertension
  • Alzheimer's disease
- **Medical Conditions** (3, select_one, optional): Select one condition that best describes the patient's medical status.
- **Pain Management** (4, select_multiple, optional): Check if the patient is receiving pain management treatment.
  • Yes
  • No
- **Psychological Impact** (5, select_multiple, optional): Check if the patient is experiencing psychological impact.
  • Yes
  • No
- **Social Support** (6, select_multiple, optional): Check if the patient has social support in place.
  • Yes
  • No
