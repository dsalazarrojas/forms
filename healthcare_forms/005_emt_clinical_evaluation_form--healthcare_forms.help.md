# EMT Clinical Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMT Clinical Evaluation Form is a tool designed to gather essential information from EMTs about patients in emergency situations. This form helps EMTs to quickly and accurately assess the patient's condition and provide effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions carefully before starting.
2. Ensure you are completing the form in the correct context (emergency patient evaluation).
3. Fill out the form as you would in a real-world scenario.
4. Answer each question to the best of your ability, using your professional judgment.
5. If a question does not apply, leave it blank or select "None" or "Unknown" as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birthday`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical condition.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable vital signs that apply to the patient's condition. Choose from:
	+ Tachycardia
	+ Bradycardia
	+ Hypotension
	+ Hypertension
	+ Bradypnea
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable symptoms that apply to the patient's condition. Choose from:
	+ Cardiac Arrest
	+ Respiratory Arrest
	+ Seizures
	+ None
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the patient's status:
	+ Critical
	+ Stable
	+ Unknown
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, text, optional): Describe any treatment provided to the patient.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and title.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the facility's name.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Use professional judgment when assessing the patient's condition.
* If in doubt, leave a question blank or select "None" or "Unknown" as applicable.
* This form is not a substitute for medical expertise; always consult with a healthcare professional if unsure about a patient's condition.
