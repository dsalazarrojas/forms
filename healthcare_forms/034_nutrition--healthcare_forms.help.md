# Nutrition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients, including their personal details, medical history, and appointment scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your appointment in the "Date" field (if applicable).
3. Enter the time of your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field (if applicable).
4. Select your insurance provider from the drop-down menu in the "Insurance Provider" field.
5. List any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6. Enter any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field (this field is required).
7. Enter your medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field (this field is required).
8. Enter your patient ID in the "Patient ID" field (this field is required).
9. Select your medical group from the drop-down menu in the "Medical Group" field.
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
11. Enter your city in the "City" field (this field is required).
12. Select your state from the drop-down menu in the "State" field.
13. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field (this field is required).
14. If you have any allergies, please list them in the "Medication Allergies" field.
15. If you have a contact person to reach out to, enter their name in the "Contact Person" field.
16. If you have a contact person's email, enter it in the "Contact Person Email" field.
17. Enter any insurance card number in the "Insurance Card Number" field.
18. Finally, enter any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your appointment (if applicable).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your appointment (if applicable).
* **Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your appointment or medical history.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter the contact number of a person to reach out to.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the contact email of a person to reach out to.
* **Insurance Provider** (select_one, optional): Select your insurance provider from the drop-down menu.
* **Medical Conditions** (select_multiple, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medications** (text, required): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Appointment** (date, optional): Enter the date of your appointment (if applicable).
* **Appointment Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your appointment (if applicable).
* **Follow Up** (date, optional): Enter the follow-up appointment date (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Time** (time, optional): Enter the follow-up appointment time (if applicable).
* **Contact Person** (text, optional): Enter the name of a contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (email, optional): Enter the contact email of a contact person.
* **Insurance Card Number** (text, optional): Enter any insurance card number.
* **Medical History** (text, required): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Medication Allergies** (select_multiple, optional): List any medication allergies you have.
* **Medical Record Number** (text, required): Enter your medical record number.
* **Patient ID** (text, required): Enter your patient ID.
* **Medical Group** (select_one, optional): Select your medical group from the drop-down menu.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (select_one, optional): Select your state from the drop-down menu.
* **Zip** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
