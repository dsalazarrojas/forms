# Alcoholism Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alcoholism Testing Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to assess and manage patients with potential alcohol use disorders. It includes two sections: Clinical Administration and AUDIT Screening Section, and Clinical Findings and Recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, healthcare professionals should:

1. Review the patient's record and gather relevant information in the Clinical Administration section.
2. Administer the AUDIT Screening Section to the patient, asking them about their personal experiences with alcohol use.
3. Evaluate the patient's answers and record their findings in the Clinical Findings and Recommendation section.
4. Provide recommendations for further medical attention or intervention as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician/Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional administering the test.
* **Patient Record ID**: Enter the patient's ID number or unique identifier.
* **Date of Testing**: Record the date of the test administration.
* **How often do you have a drink containing alcohol?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your usual drinking frequency:
	+ 0 - Never
	+ 1 - Monthly or less
	+ 2 - 2 to 4 times a month
	+ 3 - 2 to 3 times a week
	+ 4 - 4 or more times a week
* **How many drinks containing alcohol do you have on a typical day when you are drinking?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your typical drinking amount:
	+ 0 - 1 or 2
	+ 1 - 3 or 4
	+ 2 - 5 or 6
	+ 3 - 7, 8, or 9
	+ 4 - 10 or more
* **How often do you have six or more drinks on one occasion?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your drinking frequency:
	+ 0 - Never
	+ 1 - Less than monthly
	+ 2 - Monthly
	+ 3 - Weekly
	+ 4 - Daily or almost daily
* **Have you ever felt you ought to cut down on your drinking?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your attitude towards your drinking:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have people annoyed you by criticizing your drinking?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your experiences:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have you ever felt bad or guilty about your drinking?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your feelings:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have you ever had a drink first thing in the morning to steady your nerves or get rid of a hangover?**: Answer this question by selecting the option that best describes your behavior:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Physical Signs of Chronic Alcohol Use**: Record any physical signs or symptoms of chronic alcohol use you have observed.
* **Total Screening Score**: Enter the total score from the AUDIT questions.
* **Clinical Recommendation**: Select the recommended course of action based on the total score:
	+ No intervention required
	+ Brief intervention/education
	+ Specialized treatment referral
	+ Immediate detox required
* **Follow-up Appointment Date**: Schedule a follow-up appointment to review the patient's progress.
* **Physician Signature Confirmation**: Electronically sign to confirm the test administration.

Note: The form is designed to be used by healthcare professionals to assess and manage patients with potential alcohol use disorders.
