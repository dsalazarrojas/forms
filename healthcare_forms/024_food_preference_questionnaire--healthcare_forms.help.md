# Food Preference Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Food Preference Questionnaire" form is a tool used to collect information about a user's food preferences, dietary restrictions, allergies, and other related details. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the user's needs and preferences to provide better care and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your personal details in the "user_info" field.
2. Indicate your dietary restrictions and allergies in the "dietary_restrictions" field.
3. Select your favorite cuisines in the "favorite_cuisines" field.
4. Provide any special requests or instructions for your care in the "special_requests" field.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, in the "contact_info" field.
6. Indicate your preferred time for food in the "food_preferred_time" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or information in the "additional_comments" field.
8. Provide details about any food allergies in the "food_allergy_info" field.
9. Indicate how often you prefer to eat in the "food_preference_frequency" field.
10. Answer the follow-up questions about food preference frequency in the "food_preference_frequency_2" and "food_preference_frequency_3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other identifying information. This field is not required.
* **Food Preferences**: Select up to three cuisines that you prefer. This field is required.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Indicate any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., dairy, gluten, vegan). This field is required.
* **Food Allergies**: Enter any food allergies you have. This field is not required.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your care. This field is not required.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact email and phone number. This field is not required.
* **Preferred Time for Food**: Select your preferred time for eating. This field is not required.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your preferences. This field is not required.
* **Food Allergy Info**: Enter any details about your food allergies. This field is not required.
* **Food Preference Frequency**: Indicate how often you prefer to eat (e.g., always, occasionally, never). This field is required.
* **Additional Comments 2 & 3**: Enter any additional comments or information. These fields are not required.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
