# Vaccination Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Billing Form is designed to collect vital information for medical billing purposes, ensuring accurate and timely processing of vaccination-related charges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Info** section with the patient's essential details.
2. Provide the necessary **Insurance Details** (if applicable).
3. Enter the **Service Details** related to the vaccination, including any relevant medical procedures or services.
4. Add any additional **Notes** about the patient visit (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (required)**:
	+ Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other essential contact information.
	+ This information is crucial for accurate billing and future reference.
* **Insurance Details (optional)**:
	+ Enter the patient's insurance provider's name, policy number, and any other relevant details.
	+ If the patient is uninsured, please mark the 'None' option.
* **Service Details (required)**:
	+ Describe the vaccination service provided (e.g., type of vaccine, administration method, etc.).
	+ Include any relevant medical procedures or services related to the vaccination.
* **Notes (optional)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or instructions about the patient visit.
	+ This can include any specific details about the patient's condition, medical history, or treatment.
* **Submit**:
	+ Click the **Submit** button to complete and finalize the form.
	+ Review your entries before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the billing process.
- Double-check the accuracy of your entries, especially insurance details and service descriptions.
- Use the notes section to provide any additional context or instructions that might aid in the billing process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
