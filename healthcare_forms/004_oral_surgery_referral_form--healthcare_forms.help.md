## Oral Surgery Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral Surgery Referral Form is designed for healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's referral for oral surgery. This form helps gather necessary data for further consultation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Select the doctor who is referring the patient from the "Doctor Referring" dropdown list.
4. Choose the patient's gender from the "Gender" dropdown list.
5. Enter the patient's condition or reason for referral in the "Condition" field.
6. Select the type of surgery the patient is undergoing from the "Type of Surgery" dropdown list.
7. Enter the date and time of the surgery in the "Surgery Date" and "Surgery Time" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the patient's address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Doctor Referring**: Select the doctor who is referring the patient for oral surgery.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender from the available options.
* **Condition**: Enter the reason for the patient's referral for oral surgery.
* **Type of Surgery**: Select the type of surgery the patient is undergoing.
* **Surgery Date**: Enter the date of the surgery in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Surgery Time**: Enter the time of the surgery in the format 12:00 PM or 12:00 AM.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
