# IMC Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IMC Assessment Form is designed to gather vital information about a patient's physical characteristics, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions about their health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's height in centimeters or other units (Meter, Centimeter, or Inch) in the "Height in cm" field.
2. Enter the patient's weight in kilograms in the "Weight in kg" field.
3. Enter the patient's age in years in the "Age" field.
4. Select the height unit used to measure the patient's height in the "Height unit" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the date of the IMC calculation in the "IMC calculation" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Height in cm**: Enter the patient's height in centimeters, a numerical value. 
* **Weight in kg**: Enter the patient's weight in kilograms, a numerical value. 
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in years, a numerical value. 
* **Height unit**: Select the unit used to measure the patient's height (Meter, Centimeter, or Inch). 
* **IMC calculation**: (Optional) Enter the date of the IMC calculation, a date value. 
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition, a text field.
