# medical_device_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the release of medical device records for patients, ensuring that these records are handled correctly and according to regulatory guidelines. It helps identify the patient, their relationship to the device, and whether the records should be shared or not.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help in the proper handling of medical device records.
2. Ensure that you fill in all required fields. The fields marked as required are essential for the processing of the form.
3. Use the provided hints for guidance on any unclear fields.
4. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier** (`patient_identifier`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the patient, providing a unique identifier for their medical records.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, select_one, required): Please select the relationship between you and the patient, such as "Primary Caregiver".
* **Device Identifier** (`device_identifier`, text, required): This field is used to record the identification number of the medical device.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you agree to share the medical device records or not. This could be either "True" or "False".
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional): If the patient has passed away, enter their date of death in the correct format. This is not required if the patient is still alive.
* **Medical Device Records** (`medical_device_records`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments about the medical device records.
* **Medical Device Records Shoulder** (`medical_device_records_shoulder`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's shoulder region.
* **Medical Device Records Waist** (`medical_device_records_waist`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's waist region.
* **Medical Device Records Knee** (`medical_device_records_knee`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's knee region.
* **Medical Device Records Foot** (`medical_device_records_foot`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's foot region.
* **Medical Device Records Hip** (`medical_device_records_hip`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's hip region.
* **Medical Device Records Neck** (`medical_device_records_neck`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's neck region.
* **Medical Device Records Chest** (`medical_device_records_chest`, text, optional): Record any specific details about the medical device's chest region.
* **Medical Device Records Other** (`medical_device_records_other`, text, optional): Record any other specific details about the medical device's location.
