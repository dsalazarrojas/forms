# Friends RTT Intake Form Liva Levica - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friends RTT Intake Form Liva Levica is designed to collect and document contact and medical information about a patient, helping healthcare professionals provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields for the patient's contact information (Patient First Name, Patient Last Name, and Relationship).
2. Provide any relevant medical history, medical conditions, and medications for the patient.
3. If applicable, include any additional medical history information (Medical History Additional 1-8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): The first name of the patient.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): The last name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): The patient's date of birth.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): The first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): The last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): The email address of the contact person.
* **Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, select_one, required): The relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider (Primary Caregiver or Secondary Caregiver).
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes about the patient.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): A general summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): A list of the patient's current medications.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): The contact information for the patient's emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): A list of the patient's current medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): A list of the patient's allergies.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): A record of the patient's recent medical tests.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): A list of the patient's current medications.
* **Medical History Additional 1-8** (`medical_history_additional_1-8`, text, optional): Additional medical history information ( Medical History Additional 1-8).

Note: This help guide is for reference only and might not cover every possible scenario. Always consult a medical professional for guidance on specific patient cases.
