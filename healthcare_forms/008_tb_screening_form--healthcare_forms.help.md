## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**:
	* `Patient Name`: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records or ID card.
	* `Date of Birth`: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	* `Contact Info`: Enter the patient's contact information, including name and phone number.
2. **Medical History (Page 2)**:
	* `Medical History 1`: Describe any previous medical conditions or treatments that may be relevant to the patient's current condition.
	* `Medical History 2`: Describe any additional medical history that may be relevant.
	* `Medical History 3`: Describe any further medical history that may be relevant.
3. **Travel History (Page 3)**:
	* `Travel History 1`: Describe any recent travel to high-risk areas or countries with high TB prevalence.
	* `Travel History 2`: Describe any other recent travel to high-risk areas or countries with high TB prevalence.
4. **Symptoms (Page 4)**:
	* `Symptoms 1`: Describe any symptoms the patient has experienced, such as coughing, fever, or weight loss.
	* `Symptoms 2`: Describe any additional symptoms the patient has experienced.
5. **Screening Results (Page 5)**:
	* `Screening Result 1`: Enter the results of any previous TB screenings.
	* `Screening Result 2`: Enter the results of any other previous TB screenings.
6. **Vaccination Status (Page 6)**:
	* `Vaccination Status 1`: Indicate if the patient has received any vaccinations for TB.
	* `Vaccination Status 2`: Indicate if the patient has received any other vaccinations for TB.

Please note that some fields are optional and may not be applicable to all patients. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate that it is not relevant. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This field is used to identify the patient and match their information with their medical records.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is used to verify the patient's age and medical history.
* **Contact Info**: This field is used to contact the patient for follow-up or to gather additional information.
* **Medical History 1, 2, 3**: These fields are used to gather information about the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions, treatments, or hospitalizations.
* **Travel History 1, 2**: These fields are used to gather information about the patient's travel history, including any recent or past travel to high-risk areas or countries with high TB prevalence.
* **Symptoms 1, 2, 3**: These fields are used to gather information about the patient's symptoms, including any recent or recurring symptoms.
* **Screening Result 1, 2, 3**: These fields are used to gather information about the patient's screening results, including any positive or negative results.
* **Vaccination Status 1, 2**: These fields are used to gather information about the patient's vaccination status, including any vaccinations they may have received.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate that it is not relevant.
