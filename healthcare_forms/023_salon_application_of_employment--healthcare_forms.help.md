# salon_application_of_employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Application of Employment form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for employment opportunities within the salon industry. This form aims to gather relevant details from applicants, including their user and contact information, as well as their employment history and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **User Information**, including your full name and any other relevant personal details.
2. Enter your **Contact Information**, such as your email address and phone number, to ensure we can easily get in touch with you.
3. Fill out your **Employment Information**, describing your previous work experience and qualifications relevant to the salon industry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (Page 1)**: This is where you can enter your full name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: Here, you can provide your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Employment Information (Page 3)**: Describe your previous work experience and qualifications relevant to the salon industry, such as cosmetology certifications or salon management experience.
