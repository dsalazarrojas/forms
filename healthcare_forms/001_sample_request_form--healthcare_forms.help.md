# Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Request Form is a tool for users to submit information about their product or service requests. It is a simple form that helps to gather relevant data from users and allows them to submit their requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are looking for a sample.
2. Provide a brief description of the product or service you are requesting in the "What is the product you are requesting?" field.
3. Specify the type of example you are looking for in the "What type of example?" field.
4. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Name" field.
5. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are submitting the request.
8. Click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sample Request** (sample_request_form_1, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select if you are looking for a sample or not.
* **What is the product you are requesting?** (product_request_1, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product or service you are looking for.
* **What is the service you are requesting?** (product_request_2, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the service you are looking for.
* **What is the example of the product you are looking for?** (sample_request_form_3, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the example you are looking for.
* **Where are you looking for an example?** (example_location, select_one, required: false): Select where you are looking for an example.
* **What type of example?** (example_location_2, select_one, required: false): Select the type of example you are looking for.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Submit** (submit, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you are submitting the request by selecting "Yes".
