# Genetic Data Sharing Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant consent for the sharing of genetic data with researchers. It is intended to provide a clear understanding of the research purposes, data sharing terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your participant information (page 1).
2.  Indicate whether you agree to share your genetic data (page 2).
3.  Specify the research purposes for which you are sharing your data (page 3).
4.  Choose the method of data collection (page 4).
5.  Confirm your consent for data sharing (page 5).
6.  Specify the data sharing terms (page 6).
7.  Select the contact information (page 7).
8.  Choose the data destruction policy (page 8).
9.  Determine the level of data access (page 9).
10.  Provide contact information for the researcher (page 10).
11.  Sign to confirm your consent (page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (page 1)**: Fill in your name and any other relevant identification information.
* **Genetic Data Sharing (page 2)**: Check "True" to agree to share your genetic data with researchers.
* **Research Purposes (page 3)**: Specify the research projects or purposes you are consenting to participate in.
*   *Example: "I am consenting to share my data for research on Parkinson's disease."*
* **Data Collection (page 4)**: Choose the method(s) by which you are consenting to your data being collected (e.g., blood, saliva, or other).
* **Consent (page 5)**: Check "agree" to confirm your consent to share your data.
* **Data Sharing Terms (page 6)**: Specify any terms or conditions you wish to impose on data sharing (e.g., anonymity, confidentiality).
*   *Example: "Please share my data only with researchers at my institution."*
* **Contact Information (page 7)**: Choose the contact method(s) you prefer to be contacted (e.g., email, phone).
* **Data Destruction (page 8)**: Choose the policy for data destruction after it is collected (e.g., destroy, retain).
* **Data Access (page 9)**: Determine the level of access you are granting researchers to your data (e.g., restricted, open access).
* **Contact Person (page 10)**: Fill in the name and contact information of the researcher who will be accessing your data.
* **Signature (page 11)**: Sign to confirm your consent to share your genetic data with researchers.

Tips

*   Please take the time to carefully review the form and understand the implications before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact your project administrator or supervisor for guidance.
*   This form is a formal document and is legally binding. Please ensure that you understand its contents and implications before submitting it.
