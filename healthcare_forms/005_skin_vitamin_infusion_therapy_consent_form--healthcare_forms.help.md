# Skin Vitamin Infusion Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential patient information and obtain consent for skin vitamin infusion therapy, a medical treatment that involves the administration of vitamins or nutrients to improve skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name** in the first field, which is a required field.
2. Provide your **Contact Details** in the second field, which is a required field.
3. Share your **Medical History** in the third field, which is a required field.
4. Select your response to **Treatment Consent** by choosing either "Yes" or "No" in the fourth field.
5. Optionally, you can enter your **Clinic Name** if you are a patient at a specific clinic.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the sixth field, which is a required field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Choose the time of your **Time of Visit** in the seventh field, which is a required field.
8. Share the **Treatment Details** of your skin vitamin infusion therapy in the last field, which is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your phone number and/or email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Medical History**: Share any previous health issues or medical conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Treatment Consent**: This question will help us know if you're okay with undergoing this treatment.
* **Clinic Name**: If you are a patient at a specific clinic, you can enter the name of your clinic here.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time slot for your treatment appointment.
* **Treatment Details**: Share any additional information about the treatment you are undergoing, such as the type of vitamins or nutrients you will be receiving.
