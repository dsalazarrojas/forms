# School Based Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Based Therapy Referral Form is used to collect information for school-based therapy referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (indicated by *) and any additional fields that you feel are relevant to the referral.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info (id: 1)**: This field is a general description of the referral. Please include any relevant details or comments about the student's situation.
* **Student Info (id: 2)**: Enter the student's name and any other identifying information.
* **Parent Info (id: 3)**: Enter the parent/guardian's name and contact information.
* **Therapist Info (id: 4)**: Enter the therapist's name and contact information.
* **Referral Info (id: 5)**: Enter any additional referral information or comments.
* **Student Support (id: 6)**: Describe the type of support the student requires.
* **School Info (id: 7)**: Enter the school's name and address.
* **Teacher Info (id: 8)**: Enter the teacher's name and contact information.
* **School Admin Info (id: 9)**: Enter the school administrator's name and contact information.
* **Other Support (id: 10)**: Describe any other support the student requires.
* **Email (id: 12)**: Enter the email address for the referral.
* **Phone (id: 13)**: Enter the phone number for the referral.
* **Date of Referal (id: 14)**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Time of Referal (id: 15)**: Enter the time of the referral.
* **Select Therapist (id: 16)**: Choose whether the referral is for a new or existing therapist.
* **Select Therapist 2 (id: 17)**: Choose whether the referral is for a new or existing therapist.
* **Select Therapist 3 (id: 18)**: Choose whether the referral is for a new or existing therapist.
* **School Territory (id: 19)**: Choose the relevant school territory.
* **School Territory 2 (id: 20)**: Choose the relevant school territory.

## Tips
