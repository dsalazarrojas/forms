# Appendix C To Sec. 1910.134 - OSHA Respirator Medical Evaluation Questionnaire (Mandatory) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OSHA Respirator Medical Evaluation Questionnaire (Mandatory) is used to gather necessary medical information from employees who will be required to use respirators in the workplace. This information will help healthcare professionals assess the employee's medical fitness for respirator use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and date of evaluation as required.
2. Provide your height and weight, including feet and inches for height, and pounds for weight.
3. Select your sex and job title.
4. Enter your phone number and best contact time for the healthcare professional to reach you.
5. Confirm if your employer has instructed you on how to contact the healthcare professional.
6. Answer the medical history questions, including smoking status, seizure history, diabetes history, allergic reactions, claustrophobia history, and lung and heart problems history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Evaluation** (2): Enter the date of this evaluation in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Employee Name** (3): Enter your full name as it should be on the respirator fit test record.
* **Your Age (to the nearest year)** (4): Enter your age as a whole number.
* **Sex** (5): Select your sex, either 'Male' or 'Female'.
* **Your Height (Feet)** (6): Enter your height in feet as a whole number.
* **Your Height (Inches)** (7): Enter your height in inches as a whole number.
* **Your Weight (Lbs)** (8): Enter your weight in pounds as a whole number.
* **Your Job Title** (9): Enter your job title or job function.
* **A phone number where you can be reached by the healthcare professional** (10): Enter your phone number for the healthcare professional to contact you.
* **The best time to phone you at this number** (11): Enter the best time to contact you at this number.
* **Has your employer told you how to contact the healthcare professional who will review this questionnaire?** (12): Indicate if your employer has instructed you on how to contact the healthcare professional.
* **Do you currently smoke tobacco, or have you smoked tobacco in the last month?** (14): Select 'True' if you smoke or have smoked tobacco in the past month.
* **Have you ever had seizures?** (15): Select 'True' if you have ever had seizures.
* **Have you ever had diabetes (sugar disease)?** (16): Select 'True' if you have ever had diabetes.
* **Have you ever had allergic reactions that interfere with your breathing?** (17): Select 'True' if you have ever had allergic reactions that interfere with your breathing.
* **Have you ever had claustrophobia (fear of closed-in places)?** (18): Select 'True' if you have ever had claustrophobia.
* **Have you ever had any of the following lung problems - Asbestosis, Asthma, Bronchitis, Emphysema, Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Silicosis, Pneumothorax, Lung Cancer, Broken Ribs, Any chest injuries or surgeries, Any other lung problem?** (19): Select 'True' if you have ever had any of the lung problems listed.
* **Have you ever had any of the following cardiovascular problems - Heart attack, Stroke, Angina, Heart failure, Swelling in legs or feet, Heart arrhythmia, High blood pressure, Any other heart problem?** (20): Select 'True' if you have ever had any of the cardiovascular problems listed.
