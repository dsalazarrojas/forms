# Gym Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is the Gym Registration Form Template, used to collect user registration information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the pages to provide your registration information.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions on each page.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This is the first page where you will enter general information.
	+ **Page 1**: Enter your information here.
* **Page 2**: This is the second page where you will enter additional information.
	+ **Page 2**: Enter your information here.
* **Page 3: Acceptance of Terms**: This page asks if you agree with the terms and conditions.
	+ **Acceptance**: Check 'Yes' if you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Page 4: Acceptance of Terms (Note)**: This is a non-editable field where the terms and conditions are listed.
* **Page 5: Time**: Not applicable (no information provided).
* **Page 6: Date**: This is not a required field.
* **Page 7: Phone number**: Enter your phone number here.
	+ **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Page 8: Email**: Enter your email address here.
	+ **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Page 9: Additional information**: Enter any additional information here.
	+ **Additional information**: Enter any relevant information about yourself.
