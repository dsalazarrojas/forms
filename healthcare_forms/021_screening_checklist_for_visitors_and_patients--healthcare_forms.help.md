# Screening Checklist For Visitors And Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from visitors and patients in a healthcare setting, including their visitor information, symptoms, medical conditions, medical history, and screening results. It's meant to be filled out by visitors and patients upon arrival or during a visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For visitor information, enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
3. If you have any symptoms, select "Yes" for each relevant option.
4. Select your medical conditions and medical history as applicable.
5. Answer the screening questions and select the corresponding options.
6. Ensure all fields are completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Information** (`visitor_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information to help us identify you and contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Has Symptoms** (`has_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for each symptom you are experiencing or have experienced:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have contact information:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for each medical condition you have:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Note any relevant medical history information.
* **Screening Checklist** (`screening_checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for each screening checklist item:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Patient Screening** (`patient_screening`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have any active or recent medical screening:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Patient Screening Result** (`patient_screening_result`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" for a positive or recent screening result:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Contact Trace** (`contact_trace`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have had close contact with someone with a medical condition:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Screening Checklist 2** (`screening_checklist_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for each screening checklist item:
	+ 'Yes'
* **Patient Screening Result 2** (`patient_screening_result_2`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" for a positive or recent screening result:
	+ 'Yes'

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have any concerns or questions, please ask a healthcare professional.
