# COI Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COI Form is used to collect information about potential conflicts of interest from individuals who need to disclose them for a particular project or task. This form helps ensure transparency and accountability in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all the required questions truthfully.
2. Start by filling out the personal contact information:
   - Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
   - Enter your primary email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide information about your organization:
   - Enter the name of your affiliated organization in the "Organization Name" field.
   - Enter your position or title in the "Position or Title" field.
4. If you have financial interest in any related entities, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you have financial interest in any related entities" field.
5. If you have selected "Yes", please describe the financial interest details in the "Please describe the financial interest details" field.
6. If you have family relationships with decision makers, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you have family relationships with decision makers" field.
7. If you have selected "Yes", describe the family relationships in the "Describe family relationships if applicable" field.
8. If you have other potential conflicts of interest, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Other potential conflicts of interest" field.
9. If you have selected "Yes", describe the other conflicts in the "Describe other conflicts" field.
9. Finally, review and sign the form by selecting "I Agree" or "I Do Not Agree" in the "I certify that this disclosure is accurate and complete" field. Please note that this is a certification of accuracy and completeness.
10. Date of the disclosure must be filled out in the "Date of Disclosure" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (text, required): This is your complete name as it appears on official records.
* **Email Address** (email, required): This is your primary email contact.
* **Organization Name** (text, required): This is the name of your affiliated organization.
* **Position or Title** (text, required): This is your role or position.
* **Do you have financial interest in any related entities** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have any financial interest in entities related to the project or task.
* **Please describe the financial interest details** (text, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, please provide a detailed description of your financial interest.
* **Do you have family relationships with decision makers** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have any family relationships with individuals who are involved in the decision-making process.
* **Describe family relationships if applicable** (text, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, please provide a detailed description of your family relationships.
* **Other potential conflicts of interest** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have any other potential conflicts of interest.
* **Describe other conflicts** (text, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, please provide a detailed description of your other conflicts.
* **I certify that this disclosure is accurate and complete** (select_one, required): Please select "I Agree" to confirm that you have accurately and truthfully completed this form.
* **Date of Disclosure** (date, required): The date you are submitting this form.
