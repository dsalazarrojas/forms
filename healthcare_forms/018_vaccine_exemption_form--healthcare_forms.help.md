# Vaccine Exemption Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request exemption from receiving a vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the `parent_name` field with the name of the parent or guardian requesting the exemption.
2.  Enter the `date_of_birth` of the individual who is requesting the exemption.
3.  Provide the `reason_for_exemption` in the space provided.
4.  If a healthcare provider has been involved in the decision, provide their name in the `healthcare_provider` field.
5.  Check the `medical_certificate` field and select either 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' depending on the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian requesting the exemption.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual who is requesting the exemption.
* **Reason for Exemption**: Provide the reason why the individual is requesting exemption from the vaccine.
* **Healthcare Provider**: If a healthcare provider has been involved in the decision, enter their name here.
* **Medical Certificate**: Check if there is a medical certificate or reason for exemption.
