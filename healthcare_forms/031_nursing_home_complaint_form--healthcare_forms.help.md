# Nursing Home Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Home Complaint Form is a document used to report incidents or issues that have occurred in a nursing home. It's a tool for the facility's administration to gather information about the complaint and take necessary actions to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the nursing home where the complaint occurred.
2. Provide the name of the person complaining (e.g., resident, family member, staff).
3. Enter the name of the resident (if applicable).
4. Record the date the complaint was made.
5. Describe the issue or problem that led to the complaint.
6. Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss the complaint.
7. Enter the address of the nursing home where the complaint occurred.
8. Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for further communication).
9. Select the staff member(s) involved in the complaint.
10. Check the types of issues that apply to the complaint.
11. Enter any additional details about the complaint.
12. Describe the action taken to address the complaint or what is planned to be done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nursing Home**: <input name="nursing_home" type="text"> This field is used to specify the nursing home where the complaint occurred.
* **Complainant**: <input name="complainant" type="text"> This field is used to identify the person or entity making the complaint.
* **Resident Name**: <input name="resident_name" type="text"> This field is used to enter the name of the resident (if applicable).
* **Date of Complaint**: <input name="date_of_complaint" type="date"> This field is used to enter the date the complaint was made.
* **Description of Complaint**: <input name="description_of_complaint" type="text"> This field is used to provide a detailed description of the issue or problem that led to the complaint.
* **Contact Number**: <input name="contact_number" type="text"> This field is used to enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss the complaint.
* **Address**: <input name="address" type="text"> This field is used to enter the address of the nursing home where the complaint occurred.
* **Email**: <input name="email" type="email"> This field is used to enter your email address (optional, but recommended for further communication).
* **Staff Member**: <select name="staff_member" options="['Caregiver', 'Nurse', 'Physical Therapist', 'Physician', 'Other']" multiple> Select the staff member(s) involved in the complaint.
* **Complaint Type**: <select name="complaint_type" options="['Equipment', 'Facilities', 'Rules', 'Processes', 'Other']" multiple> Choose the type(s) of issues that apply to the complaint.
* **Complaint Details**: <input name="complaint_details" type="text"> This field is used to enter any additional information about the complaint.
* **Action Taken**: <input name="action_taken" type="text"> This field is used to describe the action taken or planned to address the complaint.
