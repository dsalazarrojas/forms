# Daily Encounter Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Encounter Log form is used to record and document daily interactions between patients and healthcare staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of interaction that occurred (Meeting, Visit, Incident, or Other).
2. Enter the date of the interaction.
3. Enter the time of the interaction.
4. Choose the staff member who was involved in the interaction.
5. Describe the interaction in the space provided.
6. If necessary, enter the location where the interaction took place.
7. Indicate whether follow-up is required (True or False).
8. Enter any additional notes about the interaction.
9. If applicable, enter the phone number of the patient.
10. If you need to reach the patient, enter their email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interaction Type** (`interaction_type`, select_one): Select the type of interaction that occurred (e.g., Meeting, Visit, Incident).
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date on which the interaction took place.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time at which the interaction took place.
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, select_one): Choose the healthcare staff member who was involved in the interaction.
* **Interaction Description** (`interaction_description`, text): Describe the interaction in this field.
* **Location** (`location`, text): If the interaction took place in a specific location, enter it here.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one): Indicate whether follow-up is required (True or False).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes about the interaction.
* **Email** (`email`, email): If you need to reach the patient, enter their email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): If applicable, enter the phone number of the patient.
