> **General Patient Information Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The General Patient Information Form is a comprehensive form used to collect essential information about a patient. This form helps gather detailed medical history, allergies, and personal details for effective patient care and treatment. By filling out this form, patients can provide accurate and up-to-date information about their medical conditions, medications, surgeries, and other relevant health aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the General Patient Information Form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the patient's name and social security number (if applicable).
2. Provide information about the patient's medical conditions, including any allergies or sensitivities.
3. List the patient's current medications.
4. Disclose any past or planned surgeries.
5. Enter any relevant medical history.
6. Report any allergies or sensitivities.
7. Provide the patient's family medical history.
8. Enter the patient's email address and phone number.
9. Enter the patient's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Medical Conditions** (`patient_medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing or has experienced in the past.
* **Patient Medications** (`patient_medications`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
* **Patient Health Habits** (`patient_health_habits`, text, optional): Describe the patient's daily habits and routines that may affect their health.
* **Patient Surgeries** (`patient_surgeries`, text, optional): Document any surgeries or medical procedures the patient has undergone or will undergo.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any chronic medical conditions, such as diabetes or heart disease.
* **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, text, optional): Describe any past or planned surgeries.
* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Report any significant medical history from the patient's family members.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's social security number (if applicable).
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, text, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
* Be thorough when listing medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
* If applicable, include any relevant medical history or family history.
* Double-check the patient's contact information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
