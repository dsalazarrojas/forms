# Patient Privacy Protection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and record patient information to protect their privacy and confidentiality during medical care. It helps healthcare providers understand and respect patients' preferences for their personal and medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your patient's **Patient ID** if it is available.
*   Select your desired **privacy level**: Choose between High (highly private) or Low (less private).
*   Enter your **date of birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
*   Enter your **social security number** for identification purposes.
*   Select one of your **phone numbers** (if multiple).
*   Enter your **email address** for secure communication.
*   Enter your **Address** for contact and visit purposes.
*   Select your preferred **date of visit**.
*   Provide the time of visit.
*   Enter your **Medical Record** for any previous medical history.
*   Enter your **Doctor's comments** or notes about your visit.
*   Enter the **Hospital Name** where you received care.
*   Enter your **Patient Name** for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient ID**: Enter your patient ID if you have one. If not, leave this field blank.
*   **Privacy Level**: Choose between High or Low. A High level means your personal and medical information will be kept private. A Low level means your information will be shared with medical professionals, but not with others.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
*   **Phone Number (2)**: If you have multiple phone numbers, select one of them.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for secure communication.
*   **Address**: Enter your address for contact and visit purposes.
*   **Date of Visit**: Select your preferred date of visit.
*   **Time**: Provide the time of visit.
*   **Medical Record**: Enter your medical record for any previous medical history.
*   **Doctor Comments**: Enter your doctor's comments or notes about your visit.
*   **Hospital Name**: Enter the hospital name where you received care.
*   **Patient Name**: Enter your patient name for identification purposes.
