# Depression Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to screen for depression symptoms in patients, helping healthcare providers assess their mental health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully about your symptoms over the last two weeks.
2. Select the correct response for each question.
3. Enter your email address and other relevant patient information.
4. Fill in any additional comments or questions you may have.
5. Confirm that you have completed the survey by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please read this short introduction before starting the survey. 
* **How many days in the last 2 weeks did you feel sad, empty, or depressed?**: This question asks about the duration and intensity of depressive feelings over the last two weeks.
* **Have you experienced a significant change in appetite?**: This question assesses changes in eating habits related to mood or emotional state.
* **How many hours of sleep do you usually get per night?**: This question investigates sleep patterns and potential insomnia or oversleeping.
* **Have you experienced a loss of pleasure in doing things?**: This question probes for a decrease in enjoyment or satisfaction from activities.
* **Have you experienced a loss of interest in activities?**: This question evaluates changes in motivation and interest in daily activities.
* **Have you experienced significant weight loss or gain?**: This question explores the relationship between mood and changes in weight.
* **Have you experienced a loss of energy?**: This question examines decreased energy levels or fatigue.
* **Have you felt hopeless?**: This question assesses feelings of despair or desperation.
* **Have you felt restless?**: This question investigates agitation or restlessness.
* **Have you felt worth-less?**: This question probes for feelings of guilt or self-worthlessness.
* **Have you experienced difficulty concentrating?**: This question examines attention and concentration issues.
* **Have you experienced difficulty making decisions?**: This question assesses decision-making ability.
* **Have you experienced feelings of worthlessness?**: This question explores feelings of self-worth or guilt.
* **Have you experienced thoughts of death or suicidal thoughts?**: This question investigates self-harm or suicidal ideation.
* **Additional comments or questions?**: This section allows you to add any additional information or questions you may have.
* **Patient email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with further information.
* **Patient information**: Please provide some background information about yourself.
* **Healthcare provider information**: Please provide information about your healthcare provider.
* **Follow-up information**: This section is optional; fill it in if you have any further appointments or information.
* **Survey completed by**: Please confirm the person who completed the survey.
* **Date survey completed**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time survey completed**: Enter the time you completed this survey.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you have finished the survey.
