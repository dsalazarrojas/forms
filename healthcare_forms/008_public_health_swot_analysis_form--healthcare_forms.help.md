# Public Health SWOT Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Health SWOT Analysis Form is designed to help healthcare professionals identify and analyze the internal and external factors that affect public health policies, programs, and services. SWOT stands for Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. This form is used to gather information and insights from healthcare experts to inform decision-making and improve public health services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go through each section (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) and provide your honest and detailed answers.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing each factor.
3. Use the space provided for each section to write your thoughts and insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Strengths
* **Strengths** (`Strengths`, `text`, `required`): Describe the internal factors that are favorable to public health policies, programs, and services. This could include strong partnerships, effective communication channels, or well-established processes.

### Weaknesses
* **Weaknesses** (`Weaknesses`, `text`, `required`): Identify the internal factors that are unfavorable or detrimental to public health policies, programs, and services. This could include limited resources, inadequate funding, or lack of expertise.

### Opportunities
* **Opportunities** (`Opportunities`, `text`, `required`): Describe the external factors that can be leveraged to improve public health policies, programs, and services. This could include new technologies, changing social trends, or increased funding.

### Threats
* **Threats** (`Threats`, `text`, `required`): Identify the external factors that could negatively impact public health policies, programs, and services. This could include natural disasters, economic downturns, or changing government regulations.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding public health policies, programs, and services. This is an optional field for you to share any extra insights or ideas you may have.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing each factor.
- Use concrete examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
- If unsure about any factor, please provide your best effort to identify it.
