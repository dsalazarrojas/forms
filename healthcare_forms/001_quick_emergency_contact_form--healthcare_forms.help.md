# quick_emergency_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "quick_emergency_contact_form" is designed to collect contact information for emergency contacts, such as family members, friends, or coworkers, in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact type (e.g., Employee, Patient, Family Member, Friend, or Other).
2. Enter the name of the emergency contact.
3. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
4. Enter the email of the emergency contact.
5. Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Family, Friend, Coworker, or Other).
6. If the relationship is "Other," enter the specific relationship in the "Other Relationship" field.
7. Enter the address of the emergency contact.
8. Enter the city of the emergency contact.
9. Enter the state of the emergency contact.
10. Enter the zip code of the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter the contact information.
	+ **Contact Type**: Choose the type of relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Employee, Patient, Family Member, Friend, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Family, Friend, Coworker, or Other).
* **Other Relationship**: If you selected "Other," enter the specific relationship in this field.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **City**: Enter the city of the emergency contact.
* **State**: Enter the state of the emergency contact.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the emergency contact.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Save and submit the form when complete.
