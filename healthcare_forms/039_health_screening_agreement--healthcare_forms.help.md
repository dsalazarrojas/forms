# Health Screening Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document employee health information, including medical conditions, medical history, and other relevant details. It is likely used in a workplace setting to screen employees for potential health risks or to gather information for employee benefits or insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details, including your name and employee ID.
2. Select any health conditions or medical issues you may have.
3. Check if you agree to the health screening process.
4. Sign and date the form.
5. If a manager is required to sign, fill in their information and have them sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`): Please fill in your employee details, including your name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Select the methods of contact you prefer to use (phone, email, address, or fax).
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`): Choose any health conditions or medical issues you may have, such as Allergies, High Blood Pressure, or Asthma.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Select any medical issues or conditions you may have, similar to `health_conditions`.
* **Family Medical History** (`family_medical_history`): Choose any medical conditions or issues that may be present in your family history.
* **Health Screening Agreement 2** (`health_screening_agreement_2`): Indicate whether you have read, understand, and agree to the health screening process.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the health screening process.
* **Date** (`date`): Fill in the date of employment, last evaluation, or other relevant dates.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Fill in your employee ID number.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`): If required, sign and date this section to verify the data.
* **Manager Signature Type** (`manager_signature_type`): Choose whether you are signing as Manager or using initials.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Fill in your manager's name.
* **Department** (`department`): Fill in your department name.
* **Position** (`position`): Fill in your job position.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Fill in your name.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`): Fill in your manager's ID number.
* **Date of Employment** (`date_employment`): Fill in your date of employment.
* **Last Evaluation Date** (`last_evaluation`): Fill in your last evaluation date.
* **Manager Date** (`manager_date`): If required, fill in the date your manager signed the form.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`): Choose whether you are a full-time, part-time, contract, or temporary employee.
* **Manager** (`manager`): This field is required for managers to sign and verify the data.

Note: Some fields are not required, but please fill them in according to your needs and requirements.
