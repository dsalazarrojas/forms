# Health Advisory Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Advisory Communication Form is designed to collect relevant information from patients or individuals about their health status, medical history, and medical conditions. This information can be used by healthcare professionals to assess and address any potential health concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **patient information** (page 1)
	* Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information in the "patient info" field.
2. Share your **medical history** (page 1)
	* Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
3. List your **symptoms** (page 1)
	* Describe any current symptoms or health concerns you are experiencing.
4. Share your **treatment plan** (page 1)
	* Outline any treatments, medications, or therapies you are currently undergoing.
5. List your **medications** (page 1)
	* Enter any medications, supplements, or vitamins you are currently taking.
6. Share your **allergies** (page 1)
	* List any allergies, intolerances, or sensitivities you have.
7. Describe your **medical conditions** (page 1)
	* List any medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or other health conditions you have.
8. Share your **lab results** (page 1)
	* Enter any recent lab results or test results related to your health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (patient info)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History (medical history)**: Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
* **Symptoms (symptoms)**: Describe any current symptoms or health concerns you are experiencing.
* **Treatment Plan (treatment plan)**: Outline any treatments, medications, or therapies you are currently undergoing.
* **Medications (medications)**: Enter any medications, supplements, or vitamins you are currently taking.
* **Allergies (allergies)**: List any allergies, intolerances, or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Conditions (medical conditions)**: List any medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or other health conditions you have.
* **Lab Results (lab results)**: Enter any recent lab results or test results related to your health.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when completing this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the healthcare professional who provided you with this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the healthcare professional who provided you with this form.
* This form is for your health and well-being. Please take care of yourself and your health.
