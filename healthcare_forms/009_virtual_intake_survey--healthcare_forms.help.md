# Virtual Intake Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Virtual Intake Survey is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare professionals better understand their needs and preferences. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide your name and contact information
* Share your date of birth
* Indicate your preferred session days
* Confirm or decline consent for contact
* Choose a preferred session time

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred session days from the list in the "Preferred Session Days" field.
6. Confirm your consent for contact by selecting "True" in the "Consent" field.
7. Choose your preferred session time from the available options in the "Session Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Intake Survey Form**: A text field to enter a brief description of your virtual intake survey. (Optional)
* **Name**: Enter your name. (Optional)
*	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (Optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Optional)
* **Preferred Session Days**: Select the days you prefer for your sessions. Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. (Optional)
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent for contact by selecting "True". (Optional)
* **Session Time**: Choose your preferred session time. (Optional)

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
* We will use the information collected from this form to tailor our services to your needs and preferences.
* You can withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time.
* We will keep your personal data confidential and secure.
