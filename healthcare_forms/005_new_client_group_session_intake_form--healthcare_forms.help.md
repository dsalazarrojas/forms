# New Client Group Session Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect client details and health history for new client group sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page and fill out the required information for each field.
2. Review and confirm the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (<font face="Arial" style="font-weight:normal; font-size:10"> (text field)**: This field is used to collect general client information.
* **Health History** (<font face="Arial" style="font-weight:normal; font-size:10"> (text area)**: This field is used to provide a brief overview of the client's medical history.
* **Consent** (<font face="Arial" style="font-weight:normal; font-size:10"> (select multiple)**: Please select all that apply regarding consent for the client group sessions. 

## Tips

* Ensure that you submit the form with accurate and complete information.
* Please note that this form is not intended for emergency or urgent situations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare professionals administering the client group sessions.
