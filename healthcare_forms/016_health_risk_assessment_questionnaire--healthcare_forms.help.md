# health_risk_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a patient's health risk factors, including their medical history, allergies, and current health status. It is intended to be completed by a healthcare professional for accurate and thorough evaluation of the patient's condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Enter your information in the designated fields.
3. Make sure to review and revise your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (Patient Identification, text, optional)**: Please enter your name and any other identifying information required for your healthcare records.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details, text, optional)**: Enter your contact information so that your healthcare provider can reach you for any further questions or concerns.
* **Risk Factors (Risk Factors, select multiple, optional)**: Select all the risk factors that apply to your current health status.
	+ Yes: You have experienced a risk factor.
	+ No: You have not experienced a risk factor.
* **Medical History (Medical History, text, optional)**: Provide detailed information about your medical history, including any previous health issues or medical conditions.
* **Family Medical History (Family Medical History, text, optional)**: Enter any information about your family medical history, including any inherited or genetic conditions.
* **Medications (Medications, text, optional)**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies (Allergies, text, optional)**: Enter any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions, text, optional)**: List any current or past medical conditions you have.
* **Risk Score (Risk Score, select one, optional)**: Select whether you have experienced any risk factors.
	+ Yes: You have experienced a risk factor.
	+ No: You have not experienced a risk factor.
* **Patient Outcome (Patient Outcome, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your last follow-up appointment or any other relevant dates.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can complete them as needed. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider for clarification.
