# General Health Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather general health information from patients or users to assess their overall health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each section of the form to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all questions and fill out each section completely and accurately.
3. Select the appropriate options from the drop-down menus for select_multiple and select_one fields.
4. Enter your information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Medical History**
Medical history is a critical component of this form, where you are required to describe any significant medical events or experiences you have had in the past.

### **Allergies**
This section is used to list any allergies you may have, including food allergies, medicinal allergies, or other allergies that may affect your overall health.

### **Medications**
Please list any medications you are currently taking, including prescription medications, over-the-counter medications, and herbal supplements.

### **Medical Conditions**
Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the drop-down menu.

### **Contact Info**
Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.

### **Overall Health**
Select your overall health status from the drop-down menu.

### **Health Risks**
This section is used to list any health risks you may have, including risks to your physical health or safety.

### **Health Practices**
Enter any health practices or habits you have, such as regular exercise, diet, or other healthy habits.

### **Health Status**
Enter your current health status in this section.

### **Medical History 2-10**
Please note that Medical History 2-10 are repeated fields for medical history and health risks, and you are required to fill out each section completely and accurately.

### **Health Risks 2-7**
Please note that Health Risks 2-7 are repeated fields for health risks, and you are required to fill out each section completely and accurately.
