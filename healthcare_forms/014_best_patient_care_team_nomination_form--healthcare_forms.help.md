# Best Patient Care Team Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and recognition for outstanding patient care teams in healthcare facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct relationships and ratings for each question.
3. Provide additional comments or feedback as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Your Relationship to Patient** (`nominator_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how you are related to the patient (Patient, Family Member, Friend, Healthcare Colleague, Other).
* **Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up questions.
* **Healthcare Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required): Enter the city and state of the healthcare facility.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department or unit where you received care from (e.g. Emergency Department, Intensive Care Unit).
* **Care Team Member Names** (`team_member_names`, text, required): List the names of team members you would like to nominate.
* **Period of Care** (`care_period`, text, required): Enter the duration of your care from this team.
* **Overall Care Quality Rating** (`care_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of care you received (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Compassion and Empathy Rating** (`compassion_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how compassionate and empathetic the care team was (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the care team communicated with you (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Responsiveness Rating** (`responsiveness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how responsive the care team was to your needs (Very Responsive, Mostly Responsive, Somewhat Responsive, Slow to Respond, Unresponsive).
* **Professionalism Rating** (`professionalism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the care team's professionalism (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Standout Qualities** (`standout_qualities`, select_multiple, required): Select qualities that made this care team exceptional (e.g., Compassionate Care, Clear Communication, Quick Response Time).
* **Describe Your Care Experience** (`care_experience`, text, required): Share details about your experience with this care team.
* **How Did This Team Make a Difference** (`impact_description`, text, required): Describe the positive impact on patient outcomes or experience.
* **Would You Recommend This Care Team** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): State if you would recommend this team to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or recognition you would like to share.
