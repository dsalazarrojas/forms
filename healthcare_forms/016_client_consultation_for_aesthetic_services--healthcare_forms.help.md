# Client Consultation For Aesthetic Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients regarding their aesthetic services consultation. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the consultation process is conducted efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully, and if you're unsure about any field, please leave a note or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (client_first_name, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (client_last_name, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (client_email, email, required): Provide your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (client_phone, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (gender, select_one, required): Select your gender from the available options.
* **Occupation** (occupation, text, optional): Enter your current job or occupation.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (referral_source, select_one, required): Select how you heard about us.
* **Services You Are Interested In** (service_interest, select_multiple, required): Select all the aesthetic services you are interested in.
* **Primary Skin or Aesthetic Concern** (primary_concern, text, required): Enter the main issue you'd like to address.
* **Skin Type** (skin_type, select_one, optional): If known, select your skin type.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, optional): Enter any diagnosed medical conditions.
* **Medications** (medications, text, optional): Include any medications or supplements you're currently taking.
* **Allergies** (allergies, text, optional): List any medication, food, or environmental allergies.
* **Have You Had Aesthetic Treatments Before?** (previous_aesthetic_treatments, select_one, required): Select if you have had aesthetic treatments before.
* **Previous Treatments Details** (previous_treatments_detail, text, optional): List any previous treatments and dates.
* **Do You Smoke?** (smoking_status, select_one, required): Select your smoking status.
* **Are You Pregnant or Breastfeeding?** (pregnancy_status, select_one, required): Select your pregnancy status.
* **Do You Have a History of Keloid Scarring?** (keloid_scarring, select_one, required): Select if you have a history of keloid scarring.
* **Do You Have Any Autoimmune Disorders?** (autoimmune_disorders, select_one, required): Select if you have any autoimmune disorders.
* **What Are Your Treatment Goals?** (treatment_goals, text, required): Enter your desired treatment outcomes.
* **Budget Range for Treatment** (budget_range, select_one, optional): Select your estimated spending range.
* **How Much Downtime Can You Accept?** (downtime_tolerance, select_one, required): Select your downtime tolerance.
* **I Consent to Skin Examination and Consultation** (consent_to_examination, select_one, required): Select to confirm consent to examination and consultation.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate** (information_accuracy, select_one, required): Select to confirm the truthfulness of the provided information.
* **Consultation Date** (consultation_date, date, required): Enter the date of the consultation.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, please leave a note or ask for clarification.
* Make sure to review and confirm the provided information before submitting the form.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform the aesthetic professional about them.
* The aesthetic professional will review and discuss your information with you before proceeding with any treatment.
