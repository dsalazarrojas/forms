# Emergency Care Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about an individual's emergency care plan. It helps healthcare professionals quickly and accurately assess a patient's situation and respond effectively in critical situations. Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are filled out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully**: Take a few moments to read through the form and understand its layout.
2. **Fill out the General Information page**: Answer the questions on this page, providing as much detail as possible.
3. **List the patient's medical conditions**: Describe any existing medical conditions that may impact their care in an emergency.
4. **Identify emergency contacts**: Choose at least one primary contact, and optionally, select secondary contacts and/or a doctor's name and phone number.
5. **Specify medications**: Choose any medications currently being taken by the patient.
6. **Submit the form**: Once complete, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **Name** (`text`, **Required**): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`text`, **Required**): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`text`, **Required**): Enter the patient's home address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, **Required**): Enter the patient's phone number.

### Patient Medical Conditions (Page 2)
* **List Medical Conditions** (`text`, **Required**): Provide a detailed description of any existing medical conditions that may impact their care in an emergency.

### Emergency Contacts (Page 3)
* **Primary Contact** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): Select at least one contact who should be notified in an emergency. This could be a family member, caregiver, or friend.
* **Secondary Contact** (`select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select additional contacts who should be notified in case the primary contact is unavailable.
* **Doctor's Name** (`select_multiple`, **Optional**): Enter the name of the patient's doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone Number** (`select_multiple`, **Optional**): Enter the doctor's phone number.

### Medications (Page 4)
* **Medications** (`select_one`, **Required**): Select any medications currently being taken by the patient. Choose from the options provided:
	+ Aspirin
	+ Blood Pressure Medication
	+ Insuline
	+ None
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ Oxygen
