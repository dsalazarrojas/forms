# Maternity Support Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Maternity Support Registration form is used to collect information from expectant mothers to provide personalized support during their pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Clinic Name** where the expectant mother is receiving support.
2. Enter the **Doula's Name** responsible for providing support.
3. Enter the **Email** of the expectant mother, if available.
4. Enter the **Phone Number** of the expectant mother, if available.
5. Select the **Maternity Status** from the options: Expecting a baby, Pregnant, Just gave birth, or Postpartum.
6. If necessary, describe the **Support Needs** of the expectant mother.
7. Enter a **Follow-up Date** for any further support sessions, if applicable.
8. Enter a **Follow-up Time** for any further support sessions, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the clinic where the expectant mother is receiving support.
* **Doula's Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the doula responsible for providing support.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the expectant mother (if available).
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the expectant mother (if available).
* **Maternity Status** (Select One, Required): Select the current maternity status of the expectant mother.
* **Support Needs** (Text, Optional): If necessary, describe the specific support needs of the expectant mother.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Enter a date for any further support sessions, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Time** (Time, Optional): Enter a time for any further support sessions, if applicable.
