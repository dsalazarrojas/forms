# Sleeping Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sleeping Questionnaire form is designed to collect information about an individual's sleep habits and medical history. The form is intended to be completed by individuals who are seeking to assess their sleep quality and identify any potential sleep-related issues or disorders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out each field accurately and completely.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help assess your sleep habits and medical history.
3. If a field is optional, you may leave it blank if it is not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sleep history** (text): Describe your typical sleep schedule, including your bedtime, wake-up time, and any regular sleep patterns you have.
* **sleep issues** (text): List any sleep-related issues you are experiencing, such as insomnia, sleep apnea, or other sleep disorders.
* **medical history** (text): Provide a brief medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or other health concerns that may impact your sleep.
* **medications** (text): List any medications you are currently taking that may affect your sleep.
* **sleep patterns** (text): Describe your regular sleep patterns, including your sleeping and waking hours, and any unusual sleep patterns you experience.
* **sleep quality** (text): Evaluate your current sleep quality, using numbers or descriptive terms (e.g., "good," "average," or "poor").
* **sleep schedule** (text): Describe your typical daily sleep schedule, including work or school hours, meal times, and other factors that impact your sleep.
* **snoring** (select_multiple): Select all that apply for any snoring that occurs:
	+ True: You experience snoring regularly.
	+ False: You do not experience snoring.
	+ Rarely: You sometimes experience snoring.
* **sleep disorders** (select_one): Select one from the options:
	+ Insomnia: Persistent difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep.
	+ Sleep Apnea: Pauses in breathing during sleep, often causing snoring or gasping.
	+ Other: Describe any other sleep disorder you are experiencing.
* **sleep disorder description** (text): If you selected "other" for sleep disorders, provide a detailed description of your condition.
* **sleep_disorder_description**: This field is only visible when you select "Other" for sleep disorders.
