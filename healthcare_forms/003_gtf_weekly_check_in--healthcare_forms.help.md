# gtf_weekly_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and progress updates from individuals on a weekly basis, allowing coaches to track progress and receive feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Coach Name** as prompted.
2. If you have an email address, you can optionally provide it.
3. Enter your **Progress Update** in the designated field.
4. Select your **Overall Feeling** from the provided options (good, okay, bad, or very bad).
5. If you have any additional comments, you can provide them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your ID or documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your ID or documents.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, enter it here. This is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer.
* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of your coach or mentor who is guiding you.
* **Progress Update**: Provide a brief description of your progress towards your goal or project.
* **Overall Feeling**: Select how you're feeling about your current situation. The options are:
	+ **good**
	+ **okay**
	+ **bad**
	+ **very bad**
* **What is your feedback to your coach?**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have for your coach.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback you have, if any.
