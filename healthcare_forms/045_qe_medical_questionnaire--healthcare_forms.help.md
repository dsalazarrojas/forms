# QeMedicalQuestionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The QeMedicalQuestionnaire form is designed to collect patient information for medical purposes, including medical questionnaires, medical history, patient identifiers, medical procedures, medical test results, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the relevant fields as necessary.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect and handle patient data.
3. Enter relevant information for each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Questionnaire** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical questionnaires or assessments that have been conducted on the patient.
* **Medical History** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history or allergies that may be applicable.
* **Patient Identifiers** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any patient identifiers, such as ID numbers, dates of birth, or other relevant information.
* **Patient Medical History** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if the patient has a medical history (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Medical Procedure** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical procedures or treatments that have been or will be conducted on the patient.
* **Medical Diagnosis** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if the patient has any medical diagnoses (`Yes` or `No` for each option).
* **Medical Treatment** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if the patient has received any medical treatment (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Patient Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient.
* **Contact Details** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter any contact information for the patient or their representatives.
* **Medical Test Results** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if the patient has received any medical test results (`Yes` or `No` for each option).
* **Contact Details 1-7** (`11-17`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the patient or their representatives.
* **Medical Procedure 1-4** (`14-16`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional medical procedures or treatments that have been or will be conducted on the patient.
* **Medical Test Results 1-2** (`21-22`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional medical test results for the patient.

Please note that some fields are not required, and you should only fill them in if they are relevant to the patient's case. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your supervisor or medical authority.
