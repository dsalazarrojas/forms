# Aesthetic Note - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Aesthetic Note" form is designed for aesthetic medical practitioners to document patient information and treatment outcomes after aesthetic procedures. It captures essential details regarding patient specifics, skin analysis, treatment procedures, and post-treatment follow-up plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Start by filling out the patient's personal details, including **Patient Name**, **Date of Birth**, and **Treatment Date**. These are mandatory fields.
2. **Skin Analysis**: After collecting the patient's basic information, move on to the **Skin Analysis / Concern** section, which is also required for a comprehensive understanding of the patient's condition and the treatment's focus.
3. **Procedure Details**: Document the specific **Procedure Performed**, **Product Used**, and other detailed information about the treatment, including **Lot / Batch Number** and **Dosage / Units / Volume**. Ensure that **Adverse Events** is filled out accurately, noting any complications or reactions observed.
4. **Post-Procedure Follow-up**: Finally, document the **Immediate Outcome** and any follow-up plans in the **Follow-up Plan** section. This is optional but essential for ongoing care and future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: A field for the patient's name to ensure accurate and personal addressing.
* **Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth for reference and identification.
* **Treatment Date**: The date the treatment was performed for accurate tracking and record-keeping.
* **Practitioner Name**: The name of the practitioner performing the aesthetic procedure.
* **Skin Analysis / Concern**: A detailed description of the patient's skin type and concerns, including any notable observations or issues.
* **Fitzpatrick Skin Type**: A selection of Fitzpatrick skin types for classification.
* **Procedure Performed**: A selection of specific procedures that were undertaken (e.g., Botox, fillers, laser treatments).
* **Product Used**: The brand and name of the product used for the procedure.
* **Lot / Batch Number**: A tracking number for the product used in the procedure.
* **Dosage / Units / Volume**: A field for specifying the dosage, units, or volume of the product used.
* **Treatment Area / Injection Sites**: A description of where the treatment was applied.
* **Immediate Outcome**: An account of the patient's immediate reaction or outcome after the treatment.
* **Adverse Events**: A selection or text field for documenting any complications or reactions observed during or after the treatment.
* **Follow-up Plan**: A field for outlining any follow-up plans or next steps recommended for the patient.

This guide is created to ensure that users understand the purpose and usage of each field within the "Aesthetic Note" form, making it easier for them to collect and document the necessary information accurately and effectively.
