# Hand Hygiene Competency Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate hand hygiene competency among healthcare workers. The form is intended to be a self-assessment tool for workers to evaluate their hand hygiene practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each section according to your understanding of hand hygiene practices.
2. Complete each field based on your personal experience and knowledge.
3. Please note that some fields are optional, and you are not required to fill them out if you are unsure or do not have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Preparation** (Patient Preparation, note, optional): This section asks you to describe how you prepare patients for hand hygiene procedures. Please write a short answer explaining your current practices.
* **Hand Preparation** (Hand Preparation, note, optional): In this section, describe how you maintain your hands before performing hand hygiene procedures. Be as specific as possible about your hand preparation methods.

* **Patient Transportation** (Patient Transportation, note, optional): If you transport patients, describe your practices for maintaining hand hygiene during transport.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of hand hygiene, it's okay to ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Remember that hand hygiene is a critical aspect of maintaining a clean and safe environment for patients.
* This form is a self-assessment tool, and your responses will be kept confidential.
