# Surgical Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for referral to a surgical specialist for a patient's treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the patient information page (page 1).
* Select all relevant medical histories that apply to the patient's condition.
* Provide referral information, including any relevant details about the patient's condition.
* Fill out the insurance information page (page 2) with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care (page 3).
* Obtain patient consent and sign the form (page 4).
* Select the submit option to complete the form, or cancel if you need to make changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant medical histories that apply to the patient's condition, such as Cardiac, Pulmonary, Gastrointestinal, etc.
* **Referral Info**: (`referral_info`, text, required): Provide details about the reason for the referral, including any relevant medical information.
* **Surgical Info**: (`surgical_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the patient's surgical needs or preferences.
* **Insurance Info**: (`insurance_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's insurance information, including any relevant policy numbers or IDs.
* **Additional Info**: (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* **Patient Consent**: (`consent`, note, required): Obtain the patient's consent for the referral process.
* **Submit**: (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select the "Submit" option to complete the form, or "Cancel" to make changes.
* **Patient Signature**: (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the patient's signature, but only if required by your organization's policies.
* **Healthcare Provider Info**: (`healthcare_provider_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the healthcare provider, but only if required.
* **Patient Signature Image**: (`patient_signature_image`, text, optional): Upload a signature image, but only if required.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's signature, but only if required.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to contact the form's maintainers at `form@gic.mx`.
