# Financial Insurance Application Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a financial insurance application. It is intended for individuals or entities looking to apply for financial insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are necessary for a complete application.
2. Provide the requested information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Financial Insurance Request Form
* **Financial Insurance Request Form** (\*) This is the main section of the form. Enter a brief description of the reason for your financial insurance application.
* **Loan Amount** (\*) Enter the amount of money being requested for financial insurance.
* **Credit Line Request** (Select at least one option) Choose the type of credit line being requested.
* **Loan Interest Rate** Enter the interest rate for the loan.
* **Loan Term** (*) Enter the length of time the loan is being requested for.
* **Employment Status** (Select one option) Indicate your employment status.
* **Income Level** Enter your current income level.
* **Loan Fees** Enter any additional fees associated with the loan.
* **Loan Terms and Conditions** Enter any special terms or conditions of the loan.
* **Financial Institutions** (Select one option) Choose a financial institution to handle the loan.
* **Loan Decision** Enter any additional information about the loan decision process.
* **Notes and Comments** Enter any additional comments or notes about the application.
### Page 2
* **Employment Status Details** Enter additional details about your employment status.
* **Income Proof** Enter proof of income.
* **Employment Proof** Enter proof of employment.
* **Employment Verification** Enter verification of employment.
* **Loan Application Date** (*) Enter the date the loan application was submitted.
* **Financial Institution** Enter the name of the financial institution handling the loan.
* **Loan Agreed** Enter the terms of the loan agreement.
* **Loan Approved** Enter the loan status.
* **Financial Insurance Provider** Enter the provider of the financial insurance.
* **Loan Fees Details** Enter any additional fees details.
* **Employment Type** Enter your employment type.
* **Loan Status** Enter the current loan status.
* **Loan Application Status** Enter the current loan application status.
* **Loan Application Status** Enter the current loan application status.
