# Client Satisfaction Survey AH - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their recent visit to a healthcare facility, allowing us to gauge overall satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all necessary information and documentation ready, such as your Patient ID (if you have one).
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If a question does not apply to your visit, select "Not Applicable" or "Did Not Receive Instructions" as needed.
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the "Additional Comments or Concerns" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Satisfaction Survey** (1):
	+ A brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Patient ID** (2):
	+ A unique identifier assigned to you for your visit.
	+ If you don't have a Patient ID, please leave this field blank.
* **Date of Visit** (3):
	+ The date of your visit to the healthcare facility.
	+ This is a required field.
* **Department Visited** (4):
	+ The department or specialty you visited during your appointment.
	+ Select the department that best matches your visit.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (5):
	+ The name of the healthcare provider who attended to your needs.
	+ If you don't know the provider's name, please leave this field blank.
* **Appointment Type** (6):
	+ The type of appointment you had with the healthcare provider.
	+ Select the type that best describes your visit.
* **How Would You Rate the Wait Time** (7):
	+ Rate the time it took for you to receive care or attention from the healthcare provider.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Approximate Wait Time** (8):
	+ Estimate the total time you spent waiting before seeing the healthcare provider.
	+ Select the range that best represents your experience.
* **How Would You Rate Facility Cleanliness** (9):
	+ Rate the cleanliness and organization of the facility.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Would You Rate Staff Friendliness** (10):
	+ Rate the friendliness and courtesy of the healthcare staff.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Would You Rate Staff Professionalism** (11):
	+ Rate the professionalism and expertise of the healthcare staff.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Did the Provider Listen to Your Concerns** (12):
	+ Rate how well the healthcare provider listened to your concerns or questions.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Did the Provider Explain Things Clearly** (13):
	+ Rate how well the healthcare provider explained your diagnosis, treatment, or care plan.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Did the Provider Treat You with Respect** (14):
	+ Rate how you were treated by the healthcare provider.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Did You Feel You Had Enough Time with the Provider** (15):
	+ Rate how much time you felt you had with the healthcare provider.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Would You Rate the Overall Quality of Care** (16):
	+ Rate the overall quality of care you received during your visit.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Well Was Your Pain or Discomfort Managed** (17):
	+ Rate how well your pain or discomfort was managed during your visit.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Were Discharge Instructions Clear** (18):
	+ Rate how clear the instructions given to you after your visit were.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Clear Was the Billing Information** (19):
	+ Rate how clear the billing information was.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Overall How Satisfied Are You with Your Visit** (20):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Likely Are You to Return for Future Care** (21):
	+ Rate how likely you are to return to the healthcare facility for future care.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Our Facility to Others** (22):
	+ Rate how likely you are to recommend the healthcare facility to others.
	+ Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **What Was the Best Aspect of Your Visit** (23):
	+ Describe the best part of your visit.
	+ Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **What Areas Need Improvement** (24):
	+ Describe areas where we could improve.
	+ Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (25):
	+ Share any additional comments or concerns you may have.
	+ Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up** (26):
	+ Select whether you give permission to contact you for follow-up regarding your feedback.
	+ Please select "True" if you are willing to be contacted.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
