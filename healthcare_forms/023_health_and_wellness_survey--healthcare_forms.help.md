# Health And Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health and wellness to help healthcare providers better understand your situation. Your answers will be used to create a comprehensive picture of your health and wellness, which will aid in providing you with more effective care.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any medical conditions or health concerns, please list them in the text fields provided.
4. If you have any medical professionals you are seeing, please list their names and contact information.
5. If you have any additional comments or information, please share them in the text fields provided.
6. When you have completed the form, review it carefully and submit it to your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **main_information** (Required: Optional)
	+ This is the first section of the form where you can provide any general information about your health and wellness.
* **medical_professionals** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can list any medical professionals you are currently seeing, such as your primary care physician or specialist.
* **health_conditions** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can list any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with or are currently experiencing, such as heart conditions or diabetes.
* **lifestyle_questions** (Required: Optional)
	+ This section is where you can answer questions about your lifestyle habits, such as your exercise frequency and diet.
* **exercise_frequency** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select how often you exercise per week, which can help healthcare providers understand your physical activity level.
* **diet_questions** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can provide any additional information about your diet, such as any food allergies or sensitivities.
* **health_behaviors** (Required: Optional)
	+ This section is where you can list any health behaviors you have, such as smoking or substance use.
* **health_outcomes** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is for any information about your health outcomes, such as hospitalizations or medications.
* **healthcare_coverage** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can select whether you have healthcare coverage, such as insurance or a medical savings plan.
* **medical_history** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide your medical history, including any past illnesses or surgeries.
* **health_insights** (Required: Optional)
	+ This section is where you can share any additional insights about your health and wellness.
* **additional_comments** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can add any additional comments or information about your health and wellness.
* **healthcare_provider** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is for the contact information of your healthcare provider, such as their name and phone number.
* **patient_contact_info** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can provide your contact information, such as your phone number and email.
* **healthcare_provider_contact_info** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is for the contact information of your healthcare provider, such as their address and phone number.
* **patient_symptoms** (Required: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can list any symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* **medical_conditions** (Required: Optional)
	+ In this field, you can provide any additional information about your medical conditions, such as any medications or treatments.
* **health_insurance_provider** (Hidden Field, Not Shown in Form)
	+ (This field is not visible in the form but is used to store information about your health insurance provider.)

## Tips
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider.
3. Keep in mind that this form is not intended to diagnose or treat any medical conditions.
