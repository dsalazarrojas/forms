# no_show_policy_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's no-show policy, including business information, customer information, policy details, no-show reasons, business signature, no-show policy date, and no-show policy time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's **Business Information** in the text field on Page 1.
2. Enter the patient's **Customer Information** in the text field on Page 1.
3. Provide the **Policy Details** in the text field on Page 1.
4. Select one or more **No Show Reasons** from the drop-down list on Page 2.
5. Sign and date the **Business Signature** on Page 2.
6. Enter the **No Show Policy Date** using the date picker on Page 3.
7. Enter the **No Show Policy Time** using the time picker on Page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Information** (Page 1, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the patient's business information.
* **Customer Information** (Page 1, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the patient's customer information.
* **Policy Details** (Page 1, text, Optional):
	+ Provide the details about the policy.
* **No Show Reasons** (Page 2, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select one or more reasons for the patient's no-show status.
* **Business Signature** (Page 2, text, Optional):
	+ Sign and date this field with the business signature.
* **No Show Policy Date** (Page 3, date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date of the no-show policy.
* **No Show Policy Time** (Page 3, time, Optional):
	+ Enter the time of the no-show policy.
 
## Tips
- Ensure that the patient's no-show status is not already documented in their chart.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your supervisor or the healthcare organization's support team.
