# Agreement For Long Term Controlled Substance Prescriptions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to agree to long-term controlled substance prescriptions. It ensures that you understand and agree to take your medication as prescribed, store it securely, and notify your physician of any changes in your condition or side effects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your personal details, such as full name, date of birth, address, and phone number.
2. Provide your prescribing physician's name and the name of the medical practice or clinic.
3. Enter the controlled substance prescribed and its dosage amount.
4. Specify the frequency of use and treatment duration.
5. Agree to follow the prescription directions, not share the medication with others, store it securely, and notify your physician of any changes.
6. Confirm your understanding of the potential risks and benefits of long-term controlled substance use and the possibility of addiction or dependence.
7. Acknowledge your agreement to undergo urine screening tests as required by your physician.
8. Confirm that you will receive this controlled substance from only one prescriber and not request early refills.
9. Provide a digital signature indicating your agreement to these terms.
10. Enter the date you are signing this agreement.
11. Your physician will sign this agreement, acknowledging your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name (1)**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Patient Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Patient Address (3)**: Enter your current address.
* **Patient Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Prescribing Physician Name (5)**: Enter the name of your prescribing physician.
* **Medical Practice/Clinic (6)**: Enter the name of the medical practice or clinic.
* **-- Medication Details -- (7)**: This is a header for the following fields, which capture information about the medication you have been prescribed.
* **Controlled Substance Prescribed (8)**: Enter the name of the controlled substance prescribed.
* **Dosage Amount (9)**: Enter the dosage amount prescribed.
* **Frequency of Use (10)**: Enter how often you should take the medication.
* **Treatment Duration (11)**: Enter the expected duration of treatment.
* **-- Agreement Terms -- (12)**: This is a header for the following fields, which capture your agreements related to the medication.
* **Agreement to Follow Prescription Directions (13)**: Agree to take the medication exactly as prescribed.
* **Agreement Not to Share Medication (14)**: Agree not to share this medication with others.
* **Agreement to Secure Medication (15)**: Agree to store the medication away from children and unauthorized persons.
* **Agreement to Notify of Changes (16)**: Agree to notify your physician of any changes in your condition or side effects.
* **Agreement to Regular Monitoring (17)**: Agree to attend all scheduled monitoring appointments.
* **Understanding of Risks and Benefits (18)**: Confirm your understanding of the potential risks and benefits of long-term controlled substance use.
* **Awareness of Addiction Potential (19)**: Confirm your awareness of the possibility of addiction or dependence with this medication.
* **Agreement to Urine Screening (20)**: Agree to undergo urine screening tests as required by your physician.
* **Agreement to Single Prescriber (21)**: Agree to receive this controlled substance from only one prescriber.
* **Agreement to No Early Refills (22)**: Agree not to request early refills of this medication.
* **Patient Signature (23)**: Provide a digital signature indicating your agreement to these terms.
* **Signature Date (24)**: Enter the date you are signing this agreement.
* **Physician Signature (25)**: Your physician will sign this agreement, acknowledging your understanding of the terms and conditions.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by patients taking controlled substances. Please review all fields carefully and ensure you understand the implications before signing.
