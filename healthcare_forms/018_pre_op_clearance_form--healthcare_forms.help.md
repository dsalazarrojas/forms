# Pre Op Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's pre-operative status and to obtain their consent for surgery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions.
4. **Surgical Details**: Provide a brief description of the surgery, including the type of procedure and any relevant details.
5. **Allergies**: Indicate if the patient has any allergies. Select "Yes" if they have allergies and "No" if they do not.
6. **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
7. **Anesthesia**: Indicate if the patient will be under anesthesia during the surgery. Select "Yes" if they will be under anesthesia and "No" if they will not.
8. **Surgical Team**: Indicate if the patient will be working with a surgical team. Select "Yes" if they will be and "No" if they will not.
9. **Patient Condition**: Describe the patient's current condition.
10. **Lab Results**: Provide any relevant lab results related to the surgery.
11. **Radiology Results**: Provide any relevant radiology results related to the surgery.
12. **Pathology Results**: Provide any relevant pathology results related to the surgery.
13. **Other Tests**: Indicate if any additional tests are required before the surgery. Select "Yes" if they are and "No" if they are not.
14. **Pre Op Clearance**: Indicate if the patient is cleared for surgery. Select "Yes" if they are and "No" if they are not.
15. **Surgical Procedure**: Provide a brief description of the surgical procedure.
16. **Post Anesthesia Care**: Describe the post-anesthesia care required for the patient.
17. **Post Op Disposition**: Describe the patient's post-operative disposition, including any special instructions.
18. **Anesthesia Provider**: Identify the anesthesia provider for the surgery.
19. **Surgical Team Leader**: Identify the surgical team leader for the procedure.
20. **Surgeon**: Identify the surgeon performing the procedure.
21. **Surgical Nurse**: Identify the surgical nurse assisting with the procedure.
22. **Anesthesiologist**: Identify the anesthesiologist for the procedure.
23. **Recovery Room Nurse**: Identify the recovery room nurse for the procedure.
24. **Date of Surgery**: Enter the date of the surgery.
25. **Time of Surgery**: Enter the time of the surgery.
