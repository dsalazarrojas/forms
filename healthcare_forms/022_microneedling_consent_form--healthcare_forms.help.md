# Microneedling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from patients before undergoing microneedling treatments to ensure their safety and understand their emergency contact and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If you have an Emergency Contact, provide their First Name and Last Name in the "Emergency Contact First Name" and "Emergency Contact Last Name" fields. 
7. Specify your relationship with your Emergency Contact in the "Relationship" field.
8. Enter your Emergency Contact's Phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you reside.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: This field is for your emergency contact's first name. If you do not have an emergency contact, please leave this field blank.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: This field is for your emergency contact's last name. If you do not have an emergency contact, please leave this field blank.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g. parent, sibling, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
