# Hospice Medical Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order medical equipment for hospice care. It is crucial that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date to ensure the best possible treatment and care for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person ordering the equipment.
3. Specify the medical equipment ordered.
4. Enter the cost of the equipment.
5. Enter any relevant insurance information.
6. Confirm the order details, including the date and time of the order.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm the order.
