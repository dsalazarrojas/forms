# Health Check Up Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist patients in scheduling a health check-up appointment with a healthcare provider. It gathers essential information needed for a successful and efficient appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Patient Information** section with your basic contact information and any additional relevant details.
2. Select the desired **Appointment Date** for your scheduled appointment.
3. Choose the **Appointment Time** that suits you best for the appointment.
4. Select your preferred **Healthcare Provider** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional)
This field is for patients to provide their basic contact information and any other relevant personal details.

### **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional)
Select the date for your scheduled appointment.

### **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional)
Choose the time slot for your scheduled appointment.

### **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required/optional)
Select your preferred healthcare provider for the appointment.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check the appointment date and time for accuracy.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please note them in the **Patient Information** section.
- If you're unsure about which healthcare provider to select, you can contact the office for guidance.
