# ycp-retail-covid-19-vaccine-consent-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about a patient, including contact information, vaccination details, and consent status, in the context of administering a COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter the following required information:
	* **Patient Name**: Type in the patient's first and last name.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* **Medical Provider Name**: Enter the name of the medical provider administering the vaccine.
	* **Medical Identifier Number**: Enter the patient's medical identifier number.
	* **Medical Provider NPI**: Enter the medical provider's NPI number.
	* **Patient Consent**: Select 'True' if the patient has given consent for the vaccine administration.
	* **Date of Administration**: Enter the date the vaccine was administered.
	* **Time of Administration**: Enter the time the vaccine was administered.
	* **Vaccine Administered by**: Select the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
	* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the form.
	* **Medical Provider Signature**: Sign and date the form.
2. Enter the following optional information:
	* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information.
	* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
	* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
	* **Medical Provider Address**: Enter the medical provider's address.
	* **Medical Provider City**: Enter the medical provider's city.
	* **Medical Provider State**: Enter the medical provider's state.
	* **Medical Provider Zip**: Enter the medical provider's zip code.
	* **Medical Provider Phone Number**: Enter the medical provider's phone number.
	* **Medical Provider Fax Number**: Enter the medical provider's fax number.
	* **Medical Provider Specialty**: Enter the medical provider's specialty.
	* **Medical Provider Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's medical care.
	* **Patient Allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: A patient's full name, typically in the format "John Doe".
* **Contact Information**: Any additional contact information for the patient, such as emergency contact or phone number.
* **Phone Number**: A phone number associated with the patient.
* **Address**: The patient's home or work address.
* **Email**: The patient's email address.
* **Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Provider Name**: The name of the healthcare professional providing care.
* **Medical Identifier Number**: A unique identifier for the patient in the medical system.
* **Medical Provider NPI**: A unique identifier for the medical provider in the medical system.
* **Medical Provider Address**: The address of the healthcare professional's practice.
* **Medical Provider City**: The city where the healthcare professional's practice is located.
* **Medical Provider State**: The state where the healthcare professional's practice is located.
* **Medical Provider Zip**: The zip code of the healthcare professional's practice.
* **Medical Provider Phone Number**: A phone number associated with the healthcare professional's practice.
* **Medical Provider Fax Number**: A fax number associated with the healthcare professional's practice.
* **Medical Provider Specialty**: The specialty of the healthcare professional.
* **Medical Provider Notes**: Any additional notes about the patient's medical care.
* **Patient Allergies**: Any allergies the patient has.
* **Patient Signature**: A signature from the patient confirming they are giving consent.
* **Vaccine Type**: The type of vaccine administered (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, Janssen).
* **Medical Provider Signature**: A signature from the healthcare professional confirming they administered the vaccine.
* **Patient Consent**: A confirmation from the patient that they have given consent for the vaccine administration.
* **Date of Administration**: The date the vaccine was administered.
* **Time of Administration**: The time the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Administered by**: The healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
* **Patient Signature**: A signature from the patient confirming they received the vaccine.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of all information entered.
* Use a secure method for storing and retrieving patient data.
