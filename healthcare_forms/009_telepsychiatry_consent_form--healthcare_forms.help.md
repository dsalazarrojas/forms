# Telepsychiatry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telepsychiatry Consent Form is used to collect patient information and consent for telepsychiatric services. This form helps healthcare providers to obtain essential information and consent from the patient before starting telepsychiatric sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details in the form.
2. Select your medical group.
3. Provide your medical record number, if you have one.
4. Indicate your agreement to the terms of consent.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, required: false): Select the medical group that you are a part of.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required: false): Enter your medical record number if you have one.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last medical record update if you have one.
* **Medical Record Provider** (`medical_record_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the options that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: I agree to receive telepsychiatric services from [Provider Name].
	+ Option 2: I understand that telepsychiatric services may involve online communication and video conferencing.
	+ Option 3: I understand that I can withdraw my consent at any time.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Date and sign the form.

## Tips
- Please fill the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for your safety and our record-keeping purposes only.
- Please keep a copy of this form for your records.
