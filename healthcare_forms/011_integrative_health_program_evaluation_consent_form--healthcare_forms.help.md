# Integrative Health Program Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information from participants in an integrative health program for evaluation purposes. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the participant's data.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Read the Form Instructions

*   Read the form carefully before filling it out, and make sure you understand the purpose and the instructions provided.
*   Ensure that you have all necessary information to complete the form before starting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our team for assistance.

### Step 2: Complete Each Section

1.  **Participant Information**
    *   This section collects general participant information for the evaluation process.
    *   Enter your participant name and any other relevant details.

2.  **Contact Information**
    *   Provide your contact information, including phone number, address, and any other relevant details.

3.  **Program Details**
    *   Share any relevant information about the program you are participating in, such as the program name and dates of participation.

4.  **Informed Consent**
    *   This section is a confirmation that you have received and understood the program details and requirements.
    *   Check the box to confirm your informed consent.

5.  **Medical History**
    *   Provide any relevant medical history or allergies for the evaluation process.
    *   Ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date.

6.  **Contact Preferences**
    *   Indicate your preferred contact method for future communication.
    *   This will aid the program team in staying in touch with you efficiently.

7.  **Emergency Contact**
    *   Identify an emergency contact person and their contact information.
    *   This will help the program team reach out to someone you trust in case of an emergency.

8.  **Participant Signature**
    *   A signature from a participant is required to confirm their agreement with the provided information.
    *   Sign the form in the designated box.
