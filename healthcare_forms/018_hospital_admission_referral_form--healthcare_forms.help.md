# Hospital Admission Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from healthcare providers about a patient's medical history, contact details, and other relevant data for efficient hospital admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing patient's information.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
  - Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
  - Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
  - Please enter the name of the person responsible for the patient's care.
  - Enter the phone number and email address of the contact person.

- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, hospitalizations, or surgeries.
  - Please provide detailed information about the patient's medical history, including dates and details of any hospitalizations or surgeries.

- **Diagnosis And Treatment** (`diagnosis_and_treatment`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's diagnosis, treatment plan, and any relevant medical notes.
  - Please enter the patient's diagnosis and treatment plan in detail, including any relevant medical notes or test results.

- **Medication And Allergies** (`medication_and_allergies`, text, required/optional): List the patient's current medications, allergies, and any other relevant medical information.
  - Please list all the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequencies.
  - Indicate any allergies, including food, environmental, or medication allergies.

- **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and oxygen saturation.
  - Please enter the patient's vital signs, including blood pressure, pulse, and oxygen saturation.

- **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, text, required/optional): Obtain the patient's consent for treatment, hospitalization, or any other medical procedure.
  - Please obtain the patient's consent in writing, signed by the patient or their authorized representative.

- **Admission Info** (`admission_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's admission, such as date, time, and reason for admission.
  - Please enter any additional information about the patient's admission, such as date, time, and reason for admission.
