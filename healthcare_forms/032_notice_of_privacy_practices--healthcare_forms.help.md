# Notice Of Privacy Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Notice Of Privacy Practices" form is a document that provides patients with information about the types of information that will be collected, used, and shared by healthcare providers. This form is designed to inform patients about their rights and how their information will be protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the types of information that will be collected, used, and shared.
* Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information (Patient Information, text, required/optional: true)
This section is intended to collect basic information about the patient, such as name, address, and contact details.

### Purpose And Use (Purpose And Use, text, required/optional: false)
In this section, please describe the purpose and use of the information being collected. This may include the types of services you will be receiving and how the information will be used to provide those services.

### Categories Of Information (Categories Of Information, select_multiple, required/optional: false)
Select all categories of information that will be collected, such as medical history, billing information, and demographic data.

### Third Party Sharing (Third Party Sharing, select_one, required/optional: false)
Select whether your information will be shared with third parties, such as insurance companies, other healthcare providers, or research institutions.

### Access Control (Access Control, text, required/optional: false)
In this section, describe how access to your information will be controlled, including who has access and what measures are in place to protect it.

### Patient Rights (Patient Rights, text, required/optional: false)
In this section, describe the patient's rights, including the right to request a copy of their medical record and the right to have their information amended or corrected.

### Effective Date (Effective Date, date, required/optional: false)
Enter the date on which this notice becomes effective.

## Tips
* Be sure to read and understand each section carefully before completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is an important document that will inform you about your rights and how your information will be protected.
