# Home Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Assessment Form is a tool used to gather information about a patient's health status in their home environment. This form helps healthcare providers to assess the patient's medical condition, identify potential health risks, and develop a care plan tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are completing the form for the correct patient.
2. Fill out the form with the patient's information as accurately and completely as possible.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Health Assessment Form Data** (`home_health_assessment_form_data`):
	+ A free-text field for the patient to provide any relevant information about their health status or concerns.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`):
	+ The patient's full name, as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`):
	+ The patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ The patient's phone number where they can be reached for further information or contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`):
	+ The patient's home address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`):
	+ A brief description of the patient's current medical condition or health status.
* **Medication** (`medication`):
	+ A list of the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`):
	+ A brief history of the patient's medical conditions, surgeries, or other health events.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`):
	+ A list of the patient's known allergies, including the allergen and the severity of the reaction.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`):
	+ The patient's social security number, as it appears on their medical record.
* **Patient Medical Insurance** (`patient_medical_insurance`):
	+ The type of medical insurance the patient has, such as Kaiser, Aetna, Blue Cross, or None.
* **Medical Plan** (`patient_medical_insurance_plan`):
	+ Whether the patient has a medical plan in place, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you are completing the form for a patient with a medical history or condition that requires special handling, please ensure the form is handled accordingly.
