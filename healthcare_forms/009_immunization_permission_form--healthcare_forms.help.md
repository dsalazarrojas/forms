# Immunization Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental permission for a child's immunizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Enter your child's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. If your child has received immunizations, please describe their immunization history in the "Immunization History" field.
5. If your child has any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Child Name** (Child Name)
*   Enter your child's full name as it appears on their official documents.

### **Parent Name** (Parent Name)
*   Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.

### **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth)
*   Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Immunization History** (Immunization History)
*   If the child has received any immunizations, provide a brief description of their immunization history. For example, "MMR, DTaP, Hib."

### **Allergies** (Allergies)
*   If the child has any allergies, list them here. For example, "peanuts, eggs, etc."
