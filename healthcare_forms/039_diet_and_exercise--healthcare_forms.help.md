# diet_and_exercise - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "diet_and_exercise" form collects personal and contact information from users for healthcare-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up or consultation purposes.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the healthcare service for assistance.
