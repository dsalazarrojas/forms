# Care Transfer Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Care Transfer Assessment Form is used to assess patients who are being transferred from one healthcare facility to another. It captures information about the patient, the transfer process, and the receiving facility, ensuring a smooth transition of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, the facility they are transferring from, and the facility they are transferring to.
2. Provide the scheduled transfer date and time.
3. Summarize the patient's current clinical status.
4. List any essential equipment that needs to accompany the patient during transfer.
5. Record the patient's current medications and dosages.
6. Provide contact information for any queries or issues during the transfer process.
7. Confirm whether the patient's family has been notified of the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, required): The name of the patient being transferred.
* **Transferring facility** (`transferring_facility`, required): The healthcare facility where the patient is currently located.
* **Receiving facility** (`receiving_facility`, required): The healthcare facility where the patient will be transferred to.
* **Transfer date** (`transfer_date`, required): The scheduled date and time of the transfer.
* **Current clinical status** (`clinical_status`, required): A summary of the patient's current medical condition and stability.
* **Essential equipment** (`essential_equipment`, optional): List any necessary equipment that must accompany the patient during the transfer (e.g., oxygen monitors, etc.).
* **Medication list** (`medication_list`, required): A list of the patient's current medications and dosages.
* **Contact for queries** (`contact_for_queries`, required): A contact phone number or email for any queries or issues during the transfer process.
* **Family notified** (`family_notified`, required): Confirmation of whether the patient's family has been notified of the transfer.

Tips:

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If unsure about any information, please consult with the transferring and receiving facilities' staff.
* This form serves as a template for assessing patients during transfer; do not modify it without proper review and approval.
