# Patient Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Safety Assessment Survey form is designed to collect information about patient safety incidents, allowing healthcare professionals to identify potential safety issues and take corrective actions to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are in the correct context to complete this form.
2. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
3. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Healthcare Setting** (Healthcare Setting, select_multiple, required)
This field asks for the location where the incident occurred. Select all relevant options that apply to the incident.

### 2. **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional)
If you have any comments or suggestions about the patient safety incident, please provide them in this field. This will help us improve our services.

### 3. **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional)
Please use this field to provide any comments or suggestions about the patient safety incident.

### 4. **Healthcare Setting** (Healthcare Setting, select_multiple, required)
Select all relevant healthcare settings where the incident occurred. This is a required field.

### 5. **Date of Incident** (Date of Incident, date, optional)
Enter the date of the incident (MM/DD/YY format).

### 6. **Time of Incident** (Time of Incident, time, optional)
Enter the time of the incident (HH:MM AM/PM format).

### 7. **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, optional)
Enter the patient's name.

### 8. **Contact Information** (Contact Information, email, optional)
Enter the patient's contact information (email).

### 9. **Incident Description** (Incident Description, text, optional)
Describe the incident in detail.

### 10. **Root Cause** (Root Cause, select_one, required)
Select the root cause of the incident from the given options. This is a required field.

### 11. **Action Plan** (Action Plan, text, optional)
Enter a plan to prevent future incidents.
