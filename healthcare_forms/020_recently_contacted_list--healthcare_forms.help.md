# Recently Contacted List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Recently Contacted List" form is used to document and track information about individuals who have recently been in contact with healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the "First Contacted" page and enter the name of the individual in the "Name Of First Contacted" field.
2. Enter the date of the last contact in the "Contact Date Last" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. If the individual contacted you by phone, enter their phone number in the "Phone Number Of Last Contacted" field. If they contacted you via email, enter their email address in the "Email Of Last Contacted" field.
4. In the "Symptoms First Contacted" field, briefly describe the reason for the individual's visit or reason for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Contacted** (`First Contacted`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual who was first contacted.
* **Name Of First Contacted** (`Name Of First Contacted`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the individual who was first contacted.
* **Last Contacted** (`Last Contacted`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual who was last contacted.
* **Contact Date Last** (`Contact Date Last`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last contact in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Name Of Last Contacted** (`Name Of Last Contacted`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the individual who was last contacted.
* **Phone Number Of Last Contacted** (`Phone Number Of Last Contacted`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual who was last contacted. Please use the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Email Of Last Contacted** (`Email Of Last Contacted`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual who was last contacted. Please ensure the email format is correct (e.g., [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com)).
* **Symptoms First Contacted** (`Symptoms First Contacted`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the individual's visit or reason for contact.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form, as this information will be used for patient care and coordination.
* If you are unsure about any field, please verify the information with your supervisor or team lead.
* This form is an essential part of our patient care process, and your attention to detail is crucial.
