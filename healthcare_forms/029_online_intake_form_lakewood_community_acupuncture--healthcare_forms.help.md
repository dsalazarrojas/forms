# online_intake_form_lakewood_community_acupuncture - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for online intake, collecting patient or client information for healthcare services at Lakewood Community Acupuncture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Fill out the "Phone" field with your contact phone number.
6.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7.  Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.
8.  List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
9.  Describe any medical procedures you have undergone in the "Medical Procedures" field.
10. List any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
11. List any emergency contacts with their relationship and information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
12. Select your preferred medical group: Acupuncture, Massage Therapy, or Chiropractic in the "Medical Group" field.
13. Indicate whether you have medical insurance or do not have insurance in the "Medical Insurance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your address for communication and treatment purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for scheduling and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in 12-hour format (e.g., 01:30 PM).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): List any medical history, including surgeries, allergies, etc.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any current medications.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): List any past medical procedures or surgeries.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): List an emergency contact with their relationship and phone number.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred medical group: Acupuncture, Massage Therapy, or Chiropractic.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have medical insurance or do not have insurance.

Note: The "optional" label indicates that the field is not required to be filled out.
