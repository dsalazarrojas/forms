# Health Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect information from users to provide them with a personalized health insurance quote. Please follow the steps below to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Age.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide your Address Line 1 and Address Line 2.
4. Enter your City and State.
5. Enter your Zip code.
6. Choose the type of insurance you are looking for: Life, Health, or Disability.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
- **Age** (`age`, Number, Optional): Enter your age.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, Text, Optional): Enter your street address.
- **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, Text, Optional): Enter your apartment or suite number, if applicable.
- **City** (`city`, Text, Optional): Enter your city of residence.
- **State** (`state`, Text, Optional): Enter your state of residence.
- **Zip** (`zip`, Text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of insurance you are looking for: Life, Health, or Disability.
- **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Not Applicable): Submit the form to get your personalized quote.
