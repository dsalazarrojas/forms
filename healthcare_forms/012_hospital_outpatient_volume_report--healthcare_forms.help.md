# Hospital Outpatient Volume Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Outpatient Volume Report is a form used to collect data on the number of patient visits, cancellations, and wait times in outpatient settings. This report provides valuable information for healthcare administrators and practitioners to make data-driven decisions and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Enter a brief description of the data you are submitting.
2. **Visit Information**: Provide details about the visits that occurred during the reporting period.
3. **Visit Details**: Enter the actual time of the visit, if applicable.
4. **Cancellations**: Select the reason for cancellations (No Show, Patient Cancelled, or Clinician Cancelled).
5. **Reasons for Cancellation**: Select multiple reasons for cancellations that apply.
6. **Wait Time**: Enter the average wait time for patients.
7. **Number of Cancellations**: Enter the total number of cancellations.
8. **No-shows**: Enter the number of patients who failed to show up for their appointments.
9. **Operational Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the reporting period.
10. **Date**: Enter the date for which the data is being reported.
11. **Review and Submit**: Review your entries carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the data you are submitting.
* **Visit Information (Page 2)**: Provide details about the visits that occurred during the reporting period.
* **Visit Details (Page 3)**: Enter the actual time of the visit, if applicable. This field is not required, but if you enter a time, it will help with analysis.
* **Cancellations (Page 4)**: Select the reason why patients cancelled their appointments. You can select multiple options.
* **Reasons for Cancellation (Page 5)**: Select multiple reasons for cancellations that apply. Make sure to select all applicable reasons.
* **Wait Time (Page 6)**: Enter the average wait time for patients. This field is required and should be a numeric value.
* **Number of Cancellations (Page 7)**: Enter the total number of cancellations. This field is required and should be a numeric value.
* **No-shows (Page 8)**: Enter the number of patients who failed to show up for their appointments. This field is required and should be a numeric value.
* **Operational Notes (Page 9)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the reporting period.
* **Date (Page 10)**: Enter the date for which the data is being reported. This field is required.
* **Review and Submit (Page 11)**: Review your entries carefully and submit the form. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting.
