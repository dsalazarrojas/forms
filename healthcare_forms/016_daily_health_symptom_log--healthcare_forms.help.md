# Daily Health Symptom Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Symptom Log is a form used to track your daily health symptoms, allowing you to monitor your health and report any issues to your healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
2. Fill in the symptom-specific information, such as severity or duration.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments.
4. If you want to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address.
5. Complete any other required or optional fields as needed.
