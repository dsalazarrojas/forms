# Daily Health Symptom Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Symptom Log is a form used to track your daily health symptoms, allowing you to monitor your health and report any issues to your healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
2. Fill in the symptom-specific information, such as severity or duration.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments.
4. If you want to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address.
5. Complete any other required or optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing today from the list. Choose 'Yes' for each symptom that applies.
* **Headache** (`symptom_1`, text): If you selected 'Yes' for Headache, enter any details about your headache here.
* **Fever** (`symptom_2`, text): If you selected 'Yes' for Fever, enter any details about your fever here.
* **Cough** (`symptom_3`, text): If you selected 'Yes' for Cough, enter any details about your cough here.
* **Diarrhea** (`symptom_4`, text): If you selected 'Yes' for Diarrhea, enter any details about your Diarrhea here.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you are logging your symptoms.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time of day you are experiencing your symptoms.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Add any additional comments or details about your symptoms or experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up.
* **Daily Health Symptom Log** (`title`, text): This field is not a field to fill in but is the title of the form.
