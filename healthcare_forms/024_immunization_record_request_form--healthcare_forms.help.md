# Immunization Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Record Request Form is used to request access to a patient's immunization records. This form is for patients or their legal representatives who need to obtain a copy of their immunization records for personal or official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **First name** in the field labeled "First name". This is the first name of the patient or the representative.
2. Fill in the **Last name** of the patient or representative in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the **Email** address associated with the patient or representative in the field labeled "Email".
4. Specify the **Purpose for request** in the field with the same label. This will help the healthcare provider understand why you need the immunization records.
5. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
6. Enter the name and details of the **Healthcare Provider** who provided the immunization services in the field labeled "Healthcare Provider".
7. If you have a **Contact Phone**, please enter it in the field labeled "Contact Phone". This will help us contact you for further information if needed.
8. If you have a **Contact Address**, please enter it in the field labeled "Contact Address". This will help us contact you for further information if needed.
9. Please provide detailed information about the **Immunization Record Request Details** in the field with the same label. This will help us understand your request better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the patient or representative.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the patient or representative.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address associated with the patient or representative.
* **Purpose for request** (text, required): Specify why you need the immunization records.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the date of birth of the patient or representative in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Healthcare Provider** (text, required): Enter the name and details of the healthcare provider who provided the immunization services.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter a contact phone number if you have one.
* **Contact Address** (text, optional): Enter a contact address if you have one.
* **Immunization Record Request Details** (text, required): Explain in detail why you are requesting the immunization records and what you plan to do with them.
