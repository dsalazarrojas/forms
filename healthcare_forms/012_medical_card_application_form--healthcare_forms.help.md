# medical_card_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for creating and managing medical cards. Please complete the form with accurate details to ensure accurate card creation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your patient's name in the `Patient Name` field.
2.  Select your patient's `Date of Birth` from the calendar or enter the date manually using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Type any relevant `Contact Information` in the designated field.
4.  Enter the name of the `Medical Card Provider` who will be handling your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or medical records. This field is not required but is helpful for card creation.
* **Date of Birth**: Select or enter your date of birth using the MM/DD/YYYY format. This field is not required but helps in verifying your identity.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses, that the medical card provider might need to reach you.
* **Medical Card Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional responsible for your case. This field is optional but recommended for accurate record-keeping.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* If you have any medical records or history, please provide this information as well.
* Contact the form provider for any questions or issues with form completion.
