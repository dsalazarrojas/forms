# Healthcare Provider Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect healthcare provider information from individuals or organizations providing medical services. Please ensure you fill it out accurately and completely to avoid delays or errors in processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled before submitting it.
2. Review your information carefully to ensure its accuracy.
3. Submit the form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required):
    Your first name as it appears on your documents or records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required):
    Your last name as it appears on your documents or records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
    Enter your email address where you can be contacted for professional purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
    Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent or non-urgent matters.
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, select_one, required):
    Select the area of medical expertise you possess.
    * Options: General, Cardiology, Dermatology
* **Clinic** (`clinic`, text, required):
    Enter the name of your medical clinic or practice.
* **Hospital** (`hospital`, text, required):
    Enter the name and location of the hospital you are associated with.
* **Tele-Health** (`tele_health`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select the modes of communication you offer or prefer:
    * In person
    * Phone
    * Video
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, required):
    Enter the type of medical license you hold.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, required):
    Enter your unique medical license number as issued by the relevant authority.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required):
    Enter your practice or office address for billing or other purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, required):
    Enter the city of your practice or office location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required):
    Enter the state where you practice or operate.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required):
    Enter your practice or office zip code for billing or other purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate to facilitate communication.
* Double-check your medical license details for accuracy to avoid delays.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, contact us for assistance.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is best to contact our support team for clarification.
