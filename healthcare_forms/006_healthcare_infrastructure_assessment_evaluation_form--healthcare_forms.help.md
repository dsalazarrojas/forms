# healthcare_infrastructure_assessment_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the healthcare infrastructure of a facility. The form is intended to collect information about a healthcare provider's services, facility rating, and evaluation date and time. This information is crucial for evaluating the effectiveness of the healthcare infrastructure and identifying areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Facility Address" field with the complete address of the healthcare facility.
2. Enter the "Healthcare Provider Name" in the corresponding field.
3. Select the "Facility Services" offered by the healthcare provider from the list provided.
4. Rate the facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
5. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
7. Provide any additional comments about the assessment in the "Assessment Comments" field.
8. If applicable, enter the "Healthcare Provider License" number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the healthcare facility.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Facility Services** (`facility_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the healthcare services offered by the facility. Options include: "Healthcare services 1", "Healthcare services 2", "Healthcare services 3".
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments about the assessment.
* **Healthcare Provider License** (`healthcare_provider_license`, text, required/optional): Enter the healthcare provider's license number if applicable.
