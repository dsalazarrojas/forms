# Custom Prosthetic Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare providers to order custom prosthetics for their patients. It gathers essential information to ensure accurate and complete prosthetic device production. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Patient Information

*   **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any additional comments.
*   **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Patient Image**: Optionally, upload a photo of the patient.
*   **Patient Signature**: Get the patient's signature, if required.

### Step 2: Order Prosthetic Details

*   **Prosthetic Device Details**: Describe the custom prosthetic device ordered.
*   **Prosthetic Device Size**: Enter the size of the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Weight**: Enter the weight of the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Materials**: Select the materials for the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Sockets**: Select the sockets for the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Color**: Select the color of the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Fabric**: Select the fabric of the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the prosthetic device.
*   **Prosthetic Device Order Date**: Enter the date of the prosthetic device order.
*   **Prosthetic Device Image**: Optionally, upload an image of the prosthetic device.
*   **Custom Prosthetic Order Form Notes**: Enter any additional comments related to the order.

### Step 3: Verify and Sign

*   **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Get the healthcare provider's signature, if required.
*   **Healthcare Provider Image**: Optionally, upload a photo of the healthcare provider.
*   **Healthcare Provider Comments**: Enter any comments related to the order.
*   **Custom Prosthetic Order Form Notes**: Enter any additional comments related to the order.

### Step 4: Review and Submit

*   Review all the information entered.
*   Submit the form once complete and accurate information is entered.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Use the "Custom Prosthetic Order Form Notes" field for any additional comments or instructions.
*   Upload images and signatures as needed.
*   Review the form carefully before submission.
