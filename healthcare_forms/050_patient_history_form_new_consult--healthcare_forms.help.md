# Patient History Form NEW CONSULT - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient History Form NEW CONSULT is designed to collect essential information from patients during a new consultation. This form will help healthcare professionals quickly gather basic details about the patient, which will aid in providing accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Provide General Information**
	* Fill in the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
	* Enter the date of birth in the "date of birth" field in the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
	* If available, provide a valid phone number in the "phone" field.
2. **Step 2: Verify Contact Information (Optional)**
	* Enter the patient's email address in the "email" field if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_consult**: This field is meant for a brief note about the reason for the new consultation. Please provide a short description of the visit.
* **patient name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or record.
* **date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY) to help with medical recordkeeping and age-related calculations.
* **phone**: If available, enter a valid phone number for the patient to enable easy communication.
* **email**: Enter the patient's email address if available to facilitate electronic communication.

## Tips
- Please ensure the date of birth is entered correctly to avoid errors.
