# Infectious Disease Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infectious Disease Report Form is used by healthcare professionals to report cases of infectious diseases. This form helps track and monitor disease outbreaks, enabling public health officials to respond quickly and effectively to prevent the spread of disease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to submit this form.
2. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, contact info, and address.
3. Select the disease type (e.g., Influenza, Common cold, HIV, or COVID-19).
4. Enter the onset date of the disease.
5. Describe the symptoms experienced by the patient.
6. Select the healthcare provider who treated the patient (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Thompson).
7. Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and address.
* **Disease Type** (`disease_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of infectious disease (e.g., Influenza, Common cold, HIV, or COVID-19).
* **Onset Date** (`onset_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the disease symptoms first appeared.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, note, required: false): Describe the symptoms experienced by the patient.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, select_one, required: false): Select the healthcare provider who treated the patient (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Thompson).
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's location (e.g., hospital, clinic, or home address).
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's address.
* **Disease Outcome** (`disease_outcome`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the outcome of the disease (e.g., resolved, ongoing, or deceased).
