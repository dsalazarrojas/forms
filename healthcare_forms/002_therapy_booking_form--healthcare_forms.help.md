# therapy_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage therapy sessions for patients or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the desired date for your therapy session.
3. Choose the time slot for your session.
4. Select the type of service you require (Consultation, Session, or Therapy).
5. Specify the duration of your session.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments.
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Therapy Booking Form** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form, which is already pre-populated as "Therapy Booking Form".
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the patient or client.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to enable communication and confirmation of your booking.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to book your therapy session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time slot for your session. Please ensure it's not already booked by another user.
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options: Consultation, Session, or Therapy. This will help the service provider prepare accordingly.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of your session in units of time (e.g., 30 minutes).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up or emergency contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your booking request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid any miscommunication.
* Double-check the time and date selected to avoid conflicts with other bookings.
* Be specific about the service and duration to ensure the therapist is prepared accordingly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the healthcare organization for assistance.
