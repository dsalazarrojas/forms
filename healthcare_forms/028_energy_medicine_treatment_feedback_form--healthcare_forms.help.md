# Energy Medicine Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Medicine Treatment Feedback Form is a survey designed to collect feedback from customers regarding their experiences with our energy medicine treatment. Your input will help us understand your perceptions and identify areas for improvement, allowing us to better serve you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer all the required fields (indicated by *) to ensure we have a complete picture of your experience.
3. Fill in your feedback in the provided text boxes.
4. Select the treatment type that best describes your experience.
5. Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Input** (optional): Enter any additional information about your experience, such as your name, date of birth, or any relevant medical conditions.
* **Treatment Date** (optional): Select the date you received energy medicine treatment.
* **Treatment Time** (optional): If applicable, select the time of day you received treatment.
* **Treatment Type** (optional): Choose from the list to describe the type of energy medicine treatment you received.
	+ Healy Resonance: Select this option if you received a specific Healy Resonance treatment.
	+ Other: Select this option if you received another type of energy medicine treatment.
* **Feedback** (optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience with energy medicine treatment.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact** (optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
