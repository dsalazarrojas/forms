# epidemic-data-research-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic data from participants and gather information about the study or research project they are involved in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:
1. Enter your personal information on the first page, "Participant Information". This page is not required but it's good for the researchers to have this information.
2. Fill in your contact information on the "Contact Information" page, if this information is required for the study.
3. Review and confirm your informed consent on the "Study Informed Consent" page. Please be aware that this section is optional.
4. Indicate your agreement or disagreement with data sharing on the "Data Sharing Consent" page.
5. Provide a brief description of the study on the "Study Description" page.
6. Specify your data collection permissions on the "Data Collection Permissions" page.
7. List any participant identifiers that apply to you on the "Participant Identifiers" page.
8. Enter your research organization information on the "Research Organization Info" page, if this information is required.
9. Enter the duration of the study on the "Study Duration" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (1)**: Enter your personal details such as name, address, and date of birth.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter your email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details if required.
* **Study Informed Consent (3)**: Review and confirm that you have been informed about the study and understand its purpose and risks.
* **Data Sharing Consent (4)**: Choose whether you agree or disagree with the sharing of your data with third parties.
* **Study Description (5)**: Briefly describe the study and its purpose.
* **Data Collection Permissions (6)**: Specify what type of data will be collected and how it will be used.
* **Participant Identifiers (7)**: List any participant identifiers such as ID numbers or names.
* **Research Organization Info (8)**: Enter information about the research organization conducting the study, if required.
* **Study Duration (9)**: Enter the duration of the study in numbers of days or weeks.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research organization conducting the study.
