# DSM 5 Level 1 Cross Cutting Symptom Measure Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the presence and severity of various symptoms that may be indicative of mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety, and other psychiatric disorders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with the options provided (Yes or No for questions 6 and 7, text for all others).
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help provide a comprehensive picture of your mental state.
3. You may refer to your healthcare provider if you have questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Awareness** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
    Answer with a brief description of your overall mental state, including any notable changes or concerns you have been experiencing.
* **Mood** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe your current emotional state, including any shifts or changes you have noticed in your mood.
* **Motivation** (`3`, `text`, required: true):
    Answer with a description of your motivation levels, including any difficulties or challenges you are facing.
* **Self Care** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain your current self-care practices, including any changes or adjustments you have made.
* **Sleep Quality** (`5`, `number`, required: false):
    Answer with a rating of your current sleep quality, using the scale provided in the form.
* **Anxiety** (`6`, `select_one`, required: true):
    Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your current level of anxiety.
* **Depression** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: true):
    Answer with all applicable 'Yes' responses to indicate any symptoms of depression you are experiencing.
* **Guilt Feelings** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any feelings of guilt or remorse you are experiencing.
* **Guilt Act** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any actions you have taken or not taken due to guilt feelings.
* **Shame** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any feelings of shame or self-doubt you are experiencing.
* **Anxiety Panic** (`11`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any anxiety or panic attacks you have experienced.
* **Anxiety Phobia** (`12`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any fears or phobias related to anxiety.
* **Obsessive Compulsive** (`13`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any obsessive or compulsive behaviors or thoughts you are experiencing.
* **Anger** (`14`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any feelings of anger or frustration you are experiencing.
* **Fear** (`15`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any fears or worries you are experiencing.
* **Cognitive Distortion** (`16`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any distorted or irrational thoughts you are experiencing.
* **Emotional Regulation** (`17`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe your current emotional regulation strategies or challenges.
* **Emotional Response** (`18`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain how you respond to emotional situations.
* **Impulse Control** (`19`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any difficulties with impulse control or impulse behavior.
* **Sleep Quality Note** (`20`, `text`, required: false):
    Add any relevant notes or comments about your sleep quality.
* **Appetite** (`21`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any changes in your appetite or eating habits.
* **Social Functioning** (`22`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any difficulties with social interactions or relationships.
* **Energy Level** (`23`, `text`, required: false):
    Rate your current energy levels.
* **Interest In Activities** (`24`, `text`, required: false):
    Explain any changes or lack of interest in activities you once enjoyed.
* **Social Support** (`25`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe any support systems or resources you are using.
