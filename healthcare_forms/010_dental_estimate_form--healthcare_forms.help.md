# Dental Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate an estimate of the costs associated with a dental procedure. It requires the following information from the patient and the healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, starting with the first page.
2. Enter the patient's name and contact information on the first page.
3. Enter the procedure name, date of service, and estimated cost on the second page.
4. Select the procedure type and enter any additional procedure details.
5. Enter the healthcare provider's name, specialty, and facility information.
6. Enter the patient's insurance payer information and procedure number.
7. Review and submit the form to generate the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (id: 3, text): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Procedure Name** (id: 2, text): Enter the name of the procedure being performed.
* **Procedure Cost** (id: 4, number): Enter the estimated cost of the procedure.
* **Date of Service** (id: 5, date): Enter the date on which the procedure will be performed.
* **Estimated Cost** (id: 6, number): Enter the estimated cost of the procedure.
* **Procedure Description** (id: 7, text): Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Procedure Type** (id: 9, select_multiple): Select the type of procedure being performed (e.g. surgery, filling, etc.).
* **Patient Contact** (id: 10, text): Enter the patient's contact information (phone, email, etc.).
* **Patient Email** (id: 11, email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor Name** (id: 12, text): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure.
* **Doctor Specialty** (id: 13, text): Enter the specialty of the healthcare provider (e.g. dentist, orthodontist, etc.).
* **Facility Name** (id: 14, text): Enter the name of the healthcare facility providing the procedure.
* **Facility Street** (id: 15, text): Enter the street address of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility City** (id: 16, text): Enter the city of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility State** (id: 17, text): Enter the state of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Zip** (id: 18, text): Enter the zip code of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Phone** (id: 19, text): Enter the phone number of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Fax** (id: 20, text): Enter the fax number of the healthcare facility (if applicable).
* **Facility Email** (id: 21, email): Enter the email address of the healthcare facility (if applicable).
* **Payer** (id: 22, text): Enter the name of the insurance payer.
* **Procedure Number** (id: 23, text): Enter the procedure number assigned by the insurance payer.
* **Submit** (id: 24, text): Click to submit the form to generate an estimate of the procedure cost.

Please note that some fields are optional, but are required for the form to function correctly.
