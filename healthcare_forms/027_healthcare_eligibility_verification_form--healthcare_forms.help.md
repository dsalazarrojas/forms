# Healthcare Eligibility Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Eligibility Verification Form is used to verify the eligibility of a patient or individual for healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your Insurance Provider from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your Insurance Type from the dropdown menu.
6. If you have an Insurance Card Number, enter it in the Insurance Card Number field.
7. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is listed on your identification or insurance card.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is listed on your identification or insurance card.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider that covers you from the dropdown menu. This is the company or organization that issued your insurance policy.
* **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance you have, such as Private, Medicare, Medicaid, or Other.
* **Insurance Card Number**: If you have an insurance card, enter the number on it here. This is the number associated with your insurance policy.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further verification.
