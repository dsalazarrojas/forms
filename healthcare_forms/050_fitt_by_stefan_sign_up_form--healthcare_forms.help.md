# Fitt By Stefan Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and medical record details from patients or medical professionals. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure accurate and complete data entry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the `Sign up` page, which will prompt you to enter your contact information.
2. On the `Contact Information` page, enter your phone number and email address.
3. On the `Medical Record Information` page, provide the required details as prompted, selecting the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign up**: Enter your name and other identifying details to begin the registration process.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Date**: Enter your date of birth or a relevant medical date.
* **Phone**: (same as above)
* **Email**: (same as above)
* **Medical Record Information**: This field collects medical record details.
* **Medical Record Information Text**: Enter any additional text-based medical information.
* **Medical Record Information Date**: Enter a date related to your medical records.
* **Medical Record Information Time**: Enter a time related to your medical records.
* **Medical Record Information Integer**: Enter a numerical value related to your medical records.
* **Medical Record Information Decimal**: Enter a decimal value related to your medical records.
* **Medical Record Information Select One**: Choose between `Yes` or `No` for a specific medical condition or treatment.
* **Medical Record Information Select Multiple**: Choose between `Yes` or `No` for multiple medical conditions or treatments.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
