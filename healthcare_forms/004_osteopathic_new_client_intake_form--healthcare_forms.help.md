# Osteopathic New Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from new clients, including contact details and medical information. This form is required to be completed by clients to register with the osteopathic healthcare service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
* Fill in the client's address in the "Address" field.
* Enter the client's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
* Select the type of medical insurance the client has from the options provided in the "Medical Insurance" field.
* If the client has medical insurance, enter the name of the provider in the "Medical Insurance Provider" field.
* Fill in the name and contact information of the client's emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): The client's full name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): The client's current address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): The client's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): The client's phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): A brief description of the client's medical history.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Medicare, Medicaid, Private Insurance.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, optional): The name of the client's medical insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): The name of the client's emergency contact, including their relationship to the client.
