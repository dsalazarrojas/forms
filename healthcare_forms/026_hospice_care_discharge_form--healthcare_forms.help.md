# hospice_care_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospice care discharge form is a document used to gather and document the necessary information for a patient's discharge from a care facility. This form helps healthcare providers to ensure a smooth transition of care and provides important information for future care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's personal information on page 1 (Patient Information).
2. Enter the patient's name and other identifying details.
3. Review the care team information on page 2 and select the relevant options.
4. Enter the discharge date and time on page 3.
5. Provide the reason for discharge and next care setting details on page 4.
6. Select the next care setting and discharge location on page 5.
7. Enter any follow-up care instructions on page 6.
8. Add any comments or notes about the patient's care on page 7.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`id: 1`): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant personal details.
* **Patient Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the patient's name as listed on the identification documents.
* **Patient Care Team** (`id: 3`): Select "Yes" if the patient has a care team; "No" otherwise.
* **Hospice ID** (`id: 4`): Select "Yes" if the patient has a hospice ID; "No" otherwise.
* **Discharge Date** (`id: 5`): Enter the date the patient is discharging from the care facility.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`id: 6`): Enter the reason for the patient's discharge.
* **Next Care Setting** (`id: 7`): Select the next care setting for the patient (e.g., home, hospital, etc.).
* **Discharge to** (`id: 8`): Select where the patient will be discharging (e.g., home, rehabilitation, etc.).
* **Follow-up Care** (`id: 9`): Enter any follow-up care instructions or advice for the patient.
* **Comments** (`id: 10`): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's care.
* **Signature** (`id: 11`): Enter your name and title as a healthcare provider.
* **Provider Signature** (`id: 12`): Enter your name and title as a healthcare provider.
* **Date of Discharge** (`id: 13`): Enter the date the patient is discharging from the care facility.
* **Time of Discharge** (`id: 14`): Enter the time the patient is discharging from the care facility.
* **Facility** (`id: 15`): Enter the name of the care facility.
* **Room Number** (`id: 16`): Enter the patient's room number in the care facility.
* **Physician Name** (`id: 17`): Enter the name of the patient's attending physician.
* **Physician Phone Number** (`id: 18`): Enter the phone number of the patient's attending physician.
* **Physician Address** (`id: 19`): Enter the address of the patient's attending physician.
* **Physician Notes** (`id: 20`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the patient's attending physician.
* **Discharge Disposition** (`id: 21`): Enter the discharge disposition (e.g., discharged, deceased, etc.).
* **Discharge Status** (`id: 22`): Select the patient's discharge status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Discharge Location** (`id: 23`): Enter the location where the patient is discharging.
* **Discharge Date/Time** (`id: 24`): Enter the date and time of discharge.
* **Facility Name** (`id: 25`): Enter the name of the care facility.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all necessary approvals and information before completing this form.
- Review the patient's medical history and care team information carefully before filling out the form.
- Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
- Keep a copy of the form for future reference and follow-up care instructions.
