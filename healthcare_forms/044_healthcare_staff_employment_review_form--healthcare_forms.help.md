# Healthcare Staff Employment Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback and performance data from healthcare staff members during review cycles. The information gathered helps assess employee performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a completed review cycle scheduled for the staff member before filling out this form.
2. Review the employee's performance and select the correct rating for their performance over the review cycle.
3. Enter the staff member's job title and department.
4. If necessary, provide comments for the supervisor.
5. Set the review status and reason for status.
6. Enter the review comments.
7. Enter the reviewer's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Performance Rating**: Rate the staff member's performance over the review cycle, with 1-2 being below expectations, 3 being meets expectations, 4 being above expectations, and 5 being excellent.
* **Job Title**: Enter the staff member's job title.
* **Department**: Select the staff member's department.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the staff member's manager's name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the staff member's ID number.
* **Date of Review**: Select the date of the review.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Provide any additional comments for the supervisor.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the staff member's overall performance over the review cycle, with higher numbers indicating better performance.
* **Review Cycle Start Date**: Select the start date of the review cycle.
* **Review Cycle End Date**: Select the end date of the review cycle.
* **Review Frequency**: Enter the review cycle frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually).
* **Next Review Date**: Select the date of the next review.
* **Review Due Date**: Select the review due date.
* **Review Status**: Select the status of the review (Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Reason for Status**: Enter the reason for the review status.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments about the review.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Email**: Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Job Performance**: Enter any comments about the staff member's job performance.
