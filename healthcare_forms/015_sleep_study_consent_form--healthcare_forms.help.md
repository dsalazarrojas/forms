# sleep_study_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sleep_study_consent_form" is designed to gather essential information from patients for a sleep study, including their contact details, medical history, and consent for the study. This form helps healthcare professionals to ensure that patients understand the potential risks and benefits of the sleep study and can make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "sleep_study_consent_form" field with the title of the form.
2. On the "first_page" section, you will find the "patient_information" field, where you can enter your name and any other identifying information.
3. Move on to the "patient_info" section where you will be asked for your date of birth.
4. You will also be asked to provide your phone number.
5. Enter your address in the "address" field.
6. The "next_steps" field is where you will find information about what to expect during and after the sleep study.
7. The "consent" section is where you will be asked to provide your consent for the sleep study.
8. Finally, you will need to sign and date the "signature" field.
