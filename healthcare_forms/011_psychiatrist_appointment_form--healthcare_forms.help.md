# Psychiatrist Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's appointment with a psychiatrist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read through each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
* Fill in the patient's personal details, including their name, address, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
* Provide clear and concise information about the appointment, including the date, time, and reason for the visit.
* Add any additional notes or medical concerns that the doctor should be aware of before the consultation.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section collects the patient's personal details, including their name, address, and contact information.
* **Appointment Information** (`appointment_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section provides details about the appointment, such as the date, time, and reason for the visit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to provide the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/true): This section is for any additional notes or medical concerns that the doctor should be aware of before the consultation.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for any additional medical information that the doctor should be aware of before the consultation.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for any information about the doctor or medical team that will be attending to the patient.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you have any additional information or concerns, please add them to the appropriate sections. The more detailed and accurate information you provide, the better the doctor can tailor the consultation to meet your needs.
