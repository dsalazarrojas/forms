# evergreen_healthcare_services_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The evergreen healthcare services application form is designed to gather information from users to better understand their healthcare needs and preferences. This form is used to collect data that will help healthcare providers tailor their services to meet the unique needs of each individual. Please ensure that you only submit this form once, as duplicate submissions may cause unnecessary processing delays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field, following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your desired date and time of service in the Date and Time fields, respectively.
6. In the Evergreen Healthcare Services field, provide any relevant information about your current health services.
7. In the Healthcare Form field, enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to receive healthcare services.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time you would like to receive healthcare services (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Evergreen Healthcare Services** (`evergreen_healthcare_services`, text, optional): Describe your current or preferred health services.
* **Healthcare Form** (`healthcare_form`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
