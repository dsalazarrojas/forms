# Journal Prompts - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Journal Prompts" form is a tool to collect information about your journaling practices and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Select all the options that apply to your situation.
3. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts or answers.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction To Journaling** (`title`, note, optional): This section is designed to get you started on the journaling journey. Write a brief introduction to journaling, if you like.
* **Feeling Overwhelmed** (`feeling_overwhelmed`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you're feeling overwhelmed and need help getting started with journaling. Choose 'No' otherwise.
* **How Are You Feeling?** (`feeling_overwhelmed_answer`, text, optional): If you chose 'Yes' to feeling overwhelmed, please describe your current state in a few words.
* **What's Next?** (`save_and_continue`, select_one, optional): Choose 'True' if you want to continue journaling and 'False' otherwise. This will help us understand your current journaling intentions.
* **What Journaling Tips Have You Found Helpful?** (`journaling_tips`, text, optional): Share any tips or techniques that have been helpful to you in your journaling practice.
* **Answer** (`journaling_tips_answer`, text, optional): Respond to the previous question, providing more details on what you've found helpful.

## Tips
Remember, this form is designed to be brief and non-intrusive. Answer honestly, and don't worry about being too brief or too long.
