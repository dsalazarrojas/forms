# Dietary Restrictions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's dietary restrictions and medical conditions to ensure their safety and well-being during treatment. By completing this form, you will be able to communicate your dietary needs and medical conditions to the healthcare team, enabling us to provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, including all required fields.
- You can select multiple options for some fields.
- Use the "Other Medical Conditions" field to provide any additional medical conditions not listed in the other fields.
- You can add comments or any additional information in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**: This field is for your name and any other identification details you'd like to share with us.
- **Food Restrictions**: Select all the food restrictions you have, such as vegetarian, gluten-free, or dairy-free.
- **Allergies**: Select all the food allergies you have, such as peanut allergy or lactose intolerance.
- **Medical Condition 1**: Select a medical condition you have, such as diabetes or hypertension.
- **Medical Condition 2**: Select another medical condition you have, such as high blood pressure or asthma.
- **Medical Condition 3**: Select a third medical condition you have, such as kidney disease or sleep apnea.
- **Other Medical Conditions**: If you have any other medical conditions not listed above, please specify them here.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
- **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all the dietary restrictions you have, such as vegan or low-sodium.
- **Signature**: Please sign the form to confirm you have read and understood the form.
- **Date**: Please enter the date you completed the form.
- **Time**: Please enter the time you completed the form.

**Note:** Please review this form carefully and ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
