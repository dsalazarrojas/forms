# sleep_study_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sleep_study_questionnaire" form is designed to collect information from patients about their sleep habits and patterns. This form is used in a healthcare setting for research or clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form introduction for context and instructions.
2.  Please answer all questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
3.  If you have any other sleep patterns not listed, please describe them in the "sleep_patterns_other" field.
4.  Use the comment field for any additional information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This is a brief introduction to the form.
*   **patient_info** (`id: 2`): Enter your patient information here.
*   **sleep_habits** (`id: 3`): Please select all that apply for your sleep habits.
*   **sleep_patterns** (`id: 4`): Select "Yes" if you have the listed sleep pattern, "No" otherwise.
*   **sleep_patterns_other** (`id: 5`): Select all that apply for other sleep patterns not listed.
*   **sleep_patterns_other_other** (`id: 6`): Describe your other sleep patterns here.
*   **sleep_patterns_other_other_other** (`id: 7`): Select "Yes" if this pattern applies, "No" otherwise.
*   **sleep_patterns_other_other_other_other** (`id: 8`): Describe your other specific patterns here.
*   **sleep_study_questionnaire_form_comments** (`id: 9`): Enter any additional comments or information here.
