# required_screening_questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential health information from patients to facilitate a comprehensive health screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant medical history and symptoms sections to report any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or sensitivities.
2. Enter your current temperature, if applicable.
3. Choose any symptoms you are currently experiencing, if any.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
5. Report any known medical conditions or recent test results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your medical history?** (`medical_history_1`, note, required): Please describe any pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities.
* **What is your current temperature?** (`temperature_check_1`, number, required): Enter your current body temperature.
* **What symptoms do you currently experience?** (`symptoms_1`, select_multiple, required): Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing from the provided options.
* **What is your contact information?** (`patient_info_1`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`patient_info_2`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **When were your test results available?** (`test_results_1`, date, required): Report the date when you received your test results.
* **What are your test results?** (`test_results_2`, date, required): Enter the date of your test results.
* **Test Results 3** (`test_results_3`, date, required): Report the date of your test results, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing medical history and symptoms.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your healthcare provider for assistance.
