# Quarterly QAPI Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and suggestions from staff after a quarterly meeting. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated as "required: true") and any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your feedback.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to all questions and fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: Please enter the date of the meeting you are providing feedback on. This will help us track which meeting your feedback pertains to.
* **Staff Present**: Check "Yes" if you attended the meeting, and "No" if you did not. This helps us know who attended and who didn't.
* **Feedback Questions**: Please provide clear and concise feedback on the following questions:
	+ Please provide clear and concise feedback on the meeting overall. 
* **Overall Satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-5, rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting. 
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Meeting Effectiveness**: Rate how effective you found the meeting in terms of achieving its goals. 
	+ 1: Very ineffective
	+ 2: Somewhat ineffective
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat effective
	+ 5: Very effective
* **Feedback**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback on the meeting. 
* **Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made to future meetings. 
* **Action Items**: If you have any action items for the team or the meeting organizers, please list them here.
* **Action Item 1**, **Action Item 2**, **Action Item 3**, **Action Item 4**, **Action Item 5**, **Action Item 6**: List any action items that you feel are necessary to improve future meetings.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or instances where you felt the meeting was effective or ineffective.
* Be concise and clear in your answers to help us understand your feedback better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the meeting organizers.
