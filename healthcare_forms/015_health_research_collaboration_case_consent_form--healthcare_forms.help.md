# Health Research Collaboration Case Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Health Research Collaboration Case Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the information required.
2. Fill in the form title to accurately describe the research study.
3. Select the relevant category for the study (Healthcare Forms/Research Forms).
4. Provide a brief description of the research study.
5. Choose your response regarding consent (yes, no, or not sure) from the drop-down menu.
6. Choose answers to participant questions (Q1, Q2, Q3) as per your understanding.
7. Select the date and time you submit this form.
8. Enter the participant's email address for communication purposes.
9. Enter the participant's phone number and any additional notes.
10. Enter the researcher's name and contact information.
11. Enter the researcher's contact information for communication purposes.
12. Choose the type of information you have about the participant (self, team, or other).
13. Provide the participant's contact information.
14. Enter any additional notes from the researcher.
15. Finally, submit the form to complete the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Research Collaboration Case Consent Form** (`form_title`, text): A brief title that describes the research study.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): This field should be set to either "Healthcare Forms" or "Research Forms" to categorize the type of study.
* **Description** (`description`, text): A brief description of the research study.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple): Answer yes or no to indicate consent from the participant.
* **Participant Questions** (`participant_questions`, select_multiple): Answer the participant's questions as per the research study requirements.
* **Submit** (`submit`, date): Choose the date and time you want to submit this form.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time): Enter the exact time you want to submit this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the participant's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the participant's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Add any additional notes from the participant.
* **Researcher Note** (`researcher_note`, note): Add any additional notes from the researcher.
* **Submitter's Note** (`submitter_note`, note): Add any additional notes from the submitter.
* **Submitter's Info** (`submitter_info`, select_one): Choose the type of submitter information (self, researcher).
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text): Enter the submitter's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter the submitter's email address for communication purposes.
* **Researcher's Info** (`researcher_info`, select_one): Choose the type of researcher's information (self, team).
* **Researcher's Phone** (`researcher_phone`, text): Enter the researcher's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Researcher's Email** (`researcher_email`, email): Enter the researcher's email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant's Info** (`participant_info`, select_one): Choose the type of participant's information (self, other).
* **Participant's Phone** (`participant_phone`, text): Enter the participant's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Participant's Email** (`participant_email`, email): Enter the participant's email address for communication purposes.
* **Researcher's Name** (`researcher_name`, text): Enter the researcher's name for the study.
* **Participant's Name** (`participant_name`, text): Enter the participant's name for the study.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number): Enter the participant's age if required.
* **Participant Sex** (`participant_sex`, select_one): Choose the participant's sex (M, F, O).

## Tips
* Make sure you have read and understood all the fields and their corresponding descriptions before submitting this form.
* Double-check for any errors or incomplete information before submitting.
* If you have any queries or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
