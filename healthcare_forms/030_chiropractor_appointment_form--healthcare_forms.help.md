# Chiropractor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients to ensure they receive the best possible care from the chiropractor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Age, and Email Address.
2. Select your Gender from the given options.
3. Enter your Phone Number and Address (if applicable).
4. Choose whether you are a new, existing, or returning patient.
5. Fill in your preferred appointment date, time, and type (e.g., Initial consultation, Follow-up, etc.).
6. Select your preferred Chiropractor (if any).
7. Specify your alternate date preferences (if any).
8. Fill in the reason for your appointment.
9. Select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing.
10. Rate your current pain level (on a scale of 1-10).
11. Specify how soon you need to be seen (e.g., today, within 2 days, etc.).
12. Select all your available days for the appointment (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
13. Specify all your available times for the appointment (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
14. If applicable, fill in your insurance information (company, policy number, etc.).
15. Indicate whether you have health insurance and fill in the company name if applicable.
16. Indicate whether you have a referral and provide additional details if necessary.
17. Specify how you heard about us (e.g., referral, internet search, etc.).
18. If necessary, mention any special needs or accessibility requirements.
19. Leave any additional comments or remarks.
20. Indicate your consent to being contacted about your appointment.
21. Provide a digital signature to confirm your identity and agreement with the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (3)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age (5)**: Enter your age in years.
* **Gender (6)**: Select your gender from the given options (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other).
* **Phone Number (7)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address (8)**: Enter your email address.
* **Address (9)**: Enter your home address (if applicable).
* **Are you a new or existing patient (10)**: Select whether you are a new, existing, or returning patient.
* **Appointment Request (11)**: Briefly describe the purpose of your appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (12)**: Select the date you would like to be seen.
* **Preferred Time (13)**: Select the time you would like to be seen.
* **Type of Appointment (14)**: Select the type of appointment you require (e.g., initial consultation, follow-up, etc.).
* **Preferred Chiropractor (15)**: If you have a preference for a specific chiropractor, enter their name (if any).
* **Alternate Date Preferences (16)**: If you have any alternative dates or times in mind, specify them here.
* **Reason for Appointment (17)**: Briefly describe the reason for your appointment.
* **Primary Complaint (18)**: List all the symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* **Current Symptoms (19)**: Select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing (e.g., back pain, neck pain, etc.).
* **Current Pain Level (20)**: Rate your current pain level on a scale of 1-10.
* **How soon do you need to be seen (21)**: Select how soon you need to be seen (e.g., today, within 2 days, etc.).
* **Your Availability (22)**: Select all the days you are available for the appointment.
* **Available Times (23)**: Select all the times you are available for the appointment.
* **Insurance Information (24)**: If applicable, provide your insurance company name and policy number.
* **Do you have health insurance (25)**: Indicate whether you have health insurance.
* **Insurance Company (26)**: If you have health insurance, enter the company name.
* **Policy Number (27)**: If you have health insurance, enter your policy number.
* **Do you have a referral (28)**: Indicate whether you have a referral.
* **Referral Required (29)**: If you have a referral, provide additional details.
* **Additional Details (30)**: Specify any additional information you believe is relevant to your appointment.
* **How did you hear about us (31)**: Select how you heard about the chiropractor (e.g., referral, internet search, etc.).
* **Do you have any special needs (32)**: If you have any special needs or accessibility requirements, specify them here.
* **Additional Comments (33)**: Leave any additional comments or remarks.
* **I consent to being contacted about my appointment (34)**: Indicate your consent to being contacted about your appointment.
* **Patient Signature (35)**: Provide a digital signature to confirm your identity and agreement with the form.
* **Date of Request (36)**: The date and time the form was completed.
