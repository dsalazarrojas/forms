# mpox-vaccine-appointment-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to share their personal and medical details with healthcare providers, so they can receive appropriate vaccine dosages and medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
2. For each field, provide the required information according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (1)**: Enter your name, contact number, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Date and Time (2)**: Select the date and time of your desired appointment. Please ensure that the selected date and time are accurate and feasible for the healthcare provider.
* **Vaccine Type (3)**: Indicate whether you want to receive the vaccine ('Yes' or 'No'). This will help healthcare providers prepare for your appointment accordingly.
* **Vaccine Dosage (4)**: If you've had the vaccine before, please indicate the dosage you received previously. This will help healthcare providers determine the correct dosage for you.
* **Medical History (5)**: Briefly describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous reactions you have that may be relevant to your vaccination appointment.
* **Contact Info (6)**: Enter any contact information you'd like the healthcare provider to use to reach you regarding your appointment.
* **Email (7)**: Provide your email address for the healthcare provider to send you appointment reminders or updates.
