# Wound Care Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wound Care Discharge Form is used to document the discharge of a patient's wound care treatment, ensuring a smooth transition of care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form with the patient's information, treatment plan, and discharge instructions.
3. Have the patient sign the form to acknowledge they understand their discharge instructions.
4. Sign the form as the healthcare provider to confirm the patient's wound care discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`wound_care_discharge_form`, text, optional): This field is for entering the patient's basic information, which includes any necessary details about their current wound care treatment plan.
* **What is the patient's current wound care treatment plan?** (`wound_care_treatment`, select_one, optional): Choose the best answer from the provided options (A, B, C) to describe the patient's current wound care treatment.
* **Discharge Instructions** (`discharge_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any important details or instructions for the patient's future care and treatment.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email, to ensure easy communication.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, note, optional): Sign this section to confirm the patient's wound care discharge.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Have the patient sign this section to acknowledge they understand their discharge instructions.
* **Wound Care Discharge** (`wound_care_discharge`, select_one, optional): Choose the best answer from the provided options (A, B, C) to describe the patient's wound care discharge status.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Ensure the patient signs the form to acknowledge their discharge instructions.
* Sign the form as the healthcare provider to confirm the patient's wound care discharge.
