# medication_reconciliation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication reconciliation form is used to collect a comprehensive list of medications taken by a patient in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the medication_list page to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each medication in the corresponding page, providing as much detail as possible (e.g., name, dose, frequency).
3. Repeat this process for each page of the form.
4. Review all the medications listed to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **medication_list** (`medication_list`, `text`, not required): This page lists all the medications taken by the patient. Please review this page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* **medication_1** (`medication_1`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the first medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_2** (`medication_2`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the second medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_3** (`medication_3`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the third medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_4** (`medication_4`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the fourth medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_5** (`medication_5`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the fifth medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_6** (`medication_6`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the sixth medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_7** (`medication_7`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the seventh medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_8** (`medication_8`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the eighth medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_9** (`medication_9`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the ninth medication taken by the patient.
* **medication_10** (`medication_10`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the tenth medication taken by the patient.

## Tips

* Be as thorough as possible when listing all the medications taken by the patient.
* If you are unsure about a medication, do not hesitate to ask the patient or their healthcare provider.
* Review all the medications carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you find any errors or discrepancies, please correct them immediately.
