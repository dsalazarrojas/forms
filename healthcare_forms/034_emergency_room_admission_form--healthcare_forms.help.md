# Emergency Room Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Room Admission Form is designed to collect information from patients or their representatives when a patient is being admitted to an emergency room for treatment. This form helps healthcare professionals quickly gather relevant information about the patient's medical history, current condition, and treatment needs. Completing this form helps ensure that the patient receives proper care and attention, and enables healthcare providers to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Answer each section of the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible about the patient.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any section, consult with the patient or their representative before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This section is for capturing general information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, text, optional): This field is for reporting the patient's current status, e.g., "stable," "unstable," or "critical."
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or allergies.
*   **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient has, including medications or substances they are allergic to.
*   **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable medical procedures the patient has undergone, such as chest X-ray, ECG, Echocardiogram, MRI, or X-ray.
*   **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, text, optional): Report any relevant lab results or test results related to the patient's condition.
*   **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, select_one, optional): Choose the patient's diagnosis from the provided options, or select "None" if none apply.
*   **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline the proposed treatment plan for the patient.
*   **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, select_one, optional): Choose the expected patient outcome from the provided options.
*   **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the treating doctor, including their name and specialty.
