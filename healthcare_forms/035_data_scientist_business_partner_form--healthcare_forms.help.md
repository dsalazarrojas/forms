# Data Scientist Business Partner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the services offered by a data scientist business partner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the relevant services offered by the partner from the options provided.
3. Fill in the information required for each service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (text, optional): A brief description of the partner's business and their services.
* **Services** (select one): Choose the service offered by the partner (Massage Therapy Services or Other Services). 
* **Data Science Services** (select multiple, optional): Choose the data science services offered by the partner (Data Science or Other Services). 
* **Data Collection** (number, optional): Enter the number of data collection projects the partner has worked on. 
* **Data Analysis** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the partner's data analysis capabilities. 
* **Data Storage** (select multiple, optional): Choose whether the partner stores data (True or False). 

Note: The partner can only store data if they are offering data storage services.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please ask the partner for clarification.
* This form is meant to be a quick and easy way to get an overview of the services offered by the partner, so please keep your answers brief and concise.
