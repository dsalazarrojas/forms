# Social Communication Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about an individual's social communication skills, support systems, and potential behavioral concerns. It helps healthcare professionals and social workers assess the individual's social situation and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question based on your knowledge of the individual.
3. Use the space provided for notes and comments where needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the person's social communication skills** (`social_communication_skills`):
	+ This question asks about the individual's ability to communicate effectively with others.
* **What is the person's violent behavior** (`violent_behavior`):
	+ This question asks about whether the individual has exhibited violent behavior, which could include physical aggression or verbal abuse.
* **What is the person's social isolation** (`social_isolation`):
	+ This question asks about whether the individual has experienced social isolation, which could include feelings of loneliness or disconnection from others.
* **What kind of social support does the person have** (`social_support`):
	+ This question asks about the types of support systems the individual has in place, such as family, friends, or community networks.
* **Who is the person's healthcare provider** (`healthcare_provider`):
	+ This question asks about the individual's primary or specialist healthcare provider.
* **If other, please specify** (`healthcare_provider_other`):
	+ If the individual has a healthcare provider other than primary care physician or specialist, provide a description.
* **Specialist** (`healthcare_provider_specialist`):
	+ This field is likely used to describe the specialist healthcare provider.
* **Primary Care Physician** (`healthcare_provider_primary_care_physician`):
	+ This field is likely used to describe the individual's primary care physician.
* **Date of last contact with healthcare provider** (`date_last_contact`):
	+ This question asks for the date of the individual's last contact with their healthcare provider.
* **Time of last contact with healthcare provider** (`time_last_contact`):
	+ This question asks for the time of the individual's last contact with their healthcare provider.
* **Note** (`note`):
	+ Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about the individual's social situation.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ This field is likely used to capture the individual's email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`):
	+ This field is likely used to capture the individual's phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`):
	+ This question asks for the individual's date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`):
	+ This question asks for the individual's age.
* **Social worker involved** (`social_worker`):
	+ This field is likely used to describe the social worker involved in the individual's care.
* **Family member involved** (`family_member`):
	+ This field is likely used to describe a family member involved in the individual's care.
* **Has the person exhibited social isolation** (`social_isolation_2`):
	+ This question asks if the individual has experienced social isolation.
* **How long has this social isolation continued?** (`social_isolation_duration`):
	+ This question asks about the duration of the individual's social isolation.
* **How frequent is social isolation** (`social_isolation_frequency`):
	+ This question asks about the frequency of the individual's social isolation.
* **Have you accessed social support services** (`social_support_services`):
	+ This question asks if the individual has accessed social support services.
* **Social Support Services Other** (`social_support_services_other`):
	+ If the individual has accessed social support services other than those listed, provide a description of the services used.
* **If other, specify the service(s)** (`social_support_services_other_description`):
	+ Provide a description of any other social support services used.
* **Social Worker Other** (`social_worker_other`):
	+ If the individual has a social worker other than those listed, provide a description of the worker.
* **If other, specify** (`family_member_other`):
	+ If the individual has a family member other than those listed, provide a description.
* **Describe** (`family_member_other_description`):
	+ Provide a description of any other family member involved in the individual's care.
