# Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their Covid-19 status, including vaccination status, symptoms, and contact information. This data will be used by healthcare teams to track and manage Covid-19 cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your current vaccination status.
4. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
5. If vaccinated, enter the date and time of your vaccination.
6. Select the medical team that is taking care of you.
7. Enter your email address (if applicable).
8. Describe any relevant medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for easier contact.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Full vaccinated
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Date of Vaccination** (`date_of_vaccination`, date, optional): If you've been vaccinated, enter the date of your vaccination.
* **Time of Vaccination** (`time_of_vaccination`, time, optional): If you've been vaccinated, enter the time of your vaccination.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter your current address for contact purposes.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the medical team that is taking care of you.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for easier contact.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or allergies.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure proper care and follow-up.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare team.
* Please note that all information will be kept confidential and used solely for public health and healthcare management purposes.
