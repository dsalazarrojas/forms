# Mental Capacity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's mental capacity, which refers to their ability to understand, evaluate, and manipulate information across various domains, including their cognitive, emotional, and social well-being. The form is used to assess a patient's mental status, medical history, and other relevant factors that may impact their cognitive function.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please fill out the form in its entirety, but do not feel rushed. Take your time to accurately answer each question.
- Answer each question truthfully, as this information will be used to inform treatment decisions and patient care.
- Use clear and concise language when providing answers, avoiding unnecessary details or jargon.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or medical conditions that may be relevant to your mental capacity.
* **Cognitive Status** (`cognitive_status`, text, required): Report your current cognitive status, including any symptoms or difficulties you experience with memory, attention, language, or other cognitive functions.
* **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, text, required): Describe your current mental status, including any symptoms or challenges you face with mood, emotions, or other mental health concerns.
* **Physical Status** (`physical_status`, text, required): Report your current physical status, including any physical limitations or disabilities that may impact your mental capacity.
* **Social Status** (`social_status`, text, required): Describe your current social status, including your relationships, work or school situation, and any social support systems you have in place.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional medical information or context that may be relevant to your mental capacity.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Specify any goals or concerns you have related to your mental capacity, such as returning to work or improving daily functioning.
* **Option 1** (`option1`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
* **Option 2** (`option2`, text, optional): Enter any further information or thoughts you'd like to add.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to the relevant healthcare professional.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice.
- Please submit this form accurately and honestly to ensure that your healthcare provider can provide the best possible care.
