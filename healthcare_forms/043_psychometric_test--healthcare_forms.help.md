# psychometric_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The psychometric test form is designed to collect information about patients or individuals for healthcare professionals to assess their psychological well-being, cognitive abilities, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field according to your personal experiences and circumstances.
3. Fill in any required fields with accurate and honest information.
4. Complete the form at your own pace and take breaks as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Intro (Page 1)
* **Intro** (Note): This page provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read carefully before proceeding.

### 2. Demographics (Page 2)
* **Demographics** (Select One): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your demographic information.

### 3. Psychological Traits (Page 3)
* **Psychological Traits** (Select Multiple): Check all that apply to indicate your psychological traits.

### 4. Cognitive Abilities (Page 4)
* **Cognitive Abilities** (Date): Fill in your date of birth to indicate your cognitive abilities.

### 5. Personality Characteristics (Page 5)
* **Personality Characteristics** (Time): This field is currently blank, but you can add any relevant information.

### 6. Test Results (Page 6)
* **Test Results** (Text): Fill in any relevant test results or comments.

### 7. Recommendations (Page 7)
* **Recommendations** (Select One): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any recommendations.

### 8. Patient Information (Page 8)
* **Patient Information** (Email): Fill in your email address to help healthcare professionals contact you.

### 9. Contact (Page 9)
* **Contact** (Text): Fill in any relevant contact information.

### 10. Test Scores (Page 10)
* **Test Scores** (Number): Fill in any relevant test scores.

### 11. Assessment (Page 11)
* **Assessment** (Number): Fill in any relevant assessment scores.

### 12. Conclusion (Page 12)
* **Conclusion** (Text): Fill in any final comments or conclusions.

Please note: This form is for informational purposes only and should be reviewed carefully before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the healthcare professionals administering the form.
