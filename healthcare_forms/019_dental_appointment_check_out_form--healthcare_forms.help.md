# dental_appointment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_appointment_check_out_form is used to confirm and finalize dental appointments, including payment details. This form is completed by the patient or authorized healthcare staff member to process patient payments and gather feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option to confirm the appointment or reschedule it if necessary.
2. Enter any relevant payment details, if applicable.
3. Provide feedback about the appointment, such as sharing thoughts or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Confirm" to finalize the appointment, or "Reschedule" to adjust the appointment time or date.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `number`, optional): Enter any relevant payment information, such as insurance details or payment methods.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount paid for the appointment.
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, `note`, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions about the appointment, such as suggestions for improvement or comments about the staff.
