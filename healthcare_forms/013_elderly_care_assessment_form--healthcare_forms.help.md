# Elderly Care Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elderly Care Assessment Form is used to gather information about the elderly care assessment of a patient. It helps healthcare professionals and caregivers to understand the patient's medical history, current conditions, and social background.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to provide all required information, as marked by the "required" label.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the patient's primary care physician or healthcare team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Elderly Care Assessment** (`elderly_care_assessment_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the main section of the form. Please provide a brief description of the patient's elderly care assessment.
* **Primary Caregiver** (`primary_caregiver`, text, optional): This field is used to record the primary caregiver's name.
* **Primary Caregiver Phone** (`primary_caregiver_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to record the primary caregiver's phone number.
* **Primary Caregiver Email** (`primary_caregiver_email`, email, optional): This field is used to record the primary caregiver's email address.
* **Secondary Caregiver** (`secondary_caregiver`, text, optional): This field is used to record the secondary caregiver's name.
* **Secondary Caregiver Phone** (`secondary_caregiver_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to record the secondary caregiver's phone number.
* **Secondary Caregiver Email** (`secondary_caregiver_email`, email, optional): This field is used to record the secondary caregiver's email address.
* **Primary Physician** (`primary_physician`, text, optional): This field is used to record the primary physician's name.
* **Primary Physician Phone** (`primary_physician_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to record the primary physician's phone number.
* **Primary Physician Email** (`primary_physician_email`, email, optional): This field is used to record the primary physician's email address.
* **Secondary Physician** (`secondary_physician`, text, optional): This field is used to record the secondary physician's name.
* **Secondary Physician Phone** (`secondary_physician_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to record the secondary physician's phone number.
* **Secondary Physician Email** (`secondary_physician_email`, email, optional): This field is used to record the secondary physician's email address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used to record the patient's medical history.
* **Other Medical History** (`other_medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional medical history not mentioned in the previous field.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional): This field is used to record any medications the patient is allergic to.
* **Other Medication Allergies** (`other_medical_allergies`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional medication allergies not mentioned in the previous field.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): This field is used to record any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`other_medical_conditions`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional medical conditions not mentioned in the previous field.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): This field is used to record any medical tests the patient has undergone.
* **Other Medical Tests** (`other_medical_tests`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional medical tests not mentioned in the previous field.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`current_medical_conditions`, text, optional): This field is used to record the patient's current medical conditions.
* **Other Current Medical Conditions** (`other_current_medical_conditions`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional current medical conditions not mentioned in the previous field.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional): This field is used to record the patient's social history.
* **Other Social History** (`other_social_history`, text, optional): This field is used to record any additional social history not mentioned in the previous field.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the patient's primary care physician or healthcare team for guidance.
* Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible care for the patient.
