# Cotc Nursing Information Meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from nurses and other staff for the Cotc Nursing Information Meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your meeting title in the "meeting_title" field.
2. Enter your registration date in the "registration_date" field in the desired format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter your registration time in the "registration_time" field in the desired 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "note" field.
7. Select one option from the "which_one" dropdown menu.
8. Select multiple options from the "select_multiple" dropdown menu.
9. Enter a value for the "required_field" field, as this is a required field.
10. Enter a value for the "non_required_field" field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **meeting_title** (Text, Required: False): Enter a brief title for the meeting.
* **registration_date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date of your registration in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **registration_time** (Time, Required: False): Enter the time of your registration in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **note** (Text, Required: False): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **which_one** (Select One, Required: False): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **select_multiple** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **required_field** (Text, Required: True): This is a required field to be filled in.
* **non_required_field** (Text, Required: False): This field is optional and does not need to be filled in.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid errors.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Double-check your selections for select fields.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, contact the relevant personnel.
