# Patient Waiting List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Waiting List form is designed to collect and manage patient information for appointments and visits. This form helps healthcare professionals prioritize and schedule appointments, ensuring that patients are seen in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in their respective fields.
2. If applicable, provide the patient's **Phone Number** for efficient communication.
3. Select the **Date** and **Time** of the appointment.
4. Add any additional **Note** about the patient's appointment.
5. Choose the **Category** of the appointment (e.g., Waiting List 1, Waiting List 2, or Waiting List 3).
6. Set the **Priority** of the appointment (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Update the **Status** of the appointment (Active, Archived, or Cancelled).
8. Select the **Assigned Tool** responsible for the appointment (e.g., chatjimmy, chatjane, or chatjohn).
9. Enter any unique **ID** number for the patient.
10. Provide a brief **Description** of the appointment.
11. Optionally, select an additional **Category 2** for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for efficient communication.
* **Date**: Select the date of the appointment.
* **Time**: Select the time of the appointment.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's appointment.
* **Category**: Choose the category of the appointment (e.g., Waiting List 1, Waiting List 2, or Waiting List 3).
* **Priority**: Set the priority of the appointment (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Status**: Update the status of the appointment (Active, Archived, or Cancelled).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool responsible for the appointment (e.g., chatjimmy, chatjane, or chatjohn).
* **ID**: Enter any unique ID number for the patient.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the appointment.
* **Category 2**: Optionally, select an additional category for the appointment.
