# Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients before undergoing plastic surgery. It helps the doctor understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and other relevant details to ensure a safe and successful procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions accurately and completely.
2. If you have any medical history or allergies, please describe them in detail.
3. Confirm your medical procedure with the doctor.
4. Sign the "Consent" field to acknowledge your understanding of the procedure.
5. Sign both the "Patient Signature" and "Doctor Signature" fields as a confirmation of the patient's and doctor's understanding of the procedure.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering all questions.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please ask the doctor.
- This form is for your safety and the doctor's protection.
- Please sign the "Consent" field only after reading and understanding the procedure.
- The doctor will review and discuss this form with you before proceeding with the procedure.
- Please ask for clarification if you're unsure about any field.
- Review the form carefully before signing.
