# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient registration information for a hospital setting, including medical history, contact information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Details section with your personal information.
2. Provide the contact information of your emergency contact person in the Contact Info section.
3. Summarize your medical history in the Medical History section.
4. List any allergies you have in the Allergies section.
5. Provide information about any medical conditions you are currently experiencing in the Medical Conditions section.
6. List any medication you are currently taking in the Medication section.
7. List any medical devices you are currently using in the Medical Devices section.
8. Provide your insurance information in the Insurance Info section.
9. Summarize any emergency procedures you have undergone in the Emergency Procedures section.
10. Add any additional medical notes or comments in the Medical Notes section.
11. Sign and date your name in the Patient Signature section to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
12. Review and sign the form in the Doctor Signature section to confirm that you have reviewed the patient's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details (1)** (`text`, required): This section is for you to provide your personal details such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Contact Info (2)** (`text`, required): This section asks for the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical History (3)** (`text`, required): This section is for you to provide a brief summary of your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact (4)** (`text`, required): This section asks for the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Allergies (5)** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to list any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions (6)** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medication (7)** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about any medication you are currently taking.
* **Medical Devices (8)** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about any medical devices you are currently using.
* **Insurance Info (9)** (`text`, required): This section is for you to provide your insurance information.
* **Emergency Procedures (10)** (`text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any emergency procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical Notes (11)** (`text`, optional): This section is for any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Patient Signature (12)** (`text`, required): This section is for you to sign and date your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Doctor Signature (13)** (`text`, required): This section is for your doctor to sign and date their name to confirm that they have reviewed the patient's information.
