# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient registration information for a hospital setting, including medical history, contact information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Details section with your personal information.
2. Provide the contact information of your emergency contact person in the Contact Info section.
3. Summarize your medical history in the Medical History section.
4. List any allergies you have in the Allergies section.
5. Provide information about any medical conditions you are currently experiencing in the Medical Conditions section.
6. List any medication you are currently taking in the Medication section.
7. List any medical devices you are currently using in the Medical Devices section.
8. Provide your insurance information in the Insurance Info section.
9. Summarize any emergency procedures you have undergone in the Emergency Procedures section.
10. Add any additional medical notes or comments in the Medical Notes section.
11. Sign and date your name in the Patient Signature section to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
12. Review and sign the form in the Doctor Signature section to confirm that you have reviewed the patient's information.
