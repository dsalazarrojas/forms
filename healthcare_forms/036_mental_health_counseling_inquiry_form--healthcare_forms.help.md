# mental_health_counseling_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health counseling inquiry form is designed for users to gather information about their mental health concerns and needs. This form will help the counselor understand the user's situation and provide the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "General Information" page and enter your basic details.
2. On the "Contact Information" page, indicate whether you have a contact person who can help in case of an emergency.
3. On the "Mental Health Information" page, select the issues that concern you.
4. On the "Medical Information" page, enter any relevant medical history or conditions.
5. On the "Emergency Contact" page, select the contact information for your emergency contact.
6. On the "Mental Health Support" page, enter any mental health support you need.
7. On the "Medical Support" page, enter any medical support you need.
8. On the "Medical Insurance" page, enter your medical insurance information (if applicable).
9. Finally, on the "Billing" page, indicate whether you want to be billed for services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_informations`, text, not required): Enter your basic details such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_informations`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether you have a contact person who can help in case of an emergency. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Mental Health Information** (`mental_health_informations`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the mental health issues that concern you.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, not required): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Mental Health Support** (`mental_health_support`, text, not required): Enter any mental health support you need.
* **Medical Support** (`medical_support`, text, not required): Enter any medical support you need.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, not required): Enter your medical insurance information (if applicable).
* **Billing** (`billing`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate whether you want to be billed for services. Select "Yes" or "No".
