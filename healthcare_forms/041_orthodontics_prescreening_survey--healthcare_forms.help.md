# Orthodontics PreScreening Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Orthodontics PreScreening Survey is a form designed to gather essential information from patients about their medical history, allergies, and emergency contacts before undergoing orthodontic treatment. This helps orthodontic professionals identify potential risks or contraindications and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each field and follow the instructions provided.
2. Enter your name and date of birth in the required fields (patient_name, date_of_birth).
3. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your abilities.
4. If you have medical conditions, list them in the medical_condition field and any other conditions in the medical_condition_other field.
5. If you have allergies, select all that apply from the list of options.
6. For medication, list the types of medication you are currently taking.
7. Provide emergency contact information in the emergency_contact and emergency_contact2 fields.
8. List any medical devices you have in your home in the medical_device, medical_device2, and medical_device3 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Select any of the following conditions if you have them:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Hypertension
	+ Allergies
	+ None
* **Other Medical Condition** (`medical_condition_other`, text, optional): If you have any other medical condition not listed above, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): If you have any relevant medical history, enter it here.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List the types of medication you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, select_multiple, optional): Select any of the following allergies if you have them:
	+ Peanuts
	+ Gluten
	+ Soy
	+ Dairy
	+ None
* **Medical Device** (`medical_device`, text, optional): List any medical devices you have in your home.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact2`, text, optional): Enter the name of another emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Medical Device 2** (`medical_device2`, text, optional): List any other medical devices you have in your home.
* **Medical Device 3** (`medical_device3`, text, optional): List any other medical devices you have in your home.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your orthodontic professional for assistance.
* If you need help, contact our office for support.
