# COVID 19 Symptoms Self Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Symptoms Self Report Form is designed to gather information about a person's health status regarding COVID-19 symptoms. This form is intended to provide medical professionals with the necessary data to assess and provide guidance on the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above and fill in the fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: Enter the current date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Report Time**: Enter the current time.
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the five-digit ZIP code of your home address.
* **What is your primary symptom**: Select the main symptom you are experiencing.
* **Select all symptoms you are experiencing**: Choose all symptoms you are experiencing from the provided list.
* **When did your symptoms start**: Enter the date when your symptoms began.
* **Are your symptoms getting better, worse, or staying the same**: Select the symptom progression status.
* **How severe are your symptoms**: Rate the severity of your symptoms on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Do you currently have a fever**: Select the fever status.
* **What is your current temperature**: Enter your current temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Are you having trouble breathing**: Select the breathing status.
* **Are your symptoms limiting your daily activities**: Select the functional status.
* **Are you able to work**: Select the work status.
* **Are you currently isolated at home**: Select the isolation status.
* **Do you live with anyone who is high-risk**: Select the household status.
* **Have you taken any medication for your symptoms**: Select the medication status.
* **List any medications you have taken**: Enter any medications taken.
* **What home remedies are you using**: Enter any home remedies used.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information.
* **I attest that the information provided is true and accurate**: Select the truth status.
