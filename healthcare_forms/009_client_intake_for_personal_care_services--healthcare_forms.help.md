# Client Intake For Personal Care Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This client intake form is designed to gather personal and health-related information from clients for effective care services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your Full Name and Phone Number. These are required fields and must be accurate and up-to-date.
2. Enter your Email Address, which is also a required field.
3. Select your Primary Service Requested from the given options, as this field is also required.
4. For the Frequency field, choose how often you typically receive the service you selected.
5. If you have any chronic health conditions or concerns, list them in the space provided.
6. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, specify them in the field provided.
7. Check if you have sensitive skin, as this may affect the service provided.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to follow all health and safety protocols during your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name accurately.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): This field is not required but may be helpful for our records.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Ensure this is up-to-date and accurate.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Service Details** (`section_service`, `note`, optional): This section is for additional service-related details.
* **Primary Service Requested** (`service_requested`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options provided.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you typically receive the service.
* **Health Information** (`section_health`, `note`, optional): This section is for any additional health concerns or conditions.
* **Chronic Conditions or Concerns** (`chronic_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any chronic health conditions or concerns you have.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (`allergy_list`, `text`, optional): Specify any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Sensitive Skin** (`sensitive_skin`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you have sensitive skin.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`section_protocols`, `note`, optional): This section is for acknowledging our health and safety protocols.
* **Protocol Agreement** (`protocol_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to follow all health and safety protocols.
* **Today's Date** (`signed_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically filled in based on the current date.

## Tips

* Please double-check the accuracy of your input before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our care service provider.
* The care service provider will review and process your information carefully and efficiently.
