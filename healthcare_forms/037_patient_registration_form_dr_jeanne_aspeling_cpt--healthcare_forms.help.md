# Patient Registration Form Dr. Jeanne Aspeling (CPT) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients about their personal details, medical history, and contact information. Completing this form accurately and truthfully helps Dr. Jeanne Aspeling (CPT) provide the best possible care for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and date of birth accurately.
2.  Provide your contact information (phone number and email address).
3.  Indicate your medical condition (if applicable).
4.  Select the assigned doctor and medical specialty (if applicable).
5.  Specify your insurance provider (if applicable).
6.  Enter the details of your emergency contact (name, relationship, and contact information).
7.  If you have a medical record number, please enter it for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication with Dr. Jeanne Aspeling (CPT) and his team.
- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your home or work address for easy contact and visitation.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please select 'Yes' to indicate this.
- **Assigned Doctor** (`assigned_doctor`, select_one, optional): If you have been assigned a doctor, select 'Yes' to confirm.
- **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, optional): If you have a specific medical specialty, select 'Yes' to confirm.
- **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_multiple, optional): If you have an insurance provider, select 'Yes' to confirm.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and details.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): If you have a medical record number, enter it for reference.

## Tips
- Please ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to ask Dr. Jeanne Aspeling (CPT) or her team for assistance.
- This form is for your medical registration and history, please fill it out carefully to ensure your medical needs are met.
