# Dental Clinic Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book a dental clinic appointment. It gathers the required information from the patient to schedule the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date for your appointment in the "Date" field.
6. Choose a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
7. Select the type of service you need (Cleaning, Check-up, or Emergency) in the "Service" field.
8. Add any additional information you would like to include in the "Additional Information" field (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (date, required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time** (time, required): Choose a time for your appointment from the available options.
* **Service** (select_one, required): Choose the type of service you need (Cleaning, Check-up, or Emergency).
* **Additional Information** (note, optional): Add any additional information you would like to include about your appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth booking process.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, include them in the "Additional Information" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the dental clinic staff for assistance.
