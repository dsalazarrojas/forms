# Mpox Vaccine Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Vaccine Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users interested in signing up for the Mpox vaccine. This form helps us gather relevant health information, contact details, and preferences for the vaccine administration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required". These fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are essential for us to process your application.
2. For text fields, enter your response in the designated text box. Please make sure to write clearly and concisely.
3. For select_one fields, choose one of the options provided from the dropdown menu.
4. For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and contact information in this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your address and any other relevant contact details in this field.
* **Vaccination Preferences** (`vaccination_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your preferences for the vaccine administration process in this field.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please describe them briefly in this field.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your eligibility for the vaccine. Options: Yes / No.
* **Medical Condition (other)** (`medical_condition_other`, text, optional):
	+ If you selected "Yes" for the medical condition field, please describe your condition in this field.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check if you have any allergies that may affect your eligibility for the vaccine. Options: Yes / No.
* **Vaccination Sites** (`vaccination_sites`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check if you would like to receive the vaccine at a specific site. Options: Yes / No.
* **Vaccine Schedule** (`vaccine_schedule`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you are available for the vaccine administration at any time. Options: Yes / No.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, select_one, optional):
	+ Check if you have received the vaccine before and need a specific dose. Options: Yes / No.

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our medical team for assistance.
- Please make sure to submit your form accurately and truthfully, as this will help us provide the best possible care.
- If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please make sure to describe them clearly and completely.
