# Medical History Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect medical history information for children, covering various aspects of their health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with the child's parent or guardian for guidance.
3.  Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4.  You can add additional comments or notes to provide further information, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Common Labor Issues (1) 
*   **Common Labor Issues**: This field is used to capture information about the child's common labor issues. If you have trouble understanding this question, please consider it as asking for any health or medical issues that the child has experienced or is currently experiencing.

### Pregnancy Issues (2) 
*   **Pregnancy Issues**: This field is used to gather information about any issues that occurred during the child's pregnancy or birth. 

### Birth Defects (3) 
*   **Birth Defects**: This field is intended to capture any birth defects or abnormalities the child has been born with.

### Family History (4) 
*   **Family History**: This field is used to gather information about any medical history that the child's parents or siblings may have.

### Medical History (5) 
*   **Medical History**: This field is used to gather a comprehensive overview of the child's medical history, including any chronic conditions or diseases they have been diagnosed with.

### Immunization (6) 
*   **Immunization**: This field is used to capture information about the child's immunization history.

### Allergy (7) 
*   **Allergy**: This field is used to gather information about any allergies that the child has.

### Current Health Status (8) 
*   **Current Health Status**: This field is used to capture the child's current health status, including any current health issues or concerns.

### Previous Conditions (9) 
*   **Previous Medical Conditions**: This field is used to gather information about any previous medical conditions that the child has had.

### Previous Surgeries (10) 
*   **Previous Surgeries**: This field is used to capture any previous surgeries that the child has undergone.

### Current Medications (11) 
*   **Current Medications**: This field is used to gather information about any current medications the child is taking.

### Additional Comments (12) 
*   **Additional Comments**: This field is used for any additional comments or information that you feel is important for the child's medical history.

**Tips**

*   Please be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
*   If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with the child's parent or guardian for guidance.
*   You can add or remove questions based on the child's specific needs.
*   Remember to sign and date the form to keep a record of the child's medical history.
