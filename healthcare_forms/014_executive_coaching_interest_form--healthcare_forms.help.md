# executive_coaching_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from executives about their interest in executive coaching. The form is designed to be completed by executives who are considering or have already started with executive coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and make sure you understand each question.
2. Read each field's label and hint for guidance.
3. Fill out the form with your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Executive Coaching Interest** (`executive_coaching_interest`, `note`, required: false)
Indicate your level of interest in executive coaching by typing a brief note explaining why you are interested or not.

### 2. **Work with Coach** (`work_with_coach`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that apply to how you would like to work with a coach. This could include options like "Individual sessions", "Group coaching", "Peer coaching", etc.

### 3. **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the reason(s) you are interested in executive coaching. This could include options like "Leadership development", "Career growth", "Job satisfaction", etc.

### 4. **Coaching Preferences** (`coaching_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that describe your preferences for executive coaching. This could include options like "Flexibility in schedule", "Confidentiality", "Coaching location", etc.

### 5. **Work Style** (`work_style`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select how you work best. This could include options like "Independent", "Collaborative", "Structured", etc.

### 6. **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate your level of job satisfaction by selecting one of the two options: "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".

### 7. **Leadership Development** (`leadership_development`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the areas where you would like to develop your leadership skills.

### 8. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
If you have any feedback or comments about executive coaching, please type them here.

### 9. **Executive Coaching Commitment** (`executive_coaching_commitment`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate your commitment level to executive coaching by selecting one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".

### 10. **Support Systems** (`support_systems`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that describe the support systems you expect to be in place during executive coaching. This could include options like "Access to resources", "Regular check-ins", "Coaching community", etc.

### 11. **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that describe your preferred communication style. This could include options like "Regular updates", "Flexible scheduling", "Direct communication", etc.

### 12. **Self-Development Plan** (`self_development_plan`, `note`, required: false)
Create a brief note about your self-development plan, including the areas you'd like to focus on and how you plan to achieve your goals.

### 13. **Executive Coaching Satisfaction** (`executive_coaching_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
Indicate your expected level of satisfaction with executive coaching by selecting one of the two options: "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can select all or some of the options that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the coach or your HR representative.
* This form will only take a few minutes to complete, and your feedback will help us better serve you with executive coaching services.
