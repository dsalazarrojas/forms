# Dental Patient Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Patient Information Update Form is designed to collect and update patient information in the dental care industry. This form helps to gather relevant data about a patient's personal details, ensuring that your records are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to update a patient's information.
2. Verify that you have not already submitted an update for this patient.
3. Fill out the form with the correct and accurate information.
4. Double-check your entries for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's current address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's state.

## Tips
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities or patient representative.
* Please do not submit incomplete or inaccurate information.
