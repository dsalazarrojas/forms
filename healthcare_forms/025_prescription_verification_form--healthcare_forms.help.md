# Prescription Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Verification Form is a tool used to verify the details of a patient's prescription. It helps ensure that patient information and prescription details are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are filled out completely.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Select the correct "prescription_type" from the dropdown menu (e.g., Rx, Non-Rx).
4. Enter the "dosing_start_date" and "dosing_end_date" for the patient's medication.
5. Enter the "medication_name" for the prescribed medication.
6. Enter the patient's "patient_address" and "patient_phone" if applicable.
7. Enter the "prescriber_name", "prescriber_address", and "prescriber_phone" if applicable.
8. Enter the "prescriber_email" and "prescriber_license_number" if applicable.
9. Select the "prescriber_license_status" from the dropdown menu (e.g., Active, Inactive).
10. Enter the "medication_strength" and "medication_route" for the prescribed medication.
11. Enter the "dosing_frequency" and "dosing_frequency_unit" for the patient's medication.
12. Enter the "dosing_instructions" for the patient's medication.
13. Enter any relevant "patient_allergies" and "patient_medication_allergies".
14. Enter the "prescriber_fax" and "prescriber_doctype" if applicable.
15. Enter the "prescription_number" for the prescription.
16. Finally, obtain a signature from the prescriber in the "prescriber_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **prescription_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of prescription (e.g., Rx, Non-Rx).
* **dosing_start_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the medication was to start.
* **dosing_end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the medication is to stop.
* **medication_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the prescribed medication.
* **patient_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the patient's address if different from their prescriber's address.
* **prescriber_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the prescriber's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **prescriber_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the prescriber's address.
* **prescriber_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the prescriber's phone number.
* **prescriber_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the prescriber's email address.
* **prescriber_license_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the prescriber's license number.
* **prescriber_license_status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of the prescriber's license (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **prescriber_doctype** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of the prescriber's license (e.g., MD, DO).
* **prescription_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of the prescription.
* **prescriber_fax** (Text, Optional): Enter the prescriber's fax number.
* **medication_strength** (Text, Optional): Enter the strength of the prescribed medication.
* **medication_route** (Text, Optional): Enter the route of administration for the medication.
* **dosing_frequency** (Text, Optional): Enter the frequency of dosing for the medication.
* **dosing_frequency_unit** (Select One, Optional): Choose the unit of dosing frequency (e.g., Days, Weeks, Months).
* **dosing_instructions** (Note, Optional): Enter any specific instructions for the medication.
* **patient_allergies** (Text, Optional): Enter any allergies the patient may have.
* **patient_medication_allergies** (Text, Optional): Enter any medication allergies the patient may have.
* **prescriber_signature** (Text, Optional): Obtain a signature from the prescriber.
