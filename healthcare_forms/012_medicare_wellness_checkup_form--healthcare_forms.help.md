# medicare_wellness_checkup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medicare Wellness Checkup Form is designed to provide healthcare professionals with a comprehensive set of questions to perform a thorough checkup on patients. This form collects patient information and medical data to ensure accurate and reliable patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are familiar with the required fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, including the patient's medical history and any relevant medical conditions.
3. Select the correct options for the Medical Doctor and State fields.
4. Enter the patient's contact information, including email and phone number (if applicable).
5. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Organization** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical organization that the patient is visiting.
* **Medical Doctor** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the name of the doctor that will be performing the wellness checkup.
* **Patient Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Visit** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Patient Weight** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Height** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the patient's height in feet.
* **Medical Conditions** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical conditions that the patient has.
* **Medication** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **City** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where the patient resides.
* **State** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the state where the patient resides.
* **Zip** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Medical License Number** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's medical license number (if applicable).
* **Medical Group** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the medical group that the patient belongs to.
* **Patient Notes** (`18`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any notes relevant to the patient's care.
* **Medical Notes** (`19`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any medical notes relevant to the patient's care.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`20`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the last contact with the patient.
* **Contact Method** (`21`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the method of contact that was used last (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Contact Number** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact number that was used (if applicable).
* **Date of Next Appointment** (`23`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the next appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`24`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the next appointment.
* **Submitted By** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
