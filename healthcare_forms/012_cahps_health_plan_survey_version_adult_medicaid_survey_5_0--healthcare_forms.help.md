# CAHPS Health Plan Survey - Adult Medicaid 5.0 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction information from Adult Medicaid beneficiaries about their current health plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your experiences with your current Medicaid plan.
2. Select the answer that best reflects your satisfaction or experience with each question.
3. For questions with a numerical rating (e.g., "health_plan_rating"), use the provided rating scale to select your response.
4. If you have feedback or comments about your plan, please provide them in the "feedback_text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of your Medicaid health plan**: Please enter the name of your current Medicaid health plan.
* **How long have you been enrolled in this plan?**: Select the duration of your enrollment in the plan.
	+ Less than 6 months: You have been enrolled in the plan for less than 6 months.
	+ 6 months to 1 year: You have been enrolled in the plan for 6 months to 1 year.
	+ 1 to 2 years: You have been enrolled in the plan for 1 to 2 years.
	+ More than 2 years: You have been enrolled in the plan for more than 2 years.
* **How often was it easy to get appointments with specialists?**: Select how often you found it easy to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Never: You have never found it easy to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Sometimes: You have occasionally found it easy to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Usually: You have generally found it easy to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Always: You have always found it easy to get appointments with specialists.
* **When you needed care right away, how often did you get it as soon as you needed?**: Select how often you received care when you needed it.
	+ Never: You have never received care when you needed it.
	+ Sometimes: You have occasionally received care when you needed it.
	+ Usually: You have generally received care when you needed it.
	+ Always: You have always received care when you needed it.
* **Are you satisfied with the number of doctors you can choose from in your plan?**: Select your level of satisfaction with the number of doctors you can choose from.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the number of doctors you can choose from.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the number of doctors you can choose from.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the number of doctors you can choose from.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the number of doctors you can choose from.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the number of doctors you can choose from.
* **How often did your health plan's customer service give you the information you needed?**: Select how often you received the information you needed from the plan's customer service.
	+ Never: You have never received the information you needed from the plan's customer service.
	+ Sometimes: You have occasionally received the information you needed from the plan's customer service.
	+ Usually: You have generally received the information you needed from the plan's customer service.
	+ Always: You have always received the information you needed from the plan's customer service.
* **How often were the forms you had to fill out for your plan easy to complete?**: Select how often you found the forms easy to complete.
	+ Never: You have never found the forms easy to complete.
	+ Sometimes: You have occasionally found the forms easy to complete.
	+ Usually: You have generally found the forms easy to complete.
	+ Always: You have always found the forms easy to complete.
* **Did the plan provide clear information about how much you would have to pay for care?**: Select whether the plan provided clear information about costs.
	+ Yes, always: The plan always provided clear information about costs.
	+ Mostly: The plan mostly provided clear information about costs.
	+ No, it was confusing: The plan did not provide clear information about costs.
* **Do you feel your plan helps coordinate your care between different doctors?**: Select whether you feel the plan helps coordinate your care.
	+ True: You feel the plan helps coordinate your care.
	+ False: You do not feel the plan helps coordinate your care.
	+ Not Applicable: You are not applicable to answer this question.
* **Rating of your plan's prescription drug coverage**: Select your rating of the plan's prescription drug coverage.
	+ Poor: You are very dissatisfied with the plan's prescription drug coverage.
	+ Fair: You are dissatisfied with the plan's prescription drug coverage.
	+ Good: You are neutral about the plan's prescription drug coverage.
	+ Very Good: You are satisfied with the plan's prescription drug coverage.
	+ Excellent: You are very satisfied with the plan's prescription drug coverage.
* **Overall rating of your health plan**: Select your overall rating of the plan.
	+ 0: This is the worst rating.
	+ 1: This is a very bad rating.
	+ 2: This is a bad rating.
	+ 3: This is a neutral rating.
	+ 4: This is a good rating.
	+ 5: This is a very good rating.
	+ 6: This is a best rating.
	+ 7: This is a better rating.
	+ 8: This is a better rating.
	+ 9: This is a best rating.
	+ 10: This is the best rating.
* **Would you recommend this health plan to your friends or family?**: Select whether you would recommend the plan to friends or family.
	+ Definitely Yes: You would definitely recommend the plan to friends or family.
	+ Probably Yes: You would probably recommend the plan to friends or family.
	+ Probably No: You would probably not recommend the plan to friends or family.
	+ Definitely No: You would definitely not recommend the plan to friends or family.
* **Please provide any specific feedback about your health plan**: Use this field to provide any feedback or comments about your health plan.
