# Medication Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Tracker Form is designed to help healthcare professionals monitor and track patient medication intake.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date of the morning schedule from the "Morning" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the "Dosage" field with the number of milligrams or units of the medication taken.
3. Enter the time the medication was taken.
4. Enter the name of the medication.
5. Enter the frequency of the medication (e.g. daily, weekly, etc.).
6. Add any notes about the medication (e.g. side effects, interactions, etc.).
7. Enter the next dose schedule (if applicable).
8. Enter the last dose taken (if applicable).
9. Enter the date and time of the last dose (if applicable).
10. Enter any other last details about the last dose (e.g. amount taken, etc.).
11. Select the time of the next dose.
12. Select the Patient ID(s) of the patient(s) for whom you are recording medication information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning** (`1`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the medication schedule from the dropdown menu.
* **Dosage** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of milligrams or units of the medication taken.
* **Time** (`3`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the medication was taken.
* **Medication** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the medication.
* **Frequency** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the frequency of the medication (e.g. daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Notes** (`6`, `note`, optional): Add any notes about the medication (e.g. side effects, interactions, etc.).
* **Next Dose** (`7`, `number`, optional): Enter the next dose schedule (if applicable).
* **Dose** (`8`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of the medication taken (if applicable).
* **Last Dose Date** (`9`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last dose taken.
* **Last Dose Time** (`10`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the last dose taken.
* **Last Dose** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter any other details about the last dose (e.g. amount taken, etc.).
* **Next Dose Time** (`12`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the next dose.
* **Patient ID** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the Patient ID(s) of the patient(s) for whom you are recording medication information.

## Tips

* Use the "Morning" field to select the correct date for the medication schedule.
* Use the "Dosage" field to enter the exact amount of medication taken.
* Use the "Time" field to record the time the medication was taken.
* Use the "Medication" field to enter the name of the medication being taken.
* Use the "Frequency" field to indicate how often the medication is taken.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional information about the medication.
* Use the "Next Dose" field to schedule the next dose.
* Use the "Last Dose" fields to record the date and time of the last dose taken.
* Use the "Next Dose Time" field to schedule the next dose time.
* Use the "Patient ID" field to select the patient(s) for whom you are recording medication information.
