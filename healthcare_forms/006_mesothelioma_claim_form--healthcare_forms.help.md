# Mesothelioma Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mesothelioma Claim Form is designed to collect patient information, medical history, and insurance details related to mesothelioma diagnosis. It is a crucial document for processing claims and ensuring accurate medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:
1. Enter your patient's identifier number.
2. Provide your patient's name.
3. Enter your contact number.
4. Specify the claim date.
5. Select your insurance company from the provided options.
6. Enter the claim amount.
7. Describe your claim in detail.
8. Confirm your physician's confirmation of the mesothelioma diagnosis.
9. Specify your treatment plan for the mesothelioma diagnosis.
10. Upload or provide any relevant claim documents.
11. Enter your medical provider's information.
12. Provide your medical provider's contact information.
13. Specify your patient's family history regarding the mesothelioma diagnosis.
14. Select your claim status.
15. Enter your patient's address.
16. Enter your patient's city.
17. Specify your patient's state (related to the claim address).
18. Enter your patient's ZIP code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier**: Enter your patient's unique identifier number.
* **Patient Name**: Enter your patient's name in full, as it appears on their ID or driver's license.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further information or questions.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **Insurance Company**: Select the insurance company that covers your patient's mesothelioma treatment.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the total amount you are claiming for the treatment.
* **Claim Description**: Describe your claim in detail, including any relevant medical history or treatment plans.
* **Physician Confirmation**: Confirm your physician's mesothelioma diagnosis.
* **Patient Diagnosis**: Select the type of mesothelioma diagnosis (malignant pleural mesothelioma or other).
* **Treatment Plan**: Specify the treatment plan for your patient's mesothelioma diagnosis (yes or no).
* **Claim Documents**: Upload or provide any relevant claim documents (e.g., medical records, bills, or receipts).
* **Medical Provider**: Enter your medical provider's name and title.
* **Medical Provider Contact Info**: Enter your medical provider's contact information (e.g., phone number, address).
* **Patient Family History**: Specify your patient's family history regarding mesothelioma (yes or no).
* **Claim Status**: Select the current status of your claim (active or inactive).
* **Patient Address**: Enter your patient's claim address.
* **Patient City**: Enter your patient's city.
* **Patient State**: Select the state where your patient's claim address is located (yes or no).
* **Patient ZIP**: Enter your patient's ZIP code.
