# new_patient_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from new patients. This form helps our medical staff understand the patient's medical history, current conditions, and insurance details, ensuring a smooth and efficient intake process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details as prompted on the **Patient Info** page.
2. Share your medical history on the **Medical History** page.
3. Enter your insurance information on the **Insurance Info** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): 
  Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): 
  Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies. Be as detailed as possible to help our medical team provide the best care.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): 
  Share your insurance provider and policy number for accurate billing purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can modify this form as needed to suit your specific needs.
* Contact us at <form@gic.mx> if you have any questions or need further assistance.
