# guest_self_declaration_form_covid_19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests about their travel history, symptoms, and other relevant details in the context of a COVID-19 scenario.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the answer for "Guest Details" from the options provided.
* Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
* Enter the guest's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
* Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
* Select the answer for "Travel History" from the options provided.
* Enter any symptoms the guest is experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
* Select the answer for "Travel Restrictions" from the options provided.
* Enter the date and time the guest was last contacted in the "Last Contacted" field.
* Enter the time of arrival in the "Time Arrived" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the guest has any travel history or symptoms that may affect their stay.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's name in this field.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the guest's contact number in this field.
* **Email**: Enter the guest's email address in this field.
* **Travel History**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the guest has any travel history that may affect their stay.
* **Symptoms**: If the guest is experiencing any symptoms, enter them in this field.
* **Travel Restrictions**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the guest has any travel restrictions that may affect their stay.
* **Last Contacted**: Enter the date and time the guest was last contacted in this field. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD" for date and "HH:MM" for time.
* **Time Arrived**: Enter the time the guest arrived in the "HH:MM" format.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* If the guest has any travel history or symptoms, please select "Yes" in the corresponding fields.
* If the guest is experiencing symptoms, describe them in detail in the "Symptoms" field.
* If the guest has any travel restrictions, please select "Yes" in the "Travel Restrictions" field.
* Date and time fields should be entered in the formats "YYYY-MM-DD" and "HH:MM" respectively.
