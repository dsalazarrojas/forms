# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect data on customers' COVID-19 vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field. The format should be mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
6. If you have received the vaccination, enter the date you received it in the Vaccination Date field. If not, leave this field blank.
7. If you have received the vaccination, enter the site where you received it in the Vaccination Site field. If not, leave this field blank.
8. If you have received the vaccination, enter the medical office where you received it in the Medical Office field. If not, leave this field blank.
9. If you have received multiple doses of the vaccination, enter the number of doses you have received in the Vaccination Dose field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, `date`, required/optional): If you have received the vaccination, enter the date you received it. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, `text`, required/optional): If you have received the vaccination, enter the site where you received it. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Medical Office** (`medical_office`, `text`, required/optional): If you have received the vaccination, enter the medical office where you received it. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Vaccination Dose** (`vaccination_dose`, `number`, required/optional): If you have received multiple doses of the vaccination, enter the number of doses you have received.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have not already completed this form before.
- Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact information].
