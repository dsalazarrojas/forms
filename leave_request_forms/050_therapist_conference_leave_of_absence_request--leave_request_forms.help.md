This help guide is intended for use by therapists who are requesting a leave of absence for a conference. This form is used to notify their supervisor and team about the request and ensure all necessary approvals are obtained before the leave is approved. Please review the form carefully before completing it to ensure you are requesting the correct leave dates and providing all necessary information. If you have previously requested a leave of absence for the same dates, please do not submit this form as your supervisor will not be able to approve the duplicate request.

## Therapist Conference Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a leave of absence for a conference from your work schedule. This form should be used when you have a pre-approved conference or workshop that you need to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the start and end dates of your conference.
2. Specify the number of days off you are requesting.
3. Describe your purpose for the request.
4. Select your supervisor's approval level (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Select the team leadership approvals you are requesting (Option 1, Option 2, and/or Option 3).
6. Provide any additional information related to your request.
7. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Request** (`conference_request`, text, required/optional): This field should be filled with a brief description of the conference or workshop you plan to attend.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your conference or workshop.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your conference or workshop.
* **Number of Days Off** (`days_off`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of days off you are requesting.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required/optional): Describe your reason for requesting this leave of absence.
* **Supervisor Review** (`supervisor_review`, select_one, required/optional): Select your supervisor's level of approval for this request.
* **Team Leadership Approvals** (`team_leadership_approvals`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select which team leadership approvals you are requesting.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional context or information related to your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so your supervisor and team can contact you regarding your request.
