# Fashion Show Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Leave of Absence Request form is designed to allow employees to submit leave requests for Fashion Shows where they have a role. This form will gather necessary information to ensure that your absence is properly documented and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event date and time of the Fashion Show you will be attending.
2. Enter the duration of the event.
3. Enter your role in the event.
4. Select the shift you will be working.
5. If you are not working on the day of the event, select 'True' for Time Off.
6. If you are working during work hours, select 'True' for Supervisor Notification.
7. Enter any notes about your leave request.
8. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the Fashion Show you will be attending.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the Fashion Show you will be attending.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift at the Fashion Show.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift at the Fashion Show.
* **Duration** (Duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of the event, e.g., number of hours.
* **Show Name** (Show Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the Fashion Show.
* **Role** (Role, text, optional): Enter your role in the Fashion Show, e.g., producer, coordinator.
* **Shift** (Shift, text, optional): Enter the shift you will be working, e.g., morning, afternoon.
* **Supervisor** (Supervisor, select_one, optional): Select your supervisor, options: Producer, Coordinator, Manager.
* **Notify Supervisor** (Notify Supervisor, select_multiple, optional): If you are working during work hours, select 'True' to notify your supervisor.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your leave request.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Staff** (Event Staff, select_multiple, optional): If you are working on the event, select 'True'.
* **Time Off** (Time Off, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you will be taking off during the event.
* **Approved** (Approved, select_multiple, optional): This field will be populated automatically after your request has been approved or rejected by your supervisor.

Please note that the form will only be submitted for Fashion Show events, and all fields are optional unless marked as required.
