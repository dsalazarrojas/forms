# Elderly Care Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elderly Care Leave Form is designed to help caregivers, family members, and caregivers' organizations to record and manage leave requests and dates related to elderly family members who need care. This form is used to collect information about the care recipient, the start and end dates of the leave, and the reason for the leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the elderly family member you are requesting leave for from the "Care For Family Member" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the start date of the leave in the "Start Date" field.
3. Enter the end date of the leave in the "End Date" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the reason for the leave in the "Reason For Leave" text field.
5. Enter the expected return date in the "Return Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Care For Family Member**: Select the elderly family member you are requesting leave for.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you will begin taking care of the elderly family member.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you will stop taking care of the elderly family member.
* **Reason For Leave**: Provide a brief description of why you are taking a leave of absence.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date when you will return to your caregiving duties.
