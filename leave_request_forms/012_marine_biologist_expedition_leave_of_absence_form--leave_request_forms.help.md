# Marine Biologist Expedition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a marine biologist's leave of absence for an expedition. It helps to document and track the biologist's absence, ensuring smooth communication and preventing non-duplication of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
2. Enter the dates of the expedition.
3. Provide information about the research institution, including the URL if applicable.
4. Specify the expedition type, location, and duration.
5. Enter contact information for the biologist, including their phone number and email.
6. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expedition Dates** (Expedition Dates, date, *): Enter the dates of the expedition.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, *): Enter the start date of the expedition.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, *): Enter the end date of the expedition.
* **Research Institution** (Research Institution, text): Enter the name of the research institution.
* **Lab** (Lab, text): Enter the lab number or name where the biologist will be working during the expedition.
* **Department** (Department, text): Enter the department of the research institution where the biologist will be working during the expedition.
* **Location** (Location, text): Enter the location of the research institution where the biologist will be staying during the expedition.
* **Reason for leaving** (Reason for leaving, text): Enter a short description of why the biologist is taking leave for the expedition.
* **Expected Return Date** (Expected Return Date, date, *): Enter the expected date of return from the expedition.
* **Actual Return Date** (Actual Return Date, date): Enter the actual date of return from the expedition.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text): Enter the name of the contact person at the research institution.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, *): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Research Institution URL** (Research Institution URL, text): Enter the URL of the research institution's website.
* **Expedition Type** (Expedition Type, text): Enter the type of expedition (e.g., research, conservation, expedition).
* **Expedition Location** (Expedition Location, text): Enter the location of the expedition.
* **Expedition Duration** (Expedition Duration, number): Enter the duration of the expedition in days.
