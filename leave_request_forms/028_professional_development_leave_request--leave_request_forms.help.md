# Professional Development Leave Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request professional development leave, which is time off granted to employees to attend training, attend conferences, or earn certifications for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Choose the correct options for your request.
3. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title as it is listed in the company directory.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to begin your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you wish to end your leave.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the training course you are requesting to take.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the location of the training course.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of your leave, which can be Half a day, Full day, Half days, or Multiple days.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the reason for your leave, which can be Training, Certification, Conference, or Other.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your manager has approved your request (Approved, Approved with conditions, or Not approved).
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have approved your own request (Approved, Not approved).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Scheduling Details** (`scheduling_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional details about your scheduling request.
* **Training Cost** (`training_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the cost of the training course.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, optional): If your manager has any comments, please enter them here.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or notes about your own request.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department you are affiliated with (HR, IT, Marketing, Sales).
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have a team lead (True or False).
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, select the manager(s) who approved your request.
* **Employee Approvals** (`employee_approvals`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, select the employee(s) who approved your request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your request was approved.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time your request was approved.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Choose who submitted the request (HR, IT, Manager, Other).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to you for this request.

Tips: Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form. Your manager may contact you for further information before finalizing your request.
