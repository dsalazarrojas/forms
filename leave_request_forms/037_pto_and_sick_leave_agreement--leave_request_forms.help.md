# PTO And Sick Leave Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form serves as an agreement between an employee and their manager and department head for managing PTO and sick leave. It helps establish clear expectations and guidelines for taking time off due to personal reasons or illness, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information.
2. If you are unsure about any field or section, please consult your department head or HR representative for guidance.
3. Fill out the form completely and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Policy Acknowledgments** (`employee_policy_acknowledgments`, note, optional): This section is a note field where you can acknowledge and agree to the policies outlined in the form. Please review the policies carefully and provide a clear acknowledgment of your understanding.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): This is where you sign the agreement, confirming that you have read and understood the policies.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name as it appears on your records.
* **Date Start** (`date_start`, date, optional): Enter the date you will be taking leave from.
* **Date End** (`date_end`, date, optional): Enter the date you will be returning to work.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your manager.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): This is where your manager signs to acknowledge their agreement and understanding of the policies.
* **Manager Date** (`manager_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your manager has signed the agreement.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name for identification purposes.
* **Department Head Signature** (`department_head_signature`, text, optional): This is where your department head signs to acknowledge their agreement and understanding of the policies.
* **Department Head Date** (`department_head_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your department head has signed the agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and understand the policies and procedures outlined in the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your department head or HR representative.
