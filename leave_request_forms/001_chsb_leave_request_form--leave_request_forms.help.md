# CHSB Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CHSB Leave Request Form is designed to help employees request a leave of absence from work due to various reasons such as vacation, sickness, or personal matters. This form is used to provide the necessary information for your supervisor to approve or decline your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your unique employee identification number in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your employee records in the "Full Name" field.
3. Select the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
4. Choose the type of leave you are requesting from the "Type of Leave" dropdown menu (e.g., Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).
5. Enter the start date of your leave in the "Leave Start Date" field.
6. Enter the end date of your leave in the "Leave End Date" field.
7. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
8. If you are available, enter a contact number where you can be reached during your absence in the "Contact Number During Absence" field.
9. If applicable, provide the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
10. Enter the name of your direct supervisor who will approve this request in the "Supervisor Name" field.
11. If possible, describe how your responsibilities will be covered during your absence in the "Coverage Plan During Absence" field.
12. Finally, enter the date you are submitting this request in the "Request Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Your unique employee identification number. This number is used to identify you as an employee in the system.

### Full Name
* **Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee records. This is used to verify your identity.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the department you belong to. This helps the supervisor understand your role and responsibilities during your absence.

### Type of Leave
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.). This helps the supervisor understand the nature of your leave.

### Leave Start Date
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave. This is essential for scheduling and planning purposes.

### Leave End Date
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to end your leave. This is also essential for scheduling and planning purposes.

### Total Number of Days
* **Total Number of Days** (`total_days`, number, optional): This field is automatically calculated based on the leave start and end dates. You do not need to fill this in, but it's available for your reference.

### Reason for Leave
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your leave. This helps the supervisor understand your situation.

### Contact Number During Absence
* **Contact Number During Absence** (`contact_during_absence`, text, optional): If you are available, enter a contact number where you can be reached during your absence.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your emergency contact. This is for your supervisor to contact in case of an emergency.

### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_approval`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor who will approve this request.

### Coverage Plan During Absence
* **Coverage Plan During Absence** (`coverage_plan`, text, optional): Describe how your responsibilities will be covered during your absence. This helps the supervisor plan and ensure business continuity.

### Request Submission Date
* **Request Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request. This is used to track request timelines.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your leave request is submitted in a timely manner to ensure it is processed before your absence.
* If your leave request is declined, you will receive feedback and instructions on how to resubmit your request.
