# Community Integration Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request leave for community integration activities such as volunteering, community board/committee service, and professional skills pro-bono work. This form is used to submit a request for leave for a specific period of time and to provide details about the activity, department, and employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the steps below.
  1. Enter your employee full name, ID number, and department information.
  2. Select the type of community integration leave you are requesting.
  3. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
  4. Specify the total hours you will be away from work.
  5. Provide the name of the community organization where you will be serving.
  6. Describe your planned activity and role.
  7. Explain the benefit of the activity to the local community.
  8. Check the supervisor preliminary approval status.
  9. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID badge.
- **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Department** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the department where you work.
- **Type of Community Integration Leave** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of community integration leave you are requesting.
  - Hands-on Volunteering
  - Community Board/Committee Service
  - Professional Skills Pro-bono
  - Civic Advocacy Event
  - Other Community Activity
- **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Total Work Hours Requested** (`total_hours`, number, required): Enter the total hours you will be away from work.
- **Name of the Community Organization** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the community organization where you will be serving.
- **Description of the Planned Community Activity** (`activity_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your role and activities.
- **How does this activity benefit the local community?** (`community_benefit`, text, required): Explain the positive impact of your activity on the local community.
- **Supervisor Preliminary Approval Status** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required): Check the supervisor preliminary approval status.
  - Pending
  - Provisionally Approved
  - Denied
- **Employee Electronic Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign the form electronically by typing your name.
- **Date of Request Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your supervisor preliminary approval status before submitting the form.
- The form will be reviewed by HR after submission.
