# Nursing Certification Study Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Certification Study Leave Of Absence Form is used to request a leave of absence for nursing staff to study for a certification. This form is typically used by staff members who need to take time off from work to prepare for a nursing certification exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the start and end dates for your leave of absence.
3. Choose the shift you will be taking leave from.
4. Specify the type of certification you are studying for.
5. Enter the number of study hours you plan to take.
6. Select the approval status for your supervisor, manager, and department head.
7. Enter the date your leave is approved.
8. Provide a brief reason for your leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge.
* **Start Date**: The date you would like to start your leave of absence.
* **End Date**: The date you would like to end your leave of absence.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Shift**: Select the shift you will be taking leave from (Morning, Afternoons, or Nights).
* **Certification Type**: Specify the type of certification you are studying for.
* **Study Hours**: Enter the number of hours you plan to take off for study.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Select the approval status for your supervisor (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Manager Approval**: Select the approval status for your manager (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Department Head Approval**: Select the approval status for your department head (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Leave Date Approved**: The date your leave of absence is approved.
* **Leave Reason**: Provide a brief reason for your leave of absence.

## Tips
* Make sure to get approval from all necessary parties before submitting this form.
* If your supervisor, manager, or department head has pending or denied approval, do not submit this form until you have received approval.
* This form is used for leaves of absence for study purposes only. If you are requesting leave for other reasons, please use the correct form for that purpose.
