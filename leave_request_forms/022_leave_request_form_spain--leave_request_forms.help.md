# leave_request_form_spain - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leave_request_form_spain is used to collect and process employee leave requests. This form is designed to gather relevant information from employees, such as their name, dates of leave, and reason for leave, to ensure accurate and efficient processing of their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2.  Select your start date for the leave in the "Start Date" field.
3.  Select your end date for the leave in the "End Date" field.
4.  Specify the number of days requested for the leave in the "Days Requested" field.
5.  Provide a clear reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
6.  Select your Team Lead who you report to for "Team Lead" field.
7.  For "Manager Approval", choose all applicable managers whose approval is required for your leave.
8.  Enter the date you are requesting this leave on in the "Leave Request Date" field.
9.  If applicable, select the "Leave Start Time" and "Leave End Time".
10. Submit the form for further processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your official name as it appears on your employee profile.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Select the start date of your leave request using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, required): Select the end date of your leave request using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Days Requested** (days_requested, number, required): Specify the total number of working days you are requesting for this leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (reason_for_leave, text, required): Please provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
* **Team Lead** (team_lead, select_one, optional): Select your Team Lead for approval of your leave request.
* **Manager Approval** (manager_approval, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable managers whose approval is required for this leave request.
* **Leave Request Date** (leave_request_date, date, required): Enter the date this leave request is made using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Leave Start Time** (leave_start_time, time, optional): If applicable, enter the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (leave_end_time, time, optional): If applicable, enter the end time of your leave.
* **Manager Approval** is for managers to review and approve or deny your leave. Please ensure all applicable managers are selected to avoid delays in processing.
