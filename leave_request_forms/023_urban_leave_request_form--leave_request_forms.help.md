# Urban Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Leave Request Form is a tool for employees to request leave from work. This form is used to submit a formal leave request, which will then be reviewed and approved or denied by your manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and details in the Employee Name field.
2. Enter the date you want to start your leave in the Leave Start Date field.
3. Enter the date you want to end your leave in the Leave End Date field.
4. Select the type of leave you're requesting from the Leave Type field. Please choose from the available options: Leave Request, Other, Vacation, Sick Leave, Parental Leave, Bereavement Leave, Family Care Leave, Annual Leave, or Jury Duty.
5. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the Reason field.
6. If applicable, enter the start and end times for your leave in the Start Time and End Time fields, respectively.
7. If applicable, enter the duration of your leave in the Duration field.
8. Add any additional details about your leave in the Leave Request Details field.
9. Enter your email address in the Email field.
10. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
11. Select the status of your manager's approval in the Manager's Approval field. Please choose from the available options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or in your HR system.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to start your leave. This date must be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to end your leave. This date must be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of leave you're requesting. Please choose from the available options to ensure proper processing.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): If you're requesting a leave during working hours, enter the start time in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): If you're requesting a leave during working hours, enter the end time in 12-hour format (e.g., 05:00 PM).
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): If you're requesting a leave of a specific duration, enter the number of hours or days you'll be taking off.
* **Leave Request Details** (`leave_request_details`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your leave request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager's Approval** (`manager_approvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your manager's approval. Please choose from the available options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
