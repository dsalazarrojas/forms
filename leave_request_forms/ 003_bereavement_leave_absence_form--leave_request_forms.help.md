# Bereavement Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bereavement Leave Absence Form is a document used to request bereavement leave, which is a type of leave taken after the loss of a family member or close relative. This form is essential for processing your request efficiently and fairly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
2.  Select the relevant date: Choose the date related to the bereavement.
3.  Choose your primary selection: Select the option that best describes your reason for requesting bereavement leave.
4.  Provide additional details: If required, offer any additional relevant information about your situation.
5.  Confirm your agreement: Select all that apply for the agreement options provided.
6.  Estimate value: If applicable, provide a decimal number for the estimated value of your absence.
7.  Set a preferred time: If required, enter a time for scheduling purposes.
8.  Final comments: Leave any closing remarks or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (1, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (2, email, required): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (4, text, optional): Include the country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (5, date, required): Select the relevant date related to the bereavement.
* **Primary selection** (6, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your reason for requesting bereavement leave.
* **Quantity or rating** (7, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (8, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your situation.
* **Agreement** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply for the provided options.
* **Estimated value** (10, number, optional): Use a decimal format when providing a number.
* **Preferred time** (11, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments** (12, text, optional): Leave any closing remarks or comments.
