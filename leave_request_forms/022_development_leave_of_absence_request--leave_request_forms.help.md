# Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit leave requests for development projects. This form is intended for employees to submit their leave requests to their managers for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Manager Name so that they can review and approve your request.
3. Enter the Project Title that you are currently working on.
4. Choose the Start Date and End Date for your leave.
5. Enter the Project ID to identify the project you are working on.
6. Enter the Duration of your leave.
7. Provide the Reason For Leave to help your manager understand the purpose of your request.
8. Select the Approval Status to indicate the current status of your request.
9. Add any Comments if you have additional information or explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required: false)
The Employee Name field is where you enter your name to identify yourself.

### **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, required: false)
The Manager Name field is where you enter your manager's name, so they can review and approve your request.

### **Project Title** (project_title, text, required: false)
The Project Title field is where you enter the name of the project you are currently working on.

### **Start Date** (start_date, date, required: false)
The Start Date field is where you enter the date you would like to start your leave.

### **End Date** (end_date, date, required: false)
The End Date field is where you enter the date you would like to end your leave.

### **Project ID** (project_id, number, required: false)
The Project ID field is where you enter a numerical identifier for the project you are working on.

### **Duration** (duration, number, required: false)
The Duration field is where you enter the number of days or weeks you would like to take as leave.

### **Reason For Leave** (reason_for_leave, text, required: false)
The Reason For Leave field is where you provide the reason for your request, to help your manager understand the purpose of your leave.

### **Approval Status** (approval_status, select_one, required: false)
The Approval Status field is a dropdown menu where you can select the current status of your request: Approved, Denied, or In Progress.

### **Comments** (comments, note, required: false)
The Comments field is where you can add any additional information or explanation to support your request.

### Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field to help your manager review your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager for clarification.
* Remember to review your request carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
