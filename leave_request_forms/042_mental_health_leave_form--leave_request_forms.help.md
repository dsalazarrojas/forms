Mental Health Leave Form - Help Guide
====================================

## Purpose

The Mental Health Leave Form is designed to help employees request a mental health leave from work. This form is used to provide a structured and standardized way of requesting time off for mental wellness, ensuring that necessary information is collected and communicated with your manager for review and approval.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reason for requesting mental health leave in the "Leave Reason" field. Please be as detailed as possible so your manager can understand your needs.
2. Provide the start and end dates of your requested leave.
3. Specify the duration of your leave, if it's a fixed number of days or weeks.
4. Indicate your current employee status: Active or Inactive.
5. If your manager will need to approve or decline your request, indicate if you've received manager approval or not.
6. If necessary, provide a doctor's note explaining your reason for taking mental health leave.
7. If necessary, provide any additional information or context that your manager may need to know.
8. Finally, sign and date the form with your employee ID and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Leave Reason (1)

* **Leave Reason**: A text field where you can provide a detailed reason for your mental health leave request. Please be as specific as possible so your manager understands your needs. (required)

### Start Date (2)

* **Start Date**: A date field where you should enter the date you would like to start your mental health leave. (required)

### End Date (3)

* **End Date**: A date field where you should enter the date you would like the mental health leave to end. (required)

### Duration (4)

* **Duration**: A numerical field where you can specify the number of days or weeks you need for your mental health leave. (required)

### Status (5)

* **Status**: A selection field where you can indicate your current status: Active or Inactive. (required)

### Manager Approval (6)

* **Manager Approval**: A selection field where you can indicate if you've received approval or not from your manager. (required)

### Doctor Note (7)

* **Doctor Note**: A text area field where you can provide a doctor's note explaining your reason for taking mental health leave. Please attach any relevant medical documentation. (optional)

### Manager Note (8)

* **Manager Note**: A text area field where your manager can provide additional information or context about your mental health leave. Please use this space to communicate any necessary details. (optional)

### Manager Signature (9)

* **Manager Signature**: A text field where your manager can sign and date this form to approve or decline your mental health leave. Please have your manager sign and date their name for official validation. (required)

### Employee Signature (10)

* **Employee Signature**: A text field where you should sign and date your name with your employee ID and email address. Please ensure your signature and date are accurate and authentic. (required)

### Tips

* Please be honest and transparent about your mental health needs.
* Provide all necessary information to support your leave request.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please ask your manager for guidance.
* Remember to sign and date this form before submitting it to your manager.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
