# Factory Visit Leave Of Absance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave for factory visits. It gathers information about the dates, reasons, and necessary approvals for the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as instructed below.
2. Select relevant options for each field based on your needs.
3. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Factory Visit Leave Date <date> (Page 1)
*   *Factory Visit Leave Date* is the date you plan to visit the factory.
*   Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 2. Technician Name <text> (Page 1)
*   *Technician Name* is the name of the technician making the request.
*   Please enter your name in the field.

### 3. Manager Escalation <select_one> (Page 1)
*   *Manager Escalation* is the level of escalation required for your visit.
*   Select 'Manager', 'Team Lead', or 'Supervisor' based on the level of escalation needed.

### 4. Reason For Leave <text> (Page 1)
*   *Reason For Leave* is the reason for your visit to the factory.
*   Please enter a brief description of your reason for the visit.

### 5. Start Date <date> (Page 1)
*   *Start Date* is the start date of your visit.
*   Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 6. End Date <date> (Page 1)
*   *End Date* is the end date of your visit.
*   Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 7. Factory Visit Details <text> (Page 1)
*   *Factory Visit Details* is a field to enter any additional information about your visit.
*   Please use this field to provide any relevant details about your visit.

### 8. Approved By <select_multiple> (Page 1)
*   *Approved By* is the level of approval required for your visit.
*   Select 'Manager', 'Team Lead', or 'Supervisor' based on the level of approval needed.

### 9. Comments <text> (Page 1)
*   *Comments* is a field for any additional comments or information about your visit.
*   Use this field to provide any relevant comments or questions about your visit.

### 10. Route To Manager <select_one> (Page 1)
*   *Route To Manager* is whether to route the visit to the manager.
*   Please select 'True' or 'False' based on whether you need to route the visit to the manager.

### 11. Route To Team Lead <select_one> (Page 1)
*   *Route To Team Lead* is whether to route the visit to the team lead.
*   Please select 'True' or 'False' based on whether you need to route the visit to the team lead.

### 12. Route To Supervisor <select_one> (Page 1)
*   *Route To Supervisor* is whether to route the visit to the supervisor.
*   Please select 'True' or 'False' based on whether you need to route the visit to the supervisor.
