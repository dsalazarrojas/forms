# Mining Industry Vacation Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Industry Vacation Leave Of Absence Form is a document intended for employees in the mining industry to request a period of absence due to vacation or other valid reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Start Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 06/02/2023).
2. Provide your **End Date** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 06/15/2023).
3. Choose a brief but clear **Reason** for your leave, such as "Family Leave" or "Personal Appointment".
4. Enter your **Employee Name** as it is recorded in your personnel records.
5. Fill out your **Contact Info** with your current contact phone number and address.
6. Provide your **Contact Email** address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): The date you plan to start your leave of absence. Please enter the month and day in mm/dd format, e.g., 06/02/2023.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): The date you plan to end your leave of absence. Please enter the month and day in mm/dd format, e.g., 06/15/2023.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required/optional): A brief explanation for your leave. Be as specific as possible, e.g., "Family Leave" or "Personal Appointment".
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Your name as it is recorded in your personnel records. If you are unsure, please contact your HR department for clarification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Your current contact phone number and address. Please provide accurate contact information to ensure we can reach you during your leave.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Your email address. This will be the primary contact method for any communication during your leave.
