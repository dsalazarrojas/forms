# Cultural Festival Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a leave request form for employees to request cultural festival leave.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Cultural Festival Name** field with the name of the cultural festival you will be attending.
2. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your leave in the respective fields.
3. Provide a brief **Reason for request** explaining why you need to take cultural festival leave.
4. Select the **Leave Type** as **Cultural Festival** to indicate that this is a cultural festival leave request.
5. If required, enter your **Team Lead**, **Manager**, and **Requestor** information.
6. If required, select the **Status** as either **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending**.
7. If required, select the **Manager Approval**, **Team Lead Approval**, and **Requestor Approval** as either **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Not Required**.
8. Finally, enter any **Comments** and **Approval Comments** that may be relevant to your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cultural Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of the cultural festival you will be attending.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your leave in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your leave in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for request** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why you need to take cultural festival leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select **Cultural Festival** to indicate that this is a cultural festival leave request.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): If required, enter the name and title of your team lead.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional): If required, enter the name and title of your manager.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, text, optional): If required, enter your name and title.
* **Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending** to indicate the status of your leave request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Not Required** to indicate the manager's approval status.
* **Team Lead Approval** (`team_lead_approval`, select_one, optional): Select **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Not Required** to indicate the team lead's approval status.
* **Requestor Approval** (`requestor_approval`, select_one, optional): Select **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Not Required** to indicate your approval status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments that may be relevant to your leave request.
* **Approval Comments** (`approval_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments that may be relevant to the approval of your leave request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the designated form administrator for assistance.
* This form is intended to be used for cultural festival leave requests only. If you need to request leave for other reasons, please use the appropriate form.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
