# Business Planning Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Planning Leave Form is used to request and plan for business planning leave, which can be a valuable opportunity for employees to focus on business strategy and planning without the distractions of daily work responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with accurate and detailed information, including:
  - Employee Name and ID
  - Department
  - Manager Name
  - Leave Start and End Dates
  - Number of Days Requested
  - Type of Leave (e.g., Paid Time Off, Unpaid Leave, etc.)
  - Business Planning Objectives and Focus Areas
  - Expected Outcomes
  - Coverage Arrangements During Leave
  - Backup Contact Person and Phone or Email
- Please select the correct options for the Type of Leave and Manager Approval status.
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure that all required information is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID card.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department or team name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Number of Days Requested** (`number_of_days`, number, required): Enter the total number of working days you plan to take off.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of leave you are requesting (e.g., Paid Time Off, Unpaid Leave, etc.).
* **Business Planning Objectives** (`planning_objectives`, text, required): Describe the specific business planning objectives you aim to achieve during this leave.
* **Planning Focus Areas** (`planning_focus_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant areas of focus for your business planning leave (e.g., Strategic Planning, Financial Analysis, etc.).
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the outcomes you expect to deliver after this leave.
* **Coverage Arrangements During Leave** (`coverage_arrangements`, text, required): Outline the arrangements for covering your work during the leave period.
* **Backup Contact Person** (`backup_contact`, text, required): Identify the person who should handle urgent matters during your absence.
* **Backup Contact Phone or Email** (`backup_contact_details`, text, optional): Provide contact information for the backup contact person.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your manager's approval (e.g., Approved, Pending Approval, etc.).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of manager approval.

Please fill out this form with care and attention to detail to ensure a smooth leave experience.
