# Personal Crisis Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a Personal Crisis Leave of Absence due to an urgent personal matter. This form is intended for employees to request time off from work in emergency situations such as family or medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required information in each field
- Select the correct category and reason for the crisis
- Enter your name and department information
- Select the start and end dates of your absence
- Enter the expected duration of your absence
- Provide any comments or additional information for your manager's consideration

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Urgent Leave Request** (page 1, id: 1, required: false): This is the title of the form that briefly explains the purpose of the form.
- **Category** (page 2, id: 2, required: false): Select the category of the crisis, such as Family and Medical or Other.
- **Reason for crisis** (page 2, id: 3, required: true): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your crisis.
- **Employee's name** (page 2, id: 4, required: true): Enter your full name as an employee.
- **Department** (page 2, id: 5, required: true): Enter your department name.
- **Manager's name** (page 2, id: 6, required: true): Enter your manager's name.
- **Manager's email** (page 3, id: 7, required: true): Enter your manager's email address.
- **Start date** (page 3, id: 8, required: true): Enter the date when you would like your leave to start.
- **End date** (page 3, id: 9, required: true): Enter the date when you would like your leave to end.
- **Duration** (page 3, id: 10, required: true): Enter the number of days or hours you would like your leave to last.
- **Manager approval** (page 4, id: 11, required: false): Select the manager's approval status, which will be updated automatically by the system.
- **Comments** (page 4, id: 12, required: true): Enter any additional comments or information for your manager's consideration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Select the correct category and reason for your crisis.
- Double-check your dates and duration before submitting the form.
- Enter your manager's email address correctly to ensure they receive your request.
- Be clear and concise in your comments section.
