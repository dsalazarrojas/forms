# Creative Recharge Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Creative Recharge Leave Of Absence Request" form is for employees to submit their leave requests, specifying the reasons for the leave, dates, and required approval details.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a leave request, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Request Form" section (page 1) with a brief description of the leave request.
2.  In the "Leave Details" section (page 2), provide a more detailed explanation of the leave, including the reasons and any relevant supporting information.
3.  Enter the start date of the leave in the "Start Date" field (page 3).
4.  Enter the end date of the leave in the "End Date" field (page 4).
5.  Enter the expected return date in the "Return Date" field (page 5).
6.  In the "Reason for Leave" field (page 6), provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the leave.
7.  In the "Team Impact" field (page 7), add any relevant notes about how the leave may affect the team.
8.  Select one of the options in the "Manager Approval" field (page 8) to indicate the type of approval required.
9.  Finally, click the "Submit" button (page 9) to complete and submit the leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (page 1, text, required: false):
    This field is for a brief description of the leave request.
* **Leave Details** (page 2, text, required: false):
    This field allows you to provide more details about the leave, including the reasons and any supporting information.
* **Start Date** (page 3, date, required: false):
    Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (page 4, date, required: false):
    Enter the date when you plan to end your leave.
* **Return Date** (page 5, date, required: false):
    Enter the date when you plan to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave** (page 6, text, required: false):
    Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for your leave.
* **Team Impact** (page 7, note, required: false):
    Add any relevant notes about how the leave may affect the team.
* **Manager Approval** (page 8, select_one, required: true):
    Select one of the options to indicate the type of approval required.
* **Submit** (page 9, text, required: false):
    Click this button to complete and submit your leave request.
