# Civil Engineering Site Visit Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civil Engineering Site Visit Leave Of Absence Form is used to record and track essential information related to site visits undertaken by employees in the civil engineering department. This form is a mandatory requirement to ensure that all necessary approvals and safety briefings are completed before an employee leaves for a site visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee name" field to confirm your identity.
2. Enter your internal employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field for tracking purposes.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your site visit in the "Site visit start date" and "Site visit end date" fields, respectively.
4. Provide the location and name of the site visit in the "Destination of site visit" field.
5. Briefly describe the reason for your site visit in the "Reason for leave" field.
6. Select the supervisor's approval status from the options provided in the "Supervisor approval status" field.
7. If applicable, enter your contact information during the leave in the "Contact during leave" field.
8. Confirm if a site-specific safety briefing has been completed in the "Safety briefing completed" field.
9. Submit the form with the current date in the "Submission date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your internal employee ID number for tracking and identification purposes.
* **Site visit start date** (`site_visit_start`, date, required): Enter the date of departure for your site visit.
* **Site visit end date** (`site_visit_end`, date, required): Enter the date of return from your site visit.
* **Destination of site visit** (`destination`, text, required): Provide the location and name of the site visit.
* **Reason for leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of your site visit.
* **Supervisor approval status** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required): Select the supervisor's approval status from the options provided. Options include "Approved", "Pending", and "Denied".
* **Contact during leave** (`contact_during_leave`, text, optional): If you will be reachable during your leave, provide your contact information (phone or email).
* **Safety briefing completed** (`safety_briefing_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a site-specific safety briefing has been completed.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Submit the form with the current date.

## Tips
- Make sure to select "Supervisor approval status" based on your actual approval situation.
- Enter your contact information during the leave if you will be reachable.
- Complete the "Safety briefing completed" field accurately to ensure compliance with site safety regulations.
