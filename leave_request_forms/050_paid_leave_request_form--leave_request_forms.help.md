# Paid Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request paid leave from work. It will help you submit your leave request with all the necessary details to your manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide whether you are requesting paid leave (Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Paid Leave Request" dropdown).
2. Enter the start date of your leave in the "Start Date" field.
3. Enter the end date of your leave in the "End Date" field.
4. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Select from the "Leave Type" dropdown).
5. Specify the duration of your leave in the "Leave Duration" field.
6. If you are returning to work after your leave, enter the "Return Date" field.
7. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" text field.
8. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
9. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown.
10. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
11. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
12. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Submitting Manager" dropdown, indicating whether you are submitting the leave request on behalf of your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Paid Leave Request** (`Paid Leave Request`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to specify whether you are requesting paid leave or not.
  * If you select "Yes", it means you are requesting paid leave.
  * If you select "No", it means you are not requesting paid leave.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your leave starts.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your leave ends.
- **Leave Type** (`Leave Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Select from "Yes" or "No").
  * "Yes" or "No" indicates that you are requesting leave due to the given option.
  * The meaning of "Yes" or "No" depends on the context of the leave request (e.g., vacation, sick leave, etc.)
- **Leave Duration** (`Leave Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days for your leave.
- **Return Date** (`Return Date`, `date`, required): If you are returning to work after your leave, enter the date of your return.
- **Reason for Leave** (`Reason for Leave`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for your leave.
- **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
- **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department.
- **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, `email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
- **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
- **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Submitting Manager** (`Submitting Manager`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you are submitting the leave request on behalf of your manager.
  * If you select "Yes", it means you are submitting the request on behalf of your manager.
  * If you select "No", it means you are submitting the request as the leave requester.
