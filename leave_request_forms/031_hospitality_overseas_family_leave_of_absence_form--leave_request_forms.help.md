# Hospitality Overseas Family Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the overseas family leave form. The form is designed to be simple and user-friendly, and each section is described below for your ease of understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Overseas, Domestic, or Other)
2. Enter your travel dates
3. Specify the duration of your leave
4. Provide a reason for your leave
5. Enter your contact person's name and phone number
6. Enter your email address
7. Enter your employee ID
8. Specify the status of your leave request (Applied, Approved, Denied)
9. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Family, Business, or Other)
10. If necessary, provide additional notes regarding your leave
11. Enter your name

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Leave Type**: This field allows you to specify the type of leave you are requesting. Select "Overseas" if you plan to take leave outside your country, "Domestic" if you plan to take leave within your country, or "Other" if your leave type is not listed.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter your travel dates in the format MM/DD/YY. This field is required to ensure that your leave request is properly recorded.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the number of days you plan to take for your leave. This field is required to help us estimate the duration of your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: Provide a brief reason for your leave, such as visiting family or attending to a family event.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and phone number of the person we can contact in case we need to reach you while you are on leave.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number, which helps us to identify you and manage your leave request.
* **Leave Status**: Specify the status of your leave request, such as Applied, Approved, or Denied. If your leave request is Denied, do not hesitate to contact us to discuss further.
* **Leave Type**: Select all the types of leave you are requesting. If you need to take both Family and Business leave, select both.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or notes about your leave request, if necessary.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name, which will help us to address your leave request. 

**Tips:**
- Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any delays in processing your leave request.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
- Please review your form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
