# Dental Professional Education Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for dental professionals to request a leave of absence for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have checked with your education provider to ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
3. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Leave Request** (`education_leave_request`): This is the main section of the form where you can describe your education leave request. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of your reason for requesting leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): Select the reason for your leave from the options provided. This helps the approving authority understand your request.
* **Duration of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`): Enter the number of days you are requesting for leave.
* **Start Date** (`from_date`): Enter the date you plan to start your leave from.
* **End Date** (`to_date`): Enter the date you plan to end your leave by. (Optional)
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Education Leave of Absence, Personal Leave, or Sick Leave).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Program** (`dental_program`): Select if you are affiliated with a dental program.
* **Education Provider** (`education_provider`): Enter the name of your education provider (e.g., university, institution).
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of your education provider.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of your contact person (optional).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact email of your contact person (optional).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of your contact person (optional).
* **Department** (`department`): Select if your department has approved your leave request.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select the status of your leave request (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Select if your leave request was approved by someone.
* **Approved At** (`approved_at`): Enter the date your leave request was approved.
* **Comments Approved** (`comments_approved`): Add any comments or notes about your approved leave request.
* **Comments Denied** (`comments_denied`): Add any comments or notes about your denied leave request.
* **Comments Pending** (`comments_pending`): Add any comments or notes about your pending leave request.
* **Leave Request ID** (`leave_request_id`): Enter the ID of your leave request (optional).
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Enter the date your leave request was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Enter the date your leave request was updated.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's essential to ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information for your leave request.
