# Creative Break Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a creative break leave of absence from work. It is used to submit a request for a leave of absence to your manager, including the start and end dates of the leave, the number of leave days, and a brief reason for the leave. Your manager will use this information to review and approve or decline your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your employee data, start date, end date, number of leave days, reason for leave, and any additional comments you may have.
2. Select the approval status from the manager approval field.
3. Provide any additional comments to your manager regarding your leave request.
4. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Data**: This field is for your employee information, including your name and any other relevant data.
* **Start Date**: This is the date you plan to begin your leave from work.
* **End Date**: This is the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Leave Days**: This field is for the number of days you plan to take for your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: This field is for the brief reason you are taking the leave for.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the approval status for your leave request from the available options (Approve, Decline, Hold).
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments you may have regarding your leave request.
* **Approval Reason**: This field is for your manager to provide a reason for their approval or decline of your leave request, if applicable.
