# personal_development_leave_of_absence_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal development leave of absence request form is used by employees to submit a formal request for a personal development leave of absence, which is a type of leave that allows employees to take time off for personal or professional development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Employee: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
	* Start Date: Enter the date you would like to begin your leave.
	* End Date: Enter the date you would like to end your leave.
	* Reason: Describe your reason for requesting this leave.
	* Manager Approvals: Select one of the following options:
		+ Approve with Conditions
		+ Approve without Conditions
		+ Decline
	* Employee Name: This field is automatically populated with your name.
	* Project ID: Enter the ID of the project you are working on.
	* Department: Enter your department name.
	* Team Lead: Enter the name of your team lead.
	* Manager Name: Enter the name of your manager.
	* Team: Enter the name of your team.
	* Approval Status: This field will be populated automatically after your request has been reviewed.
2. Fill out the optional fields:
	* Output File: If you have any supporting documents or files related to your request, you can upload them here.
	* Assigned Tool: Enter the tool or resource assigned to you for this task.
	* Assigned Form ID: Enter the ID of the form assigned to you for this task.
	* Submitted By: This field is automatically populated with the name of the person submitting the request.
	* Submitted Date: This field is automatically populated with the date of submission.
	* Manager Comments: If your manager has any comments or feedback, they can enter them here.
	* Approval Comment: If your request is approved, your manager can enter any comments or feedback here.
