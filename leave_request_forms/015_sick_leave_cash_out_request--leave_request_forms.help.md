# Sick Leave Cash Out Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a payment for unused sick leave hours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a sick leave cash out, such as illness or injury.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including your name, the date of the incident, and a brief description of your reason for requesting cash out.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send the request to your manager for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sick Leave Cash Out Request** (`sick_leave_cash_out_request`, text, optional): This is the main field where you will describe your reason for requesting a sick leave cash out. Please provide a brief and clear explanation of your situation.
* **Manager Review** (`manager_review`, text, optional): This field is where your manager will write their review and comments on your request. You will not need to fill this out, as it is intended for your manager's response.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): This field is also for your manager's comments and feedback on your request. Like the previous field, you will not need to fill this out, as it is intended for your manager's use.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear when explaining your reason for requesting a sick leave cash out.
* Avoid duplicate or frivolous requests, as they will not be approved.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your HR representative for guidance.
