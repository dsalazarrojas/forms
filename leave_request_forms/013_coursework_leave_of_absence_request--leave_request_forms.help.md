# Coursework Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Coursework Leave Of Absence Request" form is designed to allow you to request a leave of absence for your coursework. This form helps the institution's administrators to process your request and make necessary arrangements for your leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide the required information.
2. Fill in the relevant fields based on your specific needs.
3. Ensure you provide as much detail as possible, especially in fields like Course Details and Reason for Leave.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coursework Leave Request** (`coursework_leave_request_form`): This is the main title of the form.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a leave of absence, and "No" otherwise.
* **Leave Dates** (`leave_dates`): Provide the dates for which you are requesting the leave. This could be a single date or a range of dates.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your leave if you are selecting a specific date range.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your leave if you are selecting a specific date range.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the administrators to process your request.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`): Describe the coursework you are currently undertaking or have planned.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`): Select "Yes" if the coursework you are requesting leave for has a course code assigned.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course you are requesting leave for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
