Pride Event Participation Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request leave of absence for employees to participate in pride events or other events. It allows employees to provide necessary information to their approvers, including event type, dates, contact information, and reason for leave.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you will be participating in from the "Event Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the start date and end date of your event in the respective fields.
3. Enter the start and end times of your event, if applicable.
4. Enter the duration of your event.
5. Provide a brief reason for your leave.
6. Add any relevant contact information for your approver.
7. Enter the email address of your approver.
8. Enter the phone number of your approver.
9. Add any additional notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you will be participating in. This will help your approver understand the nature of your event.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Enter the date you will be starting your event.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Enter the date you will be ending your event.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required): Enter the time you will be starting your event, if applicable.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required): Enter the time you will be ending your event, if applicable.
* **Duration** (`Duration`, number, required): Enter the length of time you will be away for your event.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required): Enter any relevant contact information for your approver.
* **Approver** (`Approver`, text, required): Enter the name of your approver.
* **Approver Email** (`Approver Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`Approver Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your approver.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or details about your request.

Note: All fields with a red asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to submit your request.
