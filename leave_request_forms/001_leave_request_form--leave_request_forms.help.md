# Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leave Request Form is a tool for employees to submit leave requests. Please use this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee record.
2. **Start Date**: Select the date you wish to start your leave from.
3. **End Date**: Select the date you wish to end your leave to.
4. **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, or Personal Leave).
5. **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
6. **Manager Approvals**: Select all managers that need to approve your leave request (Approve, Reject, or Pending).
7. **Manager Comment**: If your manager needs to provide additional comments or feedback, please enter their comments here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: The employee's name as it appears on their employee record.
* **Start Date**: The date when you wish to start your leave.
* **End Date**: The date when you wish to end your leave.
* **Leave Type**: The type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, or Personal Leave).
* **Reason for Leave**: A brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Manager Approvals**: Select all managers that need to approve your leave request. Please select "Approve" if your manager is okay with your leave request, "Reject" if they decline, or "Pending" if they need more time to review.
* **Manager Comment**: If your manager provides additional comments or feedback, please enter their comments here.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have not already submitted a leave request for the same dates.
* If you have any outstanding leave requests, please refer to the HR department for clarification.
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your leave request.
