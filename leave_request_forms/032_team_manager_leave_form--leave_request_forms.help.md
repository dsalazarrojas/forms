# Team Manager Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for team members to request leave from their team. The team lead will review and approve or deny leave requests using this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Select your role within the team**:
	* Choose whether you are a Team Lead or a Team Member.
2. **Enter your leave details**:
	* Provide the start and end dates of your leave.
	* Enter a brief reason for your leave.
3. **Add comments (optional)**:
	* If you need to provide additional information about your leave request, enter it in the comments field.
4. **Submit your request**:
	* Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the submit button to submit your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member**: 
	+ This field is used to select your role within the team. Please choose between Team Lead and Team Member.
* **Start Date**: 
	+ Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date**: 
	+ Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason**: 
	+ Enter a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Comments**:
	+ If you need to provide additional information, enter it here.
* **Approval Status**: 
	+ This field is used to track the approval status of your leave request. It will be updated by the team lead once your request is reviewed.
* **Submit Date**: 
	+ This field is automatically updated with the date you submitted your leave request.
* **Team Lead Approval**:
	+ This field is used to track the approval status of your leave request by the team lead. It will be updated once the team lead reviews your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with submitting your request, contact your team lead or IT support.
