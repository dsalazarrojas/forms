# Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for requesting a leave of absence as a pet groomer. It is designed to gather necessary information from the pet owner to process the request in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date and end date of your leave request.
2. Select the type of leave (Holiday, Personal, or Other).
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for your leave request.
4. Enter the pet owner's email, name, and phone number (if applicable).
5. Enter your employee name and name of the manager(s) you report to.
6. Select your department and the names of your team leaders and shift leaders.
7. Enter your start and end times (if applicable).
8. Add any additional comments or information to help us process your request.
9. Confirm that you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your leave.
* **Type of Leave**: Select the reason for your leave from the following options:
	+ Holiday: For leave due to a holiday or vacation.
	+ Personal: For personal reasons such as illness or family emergency.
	+ Other: For any other reason not listed above.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief description of the reason for your leave request.
* **Pet Owner Email**: Enter the pet owner's email address.
* **Pet Owner Name**: Enter the pet owner's name.
* **Pet Owner Phone**: Enter the pet owner's phone number (if applicable).
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee Name 2**: Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of your second manager or supervisor.
* **Department**: Select your department from the following options:
	+ Groomers
	+ Reception
	+ Management
* **Team Leader**: Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Team Leader 2**: Enter the name of your second team leader.
* **Team Leader 3**: Enter the name of your third team leader.
* **Shift Leader**: Enter the name of your shift leader.
* **Shift Leader 2**: Enter the name of your second shift leader.
* **Shift Leader 3**: Enter the name of your third shift leader.
* **Shift Leader 4**: Enter the name of your fourth shift leader.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you would like to start your leave (if applicable).
* **End Time**: Enter the time you would like to end your leave (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information to help us process your request.
* **Submitted By**: Confirm that you submitted the form.
