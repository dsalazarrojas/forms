# International Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an employee's International Training Leave Of Absence request, which includes details such as training location, dates, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully before submission.
3. Attach any supporting documents as required (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
4. Submit the form to your supervisor for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_request** (1): Enter a brief summary of your request.
* **training_details** (2): Provide detailed information about the training you plan to attend, including dates, duration, and location.
* **supervisor_approvals** (3): Note any approvals from supervisors or managers related to your training leave.
* **leave_dates** (4): Specify the exact dates you plan to be on leave.
* **travel_details** (5): Include any travel-related information, such as flight itineraries or visa requirements.
* **employee_info** (6): Include your employee ID and any other relevant employee information.
* **training_location** (7): Select the location(s) where you will be attending the training.
* **company_info** (8): Provide company-specific information related to your training, such as company policies or procedures.
* **submit_form** (9): Confirm that you have read and understood the form and agree to submit it for review.
* **employee_signature** (10): Sign the form to indicate your agreement to its contents.
* **company_signature** (11): Sign the form as the company representative, confirming approval or denial.
* **submitter** (12): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **date_submitted** (13): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **form_id** (14): Enter a unique ID number for the form.
* **employee_id** (15): Enter your employee ID number.
* **company_id** (16): Enter the company ID number.
* **status** (17): Select the current status of your leave request (e.g., Awaiting Review, Approved, Denied, Reopened, or Expired).
* **approval_date** (18): Enter the date of approval or denial.
* **training_location_id** (19): Select the location(s) where you will be attending the training.
* **training_type** (20): Choose the type of training you are attending (e.g., conference, workshop, or seminar).
* **training_duration** (21): Select the duration of your training.
* **training_location** (22): Select the location(s) where you will be attending the training.
* **company_location** (23): Select the location(s) where the company is based.
* **travel_dates** (24): Select the date(s) you plan to travel.
* **leave_type** (25): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick, or training).
* **reason** (26): Briefly explain the reason for your leave request.
* **employee_notes** (27): Provide any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **company_notes** (28): Enter any notes from the company regarding your leave request.
