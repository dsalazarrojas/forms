### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to submit this form.
2. Review each field carefully and fill it in accordingly.
3. Submit the form once you've completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Crunch Time Leave Of Absence Request** (`crunch_time_leave_of_absence_request`, text, optional)
This field is the title of your leave of absence request. Please provide a clear and concise description of your request.

### 2. **Manager** (`manager_id`, text, optional)
This field is for selecting your manager's ID. Please ensure you select the correct manager for your team or department.

### 3. **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
This field is for providing your name. Please ensure your name is spelled correctly and matches your official records.

### 4. **Team Lead** (`team_lead_id`, select_one, optional)
This field is for selecting your team lead's ID. Please choose the correct team lead for your team.

### 5. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional)
This field is for selecting the start date of your leave. Please ensure the date is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 6. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional)
This field is for selecting the end date of your leave. Please ensure the date is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 7. **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional)
This field is for providing a clear and concise reason for your leave. Please be specific about your reason for requesting this leave.

## Tips

* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR department for assistance.
* Once you've submitted the form, please follow up with your manager for further discussion and approval.
