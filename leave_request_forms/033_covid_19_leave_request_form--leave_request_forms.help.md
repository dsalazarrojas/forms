# COVID-19 Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave due to COVID-19-related reasons. Ensure that you submit a complete and accurate request to help us process it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your employee details: Employee Name, Employee ID, and Department
2.  Select your manager's name from the list
3.  Provide the reason for your leave request (Reason for Leave Request) and choose the corresponding option from the dropdown menu
4.  Enter the dates for your leave: Leave Start Date and Leave End Date
5.  Specify the duration of your leave in days
6.  Select the type of leave you're requesting: Sick Leave, Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave, or Administrative Leave
7.  Provide a detailed reason for your leave
8.  Answer if you have any symptoms currently
9.  Report your test results if applicable
10.  If applicable, enter the test date
11.  Confirm that you have medical clearance obtained
12.  Specify the expected date to return to work
13.  Certify that the information is accurate

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, required/optional): Select your department from the list.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required/optional): Select your manager's name from the list.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you're submitting this request.
* **Reason for Leave Request** (`leave_reason`, required/optional): Choose the reason for your leave request from the options:
	+ Tested Positive
	+ Exposed to COVID-19
	+ Quarantine Order
	+ Vaccination Side Effects
	+ Family Member Care
	+ Health Precaution
	+ Other
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you wish to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you wish to end your leave.
* **Number of Days** (`duration_days`, required/optional): Specify the number of days you'll be taking for your leave.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_type`, required/optional): Select the type of leave you're requesting:
	+ Sick Leave
	+ Paid Leave
	+ Unpaid Leave
	+ Administrative Leave
* **Detailed Reason** (`reason_details`, required/optional): Provide a detailed explanation for your leave request.
* **Any symptoms currently** (`symptoms_present`, required/optional): Answer if you have any symptoms currently.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_list`, required/optional): Report any symptoms you're experiencing.
* **Test Results if applicable** (`test_results`, required/optional): Report your test results:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Pending
	+ Not Tested
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you received your test results.
* **Medical clearance obtained** (`medical_clearance`, required/optional): Confirm if you have obtained medical clearance.
* **Expected Return to Work Date** (`expected_return`, required/optional): Specify the date you expect to return to work.
* **I certify the information is accurate** (`agreement`, required/optional): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
