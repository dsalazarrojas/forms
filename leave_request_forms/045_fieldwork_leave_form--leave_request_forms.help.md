# Fieldwork Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fieldwork Leave Form is a request form for employees to submit their fieldwork leave requests. This form collects necessary information from the employee, which is then reviewed and approved or rejected by the fieldwork supervisor and team lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user ID to identify yourself as the employee requesting leave.
2. Select the date of your leave.
3. Provide the location of your fieldwork.
4. Enter the start and end times of your leave.
5. Specify the duration of your leave.
6. Enter a reason for your leave.
7. Select your fieldwork supervisor and team lead.
8. Enter the department you belong to.
9. (Optional) Provide additional fieldwork details.
10. Select the status of your leave request (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
11. Enter any comments regarding your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id** (`1`, `number`, **required**): Enter your unique user ID to identify yourself as the employee requesting leave.
* **Date Of Leave** (`2`, `date`, **required**): Select the date you would like to take leave for fieldwork.
* **Fieldwork Location** (`3`, `text`, **required**): Provide the location where you will be doing fieldwork, e.g., "Remote site A" or "Client site X".
* **Start Time** (`4`, `time`, **required**): Enter the start time of your fieldwork.
* **End Time** (`5`, `time`, **required**): Enter the end time of your fieldwork.
* **Duration** (`7`, `text`, **required**): Specify the duration of your fieldwork in hours or days, e.g., "8 hours" or "3 days".
* **Leave Reason** (`8`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave request.
* **Fieldwork Supervisor** (`9`, `text`, **required**): Select the name of your fieldwork supervisor.
* **Team Lead** (`10`, `text`, **required**): Select the name of your team lead.
* **Department** (`11`, `text`, **required**): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Fieldwork Details** (`12`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional details about your fieldwork, e.g., project details or equipment needed.
* **Leave Status** (`13`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the status of your leave request (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Comments** (`14`, `text`, **required**): Enter any comments regarding your leave request, e.g., clarification or additional information.
