# Medical Leave of Absence Request Nurse - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a medical leave of absence for a nurse. It captures necessary information from the requestor, including their personal details, the reason for the leave, and supporting documents. The form is used by the approver to review and manage the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and other personal details (fields 1-2).
2. Enter the Start Date and End Date of your leave (fields 3-4).
3. Provide a brief reason for your leave (field 5).
4. Upload any relevant medical certificate or doctor's certificate (field 6).
5. Enter the Return to Work Date, if applicable (field 7).
6. Enter any relevant contact information, including Phone, Email, and Department (fields 8-11).
7. Confirm your title and department (fields 16-17).
8. Provide any additional comments from your department manager (field 20).
9. Enter the approver's details, including their Title, Department, and Status (fields 22-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.

### Leave Start Date (3)
* **Leave Start Date (date)**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.

### Leave End Date (4)
* **Leave End Date (date)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.

### Reason for Leave (5)
* **Reason for Leave (text)**: Enter a brief reason for your medical leave.

### Medical Certificate (6)
* **Medical Certificate (text)**: Upload any relevant medical certificate or doctor's certificate to support your request.

### Return to Work Date (7)
* **Return to Work Date (date)**: If you know when you plan to return to work, enter it here.

### Doctor Certificate (8)
* **Doctor Certificate (text)**: Upload any relevant doctor's certificate to support your request.

### Contact Phone (9)
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number.

### Contact Email (10)
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter your contact email address.

### Department (11)
* **Department (text)**: Enter your department.

### Manager Name (12)
* **Manager Name (text)**: Enter your department manager's name.

### Manager Phone (13)
* **Manager Phone (text)**: Enter your department manager's phone number.

### Manager Email (14)
* **Manager Email (email)**: Enter your department manager's email address.

### Requestor (15)
* **Requestor (text)**: Confirm that you are the person making this request.

### Title (16)
* **Title (text)**: Confirm your title.

### Submitter Department (17)
* **Submitter Department (text)**: Confirm your department.

### Submitter Contact Phone (18)
* **Submitter Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number.

### Submitter Contact Email (19)
* **Submitter Contact Email (email)**: Enter your contact email address.

### Manager Comment (20)
* **Manager Comment (text)**: Enter any additional comments from your department manager.

### Requestor Comment (21)
* **Requestor Comment (text)**: Enter any additional comments you have.

### Approver (22)
* **Approver (text)**: Enter the approver's name.

### Approver Title (23)
* **Approver Title (text)**: Enter the approver's title.

### Approver Department (24)
* **Approver Department (text)**: Enter the approver's department.

### Status (25)
* **Status (select_multiple)**: Select the status of your leave request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
