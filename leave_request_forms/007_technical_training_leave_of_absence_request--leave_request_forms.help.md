# Technical Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technical Training Leave of Absence Request form is used to request a leave of absence for technical training purposes. This form is used to communicate with management and HR to request time off for training or other technical activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee request in the **Employee Request** field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave of absence in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
3. Provide the name of the training course or activity you will be attending in the **Training Course** field.
4. Choose the option that best describes the status of your manager's approval in the **Manager Approval** field.
5. Select the teams or individuals that need to approve your request in the **People Team Approval** field.
6. Enter the date that your request was approved by management in the **Approval Date** field.
7. Enter the date you plan to return to work in the **Return Date** field.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about your request in the **Comments** field.
9. Select the status of your employee in the **Employee Status** field.
10. Choose the status of your manager's approval in the **Manager Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Request** (`employee_request`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your request for a leave of absence.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your leave of absence.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the training course or activity you will be attending.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your manager's approval.
* **People Team Approval** (`people_team_approval`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the teams or individuals that need to approve your request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date that your request was approved.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/true): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of your employee.
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the status of your manager's approval.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for clarification.
* Duplicate requests will not be processed.
