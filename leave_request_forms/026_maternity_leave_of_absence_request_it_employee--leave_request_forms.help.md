# Maternity Leave Of Absence Request (IT Employee) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a Maternity Leave of Absence for IT employees. It is intended for use by IT employees who are expecting a child and need to take time off from work due to maternity leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the general information section with your details.
2. Enter the expected start date of your leave.
3. Enter the expected end date of your leave.
4. Specify the duration of your leave.
5. Check the box to confirm that your manager has approved your request.
6. If your manager has approved your request, enter their email address.
7. If your manager has comments on your request, enter them in the comments field.
8. If you are requesting team coverage, specify who will cover your tasks during your leave.
9. Enter any additional coverage details.
10. Specify the type of request you are making (maternity leave).
11. Confirm the status of your request (active/inactive).
12. Enter any additional notes.
13. (Optional) Attach any relevant documents or information to your request.
14. Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your request.
15. Enter a message to be sent to the approver.
16. Enter the date and time the approval was given.
17. Enter the time of approval.
18. Enter the name of the person who approved the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Fill out this section with your details, including your name, job title, and any other relevant information.
* **Expected Start Date**: Enter the date you expect your leave to start.
* **Expected End Date**: Enter the date you expect your leave to end.
* **Leave Duration**: Specify the number of days or weeks you will be taking off.
* **Manager Approval**: Check this box to confirm that your manager has approved your request.
* **Manager Comments**: If your manager has comments on your request, enter them here.
* **IT Manager**: Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Team Coverage**: If you are requesting team coverage, specify who will cover your tasks during your leave.
* **Coverage Details**: Enter any additional details on team coverage.
* **Request Type**: Specify that this is a maternity leave request.
* **Approval Status**: Confirm the status of your request (active/inactive).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information.
* **Attachments**: (Optional) Attach any relevant documents or information to your request.
* **Approval Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Approval Message**: Enter a message to be sent to the approver.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date and time the approval was given.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time of approval.
* **Approval By**: Enter the name of the person who approved the request.
