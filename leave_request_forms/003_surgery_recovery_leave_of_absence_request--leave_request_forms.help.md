## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for an employee's recovery from surgery. The form is a crucial part of the process, as it ensures that the necessary approvals are in place before the employee takes the leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. Select the start date of your recovery leave in the "Start Date" field.
4. Select the end date of your recovery leave in the "End Date" field.
5. Choose the type of surgery in the "Surgery Type" field.
6. Enter the number of days for your recovery period in the "Recovery Period" field.
7. Select the date you plan to return to work in the "Return To Work Date" field.
8. Enter a brief reason for your absence in the "Reason For Absence" field.
9. Approve or disapprove of the leave request in the "Manager Approval" field.
10. Approve or disapprove of the leave request in the "Supervisor Approval" field.
11. Approve or disapprove of the leave request in the "HR Approval" field.
12. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
13. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.

* **HR Approval** (`hr_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Approve or disapprove of the leave request.
