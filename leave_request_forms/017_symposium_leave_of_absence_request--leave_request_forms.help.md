# Symposium Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a leave request for Symposium events such as conferences, presentations, meetings, and training. The form is used to request leave from work for these events and is required for official approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have read and understood the guidelines on the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Ensure you select the correct options for your leave type and status.
4.  Enter your request details in the "Request Details" field.
5.  Enter your comments if necessary.
6.  Fill in your contact information.
7.  Select your department, project, and team members involved.
8.  Select the user assigned to the request.
9.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1): Enter a brief title for your leave request.
* **Start Date** (2): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (3): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Duration** (4): Enter the number of days you plan to be away. If not applicable, enter 0.
* **Location** (5): Enter the location of your event. If not attending an event, enter N/A.
* **Type** (6): Select the type of event:
	+ Conferences: For attending conferences.
	+ Presentations: For presentations you are giving.
	+ Meetings: For attending or participating in meetings.
	+ Training: For undergoing training or courses.
* **Status** (7): Select the status of your request:
	+ Pending: Your request is pending approval.
	+ Approved: Your request is approved.
	+ Rejected: Your request is rejected.
* **Requestor** (8): Select if you are the requestor.
* **Approver** (9): Select if you are the approver.
* **Request Details** (10): Enter details about your leave request.
* **Comments** (11): Enter any comments or notes about your request.
* **Email** (12): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (13): Enter your phone number (if necessary).
* **Department** (14): Select your department involved in the project.
* **Project** (15): Select your project involved in the event.
* **Team** (16): Select the team members involved in the event.
* **Date** (17): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (18): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (19): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **Form Submitted By** (20): Select if you are submitting the form.
* **Form Submitted On** (21): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitter** (22): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter** (23): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Assignee** (24): Select the person assigned to the request.
* **Assigned User** (25): Select the user assigned to the request.
