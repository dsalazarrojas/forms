# Training Camp Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Camp Leave Of Absence Request" form is designed for trainers and training camps to submit leave requests for approved absences from the training camp. This form is used to inform the organization about the trainer's leave dates, reason for absence, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you provide your coach's name, the request date, and return date.
3. Specify the reason for your leave request.
4. Provide the name of the training camp you are associated with.
5. Enter the name of your team, if applicable.
6. Enter your contact person's name and email, if applicable.
7. Add any additional notes about your leave request, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name (Coach Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your coach's name as it appears on our records.
* **Request Date (Request Date)** (`date`, required: false): Select the date when you would like your leave to start.
* **Reason for Request (Reason for Request)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request. This could be for personal or professional reasons.
* **Return Date (Return Date)** (`date`, required: false): Choose the date when you would like to return to the training camp.
* **Training Camp Name (Training Camp Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the training camp you are associated with.
* **Team (Team)** (`text`, required: false): If you are part of a team, enter the team name here.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person who can be reached for further information.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your leave request, if necessary.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your leave request.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your designated contact person for clarification.
