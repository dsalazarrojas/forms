# Business Workshop Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Workshop Leave Request Form is a tool for employees to submit their leave requests for workshops and training events. It helps in gathering necessary information for your manager to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details such as name, ID, email, and phone number.
* Provide your department and manager's name.
* Specify the workshop title, date, and location.
* Explain the brief description and purpose of the workshop.
* If travel is required, choose the correct option.
* Specify how the cost of the workshop will be covered.
* Describe the expected outcomes and learning objectives from the workshop.
* Estimate the cost of the workshop.
* Describe your coverage plan for your responsibilities while you are away.
* If approved, your manager will receive a note with the details of your request for their review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your full name as it appears in your employee directory.
* **Employee ID or Number**: Your unique employee identification number.
* **Employee Email**: Your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Department or Division**: The department or division you belong to.
* **Manager Name**: The name of your direct supervisor.
* **Workshop or Training Title**: The name of the workshop or training event you plan to attend.
* **Brief Description of Workshop**: A short description of what the workshop is about.
* **Will Travel Be Required?**: Select 'Yes, domestic travel', 'Yes, international travel', or 'No travel required' accordingly.
* **How Will the Cost Be Covered?**: Choose how the cost of the workshop will be covered from the given options.
* **Workshop Start Date**: The date when the workshop begins.
* **Workshop End Date**: The date when the workshop ends.
* **Total Days of Leave Requested**: The number of working days you need for the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: The location where the workshop will be held.
* **Business Justification**: Explain why attending this workshop is important for your work.
* **Expected Learning Outcomes**: Describe what you hope to gain from this workshop.
* **Estimated Cost**: Provide an estimate of the workshop fee plus travel costs (if applicable).
* **Cost Covered By**: If you have not already selected this, describe how the cost will be covered.
* **Coverage Plan for Responsibilities**: Describe who will cover your responsibilities while you are away.
* **Manager Approval**: This will be sent to your manager for their review and approval.
