# Leave Or Remote Work Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leave or Remote Work Request Form is a tool for employees to request leave or remote work from the company.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill in all required fields marked with a star (*).
*   Select the correct employee information from the dropdown menu on the second page.
*   Specify the dates you wish to take leave or the duration of remote work.
*   Choose the type of leave that applies to your situation.
*   Select the reason for your leave from the provided options.
*   Indicate whether you are actively employed by the company or not.
*   Select the approval status of your manager or team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_2_employees_info`): Select the correct employee from the dropdown menu.
* **Employee Name** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_3_employees_name`): Enter the name of the employee requesting leave or remote work.
* **Employee Email** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_4_employees_email`): Enter the email address of the employee requesting leave or remote work.
* **Employee Phone** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_5_employees_phone`): Enter the phone number of the employee requesting leave or remote work.
* **Dates of Absence** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_6_dates_of_absence`): Enter the dates of the leave or remote work in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Type of Leave** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_7_type_of_leave`): Select the type of leave you are requesting, i.e. Sick Leave, Remote Work, or Other.
* **Reason for Leave** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_8_reason`): Select the reason for your leave from the provided options, i.e. Personal/Family Leave, Bereavement Leave, Family/Medical Leave.
* **Approval Status** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_9_approval`): Select the approval status from the dropdown menu, i.e. Manager Approval, Team Lead Approval, Director Approval.
* **Comments** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_10_comments`): Enter any comments or additional information related to your leave or remote work request.
* **Employee Status** (`leave_or_remote_work_request_form_11_employees_status`): Select the status of the employee, i.e. Active, Inactive, On Leave.
