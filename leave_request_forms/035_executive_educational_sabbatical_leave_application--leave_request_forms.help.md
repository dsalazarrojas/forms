# Executive Educational Sabbatical Leave Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Executive Educational Sabbatical Leave Application form is designed to help executives apply for a temporary break from work to pursue educational goals. This form is essential for executives who wish to engage in academic activities such as taking courses, attending workshops, or participating in research projects related to their field of expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Read the form instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Answer each question accurately and honestly, providing all necessary information.
3.  Select the relevant options for each field, based on your educational pursuits.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **sabbatical_request_form**: Please select whether you are requesting a sabbatical (`Yes`) or not (`No`). Select `Yes` if you have already discussed this request with your supervisor/manager.
*   **executive_education_sabbatical_leave**: Provide a brief description of your educational goals and purposes for taking a sabbatical leave. Please be specific and concise about the activities you plan to pursue.
*   **education_program_details**: Choose the option that best describes your educational pursuit (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **dates_of_absence**: Enter the start and end dates of your planned absence from work.
*   **return_date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
*   **leave_status**: Select the status of your leave (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **approval_level**: Choose the level of approval needed for your leave (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **approval_status**: Select the status of your approval (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
*   **remarks**: Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your request.
*   **submitter**: Enter your name, as this will be used to track who submitted the form.
*   **submit_date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
*   **approval_date**: Enter the date your leave was approved.
*   **review_comment**: Enter any comments or feedback for the reviewer, if necessary.

## Tips

Please ensure to fill the form accurately and honestly, and to follow up with your supervisor/manager after submitting the form to confirm they have received it. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
