# Public Transport Advocacy Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Public Transport Advocacy Leave Time Off Form is used by employees to request time off for events or activities related to public transportation advocacy. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected for approvers to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of your request in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields.
3.  Select the reason for your request in the **Reason** field.
4.  If applicable, provide the title and description of your activity in the **Activity Title** and **Activity Description** fields.
5.  Choose the type of transport you will be using for your activity (Public Transport, Private Transport, Air Transport, or Other) in the **Transport Type** field.
6.  Enter the start and end times of your activity in the **Start Time** and **End Time** fields.
7.  Enter the duration of your activity in the **Duration** field.
8.  Add any comments about your request in the **Comments** field.
9.  Select the approver's name from the list in the **Approver Name** field.
10. Enter the approver's email address in the **Approver Email** field.
11. Enter the approver's phone number in the **Approver Phone** field.
12. Enter the approver's comment about your request in the **Approver Comment** field.
13. If your request has been approved, select the **Status** field to **Approved**.
14. If your request has been denied, enter the reason for denial in the **Denied Reason** field.
15. If your request is in progress, enter details about the progress in the **In Progress Details** field.
16. Add any public transport details about your activity in the **Public Transport Details** field.
17. Add any private transport details about your activity in the **Private Transport Details** field.
18. Add any air transport details about your activity in the **Air Transport Details** field.
19. Add any other transport details about your activity in the **Other Transport Details** field.
20. Finally, ensure you have an employee signature in the **Employee Signature** field and an approver signature in the **Approver Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (required)**: Enter your employee name to identify you as the person requesting time off.
* **Start Date (required)**: Select the start date of your activity or event.
* **End Date (required)**: Select the end date of your activity or event.
* **Reason (required)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for your request.
* **Activity Title (optional)**: Enter a title for your activity or event, if applicable.
* **Activity Description (optional)**: Enter a detailed description of your activity or event, if applicable.
* **Transport Type (required)**: Select the type of transport you will be using (Public Transport, Private Transport, Air Transport, or Other).
* **Start Time (optional)**: Enter the start time of your activity or event, if applicable.
* **End Time (optional)**: Enter the end time of your activity or event, if applicable.
* **Duration (required)**: Enter the duration of your activity or event in hours.
* **Comments (optional)**: Add any additional comments about your request.
* **Approver Name (required)**: Select the approver's name from the list.
* **Approver Email (required)**: Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone (required)**: Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Comment (required)**: Enter the approver's comment about your request.
* **Status (optional)**: If your request has been approved, select **Approved**. If denied, select **Denied**. If still in progress, select **In Progress**.
* **Approved Date (optional)**: If your request has been approved, enter the date of approval.
* **Denied Reason (optional)**: If your request has been denied, enter the reason for denial.
* **In Progress Details (optional)**: If your request is in progress, enter details about the progress.
* **Public Transport Details (optional)**: Enter any details about public transport used for your activity.
* **Private Transport Details (optional)**: Enter any details about private transport used for your activity.
* **Air Transport Details (optional)**: Enter any details about air transport used for your activity.
* **Other Transport Details (optional)**: Enter any other transport details used for your activity.
* **Employee Signature (optional)**: Ensure you sign the form as the employee making the request.
* **Approver Signature (optional)**: Ensure the approver signs the form after reviewing your request.
