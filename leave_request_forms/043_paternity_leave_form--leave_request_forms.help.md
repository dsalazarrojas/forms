# paternity leave form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a formal request for paternity leave, and it's meant for expectant parents to provide necessary information about their leave details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information. 
2. Make sure to upload the required documents.
3. Review and sign the declaration section.
4. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fathers Name** (`Fathers Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the father.
* **Fathers Surname** (`Fathers Surname`, text, required): Enter the surname of the father.
* **Fathers Date of Birth** (`Fathers Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the father.
* **Leave Start Date** (`Leave Start Date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like your leave to start.
* **Leave End Date** (`Leave End Date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like your leave to end.
* **Total Leave Days** (`Total Leave Days`, number, required): Enter the total number of days you would like to take for paternity leave.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used as your contact information.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): If there is an emergency contact, please enter their name. This is not a required field, but it's a good idea to fill it in.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact and you (e.g., Emergency Contact, Family Member, Friend, Other).
* **Declaration Declaration** (`Declaration Declaration`, text, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or declarations you may need to make.
* **Upload Document 1** (`Upload Document 1`, text, required): Upload any necessary document to support your leave request.
* **Upload Document 2** (`Upload Document 2`, text, optional): Upload an additional document to support your leave request, if needed.
* **Upload Document 3** (`Upload Document 3`, text, optional): Upload another document to support your leave request, if needed.
* **Declaration Signed** (`Declaration Signed`, select_one, required): Sign to confirm that the declaration is true.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required): This field will automatically be filled in by the system.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please add them here.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click to submit the form for processing.
