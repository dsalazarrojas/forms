# Strategy Workshop Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a leave of absence request for a Strategy Workshop. This will help your supervisor review and approve or deny your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
- Fill in the requested information accurately and completely.
- Ensure you have your supervisor's confirmation before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Leave Form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is a text field where you can write a brief description of your leave request.
* **Employee Name** (`2`, `text`, not required): This field is for your employee name. Please fill it in accurately.
* **Leave Start Date** (`3`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you plan to start your leave from.
* **Leave End Date** (`4`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you plan to end your leave by.
* **Workshop Leave Dates** (`5`, `date`, not required): If your leave request is for a specific workshop, enter the dates for that workshop.
* **Employee Leave Reason** (`6`, `text`, not required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Supervisor Confirmation** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Choose how your supervisor can respond to your leave request. Options are: Approved, Denied, Not Applicable.
* **Employee Leave Request** (`8`, `text`, not required): Write a message for your supervisor, explaining the reason for your leave request.
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, not required): This is a field for your supervisor to add any comments or additional information.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address, so your supervisor can reach out to you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and truthful to avoid any issues with your leave request.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or HR department.
- If your leave request is denied, discuss the reason with your supervisor and try to resolve any issues.
