# Pharmacy Staff Leave Month Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow pharmacy staff to select their leave month and type, which will be used to process and approve leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Leave Request** field with the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Sick, or Other).
2. Select your **Leave Month** from the dropdown list (e.g., January, February, etc.).
3. Fill in your **Start Date** and **End Date** fields with the dates of your leave period.
4. Optionally, you can specify the **Leave Duration** in days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter a brief title or description of your leave request.
- **Leave Request** (`leave_request`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more types of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Sick, or Other).
- **Leave Month** (`leave_month`, select_one, optional): Select the month of your leave request from the dropdown list.
- **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Sick, or Other).
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your leave starts.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your leave ends.
- **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, optional): Specify the duration of your leave in days.
