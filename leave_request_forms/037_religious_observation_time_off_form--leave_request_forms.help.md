# Religious Observation Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to request time off for religious or spiritual observances. It allows them to select the observance, specify the dates and duration of the time off, and assign the request to a specific person or themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate observance that applies to your situation.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your time off.
3. Enter the duration of your time off, if applicable.
4. Enter the number of days off you require.
5. Enter any additional comments.
6. Choose whether you would like to assign this request to a specific person or submit it yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select An Observance**: Choose the relevant religious or spiritual observance that applies to your situation. This will help your manager understand the reason for your request.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your time off to begin.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like your time off to end.
* **Duration**: If applicable, enter the number of days or hours you would like to be off. This will help your manager understand the length of your request.
* **Number of Days Off**: Enter the number of days you require for your time off.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations for your request.
* **Assigned To**: Choose whether you would like to assign this request to a specific person or submit it yourself.
* **Submitter**: Choose whether you would like to submit this request yourself or assign it to someone else.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about a specific field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
- If you need help, contact your manager or HR representative for assistance.
