# Retail Cashier Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a cashier employee. The form helps the retail store manager review and approve or deny the request based on the provided information and reason for leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. In the **Retail Cashier Leave Of Absence Form** field, enter a brief title or description of your request.
2. In the **Employee Name** field, enter your employee name.
3. Enter your **Start Date**, the date you wish to begin your leave of absence.
4. Enter your **End Date**, the date you wish to end your leave of absence.
5. In the **Reason For Leave** field, provide a brief explanation of your reason for requesting a leave of absence.
6. If applicable, enter the **Return To Work Date**, the date you expect to return to work.
7. If you are unsure about the number of hours you will be absent, enter the number of hours you expect to be absent in the **Hours Absent** field.
8. Select the status of your manager's review in the **Manager Review Status** field. This can be **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Pending**.
9. Optionally, add a comment from your manager in the **Manager Comment** field.
10. Finally, sign and date your form in the **Employee Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Cashier Leave Of Absence Form**: 
	+ Enter a brief title or description of your request.
* **Employee Name**: 
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or HR records.
* **Start Date**: 
	+ Enter the date you wish to begin your leave of absence (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **End Date**: 
	+ Enter the date you wish to end your leave of absence (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Reason For Leave**: 
	+ Provide a brief explanation of your reason for requesting a leave of absence, e.g., personal, vacation, medical, etc.
* **Return To Work Date**: 
	+ If applicable, enter the date you expect to return to work after your leave of absence.
* **Hours Absent**: 
	+ Enter the number of hours you expect to be absent during your leave of absence.
* **Manager Review Status**: 
	+ Select **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Pending** to indicate the status of your manager's review.
* **Manager Comment**: 
	+ Optionally, enter a comment from your manager regarding your leave of absence request.
* **Employee Signature**: 
	+ Sign and date your form to confirm that you have submitted this request.
