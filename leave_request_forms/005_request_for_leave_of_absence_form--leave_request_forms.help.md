# request_for_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a leave of absence from work. Please fill it out to submit your leave request to your department and manager for approval.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your company name, employee name, ID, and contact details.
2. Provide the reason for your leave and the dates you plan to be away.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your request.
4. Ensure your manager and department head sign and date their sections to approve your leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): This is your company's name where you work.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): This is your full name as an employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required): This is your unique employee ID number.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, required): This is the reason you are requesting a leave of absence for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, required): This is the reason for your absence.
* **Date of Returning to Work** (`returning_date`, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Select "Yes" if your department is okay with your request.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, required): Your manager's signature confirms their approval.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Department Head Signature** (`department_head_signature`, required): Your department head's signature confirms their approval.
* **Department Head Name** (`department_head_name`, required): Enter your department head's name.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, required): Enter your department head's email address.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, required): Enter your manager's title.
* **Department Title** (`department_title`, required): Enter your department's title.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, required): Enter your job position.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, required): Enter your department.
* **Department Head Phone** (`department_head_phone`, required): Enter your department head's phone number.
