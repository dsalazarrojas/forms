# Leave Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leave Request form is designed to facilitate the submission of leave requests from employees, providing a structured format for capturing relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your leave start and end dates in the `Leave Start Date` and `Leave End Date` fields, ensuring they are accurate and formatted correctly.
2. Specify the duration of your leave in the `Leave Duration` field.
3. Select the type of leave you're requesting from the `Leave Type` options (Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, or Emergency Leave).
4. Provide a brief description of your leave reason in the `Leave Reason` field.
5. Set the status of your leave request to either `Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Pending` in the `Leave Status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave. Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave. Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of days or hours you will be away.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, or Emergency Leave).
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your leave reason.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Set the status of your leave request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure your leave request is processed smoothly.
* If your leave type is not one of the options listed, you may need to contact your HR or manager for assistance.
* You can update the status of your leave request by contacting your manager or HR representative.
* For any questions or concerns, please refer to your company's leave policy or contact your HR representative.
