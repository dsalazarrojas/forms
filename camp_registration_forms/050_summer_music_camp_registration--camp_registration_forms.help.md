# Summer Music Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Music Camp Registration form is a required form for students who want to participate in the Summer Music Camp. This form is used to collect essential information about the student, their music experience, and other relevant details. Please take a few minutes to complete the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Registrant Info" section.
3. Specify your music experience in the "Music Experience" field.
4. Choose your preferred camp date in the "Camp Date" section.
5. Indicate your scholarship status in the "Scholarship" section.
6. Enter your payment method in the "Payment" section (optional).
7. List your music instruments and preferences in the "Music Instruments" and "Camp Preferences" sections.
8. Provide the contact information for an emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
9. List any other expenses that you might incur during the camp in the "Other Expenses" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Info (1)**:
	+ Name (text field): Enter your full name.
	+ (Note: This field is not explicitly labeled, but it is assumed to be the registrant's name.)
* **Music Experience (2)**:
	+ Music Experience (text field): Describe your music experience, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Camp Date (3)**:
	+ Camp Date (date field): Select the date you prefer to attend the music camp.
* **Scholarship (4)**:
	+ Scholarship (select multiple checkbox): Check 'Yes' if you are eligible for a scholarship.
* **Payment (5)**:
	+ Payment (note field): Enter any additional information about your payment method (optional).
* **Music Instruments (6)**:
	+ Music Instruments (text field): List the instruments you are proficient in.
* **Camp Preferences (7)**:
	+ Camp Preferences (text field): List any special preferences you have for the music camp.
* **Contact Info (8)**:
	+ Contact Info (text field): Enter your contact information, such as your email or phone number.
* **Emergency Contact (9)**:
	+ Emergency Contact (text field): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Other Expenses (10)**:
	+ Other Expenses (number field): Enter any additional expenses you might incur during the camp.
