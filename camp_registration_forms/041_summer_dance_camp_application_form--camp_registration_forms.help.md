# Summer Dance Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students who have already been accepted to attend the Summer Dance Camp. It is used to gather more information from students about their preferences and details, which will be used to enhance their experience at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your preferred dance style from the "dance_style" dropdown menu.
6. Select the days you plan to attend from the "days_attending" dropdown menu.
7. Choose your payment method from the "fee_type" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the amount you are paying in the "fee_amount" field.
9. Enter your arrival date and time in the "date" and "time" fields.
10. Enter your payment date and time in the "fee_date" and "fee_time" fields.
11. Select your payment status from the "fee_status" dropdown menu.
12. Enter your name as it appears on your credit card in the "name_on_card" field.
13. Enter your credit card number in the "card_number" field.
14. Enter your card security code (CVV) in the "cvv" field.
15. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
16. If necessary, enter any additional comments or notes in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number.
* **dance_style** (Select One, Not Required): Select your preferred dance style (Jazz, Ballet, HipHop).
* **days_attending** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select the days you plan to attend (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **fee_type** (Select One, Not Required): Choose your payment method (Cash, Card, Bank).
* **fee_amount** (Number, Not Required): Enter the amount you are paying.
* **date** (Date, Not Required): Enter your arrival date.
* **time** (Time, Not Required): Enter your arrival time.
* **fee_date** (Date, Not Required): Enter your payment date.
* **fee_time** (Time, Not Required): Enter your payment time.
* **fee_status** (Select One, Not Required): Select your payment status (Paid, Unpaid).
* **name_on_card** (Text, Not Required): Enter your name as it appears on your credit card.
* **card_number** (Text, Not Required): Enter your credit card number.
* **cvv** (Text, Not Required): Enter your card security code (CVV).
* **zip** (Text, Not Required): Enter your zip code.
* **note** (Note, Not Required): If necessary, enter any additional comments or notes.
* **submit** (Text, Not Required): This field is not used.
* **cancel** (Text, Not Required): This field is not used.
* **Select days attending** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select the days you plan to attend.
