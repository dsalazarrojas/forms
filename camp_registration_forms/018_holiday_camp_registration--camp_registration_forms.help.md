# Holiday Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Camp Registration form is designed for parents or guardians to submit their child's registration details for a holiday camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
2. Select the activities your child is interested in participating in from the "Activity Choice" field.
3. Provide detailed information about your child's preferences and any special requests in the "Camper Preferences" and "Special Requests" fields.
4. Confirm the length of stay for your child in the "Stay Length" field.
5. Enter the dates of the stay in the "Stay Dates" field.
6. Finally, provide accurate contact information for yourself and your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Info**: Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant details in this field.
* **Activity Choice**: Select one of the three options for activities your child would like to participate in. Please note that some activities may have additional requirements or prerequisites.
* **Camper Details**: Provide a detailed description of your child's needs, allergies, or any other relevant information that the camp staff should be aware of.
* **Parent Details**: Enter your name and contact information so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so that we can reach you if there are any issues or changes.
* **Camper Preferences**: Describe your child's preferences, such as favorite toys, foods, or activities.
* **Stay Length**: Select the number of days your child will be staying at the camp.
* **Stay Dates**: Enter the dates your child will be attending the camp.
* **Activity Preferences**: Describe your child's preferences for activities or any other information that is not covered in the previous fields.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements, such as medical needs or equipment needs.
* **Payment Info**: Enter the payment details, such as the method of payment and amount paid.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
