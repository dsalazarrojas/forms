# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Enrollment Form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's personal details, medical history, and any special needs or allergies that the camp staff should be aware of. This form helps us ensure the child's safety and comfort during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and completely, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your child's information in the "Child Information" section (Page 1).
3. Provide your contact and medical details in the "Parent Information" section (Page 2).
4. List any emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contact" section (Page 3).
5. If applicable, provide any medical records and information in the "Medical Record" section (Page 4).
6. List any medications the child is currently taking in the "Medication" section (Page 5).
7. Add any medical notes or concerns in the "Medical Notes" section (Page 6).
8. If the child has any special needs or requirements, provide information in the "Special Needs" section (Page 7).
9. Finally, list any allergies the child has in the "Allergies" section (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Child's Name**, **Age**, **Birth Date** : Enter your child's name, age, and date of birth to help us get to know them.
* **Parent Information (Page 2)**
	+ **Parent/Guardian Name**, **Contact Number**, **Email** : Provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 3)**
	+ **Name**, **Contact Number**, **Relationship** : List the name, phone number, and relationship (e.g., mother, father, aunt, etc.) of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Record (Page 4)**
	+ **Medical History**, **Conditions**, **Surgeries** : Share any relevant medical history, conditions, or surgeries to help us take care of your child.
* **Medication (Page 5)**
	+ **Medication Name**, **Dosage**, **Frequency** : List the name, dosage, and frequency of any medications the child is currently taking.
* **Medical Notes (Page 6)**
	+ **Notes, Concerns** : Add any medical notes or concerns you'd like us to be aware of.
* **Special Needs (Page 7)**
	+ **Needs, Requirements** : If your child has any special needs or requirements, please provide more information so we can accommodate them.
* **Allergies (Page 8)**
	+ **Allergies, Food, Environmental** : List any allergies your child may have, including food and environmental allergies.

Note: Please fill out all the required fields accurately and completely to ensure the best experience for your child at the camp.
