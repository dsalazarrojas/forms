# Summer Camp Drop Off & Pick Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of fields used to gather important information for your child's summer camp registration. Please fill out this form to ensure we have all the necessary details for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
Please read each field carefully and fill out the information required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (<parent_info>, Text, Required/Optional): Please enter your name and contact information as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child Information** (<child_info>, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contacts** (<emergency_contacts>, Text, Required/Optional): List any emergency contacts that we should reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Pick Up Information** (<pick_up_info, Text, Required/Optional): Provide details about who will be picking up your child, including their name and contact information.
* **Drop Off Information** (<drop_off_info, Text, Required/Optional): Specify the drop off location and time for your child.
* **Notes** (<notes, Text, Required/Optional): Add any additional notes or information about your child that might be helpful for us.
* **Email** (<email, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with important information about your child's stay.
* **Phone** (<phone, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Date** (<date, Date, Required/Optional): Select the date of when you would like your child to start attending the summer camp.
* **Time** (<time, Time, Required/Optional): Choose the time of when you would like your child to start attending the summer camp.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we have all necessary information for your child's stay.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you need to make any changes to this form, you can do so by contacting us at our office phone number or email.
