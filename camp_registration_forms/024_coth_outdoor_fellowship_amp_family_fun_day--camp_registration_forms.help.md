# COTH Outdoor Fellowship and Family Fun Day - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COTH Outdoor Fellowship and Family Fun Day form is used for registering attendees for the event. It captures essential information about each participant, including contact details, event preferences, and special requirements. This form will help us prepare for the event and ensure that we have the necessary information to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the date you plan to attend (Saturday or Sunday).
2. Enter the primary contact's name and email address.
3. Provide the phone number of the primary contact.
4. Enter the family name for registration purposes.
5. Select the number of attendees, adults, youth, children, and toddlers.
6. List all participants with their ages for participant details.
7. Choose your meal preference (e.g., Standard BBQ Plate, Vegetarian Option, etc.).
8. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions.
9. Select the activities you are interested in (e.g., Outdoor games, Team sports, etc.).
10. Indicate your interest in volunteering.
11. If applicable, provide skills you can offer as a volunteer.
12. Select whether you need transportation and provide the pickup location if necessary.
13. Indicate whether you need accommodation.
14. Mention any medical conditions that should be noted for safety purposes.
15. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
16. Accept or decline the liability waiver.
17. Grant permission for photos to be taken and used for promotion.
18. Optionally, select your preferred payment method and donation level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the primary person responsible for the registration.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact. This will be used for event updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
* **Family Name**: Enter the family name for registration purposes.
* **Event Date**: Select the date you plan to attend (Saturday or Sunday).
* **Total Number of Attendees**: Enter the total number of attendees, including family members.
* **Number of Adults**: Enter the number of adults (ages 18 and above) attending.
* **Number of Youth**: Enter the number of youth (ages 13-17) attending.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children (ages 5-12) attending.
* **Number of Toddlers**: Enter the number of toddlers (under 5 years) attending.
* **Participant Details**: List all participants with their ages for participant details.
* **T-Shirt Sizes**: List the sizes of t-shirts you would like to have available for each person (optional).
* **Meal Preference**: Select the meal preference for your group (e.g., Standard BBQ Plate, Vegetarian Option, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If applicable, list any dietary restrictions or special needs.
* **Activities Interested In**: Select all the activities you are interested in (e.g., Outdoor games, Team sports, etc.).
* **Volunteering Interest**: Indicate whether you are interested in volunteering.
* **Volunteering Skills**: If volunteering, provide a list of skills you can offer as a volunteer (optional).
* **Transportation Needed**: Select whether you need transportation and provide the pickup location if necessary.
* **Accommodation Needed**: Indicate whether you need accommodation.
* **Medical Conditions**: If applicable, mention any medical conditions that should be noted for safety purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Liability Waiver**: Accept or decline the liability waiver.
* **Photo Permission**: Grant permission for photos to be taken and used for promotion.
* **Registration Fee**: Optionally select the preferred donation level per family.
* **Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method (e.g., Cash at event, Check, Online payment, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Event**: Select the source of your referral (e.g., Church announcement, Friend or family, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Optionally provide any additional comments or questions.
