# playschool_trial_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from users to register their child for a playschool trial. This form is designed to ensure that all required details are captured to facilitate a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your user information, including your name, in the "User Information" section.
2. Enter details about your child in the "Child Information" section.
3. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
4. Grant your consent by filling in the "Consent" field.
5. Optional fields include your phone number, email, and parent's phone number and email.
6. If an emergency contact is required, provide their name and contact information.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the trial, if applicable.
8. Provide the time of the trial, if applicable.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
10. Finally, select the assigned user from the drop-down list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)**: This field is required and is used to capture your basic details.
* **Child Information (Child Information)**: This field is required and is used to capture details about your child.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: This field is required and is used to capture emergency contact information.
* **Consent (Consent)**: This field is required and is used to obtain your consent for your child's participation.
* **User Phone (User Phone)**: This field is optional and is used to capture your phone number.
* **User Email (User Email)**: This field is optional and is used to capture your email address.
* **Parent Phone (Parent Phone)**: This field is optional and is used to capture your parent's phone number.
* **Parent Email (Parent Email)**: This field is optional and is used to capture your parent's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the emergency contact's name.
* **Start Date (Start Date)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the start date of the trial.
* **End Date (End Date)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the end date of the trial.
* **Time (Time)**: This field is optional and is used to capture the time of the trial.
* **Notes (Notes)**: This field is optional and is used to capture any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned User (Assigned User)**: This field is required and is used to select the assigned user for the trial.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use a valid phone number format for the phone fields.
- Enter a valid email address for the email fields.
- Select a valid assigned user from the drop-down list.
- Double-check your details before submitting the form.
