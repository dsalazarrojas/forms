# hockey_camp_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects participant information for the hockey camp registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Specify your emergency contact details in the "Emergency Contact" field.
4. Select your participant schedule in the "Participant Schedule" field.
5. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Enter your personal details, such as your name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your address, phone number, and email address for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Schedule**: Select your available dates and times to participate in the camp.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and other relevant payment information.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Double-check your contact and payment information to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
