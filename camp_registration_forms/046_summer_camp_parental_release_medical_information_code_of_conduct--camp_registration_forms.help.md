# summer_camp_parental_release_medical_information_code_of_conduct - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical information and consent from parents or guardians of students attending a summer camp. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the camp has all necessary information to provide safe and proper care to each student. This form must be completed for each student registered for the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are a parent or guardian of the student attending the summer camp.
2. Carefully review each field to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Complete all required fields (indicated by `**Required**` in the field explanation).
4. Submit the completed form to the summer camp administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`1`, `text`, `false`): Provide a brief description of your student.
- **Medical Information** (`2`, `text`, `false`): Provide any medical information that may be relevant to your student's safety and well-being at the camp.
- **Consent** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select all options that apply to your understanding of the camp's policies and code of conduct. 
  - Option 1: I have read and understand the camp's code of conduct.
  - Other: Specify "Other" and describe any additional understanding or concerns.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`4`, `text`, `false`): If you have any concerns or questions about the camp's terms and conditions, please specify them here.
- **Student Name** (`5`, `text`, `false`): Provide the name of your student.
- **Student Date of Birth** (`6`, `date`, `false`): Enter your student's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Student Grade Level** (`7`, `number`, `false`): Enter your student's grade level (e.g. 3rd, 4th, 5th, etc.).
- **Student Health Information** (`8`, `text`, `false`): Provide any additional health information that may be relevant to your student's safety and well-being at the camp.
- **Parent Name** (`9`, `text`, `false`): Provide your name and contact information so that camp staff can reach you if needed.
- **Parent Email** (`10`, `email`, `false`): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
