# Sports Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Summer Camp Application Form is a registration form designed for campers to apply for the sports summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the sports you are interested in participating in by checking the boxes next to each sport.
2.  Enter your name in the text field for "Applicant Name".
3.  Choose your preferred schedule from the dropdown menu.
4.  Enter your contact number in the text field.
5.  Enter your email address in the text field.
6.  If you have any additional information you'd like to share, enter it in the "Additional Info" text field.

* **Additional Info**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share about yourself or your participation in the camp, enter it here.
*   This field is optional and can be left blank if you do not have any additional information to share.
*   Please keep your response brief and to the point.
*   You can share as much or as little information as you like.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to check that you have selected the sports you are interested in participating in, as this will help us get a better understanding of your preferences.
*   Double-check your contact number and email address for accuracy.
*   If you have any special needs or requirements, please let us know in the "Additional Info" field.
