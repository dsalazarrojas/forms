# Family Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Camp Registration Form is used to gather essential information from families who are interested in attending the summer camp. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for a smooth and enjoyable camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each section.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you've provided all required information.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send the form to the camp administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (`camp_registration_form_personal_info`, text, required): Enter your family's contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you or your family member has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Camper Info** (`camper_info`, text, optional): Enter information about each family member attending the camp, including their name, age, and any relevant health information.
* **Camping History** (`previous_camping_activity`, text, optional): If you or your family member has prior camping experience, describe your experience and level of skill.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional information about your family's needs or concerns.
* **Activity Level** (`select_one_field`, select one, optional): Choose the activity level that best suits your family's needs and abilities:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Special Abilities** (`select_multiple_field`, select multiple, optional): Select any special abilities or skills your family members possess:
	+ Swimming
	+ Hiking
	+ Dancing
* **Arrival Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the camp.
* **Arrival Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the camp.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future communication with the camp administration.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
