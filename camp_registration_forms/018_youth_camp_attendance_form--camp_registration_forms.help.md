# Youth Camp Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Camp Attendance Form is a vital tool for parents or guardians to provide essential information about their child's attendance at a youth camp or program. This form ensures that the camp staff has all necessary details to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for each camper.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the child:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Date of Birth
	* Phone
	* Address
	* Parent Signature
	* Date Attended
2. Provide any relevant emergency contact information:
	* Emergency Contact Info (optional)
	* Emergency Contact Relation (optional)
3. If applicable, provide information about any medical conditions or allergies:
	* Medical Info (optional)
	* Allergy (optional)
	* Medical Conditions (optional)
4. Indicate any special needs or medical conditions:
	* Medical Conditions (optional)
	* Special Needs (optional)
	* Parent Permission (optional)
5. Fill out the form with the child's details:
	* Camper Name
	* Camper Group (optional)
	* Camper Dorm (optional)
	* Camper Grade (optional)
	* Camper Age (optional)
6. Confirm the camper's attendance dates:
	* Date Attended
7. Add any notes for the camp staff:
	* Notes (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the child's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the child's email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone**: Enter the child's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the child's home address.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: If applicable, describe the relation of the emergency contact to the child.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the information provided.
* **Camper Name**: Enter the child's name.
* **Camper Group**: If applicable, enter the child's group or team name.
* **Camper Dorm**: If applicable, enter the child's dorm or accommodation name.
* **Camper Grade**: If applicable, enter the child's grade level.
* **Camper Age**: If applicable, enter the child's age.
* **Date Attended**: Enter the dates the child attended the camp.
* **Duration Attended**: If applicable, enter the duration the child attended the camp.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments for the camp staff.
* **Parent Permission**: If applicable, indicate parent permission to allow the child to participate in specific activities.
* **Medical Info**: If applicable, enter any medical information or conditions.
* **Allergy**: If applicable, enter any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions**: If applicable, enter any medical conditions.
* **Special Needs**: If applicable, indicate any special needs or requirements.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the information provided.
* **Camper Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the information provided.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the camp staff for guidance.
* Please review your answers carefully before submission.
