# Camper Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Event Registration Form is a crucial document that requires campers to provide essential information about themselves, their parents or guardians, and the event they are registering for. This form helps us understand camper needs, preferences, and requirements to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your camper's details, including first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your parent or guardian's information, including their first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select the event you are registering for and provide the event's date and start time.
4. Choose your activity interests and skill level.
5. If applicable, provide any health and special needs information.
6. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (Camper First Name, text, required)
Enter your camper's first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (Camper Last Name, text, required)
Enter your camper's last name.

### 3. **Email Address** (Camper Email Address, email, required)
Enter your camper's email address.

### 4. **Phone Number** (Camper Phone Number, text, required)
Enter your camper's phone number.

### 5. **Age** (Camper Age, number, required)
Enter your camper's age.

### 6. **Parent or Guardian First Name** (Guardian First Name, text, required)
Enter your camper's parent or guardian's first name.

### 7. **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (Guardian Last Name, text, required)
Enter your camper's parent or guardian's last name.

### 8. **Guardian Email Address** (Guardian Email Address, email, required)
Enter your camper's parent or guardian's email address.

### 9. **Guardian Phone Number** (Guardian Phone Number, text, required)
Enter your camper's parent or guardian's phone number.

### 10. **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required)
Select the event you are registering for.

### 11. **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required)
Choose the date of the event.

### 12. **Event Start Time** (Event Start Time, time, required)
Select the start time of the event.

### 13. **Event Location** (Event Location, text, required)
Enter the location of the event.

### 14. **Select activity interests** (Select activity interests, select_multiple, required)
Choose your camper's activity interests.

### 15. **Skill Level** (Skill Level, select_one, required)
Select your camper's skill level.

### 16. **Food or Environmental Allergies** (Food or Environmental Allergies, text, required)
If your camper has any allergies or sensitivities, please list them.

### 17. **Medical Conditions or Disabilities** (Medical Conditions or Disabilities, text, required)
If your camper has any medical conditions or disabilities, please list them.

### 18. **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** (Dietary Restrictions or Preferences, text, required)
If your camper has any dietary restrictions or preferences, please list them.

### 19. **I agree to event rules and liability waiver** (I agree to event rules and liability waiver, select_one, required)
Please carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to select the correct event and date.
- Be honest about your camper's skills and interests.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
- Review the terms and conditions of the event carefully before agreeing to them.
