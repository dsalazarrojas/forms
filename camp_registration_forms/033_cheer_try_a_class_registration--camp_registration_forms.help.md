# Cheer Try A Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from students who wish to try out a class offered by our organization. It will be used to assess the student's suitability for the class, ensure their safety and participation, and collect waiver and authorization for their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Select the student's age from the dropdown options.
3.  Enter the parent/guardian's full name and contact email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
4.  Select a preferred trial class date from the list of available dates.
5.  Choose the student's estimated skill level based on their experience and ability.
6.  If necessary, enter important medical notes or allergies in the "Important Medical Notes or Allergies" field.
7.  Agree to the waiver and authorization form in the "Release of Liability and Participation Agreement" field.
8.  Enter emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Name and Phone" field.
9.  Confirm their registration date and method of referral in the "Registration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`trial_student_full_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
*   **Age** (`trial_student_age`, number, required): Select the student's age from the dropdown options.
*   **Parent / Guardian Name** (`parent_full_name_contact`, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's full name and contact email address and phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`trial_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the student's contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`trial_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the student's contact phone number.
*   **Trial Class Date** (`preferred_trial_date`, select_one, required): Select a preferred trial class date from the list of available dates.
*   **Estimated Skill Level** (`current_skill_level_est`, select_one, required): Choose the student's estimated skill level based on their experience and ability.
*   **Important Medical Notes or Allergies** (`known_medical_notes`, text, optional): If necessary, enter important medical notes or allergies.
*   **Release of Liability and Participation Agreement** (`trial_class_waiver_ack`, select_one, required): Agree to the waiver and authorization form.
*   **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact_full_info`, text, required): Enter emergency contact information.
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date_logged`, date, required): Confirm their registration date and method of referral.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid any potential issues or complications.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our organization for assistance.
