# Summer Camper Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a summer camper for the camp. It is essential to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and safe experience for the camper.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Contact Phone, Email, Camper Type, Start Date, End Date, and Emergency Contact.
2. If necessary, fill out the optional fields: Date of Birth, Address, Emergency Contact, Emergency Relationship, Emergency Phone, Emergency Address, and Emergency Email.
3. Select the Camper Status as either Currently registered, New Camper, or Re-registering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the camper's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the camper's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (optional).
* **Address**: Enter the camper's address (optional).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the camper's contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the camper's email address.
* **Camper Type**: Select the type of camper: Day Camper or Overnight Camper.
* **Start Date**: Enter the camper's start date.
* **End Date**: Enter the camper's end date.
* **Camper Status**: Select the camper's status: Currently registered, New Camper, or Re-registering.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Select the relationship between the camper and the emergency contact: Parent, Guardian, or Sibling.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address (optional).
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the camp administration.
* In case of an emergency, please make sure to provide up-to-date contact information.
