# Kids Baking Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register children for a fun and educational baking camp, allowing them to learn new recipes, make new friends, and develop their baking skills in a safe and supportive environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the desired camp session from the "Select Camp Session" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the camper's name in the "Camper Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the camper's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the camper's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the camper's address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the camper's birth date in the "Birth Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. List any emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
9. If applicable, list any known medical conditions in the "Medical Condition" field.
10. List any dietary restrictions the camper has, such as food allergies or preferences, in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Camp Session**: Choose the desired camp session you would like your child to attend.
	+ This field allows you to select which camp session you would like your child to participate in.
* **Camper Name**: Enter the name of the child registering for the camp.
	+ Please enter the name of the child as it appears on their official documents.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can contact the parent or guardian with any important information.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
	+ We will use this email to send you updates and information about the camp.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
	+ We will use this number to contact you with any important updates or questions.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the parent or guardian.
	+ This field is used to ensure we can send any important information or packages to the correct address.
* **Birth Date**: Enter the child's birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ This field is used to verify the child's age and ensure they meet the camp's requirements.
* **Emergency Contact**: List any emergency contact information for the child.
	+ This field should include the name and contact information of a responsible adult who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition**: List any known medical conditions the child has.
	+ Please include any allergies, conditions, or other health concerns that affect the child's participation in the camp.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions the child has, such as food allergies or preferences.
	+ Please include any food allergies, vegetarian or vegan dietary restrictions, or other dietary concerns.
