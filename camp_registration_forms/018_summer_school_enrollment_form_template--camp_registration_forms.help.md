# summer-school-enrollment-form-template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for Summer School Enrollment, which allows parents to register their student for Summer School programs. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from parents to facilitate a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are filling out the correct information for your child.
2. Enter your Parent's information, including name and phone number.
3. Enter your child's name.
4. Select the appropriate grade level for your child.
5. Make sure to review your information carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Info (Page 1)**: This page is used to collect information about the parent or guardian responsible for enrolling the student.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is enrolling the student. This field is used to identify the parent's name.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian. This field is used to facilitate communication.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name who is enrolling in Summer School.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the current grade level of the student. This field is used to ensure correct placement in the appropriate course.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Summer School administration.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
