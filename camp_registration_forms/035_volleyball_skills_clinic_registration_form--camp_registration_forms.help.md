# Volleyball Skills Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Skills Clinic Registration Form is designed to register players for a volleyball skills clinic. This form gathers information from parents or coaches to ensure that the player's needs and preferences are met during the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred session times from the "Session Preferences" page.
2. Fill in the player's details, including name, birthdate, and other relevant information.
3. Provide emergency contact information for the player, including name, phone number, and email.
4. Answer the coach consent question, indicating whether you are the coach or have permission from the coach.
5. Repeat the process for the second player details page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred session time for the clinic. Select one or more options: Morning, Afternoon, or Full day.
* **Player Details** (`Player Details`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the player's name, birthdate, and other relevant information for the clinic.
* **Player Name** (`Player Name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the player's name.
* **Birthdate** (`Birthdate`, `date`, required: false): Enter the player's birthdate.
* **Parent Consent** (`Parent Consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "I give permission" if you are the parent and are giving permission for the player's participation in the clinic.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`Emergency Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide emergency contact information for the player, including phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Player** (`Relationship to Player`, `text`, required: false): Specify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the player.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact email.
* **Coach Consent** (`Coach Consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "I give permission" if you are the coach or have permission from the coach.
* **Coach Contact Info** (`Coach Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide coach contact information if you are not the coach.
* **Coach Consent Hint** (`Coach Consent Hint`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing coach contact information if you are not the coach.
* **Session Preferences 2** (`Session Preferences 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred session time for the second player.
* **Player Details 2** (`Player Details 2`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the second player's name, birthdate, and other relevant information.
* **Player Name 2** (`Player Name 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide the second player's name.
* **Birthdate 2** (`Birthdate 2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the second player's birthdate.
* **Parent Consent 2** (`Parent Consent 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "I give permission" if you are the parent and are giving permission for the second player's participation in the clinic.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (`Emergency Contact Info 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide emergency contact information for the second player, including phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (`Emergency Contact Name 2`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the name of the emergency contact person for the second player.
* **Relationship to Player 2** (`Relationship to Player 2`, `text`, required: false): Specify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the second player.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`Emergency Contact Phone 2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact phone number for the second player.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`Emergency Contact Email 2`, `email`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact email for the second player.
* **Coach Consent 2** (`Coach Consent 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "I give permission" if you are the coach or have permission from the coach for the second player.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide accurate information.
* Use the same format and layout for both players if filling out the form for multiple players.
