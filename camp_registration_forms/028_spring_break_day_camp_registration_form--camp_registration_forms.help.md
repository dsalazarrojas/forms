# spring_break_day_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spring break day camp registration form is used to register campers for the spring break day camp event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select your payment method (cash, bank transfer, or credit card).
3. Enter the payment amount.
4. If necessary, upload a file and provide camper information or add any notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name (optional).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment method** (`fee_payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method (cash, bank transfer, or credit card).
* **Payment amount** (`fee_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **File upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, optional): If necessary, upload a file related to your camper info or camp registration.
* **Camper info** (`camper_info`, `note`, optional): If necessary, add any additional camper information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are eligible to register before completing this form.
* Double-check your payment method and amount to avoid any issues with payment processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
