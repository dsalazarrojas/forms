# fresno_grizzlies_baseball_camp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresno Grizzlies Baseball Camp is a program for children to learn and play baseball. This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for their child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to understand the requirements.
2. For the "Parent Given Form" field (form_1), select 'Yes' if the camp staff will be providing care and supervision for your child, or 'No' if the parent or guardian will be present during the camp activities.
3. In the "Medical History" field (form_2), provide any medical conditions that may affect your child's ability to participate in the camp activities.
4. For the "Medical Insurance" field (form_3), indicate whether you have medical insurance coverage for your child.
5. Select the primary emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact" field (form_4).
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Home, Work, Other) in the "Contact Information" field (form_5).
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field (form_6).
8. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field (form_7).
9. Indicate whether you have signed the waiver for your child's participation in the camp activities (form_8).

* **Parent Given Form** (form_1, select_multiple):
