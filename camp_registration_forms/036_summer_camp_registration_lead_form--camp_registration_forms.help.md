# Summer Camp Registration Lead Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Registration Lead Form is used to collect essential information from parents registering their children for summer camps. This form helps in understanding the parent's contact details, child's name and age, and program preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by entering the required details:
   - Enter the parent's first name under "Parent's First Name".
   - Enter the parent's last name under "Parent's Last Name".
   - Enter your contact number under "Contact Number" for easy communication.
   - Enter a valid email address under "Email" to stay up-to-date with camp news.
   - Provide the child's name under "Child's Name".

2. Continue with the demographic details:
   - Enter the child's age under "Child's Age" to ensure they fit the camp's age criteria.
   - Choose the preferred camp program under "Camp Program" to align with your needs.
   - Select the start date of the session under "Session Start Date" to ensure you don't miss the first day.
   - Choose the session duration under "Session Duration" for a more comfortable time frame.

3. Schedule a follow-up:
   - Select a follow-up method under "Follow-up Method" to receive updates on your child's progress.
   - Choose the follow-up frequency under "Follow-up Frequency" to stay updated on a schedule that suits you.

4. Make a payment plan:
   - Select a payment method under "Payment Method" to secure your spot.
   - Choose a payment term under "Payment Terms" to fit your financial plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent's First Name
* **Parent's First Name** (`parent_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the parent.
### Parent's Last Name
* **Parent's Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the parent.
### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid contact number for easy communication.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to stay up-to-date with camp news.
### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address for camp visits or other communication purposes.
### Child's Name
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the child registering for the summer camp.
### Child's Age
* **Child's Age** (`child_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the child to fit the camp's age criteria.
### Camp Program
* **Camp Program** (`camp_program`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred camp program for your child.
### Session Start Date
* **Session Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the session to avoid missing the first day.
### Session Duration
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `time`, required): Choose the session duration for a more comfortable time frame.
### Follow-up
* **Schedule a Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required): Select a follow-up method to stay updated on your child's progress.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or details about your child's needs.
### Program Type
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of program your child is interested in.
### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment term that suits your financial plan.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose a payment method to secure your spot.
### Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you registered for the camp.
### Follow-up Method
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_one`, required): Select a follow-up method for updates on your child's progress.
### Follow-up Frequency
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose a follow-up frequency to stay updated at a schedule that suits you.

## Tips
* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure all details are accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the camp's age criteria for the child's age before selecting the camp program.
* Choose a follow-up method that suits your needs for updates on your child's progress.
