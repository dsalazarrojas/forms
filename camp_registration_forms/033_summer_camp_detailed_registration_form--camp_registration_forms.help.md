# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants to a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant email" field.
3. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant phone" field.
4. Enter the start date of the camp in the "Start date" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Enter the end date of the camp in the "End date" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Enter the start time of the camp in the "Start time" field in HH:MM AM/PM format.
7. Enter the end time of the camp in the "End time" field in HH:MM AM/PM format.
8. Enter the registration date in the "Registration date" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter camp name**: This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Participant name**: This field is required and should be filled in with the participant's name.
* **Participant email**: This field is required and should be filled in with the participant's email address.
* **Participant phone**: This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Start date**: This field is optional and should be filled in with the start date of the camp in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **End date**: This field is optional and should be filled in with the end date of the camp in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start time**: This field is optional and should be filled in with the start time of the camp in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **End time**: This field is optional and should be filled in with the end time of the camp in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Registration date**: This field is not required and cannot be left blank. If you are using this form for the first time, please fill in the current date.
