# outdoor-adventure-camp-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants to ensure their safety and well-being during the outdoor adventure camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Email address, which will be used for communication with you during the camp.
3. Enter your Birth Date.
4. Enter the name and details of your emergency contact.
5. Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the camp.
6. Select any medical conditions that apply to you from the list provided.
7. Provide details about any medical treatment you are currently undergoing.
8. Select the relationship type of your emergency contact.
9. Provide details about your emergency contact relationship.
10. Indicate whether you have first aid certification.
11. Provide details about any first aid training you have received.
12. Select which activities you will be participating in during the camp.
13. Provide details about any medical treatment conditions that apply to you.
14. Provide details about your medical insurance.
15. Provide details about your medical insurance provider.
16. Enter your medical insurance ID.
17. Provide details about your medical insurance plan.
18. If you have any previous injury, please describe it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use for communication.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the camp.
* **Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions_options`, select_one, optional): Select from the list of medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Participant Medical Treatment** (`participant_medical_treatment`, text, optional): Provide details about any medical treatment you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (`participant_medical_treatment_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your medical treatment.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationshiptype`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship type of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Details** (`emergency_contact_relationsship`, text, optional): Provide details about your emergency contact relationship.
* **First Aid Certification** (`first_aid_certification`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have first aid certification.
* **First Aid Training** (`first_aid_training`, text, optional): Provide details about any first aid training you have received.
* **Participant Activities** (`participant_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select which activities you will be participating in during the camp.
* **Activities** (`participant_activities_options`, select_one, optional): Select from the list of activities that apply to you.
* **Participant Medical Treatment Conditions** (`participant_medical_treatment_conditions`, text, optional): Provide details about any medical treatment conditions that apply to you.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (`participant_medical_treatment_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your medical treatment.
* **First Aid Instruction** (`first_aid_instruction`, text, optional): Provide details about first aid instruction you have received.
* **First Aid Training Instruction Details** (`first_aid_training_instruction_details`, text, optional): Provide details about first aid training instruction details.
* **Participant Medical Insurance** (`participant_medical_insurance`, text, optional): Provide details about your medical insurance.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`participant_medical_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Provide details about your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance ID** (`participant_medical_insurance_id`, text, optional): Enter your medical insurance ID.
* **Medical Insurance Plan** (`participant_medical_insurance_plan`, text, optional): Provide details about your medical insurance plan.
* **Previous Injury** (`participant_previous_injury`, text, optional): If you have any previous injury, please describe it.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the safety and well-being of all participants during the camp. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
