# Academic Cultural Enrichment ACE and IAT Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from parents or guardians to register their child for cultural enrichment and interactive technology programs, such as afterschool and summer camps. It's essential to provide accurate and thorough information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
*   Review each field carefully and ensure that you understand what information is being asked.
*   Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Parent or Guardian Information**
    • **Parent or Guardian Information (3)** (text, required): This field is for you or the parent/guardian's first name.
    • **Parent or Guardian Information (4)** (text, required): This field is for the last name of the parent/guardian.
    • **Parent or Guardian Email (5)** (email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
    • **Parent or Guardian Phone Number (6)** (text, required): This field is for your phone number.
    • **Home Address (7)** (text, required): Enter your home address, including your street, city, state, and zip code.
    • **Parent or Guardian Signature Acknowledgment (25)** (text, required): Please type your full name as your electronic signature, confirming the accuracy of the information provided and agreeing to the program terms.

### 2. **Child Information**
    • **Child Information (8)** (note, optional): This is a section header for the child's information.
    • **Child First Name (9)** (text, required): This field is for the child's first name.
    • **Child Last Name (10)** (text, required): This field is for the child's last name.
    • **Child Date of Birth (11)** (date, required): Enter the child's date of birth.
    • **Child Age (12)** (number, required): Enter the child's age.
    • **Current Grade Level (13)** (select_one, required): Select the child's current grade level from the provided options.
    • **School Name (14)** (text, required): Enter the name of the school the child currently attends.

### 3. **Program Selection**
    • **Program Selection (15)** (select_one, required): Choose which program your child is registering for, including Afterschool Program, Summer Camp, or Both Afterschool and Summer Camp.

### 4. **Health and Emergency Information**
    • **Health and Emergency Information (16)** (note, optional): This is a section header for the health and emergency information.
    • **Does Your Child Have Any Allergies? (17)** (text, required): List any known allergies or type "None" if none exist.
    • **Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions? (18)** (text, required): List any medical conditions or type "None" if none exist.
    • **Is Your Child Taking Any Medications? (19)** (text, required): List any medications and their dosages or type "None" if none exist.
    • **Emergency Contact Information (20)** (text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name (other than the parent or guardian).
    • **Emergency Contact Phone (21)** (text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
    • **Emergency Contact Relationship (22)** (text, required): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
    • **Authorized Pick-up Information (23)** (text, required): List names and relationships of individuals authorized to pick up your child.
    • **Photo Consent (24)** (select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to your child's photo being taken for program activities and publications.

**Tips**

*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure your child's successful participation in the program.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being asked.
*   Ask for help if you're unsure about any field or require assistance with the registration process.
