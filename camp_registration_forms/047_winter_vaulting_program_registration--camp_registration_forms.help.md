# Winter Vaulting Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide information about themselves and their participation in the Winter Vaulting Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required information in each field.
2. If you encounter any issues, please contact the form administrators for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`): Enter any relevant medical information, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Waiver Info** (`waiver_info`): Enter any waiver or release information required for your participation.
* **Emergency Info** (`emergency_info`): Enter emergency contact information and any relevant details.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter any additional details about yourself as a participant.
* **Horse Details** (`horse_details`): Enter any information about the horse you will be riding.
* **Session Info** (`session_info`): Enter information about the sessions you will be participating in.
* **Program Info** (`program_info`): Enter any information about the program or event you are participating in.
* **Waiver Acceptance** (`waiver_acceptance`): Enter your waiver acceptance, if required.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Enter payment information, if applicable.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): Confirm your registration and details.

## Tips
1. Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and thoroughly.
2. If you need to make any changes, please contact the form administrators for support.
3. Please review the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and complete.
