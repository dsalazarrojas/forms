# Football Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Football Registration Form, used to collect and record essential details about players for the football team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, including player name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.
2. Answer the "Player Position" and "Player Skills" questions accurately, as they are crucial for team management and strategy.
3. Select the correct "Team Experience" level to reflect the player's experience on the team.
4. Choose the correct "Player Diet" preference to ensure players' dietary needs are met during team events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Player Information** (`new_player_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief introduction or background information about the player, such as their background, interests, or previous football experience.
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the player, including their playing style or any relevant skills.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_one, required: false):
	+ If you're a new player, choose 'Yes' if you're familiar with the position or have experience playing it.
	+ If you're a coach, select the position for the player you're registering.
* **Team Information** (`team_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant team information, such as the team's name, coach, or practice schedule.
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, required: false): Add any additional player information, such as their strengths or weaknesses.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter the player's full name.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required: false): Enter the player's email address.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Team Experience** (`team_experience`, note, required: false): Describe the player's experience level on the team, using a brief narrative or checklist.
* **Player Skills** (`player_skills`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all skills the player possesses, such as passing, shooting, or defense.
	+ Be honest about your skills and experience.
* **Team Affiliations** (`team_affiliations`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all teams the player has affiliated with, such as local clubs or previous teams.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the emergency contact person and their relationship to the player.
* **Player Diet** (`player_diet`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the player's dietary preference, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

Note: This guide is not exhaustive, and additional information may be required based on the team's specific policies and procedures. If you're unsure about any field, please consult the team administrators or coaches for guidance.
