# Summer Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Sports Registration Form is a mandatory form for individuals to register for a summer sports program. It collects personal, health, sports-specific, and contact information from participants to place them into suitable groups.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section with your name and any other requested details.
2. Select your age group to ensure you are participating in the correct group.
3. Provide any necessary health information to help us understand your needs.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please list them.
5. Choose your level of sports experience to help us place you in the right group.
6. Select your primary sport of interest (e.g., basketball, soccer, etc.).
7. Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
8. Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. personal_info
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. age
* **Age** (Number, Required): Select your age group from the dropdown.
### 3. health_info
* **Health Information** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant health information or allergies we should be aware of.
### 4. medical_condition
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, list them here.
### 5. sports_experience
* **Sports Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your level of experience in sports, e.g., beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
### 6. sports_level
* **Sports Level** (Select One, Required): Choose your level of sports experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
### 7. sports_familiarity
* **Sports Familiarity** (Select One, Required): Select your primary sport(s) of interest:
	+ Basketball
	+ Soccer
	+ Hockey
	+ Volleyball
	+ Tennis
	+ None
### 8. contact_info
* **Contact Info** (Text, Required): Enter your email address or phone number so we can reach out to you.
### 9. additional_info
* **Additional Info** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your participation.

# Tips

* Please ensure you answer all required fields to ensure we can process your registration correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
