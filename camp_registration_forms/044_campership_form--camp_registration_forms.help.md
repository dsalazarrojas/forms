# Campership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campership Form is designed to help families in need cover the cost of camp tuition. To be eligible, families must meet specific financial requirements and provide accurate information about their situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form with the required information for each section.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please describe your situation in the space provided.
4. Make sure to sign the form with your full name before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the child or children you are applying for.
- **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the child or children you are applying for.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the birth date of the child or children you are applying for.
- **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the current age of the child or children you are applying for.
- **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the child's or children's gender.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_1_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian who will be completing this form.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Email** (`parent_guardian_1_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who will be completing this form.
- **Parent or Guardian 1 Phone** (`parent_guardian_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian who will be completing this form.
- **Parent or Guardian 2 Full Name** (`parent_guardian_2_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the full name of the second parent or guardian.
- **Parent or Guardian 2 Phone** (`parent_guardian_2_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the second parent or guardian.
- **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of your home.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town where you reside.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
- **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
- **Number of Children in Family** (`number_of_children_in_family`, number, required): Enter the total number of children in your family.
- **Number of Children Attending Camp** (`number_attending_camp`, number, required): Enter how many children will be attending the camp.
- **Annual Household Income** (`household_income`, select_one, required): Select the range of your household income.
- **Do You Receive Financial Assistance** (`financial_assistance_programs`, select_multiple, required): Select all financial assistance programs you or your family receive.
- **Employment Status Parent or Guardian 1** (`employment_status_parent_1`, select_one, required): Select the employment status of the first parent or guardian.
- **Please Explain Your Financial Hardship** (`financial_hardship_explanation`, text, required): Describe your current financial situation.
- **Why Do You Need Financial Assistance?** (`reason_for_needing_assistance`, select_multiple, required): Select all reasons why you need financial assistance.
- **Amount of Assistance Needed** (`amount_of_assistance_needed`, select_one, required): Select the percentage of camp fee you are requesting assistance for.
- **Camp Session Requested** (`camp_session_requested`, select_one, required): Select the camp session you are requesting.
- **Have You Received Campership Before?** (`previous_campership_received`, select_one, required): Select whether you have received a campership before.
- **How Would Attending Camp Benefit Your Child?** (`how_would_camp_benefit_child`, text, required): Describe how attending the camp will benefit your child.
- **Have You Applied for Other Scholarships?** (`other_scholarships_applied`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have applied for other scholarships.
- **Can You Provide References?** (`references`, select_one, optional): Select whether you can provide references upon request.
- **Are You Interested in Volunteering in Exchange?** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are interested in volunteering in exchange for assistance.
- **Would a Payment Plan Be Helpful?** (`payment_plan_interest`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are interested in a payment plan for assistance.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your full name.
- **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please describe your situation in the space provided.
- Make sure to complete the form with your full name before submitting.
- If you have any additional information you would like to share, please use the "Additional Information" field.
