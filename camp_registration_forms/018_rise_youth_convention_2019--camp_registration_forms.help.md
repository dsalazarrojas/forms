# rise_youth_convention_2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rise Youth Convention 2019" registration form is designed to collect general information, registration type, and other details from attendees. This form is a helpful tool for organizers to gather relevant information and manage the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing general information in page 1, "General Information".
2.  In page 2, select your desired "Registration Type" from the provided options.
3.  In page 3, choose your preferred "T-Shirt Size" and "T-Shirt Type".
4.  In page 4, select your preferred "Payment Method" and provide additional payment details.
5.  In page 5, indicate if you are granting "Parent Release" and select the relevant option.
6.  In page 6, provide your "Leader Email" and "Leader Name" details.
7.  In page 7, provide a "Personalized Email" if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`page: 1`, `type: text`, required: `false`): This field collects general information about the attendee.
* **Registration Type** (`page: 2`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select the type of registration you're interested in.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`page: 3`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): Choose your preferred T-Shirt size.
* **T-Shirt Type** (`page: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the type of T-Shirt you'd like to wear.
* **Payment Method** (`page: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Details** (`page: 4`, `type: text`, required: `false`): Provide additional details for your payment method.
* **Parent Release** (`page: 5`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): If you are a parent, indicate if you are granting permission for your child to participate.
* **Leader Email** (`page: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: `false`): If you are a leader, provide your email address.
* **Leader Name** (`page: 6`, `type: text`, required: `false`): If you are a leader, provide your name.
* **Personalized Email** (`page: 7`, `type: text`, required: `false`): If you need to provide a personalized email, type it here.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields marked as required to ensure accurate registration processing.
* Be accurate when providing your contact information to avoid delays or issues with payment processing.
