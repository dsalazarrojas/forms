# CJH Invoice Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CJH Invoice Generator form is designed to create a customized invoice for camps or events. It captures essential details about the billing party, invoice specifics, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Billing to field with the name of the person or organization you are invoicing.
2. Enter the billing address in the Billing address field.
3. Select the invoice date using the Invoice date field.
4. Provide a unique invoice number in the Invoice number field.
5. Describe the item being invoiced in the Item description field.
6. Specify the quantity of the item being invoiced in the Quantity field.
7. Enter the unit price for the item in the Unit price field.
8. If you want to compute the total amount, the form will automatically calculate it based on the quantity and unit price. You can also manually enter the total amount in the Total amount field.
9. If needed, specify the payment terms in the Payment terms field.
10. Enter the contact phone number in the Contact phone field for payment questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing to**: <label> (Billing to, required): Enter the name of the person or organization to be billed.
* **Billing address**: <label> (Billing address, optional): Enter the street address and city of the billing party.
* **Invoice date**: <label> (Invoice date, required): Enter the date when the invoice is issued.
* **Invoice number**: <label> (Invoice number, required): Enter a unique internal invoice reference.
* **Item description**: <label> (Item description, required): Describe the item being invoiced in a few words.
* **Quantity**: <label> (Quantity, required): Enter the number of units or attendees being invoiced.
* **Unit price**: <label> (Unit price, required): Enter the price per unit in local currency.
* **Total amount**: <label> (Total amount, required): The form will automatically calculate this field based on quantity and unit price, or you can enter it manually.
* **Payment terms**: <label> (Payment terms, optional): Specify the payment terms, e.g., Net 30 or due on receipt.
* **Contact phone**: <label> (Contact phone, optional): Enter the phone number for payment questions or inquiries.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to generate the correct invoice.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, contact your billing administrator.
