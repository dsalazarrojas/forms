# Youth Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Retreat Registration Form is used to collect essential information from participants and emergency contacts to ensure their safe and enjoyable experience during the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2. For each field, ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Save and submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required/optional): This section is for you to provide your name and any other identification information.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, select multiple, optional): Please select any emergency contacts that will be available during the retreat.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`Dietary Preferences`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you have any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the retreat.
* **Emergency Contacts Notes** (`Emergency Contacts Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your emergency contacts.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you during the retreat.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you quickly.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Assigned Staff** (`Assigned Staff`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you have any assigned staff members who will be looking after you during the retreat.
* **Label for Assigned Staff** (`Label for Assigned Staff`, text, optional): If you have an assigned staff member, enter their name or title here.
* **Emergency Staff** (`Emergency Staff`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you have any emergency contacts who will be available during the retreat.
* **Label for Emergency Staff** (`Label for Emergency Staff`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name or title here.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`Participant Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for us to keep in mind during the retreat.
* **Label for Participant Date of Birth** (`Label for Participant Date of Birth`, text, optional): Enter a label for your date of birth, e.g., 'Age Group'.
* **Participant Date of Birth Format** (`Participant Date of Birth Format`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you want your date of birth in the format 'DD/MM/YY' or 'MM/DD/YY'.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the retreat organizers or staff for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields are self-explanatory and that there is no need for further explanations other than those already provided. If you need additional support or have any doubts, please contact the retreat organizers or staff.
