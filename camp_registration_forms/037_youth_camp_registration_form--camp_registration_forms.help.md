# Youth Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Camp Registration Form is used to collect information from youth camp attendees, including their personal details, program preferences, and medical information. This form is necessary to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each camper, which helps us to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the programs that you would like to register for. Please choose one or more programs from the list below:
  - Program 1
  - Program 2
  - Program 3
2. Enter your name as it would be printed in a name tag.
3. Enter your age, as it will be used to ensure proper supervision and care during the camp.
4. Enter your valid email address, which will be used for communication.
5. Enter your phone number, which will be used in case of an emergency.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the space provided.
7. If you have any allergies, please list them in the space provided.
8. If you have any medical conditions or take any medications, please list them in the space provided.
9. Enter your emergency contact information, including their name and phone number.
10. Please provide any relevant medical information that we should be aware of.
11. Finally, confirm the details of your program selection, including dates and times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Details**: This is the first page of the form, where you will enter your basic details such as name, age, and contact information.
* **Select Programs**: This section allows you to choose which programs you would like to register for. Choose one or more programs from the list provided.
* **Camper Name**: Enter your name as it would be printed in a name tag.
* **Camper Age**: Enter your age, as it will be used to ensure proper supervision and care during the camp.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used in case of an emergency.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in this space.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them in this space.
* **Medications**: If you have any medical conditions or take any medications, please list them in this space.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Medical Info**: Please provide any relevant medical information that we should be aware of.
* **Program Details**: Confirm the details of your program selection, including dates and times.
* **Payment Terms**: This section is for administrative purposes only and should not be completed by the camper.
