# Summer Chess Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Chess Camp Registration Form is used to register participants for the summer chess camp program. This form collects essential information about the participant, including their name, contact information, and medical notes. The form also asks about the participant's age and special needs, if any. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all participants have a smooth and enjoyable experience at the camp.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Summer Chess Camp Registration Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information (participant_name, participant_email, and participant_phone).
2. Enter your age (participant_age).
3. Provide your parent's/guardian's name (participant_parental_name).
4. Choose your attendance status (participant_attendance).
5. If applicable, provide any special needs or medical information.
6. Add any important notes or comments (participant_important_notes_2).
