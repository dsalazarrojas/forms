# Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for campers and their parents to provide information necessary for a summer camp scholarship application. The form helps us evaluate the eligibility of applicants for our summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Camper Information, including the camper's name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your Parent Information, including your name and contact details.
3. Indicate your family's income level to help us understand your financial situation.
4. Explain the reason for your application.
5. Provide your contact phone number.
6. Enter your child's name.
7. Enter your child's date of birth.
8. Specify the relationship between the parent and the camper.
9. Select your child's grade level.
10. Choose your child's gender.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Information** (`camper_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's name and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Income Information** (`income`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your family's income level to help us understand your financial situation.
	+ Options: None, $25,001 - $35,000, $35,001 - $45,000, $45,001+, Undisclosed
* **Reason for Application** (`reason_for_app`, `text`, required: false): Explain why you are applying for a scholarship.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's name.
* **Camper's Date of Birth** (`camper_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Camper's Parents** (`camper_parents`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify the relationship between the parent and the camper.
	+ Options: Mother, Father, Both, Other
* **Camper's Grade** (`camper_grade`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your child's grade level.
	+ Options: 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, 4th Grade, 5th Grade, 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade, 9th Grade, 10th Grade
* **Camper's Gender** (`camper_gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your child's gender.
	+ Options: Female, Male, Other
