# Kids Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from parents and guardians for their child's summer camp registration. This form is used to gather demographic details, contact information, and sensitive information such as medical history and authorization for camp participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide all required information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the camp staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's last name.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Please enter your child's birth date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Please enter your child's current age.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select one, not required): Please select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the child (e.g., John, Jane, other).
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_contact_number`, text, not required): Please enter your contact number.
* **Parent E-mail** (`parent_email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, not required): Please enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, not required): Please enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, not required): Please enter the contact address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Please enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Child History** (`child_history`, text, not required): Please provide any relevant child history or background information.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, text, not required): Please provide any relevant medical information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, not required): Please sign and date this form to indicate your consent to participate.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, not required): Please select "I consent to the above information" to indicate your authorization for camp participation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Please add any additional comments you may have.
