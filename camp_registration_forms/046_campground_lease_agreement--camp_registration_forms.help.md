# Campground Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a lease agreement between the lessor and lessee for a campground lease. It ensures both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the lease, including pet policy, total rental fee, and payment schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the lessor's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Provide the lessee's information, including their name and contact details.
3. Select the pet policy that applies to the lease.
4. Enter the total rental fee for the lease period.
5. Choose the payment schedule that suits your needs.
6. If necessary, provide additional utilities terms.
7. Sign the agreement by signing your name as the lessor and lessee.
8. Date both signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Policy**: 
    - **Pets Allowed**: If you select this option, you are allowed to bring pets onto the property.
    - **Pets Allowed with Deposit**: If you select this option, you are allowed to bring pets onto the property, but you must pay a deposit.
    - **No Pets Allowed**: If you select this option, pets are not allowed onto the property.
    - **Service Animals Only**: If you select this option, only service animals are allowed onto the property.
* **Total Rental Fee**: Enter the total rental fee for the lease period.
* **Payment Schedule**: 
    - **Full Payment Due at Signing**: Pay the full rental fee at the start of the lease.
    - **50 Percent Deposit - Balance on Arrival**: Pay 50% of the rental fee at the start of the lease and the remaining balance when you arrive at the campground.
    - **Monthly Payments**: Pay the rental fee monthly.
    - **Other Arrangement**: Select this option if you have a custom payment schedule.
* **Is Insurance Required?**: Select whether insurance is required or not.
    - **Yes - Lessee must Provide**: The lessee is responsible for obtaining and maintaining insurance.
    - **No - Covered by Lessor**: The lessor is responsible for obtaining and maintaining insurance.
    - **Recommended but not Required**: Insurance is suggested but not mandatory.
* **Lessor Signature**, **Lessee Signature**, **Date Signed**, and **Lessee Signature Date**: Sign and date the agreement with your name as the lessor and lessee, respectively.
