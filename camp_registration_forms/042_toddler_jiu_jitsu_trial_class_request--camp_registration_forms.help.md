# Toddler Jiu Jitsu Trial Class Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents to request a trial class for their toddler to experience Jiu Jitsu at your organization. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
2. Provide your contact phone number.
3. Choose a preferred date for the trial class.
4. Select the start and end times of the trial class.
5. Indicate the desired class type (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday).
6. Choose the trial duration (30 minutes, 45 minutes, or 60 minutes).
7. Input your child's age.
8. Provide your consent for the trial class.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about your child's needs or expectations (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the trial class.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the trial class. Please note that we will try our best to accommodate your preferred date.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Choose the start time for the trial class.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Choose the end time for the trial class.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select multiple, required): Select one or more class days. Please choose from the following options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday.
* **Trial Duration** (`trial_duration`, select one, required): Select the duration of the trial class. Please choose from the following options: 30 minutes, 45 minutes, or 60 minutes.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Input your child's age.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, required): Provide your consent for the trial class. Please select 'True' if you agree to let your child attend the trial class.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, required): This field is required to confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms of the trial class. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your child's needs or expectations. This will help us to better understand your child's requirements and prepare the trial class accordingly.
