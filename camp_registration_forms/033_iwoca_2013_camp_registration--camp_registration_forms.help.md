# IWOCA 2013 Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the 2013 camp event. Please provide the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of camp you are registering for.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional).
4. Specify your purpose of attendance (optional).
5. Agree to the terms and conditions (optional).
6. Enter the details of the camp (optional).
7. Select the start and end dates of the camp (optional).
8. Enter any additional comments or information (optional).
