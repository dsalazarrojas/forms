# 2020 SLC Registration Form Camp John Hope - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect information from campers registering for the 2020 season at Camp John Hope.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please read this entire form carefully before filling it out.
* Fill in all required fields (marked as `required`).
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We'll use this for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Include your area code for US numbers.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your birthday in the format MM/DD.
* **Age** (`age`, `integer`, required): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your gender identity.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (if under 18)** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, optional): If you're under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `phone`, optional): Provide your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `phone`, required): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`relationship_to_emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Explain your relationship to your emergency contact person.
* **Any Medical Conditions or Allergies?** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Experience Level at Camp** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level at Camp John Hope.
* **Cabin Preference** (`cabin_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred age group cabin.
* **Activities Interested In** (`activities_interested_in`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any activities you'd like to participate in.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Food Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **How Did You Hear About Camp John Hope?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about the camp.
* **Scholarship Request** (`scholarship_request`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you need financial assistance.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations?** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): List any special needs or accommodations you require.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **I Agree to the Camp Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm you agree to the camp terms and conditions.
* **Signature (Parent/Guardian)** (`signature_parent_guardian`, `text`, required): Type your full name to sign the form.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Ensure you answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at [contact email].
