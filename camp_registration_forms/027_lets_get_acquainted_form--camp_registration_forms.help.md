# Lets Get Acquainted Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lets Get Acquainted" form is designed to gather essential information about each camper to ensure a safe and enjoyable summer camp experience. This information will be used to prepare camp administrators, counselors, and other staff members to cater to each camper's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you've gathered all necessary information about the camper.
2. Start with the first page, "Summer Camp Details", and fill in the required fields.
3. Proceed to the subsequent pages, providing as much information as possible for each section.
4. Be honest and thorough when listing camper allergies, diet restrictions, and any other relevant details.
5. Select only one option for camper organization and past experiences.
6. Complete any additional text fields, such as camper notes and counselor/administrator notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camp Details (<summer_camp_details>)
- **Camp Details**: This text field is for you to provide any additional information about the camp you're registering for.
 
### Camper Information (<camper_information>)
- **Camper Information**: Please provide any personal details about the camper in this text field.

### Camper Restrictions (<camper_restrictions>)
- **Camper Restrictions** (Select Multiple): If applicable, list any allergies the camper has in the dropdown options. Options include:
  • Allergies
  • Diet Restrictions
  • Other (please specify)

### Camper Story (<camper_story>)
- **Camper Story**: Share a brief story about the camper in this text field.

### Camper Fears (<camper_fears>)
- **Camper Fears**: In this text field, please list any fears or concerns the camper may have.

### Camper Hobbies (<camper_hobbies>)
- **Camper Hobbies** (Select One): Choose the primary hobby or interest of the camper from the provided options. Options include:
  • Swimming
  • Dancing
  • Sports
  • Arts
  • Music
  • Others

### Camper Past Experiences (<camper_past_experiences>)
- **Camper Past Experiences**: Select only one relevant past experience from the dropdown options.

### Camp Organization (<camp_organization>)
- **Camp Organization**: Choose the summer camp the camper is registered for from the provided options. Options include:
  • Summer Camp 1
  • Summer Camp 2
  • Summer Camp 3
  • Others

### Camper Notes (<camper_notes>)
- **Camper Notes**: In this text field, add any additional notes or comments about the camper.

### Camper Allergies (<camper_allergies>)
- **Camper Allergies**: Select only one allergy from the dropdown options. Options include:
  • Peanut allergy
  • Dairy allergy
  • Other

### Camper Diet Restrictions (<camper_diet_restrictions>)
- **Camper Diet Restrictions** (Select One): Choose the camper's diet restriction from the provided options. Options include:
  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan
  • Other

### Notes for Counselors (<notes_for_counselors>)
- **Notes for Counselors**: Add any essential information for the camp counselors in this text field.

### Notes for Administrator (<notes_for_administrator>)
- **Notes for Administrator**: Add any important notes for the camp administrators in this text field.

### Camper Phone (<camper_phone>)
- **Camper Phone**: Enter the camper's phone number in this text field.

Tips:
- Be thorough and honest when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the camp administrators or counselors.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you need help, contact the camp administrators or counselors for support.
