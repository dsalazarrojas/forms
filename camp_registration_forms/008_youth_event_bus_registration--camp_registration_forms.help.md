# Youth Event Bus Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Event Bus Registration form is designed to collect information from riders and their guardians for youth events. This form will be used by event organizers or administrators to manage registrations for these events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have permission to access this form.
2. Read through each field carefully and enter the required information.
3. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Rider Details
* Rider Details: This field asks for general information about the rider, including their name and contact details. This field is not required but is useful for event organizers to have a record of each rider.

### Rider Guardian
* Rider Guardian: Enter the contact information of the rider's guardian or parent. 

### Consents
* Rider Consents: Check the box to indicate the rider's consents, if applicable. This field is not required but can be useful for event organizers to know if the rider has given their consent.

### Contact Details
* Contact Details: Enter the contact information of the individual filling out the form. 

### Rider Guardian 1/2
* Rider Guardian 1: Enter the contact information of the first guardian or parent.
* Rider Guardian 2: Enter the contact information of the second guardian or parent.

### Additional Guardian 1/2
* Additional Guardian 1: Enter the contact information of any additional guardians or parents.
* Additional Guardian 2: Enter the contact information of any additional guardians or parents.

### Rider Ride Details
* Rider Ride Details: This field is intended for the rider to provide information about their ride details. There are 15 fields in this section, each one asking for a different ride detail. 

### Submit
* Submit: Click "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the form is complete and ready to be submitted. 

Please note that this form has a large number of fields for ride details, and it may be beneficial to review each field carefully to ensure accurate information is entered. 

Tips:
- Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is entered.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event organizer or administrator for clarification.
