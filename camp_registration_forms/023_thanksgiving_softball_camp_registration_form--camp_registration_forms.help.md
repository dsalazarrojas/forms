# thanksgiving_softball_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The thanksgiving_softball_camp_registration_form is used to register participants for a softball camp program during the Thanksgiving holiday season. This form collects essential information from users, including their personal details, contact information, and specific camp-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** (`user_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
2. Provide your **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
3. Enter your **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
5. Select your **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`): Choose your T-shirt size from the available options (S, M, L, XL, XXL, or Other).
6. Select your **Role** (`role`): Choose your role at the camp from the available options (Parent, Child, or Staff).
7. Enter your **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
8. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
9. Enter your **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
10. Enter your **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date of the registration (if applicable).
11. Select your **Camp Type** (`camp_type`): Choose the type of camp from the available options (Baseball, Softball, or Both).
12. Select your **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Choose your payment status from the available options (Paid, Pending, Unpaid, or Other).
13. Enter your **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or camp session.
14. Enter your **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or camp session.
15. Enter any additional **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or details relevant to your registration.
16. Select your **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`): Repeated selection from step 5.
17. Select your **Event Type** (`event_type`): Choose the type of event from the available options (Baseball, Softball, or Both).
18. Enter your **Camp Address** (`camp_address`): Enter the address of the camp location.
19. Enter your **Event Address** (`event_address`): Enter the address of the event location.
20. Enter your **Camp City** (`camp_city`): Enter the city of the camp location.
21. Enter your **Camp State** (`camp_state`): Enter the state of the camp location.
22. Enter your **Event City** (`event_city`): Enter the city of the event location.
23. Enter your **Event State** (`event_state`): Enter the state of the event location.
24. Enter your **Event ZIP** (`event_zip`): Enter the ZIP code of the event location.
