# Outdoor Education Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor education camp registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in working at or participating in the camp in a staff capacity. This form helps the camp administrators review and process registration requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Address).
2. Provide your emergency contact information (Emergency Contact First Name, Last Name, Relationship, Phone Number, and Email).
3. Choose your preferred camp session (Summer, Winter, or Both).
4. Select your desired camp duration (Week, Two Weeks, Three Weeks, or Four Weeks).
5. Input your start and end dates for the camp.
6. Choose your preferred start and end time for the camp.
7. Provide the title and description of the program you want to participate in.
8. Enter your staff details (Staff First Name, Staff Last Name, Staff Phone Number, and Staff Email).
9. Report any medical conditions or medical information.
10. Select any emergency services required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your home or work address where you can be reached.

### Emergency Contact First Name
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Last Name
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).

### Emergency Contact Phone Number
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

### Camp Session
* **Camp Session** (`camp_session`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the session you wish to participate in (Summer, Winter, or Both).

### Camp Duration
* **Camp Duration** (`camp_duration`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the duration of your participation (Week, Two Weeks, Three Weeks, or Four Weeks).

### Camp Start Date
* **Camp Start Date** (`camp_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your participation.

### Camp End Date
* **Camp End Date** (`camp_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your participation.

### Camp Start Time
* **Camp Start Time** (`camp_start_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of your participation.

### Camp End Time
* **Camp End Time** (`camp_end_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of your participation.

### Program Title
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the program you wish to participate in.

### Program Description
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the program you wish to participate in.

### Staff First Name
* **Staff First Name** (`staff_first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the first name of the staff member.

### Staff Last Name
* **Staff Last Name** (`staff_last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the last name of the staff member.

### Staff Phone Number
* **Staff Phone Number** (`staff_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the staff member.

### Staff Email
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the staff member.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, `note`, required/optional): Report any medical conditions or allergies.

### Medical Information
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional medical information.

### Emergency Services
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, `note`, required/optional): Select any emergency services required.

Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful registration process.
