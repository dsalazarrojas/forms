# summer_camp_pre_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer_camp_pre_registration_form is designed to collect camper information for a summer camp pre-registration process. The form is intended to gather various types of camper information, including personal details, medical history, guardian information, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page to view the available fields.
2. Fill in each field with the required information.
3. Ensure that you have filled in all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Details** (`camper_details`, text, optional): Please provide personal details about the camper, such as name, age, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact the camper's participation in the summer camp.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, text, optional): Please provide contact information for the camper's guardian or parent.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Please provide contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Medical History Form** (`medical_history_form`, text, optional): This field may be used to provide more detailed medical information.
* **Medical Form** (`medical_form`, text, optional): This field may be used to provide more detailed medical information.
* **Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`, text, optional): Please read and agree to the release of liability terms.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Please provide payment information, such as payment method and amount.
* **Refund Policy** (`refund_policy`, text, optional): Please read and understand the refund policy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the form contact: form@gic.mx.
