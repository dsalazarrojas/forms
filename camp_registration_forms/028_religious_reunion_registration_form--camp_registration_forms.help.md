# religious_reunion_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering attendees and collecting their associated information for a religious gathering or event. The form is intended for multiple events or sessions, and users will be filling out this form for each separate event or session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First name: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
	* Last name: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
	* Email: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number (optional).
2. RSVP and event details:
	* RSVP: Select your response to attending the event ('True', 'False', or 'Maybe').
	* Date: Select the date of the event (if required).
	* Time: Select the time of the event (if required).
	* Note: Add any additional information about the event (optional).
	* Attendees: Select the attendees you will bring (optional, multiple selections allowed).
	* Additional info about attendees: Add any additional information about your selected attendees (optional).
3. Repeat the same steps for each subsequent event or session, as indicated by the "1", "2", "3", etc. labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
- **RSVP**: Select your response to attending the event ('True', 'False', or 'Maybe').
- **Date**: Select the date of the event (if required).
- **Time**: Select the time of the event (if required).
- **Note**: Add any additional information about the event (optional).
- **Attendees**: Select the attendees you will bring (optional, multiple selections allowed).
- **Additional info about attendees**: Add any additional information about your selected attendees (optional).
- **RSVP 2, 3**: Repeat the RSVP field for subsequent events or sessions.
- **Date 2, 3**: Repeat the date field for subsequent events or sessions.
- **Time 2, 3**: Repeat the time field for subsequent events or sessions.
- **Note 2, 3**: Repeat the note field for subsequent events or sessions.
- **Attendees 2, 3**: Repeat the attendees field for subsequent events or sessions.
- **Additional info about attendees 2, 3**: Repeat the additional info about attendees field for subsequent events or sessions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for each event or session.
- Be accurate when selecting attendees and providing additional information.
- Review your form submissions before submitting to ensure everything is complete and correct.
