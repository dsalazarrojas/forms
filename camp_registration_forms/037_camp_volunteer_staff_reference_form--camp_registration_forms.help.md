# Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form is designed to collect information from referees about an applicant's reliability, suitability, and potential as a volunteer. This form helps to ensure that volunteer placements are made based on thorough and reliable assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read the instructions and field explanations carefully. Fill out the form completely and truthfully, providing as much information as possible. Make sure to answer all required fields and provide supporting details where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee Name** (`Referee Name`, required): Enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Referee Contact Info** (`Referee Contact Info`, required): Enter the contact information (phone or email) of the referee.
* **Applicant Name** (`Applicant Name`, required): Enter the name of the volunteer applicant.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`Relationship to Applicant`, required): Describe how you know the applicant (e.g., colleague, friend, family member).
* **Reliability Rating** (`Reliability Rating`, required): Select one of the following options to rate the applicant's reliability:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Suitability Comments** (`Suitability Comments`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why you think the applicant is suitable for volunteer work.
* **Any Concerns** (`Any Concerns`, optional): Note any reservations or concerns you may have about the applicant.
* **Recommendation** (`Recommendation`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Date of Reference** (`Date of Reference`, optional): Enter the date this reference was provided.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, optional): Provide any other relevant comments or information about the applicant.
