# College Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Camp Registration Form is used to collect essential information from registrants to process their registration for the college camp. This form requires users to provide their personal and academic details, including name, contact information, academic background, and medical history. The form also asks about their expectations from the camp and any accessibility needs they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are registering for the college camp and are familiar with the camp's policies and procedures.
2. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact information, and date of birth.
3. Select your field of study or major from the provided options.
4. Choose your year in school from the provided options.
5. Indicate your current GPA (if applicable).
6. Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact.
7. List any known medical conditions and medications you are taking.
8. Indicate any dietary restrictions you may have.
9. Select the events or workshops you plan to attend at the camp.
10. Choose your T-shirt size from the provided options.
11. Describe your expectations from the camp.
12. Indicate if you have attended the camp before.
13. Specify how you heard about the camp.
14. Indicate any accessibility needs you may have.
15. Confirm your agreement to the liability waiver and camp policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (registrant_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (registrant_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (registrant_email, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (registrant_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **College or University** (college_name, text, required): Enter the name of your current or prospective college or university.
* **Field of Study or Major** (field_of_study, text, required): Enter your field of study or major.
* **Year in School** (year_in_school, select_one, required): Select your current year in school.
* **Current GPA** (gpa, number, optional): Enter your current GPA on a 4.0 scale if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact_relation, text, required): Indicate your relationship to your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
* **Known Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions, text, optional): List any known medical conditions or allergies.
* **Current Medications** (medications, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions, select_multiple, required): Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, etc.).
* **Events or Workshops to Attend** (events_to_attend, select_multiple, required): Select all events or workshops you plan to attend at the camp.
* **Camp T-Shirt Size** (tshirt_size, select_one, required): Choose your T-shirt size.
* **Expectations from the Camp** (camp_expectations, text, required): Describe your expectations from the camp.
* **Have You Attended this Camp Before?** (previous_camp_attendance, select_one, required): Indicate if you have attended the camp before.
* **How Did You Hear about this Camp?** (referred_by, select_one, required): Specify how you heard about the camp.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (accessibility_needs, text, optional): Indicate any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Liability Agreement** (liability_agreement, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the liability waiver and camp policies.

## Tips
- Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the camp organizers for clarification.
- Complete the form carefully, as incomplete information may delay the processing of your registration.
