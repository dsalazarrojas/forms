# Camp Registration Form PayPal Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Form is designed for registering attendees or participants in various camp sessions while also facilitating payment through PayPal. This form gathers necessary details for both registration and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your full name for registration purposes.
2. Enter a valid email address for PayPal confirmation.
3. Select the session you wish to register for.
4. Enter the total amount due for registration.
5. Input your preferred PayPal email address for payment processing.
6. Enter any applicable coupon code for payment discounts.
7. Enter your billing address for payment record-keeping (optional but recommended).
8. Confirm your emergency contact information.
9. Agree to the camp's terms and conditions.
10. Add any additional notes or instructions for the camp staff.
11. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Full Name**: Enter your full name for registration and camp record-keeping purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that PayPal will use for confirmation and payment-related communications.
* **Session Selected**: Choose the session you wish to register for from the available options.
* **Amount Due**: Enter the total amount you will be paying for the selected session.
* **PayPal Email**: Enter your preferred email address associated with your PayPal account.
* **Coupon Code**: If you have a coupon code, enter it here to receive payment discounts. (Optional)
* **Billing Address**: Enter your address associated with payment (recommended for record-keeping purposes but not mandatory). 
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the camp's policies and terms and conditions.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any specific instructions or special requests for the camp staff. 
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Please choose 'True' to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the camp's policies and terms.

## Tips
- Be sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- Ensure that you have entered accurate and valid information for all fields.
- Use a valid email address for PayPal confirmation and payment purposes.
- If you have a coupon code, be sure to input it correctly for payment discounts.
- Emergency contact information should be up-to-date and relevant.
