# Online Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in attending a summer camp program. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact number in the "name" and "contact_number" fields respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your available dates for attending the camp in the "dates_available" field.
5. Select your preferred camps from the "selected_camp" field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the second camp preference question in the "selected_camp_2" field.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments about your application in the "notes" field.
8. Add any comments about your application in the "comments" field.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official government ID or documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Available Dates** (`dates_available`, date, required): Select the dates you are available to attend the camp.
* **Selected Camps** (`selected_camp`, select_multiple, required): Choose the camps you are interested in attending.
* **Second Camp Preference** (`selected_camp_2`, select_one, required): Choose your second preferred camp.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your application.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual who submitted the form (e.g., parent/guardian).

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional" but are recommended to be filled in for better communication with the summer camp team. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the summer camp team.
* Submission of incomplete or false information may lead to delays or cancellation of your application.
