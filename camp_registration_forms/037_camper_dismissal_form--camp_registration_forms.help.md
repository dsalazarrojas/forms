# Camper Dismissal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camper Dismissal Form is used to document a camper's early departure from the camp. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected to facilitate a smooth and safe transition for the camper.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2.  Select the appropriate reason for dismissal from the provided options.
3.  Provide detailed information about the dismissal reason if "Other" is selected.
4.  Enter the date and time of the camper's departure.
5.  Enter the name and relationship of the person picking up the camper.
6.  Enter the contact information of the person picking up the camper.
7.  Verify the identity of the person picking up the camper with their ID number.
8.  Confirm that the camper's belongings have been packed and that all property and medications have been returned.
9.  Indicate if there are any outstanding fees.
10.  Enter any additional comments or notes from the counselor.
11.  Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name**: Enter the first name of the camper.
* **Camper Last Name**: Enter the last name of the camper.
* **Camper Age**: Enter the current age of the camper.
* **Camp Session**: Select the camp session the camper is being dismissed from.
* **Cabin or Group Number** (if applicable): Enter the cabin or group number if applicable.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Dismissal Date**: Enter the date of the camper's departure.
* **Dismissal Time**: Enter the time of the camper's departure.
* **Reason for Dismissal**: Select the reason for dismissal from the provided options.
* **Dismissal Reason Details** (if "Other" is selected): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for dismissal if "Other" is selected.
* **Person Picking Up Camper**: Enter the full name of the person picking up the camper.
* **Relationship to Camper**: Enter the relationship to the camper (e.g., parent, guardian, sibling, etc.).
* **Pickup Person Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person picking up the camper.
* **Pickup Person ID Verification**: Enter the ID number of the person picking up the camper.
* **Is This Person Authorized for Pickup**: Select whether the person picking up the camper is authorized.
* **Transportation Method**: Select the method the camper will be using to leave the camp.
* **Have All Belongings Been Packed?**: Confirm that the camper's belongings have been packed.
* **Items Left Behind**: List any items left behind.
* **Camper Health Status**: Select the camper's health status.
* **Health Notes** (if applicable): Provide any health-related notes.
* **Have All Medications Been Returned?**: Confirm that all medications have been returned.
* **Has All Camp Property Been Returned?**: Confirm that all camp property has been returned.
* **Are There Any Outstanding Fees?**: Confirm if there are any outstanding fees.
* **Counselor Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes from the counselor.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign and date to confirm completion.
* **Staff Member Signature**: Sign and date to confirm release.
* **Actual Dismissal Time**: Enter the actual time of the camper's departure.
* **Date**: Enter the date of dismissal.
