# Youth Football Combine Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Football Combine Registration form is used to collect essential information about players participating in the youth football combine event. This form is designed to help us assess each player's skills and provide feedback to parents, coaches, and players.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the player's details section, which includes information such as date of birth, time, and height.
2.  Select the player's position in the sport.
3.  Provide contact information for emergency contacts, including a contact name, phone number, and email address.
4.  Add any additional notes or comments about the player.
5.  Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Date of Birth** (`date`, required): Please enter the player's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Time** (`time`, required): Please enter the player's preferred time for the event. 
*   **Height** (`select_one`, required): Select the player's height from the available options.
*   **Position** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all the positions the player can play.
*   **Emergency Contact 1** (`text`, optional): Please enter the name of the first emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact 2** (`text`, optional): Please enter the name of the second emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact 3** (`text`, optional): Please enter the name of the third emergency contact.
*   **Notes** (`text`, optional): Please add any additional comments or notes about the player.
