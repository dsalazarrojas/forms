# Baseball Scouting Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Scouting Showcase Registration Form is a comprehensive registration form designed to collect detailed information about players participating in the Baseball Scouting Showcase. This form will be used to gather essential data about each player's personal, athletic, and academic background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and fill out each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. For each field, follow the provided instructions and guidelines for formatting and submission.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the designated contact person or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (player_name): Enter your complete name in the format "First Name Last Name".
- **Player Email** (player_email): Enter your personal email address, which will be used for correspondence and updates.
- **High School Graduation Year** (graduation_year): Provide the year you graduated from high school.
- **High School Name** (high_school): Enter the name of the high school you attended.
- **Primary Position** (primary_position): Select your primary position (e.g., Pitcher, Catcher, First Base, etc.).
- **Secondary Position** (secondary_position): Select your secondary position (e.g., Pitcher, Catcher, Infield, etc.).
- **Height and Weight** (height_weight): Provide your height and weight in the format "Height (e.g., 6 foot 2 inches) and Weight (e.g., 190 lbs)".
- **Bats / Throws** (bats_throws): Select your batting and throwing hand (e.g., R/R, L/L, S/R, etc.).
- **Current GPA** (gpa): Enter your current GPA (if applicable).
- **SAT or ACT Scores** (sat_act_scores): Enter your SAT or ACT scores (if applicable).
- **Link to Highlights Video** (highlights_video_link): Provide a link to a YouTube, Vimeo, or recruiting profile video showcasing your skills.
- **Scouting Invitation Code** (scout_invitation_code): Enter any applicable scouting invitation code.
- **Showcase Jersey Size** (shirt_size): Select the size of the showcase jersey you will require.
- **Parent or Guardian Contact Name and Phone** (parent_contact_info): Enter your parent or guardian contact name and phone number.
- **Registration Date** (registration_date): Enter the date of your submission.
