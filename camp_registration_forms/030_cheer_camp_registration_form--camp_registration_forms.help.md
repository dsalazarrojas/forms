# Cheer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cheer Camp Registration Form is used to collect information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the camp. This form helps us understand your child's personal details, medical history, and contact information to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information**. Make sure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
2. **Review the form carefully** before submitting it, ensuring you have filled out all the necessary details.
3. **Submit the form** once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: <label> Participant Full Name <label> Enter your child's full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Age**: <label> Age <label> Enter your child's age in years.
* **Grade (Upcoming School Year)**: <label> Grade (Upcoming School Year) <label> Select the grade your child is currently in or will be in for the upcoming school year.
* **T-Shirt Size**: <label> T-Shirt Size <label> Choose your child's t-shirt size from the available options.
* **Parent / Guardian Name**: <label> Parent / Guardian Name <label> Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the participant.
* **Contact Email**: <label> Contact Email <label> Enter your email address where you can be reached regarding camp information.
* **Phone Number**: <label> Phone Number <label> Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact (Secondary)**: <label> Emergency Contact (Secondary) <label> Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: <label> Emergency Phone <label> Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: <label> Medical Conditions or Allergies <label> List any medical conditions or allergies your child may have.
* **Camp Session Date**: <label> Camp Session Date <label> Select the camp session date your child will be attending.
* **Registration Type**: <label> Registration Type <label> Choose the registration type that applies to your situation.
* **Photo and Video Release**: <label> Photo and Video Release <label> Choose whether or not you consent to photos and videos of your child being taken.
* **Liability Waiver and Release**: <label> Liability Waiver and Release <label> Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions of participation.
* **Digital Signature Acknowledgment**: <label> Digital Signature Acknowledgment <label> Type your full name to acknowledge the terms and conditions.

By following these instructions, you will be able to fill out the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child at the camp.
