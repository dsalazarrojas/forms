# Camp Fun Quest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Fun Quest Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from campers for a fun and exciting experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the camper's full name as it appears on their official records.
2. Enter the camper's date of birth.
3. Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number for emergency updates.
4. Enter the parent or guardian's email address for camp communications.
5. Select the preferred camp session.
6. Select the camper's T-shirt size (optional).
7. Enter any medical information, medications, or allergies.
8. Enter the names of people authorized to pick up the camper in case of an emergency.
9. Provide permission for the use of camper photos for camp media.
10. (Optional) Enter any special requests or accommodations needed during the camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Camper Full Name** (`camper_name`, `text`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter the camper's full name as it appears on their official records.
### 2. **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter the camper's date of birth.
### 3. **Parent or Guardian Contact Phone** (`parent_guardian_contact`, `text`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number for emergency updates.
### 4. **Parent or Guardian Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter the parent or guardian's email address for camp communications.
### 5. **Session Choice** (`session_choice`, `select_one`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can select the preferred camp session.
### 6. **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional)
* This field is optional, select the camper's T-shirt size.
### 7. **Medical Information and Allergies** (`medical_info`, `text`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter any medical information, medications, or allergies the camper may have.
### 8. **Emergency Pickup Authorization** (`emergency_pickup`, `text`, required)
* This field is a required field where you can enter the names of people authorized to pick up the camper in case of an emergency.
### 9. **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, `select_one`, required)
* This field is a required field where you provide permission for the use of camper photos for camp media.
### 10. **Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional)
* This field is optional, enter any special requests or accommodations needed during the camp.

Please note that some fields are required, and you must complete them before proceeding. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
