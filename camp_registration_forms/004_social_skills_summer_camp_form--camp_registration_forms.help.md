# Social Skills Summer Camp Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Skills Summer Camp Form is designed to gather information from parents and participants about their registration and participation in the summer camp. This form helps us collect essential details about the participant, their contact and medical information, emergency contact, social skills, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide information about the emergency contact person, in case we need to reach them in case of an emergency.
4. Share any medical information that may be relevant to the participant's health and well-being.
5. List the participant's social skills or any other relevant information that may be helpful for our staff.
6. Enter your parent or guardian's information, including their name and contact details.
7. Add any additional information that you believe would be helpful for us to know about the participant.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, `text`, required/optional): This field is the first page of the form where you will enter your name, email address, and other contact information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name, email address, and phone number of the emergency contact person, in case we need to reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required/optional): Share any medical information that may be relevant to the participant's health and well-being.
* **Social Skills** (`social_skills`, `text`, required/optional): List the participant's social skills or any other relevant information that may be helpful for our staff.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your parent or guardian's name and contact details.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information that you believe would be helpful for us to know about the participant.
* **Submit Info** (`submit_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not explicitly labeled, but it is the final step to submit the form and complete the registration process.
* **Camp Info** (`camp_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the camp, such as the session dates and times.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about the participant, such as their name, age, and any relevant health information.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter payment details, including the payment method and amount.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm the payment details after submitting the form.
