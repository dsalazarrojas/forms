# summer_camp_child_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for parents or guardians to register their child for a summer camp. It gathers essential information about the child, including personal details, medical history, emergency contacts, and any relevant medical allergies or conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's personal details, such as their name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Provide any medical history, including previous illnesses, conditions, or allergies that may be relevant to the child's safety at the camp.
3. List any emergency contacts, such as a parent or guardian who can be reached in case of an emergency.
4. If applicable, list any medical allergies or conditions that the child has, including any required medical treatment plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info**: Provide the child's name, date of birth, and contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Medical History**: Describe any previous illnesses, conditions, or treatments that may affect the child's health and well-being at the camp.
* **Emergency Contact**: List the name and relationship of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency (e.g., parent, guardian, family friend).
* **Medical Allergies**: List any medical allergies or conditions that the child has, including any required medical treatment plans.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect the child's health and well-being at the camp.
* **Medical Treatment Plan**: Describe any specific medical treatment plans that the child requires.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship of the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, family friend).
* **Camp Details**: Provide any additional information about the child's camp experience, such as their camper ID number or any specific needs or requirements.
