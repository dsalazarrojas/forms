# Camp Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Participant Registration Form is used to collect information from participants attending a camp program. This information helps us understand their needs, preferences, and medical conditions to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's full name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is a required field, so please enter a valid date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter the name of the primary contact person for the participant in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
4. Provide a contact phone number for the participant in the "Contact Phone" field. This number will be used for updates and notifications.
5. Select the session or program that the participant will be attending from the "Session Selected" dropdown menu.
6. If the participant has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the "Medical Conditions and Allergies" field.
7. Indicate the participant's swim ability in the "Swim Ability" dropdown menu, which is optional but helpful for water activities.
8. List the names and relationship of the authorized pick-up persons in the "Authorized Pick Up Persons" field.
9. Select the payment method for the registration fees from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu, which is also a required field.
10. If there are any special requests or accommodations, please mention them in the "Special Requests or Accommodations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (text, required): Fill in the participant's full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for the participant.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter a contact phone number for the participant.
* **Session Selected** (select_one, required): Select the session or program that the participant will be attending.
* **Medical Conditions and Allergies** (text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies the participant may have.
* **Swim Ability** (select_one, optional): Select the participant's swim ability level.
* **Authorized Pick Up Persons** (text, required): List the names and relationship of the authorized persons who can pick up the participant.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method for the registration fees.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (text, optional): Mention any special requests or accommodations the participant may need.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the participant's name, date of birth, and contact phone number are accurate to avoid duplication or errors.
* Select the correct swim ability level to ensure the participant's safety in water activities.
* Make sure to list any authorized pick-up persons with their relationship for easy contact.
* Choose a suitable payment method that suits your needs.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know so we can accommodate them accordingly.
