# Fitness Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Camp Registration Form is used to gather information about users registering for a fitness camp.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Select your Fitness Goals from the dropdown list.
5. Choose the available dates for the camp in the Date field. 
6. Select the time of day you plan to attend in the Time of Day field.
7. Choose your preferred contact method in the Contact Method field.
8. Enter any additional comments in the Comments field.
9. Enter your assigned tool or form ID in the Assigned Tool field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, Required: No): Enter your first name in this text field.
* **Last Name** (`text`, Required: No): Enter your last name in this text field.
* **Email** (`email`, Required: No): Enter your valid email address.
* **Fitness Goals** (`select_one`, Required: No): Select your fitness goals from the list:
	+ Maintain current fitness level
	+ Gain muscle mass
	+ Lose weight
	+ Improve cardiovascular health
* **Available Dates** (`date`, Required: No): Select the dates you are available to attend the camp.
* **Time of Day** (`time`, Required: No): Select the time of day you plan to attend in 24-hour format.
* **Contact Method** (`select_multiple`, Required: No): Select your preferred contact method:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In person
* **Comments** (`note`, Required: No): Enter any additional comments about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, Required: No): Enter your assigned tool or form ID.
* **Form Category** (`select_one`, Required: No): Select the form category from the list:
	+ Camp Registration Forms
	+ Program Registration Forms
	+ Test Form
