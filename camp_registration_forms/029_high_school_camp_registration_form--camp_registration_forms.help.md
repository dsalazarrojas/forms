# High School Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Camp Registration Form is a form used to register students for a high school camp. This form collects essential information from students, parents, and guardians to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's high school information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the student's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the parent's name, email, and phone number.
4. List any emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
5. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
6. Provide any necessary medical information, including allergies and medical notes.
7. Choose the student's dietary restrictions.
8. Enter payment information, including payment method and terms.
9. Specify the camp start and end dates.
10. Choose the payment method and specify any other payment terms.
11. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **High School Information**: Enter the student's high school information, including the name, email, and phone number.
  - **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
  - **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
  - **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
- **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
- **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
- **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
- **Parent Information**: Enter the parent or guardian's name, email, and phone number.
- **Parent Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's name.
- **Parent Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
- **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
- **Contact Name**: Enter the contact person's name.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Medical Information**: Provide any necessary medical information, including allergies and medical notes.
- **Allergy Information**: List any allergies or medical conditions.
- **Student Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes for the student.
- **Dietary Restrictions**: Choose the student's dietary restrictions, including:
  - Lactose intolerant
  - Vegetarian
  - Vegan
  - Gluten-free
  - Other
- **Payment Information**: Enter payment information, including the method and terms.
- **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method, including:
  - Cash
  - Check
  - Credit Card
- **Payment Terms**: Enter any other payment terms or instructions.
- **Camp Fees**: Enter the total camp fees.
- **Camp Start Date**: Enter the camp start date.
- **Camp End Date**: Enter the camp end date.
- **Time**: Enter the camp time.
- **Total Camp Days**: Enter the total number of camp days.
- **Total Students**: Enter the total number of students.
