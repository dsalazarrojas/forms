# Camp Leaders Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling the Camp Leaders Event Registration Form, covering each field's explanation and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading through the form carefully, understanding the type of event you're registering for (e.g., Leadership Summit, Counselor Skills Workshop, etc.). Ensure you have all necessary information ready before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by the `required` property in the YAML), and provide accurate information for each section.
3. For select fields, ensure you've chosen the most relevant option based on your situation (e.g., dietary needs, travel mode, workshop interests).
4. If unsure about any field, consider the context and available options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Leader Full Name
* **Leader Full Name (leader_full_name_event)** (`leader_full_name_event`, `text`, `required/true`): This field is for your full name, ensuring accurate identification for event organizers and participants.
### Email Address
* **Email Address (leader_email_event)** (`leader_email_event`, `email`, `required/true`): This field is for your email address, which will be used for communication with event organizers and other participants.
### Current Role - Position
* **Current Role - Position (leader_position_title_event)** (`leader_position_title_event`, `text`, `required/true`): Please enter your current camp position to help event organizers tailor the event content and arrangements appropriately.
### Select the Event You Are Attending
* **Select the Event You Are Attending (event_selection)** (`event_selection`, `select_one`, `required/true`): Choose the specific event you're attending from the given options (e.g., Leadership Summit 2026, Counselor Skills Workshop).
### Event Dates
* **Event Dates (event_session_dates)** (`event_session_dates`, `text`, `required/true`): Provide the dates you'll be attending the event.
### Dietary Requirements
* **Dietary Requirements (dietary_needs_leader_event)** (`dietary_needs_leader_event`, `select_multiple`, `required/true`): Select any dietary needs or preferences you may have (e.g., no restrictions, vegetarian, vegan).
### Travel Mode
* **Travel Mode (travel_arrangements_event)** (`travel_arrangements_event`, `select_one`, `required/true`): Choose your mode of travel (e.g., driving personal vehicle, carpooling, taking the camp shuttle, public transportation).
### Roommate Preference (If Applicable)
* **Roommate Preference (If Applicable) (lodging_preference_event)** (`lodging_preference_event`, `text`, `optional/true`): If you'd like to room with another leader, provide their name.
### Workshop Breakout Choices
* **Workshop Breakout Choices (workshop_interests_event)** (`workshop_interests_event`, `select_multiple`, `required/true`): Select up to three topics of interest for the workshops (e.g., conflict resolution, advanced outdoor leadership).
### Emergency Contact Name and Phone
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone (leader_emergency_contact_event)** (`leader_emergency_contact_event`, `text`, `required/true`): Provide the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of emergencies.
### Accessibility or Special Accommodations
* **Accessibility or Special Accommodations (special_accommodations_event)** (`special_accommodations_event`, `text`, `optional/true`): If you have any specific needs for participation, such as physical limitations or equipment requirements.
### Participation Agreement
* **Participation Agreement (attendance_participation_consent)** (`attendance_participation_consent`, `select_one`, `required/true`): Indicate your willingness to participate fully in all scheduled event activities.
### Date of Registration
* **Date of Registration (leader_event_registration_date)** (`leader_event_registration_date`, `date`, `required/true`): Please enter the date you submit this form.

Please review each field carefully, following the instructions above to ensure accurate and complete registration for the event. If you have any questions or concerns, refer back to the form's context and available options.
