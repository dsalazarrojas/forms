# Summer Camp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Waiver Form is for parents to provide information about themselves and their child. This information is used to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary information in the "Parent Information" page.
2. Enter the contact information for emergency situations on the "Contact Information" page.
3. List any medical conditions that may affect the child's health on the "Medical Conditions" page.
4. Describe any food or environmental allergies on the "Allergies" page.
5. List any medications the child is currently taking on the "Medications" page.
6. Specify any emergency procedures that should be followed in case of an injury or illness on the "Emergency Procedure" page.
7. Provide any necessary medical authorization on the "Medical Authorization" page.
8. Read and acknowledge the "Camp Policies" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Information
* **Parent Information (text)**: This is the first page of the form where you should provide information about yourself and your child. Please fill out this page completely and accurately.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (text)**: This page is for providing emergency contact information. Please enter the contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: This page is for providing information about who to contact in case of an emergency. Please make sure to include a phone number and address.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions (text)**: This page is for listing any medical conditions that may affect the child's health. Please be as detailed as possible.

### Allergies
* **Allergies (text)**: This page is for listing any food or environmental allergies that the child may have. Please make sure to include any necessary medical treatment or reactions.

### Medications
* **Medications (text)**: This page is for listing any medications that the child is currently taking. Please include the medication name, dosage, and any relevant medical history.

### Emergency Procedure
* **Emergency Procedure (text)**: This page is for specifying any emergency procedures that should be followed in case of an injury or illness. Please be as detailed as possible.

### Medical Authorization
* **Medical Authorization (text)**: This page is for providing any necessary medical authorization. Please include any relevant medical history or treatment plans.

### Camp Policies
* **Camp Policies (text)**: This page is for reading and acknowledging the camp's policies. Please make sure to understand and agree to the policies before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask the camp staff for clarification.
* The camp staff will use this information to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the summer camp.
