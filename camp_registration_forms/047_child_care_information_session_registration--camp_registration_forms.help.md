# Child Care Information Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians interested in registering for a child care information session. This information will help child care centers or agencies understand your needs and preferences for care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each page of the form carefully.
2. Enter your information in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. If you have any special needs or concerns, please describe them in the "Key questions or concerns about child care" field.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`parent_first_name`, text, \*): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`parent_last_name`, text, \*): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, \*): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the information session and other important updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, \*): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about the information session and other important updates.
* **Number of children** (`number_of_children`, number, \*): Enter the number of children interested in care.
* **Ages of children** (`child_ages`, text): Describe the ages of your children.
* **Age of oldest child** (`oldest_child_age`, select_one): Select the age of your oldest child from the options provided:
	+ Infant
	+ Toddler (1-3)
	+ Preschool (3-5)
	+ School age (6-12)
	+ Teenager
* **Age of youngest child** (`youngest_child_age`, select_one): Select the age of your youngest child from the options provided:
	+ Newborn
	+ Infant
	+ Toddler (1-3)
	+ Preschool (3-5)
	+ School age
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, \*): Select the date you are interested in attending the information session.
* **Session time** (`session_time`, select_one): Select your preferred session time from the options provided:
	+ Morning (9 AM - 12 PM)
	+ Afternoon (1 PM - 4 PM)
	+ Evening (5 PM - 7 PM)
	+ Weekend
* **Session format** (`session_format`, select_one): Select your preferred session format from the options provided:
	+ In-person tour
	+ Virtual session
	+ One-on-one meeting
	+ Group information session
	+ No preference
* **Facility tour** (`facility_visit`, select_one): Select whether you would like a facility tour:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Type of care interested in** (`care_type_interest`, select_multiple): Select all the types of care you are interested in:
	+ Full-time care
	+ Part-time care
	+ Drop-in care
	+ Infant care
	+ Preschool
	+ After-school care
	+ Summer camp
* **Required schedule or hours** (`schedule_needs`, text): If you have specific schedule or hour requirements, please describe them here.
* **Current child care arrangement** (`current_arrangement`, select_one): Select your current child care arrangement:
	+ Family care
	+ Home-based provider
	+ Center-based care
	+ Nanny
	+ No current care
	+ Other
* **Expected enrollment timeline** (`enrollment_timeline`, select_one): Select when you expect to enroll your child in care:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6+ months
	+ Unsure
* **Key questions or concerns about child care** (`key_questions`, text): If you have any key questions or concerns about child care, please describe them here.
* **Special needs or accommodations needed** (`special_needs`, text): If you or your child has any special needs or accommodations, please describe them here.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, select_one): Select how you heard about us:
	+ Website
	+ Facebook
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Referral from friend
	+ Google search
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
