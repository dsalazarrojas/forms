# Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Camp Registration Form is used to collect demographic information, contact details, and emergency contact information from campers and their families. Please fill out this form to register your child for the day camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Camper Info" section with the camper's name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide the contact information for the camper's emergency contact.
3. Select the days the camper will be attending camp.
4. Add any additional notes or medical conditions (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, optional): Enter the camper's name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_details`, email, optional): Enter the contact information for the camper's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required): Select the name of the emergency contact from the available options (e.g., Mother, Father, Other, Grandparent, Guardian, Stepparent).
* **Days Attending** (`camp_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days the camper will be attending the camp (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the camper.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, optional): Enter any medical conditions the camper may have that need to be disclosed for their safety.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the camper's age.
 
## Tips
Please ensure you've filled out the form accurately and truthfully, and make sure to include any necessary medical information or contact details for emergency purposes.
