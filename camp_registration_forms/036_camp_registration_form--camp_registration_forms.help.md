# Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from parents or guardians about their child's contact details, medical needs, and preferences for a summer camp or recreational activity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
- For each field, follow the instructions provided in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent or Guardian Contact Information
**-- Parent or Guardian Contact Information --** (Page 1)
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`primary_parent_name_reg_std`, `text`, required/required): This field collects the full name of the parent or guardian who is registering the child.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone_reg_std`, `text`, required/required): This field captures the primary contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address** (`primary_contact_email_reg_std`, `email`, required/required): This field gathers the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Secondary Contact Name and Phone** (`secondary_contact_info_reg_std`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for emergency contact information, if applicable.
### Camper Information
**-- Camper Information --** (Page 2)
* **Camper Full Name** (`camper_full_name_reg_std`, `text`, required/required): This field collects the full name of the child.
* **Date of Birth** (`camper_date_of_birth_reg_std`, `date`, required/required): This field captures the child's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`camper_gender_reg_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field selects the child's gender.
* **Grade Level (Next Fall)** (`grade_level_upcoming_reg_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field chooses the child's grade level.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field selects the child's T-shirt size.
### Camp Session and Needs
**-- Camp Session and Needs --** (Page 3)
* **Preferred Camp Session** (`camp_session_selection_reg_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field selects the preferred camp session.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Concerns** (`dietary_restrictions_std`, `text`, required/required): This field gathers any dietary restrictions or concerns the child may have.
* **Known Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions_std`, `text`, required/required): This field captures any known medical conditions or allergies the child may have.
* **Swimming Proficiency** (`swimming_proficiency_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field chooses the child's swimming proficiency level.
* **Buddy Request (Optional)** (`buddy_request_std`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for a buddy request, if applicable.
### Consent and Date of Registration
**-- Consent and Date of Registration --** (Page 4)
* **Liability Waiver and Participation Consent** (`liability_waiver_consent_std`, `select_one`, required/required): This field captures the parent or guardian's consent for the camp liability waiver and participation terms.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date_std`, `date`, required/required): This field captures the date of registration.
* **Parent - Guardian Electronic Signature** (`parent_digital_signature_std`, `text`, required/required): This field gathers the parent or guardian's electronic signature.

## Tips
- Please double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Review the camp terms and conditions before completing this form.
