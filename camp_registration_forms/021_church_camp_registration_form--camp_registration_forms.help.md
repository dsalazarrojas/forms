# Church Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to register their children for a church camp. Please fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully and completely.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the registration type that suits your child's needs.
4. Agree to the liability release and medical consent.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camper Information
* **Camper Full Name** (`camper_full_name`, text, \*): Enter the full name of the child you are registering.
* **Grade Entering (Next Fall)** (`grade_entering`, select_one, \*): Select the grade the child will be in during the next school year.
* **Date of Birth** (`camper_date_of_birth`, date, \*): Enter the child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Camper Gender** (`camper_gender`, select_one, \*): Select the child's gender.
* **Camper T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, \*): Choose the size of the t-shirt the child prefers.

### Parent/Guardian Information
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, \*): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, \*): Provide your email address for camp confirmation and packet delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`contact_phone_primary`, text, \*): Enter your primary phone number.

### Medical and Dietary
* **List any Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, text, ): List any food allergies the child has, such as peanuts or dairy.
* **Medical Conditions/Medications** (`medical_conditions`, text, ): Provide any medical conditions or medications the staff should be aware of.
* **Roommate Request** (`roommate_request`, text, ): If desired, suggest a roommate for the child.

### Registration and Liability
* **I Agree to the Liability Release and Medical Consent** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, \*): Select to agree to the terms of the liability release and medical consent.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_option`, select_one, \*): Choose the type of registration that suits your child's needs, such as Early Bird or Volunteer Child.

**Tips**
* Ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the camp administration.
