# Swim Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swim camp registration form is used to register swimmers for the swim camp event. This form is required for registration and should be filled out completely and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Choose your swimmer activities from the options provided.
3. Enter your birthdate accurately.
4. Check the swim camp box if you wish to attend.
5. Review your registration information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swimmer Information** (1, text, not required):
	+ Enter a brief description of yourself as a swimmer.
* **Registration Fees** (2, number, required):
	+ Enter the amount of money you are willing to pay for registration fees. Please enter a whole number.
* **Swimmer Activities** (3, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose all the swimming activities you are interested in participating in. You can select multiple activities.
	+ Options: Dive, Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke, Butterfly
* **Swimmer Contact** (4, text, not required):
	+ Enter any additional contact information for yourself as a swimmer.
* **Swim Camp** (5, select_one, not required):
	+ Check the box if you wish to attend the swim camp event.
* **Birthdate** (6, date, required):
	+ Enter your correct birthdate.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Review your registration information before submitting.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact the event organizers.
