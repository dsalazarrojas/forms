# Teen Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from teen summer camp attendees. It is a crucial part of the registration process for the camp season.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill in the form, please follow the steps below:

* Start by filling in the basic information section, which includes your first name, last name, age, address, parent's name, and contact number.
* Next, you will be asked to provide medical information, including any medical conditions, medical information, allergies, and medical equipment you may have.
* After that, you will be asked to select the camps you are interested in attending. Please select yes or no for each camp to indicate your interest.
* Calculate the total cost by filling in the total price field.
