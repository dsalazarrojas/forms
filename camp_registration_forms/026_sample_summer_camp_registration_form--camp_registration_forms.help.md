# Sample Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from campers and their guardians for the upcoming summer camp. The information collected will be used to create camper profiles and ensure that each camper has a fun and safe experience at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. You can submit this form multiple times if you are registering multiple campers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Details (<camper_details>)**: This is the primary contact information for the camper. Please enter the camper's name and any other relevant details.
* **Guardian Details (<guardian_details>)**: This field is for the parent or guardian's information. Please provide their name and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact (<emergency_contact>)**: This is the contact information for an emergency contact person. In case of an unexpected situation, we will reach out to this person.
* **Cabin Buddies (<cabin_buddies>)**: You can select up to three campers who will share a cabin with this camper. This will help us create a fun and social environment.
* **Consent (<consent>)**: This field is for any additional comments or concerns you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you are registering multiple campers, you can submit each form separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
