# summer-day-camp-registration-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a summer day camp or overnight camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the required fields with your contact information.
- Select the type of camp you would like to attend (summer-day-camp or overnight-camp).
- Choose the duration of your camp session (1-week or 2-weeks).
- Provide your start and end dates for the camp session.
- Select your preferred payment method (credit-card or bank-transfer).
- You can add any additional notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address.
* **Camp Type** (Select One, Required): Choose between Summer Day Camp or Overnight Camp.
* **Camp Duration** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose between 1-week or 2-weeks.
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the start date of your camp session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the end date of your camp session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose between Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.

**Tips**: Please make sure to fill out all required fields to complete your registration.
