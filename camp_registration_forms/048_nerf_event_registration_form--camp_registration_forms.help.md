# Nerf Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Nerf Event Registration Form. The form is divided into different sections, each with its own specific instructions and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by completing the "Event Details" section (page 1). This section requires you to provide information about the event, such as any specific details about the event that are relevant to your participation.
2. Next, move on to the "Participant Details" section (page 2). This section is where you will provide information about yourself as a participant, including any relevant details about your participation in the event.
3. Review the "Waiver" section (page 3). This section contains a release that you will need to review and agree to before proceeding.
4. In the "Equipment Needs" section (page 4), select the equipment you will need for the event from the list provided.
5. The "Participant Information" section (page 5) is for any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation.
6. In the "Contact Information" section (page 6), provide any contact information that you feel is necessary.
7. Review the "Parent Consent" section (page 7). This is an agreement that your parent or guardian must sign if you are under 18.
8. Finally, provide any emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" section (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Page 1, text, required: false): This section is where you will provide any specific details about the event that are relevant to your participation.
* **Participant Details** (Page 2, text, required: false): This section is where you will provide information about yourself as a participant, including any relevant details about your participation in the event.
* **Waiver** (Page 3, note, required: false): Please review and agree to this release before proceeding.
* **Equipment Needs** (Page 4, select_multiple, required: false): Select the equipment you will need for the event from the provided list.
* **Participant Information** (Page 5, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation.
* **Contact Information** (Page 6, text, required: false): Provide any contact information that you feel is necessary.
* **Parent Consent** (Page 7, select_one, required: false): If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must sign this agreement.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Page 8, text, required: false): Provide any emergency contact information that you feel is necessary.

Note: The required status of each field does not affect the explanation, as it's assumed that you will follow the instructions provided and fill out all required fields.
