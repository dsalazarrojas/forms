# Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Soccer Camp Registration Form is used to collect essential information from individuals registering for the camp. The form helps us to understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to provide a suitable experience for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the camp.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time, feel free to fill it out. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Camp Type** (`camp_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of camp you are registering for:
	+ Soccer Camp
	+ Summer Camp
	+ Sports Camp
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or concerns, feel free to include them here.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, text, required): Enter your parent's or guardian's name and contact information.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player you are registering.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): If you are registering a minor, enter their birth date.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and contact information.

Please note that this form is subject to change, and we may contact you if we need any additional information.
