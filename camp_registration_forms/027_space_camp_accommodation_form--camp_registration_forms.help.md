# Space Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Space Camp Accommodation Form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from children and their parents/guardians for the Space Camp program. This information is used to create a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Parent Name** in the first field.
2. Provide the **Contact Number** for the parent/guardian in the second field.
3. Enter the **Child Name** in the third field.
4. Enter the **Child Age** in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid phone number where the parent or guardian can be reached.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the child registering for the Space Camp program.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the age of the child, as a numerical value.
