# Language Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Camp Accommodation Form is designed to collect information from users about their language camp accommodation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with a asterisk (*) symbol.
3. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank or fill them in if you have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, required): Enter your name and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Accommodation Type** (Accommodation Type, select_one, required): Choose your preferred accommodation type (Single Room, Double Room, Shared Room).
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your address for the accommodation purpose.
* **Postcode** (Postcode, text, required): Enter your postcode for the accommodation purpose.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter the city where you'll be staying.
* **Country** (Country, text, required): Enter the country where you'll be staying.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Message** (Message, text, required): Enter any additional message you might have for us.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and accommodation arrangements.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Message" field.
