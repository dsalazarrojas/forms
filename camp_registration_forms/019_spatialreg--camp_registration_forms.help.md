# SPATIALreg - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, SPATIALreg, is designed for registration purposes. It collects demographic information from individuals, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and other relevant data. The form is likely intended for internal use within an organization, such as a university, hotel, or other type of business that requires attendee registration.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email in the Email field (if this field is required for your intended use case).
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the type of lodging you require (Standard, Deluxe, or Shared) from the Lodging options.
6. Fill in your emergency contact's First Name in the Emergency Contact First Name field.
7. Enter your emergency contact's Last Name in the Emergency Contact Last Name field.
8. Confirm whether your emergency contact is related to you (Yes or No) in the Relationship field.
9. Enter your Address in the Address field.
10. Enter your City in the City field.
11. Enter your State in the State field.
12. Enter your Zip code in the Zip field.
13. Enter your emergency contact's Phone number in the Emergency Contact Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Lodging** (Lodging, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of lodging you require.
	+ Standard: Default accommodation option.
	+ Deluxe: Luxury accommodation option.
	+ Shared: Shared accommodation option.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Emergency Contact First Name, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Emergency Contact Last Name, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, select_one, optional): Confirm the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
	+ Yes: Confirm that your emergency contact is related to you.
	+ No: Confirm that your emergency contact is not related to you.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (City, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (State, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields (if any).
* Double-check the accuracy of your emergency contact information.
* If any field is left empty, you may not be able to submit or save your form data.
