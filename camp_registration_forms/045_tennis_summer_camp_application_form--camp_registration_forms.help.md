# Tennis Summer Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for parents or guardians to register their children for a summer tennis camp. It collects necessary information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields to provide complete and accurate information.
2. Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for important communications.
3. If you're registering a child with special medical needs, please be as detailed as possible in the "Medical Conditions" and "Medical Info" fields.
4. Make sure to select at least one emergency contact, providing their name and phone number.
5. Confirm your emergency contact's relationship with you and your child, and whether they're allowed to be contacted in case of an emergency.
6. Choose the preferred camp schedule for your child.
7. If necessary, add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the camp staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
* **Address**: Enter your child's address.
* **Email**: Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your child's phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Parent's Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent's Contact Info**: Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Player's Interests**: Briefly describe your child's interests or favorite activities.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child (e.g., parent, grandparent, sibling, guardian).
* **Do you give permission for our staff to contact your emergency contact?**: Select whether you give permission for the camp staff to contact your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
* **Which of the following camps do you prefer?**: Choose one or more of the available camp schedules that suit your child's needs.
* **Any additional comments**: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the camp staff to know.
