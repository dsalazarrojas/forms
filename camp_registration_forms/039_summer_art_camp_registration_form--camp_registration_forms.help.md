# summer_art_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for the summer art camp. Please fill out the form to let us know you are interested in joining the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields according to the instructions provided below.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **summer_art_camp_registration_form** (`summer_art_camp_registration_form`, text, required/optional): Please enter a brief description of why you are interested in joining the summer art camp.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding the camp.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you in case of any questions or issues.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are planning on attending the camp.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the camp.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, contact us at our email address.
* We will review your form and get back to you with further instructions or confirmation of your registration.
