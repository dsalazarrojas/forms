# Girl Scout Product Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Product Permission Form is a form designed to gather information from customers about their product interests and preferences. This form is not intended for making purchases or placing orders, but rather to provide a way for Girl Scouts to understand customer demands and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide details about the product you are interested in, such as the type and price range.
3. Specify the quantity you are interested in purchasing.
4. Choose the type of product you are interested in (cookies, candy, or beverage).
5. Select a delivery date and time that suits you.
6. Add any additional notes about your order (e.g., special requests).
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
8. Choose a payment term that works for you (14, 28, or 30 days).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Text): Enter your name and contact information to help us understand who you are and how to get in touch with you.
* **Product Information** (Text): Describe the product you are interested in, including its type and price range.
* **Product Price Range** (Select One): Choose the price range that suits your budget (Less than $50, $50-$100, Over $100).
* **Product Quantity** (Number): Specify the number of products you are interested in purchasing.
* **Product Type** (Select One): Select the type of product you are interested in (Cookie, Candy, or Beverage).
* **Delivery Date** (Date): Choose a delivery date that works best for you.
* **Delivery Time** (Time): Select a delivery time that suits your schedule.
* **Additional Notes** (Text): Add any special requests or comments about your order.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Payment Term** (Select Multiple): Choose a payment term that works for you (14, 28, or 30 days).

Note: Please note that this form is not for making actual purchases or orders, but rather for gathering information about your product interests and preferences. If you are interested in purchasing a product, please contact us directly.
