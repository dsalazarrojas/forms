# RV Loan Online Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for RV loan payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as needed.
2. Review the fields that are not required but may be relevant to your payment or loan status.
3. Submit the form once all necessary information has been provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`): A field to enter any additional information about the payment or loan.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`): Enter the total amount of the loan.
* **Term Length** (`term_length`): Enter the length of the loan term in years.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`): Enter the interest rate on the loan (format: XXX.XX%).
* **Monthly Payment** (`monthly_payment`): Enter the monthly payment amount.
* **Total Interest** (`total_interest`): The total interest accrued on the loan.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method used (PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Bank Transfer Info** (`bank_transfer_info`): Additional information about the bank transfer.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`): Enter a user signature to confirm payment.
* **User Notes** (`user_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the payment or loan.
* **Second User Signature** (`user_signature_2`): Another field for a user signature.
* **Second User Notes** (`user_notes_2`): Additional information from the user.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the current payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`): Select the current loan status (Active or Inactive).
* **User Email** (`user_email`): Enter the user's email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter the user's phone number.
* **User Address** (`user_address`): Enter the user's address.
* **Loan ID** (`loan_id`): Enter the loan ID number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Additional information about the payment or loan.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any payment terms related to the loan.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms2`): Another field for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 3** (`payment_terms3`): Yet another field for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 4** (`payment_terms4`): And another field for payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 5** (`payment_terms5`): And finally, another field for payment terms.

## Tips

* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have provided all required information to avoid form errors.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the relevant parties for assistance.
