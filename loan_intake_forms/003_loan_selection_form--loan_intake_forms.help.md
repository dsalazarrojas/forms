# Loan Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Selection Form is designed to collect information from users regarding loan selections. This form is used to gather relevant details for loan processing, ensuring accurate and efficient loan processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have selected all required fields.
2. Fill in the loan selection form with the necessary information.
3. Submit the form to proceed with the loan process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_chooser_1** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`, `No`)
	+ Select one of the options for the first loan chooser.
* **loan_type** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Choose the type of loan you're interested in.
* **loan_amount** (`number`)
	+ Enter the amount of loan you're considering.
* **loan_term** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Select the loan term that suits your needs.
* **loan_interest_rate** (`number`)
	+ Enter the interest rate for your loan.
* **loan_status** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Choose the status of your loan.
* **note** (`note`)
	+ Add any additional notes regarding your loan.
* **email** (`email`)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (`text`)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **date** (`date`)
	+ Enter the date you're submitting the form.
* **time** (`time`)
	+ Enter the time you're submitting the form.
* **assigned_user** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Choose the user assigned to handle your loan.
* **submitted** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Select the status of your submission.
* **submitted_user** (`select_one`, `Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`)
	+ Choose the user who submitted the loan.
* **submitted_date** (`date`)
	+ Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **submitted_time** (`time`)
	+ Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **assigned_user_name** (`text`)
	+ Enter the name of the user assigned to handle your loan.
* **submitted_user_name** (`text`)
	+ Enter the name of the user who submitted the loan.
* **submitted_user_email** (`text`)
	+ Enter the email address of the user who submitted the loan.
* **submitted_user_phone** (`text`)
	+ Enter the phone number of the user who submitted the loan.
* **submitted_user_note** (`note`)
	+ Add any additional notes regarding the submission.
* **assigned_user_email** (`text`)
	+ Enter the email address of the user assigned to handle your loan.
* **assigned_user_phone** (`text`)
	+ Enter the phone number of the user assigned to handle your loan.

Note: This help guide assumes that all fields are optional and not required, as per the provided YAML schema. If any field is required, please update the help guide accordingly.
