# property_financing_assistance_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for property financing assistance applications. It gathers details about the property, the applicant's contact information, and other relevant aspects to facilitate the processing of financing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out each field according to your knowledge and records.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy, as this information will be used to determine your loan eligibility.
4. Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details (1)**: Enter your property's description to provide context for your financing request.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Provide your contact information so we can reach you for further questions or updates.
* **Property Funding Request (3)**: Briefly describe your reason for requesting property financing.
* **Property Value (4)**: Enter the monetary value of your property, if applicable.
* **Property Age (5)**: Specify the age of your property in years.
* **Property Status (6)**: Indicate whether your property is active or inactive.
* **Lender Info (7)**: If you've already dealt with a lender, provide their name or contact info.
* **Property Funding Amount (8)**: If applicable, provide the amount of funds you're requesting for your property.
* **Broker Info (9)**: If you've worked with a broker, provide their name or contact info.
* **Additional Notes (10)**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your financing request.
* **Property Type (11)**: Indicate whether you've purchased or are requesting to purchase a property, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Property Funding Status (12)**: Indicate whether your property funding is active or inactive.
* **Contact Preference (13)**: Provide your preferred contact method (e.g., email or phone number).
* **Phone Number (14)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out as they are crucial for processing your application.
- Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
