# Advance Loan VIP Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advance Loan VIP Registration form is designed to collect personal and financial information from users to determine their eligibility for a loan. By providing accurate and complete information, users can expedite the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your Residential Address, Employer Name, Job Title, and Employment Duration.
3. Enter your Annual Income and Monthly Net Income.
4. Specify the Requested Loan Amount and Loan Purpose.
5. Choose the Preferred Repayment Term.
6. If you are an existing customer, select 'Yes' and provide your Account Number (if applicable).
7. Finally, provide the reason for your VIP Status (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Residential Address** (`Residential Address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address where you reside.
* **Employer Name** (`Employer Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required): Enter your current position.
* **Employment Duration** (`Employment Duration`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have been employed at this position (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Annual Income** (`Annual Income`, `number`, required): Enter your gross annual income in USD.
* **Monthly Net Income** (`Monthly Net Income`, `number`, required): Enter your monthly income after deductions.
* **Requested Loan Amount** (`Requested Loan Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you would like to borrow.
* **Loan Purpose** (`Loan Purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose the intended use of the loan (Home improvement, Business investment, Medical expenses, Education, Debt consolidation, Vacation, Emergency, Other).
* **Preferred Repayment Term** (`Preferred Repayment Term`, `select_one`, required): Choose the desired duration to repay the loan (3 months, 6 months, 12 months, 24 months, 36 months, 48 months, 60 months).
* **Existing Customer** (`Existing Customer`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are an existing customer with our institution.
* **Account Number (if applicable)** (`Account Number (if applicable)`, `text`, required): Enter your existing account number if you are a current customer.
* **VIP Status Reason** (`VIP Status Reason`, `text`, optional): Provide the reason for your VIP Status.
