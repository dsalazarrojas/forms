# Credit Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Services Intake Form is designed to collect necessary information for credit services. Please fill out this form to provide your personal and employment details for credit checks or loan applications.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: 
  - Label: First Name
  - This field is for your personal first name.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Last Name**: 
  - Label: Last Name
  - This field is for your personal last name.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Address**: 
  - Label: Address
  - This field is for your address where you reside.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Email**: 
  - Label: Email
  - This field is for your email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true

- **Phone**: 
  - Label: Phone
  - This field is for your contact phone number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Social Security Number**: 
  - Label: Social Security Number
  - This field is for your social security number. Please enter it if you have one.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Date of Birth**: 
  - Label: Date of Birth
  - This field is for your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Credit Score**: 
  - Label: Credit Score
  - This field is for your credit score.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Employment Status**: 
  - Label: Employment Status
  - This field is for your employment status, which can be Full-time, Part-time, Self-Employed, or Retired.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: true

- **Income**: 
  - Label: Income
  - This field is for your monthly income.
  - Type: number
  - Required: true

## Tips
- Double-check the information you provide for accuracy.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.
