# Lending Responsibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lending Responsibility Assessment form is used to evaluate the creditworthiness of potential borrowers and assess their ability to repay a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages to understand the questions that need to be answered.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely, providing relevant information about yourself.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *) on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. First Page (form_1)** (`text`, optional): This page may include a brief introduction to the form and instructions.
* **2. Second Page (form_2)** (`text`, optional): This page may ask questions about your financial history, such as income, expenses, or credit history.
* **3. Third Page (form_3)** (`text`, optional): This page may include questions about your employment and employment history.
* **4. Fourth Page (form_4)** (`text`, optional): This page may ask questions about your assets and liabilities, such as property ownership or outstanding debts.
* **5. Fifth Page (form_5)** (`text`, optional): This page may include questions about your credit history, such as credit cards, loans, or bankruptcies.
* **6. Sixth Page (form_6)** (`text`, optional): This page may ask questions about your rental history, such as rental agreements or lease details.
* **7. Seventh Page (form_7)** (`text`, optional): This page may include questions about your co-signers or guarantors.
* **8. Eighth Page (form_8)** (`text`, optional): This page may ask questions about your property ownership or investment properties.
* **9. Ninth Page (form_9)** (`text`, optional): This page may include questions about your business or business history.
* **10. Tenth Page (form_10)** (`text`, optional): This page may ask questions about your loan history, such as previous loan approvals or rejections.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and completely.
- Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
