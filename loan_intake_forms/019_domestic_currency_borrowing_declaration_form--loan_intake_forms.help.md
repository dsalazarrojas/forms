# Domestic Currency Borrowing Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to declare domestic currency borrowing transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Domestic Currency Borrowing" to indicate whether or not a borrowing transaction has occurred.
3. Enter the borrower's name, if applicable.
4. Enter the date of the borrowing transaction.
5. Enter the amount of the borrowing transaction.
6. Provide additional comments about the transaction, if necessary.
7. Check the "Loan Agreement Signed" field to indicate if the loan agreement has been signed by both parties.
8. Provide any additional comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domestic Currency Borrowing** (Select "Yes" or "No"): This field is used to indicate whether or not a domestic currency borrowing transaction has occurred.
* **Borrower Name** (Text): Enter the name of the borrower, if applicable.
* **Borrowing Date** (Date): Enter the date of the borrowing transaction.
* **Borrowing Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the borrowing transaction.
* **Borrower Contact Info** (Note): Provide any additional contact information about the borrower.
* **Purpose of Borrowing** (Text): Provide a brief description of the purpose of the borrowing transaction.
* **Collateral** (Note): List any collateral used in the borrowing transaction.
* **Collateral Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of collateral involved in the transaction.
* **Interest Rate** (Number): Enter the interest rate agreed upon for the loan.
* **Payment Terms** (Text): Describe the payment terms of the loan, including payment frequency and amount.
* **Loan Agreement Signed** (Select "Yes" or "No"): Check this field to indicate if the loan agreement has been signed by both parties.
* **Additional Comments** (Note): Provide any additional information about the transaction, if necessary.
