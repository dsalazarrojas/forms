# Wealth Management Loan Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Management Loan Inquiry form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking loans for various purposes. The form helps to understand the individual's financial situation, loan requirements, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your loan details, such as the loan amount, term, and purpose.
3. Specify your employment status, length, and type.
4. Enter your annual income.
5. Select the loan term frequency and purpose (if not one of the listed options).
6. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Text): This field is for any additional information about yourself that you would like to share.
* **Contact Info** (Text): Please enter your name, which will be used to contact you regarding your loan inquiry.
* **Loan Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of money you are seeking as a loan.
* **Employment Status** (Text): Select your employment status to help us understand your financial situation.
	+ Employee: You are currently employed full-time or part-time.
	+ Freelance: You work on a freelance or contract basis.
	+ Other (please specify): Select this option if your employment status is not listed.
* **Employment Length** (Number): Enter how long you have been employed.
* **Annual Income** (Number): Enter your annual income.
* **Loan Term** (Select One): Select the loan term that suits your needs.
	+ 12 months
	+ 24 months
	+ 36 months
	+ 48 months
	+ 60 months
* **Credit Score** (Select Multiple): Select your credit score range to help us understand your creditworthiness.
	+ 600-699
	+ 700-759
	+ 760-799
	+ 800-809
	+ 810-839
	+ 840-889
	+ 890-939
	+ 940-989
	+ 990-1049
	+ 1050-1099
* **Purpose Loan** (Select One): Select the purpose of the loan you are seeking.
	+ House Purchase
	+ Vehicle Purchase
	+ Education
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Loan Term Frequency** (Time): Enter the frequency of the loan term, e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.
* **Employment Type** (Select One): Select your employment type to help us understand your work arrangement.
	+ Employee
	+ Freelance
	+ Owner/Operator
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other Loan Purpose** (Text): If the purpose of your loan is not listed, please specify.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person contacting you.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person contacting you.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person contacting you.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient loan inquiry process.
