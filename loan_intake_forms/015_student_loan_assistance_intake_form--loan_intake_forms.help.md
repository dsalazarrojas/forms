# Student Loan Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Loan Assistance Intake Form is used to collect information from students who are in need of loan assistance. This form helps the student services team understand the student's situation and provide the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student information in the "Student Info" section.
2. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" section.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" section.
4. Select the type of loan you are applying for in the "Loan Type" section.
5. Enter your current school name in the "Current School" section.
6. Enter your graduation date in the "Graduation Date" section.
7. Enter your current GPA in the "Current GPA" section.
8. Select your employment status in the "Employment Status" section.
9. Select your income range in the "Income" section.
10. Enter your current address in the "Current Address" section.
11. Enter your city in the "City" section.
12. Enter your state in the "State" section.
13. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" section.
14. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
15. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
16. Enter the date of contact in the "Date of Contact" section.
17. Enter the time of contact in the "Time of Contact" section.
18. Enter the date of last contact in the "Date of Last Contact" section.
19. Enter the number of dependents in the "Number of Dependents" section.
20. Enter the length of your employment in the "Employment Length" section.
21. Enter the start date of your employment in the "Employment Start Date" section.
22. Enter the end date of your employment in the "Employment End Date" section.
23. Enter any comments about your student loan assistance situation in the "Student Loan Assistance Form Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info**: Enter your student name, student ID, and any other relevant student information.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full student name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Loan Type**: Select the type of loan you are applying for.
	+ High Cost of Living: For students with high living expenses.
	+ Federal: For federal student loans.
	+ Private: For private student loans.
* **Current School**: Enter your current school name.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status.
	+ Employed: You are currently employed.
	+ Unemployed: You are currently unemployed.
	+ Student: You are a student.
