# Loan Application Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Intake Form is designed to collect information for a loan application. It is meant for individuals who are interested in applying for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select relevant options from drop-down menus.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **address** (text, required): Enter your current address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **loan_amount** (number, required): Enter the loan amount you're applying for.
* **loan_term** (number, required): Enter the loan term (e.g., months or years).
* **credit_score** (number, required): Enter your credit score.
* **credit_limit** (number, required): Enter your credit limit.
* **monthly_payment** (number, required): Enter your monthly payment amount.
* **income** (number, required): Enter your income.
* **loan_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of loan you're applying for. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **employment_status** (select_multiple, required): Select your employment status. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **loan_term_length** (time, required): Enter the length of the loan term.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **application_date** (date, required): Enter the date of application.
* **application_date_time** (time, required): Enter the time of application.
* **application_date_date** (date, required): Enter the date of application again.
* **credit_age** (number, required): Enter the age of your credit history.
* **loan_status** (select_one, required): Select the current loan status. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **employment_length** (number, required): Enter the length of your employment.
* **employment_type** (select_one, required): Select your employment type. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **employment_status** (select_multiple, required): Select your employment status again. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **income_verification** (select_multiple, required): Select your income verification method. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **application_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of application. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.

Note: Some fields are marked as required, so be sure to fill those out carefully to ensure accurate loan processing.
