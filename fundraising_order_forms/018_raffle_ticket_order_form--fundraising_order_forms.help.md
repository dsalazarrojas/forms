# Raffle Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers or donors to purchase raffle tickets, provide payment information, and claim a winner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the donor fields.
* Enter the raffle ticket number or quantity you wish to purchase.
* Enter the price of the raffle ticket.
* Enter any comments or notes about your order.
* Select your payment method (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
* Enter your payment details (if applicable).
* Select the order status (Confirmed or Unconfirmed).
* Select the payment gateway (Stripe or PayPal).
* Select the payment status (Success or Unsuccess).
* Enter the winner's name, email, phone, and comments (if applicable).
* Enter the draw date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **raffleTicketOrderForm**: Enter your name, email, and phone number here.
* **raffleNumber**: Enter the raffle ticket number or quantity.
* **donorName**: Enter your name, please.
* **donorEmail**: Enter your email address.
* **donorPhone**: Enter your phone number.
* **raffleTicketPrice**: Enter the price of the raffle ticket.
* **raffleQuantity**: Enter the quantity of raffle tickets.
* **orderComments**: Add any comments or notes about your order.
* **paymentMethod**: Select your payment method (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
* **paymentDetails**: Enter additional payment information (if applicable).
* **orderDate**: Enter the date and time of your order submission.
* **orderTime**: Enter the time of your order submission.
* **formSubmission**: Enter the status of your form submission.
* **orderStatus**: Select the status of your order (Confirmed or Unconfirmed).
* **paymentGateway**: Select the payment gateway (Stripe or PayPal).
* **paymentStatus**: Select the payment status (Success or Unsuccess).
* **raffleTicketWinner**: Enter the winner's name.
* **winnerEmail**: Enter the winner's email address.
* **winnerPhone**: Enter the winner's phone number.
* **winnerComments**: Enter the winner's comments.
* **winnerDetails**: Enter additional winner information.
* **drawDate**: Enter the date of the draw or raffle event.
* **drawTime**: Enter the time of the draw or raffle event.
