# Charity Donor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect relevant information from donors to facilitate accurate and timely donation processing, as well as to provide personalized communication with donors about their giving preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for donation receipts and future communication.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you wish to be contacted.
4. Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code in the address fields.
5. Select your preferred donation frequency from the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your primary area of interest from the options listed. You can select one or multiple options, depending on your preferences.
7. If you do not wish for your donation to be anonymous, please select "False" in the "Would you like this donation to remain anonymous" field.
8. If you wish to receive our monthly newsletter, please select "Yes, please" in the "Sign up for our monthly newsletter" field.
9. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Additional Comments" field, if you have any specific requests or information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prefix**: Choose a prefix that you would like to be used for your name.
	+ Mr.
	+ Ms.
	+ Mrs.
	+ Dr.
	+ Other
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, for donation receipts and future communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address, including house/Building number and suite/apartment number.
* **City**: Enter your city or town name.
* **State / Province**: Enter your state or province name.
* **Zip / Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose your preferred donation frequency:
	+ One-time
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Primary Area of Interest**: Select one or multiple primary areas of interest:
	+ Education
	+ Health
	+ Environment
	+ Disaster Relief
	+ General Fund
* **Would you like this donation to remain anonymous**: If you wish for your donation to be anonymous, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Sign up for our monthly newsletter**: If you wish to receive our monthly newsletter, select "Yes, please". Otherwise, select "No, thank you".
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions, if you have any specific requests or information to share.
