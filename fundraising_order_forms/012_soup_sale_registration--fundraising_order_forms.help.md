# Soup Sale Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soup Sale Registration form is used to gather information about customer orders for internal analysis and tracking purposes. It is not intended for actual order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the first field.
2. Provide customer information in the second field.
3. Select a delivery option from the third field.
4. Add any notes or comments in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order. (<label> `Order Details`, `<type>` `text`, required/optional: false)
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details. (<label> `Customer Information`, `<type>` `text`, required/optional: false)
* **Delivery Option**: Select how you would like to receive your order: Pickup, Delivery, or Other. (<label> `Delivery Option`, `<type>` `select_one`, required/optional: false)
* **Order Notes**: Add any comments or special requests for your order. (<label> `Order Notes`, `<type>` `text`, required/optional: false)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields (although this form is currently not required for any fields).
* If you have any specific requests, please enter them in the `Order Notes` field.
* If your delivery option is not Pickup or Delivery, please specify in the `Order Notes` field.
