# Blood Donation Campaign Fund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blood Donation Campaign Fund Form is a tool used to accept donations for the Blood Donation Campaign, allowing individuals or organizations to support various blood donation initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
3. Review and confirm the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`Donor Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name or the name of the organization donating on your behalf.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address to receive updates and receipts for your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your contact number in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, `optional`): If you're donating on behalf of a company, enter the name of the organization.
* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the amount you'd like to donate: $25, $50, $100, $250, $500, $1000, or Other Amount.
* **Custom Amount** (`Custom Amount`, `number`, `optional`): If you've selected "Other Amount," enter the amount in the space provided.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how often you'd like to make this donation: One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the method of payment: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Card Number** (`Card Number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your card number if you're paying by card.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the expiration date of your card, formatted as MM/YY.
* **CVV** (`CVV`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the security code (CVV) for your card.
* **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your billing address if you're paying by card.
* **Campaign Designation** (`Campaign Designation`, `select_one`, `required`): Select which campaign to support: General Blood Donation Fund, Emergency Response Fund, Pediatric Blood Bank, Mobile Unit Operations, Donor Recruitment, or Where Needed Most.
* **Tribute Donation** (`Tribute Donation`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if this is a tribute donation: Yes In Honor Of, Yes In Memory Of, or No General Donation.
* **Honoree Name** (`Honoree Name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the person being honored.
* **Send Notification** (`Send Notification`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose if you'd like to send a notification to someone: Yes Send Card, Yes Send Email, or No (False).
* **Notification Address** (`Notification Address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the address to send the notification to, if applicable.
* **Employer Matching** (`Employer Matching`, `select_one`, `optional`): Check if your employer will match your donation: True, False, or Not Sure.
* **Receipt Preference** (`Receipt Preference`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how you'd like to receive your receipt: Email, Mail, or Both.
* **Newsletter Signup** (`Newsletter Signup`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you'd like to receive campaign updates: Yes Sign Me Up, No Thanks.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.
