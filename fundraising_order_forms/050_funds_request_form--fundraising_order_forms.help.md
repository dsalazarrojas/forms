# Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funds Request Form is designed to gather information about your institution's needs and requirements for financial support. This form should be used for official purposes only, such as submitting a request for funds to a specific team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Make sure to select the correct institution type from the options in the "Institution Details" field.
3. Choose the correct financial details from the options in the "Financial Details" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
5. If necessary, choose a specific tool that will be assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (\*): Enter your name and address to contact you regarding your funds request.
* **Institution Details** (\*): Select the type of institution where you work. This could be a public, private, or non-profit organization.
* **Financial Details** (\*): Choose the financial details you are requesting. These may include salary, benefits, travel, equipment, training, or software.
* **Time Period** : Indicate the period for which you are requesting funds. This field is optional.
* **Submitted By** (\*): Enter your ID number.
* **Email** (\*): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (\*): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: If a tool has been assigned to you, enter its name in this field. This is optional.
 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team or organization directly.
* You can also attach any supporting documents to your form as required.
* Please note that the team or organization will review your request and contact you for further information or clarification if needed.
