# Mpox Engagement Fund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Engagement Fund Form is used for collecting donations and information from donors, prophets, and other stakeholders to support the Mpox Engagement Fund. This form helps to track and record donations, their purpose, and the contact information of the donors and prophets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Donor Name** field with the name of the person or organization making the donation.
2. Enter the **Donation Date** in the date format you are familiar with (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Provide the **Donation Total** in the amount of the donation.
4. Select the **Fund Use** purpose of the donation from the options provided (Mpox Prevention, Mpox Education, or Public Health Response).
5. If you want to leave a message for the Mpox team, you can do so in the **Donor Message** field.
6. Fill in the **Prophet's Name**, **Prophet's Phone**, and **Prophet's Email** fields with the relevant information of the prophet who will be using the funds.
7. Select the **Assigned Tool** used for the donation from the options provided (chatjimmy or Other).
8. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool 2** used for the donation from the options provided (chatjimmy or Other).
9. Provide the **Output File** with any additional information or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Name** (donor_name): Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation.
- **Donation Date** (donation_date): Enter the date of the donation in a recognized date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Donation Total** (donation_total): Enter the total amount of the donation.
- **Fund Use** (fund_use): Select the purpose of the donation from the options provided:
	+ Mpox Prevention: To support the prevention of Mpox.
	+ Mpox Education: To support education related to Mpox.
	+ Public Health Response: To support public health responses related to Mpox.
- **Donor Message** (donor_message): A text field for the donor to leave a message for the Mpox team.
- **Prophet's Name** (prophets_name): Enter the name of the prophet associated with the donation.
- **Prophet's Phone** (prophets_phone): Enter the phone number of the prophet.
- **Prophet's Email** (prophets_email): Enter the email address of the prophet.
- **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): Select the tool assigned to the prophet from the options provided:
	+ chatjimmy: A specific tool used for the donation.
	+ Other: If the tool is not chatjimmy, select this option.
- **Assigned Tool 2** (assigned_tool_2): Select the tool assigned to the prophet from the options provided:
	+ chatjimmy: A specific tool used for the donation.
	+ Other: If the tool is not chatjimmy, select this option.
- **Output File** (output_file): Upload any additional information or attachments related to the donation.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in to complete the form.
