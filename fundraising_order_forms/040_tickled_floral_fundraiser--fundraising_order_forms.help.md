# Tickled Floral Fundraiser - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tickled Floral Fundraiser form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to place an order but have not yet provided their personal details. This form allows users to provide their contact information and order details in a straightforward and easy-to-use interface.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the correct information.
2. Review your answers for accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supporter Information** (supporter_information, text, optional):
	- This field is for a general description of the type of information you want to share about the supporter's order.
* **Supporter Name** (supporter_name, text, optional):
	- Enter the name of the supporter placing the order.
* **Contestant Name** (contestant_name, text, optional):
	- Enter the name of the contestant for the order.
* **Order of Product** (order_of_product, text, optional):
	- Enter the specific product and quantity ordered.
* **Supporter Order** (supporter_order, number, optional):
	- Enter the number of items ordered. This field is not a description, but rather a quantity.
* **Supporter Email** (supporter_email, email, optional):
	- Enter the email address of the supporter.
* **Supporter Phone** (supporter_phone, text, optional):
	- Enter the phone number of the supporter.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, note, optional):
	- Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
