# first_data_online_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "first_data_online_donation_form" is a form designed to collect online donation information from donors. This form helps us gather essential details about the donation, such as the type of donation, frequency, and amount. It ensures that we have accurate and complete data for our internal records and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the relevant options for the donation type and gift frequency as required.
3. Enter the donation amount in the specified format.
4. Choose the donor's contact method from the provided options.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Information
* **Donation Information** (`donation_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is the brief description of the donation being made.
 
* **Donor Information** (`donor_information`, `text`, required: false): This field should be filled with any relevant details about the donor that you want to provide.

* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of donation being made (option 1, option 2, or option 3).

* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how often the gift is given (option 1, option 2, or option 3).

* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the gift.

* **Donor Contact** (`donor_contact`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the best way to contact the donor (option 1, option 2, or option 3).

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you're selecting the correct options and filling in the correct information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your team for assistance.
- Ensure that you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.
