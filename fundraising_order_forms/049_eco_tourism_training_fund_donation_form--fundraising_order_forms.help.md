# Eco Tourism Training Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting donations to support the Eco Tourism Training Fund, a initiative to support environmental education and conservation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Specify Your Donation Amount
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, text): Enter the amount of your donation in the specified currency. Please note that the default currency is USD, but you can select EUR or Other if needed.

### Step 2: Choose Your Currency
- **Currency** (`currency`, select_one): Select the currency of your donation from the options: USD, EUR, or Other. If you choose Other, please specify the currency in the text field below.

### Step 3: Select Your Payment Method
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple): Choose one or multiple payment methods for your donation. Options include Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.

### Step 4: Specify Donation Frequency (Optional)
- **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one): If you are making an ongoing donation, specify the frequency of your donations (e.g., monthly, quarterly).

### Step 5: Provide Additional Frequency Details (Optional)
- **Frequency Details** (`frequency_details`, text): If you selected an ongoing donation frequency, provide additional details about your donation frequency (e.g., start date).

### Step 6: Enter Your Contact Information
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your name as the contact person for this donation.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Organization** (`organization`, text): Enter the name of the organization you represent, if any.
- **Organization URL** (`organization_url`, text): Enter the URL of your organization, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate, as it will be used for follow-up communications.
* You can make a one-time or ongoing donation.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure all fields are accurate.
