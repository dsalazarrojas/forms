# Fundraising Campaign Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and permissions from participants in a fundraising campaign.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required details of the participant, including their name, organization, and contact information.
2. Check the "participant_agreement" field to indicate if the participant has granted permission to release their data to the campaign organizers.
3. Enter the participant's address, email, phone number, and any fundraising efforts they have been involved in.
4. Add any additional information or notes about the participant in the "additional_info" field.
5. Date and time the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **organization_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the participant's organization or group.
* **participant_agreement** (select_one, optional): Check the box if the participant has granted permission to release their data. (Yes/No)
* **participant_address** (text, optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **participant_fundraising_efforts** (text, optional): Describe the participant's fundraising efforts.
* **participant_signature** (text, optional): Enter the participant's signature (if required).
* **date** (date, optional): Date the form was completed.
* **time** (time, optional): Time the form was completed.
* **additional_info** (text, optional): Add any additional information about the participant.
