# Hero T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hero T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering t-shirts for fundraising purposes. This form is used to gather customer details, shirt preferences, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Select your preferred Shirt Size from the "Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred Shirt Color from the "Shirt Color" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the desired Quantity of t-shirts in the "Quantity" field.
7. Select the type of T-Shirt you would like to order from the "T-Shirt Type" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date and time of your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields respectively.
9. Enter any additional Comments about your order in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the Total Number of t-shirts you would like to order in the "Total" field.
11. Select your preferred Payment Method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
12. Enter your Address Line 1 in the "Address Line 1" field.
13. Enter your Address Line 2 in the "Address Line 2" field.
14. Enter your City in the "City" field.
15. Enter your State in the "State" field.
16. Enter your Zip in the "Zip" field.
17. Enter your Phone in the "Phone" field.
18. Enter any Order Notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.
19. Select if you are part of a T-Shirt Fundraising Group in the "T-Shirt Fundraising Group" dropdown menu.
20. Enter the School Year in the "School Year" field.
21. Enter the School in the "School" field.
22. Enter your Student ID in the "Student ID" field.
23. Enter your Teacher ID in the "Teacher ID" field.
24. Enter the Order Confirmation code to confirm your order in the "Order Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your First Name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your Last Name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): Provide your Email address for order updates and communication.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required/optional): Select the size of the t-shirt you prefer.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, required/optional): Select the color of the t-shirt you prefer.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **T-Shirt Type** (`tshirt_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of t-shirt you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your order.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Total** (`total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your Address Line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your Address Line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your City.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your State.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your Zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your Phone number.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any Order Notes about your order.
* **T-Shirt Fundraising Group** (`tshirt_fundraising_group`, select_one, required/optional): Select if you are part of a T-Shirt Fundraising Group.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required/optional): Enter the School Year.
* **School** (`school`, text, required/optional): Enter the School.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your Student ID.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your Teacher ID.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, note, required/optional): Enter the Order Confirmation code to confirm your order.
