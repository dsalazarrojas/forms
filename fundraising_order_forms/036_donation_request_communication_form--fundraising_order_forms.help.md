# Donation Request Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from users regarding donation requests, including contact details and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Name**, which should be your full name.
2. Provide your **Email** address to allow us to contact you.
3. If you have a **Phone** number, please enter it.
4. Please describe the **Donation Request** you are interested in.
5. Check the **Payment Method**, indicating whether you are willing to make a payment (Yes/No).
6. Select the **Payment Date**, if you are planning to make a payment (optional).
7. Indicate the **Payment Frequency**, if you are willing to make regular payments (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your full name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for easier communication.
* **Donation Request** (`donation_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your donation request.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are willing to make a payment, "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to make the payment (if applicable).
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you plan to make regular payments, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to help us process your request efficiently.
* Please do not duplicate existing requests or contact us if you have already spoken with a team member about this request.
* We appreciate your feedback and will be in touch shortly after submitting this form.
