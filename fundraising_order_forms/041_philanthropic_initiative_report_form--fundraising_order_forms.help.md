# Philanthropic Initiative Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on philanthropic initiatives, including general information, contact details, donation details, and other relevant data. It is intended for reporting purposes, and all fields are optional. Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your data is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with any relevant details about the initiative.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. If applicable, enter the donation amount and date received.
4. If necessary, specify the contact person responsible for the initiative.
5. Enter any relevant information about the organization associated with the initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any general information about the initiative.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.

### Donation Information
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation received.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the donation was received.

### Initiative Details
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person responsible for the initiative.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the organization associated with the initiative.

### Additional Contact Information
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided instructions or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is for reporting purposes only, and all submitted data will be treated confidentially.
