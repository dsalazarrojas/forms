# Asia and Kloeys CHEER With Cards Fundraiser - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a fundraiser order from a customer. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Customer Full Name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the type of card box you'd like to purchase from the "Select Your Card Box Type" field.
5. Enter the number of boxes you'd like to order in the "Number of Boxes" field.
6. Confirm the total order amount based on the price per box (30 USD).
7. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
8. Choose your preferred delivery method from the "Preferred Delivery Method" field. If you select shipping, you will be required to provide a shipping address.
9. If you selected shipping, enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
9. Finally, specify which cheerleader should receive credit for this sale in the "Which cheerleader should receive credit for this sale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_name_cf`, text, required): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone_cf`, text, required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email_cf`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Select Your Card Box Type** (`card_box_selection_cf`, select_one, required): Choose the type of card box you'd like to purchase. Options include All-Occasion Box 1, All-Occasion Box 2, Birthday Box, Thanks and Blanks Box, and Holiday Card Box.
* **Number of Boxes** (`box_quantity_cf`, number, required): Enter the number of boxes you'd like to order.
* **Total Order Amount** (`total_order_amount_cf`, number, required): Confirm the total order amount based on the price per box (30 USD).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method_cf`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method: Venmo, Cash, Check, or Credit Card Online.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method_cf`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred delivery method: Porch Pickup, Home Delivery, or Ship to My Address (additional fee applies).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address_cf`, text, false): Only required if you selected "Ship to My Address" as your delivery method. Enter your shipping address in this field.
* **Which cheerleader should receive credit for this sale** (`cheerleader_credit_cf`, select_one, required): Specify which cheerleader should receive credit for this sale: Asia, Kloey, or Both.
