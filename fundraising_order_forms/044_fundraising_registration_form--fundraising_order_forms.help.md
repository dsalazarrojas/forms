# Fundraising Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Registration Form is used to gather information from users who want to participate in fundraising. This form helps us track and manage donations for our campaigns and ensure accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the user's information (page 1).
2. Fill out the contact information (page 2).
3. Indicate your preferred donation frequency (page 3: Donation Frequency).
4. Choose your preferred donation method (page 3: Donation Method).
5. Provide your preferred payment date and time (page 3: Payment Date and Payment Time).
6. Add any additional payment notes (page 3: Payment Note).
7. Enter your email address and phone number (page 4: Email and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`): Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information such as address, phone number, and any other details we need to get in touch with you.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount you'd like to donate. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you'd like to.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`): Choose how often you'd like to donate: Weekly, Monthly, or One-time. This is a required field.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`): Select how you'd like to make your donation: Online Payments, Offline Payments, or both. This is a required field.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): If you've chosen Online Payments, enter the date you'd like to make your payment. This field is not required.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`): If you've chosen Online Payments, enter the time you'd like to make your payment. This field is not required.
* **Payment Note** (`payment_note`): Add any additional notes or comments about your payment. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Description** (`description`): This field is not used in this form but is available for future extensions. You can enter any additional information you'd like to provide. This field is not required.
