# School Pie Fundraiser Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Pie Fundraiser Order Form is used by students at your school to place orders for School Pies. This form helps the school collect orders and track the number of pies each student wants to order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your school name from the available options (if any) or type it in the text field.
2. Enter the number of pies you would like to order.
3. Enter your name as a student.
4. Enter your school email address.
5. Enter your school phone number (if you have one).
6. If you are placing an order for multiple parents, please enter the number of people in your household.
7. Enter the total cost of the pies you are ordering.
8. Enter your school address.
9. Enter your school phone number (if your school has one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name (School Name)**: Enter your school name to confirm your affiliation.
* **Number of Pies Ordered (number_of_pies_ordered)**: Please enter the number of pies you would like to order.
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email (student_email)**: Enter your school email address.
* **Student Phone (student_phone)**: Enter your school phone number (if available).
* **Number of Parents (number_of_parents)**: If you are placing an order for multiple parents, enter the number of people in your household.
* **Total Price (total_price)**: Enter the total cost of the pies you are ordering.
* **School Address (school_address)**: Enter your school address.
* **School Phone (school_phone)**: Enter your school phone number (if available).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using your school email address and phone number.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your school administration for assistance.
* Please double-check your form for any errors before submitting it.
