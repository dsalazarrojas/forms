# Custom Coupon Booklet Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Coupon Booklet Order Form is used to collect information necessary for the creation and delivery of custom coupon booklets. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the required fields**: Make sure to fill out fields marked as "required".
2. **Select options carefully**: Choose the correct option for fields like "Product", "Shipping", and "Custom Booklet Type".
3. **Double-check your information**: Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Custom Coupon Booklet Order Form** (id: 1) - This is the title of the form, which is pre-filled and cannot be changed.
* **Full Name** (id: 2) - Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (id: 3) - Enter your email address.
* **Quantity** (id: 4) - Enter the quantity of booklets you want to order.
* **Product** (id: 5) - Select the type of product (booklet) you want to order. Please choose from the available options: Booklet 1, Booklet 2, or Booklet 3.
* **Shipping** (id: 6) - Select the shipping method: Standard, Express, or UPS.
* **Address** (id: 7) - Enter your address for shipping.
* **City** (id: 8) - Enter your city.
* **State** (id: 9) - Enter your state.
* **Zip** (id: 10) - Enter your zip code.
* **Date** (id: 11) - Enter the date.
* **Time** (id: 12) - Enter the time.
* **Notes** (id: 13) - Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Phone** (id: 14) - Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Company** (id: 15) - Enter your company name (optional).
* **Fax** (id: 16) - Enter your fax number (optional).
* **Website** (id: 17) - Enter your website (optional).
* **Comments** (id: 18) - Enter any additional comments (optional).
* **Custom Product** (id: 19) - Enter any custom product details (optional).
* **Custom Booklet Type** (id: 20) - Enter any custom booklet type details (optional).
* **Custom Booklet Color** (id: 21) - Enter custom booklet color (optional).
* **Custom Booklet Size** (id: 22) - Select the size of the custom booklet: A3, Letter, Legal, or Tabloid.
* **Custom Booklet Layout** (id: 23) - Select the layout of the custom booklet: 2-up, 3-up, or 4-up.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 24) - Enter any payment terms or conditions (optional).
* **Payment Terms Agreement** (id: 25) - Accept or Decline the payment terms. Please note that this field is required for order processing.
