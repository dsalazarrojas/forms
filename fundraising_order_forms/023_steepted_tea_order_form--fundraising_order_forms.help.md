# Steepted Tea Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Steepted Tea Order Form is a tool designed to help us gather information about your preferences and needs when it comes to your tea order. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form, and we'll do our best to ensure you receive the perfect cup of tea!

## How to Complete This Form
- Start by selecting your preferred type of tea from the "What type of tea do you prefer?" dropdown menu. You can choose from options like Black, Green, Herbal, or White.
- If you have any known food allergies, please select the corresponding option from the "Do you have any food allergies?" checkbox.
- Next, let's chat about your tea drinking frequency. How often do you enjoy a cup of tea? Select "Rarely", "Occasionally", or "Frequently" from the options provided.
- Enter your contact email address in the format "email@example.com". This is a required field for us to stay in touch with you.
- In the "Do you have any special requests?" text field, feel free to let us know if you have any specific tea preferences or requirements. This is an optional field, but it helps us better serve you!
- If you need tea bags, please specify the number in the "How many tea bags do you need?" field. This is an optional field, but it helps us prepare your order correctly.
- Choose your preferred method of payment from the "What is your preferred method of payment?" dropdown menu. You can select Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal.
- If you'd like to join our newsletter, please select "Option A" or "Option B" from the "Do you want to join our newsletter?" checkbox.
- Finally, please provide your order date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" and your contact phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
- If you're a member of our loyalty program, please select "True" or "False" from the "Are you a member of our loyalty program?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What type of tea do you prefer?** (`customer_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the type of tea you enjoy the most. This will help us choose the right tea for you.
- **What is your date of birth?** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): If you're willing to share, please provide your date of birth. This information helps us get to know you better.
- **Do you have any food allergies?** (`food_allergies`, `checkbox`, required/optional): If you have any food allergies, please let us know. We'll take care of you.
- **How often do you drink tea?** (`tea_drinking_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Tell us about your tea drinking habits. This helps us serve you better.
- **What is your contact email address?** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address. This is how we'll communicate with you.
- **Do you have any special requests?** (`special_requests`, `text`, required/optional): Share any special tea preferences or requirements you might have. We're here to listen!
- **How many tea bags do you need?** (`tea_bags_needed`, `number`, required/optional): If you require tea bags, please let us know how many. We'll take care of the rest.
- **What is your preferred method of payment?** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method. We accept Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal.
- **Do you want to join our newsletter?** (`newsletter_subscription`, `checkbox`, required/optional): If you'd like to stay updated on our latest tea news, please select "Option A" or "Option B".
- **What is your order date?** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Please provide your order date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **What is your contact phone number?** (`phone_number`, `number`, required/optional): Share your contact phone number with us. This helps us stay in touch.
- **Are you a member of our loyalty program?** (`loyalty_membership`, `select_one`, required/optional): If you're a part of our loyalty program, please select "True". Otherwise, choose "False".

Please take a moment to review each field carefully, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
