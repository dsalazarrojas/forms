# veggie_fundraising_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from individuals interested in participating in a veggie fundraising campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the questions and select your preferred options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery or Pickup** (`delivery_or_pickup`, Select One, required: false): Select how you would like to receive your fundraiser's orders: Delivery or Pickup.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, Select Multiple, required: false): Indicate your level of interest in volunteering for the campaign.
* **Fundraiser Orders** (`orders`, Text, required: false): Enter any orders or comments you may have for the fundraiser.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, Email, required: false): Provide your contact email for the campaign team to reach you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you've already participated in a similar campaign, please do not fill this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the campaign, reach out to the campaign team using the provided contact email.
