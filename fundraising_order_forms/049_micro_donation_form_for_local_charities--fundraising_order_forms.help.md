# Micro Donation Form For Local Charities - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Micro Donation Form For Local Charities is a simple form designed to collect information and data related to donations for local charities. This form is meant to help the charity understand the donor's preferences and donation details. Please note that this form is not meant for soliciting donations for personal gain or for testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donation amount in the Donation Amount field. This will be the amount you are willing to donate.
2. Enter your name in the Name field. This will be the name of the person making the donation.
3. Enter your Email field with your valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone field with your phone number.
5. Select your preferred Donation Frequency from the dropdown menu (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
6. Select your preferred Payment Gateway from the dropdown menu (Stripe, PayPal, or Square).
7. Select your preferred Payment Method from the dropdown menu (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
8. If applicable, enter any additional comments in the Note field.
9. Confirm that you want to receive email confirmations (True or False).
10. Confirm that you want to pay for the donation (True or False).
11. Confirm that you want to make the donation (True or False).
12. If you are representing another organization, enter the Donor Organization field.
13. Enter the Donation Date and Donation Time fields with the date and time of the donation.
14. Enter any Donor Comments if you have any comments about the donation.
15. Finally, enter the Form Submitted By field with the name of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to donate to the local charity.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person making the donation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to make the donation (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, required): Select the payment gateway you would like to use (Stripe, PayPal, or Square).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you would like to use (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the donation.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm if you want to receive email confirmations (True or False).
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you want to pay for the donation (True or False).
* **Confirm Donation** (`confirm_donation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you want to make the donation (True or False).
* **Donor ID** (`donor_id`, text, optional): Enter the Donor ID if you have one.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor City** (`donor_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor State** (`donor_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Postal** (`donor_postal`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Country** (`donor_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the person or organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donor Organization** (`donor_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization making the donation on your behalf.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the donation.
* **Donor Comments** (`donor_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the donation.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
