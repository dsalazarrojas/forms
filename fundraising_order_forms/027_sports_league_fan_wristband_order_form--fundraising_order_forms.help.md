# Sports League Fan Wristband Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering custom wristbands related to sports leagues. It will help the sports league management team to process and prepare your custom order.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below.

1. Enter your **League Name** in the first field. This is the name of the sports league you are affiliated with.
2. Enter your **first_name** and **last_name** to help us identify you.
3. Enter your **email** address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
5. Choose your **T-Shirt Size** from the available options: Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large.
6. Select the desired **Wristband Color** from the options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
7. Enter the quantity of wristbands you would like to order in the **Quantity** field.
8. Enter the name of your sports team in the **Team Name** field.
9. If you have any additional comments or instructions, you can type them in the **Notes** field.
10. Confirm your email address in the **Confirm Email** field to ensure we have the correct email on file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **League Name**: Enter the name of the sports league you are affiliated with.
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and confirmation of your order.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to help us contact you.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your T-Shirt size: Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large.
* **Wristband Color**: Choose the color of your wristband: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of wristbands you would like to order.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your sports team.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm the email address you entered to ensure we have the correct email on file.
