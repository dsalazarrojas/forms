# Fundraising Campaign Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to renew a fundraising campaign, updating existing campaign details and ensuring continued progress. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to maintain consistency across the campaign data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the field label.
2. For fields with select options, choose the most relevant or accurate option.
3. Enter your contact information correctly, including email and any other relevant details.
4. Review and submit your form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the fundraising campaign.
* **Campaign ID** (`campaign_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the campaign to be renewed.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the campaign.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Select the date for the renewal.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the renewal (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the campaign.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any extra details or remarks about the campaign.
* **Campaign Type** (`campaign_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of the campaign (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
* **Fund Type** (`fund_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the fund types (Fund 1, Fund 2, or Fund 3) involved in the campaign.
* **Fund ID** (`fund_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the fund.
* **Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`, number, required): Enter the term for the renewal.
* **Renewal Amount** (`renewal_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the renewal.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment (Method 1 or Method 2).
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, select_one, required): Choose the term for the payment (Term 1 or Term 2).
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment frequency (Frequency 1 or Frequency 2).
* **Fund Name** (`fund_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the fund.
* **Fund Description** (`fund_description`, text, optional): Enter the description of the fund.
* **Campaign URL** (`campaign_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the campaign.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the contact address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the contact city.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Enter the contact state.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, optional): Enter the contact zip code.

Note: The guide assumes that the form fields and their labels are up-to-date and correct. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
