# Pledge Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pledge Drive Form is designed to collect pledges for specific events, organizations, or initiatives. It helps to gather information about the user's pledge amount, frequency, and duration of the pledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the "User Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter the **Pledge Amount** in the "Pledge Amount" field. This is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. This field is not required but is recommended for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This field is not required.
5. Add any additional **Note** about your pledge in the "Note" field. This field is not required.
6. Select the **Pledge Type** for which you are pledging (e.g., Event, Organization, or Initiative).
7. Choose the **Frequency** of your pledge (e.g., One Time, Recurring, or Monthly).
8. Enter the **End Date** of your pledge.
9. Enter the **Start Date** of your pledge.
10. If your pledge is frequency-based, enter the **Frequency Start Date** and **Frequency End Date**.
11. Select the **Donation Frequency** (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
12. Enter the **Donation Start Date** and **Donation End Date**.
13. Enter the **Frequency Donation Start Date** and **Frequency Donation End Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your card or ID.
- **Pledge Amount** (`pledge_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are pledging.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional note about your pledge.
- **Pledge Type** (`pledge_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of pledge (e.g., Event, Organization, or Initiative).
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you want to donate (e.g., One Time, Recurring, or Monthly).
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which you want to end your pledge.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date from which you want your pledge to start.
- **Frequency Start Date** (`frequency_start_date`, date, required): If your pledge is frequency-based, enter the start date of your frequency.
- **Frequency End Date** (`frequency_end_date`, date, required): If your pledge is frequency-based, enter the end date of your frequency.
- **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you want to donate (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
- **Donation Start Date** (`donation_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date from which you want your donations to start.
- **Donation End Date** (`donation_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which you want your donations to end.
- **Frequency Donation Start Date** (`frequency_donation_start_date`, date, required): If your pledge is frequency-based, enter the start date of your donation frequency.
- **Frequency Donation End Date** (`frequency_donation_end_date`, date, required): If your pledge is frequency-based, enter the end date of your donation frequency.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
