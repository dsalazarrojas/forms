# Youth Activity Funding Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Activity Funding Expression of Interest Form is a tool for youth groups to express their interest in funding for their activities. This form is used to gather information about the group's funding needs, project details, and contact information. The form helps the youth group to present their request for funding in a structured and clear manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill in your details in the respective fields, such as project title and description, funding amount, and funding source.
3. Select your youth group's name and contact information.
4. Choose the funding source and end dates for your funding request.
5. If you need to add any additional notes, you can do so in the "Notes" field.
6. Finally, review all the information you've entered and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Activity Funding Form** (`youth_activity_funding_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your youth activity.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of funding you need for your activity.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, select_one, not required): Select the source of funding you are interested in, such as "Community Fund" or "Foundation Fund".
* **Youth Group Name** (`youth_group_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your youth group.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the contact person for your group.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the contact person for your group.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your group.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, not required): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Funding Request Start Date** (`funding_request_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date for your funding request.
* **Funding Request End Date** (`funding_request_end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date for your funding request.
* **Youth Council Fund** (`youth_council_fund`, select_multiple, not required): Select the funding sources you are interested in, such as "Community Fund" and "Foundation Fund".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about your funding request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click submit to submit your form.
