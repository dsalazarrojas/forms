# Campaign Fundraising Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campaign Fundraising Declaration Form is a document designed to be completed by individuals and committees to declare their fundraising activities. It is a critical tool to ensure that campaign finance laws are adhered to, and the fundraising activities are reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the committee and candidate information section (page 1) with your name and contact details.
2. Provide a detailed description of your fundraising event or activity in the "Description of Fundraising Event or Activity" section (page 2).
3. Enter the total gross proceeds raised, total activity expenses, and net proceeds realized in the financial summaries section (page 3).
4. Break down your funding sources in the "Breakdown of Funding Sources" section (page 4).
5. Acknowledge compliance with campaign finance laws in the "Compliance Acknowledgment" section (page 5).
6. Enter the reporting date and treasurer's electronic signature in the final sections (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate or Committee Name** (`candidate_committee_name_decl`, text, required): Enter the name of the committee or candidate you are raising funds for.
- **Treasurer Full Name** (`treasurer_name_full_decl`, text, required): Enter your full name as the treasurer.
- **Treasurer Email** (`treasurer_contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Description of Fundraising Event or Activity** (`event_activity_description_decl`, text, required): Describe the fundraising event or activity, e.g., "Gala Dinner," "Online Crowdfunding," or "Direct Mail."
- **Date(s) of Activity** (`activity_date_range_decl`, text, required): Enter the date(s) of the fundraising event or activity.
- **Total Gross Proceeds Raised** (`total_gross_proceeds_decl`, number, required): Enter the total amount of money raised before expenses.
- **Total Activity Expenses** (`total_activity_expenses_decl`, number, required): Enter the total cost to run the event or activity.
- **Net Proceeds Realized** (`net_proceeds_calculated_decl`, number, required): This field will be calculated automatically.
- **Breakdown of Funding Sources** (`source_of_funds_summary`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable funding sources (e.g., Individual Contributions, Corporate Contributions, Political Action Committee (PAC), Candidate Personal Funds, Small Donor Committee).
- **Compliance Acknowledgment** (`compliance_acknowledgement_decl`, select_one, required): Acknowledge compliance with campaign finance laws. You can choose from two options: "I Acknowledge and Certify" or "Other."
- **Reporting Date** (`declaration_reporting_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are reporting the fundraising activity.
- **Treasurer Electronic Signature** (`treasurer_signature_digital_decl`, text, required): Enter your full name as the treasurer.
- **Auditor Initials (Internal Use)** (`auditor_initials_internal_decl`, text, optional): Enter your initials as the internal auditor (not required for external users).
- **Filing Reference ID** (`filing_id_reference_decl`, text, optional): Enter a reference ID for internal use.
