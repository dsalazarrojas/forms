# fundraising_campaign_license_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fundraising campaign license form is used to license fundraising campaigns and order forms. It's a crucial tool for users to provide information about their campaigns, ensuring they are properly categorized and licensed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct **form category** from the dropdown list. Choose "Fundraising" for fundraising campaigns and "Order Forms" for order forms.
2.  Enter the **title** of your campaign in the text box. This will be the title of your fundraising campaign or order form.
3.  Provide a brief **description** of your campaign in the text box. This will give users an idea of what your campaign is about.
4.  Determine whether your campaign is **Assigned To** someone. If it is, select "Yes" from the dropdown list.
5.  Choose the **Output File** name from the dropdown list. This will be the filename for the generated output file containing your campaign information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Form ID (1)**: A unique ID for your form. This is not required but can be useful for tracking purposes.
*  **Form Category (2)**: Select the category for your campaign. Choose "Fundraising" for fundraising campaigns and "Order Forms" for order forms.
*  **Form Description (3)**: A brief description of your campaign.
*  **Title (4)**: The title of your campaign.
*  **Category (5)**: The category of your campaign. This should match the **Form Category** field above.
*  **Description (6)**: A brief description of your campaign.
*  **Assigned To (7)**: Check if your campaign is assigned to someone.
*  **Output File (8)**: Choose the filename for the generated output file containing your campaign information.
