# Scentsy Buddy Drive Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scentsy Buddy Drive Order Form is used to collect information from customers who are placing an order for a fundraiser. This form is used to gather details about the order, including customer name, order date and time, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
3. Enter the order details in the Order Form Details field.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. If you have any special notes or requests, use the Order Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form Details** (`order_form_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are placing the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are placing the order.
* **Order Amount** (`order_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any special notes or requests you have about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your sales representative.
* Review your order details carefully before submitting the form.
