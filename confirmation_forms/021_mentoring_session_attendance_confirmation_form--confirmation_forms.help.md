# Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the attendance of mentors and mentees to mentoring sessions, ensuring both parties are present and aware of the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the session ID of the mentoring session you are confirming attendance for.
2. Provide the details of the mentee, including their name or contact information.
3. Select the appropriate agreement (Mentor's Terms or Mentee's Terms) that applies to this session.
4. Indicate whether the mentor and mentee will be attending the session.
5. Enter the start and end dates and times of the session.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Add any additional notes about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session ID (session id)** (`text`, required): Enter the ID number of the mentoring session you are confirming attendance for. This will help identify the session in case of any issues.
* **Mentee Details (mentee details)** (`text`, required): Enter the name, contact information, or any other details about the mentee who will be attending the session.
* **Agreement (agreement)** (`select multiple`, required): Choose the agreement that applies to this session, either Mentor's Terms or Mentee's Terms.
* **Mentor Attendance (mentor attendance)** (`select one`, required): Indicate whether the mentor will be attending the session by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Mentee Attendance (mentee attendance)** (`select one`, required): Indicate whether the mentee will be attending the session by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Notes (notes)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the session that may be helpful for future reference.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach you if needed.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, required): Enter the start date of the session.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, required): Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session Start Date (session_start_date)** (`date`, required): Enter the start date of the session.
* **Session Start Time (session_start_time)** (`time`, required): Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session End Date (session_end_date)** (`date`, required): Enter the end date of the session.
* **Session End Time (session_end_time)** (`time`, required): Enter the end time of the session.
