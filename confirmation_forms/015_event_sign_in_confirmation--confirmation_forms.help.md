# Event Sign In Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sign In Confirmation form is designed to collect and verify participant information for specific events. It is intended to be used in a controlled environment to ensure accurate and reliable participant records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's details in the text field "participant_details".
2. Enter the participant's name in the "participant_name" text field.
3. Review and confirm your input to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_details** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the participant, such as their role or affiliation.
* **participant_name** (`text`, optional): Enter the participant's name as it should be recorded for the event.
* **event_sign_in_confirmation** (`number`, optional): This field is not currently used and may be removed or modified in the future. Please do not fill it in unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure correct participant records.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the event organizer or refer to the event's guidelines.
