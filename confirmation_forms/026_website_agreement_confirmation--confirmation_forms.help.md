# Website Agreement Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to confirm that a user has reviewed and agreed to the website's terms of use. It is typically used as a final step before granting access to certain features or data on the website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Confirmation" field with your name, confirming that you are the correct user.
2. Select your answer to "Agreed to Terms", indicating whether you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of use.
3. If you have agreed to the terms, enter your name and date in the "Signed By" field, following the format "Name: [Your Name], Date: [mm/dd/yyyy]".
4. If you have agreed to the terms, enter the date you signed in the "Signed At" field, in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Confirmation**: <input type="text" name="user_confirmation"> Enter your name to confirm that you are the correct user. This field is not required but it helps to ensure that the right person is confirming.
- **Agreed to Terms**: <select name="agreed_to_terms"> Choose from the options "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of use.
- **Signed By**: <textarea name="signed_by"> Enter your name and date in the format "Name: [Your Name], Date: [mm/dd/yyyy]" to confirm your agreement.
- **Signed At**: <input type="date" name="signed_at"> Enter the date you signed the agreement in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" if you have agreed to the terms.
