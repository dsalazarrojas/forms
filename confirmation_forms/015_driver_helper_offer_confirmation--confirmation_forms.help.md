# Driver Helper Offer Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Helper Offer Confirmation form is designed to confirm and record the details of a job offer made to a driver. This form is used to ensure that both the driver and the company are on the same page and that the terms of the offer are understood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Submission" field, which is the main text box for recording the details of the job offer.
2. Fill in the "Driver Helper Name" field with the name of the driver who will be performing the task.
3. In the "Job Offer Details" field, enter a brief description of the job offer, including any relevant details such as the job title, duration, and pay rate.
4. In the "Job Offer Description" field, provide a more detailed description of the job offer, including any specific tasks or responsibilities that the driver will be expected to perform.
5. Enter the "Job Offer ID" field with the unique identifier for the job offer.
6. Choose the "Offer Start Date" field to select the date when the job offer is expected to start.
7. Choose the "Offer End Date" field to select the date when the job offer is expected to end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Submission** (text): Enter the details of the job offer, including any relevant information such as job title, duration, and pay rate.
- **Driver Helper Name** (text): Enter the name of the driver who will be performing the task.
- **Job Offer Details** (text): Enter a brief description of the job offer, including any relevant details.
- **Job Offer Description** (text): Provide a more detailed description of the job offer, including any specific tasks or responsibilities.
- **Job Offer ID** (text): Enter the unique identifier for the job offer.
- **Offer Start Date** (date): Select the date when the job offer is expected to start.
- **Offer End Date** (date): Select the date when the job offer is expected to end.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields to ensure that both the driver and the company have a clear understanding of the job offer.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the job offer, do not hesitate to contact the relevant parties to clarify the details.
- Save the form after filling out all the fields to generate a confirmation document that can be used as a reference later.
