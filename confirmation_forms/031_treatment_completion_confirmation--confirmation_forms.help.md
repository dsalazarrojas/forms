# Treatment Completion Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Treatment Completion Confirmation" form is designed to document and record the completion of medical treatments. This form ensures that healthcare providers accurately report their treatments and services, maintaining continuity of care and accurate patient records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "completed treatment" information, including a brief description of the treatment.
2. Enter the name of the provider who administered the treatment.
3. Record the date the treatment was completed.
4. (Optional) Include any notes from the provider, such as comments or additional information.
5. Record the date of the provider's signature.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm that the treatment has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Completed Treatment** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the treatment that has been completed.
  This field is for providing a concise summary of the treatment.
- **Provider Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the treatment.
  This field is for recording the name of the healthcare provider responsible for the treatment.
- **Date of Treatment** (`date`): Enter the date the treatment was completed.
  This field is for recording the exact date the treatment was finished.
- **Provider's Notes** (`note`): (Optional) Provide any additional comments or information from the healthcare provider about the treatment.
  This field is for the provider to include any relevant details about the treatment not covered in the other fields.
- **Provider Signature** (`date`): Enter the date of the healthcare provider's signature.
  This field is for recording the date the healthcare provider signed the form.
- **Provider Signature Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the healthcare provider's signature.
  This field is a duplicate of the "Provider Signature Date" field, for redundancy and verification purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use this form for treatments that have actually been completed and not for pending or hypothetical treatments.
* Verify that the healthcare provider's signature and date match the treatment completion date.
* Store the completed form in the patient's record for future reference and continuity of care.
