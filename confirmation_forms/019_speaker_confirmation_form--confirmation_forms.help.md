# Speaker Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Confirmation Form is designed to collect information from speakers to confirm their participation in an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Speaker Confirmation Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your Speaker Information, including any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
2. Confirm your participation in the event.
3. Confirm your travel arrangements.
4. Confirm your lodging arrangements.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information** (`speaker_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself as a speaker.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event you are participating in.
* **Confirm Participation** (`confirm_participation`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you will participate in the event, 'No' if you will not participate.
* **Confirm Travel Arrangements** (`confirm_travel`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have confirmed your travel arrangements, 'No' if you have not confirmed them yet.
* **Confirm Lodging** (`confirm_lodging`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have confirmed your lodging arrangements, 'No' if you have not confirmed them yet.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your participation.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate event planning.
* If you have any questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
