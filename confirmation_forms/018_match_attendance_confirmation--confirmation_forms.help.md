# Match Attendance Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the attendance of two teams to a match.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Team Name**, **Attendance Confirmation**, and **Game Date/Time**.
2. Select the correct team names and attendance status.
3. Add any additional team members or leads if necessary.
4. Enter the game details and location.
5. Submit the form to confirm the match attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: Select the name of the team you are confirming attendance for.
* **Team Name Others**: Select any other team names that are involved in the match.
* **Attendance Confirmation**: Confirm if the teams have attended the match.
* **Other Team**: Select the name of the other team that attended the match.
* **Team Leads**: Enter the names of the team leads who attended the match.
* **Team Members**: Enter the names of the team members who attended the match.
* **Game Date**: Enter the date of the match.
* **Game Time**: Enter the time of the match.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the match.
* **Game Details**: Enter any additional details about the match.
* **Game Date/Time**: Enter the date and time of the match.
* **Team Names 2**: Enter the names of the teams involved in the match.
* **Team Leads 2**: Enter the names of the team leads who attended the match.
* **Team Members 2**: Enter the names of the team members who attended the match.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate match attendance confirmation.
* Double-check the team names and attendance status to avoid any errors.
* Enter any additional team members or leads if necessary.
* Enter the game details and location accurately.
