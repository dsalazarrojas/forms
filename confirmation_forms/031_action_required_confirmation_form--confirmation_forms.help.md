# Action Required Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and confirm specific actions required within an organization, ensuring that individuals are held accountable for their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the person confirming the action in the `Name of Person Confirming` field.
2. Enter the job title or position of the person confirming the action in the `Position or Title` field.
3. Select the department where the person confirming the action works in the `Department` field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the required action in the `Description of Required Action` field.
5. Specify who gave the instruction or requirement in the `Source of Instruction` field.
6. Enter the due date for the required action in the `Due Date for Action` field. This will be a date field.
7. Select the level of urgency for the required action in the `Priority Level` field. Options are "Low", "Medium", "High", "Critical".
8. Confirm that you understand the required action and its importance in the `Confirmation of Understanding` field.
9. Commit to completing the required action by the specified due date in the `Commitment to Completion` field.
10. If required, specify the resources needed to complete the action in the `Resources Needed` field.
11. If required, mention any potential obstacles that might prevent completion in the `Potential Obstacles` field.
12. Finally, enter the date when this confirmation is made in the `Confirmation Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Person Confirming (confirmee_name)**: Enter the full name of the person acknowledging the required action.
* **Position or Title (confirmee_position)**: Enter the job title or position of the person confirming the action.
* **Department (confirmee_department)**: Select the department where the person works.
* **Description of Required Action (action_description)**: Provide a detailed description of the action that is required.
* **Source of Instruction (instruction_source)**: Specify who gave the instruction or requirement.
* **Due Date for Action (due_date)**: Enter the date by which the required action needs to be completed.
* **Priority Level (priority_level)**: Select the level of urgency for the required action: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Confirmation of Understanding (confirmation_acknowledgment)**: Confirm that you understand the required action and its importance.
* **Commitment to Completion (commitment_to_completion)**: Commit to completing the required action by the due date.
* **Resources Needed (resource_needs)**: If required, specify the resources needed to complete the action.
* **Potential Obstacles (potential_obstacles)**: If required, mention any potential obstacles that might prevent completion.
* **Confirmation Date (confirmation_date)**: Enter the date when this confirmation is made.
