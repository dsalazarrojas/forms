# meeting_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The meeting confirmation form is used to confirm the details of a meeting, including the date, start and end times, location, and participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the meeting date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g. 01/15/2024).
3. Select the start and end times of the meeting.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. Enter the participant's name and email address (optional).
6. Enter the participant's phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g. 01/15/2024).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the meeting.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the meeting (e.g. Conference Room A).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant attending the meeting.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant attending the meeting.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant attending the meeting.
