# Stt Supervisor Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from supervisors to confirm student details and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct "Work Period" to indicate the student's work hours.
2. Enter the Supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
3. Sign your name in the "Supervisor Signature" field.
4. If applicable, enter the Supervisor's work period in the "Supervisor Work Period" field.
5. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
6. If applicable, enter the student's sponsor's name in the "Student Sponsor" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
8. Select "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" in the "Supervisor Confirmation 2" field to confirm or deny the student's activities.
9. Select "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" in the "Student Confirmation" field to confirm or deny the student's activities.
10. If applicable, enter the supervisor's department in the "Supervisor Department" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supervisor Confirmation**: Enter a brief confirmation or denial of the student's activities.
* **Work Period**: Select the time period during which the student worked (morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor who is confirming or denying the student's activities.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign your name as the supervisor to confirm your identity and authority.
* **Supervisor Work Period**: Enter the time period during which you worked as a supervisor (morning, afternoon, evening). (Optional)
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student whose activities are being confirmed or denied.
* **Student Sponsor**: Enter the name of the student's sponsor, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the student's activities.
* **Supervisor Confirmation 2**: Select "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" to confirm or deny the student's activities.
* **Student Confirmation**: Select "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed" to confirm or deny the student's activities.
* **Supervisor Department**: Enter the name of the department you belong to, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
