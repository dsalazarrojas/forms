# Live Demo Session Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Demo Session Confirmation Form is designed to gather details about a live demo session, including the date, start time, session topic, and participant information. This form helps to confirm attendance and gather essential details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Live Demo Session Confirmation Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the live demo session in the `Live Demo Session Confirmation Form` field.
2. Select the date of the live demo session.
3. Enter the start time of the live demo session.
4. Enter the session topic.
5. Provide participant information:
	* Enter the participant's name.
	* Enter the participant's email address.
	* Enter the participant's phone number.
	* Enter the participant's company name.
	* Enter the participant's job title.
6. Confirm attendance by selecting `Yes` or `No`.
7. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the `Comments` field.
8. Answer follow-up questions 1-6.
