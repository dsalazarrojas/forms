# DSWD Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DSWD Form Template is a comprehensive template designed to gather information about a client's service history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form pages and understand the specific fields to be completed on each page.
2. Enter the client's service goal, type, and details on page 1.
3. Provide demographic information, such as client name, age, and address on pages 2-3.
4. Record the service provider, service start and end dates, and follow-up details on pages 4-5.
5. Complete the service status, follow-up date and time, and follow-up location and notes on pages 6-7.
6. Add any additional service frequency details and emails on pages 8-9.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Goal** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the service provided to the client.
* **Service Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the service type from the provided options.
* **Service Type Option** (Select One, Optional): Choose a specific service type from the provided options.
* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's name.
* **Age** (Number, Required): Enter the client's age.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Service Begin Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the service began.
* **Service End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the service ended.
* **Service Begin Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time the service began.
* **Service End Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time the service ended.
* **Service Provider** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Case Worker Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes from the Case Worker.
* **Client Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Case Worker Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the Case Worker's phone number.
* **Service Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the service status from the provided options.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional follow-up notes.
* **Service Frequency** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the frequency of services from the provided options.
* **Client EMail** (Email, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Case Worker EMail** (Email, Optional): Enter the Case Worker's email address.
* **Client Signature** (Note, Required): Date and sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Case Worker Signature** (Note, Required): Date and sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Service Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the service.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field-by-field explanation.
