# travel_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the details of your travel reservation and provide any necessary information for a smooth travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Enter the details of your reservation, including flight and contact information.
3. Confirm your reservation and any additional details that may be necessary.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel confirmation details** (text): Enter a brief description of your reservation, including any relevant details about your travel plans.
* **reservation confirmation** (select_one): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have received and reviewed your reservation details.
* **passenger confirmation** (select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for each passenger that has confirmed their reservation.
* **flight details** (text): Enter any additional information about your flight, such as flight numbers or departure times.
* **payment details** (text): Enter any payment information that is required or relevant to your reservation, such as payment methods or receipts.
* **contact information** (text): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
* We will use the information provided in this form to confirm your reservation and ensure a smooth travel experience.
