# Order Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Confirmation Form is used to confirm and review order details with customers. This form helps ensure accurate information and status updates are communicated effectively, reducing errors and misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Order Confirmation** title in the first field, which is the heading of the form.
2. Provide your **Customer Name** in the second field, as per the customer's preference or company name.
3. Enter the **Order Number** in the third field, which is a unique identifier for the order.
4. Select the **Order Date** for the order from the calendar provided in the fourth field.
5. Provide your **Shipping Name** in the fifth field, as per the shipping company name or your company name.
6. Enter the **Shipping Address** in the sixth field, which should include the address where the order will be shipped.
7. Enter the customer's **Email Address** in the seventh field, which will help in communicating with them about order updates.
8. Provide your **Customer Phone Number** in the eighth field, which will help in case of any urgent queries or updates.
9. Select the **Order Status** from the dropdown options in the ninth field, indicating the current order status.
10. Add any relevant **Note** to the tenth field, which could be about any concerns, questions, or special requests from the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Confirmation** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the order confirmation, typically "Order Confirmation" or a similar heading.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name or the name of the company placing the order.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the order, which helps track and manage orders.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date the order was placed or is scheduled to be delivered.
* **Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the shipping company or your company name.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the order will be shipped.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address for order updates and communications.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for any urgent queries or updates.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the order from the provided options.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the order, such as concerns or special requests from the customer.
