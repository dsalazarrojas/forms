# Co Branding Campaign Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm details about a co-branding campaign between two parties, typically a brand and a partner.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully to understand the required information.
- Fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Ensure that you have approvals from both parties before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirmation Date**: <i>This date is used to record the confirmation date of the campaign. Please enter the date in [YYYY-MM-DD] format.</i>
* **Primary Brand Contact Name**: <i>This field is used to capture the name of the primary brand contact. Please enter the name of the contact person in charge of the campaign.</i>
* **Primary Brand Email**: <i>This field is used to capture the email address of the primary brand contact. Please enter a valid email address.</i>
* **Primary Brand Phone**: <i>This field is used to capture the phone number of the primary brand contact. Please enter the phone number in the international format.</i>
* **Primary Brand or Company Name**: <i>This field is used to capture the name of the primary brand or company. Please enter the name as it appears on official documents.</i>
* **Partner Brand or Company Name**: <i>This field is used to capture the name of the partner brand or company. Please enter the name as it appears on official documents.</i>
* **Partner Brand Contact Name**: <i>This field is used to capture the name of the partner brand contact. Please enter the name of the contact person in charge of the campaign.</i>
* **Partner Brand Contact Email**: <i>This field is used to capture the email address of the partner brand contact. Please enter a valid email address.</i>
* **Campaign Name**: <i>This field is used to capture the official title of the campaign. Please enter a descriptive title that represents the campaign's purpose.</i>
* **Campaign Objective**: <i>This field is used to capture the objective of the campaign. Please enter a brief description of what the campaign is trying to achieve.</i>
* **Campaign Start Date**: <i>This field is used to capture the start date of the campaign. Please enter the date in [YYYY-MM-DD] format.</i>
* **Campaign End Date**: <i>This field is used to capture the end date of the campaign. Please enter the date in [YYYY-MM-DD] format.</i>
* **Campaign Channels**: <i>This field is used to capture the channels through which the campaign will be executed. Please select all applicable channels from the list.</i>
* **Target Audience Description**: <i>This field is used to capture a description of the target audience. Please enter a brief description of who the campaign is targeting.</i>
* **Budget Allocation**: <i>This field is used to capture the allocation of the budget for the campaign. Please enter a description of how the budget will be split.</i>
* **Success Metrics**: <i>This field is used to capture how the campaign's success will be measured. Please enter a description of the metrics used to measure success.</i>
* **Approval Status**: <i>This field is used to confirm that both parties have approved the campaign details. Please select the correct approval status from the list.</i>

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
