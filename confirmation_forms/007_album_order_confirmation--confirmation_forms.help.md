# Album Order Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Album Order Confirmation form is used to collect information and confirmation from customers regarding their album order details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the digital mockup of your album order provided by our production team.
2. Go to this form and fill out your information in the required fields.
3. Review each field carefully and make sure it accurately reflects your order details.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the "Confirm and Approve" button to finalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number** (1, text, required): Your order reference number, which you can use to track your order.
* **Order Date** (2, date, required): The date the order was placed, to ensure accurate production scheduling.
* **Customer Name** (3, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your order.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Your contact email for order updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Your contact number for order inquiries.
* **Album Style** (6, select_one, required): Select the album style you ordered, such as Classic Leather or Modern Linen.
* **Album Size** (7, select_one, required): Select the size of the album, such as 8x8 inches or 12x12 inches.
* **Album Color** (8, select_one, required): Select the color of the album cover, such as Black or White.
* **Number of Pages** (9, number, required): Enter the total number of pages in your album.
* **Page Layout** (10, select_one, required): Select the layout style for your photos, such as single image per page or 2 images per page.
* **Cover Text** (11, text, optional): Enter any text to be embossed on the cover of your album.
* **Page 1 Approval Status** (12, select_one, required): Review and approve the design of your first page.
* **Page 1 Comments** (13, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments for your first page.
* **Page 2 Approval Status** (14, select_one, required): Review and approve the design of your second page.
* **Page 2 Comments** (15, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments for your second page.
* **Page 3 Approval Status** (16, select_one, required): Review and approve the design of your third page.
* **Page 3 Comments** (17, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments for your third page.
* **Overall Order Approval** (18, select_one, required): Confirm whether you approve this order for production.
* **Special Instructions** (19, text, optional): Enter any additional requirements or notes for your order.
* **Customer Confirmation** (20, select_one, required): Confirm your approval of this order.
