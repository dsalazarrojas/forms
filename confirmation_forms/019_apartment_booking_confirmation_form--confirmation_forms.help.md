# Apartment Booking Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the details of an apartment booking, ensuring accuracy and completeness of the information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the booking from the calendar.
2. Enter the booking reference number provided by the booking system.
3. Fill out the tenant or guest's full name.
4. Enter the tenant's email address.
5. Enter the tenant's phone number.
6. Enter the full apartment address.
7. Enter the apartment unit number (if applicable).
8. Enter the city where the apartment is located.
9. Enter the state or province where the apartment is located.
10. Enter the zip or postal code of the apartment.
11. Select the check-in date from the calendar.
12. Select the check-out date from the calendar.
13. Enter the number of nights the apartment will be booked for.
14. Select the type of occupancy (e.g., short-term rental, lease agreement).
15. Enter the number of occupants.
16. Select whether pets are allowed in the apartment.
17. Enter the monthly rent amount in dollars.
18. Enter the security deposit amount in dollars.
19. Select the utilities included in the rent (e.g., water, electricity).
20. Select whether parking is available and included in the rent.
21. Rate the apartment condition at move-in (e.g., excellent, good, fair).
22. Enter the lease term length in months.
23. Confirm that the booking details are accurate.
24. Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking date**: Select the date of the booking from the calendar to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Booking reference number**: Enter the unique reference number provided by the booking system to reference the booking details.
* **Tenant or guest full name**: Enter the full name of the tenant or guest booking the apartment.
* **Tenant email address**: Enter the email address of the tenant or guest for communication purposes.
* **Tenant phone number**: Enter the phone number of the tenant or guest for contact purposes.
* **Apartment address**: Enter the full address of the apartment, including the street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Apartment unit**: Enter the unit number of the apartment (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where the apartment is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where the apartment is located.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of the apartment.
* **Check-in date**: Select the date the tenant or guest will check-in from the calendar.
* **Check-out date**: Select the date the tenant or guest will check-out from the calendar.
* **Number of nights**: Enter the number of nights the apartment will be booked for.
* **Type of occupancy**: Select the type of occupancy (e.g., short-term rental, lease agreement).
* **Number of occupants**: Enter the number of people staying in the apartment.
* **Pets in the apartment**: Select whether pets are allowed in the apartment (e.g., no pets, dogs only, cats only).
* **Monthly rent amount**: Enter the monthly rent amount in dollars.
* **Security deposit amount**: Enter the security deposit amount in dollars.
* **Utilities included**: Select the utilities included in the rent (e.g., water, electricity).
* **Parking available**: Select whether parking is available and included in the rent.
* **Apartment condition at move-in**: Rate the apartment condition at move-in (e.g., excellent, good, fair).
* **Lease term length**: Enter the lease term length in months.
* **Confirmation agreement**: Confirm that the booking details are accurate.
* **Signature date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the booking details.
