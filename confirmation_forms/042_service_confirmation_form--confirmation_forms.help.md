# Service Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Confirmation Form is a digital template used to record and track service-related information, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in service delivery confirmation processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the service confirmation, which will serve as a reference for the service instance.
2. Provide the employee's name involved in the service delivery.
3. Select the current status of the service: Completed, Not Completed, or In Progress.
4. Enter the date the service was performed.
5. Enter the time the service was performed.
6. Leave any additional comments about the service, if necessary.
7. Enter the employee's contact email address, in case further communication is required.
8. Enter the employee's phone number, in case further communication is required.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Confirmation Form**: Enter a brief title for the service confirmation, which will serve as a reference for the service instance.
* **Employee Name**: Provide the employee's name involved in the service delivery.
* **Service Status**: Select the current status of the service: Completed, Not Completed, or In Progress.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date the service was performed.
* **Service Time**: Enter the time the service was performed.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about the service, if necessary.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's contact email address, in case further communication is required.
* **Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number, in case further communication is required.
* **Service Note**: Optionally, add any additional notes about the service.
