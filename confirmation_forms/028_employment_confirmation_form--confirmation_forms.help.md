# Employment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm an individual's employment status, including their employment type, start and end dates, eligibility status, employer details, and job title.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct employment status from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of employment (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
3. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of employment.
4. Select the employment eligibility status.
5. Enter the employer's name.
6. Optionally, enter the employer's address.
7. Enter the job title.
8. (If applicable) Enter the length of employment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employment status** (confirmation_status): Confirm the current employment status (e.g., Active, Terminated, Retired, Other)
- **Employment type** (employment_type): Select the type of employment (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary)
- **Employment start date** (employment_start_date): Enter the start date of employment (Format: MM/DD/YYYY)
- **Employment end date** (employment_end_date): Enter the end date of employment (Format: MM/DD/YYYY)
- **Employment eligibility** (employment_eligibility): Confirm if the employee is eligible or ineligible
- **Employer name** (employer_name): Enter the name of the employer
- **Employer address** (employer_address): Optionally, enter the address of the employer
- **Job title** (job_title): Enter the job title
- **Employment length** (employment_length): Optionally, enter the length of employment in months
- **Confirmation notes** (confirmation_notes): Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes about the employment confirmation
