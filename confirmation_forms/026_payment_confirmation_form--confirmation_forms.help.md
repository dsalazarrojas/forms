# payment_confirmation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The payment confirmation form is used to verify payment details after a transaction has been made. This form helps ensure that the payment has been successfully completed and that all the necessary information is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the payment details provided on each page to ensure accuracy.
2. Select the correct options for each field as necessary.
3. Fill in any required fields with the relevant information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Confirm Payment** (confirm_payment, note, required/optional): This is a note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Confirm Payment Details** (confirm_payment_details, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the payment details that apply to your transaction. These details will vary depending on the specific options provided.
* **Confirm Payment Date** (confirm_payment_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Confirm Payment Time** (confirm_payment_time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Confirm Payment Method** (confirm_payment_method, select_one, required/optional): Select the method of payment used for this transaction.
* **Confirm Payment Status** (confirm_payment_status, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the payment, which can indicate whether it was successful or failed.
* **Confirm Payment Amount** (confirm_payment_amount, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the payment.
* **Confirm Payment Currency** (confirm_payment_currency, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency used for this transaction.
