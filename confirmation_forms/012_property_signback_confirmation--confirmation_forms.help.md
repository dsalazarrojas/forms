# Property Signback Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Signback Confirmation form is used to verify updates or changes made to a property's details. This form is intended to ensure that all parties are informed and in agreement with the changes, reducing potential errors or disputes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and email address to provide identification and contact information.
* Provide your phone number so that we can reach you for further clarification.
* Select the correct option from the Signature field to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the property updates.
* If necessary, add any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.
* If you are an authorized representative, select the correct option for Assigned To and enter the assigned date for the confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other name(s) associated with the property update.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the provided options to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the property updates.
	+ Client Acknowledged and accepted the updates
	+ Client did not understand and needs clarification
	+ Client is not interested and does not want to confirm
	+ Client declined to confirm
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have regarding the property updates.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the property update.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the property update.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any relevant property details that require updates or changes.
	+ Location
	+ Status
	+ Type
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: true): Select the name of the authorized representative who is responsible for the property update.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the property update was assigned to the representative.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the property updates before confirming.
* If you are unsure about any of the updates, please select the "Other" option for Signature and provide further comments.
* If you are an authorized representative, ensure that you select the correct option for Assigned To and enter the correct assigned date.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
