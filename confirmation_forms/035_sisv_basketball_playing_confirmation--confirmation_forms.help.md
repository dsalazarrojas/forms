# sisv_basketball_playing_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sisv_basketball_playing_confirmation form is used to confirm the availability of a player in a basketball context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Confirm your availability by selecting the correct options from the "Confirm Your Availability" section.
2. Enter the date and time of the event you are confirming, if applicable.
3. Enter your name and team name for accurate record-keeping.
4. Confirm the date and time you are available until, if applicable.
5. Add any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
6. Enter your contact phone number and email address, if applicable.

* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you are confirming, if applicable.

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team you are part of, if applicable.
* **Confirm Until Date** (`confirm_until_date`, date, required): Confirm the date until which you are available.
* **Confirm Until Time** (`confirm_until_time`, time, optional): Confirm the time until which you are available.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your availability.
