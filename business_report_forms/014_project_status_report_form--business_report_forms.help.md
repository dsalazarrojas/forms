# Project Status Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Status Report Form is designed to gather essential information about a project's current status. This form helps project managers and team members to stay on top of project progress, identify potential issues, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project's name and status in the "Project Status" field.
2. List the team members involved in the project, including their roles and responsibilities.
3. Mention any team meetings that have taken place or are scheduled.
4. Enter the project's budget and actual costs incurred so far.
5. Provide an update on the project's current status, including any challenges or successes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Status** (`project_status_report_form`, text, required): This field is where you provide a brief description of the project's current status. Be as concise as possible and include any relevant details.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the team members involved in the project, including their roles and responsibilities.
* **Team Meetings** (`team_meetings`, text, optional): Mention any team meetings that have taken place or are scheduled.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, text, optional): Enter the project's budget, including any allocations for different components.
* **Actual Costs** (`actual_costs`, text, optional): Enter the actual costs incurred so far, including any notable expenses or savings.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your updates.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists when providing details.
* Attach relevant supporting documents or data, if necessary.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your project manager or team lead for guidance.
