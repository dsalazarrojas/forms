# Digital Manufacturing Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Manufacturing Efficiency Report Form is a tool used to collect data on a specific production shift in the manufacturing process. This data is used for reporting and performance measurement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the relevant details for the production shift being reported on.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **production-shift** (1, number, required): Enter the shift number for the production shift being reported on.
* **machine-type** (2, select_one, optional): Select the type of machine used during the production shift (Production Equipment, Manual Equipment, or Other).
* **production-duration** (3, number, required): Enter the total duration of the production shift.
* **production-quantity** (4, number, required): Enter the total quantity produced during the production shift.
* **production-hours** (5, number, required): Enter the total hours worked during the production shift.
* **production-start-time** (6, time, required): Enter the start time of the production shift.
* **production-end-time** (7, time, required): Enter the end time of the production shift.
* **production-date** (8, date, required): Enter the date of the production shift.
* **production-shift-id** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable shift IDs (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **notes** (10, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the production shift.

## Tips

* Ensure that the data entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If multiple machines were used during the production shift, select all applicable machine types in the production-shift-id field.
* If any issues or anomalies occurred during the production shift, include them in the notes field.
