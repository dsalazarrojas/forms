# Daily Gardening Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Gardening Report is a form designed to gather information about the state of a garden on a specific day. It helps gardeners track their progress, report on plant health, and document weather conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Gardening Tasks" page with any tasks you completed on that day, such as watering, pruning, or planting.
2. On the "Plant Health" page, select all the plants that are healthy and indicate which ones are not.
3. In the "Site Details" section, describe any notable site conditions, such as soil moisture or sunlight exposure.
4. On the "Garden Area" page, select whether the garden area is affected by pests or not.
5. If you have other areas in the garden that are not listed, describe them in the "Other (please specify)" field.
6. Check the weather conditions on the "Weather" page and report any notable weather events.
7. Finally, indicate whether any pest control measures were taken on the "Pest Control" page.
8. If you have other pest control measures not listed, describe them in the "Other (please specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Gardening Tasks
- **Gardening Tasks** (`text`, required/optional): Describe any tasks you completed on that day, such as watering, pruning, or planting.

### Plant Health
- **Plant Health** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the plants that are healthy. Note that plants that are not healthy will be indicated automatically.

### Site Details
- **Site Details** (`text`, required/optional): Describe any notable site conditions, such as soil moisture or sunlight exposure.

### Garden Area
- **Garden Area** (`select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether the garden area is affected by pests.

### Other (please specify)
- **Other (please specify)** (`text`, required/optional): If you have other areas in the garden that are not listed, describe them here.

### Pest Control
- **Pest Control** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether any pest control measures were taken.

### Other (please specify)
- **Other (please specify)** (`text`, required/optional): If you have other pest control measures not listed, describe them here.

### Weather
- **Weather** (`date`, required/optional): Check the weather conditions on this day.

### Other (please specify)
- **Other (please specify)** (`text`, required/optional): If you have other weather-related observations not listed, describe them here.

### Tasks Completed
- **Tasks Completed** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether any tasks were completed on this day.

## Tips
- Make sure to report all tasks completed, including those that may not be explicitly listed in the form.
- Be as specific as possible when describing site conditions and weather events.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
- This form is intended to be a record of your daily activities, so be as detailed as possible.
