# Renewable Energy Utilization Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Utilization Report Form is used to collect data on the utilization of renewable energy sources within a company. This form helps to gather information on the type and amount of energy used, as well as other relevant details such as the company name and email. The form is designed to be completed by a company representative and is used to generate a report that can be used for further analysis and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the "User Name" field.
2. Select the "Report Start Date" and "Report End Date" fields to specify the date range for which you are reporting on.
3. Indicate whether each energy source type (e.g. solar, wind, hydro) is used by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Energy Source Type" field.
4. Enter the total amount of each energy source used in the "Energy Source Amount" field.
5. Specify the unit of measurement for each energy source in the "Energy Source Unit" field.
6. Enter your Company Name and Company Email in the "Company Name" and "Company Email" fields respectively.
7. Enter your Company Phone number in the "Company Phone" field.
8. Add any additional notes about the report in the "Report Notes" field.
9. Select the tool assigned to you to complete this form in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. If applicable, enter a Form ID in the "Form ID" field.
11. Enter a Category and Description for this report in the "Category" and "Description" fields respectively.
12. Specify the name of the file you would like to output in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your User Name to identify yourself as the person completing the report.
* **Report Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the start date of the period for which you are reporting on.
* **Report End Date** (Date, Optional): Select the end date of the period for which you are reporting on.
* **Energy Source Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether each energy source type is used by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Energy Source Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of each energy source used.
* **Energy Source Unit** (Select One, Optional): Specify the unit of measurement for each energy source (e.g. kWh, MWh, etc.).
* **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your Company Name.
* **Company Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your Company Phone number.
* **Report Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the report.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Select the tool assigned to you to complete this form.
* **Form ID** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter a Form ID.
* **Category** (Text, Optional): Enter a Category for this report.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a Description for this report.
* **Output File** (Text, Optional): Specify the name of the file you would like to output.
* **Assigned Form** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the form you are completing.
