# Depot Receival Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Depot Receival Report is used to report the receipt of goods or items delivered to a depot. This form ensures accurate and efficient tracking of inventory and deliveries, and may be used to record any discrepancies or issues related to the delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Delivery Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date the delivery was received at the depot.
2. Enter the **Delivery ID**. This is a unique identifier assigned to the delivery.
3. Enter the **Driver ID**. This is the ID of the driver who delivered the goods or items.
4. Select the **Delivery Status**:
	* **Delivered**: The delivery was received and recorded correctly.
	* **Delayed**: The delivery was received, but there were issues or problems with it.
	* **Lost**: The delivery was lost or did not arrive at the depot.
5. Enter any **Discrepancies** that occurred during the delivery. This is an optional field for reporting any issues or problems with the delivery.
6. Enter **Remarks** about the delivery. This is an optional field for recording any additional comments or information about the delivery.
7. Enter the **Warehouse ID**. This is a unique identifier assigned to the warehouse where the goods or items were received.
8. Enter the **Signature ID**. This is the ID of the signature attached to the delivery documentation.
9. Enter the **Signature Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date the delivery documentation was signed.
10. Enter the **Delivery Person ID**. This is the ID of the person who received the delivery at the depot.
11. Enter **Remarks 2**, **Remarks 3**, and **Remarks 4**. These are optional fields for recording any additional comments or information about the delivery.
12. Enter the **Warehouse Location**. This is an optional field for recording the location within the warehouse where the goods or items were received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the delivery was received at the depot.
- **Delivery ID** (`delivery_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier assigned to the delivery.
- **Driver ID** (`driver_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the driver who delivered the goods or items.
- **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the delivery:
	* **Delivered**: The delivery was received and recorded correctly.
	* **Delayed**: The delivery was received, but there were issues or problems with it.
	* **Lost**: The delivery was lost or did not arrive at the depot.
- **Discrepancies** (`discrepancies`, text, required/optional): Enter any discrepancies or issues that occurred during the delivery. This is an optional field.
- **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the delivery. This is an optional field.
- **Warehouse ID** (`warehouse_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier assigned to the warehouse where the goods or items were received.
- **Signature ID** (`signature_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the signature attached to the delivery documentation.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the delivery documentation was signed.
- **Delivery Person ID** (`delivery_person_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of the person who received the delivery at the depot.
- **Warehouse Location** (`warehouse_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location within the warehouse where the goods or items were received.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Take note of any discrepancies or issues that occurred during the delivery and record them in the **Discrepancies** field.
* Use the **Delivery Status** field to indicate the current status of the delivery.
* Enter any additional comments or information about the delivery in the **Remarks** fields.
* Complete all relevant fields to ensure accurate and efficient tracking of inventory and deliveries.
