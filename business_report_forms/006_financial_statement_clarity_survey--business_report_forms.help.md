# Financial Statement Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the clarity of financial statements, helping stakeholders evaluate and improve the financial reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Answer the rating questions (e.g., "How clear is the financial statement?" and "How clear is the financial statement compared to last year?") by selecting a number from 1-5, where 1 indicates "not clear at all" and 5 indicates "very clear".
*   Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" or "additional comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How clear is the financial statement?** (`rating_1`, number, required: false):
    The rating question assesses how well the financial statement conveys information. Choose a number that reflects how clear or unclear you find the statement.
*   **How easy is it to understand the financial statement?** (`rating_2`, number, required: false):
    This question evaluates the ease of understanding the financial statement. Select a number that represents how easy or difficult you find it to comprehend the statement.
*   **Comments** (`comments_1`, text, required: false):
    Provide any additional comments or feedback about the financial statement in this field.
*   **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`, text, required: false):
    Enter any extra comments or insights related to the financial statement.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false):
    Enter the date on which the feedback was submitted.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false):
    Select the time of day when the feedback was submitted.
*   **How clear is the financial statement compared to last year?** (`rating_3`, number, required: false):
    This question compares the clarity of the current financial statement with that of the previous year. Choose a number that reflects how clear or unclear you find the current statement compared to last year.
*   **Additional Comments 2** (`comments_3`, text, required: false):
    Add any extra comments or observations about the comparison with last year's financial statement.
*   **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, required: false):
    Enter the date when the feedback was submitted for comparison.
*   **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, required: false):
    Select the time of day when the feedback was submitted for comparison.

## Tips

*   Be honest and objective when answering the rating questions.
*   Provide specific examples or reasons for your comments and feedback.
*   Ensure you answer all the questions and fields as completely as possible.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy before submission.
