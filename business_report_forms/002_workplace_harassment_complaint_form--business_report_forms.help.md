# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Complaint Form is a tool for employees to report incidents of workplace harassment. It helps the HR department to gather information and investigate complaints in a systematic and fair manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including the date, time, and location where it occurred.
2. **Harassment Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as you can, including the actions taken by the harasser and the impact on you.
3. **Harassment Type**: Select the type of harassment you experienced (e.g., Harassment, Bullying, Discrimination, Retaliation, Hostile Work Environment).
4. **Harasser**: Identify the person or group responsible for the harassment (e.g., Manager, Supervisor, Coworker, Customer, Vendor, Client, Other).
5. **Action Taken**: Describe any actions you took or are taking to address the issue.
6. **Resolution**: Indicate whether the issue has been resolved or not.
7. **Workplace Harassment Policy**: Check if your workplace has an established policy on harassment.
8. **Reported To**: Identify who you reported the incident to (e.g., HR, Supervisor, Manager, Security, Other).
9. **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
10. **Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
11. **Location**: Identify where the incident occurred (e.g., Office, Home, Remote, Other).
12. **Witness**: Identify any witnesses to the incident (e.g., Manager, Supervisor, Coworker, Customer, Vendor, Client, Other).
13. **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
14. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
15. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
16. **Address**: Enter your work address.
17. **City**: Enter your city.
18. **State**: Select your state.
19. **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
20. **Reporting Party**: Provide your name and title.
