# Store Update Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on store updates for reporting and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Product Name field with the name of the product you want to report on.
2. Select the appropriate category from the Product Category dropdown menu.
3. Enter the product price in the Product Price field. If the product is free, leave this field blank.
4. Enter the quantity of the product being updated.
5. Choose a date for the Date of Update field using the calendar tool provided.
6. Select a time for the Time of Update field using the clock tool provided.
7. Choose who made the update by selecting from the options in the Updated By dropdown menu.
8. Optionally, enter any comments in the Comments field.
9. Enter the Email of the person making the update.
10. Enter the Phone number of the person making the update.
11. Enter the Address of the person making the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Name (text)
Enter the name of the product being updated.
### Product Category (select_one)
Select the category of the product (e.g. Clothing, Electronics, Furniture).
### Product Price (number)
Enter the price of the product. If the product is free, leave this field blank.
### Quantity (number)
Enter the quantity of the product being updated.
### Date of Update (date)
Choose the date of the update using the calendar tool provided.
### Time of Update (time)
Select the time of the update using the clock tool provided.
### Updated By (select_one)
Select who made the update (e.g. Sales Person, Manager, Owner).
### Comments (note)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the update.
### Email (email)
Enter the email of the person making the update.
### Phone (text)
Enter the phone number of the person making the update.
### Address (text)
Enter the address of the person making the update.
