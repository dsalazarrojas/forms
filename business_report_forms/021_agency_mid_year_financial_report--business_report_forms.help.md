# Agency Mid Year Financial Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agency Mid Year Financial Report is a document used to track and analyze the financial performance of agencies throughout the year. It provides an update on the current financial situation, including revenue, expenses, and cash position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the agency's name in the "Agency name" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period in the "Reporting period start" and "Reporting period end" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the total revenue generated during the reporting period in the "Total revenue" field.
4. Enter the total expenses incurred during the reporting period in the "Total expenses" field.
5. Calculate the net income by subtracting the total expenses from the total revenue.
6. Enter the current cash position or available liquidity in the "Current cash position" field.
7. If there are any significant variances or notes, enter them in the "Budget variances summary" field.
8. Provide management commentary and next steps in the "Notes and recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency name (Agency name)**: Enter the legal business name of the agency.
* **Reporting period start (Reporting period start)**: Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting period end (Reporting period end)**: Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Total revenue (Total revenue)**: Enter the total revenue generated during the reporting period.
* **Total expenses (Total expenses)**: Enter the total expenses incurred during the reporting period.
* **Net income (Net income)**: The sum of total revenue minus total expenses.
* **Budget variances summary (Budget variances summary)**: Explain any significant variances or discrepancies.
* **Current cash position (Current cash position)**: Enter the current cash or available liquidity.
* **Notes and recommendations (Notes and recommendations)**: Provide management commentary and next steps.
