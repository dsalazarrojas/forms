# transportation_efficiency_metrics_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "transportation_efficiency_metrics_report_form" is designed to collect data on vehicle usage, fuel consumption, and other efficiency metrics to help users understand and analyze their transportation operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the relevant fields with the desired information. You can choose from a variety of metrics, including vehicle usage, fuel consumption, and more. Not all fields are required, so you can fill out as much or as little information as you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Usage** (`vehicle_usage`, number, optional): Enter the number of vehicles used for your transportation operation.
* **Fuel Consumption** (`fuel_consumption`, number, optional): Enter the amount of fuel consumed by your vehicles.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of vehicles used in your operation, such as Car, Truck, or Bike.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of fuel used in your operation, such as Gas, Diesel, or Electric.
* **Usage Frequency** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often your vehicles are used, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Vehicle Capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the capacity of your vehicles.
* **Fuel Efficiency** (`fuel_efficiency`, number, optional): Enter the fuel efficiency of your vehicles.
* **Average Miles Driven** (`average_miles_driven`, number, optional): Enter the average miles driven by your vehicles.
* **Average MPH** (`average_mph`, number, optional): Enter the average miles per hour driven by your vehicles.
