# weekly_objectives_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly objectives form is designed to collect information on the objectives set by employees for the week. This form helps employees to set clear goals and track their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Employee** field with your name or identifier.
2. Enter the **Week Starting Date** and **Week End Date** to specify the period for which you are setting objectives.
3. Select one or more **Objectives** from the available options that you intend to achieve during the week.
4. Choose the status of your **Objective Status** from the available options to track your progress.
5. Add any additional **Notes** you have for your objectives in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or identifier as an employee.
* **Week Starting Date** (`week_starting_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the week starts.
* **Week End Date** (`week_end_date`, text, required/optional): Enter the date when the week ends.
* **Objectives** (`objectives`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more objectives that you intend to achieve during the week.
	+ Options: Objective 1, Objective 2, Objective 3, Objective 4, Objective 5
* **Objective Status** (`objective_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your objectives from the available options.
	+ Options: Not Started, In Progress, On Track, Complete, At Risk
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes you have for your objectives.

## Tips

* Make sure to select one or more objectives that are relevant to your work during the week.
* Choose the correct status for each objective to accurately track your progress.
* Use the **Notes** field to provide any additional context or details for your objectives.
* Remember to review and update your objectives regularly to ensure you are on track to achieve them.
