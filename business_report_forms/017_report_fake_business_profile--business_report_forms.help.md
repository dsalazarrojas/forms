# Report Fake Business Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for reporting fake or suspicious business profiles, helping maintain a trustworthy community on online platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category for the reported profile (Business Report Forms or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the reason for reporting the fake profile.
3. Specify the report type (Suspended Listing or Other).
4. Enter the fake business profile's URL, if applicable.
5. If a screenshot is available, provide the URL where it can be found.
6. Enter a reason for reporting the profile (e.g., fake account, scam, etc.).
7. Update the reporting status to New, In Progress, or Resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, Select One, required): Choose the category for the reported profile, either Business Report Forms or Other.
* **Description** (`Description`, Text, required): Briefly explain the reason for reporting the profile. This helps the platform's moderation team understand why the profile was flagged.
* **Report Type** (`Report Type`, Select One, required): Specify the report type as Suspicious Listing or Other to help the platform assess the report's severity.
* **Fake Business Profile URL** (`Fake Business Profile URL`, Text, optional): Enter the URL of the fake business profile, if available.
* **Screenshot URL** (`Screenshot URL`, Text, optional): Provide the URL where the screenshot can be found, if applicable.
* **Reason for Reporting** (`Reason for Reporting`, Text, required): Enter a brief explanation of why the profile was flagged as fake (e.g., fake account, scam, etc.).
* **Reporting Status** (`Reporting Status`, Select One, required): Update the reporting status to New, In Progress, or Resolved to keep the platform's moderation team informed about the report's status.
