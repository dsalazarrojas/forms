# Weekly Sales Activity Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Activity Report is a form used to track and record sales activity for the week. This information will be used to analyze sales performance and make informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your sales representative's name (Rep) in the designated field.
2. Enter the territory they are working in.
3. Input the weekly target sales figure (Weekly Target).
4. Enter the actual sales figure for the week (Weekly Actuals).
5. Provide any key challenges that were faced during the week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rep** (Rep, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales representative.
* **Territory** (Territory, text, optional): Enter the territory that this sales representative is working in.
* **Weekly Target** (Weekly Target, number, optional): Enter the weekly target sales figure for this representative.
* **Weekly Actuals** (Weekly Actuals, number, optional): Enter the actual sales figure achieved by this representative for the week.
* **Key Challenges** (Key Challenges, text, optional): Provide any key challenges that were faced during the week, such as obstacles or concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately input all information for each field.
* Double-check for accuracy to ensure data quality.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or sales manager.
* Ensure to save your completed form for record-keeping purposes.
