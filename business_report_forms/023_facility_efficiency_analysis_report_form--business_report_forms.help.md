# Facility Efficiency Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on facility efficiency, covering various aspects such as energy consumption, space utilization, and maintenance costs. The form aims to provide a comprehensive analysis of facility performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter general information about the facility in the **General Information** field.
2. Provide details about the facility in the **Facility Details** field.
3. Record the energy usage in the **Energy Usage** field.
4. Enter space utilization data in the **Space Utilization** field.
5. Enter energy consumption data in the **Energy Consumption** field.
6. Specify maintenance costs in the **Maintenance Costs** field.
7. Allocate spaces in the **Space Allocation** field by selecting one or multiple options (e.g., Office 1, Office 2, Office 3).
8. Enter energy efficiency data in the **Energy Efficiency** field.
9. Schedule maintenance in the **Maintenance Schedule** field by selecting one of the options (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual).
10. Enter space utilization percentage data in the **Space Utilization Percentage** field.
11. Record energy costs in the **Energy Costs** field.
11. Record maintenance bills in the **Maintenance Bills** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (text): Enter a brief description of the facility.
* **Facility Details** (text): Provide more details about the facility, such as its location and size.
* **Energy Usage** (number): Record the total energy used by the facility.
* **Space Utilization** (number): Record the total space used by the facility.
* **Energy Consumption** (number): Record the energy consumed by the facility per unit time.
* **Maintenance Costs** (number): Record the maintenance costs associated with the facility.
* **Space Allocation** (select multiple): Select the spaces allocated in the facility.
