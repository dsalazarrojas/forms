# Weekly Experiment Findings Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Experiment Findings Report Form is designed to help team members document and report their experiment outcomes, analysis, and progress on a weekly basis. This form aims to gather valuable insights and findings from experiments conducted within a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the week(s) this report applies to by choosing from the options provided in the "Week" field.
2. Choose the outcome of the experiment from the "Experiment Outcome" field.
3. Provide a detailed analysis of the experiment in the "Experiment Analysis" field.
4. List the team members who participated in the experiment in the "Team Members" field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. If required, select the corresponding week(s) and team members.
7. Fill in the "Submit Date" field with the date the report is being submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Experiment Findings Report** (`page_1`): This is the main section where you can describe the experiment findings in detail.
* **Experiment Outcome** (`experiment_outcome`): Choose from the options to select the outcome of the experiment, which can be Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Experiment Analysis** (`experiment_analysis`): Provide a detailed analysis of the experiment, including any insights or observations.
* **Weekly Progress** (`progress`): Note your progress on the experiment, including any challenges or successes.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the experiment.
* **Week** (`week`): Select the week(s) this report applies to, which can be 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): List the team members who participated in the experiment, including their names or roles.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any other comments or notes about the experiment.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`): Fill in the date the report is being submitted, which helps track when the report was completed.
