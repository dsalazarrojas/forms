# Alcohol Sales Summary Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alcohol Sales Summary Report is a tool used to track and report on a business's alcohol sales, incidents, and compliance status during a specific reporting period. This report helps businesses to identify areas of improvement and ensure compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review and verify the data before submitting the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report ID** (1, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for this report.
* **Business Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of your business establishment.
* **Business License Number** (3, text, required): Enter your business's alcohol license number.
* **Business Address** (4, text, required): Enter your business's full address.
* **Report Period Start Date** (5, date, required): Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Report Period End Date** (6, date, required): Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Business Type** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of establishment (e.g., Restaurant, Bar/Pub, Liquor Store, etc.).
* **Total Beer Sales** (8, number, required): Enter the total beer sales amount during the reporting period.
* **Beer Units Sold** (9, number, required): Enter the number of beer units sold during the reporting period.
* **Total Wine Sales** (10, number, required): Enter the total wine sales amount during the reporting period.
* **Wine Units Sold** (11, number, required): Enter the number of wine units sold during the reporting period.
* **Total Spirits Sales** (12, number, required): Enter the total spirits sales amount during the reporting period.
* **Spirits Units Sold** (13, number, required): Enter the number of spirits units sold during the reporting period.
* **Other Alcohol Sales** (14, number, optional): Enter sales of other alcoholic products.
* **Total Alcohol Sales** (15, number, required): Enter the sum of all alcohol sales during the reporting period.
* **Total Alcohol Transactions** (16, number, required): Enter the number of alcohol transactions during the reporting period.
* **Alcohol Tax Collected** (17, number, required): Enter the total tax collected on alcohol sales.
* **Peak Sales Day** (18, select_one, required): Select the day with the highest sales.
* **Peak Sales Hour** (19, select_one, optional): Select the hour with the highest sales.
* **Inventory Discrepancies** (20, select_one, required): Report any discrepancies found in the inventory.
* **Alcohol-Related Incidents** (21, select_one, required): Report any incidents during the reporting period.
* **Incident Details** (22, text, optional): Describe any incidents in detail.
* **Compliance Issues** (23, select_one, required): Report any compliance issues during the reporting period.
* **Staff Training Completed** (24, select_one, required): Report the status of alcohol service training for staff.
* **Report Prepared By** (25, text, required): Enter the name of the person preparing this report.
* **Preparation Date** (26, date, required): Enter the date this report was prepared.
* **Additional Notes** (27, text, optional): Enter any additional relevant information.

Note: If a field has the "required" attribute, it must be filled in for the report to be complete. If a field has the "optional" attribute, it can be left blank.
