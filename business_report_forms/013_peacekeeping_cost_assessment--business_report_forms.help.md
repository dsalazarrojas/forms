# Peacekeeping Cost Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data for a Peacekeeping Cost Assessment, which is likely a form used to document and track the costs associated with peacekeeping operations or missions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by filling in the mission details, including the Estimated Costs, Actual Costs, and Mission Details.
2.  Choose the Cost Comparison options for each mission, if applicable.
3.  Enter the dates and times for each mission.
4.  Provide the email and phone number for each mission contact.
5.  Enter any additional mission details as required.
6.  Repeat the process for each mission, as there are multiple pages for different missions.
7.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
