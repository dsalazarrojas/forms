# Product Branding Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Branding Performance Report Form is a tool used to assess the branding performance of a product. It helps to gather information about the product's strengths and weaknesses, potential improvement ideas, and other relevant details. This form is designed to provide a standardized and structured way to evaluate a product's branding performance and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Brand Name**: Type the name of the product being assessed in this field.
2. **Brand Category**: Type a brief description of the product category or industry it belongs to.
3. **Strengths**: List the product's strengths, such as its features, benefits, or unique selling points. 
4. **Weaknesses**: List the product's weaknesses, such as its limitations, flaws, or areas for improvement. 
5. **Strengths List**: Select the strengths you've listed in the previous step from the dropdown menu.
6. **Weaknesses List**: Select the weaknesses you've listed in the previous step from the dropdown menu.
7. **Improvement Ideas**: Enter any ideas you have for improving the product's branding performance.
8. **Tool Used**: Select the tool used to assess the product's branding performance from the dropdown menu.
9. **Output Filename**: Enter a filename for the report output.
10. **Output Format**: Select the desired format for the report output (CSV, Excel, PDF, or Word Document).
11. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
12. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
13. **Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
14. **Time**: Enter the time of the assessment.
15. **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
16. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Name** (text): Enter the name of the product being assessed.
* **Brand Category** (text): Enter a brief description of the product category or industry it belongs to.
* **What are the strengths?** (note): List the product's strengths, such as its features, benefits, or unique selling points.
* **What are the weaknesses?** (note): List the product's weaknesses, such as its limitations, flaws, or areas for improvement.
* **Strengths List** (select multiple): Select the strengths you've listed from the dropdown menu.
* **Weaknesses List** (select multiple): Select the weaknesses you've listed from the dropdown menu.
* **Improvement Ideas** (text): Enter any ideas you have for improving the product's branding performance.
* **Tool Used** (select one): Select the tool used to assess the product's branding performance.
* **Output Filename** (text): Enter a filename for the report output.
* **Output Format** (select one): Select the desired format for the report output (CSV, Excel, PDF, or Word Document).
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
* **Additional Notes** (text): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the assessment.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
