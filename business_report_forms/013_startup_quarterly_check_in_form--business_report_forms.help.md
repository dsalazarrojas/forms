# Startup Quarterly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Tips

### 1. **Quarterly Progress** 
* **Quarterly Progress** (`quarterly_progress`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the main developments, successes, and challenges faced by your startup in the last quarter. This could include notable events, milestones, customer feedback, or any other notable occurrences that have impacted your progress.

### 2. **Current Status** 
* **Current Status** (`current_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the following options that best describes your current status:
	+ Option 1: Active Development
	+ Option 2: On Hold
* If you select "Other (please specify)", describe your current status in this field.

### 3. **Current Challenges** 
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any ongoing or recent challenges that have impacted your progress or growth. Be as specific as possible to help us better understand the obstacles you're facing.

### 4. **Key Achievements** 
* **Key Achievements** (`key_achievements`, `text`, required/optional): List any notable accomplishments, milestones, or successes your startup has reached in the last quarter. This could include product launches, team additions, or significant revenue milestones.
