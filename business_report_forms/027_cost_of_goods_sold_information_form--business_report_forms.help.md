# Cost Of Goods Sold Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) information form is used to document the cost associated with the sale of an inventory item. This form helps in accurately calculating the COGS, which is an essential component of the accounting process for businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
*   Enter the inventory item's details.
*   Provide the quantity purchased and quantity sold for the item.
*   Enter the cost per unit (optional) to calculate the total cost.
*   Enter the total cost, which is calculated based on the quantity purchased and cost per unit.
*   Enter any relevant notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Inventory Item
*   **Inventory Item** (`inventory_item_id`, text, required): Enter the name of the inventory item being sold.
*   **Quantity Purchased** (`quantity_purchased`, number, required): Enter the number of units purchased for the item.
*   **Quantity Sold** (`quantity_sold`, number, required): Enter the number of units sold for the item.
*   **Cost Per Unit** (`cost_per_unit`, number, optional): Enter the cost per unit (optional, but recommended for accurate calculation).
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): This field is calculated based on the quantity purchased and cost per unit.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold** (`cogs`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the COGS entry.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold** (`cost_of_goods_sold`, date, required): Select the date when the COGS was recorded.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold Amount** (`cost_of_goods_sold_amount`, number, required): This is the total COGS for the sale.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold Notes** (`cost_of_goods_sold_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the COGS entry.
*   **Total Cogs** (`total_cogs`, number, required): This is the calculated total COGS.
*   **Total Cogs Amount** (`total_cogs_amount`, number, required): This field is calculated based on the quantity purchased and sold.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold Total** (`cost_of_goods_sold_total`, number, required): This is the total COGS for the sale.
*   **Cost Of Goods Sold Total Amount** (`cost_of_goods_sold_total_amount`, number, required): This field is calculated based on the total COGS for the sale.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the form.

This form helps in tracking the cost associated with the sale of an inventory item, ensuring accurate accounting and financial reporting.
