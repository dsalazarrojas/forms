# Daily Fish Unloading Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Fish Unloading Report is a form used to collect information about fish unloading reports from fishermen or fishing vessels. It helps to track and manage fish landings, including the species of fish unloaded, quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Unloading Date**: Enter the date on which the fish was unloaded from the vessel. Select it using the calendar tool provided.
2. **Vessel**: Enter the name of the fish vessel. This information helps to identify the source of the unloading report.
3. **Landing Site**: Enter the location where the fish was unloaded. This could be a specific dock or port.
4. **Fish Species**: Select the type of fish unloaded from the options provided (e.g., Atlantic Cod, Halibut, Herring, etc.). Multiple species can be selected if applicable.
5. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of fish unloaded. This field is likely a numeric value.
6. **Condition**: Provide a brief description of the condition of the fish unloaded (e.g., fresh, frozen, spoiled, etc.).
7. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the unloading report.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address. This is likely used for contacting you for further information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unloading Date** (`unloading_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the fish was unloaded.
* **Vessel** (`vessel`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the fish vessel.
* **Landing Site** (`landing_site`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the fish was unloaded.
* **Fish Species** (`fish_species`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of fish unloaded from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of fish unloaded.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the condition of the fish unloaded.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the unloading report.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields, especially the **Fish Species** field if you have multiple species to report.
* Double-check the **Quantity** field to ensure accuracy.
* Be as detailed as possible in the **Notes** field to provide useful information for fisheries management.
* Use the **Email** field to ensure you can be contacted for further clarification or questions.
