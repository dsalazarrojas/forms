# Annual Business Report Filing Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Annual Business Report Filing Intake Form is designed to gather the necessary information for the annual report filing process. This form is used to ensure that all required fields and documents are submitted correctly to avoid delays or issues during the filing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Company Information section:
	* Enter the Company Name in the Company Name field.
	* Enter the Company Registration Number in the Company Registration Number field.
2. Provide Contact Information:
	* Enter your name in the Contact Person Name field.
	* Enter your position in the Contact Person Title field.
	* Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
	* Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field (optional).
3. Complete the Report Details section:
	* Select the Reporting Period in the Reporting Period field.
	* Select the Type of Report Being Filed in the Report Type field.
	* Enter the Filing Deadline Date in the Filing Deadline Date field.
4. Provide Filing Information:
	* Select the Filing Jurisdiction in the Filing Jurisdiction field.
	* Check the Financial Statements Ready field to indicate the status of your financial statements.
5. Upload Supporting Documents:
	* Select the status of your supporting documents in the Supporting Documents Status field.
	* Select all required documents from the Required Documentation Checklist field.
6. Review and Approve:
	* Check the Internal Review Status field to ensure internal review has been completed.
	* Enter the Approval Signatory Name field with the person responsible for sign-off.
7. Add Additional Information:
	* Enter any special considerations or notes in the Special Considerations or Notes field (optional).
	* Enter any additional information in the Additional Information field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Company Registration Number** (Optional): Enter the government registration number of your company.
* **Fiscal Year End Date** (Required): Enter the date when your fiscal year ends.
* **Contact Person Name** (Required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Person Title** (Optional): Enter your position.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Reporting Period** (Required): Select the fiscal year being reported on.
* **Report Type** (Required): Select the type of report being filed (e.g. Annual Report, Financial Statements, etc.).
* **Filing Deadline Date** (Required): Enter the date by which the report must be filed.
* **Filing Jurisdiction** (Required): Select the location where the report will be filed (e.g. Federal, State, etc.).
* **Financial Statements Ready** (Required): Check if your financial statements are prepared.
* **Supporting Documents Status** (Required): Select the status of your supporting documents.
* **Required Documentation Checklist** (Optional): Select all required documents that have been prepared.
* **Internal Review Status** (Required): Check if internal review has been completed.
* **Approval Signatory Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for sign-off.
* **Special Considerations or Notes** (Optional): Enter any special considerations or notes.
* **Additional Information** (Optional): Enter any additional information or details.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors or missing information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for general use and may not be applicable to all businesses or situations. It is your responsibility to ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
