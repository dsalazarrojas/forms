# Seasonal Trend Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Trend Report Form is designed to collect information from users about their seasonal trend report preferences. The purpose of this form is to gather details on which season they are interested in, their role in the company, the type of data they want to collect, and their preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the season you are interested in from the options provided.
2.  Indicate your role in the company from the options given.
3.  Specify the type of data you want to collect.
4.  Enter your email address.
5.  Decide if you need a phone number.
6.  Choose your preferred contact method.
7.  Select the date you want to receive the report.
8.  Choose the time of day you want to receive the report.
9.  Enter any additional comments.
10.  Provide any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Which Season? (Select One):** This question helps the system understand which season you are interested in. Choose the correct season from the options provided.
*   **What is Your Role? (Select One):** Please select your role in the company from the options given. This information helps the system understand your requirements.
*   **What Data Are You Looking to Collect? (Text):** Enter the type of data you want to collect. Be as specific as possible.
*   **Seasonal Data Collection Frequency? (Select One):** Choose how often you want to collect seasonal data.
*   **Contact Email Address? (Email):** Enter your email address where you want to receive the report.
*   **Do You Need A Phone Number? (Checkbox):** Check if you need a phone number.
*   **Preferred Contact Method? (Select One):** Please choose how you want to receive the report.
*   **When Do You Want to Receive the Report? (Date):** Enter the date you want to receive the report.
*   **What Time Do You Want to Receive the Report? (Time):** Choose the time of day you want to receive the report.
*   **Additional Comments? (Text):** Enter any additional comments or information you have.
*   **Other Information? (Text):** Provide any other relevant information that you think is necessary.
*   **Additional Details? (Text):** Enter any additional details about your request.
