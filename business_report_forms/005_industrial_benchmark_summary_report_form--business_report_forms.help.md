# industrial_benchmark_summary_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial benchmark summary report form is used to collect data on industrial benchmarks, providing a comprehensive overview of the industrial sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select whether the industrial benchmark exists (`industrial_benchmark`)
2. Enter the name of the company (`company_name`)
3. Select the industry (`industry`)
4. Enter the benchmark date (`benchmark_date`)
5. Enter the benchmark value (`benchmark_value`)
6. Select whether the industrial benchmark is of type 1 or type 2 (`benchmark_type`)
7. Select whether the industrial benchmark unit is 1 or 2 (`benchmark_unit`)
8. Select whether the industrial benchmark frequency is 1 or 2 (`benchmark_frequency`)
9. Enter the company email (`company_email`)
10. Enter the company phone number (`company_phone`)
11. Enter the company website (`company_website`)
12. Enter the company name 2 (if necessary) (`company_name_2`)
13. Enter any additional comments or remarks about the industrial benchmark (`additional_comments`)
14. Enter any notes about the industrial benchmark (`benchmark_notes`)
15. Enter the contact name (`contact_name`)
16. Enter the contact email (`contact_email`)
17. Enter the contact phone number (`contact_phone`)
18. Enter the contact title (`contact_title`)
19. Enter the contact company (`contact_company`)
20. Enter the report date (`report_date`)
21. Enter the report time (`report_time`)
22. Enter the report location (`report_location`)
23. Enter the company name 3 (if necessary) (`company_name_3`)
24. Enter the benchmark value 2 (`benchmark_value_2`)
25. Enter the benchmark value 3 (`benchmark_value_3`)
