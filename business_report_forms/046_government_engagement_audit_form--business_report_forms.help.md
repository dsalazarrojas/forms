# Government Engagement Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Engagement Audit Form is designed to record and document government engagement activities, ensuring transparency and accountability within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant government interactions (e.g., Meeting, Emails, Phone Calls, Other) that occurred during the engagement. 
   (Required: Government Interactions)
2. Provide the meeting details, including the date and time. (Required: Meeting Details, Meeting Date, Meeting Time)
3. Indicate whether the engagement was aligned with government policy. (Required: Policy Alignment)
4. Identify the government officials involved. (Required: Government Officials Involved)
5. Enter the date of submission. (Required: Form Date)
6. Specify the submitted-by details. (Required: Submitted By)
7. Record the submission date. (Required: Submitted On)
8. Select the review status. (Optional: Review Status)
9. Provide any comments or feedback. (Optional: Comments)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Government Interactions** (Select multiple):
	+ Select all interactions that occurred during the engagement, such as Meeting, Emails, Phone Calls, or Other.
* **Meeting Details** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Policy Alignment** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether the engagement was aligned with government policy.
* **Government Officials Involved** (Select Multiple):
	+ Identify the government officials involved in the engagement.
* **Meeting Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (Time):
	+ Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (Text):
	+ Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Audit Results** (Note):
	+ Provide the results of the audit.
* **Next Steps** (Note):
	+ Describe the next steps or actions to take.
* **Auditor Comments** (Note):
	+ Enter any additional comments from the auditor.
* **Auditor Signature** (Text):
	+ Enter the auditor's signature.
* **Auditor Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the auditor's name.
* **Auditor Email** (Email):
	+ Enter the auditor's email address.
* **Government Agency** (Text):
	+ Enter the government agency involved.
* **Auditor Title** (Text):
	+ Enter the auditor's title.
* **Auditor Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the auditor's phone number.
* **Government Office** (Text):
	+ Enter the government office involved.
* **Government Department** (Select One):
	+ Select the government department involved.
* **Form Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of submission.
* **Auditor Organisation** (Text):
	+ Enter the auditor's organisation.
* **Submitted By** (Text):
	+ Enter the submitted-by details.
* **Submitted On** (Date):
	+ Enter the submission date.
* **Review Status** (Select One):
	+ Select the review status (e.g., Not Reviewed, Completed, On Review).
* **Comments** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional comments.
