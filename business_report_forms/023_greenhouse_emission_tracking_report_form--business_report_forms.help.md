# Greenhouse Emission Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on greenhouse gas emissions, reduction actions, and supporting reports. It's intended for use by researchers, scientists, or professionals in the field of environmental or scientific research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Greenhouse Emission Input" field with relevant information about emissions, such as quantity, unit, or other relevant details.
2. In the "Reduction Action Input" field, describe any actions taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
3. If you have any supporting reports or additional information, provide it in the "Supporting Report Input" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Emission Input** (`greenhouse_emission_input`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter information about greenhouse gas emissions, such as the amount of emissions, the unit of measurement (e.g., kg CO2, tons CO2), or other relevant details.
* **Reduction Action Input** (`reduction_action_input`, text, optional): This field is for describing any actions taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, such as implementing energy-efficient measures, increasing renewable energy sources, or other reduction strategies.
* **Supporting Report Input** (`supporting_report_input`, text, optional): This field is for providing any supporting reports or additional information related to the emissions and reduction actions. You can attach relevant documents, images, or links to support your entry.

## Tips
When completing this form, be as detailed and specific as possible, especially in the Greenhouse Emission Input field. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please refer to the form's purpose and instructions above.
