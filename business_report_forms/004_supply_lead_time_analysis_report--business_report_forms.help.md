# Supply Lead Time Analysis Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Lead Time Analysis Report is used to analyze and evaluate supplier performance and lead times. This report helps businesses to identify areas of improvement and optimize their supplier relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above. Enter the necessary data and select the relevant options to gain insights into your supplier's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Lead Times** (1, **supplier_lead_times**, Required/Optional): Enter the supplier lead times for each specified time period.
* **Document Delays** (2, **document_delays**, Required/Optional): Report any document delays experienced by the supplier, if applicable.
* **Delivery Performance** (3, **delivery_performance**, Required/Optional): Assess the delivery performance of the supplier using a numeric scale.
* **Total Lead Time** (4, **total_lead_time**, Required/Optional): Calculate the total lead time by adding the supplier lead times and document delays.
* **Supplier Rating** (5, **supplier_rating**, Required/Optional): Assign a supplier rating based on their performance.
* **Lead Time Analysis** (6, **lead_time_analysis**, Required/Optional): Choose the relevant lead time analysis category from the options provided.
* **Document Status** (7, **document_status**, Required/Optional): Select the current status of the documents received from the supplier.
