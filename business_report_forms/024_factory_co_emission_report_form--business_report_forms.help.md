# Factory CO₂ Emission Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory CO₂ Emission Report Form is used to track and report emissions of CO₂ from factories and industrial sites. The form is intended to provide detailed information on the types and amounts of CO₂ released by a particular factory during specific reporting periods. This form is designed to help factories accurately document their emissions and report them according to regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the reporting periods for which you are reporting emissions (step 1).
2. Enter the factory details, including the name and address of the factory (step 2).
3. Provide the total emissions in tons of CO2 for the selected period (step 3).
4. Choose the year for which the emissions report applies (step 4).
5. Add any additional notes or comments related to the reporting period (step 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Factory Details (FactoryDetails)
* **Factory Details** (`factory_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and address of the factory.

### Reporting Periods (ReportingPeriods)
* **Reporting Periods** (`reporting_periods`, select_one, required/optional): Select the reporting period for which you are reporting emissions. Options include Q1, Q2, and Q3.

### Emission Totals (EmissionTotals)
* **Emission Totals** (`emission_totals`, number, required/optional): Enter the total emissions in tons of CO2 for the selected period.

### Reporting Years (ReportingYears)
* **Reporting Years** (`reporting_years`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the year for which the emissions report applies. Options include 2018, 2019, and 2020.

### Notes (Notes)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the reporting period.
