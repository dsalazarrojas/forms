# investor_milestone_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The investor_milestone_report_form is a tool used to report milestones achieved in investments, allowing investors to track and document key progress, outcomes, and associated materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **investment_update** date in the first field.
2. Provide a brief description of the **milestone_name** in the text field.
3. Enter the **milestone_date** for the milestone.
4. In the **milestone_details** field, provide a detailed description of the milestone.
5. Describe the **milestone_outcomes** achieved in the text field.
6. Enter the **next_steps** to be taken in the text field.
7. Add any relevant **milestone_image** or **milestone_link** associated with the milestone.
8. If applicable, attach any relevant **milestone_document** or **milestone_video**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **investment_update (Date)**: This field is used to indicate the date of the milestone update.
* **milestone_name (Text)**: Describe the milestone in a brief manner.
* **milestone_date (Date)**: Enter the date of the milestone achievement.
* **milestone_details (Text)**: Provide a detailed description of the milestone, including any relevant context or background information.
* **milestone_outcomes (Text)**: Describe the outcomes achieved as a result of the milestone.
* **next_steps (Text)**: Enter the next steps or actions to be taken following the milestone.
* **milestone_image (Text)**: Add any relevant images or attachments related to the milestone.
* **milestone_link (Text)**: Enter any associated links or URLs related to the milestone.
* **milestone_document (Text)**: Attach any relevant documents or files associated with the milestone.
* **milestone_video (Text)**: Attach any relevant videos or media related to the milestone.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary authority and are completing this form for a valid investment.
- Fill in all relevant fields accurately and honestly.
- Attach any supporting materials that are relevant to the milestone.
- Review and update the form regularly for accurate and timely milestone reporting.
