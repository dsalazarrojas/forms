# Hospitality Customer Loyalty Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the loyalty programs implemented within hospitality businesses. It helps hospitality professionals evaluate the effectiveness of their loyalty programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the relevant internal documentation and ensure that your answers accurately reflect the current state of your loyalty program.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes the property ID, date of visit, customer ID, and reviewer's name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide detailed answers to each field, including the loyalty program name, type, status, implementation, target audience, program objectives, key performance indicators, data collection tools, and overall satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_id** (`property_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier of the property where the loyalty program is implemented.
* **date_of_visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit where the audit was conducted.
* **customer_id** (`customer_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the customer associated with the loyalty program.
* **loyalty_program_name** (`loyalty_program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the loyalty program being audited.
* **program_type** (`program_type`, text, required): Describe the type of loyalty program, e.g., rewards, referral, or retention.
* **program_status** (`program_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the program, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **program_implementation** (`program_implementation`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the program is implemented at the property or region level.
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Specify the target audience of the loyalty program.
* **program_objectives** (`program_objectives`, text, optional): Describe the program objectives, e.g., increase customer satisfaction, boost revenue.
* **key_performance_indicators** (`key_performance_indicators`, text, optional): List the key performance indicators used to measure the program's effectiveness.
* **data_collection_tools** (`data_collection_tools`, text, optional): Specify the tools used for data collection, e.g., surveys, focus groups.
* **form_submission_date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **form_submission_user** (`form_submission_user`, text, required): Enter the user who submitted the form.
* **program_goals** (`program_goals`, text, optional): Describe the program goals, e.g., increase customer loyalty, improve customer retention.
* **key_findings** (`key_findings`, text, optional): Highlight any key findings or observations from the audit.
* **areas_for_improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas for improvement within the program.
* **action_items** (`action_items`, text, optional): List any action items or recommendations for future improvements.
* **next_steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline the next steps or follow-up actions.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, optional): Evaluate the overall satisfaction with the program.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations.
* **form_submission_notes** (`form_submission_notes`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the form submission process.
* **review_date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review or audit.
* **reviewer_name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer or auditor.
* **reviewer_email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the reviewer or auditor.
* **reviewer_phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the reviewer or auditor.
