# Monthly Audit Observations Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect observations and comments from auditors during a monthly audit process. It is intended to be completed by auditors to provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Input the title of the audit report in the "Monthly Audit Observations Report" field.
2. Select the department being audited in the "department" field.
3. Input the date of the audit in the "date" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Input the time of the audit in the "time" field in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Select the type of findings made during the audit in the "findings" field.
6. Input any additional comments or observations in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Audit Observations Report** (`monthly_audit_observations_report`, text, required/optional): 
  Input the title of the report.
* **department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): 
  Select the department being audited.
* **date** (`date`, date, required/optional): 
  Input the date of the audit in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **time** (`time`, time, required/optional): 
  Input the time of the audit in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **findings** (`findings`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  Select the type of findings made during the audit.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): 
  Input any additional comments or observations.

## Tips
- Make sure to input accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure the report is comprehensive and useful.
- Complete all fields to provide a complete report.
- Use the format specified in each field to ensure accuracy and consistency.
