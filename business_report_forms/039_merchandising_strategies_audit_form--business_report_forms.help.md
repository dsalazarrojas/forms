# Merchandising Strategies Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchandising Strategies Audit Form is used to collect information about sales performance and merchandising strategies in a store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the correct information.
3. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of this page, if needed.
* **First Page Section** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of this section, if needed.
* **Merchandising Objectives** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the store has merchandising objectives, or 'No' otherwise.
* **First Page Section Items** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more 'Yes' if specific items are used in this section.
* **Store ID** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's ID number, if applicable.
* **Store Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's name.
* **Store Address** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's address.
* **Sales Period** (`8`, `date`, optional): Enter the sales period, e.g., month and year.
* **Audit Date** (`9`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Sales Amount** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter the total sales amount.
* **Sales Target** (`11`, `number`, optional): Enter the sales target amount.
* **Merchandise Category** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the store has merchandise in this category.
* **Merchandise Description** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the merchandise.
* **Sales Channel** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the sales channel, e.g., online, physical store.
* **Other Sales Channel** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter any other sales channels.
* **Merchandising Objectives Items** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more 'Yes' if specific items are used in this section.
* **Merchandising Strategy** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the merchandising strategy, if applicable.
* **Other Merchandising Strategy** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter any other merchandising strategies.
* **Merchandising Tactics** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter merchandising tactics, if applicable.
* **Other Merchandising Tactics** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter any other merchandising tactics.
* **Merchandising Results** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter merchandising results, if applicable.
* **Other Merchandising Results** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter any other merchandising results.
* **Sales Outlet** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the sales outlet, if applicable.
* **Other Sales Outlet** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any other sales outlets.
* **Submitted By** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter who submitted the form.
