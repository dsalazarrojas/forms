# Nutritional Label Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Label Review Report Form is a tool used to document the review of a product's nutritional label, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report form header information.
2. Provide the product name and serving size details.
3. List the ingredients and their quantities.
4. Enter the nutrition facts table and list.
5. Evaluate the label compliance status and provide reasons.
6. Record the review date, time, and details.
7. Select the reviewer's name and role.
8. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form Header** (`1`): Enter a brief description of the report, such as "Nutritional Label Review Report for Product X."
* **Label** (`2`): Enter a brief description of the label, e.g., "Nutritional Facts Label for Product X."
* **Product Name** (`3`): Enter the name of the product being reviewed.
* **Serving Size** (`4`): Enter the serving size of the product, e.g., "1 serving = 100g."
* **Ingredient Name** (`5`): List the ingredients used in the product.
* **Ingredient List** (`6`): List the ingredients in a specific unit, e.g., "per 100g."
* **Serving Size Unit** (`7`): Select the unit for the serving size, e.g., "g" for grams.
* **Nutrition Fact Table** (`8`): Enter the nutrition facts table, including information on calories, fat, sodium, and other essential nutrients.
* **Nutrition Facts** (`9`): Enter any additional nutrition facts not included in the table.
* **Nutrition Facts List** (`10`): List any other essential nutrients or information not included in the table.
* **Label Compliance** (`11`): Select whether the label is compliant or not compliant with regulations and standards.
* **Label Compliance Reason** (`12`): Enter the reason for the label compliance status selection.
* **Review Date** (`13`): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (`14`): Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`15`): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Comments** (`16`): Enter any comments or observations about the review.
* **Review Status** (`17`): Select the status of the review, e.g., completed, in progress, or not started.
* **Review Notes** (`18`): Enter any additional notes about the review.
* **Additional Comments** (`19`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Label Reviewed By** (`20`): Select who reviewed the label, e.g., QA Team, Regulatory Specialist, or Dietitian.
* **Review Version** (`21`): Enter the version of the review, if applicable.
* **Label Reviewed Date** (`22`): Enter the date of the label review.
* **Review Completed By** (`23`): Enter the person who completed the review.
* **Label Reviewed Time** (`24`): Enter the time of the label review.
* **Review Closed Date** (`25`): Enter the date when the review was closed.
