# Industrial Process Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Process Improvement Report Form is designed to collect data about industrial process improvements, helping to identify areas for enhancement and optimize the process efficiency and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and concise information.
2. Choose the correct options for each field from the dropdown menus.
3. If you don't see an option that matches your improvement objective or current process, use the "Other" field to provide a custom description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Industrial Process Improvement Report Form** (`industrial_process_improvement_report_form`): 
This is the title of the form, which is automatically populated based on the page ID.
* **Process Description** (`process_description`): 
Describe the current industrial process, including its purpose and main activities.
* **Improvement Objectives** (`process_improvement_objectives`): 
Choose one or more objectives for the process improvement:
	+ Improvement
	+ Optimization
	+ Efficiency
* **Improvement Scope** (`improvement_scope`): 
Describe the scope of the improvement, including the area to be improved.
* **Other Improvement Scope** (`improvement_scope_other`): 
Use this field to provide a custom description if the above option does not fit your improvement scope.
* **Improvement Objectives** (`improvement_objectives`): 
Choose one or more objectives for the process improvement:
	+ Cost
	+ Time
	+ Quality
* **Other Improvement Objectives** (`improvement_objectives`): 
Use this field to provide a custom description if the above options do not fit your improvement objectives.
* **Target Improvement Area** (`target_improvement_area`): 
Describe the area to be improved in the process.
* **Current Process** (`current_process`): 
Describe the current process, including its purpose and main activities.
* **Other Current Process** (`current_process_other`): 
Use this field to provide a custom description if the above option does not fit your current process.
* **Proposed Process** (`proposed_process`): 
Describe the proposed process, including its purpose and main activities.
* **Other Proposed Process** (`proposed_process_other`): 
Use this field to provide a custom description if the above option does not fit your proposed process.
* **Process Improvement Status** (`process_improvement_status`): 
Choose the status of the process improvement:
	+ In Progress
	+ Complete
* **Comments** (`comments`): 
Provide any additional comments or notes about the process improvement.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): 
Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): 
Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): 
Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted User** (`submitted_user`): 
Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted User Email** (`submitted_user_email`): 
Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted User Phone** (`submitted_user_phone`): 
Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted User Title** (`submitted_user_title`): 
Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Title** (`submitted_by_title`): 
Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
* **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`): 
Choose the completion status of the form:
	+ Completed
	+ Incomplete
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`): 
Provide any additional comments or notes about the process improvement.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`): 
Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`): 
Enter the time the form was submitted.

Please note that all fields marked as "Other" are for providing a custom description if the above options do not fit your improvement objectives or current process.

Tips:
* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the process and its current state before completing this form.
* Double-check your entries to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, use the "Other" option to provide a custom description.
* Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
