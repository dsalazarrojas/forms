## Weekly Retail Performance Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Retail Performance Report form is designed to collect key performance metrics for a specific retail store over a one-week period. This report will help store managers and administrators monitor and analyze the sales performance, inventory levels, and customer foot traffic of the store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of the week by choosing a date within the given date picker. The start date will determine the week for which you want to report the performance metrics.
2. Select the end date of the week by choosing a date within the given date picker. The end date should be the last day of the week for which you are reporting the performance metrics.
3. Select the ID of the retail store from which you are reporting the performance metrics. This will ensure that the correct store's performance is being reported.
4. Enter the location of the retail store. This will help identify the specific store's location and its corresponding performance metrics.
5. Enter the total number of transactions that took place during the week. This will indicate the volume of business activity.
6. Enter the total sales amount for the week. This will give an indication of the store's revenue.
7. Enter the total inventory levels at the end of the week. This will help monitor the store's inventory management.
8. Enter the total foot traffic count for the week. This will indicate the number of customers that visited the store during the week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start date** (Week Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the week for which you are reporting the performance metrics.
* **End date** (Week End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the week for which you are reporting the performance metrics.
* **Store** (Store ID, number, required): Enter the ID of the retail store from which you are reporting the performance metrics.
* **Location** (Store Location, text, required): Enter the location of the retail store.
* **Total transactions** (Total Transactions, number, required): Enter the total number of transactions that took place during the week.
* **Total sales** (Total Sales, number, required): Enter the total sales amount for the week.
* **Total inventory** (Total Inventory, number, required): Enter the total inventory levels at the end of the week.
* **Foot traffic** (Foot Traffic, number, required): Enter the total foot traffic count for the week.

## Tips

- Make sure to select the correct start and end dates for the week you are reporting.
- Ensure that you have access to the correct store's ID and location.
- Double-check the entered values for total transactions, sales, inventory, and foot traffic to ensure accuracy.
- If you need further assistance or clarification, contact your store administrator.
