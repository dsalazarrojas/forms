# Cash Flow Statement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Flow Statement Report Form is a financial report that provides an overview of an organization's cash inflows and outflows over a specific period. It helps in tracking the movement of cash within the organization and is used for financial analysis and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with accurate information.
2. Enter the company name, report date, fiscal year or period, and report prepared by fields.
3. Provide the net income or loss, depreciation and amortization, and other changes in working capital fields.
4. Enter the changes in accounts receivable, inventory, and accounts payable fields.
5. Calculate the net cash from operating activities.
6. Enter the capital expenditures, proceeds from asset sales, investments made, and other investing activities fields.
7. Calculate the net cash from investing activities.
8. Enter the proceeds from debt, debt repayment, equity financing received, and dividends paid fields.
9. Calculate the net cash from financing activities.
10. Enter the total net increase or decrease in cash.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (*): Enter the name of the company or organization.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Fiscal Year or Period**: Enter the fiscal year or period for which the report is being prepared.
* **Report Prepared By**: Enter the name and title of the person preparing the report.
* **Net Income or Loss**: Enter the net income or loss for the given period.
* **Depreciation and Amortization**: Enter the depreciation and amortization for the given period.
* **Changes in Working Capital**: Enter the changes in working capital for the given period.
* **Changes in Accounts Receivable**: Enter the change in accounts receivable for the given period.
* **Change in Inventory**: Enter the change in inventory for the given period.
* **Change in Accounts Payable**: Enter the change in accounts payable for the given period.
* **Net Cash from Operating Activities**: Calculate the net cash from operating activities using the above fields.
* **Capital Expenditures**: Enter the capital expenditures for the given period.
* **Proceeds from Asset Sales**: Enter the proceeds from asset sales for the given period.
* **Investments Made**: Enter the investments made for the given period.
* **Net Cash from Investing Activities**: Calculate the net cash from investing activities using the above fields.
* **Proceeds from Debt**: Enter the proceeds from debt for the given period.
* **Debt Repayment**: Enter the debt repayment for the given period.
* **Equity Financing Received**: Enter the equity financing received for the given period.
* **Dividends Paid**: Enter the dividends paid for the given period.
* **Net Cash from Financing Activities**: Calculate the net cash from financing activities using the above fields.
* **Net Increase or Decrease in Cash**: Calculate the total net increase or decrease in cash using the above fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the calculations for net cash from operating, investing, and financing activities.
* Ensure that the company name and report date are accurate.
* If any field is unsure or unclear, consult with the financial team or the person preparing the report.
* Review the completed form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it for review.
