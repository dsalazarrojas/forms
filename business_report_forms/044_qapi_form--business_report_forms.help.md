# qapi_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The qapi_form is a tool for healthcare providers to document a patient's visit, gathering information about their visit type, duration, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Home visit", "Outpatient visit", or "Inpatient visit" type of visit that the patient underwent.
2. Record the duration of the visit using the "visit_duration" field.
3. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and date of visit.
4. Identify the staff member who performed the visit by entering their name and role in the "staff_member_name" and "staff_member_role" fields.
5. Document the patient's room number.
6. Provide the reason for the visit in the "reason_for_visit" field.
7. Finally, sign and date the form with the "staff_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **form_section** (optional)
* A text field for any additional comments or information about the patient's visit.
### 2. **date_of_visit** (optional)
* The date the patient visited the healthcare facility.
### 3. **time_of_visit** (optional)
* The time the patient visited the healthcare facility.
### 4. **patient_name** (optional)
* The patient's full name.
### 5. **patient_dob** (optional)
* The patient's date of birth.
### 6. **staff_member_name** (optional)
* The name of the healthcare staff member who performed the visit.
### 7. **staff_member_role** (optional)
* The role or position held by the healthcare staff member who performed the visit.
### 8. **patient_room** (optional)
* The number of the patient's room in the facility.
### 9. **visit_type** (select_one)
* The type of visit the patient underwent, which can be "Home visit", "Outpatient visit", or "Inpatient visit".
### 10. **visit_duration** (number)
* The duration of the patient's visit in hours.
### 11. **reason_for_visit** (optional)
* A text field for any reason or purpose of the patient's visit.
### 12. **staff_signature** (optional)
* The healthcare staff member's signature and date of completion.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully to maintain accurate records.
* Double-check for any inconsistencies or discrepancies before submitting the form.
* Review and update the form as needed to reflect any changes or updates to the patient's information.
* Keep the form clean and organized to avoid clutter and ensure easy reference.
