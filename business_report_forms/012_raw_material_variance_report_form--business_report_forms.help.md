# Raw Material Variance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Material Variance Report Form is designed to collect information on variances in raw material received and used in the production process. This form helps quality control and production teams to investigate and resolve material variance issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Begin by filling out the "Material Received" field with a brief description of the material received.
3. Enter the "Material Used" field with the quantity of material used.
4. In the "Usage Variance" field, describe the variance observed.
5. If relevant, provide a "Root Cause Analysis" in the "Root Cause Analysis" field.
6. Document any "Corrective Action" taken to address the issue.
7. Add any additional "Notes" regarding the issue.
8. Select the relevant "Category" (e.g., Manufacturing, Quality, Production).
9. Choose the applicable "Plant" (e.g., HQ, MQ, HQ1, MQ1).
10. Enter the "Date Received" and "Time Received" fields with the actual dates and times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Received** (`material_received`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the material received.
* **Material Used** (`material_used`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of material used.
* **Usage Variance** (`usage_variance`, text, optional): Describe the variance observed.
* **Root Cause Analysis** (`cause_analysis`, text, optional): Provide a detailed analysis of the root cause of the variance.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, optional): Document any corrective actions taken.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the issue.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the relevant category (e.g., Manufacturing, Quality, Production).
* **Plant** (`plant`, select_multiple, optional): Select the applicable plant (e.g., HQ, MQ, HQ1, MQ1).
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the actual date the material was received.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, optional): Enter the actual time the material was received.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to facilitate efficient investigation and resolution of material variance issues.
* Use the "Root Cause Analysis" field to provide a detailed analysis of the root cause of the variance.
* Document all corrective actions taken in the "Corrective Action" field.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional information that may be relevant to the issue.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed. 

This guide is intended to be a general reference to help users complete the form accurately and efficiently. It is not intended to be a substitute for proper business process training or procedure documentation.
