# Payroll Analytics Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request payroll analytics reports that will help in the analysis and optimization of an organization's compensation and benefits packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide company information in the Company Information field.
2. Fill out the Report Information field with the required details.
3. Choose the pay period for which you want to receive the report.
4. Select the report type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
5. Choose the report frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
6. Enter the start and end dates for the report.
7. List the employees for whom you want to receive the report.
8. Provide manager information to ensure that the report is directed to the correct person.
9. Enter additional employee details as required.
10. Finally, click the Submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information**: Enter the company name and any additional information that will help us generate the report.
* **Report Information**: Enter a brief description of the report you are requesting.
* **Pay Period**: Choose the pay period for which you want to receive the report. This will help us generate accurate data.
* **Report Type**: Select the type of report you want to receive (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Report Frequency**: Choose how frequently you want to receive the report (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the report period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the report period.
* **Employee List**: Select the employees for whom you want to receive the report.
* **Manager Information**: Enter the manager's name, email, and phone number to ensure that the report is directed to the correct person.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee for whom you want to receive the report.
* **Department**: Enter the department of the employee.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the employee.
* **Job Category**: Select the job category of the employee.
* **Job Function**: Enter the job function of the employee.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send the form and receive the report.</blockquote>
