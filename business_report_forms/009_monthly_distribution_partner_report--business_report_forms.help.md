## Monthly Distribution Partner Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monthly Distribution Partner Report" form is used to collect sales and inventory data from distribution partners for a specific month. This report helps management review and analyze the performance of each partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **monthly_distributions** field with the total number of distributions made to the partner for the month.
2. Enter the **partner_name** of the partner in the corresponding field.
3. Add the **partner_address** of the partner in the corresponding field.
4. Enter the total **sales** generated by the partner for the month.
5. Set the **sales_target** for the partner for the month (optional).
6. Enter the total **inventory** managed by the partner for the month.
7. Add any comments about the partner in the **partner_comments** field (optional).
8. Select the **assigned_tool** used by the partner for the month from the options provided (options: chatjimmy, other).
9. Indicate if the form has been completed (options: True, False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **monthly_distributions**: Enter the total number of distributions made to the partner for the month. If unsure, leave blank (0) and report back to the partner for accurate data.
* **partner_name**: Enter the name of the partner, as provided by the partner or management.
* **partner_address**: Enter the address of the partner, as provided by the partner or management.
* **sales**: Enter the total sales generated by the partner for the month. This will help in reviewing the performance of the partner.
* **sales_target**: This field is optional and allows setting a target for sales for the month. If left blank, no sales target will be set.
* **inventory**: Enter the total inventory managed by the partner for the month. This will help in reviewing stock levels and supply chain management.
* **partner_comments**: Add any comments about the partner, such as performance issues or concerns.
* **assigned_tool**: Select the tool assigned to the partner from the provided options: chatjimmy or other. If unsure, select "other" and report back to the partner.
* **form_completed**: Indicate if the form has been completed (options: True, False). If left blank, it will be assumed as "False".

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If unsure about any field, contact the partner or management for clarification.
* Ensure all fields are filled accurately to provide a comprehensive report.
