# work_performance_and_resource_usage_report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on employee work performance and resource usage. It helps the business to monitor and analyze employee productivity and resource utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including employee ID, start and end dates, resource ID, and other details.
2. Enter the correct resource type (yes or no) and resource name (if applicable).
3. Provide accurate resource usage hours and resource cost information.
4. Enter the employee's email address and phone number.
5. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Performance** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the employee's work performance.
* **Resource Usage** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Similar to work performance, this field is used to capture any additional information about resource usage.
* **Employee ID** (`3`, `number`, required): This is a unique identifier for the employee, usually a numeric value.
* **Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the period being reported on.
* **End Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the period being reported on.
* **Resource ID** (`6`, `number`, required): This is a unique identifier for the resource, usually a numeric value.
* **Resource Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of resource used (yes or no).
* **Resource Name** (`8`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the resource used.
* **Resource Usage Hours** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours of resource usage.
* **Resource Cost** (`10`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of the resource used.
* **Work Hours** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours worked during the period.
* **Work Cost** (`12`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of work performed during the period.
* **Employee Name** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's name (if not provided, it will default to the employee ID).
* **Department** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's department (if applicable).
* **Location** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the work was performed (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`16`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the report.
* **Email** (`17`, `email`, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's phone number.
