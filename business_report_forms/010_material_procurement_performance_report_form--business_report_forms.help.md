# Material Procurement Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the performance of material suppliers. The goal is to gather data that will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future business relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully and make sure you're providing accurate and honest feedback.
*   Fill out the form with the required information and any additional notes or comments.
*   Make sure to rate each category honestly and based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): This field is used to input any relevant information about the supplier you are providing feedback for.
*   **Supplier Rating** (`supplier_rating`, select_multiple, optional):
    +   Choose from the options "High", "Medium", or "Low" to rate the supplier's performance. This will help us understand how well they met our expectations.
*   **Material Quality** (`material_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the materials provided by the supplier, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Delivery on Time** (`delivery_on_time`, select_one, optional):
    +   Choose from the options "True" or "False" to indicate whether the supplier delivered on time or not.
*   **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate the supplier's communication effectiveness, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Input the total cost of the materials or services provided by the supplier.
*   **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional):
    +   Choose from the options "Excellent", "Good", or "Fair" to rate the overall performance of the supplier.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the supplier.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Input the supplier's email address (if available).
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Input the supplier's phone number (if available).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback about your experience with the supplier.
