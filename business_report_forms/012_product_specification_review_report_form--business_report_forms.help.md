# Product Specification Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a product's specification and its compliance status. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to provide a comprehensive review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the product and identifying any issues or problems you've encountered.
2. Answer the questions on the form truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Be sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Review and update your responses as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Capture (2)** (`number`, optional): Use this field to capture any issues or problems you've encountered during the review process.
* **Improvement Recommendations (3)** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you have any improvement recommendations for the product. Possible options include `Yes` and `No`.
* **Improvement Recommendations Details (6)** (`select_multiple`, required): If you answered `Yes` to the previous question, choose the areas where the improvement recommendations apply (e.g., `Design`, `Functionality`, `Performance`, etc.).
* **Product Compliance (4)** (`number`, optional): Enter the product's compliance status (e.g., 0, 1, 2, etc.).
* **Issue Capture Notes (5)** (`text`, optional): Use this field to provide additional notes or comments about the issue.
* **Review Status (10)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the current review status of the product (e.g., `Not Started`, `In Progress`, `Completed`).
* **Issue Status (11)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the issue (e.g., `Active`, `Resolved`, `Closed`).
* **Product Status (12)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the product (e.g., `Active`, `Inactive`).
* **Review Notes (13)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the review process.
* **Email (14)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (15)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Issue Capture Date (16)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date when the issue was captured.
* **Issue Capture Time (17)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time when the issue was captured.
* **Issue Capture Status (18)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the issue (e.g., `Active`, `Resolved`, `Closed`).
* **Product Capture Status (19)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the product (e.g., `Active`, `Inactive`).
* **Notes (20)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Capture (21)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for capture purposes.
* **Phone Capture (22)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for capture purposes.
* **Product Capture Date (23)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date when the product was captured.
* **Product Capture Time (24)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time when the product was captured.
* **Issue Capture Notes Capture Date (25)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date when the issue capture notes were captured.
