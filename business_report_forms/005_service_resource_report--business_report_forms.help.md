# Service Resource Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Resource Report is a form used to track and report on services and their associated resources, helping to provide valuable information for management and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service resources you want to report on from the "Service Resources" page.
2. Enter the resource IDs, service name, and other relevant details on the subsequent pages.
3. Choose the resource status and availability on the relevant pages.
4. Enter any comments or additional information on the "Comments" page.
5. Review and submit the form to generate the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Resources** (`service_resources`, select_multiple): Choose the services you want to report on from the list of available options.
* **Resource IDs** (`resource_ids`, number): Enter the unique IDs for the selected resources.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, text): Enter the name of the service.
* **Resource Status** (`resource_status`, select_one): Choose the current status of the resource (Active or Inactive).
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`, number): Enter the availability of the resource.
* **Resource Utilization** (`resource_utilization`, number): Enter the utilization of the resource.
* **Resource Utilization Percentage** (`resource_utilization_percentage`, number): Enter the percentage of utilization of the resource.
* **Resource Availability Percentage** (`resource_availability_percentage`, number): Enter the percentage of availability of the resource.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or information about the resource.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date): Displays the date and time the resource was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date): Displays the date and time the resource was last updated.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one): Choose if the resource is assigned to someone (Yes or No).
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_one): Choose if someone is assigned to the resource (Yes or No).
* **Resource Tags** (`resource_tags`, select_multiple): Choose the tags associated with the resource (Yes or No).

Note: The required fields are indicated in bold.
