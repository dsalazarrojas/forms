# Logistics Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about logistical issues that occur during the shipment and delivery process. This form helps to identify and document incidents, provide detailed information, and assign tasks for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "incident_details" field.
2. Enter the date the shipment was due, in the "ship_date" field.
3. If available, enter the shipment time in the "ship_time" field.
4. Provide information about the warehouse involved in the incident, in the "warehouse_details" field.
5. Enter the product ID related to the incident, in the "product_id" field.
6. Select the type of issue that occurred (e.g., "issue types" or "prophets") from the "issue_type" field.
7. Describe the incident in more detail, if necessary, in the "description" field.
8. Indicate whether follow-up actions are required, by selecting "True" or "False" in the "follow_up" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the "notes" field.
10. Enter the email and phone number of the person responsible for the incident in the "email" and "phone" fields, respectively.
11. Select the team member assigned to follow-up on the incident from the "assignee" field.
12. If a follow-up date is required, enter it in the "follow_up_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_details** (required): A brief description of the incident, including details about what happened.
* **ship_date** (required): The date the shipment was due.
* **ship_time** (optional): If available, the time of shipment.
* **warehouse_details** (optional): Information about the warehouse involved in the incident.
* **product_id** (optional): The product ID related to the incident.
* **issue_type** (required): The type of issue that occurred, selected from "issue types" or "prophets".
* **description** (optional): A more detailed description of the incident, if necessary.
* **follow_up** (required): Indicate whether follow-up actions are required.
* **notes** (optional): Additional notes about the incident.
* **email** (optional): The email of the person responsible for the incident.
* **phone** (optional): The phone number of the person responsible for the incident.
* **assignee** (required): The team member assigned to follow-up on the incident.
* **follow_up_date** (optional): If a follow-up date is required, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and guidelines above.
