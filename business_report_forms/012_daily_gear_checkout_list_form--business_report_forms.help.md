# daily_gear_checkout_list_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily gear checkout list form is used to record the daily gear checkout and return of equipment. This form helps in tracking the usage of equipment and ensures that equipment is returned on time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form ID (Field 1)
2. Select the assigned tool (Field 2)
3. Indicate whether the equipment was checked out (Field 3)
4. Enter the name of the equipment (Field 4)
5. Choose the type of equipment (Field 5)
6. Enter the date the equipment was checked out (Field 6)
7. Enter the date the equipment is due to be returned (Field 7)
8. Provide any remarks or comments (Field 8)
9. Enter the person who submitted the form (Field 9)
10. Enter the date the form was submitted (Field 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_id** (Field 1): A unique identifier for the form.
- **assigned_tool** (Field 2): The tool that was assigned to the user for checkout.
- **equipment_id** (Field 3): Indicate whether the equipment was checked out or not.
- **equipment_name** (Field 4): Enter the name of the equipment that was checked out.
- **equipment_type** (Field 5): Select the type of equipment that was checked out.
- **checkout_date** (Field 6): Enter the date the equipment was checked out.
- **return_date** (Field 7): Enter the date the equipment is due to be returned.
- **remarks** (Field 8): Provide any comments or remarks about the equipment checkout or return.
- **submitted_by** (Field 9): Enter the person who submitted the form.
- **submitted_date** (Field 10): Enter the date the form was submitted.
