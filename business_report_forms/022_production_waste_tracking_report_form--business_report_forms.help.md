The purpose of this form is to track production waste, which is essential for identifying and reducing waste during the production process. This form helps to record production waste incidents, including the employee involved, the production site and area, the time and date of the incident, and the actions taken to address the issue. The form also captures data on the quantity and weight of the waste produced, as well as a brief description of the incident. This information helps to identify patterns and trends in production waste, which can inform strategies for improvement and reduce waste in the long run.

When filling out this form, please ensure that you have accurately documented all relevant details of the production waste incident. If you are unsure about any information or need help, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

The form is divided into several sections that guide you through the process. Please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the employee ID of the person who identified or reported the production waste incident.
2.  Enter the date and time of the incident.
3.  Select the production site where the incident occurred.
4.  Enter the production area where the incident occurred.
5.  Select whether the production waste was produced (`Yes` or `No`).
6.  Enter the quantity of waste produced.
7.  Enter the weight of waste produced.
8.  Enter a brief description of the incident.
9.  Select whether action was taken to address the issue (`Yes` or `No`).
10. If action was taken, enter a brief description of the actions taken.
11. Enter the date the issue was signed off.
12. Select whether you have signed off on the issue (`Yes` or `No`).
13. Enter the time you signed off on the issue.
14. Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, **required**): Enter the ID of the employee who identified or reported the production waste incident.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the production waste incident.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time of the production waste incident.
*   **Production Site** (`production_site`, `text`, **required**): Enter the production site where the incident occurred.
*   **Shift** (`shift`, `number`, **required**): Enter the shift during which the incident occurred.
*   **Production Area** (`production_area`, `text`, **required**): Enter the production area where the incident occurred.
*   **Waste Code** (`waste_code`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select `Yes` to indicate that waste was produced during this incident, or `No` if no waste was produced.
*   **Waste Quantity** (`waste_quantity`, `number`, **required**): Enter the quantity of waste produced during this incident.
*   **Waste Weight** (`waste_weight`, `number`, **required**): Enter the weight of waste produced during this incident.
*   **Waste Description** (`waste_description`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief description of the production waste incident.
*   **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `select_one`, **required**): Select `Yes` to indicate that action was taken to address the issue, or `No` if no action was taken.
*   **Action Details** (`action_details`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief description of the actions taken to address the issue.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you signed off on the issue.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, **required**): Select `Yes` to confirm that you have signed off on the issue, or `No` if you have not.
*   **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time you signed off on the issue.
*   **Production Waste Comments** (`production_waste_comments`, `text`, **required**): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name, the employee who identified or reported the production waste incident.

Please note that this form is for internal use only and should be kept confidential. The information provided will be used for the purposes of production waste management and improvement.
