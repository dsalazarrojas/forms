# Workshop Profitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the profitability of a workshop or training session. It helps organizers evaluate its financial performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **revenue**, **costs**, **participant_feedback**, **facilitator_rating**, and **team_rating**.
2. Enter the total revenue earned from the workshop in the **revenue** field.
3. Enter the total costs associated with the workshop in the **costs** field.
4. Provide your feedback on the workshop's participants in the **participant_feedback** field.
5. Rate the facilitator's performance in the **facilitator_rating** field.
6. Rate the team's performance in the **team_rating** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workshop_profitability_assessment**: This is the main title of the form.
* **revenue**: The total amount of money earned from the workshop. This is a required field.
* **costs**: The total costs associated with holding the workshop. This is a required field.
* **participant_feedback**: Your feedback on the workshop's participants, including their engagement and satisfaction. This field is optional.
* **facilitator_rating**: A rating (1-5) of the facilitator's performance, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent. This field is required.
* **team_rating**: A rating (1-5) of the team's performance, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent. This field is required.
* **team_rating**: Note: This field is a duplicate of the **team_rating** field, and should be ignored. 

**Tips:**

* Make sure to enter accurate numbers for **revenue** and **costs** to ensure accurate calculations.
* Use specific and constructive feedback in the **participant_feedback** field.
* Rate the facilitator and team based on their performance during the workshop.
* Complete this form as soon as possible after the workshop to ensure timely evaluation.
