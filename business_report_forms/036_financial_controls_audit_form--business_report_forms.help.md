# Financial Controls Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Controls Audit Form is a tool used to collect and record information about financial controls, their status, and the follow-up planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have access to the relevant information and data.
2. Fill out the form section by section, providing accurate and complete answers to each question.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and consistently with the company's financial control policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Controls Audit**: This section is used to record the status of financial controls, including their type and location. (Page 1)
* **Risk Notes**: Enter a brief description of any risks identified during the audit process. (Page 2)
* **Follow-up Planning**: Indicate if follow-up planning is required. (Page 3)
* **Control ID**: Select the ID of the financial control being audited. (Page 4)
* **Control Description**: Enter a brief description of the financial control. (Page 5)
* **Control Location**: Enter the location of the financial control. (Page 6)
* **Control Owner**: Indicate the owner of the financial control. (Page 7)
* **Control Status**: Select the status of the financial control. (Page 8)
* **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the last audit. (Page 9)
* **Audit Time**: Enter the time of the last audit. (Page 10)
* **Review Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of reviews for the control. (Page 11)
* **Risk Level**: Select the level of risk associated with the control. (Page 12)
* **Risk Rating**: Enter a numerical rating of the risk level. (Page 13)
* **Risk Description**: Enter a description of the risk associated with the control. (Page 14)
* **Action Plan**: Enter a brief description of any action items identified during the audit. (Page 15)
* **Action Taken**: Indicate if any action has been taken on the identified action items. (Page 16)
* **Follow-up Status**: Indicate the status of follow-up actions. (Page 17)
* **Follow-up Due Date**: Enter the date by which follow-up actions are due. (Page 18)
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of follow-up reviews. (Page 19)
* **Follow-up Last Date**: Enter the date of the last follow-up review. (Page 20)
* **Follow-up Next Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up review. (Page 21)
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the follow-up process. (Page 22)
