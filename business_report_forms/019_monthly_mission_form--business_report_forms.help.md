# Monthly Mission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Mission Form is used to document and track business objectives and goals for each month. It helps management to focus on prioritizing and reviewing progress towards specific, measurable outcomes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Monthly Mission Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of your company.
2. Describe the mission goal for the current month.
3. Select the type of goal you are trying to achieve (Strategic, Operational, Financial).
4. Choose the expected target outcome (Increase Revenue, Decrease Costs, Improve Processes).
5. Enter the target metric to measure the outcome.
6. Set the target start date and target end date for the mission.
7. Define the target budget and target bounty for the mission.
8. Add any additional notes for reference.
9. Select the desired review frequency for the mission (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Mission Goal** (`mission_goal`, `text`, required): Describe the mission goal for the current month in a short paragraph.
* **Goal Type** (`goal_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of goal you are trying to achieve (Strategic, Operational, Financial). Note: This field is not required, but can be useful for categorizing and prioritizing missions.
* **Target Outcome** (`target_outcome`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the expected target outcome (Increase Revenue, Decrease Costs, Improve Processes). Like `Goal Type`, this field is not required, but can aid in prioritization.
* **Outcome Metric** (`outcome_metric`, `text`, required): Enter a metric that will be used to measure the outcome of the mission. This could be revenue, cost reduction, or process efficiency, for example.
* **Target Start Date** (`target_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date for the target period of the mission.
* **Target End Date** (`target_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date for the target period of the mission.
* **Target Budget** (`target_budget`, `number`, required): Enter the budget allocated for the mission.
* **Target Bounty** (`target_bounty`, `number`, required): Enter the bounty or reward that will be earned or lost when the mission is completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or reference information about the mission.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the desired review frequency for the mission (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
