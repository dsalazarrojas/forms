# Commission Report Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commission Report Submission Form is used to collect sales commission data from agents on a regular basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Report Information section with the required details about the report.
2. Select the Report Date and Report Period from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter the Agent Information, including the Agent Name, Agent ID, and Agent Email.
4. Provide details about the Sales Summary, including the Total Transactions, Total Sales Volume, and Total Commission Earned.
5. Enter the Commission Breakdown, including the Base Commission, Bonus Commission, SPIFF Incentives, and Deductions Adjustments.
6. Select the Net Commission and Agent Certification.
7. Upload any supporting documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Information** (optional): Details about the report, including a brief description of the report.
* **Report Date** (required): Date of report submission.
* **Report Period** (required): Time period this report covers.
* **Agent Name** (required): Full name of the sales agent.
* **Agent ID** (required): Agent identification number.
* **Agent Email** (required): Contact email address.
* **Region Territory** (required): Assigned sales region or territory.
* **Sales Summary** (optional): Overview of sales performance.
* **Total Transactions** (required): Number of transactions completed.
* **Total Sales Volume** (required): Total value of all sales in USD.
* **Total Commission Earned** (required): Total commission amount in USD.
* **Average Deal Size** (optional): Average value per transaction in USD.
* **Commission Breakdown** (optional): Detailed commission information.
* **Base Commission** (required): Base commission amount in USD.
* **Bonus Commission** (optional): Bonus commission amount in USD.
* **Spiff Incentives** (optional): SPIFF or incentive amounts in USD.
* **Deductions Adjustments** (optional): Any deductions or adjustments in USD.
* **Net Commission** (required): Final net commission in USD.
* **Top Performing Products** (optional): Best selling products or services.
* **Challenges Faced** (optional): Any obstacles or challenges encountered.
* **Goals Next Period** (optional): Sales goals for next reporting period.
* **Supporting Documents** (optional): Upload any supporting documentation.
* **Agent Certification** (required): I certify this report is accurate and complete.

Note: All fields with the label "required" must be filled in order to complete the form successfully.
