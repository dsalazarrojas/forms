# This form is a job log form designed for businesses to record and track employee work. It's meant to capture essential information about job assignments, including job titles, staff names, dates, start and end times, work descriptions, materials used, and files related to the job.

## Job Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Form is a simple form designed to record and track employee work assignments, providing a quick and easy way to log job details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as needed.
2. Enter the job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Provide the staff member's name in the "Staff Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the job in the "Date" field.
5. Record the start time of the job in the "Start Time" field.
6. Record the end time of the job in the "End Time" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the job in the "Work Description" field.
8. List any materials used during the job in the "Materials Used" field.
9. Attach any relevant files to the job in the "Files Related" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the job being performed.

### Staff Name
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member who performed the job.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the job was performed.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the job.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the job.

### Work Description
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the work performed.

### Materials Used
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, `text`, required: false): List any materials used during the job.

### Files Related
* **Files Related** (`files_related`, `text`, required: false): Attach any files related to the job, such as images or documents.

## Tips

- Double-check your input for accuracy.
