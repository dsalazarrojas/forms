# Monthly Business Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about business expenses incurred during a given month. It is used to document and review expenses for accuracy and transparency in financial reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as needed.
2. Select the correct approved by person from the options provided.
3. Upload any supporting files as necessary.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monthly Business Expense Report Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report. This helps to identify the time period for which the expenses are being reported.
* **Business Expense**: Enter a detailed description of the expense, including the date of the expense, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Business Expense 1-7**: These are fields for additional expenses. You can enter up to 7 business expenses.
* **Total Expenses**: Enter the total amount of expenses incurred.
* **Approved By**: Select the person who reviewed and approved the expenses.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information related to the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Upload File**: Upload any supporting documents or files related to the form.
* **Upload File 1**: Upload any additional supporting documents or files related to the form.

Please note that this form is for legitimate business expenses only. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or accounting department for assistance.
