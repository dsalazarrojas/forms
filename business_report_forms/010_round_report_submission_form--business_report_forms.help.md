## Round Report Submission Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Round Report Submission Form is used to collect information about completed rounds of work, including job titles, locations, start and end dates, and other relevant details. This form is essential for tracking and analyzing work performance data.

### How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.** Ensure that all required fields are filled in and that you have permission to submit reports for the specified rounds.
2. **Enter your name and job title.** Provide your full name and current job title in the first_name and job_title fields respectively.
3. **Select the job location.** Enter the location where the work was performed in the job_location field.
4. **Enter the start and end dates of the round.** If applicable, enter the start_date and end_date fields with the dates of the round.
5. **Enter the duration of the round.** If applicable, enter the round_duration field with the duration of the round.
6. **Enter any shift notes.** If applicable, enter any shift_notes that may be relevant to the round.
7. **Enter the round number(s).** Enter the round_number field(s) with the number(s) of the round(s) you are reporting on.
8. **Submit the form.** Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, if you want to associate your report with your personnel record.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title as it relates to the work performed during the round.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the work was performed during the round.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the round if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the round if applicable.
* **Round Duration** (`round_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the round if applicable.
* **Shift Notes** (`shift_notes`, text, optional): Enter any shift notes that may be relevant to the round.
* **Round Number** (`round_number`, number, required): Enter the number of the round you are reporting on.
* **Round Number 2** (`round_number_2`, number, optional): Enter any additional round numbers that may be associated with the round.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit the form.

### Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
*   If you are submitting reports for multiple rounds, ensure that you have permission to do so and that the form is filled out correctly for each round.
