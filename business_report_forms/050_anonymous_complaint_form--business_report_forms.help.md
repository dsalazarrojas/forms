# Anonymous Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymous complaints from customers, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and address customer concerns in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the category of your complaint from the list of options.
2. Fill in the date and time of the incident, if applicable.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4. Identify the department involved, if applicable.
5. If you know the name of the employee involved, provide it.
6. Describe the employee's behavior or actions.
7. If you have an account with us, provide the account number.
8. If the incident is related to an order, provide the order number.
9. Describe the product or service involved.
10. Provide a detailed description of what happened.
11. Check if you have contacted us before about this issue.
12. If you have contacted us before, provide details about the previous contact.
13. If this issue was previously resolved, select the relevant option.
14. Check if you have evidence related to the incident.
15. Select the type of evidence you have.
16. Describe how this incident affected you.
17. Indicate the level of severity of the issue.
18. Describe the desired resolution.
19. Check if you are seeking compensation.
20. If you are seeking compensation, describe the type of compensation you seek.
21. Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up.
22. If you prefer to be contacted by phone or email, provide the relevant contact information.
23. Choose your desired level of anonymity.
24. Check if you are concerned about retaliation.
25. Select the level of urgency for your issue.
26. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Complaint Type
* **Complaint Type** (select_one): Select the category of your complaint from the list of options to help us understand the nature of your issue.

### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (date): If applicable, fill in the date of the incident. This is optional, but provides valuable information for our reference.

### Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (time): If applicable, fill in the time of the incident. This is optional, but helpful for our investigation.

### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (text): Specify the location where the incident occurred.

### Department Involved
* **Department Involved** (select_one): If relevant, select the department that handled the incident or had involvement.

### Employee Involved
* **Employee Involved** (text): If you know the name of the employee who was involved, provide it.

### Employee Description
* **Employee Description** (text): Describe the employee's behavior or actions.

### Customer Account
* **Customer Account** (text): If you have an account with us, provide the account number. This helps us look up information related to your account.

### Order Number
* **Order Number** (text): If the incident is related to an order, provide the order number.

### Product or Service
* **Product or Service** (text): Describe the product or service involved.

### Complaint Description
* **Complaint Description** (text): Provide a detailed description of what happened.

### Previous Contact
* **Previous Contact** (select_one): Check if you have contacted us before about this issue.

### Previous Contact Details
* **Previous Contact Details** (text): If you have contacted us before, provide details about the previous contact.

### Previous Resolution
* **Previous Resolution** (select_one): If this issue was previously resolved, select the relevant option.

### Evidence Available
* **Evidence Available** (select_one): Check if you have evidence related to the incident.

### Evidence Type
* **Evidence Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of evidence you have.

### Impact Description
* **Impact Description** (text): Describe how this incident affected you.

### Severity Level
* **Severity Level** (select_one): Indicate the level of severity of the issue.

### Desired Resolution
* **Desired Resolution** (text): Describe the resolution you desire from us.

### Compensation Requested
* **Compensation Requested** (select_one): Check if you are seeking compensation.

### Compensation Details
* **Compensation Details** (text): Describe the type of compensation you seek.

### Contact Preference
* **Contact Preference** (select_one): Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up.

### Contact Email/Phone
* **Contact Email/Phone** (text): If you prefer to be contacted by phone or email, provide the relevant contact information.

### Anonymity Level
* **Anonymity Level** (select_one): Choose your desired level of anonymity.

### Retaliation Concern
* **Retaliation Concern** (select_one): Check if you are concerned about retaliation.

### Urgency
* **Urgency** (select_one): Select the level of urgency for your issue.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (text): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your complaint.
