# Digital Ad Performance Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a digital ad performance report. Please provide the required information to customize your report according to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Campaign Details** section with your campaign's name and ID.
2. Choose the **Metric** you are interested in (e.g., Ad Click Rate, Ad Conversion Rate, Ad CTR).
3. Select the **Ad Type** (e.g., Display Ads, Video Ads, Audio Ads).
4. Choose the **Ad Channel** (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Twitter Ads).
5. Select the **Ad Placement** (e.g., Mobile Ads, Desktop Ads, Web Ads).
6. Choose the **Ad Size** (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
7. Enter your **Ad Budget**.
8. Select the **Report Frequency** (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
9. Choose the **Report Type** (e.g., PDF, CSV).
10. Select the **Date Range** (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
11. Enter your **Email** address.
12. Add any additional **Notes** or **Comments** about your report.
13. Click the **Submit** button to request your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details**: Enter your campaign's name and ID.
* **Metric Name**: Select the metric you are interested in (e.g., Ad Click Rate, Ad Conversion Rate, Ad CTR).
* **Ad Type**: Choose the type of ad you are interested in (e.g., Display Ads, Video Ads, Audio Ads).
* **Ad Channel**: Select the platform where the ad was displayed (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Twitter Ads).
* **Ad Placement**: Choose where the ad was placed (e.g., Mobile Ads, Desktop Ads, Web Ads).
* **Ad Size**: Select the size of the ad (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Ad Budget**: Enter your ad budget.
* **Report Frequency**: Select the frequency of your report (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Report Type**: Choose the format of your report (e.g., PDF, CSV).
* **Date Range**: Enter the date range for your report.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Additional Details**: Add any additional comments or notes about your report.
* **Report Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about your report.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your report.

This guide provides a clear and concise explanation of each field in the digital ad performance report request form. By following these steps, users should be able to easily complete the form and request the desired report.
