# Wastewater Treatment Output Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wastewater Treatment Output Report Form is a tool used by plant operators or management to provide updates on the performance of a wastewater treatment plant. It helps to collect data on plant capacity, flow rate, treatment efficiency, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each section carefully and accurately**. This form is a snapshot of your plant's performance at a given time.
2. **Use the correct format for each field**. For example, use numbers for fields labeled as numbers and dates for fields labeled as date.
3. **Be as detailed as possible**. Provide as much information as you can about your plant's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plant Performance** (`plant_performance`): Check this box if your plant is currently performing normally. If not, provide a brief explanation.
* **Plant Capacity** (`plant_capacity`): Enter the total capacity of your treatment plant, expressed in units of volume or flow rate (e.g., "1000 m3/day").
* **Flow Rate** (`flow_rate`): Enter the current flow rate of your treatment plant, expressed in units of volume or flow rate per unit of time (e.g., "500 m3/hour").
* **Treatment Plant Performance** (`treatment_plant_performance`): Check "Yes" if your plant is currently treating wastewater normally, or "No" if it's not.
* **Wastewater Production** (`wastewater_production`): Check all applicable items if you are producing wastewater in the following categories:
	+ Check "Yes" for each applicable category.
* **Wastewater Removal Efficiency** (`wastewater_removal_efficiency`): Check "Yes" if your plant is efficiently removing wastewater, or "No" if it's not.
* **Total Treatment Capacity** (`total_treatment_capacity`): Enter the total treatment capacity of your plant, expressed in units of volume or flow rate (e.g., "200 m3/hour").
* **Plant Location** (`plant_location`): Enter the location of your plant, including any relevant coordinates or addresses.
* **Treatment System Description** (`treatment_system_description`): Provide a brief description of your plant's treatment system.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`): Enter your email address or name as the person submitting this report.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`): Enter the date and time you are submitting this report.

Note: Some fields are optional and may be skipped if not applicable.
