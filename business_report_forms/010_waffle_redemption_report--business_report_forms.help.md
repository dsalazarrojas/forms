# Waffle Redemption Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers who have redeemed their waffles and track their redemption status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that the customer has provided all the necessary details about their waffle redemption.
2. Fill in the customer details section with the customer's name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the redemption status of the customer from the options provided (Redemptions Completed, In Progress, Not Redemmed yet).
4. Enter the date and time of redemption in the corresponding fields.
5. Provide any additional notes about the customer's redemption.
6. Enter the customer's email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waffle Redemption Report** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description of the customer's waffle redemption.
* **Customer Details** (`id: 2`): Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
* **Voucher Details** (`id: 3`): Enter any additional details about the voucher used for redemption.
* **Redemption Status** (`id: 4`): Select the status of the customer's redemption from the options provided.
* **Date of Redemption** (`id: 5`): Enter the date of the customer's waffle redemption.
* **Date Submitted** (`id: 6`): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`id: 7`): Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Notes** (`id: 8`): Enter any additional notes about the customer's redemption.
* **Email** (`id: 9`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 11`): Enter the customer's email address again, if different from the above.
* **Customer Phone** (`id: 12`): Enter the customer's phone number again, if different from the above.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the customer's details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If there are any issues with the customer's redemption, please enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
* If the customer's email or phone number changes, please update the corresponding fields accordingly.
