# Mobile Commerce Trend Report Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Commerce Trend Report Registration form is a tool for capturing participant information for the Mobile Commerce Trend Report. The report aims to monitor and analyze business trends in the mobile commerce industry, providing valuable insights for businesses to adapt and innovate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
3. Provide your Email to be contacted for any further questions or updates.
4. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
5. Fill out the Company name associated with your participation.
6. Specify your current Position within the Company.
7. Grant consent by checking the Consent box.
8. Select your Business Type from the provided options.
9. Identify your industry by selecting multiple options that apply to your business.
10. Specify the location of your business.
11. Enter the URL of your business's website.
12. Provide a brief Description of your business for context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be represented in the report.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be represented in the report.
* **Email** (`email`, Text, Optional): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, Optional): Enter your company name as it will be represented in the report.
* **Position** (`position`, Text, Optional): Specify your position within the company.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Text, Optional): Checking this box indicates your consent to be included in the report.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, Select One, Optional): Select your type of business from the options provided:
	+ Retail
	+ Wholesale
	+ Other
* **Industry** (`industry`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the industries that apply to your business:
	+ Digital Marketing
	+ E-Commerce
	+ Retail
	+ Wholesale
	+ Other
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of your business.
* **Website** (`website`, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of your business's website.
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your business for context.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your representation in the report.
- Double-check your selections for Business Type and Industry to ensure they accurately reflect your business.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for further assistance.
