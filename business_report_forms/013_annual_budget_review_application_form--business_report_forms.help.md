# Annual Budget Review Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and update annual budgets within the company. It helps track and analyze expenses, ensuring accuracy and informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
	* Provide your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
2. Select your department:
	* Choose your department from the options provided in the "Department" field.
3. Enter budget details:
	* Select the budget period for which you are submitting this review (e.g., Fiscal Year 2026, Fiscal Year 2025, etc.).
	* Choose the budget category (e.g., Operating Budget, Capital Budget, etc.).
4. Enter budget amounts:
	* Fill in the "Previous Budget Allocated" field with the amount allocated in the previous period.
	* Enter the "Previous Budget Spent" field with the actual amount spent.
	* Calculate the "Budget Variance" by subtracting the "Previous Budget Spent" from "Previous Budget Allocated".
5. Request and justify budget:
	* Enter the "Requested Budget Amount" for the next period.
	* Provide a justification for the budget increase or decrease in the "Budget Justification" field.
6. List key initiatives:
	* Select the key initiatives that this budget will support (e.g., Staff Expansion, Technology Upgrades, etc.).
7. Provide additional information:
	* Attach any supporting documents or files that may be relevant to your review.
	* Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name to associate with this review.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your contact email address for future reference.
* **Department (4)**: Choose your department to categorize your review.
* **Position Title (5)**: Enter your job title to provide context.
* **Budget Period (6)**: Select the budget period for which you are submitting this review.
* **Budget Category (7)**: Choose the budget category for this review (e.g., Operating Budget, etc.).
* **Previous Budget Allocated (8)**: Enter the amount allocated in the previous period.
* **Previous Budget Spent (9)**: Enter the actual amount spent in the previous period.
* **Budget Variance (10)**: Calculate the difference between allocated and spent amounts.
* **Variance Explanation (11)**: Explain any significant variances.
* **Requested Budget Amount (12)**: Enter the requested budget amount for the next period.
* **Budget Increase Percentage (13)**: Enter the percentage change from the previous period.
* **Budget Justification (14)**: Provide a justification for the budget increase or decrease.
* **Key Initiatives (15)**: Select the key initiatives that this budget will support.
* **Expected Return on Investment (16)**: Enter the expected benefits or ROI.
* **Risk Factors (17)**: List potential risks to budget execution.
* **Cost Saving Measures (18)**: List planned cost reductions.
* **Staffing Changes (19)**: Select any staffing changes planned.
* **Staffing Details (20)**: Describe the staffing changes planned.
* **Capital Expenditures (21)**: Enter the amount of planned capital expenditures.
* **Current Approval Status (22)**: Select the current status of this review (e.g., Draft, Under Review, etc.).
* **Approver Name (23)**: Enter the name of the person reviewing this budget.
* **Supporting Documents (24)**: List any attached documentation.
* **Additional Comments (25)**: Add any other relevant information.
