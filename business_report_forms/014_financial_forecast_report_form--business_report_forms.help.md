# Financial Forecast Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a financial forecast report that outlines a company's predicted financial performance over a specified period. The report can be used for planning and forecasting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the **Financial Forecast** page.
2. Choose the **Forecast Period** from the dropdown menu (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the forecast period in the respective date fields.
4. Enter the predicted **Revenue**.
5. (Optional) Enter the **Expenses** and **Net Income**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Forecast** (text): A brief description of the forecast, including any relevant details about the period.
* **Forecast Period** (select_one): Choose the duration of the forecast period (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Forecast Start Date** (date): The start date of the forecast period.
* **Forecast End Date** (date): The end date of the forecast period.
* **Revenue** (number): The predicted revenue for the forecast period.
* **Expenses** (number): The predicted expenses for the forecast period (optional).
* **Net Income** (number): The predicted net income for the forecast period (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and realistic projections to ensure the forecast is reliable.
* Review and update the forecast regularly to reflect changes in your business performance.
* Use this form for planning and forecasting purposes, but not for actual financial reporting or record-keeping.
