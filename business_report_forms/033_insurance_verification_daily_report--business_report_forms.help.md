**Insurance Verification Daily Report - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Insurance Verification Daily Report form is designed for insurance agents and adjusters to gather and report on daily activities related to insurance verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Main Page** with any relevant information or notes.
2. Select the type of **Insurance Info** that applies to the policy (e.g., health, auto, home, etc.).
3. Enter the **Policy Number** if available.
4. Record the **Effective Date**, **Expiration Date**, and **Coverage Start Date** and **Coverage End Date**, if applicable.
5. Choose the **Policy Type** that best describes the policy (e.g., individual, group, commercial, etc.).
6. Enter the name of the **Agent Name** and their **Agent Phone** and **Agent Email**.
7. Select the name of the **Adjuster Name** who is handling the claim.
8. Enter the **Claimant Name** and their **Claimant Address**.
9. Select the type of **Claim Type** (e.g., property, liability, etc.).
10. Choose the **Location** where the claim occurred or is related (e.g., office, home, etc.).
11. Select the **Claim Status** (e.g., new, pending, settled, etc.).
12. Enter any additional **Notes** or comments regarding the claim.
13. Finally, submit the form.
