# Financial Reporting Modernization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Reporting Modernization Program Registration is a form designed to register interested parties for the Financial Reporting Modernization Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your answer for "Program Enrollment" by choosing one of the options provided.
2. Fill out the "Finance Leader Details" text field with information about your finance leader.
3. Provide details about yourself or your organization in the "Participant Details" text field.
4. In the "Expectations" text field, write down your expectations from the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Enrollment (Yes/No)**: 
Select "Yes" if you are interested in registering for the Financial Reporting Modernization Program, and "No" otherwise.
* **Finance Leader Details**: 
Provide information about your finance leader, such as their name, title, and contact details.
* **Participant Details**: 
Provide details about yourself or your organization, such as your name, title, and contact details.
* **Expectations**: 
Write down what you expect from the program, such as what benefits you hope to gain or what support you need.
