# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a walkthrough on how to complete the Business Report Form for submitting reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Select the report year from the dropdown menu in the "Report Year" field.
3. Select the type of report from the "Report Type" field (optional).
4. Choose whether your organization has "Yes" or "No" to the "Organization Type" question.
5. Enter the URL of any supporting documents in the "Report URL" field (optional).
6. Add any submission comments in the "Submission Comments" field.
7. Specify who submitted the report in the "Submitted By" field.
8. Select a date for the report from the "Submit Date" field (optional).
9. Finally, choose a submit time from the "Submit Time" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Report Year** (`report_year`, required): Select the year for which the report is submitted.
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, optional): Choose the type of report you are submitting (e.g., Report, Financial, Operations).
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, optional): Select whether your organization has "Yes" or "No" to the organization type question.
* **Report URL** (`report_url`, optional): Enter the URL of any supporting documents.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Enter a brief description of the supporting documents.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, optional): Add any comments related to the submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, required): Specify the person or people who submitted the report.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, optional): Select the date on which the report was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, optional): Choose the time on which the report was submitted.
