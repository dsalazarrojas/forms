# Agent Productivity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an agent's productivity over a specific reporting period. It helps in tracking progress, identifying challenges, and setting future goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Agent name" field.
2. Enter the date of the report in the "Report date" field.
3. Enter the total number of hours you worked during the reporting period in the "Hours worked" field.
4. Provide a short summary of the tasks you completed in the "Tasks completed" field.
5. If applicable, enter the number of sales you closed during the reporting period in the "Sales closed number" field.
6. Enter the number of leads you contacted during the reporting period in the "Leads contacted" field.
7. Describe any blockers or issues that might be affecting your productivity in the "Blockers or issues" field.
8. Enter your objectives for the next reporting period in the "Next period goals" field.
9. Include any comments or feedback from your manager in the "Manager comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Report date**: Enter the date of this productivity report.
* **Hours worked**: Enter the total number of hours you worked during the reporting period.
* **Tasks completed**: Provide a short summary of the tasks you completed, including any notable achievements.
* **Sales closed number**: Enter the number of sales you closed during the reporting period (if applicable).
* **Leads contacted**: Enter the total number of leads you contacted during the reporting period (if applicable).
* **Blockers or issues**: Describe any blockers or issues that might be affecting your productivity.
* **Next period goals**: Enter your objectives for the next reporting period.
* **Manager comments**: Include any comments or feedback from your manager for review and reference.
