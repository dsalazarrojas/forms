# Batch Sampling Frequency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Batch Sampling Frequency Report Form is used to collect data on the sampling process for quality control and process monitoring. This form is used to track the sampling frequency, sampling technician, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Report ID**, a unique identifier for the report.
2. Select the **Report Date** of when the sampling occurred.
3. Enter the **Batch ID** being sampled.
4. Enter the **Product Name** being sampled.
5. Choose the **Production Line** for the sampling.
6. Select the **Sampling Technician** who performed the sampling.
7. Choose the **Sampling Frequency** (e.g., Every Hour, Every 2 Hours, Every 8 Hours, etc.).
8. Enter the **Sample Size** taken per sampling period.
9. Choose the **Sampling Method** used (e.g., Random Sampling, Systematic Sampling, Stratified Sampling, Grab Sampling, etc.).
10. Enter the **Sampling Location** where samples were taken.
11. Enter the **Total Samples Taken**, **Samples Passed**, and **Samples Failed** to track the sampling results.
12. Enter the percentage of **Pass Rate Percentage**.
13. Choose the **Parameters Tested** (e.g., pH Level, Viscosity, Temperature, etc.).
14. Indicate if **Deviations Noted** were detected.
15. Enter the **Deviation Details** if deviations were noted.
16. Choose the **Trend Observed** (e.g., Stable, Improving, Declining, Variable).
17. Enter any **Recommendations** for future sampling.
18. Enter the **Next Scheduled Sampling Date** when the next sample is due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report ID**: Unique identifier for the report.
* **Report Date**: Date of when the sampling occurred.
* **Batch ID**: Batch being sampled.
* **Product Name**: Product being sampled.
* **Production Line**: Production line where the sampling occurred.
* **Sampling Technician**: Who performed the sampling.
* **Sampling Frequency**: How often samples are taken.
* **Sample Size**: Quantity per sample.
* **Sampling Method**: How samples are collected.
* **Sampling Location**: Where samples were taken.
* **Total Samples Taken**: Number of samples in period.
* **Samples Passed**: Number that passed.
* **Samples Failed**: Number that failed.
* **Pass Rate Percentage**: Percentage passing.
* **Parameters Tested**: What was tested.
* **Deviations Noted**: Were any out of spec results noted.
* **Deviation Details**: Describe any deviations.
* **Trend Observed**: Any patterns noticed.
* **Recommendations**: Suggested actions for future sampling.
* **Next Scheduled Sampling**: When the next sample is due.
