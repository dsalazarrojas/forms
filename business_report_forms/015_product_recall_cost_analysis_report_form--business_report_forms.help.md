# Product Recall Cost Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information related to product recalls, including the costs involved. It helps in managing and analyzing the financial impacts of product recalls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the requested information:
	* `Product Recall Form`: This is the main section of the form where you can provide details about the product recall.
	* `Recall Details`: This is where you can enter any relevant information about the recall, such as the reason for the recall.
	* `Cost Breakdown`: This is where you can break down the costs associated with the recall.
	* `Additional Info`: This is for entering any additional comments or information not covered in the previous fields.
	* `Submission Status`: This field indicates the status of your submission, such as whether it's submitted, drafted, or cancelled.
	* `Cost Type`: This field indicates the type of cost related to the recall, such as whether it's for `Product Recalls` or `Other`.
	* `Cost Type Subcategory`: This is a field where you can select multiple subcategories of costs associated with the recall.
	* `Cost Category`: This is where you can select multiple categories of costs related to the recall.
	* `Cost Amount`: This is the amount of the cost associated with the recall.
	* `Date Occurred`: This field indicates the date on which the recall occurred.
	* `Time Occurred`: This field indicates the time on which the recall occurred.
	* `Product Name`: This is the name of the product that was recalled.
	* `Recall Date`: This field indicates the date on which the recall occurred.
	* `Cost Category Subcategory`: This is a field where you can select multiple subcategories of costs associated with the recall.
	* `Cost Amount Sub`: This is the amount of the cost associated with the recall.
	* `Cost Total`: This field indicates the total cost of the recall.
	* `Submission Status Label`: This is a field to enter a label for the submission status.
	* `Submission Status Color`: This is a field to enter the color of the submission status.
	* `Submission Status`: This field indicates the status of your submission.
	* `Recall Date Label`: This is a field to enter a label for the recall date.
	* `Recall Date Color`: This is a field to enter the color of the recall date.
	* `Submitted By`: This is the person who submitted the form.
	* `Submitted By Label`: This is a field to enter a label for the submitted by field.
	* `Submitted By Color`: This is a field to enter the color of the submitted by field.
	* `Form ID`: This is the unique identifier for the form.
