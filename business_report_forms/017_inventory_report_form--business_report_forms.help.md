# Inventory Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Report Form is used to report and track inventory levels within the organization, providing valuable information to management and other stakeholders to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the Inventory Report Form.
2. Fill in the stock level for each item.
3. Enter the stock count for each item.
4. Enter the stock value for each item.
5. Click submit to complete the form and generate the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Report Form**: This is the main content section of the form where you can add your comments and details about the inventory report.
* **Stock Level (#2)**: Enter the current stock level for each item. This is the number of units available in stock at the current moment.
* **Stock Count (#3)**: Enter the total number of units in stock for each item. This includes both available and sold-out items.
* **Stock Value (#4)**: Enter the total value of the stock for each item. This is the cost or value of the entire stock quantity.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the report is reliable and useful.
* Review the form before submitting to catch any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or inventory manager for assistance.
