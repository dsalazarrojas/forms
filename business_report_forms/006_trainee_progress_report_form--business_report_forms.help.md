# Trainee Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to track the progress of trainees in training programs, providing a snapshot of their status and advancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide trainee information in the **Trainee Information** section, including any relevant details that may be required for progress tracking.
2. Report the trainee's **progress** in the corresponding section, detailing their current stage in the training program.
3. Enter the relevant details in the **Training Program** section, including any specific program or course information.
4. Add any **notes** or additional comments that may be relevant to the trainee's progress.
5. Select the trainee's current **status** from the available options (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
6. If applicable, enter the **Completion Date** and **Completion Time** for the training program.
7. Provide any **comments** or feedback from the trainee.
8. Add any **Trainer Comments** or feedback for the trainee.
9. Finally, enter the **Evaluation Date** for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Information**: Enter the name, ID, or any other relevant details about the trainee.
* **Progress**: Describe the trainee's current stage in the training program.
* **Training Program**: Provide specific details about the training program, including course or module names.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the trainee's progress.
* **Trainee Status**: Choose from the available options to select the trainee's current status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Completion Date**: Enter the date the trainee completed the training program.
* **Completion Time**: Enter the time the trainee completed the training program.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback from the trainee.
* **Trainer Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the trainer.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date the form was evaluated or reviewed by the trainer.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the trainee's progress.
- Use the **Trainee Status** field to track the trainee's advancement.
- Enter relevant details in the **Training Program** field to provide context about the training.
- Use the **Notes** field to add any additional information that may be useful for tracking.
- Select the correct **Trainee Status** to accurately reflect the trainee's current state.
- Enter accurate dates and times for **Completion Date** and **Completion Time**, if applicable.
