# Quarterly Business Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture quarterly progress updates from team leads, track outcomes, and set priorities for the next quarter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team's quarterly updates for the current quarter.
2. Select the team leads who contributed to the updates.
3. Enter any notable outcomes or achievements for your team.
4. Choose the priority level for next quarter's goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quarterly Updates (1)**: Enter your team's progress and updates for the current quarter.
* **Team Leads (2)**: Select the team members who contributed to the updates. You can choose one or multiple team leads.
* **Outcomes (3)**: Describe any notable outcomes or achievements your team has had this quarter.
* **Next Quarter Priorities (4)**: Choose the priority level for your team's goals in the next quarter. You can select High, Medium, or Low priority.

## Tips
- Be concise and clear in your updates, outcomes, and priority selection.
- Ensure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
