# Profit And Loss Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Profit And Loss Report Form is used to record and report financial performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options in the "Select One" field and choose the correct answer.
2. Enter the date in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time in the "Time" field.
4. Enter the decimal amount in the "Decimal" field.
5. Enter any additional text information in the "Text", "Text Area", "Text Area 1", and "Notes" fields.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Phone" and "Email" fields.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Text Area" and "Text Area 1" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select One** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the correct answer to indicate the type of financial metric being reported. This field is required to ensure accurate reporting.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the financial transaction or event.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the financial transaction or event.
* **Decimal** (`number`, optional): Enter a decimal amount, such as $10.99 or -5.99.
* **Text** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional text information about the financial metric.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the financial metric.
* **Text Area** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional text information about the financial metric.
* **Text Area 1** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional text information about the financial metric 1.
* **Date Time** (`text`, optional): Enter the date and time of the financial transaction or event.

## Tips
- Make sure to choose the correct answer in the "Select One" field to ensure accurate reporting.
