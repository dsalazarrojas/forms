# Incubator Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incubator Inspection Report form is used to document and track the inspection process of incubators in facilities. It helps ensure the safety and quality of incubators and ensures that any issues found are addressed and corrected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and data to complete the form accurately.
2. Review each field and provide the required information in the corresponding field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incubator Status** (incubator_status, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incubator's status, such as "Functional" or "Non-Functional".
* **Date Of Inspection** (date_of_inspection, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Temperature** (temperature, number, required: false): Enter the current temperature reading in the incubator.
* **Humidity** (humidity, number, required: false): Enter the current humidity reading in the incubator.
* **Pressure** (pressure, number, required: false): Enter the current pressure reading in the incubator.
* **Issues Found** (issues_found, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all issues found during the inspection, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Issue Description** (issue_description, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of any issues found during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action** (corrective_action, text, required: false): Describe any corrective actions taken to address issues found during the inspection.
* **Next Inspection Due Date** (next_inspection_due_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Facility Name** (facility_name, text, required: false): Provide the name of the facility where the incubator is located.
* **Facility Address** (facility_address, text, required: false): Enter the address of the facility where the incubator is located.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required: false): Provide the name of the person responsible for the facility.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
