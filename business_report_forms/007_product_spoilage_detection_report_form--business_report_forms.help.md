# Product Spoilage Detection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track product spoilage incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details (Product ID).
2. Select the type of spoilage (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
3. Enter the date of the incident (Incident Date).
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred (Incident Location).
5. Describe the evidence captured during the incident (Evidence Capture).
6. Enter the loss amount due to spoilage (Spoilage Loss).
7. Enter the value of the spoiled product (Spoilage Value).
8. Provide a description of the incident (Incident Description).
9. Add any additional notes about the incident (Notes).
10. Select the person who submitted the form (Form Submitted By).
11. Enter the date the form was submitted (Form Submitted Date).
12. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form (Email).
13. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form (Phone).
14. Enter the name of the product affected (Product Name).
15. Enter the rate of spoilage (Spoilage Rate).
16. Enter the reason for spoilage (Spoilage Reason).
17. Select the status of the incident (Incident Status).
18. Assign a follow-up action (Follow-up).
19. Select the person assigned to follow-up on the action (Follow-up By).
20. Enter the date for follow-up (Follow-up Date).
21. Select the status of the follow-up (Follow-up Status).
22. Enter any comments for the follow-up (Follow-up Comment).
23. Enter any comments from the person following up on the action (Follow-up By Comment).
24. Enter any comments from the person who submitted the form (Form Submitted By Comment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Spoilage Detection Report Form** (`product_spoilage_detection_report_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`): Enter the ID of the product affected.
* **Spoilage Type** (`spoilage_type`): Select the type of spoilage that occurred (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the spoilage incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Evidence Capture** (`evidence_capture`): Describe the evidence captured during the incident.
* **Spoilage Loss** (`spoilage_loss`): Enter the loss amount due to spoilage.
* **Spoilage Value** (`spoilage_value`): Enter the value of the spoiled product.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a description of the incident.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Date** (`form_submitted_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the product affected.
* **Spoilage Rate** (`spoilage_rate`): Enter the rate of spoilage.
* **Spoilage Reason** (`spoilage_reason`): Enter the reason for spoilage.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Select the status of the incident (Pending, Resolved, or Closed).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Assign a follow-up action.
* **Follow-up By** (`follow_up_by`): Select the person assigned to follow-up on the action.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`): Select the status of the follow-up (Pending, Completed, or Closed).
* **Follow-up Comment** (`follow_up_comment`): Enter any comments for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up By Comment** (`follow_up_by_comment`): Enter any comments from the person following up on the action.
* **Form Submitted By Comment** (`form_submitted_by_comment`): Enter any comments from the person who submitted the form.
