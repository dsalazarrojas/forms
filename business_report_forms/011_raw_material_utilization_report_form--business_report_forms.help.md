# Raw Material Utilization Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help production team members report on the utilization of raw materials during production planning and execution. It tracks production costs, material usage, and status updates to improve production planning and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and required fields.
2. Enter the relevant information for each field as requested, using the following guidelines:
	* For fields requiring numbers (e.g., material used, production cost, etc.), enter exact figures or quantities.
	* For fields asking for text responses (e.g., notes, material category), provide clear and concise descriptions.
	* For fields requesting select options (e.g., production status, material category), choose the most accurate and relevant answer from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Planning** (page 1): Enter a brief description of the production planning process, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Material Used** (page 1): Enter the total quantity of material used during production.
* **Wastage Details** (page 1): Describe any material wastage or issues encountered during production.
* **Production Details** (page 1): Provide a brief overview of production activities, including any special instructions or comments.
* **Production Date** (page 1): Enter the date when production took place.
* **Material Ordered** (page 2): Enter the total quantity of material ordered or required for production.
* **Production Cost** (page 2): Enter the total cost of production, including any expenses or costs associated with material procurement or production.
* **Total Production Cost** (page 2): Enter the total production cost for the period reported on.
* **Material Savings** (page 2): Describe any material savings or cost reductions achieved during production.
* **Production Status** (page 2): Choose the current status of the production task, such as "Available," "In Production," "Completed," or "Cancelled."
* **Production Shift** (page 2): Select the shift during which production occurred, including "Morning," "Afternoon," "Night," or "Weekend."
* **Material Category** (page 2): Choose the category of material used, including "Raw Materials," "Wastage Materials," "Finished Goods," or "Other."
* **Production Location** (page 3): Enter the production location or facility where production occurred.
* **Production Shift Start Time** (page 3): Enter the start time of the production shift.
* **Production Shift End Time** (page 3): Enter the end time of the production shift.
* **Production Hours** (page 3): Enter the total hours worked during production.
* **Production Quantity** (page 3): Enter the total quantity produced during the shift.
* **Material Ordered 2** (page 4): Enter the total quantity of material ordered or required for production (second instance).
* **Production Cost 2** (page 4): Enter the total cost of production for the second instance (if applicable).
* **Total Production Cost 2** (page 4): Enter the total production cost for the second instance.
* **Material Savings 2** (page 4): Describe any material savings or cost reductions achieved during the second production instance.
* **Production Status 2** (page 4): Choose the current status of the second production task, such as "Available," "In Production," "Completed," or "Cancelled."
* **Production Shift 2** (page 4): Select the shift during which the second production occurred, including "Morning," "Afternoon," "Night," or "Weekend."
* **Material Category 2** (page 4): Choose the category of material used for the second production instance, including "Raw Materials," "Wastage Materials," "Finished Goods," or "Other."
* **Notes** (page 5): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding production planning and execution.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out fields requiring text responses.
* Review and update the form regularly to track production progress and identify areas for improvement.
* Consult with production team members or management if you have questions or concerns about form completion or reporting requirements.
