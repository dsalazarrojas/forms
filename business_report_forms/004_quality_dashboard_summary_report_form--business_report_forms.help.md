# Quality Dashboard Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Dashboard Summary Report Form is a report that collects information about the quality of a project or process. It is used to track and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) that measure quality, identify issues, and provide context for improvement decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with any relevant details about the project or process being reported on.
2. Enter a brief summary of the quality status in the Quality Dashboard Summary section.
3. List any quality issues that have been identified in the Quality Issues section.
4. Enter relevant KPI data in the Quality KPIs section.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
6. Select the submitter's name from the list in the Submitter section.
7. Enter the submitter's email address and phone number in the Submitter Email and Submitter Phone sections.
8. Choose the submitter's department from the list in the Submitter Department section.
9. Select the submitter's job title from the list in the Submitter Job Title section.
10. Enter the date and time of submission in the Submit Date and Submit Time sections.
10. Select all relevant quality issues from the Quality Issues List section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (text): Enter a brief description of the project or process being reported on.
- **Quality Dashboard Summary** (text): A short summary of the current quality status.
- **Quality Issues** (text): List any quality issues that have been identified.
- **Quality KPIs** (text): Enter relevant KPI data.
- **Notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Quality Issues List** (select multiple): Select all relevant quality issues.
- **Quality KPI 1** (number): Enter a KPI value, such as a percentage or number.
- **Quality KPI 2** (number): Enter a second KPI value, such as a percentage or number.
- **Submit Date** (date): Enter the date of submission.
- **Submit Time** (time): Enter the time of submission.
- **Submit Date** (date) and **Submit Time** (time) should only be filled out by users who are submitting the form.
- **Email** (email): Enter the submitter's email address.
- **Phone Number** (text): Enter the submitter's phone number.
- **Submitter** (select one): Select the submitter's name from the list.
- **Submitter Email** (email): Enter the submitter's email address.
- **Submitter Phone** (text): Enter the submitter's phone number.
- **Submitter Department** (select one): Select the submitter's department from the list.
- **Submitter Job Title** (select one): Select the submitter's job title from the list.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter relevant and accurate information for each field.
- Use the Quality Issues List to select all relevant issues.
- Make sure to enter the correct submitter's information.
- Review and double-check your submission before clicking the Submit button.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or manager for clarification.
