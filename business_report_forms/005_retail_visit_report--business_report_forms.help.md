# retail_visit_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_visit_report_form is used to collect feedback from store visits. This feedback is crucial for the retail business to understand what they are doing well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide detailed information about your visit to the store, including any issues you encountered.
2. Rate your overall experience at the store on a scale of 1-5.
3. If you have any issues with the store, describe them in the text field.
4. If you need to follow up with the store, provide your contact information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_visit_details** (text, required: false): Please provide detailed information about your visit to the store. This could include the date of your visit, the store location, and any products you purchased.
* **overall_experience** (number, required: false): Rate your overall experience at the store on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **store_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the store itself on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **follow_up** (text, required: false): If you need to follow up with the store, please provide your contact information in this field.
* **store_issues** (text, required: false): If you encountered any issues during your visit, describe them in this field.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the store_visit_details field.
* If you have a suggestion or comment about the store, but no issues, you may still use the store_issues field to provide it.
* If you need to follow up with the store, make sure to include your contact information in the follow_up field.
