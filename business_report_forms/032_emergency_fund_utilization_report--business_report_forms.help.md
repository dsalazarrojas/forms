# Emergency Fund Utilization Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This report is used to track and report on the usage of emergency funds within an organization. It helps in identifying trends, patterns, and potential misuse of emergency funds, allowing for more effective management and allocation of resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the "Emergency Fund Utilization" page.
2.  Fill out the fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Fund Utilization** (`emergency_fund_utilization`, note, required: false): This field is used to enter a description or notes about the utilization of emergency funds. Please provide a brief summary of the purpose and usage of the emergency funds.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required: false): This field is used to select or enter the team name that is utilizing the emergency funds. Please select the team from the available options or enter the name if it is not listed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is used to select the date of the emergency fund utilization. Please ensure that the date is accurate and relevant to the utilization of the emergency funds.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false): This field is used to enter the amount of emergency funds utilized. Please provide the exact amount, taking into account any applicable currency and decimal places.

## Tips
* Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct team and date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for clarification.
* Please provide accurate and detailed information for each field to ensure the report is comprehensive and helpful.
