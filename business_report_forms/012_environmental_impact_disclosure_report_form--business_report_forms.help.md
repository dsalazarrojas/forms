# Environmental Impact Disclosure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Disclosure Report Form is designed to collect information about the potential environmental impacts of a project, including project details, site information, project contacts, site contacts, and supporting documents. This form helps the Environmental Impact Review Team assess the project's environmental implications and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter project information on page 1, including project name and description.
2. Provide site information on page 2, including site location and features.
3. List project contacts on page 3.
4. List site contacts on page 4.
5. Describe any potential environmental impacts on page 5.
6. Attach supporting documents on page 6.
7. Rate the project's environmental impact on page 7 (Environmental Impact Rating) and page 8 (Environmental Impact Rating 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Project Info
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the project, including its name and purpose.

### Page 2: Site Info
* **Site Info** (`site_info`, `text`, optional): Describe the site location and any notable features.

### Page 3: Project Contacts
* **Project Contacts** (`project_contacts`, `text`, optional): List the key people involved in the project, including their names, titles, and contact information.

### Page 4: Site Contacts
* **Site Contacts** (`site_contacts`, `text`, optional): List the key people associated with the site, including their names, titles, and contact information.

### Page 5: Impact Disclosures
* **Impact Disclosures** (`impact_disclosures`, `text`, optional): Describe any potential environmental impacts associated with the project, including risks, mitigation measures, and compliance status.

### Page 6: Supporting Documents
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach relevant documents, such as permits, licenses, and other regulatory documents.

### Page 7: Environmental Impact Rating
* **Environmental Impact Rating** (`environmental_impact_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate the project's environmental impact on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being minimal and 5 being significant.

### Page 8: Environmental Impact Rating 2
* **Environmental Impact Rating 2** (`environmental_impact_rating_2`, `number`, optional): Rate the project's environmental impact on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being minimal and 5 being significant.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Attach relevant documents to support your responses.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult the Environmental Impact Review Team for guidance.
