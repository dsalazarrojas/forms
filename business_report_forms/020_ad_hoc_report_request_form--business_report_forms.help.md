# Ad Hoc Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from business users for one-time report requests. It helps us understand the requirements for the report and ensures that we deliver the right information to you at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out the form, please make sure you have all the required information ready. The form is divided into two main sections: Requester Information and Report Specifications. 

- In the Requester Information section, please enter your name, department, and email address. 
- In the Report Specifications section, describe the purpose of the report, specify the data sources and periods required, and choose the preferred output format and delivery deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter your full name to help us identify you as the requester.
* **Department / Business Unit** (`department`, select_one, **Required**): Select the department or business unit that you belong to. This will help us understand the context of your request.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you efficiently.
* **Purpose of the Report** (`report_purpose`, text, **Required**): Describe the purpose of the report, e.g., budgeting, performance review.
* **Requested Data Sources / Systems** (`data_sources`, text, **Required**): Specify the data sources or systems that you need to access, e.g., ERP, CRM, Google Analytics.
* **Data Period Start Date** (`time_period_start`, date, **Required**): Enter the start date of the period for which you need the report.
* **Data Period End Date** (`time_period_end`, date, **Required**): Enter the end date of the period for which you need the report.
* **Key Metrics / KPIs to Include** (`key_metrics`, text, **Required**): List the specific data points or KPIs that you need included in the report.
* **Specific Filters or Segmentations** (`data_filters`, text, **Optional**): Specify any filters or segmentations that you need applied to the report data.
* **Preferred Output Format** (`output_format`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the output format that you prefer, e.g., Excel / CSV, PDF Document, Interactive Dashboard.
* **Requested Delivery Deadline** (`delivery_deadline`, date, **Required**): Enter the date by which you need the report delivered.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the level of urgency for your report, e.g., Low - Next Reporting Cycle, Medium - Within 1 Week, High - Critical Business Decision Needed.
* **I certify that I have the necessary clearance to access this data** (`security_clearance_ack`, select_one, **Required**): Please confirm that you have the necessary clearance to access the data.
* **Additional Instructions or Notes** (`additional_instructions`, text, **Optional**): If you have any additional information or instructions that will help us deliver the report as expected, please include them here.
