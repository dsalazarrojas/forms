# Qualified Report Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about reports, including category, files, priority, comments, and submitter details. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the integrity and accuracy of our reports.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant category for your report from the drop-down menu.
2. Upload or select the relevant files associated with your report.
3. Choose the priority level of your report, which indicates its severity or urgency.
4. Add any additional comments regarding your report.
5. Select the date and time you wish to submit your report.
6. Enter your name as the submitter of the report.
7. Enter the name of any additional submitter.
8. Enter the time zone you are submitting from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Select One, Required):
    Select the category that best describes your report.
    Examples: High, Medium, Low.
* **Files** (Select Multiple, Required):
    Choose the files associated with your report.
    Upload or select the relevant files from the list of options.
* **Priority** (Select One, Required):
    Indicate the priority level of your report.
    Options: Urgent, High, Low.
* **Comments** (Note, Required):
    Add any additional comments regarding your report.
* **Submit Date** (Date, Required):
    Select the date you wish to submit your report.
* **Submit Time** (Time, Required):
    Select the time you wish to submit your report.
* **Submitter** (Text, Required):
    Enter your name as the submitter of the report.
* **Report Submitter 2** (Text, Required):
    Enter the name of any additional submitter.
* **Submit Time Zone** (Text, Required):
    Enter the time zone you are submitting from.
* **Priority Rating** (Select Multiple, Required):
    Select the priority rating of your report.
    Options: Very Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.
