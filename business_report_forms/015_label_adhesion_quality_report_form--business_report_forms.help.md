# Label Adhesion Quality Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Label Adhesion Quality Report Form is designed to collect data for the label adhesion test, ensuring that all relevant information is gathered and reported for quality control and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the surface material used for the label adhesion test.
2. Enter the date and time of the test.
3. Record the environmental conditions of the test area, including temperature, humidity, and pressure.
4. Specify the test area where the label was applied.
5. Check if the adhesion test area was successful.
6. Select the result of the test (Pass, Fail, or N/A).
7. Provide any additional remarks or notes about the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Surface Material** (`surface_material`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the material used for the label adhesion test.
* **Date Of Test** (`date_of_test`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the test.
* **Time Of Test** (`time_of_test`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the test.
* **Temperature** (`temperature_test_area`, `number`, required/optional): Record the temperature of the test area.
* **Humidity** (`humidity_test_area`, `number`, required/optional): Record the humidity of the test area.
* **Pressure** (`pressure_test_area`, `number`, required/optional): Record the pressure of the test area.
* **Test Area** (`test_area`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Specify the area where the label was applied.
* **Adhesion Test Area** (`adhesion_test_area`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if the adhesion test area was successful.
* **Result** (`result`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the result of the test (Pass, Fail, or N/A).
* **Customer** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional remarks about the test.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the test.
