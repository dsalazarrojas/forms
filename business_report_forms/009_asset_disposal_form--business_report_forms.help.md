# Asset Disposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Disposal Form is a tool to document the disposal of assets within the organization. It helps track the asset's history, including its disposal method, revenue from disposal, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Review the asset's information, including its description, category, and original purchase date.
3. Enter the disposal date, method, and revenue from disposal, if applicable.
4. Fill out the accumulated depreciation and book value fields.
5. Enter the asset's condition at disposal and whether it is being replaced.
6. Select the department responsible for the asset.
7. Enter the manager's name for approval.
8. Specify the asset's location at the time of disposal.
9. Confirm if the final inspection was completed before disposal.
10. Verify if the disposal documentation is attached.
11. Confirm if environmental compliance was verified.
12. Add any additional notes or special circumstances.
13. Enter the preparer's name and date the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the legal name of the company.
* **Disposal Date**: Enter the date the asset was disposed of.
* **Asset ID Number**: Enter the company's asset tracking number.
* **Asset Description**: Enter a detailed description of the asset.
* **Asset Category**: Select the category of the asset (e.g., Equipment, Machinery, etc.).
* **Original Purchase Date**: Enter the date the asset was acquired.
* **Original Purchase Cost**: Enter the original cost of the asset.
* **Accumulated Depreciation**: Enter the total depreciation of the asset.
* **Current Book Value**: Enter the current value of the asset after depreciation.
* **Disposal Method**: Select the method of disposal (e.g., Sold, Donated, etc.).
* **Revenue from Disposal**: Enter any revenue received from the sale or donation of the asset.
* **Gain or Loss on Disposal**: Calculate the gain or loss from the asset's disposal.
* **Asset Condition at Disposal**: Select the asset's condition at the time of disposal.
* **Is Asset Being Replaced?**: Confirm if the asset is being replaced.
* **Replacement Asset Description**: Enter a description of the new asset, if applicable.
* **Department Responsible**: Enter the department responsible for the asset.
* **Manager Approval**: Enter the manager's name for approval.
* **Asset Location at Time of Disposal**: Enter the location where the asset was disposed of.
* **Final Inspection Completed**: Confirm if the final inspection was completed before disposal.
* **Disposal Documentation Attached**: Confirm if the disposal documentation is attached.
* **Environmental Compliance Verified**: Confirm if environmental compliance was verified.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any special circumstances or notes.
* **Form Prepared By**: Enter the preparer's name.
* **Date Form Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed.

Note that this form is intended to capture detailed information about an asset's disposal, ensuring accuracy and completeness in documenting the asset's history.
