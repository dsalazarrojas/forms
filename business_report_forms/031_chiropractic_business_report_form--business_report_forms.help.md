# Chiropractic Business Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from chiropractors about their business performance and key metrics. The report should be completed for each reporting period (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually) and should reflect the most accurate information possible.

## How To Complete This Form

* Review the report date field to ensure you're reporting on the correct period.
* Select the correct reporting period (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, or Annual) from the report period dropdown menu.
* Enter your chiropractic practice name exactly as it's registered with the relevant authorities.
* Type the name of the person responsible for managing the practice.
* Enter the total revenue for the reporting period.
* Enter the total number of patient visits for the reporting period.
* (Optional) Provide the average cost per visit if you want to report this metric.
* Enter the total operating expenses for the reporting period.
* Enter the net profit or loss for the reporting period.
* Report the number of new patients acquired during the reporting period.
* Report the number of returning patients from the previous period.
* (Optional) Enter the patient retention rate as a percentage.
* (Optional) Rate your average patient satisfaction score from 1 to 5.
* (Optional) List the most common conditions treated during the reporting period.
* (Optional) List the services offered beyond spinal adjustments (e.g., massage, traction, etc.).
* (Optional) Provide any challenges encountered during the reporting period.
* (Optional) List your business goals for the next reporting period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: The date of the reporting period for which you're submitting this form.
* **Report Period**: The duration of the reporting period (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, or Annual).
* **Chiropractic Practice Name**: The name of your practice as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Practice Manager Name**: The name of the person responsible for managing your practice.
* **Total Revenue**: The total revenue for the reporting period.
* **Total Patient Visits**: The total number of patient visits for the reporting period.
* **Average Cost per Visit**: (Optional) The average cost per patient visit.
* **Operating Expenses**: The total operating expenses for the reporting period.
* **Net Profit or Loss**: The net profit or loss for the reporting period.
* **New Patients**: The number of new patients acquired during the reporting period.
* **Returning Patients**: The number of returning patients from the previous period.
* **Patient Retention Rate**: (Optional) The patient retention rate as a percentage.
* **Average Patient Satisfaction Score**: (Optional) Rate your average patient satisfaction score from 1 to 5.
* **Most Common Conditions Treated**: (Optional) List the most common conditions treated during the reporting period.
* **Treatments Services**: (Optional) List the services offered beyond spinal adjustments (e.g., massage, traction, etc.).
* **Challenges Encountered**: (Optional) List any challenges encountered during the reporting period.
* **Business Goals Next Period**: (Optional) List your business goals for the next reporting period.
* **Business Performance This Period**: Rate your overall business performance for this reporting period (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement).
* **Staff Productivity Level**: (Optional) Rate your staff's productivity level from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Spinal Adjustments Performed**: The total number of spinal adjustments performed for the reporting period.
* **Other Therapeutic Services**: (Optional) List the services offered beyond spinal adjustments (e.g., massage, traction, etc.).
