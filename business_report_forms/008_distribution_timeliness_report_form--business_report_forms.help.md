# Distribution Timeliness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report instances where a delivery was not made on time. Please fill out this form to document the delay and help us identify areas for improvement in our delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are reporting a real instance of a delayed delivery. If you are unsure, check with a supervisor or colleague.
2. Enter the correct **form_location** (1) value, which should match the location where the delay occurred.
3. Enter the **delivery_date** (2) when the delivery was supposed to be made or when it was actually made.
4. Select the correct **delivery_type** (3), which can be On-time, Delayed, or Undeliverable.
5. If the delivery was delayed, enter the reason for the delay in the **delay_reason** (4) text field.
6. Select the **location** (5) where the delivery was supposed to be made.
7. Enter the **time** (6) when the delivery was supposed to be made.
8. Select the correct **time_zone** (7) for the location.
9. If needed, enter any additional **notes** (8) about the delivery.
10. If you have further contact information, enter your **email** (9) and **phone** (10) number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Location (1)**: The location where the delivery was supposed to be made.
* **Delivery Date (2)**: The date when the delivery was supposed to be made or the date it was actually made.
* **Delivery Type (3)**:
	+ On-time: The delivery was made within the expected timeframe.
	+ Delayed: The delivery was made after the expected date.
	+ Undeliverable: The delivery was not made at all.
* **Delay Reason (4)**: If the delivery was delayed, enter the reason for the delay.
* **Location (5)**: Select the location where the delivery was supposed to be made.
* **Time (6)**: Enter the time when the delivery was supposed to be made.
* **Time Zone (7)**: Select the time zone for the location, which helps with calculating time differences across regions.
* **Notes (8)**: Provide any additional comments or context about the delivery.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your contact email address, if you need to be contacted about the delivery.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your contact phone number, if you need to be contacted about the delivery.
