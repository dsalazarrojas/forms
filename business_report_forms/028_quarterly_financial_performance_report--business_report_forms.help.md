# quarterly financial performance report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Quarterly Financial Performance Report is used to collect and analyze a company's financial data across different quarters and years.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the quarter for which you want to report on.
2. Choose the financial year for which you want to report on.
3. Enter the company name.
4. Enter the financial period (date) for which you want to report on.
5. Enter the sales revenue.
6. Enter the costs associated with the financial period.
7. Enter the profit made during the financial period.
8. Enter the operating expenses.
9. Enter the operating income.
10. Enter the net profit.
11. Assess the financial risk (low, moderate, high).
12. Evaluate the financial performance (good, poor, excellent).
13. Determine the financial focus (short-term, mid-term, long-term).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quarter**: Select the quarter for which you want to report on. (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
* **Financial Year**: Choose the financial year for which you want to report on. (FY2019, FY2020, FY2021, FY2022)
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company. (Text input)
* **Financial Period**: Enter the date for which you want to report on. (Date input)
* **Sales**: Enter the sales revenue for the selected quarter and financial year. (Number input)
* **Costs**: Enter the costs associated with the financial period. (Number input)
* **Profit**: Enter the profit made during the financial period. (Number input)
* **Operating Expenses**: Enter the operating expenses for the selected quarter and financial year. (Number input)
* **Operating Income**: Enter the operating income for the selected quarter and financial year. (Number input)
* **Net Profit**: Enter the net profit for the selected quarter and financial year. (Number input)
* **Financial Risk**: Assess the financial risk (low, moderate, high) for the selected quarter and financial year.
* **Financial Performance**: Evaluate the financial performance (good, poor, excellent) for the selected quarter and financial year.
* **Financial Focus**: Determine the financial focus (short-term, mid-term, long-term) for the selected quarter and financial year.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete data for each field.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
- If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or financial team lead for guidance.
