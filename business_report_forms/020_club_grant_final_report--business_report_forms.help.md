# Club Grant Final Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Grant Final Report is a form used by clubs to submit their final report on their grant project. This report is essential for the grant committee to evaluate the project's performance and make informed decisions for future funding allocations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Report Submission Date** in the designated date field.
2. Provide the **Club Name** and **Grant ID or Reference Number** to identify your club and project.
3. Enter the **Original Grant Amount** to show the total amount awarded for the project.
4. Fill in the **Grant Period Start Date** and **Grant Period End Date** to define the project timeline.
5. Enter a brief **Project Title** that reflects the project's main goal.
6. Give a concise **Project Summary** of the project's objectives and achievements.
7. Indicate whether **Original Objectives Met** and provide more details on what was accomplished in the **Objective Details** field.
8. Enter the **Number of Participants Reached** to demonstrate the project's impact.
9. Provide the **Total Amount Spent** to show actual funds expended.
10. Detail the **Expenditure Breakdown by Category** to show how money was spent.
11. Indicate whether **Supporting Documentation** is attached or available.
12. If applicable, describe any **Challenges Encountered** faced during the project.
13. Share key **Lessons Learned** from the project.
14. Outline **Future Plans or Recommendations** for future projects or initiatives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Submission Date**: Enter the date the report is submitted.
* **Club Name**: Type in the name of your club or organization.
* **Grant ID or Reference Number**: Enter the unique identifier for your grant project.
* **Original Grant Amount**: Enter the total amount awarded for the project.
* **Grant Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the grant period.
* **Grant Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the grant period.
* **Project Title**: Type in a brief title that describes the project.
* **Project Summary**: Give a concise overview of the project, including its main goals and achievements.
* **Original Objectives Met**: Check if all, most, some, or few objectives were met or if objectives were not met at all.
* **Objective Details**: Provide more information on what was accomplished in relation to the objectives.
* **Number of Participants Reached**: Enter the total number of people impacted by the project.
* **Total Amount Spent**: Enter the actual funds expended during the project.
* **Expenditure Breakdown by Category**: Detail how money was spent, including categories and subcategories.
* **Supporting Documentation Attached**: Indicate if supporting documentation is attached or available.
* **Challenges Encountered**: Describe any obstacles or issues faced during the project.
* **Lessons Learned**: Share key takeaways from the project.
* **Future Plans or Recommendations**: Outline plans or recommendations for future projects or initiatives.
