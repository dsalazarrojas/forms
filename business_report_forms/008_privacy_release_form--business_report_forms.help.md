# Privacy Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify the personal information of individuals for business-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your employee ID (if applicable) in the Employee ID field.
4. Enter the name of your company in the Company field.
5. Enter your address information in the Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, and Zip fields.
6. Enter your email in the Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name, e.g., Doe.
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): Enter your employee ID, if you have one (e.g., 1234).
* **Company** (text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Address Line 1** (text, optional): Enter the first line of your address, e.g., 123 Main St.
* **Address Line 2** (text, optional): Enter the second line of your address, e.g., Apartment 101.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter your city or town.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to ensure the form's purpose is fulfilled correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator or the relevant department.
* Please note that this form is for business-related purposes only. If you are a visitor or guest, please do not complete this form.
