# Business Model Evaluation Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluators assess the business model of a company, providing a structured framework for evaluating its various components. The form covers key aspects of a business model, including its value proposition, customer segments, revenue streams, key resources, and competitive position. By filling out this form, evaluators can gain a comprehensive understanding of a company's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the date of your evaluation.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person conducting the evaluation.
3. Identify the company being evaluated, including its name and industry.
4. Rate the clarity and strength of the company's value proposition.
5. Assess the company's customer segments, including their definition and reach.
6. Determine the number of revenue streams and evaluate their sustainability and growth potential.
7. Evaluate the key resources and activities of the company.
8. Assess the company's competitive position and market viability.
9. Provide specific recommendations for improvement.
10. Identify key risks and mitigation strategies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date (1)** (`evaluation_date`, required): Enter the date of your evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name (2)** (`evaluator_name`, required): Enter your name as the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Email (3)** (`evaluator_email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Company Name (4)** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of the company being evaluated.
* **Industry (5)** (`company_industry`, required): Select the industry where the company operates.
* **Value Proposition - Clarity Score (6)** (`value_proposition_clarity`, required): Rate the clarity of the company's value proposition on a scale of 1-10.
* **Value Proposition - Strength Score (7)** (`value_proposition_strength`, required): Rate the strength of the company's value proposition on a scale of 1-10.
* **Customer Segment Definition Score (8)** (`customer_segment_definition`, required): Rate how well-defined the company's customer segments are on a scale of 1-10.
* **Customer Segment Reach Score (9)** (`customer_segment_reach`, required): Rate the company's ability to reach defined customer segments on a scale of 1-10.
* **Number of Revenue Streams (10)** (`revenue_streams_count`, optional): Enter the number of distinct revenue streams.
* **Revenue Stream Sustainability Score (11)** (`revenue_stream_sustainability`, required): Rate the sustainability of the company's revenue streams on a scale of 1-10.
* **Revenue Growth Potential Score (12)** (`revenue_stream_growth`, required): Rate the growth potential of the company's revenue streams on a scale of 1-10.
* **Key Resources Strength Score (13)** (`key_resource_strength`, required): Rate the company's key resources on a scale of 1-10.
* **Key Activities Efficiency Score (14)** (`key_activities_efficiency`, required): Rate the efficiency of the company's core activities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Partnership Quality Score (15)** (`partnership_quality`, optional): Rate the quality of the company's partnerships on a scale of 1-10.
* **Cost Structure Optimization Score (16)** (`cost_structure_optimization`, required): Rate the optimization of the company's cost structure on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Business Model Score (17)** (`overall_business_model_score`, required): Rate the overall assessment of the company's business model on a scale of 1-10.
* **Competitive Position (18)** (`competitive_position`, required): Select the company's competitive position (Very Strong, Strong, Moderate, Weak, Under Threat).
* **Market Viability (19)** (`market_viability`, required): Select the market viability (Highly Viable, Viable, Questionable, Risky, Not Viable).
* **Key Strengths (20)** (`key_strengths`, required): Enter the main business strengths.
* **Key Weaknesses (21)** (`key_weaknesses`, required): Enter areas that need improvement.
* **Improvement Recommendations (22)** (`improvement_recommendations`, required): Provide specific recommendations for improvement.
* **Risk Assessment (23)** (`risk_assessment`, optional): Enter key risks and mitigation strategies.
* **Implementation Priorities (24)** (`implementation_priorities`, optional): Enter what should be addressed first.

Note: This help guide assumes that you have filled out the form correctly, but if you need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
