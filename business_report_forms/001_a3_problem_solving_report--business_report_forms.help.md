# A3 Problem Solving Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The A3 Problem Solving Report is a tool for teams to document and analyze issues affecting their operations. It guides the process of problem identification, analysis, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "Reporter name" field.
2. Select the department or team responsible for the process in the "Department or team" field.
3. Describe the problem in the "Problem statement" field.
4. Summarize the current condition and how often the issue occurs in the "Current condition summary" field.
5. Identify the root causes of the problem in the "Root cause analysis summary" field.
6. Propose countermeasures to resolve the problem, selecting all applicable options in the "Proposed countermeasures" field.
7. Describe your implementation plan and expected completion dates in the "Implementation plan and timeline" field.
8. Define how success will be measured and review dates in the "Follow up metrics" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter name** (`Reporter name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person completing this report.
* **Department or team** (`Department or team`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department or team responsible for addressing this problem.
* **Problem statement** (`Problem statement`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of the issue you are trying to address.
* **Current condition summary** (`Current condition summary`, `note`, optional): Summarize what is happening now and how often the issue occurs. This is an optional but helpful field for providing context.
* **Root cause analysis summary** (`Root cause analysis summary`, `text`, required): Identify the key causes of the problem.
* **Proposed countermeasures** (`Proposed countermeasures`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all applicable actions to resolve the problem, such as process changes, trainings, tool updates, or staffing adjustments.
* **Implementation plan and timeline** (`Implementation plan and timeline`, `text`, optional): Describe your plan to address the problem and expected completion dates.
* **Follow up metrics** (`Follow up metrics`, `text`, optional): Define how success will be measured and review dates for the resolution of the problem.
