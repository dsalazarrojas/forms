# Investigation Findings Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investigation Findings Report form is designed to collect information about the progress and findings of an investigation. This form is used to gather data on the investigation's status, actions taken, and any evidence attached.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the investigation findings report form by selecting the "Investigation Findings Report" page.
2. Click on each field to access the corresponding input fields.
3. Enter the relevant information in each field, including:
	* Evidence attached: A description of any evidence collected or reviewed during the investigation.
	* Actions taken: A detailed description of any actions taken during the investigation.
	* Team reviewed: The team that reviewed or is currently reviewing the investigation.
	* Date completed: The date the investigation was completed.
	* Completed by: A list of team members who have reviewed or completed the investigation.
	* Status: The current status of the investigation (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
	* Comments: Any additional comments or notes about the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investigation Findings Report**: A summary of the investigation's findings and progress.
* **Evidence Attached**: A description of any evidence collected or reviewed during the investigation. Please provide a brief description of the evidence attached.
* **Actions Taken**: A detailed description of any actions taken during the investigation. Please provide a thorough explanation of the actions taken.
* **Team Reviewed**: Select the team that reviewed or is currently reviewing the investigation.
* **Date Completed**: The date the investigation was completed.
* **Completed By**: A list of team members who have reviewed or completed the investigation. Please select all applicable team members.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the investigation (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about the investigation. Please keep this field brief and focused on key points.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in the "Actions Taken" field.
* When selecting team members in the "Completed By" field, ensure all relevant team members are included.
* The "Date Completed" field should be in the correct date format.
* Keep the "Comments" field brief and focused on key points.
