# Venue Management Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Venue Management Report form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant pages for your report:
	* Event Status Update: Use this page to document changes to the status of an event space.
	* Incident Report: Report any incidents or issues that occurred in the event space.
	* Maintenance Request: Request maintenance for the event space.
	* Venue Details: Choose the specific event space(s) and floor(s) where the report applies.
	* Facility Details: Choose the specific floor(s) where the incident or maintenance request applies.
	* Maintenance Status: Update the status of maintenance requests.
	* Date Of Occurrence: Enter the date the incident or maintenance activity occurred.
	* Time Of Occurrence: Enter the time the incident or maintenance activity occurred.
	* Contact Info: Enter contact information for the person involved or responsible for the report.
	* Assigned To: Enter the name of the person or team assigned to handle the incident or maintenance request.
	* Incident Date: Enter the date the incident occurred.
	* Incident Time: Enter the time the incident occurred.
	* Incident Details: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
	* Maintenance Needed: Specify the maintenance needed for the event space.
	* Next Status Update: Enter the next status update for the maintenance request.
	* Status Update Comments: Add any comments or additional information for the next status update.
	* Report To: Enter the name of the person or team the report is being submitted to.
	* Contact Method: Enter how the report is being submitted (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
	* Contact Phone: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the report.
	* Contact Email: Enter the email address of the person submitting the report.
	* Incident ID: Enter the ID number for the incident.
	* Maintenance ID: Enter the ID number for the maintenance request.
	* Comments: Enter any additional comments for the report.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the report.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (dd/mm/yyyy and hh:mm:ss).
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant page description or contact the person responsible for the report.
* Keep the report concise and clear, but detailed and accurate.
* Proofread the report before submitting it to ensure it is complete and free of errors.
