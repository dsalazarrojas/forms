# Flower Inventory Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the current state of a flower inventory. It helps the user to report on the levels of flowers in stock, their condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options for "Flower Stock Levels" page to report on the current state of flowers in stock.
2. Choose whether flowers are in good condition or not in the "Flower Condition" section.
3. Enter the last date of replenishment for the flowers in the "Last Replenishment Date" field.
4. Enter the quantity of flowers replenished in the "Quantity Replenished" field.
5. Enter the total value of the flowers in the "Total Value" field.
6. Select the options for "Replenishment Needs" to indicate whether the flowers need to be replenished or not.
7. Select the type of flowers in the "Flower Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flower Stock Levels** (Page 1): This field reports on the current state of flowers in stock. You can select options to indicate the level of stock.
* **Flower Condition** (Page 1): Select this option to indicate whether flowers are in good condition or not.
* **Last Replenishment Date** (Page 1): Enter the last date when the flowers were replenished.
* **Quantity Replenished** (Page 1): Enter the quantity of flowers replenished.
* **Total Value** (Page 1): Enter the total value of the flowers.
* **Replenishment Needs** (Page 1): Select this option to indicate whether the flowers need to be replenished or not.
* **Flower Type** (Page 1): Select the type of flowers in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your team or supervisor for clarification.
* This form is intended for reporting on flower inventory levels, condition, and other relevant details. Please do not use it for other purposes.
