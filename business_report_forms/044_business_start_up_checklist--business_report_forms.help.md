# Business Start Up Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide new business owners and entrepreneurs through the essential steps of setting up and launching a successful business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the sections below. Please fill out each section as you progress through the start-up process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Idea Well-Defined** (`business_idea_defined`, select_one, required): Have you clearly defined your business concept? This field asks if you have a well-defined idea of what your business is about and what problem it solves. Please select the option that best describes your current state of your business idea.
* **Market Research Completed** (`market_research_completed`, select_one, required): Have you researched your market? This field asks if you have gathered information about your target audience, competitors, and market trends. Please select the option that best describes your current state of market research.
* **Business Plan Written** (`business_plan_written`, select_one, required): Do you have a written business plan? This field asks if you have a comprehensive document that outlines your business model, goals, and strategies. Please select the option that best describes your current state of your business plan.
* **Competitive Analysis Completed** (`competitive_analysis_done`, select_one, required): Have you analyzed your competitors? This field asks if you have studied your competitors' strengths, weaknesses, and market position. Please select the option that best describes your current state of competitive analysis.
* **Startup Budget Developed** (`startup_budget_developed`, select_one, required): Have you estimated your startup costs? This field asks if you have a budget for your business, including initial investment and ongoing expenses. Please select the option that best describes your current state of budget development.
* **Financial Projections Created** (`financial_projections`, select_one, required): Do you have 3-year financial projections? This field asks if you have a detailed financial plan, including income and expense projections, and cash flow statements. Please select the option that best describes your current state of financial projections.
* **Funding Secured** (`funding_secured`, select_one, required): Have you arranged financing for your business? This field asks if you have secured funding, including loans, grants, or investments. Please select the option that best describes your current state of funding.
* **Business Structure Chosen** (`business_structure_chosen`, select_one, required): Have you decided on a business structure? This field asks if you have decided on a business structure, such as sole proprietorship, LLC, or partnership. Please select the option that best describes your current state of business structure.
* **Business License Applied For** (`business_license_applied`, select_one, required): Have you applied for a business license? This field asks if you have submitted a license application for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of license application.
* **EIN Obtained** (`ein_obtained`, select_one, required): Have you obtained a Federal Tax ID number? This field asks if you have a Employer Identification Number (EIN) for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of EIN application.
* **Business Insurance Obtained** (`insurance_obtained`, select_one, required): Have you obtained business insurance? This field asks if you have secured liability insurance for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of insurance application.
* **Location Selected** (`location_selected`, select_one, required): Have you found a location for your business? This field asks if you have identified a suitable location for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of location search.
* **Equipment and Supplies Sourced** (`equipment_sourced`, select_one, required): Have you found suppliers and prices for equipment and supplies? This field asks if you have identified potential suppliers and prices for equipment and supplies. Please select the option that best describes your current state of supplier identification.
* **Technology Infrastructure Set Up** (`technology_setup`, select_one, required): Have you set up technology infrastructure for your business? This field asks if you have established a computer, phone, or internet infrastructure for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of technology setup.
* **Brand Identity Created** (`brand_identity_created`, select_one, required): Have you created a brand identity? This field asks if you have developed a logo, colors, and messaging for your brand. Please select the option that best describes your current state of branding.
* **Website Built** (`website_built`, select_one, required): Have you built a business website? This field asks if you have established a business website. Please select the option that best describes your current state of website development.
* **Marketing Plan Created** (`marketing_plan_created`, select_one, required): Have you created a marketing plan? This field asks if you have defined a strategy and tactics for promoting your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of marketing plan development.
* **Key Team Members Identified** (`team_identified`, select_one, required): Have you identified key team members? This field asks if you have found advisors, mentors, or co-founders for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of team identification.
* **Hiring Plan Created** (`hiring_plan_created`, select_one, required): Do you have a hiring plan? This field asks if you have a plan for hiring employees for your business. Please select the option that best describes your current state of hiring plan development.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form in a sequential manner, starting from the initial steps of business planning and moving on to financial planning, legal and compliance, operations setup, marketing and branding, and finally, staffing and team building.
* Make sure to review each section carefully and select the option that best describes your current state of progress.
* If you are unsure about any section, please reach out to a business advisor or mentor for guidance.
