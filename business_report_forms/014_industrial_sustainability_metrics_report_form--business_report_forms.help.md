# Industrial Sustainability Metrics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect industrial sustainability metrics for a company, allowing for evaluation and improvement of its environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2.  Ensure that you've understood the implications and purpose of each question before proceeding.
3.  Review the form for any missing or incorrect information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the company.
- **Company Sector** (`company_sector`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select whether the company is in the industry or not.
- **Company Location** (`company_location`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the company's location.
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of company.
- **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the reporting period for which the data is being reported.
- **Year** (`year`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the year for which the metrics are being reported.
- **ESG Scope 1** (`esg_scope1`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any relevant ESG Scope 1 information.
- **ESG Scope 2** (`esg_scope2`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select whether ESG Scope 2 is applicable.
- **ESG Scope 3** (`esg_scope3`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select whether ESG Scope 3 is applicable.
- **Carbon Intensity** (`carbon_intensity`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's carbon intensity.
- **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (`greenhouse_gas_emissions`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Water Intensity** (`water_intensity`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's water intensity.
- **Waste Generation** (`waste_generation`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's waste generation.
- **Renewable Energy** (`renewable_energy`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's renewable energy.
- **ESG Score** (`esg_score`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the company's ESG score.
- **ESG Category 1** (`esg_category1`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the ESG Category 1.
