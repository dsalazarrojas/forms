# Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form is designed to collect feedback from users on the vendor's performance. This form helps the organization to evaluate and improve the services or goods provided by the vendor. Please use this form to submit your feedback on a vendor who has provided services or goods that have not met your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the rating that best reflects your experience with the vendor.
3. Provide any additional comments you may have about your experience with the vendor.
4. Enter the date and time you submitted the form (if applicable).
5. Enter the vendor's name and ID (if applicable).
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Rating** (Vendor Rating, text, required/optional): Please select one of the four options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to rate your experience with the vendor. This will help us to quickly assess the quality of service or goods provided.
* **Rating Scale** (Rating Scale, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the four rating options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) that best describes your experience with the vendor.
* **Vendor Comments** (Vendor Comments, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have about your experience with the vendor. This is a text area where you can provide more detailed feedback.
* **Date Submitted** (Date Submitted, date, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (Time Submitted, time, required/optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Vendor Name** (Vendor Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor you are providing feedback on.
* **Vendor ID** (Vendor ID, text, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the vendor you are providing feedback on.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the vendor or their representative for further discussion or clarification.
