# Void Items Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Void Items Record Form is used to record information about items that are no longer available or have been removed from stock. This form helps to track and manage voided items, ensuring accurate inventory levels and preventing financial discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information:
	* **Void Items Record Form** (page 1): Enter the relevant information about the item that has been voided.
	* **Void Date** (page 2): Enter the date when the item was voided.
	* **Void Time** (page 3): Enter the time when the item was voided.
	* **Void User** (page 4): Enter the name of the person who voided the item.
	* **Void Item** (page 5): Enter the description of the item that was voided.
	* **Void Reason** (page 6): Enter the reason why the item was voided.
	* **Void Items Returned** (page 7): Enter any items that were returned or exchanged.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Void Items Record Form**: This is the main page where you will enter the details about the voided item.
* **Void Date**: This field is used to enter the date when the item was voided.
* **Void Time**: This field is used to enter the time when the item was voided.
* **Void User**: This field is used to enter the name of the person who voided the item.
* **Void Item**: This field is used to enter the description of the item that was voided.
* **Void Reason**: This field is used to enter the reason why the item was voided.
* **Void Items Returned**: This field is used to enter any items that were returned or exchanged.
