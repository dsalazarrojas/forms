# Factory Operation Efficiency Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on factory operations and help factory managers track and analyze the performance of their production processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and data ready.
2.  Select the "Equipment" field to enter the equipment ID.
3.  Enter the "Production Time" and "Production Capacity" to provide information about the production schedule.
4.  Select the "Shift" field to enter the shift ID.
5.  Enter the "Start Time" and "End Time" to provide information about the production schedule.
6.  Enter the "Production Cost" to provide information about the cost of production.
7.  Enter the "Equipment IDs" to provide additional information about the equipment used.
8.  Optionally, enter any "Comments" to provide additional information.
9.  Optionally, enter the "Factory IDs" to provide information about the factory associated with the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment** (`Equipment`, text, required):
	+ Enter the ID of the equipment used in the production process.
* **Production Time** (`Production Time`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the total production time for the reporting period.
* **Production Capacity** (`Production Capacity`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the total production capacity for the reporting period.
* **Shift** (`Shift`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the ID of the shift during which the production took place.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required):
	+ Enter the start time of the production.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required):
	+ Enter the end time of the production.
* **Production Cost** (`Production Cost`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the total cost of production.
* **Equipment IDs** (`Equipment IDs`, text, optional):
	+ Enter additional information about the equipment used.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about the production process.
* **Factory IDs** (`Factory IDs`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the ID of the factory associated with the report.
