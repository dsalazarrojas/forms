# Medical Abstract Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Abstract Submission Form is designed to collect essential information from researchers who want to submit their abstracts for medical research papers. This form helps streamline the submission process, making it easier for researchers to share their work with a wider audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* Provide a clear and concise title for your abstract presentation.
* Describe the equipment you will need for your presentation.
* Select the featured abstract topics that best relate to your research.
* Enter details about the institution where your research was conducted.
* Describe your study design and methodology.
* Submit your abstract for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Presentation** (`abstract_presentation`, text, required: false): Describe your abstract presentation in a few sentences, highlighting its key points and takeaways.
* **Equipment Necessities** (`equipment_necessities`, text, required: false): List the equipment you will need to present your abstract, including any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Featured Abstract Topics** (`abstract_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the abstract topics that best relate to your research. These topics are pre-defined for your convenience.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the institution where your research was conducted.
* **Study Design** (`study_design`, text, required: false): Describe the study design and methodology used in your research.
* **Methodology** (`methodology`, text, required: false): Outline the methodology behind your research, including any specific techniques or approaches.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting your form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
