# Research Paper Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Paper Upload Form is designed to collect information about research papers submitted for evaluation or review.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have provided all required information (indicated by a star *).
3. Upload the manuscript files as prompted.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number so that we can get in touch with you after a review or evaluation is completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paper Submission** (*: text, required): In this field, please upload the manuscript of your research paper. Make sure to include any additional information or context that may be helpful for the review process.
* **author_information** (*: text, required): This field is for additional information about yourself as the author. You can provide any details about your research, such as your name, affiliation, or institutional email.
* **Manuscript Files** (*: text, required): Upload any additional files required for the review process, such as references or supplemental materials.
* **Contact Email** (*: email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you after the review or evaluation is completed.
* **Contact Email** (repeat: email, optional): This is a duplicate of the "Contact Email" field, but it will be displayed differently in the submission system. Please enter the same email address as above if you want to use the same contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (*: text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you after the review or evaluation is completed.
* **Contact Phone** (repeat: text, optional): This is a duplicate of the "Contact Phone" field, but it will be displayed differently in the submission system. Please enter the same phone number as above if you want to use the same contact information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to use the same format for your email and phone number fields (e.g., if you enter a phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, enter it in the same format in the "Contact Phone Repeat" field). This will ensure that your contact information is displayed consistently throughout the system.
