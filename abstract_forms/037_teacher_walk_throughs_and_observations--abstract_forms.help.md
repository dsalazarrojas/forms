# Teacher Walk Throughs And Observations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document Teacher Walk Throughs and Observations, which are activities where educators engage in guided observations of student learning to inform instruction and improve teaching practices. The form captures information about the walk through, including the category, description, form ID, assigned tool, output file, date, and time. This form is intended to be completed by educators to record and report on their observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the "Category" field to select the category of the walk through.
3. Provide a detailed description of the walk through in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the unique "Form ID" that identifies the walk through.
5. Indicate if a tool was "Assigned" or not.
6. Upload or link the "Output File" associated with the walk through.
7. Enter the date of the walk through using the "Date" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. Select the time of the walk through using the "Time" field in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
9. Add any additional notes about the walk through in the "Note" field (optional).
10. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person for the walk through (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_category`, select_one, required) Select the category of the walk through from the options provided.
* **Description**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_description`, text, required) Provide a detailed description of the walk through.
* **Form ID**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_form_id`, number, required) Enter the unique ID that identifies the walk through.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_assigned_tool`, select_one, optional) Indicate if a tool was assigned to the walk through.
* **Output File**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_output_file`, text, required) Upload or link the output file associated with the walk through.
* **Date**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_date`, date, required) Enter the date of the walk through in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_time`, time, required) Select the time of the walk through in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Note**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_note`, note, optional) Add any additional notes about the walk through.
* **Email**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_email`, email, optional) Enter the email of the contact person for the walk through.
* **Phone**: (`teacher_walk_throughs_and_observations_phone`, text, optional) Enter the phone number of the contact person for the walk through.
