# **future-cities-project-submission - Help Guide**

## **Purpose**
The Future Cities Project Submission form is a critical part of the project's evaluation and selection process. This form allows you to submit a comprehensive proposal for your city's innovative idea, including essential details such as team composition, project timeline, and supporting documentation. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of your proposal being selected.

## **How To Complete This Form**
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Urban Innovation Proposal** field with a brief description of your project's innovative idea.
2. Provide detailed information about your **Team Details**, including the team members and their roles.
3. If your project involves a team, select **Yes** for **Team Members** field.
4. Specify the **University/Hackathon/City Innovation** type of your project.
5. Enter the proposed **Innovation Proposal Date** and **Innovation Proposal Time** for your project.
6. You can add any additional **Submission Description** and/or **Submission Files** if required or relevant to your project.
7. Ensure you've filled out all required fields and review your submission for accuracy.

## **Field-by-Field Explanation**
### **1. Urban Innovation Proposal**
 * **Urban Innovation Proposal**: Enter a brief description of your innovative idea, highlighting its key aspects and benefits. Keep it concise but informative, around 500-1000 characters or less.

### **2. Team Details**
* **Team Details**: Describe your project team, including their names, roles, and contributions to the project.

### **3. Team Members**
* **Team Members**: Indicate whether you have a team (Yes) or not (No). If you select **Yes**, you'll be able to add team member information in the **Team Details** field.

### **4. University/Hackathon/City Innovation**
* **University/Hackathon/City Innovation**: Select the type of innovation your project represents (e.g., University, Hackathon, City Innovation).

### **5. Innovation Proposal Date**
* **Innovation Proposal Date**: Enter the proposed date for your project's innovation proposal.

### **6. Innovation Proposal Time**
* **Innovation Proposal Time**: Enter the proposed time for your project's innovation proposal.

### **7. Submission Description**
* **Submission Description**: Provide a brief overview of your project, around 500-1000 characters or less.

### **8. Submission Files**
* **Submission Files**: You can add additional files or attachments that support your project's proposal, if necessary.

### **9. Submission Files 2**
* **Submission Files 2**: Another opportunity to add supporting files or attachments for your project.
