# Employee Start Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees report their daily progress, goals, and experiences. It's used to track and record the employee's workday, providing a clear overview of their activities and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the form and understanding what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter a brief description of your job title and responsibilities in the "job_title" field.
4. Provide a more detailed description of your job responsibilities and goals in the "job_description" field.
5. Enter the number of hours worked in the "work_hours" field.
6. Enter your expected salary in the "salary" field.
7. Select the relevant option from the "select_one" dropdown menu.
8. Select multiple relevant options from the "select_multiple" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the date of the report in the "date" field.
10. Enter the time of the report in the "time" field.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
14. Select the relevant option from the "select_one_2" dropdown menu.
15. Select multiple relevant options from the "select_multiple_2" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the date of the second report in the "date_2" field.
17. Enter the time of the second report in the "time_2" field.
18. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note_2" field.
19. Enter your email address in the "email_2" field.
20. Enter your phone number in the "phone_2" field.
21. Select the relevant option from the "select_one_3" dropdown menu.
22. Select multiple relevant options from the "select_multiple_3" dropdown menu.
23. Enter the date of the third report in the "date_3" field.
24. Enter the time of the third report in the "time_3" field.
25. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note_3" field.

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a free text field where you can enter a brief introduction about your day.

* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a more detailed description of your job responsibilities and goals.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you worked today.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, optional): Enter your expected salary.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple relevant options from the dropdown menu.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the report.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the report.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relevant option from the second dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple relevant options from the second dropdown menu.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the second report.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the second report.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.

* **Note 3** (`note_3`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or the project lead for clarification.
* Save a copy of your form for future reference.
