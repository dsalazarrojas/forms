# research_theory_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "research_theory_submission_form" is a form used for submitting research theories and papers. It is intended for authors to provide information about their published or unpublished research, including title, author details, abstract, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information, including theory title, author details (name, institution), and abstract.
* Select the subject area of the research.
* Upload a manuscript file, if necessary.
* If applicable, fill in the review date and time, as well as the name of the reviewer.
* Leave notes for the reviewer in the "Review" field, if needed.
* Select the submission status of the research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theory Title** (`theory_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the research theory.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the author or primary researcher.
* **Author Institution** (`author_institution`, text, required): Enter the institution affiliated with the author or primary researcher.
* **Abstract** (`abstract`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the research theory.
* **Manuscript** (`manuscript`, text, required): Enter the title of the manuscript associated with the research theory.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, optional): Select the subject area of the research (Yes or No).
* **Manuscript File** (`manuscript_file`, text, optional): Upload a file associated with the manuscript, if applicable.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review, if applicable.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the review, if applicable.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer, if applicable.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Enter notes for the reviewer.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current submission status of the research (Active or Inactive).
