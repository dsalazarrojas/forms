# Content Submission For Presentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about the presenter and their proposed presentation for the review committee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Presenter Information**
	* Start by filling out the presenter's name and email address. This will help the review committee contact you with any questions or requests.
2. **Presentation Details**
	* Provide a clear and concise title for your presentation, as well as an abstract that summarizes what the audience will learn. Ensure that the abstract is within the 200-300 word limit.
3. **Presentation Duration**
	* Choose the preferred duration of your presentation, taking into account the available time slots and the complexity of your content.
4. **Technical Requirements**
	* If your presentation has any special technical requirements (e.g., audio, projector, internet), select the relevant options.
5. **Submission History**
	* Indicate whether you have presented on a similar topic before, both in a similar conference setting or internally/local meetups.
6. **Submissions and Availability**
	* Confirm that you are willing to present for the full duration you have selected, and that you are available on the designated submission date.
7. **Additional Comments**
	* Provide any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to the review committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter Full Name**: A required field to collect the presenter's full name.
* **Presenter Email**: A required field to contact you with any questions or requests.
* **Professional Bio (Short)**: A required field to provide a brief overview of your background and experience.
* **Link to Professional Headshot**: A field to upload a link to your professional headshot, if available.
* **Presentation Title**: A required field to provide a clear and concise title for your presentation.
* **Session Abstract**: A required field to summarize what the audience will learn.
* **Target Audience Level**: A required field to indicate the level of knowledge the audience will need.
* **Preferred Session Duration**: A required field to select the duration of your presentation.
* **Technical Requirements**: A field to indicate any special technical requirements.
* **Have you presented on this topic before**: A field to indicate your previous presentation experience.
* **Date of Submission**: A required field to indicate the date of your submission.
* **I Agree to be present for the full event**: A required field to confirm your availability.
* **Additional Comments**: A field for any additional comments or information.
