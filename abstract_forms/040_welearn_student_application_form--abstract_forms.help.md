# Welearn Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Welearn Student Application Form is used to collect essential details from students who are applying to Welearn.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure it is filled out correctly.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welearn Student Application Form Title** (`welearn_student_application_form_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of your application, if desired.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Name** (`welearn_student_application_form_name`, text, not required): Enter your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Email** (`welearn_student_application_form_email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Phone** (`welearn_student_application_form_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Date of Birth** (`welearn_student_application_form_date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (e.g., mm/dd/yy or yy-mm-dd).
* **Welearn Student Application Form Time of Birth** (`welearn_student_application_form_time_of_birth`, time, not required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Program Start Time** (`welearn_student_application_form_program_start_time`, date, not required): Enter the start date of your desired program.
* **Welearn Student Application Form Program Finish Time** (`welearn_student_application_form_program_finish_time`, date, not required): Enter the finish date of your desired program.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Welearn team.
* You can only submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
