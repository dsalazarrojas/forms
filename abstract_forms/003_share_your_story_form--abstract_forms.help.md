# Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users share their personal stories or testimonials, allowing them to provide their account of events or experiences. The form collects a brief introduction, multiple sections of their story, and consent for sharing, followed by a review of their submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by writing a brief introduction to your story in the "Share Your Story" field.
2. Share more of your story in subsequent fields, answering the questions as prompted.
3. For each section, select one of the consent options ("I agree" or "I do not agree") to indicate your consent for sharing your story.
4. Finally, review your submission in the "Review Your Story" field before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Share Your Story** (1, Text, Optional): Write a brief introduction to your story.
* **What is Your Story?** (2, Text, Optional): Share more of your story.
* **Give Your Consent** (3, Select One, Optional): Indicate your consent for sharing your story. Select "I agree" to grant consent.
* **Share More of Your Story** (4, Text, Optional): Share more details of your story.
* **Give Consent** (5, Select One, Optional): Indicate your consent for sharing your story. Select "I agree" to grant consent.
* **Share Even More of Your Story** (6, Text, Optional): Share more of your story, if needed.
* **Consent 3** (7, Select One, Optional): Indicate your consent for sharing your story. Select "I agree" to grant consent.
* **Share the Rest of Your Story** (8, Text, Optional): Share the remaining parts of your story.
* **Consent 4** (9, Select One, Optional): Indicate your consent for sharing your story. Select "I agree" to grant consent.
* **Share Your Final Thoughts** (10, Text, Optional): Share your final thoughts or closing statement.
* **Consent 5** (11, Select One, Optional): Indicate your consent for sharing your story. Select "I agree" to grant consent.
* **Review Your Story** (12, Text, Optional): Review and confirm your submission before submitting the form.
