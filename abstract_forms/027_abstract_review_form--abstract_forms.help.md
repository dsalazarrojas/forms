# Abstract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Abstract Review Form is designed to gather feedback from reviewers on various aspects of an abstract, including its relevance, originality, clarity, methodology, and overall quality. This feedback will help authors improve their work and provide a more comprehensive evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your reviewer information (Reviewer full name, Email address, and Date of review).
2. Rate the abstract's relevance, originality, clarity, methodology, and overall quality using the provided scales.
3. Provide a recommendation (Accept, Accept with minor revisions, Revise and resubmit, or Reject).
4. List the abstract's key strengths and weaknesses.
5. Include any confidential comments for the review committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer full name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Fill out your full name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer email address** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of review** (`review_date`, date, required): Select the date when you reviewed the abstract.
* **Abstract reference ID or number** (`abstract_id`, text, required): Enter the abstract's reference ID or number.
* **Title of the abstract** (`abstract_title`, text, required): Fill out the abstract's title.
* **Author name(s)** (`author_name`, text, required): Enter the name(s) of the abstract's author(s).
* **Relevance to the topic or program** (`relevance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the abstract's relevance using the provided scale.
* **Originality and contribution** (`originality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the abstract's originality using the provided scale.
* **Clarity and quality of writing** (`clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the abstract's clarity using the provided scale.
* **Soundness of methodology or approach** (`methodology_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the abstract's methodology using the provided scale.
* **Overall quality rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the abstract's overall quality using the provided scale.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose a recommendation based on the provided options.
* **Key strengths of the abstract** (`strengths`, text, required): List the abstract's key strengths.
* **Key weaknesses or areas for improvement** (`weaknesses`, text, optional): List the abstract's key weaknesses or areas for improvement.
* **Confidential comments for the review committee** (`confidential_comments`, text, optional): Provide any confidential comments for the review committee.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing constructive and fair feedback to help authors improve their work. Remember to review the abstract carefully before providing your evaluation and recommendation.
