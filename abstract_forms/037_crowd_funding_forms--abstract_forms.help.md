# crowd_funding_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for crowd funding purposes, specifically to collect information about forms for a project or campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the **Form Name** field.
2. Enter a title for the reward in the **Reward Name** field.
3. Enter a description of the reward in the **Reward Description** field.
4. Enter a monetary value for the reward in the **Reward Value** field.
5. Enter the name of the contributor in the **Contributor Name** field.
6. Enter the email of the contributor in the **Email** field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contributor in the **Phone** field.
8. Enter any relevant experience or background information about the contributor in the **Experience** field.
9. Finally, select the desired option in the **Submit** field to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (text, optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Reward Name** (text, optional): Enter a title for the reward.
* **Reward Description** (text, optional): Enter a description of the reward.
* **Reward Value** (number, optional): Enter a monetary value for the reward.
* **Contributor Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contributor.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the contributor.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contributor.
* **Experience** (text, optional): Enter any relevant experience or background information about the contributor.
* **Submit** (select multiple, required): Select whether to **Submit Form**, **Save as Draft**, or **Cancel** the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure it is correct and complete.
* If you encounter any errors, try to submit or save the form again to review and correct your entries.
