# Healthcare Research Study Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Research Study Submission Form is designed to collect information from investigators and institutions about their research studies. This form helps streamline the study submission process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Investigator Name field with the name of the principal investigator or contact person associated with the study.
2. Provide the name of the institution where the study will be conducted in the Institution field.
3. Enter a brief description of the research proposal in the Research Proposal field.
4. Upload any relevant supporting documents in the Supporting Documents field.
5. Enter the contact number of the investigator or institution in the Contact Number field.
6. Enter the email address of the investigator or institution in the Email field.
7. Specify the start date of the study in the Study Start Date field.
8. Specify the end date of the study in the Study End Date field.
9. Enter the duration of the study in the Study Duration field.
10. Specify the funding source for the study in the Funding Source field.
11. Provide a detailed description of the study in the Study Description field.

* **Investigator Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the principal investigator or contact person associated with the study.
* **Institution** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution where the study will be conducted.
* **Research Proposal** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the research proposal.
* **Supporting Documents** (text, required/optional): Upload any relevant supporting documents for the study.
* **Contact Number** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the investigator or institution.
* **Email** (text, required/optional): Enter the email address of the investigator or institution.
* **Study Start Date** (date, required/optional): Specify the start date of the study.
* **Study End Date** (date, required/optional): Specify the end date of the study.
* **Study Duration** (number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the study.
* **Funding Source** (text, required/optional): Enter the funding source for the study.
* **Study Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the study.
