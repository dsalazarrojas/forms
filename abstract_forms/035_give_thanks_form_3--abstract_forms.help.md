# Give Thanks Form 3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Give Thanks Form 3" is a form designed to collect feedback and information from users about their interactions with specific individuals or entities. It is intended to gather details about the frequency and nature of these interactions, as well as any notable events or circumstances surrounding them. This form will help us better understand how our users interact with our platform and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the individual you are thankful for.
2. Choose the specific reasons you are thankful for.
3. Indicate the frequency of your interactions.
4. Provide the time of day you usually meet with them.
5. Choose how old the individual is.
6. Confirm if the individual has been assigned to you.
7. Select a background image to represent your interaction.
8. Indicate how many years you've interacted with them.
9. Select any notable characteristics about your interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Image**: Choose a background image that represents your interaction with the individual. This will help us better understand the context of your interaction.
* **Who Are You Thankful For?**: Select the individual you are thankful for. This could be a person, organization, or entity.
* **What Are You Thankful For?**: Describe the reasons you are thankful for. This could be anything from personal support to professional benefits.
* **How Often Do You Meet Them?**: Indicate the frequency of your interactions with the individual. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
* **What Time Did You Meet Them?**: Enter the time of day you usually meet with them. This will help us understand your schedule and how we can best accommodate your interactions.
* **How Old Are They?**: Choose the age range of the individual. This will help us understand their demographic profile.
* **Assigned To**: Confirm if the individual has been assigned to you. This ensures that the interaction is legitimate and relevant to your context.
* **Assigned By**: Choose who assigned the individual to you. This helps us understand the chain of command and authority involved.
* **Email Address**: This is an optional field for you to provide your email address, which will help us contact you for follow-up or clarification.
* **Phone Number**: This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number, which will help us contact you in case we need to reach you.
* **Notes**: Write any additional comments or details about your interaction with the individual. This is an optional field for you to provide context and insights.
* **How Often Do You Meet Them Often?**: This field is not strictly necessary, but it helps us understand the regularity of your interactions.
* **Time Of Day**: This field is not strictly necessary, but it helps us understand your schedule.
* **How Many Years?**: This field is not strictly necessary, but it helps us understand the duration of your interactions.
* **Question 10**, **Question 11**, **...**, **Question 16**: These fields are optional and not strictly necessary. They provide additional context and insights about your interaction, but are not required to be filled out.
