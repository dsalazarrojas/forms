# Online Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Risk Assessment form is used to collect information related to online risk assessments. It's essential to review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness of your responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the relevant option that best answers the question.
3. For text fields, enter your response in the provided text box.
4. For numerical fields, enter a numerical value in the correct format (e.g., integer or decimal).
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
