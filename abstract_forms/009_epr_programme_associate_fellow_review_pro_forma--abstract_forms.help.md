# epr_programme_associate_fellow_review_pro_forma - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to associate a fellow review with a program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the first name and last name of the individual to be associated with the review.
2. Enter the email of the individual.
3. Enter the phone number of the individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the individual to be associated with the review.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the individual to be associated with the review.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the individual to be associated with the review.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the individual to be associated with the review.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct information for the individual to be associated with the review.
* Double-check that the individual is aware of their association with the review.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or the process, please contact your supervisor or system administrator.
