# Tech Competition Project Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting project proposals to a tech competition. It captures essential information about the project, including team details and project tags.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide a brief description of your project, including a link to the project's web URL if applicable.
4. Confirm that you are submitting a project proposal (select "Team Project Submission" or other options).
5. Confirm that the team members are correctly represented (select "Team Member 1" to "Team Member 5", or other options).
6. Confirm that the files are correctly selected (select "File 1" to "File 5", or other options).
7. Choose relevant project and team tags.
8. Select a category for your project.
9. Optionally, you can provide additional project and team tags.
10. Finally, submit your project proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (not required).
* **Team Size** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of team members.
* **Project Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief title for your project.
* **Project Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a description of your project.
* **Project URL** (Text, Optional): Enter the URL of your project (if available).
* **Project File** (Text, Optional): Enter the path to a file associated with your project.
* **Confirm Project** (Select, Optional): Confirm that this is a project proposal.
* **Confirm Team** (Select, Optional): Confirm that the team members listed are correct.
* **Confirm File** (Select, Optional): Confirm that the files listed are correct.
* **Project Tags** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose relevant tags for your project.
* **Team Tags** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose relevant tags for your team.
* **Project Category** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose a category for your project.
* **Project Tags** (Text, Optional): Enter additional project tags.
* **Team Tags** (Text, Optional): Enter additional team tags.
* **Project Description 2** (Text, Optional): Enter an extended description of your project.
* **Project Tags 2** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose additional tags for your project.
* **Team Tags 2** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose additional tags for your team.
* **Project Category 2** (Select, Multiple, Optional): Choose an additional category for your project.
* **Project File 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the path to an additional file associated with your project.
