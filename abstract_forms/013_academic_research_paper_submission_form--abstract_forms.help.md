# Academic Research Paper Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields required for the successful submission of academic research papers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the label. These fields include:
    * Corresponding author full name
    * Corresponding author email
    * Institutional affiliation
    * Title of the paper
    * Abstract (250 words maximum)
    * Keywords (3-5 keywords separated by commas)
    * Subject area or discipline
    * Type of submission
    * Target journal or conference name
    * Approximate word count of the manuscript
    * Funding sources or grant numbers (if applicable)
    * Does this research involve human subjects or animals requiring ethical approval
    * Conflict of interest declaration (if yes, describe the conflict)
    * Has this paper been previously submitted to another journal or conference
    * Date of submission
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Corresponding author full name**: Please enter the name of the corresponding author for this submission.
*   **Corresponding author email**: Enter the email address of the corresponding author for contact purposes.
*   **Institutional affiliation**: Enter the name of the institution where you are affiliated with.
*   **Title of the paper**: Enter the title of your research paper.
*   **Abstract (250 words maximum)**: Provide a concise summary of your research, limited to 250 words.
*   **Keywords (3-5 keywords separated by commas)**: Enter 3-5 relevant keywords for your paper.
*   **Subject area or discipline**: Select the subject area or discipline relevant to your research.
*   **Type of submission**: Choose the type of submission that best describes your paper.
*   **Target journal or conference name**: Enter the name of the target journal or conference for this submission.
*   **Approximate word count of the manuscript**: Enter the approximate word count of your manuscript.
*   **Funding sources or grant numbers (if applicable)**: Enter any funding sources or grant numbers relevant to your research.
*   **Does this research involve human subjects or animals requiring ethical approval**: Indicate if your research involves human subjects or animals and whether you have obtained or are pending ethical approval.
*   **Conflict of interest declaration**: Declare if you have any conflict of interest; if yes, please describe the conflict.
*   **Has this paper been previously submitted to another journal or conference**: Indicate if your paper has been previously submitted to another journal or conference.
*   **Date of submission**: Enter the date of submission.
