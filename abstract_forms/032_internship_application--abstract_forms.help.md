# internship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for an internship opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Information**: Provide your basic user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, such as your email and phone number.
3. **Upload Resume**: If applicable, upload a copy of your resume.
4. **Education Background**: Share your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
5. **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience, if applicable.
6. **Skills**: Select the skills you have, such as Software Development, Cloud Computing, Database Management, and Data Analysis.
7. **Languages**: Choose the languages you are proficient in, such as English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, and Spanish.
8. **Career Goal**: Describe your career goal and what you are looking for in an internship.
9. **Motivation Statement**: Explain why you are a good fit for this internship opportunity.
10. **Expected Start Date**: Enter your expected start date for the internship.
11. **Expected End Date**: Enter your expected end date for the internship.
12. **Work Status**: Select your current work status, such as Part-time, Full-time, or Intern.
13. **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start the internship.
