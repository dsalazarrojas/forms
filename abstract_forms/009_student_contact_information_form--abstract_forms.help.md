# Student Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Contact Information Form is designed to collect essential details about students, including their contact information and emergency contacts. This form helps maintain accurate and up-to-date student records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name.
2. Enter the student's last name.
3. Provide the student's email address.
4. Enter the student's address.
5. Add the student's phone number.
6. For the emergency contact, enter the first name of the person.
7. Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
8. Select the relationship to the student (e.g., parent, guardian, family member).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the student.
* **Last Name**: (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the student.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Address**: (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the student's address.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Student**: (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Select the relationship to the student (e.g., parent, guardian, family member).
