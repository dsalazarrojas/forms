# Scientific Research Abstract Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scientific Research Abstract Entry Form is designed for researchers to submit a concise and informative abstract of their scientific research project. It helps in collecting and organizing research projects' information for future reference and potential collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have not already submitted a similar research abstract.
2. Choose the correct options for each field according to your research's requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Title** (`research_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of your research project.
* **Research Abstract** (`research_abstract`, text, optional): Provide a concise and informative abstract of your research project (max 500 characters).
* **Research Keywords** (`research_keywords`, text, optional): Enter relevant keywords related to your research project.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_info`, select_one, optional):
	+ 'Yes': You have completed a research project in the past.
	+ 'No': You have not completed a research project in the past.
* **Writer Information** (`writer_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ 'Yes': You have writing experience in the research field.
	+ 'No': You have not writing experience in the research field.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, select_one, optional):
	+ 'Yes': You have a resume related to your research project.
	+ 'No': You do not have a resume related to your research project.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, optional): If you have a resume, attach it here.
* **Entry Form** (`entry_form`, text, optional): This field is not used in the current form, but it's left here for any other information you might need to include.
* **Research Information** (`research_info`, text, optional): This field is not used in the current form, but it's left here for any other information you might need to include.
* **Research Abstract Multiple** (`research_abstract`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ 'Yes': You have multiple abstracts for different research projects.
	+ 'No': You do not have multiple abstracts for different research projects.

Note: Some fields may seem redundant, but they are left here for future development or customization purposes.
