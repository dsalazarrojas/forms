# Conference Poster Presentation Proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Poster Presentation Proposal form is designed to gather information for conference presenters to submit their proposals and showcase their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your presenter information:
	* Enter your full name in the "Presenter full name" field.
	* Provide a valid contact email in the "Presenter email address" field.
	* Select your institution or organization in the "Institution or company" field.
2. Add co-authors (optional):
	* If applicable, list co-authors in the "Co authors" field, separated by commas.
3. Enter poster details:
	* Enter your poster title in the "Poster title" field. Keep it under 15 words when possible.
	* Write a 250-500 word abstract summary in the "Abstract summary" field.
4. Select keywords and preferred session track (optional):
	* Choose up to 5 keywords from the available options in the "Keywords" field.
	* Select your preferred session track from the available options in the "Preferred session track" field.
5. Add poster dimensions and accessibility requirements (optional):
	* Enter your preferred poster dimensions in the "Poster dimensions preference" field, using meters or inches.
	* If necessary, indicate any accessibility requirements in the "Accessibility requirements" field.
6. Confirm agreement:
	* Select "True" in the "Agreement to display poster if accepted" field to confirm that you agree to display your poster if it is accepted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter full name (Presenter full name)**: Enter your full name as you want it to appear on the conference program.
* **Presenter email address (Presenter email)**: Provide a valid contact email for conference staff to use for notifications.
* **Institution or company (Presenter affiliation)**: Select your institution or organization.
* **Co authors (Co authors)**: List co-authors separated by commas.
* **Poster title (Poster title)**: Enter your poster title, keeping it under 15 words when possible.
* **Abstract summary (Abstract summary)**: Write a 250-500 word abstract summary of your research.
* **Keywords (Keywords)**: Select up to 5 keywords from the available options.
* **Preferred session track (Preferred session track)**: Select your preferred session track from the available options.
* **Poster dimensions preference (Poster dimensions)**: Indicate your preferred poster dimensions using meters or inches.
* **Accessibility requirements (Accessibility requirements)**: If necessary, list any accessibility requirements for your poster.
* **Agreement to display poster if accepted (Agreement to display poster if accepted)**: Select "True" to confirm that you agree to display your poster if it is accepted.
