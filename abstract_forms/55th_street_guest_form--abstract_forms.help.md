# 55th Street Guest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 55th Street Guest Form is a form designed to collect essential information from guests visiting 55th Street, a boarding house or hostel. This information enables smooth guest management and ensures a comfortable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to sign the guest consent terms and provide a typed signature for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest full name** (`guest_full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of birth** (`guest_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your legal birth date for identification purposes.
* **Email address** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for booking confirmations and notices.
* **Phone number** (`guest_phone`, phone, required): Enter your best contact number while on site.
* **Arrival date** (`stay_arrival_date`, date, required): Choose the date you plan to arrive.
* **Departure date** (`stay_departure_date`, date, required): Choose the date you plan to leave.
* **Room or space preference** (`room_or_space_requested`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred accommodation type subject to availability.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, phone, required): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact, including the country code if applicable.
* **Vehicle registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and registration number if you plan to bring a vehicle.
* **Expected arrival time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Provide an approximate time of arrival.
* **I agree to the host's terms, house rules, and liability waiver** (`guest_consent_terms`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Signature (Typed full name)** (`guest_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name to sign the form.
* **Special requests or accessibility needs** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Describe any special requests or accessibility needs you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the guest consent terms and sign the form before submitting.
* If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, please describe them in the "Special requests" field.
