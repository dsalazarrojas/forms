# Culture Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal details and cultural background information from users.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in their respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to help us contact you if needed.
3. If applicable, provide your **Date Of Birth** to help us understand your age and demographics.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for additional contact information.
5. Select your **Culture** from the provided options.
6. Select your **Language** from the provided options.
7. Add any additional **Notes** you'd like to provide about your cultural background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Culture** (`culture`, select_one, required): Select the culture that best describes you.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Select the language you use most frequently.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your cultural background that you'd like to share.

**Tips**
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Use the standard date format for the **Date Of Birth** field.
* Select only one culture and language from the provided options.
* Use the space provided for **Notes** to provide any additional information you'd like to share.
