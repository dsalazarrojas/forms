# Veterinary Conference Presentation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for speakers to submit their presentations to the veterinary conference. It includes fields for abstract, speaker details, session proposal, speaker image, session time, speaker bio, speaker phone, speaker email, and presentation date.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out.
*   Review the form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Abstract** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): The abstract is a short summary of your presentation. It should be a concise and clear description of your session.
*   **Speaker Details** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to 3 relevant options for speaker details.
    *   Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
*   **Session Proposal** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you provide a detailed outline of your session.
*   **Speaker Image** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for speaker image.
*   **Session Time** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one relevant option for session time.
    *   Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
*   **Speaker Bio** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for speaker bio.
*   **Speaker Phone** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): A text field for speaker phone.
*   **Speaker Email** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): A field for speaker email address. Ensure it is a valid email address.
*   **Presentation Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): A date field for the presentation date.

## Tips

*   Be sure to provide accurate and clear information for each field.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out.
*   Review the form before submitting.
