# health_conference_presentation_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `health_conference_presentation_submission_form` is designed to collect information for presentations to be submitted to a health conference. This form is used to gather details about the presentation, including its abstract, author information, and session details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief abstract of your presentation in the **Abstract** field.
2. If you have co-authors for your presentation, select the corresponding options in the **Authors Info** field.
3. Enter the title of your presentation in the **Session Title** field.
4. Describe your presentation in the **Session Description** field.
5. Indicate whether your presentation has co-authors by selecting the relevant option in the **Authors** field.
6. Enter your contact information in the **Email**, **Phone**, and **File** fields.
7. Enter the date and time of your presentation in the **Date** and **Time** fields respectively.
8. If applicable, upload any additional file related to your presentation in the **File Upload** field.
9. Indicate the purpose of your presentation in the **Session Presentation Purpose** field.
10. Specify the duration of your presentation in the **Session Presentation Duration** field.
11. Enter the location of your presentation in the **Session Location** field.
12. Choose the level of your presentation (High or Low) in the **Session Level** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Abstract** (text, required): Enter a brief summary of your presentation.
* **Authors Info** (select multiple, optional): Select whether your presentation has co-authors.
* **Session Title** (text, optional): Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Session Description** (text, optional): Describe your presentation in more detail.
* **Authors** (select multiple, optional): Indicate whether your presentation has co-authors.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your presentation.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your presentation.
* **File Upload** (text, optional): Upload any additional file related to your presentation.
* **File** (text, optional): Enter the name of any additional file related to your presentation.
* **Session Presentation Purpose** (select one, optional): Indicate the purpose of your presentation.
* **Session Presentation Duration** (number, optional): Specify the duration of your presentation.
* **Session Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your presentation.
* **Session Level** (select multiple, optional): Choose the level of your presentation (High or Low).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting your form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided guidelines or seek further clarification from the conference organizers.
