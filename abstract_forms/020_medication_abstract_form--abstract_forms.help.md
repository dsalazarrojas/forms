# medication_abstract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about medication shipments for abstract purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields:
	- shipment_details (text)
	- purchase_info (text)
	- medication_details (text)
	- shipment_date (date)
	- shipment_time (time)
	- medication_price (number)
	- shipping_cost (number)
	- delivery_status (select from Active or Inactive)
	- medication_id (number)
	- purchase_date (date)
	- customer_name (select multiple options)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipment_details** (shipment_details, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the shipment, if needed.
* **purchase_info** (purchase_info, text, not required): Provide any relevant information about the purchase, if needed.
* **medication_details** (medication_details, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the medication, if needed.
* **shipment_date** (shipment_date, date, not required): Enter the date of the shipment.
* **shipment_time** (shipment_time, time, not required): Enter the time of the shipment.
* **medication_price** (medication_price, number, not required): Enter the price of the medication.
* **shipping_cost** (shipping_cost, number, not required): Enter the cost of shipping.
* **delivery_status** (delivery_status, select_one, not required): Select either Active or Inactive as the delivery status.
* **medication_id** (medication_id, number, not required): Enter the ID of the medication.
* **purchase_date** (purchase_date, date, not required): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **customer_name** (customer_name, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more of the Yes or No options. 

## Tips
This form is for testing purposes only, so please use it responsibly and in a controlled environment. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the form administrator.
