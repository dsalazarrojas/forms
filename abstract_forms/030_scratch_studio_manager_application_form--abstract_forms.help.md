# Scratch Studio Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Scratch Studio Manager Application Form" is used to collect information from applicants for studio management positions within the company. This form helps to gather essential details about the applicant's qualifications, experience, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form with the required information. Each field is designed to help us understand your qualifications and experience as a candidate for the studio manager position. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the studio or company you are applying to.
* **Email (Email)**: Provide your professional email address for future correspondence.
* **Job Description (Text)**: Briefly describe your understanding of the studio manager role and your qualifications for it.
* **Studio Manager Position (Select One)**: Indicate whether you have prior experience as a studio manager or not.
* **Job Type (Text)**: If applicable, please specify the type of job or position you are interested in.
* **Location (Text)**: Mention your location or the location you are willing to work in.
* **Resume (Note)**: Attach your resume or CV, which should be in PDF or Word format.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Provide your contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Application Letter (Text)**: Attach an optional letter of application, explaining why you are a good fit for the position. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Attach all required documents, like your resume.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it's correct.
