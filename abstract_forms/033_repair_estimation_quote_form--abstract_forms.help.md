# Repair Estimation Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Estimation Quote Form is designed to collect necessary information from a customer and a service provider for the estimation of repair costs. This form helps ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of the job details, quote terms, and their respective responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Info** field with your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about the **Job Details** in the space provided.
3. Enter the **Quote Info**, including any relevant pricing and services needed.
4. Sign and date the **Customer Signature** field to confirm your understanding of the quote.
5. Enter your **Service Provider Info** and sign the **Service Provider Signature** field to confirm your acceptance of the quote.
6. Select "True" if you **Acceptable** for the **Quote Terms Confirmation** field.
7. Confirm that the quote terms are **Acceptable** by selecting "Acceptable" in the **Quote Terms Confirmation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information to allow us to get in touch with you regarding the repair estimate.
* **Job Details** (Job Details, text, required: false): Describe the job in detail, including any necessary parts or services needed for the repair.
* **Quote Info** (Quote Info, text, required: false): Enter any additional pricing or services needed for the job.
* **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, note, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm your understanding of the quote.
* **Service Provider Info** (Service Provider Info, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information as the service provider.
* **Service Provider Signature** (Service Provider Signature, note, required: false): Sign and date this field to confirm your acceptance of the quote.
* **Service Provider Terms** (Service Provider Terms, note, required: false): This field is for the service provider to confirm their understanding of their terms and responsibilities.
* **Customer Confirmation** (Customer Confirmation, select_one, required: false): Select "True" if you confirm that you have read and understand the quote.
* **Service Provider Confirmation** (Service Provider Confirmation, select_one, required: false): Select "True" to confirm that you have reviewed the quote and understand its terms.
* **Quote Terms Confirmation** (Quote Terms Confirmation, select_one, required: false): Select "Acceptable" if you confirm that the quote terms are acceptable.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the entire form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Double-check the quote terms and confirm that you understand them before proceeding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for internal use only and does not guarantee the accuracy of the repair estimate.
