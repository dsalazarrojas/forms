# Paid Clock Time Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paid Clock Time Form is designed to help employees accurately track their paid time by recording clock in and out times and hours worked. This helps ensure accurate payroll and employee record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are clocking in for work at the correct time.
2. Review the form carefully and enter your clock in time in the "clock in time" field.
3. Enter your clock out time in the "clock out time" field.
4. Enter the hours you have worked during this period in the "work hours" field.
5. Submit the form to record your clock in and out times and hours worked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clock in time**: Enter the exact time you started working today (e.g., 08:00).
* **Clock out time**: Enter the exact time you finished working today (e.g., 17:30).
* **Work hours**: Enter the number of hours you worked during this period (e.g., 8).
