# Security Officer Daily Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Officer Daily Shift Form is used for reporting incidents that occurred during a security officer's shift. It helps to document and track incidents, gather information about the incident, and provide a clear understanding of what happened during the shift. This form is a crucial tool for security officers to accurately report their experiences and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2.  Fill in the **daily_shift** field with a brief description of your daily shift.
3.  If an incident was reported, fill in the **incident_reported** field with the number of incidents reported during the shift.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the **incident_description** field.
5.  If applicable, select the type of incident that occurred in the **incident_type** field.
6.  Record any observations you made during the shift in the **observation_reported** field.
7.  Describe your observations in detail in the **observation_description** field.
8.  If the incident was investigated, indicate whether it was **incident_investigated**.
9.  If the incident was investigated, describe the reason why it was investigated in the **incident_investigated_why** field.
10.  If the incident was investigated, provide the name of the person who investigated it in the **incident_investigated_who** field.
11.  If the incident was investigated and you want to provide more information, use the **incident_investigated_other** field.
12.  If the incident was investigated, select the date of the investigation in the **incident_investigated_location** field.
13.  If the incident was investigated, provide a description of the investigation in the **incident_investigated_description** field.
14.  If the incident was investigated, describe the actions taken during the investigation in the **incident_investigated_actions_taken** field.
15.  If the incident was investigated and you want to provide more information about the actions taken, use the **incident_investigated_actions_taken_other** field.
16.  If you have a start time for your shift, fill in the **shift_start_time** field.
17.  If you have an end time for your shift, fill in the **shift_start_time_ending** field.
18.  If you want to report the duration of your shift, fill in the **shift_duration** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **daily_shift** (Required): Enter a brief description of your daily shift.
* **incident_reported** (Optional): Report the number of incidents you encountered during your shift.
* **incident_description** (Optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **incident_type** (Optional): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **observation_reported** (Optional): Record any observations you made during the shift.
* **observation_description** (Optional): Describe your observations in detail.
* **incident_investigated** (Optional): Indicate whether the incident was investigated.
* **incident_investigated_why** (Optional): Describe the reason why the incident was investigated.
* **incident_investigated_who** (Optional): Provide the name of the person who investigated the incident.
* **incident_investigated_other** (Optional): Provide more information about the incident investigation.
* **incident_investigated_location** (Optional): Select the date of the investigation.
* **incident_investigated_description** (Optional): Describe the investigation in detail.
* **incident_investigated_actions_taken** (Optional): Describe the actions taken during the investigation.
* **incident_investigated_actions_taken_other** (Optional): Provide more information about the actions taken during the investigation.
* **shift_start_time** (Optional): Fill in the start time of your shift.
* **shift_start_time_ending** (Optional): Fill in the end time of your shift.
* **shift_duration** (Optional): Report the duration of your shift.
