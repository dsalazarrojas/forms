# Doula Service Timesheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Service Timesheet is a form used to track the service hours worked by doulas for clients. It's essential to accurately document client interactions, service hours, and any relevant details to ensure efficient agency management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as required.
2. Ensure that you only complete the form for unique client sessions.
3. Be accurate and clear in your reporting to facilitate easy review and reference by the agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Hours (1)** (`service_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked for the client.
* **Visit Details (2)** (`visit_details`, text, optional): Describe the details of the visit, including any relevant events or activities.
* **Client Session (3)** (`client_session`, date, optional): Select the date of the client session.
* **Service Type (4)** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of service provided (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Notes (5)** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the client or session.
* **Client Email (6)** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (7)** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Agency (8)** (`agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of the agency you're working with.
* **Doula Name (9)** (`doula_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the doula providing the service.
