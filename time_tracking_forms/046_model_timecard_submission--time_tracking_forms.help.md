# Model Timecard Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Model Timecard Submission" form is a tool for users to submit their time tracking data for specific jobs. It helps in maintaining an accurate record of hours worked, start and end times, and other relevant details for each job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant job from the "Job" field.
2. Enter the date of submission using the "submission_date" field.
3. Choose the start and end times for the job using the "start_time" and "end_time" fields.
4. Enter the total hours worked on the job using the "hours" field.
5. Provide any additional notes about the submission in the "notes" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" fields.
8. Select the relevant job ID from the "job_id" field.
9. Enter a unique submission ID, which will be generated automatically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job** (select_one): Select the job you worked on from the list of available options.
* **submission_date** (date): Enter the date of submission in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **start_time** (time): Enter the start time of the job.
* **end_time** (time): Enter the end time of the job.
* **hours** (number): Enter the total hours worked on the job.
* **notes** (note): Provide any additional notes about the submission.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **job_id** (select_one): Select the relevant job ID from the list of options.
* **submission_id** (number): This field will be generated automatically, no need to fill it.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling this form, contact your time tracking administrator for assistance.
