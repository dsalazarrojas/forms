# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is a tool used by employees to submit their time off requests, providing necessary details for processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Select the start and end dates of your requested time off.
3. Choose the time interval (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) when you would like to take your time off.
4. If you have any additional comments or reasons for your request, please provide them in the text field.
5. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (`contact_information`, Text, Optional):** Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you for any further information.
* **Start Date (`start_date`, Date, Required):** Choose the date you would like to start your time off from the calendar.
* **End Date (`end_date`, Date, Required):** Select the date you would like to end your time off from the calendar.
* **Time Interval (`time_interval`, Select, Required):** Choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening to specify the time of your time off.
* **Comments (`comments`, Note, Optional):** Provide any additional comments or reasons for your time off request.
* **Tips:**

	* Ensure that you have not already submitted a time off request for the same dates or overlapping dates.
	* If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
