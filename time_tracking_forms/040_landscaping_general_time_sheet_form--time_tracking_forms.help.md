# Landscaping General Time Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping General Time Sheet Form is a tool used to track work hours and status on various landscaping jobs. This form is designed to provide a clear overview of each job's progress and allow staff to report their work hours accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant job ID from the provided options.
2. Enter the job date when the work was performed.
3. Enter your employee name as the person who performed the work.
4. Choose the job status from the drop-down menu (e.g., Completed, In Progress, On Hold).
5. Enter the start time when you began working on the job.
6. Enter the end time when you finished working on the job.
7. (Optional) Enter additional work notes or context about the job.
8. Repeat steps 4-6 for any additional job instances or tasks.
