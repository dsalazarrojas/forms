# Selection And Time Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on your allocation of time for various activities and your level of satisfaction with the current selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that ask for a numerical answer, please provide a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
3. For questions that ask for a selection from a list, choose the option that best represents your answer.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible in the text box.
5. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (1: selection_and_time_tracking_survey, type: text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is for your reference only.
* **Time Allocation** (2: time_allocation, type: number, required: false): Please select the time you allocated for the activities listed, using the numbers 1 to 5 to represent your level of time spent.
* **Satisfaction** (3: satisfaction, type: select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the current selection process, using the numbers 1 to 5 to represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Activities** (5: activity_choices, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select all the activities that you have allocated time for, using the letters a, b, c, d, or e.
* **Other Activities** (6: other_activities, type: text, required: false): If you have allocated time for any other activities not listed, please describe them in this text box.
* **Additional Information** (10: additional_info, type: text, required: false): If you have any additional information or comments, please share them here.
