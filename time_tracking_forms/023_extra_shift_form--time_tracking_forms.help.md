# Extra Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extra Shift Form is designed to gather information about your availability for additional shifts. This form helps us understand your interest and capacity to work extra hours, ensuring that we can effectively manage shift assignments and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your availability and preferences.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about the shifts you are interested in.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Are you available for additional shifts** (available_shifts, text, optional): Check the box if you are available to work extra shifts.
* **Start Time** (start_time, text, optional): Enter the start time of the shift you are interested in.
* **End Time** (end_time, text, optional): Enter the end time of the shift you are interested in.
* **Shift ID** (shift_id, text, optional): Enter the ID of the shift you are interested in.
* **Shift Name** (shift_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the shift you are interested in.
* **Shift Date** (shift_date, date, optional): Select the date of the shift you are interested in.
* **Shift Time** (shift_time, time, optional): Select the time of the shift you are interested in.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for the shift.
* **Assigned User** (assigned_user, text, optional): Enter the user assigned to work with you for the shift.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be reviewed by designated personnel to manage shift assignments and resource allocation.
