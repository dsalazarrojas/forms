# Employee Time Clock Check In/Check Out Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Clock Check In/Check Out Log form is used to record an employee's time clock check in and check out information, including shift hours, check date and time, location, supervisor, employee name, note, phone number, and email. This form helps in maintaining accurate and timely records of employee attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the clock in option from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the shift hours worked.
3. Enter the check date and time.
4. Provide the location.
5. Select the supervisor.
6. Enter the employee's name.
7. Add any additional note.
8. Enter the phone number.
9. Enter the email.
10. Select one or more categories (Category 1-7) relevant to the task or activity being logged.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Clock In/Out** (`clock_in_check_out_log_form`, select_one): Select the clock in or clock out option from the drop-down menu.
- **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`, number): Enter the number of hours worked during the shift.
- **Check Date** (`check_date`, date): Enter the date when the check in or check out was recorded.
- **Check Time** (`check_time`, time): Enter the time when the check in or check out was recorded.
- **Location** (`location`, text): Provide the location where the check in or check out was recorded.
- **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text): Enter the name of the supervisor.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text): Enter the employee's name.
- **Note** (`note`, text): Add any additional note or comments.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the employee's email address.
- **Category 1-7** (`category_1-category_7`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories relevant to the task or activity being logged.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct clock in or clock out option from the drop-down menu.
- Enter accurate shift hours, check date, and check time.
- Enter a valid phone number and email address.
- Select relevant categories for the task or activity being logged.
- Use additional notes for any important comments or details.
