# Weekly Shift Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Shift Report form is used to collect information from employees about their weekly shifts, including their employee ID, start date, and end date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID in the first field.
2. Select the start date of your shift from the calendar.
3. Select the end date of your shift from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (employee_id, text, required/optional): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **start_date** (start_date, date, required/optional): Select the date your shift started.
* **end_date** (end_date, date, required/optional): Select the date your shift ended.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct dates for your shift to ensure accurate reporting.
