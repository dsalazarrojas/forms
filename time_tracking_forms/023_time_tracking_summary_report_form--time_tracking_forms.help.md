# Time Tracking Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Tracking Summary Report Form is a report used to track employee work hours, breaks, and other relevant details. It provides a summary of an employee's work activities and time spent on tasks, projects, and clients. This form is used for both internal and external reporting and can be completed by employees to submit their time worked and activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your worked hours for the selected time period.
2. Choose whether you worked on any clients or projects for the selected time period.
3. Select the specific tasks you worked on for the selected time period.
4. Enter the date you worked on the selected time period.
5. Enter the duration of any breaks you took during the selected time period.
6. Select how frequently you took breaks during the selected time period.
7. Add any comments about your work or activities for the selected time period.
8. Enter your contact information for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Worked Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the total hours worked during the selected time period.
- **Client** (`client`, select_one, required/optional): Select the clients you worked with during the selected time period. Select 'Yes' if you worked on client-related tasks.
- **Project** (`project`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the projects you worked on during the selected time period. Select 'Yes' for each project you worked on.
- **Task** (`task`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the specific tasks you worked on during the selected time period. Select 'Yes' for each task you worked on.
- **Work Date** (`work_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you worked during the selected time period.
- **Worked Hours** (`worked_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the total hours worked during the selected time period.
- **Break Duration** (`break_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the total duration of breaks you took during the selected time period.
- **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how frequently you took breaks during the selected time period. Select 'Yes' if you took regular breaks.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any comments or feedback about your work or activities during the selected time period.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Report Frequency** (`report_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how frequently you want to receive reports. Select 'Yes' for regular reports.
