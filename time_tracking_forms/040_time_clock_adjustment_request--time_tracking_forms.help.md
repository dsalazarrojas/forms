# Time Clock Adjustment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Clock Adjustment Request form is designed to allow employees to submit requests for adjustments to their scheduled work hours. This form will guide you through the process of completing it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your job title from the list provided in the "Job Title" field.
3. Enter the date you want to start working in the "Start Date" field.
4. Enter the time you want to start working in the "Start Time" field.
5. Enter the date you want to end working in the "End Date" field.
6. Enter the time you want to end working in the "End Time" field.
7. Provide a brief reason for the adjustment in the "Adjustment Reason" field.
8. Select the "Approval Status" from the options provided.
9. Enter the name of the person responsible for approving the adjustment in the "Approver Name" field.
10. Enter the email address of the approver in the "Approver Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Employee Name, text, required)**: Please enter your official employee name as it appears on your pay stub or personnel records.
* **Job Title (Job Title, select_one, required)**: Select your current job title from the provided options.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you want to start working. Format is dd/mm/yyyy or dd/mm/yy.
* **Start Time (Start Time, time, required)**: Enter the time you want to start working in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 13:30).
* **End Date (End Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you want to end working. Format is dd/mm/yyyy or dd/mm/yy.
* **End Time (End Time, time, required)**: Enter the time you want to end working in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 17:30).
* **Adjustment Reason (Adjustment Reason, text, required)**: Provide a brief explanation for the time clock adjustment.
* **Approval Status (Approval Status, select_one, required)**: Select the status of your adjustment request from the options provided.
* **Approver Name (Approver Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for approving your adjustment.
* **Approver Email (Approver Email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of the approver.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the correct approver's email address.
* Double-check the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields for accuracy.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for the adjustment in the "Adjustment Reason" field.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to your HR department for assistance.
