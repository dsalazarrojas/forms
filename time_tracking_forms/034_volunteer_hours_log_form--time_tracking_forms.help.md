# Volunteer Hours Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Volunteer Hours Log Form is used to log volunteer hours for volunteers working with your organization. This form helps to track and record the hours volunteered, including the volunteer's name, organization, hours logged, and other relevant details. The form is essential for maintaining an accurate and up-to-date record of volunteer hours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Open the form and review the fields provided.
2. Enter the volunteer's name in the "Volunteer Hours Volunteer Name" field.
3. Enter the organization the volunteer worked with in the "Volunteer Hours Organization" field.
4. Enter the date of the volunteer hours logged in the "Volunteer Hours Date" field.
5. Enter the start time of the volunteer hours logged in the "Volunteer Hours Start Time" field.
6. Enter the end time of the volunteer hours logged in the "Volunteer Hours End Time" field.
7. Enter the total hours logged in the "Volunteer Hours Hours Logged" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the volunteer hours logged in the "Volunteer Hours Additional Comments" field.
9. Enter the volunteer's email address in the "Volunteer Hours Volunteer Email" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Hours Log Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Volunteer Hours Entry**: Enter a brief description of the volunteer hours logged (e.g., "Event Planning").
* **Volunteer Hours Date**: Enter the date of the volunteer hours logged (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Volunteer Hours Start Time**: Enter the start time of the volunteer hours logged (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Volunteer Hours End Time**: Enter the end time of the volunteer hours logged (e.g., 05:00 PM).
* **Volunteer Hours Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the volunteer hours logged.
* **Volunteer Hours Volunteer Name**: Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **Volunteer Hours Organization**: Enter the organization the volunteer worked with.
* **Volunteer Hours Hours Logged**: Enter the total hours logged.
* **Volunteer Hours Volunteer Email**: Enter the email address of the volunteer (optional).
