# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Timesheet Template is a form designed to help employees track their working hours and activities on various projects. This form aims to provide a standardized way of recording work hours, allowing for accurate tracking and reporting of employee work time. The form is typically used by employees to log their work hours at the start and end of each day, or week, to ensure that their work time is accurately represented in the company's records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates for the period you wish to track.
3. Enter the name of the project you were working on during this time.
4. Record the total hours worked on the project.
5. Add any additional notes about your work if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your employee ID or name to uniquely identify yourself in the company's records.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you started working on the project.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you finished working on the project.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of the project you were working on.
- **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, required: false): Record the number of hours you spent on the project. Please enter the total hours as a decimal number.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations about your work on the project.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when entering project and start/end dates.
- If you're tracking hours for multiple projects, please fill out a separate form for each project.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
