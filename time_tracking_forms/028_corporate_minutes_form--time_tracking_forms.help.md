# Corporate Minutes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Minutes Form is a tool used to document the details of corporate meetings in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Corporate Minutes Form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the form with the information from the meeting that you participated in.
* Enter the meeting date using the "Meeting Date" field, in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* Enter the start and end times of the meeting using the "Meeting Start Time" and "Meeting End Time" fields, respectively.
* Provide the location of the meeting in the "Meeting Location" field.
* Indicate whether or not meeting attendees were present in the "Meeting Attendees" field, with options to select all or none.
* Enter any relevant notes or observations from the meeting in the "Meeting Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date (1)** (required: false, type: date): Enter the date of the meeting in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Meeting Start Time (2)** (required: false, type: time): Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format, e.g. 09:00 or 13:00.
* **Meeting End Time (3)** (required: false, type: time): Enter the end time of the meeting in 24-hour format, e.g. 09:00 or 13:00.
* **Meeting Location (4)** (required: false, type: text): Enter the location where the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Attendees (5)** (required: false, type: select multiple): Indicate whether meeting attendees were present by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Meeting Notes (6)** (required: false, type: text): Enter any relevant notes or observations from the meeting. 

Note: Please ensure that you have reviewed and filled out the meeting report for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
