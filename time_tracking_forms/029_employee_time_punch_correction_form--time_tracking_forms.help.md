# Employee Time Punch Correction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to correct an employee's time punches. Time punches are recorded during an employee's workday to track their work hours. This form allows employees to correct any errors or discrepancies in their punches, ensuring accuracy in their recorded time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID of the employee whose time punch needs to be corrected.
2. Select the date and time when the error occurred, or the date and time when the employee worked on the task.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the correction.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes from the supervisor, if necessary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm the correction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID (page 1)
* **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee who needs to have their time punch corrected. This is a unique identifier for each employee.

### Start Date (page 1)
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the error occurred or when the employee started working on the task. This will help us to track the accurate work hours.

### Start Time (page 1)
* **Start Time**: Enter the time when the employee started working on the task. This will help us to track the accurate duration of the task.

### End Date (page 1)
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the error occurred or when the employee finished working on the task. This will help us to track the accurate work hours.

### End Time (page 1)
* **End Time**: Enter the time when the employee finished working on the task. This will help us to track the accurate duration of the task.

### Reason (page 1)
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the correction. Be as specific as possible to help us understand the error.

### Supervisor Comments (page 1)
* **Supervisor Comments**: If the supervisor needs to add any comments or notes, enter them here. This will help us to understand the situation better.

### Employee Signature (page 1)
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date this page to confirm that the correction is accurate and complete.

# Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all the information is accurate.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form should only be used for corrections, not for new punches.
* Make sure to keep a record of the corrected time punches to ensure accurate reporting.
