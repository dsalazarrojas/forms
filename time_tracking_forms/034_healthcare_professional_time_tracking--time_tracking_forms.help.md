# Healthcare Professional Time Tracking - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Healthcare Professional Time Tracking form is used to record and track time spent on various healthcare professional-related activities, such as shifts, roles, units, and more. This form helps healthcare professionals manage their time efficiently and report their activities accurately.

<thinking>Before completing the form, please ensure you have a valid reason to submit this form. Review your current shifts, roles, units, and other relevant activities to ensure accuracy and completeness.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the staff shifts, roles, and units options to select the relevant activities you have been involved in.
2. Fill in the start and end dates and times, if applicable.
3. Enter the duration of the activity, if applicable.
4. Provide any comments or notes regarding the activity.
5. Enter your email and phone number.
6. Select the assigned staff ID, shift type, staff, unit, department, location, and shift status.
7. Review and submit the form for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Shifts** (`id: 1`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the shifts you have worked, such as morning, evening, or night shifts.
* **Roles** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Check the role you have been assigned to (e.g., doctor, nurse, etc.).
* **Units** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the units you have been involved in (e.g., hospital, clinic, etc.).
* **Key Activities** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have performed any key activities (e.g., surgeries, etc.).
* **Start Time** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the activity, if applicable.
* **End Time** (`id: 6`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the activity, if applicable.
* **Duration** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the activity, if applicable.
* **Start Date** (`id: 8`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the activity, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the activity, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`id: 10`, `type: note`, required: false): Provide any comments or notes regarding the activity.
* **Email** (`id: 11`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Staff ID** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Check if you are assigned to any staff.
* **Shift Type** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of shift you worked on (e.g., day shift, night shift, etc.).
* **Staff** (`id: 15`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the staff member you were working with.
* **Unit** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the unit you worked on (e.g., hospital, clinic, etc.).
* **Department** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the department you were working in.
* **Location** (`id: 18`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the location you worked at.
* **Shift Status** (`id: 19`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your shift (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Break Time** (`id: 20`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the break time taken during the activity, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check your selections and entries for correctness and consistency.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure completeness and accuracy.
