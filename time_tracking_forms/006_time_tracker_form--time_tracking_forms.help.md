# Time Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Tracker Form is used to record and track work hours for employees in your organization. It helps managers and team leaders monitor work hours, improve time tracking, and provide accurate reporting. This form is typically used for time tracking, reporting, and payroll purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Time Tracker Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the company for which you are tracking time.
2. Enter your job title.
3. Enter your work start time.
4. Enter your work end time.
5. Select the duration of your work period.
6. Enter any relevant work notes.
7. Enter your employee email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company for which you are tracking time.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, date, optional): Enter the start time of your work period.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, date, optional): Enter the end time of your work period.
* **Work Duration** (`work_duration`, number, optional): Select the duration of your work period in hours.
* **Work Notes** (`work_notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant notes about your work period.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your employee email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
* If you are unsure about the company or job title, please contact your manager or HR department for clarification.
* If you need to track multiple work periods, please submit the form for each period separately.
* If you need to edit or modify your previously submitted form, please contact the time tracking administrator for assistance.
