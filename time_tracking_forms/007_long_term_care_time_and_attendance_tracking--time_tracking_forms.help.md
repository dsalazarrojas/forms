# Long Term Care Time And Attendance Tracking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track time and attendance for Long Term Care services, allowing you to record shifts and hours worked by caregivers in real-time. The form ensures accurate and efficient tracking of services, enabling better management and billing. It is a crucial tool for home care agencies to manage their workforce and clients' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Home Care Agency** where the services are being provided (Page 1).
2. Enter the **Caregiver's Name** responsible for the services (Page 2).
3. Choose all **Caregiver(s) assigned to the service** (Page 3).
4. Enter the **Date** of the service (Page 4).
5. Select the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the service (Page 5).
6. Specify the **Duration** of the service (Page 6).
7. Provide any relevant **Service Details** about the service (Page 7).
8. Input the total **Service Hours** worked during the service (Page 8).
9. Leave any additional **Comments** about the service (Page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Care Agency**: Choose the agency providing the home care services.
* **Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the caregiver responsible for the service.
* **Caregiver(s) assigned to the service**: Select all caregivers who worked on this service. This is a critical field as it allows for accurate billing and management of services.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which the service was provided.
* **Start Time**: Select the time when the service started.
* **End Time**: Select the time when the service ended.
* **Duration**: Enter the total time spent providing the service.
* **Service Details**: Provide a brief description of the service, including any important details that should be noted.
* **Service Hours**: Enter the total number of hours worked during the service.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about the service, such as any notable events or issues that occurred.
