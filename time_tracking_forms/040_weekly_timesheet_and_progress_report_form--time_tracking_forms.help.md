# Weekly Timesheet And Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit weekly time-tracking and progress report data for individual work hours, goals, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the week start and end dates to report on the period of time for which you are submitting this form.
2. Enter the start and end times of your weekly work hours, including any breaks or time off.
3. Report your daily work hours, earned hours, completed hours, and weekly goal.
4. Choose the status of your progress (Active, In Progress, or Not Started).
5. Add any comments or notes for your team lead or manager.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Start Date** (`week_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the week you are reporting on.
* **Weekly End Date** (`week_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the week you are reporting on.
* **Weekly Start Time** (`week_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your weekly work hours.
* **Weekly End Time** (`week_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your weekly work hours.
* **Daily Work Hours** (`daily_work_hours`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked for the week.
* **Daily Work Earned** (`daily_work_earned`, number, required): Enter the total hours earned for the week.
* **Daily Work Completed** (`daily_work_completed`, number, required): Enter the total hours completed for the week.
* **Weekly Goal** (`weekly_goal`, number, required): Enter your weekly goal or target hours.
* **Weekly Status** (`weekly_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your progress (Active, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Add any comments or notes for your team lead or manager.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
