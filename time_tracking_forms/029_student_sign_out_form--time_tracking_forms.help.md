# Student Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Sign Out Form is a tool used to record student sign-out events. It is meant to be completed by students when they need to leave the premises or take a break during school hours. This form helps ensure that students do not miss any important information or deadlines and that teachers or staff are aware of their whereabouts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `date_signed_out` field with the date you signed out.
2. Enter the time you signed out in the `time_signed_out` field.
3. Provide a brief reason for signing out in the `reason_for_signing_out` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **date_signed_out** (`date`, required): Enter the date you signed out using the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date you will return to school.
* **time_signed_out** (`time`, required): Enter the time you signed out using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 9:00 AM or 3:00 PM).
* **reason_for_signing_out** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you are signing out. This can be for any reason, such as a family emergency, personal appointment, or simply to take a break.
