# Student Volunteer Hour Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to track and record their volunteer hours, allowing them to accurately report their service hours to the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of "Time Tracking Forms" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the number of volunteer hours you have completed in the "Student Volunteer Hours" field.
3. If applicable, select the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4. Calculate the number of volunteer hours earned, if any, in the "Volunteer Hours Earned" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`time_tracking_forms`): Select the category of the form. This field is used to group forms for easier navigation. Options include:
	+ Time Tracking Forms
	+ Other
* **Student Volunteer Hours Form** (`student_volunteer_hours_form`): This field is a dropdown menu that allows you to select the specific type of form you are filling out. Options include:
	+ Student Volunteer Hour Log Form
	+ Other
* **Student Volunteer Hours** (`student_volunteer_hours`): Enter the total number of hours you have volunteered. This field is a numeric field where you can enter a whole number.
* **Student Volunteer Hours Ordinal** (`student_volunteer_hours_ordinal`): This field is optional and seems to be related to the order or priority of the volunteer hours. The purpose of this field is unclear without further information, so please use with caution.
* **Volunteer Hours Earned** (`volunteer_hours_earned`): Enter the total number of hours you have earned from your volunteer work. This field is numeric and used to track the hours earned.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool assigned to you for this volunteer work. Options include:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other

Note: The field "Student Volunteer Hours Ordinal" is optional and its purpose is not clearly defined in the provided YAML. Please use with caution and consult additional resources if you are unsure of its use.
