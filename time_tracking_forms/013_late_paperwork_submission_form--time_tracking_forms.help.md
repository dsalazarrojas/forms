# Late Paperwork Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Late Paperwork Submission Form is a crucial tool for tracking and managing late paperwork submissions. Its primary goal is to provide a standardized platform for reporting and verifying overdue documents, ensuring that all necessary paperwork is submitted on time and properly authorized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Late Paperwork Submission Form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct submission option on the first page of the form.
2. Enter the submission date on the second page.
3. Provide your Employee ID, Document Type, and Document Description on the subsequent pages.
4. Upload any required documents.
5. Enter your signature date, signature time, and approver information.
6. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Form** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_first_page`): This is the first page of the form where you select whether the submission is for 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`): Choose the type of document being submitted (e.g., Late Paperwork, Overdue Documents, Other).
* **Document Description** (`paperwork_description`): Provide a brief description of the document being submitted.
* **Upload Overdue Documents** (`upload_paperwork_file`): Upload any required documents or files.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Choose the status of the submission (Active or Inactive).
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Add any notes or comments from the employee.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`): Enter the approver's signature.
* **Approver Date** (`approver_date`): Enter the date of the approver's signature.
* **Approver Time** (`approver_time`): Enter the time of the approver's signature.
* **Additional Notes** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_additional_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Comments** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_comments`): Add any comments or notes about the submission.
* **Employee Signature** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_employee_signature`): Enter your signature as the employee.
* **Employee Name** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_employee_name`): Enter your name as the employee.
* **Employee Position** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_employee_position`): Enter your position.
* **Department** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_department`): Enter the department.
* **Section** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_section`): Enter the section.
* **Submitter** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_submitter`): Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submit** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_submit`): Click the submit button to finalize the form.
* **Reset** (`late_paperwork_submission_form_reset`): Click the reset button to reset the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are submitting paperwork for the correct employee and document type.
* Double-check your submission date and signature details before submitting the form.
* Review the submission status carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter all required fields to avoid errors.
* Use additional comments or notes to provide clarity and context to your submission.
* Verify that the approver's details are correct before submitting the form.
