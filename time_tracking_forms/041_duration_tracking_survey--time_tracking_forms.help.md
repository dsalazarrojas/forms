# Duration Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Duration Tracking Survey** is a form used to record the duration of various activities, such as work hours and breaks, for administrative, billing, or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct start and end dates for your activity.
2. Enter a brief description of your activity (optional).
3. Enter the total work hours and break hours you spent on the activity.
4. Enter the start time for your activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you began the activity.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the activity.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the activity (optional).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked on the activity.
* **Break Hours** (`break_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of break hours taken during the activity.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your activity.

## Tips
* Double-check your dates and times for accuracy.
* Use consistent formatting for your dates and times.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or consult with your supervisor.
