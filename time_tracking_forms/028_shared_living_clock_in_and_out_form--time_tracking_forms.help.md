# Shared Living Clock In And Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shared Living Clock In And Out Form is used to track clock in and clock out activities, allowing users to record their clock in and clock out times, dates, and reasons. This form is meant to be used for tracking time spent working or performing specific tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by selecting whether you are clocking in or out.
*   Fill out the appropriate sections for clock in or clock out activities, providing required information such as date, time, reason, and status.
*   For clock out activities, provide your driver ID, clock out status, note, and shift ID.
*   Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it for record-keeping purposes.
