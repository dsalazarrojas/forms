# Work Shift Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Shift Scheduling Form is designed to gather detailed information about employee shifts, including start and end dates, shift times, employee details, and other relevant notes. This form helps in efficiently managing and coordinating employee shifts, ensuring accurate documentation and reducing administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Start Date field with the date you started your shift.
2. Fill in the End Date field with the date you ended your shift.
3. Enter your Employee Name as it appears in our records.
4. Enter the Shift Start Time and Shift End Time to record the duration of your shift.
5. Fill in the Shift Date to record the specific date of your shift.
6. Enter your Employee Email and Employee Phone number for contact purposes.
7. If applicable, provide any notes in the Employee Notes field to clarify any specific shift details.
8. Select any team members involved in the shift from the Team Members dropdown list.
9. Enter any Supervisor Notes to provide context and guidance on the shift.
10. Choose the relevant Department (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Service) from the dropdown list.
11. Enter any Department Notes to provide additional context and guidance.
12. Enter the total Shift Hours worked in numbers.

* **Start Date** (id: 1, date, required: false): Enter the date you started your shift.
* **End Date** (id: 2, date, required: false): Enter the date you ended your shift.
* **Employee Name** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter your employee name as it appears in our records.
* **Shift Start Time** (id: 4, time, required: false): Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (id: 5, time, required: false): Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **Shift Date** (id: 6, date, required: false): Enter the specific date of your shift.
* **Employee Email** (id: 7, email, required: false): Enter your employee email address for contact purposes.
* **Employee Phone** (id: 8, text, required: false): Enter your employee phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employee Notes** (id: 9, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your shift, if necessary.
* **Team Members** (id: 10, select_multiple, required: false): Select team members involved in the shift from the list.
* **Supervisor Notes** (id: 11, note, required: false): Enter any supervisor notes for context and guidance.
* **Department** (id: 12, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant department for your shift.
* **Department Notes** (id: 13, note, required: false): Enter any additional department notes for context.
* **Shift Hours** (id: 14, number, required: false): Enter the total number of hours worked during the shift.
