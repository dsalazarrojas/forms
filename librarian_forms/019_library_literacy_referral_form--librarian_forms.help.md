# Library Literacy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Literacy Referral Form is designed to record and track referrals for literacy services provided by libraries. This form helps librarians and stakeholders accurately gather and store necessary information to ensure effective and efficient referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that each referral is unique and accurately represents the individual's needs and circumstances.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the individual being referred.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual being referred.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual being referred.
* **select_one_referral_source** (select one, required): Select the source of the referral (library, community organization, or social media).
* **library_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the library or location.
* **user_notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the individual.
* **referral_date** (date, optional): Record the date of the referral.
* **follow_up_date** (date, optional): Record the follow-up date.
* **follow_up_notes** (note, optional): Add any follow-up notes or comments.
* **select_multiple_literacy_services** (select multiple, required): Select the literacy services needed by the individual (basic literacy skills, adult basics, advanced literacy skills, technology skills).
* **assigned_tool** (select one, required): Choose the tool assigned for the referral (chatjimmy or other).
* **follow_up_notes_by** (text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the follow-up.
* **library_id** (number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the library.
* **user_id** (number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the individual.
* **form_id** (number, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **form_version** (number, optional): Enter the form version.
* **submitter** (select one, required): Indicate who submitted the form (user or other).
* **notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it's essential to fill in the required fields accurately to ensure the referral process is complete and effective.
