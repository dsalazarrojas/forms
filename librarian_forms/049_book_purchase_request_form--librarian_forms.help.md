# Book Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Purchase Request Form is a tool for librarians to gather information about book purchase requests from staff or patrons. It is designed to be a streamlined and efficient way to collect necessary details about the requested book, making it easier to process and fulfill purchase requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
* Fill in your name and email address, if you are the staff member submitting the request.
* Provide the book title and author you are requesting.
* Give your reason for requesting this book, including why you think it would be a good fit for the library's collection.
* If you know the ISBN, feel free to include it for further identification.
* You can skip the email address if you are not submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (Requester name): The name of the staff or patron submitting the request. This will help the library staff track and follow up with you.
* **Email address** (Email address): If you are submitting this form on behalf of someone else, please enter the email of the person making the request. This helps the library follow up with the requester, if needed.
* **Book title** (Book title): The actual title of the book being requested, as it appears on the book's cover or catalog information.
* **Author** (Author): The author of the book, which can be found on the book's cover or in the catalog information.
* **ISBN** (ISBN): The International Standard Book Number, which can help the library confirm the book's existence and identify it in the catalog.
* **Justification** (Justification for purchase): Please provide a brief explanation of why you think this book should be purchased for the library's collection. Be as specific as possible about its educational value, potential impact on readers, or relevance to our collection goals.
* **Budget code** (Budget code): If you have an internal budget code for this expense, please provide it. This helps the library process your request efficiently.

## Tips
* Please double-check that you are not submitting a duplicate request, as this can cause unnecessary work for library staff.
* If you are requesting multiple books, please use one form submission per book request.
* You can use this form for one-time requests, or for recurring requests, as long as you provide clear justification for the repeated interest.
