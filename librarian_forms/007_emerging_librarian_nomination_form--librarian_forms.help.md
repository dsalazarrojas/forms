# Emerging Librarian Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emerging Librarian Nomination Form is used to collect information about librarians who are making significant contributions to the field.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the nominee and nominator.
2. Provide supporting documentation if applicable.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (id: 1, text, not required): This field is for the title of the nomination.
* **Nominee Last Name** (id: 2, text, not required): Enter the last name of the librarian being nominated.
* **Nominee First Name** (id: 3, text, not required): Enter the first name of the librarian being nominated.
* **Nominee Middle Initial** (id: 4, text, not required): Enter the middle initial of the librarian being nominated.
* **Nominator Last Name** (id: 5, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Nominator First Name** (id: 6, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Nominator Middle Initial** (id: 7, text, not required): Enter your middle initial.
* **Nominator Department** (id: 8, text, not required): Enter your department name.
* **Nominee Supervisor Last Name** (id: 9, text, not required): Enter the last name of the nominee's supervisor.
* **Nominee Supervisor First Name** (id: 10, text, not required): Enter the first name of the nominee's supervisor.
* **Nominee Supervisor Middle Initial** (id: 11, text, not required): Enter the middle initial of the nominee's supervisor.
* **Nominee Supervisor Department** (id: 12, text, not required): Enter the supervisor's department name.
* **Nominee Title** (id: 13, text, not required): Enter the nominee's title.
* **Nominee Institution** (id: 14, text, not required): Enter the nominee's institution name.
* **Nomination Date** (id: 15, date, not required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents** (id: 16, text, not required): Upload any supporting documentation.
* **Nominator Title** (id: 17, text, not required): Enter your title.
* **Nominator Institution** (id: 18, text, not required): Enter your institution name.
* **Nominator Department** (id: 19, text, not required): Enter your department name.
* **Nominator Email** (id: 20, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone** (id: 21, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator Message** (id: 22, text, not required): Enter a message to support the nomination.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the nominee is not already being recognized for the same achievement.
* Double-check the information entered in each field for accuracy.
* Supporting documentation can be uploaded in any format (e.g., PDF, Word doc, etc.).
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators for assistance.
