# Library Poetry Slam Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for the Library Poetry Slam Tournament, where poets can submit their entries to participate in the competition. The form is designed to collect essential information about the poet and their poem to facilitate the evaluation and organization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Enter the number of the performer you are submitting for**: This field is used to identify the performer(s) entering the competition. You can enter the number of performers as per the instructions provided by the event organizers.
2.  **Enter the name of the poet**: This field is for the name of the poet submitting the entry. Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
3.  **Enter the title of the poem**: This field is for the title of the poem being submitted for the competition. This will be used to identify the poem during the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performer(s)** (Number): Enter the number of the performer you are submitting for. This will help us track entries by performer.
*   **Poet Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
*   **Poem Title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the poem being submitted for the competition.
