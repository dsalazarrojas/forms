## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from library patrons about their experiences with the library's reading program. Your input will help us evaluate the program's effectiveness and plan future improvements.

1. **Event Details**:

2. **Participant Info**:
	* Please provide your name and contact information (email and phone number, if applicable).
3. **Reading Experience**:
	* Rate your experience at the event using the scale: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. **Rating**:
	* Rate your satisfaction with the event using the scale: Highly Satisfactory, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Not Satisfactory.
5. **Comments**:
	* Share any comments or suggestions you have about the event.

7. **Phone**:
	* Enter your phone number (if you wish to receive follow-up communication from us).
8. **Date of Event**:
	* Enter the date of the event you attended.
9. **Time of Event**:
	* Enter the time of the event you attended.
10. **Follow-Up**:
	* This is a text area for any additional comments or suggestions.
