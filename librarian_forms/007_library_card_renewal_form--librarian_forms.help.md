## Tips

* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your library card.
* **Member ID** (Member ID, text, required): Enter your library member ID number.
* **Renewal Date** (Renewal Date, date, required): Select a new date for your library card to be renewed. Please format the date as MM/DD/YYYY.
