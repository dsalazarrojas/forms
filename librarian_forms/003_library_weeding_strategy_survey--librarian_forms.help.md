# Library Weeding Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Weeding Strategy Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information on your library's weeding strategy. The survey aims to understand your current weeding practices, challenges, and ideas for improvement. This information will help inform decisions about what books to keep or discard from your library collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your weeding frequency by choosing one of the three options: Low, Medium, or High.
2. In the Decision Criteria field, describe your criteria for weeding books in your collection.
3. Indicate the challenges you face when deciding what to weed, selecting all that apply.
4. Share your improvement ideas for the weeding process in the Improvement Ideas field.
5. Choose the type of library you work in (Academic, Public, School, or Special).
6. Rate how frequently you weed on a scale from 1 to 5.
7. Rate how frequently you consider the decision criteria on a scale from 1 to 5.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.
9. If you have any additional comments, provide them in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

## Tips

* Be honest when rating your weeding frequency and decision criteria, as this will help us better understand your processes.
* If you have any specific feedback or concerns, please share them in the Staff Feedback field.
* If you have any additional comments, provide them in the Comments and Additional Comments fields.
