# Library Book Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users when they request access to a book within the library's collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the book you are requesting in the "Form Title" field.
2. Enter your ID (or any other identifying information) in the "book_requester_id" field.
3. Enter the title of the book you are requesting in the "book_title" field.
4. Enter the author of the book you are requesting in the "book_author" field.
5. Enter the ISBN (or any other identifying information) of the book you are requesting in the "book_isbn" field.
6. Enter the reason for your request in the "book_request_reason" field.
7. Select the date you would like to request the book in the "book_request_date" field.
8. Select the time you would like to request the book in the "book_request_time" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "book_request_email" field, if you wish to be contacted by the library staff.
10. Enter your phone number in the "book_request_phone" field, if you wish to be contacted by the library staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form that you are requesting.
- **book_requester_id** (`book_requester_id`, number, optional): Enter any identifying information you may have.
  This is useful for the library staff to quickly verify your identity and process your request.
- **book_title** (`book_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the book you are requesting.
  Please be as specific as possible to ensure the library staff can locate the book.
- **book_author** (`book_author`, text, optional): Enter the author of the book you are requesting.
  If the book has multiple authors, list them separated by commas.
- **book_isbn** (`book_isbn`, text, optional): Enter the ISBN of the book you are requesting.
  This is useful for the library staff to quickly locate the book.
- **book_request_reason** (`book_request_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your request.
  This can help the library staff understand your needs and process your request efficiently.
- **book_request_date** (`book_request_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to request the book.
  This will help the library staff schedule the book according to your preferred date.
- **book_request_time** (`book_request_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to request the book.
  This will help the library staff schedule the book according to your preferred time.
- **book_request_email** (`book_request_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted by the library staff.
  This will allow the library staff to contact you with updates about your request.
- **book_request_phone** (`book_request_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by the library staff.
  This will allow the library staff to contact you with updates about your request.
