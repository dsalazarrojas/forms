# Book Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors who are giving away books to the library.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donor's name in the "Donor name" field.
2. Enter the donor's email address in the "Email address" field. This will help us keep in touch with the donor.
3. Provide the book's title and author in the respective fields.
4. If available, enter the book's ISBN.
5. Describe the book's condition using one of the options (New, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Indicate if you need collection arrangements to be made for the book by selecting "True" or "False".
7. If collection is required, provide the address for collection in the "Pickup address" field.
8. Submit the form to complete the donation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor name** (`Donor name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person donating the book.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, optional): Enter the donor's email address for acknowledgement and further communication.
* **Book title** (`Book title`, text, required): Enter the title of the book being donated.
* **Author** (`Author`, text, required): Enter the author of the book.
* **ISBN** (`ISBN`, text, optional): If available, enter the book's ISBN for cataloging purposes.
* **Condition** (`Condition`, select_one, required): Describe the book's condition using one of the options (New, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Pickup required** (`Pickup required`, select_one, required): Indicate if collection arrangements need to be made for the book by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Pickup address** (`Pickup address`, text, optional): If pickup is required, enter the address for collection.
