# Library Archival Research Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Archival Research Leave Of Absence Form is used to request approval for a leave of absence for archival research purposes from the library. This form should only be filled out by users who have already been approved for a research leave of absence and are seeking additional approval from the library.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each page of the form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to include all required information.
- Sign and date each page as required.
- Submit the form to the library for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Archival Research Leave Requests** (Page 1)
	+ This is the title of the form, which should be filled out with the title of the research leave of absence request.
* **What Is The Request For?** (Page 2)
	+ This field is used to describe the specific reason for the research leave of absence request.
* **What Are The Details Of Your Research?** (Page 2)
	+ This field is used to provide more detailed information about the research to be conducted during the leave of absence.
* **Start Date** (Page 3)
	+ This field is used to enter the start date of the research leave of absence.
* **End Date** (Page 3)
	+ This field is used to enter the end date of the research leave of absence.
* **Institutional Approval** (Page 4)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the institutional approver.
* **First Approver** (Page 4)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the first approver.
* **Second Approver** (Page 4)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the second approver.
* **Approver Signature** (Page 4)
	+ This field is used to get the signature of the approver.
* **Institutional Approval Date** (Page 5)
	+ This field is used to enter the date of the institutional approval.
* **Institutional Approval Status** (Page 5)
	+ This field is used to select the status of the institutional approval (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Additional Comments** (Page 6)
	+ This field is used to provide any additional comments about the research leave of absence request.
* **Comments** (Page 6)
	+ This field is used to enter any additional comments about the request.
* **Submit** (Page 6)
	+ This field is used to submit the form for review and approval.
* **Form Submitted By** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used to enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used to enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted IP** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used to enter the IP address of the person submitting the form.
* **Form Submitted Browser** (Page 7)
	+ This field is used to enter the type of browser used to submit the form.
* **Email** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Confirm Email** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to confirm the email address entered.
* **Confirm Phone** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to confirm the phone number entered.
* **Email/Phone Note** (Page 8)
	+ This field is used to enter any notes about the email and phone entered.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 9)
	+ This field is used to enter any additional comments about the form submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Check the institutional approval status before submitting the form.
- Follow up with the library after submitting the form for review and approval.
- Coordinate with the library for any additional information or clarification needed.
