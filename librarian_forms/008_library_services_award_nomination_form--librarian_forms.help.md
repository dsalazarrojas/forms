# library_services_award_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a library services award nomination, intended to recognize and reward outstanding library services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the library service being nominated for the award.
2. Provide the name of the nominator and their contact information (name, email, phone, and institution).
3. Enter the name and job title of the nominee being nominated.
4. Choose the award category for the nomination (Public Library, Academic Library, or Other).
5. Enter the year the award was given for this service.
6. Select the date and time of the nomination.
7. If desired, add any additional comments about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **library_services_form_1** (1): Enter a brief description of the library service being nominated.
* **nominator_name** (2): Enter your name as the person nominating the library service.
* **nominator_email** (3): Enter your email address.
* **nominator_phone** (4): Enter your phone number.
* **nominee_name** (5): Enter the name of the library service's user or team member being nominated.
* **nominee_job_title** (6): Enter the job title of the person being nominated.
* **award_name** (7): Enter the name of the award being nominated.
* **nominating_librarian_name** (8): Enter your name as the librarian submitting the nomination.
* **nominating_librarian_title** (9): Enter your job title.
* **institution_name** (10): Enter the name of the library institution.
* **institution_address** (11): Enter the address of the library institution.
* **nominator_institution** (12): Enter the name of your institution.
* **nominator_phone** (13): Enter your phone number.
* **nominator_email** (14): Enter your email.
* **nominator_notes** (15): If desired, add any additional comments about the nomination. (Optional)
* **award_year** (16): Enter the year the award was given for this service.
* **nominator_date** (17): Select the date of the nomination.
* **nominator_time** (18): Select the time of the nomination.
* **award_category** (19): Choose the category of the award (Public Library, Academic Library, or Other).
* **nominator_comments** (20): If desired, add any additional comments about the nomination. (Optional)

Note: Fields marked as "Optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
