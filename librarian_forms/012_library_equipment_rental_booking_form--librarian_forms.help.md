# Library Equipment Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Equipment Rental Booking Form is used to book library equipment such as bookshelves and desks for a specific time period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the appropriate equipment type from the options.
3. Choose a start and end date for your booking.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This is the main form field where you can provide any relevant information about your booking.
	+ This field is optional, but it's a good idea to fill it in if you have any specific details or comments about your booking.
* **Start Date**: This field is used to select the date when you would like to start using the equipment.
* **End Date**: This field is used to select the date when you would like to return the equipment.
* **Equipment ID**: This field is used to select the type of equipment you would like to book (e.g., Bookshelf, Desk).
* **Status**: Select the status of your booking (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Department**: Select the department that the equipment is being used for (e.g., Arts & Sciences, Engineering, Humanities).
* **Student ID**: This field is used to provide your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Student Name**: This field is used to provide your name (if you're a student).
* **Staff Name**: This field is used to provide your name (if you're a staff member).
* **Email**: This field is used to provide your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: This field is used to provide your phone number (if applicable).
* **Time Window**: This field is used to select the time period during which you would like to use the equipment.
* **Reservation Note**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your booking information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or have any questions, feel free to contact the library staff for assistance.
