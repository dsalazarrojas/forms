# library_user_avatar_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The library user avatar upload form is designed to allow users to upload their avatars for their library user account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the photo you want to upload from your device.
2. Choose the correct label for your photo from the options provided.
3. Fill in your contact details and membership numbers if applicable.
4. Provide your consent for the photo upload.
5. Click the "Upload Photo" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library User Avatar Upload Form** (`photo_upload`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to select the label for your photo from the provided options.
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_button`, text, optional):
	+ This is the button to upload your selected photo.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional):
	+ Fill in your contact details if you want to be contacted about your uploaded photo.
* **Membership Numbers** (`membership_details`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your membership numbers if you are a member of the library.
* **Consent Form** (`consent`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your consent for the photo upload by checking this field.
* **Library User Avatar** (`library_user_avatar`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for your uploaded photo, which will be displayed as your library user avatar.
* **Upload Photo Button** (`upload_photo_button`, text, optional):
	+ This button is for uploading your selected photo.
