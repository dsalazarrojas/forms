# Book Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Book Check Out Form" is used by librarians to collect necessary information from borrowers when checking out books. This form ensures that both the borrower and librarian have a clear understanding of the checkout details and due date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the borrower's personal details: **Borrower Full Name**, **Library Card Number**, and **Email Address**.
2. Select the **Borrower Type** (Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, Visiting Researcher, or Other).
3. Enter the **Book Title**, **Book Author**, and **ISBN Number** (if applicable).
4. Select the **Book Category** (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Reference, Textbook, Children, Young Adult, or Periodical).
5. Choose the **Checkout Date** and **Due Date** for the book.
6. Select the **Loan Period** (7 days, 14 days, 21 days, 30 days, or Semester loan).
7. Check if the book is **Renewable**.
8. Select the number of **Previous Renewals**.
9. Note any pre-existing **Book Condition** and **Damage Notes**.
10. Add any **Special Instructions** for handling the book.
11. Enter the **Processing Librarian's Name**.
12. Confirm that you agree to return the book by the **Due Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Borrower Full Name**: Enter the first and last name of the borrower.
* **Library Card Number**: Enter your library card or member ID number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for checkout notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact number for reminders. (Optional)
* **Borrower Type**: Select your category (Student, Faculty, Staff, Community Member, Visiting Researcher, or Other).
* **Book Title**: Enter the full title of the book.
* **Book Author**: Enter the author or editor of the book.
* **ISBN Number**: Enter the International Standard Book Number. (Optional)
* **Book Category**: Select the category of the book (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Reference, Textbook, Children, Young Adult, or Periodical).
* **Checkout Date**: Enter the date you are checking out the book.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date you must return the book.
* **Loan Period**: Select the length of the loan period (7 days, 14 days, 21 days, 30 days, or Semester loan).
* **Is this book renewable?**: Check if the book is eligible for renewal.
* **Number of Previous Renewals**: Enter the number of times this book has been renewed. (Optional)
* **Book Condition at Checkout**: Select the book's condition (New, Like New, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Notes on Existing Damage**: Describe any pre-existing damage. (Optional)
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special handling notes. (Optional)
* **Processing Librarian**: Enter the name of the librarian processing the checkout.
* **Borrower Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you agree to return the book by the due date.
