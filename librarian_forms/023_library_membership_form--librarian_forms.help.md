# Library Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll as a library member.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have all required information before filling out the form.
*   Please fill out the form in the following order: First name, Last name, Email address, Phone number, Date of birth, Date of sign up, Membership type, Member card ID.
*   If a field is not required (Member card ID), you can leave it blank if you don't have a library card ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and notifications. Ensure it is accurate.
*   **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact and notifications.
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth. This will help us understand your eligibility for certain services.
*   **Date of sign up** (`sign_up_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you signed up for membership. This will help us track your membership history.
*   **Membership type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your membership type from Student, Member, or Other.
*   **Member card ID** (`member_card_id`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your library card ID, if you have one. If not, you can leave it blank.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
*   If you need help or have any issues, feel free to contact us.
*   This form is for library membership only. If you need services from other departments, please use the corresponding forms.
