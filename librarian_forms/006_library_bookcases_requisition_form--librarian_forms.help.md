# Library Bookcases Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users regarding their bookcase needs or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the details of your request, including the number of bookcases you need, the type of bookcases required, and any additional comments.
2. Select the department or area where the bookcases will be used.
3. Enter the date and time you would like the bookcases to be delivered.
4. Enter any relevant contact information, including email, phone number, and contact person details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requisition Details
* **Requisition Details (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your bookcase request. This field is not required, but can be useful for additional context.
* **Bookcases Needed** (`number`): Enter the number of bookcases you need. This field is not required, but is useful for understanding the scope of your request.
* **Bookcases Type** (`select_one`): Select the type of bookcases you require from the options provided. This field is required.
* **Quantity** (`number`): Enter the quantity of bookcases you need, if different from the number selected in the previous field. This field is not required.
* **Department** (`select_multiple`): Select the department or area where the bookcases will be used. This field is not required.
* **Delivery Date** (`date`): Enter the date you would like the bookcases to be delivered. This field is not required.
* **Delivery Time** (`time`): Enter the time you would like the bookcases to be delivered. This field is not required.
* **Comments** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **Phone** (`text`): Enter your phone number. This field is not required.
* **User Name** (`text`): Enter your name. This field is not required.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **Contact Number** (`text`): Enter your contact number. This field is not required.
* **Department 2** (`select_multiple`): Select the department or area where the bookcases will be used. This field is not required.
* **Comments** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request. This field is not required.
* **Contact Person** (`text`): Enter the name of the contact person. This field is not required.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`text`): Enter the contact person's phone number. This field is not required.
* **Contact Person Email** (`email`): Enter the contact person's email address. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have checked the internal library inventory before submitting this form to avoid duplicating a previous request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your librarian for assistance.
