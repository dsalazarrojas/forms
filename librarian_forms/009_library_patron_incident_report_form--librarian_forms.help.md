# Library Patron Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report incidents that have occurred in the library. It is used to document and track incidents, and to provide information for follow-up and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Incident Description**: Enter a brief description of the incident that occurred in the library. This could include details about what happened and who was involved.
2. **Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. **Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
4. **Patron Info**: Enter any relevant information about the patron involved in the incident.
5. **Incident Info**: Enter any additional information about the incident.
6. **Supporting Documents**: If available, upload any supporting documents related to the incident, such as photos or videos.
7. **Follow-up**: Indicate if follow-up is needed or has been completed.
8. **Staff Member**: Enter the name of the staff member who was present or involved in the incident.
9. **Library Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
10. **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. public library, school library, academic library).
11. **Library Name**: Enter the name of the library where the incident occurred.
12. **Incident Status**: Indicate the current status of the incident (e.g. open, closed, follow-up needed).
13. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date follow-up is needed or has been completed.
14. **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time follow-up is needed or has been completed.
15. **Follow-up Status**: Indicate the status of follow-up (e.g. completed, not done, in progress).
16. **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
17. **Staff Member Signature**: Enter the signature of the staff member who received the report.
18. **Incident Reporter Signature**: Enter the signature of the person reporting the incident.
19. **Date Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
20. **Time Reported**: Enter the time the incident was reported.
21. **Reporting Person**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
22. **Reporting Unit**: Enter the unit or department of the person reporting the incident.
23. **Reporting Date**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
24. **Reporting Time**: Enter the time the incident was reported.
25. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask for guidance from the library staff.
* Keep the form for your records and reference.
