# Library Resources Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patrons about library resources, allowing librarians to better understand how patrons use and interact with these resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please ensure you are completing this form to provide genuine feedback about library resources.**
2. Fill out this form to help us understand your experiences with our library resources.
3. Provide your feedback honestly and thoroughly, as it will help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patron Feedback** (`patron_feedback`): 
    - This field is a text box where you can express your thoughts and opinions about your experiences with our library resources.
* **How often do you use the resource?** (`resource_usage`):
    - Select one or more options that apply to your frequency of using the resource:
        - **Always**: You use this resource regularly.
        - **Occasionally**: You occasionally use this resource.
        - **Never**: You do not use this resource.
* **What is your level of satisfaction?** (`satisfaction_level`):
    - Choose one option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the resource:
        - **Highly Satisfied**: You are very pleased with the resource.
        - **Somewhat Satisfied**: You are somewhat pleased with the resource.
        - **Not Satisfied**: You are not satisfied with the resource.
* **What opportunities for improvement do you see?** (`improvement_opportunities`):
    - This field is another text box where you can suggest improvements or changes we could make to our library resources.
* **Additional Feedback** (`resource_feedback`):
    - Use this text box to add any further comments or suggestions about our library resources.
* **Email** (`email`): 
    - Enter your email address to help us get back to you with any follow-up questions or to share your feedback with our team.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
    - Enter your phone number to help us get back to you with any follow-up questions or to share your feedback with our team.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`resource_suggestions`):
    - Use this text box to suggest new resources you think we should consider adding or improving.
* **Recommended Resources** (`resource_recommendations`):
    - Suggest other resources that you find useful and think we should know about.
* **Librarian Response** (`librarian_response`):
    - This field is not currently used; feel free to add any further comments or suggestions.
    
## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experiences with the resources.
* Share your honest opinions about the resource and any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* We appreciate your time and effort to help us improve our library resources.
