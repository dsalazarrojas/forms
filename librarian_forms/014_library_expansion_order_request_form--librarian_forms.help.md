# library_expansion_order_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request expansion materials for branches, such as books, media, or other items needed to support library services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Branch** (`branch`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the branch that requires expansion materials.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required): Enter the budget amount allocated for the expansion materials.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select the date by which the expansion materials should be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Select the time by which the expansion materials should be delivered.
* **Delivery Needs** (`delivery_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any special delivery requirements or instructions.
* **Expansion Materials** (`expansion_materials`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of expansion materials needed from the options listed.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the branch's current budget and expansion needs before filling out the form.
* Choose the correct delivery date and time to ensure timely fulfillment of the request.
* Select the most suitable expansion materials that meet the branch's needs and budget constraints.
