# University Library Resource Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Library Resource Requisition Form is designed to gather information for library resources requests from departments within the university. This form allows you to submit a new request for a resource, ensuring that it is properly documented and reviewed by the relevant staff member.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Full Name**, which is a required field for identification purposes.
2.  Enter your **Email**, also a required field for communication purposes.
3.  Specify your **Department**, which helps the library staff to categorize and prioritize your request accordingly.
4.  Select the **Resource Type** you require, which could be **Academic Books**, **Academic Journals**, **Database Access**, or **Other**. Be specific and accurate with your choice.
5.  Indicate the **Number of Copies** you need for your request.
6.  Specify the **Priority Date** when you need the resource. This will help the library staff prioritize your request efficiently.
7.  Enter the **Review Date**, which is the date when the review of your resource request will take place.
8.  Select the **Review Status** of your request from **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending**.
9.  If needed, leave a brief **Comments** section to further explain or provide additional information about your request. While this is not required, it can help facilitate communication between you and the library staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (name)** (Required): Please fill this in accurately to help our library staff identify you when processing your request.
* **Email (email) (Required)**: A valid email address is essential for communication and updates from the library staff.
* **Department (department)** (Required): Specify your department to ensure that we direct your request to the right staff member.
* **Resource Type (resource_type) (Required)**: Choose from the options provided: **Academic Books**, **Academic Journals**, **Database Access**, or **Other**.
* **Number of Copies (resource_count) (Required)**: Indicate the number of copies you need for your resource.
* **Priority Date (priority_date) (Required)**: Enter the date when you need the resource by, so we can schedule it efficiently.
* **Review Date (review_date) (Required)**: The date when the review of your resource request will take place.
* **Review Status (review_status) (Required)**: Choose from **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending** to keep track of your request.
* **Comments (comments) (Optional)**: If needed, provide additional comments or explanations about your request to facilitate communication.

## Tips

* Always be specific and accurate with your input to ensure efficient processing and review of your request.
* Make sure to submit a new request for a resource that you do not already have.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the library staff for assistance.
