# Book Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from readers about a book they've read. It helps librarians and readers track and analyze book reviews, informing collection development and reader preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Library Card Number** (if applicable) on pages 1-3.
2. Enter the **Book Title** and **Author** on pages 4-5.
3. Select the **Genre** of the book on page 7.
4. Choose your **Overall Rating** on page 9.
5. Fill in the **Brief Summary** and **Strengths** on pages 14-15.
6. Select **Yes/No** for **Would You Recommend This Book** on page 19.
7. Choose the target audience for **Who Would Enjoy This Book** on page 20.
8. Enter the **Review Date** on page 24.
9. Select **Yes/No** for **May We Share Your Review** on page 25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email.
* **Library Card Number**: Your library card number if applicable.
* **Book Title**: The full title of the book.
* **Book Author**: The author of the book.
* **Genre**: Select the genre of the book (e.g. Fiction, Non-Fiction, Mystery, etc.).
* **When Did You Read This Book**: Approximate date you finished reading the book.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the book on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **Plot Rating**: Rate the plot on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **Character Development Rating**: Rate the character development on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **Writing Style Rating**: Rate the writing style on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **Pacing Rating**: Rate the book's pacing on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **Brief Summary**: Summarize the book without spoilers.
* **What Did You Like**: Describe the strengths of the book.
* **What Could Be Improved**: Describe any weaknesses of the book.
* **Favorite Character**: Choose your favorite character.
* **Favorite Quote**: Share a memorable quote.
* **Would You Recommend This Book**: Select Yes/No to indicate whether you'd recommend the book.
* **Who Would Enjoy This Book**: Select the target audience for the book.
* **Similar Books**: List comparable books.
* **Content Warnings**: Enter any content to note.
* **Full Review**: Share your complete thoughts.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of your review.
* **May We Share Your Review**: Select Yes/No to indicate whether you'd like to share your review publicly.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be honest and concise in your responses.
* Use clear and respectful language.
* Proofread your responses for grammar and spelling errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us.
