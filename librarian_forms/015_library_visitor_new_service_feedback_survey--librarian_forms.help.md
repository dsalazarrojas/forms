# Library Visitor New Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from library visitors about their experience with a new service provided by the library.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "New Service" field and provide a brief description of your experience with the new service.
2. Fill in the "New Service Name" field with the name of the new service you are providing feedback on.
3. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate the quality of the service using the "Rating" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the "Suggestion" field.
6. Select the "Date of Visit" and "Visit Time" fields to record the date and time you used the new service.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Email", "Phone", and "Contact Method" fields.
8. If you need to provide additional contact information, please use the "Other Contact Method" field.
9. Enter the librarian's contact information in the "Librarian Name", "Department", "Librarian Email", "Librarian Phone", and "Librarian Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Service Feedback**: A field to describe your experience with the new service. (`New Service Feedback`, text, required)
* **New Service Name**: Enter the name of the new service being provided. (`New Service Name`, text, required)
* **Feedback**: Optional field for additional comments. (`Feedback`, text, optional)
* **Rating**: Rate the quality of the new service on a scale (1-5). (`Rating`, number, required)
* **Suggestion**: Optional field for visitors to provide suggestions for improvement. (`Suggestion`, text, optional)
* **New Service Rating**: Select the quality of the new service (Good or Fair). (`New Service Rating`, select_one, optional)
* **Date of Visit**: Record the date you used the new service. (`Date of Visit`, date, required)
* **Visit Time**: Record the time you used the new service. (`Visit Time`, time, required)
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address. (`Email`, email, required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (`Phone`, text, required)
* **Contact Method**: Select the method of contact you used (Phone, Email, or Other). (`Contact Method`, select_multiple, required)
* **Other Contact Method**: Optional field for additional contact information. (`Other Contact Method`, text, optional)
* **Librarian Name**: Enter the name of the librarian who provided the service. (`Librarian Name`, text, required)
* **Department**: Enter the department the librarian works in. (`Department`, text, required)
* **Librarian Email**: Enter the librarian's email address. (`Librarian Email`, email, required)
* **Librarian Phone**: Enter the librarian's phone number. (`Librarian Phone`, text, required)
* **Additional Information**: Optional field for additional comments from the librarian. (`Additional Information`, note, optional)
* **Survey Submitted By**: Optional field for the name of the person submitting the survey. (`Survey Submitted By`, text, optional)
* **Submission Date**: Optional field for the date the survey was submitted. (`Submission Date`, date, optional)
* **Submission Time**: Optional field for the time the survey was submitted. (`Submission Time`, time, optional)
* **Librarian Comments**: Optional field for the librarian's comments. (`Librarian Comments`, text, optional)
* **Visitor Feedback**: Optional field for visitor's feedback. (`Visitor Feedback`, text, optional)
* **New Service Feedback 2**: Optional field for additional feedback on the new service. (`New Service Feedback 2`, text, optional)
* **New Service Suggestion**: Optional field for suggestions for improvement. (`New Service Suggestion`, text, optional)
* **Submission Comments**: Optional field for comments on the submission. (`Submission Comments`, text, optional)
