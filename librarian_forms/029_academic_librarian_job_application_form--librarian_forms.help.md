# Academic Librarian Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Librarian Job Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Academic Librarian position. The form is used to evaluate applicants' suitability for the role and gather the necessary details for further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents in the **Full name** field.
2. Provide your preferred contact email in the **Contact email** field.
3. Enter your contact phone number, including the country or area code, in the **Contact phone number** field.
4. If applicable, enter your current employer or affiliation in the **Current employer or affiliation** field.
5. List your qualifications and certifications in the **Qualifications and certifications** field.
6. Enter the number of years you have experience in the library field in the **Years of library experience** field.
7. Select the **Areas of specialization** that match your expertise, choosing one or multiple options from the list.
8. Share a link to your portfolio or CV in the **Portfolio or CV link** field.
9. Provide two professional references in the **References** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (required): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact email** (required): Provide your preferred contact email.
* **Contact phone number** (optional): Enter your contact phone number, including the country or area code.
* **Current employer or affiliation** (optional): If applicable, enter your current employer or affiliation.
* **Qualifications and certifications** (required): List your qualifications and certifications.
* **Years of library experience** (required): Enter the number of years you have experience in the library field.
* **Areas of specialization** (optional): Select the areas where you have expertise, choosing one or multiple options.
* **Portfolio or CV link** (required): Share a link to your portfolio or CV.
* **References** (required): Provide two professional references.
