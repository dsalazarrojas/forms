# Reading Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading Interest Survey is designed to collect information about your reading habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, as it will help us create targeted reading programs and recommendations.
2. Fill out each field according to your reading habits and preferences.
3. Make sure to submit your completed form once you have finished answering all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you like to read?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_1`, text, required: false): Please describe the types of books or authors you enjoy reading.
* **Do you like reading book series?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_2`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you enjoy reading book series, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Favorite author** (`reading_interest_survey_form_3`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your favorite author.
* **Favorite book** (`reading_interest_survey_form_4`, text, required: false): Describe your favorite book.
* **Where do you typically read?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_5`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you read at home, at work, or at other locations, and 'No' if you don't read at these places.
* **How often do you read?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_6`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you read frequently, and 'No' if you rarely read.
* **How often do you read at school?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_7`, text, required: false): Describe how often you read at school.
* **How often do you read at home?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_8`, text, required: false): Describe how often you read at home.
* **How often do you read at work?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_9`, text, required: false): Describe how often you read at work.
* **How often do you read at other locations?** (`reading_interest_survey_form_10`, text, required: false): Describe how often you read at other locations.
* **Favorite genre** (`reading_interest_survey_form_8`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the genres of books you enjoy reading.
