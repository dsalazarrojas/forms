# Education Librarian Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Librarian Job Application Form is used to collect information from candidates applying for the Education Librarian position. This form helps to gather details about each candidate's qualifications, education, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired job title in the "job_title" field.
2. Provide your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your highest level of education completed in the "education" field.
6. List your relevant skills in the "skills" field.
7. Attach your resume in the "resume" field.
8. Enter your work history, including the dates you worked and the job roles you held, in the "work_history" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_title** (Text): Enter the job title you are applying for. This could be a specific job title, such as "Education Librarian" or "Library Assistant".
* **name** (Text): Please provide your full name.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **location** (Text): This field is not currently used. Please leave it blank.
* **education** (Select One): Select your highest level of education completed, such as Master's, Bachelor's, or Associate's.
* **skills** (Text): List your relevant skills, such as data analysis or communication.
* **resume** (Text): Please attach your resume in this field.
* **work_history** (Date): Enter your work history, including the dates you worked and the job roles you held.
