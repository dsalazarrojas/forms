# Library Patron Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions and feedback from library patrons on materials and services they would like to see improved or offered in the library. The form is intended to gather anonymous and confidential information to help librarians better understand what their patrons need and want.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the first page of the form, which is titled "form_page_1".
2.  On this page, you will see fields where you can provide your suggestions and feedback.
3.  Please answer all required fields, marked with a red asterisk \*.
4.  For optional fields, you can choose to provide or leave blank as desired.
5.  Once you have filled out all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your suggestions and feedback to the library.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title (required)**: This field is for the title of the material you are suggesting or providing feedback on. Please provide a brief and descriptive title of the material.
- **Material Type (optional)**: This field allows you to select the type of material you are suggesting, such as book, movie, music, or game.
- **Service Improvement (optional)**: This field is for any suggestions you have on how the library can improve its services.
- **Contact Email (optional)**: This field is for you to provide your email address so the library can follow up with you on your suggestions.
- **Contact Phone (optional)**: This field is for you to provide your phone number so the library can follow up with you on your suggestions.
- **Follow Up (required)**: This field is for any additional comments or questions you have, but please note that it should be as specific as possible so the library can better understand your suggestions.
- **Community Needs (required)**: This field is for any specific community needs or concerns that you are aware of that the library can address.
- **Form Page 2 (optional)**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have that do not fit in the other fields.

## Tips
*   Please be as specific as possible when providing your suggestions and feedback.
*   Remember that this form is anonymous and confidential, so you can provide your honest and open-minded opinions without fear of judgment.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the library directly.
