# Library Student Entry Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Student Entry Check In Form is a tool designed to help librarians record and track student visits during their time at the library.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Library Student Entry Check In Form" to open the form.
2. Select the "Student Grade" option that matches the student's grade level.
3. Select all relevant "Student Subject" options that match the student's subject of interest.
4. Select the "Student Room" where the student is currently located.
5. Enter the "Student Date" and "Student Time" of the student's visit.
6. Enter the "Librarian Name" and "Librarian Email" to record the librarian's information.
7. Add any additional "Notes" about the student's visit if necessary.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level (1-6).
* **Student Subject** (`student_subject`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant subjects of interest to the student.
* **Student Room** (`student_room`, select_one, optional): Select the room where the student is currently located.
* **Student Date** (`student_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the student's visit.
* **Student Time** (`student_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the student's visit.
* **Librarian Name** (`librarian_name`, text, optional): Enter the librarian's name.
* **Librarian Email** (`librarian_email`, email, optional): Enter the librarian's email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the student's visit.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, number, optional): Enter the ID number of the person submitting the form (usually the librarian's ID).
* **Submitted at** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Student Visit** (`student_visit`, number, optional): Enter a visit number, usually a sequential number to track repeat visits.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate recording of student visits.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any important information about the student's visit.
* Use the "Submitted by" field to track who submitted the form, usually the librarian's ID.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
