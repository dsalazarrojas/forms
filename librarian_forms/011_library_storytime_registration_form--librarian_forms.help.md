# Library Storytime Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from library staff or volunteers participating in the "Library Storytime" event. It's intended to help us understand who's attending, what they're doing, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the information requested for each field. You can select "Yes" or "No" for storytime registrations, enter your name, age, and other details as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storytime Registrations** (`storytime_registrations`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you're participating in the "Library Storytime" event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you'd like to be listed in the event records.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): If applicable, enter your age to help us better understand the audience demographics.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can contact you about event updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to get in touch with you.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the event you'll be participating in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the event you'll be attending.
* **Event Time Ending** (`event_time_ending`, time, required: false): Select the ending time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Ending** (`event_ending`, time, required: false): Choose the actual ending time of the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes about the event, if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to select "Yes" or enter accurate information for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the event organizer or library management for clarification.
- We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at "Library Storytime"!
