# Library Patron Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Patron Check In Form is designed to help library patrons check in for various library services, including item dropoffs, meeting room reservations, and program participation. This guide will walk you through each field and provide explanations on how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Fill out your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address (if applicable) in the Email field.
4. Select your Check-in Time, which is required for accurate processing.
5. Choose your Purpose of Visit from the dropdown menu, which indicates why you are checking in.
6. If necessary, select one or more Staff Assisted options to indicate what services you require from library staff.
7. Optionally, provide additional Comments or feedback in the Comments field.
8. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field (if applicable).
9. If you have any further communication requests, enter your Email address in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or library record.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or library record.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, enter it to allow for communication and further assistance.
* **Check-in Time**: Select your check-in time to ensure accurate processing and scheduling.
* **Purpose of Visit**: Choose the reason you are visiting the library, which helps staff prepare for your visit.
	+ **Library Book Dropoff**: For returning items or depositing items for processing.
	+ **Library Meeting Room Reservations**: For reserving a meeting room.
	+ **Library Program Participation**: For attending a library program or event.
	+ **Other**: For any other purpose not listed.
* **Staff Assisted**: If you need assistance from library staff, choose one or more options from the list.
	+ **Reference**: For assistance with research or information requests.
	+ **Program**: For assistance with library programs or events.
	+ **Computer**: For assistance with library computers or technology.
	+ **Other**: For any other assistance not listed.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information or feedback for library staff to consider.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it for future communication.
* **Note**: Enter any further communication requests or notes for library staff.
