# Library Reading Challenge Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for librarians or library staff to sign up for or manage a reading challenge or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field. This is the person's name who will be participating in the reading challenge or program.
2. Enter your **email** in the "email" field. This is your contact information.
3. Select your **School or Library** from the dropdown menu. This helps us know in which context you're working.
4. Enter your **Librarian Name** in the "Librarian Name" field. This is your name as the librarian managing this challenge or program.
5. Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field. This is your contact email, in case we need to get in touch.
6. Enter your **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field. This is your contact phone number.
7. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the challenge or program in the respective date fields.
8. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the challenge or program in the respective time fields.
9. Select the **Program Type** from the dropdown menu. This helps us know the type of program you're managing.
10. Enter a brief **Program Description** in the "Program Description" field. This is a description of the challenge or program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant in this field.
* **email**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **School or Library**: Select the type of institution where you work (School or Library).
* **Librarian Name**: Enter your name as the librarian managing this challenge or program.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email in this field.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in this field.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the challenge or program.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the challenge or program.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the challenge or program.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the challenge or program.
* **Program Type**: Select the type of program you're managing from the dropdown menu (Reading Challenge, Reading Program, or Reading Program for Schools).
* **Program Description**: Enter a brief description of the challenge or program in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your library or institution's guidelines for clarity.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your library or institution's administration.
