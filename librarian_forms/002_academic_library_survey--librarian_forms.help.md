# Academic Library Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Library Survey is designed to gather feedback from library users on their experiences, satisfaction, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your type of affiliation with the library (e.g., Undergraduate Student, Faculty).
2.  Indicate how often you visit the library (e.g., Daily, Several times a week).
3.  Choose your primary reason for using the library (e.g., Studying or homework, Research and databases).
4.  Rate your satisfaction with the library's collection of books, journals, and digital resources.
5.  Rate the quality and accessibility of online databases and e-resources.
6.  Assess the helpfulness of the library staff.
7.  Evaluate the physical facilities (seating, lighting, cleanliness).
8.  Rate your satisfaction with the technology available (computers, Wi-Fi, printers, scanners).
9.  Assess your satisfaction with the library's operating hours.
10. Rate the noise level in study areas (optional).
11.  Provide suggestions for improvements and services you would like to see added or enhanced.
12.  Rate your overall satisfaction with your library experience.
13.  Indicate how likely you are to recommend the library to a peer (optional).
14.  Provide any additional comments or feedback about the library.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of library user are you**: This question helps us understand your affiliation with the library (e.g., Undergraduate Student, Faculty).
* **How often do you visit the library**: This question gauges your frequency of visiting the library.
* **What is your primary reason for using the library**: Select all that apply to help us understand your primary purpose of visiting the library.
* **How satisfied are you with the library's collection of books, journals, and digital resources**: This question assesses your satisfaction with the library's collections and resources.
* **How would you rate the quality and accessibility of online databases and e-resources**: This question helps us evaluate the online resources available to you.
* **How helpful is the library staff**: This question measures the staff's helpfulness in addressing your needs.
* **How would you rate the physical facilities (seating, lighting, cleanliness)**: This question assesses the comfort and usability of the library's physical environment.
* **How satisfied are you with the technology available (computers, Wi-Fi, printers, scanners)**: This question rates your satisfaction with the technology available in the library.
* **Are you satisfied with the library's operating hours**: This question gauges your satisfaction with the library's hours of operation.
* **How would you rate the noise level in study areas**: This question is optional and helps us understand your comfort level with noise in study areas.
* **What services or resources would you like to see added or improved**: This question invites you to provide suggestions for improvements.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your library experience**: This question rates your overall satisfaction with the library.
* **How likely are you to recommend this library to a peer**: This question is optional and measures your willingness to recommend the library.
* **Any additional comments or feedback about the library**: This question invites you to share any additional thoughts or suggestions.
