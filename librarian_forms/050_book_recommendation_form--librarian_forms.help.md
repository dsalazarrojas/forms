# Book Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect book recommendation information from users. You can use this form to suggest a book you think others might find interesting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Click the "Start" button to begin.
*   Enter your name in the "Recommender name" field, if you want to be associated with your recommendation.
*   Provide your email address in the "Email address" field, if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
*   In the "Book title" field, enter the title of the book you recommend.
*   Enter the author's name in the "Author" field.
*   Provide a brief reason for recommending the book in the "Reason for recommendation" field.
*   Choose the intended audience for the book in the "Intended audience" field.
*   Add any additional notes or information about the book in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Recommender name** (Recommender name, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can enter your name if you'd like to be associated with your recommendation.
*   **Email address** (Email address, email, optional): If you enter your email address, the form administrators might contact you for further clarification or information about your recommendation.
*   **Book title** (Book title, text, required): Enter the title of the book you recommend.
*   **Author** (Author, text, required): Enter the author's name.
*   **Reason for recommendation** (Reason for recommendation, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you think others should read this book.
*   **Intended audience** (Intended audience, select_one, optional): Choose who you think would benefit from reading this book.
*   **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about the book that might be helpful to others.
 
## Tips
*   Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you're unsure about any information, just leave it out or provide a note indicating so.
*   Remember that your recommendation is a suggestion, and others might have different opinions.
