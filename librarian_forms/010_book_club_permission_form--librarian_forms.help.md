# Book Club Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Club Permission Form is a mandatory document that must be filled out by parents or guardians before their child can participate in a book club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately. Make sure to include all required information, including the participant's contact information and medical conditions.
2. If you are filling out the form for more than one participant, please create a separate form for each child.
3. If you are not sure about any of the questions, please ask your librarian for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`): This is the full name of the participant, including their first, middle, and last name.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`): This is the birth date of the participant.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): This is the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`): This is the contact email of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`): This is the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`): This is the full street address of the participant.
* **City** (`city`): This is the city where the participant resides.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`): This is the state or province where the participant resides.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`): This is the postal or ZIP code of the participant.
* **Book Club Name** (`book_club_name`): This is the name of the book club.
* **Meeting Schedule** (`meeting_schedule`): This is the schedule of the book club meetings (e.g., Every Monday 4pm-5pm).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): This is the location of the book club meetings (e.g., Main Library Conference Room).
* **Permission to Participate** (`permission_granted`): This is a confirmation that the parent or guardian grants permission for the participant to participate in the book club.
* **Permission for Transportation** (`transportation_permission`): This is a confirmation that the parent or guardian grants permission for transportation to off-site events.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`): This is a confirmation that the parent or guardian grants permission for photos and videos to be taken of the participant for promotional purposes.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`): This is a field for the parent or guardian to list any medical conditions or allergies of the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): This is the full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): This is the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): This is the relationship of the emergency contact to the participant (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`liability_waiver`): This is a confirmation that the parent or guardian understands and accepts the liability terms of the book club.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`): This is the signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): This is the date of signing.
