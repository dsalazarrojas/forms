# Library Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Feedback Form is a way for library patrons to share their thoughts, suggestions, and concerns with library staff. It helps us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information and comments. Please make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in this guide).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your name, as registered with the library or used for identification purposes.
- **Last Name**: Your surname, as registered with the library or used for identification purposes.
- **Email**: Your email address, used to contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
- **Comments**: Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the library's services. This field is required.
- **Rating**: Rate your experience with the library. You can choose from Very poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
- **Category**: Select the category that best describes your feedback. You can choose from Complaint, Praise, Problem, or Suggestion.
- **Other category**: If none of the above categories fit your feedback, choose "Other category" and type in your own description.
- **Other comments**: If you have any additional comments or details about your feedback, type them in here.
- **Other category 2**: If you want to provide more context about your feedback, select one of the categories from the list.
- **Other rating**: Rate your experience with the library, with more specific details.
- **Other comments 2**: Provide any additional comments or details about your feedback.
- **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback to us. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your feedback.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your feedback.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, try to be constructive and respectful.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact library staff for assistance.
