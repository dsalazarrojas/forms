## Food Festival Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Festival Attendance Form is designed to collect information about food festival attendees. This form will help organizers collect the necessary details about festival-goers such as their name, contact information, and attendance preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. For the "Food Festival Name" field, enter the name of the festival you are attending.
3. Enter the name of the person organizing the festival in the "Organiser Name" field.
4. Enter the email address of the festival organizer in the "Organiser Email" field (if required).
5. Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Attendee Phone" field (if required).
7. Select your check-in time in the "Checkin Time" field (24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).
8. Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the date of your festival registration in the "Registration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Festival Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the festival you are attending.
* **Organiser Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the person organizing the festival.
* **Organiser Email** (3, email, optional): Enter the email address of the festival organizer.
* **Attendee Name** (4, text, required): Enter your name as the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (5, text, optional): Enter your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Checkin Time** (6, time, optional): Select your check-in time in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30.
* **Notes** (7, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Registration Date** (8, date, required): Select the date you registered for the festival.

### Tips
* Double-check that you've filled in all required fields (*).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the festival organizers for clarification.
* Make sure to enter your phone number in the correct format, e.g., 123-456-7890.
