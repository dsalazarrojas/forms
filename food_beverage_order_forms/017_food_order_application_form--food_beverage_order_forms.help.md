# Food Order Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Order Application Form is a digital form used to collect information from customers for food orders. This form is used to gather details such as customer name, order date and time, contact information, food items, quantity, special instructions, total cost, payment method, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the date and time of the order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields, respectively.
3. Provide the customer's email address in the "Customer E-mail" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Select the food items the customer would like to order using the "Food Item" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the quantity of each food item in the "Food Quantity" field.
7. If there are any special instructions or requests, type them in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Enter the total cost of the order in the "Total Cost" field.
9. Choose the payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
10. Finally, select the order status (e.g., "Confirmed", "In Progress", "Delivered", etc.) in the "Order Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: The field where you enter the name of the customer who is placing the order.
- **Order Date**: The date when the order is to be fulfilled.
- **Order Time**: The time when the order is to be fulfilled.
- **Customer E-mail**: The email address of the customer.
- **Customer Phone**: The phone number of the customer.
- **Food Item**: A dropdown menu where you select the food items to be ordered, such as "Pizza", "Burger", "Salad", etc.
- **Food Quantity**: The quantity of each food item ordered.
- **Special Instructions**: Any special requests or instructions from the customer.
- **Total Cost**: The total cost of the order.
- **Payment Method**: The method of payment, either "Cash" or "Card".
- **Order Status**: The status of the order, such as "Confirmed", "In Progress", "Delivered", etc.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid errors in the order.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant customer service team for assistance.
* If you have any special instructions or requests from the customer, type them in the "Special Instructions" field. 
* Please note that the order status fields are predefined and cannot be edited once the form is submitted.
