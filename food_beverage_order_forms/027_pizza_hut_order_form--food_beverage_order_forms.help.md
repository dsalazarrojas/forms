# Pizza Hut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for pizza from Pizza Hut. You can choose between delivery and takeout options, and provide your contact information and order preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose your delivery method: Select "Delivery" if you'd like to have your pizza delivered to your doorstep, or "Carryout" if you'd prefer to pick up your order.
2.  Enter your contact information: Provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you about your order.
3.  Enter your address: This will help us ensure that your order is delivered to the correct location.
4.  Select your order details: Choose from options like "Small 2-topping pizza" or "Medium 2-topping" to customize your order.
5.  Specify the quantity: Enter the number of pizzas you'd like to order.
6.  Choose your delivery time: Select from options like "30 min", "60 min", or "90 min" to schedule your delivery.
7.  Select your payment method: Choose from options like "Credit Card", "Cash", or "Bank Transfer".
8.  Add any additional notes: If you have any special requests or instructions, you can include them in this field.
