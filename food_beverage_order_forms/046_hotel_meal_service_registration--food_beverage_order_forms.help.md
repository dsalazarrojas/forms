# Hotel Meal Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests about their meal preferences and dietary restrictions while staying at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. On the first page, "Guest Preferences", you can enter any additional information you'd like to share with the hotel staff.
3. On the second page, "Meal Preference", choose the type of meal you'd prefer (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
4. On the third page, "Dietary Restrictions", you can enter any dietary restrictions you have, such as food allergies or preferences.
5. On the fourth page, "Schedule Service", select a time for the hotel staff to provide the meal service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preferences`, text, optional): This field allows you to share any additional information about your stay or preferences with the hotel staff.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the type of meal you'd prefer (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you have, such as food allergies or preferences.
* **Schedule Service** (`schedule_service`, time, optional): Select a time for the hotel staff to provide the meal service.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select a time that works for the hotel staff to provide the meal service.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please make sure to list them clearly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hotel staff for assistance.
