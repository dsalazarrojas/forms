# Detox Drinks Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Detox Drinks Order Form is designed to collect order information for detox drinks. This form is used to gather customer details, order specifications, and status updates for detox drinks orders. It is intended for customers to provide their information and order details, and for the business to manage and track orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Provide your name in the "User Name" field.
4. Specify the name of the detox drink in the "Drink Name" field.
5. Enter the quantity of the detox drink you wish to order.
6. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
7. Select the shipping method for your order.
8. Enter any special instructions for your order in the "Special Instructions" field.
9. Enter the date you would like to have your order fulfilled or shipped.
10. Select the status of your order, including payment, shipping, and order date.
11. Enter any order comments or notes.
12. Optionally, enter your phone number and any additional details in the "Order Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Email (*)
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you with order updates and notifications.

### User Name (*)
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name here. This will be used to identify you as the customer.

### Drink Name (*)
* **Drink Name** (`drink_name`, text, required): Specify the name of the detox drink you wish to order.

### Quantity (*)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the detox drink you wish to order.

### Shipping Address (*)
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address here. This will be used to fulfill your order.

### Order Date (*)
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to have your order fulfilled or shipped.

### Shipping Method (*)
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Select the shipping method you prefer for your order.

### Special Instructions (*)
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required): Enter any special instructions or requests for your order.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here. This will be used to contact you if needed.

### User Phone
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.

### Order Total (*)
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of your order.

### Payment Method (*)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer for your order.

### Order Status (*)
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your order.

### Shipping Status (*)
* **Shipping Status** (`shipping_status`, select_one, required): Select the shipping status of your order.

### Payment Method Status
* **Payment Method Status** (`payment_method_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your payment method.

### Order Date Status (*)

### Order Note Status
* **Order Note Status** (`order_note_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the order notes.

### Shipping Method Status

### Payment Method Details

### Shipping Method Details

### Order Comments
