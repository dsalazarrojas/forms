# Protein Bar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place an order for a protein bar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go to the "Order Details" field and enter any additional information about your order.
2.  Proceed to the "Payment Details" field. This is a required field, so you must complete it to finish the order.
3.  In the "Order Summary" field, confirm your order details.
4.  In the "Order Info" field, provide information about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Order Details (Text)**: This field is where you can provide any additional information about your order. It's optional.
*   **Payment Details (Text, Required)**: This field is required and is where you must provide payment information to complete the order.
*   **Order Summary (Text, Optional)**: This field is where you can confirm your order details.
*   **Order Info (Text, Required)**: This field is where you must provide information about your order.
