# Breakfast Delivery Time Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Breakfast Delivery Time Selection" form is used to collect information for breakfast delivery services, ensuring that your breakfast needs are met with precision and convenience. This form helps us understand your business or organization's requirements, contact details, and delivery preferences, allowing us to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business or organization's name, including any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact person's name and phone number for order confirmation.
3. Enter your contact email address for order updates and communication.
4. Specify the delivery address for the breakfast service.
5. Choose the number of people that will be eating.
6. Select any special dietary requirements, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and others.
7. Choose a date when you need breakfast delivered.
8. Select your preferred delivery time slot.
9. Specify the service level, including drop-off only, drop-off with setup, or full catering service.
10. Choose a breakfast package that suits your needs, such as Continental - Light, Classic - Standard, or Deluxe - Premium.
11. Select any beverages you'd like included, such as coffee, tea, or orange juice.
12. Add any special menu items you'd like included.
13. Provide setup location details if applicable.
14. Choose the serving ware preference, such as disposable, reusable, or individual boxes.
15. Select your estimated budget per person.
16. Add any special instructions or notes.
17. If this is a recurring order, choose your schedule (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for order confirmation and communication.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address for order updates and communication.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the complete delivery address for the breakfast service.
* **Number of People to Feed**: Enter the total number of people that will be eating.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any special diets you need, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and others.
* **Breakfast Delivery Date**: Choose the date when you need breakfast delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Select your preferred delivery time slot.
* **Service Level**: Choose the service level that suits your needs, such as drop-off only, drop-off with setup, or full catering service.
* **Breakfast Package Selection**: Choose a breakfast package that suits your needs, such as Continental - Light, Classic - Standard, or Deluxe - Premium.
* **Beverages Included**: Select any beverages you'd like included, such as coffee, tea, or orange juice.
* **Special Menu Items**: Enter any specific items you'd like included.
* **Setup Location Details**: Provide details for where the breakfast should be set up.
* **Serving Ware Preference**: Choose your preference for serving ware, such as disposable, reusable, or individual boxes.
* **Estimated Budget Per Person**: Select your estimated budget per person.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Add any additional requirements or notes.
* **Recurring Order**: Choose if this is a recurring order and select your schedule (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
