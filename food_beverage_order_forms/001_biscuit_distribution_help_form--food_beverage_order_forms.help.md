# Biscuit Distribution Help Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a biscuit distribution program. It will help us to ensure that the biscuits are distributed correctly and safely to the intended recipients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name and phone number.
2. Enter your email address, so we can keep you updated on the delivery status.
3. Provide your full delivery address, including unit or apartment number.
4. Indicate the number of people in your household for proper portioning.
5. Select your biscuit preferences, if any.
6. List any dietary restrictions you have, if any.
7. Choose a preferred delivery date.
8. Select a preferred delivery time window.
9. If you have any special instructions for delivery, enter them in the additional notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (2, `contact_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone number** (3, `contact_phone`, required): Include your country code if you are outside the local area.
* **Email address** (4, `contact_email`, optional): Enter your email address for delivery updates.
* **Delivery address** (5, `delivery_address`, required): Enter your full street address, including unit or apartment number.
* **Household size** (6, `household_size`, required): Indicate the number of people in your household for proper portioning.
* **Biscuit preferences** (7, `biscuit_preferences`, optional): Select all types of biscuits you prefer.
	+ Plain
	+ Chocolate
	+ Oat and Honey
	+ Gluten Free
	+ Vegan
* **Dietary restrictions** (8, `dietary_restrictions`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
	+ None
	+ Nuts
	+ Dairy
	+ Gluten
	+ Soy
* **Preferred delivery date** (9, `delivery_date`, required): Choose a date for delivery.
* **Preferred delivery time window** (10, `delivery_window`, optional): Select a time for delivery.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Additional notes** (11, `additional_notes`, optional): Enter any special instructions for delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
