# Beverage CO₂ Cylinder Refill Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beverage CO₂ Cylinder Refill Order Form is designed to help you order refills for your CO₂ cylinders. This form collects necessary information about your company, order details, and account information to process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Company or Business Name, Contact Person Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Business Address).
2. Select the size of CO₂ cylinder you need (Cylinder Size) and the number of cylinders required (Number of Cylinders).
3. Choose your preferred delivery method (Delivery Method) and the date you need the cylinders delivered (Preferred Delivery Date).
4. If applicable, enter your previous supplier's name (Previous CO₂ Supplier) and your customer account number (Account Number).
5. Select your preferred pricing arrangement (Pricing Preference).
6. Estimate your monthly usage of CO₂ cylinders (Estimated Monthly Usage (cylinders)).
7. Specify the type of business you operate (Type of Business).
8. Finally, enter any special requests or notes about your order (Special Requests or Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information
* **Company or Business Name** (`company_name`): Enter your organization's official name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Name of the person placing this order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): We will send order confirmation here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Contact number for order updates.
* **Delivery Address** (`business_address`): Full street address for cylinder delivery or pickup.

### Order Details
* **Cylinder Size** (`cylinder_type`): Select the size of CO₂ cylinder you need. Options: 5 lb, 10 lb, 20 lb, 50 lb, 100 lb.
* **Number of Cylinders** (`quantity`): How many cylinders do you need?
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Choose how you want to receive your order. Options: Delivery to location, Pickup at warehouse, Courier service.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`): When do you need the cylinders?
* **Previous CO₂ Supplier** (`previous_supplier`): If applicable, enter your previous supplier's name.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`): Leave blank if this is your first order.

### Account Information
* **Customer Account Number** (`account_number`): Leave blank if this is your first order.
* **Previous CO₂ Supplier** (`previous_supplier`): If applicable, enter your previous supplier's name.

### Usage Information
* **Estimated Monthly Usage (cylinders)** (`monthly_usage`): How many cylinders do you typically use per month?
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`): What industry are you in? Options: Restaurant or cafe, Bar or nightclub, Retail beverage store, Gas supplier/distributor, Other.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`): Any additional information about your order?
