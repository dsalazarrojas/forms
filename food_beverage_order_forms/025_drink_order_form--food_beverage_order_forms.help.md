# drink order form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers to submit their drink order requests. It is designed to capture necessary customer information and order details for efficient processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "drink order form" field with your drink order request.
2. If you have any additional information to share, use the "customer info" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
5. If you have any additional notes, use the "note" field.
6. Click the "submit" button to submit your order.
7. If you need to cancel your order, click the "cancel" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **drink order form** (drink order form, text, optional): Enter your drink order request in this field.
* **customer info** (customer info, text, optional): If you have any additional information to share, type it here.
* **phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **note** (note, note, optional): If you have any additional notes, type them here.
* **submit** (submit, text, optional): Click this button to submit your order.
* **cancel** (cancel, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Review your order carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
