# food festival license application form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for food vendors to apply for a license to participate in a food festival. It will be used to evaluate your application and ensure a smooth festival experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **festival name**: (`food festival_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the food festival you are applying for. Please use the exact name of the festival as it appears on the event website or promotional materials.
- **vendor name**: (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your licenses and permits. This is the name that will be displayed on your festival booth.
- **vendor email**: (`vendor_email`, text, required): Enter your official email address where the festival organizers can contact you. This should be the email address you use for your business communications.
- **phone**: (`vendor_phone`, text, false): Provide your business phone number where you can be reached for any festival-related inquiries.
- **address**: (`vendor_address`, text, false): Enter your business address where festival organizers can send any necessary documents or communications.
- **food items**: (`food items`, select_multiple, required): Choose the food items you plan to sell at the festival. Select all relevant food items from the list to ensure accurate categorization. Please ensure you only select items that your business currently offers or will offer during the festival.
