# Shake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shake Order Form is used to collect information for a custom shake order from a customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your shake order is fulfilled correctly.
2. If you have any specific requests or notes for your order, please include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
3. Review your order carefully before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shake Order Form** (1) (`shake_order_form`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide any additional information about your order.
* **Shake Name** (2) (`shake_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your shake order.
* **Shake Type** (3) (`shake_type`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you would like a milkshake, 'No' if you would not.
* **Shake Ice** (4) (`shake_ice`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you would like ice in your shake, 'No' if you would not.
* **Shake Flavor** (5) (`shake_flavor`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any flavors you would like in your shake from the options provided.
* **Shake Toppings** (6) (`shake_toppings`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any toppings you would like on your shake from the options provided.
* **Shake Sugar** (7) (`shake_sugar`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you would like sugar in your shake, 'No' if you would not.
* **Additional Notes** (8) (`shake_additional_notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes or requests you have for your shake order can be included here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting flavors and toppings to ensure your shake is made to your liking.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your order carefully before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
