# Menu Redesign Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Redesign Material Order Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in redesigning their menu. This form gathers details such as contact information, material request, order date and time, and other relevant details to ensure that the team can process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for the "Menu Redesign Material Request" field.
3. Provide details for the material you are requesting in the "Material Request Quantity" and "Material Request Description" fields.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Menu Redesign Material Request** (`menu_redesign_material_request`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the available options: Menu Redesign, Menu Revamp, or Menu Refresh. This field helps us understand the scope of your redesign project.
* **Material Request Quantity** (`material_request_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of materials you are requesting. Please keep in mind that this field is required, as providing an estimate of the materials needed helps us process the request accurately.
* **Material Request Description** (`material_request_description`, text, required: false): Describe the materials you are requesting, providing as much detail as possible. This field will help us understand your needs and ensure we fulfill them correctly.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so that we can contact you to discuss the details of your menu redesign project.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach out to you directly.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Select the date when you would like to order the materials.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Select the time when you would like to order the materials.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you have regarding your menu redesign project.
* **Menu Redesign** (`menu_redesign`, text, required: false): This field is currently not being used in the form, but it might be used in the future. Please ignore it for now.
* **Material Request Details** (`material_request_details`, text, required: false): This field is currently not being used in the form, but it might be used in the future. Please ignore it for now.
