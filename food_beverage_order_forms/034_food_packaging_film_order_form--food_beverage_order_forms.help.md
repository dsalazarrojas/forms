# Food Packaging Film Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders for food packaging films. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and timely processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your customer information on page 1.
2. Enter your order details on page 2.
3. Select the products you want to order on page 3.
4. Choose your preferred payment gateway on page 4.
5. Review your order details on page 5.
6. Confirm your order date on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Page 1)**: This field is for you to enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant customer information.
* **Order Details (Page 2)**: Describe the order you want to place, including the quantity and type of food packaging films you need.
* **Product Selection (Page 3)**: Choose the specific products you want to order from the list provided. You can select one or multiple products.
* **Payment Gateway (Page 4)**: Select your preferred payment method. You can choose from the available options. Note: This field is not required but is recommended for timely processing of your order.
* **Order Review (Page 5)**: Review and verify the order details you have entered. This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Order Confirmation (Page 6)**: Confirm the date you want to receive your order. This will help us ensure timely delivery and processing of your order.
