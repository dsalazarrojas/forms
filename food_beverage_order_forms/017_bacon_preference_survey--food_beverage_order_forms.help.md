# Bacon Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bacon Preference Survey is designed to understand your preferences and habits related to bacon consumption. This survey aims to gather information about your age group, consumption habits, preferred cooking methods, bacon types, and dietary restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and email address (fields 2 and 3).
2. Choose your age group (field 4).
3. Indicate how often you eat bacon (field 6).
4. Express your liking or disliking of bacon (field 7).
5. Select your preferred bacon style, cooking method, and fat level (fields 9, 10, and 11).
6. Choose the types of bacon you enjoy (field 13).
7. Indicate the meals where you typically eat bacon (field 16).
8. Answer whether you like bacon as an ingredient in other dishes (field 17).
9. Select any dietary restrictions affecting your bacon consumption (field 19).
10. Indicate how important health/nutrition is when choosing bacon (field 21).
11. Express your concern for sustainability when purchasing bacon (field 22).
12. Confirm if you are willing to try alternative bacon products (field 23).
13. Share any product suggestions or improvements (field 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (field 2): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (field 3): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Age Group** (field 4): Select your age group from the options provided.
* **How Often Do You Eat Bacon?** (field 6): Choose how often you eat bacon:
	+ Multiple times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ 2-3 times per month
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Do You Like Bacon?** (field 7): Express your preference for bacon:
	+ Love it
	+ Like it
	+ Neutral
	+ Dislike it
	+ Do not eat bacon
* **Preferred Bacon Cooking Style** (field 9): Choose your preferred bacon cooking style:
	+ Crispy
	+ Slightly Crispy
	+ Chewy
	+ Soft
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Cooking Method** (field 10): Select your preferred cooking method:
	+ Oven-baked
	+ Skillet/Pan
	+ Microwave
	+ Grilled
	+ Air Fryer
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Fat Level** (field 11): Choose your preferred fat level:
	+ Extra Fatty
	+ Fatty
	+ Balanced
	+ Lean
	+ Extra Lean
* **Types of Bacon You Enjoy** (field 13): Select all the types of bacon you enjoy:
	+ Pork Bacon
	+ Turkey Bacon
	+ Beef Bacon
	+ Plant-based Bacon
	+ Applewood Smoked
	+ Maple Bacon
	+ Thick Cut
	+ Thin Cut
* **Meals Where You Typically Eat Bacon** (field 16): Select all the meals where you typically eat bacon:
	+ Breakfast
	+ Lunch
	+ Dinner
	+ Snacks
	+ Salads
	+ Sandwiches
	+ Baked Goods
	+ Pizza
* **Do You Like Bacon As an Ingredient in Other Dishes?** (field 17): Express your preference for bacon as an ingredient:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Sometimes
* **Dietary Restrictions Affecting Bacon Consumption** (field 19): Select any dietary restrictions affecting your bacon consumption:
	+ No
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Halal
	+ Kosher
	+ Low-Fat Diet
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Other
* **How Important is Health/Nutrition When Choosing Bacon?** (field 21): Indicate how important health/nutrition is when choosing bacon:
	+ Very Important

* **Does Sustainability Influence Your Bacon Purchase?** (field 22): Express your concern for sustainability:
	+ Yes

	+ Yes

* **Product Suggestions or Improvements** (field 24): Share any product suggestions or improvements for bacon products.
* **Thank You for Completing This Survey** (field 25): Confirm that you have completed the survey.

Note: Please answer all the required questions to proceed.
