# Corporate Catering Menu Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from customers for corporate catering orders. It collects data on the order form, customer details, menu selection, and other relevant information to facilitate efficient catering services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Complete the **Order Form** page with the required details (Order Number, Order Form).
	* Move on to the **Customer Info** page and provide the required customer information.
	* Proceed to the **Menu Selection** page and select the relevant menu items for the event.
	* On the **Menu Item** page, choose the desired menu items from the provided options.
	* Enter the **Quantity** of each selected menu item.
	* Enter the **Price** for each menu item, ensuring accurate pricing information.
	* Provide the **Event Date**, **Event Time**, and **Event Location** details for the corporate event.
	* Select the relevant **Corporate Catering**, **Food and Beverage**, **Corporate Events**, and **Corporate Services** options for the order.
	* Answer **Special Requests** questions with "Yes" if required.
	* Enter contact details such as the **Contact Person**, **Contact Number**, **Email**, and **Notes** (if necessary) on the **Contact Info** page.
	* Choose a **Payment Method** for the order and specify the **Payment Status**.
	* Finally, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form**:
	+ A text field for the order number or form submission.
* **Customer Info**:
	+ A text field for customer details such as name and contact information.
* **Event Details**:
	+ A text field for additional event details (not always required).
* **Menu Selection**:
	+ A multi-select field for choosing the desired menu items for the event.
* **Menu Item**:
	+ A single-select field for choosing a specific menu item (if applicable).
* **Quantity**:
	+ A numeric field for specifying the quantity of each selected menu item.
* **Price**:
	+ A numeric field for specifying the price of each selected menu item.
* **Event Date**:
	+ A date field for specifying the date of the corporate event.
* **Event Time**:
	+ A time field for specifying the time of the corporate event.
* **Event Location**:
	+ A text field for specifying the location of the corporate event.
* **Corporate Catering**:
	+ A multi-select field for rating the quality of the corporate catering services.
* **Food and Beverage**:
	+ A multi-select field for choosing the quality of food and beverage services.
* **Corporate Events**:
	+ A multi-select field for rating the quality of corporate events services.
* **Corporate Services**:
	+ A multi-select field for rating the quality of corporate services.
* **Special Requests**:
	+ A multi-select field for special requests such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact Person**:
	+ A text field for specifying the contact person's name.
* **Contact Number**:
	+ A text field for specifying the contact number.
* **Email**:
	+ An email field for specifying the contact email address.
* **Notes**:
	+ A text field for additional comments or notes.
* **Submit**:
	+ A text field for submitting the form for review.
