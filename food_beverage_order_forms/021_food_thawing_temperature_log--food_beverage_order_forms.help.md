# Food Thawing Temperature Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Thawing Temperature Log is used to record the temperature and date information of food during its thawing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct option for "Food Thawing Information" which will ask for either the "Temperature of Food at Receipt" or "Other". This field is not required and is used to determine the starting point of the thawing process.
2. Enter the "Date Received" of the food. This is the date the food was received for thawing.
3. If applicable, enter the "Expected Thawing Time" for the food. This is the time the food is expected to take to thaw.
4. Enter the "Temperature of Food at Thawing" which is the initial temperature of the food when it is first thawed.
5. Enter the "Date of Thawing" which is the date the food was thawed.
6. Enter the "Temperature of Food at Final Check" which is the temperature of the food after it has been thawed and checked for consistency.
7. Enter the "Final Check Date" which is the date the food was checked for consistency after it has been thawed.
8. Enter the "Temperature of Food at Storage" which is the temperature of the food after it has been stored.
9. Enter the "Storage Date" which is the date the food was stored.
10. Enter the "Temperature of Food at End of Storage" which is the temperature of the food after it has been stored for the expected period of time.
11. Enter the "End of Storage Date" which is the date the food was stored for the expected period of time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Thawing Information** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the correct option for "Temperature of Food at Receipt" to select the temperature of the food at the point of receipt, or choose "Other" if the temperature is not applicable.
* **Date Received** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_2`, date, optional): Enter the date the food was received for thawing.
* **Expected Thawing Time** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_3`, number, optional): Enter the expected time the food will take to thaw if applicable.
* **Temperature of Food at Thawing** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_4`, number, optional): Enter the initial temperature of the food when it is first thawed.
* **Date of Thawing** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_5`, date, optional): Enter the date the food was thawed.
* **Temperature of Food at Final Check** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_6`, number, optional): Enter the temperature of the food after it has been thawed and checked for consistency.
* **Final Check Date** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_7`, date, optional): Enter the date the food was checked for consistency after it has been thawed.
* **Temperature of Food at Storage** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_8`, number, optional): Enter the temperature of the food after it has been stored.
* **Storage Date** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_9`, date, optional): Enter the date the food was stored.
* **Temperature of Food at End of Storage** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_10`, number, optional): Enter the temperature of the food after it has been stored for the expected period of time.
* **End of Storage Date** (`food_thawing_temperature_log_11`, date, optional): Enter the date the food was stored for the expected period of time.
