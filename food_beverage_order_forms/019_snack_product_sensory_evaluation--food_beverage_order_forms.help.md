# Snack Product Sensory Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Product Sensory Evaluation form is used to gather feedback on a snack product's taste, texture, and satisfaction. This feedback is used to evaluate the product's quality and make data-driven decisions in the development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
* Select the options that best describe your experience with the snack product.
* Fill out each required field (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Taste**: Select all the flavors you experience when eating the snack product. You can choose multiple flavors if you experience them.
* **Texture**: Select the texture that best describes the snack product.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the snack product on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Comments**: Please describe your experience with the snack product. This field is optional but helps us understand your thoughts and feelings.
* **Recommended?**: Would you recommend this snack product to others?
* **Rating**: Rate the snack product on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Favorite Color**: If you have a favorite color, please enter it here. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Introduction**: This is a one-time opportunity to write a brief introduction before starting the evaluation.
* **Favorite Color**: Please note that this field is optional and only for those who want to express their personal preference. 

Note: Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if you are not willing to provide the information.
