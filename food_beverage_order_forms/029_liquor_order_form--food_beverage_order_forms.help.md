# Liquor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture information about a liquor order, allowing for accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Liquor Order Form page with the required information.
2. Select the type of liquor you are ordering from the options provided.
3. Enter the quantity of the liquor you are ordering in the required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liquor Order Form**: 
    This is the main form section where you need to enter your order details. Please provide the relevant information for your order.
* **Type**: 
    Select the type of liquor you are ordering from the options provided: Whiskey, Vodka, Rum. This helps us identify the type of drink accurately.
* **Quantity**:
    Enter the quantity of the liquor you are ordering. This will help us process your order efficiently. 

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you need to make changes to your order, contact us directly for assistance.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, refer to this help guide for further assistance.
