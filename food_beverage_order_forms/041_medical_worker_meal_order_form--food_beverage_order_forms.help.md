# Medical Worker Meal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Worker Meal Order Form is used to collect information from medical workers for meal orders, including their meal preferences, dietary restrictions, and contact information. This form is intended for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant options for your meal preferences.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Ensure that you are accurate in your responses to ensure that your meal order is fulfilled correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your meal preference for lunch?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_1`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select one of the options:
		- Yes: You would like to order lunch.
		- No: You do not want to order lunch.
		- Other: You have a specific preference or requirement for lunch.
* **What is your meal preference for dinner?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_2`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select one of the options:
		- Yes: You would like to order dinner.
		- No: You do not want to order dinner.
		- Other: You have a specific preference or requirement for dinner.
* **Do you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_3`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select one of the options:
		- Yes: You have a food allergy or dietary restriction.
		- No: You do not have a food allergy or dietary restriction.
		- Other: You have a specific preference or requirement.
* **Do you have any special requests for your meal?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_4`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter any specific requests you have for your meal.
* **What is your contact information?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_5`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **What is your preferred time for your meal delivery?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_6`, `time`, required)
	+ Please select a specific time for your meal delivery.
* **Would you like to add any additional contact information?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_7`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter any additional contact information you would like to share.
* **Do you want to order a snack?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_8`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select one of the options:
		- Yes: You would like to order a snack.
		- No: You do not want to order a snack.
		- Other: You have a specific preference or requirement for a snack.
* **Is there a specific date for your meal order?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_9`, `date`, required)
	+ Please select a specific date for your meal order.
* **Do you have any further requests or comments?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_10`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter any further requests or comments you have.
* **Do you need any further assistance with your meal order?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_11`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter any further requests for assistance.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`medical_worker_meal_order_form_page_12`, `text`, required)
	+ Please enter any other comments or feedback you have.
