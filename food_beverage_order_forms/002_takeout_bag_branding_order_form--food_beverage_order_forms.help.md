# Takeout Bag Branding Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Takeout Bag Branding Order Form is used to process orders for custom takeout bags with branding for your store. This form is used to collect information about the order, including bag preferences and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Bag Preferrred Color" and "Bag Preferrred Design" options to choose the design and color of the bag.
2. Select the logo position on the bag using the "Logo Position" option.
3. Enter the "Order Date" and "Order Time" of when the order is to be fulfilled.
4. Enter the "Total Cost" of the order.
5. Select the payment method using the "Payment Method" option.
6. Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
7. Enter the store name, address, city, state, and zip code.
8. Enter any additional "Order Instructions" or "Customer Notes".
9. Select the "Bag Color" and "Bag Preferrred Size" options to choose the color and size of the bag.
10. Enter the "Bag Material" if needed.
11. Enter the store's hours of operation in the "Store Hours" field.
12. Enter any additional "Order Notes".
13. Enter any customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
14. Enter any additional order comments in the "Order Comments" field.
15. Enter the store's phone number in the "Store Phone" field.
