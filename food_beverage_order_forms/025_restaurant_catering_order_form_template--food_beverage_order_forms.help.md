# Restaurant Catering Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Catering Order Form Template is a document that allows customers to provide their information and select options for their catering order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Order Form section with the requested information.
2. Provide your Customer Information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
3. Select the desired date and time for your order.
4. Check the "Order" box if you would like to place an order.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the Notes section.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will enter the details of your order. Please provide all necessary information to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* **Customer Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name and any relevant contact information so that we can reach out to you with updates or confirmations.
* **Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive your order. Please note that this is not a calendar picker, so please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`4`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your order. Please note that this is not a 24-hour clock, so please enter the time in the format 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM.
* **Menu** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If you have specific menu requests, please enter them here. This could include any special requests or preferences.
* **Order** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check this box if you would like to place an order.
* **Notes** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes here. This could include any specific instructions or requests for your order.
* **Confirm** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Confirm that you would like to place an order.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you with updates or confirmations.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you with updates or confirmations.
* **Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes here. This could include any specific instructions or requests for your order.
