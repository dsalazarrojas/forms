# Food Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Inspection Form is used to document the results of a food safety inspection, including facility information, inspection dates and times, food safety risks, corrective actions taken, and follow-up actions planned or taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the facility information section to ensure the correct facility is being targeted for inspection.
2. Select the correct answer in the facility information section to reflect the facility type.
3. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the inspection date/time section.
4. Identify any food safety risks found during the inspection and select them in the food safety risks section.
5. Record any corrective actions taken in the corrective actions taken section.
6. Plan and record follow-up actions in the follow-up actions planned section.
7. Document any follow-up actions taken in the follow-up actions taken section.
8. Enter the date and time of any follow-up actions taken in the follow-up date/time section.
9. Enter any additional comments or observations in the follow-up remarks section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This section is an overview of the inspection and its purpose.

### Facility Information
* **Facility Information** (`facility_information`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of facility being inspected.

### Facility Location
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required: false): Enter the physical address or location of the facility being inspected.

### Inspection Date/Time
* **Inspection Date/Time** (`inspection_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the inspection.

### Food Safety Risks
* **Food Safety Risks** (`food_safety_risks`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify any food safety risks found during the inspection by selecting them from the provided options.

### Corrective Actions Taken
* **Corrective Actions Taken** (`corrective_actions_taken`, select_one, required: false): Record any corrective actions taken as a result of the inspection.

### Follow-Up Actions Planned
* **Follow-Up Actions Planned** (`follow_up_actions_planned`, select_multiple, required: false): Plan and record any follow-up actions to be taken after the inspection.

### Follow-Up Actions Taken
* **Follow-Up Actions Taken** (`follow_up_actions_taken`, select_one, required: false): Document any follow-up actions taken as a result of the inspection.

### Follow-Up Date/Time
* **Follow-Up Date/Time** (`follow_up_date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of any follow-up actions taken.

### Follow-Up Location
* **Follow-Up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of any follow-up actions taken.

### Follow-Up Remarks
* **Follow-Up Remarks** (`follow_up_remarks`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the follow-up actions taken.

### Facility Operations Manager
* **Facility Operations Manager** (`facility_operations_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility operations manager.

### Facility Safety Manager
* **Facility Safety Manager** (`facility_safety_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility safety manager.

### Facility Supervisor
* **Facility Supervisor** (`facility_supervisor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility supervisor.

### Facility Owner
* **Facility Owner** (`facility_owner`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility owner.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and accurately complete each section.
* Double-check the correct answers for each question.
* If unsure about any part of the form, seek guidance from a qualified authority.
