# Picnic Food Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences for a picnic event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction page, which is optional.
2. Select your preferred main course from the options provided.
3. Choose as many side dish options as you prefer from the options provided.
4. Select your preferred beverage from the options provided.
5. Choose your preferred dessert from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This page is an introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Main Course** (`Main Course`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the main course you would like to have at the picnic. Options include:
	+ Apple Pie
	+ Mac and Cheese
	+ Grilled Chicken
	+ Salads
* **Side Dish** (`Side Dish`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select as many side dish options as you prefer. Options include:
	+ Mixed Greens
	+ Potato Salad
	+ Macaroni and Cheese
	+ Chips
* **Beverage** (`Beverage`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred beverage for the picnic. Options include:
	+ Water
	+ Soda
	+ Juice
	+ Beer
* **Dessert** (`Dessert`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred dessert for the picnic. Options include:
	+ Fruit Salad
	+ Chocolate
	+ Ice Cream
	+ Cake

## Tips
- Be sure to choose options that you are willing to actually eat or drink at the picnic.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, you can always contact the form administrators for guidance.
