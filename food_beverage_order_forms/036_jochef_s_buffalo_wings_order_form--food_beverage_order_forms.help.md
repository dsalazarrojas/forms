# jochefs_buffalo_wings_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for Buffalo wings from jochefs. Please fill out the form to submit your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Order Form with your order details.
2. Proceed to the Payment Form to enter your payment information.
3. Complete the Shipping Form with your shipping details.
4. Select "Submit" to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, required): This is the main order form where you will enter your order details.
* **Payment Form** (`payment_form`, `text`, required): This is where you will enter your payment information.
* **Shipping Form** (`shipping_form`, `text`, required): This is where you will enter your shipping details.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, `select_one`, required): Select "Submit" to confirm your order, or choose "Other" if you have any additional comments.
