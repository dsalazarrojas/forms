# Online Grill Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to place orders for grilled food or related products. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that our team can process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the page where you want to enter your information.
2. Fill out each field with the required and optional information asked.
3. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Grill Order Form** (Grill Order Form, text, optional)
* This field is where you can enter any specific details about your order, such as your preferred type of grill item or any special requests.

### 2. **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, optional)
* Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to you for confirmation or to provide updates on your order.

### 3. **Order Details** (Order Details, text, optional)
* Provide any additional comments or details about your order here, such as quantities, specific items, or any special requests.

### 4. **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, optional)
* In this field, you can enter any payment-related information, such as payment methods or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
