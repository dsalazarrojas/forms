# restaurant_preorder_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant preorder form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a restaurant's preorder system. The form will be used by restaurant staff to collect and process orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name and contact information in the "Customer Information" page.
2. Provide the order details, including the food and drink items, in the "Order Details" page.
3. If necessary, select the "Order Status" to update the order's status in real-time.
4. Leave any additional comments or special requests in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name and contact information.

### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Provide the order details, including the food and drink items.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): This field is not necessary for the preorder process, but can be used for future payments or reference.

### Order Status
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the order status to update the order's status in real-time.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or special requests for the restaurant staff.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the order is processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact restaurant staff for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
* This form is for preorder purposes only, if you need to place an order for immediate pickup or delivery, please use the regular ordering system.
