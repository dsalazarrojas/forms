# Farm To Table Product Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farm To Table Product Delivery Form is a form designed for customers to place orders for fresh produce delivery. This form collects essential information to process and fulfill the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Details** section with your order requirements.
2. Enter your **Customer Info** to ensure your order is delivered to the correct address.
3. Specify the **Delivery Schedule** for your order.
4. Provide **Payment Info** for your order.
5. Confirm the **Order Total** for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your order, including the type and quantity of products you want to purchase.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information so we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Delivery Schedule** (`delivery_schedule`, text, optional): Choose a delivery date and time that suits you. If you don't specify, we will deliver your order on the next available schedule.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method and details, such as credit card number or bank transfer information.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Confirm the total cost of your order to ensure accuracy.
