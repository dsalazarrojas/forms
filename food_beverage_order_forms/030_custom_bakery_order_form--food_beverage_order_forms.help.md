# Custom Bakery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing custom bakery orders. Please fill it out accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read through each page and fill out the corresponding fields. You can submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): This is the main section for entering your order details. Please provide a brief description of your order, including any special requests or instructions.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): In this section, please enter your name and any other relevant contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, required/optional): If you need your order delivered, enter the delivery details here. This includes the date and time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): This is where you'll enter payment details for your order. Please ensure you have the correct payment information to complete the form.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
