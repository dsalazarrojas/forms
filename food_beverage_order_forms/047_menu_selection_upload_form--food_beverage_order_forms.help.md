# Menu Selection Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form allows you to upload and manage menu items and associated files in a food and beverage establishment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the instructions below for each page to successfully complete the form.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Page**: Enter any additional comments or information related to the menu upload. (Form Page, text, optional)
* **Menu Upload Page**: Select whether this menu upload is for "Yes" or "No" purposes. (Menu Upload Page, select_multiple, optional)
* **File Upload**: Upload any additional files associated with the menu upload. (File Upload, text, optional)

## Tips

* If you need to upload multiple menu items, repeat the form process for each item.
* Ensure to save your progress before navigating away from the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or the upload process, contact your administrator for assistance.
