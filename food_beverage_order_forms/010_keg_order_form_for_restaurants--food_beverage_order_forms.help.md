# Keg Order Form For Restaurants - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect keg beer orders from customers for restaurants.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Customer Details" page.
2. Enter the details of the keg order in the "Keg Order" page.
3. Specify the delivery details for the order in the "Delivery Details" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you after processing your order.
* **Keg Order** (`Keg Order`, text, required: false): Describe the type and quantity of the keg order you'd like to place.
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, text, required: false): Specify the delivery location and any additional delivery instructions.
