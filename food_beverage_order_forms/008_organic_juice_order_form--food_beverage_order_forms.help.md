# Organic Juice Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for organic juices. Please fill out the form carefully and ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organic Juice Order Form** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the name of the form, and you don't need to fill it out. It's simply a heading to identify the form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required): Describe the type and quantity of organic juice you are interested in purchasing.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Venmo
* **Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of organic juices you would like to order.
* **Frequency** (`order_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of your order:
	+ Once
	+ Regularly
	+ Weekly
* **Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, please add them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask your sales team for assistance.
* Please be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure a smooth ordering process.
