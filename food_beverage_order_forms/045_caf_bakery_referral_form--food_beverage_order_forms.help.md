# Café Bakery Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for referrers to provide information about businesses or customers they are referring to our café and bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title as the person making the referral.
2. Provide the name or contact information for the business or customer being referred.
3. Describe the reason for the referral (e.g. wholesale partnership, customer referral).
4. If applicable, provide your contact information for the referrer.
5. Indicate your preferred method of follow-up (e.g. email, phone, in person).
6. Enter the date the referral was made (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title as the person making the referral.
* **Referred business or customer** (`referred_business`, `text`, required): Provide the name or contact information for the business or customer being referred.
* **Reason for referral** (`referral_reason`, `text`, optional): Describe the reason for the referral, e.g. wholesale partnership or customer referral.
* **Contact information for referral** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): If you have contact information for the referrer, enter it here.
* **Preferred follow up method** (`preferred_follow_up`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up, e.g. email, phone, in person.
* **Referral date** (`referral_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the referral was made, if applicable.
* **Consent to contact** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that it is okay to contact the referred party.
