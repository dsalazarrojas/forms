# BCC Pantry Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BCC Pantry Orders form is designed to help members place orders for bulk pantry items, produce, proteins, dairy, and household essentials. This form ensures accurate and efficient order fulfillment while allowing members to specify their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Member Name**, a required field indicating the name of the member placing the order.
2. Enter your **Member ID Number**, a required field for identification purposes.
3. Provide your **Email Address**, a required field for order confirmation and communication.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, a required field for contact purposes.
5. Specify the **Order Date**, which is crucial for fulfilling orders and ensuring timely delivery.
6. Choose your preferred **Preferred Pickup Date**, which is necessary for scheduling order fulfillment.
7. Select the **Grain Items** you need from the options provided. This includes items like Rice, Pasta, Bread, Flour, Oats, and Quinoa.
8. Choose the **Protein Items** needed, which includes Canned tuna, Canned Chicken, Beans, Lentils, Peanut butter, Eggs, and None.
9. Select the **Dairy Items** required, such as Milk, Cheese, Yogurt, Butter, and None.
10. Choose the **Fresh Produce** you need, like Apples, Bananas, Oranges, Potatoes, Carrots, Onions, Leafy greens, and None.
11. Select the **Pantry Staples** essential for your household, such as Cooking oil, Salt and pepper, Sugar, Canned vegetables, Canned fruit, Soup, Cereal, and None.
12. (Optional) If applicable, select **Baby Items**, including Baby formula, Baby food, Diapers, Baby wipes, or Not applicable.
13. (Optional) Choose the **Household Items** you need, like Dish soap, Laundry detergent, Paper towels, Toilet paper, Trash bags, and None.
14. (Optional) Add **Quantity Notes** if you need to specify quantities or have special requests.
15. (Optional) Enter any **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** you have, which will help us provide suitable alternatives.
16. Finally, add any **Special Requests** you may have, such as delivery instructions or comments.
