# Frozen Beverage Shop Payment Plan Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frozen Beverage Shop Payment Plan Enrollment form is used to manage customer payment plans for recurring purchases of frozen beverages. This form allows customers to provide information about their payment preferences, including their preferred payment method, start and end dates, and frequency of payments. The form also allows customers to input any additional comments or notes they wish to share with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer information in the **Customer Information** section.
2.  Enter your address in the **Address** section.
3.  Select your preferred **Customer Plan** in the **Customer Plan** section. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" options.
4.  Select your preferred **Payment Method** in the **Payment Method** section. You can choose between "Credit Card," "PayPal," and "Bank Transfer" options.
5.  Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for your payment plan in the **Start Date** and **End Date** sections, respectively.
6.  Enter the price of your **Customer Plan Price** in the **Price** section.
7.  Enter the duration of your **Customer Plan Duration** in the **Duration** section.
8.  Select your payment frequency in the **Frequency** section. You can choose between "Daily," "Weekly," and "Monthly" options.
9.  Select the **Status** of your payment plan in the **Status** section. You can choose between "Active," "Inactive," and "Pending" options.
10.  Select the **Payment Method** for your payment plan in the **Payment Method** section again.
11.  Enter any **Comments** you wish to share with our team in the **Comments** section.
12.  Enter any additional **Notes** in the **Notes** section.
13.  Select the reason for canceling your payment plan in the **Cancel Reason** section. You can choose between "Not Interested," "Price," and "Other" options.
14.  Enter the **Cancel Date** for your payment plan in the **Cancel Date** section.
15.  Select whether to **Renew** your payment plan in the **Renewal** section.
16.  Enter the **Expiration Date** for your payment plan in the **Expiration Date** section.
17.  Enter the **Last Payment Date** for your payment plan in the **Last Payment Date** section.
18.  Enter the **Last Payment Amount** for your payment plan in the **Last Payment Amount** section.
19.  Enter the **Next Payment Date** for your payment plan in the **Next Payment Date** section.
20.  Enter the **Next Payment Amount** for your payment plan in the **Next Payment Amount** section.
21.  Select the status of your next payment in the **Next Payment Status** section. You can choose between "Paid," "Pending," and "Failed" options.
22.  Enter any additional details about your **Payment Schedule** in the **Payment Schedule** section.
23.  Enter the date and time of your **Payment Frequency Date** in the **Payment Frequency Date** section.
24.  Enter the time of your **Payment Frequency Time** in the **Payment Frequency Time** section.
25.  Enter any **Customer Notes** in the **Customer Notes** section.
26.  Enter any additional **Admin Notes** in the **Admin Notes** section.
