# Daily Fridge Temperature Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Fridge Temperature Log is a form used to record the temperature reading of your fridge and freezer daily. This information will help you monitor and maintain a safe food storage environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Fridge Temperature field with the current temperature reading of your fridge.
* Select the Reading Time field to enter the date and time when you took the temperature reading.
* Select the Freezer Temperature field to enter the current temperature reading of your freezer.
* Choose the Freezer Temperature Unit field to select the unit of measurement for the freezer temperature (Celsius or Farenheit).
* Select the Fridge Storage Location field to specify where in the fridge the storage is located (Main Fridge, Main Freezer, or Other).
* Enter your User Name so we can identify you in case of any further questions.
* Enter any additional Reading Notes if you need to record any other information.
* Optionally, enter your Email and Phone number for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fridge Temperature** (Fridge Temperature, Number, Required): Enter the current temperature reading of your fridge.
- **Reading Time** (Reading Time, Date, Required): Enter the date and time when you took the temperature reading.
- **Freezer Temperature** (Freezer Temperature, Number, Required): Enter the current temperature reading of your freezer.
- **Freezer Temperature Unit** (Freezer Temperature Unit, Select One, Required): Select the unit of measurement for the freezer temperature (Celsius or Farenheit).
- **Fridge Storage Location** (Fridge Storage Location, Select Multiple, Required): Select where in the fridge the storage is located (Main Fridge, Main Freezer, or Other).
- **User Name** (User Name, Text, Required): Enter your name so we can identify you in case of any further questions.
- **Reading Notes** (Reading Notes, Note, Optional): Enter any additional information you want to record.
- **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for future reference.
- **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for future reference.
