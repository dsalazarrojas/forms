# Weekly Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Lunch Order Form is used to submit your weekly lunch order preferences. Please fill out this form to let us know what you'd like to order for the week.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Select the days you would like to order food for (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday).
3. Choose if you would like to order food for each selected day.
4. Enter the quantity of food you would like to order.
5. Add any additional notes about your order.
6. Select who the order is assigned to.
7. Choose the tool to be used for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Lunch Order** (`1`, `text`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`): Enter your email address.
* **Order** (`3`, `select_multiple`): Select the days you would like to order food for. This field is for choosing specific days, such as Monday, Tuesday, etc.
* **Name** (`4`, `select_multiple`): Choose whether you would like to order food for this day (yes/no).
* **Food** (`5`, `select_multiple`): Choose whether you would like to order food for this day (yes/no).
* **Quantity** (`6`, `number`): Enter the quantity of food you would like to order.
* **Note** (`7`, `note`): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Assigned to** (`8`, `email`): Select who the order is assigned to.
* **Assigned Tool** (`9`, `select_one`): Choose the tool to be used for the order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you select multiple days, be sure to choose "yes" for each day in the "Name" field.
* If you have any special requests or questions, use the "Note" field to add additional information.
