# Ramadan Menu Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Ramadan Menu Order Form Template. Please take a few minutes to read through this guide before starting the form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the "Main Page" section, which asks if you want certain food options for the month of Ramadan. Select all the options that you would like to have, and type "Yes" in the corresponding boxes.
2. In the "Main Page Group 1" and "Main Page Group 2" sections, provide any additional information that you would like to include with your order.
3. Choose a date for your order in the "Date" field.
4. Select a time for your order in the "Time" field.
5. If you have any special instructions or requests, please type them in the "Notes" field.
6. Finally, provide your contact information in the "Telephone" and "Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`Main Page`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that you would like to have for the month of Ramadan.
* **Main Page Group 1** (`Main Page Group 1`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you would like to include with your order.
* **Main Page Group 2** (`Main Page Group 2`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you would like to include with your order.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your order.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select a time for your order.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests, please type them in here.
* **Telephone** (`Telephone`, text, optional): Provide your contact information.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Provide your contact information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you select the correct options and provide accurate contact information to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for completing this form, and we look forward to serving you during the month of Ramadan.
