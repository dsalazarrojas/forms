# CSA Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSA Order Form is designed to collect customer information, order details, and payment method for Community-Supported Agriculture (CSA) orders. This form ensures accurate and complete information for efficient order fulfillment and delivery management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Customer ID or Member Number** (if applicable).
2. Select your preferred **CSA Share Type** from the options provided.
3. Choose your **Share Size** from the available options.
4. Select your **Delivery Frequency** (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or seasonal).
5. Choose the **Season Selection** you desire (e.g., Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, or Year-round).
6. Determine the **Number of Shares** you would like to order.
7. If applicable, enter the **Seasonal Cost per Share**.
8. List any **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** you have.
9. Specify your preferred **Preferred Items**.
10. List any **Items to Avoid**.
11. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (e.g., credit card, debit card, check, EBT or SNAP, or cash at pickup).
12. Select how you prefer to receive your share (e.g., **Pickup at Farm**, **Farmers Market**, **Home Delivery**, or **Alternative Location**).
13. If applicable, enter a specific **Pickup Location**.
14. Provide any **Special Instructions or Notes**.
15. Confirm you agree to the **CSA Terms and Conditions** by selecting "Yes, I agree."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for potential follow-up communication.
* **Customer ID or Member Number**: If you are an existing customer, enter your customer ID or member number for reference.
* **CSA Share Type**: Select the type of CSA share that suits your needs (e.g., Vegetable, Fruit, Mixed Produce, Small, Large, Family).
* **Share Size**: Choose the size of your CSA share (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Delivery Frequency**: Select how often you would like to receive your shares (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, seasonal).
* **Season Selection**: Choose the seasons you would like to receive your shares (e.g., Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Year-round).
* **Number of Shares**: Enter the quantity of shares you would like to order.
* **Seasonal Cost per Share**: If applicable, enter the price for the season.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences**: List any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g., allergies, organic preferences).
* **Preferred Items**: Specify your preferred produce items.
* **Items to Avoid**: List any produce items you would like to avoid.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Pickup or Delivery**: Choose how you would like to receive your share (e.g., pickup at farm, farmers market, home delivery, alternative location).
* **Pickup Location**: If applicable, enter a specific pickup point.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Provide any special requests or notes.
* **I Agree to CSA Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement to the CSA terms and conditions by selecting "Yes, I agree."
