# Restaurant Delivery Settings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Delivery Settings Form is a tool designed to help you configure delivery settings for your restaurant. This form collects information about your service areas, fees, availability, and service hours to ensure smooth delivery operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Service Areas" page and choose the areas from which you can accept delivery orders.
2. On the "Fees" page, select the applicable fee options for your restaurant.
3. On the "Availability" page, set a date for when your restaurant is available to deliver orders.
4. On the "Service Hours" page, enter the hours during which you offer delivery services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Areas**: Select the areas from which your restaurant can accept delivery orders. This field is used to determine which locations are eligible for delivery.
* **Fees**: Choose the applicable fee options for your restaurant. This field helps determine additional fees for delivery orders.
* **Availability**: Set a date for when your restaurant is available to deliver orders. This field ensures that delivery orders are only accepted during your available time.
* **Service Hours**: Enter the hours during which you offer delivery services. This field is used to determine the time slots available for delivery orders.
