# Treat Package Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential details for a treat package order, ensuring that your treats are delivered on time and to the right place. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Customer Info** with your basic details, such as name and contact information.
2. Provide your **Order Details** if you have any special requests or instructions for your order.
3. Choose your **Delivery Date** for the treat package.
4. Fill out your **Order Total** with the correct cost of your order.
5. Select a **Payment Method** to indicate if you're paying or not.
6. Enter your **Shipping Address** to ensure the treat package reaches you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Info** (<required: false>)
• **Customer Info**: <text> - This field is intended to collect basic customer information, such as name and contact details, for identification and communication purposes.

### 2. **Order Details** (<required: false>)
• **Order Details**: <text> - This field is for customers to provide additional details about their order, such as special requests or instructions.

### 3. **Delivery Date** (<required: false>)
• **Delivery Date**: <date> - Fill this field with the date on which you want the treat package to be delivered.

### 4. **Order Total** (<required: true>)
• **Order Total**: <number> - This field is required to display the total cost of the order. Please enter the correct amount.

### 5. **Payment Method** (<required: false>)
• **Payment Method**: <select_one> - Select "Yes" if you're paying or "No" if you're not.

### 6. **Shipping Address** (<required: false>)
• **Shipping Address**: <text> - Enter your shipping address to ensure the treat package reaches you on time and correctly.
