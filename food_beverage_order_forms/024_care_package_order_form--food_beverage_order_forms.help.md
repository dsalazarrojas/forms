## Care Package Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Care Package Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for care packages, providing the necessary information for delivery and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name to identify yourself as the person placing the order in the "Requester name" field.
2. Ensure you provide your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field for delivery coordination.
3. Optionally, provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field for order confirmation.
4. Choose the type of care package from the options in the "Package type" field.
5. Enter the quantity of care packages you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Provide the full address for delivery in the "Delivery address" field.
7. If desired, specify a preferred delivery date in the "Preferred delivery date" field.
8. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions or special instructions in the "Dietary restrictions" and "Special instructions" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for delivery coordination.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Package type** (`package_type`, select_one, required): Choose the package variant you wish to order (Basic, Standard, or Premium).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of care packages you wish to order.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the full address for delivery.
* **Preferred delivery date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, date, optional): Specify a desired delivery date.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any allergy or dietary needs.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Provide notes for packers or drivers.

Please note that this form is for submitting orders for care packages. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
