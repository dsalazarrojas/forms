# Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to subscribe to a meal kit delivery service, allowing you to choose your meal kit preferences and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all required fields. If a field is marked as "required", it's essential to complete it to ensure your subscription is processed correctly.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you're filling it out as intended by the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your subscription.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you'd like to receive your meal kit delivery.
* **Order Frequency** (`order_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you'd like to receive your meal kit deliveries. Options include:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Biweekly
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method you'd like to use for your subscription. Options include:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Cash

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid errors in processing your subscription.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at our support team.
