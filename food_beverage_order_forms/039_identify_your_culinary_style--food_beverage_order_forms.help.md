# Identify Your Culinary Style - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your culinary style, including your favorite cuisine, cooking methods, and dietary preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your favorite cuisine from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred cooking methods, such as grilling, baking, or boiling.
3. Indicate whether you have any dietary restrictions, such as being vegan, gluten-free, or low-sodium.
4. Select your meal time, including morning, afternoon, or evening.
5. Choose your preferred food categories, such as grains, fruits, and vegetables.
6. Indicate your level of preference for spicy or rare food.
7. Select your preferred meal frequency, such as breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
8. Choose your preferred cooking methods, such as grilling, baking, or roasting.
9. Select your preferred time of day for meals.
10. Choose your preferred food categories, such as breakfast, lunch, or snack.
