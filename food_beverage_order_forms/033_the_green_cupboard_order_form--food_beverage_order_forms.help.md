# The Green Cupboard Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "The Green Cupboard Order Form" is designed to collect information for ordering food and beverages. Fill out this form to place an order with us.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct delivery location from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the required delivery fee.
3.  Enter the customer's name.
4.  Enter the customer's contact number.
5.  Enter the customer's email.
6.  Enter any additional notes for the order.
7.  List the items to be ordered.
8.  Provide any order instructions.
9.  Enter the address for delivery.
10. Confirm the order status.
11. Choose a payment method.
12. Enter the total amount of the order.
13. Assign a delivery person (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Delivery Date* (`delivery_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
*   *Cutoff Date* (`cutoff_date`, date, not required): Enter the date after which orders will not be accepted.
*   *Min Order* (`min_order`, number, not required): Enter the minimum order amount required.
*   *Delivery Location* (`delivery_location`, select_one, required): Select the location where you would like to receive your order (e.g. Nuvali villages, Sta. Rosa Villages, Laguna via Lalamove).
*   *Delivery Fee* (`delivery_fee`, text, not required): Enter any additional delivery fee for your order.
*   *Name* (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
*   *Contact Number* (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your contact number.
*   *Email* (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
*   *Note* (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes for your order.
*   *Order Items* (`order_items`, text, not required): List the items to be ordered.
*   *Order Instructions* (`order_instructions`, text, not required): Provide any instructions for the order.
*   *Address* (`address`, text, not required): Enter the delivery address.
*   *Total Order* (`total_order`, number, not required): Enter the total order amount.
*   *Order Confirmation* (`order_confirmation`, select_multiple, not required): Confirm or reject your order.
*   *Special Instructions* (`special_instructions`, select_multiple, not required): Specify any special instructions for your order.
*   *Delivery Time* (`delivery_time`, time, not required): Enter the delivery time.
*   *Order Status* (`order_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of your order (e.g. delivered, pending, cancelled).
*   *Message* (`message`, text, not required): Enter a message for the delivery person.
*   *Payment Method* (`payment_method`, select_multiple, not required): Choose a payment method (e.g. cash, bank transfer, etc).
*   *Payment Status* (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of your payment (e.g. paid, pending, not paid).
*   *Delivery Person* (`delivery_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person delivering the order.
*   *Delivery Note* (`delivery_note`, note, not required): Enter any notes for the delivery person.
*   *Payment Person* (`payment_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person making the payment.
*   *Total Amount* (`total_amount`, number, not required): Enter the total amount of the order.
*   *Assigned Tool* (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter the assigned tool for the order.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and aims to provide clear explanations for each field in simple terms.
