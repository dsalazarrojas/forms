# Beverage Stock Reconciliation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Stock Reconciliation Order Form is designed to aid in the accurate counting and reconciliation of beverage stock for your bar, restaurant, or hospitality venue. The form covers all types of beverages, including alcohols, non-alcohols, and coffee/tea supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Location Information section with the name of your venue, the manager's name and contact information, and the date and time of the count.
2. For each type of alcohol, enter the current count of bottles in stock.
3. For non-alcoholic beverages, enter the stock level or quantity.
4. In the Damage and Loss Report section, list any damaged or expired items.
5. In the Unexplained Loss or Shrinkage section, document any unexplained discrepancies in the inventory.
6. In the Replenishment Orders section, list items that need to be reordered or replenished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location or Establishment Name (2)**: Enter the name of your bar, restaurant, or hospitality venue.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name (3)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Manager Email (4)**: Enter the email of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Manager Phone Number (5)**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for inventory management.
* **Reconciliation Date (6)**: Enter the date of the stock count.
* **Reconciliation Time (7)**: Enter the time of the count (optional).
* **Vodka Bottles - Current Count (9)**: Enter the current count of vodka bottles in stock.
* **Gin Bottles - Current Count (10)**: Enter the current count of gin bottles in stock.
* **Rum Bottles - Current Count (11)**: Enter the current count of rum bottles in stock.
* **Whiskey Bottles - Current Count (12)**: Enter the current count of whiskey bottles in stock.
* **Tequila Bottles - Current Count (13)**: Enter the current count of tequila bottles in stock.
* **Wine Bottles - Current Count (14)**: Enter the total count of wine bottles in stock.
* **Beer Cases or Units - Current Count (15)**: Enter the current count of beer units in stock.
* **Fully Stocked, Adequate, Low Stock, or Out of Stock (27)**: Enter the current status of coffee and tea supplies.
* **Damaged or Broken Items (29)**: List any damaged or broken items.
* **Expired Items Found (30)**: List any expired items.
* **Unexplained Loss or Shrinkage (31)**: Document any unexplained discrepancies in the inventory.
* **Items Requiring Reorder (33)**: List items that need to be reordered or replenished.
* **Items Needed Urgently (34)**: List items that need to be reordered ASAP.
* **Preferred Supplier Contact (35)**: Enter the name or phone number of your usual supplier.
* **Additional Notes or Comments (36)**: Enter any additional comments or observations.

## Tips
* Ensure that you accurately count and report all stock levels and discrepancies.
* If a discrepancy is found, investigate the cause and document it in the relevant section.
* Use this form as a reference guide for future stock counts and reconciliations.
