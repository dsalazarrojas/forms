# Comfort Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for comfort food items. It guides you through the process of ordering your preferred items, selecting delivery or pickup options, and finalizing your order with payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** to provide contact information for order processing and confirmation.
2. Select the **Order Type** you prefer: **Pickup**, **Delivery**, or **Dine-In**.
3. Choose your desired items from the respective sections (**Appetizers**, **Main Dishes**, **Side Dishes**, and **Desserts**). Each option is labeled with its price.
4. For some items, you can choose the quantity you want.
5. In the **Delivery Details** section, provide the **Delivery Address** and **Preferred Delivery Time** if you've chosen delivery.
6. Enter any **Special Instructions** or allergies in the designated field.
7. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
8. Review the **Total Amount**, including tax, to confirm your order total.
