# Spoiled Food Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spoiled Food Incident Report form is designed to collect information about incidents where food or beverages have spoiled or gone bad. This form is used to report these incidents to the relevant authorities and ensure that corrective actions are taken to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct date of the incident from the calendar or enter the date manually.
2. Select all relevant food types that were affected by the incident, such as "Apple" or "Banana".
3. Briefly describe the reason for the spoilage or why the food went bad.
4. Identify the location where the incident occurred, such as "Kitchen" or "Freezer".
5. Enter the approximate number of items affected by the incident.
6. Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
7. Enter the phone number of the employee involved in the incident (if applicable).
8. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident. This can be selected from the calendar or entered manually.
* **Food Type** (`food_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the types of food or beverages that were affected by the incident. Multiple options are available to choose from.
* **Reason for Spoilage** (`reason_for_spoilage`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the reason why the food went bad or spoiled.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required: false): Identify the location where the incident occurred. Options include "Kitchen", "Freezer", "Warehouse", or other locations.
* **Number of Items Affected** (`number_of_items_affected`, number, required: false): Enter the approximate number of items affected by the incident.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee involved in the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, note, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
