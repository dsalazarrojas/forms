# Catering Description And Photo Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about catering vendors for event planners. It helps us create profiles for vendors in our directory, making it easier for event planners to find the right vendors for their events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor information in the "Vendor Information" section.
2. Describe your catering services, specialties, and unique offerings in the "Service Profile" section.
3. Upload high-resolution photos of your food and setup in the "Photo Submission Instruction" section (we will provide a secure upload link once you've submitted the form).
4. Provide your online presence information (optional) in the "Online Presence" section.
5. Fill in your operational details in the "Operational Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Catering Company Name** (Vendor Name): Enter the name of your catering business.
* **Primary Contact Person** (Contact Person Name): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business's communication.
* **Business Email Address** (Business Email Address): Enter your company's email address.
* **Business Phone Number** (Business Phone Number): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Service Profile** (Service Profile): Select your primary catering specialty (e.g. Traditional/Continental, Italian and Mediterranean, etc.).
* **Years in Operation** (Years in Operation): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Brief Description of Services** (Brief Description of Services): Describe your business's style, mission, and unique offerings.
* **Sample Menu Description** (Sample Menu Description): List 3-5 signature dishes your business offers.
* **Please Ensure You Have High-Resolution Images of Your Food and Setup** (Photo Submission Instruction): We will provide a secure upload link once you've submitted the form.
* **Company Website** (Company Website): Enter your business's website URL (optional).
* **Instagram or Portfolio Link** (Instagram or Portfolio Link): Enter your business's Instagram or portfolio link (optional).
* **Service Area (Cities/Regions Covered)** (Service Area (Cities/Regions Covered)): Enter the cities or regions your business serves.
* **Typical Price Range per Guest** (Typical Price Range per Guest): Select your business's typical price range.
* **Currently Accepting New Bookings** (Availability Status): Select whether your business is currently accepting new bookings.
* **Any Other Information for Event Planners** (Additional Vendor Notes): Enter any other relevant information for event planners.

Note: Asterisks are used to denote the field names as per the original YAML format.
