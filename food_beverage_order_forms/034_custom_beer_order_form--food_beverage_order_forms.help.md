# Custom Beer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Beer Order Form is a tool used to collect and organize customer information for the purpose of placing a custom beer order. To ensure that your order is processed correctly, please read this help guide carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer details, including name, address, and other relevant information.
2. Specify the order details, including the type and quantity of beer ordered.
3. Provide your payment details for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (`customer_details`, text, required):*Enter your name and other relevant contact information.*
* **Order (`order`, text, required):*Describe the type and quantity of beer you would like to order.*
* **Delivery Address (`delivery_address`, text, required):*Enter your delivery address for the beer order.*
* **Payment Details (`payment_details`, text, required):*Provide your payment information for the order, including any relevant payment methods or instructions.*
