# in_flight_meal_options_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your in-flight meal preferences and dietary requirements. It will help the flight crew to provide you with a personalized meal service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the form page by page, starting with Page 1.
2. Enter your passenger name in the `passenger_name` field.
3. Select your preferred meal option from the `meal-preference` dropdown menu.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them from the `dietary_restrictions` dropdown menu.
5. Leave any additional comments or feedback in the `feedback` field.
6. Indicate your preferred meal time from the `preferred-meal-time` field.
7. If you have any special requests, select them from the `special-requests` dropdown menu.
8. Enter any additional comments or questions in the `passenger-comments` field.
9. Enter any further comments or questions in the `passenger-comments` field.
10. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`): Enter your name, as it appears on your boarding pass or ID.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal-preference`): Choose your preferred meal option (Chicken, Beef, Fish).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`): If you have any dietary restrictions, select them (None, Vegetarian, Vegan).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Leave any additional comments or feedback about your meal preferences.
* **Preferred Meal Time** (`preferred-meal-time`): Indicate your preferred meal time.
* **Special Requests** (`special-requests`): If you have any special requests, select them (Food allergy, Dietary restriction, Other (please describe)).
* **Passenger Comments** (`passenger-comments`): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger-name`): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Passenger Email** (`passenger-email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Form Submission** (`form-submission`): Choose how to proceed with your form submission (Save and close, Continue to next page, Submit).

Note: The form will proceed to the next page after you select "Continue to next page" or submit the form after selecting "Submit".
