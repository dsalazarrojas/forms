# Caffe Ibis Coffee Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order at Caffe Ibis Coffee. It captures essential information to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your order. Please fill out all required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Your Name** (full name) in the text field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** (required for order confirmation) in the text field.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number** (contact number for order issues) in the text field.
4.  Select your **Order Type** (pickup, curbside pickup, or delivery) from the dropdown options.
5.  Choose your **Coffee Selection** from the dropdown options (Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, etc.).
6.  Select your **Coffee Size** from the dropdown options (8 oz, 12 oz, 16 oz, etc.).
7.  Choose your **Milk Preference** from the dropdown options (Whole milk, Skim milk, Almond milk, etc.).
8.  Select your **Sweetness Level** (No sugar, Light sugar, Regular sugar, etc.) from the dropdown options.
9.  If you'd like to add any flavor syrups, select them from the dropdown options (Vanilla, Caramel, Hazelnut, etc.). You can select multiple flavors.
10. Select your **Temperature** preference for the drink (Hot, Iced, Extra hot, etc.).
11. Choose the **Quantity** of drinks you'd like to order (enter a number).
12. If you'd like to add a pastry with your order, select one from the dropdown options (Croissant, Muffin, Scone, etc.). 
13. Enter any **Special Instructions** you'd like to include with your order in the text field.
14. Select your **Preferred Pickup Time** (when you'd like to pick up the order) from the dropdown options.
15. Finally, select your **Payment Method** (Credit card, Debit card, Cash, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (customer_name): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your contact phone number for order issues.
* **Order Type** (order_type): Select how you'd like to receive your order.
* **Coffee Selection** (coffee_selection): Choose your desired coffee type.
* **Coffee Size** (coffee_size): Select the size of your coffee.
* **Milk Preference** (milk_preference): Choose your preferred type of milk.
* **Sweetness Level** (sweetness_level): Select your desired level of sweetness.
* **Flavor Add-ons** (flavor_add_ons): Select any flavor syrups you'd like to add.
* **Temperature** (temperature): Choose the temperature of your drink.
* **Quantity** (quantity): Enter the number of drinks you'd like to order.
* **Pastry Add-on** (pastry_add_on): Select a pastry to add to your order if desired.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions): Enter any special requests you have for your order.
* **Preferred Pickup Time** (pickup_time): Select when you'd like to pick up your order.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select how you'd like to pay for your order.
