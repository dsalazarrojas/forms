# Herbs And Foods Crop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow farm owners to place orders for herbs and foods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Review the form fields and understand what information is required for each field.
- Fill in your farm's details in the "Farm Details" field.
- Select the types of crops you would like to order from the "Crop List".
- Enter any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.
- Select the payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
- Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" in the "Submit" field.
- Choose the date and time for your order in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
- If required, provide your email address and phone number to enable communication with the farm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Details (farm_details)**: Enter your farm's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Crop List (crop_list)**: Select the types of crops you would like to order. You can select multiple options.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Enter any additional order details such as quantity, quality, etc.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter your payment information such as amount, method, etc.
* **Submit (submit)**: Confirm your order by selecting "Yes".
* **Date (date)**: Choose the date for your order.
* **Time (time)**: Choose the time for your order.
* **Email (email)**: Provide your email address to enable communication with the farm.
* **Phone (phone)**: Provide your phone number to enable communication with the farm.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your order carefully before submitting it.
- Double-check your payment details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant party for assistance.
- The farm may contact you via email or phone to confirm your order.
