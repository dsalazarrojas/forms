# Food Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their dining experience at our restaurant. It helps us evaluate the food, service, and overall satisfaction of our customers, providing valuable insights for us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Provide detailed and honest feedback in the comments section.
3. Submit the form to let us know about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Food Order
* **food_order** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the food item you ordered.

### Taste Rating
* **taste_rating** (number, required/optional): Rate the taste of your food on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Quality Rating
* **quality_rating** (number, required/optional): Rate the quality of your food on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Service Rating
* **service_rating** (text, required/optional): Rate the quality of our service on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Food Service Quality Rating
* **food_service_quality_rating** (number, required/optional): Rate the quality of food service on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Food Quality Rating
* **food_quality_rating** (number, required/optional): Rate the quality of food on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **overall_satisfaction** (number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Comments
* **comments** (text, required/optional): Share your thoughts and suggestions for us to improve our services.
