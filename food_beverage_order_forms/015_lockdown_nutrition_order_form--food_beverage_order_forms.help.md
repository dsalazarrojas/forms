# Lockdown Nutrition Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lockdown Nutrition Order Form is designed to help businesses receive orders for essential food and beverage products during the lockdown period. This form is intended for customers who want to place orders for these products online while adhering to social distancing measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Contact Details** section with your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you regarding your order.
2.  List the **Products List** you want to order, separating each product with a comma or a new line.
3.  If you have any **Product Images** that support your order (e.g., product packaging), please attach them here.
4.  Enter the **Product Prices** for each product in your order.
5.  In the **Order Details** section, provide any additional information you think is necessary to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you about your order.
*   **Products List** (`Products List`, `text`, required): List each product you want to order, separated by commas or new lines.
*   **Product Images** (`Product Images`, `text`, optional): Attach images that support your order, such as product packaging.
*   **Product Prices** (`Product Prices`, `text`, required): Enter the price for each product in your order.
*   **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you think is necessary to complete your order.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure we can contact you about your order.
*   Be specific when listing the products you want to order to avoid any misunderstandings.
*   Attach clear and relevant product images to support your order.
*   Double-check the product prices you enter to avoid any errors.
*   If you have any special requests or questions, use the order details section to communicate them to us.

This help guide will assist customers in understanding how to fill out the form correctly and efficiently. It will ensure that all required information is provided, and that orders are processed smoothly.
