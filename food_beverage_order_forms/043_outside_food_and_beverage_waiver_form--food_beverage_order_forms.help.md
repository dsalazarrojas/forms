# Outside Food And Beverage Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outside Food and Beverage Waiver Form is a document that requires individuals to acknowledge and confirm that they are aware of the venue's policies regarding outside food and beverage services. This form helps to ensure that the venue's policies are respected and understood by all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the introduction and acknowledgements sections to understand the venue's policies.
2. Provide your details in the venue_details section.
3. Enter the event details in the event_details section.
4. Describe the food and beverage services you are offering in the food_and_beverage_details section.
5. Indicate whether or not you are requesting a waiver by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the waiver_request section.
6. Sign and date the form with your signature in the signature section.
7. Sign and date the form with the organizer's signature in the organizer_signature section.
8. Sign and date the form with the venue's signature in the venue_signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the venue's policies regarding outside food and beverage services. Please review this section carefully to understand the rules and regulations.
* **Acknowledgements**: Please select 'Yes' to acknowledge that you have read and understand the venue's policies.
* **Venue Details**: Please provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself and your food and beverage services.
* **Organizer Details**: Please enter the name and details of the event organizer.
* **Event Details**: Please enter the details of the event, including the date, time, and location.
* **Food and Beverage Details**: Please describe the food and beverage services you are offering, including the menu and any other relevant details.
* **Waiver Request**: If you are requesting a waiver, please select 'Yes' to indicate that you are aware of the venue's policies and are willing to take the risk of not being able to use the venue's food and beverage services.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understand the venue's policies.
* **Organizer Signature**: Please sign and date this section to confirm that the event organizer is aware of the venue's policies.
* **Venue Signature**: Please sign and date this section to confirm that the venue is aware of your food and beverage services.
