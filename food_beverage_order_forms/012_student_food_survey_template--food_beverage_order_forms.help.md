# Student Food Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect information about a student's food preferences, eating habits, and demographic details. The survey aims to gather data to better understand students' eating habits, preferences, and frequency of consumption of different food and beverage items. This information will be used to improve food services and offerings on campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the survey information section (page 1). Enter your name and any other relevant details.
2. Proceed to the food preferences section, where you can select multiple food options from the dropdown menu.
3. In the eating habits section, provide a brief description of your eating habits.
4. Choose your favorite food and beverage options from the dropdown menus.
5. Enter your demographic details, including survey time and date, and contact information (phone number and email).
6. If you have any additional comments or further information, use the notes section.
7. Submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant details (Page 1).
  • **name** (text): Enter your name.
  • **email** (email): Enter your email address. (Optional)
- **food_preferences**: Select multiple food options that you enjoy (Page 2).
  • **Pizza**
  • **Burgers**
- **beverage_preferences**: Select multiple beverage options that you enjoy (Page 3).
  • **Water**
  • **Soda**
  • **Juice**
- **food_habits**: Describe your eating habits (Page 4).
  • Enter a brief description of your eating habits.
- **favorite_food**: Select your favorite food option (Page 5).
  • **Pizza**
  • **Burgers**
  • **Salad**
  • **Soup**
  • **Fruit**
- **favorite_beverage**: Select your favorite beverage option (Page 6).
  • **Water**
  • **Soda**
  • **Juice**
  • **Milk**
  • **Fruit Juice**
- **eating_habits**: Describe your eating habits (Page 7).
  • Enter a brief description of your eating habits.
- **comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share (Page 8).
  • Use this section to provide further details.
- **survey_time**: Enter the time of the survey (Page 9).
  • Enter the time you completed the survey.
- **survey_date**: Enter the date of the survey (Page 10).
  • Enter the date of the survey.
- **phone**: Enter your phone number (Page 11).
  • Enter your phone number.
- **email**: Enter your email address (Page 12).
  • Enter your email address. (Optional)
- **food_frequency**: Enter the frequency of consumption for each food option (Page 13-20).
  • Enter the frequency of consumption for each food option.
  • **food_frequency_2**: Enter any additional information (Page 21-25).
  • Enter any additional information about food frequency.
  • **food_frequency_3** to **food_frequency_10**: Enter any further additional information (Page 24-25).
  • Enter any further additional information about food frequency.

Note: The survey is 12 pages long; however, the last 8 pages (pages 13-20) are for entering food frequency, which will likely only be used by students who select specific food options in the previous pages. If you don't select any food options, you will only need to complete pages 1-12.
