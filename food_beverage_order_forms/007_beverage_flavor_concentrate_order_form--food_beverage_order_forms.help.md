# Beverage Flavor Concentrate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for beverage flavor concentrates. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the customer's order, including their contact and shipping details, flavor preferences, and delivery requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your Buyer Information**:
	* **Buyer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Company Name** (if applicable): Enter your business or organization's name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter a contact email address for order confirmation.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact number for updates.
2. **Enter Shipping Information**:
	* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive the order.
	* **City**: Enter the city where you would like to receive the order.
	* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like to receive the order.
	* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you would like to receive the order.
	* **Country**: Enter the country where you would like to receive the order.
3. **Select Concentrate Options**:
	* **Flavor Concentrate Varieties**: Select all the flavors you would like to order.
	* **Concentrate Strength Preference**: Choose your preferred strength of flavor.
	* **Order Unit Size**: Choose the size of containers you would like to receive.
	* **Quantity to Order**: Enter the number of units you would like to order.
4. **Special Specifications**:
	* **Sweetener Preference**: Choose your preferred sweetener option.
	* **Additives or Ingredients**: Select any special requirements you may have.
	* **Concentrate Color Preference**: Choose your preferred color.
5. **Delivery and Logistics**:
	* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
	* **Storage Condition Requirements**: If you have any special storage requirements, enter them here.
6. **Additional Information**:
	* **Have You Ordered From Us Before**: Indicate if you have ordered from us before.
	* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any additional information about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure accurate order delivery.
* Choose your flavor options carefully, as they will affect the taste and quality of your order.
* Enter the correct quantity and unit size to ensure you receive the correct amount of product.
* Be specific about your sweetener and color preferences to get the desired taste and appearance.
* Consider any special storage requirements for your order, especially if you plan to store it for an extended period.
* Be honest about your ordering history to ensure you receive the correct service level.
