# Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form is used by customers to place an order for freshly caught seafood from a market or restaurant. This form helps us gather essential information to fulfill your order correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Select the date and time of the catch.
3. Choose the type of seafood you would like to order.
4. Provide the weight of the seafood.
5. Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
6. If you've chosen credit card as your payment method, please provide your credit card information.
7. Select the type of order you would like to make (e.g., fish, shellfish, shrimp, cods).
8. Specify the quantity of each order item.
9. Add any additional comments about your order.
10. Finally, provide your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Please fill in your name as we will need it for communication and order fulfillment.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your order status.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code, so we can easily contact you if needed.
* **Catch Date**: Select the date of the catch, if you have this information available.
* **Catch Time**: Select the time of the catch, if you have this information.
* **Catch Weight**: Enter the weight of the caught seafood.
* **Payment Type**: Choose your preferred method of payment from the options provided (Credit Card, Cash, Bank Transfer).
* **Credit Card Info**: If you have chosen credit card as your payment method, please provide your credit card information, including the card number, expiration date, and security code.
* **Order Item**: Select the type of seafood you would like to order from the options provided (e.g., fish, shellfish, shrimp, cods).
* **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each order item you would like to order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional information about your order here, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Customer Signature**: Please provide your signature, as proof of your order.

## Tips

* Please double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff.
* We will contact you to confirm your order and provide further instructions.
