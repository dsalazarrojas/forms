# Reusable Food Wrap Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information for food wrap orders. It is designed to be easily adaptable and reusable for different types of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Info** section with the customer's name and contact information.
2. Provide the **Shipping Address** where the order will be delivered.
3. Select the type of **Wrap Order** from the provided options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
4. Add any additional **Customer Notes** that are relevant to the order (e.g., special requests or instructions).
5. Choose the payment option from the **Payment Info** section (e.g., Option 9, Option 10, etc.).
6. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Shipping Address**: Provide the address where the order will be delivered.
* **Wrap Order**: Select the type of wrap order you are placing. This option will determine the specific wrap type and contents.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional information or special requests for the customer.
* **Payment Info**: Choose the payment method for the order.
* **Submit**: Complete the form by clicking this button, and the order will be processed.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is only for online orders, not for in-person or phone orders.
