# Strawberry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strawberry Order Form is a straightforward form designed for customers to place orders for fresh strawberries. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the form and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Details** page with the required and optional information. 
2. On the **Customer** page, enter your contact information.
3. Choose the type of strawberries you would like to order from the options provided.
4. Specify the quantity of strawberries you need.
5. Select a delivery date and time that suits you.
6. Add any additional notes or messages you may have.
7. Finally, confirm your order by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details (Order Details)**: Enter your order preferences and details here.
* **customer_info (Customer)**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **customer_name (Customer Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **customer_email (Customer Email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **customer_phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **strawberry_type (Strawberry Type)**: Choose the type of strawberries you would like to order from the options provided.
* **quantity (Quantity)**: Specify the number of strawberries you need.
* **delivery_date (Delivery Date)**: Select a date for delivery that suits you.
* **delivery_time (Delivery Time)**: Select a time for delivery that suits you.
* **notes (Notes)**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **assigned_tool (Assigned Tool)**: Enter any specific equipment or tools you would like to be used for delivery.
* **customer_message (Customer Message)**: Enter any message or comment you would like to add.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us. 
* Our delivery team will be in touch with you after submitting the form to confirm your order details. 

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your order is processed smoothly. Thank you for using the Strawberry Order Form!
