# Restaurant Takeout Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information for their takeout orders at your restaurant. Please fill out the form to help us process your order efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out the order details**:
	* Provide a brief description of your order, including any special instructions or requests.
2. **Enter order summary**:
	* Summarize your order for our staff to review and confirm.
3. **Enter payment information**:
	* Provide your payment method and any relevant payment details (e.g., card number, expiration date).
4. **Enter customer information**:
	* Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
5. **Enter delivery address (if applicable)**:
	* Provide the address where your order will be delivered to if it's not for pickup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (Text field)**: This field is where you can describe your order, including any special instructions or requests. Please provide as much detail as possible so our staff can prepare your order correctly.
* **Order Summary (Text field)**: This field is a summary of your order for our staff to review and confirm. Please be brief but clear, including any important details about your order.
* **Payment Information (Text field)**: This field is where you can provide your payment method and any relevant payment details, such as your card number and expiration date. Please ensure this information is accurate to avoid any delays or issues with your payment.
* **Customer Information (Text field)**: This field is where you can enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details. Please ensure this information is correct to ensure we can contact you efficiently and accurately.
* **Delivery Address (Text field)**: If your order is to be delivered to a different address, please provide this information here, including the address where your order will be delivered to.
