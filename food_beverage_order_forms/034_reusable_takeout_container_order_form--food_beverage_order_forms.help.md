# Reusable Takeout Container Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reusable Takeout Container Order Form is designed to collect information for the purpose of placing orders for reusable takeout containers from customers. This form requires customers to provide details such as restaurant information, order preferences, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **restaurant** field with the name of the restaurant that will be picking up or delivering the order.
2.  Select the **pickup** option if you would like to pick up your order directly from the restaurant, or choose **Other** and fill in the **pickup_other** field with alternative pickup instructions.
3.  Select the **delivery** option to place an order for delivery, or if you would like to specify a different delivery method.
4.  Enter the date of the order using the **order_date** field.
5.  Enter the time of the order using the **order_time** field.
6.  Select the **container_type** you would like to order, including Small, Medium, or Large.
7.  Enter the quantity of containers you would like to order in the **container_number** field.
8.  Select your preferred **payment_method**, including Cash, Credit, or PayPal.
9.  Review the **subtotal** and **tax** fields for accuracy, as they will be calculated based on your order details.
10. Enter the total price of the order in the **total** field.
11. Enter any additional **order_comments** you would like to include.
12. Review all fields for accuracy before submission to ensure a smooth ordering process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **restaurant**: Enter the name of the restaurant that will be picking up or delivering your order.
*   **pickup**: Choose whether you would like to pick up your order directly from the restaurant or choose **Other** for alternative pickup instructions.
*   **pickup_other**: If you select **Other**, fill in the alternate pickup instructions in this field.
*   **delivery**: Select the delivery option or fill in a different delivery method if needed.
*   **delivery_time**: Enter the time you would like your order delivered.
*   **container_type**: Select the size of container you would like to order, including Small, Medium, or Large.
*   **container_number**: Enter the number of containers you would like to order.
*   **order_date**: Enter the date of the order.
*   **order_time**: Enter the time of the order.
*   **subtotal**: The system will automatically calculate this field based on your order details.
*   **tax**: Enter the tax amount for your order.
*   **total**: Enter the total price of your order.
*   **payment_method**: Select the preferred method of payment for your order, including Cash, Credit, or PayPal.
*   **order_notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions you would like to include with your order.
