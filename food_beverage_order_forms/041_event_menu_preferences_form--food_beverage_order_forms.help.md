## Purpose
The Event Menu Preferences Form is designed to capture event details and preferences from guests or attendees. This form helps event organizers or hosts gather information about food and drink preferences, dietary needs, allergies, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such in each field's label.
3. Select the options that apply to you from the select multiple and select one fields.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Additional Notes field.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
