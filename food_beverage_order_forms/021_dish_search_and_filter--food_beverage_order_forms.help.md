# dish_search_and_filter - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in searching and filtering for dishes that match their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, meat-lover, etc.)
* Choose your favorite ingredients (chicken, beef, fish, etc.)
* Select your preferred cooking method (grilling, baking, searing, etc.)
* Select your dietary type (keto, low-carb, gluten-free, etc.)
* Choose your recipe filters (savory, sweet, etc.)
* Select the type of meal you are looking for (breakfast, lunch, dinner, etc.)
* Type in the name of the dish
* List the ingredients used in the dish

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Preferences** (`search_preferences`): Select one or more options that describe your dietary preferences.
* **Favorite Ingredient 1** (`ingredient_1`): Choose one of the following options: Chicken, Beef, Fish.
* **Cooking Method** (`cooking_method`): Select one of the following options: Grilling, Baking, Searing.
* **Diet Type** (`diet_type`): Choose one of the following options: Keto, Low-Carb, Gluten-Free.
* **Recipe Filters 1** (`recipe_filters_1`): Select one or more options that describe your desired flavor profile (Savory, Sweet, Savory-Sweet).
* **Recipe Filters 2** (`recipe_filters_2`): Select one or more options that describe the preparation method (Quick-Service, Slow-Cooker, One-Pot).
* **Meal Type** (`meal_type`): Choose one of the following options: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.
* **Dish Name** (`dish_name`): Type in the name of the dish you are looking for.
* **Ingredients** (`ingredients`): List the ingredients used in the dish.
* **Serving Size** (`serving_size`): Select the number of people the dish serves (Serves 1-2, Serves 3-4, Serves 5+).
* **Cooking Time** (`cooking_time`): Choose one of the following options: Less than 15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes.
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`): Select one of the following options: Easy, Medium, Hard.
* **Cuisine** (`cuisine`): Type in the type of cuisine or dish you are looking for.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your search query.
