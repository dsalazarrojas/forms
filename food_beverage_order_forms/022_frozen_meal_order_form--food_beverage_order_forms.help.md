# Frozen Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frozen Meal Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for ordering frozen meals. This form includes selecting a meal type, specifying the quantity, and providing contact information for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meal you'd like to order from the "Select Meal" dropdown menu.
2. If needed, enter the quantity of the meal you'd like to order.
3. Fill in your name and other contact information in the respective fields.
4. Specify your preferred delivery date and time.
5. If you have any special instructions or notes for your order, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method and enter any additional payment details if necessary.
7. Review your order summary to ensure everything is accurate.
8. Submit your order to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
• **Customer Information**: Enter your name.
• **Select Meal**: Select the type of meal you'd like to order. Options include Chicken Fajita, Beef Fajita, Veggie Fajita, Beef Tacos, Chicken Tacos, and Veggie Tacos.
• **Quantity**: Enter the number of meals you'd like to order.
• **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number for delivery coordination.
• **Address**: Enter your address for delivery.
• **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or notes for your order.
### Contact Information
• **Name**: Enter your name as the recipient of the order.
• **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
• **Time**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
• **Delivery Time**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
• **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the order.
### Payment Information
• **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method: Bank Transfer, PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash.
• **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details if necessary.
• **Customer Note**: Enter any notes or comments for the payment.
### Order Information
• **Order ID**: Enter your order ID for reference.
• **Total Cost**: Review the total cost of your order.
• **Payment Status**: Review the status of your payment.
• **Delivery Status**: Review the status of your order delivery.
• **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the order.
• **Payment Method Details**: Enter any additional payment method details if necessary.
• **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
• **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for delivery coordination.
• **Address 2**: Enter your address for delivery.
• **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the order.

## Tips
• Be accurate when entering your contact information for delivery purposes.
• Choose the correct meal type and quantity to ensure timely delivery and accurate billing.
• Review your order summary carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
• If you have any special instructions, enter them clearly and concisely.
