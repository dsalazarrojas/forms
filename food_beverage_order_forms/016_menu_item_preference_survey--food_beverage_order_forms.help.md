# Menu Item Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Item Preference Survey is a set of questions designed to gather information about your dining habits and preferences. By completing this form, you'll help us understand what menu items and dining experiences you enjoy, and how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences and habits.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
