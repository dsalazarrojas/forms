# Meal Subscription Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Subscription Order Form is used to collect information from customers for meal subscriptions, including delivery dates, times, payment methods, and customer details. This form helps restaurants and cafes streamline the process of managing meal orders and subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Meal**: Choose the meal you would like to order from the options provided.
2. **Enter Delivery Date and Time**: Select the date and time you would like your meal to be delivered.
3. **Add a Note**: If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them in the "Note" field.
4. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
5. **Select Dietary Preferences**: Choose any dietary restrictions or preferences you have.
6. **Select Subscription Plan**: Choose the subscription plan that suits your needs.
7. **Confirm Meal Plan**: Confirm the meal plan you have selected.
8. **Select Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive your meal deliveries.
9. **Select Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you would like to use for your subscription.
10. **Enter Billing and Delivery Addresses**: Select whether you would like to use your billing or delivery address for your subscription.
11. **Enter Payment Method Note**: If necessary, provide additional information about your payment method.
12. **Enter Customer Details**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
13. **Confirm Subscription Status**: Confirm whether your subscription is active or inactive.
14. **Select Subscription Type**: Choose the type of subscription that suits your needs.
15. **Select Meal Type**: Choose the type of meal you would like to receive.
16. **Select Meal Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive meals.
17. **Total Meals**: Confirm the total number of meals you would like to receive.
