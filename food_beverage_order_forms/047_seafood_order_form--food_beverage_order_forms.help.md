# Seafood Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seafood Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for a seafood order. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements, ensuring we can process their orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred seafood option from the "Seafood Options" section.
2. Choose the type of seafood you would like to order (Fresh, Frozen, or Canned).
3. Indicate the quantity of seafood you would like to order.
4. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
5. Provide your name, email address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seafood Options** (Seafood Options, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following seafood options: Salmon, Shrimp, or Lobster.
* **Seafood Type** (Seafood Type, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of seafood you would like to order (Fresh, Frozen, or Canned).
* **Seafood Quantity** (Seafood Quantity, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of seafood you would like to order.
* **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, required: false): This field will display the total cost of your order, calculated based on your selections.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to have your order delivered or processed.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to have your order processed.
* **Name** (Name, text, required: false): Enter your name for contact and order purposes.
* **EMail** (EMail, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication and order updates.
