# Placemat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Placemat Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for placing orders for placemats. It is used to streamline the ordering process and ensure that all necessary information is collected correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer information fields, including name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select the order details, including the type of placemat desired and any special notes.
3. Choose the delivery time and date.
4. Select the payment terms and method.
5. Add any additional order comments or customer notes.
6. Review and submit the form to place your order.
