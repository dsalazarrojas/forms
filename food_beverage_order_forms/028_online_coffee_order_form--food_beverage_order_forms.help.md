# Online Coffee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Coffee Order Form is designed to collect information for an online coffee order. Fill this form to submit your order and ensure accurate processing.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your name**: Enter your name in this field to identify yourself.
2. **Enter email**: Provide a valid email address to communicate with our team.
3. **Enter phone**: Enter a valid phone number, including area code, to reach you if needed.
4. **Choose your preferred coffee**: Select between Coffee, Tea, or Juice.
5. **Add order notes (optional)**: If you'd like to provide extra details about your order, do so here.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (Text Field)**: Enter any relevant details about your order, such as quantity or special requests.
* **Name (Text Field)**: Provide your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email (Email Field)**: Enter a valid email address to ensure our team can contact you.
* **Phone (Text Field)**: Enter a valid phone number, including area code, to reach you if needed.
* **Preferred Coffee (Select One Field)**: Choose between Coffee, Tea, or Juice.
* **Order Notes (Note Field)**: Add any extra details about your order if you'd like.
* **Submit (Text Field)**: Click this button to submit your order.

Note: Ensure that all required fields, currently none, are filled out to submit a correct and accurate order.
