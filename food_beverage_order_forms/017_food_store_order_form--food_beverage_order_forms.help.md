# Food Store Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their orders at the food store.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Order Form" section with your order details.
2. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" section.
3. Specify your payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Order Notes" section.
5. Sign and date your order in the "Customer Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form (order_form)**: Fill out this section with the details of your order, including the items you want to purchase.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Add any additional information about your order, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Total (total)**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Order Status (order_status)**: Specify the status of your order, such as "pending" or "shipped".
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose how you plan to pay for your order, such as credit card or cash.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Add any additional payment details, such as your payment method's security code.
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Signature (customer_signature)**: Sign and date your order to confirm your information.
* **Order Number (order_number)**: Receive your order number for reference.
* **Payment Total (payment_total)**: Enter the total amount you paid for your order.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Enter the date and time you placed your order.
* **Payment Gateway (payment_gateway)**: Specify the payment gateway used for your payment.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Enter the time you placed your order.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by customers, and the fields are self-explanatory. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the food store staff.
