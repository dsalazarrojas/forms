# Organic Market Booth Setup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to configure and set up an organic market booth, including setup dates, payment details, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Setup Dates" section with the desired start and end dates of your booth setup.
2. Enter the payment details in the "Payment Details" section.
3. Select the "Booth Selections" option to choose whether you have a booth available or not.
4. In the "Booth Details" text field, provide any additional information about your booth, such as its location or unique features.
5. For the "Product Selections" dropdown, choose from the available options (Apple, Carrot, and Broccoli) to indicate which products you plan to offer at your booth.
6. In the "Payment Terms" text field, specify any payment terms or conditions for your products.
7. Add any additional notes about your booth setup in the "Notes" text area.
8. Finally, select your preferred contact information method (Phone or Email) in the "Contact Information" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Setup Dates** (1, date, required): Enter the start and end dates for your booth setup, including the date range when you plan to operate.
* **Payment Details** (2, number, required): Enter the payment details, including the price and any other relevant financial information.
* **Vendor Details** (3, text, required): Provide any additional details about your vendor, such as their name or business name.
* **Booth Selections** (4, select_one, required): Choose whether you have a booth available or not.
* **Booth Details** (5, text, required): Enter any additional information about your booth, such as its location or unique features.
* **Product Selections** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose from the available options (Apple, Carrot, and Broccoli) to indicate which products you plan to offer at your booth.
* **Payment Terms** (7, text, required): Specify any payment terms or conditions for your products.
* **Notes** (8, text, required): Add any additional notes about your booth setup.
* **Contact Information** (9, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact information method (Phone or Email).
