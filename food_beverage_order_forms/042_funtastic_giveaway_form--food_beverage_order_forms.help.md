# FunTastic Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FunTastic Giveaway Form is designed to collect necessary information for categorizing, searching, and reporting on giveaway events. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure proper processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Form Title** field to give your event a title, e.g., "FunTastic Giveaway Event 1".
2. Enter your **Name** to associate your event with your name for internal reference.
3. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, which will be used for follow-up communications.
5. Enter your **Address**, which will be used for mailing purposes.
6. Select a **Category** for your event from the drop-down menu (Food, Beverages, or Other).
7. Enter a brief **Description** of your event to provide context.
8. Select a **Date** and **Time** for your event, using the standard date and time formats.
9. Enter a **Form Phone** contact number in case we need to contact you about your event.
10. Enter an **Email** address for a secondary contact person (optional).
11. Use the **Note** field to add any additional comments or notes about your event.
12. If you have a second category, select it from the drop-down menu (Food, Beverages, or Other).
13. Add a second **Description** field for providing additional context or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (Text)**: Enter a descriptive title for your event, e.g., "FunTastic Giveaway Event 1".
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name as the event organizer or associated person.
* **Email (Text)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter a phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter the address for mailing purposes.
* **Category (Select One)**: Select a category for your event from the drop-down menu (Food, Beverages, or Other).
* **Description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Date (Date)**: Select a date for your event using the standard date format.
* **Time (Time)**: Select a time for your event using the standard time format.
* **Form Phone (Text)**: Enter a contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Form Email (Email)**: Enter a secondary contact email address.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your event.
* **Form Category 2 (Select One)**: If you have a second category, select it from the drop-down menu (Food, Beverages, or Other).
* **Form Description 2 (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your second category or event details.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure proper processing.
- Double-check that you are not duplicating any existing records.
- If unsure, contact the administrator for guidance.
- Add additional rows for multiple categories or descriptions.
