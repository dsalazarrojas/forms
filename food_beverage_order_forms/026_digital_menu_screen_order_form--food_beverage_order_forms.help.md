# Digital Menu Screen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Menu Screen Order Form is used to manage and customize digital menu screens for customers in a restaurant or food and beverage establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Digital Menu" page to input menu information.
2. On the "Select Options" page, choose any options that apply to the menu item (e.g. "None", "Other").
3. On the "Order Type" page, choose the type of order (e.g. "None", "Other").
4. On the "Pickup Time" and "Delivery Time" pages, input the desired time for pickup or delivery.
5. On the "Instructions" page, include any special instructions or comments for the order.
6. On the "Order Total" page, input the total cost of the order.
7. On the "Payment Method" page, choose the payment method (e.g. "None", "Other").
8. On the "Customer Comments" page, include any additional comments or feedback.
9. On the "Email" page, input the customer's email address.
10. On the "Phone" page, input the customer's phone number.
