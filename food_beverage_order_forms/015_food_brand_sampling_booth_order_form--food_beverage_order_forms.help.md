# food_brand_sampling_booth_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "food_brand_sampling_booth_order_form" is a user-facing form designed to collect information from potential customers regarding sampling requests for food and beverage products. The form is meant to be completed by individuals who wish to request sampling of food or beverage products from a food brand.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your booth details in the "Booth Details" field.
2. Provide your booth address in the "Booth Address" field.
3. Indicate if you have a booth by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Booth Type" field.
4. Enter the name of the food brand you are interested in sampling from in the "Vendor Name" field.
5. Specify the number of products you wish to sample in the "Sampling Requests" field.
6. Select the dates on which you would like to receive sampling from in the "Sampling Dates" field.
7. Choose specific times for sampling in the "Sampling Times" field.
8. Indicate if you would like to receive sampling on a regular basis in the "Sampling Frequency" field.
9. Provide any product-specific information in the "Product Info" field.
10. Enter any contact information in the "Contact Info" field.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booth Details** (`booth_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your booth.
* **Booth Address** (`booth_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your booth.
* **Booth Type** (`booth_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a booth, "No" otherwise.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the food brand you would like to sample from.
* **Sampling Requests** (`sampling_requests`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of products you would like to sample.
* **Sampling Dates** (`sampling_dates`, date, required/optional): Select the dates on which you would like to receive sampling.
* **Sampling Times** (`sampling_times`, time, required/optional): Choose specific times for sampling.
* **Sampling Frequency** (`sampling_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to receive sampling on a regular basis, "No" otherwise.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any product-specific information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information.


## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field to ensure that your sampling request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the brand's customer service team.
