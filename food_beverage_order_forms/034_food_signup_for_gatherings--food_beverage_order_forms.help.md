# Food Signup For Gatherings - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Signup For Gatherings form is used to collect information about food items that will be brought to a potluck or event. This form helps hosts and organizers plan and manage the food offerings for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field explanation below for guidance on how to complete each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Potluck Name
**potluck_name** (Required: false)
This is the name of the potluck or event. Enter the name of the potluck or event in this field.

### Host Name
**host_name** (Required: true)
Enter the name of the host or organizer of the potluck or event.

### Dish Name
**dish_name** (Required: true)
Enter the name of the dish you are bringing to the potluck or event.

### Serving Size
**serving_size** (Required: false)
If you're bringing multiple items, please indicate how many people your dish is intended to serve.

### Serving Information
**serving_info** (Required: false)
Provide additional information about your dish, such as its ingredients, serving size, or any dietary restrictions.

### Dietary Restrictions
**dietary_restrictions** (Required: false)
Select from the list of options if your dish has any dietary restrictions, such as gluten-free or vegan.

### Email
**email** (Required: true)
Enter a contact email or phone number so the host or organizer can get in touch with you.

### Phone
**phone** (Required: false)
Optional contact phone number.

### Notes
**notes** (Required: false)
Add any additional notes or comments about your dish or contribution.

### Dish Type
**dish_type** (Required: false)
Select from the list of options if you're bringing a specific type of dish, such as veggie or meat.

### Ingredients
**ingredients** (Required: true)
Enter a list of ingredients used in your dish, including any allergens or common allergens.

### Allergy
**allergy** (Required: false)
Enter any allergens or potential allergens present in your dish.

### Allergy Information
**allergy_info** (Required: false)
Provide additional information about allergens or potential allergens in your dish.

### Food Group
**food_group** (Required: true)
Select from the list of options if your dish belongs to a specific food group, such as fruits or grains.

### Category
**category** (Required: false)
Select from the list of options if your dish fits into a specific category, such as desserts or main course.

### Serving Type
**serving_type** (Required: true)
Select from the list of options if your dish is intended for individual or family consumption.

### Number of People
**num_people** (Required: true)
Enter the number of people your dish is intended to serve.

### Food Type
**food_type** (Required: true)
Select from the list of options if your dish is a specific type, such as salad or savory.

### Serving Location
**serving_location** (Required: true)
Select from the list of options if your dish will be served at a specific location, such as home or office.

### Serving Time
**serving_time** (Required: false)
If relevant, enter the time your dish will be served, using the format: 3:00 PM, 6:00 PM, etc.

### Serving Date
**serving_date** (Required: false)
If relevant, enter the date your dish will be served.

### Serving Frequency
**serving_frequency** (Required: false)
If your dish is regularly served, select from the list of options, such as weekly or monthly.

### Serving Time Frequency
**serving_time_frequency** (Required: false)
If your dish is regularly served at a specific time, select from the list of options, such as daily or weekly.

### Serving Location Frequency
**serving_location_frequency** (Required: true)
Select from the list of options if your dish is regularly served at a specific location, such as daily or weekly.

Note: The fields marked as "Required: true" are necessary to complete the form. Please fill those fields before submitting the form.
