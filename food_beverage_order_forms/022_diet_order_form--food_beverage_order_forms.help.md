# Diet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for ordering diet-related food and beverages. It is designed to gather information from users to process their orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User Input field with your name and other relevant details.
2. Select your preferred food from the Food Selection dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred beverage from the Beverage Selection dropdown menu (you can select multiple options).
4. Enter the date and time you would like to pick up your order.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
6. Enter your contact email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text, required): This is the main input field where you can enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Food Selection** (select_one, optional): Select the food you would like to order from the available options.
* **Beverage Selection** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available beverages to accompany your order.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you about your order.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can contact you about your order.
