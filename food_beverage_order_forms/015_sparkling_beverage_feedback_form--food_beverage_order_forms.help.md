# Sparkling Beverage Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sparkling Beverage Feedback Form is a tool for customers to share their thoughts and opinions on our sparkling beverage products. This form is designed to gather feedback on the taste, quality, and overall experience of our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2.  On the first page, you will be asked about your overall experience with our sparkling beverage. Please answer this question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3.  On the second page, you will be asked to rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4.  The third page is where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions about our sparkling beverage products.
5.  On the last two pages, you will be asked to rate how likely you are to recommend our sparkling beverage to friends and family, on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Please rate your overall experience with our sparkling beverage product. This will help us understand your satisfaction level.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_text`, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about our sparkling beverage products. This is your chance to tell us what we can improve on.
* **Recommended to Friends** (`recommended_to_friends`, number, required: false): Rate how likely you are to recommend our sparkling beverage to your friends. This will help us understand the product's social impact.
* **Recommended to Family** (`recommended_to_family`, number, required: false): Rate how likely you are to recommend our sparkling beverage to your family. This will help us understand the product's family-friendly value.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact our customer support team.
* We appreciate your feedback and value your opinion. Your input will help us improve our products and services.
