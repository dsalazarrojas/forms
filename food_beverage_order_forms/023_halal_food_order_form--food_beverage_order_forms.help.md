# Halal Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process their halal food orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure a successful order submission.
2. Enter your name in the "Orderer Name" field.
3. Select your food preference in the "Halal Food Preference" field.
4. Enter the date and time you would like to pick up your order in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Orderer Name** (`orderer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Halal Food Preference** (`food_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Halal Food" if you prefer halal food, "Non-Halal Food" if you don't, or "Other" for any other preference.
* **Date** (`order_date`, text, optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
