# Restaurant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place their orders for pickup or delivery. Please fill in the required information to ensure efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Select your pickup location or fill in your delivery address in the "Pickup Location" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose the date and time of your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
5. If you're paying online, select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
6. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Note" field.
7. Review all fields for accuracy before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where you'd like to pick up your order. If you're having your order delivered, you can enter your address here.
* **Delivery Address**: If you're having your order delivered, enter your delivery address here.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for updates or confirmations.
* **Order Date**: Choose the date you'd like to place your order.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time you'd like to place your order.
* **Total Cost**: This field is not required, but it's used for internal tracking purposes.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you'd like to use for your order. If you're paying online, select one of the available options.
* **Order Note**: Add any additional information about your order here. This can include special requests or instructions for the chef.

## Tips

* Make sure to review all fields for accuracy before submitting your order.
* If you're having trouble with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you're paying online, ensure that you have the necessary payment information ready to avoid delays in processing your order.
