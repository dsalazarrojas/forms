# Food Vendor License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for food vendors who are applying for a food vendor license. It is used to collect information about the vendor's business, including their name, address, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
3. Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business name as you would like it to appear on the license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Date** (`business_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your business was established.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your business license number.
* **Business License Type** (`business_license_type`, text, required/optional): Select the type of business license you are applying for.
* **Business License Status** (`business_license_status`, text, required/optional): Select the status of your business license.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of application you are submitting (License, Renewal, Transfer).
* **License Category** (`license_category`, text, required/optional): Enter the category of your license.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your store.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your store.
* **Store Phone Number** (`store_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your store's phone number.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, required/optional): Enter your store's operating hours.
* **Store Date** (`store_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your store was established.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Relationship** (`manager_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe the manager's relationship to the business.
* **Manager Phone Number** (`manager_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Hours** (`manager_hours`, time, required/optional): Enter the manager's operating hours.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your employee.
* **Employee Relationship** (`employee_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe the employee's relationship to the business.
* **Employee Phone Number** (`employee_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Hours** (`employee_hours`, time, required/optional): Enter the employee's operating hours.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your business.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Review and submit the form for review and processing.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a complete and accurate application.
