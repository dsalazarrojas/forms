# Milkshake Flavor Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their flavor preferences for milkshakes. It helps us understand which flavors are most popular among our customers and what they think about our current offerings. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and let us know about your preferences!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Customer Name** in the field below. This is the name of the person taking the survey.
2. Enter your **Email** in the field below. This is to ensure we can contact you if needed.
3. Select your **Picked Flavor** from the list. Choose the flavor of milkshake you like best.
4. If you'd like, you can select your **Preferred Flavor** from the list of options. This will help us know what flavors we should offer more.
5. Enter a score between 1-10 in the **Satisfaction Score** field to rate your satisfaction with our current offering.
6. If you'd like to add anything more about your satisfaction, you can provide a short **Satisfaction Comment** below.
7. Choose how interested you are in our **New Offerings** from the list. Select all that apply.
8. If you'd like to provide more information about your interest in new offerings, you can leave a **New Offering Comment** below.
9. Select your favorite **Favorite Flavor** from the list.
10. If you'd like to add anything more about your favorite flavor, you can leave a **Favorite Flavor Comment** below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person taking the survey.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the survey taker.
* **Picked Flavor** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the flavor of milkshake you like best.
* **Preferred Flavor** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the flavors you would like us to offer more of.
* **Satisfaction Score** (number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with our current offering (1-10).
* **Satisfaction Comment** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments about your satisfaction.
* **New Offerings** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all areas you are interested in exploring for new offerings.
* **New Offering Comment** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments about new offerings.
* **Favorite Flavor** (select_one, required/optional): Choose your favorite flavor from the list.
* **Favorite Flavor Comment** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments about your favorite flavor.
