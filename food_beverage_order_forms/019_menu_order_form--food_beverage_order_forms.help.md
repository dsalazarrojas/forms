# Menu Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Order Form is designed to collect information for a food and beverage order, making it easier for customers to place their orders and for the business to process payments and communicate with them.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  For fields with multiple options, select the relevant choice from the dropdown menu.
3.  Use the "text" fields to enter your order details and personal contact information.
4.  For payment information, select the payment method and enter the total amount.

* **form_data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **order_details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter more detailed information about your order.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment information.
* **menu_options** (`menu_options`, select_one, optional): Select the options you'd like to order.
* **payment_total** (`payment_total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your order.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your order.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **payment** (`payment`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
