# Thanksgiving Pie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Pie Order Form" is used to collect information from customers to process their pie order for Thanksgiving. Please complete the form carefully to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field. This is the person who will be receiving the pie.
2. Select the number of pies you would like to order in the "Pie Number" field.
3. Enter the phone number of the person who will be receiving the pie in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the email address of the person who will be receiving the pie in the "Email" field.
5. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Note" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Select One Pie" to indicate if you would like to order one pie.
7. Select multiple pies if you would like to order more than one pie.
8. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Select One Cust Service" to indicate if you would like to order cust service.
9. Select multiple cust services if you would like to order more than one cust service.
10. Enter the date and time you would like to pick up your pie in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
11. Repeat steps 6-10 for "Select One Cust Service", "Select Multiple Pies", "Select One Cust Service2", "Select Multiple Pies2", "Select One Cust Service3", "Select One Pie Service", "Select Multiple Pies3" and "Select Multiple Cust Service" fields.
12. Enter any additional phone numbers for the person who will be receiving the pie in the "Phone" and "Phone2" fields.
