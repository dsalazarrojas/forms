# Food Presentation Plate Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the customer's preferences for the presentation of the food on the plate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the presentation preferences (color, shape, and size) that you would like for your order.
3. Enter the quantities of the order in the "Order Quantities" field.
4. Select the specific items to be ordered from the "Order Items" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Use this field to enter any additional information about your order.
* **Presentation Preferences** (`presentation_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options to select the presentation preferences for your order (Color, Shape, Size).
* **Order Quantities** (`order_quantities`, text, optional): Enter the quantities of the items you would like to order.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific items to be ordered from the dropdown menu (Food 1, Food 2, Food 3).
