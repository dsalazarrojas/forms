# Catering Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any issues or complaints about our catering services. Please fill out this form to provide us with essential details about your complaint, and we will do our best to resolve the issue as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information (Your Name, Email Address, and Phone Number) to help us get in touch with you.
2. Provide details about the event, including the Event Date, Event Name, and Event Location.
3. Choose the Complaint Category that best describes the issue you faced.
4. Describe the problem you experienced in the Issue Description field.
5. Rate the severity of the issue using the Severity Rating options.
6. Specify the Desired Resolution you would like to see for this issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`complainant_name`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your company's name.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, required): Enter the date of the complaint.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, optional): If you have an order confirmation, please enter it.
* **Catering Provider** (`catering_provider`, required): Name of the caterer providing the service.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, required): Choose the type of issue you faced (Food Quality, Food Temperature, Portion Size, Service Issues, etc.).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, required): Describe the problem you experienced.
* **When Did the Issue Occur** (`when_occurred`, optional): If known, provide the time or phase when the issue occurred.
* **Staff Members Involved** (`staff_involved`, optional): If applicable, enter the names of the staff members involved.
* **Severity Rating** (`severity_rating`, required): Rate the severity of the issue (Minor, Moderate, Serious, Very Serious, Critical).
* **Impact on Event** (`impact_on_event`, optional): Describe how the issue affected your event.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, optional): If there were any witnesses, enter their names.
* **Evidence Available** (`evidence_available`, optional): If you have any evidence (photos, videos, receipts), please attach them or provide a link.
* **Have You Complained Before** (`previous_complaints`, optional): If you have complained to this provider before, select "True".
* **Desired Resolution** (`resolution_requested`, required): Choose the desired resolution (Refund, Partial Refund, Credit for Future, Apology, etc.).
* **Resolution Details** (`resolution_details`, optional): If you have any additional comments, please explain.
* **Expected Response Timeframe** (`response_timeframe`, optional): If you need a response within a specific timeframe, select it.
* **Willing to Discuss by Phone** (`willing_to_discuss`, required): Select "True" if you are willing to discuss this issue by phone.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any other information, please attach it.
