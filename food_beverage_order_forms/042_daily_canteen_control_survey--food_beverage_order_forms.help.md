# Daily Canteen Control Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and data from customers about their experience with the daily canteen.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked with <font color="red">required</font>.
2. For any special requests or comments, use the "Special Requests" or "Additional Comments" fields.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required): 
    Enter a brief description of your overall experience with the canteen.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, select_multiple, required): 
    Select all the types of food that you would like to see offered in the canteen, e.g. "Yes" for yes, or "No" for no.
* **Menu Preferences** (`menu_preferences`, select_one, required): 
    Select one of the options that best describes the type of menu you would prefer.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): 
    Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): 
    Enter the time of your visit.
* **Canteen Manager** (`canteen_manager`, select_one, required): 
    Select the canteen manager who handled your order.
* **Canteen Email** (`canteen_email`, email, required): 
    Enter the email address of the canteen manager who handled your order.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): 
    Enter your contact phone number so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): 
    Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): 
    Enter your contact phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): 
    Enter any comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): 
    Enter your customer name.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, required): 
    Enter the order ID provided to you.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): 
    Select the type of order you placed, e.g. "Breakfast", "Lunch", "Dinner", or "Dessert".
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required): 
    Enter any special requests you may have had during your visit.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required): 
    Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, required): 
    Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): 
    Select whether you are satisfied or not with your experience.
* **Reason for Satisfaction or Dissatisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction_reason`, text, required): 
    Enter the reason why you selected your level of satisfaction.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): 
    Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Customer Survey Response** (`customer_survey_response`, text, required): 
    Enter your response to the survey.
* **Staff Survey Response** (`staff_survey_response`, text, required): 
    Enter your response to the staff survey.
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, text, required): 
    Enter any final comments or feedback you may have.

Please note that all fields marked with <font color="red">required</font> are mandatory and must be filled out in order for your submission to be complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
