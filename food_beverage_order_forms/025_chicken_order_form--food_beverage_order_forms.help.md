# Chicken Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chicken Order Form is designed to collect information for a poultry order. It is intended for use by customers or their representatives to place an order for fresh poultry products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields. These fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For optional fields, you can choose whether or not to fill them out.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for fields with multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business or restaurant name** (\*\*): Enter the name of the business or restaurant placing the order.
* **Contact person name** (\*\*): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the order.
* **Email address** (\*\*): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone number** (\*\*): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Desired delivery date** (\*\*): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Type of chicken product** (\*\*): Select the type of chicken product you are ordering (e.g. Whole chicken, chicken breasts, etc.).
* **Meat quality grade** (\*\*): Select the quality grade of the meat (e.g. Grade A, Grade B, etc.).
* **Preparation style** (\*\*): Select how you would like the chicken to be prepared (e.g. fresh, frozen, etc.).
* **Quantity** (\*\*): Enter the quantity of chicken you would like to order.
* **Unit of measurement** (\*\*): Select the unit of measurement for the quantity (e.g. pounds, kilograms, etc.).
* **Price per unit** (\*\*): Enter the price per unit of the chicken.
* **Delivery address** (\*\*): Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
* **City** (\*\*): Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
* **State or province** (\*\*): Enter the state or province where you would like to receive your order.
* **Postal code** (\*\*): Enter the postal code where you would like to receive your order.
* **Preferred delivery time window** (\*\*): Select a time window when you would like to receive your order (e.g. early morning, morning, etc.).
* **Is this a recurring order?** (\*\*): Select if this is a one-time or recurring order.
* **Additional order notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **I agree to the delivery and payment terms**: Accept or decline the delivery and payment terms.
* **Special preparation requests** (optional): Enter any special requests for your order.
* **Special handling or storage requirements** (optional): Enter any special handling or storage requirements for your order.
* **Recurring order type** (optional): Enter the type of recurring order you are placing (e.g. weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
