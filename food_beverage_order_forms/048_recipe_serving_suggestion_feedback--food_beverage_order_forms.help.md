# Recipe Serving Suggestion Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on serving and plating suggestions for a recipe. Your input will help us improve the presentation and preparation of our dishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the recipe in the "Recipe Serving Suggestion Feedback" field.
2. Select the category of food or beverage you would like to provide feedback on.
3. Share your idea on how you like to serve this dish in the "How do you like to serve this dish?" field.
4. Share your idea on how you like to plate this dish in the "How do you like to plate this dish?" field.
5. If you have any comments or feedback, you can type them in the "Any comments or feedback?" field.
6. If you have a preferred email address or phone number you'd like to be contacted at, please enter them in the corresponding fields.
7. If you have any other contact information, please enter it in the "Other contact info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipe Serving Suggestion Feedback** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the recipe to identify it for our staff.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of food or beverage you would like to provide feedback on (e.g. Apple, Banana, Orange, Pear, Other).
* **How do you like to serve this dish?** (`serving_idea`, text, required/optional): Share your idea on how you like to serve this dish.
* **How do you like to plate this dish?** (`plating_idea`, text, required/optional): Share your idea on how you like to plate this dish.
* **Any comments or feedback?** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Type any comments or feedback you have about serving and plating suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to be contacted at for follow-up questions or further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted at for further discussion.
* **Other contact info** (`other_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any other contact information you'd like to provide.
