# Ingredient Safety Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help businesses provide accurate and up-to-date information about their ingredients, certifications, and safety status. It is crucial for maintaining public health and safety standards and complying with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Review and double-check your submission for any errors or inconsistencies.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information (Page 1/1)** (`<name>`, `text`, required): This section is for providing basic company details.
* **Business Name (Page 1/1)** (`business_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your business name.
* **Ingredient Name (Page 1/1)** (`ingredient_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the ingredient you are renewing.
* **Safety Data (Page 1/1)** (`safety_data`, `note`, optional): If you have any safety data or notes related to the ingredient, provide them here.
* **Safety Renewal Date (Page 1/1)** (`safety_renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are seeking to renew the safety status of the ingredient.
* **Certification Attachment (Page 1/1)** (`certification_attachment`, `text`, optional): If you have any certification attachments or documents related to the ingredient, provide them here.
* **Certification Status (Page 1/1)** (`certification_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current certification status of the ingredient from the options provided.
* **Certification Date (Page 1/1)** (`certification_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a certification date related to the ingredient, provide it here.
* **Certification Expiration (Page 1/1)** (`certification_expiration`, `date`, optional): If you have a certification expiration date related to the ingredient, provide it here.
* **Submission Date (Page 1/1)** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting the ingredient safety renewal application.
* **Submission Status (Page 1/1)** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current submission status of the ingredient from the options provided.
* **Certification Attachment 2 (Page 1/1)** (`certification_attachment_2`, `text`, optional): If you have any further certification attachments or documents related to the ingredient, provide them here.
* **Review Note (Page 1/1)** (`review_note`, `note`, optional): If you have any notes or comments related to the ingredient, provide them here.
* **Renewal Note (Page 1/1)** (`renewal_note`, `note`, optional): If you have any renewal notes or comments related to the ingredient, provide them here.
* **Business Address (Page 2/1)** (`business_address`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your business address.
* **Business City (Page 2/1)** (`business_city`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your city.
* **Business State (Page 2/1)** (`business_state`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your state or province.
* **Business ZIP (Page 2/1)** (`business_zip`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your ZIP code.
* **Business Phone (Page 2/1)** (`business_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email (Page 2/1)** (`business_email`, `email`, optional): If applicable, provide your business email.
* **Certification Number (Page 2/1)** (`certification_number`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide your certification number.
* **Certification Status 2 (Page 2/1)** (`certification_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current certification status from the options provided.
* **Certification Status 3 (Page 2/1)** (`certification_status_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current certification status from the options provided.
* **Certification Status 4 (Page 2/1)** (`certification_status_4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current certification status from the options provided.
* **Certification Status 5 (Page 2/1)** (`certification_status_5`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current certification status from the options provided.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and double-check your submission for errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or customer support.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid errors in the submission process.
