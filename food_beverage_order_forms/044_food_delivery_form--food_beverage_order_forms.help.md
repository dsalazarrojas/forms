# Food Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place food and beverage orders for delivery or pickup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the delivery date and time fields with the date and time you would like your order to be delivered.
2. Enter your pickup address if you would like to pick up your order.
3. Select your preferred payment method and enter the corresponding payment information if required.
4. Enter any special instructions or notes if you have any specific requests.
5. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
6. Select the type of delivery you would like (pickup, delivery, or both).
7. Finally, select the status of your payment and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Pickup Address** (`pickup_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to pick up your order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, required: false): Enter any special instructions or notes you have.
* **Extra Instructions** (`special_instructions_2`, `note`, required: false): Enter any extra special instructions or notes you have.
* **More Instructions** (`special_instructions_3`, `note`, required: false): Enter any more special instructions or notes you have.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your credit or debit card number.
* **Card Expiration Date** (`card_expiration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expiration date of your credit or debit card.
* **Security Code** (`card_security_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the security code of your credit or debit card.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or comments you have.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your delivery.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of delivery you would like (pickup, delivery, or both).
* **Pickup Address** (`pickup_address_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to pick up your order.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of your payment.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any special instructions or notes, please enter them in the special instructions or notes field.
