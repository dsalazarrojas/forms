# restaurant_takeaway_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their delivery or takeout order information to the restaurant. It helps the restaurant to understand the customer's order requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your delivery address, order time, customer name, order comments, and any other relevant information.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
4. Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
5. Choose a date and time for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Address**: The delivery address where your order should be delivered.
* **Order Time**: The time you would like to receive your order.
* **Customer Name**: Your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Comments**: Any additional comments or requests you have for your order.
* **Payment Method**: You can choose to pay with Cash, Card, or Mobile Money.
* **Order Details**: Any additional notes about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Order Date**: The date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Time (again)**: You can choose a different time for your order if the first option does not work for you.
* **Order Notes**: Any further comments or notes about your order.
* **Restaurant Name**: The name of the restaurant you are placing an order with.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.
