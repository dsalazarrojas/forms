# Girl Scout Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for placing orders for Girl Scout cookies. You will select the type of cookie you would like to order, choose the size, delivery date and time, and payment method. This form is for end-users who are not part of the Girl Scout organization but are interested in purchasing cookies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of cookie you want to order from the options provided.
2. Choose the size of the cookie you want to order.
3. Enter the delivery date and time you would like to receive your cookies.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
5. Enter the quantity of cookies you would like to order.
6. Enter your name and contact information.
7. Choose how you heard about the Girl Scout cookie sale.
8. Select your payment method.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about your second order (if applicable).
9. Enter the total cost of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cookie Order** (`Oatmeal Raisin`, `Peanut Butter`, `Chocolate Chip`, `Shortbread`, `Trefoil`, `Lemon`, `<type>: select_one>`): Select the type of cookie you would like to order. This is the main item you are purchasing.
* **Cookie Size** (`Single`, `Double`, `Family`, `Half`, `<type>: select_multiple>`): Choose the size of the cookie you would like to order. This will affect the price and quantity you receive.
* **Delivery Date** (`<type>: date>`): Enter the date you would like to receive your cookie order.
* **Delivery Time** (`<type>: time>`): Enter the time you would like to receive your cookie order.
* **Note** (`<type>: note>`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order. This is optional and can be used to specify any special instructions or requests.
* **Quantity** (`<type>: number>`): Enter the number of cookies you would like to order.
* **Name** (`<type>: text>`): Enter your name so we can contact you about your order.
* **Email** (`<type>: email>`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Contact Info** (`<type>: text>`): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Delivery Date 2** (`<type>: date>`): Enter the date you would like to receive your second order of cookies.
* **Delivery Time 2** (`<type>: time>`): Enter the time you would like to receive your second order of cookies.
* **Payment Method** (`Cash`, `Credit`, `PayPal`, `<type>: select_one>`): Select your payment method. We accept cash, credit, and PayPal.
* **First Order** (`True`, `False`, `<type>: select_one>`): Enter whether this is your first order.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`Social Media`, `Friend`, `Word of Mouth`, `Flyer`, `<type>: select_one>`): Enter how you heard about the Girl Scout cookie sale.
* **Payment Method 2** (`Cash`, `Credit`, `PayPal`, `<type>: select_one>`): Select your second payment method.
* **Note 2** (`<type>: note>`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your second order.
* **Total** (`<type>: text>`): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method 3** (`Cash`, `Credit`, `PayPal`, `<type>: select_one>`): Select your third payment method.
