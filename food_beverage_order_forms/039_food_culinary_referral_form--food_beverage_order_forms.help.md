# Food Culinary Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about food referrals or complaints in a food and beverage establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name and email address (if you have one).
2. Select the type of food product or service you are referring (e.g., "Type 1", "Type 2", or "Type 3").
3. Enter the date of the incident (if relevant).
4. Provide any additional information about the food referral or complaint.
5. Enter your follow-up date (if you have one).
