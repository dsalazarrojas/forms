# Restaurant Safety Certification Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Safety Certification Order Form is designed to help restaurants demonstrate their commitment to providing a safe dining environment for their customers and staff. By completing this form, restaurants can showcase their safety practices and procedures, which can help establish trust with customers and improve their overall reputation. This form is typically used by restaurants to certify that they meet the required safety standards, such as proper food handling, cleaning, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your restaurant, including the name and address of your business.
2. Provide contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your payment information to process the certification order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Safety Certification** (`restaurant_safety_certification`):
	+ This field is where you will enter the name of your restaurant's safety certification or compliance program. This could include certifications like Food Safety Certification, Health and Safety Certification, or other relevant safety programs.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
	+ This field is where you will enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This is how we will get in touch with you to process your certification order.
* **Payment** (`payment`):
	+ This field is where you will enter your payment information, such as credit card details or other payment methods we accept. Please ensure that your payment information is complete and accurate to avoid any issues with processing your order.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Double-check your payment information to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
