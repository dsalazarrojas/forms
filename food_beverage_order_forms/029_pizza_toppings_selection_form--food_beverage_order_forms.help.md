# Pizza Toppings Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by customers to select their desired toppings for a pizza order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Topping 1" section, which will allow you to choose multiple toppings for your pizza.
2. In the "Select Toppings" section, choose multiple toppings from the list of options provided.
3. In the "Select Toppings 2" section, choose a single topping from the list of options.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes for your order, please enter them in the "Comments" section.
5. Finally, enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or questions about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Topping 1** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): This section allows you to choose whether or not you want to include a topping on your pizza. Select "Yes" if you want to include the topping, and "No" if you don't.
* **Select Toppings** (`Yes`, `No`, required/optional): This section allows you to choose multiple toppings for your pizza from the list of options provided. Select as many or as few toppings as you like.
* **Select Toppings 2** (`Cheese`, `Pepperoni`, `Mushroom`, `Onions`, `Bacon`, required/optional): This section allows you to choose a single topping for your pizza from the list of options provided.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): This section is where you can enter any additional comments or notes for your order, such as special requests or dietary restrictions.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): This is where you enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or questions about your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the desired toppings for your pizza in the "Select Toppings" section.
* If you have any special requests or comments, be sure to include them in the "Comments" section.
* Enter your correct email address in the "Email" section so that we can contact you with any updates or questions about your order.
