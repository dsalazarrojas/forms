# Cafe Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cafe Order Form is a simple online order form for customers to place orders for food and beverages from a cafe.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Select "Order Type" from the options provided (Pickup - ASAP, Pickup - Scheduled, Dine-in, or Local Delivery).
3. Enter your "Contact Phone" and "Customer Name" to ensure order updates can reach you.
4. Choose any desired "Beverage" options, including "Milk Preference" and "Sweetness/Syrup Add-ons."
5. Select any "Non-Coffee Beverages" you'd like.
6. Choose your "Food and Snacks" from the available options.
7. If applicable, provide "Special Instructions" for your order.
8. Review the "Estimated Total" for your order.
9. Select your preferred "Payment Method" (Pay Online, Pay in Store, or Apple/Google Pay).
10. Confirm your "Email for Order Confirmation" to receive updates on your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Your Name
* **Your Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 3. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about your order.

### 4. Order Type
* **Order Type** (Select One, Required): Choose from options:
	+ Pickup - ASAP
	+ Pickup - Scheduled
	+ Dine-in
	+ Local Delivery

### 5. Milk Preference
* **Milk Preference** (Select One, Required): Choose from options:
	+ Whole Milk
	+ Skim Milk
	+ Oat Milk
	+ Almond Milk
	+ Soy Milk
	+ No Milk

### 6. Sweetness Level
* **Sweetness Level** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from options:
	+ None
	+ Vanilla
	+ Caramel
	+ Hazelnut
	+ Sugar (1 pack)
	+ Sugar (2 packs)
	+ Honey

### 7. Non-Coffee Drinks
* **Non-Coffee Drinks** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from options:
	+ English Breakfast Tea
	+ Green Tea
	+ Chai Latte
	+ Hot Chocolate
	+ Fresh Juice
	+ Smoothies

### 8. Food and Snacks
* **Food and Snacks** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from options:
	+ Avocado Toast
	+ Egg and Bacon Roll
	+ Granola Bowl
	+ Croissant
	+ Muffin

### 9. Special Instructions
* **Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Provide any special requests or dietary requirements for your order.

### 10. Estimated Total
* **Estimated Total** (Number, Required): Review the total cost of your order before proceeding.

### 11. Payment Method Preference
* **Payment Method Preference** (Select One, Required): Choose from options:
	+ Pay Online (Card)
	+ Pay in Store
	+ Apple/Google Pay

### 12. Loyalty Number
* **Loyalty Number** (Text, Optional): If you're a loyalty program member, enter your member number.

### 13. Email for Order Confirmation
* **Email for Order Confirmation** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates on your order.

## Tips
- Review your order before proceeding to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact cafe staff for assistance.
- Please allow 15-30 minutes for order fulfillment, depending on the order type.
