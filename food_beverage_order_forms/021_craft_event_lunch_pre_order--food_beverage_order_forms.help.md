# Craft Event Lunch Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Craft Event Lunch Pre-Order form is a tool for events staff to collect lunch pre-order requirements from attendees. This form helps streamline the process of ordering and delivering food to each attendee's liking.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Fill out Event Details**: Enter the event name, date, and any other relevant details about the event.
2. **Enter Contact Details**: Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you if needed.
3. **Choose your Lunch**: Select one or more lunch options that suit your taste.
4. **Indicate Your Food Needs**: Select your dietary preference: Vegetarian, Vegan, or Omnivore.
5. **Enter Payment Details**: If you have any special payment instructions, enter them here.
6. **Submit the Form**: Choose "Submit" to finalize your order, or "Reset" to start over.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Page 1)**: Enter the name of the event and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (Page 1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Lunch Choices (Page 2)**: Select one or more lunch options that you'd like to order.
  + **Option 1**: Choose this option if you like...
  + **Option 2**: Choose this option if you like...
  + **Option 3**: Choose this option if you like...
* **Food Needs (Page 2)**: Select your dietary preference.
  + **Vegetarian**: You do not eat meat.
  + **Vegan**: You do not eat meat or any products derived from animals.
  + **Omnivore**: You eat a little bit of everything.
* **Payment Details (Page 3)**: Enter any special payment instructions, if applicable.
* **Submit Form (Page 4)**: Select "Submit" to finalize your order, or "Reset" to start over.
