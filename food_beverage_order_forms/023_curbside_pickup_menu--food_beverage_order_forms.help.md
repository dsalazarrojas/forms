# Curbside Pickup Menu - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curbside Pickup Menu form is designed for customers to place curbside pickup orders from our restaurant. This form is meant for customers who prefer to pick up their orders instead of having it delivered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your meal type by selecting one or more options from the "Meal Type" dropdown menu. You can select Grilled, Baked, or Fried.
2. Enter your name in the field labeled "Name."
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone Number."
4. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email."
5. Enter your address in the field labeled "Address."
6. Select your desired pickup time from the "Pickup Time" field. You can choose from a variety of time slots that suit your schedule.
7. Set your order time by selecting a date from the "Order Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meal Type** (`meal_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more meal types that you'd like to order. You can select Grilled, Baked, or Fried.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name so we know who is picking up the order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about your order.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address so we can ensure accurate delivery of your order.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, optional): Select a convenient time for pickup that suits your schedule.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `date`, optional): Set the date for your order pickup.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select all required fields correctly to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please include them in the "Special Requests" field, which is not currently implemented but will be available in the future.
