# Custom Basket Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Basket Creation Survey is a form for creating tailored gift baskets that take into account the individual preferences and needs of the gift recipient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the gift recipient's preferences and needs.
2. Provide details about the gift basket, including the number of items and any specific items you'd like to include.
3. Decide on the gift card details, such as the message and amount.
4. Choose the shipping method and date for the gift basket.
5. Select a payment method for the gift basket.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to know about your gift recipient?** (`title`, `text`, optional): This field is meant to capture any additional information about the gift recipient that you'd like to know, such as their interests or preferences.
* **Gift recipient details** (`gift_recipient_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the gift recipient's details from the list, such as if they are male/female, age, etc.
* **Basket details** (`basket_details`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of items you'd like to include in the gift basket.
* **Gift item details** (`gift_item_details`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you'd like to include any specific gift items, such as flowers or chocolates.
* **Gift card details** (`gift_card_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the gift card details, such as the amount and message.
* **Gift card message** (`gift_card_message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message to include with the gift card.
* **Shipping information** (`shipping_info`, `date`, optional): Enter the shipping date and time.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the shipping method, such as express or standard.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping address of the gift recipient.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Tips**
* Make sure to answer honestly and thoroughly to create a thoughtful gift basket.
* If you're unsure about any question, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form will help us create a custom gift basket that takes into account your preferences and needs.
