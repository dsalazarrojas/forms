# Festival Food Order Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Food Order Template is used to collect food and beverage orders from festival attendees. This form is essential for processing and delivery of orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Food field, where you can enter the type of food or beverage you are interested in ordering.
2. Select the Food Type from the dropdown menu to specify the type of food or beverage you would like to order.
3. Enter your Customer Name in the text field, if you would like to be associated with this order.
4. Enter the Food Item you would like to order in the text field.
5. Specify the Quantity of the food or beverage you would like to order.
6. Add any additional Order Notes if required.
7. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
8. Enter your Phone number for any further communication or delivery updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food** (`festival_food_order_template_1`, text, optional): Enter the type of food or beverage you would like to order.
- **Food Type** (`food_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of food or beverage you would like to order. For example, select Option 1 or Option 2 from the dropdown menu.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so that we can associate this order with you.
- **Food Item** (`food_item`, text, optional): Enter the specific food item you would like to order.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of the food or beverage you would like to order.
- **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions about your order.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication or delivery updates.
