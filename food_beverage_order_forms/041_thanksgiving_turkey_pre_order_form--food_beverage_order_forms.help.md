# Thanksgiving Turkey Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Turkey Pre Order Form is designed to allow customers to pre-order their Thanksgiving turkeys. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and select your preferences for the turkey order. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of fields. Please make sure to review each page carefully to ensure you have filled in all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for our records.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your address so we can deliver your turkey order.
### Page 2
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to pick up or receive your order.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Please select the number of guests you will be hosting this Thanksgiving.
### Page 3
* **Turkey Size** (`turkey_size`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size of turkey you would like to order (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Turkey Type** (`turkey_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of turkey you would like (Whole, Boneless, Organic, or Kosher).
### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or questions, please enter them here.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_multiple, true): Please confirm that this is your order by selecting 'True'.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Phone and Confirmation) for a successful order.
* Selecting the correct turkey size and type will ensure we can fulfill your order correctly.
* If you have any special requests, please enter them in the Additional Information field.
* Review your order carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
