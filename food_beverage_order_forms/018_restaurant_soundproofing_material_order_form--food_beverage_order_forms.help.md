# restaurant_soundproofing_material_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help restaurants and food establishments order soundproofing materials to enhance their dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
4. Enter your address in the "address" field.
5. Specify the quantity of soundproofing materials you wish to order.
6. Enter the unit price of the materials (optional).
7. Calculate the total price of your order by multiplying the unit price with the specified quantity.
8. Select the type of soundproofing material you wish to order from the options provided.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about your order.
10. Click the "submit" button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name for order tracking purposes.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and order confirmation.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address for delivery or pickup purposes.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the number of soundproofing materials you wish to order.
* **unit_price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the soundproofing materials.
* **total_price** (`total_price`, number, required): Calculate the total price by multiplying the unit price with the quantity.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your order.
* **soundproofing_materials** (`soundproofing_materials`, select_one, required): Select from Acoustic Panels, Soundproofing Material 2, Soundproofing Material 3, or Soundproofing Material 4.
* **additional_notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the seller.
