# Staff Use Mobile Food Delivery Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Staff Use Mobile Food Delivery Intake Form" is designed to capture essential details about customer orders received through mobile devices, allowing staff to process orders efficiently and communicate effectively with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by entering the order date and time** in the "Order Date" field. This is an optional field but is crucial for tracking order timelines and ensuring timely delivery.
2. **Enter the staff ID** to identify yourself as the staff member processing the order. This is an optional field but helps in maintaining order records.
3. **Provide the customer's name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is an essential piece of information for addressing customers and ensuring order accuracy.
4. **Enter the customer's phone number** in the "Customer Phone" field. This is an optional field but helps in case of any order follow-ups or communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_date (Order Date)**: Enter the date and time of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **staff_id (Staff ID)**: Enter your staff ID number.
* **customer_name (Customer Name)**: Enter the customer's full name as they are known to you.
* **customer_phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number for any follow-ups or communication.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information to complete the form correctly.
* If you have any doubts or issues while filling out the form, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
