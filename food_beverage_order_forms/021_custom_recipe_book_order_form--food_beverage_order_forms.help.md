# Custom Recipe Book Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture order information for custom recipe books. The goal is to collect details such as customer contact information, order specifics (category, quantity, price), and additional notes to ensure accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the category of the recipe book order (e.g., Cooking, Baking, Food, Beverage).
7. Indicate the quantity of the recipe book ordered.
8. Enter the price of the order.
9. Add any additional notes about the order in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the date of the order.
11. Choose the time of the day for the order.
12. If applicable, enter the Form ID and Category ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the recipe book order (e.g., Cooking, Baking, Food, Beverage).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the recipe book ordered.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the order.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of day for the order.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the Form ID.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the assigned tool or category ID.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* Review your order carefully before submission to ensure that the information is accurate.
