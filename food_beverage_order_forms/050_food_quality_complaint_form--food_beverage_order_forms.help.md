# Food Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Quality Complaint Form is a tool used to report any quality issues with food products ordered from our establishment. This form helps us identify and address potential problems with our food quality, ensuring that we provide the best possible service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This field is required.
2.  Enter a description of the issue you experienced with your order in the "Complaint Details" field. This field is required.
3.  If you have a contact email address, please enter it in the "Contact Email" field. This is an optional field.
4.  If you have a contact phone number, please enter it in the "Contact Phone" field. This is an optional field.
5.  Enter the **Order ID** (a unique identifier for your order) in the "Order ID" field. This field is required.
6.  Enter the name of the **Product Name** that was the subject of the complaint in the "Product Name" field. This field is required.
7.  Enter the date when you experienced the **Complaint Date** in the "Complaint Date" field. This field is optional.
8.  Describe the specific **Product Issue** you experienced in the "Product Issue" field. This field is required.
9.  Enter a detailed description of your **Complaint Message** in the "Complaint Message" field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order receipt or ID.
*   **Complaint Details**: Describe the quality issue you experienced with your order, including any relevant details such as the date, time, and location.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you would like us to contact you about your complaint.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you about your complaint.
*   **Order ID**: The unique identifier for your order, usually found on your order receipt.
*   **Product Name**: The name of the product that had the quality issue.
*   **Complaint Date**: The date when you experienced the quality issue. If you're not sure, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Product Issue**: Describe the specific quality issue you experienced with the product.
*   **Complaint Message**: Enter a detailed description of your quality issue, including any relevant details that can help us identify the problem.

## Tips
*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Complaint Details" and "Complaint Message" fields so that we can best understand the issue you experienced.
*   If you have any supporting documentation or evidence (such as a photo of the product or a receipt), please attach it to your complaint form.
*   We appreciate your feedback and will do our best to resolve the issue as soon as possible.
