# Frozen Ingredient Replenishment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you manage the frozen ingredient replenishment process for your business. It allows you to submit orders for frozen ingredients, providing essential information for efficient inventory management and logistics.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter product name**: In the "Product Name" field, type the name of the frozen ingredient you need to replenish.
2. **Enter product ID**: In the "Product ID" field, enter the unique identifier for the product.
3. **Select ingredient type**: In the "Ingredient Type" field, select "Frozen" to indicate that the ingredient is frozen.
4. **Enter quantity**: In the "Quantity" field, enter the amount of the ingredient you need.
5. **Select reorder frequency**: In the "Reorder Frequency" field, choose how often you want to reorder the product (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
6. **Set reorder date and time**: In the "Reorder Date" and "Reorder Time" fields, enter the specific date and time you want to reorder the product.
7. **Add notes**: In the "Notes" field, you can enter any additional comments or instructions for the reorder.
8. **Enter contact information**: In the "Email", "Phone", "Address", "City", "State", "Zip Code", and "Country" fields, enter your contact information.
9. **Submit order**: In the "Submit" field, confirm that you are ready to submit your order.
10. **Confirm order**: In the "Confirm" field, confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the order details.
11. **Enter comments**: In the "Comments" field, you can add any additional comments or questions about your order.
12. **Assign order**: In the "Assigned To" and "Assigned Tool" fields, enter the person or team responsible for fulfilling your order.
13. **Date and time stamps**: The "Created Date", "Updated By", and "Updated Date" fields are automatically populated with system-generated information.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the frozen ingredient you need to replenish.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the product.
* **Ingredient Type** (`ingredient_type`, select_one, required): Select "Frozen" to indicate that the ingredient is frozen.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the amount of the ingredient you need.
* **Reorder Frequency** (`reorder_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you want to reorder the product (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Reorder Date** (`reorder_date`, date, required): Enter the specific date you want to reorder the product.
* **Reorder Time** (`reorder_time`, time, required): Enter the specific time you want to reorder the product.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the reorder.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your location.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your location.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country of location.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are ready to submit your order.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the order details.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about your order.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the person or team responsible for fulfilling your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or resource used to fulfill your order.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, required): Automatically populated with the date the order was created.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Automatically populated with the person who created the order.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required): Automatically populated with the person who last updated the order.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, required): Automatically populated with the date the order was last updated.
* **Version** (`version`, text, optional): Enter any version information about your order.

Please review this form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required for your frozen ingredient replenishment order.
