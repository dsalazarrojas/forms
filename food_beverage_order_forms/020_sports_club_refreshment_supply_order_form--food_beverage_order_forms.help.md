# Sports Club Refreshment Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Refreshment Supply Order Form is used to collect information for a refreshment supply order from a sports club. This form will help us gather the necessary details for a successful order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the type of food or beverage item you would like to order (food_item).
2. Select the quantity of the item you need.
3. If applicable, enter the price of the item.
4. Choose the date for your order (order_date).
5. Provide your contact information, including your name (contact_name), email address (contact_email), and phone number (contact_phone).
6. If you have any special requests or comments, type them in the order_comment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Item** (`food_item`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of food or beverage item you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of items you need to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the item, if known.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Provide your name so we can contact you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Order Comment** (`order_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or special requests for your order.
