# Healthy Meal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to select their meal preferences, enter their contact details, and confirm their order.

## How To Complete This Form

### Select Your Meal
* **Select Your Meal** (`meal_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the meal options that appeal to you. You can select multiple options if you'd like.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your meal.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your meal.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or requests you have for your meal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Meal**: Please select all the meal options that appeal to you. You can select multiple options if you'd like.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your meal. Please use the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive your meal. Please use the format HH:MM.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or requests you have for your meal. This is a text field where you can type in as much or as little information as you need.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order. Please make sure to enter your email address correctly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your order. Please make sure to enter your phone number correctly.
* **Select One**: Select a specific option from the provided choices. Please choose the option that best suits your needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate ordering.
* You can select multiple meal options if you'd like, but please be aware that this may affect the overall cost of your order.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy to ensure you receive important updates about your order.
* If you have any special requests or notes, please add them in the notes field so that we can accommodate your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
