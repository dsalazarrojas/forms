# health_declaration_form_amp_menu_59 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on a patient's health declarations. This includes any health-related statements or statements about one's health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply enter the required information in the corresponding fields. This includes:
- **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`, text): Enter any health-related statements or declarations you'd like to make.
- **Food and Beverage Order** (`food_and_beverage_order`, text): Enter your food and beverage order, if applicable.
- **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, text): List any food allergies you have, if applicable.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text): Enter any medical conditions you're experiencing, if applicable.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text): Enter any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
- **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one): Select your current health status. Choose between "Active" and "Inactive."
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date): Enter the date of your visit.
