# Quarantine Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarantine Meal Order Form is designed to collect information for meal orders during a quarantine period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Choose your preferred meal and food type.
4. Indicate your order quantity.
5. Add any additional comments or instructions.
6. Select your preferred delivery method and address (if applicable).
7. Choose the staff member responsible for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Meal Request Date** (`meal_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to receive your meal.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Food Request** (`food_request`, text, required): Describe your meal request in detail.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any special requests or instructions for your meal order.
* **Select Meal** (`select_meal`, select_one, required): Choose a meal from the available options (Apple, Burger, Pizza, Salad, Sandwich, Soup).
* **Select Food Type** (`select_food_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of food you would like to receive (Food, Non-Food).
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required): Indicate the quantity of your order.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required): The date the order was received.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, required): The time the order was received.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of where you will be receiving your meal (if not delivering or picking up).
* **Select Delivery Method** (`select_delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your meal (In-person, Delivery, Pickup).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like to receive your meal (if delivering).
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or details about your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of your order (Completed, In Progress, Waiting, Cancelled).
* **Select Staff** (`select_staff`, select_one, optional): Choose the staff member responsible for your order.
* **Order Notes 2** (`order_notes_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Comments 2** (`order_comments_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Select Service** (`select_service`, select_one, optional): Choose the service required for your order.
* **Select Service 2** (`select_service_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the service required for your order.

Note: The form is designed for users to enter information accurately and completely. Please review the form carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or need help, contact the relevant staff member.
