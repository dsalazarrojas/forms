# Chicken Biryani Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chicken Biryani Order Form is used to place an order for chicken biryani from our food establishment. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient order processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your personal and order details.
2.  Choose your preferred delivery or pickup method.
3.  Select your desired biryani type, spice level, and portion size.
4.  Indicate any dietary requirements and special instructions.
5.  Choose any additional side dishes and toppings you'd like.
6.  Add any drinks or desserts to your order.
7.  Select your payment method and promo code (if applicable).
8.  Finally, let us know if you're a returning customer and how you found us.

*   **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name to help us identify you correctly.
*   **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Order Date** (4): Select the date you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery or Pickup** (5): Choose how you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Delivery Address** (6): Enter your full delivery address if you've chosen delivery.
*   **Pickup Time** (7): Select the time you'd like to pick up your order if you've chosen pickup.
*   **Delivery Time** (8): Select the time you'd like to receive your delivery.
*   **Biryani Type** (9): Select the type of biryani you'd like to order.
*   **Spice Level** (10): Choose the spice level you'd like in your biryani.
*   **Portion Size** (11): Select the portion size you'd like for your order.

*   **Beverages** (19): Choose any drinks you'd like to add to your order.
