# grab_and_go_meals_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grab and go meals reservation form is designed to collect information from customers for their meal reservations. This form will help you provide an efficient and personalized service for your customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your reservation details in the first field.
2. In the second field, describe the meal(s) you would like to order.
3. Enter your payment details in the third field.
4. Confirm your reservation by typing "yes" in the fourth field.
5. If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reservation_details** (required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your reservation, such as date and time you would like to pick up your meal.
* **meal_details** (required): Enter the meal(s) you would like to order in this field. Please describe the meal(s) in detail so that we can prepare it correctly.
* **payment_details** (required): This field is used to collect your payment method and any other payment-related information.
* **confirm_reservation** (required): Type "yes" in this field to confirm your reservation.
* **contact_details** (optional): If you have any contact information you would like to share, such as a phone number or email, please enter it here.
* **special_requests** (optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, please enter them in this field.
* **pickup_details** (optional): If you have any specific pickup instructions, such as a preferred pickup location, please enter them in this field.
* **comments** (optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in this field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure a smooth reservation experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
* We strive to provide an efficient service. Your feedback and comments are appreciated and help us improve our service.
