# Gourmet Food Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Gourmet Food Order Form is designed for users to place orders for gourmet food products. This form helps users to select options for their order, including ingredients, recipes, sauces, dressings, garnishes, vegetables, and meats. Additionally, users can provide contact information and select a date and time for their order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by selecting the options for your order.
2. On the main page, select the ingredients, recipe, sauce, dressing, garnish, vegetable, and meat options that you would like to order.
3. On the second page, select the date and time for your order.
4. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the note field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
6. Finally, click the submit button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Ingredients**: Select the ingredients you would like to include in your order.
* **Select Recipe**: Select the recipe you would like to order.
* **Select Sauce**: Select the sauce you would like to accompany your order.
* **Select Dressing**: Select the dressing you would like to have with your order.
* **Select Garnish**: Select the garnish you would like to include with your order.
* **Select Vegetable**: Select the vegetables you would like to include in your order.
* **Select Meat**: Select the meats you would like to order.
* **Date Field**: Select the date you would like to place your order on.
* **Time Field**: Select the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Note Field**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* **Email Field**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone Field**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your order.
