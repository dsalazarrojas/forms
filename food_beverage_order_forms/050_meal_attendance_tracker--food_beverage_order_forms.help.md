# Meal Attendance Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Attendance Tracker form is used to track meal attendance for events, gathering essential information about guest count, dietary needs, and special requirements to help event organizers plan and prepare effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a brief title for the event in the "Meal Attendance Tracker" field.
2. Provide a description of the event in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the expected number of guests in the "Guest Count" field.
4. Select the number of people with specific needs, such as food allergies or special requirements, in the "Guest Needs" field.
5. Choose the type of meal to be served in the "Meal Type" field.
6. Specify any food allergies or special needs for guests in the "Food Allergies" field.
7. List any special needs for guests in the "Special Needs" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the event in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meal Attendance Tracker**: Enter a brief title for the event, such as "Lunch Meeting" or "Dinner Party".
* **Description**: Provide a short description of the event, including any notable details or context.
* **Guest Count**: Enter the expected number of guests attending the event.
* **Guest Needs**: Select all applicable options for guests with specific needs, such as food allergies or special requirements.
* **Event Details**: Choose the type of event, like "Lunch" or "Dinner".
* **Meal Type**: Select the type of meal to be served, such as "Breakfast", "Lunch", "Dinner", etc.
* **Food Allergies**: Select all applicable options for guests with food allergies or special dietary requirements.
* **Special Needs**: List any special needs or accommodations required for guests, such as wheelchair accessibility or baby-changing facilities.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event, such as seating arrangements or timing.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information about the event to help organizers prepare effectively.
* Double-check the guest count and needs to ensure that you don't miss any important details.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to catch any errors or inconsistencies.
