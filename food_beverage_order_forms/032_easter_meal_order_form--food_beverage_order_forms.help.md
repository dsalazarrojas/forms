# easter_meal_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers order Easter-themed meals for delivery. It gathers information about the customer, their meal preferences, and delivery details to ensure that the meal is delivered correctly and on time. By completing this form, you will be able to specify your meal choice, delivery date and time, and any special instructions or requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
2. Select your preferred Easter-themed meal choice from the options provided.
3. Choose your desired delivery date and time.
4. Provide any additional comments or instructions for the delivery team.
5. Sign the form to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: This field is where you will enter your name, address, and other contact information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To gather customer information for delivery purposes.
* **Choose an Easter Meal**: This field allows you to select one of three Easter-themed meal options.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: To determine which meal to deliver to you.
* **Date Night**: This field is where you will select the date for delivery.
	+ Type: date
	+ Purpose: To schedule the delivery of your meal.
* **Time Night**: This field is where you will select the time for delivery.
	+ Type: time
	+ Purpose: To schedule the delivery of your meal.
* **Special Requests**: This field is where you can enter any special requests or instructions for the delivery.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any specific requirements for the delivery.
* **Easter Theme**: This field is where you will select the Easter theme for the meal.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: To determine the theme of the meal.
* **Email**: This field is where you will enter your email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Purpose: To communicate with you regarding your order.
* **Phone**: This field is where you will enter your phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To contact you in case of any issues with your order.
* **Order Details**: This field is where you can enter any additional details about your order.
	+ Type: note
	+ Purpose: To gather any additional information about your order.
* **Order Comments**: This field is where you can enter any additional comments about your order.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any specific requirements for the delivery.
* **Other Comments**: This field is where you can enter any other comments or instructions.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any additional information about your order.
* **Customer Signature**: This field is where you will sign to confirm your order.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To confirm that you have placed the order.
* **Delivery Date**: This field is where you will select the date for delivery.
	+ Type: date
	+ Purpose: To schedule the delivery of your meal.
* **Delivery Time**: This field is where you will select the time for delivery.
	+ Type: time
	+ Purpose: To schedule the delivery of your meal.
* **Delivery Address**: This field is where you will enter your delivery address.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To ensure that the delivery is made to the correct address.
* **Delivery Instructions**: This field is where you can enter any additional instructions for the delivery.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any specific requirements for the delivery.
* **Delivery Notes**: This field is where you can enter any additional notes about the delivery.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any additional information about the delivery.
* **Delivery Status**: This field is where you will select the status of your delivery.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: To confirm the status of your order.
* **Urgent Delivery**: This field is where you can select any time-sensitive delivery options.

	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: To communicate any additional information about the delivery.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly to ensure a smooth delivery.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you have any urgent delivery requirements, please select the corresponding options in the "Urgent Delivery" field.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in the "Order Comments" or "Other Comments" fields.
