## nut_and_dry_goods_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about nut and dry goods products. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Customer Information** (Page 1) in the designated text field.
2.  Enter the **Product Information** (Page 2) about the product you are providing feedback on.
3.  Share your **Feedback** (Page 3) about your experience with the product.
4.  Enter a valid **Email** (Page 4) address to contact you for follow-up.
5.  Provide your **Phone** number (Page 5) for further contact if needed.
6.  Select your **Rating** (Page 6) for the product from the provided options.
7.  Add any additional **Comments** (Page 7) about your experience.
8.  Click on the **Submit** (Page 8) button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Page 1): Please enter your name and any other relevant customer information in this field.
* **Product Information** (Page 2): Describe the product that you are providing feedback on, including the product name and any other relevant details.
* **Feedback** (Page 3): Share your honest and detailed feedback about your experience with the product, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Email** (Page 4): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (Page 5): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Select Rating** (Page 6): Choose one of the provided ratings to rate your experience with the product (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (Page 7): Add any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Submit** (Page 8): Click this button to send your feedback and complete the form.
