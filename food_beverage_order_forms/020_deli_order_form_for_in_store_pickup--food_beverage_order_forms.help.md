# Deli Order Form For In Store Pickup - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Deli Order Form for In-Store Pickup is designed to help customers place an order for sandwiches, salads, and beverages without having to physically interact with a sales representative. This form provides a convenient and streamlined way to order in-store for customers who want to pick up their orders quickly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select the options for each category (Sandwiches, Salads, and Beverages) to create your order.
* Make sure to fill out the Pickup Details field with your pickup time and any special instructions.
* Click the "Submit" button to submit your order for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sandwiches** (`main_page_section_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple sandwiches to add to your order. Options include:
	+ Turkey Sandwich
	+ Ham Sandwich
	+ Chicken Sandwich
* **Salads** (`main_page_section_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple salads to add to your order. Options include:
	+ Garden Salad
	+ Caesar Salad
* **Beverages** (`main_page_section_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple beverages to add to your order. Options include:
	+ Water
	+ Soda
* **Pickup Details** (`main_page_section_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date and time of your pickup in the format "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM".
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required: false): Click this button to submit your order for processing. This will not affect your order in any way. It's a button to confirm that you're ready to proceed.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment information that may be needed for your order, such as your phone number or any special instructions for payment.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `note`, required: false): This button will be used by the system to process your order, not by the customer. It's not a field that needs to be filled out.
