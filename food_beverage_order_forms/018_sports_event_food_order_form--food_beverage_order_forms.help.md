# Sports Event Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Event Food Order Form is used to capture details about food and beverage orders for sports events. It includes information such as event date, total cost, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for game day foods, beverage orders, and other options as they apply to your event.
2. Enter any special instructions or notes for the event in the 'Special Instructions' field.
3. Enter the total cost for the event in the 'Total Cost' field.
4. Enter the date of the event in the 'Event Date' field.
5. Select the payment method for the event in the 'Payment Method' field.
6. Enter the status of the order in the 'Order Status' field.
7. Enter event information in the 'Event Info' field.
8. Provide your contact phone number in the 'Contact Phone' field.
9. Enter your contact email address in the 'Contact Email' field.
10. Enter the name of the event in the 'Event Name' field.
11. Enter the name of the event organizer in the 'Event Organizer' field.
12. Enter any additional special instructions for the event in the 'Special Instructions 2' field.
13. Enter any notes for the event in the 'Notes' field.
14. Enter the start time of the event in the 'Event Start Time' field.
15. Enter the end time of the event in the 'Event End Time' field.
16. Enter the food item in the 'Food Item' field.
17. Enter the total number of items in the 'Total Item' field.
18. Enter the quantity of food and drinks in the 'Quantity' field.
19. Enter the price of the food and drinks in the 'Price' field.
20. Calculate the subtotal for the event in the 'Subtotal' field.
21. Enter the tax for the event in the 'Tax' field.
22. Calculate the grand total for the event in the 'Grand Total' field.
