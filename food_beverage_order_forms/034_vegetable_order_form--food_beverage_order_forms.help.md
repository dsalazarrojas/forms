# Vegetable Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers about their vegetable order, including their name, order specifications, delivery address, and desired delivery time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Specify the type and quantity of vegetables you want to order in the "Order Details" field.
3. Provide your delivery address in the "Delivery Info" field.
4. Select your desired delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info (Customer Info)
* **Customer Info** (Text, Required/Optional: False): This field is where you can input your name.
### Order Details (Order Details)
* **Order Details** (Text, Required/Optional: False): This field is where you can specify the type and quantity of vegetables you want to order. For example, you can input "Carrots (3)" or "Spinach (2)".
### Delivery Info (Delivery Info)
* **Delivery Info** (Text, Required/Optional: False): This field is where you can provide your delivery address. Please make sure to include your house number and street name.
### Delivery Time (Delivery Time)
* **Delivery Time** (Time, Required/Optional: False): This field is where you can select your desired delivery time. Please choose a specific time of day for your delivery.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the "Order Details" field.
- For any issues with your order, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
