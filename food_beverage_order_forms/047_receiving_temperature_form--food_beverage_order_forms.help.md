# Receiving Temperature Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture temperature readings when items or materials are received in the warehouse. It allows users to record the receiving date, time, temperature readings, and other relevant information to ensure accurate tracking of temperature-sensitive items.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant date for receipt of the item(s).
2. Enter the time when the item(s) were received in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
3. Record the first temperature reading (Temperature Reading 1) using a numeric value.
4. Record the second temperature reading (Temperature Reading 2) using a numeric value.
5. Enter the receiving temperature, which is the actual temperature of the item(s) when they were received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Receiving Date** (`receiving_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the item(s) were received.
* **Receiving Time** (`receiving_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the item(s) were received in 24-hour format.
* **Temperature Reading 1** (`temperature_reading1`, number, required/optional): Record the first temperature reading using a numeric value.
* **Temperature Reading 2** (`temperature_reading2`, number, required/optional): Record the second temperature reading using a numeric value.
* **Receiving Temperature** (`receiving_temperature`, number, required/optional): Enter the actual temperature of the item(s) when they were received.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are correctly filled in to provide accurate tracking and record-keeping.
* If there are multiple items being received, ensure all relevant temperature readings and receiving information are captured.
* If an item is temperature-sensitive, ensure it is properly labeled and stored accordingly to prevent temperature-related damage or deterioration.
