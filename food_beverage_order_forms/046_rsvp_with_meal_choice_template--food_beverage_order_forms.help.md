# rsvp with meal choice template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP and meal preferences for guests attending an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** in the respective fields.
2. Indicate your **RSVP** status for the event.
3. Choose your **Meal** preference from the select options.
4. Enter your **Name** and **Email** for yourself and potentially up to three other guests.
5. Enter the **Phone** number for yourself and potentially up to three other guests.
6. Provide any additional **Comments** about your attendance or the event.
7. Choose the **Assigned Tool** for yourself and potentially up to three other guests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **RSVP**: Indicate your attendance status for the event by selecting a number.
* **Select Meal**: Choose your meal preference for the event.
* **Guest 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first guest.
* **Guest 1 Meal**: Choose the meal preference for the first guest.
* **Guest 1 Email**: Enter the email address of the first guest.
* **Guest 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the first guest.
* **Guest 1 Comments**: Provide any comments or details about the first guest.
* **Guest 2 Name**: Enter the name of the second guest.
* **Guest 2 Meal**: Choose the meal preference for the second guest.
* **Guest 2 Email**: Enter the email address of the second guest.
* **Guest 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the second guest.
* **Guest 2 Comments**: Provide any comments or details about the second guest.
* **Guest 3 Name**: Enter the name of the third guest.
* **Guest 3 Meal**: Choose the meal preference for the third guest.
* **Guest 3 Email**: Enter the email address of the third guest.
* **Guest 3 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the third guest.
* **Guest 3 Comments**: Provide any comments or details about the third guest.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or details about your attendance or the event.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to you or the guests.
* **Assigned Tool ID**: Enter the ID of the tool assigned to you or the guests.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guests.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the guests.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or details about the guests.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each guest.
* Make sure to choose a meal preference for each guest.
* Enter a valid phone number and email address for each guest.
* Provide any additional comments or details that may be helpful for the event staff.
* Choose the correct tool assigned to you or the guests.
* Enter the correct tool ID assigned to you or the guests.
