# Restaurant Food Photos - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from restaurants and food establishments about their photos of food and menu items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the most popular menu items offered by your restaurant (most_popular_menu_items)
2. Choose the status of your menu photos (menu_item_status)
3. Decide if you have menu photos (food_photos)
4. Indicate the date and time you took the photos of your menu items (photo_upload_date and photo_upload_time)
5. Specify the location where you took the photos of your menu items (photo_upload_location)
6. Provide feedback about your restaurant (restaurant_feedback)
7. Choose whether you take photos of your most popular menu items (take_photo_of_food)
8. Select the name of your restaurant (restaurant_name)
9. Input the address of your restaurant (restaurant_address)
10. Choose whether you take photos of your menu at a specific time (take_photo_of_food_at_time)
11. Upload a photo of your menu items (upload_photo)
12. Indicate whether the photo is active or inactive (menu_photo_status)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Most Popular Menu Items** (`most_popular_menu_items`): Select the most popular menu items offered by your restaurant.
* **Menu Item Status** (`menu_item_status`): Choose the status of your menu photos. This field is required but does not affect the other fields.
* **Food Photos** (`food_photos`): Decide if you have menu photos. This field is required.
* **Photo Upload Date** (`photo_upload_date`): Input the date when you took the photos of your menu items.
* **Photo Upload Time** (`photo_upload_time`): Input the time when you took the photos of your menu items.
* **Menu Photo Status** (`menu_photo_status`): Indicate whether the photo is active or inactive.
* **Take Photo Of Food** (`take_photo_of_food`): Choose whether you take photos of your most popular menu items. This field is required.
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`): Input the name of your restaurant. This field is required.
* **Restaurant Address** (`restaurant_address`): Input the address of your restaurant. This field is not required.
* **Photo Taken At** (`photo_taken_at`): Input the date and time when you took the photos.
* **Photo Taken By** (`photo_taken_by`): Choose whether you took the photo of your menu items. This field is not required.
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`): Upload a photo of your menu items. This field is not required.
* **Restaurant Feedback** (`restaurant_feedback`): Provide feedback about your restaurant.
* **Menu Photo Taken At Time** (`take_photo_of_food_at_time`): Choose whether you take photos of your menu at a specific time. This field is not required.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Input your restaurant's phone number. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`email`): Input your restaurant's email. This field is not required.
* **Restaurant ID** (`restaurant_id`): Input your restaurant's ID. This field is not required.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`): Input your order ID. This field is not required.
* **Take Photo Of Menu** (`take_photo_of_menu`): Choose whether you take photos of your menu. This field is not required.
* **Restaurant Photo Taken At** (`restaurant_photo_taken_at`): Input the date when you took the photos of your restaurant's menu.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Input your restaurant's phone number. This field is not required.
* **Email** (`email`): Input your restaurant's email. This field is not required.
* **Restaurant ID** (`restaurant_id`): Input your restaurant's ID. This field is not required.

Tips:
* Make sure to input accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that you're completing the form for the correct restaurant.
* Follow the required fields and input information accordingly.
* You can skip the fields that are not required.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification.
