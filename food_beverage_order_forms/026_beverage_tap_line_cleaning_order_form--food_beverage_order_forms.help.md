# Beverage Tap Line Cleaning Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an order for cleaning of beverage tap lines. It provides information required to perform a proper and efficient cleaning, including details about the establishment, tap lines, and desired cleaning schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the requestor information section with your name, title, email, and phone number. This helps our team contact you with any questions or updates regarding the cleaning order.
2. Provide information about the establishment, including the name, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Indicate the type of tap line system and the approximate length of the tap line.
4. Choose your desired cleaning frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, etc.) and indicate when it was last professionally cleaned.
5. Describe any current issues with the tap lines, such as pressure or flow problems, taste issues, or contamination risks.
6. Indicate if you have a service contract in place and if you are unsure of the cleaning schedule.
7. Provide your preferred date and time for the cleaning service and how long you are willing to accept the tap line to be out of service.
8. If you have any unique cleaning needs or requirements, please provide details in the additional information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Requestor**: The full name of the person requesting the cleaning service.
* **Position or Title**: Your role or title at the establishment.
* **Email Address**: The email address where you can be contacted regarding the cleaning order.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Request**: The date when you are submitting this form.
* **Establishment Name**: The name of the establishment (bar, restaurant, or venue).
* **Establishment Address**: The full street address of the establishment.
* **City**: The city where the establishment is located.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where the establishment is located.
* **Postal Code**: The zip code of the establishment's location.
* **Type of Tap Line System**: The type of tap line system you have (e.g., draft beer only, draft beer and wine, etc.).
* **Number of Taps or Lines**: The total number of tap lines to be cleaned.
* **Approximate Line Length**: The approximate length of the tap line in feet.
* **Date of Last Professional Cleaning**: When the tap line was last professionally cleaned.
* **Desired Cleaning Frequency**: How often you would like the tap line to be cleaned (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Current Issues with Tap Lines**: Describe any problems you are experiencing with the tap lines, such as pressure or flow issues, taste issues, or contamination risks.
* **Are There Pressure or Flow Issues**: Indicate if you are experiencing significant or minor pressure or flow issues.
* **Any Off-Taste or Flavor Issues**: Indicate if you are experiencing any unusual taste or flavor issues.
* **Risk of Contamination or Growth**: Indicate if you are concerned about contamination or buildup on the tap lines.
* **Do You Have a Service Contract**: Indicate if you have an active service contract in place.
* **Current Service Provider**: If you have a service contract, provide the name of the current provider.
* **Preferred Cleaning Date**: The date you would prefer for the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Time for Service**: The time you would prefer for the cleaning service (e.g., before opening, after closing, etc.).
* **Acceptable Downtime**: The maximum amount of time you are willing to accept the tap line to be out of service.
* **Special Cleaning Needs or Requirements**: If you have any unique cleaning requirements or needs, describe them here.
* **Access Instructions or Building Notes**: If there are any specific instructions or building notes that will aid our technicians in accessing the tap system, please provide them here.
