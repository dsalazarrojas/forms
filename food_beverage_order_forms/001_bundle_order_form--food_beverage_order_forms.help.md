# Bundle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bundle Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for bundles of food and beverage items. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your personal information: enter your full name, contact email for order confirmations, and contact phone number (including the country code if outside of your local area).
2. Select your preferred delivery date from the available options.
3. Choose the bundle you want to order from the list of available bundles.
4. Specify the quantity of the bundle you want to order.
5. If you need any additional items, select them from the list of available options.
6. Enter your full delivery address, including the unit or suite number.
7. Choose how you will pay for the order (credit card, invoice, or cash on delivery).
8. Provide any special instructions for delivery or packaging.
9. Confirm that you agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer full name**: Enter the full name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact email**: Provide an email for order confirmations.
* **Contact phone**: Include your country code if you are outside of your local area.
* **Preferred delivery date**: Select a date for delivery of your order.
* **Bundle selection**: Choose one bundle from the available options (Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, or Beverage).
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of bundles you want to order.
* **Additional items**: Select any additional items you need, such as Cutlery set, Napkins, or Disposable plates.
* **Delivery address**: Enter your full delivery address, including the unit or suite number.
* **Billing method**: Choose how you will pay for the order (Credit card, Invoice, or Cash on delivery).
* **Special instructions**: Provide any special instructions for delivery or packaging.
* **Agree to terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms of service. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that your preferred delivery date is in the future and not in the past.
- If you need any additional items, select them carefully to avoid overordering.
- Be clear and specific with any special instructions to ensure timely delivery.
