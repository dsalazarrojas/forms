# Food Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Drive Form is a tool for collecting information from users about their food and toiletry needs. This form helps us better understand your requirements and provide you with the most relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name and email address, as these are required to contact you.
2. Provide your address, as this will help us better understand your location.
3. Select the types of food and toiletries you need from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Enter the quantity of each item you require.
5. If you have any other items that are not listed, feel free to add them in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <h2>Page 1</h2>

* **Name** (Text, Required): This is where you enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Please enter your address to help us better understand your location.
* **Food** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the types of food you need. You can choose one or more from the following options:
	+ Bread
	+ Fruits
	+ Vegetables
	+ Proteins

### <h2>Page 2</h2>

* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of each food item you need.
* **Toiletries** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of toiletries you need from the following options:
	+ Toothbrush
	+ Toothpaste
	+ Shampoo
	+ Soap
* **Baby items** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you have any baby items, select "Yes" or "No".
* **Other** (Text, Optional): If you have any other items that are not listed, please enter them here.
* **Sync submissions with 100+ apps** (Note, Required): This is a note field for future reference, but not a field you need to fill. It's used to sync your submissions with more than 100 apps.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us.
