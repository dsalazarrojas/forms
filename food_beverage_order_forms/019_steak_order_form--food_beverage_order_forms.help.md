# Steak Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for steaks at a restaurant or steakhouse. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options for **Customer Information**:
	* Choose your **Customer Name** and **Email**.
2. Choose your **Steak Preference**:
	* Select from **Rare**, **Medium Rare**, or **Well Done**.
3. Enter your **Payment Information**:
	* Select your **Card Number**, **Expiration Date**, and **CVV**.
4. Provide **Order Information**:
	* Choose the quantity of steak you'd like to order.
	* Select an **Order Date** and **Delivery Time** for your order.
5. Add a **Customer Note** (optional):
	* Write any additional information about your order.
6. Enter your **Contact Information**:
	* Select your **Phone** and **Address**.
7. Enter your **Order Total** (optional):
	* Type in the total amount you'd like to spend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer name, email)**:
	* This field is required and asks for your name and email address.
* **Steak Preference (rare, medium rare, well done)**:
	* Select how you'd like your steak cooked.
* **Payment Information (card number, expiration date, cvv)**:
	* This field is optional, but you'll need to fill it in if you'd like to pay online.
* **Order Information (quantity, order date, delivery time)**:
	* Choose how many steaks you'd like to order and when you'd like to receive them.
* **Customer Note**:
	* Add any special instructions or notes about your order.
* **Contact Information (phone, address)**:
	* Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
* **Order Total**:
	* Enter the total amount you'd like to spend on your order.
