# Philippines Fast Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Philippines Fast Food Order Form" is a form designed to collect customer data and order information for the fast food store in the Philippines. It is intended for customers to input their order details, including store location, order time, contact information, and order items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review your entries before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Store ID (`storeid`, number, optional)
* This field is for the store identification number, which helps us to locate your order within our system. If you're unsure, you can ask your server for the store ID.

### Order Date and Time (`orderdatetime`, date, optional)
* This field is for you to input the date and time you would like to pick up your order. Please use the format MM/DD/YY.

### Customer Name (`customername`, text, optional)
* This field is where you can input your name for our records.

### Customer Email (`customeremail`, email, optional)
* Please enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your order. This is not required but helps us to keep you informed about your order.

### Contact Phone (`contactphone`, text, optional)
* This field is for you to input your contact number, so we can reach out to you regarding your order.

### Select an Item (`orderitem1`, select_one, optional)
* Please select the item you would like to order from the options provided.

### Payment Method (`paymentmethod`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select one or more payment methods that you prefer, such as credit card, cash, or other payment options available at our store.

### Store Address (`storeaddress`, text, optional)
* This field is for your reference; it's the address of our store.
* Tips:
* Fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth ordering experience.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask our staff for assistance.
