# Student Monthly Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a student's monthly food plan from the school cafeteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your meal plan from the dropdown menu in the **Select Meal Plan** field.
2. Fill out any additional information that is required by the **Student ID** and **Meal Plan Description** fields.
3. Enter any food allergies or preferences in the **Food Preferences** field.
4. Fill out the **Order Date** field with the date you would like to start your monthly meal plan.
5. Optionally, add any additional information in the **Name** field (though this field is not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Meal Plan**: This field will determine which meal plan you will be enrolled in. Choose from Regular, Premium, or Diet plans.
* **Name**: This field will allow you to add any additional information about yourself.
* **Order Date**: This field will determine when you would like to start your monthly meal plan.
* **Food Preferences**: This field is for listing any food allergies or preferences you may have (e.g. gluten-free, vegan).
* **Student ID**: This field will identify your student account or profile in the system.
* **Meal Plan ID**: This field is not currently active and is not required.
* **Select Meal Plan**: This field will determine which meal plan you will be enrolled in. Choose from Regular, Premium, or Diet plans.
* **Meal Plan Description**: This field will allow you to add any additional information about your meal plan.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your meal plan is ordered correctly.
* If you have any special requests or allergies, please list them in the **Food Preferences** field.
* You can select your **Select Meal Plan** to choose between Regular, Premium, or Diet plans.
* The **Order Date** field should be filled out with the date you would like to start your meal plan.
