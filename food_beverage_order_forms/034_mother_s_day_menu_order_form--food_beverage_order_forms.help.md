# Mother’s Day Menu Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mother's Day Menu Order Form" is a form used to take orders for food and beverage items for Mother's Day celebrations. This form will guide you through the order process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for accurate and efficient order submission.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the label.
2. Enter your mother's name or the recipient's name in the "Customer Info" field.
3. Select your contact preference in the "Contact Preference" field.
4. Provide any special requests in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Enter the date and time for the order in the "Date and Time" field.
6. Choose your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
7. Enter the order total in the "Order Total" field.
8. Select the payment status in the "Payment Status" field.
9. Enter the order status in the "Order Status" field.
10. Provide any customer service feedback in the "Customer Service" field.
11. Enter any order comments in the "Order Comments" and "Order Comments 2" fields.
12. Enter the delivery status in the "Delivery Status" field.
13. Enter any delivery comments in the "Delivery Comments" field.
