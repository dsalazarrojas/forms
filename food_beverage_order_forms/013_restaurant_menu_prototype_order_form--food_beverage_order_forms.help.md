# restaurant_menu_prototype_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant menu prototype order form is used to collect customer information and payment details for orders placed through a restaurant or food establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form by providing the required and optional information in the corresponding fields.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Restaurant Menu Prototype Order Form
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional: optional): This field is not a title in the classical sense but rather the name of this form. You can enter a descriptive name to help identify it in your records.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional: optional): This field is designed to collect customer details. You can enter any relevant information about the customer in this field.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional: optional): This field is used to collect payment information for the order. You can provide any payment-related details in this field.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any queries or need help with this form, please contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
