# Self Ordering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Ordering Form is designed to facilitate self-service ordering from your food and beverage business. It collects information on your preferences and requirements to ensure accurate and quick processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of your order in the "description" field.
2.  Select a category that best describes your order from the "category" dropdown list.
3.  Click on the "Submit" button to send in your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Description** (`description`, text, optional): 
Enter a brief description of your order, for example, "Lunch for two" or "Dinner for one".

### 2. **Category** (`category`, text, optional):
Select a category that best describes your order from the available options.

### 3. **Self Ordering Form** (text, optional):
This is the title of your order.

(Note: This field can be filled with any text, and its purpose is primarily for identification purposes.)
