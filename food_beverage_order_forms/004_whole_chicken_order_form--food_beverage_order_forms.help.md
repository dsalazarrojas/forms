# Whole Chicken Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Whole Chicken Order Form is designed to collect information about a customer's order for whole chickens. This form gathers essential details such as the customer's name, the count of whole chickens ordered, the side of chicken preferred, and any additional sauces selected. The form also captures the date and time for pick-up. Completing this form will help ensure that your whole chicken order is processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification, please.
2. **Select Whole Chickens Count**: Choose how many whole chickens you would like to order.
3. **Choose a Side of Chicken**: Select the side of chicken you would like, such as left, right, breast, leg, or wing.
4. **Select Sauces (Optional)**: Choose one or more sauces to accompany your whole chickens. Select from options like BBQ, ranch, honey mustard, or buffalo.
5. **Pick-up Time**: Enter the date and time you'd like to pick up your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name**: Enter your name for the whole chicken order.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **whole_chicken_count**: Specify the number of whole chickens you would like to order.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **side_of_chicken**: Choose the side of chicken you would like, such as left, right, breast, leg, or wing.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
* **sauce**: Select one or more sauces to accompany your whole chickens.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
* **pick_up_time**: Enter the date and time you'd like to pick up your order.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
