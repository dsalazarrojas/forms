# Easter Brunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Brunch Order Form is used to collect information for the Easter Brunch event. This form is for ordering food and beverages, and also allows the collection of guest information and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Easter Brunch information section with any relevant details.
2. Enter the guest information, including the number of guests.
3. Select the food and beverage options for your guests.
4. Add any special requests or comments in the special requests section.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Select the date and time for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Easter Brunch Info**: A text field where you can add any relevant information about the Easter Brunch event. (optional)
* **Guest Info**: Enter the number of guests attending the Easter Brunch. (number, e.g., 1, 2, 3, or 4)
* **Guest Count**: Select the number of guests attending the Easter Brunch. (Select from 1, 2, 3, or 4)
* **Food Order**: Select the food and beverage options for your guests. (Select from Easter Brunch Egg Hunt Game, Easter Brunch Food, or Easter Brunch Drinks)
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional details or instructions for your order. (text field)
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests or comments for your order. (text field)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. (email address)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (text field)
* **Date**: Select the date for your order. (date field)
* **Time**: Select the time for your order. (time field)
* **Submit Button**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your order. (Submit button)

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for your order.
- Check the form fields for any errors or invalid information before submitting.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
