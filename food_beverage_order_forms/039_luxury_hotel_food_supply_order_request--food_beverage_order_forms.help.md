# Luxury Hotel Food Supply Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request food supplies from the hotel's inventory for daily operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name, which will be associated with the order.
2. Select your delivery preferences, such as the delivery location and method.
3. Provide detailed information about the order, including item descriptions and quantities.
4. Choose the employee(s) responsible for approving the request.
5. Select the approval ace (Accountable, Confidential, or Exempt) for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **food_supply_request (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the food supply order.
* **delivery_preferences (Select Multiple)**: Select your preferred delivery location (e.g., room, office, etc.) and delivery method (e.g., hand-deliver, email, etc.). 
* **order_details (Text)**: Provide detailed information about the order, including item descriptions and quantities. 
* **approval_employee (Select One)**: Choose the employee responsible for approving the order, such as the department head or purchasing manager.
* **approval_ace (Select One)**: Select the approval ace (Accountable, Confidential, or Exempt) for the order.
