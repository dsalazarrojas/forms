# School Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students make their lunch preferences known to the school cafeteria team, which will help them prepare a suitable meal. Please complete this form with your selection to ensure that the cafeteria staff can fulfill your order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of lunch you would like to have.
2. Choose one or more side items to accompany your meal.
3. Select a juice to drink.
4. Pick one or more fruits to have with your meal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Lunch Order Form** (`school_lunch_order_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which you can ignore while filling out the form.
* **Select Main Course** (`school_lunch_order_form_page_2`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available main courses from the options: Grilled Chicken Sandwich, Pizza, or Salad.
* **Select Side** (`school_lunch_order_form_page_3`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more side items to have with your meal. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key while clicking on the items. Available options are Carrot Sticks, Chips, and Apple.
* **Select Juice** (`school_lunch_order_form_page_4`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available juices: Apple, Orange, or Cranberry.
* **Select Fruit** (`school_lunch_order_form_page_5`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more fruits to have with your meal. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key while clicking on the items. Available options are Apple, Banana, and Grapes.

## Tips
- Make sure to select at least one main course and one juice.
- You can choose as many sides and fruits as you like, but the more you select, the more variety you'll have in your meal.
