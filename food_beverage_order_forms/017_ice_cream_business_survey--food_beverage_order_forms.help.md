# Ice Cream Business Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ice Cream Business Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from customers about their experiences at our ice cream parlor.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. You can select multiple options for questions that are multiple-select type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about our ice cream?** (`ice_cream_business_survey_page_1`, select_multiple, **required**): Select one or more of the options to express your overall feelings about our ice cream. Please choose from Excellent, Good, or Poor.
* **What is your favorite ice cream flavor?** (`ice_cream_flavor`, select_one, **required**): Please select your favorite flavor from the list provided.
* **Have you visited our ice cream parlor recently?** (`ice_cream_parlor`, select_multiple, **required**): Select one or more of the options to indicate your recent visit frequency.
	+ Option 1: Yes, I visited yesterday
	+ Option 2: Yes, last week
	+ Option 3: No
* **On your most recent visit, what date did you go?** (`ice_cream_parlor_visit_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of your most recent visit.
* **Who served you on your most recent visit?** (`ice_cream_staff`, select_one, **required**): Please select the person who served you on your most recent visit.
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Doe
	+ Jim
	+ Mary
	+ Pete
	+ Bob
	+ Alice
	+ Tom
* **How would you rate your experience?** (`ice_cream_rate`, select_one, **required**): Rate your experience at our parlor from Excellent to Bad.
* **Email** (`ice_cream_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`ice_cream_phone`, text, **optional**): If you have any phone number you'd like to provide for follow-up, enter it here.
* **Additional comments** (`ice_cream_additional_comments`, note, **optional**): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your experience, enter them here.
