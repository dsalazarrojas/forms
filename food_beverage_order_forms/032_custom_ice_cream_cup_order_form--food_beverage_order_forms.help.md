# Custom Ice Cream Cup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you order a custom ice cream cup with your preferred flavors, size, and toppings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more flavors for your ice cream cup.
2. Choose the desired size for your ice cream cup.
3. Select any toppings you'd like to add to your ice cream cup.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Flavors
* **Flavors** (`ice_cream_flavors`, `select_multiple`, Not Required): Choose one or more flavors for your custom ice cream cup. Select from the available options:
	+ Vanilla
	+ Strawberry
	+ Chocolate
	+ Cookies and Cream

### Size
* **Size** (`cup_size`, `select_one`, Not Required): Select the size of your ice cream cup:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large

### Toppings
* **Toppings** (`toppings`, `select_multiple`, Not Required): Add any toppings you'd like to your ice cream cup. Select from the available options:
	+ Chocolate chips
	+ Whipped cream
	+ Sprinkles
	+ Nuts

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, Required): Confirm your order. This is the final step to complete your custom ice cream cup order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select at least one flavor for your ice cream cup.
* You can choose multiple flavors, but not all fields are required.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, you can always contact us for assistance.
* Once you submit your order, please allow 24-48 hours for processing and delivery.
* We'll do our best to accommodate your custom requests, but please note that availability of certain flavors and toppings may vary.
