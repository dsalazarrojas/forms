# stripe_online_food_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to enable customers to place an online order for food, allowing them to provide their personal details for order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Order Food" button to initiate the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Food (Order Food, text, not required)**: This is where you can type in the specific food item(s) you would like to order. This field is not required but it's recommended that you provide details for order processing.
* **Name (Name, text, not required)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email (Email, email, not required)**: Enter your email address where the order receipt and confirmation can be sent to.
* **Phone (Phone, text, not required)**: Enter your phone number where the order confirmation or any updates can be sent to.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for correct order processing.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure smooth communication.
