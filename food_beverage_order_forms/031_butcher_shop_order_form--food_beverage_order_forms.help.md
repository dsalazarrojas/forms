# Butcher Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butcher Shop Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for meat products from the butcher shop. This form gathers customer information, order details, and payment information to ensure accurate and efficient communication with the butcher shop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the order type (pickup or delivery) that suits your needs.
3. Choose your preferred pickup or delivery date and time slot.
4. Select the meat products you want to order from the available options. You can choose multiple items for each type of meat (beef, pork, chicken, and specialty).
5. Specify the quantities and details of each selected item in the "Order Details and Quantities" field.
6. Provide any special requests or instructions in the "Special Requests" field.
7. Choose your payment method from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your street address where the order will be delivered or where you will pick up the order.
* **City**: Enter the city where your address is located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your city.
* **Order Type**: Select "Pickup at shop" if you will pick up the order at the shop or "Home delivery" or "Business delivery" for delivery to your home or business.
* **Preferred Pickup or Delivery Date**: Choose a date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select a time slot when you would like to receive your order.
* **Beef Products**: Select the beef products you want to order from the available options.
* **Pork Products**: Select the pork products you want to order from the available options.
* **Chicken Products**: Select the chicken products you want to order from the available options.
* **Specialty or Other Meats**: Select the specialty or other meats you want to order from the available options.
* **Prepared Items**: Select the prepared items you want to order from the available options.
* **Order Details and Quantities**: Specify the cuts, weights, and quantities of each selected item.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Dietary or Quality Preferences**: Select any dietary or quality preferences for your order.
* **Estimated Order Total**: Estimate the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions**: Provide any additional notes or special instructions for your order.
