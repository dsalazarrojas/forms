# Taste Test Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taste Test Ranking Survey form is used to collect opinions and feedback from taste testers on a series of taste tests. The form guides the respondents through a series of pages, each containing select_one or select_multiple fields, to gather their preferences and rankings for various taste test options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the options that best match your preferences on each page.
2. For select_one fields, choose the option that best fits your response.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all the options that apply to you.
4. Progress through each page, completing the fields as instructed.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, submit the form to provide your feedback and opinions.
