**Takeaway Order Form - Help Guide**
================================

### Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information for takeaway food orders. It's intended for customers to provide their contact details, food presentation preferences, delivery schedule, and any special requests they may have. By completing this form, customers can accurately submit their orders and any additional requirements.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out this form thoroughly to ensure your order is processed correctly.
2.  Select a contact method to reach out to you (Mobile, Email, Phone, or Address).
3.  Enter a contact number or email address, depending on your selected contact method.
4.  Choose the type of food presentation that suits your preferences.
5.  Select the desired delivery date and time (optional).
6.  List any special requests or notes you have for your order.
7.  Review your order details before submitting the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
    Enter your name and any other contact details you'd like to provide.
*   **Food Presentation** (`food_presentation`):
    Choose how you'd like your order to be presented (e.g., Apple, Apple Pie, Banana Split).
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`):
    Select the date you'd like to receive your order (if different from the current date).
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`):
    Choose the time you'd like to receive your order (if different from the current time).
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`):
    List any special requests or notes you have for your order (e.g., special occasions, gift orders).
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`):
    Select how you'd like to be contacted (Mobile, Email, Phone, or Address).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`):
    Enter your contact number if you selected Mobile as your contact method.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
    Enter your contact email if you selected Email as your contact method.
*   **Special Request 1** (`special_request_01`):
    Enter any special requests or notes you have for your order (if applicable).
*   **Special Request 2** (`special_request_02`):
    Enter any additional special requests or notes you have for your order (if applicable).

### Tips
*   Review your order details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Ensure you've entered all necessary contact information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
*   Our team will process your order and contact you according to your selected contact method.
