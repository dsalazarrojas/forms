# Bellissimo Italy Super Saturday - Help Guide
## Purpose
To place an order at Bellissimo Italy Super Saturday, please complete this form to inform us of your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will send order confirmation.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is for order updates or questions.
4. Select your preferred "Order Type" (Dine-in, Takeout, Delivery, or Catering).
5. Choose your favorite Italian pasta dish from the "Pasta Selection" field.
6. Select your preferred pizza from the "Pizza Selection" field.
7. Choose any appetizers you'd like to start with from the "Appetizer Selection" field (optional).
8. Select a fresh salad to complement your meal from the "Salad Selection" field (optional).
9. Choose your preferred drink from the "Beverage Selection" field (optional).
10. Select your preferred dessert from the "Dessert Selection" field (optional).
11. Inform us of any dietary restrictions or allergies in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" field.
12. If you have any special requests, please enter them in the "Special Requests" field (optional).
13. Indicate the number of guests dining with you in the "Number of Guests" field.
14. Specify the time you'd like your order ready or table reserved in the "Preferred Time" field.
15. Select the date for your order in the "Preferred Date" field.
16. Choose your payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
17. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the "Promotional Code" field (optional).
18. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
