# Food Franchise POS Terminal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for orders processed at the terminal. It includes details about the order, payment method, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Enter the order date and time in the "Order Date" field.
3. Select the payment method from the "Payment Methods" dropdown.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee Id" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, not required): Enter the date and time of the order.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, time, not required): Enter the time of day when the order was placed.
* **Terminal Information** (Terminal Information, text, not required): Enter any specific details about the terminal where the order was processed (e.g., "Terminal 1").
* **Employee Id** (Employee Id, number, not required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Payment Methods** (Payment Methods, select_one, not required): Select how the customer paid for the order (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Mobile Wallet).
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Note** (Note, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Email** (Email, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Total** (Order Total, number, not required): Enter the total cost of the order.
