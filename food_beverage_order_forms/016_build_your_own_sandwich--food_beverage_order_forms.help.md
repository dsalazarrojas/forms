# Build Your Own Sandwich - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to build your own sandwich by selecting your preferences for various aspects, such as order type, size, bread type, protein, cheese, vegetables, sauces, and extras. You will need to complete the form with your details, including customer name, email, phone number, and pickup or delivery preferences. The form also allows for payment method selection and special instructions or allergies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your order type (Dine In, Takeout, Delivery, or Catering).
3. Choose your sandwich size (6 inch, 9 inch, 12 inch, Footlong, or Party Size).
4. Select your bread type (White Sub Roll, Wheat Sub Roll, Italian Herb and Cheese, Sourdough, Ciabatta, Rye, or Gluten Free Wrap).
5. Decide if you want your bread toasted (Yes - Lightly Toasted, Yes - Well Toasted, or No Toast).
6. Choose your protein selection (multiple options).
7. Select your cheese selection (multiple options).
8. Add any vegetables to your sandwich (multiple options).
9. Choose any sauces and condiments (multiple options).
10. Add any extras to your sandwich (multiple options).
11. If desired, enter any special instructions or allergies.
12. Enter the quantity of sandwiches you would like to order.
13. If selecting delivery, enter your delivery address.
14. Select your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Order Type** (4, select_one, required): Choose how you will receive your order (Dine In, Takeout, Delivery, or Catering).
* **Sandwich Size** (5, select_one, required): Choose your sandwich size (6 inch, 9 inch, 12 inch, Footlong, or Party Size).
* **Bread Type** (6, select_one, required): Select your bread type (White Sub Roll, Wheat Sub Roll, Italian Herb and Cheese, Sourdough, Ciabatta, Rye, or Gluten Free Wrap).
* **Bread Toast** (7, select_one, required): Decide if you want your bread toasted (Yes - Lightly Toasted, Yes - Well Toasted, or No Toast).
* **Protein Selection** (8, select_multiple, required): Choose your protein (Turkey, Ham, Roast Beef, Chicken Breast, Tuna Salad, Egg Salad, Bacon, Salami, Pepperoni, Provolone, Swiss Cheese, Cheddar Cheese, or No Protein - Veggie Only).
* **Cheese Selection** (9, select_multiple, required): Choose your cheese (American, Swiss, Cheddar, Provolone, Pepper Jack, Mozzarella, Feta, or No Cheese).
* **Vegetables** (10, select_multiple, required): Add any vegetables to your sandwich (Lettuce, Tomatoes, Cucumbers, Red Onions, Green Peppers, Black Olives, Pickles, Jalapenos, Spinach, Banana Peppers, or No Vegetables).
* **Sauces and Condiments** (11, select_multiple, required): Choose your sauces and condiments (Mayonnaise, Mustard, Ketchup, Ranch, Italian Dressing, Oil and Vinegar, BBQ Sauce, Hot Sauce, Honey Mustard, or No Sauce).
* **Extras** (12, select_multiple, not required): Add any extras to your sandwich (Extra Meat, Extra Cheese, Avocado, Guacamole, Fried Egg, Hash Browns, Side Chips, Side Salad, Cookie, or None).
* **Special Instructions** (13, text, not required): Enter any special instructions or allergies.
* **Quantity** (14, number, required): Enter the quantity of sandwiches you would like to order.
* **Pickup Time** (15, time, not required): Choose when you would like to pick up your order.
* **Delivery Address** (16, text, not required): Enter your delivery address if you selected delivery.
* **Payment Method** (17, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for your order (Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Mobile Payment, or Account Billing).
