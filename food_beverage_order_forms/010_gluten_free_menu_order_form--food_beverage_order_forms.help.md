# Gluten Free Menu Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gluten Free Menu Order Form is designed to help customers order gluten-free menu items from a food establishment. This form requires customers to provide their payment details, contact information, and gluten-free options to complete their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Gluten Free Menu Items" option to choose which gluten-free menu items you'd like to order.
2. Choose your preferred "Gluten Free Options" from the available options.
3. Enter your "Customer Information" in the text field provided.
4. Enter your "Customer Email" and "Customer Phone" in the text fields.
5. Select a "Date" for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Info (text)**: Enter your payment information to complete your order.
- **Gluten Free Menu Items (select_multiple)**: Select the gluten-free menu items you'd like to order. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Gluten Free Menu Item Selected (text)**: This field is used to select one gluten-free menu item from the options provided.
- **Gluten Free Options (select_one)**: Select one of the gluten-free options available. Options include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
- **Gluten Free Options Selected (text)**: Enter your chosen gluten-free option.
- **Customer Information (text)**: Enter your customer information in the text field provided.
- **Customer Email (email)**: Enter your customer email address.
- **Customer Phone (text)**: Enter your customer phone number.
- **Customer Name (text)**: Enter your customer name.
- **Customer Address (text)**: Enter your customer address.
- **Date (date)**: Select a date for your order.

Note: The form fields and their labels are designed to be user-friendly and clear, but if you're unsure about any field, please ask your customer support team for assistance.
