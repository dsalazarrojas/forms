# 1:1 Diet Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to provide their personal and dietary information, as well as order specifics such as date, meal plan type, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Specify your **Dietary restrictions or allergies** in the text field below.
3. Select the **Type of meal plan** you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Item or product name** and **Quantity** you would like to order.
5. Specify the **Unit price** for your order.
6. Choose your **Preferred delivery method** (Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery).
7. Select your **Preferred delivery time** (Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
8. Enter any **Special instructions or customization notes** you may have.
9. Choose your **Payment method** (Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, or PayPal).
10. Optionally, select your **Subscription frequency** (One-time order, Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
11. Enter any **Additional comments or notes** you have for the ordering team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email address**: We'll send order confirmation here.
* **Phone number**: For delivery coordination.
* **Dietary restrictions or allergies**: Specify any food allergies or dietary restrictions.
* **Type of meal plan**: Select from Standard Diet Plan, Keto Diet Plan, Vegan Diet Plan, Low Carb Plan, High Protein Plan, or Custom Plan.
* **Item or product name**: Specify the exact meal or product.
* **Quantity**: Enter number of meals or portions.
* **Unit price**: Price per meal or portion.
* **Total calories per meal**: Average calories per serving (optional).
* **Protein content (grams)**: Grams of protein per serving (optional).
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose from Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery.
* **Preferred delivery time**: Choose from Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Special instructions or customization notes**: Dietary modifications, packaging requests, etc.
* **Payment method**: Choose from Credit card, Cash, Bank transfer, or PayPal.
* **Subscription frequency**: Choose from One-time order, Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Any other information for our team.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact our support team.
* If you're interested in a custom meal plan, please specify your requirements in the "Special instructions or customization notes" field.
