# Food Safety Inspection Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Inspection Records Release Form is a document used to record and release food safety inspection records for facilities. It is a critical tool for regulatory agencies to ensure that food establishments meet the necessary standards to operate safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant facility information (Facility 1 or Facility 2) for which you want to record and release the inspection records.
2. Enter the facility's address on the Facility Address field (Facility Address 1 or Facility Address 2).
3. Enter the facility's phone number on the Facility Phone field (Facility Phone 1 or Facility Phone 2).
4. Enter the facility's contact person on the Facility Contact Person field (Facility Contact Person 1 or Facility Contact Person 2).
5. Select the relevant options for the facility's certification (Facility Certification 1 or Facility Certification 2).
6. Enter the inspection date on the Inspection Date field (Inspection Date 1 or Inspection Date 2).
7. Select the relevant options for the facility's food safety inspection records (Facility Safety Inspection Records 1 or Facility Safety Inspection Records 2).
8. Enter the facility's certification type on the Certification Type field (Certification Type 1 or Certification Type 2).
9. Enter the facility's license number on the Facility License field (Facility License 1 or Facility License 2).
10. Enter the inspector's name on the Inspector Name field (Inspector Name 1 or Inspector Name 2).
11. Select the relevant options for the inspector's certification on the Inspector Certification field (Inspector Certification 1 or Inspector Certification 2).
12. Select the relevant options for the facility's certification on the Facility Certification 2 field (Facility Certification 2).
13. Enter the facility's email address on the Facility Email field (Facility Email 1 or Facility Email 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Info (Facility 1)** (`Facility Info`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility for which you want to record and release the inspection records.
* **Facility Address (Facility 1)** (`Facility Address 1`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address to ensure accurate location identification.
* **Facility Phone (Facility 1)** (`Facility Phone 1`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Facility Contact Person (Facility 1)** (`Facility Contact Person 1`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's contact person's name and title.
* **Facility Certification (Facility 1)** (`Facility Certification 1`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility's certification type.
* **Inspection Date (Facility 1)** (`Inspection Date 1`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the food safety inspection.
* **Facility Safety Inspection Records (Facility 1)** (`Facility Safety Inspection Records 1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant inspection records for the facility.
* **Certification Type (Facility 1)** (`Certification Type 1`, `text`, required): Enter the type of certification the facility holds.
* **Facility License (Facility 1)** (`Facility License 1`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's license number.
* **Facility Address (Facility 2)** (`Facility Address 2`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address for Facility 2.
* **Facility Phone (Facility 2)** (`Facility Phone 2`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's phone number for Facility 2.
* **Facility Contact Person (Facility 2)** (`Facility Contact Person 2`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's contact person's name and title for Facility 2.
* **Facility Certification (Facility 2)** (`Facility Certification 2`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility's certification type for Facility 2.
* **Inspection Date (Facility 2)** (`Inspection Date 2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the food safety inspection for Facility 2.
* **Facility Safety Inspection Records (Facility 2)** (`Facility Safety Inspection Records 2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant inspection records for Facility 2.
* **Certification Type (Facility 2)** (`Certification Type 2`, `text`, required): Enter the type of certification Facility 2 holds.
* **Facility License (Facility 2)** (`Facility License 2`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's license number for Facility 2.
* **Facility Email (Facility 2)** (`Facility Email 2`, `email`, required): Enter the facility's email address for Facility 2.
