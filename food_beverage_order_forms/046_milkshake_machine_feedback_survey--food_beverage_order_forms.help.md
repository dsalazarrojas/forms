# Milkshake Machine Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the milkshake machine's performance, including its taste, customer service, and overall condition. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the options provided for each question to select your response.
4. Add any additional comments you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction**: This is a general introduction to the form, providing context for the following questions.
* **How did the machine perform**: This question asks about the overall performance of the milkshake machine, and how it met your expectations.
	+ **Poor**: The machine did not perform well and was not functional.
	+ **Average**: The machine performed as expected, but was not exceptional.
	+ **Excellent**: The machine performed exceptionally well and exceeded expectations.
* **How was the taste**: This question asks about your experience of the taste of the milkshake.
	+ **Too Sweet**: The milkshake was overly sweet.
	+ **Too Salty**: The milkshake was overly salty.
	+ **Just Right**: The milkshake was balanced in taste.
* **How was the customer service**: This question asks about your experience with the customer service staff, including their helpfulness and friendliness.
	+ **Poor**: The customer service staff did not meet expectations.
	+ **Average**: The customer service staff met expectations.
	+ **Excellent**: The customer service staff exceeded expectations.
* **Were there any issues encountered**: This question asks about any issues you experienced with the machine, such as failure to dispense or other problems.
	+ **Machine Failed To Dispense**: The machine failed to dispense milkshake.
	+ **Other**: Please specify any other issues you experienced.
* **How was the product quality**: This question asks about your experience with the quality of the milkshake.
	+ **Poor**: The milkshake quality was not good.
	+ **Average**: The milkshake quality was acceptable.
	+ **Excellent**: The milkshake quality was exceptional.
* **What is the condition of the machine**: This question asks about your observation of the machine's condition.
	+ **Dirty**: The machine was dirty and not well-maintained.
	+ **Average**: The machine was in an average condition.
	+ **Clean**: The machine was clean and well-maintained.
* **Any additional comments**: This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the milkshake machine.
