# Beverage Shelf Audit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Shelf Audit Order Form is used to assess the condition of a retail store's beverage shelf, ensuring that it meets company standards for proper stocking, organization, and compliance with policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and equipment for the audit.
2. Fill out the "Auditor Information" section with your details, including your name, ID or employee number, and contact information.
3. Complete the "Store Information" section with the store's details, including name, address, city, state, and type of store.
4. Move on to the "Shelf Audit Details" section, where you'll assess the shelf's condition, number of facings, and list the products on the shelf.
5. Evaluate the shelf's freshness, pricing accuracy, and signage quality.
6. Check for any compliance issues, such as promotional displays and product placement.
7. Record any additional observations or notes in the "Additional Observations" section.
8. Review and submit your findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor Name**: Enter your name and title.
* **Auditor ID or Employee Number**: Enter your ID or employee number for identification purposes.
* **Auditing Company or Organization**: Enter the name of the company or organization conducting the audit.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the audit.
* **Store Name or Location**: Enter the retail location being audited.
* **Store Address**: Enter the full street address of the store.
* **Store City**: Enter the city where the store is located.
* **Store State or Province**: Enter the state or province where the store is located.
* **Store ZIP Code**: Enter the store's zip code.
* **Type of Store**: Select the type of retail store (e.g. supermarket, convenience store, etc.).
* **Store Manager or Contact Person**: Enter the name of the responsible person at the store.
* **Store Phone Number**: Enter the store's phone number.
* **Shelf Section Location**: Enter the location of the shelf within the store (e.g. aisle, end cap, cooler, etc.).
* **Overall Shelf Condition**: Select the overall condition of the shelf (e.g. excellent, good, fair, poor, critical).
* **Number of Facings**: Enter the number of product facings visible on the shelf.
* **Beverage Products on Shelf**: List the brands and varieties of products on the shelf.
* **Competitor Products Present**: List any competing brands or products observed on the shelf.
* **Out of Stock Items**: List any items not available on the shelf.
* **Product Freshness Assessment**: Select the overall freshness of the products on the shelf (e.g. all fresh, mostly fresh, some expired, etc.).
* **Expiration Date Issues Found**: Describe any issues with expiration dates.
* **Pricing Accuracy**: Select the accuracy of prices on the shelf (e.g. all accurate, most accurate, some discrepancies, etc.).
* **Signage Quality**: Select the quality of signs and displays on the shelf (e.g. professional, good condition, poor condition, etc.).
* **Promotion Compliance**: Select whether promotional displays are properly displayed (e.g. yes, mostly compliant, some issues, not compliant, etc.).
* **Product Placement Issues**: Describe any issues with product placement on the shelf.
* **Additional Observations or Notes**: Record any other relevant information about the shelf audit.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate in your assessment of the shelf's condition.
* Take photos or videos to support your findings.
* Record any observations or notes as you go, to ensure accuracy.
* Review and submit your findings promptly.
* Use the correct terminology and criteria for assessing the shelf's condition.
