# jules_1_to_1_diet_product_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track details of a one-to-one diet product order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Order Product**: Select the product being ordered from the dropdown list.
2. **Customer Information**: Choose whether or not the customer has provided information.
3. **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was placed.
4. **Order Time**: Enter the time the order was placed.
5. **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
6. **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
7. **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
8. **Order Comments**: Add any comments related to the order.
9. **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
10. **Delivery Date**: Enter the date the order is to be delivered.
11. **Delivery Time**: Enter the time the order is to be delivered.
12. **Delivery Comments**: Add any comments related to delivery.
13. **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
14. **Assigned User**: Enter the sales representative assigned to handle the order.
15. **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product.
16. **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
17. **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product.
18. **Customer Note**: Add any notes from the customer.
19. **Order Notes**: Add any notes related to the order.
20. **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
21. **Order Tax**: Enter any tax associated with the order.
22. **Order Subtotal**: Enter the subtotal of the order (excluding tax).
23. **Order Discount**: Enter any discount applied to the order.
24. **Order Shipping**: Enter the cost of shipping.
25. **Order Taxes**: Enter any taxes paid on the order.
