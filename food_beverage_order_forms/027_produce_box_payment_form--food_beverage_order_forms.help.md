# Produce Box Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for a produce box service, allowing customers to specify their delivery details and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "delivery_details" page and enter your delivery address, delivery date, and any other relevant details about your produce box delivery.
2. Move to the "payment_details" page and enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.) and any other payment-specific details.
3. On the "customer_info" page, enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or the person placing the order.
4. Finally, on the "order_details" page, enter the details about your produce box order, including the box type, quantity, and any special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **delivery_details** (Text)
* **Label**: delivery_details
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No
* **Explanation**: Enter your delivery address and any other relevant details about your produce box delivery. This includes the date you would like to receive your delivery and any special instructions for the delivery process.

### **payment_details** (Text)
* **Label**: payment_details
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No
* **Explanation**: Enter your payment method and any other payment-specific details, such as the amount to be paid or the payment due date.

### **customer_info** (Text)
* **Label**: customer_info
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No
* **Explanation**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or the person placing the order.

### **order_details** (Text)
* **Label**: order_details
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No
* **Explanation**: Enter the details about your produce box order, including the type of box, quantity, and any special requests.
