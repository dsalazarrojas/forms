# Produce Box Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for a produce box service, allowing customers to specify their delivery details and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "delivery_details" page and enter your delivery address, delivery date, and any other relevant details about your produce box delivery.
2. Move to the "payment_details" page and enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.) and any other payment-specific details.
3. On the "customer_info" page, enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or the person placing the order.
4. Finally, on the "order_details" page, enter the details about your produce box order, including the box type, quantity, and any special requests.
