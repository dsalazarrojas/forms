# Soup Sale Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for soup sales. It is used by the sales team to process customer orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the designated field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the designated field. This is a required field.
3. Select your **Soup Type** from the available options.
4. Choose your preferred **Pickup or Delivery** method. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Contact Number**. This is a required field.
6. Provide any additional **Order Details** in the designated field (optional).
7. Enter the **Order Total**. This is a required field.
8. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: 
	+ A field to fill in your name.

### 2. Email
* **Email**: 
	+ A field to enter your email address.

### 3. Soup Type
* **Soup Type**: 
	+ A field to select your soup type preference.

### 4. Pickup or Delivery
* **Pickup or Delivery**: 
	+ A field to choose between "Yes" and "No" for pickup or delivery preference.

### 5. Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: 
	+ A field to enter your contact number.

### 6. Order Details
* **Order Details**: 
	+ A field to enter any additional order details.

### 7. Order Total
* **Order Total**: 
	+ A field to enter the total cost of your order.

### 8. Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: 
	+ A field to select your preferred payment method.
