# FoodHeadcount - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the number of food and beverage items to be ordered for a specific event or gathering. It's used to ensure that the necessary provisions are ordered in the correct quantities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the event or gathering in the **Artwork on top** field, if applicable.
2. Enter the name of the person or group that will be receiving the order in the **Name** field.
3. Enter the number of kids, if any, that will be attending the event in the **How many kids** field.
4. Select the type of items to be ordered (Food, Beverage, or Both) from the **Food & Beverage** dropdown menu.
5. Confirm that you wish to **Submit** the order or indicate that it's not ready yet (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork on top**: Enter a brief description of the event or gathering, such as a title or theme. This will help the catering team to tailor the order according to your needs.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person or group that will be receiving the order, such as the client's name or the event organizer's name.
* **How many kids**: If there are children attending the event, enter the number of kids. This will help the catering team to determine the correct quantities of child-friendly food and drinks.
* **Food & Beverage**: Select the type of items to be ordered. Options include:
	+ Food: For events where food is the primary focus.
	+ Beverage: For events where beverages (such as cocktails or wine) are the primary focus.
	+ Food and Beverage: For events where both food and beverages will be served.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you are ready to submit the order or indicate that it's not ready yet. If you select "No", the order will not be processed until you've confirmed readiness.
