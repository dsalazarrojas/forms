# Café Loyalty Program Materials Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Café Loyalty Program Materials Order Form is a crucial document used by café owners or administrators to order materials related to their loyalty programs. It collects essential information for proper processing, delivery, and fulfillment of materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the requested contact information (fields 1-5).
2. Choose the correct loyalty program status and material type for your business (fields 6-10).
3. Select the quantity needed for each material and choose the delivery speed (fields 11-14).
4. Specify any customization needs and branding assets (fields 15-16).
5. Choose the payment method (field 20).
6. Add any special instructions or notes if necessary (field 21).
7. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Café Name** (`café_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your café or business.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Provide your full name for communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your best contact number for phone calls.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Loyalty Program Status** (`loyalty_program_status`, select_one, required):
	+ **Yes, active member**: You are currently part of our loyalty program.
	+ **Yes, but need to renew**: You are in the program but require renewal.
	+ **No, but interested**: You are not yet in the program but interested in joining.
	+ **Just exploring options**: You are exploring our loyalty program options.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, required):
	+ **Loyalty cards**: You need loyalty cards.
	+ **Punch cards**: You need punch cards.
	+ **Promotional posters**: You need promotional posters.
	+ **Table tents**: You need table tents.
	+ **Counter displays**: You need counter displays.
	+ **Window decals**: You need window decals.
	+ **Mixed package**: You need a mixed package of materials.
* **Loyalty Cards Quantity** (`loyalty_card_quantity`, select_one, optional): Select the quantity of loyalty cards you need.
	+ **100 cards**: 100 cards.
	+ **250 cards**: 250 cards.
	+ **500 cards**: 500 cards.
	+ **1,000 cards**: 1,000 cards.
	+ **2,500 cards**: 2,500 cards.
	+ **5,000 cards**: 5,000 cards.
	+ **Not ordering cards**: You are not ordering any cards.
* **Punch Cards Quantity** (`punch_card_quantity`, select_one, optional):
	+ **100 cards**: 100 cards.
	+ **250 cards**: 250 cards.
	+ **500 cards**: 500 cards.
	+ **1,000 cards**: 1,000 cards.
	+ **Not ordering punch cards**: You are not ordering any punch cards.
* **Poster Size** (`poster_size`, select_one, optional):
	+ **11x17 inches**: 11x17 inches.
	+ **18x24 inches**: 18x24 inches.
	+ **24x36 inches**: 24x36 inches.
	+ **Not ordering posters**: You are not ordering any posters.
* **Poster Quantity** (`poster_quantity`, select_one, optional):
	+ **1 poster**: 1 poster.
	+ **2 posters**: 2 posters.
	+ **3-5 posters**: 3-5 posters.
	+ **6-10 posters**: 6-10 posters.
	+ **Not ordering posters**: You are not ordering any posters.
* **Customization Needed** (`customization_needed`, select_one, required):
	+ **Yes, full custom design**: You need a full custom design.
	+ **Yes, logo addition only**: You need a logo addition only.
	+ **No, use standard templates**: You do not need any custom design.
	+ **Not sure**: You are unsure about the design needs.
* **Branding Assets** (`branding_assets`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Yes, ready to upload**: You have the necessary branding assets ready to upload.
	+ **Will send later**: You will send the branding assets later.
	+ **Need design help**: You need help with designing the branding assets.
	+ **Not applicable**: This option does not apply to your business.
* **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required):
	+ **No, standard delivery**: You do not need a rush delivery.
	+ **Yes, rush delivery**: You need a rush delivery.
	+ **Yes, overnight if available**: If available, you need an overnight delivery.
* **Delivery Date Needed** (`delivery_date_needed`, date, optional): Enter the date you need materials.
* **Previous Order Number** (`previous_order_number`, text, optional): If reordering, provide your previous order number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required):
	+ **Credit card**: You will pay using a credit card.
	+ **Debit card**: You will pay using a debit card.
	+ **Invoice for business account**: You will use an invoice for your business account.
	+ **Loyalty points**: You will use loyalty points.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Add any special requests or notes.
