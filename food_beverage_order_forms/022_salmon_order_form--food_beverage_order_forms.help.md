# salmon_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The salmon_order_form is a form used to collect information related to salmon orders, including details from suppliers and customers, payment terms, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Salmon Order Form** field with the name of the salmon order.
2. Provide **Order Details** in the text field.
3. Select a **Order Date** and **Delivery Date** using the date picker.
4. Enter the **Supplier Name**, **Supplier Email**, and **Supplier Phone**.
5. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Customer Phone**, and **Customer Email**.
6. Select the **Payment Method** and **Payment Terms**.
7. Enter any additional **Order Notes**, **Customer Notes**, and **Supplier Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salmon Order Form**: A text field to enter the name of the salmon order.
* **Order Details**: A text field to enter any details related to the order.
* **Order Date** (id: 3): Select a date for the order using the date picker.
* **Order Time** (id: 4): Select a time for the order using the time picker.
* **Supplier Name** (id: 5): Enter the supplier name.
* **Supplier Email** (id: 6): Enter the supplier email address.
* **Supplier Phone** (id: 7): Enter the supplier phone number.
* **Supplier Status** (id: 12): Select the status of the supplier (Active or Inactive).
* **Delivery Address** (id: 9): Enter the delivery address.
* **Order Total** (id: 10): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Status** (id: 11): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Delivery Date** (id: 13): Select a delivery date using the date picker.
* **Delivery Time** (id: 14): Select a delivery time using the time picker.
* **Payment Method** (id: 15): Select whether the payment method is Yes or No.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 16): Select whether the payment terms are Yes or No.
* **Customer Name** (id: 17): Enter the customer name.
* **Customer Phone** (id: 18): Enter the customer phone number.
* **Customer Email** (id: 19): Enter the customer email address.
* **Customer Address** (id: 20): Enter the customer address.
* **Order Note** (id: 22): Enter any additional notes related to the order.
* **Customer Note** (id: 23): Enter any additional notes from the customer.
