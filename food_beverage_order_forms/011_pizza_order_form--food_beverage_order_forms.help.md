# Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pizza Order Form is a tool to collect information about orders of pizzas from customers. This form is used for both delivery and pickup orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Enter your delivery address in the `address` field.
5. Select the type of order you'd like to place (delivery or pickup) in the `order_type` field.
6. Add any additional order notes or special requests in the `order_notes` field (optional).
7. Enter the total cost of your order in the `total` field.
8. Select the payment method you'd like to use (bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe) in the `payment_method` field.
9. Confirm your payment method by selecting "yes" in the `confirm_payment` field.
10. Enter your customer ID and order ID, which are automatically generated and provided by the system.
11. The `created_at`, `updated_at`, and `deleted_at` fields are automatically generated and updated by the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required): Enter your name to complete this order.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **address** (text, required): Enter your delivery address for delivery orders.
* **order_type** (select_one, required): Choose between delivery and pickup options.
* **order_notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or requests for your order.
* **total** (number, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **payment_method** (select_multiple, required): Select a payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **confirm_payment** (select_one, required): Confirm your selected payment method.
* **customer_id** (text, required): Enter your customer ID, which is automatically generated by the system.
* **order_id** (number, required): Enter your order ID, which is automatically generated by the system.
* **created_at** (date, required): Automatically generated date when the order is created.
* **updated_at** (date, required): Automatically generated date when the order is updated.
* **deleted_at** (date, optional): Automatically generated date when the order is deleted.
* **deleted_by** (select_one, optional): Select "admin" or "user" to indicate who deleted the order.
* **created_by** (select_one, required): Select "admin" or "user" to indicate who created the order.
* **updated_by** (select_one, required): Select "admin" or "user" to indicate who updated the order.
* **confirm_payment** (select_one, optional): Select "yes" to confirm your payment, or "no" to cancel your order.
* **customer_id** (text, optional): Enter your customer ID, which is automatically generated by the system.
* **order_id** (number, optional): Enter your order ID, which is automatically generated by the system.

* **updated_at** (date, optional): Automatically generated date when the order is updated.
* **deleted_at** (date, optional): Automatically generated date when the order is deleted.
* **deleted_by** (select_one, optional): Select "admin" or "user" to indicate who deleted the order.
