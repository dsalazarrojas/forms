# Bar Wait Time Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bar Wait Time Complaint Form is a tool designed to help you, the customer, share your experiences and provide feedback about wait times at a bar. This information will help the bar improve their services and provide a better experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, using the options provided.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the open-ended fields, such as "Specific Issues Contributing to Wait" and "Suggestions for Improvement".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Complaint Submission**: This field is used to track when you submitted this form. You can leave this field blank as it is automatically populated.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address so we can respond to your complaint or feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of Incident**: When did the wait time issue occur? Be as specific as possible, including the date and time.
* **Time of Visit**: If possible, estimate the time of day when you experienced the wait time issue.
* **Bar Name**: Enter the name of the establishment where you experienced the wait time issue.
* **Bar Location/Address**: Provide the address or location of the bar where the issue occurred.
* **Type of Service**: Select the type of service you were receiving (Drink, Food, Both, or Payment Processing).
* **Actual Wait Time (minutes)**: Estimate the actual wait time in minutes.
* **Expected Wait Time (minutes)**: If you had an expected wait time, enter it here.
* **Was Wait Time Excessive?**: Select whether the wait time was excessively long.
* **How Crowded Was the Bar?**: Choose how crowded the bar was during your visit.
* **Number of Staff Members Visible**: Count and select the number of staff members visible during your visit.
* **Staff Appeared Busy?**: Select whether the staff members appeared busy.
* **Other Customers Waiting?**: Select whether other customers were waiting in line with you.
* **Was Wait Time Communicated?**: Select whether the wait time was communicated to you.
* **How Did the Wait Affect You?**: Select all the consequences you experienced due to the wait time.
* **Specific Issues Contributing to Wait**: Describe any specific issues that contributed to the wait time.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Offer suggestions on how the bar could improve their services.
* **Desired Compensation**: If you feel you would like some form of compensation for the wait time, enter your desired outcome.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Response**: Select how you would like to be contacted for a response to your complaint or feedback.

By following this guide, you'll be able to provide detailed and informative feedback that will help the bar understand your experience and improve their services for future customers.
