# Gourmet Ingredient Starter Pack Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gourmet Ingredient Starter Pack Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place an order for our gourmet ingredient starter pack. This form helps us understand their order requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
4. Select the date you would like to receive your order in the "order_date" field.
5. Select the time you would like to receive your order in the "order_time" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them in the "customer_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Form Information** (`order_form_info`, text, required): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your order, such as special instructions or preferences.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
- **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
