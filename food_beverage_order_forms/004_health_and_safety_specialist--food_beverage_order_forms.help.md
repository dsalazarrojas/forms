# Health and Safety Specialist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify the qualifications and responsibilities of a Health and Safety Specialist. It's a comprehensive tool to assess an individual's background, skills, and experience in the field of health and safety, particularly in food safety and environmental hazards. The form will guide you through a series of questions and prompts that will help you determine if an individual meets the requirements for this role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and job title as the Health and Safety Specialist.
2. Describe your job purpose, which is to perform basic technical work in a wide range of environmental health and safety disciplines under general supervision.
3. Identify the types of work you perform on a regular basis:
	* Food Safety risk factors
	* Workplace and environmental hazards
4. Describe your experience in conducting Food and Workplace Safety inspections and investigations:
	* Compiling and analyzing data on potential hazards
	* Identifying and investigating unsafe conditions or environmental concerns
5. Explain your ability to provide technical advice and interpretation of local state and federal regulations:
	* Understanding local state and federal regulations
	* Providing guidance on government and university policies
6. Provide your education background:
	* Bachelor's degree in an arts or science field related to your experience
7. Describe your relevant experience:
	* One year of experience in Food Safety, occupational safety, and health, emergency response, and management in an environmental science or allied field
8. Mention any relevant certifications you hold:
	* REHS (Registered Environmental Health and Safety Specialist)
	* CP-FS (Certified Professional Food Safety)
	* CSP (Certified Safety Professional)
9. Outline your physical requirements:
	* Frequently stand and sit
	* Occasionally use a telephone and write by hand
	* Rarely kneel, crawl, climb ladders, reach above the shoulder, twist, bend, stoop, or squat
	* Grasp lightly or forcefully, write by hand, sort, and file paperwork or parts
	* Operate foot and/or hand controls, carry objects that weigh 40 pounds or more
10. Describe your working conditions:
	* Wear personal protective equipment as required by job assignments
	* May be exposed to high voltage electricity, radiation, or electromagnetic fields, lasers, noise above 80 dB, TWA allergens, biohazards, chemicals, asbestos, or heavy metals
	* May rarely work in confined spaces or at heights over 10 feet
11. Explain your understanding of working standards:
	* Adhere to California Vehicle Code and Stanford University driving requirements when conducting university business
12. Describe your interpersonal skills:
	* Work well with Stanford colleagues and clients
	* Interact with external organizations
13. Explain your commitment to work standards:
	* Demonstrate personal responsibility and value for safety
14. Summarize your job duties and responsibilities:
	* Note: This is a list of typical examples of work performed by positions in this job classification
	* Specific duties may vary depending on department or program needs
	* Employees may perform other duties as assigned
