# Breakfast Menu Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breakfast Menu Order Form is designed to guide customers through the steps to place their order for a breakfast menu. This form collects customer information, meal options, and payment details to ensure a seamless order process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Customer Information**
	* Enter your **Customer Full Name**.
	* Provide your **Email Address** for order confirmation.
	* Enter your **Phone Number**.
2. **Step 2: Order Details**
	* Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for your meal.
	* Choose your **Dining Option** (Dine-In, Takeout, Delivery, or Drive-Through).
3. **Step 3: Meal Options**
	* Select your **Entree Selection** (e.g., Pancakes, Waffles, etc.).
	* If desired, add any **Entree Customization** (e.g., modifications to your meal).
	* Choose your **Side Selection** (e.g., Bacon, Sausage, etc., select multiple sides).
4. **Step 4: Beverage Options**
	* Select your **Beverage Selection** (e.g., Coffee, Juice, etc., select multiple beverages).
5. **Step 5: Payment and Loyalty**
	* Select your **Payment Method** (Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Mobile Payment, or Room Charge).
	* If applicable, enter your **Loyalty Program Number**.
6. **Step 6: Additional Information**
	* If needed, add any **Special Requests** (e.g., modifications or requests not listed).
	* Indicate the **Quantity** of identical orders.
7. **Step 7: Review and Submit**
	* Double-check your information and meal options.
	* Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: For order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any questions or issues.
* **Order Date**: Date for this order.
* **Order Time**: Time for this order.
* **Dining Option**: How you will be dining (Dine-In, Takeout, Delivery, or Drive-Through).
* **Entree Selection**: Choose your main dish (e.g., Pancakes, Waffles, etc.).
* **Entree Customization**: Any modifications to your meal (optional).
* **Side Selection**: Choose your side dishes (e.g., Bacon, Sausage, etc., select multiple).
* **Beverage Selection**: Choose your drinks (e.g., Coffee, Juice, etc., select multiple).
* **Coffee Customization**: How you like your coffee (e.g., Black, With Cream, etc., optional).
* **Dietary Requirements**: Any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Special Requests**: Any special requests (e.g., modifications or requests not listed).
* **Quantity**: Number of identical orders.
* **Payment Method**: How you will pay (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Loyalty Program Number**: If you have a loyalty program account.
* **Feedback or Comments**: Any comments or feedback for us.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting your order.
* Select multiple options where applicable (e.g., sides, beverages).
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through the provided contact information.
* This form is for breakfast menu orders only; for other menu items, please use the relevant form.
