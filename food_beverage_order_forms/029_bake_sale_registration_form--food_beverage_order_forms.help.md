# Bake Sale Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers interested in participating in a bake sale event. It will be used to manage volunteer registrations, roles, and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If applicable, enter your organization or group affiliation in the "Organization or Group" field.
5. Fill in the event details:
   a. Event Name in the "Bake Sale Event Name" field.
   b. Event Date in the "Event Date" field.
   c. Event Location in the "Event Location" field.
   d. Event Start and End Times in the "Event Start Time" and "Event End Time" fields, respectively.
6. Select your volunteer roles from the list in the "Volunteer Roles You Are Interested In" field.
7. Choose your primary volunteer role from the "Primary Preferred Role" dropdown list.
8. Specify your availability for the event in the "Time Slots You Can Help" field.
9. If you have any special skills or abilities, enter them in the "Special Skills or Abilities" field.
10. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" field.
11. If you need transportation assistance, select "Yes" in the "Do You Need Transportation Assistance?" field.
12. If you can contribute baked goods, select "Yes" in the "Can You Contribute Baked Goods?" field and list any items you can contribute in the "Items You Can Contribute" field.
13. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
14. Confirm your registration and commitment by selecting "I Confirm" in the "I Confirm My Registration and Commitment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name to identify you as a volunteer.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding event details.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Organization or Group**: If you are affiliated with a specific organization or group, enter it here.
* **Bake Sale Event Name**: Enter the name of the bake sale event you are interested in participating in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the bake sale event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the bake sale event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Volunteer Roles You Are Interested In**: Select all roles you are interested in, such as baking, setup, sales, etc.
* **Primary Preferred Role**: Choose your primary role among the selected roles.
* **Time Slots You Can Help**: Choose the time slots you can commit to helping out during the event.
* **Special Skills or Abilities**: Enter any special skills or abilities you have that can contribute to the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, list them here for event planning purposes.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance?**: Select "Yes" if you need transportation assistance.
* **Can You Contribute Baked Goods?**: Select "Yes" if you can contribute baked goods.
* **Items You Can Contribute**: If you can contribute baked goods, list the items you can contribute.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **I Confirm My Registration and Commitment**: Confirm your registration and commitment to participate in the event by selecting "I Confirm".
