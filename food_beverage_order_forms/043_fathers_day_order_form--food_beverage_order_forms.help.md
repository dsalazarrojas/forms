# Fathers Day Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fathers Day Order Form is a form used to collect information about an order, specifically for Fathers Day celebrations. The form is designed to gather details about the order, the customer, and the payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details page with the required information.
	* Enter the date of the order.
	* Select the food item(s) you wish to purchase.
2. Choose the payment method for the order.
3. Enter the customer details.
	* Enter the name of the customer.
4. Check the payment amount to ensure it matches the order total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <order_details>
* **Order Details** (order_details, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the order.
### <food_item>
* **Food Item** (food_item, select_multiple, required: false): Select the food items you wish to purchase (e.g., Yes for one item, No for another).
### <order_date>
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to order on.
### <payment_method>
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required: false): Choose your payment method (e.g., Card, Cash).
### <customer_details>
* **Customer Details** (customer_details, text, required: false): Enter any details about the customer.
### <name>
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
### <order_total>
* **Order Total** (order_total, number, required: false): Check the total amount of the order.
### <payment_amount>
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required: false): Check the payment amount to ensure it matches the order total.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all applicable food items.
* Double-check the order date and customer details.
* Ensure the payment amount matches the order total.
