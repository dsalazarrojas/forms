# Food Preservation Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for ordering food preservation equipment such as freezers, dryers, or ovens for food processing purposes. Please fill out this form to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the type of food preservation equipment you need.
2. Enter your equipment model selection if applicable.
3. Specify the quantity of equipment ordered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type** (`food_preservation_equipment_type`): Select the type of preservation equipment you are ordering. Choose from:
	+ Freezer
	+ Dryer
	+ Oven
* **Equipment Model** (`food_preservation_equipment_model`): If you selected a specific type of equipment, enter the model number to ensure accurate order fulfillment. (Optional)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Specify the number of preservation equipment units you are ordering.
