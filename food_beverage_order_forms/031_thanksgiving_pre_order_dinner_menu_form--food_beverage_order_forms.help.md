# thanksgiving_pre_order_dinner_menu_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users pre-order their Thanksgiving dinner menu. It allows users to select their preferred dishes and provide any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred dishes from the "Menu" page.
2. Enter the number of guests attending in the "Guest Count" field.
3. Enter email addresses for guests on the "Email Guests" page.
4. Enter phone numbers for guests on the "Phone Guests" page.
5. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Menu** (`Select Multiple`, required: false): Select up to 5 dishes you would like to pre-order from the "turkey", "stuffing", "mashed potatoes", "cranberry sauce", and "green beans" options.
* **Guest Count** (Number, required: false): Enter the number of guests you are expecting to attend your Thanksgiving dinner.
* **Email Guests** (Email, required: false): Enter the email addresses of guests you would like to invite. You can enter as many or as few email addresses as you need.
* **Phone Guests** (Text, required: false): Enter the phone numbers of guests you would like to invite. You can enter as many or as few phone numbers as you need.
* **Special Requests** (Text, required: false): If you have any special requests or instructions for your chef, such as dietary restrictions or food allergies, enter them here.
