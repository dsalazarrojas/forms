# Takeout Subscription Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Takeout Subscription Sign Up form is designed to allow users to easily subscribe to takeout services. By filling out this form, users will be able to select their preferred subscription plan and delivery frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Select your preferred payment method and subscription plan from the provided options.
* Choose your delivery frequency and window to ensure timely and convenient takeout deliveries.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, `required`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, `false`): Enter your valid email address to receive takeout subscription updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, `false`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, `false`): Enter your home or business address for takeout delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, `false`): Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, `false`): Enter the state where your address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, `false`): Enter the zip code for takeout delivery purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, `false`): Select one or more payment methods to use for your takeout subscription (e.g., credit card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, `false`): Select your preferred takeout subscription plan (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, `false`): Select how often you would like to receive takeout deliveries (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Delivery Window** (`delivery_window`, select_one, `false`): Select a time window for your takeout deliveries (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
