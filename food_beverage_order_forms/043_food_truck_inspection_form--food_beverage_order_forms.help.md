# Food Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and evaluate food trucks based on their license status, vehicle details, and compliance with regulations. The form helps ensure that food trucks meet the required standards for safe operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers for each field based on the actual condition of the food truck being inspected.
2. Make sure to answer every question, as they are all optional.
3. For fields that require a numerical value, enter a whole number without any decimal points.
4. For fields that require a select_one or select_multiple option, choose only one option that best describes the condition.
5. Use the note field to provide any additional comments or observations about the food truck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Truck License** (`form_truck_license`): Choose 'Yes' if the food truck has a valid food license, 'No' otherwise.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`): Enter the weight of the food truck in kilograms (kg).
* **Vehicle Length** (`vehicle_length`): Enter the length of the food truck in meters (m).
* **Vehicle Width** (`vehicle_width`): Enter the width of the food truck in meters (m).
* **Vehicle Length Unit** (`vehicle_length_unit`): Choose the unit of measurement for the vehicle length (inches, feet, or meters).
* **Vehicle Height** (`vehicle_height`): Enter the height of the food truck in meters (m).
* **Engine Model** (`engine_model`): Enter the model of the food truck's engine.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of manufacture of the food truck.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`): Enter the type of engine used in the food truck (e.g., gasoline, diesel, hybrid).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of the food truck (e.g., Ford, Toyota, etc.).
* **Vehicle Year Unit** (`vehicle_year_unit`): Choose the unit of measurement for the vehicle year (years, months, weeks).
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`): Choose the fuel level of the food truck (Full, Empty, Not Applicable).
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`): Choose the type of fuel used in the food truck (Gas, Diesel, Hybrid).
* **Food Truck Registration** (`food_truck_registration`): Choose 'Yes' if the food truck is registered, 'No' otherwise.
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`): Choose 'Yes' if the driver has a valid license, 'No' otherwise.
* **Driver Experience** (`driver_experience`): Choose 'True' if the driver has experience, 'False' if not.
* **Food License** (`food_license`): Choose 'True' if the food truck has a valid food license, 'False' if not.
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`): Choose the result of the inspection (Pass, Fail).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the food truck.
* **Food Truck Inspection Form** (`food_truck_inspection_form`): This field is currently not used in the form and can be ignored.

Note that some fields may be redundant or not applicable in specific situations, but it's essential to fill out as much information as possible for accurate assessment.
