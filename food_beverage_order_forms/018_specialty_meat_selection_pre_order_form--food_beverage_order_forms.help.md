# Specialty Meat Selection Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Specialty Meat Selection Pre-Order Form is designed for customers to select and pre-order specialty meats for pickup or delivery. This form allows customers to provide their contact information, select the meat items they want, and specify their payment method. The form is used by stores to receive and process pre-orders for specialty meats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you would like to pick up your order at the store or have it delivered.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
3. Select the meat item(s) you would like to pre-order.
4. Specify the quantity of each meat item you want.
5. Add any comments or notes about your order.
6. Choose your payment method (cash, credit/debit card, or online payment).
7. Provide any additional payment details.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup or Delivery** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): Select whether you would like to pick up your order at the store or have it delivered. This field is optional, so you can choose either option.
* **Customer Name** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (id: 4, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (id: 5, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Pickup Address** (id: 7, type: text, required: false): If you selected "Pickup", enter the address where you will be picking up your order.
* **Delivery Date** (id: 8, type: date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to have your order delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (id: 9, type: time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to have your order delivered.
* **Meat Item** (id: 10, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the meat item(s) you would like to pre-order from the list of options. You can choose multiple items.
* **Quantity** (id: 11, type: number, required: false): Enter the quantity of each meat item you want to pre-order.
* **Comments** (id: 12, type: text, required: false): Add any comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Comments** (id: 13, type: text, required: false): Add any comments or notes from the customer for the store.
* **Customer Notes** (id: 14, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Store Name** (id: 15, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Address** (id: 16, type: text, required: false): Enter the address of the store.
* **Store Phone** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **Store Notes** (id: 18, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the store.
* **Special Instructions** (id: 19, type: text, required: false): Add any special instructions for the store.
* **Store Email** (id: 20, type: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the store.
* **Store Manager** (id: 21, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Phone** (id: 22, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the store manager.
* **Payment Method** (id: 23, type: select_one, required: false): Select the payment method you would like to use (cash, credit/debit card, or online payment).
* **Payment Details** (id: 24, type: text, required: false): Add any additional payment details.
* **Store Signature** (id: 25, type: note, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter all required fields.
* If you are selecting multiple meat items, enter the quantity for each item.
* If you have any special instructions for the store, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* If you have any comments or notes for the customer, please enter them in the "Customer Comments" field.
