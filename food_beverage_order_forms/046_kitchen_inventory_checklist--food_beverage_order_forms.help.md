# kitchen_inventory_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

The kitchen inventory checklist is a tool to help keep track of the current state of the kitchen's food and beverage supplies, ensuring that all necessary items are present and up to date. This form is intended to be used by kitchen staff or administrators to conduct a thorough inventory check, identify any discrepancies or missing items, and facilitate ordering and restocking processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully, ensuring all fields are understood before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, taking note of any discrepancies or missing items.
3. Use the "Notes Section" to provide additional comments or observations about the inventory state.
4. Use the "Notes" field to add any specific notes or comments about the inventory check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Section** (`id: 1`): This section is used to record the current state of the main kitchen inventory, including but not limited to:
	+ Checkboxes for key items such as staples (e.g., flour, sugar, etc.)
	+ Space to add any additional items not covered in the checkboxes
* **Notes Section** (`id: 2`): A dedicated space for users to add any notes or comments about the inventory state.
* **Notes** (`id: 3`): A field for users to add any specific notes or comments about the inventory check. This can include details about missing items, expired or near-expired products, or other relevant observations.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate in completing the form to ensure a reliable inventory count.
* Take note of any discrepancies or missing items, and make a note of them in the "Notes" section.
* Use the "Notes Section" to keep a running log of any comments or observations about the inventory state.
* If you're unsure about a particular item or its status, use the "Notes" field to clarify.
