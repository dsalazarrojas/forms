# Mobile Food Vendor Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that have occurred during mobile food vendor events. Its purpose is to collect detailed information about the incident, including event details, vendor information, and the reporter's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed.
2. Enter a detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Details** field.
3. Provide information about the event, including the date, time, and location, in the **Event Info** field.
4. Enter details about the vendor involved, including their name and contact information, in the **Vendor Info** field.
5. Enter your name and contact information in the **Incident Reporter** field, so we can follow up with you if needed.
6. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Incident Details** (Text Field)
* A detailed description of the incident that occurred during the event.
* Please provide as much information as possible about what happened.
* If you have any supporting documentation or photos, please attach them to the form.

### **Event Info** (Text Field)
* Information about the event where the incident occurred.
* Please include the date, time, and location of the event.
* If you have any other relevant details, such as the type of event or the number of attendees, please include them as well.

### **Vendor Info** (Text Field)
* Details about the vendor involved in the incident.
* Please include their name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
* If the vendor is not a registered vendor, please provide as much information as possible about them.

### **Incident Reporter** (Text Field)
* Your name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
* Please include any other relevant contact information, such as a secondary email address or a messaging app contact.
* Your contact information will be used to follow up with you if needed.

## Tips
* Please complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your input is crucial in helping us improve our events and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all.
