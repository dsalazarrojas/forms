# Seafood Platter Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for pre-ordering a seafood platter. You can choose to pick up the order or have it delivered. Please fill in the form accurately to ensure a smooth ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your preferred delivery date (if you're choosing delivery) or pickup option (if you're picking up in-store).
* Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Card, or PayPal).
* Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Confirm your email address and phone number.
* Agree to the terms and conditions.
* Provide any additional order instructions or terms and conditions.
* Finally, confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seafood Platter Pre Order**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Pickup**: Choose between Pickup, Delivery, or Other - Please specify for alternative pickup arrangements.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter an alternative date if you cannot make it to the delivery date.
* **Pickup Option**: Choose when you'd like to pick up your order, or choose Other to specify alternative pickup details.
* **Payment Method**: Choose between Cash, Card, or PayPal for payment.
* **Payment Details**: If you've chosen to pay with Cash, enter any additional payment details required.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the seafood platter you'd like to order.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order, such as special requests.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your name for contact and order reference purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter your city for delivery purposes.
* **State**: Enter your state for delivery purposes.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Message**: Enter any additional message or instructions for your order.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address you've entered is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm that the phone number you've entered is accurate.
* **Agree to Terms**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm you agree to terms and conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Order Instructions**: Enter any additional instructions for your order.
* **Terms**: Enter any additional terms and conditions relevant to your order.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter any additional terms and conditions relevant to your order.

Please fill in the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth ordering experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
