# Food Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Pre Order Form is designed to help customers pre-order food items in advance of their visit to the restaurant. This form allows customers to provide information about their food preferences, order details, and contact information, which is used by restaurant staff to process and confirm their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. On the first page, enter your order title and provide any additional order details in the text field.
3. On the second page, select your preferred food items from the options provided.
4. On the third page, enter any additional comments or instructions in the note field.
5. On the fourth page, enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Review and confirm your order details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Food Pre Order Form Page 1
* **title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your order, such as "Lunch for 2 People".
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your order, such as special requests or dietary restrictions.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required/optional): Select the date and time of your visit to the restaurant.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including name and phone number.

### Page 2: Food Pre Order Form Page 2
* **Additional Food Selections** (`food_selections`, select multiple, required/optional): Select additional food items from the options provided. This field allows you to choose multiple items.
* **Primary Food Selection** (`food_selections_2`, select one, required/optional): Select your primary food item from the options provided.
* **Additional Food Selections** (`food_selections_3`, select multiple, required/optional): Select additional food items from the options provided. This field allows you to choose multiple items.

### Page 3: Food Pre Order Form Page 3
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order.

### Page 4: Food Pre Order Form Page 4
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter your order title and details accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
* Select your food preferences carefully to ensure that you get the right food items.
* Enter your contact information accurately to ensure that we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* Review your order details carefully before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
* If you have any additional comments or instructions, be sure to enter them in the note field.
