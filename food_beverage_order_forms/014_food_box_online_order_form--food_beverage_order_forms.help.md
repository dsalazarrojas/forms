## Food Box Online Order Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to fulfill their food box orders. It's essential to have all the required information to process your order efficiently.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Order Details section with your order information.
2. Provide your delivery information in the Delivery Info section.
3. Enter your payment information in the Payment Info section.
4. Review your order summary in the Order Summary section.
5. Select the food option you'd like to order from the Food section.
6. Enter the quantity of the food you'd like to order.
7. Enter your email address in the Email section.
8. Enter your phone number in the Phone section.
9. (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments in the Note section.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your order, including any specific instructions or preferences you have.
* **Delivery Info** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Enter your delivery address and any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Info** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Enter your payment information, such as your method of payment and payment details.
* **Order Summary** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Review your order details to ensure everything is correct.
* **Food** (Page 1, Select One, Optional): Choose the food option you'd like to order from the list below:
	+ Apple
	+ Banana
	+ Orange
* **Quantity** (Page 2, Number, Optional): Enter the quantity of the food you'd like to order.
* **Email** (Page 2, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Page 2, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (Page 2, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
