# Farm Egg And Dairy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for farm egg and dairy orders. Please use this form to accurately gather and process your orders.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out all required fields, which are marked as required.
- Ensure that the delivery date and time are accurate and convenient for you.
- Select the payment method that best suits your needs.
- Enter the correct total amount of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required): This is where you'll provide your name and any other identifying information.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Please provide your contact phone number and address for delivery or communication purposes.
- **Select Items 1** (`order_items_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one item from the list of available items, such as Apple, Banana, or Orange.
- **Select Items 2** (`order_items_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose another item from the list of available items.
- **Select Items 3** (`order_items_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the third item from the list of available items.
- **Order Description** (`order_description`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of your order, including any specific preferences or instructions.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for delivery or pickup.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time for delivery or pickup.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred payment method, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Debit Card.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Include any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Double-check your contact information for delivery purposes.
- Choose a suitable delivery date and time that suits your schedule.
- Be aware of your payment method options and ensure you have the necessary funds.
- Use the notes field to provide any additional information about your order.
