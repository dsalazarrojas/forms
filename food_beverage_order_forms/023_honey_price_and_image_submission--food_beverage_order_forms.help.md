# honey_price_and_image_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "honey_price_and_image_submission" form is used to collect the price and image URL of a honey product for e-commerce or online marketplace purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is required to be filled in to ensure you can be contacted and your identity verified.
*   Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field is not required but it's recommended to be filled in so we can contact you with further information or updates about your purchase.
*   If you want to provide a price, enter it in the "Price" field. This field is not required and can be left blank if you don't want to specify a price for your honey product.
*   Enter the **Image URL** of your honey product in the "Image URL" field. This field is not required but it's recommended to be filled in so we can display a proper image of your product.
*   Enter a short **Image Alt Text** in the "Image Alt Text" field. This field is not required but it's recommended to be filled in so we can describe your image properly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Your first name will be used to verify your identity and contact you about your purchase.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Your email will be used to contact you with further information or updates about your purchase.
*   **Price** (`Price`, `number`, optional): The price of your honey product, if you want to specify one.
*   **Image URL** (`Image URL`, `text`, optional): The URL of the image of your honey product.
*   **Image Alt Text** (`Image Alt Text`, `text`, optional): A short text describing your image, this will be displayed if your image cannot be loaded.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that you are authorized to access this form and fill it out for a valid reason.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.
