# Standard Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standard Catering Order Form is a template for collecting information from clients for catering orders. This form helps to gather essential details such as contact and delivery information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2.  Complete each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3.  Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Page**: This section is not required and is for internal use.
- **Contact Details**: This section is for providing contact information, including name, address, and phone number.
  * **Name**: Your name or the name of the contact person.
  * **Address**: Your physical address.
  * **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Delivery Details**: This section is for providing information about the delivery of your order.
  * **Address**: The address where your order should be delivered.
  * **City**: The city where your order should be delivered.
  * **State**: The state where your order should be delivered.
  * **Zip**: The zip code where your order should be delivered.
  * **Country**: The country where your order should be delivered.
* **Order Details**: Select all the order items you would like to order.
* **Order Notes**: Any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment method for your order.
* **Special Instructions**: Any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Order Total**: The total cost of your order.
* **Order Subtotal**: The subtotal of your order.
* **Order Tax**: Any tax applied to your order.
* **Order Balance**: The balance remaining after payment.
* **Signature**: The date and signature of the contact person.
* **Contact Signature**: The date and signature of the person submitting the order.
* **Delivery Signature**: The date and signature of the person receiving the delivery.
* **Payment Signature**: The date and signature of the person making the payment.
* **Client Signature**: The date and signature of the client.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Contact Fax**: Your fax number.
* **Order Form**: The form used to complete this form.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
- Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.
- Use a clear and legible handwriting or typing for all fields.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the catering service staff.
