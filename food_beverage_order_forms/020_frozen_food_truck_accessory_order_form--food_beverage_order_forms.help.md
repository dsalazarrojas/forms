# Frozen Food Truck Accessory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frozen Food Truck Accessory Order Form is used to gather information about the accessories needed for a frozen food truck. This form helps to ensure that the truck is properly equipped with the necessary accessories to operate efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the frozen food truck accessory options you need by choosing from the multiple selection options on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, select the truck model from the drop-down options.
3. Enter the order date and time in the respective fields on Page 3.
4. Describe the accessories needed in the text field on Page 4.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the text field on Page 5.
6. Enter the owner's email and phone number in the respective fields on Page 6.
7. Enter the name and/or ID of the assigned tool or technician in the text field on Page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **frozen_food_truck_accessory_order_form_label** (`yes`, `no`, multiple selection): This section allows you to choose from multiple frozen food truck accessory options. Please select all the options that apply.
* **truck_model** (`1`, `2`, `3`, select one): Select the model of the frozen food truck.
* **order_date** (date): Enter the date of the order in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **order_time** (time): Enter the time of the order in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **accessories_needed** (text): Describe the accessories needed for the frozen food truck in this text field.
* **notes** (text): Add any additional notes or comments about the order in this text field.
* **email** (email): Enter the email of the owner or point of contact for the order.
* **phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the owner or point of contact for the order.
* **owner** (text): Enter the name and/or ID of the assigned tool or technician.
* **assigned_tool** (text): Enter the name and/or ID of the assigned tool or technician.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all the necessary frozen food truck accessories to ensure proper operation.
* Enter the correct truck model to ensure the correct accessories are ordered.
* Double-check the order date and time to avoid any confusion.
* Describe the accessories needed clearly and concisely.
* Add any additional notes or comments to help with the order process.
* Enter the correct contact information to ensure proper communication.
