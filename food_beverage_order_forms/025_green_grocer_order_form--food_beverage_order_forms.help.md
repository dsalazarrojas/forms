# Green Grocer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Grocer Order Form is used to capture customer information and order details for green groceries delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your pickup location from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your customer name in the text field.
3. Enter the quantity of produce you would like to order.
4. Select the type of produce you would like to order by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your desired delivery date and time.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the text fields.
7. Add any special instructions for the order in the note field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the total cost of the order.
10. Assign a tool or equipment for the order in the text field.
11. Assign a salesperson or user for the order in the text field.
12. Enter a category for the order or produce in the text field.
13. Enter output file information in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup location** (`pickup_location`, `select_one`): Select one of the available options (Delivery or Pickup) for where you would like to pick up your order.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, `text`): Enter your name to associate with the order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`): Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Type of produce** (`type_of_produce`, `select_multiple`): Select multiple options from the list of available produce types (Carrots, Apples, Bananas, Oranges).
* **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`, `date`): Enter the desired delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery time** (`delivery_time`, `time`): Enter the desired delivery time for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`): Add any special instructions for the order.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): Select one of the available payment methods (Credit Card, Cash, PayPal).
* **Total cost** (`total_cost`, `number`): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`): Assign a tool or equipment for the order.
* **Assigned user** (`assigned_user`, `text`): Assign a salesperson or user for the order.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`): Enter a category for the order or produce.
* **Output file** (`output_file`, `text`): Enter output file information for the order.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`): Enter any other information for the order.

Please note that some fields are not required, so you can skip them if they do not apply to your order.
