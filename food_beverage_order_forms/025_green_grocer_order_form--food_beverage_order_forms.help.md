The Green Grocer Order Form is used to capture customer information and order details for green groceries delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your pickup location from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your customer name in the text field.
3. Enter the quantity of produce you would like to order.
4. Select the type of produce you would like to order by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your desired delivery date and time.
6. Enter your email address and phone number in the text fields.
7. Add any special instructions for the order in the note field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the total cost of the order.
10. Assign a tool or equipment for the order in the text field.
11. Assign a salesperson or user for the order in the text field.
12. Enter a category for the order or produce in the text field.
13. Enter output file information in the text field.
