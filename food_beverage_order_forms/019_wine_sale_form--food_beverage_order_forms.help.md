# Wine Sale Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wine Sale Form is designed for wine customers who want to purchase wine online or offline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Wine Sale Form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required and optional fields with the information requested.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
3. Review your form before submission to ensure it is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sale Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your wine purchase, such as "Merlot for a special occasion" or "Red wine for a dinner party."
- **Customer Info** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
- **Order Details** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide detailed information about your wine order, such as the type of wine, quantity, and any special requests.
- **Shipping Address** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including your name, street address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Payment Info** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your payment information, including your payment method and any other required payment details.
- **Review Info** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or reviews for your wine purchase.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submission.
- Make sure to provide accurate payment information to avoid any delays in your order.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact our customer support for assistance.
