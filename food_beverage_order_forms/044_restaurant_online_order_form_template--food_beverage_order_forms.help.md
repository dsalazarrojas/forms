## Restaurant Online Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restaurant Online Order Form Template is a tool designed to help customers place orders for food and beverages at your restaurant. This form ensures that you gather all necessary information for your team to prepare and deliver the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select your pickup location using the dropdown menu.
* Choose a date and time for your order.
* Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email).
* Indicate whether you want to have your order delivered or pick it up.
* Choose the type of food you want (e.g. breakfast, lunch, dinner).
* Select the menu items you desire (e.g. appetizers, entrees, etc.).
* Specify your preferred payment method (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
* Enter any additional payment details.
* Enter the subtotal of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Location**: Please select whether your order will be picked up in person or at the restaurant.
* **Date**: Please choose a date for your order. You can select any date within the next 7 days.
* **Time**: Choose a time for your order. You can select any time within the next hour.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can notify you of your order status.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Please select a date for your delivery order. You can choose any date within the next 7 days.
* **Pickup Time**: Choose a time for your delivery order. You can select any time within the next hour.
* **Order Type**: Indicate whether you want to pick up, have delivered, or both.
* **Food Type**: Select the type of food you are interested in (e.g. vegetarian, meat-based, etc.).
* **Menu Items**: Select the menu items you would like to order. You can choose multiple items.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details if necessary.
* **Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Discount**: Enter any applicable discounts for your order.
* **Taxes**: Enter any applicable taxes for your order.
* **Tip**: Enter any additional tip for your order.
* **Subtotal**: This field is automatically calculated and cannot be edited.
