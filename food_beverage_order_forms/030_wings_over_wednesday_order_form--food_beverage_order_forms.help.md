# Wings Over Wednesday Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wings Over Wednesday Order Form is used to record orders for food and beverage items. This form is designed to gather necessary information from customers for efficient order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required information as follows:
* Select a date to record the order date.
* Enter the customer name as requested.
* Enter the customer's contact information including phone and email.
* Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the order is for "Order Type", "Report Type", or "Active/Inactive Report Status".
* Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the wing size and flavor options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order. This can be a date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer as it appears on their identification.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Order Type**: Indicate if the order is for an "Yes" or "No".
* **Report Type**: Indicate if the report is "Yes" or "No".
* **Report Status**: Select the "Active" or "Inactive" report status.
* **Report Status (2)**: Select the "Active" or "Inactive" report status (this field may be redundant and should match the report status above).
* **Wing Size**: Indicate if the wing size is "Yes" or "No".
* **Wing Count**: Enter the number of wings ordered. This should be a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Wing Flavors**: Indicate the flavor(s) of wing(s) ordered. Choose one or multiple options depending on the selection.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor for assistance.
* Make sure to enter accurate information as it will be used for order processing and delivery.
* Review and confirm your order details carefully to avoid errors.
