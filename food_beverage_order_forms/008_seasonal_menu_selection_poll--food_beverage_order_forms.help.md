# Seasonal Menu Selection Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user preferences for a seasonal menu selection, allowing users to choose their desired menu items, flavors, and other preferences for food and beverage delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred menu items from the list on page 1, "Seasonal Menu Selection".
2. Choose your preferred seasonal flavors from the dropdown menu on page 1.
3. If you have any special drink orders, enter them in the text field on page 2, "Drink Orders".
4. Enter your email address for delivery and promotions on page 3, "Email Address".
5. Enter your phone number for delivery on page 3, "Phone Number".
6. Select your preferred delivery time on page 4, "Delivery Time".
7. Select your preferred delivery date on page 4, "Delivery Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Seasonal Menu Selection (Page 1)
* **Seasonal Menu Selection** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to choose multiple menu items from our seasonal selection. Select all the items that interest you.

### Menu Items (Page 2)
* **Menu Items** (`text`, required: false): This field is not currently populated with options, but you can enter any menu items you're interested in here.

### Seasonal Flavors (Page 3)
* **Seasonal Flavors** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of our seasonal flavors: Apple Cider Donut Holes, Pumpkin Spice Latte, or Maple Bacon Donut.

### Drink Orders (Page 3)
* **Drink Orders** (`text`, required: false): If you have any special drink orders, such as "no sugar" or "latte-style coffee", enter them here.

### Delivery Info (Page 4)
*   *This field is intended for internal use, not visible to users*

### Email Address (Page 4)
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive delivery updates and promotions.

### Phone Number (Page 4)
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for delivery confirmation and updates.

### Delivery Time (Page 4)
* **Delivery Time** (`time`, required: false): Select your preferred delivery time from the available options.

### Delivery Date (Page 4)
* **Delivery Date** (`date`, required: false): Select your preferred delivery date from the available options.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a delivery date and time that works best for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [support email].
* We will do our best to accommodate your preferences and deliver your order as selected.
