# Drive Thru BBQ Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the collection of customer orders at a drive-thru BBQ location. It gathers the order details, customer signature, and manager or staff signature for verification and order accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order note on the **Order Note** field, if you need to include any additional information about your order.
2. Sign your name on the **Customer Signature** field, indicating that you have received and accepted your order.
3. Sign your name on the **Manager Signature** field, verifying that the order has been accurately received and processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Note** (`order_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or additional information about your order, such as modifications or special requests.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have received and accepted your order.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to verify that the order has been accurately received and processed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and legibly, especially when signing your name.
- If you need to make changes to your order, use the **Order Note** field to indicate the modifications.
- Ensure that the manager or staff member verifies your order by obtaining their signature on the **Manager Signature** field.
