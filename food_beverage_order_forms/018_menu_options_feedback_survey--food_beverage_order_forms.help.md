# Menu Options Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their dining experience at our restaurant. Your input will help us improve our menu options, services, and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Choose your options from the available choices.
3. Type in your answers in the provided text fields.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What would you like to do? (Select One)
 * What would you like to do? (Select One): This question helps us understand your interest in our menu options. Please select one of the following options:
  * Yes: You are interested in exploring our menu options.
  * No: You are not interested in our menu options.
  * Other: Please provide a brief description of your interest or preference.

### 2. Do you prefer vegetarian options? (Select Multiple)
 * Do you prefer vegetarian options? (Select Multiple): This question helps us understand your dietary preferences. Please select all that apply:
  * Vegetarian option 1: You prefer vegetarian option 1.
  * Vegetarian option 2: You prefer vegetarian option 2.
  * No: You do not prefer vegetarian options.

### 3. How many times have you visited our restaurant in the past month?
 * How many times have you visited our restaurant in the past month?: This question helps us understand your frequency of visits. Please enter a number.

### 4. Contact Info
 * Contact Info: Please enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with special offers and updates.

### 5. Do you want to receive promotional emails from us? (Select One)
 * Do you want to receive promotional emails from us? (Select One): This question helps us understand your interest in promotional emails. Please select one of the following options:
  * Yes: You are interested in receiving promotional emails.
  * No: You are not interested in receiving promotional emails.

### 6. How would you rate our menu? (Select Multiple)
 * How would you rate our menu? (Select Multiple): This question helps us understand your opinion about our menu. Please select all that apply:
  * Rating 1: You rate our menu 1 out of 5.
  * Rating 2: You rate our menu 2 out of 5.
  * Poor: Our menu is poor.
  * Excellent: Our menu is excellent.

### 7. What is your phone number?
 * What is your phone number?: This question helps us understand your contact information. Please enter your phone number.

### 8. Do you have any food allergies?
 * Do you have any food allergies?: This question helps us understand your dietary restrictions. Please select all that apply:
  * Allergies 1: You have allergies 1.
  * Allergies 2: You have allergies 2.
  * Allergies 3: You have allergies 3.

### 9. How would you rate the taste of our food? (Number)
 * How would you rate the taste of our food? (Number): This question helps us understand your opinion about our food taste. Please enter a number between 1 and 5.

### 10. Do you have any additional comments or feedback?
 * Do you have any additional comments or feedback?: This question gives you the opportunity to provide any additional thoughts or suggestions about our menu or services. Please enter your comments in the text field.

### 11. Submit
 * Submit: Please click on this button to submit your feedback.

Tips:
* Please answer all questions truthfully to help us improve our services.
* You can provide additional comments or feedback if you have any further thoughts or suggestions.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort to help us improve!
