# Flavor Profile Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your flavor profile, which is a set of preferences related to taste, texture, and other aspects of food and beverage consumption. Please answer the questions accurately to provide a comprehensive view of your flavor profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form sections below.
2. For each section, choose your answers based on your genuine preferences.
3. Don't hesitate to add comments if you have anything else you'd like to mention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief welcome message to introduce the form and explain its purpose.
* **Flavor Preferences** (`Flavor Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the flavors you are interested in. For example, "Yes" for flavors you like, "No" for flavors you dislike.
* **Flavor Profile** (`Flavor Profile`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the flavor profile that best describes your current state. For example, "Option 1," "Option 2," "Option 3," "Option 4," or "Option 5."
* **Taste Preferences** (`Taste Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the tastes you prefer.
* **Texture Preferences** (`Texture Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the textures you prefer.
* **Color Preferences** (`Color Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the colors you prefer.
* **Aroma Preferences** (`Aroma Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the aromas you prefer.
* **Which of the flavor profiles are you most interested in?** (`Flavor Profile Assessment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the flavor profile you are most interested in.
* **Favorite Flavors** (`Favorite Flavors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the flavors you like.
* **Which flavors do you?** (`Flavor Profiles`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field seems to be redundant and is intended to capture the same information as "Favorite Flavors." Please choose all the flavors you like.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please add them here.
* **Which of the flavor profiles are you most interested in?** (`Flavor Preferences Important`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the flavor profile you are most interested in.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`Additional Comments 2`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please add them here.
* **Submission Comments** (`Submission Comments`, `text`, required: false): This field seems to be intended for comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submission Comments 2** (`Submission Comments 2`, `text`, required: false): This field is redundant and intended for the same purpose as "Submission Comments."
* **Submission Comments 3** (`Submission Comments 3`, `text`, required: false): This field is also intended for comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submission Comments 4** (`Submission Comments 4`, `text`, required: false): Another redundant field for comments or notes.
* **Submission Comments 5** (`Submission Comments 5`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for comments or notes.
* **Submission Comments 6** (`Submission Comments 6`, `text`, required: false): Another field for comments or notes.
* **Submission Comments 7** (`Submission Comments 7`, `text`, required: false): The last of the redundant comments fields.

Please note that this guide assumes the duplicate fields are meant for different stages of the assessment process or different types of comments. If this is not the case, please provide further clarification.
