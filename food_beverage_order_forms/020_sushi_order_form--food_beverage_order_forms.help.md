# Sushi Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sushi Order Form is designed for customers to provide their pickup location, time, name, phone number, and any comments for their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your pickup location in the "Pickup Location" field.
2. Select the time you would like to pickup your order in the "Pickup Time" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you have any comments or special requests, enter them in the "Comments" field.
6. Finally, select the date you would like to pickup your order in the "Pickup Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location where you would like to pickup your order.
* **Pickup Time** (time, required: false): Select the time you would like to pickup your order.
* **Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name so we can know who to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Comments** (note, required: false): If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
* **Pickup Date** (date, required: false): Select the date you would like to pickup your order.
