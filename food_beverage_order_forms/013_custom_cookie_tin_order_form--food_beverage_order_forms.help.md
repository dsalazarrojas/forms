# Custom Cookie Tin Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a custom cookie tin order. Fill out this form to create a unique cookie tin with your preferred specifications, including the type of cookies, tin design, color, and price. You can also add any special requests and include a personal message for the customer. The form is divided into various sections to help you navigate the process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the tin type (Apple, Cherry, or Chocolate).
2. Choose the type of cookies you want in your tin (Sugar Cookies, Snickerdoodles, or Peanut Butter).
3. Choose the size of the tin (12, 24, 36, or 48).
4. Select the tin color (Red, Blue, or Green).
5. Select the tin design (Floral, Geometric, or Abstract).
6. Enter the order date and time.
7. Enter the customer's email address.
8. Enter the customer's phone number.
9. Select whether to include tin shipping (Yes or No).
10. Enter any special requests or instructions for the tin.
11. Enter a personal message for the customer.
12. Enter the tin price.
13. Enter the customer's address.
14. Select the payment method (Credit, Debit, or Cash).
15. Select whether to include payment terms (Yes or No).
16. Enter the total payment amount.
17. Select the payment status (Active or Inactive).
18. Enter any additional customer information.
19. Upload a cookie tin image.
20. Enter the customer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_form** (Order Form): A field to enter any additional information about the order.
* **customer_name** (Customer Name): Enter the name of the customer who is placing the order.
* **tin_type** (Tin Type): Select the type of tin you want to create, such as Apple, Cherry, or Chocolate.
* **cookie_type** (Cookie Type): Choose the type of cookies you want in your tin, such as Sugar Cookies, Snickerdoodles, or Peanut Butter.
* **tin_size** (Tin Size): Select the size of the tin, such as 12, 24, 36, or 48.
* **tin_color** (Tin Color): Choose the color of the tin, such as Red, Blue, or Green.
* **tin_design** (Tin Design): Select the design of the tin, such as Floral, Geometric, or Abstract.
* **order_date** (Order Date): Enter the date you want to place the order.
* **order_time** (Order Time): Enter the time you want to place the order.
* **customer_email** (Customer Email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone** (Customer Phone): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **tin_price** (Tin Price): Enter the price of the tin.
* **customer_address** (Customer Address): Enter the customer's address.
* **tin_shipping** (Tin Shipping): Select whether to include shipping with the tin (Yes or No).
* **special_requests** (Special Requests): Enter any special requests or instructions for the tin.
* **tin_message** (Tin Message): Enter a personal message for the customer.
* **tin_note** (Tin Note): Enter any notes about the tin.
* **payment_method** (Payment Method): Select the payment method (Credit, Debit, or Cash).
* **payment_terms** (Payment Terms): Select whether to include payment terms (Yes or No).
* **payment_total** (Payment Total): Enter the total payment amount.
* **payment_status** (Payment Status): Select the payment status (Active or Inactive).
* **customer_info** (Customer Info): Enter any additional customer information.
* **cookie_tin_image** (Cookie Tin Image): Upload a cookie tin image.
* **customer_signature** (Customer Signature): Enter the customer's signature.
