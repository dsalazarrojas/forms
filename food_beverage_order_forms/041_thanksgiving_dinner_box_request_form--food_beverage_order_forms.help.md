# thanksgiving_dinner_box_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide information for a Thanksgiving dinner box request. Fill it out to let us know your details and preferences for this special occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Guest Count, Contact Number, and Email) to help us get in touch with you.
2. If you have any additional message or instructions for us, type them in the Message field.
3. Click Submit to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thanksgiving Dinner Request** (`thanksgiving_dinner_request`, `text`, Optional): Type in your preferred Thanksgiving dinner request. (e.g., "Turkey and Mashed Potatoes")
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, Optional): Enter the number of guests you're expecting for dinner.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter a contact number where we can reach you for any further information or questions.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, Optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Submit** (`thanksgiving_dinner_box_request_form_submit`, `text`, Optional): Click this button to send your request.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely for us to process your request efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
