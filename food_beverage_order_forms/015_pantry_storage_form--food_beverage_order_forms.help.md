# Pantry Storage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pantry Storage Form is designed to record and track the storage details of items in the pantry. This form helps staff members to accurately document the storage location, date, type of item, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the storage location where the item is stored from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the date of storage in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
3. Choose the type of item being stored (e.g., Item, Non-Item, Service, or Other).
4. Enter the quantity of the item being stored.
5. Add any additional notes about the item and storage process (optional).
6. Select the contact method used to communicate with the person responsible for the storage (e.g., Phone, Email, In-person).
7. Enter the phone number or email address of the contact person (optional).
8. Enter the name of the item being stored.
9. Enter the time of storage in the format of HH:MM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`): Select the storage location where the item is stored from the drop-down menu.
* **Date of Storage** (`date_of_storage`): Enter the date of storage in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Storage Type** (`storage_type`): Choose the type of item being stored (e.g., Item, Non-Item, Service, or Other).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of the item being stored.
* **Notes** (`storage_notes`): Add any additional notes about the item and storage process.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select the contact method used to communicate with the person responsible for the storage (e.g., Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter the phone number or email address of the contact person (optional).
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the email address of the contact person (optional).
* **Item Name** (`item_name`): Enter the name of the item being stored.
* **Storage Time** (`storage_time`): Enter the time of storage in the format of HH:MM.
* **Storage Location 2** (`storage_location_2`): Select additional storage locations where the item may be stored (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct storage type and location to accurately reflect the item's category and storage place.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes and item names.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the person responsible for storage or the system administrator for guidance.
* Regularly review and update stored information to ensure accuracy and consistency.
