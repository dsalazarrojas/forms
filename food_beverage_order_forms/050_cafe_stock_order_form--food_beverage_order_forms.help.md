# Cafe Stock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering stock and supplies for your cafe. It allows staff members to submit a standardized order for cafe supplies, including coffee beans, milk, and cleaning products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the **Order Reference Number**, which is a unique identifier for your order.
2. Enter your **Staff Member Name** so we know who is ordering.
3. Select the **Date of Order**, which is the date you require the supplies to be delivered.
4. Choose your **Delivery Priority**:
	* Routine: Standard delivery schedule (7-10 business days)
	* Urgent - Low Stock: Expedited delivery (3-5 business days)
	* Critical - Out of Stock: Express delivery (same day or next business day)
5. Fill in the quantities of each required supply:
	* **Espresso Beans (Total kg)**: Enter the total quantity of coffee beans you require.
	* **Single Origin Beans (Total kg)**: Enter the total quantity of single-origin coffee beans you require.
	* **Whole Milk and Skim (Liters)**: Enter the total quantity of whole milk and skim you require.
	* **Alternative Milks (Oat/Almond/Soy)**: Enter the total quantity of alternative milks you require.
	* **Paper Cups and Lids (Sleeves)**: Enter the quantity of paper cups and lids you require. Specify different sizes in the **Additional Notes** field.
	* **Sugar Packs and Stirrers (Boxes)**: Enter the quantity of sugar packs and stirrers you require.
	* **Fresh Croissants (Units)**: Enter the quantity of fresh croissants you require.
	* **Other Bakery Items**: List other bakery items you require, along with their quantities.
	* **Espresso Machine Cleaning Powder (Units)**: Enter the quantity of cleaning powder you require.
	* **Multi-surface and Glass Cleaner**: Enter the quantity of cleaning supplies you require.
6. Add any **Additional Stock Notes**, such as specific instructions for suppliers or delivery windows.
7. Enter the **Estimated Total Cost**, which is for internal tracking purposes only.
8. Finally, have your **Manager Approval** signed by the manager or person responsible for approving orders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Reference Number**: Unique identifier for your order.
* **Staff Member Name**: Name of the staff member submitting the order.
* **Date of Order**: Date you require the supplies to be delivered.
* **Delivery Priority**: Select the priority level for your delivery:
	+ Routine: Standard delivery schedule (7-10 business days)
	+ Urgent - Low Stock: Expedited delivery (3-5 business days)
	+ Critical - Out of Stock: Express delivery (same day or next business day)
* **Espresso Beans (Total kg)**: Quantity of coffee beans you require.
* **Single Origin Beans (Total kg)**: Quantity of single-origin coffee beans you require.
* **Whole Milk and Skim (Liters)**: Quantity of whole milk and skim you require.
* **Alternative Milks (Oat/Almond/Soy)**: Quantity of alternative milks you require.
* **Paper Cups and Lids (Sleeves)**: Quantity of paper cups and lids you require. Specify different sizes in the **Additional Notes** field.
* **Sugar Packs and Stirrers (Boxes)**: Quantity of sugar packs and stirrers you require.
* **Fresh Croissants (Units)**: Quantity of fresh croissants you require.
* **Other Bakery Items**: List other bakery items you require, along with their quantities.
* **Espresso Machine Cleaning Powder (Units)**: Quantity of cleaning powder you require.
* **Multi-surface and Glass Cleaner**: Quantity of cleaning supplies you require.
* **Specific Instructions for Suppliers**: Any additional notes for suppliers or delivery windows.
* **Estimated Total Cost**: Internal tracking only.
* **Manager Approval**: Name of person approving the order.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in the quantities, as this will affect the delivery schedule and availability.
* Make sure to include all required supplies in the **Other Bakery Items** field.
* If you have specific instructions for suppliers, include them in the **Additional Stock Notes** field.
* Ensure your **Manager Approval** is signed by the manager or person responsible for approving orders.
