# food_truck_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food_truck_order_form is a form designed for customers to place orders at a food truck. It collects information to manage and track orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about your order by filling in the "Order Details" field.
2. Select whether you want to make a "Menu Selection" by choosing from the available options.
3. Enter the date and time of your order using the "Date and Time" field.
4. Enter your name and email address (if required).
5. If needed, add any "Comments" about your order.
6. Enter the total amount of your order.
7. Confirm the "Order Status" of your order.
8. Decide whether to "Submit" your order.
9. Confirm your email address if it's required.
10. Select the "Assigned User" and "Assigned Form" for your order.
11. Optionally, add any "Notes" about your order.
12. If required, fill in the "Assigned Form ID" and "Assigned Date and Time" fields.
13. Choose whether to "Assign User 2" and fill in the "Assigned Form ID 2" and "Assigned Time 2" fields.
14. Choose whether to "Assign Form 2" and add any "Notes 2" about your order.
15. Select the "Assigned Form IDs" and fill in the "Assigned Form Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of your order, such as the type of food or drink you wish to order.
* **Menu Selection (2)**: Choose from the available options to select the items you want to order.
* **Date and Time (3)**: Enter the date and time when you would like to pick up your order.
* **Name (4)**: Enter your name so we can associate it with your order.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order (if required).
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comments (7)**: Add any comments or special requests for your order.
* **Total (8)**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Order Status (9)**: Confirm the status of your order as active or inactive.
* **Submit (10)**: Confirm that you're ready to submit your order.
* **Confirm Email (11)**: Re-enter your email address to confirm (if required).
* **Assigned User (12)**: Select the user assigned to handle your order.
* **Assigned Form (13)**: Select the form assigned to your order.
* **Notes (14)**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Assigned Form ID (15)**: Enter the ID of the assigned form (if required).
* **Assigned Date and Time (16)**: Enter the date and time when you want to pick up your order (if required).
* **Assigned User 2 (17)**: Select whether to assign another user to your order.
* **Assigned Form ID 2 (18)**: Enter the ID of the assigned form (if required).
* **Assigned Time 2 (19)**: Enter the time when you want to pick up your order (if required).
* **Assigned Form 2 (20)**: Select whether to assign another form to your order.
* **Notes 2 (21)**: Add additional notes about your order (if required).
* **Assigned Form IDs (22)**: Select the IDs of the assigned forms.
* **Assigned Form Name (23)**: Enter the name of the assigned form.
* **Assigned User 2 (24)**: Select whether to assign another user.
* **Assigned Form ID 2 (25)**: Enter the ID of the assigned form (if required).

Note: Some fields are marked as required, which means that you must fill in those fields for the form to be valid. Be sure to fill them in according to the provided instructions!
