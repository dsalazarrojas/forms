# Organic Order Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Organic Order Request form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place an order for organic products. This form will guide you through the process of submitting your order request, which will help us process your order efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Request** field with a brief description of the products you would like to order.
2. Select a valid **Delivery Date** (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure timely delivery.
3. Choose the specific **Delivery Time** (HH:MM AM/PM) you would like to receive your order.
4. Enter the **Address** where your order should be delivered.
5. Select the products you would like to order from the **Products** list.
6. Enter the name of the **Contact Person** who will be receiving the order.
7. Enter the **Contact Info** for the contact person.
8. If applicable, select the **Store** where you would like to receive your order from.
9. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them in the **Comments** field.
10. Provide your **Email** address to enable us to contact you about your order.
11. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can contact you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Request (organic_order_request)**: A brief description of the products you would like to order.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Select a valid date for delivery. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select a valid time for delivery. Please use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order.
* **Products (products)**: Select the products you would like to order. You can choose multiple products.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: Enter the name of the contact person who will be receiving the order.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter the contact information for the contact person.
* **Store (store)**: Select the store where you would like to receive your order (if applicable).
* **Comments (order_comments)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them here.
* **Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about your order.
* **Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
