# Annual Food Safety Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Food Safety Declaration Form is designed to collect information about food service providers' practices and commitment to food safety standards. This form is intended to help food service providers demonstrate their compliance with regulatory requirements and maintain a safe food environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and type in the "Business Name" and "Type of Food Service Business" fields.
2. Provide your contact person's name and email address in the "Contact Person Name" and "Email Address" fields.
3. Enter your facility's address in the "Facility Address" field.
4. Select the date of your last health inspection in the "Date of Last Health Inspection" field.
5. Indicate if you passed or failed the last inspection in the "Passed Most Recent Inspection" field.
6. Select the type of certifications your staff has for food safety training in the "Food Safety Training Certification" field.
7. Choose how you comply with food safety practices in the "Compliance with Food Safety Practices" field.
8. Indicate how you store and control food temperature in the "Food Storage and Temperature Control" field.
9. Select the effectiveness of your sanitation and cleaning procedures in the "Sanitation and Cleaning Procedures" field.
10. Choose how you manage food allergens in the "Allergen Management" field.
11. Select whether you provide allergen information to your customers in the "Food Allergen Information Available" field.
12. If you have any food recalls or safety incidents, describe them in the "Food Recalls or Safety Incidents" field.
13. If you have any incidents, describe them in the "Description of Incidents if Any" field.
14. If you have taken any corrective actions, describe them in the "Corrective Actions Taken" field.
15. Finally, confirm your commitment to maintaining food safety standards in the "Commitment to Food Safety Standards" field and sign the form with the name of the authorized signatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Business Name
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your food service business.
### 3. Type of Food Service Business
* **Type of Food Service Business**: Select the type of business you operate (Restaurant, Catering Company, Food Truck, etc.).
### 5. Contact Person Name
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of your primary contact person.
### 6. Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
### 7. Facility Address
* **Facility Address**: Enter the physical address of your food facility.
### 10. Compliance with Food Safety Practices
* **Compliance with Food Safety Practices**: Select how you comply with food safety practices.
### 11. Food Storage and Temperature Control
* **Food Storage and Temperature Control**: Select how you store and control food temperature.
### 12. Sanitation and Cleaning Procedures
* **Sanitation and Cleaning Procedures**: Select the effectiveness of your sanitation and cleaning procedures.
### 13. Allergen Management
* **Allergen Management**: Select how you manage food allergens.
### 14. Food Recalls or Safety Incidents
* **Food Recalls or Safety Incidents**: Indicate if you have any food recalls or safety incidents this year.
### 15. Description of Incidents if Any
* **Description of Incidents if Any**: Provide details about any food recalls or safety incidents.
### 16. Corrective Actions Taken
* **Corrective Actions Taken**: Describe any corrective actions taken for the incidents.
### 17. Commitment to Food Safety Standards
* **Commitment to Food Safety Standards**: Select your level of commitment to food safety standards.
### 18. Name of Authorized Signatory
* **Name of Authorized Signatory**: Enter the name of the person authorized to sign this declaration.

Note: If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it in accurately to ensure the form is complete and valid.
