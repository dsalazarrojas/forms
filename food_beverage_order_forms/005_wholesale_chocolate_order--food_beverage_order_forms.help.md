# Wholesale Chocolate Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process wholesale orders for chocolate products. Please fill in the required information to ensure accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. For non-required fields, provide any additional information that may be helpful for order processing.
3. If you have any customer notes or special requests, please include them in the "Customer Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you for any order updates or inquiries.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address for any communication.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address for accurate order delivery.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your order, such as any specific products or quantities.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your order, either PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of your ordered products.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): This field will be populated automatically after selecting your payment method.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any relevant customer notes or special requests for your order.
* **Company Name 2** (`company_name_2`, text, optional): Enter your company name again for verification purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address for accurate record keeping.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for your order delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose the preferred delivery time for your order.

Note: Please keep in mind that some fields are optional, but it's still required to fill them out if you want to provide more information for accurate order processing.
