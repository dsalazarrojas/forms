# food_online_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food online order form is designed to collect customer orders for food and beverages. Customers can select their preferred meal options, choose their preferred diet type, texture, temperature, quantity, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by selecting your preferred meal option in the "Select Meal" field.
2. Choose your preferred diet type in the "Select Diet Type" field.
3. Select your preferred texture in the "Select Texture" field.
4. Choose your preferred temperature in the "Select Temperature" field.
5. Select your preferred quantity in the "Select Quantity" field.
6. Choose your preferred delivery option(s) in the "Select Delivery" field (you can select multiple options).
7. Select your preferred payment method in the "Select Payment" field.
8. Add any additional order details in the "Order Details" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Note" field.
