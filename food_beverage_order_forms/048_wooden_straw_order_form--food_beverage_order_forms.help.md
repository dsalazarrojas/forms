# Wooden Straw Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for custom wooden straws with specific characteristics such as shake size, tea type, and protein amount. The form will guide you through the ordering process, ensuring you get the correct straw for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your order details.
2. Select the desired shake size for your straw from the options provided.
3. Choose the type of tea you would like your straw to be compatible with.
4. Select the protein amount that suits your needs.
5. Review your order before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional inquiries or order updates.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Shake Size** (`shake_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from 12 oz, 16 oz, or 20 oz for your straw size.
* **Tea Type** (`tea_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select from Black, Green, Oolong, or White for your tea type compatibility.
* **Protein Amount** (`protein_amount`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from 15 g, 20 g, or 25 g for your protein amount preference.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately to avoid any errors or delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
