# Meal Train Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Train Sign Up Form is used to collect information from customers who want to participate in the meal train program. It helps us to onboard customers and provide them with the necessary meals and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand what information is being asked for.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Double-check your information for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_2`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be known in the meal train program.
* **Last Meal Date** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_3`, date, required/optional): Please select the date of your last meal, if applicable.
* **Email** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_4`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address, which we will use to contact you about your meal train schedule.
* **Phone** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_5`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you about your meal train schedule.
* **Address** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_6`, text, required/optional): Please enter your address, which we will use to deliver meals to you.
* **Notes** (`meal_train_sign_up_form_7`, note, required/optional): If you have any special requests or concerns, please use this field to let us know.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
* We will review and process your form submission promptly, and you should expect to hear from us soon about your meal train schedule.
