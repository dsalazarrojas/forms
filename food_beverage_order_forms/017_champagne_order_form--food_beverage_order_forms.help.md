# Champagne Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for champagne. It will ask for information such as event details, customer contact information, and order specifics. This will help us ensure accurate and timely delivery and billing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the date you would like to place the order, found under "Order Date".
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the date you need the champagne for, under "Event Date".
4. Choose the type of champagne you would like to order, found under "Champagne Type".
5. Select the size of champagne bottle you need, found under "Bottle Size".
6. Enter the number of bottles you require, found under "Quantity of Bottles".
7. Choose your preferred payment method, found under "Payment Method".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date**: The date you would like to place the order. This date is used to ensure timely delivery and billing.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Date**: The date you need the champagne for.
* **Champagne Type**: Choose the type of champagne you would like to order from the options provided (Brut, Extra Brut, Dry, Demi-Sec, Sweet, Rose, Vintage).
* **Bottle Size**: Select the size of the champagne bottle that suits your needs.
* **Quantity of Bottles**: Enter the number of bottles you require.
* **Total Bottles Needed**: Calculate the total number of bottles you need based on your event size.
* **Price Range Preference**: Select a price range that suits your budget.
* **Type of Event**: Choose the type of event you are ordering for from the options provided (Wedding, Anniversary, Birthday, Corporate Event, Holiday Celebration, Other).
* **Number of Guests**: Approximate the number of guests that will be attending the event.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like the champagne to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose the date you would like the champagne to be delivered.
* **Delivery Method**: Select the delivery method that suits your needs (Standard Delivery, Expedited Delivery, Pickup, Event Venue Delivery).
* **Special Handling Instructions**: Enter any special requests or handling instructions.
* **Champagne Glasses Included**: Choose if you need glasses with your order.
* **Ice Service Needed**: Choose if you need chilling service with your order.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any other requirements or special requests.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Wire Transfer, Invoice).
* **Expedited Delivery**: Select if you need expedited delivery for your order.
