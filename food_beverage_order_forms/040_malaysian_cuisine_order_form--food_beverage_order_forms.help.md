# Malaysian Cusine Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Malaysian Cusine Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for food online. By providing the required information, you will be able to place your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "order_details" page with your desired food order. This page is where you can provide specific details about your order, such as the type and quantity of food you want to order.
2. Move on to the "customer_info" page to provide your personal details. This is where you can enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that we need to know to fulfill your order.
3. On the "address" page, enter your delivery or pick-up address. This is crucial for us to know where to deliver your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the page where you can provide specific details about your order, such as the type and quantity of food you want to order.
* **customer_info** (`name`, `text`, optional): On this page, you can enter your personal details, including your name and contact information, which we need to know to fulfill your order.
* **address** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your delivery or pick-up address here so that we can know where to deliver your order accurately.
