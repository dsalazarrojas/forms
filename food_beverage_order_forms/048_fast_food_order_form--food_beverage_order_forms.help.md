# Fast Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fast Food Order Form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for a fast food order. This form allows customers to specify their order type, quantity of various menu items, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "First name" field. This is the customer's first name.
2. Next, enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the type of order from the "Order type" dropdown menu.
4. Specify the quantity of each item you would like to order (burgers, pizza, and salads) in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the total cost of the order in the "Total" field.
6. Choose the payment method from the "Payment method" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone number" field.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
9. Finally, verify that the other "First name" field (first_name_2) is not filled out and can be ignored, as it seems to be a duplicate field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of order (Burgers, Pizza, Salads, or Fries).
* **Quantity of burgers** (`quantity_burgers`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of burgers you would like to order.
* **Quantity of pizza** (`quantity_pizza`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of pizza you would like to order.
* **Quantity of salads** (`quantity_salads`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of salads you would like to order.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the payment method (Credit card, Cash, or Cashier).
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **First name** (`first_name_2`, `text`, required/optional): **DO NOT FILL OUT**. This field is duplicate and should not be filled out.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any errors or issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
