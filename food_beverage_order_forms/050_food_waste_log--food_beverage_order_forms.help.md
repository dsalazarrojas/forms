# Food Waste Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Waste Log is a form used to track and record food waste and liquid waste in the kitchen. This helps ensure accurate and efficient management of waste disposal and reduces unnecessary waste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of waste you are logging (Food waste, Liquid waste, Organic waste, or Plastic waste).
2. Choose the correct date and time of the waste occurrence.
3. Enter the amount of waste logged.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waste type**: This field allows you to select the type of waste you are logging. Select the correct category from the options:
	+ Food waste (e.g., fruit and vegetables, cooked food, leftovers)
	+ Liquid waste (e.g., juice, water, soup)
	+ Organic waste (e.g., fruit peels, vegetable scraps, eggshells)
	+ Plastic waste (e.g., packaging, containers, bottles)
* **Date**: This field is for recording the date of the waste occurrence.
* **Time**: This field is for recording the time of the waste occurrence.
* **Amount**: This field is for recording the quantity of waste logged. Please enter the amount as a numerical value.
