# Breakfast Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breakfast Order Form is a tool to collect customer orders for breakfast items. This form requires customers to provide their name, contact information, order details, and payment method to process their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the Customer Name field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the Email Address field for order confirmation purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the Phone Number field for contact.
4. Select your **Order Type** from the list of options (e.g., Delivery, Pickup, Dine-in, or Takeout).
5. Enter your **Desired Order Date** in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Select your **Preferred Order Time** from the available options (e.g., 7 AM, 7:30 AM, or 8 AM).
7. Choose your **Egg Dishes** preferences from the list (e.g., Scrambled eggs, Fried eggs, or No eggs).
8. Select your **Pancakes and Waffles** options (e.g., Pancakes, Waffles, or Both).
9. Choose your **Pancake or Waffle Toppings** from the list (e.g., Maple syrup, Blueberries, or None).
10. Select your **Bacon and Sausage Options** from the list (e.g., Bacon, Sausage patties, or None).
11. Choose your **Toast or Bread** option (e.g., White toast, Wheat toast, or None).
12. Select your **Fresh Fruit Options** from the list (e.g., Berries, Melon, or Orange slices).
13. Choose your **Beverages** options (e.g., Coffee, Tea, or Orange juice).
14. Select your **Coffee Preparation** (e.g., Black, With cream, or With sugar).
15. Select any **Dietary Restrictions** you have (e.g., Gluten-free, Vegan, or None).
16. Enter any **Special Requests or Allergies** in the text field.
17. Select your **Payment Method** from the list (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, or Online banking).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for delivery or contact.
* **Order Type** (Select One, Required): Choose how you want to receive your order (e.g., Delivery, Pickup, Dine-in, or Takeout).
* **Desired Order Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you want your order to be ready.
* **Preferred Order Time** (Select One, Required): Choose the time you want your order to be ready.
* **Egg Dishes - Select Items** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your egg preferences.
* **Pancakes and Waffles** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you want pancakes, waffles, or both.
* **Pancake or Waffle Toppings** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your toppings for pancakes or waffles.
* **Bacon and Sausage Options** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your bacon or sausage preferences.
* **Toast or Bread** (Select One, Optional): Choose your bread option.
* **Fresh Fruit Selection** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your fruit options.
* **Beverages** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your beverage options.
* **Coffee Preparation** (Select One, Optional): Choose your coffee preparation.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Special Requests or Allergies** (Text, Optional): Enter any special requests or allergies.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your payment method.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your order details before submitting.
* If you have any special requests or allergies, please enter them in the Special Requests or Allergies field.
* For dietary restrictions, select any options that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
