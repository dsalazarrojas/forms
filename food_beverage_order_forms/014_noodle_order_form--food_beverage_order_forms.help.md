# Noodle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noodle Order Form is used to capture information about a customer's noodle order, including the type of noodles, prices, order notes, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the type and price of each noodle in the corresponding fields. If you have multiple noodles to order, enter each one on a new row.
2. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
7. Select the date and time of your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
8. Enter the Form ID, which may be provided to you.
9. Optionally, enter a title for your form in the "Form Title" field.
10. Optionally, select the category of your form in the "Form Category" field.
11. Optionally, enter a description of your form in the "Form Description" field.
12. Select your preferred payment gateway from the "Payment Gateway" field.
13. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Noodle 1**: Enter the type of noodle you would like to order (e.g. "Spaghetti", "Fettuccine", etc.).
* **Noodle 1 Price**: Enter the price of the first noodle.
* **Noodle 1 Image**: Enter a brief description or image of the first noodle.
* **Noodle 2**: Enter the type of second noodle you would like to order (e.g. "Ramen", "Lo Mein", etc.).
* **Noodle 2 Price**: Enter the price of the second noodle.
* **Noodle 2 Image**: Enter a brief description or image of the second noodle.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Order Total**: This field is automatically populated based on the price of the ordered noodles. Do not enter anything here.
* **Payment**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g. "Visa", "Mastercard", etc.).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Order Date**: Select the date of your order.
* **Order Time**: Select the time of your order.
* **Form ID**: Enter the provided Form ID.
* **Form Title**: Enter a title for your form (optional).
* **Form Category**: Select the category of your form (optional).
* **Form Description**: Enter a description of your form (optional).
* **Payment Gateway**: Select your preferred payment gateway (e.g. "Stripe", "PayPal", etc.).
