# Game Day Meal Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help game day organizers plan their meal planning for the event. It will help to collect information on the number of guests, their food preferences, and potential contribution commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and title for the survey as a brief description of the event.
2. Answer the "Food Preferences" question with your preferred food options for the event.
3. Enter the headcount, or number of guests, you are planning to bring.
4. Indicate your commitment level on making a contribution for the event.
5. Add any additional comments or description for the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Game Day Meal Planning Survey (text):** This field is the title of your game day event. Please enter the name of the event as it will be displayed to other attendees. It should not be more than 20 characters in length.
*   **Food Preferences (select_multiple):** Check all the food options that you prefer to be served at the event. These options will be considered when planning the food and beverages for the game day.
*   **Headcount (number):** Please enter the number of guests you plan on bringing to the event.
*   **Contribution Commitments (select_one):** Indicate your commitment level for making a contribution for the event. Your contribution commitment will help with the overall food planning.
*   **Game Day Meal Planning Survey Description (text):** This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information or comments regarding the survey. You can use it to provide details about your food preferences or anything else that you think is important for the organizers to know.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out this form only once for each game day event.
*   Be as detailed as possible with your food preferences to help with the planning.
*   If you have any special requests, please use the additional description field to provide them.
*   Your feedback and commitment commitment will help make the game day event more enjoyable for everyone.
