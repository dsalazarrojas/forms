# Lockdown Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lockdown Food Order Form is a crucial tool for customers to place food and drink orders during a lockdown. This form helps us ensure that we have accurate and complete information for order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide as much detail as possible for the "order_details" field, including the type and quantity of food and drink items you wish to order.
2. Enter the customer's information in the "customer_info" field, including their name, address, and contact details.
3. Select the delivery option for your order by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "delivery_time" field. If you select "Yes", please provide the address where you would like to receive your order in the "delivery_address" field.
4. Summarize your order in the "order_summary" field, which will help us verify your order and ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details**: A text field where you can provide detailed information about your food and drink order. Please include the type and quantity of each item you wish to order.
* **customer_info**: A text field where you can enter the customer's name, address, and contact details.
* **delivery_time**: A select field where you can choose whether you would like to have your order delivered or not. If you select "Yes", you will be asked for a delivery address in the next field.
* **delivery_address**: A text field where you can enter the address where you would like to receive your order if you have selected "Yes" for delivery time.
* **order_summary**: A text field where you can provide a brief summary of your order for our verification.
