# Wedding Package With Price Change - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a wedding package with a price change, including client name, email, wedding date, package name, price, and agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Client Name, Client Email, Wedding Date, Package Name, and Price.
2. Enter the client's name and email address accurately.
3. Choose a date for the wedding.
4. Select the package name from the list provided.
5. Enter the price of the wedding package.
6. Write a clear agreement statement in the Agreement field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's valid email address.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Package Name**: Select the name of the package from the list provided.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the package, which will be used for billing purposes.
* **Agreement**: Write a clear and concise agreement statement that outlines the terms of the package purchase.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all information is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the wedding planner or event organizer for clarification.
