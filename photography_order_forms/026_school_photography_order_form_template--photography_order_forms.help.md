# School Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order information for school photography orders. It is intended for students, parents, or administrators to provide order details, select products, and choose payment methods. The form helps to keep track of orders, ensure accuracy, and streamline the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **School Photography Order Form** (Field 1) with the name of the form.
2.  Enter the **Student Information** (Field 2) with student details such as name, class, or school ID.
3.  Enter the **Parent Information** (Field 3) with parent details such as name and contact information.
4.  Select one or more **Order Options** (Field 4) such as Order or Reprint.
5.  Choose the desired **Product List** (Field 5) from the available options.
6.  Select the **Payment Method** (Field 6) from the options provided, such as Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
7.  Fill in the **Payment Details** (Field 7) with any additional payment information.
8.  Add any **Order Comments** (Field 8) if necessary.
9.  Fill in the **Order Notes** (Field 9) for any additional order instructions.
10.  Upload a **Student Image** (Field 10) and **Parent Image** (Field 11) if required.
11.  Fill in the **Order Submission** (Field 12) with any additional order details.
12.  Select the current **Order Status** (Field 13) of the order, such as Pending, Completed, or Cancelled.
13.  Choose the **Payment Status** (Field 14) of the order, such as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
14.  Enter the **Date Ordered** (Field 15) if known.
15.  Enter the **Time Ordered** (Field 16) if known.
16.  Enter the **Order Number** (Field 17) if assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Photography Order Form** (Field 1):
	+ A text field to enter the name of the form.
* **Student Information** (Field 2):
	+ A text field to enter student details such as name, class, or school ID.
* **Parent Information** (Field 3):
	+ A text field to enter parent details such as name and contact information.
* **Order Options** (Field 4):
	+ A multi-select field to choose one or more options such as Order or Reprint.
* **Product List** (Field 5):
	+ A single-select field to choose the desired product from the available options.
* **Payment Method** (Field 6):
	+ A single-select field to choose the payment method from the options provided such as Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Details** (Field 7):
	+ A text field to enter any additional payment information.
* **Order Comments** (Field 8):
	+ A text field to add any additional order comments.
* **Order Notes** (Field 9):
	+ A text field to add any additional order notes.
* **Student Image** (Field 10):
	+ A text field to upload a student image.
* **Parent Image** (Field 11):
	+ A text field to upload a parent image.
* **Order Submission** (Field 12):
	+ A text field to add any additional order submission details.
* **Order Status** (Field 13):
	+ A single-select field to choose the current status of the order such as Pending, Completed, or Cancelled.
* **Payment Status** (Field 14):
	+ A single-select field to choose the payment status of the order such as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* **Date Ordered** (Field 15):
	+ A date field to enter the date the order was placed.
* **Time Ordered** (Field 16):
	+ A time field to enter the time the order was placed.
* **Order Number** (Field 17):
	+ A numeric field to enter the order number.
