# Santa Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Photo Order Form is designed to collect information from users to process their photography orders for the upcoming Santa Photo Sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the session type for your photo order.
2. Enter the date of your photo session.
3. Provide your name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Add any additional message or instructions for the photo order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_type** (select_one): Select the type of photo session you would like to book (e.g., Session 1, Session 2).
* **date** (date): Enter the date of your photo session.
* **name** (text): Enter your name as it should be used for the order.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **message** (note): Add any additional message or instructions for the photo order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any delays or mistakes during the processing of your order.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our staff for assistance.
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
