# Prom Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to help clients book a photography session for various occasions such as portraits, events, or weddings. It captures essential information to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the client and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of photography service you would like to book (Portrait, Event, Wedding, or Other).
2.  Enter your name and email address in the Client Information field.
3.  Provide your email address for contact purposes (this will be used to send you a confirmation and photos after the session).
4.  Choose a date and time for the photography session in the Photography Session field.
5.  Specify the location where the photography session will take place in the Location field.
6.  Select the duration of the photography session in hours.
7.  If desired, select any additional services such as hair and makeup, wardrobe styling, or other.
8.  Enter any special requests or additional details for the photography session.
9.  Indicate the number of guests you expect to be present during the session.
10. Confirm whether you would like to receive a copy of the photos after the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Photography Details**: This field allows you to select the type of photography service you would like to book. Choose from Portrait, Event, Wedding, or Other.
*   **Client Information**: Please enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter your email address for the photographer to contact you about your session.
*   **Photography Session**: If you have already selected a date and time, enter it here.
*   **Location**: Specify the exact location where the photography session will take place.
*   **Session Duration**: Choose the number of hours for the photography session.
*   **Additional Services**: If you would like to book additional services such as hair and makeup or wardrobe styling, select them here.
*   **Special Requests**: Enter any specific requests or requirements you may have for the photography session.
*   **Number of Guests**: Indicate the number of guests you expect to be present during the photography session.
*   **Additional Details**: Enter any additional information or context about the photography session.
*   **Confirmation**: Confirm whether you would like to receive a copy of the photos after the session.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check all the fields before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have entered accurate and relevant information to avoid any confusion.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the photographer directly.
