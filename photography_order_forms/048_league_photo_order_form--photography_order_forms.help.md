# League Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for ordering photography services. It collects customer information, appointment scheduling, and payment details for photoshoot services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select the photo package of your choice from the options listed.
3. Choose your preferred appointment time from the available options.
4. Add any additional notes for your photoshoot.
5. Select the tool assigned to your photoshoot.
6. Select the person or people assigned to your photoshoot.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Confirm your order details.
9. Calculate the total price for your photoshoot.
10. Choose your preferred payment method.
11. Select payment terms.
12. Add any additional order notes.
13. Calculate the final total price.
13. Select the total price formation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`<name>`, Required): Enter your name as it should appear on the photos.
* **email** (`<email>`, Required): Enter your email address to contact you for appointment scheduling and order updates.
* **photo_package** (`<photo_package>`, Required): Select the photo package of your choice from the available options.
* **appointment_time** (`<appointment_time>`, Required): Choose your preferred appointment time from the available options.
* **notes** (`<notes>`, Optional): Add any additional notes for your photoshoot.
* **Assigned Tool** (`<Assigned Tool>`, Required): Select the tool assigned to your photoshoot from the available options.
* **Assigned to** (`<Assigned to>`, Required): Select the person or people assigned to your photoshoot from the available options.
* **phone** (`<phone>`, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **confirm** (`<confirm>`, Required): Confirm your order details.
* **submit** (`<submit>`, Required): Submit your completed order.
* **photo_package_price** (`<photo_package_price>`, Required): Calculate the total price of your photo package.
* **package_price** (`<package_price>`, Required): Enter the calculated price of your photo package.
* **total_price** (`<total_price>`, Required): Enter the total price of your order.
* **total_price_formation** (`<total_price_formation>`, Required): Confirm the total price of your order.
* **payment_method** (`<payment_method>`, Required): Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
* **payment_terms** (`<payment_terms>`, Required): Select your preferred payment terms from the available options.
* **order_notes** (`<order_notes>`, Required): Add any additional order notes.
* **package_price_formation** (`<package_price_formation>`, Required): Confirm the price of your photo package.
* **total_price_formation** (`<total_price_formation>`, Required): Confirm the total price of your order.
* **package_price_total_formation** (`<package_price_total_formation>`, Required): Confirm the total price of your photo package.
* **payment_method_formation** (`<payment_method_formation>`, Required): Select the payment method for your order.
* **payment_terms_formation** (`<payment_terms_formation>`, Required): Confirm the payment terms for your order.
* **order_notes_formation** (`<order_notes_formation>`, Required): Confirm any additional order notes.
* **total_price_formation_formation** (`<total_price_formation_formation>`, Required): Confirm the total price of your order.

## Tips
* Ensure that you review your order carefully before submitting.
* Double-check your appointment time and photo package selection.
* Confirm your payment method and terms to avoid any issues.
* Use this form for single-order photoshoots. For multiple orders, please use a separate form.
