# Wedding Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wedding Photography Survey form is designed to collect necessary information from clients about their wedding event. This includes details such as client name, date of the wedding, number of guests, location, contact information, and photographer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully and enter information as prompted.
2.  Fill out all required fields (if any).
3.  If unsure about any section, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Wedding Photography Survey** (`wedding_photography_survey`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which should remain as it is.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the client or event coordinator.
*   **Date Of Wedding** (`date_of_wedding`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the wedding in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Number Of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required: false): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
*   **Location Of Event** (`location_of_event`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the wedding will take place.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you.
*   **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
*   **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): If you've already selected a photographer, enter their name. Otherwise, let us know if you have any preferences.
*   **Photographer Experience** (`photographer_experience`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the experience level of the photographer you are looking for (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
*   **Wedding Type** (`wedding_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of wedding from the options provided (Single, Wedding, Engagement, Anniversary).
*   **Services Selected** (`services_selected`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the services you are interested in (Portrait Session, Couple Portraits, Engagement, Album, Prints, Video Montage, Photo Booth, Other).
*   **Extra Services** (`extra_services`, text, required: false): If there are any extra services you'd like to inquire about, list them here.
*   **Extra Services Details** (`extra_services_details`, text, required: false): Provide details about the extra services you are interested in.
*   **Photographer Special Requests** (`photographer_special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests for the photographer, list them here.
*   **Photographer Additional Comments** (`photographer_additional_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions for the photographer, enter them here.
*   **Client Special Requests** (`client_special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests for the wedding event, list them here.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification.
*   Provide accurate and complete information to help us serve you better.
