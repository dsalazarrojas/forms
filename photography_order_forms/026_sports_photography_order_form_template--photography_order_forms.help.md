# Sports Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their photography orders, including their contact details, services required, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Photographer" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the services you require from the "Services" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the duration of your photoshoot from the "Photoshoot Duration" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the name of the package you've chosen in the "Package Name" field.
7. Add any additional details about your package in the "Package Details" field.
8. Enter the price of your order in the "Price" field.
9. Select a date for your photoshoot in the "Date" field.
10. Choose a time for your photoshoot in the "Time" field.
11. Add any notes about your order in the "Notes" field.
12. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the amount you're paying for your order in the "Payment Amount" field.
14. Add any comments about your order in the "Order Comments" field.
15. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
16. Enter your email address in the "Client Email" field.
17. Provide your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
18. Track the status of your order in the "Order Status" field.
19. Assign a user to handle your order in the "Assigned User" field.
20. Enter the date and time when the order was created in the "Created At" field.
21. Enter the date and time when the order was last updated in the "Updated At" field.
22. Enter the date and time when the order was deleted in the "Deleted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Services**: Select the services you require (e.g. Portrait Session, Headshots, Family Session).
* **Photoshoot Duration**: Choose the duration of your photoshoot (e.g. 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours).
* **Package Name**: Enter the name of the package you've chosen.
* **Package Details**: Add any additional details about your package.
* **Price**: Enter the price of your order.
* **Date**: Select a date for your photoshoot.
* **Time**: Select a time for your photoshoot.
* **Notes**: Add any notes about your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you're paying for your order.
* **Order Comments**: Add any comments about your order.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name (if you're the client).
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address (if you're the client).
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you're the client).
* **Order Status**: Track the status of your order (e.g. Pending, In Progress, Complete).
* **Assigned User**: Assign a user to handle your order.
* **Created At**: Display the date and time when the order was created.
* **Updated At**: Display the date and time when the order was last updated.
* **Deleted At**: Display the date and time when the order was deleted.
