# Photoshoot Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture essential information from clients during a photography session. This information helps the photographer understand the client's needs and expectations, ensuring a successful and satisfying experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information (client_details)**: Enter your name, address, and contact details to help us get in touch with you.
2. **Photography Session (photography_session)**: Describe the type of photography session you're interested in (e.g., wedding, portrait, commercial, etc.) and provide any specific details about the session (e.g., location, date, time).
3. **Photography Deliverables (photo_deliverables)**: List the photos you would like to receive from the photography session, including the format you prefer (e.g., digital, printed, or both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (client_details)
* **Client Details**: Enter your full name, address, and any contact information you're comfortable sharing with us. This will help us get in touch with you and keep you updated on your photography session.

### Photography Session (photography_session)
* **Photography Session**: Describe the type of photography session you're interested in (e.g., wedding, portrait, commercial, etc.). This will help us understand the scope and requirements of your session.

### Photography Deliverables (photo_deliverables)
* **Photography Deliverables**: List the specific photos you'd like to receive from the session, such as prints, digital copies, or both. This will help us ensure you get the photos you want without any confusion.

## Tips
* Fill out the form thoroughly and honestly to ensure we get the best possible results.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, don't hesitate to let us know.
* We're here to help; if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
