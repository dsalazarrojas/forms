# Wedding DVD Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding DVD Application Form is a vital document that provides the necessary information to create a personalized and memorable wedding video. This form is designed to gather details about the client's wedding video order, including the videographer, services, order date, video format, delivery date, delivery cost, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information about your wedding video order.
* Select the correct wedding video services you require.
* Enter the email address of the videographer who will be responsible for your wedding video.
* Choose the date you would like to receive your wedding video order.
* Select the preferred video format (HD or SD).
* Specify the delivery date you would like to receive your wedding video order.
* Enter the delivery cost of your order, if applicable.
* Add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding DVD Application Form**: This is the title of the form, and you should not need to fill it out as it's only for reference.
* **Wedding Video Services**: Please select the services you require for your wedding video order. You can choose multiple services if applicable. 
* **Videographer**: Enter the email address of the person who will be responsible for creating your wedding video.
* **Wedding Video Order**: Choose the date when you would like to receive your wedding video order.
* **Video Format**: Select the video format you prefer for your wedding video. You can choose between HD and SD.
* **Delivery Date**: Specify the date when you would like to receive your wedding video order.
* **Delivery Cost**: Enter the cost of your wedding video order, if applicable.
* **Videographer Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
