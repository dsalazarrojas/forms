# Nicole Lopez Photography Final Wedding Details - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and confirm the final details related to a wedding event. It's a critical step to ensure that the photographer has all necessary information to capture the perfect moments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, carefully follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form's sections and fields to understand what information is required.
2. Select the correct photographer from the list.
3. Choose the suitable time of the photography session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Select the location where the photography session will take place (Church, Outdoor, or Beach).
5. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Text).
6. Fill in any additional details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Final Details 1**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information or comments.
* **Final Details 2**: Enter any specific details or requests related to the photography session.
* **Final Details 3 (Date)**: This field is used to enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Final Details 4 (Time)**: This field is used to select the start time of the photography session.
* **Final Details 5 (Number of Guests)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide an estimate of the number of guests attending the wedding. 

Please note that each field must be completed according to the specific requirements and guidelines for the corresponding field type. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
