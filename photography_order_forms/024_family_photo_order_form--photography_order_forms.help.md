# Family Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Family Photo Order Form" is designed for customers to order photo-related services or products. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about your photo package choice, details, quantity, and shipping address. You will also be asked to input your contact number, email address, order notes, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it.
2. Select your photo package choice from the options provided.
3. Input your photo package details, quantity, and size.
4. Enter your shipping address, contact number, and email address.
5. Provide any additional order notes or comments.
6. Choose your payment method and provide any additional payment method description or price.
7. Input the submitter's name, email, and phone number.
8. Add any submitter's notes.
9. Confirm the submission date and time.
10. Select the submitted by option (Administrator, Editor, or User).
11. Choose the status of your order (Approved, Rejected, or In Progress).
12. Input any status description or update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Package Choice** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): Select the type of photo package you would like to order. Choose from Apple iPhone, Android Phone, or DSLR Camera.
* **Photo Package Details** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your photo package choice.
* **Quantity** (id: 4, type: number, required: false): Enter the quantity of photo packages you would like to order.
* **Photo Package Size** (id: 5, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose the size of your photo package. Select from Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Photo Package Price** (id: 6, type: number, required: false): Enter the price of your photo package.
* **Shipping Address** (id: 7, type: text, required: false): Enter your shipping address where you would like to receive your photo package.
* **Contact Number** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): Provide your contact number so we can reach out to you about your order.
* **Email Address** (id: 9, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Notes** (id: 10, type: note, required: false): Provide any additional order notes or comments.
* **Order Notes** (id: 11, type: note, required: false): This field is similar to Order Notes. You can input any additional information about your order.
* **Payment Method** (id: 12, type: select_one, required: false): Choose your payment method. Select from Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Method Description** (id: 13, type: text, required: false): If you selected a payment method, provide any additional description or details about your payment.
* **Payment Method Price** (id: 14, type: number, required: false): Enter the price of your payment method.
* **Order Form Submitter** (id: 15, type: text, required: false): Input the name of the person submitting the order.
* **Submitter Email** (id: 16, type: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the order.
* **Submitter Phone** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Provide the phone number of the person submitting the order.
* **Submitter Notes** (id: 18, type: note, required: false): Input any additional notes from the submitter.
* **Submission Date** (id: 19, type: date, required: false): Confirm the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (id: 20, type: time, required: false): Confirm the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (id: 21, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select who submitted the order (Administrator, Editor, or User).
* **Status** (id: 22, type: select_one, required: true): Choose the status of your order (Approved, Rejected, or In Progress).
* **Status Description** (id: 23, type: text, required: false): Enter any status description or update.
* **Order Status Description** (id: 24, type: text, required: false): Input any additional order status description or update.
* **Order Status Update** (id: 25, type: text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the order status update.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have selected the correct photo package choice and size.
- Enter a valid email address and phone number.
- Choose a payment method and provide any necessary payment method description or price.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
