# Youth Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Photography Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their reservation and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Youth Photography Order Form" link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required field: Print Type (Select one of the options: Photo Prints, Photo Gallery, Canvas Print, or Other).
3. You can optionally provide additional information such as Reservation Date, Reservation Time, Print Size, Quantity, Email, Phone, and Reservation Comments.
4. Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Photography Order Form** (`youth_photography_order_form`, text): This is the main title of the form. Enter a brief description of the event or reason for the order.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date): Enter the date of the event or party where the photos were taken. (Optional)
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time): Enter the time of the event or party where the photos were taken. (Optional)
* **Print Type** (`print_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of print you would like for your photos:
	+ **Photo Prints**: Printed individual photos
	+ **Photo Gallery**: A collection of printed photos
	+ **Canvas Print**: Printed photos on a canvas material
	+ **Other**: Custom print type (Please specify in the Reservation Comments field)
* **Print Size** (`print_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the print (if applicable, e.g., 8x10, 12x18, etc.)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of prints you would like to order
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates
* **Reservation Comments** (`reservation_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your order. For example, if you chose "Other" in Print Type, explain what type of print you would like.
