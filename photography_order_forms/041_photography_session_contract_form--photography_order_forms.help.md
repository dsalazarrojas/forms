# photography_session_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients or customers for a photography session. It collects details such as the session type, session specifics, photographer and assistant information, scheduling details, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of photography session you have chosen.
2. Fill in the session details.
3. Choose whether there will be a photographer, assistant, and select the theme.
4. Set the start and end times for the session.
5. Enter the client's contact information, including their email and phone number.
6. Provide the address for the session.
7. Specify the payment terms.
8. Decide on the photography license, copyright, print, and online rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Session** (`photography_session`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of photography session you have booked.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Fill in any additional details about the session, such as its location or any specific requirements.
* **Theme** (`theme`, select_one, required: false): Decide on the theme for the session.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the photographer handling the session.
* **Assistant** (`assistant`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the assistant who will be present during the session.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Set the start time for the session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Set the end time for the session.
* **Client** (`client`, text, required: false): Enter the client's name or title to be used in communication.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Provide the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide the address for the session.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **Photography License** (`photography_license`, select_one, required: false): Decide on the photography license.
* **Copyright** (`copyright`, select_one, required: false): Choose copyright status.
* **Print Rights** (`print_rights`, select_one, required: false): Determine if print rights are granted.
* **Online Rights** (`online_rights`, select_one, required: false): Decide on online rights.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required and optional fields accurately to avoid any misunderstandings or miscommunications.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is correct.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the photography studio or agency.
