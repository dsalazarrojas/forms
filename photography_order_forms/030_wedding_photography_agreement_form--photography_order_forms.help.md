# Wedding Photography Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for a wedding photography service where a couple can select and agree on the details of their wedding photography package.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the photographer's and client's contact information (fields 2-4).
2. Select the type of photography services you want to book (field 13).
3. Choose the number of photo sessions (field 14).
4. Select the print size and album layout (fields 15-16).
5. Choose the album cover design and size (fields 17-18).
6. Enter your report date and time for the photoshoot (fields 17-18).
7. Enter your report location (field 19).
8. Specify your photography equipment (field 20).
9. Write a little about your experience with photography (field 21).
10. Provide your license number or other relevant credentials (field 22).
11. Sign both the client and photographer sections to agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographers** (field 2, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer(s) who will be handling your wedding photography.
* **Photographer email** (field 3, email, required): Enter the email address of the photographer(s) who will be handling your wedding photography.
* **Photographer phone** (field 4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the photographer(s) who will be handling your wedding photography.
* **Client name** (field 5, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Client email** (field 6, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Client phone** (field 7, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Wedding date** (field 8, date, required): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Wedding time** (field 9, time, required): Enter the time of your wedding.
* **Wedding location** (field 10, text, required): Enter the location of your wedding.
* **Select your services** (field 11, select_one, required): Choose the type of photography service you want to book (Wedding, Engagement, Portrait).
* **Select your session** (field 12, select_multiple, required): Choose the number of photo sessions you want to book (Session 1, Session 2, Session 3).
* **Select print size** (field 13, select_one, required): Choose the print size of your album (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Select album layout** (field 14, select_one, required): Choose the layout of your album (Vertical, Horizontal).
* **Select album cover** (field 15, select_one, required): Choose the design of your album cover (Color, White).
* **Select album size** (field 16, select_one, required): Choose the size of your album (16x20, 12x16, 8x10, Other).
* **Report delivery date** (field 17, date, required): Enter the date you expect to receive your report.
* **Report time** (field 18, time, required): Enter the time you expect to receive your report.
* **Report location** (field 19, text, required): Enter the location where you will receive your report.
* **Photography equipment** (field 20, text, required): Specify any photography equipment you have or prefer to use.
* **Your experience** (field 21, text, required): Write a little about your experience with photography.
* **Your license** (field 22, text, required): Provide any relevant licenses or credentials.
* **Client signature** (field 23, text, required): Sign below to agree to the terms.
* **Photographer signature** (field 24, text, required): Sign below to agree to the terms.
* **Date signed** (field 25, date, required): Date below to confirm you have signed.
