# Preschool Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a photo session with the preschool. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure we capture the perfect shots.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the email address of the parent/guardian in the "Email" field.
5. Select the date of the photo session in the "Photo Session Date" field.
6. Select the time of the photo session in the "Photo Session Time" field.
7. Choose the desired photo package from the "Photo Package" field.
8. Select the photo location from the "Photo Location" field.
9. Select the type(s) of photos desired (e.g., color or black and white) in the "Photo Type" field.
10. If applicable, enter the number of children attending the session in the "Number of Children" field.
11. Choose the number of photos to be taken during the session in the "Number of Photos" field.
12. Select the size of the photos to be taken in the "Photo Size" field.
13. Enter any special instructions or requests in the "Special Instructions" field.
14. Add any additional comments or details about the session in the "Session Notes" field.
15. Finally, enter any additional comments or requests in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`): Enter the child's last name.
* **Parent Name** (`text`): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Photo Session Date** (`date`): Select the date of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Time** (`time`): Select the time of the photo session.
* **Photo Package** (`select_one`): Choose the desired photo package (e.g., Package 1, 2, or 3).
* **Photo Location** (`select_multiple`): Select the photo location (e.g., Studio, On-location, or Both).
* **Photo Type** (`select_multiple`): Select the type(s) of photos desired (e.g., Color, Black and White, or Both).
* **Number of Children** (`number`): Enter the number of children attending the session (if applicable).
* **Number of Photos** (`number`): Enter the number of photos to be taken during the session.
* **Photo Size** (`select_one`): Select the size of the photos to be taken (e.g., Standard, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Special Instructions** (`text`): Enter any special instructions or requests.
* **Session Notes** (`text`): Add any additional comments or details about the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`): Enter any additional comments or requests.
