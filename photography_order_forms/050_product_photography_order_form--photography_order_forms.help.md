# Product Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps customers provide their product photography order details, contact information, and any other relevant information to facilitate a smooth and successful order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the product you wish to have photographed.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
3. Specify any photography details you require, such as the type of photography and any specific requirements.
4. Enter any additional order details, such as the desired date for the photography session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Photography Order** (`product_photography_order_form`, text, required): This field is the main title of the product you wish to have photographed. Please enter the name of the product as it is currently known.

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): This field is where you will provide your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): This field is where you will provide your contact information, including your email and phone number. Please ensure that we can contact you easily.

* **Photography Details** (`photography_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can specify any additional photography details, such as the type of photography (e.g., product catalog, product lifestyle) and any specific requirements you may have.

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional order details, such as the desired date for the photography session. Please be as specific as possible.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
