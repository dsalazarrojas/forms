# Photography And Videography Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their photography and videography requests. It helps us understand their requirements for photo and video services, such as still photography and videography services, and any additional services they may need. The form also aims to gather information about the equipment required for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Photographer Information section with your details as a photographer or videographer.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the request in the Request Details section.
3. Select the services you require from the Services Ordered dropdown menu (you can choose one or more options: still, video, or additional).
4. Mention any specific photo equipment you need in the Photo Equipment field.
5. Specify any required videography equipment in the Videography Equipment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Information**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Enter your name and contact information as a photographer or videographer.
	+ Required: No
* **Request Details**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: Describe the request in a clear and concise manner.
	+ Required: No
* **Services Ordered**
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Purpose: Choose the services you require (still, video, or additional).
	+ Required: No
	+ Options: Still, Video, Additional
* **Photo Equipment**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: If necessary, specify any specific photo equipment you need for the job.
	+ Required: No
* **Videography Equipment**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Purpose: If necessary, specify any specific videography equipment you need for the job.
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information to ensure we can serve you best.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at contact@yourcompany.com.
* Please note that some fields are not required, but filling them out can help us better understand your needs.
* You can choose more than one service option in the Services Ordered field.
