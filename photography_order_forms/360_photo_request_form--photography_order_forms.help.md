# 360 Photo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Photo Request Form is designed to collect essential information from clients for photography projects, ensuring that all necessary details are captured to deliver high-quality services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full name** and **Email address** in the **Personal Information** section.
2.  Provide your **Order date**, **Item or product name**, **Quantity**, and **Unit price** in the **Product Details** section.
3.  Choose the **Primary Photo style** and **Camera type** you prefer for the shoot.
4.  Select your desired **Resolution** and **Color scheme** for the photos.
5.  Specify the **Shoot location** where you'd like the photos to be taken.
6.  In the **Delivery Preferences** section, choose your preferred **Delivery method** and provide the **Delivery address** if applicable.
7.  Select a preferred **Delivery date** and **Time** for receiving the photos, if possible.
8.  Add any **Additional comments or notes** about the project in the **Additional Notes** section.
9.  Finally, choose your preferred **Payment method** and **Payment terms** for the payment arrangement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (Required): Enter your full legal name.
*   **Email address** (Required): We will send confirmation to this address.
*   **Phone number** (Optional): Optional, for urgent updates.
*   **Order date** (Required): Date of order submission.
*   **Item or product name** (Required): Name of the product to capture.
*   **Quantity** (Required): Enter number of items.
*   **Unit price** (Required): Price per item in USD.
*   **Photo style** (Required): Choose the primary style for the shoot.
*   **Camera type** (Optional): Preferred camera for the shoot.
*   **Resolution** (Optional): Desired image resolution.
*   **Color scheme** (Optional): Select all that apply.
*   **Shoot location** (Optional): Address or description of location.
*   **Preferred delivery method** (Required): Choose how you want the photos delivered.
*   **Delivery address** (Optional): Provide if shipping is selected.
*   **Requested delivery date** (Optional): Preferred date for receiving photos.
*   **Preferred delivery time** (Optional): Time of day you prefer the delivery.
*   **Special instructions or customization notes** (Optional): Any specific requests for the shoot.
*   **Payment method** (Required): Choose how you will pay.
*   **Payment terms** (Optional): Select preferred payment arrangement.
*   **Additional comments or notes** (Optional): Any other information or questions.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any delays or issues with your request.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
