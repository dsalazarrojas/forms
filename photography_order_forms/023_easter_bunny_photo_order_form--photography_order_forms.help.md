# Easter Bunny Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Bunny Photo Order Form is designed to collect information for a photography session, specifically the Easter Bunny photo shoot. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that all necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that it is filled out correctly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Date of the photo shoot**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Time of the photo shoot**: Enter the time of the photography session.
* **Photo description**: Enter a brief description of the photo shoot.
* **Photo Order Number**: Enter your photo order number (if you have one).
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Photo Location**: Enter the location of the photography session.
* **Photo Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for the photo shoot.
* **Photo Order Type**: Select the type of photo shoot you are interested in (Easter Egg Hunt, Easter Eggstravagant, or Easter Eggstravagant Deluxe).
* **Other (please specify)**: Enter a custom photo shoot type if not listed (optional).
* **Number of prints**: Enter the number of prints you would like to order.
* **Photo size**: Select the size of the photo you would like (8x10, 11x14, 16x20, 20x24, or Other).
* **Photo orientation**: Select the orientation of the photo you would like (Portrait, Landscape, or Square).
* **Photo finish**: Select the finish of the photo you would like (Color, B/W, or Sepia).
* **Photo print quality**: Select the quality of the photo print you would like (Gloss, Matte, or Standard).
* **Quantity of prints**: Enter the number of prints you would like to order.
* **Desired delivery date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your photos.
* **Desired delivery time**: Enter the time you would like to receive your photos.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests for the photo shoot.
* **Photo signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to our payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Signature**: Enter your signature indicating agreement to payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Note**: Enter any notes or comments about your payment.
* **Photo Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to our photo terms and conditions.
* **Photo Signature**: Enter your signature indicating agreement to photo terms and conditions.
