# Pet Portrait Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a pet photography session. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and successful photography experience for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your pet's name, breed, and any other relevant information.
2.  Fill out your contact information, including your name and phone number.
3.  Choose the date and time of your pet's visit for the photo session.
4.  Select the location of the visit.
5.  Choose a photo session type (Indoor or Outdoor).
6.  Select a package option (Deluxe, Standard, or Basic).
7.  Add any additional comments or requests you have.
8.  Finally, provide the order and signature dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter your pet's name.
- **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, select one, required): Select your pet's breed (e.g., Golden Retriever, Poodle, Bulldog).
- **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the pet's owner.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit for the photo session.
- **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit for the photo session.
- **Location of Visit** (`location_of_visit`, text, optional): Enter the location where the photo session will take place.
- **Photo Session** (`photo_session`, select one, required): Choose between Indoor and Outdoor photo sessions.
- **Package Option** (`package_option`, select multiple, required): Select one or more package options (Deluxe, Standard, Basic).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you have.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to order the photos.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to sign the contract or agree to the terms.

By following this guide, you'll be able to fill out the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a successful and enjoyable pet photography experience.
