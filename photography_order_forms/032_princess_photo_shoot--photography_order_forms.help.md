# princess_photo_shoot - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a photo shoot with the photographer. It gathers the client's contact information and details about the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of photo shoot you would like to book from the "Session Type" dropdown menu:
	* **Royal Portraits**: Formal photos for special occasions.
	* **Princess Party**: Fun and playful photos for children's parties.
	* **Family Photos**: Photos of you and your loved ones.
	* **Other**: Specify your own custom photo shoot type.
6. Choose a date for your photo shoot in the "Date" field.
7. Select a time for your photo shoot from the available options.
8. Add any additional **Note** or comments for the photographer in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of photo shoot you would like to book:
	+ **Royal Portraits**: Formal photos for special occasions.
	+ **Princess Party**: Fun and playful photos for children's parties.
	+ **Family Photos**: Photos of you and your loved ones.
	+ **Other**: Specify your own custom photo shoot type.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your photo shoot.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your photo shoot. 
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the photographer.
