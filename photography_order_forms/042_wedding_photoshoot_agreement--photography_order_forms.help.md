# Wedding Photoshoot Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Wedding Photoshoot Agreement" form is used to capture essential information for a wedding photoshoot, ensuring that both the photographer and client are on the same page about the shoot's details, including photographer and client contact information, event details, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by the "required" label in the field explanations below.
2. Enter the photographer's details in the "Photographer Details" field.
3. Enter the client's details in the "Client Details" field.
4. Provide event details in the "Event Details" field.
5. Confirm the photoshoot agreement in the "Photoshoot Agreement" field.
6. Review and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" field.
7. Sign and date the "Signature" field.
8. Enter the photoshoot date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Photoshoot Agreement**: Provide a brief description of the photoshoot.
- **Photographer Details**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about your role as the photographer.
- **Client Details**: Enter your client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about their role.
- **Event Details**: Provide the event details, including date, time, location, and any other relevant information.
- **Photoshoot Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the photoshoot.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the photoshoot.
- **Signature**: Sign and date the agreement to confirm your commitment to the photoshoot.
- **Date**: Enter the date of the photoshoot.
- **Time**: Enter the time of the photoshoot.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the agreement.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays or cancellations.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer or client to discuss.
