# Ata Ur Rehman Photography School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for Ata Ur Rehman Photography School. It is designed to collect essential information from potential students to assess their suitability for our courses. The form will help us understand your background, preferences, and requirements, ensuring that we provide you with the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
2. Fill in your address, city, and country of residence.
3. Select your current education level.
4. Indicate your prior photography experience.
5. Choose your preferred learning mode (in-person or online).
6. Select all the courses you are interested in learning.
7. Specify your preferred course duration.
8. Choose your preferred class schedule.
9. Select your main learning goal.
10. Indicate if you have a camera or design software available.
11. Choose how you heard about us.
12. Select your preferred payment mode.
13. If you have any questions or special requests, please let us know in the "Any Questions or Special Requests" field.
14. Confirm your commitment to completing the course.
14. Finally, enter the date you registered for the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required): This is your last name.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): This is your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **WhatsApp Number** (`student_whatsapp`, `text`, required): This is your WhatsApp contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is your birth date.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This is your full address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): This is the city where you reside.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): This is the country where you reside.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us understand your current educational background.
* **Prior Photography Experience** (`photography_experience`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates your level of experience in photography.
* **Preferred Learning Mode** (`learning_mode`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us determine your learning preferences (in-person or online).
* **Courses You Want to Learn** (`courses_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the courses you are interested in learning.
* **Preferred Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates how long you are willing to commit to the course.
* **Preferred Class Schedule** (`class_schedule_preference`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us determine your availability for classes.
* **Main Learning Goal** (`learning_goal`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us understand your main motivation for learning photography.
* **Photography Equipment Available** (`equipment_available`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates if you have a camera or design software available.
* **Type of Camera/Equipment** (`equipment_type`, `text`, optional): If you have a camera or design software, please specify the type.
* **Design Software Available** (`software_available`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates if you have Adobe software installed.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required): This field helps us understand how you found out about our school.
* **Payment Mode Preference** (`payment_mode`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates how you prefer to pay for the course.
* **Any Questions or Special Requests** (`additional_questions`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or special requests, please let us know here.
* **I am committed to completing the course** (`commitment_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): This field confirms your commitment to completing the course.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): This field indicates the date you registered for the course.
