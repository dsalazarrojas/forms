# Photography Session Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book and finalize a photography session. It gathers necessary details about the session, including the location, date and time, coverage areas, and terms of service. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and successful photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1: Choose a Session Location**
	+ Select a location for the photo shoot from the options provided. This will be the site where the photographer will take your photos.
- **Step 2: Choose a Session Date**
	+ Enter the date of the photo shoot in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This date will be used to schedule the shoot and ensure the photographer is available.
- **Step 3: Choose a Session Time**
	+ Select the time of the photo shoot in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`. This time will be used to schedule the shoot and ensure everyone is on the same page.
- **Step 4: Choose Coverage Areas**
	+ Select which areas of the location will be covered during the shoot. Options include:
		- Location
		- Environment
		- Model Release
		- Property Release
		- Model/Subject
		- Property
- **Step 5: Choose Session Duration**
	+ Enter the expected duration of the photo shoot in minutes. This will help the photographer plan the shoot and ensure enough time is allocated.
- **Step 6: Enter Session Details**
	+ Provide any additional details or instructions about the photo shoot. This can include any specific needs or requests you have.
- **Step 7: Agree to Terms and Conditions**
	+ Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the photo shoot.
- **Step 8: E-Sign the Form**
	+ Enter your email address to digitally sign the form and finalize the agreement.
- **Step 9: E-Sign Again**
	+ Enter your email address again to confirm your E-Signature and finalize the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Location**: Enter the location where the photo shoot will take place.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the photo shoot in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the photo shoot in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Coverage Areas**: Choose which areas of the location to cover during the shoot:
	+ Location: The location itself, such as a park or studio.
	+ Environment: The surroundings or setting of the location, such as natural or urban.
	+ Model Release: The model or subject of the shoot, such as a person or object.
	+ Property Release: The property or location where the shoot will take place, such as a private residence or public space.
	+ Model/Subject: A combination of a model and property release.
	+ Property: The property or location where the shoot will take place.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the photo shoot in minutes.
* **Session Details**: Enter any additional details or instructions about the photo shoot.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the photo shoot.
* **E-Signature**: Enter your email address to digitally sign the form and finalize the agreement.
* **E-Signature 2**: Enter your email address again to confirm your E-Signature and finalize the agreement.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure a smooth and successful photo shoot.
- Make sure to read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer for clarification.
