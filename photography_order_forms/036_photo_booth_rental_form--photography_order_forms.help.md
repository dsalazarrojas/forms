# Photo Booth Rental Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for clients or customers who want to inquire about renting a photo booth for an event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact name and email address in the "contact_name" and "contact_email" fields, respectively.
2. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "contact_phone" field.
3. Provide a brief message about your event and the reason for renting a photo booth, if needed.
4. Select the date(s) you would like to rent the photo booth for in the "Rental Dates" field.
5. Choose the type of photo booth you would like to rent from the "Photo Booth Type" dropdown menu.
6. Indicate how many photo booths you would like to rent in the "Photo Booth Count" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required): Your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): A brief message about your event and rental.
* **Rental Dates** (`rental_dates`, date, required): The date(s) you would like to rent the photo booth.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The end date of the rental period.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The start date of the rental period.
* **Photo Booth Type** (`photo_booth_type`, select_one, optional): The type of photo booth you would like to rent.
* **Photo Booth Count** (`photo_booth_count`, number, optional): The number of photo booths you would like to rent.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* You can rent a photo booth for any date and duration you need.
* If you have any specific requests or needs, please include them in the message field.
* We will be in touch with you to confirm the details of your rental after you submit this form.
