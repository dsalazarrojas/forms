# Digital Photo Transfer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order digital photos. Please fill in the required information to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select your preferred photo size from the options provided.
3. Choose the type of photo you would like to receive (color or black and white).
4. Enter any additional design details if needed.
5. Add any review comments or messages.
6. Click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the digital photo.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with the status of your order.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **photo_count** (`photo_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of photos you would like to transfer.
* **photo_size** (`photo_size`, select, optional): Choose the size of the photo you would like to transfer from the options:
	+ 4x6
	+ 5x7
	+ 8x10
* **photo_type** (`photo_type`, select, optional): Choose the type of photo you would like to transfer from the options:
	+ color
	+ black and white
* **design_details** (`design_details`, text, optional): Enter any special design details or instructions for your photo transfer.
* **review_message** (`review_message`, text, optional): Enter any review comments or messages for our staff to review your order.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please make sure to fill in all required fields (name and email) to ensure we can process your order correctly.
