# kings_and_queens_photography_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "kings_and_queens_photography_order_form" is a form designed to collect information from users for a photography order. This form is intended for users who want to book a photography service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a Date for the photo shoot in the Date field.
6. Select a Time for the photo shoot in the Time field.
7. Choose one option from the Select One dropdown list.
8. Choose multiple options from the Select Multiple dropdown list.
9. Enter a single-line note in the Note field.
10. Enter another single-line note in the Note 2 field.
11. Choose one option from the Select One 2 dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, First Name, optional): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (text, Last Name, optional): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (email, Email, optional): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, Phone, optional): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **Date** (date, Date, optional): Select a date for the photo shoot.
* **Time** (time, Time, optional): Select a time for the photo shoot.
* **Select One** (select_one, Select One, optional): Choose one option from the list to customize your photo shoot.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, Select Multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list to customize your photo shoot.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Note** (note, Note, optional): Enter a single-line note about your preferences.
* **Note 2** (note, Note 2, optional): Enter another single-line note about your preferences.
* **Select One 2** (select_one, Select One 2, optional): Choose one option from the list to further customize your photo shoot.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- Please review your selections carefully to ensure they match your preferences.
