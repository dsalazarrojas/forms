# Building Information Model Access Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to building information models (BIMs) for various stakeholders, ensuring that all necessary information is provided and that the requesting party agrees to the terms and conditions of access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your name, job title, company, email, phone number, and project name.
2. Select the project phase and purpose of access you need.
3. Choose the type of access you are requesting (e.g., view-only, comment and markup, edit).
4. Specify the model elements you need access to (e.g., architectural, structural, MEP).
5. Select the software platform you will use to access the BIM.
6. Choose the duration of access you need (e.g., one-time, less than 1 month, project duration).
7. Provide the start date and end date of access (if applicable).
8. Specify the number of team members who need access.
9. Provide a list of team members with their names and emails (if applicable).
10. Agree to the data security and confidentiality agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as the representative of the company or team requesting access.
* **Job Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your role in the project.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your application.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Number** (`project_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the project number.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, text, required): Enter the location or address of the building.
* **Project Phase** (`project_phase`, select_one, required): Select the current phase of the project.
* **Purpose of Access** (`access_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for requesting access.
* **Access Description** (`access_description`, text, required): Describe in detail why you need access to the BIM.
* **Access Level Requested** (`access_level`, select_one, required): Choose the type of access you need (e.g., view-only, comment and markup).
* **Model Elements Needed** (`model_elements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the model elements you need access to (e.g., architectural, structural, MEP).
* **Software Platform** (`software_platform`, select_one, required): Choose the software platform you will use to access the BIM.
* **Access Duration** (`access_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of access you need (e.g., one-time, less than 1 month).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of access.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of access.
* **Number of Team Members** (`team_members`, number, required): Enter the number of team members who need access.
* **Team Member List** (`team_member_list`, text, optional): If applicable, enter a list of team members with their names and emails.
* **Data Security Agreement** (`data_security_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to the data security terms.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to maintain confidentiality.
* **Intended Use of Model** (`intended_use`, text, required): Describe how you will use the model.
* **Distribution Plan** (`distribution_plan`, select_one, required): Choose how you will share the model (e.g., internal use only, with approved parties).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Attach any supporting documents required.
* **Approval Required From** (`approval_required`, text, optional): Specify who needs to approve access.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information relevant to your access request.
* **Yes/No** (`yes_no`, boolean, required): This field is not actually required as it is part of the agreement, and will be checked upon submission. However, it can be displayed as a field in the form for the user. It will not impact the form's functionality or output. 

Note that the "Yes/No" field will not impact the form's functionality, and will be treated as a required field for the end-user but not in the actual submission. 

Note that the "Supporting Documents" field will not be required in the form submission, but it can be displayed for user input for the end-user's information. 

Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
