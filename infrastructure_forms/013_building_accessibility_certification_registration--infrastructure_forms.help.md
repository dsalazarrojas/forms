# Building Accessibility Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Accessibility Certification Registration form is designed to collect information about a building's accessibility features and certification status. This process evaluates the building's compliance with accessibility standards and ensures equal access and opportunities for people with disabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please start by filling out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for the certification process.
2.  Please provide accurate and truthful information about your building's accessibility features and certification status.
3.  For fields with options, choose the correct answer that best describes your building's condition.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the guidelines or consult with the relevant authorities.
5.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property owner or manager name**: \* Please enter the name of the property owner or manager.
* **Contact email**: \* Enter the contact email of the property owner or manager.
* **Contact phone**: \* Enter the contact phone number of the property owner or manager.
* **Building name**: \* Enter the building's name.
* **Building address**: \* Enter the building's address.
* **Building type**: \* Choose the building type from the dropdown menu (e.g., Office building, Residential, etc.).
* **Year of construction**: \* Enter the year the building was constructed.
* **Year of last major renovation** (if applicable): \* Enter the year of the last major renovation, if applicable.
* **Total number of floors**: \* Enter the total number of floors in the building.
* **Total square footage**: \* Enter the approximate total square footage of the building.
* **Current occupancy**: \* Enter the number of daily occupants in the building.
* **Main entrance accessible**: \* Choose whether the main entrance is wheelchair-accessible or not.
* **Accessible parking spaces**: \* Choose whether there are adequate or limited parking spaces available.
* **Ramp availability**: \* Choose whether all entrances, the main entrance, or no ramps are present.
* **Elevators or vertical access**: \* Choose whether fully functional or limited access elevators are available.
* **Accessible restrooms**: \* Choose whether all or some floors have accessible restrooms.
* **Accessible routes throughout building**: \* Choose whether there are comprehensive or partial accessible routes.
* **Braille and tactile signage**: \* Choose whether braille and tactile signage are present or not.
* **Visual aids and announcements**: \* Choose whether comprehensive or limited visual aids and announcements are available.
* **Emergency egress routes**: \* Choose whether these routes are fully accessible or not.
* **Audible and visual emergency alarms**: \* Choose whether both or one type of alarm is present.
* **Designated accessibility coordinator**: \* Choose whether a named staff member or shared responsibility is designated.
* **Recent modifications**: \* Describe any recent accessibility improvements made.
* **Currently certified as accessible**: \* Choose whether the building currently holds certification or not.
* **Date of last certification**: \* Enter the date of the last certification.
* **Authorize building assessment**: \* Choose whether to authorize the building assessment.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the guidelines or consult with the relevant authorities.
*   Incomplete forms may not be processed. If you are unable to fill out all the required fields, please contact us for assistance.
*   Please choose the correct options for each field based on your building's condition.
