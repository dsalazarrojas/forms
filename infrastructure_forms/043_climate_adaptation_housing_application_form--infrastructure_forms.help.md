## Climate Adaptation Housing Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Climate Adaptation Housing Application Form is designed to help homeowners and property owners in high-risk areas assess their climate vulnerabilities and identify potential improvements to enhance their property's resilience to extreme weather events. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you determine the best course of action to protect your property and ensure your safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the form carefully and understanding the purpose.
2. Begin with the "Applicant Information" section, where you will provide your basic details such as name, date of birth, email, phone number, and household size.
3. Move on to the "Household Information" section, where you will answer questions about your household income, the number of dependents, and your current property type and ownership status.
4. In the "Climate Vulnerability Assessment" section, assess the climate risks your property faces and describe any previous climate-related damage.
5. Identify the adaptation needs and improvements you require, and select your top priority improvement.
6. Finally, provide any additional information or comments you have about your climate adaptation needs and improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`): This is a required field where you will provide your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): This is a required field where you will provide your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email Address** (`email`): This is a required field where you will provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): This is a required field where you will provide your phone number.
* **Number of Household Members** (`household_size`): This is a required field where you will provide the number of members in your household.
* **Number of Dependents** (`dependents`): This is an optional field where you will provide the number of dependents you have.
* **Annual Household Income** (`household_income`): This is a required field where you will select your household's annual income range.
* **Can You Provide Income Verification?** (`income_verification`): This is a required field where you will select whether you can provide income verification or not.
* **Current Property Type** (`property_type`): This is a required field where you will select the type of your current property (e.g., Single Family Home, Multi-Family Home, etc.).
* **Do You Own or Rent?** (`property_ownership`): This is a required field where you will select whether you own or rent your property.
* **Years at Current Property** (`years_at_property`): This is an optional field where you will provide the number of years you have been living at your current property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): This is a required field where you will provide the street address of your property.
* **Climate Risks Your Property Faces** (`climate_risks`): This is a required field where you will select all the climate risks your property faces (e.g., Flooding, Wildfire, Drought, etc.).
* **Previous Climate-Related Damage** (`previous_damage`): This is a required field where you will select whether you have experienced climate-related damage to your property.
* **Describe Previous Damage** (`damage_description`): This is an optional field where you will describe any previous climate-related damage to your property.
* **Current Vulnerabilities or Concerns** (`current_vulnerabilities`): This is a required field where you will describe your current vulnerabilities or concerns about climate impacts.
* **Desired Climate Adaptation Improvements** (`desired_improvements`): This is a required field where you will select all the climate adaptation improvements you desire.
* **Top Priority Improvement** (`improvement_priority`): This is a required field where you will select your top priority improvement.
* **Preferred Timeline for Improvements** (`preferred_timeline`): This is an optional field where you will select your preferred timeline for the improvements.
* **Commitment to Building Resilience** (`commitment_to_resilience`): This is a required field where you will select your level of commitment to building resilience.
* **Funding Sources You Can Access** (`funding_sources`): This is an optional field where you will select all the funding sources you can access.
* **Do You Have a Preferred Contractor?** (`contractor_preference`): This is an optional field where you will select whether you have a preferred contractor.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information or comments.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to help us better understand your climate adaptation needs.
* Make sure to provide detailed descriptions for any previous climate-related damage and your current vulnerabilities or concerns.
* Select all the climate adaptation improvements that apply to your property and prioritize them based on your needs.
* Remember to commit to building resilience and select a funding source that works best for you.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in the "Additional Information or Comments" field.
