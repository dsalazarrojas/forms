# Critical Infrastructure Security Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you identify potential security risks associated with critical infrastructure, such as power plants, water treatment facilities, or other essential systems that support the functioning of a city or community. By completing this form, you'll be able to assess the current security posture of these critical systems and identify areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your current situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question or have additional information that's not covered, please provide an explanation or note in the comments section.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `note`, not required): This section is intended to collect general information about the critical infrastructure system you're assessing. Please provide any relevant background details, such as system description, location, or contact information.

### Physical Security (Page 2)

* **Physical Security** (`physical_security`, `text`, not required): This section is meant to assess the physical security measures in place to protect the critical infrastructure system. Describe any fences, gates, cameras, or other physical barriers that prevent unauthorized access.

### Personnel Security (Page 3)

* **Personnel Security** (`personnel_security`, `text`, not required): This section is focused on the security practices of personnel involved with the critical infrastructure system. Describe any background checks, security training, or access control measures in place.

### Access Controls (Page 4)

* **Access Controls** (`access_controls`, `select_one`, not required): This section is intended to evaluate the controls in place to limit access to sensitive areas or resources within the critical infrastructure system. Choose the level that best describes your current access controls:
	+ Low: Basic controls, such as badges or keys
	+ Moderate: Combination of physical and digital controls, such as biometric identification and access logs
	+ High: Advanced controls, such as multi-factor authentication and intrusion detection systems

### Asset Protection (Page 5)

* **Asset Protection** (`asset_protection`, `number`, not required): This section is focused on the measures taken to protect critical assets within the infrastructure system. Please describe any measures, such as surveillance cameras, alarms, or secure storage, used to safeguard these assets.

### Incident Management (Page 6)

* **Incident Management** (`incident_management`, `text`, not required): This section is intended to assess the incident response procedures and protocols in place for the critical infrastructure system. Describe any incident response teams, communication protocols, or procedures for reporting and managing incidents.

### Security Management (Page 7)

* **Security Management** (`security_management`, `text`, not required): This section is focused on the overall security management practices of the critical infrastructure system. Describe any security policies, procedures, or protocols in place to ensure the system's security.

### Training (Page 8)

* **Training** (`training`, `text`, not required): This section is intended to evaluate the security training and awareness practices for personnel involved with the critical infrastructure system. Describe any security awareness programs, training sessions, or workshops held for employees.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, please provide a comment or note to clarify.
* This form is a starting point for further investigation and mitigation efforts.
