# Report Storm Drain Issues In Your Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about storm drain issues in your community. This will help city or government officials identify and address problems before they become major concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Submit a Report**: Start by providing a brief description of the issue you've encountered.
2. **Upload Photos**: If possible, upload any relevant photos or videos that demonstrate the problem.
3. **Report Location**: Enter the address or location of the issue.
4. **Describe the Problem**: Explain the details of the issue, including its severity and any previous attempts to address it.
5. **Report Date and Time**: Provide the date and time you observed the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit a Report** (`submit_a_report`, text, optional): This is the main area where you can describe the problem you've encountered with the storm drain.
* **Upload Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): If you have any photos or videos that showcase the issue, you can upload them here. This will help officials better understand the situation.
* **Report Location** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address or location where the storm drain issue is happening. This will help officials pinpoint the location and prioritize their response.
* **Describe the Problem** (`problem_description`, text, optional): Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue, including how long it's been happening and any previous attempts to address it.
* **Report Date and Time** (`report_date`/`report_time`, date/time, optional): Enter the date and time you observed the issue. This will help officials track the issue over time.
