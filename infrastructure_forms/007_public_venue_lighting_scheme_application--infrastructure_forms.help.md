# Public Venue Lighting Scheme Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a public venue lighting scheme application. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the project, including project dates, venue requirements, and organizer requirements. Please fill out this form to initiate the application process for public venue lighting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Double-check your answers for completeness and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief description of your project, including the location and purpose of the lighting scheme.
* **Dates** (`dates`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide the start and end dates for your project. If your project is a one-time event, enter the dates of the event.
* **Venue Requirements** (`venue_requirements`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe the lighting needs and requirements for the venue. This includes any unique features or conditions that should be considered when designing and installing the lighting scheme.
* **Organizer Requirements** (`organizer_requirements`, text, required: false)
	+ Specify any specific requirements or restrictions the venue organizer has for the lighting scheme. This could include any branding or sponsorship considerations.

## Tips
- Please be accurate and thorough when completing this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
- Double-check your answers for completeness and consistency before submitting the form.
