# Installation And Disruption Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Installation and Disruption Report form is designed to capture and document information about disruptions that occur during the installation process of infrastructure. This form helps to improve the accuracy of installation and maintenance processes by providing standardized data collection and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the fields below and ensure that you are reporting a real disruption.
*   Enter a short description of the disruption that occurred during the installation process.
*   Fill out each field with as much detail as possible, including any specific dates, times, locations, and causes of the disruption.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **installation_outcome** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of what happened during the installation or service disruption.
*   **service_interruption** (text, required/optional): Enter a description of the service or installation that was disrupted.
*   **date_of_occurrence** (date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the disruption occurred.
*   **time_of_occurrence** (time, required/optional): Enter the time when the disruption occurred.
*   **duration_of_disruption** (number, required/optional): Enter the length of time the disruption lasted.
*   **location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location where the disruption occurred.
*   **description_of_disruption** (text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the disruption.
*   **impact** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the impact the disruption had on the installation process.
*   **root_cause** (text, required/optional): Enter the reason for the disruption, if known.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
*   Ensure that each field is filled out for every real disruption that occurs during the installation process.
*   Review and proofread your form before submitting it to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
