# Cloud Commerce Infrastructure Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your cloud commerce infrastructure application needs in order for our team to provide personalized solutions and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions and provide as much detail as possible about your business and infrastructure requirements. You can select multiple options for questions with multiple-choice answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (1)**: This is the name of your company or business.
* **Applicant Name (2)**: This is the full name of the person filling out this form.
* **Job Title (3)**: This is your position or role within the company.
* **Email Address (4)**: This is your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (5)**: This is your contact phone number.
* **Business Information (6)**: This is a notes field for you to provide additional details about your business. You can use this field to provide any relevant information about your business that may be helpful for our team to know.
* **Type of Business (7)**: This question asks for the type of business you have. Please select one of the options that best fits your business model.
* **Annual Revenue (8)**: This question asks for your annual revenue. Please select one of the options that best represents your revenue.
* **Monthly Transaction Volume (9)**: This is the average number of transactions you process each month.
* **Current Commerce Platform (11)**: This is the platform you are currently using for your commerce operations.
* **Current Infrastructure Type (12)**: This question asks for the type of infrastructure you currently have in place. Please select one of the options that best fits your infrastructure setup.
* **Estimated Monthly Unique Visitors (13)**: This is the average number of unique visitors to your website or application.
* **Peak Traffic Multiplier (14)**: This is the peak traffic as a multiple of your average traffic.
* **Type of Cloud Commerce Infrastructure Needed (16)**: This question asks for the type of infrastructure you need to migrate to or upgrade your current setup. Please select one of the options that best fits your needs.
* **Target Cloud Platform (17)**: This question asks for the cloud platform you are interested in migrating to. Please select one of the options that best fits your needs.
* **Required Infrastructure Components (19)**: This question asks for the components you require for your cloud commerce infrastructure. Please select all the options that apply to your needs.
* **Required Uptime SLA (20)**: This question asks for the required uptime for your application. Please select one of the options that best fits your needs.
* **Compliance Requirements (21)**: This question asks for any compliance requirements you have. Please select all the options that apply to your needs.
* **Desired Implementation Timeline (23)**: This question asks for your desired implementation timeline. Please select one of the options that best fits your needs.
* **Expected Business Impact (24)**: This is a text field for you to provide any expected business impacts or benefits from migrating or upgrading your infrastructure.
* **Any Additional Requirements or Constraints (25)**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional requirements or constraints that may be relevant to your project.

## Tips
- Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
- This form is designed to be filled out by customers who are interested in migrating or upgrading their existing infrastructure. If you are already a customer, please contact your account manager or sales representative to discuss your needs and schedule a consultation.
