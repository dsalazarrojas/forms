# Building Safety Partnership Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Building Safety Partnership Application form is designed for organizations to apply for partnership with [Your Organization/Company Name]. This form helps us understand your organization's experience, expertise, and goals for partnering with us to promote building safety. Please ensure you submit all required information and attach relevant documents to support your application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the application form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Select the correct answers to each question, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Attach any supporting documents, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): The date you are submitting this application.
* **-- Organization Information --** (`organization_section`, note, optional): Details about your organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): The legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of organization from the provided options: Non-Profit, Government Agency, Private Company, Educational Institution, Trade Association, Consulting Firm, or Other.
* **Primary Focus** (`organization_focus`, text, required): The main area of expertise your organization is focused on.
* **Headquarters Address** (`headquarters_address`, text, required): The full address of your organization's headquarters.
* **-- Primary Contact --** (`contact_section`, note, optional): Main point of contact for this application.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Full name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Job title or position of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Direct email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Direct phone number of the contact person.
* **-- Experience and Credentials --** (`experience_section`, note, optional): Safety expertise and experience details.
* **Years of Experience in Safety** (`years_in_safety`, number, required): Number of years of experience in the industry.
* **Building Safety Expertise** (`building_safety_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific safety areas your organization has experience in from the provided options.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant professional certifications.
* **Describe Previous Safety Initiatives** (`case_studies`, text, required): Examples of past safety-related projects or initiatives your organization has completed.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional): Number of staff members involved in safety-related work.
* **-- Partnership Goals --** (`partnership_goals`, note, optional): Expected outcomes from partnership.
* **Desired Focus Areas for Partnership** (`partnership_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific areas you hope to achieve through this partnership from the provided options.
* **Target Building Types** (`target_buildings`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of buildings you wish to focus on.
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, text, required): Service area of your organization.
* **Describe Your Safety Program** (`program_description`, text, required): Detailed overview of your safety program.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Improvements or results you expect from this partnership.
* **Resource Requirements** (`resource_requirements`, text, optional): Budget, space, or equipment needed for the partnership.
* **Supporting Documents** (`documents_attached`, select_one, optional): Whether you are attaching supporting documents to your application.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): Additional relevant certifications.
