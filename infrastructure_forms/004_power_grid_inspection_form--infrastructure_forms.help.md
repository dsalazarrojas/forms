# Power Grid Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Grid Inspection Form is designed to collect data on the status of power grid assets during inspection, including condition, issues, and the date of the inspection. This form aims to streamline and standardize the inspection process for utility companies, regulatory agencies, and other organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate asset being inspected from the dropdown options.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. List the team members attending the inspection.
4. Select the condition of the grid asset from the options provided.
5. Provide a description of any findings or issues observed during the inspection.
6. Select any relevant photos taken during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grid Asset**: A text field where you can enter the name or identifier of the grid asset being inspected. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Inspection Date**: A date field where you can enter the date of the inspection. (Required: False, Type: Date)
* **Team Members**: A field for selecting multiple team members attending the inspection from the available options. (Required: False, Type: Select Multiple)
* **Grid Condition**: A field for selecting the condition of the grid asset from the provided options. (Required: False, Type: Select One)
* **Findings**: A text field where you can describe any issues or problems observed during the inspection. (Required: False, Type: Note)
* **Photos**: A field for selecting multiple photos taken during the inspection from the available options. (Required: False, Type: Select Multiple)

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately select the grid asset being inspected.
- Ensure to provide clear and concise descriptions of any findings or issues.
- Select all relevant photos taken during the inspection to provide a comprehensive record.
- This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel, and information collected is for the purpose of asset management, maintenance, and improvement.
- If you are unsure about any field or option, please reach out to the form creator or your organization's asset management team for clarification.
