# Power Line Integrity Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide inspectors in collecting data during power line inspections. It helps in documenting the condition of power lines, identifying potential hazards, and tracking maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter the inspector's name and other relevant details in the "Inspector" field.
3. Select the status of the power line from the options provided in the "Powerline Status" field.
4. Flag any hazards identified during the inspection in the "Hazards Flagged" field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "Comments" and "Notes" fields.
6. Indicate whether the inspection was completed or not in the "Inspection Completed" field.
7. Choose the maintenance status of the power line from the options in the "Maintenance Status" field.
8. Finally, enter the unique ID of the power line being inspected in the "Powerline ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Inspector** (`inspector`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as the inspector.
- **Powerline Status** (`powerline_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the statuses that apply to the power line being inspected. The options provided are for general statuses and may need to be adjusted based on specific power line types or locations.
- **Hazards Flagged** (`hazard_flag`, select_one, required: false): Flag any hazards identified during the inspection as 'True' or 'False'.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection that are not captured in the other fields.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about the inspection that are not captured in the other fields.
- **Inspection Completed** (`inspection_completed`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the inspection was completed or not. Select 'True' if the inspection was completed and 'False' otherwise.
- **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the maintenance status of the power line from the options provided. This could reflect whether maintenance is in progress, completed, or not applicable.
- **Powerline ID** (`powerline_id`, text, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the power line being inspected, which is essential for tracking and referencing the inspection report.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required information accurately and completely.
- Use the "Powerline Status" field to capture the overall condition of the power line.
- Flagging hazards is crucial for safety and maintenance planning.
- Be detailed in your "Notes" and "Comments" fields for a thorough inspection report.
- The "Inspection Completed" and "Maintenance Status" fields help in tracking the progress and completion of maintenance activities.
- Verify the "Powerline ID" for correct referencing and tracking.
