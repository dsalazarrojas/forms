# Power Plant Efficiency Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help power plant operators and engineers assess the efficiency of a power plant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the relevant fields with the required information. Each page is focused on a specific aspect of the plant's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Audit Results
* **Audit Results** (`audit_results`, `note`, required: false): Please note down any observations or comments about the plant's performance.

### Page 2: Plant Capacity
* **Plant Capacity** (`plant_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the plant's total capacity.
* **Nameplate Rating** (`nameplate_rating`, `number`, required: false): Enter the plant's nameplate rating.
* **Name Plate Rating** (`name_plate_rating`, `number`, required: false): Enter the plant's nameplate rating.
* **Actual Capacity** (`actual_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the actual capacity of the plant.
* **Max Capacity** (`max_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maximum capacity of the plant.

### Page 3: Capacity Details
* **Max Capacity 2** (`max_capacity_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maximum capacity of the plant.
* **Min Capacity** (`min_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the minimum capacity of the plant.
* **Operating Temperature** (`operating_temperature`, `number`, required: false): Enter the operating temperature of the plant.
* **Operating Temperature 2** (`operating_temperature_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the operating temperature of the plant.
* **Max Operating Temp** (`max_operating_temp`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maximum operating temperature of the plant.

### Page 4: Cooling Water Details
* **Cooling Water Usage** (`cooling_water_usage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the cooling water usage of the plant.
* **Cooling Water Usage 2** (`cooling_water_usage_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the cooling water usage of the plant.
* **Cooling Water Consumption** (`cooling_water_consumption`, `number`, required: false): Enter the cooling water consumption of the plant.

### Page 5: Steam Details
* **Steam Usage** (`steam_usage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the steam usage of the plant.
* **Steam Usage 2** (`steam_usage_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the steam usage of the plant.
* **Steam Generation** (`steam_generation`, `number`, required: false): Enter the steam generation of the plant.
* **Steam Generation 2** (`steam_generation_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the steam generation of the plant.

### Page 6: Condensate Water Details
* **Condensate Water Usage** (`condensate_water_usage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the condensate water usage of the plant.
* **Condensate Water Usage 2** (`condensate_water_usage_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the condensate water usage of the plant.
* **Condensate Water Usage 3** (`condensate_water_usage_3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the condensate water usage of the plant.
* **Water Usage** (`water_usage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the water usage of the plant.
* **Water Usage 2** (`water_usage_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the water usage of the plant.
* **Water Usage 3** (`water_usage_3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the water usage of the plant.
