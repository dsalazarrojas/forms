# Smart Building Research Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Building Research Registration Form is designed to collect information from researchers interested in participating in smart building research projects. This form helps to:
* Record participant and researcher information
* Facilitate communication and collaboration among project stakeholders
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date project records

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the building in the "Building Details" field.
3. Indicate your research interests by selecting one or more options from the "Research Interests" dropdown menu.
4. Enter a valid email address in the "Participant Email" field.
5. Enter a phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
6. Enter the name of the researcher(s) associated with this project in the "Researcher Name" field.
7. Enter a valid email address for the researcher(s) in the "Researcher Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the researcher(s) in the "Researcher Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required: false**): Enter the name of the participant registering for the research project. This field is not required but is helpful for record-keeping purposes.
* **Building Details** (`building_details`, note, **required: false**): Describe the building related to the research project in a few sentences. This field is optional but provides context for the research project.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Select one or more options that indicate your research interests in the smart building project. Options are: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **required: false**): Enter a valid email address associated with the participant. A valid email address is required to ensure proper communication and notification. This field is not required but recommended for project organizers.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, **required: false**): Enter a phone number associated with the participant. A phone number is required for efficient communication and follow-up.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, text, **required: false**): Enter the name of the researcher(s) associated with this project. This field is optional but recommended for collaboration and record-keeping purposes.
* **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, email, **required: false**): Enter a valid email address for the researcher(s). A valid email address is required for proper communication and collaboration. This field is not required but recommended.
* **Researcher Phone** (`researcher_phone`, text, **required: false**): Enter a phone number for the researcher(s). A phone number is required for efficient communication and follow-up. This field is not required but recommended.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper project management.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact project organizers for clarification.
