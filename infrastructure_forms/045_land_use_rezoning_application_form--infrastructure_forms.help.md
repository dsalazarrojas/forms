# Land Use Rezoning Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applying for land use rezoning, which involves modifying the zoning designation of a specific property to accommodate new land uses or development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. Ensure that you have attached any necessary supporting documents.
3. Clearly explain the justification for the zoning change in the "Zoning Change Description" field.
4. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **User Email** (email): Enter your official email address.
* **User Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Name** (text): Enter the name of the property you are requesting rezoning for.
* **Property Address** (text): Enter the address of the property you are requesting rezoning for.
* **Zoning Change Description** (text): Clearly explain the justification for the zoning change request. This should include any details about the proposed land use or development.
* **Supporting Documents** (note): Attach any necessary documents that support your rezoning request.
* **Category** (select_one): Select the category of the rezoning request from the provided options.
* **Subcategory** (select_multiple): Select one or more subcategories of the rezoning request from the provided options.
* **Submit** (text): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach all necessary supporting documents.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or department for assistance.
