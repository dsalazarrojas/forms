# Franchise Energy Transition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for franchisees to apply for energy transition support. The purpose of this form is to collect information from franchisees who want to transition their energy sources or energy-efficient solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and honestly. Each field is optional, so you can fill out as much or as little information as you need.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Energy Transition Application Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not required to be filled out.
* **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please provide your first name for better communication.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name. Please provide your last name for better communication.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. We will use it to communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. We will use it to contact you about your application.
* **Location Name**: This field is for the name of your location or business. Please provide the official name of your location.
* **City**: This field is for the city where your location is situated. Please provide the correct city name.
* **State**: This field is for the state where your location is situated. Please provide the correct state name.
* **Country**: This field is for the country where your location is situated. Please provide the correct country name.
* **Motivation**: This field is for your motivation or reason behind your energy transition application. Please provide a brief description of your motivation.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application and get back to you as soon as possible.
