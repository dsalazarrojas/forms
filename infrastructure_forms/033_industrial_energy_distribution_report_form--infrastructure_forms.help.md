# Industrial Energy Distribution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Energy Distribution Report Form is a tool to collect data on industrial energy distribution systems. This form helps in tracking energy sources, operational issues, and maintenance schedules to optimize system performance and energy usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the energy sources that are used in your industrial energy distribution system. You can choose from Utility, Generator, or Other options.
2. Enter the total energy distributed, if available.
3. Describe any operational issues with the equipment or process.
4. Enter the total energy capacity of your system.
5. Enter the peak demand of your system.
6. Enter the average demand of your system.
7. Select the energy distribution channels used in your system, such as Pipe, Wire, or Other.
8. Enter the energy transmission capacity of your system.
9. Select the factors affecting energy consumption, such as Temperature, Humidity, or Other.
10. Choose the operational status of your system, which can be Active, Inactive, or Offline.
11. Select the maintenance schedules for your system, such as Planned or Unscheduled.
12. Enter the operational capacity of your system.
13. Enter the total energy returned, if applicable.
14. Enter the energy returned rate, if applicable.
15. Enter the energy consumption rate, if applicable.
16. Enter the operational performance of your system.
17. Describe any maintenance cost associated with operational equipment.
18. Select the maintenance schedules for your system.
19. Describe any operational issues with equipment or process.
20. Enter any comments or remarks about your system.
21. Enter the name of the person who created the report.
22. Enter the date the report was created.
23. Enter the name of the person who updated the report.
24. Enter the date the report was last updated.
25. Enter the total energy used by your system, if available.
