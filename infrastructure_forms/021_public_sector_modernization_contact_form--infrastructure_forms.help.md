# public_sector_modernization_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The public sector modernization contact form is used to gather information from the public sector about their modernization initiatives. This form helps us understand their needs and requirements for modernization, enabling us to provide more effective support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
	* **Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your full name.
	* **Email** (`contact_email`): Enter a valid email address.
	* **Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
	* **Follow up on** (`follow_up`): Choose a date for follow-up.
2. Provide additional information in the optional field:
	* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional details about your modernization initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Modernization initiative** (`modernization_initiative`): This field is used to describe the modernization initiative you are inquiring about. Provide a brief description of your initiative.
* **Name** (`contact_name`): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name for accurate contact information.
* **Email** (`contact_email`): This field requires a valid email address. Please ensure it is a correct and active email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`): This field is for your phone number. Please enter a correct phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow up on** (`follow_up`): This field is for scheduling follow-up. Select a date for our team to contact you back.
* **Comments** (`comments`): This optional field is for providing additional information about your modernization initiative. This can be a good place to add context or details that might not fit in other fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review and respond to your submission within 3-5 business days.
