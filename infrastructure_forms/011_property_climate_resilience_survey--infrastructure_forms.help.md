# property-climate-resilience-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to assess the climate resilience of properties, specifically in regards to asbestos removal and management. The goal is to collect data on the building's materials, location, and asbestos removal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Select one of the following climate risk categories:
	* Building
	* Civil Infrastructure
	* Coastal Zone
	* Disaster Risk
	* Flood Zone
	* High Risk
	* Low Risk
	* Other
	* Riverine
	* Storm Surge
	* Urban Zone
3. Choose the building materials used for the building:
	* Brick
	* Concrete
	* Glass
	* Metal
	* Steel
	* Wood
	* You can select multiple materials.
4. Enter the building's slope (optional).
5. Enter the building's size (optional).
6. Estimate the climate change risk impact on the building (optional).
7. Enter the building's age (optional).
8. Indicate if the building contains asbestos:
	* True
	* False
9. Enter the date of asbestos removal (if applicable).
10. Choose the method of asbestos removal:
	* Encapsulated
	* Removed
	* Other
	* You can select multiple methods.
11. Enter the cost of asbestos removal (if applicable).
12. Enter the cost of asbestos removal per unit (if applicable).
13. Enter the date of asbestos removal completion (if applicable).
14. Indicate if the building has been issued asbestos removal certificates:
	* True
	* False
15. Select the methods used for asbestos removal (if applicable):
	* Encapsulated
	* Removed
	* Other
	* You can select multiple methods.
16. Indicate if the certificates have been issued for each method:
	* True
	* False
17. Select the authority that issued the certificates:
	* Local Authority
	* National Authority
	* Other
18. Enter the name of the authority that issued the certificates (if applicable).
19. Enter the date of certificate issue (if applicable).
20. Select the authority that issued the certificates for each method:
	* Local Authority
	* National Authority
	* Other
21. Enter the date of issue for each method (if applicable).
22. Enter the name of the authority that issued the certificates for each method (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Climate Risk Category** (`Climate Risk Category`, select_one, required): Select one of the following climate risk categories that best describes the building's location and potential exposure to climate-related risks.
* **Building Materials Used** (`Building Materials Used`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the materials used to construct the building. You can select multiple options.
* **Building Slope** (`Building Slope`, number, optional): Enter the building's slope (e.g., 0-10).
* **Building Size** (`Building Size`, number, optional): Enter the building's size (optional).
* **Climate Change Risk Impact** (`Climate Change Risk Impact`, time, optional): Estimate the climate change risk impact on the building (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Building Age** (`Building Age`, number, optional): Enter the building's age (optional).
* **Building Asbestos** (`Building Asbestos`, select_one, required): Indicate if the building contains asbestos. If the building contains asbestos, please answer "True" or "False".
* **Building Asbestos Removal Date** (`Building Asbestos Removal Date`, date, optional): If the building contains asbestos, enter the date of asbestos removal (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Method** (`Building Asbestos Removal Method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of asbestos removal used. You can select multiple methods.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Cost** (`Building Asbestos Removal Cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of asbestos removal (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Method Cost** (`Building Asbestos Removal Method Cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of asbestos removal per unit (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Completion Date** (`Building Asbestos Removal Completion Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of asbestos removal completion (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the building has been issued asbestos removal certificates. You can select multiple methods.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Methods Used** (`Building Asbestos Removal Methods Used`, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods used for asbestos removal. You can select multiple methods.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issued** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issued`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the certificates have been issued for each method. You can select multiple methods.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issuer** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issuer`, select_one, optional): Select the authority that issued the certificates.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issuer Name** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issuer Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the authority that issued the certificates (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of certificate issue (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Methods Used Certificates Issuer** (`Building Asbestos Removal Methods Used Certificates Issuer`, select_one, optional): Select the authority that issued the certificates for each method.
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date Issued** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date Issued`, date, optional): Enter the date of issue for each method (if applicable).
* **Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date Issuer Name** (`Building Asbestos Removal Certificates Issue Date Issuer Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the authority that issued the certificates for each method (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure the building's climate resilience assessment.
* If the building contains asbestos, please provide detailed information on the removal process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the property's owner or management for guidance.
