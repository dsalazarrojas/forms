# Urban Land Management Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help landowners and municipal officials manage urban land applications, including submissions, uploaded files, organized data, landowner details, municipal details, and review status. It is crucial to accurately and thoroughly fill out this form to ensure efficient processing and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, select "Submit Application" if you wish to submit an application.
2.  On the second page, upload relevant files, such as documents and images, by clicking on the "Upload Files" field.
3.  On the third page, enter the organized application data, which will help officials understand your application better.
4.  On the fourth page, provide landowner details by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5.  On the fifth page, enter municipal details, also by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6.  On the sixth and final page, choose your application status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Review Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submit Application** (application_submission): Select "Yes" if you want to submit the application.
*   **Upload Files** (uploaded_files): Upload relevant documents and images that support your application.
*   **Organized Application Data** (organized_data): Enter relevant application data to help officials understand your submission.
*   **Landowner Details** (landowner_details): Select "Yes" to indicate that you are a landowner.
*   **Municipal Details** (municipal_details): Select "Yes" to indicate that the application pertains to the municipality.
*   **Review Status** (review_status): Select "Active" if the application is under review, or "Inactive" if it has been completed or rejected.
