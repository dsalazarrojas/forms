# Electric Power Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Electric Power Transfer Request Form is designed to help customers request electrical services from our company. The form is used to gather necessary information to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for the service type and assigned tool.
3. Enter the correct transfer date and time.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
5. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electric Service Request** (`electric_power_transfer_request_form_1`, text, optional)
    - Briefly describe the nature of your service request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required)
    - Enter your name as it appears on your identification or account.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, required)
    - Select whether you are requesting a transfer, upgrade, or other services.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, required)
    - Enter the date you would like to schedule the transfer or service.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, time, required)
    - Enter the specific time you would like to schedule the transfer or service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
    - Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, required)
    - Select whether you would like to be assigned a specific tool or not.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select one, required)
    - Select whether you would like to be assigned a specific user or not.
* **Service Requester** (`service_requester`, select multiple, required)
    - Select any services or options you would like to be included with the request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
    - Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
    - Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional)
    - Enter your address for contact or other purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
    - Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Repeatedly filling out the form for the same or similar requests may lead to duplication of work and unnecessary delays in processing the request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
