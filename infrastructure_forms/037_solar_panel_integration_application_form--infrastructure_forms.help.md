# Solar Panel Integration Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a user's solar panel integration application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and contact information in the designated fields.
*   Select the type of property you are applying for.
*   Enter the size of your property.
*   Enter your energy usage level.
*   Indicate if you have already installed any systems related to solar panels.
*   Enter the date and time of your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
*   **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, not required):
    +   Select from the options 'Yes', 'No'.
*   **Property Size** (`property_size`, `number`, not required): Enter the size of your property.
*   **Energy Usage** (`energy_usage`, `number`, not required): Enter your energy usage level (e.g., 1-10).
*   **Installed Systems** (`installed_systems`, `select_one`, not required):
    +   Select 'Yes', or 'No'.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of your assessment.
*   **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of your assessment.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a successful assessment.
*   If you have any questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
*   This form should only be completed by the user who is applying for solar panel integration.
*   The information you provide will be used to assess your eligibility for solar panel installation or to provide additional services.
