# Road Sign Placement Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the placement of a road sign. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information to process and review road sign requests. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure timely processing and approval of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for Road Type and Road Sign Placment Features.
3. Enter the submission date and time.
4. Review and complete any additional comments if necessary.
5. Attach any relevant documents or images.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the name of the street where the road sign is requested.
* **Road Type** (`id: 3`): Select the type of road that the sign will be placed on (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Location Features** (`id: 4`): Describe any notable features of the location where the sign will be placed.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 5`): Enter the name of the contact person or entity requesting the road sign.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 6`): Enter the email address of the contact person or entity.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 7`): Enter the phone number of the contact person or entity.
* **Road Sign Request Details** (`id: 8`): Enter any additional details about the road sign request, such as the type of sign needed or the intended purpose.
* **Submission Review Status** (`id: 9`): Select the status of the submission (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 10`): Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.
* **Attachment** (`id: 11`): Attach any relevant documents or images to support the submission.
* **Road Sign Placment Features** (`id: 12`): Select the features of the location where the sign will be placed (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Submitted By** (`id: 13`): Select who submitted the request (e.g., User, Admin, Staff).
* **Submission Date** (`id: 14`): Enter the date the submission was made.
* **Submission Time** (`id: 15`): Enter the time the submission was made.
* **Submitted At** (`id: 16`): Select the time of day the submission was made (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
