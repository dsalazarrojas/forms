# Urban Land Use Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Land Use Assessment form is designed to collect information about parcels in the context of urban land use assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parcel details, including its size and shape.
2. Select the land use of the parcel from the provided options.
3. Choose the zoning status of the parcel from the available options.
4. Provide any additional comments about the parcel.
5. Select the assigned project and tool.
6. Enter the assigned date and time.
7. Choose who submitted and updated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parcel Details**: This field is used to enter the details of the parcel, such as its size and shape.
	+ **Size**: Enter the land parcel size in acres.
	+ **Shape**: Select the shape of the parcel from the options: Square, Rectangular, or Irregular.
* **Land Use**: This field is used to select the land use of the parcel from the available options: Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural, or Open Space.
* **Zoning Status**: This field is used to choose the zoning status of the parcel from the options: Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural, or Unknown.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for providing any additional comments about the parcel.
* **Assigned Project**: Select the project that this parcel is associated with.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that is assigned to this parcel.
* **Assigned Date** and **Assigned Time**: Enter the date and time that the parcel was assigned to you.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who submitted the form: admin, consultant, or user.
* **Submitted Date** and **Submitted Time**: Enter the date and time that the form was submitted.
* **Updated By**: This field is not necessary as the form is not meant to be updated multiple times by different users.
* **Updated Date** and **Updated Time**: These fields are not necessary as the form is not meant to be updated multiple times.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate information about the parcel.
* Make sure to select the correct land use and zoning status.
* If you have any additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide them.
* If you need to submit the form multiple times, make sure to use a different submit date and time each time.
