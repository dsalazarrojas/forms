# Energy Source Substitution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and track requests for energy source substitution, enabling the management of energy source changes and updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the request.
2.  Enter the **Utility Name**, identifying the utility associated with the energy source substitution request.
3.  Select the **Request Date** and **Request Time** for the energy source substitution request.
4.  Choose the **Request Duration** if applicable.
5.  Enter the **Reason for Request**, providing a brief explanation for the energy source substitution request.
6.  Upload any relevant **Files** to support the request.
7.  Select the **Energy Source Type** to indicate whether the energy source is currently **Yes** or **No**.
8.  Choose the **Energy Source Substitution Status** to mark the energy source as **Active** or **Inactive**.
9.  Enter the **Notes** for any additional information.
10.  Select the **Energy Source Change Date** for the substitution or change.
11.  Select the **Energy Source Change Time** if applicable.
12.  Fill in any additional **User Input** fields as required by the system or facility management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (text, optional): A brief description of the request.
* **Utility Name** (text, optional): The utility associated with the energy source substitution request.
* **Request Date** (date, required): The date of the energy source substitution request.
* **Request Time** (time, optional): The time of the energy source substitution request.
* **Request Duration** (number, optional): The duration of the energy source substitution request.
* **Reason for Request** (text, required): A brief explanation of the energy source substitution request.
* **Files to Upload** (text, optional): Upload any files supporting the energy source substitution request.
* **Energy Source Type** (select multiple, required): Whether the energy source is currently **Yes** or **No**.
* **Energy Source Substitution Status** (select one, required): Mark the energy source as **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Additional information about the energy source substitution request.
* **Energy Source Change Date** (date, required): The date of the energy source substitution or change.
* **Energy Source Change Time** (time, optional): The time of the energy source substitution or change.
* **User Input** (text, optional): Additional information required by the system or facility management.
