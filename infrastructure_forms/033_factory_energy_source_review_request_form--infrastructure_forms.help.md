# Factory Energy Source Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a review of your factory's energy source. It allows you to report your current energy use, planned changes, and sustainability initiatives. This information will help us assess your factory's energy consumption and identify opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you answer all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and clear information in the text fields.
3. Choose the correct option for the "Change Type" field.
4. Enter the correct dates for the planned change start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Energy Use** (`1`, text, optional)
	+ Enter a brief description of your current energy use.
* **KWh** (`2`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the total energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (KWh).
* **Planned Changes** (`3`, text, optional)
	+ Describe any changes you are planning or expecting to make to your energy source.
* **Change Type** (`4`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose the type of change you are making or planning to make:
		+ Energy efficiency upgrades
		+ Equipment replacement
		+ Lighting upgrades
		+ Renewable energy systems
		+ Other
* **Planned change start date** (`5`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the start date of the planned change.
* **Planned change end date** (`6`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the end date of the planned change.
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (`7`, text, optional)
	+ Describe any sustainability initiatives or goals you are implementing or planning to implement in your factory.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have the most up-to-date information about your factory's energy use and plans.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your facility manager or energy manager.
* This form is a request for review, not a formal application. Your submitted information will be reviewed and assessed for energy improvement opportunities.
