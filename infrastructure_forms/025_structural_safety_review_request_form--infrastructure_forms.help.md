# structural_safety_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by property or structural safety inspectors to review and report on the structural safety of properties or structures. It allows users to request reviews, upload supporting documents, and track the status of reviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "property_id" field with the ID of the property or structure you wish to review.
2. Enter the date of the review request in the "request_date" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the issue or concern in the "request_description" field.
4. Upload any supporting documents or materials relevant to the review in the "upload_supporting_documents" field.
5. List any concerns or issues that you have identified in the "list_concerns" field.
6. Enter the contact details of the person or organization responsible for the property or structure in the "contact_details" field.
7. Select the reviewer who will be conducting the review in the "reviewer" field.
8. Enter the date of the review in the "review_date" field.
9. Provide any comments or additional information in the "comments" field.
10. Select any recommendations for the review in the "recommendation" field.
11. Upload the inspection report or other relevant materials in the "upload_inspection_report" field.
12. Select the status of the review in the "review_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_id** (id: 1, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the property or structure being reviewed.
* **request_date** (id: 2, date, required: false): Enter the date of the review request.
* **request_description** (id: 3, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the issue or concern.
* **upload_supporting_documents** (id: 4, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents or materials relevant to the review.
* **list_concerns** (id: 5, select_one, required: false): List any concerns or issues that you have identified.
* **contact_details** (id: 6, text, required: false): Enter the contact details of the person or organization responsible for the property or structure.
* **reviewer** (id: 7, text, required: false): Select the reviewer who will be conducting the review.
* **review_date** (id: 8, date, required: false): Enter the date of the review.
* **comments** (id: 9, text, required: false): Provide any comments or additional information.
* **recommendation** (id: 10, select_multiple, required: false): Select any recommendations for the review.
* **upload_inspection_report** (id: 11, text, required: false): Upload the inspection report or other relevant materials.
* **review_status** (id: 12, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the review.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload any relevant supporting documents or materials to provide a comprehensive review.
* Clearly and concisely describe any concerns or issues you have identified.
* Select the correct reviewer and review date to ensure that the review is conducted in a timely and efficient manner.
* Provide any comments or additional information to help the reviewer understand the context of the review.
* Select any recommendations for the review to provide clear guidance for the reviewer.
* Ensure that the review status is accurately reflected to track the progress of the review.
