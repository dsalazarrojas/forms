# Expansion Joints Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expansion Joints Inspection Checklist is designed to aid in inspecting and documenting the condition of expansion joints in buildings or structures. This form provides a systematic way to collect data on the condition of expansion joints, including their location, description, condition, and recommended and actual actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by identifying the location of the expansion joint being inspected.
2.  Describe the expansion joint in detail, including its size, shape, and any notable features.
3.  Assess the condition of the expansion joint by selecting one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Bad.
4.  If applicable, take a photo of the expansion joint to document its condition.
5.  If a photo was taken, provide a brief description of the photo.
6.  If recommended actions are necessary, describe them in the corresponding field.
7.  Indicate whether any actions were actually taken on the expansion joint.
8.  Provide any additional comments or observations about the expansion joint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Joint Location** (`joint_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the expansion joint being inspected, including the specific area of the building or structure where it is located.
*   **Joint Description** (`joint_description`, text, optional): Describe the expansion joint in detail, including its size, shape, and any notable features.
*   **Joint Condition** (`joint_condition`, select_one, optional): Assess the condition of the expansion joint by selecting one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Bad.
*   **Photo Taken** (`photo_taken`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select all the photos taken of the expansion joint.
*   **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): If a photo was taken, provide a brief description of the photo.
*   **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, text, optional): If recommended actions are necessary, describe them in this field.
*   **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether any actions were actually taken on the expansion joint.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the expansion joint.

## Tips

*   Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure it reflects the actual condition of the expansion joint.
*   If a photo is taken, please make sure it is clear and relevant to the expansion joint being inspected.
*   If recommended actions are necessary, please be sure to describe them accurately and provide supporting details.
*   Ensure that you accurately select the condition and actions options to reflect the true condition and actions taken on the expansion joint.
