# Sewer Issues And Pest Control Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sewer Issues And Pest Control Request form is a tool used to report and document issues related to sewer problems or pest control concerns in residential areas. This form allows users to provide detailed information about the issue, its location, and priority level to facilitate timely and effective resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g., Pest Control, Sewer Issue, or Maintenance).
2. Describe the issue in detail in the Issue Description field.
3. Choose the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, or Low).
4. Specify the location where the issue is occurring (Office, Home, or Other).
5. Attach any relevant photos of the issue, if possible.
6. Provide any additional details about the issue.
7. Indicate whether or not you have photos attached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Type**: Select the type of issue you are experiencing, such as:
	+ Pest Control: Concerns related to pest control, including infestations or other pest-related problems.
	+ Sewer Issue: Problems related to the sewer system, including blockages or backups.
	+ Maintenance: Concerns related to general building maintenance or upkeep.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant symptoms or circumstances.
* **Priority**: Choose the priority level of the issue, with High indicating a critical or urgent concern, Medium indicating a moderate concern, and Low indicating a non-urgent concern.
* **Issue Location**: Specify the location where the issue is occurring, either in your office, home, or other.
* **Attached Photos**: Check "Yes" if you are attaching photos of the issue, or "No" if not.
* **Issue Details**: Provide any additional details or information about the issue that may be helpful for resolution.
* **Issue Uptime**: This is a field for specifying the time of day the issue started or is occurring.
