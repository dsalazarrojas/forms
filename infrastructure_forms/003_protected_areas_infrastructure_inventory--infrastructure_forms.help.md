# Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory form is used to collect information on protected areas, including their asset type, location, condition, and assigned tool. This information helps in tracking and maintaining the inventory of these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the asset type of the protected area from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the location of the protected area in the text box.
3. Choose one or more conditions that apply to the protected area from the dropdown menu.
4. Attach a photo of the protected area (optional).
5. Add any comments about the protected area (optional).
6. Select the assigned tool or resource from the dropdown menu.
7. Provide a category for the protected area (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **asset_type** (`asset type 1`, `asset type 2`, required: false): Select the type of asset associated with the protected area. This will help in understanding the nature of the area's infrastructure.
* **location** (`location`, required: false): Enter the exact location of the protected area, including the address or coordinates if possible.
* **condition** (`condition 1`, `condition 2`, `condition 3`, required: false): Choose one or more conditions that apply to the protected area, such as good, fair, or poor condition.
* **photo** (`photo`, required: false): Attach a photo of the protected area to provide visual evidence and support your answers.
* **comments** (`comments`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the protected area, such as maintenance history or future plans.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned tool 1`, `assigned tool 2`, required: false): Select the tool or resource assigned to the protected area, such as a maintenance crew or equipment.
* **category** (`category`, required: false): Provide a category or classification for the protected area, such as natural area or man-made area.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Be accurate and specific when entering information, especially for location and condition.
* Use clear and concise language when adding comments or descriptions.
* Attach photos that clearly show the protected area and its infrastructure.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or experts for guidance.
