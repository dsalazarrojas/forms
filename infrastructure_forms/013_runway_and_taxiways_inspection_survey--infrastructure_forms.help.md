# Runway And Taxiways Inspection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Runway And Taxiways Inspection Survey is a form used to document the condition of runway and taxiways, including any issues or conditions observed during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the provided fields to capture all relevant information.
2. Take photos of the runway and taxiways as evidence of any issues or conditions observed.
3. Describe the condition of the runway and taxiways in the provided fields.
4. Select any issues or conditions observed from the provided options.
5. Include the date and time of the inspection.
6. Enter the name of the inspector conducting the survey.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
