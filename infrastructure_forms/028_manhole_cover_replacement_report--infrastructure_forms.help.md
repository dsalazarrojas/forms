# Manhole Cover Replacement Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manhole Cover Replacement Report is a form used to document the process of replacing manhole covers in infrastructure settings. This form helps track and record the necessary information for manhole cover replacements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Manhole Cover Replacement Report, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for the report.
2. Select the category for the report (e.g., "Infrastructure Forms").
3. Provide a description of the work performed.
4. Attach any photo evidence of the replacement.
5. Indicate who signed off on the work.
6. Enter the date and time of the replacement.
7. Add any additional notes about the replacement.
8. Enter any contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, required): Enter a brief title for the report.
- **Category** (`category`, optional): Select the category for your report from the provided options. Typically, this will be "Infrastructure Forms".
- **Description** (`description`, optional): Describe the work performed for the manhole cover replacement.
- **Photo Evidence** (`photo_evidence`, optional): Upload any photo evidence related to the replacement.
- **Sign-off** (`sign_off`, optional): Select who signed off on the work. This can be an individual or team.
- **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the replacement.
- **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the replacement.
- **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Add any additional notes about the replacement.
- **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the contact email for further information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter the contact phone number for further information.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct category to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
- Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions and notes.
- Attach relevant photo evidence to support the report.
