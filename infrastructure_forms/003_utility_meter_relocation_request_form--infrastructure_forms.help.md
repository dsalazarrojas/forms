# Utility Meter Relocation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Meter Relocation Request Form is used to collect information for relocating or transferring utility meters between locations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Meter Details" field with accurate and detailed information about the meter being relocated.
2. In the "Relocation Details" field, provide information about the new location where the meter will be moved.
3. Specify your schedule for completing the meter relocation in the "Schedule Work" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`user_input`, `text`, not required): This is the introductory page with no specific instructions provided in the form's metadata.
* **Meter Details** (`meter_details`, `text`, not required): Provide a clear and concise description of the meter being relocated, including its type and any other relevant information.
* **Relocation Details** (`relocation_details`, `text`, not required): Describe the new location where the meter will be moved, including any relevant details about the meter base, the location's ID, or any special considerations.
* **Schedule Work** (`schedule_work`, `text`, not required): Specify your expected schedule for completing the meter relocation, including any necessary dates, times, or other relevant information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary authorization from your supervisor or relevant stakeholders before submitting this form.
* Double-check that you have not already submitted a similar request recently.
* Be as detailed and clear as possible in your descriptions to help with the meter relocation process.
