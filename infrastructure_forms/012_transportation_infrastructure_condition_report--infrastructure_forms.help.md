# Transportation Infrastructure Condition Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Infrastructure Condition Report is a form used to collect data on the condition of transportation infrastructure assets such as roads, bridges, and other infrastructure. It helps asset managers to monitor and report on the condition of these assets, enabling informed decision-making and prioritization of maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the asset type that the transportation infrastructure belongs to (e.g., Road, Bridge, Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the current condition of the asset.
3. Take up to 10 photos of the asset's condition (up to 10 allowed).
4. Enter the date and time of the last inspection.
5. Indicate how often the condition is observed (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely).
6. Describe how the condition affects the asset's condition.
7. If necessary, provide a maintenance recommendation.
8. If necessary, add up to 10 more photos.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Asset Type**: Choose the type of transportation infrastructure being reported on (e.g., Road, Bridge, Other).
* **Condition Description**: Briefly describe the current condition of the asset.
* **Condition Score**: Enter a numerical value to represent the asset's condition (Note: This field is currently not used, but it's expected to be used in the future).
* **Condition Description**: Briefly describe the condition of the asset.
* **Add Photos**: Upload up to 10 photos to document the condition of the asset.
* **Date of Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Time of Last Inspection**: Enter the time of the last inspection.
* **Condition Score Frequency**: Indicate how often the condition is observed (e.g., frequently, occasionally, rarely).
* **Relevance of Condition**: Describe how the condition affects the asset's condition.
* **Maintenance Recommendation**: If necessary, provide a maintenance recommendation.
* **Add Photos 2**: Upload up to 10 more photos to document the condition of the asset.
* **Condition Status**: Choose the current status of the condition (e.g., Normal, Critical, Major, None).

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear picture of the asset's condition.
* Use up to 10 photos to document the condition of the asset.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the condition and its effects on the asset.
* If a maintenance recommendation is provided, ensure it is feasible and practical.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
