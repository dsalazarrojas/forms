# Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to aid infrastructure maintenance teams in tracking and recording maintenance activities in a centralized and organized manner. By filling out this form, you can efficiently document and monitor maintenance tasks, ensuring that all parties involved are informed and aware of the status and progress of each task.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate to the "Maintenance Tasks" page.
2. Enter the maintenance tasks you want to track (e.g., "Equipment repair," "Software update," etc.).
3. Choose the status of the maintenance task from the available options (Open, In Progress, Closed).
4. Enter the date of the maintenance task (using the date field).
5. Enter the time spent on the maintenance task (using the time field).
6. Enter the responsible party for the maintenance task (e.g., "John Doe," "Department X," etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Tasks** (`maintenance_tasks`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the maintenance tasks you want to track and record. This can be as simple as a to-do list or a detailed description of the task.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the status of the maintenance task from the available options: Open, In Progress, Closed. This helps in categorizing and prioritizing tasks effectively.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date the maintenance task was performed or is scheduled to be performed. This helps in tracking the timeline of the tasks.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time spent on the maintenance task. This field is useful for time-based tracking and reporting.
* **Responsible Party** (`responsible_party`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the party responsible for the maintenance task, whether it be an individual or a department. This helps in assigning accountability and tracking tasks effectively.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in the "Maintenance Tasks" field.
* Choose the correct status for each task to ensure efficient tracking and reporting.
* Enter the correct date and time for each task to maintain a clear timeline.
* Clearly identify the responsible party for each task to ensure accountability and transparency.
* Save and submit the form when completed to ensure your data is recorded and stored securely.
