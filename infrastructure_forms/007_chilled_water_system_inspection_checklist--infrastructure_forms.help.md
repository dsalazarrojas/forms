# Chilled Water System Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chilled Water System Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect and document the results of a chilled water system inspection. This form helps inspectors gather information about the system's condition, operational status, and any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Enter the inspection date and time on page 2.
3. Provide basic details about the inspection on page 1.
4. Enter the inspector's name, company, and license/certification number on pages 2-3.
5. Enter the facility's name, address, and contact person on pages 4-5.
6. Complete the system information and location on pages 6-7.
7. Check the system's condition and operational status on pages 8-17.
8. Enter the temperature and pressure readings on pages 20-22.
9. Complete any additional notes or recommendations on pages 32 and 36.
10. Sign and date the form on page 37.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Inspection Information
* **Inspection Information** (`inspection_information`): Enter basic details about this inspection, such as the location, date, and any specific areas of concern.

### Page 2: Inspection Date and Time
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection.

### Page 3: Inspector Information
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Company/Organization** (`inspector_company`): Enter the name of the inspector's company or organization.
* **License/Certification Number** (`inspector_license`): Enter the inspector's license or certification number (if applicable).

### Page 4: Facility Information
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`): Enter the full street address of the facility.
* **Building Contact Person** (`building_contact`): Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.

### Page 5: Facility Contact Information
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number for the facility.

### Page 6: System Information
* **System ID/Tag Number** (`system_id`): Enter the system's ID or tag number (if applicable).
* **System Location** (`system_location`): Enter the location of the system, such as a room or area.
* **System Type** (`system_type`): Select the type of system, such as Primary, Primary-Secondary, Variable Primary, Secondary, or Not Sure.

### Page 7: System Capacity
* **System Capacity** (`system_capacity`): Enter the system's capacity in tons or BTU rating (if applicable).

### Page 8: Piping Condition
* **Piping Condition** (`piping_condition`): Select the overall condition of the piping, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Critical.

### Page 9: Insulation Condition
* **Insulation Condition** (`insulation_condition`): Select the overall condition of the insulation, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Missing/Damaged.

### Page 10: Leaks and Corrosion
* **Are there any visible leaks?** (`leaks_observed`): Select the presence of visible leaks, such as No Leaks Observed, Minor Seepage, Active Dripping, or Significant Leakage.
* **Signs of Corrosion** (`corrosion_signs`): Select the presence of signs of corrosion, such as None Observed, Minor Surface Corrosion, Moderate Corrosion, or Severe Corrosion.

### Page 11: Valve Operation
* **Valve Operation** (`valve_operation`): Select the operational status of the valves, such as All Valves Operational, Some Valves Stiff, Several Valves Inoperable, or Critical Valve Issues.

### Page 12: Pump Inspection
* **Pump Inspection** (`pump_inspection`): Enter any comments about the pump inspection.
* **Pump Condition** (`pump_condition`): Select the overall condition of the pump, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Failed.
* **Pump Vibration Level** (`pump_vibration`): Select the pump vibration level, such as Normal, Slightly Elevated, Excessive, or Critical.
* **Unusual Pump Noises** (`pump_noise`): Select the presence of unusual noises, such as No Unusual Noises, Minor Noise, Moderate Noise, or Loud/C concerning Noise.

### Page 13: Temperature and Pressure
* **Temperature and Pressure Readings** (`temperature_pressure`): Enter any comments about the temperature and pressure readings.
* **Supply Water Temperature** (`supply_temperature`): Enter the supply water temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
* **Return Water Temperature** (`return_temperature`): Enter the return water temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
* **System Pressure** (`system_pressure`): Enter the system pressure in PSI.

### Page 14: Flow Rate
* **Flow Rate** (`flow_rate`): Enter the flow rate in GPM (if measurable).

### Page 15: Overall System Condition
* **Overall System Condition** (`overall_condition`): Select the overall condition of the system, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Critical - Immediate Action Required.

### Page 16: Immediate Actions Required
* **Immediate Actions Required** (`immediate_actions_needed`): Select all immediate actions required, such as None, Repair Leaks, Replace Insulation, Service Pumps, Replace Valves, Chemical Treatment, System Flush, or Emergency Shutdown Recommended.

### Page 17: Follow-up
* **Follow-up Recommended** (`follow_up_recommended`): Select the recommended follow-up, such as No Follow-up Needed, Routine Follow-up in 6 Months, Follow-up in 3 Months, Follow-up in 1 Month, or Urgent Follow-up Required.

### Page 18: Additional Notes
* **Additional Inspector Notes** (`inspector_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes from the inspector.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`): Enter the digital signature or name of the inspector.

Note: This guide provides a detailed explanation of each field on the form, ensuring that users understand the purpose and requirements of each section. The guide is organized by page number for easy reference, and each field is explained in a clear and concise manner.
