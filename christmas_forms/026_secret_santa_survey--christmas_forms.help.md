# Secret Santa Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, titled "Secret Santa Survey", is designed to collect information about participants in a gift exchange. The goal is to match participants with gift-givers who can provide a thoughtful and personalized gift based on their interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details, including any necessary information about you.
2. Choose your gift preferences from the options provided.
3. Select your budget for the gift (if applicable).
4. Enter your interests and any other relevant details.
5. Provide information about your relationships with other participants (if applicable).
6. Select your hobbies and interests (if applicable).
7. Choose your gift preferences as a participant.
8. Enter your budget as a gifter (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (Page 1)**:
	+ **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, and any other details you'd like to share with your gift-givers.
* **Gift Preferences (Page 2)**:
	+ **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of gifts you would like to receive.
* **Gift Price (Page 2)**:
	+ **Gift Price** (`gift_price`, number, optional): Enter the maximum budget for the gift you would like to receive.
* **Gift Frequency (Page 2)**:
	+ **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to receive gifts.
* **Participant Budget (Page 2)**:
	+ **Participant Budget** (`participant_budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for gifts you'd like to give or receive.
* **Participant Interests (Page 2)**:
	+ **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, text, optional): Enter your interests and any other relevant details.
* **Gifter Info (Page 3)**:
	+ **Gifter Info** (`gifter_info`, note, optional): Provide any information about the person giving you a gift.
* **Gift Message (Page 3)**:
	+ **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, text, optional): Enter a message for your gift-giver.
* **Participant Relationships (Page 3)**:
	+ **Participant Relationships** (`participant_relationships`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationships you have with other participants.
* **Participant Hobbies (Page 3)**:
	+ **Participant Hobbies** (`participant_hobbies`, select_multiple, optional): Select your hobbies and interests.
* **Gifter Budget (Page 3)**:
	+ **Gifter Budget** (`gifter_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget you have for gifting others.
* **Participant Gift Preferences (Page 4)**:
	+ **Participant Gift Preferences** (`participant_gift_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of gifts you would like to give.
* **How to complete this form:**
	+ Follow the steps above to provide all the necessary information. 

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the best gift matching results.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your Secret Santa organizer.
