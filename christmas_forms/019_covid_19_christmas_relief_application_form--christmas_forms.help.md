# Covid 19 Christmas Relief Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for COVID-19 Christmas relief efforts. This form is specifically designed to gather essential details required for targeted assistance. Please ensure you're eligible for this support and do not submit this form for general applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, marked as <required> in the form.
2. Use the correct format for date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD) and email addresses (name@domain.com).
3. Select the appropriate relationship type for your emergency contact from the provided options.
4. Provide clear and accurate information for emergency contact details.
5. Enter any medical conditions or treatments if applicable.
6. Ensure you have the necessary permissions or consent for any information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Page 1):
	* *Name* (text): Enter your name, which will be used to address you in our response.
	* *Email* (Email): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
	* *Phone* (text): Enter your phone number for further communication.
	* *Date of Birth* (date): Format: YYYY-MM-DD. This is required for eligibility checks.
* **Emergency Contact Information**:
	* *Emergency Contact Name* (text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
	* *Emergency Contact Phone* (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	* *Emergency Contact Relationship* (Select One):
		+ Select your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
	* *Medical Condition* (text): If applicable, enter any existing medical conditions or concerns.
	* *Medical Treatment* (text): If applicable, describe any ongoing medical treatments.
	* *Medical Insurance Provider* (text): If applicable, enter your medical insurance provider.
	* *Emergency Medical Info* (text): If applicable, provide any necessary emergency medical information.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before submitting this form.
- Double-check for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.
