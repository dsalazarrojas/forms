# Join Our Holiday Greetings - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to share holiday messages, photos, and videos with others. It's a convenient way to send personalized greetings to your loved ones during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and choose the options that best fit your needs.
2. Enter your holiday message in the "Holiday Message" field.
3. Upload a photo in the "Holiday Photo" field, if desired.
4. Choose your consent options for sharing photos and videos.
5. Select the options for sharing videos.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

* **Holiday Messages** (`holiday_messages`, `text`, required: false): Enter a personalized holiday message for your loved ones.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the options for sharing photos, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Holiday Photo** (`holiday_photo`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo for your holiday message, if desired.
* **Holiday Photo Upload** (`holiday_photo_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo for your holiday message, if desired.

* **Video Message** (`shared_video_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter a video message, if desired.
* **Video Message Share** (`holiday_message_share`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the options for sharing video messages, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Shared Photo** (`shared_photo_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option for sharing photos, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Share Photo** (`shared_photo_share`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the options for sharing photos, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Holiday Video** (`holiday_video_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option for sharing holiday videos, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Holiday Message Share** (`holiday_message_share`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the options for sharing holiday messages, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".

## Tips

* Make sure to choose the correct options for sharing photos, videos, and messages.
* Enter your email address and phone number, if desired.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
