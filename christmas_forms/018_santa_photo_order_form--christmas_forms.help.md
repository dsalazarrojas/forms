# Santa Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Santa Photo Order Form for your holiday event or occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form fields below to understand what each field means.
2.  Answer each field based on your preferences and needs.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **holiday_event** (`holiday_event`, select multiple, optional): Choose if you are ordering photos for a holiday event or not.
*   **name_and_date_of_event** (`name_and_date_of_event`, text, optional): Input the name and date of your holiday event.
*   **name_of_child** (`name_of_child`, text, optional): Input the name of the child(ren) whose photo will be taken.
*   **name_of_parent** (`name_of_parent`, text, optional): Input the name of the parent(s) whose photo will be taken.
*   **photo_session** (`photo_session`, select one, optional): Choose if you are ordering a photo session.
*   **session_time** (`session_time`, select one, optional): Choose if you want to schedule a photo session at a specific time.
*   **special_request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Add any special requests for your photos.
*   **photo_package** (`photo_package`, select multiple, optional): Choose from various photo packages offered.
*   **delivery_method** (`delivery_method`, select multiple, optional): Select how you want to receive your photos.
*   **special_requests** (`special_requests`, select multiple, optional): Add additional special requests.
*   **holiday_event_type** (`holiday_event_type`, select multiple, optional): Categorize the type of holiday event.
*   **event_type** (`event_type`, select one, optional): Categorize the type of event or occasion.
*   **date_of_photo_session** (`date_of_photo_session`, date, optional): Input the date of your photo session.
*   **time_of_photo_session** (`time_of_photo_session`, time, optional): Input the time of your photo session.
*   **photo_size** (`photo_size`, select multiple, optional): Choose the size of your photo print.
*   **number_of_prints** (`number_of_prints`, select one, optional): Choose the quantity of prints you want.
*   **photo_type** (`photo_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of photo being taken.
*   **photo_orientation** (`photo_orientation`, select multiple, optional): Choose the orientation of your photo print.
*   **print_type** (`print_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of print or finish.
*   **quantity_printed** (`quantity_printed`, select one, optional): Choose if you want a specific quantity of prints.
*   **print_orientation** (`print_orientation`, select one, optional): Choose the orientation of your print.
*   **gift_wrap** (`gift_wrap`, select multiple, optional): Choose if you want your photo gift-wrapped.
*   **gift_wrap_type** (`gift_wrap_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of gift wrapping.
*   **gift_note** (`gift_note`, text, optional): Input a gift note or message.
*   **special_instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Add any special instructions.

## Tips
*   Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
