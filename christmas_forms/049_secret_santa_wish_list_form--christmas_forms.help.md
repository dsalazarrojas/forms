# Secret Santa Wish List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Wish List Form is a survey that collects information from participants to create a personalized gift list for each person involved in the Secret Santa gift exchange. This form should be filled out by each employee participating in the Secret Santa activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Wish List" page with your name and any personal preferences.
2. Select your gift preferences from the options provided.
3. Indicate your gift limits and budget.
4. Provide any additional gift ideas you would like to suggest.
5. Review your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it is listed on the company's contact list.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and gift notification purposes.
* **Wish List Items**: Select which of the provided options you would like as gift ideas:
	+ "Yes" to accept the idea
	+ "No" to decline the idea
* **Gift Suggestion**: If you have a specific gift idea you would like to suggest, enter it here.
* **Price Range**: If you have a specific price range in mind for the gift, enter it here.
* **Gift Limit**: Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on the gift.
* **Gift Suggestions**: Select which of the provided options you would like as gift ideas:
	+ "Yes" to accept the idea
	+ "No" to decline the idea
* **Recipient Suggestion**: If you have a specific person you would like to suggest as a recipient, enter their name here.
* **Gift Type**: Select the type of gift you would like to receive:
	+ "Yes" to accept the idea
	+ "No" to decline the idea
* **Gift Budget**: Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on the gift.
* **Gift Budget Max**: Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on the gift.
* **Gift Limit**: Enter the maximum number of gifts you are willing to receive.

Note: The Gift Limit field is intended to prevent overspending and ensure everyone's budget is respected.
