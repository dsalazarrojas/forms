# gingerbread_house_contest_voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gingerbread House Contest Voting form is used to rate the gingerbread houses in a contest. As a participant or judge, you will be rating each of the houses on various criteria such as design, creativity, and overall impression. Your ratings will help determine the winner of the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each of the contest entries carefully and make your best judgments on each criterion.
2. Choose the options that best describe your impression of each house for each rating question.
3. Fill in your ratings for each house, starting with the first rating question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contest Rating** (1, number, required): This is a general rating of the contest as a whole. Rate the contest on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Rating 1** (2, select_multiple, required): Rate the design of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 2** (3, select_multiple, required): Rate the creativity of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 3** (4, select_multiple, false): Rate the overall presentation of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 4** (5, select_multiple, false): Rate the originality of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 5** (6, select_multiple, false): Rate the overall appeal of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 6** (7, select_multiple, false): Rate the creativity of this house's design elements, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 7** (8, select_multiple, false): Rate the overall impression of this house, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating 8** (9, select_multiple, false): Rate the consistency of this house's design elements, choosing all that apply:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Comments** (10, note, false): Enter any comments you have about this house here.
* **Single Vote** (11, select_one, false): Do you have any single vote for a winner for this house? Yes or No.

**Tips**

* Make sure to rate each house on all the rating questions before submitting your form.
* Choose the options that best reflect your honest opinion.
* Remember that the ratings and comments will help determine the winner of the contest, so make sure to be fair and honest in your evaluations.
