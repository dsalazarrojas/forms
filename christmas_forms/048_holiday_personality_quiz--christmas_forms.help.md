# Holiday Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Personality Quiz form is designed to gather user information on their holiday preferences and habits to better understand their personality or interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences and habits.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the label.
