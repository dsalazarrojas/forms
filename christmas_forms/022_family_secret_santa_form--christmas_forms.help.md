# Family Secret Santa Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for Family Secret Santa participants to register and share their preferences for the gift-giving process. It's a simple way to help the gift-giver choose a gift that suits the recipient's interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category that best describes the type of gifts you'd like to receive.
2. Write a brief description of your interests or what you'd like to get as a gift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Christmas Forms, Secret Santa Forms)**: This field allows you to select the category that best suits your interests. Choose the option that best describes the type of gifts you'd like to receive.
* **Description**: In this field, please write a brief description of what you're interested in getting as a gift. This will help the gift-giver understand your preferences and choose a suitable gift.

## Tips
* Be specific and brief when writing your description, so the gift-giver can get a clear idea of what you like.
* Remember to check your category and description for accuracy before submitting the form. This will ensure that you receive a gift that suits your preferences.
