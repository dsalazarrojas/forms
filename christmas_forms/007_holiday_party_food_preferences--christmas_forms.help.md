# holiday_party_food_preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests about their preferences and needs for an upcoming holiday party.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the types of information being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as RSVP and number of guests.
3. Select your preferred options for dietary restrictions, allergies, favorite foods, dress code, and party theme.
4. Enter your guest names and arrival and departure times if applicable.
5. Submit the form to ensure your preferences and information are received by the party host.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSVP** (1, number, required): Please let us know if you will be attending the holiday party. This is a required field.
* **Dietary restrictions** (2, select_one, required): Select your dietary restrictions, such as Beef, Fish, None, Pescatarian, Vegetarian.
* **Allergies** (3, select_multiple, not required): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have, such as Gluten, None, Nuts, Shellfish, Soy, Tree nuts.
* **Holiday cuisine** (4, select_one, not required): Select your preferred holiday cuisine, such as American, Asian, Italian, Mexican, None, Seafood.
* **Favorite foods** (5, select_multiple, not required): Choose your favorite foods, such as BBQ, Burgers, Chicken, Fish, Grilled meats, Grilled veggies, None, Salads, Seafood, Tacos, Vegetarian.
* **Number of guests** (6, number, not required): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Guest names** (7, note, not required): Enter the names of your guests.
* **RSVP response** (8, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Dress code** (9, select_one, not required): Select your preferred dress code, such as Casual, Formal, None, Semi-formal.
* **Party theme** (10, select_one, not required): Select the party theme you would like to attend, such as Beach party, Black tie, Christmas, None, Summer party, Winter party.
* **Guest arrival time** (11, time, not required): Enter the estimated time of your arrival at the party.
* **Guest departure time** (12, time, not required): Enter the estimated time of your departure from the party.
