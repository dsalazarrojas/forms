# letter_to_santa_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "letter_to_santa_template" form is designed to create a personalized letter to Santa Claus, making it a fun activity for users to share their thoughts and wishes with Santa during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address (required for Santa's reply).
4. If you have a favorite food, let us know.
5. Write a Christmas Wish to share with Santa.
6. If you have a Special Request, feel free to include it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name. This field is optional, but it's nice for Santa to address you personally.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name. This field is required, as Santa needs to know who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. This field is required, as Santa will use it to reply to your letter.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, `text`, optional/optional): If you have a favorite food, let Santa know! This field is optional, and it's a fun way to get to know you better.
* **Christmas Wish** (`christmas_wish`, `text`, required/optional): Write your Christmas Wish to share with Santa. This field is required, and it's the main purpose of this form!
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, `text`, optional/optional): If you have a special request, feel free to include it. This field is optional, and it's a chance to share something extra with Santa.

## Tips
* Be creative and honest when writing your Christmas Wish and Special Request. Santa wants to know your true thoughts and wishes.
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy, as Santa will use it to reply to your letter.
* Don't worry if you don't have a favorite food; it's okay to leave it blank.
