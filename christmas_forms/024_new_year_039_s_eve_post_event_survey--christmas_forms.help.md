# New Year's Eve Post Event Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from attendees about their experience at the New Year's Eve event. Your input will help us understand what you enjoyed most about the event, what could be improved, and what suggestions you have for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in all the required fields, including your email, phone number, and other relevant information.
3. Answer all the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What Was Your Favourite Part Of The Evening?
*   *what_was_your_favourite_part_of_the_evening* (1/1): This field is a text field where you can share your favorite part of the evening. Please write a brief description of what you enjoyed most about the event.

### What Did You Enjoy Most About The Event?
*   *overall_experience* (2/1): This field is a text field where you can share your thoughts about what you enjoyed most about the event. Please write a brief description of what made the event special for you.

### What Could We Improve On?
*   *feedback* (3/1): This field is a text field where you can share your thoughts about what we could improve on. Please provide constructive feedback that will help us make future events even better.

### Do You Have Any Other Suggestions?
*   *suggestions* (4/1): This field is a text field where you can share any suggestions you have for future events. Please be as detailed as possible.

### Email
*   *email* (5/1): This field is a required email field where you need to provide your email address. Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.

### Phone Number
*   *phone_number* (6/1): This field is a text field where you need to provide your phone number. Please enter your phone number accurately.

### Did You Attend?
*   *event_attendance* (7/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you attended the event (True or False).

### Date Of Birth
*   *date_of_birth* (8/1): This field is a date field where you need to provide your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth accurately.

### Time Of Arrival
*   *time_of_arrival* (9/1): This field is a time field where you need to provide the time you arrived at the event. Please enter the time accurately.

### Time Of Departure
*   *time_of_departure* (10/1): This field is a time field where you need to provide the time you left the event. Please enter the time accurately.

### Time Spent On Site
*   *time_spent_on_site* (11/1): This field is a number field where you need to provide the amount of time you spent on the site. Please enter a whole number.

### Time Spent Outside
*   *time_spent_outside* (12/1): This field is a number field where you need to provide the amount of time you spent outside the site. Please enter a whole number.

### Age
*   *age* (13/1): This field is a number field where you need to provide your age. Please enter your age accurately.

### Age Group
*   *age_group* (14/1): This field is a select field where you need to select your age group (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+).

### Do You Have Any Children?
*   *have_children* (15/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you have any children (True or False).

### How Many Children Do You Have?
*   *children_count* (16/1): This field is a number field where you need to provide the number of children you have. Please enter a whole number.

### Do You Have A Partner?
*   *have_partner* (17/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you have a partner (True or False).

### Partner Name
*   *partner_name* (18/1): This field is a text field where you need to provide your partner's name. Please enter your partner's name accurately.

### Do You Have A Pet?
*   *have_pet* (19/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you have a pet (True or False).

### Pet Name
*   *pet_name* (20/1): This field is a text field where you need to provide your pet's name. Please enter your pet's name accurately.

### Did You Attend With Family?
*   *attended_with_family* (21/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you attended with your family (True or False).

### Did You Attend With Partners?
*   *attended_with_partners* (22/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you attended with your partners (True or False).

### Do You Have A Ticket?
*   *have_ticket* (23/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you have a ticket (True or False).

### Ticket Price
*   *ticket_price* (24/1): This field is a number field where you need to provide the ticket price. Please enter the ticket price accurately.

### Did You Attend Before?
*   *attended_before* (25/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you attended the event before (True or False).

### Did You Attend Last Year?
*   *attended_last_year* (26/1): This field is a select field where you need to select whether you attended the event last year (True or False).

## Tips

*   Please answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
*   Ensure that your email address and phone number are up-to-date.
*   If you have any suggestions or feedback, please provide as much detail as possible.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide your feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
