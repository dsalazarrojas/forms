# Christmas Card List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Card List Form is a tool to create and manage a list of Christmas cards. This form gathers information about individuals to whom cards will be sent.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the "Your Information" section, which includes your personal details such as first and last name, email address, and other relevant information.
- Once you have completed the "Your Information" section, proceed to the "Recipient Information" section.
- In this section, enter the recipient's name, address, and other required details.
- Double-check the address to ensure it is correct before proceeding.
- Finally, select the type of relationship you have with the recipient and any special notes or preferences.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Reciprocal Name** (`recipient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's name.
* **Mailing Address** (`recipient_address`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's city.
* **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the recipient's zip or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Enter the recipient's country.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Relationship to Sender** (`relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of relationship you have with the recipient (e.g., Family, Friend, Colleague, etc.).
* **Preferred Card Type** (`card_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of Christmas card you prefer (e.g., Holiday Greeting, Business holiday card, etc.).
* **Special Notes or Preferences** (`special_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any special notes or preferences for the recipient.
* **Please verify the address is correct** (`address_verification`, `select_one`, required): Double-check the address to ensure it is correct before submitting.
* **Please verify the address is correct** is not applicable as this field is not for user input but a field for verification purposes.
