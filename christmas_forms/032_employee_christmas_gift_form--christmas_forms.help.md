# Employee Christmas Gift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Christmas Gift Form is a tool used to gather information about each employee's preferences and interests during the holiday season. This information will be used to ensure that each employee receives a gift that is tailored to their individual tastes and wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee name in the first field.
2. Select your department from the list provided.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Specify the gift you would like to receive (or a message to the gift-giver).
5. Choose your preferred method of receiving the gift (though this field is optional).
6. Add any other comments or information you feel is relevant.
7. Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
8. Optionally, provide your phone number for additional contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or business cards.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Select the department you belong to for more accurate gift-giving.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your current job title for reference.
* **Desired Gift** (Text, Optional): Specify the gift you would like to receive. This could be a physical gift or a message to the gift-giver.
* **Preferred Method** (Text, Optional): Choose a method for receiving your gift (though this field is optional).
* **Other Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or information you feel is relevant.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when specifying your desired gift to increase the chances of receiving exactly what you want.
- If you have any specific requests or requirements for receiving the gift, please mention them in the Other Comments field.
- If you do not have a preferred method for receiving the gift, you can simply leave this field blank.
