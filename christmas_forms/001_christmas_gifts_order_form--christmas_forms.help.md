# Christmas Gifts Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Gifts Order Form is used to collect information from customers to process their orders for Christmas gifts. This form is required for all orders, and its completion is necessary for the order to be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer name**, which should be the full name as it should appear on the order.
2. Enter your **Email address** for order confirmation and updates.
3. Provide an optional **Phone number** for delivery questions or inquiries.
4. Enter your **Shipping address**, including street address, city, state, and postal code.
5. Choose the **Gift selection** you want to order from the available options.
6. Enter the **Quantity** of the selected gift you want to order.
7. If desired, enter a **Gift message** to include with the gift.
8. Choose your preferred **Delivery date** or leave it blank for standard shipping.
9. Select your **Payment method** from the available options.
10. If necessary, enter any **Special instructions** for delivery or packaging.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (1)
	+ Required field
	+ Enter your name as it should appear on the order.
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Email address** (2)
	+ Required field
	+ Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
	+ Example: johndoe@example.com
* **Phone number** (3)
	+ Optional field
	+ Enter your phone number for delivery questions or inquiries.
	+ Example: 123-456-7890
* **Shipping address** (4)
	+ Required field
	+ Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
	+ Example: 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345
* **Gift selection** (5)
	+ Required field
	+ Choose the gift package you want to order from the options provided.
	+ Example: Classic gift box
* **Quantity** (6)
	+ Required field
	+ Enter the number of identical gift items you want to order.
	+ Example: 2
* **Gift message** (7)
	+ Optional field
	+ Enter a short personalized message to include with the gift.
	+ Example: "Merry Christmas!"
* **Preferred delivery date** (8)
	+ Optional field
	+ Choose your desired delivery date or leave it blank for standard shipping.
	+ Example: 12/25/2023
* **Payment method** (9)
	+ Required field
	+ Select your payment option from the available options.
	+ Example: Credit card
* **Special instructions** (10)
	+ Optional field
	+ Enter any delivery or packaging requests.
	+ Example: "Please leave on porch"
