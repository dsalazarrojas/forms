# Christmas Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Potluck Invitation Form is designed to collect information from guests attending the event, ensuring the host has a clear understanding of who is attending and what they will bring.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the options carefully before selecting an answer.
3. If you have any special requests or messages for the host, use the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`guest_name`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address for event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Enter your phone number for day-of contact.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, required): Select whether you will be attending.
* **Number Attending** (`number_attending`, required): Choose the number of people attending.
* **Bringing Children** (`bringing_children`, required): Select whether children will attend.
* **Children Ages** (`children_ages`, optional): List the ages of the children attending.
* **Dish Category** (`dish_category`, required): Choose the category of dish you will bring.
* **Dish Name** (`dish_name`, required): Enter the name of the dish you will bring.
* **Dish Description** (`dish_description`, optional): Briefly describe your dish.
* **Serving Size** (`serving_size`, required): Choose the number of servings your dish will make.
* **Dietary Information** (`dietary_info`, required): Select any dietary restrictions for your dish.
* **Ingredients List** (`ingredients_list`, optional): List the main ingredients in your dish for allergy purposes.
* **Serving Equipment** (`serving_equipment`, required): Choose what equipment you need to be provided.
* **Dietary Information** (`dietary_info`, required): Select all that apply for your dish.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, optional): Choose what beverages you prefer.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, optional): Enter any special requests or questions.
* **Message to Host** (`message_to_host`, optional): Enter any message or comment for the host.
* **Can You Help Setup** (`setup_help`, required): Select whether you can help with setup.
* **Can You Help Cleanup** (`cleanup_help`, required): Select whether you can help with cleanup.
* **Bringing Extras** (`bringing_extras`, required): Select whether you will bring extra supplies.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, optional): Choose what beverages you prefer.

Note: Required fields are marked as `required` in the form definition.
