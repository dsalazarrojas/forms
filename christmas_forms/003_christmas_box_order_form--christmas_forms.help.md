# Christmas Box Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order a customized Christmas gift box with their preferred contents, size, and delivery information. The form gathers essential details to ensure proper gift delivery and customization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the customer's information section, including their name, email, and phone number (optional).
2.  Choose the preferred gift box size from the options provided.
3.  Select the contents you want to include in the gift box.
4.  Specify the number of identical boxes you would like to order.
5.  If desired, add any additional requests or personalization notes for the gift box.
6.  Confirm your preferred delivery date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person you are ordering the gift box for.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive order confirmation.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number for any delivery issue or additional information.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the full address for delivery, including street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Box size** (`box_size`, select_one, required): Select from three options: Small, Medium, or Large, to determine the gift box size.
* **Contents selection** (`contents_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose the items to include in the gift box from the list: Cookies and sweets, Hot chocolate kit, Handcrafted ornament, Holiday candles, Gift tag and card.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of identical gift boxes you would like to order.
* **Preferred delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select a date for delivery confirmation; we will confirm availability.
* **Additional requests** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Add any personalization requests or notes for the gift box.
