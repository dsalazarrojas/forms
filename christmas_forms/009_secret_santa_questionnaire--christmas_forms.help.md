# Secret Santa Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide their preferences and details for a Secret Santa gift exchange. The goal is to collect information about their gift preferences and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out this form completely and honestly, as it will help the Secret Santa organiser assign gifts that fit your preferences.
* Please provide as much detail as you can about your gift preferences and your personal details.
* If you have any specific dislikes or items you cannot receive, please let us know so the organiser can avoid those.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Preferences** (`user_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide any specific gift preferences or wishes you may have.
* **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ In this field, you can list specific gifts you would like or dislike to receive.
* **Gift Suggestion** (`gift_suggestion`, text, optional):
	+ If you have a gift idea for your Secret Santa partner, you can mention it here.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, text, optional):
	+ Indicate the amount you would like to spend on your Secret Santa gift.
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your name and any other relevant personal details here.
* **Form Submission Details** (`form_submission_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the Secret Santa organiser's use only, so please do not fill it out.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the organiser to confirm your submission.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the organiser, you can type it here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to check your email for any updates or messages from the organiser about your Secret Santa gift.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the organiser.
* Enjoy the Secret Santa gift exchange!
