# Christmas Musical Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for a Christmas musical production. It will be used to determine casting and rehearsal schedules.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your age group to help us determine the appropriate role for you.
3. Choose the roles you are interested in, such as main cast, ensemble, or backstage crew.
4. If you are a singer, select your vocal range.
5. Provide a summary of your previous performance experience, if applicable.
6. List any musical instruments you play and your skill level.
7. Indicate your availability for weekly rehearsals and any specific date conflicts.
8. Select your t-shirt size for the cast t-shirt.
9. Disclose any medical conditions or allergies that may be relevant during rehearsals and performances.
10. Consent to photography for promotional purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be referred to in the production.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group to help us determine the appropriate role for you. The options are:
	+ Child (under 12)
	+ Teen (13-17)
	+ Adult (18-64)
	+ Senior (65+)
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which we will use for rehearsal schedules and updates.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Your primary phone number.
* **Roles of Interest**: Select all the roles you are interested in participating in. The options are:
	+ Main Cast (Singing/Acting)
	+ Ensemble
	+ Dancer
	+ Backstage Crew
	+ Musician (Orchestra)
* **Vocal Range (if singing)**: Select your vocal range, if you are a singer.
* **Previous Performance Experience**: If you have had previous performance experience, please provide a brief summary.
* **Do you play any instruments?**: If yes, specify the instruments and your skill level.
* **Rehearsal Availability**: Indicate your availability for weekly rehearsals on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6-8pm. The options are:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Partially (please provide details below)
* **Specific Date Conflicts**: If you have any specific dates you cannot attend, please list them.
* **Cast T-Shirt Size**: Select your t-shirt size.
* **Any Medical Conditions or Allergies**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please disclose them for emergency use during rehearsals and performances.
* **Consent to Photography**: Select to consent to photography for promotional purposes.
