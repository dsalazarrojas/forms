# Holiday Party Song Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for partygoers to submit their favorite holiday songs, song requests, and any special requests for the upcoming holiday party. It will help the party planners create a diverse and enjoyable playlist for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your party theme from the options provided.
2. Enter the title of your favorite holiday song.
3. Specify the artist of your favorite holiday song.
4. Select the genre of music that best suits your taste.
5. If you have any special requests or song preferences, type them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Party Theme
* **Party Theme** (`holiday_party_theme`, select_one): Choose the party theme you think best fits the event. This will help the planners create a playlist that matches the party's mood and atmosphere.

### Song Title
* **Song Title** (`song_title`, text): Enter the title of your favorite holiday song.

### Artist
* **Artist** (`artist`, text): Enter the name of the artist or band that performs your favorite holiday song.

### Song Type
* **Song Type** (`song_type`, select_one): Select the genre of music that best suits your taste. Options include Rock, Pop, Jazz, and more.

### Request Song
* **Request Song** (`request_song`, text): If you have a specific song in mind that you'd like to hear at the party, type it in here.

### Favorite Song
* **Favorite Song** (`favorite_song`, text): Enter the title of your absolute favorite holiday song.

### Song Preferences
* **Song Preferences** (`song_preferences`, text): Share any special song preferences or requests you may have.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text): Type in any special requests or requests you may have for the party.

### Holiday Party Theme
* **Holiday Party Theme** (`holiday_party_theme`, select_one): Choose the theme of the party you're attending. Options include Christmas, New Year's Eve, Halloween, and Birthday.

### Music Style
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_one): Select the style of music that you enjoy most during the holiday season. Options include Classical, Country, Hip-Hop, Indie, Metal, Pop, Rock, and R&B.

### Special Requests 2
* **Special Requests 2** (`special_requests2`, text): This field is for any additional special requests or requests you may have for the party.

Tips:
* Be as specific as possible when entering song titles and artist names.
* If you have multiple favorite songs or requests, feel free to list them in the text fields provided.
* If you need any assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the party planners.
