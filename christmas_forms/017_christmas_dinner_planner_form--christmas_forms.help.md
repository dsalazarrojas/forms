# Christmas Dinner Planner Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users plan a Christmas dinner, gathering, or event. It guides the user through the essential details of the event, including event information, guest planning, menu planning, and entertainment. The form is used to capture the necessary information to create a comprehensive plan for a successful and enjoyable Christmas celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing basic event information, such as the event name, host name, and email address. This information will be used to contact you if necessary.
2. Enter the date and time of the dinner event.
3. Provide the expected number of guests and children attending.
4. Choose a dining style that suits your preferences.
5. Plan your menu by selecting the primary protein and any side dishes you plan to serve.
6. Consider any dietary restrictions of your guests and note them down.
7. Plan your decorations and entertainment, including the style and any special music or activities.
8. Add any special notes or additional details that may be helpful for the host or attendees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (2, Event Name, required): Enter the name of your Christmas event, such as "Holiday Feast" or "Christmas Party".
* **Host Name** (3, Host Name, required): Enter the name of the host or person planning the event.
* **Email Address** (4, Email Address, required): Enter the email address of the host or person responsible for the event.
* **Phone Number** (5, Phone Number, required): Enter the phone number of the host or person responsible for the event.
* **Dinner Date** (6, Dinner Date, required): Enter the date of the Christmas dinner event.
* **Dinner Time** (7, Dinner Time, required): Enter the time of the Christmas dinner event.
* **Expected Number of Guests** (9, Expected Number of Guests, required): Enter the expected number of guests attending the event.
* **Number of Children Expected** (10, Number of Children Expected, optional): Enter the expected number of children attending the event.
* **Dining Style** (11, Dining Style, required): Choose a dining style that suits your preferences, such as Formal, Casual, or Family-style.
* **Primary Protein** (13, Primary Protein, required): Select the primary protein for the main course, such as Turkey, Ham, or Vegetarian.
* **Planned Side Dishes** (14, Planned Side Dishes, optional): Choose any side dishes you plan to serve, such as stuffing or cranberry sauce.
* **Desserts Planned** (15, Desserts Planned, optional): Enter any desserts planned for the event.
* **Guests with Dietary Restrictions** (17, Guests with Dietary Restrictions, optional): Enter the number of guests with dietary restrictions.
* **Types of Dietary Restrictions** (18, Types of Dietary Restrictions, optional): Select any dietary restrictions, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, or Gluten-free.
* **Decoration Style** (20, Decoration Style, optional): Choose a decoration style that suits your preferences, such as Traditional, Modern Elegant, or Minimalist.
* **Table Setup Plan** (21, Table Setup Plan, optional): Enter any specific table setup plan or requirements.
* **Entertainment Planned** (23, Entertainment Planned, optional): Enter any entertainment or activities planned for the event.
* **Music or Background Audio** (24, Music or Background Audio, optional): Choose the type of music or background audio for the event.
* **Special Notes or Additional Details** (25, Special Notes or Additional Details, optional): Enter any special notes or additional details about the event.
