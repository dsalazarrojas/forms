# Christmas Audition Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from auditionees wishing to participate in a Christmas event. The form captures personal details, performance experience, and specific requirements for the audition process. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Age Group
	* Performance Experience Level
	* The type of talent you are auditioning for
	* Song or Piece to Perform
	* Preferred Audition Date and Time
	* Accompaniment Needed
2. Provide any relevant information regarding your emergency contact and special requirements

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the options.
* **Performance Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of performance experience from the options.
* **What type of talent are you auditioning for?** (`talent_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of talent you are auditioning for from the options.
* **Song or Piece to Perform** (`audition_song`, text, required): Enter the title and artist of the song or piece you will be performing.
* **Preferred Audition Date** (`preferred_audition_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the audition.
* **Preferred Audition Time** (`preferred_audition_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the audition.
* **Accompaniment Needed** (`accompaniment_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether you need accompaniment or not.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Previous Performances or Roles** (`previous_roles`, text, optional): If you have any relevant performance experience, list it here.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, note, optional): If you have any special requirements, list them here.
* **Any special accommodations needed?** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If you have any special accommodations needed, list them here.
* **Do you need accompaniment?** (`accompaniment_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether you need accompaniment or not.
* **Song or Piece to Perform** (`audition_song`, text, required): Provide the title and artist of the song or piece you will be performing. 

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth audition process. 
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
