# Elf On The Shelf Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elf On The Shelf Registration form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's participation in the Elf On The Shelf program.

## How To Complete This Form
To register your child in the Elf On The Shelf program, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your child's email address.
* **Schedule** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the schedule for your child's participation in the program.
* **Participant Details** (`5`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your child, such as their interests or allergies.
* **Confirm Schedule** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm the selected schedule for your child's participation.
* **Participant Age** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): If your child is participating, enter their age.
* **Participant Grade** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): If your child is participating, enter their grade level.
* **School Name** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's school name.
* **School Address** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your child's school address.
