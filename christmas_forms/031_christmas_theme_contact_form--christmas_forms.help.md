# Christmas Theme Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Theme Contact Form is designed to gather information from users about their holiday plans, traditions, and preferences. This form will help us better understand how we can assist you during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Select your preferred contact method and available time from the "Preferred Contact Method" and "Best Time to Contact" fields.
3. Share your holiday plans, traditions, and preferences in the "Your Holiday Plans", "Christmas Traditions", and "Holiday Information" sections.
4. Provide a brief message in the "Your Message" field to let us know how we can assist you.
5. Review and agree to the privacy policy in the "Privacy Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Subject** (required): Select the category of your inquiry (e.g., General Inquiry, Holiday Services, etc.).
* **-- Contact Information --**: This section is for your contact details.
* **Your Holiday Plans** (required): Select how you plan to spend the holiday season.
* **Christmas Traditions** (optional): Select the holiday traditions you follow (e.g., Decorating tree, Hanging stockings, etc.).
* **-- Holiday Information --**: This section is for any additional holiday-related information.
* **Your Message** (required): Provide a brief message on how we can assist you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (required): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (required): Select the time of day you are available for contact.
* **-- Response Preferences --**: This section is for any specific response preferences.
* **Holiday Newsletter** (required): Choose whether you want to receive seasonal updates.
* **How Did You Find Us** (required): Select how you found us (e.g., Website, Social Media, etc.).
* **Customer Status** (required): Select your customer status (e.g., First time contact, Previous customer, etc.).
* **-- Additional --**: This section is for any additional information.
* **Your Holiday Wish** (optional): Share a special holiday wish with us.
* **Favorite Holiday Memory** (optional): Share a special holiday memory.
* **Privacy Agreement** (required): Review and agree to the privacy policy.

Note: Please ensure that you have reviewed and agreed to the privacy policy before submitting the form.
