# Santa Helper Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Helper Application Form is used to gather information about potential Santa Helpers. This form is designed to match helpers with suitable tasks and deployment schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Select the days of the week you are available to help.
3. Choose your preferred 24-hour time slot for availability.
4. List any special skills you possess that would be useful as a Santa Helper.
5. Indicate your level of experience.
6. Confirm your availability for a week-long deployment.
7. If you have scheduling concerns, provide any relevant information.
8. Add any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Santa Helpers Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be known as a Santa Helper.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select all the days of the week you are available to help. (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday)
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, time, required): Choose your 24-hour time slot for availability.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, optional): Indicate the preferred location where you would be available to help.
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, text, required): List any special skills you possess that would be useful as a Santa Helper.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Indicate the number of years of experience you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are available for a week-long deployment. (True or False)
* **Scheduling Concerns** (`scheduling_concerns`, text, optional): If you have any scheduling concerns, please specify.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to know.
