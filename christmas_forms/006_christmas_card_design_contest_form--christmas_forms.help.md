# Christmas Card Design Contest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from contestants in the Christmas card design contest. The form is used to assess and judge the designs submitted for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "Contestant Information" section.
2. Provide your name, last name, email address, and phone number (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
3. In the "Brief Artist Biography" field, describe yourself as a designer, including your experience and qualifications.
4. In the "Design Information" section, provide details about your design, including the title, description, and inspiration.
5. Choose the correct submission format and card type for your design.
6. Indicate your original work and eligibility for commercial use.
