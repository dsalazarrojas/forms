# Santa Claus Parade Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for those who want to volunteer for the Santa Claus Parade. If you are interested in helping out with the parade, please fill out this form to let us know. We'll use this information to get in touch with you about your availability and role during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form by selecting or typing in the information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the volunteer schedule and any event materials.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can get in touch with you about your volunteer shift and any other parade-related information.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to confirm it's correct. This is to ensure we reach you at the correct email.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Time Available**: Enter your available times for the parade. We'll use this to find a shift that fits your schedule.
* **Date**: Enter your available dates for the parade. This will help us find a shift that suits you best.
* **Available Shifts**: Let us know if you are available to work a full shift or only a part of one. If you select 'No', you won't be able to participate in the parade.
* **Shift Start/End**: If you are available for a full shift, you can enter your preferred start and end times. If you are only available for a part of a shift, you can select 'Yes' for 'Available Shifts' and we'll work out the details with you.
  Note: The 'Shift Start' and 'Shift End' fields are only required if you select 'Yes' for 'Available Shifts'.
