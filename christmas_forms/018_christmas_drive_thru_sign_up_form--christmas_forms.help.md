# Christmas Drive Thru Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Drive Thru Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from participants and volunteers for a Christmas event or drive-thru. This form helps organizers manage the event smoothly by gathering essential details about each individual or group, including vehicle information, participation preferences, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your preferred **Type of Participation** (participant, volunteer, or both).
3. If you are participating, provide your **Vehicle Type** and **Vehicle Color**. If you are a volunteer, you can skip these fields.
4. If applicable, fill in your **License Plate Number** for organization purposes.
5. If you are a participant, select the number of **Passengers** in your vehicle.
6. If you have **Elderly or Disabled Passengers**, indicate this for special accommodations.
7. Choose your preferred **Time Slot** (morning, afternoon, evening, or late evening).
8. Select all that apply for **What you would like to receive** (holiday gifts, food items, gift cards, entertainment, holiday greetings, or all available items).
9. If you are a volunteer, select all your available **Volunteer Roles** (distribution, traffic direction, registration, greeting visitors, setup/cleanup, or other).
10. Choose all applicable **Available Volunteer Hours** (morning shift, afternoon shift, evening shift, or full day).
11. Fill in any **Special Requests or Notes** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This section is for participants and includes their first and last name, email address, and phone number.
  * **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
  * **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Type of Participation**: Choose if you are participating, volunteering, or both.
* **Vehicle Information**: This section is for participants and includes vehicle type and color.
  * **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you will be using (car, SUV, truck, motorcycle, van, or other).
  * **Vehicle Color**: Enter your vehicle's color.
  * **License Plate Number**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number for organization purposes (optional).
* **Passenger Information**: This section is for participants and includes the number of passengers in their vehicle.
  * **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of people who will be accompanying you in your vehicle.
  * **Elderly or Disabled Passengers**: Indicate if there are any elderly or disabled passengers that need special accommodations.
* **Event Details**: This section is for general information about the event.
  * **Preferred Time Slot**: Choose your preferred time slot for the event (morning, afternoon, evening, or late evening).
  * **What Would You Like to Receive**: Select all that apply for items you'd like to receive during the event (holiday gifts, food items, gift cards, entertainment, holiday greetings, or all available items).
* **Volunteer Information**: This section is for volunteers and includes their available hours and roles.
  * **Volunteer Roles**: Select all the roles you are interested in (distribution, traffic direction, registration, greeting visitors, setup/cleanup, or other).
  * **Available Volunteer Hours**: Choose all applicable shifts (morning shift, afternoon shift, evening shift, or full day).
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Fill in any special requests or notes you may have.

This help guide should provide clear and concise instructions for end-users to fill out the Christmas Drive Thru Sign Up Form.
