# Santa_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The santa_quiz form is designed to collect information about users' favorite Christmas-related topics to help the Santa's office better understand user preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields, marked with a *asterisk* below each field's label.
2. Choose your favorite Santa stories, gifts, foods, and other related topics from the provided options.
3. Select your preferred contact method for Santa (if you want to contact him directly).
4. Answer other questions about Santa's habits and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite Santa story** (`What is your favorite Santa story`, text, *required*): This question is asking you to share your favorite story about Santa.
* **What is your favorite gift** (`What is your favorite gift`, select_multiple, *required*): Choose one or more of the provided gift options.
* **What is your favorite thing about Santa photo** (`What is your favorite thing about Santa photo`, note, *required*): This field is for you to write down your favorite aspect about a photo of Santa.
* **What is your favorite Christmas food** (`What is your favorite Christmas food`, text, *required*): Choose your favorite food associated with Christmas.
* **What time of day does Santa come to your house** (`What time of day does Santa come to your house`, date, *required*): Select the time of day when Santa usually arrives at your house.
* **What is your favorite color** (`What is your favorite color`, select_one, *required*): Choose one color from the provided options.
* **What time does your favorite holiday season start** (`What time does your favorite holiday season start`, time, *optional*): Select the time when your favorite holiday season begins.
* **Can you email Santa** (`Can you email Santa`, select_one, *optional*): Choose whether you can contact Santa by email or not.
* **Can you call Santa** (`Can you call Santa`, text, *optional*): Choose whether you can contact Santa by phone or not.
* **How many Santas do you know** (`How many Santas do you know`, number, *optional*): This question is asking about how many Santas you know.
* **Is your name Santa Claus** (`Is your name Santa Claus`, note, *optional*): This field is for you to indicate whether your name is Santa Claus or not.
* **What is your favorite Santa quote** (`What is your favorite Santa quote`, select_one, *required*): Choose one quote from the provided options.
* **What is your favorite holiday saying** (`What is your favorite holiday saying`, text, *optional*): This field is for you to write down your favorite holiday saying.
* **What is your favorite Christmas carol** (`What is your favorite Christmas carol`, select_multiple, *optional*): Choose one or more of the provided Christmas carols.
* **What is your favorite Santa movie** (`What is your favorite Santa movie`, text, *optional*): This field is for you to select your favorite Santa movie.
* **What is your favorite Santa book** (`What is your favorite Santa book`, select_multiple, *optional*): Choose one or more of the provided Santa books.
* **How long does Santa live** (`How long does Santa live`, number, *optional*): This question is asking about Santa's lifespan.
* **What color is Santa's favorite** (`What color is Santa's favorite`, select_one, *optional*): Choose one color from the provided options.
* **What is your favorite Santa food** (`What is your favorite Santa food`, select_multiple, *optional*): This field is for you to select your favorite Santa food.
* **Can we contact Santa directly** (`Can we contact Santa directly`, select_one, *optional*): Choose whether you can contact Santa directly or not.
* **Can we contact Santa phone** (`Can we contact Santa phone`, select_one, *optional*): Choose whether you can contact Santa by phone or not.
* **Can we contact Santa by email** (`Can we contact Santa by email`, select_one, *optional*): Choose whether you can contact Santa by email or not.
* **What time does Santa arrive** (`What time does Santa arrive`, time, *optional*): This question is about the time of day when Santa usually arrives.
* **How many Christmas presents do you get from Santa** (`How many Christmas presents do you get from Santa`, select_multiple, *optional*): Choose one or more options about how many presents you receive from Santa.
* **What holiday is it** (`What holiday is it`, select_one, *optional*): This field is for you to select the holiday you are answering about.
