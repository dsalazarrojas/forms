# Christmas Choir Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Choir Application Form is designed for individuals who wish to join the Christmas choir. The form collects information about the applicant's personal details, musical background, and preferences for participation in the choir.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic contact information, including first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select your age group (Child, Teen, Adult, or Senior).
3. Provide information about your musical background and years of singing experience.
4. Choose your singing experience level (Professional, Advanced amateur, Intermediate, Beginner, or No prior experience).
5. Declare whether you have formal vocal training (Yes, extensive; Yes, some; or No formal training).
6. Select your vocal range (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, or Not sure).
7. Choose your preferred choir section (Any section, Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass).
8. If applicable, provide information about your previous choir experience.
9. Select all music genres you have experience with (Classical, Contemporary, Gospel, Religious, Pop, Musical theatre, or Other).
10. Confirm your availability for rehearsals and performances (Yes, all; Most; Some; or Not sure).
11. If you have any known schedule conflicts, list them.
12. Choose your transportation needs for rehearsals (I can provide my own; I need transportation; or Flexible).
13. Finally, provide a brief motivation for why you want to join the choir.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the provided options (Child, Teen, Adult, or Senior).
* **Musical Background** (`musical_background`, note, optional): Provide any information about your musical background.
* **Years of Singing Experience** (`singing_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been singing.
* **Singing Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your singing experience level from the provided options (Professional, Advanced amateur, Intermediate, Beginner, or No prior experience).
* **Formal Vocal Training** (`vocal_training`, select_one, required): Declare whether you have formal vocal training (Yes, extensive; Yes, some; or No formal training).
* **Vocal Range** (`vocal_range`, select_one, required): Select your vocal range from the provided options (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, or Not sure).
* **Preferred Choir Section** (`choir_section`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred choir section from the provided options (Any section, Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass).
* **Previous Choir Experience** (`previous_choirs`, text, optional): Provide information about your previous choir experience.
* **Music Genres Experience** (`music_genres`, select_multiple, optional): Select all music genres you have experience with (Classical, Contemporary, Gospel, Religious, Pop, Musical theatre, or Other).
* **Rehearsal & Performance Schedule** (`rehearsal_commitment`, note, optional): Confirm your availability for rehearsals and performances.
* **Can you attend weekly rehearsals?** (`rehearsal_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose whether you can attend weekly rehearsals (Yes, all; Most; Some; or Not sure).
* **Can you perform at all Christmas performances?** (`performance_dates`, select_one, required): Choose whether you can perform at all Christmas performances (Yes, all; Most; Some; or Not sure).
* **Known Schedule Conflicts** (`schedule_conflicts`, text, optional): If you have any known schedule conflicts, list them.
* **Transportation to Rehearsals** (`transportation`, select_one, optional): Choose your transportation needs for rehearsals (I can provide my own; I need transportation; or Flexible).
* **Why do you want to join the choir?** (`motivation`, text, required): Provide a brief motivation for why you want to join the choir.
