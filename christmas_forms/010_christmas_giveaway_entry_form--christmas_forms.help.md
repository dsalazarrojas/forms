# Christmas Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences for a Christmas giveaway. It helps us understand who is interested in participating in the giveaway and ensures that entrants meet the necessary requirements and eligibility criteria. Please complete the form accurately to increase your chances of winning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as such (*Required*).
2. Select your preferred prize category and entry method.
3. Confirm your eligibility and agree to the official rules.
4. Provide your social media handles for bonus entries.
5. Choose to subscribe to our newsletter or not.
6. Add any additional comments or messages if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where we will notify you if you win.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number for winner notification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth, as you must be 18 or older to enter.
* **Street Address**: Enter your full address for prize delivery.
* **City**: Enter the city for your address.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province for your address.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for your address.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Prize Category Preference**: Select which prize category interests you most.
* **How Are You Entering**: Select your entry method.
* **Social Media Following**: Select whether you follow us on social media.
* **Your Social Media Handles**: Share your social media usernames for bonus entries.
* **Join Our Newsletter**: Choose to subscribe or not.
* **Referred By**: If someone referred you, enter their name or email.
* **How Did You Hear About Giveaway**: Select how you heard about the giveaway.
* **Agree to Official Rules**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the official rules.
* **Confirm Eligibility**: Confirm that you meet all eligibility requirements.
* **Accept Privacy Policy**: Accept our privacy policy terms.
* **Publicity Consent**: Consent to use your name and likeness if you win.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any questions or messages if needed.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields accurately.
