# Secret Santa Rescheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Secret Santa organizers reschedule events, taking into account multiple factors such as event format, date, time, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event format (in-person, virtual, or both) that applies to your event.
2. Enter the date you are available to attend the event.
3. Enter the start and end dates for the event (if applicable).
4. Enter your preferred start and end dates for the event.
5. Select your preferred event time.
6. Enter your preferred location (if applicable).
7. Select your availability for each of the seven time slots provided.
8. Enter your preferred availability.
9. Enter your preferred start and end times for the event.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about your availability.
11. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Format** (`event_format`, select_multiple): Select the format for the event (in-person, virtual, or both).
* **Date Available** (`date_available`, date): Enter the date you are available to attend the event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Enter the start date for the event (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Enter the end date for the event (if applicable).
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, date): Enter your preferred start date for the event.
* **Preferred End Date** (`preferred_end_date`, date): Enter your preferred end date for the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time): Enter the event time (if applicable).
* **Preferred Event Time** (`preferred_event_time`, time): Enter your preferred event time.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter the location for the event (if applicable).
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text): Enter your preferred location.
* **Availability 1** (`availability_1`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 1.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 2.
* **Availability 3** (`availability_3`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 3.
* **Availability 4** (`availability_4`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 4.
* **Availability 5** (`availability_5`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 5.
* **Availability 6** (`availability_6`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 6.
* **Availability 7** (`availability_7`, select_one): Select your availability for time slot 7.
* **Preferred Availability** (`preferred_availability`, select_one): Select your preferred availability.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time for the event (if applicable).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time for the event (if applicable).
* **Preferred Start Time** (`preferred_start_time`, time): Enter your preferred start time for the event.
* **Preferred End Time** (`preferred_end_time`, time): Enter your preferred end time for the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
