# Secret Santa Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Secret Santa Questionnaire is a form designed to help gather information from participants about their preferences and traditions during holidays. It's a fun and interactive way to get to know each other better during the holiday season. Please answer the questions honestly, and don't be afraid to share your favorite and least favorite holidays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with your preferred answers. You can type in text answers for questions, and select from the provided options for select_one, select_multiple, and time questions.
