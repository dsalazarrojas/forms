# Christmas Market Stallholder Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Market Stallholder Application Form is designed to gather information about your business and products to ensure that we can efficiently review your application for a spot at the Christmas market.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and contact person's details (pages 1-3).
2. Provide a detailed description of your products (page 7).
3. Select the type of stall you require (page 8).
4. Indicate if you need electricity and specify your power requirement (page 9).
5. Confirm you have the necessary health and safety certificates (page 10).
6. If selling food, specify any dietary options (page 12).
7. List any previous markets you've attended (page 13).
8. Add any special requests or requirements (page 14).
9. Agree to the market's terms and conditions (page 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business or Trading Name (page 1)**: Enter the name of your business or trading name as it appears on your invoices, licenses, or other official documents.
* **Primary Contact Person (page 2)**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of any issues or queries.
* **Contact Email (page 3)**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone Number (page 4)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Address (page 5)**: Enter your business address or the address where we can send any mail or delivery.
* **Product Category (page 6)**: Select all the categories that apply to your products (e.g., Handmade Crafts, Food and Drink, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Products (page 7)**: Provide a detailed description of the products you will be selling, including prices and any other relevant details.
* **Required Stall Type (page 8)**: Select the type of stall you require (e.g., Indoor Trestle Table, Outdoor Gazebo 3m x 3m, etc.).
* **Do you require electricity? (page 9)**: Indicate if you need electricity for your stall.
* **Public Liability Insurance and Health Certificates (page 10)**: Confirm if you have valid insurance and health certificates; if not, select 'No' and provide documentation before the event.
* **Website or Social Media Links (page 11)**: Enter your website or social media links to help us promote your stall.
* **If selling food - specify dietary options (page 12)**: Select the dietary options you offer (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
* **List any previous markets you have attended (page 13)**: If you've attended other markets, list them here.
* **Special Requests or Requirements (page 14)**: Enter any special requests or requirements you have.
* **I agree to the Christmas Market Terms and Conditions (page 15)**: Confirm that you agree to the market's terms and conditions.

Note: Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential issues or delays in the application process. If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
