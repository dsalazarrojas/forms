# Christmas Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Christmas Assistance Application Form" is a tool to collect information from individuals or families in need of assistance during the Christmas season. This information helps us understand their requirements and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
2. Provide your **Phone Number**.
3. Enter the **Application Date** when you applied for assistance.
4. Answer the questions about your household's size and composition, including the **Household Size** and **Number of Children in Household**.
5. Enter the **Ages of Children** in your household (comma-separated list, e.g., 4, 7, 12).
6. Select your **Annual Household Income** from the provided options.
7. Choose your **Housing Status** from the options.
8. Select all that apply for **Current Financial Hardships**.
9. Choose all that apply for **What type of assistance does your family need this Christmas?**.
10. Write down your most **Urgent Needs** in the text field.
11. If necessary, provide any **Additional Information** about your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. (Required)
* **Application Date**: Enter the date when you applied for assistance. (Required)
* **Household Size (number of people)**: Enter the number of people living in your household. (Required)
* **Number of Children in Household**: Enter the number of children in your household. (Required)
* **Ages of Children**: Enter the ages of children in your household as a comma-separated list (e.g., 4, 7, 12). (Optional)
* **Annual Household Income**: Select your household's annual income from the provided options. (Required)
* **Housing Status**: Choose your housing status from the options. (Required)
* **Current Financial Hardships**: Select all that apply for your current financial hardships. (Required)
* **What type of assistance does your family need this Christmas?**: Select all that apply for the type of assistance your family needs. (Required)
* **What are your most urgent needs?**: Write down your most pressing needs. (Required)
* **Is there any other information we should know?**: Provide any additional information about your situation. (Optional)

## Tips

- Be accurate when entering your details to ensure assistance is allocated correctly.
- If you need help during Christmas, this form is here to support you.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out for clarification.
- Please fill out the form only once a year, as it's designed for one-time submissions.
