# model_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect your application details for the job you're interested in. Please provide accurate and complete information in each field as it will help us process your application correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the `First Name` field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your valid **Email** in the `Email` field.
4. Fill in your **Phone** number in the `Phone` field.
5. Attach your **Resume** in the `Resume` field.
6. If applicable, fill in your **Job Title** in the `Job Title` field.
7. If applicable, select your **Job Category** from the `Job Category` field.
8. If applicable, select your **Job Type** from the `Job Type` field.
9. Fill in your **Location** in the `Location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (application_details): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (last_name): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number where we can reach you about your application.
* **Resume** (resume): Attach a copy of your resume in a supported format (PDF, Docx, etc.).
* **Job Title** (job_title): If you have a specific job title that you're applying for, please enter it here. This will help us match you with the position you're applying for.
* **Job Category** (job_category): If applicable, select the job category that best describes the job you're interested in.
* **Job Type** (job_type): If applicable, select the type of job that best describes the job you're applying for.
* **Location** (location): Enter the location where you're applying from or where you're available to work.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields (marked as `true`) accurately to ensure we can review your application correctly.
* If a field is marked as optional, feel free to add any additional information that you think would be helpful.
* Make sure to attach your resume in a supported format.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us and we'll be happy to assist.
