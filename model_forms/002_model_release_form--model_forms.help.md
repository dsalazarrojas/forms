# Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release Form is used to obtain a model's consent for the use of their likeness, image, or other creative works. It is a crucial document that ensures the model's rights and interests are protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the model's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the photographer's information, including their name and contact details.
3. Specify the form type and intended use of the image (e.g., commercial, editorial, personal, etc.).
4. Sign and date the form to confirm the model's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Model Information (id: 1)**: Enter the model's full name, as it appears on their identification.
* **Photographer Information (id: 2)**: Enter the photographer's full name, as it appears on their identification.
* **Photograph (id: 3)**: Enter a brief description of the photograph or image used in the release.
* **Model Release (id: 4)**: Select the date when the model release becomes effective. This date is the start of the period for which the model release is granted.
* **Photographer Signature (id: 6)**: The photographer must sign this section to confirm their consent and agreement to the terms of the release.
* **Model Signature (id: 7)**: The model must sign this section to confirm their consent and agreement to the terms of the release.
* **Date Signed (id: 8)**: Enter the date when the model and photographer signed the form.
* **Photographer Name (id: 9)**: Enter the photographer's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Model Name (id: 10)**: Enter the model's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Form Type (id: 11)**: Select the type of form this is (e.g., model release, property release, etc.).
* **Use For (id: 12)**: Select the intended use of the image (e.g., commercial, editorial, personal, etc.).
* **Additional Use (id: 13)**: Enter any additional details about the use of the image.
* **Photographer Email (id: 14)**: Enter the photographer's email address.
* **Model Email (id: 15)**: Enter the model's email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to keep a record of the signed form for future reference.
* Ensure that the model understands the terms and conditions of the release before signing.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the model for signature.
* Store the signed form in a secure and accessible location.
