# gala_model_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Gala Model Application Form" is a form designed to collect information from users about their model submissions. It is intended to be used by users who wish to apply for model-related opportunities or participate in model-related events. The form is part of a larger process that involves multiple interactions with the user and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
3. For fields that are not required, provide as much information as you deem necessary.
4. Review the form again before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Model Information (id: 1)
* Model Information (text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief description of your model, including its type and any relevant details.

### Photos and Video (id: 2)
* Photos and Video (text, required: false): This field is for attaching photos or videos that showcase your model, if applicable.

### Experience (id: 3)
* Experience (text, required: false): This field is for describing your experience with the model, including any relevant skills or knowledge.

### Availability (id: 4)
* Availability (text, required: false): This field is for indicating your availability for the opportunity or event, including any constraints or limitations.

### Model Measures (id: 5)
* Model Measures (text, required: false): This field is for providing any relevant measurements or dimensions of your model, if applicable.

### Model Media (id: 6)
* Model Media (text, required: false): This field is for attaching any relevant media (e.g., images, videos) that showcase your model.

### Model Information (id: 7)
* Model Information (text, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about your model, if necessary.

### Submit (id: 8)
* Submit (select one, required: true): Check yes to submit your application, or no if you change your mind.

### Confirm (id: 9)
* Confirm (select multiple, required: true): Check the options that apply to your situation, such as agreeing to receive updates or confirming you are available for the opportunity or event.

### Contact Details (id: 10)
* Contact Details (text, required: false): This field is for providing your contact information, including your email and phone number.

### Emergency Contact (id: 11)
* Emergency Contact (text, required: false): This field is for providing the contact information of an emergency contact, if applicable.

### Submit (id: 12)
* This field is reserved for a potential next step in the process.

### Confirmation (id: 13)
* Confirmation (text, required: false): This field is for providing a confirmation of your submission or application.

### Submission Information (id: 14)
* Submission Information (text, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about your submission or application.

Note: The actual layout of the form may vary based on the options you provide in the YAML. The explanations above are intended to guide users through the form based on the provided fields.
