# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms of a trade for print agreement between a photographer and a model. It captures essential information about the photographer and the model, including their contact details, the date of the photo capture, and their signatures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the model who is agreeing to the terms of the trade for print agreement.
2. Enter the name of the photographer who is capturing the photos.
3. Provide the contact information of the photographer.
4. Enter the date of the photo capture.
5. Enter your signature as the photographer, including the date.
6. Enter your signature as the model, including the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **model_name** (`model_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the model who is agreeing to the terms of the trade for print agreement.
* **photographer_name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer who is capturing the photos.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the photographer, including their address, phone number, and email address.
* **date_of_capture** (`date_of_capture`, date, required): Enter the date on which the photos were captured.
* **photographer_signature_date** (`photographer_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the photographer is signing the agreement.
* **photographer_signature** (`photographer_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as the photographer, confirming that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **model_signature_date** (`model_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the model is signing the agreement.
* **model_signature** (`model_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as the model, confirming that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.

## Tips

* Ensure that both the photographer and the model have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the trade for print agreement before signing.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
* If there are any changes to the agreement, update the form accordingly and obtain new signatures.
* Use this form in conjunction with other relevant contracts or agreements to ensure that all necessary parties are covered.
