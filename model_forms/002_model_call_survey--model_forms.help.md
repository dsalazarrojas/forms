# Model Call Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their model studio experiences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted by the form.
- Enter your studio information in the "Model studio" field.
- Enter your name in the "Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Model studio** (required): Enter a short description of your model studio settings, such as your model name, version, or any specific settings that are relevant to your project.
* **Name** (optional): Enter your name or title to be associated with your model studio entry.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address to be associated with your model studio entry.
* **Contact phone** (optional): Enter your phone number to be associated with your model studio entry.

## Tips
- Please enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator or support team for clarification.
- This form is intended to be used for collecting information on model studio experiences and needs, and not for other purposes.
