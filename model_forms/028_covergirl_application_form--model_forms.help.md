# Covergirl Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about applicants who wish to become part of the Covergirl team. It's a crucial step in the application process, and providing accurate details will help us review your application effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Address in the "Address" field.
4. Describe your experience and qualifications in the "Experience Details" field.
5. Share your portfolio, if you have any relevant work or projects to showcase.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email format (e.g., [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com)).
8. If applicable, specify a date and time you are available to work.
9. If you have any specific availability, you can mention it in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (text, optional): Provide your current address for communication and potential interview purposes.
* **Experience Details** (text, optional): Share your relevant work experience, skills, and qualifications.
* **Portfolio** (text, optional): Attach a link or paste a brief description of your work portfolio.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Date** (date, optional): If applicable, specify a date you are available to work.
* **Time** (time, optional): If applicable, mention a specific time you are available to work. 

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email format for accuracy.
* If you have any specific availability or qualifications, make sure to mention them in the relevant fields.
* You can paste links to your portfolio or work examples if they are publicly accessible.
