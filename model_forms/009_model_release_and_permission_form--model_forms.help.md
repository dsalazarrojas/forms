# Model Release And Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release And Permission Form is designed to obtain consent from individuals who have been photographed or recorded for use in various media. It ensures that their rights are respected and that they have given their informed consent for their likeness and image to be used in the specified context.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Photograph Name** field. This is your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your address in the **Address** field. This should be your current address.
3. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field. This is the date you were born on.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This is your contact number.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This is your email address.
6. Describe the relationship you have with the photographer or agency in the **Relationship** field. For example, if you are a professional model, you would describe your modeling agency or your relationship with the photographer.
7. Enter the date you signed this form in the **Signature Date** field. This is the date you are signing the form.
8. Read the text in the **Model Release And Permission** field. This is a statement explaining the purpose of the form.
9. Sign your name as the subject of the photographs or recordings in the **Photographer Signature** field.
10. Enter your name as the photographer or agency in the **Photographer Name** field.
11. Enter your address as the photographer or agency in the **Photographer Address** field.
12. Enter your phone number as the photographer or agency in the **Photographer Phone** field.
13. Enter your email address as the photographer or agency in the **Photographer Email** field.
14. Sign your name as the photographer or agency in the **Agency Signature** field.
15. Enter your name as the agency in the **Agency Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photograph Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address** (text, required): Enter your current address.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Relationship** (text, required): Describe your relationship with the photographer or agency.
- **Signature Date** (date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
- **Model Release And Permission** (note, required): Read and understand the statement explaining the purpose of the form.
- **Photographer Signature** (text, required): Sign your name as the subject of the photographs or recordings.
- **Photographer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the photographer or agency.
- **Photographer Address** (text, required): Enter your address as the photographer or agency.
- **Photographer Phone** (text, required): Enter your contact number as the photographer or agency.
- **Photographer Email** (text, required): Enter your email address as the photographer or agency.
- **Agency Signature** (text, required): Sign your name as the photographer or agency.
- **Agency Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the agency.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your photographer or agency for clarification.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- Ensure that you understand the purpose and implications of signing this form before signing it.
