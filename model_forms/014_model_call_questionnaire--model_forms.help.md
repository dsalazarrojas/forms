# model_call_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide information about their experience and preferences for potential modeling gigs. Please fill out this form to help us get a better understanding of you and your skills.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the pages and fill out the required fields. Each page will have a label and an associated field type. You can select multiple options or input text as required.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): The introduction page is a brief description of your experience, skills, and background. You can write a few sentences to give us a better understanding of who you are.
- **Experience** (`experience`, `select_one`, required: false): This is where you select your relevant experience in the model world. Please choose the options that best describe your experience.
- **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List all relevant skills you have, such as portrait, wedding, pet photography. Select all options that apply.
- **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `text`, required: false): Share a link to your portfolio or attach your photos here. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your work.
- **Contact** (`contact`, `email`, required: false): If you want to be contacted, please enter your email address. This is an optional field, but it will help us reach out to you.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required: false): Select your available working hours. Please choose the options that apply to you.
- **Preferred Work** (`preferred_work`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of work you prefer doing most often. This will help us understand what you're looking for in a gig.
- **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your location. This will help us narrow down potential gigs in your area.
- **Model Call** (`model_call`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of model call you're interested in. This will help us reach out to you with suitable gigs.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or additional information, please add it here. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand you.
