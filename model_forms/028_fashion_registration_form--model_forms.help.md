# Fashion Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and fashion event details for registration and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your participant's email in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Add any additional notes about the participant in the "Participant Notes" field.
5. Enter the fashion event information in the subsequent fields, including event name, date, time, and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Participant Time** (`participant_time`, time, required: false): Enter the participant's time of attendance or availability.
* **Participant Date** (`participant_date`, date, required: false): Enter the participant's date of attendance or availability.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, text, required: false): Select the participant type (e.g., model, designer, etc.).
* **Fashion Event** (`fashion_event`, text, required: false): Enter the fashion event name.
* **Fashion Event Date** (`fashion_event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the fashion event.
* **Fashion Event Time** (`fashion_event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the fashion event.
* **Fashion Event Notes** (`fashion_event_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the fashion event.
* **Participant Agenda** (`participant_agenda`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's agenda or schedule for the fashion event.

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or support team.
* This form is for your own use and does not store any sensitive or personal data.
