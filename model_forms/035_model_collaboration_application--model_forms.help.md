# Model Collaboration Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a model or photographer collaboration. It will help both parties gather essential details about their preferences and expectations, preventing potential misunderstandings and ensuring a smooth collaboration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer.
- **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): If you have a portfolio link, please provide it here.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, note, optional): This is an optional field for any additional contact information you may have.
- **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter your agency name, if you are working with an agency.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
- **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are looking for prophets or not.
- **Collaboration Preferences** (`collaboration_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferences for collaboration, such as scheduling, communication, or payment terms.
- **Casting Agency** (`casting_agency`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are working with a casting agency or not.
- **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select specific details about your collaboration needs, such as equipment needs or location preferences.
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as phone, email, or social media.
- **Brand** (`brand`, text, optional): If you are working with a brand, enter their name here.
- **Portfolio Preferences** (`portfolio_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferences for your portfolio, such as public or private.
