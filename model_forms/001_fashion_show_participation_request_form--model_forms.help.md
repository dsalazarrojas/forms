# Fashion Show Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request participation in the fashion show. Please fill it out if you wish to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and complete it accurately to avoid errors.
- Fill in all required fields (those marked with an asterisk \*).
- If you have any special requirements or requests, use the "Special Instructions" field to provide more information.
- Review your form before submission to ensure that it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your participation.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: If you can be reached by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Height (id: 5)**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Measurements (id: 6)**: Enter your measurements for sizing purposes.
* **Age (id: 7)**: Enter your age in years.
* **Hair Color (id: 8)**: Select your hair color from the options provided.
* **Eye Color (id: 9)**: Select your eye color from the options provided.
* **Available Dates (id: 10)**: Enter dates when you are available to participate in the fashion show.
* **Preferred Time (id: 11)**: Enter your preferred time slot for participation.
* **Special Instructions (id: 12)**: If you have any special requirements or requests, please provide more information here.
