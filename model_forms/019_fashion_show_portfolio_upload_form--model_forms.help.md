# Fashion Show Portfolio Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Portfolio Upload Form is a tool for models to upload and manage their fashion show participation details. This form helps keep their portfolio up-to-date and accurately represented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself.
2. Provide your model occupation and agency information if applicable.
3. Select the fashion shows you have participated in, including 'Yes' or 'No' options for each show.
4. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the submission agreement.
5. Select the date you agree to the submission terms.
6. Upload any relevant images of your participation in the fashion shows.
7. Ensure that each uploaded image is a valid and relevant representation of you in a fashion show.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Details**: Enter a short description of yourself as a model.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Portfolio Image 1**: Upload one of your portfolio images related to the fashion show.
* **Portfolio Image 2**: Upload another of your portfolio images related to the fashion show.
* **Submission Agreement**: Select 'Yes' to agree to the submission terms.
* **Submission Agreement Terms**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm you have read and understand the submission terms.
* **Model Occupation**: Describe your occupation as a model.
* **Model Agency**: Mention your agency if you have one.
* **Portfolio Images**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each image you want to upload.
* **Submission Agreement Agree**: Select 'Yes' to confirm you agree to the submission terms.
* **Submission Agreement Date**: Select the date you agree to the submission terms.
* **Submission Agreement Date**: Select the date you agree to the submission terms.
 
## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out your details.
* Ensure all uploaded images are of good quality and relevant to the fashion shows you're uploading for.
* Review the submission terms carefully before confirming your agreement.
