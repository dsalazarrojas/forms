# Calendar Pinup Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calendar Pinup Application Form is used by potential models to provide personal and physical information, as well as experience and availability details, to modeling agencies. This form helps agencies evaluate and consider applicants for potential modeling opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your height and weight in the "Height" and "Weight (lbs)" fields, respectively.
6. Select your hair color from the "Hair Color" dropdown menu.
7. Select your eye color from the "Eye Color" dropdown menu.
8. Enter your bust, waist, and hip measurements in the "Bust Measurement (inches)", "Waist Measurement (inches)", and "Hip Measurement (inches)" fields, respectively.
9. If applicable, select your level of modeling experience from the "Modeling Experience" dropdown menu.
10. If applicable, provide any previous modeling work or portfolio in the "Previous Modeling Work or Portfolio" field.
11. If applicable, provide links or attachments to your portfolio in the "Portfolio Links" field.
12. If applicable, list your social media handles in the "Social Media Handles" field.
13. Select "Yes", "Possibly", or "No" for availability in 2026 in the "Available for 2026 Calendar?" field.
14. If applicable, select your preferred month for photoshoots from the "Preferred Month for Photoshoot" dropdown menu.
15. If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in the "Additional Information or Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Height**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight (lbs)**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Hair Color**: Select your hair color from the provided options.
* **Eye Color**: Select your eye color from the provided options.
* **Bust Measurement (inches)**: Enter your bust measurement in inches.
* **Waist Measurement (inches)**: Enter your waist measurement in inches.
* **Hip Measurement (inches)**: Enter your hip measurement in inches.
* **Modeling Experience**: Select your level of modeling experience from the provided options.
* **Previous Modeling Work or Portfolio**: If applicable, describe any previous modeling work or provide links to your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Links**: If applicable, provide links or attachments to your portfolio.
* **Social Media Handles**: If applicable, list your social media handles.
* **Available for 2026 Calendar?**: Select "Yes", "Possibly", or "No" to indicate your availability.
* **Preferred Month for Photoshoot**: Select a month from the provided options for your preferred time for photoshoots.
* **Additional Information or Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about yourself.
