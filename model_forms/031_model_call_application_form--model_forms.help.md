# Model Call Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Model Call Application Form is used to submit applications for model calls. A model call is a request to run a model on a specific dataset or scenario. Users can submit applications to use one of the available models (Model A, Model B, or Model C) and provide necessary details about the call, including location, date, time, and description.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of model you want to use (Model A, Model B, or Model C) in the "Model Type" field.
2. Enter the details of your call, including the location and date in the "Call Details" and "Call Date" fields.
3. Choose the location of your call from the options provided in the "Call Location" field.
4. Select the duration of your call in the "Call Duration" field.
5. Assign a tool to your call from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Provide a brief description of your call in the "Description" field.
7. Upload any relevant output files in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Model Type** (`model_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of model you want to use for your call.
* **Call Details** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your call.
* **Call Location** (`call_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location of your call from the provided options.
* **Call Date** (`call_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your call.
* **Call Time** (`call_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your call.
* **Call Duration** (`call_duration`, number, optional): Select the duration of your call.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose a tool to be assigned to your call from the provided options.
* **Model Status** (`model_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your model.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your call.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Upload any relevant output files related to your call.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting your application.
* Use the provided options for "Call Location" and "Assigned Tool" to choose the most suitable option for your call.
* Use the "Description" field to provide additional context about your call.
* For "Call Duration", select the duration in minutes.
* This form is not intended for internal use within the company, so please do not submit duplicate applications.
