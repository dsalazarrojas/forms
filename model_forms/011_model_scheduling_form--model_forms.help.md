# Model Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for models to provide their availability and other relevant information for scheduling photo shoots, fashion shows, or other modeling related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the `model_id` and `email` fields respectively.
2. Select your availability for the shoot date in the `shoot_date` field.
3. Choose your availability for the shoot time in the `shoot_time` field.
4. Select your availability from the options in the `model_availability` field.
5. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
6. Upload your portfolio in the `portfolio` field.
7. Enter the location of the shoot in the `location` field.
8. Provide any additional agency information in the `agency_info` field.
9. Provide any additional casting information in the `casting_info` field.
10. Provide any additional campaign information in the `campaign_info` field.
11. Enter the submission date and time of the shoot in the `submission_date` and `submission_time` fields respectively.
12. Enter the shoot location in the `shoot_location` field.
13. Provide any additional fitting information in the `fitting_info` field.
14. Provide any additional campaign details in the `campaign_details` field.
15. Provide any shoot notes in the `shoot_notes` field.
16. Provide any submission notes in the `submission_notes` field.
17. Provide any model notes in the `model_notes` field.
18. Provide any campaign notes in the `campaign_notes` field.
19. Provide any agency notes in the `agency_notes` field.
20. Provide any casting notes in the `casting_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **model_scheduling_form_model**: This field is the name of the form.
* **shoot_date**: The date of the shoot, select your availability on this date.
* **shoot_time**: The time of the shoot, select your availability on this time.
* **model_availability**: Your availability for the shoot, select from the options.
* **email**: Your email address.
* **phone**: Your phone number.
* **portfolio**: Upload your portfolio.
* **location**: The location of the shoot.
* **agency_info**: Any additional information about the agency.
* **casting_info**: Any additional information about the casting.
* **campaign_info**: Any additional information about the campaign.
* **submission_date**: The date of the submission.
* **submission_time**: The time of the submission.
* **shoot_location**: The location of the shoot.
* **fitting_info**: Any additional fitting information.
* **campaign_details**: Any additional campaign details.
* **shoot_notes**: Any notes about the shoot.
* **submission_notes**: Any notes about the submission.
* **model_notes**: Any notes about the model.
* **campaign_notes**: Any notes about the campaign.
* **agency_notes**: Any notes about the agency.
* **casting_notes**: Any notes about the casting.
* **shoot_id**: The ID of the shoot.
* **model_id**: The ID of the model.
* **campaign_id**: The ID of the campaign.
