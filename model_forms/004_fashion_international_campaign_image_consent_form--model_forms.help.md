# Fashion International Campaign Image Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion International Campaign Image Consent Form is used to collect consent from individuals who will be featured in the campaign images. This form helps us understand who will be featured and for which purposes (promotional, editorial, social, or other).

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the following information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (required for communication purposes)
	* Phone (optional)
2. Select all the campaign purposes for which you will be featured:
	* Promotional
	* Editorial
	* Social
	* Other
3. If applicable, fill out the Submission Date and Submission Time for the campaign.
4. Add any comments or notes about your involvement in the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
### Email
* **Email** (`<name>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can communicate with you effectively.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further information.
### Campaign Use
* **Campaign Use** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the campaign purposes for which you will be featured. This will help us to understand your involvement in the campaign.
	+ *Promotional*: You will be featured in promotional materials such as billboards, advertisements, or social media posts.
	+ *Editorial*: You will be featured in editorial content such as magazine articles, blog posts, or blog comments.
	+ *Social*: You will be featured in social media posts or content.
	+ *Other*: Please specify the campaign purpose not listed above.
### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
### Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (`<name>`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`<name>`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your involvement in the campaign.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields to submit the form successfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
