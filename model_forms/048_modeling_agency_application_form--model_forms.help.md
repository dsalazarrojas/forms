# Modeling Agency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from modeling agencies to apply for modeling jobs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose the most relevant option from the provided list.
3. For fields marked as "email", please use a valid email address in the format "name@example.com".
4. For fields marked as "date", please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
5. For fields marked as "text", please enter a clear and concise description or answer.
6. For fields marked as "number", please enter a numeric value.
7. For fields marked as "select_multiple", please select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Number** (3): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (4): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (5): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Address** (6): Enter your address.
* **Modeling Experience** (7): Describe your modeling experience.
* **Availability** (8): Select the type of agency you are interested in working with (Modeling Agency, Fashion Agency, Commercial Agency, Runway Agency, Print Agency).
* **Modeling Agency Preferred** (9): Select all applicable types of agencies you are interested in working with.
* **Portfolio Url** (10): Enter the URL of your portfolio website.
* **Portfolio Files** (11): Select all applicable images from your portfolio.
* **Resume** (12): Upload or enter your resume.
* **Modeling Agency ID** (13): Enter your modeling agency ID.
* **Contact Person Name** (14): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Number** (15): Enter the contact person's number.
* **Contact Person Email** (16): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Person Address** (17): Enter the contact person's address.
* **Contact Person Number 2** (18): Enter the contact person's number 2.
* **Contact Person Email 2** (19): Enter the contact person's email 2.
* **Contact Person Address 2** (20): Enter the contact person's address 2.
* **Agency Address** (21): Enter the agency's address.
* **Agency Number** (22): Enter the agency's number.
* **Agency Email** (23): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Agency Name** (24): Enter the agency's name.
* **Assigned Tool** (25): Enter the assigned tool.
