# Mother's Day Raffle Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mother's Day Raffle Form is used to collect information from users for a Mother's Day raffle event. This form helps track entries and manage ticket sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as it appears on your ticket or ID.
2. Enter your email address to contact you if you win.
3. Provide your phone number to contact you in case of a win or for event updates.
4. Fill in the date you entered the raffle.
5. Indicate the price of the ticket(s) you purchased.
6. Specify the number of tickets you bought.
7. Enter the time you entered the raffle.
8. Describe the prize you are hoping to win.
9. If a winner is chosen, provide their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ticket or ID.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you in case of a win or for event updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Entry Date**: Fill in the date you entered the raffle.
* **Entry Time**: Enter the time you entered the raffle.
* **Prize Description**: Describe the prize you are hoping to win.
* **Raffle Date**: Not explicitly required, this field likely sets the date the raffle will take place.
* **Total Tickets Sold**: Not required for user input, this field likely displays the total tickets sold for the event.
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price you paid for the ticket(s).
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you purchased.
* **Winner Contact Info**: Not required, this field likely provides a way for the winner to be contacted after the draw.
* **Name of Winner**: Not explicitly required for user input, this field likely displays the name of the winner after the draw.
