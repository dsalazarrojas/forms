# Highland Games Ticketing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit ticketing information for an event or game.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your First Name.
- Enter your Last Name.
- Provide your Email Address.
- Enter your Phone Number.
- Select the ticket type(s) you are purchasing.
- Select the date of the event.
- Select the time of the event.
- Enter the price of the ticket(s).
- Add any additional notes about the order.
- Choose the status of the ticket (New, Sold, or Cancelled).
- Add any additional information about the ticket status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Select Ticket Type** (Select One): Choose the type of ticket you are purchasing.
	+ General Admission Ticket
	+ VIP Ticket
	+ Family Ticket
* **Select Multiple Ticket Types** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable ticket types.
* **Event Date** (Date): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time): Select the time of the event.
* **Ticket Price** (Number): Enter the price of the ticket(s).
* **Note** (Note): Add any additional information about the order.
* **Ticket Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the ticket.
	+ New
	+ Sold
	+ Cancelled
* **Ticket Status Note** (Note): Add any additional information about ticket status.
* **Ticket Quantity** (Number): Enter the number of tickets purchased.
