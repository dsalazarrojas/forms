# Valentines Day Party Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for Valentine's Day party tickets. The collected data will be used to process ticket orders and provide a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  1. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
  2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
  3. Select your preferred ticket type (Single, Couple, or Family) in the "Ticket Type" field.
  4. Specify the number of tickets you would like to purchase in the "Number of Tickets" field.
  5. Choose the date of the event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
  6. Select the event time in the "Event Time" field.
  7. If you have any special requests or messages for our team, type them in the "Message" field.
  8. Finally, choose which tools will be assigned to you or your group by selecting them from the options in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your ticket(s).
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute updates or changes.
- **Ticket Type** (Ticket Type, select_one, required): Choose from Single, Couple, or Family to specify how many tickets you require.
- **Number of Tickets** (Number of Tickets, number, required): Indicate the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
- **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar to ensure you don't miss out on the fun.
- **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required): Choose the time of the event so we can schedule it accordingly.
- **Message** (Message, note, required): Type any special requests or messages for our team to know about.
- **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_multiple, required): Select the tools that will be assigned to you or your group for the event.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to guarantee the correct tickets are assigned to you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us before proceeding with your order.
