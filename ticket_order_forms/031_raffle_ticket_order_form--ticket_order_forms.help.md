# raffle_ticket_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Raffle Ticket Order Form** is a form designed to collect information for raffle tickets. This form is used to place orders for raffle tickets, which are used to raise funds for charity or other causes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form fields with the required information.
2. Select the **Ticket Type** that you wish to order (Raffle, Charity, or Fundraiser).
3. Enter the **Ticket Price** for each ticket you wish to order.
4. Specify the **Order Quantity** of tickets you wish to purchase.
5. Provide your **Customer Name** and **Email Address** so we can contact you about your order.
6. Enter the **Order Date** you would like to place your order by.
7. Provide your **Phone Number** so we can contact you if needed.
8. Choose the **Payment Method** you would like to use (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
9. Enter the **Payment Amount** for your order.
10. Select the **Payment Status** of your order (Pending, Completed, or Failed).
11. Enter the **Raffle Date** and **Raffle Time** for the raffle event.
12. Select the **Drawing Date** and **Drawing Time** for the raffle event.
13. Choose the **Raffle Result** (Not Drawn, Won, or Not Yet Drawn).
14. Enter the **Winner Name** if you are a winner.
15. Add any additional **Note** about your order if necessary.
16. Upload a **Raffle Image** if required.
17. Add any additional **Payment Note** if necessary.
18. Upload a **Payment Image** if required.
19. Upload an **Image Upload** if necessary.
20. Add any additional **Upload Note** if necessary.
21. Enter the **Raffle Ticket Number** for the ticket you are ordering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Type**: Select the type of raffle ticket you wish to order (Raffle, Charity, or Fundraiser).
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of each ticket you wish to order.
* **Order Quantity**: Specify the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name so we can contact you about your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place your order by.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you would like to use (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your order (Pending, Completed, or Failed).
* **Raffle Date**: Enter the date for the raffle event.
* **Raffle Time**: Enter the time for the raffle event.
* **Drawing Date**: Enter the date for the drawing event.
* **Drawing Time**: Enter the time for the drawing event.
* **Raffle Result**: Select the result of the raffle event (Not Drawn, Won, or Not Yet Drawn).
* **Winner Name**: Enter the name of the winner if you are a winner.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your order if necessary.
* **Raffle Image**: Upload a raffle image if required.
* **Payment Note**: Add any additional information about your payment if necessary.
* **Payment Image**: Upload a payment image if required.
* **Image Upload**: Upload an image if necessary.
* **Upload Note**: Add any additional information about your upload if necessary.
* **Raffle Ticket Number**: Enter the ticket number for the ticket you are ordering.
