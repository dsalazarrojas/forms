# Documentary Screening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Documentary Screening Pre Order Form is used to collect information about events, screenings, or conferences with ticket sales. This form helps event organizers gather details about their event, such as the title, date, address, and ticket sales information, to process and manage their event efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields for the event details: event title, event date, event time, event address, event city, and event zip.
2. Select the ticket price and ticket type for your event.
3. Choose the correct ticket quantity and payment method for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of your event, screening, or conference.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event.
* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your event.
* **Event City** (`event_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your event location.
* **Event State** (`event_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your event location.
* **Event Zip** (`event_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your event location.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of each ticket for your event.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase for your event.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of ticket you would like to buy: Individual, Bulk, or Group.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more payment methods for your order: Online Payment or Offline Payment.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any errors in processing your order.
* If you are purchasing tickets for a group, be sure to select the correct ticket type to ensure proper processing. 
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, please contact our support team for assistance.
