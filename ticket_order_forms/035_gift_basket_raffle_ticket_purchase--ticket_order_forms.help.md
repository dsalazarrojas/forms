# Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Purchase - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to purchase gift baskets and record relevant information for each purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

To purchase a gift basket raffle ticket, complete the form by:

1. Selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for "Purchase Gift Basket Raffle Ticket"
2. Providing your name
3. Entering your email address
4. Optionally providing a phone number
5. Donating an amount (if desired)
6. Selecting whether the gift basket has a value
7. Choosing the type of ticket
8. Indicating the quantity of tickets purchased
9. Optionally adding additional comments
10. Confirming the ticket details, including the ticket number, total price, and ticket purchaser name, email, phone, and ticket price

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Gift Basket Raffle Ticket** (`Yes/No`, `select_one`): Select "Yes" if you wish to purchase a gift basket raffle ticket, or "No" if you do not.
* **Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your name as the ticket purchaser.
* **Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, `optional`): If you have a phone number, enter it for further contact.
* **Donation Amount** (`number`, `optional`): If you wish to donate an amount, enter it here. This is not required.
* **Gift Basket Value** (`Yes/No`, `select_multiple`): Indicate whether the gift basket has a value or not. This could be a yes or no answer.
* **Ticket Type** (`Yes/No`, `select_one`): Choose whether the ticket is "Yes" or "No". The meaning of "Yes" and "No" here may be context-dependent.
* **Quantity** (`number`, `required`): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Comments** (`note`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or remarks regarding your purchase.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, `optional`): Add further additional comments or remarks regarding your purchase or ticket.
* **Ticket Purchaser Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter the name of the person purchasing the ticket.
* **Ticket Purchaser Email** (`email`, `optional`): Enter the email address of the person purchasing the ticket.
* **Ticket Purchaser Phone** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the phone number of the person purchasing the ticket.
* **Ticket Price** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total price of the ticket purchase.
* **Total** (`number`, `required`): Confirm the total price of the purchase.
* **Ticket Number** (`text`, `required`): Enter the number of the ticket purchased.
* **Date** (`date`, `optional`): Optionally provide the date of the purchase.
* **Time** (`time`, `optional`): Optionally provide the time of the purchase.
* **Price** (`number`, `required`): Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Raffle Raffle Ticket ID** (`text`, `required`): Enter the ID of the raffle ticket purchased.
* **Raffle Price** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the price of the raffle ticket.
* **Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Quantity** (`number`, `required`): Enter the quantity of tickets purchased.
* **Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Value** (`Yes/No`, `select_one`): Indicate whether the gift basket has a value or not.
* **Raffle Raffle Ticket** (`Yes/No`, `select_one`): Choose whether the ticket is a raffle ticket or not.
* **Payment** (`Yes/No`, `select_multiple`): Choose whether payment is "Yes" or "No" for this purchase.
