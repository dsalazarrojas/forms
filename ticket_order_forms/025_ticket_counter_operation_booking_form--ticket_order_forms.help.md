# Ticket Counter Operation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book tickets for operations at a ticket counter. It allows customers to provide information about their ticket type, booking date, start and end times, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of ticket you would like to book.
2. Enter your booking date in the required date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Select your start and end times for the ticket booking.
4. Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you.
5. Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
6. If you have any additional comments or messages, please enter them here.
7. Choose the tool that will be assigned to you for the ticket operation.
8. Select the status of your form submission (submitted, not submitted, or pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_multiple): Please select the type of ticket you would like to book. Options are Ticket 1, Ticket 2, and Ticket 3.
* **Ticket Number** (`ticket_number`, text): This field is for you to enter the ticket number you would like to book (if applicable).
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date): Enter the date you would like to book the ticket. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Select the start time for your ticket booking.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Select the end time for your ticket booking.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text): If you have any additional comments or messages, please enter them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Choose the tool that will be assigned to you for the ticket operation. Options are chatjimmy, default-tool-1, and default-tool-2.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or messages about your ticket booking.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date): This field is automatically populated with the current date when the form is submitted.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one): Select the status of your form submission. Options are submitted, not submitted, and pending.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct ticket type and entered the correct booking date and times.
* If you have any additional comments or messages, please include them in the customer message field.
* If you are unsure about the status of your submission, please contact us and we will assist you further.
