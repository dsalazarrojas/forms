# Play Ticket Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Play Ticket Release Form is used to capture information from users when releasing play tickets. This form helps event organizers and ticket vendors to manage ticket releases efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a title for the release**: In the `Form Title` field, provide a brief description or name for the ticket release.
2. **Check the performance status**: Select `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether the ticket performance has occurred or not.
3. **Specify the ticket quantity**: Enter the number of tickets being released.
4. **Choose your delivery preference**: Select `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether you prefer delivery or not.
5. **Provide contact information**: Enter your email address or phone number, depending on your preferred contact method.
6. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any notes or comments regarding the ticket release, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description or name for the ticket release.
* **Performance** (`ticket_performance`, select_one, required/optional): Check `Yes` or `No` to indicate whether the ticket performance has occurred or not.
* **Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of tickets being released.
* **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Check `Yes` or `No` to indicate your delivery preference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the ticket release.

## Tips

* Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy.
* Be clear and concise in your form title and notes entries.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the event organizers or ticket vendors for assistance.
