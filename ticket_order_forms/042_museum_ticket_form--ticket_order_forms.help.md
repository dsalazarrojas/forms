# Museum Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a museum ticket order, making it easier for the museum staff to process the order and provide the best possible experience for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "order_details" field with any relevant information about your visit, such as the date and time of your visit or any special requests you may have.
2. Provide your ticket information, including your ticket number or any other relevant details about your ticket in the "ticket_info" field.
3. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field, including your name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **order_details** (text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your visit, such as the date and time of your visit or any special requests you may have.
- **ticket_info** (text, optional): Provide your ticket number or any other relevant details about your ticket.
- **contact_details** (text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to help the museum staff contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field to ensure the museum staff can process your ticket order efficiently.
- If you have any special requests or need further assistance, please contact the museum staff directly.
