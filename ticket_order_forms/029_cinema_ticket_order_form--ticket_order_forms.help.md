# Cinema Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cinema Ticket Order Form is designed to collect information from users for cinema ticket orders. This form helps us get the ticket details and payment information needed for the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out the form.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for ticket confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any reason.
* **Select Cinema Location** (`cinema_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the location of the cinema where you'd like to watch your movie from the available options (Downtown Theater, Mall Complex, Suburban Location, Airport Location, Premium IMAX).
* **Movie Title** (`movie_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the movie you'd like to watch.
* **Movie Format** (`movie_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the movie format you prefer (2D Standard, 3D, IMAX, Dolby Cinema, Premium Large Format).
* **Show Date** (`show_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you'd like to watch the movie.
* **Show Time** (`show_time`, `time`, required): Select the time slot you'd like to watch the movie.
* **Number of Adult Tickets** (`adult_tickets`, `number`, required): Enter the number of adult tickets you'd like to purchase.
* **Number of Child Tickets** (`child_tickets`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of child tickets you'd like to purchase (Ages 3-12).
* **Number of Senior Tickets** (`senior_tickets`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of senior tickets you'd like to purchase (Ages 65+).
* **Number of Student Tickets** (`student_tickets`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of student tickets you'd like to purchase (With valid student ID).
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred seating option (Standard seating, Premium seating, Accessibility seating, No preference).
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessible_seating`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, select 'Yes' here.
* **Would You Like to Add Concessions** (`add_concessions`, `select_one`, optional): If you'd like to add any concessions, select 'Yes' here.
* **Popcorn (quantity and size)** (`popcorn_quantity`, `text`, optional): Enter the quantity and size of popcorn you'd like to purchase.
* **Beverages (quantity and size)** (`beverage_quantity`, `text`, optional): Enter the quantity and size of beverages you'd like to purchase.
* **Candy or Snacks** (`candy_items`, `text`, optional): Enter any candy or snack items you'd like to purchase.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, Online banking, Mobile payment, Gift card).
* **Promotion or Discount Code** (`promo_code`, `text`, optional): If you have a promotion or discount code, enter it here.
* **Membership or Loyalty Program Number** (`membership_number`, `text`, optional): If you're a member or loyalty program holder, enter your member number here.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to receive your ticket (Print at home, Mobile ticket, Will call pickup, Email delivery).

Note: Some fields may have additional options or requirements that are not specified here. If you're unsure, please contact us for further assistance.
