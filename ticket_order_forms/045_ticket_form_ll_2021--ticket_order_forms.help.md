# Ticket Form LL 2021 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage ticket-related data. It is intended for customers who are purchasing tickets for various events or purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form Data** (`order_form_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address 1** (`customer_address_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **Customer Address 2** (`customer_address_2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **Customer Address 3** (`customer_address_3`, text, optional): Enter your address line 3.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of ticket you are purchasing. Examples: Ticket 1, Ticket 2, Ticket 3.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the ticket.
* **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, time, optional): Enter the time you purchased the ticket.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your ticket.
* **Ticket Status** (`ticket_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple status options. Examples: Status 1, Status 2, Status 3.
* **Customer Order ID** (`customer_order_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer order ID.
* **Ticket ID** (`ticket_id`, text, optional): Enter your ticket ID.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person assigned to handle your ticket. Examples: chatjimmy, Other.
* **Ticket Date Assigned** (`ticket_date_assigned`, date, optional): Enter the date the ticket was assigned.
* **Ticket Time Assigned** (`ticket_time_assigned`, time, optional): Enter the time the ticket was assigned.
* **Ticket Status Update** (`ticket_status_update`, note, optional): Enter a status update about your ticket.
* **Ticket Type Update** (`ticket_type_update`, select_one, optional): Select a ticket type update. Examples: Ticket 1, Ticket 2, Ticket 3.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate and complete.
