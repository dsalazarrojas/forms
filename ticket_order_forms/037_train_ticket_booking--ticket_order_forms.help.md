# Train Ticket Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Train Ticket Booking form is designed to collect and process information for train ticket bookings, allowing users to book and track their tickets efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the ticket details, including ticket price and train number.
2. Provide information about the travelers, such as their number, type, and age.
3. Select the payment method (credit card, debit card, or PayPal).
4. Choose the payment status (paid, pending, or cancelled).
5. Add any additional comments about the booking.
6. Enter the travel date and time.
7. Select the train class and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Train Number** (`train_ticket_booking_train_number`): Enter the number of the train ticket you are booking.
* **Train Type** (`train_ticket_booking_train_type`): Choose the type of train (e.g., intercity, express, etc.).
* **Travelers** (`train_ticket_booking_travelers`): Enter the number of travelers booking the ticket.
* **Traveler** (`train_ticket_booking_traveler`): Enter the details of each traveler, including their type, gender, and age.
* **Contact Details** (`train_ticket_booking_contact_details`): Enter your contact information for billing and communication purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`train_ticket_booking_payment_method`): Choose the payment method (credit card, debit card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Status** (`train_ticket_booking_payment_status`): Select the payment status (paid, pending, or cancelled).
* **Comments** (`train_ticket_booking_comments`): Add any additional comments about the booking.
* **Date** (`train_ticket_booking_date`): Enter the travel date.
* **Time** (`train_ticket_booking_time`): Enter the travel time.
* **Train Station** (`train_ticket_booking_train_station`): Enter the station where the train journey starts or ends.
* **Train Journey** (`train_ticket_booking_train_journey`): Enter the details of the train journey.
* **Travelers Count** (`train_ticket_booking_travelers_count`): Enter the number of travelers.
* **Travelers Type** (`train_ticket_booking_travelers_type`): Choose the type of travelers (e.g., adults, students, etc.).
* **Travelers Gender** (`train_ticket_booking_travelers_gender`): Enter the gender of the travelers.
* **Travelers Age** (`train_ticket_booking_travelers_age`): Enter the age of the travelers.
* **Train Class** (`train_ticket_booking_train_class`): Choose the train class (e.g., first class, second class, etc.).
* **Train Status** (`train_ticket_booking_train_status`): Select the status of the train (confirmed, not confirmed, or unknown).
