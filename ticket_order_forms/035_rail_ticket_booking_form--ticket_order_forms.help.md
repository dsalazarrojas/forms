# Rail Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rail Ticket Booking Form is designed to collect essential information for rail ticket bookings. This form helps rail staff to quickly and efficiently process and manage bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a reservation date for the ticket booking.
2. Enter the departure and arrival station names.
3. Choose the travel class (First Class, Second Class, Third Class) for the ticket.
4. Select the departure and arrival times.
5. Enter the passenger's name, email, and phone number.
6. Choose the booking status (Booked or Cancelled).
7. Provide any additional passenger notes, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date of the reservation. This date will be used to determine the availability of tickets and ensure correct booking processing. Please format the date in a standard format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Departure Station**: Enter the name of the departure station. This is the station from where the passenger will depart.
* **Arrival Station**: Enter the name of the arrival station. This is the station where the passenger will arrive.
* **Travel Class**: Select the travel class (First Class, Second Class, Third Class) to choose the type of ticket to be booked.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time of departure. Please format the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time of arrival. Please format the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the passenger.
* **Passenger Email**: Enter the email address of the passenger. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Passenger Phone**: Enter the phone number of the passenger. This will be used for updates and notifications.
* **Booking Status**: Choose the booking status (Booked or Cancelled) to indicate the status of the ticket.
* **Passenger Note**: Provide any additional information about the passenger, if required. This can be used by rail staff to provide better service to the passenger.
