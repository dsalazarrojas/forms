# Season Ticket Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Season Ticket Order Form to ensure you have a smooth and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your season ticket details, including the number of tickets.
2. Choose your payment type and enter your contact information.
3. Provide your billing address, phone number, and email.
4. Add any additional information you would like to share with us.
5. Enter any comments or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season Ticket Details** (`season_ticket_order_form`): Enter your season ticket order details, including the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Tickets** (`season_ticket_order_form_tickets`): Select the number of tickets you would like to purchase. Options are 1-4 tickets, 5-8 tickets, 9-12 tickets, or 13-16 tickets.
* **Payment Type** (`season_ticket_order_form_payment_type`): Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Check, or Cash.
* **Billing Address** (`season_ticket_order_form_billing_address`): Enter your billing address for the season tickets.
* **Phone** (`season_ticket_order_form_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`season_ticket_order_form_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Additional Information** (`season_ticket_order_form_additional_information`): Add any additional information you would like to share with us about your order.
* **Comments** (`season_ticket_order_form_comments`): Enter any comments or special requests you may have about your order.
