# Entertainment Event Ticket Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Event Ticket Pre Order Form is a pre-order form for a concert event. It asks users for their details and preferences to facilitate the event ticket pre-ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Concert Details** field with the relevant information about the concert.
2. Provide details about the **Show** in the corresponding field.
3. Choose your **Ticket Type** by selecting "Yes" or "No" depending on your preference.
4. Select your seat preferences using the **Seat Selection** field.
5. Enter the **Date and Time** of the event, if applicable.
6. Specify the **Number of Tickets** you wish to purchase.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information for communication purposes.
8. Optionally, provide more details about the **Event** in the note field.
9. Confirm your **Ticket Price** (if provided, as this field is currently empty and does not appear to be required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concert Details**: This field is for entering the details of the concert, such as the name, date, and venue of the event. It's a free-text field for you to provide any necessary information about the concert.
* **Show Details**: Enter the details about the show, such as the performer or performer's name.
* **Ticket Type**: This field asks whether you want to purchase a ticket or not. Choose "Yes" if you are interested in buying a ticket, and "No" if you are not.
* **Seat Selection**: Select the seat(s) you prefer for the event. You can select multiple seats if you are buying more than one ticket.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the event if it's not already provided in the concert details.
* **Number of Tickets**: Specify how many tickets you want to buy.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and information about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so our staff can contact you if needed.
* **Event Details**: This is a note field for you to add any additional details about the event that might be useful for us to know.
* **Ticket Price**: This field is currently empty and appears to be a placeholder. However, if provided, enter the price of the ticket.
