# Child Ticket Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request tickets for children attending an event. It's designed to collect information about the child's age category, number of tickets, and specific needs to process the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you're attending.
2. Provide the name of the parent or guardian requesting the tickets.
3. Indicate the number of child tickets you're requesting.
4. Select the child's age category (Under 2 years, 2-12 years, or 13-17 years) to determine pricing and seating.
5. Check if you require accessible seating.
6. Indicate if you'll be bringing a stroller.
7. Enter your email address for electronic ticket delivery.
8. Provide your phone number for SMS alerts and reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name of Event** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you're attending, e.g., Summer Festival, Puppet Show.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian requesting the tickets.
- **Number of Child Tickets Requested** (`child_ticket_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of child tickets you're requesting.
- **Child Age Category** (`age_category_select`, select_one, required): Select the child's age category to determine pricing and seating:
  - Under 2 years - Free Entry
  - 2 to 12 years - Child Rate
  - 13 to 17 years - Junior Rate
- **Accessible Seating** (`accessible_seating_status`, select_one, required): Check if you require accessible seating for any child.
- **Stroller Access** (`stroller_access_status`, select_one, required): Check if you'll be bringing a stroller for any child.
- **Email for Ticket Delivery** (`delivery_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive electronic tickets.
- **Phone Number for SMS Alerts** (`alerts_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive event updates and reminders.
