# Spring Fair Ticket Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Fair Ticket Pre Order form is designed to collect essential information from customers for the Spring Fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the correct type of ticket you want to order.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the date and time of the event you want to attend.
6. Specify the number of tickets you want to order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Ticket Type (<label>: Ticket Type)
* **Ticket Type (ticket_type)**: Select the type of ticket you want to order (Adult, Child, or Senior).

### Name (<label>): Name
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ticket.

### Email (<label>): Email
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address to associate with your order.

### Phone (<label>): Phone
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number for any further communication regarding your order.

### Event Date/Time (<label>): Event Date/Time
* **Event Date/Time (order_date)**: Select the date and time of the event you want to attend.

### Ticket Quantity (<label>): Ticket Quantity
* **Ticket Quantity (ticket_quantity)**: Specify the number of tickets you want to order.
