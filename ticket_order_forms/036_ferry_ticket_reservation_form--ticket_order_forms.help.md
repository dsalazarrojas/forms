# Ferry Ticket Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ferry Ticket Reservation Form is designed to collect information for booking a ferry ticket. Please fill out the form with your details to complete the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Reservation Form (page 1) with your name, contact information, and travel requirements.
2. Select your travel class, number of passengers, and payment method on the following pages. Ensure you confirm each option accurately.
3. Verify your travel details, including departure and return times, dates, and pickup and dropoff points.
4. Confirm your booking by clicking the Submit button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Reservation Form** (`reservation_form`, text): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details required for your booking.
- **Passenger Info** (`passenger_info`, text): Enter passenger information, such as names and contact details.
- **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time): Select the departure time for your ferry travel.
- **Return Time** (`return_time`, time): Select the return time for your ferry travel.
- **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date): Select the departure date for your ferry travel.
- **Travel Class** (`travel_class`, select_one): Choose your preferred travel class, either Economy, Business, or First Class.
- **Number Of Passengers** (`number_of_passengers`, number): Select the number of passengers for your booking.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Select your preferred payment method, either Credit Card, Bank Deposit, or PayPal.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date): Select the payment date for your booking.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments for your booking.
- **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, select_multiple): Confirm your booking details, such as departure and return times, dates, and travel class.
- **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_multiple): Confirm your payment details, including payment method and date.
- **Confirm Pickup** (`confirm_pickup`, select_multiple): Confirm your pickup details, including location and time.
- **Confirm Return** (`confirm_return`, select_multiple): Confirm your return details, including return time and date.
- **Confirm Travel Class** (`confirm_travel_class`, select_multiple): Confirm your travel class selection.
- **Confirm Number Of Passengers** (`confirm_number_of_passengers`, select_multiple): Confirm the number of passengers for your booking.
- **Confirm Departure Time** (`confirm_departure_time`, select_multiple): Confirm your departure time.
- **Confirm Return Time** (`confirm_return_time`, select_multiple): Confirm your return time.
- **Confirm Departure Date** (`confirm_departure_date`, select_multiple): Confirm your departure date.
- **Confirm Return Date** (`confirm_return_date`, select_multiple): Confirm your return date.
- **Confirm Travel Date** (`confirm_travel_date`, select_multiple): Confirm your travel date.
- **Confirm Number Of Travelers** (`confirm_number_of_travelers`, select_multiple): Confirm the number of travelers for your booking.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text): Click the Submit button to complete your booking.
