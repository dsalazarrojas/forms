# Lets Get You AVENTURA TICKETS ! - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and ticket details for AVENTURA tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields with your contact information and ticket details.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your name?** (`name`, text, required): This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you use to receive important notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your mobile or landline number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field is used to collect the date of the event you are interested in. Please enter the date in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): This field is used to collect the time of the event you are interested in. Please enter the time in a standard 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required): This field is used to collect any additional information or comments you may have about your ticket request.
* **Additional Name 1** (`name_1`, text, required): This field is used to collect an additional name associated with your ticket request. 
* **Additional Name 2** (`name_2`, text, required): This field is used to collect another additional name associated with your ticket request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your ticket request is processed correctly.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact information].
