# Tourist Transport Ticket Order - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tourist Transport Ticket Order form is designed to collect information from tourists for their transport orders. This form helps in organizing and managing their transport requirements effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "Name".
2. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
4. Select the date of your travel in the field labeled "Date".
5. Enter the point of departure in the field labeled "Departure Point".
6. Enter the destination point of your trip in the field labeled "Destination Point".
7. Choose your preferred payment method in the field labeled "Payment Method".
8. Enter the date of payment in the field labeled "Payment Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable us to communicate with you regarding your transport order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any further communication regarding your transport order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **Departure Point** (`departure_point`, text, required): Enter the city or airport from where you plan to depart.
* **Destination Point** (`destination_point`, text, required): Enter the city or airport where you are heading.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Choose how you plan to pay for your transport order.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to make the payment for your transport order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
