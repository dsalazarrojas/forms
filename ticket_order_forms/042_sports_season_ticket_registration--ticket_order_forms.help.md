# Sports Season Ticket Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a sports season ticket. It helps us understand your preferences for the ticket type, game details, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of ticket you are interested in (Single Game, Season, or Series).
2. Provide your game details, including any relevant information about the game you are interested in.
3. Enter your team details, if applicable.
4. Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
5. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the ticket(s).
6. Provide your contact details, including any necessary information.
7. Enter any special requests or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket Type** (`Ticket Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of ticket you are interested in (Single Game, Season, or Series).
* **Game Details** (`Game Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the game you are interested in, such as the date and opponent.
* **Team Details** (`Team Details`, `text`, required/optional): If you are interested in a specific team, enter their details here.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`Ticket Quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Ticket Price** (`Ticket Price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the ticket(s).
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your contact details, including any necessary information.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special requests or comments you have.

## Tips

* If you have registered for a ticket in the past, please do not submit this form again as your information will not be updated.
* Please ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them in the Special Requests field.
