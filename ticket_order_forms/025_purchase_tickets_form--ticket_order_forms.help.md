# Purchase Tickets Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Tickets Form is a tool designed to help users purchase tickets in a controlled environment. It is meant for users with the necessary permissions and authority to place orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Ticket Form with your ticket information.
2. Select the number of tickets you wish to purchase in the Ticket Count field.
3. Indicate if you would like to register any gifts with the Gift Registry field.
4. Confirm if you would like to purchase tickets with the Purchase Tickets field.
5. Check the Available Tickets field for any restrictions on ticket availability.
6. Set your gift registry settings with the Gift Registry Settings field.
7. Submit your order with the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Form** (`ticket_form`, text, not required): This field is for entering your ticket information.
* **Ticket Count** (`ticket_count`, number, not required): Select the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Gift Registry** (`gift_registry`, select_one, not required): Choose to register any gifts you would like to purchase.
* **Purchase Tickets** (`purchase_tickets`, select_one, not required): Confirm your decision to purchase tickets.
* **Available Tickets** (`available_tickets`, select_multiple, not required): Check for any restrictions on ticket availability.
* **Gift Registry Settings** (`gift_registry_settings`, select_one, not required): Set your gift registry settings.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, not required): Click to submit your order.
* **Errors** (`errors`, note, not required): This field is not applicable as it is a note field and will display any errors that occur during the submission process.

## Tips
- Always check the Available Tickets field for any restrictions before submitting your order.
- Confirm your purchase decision carefully before submitting your order.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authority to use this form.
