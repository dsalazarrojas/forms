# event_ticket_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about events for ticketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on **Page 1**. This is the general information page where you can enter your name and any other details.
2. Move on to **Page 2**, where you can select one or more options from the dropdown menu.
3. Proceed to **Page 3**, where you can enter your date of interest.
4. On **Page 4**, you can enter a note about your selection.
5. Move on to **Page 5**, where you can enter your email address.
6. Fill out the form on **Page 6** with any additional text.
7. On **Page 7**, you can enter any further text information.
8. Proceed to **Page 8**, where you can select another option from the dropdown menu.
9. Finally, fill out **Page 9** with any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Ticket Form Page 1** (text, required): Enter your name and any other details about the event.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 2** (select multiple, required): Choose one or more options for the event.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 3** (date, optional): Select the date of interest for the event.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 4** (note, optional): Enter a note about your selection.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 5** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 6** (text, optional): Enter any additional text information about the event.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 7** (text, optional): Enter further text information.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 8** (select multiple, optional): Choose another option from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Ticket Form Page 9** (text, optional): Enter any final details about the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are filling out the form for the correct event.
* Check your information carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues, contact the event organizer.
