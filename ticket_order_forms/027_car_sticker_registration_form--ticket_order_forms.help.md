# Car Sticker Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Car Sticker Registration Form is a document used to register vehicles for different types of stickers or passes. It collects essential information about the registrant, vehicle, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the correct information, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
3. Ensure that the vehicle year and make fields are filled out with accurate information.
4. Choose the correct sticker type and its validity period.
5. Fill out the payment method and sticker registration fee fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you reside or have a registered vehicle.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city or the city where your vehicle is registered.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or the state where your vehicle is registered.
* **Zip code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code or the zip code of your registered address.
* **Vehicle year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, optional): Enter your vehicle's color (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's unique identification number.
* **License plate number** (`license_plate_number`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **License plate state** (`license_plate_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where your vehicle is registered.
* **Sticker type** (`sticker_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of sticker you want to register for (e.g., Parking Permit, Resident Sticker, etc.).
* **Sticker validity period** (`sticker_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the validity period for your sticker (e.g., Annual, Semester, etc.).
* **Sticker valid from date** (`valid_from_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date from which your sticker is valid.
* **Sticker expiration date** (`valid_to_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date until which your sticker is valid.
* **Sticker registration fee** (`sticker_cost`, `number`, required): Fill out the sticker registration fee accurately.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Additional notes or special requests** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or special requests you may have.

Please review the form carefully and fill out the required fields accurately for a successful registration.
