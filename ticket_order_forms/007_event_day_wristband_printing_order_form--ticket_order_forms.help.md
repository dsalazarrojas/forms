# Event Day Wristband Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to order wristbands for events. Fill out this form to place your order for custom-designed wristbands with your event details and shipping information. 

## How To Complete This Form

To start, fill out the form with the required information to place your order. The form is divided into sections for event details and shipping information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Order ID** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_order_id`)
    * This field is not currently used in the form and can be ignored.
    
### 2. **Event Name** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_event_name`)
    * Enter the name of the event you are ordering wristbands for.
    * This field is not required, but providing it helps us associate your order with the correct event.

### 3. **Event Date** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_event_date`)
    * Enter the date of the event for which you are ordering wristbands.
    * This field is not required, but providing it helps us confirm the event date.

### 4. **Event Time** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_event_time`)
    * Enter the time of the event for which you are ordering wristbands.
    * This field is not required, but providing it helps us confirm the event timing.

### 5. **Event Location** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_event_location`)
    * Enter the location of the event for which you are ordering wristbands.
    * This field is not required, but providing it helps us confirm the event address.

### 6. **Order Type** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_order_type`)
    * Select the type of wristband order you are placing:
        + General Admission
        + VIP Admission
        + VIP VIP (not currently supported)

### 7. **Order Quantity** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_order_quantity`)
    * Enter the number of wristbands you are ordering.

### 8. **Shipping First Name** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_first_name`)
    * Enter your first name for shipping purposes.

### 9. **Shipping Last Name** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_last_name`)
    * Enter your last name for shipping purposes.

### 10. **Shipping Address** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_address`)
    * Enter your shipping address.

### 11. **Shipping City** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_city`)
    * Enter your shipping city.

### 12. **Shipping State** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_state`)
    * Enter your shipping state.

### 13. **Shipping Zip** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_zip`)
    * Enter your shipping postal/zip code.

### 14. **Shipping Country** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_country`)
    * Enter your shipping country.

### 15. **Shipping Email** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_email`)
    * Enter your email for shipping purposes.

### 16. **Shipping Phone** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_shipping_phone`)
    * Enter your shipping phone number.

### 17. **Total Cost** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_total_cost`)
    * This field is not currently used in the form and can be ignored.
    
### 18. **Payment Type** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_type`)
    * Select the payment method for your order:
        + Credit Card
        + PayPal
        + Bank Transfer (not supported)

### 19. **Payment Card Number** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_card_number`)
    * Enter your credit/debit card number for payment.

### 20. **Payment Expiration Date** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_expires`)
    * Enter the expiration date of your credit/debit card.

### 21. **Payment CCV** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_ccv`)
    * Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) for payment.

### 22. **Payment Zip Code** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_zip`)
    * Enter the zip code for payment verification.

### 23. **Payment Country** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_country`)
    * Enter your payment country.

### 24. **Payment Note** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_note`)
    * Enter any additional comments for your payment.

### 25. **Payment Method** (`event_day_wristband_printing_order_form_payment_method`)
    * Select the payment method for your order:
        + Credit Card
        + Bank Transfer
        + PayPal

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for event details and shipping information to ensure prompt and accurate processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* We will do our best to accommodate your wristband order requirements.
