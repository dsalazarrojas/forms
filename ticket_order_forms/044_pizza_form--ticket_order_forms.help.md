# Pizza Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pizza Form" is a user-facing form designed to collect information for new orders. It is meant to be filled out by customers to provide their details and preferences for their order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as needed.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure that your order can be processed correctly.
3. Review your order details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number for potential follow-up communication.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you'd like to place your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time you'd like to place your order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for order updates and notifications.
* **Pizza Size** (`pizza_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the pizza you'd like to order (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Toppings** (`toppings`, select_multiple, optional): Select the toppings you'd like to add to your pizza (Sausage, Mushrooms, Onions, Pepperoni, Extra Cheese).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): Add any special requests or instructions for your order, such as "no crust change" or "add extra sauce".
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select your payment preference (Credit Card, Cash, PayPal).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your order (New, In Progress, Complete, Cancelled, Closed). 

## Tips
* Make sure to review your order details carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact our team for assistance.
* We will contact you based on your provided contact information to confirm your order.
