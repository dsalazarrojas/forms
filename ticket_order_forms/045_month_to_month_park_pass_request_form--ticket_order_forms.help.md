# Month To Month Park Pass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users requesting a month-to-month park pass. It's used to gather essential details about the users' contact information, vehicle details, and date preferences to process their park pass request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as requested.
2. Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
3. Double-check that you've filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)**
  • `Contact Information`: Provide your name, email, phone number, and address to help us get in touch with you about your park pass request.

*  **Vehicle Information (Page 1)**
  • `Vehicle Information`: Enter the details of your vehicle, including license plate number, make, and color, so we can identify and validate your pass.
  • This will also help us determine whether your vehicle is eligible for the park pass.
  
* **Date Preferences (Page 1)**
  • `Date Preferences`: Let us know when you would prefer your park pass to be valid from and to, so we can schedule it accurately.
  • You can enter a specific date range or an open-ended date, depending on your preference.
  • If you need a one-time pass, please enter a single date for the start date and leave the end date empty.
  • You can also request a recurring pass for specific weekdays or a fixed date range.

Tips:
  - Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can get in touch with you about your request.
  - Double-check your vehicle information for any errors.
  - Be specific about your date preferences, and we'll do our best to accommodate them.
  - If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
