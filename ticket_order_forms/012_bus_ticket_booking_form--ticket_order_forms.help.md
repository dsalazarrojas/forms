# Bus Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for bus ticket booking. It will 
collect passenger details, travel information, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and select 
your preferred options for the ticket class, seat location, and payment 
method. If you have a promotional code, you can enter it to receive 
discounts. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before 
submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (`passenger_full_name`, text, required)
Your full name as it appears on your ID.

### 2. Email Address (`passenger_email`, email, required)
The email address where the ticket will be sent to.

### 3. Phone Number (`passenger_phone`, text, required)
Your phone number for SMS updates.

### 4. Departure City (`departure_city`, text, required)
The city of departure.

### 5. Destination City (`destination_city`, text, required)
The city of destination.

### 6. Date of Travel (`travel_date`, date, required)
The date of travel.

### 7. Number of Passengers (`passenger_count`, number, required)
The number of passengers, including adults and children.

### 8. Ticket Class (`ticket_type_class`, select_one, required)
Select your preferred level of service.

### 9. Preferred Seat Location (`seat_location_preference`, select_one, required)
Select your preferred seat location, subject to availability.

### 10. Promotional Code (`promo_code_entry`, text, optional)
Enter a promotional code if applicable.

### 11. Payment Method (`payment_method_booking`, select_one, required)
Select a secure payment method.

### 12. I agree to the Cancellation and Refund Policy (`booking_terms_consent`, select_one, required)
Please agree to the cancellation and refund policy.
