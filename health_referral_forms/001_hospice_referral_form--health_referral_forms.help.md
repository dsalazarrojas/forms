# Hospice Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Referral Form is a tool used to collect and convey patient information to the designated hospice team for referral purposes. It helps ensure that the patient receives the best care and services they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's basic contact information in the "Patient Information" section, including their name and address.
2.  Provide the patient's date of birth and any relevant medical history.
3.  Identify the primary caregiver and their relationship to the patient.
4.  Enter the patient's contact number and any other relevant contact information.
5.  Select the patient's medical status and status with the hospice.
6.  Enter the hospice name and address.
7.  Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
• **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
• **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
• **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
### Patient and Caregiver Information
• **Primary Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the primary caregiver.
• **Primary Caregiver Relationship**: Select the relationship between the caregiver and the patient (e.g., "Spouse," "Family Member," "Friend," etc.).
### Patient Contact Information
• **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
• **Patient Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
### Medical Information
• **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
### Hospice Information
• **Hospice Name**: Enter the name of the designated hospice.
• **Hospice Phone**: Enter the phone number of the designated hospice.
• **Hospice Address**: Enter the address of the designated hospice.
### Referral Information
• **Patient Status**: Select the patient's status with the hospice (e.g., "Active," "Inactive," etc.).
• **Hospice Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
• **Referral Status**: Select the status of the referral (e.g., "New Referral," "Follow-up," etc.).
• **Hospice Referral Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
• **Patient Medical Status**: Select the patient's medical status (e.g., "Active," "Terminal," etc.).
