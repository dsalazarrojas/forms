# Home Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Referral Form is used to collect information about patients referred for home health care services. This form ensures that healthcare providers gather comprehensive data about patients, streamlining the referral process and enhancing patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and options.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
3. Select relevant health insurance information from the options provided.
4. Choose the necessary home health care services required by the patient.
5. Provide accurate medical history and condition details.
6. Enter contact information for the patient and emergency contact.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Info (2)**: Briefly describe the reason for the referral and any additional notes.
* **Health Insurance Info (3)**: Select the patient's current health insurance provider (e.g., Medical, Medicare, Medicaid, Tricare).
* **Home Health Care Services Needed (4)**: Choose the home health care services required by the patient (e.g., Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Other).
* **Patient Status (5)**: Enter the patient's current status (e.g., current date).
* **Date Of Birth (6)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Provider (7)**: Select the medical provider who is referring the patient (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Specialist, Other).
* **Patient Address (8)**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Condition (9)**: Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical condition.
* **Home Health Care Services Provided (10)**: Select the home health care services that will be provided to the patient (e.g., Bathing Assistance, Dressing Assistance, Feeding Assistance, Medication Management, Other).
* **Medical History (11)**: Enter any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Contact Info (12)**: Enter contact information for the patient and emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact (13)**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Patient Signature (14)**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Staff Signature (15)**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Date Completed (16)**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed (17)**: Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Submitted By (18)**: Select who submitted the referral (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Staff, Other).
* **Submitted Date (19)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time (20)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Updated By (21)**: Select who updated the form (e.g., Primary Care Physician, Staff, Other).
* **Updated Date (22)**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Updated Time (23)**: Enter the time the form was updated.
* **Submitted By User (24)**: Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Updated By User (25)**: Enter the name of the user who updated the form.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when completing the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Use clear and concise language when entering patient information.
- Review the form for any required fields that may have changed or updated since the last completion.
- Update the form accordingly to reflect any changes.
