# School Based Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for school-based therapy referrals. It helps identify the student's needs and ensures that the referral process is thorough and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

* **User Information** (`user_info`): This field is not used in this form.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`): Please provide the student's details, including name, grade, and age.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`): This field is optional but can be useful for contact information.
* **Therapy Information** (`therapy_info`): This field is not used in this form.
