# Mental Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Referral Form is used to collect information about clients seeking mental health services. This form helps healthcare professionals identify the type of mental health issues, the level of priority needed, and other essential details about the client. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name* (Text)**: Please enter the name of the client seeking mental health services.
* **Contact Info* (Text)**: Enter the contact information for the client, including phone number, email, or other relevant details.
* **Mental Health Issue* (Select)**: Choose the type of mental health issue the client is experiencing (Anxiety, Depression, Substance Abuse, or Trauma).
* **Health Professional* (Select)**: Identify if a health professional is involved in the client's care (Yes, No).
* **Referral Details (Text)**: Enter any additional information about the client's mental health needs or requirements.
* **Client Status* (Select Multiple)**: Select the current status of the client (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Priority Level* (Select)**: Determine the level of priority needed for the client's mental health issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **Client Age* (Number)**: Enter the age of the client.
* **Client Gender (Select)**: Identify the gender of the client (Male, Female).
* **Client Ethnicity (Select Multiple)**: Select the ethnicity of the client (Asian, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, White).
* **Referral Date* (Date)**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the referral.
* **Mental Health Professional Notes (Text)**: Enter any notes or comments from a mental health professional.
* **Next Steps (Text)**: Enter any next steps or follow-up actions.
* **Follow Up Appointments (Select)**: Identify if follow-up appointments are needed (Yes, No).
