# Medical Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information for referrals to a medical specialist. It helps healthcare providers communicate effectively with specialists, ensuring the patient receives the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the specialist you are referring the patient to (Cardiologist, Dermatologist, etc.).
2. Choose the reason for the referral (Patient needs specialist care, Specialist care is recommended, etc.).
3. Enter the patient's name, email, and date of birth.
4. Add the patient's address, medical record number, and any relevant allergies.
5. Enter the specialist's contact information, address, and any relevant medications.
6. Select the referral status (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
7. Choose whether to apply a fee to the specialist (if applicable).
8. Add any additional comments or notes for the specialist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Specialist** (`Medical Specialist`, select_one): 
Select the type of specialist you are referring the patient to (e.g. Cardiologist, Dermatologist, etc.).

* **Referral Reason** (`Referral Reason`, select_multiple): 
Choose the reason for the referral (e.g. Patient needs specialist care, Specialist care is recommended, etc.).

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text): 
Enter the patient's name.

* **Patient Email** (`Patient Email`, email): 
Enter the patient's email.

* **Patient Date of Birth** (`Patient Date of Birth`, date): 
Enter the patient's date of birth.

* **Patient Address** (`Patient Address`, text): 
Enter the patient's address.

* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, text): 
Enter the patient's medical record number.

* **Referral Date** (`Referral Date`, date): 
Enter the date of the referral.

* **Patient Gender** (`Patient Gender`, text): 
Select the patient's gender.

* **Specialist Contact** (`Specialist Contact`, text): 
Enter the specialist's contact information.

* **Specialist Address** (`Specialist Address`, text): 
Enter the specialist's address.

* **Referral Comment** (`Referral Comment`, note): 
Add any additional comments or notes for the specialist.

* **Referral Status** (`Referral Status`, select_one): 
Select the status of the referral (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).

* **Specialist Fee** (`Specialist Fee`, number): 
Choose whether to apply a fee to the specialist (if applicable).

* **Patient Fee** (`Patient Fee`, number): 
Choose whether to apply a fee to the patient (if applicable).

* **Patient Medication** (`Patient Medication`, text): 
Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.

* **Specialist Medication** (`Specialist Medication`, text): 
Enter any medications the specialist needs to be aware of.

* **Patient Allergies** (`Patient Allergies`, text): 
Enter any allergies the patient has.

* **Specialist Allergies** (`Specialist Allergies`, text): 
Enter any allergies the specialist needs to be aware of.

* **Patient Med History** (`Patient Med History`, text): 
Enter the patient's medical history.

* **Specialist Med History** (`Specialist Med History`, text): 
Enter the specialist's medical history.

* **Specialist Note** (`Specialist Note`, note): 
Add any additional notes for the specialist.
