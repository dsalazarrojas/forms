# Psychiatric Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychiatric Referral Form is used for collecting information from patients, healthcare providers, or mental health professionals to assess a patient's mental health needs and develop a plan for treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in sections, starting from the first page.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the healthcare provider or mental health professional assigned to the patient.
4. Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required/optional): Provide the patient's basic information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required/optional): Record the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or health conditions.
* **Mental Status** (`Mental Status`, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's current mental status, including their symptoms and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Treatment Plans** (`Treatment Plans`, text, required/optional): Outline any existing treatment plans or therapies that the patient is undergoing.
* **Discharge Information** (`Discharge Information`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the patient's previous discharge from a mental health facility or hospital.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required/optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required/optional): Record any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medical History 2** (`Medical History 2`, text, required/optional): Provide additional information about the patient's medical history, if needed.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, required/optional): Report any medical conditions the patient has, such as hypertension or diabetes.
* **Medical Procedures** (`Medical Procedures`, text, required/optional): Note any medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medications 2** (`Medications 2`, text, required/optional): List any additional medications the patient is taking.

## Tips
- Please be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please seek guidance from the healthcare provider or mental health professional.
- This form is for assessment and planning purposes only, and the information collected will be used to develop a treatment plan for the patient.
