# Generic Dental Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Generic Dental Referral Form is used to gather information about a patient's dental needs and schedule a referral to a specialist for further care. This form is a critical document that provides essential details about the patient's medical history, treatment requirements, and personal information. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly will help our referral team to provide the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Generic Dental Referral Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
3. Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Doctor** (`referral_doctor`, select_one, required): Select the specialist who will be performing the referral.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date when the patient will be receiving the referral.
* **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason why the patient is being referred to a specialist.
* **Specialist Notes** (`specialist_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the specialist's notes.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's needs or requirements.
* **Specialist Contact** (`specialist_contact`, select_multiple, required): Select the specialist's contact details (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Referral Doctor Notes** (`referral_doctor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the referral doctor's notes.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Specialist Contact Phone** (`specialist_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Specialist Email** (`specialist_email`, email, optional): Enter the specialist's email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Specialist Service** (`specialist_service`, select_one, required): Select the type of specialist service needed (e.g., orthodontic, general dentistry).
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender (Male or Female).
* **Specialist Name** (`specialist_name`, text, required): Enter the specialist's name.
* **Specialist Phone** (`specialist_phone`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's phone number.
* **Patient Street** (`patient_street`, text, required): Enter the patient's street address.
* **Patient City** (`patient_city`, text, optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State** (`patient_state`, select_one, required): Select the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip** (`patient_zip`, text, required): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Specialist Notes** (`specialist_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the specialist's notes.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's full address.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date the referral is scheduled.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the referral appointment.
