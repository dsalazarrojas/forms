# Endodontic Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from patients for the endodontic referral process. It is intended for healthcare professionals to assess and manage patient referrals for root canal treatments. By using this form, you will be able to gather essential details about the patient's information, contact details, dentist information, procedure requirements, and referral status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information for the patient, including their name, address, contact details, and any other relevant data.
2. Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
3. Provide details about the patient's dentist or the dentist who referred the patient.
4. Specify the necessary information about the procedure, including the type of procedure and any other relevant details.
5. Enter referral information, such as the referral reason and any additional comments.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Info** (Required: no)
* **Patient Name** (text): This field is used to enter the patient's name for the purpose of identification.
*   **Address** (text): Enter the patient's home or office address.
*   **Date of Birth** (text): Please enter the patient's date of birth.

### 2. **Patient Contact Info** (Required: no)
*   **Phone Number** (text): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email Address** (text): Enter the patient's email address for communication.
*   **Medical Record Number** (text): Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.

### 3. **Dentist Info** (Required: no)
*   **Dentist Name** (text): Enter the name of the referring dentist or the dentist who will be treating the patient.
*   **Dentist Office/Contact Info** (text): Enter the dentist's office address or contact information.

### 4. **Procedure Info** (Required: no)
*   **Procedure Type** (text): Specify the type of endodontic procedure required.
*   **Additional Procedure Details** (text): Enter any additional details or requirements for the procedure.

### 5. **Referral Information** (Required: no)
*   **Referral Reason** (text): Enter the reason for the referral.
*   **Referral Notes** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral.

### 6. **Additional Comments** (Required: no)
*   **Comments** (note): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the referral.
