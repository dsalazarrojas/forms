# neurology_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The neurology referral form is designed to gather comprehensive information about a patient's neurology care, helping healthcare professionals to provide accurate and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the patient's information and ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Complete the medical history section, providing details about the patient's past medical experiences and conditions.
3. Record the chief complaint, describing the patient's primary reason for consultation.
4. Document the neurological history, including any past neurological conditions or events.
5. Describe the examination results, detailing the findings and any abnormalities.
6. Provide the diagnosis and treatment plan for the patient.
7. Complete any additional patient information forms, providing further context for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: A form that captures basic patient information, including name, date of birth, and contact details. (Required)
* **Medical History**: A comprehensive section that details the patient's past medical experiences, including conditions, allergies, and medications. (Optional)
* **Chief Complaint**: A description of the patient's primary reason for consultation, explaining their main concern or issue. (Optional)
* **Neurological History**: A form that gathers information about the patient's past neurological conditions, events, or treatments. (Optional)
* **Examination**: A section where you describe the findings from the patient's examination, including any abnormalities or observations. (Optional)
* **Diagnosis**: A space to provide the diagnosis and treatment plan for the patient, summarizing the patient's condition and recommended course of action. (Optional)
* **Treatment Plan**: A detailed plan outlining the steps to be taken for the patient's care and recovery. (Optional)
* **Patient Information Form**: Additional detailed patient information can be captured on this form, providing more context for the patient's care. (Optional)
* **Medical History Form 1-6**: Additional medical history forms can be completed to provide more comprehensive information about the patient's past medical experiences. (Optional)
* **Examination Form 1-3**: Additional examination forms can be completed to document any abnormalities or findings from the patient's examination. (Optional)
* **Neurology Referral Form 1-2**: Additional referral forms can be completed to provide more detailed information about the patient's neurological care. (Optional)

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Verify that the patient's information is not already available in their medical record.
* Use the forms as a guide, but consult with a qualified medical professional if you are unsure about any information or have concerns about the patient's condition.
