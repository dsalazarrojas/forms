# Pediatric Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Referral Form is designed to collect information about a patient's health status and medical history for referral to other healthcare providers. This form helps ensure that the referring provider has all the necessary information to make informed decisions about the patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and other demographic information on page 1.
2. Provide the patient's medical record number and referring provider information on page 1.
3. Answer the medical history questions on pages 3-6, including the patient's medical test results, medical history, and cancer history.
4. Fill in the patient's next steps on page 7.
5. Review and verify all the information you have entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient Gender**: Choose the patient's gender from the dropdown menu (Male, Female, Other).
* **Patient Weight**: Enter the patient's weight in pounds.
* **Patient Height**: Enter the patient's height in inches.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number if available.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the patient's current medical provider.
* **Referring Provider**: Enter the name of the referring provider, if different from the patient's current medical provider.
* **Referral Receiving Facility**: Enter the name of the facility to which the patient is being referred.
* **Referral Receiving Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor at the facility to which the patient is being referred.

### Page 2: Referral Information
* **Reason for Referral**: Describe the reason for the referral.
* **Date of Referral**: Enter the date of referral in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Referral Status**: Choose the status of the referral from the dropdown menu (Pending, Accepted, Rejected).

### Page 3-4: Medical History
* **Patient Condition**: Enter a brief description of the patient's condition.
* **Patient Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has.
* **Patient Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Tests Requested**: List any medical tests requested for the patient.
* **Medical Test Results**: Enter any medical test results related to the patient's condition.
* **Patient Family History**: Enter any relevant family medical history.
* **Patient Social History**: Enter any relevant social history.
* **Patient Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Patient Medical History Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's medical history.

### Page 5-6: Cancer History
* **Patient Cancer History**: Enter any cancer-related medical history.
* **Patient Cancer Status**: Enter the patient's current cancer status (e.g., active, remission, etc.).

### Page 7: Next Steps
* **Patient Next Steps**: Enter the next steps planned for the patient's care.
