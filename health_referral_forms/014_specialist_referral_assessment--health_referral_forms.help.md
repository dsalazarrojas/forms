# Specialist Referral Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialist Referral Assessment form is a tool for healthcare specialists to refer patients to other specialists for specialized care. This form provides a structured format for referring specialists to gather and share information about the patient, the reason for referral, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.
2. Enter the condition being referred for in the "Condition" field.
3. Provide any additional medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
4. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.
5. Enter the contact information for the referring specialist.
6. Select the referral date and time.
7. Enter the reason for referral in the "Referral Reason" field.
8. Assess the referring specialist's opinion of the referral in the "Referring Specialist's Comment" field.
9. Select the outcome of the referral in the "Referral Outcome" field.
10. Enter the referring specialist's name, title, and contact information.
11. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
12. Enter the patient's ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Specialist** (required): Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.
* **Condition** (required): Describe the referral reason.
* **Medical Notes** (optional): Provide any additional medical notes.
* **Additional Information** (optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Contact Information** (required): Enter the contact information for the referring specialist.
* **Referral Date** (required): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (optional): Enter the time of the referral.
* **Referral Reason** (required): Select the reason for referral.
* **Referring Specialist's Comment** (optional): Assess the referring specialist's opinion of the referral.
* **Referral Outcome** (required): Select the outcome of the referral.
* **Referring Specialist's Signature** (required): Enter the referring specialist's signature.
* **Referring Specialist's Name** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's name.
* **Referring Specialist's Title** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's title.
* **Referring Specialist's Department** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's department.
* **Referring Specialist's Contact Information** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's contact information.
* **Referring Specialist's Email** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's email.
* **Referring Specialist's Phone** (optional): Enter the referring specialist's phone number.
* **Patient's Name** (required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient's Date of Birth** (required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient's Address** (required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient's ID Number** (required): Enter the patient's ID number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting.
- Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
