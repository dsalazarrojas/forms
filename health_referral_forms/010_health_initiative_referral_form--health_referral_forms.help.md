# Health Initiative Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Initiative Referral Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to refer patients to health initiatives, providing a structured way to gather and document essential patient information, medical history, and contact details. This form helps healthcare providers to ensure continuity of care and effective communication among healthcare teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section (`Patient Info`) with the patient's name, date of birth, and any relevant contact information.
2. Select the relevant health initiative(s) that the patient is referred to.
3. Provide a brief medical history for the patient, if necessary.
4. Choose the patient's status and healthcare provider from the provided options.
5. Select the desired outcome(s) for the patient.
6. If applicable, indicate the patient's health insurance provider.
7. Add any additional medical notes.
8. Set the follow-up date and time for future contact with the patient.
9. Select the preferred contact method.
10. Add any final contact note.
11. Specify the next steps for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any relevant contact information.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Health Initiatives** (`Health Initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the health initiative(s) that the patient is referred to. This could be a specific treatment plan, medication, or program.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant medical history or background information for the patient.
* **Patient Status** (`Patient Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the patient's current status, such as "Active", "Referred", or "Completed".
* **Healthcare Provider** (`Healthcare Provider`, `select_one`, required): Choose the healthcare provider who is referring the patient.
* **Patient Outcome** (`Patient Outcome`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the desired outcome(s) for the patient, such as "Improved", "Stable", or "Unknown".
* **Health Insurance** (`Health Insurance`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the patient's health insurance provider, if applicable.
* **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional medical notes for the patient.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, `date`, optional): Set a future date for follow-up contact with the patient.
* **Contact Date** (`Contact Date`, `date`, optional): Set a date for future contact with the patient.
* **Contact Time** (`Contact Time`, `time`, optional): Set a time for future contact with the patient.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the preferred method of contact, such as phone or email.
* **Follow Up Date** (`Follow Up Date`, `date`, optional): Set a date for future follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time** (`Follow Up Time`, `time`, optional): Set a time for future follow-up.
* **Contact Note** (`Contact Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional contact notes.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, `note`, optional): Specify the next steps for the patient's care.
