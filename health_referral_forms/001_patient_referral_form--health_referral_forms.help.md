# patient referral form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The patient referral form is designed to gather necessary information from the referring physician and the patient to ensure that the patient's medical needs are met and that the referring physician has provided all necessary information for the receiving physician.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- If you are not sure about any field, please ask your medical coordinator for assistance.
- Fill in the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that the form is submitted before the patient's visit or appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: This field is used to capture the patient's name.
* **Referring Physician**: This field is used to select the physician referring the patient. Please choose the physician's name from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is used to capture the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Contact Information**: This field is used to capture any contact information for the patient (phone number, address, email, etc.).
* **Referring Reason**: This field is used to capture any referring reason.
* **Patient Condition**: This field is used to capture the patient's medical condition.
* **Referring Specialty**: This field is used to select the specialty of the referring physician. Please choose the specialty from the provided options.
* **Medical Record**: This field is used to capture any medical records or information related to the patient.
* **Referral Date**: This field is used to capture the date of the referral.
* **Follow-up Date**: This field is used to capture the date to follow up with the patient.
* **Physician Notes**: This field is used to capture any notes from the referring physician.
* **Patient Insurance**: This field is used to capture the patient's insurance information.
* **Medical Facility**: This field is used to capture the medical facility where the patient will be referred to.
* **Patient Address**: This field is used to capture the patient's address.
* **Contact Phone**: This field is used to capture the contact phone number of the patient or the referring physician.
* **Contact Email**: This field is used to capture the contact email of the patient or the referring physician.
* **Patient Signature**: This field is used to capture the patient's signature.
* **Referral Signature**: This field is used to capture the referring physician's signature.
* **Patient Consent**: This field is used to select whether the patient has given consent for the referral.
* **Referring Physician Notes**: This field is used to capture any notes from the referring physician.
* **Patient Referral Comments**: This field is used to capture any comments from the patient.
* **Contact Phone 2**: This field is used to capture an additional contact phone number of the patient or the referring physician.
* **Contact Email 2**: This field is used to capture an additional contact email of the patient or the referring physician.
* **Patient Medical Condition**: This field is used to capture any additional medical condition of the patient.
* **Referral Status**: This field is used to select the status of the referral. Please choose the status from the provided options.
