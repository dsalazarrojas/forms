# Dietitian Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dietitian Referral Form is used by healthcare professionals to refer patients to a dietitian or nutritionist for dietary guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the patient's information.
2. Select the relevant specialty and referral reason for the patient's visit.
3. Specify the contact method for the dietitian.
4. Select the follow-up method for the dietitian.
5. Provide any additional medical history or medical record information as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address (optional).
* **Specialty**: Select the relevant specialty for the patient's visit:
	+ General Practice
	+ Pediatric
	+ Clinical
	+ Other
* **Referral Name**: Select the type of healthcare professional who referred the patient:
	+ Primary Care Physician
	+ Specialist
	+ Surgeon
* **Medical Condition**: Describe the patient's medical condition(s) related to their diet.
* **Referral Reason**: Provide the reason for the referral (e.g., weight management, nutrition counseling).
* **Contact Method**: Select the preferred contact method for the dietitian:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Fax
	+ In Person
* **Follow Up**: Select the follow-up frequency for the dietitian:
	+ 1 (1-2 weeks)
	+ 2 (2-4 weeks)
	+ 3 (4-6 weeks)
	+ 4 (6 weeks or more)

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the patient's consent to complete this form.
* Verify that the patient has the necessary insurance coverage for the referral.
* Keep the form concise and focused on the patient's specific needs.
* Use this form for patients with dietary concerns or conditions that require specialized guidance.
