# Physiotherapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a physiotherapy referral, including general information, patient details, referral details, and communication with physiotherapists and doctors. It is crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure that the patient receives the necessary care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Info section with any relevant information about the patient's condition or treatment.
2. Provide details about the patient in the Patient Details section.
3. Specify the referral details in the Referral Details section.
4. Enter the diagnosis in the Diagnosis section.
5. Select the relevant treatment plans for the patient in the Treatment Plans section.
6. Enter the reason for the referral in the Referral Reason section.
7. Provide the physiotherapist's name, email, and phone number in the Physiotherapist section.
8. Enter the patient's name, phone, and doctor's name, email, and phone in the Patient Details section.
9. Enter the medical record number in the Medical Record Number section.
10. Select the referral status in the Referral Status section.
11. Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's condition or treatment.
* **Patient Details** (`Patient Details`, text, optional): Provide details about the patient, such as their personal history, medical background, or any allergies or concerns.
* **Referral Details** (`Referral Details`, text, optional): Specify the details about the referral, including any relevant medical history or background information.
* **Diagnosis** (`Diagnosis`, text, optional): Enter the diagnosis of the patient's condition.
* **Treatment Plans** (`Treatment Plans`, select one or multiple, optional): Select the treatment plans that are relevant to the patient's condition, such as Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, or Speech Therapy.
* **Referral Reason** (`Referral Reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Physiotherapist** (`Physiotherapist`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the physiotherapist who will be treating the patient.
* **Physiotherapist Email** (`Physiotherapist Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the physiotherapist.
* **Physiotherapist Phone** (`Physiotherapist Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the physiotherapist.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Phone** (`Patient Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the patient.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who referred the patient.
* **Doctor Email** (`Doctor Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`Doctor Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the doctor.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, text, optional): Enter the medical record number of the patient.
* **Referral Status** (`Referral Status`, select one, optional): Select the status of the referral (Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral.
