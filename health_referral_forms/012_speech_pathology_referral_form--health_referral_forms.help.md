# Speech Pathology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare professionals or administrators to refer patients to a speech pathologist for evaluation or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the fields carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
3. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account the patient's condition and your professional judgement.
4. Review the form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter any additional information about the patient that is relevant to their referral.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): This field is where you enter the patient's full name.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, not required): This field is where you enter the patient's date of birth.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, not required): This field is where you provide the reason for referral.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, not required): This field allows you to select the source of the referral.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_multiple, not required): This field allows you to select the patient's gender.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, not required): This field is where you enter the patient's age.
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, not required): This field allows you to select the patient's status.
### Page 2: Referral Information
* **Speech Pathologist Information** (`speech_pathologist_info`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter any information regarding the speech pathologist or the referral.
### Page 2: Referral Information
* **Speech Referral Information** (`speech_referral_info`, select_multiple, not required): This field allows you to select whether the referral is for a speech pathologist or not.
### Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant department or healthcare professional.
