# Home Care Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture key information from healthcare providers when referring patients to home care services. It helps in creating a comprehensive and accurate record of the patient's referral details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Client Name, which is the name of the individual who is being referred to home care services.
2. Select the Referring Agency from the available options.
3. Provide the Referral Reason, which is the specific reason for referring the client to home care services.
4. (Optional) Enter the Referral Date, which is the date when the referral was made.
5. Enter the Contact Number of the client or their family member for communication purposes.
6. (Optional) Enter the client's Address for logistical purposes.
7. Provide the client's Medical History, which is any relevant health information that the healthcare provider wants to share.
8. Enter the client's Social Security Number for identification purposes.
9. Indicate if the client has an Emergency Contact who can be reached if needed.
10. Sign the Signature Date, which is the date when the form is signed and completed.
11. Sign the form by adding a handwritten or digital signature, which indicates acceptance and agreement of the referral details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the individual being referred to home care services.
* **Referring Agency**: Select the agency or organization that is referring the client to home care services.
* **Referral Reason**: Provide a clear and concise reason for referring the client to home care services.
* **Referral Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when the referral was made. This field is not required.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the client or their family member for efficient communication.
* **Address**: (Optional) Enter the client's address for logistical purposes.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant health information related to the client's condition or treatment.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the client's Social Security Number for identification purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Indicate if the client has an emergency contact who can be reached if needed.
* **Signature Date**: Sign the form with the date when it is completed and signed.
* **Signature**: Sign the form by adding a handwritten or digital signature, which indicates acceptance and agreement of the referral details.
