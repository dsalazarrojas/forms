# Residential Lease Surety Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting guarantor details from a residential property lease.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the guarantor's first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter the guarantor's last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide the guarantor's address in the "Address Line 1" and "Address Line 2" fields.
5. Enter the guarantor's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the guarantor's email in the "Email" field.
7. If applicable, select the guarantor's relationship to the tenant from the "Relationship" dropdown.
8. Enter the date the guarantor has been a guarantor in the "Since" field.
9. Enter any additional details about the guarantor in the "Signatory details" field.
10. Sign and date the form in the "Guarantor Signature" and "Landlord Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guarantor details**: Enter the guarantor's name and other details as required.
* **First Name**: Enter the guarantor's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the guarantor's last name.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the guarantor's street address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter the guarantor's apartment number or additional address details.
* **Phone**: Enter the guarantor's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the guarantor's email address.
* **Relationship**: Select the guarantor's relationship to the tenant (e.g., Father, Mother, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Since**: Enter the date the guarantor has been a guarantor.
* **Signatory details**: Enter any additional details about the guarantor.
* **Landlord name**: Enter the landlord's name.
* **Landlord contact**: Enter the landlord's contact information.
* **Guarantor Signature**: Sign and date the guarantor's signature.
* **Landlord Signature**: Sign and date the landlord's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the date the guarantor signed the form.
