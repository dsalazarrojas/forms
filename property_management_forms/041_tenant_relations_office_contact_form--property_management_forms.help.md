# Tenant Relations Office Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Relations Office Contact Form is a tool used to collect information from tenants about their experiences and issues with their rental property. This form is designed to help the Tenant Relations Office track and address tenant concerns and complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages to understand the questions and fields that are relevant to your experience.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the relevant questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit**: (contact_form_submit, select_one, required) This is the submit button that allows you to submit your completed form.
* **Resident Name**: (resident_name, text, required) Please enter your name as a resident of the property.
* **Resident Phone Number**: (resident_phone_number, text, required) Enter your phone number.
* **Resident Email Address**: (resident_email_address, email, required) Enter your email address.
* **Issue Description**: (issue_description, text, required) Describe the issue or concern you are experiencing with your rental property.
* **Issue Category**: (issue_category, select_one, required) Select the category of your issue (e.g. Maintenance, Leasing, etc.).
* **Submit**: (contact_form_first_page_submit, select_one, required) This is the submit button for the first page of the form.
* **Tenant ID**: (tenant_id, number, optional) If you have a tenant ID, enter it here. If not, skip this field.
* **Property ID**: (property_id, number, optional) If you have a property ID, enter it here. If not, skip this field.
