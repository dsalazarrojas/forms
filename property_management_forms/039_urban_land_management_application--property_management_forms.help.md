# urban_land_management_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The urban land management application form is designed to capture essential information required for the urban land management process. It helps applicants provide comprehensive details about their proposals, including general information, land use, and proposal location. The form's purpose is to streamline the application process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your proposal's title, application date, and application time.
2. Select the land owner from the provided options.
3. Choose the land use and specify the land use details.
4. Upload relevant files related to your proposal.
5. Add review comments for the administrators' review.
6. Select the proposal status as per your proposal's current state.
7. Fill out the contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
8. Provide a detailed proposal description.
9. Add any additional comments or review comments if necessary.
10. Upload any additional files required for the proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (text, required/optional): Enter the title of your proposal, application date, and application time.
* **Land Owner** (select_one, required/optional): Select the land owner from the provided options.
* **Land Use** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the land use for your proposal (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Land Use Details** (text, required/optional): Provide additional details about the land use.
* **Uploaded Files** (text, required/optional): Upload files related to your proposal.
* **Review Comments** (text, required/optional): Add comments for the administrators' review.
* **Proposal Status** (select_one, required/optional): Select the proposal status as per your proposal's current state.
* **Application Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of your application.
* **Application Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of your application.
* **File Upload** (text, required/optional): Upload files for your proposal.
* **Application Files** (text, required/optional): Add additional files for your proposal.
* **Municipality** (select_one, required/optional): Select the municipality where your proposal is located.
* **Application Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of application (e.g., urban, recreational, residential).
* **Land Use Category** (select_one, required/optional): Select the land use category (e.g., commercial, residential, industrial).
* **Land Area** (number, required/optional): Enter the land area.
* **Proposal Location** (text, required/optional): Provide the location of your proposal.
* **Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address** (text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Proposal Description** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
* **Application Comments** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Review Comments 2** (text, required/optional): Add review comments for the proposal.
* **File Upload 2** (text, required/optional): Upload additional files for your proposal.
* **Review Comments 3** (text, required/optional): Add final review comments.
Note: Please review each field carefully and fill them out accurately to ensure a smooth application process. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
