# Business Premises Photo Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Premises Photo Upload form is designed to collect information and photos for business premises inspections. This data will be used to document and evaluate the condition, maintenance needs, and safety concerns of the premises.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Property Name or Address**, which should be the full address of the business premises.
2. Provide the **Inspector or Submitter Name**, which is the name of the person conducting the inspection.
3. Select the **Inspector Role**, which indicates the role of the inspector (e.g., Property Manager, Insurance Adjuster, Business Owner, etc.).
4. Choose the **Purpose of Inspection**, which explains why the inspection is being conducted (e.g., Routine Review, Insurance Documentation, Damage Assessment, etc.).
5. Upload **Building Exterior Photos**, which are images of the outside of the building.
6. Upload **Main Entrance Area Photos**, which are images of the main entrance area.
7. Upload **Interior General Area Photos**, which are images of main interior spaces.
8. Optionally, upload **Office Area Photos**, **Storage Area Photos**, and **Equipment or Fixture Photos**, if relevant to the inspection.
9. Note any **Damage or Issues Identified**, including photos of any damage found.
10. Rate the **Overall Premises Condition**, using one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
11. Describe any **Maintenance or Repairs Needed**, if applicable.
12. List any **Safety Concerns** found during the inspection.
13. Add any **Compliance or Regulation Notes**, if relevant.
14. Enter any **Inspector Comments** or additional observations.
15. Indicate if a **Follow-up is Required** (True, False, or Pending Review).
16. Enter the **Recommended Follow-up Date**, if applicable.
17. Record the **Submission Date and Time**, which is when the inspection was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name or Address (1)**: Enter the full address of the business premises being inspected.
* **Property ID or Reference Number (2)**: Provide a unique identifier for this property.
* **Inspection Date (3)**: Enter the date when the photos were taken.
* **Inspector or Submitter Name (4)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Role (5)**: Select the role of the inspector (e.g., Property Manager, Insurance Adjuster, Business Owner, etc.).
* **Purpose of Inspection (6)**: Choose why the inspection is being conducted (e.g., Routine Review, Insurance Documentation, Damage Assessment, etc.).
* **Building Exterior Photos (7)**: Upload images of the outside of the building.
* **Main Entrance Area Photos (8)**: Upload images of the main entrance area.
* **Interior General Area Photos (9)**: Upload images of main interior spaces.
* **Office Area Photos (10)**: Optionally upload images of office spaces.
* **Storage Area Photos (11)**: Optionally upload images of storage spaces.
* **Equipment or Fixture Photos (12)**: Optionally upload images of key equipment.
* **Damage or Issues Identified (13)**: Describe any damage or issues noted during the inspection.
* **Damage Area Photos (14)**: Upload images of any damage found.
* **Overall Premises Condition (15)**: Rate the general condition of the premises using one of the provided options.
* **Maintenance or Repairs Needed (16)**: Describe any maintenance required.
* **Safety Concerns (17)**: List any safety concerns found during the inspection.
* **Compliance or Regulation Notes (18)**: Note any compliance issues identified.
* **Inspector Comments (19)**: Add any additional comments or observations.
* **Follow-up Required (20)**: Indicate if a follow-up inspection is needed (True, False, or Pending Review).
* **Recommended Follow-up Date (21)**: Enter the recommended follow-up date, if applicable.
* **Submission Date and Time (22)**: Record when the inspection was submitted.
