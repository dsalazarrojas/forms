# Residence Hall Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition of a residence hall's various areas. It is used to identify any issues or concerns that need to be addressed to ensure the hall is safe and well-maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the room number and building name of the residence hall being inspected.
2. Select the date of the inspection.
3. Select the status of the room (occupied, vacant, or unknown).
4. For each of the listed areas, select the status (good, warning, or critical) and provide a brief description of any issues or concerns.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* Use the status options (good, warning, or critical) consistently throughout the form.
