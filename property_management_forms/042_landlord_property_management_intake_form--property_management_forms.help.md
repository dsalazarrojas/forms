# landlord_property_management_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Property Management Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about properties under management. This form helps landlords to efficiently manage their properties by providing a centralized platform for tracking key details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Property Information" and enter a brief description of the property.
2. Provide tenant contact information, such as name and contact details (phone number and email).
3. Enter rent details, including the amount and payment frequency.
4. Choose the current status of the property (available, rented, or sold).
5. Select the available features of the property, such as parking or a pool.
6. Upload a property image to help with identification.
7. Select any relevant property documents, such as a lease agreement or property insurance.
8. Select any tenant documents, such as a rental agreement or proof of income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Information (Property Information, text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Tenant Contact (Tenant Contact, text, optional)**: Provide tenant contact information, including name and contact details.
* **Rent Details (Rent Details, number, optional)**: Enter the rent amount and payment frequency (e.g., weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
* **Property Status (Property Status, select_one, optional)**: Choose the current status of the property (available, rented, or sold).
* **Property Features (Property Features, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the available features of the property, such as parking or a pool.
* **Property Image (Property Image, text, optional)**: Upload a property image for better identification.
* **Property Documents (Property Documents, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any relevant property documents, such as a lease agreement or property insurance.
* **Tenant Documents (Tenant Documents, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any relevant tenant documents, such as a rental agreement or proof of income.
