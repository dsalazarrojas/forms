# Historic Property Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a historic property and its applicant. This information will be used to facilitate the rental process and ensure that the property is properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Property Address** field with the exact address of the historic property you are applying to rent.
2. Enter the **Owner Name** of the historic property.
3. Enter your **Applicant Name** and **Applicant Email** to be contacted and informed about the rental process.
4. Select the type of **Property Type** from the available options (Landmark, Heritage, or Other).
5. Indicate the current **Property Status** (Active, Sold, or Leased).
6. Choose the type of **Rent Type** from the available options (Lease, License, or Other).
7. Enter any additional **Applicant Notes** regarding the property or your application.
8. Fill out the **Property Location** field with the exact location of the property.
9. Upload an **Property Image** of the property (if available).
10. Enter your **Applicant Phone Number** for contact purposes.
11. Enter your **Applicant Date of Birth** and **Applicant Time of Day** (if applicable).
12. Write a detailed description of the **Property Description**.
13. Sign with your **Applicant Signature** to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the historic property you are applying to rent.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your name as the applicant.
* **Applicant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as the applicant.
* **Applicant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are applying to rent (Landmark, Heritage, or Other).
* **Property Status**: Indicate the current status of the property (Active, Sold, or Leased).
* **Rent Type**: Choose the type of rent you are applying for (Lease, License, or Other).
* **Applicant Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your application.
* **Property Location**: Enter the exact location of the property.
* **Property Image**: Upload an image of the property (if available).
* **Applicant Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Applicant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Applicant Time of Day**: Enter your time of day (if applicable).
* **Property Description**: Write a detailed description of the property.
* **Applicant Signature**: Sign with your signature to confirm your application.
