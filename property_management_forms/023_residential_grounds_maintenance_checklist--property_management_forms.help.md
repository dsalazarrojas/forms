# Residential Grounds Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Residential Grounds Maintenance Checklist" is a form used to track and document the maintenance activities performed on residential properties. It helps grounds maintenance staff or property managers to ensure that all necessary maintenance tasks are completed and documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter general information about the property in the "General Information" section.
3. Provide details about the property in the "Property Details" section.
4. Check off the maintenance activities that were performed in the "Lawn Maintenance", "Tree Trimming", "Water Feature Installation", "Water Feature Maintenance", "Irrigation System", "Fencing", and "Other" sections.
5. If applicable, indicate if the "Irrigation System" is installed or not, and if the "Water Feature Installation" includes a pool, sculpture fountain, waterfall, or repair.
6. Enter "Yes" or "No" for the "Tree Trimming" and "Irrigation System" questions.
7. Enter the date and time of the maintenance activity in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the property, such as the address, contact person, or any specific concerns or notes.

### Property Details
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Enter details about the property, such as its location, size, or any specific characteristics.

### Lawn Maintenance
* **Lawn Maintenance** (`lawn_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional): Check off the lawn maintenance activities that were performed, such as lawn mowing, weed control, irrigation system check, fertilization, pest control, tree trimming, and sod repair.

### Tree Trimming
* **Tree Trimming** (`tree_trimming`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if tree trimming was performed, or "No" otherwise.

### Water Feature Installation
* **Water Feature Installation** (`water_feature_installation`, select_multiple, optional): Check off the water feature installation activities that were performed, such as pool, sculpture fountain, waterfall, or repair.

### Water Feature Maintenance
* **Water Feature Maintenance** (`water_feature_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional): Check off the water feature maintenance activities that were performed, such as pool maintenance, sculpture fountain maintenance, waterfall maintenance.

### Irrigation System
* **Irrigation System** (`irrigation_system`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if the irrigation system is installed, or "No" otherwise.

### Fencing
* **Fencing** (`fencing`, select_multiple, optional): Check off the fencing maintenance activities that were performed, such as fencing repair or replacement, gate repair or replacement.

### Other
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Enter any additional maintenance activities that were not listed above.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the maintenance activity in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the maintenance activity in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
