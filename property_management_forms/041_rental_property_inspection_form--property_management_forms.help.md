# rental_property_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on a rental property's condition and other essential details for property management decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the property.
3. Use the select options to choose the correct status and type of the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rental_property_inspection_form_page** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in for reference purposes.
* **unit_address** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the unit to be inspected.
* **unit_status** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of the unit (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **property_status** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of the property (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **unit_type** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of property (Studio, One-bedroom, Two-bedroom, or Three-bedroom).
* **unit_notes** (`6`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the unit.
* **property_notes** (`7`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the property.
* **rent_amount** (`8`, `number`, optional): Enter the rent amount for the property.
* **lease_start_date** (`9`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **lease_end_date** (`10`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **property_owner** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **property_manager** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **inspection_date** (`13`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **inspecting_party** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who conducted the inspection (Property Manager, Landlord, or Tenant).
* **inspection_result** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the outcome of the inspection (Passed, Failed, or Deferred).
