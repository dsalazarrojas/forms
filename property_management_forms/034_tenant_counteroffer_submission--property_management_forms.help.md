# Tenant Counteroffer Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Counteroffer Submission form is used to submit a counteroffer to a landlord in response to a previously submitted offer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your counteroffer submission title.
2. Enter the landlord's response to your counteroffer.
3. Enter the date and time of your counteroffer.
4. Enter the counteroffer amount.
5. Choose the status of your counteroffer (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
6. Enter any notes or comments about your counteroffer.
7. Enter your phone number and email address.
8. Enter the landlord's response date and any notes or comments.
9. Choose the status of the landlord's response (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
10. Choose who updated the form (User, System).
11. Enter the date and time the form was updated.
12. Choose who updated the form (User, System).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Counteroffer Submission** (`tenant_counteroffer_submission`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your counteroffer.
* **Landlord Response** (`landlord_response`, Text, Optional): Enter the landlord's response to your counteroffer.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Date** (`tenant_counteroffer_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your counteroffer.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Time** (`tenant_counteroffer_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your counteroffer.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Amount** (`tenant_counteroffer_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount of your counteroffer.
* **Landlord Counteroffer Amount** (`landlord_counteroffer_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount of the landlord's counteroffer.
* **Accepted Terms** (`accepted_terms`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your counteroffer (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
* **Counteroffer Note** (`counteroffer_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your counteroffer.
* **Landlord Response Date** (`landlord_response_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the landlord's response.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Phone** (`tenant_counteroffer_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Email** (`tenant_counteroffer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the landlord's email address.
* **Counteroffer Status** (`counteroffer_status`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the counteroffer (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
* **Landlord Response Note** (`landlord_response_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the landlord's response.
* **Tenant Counteroffer Status Updated** (`tenant_counteroffer_status_updated`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your counteroffer (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
* **Landlord Counteroffer Status Updated** (`landlord_counteroffer_status_updated`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the landlord's counteroffer (Accepted, Declined, In Progress, Not Applicable).
* **Tenant Counteroffer Updated By** (`tenant_counteroffer_updated_by`, Select One, Optional): Choose who updated your counteroffer (User, System).
* **Landlord Counteroffer Updated By** (`landlord_counteroffer_updated_by`, Select One, Optional): Choose who updated the landlord's counteroffer (User, System).
* **Counteroffer Date Updated** (`counteroffer_date_updated`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the counteroffer was updated.
* **Counteroffer Time Updated** (`counteroffer_time_updated`, Time, Optional): Enter the time the counteroffer was updated.
* **Counteroffer Updated By** (`counteroffer_updated_by`, Select One, Optional): Choose who updated the counteroffer (User, System).
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, Select One, Optional): Choose who submitted the form (User, System).
* **Landlord Form Submitted By** (`landlord_form_submitted_by`, Select One, Optional): Choose who submitted the landlord's form (User, System).
* **Form Submitted Date** (`form_submitted_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
