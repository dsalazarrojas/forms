# living_room_property_management_property_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The living_room_property_management_property_information_form is designed to collect property management details for living rooms, covering essential information such as address, property type, value, and rental status.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all necessary details ready, as indicated by the required fields.
- Review each page to understand the specific fields and questions posed.
- Fill out each field according to the field-by-field instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Page**
* **Consent (id: 4)**: Please indicate your agreement or disagreement with the form's usage.
* **Address (id: 5)**: Enter the complete address of the property.
* **Property Type (id: 6)**: Choose the type of property you're referring to.
* **Rental Status (id: 8)**: Specify the current rental status of the property.

### **Second Page**
* **Property Value (id: 7)**: Enter the current value of the property.
* **Property Details (id: 9)**: Provide any additional details about the property.
* **Owner Details (id: 10)**: Enter any relevant information about the property's owner.
* **Contact Info (id: 11)**: Share contact information for further communication.
* **Additional Info (id: 12)**: Add any other relevant property-related information.

### **Third Page**
* **None, as this page is empty**

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- For fields like property value and address, provide the most up-to-date information.
- For property type and rental status, choose the correct and most accurate option.
- Use the consent field for form agreement and acknowledge its usage.
- If unsure about a field, consult the property owner or relevant resources.
- If you're still unsure about form usage or content, contact support for assistance.
