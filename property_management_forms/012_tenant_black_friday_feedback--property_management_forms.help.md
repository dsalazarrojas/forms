# Tenant Black Friday Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from tenants about their Black Friday experience, providing valuable insights into the performance and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required and optional fields as needed. You can type in your thoughts on each field, and submit your feedback at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Black Friday Feedback** (`black_friday_feedback`): Describe your overall experience on Black Friday, what you liked and disliked about it.
* **Performance** (`black_friday_performance`): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`): Provide a numerical score out of 10 to describe your experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Lessons Learned** (`black_friday_lessons_learned`): Describe any valuable lessons or insights you gained from your experience.
* **Improvements** (`black_friday_improvements`): Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made to enhance the Black Friday experience.
* **Recommendations** (`black_friday_recommendations`): Share any recommendations or suggestions for future events.
* **Suggestions** (`black_friday_suggestions`): Offer any additional suggestions or ideas.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback to provide valuable insights.
* Use a numerical score for Performance Score to give a clear understanding of your experience.
* You can type in multiple lines of text for each field as needed.
* Remember to submit your feedback to help us improve the Black Friday experience for future tenants.
