# Property Management Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Agreement Survey is designed to collect information from tenants about their experiences with the property management team. This form helps the team to improve their services and better serve the tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Provide your Email (optional)
4. Enter your Phone Number (required)
5. Enter the Date of Visit (required)
6. Enter the Time of Visit (optional)
7. Rate your experience with the property management team (optional)
8. Provide comments about your experience (optional)
9. Choose the type of property you are visiting (required)
10. Select any property issues you experienced during your visit (optional)
11. Choose the communication channels used during your visit (optional)
12. Rate your staff response during your visit (optional)
13. Select the property management option that best describes your experience (required)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
- **Email**: If you would like to be contacted by the property management team, provide your email address. This is optional.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow the property management team to contact you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
- **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit to the property.
- **Time of Visit**: If you can, enter the time of your visit for more accurate tracking.
- **Rating**: Rate your experience with the property management team, using the numbers 1-5.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the property management team.
- **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are visiting (e.g., house, apartment, condominium, etc.).
- **Property Status**: Select all property statuses that apply to your visit (e.g., clean, well-maintained, etc.).
- **Tenant Feedback**: Select how you felt about the communication during your visit.
- **Property Issues**: Select any issues you experienced during your visit.
- **Communication Channels**: Select all communication channels used during your visit (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
- **Staff Response**: Rate your staff's response during your visit.
- **Property Managements**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the property management team.
