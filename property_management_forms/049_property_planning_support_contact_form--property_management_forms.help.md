# Property Planning Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Planning Support Contact Form is designed to collect information from users who are seeking support or inquiry related to their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant property details from the "Property Details" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of property you are inquiring about from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the size of your property from the "Property Size" field.
7. Indicate the type of support you need by selecting from the "Support Needed" dropdown menu.
8. Enter any additional comments or questions you have in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `select_one`, required: false): This field provides a list of options to choose from for property-specific details. Please select the most accurate option that reflects your property's characteristics.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you should enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is the field where you should enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This is the field where you should enter your phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field provides a list of options to choose from for the type of property you are inquiring about. Please select the most accurate option from the list.
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to select the size of your property.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to choose multiple types of support you need. Select all that apply.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): This is a field where you can enter any additional comments or questions you have.
