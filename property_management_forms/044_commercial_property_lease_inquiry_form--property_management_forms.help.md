# Commercial Property Lease Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective tenants about their commercial property lease requirements. It will help our team to provide you with the most suitable options for your business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.
3. Use the available options (e.g., select_one) to make your choice.
4. If you need help, don't hesitate to ask our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Full Name** (Prospect Name): This is the name of the person or representative of the business inquiring about a lease.
* **Business Name**: This is the name of the business that will occupy the property.
* **Business Website (Optional)**: If your business has a website, please provide the URL here. This information will help us to get a better understanding of your business.
* **Preferred Property Type**: Please select one of the options provided (Retail, Office Space, Industrial / Warehouse, Medical / Professional, Restaurant / Food Service) that best describes the type of property your business needs.
* **Minimum Square Footage Needed**: Please enter the minimum square footage required for your business needs.
* **Preferred Location or Neighborhood**: Please provide the specific area or neighborhood where you would like to locate your business.
* **Monthly Lease Budget Range**: Please select one of the options provided ($1,000 - $2,500, $2,501 - $5,000, $5,001 - $10,000, $10,000+) that best describes your lease budget range.
* **Desired Lease Term**: Please select one of the options provided (1 - 2 Years, 3 - 5 Years, 5+ Years) that best describes the length of the lease term you are interested in.
* **Target Move-in Date**: Please select a date for the target move-in date for your business.
* **Contact Email**: This is the email address of the person or representative of the business inquiring about a lease.
* **Contact Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the person or representative of the business inquiring about a lease.
* **Please describe your specific needs or questions**: If you have any specific requirements or questions about the lease, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask our team.
