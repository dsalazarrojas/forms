# Roommate Agreement Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Roommate Agreement Form Template is to gather and document essential information for a harmonious living experience among roommates in a shared household. This template ensures that all roommates are aware of their responsibilities, expectations, and rules governing the shared space and utilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Roommates Information page (Page 1) with the names, contact information, and any other relevant details about each roommate.
2. On the Household Responsibilities page (Page 2), select the days of the week for each roommate's responsibility (Name, Address, or Move-Out).
3. Choose a cleaning schedule on the Cleaning Schedule page (Page 3) to determine how often the household chores will be split among roommates.
4. On the Utilities page (Page 4), select a number to determine how you will split the utilities (50/50, 60/40, 70/30).
5. On the Rent And Payments page (Page 5), select whether the rent will be paid Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually.
6. For Guest Policies (Page 6), choose whether guests can stay for 1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks.
7. Set a move-in date on the Move In And Move Out page (Page 7).
8. On the Room Rent page (Page 8), specify a room rent amount.
9. On the Utilities Split page (Page 9), choose how to split the utilities among roommates.
10. Finally, select whether household chores will be Shared Chores or Individual Chores on the Household Chores page (Page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Roommates Information** (`Roommates Information`, `note`, required: false):
  * This is the first page where you provide the names, contact information, and any other details about each roommate. Enter the information as requested.
* **Household Responsibilities** (`Household Responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  * Select the days of the week for each roommate's responsibility (Name, Address, or Move-Out).
* **Cleaning Schedule** (`Cleaning Schedule`, `time`, required: false):
  * Choose how often the household chores will be split among roommates. This will help determine the cleaning schedule.
* **Utilities** (`Utilities`, `number`, required: false):
  * Select a number to determine how you will split the utilities (50/50, 60/40, 70/30).
* **Rent And Payments** (`Rent And Payments`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Choose how often the rent will be paid (Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually).
* **Guest Policies** (`Guest Policies`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  * Determine the maximum number of weeks guests can stay in the household (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Move In And Move Out** (`Move In And Move Out`, `date`, required: false):
  * Enter the date when a roommate moves in or out.
* **Room Rent** (`Room Rent`, `number`, required: false):
  * Specify the room rent amount.
* **Utilities Split** (`Utilities Split`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  * Select how to split the utilities among roommates (50/50, 60/40, 70/30).
* **Household Chores** (`Household Chores`, `select_one`, required: false):
  * Choose whether household chores will be Shared Chores or Individual Chores.

Note: The fields with "required: false" can be filled or left blank, as they are not mandatory. Ensure you review the form carefully to understand the implications of leaving these fields blank.
