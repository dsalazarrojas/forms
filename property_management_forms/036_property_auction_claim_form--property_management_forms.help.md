# property-auction-claim-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim property auctions. The purpose of this form is to gather information about a property auction, including its type, bid date, and status. This form is used by property auctions managers to verify and manage property auction claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Email address in the "User Info" section.
2. Select the type of property being auctioned in the "Property Type" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the bid in the "Bid Date" and "Bid Time" fields, respectively.
4. Select the current status of the auction in the "Claim Status" field.
5. Indicate whether or not you are a representative for the property in the "Representative" field.
6. Select the relevant teams involved with the property in the "Property Team" field.
7. Indicate whether or not you are the submitter of the claim in the "Submitter" field.
8. Enter the date and time you submitted the claim in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields, respectively.
9. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the property in the "Notes" field.
10. Verify your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Verify your email address in the "Email Verification" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
13. Indicate whether or not you are a representative for the submitter in the "Submitter Representative" field.
14. Enter the phone number of the submitter in the "Submitter Phone" field.
15. Select the relevant teams involved with the submitter in the "Submitter Property Team" field.
16. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the submitter in the "Submitter Notes" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about the property in the "Property Auctions Manager Notes" field.
18. Enter the phone number of the property auctions manager in the "Property Auctions Manager Phone" field.
19. Enter the email address of the property auctions manager in the "Property Auctions Manager Email" field.
20. Indicate whether or not the property auctions manager is a representative in the "Property Auctions Manager Representative" field.
21. Select the relevant teams involved with the property auctions manager in the "Property Auctions Manager Property Team" field.
22. Select the status of the property auctions manager verification in the "Property Auctions Manager Verification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (user_info)**: Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to verify your identity.
* **Property Type (property_type)**: Select the type of property being auctioned.
* **Bid Date (bid_date)**: Enter the date of the bid in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Bid Time (bid_time)**: Enter the time of the bid in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Claim Status (claim_status)**: Select the current status of the auction.
* **Representative (representative)**: Indicate whether or not you are a representative for the property.
* **Property Team (property_team)**: Select the teams involved with the property.
* **Submitter (submitter)**: Indicate whether or not you are the submitter of the claim.
* **Submit Date (submit_date)**: Enter the date you submitted the claim in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Submit Time (submit_time)**: Enter the time you submitted the claim in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Notes (notes)**: Optionally, enter any additional notes about the property.
* **Phone (phone)**: Verify your phone number.
* **Email Verification (email_verification)**: Verify your email address.
* **Submitter Email (submitter_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Representative (submitter_representative)**: Indicate whether or not you are a representative for the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (submitter_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter Property Team (submitter_property_team)**: Select the teams involved with the submitter.
* **Submitter Notes (submitter_notes)**: Optionally, enter any additional notes about the submitter.
* **Property Auctions Manager Notes (property_auctions_manager_notes)**: Enter any additional notes about the property.
* **Property Auctions Manager Phone (property_auctions_manager_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the property auctions manager.
* **Property Auctions Manager Email (property_auctions_manager_email)**: Enter the email address of the property auctions manager.
* **Property Auctions Manager Representative (property_auctions_manger_representative)**: Indicate whether or not the property auctions manager is a representative.
* **Property Auctions Manager Property Team (property_auctions_manager_property_team)**: Select the teams involved with the property auctions manager.
* **Property Auctions Manager Verification (property_auctions_manager_verification)**: Select the status of the property auctions manager verification.
