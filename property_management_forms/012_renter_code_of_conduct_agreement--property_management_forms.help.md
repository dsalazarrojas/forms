# Renter Code Of Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a guide for renters to understand their code of conduct while renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your tenant details, including name, contact number, and email.
* Enter the property details, including address, and any additional information.
* Accept or decline the rental rules by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Renter Code Of Conduct field.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance of the rental rules.
* Enter any additional property details in the text fields.
