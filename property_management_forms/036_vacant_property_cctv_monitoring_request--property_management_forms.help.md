# Vacant Property CCTV Monitoring Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacant Property CCTV Monitoring Request form is a tool used by real estate property management teams to track and manage requests for CCTV monitoring on vacant properties. This form helps to ensure that these requests are properly documented and followed up on in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Property Details section with the relevant information about the vacant property.
2. Provide the Property Location in the designated field.
3. Enter the Property Contact details, which should include the name and contact information of the property's representative or owner.
4. In the Monitoring Request Details field, describe the specific CCTV monitoring request, including any relevant details such as monitoring frequency or duration.
5. In the Authorization Details field, provide any necessary information about who has authorized the monitoring request.
6. Choose a Monitoring Scheduling date and time from the calendar view.
7. Identify the Security Team Contact person who will be handling the request.
8. Choose the Follow Up Status to indicate the current status of the request.
9. Select the Submitted By field to indicate who submitted the request.
10. Enter the Submitted On date and time of when the request was submitted.
11. If applicable, enter the Submitted By Email of the person who submitted the request.
12. Add any necessary Follow Up Comments or Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the property's address, unit number, and any other relevant details to identify the property.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the specific location of the property, such as the city, state, or zip code.
* **Property Contact** (`property_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the property's representative or owner.
* **Monitoring Request Details** (`monitoring_request_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific CCTV monitoring request, including any relevant details such as monitoring frequency or duration.
* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any necessary information about who has authorized the monitoring request.
* **Monitoring Scheduling** (`monitoring_scheduling`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date and time to schedule the CCTV monitoring.
* **Security Team Contact** (`security_team_contact`, `text`, required: false): Identify the Security Team Contact person who will be handling the request.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose a status to indicate the current status of the request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of when the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the person who submitted the request.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to the follow-up status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the request.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the request.
