# Property Management Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Intake Form is designed to collect information about properties under your care, including contact information and lease details. This information will be used by property management staff to update their records and improve communication with you and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. If you are a property owner, please provide your contact information and lease details.
3. If you are a property manager, please provide your contact information and any relevant notes or comments.
4. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Management Form** (1): Enter the title of the property management form.
* **Property Owner Info** (2): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Property Details** (3): Describe the property in detail, including any relevant features or notes.
* **Tenant Contact Info** (4): Enter the contact information for the tenant or occupants.
* **Tenant Resident Info** (5): Enter the number of residents or occupants in the property.
* **Lease Start Date** (6): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (7): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Tenant Agreement Form** (8): Check if you have a written agreement with the tenant.
* **Additional Notes** (9): Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Contact Email** (10): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (11): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Manager Info** (12): Enter your name and title.
* **Property Manager Name** (13): Enter your name.
* **Property Manager Email** (14): Enter your email address.
* **Property Manager Phone** (15): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Manager Address** (16): Enter your office or mailing address.
* **Property Manager Notes** (17): Enter any additional notes or comments from the property manager.
* **Property Owner Info** (18): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Property Owner Name** (19): Enter your name.
* **Property Owner Email** (20): Enter your email address.
* **Property Owner Phone** (21): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Owner Address** (22): Enter your property address.
* **Property Owner Notes** (23): Enter any additional notes or comments from the property owner.
* **Property Notes** (24): Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Submit Button** (25): Click the submit button to complete the form and send the information to property management staff.

Note: If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact property management staff for assistance.
