### property_optimization_survey - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to collect data from property owners or managers to help identify areas for property optimization.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey. You can enter a brief description of the property or a title that you prefer.
2. **Property Location** (`property_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property, including the city, state, and zip code.
3. **Property Type** (`property_type`, select, optional): Select the type of property you own or manage. This will help us understand the specific needs of your property.
	+ Options: Residential, Commercial
4. **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): This is the area to provide any other questions or comments you may have.
5. **Question 1** (`question_1`, select multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your property.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
6. **Question 2** (`question_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last property inspection or maintenance.
7. **Question 3** (`question_3`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last property inspection or maintenance.
8. **Question 4** (`question_4`, number, optional): Enter the number of square feet or acres of the property.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): This field allows you to select the type of property you own or manage. It will help us understand the specific needs of your property.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`): This field should contain the location of the property, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`): This field allows you to select one or more options that apply to your property. It is used to gather more information about your property needs.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`): This field is used to enter the date of the last property inspection or maintenance. This information will help us understand the frequency of inspections and maintenance activities.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`): This field is used to enter the time of the last property inspection or maintenance. This information will help us understand the timing of inspections and maintenance activities.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`): This field is used to enter the size of the property in square feet or acres. This information will help us estimate the scope of work required for maintenance and improvement.

### Tips
Please note that all fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that do not apply to your property. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
