# Mailbox Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their mailbox rental agreement, which includes details about the customer, mailbox specifics, and rental terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Information field with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the Mailbox Details you wish to rent, such as Mailbox Number, Mailbox Size, and Mailbox Type.
3. If you have a rental agreement, select the date it was signed.
4. Sign the form to acknowledge your agreement.
5. Review the options for submitting the form and select "Submit" to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Mailbox Details (mailbox_details)**: Choose the specific details about the mailbox you wish to rent, such as the Mailbox Number, Mailbox Size, or Mailbox Type.
* **Rental Agreement (rental_agreement)**: If you have a rental agreement, enter the date it was signed. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Signature (signature)**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agree to the terms of the rental agreement.
* **Submit (submit)**: Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form, or choose "Other" if you have any additional information or comments.
