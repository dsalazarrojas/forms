# Vehicle Access Barrier Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Access Barrier Request Form is used to request vehicle access to a property for approved reasons such as emergency services or authorized personnel. Property owners or managers will fill out this form to ensure that the necessary information is collected and processed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose whether the vehicle access is necessary (`category` section).
2. Provide a detailed description of the requested access (`description` section).
3. Select the date and time for the requested access (`date` and `time` section).
4. Indicate if the vehicle details are necessary (`vehicle_details` section).
5. Enter your name and contact information as the requestor (`requestor_name`, `requestor_email`, and `requestor_phone` sections).
6. Provide the name of the property where the vehicle access is requested (`property_name` section).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: This field indicates whether the vehicle access is necessary for approved reasons such as emergency services or authorized personnel. Choose 'Yes' if the access is required and 'No' otherwise.
* **description**: This field is for a detailed description of the requested vehicle access. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the purpose and duration of the requested access.
* **date**: This field is for selecting the date when the vehicle access is required.
* **time**: This field is for selecting the time when the vehicle access is required.
* **vehicle_details**: If the vehicle details are necessary for the access, choose 'Yes' in this field. This information will be used to determine the vehicle's specifications and requirements.
* **requestor_name**: This field is for entering your name as the person requesting the vehicle access.
* **requestor_email**: This field is for entering your email address as the person requesting the vehicle access. This will be used for further communication and follow-ups.
* **requestor_phone**: This field is for entering your phone number as the person requesting the vehicle access. This will be used for any additional questions or requests.
* **property_name**: This field is for entering the name of the property where the vehicle access is requested.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete. If any of the fields are unclear or not applicable, please contact the relevant authority for further clarification or assistance. 

Note: This form is intended to be a helpful guide. Please refer to the relevant property regulations and local laws for further information and guidelines.
