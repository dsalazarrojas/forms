# Tenant Welfare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Welfare Inspection Form is used by property management teams to record and report on the condition of properties after an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the date and time of your visit to the property in the "Date of Visit" field.
2. Enter the name of the tenant or residents in the "Tenant Name" field.
3. Enter the unit number of the property being inspected in the "Unit Number" field.
4. Use the "Condition" field to describe the condition of the property, either as "Tenant is in good condition" or "Tenant is in poor condition".
5. Add any additional notes about the property or the visit in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit (Date)**: Enter the date and time of your visit to the property.
* **Time of Visit (Time)**: Enter the time of your visit to the property. (Note: This field is optional, but recommended for accurate record-keeping.)
* **Tenant Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the tenant or residents in the property.
* **Unit Number (Text)**: Enter the unit number of the property being inspected.
* **Condition (Select One)**: Describe the condition of the property as either "Tenant is in good condition" or "Tenant is in poor condition".
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional notes about the property or the visit. 

## Tips
* Make sure to include the date and time of your visit to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* Take photos or videos of any issues or areas of concern to supplement your inspection report.
* Use the "Note" field to provide as much detail as possible about the property's condition.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are complete and accurate.
