# Property Lifecycle Analytics Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to property lifecycle analytics, including property type, location, and manager's contact details. The form helps track access and goals related to the property analytics platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one of the options for the "Property Lifecycle Analytics Application" field.
2. Select one of the options for the "Category" field.
3. Enter the property name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter the property's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the property's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter a description of the property's goals in the "Goals" field.
7. Select one of the options for the "Property Analytics Platform" field.
8. Select one of the options for the "Property Analytics Platform Access" field.
9. Select one of the options for the "Property Analytics Platform Goals" field.
10. Enter the asset manager's contact information in the "Asset Manager Contact" field.
11. Enter the asset manager's name in the "Asset Manager Name" field.
12. Enter the investor contact information in the "Investor Contact" field.
13. Enter the investor's name in the "Investor Name" field.
14. Enter the asset manager's name and contact information in the "Asset Manager Name and Contact" field.
15. Enter the investor's name and contact information in the "Investor Name and Contact" field.
16. Select one of the options for the "Property Type" field.
17. Enter the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
18. Select one of the options for the "Property Location" field.
19. Enter the asset manager's email address in the "Asset Manager Email" field.
20. Enter the investor's email address in the "Investor Email" field.
21. Enter the property's address line 2 in the "Property Address 2" field.
22. Enter the property's city in the "Property City" field.
23. Enter the property's state in the "Property State" field.
24. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
