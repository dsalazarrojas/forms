# Eco Friendly Property Material Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Friendly Property Material Application Form is a tool designed to help property owners or managers apply for eco-friendly building materials for their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.
- Make sure to review and verify the information provided before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your property's address for location purposes.
- **Building Type** (`building_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your property is a specific type of building that is eligible for eco-friendly materials.
- **Building Materials** (`building_materials`, select_multiple, required): Select all materials that you are applying for from the given options.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission of the application form.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission of the application form.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the application.

## Tips
- Make sure to attach any required documents or supporting materials with your application.
- Review the application form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support team for assistance.
