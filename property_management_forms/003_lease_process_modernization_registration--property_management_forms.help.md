# lease_process_modernization_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from team members, team leads, and participants involved in lease process modernization. It helps maintain accurate records and facilitates communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required details about the team member/team lead/participant.
2.  Enter the team member/title/lead/participant title.
3.  Choose the participant's interest in lease process modernization.
4.  Provide a valid email address for the participant.
5.  Enter the participant's phone number.
6.  Select the consent type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Member/Team Lead/Participant** (`text`, required/true): Enter the name of the team member/team lead/participant.
*   **Team Member Title/Team Lead Title/Participant Title** (`text`, required/true): Enter the title of the team member/team lead/participant.
*   **Participant Interest** (`select_one`, required/true): Choose the participant's interest in lease process modernization.
*   **Participant Email** (`email`, required/true): Enter a valid email address for the participant.
*   **Participant Phone** (`text`, required/true): Enter the participant's phone number.
*   **Consent** (`select_multiple`, required/true): Select the type of consent given by the participant.
*   **General/Specific** (`select_multiple`, required/true): Select General or Specific as consent type.
