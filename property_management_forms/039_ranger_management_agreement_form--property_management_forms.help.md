# Ranger Management Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Ranger Management Agreement Form" is a document used to record and manage agreements between individuals and the organization regarding work assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the provided text field. This is the name you wish to be associated with this agreement.
2. Enter your **User Email** in the provided email field. This is the email address that will be associated with this agreement.
3. Enter your **User Phone** in the provided text field. This is the phone number that will be associated with this agreement.
4. Enter the **Site Name** of the location where the work assignment will take place in the provided text field. This is the name of the site that you will be assigned to.
5. Select the **Assignment Start Date** from the calendar picker or enter a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD". This is the date when the work assignment will begin.
6. Select the **Assignment End Date** from the calendar picker or enter a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD". This is the date when the work assignment will end.
7. Enter the **Assignment Length** in the provided number field. This is the duration of the work assignment in days.
8. Enter the name of the person or entity that **Assigned By** in the provided text field. This is the person or entity that assigned you the work assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name to be associated with this agreement.
* **User Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be associated with this agreement.
* **User Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be associated with this agreement.
* **Site Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the site where you will be assigned to.
* **Assignment Start Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date when the work assignment will begin.
* **Assignment End Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date when the work assignment will end.
* **Assignment Length** (number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the work assignment in days.
* **Assigned By** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or entity that assigned you the work assignment.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities.
* Review and verify the information entered before submitting the form.
