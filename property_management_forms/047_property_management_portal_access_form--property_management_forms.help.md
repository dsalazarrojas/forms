# property_management_portal_access_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The property management portal access form is used to request access to the property management portal. This form collects relevant information about the requested access, including the tenant's ID, date and time of the request, reason for access, and duration of access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting the form.
* Select the correct tenant ID from the list.
* Enter the date and time you want to access the property.
* Enter the reason for your access request.
* Enter the duration of access you require.
* Enter the start and end dates for your access period.
* Enter the start and end times for your access period.
* Enter the number of hours, minutes, and days you require access for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Tenant Id (id: 1)
* **Tenant Id**: Enter the ID of the tenant you are requesting access for. This is a required field.

### 2. Requested Date (id: 2)
* **Requested Date**: Enter the date you want to access the property. This is a required field.

### 3. Requested Time (id: 3)
* **Requested Time**: Enter the time you want to access the property. This is a required field.

### 4. Property Name (id: 4)
* **Property Name**: Enter the name of the property you are requesting access to. This is a required field.

### 5. Access Reason (id: 5)
* **Access Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for your access request. This is a required field.

### 6. Manager Name (id: 6)
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who will be granting access. This is a required field.

### 7. Access Duration (id: 7)
* **Access Duration**: Enter the number of minutes you will be accessing the property. This is a required field.

### 8. Start Date (id: 8)
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your access period. This is a required field.

### 9. End Date (id: 9)
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your access period. This is a required field.

### 10. Start Time (id: 10)
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your access period. This is a required field.

### 11. End Time (id: 11)
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your access period. This is a required field.

### 12. Access Hours (id: 12)
* **Access Hours**: Enter the total hours you will be accessing the property. This is a required field.

### 13. Access Minutes (id: 13)
* **Access Minutes**: Enter the total minutes you will be accessing the property. This is a required field.

### 14. Access Days (id: 14)
* **Access Days**: Enter the total days you will be accessing the property. This is a required field.

### 15. Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as any missing information may delay or deny your access request.
