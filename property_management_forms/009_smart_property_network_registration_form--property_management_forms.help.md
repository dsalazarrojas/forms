# Smart Property Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Property Network Registration Form is used to gather information from property owners and users to create a profile on the Smart Property Network. This form collects personal and property-related data for registration and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used as your unique identifier on the network.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. Select the **Property Type** of your property from the drop-down menu (e.g., Residential, Commercial, etc.).
5. Choose the **Property Subtype** of your property from the drop-down menu (e.g., Single-family home, Apartment, etc.).
6. Enter the **Address** of your property.
7. Enter the **City** where your property is located.
8. Enter the **Zip Code** of your property.
9. Set the **Listing Price** of your property.
10. Select the **Status** of your property (Active or Inactive).
11. Add any **Notes** about your property.
12. Enter the name of the **Contact Person** responsible for your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used as your unique identifier on the network.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are registering (e.g., Residential, Commercial, etc.).
* **Property Subtype**: Choose the subtype of your property from the options listed (e.g., Single-family home, Apartment, etc.).
* **Address**: Enter the address of your property.
* **City**: Enter the city where your property is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your property.
* **Listing Price**: Enter the listing price of your property.
* **Status**: Select the status of your property (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes**: Add any notes about your property.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your property.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
