# Household And Territory Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Household And Territory Registration" form is a data collection tool used for administrative purposes to record information about householders and their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the household details, including the type of household you reside in.
2. Select the type of property you occupy.
3. Provide the number of units in your property.
4. Fill out the resident's name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Enter the property's address, territory area, details, and status.
6. Specify the number of household members.
7. Choose the type of resident (individual or family).
8. Fill out the date and time.
9. Add any additional notes or remarks.
10. Finally, upload a file and sign off by providing your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Details**: Provide a brief description of your household.
* **Household Type**: Choose the type of household you reside in (Apartment, Duplex, House, or Other).
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you occupy (Single, Multi, Townhouse).
* **Number of Units**: Enter the number of units in your property.
* **Resident Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Resident Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Resident Address**: Enter your address as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's address.
* **Territory Area**: Enter your territory's area or region.
* **Territory Details**: Provide additional details about your territory, if applicable.
* **Property Status**: Choose the status of your property (Active or Inactive).
* **Property Notes**: Add any additional remarks or comments about your property.
* **Household Members**: Specify the number of people residing in your household.
* **Resident Type**: Choose whether you are an individual or a family.
* **Resident Type Details**: Provide additional information about your resident type, if applicable.
* **Household ID**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Property ID**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Territory ID**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional remarks or comments.
* **Signature**: Provide your signature to confirm your submission.
* **File**: Upload a supporting file, if applicable.
