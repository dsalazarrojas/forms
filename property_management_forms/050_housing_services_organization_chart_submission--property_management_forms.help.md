# Housing Services Organization Chart Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to determine if you are submitting an organization chart for the Housing Services department. This form will help us gather necessary information from you to process your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you are submitting an organization chart (Yes or No).
2. Enter detailed information about the department you are submitting an organization chart for (Department Details).
3. Select whether the department has department heads (Yes or No).
4. Provide staff information about the submitted organization chart (Staff Info).
5. Choose whether the submitted information includes organization chart data (Yes or No).
6. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Org Chart
* **Org Chart** (`Org Chart`, Select Multiple, required):
  Choose whether you are submitting an organization chart, which is a visual representation of the department's hierarchical structure.

### Department Details
* **Department Details** (`Department Details`, Text, required):
  Enter a detailed description of the department you are submitting an organization chart for. This should include any relevant information about the department's name, location, or responsibilities.

### Department Heads
* **Department Heads** (`Department Heads`, Select Multiple, not required):
  Select whether the department has department heads. If you are unsure, you can select "No" and provide additional information in the Department Details field.

### Staff Info
* **Staff Info** (`Staff Info`, Text, required):
  Provide staff information about the submitted organization chart, including any relevant details about the staff members and their roles.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`Submit`, Select One, required):
  Choose whether the submitted information includes organization chart data. If you are unsure, you can select "No" and provide additional information in the Department Details field.

### Org Chart Data
* **Org Chart Data** (`Org Chart Data`, Select One, required):
  Choose whether the submitted information includes organization chart data. If you are unsure, you can select "No" and provide additional information in the Department Details field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before proceeding.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please provide additional information in the Department Details field.
* Please review your submission carefully before clicking "Submit" to ensure that all information is accurate.
