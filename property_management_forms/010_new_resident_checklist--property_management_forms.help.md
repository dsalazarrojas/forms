# New Resident Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Resident Checklist is a form used to collect information from new residents, enabling the property management team to effectively manage their data and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the form, including unit number, name, move-in date and time, and other relevant details.
2. Choose the correct mode of transportation for your move-in (e.g., Car, Taxi, Train, Bus, Bike, Walk).
3. Indicate whether you have vehicle registration (True, False, or Not Applicable) and parking arrangement (On-site Parking, Off-site Parking, or Not Applicable) if applicable.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unit number assigned to you by the property management.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Move-in Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your move-in.
* **Move-in Time** (`move_in_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your move-in.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for property management communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Move-in Vehicle** (`move_in_vehicle`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the mode of transportation you used to move-in (e.g., Car, Taxi, Train, Bus, Bike, Walk).
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have vehicle registration (True, False, or Not Applicable).
* **Parking Arrangement** (`parking_arrangement`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your parking arrangement (On-site Parking, Off-site Parking, or Not Applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your move-in experience.
 
## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the property management team.
* Please ensure the form is filled out with the correct unit number assigned to you.
