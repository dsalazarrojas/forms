# Home Maintenance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Maintenance Intake Form is designed to collect information about property issues or concerns from property owners. This form helps the maintenance team prioritize and address these issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information:

*   Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
*   Enter a valid email address in the field labeled "Email" (optional, but recommended for communication purposes).
*   Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone" (required for scheduling purposes).
*   Enter the property address in the field labeled "Address" (recommended for accurate location).
*   Select a date for the issue to be addressed on the field labeled "Date" (optional, but recommended for scheduling).
*   Choose a **Priority** level for the issue: Urgent, High, Medium, or Low.
*   Enter a detailed description of the issue in the field labeled "Description" (required to understand the problem).
*   Select one or more team members to be assigned to the issue: Property Manager, Landlord, or Maintenance Team.
*   Enter the name of the person submitting the issue in the field labeled "Submitted by".
*   Finally, enter the date the issue was submitted in the field labeled "Submitted Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or document.
*   **Email**: If you have a valid email address, enter it for easy communication about your issue.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for scheduling and contact purposes.
*   **Address**: Enter the exact address of the property where the issue is located.
*   **Date**: Select a date for the maintenance team to schedule the issue resolution.
*   **Priority**: Choose a priority level: Urgent, High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Description**: Enter a detailed description of the issue, including any relevant information or photos.
*   **Assigned to**: Select one or more team members to be assigned to the issue resolution.
*   **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person submitting the issue.
*   **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the issue was submitted.
*   **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about the issue, enter them here (optional). 

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue to help the maintenance team address it efficiently.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the property management team for guidance.
*   Ensure you have not submitted a similar issue already before submitting this form.
