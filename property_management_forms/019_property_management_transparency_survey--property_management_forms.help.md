# Property Management Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the property management process, focusing on communication, transparency, and issue resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the survey at your own pace, answering questions on your experience with property management.
2. Choose the options that best describe your experiences and preferences.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Would You Rate Communication?** (`communication`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with communication from property management on a scale from 1-3.
* **How Transparent Do You Find Our Company?** (`transparency`, `text`, required): Describe your perception of the level of transparency in our company.
* **How Would You Rate Issue Resolution?** (`issue_resolution`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with issue resolution from property management on a scale from 1-3.
* **What Is Your Experience Of Working With Us?** (`tenant_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your experience with working with us.
* **Do You Have Any Comments?** (`comments`, `note`, required): Share any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for any communication from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for any urgent matters.
* **How Do You Prefer To Receive Communication From Us?** (`communication_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best suit your preferences for communication from us.
* **How Do You Want To Receive Issue Resolution?** (`issue_resolution_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best suit your preferences for issue resolution from us.
* **How Frequent Do You Receive Communication From Us?** (`frequency_of_communication`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the frequency of communication from us.
* **What Style Of Communication Do You Prefer?** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best suit your preferences for communication style from us.
* **How Frequent Are Issues Raised With Us?** (`issue_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the frequency of issues raised with us.
* **What Type Of Issues Do You Experience?** (`issue_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the type of issues you experience.
* **How Long Does It Takes To Resolve An Issue?** (`response_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the time it takes to resolve an issue.
* **Is There A Particular Person You Like To Contact?** (`contact_person`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the person you like to contact.
* **How Do You Prefer To Contact Us?** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best suit your preferences for contacting us.
* **Describe An Issue You Recently Experienced** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Provide a description of a recent issue you experienced.
* **How Long Does It Take To Get A Response From Us?** (`response_time_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes the time it takes to get a response from us.
* **Do You Have Any Other Comments Or Suggestions?** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share any other comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Do You Want To Receive Email Updates?** (`email_notification`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your preference for email updates.
* **Do You Want To Receive Email Notifications When Issues Are Raised?** (`contact_email_notification`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your preference for email notifications when issues are raised.
* **Do You Want To Receive Phone Notifications When Issues Are Raised?** (`phone_notification`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your preference for phone notifications when issues are raised.
* **Would You Like To Receive A Follow-Up On Your Issue?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your preference for a follow-up on your issue.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for any communication from us.
* **How Frequent Are Issues Raised With Property Management?** (`issue_frequency`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of issues raised with property management.

Note: Some fields are optional and are used for additional feedback or comments only.
