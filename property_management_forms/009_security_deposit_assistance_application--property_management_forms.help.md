# Security Deposit Assistance Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Security Deposit Assistance Application is a form to request help with security deposit assistance for a property. You will be asked to provide details about your request, including your contact information, property address, and lease dates.

## How To Complete This Form

### To start, fill in the following fields:

* **Request Details**: Please provide a brief description of your request for security deposit assistance.
* **Applicant First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Applicant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Property Street**: Enter the street address of the property.
* **Property City**: Enter the city of the property.
* **Property State**: Enter the state of the property.
* **Property Zip**: Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Property Country**: Enter the country of the property.
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you plan to move out.
* **Security Deposit Type**: Select the type of security deposit you are requesting help with (Landlord, Property Manager, or Other).
* **Security Deposit Amount**: Enter the amount of the security deposit you are requesting.
* **Security Deposit Status**: Select the status of your security deposit (Pending, Applied, Approved, Denied, Exempt, or Other).
* **Applicant Income**: Enter your annual income.
* **Applicant Employment Status**: Select your employment status (Employed, Unemployed, or Disabled).
* **Applicant Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of your lease.
* **Applicant Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of your lease.
* **Applicant Move In Date**: Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move In Support Needed**: Select the types of support you need (Rent, Utilities, Deposit, or Other).
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.
