# Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from tenants about their experiences with the resolution process of their complaints. This survey helps the property management team to understand how the process is functioning and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the survey carefully to ensure you are answering questions about your specific experience with the resolution process.
2. On each page, answer the questions according to your genuine experience.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially when filling out the "Further Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey** (Page 1: `tenant_complaint_resolution_survey_page_1`): This is the first page of the survey, which asks you to confirm that you are responding to a survey about your specific experience with the resolution process.
* **How did you find the resolution process?** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_page_2`): Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the resolution process, or "No" if you were not. This question helps us understand how effective the process was for you.
* **Was the resolution process completed within the time frame you expected?** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_page_3`): Select all that apply, as this question can be answered with multiple options. This helps us understand if the resolution process met your expectations regarding time constraints.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the resolution?** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_page_4`): This is a numerical rating, with higher numbers indicating higher satisfaction. Please select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Was the resolution process handled in a professional and respectful manner?** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_page_5`): Select "Yes" if you felt that the process was handled professionally and respectfully, or "No" if you did not. This question helps us understand how well we handled your complaint.
* **Do you have any further comments or concerns regarding the resolution process?** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_page_6`): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about the resolution process. This helps us to understand what we could improve.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any further comments or concerns, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the property management team for clarification.
* This survey is designed to be completed by tenants who have already gone through the resolution process. If you have already completed this survey, please do not fill it out again.
