# Landlord Cooperation Council Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Landlord Cooperation Council Registration form. The purpose of this form is to gather information about you as a landlord and your property to be included in our database of registered landlords. This will help you connect with other landlords in the community and access shared resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Landlord Contact Information" page.
2. Fill in your contact information (name, address, email, and phone number). This is the initial step in the registration process. Please be as detailed and accurate as possible.
3. Provide your contact person's name and any special interests or skills you'd like to highlight.
4. Answer the question about the type of property you own/are managing (yes/no).
5. Enter your property's location.
6. If you agree to participate in the program, check the "Landlord Consent" box (yes/no).
7. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your personal name or business name associated with the property.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the physical address where you live or manage the property.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Contact Person** (text, required): Enter the name of the contact person (e.g. property manager).
* **Interests** (text, optional): Mention any specific areas of interest or expertise you'd like others to know about.
* **Property Type** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you own/are managing a property.
* **Property Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your property.
* **Landlord Consent** (note, optional): Check "Yes" if you agree to participate in the program.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click "Submit" to complete the registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, please contact us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
