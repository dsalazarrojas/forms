# property_energy_audit_participation_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Energy Audit Participation Consent Form is a document that aims to collect information from property owners regarding their participation in an energy audit. This form is used to understand the property owner's willingness to participate in the energy audit process and gather essential details about the property. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Property Details**: Provide a short description of your property, including its address, location, and any notable features.
2. **Property Energy Audit Participation Consent**: Select your response to indicate whether you are willing to participate in the energy audit.
3. **Property Owner Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you with updates and results from the energy audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details**: A brief description of your property, which can include its address, location, and any notable features. This helps us understand the context of the energy audit.
* **Property Energy Audit Participation Consent**: This field allows you to choose whether you want to participate in the energy audit. Please select 'Yes' to indicate your agreement to proceed with the audit.
* **Property Owner Details**: This section is for you to provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This ensures we can reach out to you for any further questions or updates during the energy audit process.

## Tips
Please provide accurate and clear information in all fields. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
