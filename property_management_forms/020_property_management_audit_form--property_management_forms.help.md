# Property Management Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Management Audit Form is designed to collect data about a property inspection, providing an efficient and structured way to gather information about the property's condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Select the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Choose the type of inspection from the "Inspection Type" dropdown list.
4. Enter the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Building Id" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Unit Id" field.
7. Enter the building's name in the "Building Name" field.
8. Enter the unit's name in the "Unit Name" field.
9. Enter the building's address in the "Building Address" field.
10. Enter the unit's address in the "Unit Address" field.
11. Add any additional notes about the inspection in the "Inspection Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Type** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the type of inspection being performed, such as Property Inspection, Condition Assessment, or Maintenance Inspection.
* **Inspection Location** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the location of the inspection.
* **Building Id** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select "Yes" if the building is being inspected, otherwise select "No".
* **Unit Id** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select "Yes" if the unit is being inspected, otherwise select "No".
* **Building Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the building.
* **Unit Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the unit.
* **Building Address** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of the building.
* **Unit Address** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of the unit.
* **Inspection Notes** (Note, Required/Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
