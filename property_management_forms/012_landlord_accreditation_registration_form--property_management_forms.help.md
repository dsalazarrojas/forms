# Landlord Accreditation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Accreditation Registration Form is designed to collect information from landlords who want to be accredited. This form is a general template for registration and does not have specific requirements for completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each page of the form, starting from the first page titled "Landlord info" to the last page.
* For the "Code of Conduct" field, select one of the three available options by clicking on the corresponding button.
* Enter your details in the text fields as prompted, including "Property details", "Landlord details", "Property info", and "Agreement terms".
* Sign your form with your "Landlord signature" in the designated area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord info**: This is the first page of the form where you enter your basic landlord information.
* **Code of Conduct**: Select one of the three available options to agree to the code of conduct.
* **Property details**: Enter a brief description of the property you're registering for accreditation.
* **Landlord details**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Property info**: Enter any additional information about the property.
* **Code of conduct agreement**: Enter your agreement to the code of conduct you selected earlier.
* **Landlord contact**: Enter your contact information in case of any further inquiries.
* **Property address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Agreement terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the agreement.
* **Landlord signature**: Sign your form with your digital signature, indicating that you've read and agree to the terms and conditions of the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
