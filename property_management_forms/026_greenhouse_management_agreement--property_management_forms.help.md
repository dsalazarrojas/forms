# Greenhouse Management Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Greenhouse Management Agreement" form is used to collect information about the management of greenhouses, including ownership, details, and financial agreements. This form is essential for establishing a clear understanding between the parties involved in the management of the greenhouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the basic information about the greenhouse.
2.  Then, provide details about the ownership of the greenhouse.
3.  Next, specify the responsibilities of the parties involved.
4.  Set a start and end date for the rent period.
5.  Enter the rent amount and payment terms.
6.  Finally, add any additional notes or comments about the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Ownership**: Enter the name of the individual or organization that owns the greenhouse.
	+ Type: `text` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the name of the individual or organization that owns the greenhouse.
* **Greenhouse Details**: Enter any additional details about the greenhouse, such as its size or location.
	+ Type: `text` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record any additional details about the greenhouse.
* **Responsibilities**: Enter any responsibilities or obligations that you have or the other party has for the management of the greenhouse.
	+ Type: `note` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record any responsibilities or obligations that you have or the other party has for the management of the greenhouse.
* **Timeline**: Set the start and end date for the rent period of the greenhouse.
	+ Type: `date` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the start and end dates of the rent period of the greenhouse.
* **Greenhouse Manager**: Check whether you are the manager of the greenhouse or not.
	+ Type: `select_one` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record whether you are the manager of the greenhouse or not.
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment options that apply to the management of the greenhouse.
	+ Type: `select_multiple` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the payment options that apply to the management of the greenhouse.
* **Rent Start Date**: Enter the start date of the rent period.
	+ Type: `date` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the start date of the rent period.
* **Rent End Date**: Enter the end date of the rent period.
	+ Type: `date` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the end date of the rent period.
* **Rent Period**: Enter the length of the rent period in months or years.
	+ Type: `number` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the length of the rent period.
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of rent that is to be paid.
	+ Type: `number` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the rent amount.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional payment terms or conditions that apply.
	+ Type: `note` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
	+ Type: `note` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	+ Type: `text` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
	+ Type: `email` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	+ Type: `text` 
	+ Purpose: This field is used to record the phone number of the contact person.
