# Property Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Release Form is a document used to formally release a property from the ownership of the property owner or their representative. This form is essential in ensuring a smooth and legally binding property transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that the property owner or their representative is aware of the form's purpose and the information being submitted.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to the relevant authorities or parties involved in the property transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Property Release Form (id: 1)
**Property Release Form** (`Property Release Form`, `number`, required): A unique identifier for this specific property release form.

### 2. Property Owner (id: 2)
**Property Owner** (`Property Owner`, `text`, required): The individual or entity that owns the property being released.

### 3. Other Party (id: 3)
**Other Party** (`Other Party`, `text`, required): The individual or entity receiving the property being released.

### 4. Property Description (id: 4)
**Property Description** (`Property Description`, `text`, required): A brief description of the property being released, including its location, size, and any notable features.

### 5. Date (id: 5)
**Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): The date on which the property is being released.

### 6. Time (id: 6)
**Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): The time at which the property is being released.

### 7. Note (id: 7)
**Note** (`Note`, `note`, required): Any additional comments or notes regarding the property release.

### 8. Email (id: 8)
**Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): The contact email of the property owner or their representative.

### 9. Phone (id: 9)
**Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): The contact phone number of the property owner or their representative.

### 10. Property Release Form Note (id: 10)
**Property Release Form Note** (`Property Release Form Note`, `note`, required): Additional comments or notes regarding the property release form.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general explanation of the form fields. However, please consult with relevant authorities or experts for specific guidance on completing this form for official purposes.
