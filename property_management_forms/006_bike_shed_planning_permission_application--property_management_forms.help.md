# Bike Shed Planning Permission Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike Shed Planning Permission Application form collects information from applicants to assess the feasibility and potential impact of their proposed bike shed project. This form gathers details about the applicant, the bike shed, and its planned location on the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your full name as the applicant.
2. Enter the address of your property where the bike shed will be installed.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Describe the proposed dimensions of the bike shed (length x width x height).
5. Select the primary material to be used for the bike shed.
6. Enter the proposed color or finish of the bike shed.
7. Specify where the bike shed will be located on your property (e.g., rear garden, side of the house).
8. Choose the type of foundation or base for the bike shed.
9. Indicate whether the bike shed will be visible from the street.
10. Confirm if you have consulted with adjacent neighbors regarding the project.
11. Let us know if you will provide site plans or drawings separately (e.g., via email).
12. Add any additional project notes or details (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant**: Your full name as the applicant.
* **Property Address**: The address where the bike shed will be installed.
* **Contact Email**: Your contact email address for follow-up communication.
* **Proposed Dimensions (Length x Width x Height)**: Describe the dimensions of the bike shed in feet.
* **Primary Material**: Select the main material to be used for the bike shed (Wood, Metal, Plastic/Resin, Brick/Masonry).
* **Proposed Color/Finish**: Enter the proposed color or finish of the bike shed.
* **Proposed Location on Property**: Specify where the bike shed will be located (e.g., rear garden, side of the house).
* **Type of Foundation/Base**: Choose the type of foundation or base for the bike shed (Concrete Slab, Paving Stones, Wooden Decking, Directly on Grass/Soil).
* **Will the shed be visible from the street?**: Indicate whether the bike shed will be visible from the street.
* **Have you consulted with adjacent neighbors?**: Confirm if you have consulted with adjacent neighbors regarding the project.
* **Will you provide site plans or drawings?**: Indicate how you will provide site plans or drawings (e.g., via email, drop off at office).
* **Additional Project Notes**: Add any additional details or notes about your project.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter, as this will affect the approval process.
* Ensure you have consulted with adjacent neighbors before submitting this form.
* Provide site plans or drawings as required for final approval.
