# Tenant Move In/Out Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for property management and maintenance purposes. It is used to document the condition of a property at the start and end of a tenant's tenancy.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Upload a photo to support the Condition Description.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property ID Number** (`property_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the property's ID number as assigned by the property management or management system.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person moving in or out of the property.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the tenant started their tenancy.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the tenant moved out.
* **Move In/Out Photo** (`move_in_move_out_photo`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of the property at the start or end of the tenancy.
* **Condition Description** (`condition_description`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the property's condition at the start and end of the tenancy.
* **Condition Photo** (`condition_photo`, `text`, optional): This field is optional, but if used, upload a photo of the property's condition at the start or end of the tenancy.
## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Use clear and concise language in the Condition Description field.
* For the Move In/Out Photo and Condition Photo fields, upload clear and high-quality images that support your descriptions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the property management or maintenance staff for guidance.
