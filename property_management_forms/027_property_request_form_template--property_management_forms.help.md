# Property Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Request Form Template is designed to gather property management information from end users. Please use this form to provide details about your properties, ensuring that we can efficiently manage them within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the property's title and any other details as requested on the form's main page.
2. Fill out the property details section with relevant information.
3. Add your contact information, including name, number and email, and select the property type and status.
4. Provide the property address and any additional information if required.
5. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Property Request Form Title** (main_page, text, optional): This field captures the title of the property. Enter a brief title that describes the property.
 
- **Property Details** (property_details, text, optional): Enter a short description of the property, including any relevant details.

- **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter your name, title, and company name as requested on this field.

- **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Enter the contact number for the property or the individual associated with the property.

- **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the contact email associated with the property or the individual. This field must be in the format of an email address (username@domain.com).

- **Property Type** (property_type, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to describe the type of property.

- **Property Status** (property_status, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the property from the options provided.

- **Property Address** (property_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the property, including any other relevant details.

- **Additional Info** (additional_info, note, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the property not included in the other fields. This field allows you to provide additional comments or information not requested in other fields.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure we can efficiently manage your property.
* If you need to provide additional information beyond what is requested, use the "Additional Info" field.
* If you encounter any issues or errors while filling out the form, please contact us at your convenience.
