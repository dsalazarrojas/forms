# Property Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Survey form is designed to gather information about a property management system. This form will help you collect feedback, comments, and ratings on various aspects of property management, such as apartment complexes, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the following order:
	* Page 1: property-management-survey (text field)
	* Page 2: apartment-complex (select one: 'Yes' or 'No')
	* Page 3: rating (number field)
	* Page 4: comment (text field)
	* Page 5: email (email field)
	* Page 6: phone-number (text field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Management Survey (1)**: This is a text field where you can type any comments or feedback about the property management system.
* **Apartment Complex (2)**: Please select 'Yes' if you are currently managing or have managed an apartment complex, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Rating (3)**: Rate your satisfaction with the property management system on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is 'Very Dissatisfied' and 10 is 'Very Satisfied'.
* **Comment (4)**: This is a text field where you can add any additional comments or feedback about the property management system.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or responses.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you for any urgent matters.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* You can skip any questions that do not apply to your current situation.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to reach out to us for further assistance.
