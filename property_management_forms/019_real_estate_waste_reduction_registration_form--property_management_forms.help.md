# Real Estate Waste Reduction Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from property owners who are interested in reducing waste on their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Carefully read through each page and ensure you've answered all required questions.**
- **Submit the form once you've completed all the pages.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1: Registration Information**
  - **Property Owner Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Property Address** (text, required): Enter the address of your property where the waste reduction efforts will be implemented.
  - **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or updates.
  - **Email** (text, optional): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you regarding your submission.

- **Page 2: Waste Reduction Details**
  - **Description of Waste Reduction Efforts** (text, required): Describe the waste reduction strategies and practices implemented in your property. Explain the specific actions taken and results achieved.
  - **Waste Reduction Metrics** (text, required): Provide details on the metrics used to measure the success of your waste reduction efforts, such as percentage of waste reduction or tons of waste avoided.

- **Page 3: Certification**
  - **Certification Documents** (text, required): Please upload scanned copies of your certification documents, such as certifications from third-party auditors or government agencies.

- **Page 4: Submission**
  - **Submission Confirmation** (text, required): Verify that you've filled out the form accurately and truthfully. Confirm that this is your submission and that it's not a duplicate.
  - **Additional Comments** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
  - **Upload Supporting Documents** (text, optional): If you have any supporting documents that help demonstrate your waste reduction efforts, please upload them here.
