# Property Sale Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and document essential details for property sales, including owner information, sale terms, and other relevant data. It is intended to streamline the property sale process and ensure that all necessary information is accurately captured and stored.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that all relevant fields are filled in accurately and completely. If a field is not required, fill it out as necessary or leave it blank.
2. Double-check that the information entered is correct, especially for fields such as dates and contact information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Sale Authorization** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description or note about the property sale authorization.
* **Owner Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the name of the property owner as it appears on official documents.
* **Owner Contact Email** (`id: 3`): Enter the email address used by the owner for contact purposes.
* **Agent Name** (`id: 4`): Enter the name of the real estate agent or representative involved in the sale.
* **Agent Contact Number** (`id: 5`): Enter the phone number of the agent for contact purposes.
* **Property Address** (`id: 6`): Enter the full address of the property being sold, including house number and city or state.
* **Sale Date** (`id: 7`): Enter the date of the property sale, following the format specified.
* **Sale Price** (`id: 8`): Enter the monetary value of the property sale.
* **Sale Status** (`id: 9`): Select the status of the sale, such as active, inactive, or pending.
* **Notes** (`id: 10`): Enter any additional information or comments about the sale.
* **Sale Date Time** (`id: 11`): Enter the time of the property sale, following the format specified.
* **Sale Confirmation Date** (`id: 12`): Enter the date the sale was confirmed or finalized.
* **Sale Confirmation Time** (`id: 13`): Enter the time the sale was confirmed or finalized.
* **Property Sale Confirmation** (`id: 14`): Select whether the sale has been confirmed, is pending, or denied.
* **Sale Confirmation Method** (`id: 15`): Select the method used to confirm the sale, such as email, phone call, or in-person meeting.
* **Sales Contact Person** (`id: 16`): Select whether a sales contact person is involved in the sale.
