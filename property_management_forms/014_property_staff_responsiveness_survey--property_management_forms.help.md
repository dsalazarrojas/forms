# Property Staff Responsiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from property residents about the responsiveness and quality of service provided by property staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the best option that reflects your opinion for each question.
3. You can only select one option for select-one questions and multiple options for select-multiple questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Property Manager Response Time
**Property Manager Response Time** (`property_manager_response_time`, number, not required)
This question asks for your feedback on how quickly the property manager responds to your issues. You can choose from the following options: 1, 2, 3, 4, etc. (The more you respond, the faster you want the property manager to respond.)

### Staff Communication Quality
**Staff Communication Quality** (`staff_communication_quality`, select_one, not required)
This question asks about the quality of communication from property staff. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Overall Service Quality
**Overall Service Quality** (`overall_service_quality`, select_multiple, not required)
This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the service quality provided. You can choose from the following options: Good, Poor.

### Property Staff Availability
**Property Staff Availability** (`property_staff_availability`, text, not required)
This question asks when the property staff is available to meet. Please type your answer in the space provided.

### Resident Feedback on Response Time
**Resident Feedback on Response Time** (`resident_feedback_on_response_time`, select_multiple, not required)
This question asks about your satisfaction with the response time of your issues. You can choose from the following options: Yes, No.

### Communication with Property Staff
**Communication with Property Staff** (`communication_with_property_staff`, select_multiple, not required)
This question asks about your satisfaction with communication with property staff. You can choose from the following options: Yes, No.

### Feedback on Service Quality
**Feedback on Service Quality** (`feedback_on_service_quality`, select_one, not required)
This question asks about your feedback on the service quality. You can choose from the following options: Good, Poor.

### Property Staff Quality
**Property Staff Quality** (`property_staff_quality`, select_multiple, not required)
This question asks about your perception of property staff quality. You can choose from the following options: Good, Poor.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and objectively.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" section (not shown in this guide).
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your property management team.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
