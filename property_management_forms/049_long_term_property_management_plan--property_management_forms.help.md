# Long Term Property Management Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Term Property Management Plan is a document designed for property owners or managers to create a comprehensive schedule for property maintenance and management. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, completion process, and each field's explanation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing the property owner's information in the "Property Owner Info" field. This includes their contact details and other relevant information.
2. In the "Maintenance Schedule Plan" field, list the dates and times for regular maintenance and repairs. This will help in scheduling and planning.
3. In the "Tenant Management Plan" field, describe your approach to managing the tenants of the property.
4. For each maintenance or repair item, provide details in the "Maintenance Schedule Items" field. Select all relevant items.
5. In the "Maintenance Costs" field, estimate the costs for each maintenance or repair item.
6. Complete the "Emergency Contact Details" field with the contact information for emergency contacts.
7. Optionally, provide information about the property manager's contact information, address, phone number, email, and social media presence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Owner Info (required)**: 
  * A field for the property owner's contact information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Maintenance Schedule Plan (required)**: 
  * A space to list the dates and times for regular maintenance and repairs.
* **Tenant Management Plan (optional)**: 
  * A field to describe the approach to managing tenants of the property.
* **Maintenance Schedule Items (required)**: 
  * A field to select all maintenance or repair items that require attention.
  * Possible options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Maintenance Schedule Items Items (required)**: 
  * A field to select a specific maintenance or repair item from the list provided.
  * Possible options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Maintenance Costs (required)**: 
  * A field for estimating the costs for each maintenance or repair item.
* **Emergency Contact Details (optional)**: 
  * A field for contact information for emergency contacts.
* **Maintenance Frequency (optional)**: 
  * A field for scheduling the maintenance frequency.
* **Maintenance Duration (optional)**: 
  * A field for estimating the duration of each maintenance or repair.
* **Maintenance Schedule Impact (optional)**: 
  * A field for describing the potential impact of maintenance or repairs.
* **Emergency Management Plan (optional)**: 
  * A field for outlining the plan for emergency management and response.
* **Tenant Management Plan 2 (optional)**: 
  * A field for further detailing the tenant management plan.
* **Tenant Management Plan 3 (optional)**: 
  * A field for additional information about the tenant management plan.
* **Property Address (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property's address.
* **Property Manager Info (optional)**: 
  * A field for contact information about the property manager.
* **Property Manager Phone (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's phone number.
* **Property Manager Email (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's email.
* **Property Manager Address (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's address.
* **Property Manager Website (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's website.
* **Property Manager Social Media (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's social media presence.
* **Property Manager License (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's license or permit.
* **Property Manager Licence Number (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's license number.
* **Property Manager Vehicle Number (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property manager's vehicle number.
* **Property Owner Vehicle Number (optional)**: 
  * A field for the property owner's vehicle number.

Please note that some fields are not required for submission and can be filled in based on your specific property management needs.
