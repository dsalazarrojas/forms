# Water Damage Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Damage Assessment Form is a tool used to gather information about a property damage incident, including the initial contact information, incident details, and assessment results. This form is designed to be completed by the property management team or facility manager in the event of a water damage incident.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the initial contact information section with your name, contact number, and contact email.
*   Select the correct incident type from the provided options (Water Damage, Leaking Roof, or Burst Pipe).
*   Enter the date and time of the incident.
*   Describe the incident location and provide any additional details about the incident.
*   Assess the damage level of the incident (Minor, Moderate, or Severe).
*   Upload any relevant photos of the damage.
*   Enter any comments or additional information about the incident.
*   Finally, submit the form to complete the incident report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Initial Contact Information** (`Initial Contact Information`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you with any follow-up questions.
*   **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required: false):
    This is the person who completed the form.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required: false):
    This is the contact number of the person who completed the form.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required: false):
    This is the email address of the person who completed the form.
*   **Incident Type** (`Incident Type`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select the type of incident that occurred (Water Damage, Leaking Roof, or Burst Pipe).
*   **Incident Date** (`Incident Date`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time** (`Incident Time`, `time`, required: false):
    Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Location** (`Incident Location`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe the exact location of the incident.
*   **Incident Description** (`Incident Description`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Damage Level** (`Damage Level`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Assess the level of damage (Minor, Moderate, or Severe).
*   **Photos** (`Photos`, `note`, required: false):
    Upload any photos of the damage to support your report.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false):
    Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
*   **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required: false):
    This field is automatically populated and should not be edited.
*   **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, `date`, required: false):
    This field is automatically populated and should not be edited.
