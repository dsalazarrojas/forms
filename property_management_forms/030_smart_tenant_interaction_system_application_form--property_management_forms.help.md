# Smart Tenant Interaction System Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Tenant Interaction System Application Form is designed to collect essential information from tenants who are interacting with the Smart Tenant Interaction System. This form helps the system understand the tenant's contact preferences, communication preferences, and verification information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Application Form** field with your application details.
2. Enter your **Tenant Details** in the text field.
3. Specify your **Contact Preferences** in the text field.
4. Select your preferred **Communication Preferences** from the options provided.
5. Confirm your **Consent** by selecting the correct option.
6. Add any additional **Notes** in the note field.
7. Enter your **Email** address in the text field.
8. Confirm your **Email Verification** by selecting the correct option.
9. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field.
10. Select a specific **Date** and **Time** for your verification.
11. Choose your preferred **Address** from the options provided.
12. Enter your **Zip** code in the text field.
13. Confirm your **Phone Verification** by selecting the correct option.
14. Confirm your **Date Verification** by entering your date of birth in the text field.
15. Confirm your **Time Verification** by entering your time of verification in the text field.
16. Confirm your **Address Verification** by entering your address in the text field.
17. Confirm your **Zip Verification** by entering your zip code in the text field.
18. Confirm your **Phone Verification** by entering your phone number in the text field.
19. Confirm your **Tenant Verification** by entering your identity in the text field.
20. Add any additional **Notes Verification** in the text field.
21. Confirm your **Consent Verification** by selecting the correct option.
22. Confirm your **Communication Verification** by selecting your preferred communication method.
23. Confirm your **Contact Preferences Verification** by selecting your preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (1): A text field for the tenant to enter their application details.
* **Tenant Details** (2): A text field for the tenant to provide additional details about themselves.
* **Contact Preferences** (3): A text field for the tenant to specify their contact preferences.
* **Communication Preferences** (4): A select multiple field allowing tenants to choose their preferred communication methods.
* **Consent** (5): A select one field for the tenant to confirm their consent to interact with the system.
* **Notes** (6): A note field for any additional comments or notes from the tenant.
* **Email** (7): A text field for the tenant to provide their email address.
* **Email Verification** (8): A select one field to confirm the tenant's email address.
* **Phone** (9): A text field for the tenant to provide their phone number.
* **Date** (10): A date field for the tenant to select a specific date.
* **Time** (11): A time field for the tenant to select a specific time.
* **Address** (12): A select multiple field for the tenant to specify their address.
* **Zip** (13): A text field for the tenant to provide their zip code.
* **Phone Verification** (14): A select one field to confirm the tenant's phone number.
* **Date Verification** (15): A text field for the tenant to confirm their date of birth.
* **Time Verification** (16): A text field for the tenant to confirm their time of verification.
* **Address Verification** (17): A text field for the tenant to confirm their address.
* **Zip Verification** (18): A text field for the tenant to confirm their zip code.
* **Phone Verification** (19): A text field for the tenant to confirm their phone number.
* **Tenant Verification** (20): A text field for the tenant to confirm their identity.
* **Notes Verification** (21): A text field for the tenant to provide additional verification comments.
* **Consent Verification** (22): A text field for the tenant to confirm their consent.
* **Communication Verification** (23): A text field for the tenant to confirm their communication preferences.
* **Contact Preferences Verification** (24): A text field for the tenant to confirm their contact preferences.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your information is up-to-date.
* Make sure to select the correct options for your communication and contact preferences.
* Confirm your email, phone, and date of birth to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your address and zip code to ensure accuracy.
* Confirm your identity and provide additional verification comments as needed.
