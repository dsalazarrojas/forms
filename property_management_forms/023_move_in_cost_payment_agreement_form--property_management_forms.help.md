# Move In Cost Payment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Move In Cost Payment Agreement Form is designed to document the costs associated with a tenant's move-in to a property. It captures information related to move-in dates, lease terms, payment methods, and other costs involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and the information required.
2. Enter the relevant dates associated with the move-in, including first move-in date, last move-in date, lease start date, and lease end date.
3. Select the correct payment terms and options for rent payment frequency and method.
4. Enter the total move-in cost and the payment method.
5. Confirm if other move-in costs are involved and select the applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Move In Date**: The date when the tenant first moved into the property.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Last Move In Date**: The date when the tenant last moved into the property.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Lease Start Date**: The date when the lease begins.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Lease End Date**: The date when the lease ends.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Move Out Date**: The date when the tenant plans to move out of the property.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
* **Move In Costs**: The total cost associated with the move-in.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Security Deposit**: A deposit paid by the tenant to secure the property.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Rent Frequency**: The frequency of rent payments.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Rent Frequency Option**: The specific frequency of rent payments.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Rent Frequency Option 1**: The specific frequency of rent payments.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Rent Frequency Option 2**: The specific frequency of rent payments.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Payment Terms**: Whether payment terms are included in the lease.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Payment Terms Option**: Whether payment terms are included in the lease.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Payment Terms Option 1**: The specific payment term.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Payment Terms Option 2**: The specific payment term.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Rent**: The amount of rent paid by the tenant.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Total Move In Cost**: The total cost associated with the move-in.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment used by the tenant.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Payment Method Option**: The specific payment method used by the tenant.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Payment Method Option 1**: The specific payment method used by the tenant.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
* **Other Move In Costs**: Whether other costs are involved in the move-in.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Other Move In Costs Option 1**: The specific other cost involved in the move-in.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
* **Other Move In Costs Option 2**: The specific other cost involved in the move-in.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No

## Tips

* Be accurate when entering the dates and costs to avoid errors.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is included.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the property manager or landlord for clarification.
