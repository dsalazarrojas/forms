# Property Compliance Office Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Compliance Office Contact Form is a contact form used to submit information and documents to the Property Compliance Office.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by selecting the contact method of "Phone" or "Email" or "In-person" as per your preference.
- If you have an issue or concern, provide the issue details in the "issue_details" field.
- If you have documentation to upload, you can do so in the "documentation_upload" field.
- Fill in the contact information for the Property Compliance Office in the "contact_office" field.
- Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
- If necessary, enter the property address in the "property_address" field.
- In the "description" field, briefly explain your issue or the purpose of your submission.
- You may be required to indicate whether the property is "Commercial", "Residential", or "Mixed Use" in the "property_type" field.
- You may also be required to upload a document, and provide the file in the "upload_document" field.
- If you have staff information, fill it in the "staff_info" field.
- Provide the staff email in the "staff_email" field.
- Enter the staff phone number in the "staff_phone" field.
- If necessary, fill in the staff office hours in the "staff_office_hours" field.
- In the "tenant_info" field, provide any relevant tenant information.
- In the "owner_info" field, provide any relevant owner information.
- In the "staff_comments" field, provide any comments from the staff.
- In the "staff_comments_2" field, provide any further comments from the staff.
- In the "tenant_comments" field, provide any comments from the tenant.
- In the "owner_comments" field, provide any comments from the owner.
- You may be required to indicate the current status of the property in the "property_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **issue_details**: Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern you have with the property.
* **contact_office**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by the Property Compliance Office.
* **contact_info**: Enter your contact information for the Property Compliance Office to reach out to you.
* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property, if necessary.
* **description**: Briefly describe your issue or the purpose of your submission.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number, if necessary.
* **property_type**: Indicate if the property is "Commercial", "Residential", or "Mixed Use", if required.
* **upload_document**: Upload any relevant documentation, if necessary.
* **staff_info**: Provide any relevant staff information.
* **staff_email**: Enter the email of the staff member, if necessary.
* **staff_phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member, if necessary.
* **staff_office_hours**: Enter the staff office hours, if necessary.
* **tenant_comments**: Provide any comments from the tenant.
* **owner_comments**: Provide any comments from the owner.
* **property_status**: Indicate the current status of the property, if required.
* **tenant_info**: Provide any relevant tenant information.
* **owner_info**: Provide any relevant owner information.
* **staff_comments**: Provide any comments from the staff.
* **staff_comments_2**: Provide any further comments from the staff.
* **submit**: Click "submit" to submit the form.
* **submit_2**: Click "submit" to submit the form again.
* **submit_3**: Click "submit" to submit the form again.
* **submit_4**: Click "submit" to submit the form again.
* **staff_phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member, if necessary.
* **staff_email**: Enter the email of the staff member, if necessary.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
- The Property Compliance Office will review and contact you as per your preferred contact method.

Note: The "submit" fields are likely there for some kind of automation or confirmation purpose, and the actual submission is probably done when the "contact_office" field is filled. Please review and adjust the guide accordingly.
