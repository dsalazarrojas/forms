## Tenant Engagement Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record and track details of Tenant Engagement Sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Session Start Date and Session End Date fields with the actual dates of the session.
2. Enter the Session Topic, Session Objective, and Session Outcome in their respective fields.
3. Choose the Session Status and select 'Active' if the session is currently ongoing, or 'Inactive' if it has ended.
4. Enter any Session Notes if necessary.
5. Fill in the Contact Name and Contact Email for the person responsible for the session.
6. Enter the Tenant Name and Tenant Email for the person or entity being engaged.
7. Enter the Property Name and Property Address, City, and ZIP for the location of the session.
8. Enter the Property State as 'Yes' or 'No', depending on whether the property has a state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Start Date** (`session_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the engagement session started.
- **Session End Date** (`session_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the engagement session ended.
- **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the session topic.
- **Session Objective** (`session_objective`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the session objective.
- **Session Outcome** (`session_outcome`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the session outcome.
- **Session Status** (`session_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Active' if the session is currently ongoing, or 'Inactive' if it has ended.
- **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the session.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the session.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person responsible for the session.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for the session.
- **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant or person being engaged.
- **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the tenant or person being engaged.
- **Tenant Phone** (`tenant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the tenant or person being engaged.
- **Property Name** (`property_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the property involved in the session.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the property involved in the session.
- **Property City** (`property_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the property involved in the session.
- **Property State** (`property_state`, `select_one`, required/optional): Enter 'Yes' if the property has a state, or 'No' if it does not.
- **Property ZIP** (`property_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ZIP code of the property involved in the session.
