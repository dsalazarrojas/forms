# Property Owner Account Preferences - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps us understand your preferences for communication and contact with our team. Please fill out this form to ensure we provide you with the best possible services and communication that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the **Contact Details** section with your first name, last name, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select one or more **Communication Channels** that you'd like to use for communication, such as email, phone, or text.
3. If you have any additional comments or information, please add them in the **Notes** field.
4. Choose your **Preferred Language** for communication.
5. Select one or more **Contact Frequency** that suits your needs, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
6. Choose the **Email Frequency** for email communication, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
7. Select **Communication Preferences** to indicate whether you prefer to be contacted or not.
8. If you have more information or comments, you can add them in the **Notes 1**, **Notes 2**, or **Notes 3** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your first name, last name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Communication Channels (communication_channels)**: Choose one or more channels for communication, such as email, phone, or text.
* **Notes (notes)**: Additional information or comments about communication preferences or needs.
* **Preferred Language (preferred_language)**: Choose the preferred language to communicate with you.
* **Contact Frequency (contact_frequency)**: Select one or more frequencies for contact, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Email Frequency (email_frequency)**: Choose the frequency for email communication, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Communication Preferences (communication_preferences)**: Whether you prefer to be contacted or not.
* **Notes 1, 2, 3 (notes_1, notes_2, notes_3)**: More additional information or comments about communication preferences or needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we provide you with the best possible services and communication.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll review your preferences and ensure they're taken into account when communicating with you.
