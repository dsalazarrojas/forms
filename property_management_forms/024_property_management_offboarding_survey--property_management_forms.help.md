# property-management-offboarding-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the property owner or representative regarding the property's status and transition details during the offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the property's status and confirm that it has not already been offboarded or is still in the process of being offboarded.
2. Select the relevant option from the "property-management-questions" field to indicate the property's current status.
3. Enter the transition date for the property in the "transition-date" field.
4. Enter the transition time for the property in the "transition-time" field.
5. Add any additional comments about the property in the "comments" field.
6. Enter the email address of the property owner or representative in the "email-address" field.
7. Enter the primary phone number of the property owner or representative in the "contact-phone" field.
8. Enter the alternative phone number of the property owner or representative in the "contact-phone-alt" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property-management-form** (`property-management-form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **property-management-questions** (`property-management-questions`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options to indicate the property's current status:
	+ option-1
	+ option-2
	+ option-3
* **transition-date** (`transition-date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the property's transition.
* **transition-time** (`transition-time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the property's transition.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Add any additional comments about the property.
* **email-address** (`email-address`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the property owner or representative.
* **contact-phone** (`contact-phone`, text, not required): Enter the primary phone number of the property owner or representative.
* **contact-phone-alt** (`contact-phone-alt`, text, not required): Enter the alternative phone number of the property owner or representative.
