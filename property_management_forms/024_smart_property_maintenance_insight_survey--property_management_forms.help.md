# Smart Property Maintenance Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Smart Property Maintenance Insight Survey," is designed to collect valuable information from property owners or managers to gain insights into their maintenance concerns and satisfaction levels. This data will help us understand how we can improve property maintenance and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction, which provides a brief explanation of the purpose of this survey.
2. Select the maintenance concerns that currently exist at your property, or choose "Other" if none of the options match your concerns.
3. Indicate your level of satisfaction with recent maintenance activities.
4. Choose your preferred methods of communication, such as email, phone, or chat.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have a contact email or phone number, please enter it in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Maintenance Concerns** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Select all the maintenance concerns that currently exist at your property. These concerns might include:
	+ Leaks and Water Issues
	+ Pests
	+ Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field)
* **Satisfaction** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Indicate your level of satisfaction with recent maintenance activities.
* **Communication Preferences** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Choose your preferred methods of communication with our team.
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Chat
* **Comments** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them in this field.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: `false`): If you have a contact email, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have a contact phone number, please enter it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any further comments or concerns, please type them in this field.
