# Residential Building Management Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a tool for the property management team to gather feedback from residents and visitors on the overall management of the building. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the living experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions to provide a comprehensive feedback.
2. Ensure that you fill out the form once and do not repeat the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Residential Building** (`residential_building`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the residential building, including any notable issues or positive feedback.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all facilities that you found to be lacking or underperforming during your visit/stay. Choose as many as apply.
	+ Option 1: (example: Gym, Parking, etc.)
	+ Option 2: (example: Laundry, Pool, etc.)
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required: true): Choose the level of communication you experienced during your visit/stay.
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Needs Improvement
* **Safety** (`safety`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all safety concerns or issues you experienced during your visit/stay. Choose as many as apply.
	+ Option 1: (example: Lighting, Security, etc.)
	+ Option 2: (example: Fire Safety, Emergency Response, etc.)
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required: false): If you're providing feedback on a visit, enter the date you were at the building.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): If you're providing feedback on a visit, enter the time of visit.
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, text, required: false): If you're a resident, please enter your name for tracking purposes.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required: false): If you're providing feedback on a building, enter the name of the building you're referring to.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your experience.
* If you have any supporting documentation or photos, please attach them to your submission for further reference.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the property management team.
