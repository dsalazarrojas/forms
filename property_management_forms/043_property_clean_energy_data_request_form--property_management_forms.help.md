# Property Clean Energy Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property owners about their energy usage and clean energy goals. It is intended for internal use within the organization and will not be visible to the general public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the energy sources used by your property (electricity, gas, oil, coal, biomass, or other).
2. Enter the details of the energy sources you use.
3. Specify the energy use (e.g., for heating, lighting, etc.).
4. Enter the usage details.
5. Select the start and end dates for the energy usage data.
6. Enter the total energy usage.
7. Select the unit of measurement for the energy usage (kWh, MWh, or GWh).
8. Enter any additional notes or comments.
9. Provide your contact information (email, phone number, name, department, title, address, and company).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Energy Sources** (Select Multiple, required): Select all the energy sources used by your property.
	+ Options: Electricity, Gas, Oil, Coal, Biomass, Other
* **Other Energy Source** (Text, optional): If you selected "Other" as an energy source, please specify it here.
* **Energy Source Details** (Text, optional): Enter any additional details about the energy sources you use.
* **Energy Use** (Text, optional): Specify the type of energy use (e.g., heating, lighting, etc.).
* **Energy Use Details** (Text, optional): Enter any additional details about the energy use.
* **Usage Data** (Date, optional): Enter the start and end dates for the energy usage data.
* **Start Date** (Date, optional): Enter the start date for the energy usage data.
* **End Date** (Date, optional): Enter the end date for the energy usage data.
* **Total Energy** (Number, optional): Enter the total energy usage.
* **Unit of Measurement** (Select One, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the energy usage (kWh, MWh, or GWh).
* **Notes** (Text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Contact Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Name** (Text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Department** (Text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Contact Title** (Text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Contact Address** (Text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Contact Company** (Text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Notes** (Text, optional): Enter any additional contact notes.
* **Clean Energy Goals** (Text, optional): Specify your clean energy goals.
* **Clean Energy Status** (Select One, optional): Select the status of your clean energy goals (Active, Inactive, or In Progress).
* **Submit** (Text, optional): This is the final button to submit the form.
