# Property Management Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Inspection Checklist is a form used by property managers to conduct regular inspections of rental properties. It's a standardized way to identify any issues, note the property's status, and keep a record of repairs and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property's status from the "Property Status" field. This will help you determine which questions to answer based on the property's current state.
2. Enter the address of the property in the "Address" field.
3. Select the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
4. If there are any repair issues, describe them in the "Repair Issues" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the property in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter the name of the person who conducted the inspection in the "Manager Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Status** (`Property Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the property, which will help determine which questions to answer.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, date, optional): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Repair Issues** (`Repair Issues`, text, optional): Describe any repair issues or problems found during the inspection.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who conducted the inspection.
