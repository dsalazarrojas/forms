# Mountain Lodge Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mountain Lodge Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from guests interested in renting a mountain lodge. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences for a comfortable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Guest Information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and dates of stay (checkin date and checkout date).
3. Specify the number of adults, children, and rooms you require.
4. Indicate if you have any pets and what type (Dog, Cat, or None).
5. Mention any special requests you have.
6. Enter any additional message or comments you'd like to share.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions of the lodge by selecting "I Agree".
8. Sign and date your agreement.
9. Optionally, select any notes about your stay.
10. Choose a property manager to contact for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during your stay.
* **Guest Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates and information about your stay.
* **Guest Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Dates** (`dates`, date, required): Select the checkin and checkout dates of your stay.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required): Specify the date you will arrive.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Specify the date you will leave.
* **Adults** (`adults`, number, optional): Indicate the number of adults staying with you.
* **Children** (`children`, number, optional): Specify the number of children staying with you.
* **Rooms** (`rooms`, number, optional): Indicate the number of rooms you require.
* **Beds** (`beds`, number, optional): Specify the number of beds you need.
* **Pets** (`pets`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have any pets and what type (Dog, Cat, or None).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Mention any special requirements or requests you have.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or messages.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the lodge's terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your agreement with a printed or digital signature.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Date your agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, required): Specify the time you sign your agreement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any notes about your stay.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, select_one, optional): Select a property manager for further assistance.
