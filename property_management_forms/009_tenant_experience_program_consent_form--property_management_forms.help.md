# Tenant Experience Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent from tenants to participate in the Tenant Experience Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
4. Select the Program Start Date and Program End Date of your participation in the program.
5. Choose the Program Start Time and Program End Time of your participation in the program.
6. Read the Terms carefully and select "I agree to the terms" to indicate your consent.
7. Check the box to agree to the privacy policy.
8. Choose your consent status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_first_name`, text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_last_name`, text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Program Start Date** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_program_start_date`, date, required): Please select the start date of your participation in the program.
* **Program End Date** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_program_end_date`, date, required): Please select the end date of your participation in the program.
* **Program Start Time** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_program_start_time`, time, required): Please select the start time of your participation in the program.
* **Program End Time** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_program_end_time`, time, required): Please select the end time of your participation in the program.
* **Agree to Terms** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_agree_to_terms`, select_one, required): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_terms`, text): Please read the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Privacy** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_agree_to_privacy`, select_one): Please check the box to agree to the privacy policy.
* **Consent Status** (`tenant_experience_program_consent_form_consent_status`, select_one, required): Please choose your consent status to participate in the program.
