# Dairy Property Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dairy Property Lease Agreement is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a property lease between a landlord and a tenant. This form provides a comprehensive overview of the rental period, rental fees, and other essential details to ensure a clear understanding between the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the lease start date, which is the date the lease begins.
2. Enter the lease end date, which is the date the lease ends.
3. Specify the term length in years or months.
4. Enter the base rent, which is the initial rent to be paid by the tenant.
5. Select the rent increase frequency (e.g., annually, bi-annually, etc.).
6. Choose the lease type (e.g., month-to-month, year-to-year, etc.).
7. Add any additional lease terms or conditions.
8. Provide a property address and description.
9. Enter the owner's name, address, phone number, and email.
10. Enter the tenant's name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lease Start Date**: Enter the date the lease begins.
- **Lease End Date**: Enter the date the lease ends.
- **Term Length**: Specify the length of the lease in years or months.
- **Base Rent**: Enter the initial rent to be paid by the tenant.
- **Rent Increase Frequency**: Select how often the rent will be increased (e.g., annually, bi-annually, etc.).
- **Lease Type**: Choose the type of lease (e.g., month-to-month, year-to-year, etc.).
- **Lease Type Other**: If the lease type is not listed, enter a custom lease type.
- **Property Address**: Enter the property's address.
- **Property Description**: Add a brief description of the property.
- **Owner Name**: Enter the owner's name.
- **Owner Address**: Enter the owner's address.
- **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
- **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email.
- **Tenant Name**: Enter the tenant's name.
- **Tenant Phone**: Enter the tenant's phone number.
- **Tenant Email**: Enter the tenant's email.
- **Lease Terms**: Enter any additional lease terms or conditions.
- **Lease Conditions**: Add any special conditions or restrictions.
- **Lease Conditions Other**: If the conditions are not listed, enter a custom lease condition.
- **Additional Terms**: Add any additional terms or conditions not listed.
- **Termination Clause**: Specify the clause for termination of the lease.
- **Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for termination.
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date the lease is signed.
- **Signature**: Sign and date the lease.

Please review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form. If you are unsure about any field, consult the owner or tenant for clarification.
