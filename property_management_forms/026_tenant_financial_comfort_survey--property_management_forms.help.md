# Tenant Financial Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Financial Comfort Survey is a tool used to assess the financial comfort and stability of tenants in a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of 22 questions that ask about your current rent, payment habits, financial concerns, and safety nets.
2. Please answer each question honestly, as your responses will help identify areas where our services can better support you.
3. For numerical questions, please enter a dollar value or select one of the provided options.
4. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the rent you are currently paying?** (`question1`, number, required): Enter the current rent you pay for the property.
* **Is the rent too high or too low?** (`question2`, select_one, required): Select whether you think the rent is too high, too low, or if you are neutral.
* **Is the rent increasing or decreasing?** (`question3`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: increasing, decreasing, or remaining steady.
* **Rent payment date** (`question4`, date, required): Enter the date when you last paid your rent.
* **Rent payment time** (`question5`, time, required): Enter the time when you last paid your rent.
* **Are you financially comfortable about your rent?** (`question7`, select_one, required): Select whether you are very comfortable, somewhat comfortable, not very comfortable, or not comfortable at all.
* **Do you have a financial plan for rent increases?** (`question8`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a plan or do not have a plan.
* **Would you prefer to pay rent in installments?** (`question9`, select_one, required): Select whether you would prefer to pay in installments or not.
* **Have you ever experienced financial difficulties with rent?** (`question10`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: yes, no.
* **Do you have a guarantor?** (`question11`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a guarantor or do not have a guarantor.
* **Do you have a financial support system?** (`question12`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a financial support system or do not have a financial support system.
* **Do you have a rent savings plan?** (`question13`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a rent savings plan or do not have a rent savings plan.
* **Do you think you can cover your rent?** (`question14`, select_one, required): Select whether you can or cannot cover your rent.
* **Do you think you can pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month?** (`question15`, select_one, required): Select whether you can or cannot pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month.
* **Do you think 1-2 months rent is a good financial safety net?** (`question16`, select_one, required): Select whether 1-2 months rent is a good financial safety net or not.
* **Do you think 3-6 months rent is a good financial safety net?** (`question17`, select_one, required): Select whether 3-6 months rent is a good financial safety net or not.
* **Do you think 6-12 months rent is a good financial safety net?** (`question18`, select_one, required): Select whether 6-12 months rent is a good financial safety net or not.
* **Do you think 12 months+ rent is a good financial safety net?** (`question19`, select_one, required): Select whether 12 months+ rent is a good financial safety net or not.
* **Do you think you can afford to pay rent 6 months in advance?** (`question20`, select_one, required): Select whether you can or cannot afford to pay rent 6 months in advance.
* **Do you think you can pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month without financial difficulties?** (`question21`, select_one, required): Select whether you can or cannot pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month without financial difficulties.
* **Do you think 1-2 months rent is a good financial savings plan?** (`question22`, select_one, required): Select whether 1-2 months rent is a good financial savings plan or not.
* **Do you think 3-6 months rent is a good financial savings plan?** (`question23`, select_one, required): Select whether 3-6 months rent is a good financial savings plan or not.
* **Do you think 6-12 months rent is a good financial savings plan?** (`question24`, select_one, required): Select whether 6-12 months rent is a good financial savings plan or not.
* **Do you think you can pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month without financial difficulties?** (`question25`, select_one, required): Select whether you can or cannot pay 1-2 months rent in 1 month without financial difficulties.
