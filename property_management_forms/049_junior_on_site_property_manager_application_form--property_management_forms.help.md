# Junior On Site Property Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for junior on-site property managers to apply for various roles within the company. The form will help the internal team assess the applicant's qualifications and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the contact information page, which includes your name, email, and phone number.
2. Move on to the address page, where you will need to enter your physical address.
3. On the contact method page, select your preferred method of contact, whether it is email, phone, or mail.
4. On the availability page, select your availability in the format of dates.
5. Move on to the job preferences page, where you will need to select your job preferences in text form.
6. On the education page, choose your level of education.
7. On the work experience page, select your work experience in years.
8. On the job titles page, choose the job titles you are interested in.
9. On the work hours page, you will not need to fill this out unless you have a specific schedule.
10. On the job types page, choose where you prefer to work (onsite, offsite, or office).
11. On the work days page, choose the days of the week you are available to work.
12. On the job schedule page, choose your preferred work schedule.
13. On the availability pages, select your availability in the format of dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate.
* **Address** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address where you can be reached for work.
* **Contact Method** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred method of contact, whether it is email, phone, or mail.
* **Availability** (`4`, `number`, required): Select your availability in the format of years.
* **Job Preferences** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your job preferences in text form.
* **Education** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of education from the options provided.
* **Work Experience** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your work experience in years from the options provided.
* **Job Titles** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the job titles you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Work Hours** (`9`, `time`, required): This field is not required, but if you have a specific schedule, you can enter it here.
* **Job Types** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose where you prefer to work (onsite, offsite, or office).
* **Work Days** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the days of the week you are available to work.
