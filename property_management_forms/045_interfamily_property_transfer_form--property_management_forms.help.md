# Interfamily Property Transfer Form - Help Guide

# Purpose
The Interfamily Property Transfer Form is a tool to assist in the process of transferring ownership or rights of a property between family members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the "Document Type" from the dropdown menu. This will help determine the specific details required for the transfer.
2. Enter the "Transfer Date" and "Transfer Time" of the transfer. These fields are required to accurately record the date and time of the transfer.
3. Identify the "Signature Type" of the transfer, which could be E-Signature, Physical Signature, or Other.
4. Enter the "Transferor Signature" and "Transferee Signature" to acknowledge the agreement between the parties involved.
5. Describe the "Document Description" to provide context about the transfer.
6. Enter the "Transferor Name" and "Transferee Name" to identify the parties involved.
7. Enter the "Document Number" to uniquely identify the document.
8. Enter the "Document Version" (if applicable) to track changes or updates.
9. Enter the "Transfer Date 2" and "Transfer Time 2" to record the date and time of the transfer (if applicable).
10. Enter the "Document Status" to indicate the current state of the transfer (e.g., "In Progress," "Completed," etc.).
11. Select the "Transfer Method" from the options provided (e.g., Email, Hand-to-Hand, etc.).
12. Enter the "Transferor Email" and "Transferee Email" to ensure both parties are in communication.
13. Enter the "Transferor Phone" and "Transferee Phone" (if applicable).
14. Enter the "Transfer Date 3" and "Transfer Time 3" (if applicable).
15. Enter the "Transfer Date 4" (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_member_1**: Enter the name of the family member transferring the property.
* **family_member_2**: Enter the name of the family member receiving the property.
* **document_type**: Select the type of document that this transfer involves (e.g., Lease Agreement, Deed, Other).
* **transfer_date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **transfer_time**: Enter the time of the transfer.
* **signature_type**: Choose the type of signature involved (e.g., E-Signature, Physical Signature, Other).
* **transferor_signature**: Enter the signature of the transferor.
* **transferee_signature**: Enter the signature of the transferee.
* **document_description**: Describe the purpose and context of the transfer.
* **transferor_name**: Enter the name of the transferor.
* **transferee_name**: Enter the name of the transferee.
* **document_number**: Enter a unique identifier for the document.
* **document_version**: Enter the version of the document (if applicable).
* **transfer_date_2**: Enter the date of the second transfer (if applicable).
* **transfer_time_2**: Enter the time of the second transfer (if applicable).
* **document_status**: Enter the status of the document (e.g., In Progress, Completed, etc.).
* **document_type_2**: Select the type of document this transfer involves (if not already selected in "document_type").
* **transfer_method**: Choose the method of transfer (e.g., Email, Hand-to-Hand, Other).
* **transferor_email**: Enter the email of the transferor.
* **transferee_email**: Enter the email of the transferee.
* **transferor_phone**: Enter the phone number of the transferor (if applicable).
* **transferee_phone**: Enter the phone number of the transferee (if applicable).
* **transfer_date_3**: Enter the date of the third transfer (if applicable).
* **transfer_time_3**: Enter the time of the third transfer (if applicable).
* **transfer_date_4**: Enter the date of the fourth transfer (if applicable).
