# Housing Rehabilitation Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or families applying for the Housing Rehabilitation Program. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each page of the form thoroughly, providing all required information.
2. For the User Information page, enter your name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. On the Property Information page, provide specifics about the property you are applying for.
4. On the Household Details page, give details about the household members and their relationships.
5. Select your preferred method of contact on the Contact Method page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, Text, Optional): This page is where you can input your personal details such as your name and address.
* **Property Information** (Property Information, Text, Optional): Here, you can provide any relevant information about the property you are applying for. This may include its location, size, or other relevant features.
* **Household Details** (Household Details, Text, Optional): This page is for listing the household members and their relationships. Be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the available contact methods: phone, email, or in person. This will help us reach you with any further inquiries or updates about your application.
