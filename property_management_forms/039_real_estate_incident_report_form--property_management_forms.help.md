# Real Estate Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Incident Report Form is a tool used by real estate professionals to report and document incidents that occur in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Incident Date field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Select the appropriate Incident Type from the options provided.
3. In the Description field, provide a detailed description of what happened.
4. Enter the location of the incident in the Incident Location field.
5. Describe the incident details in the Incident Details field.
6. Identify the affected party in the Affected Party field.
7. Enter the incident resolution in the Incident Resolution field.
8. Enter the date the incident was resolved in the Incident Resolution Date field.
9. Fill in the Report By field with your name or title.
10. Enter the location where you reported the incident in the Report Location field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date (1)**: Enter the date the incident occurred. This field is not required.
* **Incident Type (2)**: Select the type of incident that occurred, such as damage, theft, or other events. This field is not required.
* **Description (3)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened. This field is not required.
* **Incident Location (4)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred. This field is not required.
* **Incident Details (5)**: Describe the incident details, including any specific circumstances surrounding the event. This field is not required.
* **Affected Party (6)**: Identify the party affected by the incident. This field is not required.
* **Incident Resolution (7)**: Enter the resolution or outcome of the incident. This field is not required.
* **Incident Resolution Date (8)**: Enter the date the incident was resolved. This field is not required.
* **Report By (9)**: Fill in your name or title as the person reporting the incident.
* **Report Location (10)**: Enter the location where you are reporting the incident. This field is not required.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the incident.
* Use the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* If a field is not relevant to the incident, you can leave it blank.
* Review and edit your form submissions before submitting for final review.
