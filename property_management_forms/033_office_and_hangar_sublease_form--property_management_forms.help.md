# Office And Hangar Sublease Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office And Hangar Sublease Form is designed to collect information for sublease applications and requests. This form is used to process sublease agreements between you and the landlord/owner of the office or hangar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Submit the form to the relevant parties for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office And Hangar Sublease Form** (`office_and_hangar_sublease_form`, note, required: false): This is the title of the form. It is not required to fill out.
* **Sublease Term** (`sublease_term`, text, required: false): This field is for the length of time you wish to sublease the office or hangar.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the sublease agreement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the sublease agreement.
* **Sublease Term Frequency** (`sublease_term_frequency`, text, required: false): This field is for the frequency of the sublease agreement (e.g., weekly, monthly, annually).
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, text, required: false): This field is for the notice period required before the end of the sublease agreement.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, text, required: false): This field is for the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, monthly, annually).
* **Sublease Application Status** (`sublease_application_status`, text, required: false): This field is for the status of your sublease application.
* **Sublease Request Comments** (`sublease_request_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments about your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request** (`sublease_request`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of sublease you are requesting (Sublease, Lease, Lease to own, or Rent).
* **Sublease Request Date** (`sublease_request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Frequency** (`sublease_request_frequency`, text, required: false): This field is for the frequency of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Period** (`sublease_request_period`, text, required: false): This field is for the period of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Status** (`sublease_request_status`, text, required: false): This field is for the status of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Comments** (`sublease_request_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments about your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Frequency Comments** (`sublease_request_frequency_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments about your sublease request frequency.
* **Approved** (`sublease_status_approved`, text, required: false): This field is for the status of your sublease application.
* **Sublease Request** (`sublease_request_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of sublease you are requesting (Sublease, Lease, Lease to own, or Rent).
* **Sublease Request Date** (`sublease_request_date_2`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Frequency** (`sublease_request_frequency_2`, text, required: false): This field is for the frequency of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Period** (`sublease_period_request_2`, text, required: false): This field is for the period of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Status** (`sublease_request_status_2`, text, required: false): This field is for the status of your sublease request.
* **Sublease Request Comments** (`sublease_request_comments_2`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments about your sublease request.
