# rental_property_management_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental property management permission form is used to grant permissions to individuals or companies to access and perform specific activities on rental properties. This form is essential for managing access to rental properties, ensuring that only authorized personnel can perform certain tasks or access specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **landlord_info** field with the relevant information about the landlord or property owner.
2. Provide details about the **property_details** in the field below.
3. Select one or more **management_activities** that you wish to grant permission for.
4. Enter any relevant information about the **tenant_info**.
5. If necessary, provide your **consent** to the granted permissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Info (landlord_info)**: 
    Enter the name and contact information of the property owner or landlord.
* **Property Details (property_details)**: 
    Provide a brief description of the property, including its address, location, or any other relevant information.
* **Management Activities (management_activities)**: 
    Select one or more activities that you wish to grant permission for:
    + **Property Visits**: permission to visit the property
    + **Document Access**: access to property documents
    + **Keycard Access**: permission to access the property keys
    + **Rent Payments**: permission to handle rent payments
    + **Maintenance Activities**: permission to perform maintenance tasks on the property
* **Tenant Info (tenant_info)**: 
    Enter any relevant information about the tenant or occupants of the property.
* **Consent (consent)**: 
    Provide your consent to grant the selected permissions to the individual or company.

## Tips

* Make sure to select only the necessary permissions for the specific task at hand.
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* This form is essential for maintaining secure and organized access to rental properties.
