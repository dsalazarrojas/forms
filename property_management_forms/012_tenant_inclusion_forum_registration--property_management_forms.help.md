# Tenant Inclusion Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a Tenant Inclusion Forum, a platform to provide accommodations and assistance to tenants with specific needs during events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with \*.
2. Ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
3. Review and verify your registration before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, \*): Enter your full name, as it appears on official records.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, \*): Enter your surname, as it appears on official records.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, \*): Enter your email address that can be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text): Enter a contact phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, date): Select the date you would like to attend the event.
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, time): Select the time you would like to attend the event.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`Accessibility Needs`, select_multiple, \*): Indicate if you need accessible parking, wheelchair accessibility, or other accommodations.
* **Accessibility Comments** (`Accessibility Comments`, text): Provide additional information about your accessibility needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, select_multiple, \*): Indicate if you have specific dietary needs or restrictions.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, select_multiple, \*): Indicate if you have any special requests, such as a specific seat or assistance.
* **Tenant Contact Form** (`Tenant Contact Form`, select_one): Confirm if you have already submitted a tenant contact form for this event.
* **Tenant Contact Form Comments** (`Tenant Contact Form Comments`, text): Add any additional comments about your tenant contact form.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date): Select the date of the event you would like to attend.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time): Select the time of the event you would like to attend.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Attendee Attendance** (`Attendee Attendance`, select_multiple, \*): Confirm if you will attend the event as an attendee.
* **Note** (`Note`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about your event attendance.
* **Email Sent** (`Email Sent`, select_one): Confirm if an email has been sent to you regarding the event.
* **Confirmation** (`Confirmation`, select_one, \*): Confirm if you have received a confirmation email about your event attendance.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, select_one): Confirm if you would like to be followed up with additional information.
* **Event Notes** (`Event Notes`, text): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Attendee Info** (`Attendee Info`, text): Add any additional information about the attendee.
* **Event Notes Attendee** (`Event Notes Attendee`, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text): Add any additional information that is not covered by other fields.

Note: The asterisk (\*) indicates a required field.
