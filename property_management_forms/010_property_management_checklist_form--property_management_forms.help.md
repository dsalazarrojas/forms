# Property Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and manage properties, ensuring that all necessary tasks and maintenance are up-to-date and completed on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Property Type** field with the type of property (e.g., Single Family Home, Multi Family Home, or Condo).
2. Enter the **Property Address** in the text field.
3. Select the **Maintenance Status** of the property (e.g., Good, Fair, or Needs Work).
4. Enter the **Last Inspection Date** if applicable.
5. Choose the **Next Inspection Date** if applicable.
6. Select the **Inspection Frequency** (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Annually).
7. Add any additional **Note** or comments about the property in the text field.
8. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact information for the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you are managing (e.g., Single Family Home, Multi Family Home, or Condo).
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the property, including street number and name.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the current status of the property's maintenance (e.g., Good, Fair, or Needs Work).
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection (if applicable).
* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspection Frequency**: Choose how often the property is inspected (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Annually).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Email**: Enter the email contact information for the property.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number contact information for the property.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY for dates).
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy.
* Save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.
