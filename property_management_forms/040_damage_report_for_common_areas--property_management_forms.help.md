# Damage Report For Common Areas - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report any damage that has occurred in common areas of the building, such as hallways, stairwells, or other public spaces. This form will help track and document all incidents to ensure that they are handled efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the common area has sustained any damage. If so, proceed with completing the form.
2. Answer the "Common Area" question by selecting "Yes" or "No".
3. Provide a brief description of the damage in the "Damage Description" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the damage in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Specify the location of the damage in the "Location" field.
6. (Optional) Attach any relevant photos of the damage to the "Damage Photo" field.
7. Identify who submitted this report in the "Submitted by" field.
8. Complete and submit the form to ensure that the report is saved.
