# Holiday Patrol Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Patrol Registration form is a tool used to record travel dates and details for holiday periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Double-check your entries for correctness and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Patrol Registration Form** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main title of the form, and you are expected to enter a brief description of the holiday patrol.
* **Travel Dates** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form_page_2`, time, required/optional): Select the start and end dates of your travel period.
* **Patrol Coverage Dates** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form_page_3`, select_one, required/optional): Answer whether your patrol will be covered or not during the holiday period.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you will be covering the patrol during the holiday period.
	+ Select 'No' if you will not be covering the patrol during the holiday period.
* **Patrol Details** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form_page_4`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief note about your patrol, including any relevant details or information.
* **Security Details** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form_page_5`, note, required/optional): Enter any security-related information or concerns about your patrol.
* **Additional Comments** (`holiday_patrol_registration_form_page_6`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your patrol.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team leader for guidance.
