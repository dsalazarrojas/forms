# Building Access Reconfiguration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from building occupants or tenants about access system changes that will affect their access rights. This form ensures that occupants are aware of and consent to the changes, providing a crucial step in the building's access reconfiguration process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, ensuring you understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer all required questions, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. For fields marked as optional, provide information if it applies to you.
4. If you have concerns or questions, type them in the "Comments or concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Occupant or tenant name**: Enter the name of the person or entity that will be affected by the access reconfiguration.
* **Unit or suite number**: Enter the number of the unit or suite where you live or work.
* **Building address**: Enter the address of the building where you reside or work.
* **Email address**: Enter your contact email address for communication updates.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number for communication updates.
* **Type of access reconfiguration**: Select the type of access reconfiguration that will be made.
* **Areas affected by reconfiguration**: Identify the areas of the building that will be affected by the reconfiguration.
* **Scope of reconfiguration**: Describe the scope of the access reconfiguration project.
* **Projected start date**: Enter the date when the reconfiguration is expected to start.
* **Projected completion date**: Enter the date when the reconfiguration is expected to be completed.
* **Expected disruption to occupants**: Indicate the expected duration of disruption to occupants.
* **Will emergency access be available during work?**: Confirm if emergency access will be available during the reconfiguration work.
* **Work hours**: Enter the expected work hours for the reconfiguration project.
* **Expected benefits of reconfiguration**: Select the benefits you expect from the access reconfiguration.
* **New system features and capabilities**: Describe the new features and capabilities of the access system.
* **Current access devices to be returned**: List the current access devices that need to be returned.
* **New equipment or cards to be issued**: Describe the new equipment or cards that will be issued.
* **Training required for new system**: Confirm if you will need training for the new access system.
* **Consent to access reconfiguration**: Confirm your consent to the access reconfiguration.
* **Comments or concerns**: Provide any comments or concerns you have regarding the access reconfiguration.
* **Alternative access arrangements**: Indicate if you require alternative access arrangements.
* **I confirm this information is accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any question, type "Not applicable" or "Unknown".
* If you have concerns or questions, type them in the "Comments or concerns" field.
* Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
