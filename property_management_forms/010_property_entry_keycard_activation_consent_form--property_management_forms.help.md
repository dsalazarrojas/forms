# Property Entry Keycard Activation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and obtain consent from the keycard holder for property entry keycard activation. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid errors in property entry keycard activation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Property Address to help us identify the correct property.
3. Enter a valid Email address for communication purposes.
4. Provide your Phone number for contact.
5. Select the correct Property Type (Residential, Commercial, or Mixed-Use).
6. Enter the Activation Date for the keycard.
7. Choose the correct Activation Time.
8. Write a brief note in the Consent field to indicate your understanding and agreement for keycard activation.
9. Sign the Signature field to confirm your identity and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property you are associated with.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Property Type**:
	+ **Residential**: For residential properties.
	+ **Commercial**: For commercial properties.
	+ **Mixed-Use**: For mixed-use properties.
* **Activation Date**: Enter the date you want to activate the keycard.
* **Activation Time**: Select the time of day you want to activate the keycard.
* **Consent**: Write a brief note to indicate your understanding and agreement for keycard activation.
* **Signature**: Sign and date your signature to confirm your identity and consent.
* **Signature**: Please print or sign your name to confirm your identity and consent.
