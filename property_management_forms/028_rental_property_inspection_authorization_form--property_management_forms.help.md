# rental_property_inspection_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental property inspection authorization form is used to obtain authorization from tenants for a rental property inspection. This form helps landlords to ensure that tenants are informed and agree to the inspections, which is essential for maintaining a fair and respectful relationship between landlords and tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the tenant's name in the "Tenant Name" field.
* Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
* Select the date of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" field.
* Choose the type of inspection to be performed from the "Type of Inspection" dropdown list.
* If desired, add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
* Sign and date the "Tenant Signature" and "Landlord Signature" fields to finalize the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the tenant's name as it appears on their rental contract or lease agreement.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the property address where the inspection will take place.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Select the date when the inspection will occur.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, optional): If desired, select the time of the inspection. This field is not required.
* **Type of Inspection** (`inspection_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of inspection to be performed (General, Move-in, Move-out, Quarterly, or Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the inspection.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to authorize the inspection.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm receipt of the tenant's authorization.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in the "Tenant Name" and "Property Address" fields.
* Choose the correct type of inspection from the "Type of Inspection" dropdown list.
* Ensure that both the tenant and landlord sign and date the form before proceeding with the inspection.
* Keep the "Notes" field for any additional comments or reminders related to the inspection.
