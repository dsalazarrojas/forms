# Property Tax Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Tax Billing Form is designed to collect information required for property tax billing purposes. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your property tax billing is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Property Owner field with the correct name of the property's owner.
2. Select the correct Property Type from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the Tax Amount in the format of a decimal number (e.g., 100.00).
4. Choose the Payment Method (Cash or Credit card).
5. (Optional) Enter any additional information or notes in the Note field.
6. Enter the Email address and Phone number of the person submitting the form (Submitter).
7. Select the Property Manager (if applicable) and Property Status (if applicable).
8. Fill out the Submitter Name, Email, and Phone number fields with the correct information.
9. Finally, review all fields to ensure they are complete and accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Tax Billing Form Form Info** (`property_tax_billing_form_form_info`, text, required): This field is a general description of the form and its purpose. Please fill it out with a brief description of the property tax billing form.
* **Property Owner** (`property_tax_billing_form_property_owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the property's owner.
* **Property Type** (`property_tax_billing_form_property_type`, select_one, required): Select the correct type of property from the dropdown menu.
* **Tax Amount** (`property_tax_billing_form_tax_amount`, number, required): Enter the tax amount in decimal format (e.g., 100.00).
* **Payment Method** (`property_tax_billing_form_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose between Cash or Credit card as the payment method.
* **Note** (`property_tax_billing_form_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or notes about the property tax billing.
* **Email** (`property_tax_billing_form_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`property_tax_billing_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`property_tax_billing_form_submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`property_tax_billing_form_submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submit Date/Time** (`property_tax_billing_form_submit_date_time`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Additional Info** (`property_tax_billing_form_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the property tax billing.
* **Submitter Name** (`property_tax_billing_form_submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`property_tax_billing_form_submitter_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone** (`property_tax_billing_form_submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Property Manager** (`property_tax_billing_form_property_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Status** (`property_tax_billing_form_property_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the property status (if applicable).
