# Property Value Study Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Value Study Participation Consent Form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a property value study. This form will help the researchers understand the participants' details, contact preferences, and other related matters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details, including your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
2. Give your consent for participating in the property value study by typing in your agreement in the designated field.
3. Indicate your contact preferences and the best method of contact.
4. Provide any additional comments or remarks related to your participation.
5. Sign the participant declaration, indicating that you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (1)**: Please enter your name and any other relevant participant details.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your email and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter any additional information that you would like to share with the researchers for contact purposes.
* **Participant Consent (4)**: Type in your agreement to participate in the study, indicating that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Contact Preferences (5)**: Indicate your preferred method of contact, such as phone or email.
* **Participant Conduct (6)**: Provide any additional information about your conduct during the study.
* **Participant Data (7)**: Enter any relevant data or information about yourself that is required for the study.
* **Contact Methods (8)**: List the methods of contact you would prefer, such as phone, email, or mail.
* **Participant Comments (9)**: Enter any comments or remarks related to your participation.
* **Participant Declaration (10)**: Sign this field to declare that you have read and understood the form.
* **Participant Signature (11)**: Sign your name to finalize your participation.
* **Participant Declaration Signature (12)**: Sign this field to confirm your signature in the participant declaration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the researchers for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary fields are completed.
