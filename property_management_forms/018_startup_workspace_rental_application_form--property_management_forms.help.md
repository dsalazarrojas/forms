# Startup Workspace Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help startups and entrepreneurs apply for workspace rental. It is intended for businesses looking to rent out a property for business use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your User Information (Field 1).
2. Share your Rental Preferences (Field 2).
3. Provide your Contact Information (Field 3).
4. Complete the Workspace Details (Field 4).
5. Submit your Submission Details (Field 5).
6. Sign the Agreement Form (Field 6).
7. Add a Workspace Image (Field 7).
8. Sign the Agreement (Field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (Field 1)**: 
	+ Label: User Information
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to provide your basic information such as name, title, and company or business name.
* **Rental Preferences (Field 2)**: 
	+ Label: Rental Preferences
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to share any preferences or requirements you have for the rental space, such as furniture or equipment needs.
* **Contact Information (Field 3)**: 
	+ Label: Contact Information
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to provide your contact information, including address, email, and phone number.
* **Workspace Details (Field 4)**: 
	+ Label: Workspace Details
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to describe the specific workspace you are looking to rent, including size and equipment needs.
* **Submission Details (Field 5)**: 
	+ Label: Submission Details
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to provide any additional details about your submission, such as any supporting documents or files.
* **Agreement Form (Field 6)**: 
	+ Label: Agreement Form
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Description: This field is used to confirm your agreement with the workspace rental terms.
* **Signature (Field 7)**: 
	+ Label: Signature
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
	+ Description: This field is used to digitally sign the rental agreement.
* **Workspace Image (Field 8)**: 
	+ Label: Workspace Image
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
	+ Description: This field is used to upload an image of the workspace you are renting.
* **Agreement Signature (Field 9)**: 
	+ Label: Agreement Signature
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
	+ Description: This field is used to confirm your signature on the rental agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use a clear and legible font for your signature fields.
* Ensure the workspace image you upload is clear and high-resolution.
