# parking_space_reallocation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parking_space_reallocation_consent_form is used to obtain consent and collect details for parking space reallocation within an organization. This form helps in ensuring that all necessary approvals and information are collected before making any changes to parking space allocations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary approvals and information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the User Details section with your name and any other relevant contact information.
3. Enter the parking_space_id of the parking space you want to reallocate.
4. If applicable, select 'Yes' in the parking_space_type field to indicate that the space is being reallocated.
5. Provide any relevant parking_space_details in the notes field.
6. Enter your Manager's details in the manager_details field.
7. Enter any relevant employee details in the employee_details field.
8. Enter your reason for reallocation in the reason field.
9. Leave any additional comments in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (1): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **parking_space_id** (2): Enter the ID of the parking space you want to reallocate.
* **new_parking_space_id** (3): Enter the ID of the new parking space you are allocating the space to.
* **date** (4): Enter the date of the reallocation (if applicable).
* **time** (5): Enter the time of the reallocation (if applicable).
* **parking_space_type** (6): Select 'Yes' if the space is being reallocated.
* **parking_space_details** (7): Enter any relevant details about the reallocation.
* **manager_details** (8): Enter your manager's name and contact information.
* **employee_details** (9): Enter any relevant employee details related to the reallocation.
* **reason** (10): Enter your reason for reallocation.
* **comments** (11): Leave any additional comments about the reallocation.
