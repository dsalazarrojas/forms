# property_stay_extension_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property_stay_extension_agreement_form is used for the property manager and property owner to agree on the terms of a stay extension, including check-out date, additional charges, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the form with the required information for your stay extension agreement.
3. Review the form to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
5. Return the completed form to the relevant parties involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_properties** (1): Enter a brief description of the property involved in the stay extension agreement.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_property_manager** (2): Enter your name as the property manager.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_check_out_date** (3): Select the date when the stay will be checked out.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges** (4): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether there are any additional charges related to the stay.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_property_landlord** (5): Enter the property owner's name.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_signature_property_manager** (6): Sign as the property manager, indicating your agreement with the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_signature_property_landlord** (7): Sign as the property owner, indicating your agreement with the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_date_of_extension** (8): Select the date of the stay extension agreement.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_date_of_check_out** (9): Select the date of check-out.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_agreement_term** (10): Enter a brief description of the agreement term.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges_amount** (11): Enter the total amount of additional charges.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges_description** (12): Describe the additional charges.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges_rate** (13): Enter the rate of additional charges.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges_tax** (14): Enter the tax amount for additional charges.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_total_amount_due** (15): Enter the total amount due.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_agreed_to** (16): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate agreement to the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_date_of_last_agreement** (17): Select the date of the last stay extension agreement.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_last_extension_date** (18): Select the last extension date.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_last_agreed_to** (19): Enter a brief description of the last agreed term.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_property_details** (20): Enter a brief description of the property involved in the stay extension agreement.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_agreed_to_date** (21): Select the date when you agreed to the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_last_agreed_to** (22): Enter a brief description of what was last agreed upon.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_agreed_to_terms** (23): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate agreement to the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_agree** (24): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate agreement to the terms.
* **property_stay_extension_agreement_form_additional_charges_total** (25): Enter the total amount of additional charges.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Ensure that both parties involved have read and agreed on the terms before signing the form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for reference.
