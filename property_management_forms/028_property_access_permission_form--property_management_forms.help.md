# Property Access Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Access Permission Form is designed to collect information about property access permissions. It is used to grant or deny access to individuals or entities for specific properties. The form is typically completed by property owners or administrators to manage access to their properties.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field as prompted.
2. Select the correct consent type from the dropdown menu for each property.
3. Enter the property inspection date if applicable.
4. Add any additional notes or comments as necessary.
5. Enter the email address of the individual or entity granting access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Info (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the tenant or individual granting access to the property.
    - **Required** (False)
- **Property Info (Text)**: Enter the address or identifier of the property granting access.
    - **Required** (False)
- **Inspector Info (Text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the inspector or individual granted access.
    - **Required** (False)
- **Consent Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of consent granted:
    - **Landlord**
    - **Agent**
    - **Viewer**
    - **Other**
    - **Required** (False)
- **Consent Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the consent, if applicable.
    - **Required** (False)
- **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding access permissions.
    - **Required** (False)
- **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the individual or entity granting access.
    - **Required** (False)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check the consent type and property information for correctness.
- Use the notes field for any additional information or comments.
- Enter a valid email address for the individual or entity granting access.
