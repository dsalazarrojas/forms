# Storage Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storage Service Registration form is used to register a storage facility or service provider for internal property management and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
3.  Use the help guide below to understand the purpose and requirements of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storage Service Registration**: Enter a brief description or name of the storage service.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the storage facility or service provider.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the physical address of the storage facility or service provider.
* **Move-in Date**: Enter the date the storage service became available or is scheduled to start.
* **Move-in Time**: Enter the time of day the storage service is available or scheduled to start.
* **Move-out Date**: Enter the date the storage service is planned to end or has ended.
* **Move-out Time**: Enter the time of day the storage service is planned to end or has ended.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the storage facility or service provider.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email address of the storage facility or service provider.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
