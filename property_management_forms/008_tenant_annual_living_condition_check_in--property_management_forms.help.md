# Tenant Annual Living Condition Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the annual living condition of tenants in a property. It gathers information on the maintenance priority, tenant status, and condition of the property, among other details. The form will help property managers and landlords assess the current living conditions of tenants and make informed decisions for future maintenance and upkeep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and determine which information applies to your property.
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menus or enter text in the text fields.
3. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field labels.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the property and tenant in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Priority** (`maintenance_priority`, select_one, not required): Select the priority level of the maintenance required for the property. Options are High, Medium, and Low.
* **Tenant Status** (`tenant_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select the current status of the tenant. Options are Active and Non-Active.
* **Priority Date** (`priority_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when the maintenance priority is expected to be completed.
* **Priority Time** (`priority_time`, time, not required): Enter the time when the maintenance priority is expected to be completed.
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`, select_one, required): Select the condition of the property. Options are Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Property Damage** (`property_damage`, select_multiple, not required): Select any existing property damage. Options are None, Minor, and Major.
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, not required): Enter the numerical priority level of the maintenance required.
* **Tenant Contact** (`tenant_contact`, text, not required): Enter the contact information of the tenant.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, text, not required): Enter the contact information of the property manager.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or notes about the property and tenant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the property manager.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Comment** (`property_manager_comment`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments from the property manager.

Note: This form is for information purposes only, and the information provided will not be used for marketing or solicitation purposes.
