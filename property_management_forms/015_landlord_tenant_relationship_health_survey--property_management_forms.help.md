# Landlord Tenant Relationship Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from tenants on their relationship health with their landlord or property manager. This will help landlords make informed decisions to improve their property management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and your tenant's name in the respective fields.
2. Choose how often you communicate with your landlord or property manager.
3. Select any issues that have arisen during your relationship.
4. Specify your lease start and end dates.
5. Answer any additional questions about your lease and property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Tenant Name** (2, text, required): Enter your tenant's name.
* **Communication Frequency** (3, select_multiple, not required): Choose how often you communicate with your landlord or property manager. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Responsiveness** (4, select_one, not required): Rate your landlord's or property manager's responsiveness. Options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Privacy Concerns** (5, text, not required): Please enter any privacy concerns you have.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (6, select_one, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your lease. Options: High, Low.
* **Communication Channels** (7, select_multiple, not required): Choose how you typically communicate with your landlord or property manager. Options: Email, Phone, Text Message.
* **Property Type** (8, text, not required): Enter your property type (e.g., apartment, house, etc.).
* **Property Age** (9, number, not required): Enter your property's age.
* **Property Location** (10, text, not required): Enter your property's location.
* **Issues with Landlord** (11, select_multiple, not required): Check any issues that have arisen during your relationship. Options: Rent/Security Deposits, Repairs/Improvements, Maintenance, Lease Renewal.
* **Issues with Tenant** (12, select_multiple, not required): Check any issues that have arisen during your relationship. Options: Non-Payment of Rent, Non-Payment of Utilities, Dispute over Lease End Date.
* **Lease End Date** (13, date, not required): Enter your lease end date.
* **Additional Communication Channels** (14, select_multiple, not required): Choose any additional channels you use to communicate. Options: In Person, Social Media, Mail.
* **More Communication** (15, select_multiple, not required): Choose how often you communicate with your landlord or property manager. Options: Often, Sometimes, Rarely.
* **Property Status** (16, text, not required): Enter your property's status (e.g., vacant, occupied, etc.).
* **Tenant Status** (17, text, not required): Enter your tenant's status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Landlord-Tenant Relationship Issues** (18, text, not required): Enter any relationship issues that have arisen.
* **Communication Frequency 3** (19, select_multiple, not required): Choose how often you communicate with your landlord or property manager. Options: Often, Sometimes, Rarely.
* **Lease Start Date** (20, date, not required): Enter your lease start date.
* **Landlord's Response Time** (21, text, not required): Enter your landlord's response time.
* **Tenant's Response Time** (22, text, not required): Enter your tenant's response time.
* **Property Use** (23, text, not required): Enter your property's use (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Lease Length** (24, text, not required): Enter your lease length.
* **Lease Type** (25, text, not required): Enter your lease type (e.g., month-to-month, fixed term, etc.).

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in answering the questions.
* You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your landlord or property manager.
