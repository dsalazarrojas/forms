# lease_renewal_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lease renewal event registration form is used to collect information about a property lease renewal event. This form helps event organizers to gather necessary details about the event, making it more efficient and organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event date.
2. Enter the guest names.
3. Provide your contact email.
4. Enter your contact phone number.
5. Enter any guest questions or comments.
6. Answer the question about your attendance (Yes, I'm coming, No, I'm not coming, or Maybe).
7. Add any additional comments or questions for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Guest Names**: Enter the names of the guests attending the event.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Guest Questions**: Enter any questions or comments from your guests.
* **Questions**: Enter any questions you have about the event.
* **Goals**: Enter any goals or objectives related to the event.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the event.
* **Questions 1**: Answer the question "Yes, I'm coming", "No, I'm not coming", or "Maybe".
* **Comments 1**: Enter any additional comments about your response to "Questions 1".
* **Questions 2**: Select multiple questions that apply to you (e.g., "What time is the event?", "What date is the event?", "How many guests will be attending?").
* **Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments about your selected questions in "Questions 2".
