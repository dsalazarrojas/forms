# Tenant Rules Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Rules Acknowledgment Form is used to acknowledge and document the rules and regulations of a property, including landlord name, property name, and tenant name. It is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring compliance with property rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Landlord Name: Enter the name of the landlord.
	* Property Name: Enter the name of the property.
	* Tenant Name: Enter the name of the tenant.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* Property Address: Enter the address of the property.
	* Property Type: Enter the type of property (e.g., apartment, house, etc.).
	* Property Rules: Select all applicable rules that apply to the property from the list provided (e.g., Rule 1, Rule 2, Rule 3, etc.).
	* Tenant Agreement Date: Enter the date of the tenant agreement.
	* Tenant Contact Information: Enter the contact information of the tenant.
	* Property Manager: Enter the name of the property manager.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person or company that owns the property.
* **Property Name (3)**: Enter the name of the property, such as an apartment or house number.
* **Tenant Name (4)**: Enter the name of the person or entity leasing the property.
* **Property Address (5)**: Enter the physical address of the property, including street number and name.
* **Property Type (6)**: Enter the type of property, such as apartment, house, etc.
* **Property Rules (7)**: Select all rules that apply to the property, such as:
	+ Rule 1
	+ Rule 2
	+ Rule 3
* **Tenant Agreement Date (8)**: Enter the date of the tenant agreement.
* **Tenant Contact Information (9)**: Enter the contact information of the person or entity leasing the property, such as phone number and email.
* **Property Manager (10)**: Enter the name of the person in charge of managing the property.

## Tips
Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly, and review the form before submitting to ensure completeness and accuracy.
