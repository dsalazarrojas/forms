# Property Manager Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property manager or real estate professional. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the individual's work experience, job description, and contact information, which will be used to facilitate effective property management and communication. Please ensure that you are the intended recipient of this form, as it is a sensitive and professional document.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Specify your years of experience in the Experience Year field.
5. Select your current work status (full-time, part-time, or contract) in the Work Status field.
6. Choose the times of the day you are available to work (morning, afternoon, evening, or weekends) in the Work Schedule field.
7. Identify your work experience as a landlord, property manager, or real estate agent in the Work Experience field.
8. Select the types of properties you are familiar with (single-family home, duplex, or triplex) in the Property Type field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field (optional).
10. Enter your date of hire in the Date of Hire field (optional).
11. Specify the size of the property you manage in the Property Size field.
12. Enter any additional information about the property in the Property Area field.
13. Enter your job title in the Job Title field (optional).
14. Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities in the Job Description field (optional).
15. Enter your work address in the Work Address field (optional).
16. Enter your job phone number in the Job Phone field (optional).
17. Enter your job email address in the Job Email field (optional).
18. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the Emergency Name field (optional).
19. Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person in the Emergency Number field (optional).
20. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the Emergency Email field (optional).
21. Enter the job phone number of the emergency contact person in the Emergency Job Phone field (optional).
22. Enter the job email address of the emergency contact person in the Emergency Job Email field (optional).
23. Add any additional notes or comments about your work experience and qualifications in the Notes field (optional).
