# Resident Park Improvement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Resident Park Improvement Request Form is designed for residents to submit requests for improvements or maintenance to their local park. This form is a straightforward way for residents to report issues or suggest changes to park amenities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the "park_location" field and enter the name or address of the park where the improvement request is intended.
3. In the "park_features" field, select any of the available options or type "Other" to specify a feature that is not listed.
4. Provide a detailed description of the requested improvement or maintenance in the "maintenance_request" field.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the "additional_comments" field.
6. Enter your contact information, including your name, in the "contact_info" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Park Location**: Enter the name or address of the park where you would like to request an improvement or maintenance.
  * This will help us to locate the issue and direct it to the correct team.
* **Park Features**: Select all features that you would like to see improved or maintained.
  * Select one or more options or type "Other" to specify a feature not listed.
* **Maintenance Request**: Describe the specific issue or improvement you would like to see made.
  * Please provide a detailed description of the problem or proposed change.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any extra information that might be helpful for us to understand your request.
  * This is a good place to include any supporting documentation or photos.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
  * This will help us to get back to you with updates or questions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * We will use this to contact you with updates or further questions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already reported this issue or submitted a similar improvement request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for clarification.
* We will review and address your request in a timely manner.
