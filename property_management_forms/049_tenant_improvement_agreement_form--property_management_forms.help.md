# Tenant Improvement Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and agree on the terms of a tenant improvement project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to understand the required information.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, taking care to include all necessary details.
3. Review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Improvement Details** (`tenant_improvement_details`, number, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the tenant improvement project, including the scope and objectives.
* **Tenant Responsibilities** (`tenant_responsibilities`, text, required: false): List the responsibilities of the tenant for the project.
* **Landlord Approvals** (`landlord_approvals`, select_multiple, required: false): Check 'Yes' if the landlord has approved the project; 'No' otherwise.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, note, required: false): Provide additional information about the property, if necessary.
* **Tenant Sign Off** (`tenant_sign_off`, date, required: false): Enter the date the tenant signs off on the project.
* **Landlord Sign Off** (`landlord_sign_off`, time, required: false): Enter the time the landlord signs off on the project.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of approval from the landlord.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is included.
- If there are any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant parties.
- This form should be kept up-to-date and reviewed periodically to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
