This form is called the "Property Upgrade Prioritization Survey". This form is used for the property owners to provide their input on the properties' upgrades that are needed. The purpose of this form is to help the management team to prioritize these upgrades based on the collected data.

## Property Upgrade Prioritization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Upgrade Prioritization Survey is used to gather information about the properties that require upgrades and prioritize them based on the collected data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for "Property Upgrade Type" to select the appropriate type of upgrade (Minor, Major, or both).
2. Check the current status of the property (Active, Pending, Inactive, or Incomplete).
3. Enter the priority score and estimated value of the upgrade.
4. Enter the estimated cost of the upgrade.
5. Add a description of the upgrade if necessary.
6. Choose the priority ranking of the upgrade.
7. Enter the contact person's name.
8. Enter the contact person's email address.
9. Enter the contact person's phone number.
10. Add any additional contact notes if necessary.
11. Enter any additional notes about the upgrade.
12. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property that requires an upgrade.
- **Property Upgrade Type** (`property_upgrade_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of upgrade that is needed (Minor, Major, or both).
- **Current Status** (`current_status`, select_multiple, required): Check the current status of the property (Active, Pending, Inactive, or Incomplete).
- **Priority Score** (`priority_score`, number, required): Enter a score between 1 and 5 that indicates the priority of the upgrade.
- **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number, required): Enter the estimated value of the upgrade.
- **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the upgrade.
- **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Add a description of the upgrade if necessary.
- **Priority Ranking** (`priority_ranking`, select_one, required): Choose the priority ranking of the upgrade (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the upgrade.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional contact notes if necessary.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the upgrade.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.
