# Move In Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Move In Checklist Form is a tool used to document the details of a move-in event for a property, including key dates, statuses, and comments from the tenant and property manager. This form is used to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of move-in activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a "Movein Date" from the calendar to document the date of the move-in event.
2. Choose the current "Property Status" of the property (Tenant or Vacant).
3. Select one or more "Lease Term" options to document the length of the lease.
4. Choose the "Rent Payment Frequency" options that apply to the property.
5. Mark the move-in as "Complete" or "Incomplete" based on the status.
6. Enter any "Movein Comments" to document the move-in process.
7. Provide the "Property Address" where the move-in took place.
8. Enter the "Tenant Name" associated with the move-in.
9. Enter any "Property Notes" relevant to the move-in event.
10. Enter the "Property Manager Name" responsible for the property.
11. Enter the "Property Manager Phone" and "Property Manager Email" for contact purposes.
12. Have the "Tenant Signature" for confirmation.
13. Enter the "Tenant Moveout Date" if the tenant has a move-out date scheduled.
14. Choose the "Tenant Status" (Active or Inactive) based on the move-in status.
15. Select whether you "Consent" to the move-in terms.
16. Document the "Property Condition" as observed during the move-in inspection.
17. Enter any "Tenant Movein Comments" relevant to the move-in event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Movein Date** (`tenant_movein_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the move-in event.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the property (Tenant or Vacant).
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more lease term options that apply to the property.
* **Rent Payment Frequency** (`rent_payment_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the rent payment frequency options that apply to the property.
* **Movein Status** (`movein_status`, select_one, required): Mark the move-in status as "Complete" or "Incomplete".
* **Movein Comments** (`movein_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments relevant to the move-in process.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Provide the address where the move-in took place.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant associated with the move-in.
* **Property Notes** (`property_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes relevant to the property.
* **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property manager responsible for the property.
* **Property Manager Phone** (`property_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Email** (`property_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the property manager.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required): Have the tenant sign for confirmation.
* **Tenant Moveout Date** (`tenant_moveout_date`, date, required): Enter the move-out date scheduled by the tenant, if applicable.
* **Tenant Status** (`tenant_status`, select_one, required): Choose the tenant status (Active or Inactive) based on the move-in status.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to the move-in terms.
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`, text, required): Document the condition of the property as observed during the move-in inspection.
* **Tenant Movein Comments** (`tenant_movein_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments relevant to the move-in event.

Note: Required fields are marked as "required" and must be filled out.
