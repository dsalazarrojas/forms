# Tenant Experience Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tenants about their experience with a property, including feedback and insights that can be used to improve management and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title and feedback in the "Tenant Experience Research Application Form" field.
2. Provide details about the property you visited, including its type and the date and time of your visit.
3. Select your experience level with the property (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. Choose the type(s) of property you visited (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
5. Indicate the purpose of your visit (Feedback, Research, Inspection).
6. Enter your contact information (Tenant Name, Contact Number, Email).
7. Add any additional comments you may have about your experience.
8. Finally, enter the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Experience Research Application Form (1)** (`Tenant Experience Research Application Form`, text, optional): Enter your title and feedback about your experience with the property.
* **Property Details (2)** (`Property Details`, text, optional): Provide details about the property you visited, including its type and any other relevant information.
* **Feedback (3)** (`Feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Tenant Experience (4)** (`Tenant Experience`, select_one, optional): Select your experience level with the property (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Property Type (5)** (`Property Type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type(s) of property you visited (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Date of Visit (6)** (`Date of Visit`, date, optional): Enter the date you visited the property.
* **Time of Visit (7)** (`Time of Visit`, time, optional): Enter the time you visited the property.
* **Research Purpose (8)** (`Research Purpose`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the purpose of your visit (Feedback, Research, Inspection).
* **Tenant Name (9)** (`Tenant Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the tenant who visited the property.
* **Contact Number (10)** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email (11)** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Comments (12)** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments you may have about your experience.
* **Date Submitted (13)** (`Date Submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* This form is for your convenience, and you can choose to complete it if you wish.
* If you have already provided feedback on the same property, please do not complete this form again.
