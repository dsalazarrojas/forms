# hvac_filter_replacement_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the consent of the resident for the replacement of the HVAC filter in their building or home. This form is used to ensure that the resident is aware of and agrees to the replacement process, which is a necessary step for the maintenance and upkeep of the HVAC system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with the resident's information, including their name, phone number, the date and time for the replacement, and the scheduled completion date.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **HVAC Filter Replacement Consent Form** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Fill out this field with a brief description of the form.
* **Resident Information** (`resident_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title or position of the resident, if applicable.
* **Name** (`resident_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resident's name.
* **Phone** (`resident_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the resident's phone number.
* **Filter Replacement Date** (`filter_replacement_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the filter replacement.
* **Time** (`filter_replacement_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time for the filter replacement.
* **Scheduled Completion Date** (`scheduled_completion_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the scheduled completion date for the filter replacement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand each field before filling out the form.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or maintenance team for assistance.
