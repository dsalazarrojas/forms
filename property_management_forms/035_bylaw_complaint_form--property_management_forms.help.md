# Bylaw Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report bylaw complaints in your neighborhood or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, address, and contact information in the fields marked as "required".
2. Choose the type of bylaw violation you are reporting (e.g. Property Maintenance, Noise Disturbance, etc.).
3. Describe the violation in detail, including any relevant dates and times.
4. Indicate whether you have contacted the property owner and how they responded.
5. If you have documented evidence (e.g. photos, videos), select the option indicating that you have evidence available.
6. If you are reporting an emergency, select "Yes, emergency" in the "Is This an Emergency" field.
7. Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Date of Complaint** (complaint_date): Enter the date you first observed the bylaw violation.
- **Complainant Name** (complainant_name): Enter your full name.
- **Your Address** (complainant_address): Enter your residential address.
- **Phone Number** (complainant_phone): Enter your contact number.
- **Email Address** (complainant_email): Enter your email (optional).
- **Can Your Name Be Public** (can_be_public): Select "True" if you want your name to be public, "False" if you want it to be kept private, or "Prefer Not to Say" if you don't want to disclose your name.
- **Location of Violation** (violation_location): Enter the address of the property where the violation occurred.
- **Type of Bylaw Violation** (violation_type): Select the category of the violation (e.g. Property Maintenance, Noise Disturbance, etc.).
- **Description of Violation** (violation_description): Provide a detailed explanation of the issue.
- **When Was Violation First Noticed** (violation_first_noticed): Enter the date you first observed the violation.
- **Is Violation Ongoing** (violation_ongoing): Select "Yes, ongoing" if the violation is recurring, "No, isolated incident" if it's a one-time issue, or "Intermittent" if it's an occasional issue.
- **Have You Complained Before** (previous_complaint): Select "True" if you have previously reported this issue, "False" if not, or "Not Sure" if you're unsure.
- **Property Owner Name** (property_owner): Enter the name of the property owner if you know it (optional).
- **Documented Evidence** (documented_evidence): Select "Yes, attached" if you have evidence available, "Yes, will provide" if you will provide evidence, or "No evidence" if you don't have any evidence.
- **Witnesses to Violation** (witnesses): Enter the names of people who can verify the violation (optional).
- **Have You Contacted Property Owner** (prior_contact_owner): Select "Yes, no response" if you have contacted the owner and they didn't respond, "Yes, they refused to fix" if they refused to resolve the issue, "No, did not attempt" if you haven't contacted them yet, or "Do not know owner" if you're unsure.
- **Requested Action** (requested_action): Provide a detailed explanation of what you would like to see happen to resolve the issue.
- **Is This an Emergency** (emergency_status): Select "Yes, emergency" if the issue is an immediate threat to health or safety, "No, not urgent" if it's not an emergency, or "Somewhat urgent" if it's a serious but non-emergency issue.
- **Additional Information** (additional_information): Provide any other relevant details or comments about the issue.
