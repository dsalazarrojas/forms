# Room Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Room Evaluation Checklist form is used to assess the condition of a room within a property and identify necessary maintenance or evaluation tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the room ID, which is a unique identifier for the room.
2. Provide the name of the room being evaluated.
3. Assess the condition of the room using the "condition" field. Select all applicable conditions (Good, Fair, Bad).
4. Add any additional comments about the room's condition.
5. Indicate when the room was last visited.
6. List any maintenance items needed for the room, such as Air conditioning, Bathroom fixtures, Electrical, Flooring, Painting, Plumbing, or Roofing.
7. Enter the date the form was completed.
8. Specify who completed the evaluation.
9. (Optional) Enter a date for when the evaluation was completed.
10. If follow-up is required, choose "True" and provide a reason for the follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **room_id** (`room_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the room.
* **room name** (`room name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the room being evaluated.
* **condition** (`condition`, select_multiple, optional): Assess the room's condition. Select all applicable conditions: Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the room's condition.
* **date visited** (`date visited`, date, optional): Enter the date the room was last visited.
* **maintenance items** (`maintenance items`, select_multiple, optional): List any maintenance items needed for the room. Select all applicable options: Air conditioning, Bathroom fixtures, Electrical, Flooring, Painting, Plumbing, or Roofing.
* **date completed** (`date completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was completed. This field is likely used for tracking purposes.
* **evaluated by** (`evaluated by`, text, optional): Specify who completed the evaluation.
* **evaluated at** (`evaluated at`, date, optional): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **follow up** (`follow up`, select_one, optional): Indicate if follow-up is required. Choose "True" for follow-up.
* **follow up reason** (`follow up reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the follow-up if "True" is selected.

Tips:

* Be thorough when assessing the room's condition and listing maintenance items.
* Ensure accuracy when entering dates and names.
* Use this form as a standard template for evaluating rooms within your property.
