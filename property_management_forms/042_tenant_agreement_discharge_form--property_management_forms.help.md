# Tenant Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharging of a tenant from a rental property. It is a critical step in the property management process, and it helps to track the status of agreements between landlords and tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the landlord's information in the "landlord" field.
2. Enter the date of discharge in the "discharge_date" field.
3. Enter the reasons for discharge in the "reasons_for_discharge" field.
4. Enter the tenant's signature in the "tenant_signature" field.
5. Enter the landlord's signature in the "landlord_signature" field.
6. Enter the landlord's name in the "landlord_name" field.
7. Enter the tenant's name in the "tenant_name" field.
8. Enter the lease term in the "lease_term" field.
9. Enter the discharge date in the "lease_end_date" field.
10. Enter the discharge amount in the "discharge_amount" field.
11. Enter the discharge funds in the "discharge_funds" field.
12. Select the notice period from the list of options in the "notice_period" field.
13. Select the status of the agreement from the list of options in the "agreement_status" field.
14. Select the reason for discharge from the list of options in the "reason_for_discharge" field.
15. Select the type of discharge from the list of options in the "discharge_type" field.
16. Select whether the tenant was discharged or not in the "tenant_discharged" field.
17. Enter any other reason for discharge in the "other_reason" field.
18. Enter details about the other reason for discharge in the "other_reason_details" field.
19. Enter any notes about the discharge in the "discharge_note" field.
20. Enter details about the discharge in the "discharge_note_details" field.
21. Enter the landlord's signature for the discharge note in the "discharge_note_signature" field.
22. Enter the date of the discharge note in the "discharge_note_date" field.
23. Enter the landlord's name for the discharge note in the "discharge_note_landlord_name" field.

- **landlord_name** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the landlord.
- **tenant_name** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the tenant.

- **discharge_funds** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the discharge funds.
- **notice_period** (`12`, `number`, optional): Select the notice period from the list of options.
- **agreement_status** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the agreement from the list of options.
- **reason_for_discharge** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reason for discharge from the list of options.
- **discharge_type** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of discharge from the list of options.
- **tenant_discharged** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether the tenant was discharged or not.
