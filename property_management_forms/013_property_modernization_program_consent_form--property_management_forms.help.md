# Property Modernization Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Modernization Program is a program aimed at providing support and resources to property owners to improve their properties. This form is used to collect information about the property's condition and the owner's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Complete the form accurately and honestly.
3. If you are unable to complete a field, please explain why in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details**: Please provide your name, address, and contact information for the property owner.
* **Property Info**: Describe your property, including its address, size, and type.
* **Improvement Needs**: Describe the improvements you need or would like to make to your property.
* **Signed Consent**: Please sign this form to indicate your consent to participate in the program.
* **Contact Info**: If you are unable to complete one or more fields, please provide a reason or a way for us to contact you.
* **Contact Method**: Select one or more ways you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or other).
* **Contact Other Method**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please specify your preferred contact method.
* **Contact Other Method**: Please describe your other contact method.
