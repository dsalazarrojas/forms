# Property Management Software Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and property data for property management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring you've entered all required and relevant information.
2. Enter your contact details, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your interested areas from the interested in field.
4. Indicate your lead source.
5. Choose your preferred contact method.
6. Enter your lead status.
7. Select property type and property address, city, and zip if relevant.
8. Enter property price, size, year built, and additional information if applicable.
9. Provide any additional property notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (2): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (4): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (5): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Interested In** (6): Select all interested areas that apply to you.
* **Lead Source** (7): Indicate how you came across this lead.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (9): Choose how you prefer to be contacted.
* **Lead Status** (10): Indicate the status of your lead.
* **Property Type** (23): Select the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Address** (12): Enter the property's address.
* **Property City** (13): Enter the city of the property.
* **Property State** (14): Select the state of the property.
* **Property Zip** (15): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Property Price** (16): Enter the price of the property.
* **Property Size** (17): Enter the size of the property.
* **Property Beds/Baths** (18): Select the number of beds and baths in the property.
* **Property Lot Size** (19): Enter the lot size of the property.
* **Property Year Built** (20): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Renovated Year** (21): Enter the year the property was renovated.
* **Property Age** (22): Enter the age of the property.
* **Type of Property** (23): Select the type of property (e.g., single-family home, etc.).
* **Type of Use** (24): Select the type of use for the property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Additional Property Info** (25): Enter any additional property information.
