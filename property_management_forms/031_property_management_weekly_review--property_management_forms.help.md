# Property Management Weekly Review - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Management Weekly Review form is designed to provide a structured platform for property management staff to record and track the progress of property-related activities on a weekly basis. This form helps to:

* Identify issues and defects that require attention
* Document inspection results and follow-up actions
* Monitor progress and assign tasks
* Record the status of properties

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of property being reviewed (HOA, Rental, or Lease)
2. Enter the date of the property inspection
3. Record the location of the property (Yes or No)
4. Provide inspection notes and comments
5. Document any issues or defects found during the inspection
6. Indicate the next actions to be taken
7. Specify the follow-up status (Follow up, Done, or Pending)
8. If necessary, indicate if the issue requires follow-up and set a date for follow-up
9. Assign a user to take the next action (Yes or No)
10. Confirm if the form has been submitted by a user (Yes or No)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property being reviewed (HOA, Rental, or Lease)
* **Property Inspection Date** (`property_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the property inspection
* **Property Location** (`property_location`): Indicate if the property is located inside or outside the management area (Yes or No)
* **Property Inspection Notes** (`property_inspection_notes`): Provide a brief description of the inspection process and any notable findings
* **Issues and Defects** (`issues_and_defects`): List any issues or defects identified during the inspection
* **Next Actions** (`next_actions`): Document the next steps to be taken on the property
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`): Indicate if the issue requires follow-up and select the status (Follow up, Done, or Pending)
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): If follow-up is required, enter the date for follow-up
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Select a user to take the next action (Yes or No)
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Confirm if the form has been submitted by a user (Yes or No)
