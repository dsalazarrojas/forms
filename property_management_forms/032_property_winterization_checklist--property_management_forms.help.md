# Property Winterization Checklist - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the seasonal inspection status for the property from the "seasonal_inspection" field.
2. Choose the current preparation status of the property from the "prep_status" field.
3. Select the current season from the "season" field.
4. Select the current status from the "status" field.
5. Enter the date of the inspection in the "date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Select the time of the inspection from the "time" field in 24-hour format.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person responsible for the property's maintenance in the "contact_person" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "phone_number" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the property's winterization process in the "notes_1" field.
10. Select the season for the next year from the "season_2" field.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the property's winterization process for the next year in the "notes_2" field.
12. Add any additional notes or comments about the property's winterization process in the "notes_3" field.
