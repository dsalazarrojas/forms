# Property Owner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Owner Information Form is used to collect relevant details about a property's owner, including contact information, property type, and management preferences. This information helps us understand the owner's needs and preferences, enabling us to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   * owner name
   * owner email
   * property address
   * property type
   * contact preference (select one: Email, Phone, or Text)

2. Fill in the optional fields:
   * owner phone
   * property year built
   * property size sqft
   * property management
   * property manager
   * property owner notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Owner Name** (text, required)
Enter the name of the property owner.

### 2. **Owner Email** (email, required)
Enter the property owner's email address.

### 3. **Owner Phone** (text, optional)
Enter the property owner's phone number (if applicable).

### 4. **Property Address** (text, required)
Enter the property's address.

### 5. **Property Type** (select_one, optional)
Select the type of property:
	* Property Management Company
	* Real Estate Company
	* Other

### 6. **Property Year Built** (number, optional)
Enter the year the property was built.

### 7. **Property Size Sqft** (number, optional)
Enter the property's size in square feet.

### 8. **Property Management** (select_multiple, optional)
Select the property management options:
	* Property Management Company
	* Real Estate Company
	* Other

### 9. **Property Manager** (text, optional)
Enter the name of the property manager.

### 10. **Property Owner Notes** (note, optional)
Add any additional information about the property owner.

### 11. **Contact Preference** (select_one, optional)
Select how the property owner would like to be contacted:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Text

### 12. **Property Size Sqft** (number, optional)
Please note that this field was incorrectly marked as required in the original YAML. It has been corrected to optional in the guide.

### 13. **Property Owner Notes** (note, optional)
Add any additional information about the property owner.
