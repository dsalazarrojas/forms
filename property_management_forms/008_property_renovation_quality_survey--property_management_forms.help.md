# Property Renovation Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from property owners or managers about the quality of work performed by contractors during property renovations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the relevant page and field to provide feedback.
* Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
* For select and multiple-choice fields, choose one or multiple options from the list.
* For text fields, enter your response in the provided text box.
* For date fields, select the date on which you submitted the feedback.
* For time fields, select the time at which you submitted the feedback.
* For submission location, select where the feedback was submitted (on site or off site).
* For notes fields, provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Renovation Quality (Page 1)
* **Contractor Performance**: Rate the contractor's performance as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Quality of Work**: Select one or multiple options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) that describe the quality of work.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided by the contractor.

### Page 2
* **Communication**: Enter any comments about how well the contractor communicated with you.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date on which you submitted the feedback.
* **Submission Time**: Select the time at which you submitted the feedback.
* **Submission Location**: Select where the feedback was submitted (on site or off site).
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Price**: Enter any comments about the price of the renovation.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value for money provided by the contractor.
* **Timeliness**: Rate the contractor's timeliness (On Time, Early, or Late).

### Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
