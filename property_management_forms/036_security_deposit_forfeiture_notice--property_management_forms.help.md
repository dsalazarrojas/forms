# Security Deposit Forfeiture Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the forfeiture of a security deposit for a rental property. It provides a formal notification to the tenant of the amount of the forfeited deposit and the reason for its forfeiture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **tenant's name** in the "Tenant's Name" field, which is the name of the person or entity to whom the notice is being addressed.
2.  Enter the **address** of the rental property in the "Address" field.
3.  Enter the **landlord's name** in the "Landlord's Name" field, which is the name of the person or entity responsible for the rental property.
4.  Enter the **deposit amount** in the "Deposit Amount" field, which is the amount of the security deposit being forfeited.
5.  Enter the **reason** for the forfeiture in the "Reason" field, which is a brief explanation of why the deposit is being forfeited.
6.  Enter the **date of forfeiture** in the "Date of Forfeiture" field, which is the date on which the deposit was forfeited.
7.  Enter the **date of return** in the "Date of Return" field, which is the date on which the tenant is expected to return the forfeited deposit.
8.  Enter the **notice date** in the "Notice Date" field, which is the date on which this form was issued.
9.  Sign and date the **Tenant's Acknowledgement** field, which is a confirmation that the notice has been received.
10. Sign and date the **Landlord's Signature** field, which is a confirmation that the notice has been issued.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Tenant's Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the person or entity to whom the notice is being addressed.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): This field is used to enter the address of the rental property.
*   **Landlord's Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the person or entity responsible for the rental property.
*   **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, required): This field is used to enter the amount of the security deposit being forfeited.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): This field is used to enter a brief explanation of why the deposit is being forfeited.
*   **Date of Forfeiture** (`date_of_forfeiture`, date, required): This field is used to enter the date on which the deposit was forfeited.
*   **Date of Return** (`date_of_return`, date, required): This field is used to enter the date on which the tenant is expected to return the forfeited deposit.
*   **Notice Date** (`notice_date`, date, required): This field is used to enter the date on which this form was issued.
*   **Tenant's Signature** (`signed_acknowledgement`, text, required): This field is used to confirm that the notice has been received.
*   **Landlord's Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required): This field is used to confirm that the notice has been issued.
