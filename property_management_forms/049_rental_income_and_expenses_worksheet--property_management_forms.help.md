# Rental Income And Expenses Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and calculate rental income and expenses for their property management activities. It provides a comprehensive worksheet to calculate total income, total expenses, net income, and other financial metrics related to rental properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of rental property from the options (Apartment Building, Condo, Duplex, or Single Family).
2. Choose the rental period for which you want to track income and expenses (January to December).
3. Enter the monthly income for the selected period.
4. Enter the total expenses for the selected period.
5. Enter the income tax for the selected period.
6. Enter the expense tax for the selected period.
7. Calculate the total income and total expenses for the selected period.
8. Calculate the net income for the selected period.
9. Enter any other income for the selected period.
10. Enter any other expenses for the selected period.
11. Calculate the total rent income and total rent expenses for the selected period.
12. Calculate the total other income and total other expenses for the selected period.
13. Calculate the net investment for the selected period.
14. Enter any other investment income for the selected period.
15. Enter any other investment expenses for the selected period.
16. Calculate the net other investment for the selected period.
17. Enter any notes or comments for the selected period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental properties** (id: 2, select_one): Choose the type of rental property from the options to track income and expenses for that property type.
* **Rental period** (id: 3, select_one): Select the rental period for which you want to track income and expenses (January to December).
* **Income** (id: 4, number): Enter the total monthly income for the selected period.
* **Expenses** (id: 5, number): Enter the total monthly expenses for the selected period.
* **Income tax** (id: 6, number): Enter the income tax for the selected period.
* **Expense tax** (id: 7, number): Enter the expense tax for the selected period.
* **Total income** (id: 8, number): The form will calculate the total monthly income for the selected period.
* **Total expenses** (id: 9, number): The form will calculate the total monthly expenses for the selected period.
* **Net income** (id: 10, number): The form will calculate the net income for the selected period.
* **Rent income** (id: 11, number): Enter the total rent income for the selected period.
* **Rent expenses** (id: 12, number): Enter the total rent expenses for the selected period.
* **Other income** (id: 13, number): Enter any other income for the selected period.
* **Other expenses** (id: 14, number): Enter any other expenses for the selected period.
* **Total rent income** (id: 15, number): The form will calculate the total rent income for the selected period.
* **Total rent expenses** (id: 16, number): The form will calculate the total rent expenses for the selected period.
* **Total other income** (id: 17, number): The form will calculate the total other income for the selected period.
* **Total other expenses** (id: 18, number): The form will calculate the total other expenses for the selected period.
* **Total investment income** (id: 19, number): The form will calculate the total investment income for the selected period.
* **Total investment expenses** (id: 20, number): The form will calculate the total investment expenses for the selected period.
* **Net investment** (id: 21, number): The form will calculate the net investment for the selected period.
* **Other investment income** (id: 22, number): Enter any other investment income for the selected period.
* **Other investment expenses** (id: 23, number): Enter any other investment expenses for the selected period.
* **Net other investment** (id: 24, number): The form will calculate the net other investment for the selected period.
* **Notes** (id: 25, text): Enter any notes or comments for the selected period.
