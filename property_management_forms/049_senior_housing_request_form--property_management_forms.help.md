# senior_housing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help senior citizens request assistance with their housing needs. It will collect information about their personal details, language preference, and housing requirements to ensure that they receive the appropriate support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact number, where you can be reached for further assistance.
3. Provide your email address so that we can contact you regarding your housing request.
4. If you have a preferred language, select it from the provided options.
5. Choose your preferred contact method for future communication.
6. Specify your housing needs, which can include emergency, permanent, or transitional housing.
7. Indicate the location where you would like to be housed.
8. If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, type them in the follow-up question field.
9. In the follow-up answer field, please provide a brief explanation of your follow-up question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further assistance.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address so that we can contact you.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, optional): Select the language you prefer to communicate in.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., English, Spanish, or other).
* **Housing Needs** (`housing_needs`, optional): Specify the type of housing you require (e.g., emergency, permanent, or transitional).
* **Housing Location** (`housing_location`, optional): Choose the location where you would like to be housed (e.g., North, South, East, or West).
* **Follow up Question** (`follow_up_question`, optional): If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, type them here.
* **Follow up Answer** (`follow_up_answer`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of your follow-up question.
