# Outdoor Storage Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by property managers to collect information from renters about their outdoor storage lease agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields:
	* Renter information
	* Storage unit info
	* Lease Terms
	* Property Manager
	* Renter Address
2. Fill out any additional fields you feel are relevant, such as:
	* Rental Agreement
	* Payment Terms
	* Renter signatures
	* Additional Terms
	* Notes
3. Make sure to review and understand all the terms before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter information** (renter_info, text, required): This field is for collecting the name and contact information of the renter.
* **Storage unit info** (storage_unit, text, required): This field is for the renter to provide information about the storage unit they are renting.
* **Lease Terms** (lease_terms, text, required): This field is for the renter to agree to the lease terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms, text, required): This field is for the renter to agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Rental Agreement** (rental_agreement, text, required): This field is for the renter to sign the rental agreement.
* **Property Manager** (property_manager, text, required): This field is for the property manager to sign and provide their contact information.
* **Additional Terms** (additional_terms, text, optional): This field is for any additional terms or conditions not specified in the lease agreement.
* **Renter Terms** (renter_terms, text, optional): This field is for the renter to provide any additional terms or conditions they want to add to the lease agreement.
* **Storage Facilities** (storage_facilities, text, optional): This field is for the renter to describe the storage facilities they will be using.
* **Renter Address** (renter_address, text, required): This field is for the renter to provide their address.
* **Property Info** (property_info, text, optional): This field is for the property manager to provide any additional information about the property.
* **Notes** (renter_notes, text, optional): This field is for the renter to provide any additional notes or comments about the lease agreement.
* **Renter Signatures** (renter_signatures, text, optional): This field is for the renter to sign the lease agreement and acknowledge the terms and conditions.
