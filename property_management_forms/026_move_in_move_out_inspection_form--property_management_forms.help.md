# move_in_move_out_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used during the move-in and move-out process to collect information about the current state of the property and the condition of any damages or defects found upon inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the property, including its address and any relevant contact details.
2. Provide information about the tenant, including their contact details and any relevant contact information.
3. Describe the current condition of the property, including any damages or defects found upon inspection.
4. Document the move-in and move-out dates and times.
5. Detail any property features or amenities that are relevant to the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)**: A text field for entering the property's address and any other relevant contact details.
* **Tenant Information (2)**: A text field for entering the tenant's contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Property Condition (3)**: A text field for describing the current condition of the property, including any damages or defects found upon inspection.
* **Move In Move Out (4)**: A text field for documenting the move-in and move-out dates and times.
* **Property Details (5)**: A text field for detailing any property features or amenities that are relevant to the inspection.
* **Damage and Defects (6)**: A text field for describing any damages or defects found upon inspection.
