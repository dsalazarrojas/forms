# Property Management Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Log is a form used to record and track property management activities. It is meant to be used by property management teams to maintain a log of entries related to property management activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **property_info** field with a brief description of the property being managed.
2. Enter the **date_logged** field with the date the activity was logged.
3. Enter the **date_completed** field with the date the activity was completed (if applicable).
4. Select the **priority** field to indicate the level of urgency associated with the activity (High, Low, Medium, or Urgent).
5. Select the **status** field to indicate the current status of the activity (Active, Resolved, Closed, or Waiting).
6. Enter the **action_required** field with any additional information or instructions.
7. Enter the **notes** field with any additional notes or comments.
8. Select the **follow_up** field to indicate if follow-up is required or not (Follow up required, Not required, or Follow up completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_info** (text): A brief description of the property being managed.
* **date_logged** (date): The date the activity was logged.
* **date_completed** (date): The date the activity was completed (if applicable).
* **priority** (Select One: High, Low, Medium, Urgent): The level of urgency associated with the activity (High, Low, Medium, or Urgent).
* **status** (Select One: Active, Resolved, Closed, Waiting): The current status of the activity (Active, Resolved, Closed, or Waiting).
* **action_required** (text): Any additional information or instructions.
* **notes** (Note): Additional notes or comments.
* **follow_up** (Select Multiple: Follow up required, Not required, Follow up completed): Whether follow-up is required or not (Follow up required, Not required, or Follow up completed).
