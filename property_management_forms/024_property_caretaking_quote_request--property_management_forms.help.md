# Property Caretaking Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Caretaking Quote Request form is used to request a quote for property caretaking services. It collects information about the property and the caretaker's contact details to provide a quote for maintenance and upkeep services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant contact type from the dropdown list (e.g., Caretaker, Property-Owner, or Property-Manager).
2. Enter the name of the caretaker responsible for the property.
3. Enter the quote amount.
4. Enter the contact phone number.
5. Select the time for the caretaker's rate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Required** (`property_caretaking_quote_request_required`): Select the relevant contact type for your property caretaking quote request. This field is required but has no visible impact on the form.
* **Caretaker Name** (`caretaker_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for maintaining and taking care of the property.
* **Caretaker Quote** (`caretaker_quote`): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the caretaking services.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your or the caretaker's contact phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Caretaker Rate** (`caretaker_rate`): Select the time it will take for the caretaker to complete the services, e.g., hours or days.

## Tips

* If you have already submitted a quote request for this property, please do not resubmit.
* Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information to get a reliable quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your contact phone number.
