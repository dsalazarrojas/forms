# Property Lifecycle Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Lifecycle Planning Survey form is used to collect data on the lifecycle of a property, helping the property management team to track and plan for properties based on their type, location, and other relevant attributes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type(s) of property that apply to you from the options provided.
2. Enter the location of the property.
3. Input the purchase price of the property.
4. Identify the seller of the property (yes/no).
5. Enter the details of the contract associated with the property.
6. Report the size of the property.
7. Select the type of property (residential, commercial, or industrial).
8. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Type of Property**: Choose all the types that apply to your property from the dropdown options (Yes, No).
	+ This question helps us understand the characteristics of your property for lifecycle planning.
* **Location**: Enter the location or address of the property.
	+ This field is used to pinpoint the property's location for planning and reporting purposes.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the purchase price of the property (in numeric value).
	+ This value will help us track the financial aspects of the property.
* **Seller**: Indicate if there is a seller associated with the property (True or False).
	+ This field helps us understand who the seller is, if applicable.
* **Contract**: Enter any details associated with the property contract (e.g., terms, conditions, or duration).
	+ This field helps us understand the contract conditions related to the property.
* **Property Size**: Report the size of the property (in numeric value).
	+ This field helps us track the size and capacity of the property.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property from the options provided (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
	+ This field helps us understand the property's usage and classification.
