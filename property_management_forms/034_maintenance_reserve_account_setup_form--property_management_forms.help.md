# Maintenance Reserve Account Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Reserve Account Setup Form is used to create a new maintenance reserve account for the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "formIds" field with a unique identifier for the new account.
2. Enter a descriptive title for the account in the "title" field.
3. Provide a category for the account in the "category" field.
4. Add a brief description of the account in the "description" field.
5. Enter the output file name and location in the "outputFile" field.
6. Identify the tool assigned to the account in the "assignedTool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **formIds**: (`formIds`, `number`, required: false) - Enter a unique identifier for the new maintenance reserve account.
* **title**: (`title`, `text`, required: false) - A descriptive title for the maintenance reserve account.
* **category**: (`category`, `text`, required: false) - The category for the maintenance reserve account.
* **description**: (`description`, `text`, required: false) - A brief description of the maintenance reserve account.
* **outputFile**: (`outputFile`, `text`, required: false) - The name and location of the output file for the account.
* **assignedTool**: (`assignedTool`, `text`, required: false) - The tool assigned to the maintenance reserve account.
