# property_leash_termination_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property_leash_termination_offboarding_form is used for terminating or offboarding a property lease. This form is used to document the reasons for the termination and gather information about the property, tenant, and lease terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Landlord Information section with the required details about the property and landlord.
2. Complete the Tenant Information section with the required details about the tenant.
3. Provide details about the Property Details in the respective section.
4. Document the Lease Terms and Conditions in the respective section.
5. Enter the Reason For Vacating in the Reason For Vacating section.
6. Enter the Notice Period in the Notice Period section.
7. Enter the Lease End Date and Tenant Move Out Date in the respective sections.
8. Provide the Contact Number and Email of the landlord or tenant.
9. Sign the form with a note to confirm agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Information** (Landlord Information, text, required): This section is for the landlord to provide their contact information and other relevant details.
* **Tenant Information** (tenant_information, text, required): This section is for the tenant to provide their information.
* **Property Details** (property_details, text, required): This section is for providing details about the property.
* **Lease Terms and Conditions** (lease_terms_and_conditions, text, required): This section is for documenting any conditions or terms of the lease that are relevant to the termination.
* **Reason For Vacating** (Reason For Vacating, text, required): This section is for the tenant or landlord to explain the reason for vacating the property.
* **Notice Period** (Notice Period, number, required): This section is for the number of days prior to the lease end date.
* **Lease End Date** (Lease End Date, date, required): This section is for the end date of the lease.
* **Tenant Move Out Date** (Tenant Move Out Date, date, required): This section is for the date the tenant moves out of the property.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): This section is for providing contact details of the landlord or tenant.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): This section is for providing email contact details of the landlord or tenant.
* **Tenant Signature** (Tenant Signature, note, required): This section is for the tenant to sign and date the form to confirm agreement.
* **Landlord Signature** (Landlord Signature, note, required): This section is for the landlord to sign and date the form to confirm agreement.

Please note that some fields have been left optional for further clarification on who should complete them. If in doubt, please ask your super user or refer back to the form purpose for clarity.
