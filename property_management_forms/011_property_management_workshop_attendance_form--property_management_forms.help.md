# property_management_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property_management_workshop_attendance_form is used to collect information about workshops or events and their attendees. This form helps staff or organizers gather basic details about the workshop, including its name, location, and schedule, as well as contact information for the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the workshop or event in the `workshop_name` field.
2. Provide the location of the workshop or event in the `location` field.
3. Select the date of the workshop or event from the calendar or enter it manually in the `date` field.
4. Choose the time of the workshop or event from the time selector or enter it manually in the `time` field.
5. Enter your name in the `name` field.
6. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
8. Review your information and click the `Submit Form` button to confirm and submit your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workshop_name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for the name of the workshop or event. Please enter the exact name of the workshop to help us track and identify it correctly.
* **location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the address or venue of the workshop or event.
* **date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the workshop or event from the calendar or enter it manually in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the workshop or event from the time selector or enter it manually in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Submit Form** (`Submit Form`, text, required/optional): Review and confirm your information before submitting the form. Click this button to complete and submit your attendance.
