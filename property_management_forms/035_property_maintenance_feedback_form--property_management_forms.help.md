# Property Maintenance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Maintenance Feedback Form is a tool used by property owners or managers to provide input on the condition and maintenance needs of a property. This form collects information on various aspects of the property, including its condition, maintenance needs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have filled out all required and optional fields.
3. Once you have completed the form, submit it to the designated authority for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_maintenance_form_1 (Text)**: A short description of the property.
* **property_maintenance_form_2 (Select One)**: Is the property currently maintained? (Answer: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_3 (Text)**: Description of any notable issues or concerns.
* **property_maintenance_form_4 (Text)**: Description of any planned maintenance or repairs.
* **property_maintenance_form_5 (Select One)**: Are there any ongoing maintenance issues? (Answer: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_6 (Select One)**: Are there any safety concerns? (Answer: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_7 (Text)**: Description of any maintenance schedules or frequency of maintenance.
* **property_maintenance_form_8 (Text)**: Description of any maintenance equipment or tools needed.
* **property_maintenance_form_9 (Email)**: Contact information for follow-up or communication.
* **property_maintenance_form_10 (Text)**: Additional comments or questions.
* **property_maintenance_form_11 (Select One)**: Are there any environmental concerns? (Answer: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_12 (Select Multiple)**: Are there any other concerns or issues with the property? (Select all that apply: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_13 (Date)**: Date of last maintenance or inspection.
* **property_maintenance_form_14 (Time)**: Scheduled time for next maintenance or inspection.
* **property_maintenance_form_15 (Select One)**: Is there a maintenance request for the property? (Answer: Yes or No)
* **property_maintenance_form_16 (Text)**: Description of any ongoing maintenance work or repairs.
* **property_maintenance_form_17 (Text)**: Description of any maintenance schedules or frequencies.
* **property_maintenance_form_18 (Note)**: Any additional comments or notes.
* **property_maintenance_form_19 (Text)**: Description of any other maintenance concerns.
* **property_maintenance_form_20 (Text)**: Additional comments or questions.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that the property maintenance team has the necessary information to address any issues or concerns.
