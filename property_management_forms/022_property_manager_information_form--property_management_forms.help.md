# Property Manager Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Manager Information Form is a tool for property managers to gather and store information about the properties they manage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through the multiple pages, filling in the relevant information as prompted. Start with the "First Page" (Page 1) and work your way through the form, providing details about the property and its associated contacts and visit schedules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one): Select the type of property you are managing (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
- **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the property (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Contact Numbers** (`contact_numbers`, select_multiple): Enter multiple contact numbers associated with the property.
- **Property Details** (`property_details`, text): Provide a brief description of the property.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the email address of the property manager or contact person.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter the phone number of the property manager or contact person.
- **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date): Record the date of the last visit to the property.
- **Time of Last Visit** (`time_of_last_visit`, time): Record the time of the last visit to the property.
- **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time): Record the time of the current visit to the property.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date): Record the date of the current visit to the property.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text): Enter the property's address.
- **Building Number** (`building_number`, text): Enter the building number or unit number.
- **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, text): Enter the name of the property manager.
- **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text): Enter the name of the tenant or occupant.
- **Tenant Contact Number** (`tenant_contact_number`, text): Enter the contact number of the tenant or occupant.
- **Property Notes** (`property_notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the property.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency of your visits to the property (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
