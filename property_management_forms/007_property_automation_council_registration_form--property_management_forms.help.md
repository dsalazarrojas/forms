# Property Automation Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Automation Council Registration Form is a form used to collect information from users registering with the Property Automation Council. This information is used to track and verify registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your First name, Last name, and Email.
2. Enter your Phone number for communication purposes.
3. Select your preferred Category from the dropdown menu.
4. Optionally, fill out the Company name if you have one.
5. Choose the Event type that you will be participating in (e.g., Membership, Conference, etc.).
6. Optionally, enter the tool assigned to you for the event.
7. Optionally, fill out the Form ID if provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to participate in the event or meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to participate in the event or meeting.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your registration.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category that best describes your role or interest:
	+ Property Management
	+ Real Estate Development
	+ Property Management Services
	+ Facility Management
	+ Property Maintenance
	+ Facilities Services
	+ Property Development
	+ Construction Management
	+ Landscaping
	+ Land Development
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Event** (`event`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of event you will be participating in:
	+ Membership
	+ Event
	+ Conference
	+ Meeting
	+ Workshop
	+ Seminar
	+ Training
	+ Symposium
	+ Summit
	+ Forum
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for the event.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the Form ID provided to you for tracking purposes.
