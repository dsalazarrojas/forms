# Property Damage Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare property damage, allowing users to report incidents of property damage, including the details of the damage, location, and contact information of the declarant and potentially the insurance provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact information of the declarant, including first and last name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide a detailed description of the property that has been damaged.
3. Select the type of property that was damaged from the provided options (e.g. Apartment, House, etc.).
4. Choose the location of the damaged property (e.g. Home, Office, etc.).
5. Describe the damage in detail.
6. Enter the date and time when the damage occurred.
7. Provide an estimated value of the property at the time of the damage.
8. If applicable, enter the contact information of the insurance provider, including their address and contact details.
9. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the declarant.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the declarant.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the declarant.
* **E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the declarant.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the declarant.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the declarant.
* **State** (`contact_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the declarant.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, required): Enter the ZIP code of the declarant.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property that was damaged from the provided options (e.g. Apartment, House, etc.).
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose the location of the damaged property from the provided options (e.g. Home, Office, etc.).
* **Describe Property Damage** (`property_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the property damage.
* **Date Damage Occurred** (`date_damage_occurred`, date, required): Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Time Damage Occurred** (`time_damage_occurred`, time, required): Enter the time when the damage occurred.
* **Describe Damage** (`damage_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the damage.
* **Estimated Property Value** (`property_damage_value`, number, required): Enter the estimated value of the property at the time of the damage.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Address** (`provider_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the insurance provider.
* **Contact Insurance Provider** (`contact_provider`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the insurance provider.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the claimant.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the claimant.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the claimant.
* **Claimant E-mail** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the claimant.

## Tips
Before submitting the form, please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of your damage declaration.
