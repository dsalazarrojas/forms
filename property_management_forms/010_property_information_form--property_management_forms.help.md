# Property Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a property, including its address, location, and owner details. The form is designed to be completed by property owners or managers to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property's address in the "Address" field.
2. Provide the property's location ID in the "Location ID" field.
3. Specify whether the property is located on a "Yes" or "No" location (e.g. on a street, in a building, etc.) in the "Location Name" field.
4. Select the type of address the property has (house, apartment, etc.) in the "Address Type" field.
5. Enter the building number of the property in the "Building Number" field.
6. Enter the street name of the property in the "Street" field.
7. Enter the city of the property in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state of the property in the "State" field.
9. Enter the postal code of the property in the "Postal Code" field.
10. Enter the property manager's name in the "Property Manager" field.
11. Enter the property owner's name in the "Property Owner" field.
12. Select the property's status (rent, sale, lease) in the "Property Status" field.
13. Select the type of property (single family, multi family) in the "Property Type" field.
14. Enter the year the property was built in the "Year Built" field.
15. Enter the square feet of the property in the "Square Feet" field.
16. Provide a brief description of the property in the "Property Description" field.
17. Upload a property image in the "Property Image" field.
18. Upload a property video in the "Property Video" field.
19. Enter the property's latitude in the "Latitude" field.
20. Enter the property's longitude in the "Longitude" field.
21. Enter the date the property was created in the "Created At" field.
22. Enter the date the property was last updated in the "Updated At" field.
23. Enter the date the property was deleted (if applicable) in the "Deleted At" field.
24. Enter the name of the user who created the property in the "Created By" field.
25. Enter the name of the user who last updated the property in the "Updated By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): The address of the property, including the street number and name.
* **Location ID** (`Location ID`, text, optional): A unique identifier for the location of the property.
* **Location Name** (`Location Name`, select_one, optional): Whether the property is located on a "Yes" or "No" location.
* **Address Type** (`Address Type`, select_multiple, optional): The type of address the property has (house, apartment, etc.).
* **Building Number** (`Building Number`, text, optional): The building number of the property.
* **Street** (`Street`, text, optional): The street name of the property.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): The city where the property is located.
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): The state where the property is located.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, optional): The postal code of the property.
* **Property Manager** (`Property Manager`, text, optional): The name of the person who manages the property.
* **Property Owner** (`Property Owner`, text, optional): The name of the person who owns the property.
* **Property Status** (`Property Status`, select_multiple, optional): The status of the property (rent, sale, lease).
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select_multiple, optional): The type of property (single family, multi family).
* **Year Built** (`Year Built`, number, optional): The year the property was built.
* **Square Feet** (`Square Feet`, number, optional): The square feet of the property.
* **Property Description** (`Property Description`, note, optional): A brief description of the property.
* **Property Image** (`Property Image`, text, optional): An image of the property.
* **Property Video** (`Property Video`, text, optional): A video of the property.
* **Latitude** (`Latitude`, number, optional): The latitude of the property.
* **Longitude** (`Longitude`, number, optional): The longitude of the property.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, date, optional): The date the property was created.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, date, optional): The date the property was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`Deleted At`, date, optional): The date the property was deleted (if applicable).
* **Created By** (`Created By`, text, optional): The name of the user who created the property.
* **Updated By** (`Updated By`, text, optional): The name of the user who last updated the property.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information about the property to ensure that it is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the correct address type and status to ensure that the property is classified correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact the property manager or owner for clarification.
* Remember to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that all the required information is provided.
