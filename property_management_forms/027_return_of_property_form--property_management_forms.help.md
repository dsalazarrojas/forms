# return_of_property_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the return of a property, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records for property management. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all required fields** (marked as "required" in the form) to ensure accurate documentation.
2. Follow the guidelines provided for each field below for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your property name?** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property being returned.
- **What is your property value?** (`property_value`, number, required): Enter the current value of the property.
- **Reason for return** (`reason_for_return`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the return of the property.
- **Date of return** (`date_of_return`, date, required): Select the date the property was returned.
- **Time of return** (`time_of_return`, time, required): Select the time the property was returned.
- **Return note** (`return_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the property return.
- **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information about the party returning the property.
- **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the party returning the property.
- **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the party returning the property.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the party returning the property.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a member of the property management team for assistance. 

This guide is intended to assist users in understanding each field's purpose and how to complete the form efficiently.
