# real_estate_listing_image_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Listing Image Upload Form is a tool used to collect and organize information about properties for real estate listings, including property details and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Real Estate Form Properties" page.
2. Enter the property's details in the "Property Details" field.
3. Upload property photos in the "Property Photos" field.
4. Upload property documents in the "Property Documents" field.
5. Provide additional information about the property in the "Real Estate Form Additional Info" field.
6. List the real estate agents involved in the property in the "Real Estate Form Agents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Form Properties** (`1`): Enter the property's general information, such as its name and address.
* **Property Details** (`2`): Enter the property's details, including its type (e.g., condo, townhouse, or house), number of bedrooms, number of bathrooms, and square footage.
* **Property Photos** (`3`): Upload photos of the property to showcase its appearance.
* **Property Documents** (`4`): Upload relevant documents such as property deeds, contracts, or other supporting documentation.
* **Real Estate Form Additional Info** (`5`): Provide any additional information about the property, such as its location, amenities, or special features.
* **Real Estate Form Agents** (`6`): List the names and contact information of the real estate agents involved in the property's sale or management.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and high-quality images for the property photos.
* Keep the "Property Documents" field up-to-date with the latest documentation.
* Use the "Real Estate Form Additional Info" field to provide any additional context or notes about the property.
* Verify the accuracy of the information in the "Real Estate Form Agents" field to ensure that it is correct and up-to-date.
