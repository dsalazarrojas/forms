# Property Asset Monitoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Asset Monitoring Registration Form is designed to capture key information about property assets, including their name, asset type, and location. This data is essential for effective asset management and tracking within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field to ensure accurate and comprehensive asset data.
2. Provide detailed and accurate information in all fields, as incomplete or inaccurate data can lead to errors in asset management.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Asset Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the property asset you are registering.
* **Asset Type** (2, select_one, required): Choose the correct type of asset from the provided options.
* **Property ID** (3, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the property.
* **Asset ID** (4, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the asset.
* **Asset Model** (5, select_one, optional): Select the model of the asset from the provided options.
* **Location** (6, text, required): Enter the exact location of the asset.
* **Asset Status** (7, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the asset from the provided options.
* **Asset Condition** (8, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the asset from the provided options.
* **Asset Notes** (9, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the asset.
* **Asset Email** (10, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the asset's owner or manager.
* **Asset Phone** (11, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the asset's owner or manager.
* **Asset Category** (12, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the asset from the provided options.
* **Asset Category ID** (13, number, optional): Enter the ID of the asset category.
* **Asset Serial Number** (14, text, required): Enter the serial number of the asset.
* **Asset Tag Number** (15, text, required): Enter the asset's tag number.
* **Asset Model Number** (16, text, optional): Enter the model number of the asset.
* **Asset Manufacturer** (17, text, optional): Enter the manufacturer of the asset.
* **Asset Model Year** (18, date, optional): Enter the model year of the asset.
* **Asset Purchase Date** (19, date, required): Enter the date of purchase for the asset.
* **Asset Cost** (20, number, required): Enter the original cost of the asset.
* **Asset Image** (21, text, optional): Upload an image or description of the asset.
* **Property Address** (22, text, required): Enter the address where the asset is located.
* **Property Manager** (23, text, optional): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Property Contact** (24, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the property manager.
* **Property Notes** (25, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
