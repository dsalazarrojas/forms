# property_technology_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for property technology partners to contact our team and inquire about our services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Partner Inquiries field, depending on whether you are contacting us for inquiries or not.
2. Enter the name of the contact person in the Contact Name field.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person in the Contact Email field.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the Contact Phone field.
5. Enter the name of the partner company in the Partner Company field.
6. Enter the address of the partner in the Partner Address field.
7. Enter the website of the partner in the Partner Website field.
8. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the PropTech Services field, depending on whether you are interested in our property technology services or not.
9. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Property Type field, depending on the type of property you are managing or interested in.
10. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Property Location field, depending on the location of the property you are managing or interested in.
11. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Property Management Software field, depending on whether you are using or interested in using property management software.
12. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Partner Industry field, depending on the industry your company belongs to.
13. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Inquiry Type field, depending on the type of inquiry you are making.
14. Enter any additional comments or questions in the Inquiry Comments field.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Property Tenant Inquiries field, depending on whether you are interested in our property tenant inquires services or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Inquiries** (`Partner Inquiries`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to indicate if you are contacting us for inquiries or not.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Partner Company** (`Partner Company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the partner company.
* **Partner Address** (`Partner Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the partner.
* **Partner Website** (`Partner Website`, text, required/optional): Enter the website of the partner.
* **PropTech Services** (`PropTech Services`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in our property technology services.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are managing or interested in a specific type of property.
* **Property Location** (`Property Location`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are managing or interested in a specific location of a property.
* **Property Management Software** (`Property Management Software`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are using or interested in using property management software.
* **Partner Industry** (`Partner Industry`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if your company belongs to a specific industry.
* **Inquiry Type** (`Inquiry Type`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are making a specific type of inquiry.
* **Inquiry Comments** (`Inquiry Comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Property Tenant Inquiries** (`Property Tenant Inquiries`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' for each type of property tenant inquiry you are interested in.
