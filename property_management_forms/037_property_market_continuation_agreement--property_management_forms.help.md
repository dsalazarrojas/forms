# Property Market Continuation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the continuation of a property market, ensuring that both the buyer and seller are on the same page and have agreed on the terms of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required information and documents before starting the form.
2. Enter the buyer's name, address, and contact information in the fields provided.
3. Enter the seller's name, address, and contact information in the fields provided.
4. Enter the purchase price and down payment in the fields provided.
5. Enter the property's address and type in the fields provided.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the property type field to indicate whether the property is a new or resale transaction.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed in the terms and conditions field.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name (Buyer Name)** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Name (Seller Name)** (`seller_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the seller.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the total amount of money paid for the property.
* **Down Payment** (`down_payment`, number, required): Enter the amount of money paid as down payment.
* **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the property sale. If no date is available, leave this field blank. Note: This field is only required if the closing date is applicable.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Buyer Agent** (`buyer_agent`, text, required): Enter the name of the buyer's agent.
* **Seller Agent** (`seller_agent`, text, required): Enter the name of the seller's agent.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" for a new property transaction or "No" for a resale transaction.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the terms and conditions of the transaction.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, text, required): Sign the form to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, text, required): Sign the form to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Date Signed Buyer** (`date_signed_buyer`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Signed Seller** (`date_signed_seller`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Signed Buyer Agent** (`date_signed_buyer_agent`, date, required): Enter the date the buyer's agent signed this form.
* **Date Signed Seller Agent** (`date_signed_seller_agent`, date, required): Enter the date the seller's agent signed this form.
* **Terms and Conditions Signed Buyer** (`terms_and_conditions_signed_buyer`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the terms and conditions of the transaction.
* **Terms and Conditions Signed Seller** (`terms_and_conditions_signed_seller`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the terms and conditions of the transaction.
