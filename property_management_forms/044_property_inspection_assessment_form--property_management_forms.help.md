# Property Inspection Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document inspection findings about a property. The information collected will help inform maintenance and repair decisions, ensuring that properties under care are well-maintained and safe for occupants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property address.
2. Provide a brief description of the property.
3. Identify the contact person for the property.
4. Specify the contact person's job title.
5. Select the type of property (e.g., Building, Land, or Other).
6. Evaluate the property condition (e.g., Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
7. Identify any existing conditions or issues with the property (e.g., water leak, electrical issue, or foundation problems).
8. Select a contact method (e.g., Phone, Email, or Other).
9. Provide the contact person's email address and/or phone number.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the property inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the physical address of the inspected property.
- **Property Details** (`property_details`): Provide a brief description of the property's characteristics.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Identify the person who can be contacted about the property.
- **Job Title** (`contact_person_job_title`): Provide the contact person's job title or position.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property (e.g., Building, Land, or Other).
- **Property Condition** (`property_condition`): Evaluate the property condition (e.g., Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
- **Condition Description** (`condition_description`): Select any existing conditions or issues with the property (e.g., water leak, electrical issue, or foundation problems).
- **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`): Record the date the inspection was completed.
- **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`): Identify the person who performed the inspection.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the property inspection.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select the method of contact (e.g., Phone, Email, or Other).
- **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Property Inspector Note** (`property_inspector_note`): Add any additional comments or notes from the inspector.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing property details and condition descriptions.
- Use the correct contact method and provide accurate contact information for the contact person.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
