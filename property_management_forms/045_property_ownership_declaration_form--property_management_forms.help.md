# Property Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Ownership Declaration Form is a document that confirms an individual's or entity's ownership of a property. This form is crucial for various official purposes such as tax returns, legal proceedings, and insurance claims.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill out the required fields with accurate information about the property and the owner.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding with the signature section.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Details**: Enter a brief description of the property, such as its address, location, or any unique identifiers.
*   **Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the property owner, as per official government documentation or records.
*   **Owner Address**: Enter the owner's residential or business address, which can be a street address, PO box, or similar.
*   **Owner Country**: Select the owner's country of residence or citizenship from the provided dropdown menu.
*   **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's contact phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address for official correspondence.
*   **Property Type**: Choose the type of property being declared (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Agricultural, or Recreational).
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the owner's date of birth as per official government documentation.
*   **Date of Acquisition**: Enter the date the property was acquired or transferred to the current owner.
*   **Date of Death**: Enter the date of the owner's passing, if applicable (if deceased).
*   **Last Update Date**: Enter the date of the last update or revision of this declaration.
*   **Owner Status**: Select the owner's current status (Active, Retired, Inactive, Deceased, or Unknown).
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or explanations, if necessary.
*   **Property Document**: Enter any relevant property documents or IDs, such as property deed or title.
*   **Witness Signature**: Sign the form, acknowledging that you're declaring the property ownership under your name.
*   **Witness Name**: Enter the witness's name, who can be a notary or an authorized representative.
*   **Witness Address**: Enter the witness's address, which can be a street address or a PO box.
*   **Witness Country**: Select the witness's country of residence or citizenship.
*   **Witness Phone**: Enter the witness's contact phone number.
*   **Witness Email**: Enter the witness's email address for official correspondence.
*   **Witness Status**: Select the witness's current status (Active, Retired, Inactive, Deceased, or Unknown).
*   **Date of Witness Signature**: Enter the date the witness signed the declaration.
*   **Date of Witness Last Update**: Enter the date of the last update or revision of this declaration.
*   **Declaration Signed By**: Enter your name, acknowledging that you're declaring the property ownership.
