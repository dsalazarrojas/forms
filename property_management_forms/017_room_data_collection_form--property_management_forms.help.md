# Room Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and update information about a room within a building, enabling property management teams to track and manage room details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the room whose details you want to update from the drop-down list or enter its Building ID and Campus ID.
2. Provide the room details, such as occupancy status, room size, type, and condition.
3. Upload photos of the room to help visualize its attributes.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Room Details** (`room_details`, text): Enter a brief description of the room, including its name, purpose, or any other relevant details.
* **Occupancy** (`occupancy`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if the room is occupied, 'No' if it's vacant or under renovation.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text): Upload multiple photos of the room to provide a visual representation of its condition, layout, and equipment.
* **Room Capacity** (`room_capacity`, number): Enter the maximum number of people that can be accommodated in the room.
* **Room Layout** (`room_layout`, text): Describe the room's layout, including its dimensions, furniture, and other notable features.
* **Room Equipment** (`room_equipment`, text): List the room's equipment, such as air conditioning, heating, or other appliances.
* **Room Notes** (`room_notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the room.
* **Room Status** (`room_status`, select_one): Select the room's status, such as 'Available', 'Occupied', or 'Under Renovation'.
* **Building ID** (`building_id`, text): Enter the building's ID associated with this room.
* **Campus ID** (`campus_id`, text): Enter the campus's ID associated with this room.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Describe the room's location within the building.
* **Room Size** (`room_size`, text): Enter the room's size in square meters or feet.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, text): Describe the room's purpose, such as office, classroom, or laboratory.
* **Room Condition** (`room_condition`, select_one): Select the room's condition, such as 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Photos 1-10** (`photos_1`, text): Upload up to 10 photos of the room to provide a visual representation of its condition, layout, and equipment.
