# Property Leverage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Leverage Form is a tool for property managers to collect necessary information from tenants. The form is designed to gather data on tenant interactions, property usage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and situation.
2. Provide accurate information for each field as required or necessary.
3. Review each field to ensure you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Visits** (`guest_visits`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable options for guest visits, such as family members or visitors.
* **Subleasing** (`subleasing`, select_one, false): Choose if you are subleasing the property, if not select "None".
* **Other Activities** (`other_activities`, select_multiple, false): Select any other activities that apply to you as a tenant.
* **Date** (`date`, date, false): Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, false): Enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Tenant** (`tenant`, text, false): Enter your full name or the name of the tenant if applicable.
* **Landlord** (`landlord`, text, false): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or details about the situation.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, select_one, true): Choose your tenant's signature option.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, false): Enter the date when you signed the form in YYYY-MM-DD format.
