# urban-housing-regeneration-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urban-housing-regeneration-application-form is designed to gather information from applicants about their housing regeneration needs, contact details, property specifics, and submission information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including any relevant details that you'd like to share about yourself.
2. Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you regarding your application.
3. Enter details about the property you're seeking to regenerate.
4. Share any additional application information you'd like to include.
5. If needed, submit any supporting documents for your application.
6. Finally, check the review status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): This section is for you to share any personal details you'd like to include about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional): Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Property Information** (property_info, text, optional): Share details about the property you're seeking to regenerate.
* **Application Information** (application_info, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your application.
* **Submission Information** (submission_info, text, optional): If needed, enter any submission information you'd like to provide.
* **Upload Documents** (upload_documents, text, optional): If applicable, upload any supporting documents related to your application.
* **Review Status** (review_status, text, optional): Check the review status of your application, if available.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the necessary fields to ensure your application is complete.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to your internal team or form owner for clarification.
* Please note that some fields are not required, but it's up to you to determine what information is best to share with us.
