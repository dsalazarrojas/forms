# Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form is a document that records a resident's agreement or consent to access certain amenities or facilities within their property management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Select the category of the amenity or facility you are requesting access to.
3. Enter a brief description of the amenity or facility.
4. Enter the title of the form (e.g., "Consent to Access Community Pool").
5. Select the date and time you wish to access the amenity or facility.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Category** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_category`): Select the category of the amenity or facility you are requesting access to. This field is required.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Description** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_description`): Enter a brief description of the amenity or facility you are requesting access to.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Title** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_title`): Enter the title of the form, such as "Consent to Access Community Pool".
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Date** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_date`): Select the date you wish to access the amenity or facility. This field is required.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Time** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_time`): Select the time you wish to access the amenity or facility. This field is not required.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Email** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_email`): Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Phone** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_phone`): Enter your phone number. This field is not required.
* **Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form Note** (`resident_amenity_access_tracking_consent_form_note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form before completing it.
* Review your information carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your property management team for assistance.
