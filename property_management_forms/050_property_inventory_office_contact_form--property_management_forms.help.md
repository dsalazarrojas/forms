# Property Inventory Office Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage property-related submissions, allowing the property management office to efficiently handle and track these submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the submission information, including your contact details and property details.
2. Enter accurate and complete information for each field, ensuring required fields are filled out completely.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your submission.
* **Tenant ID** (`tenant_id`, number, required): Enter the tenant ID number for the property in question.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide the contact phone number of the person submitting this form.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property in question.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required): Specify the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select one, required): Select the current status of the property (Active or Inactive).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the property or submission.
* **Documents** (`documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or supporting materials.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date the submission was made.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Select the time the submission was made.
* **Property Manager ID** (`property_manager_id`, number, optional): Enter the property manager ID (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the property manager (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review your submission before submitting it for review.
* Attach relevant documents to support your submission if necessary.
* Be clear and concise in your submission information.
