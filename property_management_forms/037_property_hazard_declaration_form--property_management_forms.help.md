# Property Hazard Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Hazard Declaration Form is a tool for reporting and documenting potential hazards on a property. This form is used to identify and document hazards, their severity and type, and the measures taken to control or mitigate them.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  The form is divided into 13 questions, each asking for a specific piece of information related to hazard declaration.
2.  The questions are grouped by category for clarity and ease of understanding.
3.  The form is designed to be filled out by one person per property.
4.  Please ensure that you enter accurate and truthful information in the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hazard Declaration** (`property_hazard_declaration`): Briefly describe the potential hazard or incident on the property.
* **Hazard Description** (`property_hazard_description`): Provide a detailed description of the hazard or incident.
* **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`): Select the severity of the hazard on a scale of High, Medium, or Low.
* **Potential Hazards** (`potential_hazards`): Choose all that apply from the list of potential hazards.
	+ Fall from heights
	+ Electrocution
	+ Fire
	+ Water
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`): Enter the date of the incident or the date the hazard was reported.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`): Enter the time of the incident or the time the hazard was reported.
* **Reporting Location** (`reporting_location`): Enter the location where the hazard was reported.
* **Hazard Level** (`hazard_level`): Describe the level of hazard (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Reporting Person** (`reporting_person`): Enter your name and title.
* **Reporting Person Contact** (`reporting_person_contact`): Enter your contact information.
* **Hazard Control Measures** (`hazard_control_measures`): Describe any control measures taken to mitigate or control the hazard.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or information related to the hazard.
