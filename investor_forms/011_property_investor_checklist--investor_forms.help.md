# Property Investor Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Investor Checklist is a form designed to gather information from investors about their investments in properties. This form is used to collect data on specific properties, including their location, type, and financial prospects. It is intended to help investors evaluate and compare different properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the text field "Investment Property" with the name of the property you are investing in.
2. Enter the location of the property in the text field "Location".
3. Select the property type by choosing one of the options in the "Property Type" dropdown menu. If the property type is not listed, please contact the administrator for assistance.
4. If applicable, provide a numerical assessment of the financial prospects of the property in the "Financial Prospects" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Property** (`investment_property`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property you are investing in. This could be a residential or commercial property, a real estate development, or any other type of investment property. Please provide the exact name of the property as it appears on public records.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property, including the city, state, or country where the property is situated.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property you are investing in. The options provided are examples of common property types, but if you have a specific type not listed, please contact the administrator for assistance.
* **Financial Prospects** (`financial_prospects`, number, optional): If you have a numerical assessment of the financial prospects of the property, please enter it here. This could be an estimate of potential returns on investment, growth rates, or other relevant financial metrics.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is intended for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional investment advice.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or have additional information to provide, please contact the administrator for assistance.
* This form is intended to be completed by investors who have a basic understanding of real estate investing.
* If you have any questions or need guidance on completing this form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
