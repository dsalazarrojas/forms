# property-investment-accelerator-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property-investment-accelerator-registration form is a crucial document that helps us understand your investment preferences and goals. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately, and our team will be in touch to discuss your investment acceleration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields as follows:
   - First name: Enter your first name.
   - Last name: Enter your last name.
   - Email: Enter your email address.
   - Phone number: Enter your phone number.
   - Experience level: Choose your level of experience (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
   - Investment goals: Select your primary investment goal (cash flow, capital appreciation, diversification).
   - Investment program: Provide details about your current investment program.
   - Investment program name: Enter the name of your investment program.
   - Investment objectives: Describe your investment objectives.
   - Investment strategy: Explain your investment strategy.
   - Investment experience: Enter your investment experience (date).
   - Coaching credentials: Select the coaching credential(s) relevant to you (real estate coaching, investment coaching, both).
   - Coaching hours: Enter the number of coaching hours you have completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Investment goals** (`investment_goal`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary investment goal (cash flow, capital appreciation, diversification).
* **Investment program** (`investment_program`, `text`, required): Provide details about your current investment program.
* **Investment program name** (`investment_program_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your investment program.
* **Investment objectives** (`investment_objectives`, `text`, required): Describe your investment objectives.
* **Investment strategy** (`investment_strategy`, `text`, required): Explain your investment strategy.
* **Investment experience** (`investment_experience`, `date`, required): Enter your investment experience (date).
* **Coaching credentials** (`coaching_credentials`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the coaching credential(s) relevant to you (real estate coaching, investment coaching, both).
* **Coaching hours** (`coaching_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of coaching hours you have completed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for your personal and investment purposes only.
