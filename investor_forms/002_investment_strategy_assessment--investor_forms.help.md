# Investment Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an investor's risk tolerance, investment goals, and other relevant factors to create a tailored investment strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete each section of the form carefully, answering each question to the best of your ability. You may skip questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Tolerance Level** (`client_risk_tolerance`, select_multiple): Select all the risk tolerance levels that apply to you. High risk tolerance means you're willing to take on more risk for potentially higher returns, while low risk tolerance means you're more conservative and want to minimize risk. You may select more than one level if applicable.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, text): Describe your investment goals, such as retirement, buying a house, or paying for education.
* **Investment Preferences** (`investment_preferences`, select_one): Indicate whether you prefer or dislike various investment types, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Risk Free** (`risk_free`, number): Enter the number of risk-free investments you have. This is the number of investments with no associated risk.
* **Risk Low** (`risk_low`, number): Enter the number of low-risk investments you have. This is the number of investments with a low level of risk.
* **Risk Medium** (`risk_medium`, number): Enter the number of medium-risk investments you have. This is the number of investments with a medium level of risk.
* **Risk High** (`risk_high`, number): Enter the number of high-risk investments you have. This is the number of investments with a high level of risk.
* **Risk None** (`risk_none`, number): Enter the number of investments with no associated risk.
* **Investment Time** (`investment_time`, date): Enter the date by which you plan to achieve your investment goals.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, number): Enter the amount you plan to invest.
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_one): Indicate whether you plan to invest frequently (Yes) or not (No).
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, select_one): Indicate whether you're interested in investing in various types, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, select_one): Indicate whether you're interested in earning returns on your investments.
* **Investment Currency** (`investment_currency`, select_one): Indicate whether you want to invest in various currencies, such as USD or foreign currencies.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one): Indicate your preferred risk level for your investments.
* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, select_one): Indicate the time horizon for your investments.
* **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, text): Describe your investment objective, such as long-term growth or income generation.
* **Investment Asset** (`investment_asset`, text): Enter any specific assets you're interested in investing in, such as real estate or stocks.
* **Investment Asset Type** (`investment_asset_type`, select_one): Indicate the type of investment asset you're interested in.
* **Investment Inflation Protection** (`investment_inflation_protection`, select_one): Indicate whether you want inflation protection in your investments.
* **Investment Tax Effect** (`investment_tax_effect`, select_one): Indicate whether you're concerned about tax effects in your investments.
* **Investment Return Target** (`investment_return_target`, number): Enter the return on investment you're targeting.
* **Investment Return Period** (`investment_return_period`, select_one): Indicate the period for your investment return target.
* **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_one): Indicate whether you plan to invest frequently.
