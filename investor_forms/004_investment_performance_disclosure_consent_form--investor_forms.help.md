# Investment Performance Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Performance Disclosure Consent Form is a mandatory document that aims to obtain your consent for the use of your contact information and the disclosure of your investment performance. This form is part of a larger process to ensure transparency and compliance in the investment industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the investment performance disclosure consent form is necessary for you.
2. Fill in your name and email address accurately.
3. Provide the date when you made the investment.
4. Choose the type of investment (Equity, Fixed Income, or Other).
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select a time that best describes when you made the investment.
7. Choose how you would describe your investment experience (Investment Beginner, Experienced Investor, Investment Professional).
8. Check 'Yes' to confirm you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
9. Check 'Yes' to consent to the use of your contact information for follow-up and communication.
10. If you have any further comments or questions, please fill in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is the investment performance disclosure consent form necessary for you, as an investor?** (`consent`, radio, required): This field is used to determine if you need to provide your investment performance data. If you select 'Yes', you will be required to fill out the rest of the form.
* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
* **When was your investment made?** (`investment_date`, date, required): The date you made your investment. Please select a date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **What type of investment is this for?** (`investment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of investment you made, which is either Equity, Fixed Income, or Other.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, number, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
* **What time did you make this investment?** (`investment_time`, time, required): Select a time that best describes when you made your investment. Please choose from the given options.
* **What is your investment experience level?** (`experience`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would describe your investment experience. You can select multiple options.
* **Have you read and understood the terms and conditions?** (`consent_terms`, radio, required): Please read and confirm that you have understood the terms and conditions.
* **Do you consent to the use of your contact information for follow-up and communication?** (`comment`, radio, required): This will grant permission for your contact information to be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Do you have any further comments or questions?** (`comment_2`, note, optional): This is an opportunity for you to provide any additional information or questions you may have.
