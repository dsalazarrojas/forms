# Investor Portfolio Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Portfolio Information Form is a collection of questions designed to gather essential information about your investment preferences, goals, and current situation. This form will help our investment advisors provide personalized recommendations and create a tailored investment plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email).
2. Specify your Portfolio Type (Individual, Joint, or Trust).
3. Indicate your Portfolio Size and Net Worth.
4. Select your Risk Tolerance (Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive).
5. Choose your Investment Objectives (Growth, Income, or Balance).
6. Select your Investment Period (Short-Term, Medium-Term, or Long-Term).
7. Indicate your Investment Horizon (a date, if applicable).
8. Specify your Investment Frequency (Regularly, Occasionally, or Rarely).
9. Choose your Financial Goals (Retirement, Wealth Building, or Cash Flow).
10. Briefly describe your Investment Experience.
11. Identify your primary Investment Assets (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, or Other).
12. Specify if this investment is Institutional (Individual, Joint, or Trust).
13. Enter any Tax ID (if applicable).
14. Provide your Address.
15. Enter your Phone number.
16. Enter your Fax number (if applicable).
17. Add any Investment Notes (if necessary).
18. List any Other Investments (if applicable).
19. Choose your Investment Type (Cash, Stock, Mutual Funds, Bond, Real Estate, or Other).
20. Select your Investment Allocation (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, or Other).
21. Choose your Investment Strategy (Active, Passive, Conservative, or Aggressive).
22. Indicate your Investment Frequency (a time interval, e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly).
22. Specify any Other Investment Information (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Provide your email address for communication and updates.
* **Portfolio Type** (select multiple, optional): Specify your portfolio type (Individual, Joint, or Trust).
* **Portfolio Size** (number, optional): Enter your portfolio size or the amount invested.
* **Net Worth** (number, optional): Provide your current net worth.
* **Risk Tolerance** (select one, optional): Choose your risk tolerance level (Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive).
* **Investment Objectives** (select multiple, optional): Select your primary investment objectives (Growth, Income, or Balance).
* **Investment Period** (select multiple, optional): Specify your investment period (Short-Term, Medium-Term, or Long-Term).
* **Investment Horizon** (date, optional): Enter a specific date for your investment horizon.
* **Investment Frequency** (select one, optional): Indicate how frequently you plan to invest (Regularly, Occasionally, or Rarely).
* **Financial Goals** (select multiple, optional): Choose your primary financial goals (Retirement, Wealth Building, or Cash Flow).
* **Investment Experience** (text, optional): Briefly describe your investment experience.
* **Investment Assets** (select one, optional): Identify your primary investment assets (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, or Other).
* **Institutional Investment** (select one, optional): Specify if this investment is Institutional (Individual, Joint, or Trust).
* **Tax ID** (text, optional): Enter any relevant tax ID number.
* **Address** (text, optional): Provide your home address for communication and mailings.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (text, optional): Enter your fax number (if applicable).
* **Investment Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional investment notes.
* **Other Investments** (text, optional): List any other investments you have (if applicable).
* **Investment Type** (select one, optional): Choose your investment type (Cash, Stock, Mutual Funds, Bond, Real Estate, or Other).
* **Investment Allocation** (select one, optional): Select your investment allocation (Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate, or Other).
* **Investment Strategy** (select one, optional): Choose your investment strategy (Active, Passive, Conservative, or Aggressive).
* **Investment Frequency** (time, optional): Enter the frequency of your investments (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly).
* **Other Investments** (text, optional): Add any other investment information (if necessary).
