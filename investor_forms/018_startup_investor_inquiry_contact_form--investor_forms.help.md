# Startup Investor Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Investor Inquiry Contact Form is a form designed to collect information from potential investors who are interested in collaborating or investing with the startup. This form allows us to gather contact information, understand how they found us, and what they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address. This will be used to contact you.
3. Enter your Phone number (optional).
4. Enter your Company name (optional).
5. Select how you heard about us. Choose from Social Media, Referral, Event, or Other.
6. Select the topic of your inquiry. Choose from Investment, Collaboration, Partnership, or Other.
7. Enter any additional message you'd like to share with us.
8. If you have a company, you may also enter your Company Name.
9. If you have a website, you may also enter your Website.
10. If you have a LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram account, you may enter the respective URL.
11. If you have an account on any other social media platform, you may enter the label and URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
