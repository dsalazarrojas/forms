# investment_bonds_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your investment goals, preferences, and expectations to provide personalized recommendations for investing in bonds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your investment goals for the next 6-12 months.
2. Choose your risk tolerance level, which is your comfort level with market fluctuations.
3. Indicate the amount of money you plan to invest in bonds.
4. Select your level of investment experience.
5. Choose the type of bond you are most interested in.
6. Indicate your current income level.
7. Specify how often you expect to invest in bonds.
8. Select your time horizon for investment in bonds.
9. Determine the bond size you plan to invest in.
10. Decide whether you prefer high-yield or low-yield bond options.
11. Choose your preferred bond term.
12. Identify your primary source of information for investment decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are your investment goals for the next 6-12 months? (`investment_goals`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to select the goals you have for your investments over the next 6-12 months. Please choose from the options provided.
* **What is your risk tolerance level? (`risk_tolerance`, select_one, required): This field is used to understand your comfort level with market fluctuations. Please select the level that best describes your risk tolerance.
* **How much money do you plan to invest in bonds? (`investment_amount`, select_one, required): This field asks for the amount of money you plan to invest in bonds. Please select from the provided options.
* **What is your investment experience level? (`investment_experience`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand your background in investing. Please select the option that best describes your experience level.
* **Which type of bond are you most interested in? (`bond_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to understand your preference for different types of bonds. Please select the one you're most interested in.
* **What is your current income level? (`income_level`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand your income level. Please select from the provided options.
* **How often do you expect to invest in bonds? (`investment_frequency`, select_one, required): This field is used to understand your investment frequency. Please select the option that best describes your investment schedule.
* **What is your time horizon for investment in bonds? (`time_horizon`, select_one, required): This field asks for the time period you're looking at for your investment. Please select from the provided options.
* **How much do you expect to invest in a single bond? (`bond_size`, select_one, required): This field is used to understand the size of your investment. Please select from the provided options.
* **Are you interested in investing in bonds with a high-yield or low-yield option? (`yield_preference`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand your preference for bond yields. Please select the option that best describes your preference.
* **What is your preferred bond term? (`term_preference`, select_one, required): This field asks for the term length of your preferred bond. Please select from the provided options.
* **What is your primary source of information for investment decisions? (`information_source`, select_one, required): This field is used to understand where you get your investment advice. Please select the option that best describes your source of information.
