# real_estate_investor_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate investors for onboarding purposes, ensuring that the data is accurate and up-to-date to facilitate a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*). For any optional fields, you can choose to provide as much or as little information as you like.
- Please fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the onboarding process goes smoothly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Investor Info** (`investor_info`, text, required): This field is for your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): This field is for your contact information, including your phone number, address, and any other relevant details.
- **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): This field is for your company's information, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.
- **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your investment strategy, including your goals, risk tolerance, and any other relevant information.
- **Target Properties** (`target_properties`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify the type of properties you're interested in investing in, such as residential, commercial, or industrial.
- **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, optional): This field is for you to rate your risk tolerance, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, number, optional): This field is for you to rate your risk score, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, number, optional): This field is for you to rate your financial status, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- **Portfolio Allocation** (`portfolio_allocation`, number, optional): This field is for you to specify the percentage allocation of your portfolio to different asset classes.
- **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, number, optional): This field is for you to specify the time frame for your investment.
- **Minimum Investment** (`minimum_investment`, number, optional): This field is for you to specify the minimum amount you're willing to invest.
- **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to select the financial institution you're using for your investment.
- **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select the type of assets you're interested in investing in, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
- **Target Assets** (`target_assets`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select the specific assets you're targeting for investment, such as specific stocks or real estate properties.
