# Investment Balance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Balance Confirmation Form is a tool used to document the current state of a client's investments, including the balance date, total investment, and cash balance. It is used by investment professionals to audit and review this information, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the date of the balance confirmation in the "Balance Date" field.
3. Enter the total investment amount in the "Total Investment" field.
4. Enter the current cash balance in the "Cash Balance" field.
5. Select the type of security position (Stock, Bond, ETF, Mutual Fund, or Other) in the "Security Positions" field.
6. Provide additional details about the security position in the "Security Position Details" field.
7. Enter the review date in the "Review Date" field.
8. Enter the audit date in the "Audit Date" field.
9. Select the auditor's title (Advisor, Accountant, or Other) in the "Auditor Signature" field.
10. Enter the auditor's name in the "Auditor Name" field.
11. Enter the auditor's company name in the "Auditor Company" field.
12. Enter the auditor's title in the "Auditor Title" field.
13. Select the review result (Pass, Fail, or Pending) in the "Review Result" field.
14. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
15. Enter the name of the person reviewing the form in the "Reviewed By" field.
16. Enter any notes from the auditor in the "Auditor Notes" field.
17. Enter the client's signature in the "Client Signature" field.
18. Enter an email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the client whose investment balance is being confirmed.
* **Balance Date** (Balance Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the balance confirmation.
* **Total Investment** (Total Investment, number, optional): Enter the current total investment amount.
* **Cash Balance** (Cash Balance, number, optional): Enter the current cash balance.
* **Security Positions** (Security Positions, select_one, optional): Select the type of security position (Stock, Bond, ETF, Mutual Fund, or Other).
* **Security Position Details** (Security Position Details, text, optional): Provide additional details about the security position.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Audit Date** (Audit Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Auditor Signature** (Auditor Signature, select_one, optional): Select the auditor's title (Advisor, Accountant, or Other).
* **Auditor Name** (Auditor Name, text, optional): Enter the auditor's name.
* **Auditor Company** (Auditor Company, text, optional): Enter the auditor's company name.
* **Auditor Title** (Auditor Title, text, optional): Enter the auditor's title.
* **Review Result** (Review Result, select_one, optional): Select the review result (Pass, Fail, or Pending).
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Reviewed By** (Reviewed By, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reviewing the form.
* **Auditor Notes** (Auditor Notes, text, optional): Enter any notes from the auditor.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, text, optional): Enter the client's signature.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter an email address (optional, but may be required in certain cases).
