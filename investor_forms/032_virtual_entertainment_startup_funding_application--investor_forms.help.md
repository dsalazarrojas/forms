# Virtual Entertainment Startup Funding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for virtual entertainment startups to apply for funding. It captures essential information about your company, including founder contact details, business information, and funding requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your founder's name, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Enter your company's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your company's address, website, and founding date.
4. Describe your company in the "Company Description" field.
5. Enter your funding requests for each type (01, 02, and 03).
6. Select your plan type from the options provided.
7. Enter your planned launch date and planned revenue.
8. Enter your planned expenses.
9. If applicable, provide a link to your pitch deck and a brief description.
10. Upload any additional pitch deck file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Name** (`founder_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the founder of your company.
* **Founder Email** (`founder_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the founder.
* **Founder Phone** (`founder_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the founder.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company's physical address.
* **Company Address 2** (`company_address2`, text, required): Enter any additional address information.
* **Company Address 3** (`company_address3`, text, required): Enter any further address information.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter your company's website.
* **Founding Date** (`company_founding_date`, date, required): Enter the date your company was founded.
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, text, required): Select the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your company.
* **Funding Request 01** (`funding_requests_01`, number, required): Enter the amount of funding you are requesting for type 01.
* **Funding Request 02** (`funding_requests_02`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for funding request 02.
* **Funding Request 03** (`funding_requests_03`, number, optional): Enter the amount of funding you are requesting for type 03.
* **Plan Type** (`plan_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of plan you are applying for.
* **Planned Launch Date** (`planned_launch_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to launch your product or service.
* **Planned Revenue** (`planned_revenue`, number, required): Enter your planned revenue.
* **Planned Expenses** (`planned_expenses`, number, required): Enter your planned expenses.
* **Pitch Deck Link** (`pitch_deck_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your pitch deck.
* **Pitch Deck Description** (`pitch_deck_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pitch deck.
* **Pitch Deck File** (`pitch_deck_file`, text, optional): Upload any additional pitch deck file.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled accurately to increase your chances of approval.
