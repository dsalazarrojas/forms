# Shareholder Financial Report Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Financial Report Voting Form is used for shareholders to submit their financial reports for review and voting. This form is crucial for the financial review process and ensures accurate and efficient voting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the reporting period for which you are submitting your financial report.
2. Enter your name and contact information for reference and verification.
3. Enter your company name and any other relevant details.
4. Provide a clear and concise financial report.
5. Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the financial report.
6. Enter your email address to be contacted for further discussion or verification.
7. Provide a file with your financial report, specifying the type, size, and name of the file.
8. Specify where the file is located and provide a brief description of the file.
9. Confirm that the file has been uploaded and specify its status.
10. Enter the date and time of submission.
11. Specify the status of your submission and provide any comments.
12. Enter the email address to whom the report should be directed and any email CC addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Selected Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, select_multiple, required): Select the quarter for which you are submitting your financial report. This will help the reviewers to focus on a specific period.
* **Shareholder Name** (`shareholder_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a shareholder for reference and verification purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name for identification and reference.
* **Financial Report** (`financial_report`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise financial report. This should include all relevant details and data.
* **Shareholder Vote** (`vote`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the financial report. This will help the reviewer to understand your stance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that you can be contacted for further discussion or verification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any additional contact.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission for accurate tracking and reference.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission for accurate tracking and reference.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Specify the status of your submission, whether it's In Progress, Completed, or Not Started.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required): Confirm that you have submitted the form accurately.
* **File** (`file`, text, required): Provide the file with your financial report.
* **File Type** (`file_type`, select_one, required): Specify the file type, such as Excel, PDF, or CSV.
* **File Size** (`file_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the file for accurate reference.
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the file for easy identification.
* **File Location** (`file_location`, text, required): Specify where the file is located.
* **File Description** (`file_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the file.
* **File Uploaded** (`file_uploaded`, select_one, required): Confirm that the file has been uploaded.
* **File Date** (`file_date`, date, required): Enter the date of file submission.
* **File Time** (`file_time`, time, required): Enter the time of file submission.
* **File Status** (`file_status`, select_one, required): Specify the status of the file, whether it's Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
* **File Comments** (`file_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the file.
* **Email to** (`email_to`, email, required): Enter the email address to whom the report should be directed.
* **Email CC** (`email_cc`, email, optional): Enter any email CC addresses for further reference.
