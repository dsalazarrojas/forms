# Clean Energy Investment Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from investors interested in clean energy investments. It collects contact and demographic details, investment preferences, and other relevant information to help us better understand your interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact information, and preferred contact method.
2. Select your investor type and other relevant information about your background.
3. Answer questions about your investment experience, preferences, and expectations.
4. Choose your areas of interest for clean energy sectors.
5. Indicate your preferred investment size and timeframe.
6. Choose your risk tolerance and preferred investment structure.
7. Select your preferred contact method and indicate your consent to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor Name** (`Investor Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name or organization name if applicable.
* **Investor Type** (`Investor Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Individual Investor, Family Office, Institutional Investor, Pension Fund, Endowment or Foundation, Corporate Investor, Investment Fund, Government Entity, Other.
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your organization if you are filling out on behalf of a company or entity.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this opportunity.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`Country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your residence or incorporation.
* **Investment Experience** (`Investment Experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Extensive, Moderate, Limited, None, or First time investor.
* **Areas of Investment Interest** (`Areas of Investment Interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all clean energy sectors of interest, such as Solar Energy, Wind Energy, etc.
* **Preferred Investment Size** (`Preferred Investment Size`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, $500,001 - $1,000,000, etc.
* **Investment Horizon** (`Investment Horizon`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Short-term - 1 to 3 years, Medium-term - 3 to 7 years, Long-term - 7 to 15 years, Very Long-term - 15+ years.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`Risk Tolerance`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive.
* **Preferred Investment Structure** (`Preferred Investment Structure`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose from: Equity Investment, Debt Investment, Convertible Notes, Revenue Sharing, etc.
* **Geographic Preference** (`Geographic Preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all regions of interest, such as North America, Europe, Asia Pacific, etc.
* **ESG Requirements** (`ESG Requirements`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes - Strict ESG criteria required, Yes - General ESG considerations, No - Focused primarily on financial returns.
* **Accredited Investor Status** (`Accredited Investor Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes - Accredited Investor, Yes - Qualified Purchaser, No - Retail Investor, Unsure.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`How Did You Hear About Us`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from: Website, Referral from Existing Investor, Industry Conference or Event, etc.
* **Additional Information Needed** (`Additional Information Needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all information you need before proceeding, such as Detailed Investment Prospectus, etc.
* **Timeline for Investment Decision** (`Timeline for Investment Decision`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Immediate, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, etc.
* **Questions or Comments** (`Questions or Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or additional information you would like to share.
* **Preferred Follow-Up Method** (`Preferred Follow-Up Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Email, Phone Call, Video Conference, In-Person Meeting, No follow-up needed at this time.
* **Consent to Contact** (`Consent to Contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes - I consent to be contacted, No - Do not contact me.
