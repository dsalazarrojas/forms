# eco-friendly-business-investor-poll-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help investors gather data on various aspects of a company's eco-friendliness and investment potential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company's name to help us identify your organization.
2. Select the answers that best fit your investment preferences.
3. Choose the impact questions that are most relevant to your investment strategy.
4. Select the drivers that influence your investment decisions.
5. Provide your preferred investment range and amount (if applicable).
6. Confirm or deny follow-up questions.
7. Add any follow-up comments.
8. Provide your contact information (if you'd like to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name to help us identify your organization.**
* **Investor Answers (`investor_answers`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the answers that best fit your investment preferences.**
* **Impact Questions (`impact_questions`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the impact questions that are most relevant to your investment strategy.**
* **Decision Drivers (`decision_drivers`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the drivers that influence your investment decisions.**
* **Investment Range (`investment_range`, select_one, required/optional): Provide your preferred investment range (Low, Medium, High).**
* **Investment Amount (`investment_amount`, number, required/optional): Provide your preferred investment amount.**
* **Follow Up Questions (`follow_up_questions`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm or deny follow-up questions.**
* **Follow Up Comments (`follow_up_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any follow-up comments.**
* **Contact Information (`contact_information`, note, required/optional): Provide your contact information (if you'd like to be contacted).**
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments.**
* **Email (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address.**
* **Phone (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number.**
* **Company Email (`company_email`, email, required/optional): Provide your company's email address.**
* **Company URL (`company_url`, text, required/optional): Provide your company's URL.**
* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, required/optional): This is a redundant field and can be ignored.**
