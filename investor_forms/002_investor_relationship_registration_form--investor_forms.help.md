# Investor Relationship Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Investor Relationship Registration Form is used to register and track investor relationships. This form helps to gather essential information about investors, including their investment instruments and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Investment Instruments
Choose all the investment instruments that apply to your relationship with this investor. You can select multiple instruments from the list.

### Step 2: Enter Investor's Name
Enter the first and last name of the investor. This information will help us to identify them and communicate with them effectively.

### Step 3: Choose Investment Instrument
Select the specific investment instrument that this relationship is related to. This will help us to categorize and manage investor relationships properly.

### Step 4: Confirm or Refuse Investor Relationship
Indicate whether this investor relationship exists or not. If it does, select "Yes". If not, select "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **investment_instruments** (Select Multiple): Choose all the investment instruments that apply to your relationship with this investor.
* **first_name** (Text): Enter the first name of the investor.
* **last_name** (Text): Enter the last name of the investor.
* **investment_instrument** (Select One): Select the specific investment instrument that this relationship is related to.
* **prophet** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether this investor relationship is a "Yes" or "No" (confirm or refute).
