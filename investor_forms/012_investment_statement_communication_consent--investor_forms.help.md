# Investment Statement Communication Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your investment statement communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Describe your investment statement in the "Investment Statement" field.
3. Select the frequency at which you would like to receive investment statements ("Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly").
4. Choose your preferred method of contact ("Phone", "Email", or "Text").
5. Select whether you would like to receive email notifications for new investment statements ("True" or "False").
6. If desired, you can also specify a second contact method or phone number.
7. Optionally, you can choose to receive email content ("True" or "False") if you select email as your contact method.
8. Enter any additional contact information you may want to provide.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Investment Statement**: Describe the investment you are making or interested in, including any relevant details such as amount, date, and expected return.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Statement Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive investment statements:
	+ **Daily**: Receive a daily investment statement.
	+ **Weekly**: Receive a weekly investment statement.
	+ **Monthly**: Receive a monthly investment statement.
* **Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact:
	+ **Phone**: Receive investment statements through phone calls.
	+ **Email**: Receive investment statements via email.
	+ **Text**: Receive investment statements via text message.
* **Email Notification**: Choose whether you would like to receive email notifications for new investment statements:
	+ **True**: Receive email notifications for new investment statements.
	+ **False**: Do not receive email notifications for new investment statements.
* **Email Content**: Choose whether you would like to receive email content for new investment statements:
	+ **True**: Receive email content for new investment statements.
	+ **False**: Do not receive email content for new investment statements.
* **Contact Info 2**: Enter any additional contact information you may want to provide.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* You can always adjust your communication preferences at any time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
