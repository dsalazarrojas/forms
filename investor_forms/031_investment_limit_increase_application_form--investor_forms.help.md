# Investment Limit Increase Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Limit Increase Application Form is designed to help investors and brokers submit applications for increasing their investment limits. The form gathers essential details to facilitate a smooth review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your user details, including any required contact information.
2. Explain the justification for requesting a limit increase.
3. Enter your broker's details, including name and email.
4. Enter your contact details, including name and email.
5. Add any additional comments or information related to your request.
6. Choose the review status and any relevant review comments.
7. Confirm your user agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your user name or account ID to identify yourself.
* **Justification for Limit Increase**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for requesting a limit increase. This field should include details about the reason for the increase and how it aligns with your investment goals.
* **Broker Details**: Enter the name and email address of your broker, as this information will be used to contact them for further communication.
* **Broker Phone**: Enter the phone number of your broker, if available.
* **Contact Details**: Enter the name and email address of your contact person, such as an account manager.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your contact person, if available.
* **Additional Comments**: Use this field to add any additional information that supports your limit increase request.
* **Review Status**: Choose one of the two options: Approved or Denied. If Approved, you can add any review comments. If Denied, explain the reason for denial.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments that the reviewer may need to know when evaluating your request.
* **User Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the investment limit increase process.
* **Agreement Details**: Enter any additional comments or details related to your agreement with the terms and conditions.

# Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any required fields.
* Provide clear and concise information to support your limit increase request.
* Be prepared to address any questions or concerns raised during the review process.
