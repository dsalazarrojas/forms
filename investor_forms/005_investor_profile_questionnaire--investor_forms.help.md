# Investor Profile Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your investment profile, risk tolerance, and financial goals. The information collected will help us understand your investment preferences and provide you with more accurate investment advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required and optional fields. The form is divided into several pages, and each page will ask you a few questions related to a specific topic. Please make sure to review each page carefully and provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`Main Page`, `note`, not required): This page is an introduction to the form and provides some general information about the purpose and use of the form.
* **investment_preferences** (`investment_preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the options that apply to your investment preferences. This could include things like growth, income, or diversification.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`Risk Tolerance`, `text`, not required): This is an opportunity for you to describe your risk tolerance level. Please provide a brief description of how you feel about taking risks in your investments.
* **Financial Goals** (`Financial Goals`, `text`, not required): Please describe your financial goals, such as saving for retirement, buying a house, or funding a business venture.
* **Financial Status** (`Financial Status`, `text`, not required): This field is used to describe your current financial status, such as income level, assets, or liabilities.
* **Investment Profile** (`Investment Profile`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief description of your investment profile, including any relevant background information, such as investment experience or financial goals.
* **Investor Info** (`Investor Info`, `text`, not required): This field is used to provide additional information about yourself, such as contact information or any other relevant details.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `select_one`, not required): Select the option that best describes your current investment status. This will help us understand your current situation and provide more relevant information.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, not required): Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your information to us.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate and honest information in the form. We will use the information to provide you with more informed investment advice. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
