# Venture Capital Investor Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for new investors who want to be onboarded into your investment process. It collects essential details and preferences to help you better understand their needs and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details in the "Investor Details" section.
2.  Describe your due diligence requirements in the "Due Diligence" section.
3.  Share your data collection preferences in the "Data Collection" section.
4.  Specify your investment preferences in the "Preferences" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Investor Details (investor_details)
- **Investor Name** (`investor_details`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
### Due Diligence (due_diligence)
- **Due Diligence Description** (`due_diligence`, text, optional): Describe the type of due diligence you require, such as financial reviews or site visits.
### Data Collection (data_collection)
- **Data Collection Description** (`data_collection`, text, optional): Specify how you'd like your data to be collected, such as through surveys or interviews.
### Preferences (preferences)
- **Investment Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Describe your investment goals, sectors, or industries of interest.

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your needs are clearly understood.
- Review each field carefully before proceeding.
- Ensure that the information you provide is up-to-date and reflects your current situation.
