# Risk Evaluation Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Evaluation Accelerator Application Form is designed to collect project information for risk assessment and evaluation purposes. This form helps project stakeholders to provide essential details about the project, including its type, status, contact information, and other relevant details. The form will help to streamline the risk evaluation process, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered and reviewed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the project details page and fill in the required fields:
   - **Project Name**: Enter the name of your project.
   - **Project Description**: Briefly describe your project.
   - **Project Type**: Choose the type of project from the options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
   - **Project Tags**: Select all relevant tags (Tag 1, Tag 2, or Tag 3) that apply to your project.
   - **Project Status**: Choose the current status of your project (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

2. Proceed to the contact information page and fill in the required fields:
   - **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your name, organization, and address.
   - **Email**: Enter your email address.
   - **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   - **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
   - **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
   - **Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.

3. Upload a file:
   - **File**: Upload a relevant file associated with your project.
   - **File Type**: Choose the type of file (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
   - **File Size**: Enter the size of your uploaded file.
   - **File URL**: Enter the URL of your file if it is hosted online.
   - **File Name**: Enter the name of your file.

4. Complete the submitter information page:
   - **Submitter Info**: Enter your name and organization.
   - **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   - **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
   - **Submitter Address**: Enter your address.
   - **Submitter Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
