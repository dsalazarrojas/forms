# Property Investor Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Investor Form Template is designed to collect information from property investors, capturing key details about their investment activities. It is a comprehensive form that covers various aspects of property investment, including the property's status, location, price, and agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and understand the purpose of each field.
2.  Start with the first page and fill in the required fields.
3.  For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose the relevant options from the available choices.
4.  For fields marked as "date", enter the date in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5.  For fields marked as "time", enter the time in the required format (HH:MM).
6.  For fields marked as "text", enter the relevant text in the required format.
7.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Property Investor Information

*   **property_investor_form_template_label** (property_investor_form_template_label, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the title of the property.
*   **property_investor_form_template_property** (property_investor_form_template_property, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the property's name or address.
*   **property_investor_form_template_property_type** (property_investor_form_template_property_type, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
*   **property_investor_form_template_buyer** (property_investor_form_template_buyer, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the property is for sale or rent.
*   **property_investor_form_template_seller** (property_investor_form_template_seller, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the property is for sale or rent.
*   **property_investor_form_template_location** (property_investor_form_template_location, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the property's location.
*   **property_investor_form_template_price** (property_investor_form_template_price, number, required/optional): This field is for entering the property's price.

### Page 2: Property Status

*   **property_investor_form_template_status** (property_investor_form_template_status, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for selecting the property's status (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **property_investor_form_template_notes** (property_investor_form_template_notes, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any additional notes about the property's status.
*   **property_investor_form_template_agreement** (property_investor_form_template_agreement, date, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_signature** (property_investor_form_template_signature, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the agreement is signed or not.
*   **property_investor_form_template_date** (property_investor_form_template_date, date, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_time** (property_investor_form_template_time, time, required/optional): This field is for entering the time of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_email** (property_investor_form_template_email, email, required/optional): This field is for entering the email of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_phone** (property_investor_form_template_phone, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the phone number of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_other** (property_investor_form_template_other, text, required/optional): This field is for entering any other relevant information.
*   **property_investor_form_template_other_notes** (property_investor_form_template_other_notes, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any other notes about the agreement.

### Page 3: Review and Revision

*   **property_investor_form_template_agreement_date** (property_investor_form_template_agreement_date, date, required/optional): This field is for entering the date of the agreement revision.
*   **property_investor_form_template_agreement_time** (property_investor_form_template_agreement_time, time, required/optional): This field is for entering the time of the agreement revision.
*   **property_investor_form_template_agreement_notes** (property_investor_form_template_agreement_notes, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any notes about the agreement revision.
*   **property_investor_form_template_reviewed_by** (property_investor_form_template_reviewed_by, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the agreement has been reviewed or not.
*   **property_investor_form_template_status_notes** (property_investor_form_template_status_notes, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any notes about the status of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_review** (property_investor_form_template_review, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the agreement has been reviewed or not.
*   **property_investor_form_template_revision_notes** (property_investor_form_template_revision_notes, note, required/optional): This field is for entering any notes about the review of the agreement.
*   **property_investor_form_template_revision** (property_investor_form_template_revision, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for selecting whether the agreement has been revised or not.
