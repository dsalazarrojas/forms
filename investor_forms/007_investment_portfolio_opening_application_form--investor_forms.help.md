# investment_portfolio_opening_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The investment portfolio opening application form is a crucial document for potential investors to submit their information and application for an investment portfolio opening. This guide will walk you through the form's fields, purpose, and completion instructions to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select your state from the provided options.
3. Enter your zip code.
4. Provide your risk tolerance, risk capital, and risk return details.
5. Enter your account number and select your account type.
6. If applicable, provide additional information about your account.
7. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Confirm your agreement to the terms by checking the box.
9. Sign the form to acknowledge your application.
10. Select the type of signature you are providing.
11. Enter the date you are agreeing to the terms and conditions.
12. Confirm your contact agreement (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed on your account.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your current address, if you have one.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (Select One, Required): Select your state from the provided options.
* **Zip** (Number, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Risk Tolerance** (Number, Required): Enter your risk tolerance level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Risk Capital** (Number, Required): Enter your risk capital amount.
* **Risk Return** (Number, Required): Enter your risk return percentage.
* **Account Number** (Text, Required): Enter your account number for verification purposes.
* **Account Type** (Select One, Required): Select your account type from the provided options.
* **Account Holder** (Text, Optional): If you are applying for someone else, enter their name.
* **Additional Info** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional information about your account.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Date, Required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions Check** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions by checking the box.
* **Signature** (Text, Required): Sign your name to acknowledge your application.
* **Signature Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of signature you are providing.
* **Agreement Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are agreeing to the terms and conditions.
* **Contact Agreement** (Select Multiple, Optional): Confirm if you agree to contact you for further information.

Tips:
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review your information carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
