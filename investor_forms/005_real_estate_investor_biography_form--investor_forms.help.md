# Real Estate Investor Biography Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Investor Biography Form is designed to collect contact and biographical information from real estate investors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling in the fields.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the select_one and select_multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Please fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, required): Enter information about your company, including the name and address.
* **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, `text`, required): Provide a brief personal biography.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share your experience and qualifications in the field, use this field.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): If you have any relevant educational background, please list it here.
* **Investment** (`investment`, `text`, optional): Describe your areas of investment interest, such as private or public equity, debt, or other.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Fill in your role within your company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any or all of the following: Private Equity, Public Equity, Debt.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the location where you're based (New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago).
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of company you have (Real Estate, Venture Capital, Private Equity).
* **Start Year** (`start_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you started your company.
* **End Year** (`end_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year your company ended (if applicable).
* **Address 1** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time zone you're based in (PST, EST, CST).
* **Bio** (`bio`, `note`, required): Write a brief bio about yourself, including your experience and qualifications.
* **Address 2** (`company_address_2`, `text`, required): Enter any additional address information.
 
## Tips
- Please be accurate and up-to-date when filling in the form.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
