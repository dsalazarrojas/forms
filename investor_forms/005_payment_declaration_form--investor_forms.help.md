# Payment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Declaration Form is used to collect information about payments made by investors for internal accounting and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the payment declaration form title.
2. Enter your name (if different from the investor's name).
3. Enter the investor's name.
4. Enter the date of the payment declaration.
5. Enter the amount paid.
6. Choose the transaction type (Bank Transfer, Credit Card Transfer, or Other).
7. If choosing "Other", enter the type of transaction.
8. Choose the payment method (Cash, Wire Transfer, or Cheque).
9. Choose the declaration status (Not Declared or Declared).
10. Add any additional notes about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Declaration Form** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a title for this payment declaration form to help identify the payment.
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): Enter your name (if different from the investor's name).
* **Investor Name** (investor_name, text, required: false): Enter the investor's name.
* **Declaration Date** (declaration_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the payment declaration.
* **Amount** (amount, number, required: false): Enter the amount paid.
* **Transaction Type** (transaction_type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of transaction (Bank Transfer, Credit Card Transfer, or Other).
* **Other Transaction Type** (other_transaction_type, text, required: false): Enter the type of transaction if it is not one of the listed options.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the payment method (Cash, Wire Transfer, or Cheque).
* **Declaration Status** (declaration_status, select_one, required: false): Choose the declaration status (Not Declared or Declared).
* **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the payment.
