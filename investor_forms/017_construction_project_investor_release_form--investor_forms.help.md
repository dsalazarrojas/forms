# Construction Project Investor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from investors about the release of funds or resources related to a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
- Review and revise your responses before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your project manager or team before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor name**: Enter the name of the entity or individual releasing funds or resources.
* **Investment reference or project**: Enter the project identifier or name related to the release.
* **Release date**: Enter the date of the release or approval.
* **Approver signature name**: Enter the name of the person signing off on the release. This serves as a signature.

Note: The other fields are not mandatory but may be useful for additional context or information.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to keep the information up-to-date and accurate.
* This form is for tracking and record-keeping purposes only; it does not create a formal contract or agreement.
