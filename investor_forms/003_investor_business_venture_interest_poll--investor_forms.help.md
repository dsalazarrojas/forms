# Investor Business Venture Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from investors about their business venture interests and preferences. It is intended to help investors express their interests and priorities in various categories related to investments and business development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a **Poll Title** (field 1) that describes the specific area of interest for the investment.
2. Select your **Category** (field 2) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in that category.
3. Indicate your **Sector** (field 3) interests by selecting multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected sector.
4. Choose your **Priority** (field 4) by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your priority level for the investment.
5. Enter your **Confidence Level** (field 5) in numbers, as this will be used to gauge your confidence in the investment.
6. Specify your **Investment Size** (field 6) in numbers, as this will be used to gauge your willingness to invest.
7. Select your **Investment Stage** (field 7) by choosing multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected stage.
8. Choose your preferred **Investment Sector** (field 8) by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in that sector.
9. Select your **Investment Priority** (field 9) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your priority level for the investment.
10. Indicate your **Investor Type** (field 10) by selecting multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected type.
11. Enter your **Investment Focus** (field 11) as a brief description of your investment focus.
12. Specify your **Industry** (field 12) in which you are interested.
13. Enter your **Company Name** (field 13) or the name of the company you are interested in investing in.
14. Enter your **Website** (field 14) for the company or organization you are interested in.
15. Enter your **Contact Information** (field 15) to provide any additional contact details.
16. Enter your **Email** (field 16) address for communication purposes.
17. Enter your **Address** (field 17) for physical contact.
18. Enter your **LinkedIn** (field 18) profile URL.
19. Enter your **Twitter** (field 19) profile URL.
20. Enter your **Facebook** (field 20) profile URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Poll Title** (`poll_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the specific area of interest for the investment.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in the category.
* **Sector** (`sector`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected sector.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your priority level for the investment.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence`, number, optional): Enter your confidence level in numbers to gauge your confidence in the investment.
* **Investment Size** (`investment_size`, number, optional): Enter your investment size in numbers to gauge your willingness to invest.
* **Investment Stage** (`investment_stage`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected stage.
* **Investment Sector** (`investment_sector`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in the sector.
* **Investment Priority** (`investment_priority`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your priority level for the investment.
* **Investor Type** (`investor_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for each selected type.
* **Investment Focus** (`investment_focus`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your investment focus.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Enter the industry in which you are interested.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you are interested in investing in.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the company or organization you are interested in.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for contact purposes.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, text, optional): Enter your Twitter profile URL.
