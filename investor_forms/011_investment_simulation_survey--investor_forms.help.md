# Investment Simulation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and investment preferences from participants to simulate investment scenarios and provide personalized investment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your relevant information.
2. Select the options that best represent your situation.
3. Answer the questions with the best effort and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, number, optional): Please rate your investment experience as a beginner, intermediate, advanced or expert.
* **Investment Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, optional): On a scale from 1-10, indicate how much risk you are willing to take.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, text, optional): Describe your investment goals, e.g., long-term, short-term, conservative, etc.
* **Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Occupation** (`participant_occupation`, select_one, optional): Select your occupation, e.g., student, teacher, manager, etc.
* **Investments** (`participant_investments`, select_multiple, optional): Select the investments you are interested in, e.g., stocks, bonds, real estate, etc.
* **Assets Under Management** (`participant_assets`, number, optional): Enter your current assets under management.
* **Net Worth** (`participant_net_worth`, number, optional): Enter your current net worth.
* **Education** (`participant_education`, select_one, optional): Select your highest level of education.
* **Annual Income** (`participant_income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income.
* **Marital Status** (`participant_marital_status`, select_one, optional): Select your marital status.
* **Country of Residence** (`participant_country_of_residence`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Native Language** (`participant_language`, select_one, optional): Select your native language.
* **How Long Investing** (`participant_how_long_investing`, select_one, optional): Select how long you have been investing.
* **Investing Frequency** (`participant_investing_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you invest.
* **Investment Influencers** (`participant_investing_influenced_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select what influences your investment decisions, e.g., family, friends, news, etc.
* **Reasons for Investing** (`participant_reasons_for_investing`, select_multiple, optional): Select your reasons for investing, e.g., goals, return, growth, etc.
* **Investment Expectations** (`participant_expectations`, select_multiple, optional): Select what you expect from your investments, e.g., return, growth, income, etc.
* **Investment Preferences** (`participant_investment_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your investment preferences, e.g., high risk, low risk, etc.
* **Investment Experience** (`participant_investment_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your investment experience.
* **Any Comments** (`participant_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or additional information about your investment preferences.

Please note that some fields may seem redundant or repetitive. Please answer each question with the best effort and accuracy. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
