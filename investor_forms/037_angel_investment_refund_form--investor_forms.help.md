# Angel Investment Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for an angel investment made to our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your investor information accurately, including your full name as it appears on our records.
2. Enter the amount you originally invested, which will be used to process the refund.
3. Give a clear explanation for the reason of the refund request.
4. Choose your preferred method of refund delivery.
5. Provide the necessary bank or payment details for the refund to be processed.
6. Enter your contact email for us to communicate with you regarding the refund.
7. Sign the form by typing your name, indicating your agreement with the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investor name** (`investor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on our records.
* **Investment amount** (`investment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount originally invested.
* **Reason for refund request** (`refund_reason`, text, required): Provide a clear explanation for the refund request.
* **Preferred refund method** (`preferred_refund_method`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose between Bank transfer, Check, or Credit back.
* **Account details for refund** (`account_details`, text, required): Provide the bank or payment details for the refund to be processed.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email for us to communicate with you regarding the refund.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your name, indicating your agreement with the information provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in the refund process.
* Ensure that the information provided matches our records for a smooth processing experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at the email address provided.
