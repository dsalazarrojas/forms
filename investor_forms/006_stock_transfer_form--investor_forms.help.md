# Stock Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record stock transfers between different entities, such as between departments or companies. It helps to track the movement of stocks, ensuring accurate accounting and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the `Company Name` in the text field.
2. Enter the `Stock Symbol` in the text field.
3. Enter the `Stock Quantity` in the number field.
4. Select the `From Date` using the calendar picker.
5. Select the `To Date` using the calendar picker.
6. (Optional) Enter a brief description of the reason for the stock transfer in the `Reason` text field.
7. (Optional) Add any additional notes about the stock transfer in the `Notes` text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (text)** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company involved in the stock transfer.
* **Stock Symbol (text)** (`stock_symbol`, text, required): Enter the symbol of the stock being transferred.
* **Stock Quantity (number)** (`stock_quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of stocks being transferred.
* **From Date (date)** (`from_date`, date, required): Select the date from which the stock transfer took place.
* **To Date (date)** (`to_date`, date, required): Select the date to which the stock transfer took place.
* **Reason (text)** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the stock transfer, if applicable.
* **Notes (text)** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the stock transfer, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are submitting this form for the correct stock transfer purpose.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in the form before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or company for clarification.
