# Banking Investment Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from investors and individuals interested in investing with the bank. It will help us to assess their suitability for investment and provide a basis for a potential investment proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for each field, based on your own information.
3. Ensure that you have attached any required supporting documents.
4. Review and confirm that you understand the terms and conditions of this investment proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (\*)**: Please fill out your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (\*)**: Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (\*)**: Enter your business contact number.
* **Investor Type**: Select the type of investor you are (Individual, Institutional, Corporate Entity, Partnership, or Trust).
* **Proposed Investment Amount (\*)**: Enter the total amount of investment you are proposing.
* **Investment Time Horizon**: Select the planned holding period for the investment (Less than 1 Year, 1-3 Years, 3-5 Years, 5-10 Years, or 10+ Years).
* **Purpose of Investment**: Describe the intended use of these funds.
* **Risk Tolerance Level**: Select your comfort level with investment risk (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive, or Very Aggressive).
* **Primary Investment Objectives**: Select your main goals for this investment (Capital Appreciation, Income Generation, Preservation of Capital, Tax Efficiency, or Diversification).
* **Annual Income**: Select your current gross annual income range.
* **Estimated Net Worth**: Select your current estimated net worth.
* **Investment Experience Level**: Select your level of investment experience (Novice, Beginner, Intermediate, Experienced, or Expert).
* **Current Portfolio Composition**: Describe your current investment portfolio composition.
* **Preferred Investment Vehicle**: Select your preferred type of investment (Stocks, Bonds, Mutual Funds, ETFs, Real Estate, Derivatives, or Mixed Portfolio).
* **Sector Preference**: Select the sectors you are interested in investing in (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Energy, Consumer, Utilities, or Manufacturing).
* **Source of Funds**: Select the origin of the capital (Employment Income, Business Ownership, Inheritance, Sale of Assets, Borrowing, or Other).
* **Current Banking Relationship**: Select whether you have an account with us (Yes, No, or Prospective Customer).
* **Supporting Documents**: Select any attached documents that support your investment proposal (Financial Statements, Tax Returns, ID Verification, Proof of Funds, or Investment Plan).
* **Have You Consulted with a Financial Advisor?**: Select whether you have consulted with a financial advisor (Yes, No).
* **I understand the terms and conditions of this investment proposal**: Select whether you understand the terms and conditions of this proposal (Yes, No).

Please ensure that you have attached any required documents and filled out all the necessary fields. This form will help us to assess your suitability for investment and provide a basis for a potential investment proposal.
