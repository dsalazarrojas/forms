# Equity Accelerator Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equity Accelerator Application form is designed for investors to collect information about potential equity accelerators that can be considered for investment. 

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by filling in the required fields, which are: 
- Accelerator Name
- Contact Person (name and email)
- Application Type (Equity Accelerator or Incubator)

Then, provide additional information about the accelerator, such as its address, location, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Accelerator Name** (`accelerator_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the accelerator.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the accelerator.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the accelerator is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the accelerator is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the accelerator.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the accelerator.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This is a secondary email contact for the accelerator.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Founding Year** (`founding_year`, text, optional): Enter the year the accelerator was founded.
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more areas of focus for the accelerator (Equity, Diversity, Equity & Inclusion, Social Impact, None).
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of application (Equity Accelerator, Incubator, None).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the accelerator.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the accelerator.
* **Twitter Handle** (`twitter`, text, optional): Enter the Twitter handle of the accelerator.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Enter the LinkedIn of the accelerator.
* **Facebook** (`facebook`, text, optional): Enter the Facebook of the accelerator.
* **Instagram** (`instagram`, text, optional): Enter the Instagram of the accelerator.
* **YouTube** (`youtube`, text, optional): Enter the YouTube of the accelerator.
* **Founder** (`founder`, text, optional): Enter the name of the founder.
* **Organization** (`org`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Organization Address** (`org_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the organization.
* **Organization Website** (`org_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the organization.
* **Organization Phone** (`org_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the organization.
* **Organization Email** (`org_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the organization.

## Tips
- Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and accurate.
- Make sure to double-check the information provided to ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
