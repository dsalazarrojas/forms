# Investor Pitch Deck Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Pitch Deck Upload Form is designed to collect information from investors for the purpose of uploading and managing pitch decks. This form is only to be filled out by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have all necessary information and are authorized to complete the form. The form consists of the following sections:

1. **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or contact information.
2. **Email**: Enter your work email address.
3. **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
4. **Pitch Deck**: Select the pitch deck you wish to upload.
5. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company associated with the pitch deck.
6. **Company Website**: Enter the website associated with the company.
7. **Pitch Programs**: Select the programs for which the pitch deck is applicable (e.g., Pitch Accelerator, Venture Capital Team, Other).
8. **Deck Upload**: Select the date and time you wish to upload the pitch deck.
9. **Deck Upload Time**: Select the specific time for the pitch deck upload (optional).
10. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the pitch deck upload.
11. **Assigned To**: Select the person to whom the pitch deck is assigned (e.g., chatjimmy, chat bob).
12. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool associated with the pitch deck (optional, and only if you have the option).
13. **Notes 2**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the pitch deck upload.
