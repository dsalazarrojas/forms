# Investment Proposal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Proposal Submission Form is designed to collect information from companies and investors who have submitted investment proposals to our organization. This form helps us track and organize the proposals we receive, making it easier for our team to review and respond to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as prompted. Start by providing basic contact information about your company, followed by the investment proposal details. Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly, as incomplete or inaccurate information may delay the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Proposal Submitted** (`text`, **optional**): This section is for the company to describe the investment proposal submitted. Please provide a brief overview of the proposal, including any relevant details or background information.
* **Company Name** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the address of your company's registered office or headquarters.
* **Investor Name** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the investor submitting the proposal.
* **Investor E-mail** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the email address of the investor.
* **Project Description** (`text`, **optional**): Describe the project or investment opportunity being proposed. Provide any relevant details, such as industry, sector, or expected outcomes.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help our team understand your investment proposal more effectively.
- If you have any supporting documents or additional information to attach, please include them with your proposal.
- Our team will review your investment proposal and contact you if we require further information or clarification.
- Thank you for taking the time to submit your investment proposal!
