# new_store_takeover_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new store takeover form is used to collect information about a store's takeover, including its details, ownership, and investor involvement. This form is essential for administrative and decision-making processes, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form fields accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the store's name in the "Store Name" field.
3. Provide the owner's name and other relevant details in the "Owner Name" field.
4. Enter the store's address in the "Store Address" field.
5. Enter the store's phone number in the "Store Phone" field.
6. Enter the owner's email address in the "Owner Email" field.
7. Select the takeover date from the calendar provided for the "Takeover Date" field.
8. Describe the reason for the takeover in the "Reason For Takeover" field.
9. Enter the store's license number in the "Store License" field (if applicable).
10. Select the store's status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Store Status" field.
11. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Store Type" field.
12. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Store Submitted By" field.
13. Select the date when the store was submitted in the "Store Submitted Date" field.
14. Enter any additional notes about the store's status in the "Store Status Note" field (if necessary).
15. Enter the investor's ID in the "Investor ID" field.
16. Enter the investor's name in the "Investor Name" field.
17. Enter the investor's contact details in the "Investor Contact" field (if applicable).
18. Enter the investor's email address in the "Investor Email" field.
19. Enter the investor's address in the "Investor Address" field.
20. Enter the investor's phone number in the "Investor Phone" field.
21. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Store Owner" field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the store owner in the "Store Owner Note" field (if necessary).
23. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Store Location" field.
24. Enter any additional notes about the store in the "Store Notes" field (if necessary).
25. Enter any notes from the investor in the "Investor Notes" field (if necessary).
26. Optionally, enter notes about who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store's owner.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required): Enter the store's street address.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, required): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the store's owner's email address.
* **Takeover Date** (`takeover_date`, date, required): Select the date of the takeover.
* **Reason For Takeover** (`reason_for_takeover`, text, required): Describe the reason for the takeover.
* **Store License** (`store_license`, text, optional): Enter the store's license number (if applicable).
* **Store Status** (`store_status`, select_one, required): Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the store's status.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" for whether the store is involved in takeover.
* **Store Submitted By** (`store_submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" for whether the store was submitted.
* **Store Submitted Date** (`store_submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the store was submitted.
* **Store Status Note** (`store_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the store's status.
* **Investor ID** (`investor_id`, number, required): Enter the investor's ID.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, text, required): Enter the investor's name.
* **Investor Contact** (`investor`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details of the investor (if necessary).
* **Investor Email** (`investor_email`, email, required): Enter the investor's email address.
* **Investor Address** (`investor_address`, text, required): Enter the investor's address.
* **Investor Phone** (`investor_phone`, text, required): Enter the investor's phone number.
* **Store Owner** (`store_owner`, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" for whether the store is owned by the investor.
* **Store Owner Note** (`store_owner_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the store owner (if necessary).
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" for whether the store is located.
* **Store Notes** (`store_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the store (if necessary).
* **Investor Notes** (`investor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the investor (if necessary).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter notes about who submitted the form (if necessary).
