# Fire Department Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about incidents responded to by the fire department. It is designed to be completed by firefighters to provide a standardized and organized way to report incidents. The information collected will be used to improve incident response and emergency management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct call type from the drop-down menu.
2. Describe the situation found in the text field.
3. Enter the name and title of the officer in charge.
4. Select the units involved in the incident from the drop-down menu.
5. Indicate the volunteer status by selecting from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Call Type**: Select the type of call that was made to the fire department. This could be a call 1, call 2, or call 3.
	+ Call 1: An emergency call, such as a reported fire or medical emergency.
	+ Call 2: A non-emergency call, such as a routine inspection or public education.
	+ Call 3: A special event call, such as a parade or festival.
* **Situation Found**: Describe the situation as it was found by the responders.
* **Officer In Charge**: Enter the name and title of the officer in charge of responding to the incident.
* **Units Involved**: Select all units that were involved in the incident.
* **Volunteer Status**: Indicate whether any volunteers were involved in the response.
	+ 'True': Yes, volunteers were involved.
	+ 'False': No volunteers were involved.
	+ Maybe: Uncertain or unknown.
