# Burn Permit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to apply for a burn permit, which is necessary to conduct a burn activity in a specific area. It is intended to gather information about the applicant's contact details, burn activity details, and safety measures to ensure a safe and compliant burn operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your full name and contact phone number.
2. Enter your email address for permit delivery and notifications.
3. Specify the property address where the burn activity will take place.
4. Select the primary purpose of the burn activity from the provided options.
5. Enter the proposed start date and estimated duration of the burn activity.
6. Confirm that you will monitor wind and weather conditions during the burn period.
7. Indicate the safety equipment available on site, such as pressurized water hose and fire extinguishers.
8. Enter the distance to the nearest water source in feet.
9. Acknowledge that you have read and understand local fire ordinances.
10. Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the weather monitoring requirement.
11. Finally, confirm the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Contact Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your contact phone number, making sure it is reachable during the burn period.
* **Email Address (Optional)**: Provide your email address for permit delivery and notifications, although it is not required.
* **Property Address for Burning (Required)**: Enter the street address where the burn activity will take place.
* **Proposed Start Date (Required)**: Enter the start date of the burn activity, typically valid for 24-48 hours.
* **Estimated Duration (Hours) (Required)**: Enter the total time fire will be active.
* **Safety Equipment Available on Site (Required)**: Select all safety equipment that will be available on site, such as pressurized water hose and fire extinguishers.
* **Distance to Nearest Water Source (Feet) (Required)**: Enter the distance in feet from the burn pile to the nearest water source.
* **I agree to monitor wind and weather conditions (Required)**: Confirm that you will monitor wind and weather conditions during the burn period.
* **I have read and understand local fire ordinances (Required)**: Acknowledge that you have read and understood local fire ordinances.
* **Date of Application (Required)**: Enter the current date, as it is the date of application.
