# Firefighter Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firefighter Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess a fire department's performance, preparedness, and overall response to emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For each section, provide a detailed description of the department's performance, preparedness, or strategy.
3. Use the provided text fields to write a concise and clear answer.
4. For the "Equipment" section, select the most relevant and up-to-date information regarding the equipment used by your team.
5. For the "Team" section, select all members who are part of your team.
6. For the "Strategy" section, provide the time spent on strategy planning and implementation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance** (`performance`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe the department's performance in responding to emergency situations.
	+ What are some strengths and weaknesses of the department's performance?
* **Preparedness** (`preparedness`, text, optional):
	+ How prepared is the department for responding to emergency situations?
	+ What resources or training programs are in place to improve preparedness?
* **Safety** (`safety`, text, optional):
	+ What safety protocols are in place during emergency responses?
	+ How effective are these protocols in preventing injuries or fatalities?
* **Physical** (`physical`, text, optional):
	+ How physically fit are the firefighters?
	+ What programs are in place to maintain or improve physical fitness?
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, select one, optional):
	+ Which of the following engines is most commonly used by the department:
		- Engine1
		- Engine2
		- Engine3
* **Team** (`team`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Which team members are part of the evaluation team?
		- Member1
		- Member2
		- Member3
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional):
	+ How effective is communication within the team during emergency responses?
	+ What channels or tools are used for communication?
* **Strategy** (`strategy`, time, optional):
	+ How much time is spent on strategy planning and implementation?
	+ Can you describe some of the strategies used during emergency responses?
