# Smoke Alarm Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smoke Alarm Authorization Form is used to collect information about smoke alarms in residential buildings. This form is required to be completed by the owner or representative of the building to ensure accurate and safe installation of the smoke alarm system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. Provide details about the smoke alarm, including the type and serial number.
4. Fill in the building information, such as the address.
5. Complete any other required fields as indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Contact Information** (`owner_contact_information`, text, required: false): Please provide your name, address, and any other contact information that you feel is relevant.
* **Smoke Alarm Details** (`smoke_alarm_details`, text, required: false): Describe the smoke alarm you want to install, including the model number and any other relevant details.
* **Tenant Contact Information** (`tenant_contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide contact information for the tenant, if any, including their name and contact number.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, note, required: false): Please sign your name as the owner or representative of the building.
* **Smoke Alarm Certificate** (`smoke_alarm_certificate`, note, required: false): If you have a smoke alarm certificate, please attach it to this form.
* **Fire Department Verification** (`fire_department_verification`, note, required: false): If you have verification from the fire department, please attach it to this form.
* **Building Information** (`building_info`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the building where the smoke alarm system will be installed.
* **Smoke Alarm Type** (`smoke_alarm_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of smoke alarm you want to install from the options provided.
* **Date Installed** (`date_installed`, date, required: false): Enter the date the smoke alarm was installed.
* **Time Installed** (`time_installed`, time, required: false): Enter the time the smoke alarm was installed.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the smoke alarm.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter a contact number for the building.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the building where the smoke alarm system will be installed.
