# Volunteer Firefighter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Firefighter Application Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteer firefighters, including their personal contact information, emergency contact information, availability to volunteer, medical certifications, skills, and experience as a firefighter.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal and contact information (sections 1-4).
2. Select your emergency contact relationship (section 7).
3. Choose your availability to volunteer (sections 8 and 9).
4. List your volunteer information (section 10).
5. Choose your volunteer start date (section 11).
6. Select your volunteer hours commitment (section 12).
7. Add any relevant medical certifications (section 14).
8. Add any relevant skills and certifications (sections 15, 16, and 17).
9. Add any relevant firefighter experience and certification information (sections 19 and 20).
10. Add any additional notes or comments (sections 13, 21, 22, and 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info**: Enter your name, email, and contact number.
	+ This is the first section of the form, where you'll enter your personal contact information.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
	+ This is where you live, and will be used for communication and training purposes.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information.
	+ This is where you can be reached for emergencies and scheduling purposes.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is your primary contact number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This is your primary email address.
* **Emergency Contact**: Choose your emergency contact relationship.
	+ This is someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Choose how you are related to your emergency contact.
	+ This is how you are related to your emergency contact.
* **Availability**: Choose your availability to volunteer.
	+ This is when you can and cannot volunteer.
* **Availability End**: Choose when you will be able to volunteer until.
	+ This is when you will stop being able to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Info**: Enter your volunteer information.
	+ This is where you can list any relevant information about your volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Choose when you would like to start volunteering.
	+ This is when you would like to start serving as a volunteer firefighter.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Choose your volunteer hours commitment.
	+ This is how many hours per week you are willing to volunteer.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your volunteer hours.
* **Medical Certifications**: Choose any relevant medical certifications you hold.
	+ This is important for training and safety purposes.
* **Skills**: Choose any relevant skills you have.
	+ This is useful for training and responding to emergencies.
* **Medical Certification**: Choose your medical certification type.
	+ This is important for medical response and training purposes.
* **Firefighter Info**: Enter any relevant information about your firefighter experience and certifications.
	+ This is useful for training and responding to emergencies.
* **Firefighter Certifications**: Choose any relevant firefighter certifications you hold.
* **Firefighter Experience**: Choose your firefighter experience level.
* **Volunteer Hours Note**: Add any additional information about your volunteer hours commitment.
* **Medical Certification Note**: Add any additional information about your medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Type**: Choose your medical certification type.
* **Firefighter Certification Note**: Add any additional information about your firefighter certification.
* **Review**: Add any final comments or questions about your application.
	+ This is your chance to ask questions or provide additional information.

Note: Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
