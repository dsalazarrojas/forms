# Fire Department Pre-Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Pre-Plan Form is a tool used by firefighters to quickly submit information about a potential fire hazard or emergency situation, allowing the fire department to better prepare and respond.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form when you have finished filling out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Department Pre-Plan Form** (`fire_department_pre_plan_form_1`, text, optional): This field is a placeholder for the form title.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please fill in the address of the location where the potential fire hazard or emergency situation is occurring.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the exact location of the potential fire hazard or emergency situation.
* **Fire Department Pre-Plan Form 5** (`fire_department_pre_plan_form_5`, text, optional): This field is intended for additional notes or comments.
* **Fire Department Pre-Plan Form 6** (`fire_department_pre_plan_form_6`, text, optional): This field is also intended for additional notes or comments.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, optional): If the location is a building, please fill in the building's name.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, text, optional): Provide the exact location of the building.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you are in contact with someone regarding this potential fire hazard or emergency situation, please fill in their name.

## Tips

* Be concise and accurate when filling out the fields.
* Use the form for official fire department use only.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please do not hesitate to contact the form administrator.
* You can always reach out to the form administrator if you need further clarification or assistance with filling out the form.
