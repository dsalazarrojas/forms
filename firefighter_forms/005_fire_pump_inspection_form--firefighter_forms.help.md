# Fire Pump Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inspect fire pumps to ensure they are in good working condition and functioning correctly. This form will help inspectors evaluate the fire pump's model, location, type, and condition.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct fire pump model from the drop-down menu.
2. Select the floor where the pump is located.
3. Choose the type of pump (electric, hydraulic, pneumatic, or hand-pump).
4. Describe the condition of the pump in the "Pump Condition" text area.
5. Select the inspector(s) conducting the inspection.
6. Choose the date and time of the inspection.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "Notes" text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Firepump Model** (`Firepump 1`, `Firepump 2`, `Firepump 3`, `Firepump 4`): Select the model of the fire pump being inspected.
* **Pump Location** (`Main Floor`, `Second Floor`, `Basement`, `Roof`): Choose the floor where the pump is located.
* **Pump Type** (`Electric`, `Hydraulic`, `Pneumatic`, `Hand-pump`): Select the type of pump (electric, hydraulic, pneumatic, or hand-pump).
* **Pump Condition** (note): Describe the condition of the pump, including any issues or defects found during the inspection.
* **Inspected By** (`John Doe`, `Jane Doe`, `Jim Brown`): Select the inspector(s) conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (date): Choose the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (time): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the fire pump is properly inspected.
* If any issues or defects are found during the inspection, make sure to document them in the "Pump Condition" text area.
* Use the "Notes" text area to add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
