# Fire Service Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting leave of absence for firefighters, which can be due to various reasons such as bereavement, court, doctor, family, or jury commitments. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the relevant department is informed and can process your leave request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Fire Number.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave of absence.
4. Choose the duration of your leave from the available options.
5. Select the department(s) you will be absent from.
6. Specify the reason for your leave from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Fire Number** (3, number, required): Enter your Fire Department-issued number, if applicable.
* **Start Date** (4, date, required): Select the date you wish to start your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (5, date, required): Select the date you wish to end your leave of absence.
* **Duration** (6, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your leave from:
	+ Less than 1 week
	+ 1-2 weeks
	+ 3-4 weeks
	+ More than 4 weeks
* **Department** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the department(s) you will be absent from:
	+ Administration
	+ Fire
	+ Operations
* **Reason** (8, select_one, required): Specify the reason for your leave from:
	+ Bereavement
	+ Court
	+ Doctor
	+ Family
	+ Jury
	+ Other

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays in processing your leave request.
- If you are unsure about the duration or department, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
- If you have any supporting documentation (e.g., doctor's note), please attach it to the form or provide it separately.
