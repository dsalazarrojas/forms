# smoke_detector_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Smoke Detector Inspection Form" is used to inspect and report on the status of smoke detectors in a building. This includes checking the functionality, type, and location of smoke detectors, as well as the status of the fire safety system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the building location from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the type of smoke detector that was inspected (Fire Alarm, Heat Detector, or Dual).
3. List the locations where the smoke detectors are installed.
4. Check if the smoke detectors are functional or not.
5. Check if the fire alarm system is present or not.
6. Check if the fire safety plan is in place or not.
7. Enter the building height (if applicable).
8. Choose the location of the smoke detector (Ground Floor, Middle Floor, or Upper Floor).
9. Check if the fire safety system has a certificate or not.
10. Enter the date of the inspection.
11. Choose the status of the fire safety system (Good, Need to be inspected, or Not working).
12. Enter any comments or additional comments.
13. Choose any action items for follow-up (True, False, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building Location** (Building Location, select_multiple): Select the building location from the dropdown list.
* **Smoke Detector Type** (Smoke Detector Type, select_one): Choose the type of smoke detector that was inspected (Fire Alarm, Heat Detector, or Dual).
* **Installed Locations** (Installed Locations, select_multiple): List the locations where the smoke detectors are installed.
* **Smoke Detector Functional** (Smoke Detector Functional, select_one): Check if the smoke detectors are functional or not.
* **Fire Alarm System** (Fire Alarm System, select_multiple): Check if the fire alarm system is present or not.
* **Fire Safety Plan** (Fire Safety Plan, select_one): Check if the fire safety plan is in place or not.
* **Building Height** (Building Height, number): Enter the building height (if applicable).
* **Smoke Detector Location** (Smoke Detector Location, select_one): Choose the location of the smoke detector (Ground Floor, Middle Floor, or Upper Floor).
* **Fire Safety System Certificate** (Fire Safety System Certificate, select_multiple): Check if the fire safety system has a certificate or not.
* **Certification Date** (Certification Date, date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Certification Time** (Certification Time, time): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date, date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (Inspector Name, text): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone** (Inspector Phone, text): Enter the phone number of the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (Inspector Email, email): Enter the email of the inspector.
* **Inspector Notes** (Inspector Notes, text): Enter any notes from the inspection.
* **Fire Safety System Status** (Fire Safety System Status, select_one): Choose the status of the fire safety system (Good, Need to be inspected, or Not working).
* **Comments** (Comments, text): Enter any comments.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text): Enter any additional comments.
* **Action Items** (Action Items, select_multiple): Choose any action items for follow-up (True, False, or Pending).
* **Fire Safety System Notes** (Fire Safety System Notes, text): Enter any notes about the fire safety system.
* **Smoke Detector Inspection Form Comments** (Smoke Detector Inspection Form Comments, text): Enter any comments about the form.
* **Smoke Detector Inspection Form Notes** (Smoke Detector Inspection Form Notes, text): Enter any notes about the form.
* **Fire Safety System Comments** (Fire Safety System Comments, text): Enter any comments about the fire safety system.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out, while others are optional and can be left blank.
