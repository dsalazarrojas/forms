# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for employees to request parental leave. It gathers necessary information such as employee details, leave dates, and reason for leave to facilitate the parental leave process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your name and ID
*   Enter the start and end dates of your requested leave
*   Provide a reason for your leave
*   If necessary, include contact details for your manager or team lead

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
*   **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your parental leave. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your parental leave. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for your parental leave.
*   **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your parental leave. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): If you wish to include your employee ID for reference, please enter it here.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, optional): If you need to provide additional information or contact details for your manager or team lead, please include them here.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): If you are requesting leave for a specific department, please select 'Yes'.
*   **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): If you have a team lead who needs to be informed of your leave, please enter their name and contact details.
*   **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): Your manager may have additional comments or questions related to your leave. Please enter their response here.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_multiple, optional): You can select multiple approval statuses. Please note that this field is for internal use and not meant to be filled by the employee.
*   **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, optional): If the form has been submitted, please select 'Yes'.
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Please note that this field is for internal use and typically populated by the system after approval.
*   **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, optional): Please note that this field is for internal use and typically populated by the system after approval.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Please note that this field is for internal use and typically populated by the system after approval.
*   **Created By User** (`created_by_user`, text, optional): This field may be populated by the system after approval.
