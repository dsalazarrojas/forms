# Fire Hydrant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Hydrant Inspection Form is used to inspect and report on the status of fire hydrants. This form is used to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of fire hydrant locations, status, and any issues that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the location of the fire hydrant in the "Fire Hydrant Location" field.
2. Select the status of the fire hydrant from the options provided (Active, Inactive, or Removed).
3. Provide a brief description of the fire hydrant in the "Fire Hydrant Description" field.
4. Take a photo of the fire hydrant, if applicable, in the "Fire Hydrant Photo" field.
5. Check the box next to any issues found with the fire hydrant in the "Fire Hydrant Notes" field.
6. Enter any comments about the fire hydrant in the "Fire Hydrant Comments" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
8. Enter your name as the inspector in the "Inspector Name" field.
9. Sign your name in the "Inspector Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Hydrant Location (text)**: Enter the location of the fire hydrant, e.g., "123 Main St."
* **Fire Hydrant Status (select_one)**: Select the current status of the fire hydrant (Active, Inactive, or Removed).
* **Fire Hydrant Description (note)**: Provide a brief description of the fire hydrant, e.g., "Fire hydrant is functioning properly."
* **Fire Hydrant Photo (text)**: If applicable, attach a photo of the fire hydrant.
* **Fire Hydrant Notes (select_multiple)**: Check the boxes next to any issues found with the fire hydrant, e.g., "Issue Found", "Leaking", etc.
* **Fire Hydrant Comments (text)**: Enter any comments about the fire hydrant.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the inspection, e.g., "2022-02-20".
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the inspection, e.g., "14:30".
* **Inspector Name (text)**: Enter your name as the inspector, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Inspector Signature (text)**: Sign your name as the inspector.
