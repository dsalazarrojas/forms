# Logistics Delivery Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Delivery Employment Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from employees about their experience with our logistics delivery services. This form helps us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Email address accurately.
2. Select your Department from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your Job Title.
4. Provide feedback on the delivery operations in the text box.
5. Select any improvement opportunities you think we can implement.
6. Rate your job experience using the number scale.
7. Provide more details about your rating in the text box.
8. Select any improvement opportunities related to your job experience.
9. Rate your overall experience using the number scale.
10. Provide more details about your rating in the text box.
11. Enter any action points you'd like us to take to improve our services.
12. Provide any next steps we should take to follow up on your feedback.
13. Select a follow-up date and time for us to contact you.
14. Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your employee name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to help us contact you for follow-up.
* **Department** (department): Select your department from the dropdown menu (Logistics, Operations, or Management).
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter your job title.
* **Feedback On Delivery Operations** (feedback_on_delivery_operations): Provide specific feedback on our delivery operations.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (improvement_opportunities): Select any areas where we can improve (Time management, Communication, Teamwork).
* **Job Experience** (job_experience): Rate your job experience using the number scale.
* **Feedback On Job Experience** (feedback_on_job_experience): Provide more details about your rating.
* **Improvement Opportunities Job Experience** (improvement_opportunities_job_experience): Select any areas where we can improve related to your job experience.
* **Overall Rating** (overall_rating): Rate your overall experience using the number scale.
* **Rating Details** (rating_details): Provide more details about your rating.
* **Improvement Plan** (improvement_plan): Enter any ideas on how we can improve.
* **Action Points** (action_points): Select any action points you'd like us to take (Improve delivery time, Reduce errors, Improve communication).
* **Next Steps** (next_steps): Enter any next steps we should take to follow up on your feedback.
* **Follow Up** (follow_up): Select a follow-up date and time for us to contact you.
* **Follow Up Time** (follow_up_time): Enter the follow-up time (if needed).
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by): Enter your name for reference.
* **Submitted On** (submitted_on): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Submission ID** (submission_id): Enter a unique identifier for your submission.
* **Form ID** (form_id): Enter a unique identifier for the form itself.
