# Non Exempt Employee Dual Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Exempt Employee Dual Service Request Form is used to request dual service or overtime compensation for non-exempt employees. This form is essential for accurately recording employees' work hours and shifts to ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
2.  Enter your job title as per your employee ID badge.
3.  Select a start and end date for your dual service or overtime request.
4.  Indicate if you hold a second job title (Yes/No).
5.  If you hold a second job title, enter the work hours for that job.
6.  Specify your shift for the dual service or overtime request (Yes/No).
7.  Enter your work location for the dual service or overtime request.
8.  Enter your manager's name, email, and phone number for approval purposes.
9.  Provide a reason for your dual service or overtime request.
10. Enter any comments or additional information related to your request.
11. Select who has approved your request (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional): This field is for the user to submit the form with their name.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as per your employee ID badge.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your dual service or overtime request.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your dual service or overtime request.
* **Second Job Title** (`job_title_2`, select_one, optional): If you hold a second job title, select 'Yes' to indicate that you have a second job title.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): If you have a second job title, enter the number of work hours for that job.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): Specify your shift for the dual service or overtime request (Yes/No).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter your work location for the dual service or overtime request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name for approval purposes.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email for approval purposes.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number for approval purposes.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your dual service or overtime request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or additional information related to your request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select who has approved your request (Yes/No).
