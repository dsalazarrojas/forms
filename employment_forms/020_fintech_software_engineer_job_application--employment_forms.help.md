# Fintech Software Engineer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from candidates for the Fintech Software Engineer position. Please fill out the form accurately to provide the necessary details for evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Use the following format for dates: MM/DD/YY.
3. Select the correct options for each field as per the available choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your updated resume or CV.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): Enter a comma-separated list of your relevant programming languages, tools, or technologies you have experience with.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your work experience relevant to the position, including job title, company, and duration.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Available to Work In** (`available_to_work`, text, required): Enter the location where you are willing to work (city, country, or region).
* **Language** (`languages`, select_multiple, required): Select the languages you are proficient in (e.g., English, Spanish, Mandarin).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Select the title of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Remote).
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, number, required): Enter your expected salary range for the position.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, time, required): Enter the notice period you are willing to provide if your application is accepted.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you are available to start work.
* **Notice End Date** (`notice_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to be available to start work.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Briefly describe why you are a good fit for the position.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years of experience you have in relevant work.
* **Available Holiday** (`available_holiday`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, number, required): Enter the expected salary range for the position.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, text, required): Enter the length of the contract you are willing to commit to.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the position you are applying for (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Job Type Status** (`job_type_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the job type you are applying for (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tools or software you are proficient in using.
