# Government Initiative Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from job seekers who are applying for government initiatives. It is intended to help government agencies process applications and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide accurate and up-to-date information in all required fields.
2. Ensure that you provide your agency's contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Attach any additional information or documents that may support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employment Application
* **Employment Application** (`employment_form_application`, text, optional): 
    Please provide a brief description of the job or position you are applying for.

### Address
* **Address** (`employment_form_address`, text, optional): 
    Provide your current address for the agency to contact you for any further instructions or updates.

### City
* **City** (`employment_form_city`, text, optional): 
    Please enter your city of residence for the agency to know your location.

### State
* **State** (`employment_form_state`, text, optional): 
    Enter the state where you reside for the agency to know your location.

### Postal Code
* **Postal Code** (`employment_form_postal_code`, text, optional): 
    Please enter your postal code (zip code) for the agency to send you any further instructions or updates.

### Country
* **Country** (`employment_form_country`, text, optional): 
    Enter the country where you reside for the agency to know your location.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`employment_form_phone`, text, optional): 
    Please provide your phone number for the agency to contact you for any further instructions or updates.

### Email
* **Email** (`employment_form_email`, email, optional): 
    Provide your email address for the agency to contact you for any further instructions or updates.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`employment_form_experience`, text, optional): 
    Please provide a brief description of your relevant work experience.

### Education
* **Education** (`employment_form_education`, text, optional): 
    Please provide your educational background for the agency to know your qualifications.

### Skills
