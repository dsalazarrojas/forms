# Fintech Role Aptitude Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fintech Role Aptitude Exam is designed to evaluate a person's suitability for a fintech role. It collects personal, contact, and professional details to assess their relevance for a position in the fintech industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
2. Type your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Describe your location or workplace in the "Location" field.
5. Share your relevant work experience in the "Experience" field.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, required/optional): Provide any other contact information you'd like to share (phone number, LinkedIn profile, etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, required/optional): Describe your current workplace or location relevant to your fintech experience.
* **Experience** (`experience`, required/optional): Briefly describe your relevant work experience in the fintech industry, including job titles, companies, and accomplishments.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, required/optional): This field is intended for any additional personal details you'd like to add, such as your education or skills.
