# Outside Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outside Employment Form is used to report outside employment activities that may potentially conflict with your work at our organization. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to maintain compliance and avoid any potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to include your outside employment start and end dates if applicable.
3. Be as detailed as possible when describing your outside employment activities.
4. If you are unsure about any field or need further guidance, please contact [your supervisor or HR representative].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or personnel record.
* **Job Title**: Briefly describe the title or role you hold in your outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager**: The name of your direct supervisor or manager in the outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager Email**: The email address of your direct supervisor or manager in the outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager Phone**: The phone number of your direct supervisor or manager in the outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager Job Title**: Briefly describe the job title of your direct supervisor or manager in the outside employment.
* **Reporting Date**: The date when you started your outside employment.
* **Reporting Time**: The time when you started your outside employment (AM/PM, 24-hour format).
* **Reporting Location**: Briefly describe the location where you work for your outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager Location**: Briefly describe the location where your direct supervisor or manager works for your outside employment.
* **Reporting Manager Department**: Briefly describe the department where your direct supervisor or manager works for your outside employment.
* **Outside Employment Start Date**: The date when you started your outside employment.
* **Outside Employment End Date**: The date when you plan to end your outside employment.
* **Outside Employment End Reason**: Choose one of the provided options to describe why you plan to end your outside employment.
* **Job Classification**: Choose one or more of the provided options that best describe your outside employment role or responsibilities.
* **Employment Type**: Briefly describe the type of employment you have (e.g., full-time, part-time, consulting, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to update your outside employment information regularly to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If your outside employment changes, please submit an updated form immediately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
