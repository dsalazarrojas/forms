# Advertising Creative Director Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your experience and approach as a creative director. It will help us assess your qualifications and fit for the role. Please provide accurate and honest answers to the best of your abilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section completely, as indicated by the page headers (-- Candidate Information --, -- Professional Background --, and -- Creative Leadership Assessment --).
2. Provide detailed answers to the questions, but keep your responses concise.
3. If you have any relevant attachments, such as portfolio links or examples, please include them with your answers.
4. Review your form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Candidate Information

* **Full Name (Full Name)** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Portfolio Link (Portfolio Link)** (`portfolio_link`, text, **required**): Link to your creative portfolio, if available.

### Professional Background

* **Years Creative Experience (Years Creative Experience)** (`years_creative_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the number of years you have experience in creative fields.
* **Previous Roles (Previous Roles)** (`previous_roles`, text, **required**): List your previous creative leadership roles.
* **Notable Clients Campaigns (Notable Clients Campaigns)** (`notable_clients_campaigns`, text, **required**): List notable clients or campaigns you have worked on.

### Creative Leadership Assessment

* **Team Management Experience (Team Management Experience)** (`team_management_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the number of people you have managed in creative roles.
* **Creative Process Approach (Creative Process Approach)** (`creative_process_approach`, text, **required**): Describe your approach to the creative process.
* **Brainstorming Facilitation (Brainstorming Facilitation)** (`brainstorming_facilitation`, text, **required**): Explain how you facilitate effective brainstorming sessions.
* **Challenging Project Example (Challenging Project Example)** (`challenging_project_example`, text, **required**): Describe a challenging creative project and how you solved it.
* **Brand Consistency Maintenance (Brand Consistency Maintenance)** (`brand_consistency_maintenance`, text, **required**): Explain how you ensure brand consistency across multiple projects.
* **Creative Brief Handling (Creative Brief Handling)** (`creative_brief_handling`, text, **required**): Describe your approach to interpreting and executing creative briefs.
* **Digital Traditional Balance (Digital Traditional Balance)** (`digital_traditional_balance`, text, **required**): Balance your approach between digital and traditional creative methods.
* **Innovation Approach (Innovation Approach)** (`innovation_approach`, text, **required**): Explain how you foster innovation within your creative team.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use specific examples when possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
