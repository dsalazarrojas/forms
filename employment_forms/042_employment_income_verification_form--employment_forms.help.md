# Employment Income Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employment Income Verification Form" is designed to provide information about an employee's employment status, income details, and company information. This form is typically used for tax purposes, benefits verification, or other employment-related activities. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date, as this form may be used to support important decisions or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Fill in the company's name in the "Company Name" field.
4. Enter the start date of the employee's employment in the "Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of the employee's employment (if applicable) in the "End Date" field.
6. Specify the employee's salary in the "Salary" field.
7. Select the employment type from the options provided in the "Employment Type" field (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
8. Select the current employment status from the options provided in the "Employment Status" field (e.g., Active, Inactive, Resigned).
9. Enter the date of verification in the "Verification Date" field.
10. Enter the name of the person or organization verifying the information in the "Verification By" field.
11. Enter the company's address in the "Company Address" field.
12. Enter the company's phone number in the "Company Phone" field.
13. Enter the company's email address in the "Company Email" field.
14. Enter the employee's tax ID number in the "Tax ID Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the employee in this field.

### Employee ID (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the ID number of the employee.

### Company Name (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the company the employee works for.

### Start Date (<name>, date, required: false)
Enter the date the employee started working for the company.

### End Date (<name>, date, required: false)
Enter the date the employee stopped working for the company (if applicable).

### Salary (<name>, number, required: false)
Enter the employee's salary.

### Employment Type (<name>, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of employment from the options provided:

* Full-time
* Part-time
* Freelance

### Employment Status (<name>, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the current employment status from the options provided:

* Active
* Inactive
* Resigned

### Verification Date (<name>, date, required: false)
Enter the date the information was verified.

### Verification By (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person or organization verifying the information.

### Company Address (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the address of the company.

### Company Phone (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the company.

### Company Email (<name>, email, required: false)
Enter the email address of the company.

### Tax ID Number (<name>, text, required: false)
Enter the tax ID number of the employee.
