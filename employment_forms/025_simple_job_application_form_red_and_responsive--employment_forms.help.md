# simple_job_application_form_red_and_responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and job-related information from users, allowing them to apply for a job. It consists of four fields: personal details, job details, resume upload, and submit form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Personal Details" field.
2. Provide information about the job you are applying for in the "Job Details" field.
3. Upload your resume in the "Resume Upload" field.
4. Click the "Submit Form" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details**: Please enter your personal details, such as your name, email, and contact information.
* **Job Details**: Enter information about the job you are applying for, including the job title, company, and location.
* **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume, which should include your work experience, skills, and qualifications.
* **Submit Form**: Click this button to submit your application.
