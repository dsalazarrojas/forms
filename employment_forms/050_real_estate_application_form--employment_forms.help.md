# Real Estate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for a real estate company. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure your application is considered.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field below and fill out the required information.
- Ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- Review your application before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Real Estate Application Form** (<id: 1>): A brief introduction to the form. You can disregard this field.
- **Applicant Name** (<id: 2>): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Job Title** (<id: 3>): Enter the position you're applying for in our company.
- **Contact Phone** (<id: 4>): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
- **Contact Email** (<id: 5>): Enter your email address for further communication.
- **Contact Address** (<id: 6>): Enter your address for us to get in touch with you.
- **Employment Status** (<id: 7>): Choose your current employment status:
	+ Active: Currently employed.
	+ Inactive: Not currently employed.
	+ On Leave: On leave from your current job.
- **Available to Start** (<id: 8>): Enter the date you can start working.
- **Start Date** (<id: 9>): Enter the expected start date if different from your available to start date.
- **End Date** (<id: 10>): Enter the expected end date if applicable.
- **Salary Range** (<id: 11>): Enter your salary expectation for this position.
- **Experience** (<id: 12>): Enter your years of experience in the real estate field.
- **References** (<id: 13>): List at least 2 professional references.
- **Resume** (<id: 14>): Attach a brief description of your resume, or link to it if shared online.
