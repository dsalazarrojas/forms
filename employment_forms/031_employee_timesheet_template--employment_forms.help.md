# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to track and report their work hours, breaks, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your work start and end times for each day.
2. Enter the date you started and ended work.
3. Record your total work hours for the period.
4. Indicate if you took a break, and if so, enter the start and end times.
5. Select whether you took breaks frequently or not.
6. If you have any supervisor or manager comments, enter their name, email, and phone number.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Timesheet Template** (`title`, `note`, optional): This is the name of the form.
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you started work for the day. (Format: 12:00 AM/PM)
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you started work for the day.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you ended work for the day. (Format: 12:00 AM/PM)
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you ended work for the day.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, optional): Record your total work hours for the day.
* **Break Start Time** (`break_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you started your break.
* **Break End Time** (`break_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you ended your break.
* **Break Duration** (`break_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your break in minutes.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you took breaks frequently or not.
* **Break Frequency Option 1** (`break_frequency_option_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of your breaks (e.g., 1 for 1 break, 2 for 2 breaks, etc.). (This field is only visible if "Break Frequency" is selected as "Yes")
* **Break Frequency Option 2** (`break_frequency_option_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of your breaks (e.g., 1 for 1 break, 2 for 2 breaks, etc.). (This field is only visible if "Break Frequency" is selected as "Yes")
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your work.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments from your supervisor.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your approver's name.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your approver's phone number.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments from your approver.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments from your manager.
