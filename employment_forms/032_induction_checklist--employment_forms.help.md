# Induction Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Induction Checklist" form is designed to collect essential information about an employee's status and relationships within the company. This data will help ensure that their details are correctly recorded and easily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select the correct answers to the questions according to your status and relationships within the company.
2.  Fill in the provided fields with accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to record your employment status within the company.
    *   Please answer whether you are employed or not.
*   **Reporting Line Manager** (`reporting_line_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks if you report directly to a manager.
    *   Choose 'Yes' if you report directly to a manager, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Reporting Line Members** (`reporting_line_members`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to indicate if you report to multiple managers.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you report to multiple managers, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to specify the emergency contact person for you.
    *   Choose 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact person, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to describe the relationship with your emergency contact person.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact relationship, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to record your work email address.
    *   Fill in your work email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to record your work phone number.
    *   Fill in your work phone number.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to record your work address.
    *   Fill in your work address.
*   **Emergency Exit Prophets** (`emergency_exit_prophets`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to indicate if you have emergency exit proclamations.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have emergency exit proclamations, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Emergency Exit Prophets Relationship** (`emergency_exit_prophets_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to describe the relationship with your emergency exit proclamations.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have emergency exit proclamations relationship, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Report To** (`report_to`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to specify who you report to.
    *   Choose 'Yes' if you report to someone, or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Report To Relationship** (`report_to_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to describe the relationship with the person you report to.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have a report to relationship, or 'No' otherwise.
