# employment_grievance_settlement_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and formalize the resolution of employment-related grievances between an employee and their employer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the complaint description field and enter a clear and concise description of the grievance.
2. Select the grievance status from the options provided.
3. Provide a detailed description of the settlement outcomes.
4. Enter the resolution date when the grievance was resolved.
5. Indicate whether a mutual agreement was reached between the employer and employee.
6. Enter the date the agreement was made, if applicable.
7. Indicate whether the employer and employee agreed to the terms.
8. Enter the signatures of both the employer and employee, as well as any other representative involved.
9. Provide additional details about the complaintant and any other parties involved.
10. Enter any additional terms or conditions that were agreed upon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Description** (Page 1): A clear and concise description of the grievance being reported.
* **Grievance Status** (Page 1): Select the current status of the grievance from the options provided.
* **Settlement Outcomes** (Page 1): A detailed description of the outcome of the grievance, including any agreements or resolutions reached.
* **Resolution Date** (Page 1): The date when the grievance was resolved.
* **Mutual Agreement** (Page 1): Indicate whether a mutual agreement was reached between the employer and employee.
* **Agreement Date** (Page 1): The date when the agreement was made, if applicable.
* **Employer Agreement** (Page 1): Indicate whether the employer agreed to the terms.
* **Employee Agreement** (Page 1): Indicate whether the employee agreed to the terms.
* **Employer Signature** (Page 1): Enter the signature of the employer.
* **Employee Signature** (Page 1): Enter the signature of the employee.
* **Complaintant Details** (Page 2): Provide additional details about the complainant.
* **Settlement Details** (Page 2): Enter any additional details about the settlement.
* **Other Terms** (Page 2): Enter any other terms or conditions that were agreed upon.
* **Employer Representative** (Page 2): Enter the name of any representative from the employer involved in the grievance.
* **Employee Representative** (Page 2): Enter the name of any representative from the employee involved in the grievance.
* **Other Parties** (Page 2): Enter the names of any other parties involved in the grievance.
