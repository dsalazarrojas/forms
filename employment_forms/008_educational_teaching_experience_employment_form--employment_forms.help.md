# Educational Teaching Experience Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your educational teaching experience. It's essential to provide accurate and honest information to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you have multiple educational experiences, please complete the relevant sections for each experience.
3. Ensure you have uploaded all required files for each experience.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Educational Experience** (`first_educational_experience`): Describe your first educational experience, including the name of the institution and your role or position.
* **First Educational Experience Start Date** (`first_educational_experience_start_date`): Enter the start date of your first educational experience.
* **Second Educational Experience** (`second_educational_experience`): Describe your second educational experience, including the name of the institution and your role or position.
* **Second Educational Experience Start Date** (`second_educational_experience_start_date`): Enter the start date of your second educational experience.
* **Third Educational Experience** (`third_educational_experience`): Describe your third educational experience, including the name of the institution and your role or position. (Optional)
* **Third Educational Experience Start Date** (`third_educational_experience_start_date`): Enter the start date of your third educational experience. (Optional)
* **Educational Employment Form Upload File 1** (`educational_employment_form_upload_file_1`): Upload a file related to your first educational experience, such as a contract or certificate.
* **Educational Employment Form Upload File 2** (`educational_employment_form_upload_file_2`): Upload a file related to your second educational experience, such as a contract or certificate.
* **Educational Employment Form Uploads** (`educational_employment_form_uploads`): Indicate whether you have uploaded any additional files related to your educational experiences. Choose 'Yes' if you have uploads.
* **Educational Employment Form Upload File 4** (`educational_employment_form_upload_file_4`): Upload a file related to your educational experiences. (Optional)
* **Submission** (`submission`): Enter the date of submission for your educational experiences.
