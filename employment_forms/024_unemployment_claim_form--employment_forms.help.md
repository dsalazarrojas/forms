# Unemployment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Unemployment Claim Form, which is used to claim unemployment benefits. Please note that this form is only for employees who are eligible for unemployment benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2.  Enter your start and end dates of employment.
3.  Provide your previous job title.
4.  Enter the name and address of your previous employer.
5.  Describe the reason you left your job.
6.  Enter the number of days you gave as notice to your previous employer.
7.  Enter your last wage pay.
8.  If necessary, enter an additional previous job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
*   **last_name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
*   **email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **start_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you started your employment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **end_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you ended your employment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **prev_job_title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title from your previous employment.
*   **prev_employer** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and address of your previous employer.
*   **reason_for_leaving** (Text, Optional): Describe the reason you left your job.
*   **notice_period** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of days you gave as notice to your previous employer.
*   **last_wage_pay** (Number, Optional): Enter your last wage pay.
*   **prev_job_title_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional previous job titles.

## Tips
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your local unemployment office for guidance.
*   Make sure to submit the form only once for each claim period.
