# Form For Applicants - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants who are interested in applying for a job. The form is used to gather relevant details about the applicant, such as their job title, contact information, experience, and availability. This form is designed to be completed by the applicant, and the information collected will be reviewed for job suitability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* If you are available to work full-time or part-time, enter the corresponding number in the "Experience" field.
* Enter your available hours of work in the "Available Hours" field.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for "Job Type" and enter any additional job type information in the "Job Types" field.
* Enter any additional job titles you are qualified for in the "Job Title 2", "Job Title 3", etc. fields.
* Enter a brief job description and job requirements in the "Job Description", "Job Requirements" fields respectively.
* Enter any relevant information about your education background in the "Education" field.
* Enter any information about your availability, such as time off and vacation days, in the "Availability" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Title
* **Job Title (1)**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Experience (5)**: Enter your years of experience (0-100).
* **Available Hours (6)**: Enter your available hours of work per week.
* **Job Type (7)**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in full-time or part-time work, "No" otherwise.
* **Job Types (8)**: Enter any additional job types you are interested in (e.g. freelance, contract, etc.).
* **Job Title 2 (9)**: Enter any additional job titles you are qualified for.
* **Job Title 3 (10)**: Enter any additional job titles you are qualified for.
* **Job Description (11)**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Requirements (12)**: Enter a brief description of your job requirements.
* **Education (13)**: Enter any relevant information about your education background.
* **Availability (14)**: Enter any information about your availability, such as time off and vacation days.
* **Availability 2 (15)**: Enter any additional availability information.
* **Availability 3 (16)**: Enter any other availability information.
* **Job Type 2 (17)**: Enter any additional job types you are interested in.
* **Job Type 3 (18)**: Enter any other job types you are interested in.
