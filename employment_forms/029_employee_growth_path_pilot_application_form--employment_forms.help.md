# Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form is designed to support employees in their career development and growth within the organization. The form helps identify areas of interest and potential job roles that align with the employee's background and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of [X] pages with [Y] fields.
2. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review each page carefully before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Growth Path Pilot Application**: This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank as it is already filled out.
* **Key Role**: A key role that you currently hold or have held in the past. This can be a job title or a specific function within your current or past roles.
* **Tenure**: The length of time you have been working with the company.
* **Motivation**: A brief statement describing your motivation for applying to the Employee Growth Path Pilot program.
* **Education**: Your level of education (Bachelor, Associate, Master, Doctorate).
* **Education Level**: Your level of education.
* **Programs of Interest**: Select all the programs that interest you.
* **Current Job Title**: Your current job title.
* **Other Current Job Title**: A title for other roles or responsibilities you hold besides your primary job title.
* **Expected Job Title**: Your expected job title after completing the program.
* **Other Expected Job Title**: A title for other roles or responsibilities you expect to have after completing the program.
* **Prophets Path Pilot Application**: A flag indicating whether you have applied for the program before or not.
* **Employees Served**: The number of employees you have supervised or worked with.
* **Employees Served Years**: The number of years you have supervised or worked with employees.
* **Programs**: List any programs you have completed or participated in.
* **report Path Pilot Application Education Years**: A field for reporting education years.
