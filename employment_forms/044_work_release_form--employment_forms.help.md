# Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Release Form is designed to be completed by an employee to provide necessary information for their employer to release them from work obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required information:
	* Employee name
	* Employment start date
	* Employment end date
	* Reason for recovery, work, or other relevant information (select from provided options)
2. If necessary, provide additional information:
	* Employer name (for verification)
	* Date submitted to employer
3. Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name** (`employeename`): Enter the name of the employee who is requesting the work release.
* **Employment start date** (`employmentstart`): Enter the start date of the employee's employment.
* **Employment end date** (`employmentend`): Enter the end date of the employee's employment.
* **Reason for recovery, work, or other relevant information** (`workreasonfor`, `medicalcertification`): Select the reason for the work release from the provided options.
* **Physician's information** (`physicianinformation`): Provide additional medical information as required.
* **Medical certification** (`medicalcertification`): Attach any relevant medical certifications or documents.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Date submitted to employer** (`employerdate`): Record the date the form was submitted to the employer.
* **Employer's signature** (`employer`): The employer's representative should sign to confirm receipt and understanding of the form.
* **Date signed** (`employerdate_2`): Record the date the employer signed the form.
* **Employer's address** (`employeraddress`): Provide the employer's address for correspondence.
* **Employee name (2)** (`employeename2`, `employeename_2`): Enter a second version of the employee's name (for consistency).
* **Employer name (2)** (`employername2`, `employername_2`): Enter the second version of the employer's name (for verification).
* **Reason for employment** (`employmentreasonfor`): Select a reason for employment from the provided options.
* **Reason for work** (`workreasonfor`): Select a reason for work from the provided options.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields and sign the form to confirm completion.
- Ensure accurate and clear information to avoid confusion.
- Attach any required medical certifications or documents.
- Submit the form to the employer for approval and understanding.
- Review and revise the form before submitting it to the employer.
