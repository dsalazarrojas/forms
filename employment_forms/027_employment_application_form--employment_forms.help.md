# Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about the candidate's employment application. It includes fields for basic contact information, job details, and work status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your name in the **candidate_name** field.
3. Select the job you are applying for in the **job_applied_for** field.
4. Enter your contact email in the **contact_email** field.
5. Enter your work phone number in the **work_phone** field.
6. Enter any alternate contact information in the **contact_alternate** field.
7. Describe your work experience in the **work_experience** field.
8. Select your current work status in the **work_status** field.
9. Enter the date you were hired or rehire in the **work_status_hire_date** and **work_status_rehire_date** fields if applicable.
10. Enter your job title in the **job_title** field.
11. Enter a brief description of your job in the **job_description** field.
12. Enter the location of your job in the **job_location** field.
13. Select the type of job you are applying for in the **job_type** field.
14. Select the specific job type in the **job_type_2**, **job_type_3**, and **job_type_4** fields.
15. Select your current work status in the **work_status_2**, **work_status_3**, and **work_status_4** fields.
16. Select the jobs you are applying for in the **job_applied_for_2**, **job_applied_for_3**, and **job_applied_for_4** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **candidate_name** (text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **job_applied_for** (text, optional): Select the job you are applying for.
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **work_phone** (text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **contact_alternate** (text, optional): Enter any alternate contact information.
* **work_experience** (text, optional): Describe your work experience.
* **work_status** (select_one, optional): Select your current work status.
* **work_status_hire_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you were hired.
* **work_status_rehire_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you were rehire.
* **job_title** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **job_description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **job_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your job.
* **job_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of job you are applying for.
* **job_type_2** (select_one, optional): Select the specific job type.
* **job_type_3** (select_one, optional): Select the specific job type.
* **job_type_4** (select_one, optional): Select the specific job type.
* **work_status_2** (select_one, optional): Select your current work status.
* **work_status_3** (select_one, optional): Select your current work status.
* **work_status_4** (select_one, optional): Select your current work status.
* **job_applied_for_2** (select_multiple, optional): Select the jobs you are applying for.
* **job_applied_for_3** (select_multiple, optional): Select the jobs you are applying for.
* **job_applied_for_4** (select_multiple, optional): Select the jobs you are applying for.
* **job_applied_for_5** (select_multiple, optional): Select the jobs you are applying for.

**Tips:**
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your recruiter or HR representative for guidance.
