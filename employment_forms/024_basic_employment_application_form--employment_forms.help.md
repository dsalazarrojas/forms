# Basic Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect employment application information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your address information, including street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
3. Select the position title and type you are applying for.
4. If you have previous employment experience, indicate if you have worked before and provide details about your most recent employer.
5. If you have a high school diploma or higher, select your highest level of education.
6. List any relevant skills or qualifications you have for the position.
7. If you have professional certifications or licenses, list them.
8. If you speak any languages, indicate them.
9. Confirm that you are willing to pass a background check.
10. Indicate if you have a felony conviction.
11. Finally, confirm that all information provided is true and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Your first name. (Required)
* **Last name**: Your last name. (Required)
* **Middle initial**: Your middle initial (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Date of birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (Required)
* **Email address**: Your email address. (Required)
* **Phone number**: Your phone number. (Required)
* **Street address**: Your street address. (Required)
* **City**: The city where you live. (Required)
* **State**: The state where you live. (Required)
* **ZIP code**: The ZIP code where you live. (Required)
* **Position title**: The position title you are applying for. (Required)
* **Position type**: The type of position you are applying for (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Temporary, Seasonal, Contract). (Required)
* **Available start date**: The date you are available to start working. (Required)
* **Have you worked before?**: If you have previous employment experience, indicate 'True' or 'False'. (Required)
* **Most recent employer**: If you have previous employment experience, provide the name of your most recent employer. (Optional)
* **Employment dates**: If you have previous employment experience, provide the dates of your employment. (Optional)
* **Job title**: If you have previous employment experience, provide the job title of your most recent position. (Optional)
* **Highest level of education**: Your highest level of education (e.g., High School Diploma, GED, Some College, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Graduate Degree, Other). (Required)
* **School/University name**: The name of the school or university where you completed your highest level of education. (Optional)
* **Graduation year**: The year you graduated. (Optional)
* **Relevant skills**: A list of relevant skills or qualifications you have for the position. (Required)
* **Professional certifications or licenses**: A list of any professional certifications or licenses you have. (Optional)
* **Languages spoken**: A list of languages you speak. (Optional)
* **Do you have a felony conviction?**: If you have a felony conviction, indicate 'True' or 'False'. (Required)
* **Are you willing to pass a background check?**: Indicate 'True' or 'False'. (Required)
* **I certify that all information provided is true and complete**: Confirm that all information provided is true and complete. (Required)
