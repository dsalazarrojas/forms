# Job Channel Agency - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about job channel agencies, which connect job seekers with potential employers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of job you are listing (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
2. Choose the locations where the job is available (e.g. Yangon, Mandalay, Nay Pyi Taw, Naypyitaw, Pyi).
3. Select the category of the job (e.g. Sales, IT, Marketing).
4. Enter the job position title.
5. Provide a brief description of the job.
6. Enter the contact person's name.
7. Select the status of the job (e.g. Open, Closed).
8. Enter the URL of the job listing (if applicable).
9. Enter the contact person's email address (if applicable).
10. Enter the contact person's phone number (if applicable).
