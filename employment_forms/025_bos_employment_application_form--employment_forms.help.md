# BOS Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment application information from applicants for potential job openings within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes your Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Current Address.
2. Proceed to the Position Information section, where you will provide details about the position title you are applying for and your preferred work location.
3. In the Employment Type section, select the type of employment you seek, such as Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
4. Move on to the Work Experience section, where you will describe your years of relevant experience and list your previous employers (limited to your last 3 employers).
5. The Education section asks for your highest level of education and the field of study.
6. Finally, you will be asked to list any relevant certifications or licenses you possess and provide additional information or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Email Address**: A valid email address you use for communication.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true
- **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Current Address**: Your street address.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **City, State, ZIP**: Your city, state, and ZIP code.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Position Title Applied For**: The title of the position you are applying for.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Preferred Work Location**: The preferred location for your work.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Type of Employment Sought**: Select the type of employment you seek, such as Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
- **When Can You Start?**: The date you can start work.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
- **Years of Relevant Experience**: Number of years of experience relevant to the position.
  - Type: number
  - Required: true
- **Previous Employers**: List your last 3 employers.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Key Accomplishments**: Describe your major achievements.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Highest Level of Education**: Select your highest educational degree level.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
- **Field of Study**: The field of study for your highest level of education.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Relevant Certifications or Licenses**: List any relevant certifications or licenses.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Technical Skills**: List your technical skills (e.g., software, systems, tools).
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Languages Spoken**: List the languages you speak.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Additional Information or Comments**: Any additional information or comments you would like to provide.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly, and if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
