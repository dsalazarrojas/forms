# Courier Service Driver Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from drivers who work for a courier service. It helps us understand and track essential details about each driver, including their name, vehicle details, contact information, and agreement date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to enter the correct vehicle registration number.
3. Enter your signature to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the driver. This field is not required, but it's essential to have accurate and up-to-date information about each driver.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of this agreement. This field helps us track when the agreement was signed.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the vehicle used for the courier service. This could include make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle registration number. This is crucial for identification and regulatory purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the driver's contact number. This allows us to reach out to the driver for any necessary communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter the driver's email address. This is an optional field for communication purposes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Enter your signature to confirm your agreement. This is an essential field to demonstrate your commitment to the terms of the agreement.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the assigned tool or equipment. This is an optional field for tracking purposes.
* **Other Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, required: false): Enter any other assigned tool or equipment not listed. This is also an optional field for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, and we will keep your information confidential.
