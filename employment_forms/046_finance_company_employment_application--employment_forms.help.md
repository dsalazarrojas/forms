# Finance Company Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for employment opportunities within our finance company. It is intended to gather essential information about your background and qualifications for us to consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each section carefully to understand what information we need from you.
2. Fill out the contact information section with your relevant details.
3. Provide a brief description of your employment history.
4. Share your education background if it's relevant to the position applied for.
5. Upload your resume to showcase your qualifications.
6. Write a compelling job application statement explaining why you're interested in this opportunity.
7. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about your application.
* **Employment Details** (`Employment Details`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your previous work experience, focusing on relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, required/optional): If your education background is relevant to the position applied for, please include details about your degrees, certifications, or courses.
* **Resume Upload** (`Resume Upload`, `text`, required/optional): Upload your resume to showcase your skills and qualifications for the position applied for.
* **Job Application** (`Job Application`, `text`, required/optional): Write a compelling statement explaining why you're interested in this opportunity and how your skills match the job requirements.
* **Submit Application** (`Submit Application`, `text`, required/optional): Double-check all your information is accurate and complete before submitting your application.
