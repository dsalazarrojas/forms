# Sports Industry Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Industry Employment Application form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for positions in the sports industry. This form helps us understand your background, experience, and qualifications, making it easier for us to assess your suitability for potential roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Candidate Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Contact Details, including your address and contact numbers.
3. Indicate your Employment Status, selecting whether you are Employed, Not Employed, or Self-Employed.
4. Specify your Employment Type, choosing from Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal, or Project.
5. Enter your Work Experience, listing previous work experience in the sports industry.
6. List your Education and Qualifications, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
7. Specify your Skills, including any relevant skills or training you have acquired.
8. Provide References, listing 2 professional references.
9. Upload any supporting files or documents relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are currently Employed, Not Employed, or Self-Employed.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select from Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal, or Project.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, note, optional): List your previous work experience in the sports industry, highlighting relevant roles and responsibilities.
* **Education** (`education`, note, optional): List your relevant education and qualifications, including degrees or certifications.
* **Skills** (`skills`, note, optional): Specify any relevant skills or training you have acquired, such as coaching, refereeing, or sports-specific skills.
* **References** (`references`, note, optional): List 2 professional references, including their names, positions, and contact information.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files or documents relevant to your application.
