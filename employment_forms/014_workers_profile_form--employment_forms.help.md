# Workers Profile Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workers Profile Form is used to collect and store essential information about workers. It helps ensure accurate and complete records for each worker.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the form pages using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately.
3. Make sure to save your progress as you go along, as the form does not auto-save.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the worker's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the worker's last name.
* **Job Title**: Enter the worker's job title.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the worker is employed.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the worker's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the worker's job responsibilities.
* **Job Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the job is active or inactive.
* **Job Category**: Select multiple 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the job categories the worker belongs to.
* **Hire Date**: Enter the date the worker was hired.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the worker was terminated. If not terminated, leave blank.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Enter the reason why the worker left the company.
* **Job Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the current job status.
* **Work Location**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the worker's work location.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes, use the "Previous" button to return to the previous page.
* If you have any issues or errors, contact your HR administrator for assistance.
