# self_employment_records_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-employment records form is used to collect information about your income and expenses for tax purposes. This form helps us to better understand your financial situation and ensure that your records are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the form in sequence, starting from the "Income" page.
2. Enter your income for the relevant tax year in the "Income" page.
3. Go to the "Expenses" page and enter any expenses you have incurred during the tax year.
4. On the "Tax Details" page, select all applicable options from the dropdown list.
5. Provide income details on the "Income Details" page, including the date of any relevant financial events.
6. Select the tax year for which you are providing information on the "Tax Year" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income** (`income_page`, Number, Required: False): Enter your total income for the relevant tax year.
* **Expenses** (`expense_page`, Text, Required: False): Enter any expenses you have incurred during the tax year.
* **Tax Details** (`tax_details_page`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Select all applicable options from the dropdown list.
* **Income Details** (`income_details_page`, Date, Required: False): Provide income details, including the date of any relevant financial events.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, Select One, Required: False): Select the tax year for which you are providing information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and true to the best of your knowledge.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
- Please complete the form in sequence, starting from the "Income" page.
- If you need to provide information for multiple tax years, please submit a separate form for each year.
