# tip_pool_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tip pool agreement form" is a document used to obtain employee consent and record tip-sharing policies and agreements. This form is typically used when a company has a tip-sharing program in place, and employees are required to sign an agreement to participate in it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have obtained the necessary information from your manager or HR representative regarding the company's tip-sharing policies.
2. Fill out the "Employee Consent" section with your personal details.
3. Select your answer to "Tip Sharing Policies" to indicate whether you consent to participate in the tip-sharing program.
4. If you select "Yes" to "Tip Sharing Policies", you will be asked to choose the distribution methods for tips, such as which employees will receive tips and how often. 
5. If you need to attach a signed agreement, please enter your name and date in the "Signed Agreement" field.
6. If you are providing a signed agreement, please enter the name of the document to be attached in the "Agreement Copy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Consent**: Enter your personal details such as name, date, and signature.
* **Tip Sharing Policies**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you consent to participate in the tip-sharing program. 
* **Distribution Methods**: If you select "Yes" to "Tip Sharing Policies", select from the list of methods to distribute tips to other employees, such as who will receive tips and how often.
* **Signed Agreement**: If you have a signed agreement, enter your name and date in this field.
* **Agreement Copy**: Enter the name of the document to be attached, typically a signed copy of the tip pool agreement. 
* **Agreement Copy** is used to upload or attach a file, but in this form it's just for recording the name of the document to be attached.
