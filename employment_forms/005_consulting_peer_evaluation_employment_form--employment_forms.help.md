# Consulting Peer Evaluation Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a peer-based evaluation of an employee's performance, providing a comprehensive framework to assess their strengths, areas of improvement, and readiness for growth and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* Employee Being Evaluated: Enter the full name of the employee being reviewed.
	* Peer Evaluator Name: Enter your full name.
	* Relation to Employee: Select how you work with the employee (Manager, Teammate, Direct Report, Other).
	* Project Name: Enter the name of the project you worked on with the employee.
	* Evaluation Date: Select today's date.
2. Rate the employee's performance using the rating system:
	* Problem Solving and Analysis
	* Teamwork and Collaboration
	* Verbal and Written Communication
	* Time Management and Reliability
	* Leadership Potential
3. Identify the employee's key strengths and areas for improvement:
	* Key Strengths: Describe the employee's single biggest strength.
	* Development Opportunities: Identify areas the employee should focus on improving.
4. Determine the employee's readiness for growth and development:
	* Promotion Readiness: Assess if the employee is ready for more responsibility.
5. Provide any additional feedback:
	* Evaluator Comments: Share any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Being Evaluated**: Enter the full name of the person you are reviewing.
* **Peer Evaluator Name**: Enter your full name, as you are the one evaluating the employee.
* **Relation to Employee**: Select how you work with the employee:
	+ Teammate: You work together as part of a team.
	+ Manager: You are the employee's manager.
	+ Direct Report: You are the employee's direct supervisor or manager.
	+ Other: Select this option for other work relationships.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you worked on with the employee.
* **Problem Solving and Analysis**: Rate the employee's ability to handle complex tasks (1-5, Poor to Excellent).
* **Teamwork and Collaboration**: Rate the employee's ability to work effectively with others (1-5, Poor to Excellent).
* **Verbal and Written Communication**: Rate the employee's ability to share information clearly (1-5, Poor to Excellent).
* **Time Management and Reliability**: Rate the employee's ability to meet deadlines (1-5, Poor to Excellent).
* **Leadership Potential**: Rate the employee's leadership potential (1-5, Poor to Excellent).
* **Key Strengths**: Describe the employee's single biggest strength.
* **Development Opportunities**: Identify areas the employee should focus on improving.
* **Promotion Readiness**: Assess if the employee is ready for more responsibility (1-4, Ready Now, Ready in 6 Months, Ready in 12+ Months, Not Recommended at this time).
* **Evaluator Comments**: Share any other relevant information.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select today's date.
