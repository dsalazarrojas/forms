# Job Description Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document job descriptions for job postings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the job you are describing in the **Job Title** field.
2. Enter the name of the company posting the job in the **Company Name** field.
3. Select the type of job (Full-time, Freelance, etc.) from the **Job Type** field.
4. In the **Job Responsibilities** field, provide a brief description of the job's primary duties and responsibilities.
5. In the **Job Requirements** field, list the essential skills and qualifications required for the job.
6. Select the **Required Skills** needed for the job from the dropdown list.
7. Enter the location where the job is based in the **Job Location** field.
8. Add any relevant tags or keywords for the job in the **Job Tags** field.
9. Describe the application process for the job in the **Application Process** field.
10. Enter the contact email for the job poster in the **Contact Email** field.
11. Enter the contact phone number for the job poster in the **Contact Phone** field.
12. Enter the name of the contact person for the job poster in the **Contact Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job posting. This is a required field.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company posting the job. This is a required field.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job (Full-time, Freelance, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Job Summary**: Provide a brief description of the job's primary duties and responsibilities. This field is optional.
* **Job Responsibilities**: Describe the job's primary duties and responsibilities. This field is optional.
* **Job Requirements**: List the essential skills and qualifications required for the job. This field is optional.
* **Required Skills**: Select the skills and qualifications required for the job. This field is optional.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where the job is based. This field is optional.
* **Job Tags**: Add any relevant tags or keywords for the job. This field is optional.
* **Application Process**: Describe the application process for the job. This field is optional.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email for the job poster. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the job poster. This field is optional.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the job poster. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating any existing job postings.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your HR department or relevant teams.
* Double-check your form for accuracy before submitting it.
