# Faculty Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for applicants to provide information about themselves for a faculty job application. It is intended to collect contact details, education background, work experience, and other relevant information to evaluate candidates for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Information** page with your basic contact details and any additional information you wish to share with the institution.
2. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your email and phone number if you have one.
3. Provide a brief summary of your **Education Background**.
4. Describe your **Work Experience**, including relevant roles and positions you have held.
5. If applicable, provide references and **Upload References** as a supporting document.
6. **Upload Resume**, **Upload Portfolio**, **Upload Cover Letter**, **Upload Transcript**, and **Upload Certificates** to demonstrate your qualifications and experience.
7. Add any additional **Other Documents** that support your application.
8. Finally, **Submit** your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information**: A brief description of your application and any additional information you wish to share with the institution.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email and phone number to enable the institution to contact you.
* **Education Background**: Briefly describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience, including roles and positions held.
* **References**: Provide references from people who can vouch for your qualifications.
* **Upload Resume**: Attach a PDF or other document file of your resume.
* **Upload Portfolio**: Attach a PDF or other document file of your portfolio.
* **Upload Cover Letter**: Attach a PDF or other document file of your cover letter.
* **Upload Transcript**: Attach a PDF or other document file of your transcript.
* **Upload Certificates**: Attach a PDF or other document file of your certificates.
* **Upload Other Documents**: Attach any additional documents that support your application.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your completed application.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled out as accurately and completely as possible.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your education and work experience.
* Attach documents in PDF or other formats that are easily readable.
* Proofread your application before submitting to avoid errors.
* Contact the institution if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
