# Reference Check For Team Members - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a team member's past employment history, professional behavior, and current performance. The data collected will be used to evaluate their performance and make informed decisions about their role or position within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Team Member Information**: Fill in the details about the team member, such as name, title, and department.
2. **Reference Check Information**: Provide information about the team member's previous employers, work experience, and job titles.
3. **Evaluation and Recommendation**: Share your professional evaluation of the team member's performance and make any necessary recommendations for improvement.
4. **Comments and Status**: Add any additional comments or status updates about the team member's performance.
5. **Meta Data**: Fill in any additional meta data or comments about the form.

  * **<label>Evaluation and Recommendation</label>** (`<type>`):

  * **<label>Comments and Status</label>** (`<type>`):

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and honest information about the team member's performance.
- Be specific and detailed in your evaluations and recommendations.
- Use a professional and respectful tone when writing your comments and status updates.
