# Biotechnology Sector Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotechnology Sector Job Application Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your background, qualifications, and experience for a job opening in the biotech industry. It will help us understand your fit for the position and streamline the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing personal information on pages 1-7.
2. On pages 8-10, enter details about the position you're applying for and your employment type preference.
3. On pages 11-15, enter your education background and relevant skills.
4. On pages 16-20, enter details about your work experience.
5. On pages 21-25, add any additional information about your technical skills, software experience, and certifications.
6. On pages 26-30, upload supporting documents and provide links to your portfolio or website.
7. Finally, on pages 31-40, enter any additional information about your background and interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Personal Information -- (Pages 1-7)**
	+ **First Name** (`first_name`): Your first name.
	+ **Last Name** (`last_name`): Your last name.
	+ **Email Address** (`email`): Your primary contact email.
	+ **Phone Number** (`phone`): Your contact phone number.
	+ **Current Address** (`address`): Your current street address.
	+ **Willing to Relocate** (`location_willing`): Indicate if you're willing to relocate for the position.
* **-- Position Information -- (Pages 8-10)**
	+ **Position Title You Are Applying For** (`position_applied`): Enter the job title you're applying for.
	+ **Position Category** (`position_category`): Select the type of role you're applying for.
	+ **Employment Type Preference** (`employment_type_pref`): Choose your desired employment arrangement.
* **-- Education -- (Pages 11-15)**
	+ **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`): Select your highest degree earned (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD).
	+ **Field of Study** (`degree_field`): Enter your major or specialization.
	+ **University or Institution** (`university_name`): Enter the name of your school.
	+ **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`): Enter the date you graduated.
* **-- Professional Experience -- (Pages 16-20)**
	+ **Years of Biotech Experience** (`years_biotech`): Enter your total years of experience in the biotech field.
	+ **Current Employer** (`current_employer`): Enter your current company name.
	+ **Current Position Title** (`current_position`): Enter your current job title.
	+ **Previous Relevant Positions** (`previous_roles`): Describe your past biotech roles.
* **-- Technical Skills -- (Pages 21-25)**
	+ **Technical Skills and Expertise** (`technical_skills`): Select all relevant technical skills and expertise.
	+ **Software and Tools Experience** (`software_tools`): Enter any software and tools experience.
	+ **Laboratory Equipment Experience** (`laboratory_equipment`): Enter equipment you've used in your work.
* **-- Application Documents -- (Pages 26-30)**
	+ **Upload Resume or CV** (`resume_upload`): Upload your resume or CV in PDF or Word format.
	+ **Upload Cover Letter** (`cover_letter_upload`): Upload a cover letter in PDF or Word format (optional).
	+ **Portfolio or Website Link** (`portfolio_link`): Enter a link to your portfolio or website.
	+ **Publications or Patents** (`publications`): List any relevant publications or patents.
* **-- Additional Information -- (Pages 31-40)**
	+ **Why Are You Interested in This Position** (`why_interested`): Explain your interest in this position.
	+ **Key Achievements Relevant to This Role** (`relevant_achievements`): Describe your relevant accomplishments.
	+ **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`): Confirm your consent to a background check.
	+ **Are You Legally Authorized to Work in This Country?** (`legal_authorization`): Confirm your work authorization in the country.
