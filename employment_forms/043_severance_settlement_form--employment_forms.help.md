# Severance Settlement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and process employment terminations, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Enter employee information, including employee name, employee ID, and other relevant details.
* Provide details about the employment term, including the reason for termination.
* Document the severance package offered to the employee.
* Specify outplacement services to be provided.
* Determine if benefits will be continued for the employee.
* Enter the severance date and date of termination.
* Specify any other terms or agreements.
* Add any comments or notes.
* Obtain employee signature and manager signature.
* Enter manager details, including title and date.
* Record the date the form was submitted, received, posted, approved, and reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's name and other relevant details.
* **Employment Term** (`employment_term`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for termination.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `text`, required: false): Enter the number of notice days provided to the employee.
* **Severance Package** (`severance_package`, `text`, required: false): Specify the severance package offered to the employee, including pay, benefits, or other details.
* **Outplacement Package** (`outplacement_package`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outplacement services to be provided to the employee.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date of termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for termination.
* **Severance Pay** (`severance_pay`, `number`, required: false): Specify the severance pay offered to the employee.
* **Benefits Continued** (`benefits_continued_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Determine if benefits will be continued for the employee.
* **Severance Date** (`severance_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the severance package was agreed upon.
* **Outplacement Services** (`outplacement_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if outplacement services will be provided.
* **Severance Terms Agreed** (`severance_terms_agreed`, `select_one`, required: false): Determine if the severance terms have been agreed upon.
* **Other Terms** (`other_terms`, `text`, required: false): Specify any other terms or agreements.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the severance process.
* **Employee Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Obtain the employee's signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required: false): Obtain the manager's signature.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's title.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was received.
* **Date Posted** (`date_posted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was posted.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was approved.
* **Date Denied** (`date_denied`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was denied.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
