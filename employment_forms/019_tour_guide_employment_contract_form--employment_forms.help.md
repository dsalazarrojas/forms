# Tour Guide Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guide Employment Contract Form is used to gather information about the employee's personal and professional details for employment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is optional.
2. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. This field is optional.
3. Choose the type of employment you are interested in from the options provided in the "Employment Type" field. Options include **Hourly**, **Contract**, and **Seasonal**. This field is optional.
4. Select the **Job Start Date** for your employment contract. This field is optional.
5. Enter the **Work Location** where you will be working. This field is optional.
6. Describe the **Compensation Structure** you expect to receive. This field is optional.
7. Choose the payment methods you would like to receive from the options provided in the "Payment Methods" field. Options include **Cash**, **Check**, and **Bank Transfer**. This field is optional.
8. Enter the **Job Duration** in number of **Hours** or **Days**. This field is optional.
9. Enter your **Compensation** amount. This field is optional.
10. Describe the **Benefits** you will receive as an employee. This field is optional.
11. Enter your **Contact Info** for communication purposes. This field is optional.
12. Provide **Emergency Info** if you need to be contacted in case of an emergency. This field is optional.
13. Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact" field. This field is optional.
14. Enter the **Emergency Address** where you can be contacted during an emergency. This field is optional.
15. Specify the **Emergency Notice Period** in hours or days. This field is optional.
16. Choose the **Emergency Contact Relationship** from the options provided. Options include **Spouse**, **Parent**, and **Child**. This field is optional.
17. Choose the unit for the **Emergency Notice Period** from the options provided. Options include **Hours** and **Days**. This field is optional.
18. Enter the **Job End Date** if your employment contract will end on a specific date. This field is optional.
19. Describe the reason why your employment will end in the "Job End Reason" field. This field is optional.
20. Enter the **Job Salary** you expect to receive. This field is optional.
21. Enter any **Job Bonus** you expect to receive. This field is optional.
22. Enter your **Tax ID Number** for tax purposes. This field is optional.
23. Enter your **Social Security Number** for tax purposes. This field is optional.
24. Enter your **Driver's License Number** for identification purposes. This field is optional.
25. Provide any **Medical Certification** that may be required. This field is optional.
