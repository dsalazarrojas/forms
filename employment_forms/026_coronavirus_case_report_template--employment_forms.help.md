# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Coronavirus Case Report Template is a form used to report cases related to COVID-19 in a workplace setting. It is intended to collect information in a structured and consistent manner to help track and respond to potential cases.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the reported case from the options provided in the "Reported Case" field (1). This field indicates the nature of the reported case.
2.  Choose the type of case from the options in the "Case Type" field (2). This helps to further categorize the case.
3.  Select all symptoms experienced or witnessed from the options provided in the "Symptomatic" field (3). This field helps to understand the nature of symptoms associated with the case.
4.  Enter the location where the case was reported or occurred in the "Location" field (4). This field helps to identify the specific location related to the case.
5.  Provide the name and title of the employer where the case was reported in the "Employer" field (5). This information is helpful for contact tracing and reporting.
6.  Enter the total number of employees affected by the case in the "Employee Count" field (6). This field provides insight into the scale of the case.
7.  Enter the date the case was first reported in the "Date Reported" field (7). This ensures timely reporting and helps in tracking case timelines.
8.  Enter the time the case was reported in the "Time Reported" field (8). This field provides additional context to the reporting time.
9.  Enter the contact number of the person reporting or responsible for the case in the "Contact Number" field (9). This allows for follow-up communication.
10.  Enter the email address of the person reporting or responsible for the case in the "Email" field (10). This provides an alternative contact method.
11.  Choose the person or department that was reported to in the "Reported To" field (11). This field helps in directing the case to the appropriate authority.
12.  Select all applicable case statuses from the options provided in the "Case Status" field (12). This field helps in tracking the progress or resolution of the case.
13.  Provide any additional comments or context about the case in the "Comments" field (13). This field allows for further explanation or clarification of the case.
