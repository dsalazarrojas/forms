# Tech Developer Hiring Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Developer Hiring Employment Form is designed to collect information from potential tech developers for hiring purposes. It aims to gather relevant details that will help recruiters and tech companies make informed decisions during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select your skills from the dropdown list.
3. Ensure you have a valid resume attached (either uploaded or linked).
4. Choose your job type and employment status from the dropdown lists.
5. Specify your available start date and time.
6. Indicate your available location (if applicable).
7. Add any additional comments or details if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Your work or personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): A brief description of your current or desired job title.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Upload or link your updated resume for review.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, `text`, optional): Your LinkedIn profile URL for networking purposes.
* **GitHub** (`github`, `text`, optional): Your GitHub profile URL for coding and development purposes.
* **Stack Overflow** (`stack_overflow`, `text`, optional): Your Stack Overflow profile URL for coding and development purposes.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `text`, optional): A link to your portfolio or website showcasing your work.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant tech development skills you possess, such as Development, Design, Testing, Data Science, Data Analysis.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, `text`, required): Attach or upload your resume for review.
* **Resume Link** (`resume_link`, `text`, optional): A URL to your online resume for review.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Available to Start** (`available_to_start`, `date`, required): The earliest date you can start working.
* **Earliest Available** (`earliest_available`, `date`, required): The earliest date and time you can start working.
* **Job Type Details** (`job_type_details`, `text`, optional): Additional information about your job type.
* **Employment Details** (`employment_details`, `text`, optional): Additional information about your employment status.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your expected salary range.
* **Available Location** (`available_location`, `text`, optional): Your preferred location for work.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, optional): A secondary job title.
* **Employment Type 2** (`employment_type_2`, `text`, optional): A secondary employment type.
* **Available to Start Date** (`available_to_start_date`, `date`, required): The date you can start working.
* **Available to Start Time** (`available_to_start_time`, `time`, required): The time you can start working.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Additional comments or details about your application.

Note: This form is designed to be used for tech developers, but some fields may be applicable to other industries as well. Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth hiring process.
