# Direct Deposit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Deposit Form is used to collect information from employees about their direct deposit details, including their direct deposit status and bank information. This form helps to maintain accurate employee records and facilitate direct deposit-related communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Indicate your Direct Deposit status by selecting the correct option for Direct Deposit Employer.
5. Identify your Direct Deposit Bank.
6. Enter your Direct Deposit Routing number.
7. Enter your Direct Deposit Account number.
8. Select the frequency of your Direct Deposit payments.
9. Click on the Submit button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Direct Deposit Employer** (select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a direct deposit employer by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Direct Deposit Bank** (select_one, required): Identify your direct deposit bank by selecting the correct option.
* **Direct Deposit Routing** (text, optional): Enter your direct deposit routing number.
* **Direct Deposit Account** (text, optional): Enter your direct deposit account number.
* **Direct Deposit Frequency** (select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your direct deposit payments. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to submit your information.
* **Confirm Password** (text, optional): This field is for confirmation of your password (not for actual password submission). It seems to be a redundant field and might be removed or modified in the future.
