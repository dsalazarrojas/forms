# International Employment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Employment Agreement Form is a document used to capture and record essential details about a job, including job title, description, location, and employment status. This form is intended to facilitate clear communication and ensure that all parties involved in the employment process have access to the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job title in the designated field.
2. Write a concise description of the job in the job description field.
3. Select the job type (full-time, part-time, or freelance) from the options provided.
4. Enter the job location.
5. Select the job level (entry, mid, or senior) from the options provided.
6. Choose the employment status (active, inactive, or leave) from the options provided.
7. Choose the job status (ongoing or completed) from the options provided.
8. Select the employment type (full-time, part-time, or freelance) from the options provided.
9. Enter any additional job notes.
10. If applicable, enter the employment agreement and agreement terms.
11. Enter the company address.
12. Enter the employee address.
13. Select the employment type (full-time, part-time, or freelance) from the options provided.
14. Select the agreement term.
15. Mark the employment agreement as signed or not signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter a brief description of the job title.
* **Job Description**: Write a concise description of the job.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where the job is based.
* **Job Level**: Select the level of the job (entry, mid, or senior).
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of the job.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the end date of the job.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Job Notes**: Add any additional information about the job.
* **Agreement Terms**: Enter any terms of the employment agreement.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company.
* **Employee Address**: Enter the address of the employee.
* **Employment Status**: Select the employment status (active, inactive, or leave).
* **Job Status**: Select the job status (ongoing or completed).
* **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* **Job Category**: Enter a category for the job.
* **Job Category Type**: Enter a type for the job category.
* **Job Category Subcategory**: Enter a subcategory for the job category.
* **Job Description**: Write a brief description of the job.
* **Employment Agreement Signed**: Mark whether the employment agreement has been signed.

## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a field is optional, fill it out only when applicable.
