# Career Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Counseling Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients about their employment status, goals, and challenges. This form helps us understand their needs and provide personalized career counseling services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Client name** field (required).
2. Provide your contact email address for scheduling follow-up (required).
3. Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
4. Describe your short-term and long-term career goals in the **Career goals** field.
5. If applicable, list your education background and relevant skills in the **Education background** and **Skills and strengths** fields.
6. If you are facing any challenges, list them in the **Challenges facing** field.
7. Choose your preferred counseling format (in person, virtual, or phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for scheduling follow-up.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Current employment status** (`current_employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Student, or Other).
* **Career goals** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your short-term and long-term career goals.
* **Education background** (`education_background`, text, optional): List your highest level of education achieved.
* **Skills and strengths** (`skills_and_strengths`, text, optional): List your relevant skills and strengths.
* **Challenges facing** (`challenges_facing`, text, optional): List any challenges you are facing.
* **Preferred counseling format** (`preferred_counseling_format`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred format for counseling sessions (In person, Virtual, or Phone).
