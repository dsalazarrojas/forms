# Adjunct Faculty Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adjunct Faculty Employment Application form is used to gather information from potential adjunct faculty applicants. This form helps to streamline the hiring process by collecting necessary contact and educational information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name as it appears on legal documents.
2.  Enter your email address for recruitment communications.
3.  Provide your best phone number for contact.
4.  List your academic qualifications, such as degrees and certifications earned.
5.  Briefly describe your teaching experience, including courses taught and years of experience.
6.  Indicate which courses you are interested in teaching.
7.  Specify your availability for teaching, including days and times.
8.  Provide contact details for two professional references.
9.  If applicable, include any additional information relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on legal documents.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for recruitment communications.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your best phone number for contact.
*   **Academic qualifications** (`academic_qualifications`, `text`, required): List your degrees and certifications earned.
*   **Teaching experience** (`teaching_experience`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your teaching experience, including courses taught and years of experience.
*   **Courses interested** (`courses_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the courses for which you would like to teach.
*   **Availability for semester** (`availability`, `text`, required): Specify your availability for teaching, including days and times.
*   **References contact details** (`references`, `text`, required): Provide email and name or phone and name of two professional references.
*   **Additional information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Include any additional relevant details that will help us consider your application.
