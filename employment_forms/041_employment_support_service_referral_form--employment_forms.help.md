# employment_support_service_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employment_support_service_referral_form" is a form used to gather information about job seekers who require employment support services. This form helps the employment support service provide the necessary resources and support to help job seekers find employment opportunities that match their needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job seeker's First Name.
2. Enter the job seeker's Last Name.
3. Enter the job seeker's Email address.
4. Enter the job seeker's Phone number.
5. Select the job seeker's preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Mail).
6. Indicate the job seeker's current Employment Status (Employed, Unemployed, Not Looking, or Retired).
7. Choose the job seeker's Employment Type (Part-time, Full-time, Seasonal, or Other).
8. Provide a brief description of the job seeker's Job Seeker Details.
9. (Optional) Enter the job seeker's Organization.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the job seeker's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the job seeker's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the job seeker's email address. This is a valid email address for the job seeker to receive communication and updates from the employment support service.
* **Phone**: Enter the job seeker's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the job seeker's preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Mail). This will influence how the employment support service will reach out to them.
* **Employment Status**: Indicate the job seeker's current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Not Looking, or Retired). This will help the employment support service tailor its support services accordingly.
* **Employment Type**: Choose the type of employment the job seeker is seeking (Part-time, Full-time, Seasonal, or Other). This will help the employment support service provide relevant resources and opportunities.
* **Job Seeker Details**: Provide a brief description of the job seeker's situation, goals, and needs. This will help the employment support service better understand the job seeker's requirements.
* **Organization**: (Optional) Enter the job seeker's current or previous organization. This can be helpful for the employment support service to provide industry-specific support.
* **Organization**: Note that if the job seeker does not have a current or previous organization, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are not submitting this form for a job seeker who has already been referred to or received support from the employment support service.
* If you're unsure about a job seeker's status or eligibility for the employment support service, contact the administrator for guidance.
* Be accurate and detailed when filling out the job seeker's details to ensure the employment support service can provide the best possible support.
