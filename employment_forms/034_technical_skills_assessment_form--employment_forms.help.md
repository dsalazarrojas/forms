# technical_skills_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technical skills assessment form is used to gather information about your technical skills and experience. This form will help us assess your skills level and provide you with feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required: false): This is where you will enter your basic candidate information, such as name and contact details.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your relevant technical skills from the dropdown list. Select all that apply.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required: false): Enter your relevant work experience in this field. This will help us assess your skills level.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your proficiency in programming languages from the dropdown list.
* **Operating Systems** (`operating_systems`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your experience with operating systems from the dropdown list.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required: false): List any relevant certifications you have earned.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required: false): Enter your educational background in this field.
* **Resume File** (`resume_file`, text, required: false): Upload your resume as a file.
* **Skill Level** (`skills_level`, text, required: false): Rate your skill level on a scale of 1-5.
* **Skills Rating** (`skills_rating`, text, required: false): Rate your skills proficiency on a scale of 1-5.

Note: The "required" property for each field is false, indicating that these fields are not required to be filled out.
