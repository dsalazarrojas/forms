# retail_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail manager application form is designed to help job applicants apply for retail management positions. It gathers essential information from applicants, including contact details, education qualifications, job preferences, and employment history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all the required fields.
2. Complete the "Retail Manager" page by providing your experience in retail management, store location, and job category.
3. Move to the "Contact Information" page and enter your contact details, including email, phone number, and address.
4. On the "Job Details" page, select your job preferences, job title, department, job status, job type, and job category 2.
5. Upload your resume and other documents if required.
6. Finally, review all the fields and submit the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location (Store Location)** (`store_locationchartinstance`, Required): Enter the location of the retail store you're applying to.
* **Job Category (Job Category)** (select_one, Required): Select whether you have experience in retail management.
* **Years of Experience (Years of Experience)** (number, Required): Enter the number of years you have worked in retail management.
* **Education Qualifications (Education Qualifications)** (text, Required): Describe your educational background and any relevant qualifications.
* **Job Preferences (Job Preferences)** (text, Optional): List your job preferences, if any.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (note, Required): Enter your contact details, including your email, phone number, and address.
* **Upload Resume (Upload Resume)** (select_multiple, Required): Select whether you're willing to upload your resume.
* **Upload Other Documents (Upload Other Documents)** (select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you're willing to upload other documents.
* **Submit (Submit)** (text, Optional): Click the submit button to complete the application process.
* **Email (Email)** (email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date (Date)** (date, Required): Select the date for the interview or application submission.
* **Time (Time)** (time, Required): Select the time for the interview or application submission.
* **Address (Address)** (text, Required): Enter your address for correspondence.
* **Zip Code (Zip Code)** (number, Required): Enter your zip code for mailing purposes.
* **City (City)** (text, Required): Enter the city where you're applying from.
* **State (State)** (text, Required): Enter the state where you're applying from.
* **Country (Country)** (text, Required): Enter the country where you're applying from.
* **Job Description (Job Description)** (select_multiple, Required): Select whether you're interested in the job description.
* **Job Title (Job Title)** (text, Required): Enter the job title you're applying for.
* **Department (Department)** (text, Required): Enter the department you're applying to.
* **Job Status (Job Status)** (select_one, Required): Select the job status you're currently in.
* **Job Type (Job Type)** (select_one, Required): Select whether you're willing to work as a full-time or part-time employee.
* **Job Category 2 (Job Category 2)** (select_multiple, Required): Select any additional job categories you're interested in.
