# Passenger Disclosure And Attestation To The United States Of America - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about passenger travel to the United States of America for the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. Select your country of citizenship from the list in the "Citizenship Country" field.
6. Enter your flight information in the "Flight Info" field.
7. Enter your flight number in the "Flight Number" field.
8. Enter your departure airport in the "Flight Departure Airport" field.
9. Enter your arrival airport in the "Flight Arrival Airport" field.
10. Enter the duration of your flight in the "Flight Duration" field.
11. Enter the frequency of your flight in the "Flight Frequency" field.
12. Enter the type of flight you are taking in the "Flight Type" field.
13. Enter the type of travel you are undertaking in the "Travel Type" field.
14. Enter the destination of your flight in the "Destination" field.
15. Enter the purpose of your travel in the "Purpose" field.
16. Enter the country you are visiting in the "Country Visited" field.
17. Enter the name of the airline you are traveling with in the "Airline" field.
18. Enter the type of passenger you are (adult, child, infant) in the "Passenger Type" field.
19. Enter the number of your seat in the "Seat Number" field.
20. Enter the class of seat you are traveling in the "Seat Class" field.
21. Enter the time of your flight boarding in the "Flight Boarding" field.
22. Enter the name of your travel agent in the "Travel Agent" field.
23. Enter the contact phone number of your travel agent in the "Travel Agent Phone" field.
24. Enter the email address of your travel agent in the "Travel Agent Email" field.
25. Enter the time of your flight boarding in the "Flight Boarding Time" field.

- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.

- **Country Visited** (text, optional): Enter the country you are visiting.
- **Airline** (text, optional): Enter the name of the airline you are traveling with.
- **Passenger Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of passenger you are (adult, child, infant).
- **Seat Number** (number, optional): Enter the number of your seat.
