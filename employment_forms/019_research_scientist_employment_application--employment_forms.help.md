# Research Scientist Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Scientist Employment Application form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a research scientist position. The form gathers data on the candidate's contact details, research experience, education, references, CV, employment status, and job title. This information will help the employer or hiring manager to quickly and efficiently evaluate the candidate's qualifications and suitability for the research scientist role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the Contact Info section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief description of your research experience in the Research Experience section.
3. List your educational background and qualifications in the Education section.
4. Enter the names and contact information of your references in the References section.
5. Upload your CV in the CV section.
6. Select your current employment status from the Employment Status dropdown menu.
7. Enter your job title in the Job Title field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (text, optional)**: This section is for you to provide your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Research Experience (text, optional)**: This section is for you to describe your research experience, including your research background, research focus, and relevant skills. Please provide a brief summary of your research experience.
* **Education (text, optional)**: This section is for you to list your educational background, including your highest level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D.) and relevant courses or specializations.
* **References (text, optional)**: This section is for you to provide the names and contact information of your references. Please ensure that you have permission from your references to share their information.
* **CV (text, optional)**: This section is for you to upload your CV, which should include your work experience, research experience, and education.
* **Employment Status (select_one, optional)**: This section is for you to select your current employment status (Employed, Self-Employed, Student, or Retired).
* **Job Title (text, optional)**: This section is for you to enter your job title.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the employer or hiring manager can quickly and efficiently evaluate your qualifications.
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting it to ensure that there are no errors or typos.
* If you are uploading a CV, please ensure that it is in a format that is easily readable (e.g., PDF, Word doc).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
