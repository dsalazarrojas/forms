# Energy Industry Analyst Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information from applicants for an Energy Industry Analyst position. It is intended to gather personal, professional, and background information from candidates to help employers make informed hiring decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal details on **Personal Info**.
2. Provide your contact information in **Contact Info**.
3. Describe your current and previous employment history in **Employment Info**.
4. Share your educational background in **Education**.
5. List your work experience and accomplishments in **Work Experience**.
6. Mention any relevant skills you possess in **Skills**.
7. If applicable, list your certifications or additional qualifications in **Certifications**.
8. Finally, upload or attach your resume in **Resume**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (Page 1)**: This field is for general contact information, including your name, email, and other personal details.
* **Contact Info (Page 2)**: Provide your email, phone number, and other contact details.
* **Employment Info (Page 3)**: Describe your current and previous employment history, including job titles, company names, and work dates.
* **Education (Page 4)**: List your educational background, including institutions attended, degrees earned, and dates of attendance.
* **Work Experience (Page 5)**: Share your work history and experience, including job titles, company names, and duration of employment.
* **Skills (Page 6)**: List any relevant skills you possess, including programming languages, tools, or software proficiency.
* **Certifications (Page 7)**: If applicable, list any certifications or additional qualifications you hold.
* **Resume (Page 8)**: Attach or upload your resume for further review.

## Tips
Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form. Ensure that your responses are relevant to the Energy Industry Analyst position and the requirements specified in the job posting. If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact the hiring team for assistance.
