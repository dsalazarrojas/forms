# Agent Commission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Commission Form is used for tracking and calculating commissions for sales agents. It captures information about a specific transaction or policy sale, including the agent's name, employee ID, sales details, and commission-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Agent Name** (Form ID 2) and **Employee ID** (Form ID 3) in the respective fields. These are both required fields.
2. Provide the **Transaction or Policy Number** (Form ID 5), **Date of Sale** (Form ID 6), and **Total Sales Amount** (Form ID 7) for the transaction. These fields are also required.
3. Select the **Commission Tier** (Form ID 8) that applies to the sales amount based on the provided options (Standard, Silver, Gold, Platinum).
4. Enter the **Applicable Commission Rate** (Form ID 9) as a percentage.
5. Calculate the **Calculated Commission Amount** (Form ID 10) using the provided commission rate.
6. If any **Deductions or Adjustments** (Form ID 11) need to be accounted for, enter them here.
7. Enter the **Net Commission Payable** (Form ID 12), which is the final amount to be paid after any deductions.
8. Finally, select the **Approval Status** (Form ID 13) as (Pending Review, Approved for Payment, Disputed, or Rejected) for management review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name (Agent Name)**: Enter the name of the sales agent responsible for the transaction.
* **Employee ID (Employee ID)**: Enter the unique identifier for the sales agent.
* **Transaction or Policy Number (Transaction or Policy Number)**: Provide the reference number for the sale.
* **Date of Sale (Date of Sale)**: Enter the date of the sale.
* **Total Sales Amount (Total Sales Amount)**: Enter the gross value of the transaction.
* **Commission Tier (Commission Tier - Standard, Silver, Gold, Platinum)**: Select the commission tier that applies to the sales amount.
* **Applicable Commission Rate (Applicable Commission Rate - %)**: Enter the percentage used for commission calculation.
* **Calculated Commission Amount (Calculated Commission Amount)**: This field is automatically calculated based on the provided commission rate.
* **Deductions or Adjustments (Deductions or Adjustments - if any)**: Enter any negative adjustments.
* **Net Commission Payable (Net Commission Payable)**: This is the final amount to be paid after any deductions.
* **Approval Status (Approval Status)**: Select the status for management review.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any errors in commission calculations.
- If there are any doubts about the commission tier or rate, consult with your sales management.
- Double-check the calculated commission amount to ensure it's correct.
- Keep in mind that any deductions or adjustments will affect the final amount payable.
