# Recruitment of Managers Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about manager positions available for recruitment. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant details to help attract the right candidates for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name as they appear on your official documents.
2. Enter your email address that you use for professional correspondence.
3. Select the title of the manager position you are applying for.
4. Provide a brief job description to help candidates understand the responsibilities and requirements of the role.
5. Enter your availability for an interview or work schedule.
6. Enter the number of work hours you are willing to commit to.
7. Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
8. Enter the location where you will be working.
9. Select the type of employment you are seeking (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
10. Select the status of the job (Open, Closed, or Pending).
11. Choose the employment type that applies to your job (Salary, Benefits, or Other).
12. Select the level of work experience you have.
13. List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess.
14. Finally, review your submission to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address you use for professional correspondence.
* **Manager Title** (select_multiple, required): Select the title of the manager position you are applying for (Senior Manager, Team Lead, Department Head).
* **Job Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the job and its responsibilities.
* **Availability** (date, required): Enter your availability for an interview or work schedule.
* **Work Hours** (number, required): Enter the number of work hours you are willing to commit to.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Job Location** (text, required): Enter the location where you will be working.
* **Job Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of employment you are seeking (Full Time, Part Time, Contract).
* **Job Status** (select_one, required): Select the status of the job (Open, Closed, Pending).
* **Employment Type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the employment type that applies to your job (Salary, Benefits, Other).
* **Work Experience** (select_one, required): Select the level of work experience you have (0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years).
* **Skills** (text, required): List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess.
