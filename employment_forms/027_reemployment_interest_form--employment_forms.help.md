# reemployment_interest_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on a user's former employment history, their availability to be reemployed, role preferences, and job title. It is meant to be filled out by individuals who are interested in being reemployed or re-hired by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and ensure that you have a clear understanding of what the form is asking for.
2. Answer the questions accordingly, and make sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Former Employment** (former_employment, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks whether you have had any previous employment experience. Please select all that apply.
* **Availability** (availability, date, required/optional): Please enter your availability date, in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help us understand your reemployment schedule.
* **Role Preferences** (role_preferences, select_one, required/optional): This field asks about your role preferences. Please select one of the options that best suits your needs.
* **Role** (role_b, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks for your preferred roles. Please select all that apply.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required/optional): This field asks for your job title. Please enter a brief description of your job title.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, and avoid leaving any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to do so.
