# Tech Industry Developer Job Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for job applicants to provide their employment details and upload their resume for consideration for tech industry development positions. Please ensure you have all necessary documents ready before starting the application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the application process.
2. Upload your resume in the designated field.
3. Fill out the required fields with your job title, company name, experience, contact email, and preferred resume language.
4. Select your relevant skills from the drop-down menu.
5. Provide your education background, if applicable.
6. Add any additional links to your portfolio or personal website, if applicable.
7. Enter your contact phone number, website, LinkedIn, GitHub, and Twitter profiles, if applicable.
8. Specify your visa status and work authorization, if applicable.
9. Attach any necessary documents for work authorization.
10. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume Upload** (Resume Upload, text, required): Upload your resume in PDF or Word format. Please ensure your resume is up-to-date and tailored to the tech industry.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your current or desired job title in the tech industry.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the name of your current or past employer in the tech industry.
* **Skills** (Skills, text, optional): Select one or more relevant skills from the drop-down menu to showcase your expertise in the tech industry.
* **Experience** (Experience, select_one, required): Choose your relevant experience in the tech industry.
* **Education** (Education, text, optional): Provide your educational background, including degree(s) earned and institution(s) attended.
* **Portfolio** (Portfolio, text, optional): Add a link to your portfolio or personal website, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Website** (Website, text, optional): Add a link to your personal website or blog, if applicable.
* **LinkedIn** (LinkedIn, text, optional): Add your LinkedIn profile link.
* **Github** (Github, text, optional): Add your GitHub profile link.
* **Twitter** (Twitter, text, optional): Add your Twitter profile link.
* **Resume Language** (Resume Language, select_one, required): Select the language in which your resume is written.
* **Work Authorization** (Work Authorization, select_multiple, optional): Specify if you have work authorization or not. If not, provide an explanation for "Other - Specify".
* **Visa Status** (Visa Status, select_multiple, optional): Specify your visa status. If not, choose "Other - Specify" and add an explanation in the "Visa Type" and "Visa Number" fields.
* **Visa Type** (Visa Type, text, optional): Enter your visa type, if applicable. This field is only visible if you chose "Other - Specify" in the "Visa Status" field.
* **Visa Number** (Visa Number, number, optional): Enter your visa number, if applicable.
* **Passport Number** (Passport Number, number, optional): Enter your passport number, if applicable.
* **Work Permit** (Work Permit, text, optional): Enter your work permit number, if applicable.
* **I-94 Number** (I-94 Number, text, optional): Enter your I-94 number, if applicable.
* **I-94 Number** (I-94 Number, text, optional): Enter your I-94 number, if applicable.
* **Work Authorization Upload** (Work Authorization Upload, text, optional): Upload any necessary documents for work authorization.
* **Additional Documents** (Additional Documents, text, optional): Upload any additional relevant documents.
* **Submission Agreement** (Submission Agreement, note, required): Carefully read and agree to the submission agreement terms and conditions before proceeding.
