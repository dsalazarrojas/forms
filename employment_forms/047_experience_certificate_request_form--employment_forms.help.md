# Experience Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experience Certificate Request Form is a document used to request an experience certificate for a variety of purposes, including employment, professional development, or other reasons. This form gathers information from the employee, manager, and other stakeholders to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
   - Employee Name
   - Employee ID
   - Reporting Manager
   - Company Name
   - Department
   - Job Title
   - Reporting Start Date
   - Reporting End Date
   - Reason for Leave
   - Request Date
   - Certificate Number
   - Issued By
   - Issued Date
   - Certificate Status
   - Signature of Employee
   - Signature of Manager
   - Signature of Administrator
   - Certificate Type (choose all relevant options)
   - Uploaded Document (optional)
2. If applicable, select all relevant options for Certificate Required For and Certificate Type.
3. Ensure accurate and legible signature of the employee, manager, and administrator.
4. Upload any relevant documents as proof of experience.
5. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (<id: 1>):** Enter the name of the employee requesting the certificate.
* **Employee ID (<id: 2>):** Enter the unique identification number of the employee.
* **Reporting Manager (<id: 3>):** Enter the name of the reporting manager.
* **Company Name (<id: 4>):** Enter the name of the company where the employee worked.
* **Department (<id: 5>):** Enter the department where the employee worked.
* **Job Title (<id: 6>):** Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Reporting Start Date (<id: 7>):** Enter the start date of employment.
* **Reporting End Date (<id: 8>):** Enter the end date of employment.
* **Reason for Leave (<id: 9>):** Explain the reason for requesting an experience certificate.
* **Request Date (<id: 10>):** Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted By (<id: 11>):** Select the person who submitted the form (John Doe, Jane Smith, or Jim Johnson).
* **Submitted To (<id: 12>):** Select all relevant recipients (HR, Administrator, or Management).
* **Certificate Required For (<id: 13>):** Choose all relevant options for certificate purpose (Employment, Professional Development, or Other).
* **Other (please specify) (<id: 14>):** If Other is selected, provide a brief description.
* **Signature of Employee (<id: 15>):** Ensure a legible and accurate signature of the employee.
* **Signature of Manager (<id: 16>):** Ensure a legible and accurate signature of the manager.
* **Signature of Administrator (<id: 17>):** Ensure a legible and accurate signature of the administrator.
* **Certificate Number (<id: 18>):** Enter a unique number assigned to the certificate.
* **Issued By (<id: 19>):** Enter the name of the person issuing the certificate.
* **Issued Date (<id: 20>):** Enter the date of issuance.
* **Certificate Status (<id: 21>):** Select the status of the certificate (In Progress, Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Notes (<id: 22]):** Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Uploaded Document (<id: 23]):** If applicable, upload any relevant documents as proof of experience.
* **Uploaded Document Type (<id: 24]):** Select the type of document uploaded (PDF, Word Docx, Excel, Powerpoint, CSV, or Text File).
* **Uploaded Document Size (<id: 25>):** Enter the size of the uploaded document.
