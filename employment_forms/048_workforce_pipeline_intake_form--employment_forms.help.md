# Workforce Pipeline Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Pipeline Intake Form is a tool used to collect information from candidates for job openings within the workforce pipeline. This form is designed to gather essential details about the candidate's qualifications, availability, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select your job title from the list of available options.
6. Choose your job type (full-time, contract, internship, or part-time) and select all relevant options.
7. Upload or paste your resume in the "Resume" field.
8. Enter your start date availability in the "Availability (Start Date)" field.
9. Enter your end date availability in the "Availability (End Date)" field.
10. Enter your work hours in the "Work Hours" field.
11. Describe your job experience and skills in the "Job Description" field.
12. Enter your availability for work hours in the "Page 12" field (note: this field is identical to "Work Hours" and is likely a duplicate, please ignore).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`page_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`page_3`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`page_4`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (`page_5`, select_one, required/optional): Select your job title from the list:
	+ Software Engineer
	+ Data Scientist
	+ Project Manager
	+ Other
* **Job Type** (`page_6`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more job types:
	+ Full-time
	+ Contract
	+ Internship
	+ Part-time
* **Resume** (`page_7`, text, required/optional): Upload or paste your resume.
* **Availability (Start Date)** (`page_8`, date, required/optional): Enter your start date availability.
* **Availability (End Date)** (`page_9`, date, required/optional): Enter your end date availability.
* **Work Hours** (`page_10`, time, required/optional): Enter your work hours.
* **Job Description** (`page_11`, text, required/optional): Describe your job experience and skills.
* **Page 12** (`page_12`, time, required/optional): Enter your availability for work hours (note: this field is identical to "Work Hours" and is likely a duplicate, please ignore).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your resume is in a compatible format (PDF, Docx, or plain text).
* Use a 24-hour clock format for work hours (e.g., 08:00, 12:00, 16:00).
* If you have any trouble completing this form, please ask your hiring manager or recruiter for assistance.
