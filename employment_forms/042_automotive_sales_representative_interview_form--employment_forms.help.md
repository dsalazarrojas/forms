# Automotive Sales Representative Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a candidate's experience, skills, and qualifications for a sales position in the automotive industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the candidate's name, email address, phone number, and position applied for sections (fields 2, 3, 4, and 5).
2. Choose one option for each of the select_one fields, as requested (fields 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, and 25).
3. Provide a detailed description for the text fields (fields 23 and 24).
4. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (Field 2, text, *): Please enter the candidate's name.
* **Email Address** (Field 3, email, *): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Field 4, text, *): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Position Applied For** (Field 5, text, *): Choose the sales position the candidate is applying for.
* **Interview Date** (Field 7, date, *): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (Field 8, time, *): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (Field 9, text, *): Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Years of Sales Experience** (Field 11, number, *): Enter the number of years the candidate has worked in sales.
* **Years of Automotive Sales Experience** (Field 12, number, *): Enter the number of years the candidate has worked in the automotive sales industry.
* **Previous Dealerships** (Field 13, text): Enter any previous dealerships the candidate has worked at.
* **Product Knowledge** (Field 15, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's knowledge of automotive products:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Limited
  * Willing to Learn
* **Vehicle Features and Specs** (Field 16, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's knowledge of vehicle features and specs:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Poor
* **Financing Knowledge** (Field 17, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's knowledge of auto financing options:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Limited
* **Communication Skills Rating** (Field 19, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's communication skills:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Needs Development
* **Customer Rapport** (Field 20, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's ability to build customer rapport:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Needs Development
* **Sales Closing Ability** (Field 21, select_one, *): Choose your evaluation of the candidate's sales closing ability:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Needs Development
* **Objection Handling** (Field 23, text): Describe how the candidate would handle customer objections.
* **Hiring Recommendation** (Field 25, select_one, *): Choose your overall recommendation for the candidate:
  * Highly Recommend
  * Recommend
  * Consider
  * Do Not Recommend
