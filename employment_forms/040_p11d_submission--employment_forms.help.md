# P11DSubmission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's vehicle details for P11D submissions. P11D is a tax form used by employees to report their company-provided vehicles. This form helps in gathering accurate and comprehensive data for the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your personal details carefully.
2.  Start by filling in your personal information:
    * Employee Name: Enter your name as it is recorded by the company.
    * Employee ID: Enter your employee ID number.
3.  Next, provide details about your vehicle:
    * Car Details: Briefly describe your vehicle.
    * Car Type: Select the type of vehicle you have (Car, Van, or Truck).
    * Car Year: Enter the year of purchase for your vehicle.
    * Car Color: Select the color of your vehicle (Red, Blue, or Green).
4.  Provide dates for your vehicle usage:
    * Start Date: Enter the date you started using the vehicle.
    * End Date: Enter the date you stopped using the vehicle.
5.  Indicate your employee status and benefits status:
    * Employee Status: Select your current status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave).
    * Benefits Status: Select your benefits eligibility status (Eligible, Not Eligible, or Pending).
6.  If you have any vehicle-related benefits:
    * Benefits Start Date: Enter the date your vehicle benefits started.
    * Benefits End Date: Enter the date your vehicle benefits ended.
7.  Optionally, you can add notes about your vehicle and your benefits.
8.  If you have a manager overseeing your vehicle details:
    * Manager Name: Enter your manager's name.
    * Manager Email: Enter your manager's email address.
    * Manager Phone: Enter your manager's phone number.
9.  Finally, provide information about your department and job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name): Enter your personal name as it is recorded by the company. (Required)
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID): Enter your employee ID number. (Required)
* **Car Details** (Car Details): Briefly describe your vehicle. (Required)
* **Car Type** (Car Type): Select the type of vehicle you have (Car, Van, or Truck). (Required)
* **Car Year** (Car Year): Enter the year of purchase for your vehicle. (Required)
* **Car Color** (Car Color): Select the color of your vehicle (Red, Blue, or Green). (Required)
* **Start Date** (Start Date): Enter the date you started using the vehicle. (Required)
* **End Date** (End Date): Enter the date you stopped using the vehicle. (Required)
* **Employee Status** (Employee Status): Select your current status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave). (Required)
* **Benefits Status** (Benefits Status): Select your benefits eligibility status (Eligible, Not Eligible, or Pending). (Required)
* **Benefits Start Date** (Benefits Start Date): Enter the date your vehicle benefits started. (Required)
* **Benefits End Date** (Benefits End Date): Enter the date your vehicle benefits ended. (Required)
* **Notes** (Notes): Optionally, add any notes about your vehicle. (Not Required)
* **Notes for benefits** (Notes for benefits): Optionally, add any notes about your vehicle benefits. (Not Required)
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name): Enter your manager's name. (Not Required)
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email): Enter your manager's email address. (Not Required)
* **Manager Phone** (Manager Phone): Enter your manager's phone number. (Not Required)
* **Department** (Department): Enter your department information. (Not Required)
* **Job Title** (Job Title): Enter your job title information. (Not Required)

This form is designed to be user-friendly and helps to ensure accurate and comprehensive submission of P11D.
