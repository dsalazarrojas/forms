# Probationary Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the terms and conditions of a probationary employment contract, ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately and efficiently. It's essential to fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or misunderstandings during the employment period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details, including their name.
2. Provide information about the job title and employment type.
3. Specify the date of hire and start date of probation.
4. Enter the end date of probation and end date of the contract.
5. Choose payment methods and frequency.
6. Select benefits and performance measures applicable to the employee.
7. Enter notice periods and severance pay out (if applicable).
8. Specify the notice period end and end of probationary period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Specify the title of the position the employee is hired for.
* **Job Terms and Conditions** (Job Terms and Conditions, text, optional): Describe the terms and conditions of the employment, including any specific requirements or expectations.
* **Employment Type** (Employment Type, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the employment is permanent, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date of Hire** (Date of Hire, date, optional): Enter the date the employee starts working for the company.
* **Start Date of Probation** (Start Date of Probation, date, optional): Enter the date the probationary period starts.
* **End Date of Probation** (End Date of Probation, date, optional): Enter the date the probationary period ends.
* **Salary or Wage** (Salary or Wage, text, optional): Specify the employee's salary or wage rate.
* **Pay Period** (Pay Period, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if payments are made during the probationary period, 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, optional): Select all payment methods applicable to the employee (e.g., direct deposit, check, etc.).
* **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of payments made (e.g., weekly, biweekly, etc.).
* **Notice Period** (Notice Period, number, optional): Enter the notice period required for termination.
* **Contract End Date** (Contract End Date, date, optional): Enter the date the employment contract ends.
* **Reason for End of Contract** (Reason for End of Contract, text, optional): Describe the reason for termination.
* **Termination Notice Period** (Termination Notice Period, number, optional): Enter the notice period required for termination.
* **Severance Pay Out** (Severance Pay Out, number, optional): Specify the severance pay amount.
* **Benefits** (Benefits, select_multiple, optional): Select benefits applicable to the employee (e.g., health insurance, retirement plan, etc.).
* **Performance Measures** (Performance Measures, select_multiple, optional): Choose performance measures applicable to the employee (e.g., attendance, productivity, etc.).
* **Termination Notice Period (Remaining)** (Termination Notice Period (Remaining), number, optional): Enter the remaining notice period after termination.
* **Notice Period End** (Notice Period End, date, optional): Enter the date the notice period ends.
* **Probationary Period** (Probationary Period, date, optional): Enter the date the probationary period starts.
* **End of Probationary Period** (End of Probationary Period, date, optional): Enter the date the probationary period ends.
* **Notice Period End of Probation** (Notice Period End of Probation, date, optional): Enter the date the notice period ends.
* **End Date of Probation** (End Date of Probation, date, optional): Enter the date the probationary period ends.
