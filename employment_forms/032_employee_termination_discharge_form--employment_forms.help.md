# Employee Termination Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Termination Discharge Form is used to document the details of an employee's termination, including the reason for termination, effective date, and manager approval. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the employee's information, including their name and any necessary contact details.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for the employee's termination.
3. Enter the effective date of the termination.
4. Select the manager's approval for the termination.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm the termination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Data**: Enter the employee's name and any other relevant contact information. (Type: Text, Required: No)
* **Reason for Termination**: Enter a clear and concise reason for the termination. This will help the HR department understand the circumstances surrounding the termination. (Type: Text, Required: No)
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date the termination is to take effect. (Type: Date, Required: No)
* **Manager Approval**: Select the manager's approval for the termination. Please choose one of the provided options. (Type: Select One, Required: No)
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the termination. This is a required field. (Type: Text, Required: Yes)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure you have the necessary approvals from the relevant parties.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
