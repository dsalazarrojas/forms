# Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Form is used to assess an employee's performance over a specific period. This form will help managers evaluate an employee's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. Please ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not duplicating efforts with other evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select the evaluation period by using the **Evaluation Period** field.
3. Provide a numerical rating for the employee's performance, using a scale of 1-10, in the **Performance Rating** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period**: Choose the specific period for which you are evaluating the employee's performance (e.g., last quarter, last six months, last year).
* **Performance Rating**: Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent. Please be as specific as possible when providing your rating and include any comments or feedback to justify your score.
