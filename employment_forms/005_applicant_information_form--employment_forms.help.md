# Applicant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicant Information Form is a document used to gather essential details from applicants for the purpose of evaluating and processing job applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the **Full Name** field with your first and last name.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** to ensure we can contact you with updates on your application.
3.  Provide your **Phone Number** (optional) for additional contact purposes.
4.  Indicate the **Position Applied For** that you are interested in applying for.
5.  If you have attached a **Resume Upload** file, enter the filename in this field.
6.  If you have a **Portfolio or Professional Website**, provide the URL here.
7.  If you are available to start work, select your earliest **Availability to Start** date.
8.  List two **References** with contact details, if applicable.
9.  Provide any **Additional Information** you wish to share about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Required)**: Enter your first and last name to be identified as the applicant.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your primary contact email to receive updates on your application.
* **Phone Number (Optional)**: Enter your mobile or landline phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Position Applied For (Required)**: Indicate the job title being applied for.
* **Resume Upload Filename (Optional)**: If you have attached a resume, enter the filename here.
* **Portfolio or Professional Website (Optional)**: Provide a URL if you have a portfolio or professional website.
* **Availability to Start (Optional)**: Select your earliest availability to start work.
* **References (Optional)**: List two professional references with contact details, if applicable.
* **Additional Information (Optional)**: Provide any other information you wish to share about yourself.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
