# tourism_multilingual_capability_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's tourism-related job title, type, language skills, location, experience, availability, resume, contact number, and any additional information. This form is used to gather data for employment purposes, likely for HR or recruitment teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Job Title field with your current or desired job title in the tourism industry.
2. Select your Job Type from the provided options (Part-time, Full-time, or Contract).
3. Choose the languages you are proficient in from the provided options (English, Spanish, French, or Chinese).
4. Enter your Location (city or region).
5. Fill out the Experience field with your relevant experience in numbers (e.g., 5 years).
6. Select your availability status (Available or Not available).
7. If applicable, attach a resume to showcase your professional background.
8. Enter your contact number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your current or desired job title in the tourism industry.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (select_one): Select your job type from the options:
	+ Part-time: You work part-time hours in the tourism industry.
	+ Full-time: You work full-time hours in the tourism industry.
	+ Contract: You work on a contract basis in the tourism industry.

### Language
* **Language** (select_multiple): Choose the languages you are proficient in:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Chinese

### Location
* **Location** (text): Enter your current or desired location (city or region).

### Experience
* **Experience** (number): Enter your relevant experience in numbers (e.g., 5 years).

### Availability
* **Availability** (select_one): Select your availability status:
	+ Available: You are available to work in the tourism industry.
	+ Not available: You are not available to work in the tourism industry.

### Resume
* **Resume** (note): Attach a resume to showcase your professional background.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (note): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your employment in the tourism industry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when answering the questions.
* Attach a professional resume to showcase your experience and skills.
* Ensure your contact number is up-to-date and accurate.
