# Employment Law Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Law Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals or organizations regarding employment-related legal issues. This form is designed to be a general-purpose form, not limited to employment law experts only. It's intended to be user-friendly and accessible to anyone with employment-related legal questions or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes your employment-related legal issue.
2. Briefly describe your employment-related legal issue in the provided text field.
3. Provide your email address for us to contact you back.
4. Share your contact number (optional).
5. Enter your name (optional).
6. Provide a message with additional details about your inquiry (optional).
7. Check if you have already submitted a similar form or inquiry before.
8. Select the date and time you submitted the form.
9. Indicate who submitted the form (Client, Agent, or Employee).
10. Choose the area of employment law that best describes your issue.
11. Select the specific employment law issue you would like to inquire about.
12. Indicate to whom you submitted the form (HR Manager, Lawyer, or Other).
13. Choose the employment law inquirer (Individual or Organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`employment_law_inquiry_form_fields`): Select the category that best describes your employment-related legal issue. Choose from Employment Forms, Other, or select multiple options.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): This is a unique identifier for the form submission.
* **Category** (`category`): Select a category related to employment law.
* **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe your employment-related legal issue.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Share your contact number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name (optional).
* **Message** (`message`): Provide additional details about your inquiry.
* **Already Submitted** (`form_submitted`): Check if you have already submitted a similar form or inquiry before.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Select the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): Select the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Indicate who submitted the form (Client, Agent, Employee).
* **Area of Employment Law** (`employment_law_area`): Select the area of employment law that best describes your issue.
* **Specific Employment Law Issue** (`employment_law_issue`): Choose the specific employment law issue you would like to inquire about.
* **To Whom Submitted** (`submitted_to`): Indicate to whom you submitted the form (HR Manager, Lawyer, Other).
* **Employment Law Inquirer** (`employment_law_inquirer`): Choose the employment law inquirer (Individual or Organization).
