# Real Estate Agent Onboarding Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Real Estate Agent Onboarding Employment Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from real estate agents who have completed our onboarding process. The form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our onboarding program, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions to better support our agents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. If you have any comments or feedback that didn't fit in the provided options, you can use the "Comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was your onboarding experience like (page 1)
- **What was your onboarding experience like** (<name>, <type>: text): This field allows you to describe your overall experience during the onboarding process. Please provide a detailed account of your experience, highlighting any successes and challenges you faced.

### Overall experience (page 2)
- **Overall experience** (<name>, <type>: select_one): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with our onboarding program. You can select one of the following options:
	* Agent: I was satisfied with the onboarding experience.
	* Office: I was satisfied with the support I received at the office.
	* Training: I was satisfied with the training provided.
	* Support: I was satisfied with the support provided.
	* Other: Please specify a different reason for your feedback.

### How would you rate the effectiveness of our training program (page 3)
- **How would you rate the effectiveness of our training program** (<name>, <type>: number): This field asks you to rate the effectiveness of our training program on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "not effective at all" and 10 is "very effective".

### How would you rate the level of support you received (page 4)
- **How would you rate the level of support you received** (<name>, <type>: number): This field asks you to rate the level of support you received on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "not supportive at all" and 10 is "very supportive".

### Which office location did you work with (page 5)
- **Which office location did you work with** (<name>, <type>: select_multiple): This field asks you to select the office location where you worked during the onboarding process. You can choose one or more of the following options:
	* New York
	* Los Angeles
	* Chicago
	* Other (please specify)

### Comments (page 6)
- **Comments** (<name>, <type>: note): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have that didn't fit in the provided options. Feel free to share as much detail as you'd like.

### JotFormView (page 7)
- **JotFormView** (<name>, <type>: number): This field is not relevant to the form's purpose and is likely a placeholder. Please do not fill out this field.
