# Employee Referral - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Referral form is used to collect information about a person referred for a job opportunity within the organization. This form helps to gather essential details such as contact information, job title, department, and company to facilitate a smooth referral process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the referred employee's first name and last name in the respective fields.
3. Ensure you have permission from the referred employee before sharing their contact information.
4. Fill in the referred employee's contact information, job title, department, and company accurately.
5. Enter your own contact information to enable easy communication with the referred employee.

## Tips

- Please ensure you have the authority to refer this candidate.
- If the referred candidate is already employed within the organization, use the existing referral system to update their details instead.
- Double-check the accuracy of the provided information before submitting the form.
- Be respectful when referring employees for job opportunities within the organization.
