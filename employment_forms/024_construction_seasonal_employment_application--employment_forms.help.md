# Construction Seasonal Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Seasonal Employment Application form is a tool used to collect information from applicants interested in seasonal employment opportunities within the construction industry. The form is designed to help us determine your eligibility and match you with suitable positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full name** in the "Full name" field. This is your legal name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your **Contact email** in the "Contact email" field. This should be the best email address we can use to communicate with you.
3. Enter your **Contact phone number** in the "Contact phone" field. This should be your mobile phone number that we can use to contact you.
4. Indicate the **Position applied for** in the "Position applied for" field. This is the specific role you are applying to.
5. If applicable, specify your **Availability start date** and **Availability end date** in the "Availability start date" and "Availability end date" fields, respectively. These dates are not required, but they help us understand your schedule.
6. If you have any **Certifications** held, such as CSCS or First aid, please list them in the "Certifications held" field. This is optional.
7. If you have relevant **Prior experience years**, please enter the number of years you have spent working in the construction industry in the "Prior experience years" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full name
* **Full name (Text, Required)**: This is your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* 
### Contact email
* **Contact email (Email, Required)**: This is the best email address we can use to communicate with you.
* 
### Contact phone
* **Contact phone (Text, Required)**: This is your mobile phone number that we can use to contact you.
* 
### Position applied for
* **Position applied for (Text, Required)**: This is the specific role you are applying to.
* 
### Availability start date
* **Availability start date (Date, Optional)**: This date represents when you can start work. If not specified, we will assume you can start immediately.
* 
### Availability end date
* **Availability end date (Date, Optional)**: This date represents when you will end your work. If not specified, we will assume you will work until the end of the season.
* 
### Certifications
* **Certifications held (Text, Optional)**: This is a list of certifications you hold, such as CSCS or First aid.
* 
### Prior experience years
* **Prior experience years (Number, Optional)**: This is the number of years you have spent working in the construction industry.
* 
## Tips
* Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Clearly specify your availability dates to help us match you with suitable positions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
