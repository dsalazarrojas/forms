# public_relations_application_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose

The public_relations_application_tracker form is designed to track and monitor interactions with individuals who have contacted the public relations department for job inquiries or other purposes. This form helps the public relations team keep a record of contact information, job application status, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Enter the contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select the job applied for from the "Job Applied" field.
4. Upload or enter the resume in the "Resume" field.
5. Enter any cover letter or relevant document in the "Cover Letter" field.
6. Enter the email address of the applicant in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the applicant in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter the address of the applicant in the "Address" field.
9. Select the job position applied for in the "Job Position" field.
10. Choose the status of the job application from the "Job Status" field.
11. If applicable, enter the interview date in the "Interview Date" field.
11. Indicate whether you have followed up with the applicant in the "Follow Up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: A field for entering the applicant's contact information.
	+ Type: Text fields for entering name, address, etc.
* **Job Applied**: A field for selecting the job applied for.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Enter the job title or job description.
* **Resume**: A field for uploading or entering the resume.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Upload or enter the resume in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Cover Letter**: A field for entering any cover letter or relevant document.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Upload or enter the cover letter in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Email**: A field for entering the applicant's email address.
	+ Type: Email field.
	+ Hint: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: A field for entering the applicant's phone number.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Address**: A field for entering the applicant's address.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Enter a valid address.
* **Job Position**: A field for selecting the job position applied for.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Hint: Enter the job title or job description.
* **Job Status**: A field for selecting the status of the job application.
	+ Type: Select_one field with options: Interviewing, Rejected, Hired, Applied, Declined, On Hold.
	+ Hint: Select the current status of the job application.
* **Interview Date**: A field for entering the interview date.
	+ Type: Date field.
	+ Hint: Enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Follow Up**: A field for indicating whether you have followed up with the applicant.
	+ Type: Select_multiple field with options: Yes, No, Unknown.
	+ Hint: Select whether you have followed up with the applicant, and if not, why.
