# Education Credentials Verification Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal details from individuals to verify their education credentials for employment purposes. It's likely to be used by HR departments or recruiters to gather relevant information from candidates during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
2. Ensure that you provide your consent for the verification process.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`education_credentials_1`, `text`, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is intended to capture your basic contact information for verification purposes.
* **Education Verification** (`education_credentials_2`, `text`, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is meant to verify your education background, please provide your relevant details such as school names, dates of attendance, and degrees earned.
* **Candidate Consent** (`education_credentials_3`, `text`, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is a mandatory section where you must provide your consent for the education verification process. Please indicate your agreement to allow the employer to verify your education credentials.
