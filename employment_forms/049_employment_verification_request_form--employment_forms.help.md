# Employment Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting employment verification for a specific period of time. The goal of this form is to gather necessary information from employers or other relevant parties to verify an individual's employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please review the following steps:

1. Make sure you have all necessary information and documentation required to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, as incomplete forms will not be accepted.
3. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employment Verification Request** (`employment_verification_request`, text, optional): A brief description of why you are requesting employment verification.
  This field allows you to provide a short explanation for your request, such as requesting to verify employment for a new job application or to clarify your employment status for tax purposes.
  
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): The name of the employer who can verify your employment status.
  This field is for the name of the company or organization that you are currently or have been employed with.
  
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): The title of your position at the company.
  This field is for the job title or position that you held at the time of your employment.
  
- **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): The date you started working for the company.
  This field is for the date you began your employment with the company, in the format of month/day/year.
  
- **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): The date you ended your employment with the company.
  This field is for the date you ended your employment with the company, in the format of month/day/year.
  
- **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): A brief explanation for why you left the company.
  This field is for any reason why you left your previous job, such as resignation, layoff, or other reasons.
  
- **Authorized Person** (`authorized_person`, text, optional): The person authorized to verify your employment status.
  This field is for the name of the person who can verify your employment status, such as an HR representative or a supervisor.
  
- **Authorized Title** (`authorized_title`, text, optional): The title of the authorized person.
  This field is for the title of the person who can verify your employment status.
  
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): The name of the organization that can verify your employment status.
  This field is for the name of the company or organization that can verify your employment status.
  
- **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, optional): The address of the organization.
  This field is for the address of the company or organization that can verify your employment status.
  
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): The current status of your employment.
  This field is for selecting whether you are currently employed, not employed, or other options.
  
- **Job Status Reason** (`job_status_reason`, text, optional): A brief explanation for your job status.
  This field is for any reason why you have chosen your job status.
  
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): A contact number for the organization.
  This field is for the contact phone number of the organization.
  
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): A contact email for the organization.
  This field is for the contact email of the organization.
  
- **Verification Requested By** (`verification_requested_by`, text, optional): The person requesting verification.
  This field is for the name of the person requesting verification.
