# Healthcare Nursing Skills Employment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate a healthcare nurse's employment history and skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are filling it out for the correct individual.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the facility representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info

* **Name of Nurse** (`name_of_nurse`, required, text): Please enter the name of the nurse being evaluated.
* **Name of Facility** (`name_of_facility`, required, text): Enter the name of the facility where the nurse is working.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, required, date): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, required, time): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Location of Submission** (`location_of_submission`, required, text): Enter the location where you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, required, text): Enter your name and title (if applicable).
* **Date of Last Update** (`date_of_last_update`, required, date): Enter the last date this form was updated.
* **Time of Last Update** (`time_of_last_update`, required, time): Enter the last time this form was updated.
* **Updated by** (`updated_by`, required, text): Enter the name and title of the person who last updated this form.

### Contact Information

* **Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, required, text): Enter the name and contact information for the facility representative who needs to be contacted.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, required, text): Enter the email of the facility representative who needs to be contacted.
* **Facility Phone Number** (`facility_phone_number`, required, text): Enter the phone number of the facility representative who needs to be contacted.

### Medical History

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, optional, text): List any medical history that may impact the candidate's ability to work in the facility.

### Work Experience

* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, optional, text): Describe the candidate's work experience in the healthcare field.

### Skills

* **Leadership** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the leadership skills the candidate possesses.
* **Communication** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the communication skills the candidate possesses.
* **Teamwork** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the teamwork skills the candidate possesses.
* **Time Management** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the time management skills the candidate possesses.
* **Problem Solving** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the problem-solving skills the candidate possesses.

### Certifications

* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_one, optional): Select the type of certification the candidate holds (e.g., BLS/ACLS, NRP, etc.).

### References

* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, required): Select all the references the candidate has.
