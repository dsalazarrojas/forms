# Biotech Research Publication Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Biotech Research Publication Employment Form is designed to capture information about an individual's personal, professional, and research experience, as well as their technical skills and education, to assess their qualifications for a position in a biotech research setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your personal and contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of position you are applying for (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Postdoctoral Fellow, Graduate Student, Visiting Scholar).
3. Enter your desired start date for the position.
4. Provide information about your education background, including your highest degree, field of study, institution, and year of graduation.
5. Describe your professional experience in biotech or research, including the number of years you have worked in this field.
6. List your current or most recent employer and title.
7. Specify your research focus areas and list your key publications, including your first-author publications.
8. Select your technical skills, including programming languages and laboratory skills.
9. Mention any professional certifications you hold.
10. Confirm your biohazard or safety training status.
11. Provide information about your professional references, including names, titles, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`): Enter your first name in the format as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`): Enter your last name in the format as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter your primary email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Address** (`applicant_address`): Enter your current street address.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_title`): Select the job title or position name you are applying for.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`): Choose the type of position you are applying for (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Postdoctoral Fellow, Graduate Student, Visiting Scholar).
* **Desired Start Date** (`desired_start_date`): Enter the date you can start working if selected for the position.
* **Expected Salary** (`salary_expectations`): Enter your expected annual salary range (optional).
* **Highest Degree** (`highest_degree`): Select your highest degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, MD, Other Professional Degree).
* **Field of Study** (`degree_field`): Enter your major or field of study.
* **Institution** (`degree_institution`): Enter the name of the university or college where you earned your degree.
* **Year of Graduation** (`graduation_year`): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`): Enter the number of years you have worked in biotech or research.
* **Current or Most Recent Employer** (`current_employer`): Enter the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **Current or Most Recent Title** (`current_title`): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Duration at Current Position** (`employment_duration`): Enter the length of time you have been at your current or most recent position (optional).
* **Research Focus Areas** (`research_focus`): Describe your primary research interests.
* **Number of Publications** (`publication_count`): Enter approximately how many peer-reviewed publications you have.
* **First-Author Publications** (`first_author_publications`): Enter the number of publications where you are the first author.
* **List of Key Publications** (`publications_list`): List 3-5 most relevant publications that showcase your research experience.
* **H-Index** (`h_index`): Enter your H-Index value, if available (optional).
* **Grants or Funding Received** (`grants_received`): Enter any grants or funding you have secured (optional).
* **Technical or Laboratory Skills** (`technical_skills`): Select relevant technical or laboratory skills, such as DNA sequencing, bioinformatics, or microscopy.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`): Select languages you are proficient in, such as Python, R, or MATLAB.
* **Professional Certifications** (`certifications`): Enter any relevant certifications you hold.
* **Biohazard or Safety Training** (`biohazard_training`): Check whether you have current or expired biohazard or safety training.
* **Professional References** (`references`): Provide the names, titles, and contact information of 3 professional references.
* **Certification** (`authorization`): Confirm that the information provided is true and complete by selecting the certification option.
