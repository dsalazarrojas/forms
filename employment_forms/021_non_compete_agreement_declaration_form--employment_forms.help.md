# Non Compete Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a declaration of non-compete agreement, which is used by companies to ensure that employees do not enter into competing business activities or ventures after leaving the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Please fill in your form ID, which is a unique identifier for this form.
2. If you are filling this form in a versioned manner, please select the version that applies to you.
3. If your employment involves an agreement term, please specify whether you agree or disagree.
4. If relevant, please provide the start and end dates of your agreement term.
5. Enter your company name and job title.
6. Please select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to indicate your non-compete agreement status.
7. If applicable, provide the date of your declaration.
8. Finally, enter your employee ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`Form Id`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for this form.
* **Version** (`form_version`, text, required: false): The version of this form that applies to you.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, select_multiple, required: false): If your employment involves an agreement term, please select one of the options provided.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): The start date of your agreement term, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): The end date of your agreement term, if applicable.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Your job title.
* **Non Compete Agreement** (`non_compete_agreement`, select_one, required: true): Please select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to indicate your non-compete agreement status.
* **Date Of Declaration** (`date_of_declaration`, date, required: false): The date of your declaration, if applicable.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: false): Your employee ID for reference.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult your employment contract or HR representative.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
