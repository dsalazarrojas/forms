# Medical Advisor Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Advisor Job Application form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in working as medical advisors. This form is used to assess the applicant's qualifications, work experience, and availability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Medical Advisor Job Application form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check yes or no to indicate if you are applying for this position.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Reason for Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in this position.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter your expected salary for the position.
* **Available to Start** (`available_to_start`, date, required): Select a date when you can start working as a medical advisor.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): This field is reserved for internal use and should not be filled in.
