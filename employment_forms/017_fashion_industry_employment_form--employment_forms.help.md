# Fashion Industry Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide essential information about your application for a job in the fashion industry. This form helps our team to get to know you better and understand how you can contribute to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is divided into the following sections:

1.  **Applicant Details**: This section is where you can provide basic information about yourself.
2.  **Contact Information**: Please fill out your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
3.  **Portfolio**: If you have any relevant work or portfolio pieces, please share them with us.
4.  **Resume**: Upload your resume for us to review.
5.  **Job Preferences**: Select the areas you're interested in within the fashion industry.
6.  **Work Experience**: If applicable, please provide your work experience in the fashion industry.
7.  **Education**: Select your level of education in the fashion industry.
8.  **Skills**: Select the skills you have that are relevant to the fashion industry.
9.  **Portfolio Link**: If you have a portfolio website or online presence, please share the link.
10. **Portfolio Attachment**: If you have a physical portfolio, please attach it to this form.
11. **References**: Provide any relevant references or contacts we can contact.
12. **Email**: Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
13. **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
14. **Note**: Any additional information about yourself you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (applicant_details)**: Please provide your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself. This can include your nickname, alias, or any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your email address and phone number so our team can get in touch with you. Please make sure to use a valid email address and phone number.
* **Portfolio (portfolio)**: If you have any relevant work or project that demonstrates your skills, please share it with us. This can be a link or attachment.
* **Resume (resume)**: Upload your resume for us to review.
* **Job Preferences (job_preferences)**: Select the areas you're interested in within the fashion industry. This can be Fashion, Marketing, Design, Operations, or Other.
* **Work Experience (work_experience)**: If you have experience in the fashion industry, provide the dates and details of your work.
* **Education (education)**: Select your level of education in the fashion industry, such as High School, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate.
* **Skills (skills)**: Select the skills you have that are relevant to the fashion industry, such as Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite, Proficient in Microsoft Office, etc.
* **Portfolio Link (portfolio_link)**: If you have a portfolio website or online presence, please share the link.
* **Portfolio Attachment (portfolio_attachment)**: If you have a physical portfolio, please attach it to this form.
* **References (references)**: Provide any relevant references or contacts we can contact.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Note (note)**: Any additional information about yourself you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for contact information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* Attach your portfolio or share your portfolio link if applicable.
