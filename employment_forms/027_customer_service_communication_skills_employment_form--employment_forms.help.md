# Customer Service Communication Skills Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Communication Skills Employment Form is designed to help employers assess a candidate's communication skills and employment history, particularly in customer service roles.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Please fill out your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Fill out your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your work email address or any other email address that you would like to use for communication.
4. **Phone**: Enter your work phone number or any other phone number that you would like to use for communication.
5. **Job Title**: Fill out your current or previous job title as it appears on your resume or identification documents.
6. **Work Experience**: Select your work experience in the relevant years of experience category.
7. **Communication Skills**: Choose the communication skills you have experience with.
8. **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume, or a brief summary of your work experience.
9. **Cover Letter**: Attach a cover letter or write a brief explanation of why you are a good fit for the position.
10. **Availability**: Enter your availability for the position, including any dates you are available to start work.
11. **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date you started your current or previous employment.
12. **Employment End Date**: Enter the date your current or previous employment ended.
13. **Employment Length**: Enter the length of your current or previous employment.
14. **Job Applicant ID**: This field is not currently used for the purposes of this form. Please ignore it.
15. **Job Title**: Fill out your job title as it appears on your identification documents.
16. **Job Title ID**: This field is not currently used for the purposes of this form. Please ignore it.
17. **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job duties and responsibilities.
18. **Company**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
19. **Manager**: Enter the name of your current or previous manager.
20. **Work Email**: Enter your work email address or any other email address that you would like to use for communication.
21. **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number or any other phone number that you would like to use for communication.
22. **Work Address**: Enter your work address or any other address that you would like to use for communication.
23. **Job Status**: Enter your current or previous job status (e.g. employed, unemployed, etc.).
24. **Job Type**: Enter the type of job you are applying for (e.g. part-time, full-time, etc.).
25. **Submission Date**: This field is automatically filled out by the system and should be ignored.
