# police_off_duty_employment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The police_off_duty_employment_request_form is a request form for non-duty tasks, such as part-time work, freelance, or personal projects. It helps track and manage such requests to ensure they do not conflict with official duties or compromise officer's availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Officer Details" section with your full name, badge number, and unit assignment.
2. Provide a brief description of the job or project you are requesting in the "Job Details" field.
3. Specify your desired shift or work schedule in the "Shift Details" field.
4. Indicate whether you have the department's approval to work on this project by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Work Agreed" field.
5. Enter the date of the request in the "Date" field.
6. Specify the time you are requesting to work in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional comments or information in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Details (1)**: Enter your full name, badge number, and unit assignment to identify yourself as the requesting officer.
* **Job Details (2)**: Briefly describe the job or project you are requesting to perform non-duty tasks.
* **Shift Details (3)**: Specify your desired shift or work schedule to ensure it does not conflict with official duties.
* **Work Agreed (4)**: Select "True" if you have already obtained approval from your superiors to work on this project, or "False" if you have not.
* **Date (5)**: Enter the date you are requesting to start or perform this job or project.
* **Time (6)**: Specify the time you are requesting to work, using 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Notes (7)**: Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Email (8)**: Enter your departmental email address to ensure that your superiors can contact you with updates or concerns.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your departmental phone number to facilitate communication about your request.
