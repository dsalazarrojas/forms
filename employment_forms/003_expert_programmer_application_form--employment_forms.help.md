# expert_programmer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "expert_programmer_application_form" is a tool for collecting information from potential expert programmers. It is intended for use by those who are interested in applying for a position or collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Contact Information, such as your phone number and address.
3. Fill out the Work Info section with your current or relevant work experience.
4. Describe your educational background in the Education Info section.
5. Share your portfolio of work in the Portfolio Info section.
6. Optionally, submit any additional information in the Submit Info field.
7. If applicable, upload a file by providing a description of the file and uploading it using the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information, Text, Optional)**: This field is for entering your basic details such as name and email address.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number and address to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Work Info (Work Info, Text, Optional)**: Describe your relevant work experience or current position.
* **Education Info (Education Info, Text, Optional)**: Provide your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Portfolio Info (Portfolio Info, Text, Optional)**: Share your portfolio of work, including examples or URLs.
* **Submit Info (Submit Info, Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Upload File (Upload File, Text, Optional)**: If you have a file to share, enter a description and upload it using this field.
* **File Info (File Info, Text, Optional)**: Enter details about the file you have uploaded.
