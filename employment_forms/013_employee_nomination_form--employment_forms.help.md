# Employee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Nomination Form is used to nominate an employee for a specific position or recognition. This form helps the management team to keep track of employee nominations and evaluate them for potential awards or promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the employee you wish to nominate from the drop-down menu in "Employee" field.
2. Choose the employment type of the employee you are nominating in the "Employment Type" field.
3. Choose the employment status of the employee you are nominating in the "Employment Status" field.
4. Choose the manager and supervisor of the employee you are nominating in the "Manager" and "Supervisor" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the job title of the employee you are nominating in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter any additional information about the employee's education, skills, or other relevant details in the "Additional Info" fields.
7. Select your agreement or disagreement with the nomination in the "Agree" field.
8. Enter the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.
9. Enter the time of the nomination in the "Nomination Time" field.
10. Enter any comments or additional information about the nomination in the "Comments" field.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit the form.
