# Health Compliance Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Compliance Employment Form is used to gather information about an employee's employment status, including their employment type, start and end dates, and any health risks or medical clearance requirements. This form is typically completed by employers to ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Employer Information**: Enter the employer's name in the "Employer Name" field.
2. **Enter Employee Details**: Enter the employee's name, employee ID, job title, and employment start and end dates in the respective fields.
3. **Select Employment Type**: Choose the employee's employment type from the options provided.
4. **Answer Medical Attestation and Workplace Readiness Questions**: Select the correct answers to the medical attestation and workplace readiness questions.
5. **List Health Risks**: Select any health risks that apply to the employee.
6. **Provide Medical Clearance**: Indicate if the employee has medical clearance.
7. **Add Employee Notes**: If necessary, enter any additional notes about the employee.
8. **Sign and Date the Form**: Both the employer and employee must sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of the employer as it appears on official documents.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the full name of the employee as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number or badge number.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Employment Type**: Choose the employee's employment type, such as 'Yes' for full-time or 'No' for contract work.
* **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date the employee started working for the company.
* **Employment End Date**: Enter the date the employee's employment ended or will end.
* **Medical Attestation**: Select 'Yes' if the employee has medical attestation, 'No' otherwise.
* **Workplace Readiness Details**: Select 'Yes' if the employee is ready to work at the workplace, 'No' otherwise.
* **Health Risks**: Select any health risks that apply to the employee.
* **Medical Clearance**: Indicate if the employee has medical clearance.
* **Employee Notes**: Add any additional notes about the employee if necessary.
* **Employer Signature**: The employer must sign and date this section to confirm completion.
* **Employee Signature**: The employee must sign and date this section to confirm completion.
* **Date Submitted**: The date the form was completed and signed.
