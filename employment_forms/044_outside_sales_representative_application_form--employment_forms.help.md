# Outside Sales Representative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outside Sales Representative Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in sales positions outside of an office setting. This form gathers essential details about the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications to determine their suitability for sales roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or the job title you are applying for.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your current or previous company.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it should be listed on your resume or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number for communication.
* **Experience**: Briefly describe your sales experience and relevant qualifications.
* **Sales Area**: Specify the area you are interested in, such as medical devices, software, etc.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of sales role you are interested in, such as sales representative, account manager, etc.
* **Job Type 2**, **Job Type 3**, **Job Type 4**, **Job Type 5**, **Job Type 6**: List any additional sales roles you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume, which should include your work history, qualifications, and relevant experience.
* **Skills**: List any relevant skills you have, such as language proficiency, industry-specific knowledge, etc.

## Tips
* Please ensure your resume is attached to the form as a note field.
* If you have any additional relevant skills, please list them in the skills field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in our sales roles, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
