# Healthcare Psychologist Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your education, work experience, and other relevant details that are required for employment purposes as a healthcare psychologist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each field accordingly.
2. Fill in the text fields (e.g., Contact Details, Clinical Experience, Additional Comments) with relevant information.
3. Upload any required documents if you choose 'Yes' for the "Uploaded Documents" field.
4. Select the correct submission status and other relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose whether you are applying for a job or other employment-related opportunity. You can select multiple options.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter your contact information (e.g., name, phone number, email address).
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate whether you have completed your education (e.g., PhD, Master's, Bachelor's).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Indicate your relevant work experience (e.g., years, position, industry).
* **Clinical Experience** (`clinical_experience`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your clinical experience, if applicable.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate whether you have relevant skills and certifications (e.g., psychology licenses, certifications).
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose whether you have references that can vouch for your work experience and skills.
* **Submission** (`submission`, date, required):
	+ Select a submission date for your application.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Indicate whether you have uploaded required documents (e.g., resume, transcripts).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information not covered in other fields.
* **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate whether you have reviewed and agree to employment terms.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Leave a note for reviewers.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the status of your submission (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate who submitted the form (e.g., you, HR representative).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate who updated the form (e.g., you, HR representative).
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate who created the form (e.g., you, HR representative).
* **Created On** (`created_on`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time the form was created.
* **Submission Time** (`submitted_at`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Update Time** (`updated_at`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Create Time** (`created_at`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time the form was created.
* **Last Updated On** (`last_updated_at`, time, required):
	+ Select the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required):
	+ Enter a unique form ID.
* **Jotform ID** (`jotform_id`, text, required):
	+ Enter a Jotform ID.

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
