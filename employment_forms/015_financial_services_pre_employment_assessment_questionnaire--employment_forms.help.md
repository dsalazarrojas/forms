# Financial Services Pre Employment Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to collect relevant information from candidates before an employment decision. It covers various aspects of a candidate's background, experience, and preferences to help make informed decisions during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact email address in the Candidate Name and Contact Email fields, respectively.
2. Provide a detailed description of your work experience in the Work Experience field.
3. Describe your education background in the Education field.
4. Select your level of financial knowledge and satisfaction with your current job in the Financial Knowledge and Job Satisfaction fields.
5. Enter your expected salary and job preferences in the Expected Salary and Job Type fields.
6. Indicate your availability to start, preferred work location, and notice period in the Available Start Date, Work Location, and Notice Period fields.
7. Select your job category and work status in the Job Category and Work Status fields.
8. Describe your current job and desired job in the Job Description and Job Requirements fields.
9. List your job qualifications and terminology in the Job Qualifications and Job Terminology fields.
10. Enter any additional job references, such as a previous employer or a colleague's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Resume** (4, text, optional): Attach a copy of your resume for review.
* **Work Experience** (5, note, optional): Describe your relevant work experience in financial services.
* **Education** (6, text, required): Describe your educational background and qualifications.
* **Job Satisfaction** (7, select_one, optional): Rate your current job satisfaction level.
* **Financial Knowledge** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select your areas of financial knowledge.
* **Available Start Date** (9, date, optional): Indicate your availability to start work.
* **Expected Salary** (10, number, optional): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Job Type** (11, select_one, optional): Select the type of job you are looking for.
* **Work Location** (12, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred work location.
* **Notice Period** (13, select_one, optional): Select your preferred notice period.
* **Job Title** (14, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Job Reference** (15, text, required): Enter a reference for your current job.
* **Work Hours** (16, text, optional): Enter your work hours.
* **Available Days** (17, text, optional): Enter your available days.
* **Work Status** (18, select_one, optional): Select your current work status.
* **Job Category** (19, select_one, optional): Select the category of your job.
* **Job Description** (20, note, required): Describe your current job and desired job.
* **Job Requirements** (21, text, required): Describe the requirements of your current and desired job.
* **Job Qualifications** (22, text, required): List your job qualifications.
* **Job Terminology** (23, text, optional): Enter any relevant job terminology.
* **Job Reference 2** (24, text, required): Enter a reference for your desired job.
* **Job Reference 3** (25, text, required): Enter an additional reference for your desired job.
