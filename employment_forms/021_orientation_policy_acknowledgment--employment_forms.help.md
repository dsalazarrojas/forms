# orientation_policy_acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's acceptance of orientation policies, a crucial step in the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the relevant employment forms that apply to you from the list of options.
2. Enter your employee details, including your name and signature.
3. Enter your manager's name and signature.
4. Record the date and time of completion.
5. Read the orientation policy acknowledgment form help text to understand the policies and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employment_forms**:
  - Select all the employment forms that apply to you.
* **employee_signature_1**:
  - Enter your signature to confirm your acceptance of the orientation policies.
* **manager_signature_1**:
  - Enter your manager's signature to confirm their acknowledgment of your understanding.
* **manager_name**:
  - Enter your manager's name.
* **employee_name**:
  - Enter your name.
* **date**:
  - Enter the date of completion.
* **time**:
  - Enter the time of completion.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read and understand the orientation policies before completing this form.
