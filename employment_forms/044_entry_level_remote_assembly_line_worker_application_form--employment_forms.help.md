# Entry Level Remote Assembly Line Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Entry Level Remote Assembly Line Worker Application Form" is a form designed for applicants interested in working on an assembly line in a remote setting. This form helps applicants provide their personal information, work experience, job preferences, and contact details to be considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information.
2. Choose your relevant work experience from the provided options.
3. Select your available shifts, days, and hours that match the job requirements.
4. Provide your educational background and any relevant certifications.
5. Upload any additional documents required for the application.
6. Enter your contact information, including emergency contacts.
7. Provide any relevant references.
8. Sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and other personal details to be contacted for further information.
* **Work Experience (2)**: Select your relevant work experience from 5 Years, 6 Years, etc. (Note: This will help match your qualifications with job requirements.)
* **Job Preferences (3)**: Enter any job preferences you have, such as location or job type.
* **Available Shifts (7)**: Choose your available shifts (1st shift or 2nd shift).
* **Available Days (8)**: Select your available days to work (Monday to Sunday).
* **Available Hours (9)**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work.
* **Education (10)**: Choose your level of education (High School, Some College, Bachelor's, or Associate's).
* **Skills (11)**: Enter any relevant skills you have.
* **Certifications (12)**: Select any certifications you hold.
* **Emergency Contacts (13)**: Enter the contact information of your emergency contacts.
* **References (14)**: Provide any relevant references.
* **Resume Upload (15)**: Upload your resume to demonstrate your qualifications.
* **Other Docs (16)**: Upload any other documents required for the application.
* **Notes (17)**: Leave any additional notes about your application.
* **Email (18)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone (19)**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Address (20)**: Enter your home address.
* **City (21)**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State (22)**: Enter the state of your residence (Note: This field is optional).
* **Postal Code (23)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Zip (24)**: Verify your zip code.
* **Signature (25)**: Sign the form to confirm your application.
