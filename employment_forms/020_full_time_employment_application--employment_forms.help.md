# Full Time Employment Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and employment information from applicants for full-time employment opportunities. It is intended for applicants to provide their contact details, work experience, education background, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Contact Information" page, which includes the applicant's contact details such as name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Next, proceed to the "Personal Details" page, where you will be asked to provide your personal details such as education background, work experience, and job preferences.
3. The "Employment History" page is where you will provide information about your previous work experience and job history.
4. Move on to the "Education Background" page, where you will provide details about your educational qualifications and background.
5. In the "Work Experience" page, you will provide information about your previous work experience and job history.
6. The "Job Preferences" page is where you will specify your job preferences, such as job titles, job type, and work status.
7. "Availability" page is where you will provide your availability dates.
8. The "Contact Method" page is where you can choose your preferred contact method.
9. "Emergency Contact" page is where you will provide information about your emergency contact person.
10. Finally, on the "Skills" page, you will list your skills and qualifications.
11. On the "Job Title" page, you will specify the job title you are applying for.
12. The "Role" page is where you will specify your role preference.
13. "Work Status" page is where you will specify your current work status.
14. "Job Status" page is where you will specify your job status.
15. The "Available Start Date" page is where you will specify your available start date.
16. The "Available End Date" page is where you will specify your available end date.
17. On the "Contact Name" page, you will enter the contact person's name.
18. On the "Contact Email" page, you will enter the contact person's email.
19. The "Contact Phone" page is where you will enter the contact person's phone number.
20. On the "Contact Address" page, you will enter the contact person's address.
21. The "Emergency Contact" page is where you will provide information about your emergency contact person.
22. The "Emergency Contact Name" page is where you will enter the emergency contact person's name.
23. On the "Emergency Contact Phone" page, you will enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
24. The "Relationship with Applicant" page is where you will specify your relationship with the applicant.
25. The "Job Title Label" page is where you will specify the job title label.
