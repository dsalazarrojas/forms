# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to submit their job applications for potential employment opportunities. It collects essential information about the applicant, including name, contact details, work experience, and any other relevant credentials.

## How To Complete This Form

### To start the application process, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information about yourself.
2. Review your submission to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

### If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: A required field to input your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A required field for your contact email address. Please ensure it's accurate and active.
* **Phone**: An optional field for your contact phone number.
* **Address**: An optional field for your residential or work address. Please be cautious about sharing sensitive information.
* **Education**: An optional field to describe your educational background and qualifications.
* **Company**: An optional field to mention your current or previous company name.
* **Job Position**: An optional field to describe your current or desired job position.
* **Years Employed**: An optional field to input the number of years you have been employed.
* **Job Status**: A select-one field with options: Employed, Unemployed, or Self-Employed. Please select the one that best describes your current employment status.
* **Job Title**: An optional field to input your current or desired job title.
* **Name 2**: An optional field for secondary contact name.
* **Email 2**: An optional field for secondary contact email.
* **Job**: An optional field to describe your current or desired job.
* **Position**: An optional field to describe your current or desired position.
* **Years Employed 2**: An optional field to input the number of years you have been employed in your current or desired job.
* **Additional Info**: An optional field for any additional information you'd like to share with the employer.
* **Uploaded Documents**: A select-multiple field allowing you to upload up to three documents (Resume, Cover Letter, and/or References). Please ensure uploaded documents are relevant and well-formatted.

### Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your submission.
- Ensure all uploaded documents are relevant to your application.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
