# Find Dentist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Find Dentist" form is designed to assist users in locating a dentist that matches their specific needs. It is not intended for actual medical diagnosis or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Enter your search criteria in the text field labeled "search_for_dentist".
* Select the type of dentist you are looking for by choosing one or more options in the "search_by_profession" field.
* Enter your location, or address, in the "location" field.
* Choose the desired rating for the dentist from the "search_by_rating" field.
* If you have a specific dentist's name, enter it in the "dentist_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search for Dentist** (`search_for_dentist`, text, required: no): Enter a description of the type of dentist you are looking for (e.g., "dentist for implants" or "pediatric dentist").
* **Search by Profession** (`search_by_profession`, select_multiple, required: no): Select the profession of the dentist you are looking for, such as General Dentist, Specialist, Pediatric Dentist, Periodontist, or Orthodontist.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: no): Enter the address or city where you want to search for a dentist.
* **Search by Rating** (`search_by_rating`, select_one, required: no): Select the rating for the dentist you are looking for, such as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`, text, required: no): Enter the name of a specific dentist if you know it.
