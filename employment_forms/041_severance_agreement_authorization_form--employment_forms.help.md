# severance_agreement_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Severance Agreement Authorization Form is designed to collect information and obtain agreements from employees who are leaving or have left the company. This form is used to formalize the severance process and ensure that all necessary details are documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all the required fields are completed accurately.
2. Enter your employee details, including your name, job title, and date of employment.
3. Select the reason for your severance from the provided options.
4. Choose the date of your last day of work.
5. Read and agree to the terms of the Severance Agreement.
6. Sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Agreement** (`employee_details`): Enter your personal details, including your name, job title, and date of employment.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Date of Employment** (`employee_date_of_employment`): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Reason for Severance** (`reason_for_severance`): Select the reason for your severance from the provided options. This will help the HR department understand the circumstances surrounding your departure.
* **Severance Date** (`severance_date`): Enter the date of your last day of work, or the date when you will be leaving the company.
* **Agreement Acknowledgement** (`agreement_acknowledgement`): Select one of the options provided, indicating that you have read and understand the terms of the Severance Agreement. If you do not understand or disagree with any term, you must inform the HR department in writing.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the terms of the Severance Agreement.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`): If applicable, sign as your manager or representative, indicating their acknowledgement of the severance agreement.
