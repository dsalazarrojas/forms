# Summer Sales Program Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the terms and conditions of a sales representative's participation in the summer sales program.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter the **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the **Program Name** in the "Program Name" field.
3. Enter your **Sales Rep Number** if applicable in the "Sales Rep Number" field.
4. Enter your **Manager's Name** if applicable in the "Manager Name" field.
5. Enter your **Sales Representative's Email** in the "Sales Rep Email" field.
6. Enter your **Sales Representative's Phone Number** in the "Sales Rep Phone" field.
7. Enter your **Participant Address** in the "Participant Address" field.
8. Read and agree to the **Program Terms** in the "Program Terms" field.
9. Select the **Date Start** of the program in the "Date Start" field.
10. Select the **Date End** of the program in the "Date End" field.
11. Select the **Signature Date** of the agreement in the "Signature Date" field.
12. Sign the **Sales Representative's Signature** field.
13. Sign the **Manager's Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the sales representative participating in the program.
* **Program Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the program the sales representative will be participating in.
* **Sales Rep Number** (Number, optional): Enter your sales representative's number if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (Text, optional): Enter your manager's name if applicable.
* **Sales Rep Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Sales Rep Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (Text, optional): Enter your participant address.
* **Program Terms** (Text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Date Start** (Date, required): Select the start date of the program.
* **Date End** (Date, required): Select the end date of the program.
* **Signature Date** (Date, required): Select the date you sign the agreement.
* **Sales Rep Signature** (Text, required): Sign the agreement as the sales representative.
* **Manager Signature** (Text, required): Sign the agreement as the manager.
