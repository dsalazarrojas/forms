# Workplace Probation Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension of your probation period. It is designed to gather the necessary information for managers to review and decide on your extension request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are eligible to submit this form.
2. Enter your name and your manager's name in the respective text fields.
3. Provide a clear reason for your extension request in the "Reason for Extension" field.
4. Choose the new end date for your probation period from the calendar.
5. Enter the number of days you are requesting to extend your probation period.
6. If you are requesting an extension, select the corresponding status from the dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your manager's email address and phone number.
9. Finally, click the submit button to send the form to your manager for review.
