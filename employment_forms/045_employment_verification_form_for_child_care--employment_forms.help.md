# Employment Verification Form For Child Care - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to verify the employment status of an individual, specifically for child care purposes. This form is designed to gather essential information about the employee, such as their name, ID number, hire date, employer, and job title.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter employee information**: Provide the employee's name, ID number, and date of hire.
2. **Enter employer information**: Enter the employer's name and the employee's job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the employee as it appears on their government-issued identification.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the employee's identification number, if applicable.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee started working with the employer.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employer, including the business name and any relevant department or division names.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title or position within the company.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the employment verification process is effective.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask the employee to provide clarification.
- This form is a basic employment verification tool and is not intended for high-stakes or sensitive purposes.
- Please review and submit the form carefully before proceeding with the employment verification process.
