# Job Opportunity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a job opportunity from candidates, which will aid in determining the suitability of the opportunity for the company's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
* Fill out the form in the following order: candidate details, resume upload, and job opportunity specifics.
* Use the provided options for select fields (e.g. "Yes", "No", etc.).
* Enter a brief job description to help us understand the opportunity.
* Fill out the contact info fields with accurate email and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate Details (<type>: text) 
*   This field is for providing a brief overview of the candidate. Please provide any relevant information about the candidate that we should know.

### 2. Resume (<type>: text) 
*   Please upload your resume as a PDF or Word document. This will help us in assessing your qualifications for the job opportunity.

### 3. Role Preferences (<label>: Role Preferences, <type>: select_one, <options>: Yes, No) 
*   If you have any specific role preferences, please select the option that best describes your preference.

### 4. Job Type (<label>: Job Type, <type>: select_one, <options>: Yes, No) 
*   If you have a preference for a specific job type, please select the option that best describes your preference.

### 5. Job Category (<label>: Job Category, <type>: select_one, <options>: Yes, No) 
*   If you have a preference for a specific job category, please select the option that best describes your preference.

### 6. Job Level (<label>: Job Level, <type>: select_one, <options>: High, Low) 
*   Please select the job level that best describes the job opportunity (High, Low).

### 7. Job Family (<label>: Job Family, <type>: select_multiple, <options>: Yes, No) 
*   If you have a preference for a specific job family, please select all that apply.

### 8. Contact Info (<label>: Contact Info, <type>: email) 
*   Please enter your email address and any other contact information you'd like to share.

### 9. Job Title (<label>: Job Title, <type>: text) 
*   Please enter the job title of the opportunity.

### 10. Job Description (<label>: Job Description, <type>: text) 
*   Please provide a brief description of the job opportunity.

### 11. Job Status (<label>: Job Status, <type>: select_one, <options>: Active, Inactive) 
*   Please select the job status of the opportunity (Active, Inactive).
