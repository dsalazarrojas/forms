# Pharmacy Staff Leave Month Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pharmacy Staff Leave Month Selection" form is used to report leave or time off for pharmacy staff. This form is intended to collect information about the month, leave type, and other relevant details related to staff absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the month for which you are reporting leave or time off.
2. (Optional) If applicable, enter the start and end dates of your leave.
3. Choose the type of leave (e.g., Annual, Sick, Family Leave, etc.).
4. Enter the number of days off you will be taking.
5. Provide comments or additional information, if necessary.
6. Enter your email address, if you have not already done so.
7. Enter your phone number, if you have not already done so.
8. Select the tool assigned to you (if applicable) and enter the tool ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a month**: Select the month for which you are reporting leave or time off.
  * Please choose one of the 12 months listed.
* **Start Date**: (Optional) Enter the start date of your leave, if applicable.
  * Enter the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **End Date**: (Optional) Enter the end date of your leave, if applicable.
  * Enter the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave (e.g., Annual, Sick, Family Leave, etc.).
  * Select one of the options listed (e.g., Annual, Sick, Family Leave).
* **Days Off**: Enter the number of days off you will be taking.
  * Enter a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your leave.
  * This field is for any additional information you would like to share.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * This is your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * This is your work phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you.
  * Choose one of the options listed (e.g., chatjimmy).
* **Tool ID**: Enter the tool ID assigned to you.
  * Enter the ID of the tool you are using.
* **Other Tool**: (Optional) If the assigned tool is "Other", enter the name of the tool.
  * Enter the name of the tool you are using.
* **Tool ID**: (Optional) If the assigned tool is "Other", enter the ID of the tool you are using.
  * Enter the ID of the tool.
