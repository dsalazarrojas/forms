# Employee Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to extend their existing contracts. The information provided will be used to determine the status of the contract extension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- Fill in your employee details such as name, job title, and employee ID.
- Provide the start and end dates of your contract, if applicable.
- Select the type of contract you are extending.
- Specify the duration of the contract extension.
- Provide any relevant contract status information, such as whether the contract is active or inactive.
- Enter your salary and any other relevant details.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
- If applicable, provide your email address and phone number.
- Enter any additional notes or comments.
- Finally, specify the approval status of the contract extension and the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as assigned by your employer.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): If you have a contract start date, enter it here.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): If you have a contract end date, enter it here.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of contract you are extending (e.g. Option A, Option B, Option C).
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of the contract extension in months.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the contract (e.g. Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Employee Salary** (`employee_salary`, number, required): Enter your current salary.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, date, required): Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, date, required): Your employer must sign and date this section to confirm their agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): If you have any terms or conditions to include with your contract extension, enter them here.
* **Agreement Number** (`agreement_number`, text, required): Enter the agreement number assigned by your employer.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): If you have a renewal date, enter it here.
* **Extension Reason** (`extension_reason`, text, optional): If you have an extension reason, enter it here.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Employer Notes** (`employer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from your employer.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the contract extension (e.g. Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of approval.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, required): Enter the contract number.
* **Contract Number Prefix** (`contract_number_prefix`, text, optional): Enter a prefix for the contract number.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID.

Note to users: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
