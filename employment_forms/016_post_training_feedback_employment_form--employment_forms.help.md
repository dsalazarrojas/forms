# Post Training Feedback Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Training Feedback Employment Form is a survey designed to collect feedback from participants about their employment status after a training program. This form helps us understand their experiences, successes, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your participant information, including any relevant details about your job status and training program participation.
2.  Select your current job status from the options provided.
3.  Choose all applicable training programs that you participated in.
4.  Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
5.  Enter the date of your employment outcome (if applicable).
6.  Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This section is for providing basic contact information and details about your participation in the training program.
	+ Enter your name and any other details as prompted.
* **Job Status**: Please select your current job status:
	+ "Active" - You are currently employed.
	+ "Inactive" - You are not currently employed.
* **Employer Details**: Provide any relevant details about your employer, such as company name, job title, or department.
* **Training Program**: Select all training programs that you participated in:
	+ Value 1, Value 2, Value 3 (select all that apply)
* **Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the training program.
* **Employment Outcome**: Enter the date of your employment outcome (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the training program.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you only submit feedback that is specific to this training program.
