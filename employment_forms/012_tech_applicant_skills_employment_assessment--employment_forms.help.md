# Tech Applicant Skills Employment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the applicant's identity and employment history in order to assess their suitability for employment in a tech role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that we will use this address to contact you for further discussion or interview purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us to contact you for any additional information we may need.
4. Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the number of years of experience you have in the tech industry in the "Years of Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have spent working in the tech industry.
