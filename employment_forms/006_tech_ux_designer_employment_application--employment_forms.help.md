# Tech UX Designer Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech UX Designer Employment Application is a form designed to collect information from potential tech UX designers for hiring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Enter your name in the `Name` field.
* Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
* Attach your resume in the `Resume` field.
* Select your relevant skills from the `Skills` field.
* Indicate your experience in the `Experience` field.
* Attach your portfolio in the `Portfolio` field.
* Enter your availability in the `Availability` field (in date format).
* Enter your notice period in the `Notice Period` field (in time format).
* Enter your contact information in the `Contact` field.
* Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
* Enter your address in the `Address` field.
* Enter your expected salary in the `Salary` field (in numerical format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false)
Your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false)
Your email address that will be used for communication.

### 3. **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required: false)
Attach your resume, highlighting your relevant experience and skills.

### 4. **Skills** (`Skills`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the skills you have that are relevant to the position:

* Yes
* No

### 5. **Experience** (`Experience`, select_one, required: false)
Indicate your experience level:

* Yes
* No

### 6. **Portfolio** (`Portfolio`, text, required: false)
Attach your portfolio, showcasing your work and achievements.

### 7. **Availability** (`Availability`, date, required: false)
Enter your availability in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 8. **Notice Period** (`Notice Period`, time, required: false)
Enter your expected notice period in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### 9. **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required: false)
Enter your contact information, including name and phone number.

### 10. **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number.

### 11. **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false)
Enter your work address.

### 12. **Salary** (`Salary`, number, required: false)
Enter your expected salary in numerical format.

### 13. **Other** (`Other`, text, required: false)
Enter any other relevant information.
