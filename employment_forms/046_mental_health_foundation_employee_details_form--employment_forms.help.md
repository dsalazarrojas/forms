# Mental Health Foundation Employee Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update employee information for administrative purposes. It is essential to fill this form accurately and completely to ensure that our employee database is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Employee Name in the designated field.
2. Enter your Employee ID, which is a unique identification number.
3. Provide your work-related email address.
4. Enter your contact number for easy communication.
5. Describe your Job Title and Job Description.
6. Specify your Department and Work Location.
7. Enter the Start Date of your employment.
8. Enter the Termination Date (if applicable).
9. Select your Job Status from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is the name of the employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): A unique identification number assigned to each employee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The employee's work-related email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The employee's contact number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): A brief description of the employee's job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the employee's job role and responsibilities.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department the employee belongs to within the organization.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): The location of the employee's work.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date the employee started working.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): The date the employee's employment ended (if applicable).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the employee's job.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the dates for correctness and consistency.
* If an employee has left the organization, ensure to mark the job status as 'Inactive'.
