# Hostess Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hostess Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from potential hostesses interested in working as hostesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Hostess Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Hostess Contact" section.
3. Enter your emergency contact's information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
4. Select your relevant experience from the "Hostess Experience" section.
5. If applicable, upload additional files as needed in the "File" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hostess Details** (1, Hostess Details, Required): This section is for your personal details, including your name, address, etc.
* **Hostess Contact** (2, Hostess Contact, Required): Please enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (3, Emergency Contact, Required): Enter the contact information for your emergency contact, in case we need to reach out to them.
* **Hostess Experience** (4, Hostess Experience, Required): Select your relevant experience from the options provided.
* **Hostess Availability** (5, Hostess Availability, Optional): If you are available to work as a hostess, please indicate your availability in this field.
* **Hostess Notes** (6, Hostess Notes, Optional): Any additional notes you'd like to provide about yourself can be entered here.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (7, Emergency Contact Notes, Optional): If you have any notes about your emergency contact, you can enter them here.
* **File 1** (8, File 1, Optional): Upload any additional information you'd like to share, such as a resume or portfolio.
* **File 2** (9, File 2, Optional): Upload another file if needed.
* **File 3** (10, File 3, Optional): Upload yet another file if needed.
* **File 4** (11, File 4, Optional): Upload additional files if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
