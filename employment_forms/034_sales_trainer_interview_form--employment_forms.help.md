# Sales Trainer Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sales Trainer Interview Form is designed to gather information from potential candidates for sales trainer positions. This form helps to assess their qualifications, experience, and performance in sales roles. It will be used to determine the best fit for the job and to ensure that the candidate meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill in your contact information in the Contact Information section (Field 1).
2. Share your work experience in the Experience section (Field 2).
3. Provide references in the References section (Field 3).
4. Select your job title and education background in the Job Title and Education sections (Fields 4 and 5).
5. List your skills in the Skills section (Field 6).
6. Indicate your work arrangement and employment type in the Work Arrangement and Employment Type sections (Fields 7 and 8).
7. Specify your previous employers in the Previous Employers section (Field 9).
8. Describe your current and previous job status in the Job Status and Previous Job Status sections (Fields 10 and 11).
9. Select your sales managerial experience, sales experience, and years of experience in the Sales Managerial Experience, Sales Experience, and Years of Experience sections (Fields 12, 13, and 14).
10. Describe your job and sales performances in the Job Performance and Sales Performance sections (Fields 15 and 16).
11. Provide detailed descriptions for any other job type, employment type, job status, and other fields as requested (Fields 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, and 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **2. Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required): Describe your previous work experience, including job title, company, and duration.
* **3. References** (`references`, `text`, required): Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references.
* **4. Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current or previous job title.
* **5. Education** (`education`, `text`, required): Share your educational background, including degree and institution.
* **6. Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required): List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **7. Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are willing to work in a flexible or fixed schedule.
* **8. Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are employed or not.
* **9. Previous Employers** (`previous_employers`, `text`, required): List your previous work places, including company name and duration.
* **10. Job Status** (`job_status`, `text`, required): Describe your current job status, e.g., employed, unemployed, or on parental leave.
* **11. Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Check if your job type is sales or non-sales.
* **12. Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are employed or not.
* **13. Job Type Other** (`job_type_other`, `text`, required): If your job type is not sales or non-sales, provide a detailed description.
* **14. Employment Type Other** (`employment_type_other`, `text`, required): If your employment type is not employed or not, provide a detailed description.
* **15. Job Status Other** (`job_status_other`, `text`, required): If your job status is not employed, unemployed, or on parental leave, provide a detailed description.
* **16. Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are willing to work in a flexible or fixed schedule.
* **17. Job Type Other Description** (`job_type_other_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description for any other job type.
* **18. Employment Type Other Description** (`employment_type_other_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description for any other employment type.
* **19. Job Status Other Description** (`job_status_other_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description for any other job status.
* **20. Sales Managerial Experience** (`sales_managerial_experience`, `text`, required): Share your experience in sales management.
* **21. Sales Experience** (`sales_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your sales experience, including sales targets and sales achievements.
* **22. Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been working.
* **23. Job Performance** (`job_performance`, `text`, required): Describe your job performance, including achievements and strengths.
* **24. Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, `text`, required): Describe your sales performance, including achievements and strengths.
* **25. Job Performance Description** (`job_performance_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description for your job performance.

Please note that you must provide detailed answers for each field to ensure accurate assessment and a fair chance of being considered for the position.
