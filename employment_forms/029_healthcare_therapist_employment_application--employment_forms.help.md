# Healthcare Therapist Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from healthcare therapist job applicants. Please fill out the form to demonstrate your interest in this role and to provide essential details about your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the "Job Title" field, where you will provide a brief description of your current or previous healthcare therapist position.
2. In the "Job Description" field, describe your experience as a healthcare therapist, highlighting your responsibilities, achievements, and qualifications.
3. In the "Job Requirements" field, outline the skills and qualifications you possess that align with the job requirements.
4. Attach your "Resume" as a PDF or document file, which should include your education, work experience, and relevant certifications.
5. Finally, write a brief "Cover Letter" explaining why you're the ideal candidate for the role and how your experience aligns with the job requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (required): A brief description of your current or previous healthcare therapist position.
* **Job Description** (optional): Describe your experience as a healthcare therapist, highlighting your responsibilities, achievements, and qualifications.
* **Job Requirements** (optional): Outline the skills and qualifications you possess that align with the job requirements.
* **Resume** (optional): A PDF or document file containing your education, work experience, and relevant certifications.
* **Cover Letter** (optional): A brief explanation of why you're the ideal candidate for the role and how your experience aligns with the job requirements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your resume and cover letter are well-written and formatted according to industry standards.
* Proofread your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our support team.
