# COVID 19 Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to obtain authorization for employees to work on-site or in specific locations during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
Fill out the form by following the page instructions. Each page is designed to guide you through the necessary information. Make sure to review each field carefully and select the correct options to ensure accurate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: Employee Information**
* **Page 2: Employee Identification**
* **Page 3: Employee Details**
* **Page 4: Work Location**
* **Page 5: Job Information**
* **Page 6: Work Location and Contact Information**
* **Page 7: COVID-19 Status**
* **Page 8: Exemption**
* **Page 9: Health Assessment**
* **Page 10: Safety Agreement**
* **Page 11: Authorization Status**
