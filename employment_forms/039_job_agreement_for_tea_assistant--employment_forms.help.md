# Job Agreement For Tea Assistant - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting information related to the employment of a tea assistant. It is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of the agreement between the tea assistant and their employer. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all parties have a clear understanding of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions being asked.
2.  Enter your details accurately and truthfully.
3.  Check the "Tea Assistant Contract" section to ensure you have selected the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Title:** (`Job Agreement For Tea Assistant`)
    An optional field to enter a title for the agreement, which may be used for reference or filing purposes.
*   **Employment Terms:** (`employment_terms`)
    Enter the terms and conditions of employment, including any specific details about the tea assistant's role and responsibilities.
*   **Working Hours:** (`working_hours`)
    Enter the number of hours the tea assistant is expected to work per week.
*   **Tea Assistant Responsibilities:**
    (`tea_assistant_responsibilities`)
    Enter the specific tasks and duties that the tea assistant will be responsible for, such as serving tea, handling customer inquiries, or managing inventory.
*   **Tea Assistant Contract:**
    (`tea_assistant_contract`)
    Select 'Yes' if you are agreeing to a contract, or 'No' if you are not.
*   **Agreement Date:**
    (`agreement_date`)
    Enter the date the agreement is signed, which helps to establish a timeline for the employment period.
