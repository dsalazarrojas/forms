# Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Order Form is designed to enable employees to request and track equipment orders for their work needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID, which is required for order tracking and processing.
2. Select the type of equipment you need from the Equipment Type dropdown menu.
3. Specify the quantity of equipment required.
4. Provide the order date, which should be the date you would like the equipment to be ordered.
5. Optionally, provide a delivery date for when you would like the equipment to be delivered.
6. Select the status of your order from the Status dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional notes about your order, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID to track your order.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of equipment you require, such as Computer, Printer, or Monitor.
* **Equipment Quantity** (`equipment_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of units of the equipment you need.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like the equipment to be ordered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): If possible, select a delivery date for when you would like the equipment to be delivered.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your order from Ordered, In Progress, Delivered, or Returned.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests related to your order.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select the person or team member responsible for fulfilling your order, or choose 'Other' to specify a different person.
