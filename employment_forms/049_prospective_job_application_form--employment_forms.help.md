# Prospective Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Job Application Form is designed to help job applicants provide their contact information, job experience, qualifications, and other relevant details to prospective employers. It's meant to streamline the hiring process by gathering necessary information in one place, reducing the initial interviews and phone screens.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you have any attachments or files to accompany your application, upload them as necessary.
4. Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Applicant Information** (`job_applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief introduction of yourself, including your name, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the employer.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional): List your relevant qualifications, skills, and certifications.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach a copy of your resume or CV.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): List any professional references, including names, titles, and contact information.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current job status, including Applied, In Progress, and Hired.
