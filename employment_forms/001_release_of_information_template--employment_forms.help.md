# release_of_information_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "release_of_information_template" form is used to collect and document release information related to employment. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review all the fields carefully and ensure you understand their purpose.
2. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
3. Verify the accuracy of the information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employment Fields** (`employment_fields`, Note, Required: False): This section provides a brief introduction to the employment-related information being released.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the company associated with the employment.
*   **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the employment was released.
*   **Release Date** (`release_date`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the information will be released.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the employee associated with the employment.
*   **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, Text, Required: False): Enter the title of the employee.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required: False): Enter the job title associated with the employment.
*   **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, Text, Required: False): Enter a brief explanation for the release.
*   **Release By** (`release_by`, Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the person releasing the information.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date of birth of the employee (if applicable).
*   **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, Text, Required: False): Enter the social security number of the employee (if applicable).
*   **Address** (`address`, Text, Required: False): Enter the address of the employee (if applicable).
*   **City** (`city`, Text, Required: False): Enter the city of the employee (if applicable).
*   **State** (`state`, Text, Required: False): Enter the state of the employee (if applicable).
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, Text, Required: False): Enter the zip code of the employee (if applicable).
*   **Release By Date** (`release_by_date`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the release is to be executed.
*   **Employer ID Number** (`employer_id_number`, Text, Required: False): Enter the ID number of the employer (if applicable).
*   **Release By Title** (`release_by_title`, Text, Required: False): Enter the title of the person releasing the information.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, Text, Required: False): Enter the contact information for the person or entity releasing the information.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required: False): Enter the phone number of the contact person or entity.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required: False): Enter the email address of the contact person or entity.
*   **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, Note, Required: False): Enter any additional notes for the contact person or entity.
*   **Release Note** (`release_note`, Note, Required: False): Enter any additional notes for the release.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required: False): Enter the signature of the person releasing the information.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date the information was signed.

Please review this guide carefully and let me know if you have any questions or need further clarification.
