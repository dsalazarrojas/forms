# Stage Management Position Acceptance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from stage management employees about their position, job offer, and acceptance details. It is meant to ensure accurate and comprehensive documentation of key employment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the position title and any other relevant job offer details in the `position_title` field.
2. Enter the date of the job offer in the `job_offer_date` field (if applicable).
3. Enter the contract period duration and units in the `contract_period_duration` and `contract_period_units` fields (if applicable).
4. Enter the start date of your pay period in the `start_date` field (if applicable).
5. Select your role expectations and job offer details in the `role_expectations`, `job_offer_details`, and `job_offer_email` fields.
6. Confirm your union approval, theatre acceptance, and job approval in the `stage_management_union_approval`, `theatre_acceptance_confirmation`, and `job_approval` fields (if applicable).
7. Sign the role and contract with the `role_signature` and `contract_signer` fields.
8. Finally, select the pay period frequency and enter the end date of your pay period in the `pay_period_frequency` and `pay_period_end_date` fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter the title of the stage management position offered.
* **Job Offer Date** (`job_offer_date`): Enter the date of the job offer (if applicable).
* **Contract Period** (`contract_period`): Enter the duration of the contract (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your pay period (if applicable).
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`): Enter the frequency and end date of your pay period (if applicable).
* **Role Expectations** (`role_expectations`): Describe your expected role duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Offer Details** (`job_offer_details`): Describe the job offer details.
* **Job Offer Email** (`job_offer_email`): Enter the email used for job offer communication.
* **Stage Management Union Approval** (`stage_management_union_approval`): Confirm or deny union approval.
* **Theatre Acceptance Confirmation** (`theatre_acceptance_confirmation`): Confirm or deny theatre acceptance.
* **Job Approval** (`job_approval`): Confirm or deny job approval.
* **Role Signature** (`role_signature`): Sign the role acceptance.
* **Contract Signer** (`contract_signer`): Sign the contract.
* **Contract Period Units** (`contract_period_units`): Enter the units of the contract period (e.g., days, weeks, months).
* **Contract Period Duration** (`contract_period_duration`): Enter the duration of the contract period.
* **Pay Period Units** (`pay_period_units`): Enter the units of the pay period (e.g., days, weeks, months).
* **Pay Period Duration** (`pay_period_duration`): Enter the duration of the pay period.
* **Pay Period Frequency** (`pay_period_frequency`): Select the frequency of the pay period (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Pay Period Start Date** (`pay_period_start_date`): Enter the start date of the pay period (if applicable).
* **Pay Period End Date** (`pay_period_end_date`): Enter the end date of the pay period (if applicable).
* **Pay Period Wage** (`pay_period_wage`): Enter the wage of the pay period.
* **Pay Period Benefits** (`pay_period_benefits`): Select if benefits are included in the pay period.

**Tips**:
* Ensure accurate and comprehensive documentation to avoid delays or issues in the future.
* Double-check the fields for completeness and accuracy before submission.
