# Non Compete Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Compete Commitment Form is a legal document that confirms an employee's commitment not to work with competing firms during and after the employment period. This form is used to standardize the process of getting employee consent and ensure that the company's intellectual property and trade secrets remain protected.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2.  Provide your personal details, such as your name and title.
3.  Specify the start and end dates of your employment.
4.  Enter your signature date.
5.  If you have a manager, provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Non Compete Start Date** (`non_compete_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your employment starts.
* **Non Compete End Date** (`non_compete_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your employment ends.
* **Non Compete Agreement** (`non_compete_agreement`, note, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions of your non-compete agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position within the company.
* **Non Compete Terminating Date** (`non_compete_terminating_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your non-compete agreement terminates.
* **Employee Manager** (`employee_manager`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or manager.
