# Pharmaceutical Industry Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your employment status in the pharmaceutical industry. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employer information in the "Employer Information" field.
2. Provide details about the job you are applying for in the "Job Information" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Indicate your employment status in the "Employment Info" field.
5. If required, upload your resume in the "Upload Resume" field.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.
7. Review and confirm your application in the "Confirm" field.
8. Add your signature in the "Signature" field.
9. Enter the date of your signature in the "Signature Date" field.
10. Provide any additional job information in the "Job Application" field.
11. Enter a job reference if applicable in the "Job Reference" field.
12. Describe the job in the "Job Description" field.
13. Specify the job location in the "Job Location" field.
14. Choose the type of job you are applying for in the "Job Type" field.
15. Choose your employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
16. Enter your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
17. Specify your education level in the "Education Level" field.
18. Enter the job start date if applicable in the "Job Start Date" field.
19. Enter the job end date if applicable in the "Job End Date" field.
20. Choose your job status in the "Job Status" field.
21. Enter additional company information in the "Company Information" field.
22. Enter any other information about the job in the "Other Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Information**: Enter the name and contact information of your current or previous employer.
* **Job Information**: Describe the job you are applying for, including the job title and description.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Employment Info**: Indicate whether you are currently employed or not, and if not, the reason why.
* **Upload Resume**: If required, upload your resume as a PDF file.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.
* **Confirm**: Review and confirm your application before submitting.
* **Signature**: Add your digital signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Job Application**: Enter any additional information about your job application.
* **Job Reference**: Enter a job reference if applicable.
* **Job Description**: Describe the job you are applying for in detail.
* **Job Location**: Specify the location of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Type**: Choose the type of job you are applying for (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Employment Status**: Choose your current employment status (open or closed).
* **Work Experience**: Enter your work experience in years.
* **Education Level**: Choose your education level (bachelor, master, high school, doctorate, or other).
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of your job if applicable.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the end date of your job if applicable.
* **Job Status**: Choose your job status (active or inactive).
* **Company Information**: Enter any additional information about the company.
* **Other Info**: Enter any other information about the job.
