# Employment Offer And Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Offer And Policy Acknowledgment Form is a crucial document that acknowledges employment offers and ensures both the employee and the employer understand their respective obligations and responsibilities. It confirms the terms of employment, including job title, job description, and company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:
* Enter the new employment offer details, such as job title and job description.
* Review and acknowledge the employment terms, which include company policies and expectations.
* Enter the date and time of acknowledgment.
* Provide your signature as an employee, and your manager's signature, indicating your understanding and acceptance of the employment offer.
* Enter your date of birth and social security number (if applicable).
* Provide your employee ID (if applicable).
* Select your job title from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employment Offer** (`new_employment_offer`): Enter the details of the new employment offer, including job title and job description.
* **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`): Review and acknowledge the employment terms, which include company policies and expectations. This is a read-only field, where you can read the terms carefully.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`): Enter the date of acknowledgment, when you have read and understood the employment terms.
* **Acknowledgment Time** (`acknowledgment_time`): Enter the time of acknowledgment.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Provide your signature as an employee, indicating your understanding and acceptance of the employment offer.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`): Provide your manager's signature, indicating their understanding and acceptance of the employment offer.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth, if required.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`): Enter your social security number, if required.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID, if required.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select your job title from the options provided, such as Intern, Full-time Employee, Part-time Employee, Contractor, Freelancer, or Temporary Employee.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand the employment terms carefully before signing the form.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Keep the form signed and dated for your records, and provide it to your manager and HR department.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your HR representative.
