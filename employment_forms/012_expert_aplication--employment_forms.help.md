# Expert Aplication - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expert Aplication form is designed to collect information from experts who are interested in applying for various job opportunities. This form helps us understand your qualifications, job preferences, and availability to match you with suitable job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Choose your job type (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
3. Provide a brief job description, highlighting your areas of expertise and relevant experience.
4. Enter your expected job hours and pay rate.
5. List your job requirements, specifying the skills and qualifications you need to excel in a particular role.
6. Select your availability for each of the job openings you are interested in.
7. Upload your resume for us to review.
8. Click "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (Text)**: This is the title of your application, which will be used to identify you in our database.
* **Job Type (Select One)**: Choose whether you are looking for a full-time, part-time, or freelance job opportunity.
* **Job Description (Text)**: Briefly describe your areas of expertise and relevant experience.
* **Job Hours (Number)**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Job Pay (Number)**: Enter your expected hourly or daily pay rate.
* **Job Requirements (Text)**: Specify the skills and qualifications you need to excel in a particular role.
* **Contact Information (Text)**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Upload Resume (Text)**: Upload your resume for us to review.
* **Expert Availability (Select Multiple)**: Choose when you are available to start working on a job (e.g., Available Now, In 1 month, In 3 months, After 3 months).
* **Job Availability (Select One)**: Choose the type of job you are looking for (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* **Job Availability 2 (Select Multiple)**: Choose when you are available to start working on this type of job (e.g., Available Now, In 1 month, In 3 months, After 3 months).
* **Job Availability 3 (Select Multiple)**: Choose when you are available to start working on this type of job (e.g., Available Now, In 1 month, In 3 months, After 3 months).
* **Job Availability 4 (Select Multiple)**: Choose when you are available to start working on this type of job (e.g., Available Now, In 1 month, In 3 months, After 3 months).

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and need to be filled in order to complete the form.
