# Maintenance Worker Interview Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule interviews with maintenance workers for various purposes such as performance evaluation, training, or other meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Schedule Date of the interview using the calendar control.
2. Choose the Selected Location where the interview will take place from the available options.
3. Enter the Candidate Name of the maintenance worker being interviewed.
4. Enter the Candidate Email and Candidate Phone number of the maintenance worker.
5. Select the Interviewers who will conduct the interview from the list of available options.
6. Select the Start Time and End Time of the interview using the time control.
7. Enter the Duration of the interview.
8. Choose the Location where the interview will take place from the available options.
9. Enter the Candidate ID of the maintenance worker.
10. Select the Interviewers 2 who will conduct the interview from the list of available options.
11. Choose the Schedule Time of the day when the interview will take place from the available options.
12. Choose the Schedule Day of the week when the interview will take place from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Date** (Date): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Selected Location** (Select One): Choose the location where the interview will take place.
* **Candidate Name** (Text): Enter the name of the maintenance worker being interviewed.
* **Candidate Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the maintenance worker.
* **Candidate Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the maintenance worker.
* **Interviewers** (Select Multiple): Select the interviewers who will conduct the interview.
* **Start Time** (Time): Select the start time of the interview.
* **End Time** (Time): Select the end time of the interview.
* **Duration** (Number): Enter the duration of the interview.
* **Location** (Select One): Choose the location where the interview will take place.
* **Candidate ID** (Text): Enter the unique identifier for the maintenance worker.
* **Interviewers 2** (Select Multiple): Select the additional interviewers who will conduct the interview.
* **Schedule Time** (Select One): Choose the time of day when the interview will take place.
* **Schedule Day** (Select One): Choose the day of the week when the interview will take place.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
