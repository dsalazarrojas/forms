# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employment information from employees, including name, contact details, employment type, and contract version. It is essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure correct record-keeping and administrative processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with your accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you use for work-related communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this contract.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you signed this contract.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign this form with your signature, preferably in print or digital format.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's or manager's name.
* **Employer Title** (`employer_title`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's or manager's title.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter your workplace address.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of employment you have, such as Full-Time, Part-Time, Intern, or Contract.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, number, required): Enter the version number of this employment contract.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are already employed with the company, do not fill out this form again.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
