# Work Opportunities In Europe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for job opportunities in Europe. Please fill out the form with your relevant details to help us get in touch with you.
## How To Complete This Form
- To ensure accurate submission, please fill out the form completely.
- You can navigate through the form by scrolling down or using the next/previous buttons.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your application.
  - Please be concise and clear in your description.
- **What makes you eligible for this job** (`work_eligibility`, text, optional): Describe your qualifications, experience, or any other factors that make you eligible for this role.
  - This is an opportunity to showcase your relevant skills or experience.
- **What is your earliest start date?** (`available_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your expected start date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  - Be realistic, as you will need to be able to start working on the agreed-upon date.
- **Work Preferences** (`work_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select all regions where you would like to work in Europe.
  - This is a chance to express your location preferences.
- **Work Preferences - Other** (`work_preferences_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please specify other regions.
  - Be specific about where you would like to work.
- **Available to work hours** (`available_work_hours`, select_one, optional): Select your availability to work hours.
  - Choose a time frame that suits you best.
- **Available to work hours - Other** (`available_work_hours_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please specify your available hours.
  - Be clear about your work schedule.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information that you'd like to share.
  - Keep it short and concise.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
  - Use your actual email address, not a work-related one.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - Make sure it's a number you're comfortable sharing.
- **Resume** (`resume`, note, optional): Attach your resume.
  - This will be used to review your qualifications.
- **Work History** (`work_history`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of work experience.
  - This will help us gauge your experience and skills.
- **Work History - Other** (`work_history_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please specify your work experience.
  - Be honest and accurate about your background.
- **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Describe your educational background.
  - Highlight relevant courses or degrees.
- **Level of Education** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest level of education.
  - Choose the best fit for your education level.
- **Education - Other** (`education_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please specify your level of education.
  - Be clear and concise in your description.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted.
  - This will help us reach out to you efficiently.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
  - Use your actual email address.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - Be comfortable sharing your phone number.
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Choose your preferred social media platform.
  - This will help us get in touch with you.
- **LinkedIn** (`social_media_linkedin`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
  - Share your actual profile link.
- **Facebook** (`social_media_facebook`, text, optional): Enter your Facebook profile URL.
  - Share your public Facebook profile link.
- **Twitter** (`social_media_twitter`, text, optional): Enter your Twitter profile URL.
  - Share your public Twitter profile link.
- **Social Media - Other** (`social_media_other`, text, optional): If none of the above options fit, please specify your preferred social media platform.
  - Be honest about your social media presence.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
  - Double-check your submission before clicking.

## Tips
- Please ensure you're submitting accurate and complete information.
- Double-check the form before submitting.
