# Assembly Line Worker Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in working on an assembly line. The form is used to gather essential details about the applicant's identity, experience, availability, and qualifications. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Answer questions regarding your experience, availability, and qualifications honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name (applicant_name_worker)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Phone Number (contact_phone_worker)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email Address (contact_email_worker)**: Enter your valid email address for communication.
- **Years of Manufacturing Experience (experience_years_worker)**: Report the number of years you have worked in a manufacturing environment.
- **Available Shifts (shift_availability_worker)**: Select all shifts you are available to work, using the options provided.
- **Can Lift 50 lbs Repeatedly (physical_requirements_check)**: Answer 'True' if you are able to lift up to 50 lbs repeatedly, or 'False' otherwise.
- **Hand-eye Coordination Self-rating (dexterity_self_rating)**: Rate your hand-eye coordination ability from 1 to 5, with 1 representing poor and 5 representing excellent.
- **Safety Certifications (safety_certs_worker)**: If you have relevant safety certifications, such as OSHA 10 or Forklift license, list them here.
- **Last Manufacturing Employer (previous_employer_worker)**: Enter the name of your previous manufacturing employer.
- **Professional Reference Name (reference_name_worker)**: Enter the name of a professional reference who can vouch for your work experience and qualifications.
- **Highest Level of Education Completed (education_level_worker)**: Select the highest level of education you have completed from the provided options.
- **Date Available to Start Work (start_date_worker)**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
- **Background and Drug Screening Consent (background_check_consent)**: Select 'I Consent' to agree to a background and drug screening, or choose 'Other' if you have concerns.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately to ensure a smooth application process.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
