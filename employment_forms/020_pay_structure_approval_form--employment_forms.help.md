# Pay Structure Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for a new or updated pay structure for an employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Proposed Effective Date, which is the date when the new pay structure will take effect.
2. Provide a clear and concise Proposed Justification for the pay structure change, including the reasons for the change.
3. Select the Manager Approvals required for the pay structure change. Typically, this will include at least two managers, but may include up to three managers.
4. Enter the Employee ID, which is the unique identifier for the employee whose pay structure is being changed.
5. Enter the Pay Rate Change, which is the amount of change in the employee's pay rate.
6. If necessary, enter the Effective Date, which is the date when the pay structure change will actually take place.
7. Enter any Manager Comments regarding the pay structure change.
8. Select the Approved Effective Date, which is the date when the pay structure change will actually be approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pay Structure** (`pay_structure`, text, optional): Enter a description of the new or updated pay structure you are requesting.
* **Proposed Effective Date** (`proposed_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the new pay structure will take effect.
* **Proposed Justification** (`proposed_justification`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for the pay structure change.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three managers that need to approve the pay structure change.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique employee ID for the employee whose pay structure is being changed.
* **Pay Rate Change** (`pay_rate_change`, number, optional): Enter the amount of change in the employee's pay rate.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the pay structure change will actually take place.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from managers regarding the pay structure change.
* **Approved Effective Date** (`approved_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the pay structure change will be actually approved.
* **Pay Structure Approved** (`pay_structure_approved`, select_one, optional): Select whether the pay structure change is approved or not.
