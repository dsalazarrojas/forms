# Nepotism Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nepotism Form is used to identify potential nepotism instances within the company. It helps HR or other personnel department staff gather accurate and complete information about an employee's employment status and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each field. This will ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
2. Make sure to answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details
* **Employee Details** (1, text, required): Fill in the employee's name, surname, and employee ID or any other relevant identifier.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (2, text, optional): If the employee has a title within the company, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
### Years of Service
* **Years of Service** (3, number, required): Enter the employee's years of service with the company.
### Family Relationship
* **Family Relationship** (4, select multiple, required): Choose all the family relationships that apply to the employee (e.g., spouse, sibling, child).
### Nepotism Level
* **Nepotism Level** (5, select one, required): Select the level of nepotism concern related to the employee (e.g., none, slight, moderate, severe).
### Date of Hire
* **Date of Hire** (6, date, optional): If the employee's date of hire is relevant, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
### Department
* **Department** (7, text, required): Enter the department the employee works in.
### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
### Report Date
* **Report Date** (9, date, required): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
### Signature
* **Signature** (10, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask HR or the personnel department staff for guidance.
* If you need to complete the form multiple times, please submit each form separately.
