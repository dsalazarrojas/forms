# Task 1.2 Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from job seekers for job applications. The form is divided into several sections to gather essential details about the job position, location, salary, experience, education, and more. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered for the job.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2.  Provide your job application message in the `job_application_message` field.
3.  Upload your resume, cover letter, and any additional required files in the `resume`, `job_cv`, `job_cover_letter`, and `job_additional_files` fields.
4.  Select your relevant experience level with the `job_experience` field (Yes or No).
5.  Choose your level of education from the options provided in the `job_education` field (Bachelor, Master, Ph.D.).
6.  Select your employment status from the options provided in the `job_employment_status` field (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
7.  Fill in any other relevant details in the `job_job_type`, `job_job_type_description`, `job_employment_status_description`, `job_position_description`, and `job_department` fields.
8.  If none of the provided options fit your situation, you can provide a custom answer in the `job_job_type_other`, `job_employment_status_other` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Job Position**: Enter the position you are applying for.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Salary**: Enter your expected salary or the range you are willing to consider.
* **Job Experience**: Indicate if you have relevant experience for the position (Yes or No).
* **Job Education**: Choose your highest level of education (Bachelor, Master, Ph.D.).
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in a PDF or Word document format.
* **CV**: Upload your CV in a PDF or Word document format.
* **Cover Letter**: Upload your cover letter in a PDF or Word document format.
* **Additional Files**: Upload any other relevant files (e.g., certificates, diplomas).
* **Job Application Message**: Write a brief message to explain why you are a good fit for the position.
* **Job Application Status**: Select the status of your application (Pending, Accepted, Rejected).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Interview Date**: Schedule an interview date and time (if applicable).
* **Interview Time**: Schedule an interview time (if applicable).
* **Reference**: Provide a professional reference (e.g., previous employer, colleague).
* **Job Employer**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Job Department**: Enter the department you are applying to.
* **Job Position Description**: Briefly describe the job position.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job you are applying for (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Job Type Other**: If none of the provided options fit your situation, enter a custom job type.
* **Job Type Description**: Describe the job type you selected.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Employment Status Other**: If none of the provided options fit your situation, enter a custom employment status.
* **Employment Status Description**: Briefly describe your employment status.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and assumes a basic understanding of job application concepts. If you need further clarification or have specific questions, please let me know!
