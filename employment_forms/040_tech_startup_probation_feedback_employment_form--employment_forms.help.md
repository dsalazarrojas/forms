# Tech Startup Probation Feedback Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for managers and employees to collect and document feedback and evaluation on probationary tech startups. It's used to provide a structured and standardized way of capturing and storing important information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields as instructed.
2. Ensure the Manager Notes field is filled out with relevant information about the probationary startup.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the Manager Recommendations field, depending on your assessment.
4. Select a valid date for the Probation Period field.
5. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the Evaluation Outcome field, depending on the outcome.
6. Provide a valid email address for the Manager Signature field.
7. Enter the Employee Name and Employee Title fields with accurate information.
8. Select a valid date for the Evaluation Date field.
9. Fill out the Manager Comments and Employee Comments fields with any additional comments or feedback.
10. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the Form Version field, but this is optional.
11. Select a valid time for the Created At, Updated At, and Submitted At fields.
12. Enter the Submitted By field with the name of the person submitting the form.
13. Fill out the Employee Notes and Employee Recommendations fields with any additional information.
14. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the Evaluation Outcome Reason field, but this is optional.
15. Fill out the Evaluation Outcome Comment field with any additional comments or feedback.
16. Choose "Good" or "Fair" for the Manager Rating field, but this is optional.
17. Fill out the Manager Rating Comment field with any additional comments or feedback.
18. Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for the Evaluation Outcome Status field.
19. Fill out the Evaluation Outcome Description field with any additional information.
20. Fill out the Evaluation Outcome Notes field with any additional information.
