# energy_sector_skilled_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The energy sector skilled employment form is designed to help employers or recruiters match candidates with suitable job openings in the energy sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of job you are looking for (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
2. Select the specific job positions you are interested in (Engineer, Technician, Tradesperson, or one of the other options).
3. Select up to two locations where you are willing to work (Australia, UK, or Canada).
4. Enter your contact information, including a contact number and email.
5. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### energy_sector_skills
* **energy_sector_skills** (`energy_sector_skills`, text, Optional): Please enter a brief description of your relevant skills in the energy sector.

### job_type
* **job_type** (`job_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of job you are looking for:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contract

### job_positions
* **job_positions** (`job_positions`, select_multiple, Optional): Select up to three specific job positions you are interested in:
	+ Engineer
	+ Technician
	+ Tradesperson

### job_locations
* **job_locations** (`job_locations`, select_one, Optional): Select up to two locations where you are willing to work:
	+ Australia
	+ UK
	+ Canada

### contact_number
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### contact_email
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter your contact email address.

### contact_name
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name.

### job_duration
* **job_duration** (`job_duration`, date, Optional): Enter the expected job duration (format: dd/mm/yyyy).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when selecting your job positions and locations.
* You can select up to three job positions and two locations.
* If you have any other relevant skills or information, please include it in the text field for energy_sector_skills.
