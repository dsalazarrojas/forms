# Performance Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review form is used by managers to collect feedback and performance metrics on employees during a specific period. The form helps managers evaluate the employee's performance, identify areas for improvement, and provide guidance for future performance goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the Performance Period field to ensure you are evaluating performance for the correct time frame.
2. Enter the Employee Name, Job Title, and Job Title Held fields with the relevant information.
3. Select the Performance Level for the employee based on their performance during the reviewed period.
4. Provide Performance Comments to elaborate on the employee's performance and any areas for improvement.
5. List Recommendations for the employee to work on during the next performance period.
6. Choose Action Items for the employee to address specific performance issues.
7. Select the Overall Rating based on the employee's overall performance.
8. Select the Assigned To field with the name of the employee who will be responsible for reviewing this performance report.
9. Enter the Date and Time fields to indicate when the performance review was completed.
10. Enter the Output File field if you want to attach a document or file to support the performance review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Period**: This field indicates the time frame being evaluated. Enter the start and end dates of the period being reviewed.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title Held**: Enter the job title the employee held during the performance period.
* **Performance Level**: Select the level of performance based on the employee's work (Excellent, Good, or Poor).
* **Performance Comments**: Provide detailed comments on the employee's performance, including strengths and weaknesses.
* **Recommendations**: List specific recommendations for the employee to work on during the next performance period.
* **Action Items**: Select specific actions the employee needs to take to address performance issues.
* **Overall Rating**: Select the overall rating of the employee's performance (Excellent, Good, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Date**: Enter the date the performance review was completed.
* **Time**: Enter the time the performance review was completed.
* **Assigned To**: Select the name of the employee who will review this performance report.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or resource used for the performance review.
* **Output File**: Attach a document or file to support the performance review, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the performance period carefully to ensure you are evaluating the correct time frame.
* Provide specific and actionable recommendations for the employee to improve their performance.
* Ensure the overall rating accurately reflects the employee's performance.
* Attach any relevant supporting documents or files to the performance review.
