# FullStack Team Lead Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FullStack Team Lead Application form is a comprehensive online form used to gather information from interested candidates for the Team Lead position. This form helps our team to quickly and efficiently evaluate your qualifications and assess your suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required fields to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you only submit a single application per person. Duplicate applications will be discarded.
3. Be honest and accurate when providing information.
4. Use a clear and concise writing style for free-text fields.
5. Attach your resume to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FullStack Team Lead Application** (1, text, required): This is the main title of your application. Please enter a clear and descriptive title that includes your name and relevant experience.
* **category** (2, select_one, optional): Please select the category that best describes this application. This will help us sort and categorize your submission.
