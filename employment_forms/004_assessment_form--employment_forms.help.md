# Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate and assess employee performance, skills, and behavior in a specific assessment period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's first name, last name, and ID number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the employee's department from the provided options.
3. Enter the current position title of the employee.
4. Choose the assessment period start and end dates.
5. Enter the name and signature of the individual conducting the assessment.
6. Rate the employee's performance on the following aspects:
	* Job knowledge and skills
	* Communication skills
	* Teamwork and collaboration
	* Reliability and punctuality
	* Problem-solving ability
	* Customer service focus
	* Goal achievement
7. Enter any areas for improvement or strengths of the employee.
8. Provide a final overall performance rating and any suggestions for growth.
9. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the employee's first name.
  \* (Required)
- **Last Name**: Enter the employee's last name.
  \* (Required)
- **Employee ID Number**: Enter the employee's identification number.
  \* (Required)
- **Department**: Select the employee's department from the options provided.
  \* (Required)
- **Current Position Title**: Enter the employee's current job title.
  \* (Required)
- **Assessment Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the assessment period.
  \* (Required)
- **Assessment Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the assessment period.
  \* (Required)
- **Reporting Manager Name**: Enter the name of the individual who is directly supervising this employee.
  \* (Required)
- **Job Knowledge and Skills**: Rate the employee's job knowledge and skills on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Communication Skills**: Rate the employee's communication skills on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Teamwork and Collaboration**: Rate the employee's teamwork and collaboration on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Reliability and Punctuality**: Rate the employee's reliability and punctuality on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Problem Solving Ability**: Rate the employee's problem-solving ability on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Customer Service Focus**: Rate the employee's customer service focus on a scale of 1-5.
  \* (Scale: 1 - Needs Improvement, 2 - Below Expectations, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Exceptional)
  \* (Required)
- **Goal Achievement**: Enter how well the employee met stated goals.
  \* (Required)
- **Key Strengths**: Describe the employee's key strengths.
  \* (Required)
- **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the employee needs improvement.
  \* (Required)
- **Suggested Development Plan**: Enter any recommendations for the employee's growth.
  \* (Optional)
- **Overall Performance Rating**: Final overall rating of the employee's performance.
  \* (Required)
  \* (Scale: Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Far Exceeds Expectations)
- **Assessor Name**: Enter the name of the individual conducting the assessment.
  \* (Required)
- **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
  \* (Required)

## Tips

- Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
- Be honest and objective when rating the employee's performance.
- Consider providing specific examples or anecdotes to support the ratings and comments.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
- Use the provided fields for any additional comments or recommendations.
- Keep the assessment professional and respectful.
