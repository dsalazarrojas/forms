# Bakery Pastry Chef Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and store information about new bakery and pastry chefs during the onboarding process. This information helps the bakery to efficiently manage employee data, streamline communication, and ensure that all necessary requirements and certifications are met.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and home address.
2. Select your employment details like position title, start date, employment type, and preferred shift.
3. Provide your years of baking/pastry experience, professional certifications or credentials, and details about your previous employers.
4. If applicable, list any food allergies or intolerances.
5. Confirm your work authorization status and willingness to complete a background check.
6. Indicate your food handler certification status and emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address for internal communication and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, required): Please enter your phone number for internal communication and notifications.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your current home address for internal use.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, select_one, required): Please select your current or desired position in the bakery.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Please enter your expected start date or last day of work at your previous employer.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Please select your employment type (full-time, part-time, contract, or seasonal).
* **Preferred Shift** (`shift_preference`, select_one, required): Please select your preferred shift (early morning, day shift, evening, or flexible).
* **Years of Baking/Pastry Experience** (`years_experience`, integer, required): Please enter the number of years of experience you have in baking or pastry arts.
* **Professional Certifications or Credentials** (`certifications`, text, optional): Please list any professional certifications or credentials you hold (culinary degree, pastry certification, etc.).
* **Previous Bakery or Pastry Employers** (`previous_employers`, text, required): Please list 2-3 of your most recent previous employers.
* **Specialty Skills or Techniques** (`specialties`, text, optional): Please list any specialty skills or techniques you possess (bread making, cake decorating, chocolate work, etc.).
* **Work Authorization Status** (`work_authorization`, select_one, required): Please select your work authorization status (citizen, permanent resident, work visa, or need sponsorship).
* **Willing to Complete Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Please confirm your willingness to complete a background check.
* **Food Handler Certification** (`food_handler_cert`, select_one, required): Please select your food handler certification status (current and valid, expired, or none).
* **Food Allergies or Intolerances** (`allergies`, text, optional): Please list any food allergies or intolerances you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, phone, required): Please enter your emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the bakery's HR department for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and is not a public document.
* Please fill out the form honestly and accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
