# Employee Separation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Separation Form is a document used to record an employee's separation from the company. It's essential for maintaining accurate records and providing a smooth transition for the departing employee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the employee's **Employee Name** in the provided text field.
2. Enter the **Job Title** that the employee held while at the company.
3. Select the **Date of Separation**, which marks the last day the employee was actively working.
4. Choose the **Reason for Separation** from the provided options or describe the reason in the text field.
5. Select the **Separation Type** as **Voluntary**, **Involuntary**, or **Unknown**.
6. Enter the **Last Day of Work**, which should match the **Date of Separation** if the separation occurred on the last working day.
7. If applicable, enter the **Notice Period** that the employee received before separation.
8. Select the **Separation Benefit** options to indicate if the employee is eligible for benefits.
9. Enter the **Termination Date**, which should match the **Date of Separation** if the separation date is different from the last day of work.
10. Add any **Comments** about the separation in the text field.
11. Select the **Employee Status** as **Separated**, **Active**, **Retired**, or **Deceased**.
12. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** that the employee used during their employment.
13. Select **User** and **Manager** status for the departing employee.
14. Finally, confirm the employee's **Employee** status as **Yes** or **No**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears in the company's records.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title the employee held at the time of separation.
* **Date of Separation**: The date when the employee stopped working, usually the last day of work.
* **Reason for Separation**: A brief explanation for why the employee is leaving the company.
* **Separation Type**: Choose from **Voluntary**, **Involuntary**, or **Unknown** to indicate the reasons for the separation.
* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the date when the employee's employment ended, usually the same as the **Date of Separation**.
* **Notice Period**: If applicable, enter the number of days or weeks the employee received notice before separation.
* **Separation Benefit**: Select whether the employee is eligible for benefits or not.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date when the employee's employment officially ended.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the separation.
* **Employee Status**: Select the status as **Separated**, **Active**, **Retired**, or **Deceased** to reflect the employee's current status.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, enter the tool or equipment assigned to the employee during their employment.
* **User**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if the user is the employee or not.
* **Manager**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if the manager is the employee's manager.
* **Employee**: Select **Yes** or **No** to confirm the employee's separation status.

Note: This form is a crucial document for maintaining accurate records and facilitating smooth transitions. Ensure that you fill it out with precision and attention to detail.
