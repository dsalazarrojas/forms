# HR Process Intelligence Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help HR teams gather information from applicants about their goals and challenges related to the pilot position. This will aid in better matching candidates with suitable roles within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On page 1, enter your name, title, and other relevant details about yourself.
2. On page 2, indicate whether you have experienced any HR-related challenges in your previous roles or during your career in general. Select all that apply.
3. On page 3, describe your short- and long-term goals related to the pilot position.
4. On page 4, provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
5. On the final page, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`form_1_page_1`, text, required): Please enter your name, title, and other relevant details about yourself.
* **HR Challenges** (`form_2_page_2`, select multiple, required): Check all that apply if you have experienced HR-related challenges. This could include difficulties with communication, conflict resolution, or other HR-specific issues.
* **Goals** (`form_3_page_3`, text, required): Describe your short- and long-term goals related to the pilot position. Be specific about what you hope to achieve and why this role is a good fit for you.
* **Additional Information** (`form_4_page_4`, text, optional): If you have any other relevant information that you would like to share with us, please enter it here. This could include relevant experience, skills, or qualifications.
* **Submit** (`form_5_page_5`, text, required): Double-check your application and submit it to us.
