# Simple Job Application Form White And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Job Application Form White And Responsive is designed to collect information about job seekers and their preferences for employment. This form is used to help us better match job openings with the right candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, using the provided explanations for guidance.
3. Submit the completed form to proceed with your job application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your email address, so we can reach you about your application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Status** (`job status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your job, whether it's Active or Inactive.
* **Job Type** (`job type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have a job or not.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the skills you have that match the job requirements.
* **Work Experience** (`work experience`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have experience in the job or not.
* **Job Level** (`job level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of your job, whether High or Low.
* **Job Status** (`job status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your job, whether Active or Inactive.
* **Job Education** (`job education`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have a job-related education or not.
* **Job Salary** (`job salary`, select_one, optional): Select whether you receive a salary for your job.
* **Job Employment Status** (`job employment status`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are employed or not.
* **Job Industry** (`job industry`, text, optional): Enter the industry of your job.
* **Job Job Type** (`job job type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have a job or not.
* **Job Work Location** (`job work location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your job.
* **Job Availability** (`job availability`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Job Shift** (`job shift`, select_one, optional): Choose your work shift, whether Yes or No.
* **Job Start Date** (`job start date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your current job.
* **Job End Date** (`job end date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your current job.
* **Job Notes** (`job notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your job.
* **Job References** (`job references`, text, optional): Enter the names of people who can vouch for your work experience.
* **Job Agreed Terms** (`job agreed terms`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have agreed to the terms of your employment.
* **Job Education** (`job education`, text, optional): Enter information about your education related to your job.
 
Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if you do not have the necessary information. Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible matches for job openings.
