# Sourcing Expert Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from sourcing experts who are interested in expressing their interest in potential job opportunities. It will help the employer's sourcing team to quickly assess the candidate's suitability for the job and match them with job openings that fit their skills and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the job you are interested in, including job title, current company, and job type.
3. Select the skills and experience you have that match the job requirements.
4. If applicable, upload your resume and cover letter.
5. Finally, click the submit button to submit your expression of interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section is used to collect your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you about the job opportunity.
* **Job Information**: Please provide details about the job you are interested in, including job title, current company, and job type. This will help us match you with the right job openings.
* **Skills**: Select all the skills that you have that match the job requirements. This will help us assess your suitability for the job.
* **Experience**: Select all the experience you have that match the job requirements. This will help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Education**: Provide any relevant education details that match the job requirements.
* **Availability**: Indicate whether you are available to start immediately or not.
* **Job Title**: If you have a specific job title that you are interested in, please enter it here.
* **Current Company**: If you are currently working, please enter your current company name.
* **Job Type**: If you have a specific job type in mind, please enter it here.
* **Work Location**: If you have a specific location where you would like to work, please enter it here.
* **Work Status**: Indicate whether you are currently active in the job market or not.
* **Resume**: If you have a resume, please upload it here. This will help us get a better understanding of your background and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter**: If you have a cover letter, please upload it here. This will help us understand your motivation and interest in the job opportunity.
* **Job References**: If you have job references, please provide their names and contact information. This will help us verify your work experience.

Tips:

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific and clear in your answers to help us assess your suitability for the job.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
