# Consulting Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a consulting agreement between you (the consultant) and your client. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the scope of services, payment expectations, and termination conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Consultant Full Name" field.
2. Enter the client's company name in the "Client Company Name" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of the agreement using the date picker.
4. Enter a detailed list of tasks and deliverables in the "Description of Services" field.
5. Specify the estimated hours per week you will commit to in the "Estimated Hours Per Week" field (if applicable).
6. Select how you will be paid in the "Consulting Fee Type" field.
7. Enter the fee amount for the selected fee type in the "Fee Amount" field.
8. Choose the payment frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or upon completion) in the "Payment Frequency" field.
9. If applicable, specify the number of days' notice required to terminate the agreement in the "Termination Notice Period" field.
10. Select whether you agree to keep the client's data private in the "Confidentiality Agreement" field.
11. Finally, sign the agreement by entering today's date in the "Consultant Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name to ensure we have your correct information on file.
* **Client Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the client's company to help us identify the client.
* **Agreement Start Date** (Date, Required): Select the start date of the agreement to mark its beginning.
* **Agreement End Date** (Date, Optional): Select the end date of the agreement to mark its termination.
* **Description of Services** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed list of the tasks and deliverables you will provide to the client.
* **Estimated Hours Per Week** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the approximate hours you will commit to per week.
* **Scope of Services** (Note, Non-Required): A placeholder for you to add additional information about the scope of services.
* **Consulting Fee Type** (Select One, Required): Select how you will be paid (Hourly Rate, Fixed Project Fee, Monthly Retainer, Commission Based).
* **Fee Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the dollar amount for the selected fee type.
* **Payment Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose the payment frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Upon Completion).
* **Additional Terms** (Note, Non-Required): A placeholder for you to add any additional terms or agreements.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Select One, Required): Select whether you agree to keep the client's data private (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
* **Termination Notice Period** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the number of days' notice required to end the agreement.
* **Additional Terms** (Note, Non-Required): A placeholder for you to add any additional terms or agreements.
* **Consultant Signature Date** (Date, Required): Enter today's date to sign the agreement.

This guide is designed to help you complete the consulting agreement form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
