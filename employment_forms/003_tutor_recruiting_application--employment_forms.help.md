# Tutor Recruiting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential tutors to aid in the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your "Email" address, this will be used as a unique identifier for our internal processes.
3. Enter your "Phone" number, if you have one.
4. Mention your "Instrument" of choice (e.g., Guitar, Piano, etc.).
5. Specify your "Desired Skill" (e.g., teaching, tutoring, etc.).
6. Share your "Experience" in the relevant field (if any).
7. Select your "Availability" in a standard time format (e.g., 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, etc.).
8. Finally, enter a brief "Message" about yourself and your qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used as a unique identifier for our internal processes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, text, optional): Mention the instrument you play or have experience with (e.g., Guitar, Piano, etc.).
* **Desired Skill** (`desired_skill`, text, optional): Specify the skill you are looking to use or have experience with (e.g., teaching, tutoring, etc.).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant experience you have in the field (if any).
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Select your availability in a standard time format (e.g., 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, etc.).
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message about yourself and your qualifications.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email).
- If you have any relevant experience or skills, please mention them in the "Experience" and "Desired Skill" fields.
- Be specific about your availability, so we can schedule interviews or lessons accordingly.
- Keep your "Message" brief and to the point, we would like to get a clear understanding of your qualifications and experience.
