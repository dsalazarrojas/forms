# Marketplace Performance Specialist Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Performance Specialist Interview form is a document used to gather relevant details about a job candidate's experience and qualifications for the role of a Marketplace Performance Specialist. This form is only to be completed for candidates who have a direct request for an interview, or if a hiring manager has assigned it to a specific candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form for candidates who have been requested by a hiring manager or who have a direct request for an interview.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete the form, as it may contain sensitive information.
3. Be accurate and truthful in your responses, as this information will be used to assess the candidate's suitability for the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Candidate Details
* **Candidate Details** (1): Enter the candidate's full name, job title, and any other relevant details about the candidate.
* **Marketplace Experience** (2): Select all marketplaces where the candidate has experience working with. This will help us understand their breadth of experience and potential fit for the role.
* **Interview Schedule** (3): Enter the date and time of the interview, if applicable.
* **Candidate Email** (4): Enter the candidate's email address for communication purposes.
* **Candidate Phone** (5): Enter the candidate's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (6): Enter any additional comments or notes about the candidate.
* **Other Experience** (7): Select any other experiences or certifications the candidate has that may be relevant to the role.
* **Skills** (8): Select all relevant skills the candidate possesses that may be useful for the role.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use the interview schedule field only if the interview has been scheduled.
* Be thorough in listing all marketplaces, experiences, and skills for the candidate.
* Keep the note field concise and focused on the most important information about the candidate.
