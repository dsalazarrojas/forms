# Sickness Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sickness Leave Form is a comprehensive and standardized template designed to collect essential details about employee sickness leave, ensuring seamless processing and evaluation by management and HR teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sickness Leave Form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information as requested.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details**: This section collects your basic employee information, including your employee ID.
* **Sickness Leave Dates**: Please provide the dates of your sickness leave, including start and end dates.
* **Sickness Reason**: Enter a clear and concise description of your reason for sickness leave.
* **Doctor Details**: Share information about your doctor's visit, including their name and contact details.
* **Pay Details**: Specify any pay-related information, such as salary details or leave compensation.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Check if your supervisor has approved your sickness leave.
* **Employee Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Submission**: Confirm the submission status of your leave application.
* **Submitted By**: Confirm who submitted the application.
* **Submission Status**: Check the status of your leave application.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your sickness leave.
* **Attachments**: Attach any supporting documents, such as medical certificates or reports.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the information provided to avoid errors and misunderstandings.
*   Attach any necessary supporting documents to facilitate quick processing and evaluation of your leave application.
