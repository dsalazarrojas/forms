# Job Screening Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Screening Registration Form is designed to collect information from candidates for job screening purposes. This form is intended to gather details about a candidate's employment history, skills, education, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the candidate information page (Page 1). Enter your name, as you would like to be known for the purpose of this registration.
2. On the Resume Upload page (Page 2), upload a copy of your current resume or a relevant document that showcases your experience.
3. Move on to the Contact Information page (Page 3), where you'll provide your contact information for further communication.
4. On the Employment Details page (Page 4), share your current job title, company, and a brief description of your role.
5. Next, fill out the Education page (Page 5), by listing your highest level of education completed, the name of your alma mater, and a brief description of your relevant studies or courses.
6. On the Skills page (Page 6), indicate any skills you possess that are relevant to the position you are applying for.
7. For the Job Opening page (Page 7), provide any information about the job or role you are applying for.
8. Finally, on the Document Upload page (Page 8), upload any supporting documents required for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information (`Page 1`: candidate-information):** This is where you'll introduce yourself to the hiring team. Enter your name as you would like to be known for the purpose of this registration.
* **Resume Upload (`Page 2`: resume-upload):** Please upload a copy of your current resume or any document that highlights your relevant experience.
* **Contact Information (`Page 3`: contact-information):** This is where you can provide your contact information to the hiring team for further communication.
* **Employment Details (`Page 4`: employment-details):** Share your current job title, the name of your current or previous employer, and a brief description of your role or responsibilities.
* **Education (`Page 5`: education):** List your highest level of education completed, the name of your institution, and a brief description of any relevant studies or courses.
* **Skills (`Page 6`: skills):** Indicate any skills you believe are relevant to the position you're applying for.
* **Job Opening (`Page 7`: job-opening):** This field is meant to provide additional information about the job you're applying for, or the position you are interested in.
* **Upload Document (`Page 8`: upload-document):** Upload any documents that may support your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Ensure that your uploaded documents are relevant to your application.
- Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
