# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects essential medical employment information from users to ensure accurate and up-to-date records. It's used to provide necessary details about an employee's medical history, emergency contacts, and insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
4. If you have an emergency contact, enter their First Name in the **Emergency Contact First Name** field.
5. If you have an emergency contact, enter their Last Name in the **Emergency Contact Last Name** field.
6. Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, address, email) in the **Contact Information** field.
7. If you have medical insurance, enter your insurance provider's name in the **Insurance Provider** field.
8. If you have medical insurance, enter your policy number in the **Insurance Policy Number** field.
9. Indicate whether you have medical insurance by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Medical Insurance Type** field.
10. If you have medical insurance, enter the group name or number in the **Medical Insurance Group** field.
11. If you have an emergency contact, indicate the relationship (e.g., family member, friend) in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
12. If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
13. If you have an emergency contact, enter their address in the **Emergency Contact Address** field.
14. List any medical history in the **Medical History** field.
15. List any medications in the **Medications** field.
16. Provide any medical notes in the **Medical Notes** field.
17. Enter the date of your medical employment in the **Medical Employment Info** field.
18. Indicate whether you need medical clearance by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Medical Clearance** field.
19. If you need medical clearance, enter other information in the **Medical Clearance Other** field.
20. Provide details about your medical clearance in the **Medical Clearance Details** field.
21. Enter any medical condition in the **Medical Condition** field.
22. Enter details about your medical condition in the **Medical Condition Details** field.
23. Enter any other medical history in the **Other Medical History** field.
24. Enter your medical employment history in the **Medical Employment History** field.
25. Indicate whether you have emergency contact information by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Medical Emergency Contact** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (text, optional): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text, optional): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Information** (text, required): Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, address, email).
* **Insurance Provider** (text, required): Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (text, required): Enter your policy number.
* **Medical Insurance Type** (select one, optional): Indicate whether you have medical insurance by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Insurance Group** (text, optional): Enter the group name or number of your medical insurance.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select one, optional): Indicate the relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (text, optional): List any medical history.
* **Medications** (text, required): List any medications.
* **Medical Notes** (text, required): Enter any medical notes.
* **Medical Employment Info** (date, required): Enter the date of your medical employment.
* **Medical Clearance** (select one, optional): Indicate whether you need medical clearance by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Medical Clearance** (text, optional): Enter other medical clearance information.
* **Medical Clearance Details** (text, optional): Enter details about your medical clearance.
* **Medical Condition** (text, optional): Enter any medical condition.
* **Medical Condition Details** (text, optional): Enter details about your medical condition.
* **Other Medical History** (text, optional): Enter any other medical history.
* **Medical Employment History** (text, optional): Enter your medical employment history.
* **Medical Emergency Contact** (select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have emergency contact information by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
