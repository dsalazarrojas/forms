# Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form" is used to document an employee's religious exemption from receiving the COVID-19 vaccine in the workplace. This form is typically used to support employees who object to vaccination due to religious beliefs and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling it out correctly.
2.  Provide the required information as prompted.
3.  Submit the completed form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`):
  • **Label:** employee_info
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Purpose:** Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.

* **Religion Name** (`religion_name`):
  • **Label:** religion_name
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Purpose:** Enter the name of your religion or belief system that you believe in.

* **Exemption Reason** (`exemption_reason`):
  • **Label:** exemption_reason
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Purpose:** Explain in detail why you are seeking a religious exemption from the COVID-19 vaccine.

* **Doctor's Certificate** (`doctor_certificate`):
  • **Label:** doctor_certificate
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Purpose:** If applicable, attach a doctor's certificate or note to support your exemption request.

* **Signature** (`signature`):
  • **Label:** signature
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Purpose:** Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the exemption request.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any section or field, please contact your HR or HR representative.
* This form is for your personal and confidential information only, so be sure to keep it private.
