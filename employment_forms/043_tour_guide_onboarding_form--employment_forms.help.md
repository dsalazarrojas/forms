# Tour Guide Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tour Guide Onboarding Form is a form used to track the onboarding process of tour guides. This form captures crucial information such as tour guide applications, onboarding status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all of the applications that you are applying for.
   * **Tour Guide Applications** (`tour_guide_applications`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to choose the tour guide applications you are applying for. Choose all the relevant options from the list.
2. Provide a brief description of your onboarding details.
   * **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your onboarding process, including any relevant information about your tour guide experience and qualifications.
3. Indicate the status of your onboarding.
   * **Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your onboarding process (Active or Inactive).
4. Enter your name as the tour operator.
   * **Tour Operator Name** (`tour_operator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the tour operator.
5. Enter your name as the travel agency (optional).
   * **Travel Agency Name** (`travel_agency_name`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a travel agency, enter their name here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the experience provider you are affiliated with.
   * **Experience Provider Name** (`experience_provider_name`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an experience provider, enter their name here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
7. Enter the date of your onboarding.
   * **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your onboarding.
8. Enter the name of the person assigned to you.
   * **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Enter the name of the person assigned to you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields marked as required.
- Double-check that you have entered the correct information for each field.
