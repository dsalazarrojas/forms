# Payroll Record Review Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Record Review Consent Form is used by employees to request review and/or modification of their payroll records. It is an internal form submitted to the HR or payroll department for processing payroll record requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Information** (page 1) with your details, as required (if any).
2. Describe your **Payroll Record Review Request** on **Payroll Record Review** (page 2), including a clear explanation of your request.
3. Choose an option on **Authorize Payroll Record Review** (page 3) to indicate your approval or request for review/modification.
4. Sign your name on **Signature** (page 4), as this form requires a digital or handwritten signature for confirmation.
5. Select a **Date** (page 5) for the form submission, which is the date you are requesting the review.
6. If you have any specific **Goals** for the review/modification, describe them on **Goals** (page 6).
7. Add any **Comments** (page 7) you'd like to provide context for your request.
8. Enter your **Employee ID** (page 8) if required (if any), which is a unique identifier for you as an employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (employee_information): A page to collect employee details, likely for HR/payroll purposes.
* **Payroll Record Review** (payroll_record_review): A text field to describe your payroll record review request, please provide a clear explanation.
* **Authorize Payroll Record Review** (authorization_request): Choose one of the two options: authorize or request modification.
* **Signature** (signature): A field to capture a digital or handwritten signature, confirming the form submission.
* **Date** (date): A date field to select the date of the form submission.
* **Goals** (goals): A text field to describe specific goals related to the payroll record review/modification.
* **Comments** (comments): A text field to add any additional comments or context to your request.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): A field to enter your unique employee ID, if required.
 
## Tips
- Be sure to provide clear and specific information to help the HR/payroll department process your request efficiently. 
- Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
