# employment_contract_acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to acknowledge an employee's contract information, ensuring that their employment contract is properly recorded and verified.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Select the date you received your employment contract.
3. Select the date you reviewed your employment contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contract Date Received**: The date on which you received your employment contract.
* **Contract Date Reviewed**: The date on which you reviewed and agreed to the terms of your employment contract.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or manager.
* This form is meant to be a record of your employment contract, if you have any amendments or updates to the contract, please notify your HR representative promptly.
* Please fill out this form carefully and completely, as it will be used as a reference for future HR purposes.
