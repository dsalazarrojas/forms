# MAM Career Readiness Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MAM Career Readiness Application form is designed to help you showcase your career readiness and job preferences for a specific job opportunity. This form will guide you through a series of questions and fields to help you present your skills, experience, and background in the best possible light.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read through the job description and requirements provided to you carefully.
2. Fill out each field according to your relevant experience, skills, and background.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Required: False): A brief introduction about yourself, if you have any specific experiences or qualifications that you believe are relevant to the job.
* **Questions** (Page 2, Required: False): If you have any questions or concerns about the job opportunity, feel free to express them here.
* **Attention** (Page 3, Required: False): Rate your level of attention to detail and ability to focus on tasks. Please select "Yes" if you can work with minimal supervision, or "No" if you need close monitoring.
* **Skills** (Page 4, Required: True): Select "Yes" if you have the skills required for the job, or "No" if you are missing any of the essential skills.
* **Experience** (Page 5, Required: False): Select "Yes" if you have experience in the relevant field, or "No" if you are new to the field.
* **Application Process** (Page 6, Required: False): Provide any additional information you think would be helpful to know for the application process.
* **Self Reflection** (Page 7, Required: True): Enter the date you are reflecting on your self-improvement plan.
* **Job Facts** (Page 8, Required: False): If you have any information about the job that you think is relevant, feel free to provide it.
* **Career Goals** (Page 9, Required: True): Select "Yes" if you have set long-term career goals related to the job, or "No" if you are just looking for a job.
* **Education Facts** (Page 10, Required: True): Provide any relevant education or training background you may have.
* **Personal Info** (Page 11, Required: False): If you have a personal connection to the job or the company, you can include it here.
* **Contact Info** (Page 12, Required: True): Please enter your email address and other relevant contact information for follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 13, Required: False): Any additional information you may have about yourself that you feel is relevant to the application.
* **Submission** (Page 14, Required: True): Select "Yes" to indicate you have submitted your application, or "No" if you have not.
* **Job Title** (Page 15, Required: True): Select "Yes" if you are applying for a job in the same role, or "No" if you are applying for a different role.
* **Job Description** (Page 16, Required: True): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (Page 17, Required: True): Select "Yes" if the job is full-time, or "No" if it is part-time or freelance.
* **Job Industry** (Page 18, Required: True): Select "Yes" if you have experience in the same industry, or "No" if you are new to the industry.
* **Job Category** (Page 19, Required: True): Select "Yes" if you have experience in the same category, or "No" if you are new to the category.
* **Job Salary** (Page 20, Required: True): Enter the salary range you are expecting for the job.
* **Job Location** (Page 21, Required: True): Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Employer** (Page 22, Required: True): Enter the name of the company that hired you for the job.
* **Submission Date** (Page 23, Required: True): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Submission Time** (Page 24, Required: True): Enter the time of submission.
