# Employment Continuation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for employees who wish to request to continue their work after their initial contract ends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

- **Employee Details**: Provide your name, ID or badge number, and a brief description of your request to continue working.

- **Continuation of Service Details**: Give a clear explanation of why you want to continue working and what you can do to help with the transition to ensure continuity of service.

- **Supporting Documents**: Attach any documents that support your request to continue working, such as references or letters of recommendation.
