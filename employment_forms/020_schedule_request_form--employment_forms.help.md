# Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Request Form is used to request approval for schedule changes. This form is used by employees to request time off or changes to their schedule and requires approval from their manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the schedule request questions on the first two pages. Choose whether the schedule change is for weekly, monthly, or yearly.
2. On the third page, select the frequency of your request. You can choose multiple frequencies if desired.
3. Fill in your start and end dates for the schedule change.
4. Enter the number of work hours and work days you will need to take off.
5. Add any comments you may have about your schedule request.
6. If your manager has approved your request, mark 'Yes' for manager approval.
7. Add any comments from your manager.
8. If you have approved your manager's request, mark 'Yes' for employee approval.
9. Add any comments from the employee.
10. Fill in your manager's name, phone number, email, address, and any notes.
11. Fill in your employee's name, phone number, email, address, and any notes.
