# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Form is a document used to verify the employment status of an employee by gathering information from a referee (someone who can vouch for the employee's employment) that confirms the employee's employment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you should:
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Make sure the referee is authorized to provide this information.
3. Ensure the referee is aware of the purpose of the form.
4. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Please fill in the name of the referee as it appears on their name card.
* **Position (2)**: Fill in the position of the referee as it appears on their name card.
* **Organization (3)**: Please fill in the organization of the referee as it appears on their name card.
* **Phone (4)**: Please fill in the referee's phone number for further contact.
* **Email (5)**: Please fill in the referee's email address for further contact.
* **Relationship (6)**: Choose the relationship between you and the referee from the following options:
	+ Manager
	+ Supervisor
	+ Colleague
	+ Other
* **Since (7)**: Please fill in the date the referee has known the employee.
* **Message (8)**: This field is not mandatory. If you want to add any additional information or comments about the employee, you can use this text box.
* **Form Section 2 (9)**: This section is not a field for your information, it is a placeholder for other fields, please leave it blank.
* **Referee Agreement (10)**: Please select one of the options:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree
