This form is designed to collect information from evaluators regarding a peer's performance, behavior, or skills in a specific area. The goal is to gather feedback that can be used to support professional development, training, or personnel decisions. To ensure relevance, please fill out this form only when it's necessary and not as a formality or administrative task.

## Peer Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect peer feedback and evaluation on a specific peer's performance or behavior. It's an opportunity for evaluators to provide constructive feedback that can help improve individual growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name and last name of the peer you are evaluating.
2. Rate the peer's performance or behavior using the provided rating scale.
3. Add a comment or suggestion for improvement.
4. Indicate if you would recommend the peer for a specific opportunity or role.
5. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Select a date and time if relevant to the evaluation.
7. Write any additional notes or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the peer you are evaluating.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the peer you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (`form_rating`, number, optional): Rate the peer's performance or behavior using the provided scale. (Note: This field is intended to measure performance or behavior, but the specific scale is not defined in the provided YAML. It's assumed to be a standard 1-5 or 1-10 scale.)
* **Comment** (`form_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Recommend** (`form_recommend`, select_one, optional, 'True' or 'False'): Indicate if you would recommend the peer for a specific opportunity or role.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Select a date that's relevant to the evaluation.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Select a time that's relevant to the evaluation.
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Write any additional notes or feedback about the peer.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Focus on specific behaviors or actions rather than general traits.
* Keep your comments clear and concise.
* Use the provided fields to provide specific information about the peer's performance or behavior.
