# Job Offer Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Job Offer Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to evaluate job offers received by the company. This form helps gather essential information to make informed decisions about which job offers to pursue.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the type of job**: Choose the type of job you are evaluating, such as 'Job A', 'Job B', or 'Job C'.
2. **Enter job details**: Provide a brief description of the job offer, including any relevant details about the position.
3. **Enter job salary**: Enter the salary offered for the job.
4. **Enter job location**: Enter the location of the job.
5. **Enter job type**: Select the type of job, such as full-time or part-time.
6. **Enter job duration**: Enter the duration of the job.
7. **Enter job status**: Select the status of the job, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
8. **Enter job notes**: Provide any additional comments about the job offer.
9. **Enter company information**: Enter the name of the company offering the job.
10. **Enter contact information**: Enter the contact person's name and any other relevant contact information.
11. **Enter job offer value**: Enter a value for the job offer, which could be a numeric value or a comment.
12. **Evaluation date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
13. **Evaluation time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
14. **Main page comments**: Provide any additional comments about the main page.
15. **Job offer comments**: Provide any comments about the job offer.
16. **Company comments**: Provide any comments about the company.
17. **Contact comments**: Provide any comments about the contact person.
18. **Job status comments**: Provide any comments about the job status.
19. **Job type comments**: Provide any comments about the job type.
20. **Job salary comments**: Provide any comments about the job salary.
21. **Job location comments**: Provide any comments about the job location.
22. **Job duration comments**: Provide any comments about the job duration.
23. **Main page checkbox**: Select whether the main page is complete or not.
24. **Main page checkbox label**: Enter any label for the main page checkbox.
25. **Evaluation date comments**: Provide any comments about the evaluation date.
