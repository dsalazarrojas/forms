# Food Service Worker Recommendation Letter Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for requesting a letter of recommendation for a food service worker. It typically requires information from the worker who is requesting the letter, such as their name and contact details, as well as details about their employment history, including their job title, years of service, and job start date. This information will be used to create a formal letter recommending the worker for their position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the applicant's contact information, including their name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
2.  Enter the recommender's information, including their name, title, and contact details.
3.  Provide the applicant's job details, such as their job title, years of service, and job start date.
4.  Review and submit the form to create the letter of recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information** (Text, Not Required): This field is where you can enter the applicant's contact information, including their name and any other relevant details.
*   **Name** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the applicant's name. Please enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Email** (Email, Not Required): This field is for the applicant's email address. Please enter a valid email address that the applicant can be reached at.
*   **Recommender Information** (Text, Not Required): This field is for additional information about the recommender, such as their role or relationship with the applicant.
*   **Name** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the recommender's name. Please enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Title** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the recommender's title or position.
*   **Company** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the name of the company the recommender works for or represents.
*   **Contact Information** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the recommender's contact details, such as their phone number or physical address.
*   **Name** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the recommender's name. Please enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Job Title** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the job title of the applicant.
*   **Years of Service** (Text, Not Required): This field is for the number of years the applicant has worked in their job position.
*   **Job Start Date** (Date, Not Required): This field is for the date the applicant started their job. Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to create a complete and accurate letter of recommendation.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the form owner for assistance.
*   The letter of recommendation will be sent to the applicant once you have submitted the form.
