# Manufacturing Worker Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manufacturing Worker Employment Form is designed to collect vital information about an individual's employment status and other essential details. This form serves as a tool for gathering relevant data in an administrative or managerial context, particularly in the manufacturing industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1. Provide accurate contact information (page 1).
2. Enter your personal details (page 2).
3. Specify your current job details (page 3).
4. Confirm your employment status (page 4).
5. List your emergency contacts and specify the relationships you have with them (pages 5-6).
6. Indicate your shift preferences (page 7).
7. Select the emergency contacts you have relationships with (pages 8-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_information`, text, required: false):
Enter your name and contact information for official purposes.
* **Personal Details (Page 2)** (`personal_details`, text, required: false):
Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Job Details (Page 3)** (`job_details`, text, required: false):
Provide detailed information about your current job position or role.
* **Employment Status (Page 4)** (`employment_status`, text, required: false):
Select your current employment status.
* **Emergency Contacts (Page 5)** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false):
Enter details about your emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Relationships (Page 6)** (`emergency_contact_relationships`, select_multiple, required: false):
Indicate the relationship you have with each emergency contact (e.g., parent, partner, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Relationships 2 (Page 7)** (`emergency_contact_relationships_2`, select_one, required: false):
Specify the nature of the relationship with your first emergency contact.
* **Shift Preferences (Page 8)** (`shift_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false):
Select your preferred shifts or schedules.
* **Emergency Contact 1 (Page 9)** (`emergency_contact_1`, select_one, required: false):
Select whether you have an emergency relationship with this contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 (Page 10)** (`emergency_contact_2`, select_one, required: false):
Specify the nature of the relationship with your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 3 (Page 11)** (`emergency_contact_3`, select_one, required: false):
Specify the nature of the relationship with your third emergency contact.

Note: If a field is marked as required, ensure it is completed. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
