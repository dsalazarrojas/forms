# Global Employment Separation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Global Employment Separation Form is used to track and document information related to employment separation, including offboarding process, reasons for separation, and follow-up interactions. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and stored consistently, making it easier to analyze and act on it.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form sections to understand what information is being collected.
2.  Complete each section accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Use the available options to select the most appropriate answers.
4.  Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Offboarding Process
* **Offboarding Process** (`offboarding_process`, note, required: false): This field provides an opportunity to describe the offboarding process used in your organization. Please include any relevant details about the procedures and steps taken to ensure a smooth transition.

### Reason for Separation
* **Reason for Separation** (`reason_for_separation`, select_one, required: false): Choose the reason for the employee's separation, such as resignation, termination, or retirement.

### Date of Separation
* **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the separation occurred.

### Reason for Departing Manager
* **Reason for Departing Manager** (`reason_for_departing_manager`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable reasons why the manager left or was let go.

### Manager Who Served as Offboarding
* **Manager Who Served as Offboarding** (`manager_who_served_as_offboarding`, select_one, required: false): Identify the manager who handled the offboarding process.

### Date of Last Interaction
* **Date of Last Interaction** (`date_of_last_interaction`, date, required: false): Record the date of the last interaction with the employee.

### Last Interaction Method
* **Last Interaction Method** (`last_interaction_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the method used for the last interaction, such as email, phone call, or meeting.

### Last Interaction Details
* **Last Interaction Details** (`last_interaction_details`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the last interaction with the employee.

### Last Interaction Outcome
* **Last Interaction Outcome** (`last_interaction_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Record the outcome of the last interaction.

### Reason for Non-Compliance
* **Reason for Non-Compliance** (`reason_for_non_compliance`, select_one, required: false): Identify the reason for non-compliance, if applicable.

### Manager Who Served as Non-Compliance
* **Manager Who Served as Non-Compliance** (`manager_who_served_as_non_compliance`, select_one, required: false): Identify the manager who handled non-compliance issues.

### Last Interaction Outcome Details
* **Last Interaction Outcome Details** (`last_interaction_outcome_details`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the last interaction outcome.

### Last Interaction Status
* **Last Interaction Status** (`last_interaction_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the last interaction, such as complete, incomplete, or pending.

### Manager Who Served Last Interaction
* **Manager Who Served Last Interaction** (`manager_who_served_last_interaction`, select_one, required: false): Identify the manager who handled the last interaction.

### Date of Last Communication
* **Date of Last Communication** (`date_of_last_communication`, date, required: false): Record the date of the last communication with the employee.

### Next Steps to Take
* **Next Steps to Take** (`next_steps_to_take`, text, required: false): Describe the next steps to be taken in the employment separation process.

### Manager Who Served as Next Steps
* **Manager Who Served as Next Steps** (`manager_who_served_as_next_steps`, select_one, required: false): Identify the manager who handles the next steps.

### Follow-up Action
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, text, required: false): Describe the follow-up action taken.

### Follow-up Status
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the follow-up action, such as complete, incomplete, or pending.

### Last Follow-up Date
* **Last Follow-up Date** (`last_follow_up_date`, date, required: false): Record the date of the last follow-up action.

### Last Follow-up Details
* **Last Follow-up Details** (`last_follow_up_details`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the last follow-up action.

### Last Follow-up Outcome
* **Last Follow-up Outcome** (`last_follow_up_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Record the outcome of the last follow-up action.

### Last Follow-up Reason
* **Last Follow-up Reason** (`last_follow_up_reason`, text, required: false): Provide the reason for the last follow-up action.

### Last Follow-up Status
* **Last Follow-up Status** (`last_follow_up_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the last follow-up action, such as complete, incomplete, or pending.

### Last Follow-up Comment
* **Last Follow-up Comment** (`last_follow_up_comment`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the last follow-up action.

### Tips

* Make sure to review and update your answers regularly to ensure they are up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the available options to select the most suitable answers for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your organization's policies and procedures or consult with your manager.
