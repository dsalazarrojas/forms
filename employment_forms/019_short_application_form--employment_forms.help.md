# Short Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information, employment details, and job application data from applicants. It is intended for both active and inactive job status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Upload your resume and any required attachments.
3. Provide your employment start and end dates, as well as your current job status.
4. Select your job type and category.
5. Enter your education level and any relevant skills you possess.
6. Choose your job location and status.
7. Enter your job level and category.
8. Select your application status and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Upload your resume.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter your employment start date.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter your employment end date.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required: false): Enter your job type.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false): Enter your relevant skills.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your education level (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required: false): Enter your job location.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, text, required: false): Enter your job type 2.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, required: false): Enter your job level.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, required: false): Enter your job category.
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose your job status 2 (Active or Inactive).
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose your application status (Active or Inactive).
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required: false): Enter the application date.
* **Resume Attachment** (`resume_attachment`, text, required: false): Upload your resume attachment.
* **Cover Letter Attachment** (`cover_letter_attachment`, text, required: false): Upload your cover letter attachment.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, text, required: false): Enter your job reference.

This help guide should give users a clear understanding of the form's purpose and how to complete it.
