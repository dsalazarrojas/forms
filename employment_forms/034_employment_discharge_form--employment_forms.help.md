This guide is intended for HR personnel and management staff who need to complete the employment discharge form for an employee. The purpose of this form is to document an employee's departure from the organization. To ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly, please review the following guide carefully.

## Employment Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment discharge form is a crucial document that outlines the reason for an employee's departure from the company. It is essential to complete this form to maintain accurate records and for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before starting the form.
2. Fill in the employee's details:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID or in your HR records.
	* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique ID number as per the company's ID system.
3. Provide a clear and concise reason for discharge:
	* **Reason for Discharge**: Explain why the employee is being discharged, ensuring it is professional and respectful.
	* **Reason for Discharge Details**: Provide additional details about the reason for discharge if necessary.
4. Document the dates:
	* **Discharge Date**: Record the date of the employee's last working day.
	* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the date the employee's employment officially ended.
	* **Termination Date**: If applicable, enter the date the employee's employment was terminated (e.g., due to contract expiration).
5. Identify who the employee was assigned to:
	* **Assigned to**: Select the person or team the employee will be reporting to after their departure.
6. Add any additional information:
	* **Notes**: Include any other relevant details about the employee's departure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: The employee's name as it appears on their ID or in your HR records.
* **Employee ID**: The employee's unique ID number as per the company's ID system.
* **Reason for Discharge**: A clear and concise explanation for the employee's departure.
* **Reason for Discharge Details**: Additional details about the reason for discharge if required.
* **Discharge Date**: The date of the employee's last working day.
* **Last Day of Work**: The date the employee's employment officially ended.
* **Termination Date**: The date the employee's employment was terminated (if applicable).
* **Assigned to**: The person or team the employee will be reporting to after their departure.
* **Notes**: Any other relevant details about the employee's departure.
