# Mining Industry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect job application information from candidates for job roles within the mining industry. It will be used by mining companies to evaluate potential candidates and manage job applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, providing as much relevant information as possible.
2. Ensure that you answer all the required questions and provide any supporting documentation as requested.
3. Review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Contact Information**: This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email. Please provide your current address and contact details.
* **2. Shift Work Details**: This field is used to collect information about your work schedule and availability. Please describe your work experience and availability for shift work.
* **3. Remote Sites**: If you have experience working at remote sites, please provide the names of the sites where you have worked.
* **4. Available Dates**: Please provide the dates when you are available to start work, considering your availability and any constraints you may have.
* **5. Submitting Documents**: Please upload any relevant supporting documents, such as your resume, ID, or any other documents required by the employer.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your application is considered properly.
* Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can reach us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
