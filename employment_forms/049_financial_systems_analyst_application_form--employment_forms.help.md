# Financial Systems Analyst Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for the Financial Systems Analyst position within the company. It is used to evaluate your qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already submitted a similar application for this position.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter a valid Email and Phone number in the respective fields.
4. Select your education level from the options provided in the Education Level 1 and Education Level 2 fields. You may select one or multiple options in Education Level 2.
5. Enter your experience in the Experience field.
6. List any relevant Skills you possess in the Skills field.
7. Upload a Resume that best represents your professional experience.
8. Provide References for your work experience.
9. Finally, select your agreement to the terms by choosing one of the options in the Agree field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth (date, required/optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (text, required/optional)**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
* **Education Level 1 (select_one, required/optional)**: Select one of the provided options to describe your highest level of education.
* **Education Level 2 (select_multiple, required/optional)**: Select one or multiple options that best describe your education background.
* **Experience (number, required/optional)**: Enter your experience in years or months.
* **Skills (text, required/optional)**: List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess.
* **Resume (note, required/optional)**: Attach a Resume that best represents your professional experience.
* **References (text, required/optional)**: Provide references for your work experience.
* **Agree (select_one, required/optional)**: Select one of the provided options to agree to the terms of the application.

# Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and clearly, and make sure to attach a valid resume and provide adequate references.
