# Employment Lawyer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for employment lawyers to report their employment status and legal issues.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct page from the navigation bar.
2. Fill out the relevant fields on each page.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Details

* **Applicant Details** (text, optional): Enter the applicant's details.
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): Enter the contact details of the applicant.

### Contact Details

* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter the contact number of the applicant.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the applicant.

### Employment Status

* **Employment Status** (select_one, optional): Select the employment status of the applicant.
	+ Options: Active, Inactive

### Legal Issue

* **Legal Issue** (text, optional): Enter the legal issue that the applicant is handling.

### Case Status

* **Case Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the case.
	+ Options: Active, Inactive

### Representation Preferences

* **Representation Preferences** (select_one, optional): Select whether you want to be represented by a lawyer.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Communication Channels

* **Communication Channels** (select_multiple, optional): Select the communication channels you are comfortable with.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Address

* **Address** (select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to provide your address.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### File Upload

* **File Upload** (text, optional): Upload any relevant files.

### Tips

* Please ensure that you are filling out the correct form.
* Please enter accurate and complete information.
* If you are not sure about any field, please ask for help or clarification before submitting the form.
* Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
