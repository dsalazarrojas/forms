# Unemployment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who are applying for unemployment benefits. It is used to collect information and supporting documentation required to process your unemployment claim. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your First Name, Last Name, Social Security Number, Date Of Birth, Address, Zip Code, Phone, and Email.
2. Enter your reason for applying for unemployment benefits in the "Reason For Applying" field.
3. Provide your work history, including your Job Title, Work Start Date, and Work End Date.
4. Enter the hours you worked and your gross income.
5. If applicable, select the reason for your separation from your previous job.
6. If applicable, provide additional comments about your application.
7. Upload the required documents, including Proof of Identity, Proof of Employment, Payroll, and Employment Contract.
8. Confirm the uploaded documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your residential address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your zip code as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Reason For Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, text, required): Please enter a reason for applying for unemployment benefits.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you worked per week.
* **Gross Income** (`gross_income`, number, required): Enter your gross income as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Select Reason For Separation** (`select_reason_for_separation`, select_multiple, required): Select the reason for your separation from your previous job. Options include Fired, Laid off, Voluntarily left, Other, and Reason for Separation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your application.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, required): Upload the required document to support your application.
* **Select Document Type** (`select_document_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of document uploaded. Options include Employment Contract, W2, Payroll Stub, and Employment Contract.
* **Document URL** (`document_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the document uploaded.
* **Upload Proof of Identity** (`upload_proof_of_identity`, text, required): Upload your proof of identity document.
* **Confirm Proof of Identity** (`confirm_proof_of_identity`, select_one, required): Confirm that the uploaded proof of identity document is correct.
* **Upload Proof of Employment** (`upload_proof_of_employment`, text, required): Upload your proof of employment document.
* **Confirm Proof of Employment** (`confirm_proof_of_employment`, select_one, required): Confirm that the uploaded proof of employment document is correct.
* **Upload Payroll** (`upload_payroll`, text, required): Upload your payroll document.
* **Confirm Payroll** (`confirm_payroll`, select_one, required): Confirm that the uploaded payroll document is correct.
* **Upload Employment Contract** (`upload_employment_contract`, text, required): Upload your employment contract document.
* **Confirm Employment Contract** (`confirm_employment_contract`, select_one, required): Confirm that the uploaded employment contract document is correct.
* **Confirm Document** (`confirm_document`, select_one, required): Confirm that the uploaded document is correct. Options include Confirmed, Other.
* **Upload Proof of Identity** (`upload_proof_of_identity`, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the field above. Upload your proof of identity document.
* **Confirm Proof of Identity** (`confirm_proof_of_identity`, select_one, required): This field is a duplicate of the field above. Confirm that the uploaded proof of identity document is correct.
