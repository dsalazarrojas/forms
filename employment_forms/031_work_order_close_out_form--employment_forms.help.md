# Work Order Close Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Work Order Close Out Form is used to document and finalize the completion of work orders. It gathers essential details such as contract numbers, job numbers, dates, costs, and materials used to ensure accurate record-keeping and proper financial reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Number (id: 2)** (`Contract Number`, optional) - Enter the contract number related to the work order.
* **Job Number (id: 3)** (`Job Number`, optional) - Enter the job number associated with the work order.
* **Work Order Close Out Date (id: 4)** (`Work Order Close Out Date`, optional) - Enter the date the work order was closed out.
* **Total Cost (id: 5)** (`Total Cost`, optional) - Enter the total cost associated with the work order.
* **Hours Worked (id: 6)** (`Hours Worked`, optional) - Enter the total hours worked on the project.
* **Materials Used (id: 7)** (`Materials Used`, optional) - Indicate whether materials were used on the project, selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Notes (id: 8)** (`Notes`, optional) - Add any additional comments or explanations related to the work order.
* **Assigned To (id: 9)** (`Assigned To`, optional) - Indicate whether the project is assigned to someone, selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Supervisor Review Status (id: 10)** (`Supervisor Review Status`, optional) - Select the review status of the supervisor, choosing from 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
