# Non Compete And Non Solicitation Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to have an employee confirm that they will not engage in any activities that could be considered competitive to the company, including but not limited to direct or indirect solicitation of customers or employees, or any other activities that could be considered competitive.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, make sure to review the following:

* Answer the questions accurately and honestly
* Review company policies and procedures related to non-compete and non-solicitation agreements
* Sign and date the form when completed

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Compete And Non Solicitation Agreement Form** (`1`, select_one):
  + This is the primary question to be answered. Please select "Yes" if you are not going to engage in any activities that could be considered competitive to the company, or "No" otherwise.
* **Employer** (`2`, text):
  + Please enter your employer's name as it appears on your pay stub or company ID card.
* **Non Solicitation** (`3`, select_one):
  + Please select "Yes" if you are not going to engage in direct or indirect solicitation of customers or employees, or "No" otherwise.
* **Non Compete** (`4`, select_one):
  + Please select "Yes" if you are not going to engage in any activities that could be considered competitive to the company, or "No" otherwise.
* **Confidential** (`5`, select_one):
  + Please select "Yes" if you are willing to keep company confidential information confidential, or "No" otherwise.
* **Non Solicitation Duration** (`6`, number):
  + If you select "Yes" for Non Solicitation, please indicate for how long you are agreeing to not solicit customers or employees.
* **Non Compete Duration** (`7`, number):
  + If you select "Yes" for Non Compete, please indicate for how long you are agreeing to not engage in any activities that could be considered competitive to the company.
* **Signature Date** (`8`, date):
  + Please enter the date you sign this form.
* **Employee Name** (`9`, text):
  + Please enter your name as it appears on your company ID card or pay stub.
* **Employee Title** (`10`, text):
  + Please enter your job title.
* **Company Name** (`11`, text):
  + Please enter your company's name.
* **Manager Name** (`12`, text):
  + Please enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Title** (`13`, text):
  + Please enter your manager's job title.
* **Date** (`14`, date):
  + Please enter the date this form is signed.
* **Time** (`15`, time):
  + Please enter the time this form is signed.
* **Employee ID** (`16`, number):
  + Please enter your company ID number.
* **Employee Signature** (`17`, text):
  + Please sign this form to confirm that you will not engage in any activities that could be considered competitive to the company.
* **Manager Signature** (`18`, text):
  + Please sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and discussed this non-compete and non-solicitation agreement with the employee.
* **Manager Date** (`19`, date):
  + Please enter the date this form is signed by the manager.
* **Manager Time** (`20`, time):
  + Please enter the time this form is signed by the manager.
* **Manager ID** (`21`, number):
  + Please enter the manager's ID number.

Please review the form and make sure to answer the questions accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR department.
