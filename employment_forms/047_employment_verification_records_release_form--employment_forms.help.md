# Employment Verification Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employment verification purposes, where an employee or their representative can provide records release information for their previous employers. The form is designed to gather information about an employee's employment history, including their job titles, start and end dates, and manager names for multiple employers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your employment verification records by filling in the relevant fields for each employer.
2. Select the verification reason for releasing your employment records.
3. Choose the verification date or date you would like the records to be valid from.
4. Confirm that you have agreed to the verification of your employment records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employment Verification Records Release Form
*   **Employment Verification Records Release Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Employer 1 Employer Name**: Enter the name of your first employer.
*   **Employer 1 Job Title**: Enter your job title at your first employer.
*   **Employer 1 Start Date**: Enter the start date of your employment at your first employer.
*   **Employer 1 End Date**: Enter the end date of your employment at your first employer.
*   **Employer 1 Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager at your first employer.
*   **Employer 2 Employer Name**: Enter the name of your second employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 2 Job Title**: Enter your job title at your second employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 2 Start Date**: Enter the start date of your employment at your second employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 2 End Date**: Enter the end date of your employment at your second employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 2 Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager at your second employer (if applicable).
*   **Verification Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for why you are releasing your employment verification records.
*   **Verification Date**: Enter the date you would like the records to be valid from.
*   **Employer 3 Employer Name**: Enter the name of your third employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 3 Job Title**: Enter your job title at your third employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 3 Start Date**: Enter the start date of your employment at your third employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 3 End Date**: Enter the end date of your employment at your third employer (if applicable).
*   **Employer 3 Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager at your third employer (if applicable).
*   **Agreed**: Confirm that you have agreed to the verification of your employment records.
*   **Submit**: Submit the form to complete the employment verification records release.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have permission from your current or former employers before submitting this form.
*   This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing.
*   The verification date will be used to determine the date from which the records will be valid.
