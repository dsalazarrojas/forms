# New Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal details from individuals who are interested in applying for a new job within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given or commonly used first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family or surname.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with you. This will be used as a primary contact method.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/15/1990).
