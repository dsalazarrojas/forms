# HR AI Experimentation Pilot Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR AI Experimentation Pilot Application is a form designed to gather information about your proposal for the HR AI experimentation pilot project. This form is meant to help you present your idea, explain its benefits, and provide any necessary resources for a successful implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed, following these steps:

1. In the **Pilot Proposal** field (Field 1), briefly describe your idea for the HR AI experimentation pilot project. This section should include your main objectives and goals for the pilot.
2. In the **Goals** field (Field 2), specify what you hope to achieve through this pilot project. What are the key benefits that you think the project will bring to the organization?
3. In the **Benefits** field (Field 3), elaborate on the positive outcomes and value that this project will bring to the organization and its stakeholders.
4. In the **Resource Needs** field (Field 4), list any necessary resources you need to complete the project, such as personnel, budget, or equipment.
5. Choose one of the available options in the **Submission** field (Field 5): "AI Pilot Proposal", "AI Pilot Plan", or "AI Pilot Status", depending on your project's current status.
6. In the **Name** field (Field 6), provide your name or the name of your team/department.
7. In the **Email** field (Field 7), enter your work email address or the email address of your team/department.
8. In the **Phone** field (Field 8), enter your work phone number or the phone number of your team/department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pilot Proposal** (Field 1, text, **optional**): A brief description of your idea for the HR AI experimentation pilot project.
* **Goals** (Field 2, text, **optional**): A clear statement of what you hope to achieve through this pilot project.
* **Benefits** (Field 3, text, **optional**): An explanation of the positive outcomes and value that this project will bring to the organization and its stakeholders.
* **Resource Needs** (Field 4, text, **optional**): A list of any necessary resources, such as personnel, budget, or equipment, needed to complete the project.
* **Submission** (Field 5, select_one, **optional**): Choose "AI Pilot Proposal", "AI Pilot Plan", or "AI Pilot Status" to specify your project's current status.
* **Name** (Field 6, text, **optional**): Your name or the name of your team/department.
* **Email** (Field 7, email, **optional**): Your work email address or the email address of your team/department.
* **Phone** (Field 8, text, **optional**): Your work phone number or the phone number of your team/department.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to provide the necessary information for your project.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Once you have completed this form, submit it to the relevant authorities for further review and consideration.
