# startup_team_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a startup team for the purpose of conducting an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select "How to Complete this Form" and fill in the required fields. Each field is marked as either required or optional, as indicated by the "Required" label. Please ensure that you provide accurate information for the required fields. If you are unsure about a field, please contact the relevant team member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: true): Enter the job title or role of the candidate.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the candidate's relevant work experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the candidate.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the candidate is affiliated with.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: true): Select the date the candidate started their job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required: false): Select the date the candidate ended their job.
* **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the number of months the candidate spent in the job.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required: true): Select the date of the interview.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one, required: true): Select the size of the startup team.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the startup team.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the person contacting you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: true): Enter the email address of the person contacting you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: true): Enter the phone number of the person contacting you.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the candidate.

## Tips
Please ensure that you are providing accurate information and that you are completing the form correctly to ensure a smooth interview process. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant team member for assistance.
