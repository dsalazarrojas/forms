# software_qa_tester_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for the software QA tester position. It includes fields for contact information, skills, and resume upload.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and understand each section before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate** (`candidate_info`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself and your relevant experience for the software QA tester position.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact name to be able to reach out to you for further discussion or next steps.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required/optional): List your relevant skills and certifications related to software QA testing.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required/optional): Upload your resume or CV as a PDF or text file.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Review your information and submit your application.

## Tips
* Please make sure to proofread your information carefully before submitting your application.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact email address.
