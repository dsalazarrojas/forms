# Workplace Drug Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that employees understand and acknowledge their company's workplace drug policy. It's a crucial step in maintaining a safe and compliant work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the company's workplace drug policy, as stated on page 1.
2. Select the option that best describes your understanding of the policy on page 2.
3. Confirm your acknowledgment of the policy on page 3.
4. Provide the date of your acknowledgment on page 4.
5. Sign the form to indicate your understanding and agreement.
6. If applicable, your manager will sign and provide the date of their confirmation on page 7.
7. Finally, provide the date of your manager's confirmation on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Read and understand the workplace drug policy** (page 1): Please carefully read the policy document and make sure you understand its contents.
* **I acknowledge that I have read and understand the company’s workplace drug policy** (page 2): Select the option that accurately describes your understanding of the policy.
	+ If you have read and understand the policy, choose "I have read and understand the company’s workplace drug policy."
	+ If you have not read and understand the policy, choose "I have not read and understand the company’s workplace drug policy."
* **Confirm your acknowledgment** (page 3): Select the option that confirms your previous acknowledgment.
	+ If you have read and understand the policy, choose "I confirm I have read and understand the company’s workplace drug policy."
	+ If you are unsure or have not read the policy, choose "I am unsure about the policy" or "I confirm I have not read and understand the company’s workplace drug policy."
* **Confirmation date** (page 4): If you confirm that you have read and understand the policy, enter the date on which you completed this acknowledgment.
* **Employee signature** (page 5): Sign your name to indicate your understanding and agreement with the policy.
* **Date signed** (page 6): If you sign the form, enter the date on which you signed it.
* **Manager signature** (page 7): If your manager signs the form, they confirm their acknowledgment of your understanding of the policy.
* **Manager confirmation date** (page 8): If your manager signs the form, enter the date of their confirmation. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete the form carefully and accurately.
* Review the form with your manager to ensure that both you and they understand the policy.
* Keep the completed form for future reference.
