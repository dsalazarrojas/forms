# Development Goals Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Goals Employment Form is designed to collect information about a candidate's background and skills. This form is used to assess a candidate's development goals, priorities for growth, and relevant experience or education. The data collected will be used to make informed decisions about potential employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **employment** field with your current or desired employment status.
2. Provide detailed information in the **candidate_details** field about yourself.
3. List your **development_goals** for your career or personal growth.
4. Specify your **growth_priorities**, if any.
5. Share your **candidate_comments**, if any.
6. Describe your **candidate_experience** relevant to the field you're applying for.
7. List your **candidate_education**, including degrees or certifications.
8. List any relevant **professional_certifications** you've obtained.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employment**: Describe your current or desired employment status (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **candidate_details**: Please provide a brief description about yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **development_goals**: List your goals for career growth or personal development, e.g., "Improve coding skills in Python" or "Learn data analysis."
* **growth_priorities**: Specify any areas where you'd like to focus on for growth, e.g., "Improve communication skills" or "Increase productivity."
* **candidate_comments**: Add any additional comments or information that you'd like to share with us.
* **candidate_experience**: Describe any relevant experience you have, e.g., "I've been working as a software engineer for 5 years" or "I've completed courses in machine learning."
* **candidate_education**: List your degrees or certifications relevant to the field you're applying for, e.g., "Bachelor's degree in Computer Science" or "Certified Data Analyst."
* **professional_certifications**: List any relevant certifications you've obtained, e.g., "AWS Certified Developer" or "Google Analytics Certification."
