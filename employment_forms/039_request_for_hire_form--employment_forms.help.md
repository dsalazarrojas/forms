# request_for_hire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request employment opportunities. You can use it to provide your contact information and details about the job you are seeking or offering.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Fill in your information accurately and completely.
* Choose the type of job you are applying for or offering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Description** (1, text, optional): Please provide a description of the job you are interested in or applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (2, text, optional): List any specific requirements for the job, such as experience, skills, or education.
* **Job Type** (3, select_one, required): Select the type of job you are applying for or offering. Options are:
	+ Full-time
	+ Contract
	+ Temporary
* **Contact Name** (4, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person to contact.
* **Company Email** (5, email, optional): Enter your company's email address or the email of the person to contact.
* **Job Title** (6, text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for or offering.
