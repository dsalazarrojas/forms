# Return To Work Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees and managers track and document the employee's status after returning to work after an absence. It collects information about the employee's first day back, work arrangements, and comments from supervisors and employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your first day back to work using the "first_worked_day" field.
2. Answer the first question about your status on your first day back using the "What is your status on your first day back?" field.
3. Indicate your work arrangement for the first day back by selecting one of the options in the "work_from_home_status" field.
4. Select your work arrangements by choosing one or more options in the "work_arrangements" field.
5. Enter any comments from your supervisor in the "supervisor_comments" field.
6. Enter any comments from you, the employee, in the "employee_comments", "employee_comments_2", and "employee_comments_3" fields.
7. Enter any comments from the manager in the "manager_comments" and "manager_comments_2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_worked_day (Date)** (Required/Optional):
	+ A field to select the date of your first day back to work.
* **What is your status on your first day back? (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the employee to enter their status on their first day back, such as "I'm feeling well" or "Still adjusting".
* **work_from_home_status (Select One)** (Optional):
	+ Select one of the options: "WFH", "Working from Office", or "Hybrid" to indicate your work arrangement on your first day back.
* **work_arrangements (Select Multiple)** (Optional):
	+ Select one or more options: "WFH", "Working from Office", or "Hybrid" to indicate your work arrangements.
* **supervisor_comments (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the supervisor to enter any comments.
* **employee_comments (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the employee to enter any comments about their return to work.
* **employee_comments_2 (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the employee to enter additional comments about their return to work.
* **employee_comments_3 (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the employee to enter more comments about their return to work.
* **manager_comments (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the manager to enter any comments.
* **manager_comments_2 (Text)** (Optional):
	+ A field for the manager to enter additional comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Enter as much detail as possible in the optional fields to help others understand your return to work status.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
