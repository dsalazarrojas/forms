# Employee Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about an employee from an HR or personnel department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. If applicable, enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select your department from the list.
4. Enter the date you were hired.
5. Enter your unique employee ID.
6. Enter your manager's name if you have one.
7. Enter your employee email address.
8. Enter your job phone number.
9. Enter your employee address.
10. If you have a manager, enter their phone number.
11. If you have a manager, enter their email address.
12. If available, enter the emergency contact person's name and information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your full name as an employee. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title**: If applicable, enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list to categorize your information accurately.
* **Date Hired**: Enter the date you were hired in the company.
* **Employee ID**: Your unique employee ID number.
* **Manager Name**: If you have a manager, enter their name here.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your official employee email address.
* **Job Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Employee Address**: Enter your work address.
* **Manager Phone**: If you have a manager, enter their phone number.
* **Manager Email**: If you have a manager, enter their email address.
* **Emergency Contact**: If available, enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: If available, enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Phone**: If available, enter your emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your official employment documents for confirmation.
* Double-check your manager's and emergency contact's information if you have them listed.
