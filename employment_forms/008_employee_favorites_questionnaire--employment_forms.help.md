# Employee Favorites Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's personal preferences and interests. It is meant to be a fun and engaging way to get to know employees better and help them bond with their colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the "employee_favorites_questionnaire" page, and start filling out the fields. Each field has a label and a corresponding description to guide you through the process. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and don't worry about spelling or grammar - the goal is to have fun and be creative!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are some favorite things? (employee_favorites_questionnaire)**: This is an open-ended field where you can share your favorite hobbies, interests, or activities. Feel free to get creative and be as detailed as you'd like.
* **Food item 1 (favorite_item_1)**: Please share the name of one of your favorite food items.
* **Food item 2 (favorite_item_2)**: Share the name of another favorite food item. Don't worry if you can't think of two - just share one and we'll take care of the rest!
* **What do you like most? (favorite_food_category)**: This is where you can share the type of food that you like most (e.g., Italian, Mexican, Indian, etc.).
* **Hobbies 1 (favorite_hobby_1)**: Please share one of your favorite hobbies or activities you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **Hobbies 2 (favorite_hobby_2)**: Share another favorite hobby or activity. Again, feel free to get creative!
* **Hobbies 3 (favorite_hobby_3)**: Share yet another favorite hobby or activity.
* **Favorite travel destination (favorite_travel_destination)**: Share the name of a travel destination that you've been to or would love to visit.
* **Favorite activity 1 (favorite_activity_1)**: Please share the name of one of your favorite activities or things you enjoy doing.
* **Favorite activity 2 (favorite_activity_2)**: Share another favorite activity or thing you enjoy doing.
* **Favorite music artist (favorite_music_artist)**: Share the name of your favorite music artist or band.
* **Favorite movie (favorite_movie)**: Share the name of your favorite movie.
* **Favorite book (favorite_book)**: Share the name of your favorite book.
* **Favorite food (favorite_food)**: Share the name of your favorite food item.
* **Do you like it? (do_you_like_it)**: Please answer with a "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you like the previous favorite item.

Note: All answers will be kept confidential and used for internal purposes only.
