# Digital Optimization Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Optimization Internship Application Form is designed for individuals interested in gaining practical experience in digital optimization. By filling out this form, you'll be providing us with the necessary information to evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Resume** fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your relevant **Skills** from the provided options (Web Development, Data Science, Marketing, or Design).
3. If applicable, provide a brief description of your **Experience** in the field.
4. Add any relevant **Education** details.
5. Share your online **Portfolio** if applicable.
6. Specify your **Availability** for the internship.
7. Answer the **Why Internship** question honestly, explaining why you're interested in this opportunity.
8. Choose your expected **Expected Start Date**, **Expected End Date**, and **Expected Duration** for the internship.
9. Select your expected **Expected Work Hours** for the internship.
10. If applicable, provide an estimate of your expected **Expected Salary**.
11. For **Reference 1**, **Reference 2**, and **Reference 3**, provide professional references who can vouch for your skills and experience.
12. Finally, review your answers and make sure everything is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Your professional email address for communication.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF or DOC format for us to review.
* **Skills**: Select all relevant skills you have experience with (Web Development, Data Science, Marketing, or Design).
* **Experience**: Briefly describe any relevant work or internship experience you have.
* **Education**: Provide any relevant educational background.
* **Portfolio**: Share a link to your online portfolio or a brief description of your work.
* **Availability**: Specify your availability for the internship, including the days and hours you can work.
* **Why Internship**: Explain why you're interested in this internship opportunity and how it aligns with your goals.
* **Expected Start Date** and **Expected End Date**: Choose the start and end dates for your internship.
* **Expected Duration**: Specify the expected length of your internship.
* **Expected Work Hours**: Select the hours you plan to work per week.
* **Expected Salary**: Estimate your expected salary for the internship.
* **Reference 1**, **Reference 2**, and **Reference 3**: Provide professional references who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Availability Days**: Specify any specific days you are unavailable for work.
* **Confirm**: Double-check your application before submitting.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your completed application form.
