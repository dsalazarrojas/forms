## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your application is considered.
*   Review each section thoroughly before moving on to the next one.
*   Make sure to answer all required fields.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information** (`form_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief introduction about yourself and your qualifications.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Select one way you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Both).
*   **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience in the pharmaceutical lab field.
*   **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of education (Bachelor's degree or higher, Associate's degree or higher, or Some college).
*   **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked as a pharmaceutical lab technician.
*   **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant certifications you have (OSHA certification or Other).
*   **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Indicate when you are available to start work (no specific format provided, but enter your availability in the correct format).
*   **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you have for the job.
*   **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide names and contact information for your professional references.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your desired job title.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to work.
*   **Work Shift** (`work_shift`, select_one, required): Select the type of shifts you are available for (Monday to Friday, Every day, or Rotating shifts).
*   **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, select_multiple, required): Select the length of shifts you are available for (8-12 hours, 13-16 hours, 17-20 hours, or Other).
*   **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, optional): Enter your desired salary range.
*   **Salary Frequency** (`salary_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of your pay (not applicable in this form).
*   **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status (Full time, Part time, or Other).
*   **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, optional): Enter your employment type.
*   **Availability Start Date** (`availability_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start work.
*   **Availability End Date** (`availability_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to end work.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job you are looking for (Clinical, Lab, or Other).
*   **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to work.
*   **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your desired job title.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your desired job.
*   **Certifications 2** (`certifications_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional relevant certifications.
*   **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): If you have a resume, you can upload it for the hiring manager's review.
