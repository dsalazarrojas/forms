# Simple Time Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Simple Time Sheet Form is designed for employees to record their work hours and other relevant information for time-tracking or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your Employee ID.
2. Select the Job ID related to your work activities.
3. Choose the Job Type corresponding to your activities (e.g., Job 1, Job 2, or Job 3).
4. Enter your Email address for record-keeping purposes.
5. Optionally, enter any additional comments about your work tasks or activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (employee_id, 0): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Job ID** (job_id, 0): Select the Job ID related to your work activities.
* **Date** (date, 0): Enter the date for which you are recording your work hours.
* **In Work Hours** (in_work_hours, 0): Enter the number of hours you worked on the selected Job ID.
* **Out Work Hours** (out_work_hours, 0): Enter the number of hours you were not working on the selected Job ID.
* **Job Type** (job_type, 0): Choose the Job Type corresponding to your activities (e.g., Job 1, Job 2, or Job 3).
* **Task ID** (task_id, 0): Optionally, enter the Task ID related to your activities.
* **Hours** (hours, 0): Enter the total hours you worked on the Task ID (if provided).
* **Comments** (comments, 0): Add any additional comments or notes about your work activities.
* **Email** (email, 0): Enter your work Email address for record-keeping purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, 0): (Optional) Enter your work Phone number.
