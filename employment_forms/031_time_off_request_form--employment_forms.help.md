# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is used to gather information from employees about their time off requests, including employee details, dates and duration of absence, comments, and contact information. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected, making it easier for HR and management to process and respond to these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your Employee Information, providing your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the start date of your absence.
3. Enter the end date of your absence.
4. Specify the time interval you will be away (e.g., hours, days).
5. Enter the duration of your absence (e.g., number of days).
6. Provide any additional comments or information about your absence.
7. Enter your contact number.
8. Enter your contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required: false)
Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us identify you.

### 2. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false)
Specify the start date of your absence.

### 3. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the end date of your absence.

### 4. **Time Interval** (`time_interval`, number, required: false)
Specify the time interval you will be away (e.g., hours, days).

### 5. **Duration** (`duration`, number, required: false)
Enter the duration of your absence (e.g., number of days).

### 6. **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or information about your absence.

### 7. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false)
Enter a contact number to help us reach you during your absence.

### 8. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter a contact email to help us reach you during your absence.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for your request to be processed correctly.
- If any field is unclear, please ask your HR or supervisor for clarification.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors or delays in processing.
- Be prepared to provide more information or clarification if needed during the processing of your request.
