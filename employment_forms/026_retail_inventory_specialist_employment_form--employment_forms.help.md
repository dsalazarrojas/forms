# Retail Inventory Specialist Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from candidates who are interested in working as a retail inventory specialist. The gathered data will help our team assess their suitability for the position and process their application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the candidate's contact information (fields 1-4).
2. Provide employment details, such as job type, job location, and job department (fields 5-8).
3. Share your work experience, education, and any relevant certifications (fields 9-12).
4. Upload your resume and any additional documents (fields 13-16).
5. Confirm your contact information and sign the form (fields 17-20).
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete your application (field 21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`title`, `text`, not required): This section gathers basic contact information such as name, address, and other details.
* **Job Information** (`title`, `text`, not required): Provide details about the job you are applying for, including job type, location, and department.
* **Contact Information** (`title`, `text`, not required): Share your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Employment Information** (`title`, `text`, not required): Provide details about your previous employment, including job titles and dates.
* **Work Experience** (`title`, `text`, not required): Describe your relevant work experience, including job roles and job responsibilities.
* **Education** (`title`, `text`, not required): Share information about your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Certifications** (`title`, `text`, not required): List any relevant certifications or licenses you possess.
* **Skills** (`title`, `text`, not required): Share any relevant skills you have, including proficiency levels.
* **Upload Resume** (`title`, `text`, not required): Attach your resume or CV as a PDF or image file.
* **Upload Additional Docs** (`title`, `text`, not required): Upload any additional documents that support your application.
* **Upload Signature** (`title`, `text`, not required): Upload a scanned copy of your signature.
* **Job Type** (`title`, `text`, not required): Specify the type of job you are applying for.
* **Job Location** (`title`, `text`, not required): Indicate the location where you are willing to work.
* **Job Position** (`title`, `text`, not required): Specify the job position you are applying for.
* **Job Department** (`title`, `text`, not required): Identify the department where you would be working.
* **Job Category** (`title`, `text`, not required): Specify the category of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Status** (`title`, `text`, not required): Indicate your current work status, such as full-time or part-time.
* **Employment Type** (`title`, `text`, not required): Specify your employment type, such as permanent or temporary.
* **Job Title** (`title`, `text`, not required): Specify the job title you are applying for.
* **Contact Phone** (`title`, `text`, not required): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`title`, `email`, not required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Submit** (`title`, `text`, not required): Click the "Submit" button to complete your application.

Note: This form is designed to be completed online, so please ensure that you have a stable internet connection while filling it out. If you encounter any issues or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
