# Sales Associate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in becoming sales associates. The form is designed to gather details about the applicant's job title, job description, job status, name, email, phone number, resume, skills, previous companies, available shifts, salary range, available start date, work hours, work experience, education, education level, available days off per week, available days off per month, references, availability, available languages, emergency contact name, emergency contact relationship, and emergency contact phone number.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your job title and job description.
3. Select the correct job status from the available options.
4. Enter your name and email address.
5. Provide a phone number where you can be reached.
6. Attach your resume to demonstrate your skills and experience.
7. Select the skills you possess that are relevant to the sales associate role.
8. List your previous companies and a brief description of your work experience.
9. Choose the shifts you are available to work.
10. Enter your desired salary range.
11. Select your available start date.
12. Enter your work hours.
13. Describe your work experience in the space provided.
14. Provide information about your education and education level.
15. Enter the number of days you are available to take off per week and per month.
16. Provide the names and contact information of your references.
17. Select your availability (flexible or fixed).
18. Choose the languages you are proficient in.
19. Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
20. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): This field is used to describe your job title as it is currently or will be in the future.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your job responsibilities and role.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the current status of your job.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach your resume to demonstrate your skills and experience.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select the skills you possess that are relevant to the sales associate role.
* **Previous Companies** (`previous_companies`, text, optional): List your previous companies you have worked for.
* **Available Shifts** (`available_shifts`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shifts you are available to work.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, optional): Enter your desired salary range.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, optional): Select your available start date.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, note, optional): Describe your work experience in the space provided.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Provide information about your education.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your education level.
* **Available Days Off Per Week** (`available_days_off_weekly`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you are available to take off per week.
* **Available Days Off Per Month** (`available_days_off_monthly`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you are available to take off per month.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of your references.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select your availability (flexible or fixed).
* **Available Languages** (`available_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you are proficient in.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Select the relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
