# Corporate Manager Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Manager Employment Form is designed to collect and record essential details of corporate managers. This form aims to provide an accurate and comprehensive overview of the manager's employment, making it easier for the company to manage employee data and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by navigating to the first page, "Employee Details".
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the manager.
3. Move to the next page, "Contact Details", and fill in the relevant information for communication purposes.
4. Proceed to "Onboarding Details" and provide any necessary information regarding the manager's onboarding process.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Fill in the employee's basic details such as name, employee ID, job title, and department.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the employee's contact information, including email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact preferences.
- **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, text, optional): Provide any necessary information regarding the manager's onboarding process, such as dates, times, and locations.
- **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or remarks related to the submission.
