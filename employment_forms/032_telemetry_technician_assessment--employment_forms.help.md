# Telemetry Technician Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants about their experience, skills, and qualifications for a Telemetry Technician position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your general information, including your name and contact details.
3. Select your relevant work experience, technical skills, and communication skills.
4. Indicate your shift readiness and any emergency procedures you are trained in.
5. Check your certification status and select the correct option.
6. Describe your work environment and references.
7. Provide any additional education details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name, address, and contact details. This information will be used to contact you after the assessment.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the level of your experience as a Telemetry Technician. Choose from the options: Telemetry Technician (1-3 years), Telemetry Technician (4-6 years), Telemetry Technician (7-10 years).
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your programming skills, experience with database systems, and experience with network protocols such as TCP/IP and HTTP.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `note`, required): Describe your communication skills, including language proficiency and any other relevant skills.
* **Shift Readiness** (`shift_readiness`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your availability to work morning shifts.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, `time`, required): Select the emergency procedures you are trained in.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are certified in Basic Life Support.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, `note`, required): Describe your work environment, including any relevant work settings or circumstances.
* **References** (`references`, `note`, required): Provide any relevant references, including name, title, and contact details.
* **Education** (`education`, `note`, required): Describe any additional education, including degrees or certifications.
* **Shift Readiness 2** (`shift_readiness_2`, `note`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Emergency Procedure 2** (`emergency_procedure_2`, `time`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your relevant work experience if not already selected.
* **Technical Skills 2** (`technical_skills_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your technical skills if not already selected.
* **Communication Skills 2** (`communication_skills_2`, `note`, optional): Describe any additional communication skills if not already described.
* **Certification 2** (`certification_2`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you have any other certifications if not already selected.
* **Emergency Procedure 3** (`emergency_procedure_3`, `time`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Work Environment 2** (`work_environment_2`, `note`, optional): Describe your work environment if not already described.
* **References 2** (`references_2`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional references if not already provided.
* **Education 2** (`education_2`, `note`, optional): Describe any additional education if not already described.
* **Shift Readiness 3** (`shift_readiness_3`, `note`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Emergency Procedure 4** (`emergency_procedure_4`, `time`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
