# employee_offboarding_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The employee_offboarding_records_release_form is used to document the offboarding process for an employee. This process typically involves a series of steps that must be completed in order to ensure a smooth transition when an employee leaves the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the instructions and guidelines above to ensure you are aware of the form's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the offboarding process owner or manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offboarding Process** (`offboarding_process`, text, optional): Please describe the steps that have been taken to offboard the employee. This can include any relevant details or events that occurred during the process.
* **Offboarding Date** (`offboarding_date`, date, optional): Enter the date that the employee's offboarding process began or is expected to begin.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Please select 'True' if the manager has approved the offboarding process, or 'False' if the manager has not yet approved it.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback from the employee regarding their offboarding process, please enter them here.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee is expected to leave the company.
* **Company Resources** (`company_resources`, text, optional): Describe any company resources that the employee is expected to return or repatriate, such as a laptop or company credit cards.
* **Exit Interview** (`exit_interview`, text, optional): Please describe any exit interview that was conducted with the employee during the offboarding process.
