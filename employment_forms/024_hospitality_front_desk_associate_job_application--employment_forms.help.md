# Hospitality Front Desk Associate Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in applying for a front desk associate position in a hospitality setting. It collects basic contact information, job title, and work schedule preferences to help the hiring team assess the applicant's qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your address.
6. Select your job title from the available options (Front Desk Associate, Housekeeping, Maintenance, Kitchen Staff, Front Office, Concierge, or Other).
7. Choose your availability for the role, including your preferred shift (Daytime, Nighttime, Weekdays, or Weekend).
8. Select your available shifts and time slots.

* **First name** (`main_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your job title from the available options.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required: false): Choose your availability for the role.
* **Shift** (`shift`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred shift (Daytime, Nighttime, Weekdays, or Weekend).
* **Availability 2/3** (`availability_2/3`, `time`, required: false): Choose specific time slots for your availability.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is valid and can be used for contact purposes.
* Enter your availability and shift preferences accurately, as this will help the hiring team assess your suitability for the role.
