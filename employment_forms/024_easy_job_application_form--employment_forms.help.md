# Easy Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from job applicants to facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the personal information fields.
  - Enter your personal details in the **Personal information** section.
  - This includes filling out the **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip**, and **Country** fields.
  - Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** numbers.
- Provide information about your employment status and job aspirations.
  - Select your current **Current Employment Status** from the options provided (Unemployed, Employed, Self-Employed).
  - Choose your **Desired Position** from the list (Accountant, Software Developer, Data Analyst, Marketing Manager, Sales Representative, HR Manager, Operations Manager).
- Add your professional details.
  - If applicable, upload your **Resume** to demonstrate your experience and qualifications.
  - If you have additional **Other Documents** to share, you can upload them here.
- Specify your availability and salary expectations.
  - Enter the **Available Start Date**, which should be the date you're available to begin work.
  - Enter your **Salary Expectation**, which should be your expected salary for the position.
  - Select the **Salary Currency** (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.) and enter the corresponding **Currency Code**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Personal information** (Personal Information)
  * **Address**: Enter your home address.
  * **City**: Enter your city.
  * **State**: Enter your state or region.
  * **Zip**: Enter your zip or postal code.
  * **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
  * **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
  * **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.

- **Current Employment Status**
  * **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (Unemployed, Employed, Self-Employed).

- **Desired Position**
  * **Desired Position**: Choose your desired job title (Accountant, Software Developer, Data Analyst, Marketing Manager, Sales Representative, HR Manager, Operations Manager).

- **Available Start Date**
  * **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to begin work.

- **Resume Upload**
  * **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume to share with the hiring team.

- **Other Documents**
  * **Other Documents**: Upload any other relevant documents.

- **Contact Phone**
  * **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.

- **Employed Since (Years)**
  * **Employed Since (Years)**: Enter the number of years employed.

- **Notice Period**
  * **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days' notice required to leave your current job.

- **Notice Period Unit**
  * **Notice Period Unit**: Select the unit for your notice period (days, weeks, months).

- **Notice Period 2**
  * **Notice Period 2**: This field seems to be a duplicate of **Notice Period**; if so, it can be skipped.

- **Salary Expectation**
  * **Salary Expectation**: Enter your expected salary for the position.

- **Salary Currency**
  * **Salary Currency**: Enter the currency of your expected salary (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).

- **Currency Code**
  * **Currency Code**: Enter the code for your expected salary currency.

- **Employment Status Since (Years)**
  * **Employment Status Since (Years)**: Enter the number of years since you've been employed.

- **Job Title**
  * **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title.
