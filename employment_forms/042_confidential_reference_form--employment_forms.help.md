# Confidential Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an employee or candidate from a person who can vouch for their work performance and abilities. It's essential to complete this form accurately and honestly, as it will be used to assess the candidate's qualifications and suitability for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure you have the necessary information about the candidate and your relationship with them. Answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or seek additional information from the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`reference_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your professional contact information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Relationship to Candidate** (`relationship_to_candidate`, select_one, required): Describe how you know the candidate, such as "Former Supervisor", "Current Supervisor", "Colleague", etc.
* **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the candidate you are referencing.
* **Candidate Position** (`candidate_position`, text, required): Enter the job title or position of the candidate.
* **Employment Dates** (`employment_dates`, text, required): Describe the dates during which you worked with or had a relationship with the candidate.
* **Type of Reference** (`reference_type`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of this reference (e.g., Employment, Promotion, Academic Program, etc.).
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's overall performance as "Outstanding", "Exceeds Expectations", "Meets Expectations", etc.
* **Quality of Work** (`work_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's work quality as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Reliability** (`reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's reliability as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's communication skills as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's ability to work in a team as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership`, select_one, optional): Rate the candidate's leadership skills as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc. (optional if not applicable).
* **Problem-Solving Ability** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's analytical skills as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's self-starter ability as "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", etc.
* **Areas of Strength** (`areas_of_strength`, text, required): Describe the areas where the candidate excels.
* **Areas for Development** (`areas_for_development`, text, optional): Describe areas where the candidate could improve (optional).
* **Major Accomplishments** (`major_accomplishments`, text, optional): List key achievements and accomplishments of the candidate (optional).
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the candidate's departure (if known, optional).
* **Would You Rehire** (`rehire_eligible`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would rehire the candidate (yes or no).
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend the candidate for this position (yes or no).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the candidate.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (`confidentiality_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that this reference is confidential.
* **Reference Confirmation** (`reference_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.

## Tips
Please review all fields carefully and ensure accurate information. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or seek additional information from the candidate. This form is essential for providing a comprehensive and honest assessment of the candidate's qualifications and suitability for a position.
