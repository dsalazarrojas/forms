## University Arts Faculty Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in joining the faculty at a university. It collects information about your qualifications, experience, and preferences to help the hiring committee assess your suitability for a position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your name**: In the "name" field, enter your full name as it should be displayed in the university's records.
2. **Enter your contact information**: In the "contact-info" field, enter your email address so that the hiring committee can get in touch with you.
3. **Choose your position**: Select the position you are interested in applying for from the options provided (Lecturer, Professor, or Adjunct).
4. **Select your department**: Choose the department you are interested in joining from the options provided (Fine Arts, Music, or Drama).
5. **Provide your date of birth**: If you want to be considered for a position, you can enter your date of birth in the "date-of-birth" field. This information will help the hiring committee understand your age and experience.
6. **Enter your availability**: If you have specific time commitments or preferences, you can enter them in the "time" field.
7. **Attach your resume**: In the "resume" field, attach a copy of your resume, which should include your qualifications, experience, and any relevant certifications.
8. **Write a cover letter**: In the "cover-letter" field, write a brief introduction explaining why you are interested in the position and how your skills and experience make you a strong candidate.
9. **List your references**: In the "references" field, list the names and contact information of at least two academic or professional references who can vouch for your skills and experience.
10. **Provide your academic background**: If you have an academic background in a relevant field, you can enter it in the "academic-background" field.
11. **Enter your education**: If you have a degree in a relevant field, you can select it from the options provided (Bachelors, Masters, or PhD).
12. **Enter any other information**: If you have any other relevant information you'd like to share, you can enter it in the "other-info" field.
