# IT Sector Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information for employment opportunities in the IT sector. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to attract the right candidates for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide a valid Email address.
4. Upload your Resume.
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Specify your Company.
7. Select your level of experience (Intern, Junior, Mid-Level, Senior, or Executive).
8. Enter your Location.
9. Choose your relevant skills (Programming, Data Science, Web Development, or Cloud Computing).
10. If applicable, select your Availability (date).
11. Choose your Work Arrangement (Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance).
12. Enter your desired Salary Range.
13. Select the unit of your Notice Period (Weeks, Months, or Years).
14. Choose your Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship).
15. Specify your Job Status (Open, Closed, or Archived).
16. Choose your Job Category (IT, Engineering, Sales, or Marketing).
17. Enter any Job Tags.
18. Write a detailed Job Description.
19. List the Job Requirements.
20. Specify any Job Qualifications.
21. Write any Job References.
22. Enter your Job Application.
23. Enter any Job Contact Information.
24. Add any Job Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your Resume as a PDF or Word document.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of experience:
  + Intern
  + Junior
  + Mid-Level
  + Senior
  + Executive
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your current location.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your relevant skills:
  + Programming
  + Data Science
  + Web Development
  + Cloud Computing
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): If applicable, select your availability.
* **Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, select_one, required): Choose your work arrangement:
  + Full Time
  + Part Time
  + Freelance
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, text, required): Enter your desired salary range.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, optional): Enter your notice period.
* **Notice Period Unit** (`notice_period_unit`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of your notice period:
  + Weeks
  + Months
  + Years
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Choose your job type:
  + Full-time
  + Part-time
  + Contract
  + Internship
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose your job status:
  + Open
  + Closed
  + Archived
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your job category:
  + IT
  + Engineering
  + Sales
  + Marketing
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, text, optional): Enter any job tags.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Write a detailed job description.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): List the job requirements.
* **Job Qualifications** (`job_qualifications`, text, optional): Specify any job qualifications.
* **Job References** (`job_references`, text, optional): Write any job references.
* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, optional): Enter your job application.
* **Job Contact** (`job_contact`, text, optional): Enter any job contact information.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, text, optional): Add any job notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the correct format for uploading your Resume (PDF or Word document).
* Be specific when selecting your experience, job type, and job status.
* Use relevant job tags to attract the right candidates.
* Write a detailed job description and requirements.
* Specify any job qualifications and references.
* Enter accurate contact information.
* Add any job notes.
