# sales_operations_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales operations manager application form is used for candidates to apply for sales operations manager positions in our company. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant information to increase your chances of getting hired.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic candidate information: Enter your name, contact details, and education background to get started.
2. Share your employment history: Describe your previous work experience, job descriptions, and job requirements.
3. Explain why you're applying: Write a brief explanation of why you're interested in this role and why you're the best fit.
4. Share your work experience: Answer "Yes" or "No" to questions about your work experience, employment status, and availability.
5. Attach your resume: Upload your resume to showcase your qualifications and experience.
6. Share references: Attach references from previous employers or colleagues, if applicable.
7. Provide additional information: Use the "Additional Info" field to share any relevant details that didn't fit into the previous fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, `text`, Required/Optional): Provide your basic contact details, including your name, address, and phone number.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter your contact email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
- **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, Required/Optional): Describe your previous work experience, job descriptions, and job requirements.
- **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, Required/Optional): Share your educational background, including degree earned and institution attended.
- **References** (`references`, `text`, Required/Optional): List the names and contact details of your previous employers or colleagues who can vouch for your skills and experience.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Choose your current employment status (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
- **Employed** (`employment_status_0`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you're currently employed.
- **Self-Employed** (`employment_status_1`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you're self-employed.
- **Retired** (`employment_status_2`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you're retired.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, Required/Optional): Describe your job description, job requirements, and responsibilities.
- **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, Required/Optional): Explain the job requirements and responsibilities you're looking for.
- **Why Are You Applying** (`why_are_you_applying`, `text`, Required/Optional): Write a brief explanation of why you're interested in this role and why you're the best fit.
- **Why Our Company** (`why_our_company`, `text`, Required/Optional): Explain why you're interested in our company and what makes you the best fit.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter your availability date.
- **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `time`, Required/Optional): Enter your work hours (if applicable).
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to questions about your work experience.
- **Less than 2 years** (`work_experience_0`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have less than 2 years of work experience.
- **2-5 years** (`work_experience_1`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have 2-5 years of work experience.
- **More than 5 years** (`work_experience_2`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have more than 5 years of work experience.
- **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, Required/Optional): Attach your resume to showcase your qualifications and experience.
- **Resume Attachment** (`resume_attachment`, `note`, Required/Optional): Attach your resume as a note (if not possible to upload).
- **References** (`references`, `text`, Required/Optional): List the names and contact details of your previous employers or colleagues who can vouch for your skills and experience.
- **References Attachment** (`references_attachment`, `note`, Required/Optional): Attach references as a note (if not possible to upload).
- **Certification Attachment** (`certification_attachment`, `note`, Required/Optional): Attach certification as a note (if not possible to upload).
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, Required/Optional): Use this field to share any relevant details that didn't fit into the previous fields.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Attach relevant documents (e.g., resume, references, certification) to support your application.
- Review and proofread your form before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our HR department for assistance.
