# Research Grant Writer Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "research_grant_writer_employment_application" form is used to collect information from writers applying for research grants. This form helps to capture essential details about the applicant's employment status, contact information, and professional background, making it easier for evaluators to assess their eligibility and suitability for the research grant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the application form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
3. Select the correct employment status from the provided options.
4. Provide valid contact information, including your email and phone number.
5. Describe your professional background, including work experience and education.
6. Attach any relevant supporting documents, such as a resume or other attachments.
7. Review your submission before submitting the form for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted regarding the research grant.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further inquiry.

### Address
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Provide your mailing address if you have a specific address that needs to be associated with your application.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`Experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant work experience or research background you have.

### Education
* **Education** (`Education`, text, optional): Mention any relevant educational qualifications or certifications you possess.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text, optional): List any specialized skills or areas of expertise relevant to the research grant application.

### Attachments
* **Attachments** (`Attachments`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents, such as a resume or supporting letters, to your application.

### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, select_one, required): Select the current employment status from the provided options.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): If employed, enter your current job title.

### Work History
* **Work History** (`Work History`, text, optional): Describe your work history, including any previous positions held.

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, optional): Attach your resume for further evaluation.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Attach supporting documents where necessary.
* Double-check your submission for errors before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or clarification, contact the relevant authorities for further guidance.
