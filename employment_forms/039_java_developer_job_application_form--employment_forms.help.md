# Java Developer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to apply for a Java Developer position. The form is intended to gather information about the applicant's contact details, education, job interests, skills, job experiences, and job expectations. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Contact Information**: Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Resume**: Attach your resume for our review.
3. **Education**: Describe your educational background and relevant courses.
4. **Job Interests**: Select all the job interests that you have.
5. **Job Experiences**: Write about your previous job experiences.
6. **Job History**: Write about your job history.
7. **Skills**: Select all the skills you possess.
8. **Job Preferences**: Describe your job preferences.
9. **Personal Info**: Select all the personal info you wish to share.
10. **Job Expectations**: Describe your job expectations.
11. **Job Status**: Describe your job status.
12. **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment you are looking for (Software Developer, Consultant, Freelancer, Part Time, Full Time).
13. **Job Title**: Provide your job title.
14. **Job Location**: Provide your job location.
15. **Job Description**: Describe your job description.
16. **Job Hours**: Provide your job hours.
17. **Job Start Date**: Provide your job start date.
18. **Job End Date**: Provide your job end date.
19. **Job Duration**: Provide the duration of your job.
20. **Job Rate**: Provide your job rate.
21. **Contact Name**: Provide your contact name.
22. **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email.
23. **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
24. **Contact Address**: Provide your contact address.
25. **Job Status Description**: Provide a description of your job status.
