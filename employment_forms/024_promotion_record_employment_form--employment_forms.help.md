# promotion_record_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document promotions within an organization, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the promotion process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's identifier in the "Employee Identifier" field.
2. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. Enter the new position the employee will be promoted to in the "New Position" field.
4. Select the "Date of Effective Date" for the promotion.
5. Provide a reason for the promotion in the "Reason for Promotion" field.
6. Select the manager's approval status in the "Manager Approval" field.
7. Select the employee's confirmation in the "Employee Confirmation" field.
8. Select the manager's confirmation in the "Manager Confirmation" field.
9. Enter the "Effective Date Approved" and "Date of Confirmation" fields.
10. Select the "Promotion Date" field.
11. Enter the "Pay Rate Change" field.
12. (Optional) Provide a reason for termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
13. Select the manager's termination reason in the "Manager Termination Reason" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the "Date of Termination" field.
15. Select the termination reason in the "Termination Reason" field.
16. Select the termination status in the "Termination Status" field.
17. Enter the pay increase or decrease in the "Pay Increase" or "Pay Decrease" fields.
18. Enter the pay rate in the "Pay Rate" field.
19. Enter the reason for promotion in the "Reason for Promotion" field.
20. Select the manager's approval status in the "Manager Approval Status" field.
21. Select the employee's acknowledgement in the "Employee Acknowledgement" field.
22. Select the manager's acknowledgement in the "Manager Acknowledgement" field.
23. Select the promotion status in the "Promotion Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Identifier** (`employee_identifier`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's identifier, typically an employee ID or badge number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the manager who approved the promotion.
* **New Position** (`new_position`, `text`, required): Enter the new position the employee will be promoted to.
* **Date of Effective Date** (`date_of_effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the promotion will take effect.
* **Reason for Promotion** (`reason_for_promotion`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief reason for the promotion.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, required): Select the manager's approval status.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the employee's confirmation options.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the manager's confirmation options.
* **Effective Date Approved** (`effective_date_approved`, `date`, required): Enter the date the promotion was approved.
* **Date of Confirmation** (`date_of_confirmation`, `date`, required): Enter the date the promotion was confirmed.
* **Promotion Date** (`promotion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the promotion was made official.
* **Pay Rate Change** (`pay_rate_change`, `number`, required): Enter the pay rate change amount.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Provide a reason for termination.
* **Manager Termination Reason** (`manager_termination_reason`, `select_one`, optional): (Optional) Select the manager's termination reason.
* **Date of Termination** (`date_of_termination`, `date`, optional): (Optional) Enter the date of termination.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `select_multiple`, optional): (Optional) Select the termination reason options.
* **Termination Status** (`termination_status`, `select_one`, optional): (Optional) Select the termination status.
* **Pay Increase** (`pay_increase`, `number`, required): Enter the pay increase amount.
* **Pay Decrease** (`pay_decrease`, `number`, required): Enter the pay decrease amount.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the new pay rate.
* **Reason for Promotion** (`reason_for_promotion`, `text`, required): Enter a brief reason for the promotion.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): (Optional) Select the manager's approval status.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the employee's acknowledgement options.
* **Manager Acknowledgement** (`manager_acknowledgement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the manager's acknowledgement options.
* **Promotion Status** (`promotion_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the promotion status.
