# Employment Status Clarification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Employment Status Clarification Request Form" is a tool used by HR teams to gather information from employees regarding their employment status, ensuring that data is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible, including providing your employment status and reason for any changes.
2. If you are currently an employee, provide your manager's email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. If you are the manager of the employee, provide your email address and phone number for communication regarding the employee's employment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional)
Provide your name as an employee.

### **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional)
If you are an employee, provide your manager's email address for contact purposes. If you are the manager, provide your own email address.

### **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional)
If you are an employee, provide your manager's phone number for contact purposes. If you are the manager, provide your own phone number.

### **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the following employment status options:
	* Active: Currently employed and actively working.
	* Inactive: Currently not actively working due to leave or other reasons.
	* Terminated: Employment has been terminated due to reasons such as resignation or termination.
	* Other: Select this option if your employment status is not listed above.

### **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional)
Provide a brief explanation for any changes to your employment status.

### **Date of Change** (`date_of_change`, date, optional)
If your employment status has changed, provide the date of the change.

### **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, optional)
If you are the manager, provide any additional notes or comments regarding the employee's employment status.

### **HR Note** (`hr_note`, note, optional)
If you are an HR representative, provide any additional notes or comments regarding the employee's employment status.

### Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that data is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation above or contact your HR team for clarification.
