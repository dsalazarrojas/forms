# Marketing Creative Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Creative Employment Form is designed to collect information about applicants for marketing creative positions. This form helps the hiring team to gather essential details about the applicant, including their name, contact information, work experience, and other relevant background information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Your Name**: Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Enter Your Email**: Provide your email address that you use most frequently.
3. **Enter Your Contact Number**: This is your mobile number where you can be reached for any communication.
4. **Share Your Work Experience**: Select the date you started and ended your previous work, e.g., "Jan 2019 - Jun 2022".
5. **Upload Your Portfolio**: If you have a link to your portfolio, please provide it in the "Portfolio" field. If not, please select "Yes" to indicate that you have a portfolio.
6. **Check Your Availability**: Let us know if you are available to work with us.
7. **Upload Your Resume**: If you have a resume, please upload it as a PDF or Word document.
8. **Provide References**: If you have any professional references, please provide them here.
9. **Add Any Additional Note**: If you have any other information that you think is relevant to your application, please provide it here.

- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
