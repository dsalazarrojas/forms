# Rehire Incentive Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for rehire incentive applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the department from the dropdown list in the "Department" field.
4. Enter the date of hire in the "Date of Hire" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. If applicable, enter the rehire date in the "Rehire Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter a brief reason for rehire in the "Reason for Rehire" field.
7. Enter the email of the employee in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the employee in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the submission date in the "Submission Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
10. Select who submitted the application from the dropdown list in the "Submitted By" field.
11. Select who this application was submitted to from the dropdown list in the "Submitted To" field.
12. If applicable, select who approved the application in the "Approved By" field.
13. Enter the incentive amount in the "Rehire Incentive Amount" field.
14. Select the currency of the incentive payment from the dropdown list in the "Rehire Incentive Currency" field.
15. If applicable, enter any additional notes in the "Rehire Incentive Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee, as per their official records.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job the employee will be rehired into.
* **Job Code**: This is an optional field for entering a job code related to the job the employee will be reemployed in.
* **Department**: Select the department that the employee will be reemployed in from the provided dropdown list.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the employee will be working.
* **Date of Hire**: Enter the date the employee was originally hired in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Rehire Date**: If applicable, enter the date the employee is to be reemployed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason for Rehire**: Provide a brief reason for rehiring the employee.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the employee.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee, if applicable.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the rehire application was submitted in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the application from the provided dropdown list.
* **Submitted To**: Select who the application was submitted to from the provided dropdown list.
* **Approved By**: If applicable, select who approved the application from the provided dropdown list.
* **Rehire Incentive Amount**: Enter the amount of the rehire incentive.
* **Rehire Incentive Currency**: Select the currency of the rehire incentive payment from the provided dropdown list.
* **Rehire Incentive Notes**: If applicable, enter any additional notes about the rehire incentive.
* **Submitted By 1**: This field is reserved for additional information and will not be used in this form.
* **Submitted By 2**: This field is a text box for any additional comments.
* **Submitted To 1**: This field is reserved for additional information and will not be used in this form.
* **Submission Date**: This field is reserved for additional information and will not be used in this form.
* **Rehire Incentive Notes**: This field is a text box for additional notes about the rehire incentive.
