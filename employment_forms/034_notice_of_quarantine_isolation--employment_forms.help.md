# Notice Of Quarantine/Isolation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notice of Quarantine/Isolation form is used to document a situation where an individual has been exposed to or is currently under quarantine or isolation due to a positive test result. This form is used to provide a record of the exposure history and to notify relevant parties of the individual's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the form, which should describe the reason for the quarantine or isolation.
2. Fill out the notice of exposure field, which should describe the event or situation that led to the individual being exposed to or quarantined.
3. Enter the date and time of exposure, which should be the date and time the individual was first exposed or quarantined.
4. Specify the location where the exposure occurred.
5. Answer the question about whether the exposure was to someone with a positive result, yes or no.
6. Answer the question about whether the individual is under quarantine or isolation, yes or no.
7. (Optional) Enter the reason for the positive result, if applicable.
8. Enter the date and time of the positive test result.
9. Enter the location of the positive test result.
10. Finally, sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Notice of Quarantine/Isolation** (`title`, required): This is the title of the form, which should describe the reason for the quarantine or isolation.
- **Notice of Exposure** (`notice_of_exposure`, required): This field should describe the event or situation that led to the individual being exposed to or quarantined.
- **Date of Exposure** (`date_of_exposure`, required): This field should contain the date the individual was first exposed or quarantined.
- **Time of Exposure** (`time_of_exposure`, required): This field should contain the time the individual was first exposed or quarantined.
- **Location of Exposure** (`location_of_exposure`, optional): This field should specify the location where the exposure occurred.
- **Notice Type** (`notice_type`, required): This field asks if the exposure was to someone with a positive result, answer yes or no.
- **Notice of Quarantine or Isolation Type** (`notice_of_quarantine_isolation_type`, required): This field asks if the individual is under quarantine or isolation, answer yes or no.
- **Reason for Positive Result** (`reason_for_positive_result`, optional): This field is used to enter the reason for the positive test result, if applicable.
- **Date of Positive Result** (`date_of_positive_result`, required): This field should contain the date of the positive test result.
- **Time of Positive Result** (`time_of_positive_result`, required): This field should contain the time of the positive test result.
- **Location of Positive Result** (`location_of_positive_result`, optional): This field should specify the location where the positive test result was taken.
- **Signature** (`signature`, required): This field should be signed by the individual providing the information to confirm that it is accurate.
