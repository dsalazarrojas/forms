# Daily Shift Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Shift Report Template is a form designed to track an employee's daily activities and work hours. This form is meant to be filled out at the end of each shift and is used to ensure that employees are following the correct procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the start time of your shift.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
3. Enter the date of the shift.
4. Select the location where you started your shift.
5. Select the location where you ended your shift.
6. Enter the number of hours worked during your shift.
7. Add any notes about your shift, such as notable events or issues that occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the time you began your shift.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Work Date** (work_date): Enter the date of the shift.
* **Start Location** (start_location): Select the location where you began your shift (Home or Office).
* **End Location** (end_location): Select the location where you ended your shift (Home or Office).
* **Work Hours** (work_hours): Enter the number of hours worked during your shift.
* **Shift Notes** (shift_notes): Add any notes about your shift, such as notable events or issues that occurred.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): (This field is currently empty and not required, it seems to be a placeholder or a future addition)

Note: This form is designed to be filled out at the end of each shift, so it's best to complete it as soon as possible after your shift ends.
