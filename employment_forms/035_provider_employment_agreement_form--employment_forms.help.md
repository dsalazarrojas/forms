# Provider Employment Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Provider Employment Agreement Form" is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of the employment between the provider (service provider) and the employer (client). It's essential to capture the provider's details, agreement type, start date, compensation terms, and signatures to establish a comprehensive understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the provider's details in the "Provider Details" field, providing a brief description of the services or tasks to be performed.
2. Select the type of agreement that best suits the employment terms from the "Agreement Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the start date of the employment agreement, or leave it blank if not applicable.
4. Describe the compensation terms and conditions in the "Compensation Terms" field.
5. Finally, include any signatures or names of parties involved in the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Details (Provider Details, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of the services or tasks to be performed by the provider. This field is crucial in establishing a clear understanding of the employment agreement.
* **Agreement Type (Agreement Type, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of agreement that best suits the employment terms. This field determines the terms and conditions of the employment.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, optional)**: Enter the start date of the employment agreement, if applicable.
* **Compensation Terms (Compensation Terms, text, optional)**: Describe the compensation terms and conditions of the employment, including payment details, payment frequency, and any other relevant information.
* **Signatures (Signatures, text, optional)**: Include the signatures or names of parties involved in the agreement. This is a mandatory field for the agreement to be binding and enforceable.
