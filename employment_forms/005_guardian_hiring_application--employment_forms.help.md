# Guardian Hiring Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guardian Hiring Application form is used to collect personal and professional information from candidates or new employees as part of the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with your personal information, including your first name in the "first-name" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact-info" field.
3. Indicate whether you have work experience by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "work-experience" field.
4. Enter your work availability in the "work-availability" field, using the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
5. List your relevant skills in the "skills" field.
6. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "references" field, indicating whether you have provided references.
7. If required, upload any relevant documents in the "upload-docs" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
10. Upload any required documents in the "upload-docs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your address, city, state, zip code, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Work Experience**: Select "Yes" if you have relevant work experience, or "No" if you do not.
* **Work Availability**: Enter your work availability using the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Skills**: List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess, including soft skills and technical skills.
* **References**: Check "Yes" if you have provided references, or "No" if not.
* **Upload Docs**: If required, upload any relevant documents such as your resume, ID document, or other supporting documentation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format [your_name]@example.com.
* **Upload Docs**: If required, upload any additional documents supporting your application.

Note: Please make sure to double-check the required fields and the format of each field to ensure accurate submission.
