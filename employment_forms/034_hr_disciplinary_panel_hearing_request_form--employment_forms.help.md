# HR Disciplinary Panel Hearing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an official disciplinary hearing. It is meant to be completed by an employee or their representative when they wish to address a disciplinary matter through a formal hearing process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select one of the options for "First Page" to indicate the type of hearing you are requesting. "Yes" or "No" will be sufficient to answer this question.
- Enter the employee's information on the "Employee Info" field. This should include any relevant details about the employee, such as their name and department.
- If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" for "Disciplinary Action" to indicate if this is a disciplinary matter related to a specific action or behavior.
- Choose a date and time for the hearing by selecting a date and entering a time on the "Hearing Date" and "Hearing Time" fields respectively.
- If the hearing will take place at a specific location, select "Yes" or "No" for "Location" and enter the address or other relevant details on the "Manager Info" field.
- In the "Manager Info" field, include any relevant information about the manager or supervisor who will be involved in the hearing process.
- Enter a brief explanation or request for the hearing on the "Hearing Request" field.
- If there are any witnesses that should be included in the hearing process, list them on the "Witness List" field. Note that this field is a text area and can handle multiple entries.
- If there is any additional information that you wish to include in the hearing process, enter it on the "Additional Info" field.
- Finally, click on the "Submit" field to submit the form and initiate the hearing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of hearing you are requesting. This field is used to categorize the type of hearing.
* **Employee Info**: Enter the employee's name, department, and any other relevant details.
* **Disciplinary Action**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this is a disciplinary matter related to a specific action or behavior.
* **Hearing Date**: Choose a date for the hearing to take place.
* **Hearing Time**: Enter the time for the hearing to take place.
* **Location**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the hearing will take place at a specific location.
* **Manager Info**: Enter any relevant information about the manager or supervisor who will be involved in the hearing process.
* **Hearing Request**: Enter a brief explanation or request for the hearing.
* **Witness List**: List any witnesses that should be included in the hearing process. This field is a text area and can handle multiple entries.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that you wish to include in the hearing process.
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form and initiate the hearing process.
