# Simple Employee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Employee Application form is a tool for prospective employees to provide their personal details for employment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already filled out this form or have been previously registered in the system.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required: false): Enter your address line 1.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
* Please ensure your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
