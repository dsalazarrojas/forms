# Employee Absence Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Absence Return To Work Form is a document used to record an employee's return to work after an absence due to illness or injury. It provides essential information from the employee's physician, ensuring a smooth and safe return to work process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Name field with the name of the employee returning to work.
2. Enter the Employee SSN of the employee returning to work.
3. Provide the Physician Name and their title, as per the information given by the employee's doctor.
4. Sign the form by adding your handwritten signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (Employee Name)**: Enter the name of the employee returning to work.
* **Employee SSN (Employee SSN)**: Enter the Social Security Number of the employee returning to work.
* **Physician Name (Physician Name)**: Enter the name of the employee's attending physician.
* **Physician Title (Physician Title)**: Enter the title of the employee's attending physician.
* **Return Date (Return Date)**: Enter the date on which the employee is expected to return to work.
* **Signature**: Add your handwritten signature to confirm that you are the attending physician or authorized to provide clearance for the employee's return to work.

Note: All fields are optional, but it is essential to fill in the required fields for the form to be valid.
