# Education Faculty Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Faculty Interview Form is a tool used to evaluate a faculty member's performance and suitability for a position. It is designed to provide a structured and organized way to document the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Faculty Member Information**
	* Fill in the Faculty Member field with the name of the faculty member being evaluated.
	* Enter the Faculty Member's Position in the Position field.
2. **Enter Evaluation Details**
	* Select the Evaluation Date and Evaluation Score using the provided date pickers.
	* Provide Evaluation Comments in the text field.
3. **Add Candidate Information**
	* Enter the Candidate's Email and Phone Number in the respective fields.
	* Enter the Evaluation Location and Evaluation Duration.
4. **Set Evaluation Dates**
	* Enter the Evaluation Start Date and Evaluation End Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Faculty Member** (text): Enter the name of the faculty member being evaluated.
* **Position** (text): Enter the faculty member's position or role.
* **Evaluation Date** (date): Enter the date when the evaluation was done.
* **Evaluation Score** (number): Enter the score of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Comments** (text): Enter any comments or feedback provided during the evaluation.
* **Faculty Member Name** (text): Enter the name of the faculty member (if different from the Faculty Member field).
* **Faculty Member Title** (text): Enter the faculty member's title (if applicable).
* **Department** (text): Enter the department where the evaluation was done.
* **Evaluation Time** (time): Enter the time of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Candidate Email** (email): Enter the candidate's email address (if applicable).
* **Candidate Phone** (text): Enter the candidate's phone number (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Location** (text): Enter the location where the evaluation was done.
* **Evaluation Duration** (text): Enter the duration of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Evaluation End Date** (date): Enter the end date of the evaluation (if applicable).
