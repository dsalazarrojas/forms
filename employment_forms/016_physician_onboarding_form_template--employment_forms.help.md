# Physician Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from physicians during the onboarding process, ensuring accurate and efficient data capture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Information" section with your basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
2. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your preferred contact method and any other relevant contact information.
3. In the "Medical Credentials" section, enter your medical credentials, such as certifications and licenses.
4. If applicable, list your "Work Experience" in the provided space.
5. In the "Education" section, describe your educational background.
6. If you have multiple medical specialties, select all relevant options from the "Medical Specialties" field.
7. Choose your language skills from the "Language Skills" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional information in the "Additional Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information
* **User Information** (`User Name`, `Address`, `Phone`, `Email`, `Other`, `Required`: `Yes`): Enter your basic information such as your name, address, phone number, email, and any other relevant details.

### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Method`, `Phone`, `Phone Extension`, `Email`, `Other`, `Required`: `Yes`): Provide your preferred contact method and any other relevant contact information.

### 3. Medical Credentials
* **Medical Credentials** (`Certifications`, `Licenses`, `Specialty Certifications`, `Required`: `Yes`): Enter your medical credentials, such as certifications, licenses, and specialty certifications.

### 4. Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `Required`: `No`): If applicable, describe your work experience in this space.

### 5. Education
* **Education** (`Degree`, `Institution`, `Graduation Date`, `Required`: `No`): Describe your educational background.

### 6. Medical Specialties
* **Medical Specialties** (`Cardiology`, `Dermatology`, `Endocrinology`, `Gastroenterology`, `Nephrology`, `Required`: `No`): Select all relevant medical specialties.

### 7. Language Skills
* **Language Skills** (`English`, `Spanish`, `Mandarin`, `Arabic`, `Required`: `No`): Choose your language skills.

### 8. Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Info`, `Required`: `No`): Provide any additional information.
