# Job Search Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect job search information from users. It is meant to be filled out once for each job being searched, to avoid duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Job Search Log** field, which is the main field for writing the job title and description.
2. The next field is **Job Title**, where you can provide a brief summary of the job title or any relevant details.
3. **Job Type** field has two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Choose 'Yes' if the job is suitable for your qualifications or interests, and 'No' otherwise.
4. **Job Location** field is a text field for you to write in the location of the job, whether it's an office location, remote work arrangement, or anything in between.
5. The last field, **Job Company**, is a text field for you to enter the name of the company that is offering or has offered the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Search Log**: This is the main field for writing a brief description of the job you are searching for. Please provide as much detail as you need to remember the job.
* **Job Title**: A concise title of the job, such as "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".
* **Job Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you are qualified for or interested in this job type, or 'No' otherwise. This helps us categorize job search data.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where the job is based, whether it's an office, home, or anywhere else.
* **Job Company**: Enter the name of the company offering or has offered the job.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible in the **Job Search Log** field.
- For the **Job Type** field, choose 'Yes' if you think you are suitable for the job or 'No' if not.
- If you're unsure about the **Job Location**, you can search for it online or check the company's website or contact them directly for more information.
