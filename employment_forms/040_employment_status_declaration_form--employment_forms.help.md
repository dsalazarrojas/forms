# employment_status_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a user's employment status, which includes their current or past job details, such as job title, job description, years of experience, and company size. This form is used to declare one's employment status for various purposes, such as insurance, tax, or financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate employment status from the dropdown menu.
2. If currently employed, enter your company name in the text box.
3. If currently or previously employed, select your job title from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter a brief description of your job in the text box.
5. Enter the number of years you have been in your current or previous job.
6. If applicable, select your job level from the dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, select the category your job falls under from the dropdown menu.
8. Select any relevant job functions from the dropdown menu.
9. If applicable, enter any job responsibilities in the text box.
10. If applicable, select the size of your company from the dropdown menu.
11. If applicable, enter your department name in the text box.
12. If applicable, select the date you started your current or previous job.
13. If applicable, select the date you ended your current or previous job.
14. Select your current or previous job status from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false): 
  This field asks you to select your current or previous employment status, such as employed, unemployed, self-employed, retired, or student.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): 
  If you are currently employed, enter the name of your company here.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, select_one, required: false): 
  If you are currently or previously employed, select your job title from the options provided.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): 
  Enter a brief description of your job here.
* **Years of experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required: false): 
  Enter the number of years you have been in your current or previous job.
* **Job level** (`job_level`, select_one, required: false): 
  If applicable, select the level of your job from the options provided.
* **Job category** (`job_category`, text, required: false): 
  If applicable, select the category your job falls under from the options provided.
* **Job functions** (`job_function`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select any relevant job functions that apply to you.
* **Job responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, note, required: false): 
  Enter any additional job responsibilities in this text box.
* **Company size** (`company_size`, select_one, required: false): 
  If applicable, select the size of your company from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): 
  If applicable, enter the name of your department here.
* **Job start date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: false): 
  If applicable, enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Job end date** (`job_end_date`, date, required: false): 
  If applicable, enter the date you ended your current or previous job.
* **Job status** (`job_status`, select_one, required: false): 
  Select your current or previous job status from the options provided.
