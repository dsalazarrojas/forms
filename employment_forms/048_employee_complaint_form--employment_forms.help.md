# Employee Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Complaint Form is a tool used to report incidents or complaints experienced by employees in the workplace. This form helps HR or relevant authorities to investigate and address the issues in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Start by describing the issue you are experiencing in the "Description of the complaint" field.
2.  Provide details about what happened in the "Incident description" field.
3.  Describe the action you took or plan to take in response to the incident in the "Action taken" field.
4.  If there were any witnesses, list their names in the "Witness" field.
5.  Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident date" field and the time of the incident in the "Incident time" field.
6.  In the "Incident location" field, provide the location where the incident occurred.
7.  In the "Relationship between you and the complainant" field, select the options that apply to your interaction with the person involved in the incident.
8.  Choose the type of incident that occurred in the "Type of incident" field.
9.  Provide any additional details or information about the incident in the "Incident details" field.
10. If you have any supporting documentation, you can upload it in the "Supporting documentation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Description of the complaint** (`employee_complaint_form_page_1`, text, required): Please describe the issue you are experiencing.
*   **Incident description** (`employee_complaint_form_page_2`, text, required): Provide details about what happened.
*   **Action taken** (`employee_complaint_form_page_3`, text, required): Describe the action taken by you.
*   **Witness** (`employee_complaint_form_page_4`, text, required): If there were any witnesses, please list their names.
*   **Incident date** (`employee_complaint_form_page_5`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Incident time** (`employee_complaint_form_page_6`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident location** (`employee_complaint_form_page_7`, text, required): Provide the location of the incident.
*   **Relationship between you and the complainant** (`employee_complaint_form_page_8`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that apply to your interaction with the person involved in the incident.
    +   Other
    +   Employee
    +   Supervisor
*   **Type of incident** (`employee_complaint_form_page_9`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of incident that occurred.
    +   Other
    +   Verbal Abuse
    +   Physical Threat
*   **Incident details** (`employee_complaint_form_page_10`, text, required): Provide any additional details or information about the incident.
*   **Supporting documentation** (`employee_complaint_form_page_11`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documentation for this incident.
