# Part Time Job Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for part-time employees to declare their current or expected employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
Please select the correct options for each field and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Arrangement** (Yes, No): 
  Please indicate whether you are currently working part-time or not.
* **Outside Work**: 
  Select all the days you work outside of the company, separated by commas. For example: "Monday, Wednesday, Friday"
* **Employer**: 
  Enter the name and address of your current or expected employer.
* **Job Title**: 
  Enter your current or expected job title.
* **Outside Hours**: 
  Enter the number of hours you work outside of the company. This field accepts numerical input.
* **Work Address**: 
  Enter your work address, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Work Zip**: 
  Enter your work zip code.
* **Work Phone**: 
  Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email**: 
  Enter your work email address.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- This form is for part-time employment only.
- If you are unsure about any field, please do not leave it blank.
