# Job Offer And Role Terms Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about job offers and employment terms to help you keep track of job details and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Use the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Job Offer** (`job_offer`, text, optional)
 Enter a brief description of the job offer.

### 2. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
 Enter the specific job title for the offer.

### 3. **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional)
 Enter the type of job (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, freelance).

### 4. **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, optional)
 Enter the status of the job (e.g., new, open, closed).

### 5. **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, optional)
 Enter the category of the job (e.g., software development, marketing, sales).

### 6. **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional)
 Enter a brief description of the job responsibilities.

### 7. **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`, text, optional)
 Enter any specific employment terms or conditions.

### 8. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, text, optional)
 Enter the employment status (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).

### 9. **Employment Terms Date** (`employment_terms_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the date of the employment terms or conditions.

### 10. **Job Type Details** (`job_type_details`, text, optional)
 Enter any additional details about the job type.

### 11. **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the start date of the job.

### 12. **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the end date of the job.

### 13. **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, text, optional)
 Enter the duration of the job.

### 14. **Job Responsibilities Description** (`job_responsibilities_description`, text, optional)
 Enter a detailed description of the job responsibilities.

### 15. **Employment Terms Description** (`employment_terms_description`, text, optional)
 Enter a description of the employment terms or conditions.

### 16. **Employment Status Description** (`employment_status_description`, text, optional)
 Enter a description of the employment status.

### 17. **Job Category Description** (`job_category_description`, text, optional)
 Enter a description of the job category.

### 18. **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional)
 Select the employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, freelance).

### 19. **Employment Status 2** (`employment_status_2`, select_multiple, optional)
 Select multiple employment statuses (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).

### 20. **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the start date of employment.

### 21. **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional)
 Enter the end date of employment.

### 22. **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional)
 Enter a secondary job title.

### 23. **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, text, optional)
 Enter the status of the second job.

### 24. **Job Category 2** (`job_category_2`, text, optional)
 Enter the category of the second job.

### 25. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
 Enter any additional notes or comments about the job or employment terms.

Please review each field carefully and ensure accuracy when completing this form.
