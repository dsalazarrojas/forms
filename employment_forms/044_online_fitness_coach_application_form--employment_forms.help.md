# Online Fitness Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Fitness Coach Application Form" is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from potential candidates interested in becoming certified online fitness coaches. This form helps us assess your qualifications, experience, and suitability for the role. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of success in the application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Enter your first and last name in the respective "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your birthdate in the "Birth Date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Describe your current fitness level in the "Fitness Level" field.
7. Share your experience in the "Experience" field.
8. List your goals for becoming a certified online fitness coach.
9. Disclose any health-related information in the "Health History" field.
10. Provide emergency contact information, including the contact's name, phone number, address, and relationship type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Birth Date** (5, date, required): Enter your birthdate in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Fitness Level** (6, text, required): Describe your current fitness level, e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced.
* **Experience** (7, text, required): Describe your relevant experience in the fitness industry.
* **Goals** (8, text, required): List your goals for becoming a certified online fitness coach.
* **Health History** (9, text, required): Disclose any health-related information that may impact your ability to perform the role.
* **Emergency Contact** (22, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (23, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Address** (24, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Relationship** (25, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Relationship Type** (19, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact from the list (Spouse, Partner, Family, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Phone Relationship Type** (20, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact's phone from the list (Spouse, Partner, Family, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Address Relationship Type** (21, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact's address from the list (Spouse, Partner, Family, Friend, Other).
* **Contact 2** (22, text, optional): Provide an additional contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone 2** (23, text, optional): Enter the additional contact's phone number.
* **Address** (24, text, optional): Enter the additional contact's address.
* **Emergency Relationship 2** (25, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the additional contact.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number where you can be reached.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and relationships with your emergency contacts.
* Double-check for errors before submitting the form.
