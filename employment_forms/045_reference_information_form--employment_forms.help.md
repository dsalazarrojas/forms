# Reference Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect reference information about a candidate or employee, including job details and employment history. This form is designed to aid in the hiring or promotion process by gathering essential data that can be used to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's reference information, including:
	* Candidate Reference
	* Referrer Name
	* Referrer Title
	* Job Title
	* Department
	* Supervisor Name
	* Supervisor Title
	* Job Level
	* Job Type
	* Job Function
	* Salary
	* Employment Status
	* Hire Date
	* Termination Date
	* Job Description
	* Job Requirements (1)
	* Job Requirements (2)
	* Candidate Notes
	* Referrer Phone
	* Referrer Email
	* Referrer Comments
	* Submitted By

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Reference**: Enter the candidate's reference information, such as their name and contact details.
* **Referrer Name**: Enter the name of the person referring the candidate.
* **Referrer Title**: Enter the title or position of the person referring the candidate.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the candidate.
* **Department**: Enter the department the candidate works in or would work in.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the candidate's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Title**: Enter the title or position of the candidate's supervisor.
* **Job Level**: Enter the level of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job Function**: Enter the function or role the candidate would be performing in the job.
* **Salary**: Enter the salary of the candidate.
* **Employment Status**: Enter the current employment status of the candidate.
* **Hire Date**: Enter the date the candidate was hired.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the candidate's employment was terminated.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the candidate's job.
* **Job Requirements**: Enter the requirements for the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job Requirements 2**: Enter any additional requirements for the job.
* **Candidate Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the candidate.
* **Referrer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person referring the candidate.
* **Referrer Email**: Enter the email address of the person referring the candidate.
* **Referrer Comments**: Enter any comments from the person referring the candidate.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
