# Full Time Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track full-time employment contracts for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Contract Title**, a brief description of the contract.
2. Enter the **Start Date**, the date when the employment contract starts.
3. Enter the **End Date**, if applicable, the date when the contract ends.
4. Provide a brief **Contract Description**, a description of the employment contract.
5. Select the **Employment Status**, whether the contract is Active or Inactive.
6. Enter the **Employee Name**, the name of the employee under contract.
7. Enter the **Job Title**, the job title of the employee.
8. Enter the **Manager's Name**, if applicable, the name of the manager for the employee.
9. Enter the **Manager's Title**, if applicable, the title of the manager.
10. Enter the **Contract Terms**, a brief description of the terms of the contract.
11. Enter the **Employment Agreement**, a statement of agreement between the employer and employee.
12. Enter the **Agreement Date**, the date when the employment agreement is signed.
13. Enter the **Employment Contract End Date**, if applicable, the date when the contract ends.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Title** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the employment contract.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the employment contract starts.
* **End Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date when the employment contract ends.
* **Contract Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the employment contract.
* **Employment Status** (select_one, required): Select whether the contract is Active or Inactive.
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee under contract.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Manager's Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the manager for the employee.
* **Manager's Title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the manager.
* **Contract Terms** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the contract terms.
* **Employment Agreement** (text, required): Enter a statement of agreement between the employer and employee.
* **Agreement Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the employment agreement is signed.
* **Employment Contract End Date** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date when the employment contract ends.
