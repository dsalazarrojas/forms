# Online Course Developer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for the online course developer position. This form will help us assess your qualifications, skills, and experiences to determine your suitability for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Ensure that you have attached the required documents, such as your resume and cover letter.
3. Provide a clear and concise personal statement.
4. Choose the correct job title and job status options.
5. Enter your contact email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application** (`Job Application`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your application. Please write a short paragraph about why you are interested in this position and how you can contribute to our team.
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, text, optional): This field is used to provide information about your educational background. Please list your degrees, institutions, and relevant courses.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, text, optional): This field is used to provide information about your relevant work experience. Please list your previous work experience, especially in online course development or a related field.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text, optional): This field is used to provide information about your skills and qualifications. Please list your relevant skills, especially in online course development, programming languages, or tools.
* **Portfolio** (`Portfolio`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a link to your online portfolio or a brief description of your work. Please provide a link or a description of your online course development projects.
* **Personal Statement** (`Personal Statement`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief statement about yourself. Please write a short paragraph about your background, skills, and why you are a good fit for this position.
* **Upload Resume** (`Upload Resume`, text, optional): This field is used to upload your resume as a PDF or Word document. Please ensure that your resume is in a format that can be easily read by our system.
* **Upload Cover Letter** (`Upload Cover Letter`, text, optional): This field is used to upload your cover letter as a PDF or Word document. Please ensure that your cover letter is in a format that can be easily read by our system.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select your current job title. Please choose the correct job title that reflects your current position.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select your current job status. Please choose the correct job status that reflects your current employment status.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter your contact email address. Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your contact phone number. Please ensure that your phone number is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Submit Application** (`Submit Application`, text, optional): This field is used to submit your application. Once you have filled out the form and attached the required documents, click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have attached the required documents, such as your resume and cover letter.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Write clear and concise responses to each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
