# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee details, laptop information, and agreement terms for company-owned laptops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your employee details in the **Employee Details** field.
2.  Enter information about the laptop you are using or would like to use in the **Laptop Information** field.
3.  Read and agree to the **Agreement Terms** in the following field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Details** (employee_laptop_agreement_form_1):
    *   Enter your employee number, name, or any other identifier to help the system identify you.
*   **Laptop Information** (employee_laptop_agreement_form_2):
    *   Enter details about the laptop, including its serial number, model, and any relevant information about the device.
*   **Agreement Terms** (employee_laptop_agreement_form_3):
    *   Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions set forth in this section, ensuring you understand your responsibilities and the expectations for laptop usage.
