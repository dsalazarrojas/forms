# Domestic Worker Biodata Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Domestic Worker Biodata Form is designed to capture the essential biodata from domestic workers to aid our organization in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling out the form title field with a brief description of your role or position.
2. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
3. Provide your last name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
5. Provide a valid phone number in the Phone field.
6. If applicable, enter your experience in months in the Experience field.
7. List your skills separated by commas in the Skills field.
8. List your education qualifications separated by commas in the Education field.
9. If applicable, select the employment type from the Employment Type field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This field is for a brief description of your role or position.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that will be used for internal communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter your experience in months.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required/optional): List your skills separated by commas.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): List your education qualifications separated by commas.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): If applicable, select the employment type from the list.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* Use commas to separate multiple skills and education qualifications.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* If you are providing a fake or incorrect email address, phone number, or experience, your form will not be processed.
