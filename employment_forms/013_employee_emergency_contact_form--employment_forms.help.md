# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact Form is used to collect critical information from employees in case of an emergency or other urgent situations. This ensures that the HR or management team has the necessary contact details to reach out to the employee's emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the correct form and are not duplicating your efforts.
2. Provide clear and accurate information for each field.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
4. Use the hints provided for each field to guide your input.
5. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact): This field is a text input where you can provide a short description or note about your emergency contact.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name): Enter the name of the person you've designated as your emergency contact.
* **Relation Type** (Relation Type): Select one of the following options:
	+ Parent: Immediate family member (e.g., mother, father, child)
	+ Spouse: Spousal relationship
	+ Child: Immediate family member (e.g., son, daughter)
	+ Other: Any other relationship (e.g., partner, friend)
* **Contact Type** (Contact Type): Select all applicable options for where you can be reached:
	+ Home: Residence or home address
	+ Work: Workplace
	+ Mobile: Mobile phone number
	+ Other: Any other contact method (e.g., work email, messenger app)
* **Contact Number 1** (Contact Number 1): Enter a contact phone number.
* **Contact Number 2** (Contact Number 2): Enter an additional contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Relationship Address** (Relationship Address): Enter the address of the person you've designated as your emergency contact.
* **Contact Relationship** (Contact Relationship): Provide a description of your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact): Enter an email address for your emergency contact.
* **Other (Relationship)** (Contact Relationship Other): If you've selected "Other" as your relation type, provide a description of your relationship with the emergency contact.
