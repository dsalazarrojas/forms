# healthcare_credential_verification_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify employment information for healthcare professionals. It helps medical institutions, hospitals, or healthcare organizations assess an individual's eligibility for certain benefits, services, or privileges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all required fields.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. If you are a healthcare professional, please provide your employment status and history.
4. If you are being hired or already employed in the healthcare industry, please provide your employer information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Employment status**: Select your current employment status (Employed, Not employed, Retired).
* **Employment type**: Select all the types of employment that apply to you (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, Other).
* **Job title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Employer name**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Verification authorization**: Select the status of your employment verification (Verified, Unverified, Pending).
* **Employment date**: Enter your employment start date.
* **Job start date**: Enter your job start date.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your hiring manager or HR department for guidance.
* This form is only relevant if you are being hired or already employed in the healthcare industry. If you are not, please do not fill out this form.
