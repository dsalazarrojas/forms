# Retail New Location Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for retail job openings at a new location.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional fields.
- Ensure that you are submitting the correct job application and resume.
- Review the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_info** (store info, text, required: false): Enter the store's name, address, and other relevant information.
* **contact_details** (contact details, text, required: false): Provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **job_applications** (job applications, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable job applications you are interested in (e.g., Application 1, Application 2, Application 3).
* **job_title** (job title, text, required: false): Enter the job title you are applying for (e.g., Sales Associate, Store Manager).
* **job_type** (job type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of job you are applying for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **work_location** (work location, text, required: false): Enter the location where you would like to work (e.g., Store A, Store B).
* **job_description** (job description, note, required: false): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **job_requirements** (job requirements, text, required: false): Outline the requirements and qualifications you possess for the job.
* **application_date** (application date, date, required: false): Enter the date you applied for the job.
* **resume** (resume, text, required: false): Attach your resume or CV to your application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the hiring team.
