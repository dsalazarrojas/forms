# Damaged Equipment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Damaged Equipment Form" is designed to report and track damaged equipment within the organization. It helps gather information about the damage, including location, type, and date/time. This form also collects submitter's information, such as name, department, and contact details, to facilitate proper reporting and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Damaged Equipment Form" efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant location where the damage occurred.
2. Choose the type of damage (physical, electrical, or water damage, or other).
3. Enter the date and time the damage was done.
4. Describe the damage briefly.
5. Select the equipment that was damaged (multiple selections are possible).
6. Enter the name, department, email, phone number, and other relevant details of the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Equipment Description
* **Equipment Description** (`Equipment Description`, text, not required): Briefly describe the equipment that was damaged.

*   **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select the correct location where the damage occurred.

*   **Damaged Equipment** (`damaged_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the equipment that was damaged.

*   **Type of Damage** (`type_of_damage`, select_one, required): Choose the type of damage done to the equipment (physical, electrical, water damage, or other).

*   **Date of Damage** (`date_of_damage`, date, required): Enter the date the damage occurred.

*   **Time of Damage** (`time_of_damage`, time, required): Enter the time the damage was done.

*   **Submitter's Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.

*   **Submitter's Department** (`submitter_department`, select_one, required): Enter your department or team.

*   **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

*   **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email.

*   **Submitter's Notes** (`submitter_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the incident.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct location and date/time to accurately report the incident.
*   Choose the correct type of damage and equipment involved.
*   Enter a brief description of the damage.
*   Ensure you enter accurate submitter details for proper follow-up and record-keeping.
*   If you need to report multiple equipment, select all relevant options.
*   Use the submitter's notes field to add any additional information that may be helpful.
