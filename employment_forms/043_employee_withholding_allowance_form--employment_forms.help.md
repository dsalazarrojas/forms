# Employee Withholding Allowance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine the withholding allowance for employees. It collects information about the employee's details, allowance, salary, address, and other relevant information to calculate their withholding allowance accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee's details, including their name, address, and date of birth.
2. Select the tax year for which the allowance is being claimed.
3. Indicate whether the employee has claimed an allowance or not.
4. Enter the employee's annual salary.
5. Enter the employee's allowance amount.
6. Select the frequency at which the allowance is claimed (e.g., monthly or quarterly).
7. Enter the allowance rate.
8. Enter the total allowance amount claimed.
9. Select the employee's nationality.
10. Enter any other relevant details, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required): This section collects the employee's personal details, including their name, address, and date of birth.
  - **Claimed Allowance** (`claimed_allowance`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether the employee has claimed an allowance or not.
  - **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `number`, required): Select the tax year for which the allowance is being claimed.
  - **Employee Allowance** (`employee_allowance`, `number`, required): Enter the employee's allowance amount.
  - **Employee Salary** (`employee_salary`, `number`, required): Enter the employee's annual salary.
  - **Address** (`employee_address`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's address.
  - **Claimed Allowance Frequency** (`claimed_allowance_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency at which the allowance is claimed (e.g., monthly or quarterly).
  - **Claimed Allowance Rate** (`claimed_allowance_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the allowance rate.
  - **Claimed Allowance Amount** (`claimed_allowance_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total allowance amount claimed.
  - **Employee Nationality** (`employee_nationality`, `select_one`, required): Select the employee's nationality.
  - **Employee DOB** (`employee_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the employee's date of birth.
  - **Tax Identification Number** (`tax_identification_number`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's tax identification number.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any errors in the withholding allowance calculation.
