# Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Form is a crucial tool for employees to provide feedback and insights about their departure from the company. It helps in understanding the reasons for leaving, gathering valuable information for future improvements, and ensuring a smooth transition. The form is designed to collect relevant details about the employee's last day of work, reasons for leaving, and feedback for improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
* Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Provide clear and concise answers for each field, and double-check any information you've filled in if necessary.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Why are you leaving? (page 1)
* **Why are you leaving?** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for your reason for leaving the company.

### 2. Other (please specify) (page 1)
* **Other (please specify)** (`reason_for_leaving_other`, text, optional): If your reason for leaving is not listed, provide a detailed explanation.

### 3. Last day of work (page 2)
* **Last day of work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, optional): Provide the date you last worked.

### 4. Last day of work (page 2)
* **Last day of work** (`last_date_of_work`, time, optional): Provide the time you last worked.

### 5. Supervisor Name (page 3)
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Fill in your supervisor's name.

### 6. Supervisor Title (page 3)
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, optional): Provide your supervisor's title.

### 7. Manager Name (page 4)
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Fill in your manager's name.

### 8. Manager Title (page 4)
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Provide your manager's title.

### 9. Company Name (page 5)
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Fill in the name of the company you worked for.

### 10. Department (page 5)
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Provide the name of the department you belonged to.

### 11. Job Title (page 6)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Fill in your job title.

### 12. Last Supervisor Name (page 6)
* **Last Supervisor Name** (`last_supervisor_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your last supervisor.

### 13. Last Supervisor Title (page 7)
* **Last Supervisor Title** (`last_supervisor_title`, text, optional): Fill in the title of your last supervisor.

### 14. Last Manager Name (page 7)
* **Last Manager Name** (`last_manager_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your last manager.

### 15. Last Manager Title (page 8)
* **Last Manager Title** (`last_manager_title`, text, optional): Fill in the title of your last manager.

### 16. Date of Exit (page 8)
* **Date of Exit** (`exit_date`, date, optional): Provide the date you left the company.

### 17. Reasons for Leaving (page 9)
* **Reasons for Leaving** (`exit_reasons`, select_one, required): Select one of the reasons for leaving listed.

### 18. Other Reason (page 9)
* **Other Reason** (`exit_reason_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a clear explanation.

### 19. Feedback for Improvement (page 10)
* **Feedback for Improvement** (`feedback_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share your feedback for future improvements.

### 20. Comments (page 10)
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments you'd like to provide.

### 21. Contact Information (page 11)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide any contact information you'd like to share.

### 22. Manager Sign Date (page 12)
* **Manager Sign Date** (`manager_sign_date`, date, optional): Fill in the date your manager signed the form.

### 23. Manager Sign Time (page 12)
* **Manager Sign Time** (`manager_sign_time`, time, optional): Provide the time your manager signed the form.

### 24. Manager Comments (page 12)
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): Any comments from your manager.

### 25. Submit (page 13)
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Click "Yes" to submit the form.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure you've filled in all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.
* Submit the form when you've completed all the necessary steps.
