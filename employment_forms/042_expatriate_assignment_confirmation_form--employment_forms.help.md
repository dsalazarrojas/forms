# Expatriate Assignment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expatriate Assignment Confirmation Form is a document used to confirm employee details and terms of employment for expatriate assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Employee Details: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information in the "Employee Details" page.
2. Specify Assignment Location: Enter the location where you will be assigned to work in the "Assignment Location" page.
3. Enter Job Start and End Dates: Provide the start and end dates of your job assignment in the "Job Start Date" and "Job End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Accept Terms: Select "Acceptance" from the "Terms Accepted" dropdown menu to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of your assignment.
5. Add Confirmation Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about your assignment in the "Confirmation Notes" field.
6. Specify Assigned Tool: If applicable, enter the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Assignment Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the specific location where you will be assigned to work.
* **Job Start Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your job assignment.
* **Job End Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your job assignment.
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Acceptance" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of your assignment.
* **Confirmation Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your assignment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`tool`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the tool assigned to you.
