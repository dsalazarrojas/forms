# Screening Checklist For Visitors And Employees - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Screening Checklist for Visitors and Employees is designed to gather essential information and screening results for all individuals entering the premises. This form is a crucial part of the organization's security protocols to ensure that everyone is thoroughly vetted before granting access.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    - Fill out the **Visitor Details** page with the required information.
    - On the **Screening Results** page, choose your answer to the screening question from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Visitor Details** (1) - Provide your name and any other relevant details as prompted.
    *   This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have any relevant information to share.
*   **Contact Person** (2) - Enter the name of the person you'll be meeting with or the department you'll be visiting.
    *   This field is required to ensure that we can direct you to the right person or department.
*   **Screening Results** (3) - Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you've completed the screening process or not.
    *   This field is required and should only be filled out with either 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask a member of our staff for help.
*   Remember to save your progress regularly so that your work isn't duplicated.
