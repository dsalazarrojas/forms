# Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's proficiency with camera equipment. This information is used to assess their suitability for a camera-related position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Select the correct option for "Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form" on the first page.
* Select all camera types you have experience with from the options provided on page 2.
* Enter the number of years of experience you have with the cameras and their accessories on page 3.
* Select the most complicated camera system you have worked with on page 4.
* Select your experience level with camera accessories on page 5.
* Select the types of camera equipment you need help with on page 6.
* Indicate whether you can perform camera maintenance, testing, and rigging on page 7.
* Confirm whether you can perform the tasks listed on page 8.
* Confirm whether you have performed on-location shoots on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form** (Page 1)
	+ Select the correct option to indicate which type of camera equipment you have experience with.
* **Which of the following cameras do you have experience with?** (Page 2)
	+ Select all camera types you have experience with.
* **How many years of experience do you have with the camera?** (Page 3)
	+ Enter the number of years of experience you have with the camera.
* **How many years of experience have you had with the camera's accessories?** (Page 3)
	+ Enter the number of years of experience you have with the camera's accessories.
* **What is the most complicated camera system you have worked with?** (Page 4)
	+ Select the most complicated camera system you have experience with.
* **What is your experience level with camera accessories?** (Page 5)
	+ Select your experience level with camera accessories.
* **On what type of camera equipment do you need help?** (Page 6)
	+ Select all types of camera equipment you need help with.
* **Can you perform camera maintenance, including cleaning and adjustments?** (Page 7)
	+ Select yes or no to indicate whether you can perform camera maintenance.
* **Can you perform camera testing?** (Page 7)
	+ Select yes or no to indicate whether you can perform camera testing.
* **Can you perform camera rigging?** (Page 7)
	+ Select yes or no to indicate whether you can perform camera rigging.
* **Have you performed on-location shoots?** (Page 8)
	+ Select yes or no to indicate whether you have performed on-location shoots.
