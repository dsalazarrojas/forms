# Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's proficiency with camera equipment. This information is used to assess their suitability for a camera-related position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Select the correct option for "Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form" on the first page.
* Select all camera types you have experience with from the options provided on page 2.
* Enter the number of years of experience you have with the cameras and their accessories on page 3.
* Select the most complicated camera system you have worked with on page 4.
* Select your experience level with camera accessories on page 5.
* Select the types of camera equipment you need help with on page 6.
* Indicate whether you can perform camera maintenance, testing, and rigging on page 7.
* Confirm whether you can perform the tasks listed on page 8.
* Confirm whether you have performed on-location shoots on page 9.
