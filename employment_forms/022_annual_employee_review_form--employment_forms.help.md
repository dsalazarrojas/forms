# Annual Employee Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Employee Review Form is a tool used by managers to assess and document the performance of their employees. It is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of an employee's strengths and weaknesses, areas for improvement, and areas of growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all necessary information and documents before starting the review.
2. Complete each section thoroughly, providing specific examples and supporting evidence where possible.
3. Be honest and objective in your assessment, focusing on factual observations and data-driven feedback.
4. Consider the employee's goals and development needs when completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their personnel file.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional):
	+ If available, enter the employee's unique identifier.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the employee's current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the employee's department from the provided options.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your name and title.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter the employee's hire date.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the review period from the provided options.
* **Job Knowledge** (`job_knowledge`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's understanding of their role.
* **Quality of Work** (`quality_of_work`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's work quality.
* **Productivity** (`productivity`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's output and efficiency.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's communication abilities.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's collaboration skills.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's analytical abilities.
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's self-motivation.
* **Dependability** (`dependability`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's reliability.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's attendance record.
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's timeliness.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's flexibility.
* **Leadership Potential** (`leadership_potential`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Rate the employee's leadership abilities. (This field is optional and should only be filled in if applicable.)
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Rate the employee's overall performance.
* **Key Accomplishments** (`key_accomplishments`, `text`, required):
	+ List major achievements and accomplishments.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, required):
	+ Identify areas requiring development and growth.
* **Goals for Next Period** (`goals_next_period`, `text`, required):
	+ Set objectives for the coming year.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `text`, optional):
	+ Identify development needs.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide employee response or feedback.
* **Recommended Raise** (`recommended_raise`, `number`, optional):
	+ Suggest a percentage increase.
* **Promotion Recommendation** (`promotion_recommendation`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Assess the employee's readiness for promotion.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your name and title.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter the review date.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate in your assessment to provide valuable feedback and support for employee growth.
* Use specific examples and facts to support your evaluations.
* Ensure the employee has a clear understanding of their strengths and weaknesses.
* Store the completed form in a secure location to maintain employee confidentiality.
* Consider providing a copy of the form to the employee for their records.
* Regularly review and update the form to reflect changes and progress over time.
