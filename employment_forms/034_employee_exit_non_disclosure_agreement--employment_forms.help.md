The form's purpose is to capture an Employee's acknowledgement and agreement to the company's non-disclosure requirements upon exit. It serves as a formal acknowledgement that the employee has not taken any confidential company information with them upon leaving.

To complete this form, you will be asked to provide your acknowledgement of the agreement, sign the form, and optionally select whether you have taken any confidential company information with you.

Here is the help guide in Markdown format:
# Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement is a formal acknowledgement that you, as an employee, have not taken any confidential company information with you upon leaving the company. This form is an internal process to ensure that the company's intellectual property and sensitive data remain secure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement Acknowledgments section and ensure you understand the terms outlined.
2. Sign the Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement Signature section to confirm your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement Acknowledgments** (`employee_exit_non_disclosure_agreement_acknowledgments`, text, optional): Please read and understand the terms of the non-disclosure agreement, and type out your acknowledgement.
* **Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement Signature** (`employee_exit_non_disclosure_agreement_signature`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have not taken any confidential company information with you. If you have taken any, select 'No' and explain the circumstances in the space provided.
* **Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement Date** (`employee_exit_non_disclosure_agreement_date`, date, optional): Please enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
