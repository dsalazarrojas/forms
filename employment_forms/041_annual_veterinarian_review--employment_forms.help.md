# Annual Veterinarian Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Veterinarian Review form is used to assess the performance and growth of veterinarians, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their professional skills, areas for improvement, and goals for the next year. This form helps veterinarians reflect on their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for development, ultimately supporting their professional growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question thoroughly, being as specific as possible.
2. Use the rating options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement) for questions that ask for subjective assessments of your skills or performance.
3. Use the free-text fields (Veterinarian Name, Practice Name, Continuing education, Major Accomplishments, Challenges Faced, Professional Goals for Next Year, Strengths, Areas for Professional Development) to provide detailed answers.
4. Make sure to review and adjust your answers before submitting the form.
