# Hospitality Part Time Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in or currently employed in the hospitality industry to apply for part-time job opportunities. It is intended for those who are looking for flexible work arrangements and are available to work at various shifts and days.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your information before submitting to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can describe your interest in working in the hospitality industry, your relevant experience, and why you would be a good fit for a part-time position.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): If you have previous work experience in the hospitality industry, please describe it here.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Please select your current employment status from the options below:
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you are currently or previously employed, please provide your job title.
* **Work Shift** (`work_shift`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you are available to work:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Night
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): If you have a preferred number of hours to work, please enter it here.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of job you are interested in:
	+ Part-time
	+ Full-time
	+ Contract
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job level:
	+ Entry Level
	+ Mid-level
	+ Senior
	+ Manager
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, number, optional): If you are available to work, please enter the number of days you are able to work.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available to work:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are currently employed, please provide your supervisor's name and contact information.
* If you have any other relevant skills or qualifications, please describe them in the job application section.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please describe them in the job application section or in the availability section.
* Please review your information before submitting to ensure it is accurate and complete.
