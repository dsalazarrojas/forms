# Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Application form is a comprehensive tool used to gather information from candidates applying for employment opportunities. This form helps to assess a candidate's qualifications, experience, and availability to determine the best fit for a job opening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal details such as your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your job title, work experience, education, and job type from the provided options.
3. Choose your availability, job status, and employment type from the respective dropdown menus.
4. If applicable, select your job category, work schedule, and work location.
5. Enter your available date and time, as well as any additional job notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required/optional): Select the job title that best describes your professional role.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles and company names.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your availability for work, including full-time, part-time, or freelance.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of job you are applying for, including salary, contract, or freelance.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current job status, including active, inactive, or pending.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of employment you are looking for, including full-time, part-time, or intern.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate where you are willing to work, including remote, in-office, or hybrid.
* **Job Schedule** (`job_schedule`, time, required/optional): Enter your preferred work schedule, including start and end times.
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, text, required/optional): Enter your available work hours, including morning, afternoon, or evening shifts.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Experience Level** (`work_experience_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your level of work experience, including entry-level, mid-level, senior, or lead.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the job you are applying for, including IT, finance, marketing, or sales.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your employment status, including single, married, widow, or divorced.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, text, required/optional): Enter the days you are available to work.
* **Job Status Update** (`job_status_update`, text, required/optional): Enter any updates or changes to your job status.
* **Job Status Update Date** (`job_status_update_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the job status update.
* **Job Category Update** (`job_category_update`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any updates or changes to your job category.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your available time for work.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the job you are applying for.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified otherwise.
