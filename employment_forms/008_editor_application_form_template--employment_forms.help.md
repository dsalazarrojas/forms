# Editor Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from editors who are interested in working with a content creation company.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with the required information to the best of your abilities.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your experiences, skills, and availability.
- You can fill out the form in multiple pages if you have a lot of information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channel name (Channel name)** (`channel_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your editor channel name on YouTube or other platforms where you have a presence.
* **YouTube Channel ID (youtube_channel_id)** (`youtube_channel_id`, text, required: false): Enter your YouTube channel ID if you have a YouTube channel.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following categories: Gaming, Music, or Vlogging.
* **Description (description)** (`description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your editorial style and experience.
* **Experience Level (experience_level)** (`experience_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following experience levels: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Equipment Used (equipment_used)** (`equipment_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following equipment used: Adobe Premiere, Avid, or DaVinci.
* **Education (education)** (`education`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following educational background: Bachelor, Masters, or PhD.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth (optional, but can be useful for age verification).
* **Availability (availability)** (`availability`, time, required: false): Select your availability in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Availability 2 (availability_2)** (`availability_2`, time, required: false): Select your secondary availability in 24-hour format (e.g., 18:00-20:00).
* **YouTube URL (youtube_url)** (`youtube_url`, text, required: false): Enter your YouTube channel URL if you have one.
