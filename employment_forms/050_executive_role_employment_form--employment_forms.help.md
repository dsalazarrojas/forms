# Executive Role Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Role Employment Form is designed to gather essential information from executives interested in exploring new opportunities. Completing this form will help us assess your qualifications and potential fit for a position that suits your skills and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form and its sections to ensure you're filling out the correct information.
2. Fill in your Application Information, including your name, title, and any other relevant details.
3. Upload your Resume, Cover Letter, and any additional documents required for the position.
4. Select the Executive Role that best aligns with your skills and experience.
5. Enter your Contact Information, including your Email and Phone Number.
6. Once you have filled out all required fields, click on the Submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Information** (`application_information`, text, required: false): This section is for providing your personal details, such as your name, title, and any other relevant information.
* **Resume And Cover Letter** (`resume_and_cover_letter`, text, required: false): Please upload your resume and cover letter as separate documents. This will help us assess your qualifications and experience.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This section is for providing your contact details, including your email and phone number. We'll use this to get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required: false): Provide information about your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide the names and contact information of your professional references.
* **Executive Role** (`executive_role`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the Executive Role that you're interested in applying for. Please choose the one that best aligns with your skills and experience.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required: true): Please upload any additional documents required for the position. Check the job posting for specific requirements.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): This section is for your digital signature. Please enter your name and title as a form of identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your professional email address. We'll use this to contact you regarding your application.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: true): Enter your professional phone number. We'll use this to get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, required: true): Please upload your resume as a PDF or Word document.
* **Upload Cover Letter** (`upload_cover_letter`, text, required: true): Please upload your cover letter as a PDF or Word document.
* **Upload Additional Documents** (`upload_additional_documents`, text, required: true): Please upload any additional documents required for the position. Check the job posting for specific requirements.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Once you have filled out all required fields, click on this button to send your application.
