# hr_operations_simplification_proposal_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from employees for HR operations simplification proposals. It is meant to capture the details required for evaluating and implementing HR operations simplification projects within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you are submitting only new proposals or indicate the version number if you are revisiting an existing proposal.
3. Review and submit the form once you have entered all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title (3)**: Enter your current job title or role within the organization.
* **Problem Statement (4)**: Describe the problem or issue you are trying to address with the proposal.
* **Proposal Solution (5)**: Provide the proposed solution to the problem statement.
* **Expected Outcome (6)**: Describe the expected outcome or results of the proposal.
* **Submission Date (7)**: Enter the date you are submitting the proposal.
* **Submission Status (8)**: Select the status of your proposal (Active or Inactive).
* **Reviewer Comments (9)**: Leave comments for the reviewer to refer to when reviewing your proposal.
* **Attachment (10)**: Attach any relevant supporting documents or files.
* **Description (11)**: Enter a brief description of your proposal.
* **Reviewer Name (12)**: Enter the name of the person reviewing your proposal.
* **Reviewer Email (13)**: Enter the email address of the person reviewing your proposal.
* **Reviewer Notes (14)**: Leave notes for the reviewer to refer to when reviewing your proposal.
* **Proposal Version (15)**: Enter the version number of your proposal (e.g., 1.0, 1.1, etc.).
* **Submission Time (16)**: Enter the time you are submitting the proposal.
* **Last Modified By (17)**: Enter the person who last modified your proposal.
* **Signature (18)**: Sign and date your proposal to confirm submission.

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed before submitting the form.
