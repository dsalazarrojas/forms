# Esp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esp Staff Application Form is designed to collect information from potential employees, including personal details, work experience, and attachments. This form is used to evaluate applicants for available positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Personal Details** field.
2. Select your **Contact Details** from the provided list.
3. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. Choose your **Work Experience** by selecting one or more options from the list.
5. Select your **Available To Work** schedule.
6. Choose when you are **Available To Start**.
7. Upload necessary attachments in the **Upload Attachments** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details**: Please fill in your full name, email address, and other contact information.
* **Contact Details**: Select one or both of the provided options for your contact details.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 12/03/1990).
* **Work Experience**: Select one or more options from the list to describe your work experience.
* **Available To Work**: Choose your preferred work schedule.
* **Available To Start**: Select when you are able to start working.
* **Upload Attachments**: Upload any necessary attachments such as a resume, cover letter, or other relevant documents. Each **Upload Attachments** field is for uploading different types of attachments.
