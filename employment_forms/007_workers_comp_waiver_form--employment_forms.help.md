# Workers Comp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees regarding their waiver of liability for work-related injuries or accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the first field.
2. Enter your job title in the second field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY in the third field.
4. Enter your address in the fourth field.
5. Enter the date of the accident or injury in the fifth field.
6. Briefly describe the injury or accident in the sixth field.
7. If applicable, select the reason for the waiver in the seventh field.
8. Enter the waiver terms and conditions in the remaining fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is waiving liability.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee who is waiving liability.
* **Employee Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the employee in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Address**: Enter the address of the employee who is waiving liability.
* **Work Date**: Enter the date of the accident or injury.
* **Injury Details**: Briefly describe the injury or accident.
* **Release Details**: Briefly describe why the employee is being released from liability.
* **Employee Signature**: Enter the date and time of signature by the employee.
* **Employer Signature**: Enter the date and time of signature by the employer.
* **Date of Waiver**: Enter the date the waiver is being signed.
* **Reason for Waiver**: Select the reason for the waiver from the list provided.
* **Waiver Terms**: Enter a detailed description of the waiver terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Terms 2-14**: Repeat the description of waiver terms and conditions for each corresponding field.
