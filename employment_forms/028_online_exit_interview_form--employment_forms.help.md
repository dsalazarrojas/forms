# Online Exit Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This online exit interview form is designed to gather information from departing employees to understand their reasons for leaving the company, their experiences, and their overall feedback. The data collected will help management identify areas of improvement and create a better work environment for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions thoroughly and accurately based on your experiences while working with our company.
2. Make sure to provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field for that purpose.
4. Select "Next Steps" to indicate what you would like the company to consider for future employees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information**: Please provide the company name where you worked.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it was known in the company.
* **Department**: Type the department name where you were working.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title while working at the company.
* **Reason for Departure**: Please select the reason why you are leaving the company. This will help us understand your perspective.
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments about your experience, please type them here.
* **Date Joined**: Enter the date you joined the company.
* **Date Separated**: Enter the date you left the company.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address (or any other contact information you want to provide).
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number where we can reach you.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Job Performance**: Rate your job performance (1-5).
* **Company Climate**: Rate the company climate (1-5).
* **Communication with Management**: Rate the communication with management (1-5).
* **Communication with Colleagues**: Rate the communication with colleagues (1-5).
* **Benefits**: If you received any benefits while working with the company, please describe them here.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your job satisfaction (1-5).
* **Company Culture**: Rate the company culture (1-5).
* **Opportunities for Growth**: Rate the opportunities for growth (1-5).
* **Overall Experience**: Describe your overall experience while working with the company.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions you have for the company.
* **Job Description**: If you would like to suggest any changes to your job description, please type them here.
* **Recommended Job**: If you have any recommendations for job opportunities, please enter them here.
* **Job Suggestions**: Any suggestions for job opportunities you would like to see in the company.
* **Next Steps**: Please select the next steps you would like the company to take (e.g. Career Development, Career Advancement, etc.).
