# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of restaurant employees, providing a structured assessment of their work.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2.  Enter the name of the employee's supervisor in the `supervisor` field.
3.  Enter the date of the evaluation in the `date` field using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  Provide a brief description of the evaluation period in the `evaluation_period` field.
5.  Give an assessment of the employee's performance in the `performance` field.
6.  Provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement in the `feedback` field.
7.  Suggest any recommendations for future development in the `recommendations` field.
8.  Select the type of evaluation using the dropdown menu in the `evaluation_type` field (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
9.  Enter a numerical score out of 100 for the employee's performance in the `score` field.
10.  Enter the employee's ID in the `employee_id` field.
11.  Enter the due date for the evaluation in the `evaluation_due_date` field.
12.  Enter the name of the person who submitted the evaluation in the `submitted_by` field.
13.  Enter the date and time the evaluation was submitted in the `submitted_on` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
*   **supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
*   **date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the evaluation using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **evaluation_period** (`evaluation_period`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the evaluation period.
*   **performance** (`performance`, text, required: false)
	+ Assess the employee's performance (e.g., strengths, weaknesses).
*   **feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide feedback or suggestions for improvement.
*   **recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required: false)
	+ Suggest any recommendations for future development.
*   **evaluation_type** (`evaluation_type`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the type of evaluation (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **score** (`score`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter a numerical score out of 100 for the employee's performance.
*   **employee_id** (`employee_id`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the employee's ID.
*   **evaluation_due_date** (`evaluation_due_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the due date for the evaluation.
*   **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the person who submitted the evaluation.
*   **submitted_on** (`submitted_on`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the date and time the evaluation was submitted.
