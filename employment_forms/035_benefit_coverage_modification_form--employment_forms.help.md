# Benefit Coverage Modification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Benefit Coverage Modification Form is used to request changes to an employee's benefit coverage details. This includes modifying their health, dental, or life insurance plans, adding or removing dependents, and requesting changes to their coverage levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the employee information section, which includes their name, ID, and email address.
2. Enter the date of the life event that prompted the change (if applicable).
3. Select the reason for the change from the provided options.
4. Choose the effective date for the change.
5. Select the new coverage changes desired, including health, dental, and life insurance.
6. If adding new dependents, enter their names and relationships.
7. Finally, review and agree to the changes and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
- **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number (optional).
- **Department**: Enter your department name (optional).
- **Employment Status**: Select your employment status (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary).
- **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
- **Requested Effective Date**: Enter the date you want the changes to take effect.
- **Reason for Change**: Select the reason for the change from the provided options.
- **Date of Life Event**: Enter the date of the life event that prompted the change (if applicable).
- **Current Health Plan**: Select the current health insurance plan you have.
- **Current Dental Coverage**: Select whether you currently have dental coverage.
- **Current Vision Coverage**: Select whether you currently have vision coverage.
- **Current Life Insurance**: Select whether you currently have life insurance.
- **Health Plan Change**: Select the new health insurance plan you desire.
- **New Health Plan**: Enter the new health insurance plan you want to choose (if not choosing to switch plans).
- **Add Dental Coverage**: Select whether you want to add dental coverage.
- **Add Vision**: Select whether you want to add vision coverage.
- **Add Life Insurance**: Select whether you want to add life insurance.
- **Dependent Changes**: Select whether you are adding or removing dependents.
- **Dependent Details**: Enter the names and relationships of new dependents (if adding dependents).
- **I Agree**: Acknowledge that you agree to the changes.
- **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you submit this form.
