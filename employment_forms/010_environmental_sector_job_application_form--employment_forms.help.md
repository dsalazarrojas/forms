# Environmental Sector Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants in the environmental sector. It is intended to help hiring managers and recruiters gather relevant details about the applicant's personal background, job preferences, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your job application details in the **Job Application** field.
2. Provide your **Personal Details**, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your **Contact Details**, such as your address and any other relevant contact information.
4. Describe your **Job Details**, including the type of job you are applying for.
5. Attach a **Resume Attachment**, if applicable.
6. Provide **Work Experience**, highlighting relevant work history.
7. List your **References**, if applicable.
8. Describe your **Education Background**, including any relevant education or certifications.
9. List your **Skills**, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
10. Select your preferred **Job Sector**, such as Conservation, Environmental Consulting, or Sustainability.
11. Choose your desired **Job Type**, such as Full-time, Part-time, Temporary, or Contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application**: Enter a brief description of your job application, including your job title, industry, and relevant experience.
* **Personal Details**: Provide your name, email, and phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your mailing address, social media profiles, or any other contact information you would like to share.
* **Job Details**: Describe the job you are applying for, including the type of job, industry, and desired salary range.
* **Resume Attachment**: Attach your resume or CV as a PDF or JPEG file to support your application.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience in the environmental sector, including job titles, durations, and responsibilities.
* **References**: List two or three professional references, including their name, title, company, and contact information.
* **Education Background**: Describe your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant coursework.
* **Skills**: List your skills or qualifications, such as proficiency in a programming language, software, or industry-specific tools.
* **Job Sector**: Select the sector you are applying for, such as Conservation, Environmental Consulting, or Sustainability.
* **Job Type**: Choose your desired job type, such as Full-time, Part-time, Temporary, or Contract.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach a clear and concise resume or CV to support your application.
* Use specific examples to describe your work experience and skills.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
