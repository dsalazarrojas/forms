# Tech Industry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Industry Job Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants in the tech industry. This form helps us understand your work experience, education, skills, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself.
* Use the provided options to select relevant answers for the select_one and select_multiple fields.
* For the date fields (Available Start Date and Expected Termination Date), use the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* For the number field (Salary), enter a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form**: A brief description of your job application.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on any company communication.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience in the field of tech industry.
* **Education Background**: Mention your educational background relevant to the tech industry.
* **Skills**: Select all the skills you possess that are relevant to the tech industry.
* **Job Position**: Select if you are looking for a job position.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Desired Location**: Mention the location where you would like to work.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Expected Termination Date**: Enter the date you expect to terminate your contract.
* **Salary**: Enter the salary you expect to be paid.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type Status**: Select if you have any job type status (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Job Location Status**: Select the status of your job location (Yes or No).
* **Job Status**: Select the status of the job you are applying for (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Description**: Describe the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description Status**: Select the status of the job description (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type Status 2**: Select the status of the job type 2 (Yes or No).
* **Job Title Status**: Select the status of the job title (Yes or No).
* **Job Location Status**: Select the status of your job location (Yes or No).
