# waitstaff_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The waitstaff registration form is used to gather essential information from waitstaff members about their availability and work preferences for scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have the necessary authorization to use this form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number for direct contact.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to start working.
* **Available End Date** (`available_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to end working.
* **Available Start Time** (`available_start_time`, time, required): Select the time you are available to start your shift.
* **Available End Time** (`available_end_time`, time, required): Select the time you are available to end your shift.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Briefly describe your availability in terms of days of the week.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Select the date you were hired.
* **Hire Time** (`hire_time`, time, required): Select the time you were hired.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Optional field for any additional comments or information.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to work from the given options.
* **Availability 3** (`availability_3`, select_multiple, required): Select the times of the day you are available to work from the given options.
* **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of shift you are comfortable working (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Rotating).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, required): Select the location where you work (Restaurant, Hotel, Event Venue).
* **Shift Start** (`shift_start`, time, required): Select the time you would like to start your shift.
* **Shift End** (`shift_end`, time, required): Select the time you would like to end your shift.
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your shift.
* **Breaks** (`breaks`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you take breaks during your shift.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you would like to take breaks.
* **Break Length** (`break_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your breaks.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work in a shift.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to work from the given options.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool you have been assigned to use during your shifts.

Note: This guide is meant to provide a general understanding of the form and its fields, however, it's always best to refer to your specific workplace policies and procedures for any specific requirements or variations.
