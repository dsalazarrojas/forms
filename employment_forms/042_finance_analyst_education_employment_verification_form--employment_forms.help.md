# Finance Analyst Education Employment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Analyst Education Employment Verification Form is designed to collect specific details about your finance analyst employment history. This form helps to verify your credentials and validate your experience in the finance industry. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to facilitate a quick and efficient verification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "candidate info" field with your name, address, and other identifying information.
2.  In the "education" field, provide information about your highest level of formal education completed.
3.  In the "finance analyst info" field, provide details about your relevant finance analyst experience, including job titles, dates, and companies worked for.
4.  In the "employment" field, describe your employment history as a finance analyst, including dates of employment and companies worked for.
5.  In the "contact info" field, provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further verification.
6.  Finally, confirm your submission by typing "yes" in the "confirm submit" field and click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **candidate info** (text, required): This field is used to gather your identifying information, such as name and address. Please provide your full name and contact information so we can reach out to you for further verification.
* **education** (text, required): In this field, describe your highest level of formal education, such as a Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant field.
* **finance analyst info** (text, required): Describe your relevant finance analyst experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and companies worked for.
* **employment** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of your employment history as a finance analyst, including dates of employment and companies worked for.
* **contact info** (text, required): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further verification.
* **submit** (text, required): Type "yes" to confirm your submission and finalize your verification.
* **confirm submit** (text, required): Type "yes" to confirm that the information you provided is accurate and complete.
