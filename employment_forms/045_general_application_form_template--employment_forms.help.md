# General Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Application Form Template is designed to collect basic information from applicants when applying for a job or any other position. It is a general template that can be used by multiple companies and hiring managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Form** field, which is where you can provide a brief description about yourself and the job you are applying for.
2. Enter your **Name**, which is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Indicate whether you are looking for a **Position Sought**, which is whether or not you are looking for a job at this time.
4. Enter your **Contact No**, which is your contact phone number.
5. Enter your **Email**, which is your personal or professional email address.
6. If you have any work experience, you may want to upload or paste your **Resume** and **Cover Letter**, which should be a brief summary of your work experience and qualifications.
7. Select your **Skills**, which is a list of your relevant skills.
8. If you have work experience, enter your **Work Experience**, which is a date of when you started and ended your employment.
9. Enter your **Education**, which is a brief description of your education and qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required/optional): A brief description about yourself and the job you are applying for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Position Sought** (`position_sought`, select_one, required/optional): A boolean indicating whether you are looking for a job at this time.
* **Contact No** (`contact_no`, text, required/optional): Your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Your personal or professional email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required/optional): A brief summary of your work experience and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your education and qualifications.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required/optional): A list of your relevant skills.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, date, required/optional): A date of when you started and ended employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your education and qualifications.
