# Recruitment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to provide information about themselves in order to be considered for a job opportunity. It gathers information about the applicant's personal details, professional experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field is required.
4. Select your **Job Title** from the list in the "Job Title" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. This is not a required field.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is a required field.
7. Attach your **Resume** document to the "Resume" field. This field is not required.
8. Describe your **Job Description** in the "Job Description" field. This is a required field.
9. Provide a brief overview of your **Work History** in the "Work History" field. This is a required field.
10. Describe your **Education** background in the "Education" field. This is a required field.
11. Select your level of **Job Satisfaction** from the "Job Satisfaction" field. This is a required field.
12. Select all the **Available Languages** you are proficient in from the "Available Languages" field. This is a required field.
13. Select all the **Available Technologies** you are skilled in from the "Available Technologies" field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (text, required): Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Job Title** (select_one, required): Select the job title you are applying for from the list.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth if you want to provide it.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume** (note, optional): Attach a document containing your resume.
* **Job Description** (text, required): Describe the job you are applying for and how you fit the role.
* **Work History** (text, required): Provide a brief overview of your work experience and achievements.
* **Education** (text, required): Describe your educational background and any relevant qualifications.
* **Job Satisfaction** (select_one, required): Select how satisfied you are with your current job.
* **Available Languages** (select_multiple, required): Select all the languages you are proficient in.
* **Available Technologies** (select_multiple, required): Select all the technologies you are skilled in.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact us.
