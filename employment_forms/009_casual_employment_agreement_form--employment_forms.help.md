# Casual Employment Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Casual Employment Agreement Form is a document used to establish the terms and conditions of casual employment between an employer and an employee. It outlines the basic terms of the employment, including pay, hours, and notice periods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the instructions and terms carefully before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure you have the necessary approvals and agreements before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registered Business Name (Employer)** (`employer_name`, required): The name of the business or entity employing the individual.
* **Full Legal Name (Employee)** (`employee_name`, required): The full name of the casual employee, as per their identification documents.
* **Residential Address** (`employee_address`, required): The address where the employee resides.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, required): The job title or role the employee will be undertaking.
* **Commencement Date** (`start_date`, required): The date the casual employment commences.
* **Base Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`, required): The rate of pay the employee will receive per hour, before taxes and other deductions.
* **Casual Loading Percentage** (`casual_loading`, required): A percentage representing the loading applied to the employee's base rate for casual work.
* **Payment Frequency** (`pay_cycle`, required): The frequency at which the employee will be paid, such as weekly or monthly.
* **I Understand and Acknowledge** (`casual_definition_ack`, required): The employee acknowledges their understanding of the casual employment nature.
* **Shift Allocation Process** (`shift_offer_process`, required): How shifts are allocated to the employee, e.g. SMS, App, or Email.
* **Minimum Notice Required for Canceling a Shift** (`notice_period`, required): The minimum amount of notice the employee must give to cancel a shift.
* **Notice Period for Termination** (`termination_notice`, required): The period of notice required for terminating the agreement, by either party.
* **Electronic Signature (Full Name)** (`signed_by_name`, required): The full name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Date of Signing** (`signing_date`, required): The date the agreement is signed.

Please ensure you understand and have completed all the required fields before submitting the form.
