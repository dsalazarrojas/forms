# Job Offer Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Job Offer Approval Form" is a document used for review and approval of job offers. It serves as a tool for departments and HR to verify job details and ensure that all necessary steps have been taken before proceeding with the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant job offer details, including job title, department, job type, and start date.
2.  Enter the job description, salary, salary range, and job location.
3.  Select the job status (Open or Closed) and job level (Entry Level, Mid Level, or Senior).
4.  Enter any additional job type details and reporting information.
5.  Provide comments for HR review and departmental review.
6.  Specify whether approval is required and the current approval status.
7.  Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job being offered.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department the job belongs to.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of job, whether it's Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the job.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the job.
*   **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required): Enter the salary of the job.
*   **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `text`, required): Enter the salary range of the job.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the job.
*   **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the job is Open or Closed.
*   **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the level of the job, whether it's Entry Level, Mid Level, or Senior.
*   **Job Type Details** (`job_type_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional details about the job type.
*   **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, `text`, required): Enter the reporting line for the job.
*   **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `text`, required): Enter the reporting manager's name.
*   **Job Openings** (`job_openings`, `text`, required): Enter the number of job openings.
*   **Job Opening Comments** (`job_opening_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments about the job openings.
*   **Departmental Review Comments** (`departmental_review_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments from the departmental review.
*   **HR Review Comments** (`hr_review_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments from the HR review.
*   **Approval Required** (`approval_required`, `select_one`, required): Specify whether approval is required for the job offer.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `text`, required): Enter the current approval status.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about the job offer.
*   **Job Number** (`job_number`, `text`, required): Enter the job number.
*   **Job ID** (`job_id`, `text`, required): Enter the job ID.
*   **Approval Form** (`approval_form`, `text`, required): Enter the approval form for the job offer. 

## Tips
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary details have been entered.
*   Check the form for errors before submitting.
*   Use the form as a tool for review and approval, not for job posting or candidate application. 

This help guide provides a clear and concise explanation of the "Job Offer Approval Form" purpose and how to complete it, along with a field-by-field explanation of each section. By following these steps and guidelines, users can effectively use the form to ensure accurate and efficient job offer approvals.
