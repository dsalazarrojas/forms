# Renewable Energy Solar Engineer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your background, experience, and skills as a candidate for a renewable energy solar engineer position. Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to help us assess your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting, especially your contact details (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Email address and Phone number, including the country code if necessary.
4. Select the number of years of experience you have in the field of renewable energy and solar engineering.
5. Choose the relevant Project Background and Project Location options for your experience.
6. List the skills and qualifications you have acquired during your education.
7. Describe your previous projects and work history, if applicable.
8. Select any additional skills you possess that are relevant to the position.
9. Specify your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
10. Describe the project you are applying for, including its duration and start and end dates, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number including the country code, e.g., `1-234-567-8901`.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, Number, Required): Enter the number of years you have worked in the field of renewable energy and solar engineering.
* **Project Background** (`project_background`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose relevant options for your project background, e.g., residential, commercial, or industrial solar projects.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, Note, Optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience, focusing on relevant projects and achievements.
* **Skills** (`skills`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the skills and qualifications you possess that are relevant to the position, e.g., programming languages, software tools, or certifications.
* **Education** (`education`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the degrees and institutions relevant to your education and solar engineering background.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the project you are applying for, focusing on its objectives, technologies, and outcomes.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the locations where you have worked or the project was situated.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the project in months or years.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the project.
