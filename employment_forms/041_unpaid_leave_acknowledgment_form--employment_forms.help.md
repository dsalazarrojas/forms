# Unpaid Leave Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Unpaid Leave Acknowledgment Form is used to document an employee's request for unpaid leave, including the dates of the leave and the reason for it. The form is used by both the employee and the manager to ensure that all parties are aware of the details of the leave.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to include all required fields.
2. The employee should enter their name, the start and end dates of the leave, and the reason for the leave (if applicable).
3. The employee should select the type of leave they are taking (e.g. Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, etc.).
4. The employee should enter the number of days they will be taking off.
5. The employee should enter the date they submitted the form.
6. The employee should sign the form to acknowledge that they have submitted it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): If you are taking leave for a reason other than vacation, please enter the reason here.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are taking (e.g. Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, etc.).
* **Number Of Days Taken** (`number_of_days_taken`): Enter the number of days you will be taking off.
* **Leave Approved By** (`leave_approved_by`): If your leave has been approved, enter the name of the person who approved it.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`): If your leave has been approved, enter the date it was approved.
* **Approved By Name** (`approved_by_name`): If your leave has been approved, enter the name of the person who approved it.
* **Notes** (`notes`): If you have any additional information or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): If you want to be reached by phone, enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): If you want to be reached by email, enter your email address.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign the form to acknowledge that you have submitted it.
* **Leave Type Approved** (`leave_type_approved`): If your leave has been approved, select the type of leave that was approved.
* **Date Approved Approved** (`date_approved_approved`): If your leave has been approved, enter the date it was approved.
* **Leave Type Approved Date** (`leave_type_approved_date`): If your leave has been approved, enter the date it was approved.
* **Leave Approved** (`leave_approved`): Select whether your leave has been approved or not.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`): Sign the form to acknowledge that you have received it.
* **Reason For Leave Approved** (`reason_for_leave_approved`): If your leave has been approved, enter the reason for it.
* **Notes Approved** (`notes_approved`): If you have any additional information or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Submission Date Approved** (`submission_date_approved`): If you have approved this form, enter the date you did so.
* **Submitted By Approved** (`submitted_by_approved`): If you have approved this form, enter your name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): If you have any notes or concerns, please enter them here.
