# personnel_action_notice_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **personnel_action_notice_form** is used to record and track personnel actions, such as new hires, changes in job title, salary, status updates, or manager approvals. This form helps to keep track of important personnel information and ensure that necessary notifications are sent to the relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.
2. Select the relevant action from the options provided on the new hire page.
3. Enter the required information for the selected action.
4. Review and update the status of the employee accordingly.
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Hire (`new_hire`, date, required: false)
* The **New Hire** field is used to record the date of the new hire.

### Job Title (`job_title`, text, required: false)
* The **Job Title** field is used to record the job title of the new hire or any changes made to the job title.

### Employee Salary (`employee_salary`, number, required: false)
* The **Employee Salary** field is used to record the employee's salary or any updates made to their salary.

### Status (`status`, select multiple, required: false)
* The **Status** field is used to record the current status of the employee. Options include:
	+ Active: The employee is actively working with the company.
	+ Inactive: The employee is no longer with the company.

### Manager Approval (`manager_approval`, select one, required: false)
* The **Manager Approval** field is used to record the approval status of the action. Options include:
	+ Approve: The manager has approved the action.
	+ Decline: The manager has declined the action.
	+ Refer to Manager: The manager requires further review or input before approval or decline.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review and update the form before submitting it for review.
* Use the form for internal purposes only.
* Submit the form for review and approval by authorized personnel.
