# Employee Meal Break Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track employee meal breaks, ensuring that employees are following the company's meal break policy and taking required breaks to rest and recharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Name field with your official employee name.
2. Select your job title from the Job Title dropdown menu.
3. Choose your department from the Department dropdown menu.
4. Enter the duration of your meal break in the Meal Break Duration field.
5. Enter the start and end times of your meal break in the Start Time and End Time fields, respectively.
6. Select the reason for your meal break from the Reason for Break dropdown menu.
7. Check if you are following the company's Meal Break Policy. If yes, select 'True', if no, select 'False'.
8. Sign and date your form to acknowledge that you have taken a meal break.
9. Your manager will also need to sign and date the form to confirm that you took the meal break.
10. If your department manager is not your direct manager, they will also sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as an employee of the company.
* **Job Title**: Select your official job title from the dropdown menu. This will help track your role in the company.
* **Department**: Choose the department you belong to from the dropdown menu. This will help track your work responsibilities.
* **Meal Break Duration**: Enter the duration of your meal break, which is usually 30-60 minutes.
* **Break Schedule**: Enter the date and time of your meal break.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your meal break.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your meal break.
* **Reason for Break**: Select the reason for your meal break from the dropdown menu. You can choose 'Short break', 'Long break', or 'Other (please specify)'.
* **Meal Break Policy**: Check if you are following the company's meal break policy. If yes, select 'True', if no, select 'False'.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date your form to acknowledge that you have taken a meal break.
* **Manager Signature**: Your manager will sign and date the form to confirm that you took the meal break.
* **Department Manager Signature**: If your department manager is not your direct manager, they will also sign and date the form.
* **Date (4)**: Enter the current date for your reference.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form correctly and accurately, as this will help track your meal breaks and ensure you are following company policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* Remember to sign and date the form after each meal break, and have your manager sign and date the form as well.
