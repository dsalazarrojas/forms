# COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document employee consent and medical information to facilitate safe and proper administration of the COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Answer questions about your medical history, including any medical conditions, allergies, and current medications.
3. Confirm your understanding of the benefits and risks of the COVID-19 vaccine.
4. Sign the form to indicate your consent to receive the vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employment records.
* **Date of Birth** (2, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Employee ID** (3, Text, Optional): Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Department** (4, Text, Optional): Enter your department name.
* **Email Address** (5, Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (6, Text, Required): Enter your work phone number.
* **I understand the benefits and risks of COVID-19 vaccination** (7, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you understand the benefits and risks of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Do you have any serious medical conditions** (8, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you have any serious medical conditions that might affect vaccine safety.
* **Known Allergies** (9, Text, Optional): List any known allergies, especially to vaccine components.
* **Current Medications** (10, Text, Optional): List any current medications that might interact with the vaccine.
* **Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding** (11, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you are currently pregnant or breastfeeding.
* **Other vaccines received in past 14 days** (12, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you have received any other vaccines in the past 14 days.
* **Had COVID-19 infection in past 3 months** (13, Select One, Optional): Choose 'True' if you have had COVID-19 in the past 3 months.
* **Currently experiencing fever or illness** (14, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you are currently experiencing fever or illness.
* **Previous adverse vaccine reactions** (15, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if you have had previous adverse vaccine reactions.
* **Describe Previous Reaction** (16, Text, Optional): Describe any previous adverse reactions.
* **All my questions about COVID-19 vaccination have been answered** (17, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' if all your questions have been answered.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (18, Select One, Required): Choose 'True' for consent.
* **Electronic Signature** (19, Text, Required): Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Date Signed** (20, Date, Required): Enter the date you signed the form in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
