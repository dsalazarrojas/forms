# Equipment Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the equipment release process, which is used to release company equipment from an employee's possession after they have borrowed it for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your employee ID.
2. Select the type of equipment you are releasing. This will update the equipment status and options for subsequent fields.
3. Enter the start date you received the equipment.
4. Enter the date you are returning the equipment.
5. Provide a reason for why you are releasing the equipment.
6. Enter any comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
7. If you are an employee, sign and date the form.
8. If you are a manager, sign and date the form to confirm the release.
9. Enter your department (if applicable).
10. Enter your name and email address (if you are the manager).
11. Enter your phone number (if you are the manager).
12. Enter any additional comments or notes from the manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1): Enter your employee ID as assigned by HR.
* **Equipment ID** (2): Select the type of equipment you are releasing (e.g. Computer, Smartphone, Laptop).
* **Start Date** (3): Enter the date you received the equipment.
* **Return Date** (4): Enter the date you are returning the equipment.
* **Reason for Equipment Borrow** (5): Provide a brief explanation for why you borrowed the equipment.
* **Employee Signature** (6): This is for the employee's electronic signature.
* **Manager Signature** (7): This is for the manager's electronic signature.
* **Department** (8): If you are in a specific department (e.g. Sales, Marketing, Other).
* **Manager Name** (9): Enter the name of the manager who is signing off on this release.
* **Manager Email** (10): Enter the email address of the manager.
* **Manager Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Manager Note** (12): Enter any additional comments or notes from the manager.
* **Employee Name** (13): Enter your name (if you are the employee).
* **Employee Email** (14): Enter your email address (if you are the employee).
* **Employee Phone** (15): Enter your phone number (if you are the employee).
* **Employee Note** (16): Enter any additional comments or notes from the employee.
* **Equipment Status** (17): Select the status of the equipment (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Status Note** (18): Enter any comments or notes about the equipment's status.
* **Equipment Condition** (19): Select the condition of the equipment (e.g. Good, Bad).
* **Condition Note** (20): Enter any comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
* **Employee Approved** (21): Confirm that the employee has approved the release.
* **Approved By** (22): Select the level of approval (e.g. Manager, Director).
* **Approved Date** (23): Enter the date of approval.
* **Approved By Name** (24): Enter the name of who approved the release.
* **Approved By Email** (25): Enter the email address of the approver.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use accurate and clear communication to ensure a smooth equipment release process.
* Review and update the equipment status and condition regularly to keep track of company assets.
