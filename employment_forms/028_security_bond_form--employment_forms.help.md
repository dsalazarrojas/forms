# Security Bond Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Bond Form is a tool for companies to submit their security bond details. It is used to gather information about the company's name, address, and employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Fill out the "Address" field with your company's address.
3. Enter the city of your company's location in the "City" field.
4. Enter your company's postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
5. Provide your name in the "Employee Name" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select the start date of your employment with the company in the "Start Date" field.
8. Select the end date of your employment with the company in the "End Date" field.
9. Enter the amount of money paid to you each pay period in the "Pay Amount" field.
10. Enter the total bond amount in the "Bond Amount" field.
11. Enter the frequency of your pay in the "Pay Frequency" field.
11. Select the last date you were paid in the "Last Pay Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter your company's city.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, required): Enter your company's postal code.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Select the start date of your employment.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Select the end date of your employment.
* **Pay Amount** (`Pay Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of money paid to you each pay period.
* **Bond Amount** (`Bond Amount`, number, required): Enter the total bond amount.
* **Pay Frequency** (`Pay Frequency`, text, required): Enter the frequency of your pay.
* **Last Pay Date** (`Last Pay Date`, date, required): Select the last date you were paid.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you need help, contact our support team.
- The form will be reviewed for completeness and accuracy.
- You will receive a confirmation email once the form is submitted.
