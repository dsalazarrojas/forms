# Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for drivers to submit their applications for a job position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your basic information (Name, Address, Phone Number, Email)
2. Provide your employment experience and status
3. Answer questions about your vehicle and insurance details
4. Confirm your availability and skills
5. List your referees
6. Select your employment status and job satisfaction
7. Confirm your salary and job type

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your residential address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have experience as a driver.
- **License Number** (`license_number`, number, optional): Enter your driver's license number.
- **Job Position** (`job_position`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you are looking for a job.
- **Education** (`education`, select_one, optional): Indicate your education level.
- **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your availability for work.
- **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): List your relevant skills.
- **Referees** (`referees`, select_multiple, optional): List 2 referees for job verification.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Confirm your current employment status.
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Indicate your job satisfaction level.
- **Salary** (`salary`, select_one, optional): Confirm your salary expectations.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job you are applying for.
- **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the types of vehicles you have experience with.
- **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have a registered vehicle.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider** (`vehicle_insurance_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your vehicle insurance provider.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider License** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_license`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle insurance provider's license number.
- **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle registration number.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider Name** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle insurance provider's name.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider Address** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_address`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle insurance provider's address.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider Phone** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle insurance provider's phone number.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider Licence** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_licence`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle insurance provider's license number (note: this field has a different label, but same purpose as `vehicle_insurance_provider_license`).
