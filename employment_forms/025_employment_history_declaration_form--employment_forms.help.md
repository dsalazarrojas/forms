# Employment History Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your employment history, including job details, employment status, and work-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employment form section where you'll provide information about your job details.
2. Fill in the job details section with relevant information about your current or past job, such as job title, employer name, address, phone number, and end date.
3. Provide your employment status (active or inactive) and choose the start date and month for your current or most recent job.
4. Select the start year from the provided options for your current or most recent job.
5. Enter the total hours worked for the job or most recent job.
6. Fill in the employer's name, address, city, state, and zip for your current or most recent job.
7. Enter your phone number for your current or most recent job.
8. Select the end date for the job or most recent job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employment Form Section**: A section where you'll provide information about your current or past job.
* **Job Details**: Fill in relevant information about your current or past job.
* **Employment Status**: Select whether you are currently employed (active) or not employed (inactive).
* **Start Date**: Choose the start date of your current or most recent job.
* **Start Month**: Choose the start month of your current or most recent job.
* **Start Year**: Select the start year of your current or most recent job.
* **Hours**: Enter the total hours worked for your current or most recent job.
* **Employer**: Enter the name of your current or past employer.
* **Address**: Enter the address of your current or past employer.
* **City**: Enter the city of your current or past employer.
* **State**: Enter the state of your current or past employer.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip of your current or past employer.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your current or past employer.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of your current or most recent job.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure you're providing information about your current or most recent job.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your HR department or related personnel for assistance.
