# Candidate Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candidate Experience Survey aims to collect feedback from candidates about their experience throughout our hiring process. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to provide a better experience for future candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your genuine experience.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Skip any questions that don't apply to your situation.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Position(s) Applied For** (`position_applied`, text, required): Type the title of the job position you applied for.
* **Date of Application** (`application_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the date you applied.
* **At which stage of the hiring process are you?** (`hiring_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the current stage of the hiring process you are at.
* **How easy was the application process?** (`application_ease`, select_one, required): Rate the difficulty of the application process.
* **Was the application length appropriate?** (`application_length`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options regarding the application length.
* **How clear were the job requirements?** (`clarity_of_requirements`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the job requirements.
* **How was the interview scheduling experience?** (`interview_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with the interview scheduling.
* **How would you rate the professionalism of the interviewers?** (`interviewer_professionalism`, select_one, optional): Rate the professionalism of the interviewers.
* **Were interviewers well-prepared?** (`interview_preparation`, select_one, optional): Select one regarding the interviewers' preparation.
* **How frequent was communication from the hiring team?** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, required): Rate the frequency of communication from the hiring team.
* **Did you receive any feedback about your application?** (`feedback_provided`, select_one, required): Select one regarding receiving feedback.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your candidate experience?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the candidate experience.
* **Would you recommend our company as a good place to work?** (`recommend_company`, select_one, required): Select one regarding recommending our company as a good place to work.
* **What could we improve about our hiring process?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **What did you like best about our hiring process?** (`positive_comments`, text, optional): Share positive feedback about our hiring process.
* **May we contact you for follow-up?** (`contact_permission`, select_one, optional): Select one regarding contact permission.

## Tips
1. Please answer each question based on your genuine experience.
2. Be as detailed as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the most accurate options for each question.
4. Don't hesitate to provide any comments or suggestions you might have.
