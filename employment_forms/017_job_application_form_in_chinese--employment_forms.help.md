# Job Application Form In Chinese - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from job applicants in order to assess their suitability for a job position. This form is intended for job seekers to provide details about themselves, their work experience, education background, and other relevant qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your Applicant Information, including your name, address, and contact details.
2. Next, describe your Job Title and work experience in the provided text fields.
3. Please provide a brief overview of your Education Background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
4. In the Job Description field, describe the job you are applying for and why you are a good fit.
5. List your Language Skills, including any languages you are proficient in.
6. Finally, upload your Resume and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter your name, address, and contact details.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Please enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your previous work experience, including job responsibilities and achievements.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required: false): Provide a brief overview of your educational background, including degrees and certifications.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Describe the job you are applying for and why you are a good fit.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, text, required: false): List the languages you are proficient in, including reading, writing, and speaking skills.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, required: false): Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Job Title (2)** (`job_title_2`, text, required: false): Please enter the job title of the job you are applying for.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to send your application.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all fields correctly and truthfully.
* Proofread your application before submitting it to ensure there are no errors.
* You can upload your resume in PDF or Word format to make it easy for the hiring manager to review.
