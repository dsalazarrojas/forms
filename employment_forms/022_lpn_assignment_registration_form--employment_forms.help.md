# LPN Assignment Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LPN Assignment Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from LPNs (Licensed Practical Nurses) for their assignments. This form is used to gather necessary details for internal registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Information" page, providing your name and any other relevant details as required.
2. Move on to the "LPN Information" page, where you will enter your LPN details.
3. On the "Placement Preferences" page, specify your assignment preferences.
4. In the "Assignment Details" page, enter any additional details about your assignment.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Input)** (`User Information`, `text`, **required**): This page is where you will enter your name and any other relevant user information.
* **LPN Information (LPN Info)** (`LPN Information`, `text`, **optional**): This is an optional page where you can provide additional LPN details.
* **Placement Preferences (Placement Preferences)** (`Placement Preferences`, `text`, **required**): This page allows you to specify your assignment preferences.
* **Assignment Details (Assignment Details)** (`Assignment Details`, `text`, **optional**): This page is where you can enter any additional details about your assignment.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`Submit`, `text`, **required**): Click this button to complete your registration.
