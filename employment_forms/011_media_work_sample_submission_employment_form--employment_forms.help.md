# Media Work Sample Submission Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help hiring managers and staff evaluate a candidate's work samples and assess their fit for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the job application section with any relevant job application information.
2. Upload any relevant work samples that demonstrate your skills and experience.
3. Provide notes for review and feedback in the review section.
4. Select the relevant newsroom agencies or content teams associated with your work samples.
5. Choose the tool or platform used for your work samples.
6. Select the candidate screening status for your work samples.
7. Indicate whether you collected data or have any other relevant information to share.
8. Fill out the media section with any relevant details.
9. Provide any additional information about the work samples or your experience.
10. Identify the contact person responsible for uploading and reviewing the samples.
11. Enter any additional contact information for the contact person.
12. Set the assigned date for the work samples.
13. Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
14. Add any final notes about the work samples.
15. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (Job Application): This field is for providing any relevant job application information, such as job title, date applied, or company name.
* **Uploaded Work Samples** (Uploaded Work Samples): Select up to 2 work samples that demonstrate your skills and experience.
* **Review** (Review): Leave notes for our review team to provide feedback and assessment on your work samples.
* **Newsroom Agencies Or Content Teams** (Newsroom Agencies Or Content Teams): Select the newsroom agencies or content teams associated with your work samples.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool): Choose the tool or platform used for your work samples.
* **Candidate Screening** (Candidate Screening): Select the candidate screening status for your work samples.
* **Data Collection** (Data Collection): Indicate whether you collected data or have any other relevant information to share.
* **Media** (Media): Enter any relevant media details, such as images or videos.
* **Other** (Other): Enter any additional information about your work samples or experience.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person): Identify the person responsible for uploading and reviewing the samples.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info): Enter any additional contact information for the contact person.
* **Assigned Date** (Assigned Date): Set the assigned date for the work samples.
* **Completed** (Completed): Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any final notes about the work samples.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
