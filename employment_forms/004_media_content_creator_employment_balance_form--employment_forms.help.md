# media_content_creator_employment_balance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "media_content_creator_employment_balance_form" is a form used to collect employment details of a media content creator. This form is essential for tracking and evaluating a content creator's work-life balance and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Creator Name** in the field "Creator Name". This field is required.
2. Enter the **Job Title** in the field "Job Title". This field is also required.
3. Enter the number of **Work Hours** spent on content creation. This field is required.
4. Optionally, provide a note about your **Work Hours** (if needed).
5. Select your **Work Life Balance** by choosing between "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list. This field is required.
6. If you'd like to provide further explanation about your **Work Life Balance**, enter it in the field "Work Life Balance Note". This field is optional.
7. Enter your **Creator Email** address. This field is required.
8. Enter your **Creator Phone** number. This field is required.
9. Optionally, provide information about your **Creator Workload**. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Name**: Enter your full name as a content creator.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as a content creator.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours spent on content creation.
* **Work Hours Note**: Provide any additional information about your work hours.
* **Work Life Balance**: Select "Yes" if you feel you have a good balance between work and life, or "No" if you do not.
* **Work Life Balance Note**: Enter any further explanation about your work-life balance.
* **Creator Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Creator Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Creator Workload**: If you'd like to provide more details about your current workload, enter it here.
