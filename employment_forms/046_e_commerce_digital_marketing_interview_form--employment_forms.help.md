# E Commerce Digital Marketing Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your background and experience in digital marketing for the purpose of an interview or assessment. It's a tool to help the hiring team get to know your qualifications and fit for a role in digital marketing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in all required fields. You can usually find the required fields by looking for the label with the "Required" tag.
* Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
* Type in your answers for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your background in Digital Marketing?**
	+ This field is a text field where you can describe your background in digital marketing.
* **Which e-commerce channels have you worked with?**
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with two options: "Yes" and "No". Please select "Yes" if you have experience with e-commerce channels.
* **What is your experience level in Digital Marketing?**
	+ This field is a text field where you can describe your experience level in digital marketing.
* **What is your current Job Title?**
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with options: Amazon Seller, Brand Manager, Content Manager, E-commerce Manager, Marketing Manager, and Other. Please select the most relevant option for your current position.
* **Company name**
	+ This field is for you to enter the name of your current or previous company where you have experience in digital marketing.
* **What is your experience in Digital Marketing (Years)?**
	+ This field is for you to describe your experience in digital marketing, including the number of years you have been working in the field.
* **What are your key skills?**
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with options: Data Analysis, E-commerce, Email Marketing, Marketing Strategy, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Social Media, Web Analytics, and Other. Please select all that apply to your skills.
* **What is your educational background?**
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with options: Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, and Other. Please select the most relevant option for your educational background.
* **What is your work experience in the field of Digital Marketing?**
	+ This field is a number field where you need to enter the number of years you have been working in digital marketing.
