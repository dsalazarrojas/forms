# Recruitment Advertisement Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Advertisement Requisition Form is used to create and manage job postings for recruitment purposes. It captures various details about the job, including job title, description, department, job category, required skills, pay rate, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Set the job posting status to "Draft" or "Published" depending on your intention.
2. Fill in the job title and description.
3. Select the department and job category.
4. Choose the required skills and pay rate.
5. Fill in the working hours, job type, required travel, job location, and job status.
6. Select the job posting end and start dates.
7. Set the job posting status, type, and source.
8. Add any required documents and job posting notes.
9. Add tags and job posting tags.
10. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the job posting.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Describe the job in detail, including responsibilities and requirements.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department that the job belongs to.
* **Department A** (`Department A`, select_one, optional): Select Department A.
* **Department B** (`Department B`, select_one, optional): Select Department B.
* **Department C** (`Department C`, select_one, optional): Select Department C.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple job categories that the job belongs to.
* **Category 1** (`Category 1`, select_multiple, optional): Select Category 1.
* **Category 2** (`Category 2`, select_multiple, optional): Select Category 2.
* **Category 3** (`Category 3`, select_multiple, optional): Select Category 3.
* **Required Skills** (`required_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the required skills for the job.
* **Skill 1** (`Skill 1`, select_multiple, optional): Select Skill 1.
* **Skill 2** (`Skill 2`, select_multiple, optional): Select Skill 2.
* **Skill 3** (`Skill 3`, select_multiple, optional): Select Skill 3.
* **Skill 4** (`Skill 4`, select_multiple, optional): Select Skill 4.
* **Working Hours** (`working_hours`, time, optional): Enter the working hours for the job.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, optional): Enter the pay rate for the job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the job type (Full-time or Part-time).
* **Required Travel** (`required_travel`, select_one, optional): Choose if the job requires travel (True or False).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location where the job is located.
* **Shift Details** (`shift_details`, note, optional): Add any additional shift details.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Set the job status (Active, Closed, or Pending).
* **Job Posting End Date** (`job_posting_end_date`, date, optional): Set the job posting end date.
* **Job Posting Start Date** (`job_posting_start_date`, date, optional): Set the job posting start date.
* **Job Posting Status** (`job_posting_status`, select_one, optional): Set the job posting status (Published, Draft, or In Review).
* **Job Posting Type** (`job_posting_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the job posting type (Internal or External).
* **Job Posting URL** (`job_posting_url`, text, optional): Enter the job posting URL.
* **Job Type of Contract** (`job_type_of_contract`, select_one, optional): Choose the job type of contract (Full time, Part time, or Temporary).
* **Job Category of Contract** (`job_category_of_contract`, select_one, optional): Choose the job category of contract (Permanent, Temporary, or Contract).
* **Job Category of Employment** (`job_category_of_employment`, select_one, optional): Choose the job category of employment (Regular, Temporary, or Contract).
* **Job Posting Description** (`job_posting_description`, text, optional): Add a description of the job posting.
* **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the required documents.
* **Job Posting Notes** (`job_posting_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the job posting.
* **Job Posting Tags** (`job_posting_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the job posting tags.
* **Job Posting Source** (`job_posting_source`, select_one, optional): Choose the job posting source (Source 1 or Source 2).
