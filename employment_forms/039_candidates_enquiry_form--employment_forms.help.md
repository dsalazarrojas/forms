# Candidates Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from candidates regarding their inquiries and provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, ensure you have all the necessary details ready, such as your contact information (full name, email address, and phone number), the job role you're interested in, and the reason for your enquiry.
- Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields. Ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
- For the "Reason for contacting us" field, choose the category that best describes your query.
- In the "Which role is your enquiry about" field, type in the exact job title of the role you are enquiring about.
- If you know the Job ID, please provide it to help with the enquiry.
- In "Description of enquiry", provide as much detail as possible about your question or issue so that the team can assist you effectively.
- Select "Yes, within 30 days" if you have contacted the team within the last month, "Yes, more than 30 days" if it's been longer, or "No, this is my first time" if this is your first contact.
- If applicable, mention the name of the person you previously spoke to.
- Finally, choose how you would like to be contacted back (email, phone, or SMS), select the best time of day for you to be contacted (morning, afternoon, or evening), and acknowledge your consent for your contact information to be processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`enquiry_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`enquiry_email_addr`, `email`, required): Type in your current and active email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (`enquiry_phone_addr`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will help us reach you more conveniently.
* **Current Position** (`current_job_role_enquirer`, `text`, optional): Describe your current job title or position.
* **Reason for contacting us** (`enquiry_reason_primary`, `select_one`, required): Choose a category that best describes your query (e.g., General Inquiry, Application Status Update, Technical Issue with Portal, etc.).
* **Which role is your enquiry about** (`role_of_enquiry_interest`, `text`, required): Type the exact job title of the role you're enquiring about.
* **Job ID (if known)** (`job_id_if_known`, `text`, optional): If you know the Job ID from the vacancy description, provide it here.
* **Description of enquiry** (`enquiry_description_text`, `text`, required): Give as much detail as possible about the query or issue so the team can assist you correctly.
* **Previous contact with our team** (`previous_contact_history`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, within 30 days" if you've contacted us recently, "Yes, more than 30 days" if it's been longer, or "No, this is my first time" if it's your first contact.
* **Name of person you previously spoke to** (`previous_contact_person_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, mention the name of the person you spoke to before.
* **Preferred method of response** (`response_method_pref`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred way to receive a response (email, phone, or SMS).
* **Best time to reach you** (`best_reach_time_enquirer`, `select_one`, optional): Select the best time of the day to contact you.
* **I agree to the processing of my contact information** (`contact_data_consent_enquirer`, `select_one`, required): Acceptance of your contact information for handling and follow-up purposes.
* **Date of Enquiry** (`enquiry_submission_date_field`, `date`, required): Type in the date of your current query.

This guide is designed to ensure you understand all the necessary fields and how to complete them efficiently for the best support from the hiring team.
