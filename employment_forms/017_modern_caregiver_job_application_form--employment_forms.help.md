# Modern Caregiver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modern Caregiver Job Application Form is designed for caregivers who need to provide personal and professional information in order to be considered for employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on each section and fill in the necessary information in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter all required fields marked as "Required" and make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Upload any required documents as specified.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, Required): This section is used to collect your basic contact information. Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, Required): This section is used to provide your contact information for follow-up communications. Please enter your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, Optional): This section is used to provide details about your previous work experience as a caregiver. Please enter any relevant work history, job titles, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, Required): This section is used to collect your educational background. Please enter any relevant diplomas, degrees, or certifications you have obtained.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, Required): This section is used to list any relevant skills you have as a caregiver. Please enter any relevant skills or certifications you possess, such as CPR or first aid training.
* **References** (`references`, text, Required): This section is used to provide references for your work as a caregiver. Please enter the names and contact details of at least 2 professional references.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, text, Required): This section is used to collect any relevant medical certifications or licenses you hold. Please enter any certifications or licenses related to caregiving.
* **Medical Clearances** (`medical_clearances`, text, Optional): This section is used to provide any medical clearances or authorizations you have obtained. Please enter any relevant clearances or authorizations.
* **Languages** (`languages`, text, Optional): This section is used to list any languages you speak or are proficient in. Please enter any languages you are fluent in.
* **References 2** (`references_2`, text, Optional): This section is used to provide additional references for your work as a caregiver. Please enter any additional references.
* **Medical Certifications 2** (`medical_certifications_2`, text, Optional): This section is used to collect any additional relevant medical certifications or licenses. Please enter any additional certifications or licenses.
* **Medical Credentials** (`medical_credentials`, text, Optional): This section is used to collect any relevant medical credentials or certifications. Please enter any credentials or certifications related to your medical training.
* **Medical Credentials 3** (`medical_credentials_3`, text, Optional): This section is used to collect any additional relevant medical credentials or certifications. Please enter any additional credentials or certifications.
* **Medical Credentials 4** (`medical_credentials_4`, text, Optional): This section is used to collect any additional relevant medical credentials or certifications. Please enter any additional credentials or certifications.
* **Minimalist Form** (`minimalist_form`, text, Optional): This section is used to provide a minimalist form for your personal details. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Minimalist Form 2** (`minimalist_form_2`, text, Required): This section is used to provide a minimalistic form for your work experience. Please enter your job title, work dates, and any other relevant information.
* **Minimalist Form 3** (`minimalist_form_3`, text, Required): This section is used to provide a minimalistic form for your education. Please enter your degree, diploma, or certification, and any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to upload any required documents as specified in the form.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it for review.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
