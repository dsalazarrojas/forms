# recruitment_plan_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to approve or reject a recruitment plan, ensuring a standardized process for documentation and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant option for "recruitment_process" based on whether the recruitment is internal or external.
2.  Fill in the correct "plan_name" as specified in the recruitment plan.
3.  Enter the job title and job description as per the job requirements.
4.  Input the number of positions to be filled.
5.  Select the correct "recruitment_plan_start_date" and "recruitment_plan_end_date" for the plan.
6.  Choose the correct "approval_type" based on the individual or department required for approval (Manager, Director, or HR Manager).
7.  Enter the name of the person approving the plan.
8.  Provide their email address.
9.  Select the "approval_date" and "approval_time" for the approval action.
10.  Optionally, add any comments if needed.
11.  Upload any relevant documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employment_forms** (Select Multiple, Required: No):
    - Choose "Yes" if the form is for internal recruitment or "No" for external.
* **plan_name** (Text, Required: No):
    - The name of the plan.
* **job_title** (Text, Required: No):
    - The job title for the position.
* **job_description** (Text, Required: No):
    - A description of the job and its requirements.
* **number_of_positions** (Number, Required: No):
    - The number of positions to be filled.
* **recruitment_plan_start_date** (Date, Required: No):
    - The start date of the recruitment plan.
* **recruitment_plan_end_date** (Date, Required: No):
    - The end date of the recruitment plan.
* **recruitment_process** (Select One, Required: No):
    - Choose the type of recruitment process: Internal, External, or Both.
* **approval_type** (Select One, Required: No):
    - Choose the type of approval: Manager, Director, or HR Manager.
* **manager_name** (Text, Required: No):
    - The name of the person approving the plan.
* **manager_email** (Email, Required: No):
    - The email address of the person approving the plan.
* **approval_date** (Date, Required: No):
    - The date of the approval action.
* **approval_time** (Time, Required: No):
    - The time of the approval action.
* **comments** (Text, Required: No):
    - Any comments or reasons for approval or rejection.
* **form_uploads** (Text, Required: No):
    - Optional uploads of related documents.
