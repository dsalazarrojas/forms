# CDL Driver Pre-Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CDL Driver Pre-Qualification Form is an initial screening process for commercial driver's license (CDL) drivers. This form is used to gather essential information about the driver's qualifications, including their full name, CDL number, license class type, years of commercial driving experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your commercial driving experience and qualifications.
2. Fill in your Full Name as it appears on your license.
3. Enter your Commercial Driver License (CDL) Number.
4. Specify the state that issued your CDL.
5. Select your CDL Class Type (A, B, or C).
6. List any endorsements you currently hold (e.g., T - Double/Triple Trailers, P - Passenger, etc.).
7. Enter the total years of commercial driving experience.
8. Report the number of preventable accidents and moving violations in the last 3 years.
9. Indicate if you've ever been convicted of a felony involving a motor vehicle.
10. Confirm whether you have an Air Brake Restriction (L).
11. Confirm the status of your DOT Medical Card.
12. Consent to a Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) background check.
13. Agree to undergo pre-employment drug and alcohol testing as required by DOT regulations.
14. Sign the form by entering your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (as on license)**: This is the name on your CDL license, including any middle names or suffixes (e.g., Jr., Sr.).
* **Commercial Driver License (CDL) Number**: Enter the number printed on your CDL license.
* **State of Issuance**: Specify the state that issued your CDL.
* **License Class**: Select A, B, or C to indicate the type of CDL you hold.
* **Current Endorsements**: List any endorsements you currently hold, such as T - Double/Triple Trailers, P - Passenger, etc.
* **Total Years of Commercial Driving Experience**: Enter the total number of years you've been driving commercially.
* **Number of preventable accidents in the last 3 years**: Report any preventable accidents you've been involved in within the past 3 years.
* **Number of moving violations in the last 3 years**: Report any moving violations you've received within the last 3 years.
* **Have you ever been convicted of a felony involving a motor vehicle?**: Answer "True" if you've been convicted of a felony involving a motor vehicle, "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have an Air Brake Restriction (L)?**: Confirm whether you have an Air Brake Restriction (L) on your CDL.
* **Is your DOT Medical Card current?**: Confirm the status of your DOT Medical Card.
* **I consent to a Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) background check**: Consent to a background check to verify your driving record.
* **I agree to undergo pre-employment drug and alcohol testing**: Confirm you're willing to undergo pre-employment drug and alcohol testing as required by DOT regulations.
* **Applicant Digital Signature**: Enter your digital signature to confirm your identity.

Note: Please ensure you have all the necessary information and qualifications before submitting this form.
