# part_time_employee_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part-time Employee Offboarding Form is used to collect information and feedback from part-time employees during the offboarding process. This form helps in maintaining a positive relationship with the employee and ensuring a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and fill out the requested information.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employment Form** (`employment_form`, text area, optional): This field is a note-taking section for you to provide any relevant information about the employee's employment.
* **Exit Process** (`exit_process`, text, optional): Describe the exit process and any relevant details about how the employee will exit the company.
* **Feedback and Comments** (`feedback_and_comments`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the employee's performance, work habits, or any other relevant information.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline the next steps that need to be taken after the employee's departure.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the HR representative or the person in charge of offboarding.
* This form is a tool to aid in the offboarding process, not a substitute for in-person conversations or meetings.
