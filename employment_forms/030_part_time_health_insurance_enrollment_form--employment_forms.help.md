# Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form is used to enroll or disenroll part-time employees from company-provided health insurance benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review your current benefits status and eligibility.
2. Check the "Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment" field to select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are currently receiving or eligible to receive benefits.
3. Fill out your "Employee Name," "Job Title," "Department," and "Date Hired" fields.
4. If you are leaving the company, provide the "Date Left" and "Reason Left" fields.
5. Enter your "Employee ID" if available.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are changing your job title and whether your benefits are terminating soon.
7. Select your medical, prescription, dental, vision, life, and long-term care insurance preferences for coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment**: Select "Yes" if you are currently receiving or eligible to receive benefits, or "No" if you are not.
  • This field indicates your current benefits status.
  • If you select "Yes," the form will proceed to the benefits selection fields.
  • If you select "No," you will not be able to proceed to the benefits selection fields.
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
  • This field is required to ensure accurate benefits reporting.
  • Please enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name."
- **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
  • This field is required to determine your benefits eligibility.
- **Department**: Enter your department or workgroup name.
  • This field is required to determine your benefits eligibility.
- **Date Hired**: Enter the date you were hired.
  • This field is required to determine your benefits eligibility.
  • Use the format "mm/dd/yyyy."
- **Date Left**: If you are leaving the company, enter the date you will be leaving.
  • This field is only required if you select "No" in the "Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment" field.
- **Reason Left**: Enter the reason you are leaving the company.
  • This field is only required if you select "No" in the "Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment" field.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
  • This field is optional and may be required by HR or payroll systems.
- **Job Title Change**: Select "Yes" if you are changing your job title.
  • This field indicates whether you are changing your job title.
  • If you select "Yes," you will be asked to confirm your new job title.
- **Benefits Terminating Soon?**: Select "Yes" if your benefits are terminating soon.
  • This field indicates whether your benefits are terminating soon.
  • If you select "Yes," you will be asked to provide a benefits termination date.
- **Medical Group Plan**: Select "Yes" if you want medical coverage.
  • This field indicates your preference for medical coverage.
- **Prescription Coverage**: Select "Yes" if you want prescription coverage.
  • This field indicates your preference for prescription coverage.
- **Dental Coverage**: Select "Yes" if you want dental coverage.
  • This field indicates your preference for dental coverage.
- **Vision Coverage**: Select "Yes" if you want vision coverage.
  • This field indicates your preference for vision coverage.
- **Life Insurance**: Select "Yes" if you want life insurance.
  • This field indicates your preference for life insurance.
- **Long Term Care Insurance**: Select "Yes" if you want long term care insurance.
  • This field indicates your preference for long term care insurance.

## Tips
* Please ensure accuracy when completing this form as it affects your benefits status and eligibility.
* If you have any questions or concerns, consult with your HR representative or supervisor.
* Review your benefits eligibility and status before completing this form.
* Complete this form within the designated deadline to ensure timely benefits processing.
