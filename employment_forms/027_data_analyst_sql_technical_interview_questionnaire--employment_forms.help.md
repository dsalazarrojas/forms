# Data Analyst SQL Technical Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Analyst SQL Technical Interview Questionnaire is a tool designed to help assess a candidate's suitability for a data analyst role. It will help identify their technical skills and experience in SQL-related fields, making it a crucial tool for recruiters and hiring managers to evaluate potential candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have answered all required fields.
* For fields that are not required, but relevant to your experience, provide as much detail as possible.
* Use the provided options as a guideline, but feel free to add any additional information that you think is relevant.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Describe your current or desired job description.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your work experience related to data analysis and SQL.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, select_one, not required): Select one of the following:
	+ Database Management
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Data Visualization
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, required): Select all languages you have experience with:
	+ Java
	+ Python
	+ SQL
* **SQL Experience** (`experience_sql`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of SQL experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Most Used Database** (`most_used_database`, select_one, required): Select the database you have most experience with:
	+ MySQL
	+ PostgreSQL
	+ Oracle
* **SQL Version Control** (`sql_version_control`, text, required): Describe how you version control your SQL code.
* **Data Modeling** (`data_modeling`, text, required): Describe your experience with data modeling.
* **Data Visualization** (`data_visualization`, text, required): Describe your experience with data visualization.
* **Database Design** (`database_design`, text, required): Describe your experience with database design.
* **Database Administration** (`database_administration`, text, required): Describe your experience with database administration.
* **Query Writing** (`query_writing`, text, required): Describe your experience with writing SQL queries.
* **SQL Error Handling** (`sql_error_handling`, text, required): Describe your experience with SQL error handling.
* **Database Security** (`database_security`, text, required): Describe your experience with database security.
* **Data Architecture** (`data_architecture`, text, required): Describe your experience with data architecture.
* **Performance Tuning** (`performance_tuning`, text, required): Describe your experience with performance tuning.
* **Data Quality** (`data_quality`, text, required): Describe your experience with data quality.
* **Database Terminology** (`database_terminology`, text, required): Describe your experience with database terminology.
* **SQL Syntax** (`sql_syntax`, text, required): Describe your experience with SQL syntax.
* **SQL PL/SQL** (`sql_plsql`, text, required): Describe your experience with SQL PL/SQL.
* **Database Indexing** (`database_indexing`, text, required): Describe your experience with database indexing.
* **Indexing Techniques** (`indexing_techniques`, text, required): Describe your experience with indexing techniques.
* **SQL Indexing** (`sql_indexing`, text, required): Describe your experience with SQL indexing.
* **Data Storage** (`data_storage`, text, required): Describe your experience with data storage.
