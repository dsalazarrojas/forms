# reference_check_authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a reference check, which is a process used to verify an individual's employment history and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field.
4. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field.
5. Select the authorization options for the reference check in the "reference_check_authorization_1", "reference_check_authorization_2", and "reference_check_authorization_3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_name
* **first_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### last_name
* **last_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### email
* **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### phone
* **phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### reference_check_authorization_1
* **reference_check_authorization_1** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the options that apply to you for the reference check (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

### reference_check_authorization_2
* **reference_check_authorization_2** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one option that applies to you for the reference check (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

### reference_check_authorization_3
* **reference_check_authorization_3** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the options that apply to you for the reference check (Yes, No).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or HR representative for guidance.
* The information collected through this form will be used for a thorough reference check, so it's essential to provide as much detail as possible.
