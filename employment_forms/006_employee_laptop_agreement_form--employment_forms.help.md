# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Laptop Agreement Form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their understanding and agreement with company policies and procedures for laptop usage. This form will help the company track employee understanding and agreement, facilitating a smoother laptop management process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, employees should read the form carefully, select the relevant answers for each question, and complete all sections. Some fields are optional, so make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is explained below:

*   **Employee Laptop Agreement Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **I accept that**: This question asks the employee to select their level of responsibility for the laptop. The options are:
	+ I am fully responsible for
	+ I am partially responsible for
	+ I am not responsible for
*   **I am fully responsible for**: This question asks if the employee is fully responsible for the laptop. The options are:
	+ True
	+ False
*   **Laptop Model**: This question asks the employee to select the model of their laptop. The options are:
	+ Dell
	+ Lenovo
	+ HP
*   **I am responsible for maintenance and repairs**: This question asks if the employee is responsible for maintenance and repairs of the laptop. The options are:
	+ True
	+ False
*   **I have read the laptop use policy**: This question asks if the employee has read the laptop use policy. The options are:
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate records.
*   If unsure about any question, ask your supervisor or IT department for clarification.
*   Make sure to save a copy of your completed form for future reference.
