# Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Form is used to document and manage disciplinary actions taken against employees. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the action is properly recorded and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have a clear reason for the disciplinary action and have discussed this with the employee in question.
2.  Enter the Employee Information, including the employee's name and ID.
3.  Enter the Date of Offense to reflect when the incident occurred.
4.  Describe the offense in detail in the Description of Offense field.
5.  Choose the type of disciplinary action to be taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Suspension, or Termination) in the Disciplinary Action field.
6.  Enter the Date of Disciplinary Action, when the action was taken.
7.  Enter any Next Steps or actions that were taken as a result of the disciplinary action.
8.  Record any Manager Information, including their name and any actions they took.
9.  Enter the Date and Time of Review to reflect when the action was reviewed.
10.  Choose the Review Status (e.g., Active or Inactive).
11.  Enter any additional Comments, if necessary.
12.  Record the Employee's Acknowledgement of the action.
13.  Record the Manager's Acknowledgement of the action.
14.  Enter the employee and manager's names, along with any relevant details.
15.  Record the date and time the form was submitted and approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information**: Enter the employee's name and ID.
*   **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
*   **Date of Offense**: Enter the date of the incident that led to the disciplinary action.
*   **Description of Offense**: Describe the reason for the disciplinary action in detail.
*   **Disciplinary Action**: Choose the type of disciplinary action to be taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Suspension, or Termination).
*   **Date of Disciplinary Action**: Enter the date the action was taken.
*   **Next Steps**: Enter any subsequent actions taken as a result of the disciplinary action.
*   **Manager Information**: Record the manager's name and any actions they took.
*   **Date of Review**: Enter the date and time the action was reviewed.
*   **Review Status**: Choose the status of the review (e.g., Active or Inactive).
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details.
*   **Employee's Acknowledgement**: Record the employee's acknowledgement of the action.
*   **Manager's Acknowledgement**: Record the manager's acknowledgement of the action.
*   **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
*   **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
*   **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved the form.
*   **Approved On**: Enter the date and time the form was approved.
*   **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form.
*   **Reviewed On**: Enter the date and time the form was reviewed.
*   **Review Status**: Choose the status of the review (e.g., Active or Inactive).
*   **Comments2**: Enter any additional comments or details.
*   **Submitted To**: Enter the person to whom the form was submitted.
*   **Created On**: Enter the date and time the form was created.
*   **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
*   **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
*   **Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide**: Enter any additional information or details.
