# Seasonal Job Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seasonal job interview form is used to collect information from applicants about a seasonal job opening. This form helps the hiring team to quickly and efficiently gather relevant details about the job and applicant's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the seasonal job title that best describes the position you are applying for.
2. Select the job type that best fits your profile (e.g., Administrative Assistant, Data Entry Specialist, etc.).
3. Choose one or more categories that relate to your work experience (e.g., Sales, Marketing, etc.).
4. Enter the duration of the job you are applying for (e.g., number of months).
5. Specify your preferred shift start and end times.
6. Select the days you are available to work.
7. Enter your availability date (if applicable).
8. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
9. Enter your contact phone number (if you want to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Job Title** (seasonal_job_title, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the seasonal job you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of seasonal job you are applying for (e.g., Administrative Assistant, Data Entry Specialist, etc.).
* **Job Category** (job_category, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more categories that relate to your work experience (e.g., Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job Duration** (job_duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of the job you are applying for (e.g., number of months).
* **Shift Start Time** (shift_start_time, time, optional): Specify your preferred shift start time.
* **Shift End Time** (shift_end_time, time, optional): Specify your preferred shift end time.
* **Days Available** (days_available, select_one, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Availability** (availability, date, optional): Enter your availability date (if applicable).
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if you want to be contacted).

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in your responses.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
