# Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form" is used to create or update a job posting. This form is intended to collect necessary details such as job title, category, status, and other relevant information to facilitate the creation or update of a job posting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's fields and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. job_posting_subscription_form
* **Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form**: Enter a brief description of the job posting you wish to create or update.
*   Enter any relevant details that will help us understand the posting you are requesting.

### 2. required_info
* **Required Information**: Enter any necessary information that is required for this posting, such as job posting ID or posting date.

### 3. job_title
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job posting. This should be a clear and concise description of the position.

### 4. job_category
* **Job Category**: Select the category that best fits the job posting. This will help us understand the type of position and where it should be posted.

### 5. job_description
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of the job posting. This should include information about the job's responsibilities, requirements, and expectations.

### 6. job_type
* **Job Type**: Select whether the job posting is active or inactive.

### 7. job_status
* **Job Status**: Select whether the job posting is active or inactive.

### 8. job_level
* **Job Level**: Select the level of the job posting, whether it's high or low.

### 9. job_opening
* **Job Opening**: Select whether the job posting is open for applications.

### 10. job_opening_details
* **Job Opening Details**: Enter any additional details about the job opening.

### 11. job_posting
* **Job Posting**: Enter the job posting URL or any other relevant information.

### 12. job_posting_details
* **Job Posting Details**: Enter any additional details about the job posting.

### 13. job_posting_status
* **Job Posting Status**: Enter the status of the job posting.

### 14. job_posting_type
* **Job Posting Type**: Select whether the job posting is yes or no.

### 15. job_posting_type_details
* **Job Posting Type Details**: Enter any additional details about the job posting type.

### 16. job_submitter
* **Job Submitter**: Select whether you are the job submitter.

### 17. job_submitter_details
* **Job Submitter Details**: Enter any additional details about the submitter.

### 18. submitter_email
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the submitter.

### 19. submitter_phone
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the submitter.

### 20. job_approver
* **Job Approver**: Select whether you are the job approver.

### 21. job_approver_details
* **Job Approver Details**: Enter any additional details about the approver.

### 22. job_approver_email
* **Job Approver Email**: Enter the email of the approver.

### 23. job_approver_phone
* **Job Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the approver.

### 24. date
* **Date**: Enter the date the job posting was created or updated.

### 25. time
* **Time**: Enter the time the job posting was created or updated.
