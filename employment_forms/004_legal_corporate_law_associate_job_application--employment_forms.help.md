# Legal Corporate Law Associate Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates applying for a Legal Corporate Law Associate position in a corporation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's sections, providing your details and qualifications for the Legal Corporate Law Associate position.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, and that you provide accurate and relevant information.
3. Upload any supporting documents, such as your resume and cover letter, if applicable.
4. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Please provide your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Corporate Law Associate: This is the title of the position you are applying for.
	+ Associate Attorney: This is a different title for the position.
	+ Paralegal: This is another title for the position.
* **Resume Upload** (text, required: false): Upload your resume as a supporting document.
* **Cover Letter Upload** (text, required: false): Upload your cover letter as a supporting document.
* **Job Reference** (text, required: false): Enter the job title or ID of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Reference URL** (text, required: false): Enter a URL of the job posting or reference page.
* **Job Type** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Full-time: This is the type of employment you are looking for.
	+ Part-time: This is a part-time employment option.
	+ Contract: This is a contract employment option.
* **Employment Status** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Active: This is your current employment status.
	+ Inactive: This is your employment status.
	+ Other (specify): If none of the above applies, please specify your employment status.
* **Job Start Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you would be able to start working.
* **Job End Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to end your employment.
* **Work Status** (text, required: false): Please provide any information about your work status, if applicable.
* **Availability** (text, required: false): Please provide any information about your availability, if applicable.
* **Job Location** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Remote: This is the location of the position.
	+ Office: This is an office-based position.
* **Additional Information** (text, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your background, qualifications, or fit for the position.

Note: The fields marked as required: false are optional and can be filled out or left blank, according to your preference.
