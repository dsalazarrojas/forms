# Time Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's work hours, breaks, and other relevant details for time-tracking purposes. It's intended for employees to report their daily work hours, breaks, and submit their work-related information to their managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee name" field.
2. Fill in the "Work start time" and "Work end time" fields with the start and end of your work hours.
3. Record your break time in the "Break time" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your work in the "Work description" field.
5. Add any additional work notes in the "Work notes" field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager name" field.
7. Select the current project status from the "Project status" dropdown menu.
8. Record the total work hours spent on the task in the "Work hours" field.
9. If applicable, record any overtime hours in the "Overtime" field.
10. Enter your break duration in the "Break duration" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "Employee email" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Employee phone" field.
13. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager email" field.
14. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager phone" field.
15. Enter your company's name in the "Company name" field.
16. Enter your company's address in the "Company address" field.
17. Enter your company's phone number in the "Company phone" field.
18. Enter your company's email address in the "Company email" field.
19. Select the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
20. Enter the project name in the "Project name" field.
21. Enter the task name in the "Task name" field.
22. Enter the task ID in the "Task id" field.
23. Select the date of work in the "Work date" field.
24. Select who submitted the form from the "Submitted by" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your employee name.
* **Work start time** (`work_start_time`, time, optional): The start time of your work hours.
* **Work end time** (`work_end_time`, time, optional): The end time of your work hours.
* **Break time** (`break_time`, number, optional): The duration of your break.
* **Work description** (`work_description`, text, optional): A brief description of your work.
* **Work notes** (`work_notes`, note, optional): Additional work notes.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Your manager's name.
* **Project status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): The current project status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Work hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): The total hours spent on the task.
* **Overtime** (`overtime`, number, optional): Any overtime hours spent on the task.
* **Break duration** (`break_duration`, number, optional): The duration of your break.
* **Employee email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Employee phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Manager email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Your manager's email address.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Your manager's phone number.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Your company's name.
* **Company address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Your company's address.
* **Company phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Your company's phone number.
* **Company email** (`company_email`, email, optional): Your company's email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department you belong to.
* **Project name** (`project_name`, text, optional): The project name.
* **Task name** (`task_name`, text, optional): The task name.
* **Task id** (`task_id`, text, optional): The task ID.
* **Work date** (`work_date`, date, optional): The date of work.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Who submitted the form (User 1, User 2, or User 3).

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate information to ensure accurate time tracking.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any doubts or questions, consult your manager or HR representative.
