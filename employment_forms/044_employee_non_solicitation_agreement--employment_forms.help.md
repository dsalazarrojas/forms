# Employee Non Solicitation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain an employee's agreement not to solicit or recruit colleagues or clients for a new job or business opportunity during their employment with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Non Solicitation Agreement" field with the text of your non-solicitation agreement.
2.  Select the date you accepted the non-solicitation agreement.
3.  Choose which of the acknowledgement options apply to your situation.
4.  Select the date and time you acknowledged the agreement.
5.  Review the form carefully and submit it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Solicitation Agreement** (`non_solicitation_agreement`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter the text of your non-solicitation agreement. Please provide a clear and concise statement of your agreement not to solicit or recruit colleagues or clients for a new job or business opportunity.
* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required: false): This field is not currently used in this form, but it is intended to collect information about the employee. You may disregard this field.
* **Date of Acceptance** (`date_of_acceptance`, date, required: false): Enter the date you accepted the non-solicitation agreement.
* **Non Solicitation Agreement Text** (`non_solicitation_agreement_text`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional text related to your non-solicitation agreement. Please provide any further details or explanations as needed.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all of the acknowledgement options that apply to your situation:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of the non-solicitation agreement.
	+ Option 2: I understand the consequences of breaching the non-solicitation agreement.
	+ Option 3: I understand that I am responsible for ensuring that my actions do not breach the non-solicitation agreement.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you acknowledged the non-solicitation agreement.
* **Acknowledgement Time** (`acknowledgement_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you acknowledged the non-solicitation agreement.

## Tips
Review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are accurately completed before submitting. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
