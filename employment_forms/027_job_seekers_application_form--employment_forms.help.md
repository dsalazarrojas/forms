# Job Seekers Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Seekers Application Form is designed to collect information from job seekers who are looking for employment opportunities. This form helps hiring managers and recruiters to assess your candidacy and determine whether you are a good fit for available job openings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. You can select multiple options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
4. Please be as detailed as possible when providing information about your work experience, job requirements, and job details.
5. If you have any attachments that support your application, please upload them to the Job Seeker Attachments field.
6. Make sure to provide accurate contact information, including your phone number, email, and address.
7. Enter your date of birth, nationality, and language proficiency to help us assess your suitability for the role.
8. If you have any references, please provide their contact information.
9. Finally, review your application before submitting to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
