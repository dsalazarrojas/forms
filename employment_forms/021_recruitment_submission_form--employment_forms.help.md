# Recruitment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Submission Form is designed to collect essential information from job applicants to assist the hiring process. This form will help recruiters understand your work experience, job title, job description, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Double-check your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Contact Email (2)**: Enter your professional email address.
- **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter your primary phone number.
- **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Work Experience (5)**: Select your current or previous work experience from the provided options.
- **Notice Period (6)**: Enter the length of notice period you would provide if your employment were to end. 
- **Job Title (7)**: Enter your job title or position.
- **Job Description (8)**: Briefly describe your job duties and responsibilities.
- **Job Requirements (9)**: Outline the essential qualifications and skills required for the job.
- **Work Status (10)**: Indicate your current work status (Part-time, Full-time, Contract, Freelance, or Other).
- **Job Type (11)**: Select the type of job (Permanent, Temporary, Seasonal, Intern, or Other).
- **Employment Type (12)**: Indicate the type of employment you are looking for (Full-time, Part-time, Permanent, Temporary).
- **Job Location (13)**: Select the location of the job (Remote, On-site, Hybrid).
- **Job Level (14)**: Select your current job level (Entry-Level, Mid-Level, Senior, Executive).
- **Job Function (15)**: Briefly describe your job function.
- **Job Family (16)**: Briefly describe the job family you belong to.
- **Department (17)**: Enter the department you belong to.
- **Team Size (18)**: Enter the team size you have experience with.
- **Team Organizational Chart (19)**: Provide an organizational chart of your team, if applicable.
- **Work Shift (20)**: Enter your work shift (if applicable).
- **Work Status 2 (21)**: Indicate your current work status (if applicable).
- **Job Description 2 (22)**: Briefly describe the job you are applying for (if applicable).
- **Work Experience 2 (23)**: Briefly describe your work experience (if applicable).
- **Notice Period 2 (24)**: Enter the notice period you would provide if your employment were to end (if applicable).
- **Submission Status (25)**: Select the status of your submission (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, or Completed).

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to your situation. Please only fill out the required fields.
