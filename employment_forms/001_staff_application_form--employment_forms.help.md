# staff_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The staff application form is used to collect information from staff members for on-call positions. It helps to streamline the on-call position assignment process by providing a structured way for staff to submit their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the staff application section. This is the main section where you will provide your basic information.
2. Next, select the on-call positions you are interested in. You can choose one or multiple positions from the list. 
3. Fill in any additional information about your preferred on-call position 1, 2, or 3. This section is for providing more details about your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Application** (`staff_application`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you need to provide your basic information.
* **On call positions** (`on_call_positions`, select multiple, required): Select one or multiple on-call positions that you are interested in. This will help us understand your preferences.
* **On call position 1** (`on_call_position_1`, text, optional): If you selected "on call position 1" in the previous field, provide more details about your preferences for this position.
* **On call position 2** (`on_call_position_2`, text, optional): If you selected "on call position 2" in the previous field, provide more details about your preferences for this position.
* **On call position 3** (`on_call_position_3`, text, optional): If you selected "on call position 3" in the previous field, provide more details about your preferences for this position.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your application is complete and can be properly reviewed.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, feel free to ask our staff support team.
* Please note that this form is for on-call positions only, and you should only select positions that are currently open.
