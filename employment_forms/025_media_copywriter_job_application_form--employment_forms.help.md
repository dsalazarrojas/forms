# Media Copywriter Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential candidates to submit their application for a Media Copywriter position. It gathers information about the applicant's background, experience, and qualifications to assess their fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the Applicant Information page.
2. Complete the Contact Details page with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Provide a clear and concise Personal Statement in the Personal Statement page.
4. Select at least one relevant portfolio file in the Portfolio Files page.
5. Confirm your portfolio review in the Portfolio Review page.
6. Indicate if you have any media assets to submit in the Media Assets page.
7. Enter the job application status and application date as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (1, text, required): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you for further communication.
* **Contact Details** (2, text, required): Provide your current job title, company, and any other relevant contact information to ensure we can reach you quickly and efficiently.
* **Portfolio Information** (3, text, required): Describe your past work experience, skills, and achievements related to media copywriting.
* **Work Experience** (4, text, required): List your previous work experience in the field of media copywriting, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Education Background** (5, text, required): Share your academic background and qualifications relevant to media copywriting, such as degrees, diplomas, and certifications.
* **Personal Statement** (6, text, required): Write a short paragraph explaining why you are the best fit for the Media Copywriter position, highlighting your strengths, and what you can bring to the company.
* **Portfolio Files** (7, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following: Example Video, Other.
* **Portfolio Review** (8, select_one, required): Confirm whether we will review your selected portfolio files.
* **Media Assets** (9, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have any media assets to submit, such as images, videos, or documents.
* **Job Application Status** (10, number, required): Enter the current status of your job application, such as pending, rejected, or accepted.
* **Application Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date of your application submission in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
