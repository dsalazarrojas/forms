# Risk Assesment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assesment Form is used to identify and document potential risks in the workplace. It helps the safety team to assess the risk level, probability, and recommended controls or mitigations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Answer each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. For select_one fields, choose the most relevant option.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description.
5. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the safety team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Assessment Form** (`risk_assessment_form`): Enter a brief description of the risk you are assessing.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`): Select the level of risk (High, Low).
* **Probability** (`probability`): Enter the probability of the risk (e.g., 10%).
* **Consequence** (`consequence`): Enter a brief description of the potential consequences of the risk.
* **Control** (`control`): Select whether you currently have control over the risk (Yes, No).
* **Recommended Controls** (`recommended_controls`): Select whether recommended controls are in place (Yes, No).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the risk.
* **Risk Assessment Form Comments** (`risk_assessment_form_comments`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the form itself.
* **Risk Assessment Form Status** (`risk_assessment_form_status`): Select the status of the risk assessment form (Active, Inactive).
* **Other Status** (`risk_assessment_form_status_other`): Enter any additional status information about the risk assessment form.
* **Safety Team** (`safety_team`): Enter the name of the safety team member who is responsible for assessing the risk.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the risk assessment.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Select whether you created the risk assessment form (Yes, No).
* **Comments** (no label): Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Date** (no label): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Time** (no label): Enter the time of the risk assessment.
* **Safety Team** (no label): Enter the name of the safety team member who is responsible for assessing the risk.

Note: This guide is a general help guide and may not cover all fields. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the safety team for guidance.
