> # Sales Candidate Pre Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from sales candidates, helping hiring managers to pre-screen them for potential roles within the company. This pre-screening form aims to streamline the hiring process and identify candidates who are a good fit for the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your sales experience, highlighting relevant skills and accomplishments.
3. Share your education background, including any relevant certifications or training.
4. Confirm your availability for work and any constraints you may have.
5. List your relevant skills and qualifications.
6. Provide references who can vouch for your sales experience and abilities.
7. Describe your work history, including previous sales roles and achievements.
8. Share your experience with sales leadership, if applicable.
9. Confirm your language proficiency, choosing one of the options provided.
10. Finally, select your employment status from the options provided.
