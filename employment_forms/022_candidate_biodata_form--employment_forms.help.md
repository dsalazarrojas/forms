## Candidate Biodata Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Candidate Biodata Form is designed to collect comprehensive personal information from applicants. This form is used to gather data for internal records and is a requirement for all job applicants.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your full name, date of birth, and nationality.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Fill in your family details, such as your father's and mother's names, if applicable.
4. Provide your emergency contact information, including the name, relationship, and phone number of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
5. Fill in your professional background, including your current job title, employer, and highest academic qualification.
6. List all languages you speak fluently.
7. Indicate your willingness to relocate for the job.
8. Confirm that the biodata you provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents. (ID: 2, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY). (ID: 3, Type: date, Required: true)
* **Gender**: Select your gender for diversity and identification purposes. (ID: 4, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status. (ID: 5, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Nationality**: Enter your country of citizenship. (ID: 6, Type: text, Required: true)
* **National ID or Passport Number**: Enter your national ID or passport number. (ID: 7, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address. (ID: 8, Type: email, Required: true)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number. (ID: 9, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Permanent Address**: Enter your full home address. (ID: 10, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Father's Name**: Enter the full name of your father, if applicable. (ID: 12, Type: text, Required: false)
* **Mother's Name**: Enter the full name of your mother, if applicable. (ID: 13, Type: text, Required: false)
* **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have, if applicable. (ID: 14, Type: number, Required: false)
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency. (ID: 15, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the emergency contact person and you. (ID: 16, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person. (ID: 17, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Current Job Title**: Enter your current job title, if employed. (ID: 19, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Current Employer**: Enter your current employer's name. (ID: 20, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Highest Academic Qualification**: Enter your highest academic qualification. (ID: 21, Type: text, Required: true)
* **Key Languages Spoken**: Select all languages you speak fluently. (ID: 22, Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
* **Willingness to Relocate**: Indicate if you are willing to move for the job. (ID: 23, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Confirmation of Biodata Accuracy**: Confirm that all biodata you provided is accurate. (ID: 24, Type: select_one, Required: true)
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you submitted your application. (ID: 25, Type: date, Required: true)
