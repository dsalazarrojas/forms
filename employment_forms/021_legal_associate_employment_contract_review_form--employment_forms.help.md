# Legal Associate Employment Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by hiring managers to review employment contracts submitted by new or existing employees. It helps gather information about the contract, including whether it's been reviewed, when it was reviewed, and any comments or concerns that have been raised during the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the employment contract has been reviewed or not.
2. Enter the date when the contract was reviewed.
3. Add any comments or concerns that have been raised during the review process.
4. Evaluate the employment agreement, choosing one of the available options.
5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 for any additional sections as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employment_contracts** (employment_contracts, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the employment contract has been reviewed, 'No' otherwise.
* **contract_review_date** (contract_review_date, date, required): Enter the date when the employment contract was reviewed.
* **contract_review_comments** (contract_review_comments, note, optional): Add any comments or concerns that have been raised during the review process.
* **contract_review_concerns** (contract_review_concerns, select_multiple, optional): Select any concerns that have been raised during the review process.
* **employment_agreement_evaluation** (employment_agreement_evaluation, select_one, required): Evaluate the employment agreement, choosing one of the available options.
* **contract_review_comments_2** (contract_review_comments_2, note, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns that have been raised during the review process.
* **employment_agreement_evaluation_2** (employment_agreement_evaluation_2, select_one, optional): Evaluate the employment agreement again, choosing one of the available options.
* **contract_review_comments_3** (contract_review_comments_3, note, optional): Add any further comments or concerns that have been raised during the review process.
* **employment_agreement_evaluation_3** (employment_agreement_evaluation_3, select_one, optional): Evaluate the employment agreement once more, choosing one of the available options.

## Tips
- Make sure to select 'Yes' for each section if the employment contract has been reviewed.
- Enter the correct date when selecting 'Yes' for the first section.
- Keep comments and concerns concise and clear.
- Select only one evaluation option for each section.
- Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
