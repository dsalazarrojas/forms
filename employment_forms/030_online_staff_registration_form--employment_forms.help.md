# Online Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Staff Registration Form is designed to collect relevant information from staff members for administrative and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For select-type fields, choose one option that best describes your answer.
3. Attach your resume as a file.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title** (Text, Required: False): Enter the title of the form, if desired.
* **category** (Select One, Required: True): Choose whether the staff member is employed (Yes) or not (No).
* **description** (Text, Required: False): Provide a brief description of the job position or role.
* **address** (Text, Required: True): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **phone-number** (Text, Required: True): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **resume** (Text, Required: True): Enter a brief description of your resume.
* **resume-file** (Text, Required: True): Attach your resume as a file.
* **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address, if available.
* **email-confirmed** (Select One, Required: False): Choose whether your email has been confirmed (Yes) or not (No).
* **contact** (Select Multiple, Required: True): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Yes) or not (No).
* **employment-status** (Select Multiple, Required: True): Choose your employment status (Active/Inactive).
* **job-title** (Text, Required: True): Enter your job title.
* **department** (Text, Required: False): Enter the department you belong to, if applicable.
* **job-location** (Text, Required: True): Enter the location of your job.
* **job-description** (Text, Required: False): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **job-type** (Text, Required: False): Enter the type of job, if applicable.
* **work-hours** (Select One, Required: True): Choose whether you work (Yes) or not (No).
* **job-level** (Select One, Required: True): Choose your job level (High/Low).
* **job-category** (Select Multiple, Required: True): Choose your job category (Yes/No).
* **job-level-2** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Enter job level (High/Low), if applicable.
* **job-level-3** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Enter job level (High/Low), if applicable.
* **job-level-4** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Enter job level (High/Low), if applicable.
* **job-level-5** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Enter job level (High/Low), if applicable.
* **job-level-6** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Enter job level (High/Low), if applicable.
* **submitted-by** (Select One, Required: True): Choose whether the form has been submitted by you (Yes) or not (No).
