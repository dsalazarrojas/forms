# Mechanical Engineer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users apply for a mechanical engineer position in a company. It collects essential information about the applicant, including contact details, experience, skills, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Upload your resume in the "Resume" section.
3. Select your relevant experience from the drop-down menu in the "Experience" section.
4. If applicable, provide your qualifications and education background in the "Qualifications" and "Education" sections.
5. Describe your work history in the "Work History" section.
6. Choose your relevant skills from the drop-down menu in the "Skills" section.
7. Finally, provide your references in the "References" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your up-to-date resume that highlights your skills and experience.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required): Select your relevant experience in Mechanical Engineer, Software Engineer, or Electrical Engineer categories.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional): If you have any special certifications or qualifications, please describe them here.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional): Briefly describe your previous work experiences.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your relevant skills, such as Programming, Data Analysis, or Mechanical Drawing.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of your professional references.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a recent resume that accurately reflects your current experience and skills.
* Be specific when selecting your experience and skills to match the mechanical engineer position.
* Use proper format and grammar when entering your qualifications, education, and work history.
* Ensure that your references are professional and willing to vouch for your experience and skills.
