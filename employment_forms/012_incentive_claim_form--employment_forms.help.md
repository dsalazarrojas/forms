# Incentive Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incentive Claim Form is used to collect data on incentives claimed by employees. It is a crucial tool for the organization to process payments and ensure that incentives are distributed fairly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the relevant details for the employee and period in question.
3. Fill in the amount claimed, taking into account any deductions or adjustments.
4. Choose the correct payment frequency and payment method.
5. Indicate if the payment status is active or inactive.
6. Select the correct incentive type.
7. If applicable, enter the amount approved and amount paid.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Enter the name and ID number of the employee claiming the incentive.
* **Period**: Enter the period or date range for which the incentive is being claimed.
* **Amount Claimed**: Enter the amount of the incentive claimed, considering any deductions or adjustments.
* **Payment Frequency**: Choose the frequency at which the payment is made (e.g., monthly, bi-weekly).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment (if known).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method used to make the payment (e.g., bank transfer, check).
* **Company**: Indicate if the company is involved in the payment process.
* **Incentive Type**: Select all applicable incentive types (e.g., bonus, commission).
* **Amount Approved**: Enter the amount approved for the incentive.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount paid to the employee.
* **Payment Status**: Choose the status of the payment (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Claimant**: Indicate if the employee is the claimant.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the payment.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment process.
