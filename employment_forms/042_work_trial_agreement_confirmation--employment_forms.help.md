# work_trial_agreement_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the participant and recruiters to confirm the agreement of the work trial. It is a crucial document that ensures both parties are on the same page regarding the trial details and their roles. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields and ensure that both you and the participant have signed and dated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the participant's name and other relevant details as required.
- **Work Trial Dates** (`Work Trial Dates`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start and end dates of the work trial period.
- **Consent to Terms** (`Consent to Terms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'I agree' to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the work trial.
- **Signatures** (`Signatures`, `note`, required/optional): Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms.
- **Participant Signatures** (`Participant Signatures`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select whether the participant has signed the agreement.
- **Recruiter Signature** (`Recruiter Signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether you have obtained the participant's signature.
- **HR Signature** (`HR Signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether HR has been informed of the work trial.
- **Employment Program Signature** (`Employment Program Signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the participant is enrolled in a job program.
- **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
- **Date Signed Recruiter** (`Date Signed Recruiter`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the participant signed.
- **Date Signed Hrs** (`Date Signed Hrs`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date HR was informed.
- **Date Signed Employment** (`Date Signed Employment`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the employment was confirmed.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Please add any additional comments or notes regarding the work trial.
- **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
- **Trial Length** (`Trial Length`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the length of the work trial.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the work trial.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the work trial.
- **Work Location** (`Work Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the participant will work.
- **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title.
- **Participant Status** (`Participant Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the participant's status.
- **Recruiter** (`Recruiter`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the recruiter's name.
- **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email.
- **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
- **Recruiter Phone** (`Recruiter Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the recruiter's phone number.
- **Notes 2** (`Notes 2`, `text`, required/optional): Please add any additional comments or notes regarding the work trial.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
