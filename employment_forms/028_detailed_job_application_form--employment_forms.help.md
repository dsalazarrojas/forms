# Detailed Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Detailed Job Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect detailed information from job applicants. This form helps applicants showcase their skills, experience, and qualifications for a particular job position. By filling out this form, applicants can increase their chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the main information page (Page 1: Main Info).
2. In this page, provide your name, email, phone number, and a brief description of your availability to work.
3. If you have any prior experience in the job field or industry, go to (Page 2: Work Experience) and describe your relevant work experience.
4. If you have any relevant education or certifications, go to (Page 3: Education) and list them.
5. On (Page 4: Personal Info), provide any personal details that you feel are relevant to the job position.
6. (Page 5: Contact Details) is where you can provide your contact information for future communication.
7. If you have any professional references, go to (Page 6: References) and list them.
8. In (Page 7: Job Requirements), describe the specific job requirements you are looking for in the position.
9. Finally, click on (Page 8: Submit) to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Info** (Page 1, text, required): This page requires your main contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number, as well as a brief description of your availability to work.
* **Work Experience** (Page 2, text, optional): If you have relevant work experience, describe your prior work experience in the job field or industry.
* **Education** (Page 3, text, optional): If you have any relevant education or certifications, list them.
* **Personal Info** (Page 4, text, optional): Provide any personal details that you feel are relevant to the job position.
* **Contact Details** (Page 5, text, optional): Provide your contact information for future communication.
* **References** (Page 6, text, optional): If you have any professional references, list them.
* **Job Requirements** (Page 7, text, optional): Describe the specific job requirements you are looking for in the position.
* **Submit** (Page 8, text, required): This is the submit button to finalize your application.
