# Food Service Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the performance of food service employees, including their overall performance, communication skills, time management, job knowledge, and other essential competencies.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select the employee's **Job Title** from the available options.
3. Enter the employee's department in the **Department** field.
4. Select the start date of the evaluation in the **Evaluation Start Date** field.
5. Select the due date of the evaluation in the **Evaluation Due Date** field.
6. If applicable, select the date when the evaluation was completed in the **Evaluation Complete Date** field.
7. Rate the employee's overall performance in the **Overall Performance** field.
8. Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in **Communication Skills** (Yes/No).
9. Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in **Time Management** (Yes/No).
10. Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in **Job Knowledge** (Yes/No).
11. Provide any additional feedback for the employee in the **Feedback** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Job Title**: Select the employee's job title from the available options.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the employee works.
* **Evaluation Start Date**: Enter the date when the evaluation started.
* **Evaluation Due Date**: Enter the date when the evaluation needs to be completed.
* **Evaluation Complete Date**: If the evaluation has been completed, enter the date it was completed.
* **Overall Performance**: Rate the employee's overall performance (Yes/No).
* **Communication Skills**: Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in communication skills (Yes/No).
* **Time Management**: Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in time management (Yes/No).
* **Job Knowledge**: Indicate the employee's level of proficiency in job knowledge (Yes/No).
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback for the employee.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Keep the feedback section concise and clear, focusing on specific examples or incidents.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
