# Farm Equipment Operator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in becoming a farm equipment operator. It is a quick and straightforward way to gauge your interest and qualifications for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. If you have any work experience relevant to farm equipment operation, please provide details in the "Work History" field.
3. If you have any relevant certifications, please select "Yes" in the "Certifications" field.
4. If you have experience as a farm equipment operator, please select "Yes" in the "Experience" field.
5. Please indicate if you are available to start immediately in the "Available to Start" field.
6. Provide contact information so we can reach out to you.
7. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number.
8. Please provide the name and contact information for two references.
9. Finally, please sign the form electronically to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your full name, address, and any other contact information you would like us to have.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any work experience related to farm equipment operation, please provide details (e.g., dates of employment, job title, equipment operated).
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Please select "Yes" if you have any relevant certifications or training related to farm equipment operation.
* **Experience** (`experience`, note, optional):
	+ If you have experience as a farm equipment operator, please provide details about your experience (e.g., years of experience, equipment operated).
* **Available to Start** (`available_to_start`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select "Yes" if you are available to start immediately.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your email address and phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional):
	+ If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number.
* **Reference 1** (`reference_1`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide the name and contact information for one professional reference.
* **Reference 2** (`reference_2`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide the name and contact information for another professional reference.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your electronic signature to confirm your application.
