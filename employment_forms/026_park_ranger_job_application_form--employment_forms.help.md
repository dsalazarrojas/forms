# Park Ranger Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to apply for a Park Ranger position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, contact email, contact phone, and contact address.
2. Upload your resume and cover letter.
3. Indicate your availability, including the days and hours you are available to work.
4. Select your preferred job title, department, and location.
5. Specify your availability for shifts and hours.
6. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, contact information, and employment status.
7. Fill in any additional information about your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your current address that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Cover Letter**: Upload a cover letter outlining your qualifications and experience.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Availability**: Select all the days and hours you are available to work. (Available, Not Available, Available on Weekends, Available on Weeknights)
* **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your previous work experience related to this position.
* **Education Background**: Briefly describe your education background and qualifications.
* **Skills**: List any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial for this position.
* **References**: List any relevant references or professional associations.
* **Job Title**: Select one of the available job titles (Ranger, Conservation Officer, Park Ranger, Conservation Officer (Ranger)).
* **Department**: Select one of the available departments (Parks and Recreation, Parks and Wildlife, Parks and Conservation, Conservation and Wildlife).
* **Location**: Select one of the available locations (Mountain Park, Beach Park, City Park, State Park).
* **Shifts**: Select all the shifts you are available to work. (Morning, Afternoon, Morning and Afternoon, Varies)
* **Hours**: Select all the hours you are available to work. (8-4, 4-12, Varies)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Employed**: Select the employment status of your emergency contact. (Employed, Not Employed)
* **Emergency Contact Employed Type**: Briefly describe your emergency contact's employment type.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Add any additional information about your emergency contact.

This guide should help you fill out the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
