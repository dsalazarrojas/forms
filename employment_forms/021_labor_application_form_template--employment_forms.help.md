# Labor Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants applying for labor positions within our organization. It includes fields for basic contact information, job application details, and educational background. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your job title and job description to help us understand your background and qualifications.
3. Share your educational background by specifying your degree program and institution.
4. Confirm your graduation date to verify your eligibility for the position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): If you are currently employed, please provide your current company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Describe the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your job experience and qualifications.
* **Degree** (`degree_program`, text, required): Specify your degree level (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, etc.).
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you obtained your degree.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Confirm your graduation date to verify your eligibility for the position.
