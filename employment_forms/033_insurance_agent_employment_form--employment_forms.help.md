# Insurance Agent Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Agent Employment Form is designed to collect relevant information from individuals interested in joining our insurance agency as agents. This form helps us understand your background, qualifications, and experience to assess your fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
3. If you have any experience as an insurance agent, please select the relevant experience duration in the "Work Experience" field.
4. If you hold any certifications or licenses, please select them accordingly.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it for any errors or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Work Experience**: Select the duration of your work experience as an insurance agent. This will help us assess your level of expertise.
* **Employment Status**: Choose your current employment status to help us understand your availability for the role.
* **Education**: Select your highest level of education to demonstrate your background.
* **License**: If you hold any license related to insurance, select the relevant type.
* **Certification**: If you are certified as an insurance agent, select the corresponding type of certification.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send your application.
* **Review**: This is a note field for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.

Please note that this form is for internal use only and should not be shared or submitted by external users.
