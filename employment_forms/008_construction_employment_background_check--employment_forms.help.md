# Construction Employment Background Check - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential background information from applicants in the construction industry. It helps to verify the accuracy of provided information and ensures a safe working environment for all staff, clients, and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2.  Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3.  If you are unsure about any section, contact your HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Legal Name
*   **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Other Names Used (Maiden, etc.)** (`other_names_used`, `text`, optional): Enter any other names you have been known by, such as maiden names or aliases.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth, used for identification purposes.
*   **Social Security Number (Last 4 Digits)** (`ssn_last_four`, `text`, required): Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for background verification purposes.
*   **Current Street Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current street address, city, and state.
*   **Primary Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number in the format -(555) 555-5555.
*   **Years at Current Address** (`years_at_address`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you've lived at your current address.
*   **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your driver's license number if you operate company vehicles.
*   **Issuing State** (`license_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
*   **License Class** (`license_class`, `select_one`, required): Select the license class that best describes your driving privileges.
*   **Any motor vehicle violations in the last 3 years?** (`motor_vehicle_violations`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you've had any motor vehicle violations in the last 3 years, excluding minor parking tickets.
*   **Have you ever been convicted of a felony?** (`criminal_conviction_history`, `select_one`, required): Answer truthfully about your felony conviction status.
*   **Consent to Pre-employment Drug Screening** (`drug_test_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you consent to pre-employment drug screening.
*   **Authorization and Release Agreement** (`authorization_signature`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to authorize the background check, including credit check if applicable.
*   **Date Signed** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your submission.

Note: This form is a sample and may need adjustments based on your company's specific background check requirements. Always review and adjust the form according to your organization's policies and legal compliance.
