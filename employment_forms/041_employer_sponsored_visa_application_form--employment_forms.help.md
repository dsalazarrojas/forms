# Employer Sponsored Visa Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employers to provide information and documentation to support an employment-sponsored visa application. It is a straightforward form with multiple fields to gather specific details from you as an employer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Employer Info field with your company's details.
2. In the Applicant Info field, provide the necessary information about the applicant.
3. For Document Intake, upload or describe the documents you are providing.
4. In Employment Details, enter the job specifics and work-related information about the applicant.
5. Enter your Contact Info so we can get in touch with you.
6. Upload or describe the Applicant Documents and Employment Documents related to the application.
7. Fill out the E-Signature field and request for your signature.
8. Confirm that you have reviewed and certified the information in the Review and Certification field.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Info (1)**: This is where you provide your company's details such as name and address.
* **Applicant Info (2)**: Here, you enter the necessary information about the applicant, including their name and contact details.
* **Document Intake (3)**: Upload or describe the documents related to the applicant's employment, such as contracts or letters.
* **Employment Details (4)**: Enter the job specifics, work hours, and other relevant employment details.
* **Contact Info (5)**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Applicant Documents (6)**: Upload or describe the documents related to the applicant, such as ID or passport scans.
* **Employment Documents (7)**: Upload or describe the documents related to the employment, such as contracts or job offer letters.
* **E-Signature (8)**: Sign this field with your digital signature.
* **E-Signature Request (9)**: Confirm that you have received the E-Signature request.
* **Review and Certification (10)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and verified.
* **Employer Signature (11)**: Sign the form digitally, confirming that the information is correct and accurate.
* **Review and Certification (12)**: This field is for you to review and certify the information provided.
* **Submit (13)**: Send the form to complete the application.

## Tips
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. Upload supporting documents to back up your answers.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team at form@gic.mx.
