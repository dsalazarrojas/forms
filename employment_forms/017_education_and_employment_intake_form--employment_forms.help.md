# Education And Employment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education And Employment Intake Form is designed to collect information about an individual's education and employment background, which will be used for [briefly mention the intended use of the form].

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the education background fields (education_0, education_0_education_0, etc.) with relevant information.
3. Select the correct job title from the job_title field.
4. Choose the correct work experience from the work_experience field.
5. Select the correct employment status from the employment_status field.
6. Fill in the employment history fields (employment_history_0, etc.).
7. Select the correct education history from the education_history field.
8. Fill in the education history fields (education_history_0, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **education_background**: A brief description of your educational background.
  • **education_0**: Select all relevant education experiences.
  • **education_0_education_0**: Choose one specific education experience.
  • **education_0_education_0_0**: Fill in the details of the selected education experience.
- **job_title**: Enter your current or past job title.
- **work_experience**: Choose your relevant work experience.
  • **work_history_0**: Fill in the details of the selected work experience.
- **employment_status**: Select your current employment status.
  • **employment_status_0**: Choose your employment status for a specific job.
  • **employment_status_0_employment_status_0**: Select a specific employment status.
- **education_and_employment_background**: Briefly describe your education and employment background.
- **employment_history**: Describe your employment history.
  • **employment_history_0**: Fill in the details of the selected employment history.
- **education_history**: Select your relevant education history.
  • **education_history_0**: Choose a specific education experience.
  • **education_history_0_education_history_0**: Select a specific education history.
  • **job_title_0**: Choose a job title related to your education history.
  • **job_title_0_job_title_0**: Select a specific job title.
  • **job_title_0_job_title_0_0**: Select whether you have held this job title. 
  • **education_history_0_education_history_0_education_history_0**: Select a specific education history.
- **job_title_0**: Choose whether you have held this job title.
