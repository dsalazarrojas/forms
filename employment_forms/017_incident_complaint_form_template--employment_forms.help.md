> # Incident Complaint Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occurred on-site at your workplace. It is a template for recording incidents, gathering witness statements, and documenting resolutions. This form is meant to be completed by employees who have witnessed or been involved in incidents, as well as by management or HR personnel to document resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields and sections to ensure you understand their purpose.
2.  If you are an employee, complete the "Employee Form" sections (Employee Form 1-8) with your personal details.
3.  If you are a witness, complete the "Witness Information" sections (Witness Information 1-5).
4.  If you are a supervisor or HR representative, complete the "Resolution" sections (Resolution 1-5) with the actions taken to resolve the incident.
5.  Be sure to provide clear and concise information in each field to help ensure accurate and helpful documentation.
6.  Submit the completed form once you have filled out all the required fields.
