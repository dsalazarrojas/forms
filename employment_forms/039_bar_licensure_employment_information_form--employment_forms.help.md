# Bar Licensure Employment Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment information from individuals in the bar or restaurant industry, including application dates, personal details, work history, and background check consent. This information will be used to assess the applicant's suitability for bar licensure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete all required fields marked as "required".
2. Fill in the date fields in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
3. Enter your name, email, phone number, and address correctly.
4. Select your current employment status and years of experience in the bar or hospitality industry accurately.
5. Select all applicable bar positions held.
6. If you have a criminal conviction, provide a brief description of the conviction and date.
7. List 2-3 professional references with contact information.
8. Consent to a background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, required): Enter the date of this application in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Residential Address** (`current_address`, required): Enter your current address.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment_status`, required): Select your current job situation.
* **Current Employer Name** (`current_employer`, optional): Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Current Position Title** (`current_position`, optional): Enter your job title at current employer.
* **Length of Current Employment** (`current_employment_length`, optional): Enter how long you've been at your current job.
* **Previous Employer (Most Recent)** (`previous_employer_1`, optional): Enter the name of your previous employer.
* **Previous Position** (`previous_position_1`, optional): Enter the job title at previous employer.
* **Employment Dates** (`employment_dates_1`, optional): Enter the dates of employment with previous employer.
* **Prior Employer (Before That)** (`previous_employer_2`, optional): Enter the name of your second previous employer.
* **Prior Position** (`previous_position_2`, optional): Enter the job title at second previous employer.
* **Employment Dates** (`employment_dates_2`, optional): Enter the dates of employment with second previous employer.
* **Bar or Hospitality Experience** (`bar_related_experience`, required): Select your years of experience in the bar or hospitality industry.
* **Bar Positions Held** (`bar_positions_held`, optional): Select all applicable bar positions held.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, optional): Enter any relevant certifications.
* **Criminal Conviction History** (`criminal_history`, required): Select "Yes" if you have a criminal conviction, otherwise select "False".
* **If Yes, Provide Details** (`conviction_details`, optional): Enter a brief description of the conviction and date if applicable.
* **Professional References** (`professional_references`, optional): List 2-3 professional references with contact information.
* **I Consent to Background Check** (`willingness_verification`, required): Select "Yes" to consent to a background check.
