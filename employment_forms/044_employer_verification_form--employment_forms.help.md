# Employer Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Verification Form is used to gather essential details about an employee's job title, job description, work address, and employment status for payroll, tax, and benefits purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Select the correct employment status of the employee from the options provided.
3. Enter the employee's job title and job description in the respective fields.
4. Provide the employee's work address in the required format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to confirm whether the employee is employed or not. Please select the correct option from 'True' or 'False' to indicate the employee's employment status.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their business card or in the company's directory.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the employee's job role, including their key responsibilities and duties.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's work address in the format 'Street Address, City, State, Zip'.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): If you have already entered the employee's job title, you can skip this field. Otherwise, please enter it here.
