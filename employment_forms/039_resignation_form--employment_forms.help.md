# Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resignation Form is used by employees to notify their supervisor of their intention to leave the company. This form is a formal record of the employee's resignation, including their reason for leaving, effective date of resignation, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name, which is your official name as recorded in the company's system.
2. Provide a clear reason for your resignation in the Reason for Resigning field.
3. Enter the Effective Date of Resignation, which is the date you will be leaving the company.
4. Specify the Position Being Leaving, which is the job title and department you are leaving.
5. Enter the Last Day of Work, which is the last day you will be working before your resignation date.
6. If you are leaving, select the correct Signature from the dropdown list.
7. Enter your Employee Number, which is your unique identification number within the company.
8. Select any relevant departments you are leaving (e.g., Accounting, Sales, Marketing) in the Department field.
9. Enter the name of your supervisor, who should be informed of your resignation.
10. Review your answers carefully and ensure everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your official name as recorded in the company's system.
* **Reason for Resigning** (`reason_for_resigning`, text, required): Describe the reason you are leaving the company, e.g., career development, new opportunities, health reasons.
* **Effective Date of Resignation** (`effective_date_of_resignation`, date, required): Enter the date when your resignation becomes effective.
* **Position Being Leaving** (`position_being_leaving`, text, required): Specify the job title and department you are leaving.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, required): Enter the last day you will be working before your resignation date.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select the correct signature from the dropdown list.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, required): Enter your unique identification number within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant departments you are leaving (e.g., Accounting, Sales, Marketing).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor, who should be informed of your resignation.
