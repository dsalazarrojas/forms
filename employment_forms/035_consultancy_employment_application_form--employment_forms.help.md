# Consultancy Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Consultancy Employment Application Form collects information about your personal and professional details, work experience, and qualifications to help us determine whether you are a suitable candidate for our consultancy opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked as "true" in the form.
2. Enter your contact information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select your areas of expertise and experience honestly.
4. Choose your availability for travel if applicable.
5. Enter your education and certifications accurately.
6. Answer your skills and qualifications ratings truthfully.
7. Enter your desired salary and earliest possible start date.
8. Add any additional comments if you have a specific reason for your interest in our consultancy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name including any middle names or initials.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format (555) 555-5555.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: If you have a LinkedIn profile, enter the URL here. (Optional)
* **Current Address (Street, City, State, ZIP)**: Enter your current residential address accurately.
* **Current or Most Recent Job Title**: Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Total Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the total number of years you have worked in your profession.
* **Primary Consulting Domain/Expertise**: Select your primary area of consulting expertise.
* **Target Industries Served**: Select all industries that you have experience serving.
* **Highest Level of Education Attained**: Select your highest level of education achieved.
* **Major or Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of study from your highest level of education.
* **Relevant Professional Certifications**: Enter any relevant professional certifications you hold.
* **Data Analysis and Visualization Proficiency**: Rate your proficiency in data analysis and visualization on a scale of 1-5.
* **Executive Presentation and Communication**: Rate your proficiency in executive presentation and communication on a scale of 1-5.
* **Preferred Project Management Methodology**: Select your preferred project management methodology.
* **Willingness to Travel to Client Sites (%)**: Enter your willingness to travel to client sites as a percentage.
* **Earliest Possible Start Date**: Enter your earliest possible start date.
* **Desired Annual Base Salary ($)**: Enter your desired annual base salary excluding bonuses.
* **Why are you interested in joining our consultancy?**: Enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in joining our consultancy.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date of application.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of being considered for our consultancy opportunities.
