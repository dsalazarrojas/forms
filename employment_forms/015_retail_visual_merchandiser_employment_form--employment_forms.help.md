# Retail Visual Merchandiser Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from job applicants for retail visual merchandiser positions. Please ensure that you only complete this form for a retail visual merchandiser job application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Visual Merchandiser Employment Form** (<name>: text, optional): This is the main title of the form.
* **Job Title** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Title Description** (<label>: text, optional): Briefly describe your current or desired job title.
* **Company** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your current or desired company name.
* **Department** (<label>: select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are currently working in the department, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Job Type** (<label>: text, optional): Briefly describe the type of job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (<label>: text, optional): Briefly describe your current or desired job description.
* **Salary Range** (<label>: number, optional): Enter your current or desired salary range.
* **Available to Start Date** (<label>: date, optional): Select the date you are available to start working.
* **Available to Start Time** (<label>: time, optional): Select the time you are available to start working.
* **Available to Start Time H24** (<label>: text, optional): Enter the 24-hour time format for your available start time.
* **Email** (<label>: email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your professional phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Resume** (<label>: text, optional): Attach your resume or CV.
* **References** (<label>: text, optional): Enter relevant references, such as previous employers or colleagues.
* **Work Experience** (<label>: text, optional): Briefly describe your work experience.
* **Work Experience Start Date** (<label>: date, optional): Select the start date of your work experience.
* **Work Experience End Date** (<label>: date, optional): Select the end date of your work experience.
* **Current Employer** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your current or previous employer name.
* **Supervisor Name** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your supervisor's job title.
* **Supervisor Work Experience** (<label>: text, optional): Briefly describe your supervisor's work experience.
* **Supervisor Phone** (<label>: text, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Supervisor Email** (<label>: email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **References** (<label>: text, optional): Enter relevant references, such as previous employers or colleagues.

Please review each field carefully and provide accurate and complete information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
