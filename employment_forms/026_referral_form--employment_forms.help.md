# Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Form is designed to collect information about referrals made by employees to support the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the referral information in the "Referral Information" section.
2. Provide the contact information of the referee in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Answer questions about the referee's phone type and availability in the "Referee Phone" section.
4. Enter the referee's name and email address in the "Referee Name" and "Referee Email" sections.
5. Enter the company name and phone number in the "Company Name" and "Company Phone" sections.
6. Answer questions about the company's phone type and job status in the "Company Phone Type" and "Job Status" sections.
7. Enter the employee's name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Email" sections.
8. Enter the employee's job title and job type in the "Job Title" and "Job Type" sections.
9. Select the industry, job location, and job category in the "Industry", "Job Location", and "Job Category" sections.
10. Answer questions about the employment type and job level in the "Employment Type" and "Job Level" sections.
11. Enter any job notes or description in the "Job Description" and "Job Notes" sections.
12. Enter a referral message in the "Referral Message" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Information**: Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the person making the referral.
* **Referee Information**: Enter details about the referee, such as name, job title, and job type.
* **Referee Phone**: Select whether the referee has a phone and what type of phone they have.
* **Referee Phone Type**: Select whether the referee's phone is available.
* **Referee Name**: Enter the name of the referee.
* **Referee Email**: Enter the email address of the referee.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Company Phone Type**: Select whether the company has a phone.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee making the referral.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job the employee has.
* **Industry**: Select the industry of the company.
* **Job Location**: Select the location of the job.
* **Job Category**: Select the category of the job.
* **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of the job.
* **Job Level**: Select the level of the job.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the job.
* **Referral Message**: Enter a message for the referrer to include with their referral.
* **Job Location**: Select the location of the job.
