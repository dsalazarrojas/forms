# Editor Selection Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editor Selection Application form is used to collect relevant details about a candidate's application for an editor position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your application details in the `Application Details` field.
2. Provide your candidate's details, including their name, experience, and writing samples.
3. Specify whether the candidate has a publisher or content team experience.
4. Choose the current status of your application (Active or Inactive).
5. Enter the dates when your application was submitted and when it was published.
6. (Optional) Upload a link to your application, provide a brief text description, and specify whether it's a valid link.
7. Enter any additional comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`application_details`, `text`, `required`): Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, `required`): Enter your candidate's name and other relevant details.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, `optional`): Specify your candidate's experience in the editor role.
* **Writing Samples** (`writing_samples`, `text`, `required`): Upload a link to your candidate's writing samples.
* **Publisher Details** (`publisher_details`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Check if your candidate has publisher or content team experience.
* **Content Team Details** (`content_team_details`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Check if your candidate has content team experience.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the current status of your application (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date when your application was submitted.
* **Published Date** (`published_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date when your application was published.
* **Application Link** (`application_link`, `text`, `optional`): Upload a link to your application.
* **Application Link Name** (`application_link_name`, `text`, `optional`): Provide a brief text description of your application link.
* **Application Link Type** (`application_link_type`, `select_one`, `optional`): Specify whether the link is valid or not.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, `required`): Enter your candidate's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your candidate's phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your candidate's website URL.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your candidate's social media links.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments about your application.
* **Candidate ID** (`candidate_id`, `number`, `required`): Enter your candidate's ID.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your candidate's name.
* **Experience Length** (`experience_length`, `text`, `required`): Specify your candidate's experience length.
* **Writing Samples File** (`writing_samples_file`, `text`, `optional`): Upload a link to your candidate's writing samples.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, `optional`): Upload a link to your candidate's resume.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, `optional`): Upload a link to your candidate's cover letter.
* **Application Link URL** (`application_link_url`, `text`, `optional`): Upload a link to your application.
* **Application Link Type** (`application_link_type`, `select_one`, `optional`): Specify whether the link is valid or not.
