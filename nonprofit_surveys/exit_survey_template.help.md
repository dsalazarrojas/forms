# exit_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals completing or leaving a nonprofit program or organization. It helps the organization understand what they did well and where they can improve to better serve their participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about your overall satisfaction with the organization/program.
2. Choose the primary reason you are leaving or completing the program.
3. Select all the aspects of your involvement that you found most valuable.
4. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
5. Rate your likelihood of recommending the organization/program to others.
6. Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with your overall experience with the organization/program?**
	+ This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the program. Please choose from the following options:
		- Very satisfied
		- Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Dissatisfied
		- Very dissatisfied
* **What was the primary reason for your departure or completing the program?**
	+ This question helps us understand why you are leaving or completing the program. Choose the best answer from the options provided:
		- New opportunity
		- Personal reasons
		- Program completion
		- Relocation
		- Dissatisfaction
		- Other
* **Which aspects of your involvement did you find most valuable? (Select all that apply)**
	+ This question allows you to highlight the aspects of the program that you found most beneficial. Select all the options that apply:
		- Community connection
		- Skill development
		- Support services
		- Mission impact
		- Resources provided
* **What could the organization/program improve upon to better serve its participants?**
	+ This question is an open-ended text field for you to provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **Would you recommend this organization/program to others?**
	+ This question is a simple yes/no question about whether you would recommend the program to others. Choose from the following options:
		- Definitely yes
		- Likely yes
		- Neutral
		- Unlikely
		- Definitely no
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**
	+ This is a text field for you to provide any additional feedback or thoughts you have about the program.

## Tips

- Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help the organization improve.
- Select all that apply for the "Which aspects of your involvement did you find most valuable?" question.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
