# demographic_survey_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Demographic Survey Questionnaire** is a brief form designed to collect general demographic information for nonprofit program planning and reporting. This form will help organizations understand the characteristics and backgrounds of their target audience, which is essential for effectively developing and delivering programs that cater to their needs. By providing accurate and comprehensive demographic data, this form will assist nonprofits in making informed decisions and improving their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing the best description that applies to you.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief but accurate answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What is your age group?
 * **Age Group** (Single): Use the dropdown menu to select your age group, ranging from Under 18 to 65+.

### How do you describe your gender identity?
 * **Gender Identity** (Single): Choose how you describe your gender identity from the options provided, including Woman, Man, Non-binary, Prefer to self-describe, or Prefer not to say.

### Which of the following best describes your racial or ethnic background? (Select all that apply)
 * **Racial or Ethnic Background** (Multiple): Choose all the options that best describe your racial or ethnic background from the list provided.

### What is the highest level of education you have completed?
 * **Education Level** (Single): Select the option that best describes your highest level of education, such as High School, Some College, or Bachelor's Degree.

### What is your current employment status?
 * **Employment Status** (Single): Choose your current employment status from the options provided, such as Employed Full-time, Unemployed, or Retired.

### What is your approximate annual household income before taxes?
 * **Annual Household Income** (Single): Use the provided range to estimate your approximate annual household income before taxes.

### In which city or region do you primarily reside?
 * **Residence Location** (Text): Provide the name of the city or region where you primarily reside.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question, as your responses will help inform the development and delivery of nonprofit programs.
* If you are unsure or prefer not to answer a question, you may skip it or select the "Prefer not to say" option.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
