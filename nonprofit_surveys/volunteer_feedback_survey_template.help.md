# Volunteer Feedback Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to provide step-by-step instructions on how to complete the volunteer feedback survey template.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Overall Satisfaction** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
	* Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with your volunteer experience with our organization.
2. **Volunteer Frequency** (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, A few times a year, Rarely)
	* Choose the frequency with which you typically volunteer with our organization.
3. **Current Volunteer Role** (Engaging and fulfilling, Clearly defined tasks, Well-supported, Flexible, Challenging, Repetitive)
	* Select all the aspects that best describe your current volunteer role with our organization.
4. **Training and Resources** (Always, Mostly, Sometimes, Rarely, Never)
	* Indicate how often you felt you received adequate training and resources for your volunteer tasks.
5. **Likelihood to Recommend** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely)
	* Choose how likely you are to recommend volunteering with our organization to a friend or colleague.
6. **Additional Comments** 
	* Provide any specific feedback or suggestions for improvements to our volunteer program.
7. **Open-ended Question**
	* Share any other comments or feedback you have for our volunteer program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction**
	* This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with your volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Frequency**
	* This question is used to gauge how often you participate in volunteer activities with our organization.
* **Current Volunteer Role**
	* This is a multiple-choice question that asks you to describe your current volunteer role.
* **Training and Resources**
	* This question assesses how well-equipped you feel to perform your volunteer tasks.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**
	* This question measures how likely you are to recommend our volunteer program to others.
* **Additional Comments**
	* This is an open-ended question for you to share any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific with your feedback. Your input is crucial to helping us improve our programs and services.
* If you've had a positive experience, let us know! Your feedback is valuable for helping us build on our strengths.
* If you've had a negative experience, please be constructive with your feedback. We're committed to learning from it and making improvements.
* You can share your feedback with us anonymously or provide your name and contact information if you'd like to receive updates on our progress and changes.
