# Event planning survey template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of our recent event to help us improve future nonprofit activities and engagement. Your input will help us understand what we did well and what we can do better.

## How to Complete This Form
To provide the most valuable feedback, please answer the questions honestly and briefly. Each section is designed to gather specific information, so choose the best option for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with the overall event experience?**: This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the event. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
	+ Very satisfied: You were extremely happy with the event.
	+ Satisfied: You were somewhat happy with the event.
	+ Neutral: You had mixed feelings about the event.
	+ Dissatisfied: You were somewhat unhappy with the event.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You were extremely unhappy with the event.
* **Which aspects or sessions did you find most valuable? (Select all that apply)**: This question helps us understand what made the event valuable to you. Choose all that apply from the options below.
	+ Keynote speaker
	+ Workshop sessions
	+ Networking opportunities
	+ Panel discussions
	+ Informational booths
	+ Volunteer activities
* **Was the event well-organized and easy to navigate?**: This question helps us understand how smoothly the event was executed.
	+ true: The event was very well-organized and easy to navigate.
	+ Mostly: The event was mostly well-organized and easy to navigate.
	+ false: The event was poorly organized or difficult to navigate.
* **How likely are you to attend a similar event organized by us in the future?**: This question helps us understand your interest in future events.
	+ Very likely: You are extremely likely to attend a similar event.
	+ Likely: You are somewhat likely to attend a similar event.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about attending a similar event.
	+ Unlikely: You are somewhat unlikely to attend a similar event.
	+ Very unlikely: You are extremely unlikely to attend a similar event.
* **What was your primary motivation for attending this event?**: This question helps us understand what motivated you to attend the event.
	+ Support the cause
	+ Learn something new
	+ Network with others
	+ Socialize
	+ Specific speaker/topic
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for future events?**: This is an open-ended question for you to share any thoughts, suggestions, or ideas you have about future events.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and briefly.
- Choose the best option for each question.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the text field.
- Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve future events!
