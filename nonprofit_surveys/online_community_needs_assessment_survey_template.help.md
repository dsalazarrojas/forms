# online_community_needs_assessment_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather insights from online community members to improve their experience and support within the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You can select all that apply for the question "What do you primarily use our online community for?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our online community platform?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`)
	+ This question asks about your current level of satisfaction with the community platform. Please choose the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How often do you engage with our online community?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`)
	+ This question seeks to understand how frequently you interact with the community. Select the option that reflects your typical engagement frequency.
* **What do you primarily use our online community for?** (`Networking`, `Information sharing`, `Support/Advice`, `Event announcements`, `General discussion`, `Resource discovery`)
	+ This question aims to understand the primary purpose of your community engagement. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **What improvements would you suggest for our online community?** (`text`)
	+ This open-ended question invites you to share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the community. Be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about our online community?** (`text`)
	+ This question allows you to provide any additional comments, thoughts, or feelings about the community that you didn't mention earlier.
