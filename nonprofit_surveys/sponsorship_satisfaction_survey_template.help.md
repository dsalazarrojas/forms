# sponsorship_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from our valuable sponsors to improve their experience and strengthen our partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully, using the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
2. For the open-ended question "What specific improvements could enhance your sponsorship experience?", share your thoughts and suggestions in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your experience as a sponsor of our organization?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience as a sponsor.
* **How would you rate the clarity and frequency of communication from our organization?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Choose the rating that best reflects your experience with our communication.
* **Which of the following benefits are most important to your organization when sponsoring? (Select all that apply)** (`Brand exposure`, `Community impact`, `Networking opportunities`, `Employee engagement`, `Alignment with values`): Select all the benefits that are most important to your organization.
* **Do you feel your sponsorship effectively supports your organization's goals?** (`Strongly agree`, `Agree`, `Neutral`, `Disagree`, `Strongly disagree`): Rate how well you feel your sponsorship supports your organization's goals.
* **How likely are you to renew your sponsorship with us in the future?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Rate how likely you are to renew your sponsorship.
* **What specific improvements could enhance your sponsorship experience?**: Share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve your sponsorship experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**: Share any other feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your answers, as your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our sponsorship experience.
* Select all relevant benefits that are important to your organization.
* Share specific, actionable suggestions for improvement.
