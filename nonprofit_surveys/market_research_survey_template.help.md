# market_research_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from the public on their perception and opinions about a nonprofit organization's work in the community. The survey aims to inform strategic planning and help the organization better understand what aspects of their work are most important to the public and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options for "How would you describe your overall impression of our organization's work in the community?"
2. Select all that apply for "Which aspects of our nonprofit's work are most important to you?"
3. Choose one of the options for "How likely are you to recommend supporting our nonprofit to others?"
4. Type in your answer to "What specific improvements or changes would you suggest for our organization's efforts?"
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback regarding the nonprofit in the "Additional comments or feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your overall impression of our organization's work in the community?** (Very positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very negative): This question helps us understand your general feelings about our organization's work in the community.
* **Which aspects of our nonprofit's work are most important to you?** (Select all that apply): This question allows you to choose from a list of options that best represent what matters most to you about our organization's work.
	+ **Impact on community**: This option is for aspects of our work that have a direct impact on the community, such as providing food, shelter, or education.
	+ **Transparency**: This option is for aspects of our work that are transparent, such as clear communication and accountability.
	+ **Communication**: This option is for aspects of our work that are related to communication, such as regular updates or social media engagement.
	+ **Specific programs**: This option is for particular programs or initiatives that our organization has implemented.
	+ **Volunteer opportunities**: This option is for ways in which you can participate in or support our programs.
	+ **Mission alignment**: This option is for aspects of our work that align with our mission statement.
* **How likely are you to recommend supporting our nonprofit to others?** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely): This question helps us understand your willingness to endorse our organization to others.
* **What specific improvements or changes would you suggest for our organization's efforts?**: This question allows you to provide specific and constructive feedback on how we can improve our work.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about our organization.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- You can select all that apply for the "Which aspects of our nonprofit's work are most important to you?" question.
- You can choose only one option for the "How likely are you to recommend supporting our nonprofit to others?" question.
- Please be specific and constructive when providing feedback in the "What specific improvements or changes would you suggest for our organization's efforts?" field.
