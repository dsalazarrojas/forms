# life-insurance-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the life-insurance-intake-form, which is used to gather information about a life insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter the customer's age in the "Customer Age" field.
5. Select the type of policy from the "Policy Type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the start date of the policy.
7. Choose the end date of the policy.
8. Enter the policy's premium amount in the "Policy Premium" field.
9. Enter the policy's coverage amount in the "Policy Coverage" field.
10. Select the policy status from the "Policy Status" dropdown menu.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the policy in the "Policy Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Age**: Enter the customer's age. This will be used to determine eligibility for the policy.
* **Policy Type**: Select the type of policy (e.g. Life Insurance, Health Insurance, Disability Insurance).
* **Policy Start Date**: Enter the start date of the policy.
* **Policy End Date**: Enter the end date of the policy.
* **Policy Premium**: Enter the premium amount for the policy.
* **Policy Coverage**: Enter the coverage amount for the policy.
* **Policy Status**: Select the status of the policy (e.g. Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Policy Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the policy (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your insurance agent or representative for assistance.
* This form is designed to be filled out by the customer, but if you're entering information on their behalf, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
