# Medical Coverage Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Coverage Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about an individual's insurance coverage for medical purposes. This form is designed to be completed by the individual or their representative to provide necessary details for insurance coverage assessment. It is intended for use in medical settings where understanding an individual's insurance coverage is crucial for providing proper care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, or as much as possible, if some sections are not applicable to you.
2. Select the relevant option for the "provider" field, if applicable.
3. Fill out the "procedure" field with the specific medical procedure or treatment you are inquiring about.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General** (`general`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief overview of your medical coverage information, including your insurance provider and policy number.
* **provider** (`provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the name of the insurance provider that covers you. This may be your primary provider or an additional provider you have.
* **procedure** (`procedure`, `text`, required: false): Specify the medical procedure or treatment you are inquiring about. This could be surgery, medication, or other medical services.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure your insurance coverage can be properly assessed.
* If you are unsure about any section or field, you may ask your healthcare provider for clarification or guidance.
* If you have any concerns or questions about your insurance coverage, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
