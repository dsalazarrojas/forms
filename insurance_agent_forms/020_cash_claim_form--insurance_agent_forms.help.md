# Cash Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Claim Form is used to collect cash claim information from clients or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the claimant's information: 
   - Fill out the **Claimant Full Name** field with the claimant's full name.
   - Enter the **Claimant ID Number or Policy Number** field with the claimant's ID or policy number.
   - Enter the **Phone Number** field with the claimant's phone number.
   - Enter the **Email Address** field with the claimant's email address.
2. Enter the claim information:
   - Select the **Type of Claim** field with the type of claim (e.g., Expense Reimbursement, Petty Cash Replenishment, etc.).
   - Enter the **Date of Incident or Expense** field with the date of the incident or expense.
   - Enter the **Total Claim Amount** field with the total amount of the claim.
   - Select the **Currency** field with the currency of the claim.
   - Enter the **Description of Claim** field with a detailed explanation of the claim.
   - Enter the **Itemized List of Expenses** field with a list of itemized expenses.
3. Confirm the claim submission:
   - Indicate whether **Receipts Attached** are available or not.
   - Select the **What Documentation is Included?** field with the supporting documentation provided.
4. Confirm the approval:
   - Enter the **Department Manager Name** field with the department manager's name.
   - Indicate whether **Manager Pre-approval Obtained?** is approved or not.
   - Enter the **Account Code or Cost Center** field with the account code or cost center.
5. Specify the payment method:
   - Select the **Preferred Payment Method** field with the preferred payment method (e.g., Check, Direct Deposit, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Full Name** (Required): Enter the claimant's full name.
* **Claimant ID Number or Policy Number** (Required): Enter the claimant's ID or policy number.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Claim Number** (Optional): Enter the claim number if applicable.
* **Claim Date** (Required): Enter the claim date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Type of Claim** (Required): Select the type of claim (e.g., Expense Reimbursement, Petty Cash Replenishment, etc.).
* **Date of Incident or Expense** (Required): Enter the date of the incident or expense in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Total Claim Amount** (Required): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Currency** (Required): Select the currency of the claim.
* **Description of Claim** (Required): Provide a detailed explanation of the claim.
* **Itemized List of Expenses** (Required): List each expense with its amount.
* **Receipts Attached** (Required): Indicate whether receipts are attached or not.
* **What Documentation is Included?** (Optional): Select all supporting documentation provided.
* **Department Manager Name** (Required): Enter the department manager's name.
* **Manager Pre-approval Obtained?** (Required): Indicate whether pre-approval is obtained or not.
* **Account Code or Cost Center** (Required): Enter the account code or cost center.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Required): Select the preferred payment method (e.g., Check, Direct Deposit, etc.).
