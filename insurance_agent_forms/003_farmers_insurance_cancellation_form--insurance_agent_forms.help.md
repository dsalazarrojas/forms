## Purpose
The Farmers Insurance Cancellation Form is designed to be used by policyholders to cancel their insurance policies with Farmers Insurance. To ensure you complete the form correctly, please use it for its intended purpose only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the policyholder in the "Policyholder Name" field.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for cancellation in the "Cancellation Reason" field.
3. Briefly describe your policy details in the "Policy Details" field.

* **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your policy, including your policy number, type, or any other relevant information.
