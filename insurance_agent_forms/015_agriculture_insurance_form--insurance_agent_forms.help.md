# Agriculture Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for agriculture insurance policies. It helps us understand your farm's details and needs to provide you with the best insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your **Insured Name**, the full name of the policyholder.
2. Enter the **Business or Farm Name**, which is the name of your agricultural operation.
3. If you have an existing policy number, provide it in the **Policy Number** field.
4. Choose the type of **Policy Type** you need, such as New Application, Policy Renewal, or Claim Inquiry.
5. Select the **Insurance Coverage Type** you require, such as Crop insurance or Livestock insurance.
6. Enter your **Street Address**, which is the primary farm address.
7. Enter your **City** and **State** for the farm location.
8. Enter your **ZIP Code** for the farm location.
9. Provide your **Phone Number** and **Email Address** for contact purposes.
10. Enter the **Total Farm Acreage** you operate.
11. Select all the **Crops to Insure** you need coverage for.
12. Select all the **Livestock to Insure** you need coverage for.
13. Choose the **Desired Coverage Level** you require, such as Catastrophic (CAT) or 65% coverage.
14. Enter the **Estimated Insured Value (USD)** of your farm.
15. If you have a **Previous Insurance Claims** history, select the relevant option.
16. Provide **Claims Details** if you have a claims history.
17. Indicate if your farm is **Irrigated** or not.
18. Choose whether you use **Conservation Practices** or not.
19. Select your **Farm Ownership** status, such as Owner or Leasing.
20. Enter the number of **Years Operating the Farm**.
21. Select all the **Additional Structures** on your farm property.
22. Choose your **Desired Deductible Amount**, such as $5,000 or Not sure.
23. If you have an assigned **Insurance Agent Name**, provide it.
24. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method**, such as Phone or Email.
25. Sign with your **Applicant Signature** to complete the form.
26. Enter the **Application Date** when you're ready to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Insured Name**: Enter your full name as the policyholder.
* **Business or Farm Name**: Enter the name of your agricultural operation.
* **Policy Number**: If you have an existing policy, enter the number here.
* **Policy Type**: Choose the type of policy you need, such as New Application or Policy Renewal.
* **Insurance Coverage Type**: Select all the coverage types you require, such as Crop insurance or Livestock insurance.
* **Street Address**: Enter the primary farm address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your farm is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where your farm is located.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code for your farm location.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Total Farm Acreage**: Enter the total acres you operate.
* **Crops to Insure**: Select all the crops you need coverage for.
* **Livestock to Insure**: Select all the livestock you need coverage for.
* **Desired Coverage Level**: Choose the desired coverage level, such as 65% or 85% coverage.
* **Estimated Insured Value (USD)**: Enter the total insured value in USD.
* **Previous Insurance Claims**: Select the relevant option based on your claims history.
* **Claims Details**: Provide details about your previous claims if applicable.
* **Irrigation Status**: Indicate if your farm is irrigated or not.
* **Conservation Practices**: Choose whether you use conservation practices or not.
* **Farm Ownership**: Select your farm ownership status, such as Owner or Leasing.
* **Years Operating Farm**: Enter the number of years you've operated the farm.
* **Additional Structures**: Select all the structures on your farm property.
* **Desired Deductible Amount**: Choose the desired deductible amount, such as $5,000 or Not sure.
* **Insurance Agent Name**: If you have an assigned agent, enter their name.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose the preferred contact method, such as Phone or Email.
* **Applicant Signature**: Sign with your digital signature to complete the form.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date when you're ready to submit the form.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your insurance agent or contact us.
* Review our FAQs or comprehensive guide for more detailed information.
