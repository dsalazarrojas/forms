# Health Insurance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from policyholders about their experiences with insurance plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out each field as instructed:

1.  **Policyholder Information**: Enter the policyholder's details, including their name and other relevant information.
2.  **Insurance Plan**: Select the insurance plan you would like to evaluate.
3.  **Evaluation**: Rate your satisfaction with the selected plan on a numerical scale.
4.  **Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the selected plan on a numerical scale.
5.  **Recommendations**: Choose one or more plans you would recommend to others.
6.  **Feedback**: Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
7.  **Contact**: Enter your contact information to help us get back to you.
8.  **Contact Time**: Specify when you would like to be contacted.
9.  **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up.
10. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
11. **Policyholder's Email**: Enter the policyholder's email address. (Note: This is a separate field from yours.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder Information** (`policyholder_info`, text, required): Enter the policyholder's details, including their name and other relevant information.
*   **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, text, required): Select the insurance plan you would like to evaluate.
*   **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the selected plan on a numerical scale.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the selected plan on a numerical scale.
*   **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_one, required): Choose one or more plans you would recommend to others.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, required): Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, date, required): Enter your contact information to help us get back to you.
*   **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, required): Specify when you would like to be contacted.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Policyholder's Email** (`policyholder_email`, text, required): Enter the policyholder's email address.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is collected.
*   Be honest and accurate when answering questions and providing feedback.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
