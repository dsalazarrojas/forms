# Claim Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a claimant who has experienced an incident related to an insurance policy. The form is meant to be completed by the claimant to provide details about the incident, including the claimant's information, the incident's details, and any supporting documentation that will be submitted as evidence.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by carefully reading the instructions and explanations provided for each field.
- Ensure that you have the necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
- Enter the required information for each field, and select any relevant options from the dropdown menus.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Full Name** (`claimant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person filing the claim.
* **Relationship to Insured** (`relationship_to_insured`, `select_one`, required): Select how you are related to the insured person. This helps us understand who is filing the claim.
* **Insured Person Name** (`insured_name`, `text`, not required): If the claimant is not the insured person, enter the insured person's name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter the number of your insurance policy.
* **Group Number** (`group_number`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the group number associated with your policy.
* **Email Address** (`claimant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`claimant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`claimant_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address with city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the incident. This will help us understand the nature of the claim.
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Detailed Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Description of Damages or Losses** (`damages_description`, `text`, required): Describe what was damaged or lost.
* **Estimated Claim Amount** (`estimated_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated amount of the claim.
* **Was a Third Party Involved** (`third_party_involved`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if another person or entity was involved.
* **Third Party Information** (`third_party_info`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the third party.
* **Was a Police Report Filed** (`police_report_filed`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if a police report was filed.
* **Police Department Name** (`police_department`, `text`, not required): If a report was filed, enter the police department name.
* **Police Report Number** (`police_report_number`, `text`, not required): If a report was filed, enter the case or report number.
* **Was Medical Treatment Received** (`medical_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if medical treatment was received.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider`, `text`, not required): If medical treatment was received, enter the name of the medical provider.
* **Documentation to Attach** (`documentation_attached`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any applicable documentation to be attached.
* **Prior Claims Filed** (`prior_claims`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if prior claims were filed.
* **Legal Representation** (`attorney_representative`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no if you have legal representation.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any other relevant details about the incident.
