# Personal Accident Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Accident Claim Form is used to collect information from claimants who have experienced a personal accident. This form helps insurance agents to gather details about the claimant, the accident, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanation below.
2. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
3. Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Date of Accident** (`date_of_accident`, date, required): Enter the date of the accident in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Time of Accident** (`time_of_accident`, time, required): Enter the time of the accident in the format "HH:MM AM/PM" or "HH:MM 24-hour format".
* **Location of Accident** (`location_of_accident`, text, optional): Enter the location where the accident occurred.
* **Description of Accident** (`description_of_accident`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the accident.
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant injury details, such as the nature of the injury or any medical treatment received.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): If there were any witnesses to the accident, please provide their contact information and a brief description of their involvement.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your current contact information, including your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your current email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your insurance agent for assistance.
