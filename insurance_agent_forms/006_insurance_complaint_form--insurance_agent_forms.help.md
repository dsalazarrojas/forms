# Insurance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Complaint Form is used to collect and process complaints from policyholders, allowing insurance agents to efficiently manage and resolve issues related to policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
3. Attach any supporting documentation, such as files or signatures, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Policyholder Information
* **Policyholder Information** (required): This field is used to collect information about the policyholder, including their name, email, and contact number.
* **Policyholder** (required): Enter the name of the policyholder.
* **email** (required): Enter the email address of the policyholder.
* **Contact Number** (required): Enter the contact number of the policyholder.

### Complaint Details
* **Complaint Type** (required): Select the type of complaint from the provided options (Policyholder, Coverage, or claim).
* **Description** (optional): Provide a brief description of the complaint.
* **Insurance Company** (required): Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Date** (required): Enter the date of the complaint.
* **Details** (optional): Provide additional details about the complaint.
* **Upload File** (optional): Attach any supporting files related to the complaint.
* **Upload Signature** (optional): Attach any supporting signatures related to the complaint.

### Policy Information
* **Policy Number** (required): Enter the policy number.
* **Address** (optional): Enter the policyholder's address.
* **Policy Address** (required): Enter the policyholder's address.
* **Policy Date** (required): Enter the date of the policy.

### Agent Information
* **Agent** (required): Enter the name of the insurance agent.
* **Agent Name** (required): Enter the name of the insurance agent.
* **Policy Address** (optional): Enter the insurance company's address.
* **Policy Date** (optional): Enter the date of the policy.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the insurance company's support team for assistance.
* Attach any supporting documentation, such as files or signatures, if required.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accurate and complete information.
