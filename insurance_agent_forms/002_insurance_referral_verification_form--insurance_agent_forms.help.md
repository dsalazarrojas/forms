# Insurance Referral Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify the accuracy of information provided by an individual or organization regarding an insurance referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with the details of the individual or organization making the referral.
2. In the Referral Information section, provide a brief description of the referral.
3. In the Verification section, select one of the options to indicate the level of verification of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section is where you enter the details of the individual or organization making the referral. This may include their name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Referral Information**: This section provides a space to describe the referral in brief. You can enter any relevant details about the referral, such as the type of insurance product being referred, the client's name, or any other relevant information.
* **Verification**: This section asks you to indicate the level of verification of the information provided. Select one of the following options:
	+ **Client's information is verified and correct**: This option is selected when the information provided is accurate and trustworthy.
	+ **Client's information is verified, but some discrepancies**: This option is selected when the information provided has some inaccuracies or inconsistencies.
	+ **Client's information is not verified**: This option is selected when the information provided cannot be verified or is not trustworthy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, select the appropriate verification option to indicate your level of confidence in the accuracy of the information.
