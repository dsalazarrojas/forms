# Insurance Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity and authorization of patients or policyholders to access certain medical procedures or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to include all required fields (if any).
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, optional): Enter the patient's policy number if applicable.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the policy is effective.
* **Insurance Agent** (`insurance_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance agent handling the claim.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of policy held by the patient or policyholder.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
* **Patient/Policyholder Consent** (`policy_holder_consent`, text, optional): Obtain the patient's or policyholder's signature and consent for the form.
* **Policyholder Signature** (`policy_holder_signature`, note, optional): A space for the patient or policyholder to sign and date their consent.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance company providing the policy.
