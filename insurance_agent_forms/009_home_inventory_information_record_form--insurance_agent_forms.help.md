# Home Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture and record information about a home inventory. It will help users gather and store details about their home inventory, including contact information, insurance details, and sync status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate through its sections and fields, which are explained below.
2. Fill out each field with your relevant details according to the hints provided.
3. Once all the required fields are filled, click on the submit button to save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about your home inventory.
* **Sync Info** (`sync_info`, text, required: false): Enter any sync information about your home inventory.
* **Sync Status** (`sync_status`, select multiple, required: false): Select the sync status of your home inventory (Active or Inactive).
* **Sync App** (`sync_app`, select one, required: false): Select if you want to sync your home inventory (Yes or No).
* **Sync Type** (`sync_type`, select one, required: false): Select the type of sync you want to do (Yes or No).
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, required: false): Enter insurance information about your home inventory.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select one, required: false): Select if you have insurance for your home inventory (Yes or No).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for your home inventory.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time for your home inventory.
* **Datepicker** (`datepicker`, date, required: false): Select a date for your home inventory.
* **Timepicker** (`timepicker`, time, required: false): Select a time for your home inventory.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required: false): Enter a decimal value for your home inventory.
* **Notes1** (`notes1`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about your home inventory.
* **Notes2** (`notes2`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your home inventory.
* **Notes3** (`notes3`, note, required: false): Enter any further notes about your home inventory.
* **Notes4** (`notes4`, note, required: false): Enter any more notes about your home inventory.
* **Notes5** (`notes5`, note, required: false): Enter any final notes about your home inventory.
