# Insurance Benefits Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record a patient's or policyholder's consent for insurance benefits. It captures essential information such as category, description, and consent date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Category** from the dropdown menu. Choose one of the three options: "Insurance Agent Forms", "Insurance", or "Medical".
2. Enter a brief **Description** of the insurance benefits being consented to.
3. Indicate whether the policyholder will receive **Benefits**. Answer "True" or "False".
4. Enter the **Insurance ID** number associated with the policy.
5. Check the box for **Benefits Received**. Answer "True" or "False".
6. Enter the **Consent Date**, the date the consent was given.
7. Enter the **Facility Name**, where the policyholder received the insurance benefits.
8. Enter the **Patient Name**, the name of the policyholder receiving the benefits.
9. Sign the **Signature** section to confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
10. Add any additional **Note** if necessary.
11. Optionally, enter the **Assigned Tool** and **Output File** information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Required): Choose the relevant category from the dropdown menu. This could be "Insurance Agent Forms", "Insurance", or "Medical".
* **Description** (Required): Enter a brief description of the insurance benefits being consented to.
* **Will Have Benefits** (Required): Indicate whether the policyholder will receive benefits. Answer "True" or "False".
* **Insurance ID** (Required): Enter the insurance ID number associated with the policy.
* **Benefits Received** (Required): Check the box if the policyholder has received benefits. Answer "True" or "False".
* **Consent Date** (Required): Enter the date the consent was given.
* **Facility Name** (Required): Enter the name of the facility where the policyholder received the benefits.
* **Patient Name** (Required): Enter the name of the policyholder.
* **Signature** (Required): Sign the signature section to confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
* **Note** (Optional): Add any additional notes if necessary.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): Enter the name of the tool used for this insurance benefits consent.
* **Output File** (Optional): Enter the name of the output file used for this insurance benefits consent.
* **Form ID** (Optional): Enter a unique identifier for this form, if needed.
* **Form ID2**, **Form ID3**, **Form ID4**, **Form ID5**, **Form ID6**, **Form ID7**, **Form ID8**, **Form ID9**, **Form ID10**, and **Form ID11** (Optional): These fields may be used for internal identification purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** and **Form ID** are not typically used in practice but might be used for internal identification purposes. If you're unsure, leave these fields blank. 

Note: Ensure that you're filling out all mandatory fields (category, description, consent date, etc.) accurately before submitting the form.
