# Claimant Statement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed statements from claimants regarding incidents, including their contact information, incident dates, and witness details. The form is an essential tool for insurance agents to process claims efficiently and fairly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the claimant in the **Claimant full name** field.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number, in the **Contact information** field.
3. Enter the date of the incident in the **Incident date** field. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Write a detailed, chronological account of the events leading up to and following the incident in the **Detailed statement of events** field.
5. If applicable, include the names and contact information of any witnesses in the **Witness names and contacts** field.
6. If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them via the **Supporting documents note** field.
7. Type your name as a signature in the **Signature declaration** field, which will be accepted as your signature.
8. Read and agree to the **Privacy notice**, which explains how your statement will be used.
9. Indicate your preference for follow-up updates in the **Follow up preference** field.
10. Finally, select the current date in the **Submission date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant full name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact information**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you regarding your claim.
* **Incident date**: Record the date when the incident occurred. Use the `YYYY-MM-DD` format for accurate representation.
* **Detailed statement of events**: Describe the incident, including what happened, who was involved, and any other relevant details.
* **Witness names and contacts**: If there were witnesses to the incident, list their names and contact information here. If not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
* **Supporting documents note**: Upload any relevant documents, such as receipts, photos, or reports, that support your claim.
* **Signature declaration**: Type your name as a signature, which will be accepted as your signature.
* **Privacy notice**: Read and agree to the terms regarding how your statement will be used.
* **Follow up preference**: Indicate whether you want to receive updates on the status of your claim.
* **Submission date**: Select the current date, which will be used to record when you submitted your statement.
