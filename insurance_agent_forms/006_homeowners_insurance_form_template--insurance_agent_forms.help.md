# Homeowners Insurance Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for homeowners' insurance policies. It's essential to complete it accurately to ensure you receive the best possible coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your home address in the "Home Address" field. This is the address of your primary residence.
2. Provide your name in the "Name Of Insured" field. This is the name of the person(s) insured under the policy.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field. This is the date you were born.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is your contact phone number.
5. Select the type of your home from the options provided in the "Home Type" field. You can choose from House, Apartment, or Condo.
6. Select the location of your home from the options provided in the "Home Location" field. You can choose from Urban, Suburban, or Rural.
7. Enter the year your home was built in the "Home Year Built" field.
8. Enter the value of your home in the "Home Value" field. This is the current value of your home.
9. Select the status of your home insurance from the options provided in the "Home Insurance Status" field. You can choose from Active, Cancelled, or Expired.
10. If your policy has been cancelled, enter the reason for cancellation in the "Reason For Cancel" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address, including the house number and street name.
* **Name Of Insured**: Enter your full name, including your first and last names.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including the area code, phone prefix, and line number.
* **Home Type**:
	+ House: A single-family residence or a freestanding single-family home.
	+ Apartment: A rented or owned apartment in a building with multiple units.
	+ Condo: A condominium, often shared with other owners.
* **Home Location**:
	+ Urban: The form is located within a city or town.
	+ Suburban: The form is located within a city or town but not in the urban area.
	+ Rural: The form is located outside a city or town.
* **Home Year Built**: Enter the year your home was built or constructed.
* **Home Value**: Enter the current value of your home, including any mortgage or outstanding loans.
* **Home Insurance Status**:
	+ Active: Your policy is currently active and in effect.
	+ Cancelled: Your policy has been cancelled.
	+ Expired: Your policy has expired.
* **Reason For Cancel**: If your policy has been cancelled, enter the reason for cancellation.
