# Vehicle Damage Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and collect information about vehicle damage for insurance purposes. It is used to record details about the vehicle's condition, including the year, make, model, and VIN, as well as photos of the damage.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing the vehicle's details: year, make, model, and VIN.
2.  Describe the damage, including its location, description, and rating.
3.  Take photos of the damage (up to four) and upload them to the form.
4.  Select the damage type and location from the provided options.
5.  Enter any additional notes about the damage.
6.  Identify the inspector who performed the inspection.
7.  Enter the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **vehicle damage inspection form vehicle year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form vehicle make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of the vehicle. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form vehicle model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the vehicle. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form vehicle vin** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage location** (`damage_location`, `text`, required): Describe the location of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage description** (`damage_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form image 1** (`image1`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form image 2** (`image2`, `text`, required): Upload a second photo of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form image 3** (`image3`, `text`, required): Upload a third photo of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form image 4** (`image4`, `text`, required): Upload a fourth photo of the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage rating** (`damage_rating`, `number`, required): Select a damage rating from the provided options. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage notes** (`damage_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the damage. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form inspector** (`inspector`, `text`, required): Identify the inspector who conducted the inspection. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage type** (`damage_type`, `text`, optional): Select the type of damage from the provided options. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form damage location** (`damage_location`, `text`, optional): Select the location of the damage from the provided options. 
*   **vehicle damage inspection form inspection date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection. 

Note: The field "damage notes" is a note field and allows for free-form text input, while fields like "damage location" and "damage type" have pre-defined options, but are not explicitly marked as optional in the form schema. 

This guide is meant to be a general help guide and might not cover every possible scenario. Please reach out if you need further clarification or have specific questions about this form.
