# Vehicle Damage Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and collect information about vehicle damage for insurance purposes. It is used to record details about the vehicle's condition, including the year, make, model, and VIN, as well as photos of the damage.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing the vehicle's details: year, make, model, and VIN.
2.  Describe the damage, including its location, description, and rating.
3.  Take photos of the damage (up to four) and upload them to the form.
4.  Select the damage type and location from the provided options.
5.  Enter any additional notes about the damage.
6.  Identify the inspector who performed the inspection.
7.  Enter the date of the inspection.
