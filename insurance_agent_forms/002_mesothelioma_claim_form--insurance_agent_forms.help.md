# Mesothelioma Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mesothelioma Claim Form is designed for insurance agents to collect relevant information about mesothelioma claims. This form helps to gather essential details about the claimant, including their personal and medical history, to assess and process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with the claimant's first name.
2. Enter the claimant's last name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide the claimant's email address in the Email field.
4. Input the claimant's phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the date of mesothelioma diagnosis in the Date of Diagnosis field.
6. Describe the symptoms experienced by the claimant in the Symptoms field.
7. Provide the name of the doctor who diagnosed the mesothelioma in the Doctor Name field.
8. Specify the doctor's title in the Doctor Title field (if available).
9. Enter the claimant's date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
10. Input the date the claim was submitted in the Claim Date field.
11. Specify the name of the insurance provider in the Insurance Provider field.
12. Select the current claim status (Active, Inactive, or Closed) in the Claim Status field.
13. Choose the type of claim (Asbestos, Mesothelioma, or Other) in the Claim Type field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the claimant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the claimant's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the claimant's email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Input the claimant's phone number.
* **Date of Diagnosis** (`date_of_diagnosis`, date, required): Enter the date the mesothelioma was diagnosed.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe the symptoms experienced by the claimant.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the doctor who diagnosed the mesothelioma.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`, text, optional): Specify the doctor's title if available.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Specify the name of the insurance provider.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required): Select the current claim status (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of claim (Asbestos, Mesothelioma, or Other).

## Tips
* Ensure you have the claimant's consent to complete this form.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* Use the provided format for dates (e.g., `mm/dd/yyyy`).
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* This form is designed to collect specific and necessary information. Please fill it out carefully and accurately.
