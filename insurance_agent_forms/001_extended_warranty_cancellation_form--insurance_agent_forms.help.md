# Extended Warranty Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel an extended warranty for a customer's product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of warranty you wish to cancel.
2. Enter the warranty number associated with the product.
3. Enter the date you purchased the product.
4. Enter the date you cancelled the warranty.
5. Enter the date you received the cancellation.
6. Provide a reason for the cancellation.
7. Add any additional customer comments.
8. Choose the method of confirmation you would like to use (email, phone, or text message).
9. Select the status of the cancellation (pending, processing, or completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Warranty Type** (Warranty 1, Warranty 2, select_one)
Select the type of warranty you wish to cancel from the provided options.

### **Warranty Number** (Warranty Number, number, optional)
Enter the warranty number associated with the cancelled warranty.

### **Date Purchased** (Date Purchased, date, optional)
Enter the date you purchased the product.

### **Cancellation Date** (Cancellation Date, date, optional)
Enter the date you cancelled the warranty.

### **Date Cancellation Received** (Date Cancellation Received, date, optional)
Enter the date you received the cancellation.

### **Cancellation Reason** (Cancellation Reason, text, optional)
Provide a reason for cancelling the warranty.

### **Customer Comments** (Customer Comments, text, optional)
Add any additional comments or information about the cancellation.

### **Confirmation Method** (Email, Phone, Text Message, select_one)
Choose the method you would like to use for confirmation (email, phone, or text message).

### **Cancellation Status** (Pending, Processing, Completed, select_one)
Select the status of the cancellation (pending, processing, or completed).
