# Farmers Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary to provide an insurance quote for users. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that we can provide you with a suitable insurance quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address information, including address line 1, address line 2, city, state, and zip code.
2. Provide your date of birth and phone number.
3. Enter your vehicle's year, make, model, and license number.
4. Select your insurance type, coverage area, and coverage amount.
5. Choose your driver's license state and number.
6. Select your vehicle's license state and number.
7. Choose your driver's gender and vehicle color.
8. Enter the coverage start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, Optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, Optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, Optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, Optional): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, Optional): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, Optional): Select your insurance type.
* **Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the coverage area you wish to have.
* **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`, number, Optional): Enter the coverage amount.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of your coverage.
* **Driver License State** (`driver_license_state`, select_one, Optional): Select your driver's license state.
* **Vehicle License State** (`vehicle_license_state`, select_one, Optional): Select your vehicle's license state.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, Optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's license number.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of your coverage.
* **Vehicle Year Made** (`vehicle_year_made`, number, Optional): Enter your vehicle's year made.
* **Driver Gender** (`driver_gender`, select_one, Optional): Select your driver's gender.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, Optional): Enter your vehicle's model.
