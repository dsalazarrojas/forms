# Responsive Life Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to generate a life insurance quote. It is intended to gather contact details and coverage preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (contact)** (`text`, optional):
	+ This field is for your contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details.

### Life Insurance Quotes
* **Life Insurance Quotes (life_insurance_quotes)** (`select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select one or more life insurance quotes you are interested in:
		- Life Insurance Quotes
		- Term Life Insurance
		- Whole Life Insurance

### Coverage Period
* **Coverage Period (coverage_period)** (`number`, optional):
	+ Enter the coverage period you are interested in.

### Age
* **Age (age)** (`number`, optional):
	+ Enter your age.

### Occupation
* **Occupation (occupation)** (`text`, optional):
	+ This field is for your occupation. Please enter your profession.

### Address
* **Address (address)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter your address for contact purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.

### Email
* **Email (email)** (`email`, optional):
	+ Enter a valid email address.

### Quote
* **Quote (quote)** (`text`, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional information about your quote.

### Premium
* **Premium (premium)** (`text`, optional):
	+ This field is not required and will not affect the quote.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure the best possible quote.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you need to make changes or updates to your quote, please contact us directly.
