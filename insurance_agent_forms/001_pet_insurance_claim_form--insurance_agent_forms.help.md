# Pet Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report pet insurance claims to your insurance provider. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your claim is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your pet's information and the details of your claim.
2. Make sure to choose the correct claim type and status.
3. Review and verify the accuracy of your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Type**: Select whether your claim is 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us determine the type of claim you're submitting.
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Pet Breed**: Enter your pet's breed as it appears on your insurance policy.
* **Pet Age**: Enter your pet's age in years.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider as it appears on your policy.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date of the incident or event related to your claim.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount of the claim in the currency specified on your policy.
* **Claim Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened and why you're submitting this claim.
* **Claim Expenses**: Check all applicable expenses related to your claim, such as 'Yes' for veterinary bills and 'No' for other expenses. This will help us understand the full scope of your claim.
* **Claim Status**: Select the status of your claim as 'Active' or 'Inactive'. This will help us track your claim's progress.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to our support team for clarification.
* We'll review and process your claim in a timely and efficient manner once you've submitted the form.
