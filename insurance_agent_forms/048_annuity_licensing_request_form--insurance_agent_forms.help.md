# Annuity Licensing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from insurance agents applying for an annuity license. It is used by the licensing department to review and process the application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying close attention to the required fields.
2.  Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Ensure that all sections are filled in thoroughly.
4.  Review and submit the form only once.
5.  Sign and date the form as the last step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text): Enter your best contact number.
* **Mailing Address** (4, text): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (6, text): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **License Type** (7, select_one): Select the type of license you are applying for (Annuity Producer, Life and Annuity, Variable Annuity, Fixed Annuity Only, Renewal, Reinstatement).
* **State of Residence** (8, select_one): Select the state where you reside.
* **Licensing State** (9, text): Enter the state for which you are applying for the license.
* **NAIC Number** (10, text): If you have a NAIC number, enter it (if not, leave this field blank).
* **PNP Number** (11, text): If you have a PNP number, enter it (if not, leave this field blank).
* **Current Licenses** (12, text): List any current licenses you have (if any).
* **Current License Expiration** (13, date): Enter the expiration date of your current license (if applicable).
* **Pre-Licensing Education** (14, select_one): Select if you have completed pre-licensing education (Yes, Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Not Required).
* **Education Provider** (15, text): Enter the name of your education provider (if applicable).
* **Education Hours** (16, number): Enter the number of education hours completed (if applicable).
* **Education Completion Date** (17, date): Enter the date of your education completion (if applicable).
* **Exam Required** (18, select_one): Select if you are required to take an exam (True, False, Not Sure).
* **Exam Status** (19, select_one): Select your exam status (Passed, Scheduled, Not Scheduled, Not Required).
* **Exam Date** (20, date): Enter the date of your exam (if taken or scheduled).
* **Exam Score** (21, text): Enter your exam score (if passed).
* **Background Check** (22, select_one): Select if you have completed a background check (Yes, Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Fingerprints Submitted** (23, select_one): Select if you have submitted your fingerprints (True, False, Not Required).
* **Criminal History** (24, select_one): Select if you have any criminal history (True, False).
* **Criminal Details** (25, text): Enter any criminal history details (if yes).
* **Regulatory Actions** (26, select_one): Select if you have experienced any regulatory actions (True, False).
* **Regulatory Details** (27, text): Enter any regulatory history details (if yes).
* **Employment History** (28, text): Enter your employment history for the last 5 years (if applicable).
* **Sponsoring Insurer** (29, text): Enter the name of your sponsoring insurer (if applicable).
* **Business Entity** (30, select_one): Select your business entity type (Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Other).
* **Entity Name** (31, text): Enter your entity name (if applicable).
* **Entity FEIN** (32, text): Enter your FEIN (if applicable).
* **Application Fee** (33, number): Enter the application fee.
* **Payment Method** (34, select_one): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Money Order, Electronic Transfer).
* **Continuing Education** (35, select_one): Select if you require continuing education (Yes, Completed, Not Required, Not Applicable).
* **CE Hours** (36, number): Enter the number of continuing education hours completed (if applicable).
* **Submission Method** (37, select_one): Select how you wish to submit your application (Online, Mail, In Person, Fax).
* **Applicant Signature** (38, text): Sign your name to submit the application.
* **Signature Date** (39, date): Date of signature.
* **Additional Comments** (40, text): Enter any additional comments or information.
