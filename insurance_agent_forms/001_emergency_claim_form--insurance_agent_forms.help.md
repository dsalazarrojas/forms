# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from policyholders regarding an unexpected event or incident.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the claim in the **user_input** field.
2. Select how you wish to be contacted by the insurance company in the **contact_method** field.
3. Choose the type of emergency claim in the **report_type** field (e.g., accident, theft, natural disaster).
4. Enter the date of the emergency claim in the **claim_date** field.
5. Describe the details of the claim in the **report_details** field.
6. Enter your insurance information in the **insurance_info** field.
7. Enter your contact information in the **contact_info** field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for the **submit_button** field, which is a submission confirmation.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the **confirm_button** field, which is a confirmation of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **contact_method** (text, required: false): Select how you wish to be contacted by the insurance company (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **report_type** (text, required: false): Choose the type of emergency claim (e.g., accident, theft, natural disaster).
* **claim_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the emergency claim.
* **report_details** (note, required: false): Describe the details of the claim in more detail.
* **insurance_info** (text, required: false): Enter your insurance information.
* **contact_info** (text, required: false): Enter your contact information.
* **submit_button** (select_one, required: false): Confirm the submission of the form by selecting "Yes".
* **confirm_button** (select_one, required: false): Confirm the submission of the form by selecting "Yes".
