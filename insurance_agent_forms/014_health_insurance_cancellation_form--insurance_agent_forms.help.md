# Health Insurance Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for health insurance policy holders to initiate the cancellation process of their policies. It helps to gather the necessary details for a smooth and efficient cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for cancellation, which should be stated in the "Cancellation Reason" field.
2. Fill in your details in the "Policy Holder Name", "Policy Holder Email", "Policy Holder Phone", and "Policy Holder Address" fields with accurate information.
3. Enter your policy number in the "Policy Number" field.
4. Enter the effective date of cancellation in the "Effective Date Of Cancellation" field.
5. Confirm the cancellation in the "Confirm Cancellation" field.
6. In the "Cancellation Confirmation Message" field, provide a confirmation message for the cancellation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancellation Reason**: This field is for stating the reason why you want to cancel your health insurance policy. Please be specific and provide a clear explanation.
* **Date Of Cancellation**: This field is for entering the date when you want to cancel your policy. It should be in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Effective Date Of Cancellation**: This field is for specifying the effective date of cancellation. Enter the date when you want the cancellation to take effect.
* **Policy Holder Name**: This field is for entering your name as the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Email**: This is for entering your email address as the policy holder. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Policy Holder Phone**: This field is for entering your phone number as the policy holder.
* **Policy Number**: This field is for entering the number of your health insurance policy.
* **Reason For Cancellation**: This field is for providing the reason for cancelling your health insurance policy. Please keep it brief but clear.
* **Confirm Cancellation**: This field is for confirming that you want to cancel your policy. Please check the box to acknowledge.
* **Cancellation Confirmation Message**: This field is for providing a confirmation message for the cancellation process. Please enter a brief message.
