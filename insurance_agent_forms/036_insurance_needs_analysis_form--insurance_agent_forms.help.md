# Insurance Needs Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Needs Analysis Form is designed to help insurance agents understand new clients' insurance needs by collecting essential information about their personal details, employment status, occupation, insurance coverage, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the new client information, including their address, contact details, and relationship with the client.
2.  Select the types of policies they need, including auto, home, and life insurance.
3.  Specify their employment status, occupation, and annual income.
4.  Choose the coverage level (Low, Medium, or High) and reason for needing insurance (Health, Auto, Home, or Life).
5.  Provide any additional comments or details about their insurance needs.
6.  Specify the type and amount of insurance coverage they require.
7.  Enter the date of the insurance coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Client Info**: Enter the new client's name and other basic information.
*   **Client Policies**: Select all the types of policies the new client needs (Required).
*   **Coverage Needed**: Select all the types of coverage the new client needs (Required).
*   **New Client Address**: Enter the new client's address.
*   **New Client Contact**: Enter the new client's contact details.
*   **New Client Relationship**: Enter the new client's relationship with the client.
*   **New Client Annual Income**: Enter the new client's annual income.
*   **New Client Employment Status**: Select the new client's employment status (Required).
*   **New Client Occupation**: Enter the new client's occupation.
*   **New Client Previous Policies**: Enter any previous insurance policies the new client has.
*   **New Client Insurance Agent Name**: Enter the name of the insurance agent handling the client.
*   **New Client Insurance Agency**: Enter the name of the insurance agency the client is with.
*   **New Client Date Of Birth**: Enter the new client's date of birth.
*   **New Client Email**: Enter the new client's email address (Optional).
*   **New Client Phone**: Enter the new client's phone number (Optional).
*   **New Client Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the new client (Optional).
*   **New Client Previous Policies Details**: Enter any details about previous insurance policies the new client has.
*   **New Client Why Insurance Needed**: Enter the reason the new client needs insurance (Required).
*   **New Client Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the new client's insurance needs (Optional).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Level**: Select the level of insurance coverage the new client needs (Required).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Reason**: Select the reason for needing insurance coverage (Required).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Employer**: Enter the new client's insurance coverage employer (Optional).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Type**: Enter the type of insurance coverage the new client needs (Optional).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Amount**: Enter the amount of insurance coverage the new client needs (Optional).
*   **New Client Insurance Coverage Date**: Enter the date of the insurance coverage.

## Tips

*   Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure the analysis is accurate.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose.
*   Ask the insurance agent for help if you are unsure about any field.
*   Provide clear and concise information in each field.
