# Certificate Of Insurance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certificate of Insurance Request Form is used to request a Certificate of Insurance from an insurance company for business or organizational clients. It is a crucial document that provides proof of insurance coverage for projects or contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out your information: 
  - Request Date: Enter the date you are requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
  - Requester Name: Enter your full name.
  - Email Address: Enter your email address where you want the Certificate of Insurance to be delivered.
  - Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
  - Company Name: Enter the name of the insured business or organization.
  - Company Address: Enter the business or organization's mailing address.
- Choose the Certificate Type: Select the type of Certificate of Insurance needed.
  - General liability
  - Auto liability
  - Workers compensation
  - Professional liability
  - Property insurance
  - Umbrella coverage
  - All coverages
- Enter Certificate Holder Name and Address: 
  - Certificate Holder Name: Enter the name of the organization requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
  - Certificate Holder Address: Enter the address of the organization requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
- Additional Insured: If applicable, select to add the certificate holder as an additional insured.
- Waiver of Subrogation: If applicable, select to include the waiver of subrogation.
- Minimum Coverage Required: If applicable, enter the minimum coverage required.
- Project Description: Enter a brief description of the project or work being performed.
- Project Location: If applicable, enter the location of the project or work.
- Contract Dates: If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the contract.
- Delivery Method: Choose how you want the Certificate of Insurance to be delivered.
  - Email to requester
  - Email to certificate holder
  - Fax
  - Postal mail
  - Upload to portal
- Rush Request: If this is a rush request, select to expedite the process.
- Rush Reason: If applicable, enter the reason for the rush request.
- Special Requirements: If applicable, enter any special requirements.
- Signature: Sign your full name to confirm the request.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the Certificate of Insurance. This is the date you want the document delivered to you.
* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name as the person requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you want the Certificate of Insurance to be delivered.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the insured business or organization.
* **Company Address**: Enter the business or organization's mailing address.
* **Policy Number**: If known, enter the insurance policy number. (Optional)
* **Certificate Type**: Select the type of Certificate of Insurance needed. This could be General liability, Auto liability, Workers compensation, Professional liability, Property insurance, Umbrella coverage, or All coverages.
* **Certificate Holder Name**: Enter the name of the organization requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
* **Certificate Holder Address**: Enter the address of the organization requesting the Certificate of Insurance.
* **Additional Insured**: If this is an additional insured request, select to add the Certificate Holder as an additional insured.
* **Waiver of Subrogation**: If applicable, select to include the waiver of subrogation. 
* **Minimum Coverage Required**: If applicable, enter the minimum coverage required.
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the project or work being performed.
* **Project Location**: If applicable, enter the location of the project or work. (Optional)
* **Contract Start Date**: If applicable, enter the start date of the contract. (Optional)
* **Contract End Date**: If applicable, enter the end date of the contract. (Optional)
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want the Certificate of Insurance to be delivered. This could be Email to requester, Email to certificate holder, Fax, Postal mail, or Upload to portal.
* **Rush Request**: If this is a rush request, select to expedite the process.
* **Rush Reason**: If applicable, enter the reason for the rush request. (Optional)
* **Special Requirements**: If applicable, enter any special requirements.
* **Requester Signature**: Sign your full name to confirm the request.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the request.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you need any further assistance, contact your insurance company representative for support.
