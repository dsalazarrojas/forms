# Health Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from you so that we can provide a personalized health insurance quote. Please fill out this form to help us get to know you better and provide a quote that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section with your personal details.
2. Make sure to select the correct insurance type that applies to you.
3. Double-check that you have entered your age correctly.
4. Fill out your address and zip code accurately.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (first_name)
The first name you use in everyday life.

* This is a basic contact field to identify you.

### **Last Name** (last_name)
The last name you use in everyday life.

* This is also a basic contact field to identify you.

### **Email Address** (email)
Your personal email address.

* This is where we will send the quote and any further information.

### **Phone Number** (phone)
Your phone number.

* This is your contact number for follow-up questions or concerns.

### **Birth Date** (birth_date)
The date you were born.

* Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accuracy.

### **Insurance Type** (insurance_type)
The type of insurance you are interested in (health, life, disability, long term, or short term).

* Please select all that apply to you.

### **Age** (age)
Your age.

* Please enter your age correctly to get an accurate quote.

### **Address Line 1** (address_1)
The first line of your address.

* This is the street address where you reside.

### **Address Line 2** (address_2)
The second line of your address.

* This is usually the apartment or suite number.

### **City** (city)
The city where you reside.

* This is the city where your address is located.

### **State** (state)
The state where you reside.

* This is the state where your address is located.

### **Zip Code** (zip)
The zip code of your address.

* Please enter this correctly to ensure accurate delivery.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure we can provide you with the best possible quote.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
- We will get in touch with you with your personalized quote and any further information.
