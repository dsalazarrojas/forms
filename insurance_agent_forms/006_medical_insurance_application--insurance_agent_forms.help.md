# Medical Insurance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Insurance Application form is designed to collect information about your medical history, contact details, and policy preferences. This form helps us understand your medical needs and preferences to provide you with the best possible medical insurance options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the necessary details.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, not required): Please provide your name, last name, address, and email address. This information is used to contact you and keep you updated on your policy.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): List all medical conditions you have been diagnosed with. This helps us understand your medical needs and preferences.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Provide your contact information, including your phone number. This ensures we can reach you with any updates or questions.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your full name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your address.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Prophet** (`prophets_1`, `select_one`, not required): Select the option that best describes your situation.
*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, not required): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you would like your policy to start.
*   **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you would like your policy to end.
*   **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of policy you are interested in.
*   **Medical Insurance Company** (`medical_insurance_company`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the medical insurance company you are interested in.

## Tips

*   Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can provide you with the best possible medical insurance options.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
