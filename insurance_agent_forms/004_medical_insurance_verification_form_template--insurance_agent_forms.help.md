# Medical Insurance Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for verifying patient's medical insurance information, including their policy details and coverage status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Select the appropriate insurance plan from the **Insurance Plan** field.
3. Enter the policy number.
4. Enter the group number.
5. Provide the coverage start date.
6. Provide the coverage end date.
7. Select the policy type (Individual, Group, or Family).
8. Select the policy status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
9. Enter any additional coverage details in the **Coverage Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
- **Insurance Plan** (insurance_plan, select_one, required): Select the plan that the patient has (Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C).
- **Policy Number** (policy_number, text, required): Enter the policy number provided by the insurance company.
- **Group Number** (group_number, text, required): Enter the group number provided by the insurance company.
- **Coverage Start Date** (coverage_start_date, date, required): Enter the date when the patient's coverage started.
- **Coverage End Date** (coverage_end_date, date, required): Enter the date when the patient's coverage ends.
- **Policy Type** (policy_type, select_one, required): Select the type of policy (Individual, Group, or Family).
- **Policy Status** (policy_status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the policy (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Coverage Details** (coverage_details, note, required): Provide any additional information about the patient's coverage.

## Tips
- Double-check that the insurance plan and policy number match the patient's insurance information.
- Make sure to enter accurate start and end dates for the coverage.
- If the policy status is Active, ensure that the coverage details are up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to reach out to the patient or their insurance provider for clarification.
