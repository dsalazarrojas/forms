# Motorcycle Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information about your motorcycle and your contact details to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Carefully review and provide accurate information about your motorcycle.
* Enter your motorcycle's year, make, model, and registration number.
* Provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bike Year**: Please enter the year of your motorcycle.
* **Bike Make**: Enter the make of your motorcycle (e.g. Yamaha, Harley-Davidson, etc.).
* **Bike Model**: Enter the model of your motorcycle (e.g. FJR1300, Street Glide, etc.).
* **Bike Registration**: Enter your motorcycle's registration number.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as the owner of the motorcycle.
* **Owner Address**: Enter your address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Owner Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your insurance policy.
