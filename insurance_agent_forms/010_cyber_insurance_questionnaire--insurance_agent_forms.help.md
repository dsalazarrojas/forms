## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company information section with your company's details, including company name, contact person, and contact email.
2. Provide policy details, such as policy number, start date, end date, coverage, deductible, and term frequency.
3. Enter financial data, including policy premium, policy premium currency, and policy term frequency duration.
