# Balloon Rider Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Balloon Rider Form is a tool for policyholders to add coverage for specific items or activities. It will guide you through a series of questions to gather relevant information to provide accurate quotes and coverage options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) for each section.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your information in the text fields, and date fields as required.
4. Review your responses before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Full Name**: Enter the name of the insured person. This will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Policy Number**: Enter your existing policy number, if you have one.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for policy communications. This will be used for sending quotes, updates, and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number for policyholder communications.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP for mail correspondence.
* **Type of Rider**: Select from the dropdown menu to specify what type of coverage you are adding (e.g., Hot Air Balloon Coverage, High-Value Item Rider, etc.).
* **Item or Activity Description**: Enter a brief description of what needs coverage.
* **Declared Value**: Enter the appraised or estimated value of the item in dollars.
* **Appraisal Documentation**: Select whether you have a professional appraisal attached or not.
* **Requested Coverage Amount**: Enter the amount of coverage you need.
* **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the date when coverage should begin.
* **Coverage End Date**: Enter the date when coverage should end (only required for temporary riders).
* **Primary Location**: Enter where the item is kept or the activity takes place.
* **Security Measures**: Select multiple options that apply to protect the item (e.g., Home Security System, Safe or Vault, etc.).
* **Previous Claims**: Select whether you have filed claims for similar items.
* **Claims Details**: Enter details about previous claims if any.
* **Additional Insured Parties**: Enter the names of others who should be covered (optional).
* **Usage Frequency**: Select how often the item is used or the activity is undertaken.
* **Professional or Commercial Use**: Select whether the item is used for business or not.
* **Deductible Preference**: Choose your preferred deductible amount.
* **Premium Payment Frequency**: Select how often you will pay the additional premium.
* **Insurance Agent Name**: Enter your insurance agent's name (optional).
* **Special Conditions or Exclusions**: Enter any specific requirements or exclusions for the coverage.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Select whether you agree to the policy terms.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of this application.

Note: All required fields must be completed in order to generate a quote.
