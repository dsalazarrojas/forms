## Auto Insurance Information Collection - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an individual's auto insurance needs and preferences. It gathers personal details, vehicle information, and insurance history to help determine the best insurance coverage options for you. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate results.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to enter correct information for each field.
2. Select the correct options for each field as they apply to you.
3. Be thorough and honest when answering all questions.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: The date you were born in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your best contact number.
* **Driver License Number**: Your driver license number.
* **License State**: The state that issued your driver license.
* **License Expiration Date**: The date your driver license expires.
* **Marital Status**: Your current marital status.
* **Education Level**: The highest level of education you have completed.
* **Occupation**: Your current job title.
* **Vehicle Information**: This is not a required field, but if you wish to provide more information about your vehicle, you can do so here.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make**: The manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: The model name of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Trim**: The trim level or package of your vehicle (optional).
* **VIN**: The Vehicle Identification Number of your vehicle (optional).
* **Current Mileage**: The current odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **Primary Use of Vehicle**: How you primarily use your vehicle.
* **Estimated Annual Mileage**: The estimated miles driven per year.
* **Where is Vehicle Parked**: Where your vehicle is kept overnight.
* **Driving History**: This is not a required field, but if you have any information about your driving record, you can provide it here.
* **Accidents in Last 3 Years**: How many accidents you have had in the last 3 years.
* **Violations in Last 3 Years**: How many traffic tickets or violations you have had in the last 3 years.
* **DUI History**: If you have been convicted of DUI within the last 3 years.
* **Current Insurance Status**: Do you currently have active insurance or is it expired/never had insurance.
* **Desired Coverage Type**: What type of coverage are you seeking.
* **Additional Drivers**: Will there be other drivers using your vehicle.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How you would like to be contacted.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for help or advice when you submit the form.
