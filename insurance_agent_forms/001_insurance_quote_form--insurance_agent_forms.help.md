# Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Quote Form is a tool designed to provide you with a quick and efficient way to generate quotes for various types of insurance. This form gathers relevant information from you to help us provide an accurate quote. Please ensure you provide the correct information to get an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Insurance Quote Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Client Name** in the first field. This is the name of the person or entity requiring the insurance quote.
2. Choose the type(s) of insurance you are interested in from the **Insurance Type** field. Select one or more of the following options: Life, Health, Auto, or Home.
3. Enter your **Coverage Start Date**. This is the date you want the coverage to begin on.
4. Enter your **Coverage End Date**. This is the date you want the coverage to end on.
5. Enter your **Annual Premium**. This is the amount you are willing to pay annually for the coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Text)**: This field is for the name of the person or entity requiring the insurance quote.
* **Insurance Type (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more of the following insurance types: Life, Health, Auto, or Home.
* **Coverage Start Date (Date)**: This is the date you want the coverage to begin on.
* **Coverage End Date (Date)**: This is the date you want the coverage to end on.
* **Annual Premium (Number)**: This is the amount you are willing to pay annually for the coverage.
