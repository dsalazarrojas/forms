# Acord Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acord Cancellation Form is used to submit a request to cancel an existing insurance policy. This form is essential for ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and formatting requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Policyholder Name** (Required)
Policyholder Name is the full name of the person or entity requesting the policy cancellation. Please enter the name as it appears on their policy paperwork.

* **Policy Number** (Required)
Policy Number is the unique identifier assigned to the insurance policy. Double-check that this is correct to avoid errors.

* **Insurance Company** (Required)
Insurance Company is the name of the company that issued the policy. Please ensure you enter the correct name.

* **Agent Name** (Required)
Agent Name is the name of the insurance agent handling this cancellation request. Double-check that this is accurate.

* **Agent License Number** (Required)
Agent License Number is the state license number of the insurance agent handling this cancellation. Ensure this is accurate.

* **Requested Cancellation Date** (Required)
Requested Cancellation Date is the date when the policy cancellation should take effect. This date should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Reason for Cancellation** (Required)
Reason for Cancellation is the primary reason for cancelling the policy. Select one of the provided options or enter "Other" if the reason is not listed.

* **Premium Paid Through Date** (Required)
Premium Paid Through Date is the date through which premiums have been paid. This date should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Refund Amount (if applicable)** (Optional)
Refund Amount is the amount to be refunded to the policyholder if any. This field is only required if a refund is applicable.

* **Proration Calculation** (Optional)
Proration Calculation is the method used to calculate the refund amount. Select one of the provided options or choose "Other calculation method" if necessary.

* **Notice Provided to Insured** (Required)
Notice Provided to Insured is a confirmation of whether proper notice was given to the insured before cancellation. Choose one of the provided options.

* **Policy Effective Date** (Required)
Policy Effective Date is the original date when the policy became effective. Enter this date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

* **Type of Insurance Policy** (Required)
Type of Insurance Policy is the category of insurance being cancelled. Choose one of the provided options.

* **Additional Notes or Comments** (Optional)
Additional Notes or Comments are any additional information regarding the cancellation. This field can be used to provide further details.

Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form to ensure a smooth cancellation process. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your insurance agent or contact us for assistance.
