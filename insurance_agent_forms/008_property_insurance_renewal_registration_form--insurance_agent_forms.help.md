# Property Insurance Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather necessary information for property insurance policy renewals. You will be asked to provide details about your property, policy, and other relevant information to help us update your policy correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that your policy is updated correctly, please complete the form carefully and thoroughly. You can navigate through the form's sections, which are designed to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one): Choose the type of property you are insuring.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your property is a house, apartment, or other type of real estate.
	+ Select 'No' if your property is not a type that can be insured (e.g., a business or vehicle).
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, select_one): Indicate the location of your property.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your property is located within our coverage area.
	+ Select 'No' if your property is located outside our coverage area.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number): Enter the approximate value of your property.
	+ This field is optional but helps us assess your property's value for insurance purposes.
* **Property Year** (`property_year`, select_one): Choose the year your property was built or acquired.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your property was built or acquired in the last year.
	+ Select 'No' if your property was built or acquired in a different year.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of your current policy.
	+ This field is optional but helps us track your policy's start date.
* **Insured Party** (`insured_party`, select_one): Indicate who is insured under your policy.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are the insured party.
	+ Select 'No' if another party is insured under your policy.
* **Agent** (`agent`, select_multiple): Select any agents associated with your policy.
	+ This field is optional and allows you to choose multiple agents if necessary.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Add any additional comments or notes about your policy.
	+ This field is optional and helps us understand any unique circumstances surrounding your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of your current policy.
	+ This field is optional but helps us track your policy's end date.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one): Choose your insurance provider.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are currently using our insurance provider.
	+ Select 'No' if you are using another provider.
