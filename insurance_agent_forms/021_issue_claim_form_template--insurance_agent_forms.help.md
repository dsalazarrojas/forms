# issue_claim_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about customer claims from insurance agents, facilitating accurate and timely processing of claims.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for "Insurance Agent" from the available choices.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Enter the claim date in the correct format (month/day/year).
4. Enter the claim amount.
5. Choose the correct location where the issue occurred from the options provided.
6. Select all relevant insurance providers involved in the claim.
7. Enter any additional details about the issue in the "Issue Details" field (optional).
8. Enter the insurance policy number.
9. (Optional) Provide your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
10. (Optional) Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Insurance Agent**: This field is used to identify the insurance agent submitting the claim.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the issue to ensure accurate processing.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date when the claim was made or issue occurred.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the total amount claimed.
* **Issue Location**: Choose the correct location where the issue occurred.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select all relevant insurance providers involved in the claim.
* **Issue Details**: Enter any additional information about the issue (optional).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the claim.
* **Phone Number**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: (Optional) Enter your email address for contact purposes.
