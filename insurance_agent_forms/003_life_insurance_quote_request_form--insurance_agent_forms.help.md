# life_insurance_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are requesting a life insurance quote. It will help the insurance agent process your request and provide you with an accurate quote.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Select the type of Insurance you are interested in (Life Insurance, Term Insurance, or Universal Insurance).
7. Choose your desired Coverage Period (Short-Term or Long-Term).
8. Enter the Insurance Amount you are looking for.
9. Enter the name of the Insurance Agent assisting you.
10. Enter the email of the Insurance Agent (if different from their name).
11. Enter the phone number of the Insurance Agent (if different from their name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to reach you.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is used to collect your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Insurance Type**: This field is used to select the type of insurance you are interested in. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Life Insurance
	+ Term Insurance
	+ Universal Insurance
* **Coverage Period**: This field is used to choose your desired coverage period. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Short-Term
	+ Long-Term
* **Insurance Amount**: This field is used to enter the amount of insurance you are looking for. Please enter a valid dollar amount.
* **Agent Name**: This field is used to collect the name of the insurance agent assisting you. Please enter their name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Agent Email**: This field is used to collect the email address of the insurance agent. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact them.
* **Agent Phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the insurance agent. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to reach them.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information to ensure we can process your request efficiently.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the insurance agent assisting you for guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes. If you need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
