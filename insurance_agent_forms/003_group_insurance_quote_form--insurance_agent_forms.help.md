# Group Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from group administrators or representatives to gather details about their group's insurance needs. It will help insurance providers to provide an accurate quote that suits the group's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form with your group's basic details.
2. Provide contact information about your group, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Answer a few questions about your group's insurance needs, such as the type of insurance you are looking for and the coverage amount you require.
4. If any questions, submit your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application**: Enter your group's name and other basic details.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Insurance Quote**: Enter a brief description of the type of insurance you are looking for.
* **Group Quote Information**: Enter any additional information about your group that may help us provide a more accurate quote.
* **Quote Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the insurance quote you require.
* **Quote Details**: Enter any additional details about the quote you require.
* **Quote Submission**: Enter your quote submission.
* **Review**: Enter any feedback or questions you have about the quote.
* **What is the name of your group?**: Enter the name of your group.
* **How many members are in your group?**: Enter the number of members in your group. This is a required field.
* **What is your address?**: Enter the address of your group or organization.
* **What is your email address?**: Enter the email address of your group or organization.
* **Phone number?**: Enter the phone number of your group or organization.
* **What type of insurance are you looking for?**: Choose the type of insurance you need, such as Health, Life, or Auto.
* **How much coverage do you require?**: Enter the amount of coverage you need.
* **What is your group's purpose or use case?**: Enter a brief description of your group's purpose or use case.
* **What do you want to be quoted for?**: Enter a brief description of what you would like to be quoted for.
* **Do you have any questions or feedback?**: Enter any questions or feedback you have about the quote.
