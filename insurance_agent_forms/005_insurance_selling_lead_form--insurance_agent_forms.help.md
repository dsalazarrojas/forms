# insurance_selling_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance_selling_lead_form is designed to collect information about potential insurance customers, including their personal and insurance-specific details. This form helps insurance agents to understand the customers' needs and preferences, enabling them to provide tailored assistance and guidance.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
2. Fill out your first name, last name, and email address accurately.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Select the type of lead you represent (Individual or Business).
5. Select one or more categories that your customer is interested in (e.g., Life, Health, Auto, or Homeowners Insurance).
6. Select one or more insurance types that your customer is interested in (e.g., Life, Health, Auto, or Homeowners Insurance).
7. Choose the insurance provider your customer is interested in (e.g., Nationwide, Progressive, GEICO, or State Farm).
8. Enter the date and time of the lead (if applicable).
9. Enter the insurance agent's name and company name.
10. Finally, add any additional notes about the lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Lead Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select "Individual" or "Business" to indicate the type of lead.
* **Category** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more categories that your customer is interested in, such as Life, Health, Auto, or Homeowners Insurance.
* **Insurance Type** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more insurance types that your customer is interested in, such as Life, Health, Auto, or Homeowners Insurance.
* **Insurance Provider** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the insurance provider that your customer is interested in, such as Nationwide, Progressive, GEICO, or State Farm.
* **Lead Date** (`9`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the lead.
* **Lead Time** (`10`, `time`, optional): If applicable, enter the time of the lead.
* **Insurance Agent** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the insurance agent assisting the lead.
* **Company Name** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Address** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the customer (if applicable).
* **Note** (`14`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the lead.
