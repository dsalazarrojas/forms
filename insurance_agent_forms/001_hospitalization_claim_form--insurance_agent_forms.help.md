# Hospitalization Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an insurance claim related to hospitalization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answer for each field, and ensure that you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure that you have provided all required information.
3. Once you have completed all fields, review your answers to ensure that they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Insurance Claim (Page 1)
* **Insurance Claim** (`insurance_claim`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select whether you are claiming benefits for hospitalization. If yes, choose the relevant reason for the claim.
* **Claim Date** (Page 1):
	+ Enter the date when the hospitalization occurred.
* **Claimant Name** (Page 1):
	+ Enter the name of the claimant, the person making the claim.
* **Claimant Address** (Page 1):
	+ Enter the address of the claimant.
* **Doctor Name** (Page 1):
	+ Enter the name of the doctor who treated the claimant.
* **Patient Diagnosis** (Page 2):
	+ Select whether the patient was diagnosed with any health conditions during the hospitalization.
* **Patient Treatment** (Page 2):
	+ Enter a brief description of the treatment received.
* **Insurance Provider** (Page 2):
	+ Select whether the claimant has an insurance provider.
* **Patient Age** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the age of the claimant.
* **Additional Information** (Page 2):
	+ This field is currently not included in the form, but it may be added in the future.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your answers before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant insurance documentation or contact your insurance provider for guidance.
* Keep a copy of your completed form for your records.
