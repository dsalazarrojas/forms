# Property Damage Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report property damage, providing detailed information about the damage and the responsible party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of damage that occurred (Wind, Water, or Other).
2. Provide a detailed description of the damage.
3. Enter the date and time of the damage.
4. Identify the responsible party involved in the damage.
5. Select the insurance agent who will be handling the claim.
6. Enter the details of the insurer, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
7. Enter the cost of the damage.
8. Verify that the insurer has signed and dated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details**: Enter a brief description of the property that was damaged.
* **Damage**: Select the type of damage that occurred (Wind, Water, or Other).
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the damage.
* **Damage Date**: Enter the date of the damage (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Damage Time**: Enter the time of the damage (in 24-hour format).
* **Responsible Party**: Identify the person or entity responsible for the damage.
* **Insurance Agent Details**: Select the insurance agent who will be handling the claim (Select Insurance Agent, Agent 1, or Agent 2).
* **Insurer Details**: Enter the name of the insurer.
* **Insurer Address**: Enter the address of the insurer.
* **Damage Cost**: Enter the cost of the damage.
* **Insurer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insurer.
* **Insurer Email**: Enter the email of the insurer.
* **Insurer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the claim.
* **Insurer Signature**: Verify that the insurer has signed and dated the form.
* **Insurer Signature Date**: Enter the date of the insurer's signature (in YYYY-MM-DD format).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Verify that the insurer has signed and dated the form.
* Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting it.
