# Policy Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for policy cancellation purposes. It will help you to accurately document the cancellation of a policy, ensuring that all relevant details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your policy details.
3. Enter your reason for cancellation in the designated text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auto Vehicle** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to indicate whether the policy is related to an auto vehicle.
* **Home/Rent** (select_multiple, optional): This field is for indicating whether the policy is related to home or rent.
* **Policy Number** (number, optional): Enter the unique number assigned to the policy being cancelled.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the reason for the policy cancellation.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes related to the cancellation.
* **Assigned to** (select_one, optional): Choose whether the policy was assigned to someone else or not.
* **Assigned by** (select_one, optional): Select whether the policy was assigned by someone else or not.
* **Submitted by** (select_one, optional): Choose whether the policy was submitted by someone else or not.
* **Submitted Date** (date, optional): Enter the date on which the policy was submitted for cancellation.
* **Submitted Time** (time, optional): Enter the time at which the policy was submitted for cancellation.
