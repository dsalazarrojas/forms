# Drone Insurance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from drone users for the purpose of applying for drone insurance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (First Name, Address, Email, Phone, Date of Birth).
2. Enter your driver's license number, if applicable.
3. Select the type of insurance you are applying for (Aerial, Ground, Both).
4. Choose the type of aircraft you are insuring (Quadcopter, Multirotor, Fixed Wing).
5. Select the coverage duration (Daily, Monthly, Yearly).
6. Enter the start and end dates of the coverage period.
7. Add any additional notes about your application.
8. Enter the name of the insurance company you would like to use.
9. Enter the name of the insurance agent or representative you are working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (Year-Month-Day).
* **License Number**: Enter your driver's license number if you have one. This is not required but may be necessary for verification purposes.
* **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance you are applying for. Options are Aerial, Ground, and Both.
* **Aircraft Type**: Select the type of aircraft you are insuring. Options are Quadcopter, Multirotor, and Fixed Wing.
* **Coverage Amount**: Enter the amount of coverage you would like to have. This is required for pricing and coverage calculation.
* **Coverage Duration**: Select the duration of coverage you would like to have. Options are Daily, Monthly, and Yearly.
* **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the start date of the coverage period.
* **Coverage End Date**: Enter the end date of the coverage period.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your application that may be helpful for processing.
* **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company you would like to use.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the insurance agent or representative you are working with.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields with the label "required" are filled out.
- Enter all dates in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- Use clear and concise notes to explain your application.
- If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to us directly for assistance.
