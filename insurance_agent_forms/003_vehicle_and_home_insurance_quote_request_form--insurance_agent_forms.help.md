# Vehicle And Home Insurance Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or businesses to potentially request a quote for vehicle and home insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select "Yes" or "No" for the initial question, and then fill out the required fields for vehicle and home information. Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy to ensure a smooth and efficient quote request process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle and Home Insurance Quote Request Form** (`vehicle_and_home_insurance_quote_request_form`): Select "Yes" to indicate you are requesting a quote for both vehicle and home insurance, or "No" if you are only interested in one type of insurance.
* **First Name** (`name`): Please enter your first name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any further questions or quote details.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can send you the quote details and any updates.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address for us to locate you on the map and provide more accurate quote information.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Please enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`): Enter your home address so we can provide accurate quote information.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`): Enter the type of your home (e.g. house, apartment, condo).
* **Home State** (`home_state`): Enter the state of your home location.
* **Home City** (`home_city`): Enter the city of your home location.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, you can contact us at your provided email or phone number.
