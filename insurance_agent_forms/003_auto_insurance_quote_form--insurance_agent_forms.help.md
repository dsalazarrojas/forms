# Auto Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect personal and vehicle information from clients to provide accurate and tailored quotes for their auto insurance needs. This form serves as a crucial tool for insurance agents to efficiently gather data and facilitate the quoting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Personal Information**: Provide your first name, last name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields. Make sure to enter your correct email address, as this is where your quote will be sent.
2. **Driver Information**: Enter your date of birth, marital status, gender, and the number of years you have been licensed.
3. **Vehicle Information**: Enter your vehicle's year, make, model, body style, and annual mileage.
4. **Driving Record**: Answer yes or no questions regarding any accidents, violations, or DUI/DWI history.
5. **Quote Delivery**: Select your preferred method for receiving your quote (Email, Phone, or Text Message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: The email address where your quote will be sent.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can also contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
* **Marital Status**: Your current marital status (Single, Married, Divorced, or Widowed).
* **Gender**: Your self-identified gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Years Licensed**: Number of years you have been licensed.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: The make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model**: The model of your vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, Civic).
* **Body Style**: The type of vehicle body style (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Coupe, Van, Wagon).
* **Annual Mileage**: Estimated annual mileage.
* **Commute Miles**: If applicable, enter your one-way commute distance in miles.
* **Accidents in last 3 years**: Number of accidents you have been involved in over the last 3 years.
* **Violations in last 3 years**: Number of traffic violations you have received over the last 3 years.
* **Ever had a DUI or DWI**: Yes or no answer regarding any DUI or DWI history.
* **How should we deliver your quote**: Select your preferred method for receiving your quote (Email, Phone, Text Message).

By following these steps and understanding each field's purpose, you will be able to efficiently complete the Auto Insurance Quote Form and receive accurate and personalized quotes for your clients.
