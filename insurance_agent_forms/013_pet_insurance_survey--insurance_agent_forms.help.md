# Pet Insurance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pet owners provide information about their pet's insurance needs. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of pet you have from the list.
2. Select the age range of your pet.
3. Select the species of your pet.
4. Choose how much you would like your pet to be insured for.
5. Enter your location.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Choose your pet insurance provider.
9. Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of pet do you have?**: 
  - Select the type of pet from the list, such as "Cat" or "Dog".
* **How old is your pet?**: 
  - Choose the age range of your pet, such as "0-1 year", "1-2 years", etc.
* **What is your pet's species?**: 
  - Select the species of your pet, such as "Mammal", "Reptile", etc.
* **How much would you like your pet to be insured for?**: 
  - Choose the desired insurance payout amount, such as "$5,000", "$10,000", etc.
* **Where do you live?**: 
  - Enter your location in the text field.
* **What is your phone number?**: 
  - Enter your phone number in the text field.
* **What is your email?**: 
  - Enter your email address in the text field. (Note: This is a valid email format field)
* **Who is your pet insurance provider?**: 
  - Select the insurance provider from the list, such as "State Farm" or "Trupanion".
* **Additional comments or questions?**: 
  - Enter any additional comments or questions you have about pet insurance in this field.
