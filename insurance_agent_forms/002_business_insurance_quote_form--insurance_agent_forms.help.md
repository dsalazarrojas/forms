# Business Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect information from businesses to create a customized insurance plan that meets their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* Select the correct category for your business type.
* Enter your business name, number of years in operation, and estimated annual revenue.
* Select the types of coverage you need.
* Check if you have current insurance and select the desired coverage start date.
* Choose your preferred deductible amount and preferred contact method.
* Enter any additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (Text, Required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (Select One, Required): Choose the primary category of your business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Non-Profit, Other).
* **Years in Operation** (Number, Required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of employees working for your business.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue** (Number, Required): Enter your estimated annual revenue (in dollars).
* **Contact Person Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person we should contact regarding your quote.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the primary contact email address for your business.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your business.
* **Types of Coverage Needed** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the types of coverage you require (e.g., General Liability, Property Insurance, Workers Compensation, etc.).
* **Current Insurance Status** (Select One, Required): Check if you have current insurance (yes or no).
* **Desired Coverage Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when you need your coverage to begin.
* **Preferred Deductible** (Select One, Not Required): Choose the amount you are willing to pay out of pocket for the deductible.
* **Additional Information** (Text, Not Required): Enter any special circumstances or details that may be relevant to your quote.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted with your quote options (e.g., Email, Phone Call, Text Message, Video Call).
