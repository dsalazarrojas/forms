# Insurance Binder Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an insurance binder, which is a document that outlines the agreed-upon terms and conditions of an insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submit Your Request**: Click on the "Submit" button to send your request to the relevant team.
2. **Provide Required Information**: Fill out the form with accurate and complete information as prompted.
3. **Double Check**: Review your submission to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Request Details** (`insurance_binder_request_form`): A brief description of your request. Please provide a clear and concise description of the reason for your request.
2. **Type of Request** (`request_type`): Select the type of request you are making (New Business, New Renewal, or Other).
3. **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`): Select the name of the insurance company providing your policy.
4. **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter the policy number associated with your request.
5. **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`): Enter the effective date of your policy.
6. **Policy Expiration Date** (`policy_expiration_date`): Enter the expiration date of your policy.
7. **Policy Term** (`policy_term`): Select the term of your policy (Monthly, Quarterly, Biannually, or Annually).
8. **Policy Anniversary** (`policy_anniversary`): Enter the anniversary date of your policy.
9. **Policy Premium** (`policy_premium`): Enter the premium amount of your policy.
10. **Policy Commission** (`policy_commission`): Enter the commission amount of your policy.
11. **Agent Name** (`agent_name`): Enter the name of the agent handling your request.
12. **Agent Address** (`agent_address`): A note field for the agent's address.
13. **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`): Enter the phone number of the agent handling your request.
 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your request.
- If you need to resubmit your request, please do so with an updated version, including a brief description of the changes. 
- For any further inquiries or concerns, please contact the support team.
