# insurance_certificate_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from insurance agents for insurance certificate requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information (First name, Last name, Company, and Email).
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Select the type of insurance you're requesting (Life, Health, Home, Auto, or Other).
4. Enter your Policy Number.
5. If applicable, provide the Policy Date and Request Date.
6. Sign your name to confirm your identity.
7. Briefly state the Reason for Request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Company**: A text field to enter your company name.
* **Email**: An email field to enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Address**: A text field to enter your address.
* **Insurance Type**: A dropdown menu to select the type of insurance (Life, Health, Home, Auto, or Other).
* **Policy Number**: A text field to enter your policy number.
* **Policy Date**: A date field to enter the policy date (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Request Date**: A date field to enter the request date (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Signature**: A text field to sign your name to confirm your identity.
* **Reason for Request**: A text field to briefly state the reason for your request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department for guidance.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
