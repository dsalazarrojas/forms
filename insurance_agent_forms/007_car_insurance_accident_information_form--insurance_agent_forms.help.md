## Purpose
The Car Insurance Accident Information Form is designed to collect detailed information about car accidents, allowing insurance agents to efficiently process claims and provide the best possible support to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
- Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Policy number**: Enter the policy number associated with the insured's policy.
  - **Policy number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter the policy number of the insured's car insurance policy.
- **Date of accident**: Enter the date of the accident in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  - **Date of accident** (`claim_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the accident.
- **Time of accident**: Enter the time of the accident in 24-hour format.
  - **Time of accident** (`accident_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the accident.
- **Location of accident**: Enter the exact location of the accident, including the street address or intersection.
  - **Location of accident** (`accident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the accident occurred.
- **City and state**: Enter the city and state where the accident happened.
  - **City and state** (`accident_city_state`, `text`, required): Enter the city and state where the accident occurred.
- **Detailed description of the accident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the accident, including the direction of vehicles and any impact areas.
  - **Detailed description of the accident** (`accident_description`, `text`, required): Describe the accident in detail.
- **Insured's name**: Enter the name of the insured.
  - **Insured's name** (`insured_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the insured.
- **Insured's phone number**: Enter the insured's phone number.
  - **Insured's phone number** (`insured_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the insured's phone number.
- **Insured's email**: Enter the insured's email address.
  - **Insured's email** (`insured_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the insured's email address (optional).
- **Vehicle year, make, model**: Enter the year, make, and model of the vehicle involved in the accident.
  - **Vehicle year, make, model** (`vehicle_make_model_year`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's year, make, and model.
- **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
  - **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
- **License plate number**: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved in the accident.
  - **License plate number** (`vehicle_license_plate`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
- **Was another vehicle involved?**: Select 'True' if another vehicle was involved in the accident.
  - **Was another vehicle involved?** (`other_vehicle_involved`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if another vehicle was involved.
- **Other driver's name**: Enter the name of the other driver involved in the accident (if applicable).
  - **Other driver's name** (`other_driver_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the other driver.
- **Other driver's phone number**: Enter the phone number of the other driver (if applicable).
  - **Other driver's phone number** (`other_driver_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the other driver.
- **Other driver's insurance company**: Enter the name of the other driver's insurance company (if applicable).
  - **Other driver's insurance company** (`other_driver_insurance`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the other driver's insurance company.
- **Other driver's policy number**: Enter the policy number of the other driver's insurance policy (if applicable).
  - **Other driver's policy number** (`other_driver_policy`, `text`, optional): Enter the policy number of the other driver's insurance policy.
- **Other vehicle year, make, model**: Enter the year, make, and model of the other vehicle involved (if applicable).
  - **Other vehicle year, make, model** (`other_vehicle_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the year, make, and model of the other vehicle involved.
- **Was a police report filed?**: Select 'True' if a police report has been filed.
  - **Was a police report filed?** (`police_report_filed`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if a police report has been filed.
- **Police report number**: Enter the police report number (if applicable).
  - **Police report number** (`police_report_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the police report number (if applicable).
- **Were there witnesses?**: Select 'True' if there were witnesses to the accident.
  - **Were there witnesses?** (`witnesses_present`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if there were witnesses.
- **Witness names and contact information**: Enter the names and contact information of any witnesses.
  - **Witness names and contact information** (`witness_information`, `text`, optional): Enter the names and contact information of any witnesses.
- **Were any injuries sustained?**: Select 'True' if any injuries were sustained during the accident.
  - **Were any injuries sustained?** (`injuries_sustained`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if any injuries were sustained.
- **Details of injuries**: Enter a detailed description of any injuries sustained during the accident.
  - **Details of injuries** (`injury_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of the injuries.
- **Description of vehicle damage**: Provide a detailed description of the damage to the vehicle.
  - **Description of vehicle damage** (`vehicle_damage_description`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the vehicle damage.

## Tips
- Please double-check the accuracy of the information you enter to ensure the correct processing of the claim.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your insurance agent for assistance.
