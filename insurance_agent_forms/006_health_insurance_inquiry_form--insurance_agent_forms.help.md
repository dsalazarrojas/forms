# Health Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users about their health insurance needs and preferences. This form is used to gather relevant details to provide a better understanding of the user's requirements, enabling the insurance provider to offer tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Health Insurance Inquiry Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Date of birth in the "Date of birth" (mm/dd/yyyy) format.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Select your preferred type of insurance from the "Insurance type" dropdown menu.
6. (Optional) Add any additional information about the policy holder in the "Policy holder details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Insurance type (Select One)**: Choose from the list of available insurance types:
	+ Health
	+ Dental
	+ Life
	+ Disability
* **Policy holder details (Note)**: Provide any additional information about the policy holder, such as their name, age, or medical conditions (optional).
