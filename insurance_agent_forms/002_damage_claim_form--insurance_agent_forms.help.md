# Damage Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to damage claims, including details about the submitter, claimant, and claim itself. This form will help the user gather all necessary information to proceed with the claim process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the submitter in the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Company", "Contact Number", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" fields.
2. Enter the name of the person making the claim, the claimant, in the "Claimant" field.
3. Enter the address of the claimant in the "Claimant Address" field.
4. Provide a description of the claim in the "Claim Description" field.
5. Enter the location where the damage occurred in the "Damage Location" field.
6. Select the type of damage from the options provided in the "Damage Type" field.
7. Enter a description of the damage in the "Damage Description" field.
8. Enter the amount of damage in the "Damage Amount" field.
9. Select the status of the claim from the options provided in the "Claim Status" field.
10. Enter the title of the submitter and claimant in the "Submitter Title" and "Claimant Title" fields respectively.
11. Enter the email and phone numbers of the submitter and claimant in the "Submitter Email", "Submitter Phone", "Claimant Email", and "Claimant Phone" fields.
12. If a witness is present, mark "Yes" in the "Witness" field.
13. Enter the address of the witness in the "Witness Address" field.
14. Enter the email and title of the witness in the "Witness Email" and "Witness Title" fields.
15. Select the date of the damage in the "Damage Date" field.
15. (Optional) If the submitter wants to include a witness's information, they can enter the witness's address and phone number in the "Witness Address" and "Witness Phone" fields.
