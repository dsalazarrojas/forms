# Medical Expense Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Expense Form is used to report medical expenses for an employee. This form is a part of the employee's record and is used for tracking and management of medical expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information and necessary documents before filling out the form.
* Fill in the form with the requested information, including the employee's ID, expense date, medical expense, medical provider, and other relevant details.
* If the employee has medical benefits, select all applicable options from the list.
* Enter the medical expense amount and submit a copy of the medical receipt.
* Sign and date the form to confirm submission.
* Ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Expense Date** (expense_date, date, required): Enter the date of the medical expense.
* **Medical Expense** (medical_expense, text, required): Describe the medical expense, including any relevant details.
* **Medical Provider** (medical_provider, text, required): Enter the name of the medical provider who administered the treatment.
* **Medical Benefits** (medical_benefits, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable medical benefits that apply to the expense.
* **Medical Expense Amount** (medical_expense_amount, number, required): Enter the total amount of the medical expense.
* **Medical Receipt** (medical_receipt, text, required): Attach a scanned or uploaded copy of the medical receipt.
* **Medical Expense Details** (medical_expense_details, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the medical expense.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm submission.
* **Medical Expense Submitter** (medical_expense_submitted_by, select_one, required): Select the person who submitted the medical expense.
* **Date Submitted** (date_submitted, time, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, select_one, required): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted** (submitted, select_one, required): Select to confirm that the form has been submitted.
* **Submitted Date** (submitted_date, time, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
