# Insurance Decision Flowchart - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Decision Flowchart form is designed to help insurance agents assess a user's insurance needs and guide them through a series of questions to gather relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact details.
2. Select any relevant risk factors that apply to you.
3. Choose your preferred coverage options.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. (Optional) Enter your email address.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Fill out your contact details, including any relevant information we may need to contact you.

### Risk Factors
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any risk factors that apply to you, such as Risk 1, Risk 2, or Risk 3.

### Coverage Preferences
* **Coverage Preferences** (`coverage_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred coverage option that best suits your needs, such as Option 1 or Option 2.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of birth (HH:MM AM/PM).

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (optional).

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with the agent.
