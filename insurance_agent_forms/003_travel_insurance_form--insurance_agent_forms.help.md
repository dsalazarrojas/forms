# Travel Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients regarding their travel insurance needs. This form is designed for insurance agents or companies to assess client requirements and potentially offer quotes or recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you would like travel insurance by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the options.
2. Enter your name as the client for whom you are purchasing travel insurance.
3. Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
4. Select the travel start date.
5. Select the travel end date.
6. Enter the travel destination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Would like travel insurance** (`would_like_travel_insurance`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing travel insurance, or "No" if you are not interested.
- **Client Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the client for whom you are purchasing travel insurance.
- **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the contact number of the client, which will be used for follow-up purposes.
- **Travel start date** (`travel_start_date`, date, required: false): 
  Select the start date of the client's travel.
- **Travel end date** (`travel_end_date`, date, required: false): 
  Select the end date of the client's travel.
- **Travel destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the destination of the client's travel.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure the best results for your travel insurance needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the insurance agent or company for assistance.
