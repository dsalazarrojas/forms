# Loss Control Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loss Control Survey is designed to help insurance agents assess and control losses that can impact their clients' operations and bottom line. The form will guide you in identifying the risks, assessing their likelihood and potential impact, and implementing control measures to mitigate or prevent losses.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each field carefully and fill it out accordingly.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as the information will help the insurance agent provide the best possible advice and assistance to your client.
3. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask the insurance agent for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Risk Assessment (<name>)

* **Risk Assessment (id: 1, <type>: text, required: false):**
This field is where you will describe the nature of the loss or potential loss that you are concerned about.
Please provide a clear and concise description of the loss or potential loss.

### Risk Factors (<name>)

* **Risk Factors (id: 2, <type>: text, required: false):**
This field is where you will list the factors that contribute to the loss or potential loss. Please provide a list of the factors that you believe are contributing to the loss or potential loss.

### Loss Control Measures (<name>)

* **Loss Control Measures (id: 3, <type>: text, required: false):**
This field is where you will describe the measures that you have taken or plan to take to control or prevent the loss or potential loss. Please provide a clear and concise description of the measures you will take.

### Risk Prevention (<name>)

* **Risk Prevention (id: 4, <type>: select_multiple, required: true):**
Select all the prevention measures that apply to your situation. Please choose all that apply:
  - Prevention Measure 1
  - Prevention Measure 2
  - Prevention Measure 3

### Loss Frequency (<name>)

* **Loss Frequency (id: 5, <type>: text, required: false):**
This field is where you will describe how often the loss occurs or is likely to occur. Please provide a clear and concise description of the frequency of the loss or potential loss.

## Tips

* Be thorough and honest in your answers, as the information will help the insurance agent provide the best possible advice and assistance to your client.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask the insurance agent for clarification.
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields (marked as <required: true>) before submitting the form.
