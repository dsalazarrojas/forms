# Camp Admission Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Admission Checklist is a mandatory form for parents or guardians to provide essential information about their child's profile, emergency contacts, medical history, and dietary needs. This form helps the camp staff to better understand each camper's situation and provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Camper Full Name field with your child's full name.
2.  Enter their Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
3.  Select their grade level for the next fall in the Grade Entering (Next Fall) field.
4.  For Emergency Contact Information, provide the Primary Emergency Contact Name, Phone, and Relationship to Camper.
5.  If necessary, provide the Secondary Emergency Contact Name and Phone.
6.  In the Medical and Health Checklist section, select the status of your child's immunization record and choose their known medical conditions or concerns.
7.  List any known allergies, including food, environmental, and medication reactions, and any routine medications they take at camp.
8.  Select their dietary needs and restrictions, such as vegetarian or halal.
9.  Choose the permission to treat agreement, indicating whether you consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency.
10. Finally, add your initials as the Admissions Officer Initials for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Full Name** (Camper Full Name, text, required): Fill in your child's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter their date of birth.
* **Grade Entering (Next Fall)** (select_one, required): Select their grade level for the next fall.
* **Primary Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Provide the name of the primary emergency contact.
* **Primary Emergency Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Camper** (text, required): Specify the relationship between the contact and your child.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): If necessary, provide the name of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Phone** (text, optional): If necessary, enter the phone number of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (text, required): Enter the name of your child's health insurance provider.
* **Policy Holder Name** (text, required): Provide the name of the policy holder.
* **Known Medical Conditions or Concerns** (text, optional): List any known medical conditions or concerns affecting your child.
* **Known Allergies** (text, required): List any known allergies, including food, environmental, and medication reactions.
* **Routine Medications** (text, optional): List any routine medications your child takes at camp.
* **Dietary Needs/Restrictions** (select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary needs or restrictions, such as vegetarian or halal.
* **Status of Immunization Record** (select_one, required): Choose whether your child is up-to-date with their immunization record or has a paper copy to provide.
* **Permission to Treat Agreement** (select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **For Office Use - Initials** (text, optional): Add your initials for record-keeping purposes.
