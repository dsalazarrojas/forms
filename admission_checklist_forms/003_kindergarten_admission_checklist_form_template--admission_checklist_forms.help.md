# Kindergarten Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a student's admission to a kindergarten program. It collects data on the student's personal details, medical history, and emergency contact information, making it a crucial tool for educational institutions to ensure a smooth and efficient admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "Student First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
3. Enter the mother's name in the "Mother Name" field.
4. Enter the mother's phone number in the "Mother Phone" field.
5. Enter the student's date of birth (dob) in the "Student DOB" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
6. Enter the date of admission in the "Admission Date" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
7. Select the student's gender from the dropdown menu in the "Student Gender" field.
8. Enter any relevant information about the student's educational background in the "Educational Background" field.
9. Provide any medical reports or relevant information about the student's medical history in the "Medical Report" field.
10. Select the student's immunization status from the dropdown menu in the "Immunization Status" field.
11. Select the student's allergy status from the dropdown menu in the "Allergy Status" field.
12. Select the student's medical condition from the dropdown menu in the "Medical Condition" field.
13. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
14. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
15. Enter the relationship of the emergency contact person with the student in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
16. Enter the student's height in inches in the "Student Height" field.
17. Enter the student's weight in pounds in the "Student Weight" field.
18. Select the student's weight status from the dropdown menu in the "Student Weight Status" field.
19. Select the student's height status from the dropdown menu in the "Student Height Status" field.
20. Provide any additional comments or remarks about the student's medical history in the "Additional Comments" field.
21. Enter any relevant reasons for the student's weight status in the "Student Weight Status Reasons" field.
22. Enter any relevant reasons for the student's height status in the "Student Height Status Reasons" field.
23. Provide any medical test results or relevant information about the student's medical condition in the "Medical Test Result" field.
