# Nursing School Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather comprehensive information from applicants to streamline the admission process and ensure that all necessary information is provided in a clear and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **General Information** (page 1) with your name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your **Contact Details** (page 2) with your address, phone number, and email address.
3. Describe your **Education History** (page 3) including your previous degrees, institutions attended, and relevant coursework.
4. Outline your **Employment History** (page 4) including your previous work experience, job titles, and relevant skills.
5. Write a brief **Personal Statement** (page 5) explaining your motivations for applying to nursing school.
6. Summarize your **Academic History** (page 6) including relevant courses, grades, and academic achievements.
7. List your **References** (page 7) including their names, job titles, and contact information.
8. Attach your **Transcripts** (page 8) from previous institutions.
9. Provide your **Test Scores** (page 9) for relevant entrance exams.
10. Describe your **Language Proficiency** (page 10) including languages spoken, read, and written.
11. Share your **Nursing Background** (page 11) including any relevant healthcare experience.
12. List any **Other Certifications** (page 12) you hold.
13. Upload any required **Documents** (page 13) as per the instructions provided.
14. Review and acknowledge the information provided in the **Review and Acknowledgement** (page 14) section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (page 1)
- **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth.
- **Other Relevant Details**: Please provide any additional relevant details that may be required for admission.

### Contact Details (page 2)
- **Address**: Your permanent address.
- **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
- **Email Address**: Your email address.

### Education History (page 3)
- **Institution**: Name of the institution attended.
- **Degree**: Type of degree earned.
- **Relevant Coursework**: List relevant courses taken.

### Employment History (page 4)
- **Job Title**: Previous job title.
- **Employer**: Previous employer's name.
- **Relevant Skills**: List relevant skills used in the job.

### Personal Statement (page 5)
- **Statement**: Write a brief statement explaining your motivations for applying to nursing school.

### Academic History (page 6)
- **Coursework**: List relevant courses taken.
- **Grades**: List relevant grades earned.
- **Academic Achievements**: List relevant academic achievements.

### References (page 7)
- **Name**: Name of the reference.
- **Job Title**: Job title of the reference.
- **Contact Information**: Contact information for the reference.

### Transcripts (page 8)
- **Transcript**: Attach transcript from previous institution.

### Test Scores (page 9)
- **Test Name**: Name of the test taken.
- **Score**: Score achieved.

### Language Proficiency (page 10)
- **Language Spoken**: Languages spoken.
- **Language Read**: Languages read.
- **Language Written**: Languages written.

### Nursing Background (page 11)
- **Relevant Experience**: Describe any relevant healthcare experience.

### Other Certifications (page 12)
- **Certification Name**: Name of the certification.
- **Certification Number**: Certification number.

### Upload Documents (page 13)
- **Document**: Upload required documents as per the instructions provided.

### Review and Acknowledgement (page 14)
- **Review**: Review the information provided.
- **Acknowledgement**: Acknowledge that the information provided is accurate.
