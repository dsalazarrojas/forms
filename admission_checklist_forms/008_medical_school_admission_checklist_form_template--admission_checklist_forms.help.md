# Medical School Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a checklist for medical school admissions, used to track and manage applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the application deadline date, if applicable.
2. Provide the application fee amount, if relevant.
3. Enter your academic history, if required.
4. Write a personal statement that explains your interests, goals, and qualifications for medical school.
5. Choose the correct test scores that apply to you.
6. Fill out the interview date, if scheduled.
7. Select the correct interview time, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the medical school you are applying to.
9. Provide your contact information, if required.
10. Enter your medical history, if necessary.
11. Enter your medical school application ID, if you have one.
12. Choose the current application status, such as "Under Review" or "Complete".
13. Select all relevant medical schools you are applying to.
14. Enter the date of submission, if relevant.
15. Write down your career goals and aspirations, if desired.
16. Finally, select all relevant steps in the application process, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **application_deadline_date** (date): Enter the application deadline date for the medical school application.
- **application_fee** (number): Enter the application fee amount, if applicable.
- **academic_history** (text): Enter your academic history, including relevant coursework, degrees, and institutions attended.
- **personal_statement** (text): Write a personal statement that explains your interests, goals, and qualifications for medical school.
- **test_scores** (select one): Choose the correct test scores that apply to you.
- **interview_date** (date): Enter the interview date, if scheduled.
- **interview_time** (time): Enter the interview time, if applicable.
- **medical_school_name** (text): Enter the name of the medical school you are applying to.
- **contact_info** (text): Enter your contact information, if required.
- **medical_history** (text): Enter your medical history, if necessary.
- **medical_school_application_id** (text): Enter your medical school application ID, if you have one.
- **application_status** (select one): Choose the current application status, such as "Under Review" or "Complete".
- **medical_school** (select multiple): Select all relevant medical schools you are applying to.
- **submit_date** (date): Enter the date of submission, if relevant.
- **minimalist_goals** (text): Enter your career goals and aspirations, if desired.
- **goals** (text): Enter your career goals and aspirations, if desired.
- **application_process** (select multiple): Select all relevant steps in the application process, if required.
