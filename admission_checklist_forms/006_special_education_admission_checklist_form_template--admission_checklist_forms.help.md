# Special Education Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Admission Checklist Form Template is designed to collect comprehensive information from students and their families to ensure a thorough understanding of their needs and circumstances before providing special education services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "Required" label.
* If a field is not required, use it to provide additional information as needed.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect basic information about the student, such as name, age, and contact details.
* **Parent Or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect contact information about the student's parent or guardian.
* **Disability Status** (`disability_status`, text, required: false): This section is used to describe the student's disability status, if applicable.
* **Educational History** (`educational_history`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect information about the student's educational background.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect information about the student's medical history.
* **Special Education Services** (`special_education_services`, text, required: false): This section is used to describe the special education services the student may be eligible for.
* **Individualized Education Plan (IEP)** (`iep`, text, required: false): This section is used to outline the student's IEP, if applicable.
* **Behavioral Interventions** (`behavioral_interventions`, text, required: false): This section is used to describe any behavioral interventions the student may require.
* **Student Support Services** (`student_support_services`, text, required: false): This section is used to describe any additional support services the student may need.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect contact information about the student's emergency contact.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): This section is used to provide any additional comments or information about the student.
* **Signature And Date** (`signature_and_date`, text, required: false): This section is used to obtain a signature and date from the student or parent/guardian.
* **Notes And References** (`notes_and_references`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect any additional notes or references about the student.
* **Student Information 2** (`student_information_2`, text, required: false): This section is used to collect duplicate information about the student, if necessary.
