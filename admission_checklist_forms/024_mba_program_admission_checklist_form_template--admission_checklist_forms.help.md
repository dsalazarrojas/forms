# MBA Program Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from applicants to evaluate their eligibility for the MBA program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Date of Birth.
2. Provide your Academic Background, Resume, Transcript, and Letters of Recommendation.
3. Write a Personal Statement explaining your motivation for pursuing an MBA.
4. Specify your Highest Degree, Graduation Year, and University.
5. Choose your Degree Program (BA, MA, PhD), Graduation Year, and Degree Starting (Full time, Part time, Online).
6. Enter your Job Title, Company, Industry, Job Starting (Full time, Part time, Online), and Job Type.
7. Select your Job Ownership (Sole ownership, Shared ownership, None) and scores for Score 1, Score 2, and Score 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Academic Background** (`Academic Background`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your academic background.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required): Attach your resume or provide a link to it.
* **Transcript** (`Transcript`, text, required): Attach your transcript or provide a link to it.
* **Letters of Recommonders** (`Letters of Recommonders`, text, required): Attach letters of recommendation or provide a link to them.
* **Personal Statement** (`Personal Statement`, text, required): Write a personal statement explaining your motivation for pursuing an MBA.
* **Highest Degree** (`Highest Degree`, text, required): Enter your highest degree earned.
* **Degree Program** (`Degree Program`, select_one, required): Choose your degree program (BA, MA, PhD).
* **Graduation Year** (`Graduation Year`, number, required): Enter your graduation year.
* **University** (`University`, text, required): Enter the name of your university.
* **Degree Starting** (`Degree Starting`, select_one, required): Choose your degree starting type (Full time, Part time, Online).
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, text, required): Enter the industry you work in.
* **Job Starting** (`Job Starting`, select_one, required): Choose your job starting type (Full time, Part time, Online).
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, text, required): Enter your job type.
* **Job Ownership** (`Job Ownership`, select_one, required): Choose your job ownership type (Sole ownership, Shared ownership, None).
* **Score 1** (`Score 1`, number, required): Enter your score for score 1.
* **Score 2** (`Score 2`, number, required): Enter your score for score 2.
* **Score 3** (`Score 3`, number, required): Enter your score for score 3.
