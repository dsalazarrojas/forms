# Contact Lens Insertion And Removal Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document a patient's training process for contact lens insertion and removal. It ensures that the patient has received proper training on the necessary steps to insert and remove contact lenses safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's personal details, including name, ID number, date of birth, and training date.
2. Select the type of lenses being fit.
3. Answer the questions regarding the training steps for insertion and removal, including hand washing technique, lens handling, inspection, and other relevant topics.
4. Indicate if any of the steps have been completed independently or with assistance.
5. Sign the form to acknowledge completion and understanding of the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the patient.
* **Patient ID Number** (`Patient ID Number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Training Date** (`Training Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this training session.
* **Instructor Name** (`Instructor Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the training specialist.
* **Type of Lenses Being Fit** (`Type of Lenses Being Fit`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the lenses being fit, such as Soft Daily Disposable, Soft Bi-weekly, etc.
* **Insertion Training Steps** (`Insertion Training Steps`, `note`, required): Mark the completed steps for insertion training.
* **Hand Washing Technique** (`Hand Washing Technique`, `select_one`, required): Select the proper hand hygiene technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Lens Handling Technique** (`Lens Handling Technique`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct lens handling technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Lens Inspection Before Insertion** (`Lens Inspection Before Insertion`, `select_one`, required): Select the lens inspection technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Eyelid Holding Technique** (`Eyelid Holding Technique`, `select_one`, required): Select the proper eyelid management technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Lens Placement on Eye** (`Lens Placement on Eye`, `select_one`, required): Select the successful insertion technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Removal Training Steps** (`Removal Training Steps`, `note`, required): Mark the completed steps for removal training.
* **Pinch Removal Technique** (`Pinch Removal Technique`, `select_one`, required): Select the soft lens removal technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Slide and Blink Technique** (`Slide and Blink Technique`, `select_one`, required): Select the alternative removal method demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **RGP Removal Tool Usage** (`RGP Removal Tool Usage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the usage of RGP removal tool, either independently or with assistance.
* **Lens Care and Cleaning** (`Lens Care and Cleaning`, `select_one`, required): Select the maintenance technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Storage Case Hygiene** (`Storage Case Hygiene`, `select_one`, required): Select the case cleaning and replacement technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Solution Usage Understanding** (`Solution Usage Understanding`, `select_one`, required): Select the proper solution usage technique demonstrated, either independently or with assistance.
* **Safety Warnings Understood** (`Safety Warnings Understood`, `select_one`, required): Select when not to wear lenses, either fully understood or partially understood.
* **Follow-up Appointment Scheduled** (`Follow-up Appointment Scheduled`, `date`, required): Enter the next visit date.
* **Overall Training Assessment** (`Overall Training Assessment`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's progress, either ready for independent wear or not.
* **Instructor Signature** (`Instructor Signature`, `text`, required): Enter the digital signature of the training specialist.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's acknowledgment of training completion.

## Tips
- Ensure that the patient understands the importance of proper hand hygiene and lens handling techniques.
- Review the training steps with the patient to ensure they are comfortable and confident in their ability to insert and remove contact lenses safely and effectively.
- Schedule follow-up appointments as necessary to assess the patient's progress.
