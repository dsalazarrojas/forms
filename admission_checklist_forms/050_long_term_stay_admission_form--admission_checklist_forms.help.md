# Long Term Stay Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Term Stay Admission Form is used to gather essential information about an individual's health, medical history, and emergency contacts. This form is required for all applicants who will be staying in the facility for an extended period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information (Page 1)
- **Applicant Name**, **Name**, (Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Applicant Date of Birth**, **Date of Birth**, (Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
### Emergency Contact
- **Emergency Contact**, **Name**, (Required): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Relationship to You**, **Relationship**, (Optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
### Medical Clearance
- **Medical Clearance**, **Yes/No**, (Optional): Check yes if you have any medical clearance or clearance to be in this facility.
### Medical Conditions
- **Medical Conditions**, **Select Multiple**, (Optional): List any medical conditions you have, such as Diabetes, Hypertension, or Asthma.
### Medication List
- **Medication List**, **Text**, (Optional): List any medications you're currently taking.
### Medication Dosage
- **Medication Dosage**, **Yes/No**, (Optional): Check yes if you take medication with a specific dosage.
### Social Security Number
- **Social Security Number**, **Text**, (Required): Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification.
### Emergency Contact Relationship
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**, **Text**, (Optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
### Medical Provider
- **Medical Provider**, **Text**, (Optional): Name of your medical provider.
### Medical Provider Address
- **Medical Provider Address**, **Text**, (Optional): Address of your medical provider.
### Medical Record Number
- **Medical Record Number**, **Text**, (Optional): Number assigned by your medical provider.
### Medical Record Date
- **Medical Record Date**, **Date**, (Optional): Date of your last medical record update.
### Medical History
- **Medical History**, **Text**, (Optional): Briefly describe your medical history.
### Medical Allergies
- **Medical Allergies**, **Text**, (Optional): List any allergies you have.
### Medical Conditions 2
- **Medical Conditions 2**, **Select Multiple**, (Optional): List any additional medical conditions you have.
### Emergency Contact Information
- **Emergency Contact Information**, **Text**, (Optional): Provide contact information for the emergency contact.
### Additional Notes
- **Additional Notes**, **Note**, (Optional): Add any additional information not covered in other fields.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the applicant, but it's recommended that the applicant review and verify the information with their medical provider before submitting it.
