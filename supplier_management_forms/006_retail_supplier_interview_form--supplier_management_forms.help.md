# Retail Supplier Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retail Supplier Interview Form" is a tool used to collect information from retail suppliers. This form helps in gathering essential details about the supplier's name, contact, and address, along with assessing their ability to provide specific services or meet certain criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the supplier's name in the "supplier_name" field.
2. Provide the supplier's company name in the "company_name" field.
3. Enter the supplier's contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Enter the supplier's email address in the "email" field.
5. Provide the supplier's address in the "street", "city", "state", and "zip" fields.
6. In the "interview_details" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
7. Answer each of the "questions_*" fields with either "Yes" or "No" based on the supplier's ability to meet the specified criteria or provide the required services.

* **company_name** (Text, Required): Enter the supplier's company name as it appears on their business card or letterhead.

* **city** (Text, Required): Enter the supplier's city.
