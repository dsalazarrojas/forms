# Supplier Standardization Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Standardization Application Form is used to collect information from suppliers for the standardization of their processes and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant certifications and agreements that apply to your supplier.
3. Choose the onboarding process that best suits your supplier's needs.
4. Fill in any additional notes or information that may be helpful for our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your supplier's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your supplier's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_one, required/optional): Select any relevant certifications that apply to your supplier, such as ISO 9001 or ISO 13485.
* **Agreements** (`agreements`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any agreements that apply to your supplier, such as Master Agreement or Annex 1.
* **Onboarding Requirements** (`onboarding_requirements`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information or requirements that may be necessary for our team to onboard your supplier.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments that may be helpful for our team to review.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your supplier's information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your vendor's information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Vendor Information 2** (`vendor_info_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional vendor information.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, select_one, required/optional): Select the onboarding process that best suits your supplier's needs.
* **Onboarding Process 2** (`onboarding_process_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to the onboarding process.
