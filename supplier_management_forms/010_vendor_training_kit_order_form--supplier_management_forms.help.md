# Vendor Training Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Training Kit Order Form is used to place orders for vendor training kits. This form allows users to provide order details, shipping information, and other relevant details to ensure smooth processing of the order. It is a crucial tool for vendors to receive their training kits on time and in the right condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Order details" field to enter your order details.
- Select the kits you would like to order using the "Select kits" field.
- Enter your shipping information in the "Shipping details" field.
- Enter the shipping date for your order in the "Shipping" field.
- If you have any further comments or requests, please use the "Review and ship" field.
- Enter the name of the vendor you are ordering for in the "Vender name" field.
- Enter your email address to contact you for further instructions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order details (`order_details`, text, required: false)**
  Enter a brief description of your order, such as the type of training kit you need or the purpose of your purchase.
- **Shipping details (`shipping_details`, text, required: false)**
  Enter your shipping address, including any special instructions for delivery.
- **Select kits (`kits`, select_multiple, required: true)**
  Choose the training kits you would like to order. You can select one or multiple kits.
- **Submit request (`submit_request`, note, required: false)**
  This field is currently not used, but if you have any comments or requests, you can enter them here.
- **Review and ship (`review_and_ship`, text, required: false)**
  Enter any further comments or requests for your order, such as special handling instructions.
- **Shipping (`shipping`, date, required: false)**
  Enter the expected date for your order to be shipped.
- **Vendor name (`vendor_name`, text, required: false)**
  Enter the name of the vendor you are ordering for, if applicable.
- **E-mail (`email`, email, required: false)**
  Enter your email address to contact you for further instructions or updates.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (Select kits) to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
