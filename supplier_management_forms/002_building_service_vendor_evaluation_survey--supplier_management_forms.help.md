# Building Service Vendor Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation from service vendors on their performance and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information in the Evaluator Information section.
2. Enter the name of the service vendor being evaluated in the Vendor Company Name field.
3. Enter the type of service provided by the vendor in the Type of Service Provided field.
4. Rate the vendor's performance in various aspects such as service quality, timeliness, and professionalism.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Information**: 
  (1. Evaluator Name, 2. Department or Team, required)
  This section is for the evaluator to enter their contact information. Fill in your full name and department or team name.

* **Evaluator Name**:
  (1. Your Name, required)
  Enter your full name as the person completing this evaluation.

* **Department or Team**:
  (1. Department or Team, required)
  Enter the name of your department or team.

* **Evaluation Date**:
  (1. Evaluation Date, required)
  Enter the date this evaluation is being completed.

* **Vendor Information**:
  (1. Note)
  This section is for reference, but it is not required to be filled.

* **Vendor Company Name**:
  (1. Vendor Company Name, required)
  Enter the name of the service vendor being evaluated.

* **Contact Person at Vendor**:
  (1. Text, optional)
  Enter the primary contact name at the vendor company.

* **Type of Service Provided**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Select the category of building service provided by the vendor.

* **Contract Period Evaluated**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Select how long the vendor has provided service.

* **Service Quality Rating**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Rate the quality of work performed by the vendor.

* **Timeliness and Reliability**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Rate how promptly and reliably services are delivered.

* **Professionalism and Communication**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Rate staff professionalism and communication skills.

* **Cost Value for Service**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Is the pricing fair and competitive.

* **Problem Resolution and Responsiveness**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  How quickly are issues addressed when they arise.

* **Equipment and Materials Quality**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Quality of tools and materials used.

* **Safety and Compliance Standards**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Adherence to safety protocols and regulations.

* **Vendor Strengths**:
  (1. Text, optional)
  What does this vendor do particularly well.

* **Areas for Improvement**:
  (1. Text, optional)
  Specific areas where the vendor could improve.

* **Overall Recommendation**:
  (1. Select One, required)
  Would you recommend continuing with this vendor.

* **Additional Comments**:
  (1. Text, optional)
  Any other feedback or observations.

## Tips
1. Be honest and thorough in your evaluation.
2. Use the provided rating scale to guide your answers.
3. Provide specific feedback where possible.
4. Keep the comments concise and clear.
