# Sustainable Printing Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers who wish to become sustainable printing partners with our company. It is essential to ensure that you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to increase the chances of being considered as a partner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and contact person's information in the Supplier Name and Contact Person fields respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field. This will help us contact you regarding your application.
3. Fill in your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Provide your company name and address in the Company and Address fields respectively.
5. Select the relevant capabilities you offer, such as Sustainable Printing, Environmental Sustainability, and Social Responsibility.
6. Choose any applicable certifications, such as ISO 14001, FSC, GOTS, and Oeko-Tex 100.
7. Add any additional notes about your application in the Notes field.
8. Enter the date and time you submit this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your company address.
* **Capabilities** (`capabilities`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following capabilities: Sustainable Printing, Environmental Sustainability, and Social Responsibility.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_one, optional): Select one or more of the following certifications: ISO 14001, FSC, GOTS, and Oeko-Tex 100.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your application.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submit this form.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to follow up on your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be thorough when selecting capabilities and certifications to increase your chances of becoming a partner.
* Double-check your submission date and time for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
