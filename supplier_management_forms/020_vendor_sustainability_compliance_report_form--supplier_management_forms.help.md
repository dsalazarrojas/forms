# Vendor Sustainability Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Sustainability Compliance Report Form is designed to collect information about a vendor's sustainability practices and compliance status. This form will help the company assess the vendor's commitment to sustainability and make informed decisions about their business relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review the following steps:

1. Provide your sustainability practices by answering the questions on the first page of the form.
2. If applicable, select the certifications that you hold.
3. Choose the status of your ESG report and compliance report.
4. Upload any relevant documents that support your sustainability claims.
5. Add any comments or additional information that you would like to share with the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Sustainability Report Form** (`vendor_sustainability_report_form`): Provide a brief description of your sustainability practices and performance.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`vendor_sustainability_practices`): Describe your sustainability practices, such as using renewable energy sources, reducing waste, or implementing sustainable supply chain practices.
* **Sustainability Certifications** (`sustainability_certifications`): Check the certifications that apply to your sustainability practices, such as ISO 26000 or GRI.
* **List of Certifications** (`sustainability_certifications_list`): List any other certifications that you have obtained that demonstrate your commitment to sustainability.
* **Report Form Date** (`report_form`): The date that this report was completed.
* **ESG Report Status** (`esg_report_status`): Indicate whether your ESG report is active or inactive.
* **Compliance Report Status** (`compliance_report_status`): Indicate whether your compliance report is active or inactive.
* **Supplier Comments** (`supplier_comments`): Add any comments or additional information that you would like to share with the company.
* **Document Uploads** (`document_uploads`): Attach any relevant documents that support your sustainability claims.
  * If you select "Yes", you will be able to upload documents to support your claims.
  * If you select "No", you will not be able to upload documents.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`): The date that this report was completed.
