# Cylindrical Roller Bearing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be completed by suppliers to provide quotes for cylindrical roller bearings with specific dimensions and sizes. It is used to streamline the quote request process and ensure that quotes are provided in a standardized format.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please select the correct type of cylindrical roller bearing you are requesting a quote for. 
*   Provide a detailed description of the bearings you are requesting, if necessary. 
*   Enter the size of the bearings you are requesting. 
*   Indicate the quantity of bearings you need. 
*   Add any additional comments you have about the quote request, if necessary. 
*   Enter the email address of the contact person. 
*   Enter the phone number of the contact person. 
*   Select who requested the quote. 
*   Provide the date and time when the quote request was sent. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Quote Request** (`supplier_management_forms_cylindrical_roller_bearing_quote_request_form`, `select_one`, optional)

*   Please select the type of Cylindrical Roller Bearing you are requesting a quote for from the options provided.

### 2. **Bearing Description** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_bearing_description`, `text`, optional)

*   Provide a detailed description of the bearings you are requesting, if necessary.

### 3. **Bearing Size** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_bearing_size`, `number`, optional)

*   Enter the size of the bearings you are requesting.

### 4. **Quantity** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_bearing_quantity`, `number`, optional)

*   Indicate the quantity of bearings you need.

### 5. **Quote Request Comments** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_quote_request_comments`, `note`, optional)

*   Add any additional comments you have about the quote request, if necessary.

### 6. **Contact Email** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_contact_email`, `email`, optional)

*   Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 7. **Contact Phone** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_contact_phone`, `text`, optional)

*   Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### 8. **Requested By** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_requested_by`, `select_multiple`, optional)

*   Select who requested the quote from the provided options.

### 9. **Quote Request Date** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_quote_request_date`, `date`, optional)

*   Enter the date when the quote request was sent.

### 10. **Quote Request Time** (`supplier_management_forms_roller_bearing_quote_request_time`, `time`, optional)

*   Enter the time when the quote request was sent.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the supplier can provide a correct quote.
*   If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
