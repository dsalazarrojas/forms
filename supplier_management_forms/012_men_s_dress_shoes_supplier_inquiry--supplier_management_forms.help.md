# Men's Dress Shoes Supplier Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Men's Dress Shoes Supplier Inquiry form. The purpose of this form is to collect information from suppliers of men's dress shoes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Supplier Inquiry Form" page.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Ensure you have permission from the supplier before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Supplier Inquiry Form</u> (Page 1)
This page is the main form where you will find all the fields to be filled.

* **Supplier Inquiry Form**: This is where you'll enter any specific information or requests you have regarding the supplier.
### Supplier Name (ID: 2)
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the full name of the supplier, as this will help identify the supplier and distinguish it from others.
### Supplier Email (ID: 3)
* **Supplier Email**: Enter the supplier's email address, ensuring it's a valid email format (e.g., [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com)).
### Supplier Phone (ID: 4)
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the supplier's phone number where you can contact them.
### Supplier Address (ID: 5)
* **Supplier Address**: Provide the supplier's address where they can be contacted or received at.
### Supplier Website (ID: 6)
* **Supplier Website**: Enter the supplier's website if they have one.
### Supplier Product (ID: 7)
* **Supplier Product**: Enter a brief description of the product you are inquiring about or interested in from the supplier.
### Supplier Description (ID: 8)
* **Supplier Description**: Enter a short description of the supplier, which can include their specialty or expertise in men's dress shoes.
### Supplier Image (ID: 9)
* **Supplier Image**: Enter any image URL or link that may be relevant to the supplier or their product.
### Supplier Document (ID: 10)
* **Supplier Document**: Upload any relevant document related to the supplier or their product.
### Supplier Notes (ID: 11)
* **Supplier Notes**: Use this field for any additional information you'd like to note regarding the supplier, such as notes on their reliability or communication.
### Tips
* Ensure you have permission from the supplier before filling out this form.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions.
* Attach any relevant images or documents directly to the supplier for a smoother communication process.
