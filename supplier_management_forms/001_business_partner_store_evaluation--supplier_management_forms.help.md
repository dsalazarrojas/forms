# Business Partner Store Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of business partners' stores.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the store name and date of the evaluation.
2. Provide the full name of the business partner and the name of the evaluation manager.
3. Rate the overall store condition, cleanliness, and other aspects of store operations.
4. Describe any issues identified during the evaluation.
5. Suggest improvements for the store.
6. Schedule a follow-up visit if necessary.
7. Sign and date the evaluation completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`Store Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, date, required): Date when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Business Partner Name** (`Business Partner Name`, text, required): Full name of the business partner.
* **Evaluation Manager** (`Evaluation Manager`, text, required): Name of person conducting the evaluation.
* **Overall Store Condition** (`Overall Store Condition`, select_one, required): Rate the general condition of the store.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness and Maintenance** (`Cleanliness and Maintenance`, select_one, required): Assess the cleanliness level.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Inventory Accuracy** (`Inventory Accuracy`, select_one, required): Is inventory properly organized and accurate.
	+ Fully Accurate
	+ Mostly Accurate
	+ Partially Accurate
	+ Needs Attention
* **Product Display and Layout** (`Product Display and Layout`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of product display.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Staff Appearance and Conduct** (`Staff Appearance and Conduct`, select_one, required): Rate staff professionalism.
	+ Professional
	+ Acceptable
	+ Needs Training
	+ Unacceptable
* **Customer Service Quality** (`Customer Service Quality`, select_one, required): Assess the level of customer service.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Compliance Status** (`Compliance Status`, select_multiple, optional): Check compliance with standards.
	+ Health and Safety
	+ Pricing Accuracy
	+ Product Quality
	+ Documentation
* **Issues Identified** (`Issues Identified`, text, optional): Describe any issues found during evaluation.
* **Recommended Improvements** (`Recommended Improvements`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for the store.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, date, optional): Scheduled date for follow-up visit.
* **Evaluation Completion Date** (`Evaluation Completion Date`, date, required): Date evaluation was completed.
