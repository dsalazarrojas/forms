# procurement_payment_audit_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **procurement_payment_audit_registration_form** is a form used to collect and document information related to procurement payments. This form serves as a record of a payment made to a supplier and is used for audit and review purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Supplier Payment Info** section with details about the payment, such as the payment amount and date.
2. Provide the **Payment Link** if applicable, which can be a URL or a file attachment.
3. Enter any **Reviewer Comments** in the **Reviewer Comments** field, if necessary.
4. If you are the reviewer, enter your **Reviewer Name**, **Reviewer Email**, and **Reviewer Phone** in the respective fields.
5. Enter your department name in the **Reviewer Department** field.
6. Finally, select the **Date Reviewed** field to enter the date you reviewed the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Payment Info**: Enter details about the payment, including the payment amount and date.
* **Payment Link**: If you have a link to the payment or a file attachment, provide it here.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments you have about the payment.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer of the payment.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter your email address so others can contact you.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter your phone number so others can contact you.
* **Reviewer Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Date Reviewed**: Select the date you reviewed the payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately and thoroughly fill out the form with all required fields.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, ask your supervisor or contact the system administrator.
