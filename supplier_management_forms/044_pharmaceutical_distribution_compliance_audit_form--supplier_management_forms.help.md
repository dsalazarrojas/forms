# Pharmaceutical Distribution Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the details of a compliance audit performed on a pharmaceutical distribution company. The information collected will help in tracking the company's compliance status and ensure that they are following all the necessary regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Contact Info" page and fill in the company's contact information, including name, address, city, state, and postal code.
2. On the "Company Details" page, fill in the company's details, including name, address, city, state, and country.
3. On the "Audit Info" page, select the date and time of the audit, and fill in the location where the audit took place.
4. On the "Auditor Details" page, fill in the auditor's name, phone number, and email address.
5. On the "Audit Results" page, select the results of the audit, which can be "Good", "Needs Improvement", or "Non-Compliant".
6. On the "Additional Info" page, fill in any additional comments or notes from the audit.
7. On the "Follow-up Info" page, fill in the details for any follow-up actions, including date, location, and contact information.
