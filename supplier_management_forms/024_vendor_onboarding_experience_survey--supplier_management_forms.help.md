# Vendor Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the vendor onboarding experience. Completing this form will help the company improve its onboarding processes and provide better service to its suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier name and contact details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your supplier type from the available options.
3. Choose your supplier status from the available options.
4. Enter the date and time of your first contact with the company.
5. Select the follow-up action and date/time for any follow-up interactions.
6. Provide any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your supplier name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Contact Job Function** (`contact_job_function`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job function.
* **Contact Location** (`contact_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter your location.
* **Supplier Type** (`supplier_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your supplier type from the available options.
* **Supplier Status** (`supplier_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your supplier status from the available options.
* **Date First Contact** (`date_first_contact`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your first contact with the company.
* **Time First Contact** (`time_first_contact`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your first contact with the company.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes about your experience.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your first contact.
* **Phone Sent** (`phone_sent`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number associated with your first contact.
* **Time Sent** (`time_sent`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your first contact.
* **Date Sent** (`date_sent`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your first contact.
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the follow-up action taken.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Note** (`follow_up_note`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Sent** (`follow_up_sent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the follow-up action sent status.
* **Follow-up Received** (`follow_up_received`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the follow-up action received status.
