# Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request form is a tool for supply chain managers to predict and prepare for potential disruptions in the supply chain. This form helps gather information about the segment, region, product group, and type of disruption to identify potential risks and inform decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate segment from the list of available options.
2. Choose the correct region from the list of available options.
3. Select the relevant product group from the list of available options.
4. Select the type of disruption that best describes the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Segment (`select_segment`, required)
* Select the geographic segment where the disruption is expected to occur, such as Americas, EMEA, or Asia Pacific.

### Region (`select_region`, required)
* Choose the region where the disruption is expected to occur, such as US, Canada, Mexico, Europe, Africa, or Asia.

### Product Group (`select_product_group`, required)
* Select the product group affected by the disruption, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Disruption Type (`select_disruption_type`, required)
* Select the type of disruption that best describes the situation, such as supply chain delay, material shortage, quality issue, other, or no disruption.

## Tips
* This form is only required to be filled out when a disruption is expected or suspected.
* Ensure that you select the correct segment, region, product group, and disruption type accurately to provide valuable insights for decision-making processes.
* If the disruption type is "Other", please provide a detailed description of the disruption in the "Additional Information" field, if provided.
