# Software Hardware Supplier Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The software hardware supplier referral form is used to gather information about a supplier referral, which is a recommendation made by an employee to a colleague about a potential software or hardware supplier.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "Referral Source" field, which is where you will indicate who referred the supplier.
2.  Next, fill out the "Referred Company" field with the name of the company that the supplier represents.
3.  Complete the "Employee Name" field with your name and the name of the employee who referred you to the supplier.
4.  If you have an email address for the contact person, please enter it in the "Contact Email" field.
5.  If you have a phone number for the contact person, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field.
6.  In the "Referred Department" field, indicate which department the supplier is a part of.
7.  In the "Referred Position" field, indicate the position of the contact person.
8.  In the "Supplier Referral Reason" field, explain why you are referring this supplier.
9.  In the "Supplier Referral Note" field, add any additional information that may be helpful.
10.  In the "Referred Company URL" field, enter the website of the company the supplier is representing.
11.  In the "Referred Company Address" field, enter the address of the company the supplier is representing.
12.  In the "Referred Company Phone" field, enter the phone number of the company the supplier is representing.
13.  In the "Referred Company Email" field, enter the email address of the company the supplier is representing.
14.  In the "Referral Date" field, choose the date the referral was made.
15.  In the "Referral Time" field, choose the time the referral was made.
16.  In the "Supplier Referral Status" field, indicate the status of the referral.
17.  In the "Supplier Referral Follow Up Date" field, choose the date for the next follow-up.
18.  In the "Supplier Referral Follow Up Time" field, choose the time for the next follow-up.
19.  In the "Supplier Referral Last Follow Up Date" field, choose the date for the last follow-up.
20.  In the "Supplier Referral Last Follow Up Time" field, choose the time for the last follow-up.
21.  In the "Supplier Referral Next Follow Up Date" field, choose the date for the next follow-up.
22.  In the "Supplier Referral Next Follow Up Time" field, choose the time for the next follow-up.
23.  In the "Supplier Referral Last Contact Date" field, choose the date of the last contact.
24.  In the "Supplier Referral Last Contact Time" field, choose the time of the last contact.
25.  In the "Supplier Referral Last Contact Note" field, add any additional information about the last contact.
