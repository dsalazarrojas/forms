# Sustainability And Supplier Diversity Spend Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for sustainability and diversity spend reports. The report helps organizations track and analyze their sustainability and diversity spend, enabling informed decisions and improvements in their supply chain diversity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the relevant period for your report from the provided options.
2. Enter the start and end dates (if required).
3. List your suppliers (if required).
4. Select all relevant sustainability updates (at least one is mandatory).
5. Provide your contact information (email and phone number) for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **select_period** (required): Choose the period for your report from the provided options (e.g., quarter, half year, year).
* **start_date** (optional): Enter the start date for the period if required. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **end_date** (optional): Enter the end date for the period if required. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **supplier_list** (optional): Provide a list of your suppliers, separating each supplier with a comma or on separate lines. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **sustainability_updates** (required): Select all relevant sustainability updates that apply to your report. At least one selection is mandatory.
* **diverse_spend** (optional): Enter the amount of spend related to diverse suppliers. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **submit** (required): Select the method to submit your report (e.g., email or print).
* **email** (required): Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **phone** (required): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **notes** (optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your report.
