# Utility Cost And Vendor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about utility costs and vendor details from suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the supplier name in the `supplier-name` field.
2. Select the type of utility service provided by the supplier from the `utility-type` dropdown menu.
3. Add any relevant vendor details in the `vendor-details` field.
4. Enter the supplier's address, city, and state in the `supplier-address`, `supplier-city`, and `supplier-state` fields, respectively.
5. Enter the supplier's zip code in the `supplier-zip` field.
6. Add the contact person's name, email, and phone number in the `contact-name`, `contact-email`, and `contact-phone` fields, respectively.
7. Enter the cost of the utility service in the `cost` field.
8. Choose the start and end dates for the utility service in the `start-date` and `end-date` fields, respectively.
9. Add any additional notes about the vendor in the `vendor-notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Utility Cost And Vendor Information** (`utility_cost_and_vendor_information_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier-name`): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Utility Type** (`utility-type`): Select the type of utility service provided by the supplier from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	+ Water
	+ Gas
	+ Electricity
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor-details`): Add any relevant details about the vendor.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier-address`): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier City** (`supplier-city`): Enter the supplier's city.
* **Supplier State** (`supplier-state`): Enter the supplier's state.
* **Supplier Zip** (`supplier-zip`): Enter the supplier's zip code.
* **Contact Name** (`contact-name`): Add the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact-email`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact-phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Cost** (`cost`): Enter the cost of the utility service.
* **Start Date** (`start-date`): Choose the start date of the utility service.
* **End Date** (`end-date`): Choose the end date of the utility service.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor-notes`): Add any additional notes about the vendor.
