# jewelryConflictFreeSourcingAuditForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jewelryConflictFreeSourcingAuditForm is a form used to collect information about a supplier's conflict-free sourcing practices. This form helps ensure that suppliers are in compliance with our company's conflict-free sourcing policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information for the supplier.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for "Conflict Free" fields based on the supplier's policies and procedures.
3. Enter the correct audit date and supplier name.
4. Review and sign the "Conflict Free Declaration" section to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Conflict Free** (`conflict_free`, select_one, required): Select "Conflict Free (Yes/No)" if the supplier has conflict-free sourcing practices, "Conflict Free (Not Applicable)" if the supplier is not applicable, or "Conflict Free (Conflict)" if the supplier has a conflict.
* **Material** (`material`, select_multiple, required): Select all materials that the supplier sources conflict-free.
* **Conflict Free Note** (`conflict_free_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the supplier's conflict-free sourcing practices.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Audited By** (`audited_by`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Conflict Free Source** (`conflict_free_source`, select_one, required): Select "Conflict-Free Source".
* **Conflict Free Certification** (`conflict_free_certification`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free certifications held by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Declaration** (`conflict_free_declaration`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free declarations signed by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Documentation** (`conflict_free_documentation`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free documentation held by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Policy** (`conflict_free_policy`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free policies in place by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Process** (`conflict_free_process`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free processes followed by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Review** (`conflict_free_review`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free reviews completed by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Status** (`conflict_free_status`, select_one, required): Select the current conflict-free status.
* **Conflict Free Type** (`conflict_free_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all conflict-free types of sources held by the supplier.
* **Conflict Free Certification Number** (`conflict_free_certification_number`, text, required): Enter the conflict-free certification number.
* **Conflict Free Issue** (`conflict_free_issue`, select_multiple, required): Select any conflict-free issue.
* **Conflict Free Comments** (`conflict_free_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the supplier's conflict-free sourcing practices.
* **Conflict Free Signature** (`conflict_free_signature`, text, required): Enter your name and title to sign the conflict-free declaration.
* **Conflict Free Declaration Signed** (`conflict_free_declaration_signed`, select_multiple, required): Select "Conflict-Free Declaration Signed".
* **Conflict Free Date** (`conflict_free_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the conflict-free declaration.
