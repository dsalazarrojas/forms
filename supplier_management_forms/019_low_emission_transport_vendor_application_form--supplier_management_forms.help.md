# Low Emission Transport Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from vendors offering low-emission transport services. It's crucial to fill out this form correctly to ensure that your application is processed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, and make sure to answer all the required fields (if any).
- Select all relevant services you offer from the "Vendor Services" dropdown.
- Enter any relevant notes about your services or transport vehicles.
- Provide your contact email address and phone number.
- Upload any required documents (if applicable).
- Select your qualification status from the available options.
- If approved, enter the date and time of your approval in the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Details (Text)**: Enter your company name, address, and any other relevant information about your transport business.
* **Vendor Services (Select Multiple)**: Choose all services you offer from the dropdown menu, such as Service A, Service B, or Service C.
* **Service Review (Number)**: Rate your services, with higher numbers indicating better quality or efficiency.
* **Notes (Text Area)**: Add any additional comments or information about your services or transport vehicles.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you about your application.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Document Upload (Text)**: Upload any supporting documents required for your application, such as certificates or proof of insurance.
* **Qualification Status (Select One)**: Choose the qualification status that best describes your transport business from the available options, like Option A, Option B, or Option C.
* **Approval Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your approval (if you're already approved).
* **Approval Time (Time)**: Enter the time of your approval (if you're already approved).
