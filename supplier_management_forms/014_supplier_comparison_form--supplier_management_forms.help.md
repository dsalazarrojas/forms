# Supplier Comparison Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Comparison Form is a tool designed to collect and compare information about multiple suppliers. Its purpose is to gather and compare quotes, decision notes, supplier information, and other relevant details to make informed decisions about suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by carefully reading this help guide to understand the purpose and each field of the form.
- Review the quotes and select all relevant quotes that apply to the supplier(s) being evaluated.
- Enter any relevant decision notes to help clarify your reasoning and thoughts.
- Fill out the supplier information fields, providing as much detail as possible.
- Select the current status of the supplier (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- Add any additional notes about the supplier.
- Enter the quote date and quote time (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the supplier or any relevant general information.
  * This field should be used to include any background information about the supplier, such as their location, industry, or services offered.
  * Keep it concise and focused on the essential details.
- **Quotes** (`quotes`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple quotes provided by the supplier.
  * Review the quotes carefully and select all relevant ones that apply to the supplier.
  * This field is designed to give you a comprehensive view of the quotes provided by the supplier.
- **Decision Notes** (`decision_notes`, note, optional): This field is for adding any additional notes about the decision-making process.
  * Use this field to clarify your thoughts and reasoning behind the evaluation.
  * Keep the notes concise and focused on the key points.
- **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing more in-depth information about the supplier.
  * Use this field to include any additional details that are not covered in the general information field.
  * Be as detailed as possible to help with the comparison.
- **Supplier Status** (`supplier_status`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting the current status of the supplier.
  * Choose from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
  * This field helps to categorize the supplier based on their current status.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
  * Use this field to include any extra information that didn't fit in the other fields.
  * Keep the notes concise and focused on the key points.
- **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date of the quote.
  * If applicable, enter the date of the quote to help with tracking and comparison.
  * Format the date in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Quote Time** (`quote_time`, time, optional): This field is for entering the time of the quote.
  * If applicable, enter the time of the quote to help with tracking and comparison.
  * Format the time in the standard 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS).
