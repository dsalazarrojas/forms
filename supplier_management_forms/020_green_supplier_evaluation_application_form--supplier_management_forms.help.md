# Green Supplier Evaluation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Supplier Evaluation Application Form is used to collect and manage information about green suppliers. It helps users to evaluate and track the performance of suppliers based on their sustainability practices and ESG scores.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter the company name of the supplier in the "Company Name" field.
3. Provide the location of the supplier in the "Location" field.
4. Enter the contact person's name and email address in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Email" fields.
5. Select the relevant certifications that the supplier has in the "Certifications" field.
6. Provide additional comments or information about the supplier in the "Documents" field.
7. Enter any sustainability practices implemented by the supplier in the "Sustainability Practices" field.
8. Enter the ESG score of the supplier in the "ESG Score" field.
9. Select the evaluation method used to evaluate the supplier in the "Evaluation Method" field.
10. Provide the evaluation date and time in the "Evaluation Date" and "Evaluation Time" fields.
11. Enter any comments about the evaluation process in the "Comments" field.
12. Select the status of the supplier (Active or Inactive) in the "Status" field.
13. Indicate whether the supplier has been assigned to a specific person in the "Assigned To" field.
14. Check if the supplier was created by the current user in the "Created By" field.
15. Check if the supplier has been last modified by the current user in the "Last Modified By" field.
16. Enter the date and time when the supplier was created in the "Created At" field.
17. Enter the date and time when the supplier was last modified in the "Last Modified At" field.
18. Enter the ID of the user who created the supplier in the "Created By Id" field.
19. Enter the ID of the user who last modified the supplier in the "Last Modified Id" field.
20. Enter the ID of the user who assigned the supplier to in the "Assigned To Id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required false): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required false): Enter the name of the supplier's company.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required false): Enter the location of the supplier.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required false): Enter the website of the supplier.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, required false): Select the certifications that the supplier has.
* **Documents** (`documents`, `text`, required false): Provide any additional documents or information about the supplier.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, `text`, required false): Enter the sustainability practices implemented by the supplier.
* **ESG Score** (`esg_score`, `number`, required false): Enter the ESG score of the supplier.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, `select_one`, required false): Select the evaluation method used to evaluate the supplier.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, required false): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required false): Enter any comments about the evaluation process.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required false): Select the status of the supplier (Active or Inactive).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_multiple`, required false): Indicate whether the supplier has been assigned to a specific person.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_multiple`, required false): Check if the supplier was created by the current user.
* **Last Modified By** (`last_modified_by`, `select_multiple`, required false): Check if the supplier has been last modified by the current user.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required false): Enter the date and time when the supplier was created.
* **Last Modified At** (`last_modified_at`, `date`, required false): Enter the date and time when the supplier was last modified.
* **Created By Id** (`created_by_id`, `number`, required false): Enter the ID of the user who created the supplier.
* **Last Modified Id** (`last_modified_id`, `number`, required false): Enter the ID of the user who last modified the supplier.
* **Assigned To Id** (`assigned_to_id`, `number`, required false): Enter the ID of the user who assigned the supplier to.
* **Active/Inactive Status Explanation**: Select "Active" if the supplier is currently active and "Inactive" if the supplier is not currently active.
* **Contact Information Explanation**: Enter the contact information of the supplier, including name and email address.
* **Evaluation Method Explanation**: Select "Yes" if the supplier was evaluated, "No" otherwise.
* **Evaluating Status Explanation**: Enter the status of the evaluation process.
* **Evaluation Date and Time Explanation**: Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
