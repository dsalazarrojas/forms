# Product Specification Partner Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Specification Partner Release Form is designed to collect and verify information from partners about their released products. This form is crucial for our internal management and processing of product specifications. Please fill this form accurately and completely to ensure smooth processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your supplier name in the field provided.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm if the product is being released.
3.  (Optional) If "Yes", provide any specific product specifications in the note field.
4.  Enter the release date of the product.
5.  Provide any remarks about the product release.
6.  Enter your supplier's email address.
7.  Enter your supplier's phone number.
8.  Enter your supplier's address.
9.  Enter the product ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **supplier_name** (Text, required): This is the name of the supplier releasing the product. Please enter the correct name to ensure accurate identification.
*   **product_type** (Select, optional): This field is used to confirm if the product is being released. Select "Yes" to indicate that the product is being released.
*   **specifications** (Note, optional): This field is for providing any specific product specifications. If you have any notes or information about the product, please enter them here.
*   **release_date** (Date, optional): Enter the date of the product release for our records.
*   **remarks** (Text, required): Provide any remarks or comments about the product release.
*   **supplier_email** (Email, optional): Enter your supplier's email address for communication purposes.
*   **supplier_phone** (Text, optional): Enter your supplier's phone number for contact.
*   **supplier_address** (Text, optional): Enter your supplier's address for further communication and contact.
*   **product_id** (Number, optional): Enter the product ID if applicable. This is usually provided by the supplier.
