# Supplier Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Agreement Extension Form is used to extend a supplier agreement, updating relevant information and providing a new extension date and other details. This form is essential for managing supplier contracts and maintaining accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the supplier's details, including their name and ID.
2. Confirm or reject the agreement, specifying whether to extend it.
3. Enter the extension date and other relevant information.
4. Enter the contract number and other details.
5. Provide reasons for the extension, if applicable.
6. Confirm the supplier's contact details, including email and phone number.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier** (`supplier`, `number`, required): Enter the supplier's ID number.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether to extend the agreement (Yes or No).
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date to extend the agreement (if applicable).
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contract number.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for extension, if applicable.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the supplier's name.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current contract status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the supplier's contact phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the contract end date.
* **Supplier ID** (`supplier_id`, `number`, required): Enter the supplier's ID number.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional reason, if applicable.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contract number (if required).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes, if applicable.
