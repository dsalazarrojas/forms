# Vendor Quality Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track vendor quality issues. Please fill out this form when you identify a quality issue with a vendor.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Vendor Name** of the company that is experiencing the quality issue.
2. Provide a brief description of the **Issue Details**.
3. Select the **Issue Severity** level of the problem (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
4. Attach any relevant **Issue Evidence** that supports your claim.
5. Enter the **Issue Date** and **Issue Time** when the issue occurred.
6. Add the **Vendor Contacts** for the company experiencing the quality issue.
7. Briefly describe the **Issue Resolution** status (e.g., pending, in-progress, or resolved).
8. Select the **Issue Status** (Open, Closed, or Resolved).
9. Enter any **Vendor Comments** regarding the issue.
10. Share any **Issue Comments** about the issue's context and resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`): Enter the name of the vendor experiencing the issue.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`): Briefly describe the issue with the vendor's product or service.
* **Issue Severity** (`issue_severity`): Select the level of severity of the issue (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
*   * **Issue Evidence** (`issue_evidence`): Attach any evidence (e.g., photos, videos, or documentation) that supports your claim.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`): Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (`issue_time`): Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Vendor Contacts** (`vendor_contacts`): Enter the contact information for the vendor.
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`): Briefly describe the resolution status of the issue.
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`): Select the status of the issue (Open, Closed, or Resolved).
*   * **Vendor Comments** (`vendor_comments`): Enter any comments from the vendor regarding the issue.
* **Issue Comments** (`issue_comments`): Enter any comments about the issue's context and resolution.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to reach out to your internal team for guidance.
* Once completed, please review and submit the form to your internal team for processing.
