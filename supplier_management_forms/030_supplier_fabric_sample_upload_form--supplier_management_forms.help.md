# Supplier Fabric Sample Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Fabric Sample Upload Form is a form used to collect information about a fabric sample from a supplier. This form is used to record the details of a fabric sample, including its description, color, weight, length, and other relevant details. The form is used by the purchasing team to verify and confirm the fabric sample before making a purchase decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Form Information" page with the required and optional fields.
2. On the "Supplier Information" page, enter the supplier's details, if available.
3. On the "Fabric Sample" page, enter the fabric sample's details, including its color, weight, length, and other relevant information.
4. On the "Fabric Sample Files" page, upload any relevant files for the fabric sample, such as photos or videos.
5. Review and confirm the entered information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Information** (`id: 1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the form title and any other relevant information.
- **Supplier Information** (`id: 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the supplier's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
- **Fabric Sample** (`id: 3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's description, color, weight, length, and other relevant information.
- **Fabric Sample Files** (`id: 4`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any relevant files for the fabric sample, such as photos or videos.
- **Fabric Color** (`id: 5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's color.
- **Fabric Type** (`id: 6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's type.
- **Fabric Weight** (`id: 7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's weight.
- **Fabric Length** (`id: 8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's length.
- **Fabric Width** (`id: 9`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's width.
- **Fabric Height** (`id: 10`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's height.
- **Fabric Notes** (`id: 11`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the fabric sample.
- **Fabric Email** (`id: 12`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the supplier's email address, if available.
- **Fabric Phone** (`id: 13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the supplier's phone number, if available.
- **Fabric Date** (`id: 14`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's date.
- **Fabric Time** (`id: 15`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's time.
- **Fabric Date Time** (`id: 16`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the fabric sample's date and time.
- **Supplier Category** (`id: 17`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the supplier's category.
- **Supplier Subcategory** (`id: 18`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the supplier's subcategory.
- **Fabric Category** (`id: 19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the fabric sample's category.
- **Fabric Subcategory** (`id: 20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the fabric sample's subcategory.
- **Fabric Tags** (`id: 21`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select relevant tags for the fabric sample.
- **Fabric Description** (`id: 22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the fabric sample.
- **Fabric Image** (`id: 23`, `text`, required/optional): Upload an image of the fabric sample.
- **Fabric Video** (`id: 24`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a video of the fabric sample.
- **Fabric Audio** (`id: 25`, `text`, required/optional): Upload an audio file related to the fabric sample.

Note: The fields marked as required or optional are subject to the discretion of the purchasing team.
