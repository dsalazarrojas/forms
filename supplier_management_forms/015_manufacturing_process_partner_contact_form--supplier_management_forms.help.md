# manufacturing_process_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential manufacturing partners about their company, contact person, and manufacturing capabilities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of your company in the "Company Details" field.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for communication with your company in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your company's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your company's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select the manufacturing capabilities that apply to your company in the "Manufacturing Capabilities" field.
6. Select the type of manufacturing process your company offers in the "Manufacturing Process" field.
7. Enter your company's manufacturing location in the "Manufacturing Location" field.
8. Optionally, provide a description of your manufacturing process in the "Manufacturing Process Description" field.
9. Add any additional notes about your submission in the "Submission Notes" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`title`, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person** (`name`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for communication with your company.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Manufacturing Capabilities** (select multiple, required): Select the types of manufacturing capabilities that your company offers, such as manufacturing of small to medium-sized batches or large batch manufacturing.
* **Manufacturing Process** (select one, required): Select the type of manufacturing process that your company offers, such as injection molding or tooling.
* **Manufacturing Location** (text, required): Enter your company's location.
* **Manufacturing Process Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your manufacturing process.
* **Submission Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your submission, if desired.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your internal contact or the form's creator for clarification.
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your submission is correct and complete.
