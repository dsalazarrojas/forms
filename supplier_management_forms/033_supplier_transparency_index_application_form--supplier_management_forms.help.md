# supplier_transparency_index_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplier_transparency_index_application_form is used to collect information about a supplier's transparency levels, ethics, certifications, and other relevant details. This form will be used to assess the transparency and accountability of suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions carefully before proceeding.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information about the supplier before filling out the form.
3. Enter the supplier's general information on page 1 of the form.
4. Select the relevant certifications that apply to the supplier on page 1 of the form.
5. Enter any additional sustainability certifications that do not appear in the list on page 1.
6. Enter the supplier's contact information, including name and email, on page 2 of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, text, optional): This section is used to enter general information about the supplier. This can include their name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Transparency Index** (`2`, number, optional): This section is used to score the supplier's level of transparency. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Ethics Index** (`3`, number, optional): This section is used to score the supplier's level of ethics. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Certifications Index** (`4`, number, optional): This section is used to score the supplier's level of certifications. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Sustainability Index** (`5`, number, optional): This section is used to score the supplier's level of sustainability. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Supplier Information** (`6`, text, optional): This section is used to enter additional details about the supplier. This can include any relevant comments or notes.
* **Sustainability Certifications** (`7`, select_multiple, required): This section is used to select the relevant certifications that apply to the supplier. Please select all that apply.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when entering information about the supplier.
- Ensure you select all relevant certifications that apply to the supplier.
- Use the contact information section to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
