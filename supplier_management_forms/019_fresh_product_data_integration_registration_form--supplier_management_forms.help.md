# Fresh Product Data Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Product Data Integration Registration Form is designed to collect information about suppliers and their products, as well as contact details of the individuals or organizations involved, to support the integration of fresh products with other systems or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the **Registration Form** section (Field 1).
2.  Then, proceed to the **Supplier Information** section (Field 2), where you can input relevant details about the supplier.
3.  Next, fill in the **Product Data** section (Field 3) with the information about the product you want to integrate.
4.  Finally, provide your **Contact Information** (Field 4) for communication and follow-up purposes.
5.  After completing all sections, click the **Submit** button (Field 5) to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form** (Field 1, Text, optional): This is the initial form entry point. Simply start filling it in to initiate the registration process.
- **Supplier Information** (Field 2, Text, optional): Enter relevant details about the supplier, such as their name, location, or contact methods.
- **Product Data** (Field 3, Text, optional): Provide information about the fresh product, like its name, description, or product category.
- **Contact Information** (Field 4, Text, optional): Input your contact details, including name, email, or phone number, for the purpose of communication and follow-up.
- **Submit** (Field 5, Text, optional): Click this button to finalize your registration and submit your fresh product data integration request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and clarity.
* Contact form@gic.mx if you encounter any issues or have further questions.
