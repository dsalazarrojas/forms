# Material Source Validation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Source Validation Request Form is used to collect and validate information about the source of materials. This form helps the supplier management team verify the authenticity and quality of materials received from various suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Gather Information
Collect the necessary information about the supplier and the material, including the supplier's name, contact details, material name, and relevant documents.

### Step 2: Complete the Form
Fill out the form with the collected information, including:

* Supplier Name: Enter the name of the supplier.
* Material Name: Enter the name of the material.
* Supplier Details: Enter any additional details about the supplier, such as their contact information or notes.
* Material Certifications: Select the relevant certifications for the material (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* Document URLs: Enter any relevant URLs for documents related to the material.
* Supplier Documents: Enter any relevant documents related to the supplier.
* Material Characteristics: Enter any specific characteristics of the material, such as its dimensions or material composition.
* Supplier Contacts: Enter the contact information for the supplier.
* Review Date: Select the date of the review.
* Review Time: Select the time of the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supplier Name
* <code>Supplier Name</code> (`supplier_name`, `text`, **required**)
Enter the name of the supplier.

### Material Name
* <code>Material Name</code> (`material_name`, `text`, **required**)
Enter the name of the material.

### Supplier Details
* <code>Supplier Details</code> (`supplier_details`, `note`, **optional**)
Enter any additional details about the supplier, such as their contact information or notes.

### Material Certifications
* <code>Material Certifications</code> (`material_certifications`, `select_multiple`, **required**)
Select the relevant certifications for the material (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').

### Document URLs
* <code>Document URLs</code> (`document_urls`, `text`, **optional**)
Enter any relevant URLs for documents related to the material.

### Supplier Documents
* <code>Supplier Documents</code> (`supplier_documents`, `text`, **optional**)
Enter any relevant documents related to the supplier.

### Material Characteristics
* <code>Material Characteristics</code> (`material_characteristics`, `text`, **optional**)
Enter any specific characteristics of the material, such as its dimensions or material composition.

### Supplier Contacts
* <code>Supplier Contacts</code> (`supplier_contacts`, `text`, **optional**)
Enter the contact information for the supplier.

### Review Date
* <code>Review Date</code> (`review_date`, `date`, **optional**)
Select the date of the review.

### Review Time
* <code>Review Time</code> (`review_time`, `time`, **optional**)
Select the time of the review.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask the supplier management team for clarification.
- If you are creating a new material source, make sure to check for duplication before submitting the form.
