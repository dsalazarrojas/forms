# Supplier Financing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Financing Application Form is designed to collect information from suppliers that are requesting financing. This form will help our team assess their financial stability and determine the likelihood of approving their loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the supplier's name, contact person, company name, and company address.
2. Provide the city, state, and zip code of the company location.
3. Enter the supplier's email address.
4. If applicable, enter the supplier's phone number.
5. Specify the finance terms you are requesting.
6. Enter the finance amount.
7. Indicate the loan term in time format.
8. Specify the loan interest rate (as a percentage).
9. Choose the current status of the loan application (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
10. Optional: enter any additional loan notes if necessary.
11. Choose the bank details (Bank A, Bank B, or Bank C) if you have any.
12. If you wish to keep your credit score hidden, please enter 'N/A' in this field.
13. Choose the type of company (Private, Public, or Non-Profit).
14. Specify the industry (Retail, Service, or Technology).
15. Select the business licenses (if applicable, multiple selections allowed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Contact Person** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Company Name** (3, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier's company.
* **Company Address** (4, text, optional): Enter the address of the supplier's location.
* **City** (5, text, optional): Enter the city of the supplier's location.
* **State** (6, text, optional): Enter the state of the supplier's location.
* **Zip Code** (7, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the supplier's location.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Phone Number** (9, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Finance Terms** (10, text, optional): Specify the finance terms you are requesting.
* **Finance Amount** (11, number, optional): Enter the finance amount.
* **Loan Term** (12, time, optional): Indicate the loan term in time format.
* **Loan Interest** (13, number, optional): Specify the loan interest rate as a percentage.
* **Loan Status** (14, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the loan application (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Loan Notes** (15, note, optional): Enter any additional loan notes if necessary.
* **Bank Details** (16, select_multiple, optional): Choose the bank details (Bank A, Bank B, or Bank C) if you have any.
* **Credit Score** (17, text, optional): If you wish to keep your credit score hidden, please enter 'N/A' in this field.
* **Company Type** (18, select_one, optional): Choose the type of company (Private, Public, or Non-Profit).
* **Industry** (19, select_one, optional): Choose the industry (Retail, Service, or Technology).
* **Business License** (20, select_multiple, optional): Select the business licenses if applicable, multiple selections allowed.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as you can to ensure a complete evaluation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
