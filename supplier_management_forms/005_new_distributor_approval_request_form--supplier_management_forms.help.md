The New Distributor Approval Request Form is used to request approval from the company to become a new distributor. This form is typically completed by potential distributors who want to sell the company's products in their region. The form helps to gather necessary information about the distributor's business, contact information, and product details. 

To ensure that the form is completed accurately and efficiently, please review the following steps to complete the form:

## Purpose
This form is for requesting approval from the company to become a new distributor. If you are already an approved distributor, you should not use this form. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Request" field (page 1) with a brief description of your request.
2. On the "Business Details" page (page 2), enter your business name and address.
3. On the "Contact" page (page 3), enter your contact information.
4. On the "Product" page (page 4), enter the product name and description.
5. On the "Product Type" page (page 5), select the type of product you are requesting to distribute.
6. On the "Approval Status" page (page 6), select the current status of your request.
7. On the "Additional Request Options" page (page 7), select any additional options you require.
8. On the "Priority Request" page (page 8), select your priority level for the request.
9. On the "Additional Comments" page (page 9), enter any additional comments about your request.
10. On the "Confirmation" page (page 10), confirm that you have submitted your request correctly.
11. On the "Submission Status" page (page 11), select the status of your submission.
12. On the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" pages (page 12 and 13), enter the date and time of your submission.
13. On the "Submitter Information" page (page 14), enter your name and title.
14. On the "Department" page (page 15), enter your department.
15. On the "Job Title" page (page 16), enter your job title.
16. On the "Email" page (page 17), enter your email address.
17. Finally, click the "Submit" button (page 18) to complete and submit your request.
