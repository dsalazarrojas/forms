# Vendor KYC Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a vendor's KYC information, including their name, contact details, nationality, and identity documents. This information is used to onboard the vendor into the system and for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the FirstName field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the LastName field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the DateOfBirth field.
6. Enter your Address in the Address field.
7. Select your Country of Citizenship from the CountryOfCitizenship field.
8. Select your Country of Residence from the CountryOfResidence field.
9. Upload your identity document 1 (Document1) in the Document1 field.
10. Upload your identity document 2 (Document2) in the Document2 field.
11. (Note: Document3 and Document4 are optional)
12. (Note: Document5 is optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName** (`FirstName`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **LastName** (`LastName`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **DateOfBirth** (`DateOfBirth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **CountryOfCitizenship** (`CountryOfCitizenship`, select_one, required): Select your country of citizenship.
* **CountryOfResidence** (`CountryOfResidence`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Document1** (`Document1`, note, required): Upload your identity document 1.
* **Document2** (`Document2`, note, required): Upload your identity document 2.
* **Document3** (`Document3`, note, optional): (Optional) Upload your identity document 3.
* **Document4** (`Document4`, note, optional): (Optional) Upload your identity document 4.
* **Document5** (`Document5`, note, optional): (Optional) Upload your identity document 5.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields (FirstName, LastName, Email, Phone, DateOfBirth, Address, CountryOfCitizenship, and CountryOfResidence).
* Ensure that your email and phone number are correctly formatted.
* If you need to upload any of the identity documents, please scan or take a clear photo of them to ensure they are readable.
* Please note that not all fields are mandatory, but providing additional identity documents can improve the onboarding process.
