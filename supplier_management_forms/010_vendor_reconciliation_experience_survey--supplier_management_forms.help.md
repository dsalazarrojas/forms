# Vendor Reconciliation Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Reconciliation Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from suppliers regarding their experience with the company's reconciliation process. The goal is to gather data and insights to improve communication and finance processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Provide specific examples when possible.
3. Choose the correct rating or option for each question.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reconciliation Process** (`reconciliation_process`, text, optional): Please describe your experience with the company's reconciliation process. Provide any details about what worked well and what didn't.
* **How would you rate the communication quality?** (`communication_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with the company's communication regarding the reconciliation process. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **What improvement ideas do you have?** (`improvement_ideas`, select_multiple, optional): Provide specific examples of ways the company could improve its reconciliation process. Choose from:
	+ Process Improvement
	+ Communication
	+ Reporting
	+ Other
* **Any comments?** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the reconciliation process.
* **Finance Contact** (`finance_contact`, select_one, optional): Do you currently have a finance contact with the company?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Procurement Contact** (`procurement_contact`, select_one, optional): Do you currently have a procurement contact with the company?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Date you submitted this form.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Your name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Which department does your company belong to?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Where is your company located?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Who submitted this form for you?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Who or what is this form submitted to?
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Your company name.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, optional): Your company email.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): Your company phone number.
* **Submitted To Title** (`submitted_to_title`, text, optional): The title of the person or department submitting this form.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
