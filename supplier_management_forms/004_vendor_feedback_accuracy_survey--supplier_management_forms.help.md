# Vendor Feedback Accuracy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and performance information about vendors or suppliers. It helps evaluate the accuracy of vendor feedback and ensure that it is submitted correctly and in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form as instructed below.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback about the vendor or supplier.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for submitting and submitting information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_information`, text, optional): This field is for entering any relevant information about the vendor or supplier.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the person or team submitting the feedback.
* **Performance Assessment** (`performance_assessment`, number, optional): Provide a numerical assessment of the vendor's or supplier's performance.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the vendor or supplier.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how the vendor or supplier was contacted (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or team submitting the feedback.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or team submitting the feedback.
* **Vendor Comments** (`vendor_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the vendor or supplier.
* **Internal Comments** (`internal_comments`, text, optional): Enter any internal comments or notes about the vendor or supplier.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select whether the feedback was submitted by a person or team (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Record the date the feedback was submitted.
* **Submitted By Department** (`submitted_by_department`, select_one, optional): Select which department submitted the feedback (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted By Jobtitle** (`submitted_by_jobtitle`, select_one, optional): Select the job title of the person or team submitting the feedback (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted By Role** (`submitted_by_role`, select_one, optional): Select the role of the person or team submitting the feedback (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitted_by_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location of the person or team submitting the feedback (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted By Team** (`submitted_by_team`, select_one, optional): Select the team submitting the feedback (e.g., "Yes", "No", "Other").
* **Submitted By Comments** (`submitted_by_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the person or team submitting the feedback.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the guidelines above or consult with your supervisor or team lead.
* This form is designed to be comprehensive, so please be thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or team lead.
