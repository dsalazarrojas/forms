# Heat Resistant Material Vendor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Heat Resistant Material Vendor Application Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or the name of the person submitting on behalf of the company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the submission.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of material you are applying to supply.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your application.
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for your application.
* **Certification Attachments** (`certification_attachments`, text, optional): If you have certification attachments, please attach them here.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the material you are applying to supply.
* **Technical Documents** (`technical_documents`, text, required): Attach any relevant technical documents related to the material.
* **Heat Resistance Temperature** (`heat_resistance_temp`, number, required): Enter the heat resistance temperature of the material.
* **Maximum Working Temperature** (`max_working_temp`, number, required): Enter the maximum working temperature of the material.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Company** (`reviewer_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer company.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of your submission.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately before submitting the form.
