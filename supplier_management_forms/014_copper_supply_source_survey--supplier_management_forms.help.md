# Copper Supply Source Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential copper supply sources. It helps the organization to evaluate the suppliers' capabilities and determine if they can meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the sourcing regions from which you can supply copper materials. You can choose multiple regions.
2. Enter your available capacity to supply copper materials.
3. Check if you have any relevant certifications.
4. Enter your supply source name.
5. Enter your supply source address.
6. Enter your supply source email (if available).
7. Enter your supply source phone number (if available).
8. Enter any supply source notes (optional).
9. Rate your supply source (if you choose to do so). Enter any additional comments for your rating (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sourcing Regions** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the regions from which you can supply copper materials. This field allows you to choose multiple regions and is required.
* **Available Capacity** (`<name>`, `number`, required): Enter your available capacity to supply copper materials.
* **Certifications** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have any relevant certifications.
* **Supply Source Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your supply source.
* **Supply Source Address** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your supply source.
* **Supply Source Email** (`<name>`, `email`, required): Enter your supply source email (if available). This field is not required if you do not have an email.
* **Supply Source Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your supply source phone number (if available). This field is not required if you do not have a phone number.
* **Supply Source Notes** (`<name>`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your supply source. This field is not required.
* **Supply Source Rating** (`<name>`, `number`, required): Rate your supply source (if you choose to). Enter any additional comments for your rating (optional).
* **Supply Source Rating Notes** (`<name>`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments for your rating (if you choose to rate).
