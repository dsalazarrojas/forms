# Conscious Warehouse Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a warehouse's registration details. The purpose of this form is to ensure that warehouses are properly registered and meet the required standards for operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Registration Date" field and enter the date of the warehouse registration.
2. Enter the official name of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Name" field.
3. Enter the unique identifier for the warehouse in the "Warehouse ID" field.
4. Enter the name of the operating company in the "Company Name" field.
5. Enter the name of the primary contact person in the "Contact Person Name" field.
6. Enter the email address of the primary contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the primary contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter the full physical address of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Address" field.
9. Enter the city location of the warehouse in the "City" field.
10. Enter the state or province where the warehouse is located in the "State or Province" field.
11. Enter the country where the warehouse is located in the "Country" field.
12. Enter the postal code (ZIP or postal code) of the warehouse location in the "Postal Code" field.
13. Select the approximate size of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Size" field.
14. Enter the number of pallet positions or units of storage capacity in the "Storage Capacity" field.
15. Select the primary function of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Type" field.
16. Select whether the warehouse has climate control capabilities in the "Is Warehouse Temperature Controlled" field.
17. Select all applicable sustainability certifications in the "Sustainability Certifications" field.
18. Select all applicable environmental practices in the "Environmental Practices Implemented" field.
19. Select all applicable safety certifications in the "Safety Certifications" field.
20. Select all applicable labor practices in the "Labor Practices" field.
21. Select all applicable technology systems in use in the "Technology Systems in Use" field.
22. Select the operating hours of the warehouse in the "Operating Hours" field.
23. Select the approximate number of warehouse staff in the "Number of Employees" field.
24. Indicate whether the warehouse has an ethical sourcing policy in the "Do You Have an Ethical Sourcing Policy" field.
25. Enter the date of the most recent external audit in the "When Was Last External Audit" field (if applicable).
26. Enter any additional relevant information in the "Additional Information" field.
27. Select the registration status of the warehouse in the "Registration Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date**: The date of warehouse registration.
* **Warehouse Name**: The official name of the warehouse.
* **Warehouse ID**: Unique identifier for the warehouse.
* **Company Name**: The name of the operating company.
* **Contact Person Name**: The name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Warehouse Address**: The full physical address of the warehouse.
* **City**: The city location of the warehouse.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where the warehouse is located.
* **Country**: The country where the warehouse is located.
* **Postal Code**: The postal code (ZIP or postal code) of the warehouse location.
* **Warehouse Size**: The approximate size of the warehouse in square footage.
* **Storage Capacity**: The number of pallet positions or units of storage capacity.
* **Warehouse Type**: The primary function of the warehouse.
* **Is Warehouse Temperature Controlled**: Whether the warehouse has climate control capabilities.
* **Sustainability Certifications**: Select all applicable sustainability certifications.
* **Environmental Practices Implemented**: Select all applicable environmental practices.
* **Safety Certifications**: Select all applicable safety certifications.
* **Labor Practices**: Select all applicable labor practices.
* **Technology Systems in Use**: Select all applicable technology systems.
* **Operating Hours**: The operating hours of the warehouse.
* **Number of Employees**: The approximate number of warehouse staff.
* **Do You Have an Ethical Sourcing Policy**: Whether the warehouse has an ethical sourcing policy.
* **When Was Last External Audit**: The date of the most recent external audit.
* **Additional Information**: Any other relevant information.
* **Registration Status**: The registration status of the warehouse.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access and complete this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Please complete the form carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
