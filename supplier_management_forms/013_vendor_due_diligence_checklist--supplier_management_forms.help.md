# Vendor Due Diligence Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a critical tool used to evaluate potential or existing vendors to ensure that the partnership is secure and profitable. It helps in gathering, reviewing, and validating essential information about the vendor.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required fields for each page.
2. Complete Page 1 - Vendor Information with the required fields.
3. For any additional comments or information, proceed to Page 10 - Additional.
4. Ensure that you have reviewed and validated all the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_due_diligence_checklist`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you'll collect essential details about the vendor.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, number, optional): Please provide your contact information, such as phone or email.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select one, required): Select the type of vendor you are assessing. Options include:
	+ Reseller
	+ Manufacturer
	+ Service Provider
	+ Distributor
	+ Consultant
	+ Contractor
* **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, select multiple, optional): Choose the current status of the vendor. Options include:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Hold
	+ Archived
* **Vendor Location** (`vendor_location`, text, optional): Provide the vendor's location or address.
* **Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Email** (`vendor_email`, email, optional): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Vendor Rating** (`vendor_rating`, number, optional): Rate the vendor based on your assessment.
* **Notes** (`vendor_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the vendor.
* **Page 10 - Additional** (`page_10`, text, optional): This is the second page of the form where you can add any additional comments or information.
