# Ready Meal Supplier Onboarding Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register new ready meal suppliers during onboarding. Please fill in the required and relevant information for your ready meal supplier.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
- If a field is optional, fill it in if you think it's necessary or relevant.
- For fields that have options, select the correct or most suitable option for your ready meal supplier.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the assigned user for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your ready meal supplier. For example, "ABC Ready Meals".
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your contact person. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your contact person. This will help us contact you for any questions or concerns.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant documents you have, such as Business Registration Document, Health Certificate, or Insurance Document. This will help us understand your supplier's qualifications.
* **Coverage Details** (`coverage_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your coverage, such as insurance coverage or warranty details. This is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Coverage Start** (`coverage_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your coverage, if applicable. This will help us understand when your coverage begins.
* **Coverage End** (`coverage_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your coverage, if applicable. This will help us understand when your coverage ends.
* **Frequency** (`coverage_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your deliveries or services. For example, "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly".
* **Supplier Type** (`supplier_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your ready meal supplier, such as "Ready Meal Service" or "Grocery Supplier".
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your supplier, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Status Note** (`status_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your supplier's status. This is for internal reference only.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user responsible for your account. If left blank, it will be assigned automatically.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, time, required): This field displays the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, required): This field displays the date and time the form was last updated.
