# Vendor Product Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Product Verification Request Form is a tool for suppliers to provide detailed information about their products, which is then reviewed by the platform's moderators for verification and listing on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier information on page 1.
2. Provide product information, including details, documentation, and any additional comments.
3. Add a product description and image URL (if available).
4. If necessary, provide a product video URL (if available).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Supplier Information (page 1)**
• **Supplier Information (text)**: This is where you can provide your company name, address, and contact information.
• **Supplier Documentation (text)**: Please upload any relevant documentation or files related to your product.
• **Additional Comments (text)**: If you have any additional information or clarification to provide about your supplier or product, this is the place to do so.

### **Product Information (page 2)**
• **Product Description (text)**: Briefly describe your product, including its features and benefits.
• **Product Image URL (text)**: If you have a URL for an image of your product, provide it here.
• **Product Video URL (text)**: If you have a URL for a video of your product, provide it here.

### Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all fields relevant to your product and supplier.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact our support team for assistance.
* The form is reviewed and updated regularly to ensure accuracy and efficiency.
* You may need to resubmit or update your form after review and approval.
* Make sure to save your form for future reference or editing.
