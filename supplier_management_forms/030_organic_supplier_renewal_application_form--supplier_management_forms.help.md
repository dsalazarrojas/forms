# Organic Supplier Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Supplier Renewal Application Form is designed to gather the necessary information from suppliers to renew their organic certification with the organization. This form will help you provide the required details for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required for the renewal process.
2. Fill in each field with accurate and complete information about your supplier and their certifications.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supplier Details
* Supplier Details (`supplier_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your supplier, including their name and any other relevant details.

### Renewal Details
* Renewal Details (`renewal_details`, text, optional): Describe the details of your renewal, including any changes to your supplier's information or certifications.

### Certifications
* Certifications (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant certifications or accreditations held by your supplier.

### Product Categories
* Product Categories (`product_categories`, text, optional): Specify the categories of products your supplier provides.

### Supplier Contacts
* Supplier Contacts (`supplier_contacts`, text, optional): List the contact information for your supplier, including phone number and email.

### Product Labels
* Product Labels (`product_labels`, text, optional): Specify any labels or tags associated with the products supplied by your supplier.

### Business License
* Business License (`business_license`, text, optional): Provide any relevant business license information for your supplier.

### Sales License
* Sales License (`sales_license`, text, optional): List any sales licenses held by your supplier.

### Organic Accreditation
* Organic Accreditation (`organic_accreditation`, text, optional): Confirm or provide information on your supplier's organic accreditation status.

# Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have completed all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
