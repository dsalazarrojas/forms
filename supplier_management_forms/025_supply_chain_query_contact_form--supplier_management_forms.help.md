# Supply Chain Query Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supply Chain Query Contact Form is a form designed to gather information from suppliers for efficient communication and resolution of queries and inquiries related to the supply chain process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "Thoughts" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier_name**: Enter the supplier's name.
* **contact_person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **inquiry_date**: Enter the date of the query.
* **inquiry_details**: Enter a description of the query.
* **attachment**: (Optional) Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **contact_name**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **contact_email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **contact_position**: Enter the contact person's position.
* **contact_company**: Enter the contact person's company name.
* **contact_address**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **contact_comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the contact person.
* **query_description**: Enter a description of the query.
* **query_attachment**: (Optional) Attach any supporting documents or files.
* **query_priority**: Specify the priority level of the query (yes or no).
* **query_status**: Select the status of the query (high, medium, or low).
* **query_date**: Enter the date and time for the query.
* **query_time**: Enter the expected time for response or resolution.
* **query_comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the query.
