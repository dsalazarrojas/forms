# Goods Receiving Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Goods Receiving Form is designed to record important information about goods received from suppliers. This form captures details such as delivery dates, supplier names, and warehouse locations to ensure accurate tracking and management of incoming goods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct date of delivery from the calendar input field.
2. Enter the name of the supplier providing the goods.
3. Choose the relevant warehouse location for storing the goods.
4. Briefly describe the goods being received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required=false):
	+ Enter the date when the goods were delivered to the warehouse.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required=false):
	+ Type the name of the supplier providing the goods.
* **Warehouse Location** (`warehouse_location`, select_multiple, required=false):
	+ Choose one or more locations where the goods will be stored.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required=false):
	+ Provide a brief description of the goods being received.

## Tips
- Double-check the delivery date to ensure accuracy.
- Verify the supplier name for proper credit and payment processing.
- Select all relevant warehouse locations for goods distribution and inventory management.
- Keep the item description clear and concise for easy reference.
