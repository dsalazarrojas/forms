# Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form is designed to register participants and their supply exchange needs and offers. It helps in collecting accurate and efficient data about participants, their roles, and the supplies they need or have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your **Participant Name**, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** (optional), which is used for communication purposes.
3. Provide your **Participant Phone** number, which is a required field.
4. Enter your **Organization Name**, which is a required field.
5. Choose your **Participant Type** from the provided options (Nonprofit, Community Group, or Organizer).
6. Fill in your **Participant Role**, which is an optional field.
7. Add any additional **Participant Notes** (optional).
8. For the **Supply Offer** section, enter the supply you have to offer, which is a required field.
9. Describe the **Supply Needs** you have, which is a required field.
10. Select the **Event Date** for the supply exchange, which is a required field.
11. Choose the **Event Time** for the supply exchange, which is a required field.
12. Enter the **Event Location**, which is a required field.
13. (Optional) Provide a link to your **Participant URL**.
14. (Optional) Provide a link to your **Supply URL**.
15. Add any additional **Notes** (optional).
16. Select your **Participant Category** and **Subcategory** (optional).
17. (Optional) Select the **Supply Subcategory** and **Supply Type**.
18. Fill in the **Supply Description**, which is a required field.
19. Enter the **Supply Quantity**, which is a required field.
20. Enter the **Supply Unit**, which is a required field.
21. Enter your **Participant Address**, which is a required field.
22. Choose the **Supply Status** from the provided options (Available, Pending, or Unavailable).
23. (Optional) Enter your **Participant Address 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your name as a participant in the fair supply exchange.
* **Participant Email** (Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (Required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Participant Type** (Required): Choose your participant type from the options provided.
* **Participant Role** (Optional): Describe your role as a participant.
* **Participant Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant.
* **Supply Offer** (Required): Describe the supply you have to offer.
* **Supply Needs** (Required): Describe the supply you need.
* **Event Date** (Required): Select the date for the supply exchange event.
* **Event Time** (Required): Select the time for the supply exchange event.
* **Event Location** (Required): Enter the location for the supply exchange event.
* **Participant URL** (Optional): Provide a link to your website or social media profile.
* **Supply URL** (Optional): Provide a link to the supply you have to offer.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about the supply or participant.
* **Participant Category** (Optional): Select your category as a participant.
* **Participant Subcategory** (Optional): Select your subcategory as a participant.
* **Supply Subcategory** (Optional): Select the category for the supply you have to offer.
* **Supply Type** (Optional): Select the type for the supply you have to offer.
* **Supply Description** (Required): Describe the supply you have to offer.
* **Supply Quantity** (Required): Enter the quantity of the supply you have to offer.
* **Supply Unit** (Required): Enter the unit of the supply you have to offer.
* **Participant Address** (Required): Enter your address as a participant.
* **Supply Status** (Required): Choose the status of your supply (Available, Pending, or Unavailable).
* **Participant Address 2** (Optional): Enter an additional address for the participant.
