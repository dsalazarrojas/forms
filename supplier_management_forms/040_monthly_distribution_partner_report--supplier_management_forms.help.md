This form is used for monthly distribution partner reporting. It is intended to capture information about a partner's distribution performance, including their monthly distribution data and performance indicators. This form should only be used to report data for a specific partner on a specific month, so it's best to complete this form one partner at a time. Please do not use this form for reporting data for multiple partners or months. Ensure you have all necessary information for each field before filling out the form.

# monthly_distribution_partner_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly distribution partner report is used to record data for a partner's distribution performance on a specific month. This report is essential for tracking and analyzing partner distribution data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the distribution partner(s) you are reporting on from the 'Distribution Partners' dropdown list.
2. Choose the month and year you are reporting on from the 'Month' and 'Year' fields respectively. If you do not know the specific month and year, please leave these fields blank.
3. Provide the partner's performance data for the specified month (e.g., sales, profit, etc.).
4. Input any relevant partner performance data for inventory management (e.g., stock levels, etc.).
5. Specify any partner needs or comments that may be relevant to the report.
6. Optionally, provide a partner contact's email address and phone number.
7. Choose the category and subcategory for the partner (if applicable).
8. Enter the partner's URL and phone number (if applicable).
9. Optionally, provide any additional notes about the partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Partners**: Select all relevant distribution partners for the report.
* **Month**: Choose the month for which you are reporting on the partner's performance.
* **Year**: Choose the year for which you are reporting on the partner's performance.
* **Performance Data**: Enter any relevant performance data for the partner, such as sales, profit, etc.
* **Inventory Data**: Enter any relevant inventory data for the partner, such as stock levels, etc.
* **Partner Needs**: Enter any additional needs or comments about the partner.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional notes about the partner.
* **Email**: Enter the partner's contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the partner's contact phone number.
* **Category**: Choose the primary category for the partner.
* **Subcategory**: Choose the subcategory for the partner.
* **Partner Name**: Enter the partner's name.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the partner's contact name.
* **Partner URL**: Enter the partner's URL.
* **Partner Phone**: Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Partner Category**: Choose the primary category for the partner.
* **Partner Subcategory**: Choose the subcategory for the partner.
