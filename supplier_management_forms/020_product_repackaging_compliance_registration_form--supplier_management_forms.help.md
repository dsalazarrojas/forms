# Product Repackaging Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for suppliers to register and manage their product repackaging compliance. It gathers information and declarations on product repackaging, packaging materials, shipping addresses, and certifications to ensure regulatory compliance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field to ensure we have your correct shipping details.
2. Provide details about the product you are repackaging, including any necessary information under **Product Info**, **Product Repackaging Form**, and **Product Repackaging Declaration** fields.
3. Specify the **Packaging Details** and **Packaging Specifications** for the repackaged product.
4. Enter any relevant **Certifications**, **Certification Info**, and **Certification Status** for the product.
5. Confirm the **Product Repackaging Compliance Declaration** and **Product Repackaging Declaration Details** sections to ensure accurate and compliant repackaging.
6. Complete the **Supplier Info** and **Supplier Info Details** fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
7. Enter any additional **Repackaging Info**, **Product Labeling**, and **Product Repackaging Declaration Details 2** as necessary.
8. Finally, review and confirm all information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your correct shipping address to ensure accurate and efficient delivery of packages.
* **Product Info**: Provide detailed information about the product you are repackaging, including product name, weight, dimensions, and any other necessary specifics.
* **Product Repackaging Form**: Fill in the form with product-specific details for repackaging.
* **Product Repackaging Compliance Declaration**: Declare your company's compliance with repackaging regulations and procedures.
* **Packaging Details**: Enter packaging specifics, including material, dimensions, and weight.
* **Packaging Specifications**: Enter packaging specifications, such as printing, material, and other relevant details.
* **Certifications**: Enter any relevant certifications, such as UL, FDA, or CE.
* **Certification Info**: Provide additional certification information, such as certification numbers or dates.
* **Certification Status**: Enter the status of your certifications, such as active or inactive.
* **Supplier Info**: Enter your accurate and up-to-date supplier information, including contact details and business address.
* **Repackaging Info**: Enter any additional repackaging information, such as packaging procedures or instructions.
* **Product Labeling**: Enter product labeling information, such as labeling materials and procedures.
* **Product Repackaging Declaration Details**: Enter detailed declaration information for the repackaging process.
* **Product Repackaging Declaration Status**: Enter the status of your repackaging declaration, such as submitted or pending review.
* **Repackaging Info Details**: Enter any additional repackaging information, such as packaging instructions or procedures.
* **Product Repackaging Status**: Enter the status of your product repackaging, such as completed or in progress.
* **Product Repackaging Declaration Details 2**: Enter detailed declaration information for the repackaging process (second part).
* **Submit**: Review and submit all information for final review and processing.

Please note that some fields may seem redundant or unclear, but they are intended to provide comprehensive and accurate information for regulatory compliance. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
