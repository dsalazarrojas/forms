# Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from new suppliers that are being onboarded into the system. It helps to gather essential details about the supplier, including their contact information and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the required fields indicated by an asterisk (\*). The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions.
2. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly, as it will be used for the supplier onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire** (`supplier_onboarding_questionnaire`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is not a required field to fill out.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the supplier that may be required for their onboarding process.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the supplier.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have obtained the necessary authorization and permissions to access and complete this form.
* The information collected from this form will be used for internal purposes only and is not intended for external sharing or use.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant department or supervisor for assistance.
