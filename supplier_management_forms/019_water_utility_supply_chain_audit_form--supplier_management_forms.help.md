# Water Utility Supply Chain Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the water utility supply chain audit process. The purpose of this form is to help water utility companies evaluate their supply chain, identify potential risks, and take corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and identify the relevant information that needs to be collected.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the available options to select the correct answers.
4. Verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Management Forms** (`supplier_management_forms`, text, not required): This field is used to select the relevant supplier management form.
* **Infrastructure Forms** (`infrastructure_forms`, text, not required): This field is used to select the relevant infrastructure form.
* **Water Utility Supply Chain Audit Form** (`water_utility_supply_chain_audit_form`, text, not required): This is the main form for auditing the water utility supply chain.
* **Vendor Reviews** (`supplier_review`, text, not required): This field is used to provide a review of the vendor or supplier.
* **Risk Tracking** (`risk_tracking`, text, not required): This field is used to track and identify potential risks in the supply chain.
* **Internal Reporting** (`internal_reporting`, text, not required): This field is used for internal reporting and communication.
* **Fast Online Data Collection** (`fast_online_data_collection`, text, not required): This field is used to enable fast online data collection.
* **Vendor Reviews** (`vendor_review`, text, not required): This field is used to provide a review of the vendor or supplier.
* **Risk Rating** (`risk_rating`, text, not required): This field is used to rate the risk level.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, text, not required): This field is used to indicate the risk level.
* **Risk Rating Scale** (`risk_rating_scale`, text, not required): This field is used to select the risk rating scale.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, not required): This field is used to select the audit date.
* **Audit Time** (`audit_time`, time, not required): This field is used to select the audit time.
* **Audit Status** (`audit_status`, select_one, not required): This field is used to select the audit status (Completed, In Progress, Pending).
* **Audit Location** (`audit_location`, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to select the audit location.
* **Water Utility** (`water_utility`, text, not required): This field is used to indicate the water utility.
