# Digital Marketing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about individual preferences and behaviors related to digital marketing, social media, and communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a social media platform from the dropdown list for question 1.
2. Choose one or more options from the list for questions 2, 4, 5, 7, and 8. 
3. Enter a number for question 5 to indicate your age.
4. Select one option for question 6.
5. Answer questions 7 and 8 by choosing one or more options from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the most popular social media platform?** (`title`, `select_one`, required):
  This question asks you to select the social media platform that you use the most. Please choose one of the options: Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.

* **How often do you use the internet?** (`intro2`, `select_multiple`, required):
  This question asks you to select how often you use the internet. Please choose one or more options: Everyday, Several times a week, Several times a month, or Rarely.

* **What is your job role?** (`intro3`, `select_one`, required):
  This question asks you to select your job role. Please choose one of the options: Customer, Manager, Employee, or Student.

* **Which social media platform do you use most frequently?** (`intro4`, `select_one`, required):
  This question asks you to select the social media platform you use most frequently. Please choose one of the options: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or Other. If you do not use social media, please select "Other".

* **What is your age?** (`intro5`, `number`, required):
  This question asks you to enter your age.

* **What is your gender?** (`intro6`, `select_one`, required):
  This question asks you to select your gender. Please choose one of the options: Male, Female, or Other.

* **How often do you use email?** (`intro7`, `select_multiple`, required):
  This question asks you to select how often you use email. Please choose one or more options: Everyday, Several times a week, Several times a month, or Rarely.

* **What is your job role in the company?** (`intro8`, `select_one`, required):
  This question asks you to select your job role in the company. Please choose one of the options: Customer, Manager, Employee, or Student.
