# User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the user's experience with a website, helping us to improve the user experience and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Go to the survey page and fill out the form as per the instructions below.
2.  For each section, read the field-by-field explanation to understand what information is required.
3.  Fill out the form with your answers, making sure to include all the required fields.
4.  Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your current website** (`user_experience_survey_1`, `text`, required: `false`):
    This question is not required, but it's helpful to provide context for your feedback. If you'd like, you can share the URL of the website you are providing feedback on.
*   **Accessibility score** (`website_accessibility_score_1`, `number`, required: `false`):
    On a scale of 1-5, please rate how accessible you find the website. A higher score indicates a better user experience.
*   **How relevant is the content on your website** (`content_relevance_1`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
    Rate how relevant you find the content on the website, with 1 being least relevant and 5 being most relevant.
*   **Presentation score** (`presentation_score_1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    Select all the presentation aspects that you find satisfactory on the website. You can choose from 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
*   **How easy is it to navigate** (`website_navigation_score_1`, `number`, required: `false`):
    On a scale of 1-5, rate how easy it is to navigate the website.
*   **Usability score** (`website_usability_score_1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    Choose the usability aspects you find satisfactory on the website, with 1 being least satisfactory and 5 being most satisfactory.
*   **Comments** (`comments_1`, `note`, required: `false`):
    Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the website.
*   **Contact information** (`contact_information_1`, `select_one`, required: `true`):
    Please select how you would like to be contacted if we need to follow up with you about your feedback. You can choose from email, phone, or mail. 

Note: This guide only provides an explanation for each field. For the actual survey fields, please refer to the original YAML provided.
