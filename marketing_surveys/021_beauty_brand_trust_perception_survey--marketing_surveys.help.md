# Beauty Brand Trust Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the trust and perception of a beauty brand. It's intended for customers or users to share their thoughts and feelings about the brand, including their level of trust, satisfaction, and loyalty. The survey aims to gather valuable insights that can help the brand better understand its customers' perceptions and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the correct category that best describes your customer status with the brand from the list.
2. Indicate how frequently you purchase products from the brand.
3. Choose the time frame for how long you've known the brand.
4. Select your age group.
5-22. Answer each question by selecting one of the provided options. You can select only one option per question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Status** (`Customer Status`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your relationship with the brand. Please choose the category that best describes your status: Long-time Customer, Regular Customer, Occasional Customer, New Customer, or Never Purchased.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`Purchase Frequency`, select_one, required): How often do you buy products from this brand? Select from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Never.
* **How Long Have You Known This Brand** (`How Long Have You Known This Brand`, select_one, required): Please choose the time frame for how long you've been familiar with the brand: Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years.
* **Age Group** (`Age Group`, select_one, required): Select the age group that best describes you: 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, or 56+.
* **Overall Trust in This Brand** (`Overall Trust in This Brand`, select_one, required): Rate your overall trust in the brand: Very Low, Low, Neutral, High, or Very High.
* **Trust in Product Quality** (`Trust in Product Quality`, select_one, required): How much trust do you have in the brand's product quality? Choose: Very Distrustful, Distrustful, Neutral, Trustful, or Very Trustful.
* **Transparency about Ingredients** (`Transparency about Ingredients`, select_one, required): How transparent do you think the brand is about their ingredients? Select: Very Opaque, Opaque, Neutral, Transparent, or Very Transparent.
* **Trust in Business Practices** (`Trust in Business Practices`, select_one, required): Rate your trust in the brand's business practices: Very Low, Low, Neutral, High, or Very High.
* **Brand Authenticity** (`Brand Authenticity`, select_one, required): How authentic do you think the brand is? Choose: Not Authentic, Somewhat Authentic, Neutral, Authentic, or Very Authentic.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`Product Satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with the brand's products? Select: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Value for Money** (`Value for Money`, select_one, required): Rate your value for money perception: Very Poor Value, Poor Value, Neutral, Good Value, or Excellent Value.
* **Customer Service Quality** (`Customer Service Quality`, select_one, required): How would you rate the brand's customer service? Choose: Very Poor, Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent.
* **Return Policy Satisfaction** (`Return Policy Satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with the brand's return policy? Select: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Likelihood to Repurchase** (`Likelihood to Repurchase`, select_one, required): How likely are you to repurchase from this brand? Choose: Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, or Very Likely.
* **Brand Loyalty Level** (`Brand Loyalty Level`, select_one, required): How loyal do you feel towards the brand? Select: Not Loyal, Somewhat Loyal, Neutral, Loyal, or Very Loyal.
* **Would You Recommend to Friends** (`Would You Recommend to Friends`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this brand to friends? Choose: Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, or Definitely Yes.
* **Would You Leave Positive Online Reviews** (`Would You Leave Positive Online Reviews`, select_one, required): Would you leave positive reviews about this brand online? Select: Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, or Definitely Yes.
* **Any Trust Concerns or Issues** (`Any Trust Concerns or Issues`, text, optional): If you have any trust concerns or issues with the brand, please describe them in the space below.
* **Areas for Improvement to Build Trust** (`Areas for Improvement to Build Trust`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions on how the brand can improve to build trust, please type them below.
* **Overall Brand Impression** (`Overall Brand Impression`, select_one, required): Rate your overall impression of the brand: Very Negative, Negative, Neutral, Positive, or Very Positive.
