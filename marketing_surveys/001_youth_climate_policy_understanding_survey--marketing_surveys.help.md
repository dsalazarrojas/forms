# youth_climate_policy_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's understanding and opinions on climate policy, specifically for youth. The form aims to gather data on their awareness, attitudes, knowledge, and potential actions related to climate policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
*   For multiple-choice questions, select only one answer that best reflects your thoughts and experiences.
*   For open-ended questions, provide a clear and concise answer in the space provided.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with a star (\*).
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
