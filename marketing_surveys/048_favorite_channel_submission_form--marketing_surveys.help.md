# Favorite Channel Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' favorite channels, which can be used for marketing and research purposes. Users can choose from a list of channels and provide any additional notes they'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your favorite channels from the "Select Channel" fields. You can choose one or multiple channels depending on the specific field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" fields.
5. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.
