# Powdered Juice Consumption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your powdered juice consumption habits, preferences, and demographic details. Your input will help us better understand market trends and behavior of our target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly to help us gather accurate data.
2. You can choose one or multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. If you have a preferred flavor that is not listed, please specify it in the "Other Flavor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Powdered Juice Consumption
* **Powdered Juice Consumption** (`powdered_juice_consumption_survey`, text, not required): Please describe your powdered juice consumption habits and how often you use it.

### 2. Age Group
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, not required): Select your age group:
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65+

### 3. How often do you purchase powdered juice?
* **How often do you purchase powdered juice?** (`purchase_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Never
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semiannually
	+ Yearly

### 4. Preferred Flavor
* **Preferred Flavor** (`preferred_flavor`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple flavors you prefer:
	+ Original
	+ Mixed
	+ Fruit Punch
	+ Berry
	+ Other (please specify)

### 5. Why do you purchase powdered juice?
* **Why do you purchase powdered juice?** (`purchase_reason`, text, not required): Please provide a brief explanation for your purchase.

### 6. Satisfaction Level
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction level with powdered juice:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### 7. Will you recommend powdered juice to a friend?
* **Will you recommend powdered juice to a friend?** (`recommendation_intent`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 8. Other Flavor (please specify)
* **Other Flavor** (`other_flavor_specify`, text, not required): If you have a preferred flavor not listed, please specify it.

### 9. Other Purpose (please specify)
* **Other Purpose** (`other_purpose_specify`, text, not required): If you purchase powdered juice for a different purpose not listed, please specify it.

### 10. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, not required): Please provide your email address for communication purposes.

### 11. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, not required): Please provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out this form will help us gather more accurate and comprehensive data about your powdered juice consumption habits.
