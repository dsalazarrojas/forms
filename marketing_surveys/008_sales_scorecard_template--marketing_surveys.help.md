# Sales Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Sales Scorecard Template** is a form used by sales teams to collect data on sales performance and customer satisfaction metrics. This data helps businesses evaluate their sales progress, identify areas of improvement, and track customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your business goal in the **Business Goal** field.
2. Select the progress of your sales in the **Sales Scorecard Progress** field.
3. Enter your target sales and current sales figures in the **Target Sales** and **Current Sales** fields, respectively.
4. Rate your customer satisfaction in the **Customer Satisfaction** field, with options ranging from **Excellent** to **Poor**.
5. Choose the customer satisfaction value that best represents your customer's sentiment in the **Customer Satisfaction Value** field, with options ranging from **Excellent** to **Neutral**.
6. Enter your comments on customer service in the **Customer Service** field.
7. Rate your customer service performance in the **Customer Service Rating** field, as a numerical value.
8. Add any additional comments about your sales scorecard in the **Sales Scorecard Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Scorecard Progress**: This field indicates the progress of your sales team towards achieving your target sales. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have made progress or not.
* **Business Goal**: Enter a brief description of your business goal, such as "Increase sales by 20% in Q2" or "Expand into new markets".
* **Target Sales**: Enter your target sales figure for the current period.
* **Current Sales**: Enter your current sales figure for the current period.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your customer satisfaction, with options ranging from **Excellent** to **Poor**.
* **Customer Satisfaction Value**: Choose the customer satisfaction value that best represents your customer's sentiment, with options ranging from **Excellent** to **Neutral**.
* **Customer Service**: Enter your comments about customer service, such as "Responded promptly to customer queries" or "Improved response time by 30%". 
* **Customer Service Rating**: Rate your customer service performance as a numerical value, with higher values indicating better performance.
* **Sales Scorecard Comments**: Add any additional comments about your sales scorecard, such as "Challenges faced" or "Success stories".
