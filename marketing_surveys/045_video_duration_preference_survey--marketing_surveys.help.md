# Video Duration Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on your preferences regarding video length and the platforms you use to watch videos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred video length and the platforms you usually watch videos on. If you have any additional comments or habits you'd like to share, feel free to do so in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Length** (`video_length`, select_one, required: false): Select the length of video you prefer:
	+ Short: Less than 5 minutes
	+ Medium: 5-15 minutes
	+ Long: More than 15 minutes
* **Platforms** (`platforms`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the platforms you usually watch videos on:
	+ YouTube
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ TikTok
	+ Twitter
	+ Other
* **Viewership Habits** (`viewership_habits`, text, required: false): Describe how you usually watch videos:
	+ For example, you can share details about how often you watch videos, whether you usually watch during work hours or during your commute, or any other habits you have.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about videos, feel free to share them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about this survey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about this survey.
* **Date of Last Video Watch** (`date`, date, required: false): If you can remember, please enter the date of your last video watch.
* **Time of Last Video Watch** (`time`, time, required: false): If you can remember, please enter the time of your last video watch.
