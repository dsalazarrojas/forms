# Civic Participation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civic Participation Readiness Survey is a tool designed to measure the willingness and ability of citizens to participate in civic activities such as voting, community involvement, and social advocacy. It assesses the respondent's level of knowledge, motivation, and perceived barriers to participation, providing valuable insights to inform strategies for increasing civic engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Rate your overall readiness to engage in civic activities.
3. Indicate your readiness to participate in voting and community organizations.
4. Evaluate your knowledge of civic matters.
5. Identify areas where you need more knowledge.
6. Select the motivations that drive your participation.
7. List the barriers that prevent you from participating.
8. Specify the types of activities you are most interested in.
9. Suggest next steps for increasing your civic participation.
10. Choose your preferred method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Enter your name.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Age Group**: Select your age group.
- **Overall Readiness to Engage in Civic Activities**: Rate your overall readiness using the scale: Very ready, Ready, Somewhat ready, Not very ready, Not ready at all.
- **Readiness to Participate in Voting**: Rate your readiness to participate in voting using the scale: Very ready, Ready, Somewhat ready, Not very ready, Not ready.
- **Readiness to Participate in Community Organizations**: Rate your readiness to participate in community organizations using the scale: Very ready, Ready, Somewhat ready, Not very ready, Not ready at all.
- **How Would You Rate Your Civic Knowledge**: Rate your civic knowledge using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very poor.
- **Areas Where You Need More Knowledge**: Select all areas where you need more knowledge.
- **What Motivates You to Participate**: Select up to 3 motivations that drive your participation.
- **Current Barriers to Participation**: Select all barriers that prevent you from participating.
- **What Support Would Help You Participate More**: Enter any support that would help you participate more.
- **Types of Activities You Are Most Interested In**: Enter types of activities that interest you most.
- **Next Steps for Increasing Your Civic Participation**: Enter steps you would suggest for increasing your participation.
- **How Would You Like to Be Contacted**: Select your preferred method of contact.
