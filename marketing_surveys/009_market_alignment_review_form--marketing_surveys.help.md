# Market Alignment Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Alignment Review Form is used to evaluate the alignment of a product or service with the market, taking into account various factors such as customer segments, target audience, market growth estimates, financial investments, and competitor insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most applicable option.
3. For text fields, provide brief and clear descriptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Please select all team members involved in the project.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the product or service being reviewed.
* **Market Share** (`market_share`, number, required: false):
	+ Estimate the current market share of the product or service.
* **Competitors** (`competitors`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if there are competitors in the market.
* **Product Fit** (`product_fit`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe how the product or service fits in the market.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false):
	+ List any challenges encountered in the market.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide recommendations for improving market alignment.
* **Competitor Insights** (`competitor_insights`, text, required: false):
	+ Share any insights about competitors.
* **Product-Market Fit** (`product_market_fit`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe how well the product or service meets market needs.
* **Market Landscape** (`market_landscape`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the current market landscape.
* **Product Position** (`product_position`, text, required: false):
	+ Determine the position of the product or service in the market.
* **Market Competitors** (`market_competitors`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all market competitors.
* **Customer Segments** (`customer_segments`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the customer segments targeted by the product or service.
* **Customer Segments Details** (`customer_segments_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the customer segments targeted by the product or service.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Identify the target audience.
* **Target Audience Details** (`target_audience_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the target audience.
* **Market Growth Estimates** (`market_growth_estimates`, number, required: false):
	+ Estimate the market growth potential.
* **Financial Investments** (`financial_investments`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe financial investments made in the market.
* **Customer Loyalty** (`customer_loyalty`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if customers are loyal to the product or service.
