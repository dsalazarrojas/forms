# Nonprofit Ad Sentiment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on the impact of a specific ad on your thoughts, emotions, and actions. Your input will help us refine the ad's content and messaging to better resonate with our target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the overall effect of the ad from the options provided.
2. Choose how the ad makes you feel from the options provided.
3. Indicate if you are more likely to donate as a result of seeing the ad.
4. Select the intensity of the ad's emotional impact.
5. Share any positive thoughts evoked by the ad.
6. Share any negative thoughts evoked by the ad.
7. If you are more likely to donate, enter the amount you are willing to donate.
8. Estimate the time spent looking at the ad.
9. If applicable, select the date you viewed the ad.
10. If applicable, select the time of day you viewed the ad.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the overall effect of this ad?** (`ad_effectiveness`, Select Multiple, Required): This field is where you can choose how the ad is performing overall. Select all the options that apply to your experience with the ad.
* **How does this ad make you feel?** (`emotional_impact`, Select One, Required): This field is used to measure the ad's emotional impact. Choose one option that best describes your emotional response to the ad.
* **Are you more likely to donate as a result of seeing this ad?** (`donation_intent`, Select One, Required): Here, you can choose whether seeing the ad has motivated you to make a donation.
* **How strongly do you feel the ad's emotional impact?** (`emotional_impact_scale`, Select One, Required): This field helps us gauge the intensity of the ad's emotional effect. Choose a number from 1 to 6 that best describes your experience.
* **What positive thoughts does this ad evoke?** (`positive_thoughts`, Text, Optional): Share any positive thoughts, feelings, or emotions the ad has generated in you.
* **What negative thoughts does this ad evoke?** (`negative_thoughts`, Text, Optional): Share any negative thoughts, feelings, or emotions the ad has generated in you.
* **If yes, how much are you willing to donate?** (`donation_amount`, Number, Optional): If you have decided to donate as a result of the ad, enter the amount you are willing to donate.
* **How much time do you spend looking at this ad?** (`time_spent`, Time, Optional): Estimate the time you spent looking at the ad.
* **Date?** (`date`, Date, Optional): If you have viewed the ad, enter the date you last saw it.
* **Time of day?** (`time_of_day`, Time, Optional): If you have viewed the ad, choose the time of day you last saw it.
