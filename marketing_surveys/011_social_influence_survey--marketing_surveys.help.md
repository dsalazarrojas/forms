# Social Influence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your social influence, habits, and behavior to better understand your interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide your age and birth date if you feel comfortable sharing this information.
3. Indicate how you usually socialize (e.g., On social media, In person, Through email, or Other).
4. Select the activities you usually do on social media (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, or Other).
5. Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share in the "Additional Information" field.
6. Answer the question "What do you do most often?" with one of the provided options (e.g., Watching TV, Reading, Playing games, Listening to music, Traveling).
7. Enter the time you have available to dedicate to an activity (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, 5 hours).
8. Select the date of your birthday if you'd like to share it.
9. Answer the question "What do you do?" with one of the provided options (e.g., Watching, Reading, Listening, Playing, Traveling).
10. Select the activities you'd like to do more frequently if you'd like to share this information (e.g., On social media, In person, Through email, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date.
* **How do you usually socialize?** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose how you usually socialize (e.g., On social media, In person, Through email, or Other).
* **What activities do you usually do on social media?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select the activities you usually do on social media (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, or Other).
* **Additional Information** (`note`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **What do you do most often?** (`select_one_2`, select_one, optional): Choose what you do most often (e.g., Watching TV, Reading, Playing games, Listening to music, Traveling).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you have available to dedicate to an activity (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, 5 hours).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your birthday date.
* **What do you do?** (`select_one_3`, select_one, optional): Choose what you do (e.g., Watching, Reading, Listening, Playing, Traveling).
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select what you'd like to do more frequently (e.g., On social media, In person, Through email, or Other).
* **What do you like?** (`select_one_4`, select_one, optional): Choose what you like (e.g., Watching, Reading, Listening, Playing, Traveling).
