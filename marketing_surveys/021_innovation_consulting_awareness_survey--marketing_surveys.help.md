# Innovation Consulting Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your level of awareness and interest in innovation consulting services. Your input will help us gauge the current state of knowledge and needs in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page to navigate through the form.
2. Read each field's explanation carefully before entering your information.
3. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you are unsure about some questions.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: This is an introductory section where you can provide any questions or topics you'd like to explore further.
* **demographic_details**: Please provide your demographic details such as name, job title, and contact information. 
* **innovation_services**: This field allows you to choose from a variety of options related to innovation consulting services. Select all that apply.
* **Text Input**: This field is an open-ended space for you to provide additional information about your interest or experience with innovation consulting services.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about our services. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your input will help us improve our services and provide more relevant information to you.
