# Beverage Consumer Flavor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Consumer Flavor Inquiry Form is designed to gather information about consumer preferences and behavior related to flavored beverages. The form will help you understand your demographics, flavor preferences, and purchase behavior, providing valuable insights for market researchers and product developers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting your age group from the provided options. This will help us understand your stage of life and how it relates to your beverage preferences.
2. Choose your gender identity from the list of options. This will help us understand how your preferences might vary across different gender groups.
3. Enter your primary geographic location, which will help us understand the local market trends.
4. Select how often you consume flavored beverages. This will give us insight into your consumption habits.
5. Choose your favorite flavors from the list, selecting all that apply. Think about the flavors you enjoy most and how they might influence your purchase decisions.
6. Indicate your sweetness preference, selecting from Very sweet, Moderately sweet, Lightly sweet, Not sweet at all, or No preference.
7. Choose your flavor intensity preference, selecting from Very bold and intense, Moderately bold, Subtle and light, or No preference.
8. Select your preferred purchase triggers, choosing all that apply. We want to know what drives your buying decisions.
9. Indicate how important price is in your decision-making process, selecting from Very important, Somewhat important, Neutral, Not very important, or Not important.
10. Let us know how likely you are to try new flavors, selecting from Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very unlikely.
11. Express your interest in limited edition flavors, selecting from Very interested, Interested, Neutral, Not interested, or Not at all interested.
12. Choose the health attributes that matter most to you, selecting all that apply. We're curious about what you value in a beverage.
13. If you have a preferred brand, type it in. We're interested in understanding brand loyalty.
14. Finally, share any additional thoughts or suggestions about flavored beverages. This is your chance to provide insights or feedback.
