# Social Media Brand Insights - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help marketing teams gather insights on social media presence, engagement, and content. It will provide valuable information about the brand's social media strategy, highlighting areas where they can improve and offer new ideas and solutions to enhance their online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the relevant information for each field as it applies to your social media strategy. You can skip fields that are not relevant.
*   For fields that allow multiple selections (like "Improvement Areas"), select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter a detailed description for fields like "Social Presence", "Audience Engagement", "Social Shareable Content", "Current Solutions", and "New Ideas". This will help us understand your specific social media strategy and needs.
*   For numerical fields like "Social Media Budget", simply enter a number that represents the amount allocated for social media marketing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Social Presence**: A brief description of your brand's social media presence, including platforms used and the frequency of posting.
*   **Audience Engagement**: A description of how your audience interacts with your brand on social media, including metrics like likes, comments, and shares.
*   **Social Shareable Content**: A description of the type of content your team creates and shares on social media, including text, images, videos, and live streams.
*   **Improvement Areas**: Select all areas where you feel you need improvement, from social presence to content creation.
*   **Current Solutions**: Describe the solutions you currently have in place for your social media marketing, including tools, strategies, and best practices.
*   **New Ideas**: Share any new ideas you have for improving your social media presence, engagement, and content.
*   **Target Audience**: Describe your target audience, including demographics, interests, and behaviors.
*   **Marketing Channels**: List all the marketing channels you are using or planning to use for your social media marketing.
*   **Social Media Budget**: Enter the total budget allocated for social media marketing, including any dedicated team members, tools, and resources.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field to ensure we get a comprehensive understanding of your social media strategy.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification or additional guidance.
*   This form is designed to help us understand your current social media situation and provide recommendations for improvement.
