# Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at the nail salon.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For select-one and select-multiple questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and detailed answer.
4. Use the provided hints to help you fill out the form.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Information** (`salon_info`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the salon you visited.
* **Visit Frequency** (`how_often_visit`, text, required: false): Please describe how often you visit the salon.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): On a scale of 0-10, please rate your overall satisfaction with the salon.
* **Services Used** (`services`, select_one, required: false): Please select all the services you used during your visit (Manicure, Pedicure, Waxing, Other).
	+ Manicure: A nail shaping or nail polish service.
	+ Pedicure: A pedicure service.
	+ Waxing: Hair waxing or waxing service.
	+ Other: If you selected "Other", please provide a brief description.
* **Loyalty Program** (`loyalty_program`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the loyalty programs that apply to you (Loyalty Program, Referral Program, Neither).
	+ Loyalty Program: A program that rewards repeat customers.
	+ Referral Program: A program that rewards customers for referring friends.
	+ Neither: None of the above.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please provide your phone number.
* **Visit Habits** (`visit_habits`, date, required: false): Please provide the date of your last visit.
* **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, text, required: false): Please describe the reason for your last visit.
* **Services Improvement** (`services_improve`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the areas you would like to see improved (Services, Staff, Environment, Product).
	+ Services: The services provided by the salon.
	+ Staff: The staff friendliness and attitude.
	+ Environment: The salon environment and ambiance.
	+ Product: The products used by the salon.
* **Staff Improvement** (`staff_improve`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the areas you would like to see improved about the staff (Staff Improve, Staff Friendliness).
	+ Staff Improve: The staff's skills and knowledge.
	+ Staff Friendliness: The staff's attitude and friendliness.
* **Client Satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction with the salon.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help the salon understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the space above.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a salon representative for clarification.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
