# Market Research Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect market research information from customers to help us improve our product and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required fields.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Answer honestly and thoroughly, providing detailed feedback about your experience with our product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your name. This is a required field and must be filled out in order to complete the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address. This is an optional field, but including it will help us contact you for further feedback and questions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact-phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering your contact phone number. This is an optional field and is meant for us to follow up with you regarding your feedback.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your company name or affiliation. This is a required field and must be filled out in order to complete the form.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your role or position related to our product. Select "Yes" if you are a customer, "No" if you are not.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about the departments involved in our product. Select all that apply from the options listed.
* **Product Name** (`product-name`, text, required): This field is where you can enter the name of our product you are assessing.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): This field is for entering additional feedback or comments about your experience with our product.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall-rating`, number, required): This field is where you can rate your overall experience with our product, on a scale of 0-5.
* **Overall Rating 2** (`overall-rating-2`, select_one, optional): This field is similar to the "Overall Rating" field, but offers more specific options.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): This field is where you can enter the duration of your experience with our product.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field is where you can select the date of your experience with our product.
* **Time Zone** (`time-zone`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about your time zone. Select all that apply from the options listed.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): This field is similar to "Time Zone", but offers more specific options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): This field is for entering detailed comments about your experience with our product.
* **Comments 2** (`comments-2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting multiple options from the list of comments.
* **Comments 3** (`comments-3`, text, required): This field is for entering additional comments about your experience with our product.
* **Overall Rating 3** (`overall-rating-3`, number, required): This field is for rating your overall experience with our product, on a scale of 0-5.
* **Overall Rating 4** (`overall-rating-4`, select_one, optional): This field is similar to "Overall Rating 3", but offers more specific options.
* **Overall Rating 5** (`overall-rating-5`, select_one, optional): This field is similar to "Overall Rating 4", but offers more specific options.
* **Rating 6** (`rating-6`, number, required): This field is for rating a specific aspect of our product.
* **Rating 7** (`rating-7`, select_one, optional): This field is for rating a specific aspect of our product.
* **Overall Rating 8** (`overall-rating-8`, select_one, optional): This field is for overall product rating.
* **Overall Rating 9** (`overall-rating-9`, select_one, optional): This field is similar to "Overall Rating 8", but offers more specific options.
