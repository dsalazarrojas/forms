# labour_market_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The labour_market_survey form is used to collect information about a respondent's experience and perception of their current or expected job situation. It is used to understand the labour market and job satisfaction among various aspects of one's work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the most accurate and relevant information about your current or expected job situation.
2. Be honest and thorough in answering each question.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, such as select_one and select_multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **labour_market_survey_page** (Text, Optional): Please enter a brief description of yourself or your job situation. 
* **demographic_info** (Text, Optional): Provide your demographic information, including your age, occupation, etc.
* **age** (Number, Optional): Enter your age.
* **occupation** (Text, Optional): Enter your current or expected occupation.
* **employment_status** (Select One, Optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **industry** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the industry you work in or are expecting to work in.
* **job_satisfaction** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with your job with regards to:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Neutral
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **job_satisfaction_reason** (Text, Optional): Please provide a reason for your answer to job_satisfaction.
* **work_experience** (Time, Optional): Enter the amount of time you have spent working in your current or expected industry.
* **expected_job_change** (Date, Optional): If you are expecting to change your job, enter the expected date of the job change.
* **education** (Text, Optional): Enter your highest level of education completed.
* **work_hours** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **job_search_status** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you are searching for a new job.
* **job_search_method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how you are searching for a new job from the options provided.
* **job_offers** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of job offers you have received recently.
* **industry_satisfaction** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with your industry with regards to:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Neutral
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **industry_satisfaction_reason** (Text, Optional): Please provide a reason for your answer to industry_satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_2**, **job_satisfaction_3**, **job_satisfaction_4**, **job_satisfaction_5** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with different aspects of your job.
* **job_satisfaction_2_reason**, **job_satisfaction_3_reason**, **job_satisfaction_4_reason**, **job_satisfaction_5_reason** (Text, Optional): Please provide a reason for your answer to job_satisfaction_2, job_satisfaction_3, job_satisfaction_4, job_satisfaction_5 respectively.
