# marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. It gathers information on the campaign's performance, customer engagement, and budget utilization. The data collected will help stakeholders identify areas of improvement and optimize future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields.
- For optional fields, provide relevant information if possible.
- Use the options provided for select and select multiple fields.
- For date and time fields, follow the format MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM respectively.
- Use notes to provide additional context or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **page_1** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the marketing campaign being evaluated.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_1** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_1`, select_one, required/optional): Select the primary goal of the campaign. Please choose one of the options provided.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_1_answer** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_1_answer`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answer options for the previous question.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_2** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_2`, number, required/optional): Enter the actual sales revenue generated by the campaign.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_5** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_5`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the campaign.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_6** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_6`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the campaign.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_7** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_7`, time, required/optional): Enter the duration of the campaign.
- **marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_8** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness_survey_question_8`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations about the campaign.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If unsure about any field, please contact the relevant stakeholders or refer to the campaign documentation.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you are unable to complete this form, please notify the relevant authorities and seek assistance.
