# Economic Indicators Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your economic indicators, allowing us to better understand your current and future market conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all relevant options for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting.
