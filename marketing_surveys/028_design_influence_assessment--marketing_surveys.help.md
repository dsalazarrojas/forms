# Design Influence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Influence Assessment is a user feedback form designed to gather information on how the design elements of a product or service impact the user's perception and decision-making process.
## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers whenever possible.
- You can submit this form as many times as you like, but please note that duplicate submissions may be discarded to maintain data quality.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page_1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction to the product or service and explain how you encountered it.
* **Design Impact** (`page_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe the impact of the design on the perception of the product or service. For example, did the design elements make the product more or less appealing to you?
* **Decision Influence** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options to indicate whether the design influenced your decision to use or purchase the product or service: Yes, No, or Other (please specify).
* **Target Audience** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply to indicate the target audience for the product or service: Yes, No, or Other (please specify).
* **Rating** (`page_5`, `number`, required: false): Rate the design of the product or service on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Time Spent** (`page_6`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the amount of time you spent interacting with the product or service.
* **Email Address** (`page_7`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you or send you updates on the product or service.
* **Phone Number** (`page_8`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number (optional).
## Tips
- Please keep your responses concise and focused on the actual design elements.
- Avoid making assumptions about the intended use of the form.
- If unsure about a particular question, feel free to ask!
