# Cruelty Free Brand Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the effectiveness of our marketing strategies regarding cruelty-free branding. It aims to gather information about your perception of cruelty-free brands, awareness of our marketing efforts, and your opinions on our product development and consumer engagement strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. If you have comments or additional information to share, use the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Surveys** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): This is a brief text field for any additional comments or thoughts you have about our marketing surveys.
* **Consumer Awareness** (`consumer_awareness`, select_multiple, optional): 
  + Please select one or more options that apply to you. 
  + "Yes" if you are aware of our cruelty-free branding efforts 
  + "No" if you are not aware of our efforts 
* **Perception Of Cruelty Free Brands** (`perception_of_cruelty_free_brands`, select_one, optional): 
  + Select the option that best describes your perception of cruelty-free brands. 
  + "Yes" if you believe cruelty-free brands are important 
  + "No" if you do not believe they are important 
* **Marketing Strategies** (`marketing_strategies`, text, optional): This is a brief text field for any thoughts or comments on our marketing strategies. 
* **Product Development** (`product_development`, text, optional): This is a brief text field for any thoughts or comments on our product development. 
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your company (if applicable). 
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding your responses, please enter your email address. 
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding your responses, please enter your phone number. 
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This is a field for internal use only, please leave blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about our survey.
