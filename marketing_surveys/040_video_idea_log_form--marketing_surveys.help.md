# Video Idea Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Idea Log Form is designed to collect and record video ideas for marketing or development purposes. This form helps to categorize and analyze video ideas for better organization and project planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Video Idea Log Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the video idea in the **Video Name** field.
2. Select the video type (e.g., Action, Comedy, Drama, or Reality) in the **Video Type** field. Multiple selections are allowed.
3. Enter the expected duration of the video in the **Video Length** field.
4. Select the release date of the video in the **Release Date** field.
5. Choose the intended show type (e.g., TV, Film, or Digital) in the **Show** field.
6. Briefly describe the video's story in the **Story** field.
7. Add relevant tags to further categorize the video idea in the **Tags** field.
8. Optionally, leave a note with any additional information about the video idea in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Name** (`video_idea_log_form_title`, text, required):
	+ This field collects the title or name of the video idea.
	+ Enter a brief and descriptive name for the video.
* **Video Type** (`video_idea_log_form_video_type`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more video types (e.g., Action, Comedy, Drama, or Reality).
	+ Multiple selections are allowed, enabling you to categorize the video idea in multiple genres.
* **Video Length** (`video_idea_log_form_video_length`, number, required):
	+ This field collects the expected length of the video.
	+ Enter the video's duration in minutes.
* **Release Date** (`video_idea_log_form_release_date`, date, required):
	+ This field is used to select the release date of the video.
	+ Choose a date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Show** (`video_idea_log_form_show`, select_one, required):
	+ This field is used to select the intended show type (e.g., TV, Film, or Digital).
	+ Choose the correct show type for the video.
* **Story** (`video_idea_log_form_story`, text, required):
	+ This field collects a brief description of the video's story.
	+ Provide a concise and descriptive summary of the video's plot.
* **Tags** (`video_idea_log_form_tags`, text, optional):
	+ This field allows you to add relevant tags to further categorize the video idea.
	+ Enter tags separated by commas.
* **Note** (`video_idea_log_form_note`, note, optional):
	+ This field is used to leave a note with any additional information about the video idea.
	+ Enter any relevant details or observations about the video.
