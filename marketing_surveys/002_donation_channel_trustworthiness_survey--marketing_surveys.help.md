# Donation Channel Trustworthiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Channel Trustworthiness Survey is designed to collect information about your experience with different donation channels. The survey will ask you to rate your trust in various donation channels and provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and fill out the form accurately.
2. Answer the questions based on your experience with the donation channels.
3. Fill out the contact information sections with your details.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channel Name (Required)**: Please enter the name of the donation channel you are rating.
* **Contact Information (Required)**: Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **Email (Required)**: Please enter your email address.
* **5 Stars Goals (Required)**: Please select the goals you achieved with the donation channel out of 5 stars.
	+ 1 star: Did not achieve any goals.
	+ 2-5 stars: Achieved some goals.
* **4 Stars Goals (Required)**: Please select the goals you achieved with the donation channel out of 4 stars.
	+ 1 star: Did not achieve any goals.
	+ 2-4 stars: Achieved some goals.
* **3 Stars Goals (Required)**: Please select the goals you achieved with the donation channel out of 3 stars.
	+ 1 star: Did not achieve any goals.
	+ 2 stars: Achieved some goals.
* **2 Stars Goals (Required)**: Please select the goals you achieved with the donation channel out of 2 stars.
	+ 1 star: Did not achieve any goals.
	+ 2 stars: Achieved some goals.
* **1 Star Goals (Required)**: Please select the goals you achieved with the donation channel out of 1 star.
	+ 1 star: Did not achieve any goals.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Please provide any additional comments about your experience with the donation channel.
* **Name (Required)**: Please enter your name.
* **Company (Required)**: Please enter your company or organization name.
* **Department (Required)**: Please enter your department name.
* **Job Title (Required)**: Please enter your job title.
* **Other Job Title (Optional)**: If you have a different job title, please enter it.
* **Job Title Department (Optional)**: If you have a different job title department, please enter it.
* **Contact Information 1-20 (Optional)**: Please enter any additional contact information you would like to provide.
