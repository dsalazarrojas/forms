# Nail Polish Finish Trend Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Polish Finish Trend Survey aims to gather information about users' preferences, habits, and trends related to nail polish finish, helping us to understand current and future trends.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each one honestly.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" according to your actual preferences and habits.
3. Provide your favorite brands in the "Favorite Brands" field.
4. If you have any other favorite brands that are not listed, enter them in the "Other Brands" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly to the "Survey Questions" fields, which cover various finish preferences and trends.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, `note`, not required): This is just an introduction to the survey.
* **Favorite Finish(es)** (`finish_preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer "Yes" or "No" according to your top finish preferences.
* **Habit(s)** (`usage_habits`, `select_one`, not required): Answer "Yes" or "No" according to your usage habits.
* **Favorite Brand(s)** (`favorite_brands`, `select_one`, not required): Answer your favorite brands.
* **Other Brand(s)** (`other_brands`, `text`, not required): If you have any other favorite brands that are not listed, enter them here.
* **Favorite Finish Preference(s)** (`finish_preferences_other`, `text`, not required): If you have any other favorite finish preferences that are not listed, enter them here.
* **Survey Question(s)** (`survey_questions_1` to `survey_questions_3`, `select_multiple`, not required): Answer "Yes" or "No" according to your actual preferences and trends for these questions.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
