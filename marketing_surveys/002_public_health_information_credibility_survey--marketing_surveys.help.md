# Public Health Information Credibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on the credibility of public health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name, last name, and email fields.
2. Enter the date and time of publication.
3. Select the credibility scale and source from the options provided.
4. Provide additional details about the credibility source, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date Published** (`date_published`, date, optional): Enter the date the information was published.
- **Time Published** (`time_published`, time, optional): Enter the time the information was published.
- **Source Name** (`source_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the source.
- **Credibility Scale** (`credibility_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the credibility scale from the provided options.
- **Credibility Source** (`credibility_source`, text, optional): Enter additional details about the source of credibility.
- **Credibility Scale Source** (`credibility_scale_source`, text, optional): Enter additional details about the credibility scale source, if needed.

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly. If you're unsure about a field, refer to the field label for further clarification.
