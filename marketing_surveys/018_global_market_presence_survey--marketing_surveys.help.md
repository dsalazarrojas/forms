# Global Market Presence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your company's presence in the market. It will help us understand your current situation, challenges, and expectations. Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this survey.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately based on your current company's situation.
2. Select the options that best fit your company's characteristics.
3. If a question does not apply to your company, feel free to skip it or provide a brief explanation in the "Additional notes" field.
4. Make sure to fill in all required information (marked as required).
