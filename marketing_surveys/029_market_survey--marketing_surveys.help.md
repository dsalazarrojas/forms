# Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Survey form is a tool used to collect information about market surveys. It helps market surveyors gather data about market surveys for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, denoted by * (asterisk) next to their labels.
2. For the "Market Survey Type" field, select the type of market survey you are conducting (written, oral, or online).
3. For the "Market Survey Target Market" field, select all relevant markets for your survey (local, national, or international).
4. Enter the number of market survey participants in the "Market Survey Participation" field.
5. Enter the deadline for the market survey in the "Market Survey Deadline" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional information in the "Market Survey Participation Note" and "Market Survey Deadline Note" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market Survey Question** (1/2): This field asks for the question of the market survey. Enter the question you are asking participants.
* **Market Survey Question Answer** (2/2): Enter the answer to the question.
* **Market Survey Question Answer Note** (3/2): If necessary, add any additional information about the answer.
* **Market Survey Type** (4/2): Select the type of market survey you are conducting (written, oral, or online).
* **Market Survey Target Market** (5/2): Select all relevant markets for your survey (local, national, or international).
* **Market Survey Participation** (6/2): Enter the number of market survey participants.
* **Market Survey Participation Note** (7/2): If necessary, add any additional information about market survey participation.
* **Market Survey Deadline** (8/2): Enter the deadline for the market survey.
* **Market Survey Deadline Note** (9/2): If necessary, add any additional information about the market survey deadline.
* **Market Survey Phone Number** (11/2): Enter the phone number to contact market survey participants.
* **Market Survey Email** (12/2): Enter the email address to contact market survey participants.
* **Market Survey Website** (13/2): Enter the website associated with the market survey.
