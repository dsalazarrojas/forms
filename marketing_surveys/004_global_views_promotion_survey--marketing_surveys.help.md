# global_views_promotion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users about global views and promotions. It aims to understand their perceptions, impressions, and suggestions for future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form and the questions that will be asked.
2. Answer the questions on the following pages:
	* Which global campaigns have you seen or heard about? (Select all that apply)
	* Which region(s) are you from? (Select all that apply)
	* Provide your honest impression of the global campaign.
	* Share any ideas you have to improve future global campaigns.
3. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you (if desired).
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Click the "Submit" button when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Global Campaigns**: This question asks about the global campaigns you have seen or heard about. Select all that apply from the list provided. For example, if you've seen both Global Campaign 1 and Global Campaign 2, select both.
* **Regional Interest**: This question asks about the region(s) you are from. Select all that apply from the list provided.
* **Feedback**: This question asks for your honest impression of the global campaign. Please be as specific as possible in your response.
* **Improvement Ideas**: This question asks if you have any ideas to improve future global campaigns. Share your thoughts in as much detail as possible.
* **Email**: This field asks for your email address. We may use this to contact you about your feedback if needed.
* **Phone**: This field asks for your phone number, if you're willing to share it with us.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to complete the form. Make sure to review all your answers before submitting.
* **Review**: This question asks for your review of the campaign. Select from the options provided: Strongly Agree, Neutral, or Disagree.
* **Date**: This field asks for the date you're submitting your feedback. Please enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
