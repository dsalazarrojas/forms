# SEO Content Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Content Details Form is a tool used to gather relevant information about website content for search engine optimization (SEO) purposes. This form helps ensure that your content is properly indexed and displayed in search results.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content you are submitting.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate information about your content.
3. Review and edit your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the website the content belongs to.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of content you are submitting (e.g., title, heading, product description, etc.).
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the content.
* **Meta Description** (`meta_description`, text, required/optional): Write a short description of the content (less than 155 characters).
* **Keywords** (`keywords`, text, required/optional): List relevant keywords related to the content.
* **Author** (`author`, text, required/optional): Enter the author's name or username.
* **Content Length** (`content_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the content in words or characters.
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, number, required/optional): Rate the quality of the content on a scale of 1-10.
* **Meta Image URL** (`meta_image_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the meta image associated with the content.
* **Meta Description URL** (`meta_description_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the meta description associated with the content.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required): Choose which target audiences the content is intended for.
* **Language** (`language`, text, required/optional): Enter the language of the content.
* **Published Date** (`published_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of publication.
* **Meta Author** (`meta_author`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta author's name or username.
* **Meta Publisher** (`meta_publisher`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta publisher's name or organization.
* **Meta License** (`meta_license`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta license or copyright information.
* **Meta Rights** (`meta_rights`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta rights information.
* **Meta Version** (`meta_version`, text, required/optional): Enter the version of the meta content.
* **Meta Modified** (`meta_modified`, time, required/optional): Enter the date and time the content was last modified.
* **Meta Comment** (`meta_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the content.
* **Meta Author Email** (`meta_author_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the meta author's email.
* **Meta Publisher Email** (`meta_publisher_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the meta publisher's email.
* **Meta Phone** (`meta_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta publisher's phone number.
* **Meta Fax** (`meta_fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the meta publisher's fax number.
* **Meta Notes** (`meta_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional meta notes.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use consistent formatting and grammar for all text fields.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.
