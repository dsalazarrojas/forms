# Personalization Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the readiness of your company or organization for personalization. Personalization involves tailoring experiences, products, or services to individual customers' needs and preferences. By completing this survey, you will help us assess your organization's current state and provide insights for potential improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and input prompt to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Provide accurate and truthful answers to each question.
3. Fill out each field according to the type and instructions provided.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section 1 (Text)**: This field is a text input where you can provide any additional information or comments about your current personalization efforts.
* **Form Section 2 (Number)**: Enter a numerical value that represents your current level of personalization readiness (e.g., 1-10).
* **Form Section 3 (Number)**: Similar to Form Section 2, enter a numerical value that indicates your company's current level of customer understanding (e.g., 1-10).
* **Form Section 4 (Yes/No)**: Choose "Yes" if your company uses or plans to use personalization, and "No" otherwise.
* **Form Section 5 (Yes/No/Maybe)**: Indicate your company's stance on using personalization. Choose "Yes" if you are certain about using personalization, "No" if you are not planning to use it, and "Maybe" if you are still deciding.
* **Form Section 6 (Date)**: Enter the date when you plan to start personalization efforts or when your current personalization efforts began.
* **Form Section 7 (Time)**: Enter the time of day when you are most likely to be available to discuss personalization or when your personalization efforts usually take place.
* **Form Section 8 (Note)**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about your personalization efforts or plans. Use this space to provide any context or explanations.
* **Form Section 9 (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide further information.
* **Form Section 10 (Text)**: This text input is for any additional feedback or suggestions you might have regarding personalization.
* **Form Section 11 (Text)**: Enter any specific challenges or obstacles your company faces in implementing personalization.
* **Form Section 12 (Text)**: Provide any plans or initiatives you have for future personalization efforts.

Note: Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when completing this form.
