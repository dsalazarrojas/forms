# Product Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Testing Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from customers on their experience with our product testing process. This information will help us understand customer needs and expectations, which will help us improve our product and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best apply to your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide more information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Testing Survey** (1, product_testing_survey, required: false): This is the title of the form. Please do not make any changes to this field.
* **Customer Feedback** (2, product_testing_survey_customer_feedback, required: false): This field is for you to provide any comments or feedback you have about our product testing process.
* **Testing Process** (3, product_testing_survey_testing_process, required: false): This is a text field for you to describe your experience with our testing process.
* **Marketing Objectives** (4, product_testing_survey_marketing_objectives, required: false): Select one of the options below that best describes why you participated in our product testing:
	+ Option 1: To get a better understanding of our product.
	+ Option 2: To provide feedback on our product.
	+ Option 3: To participate in a testing process for other reasons.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (5, product_testing_survey_customer_satisfaction, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat satisfied.
	+ Option 3: Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied.
* **Testing Results** (6, product_testing_survey_testing_results, required: false): If you have any results or outcomes from your testing experience, please describe them here.
* **Product Features** (7, product_testing_survey_product_features, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions on the features of our product, please provide them here.
* **Product Description** (8, product_testing_survey_product_description, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions on the description of our product, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide more information.
* Please review your answers before submitting the form.
