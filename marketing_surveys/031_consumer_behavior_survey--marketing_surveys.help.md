# Consumer Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about consumers' behavior and preferences regarding a specific product category. The survey aims to understand how consumers interact with products, their satisfaction levels, and their likelihood to recommend the product to others. The information collected will help us better understand our target audience and make informed decisions for future product development and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Email Address (if applicable) on **Page 1**.
2. Select your Age Group on **Page 2**.
3. Choose the Product Category you are interested in on **Page 3**.
4. Indicate your Purchase Frequency and Last Purchase Date (if applicable) on **Page 4**.
5. Select the amount you typically spend on the product on **Page 5**.
6. Choose where you usually purchase the product on **Page 6**.
7. Indicate how you found out about the product on **Page 7**.
8. Rate your satisfaction level with the product on **Page 8**.
9. Evaluate the product's quality and value on **Page 9**.
10. Express your likelihood to recommend the product to others on **Page 10**.
11. Consider your intention to repurchase the product on **Page 11**.
12. Optionally, provide comments on the product features you like or dislike on **Page 12**.
13. Indicate if you have tried competitor products on **Page 13**.
14. Compare the product with competitors on **Page 14**.
15. Provide any additional comments or feedback on **Page 15**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`respondent_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (if applicable).
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range from the provided options.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your city and state for geographical context.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Select the product category we are enquiring about.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you purchase the product.
* **Last Purchase Date** (`last_purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last purchase (if applicable).
* **Amount Spent** (`amount_spent`, select_one, required): Select the average amount you typically spend on the product.
* **Where Do You Purchase** (`purchase_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all locations where you usually purchase the product.
* **Brand Awareness** (`brand_awareness`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how you found out about the product.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the product.
* **Product Quality Rating** (`product_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the product's quality.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the product's value.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate your likelihood to recommend the product to others.
* **Intention to Repurchase** (`repurchase_intention`, select_one, required): Consider your intention to repurchase the product.
* **Features You Like** (`product_features_liked`, text, optional): Provide comments on the product features you like.
* **Features You Dislike** (`product_features_disliked`, text, optional): Comment on the product features you dislike.
* **Have You Tried Competitor Products** (`competitor_products`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have tried competitor products.
* **How Does This Compare** (`comparison_rating`, select_one, optional): Compare the product with competitors.
* **Packaging Feedback** (`packaging_feedback`, text, optional): Provide comments on the product's packaging.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional feedback or comments.
