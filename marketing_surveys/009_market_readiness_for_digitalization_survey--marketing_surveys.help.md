# Market Readiness For Digitalization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Readiness for Digitalization Survey is designed to assess an organization's readiness to adopt digitalization. This form aims to gather information about your organization's size, type, location, and employee count, as well as the titles of the executives in your organization. The data collected will help us better understand your organization's digitalization readiness and provide insights to support your digital transformation journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
3. Select the size of your organization from the "Organization Size" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the type of your organization from the "Organization Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your organization's location in the "Organization Location" field.
6. Enter the number of employees in your organization in the "Number of Employees" field or select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
7. For the executive titles, select the correct title that best describes your executive team from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (text): Enter your organization's name here.
* **Organization Size** (select_one): Select the size of your organization:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Organization Type** (select_one): Select the type of your organization:
	+ Public
	+ Private
	+ Non-Profit
* **Organization Location** (text): Enter your organization's location here.
* **Number of Employees** (number): Enter the number of employees in your organization. If you have a large number of employees, you can select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
* **Executive Title 1** (text): Select the first executive title from the list.
* **Executive Title 2** (text): Select the second executive title from the list.
