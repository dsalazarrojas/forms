# Makeup Color Psychology Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your preferences and habits related to makeup. The goal is to understand how you perceive and interact with different colors in the context of makeup.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer for you.
2. Answer each question honestly, to the best of your ability.
3. For questions with multiple select options, you can choose one or more of the options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your favorite makeup shades?** (`favorite_makeup_colors`, select_multiple): This question asks about the colors of makeup that you enjoy using or are drawn to. Choose all the options that fit your preferences.
* **What color do you prefer?** (`makeup_color_preference`, select_one): This question is about the specific color you prefer for your makeup look. Choose the color that best represents your taste.
* **How often do you apply makeup?** (`makeup_frequency_use`, select_one): This question is about how frequently you use makeup in your daily life. Choose the option that matches your habits.
* **How do you typically apply makeup?** (`makeup_application_method`, select_one): This question is about the specific makeup products you use in your routine. Choose the option that best describes your process.
* **What order do you apply your makeup in?** (`makeup_application_order`, select_one): This question is about the sequence in which you apply your makeup products. Choose the option that reflects your typical order.
* **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, select_one): This question is about your age for demographic purposes. Choose the option that matches your age.
* **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!** (`survey_end`, text): This is a thank-you message at the end of the survey, indicating your participation is completed.
