# Communication Style Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Communication Style Survey is designed to help you understand your communication style, identify your strengths and weaknesses, and provide you with insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select a response for each question by choosing from the provided options.
2. For select_one questions, choose only one response from the list.
3. For select_multiple questions, choose all the answers that apply.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
5. Fill out the form completely and honestly, as it will help you gain a more accurate understanding of your communication style.
6. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it for analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction** (`survey_introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey, which explains its purpose and the benefits of taking it.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name, if you wish to be identified. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for further information. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required: true): Choose your age group from the provided options.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your current occupation or role.
* **Communication Style Assessment** (`communication_style_assessment`, note, required: false): This section is not editable, as it is a description of the assessment.
* **Directness Level** (`directness_level`, select_one, required: true): Choose how direct you are in communication, from the provided options.
* **Emotional Expression** (`emotional_expression`, select_one, required: true): Select how you express emotions, from the provided options.
* **Listening Style** (`listening_style`, select_one, required: true): Choose your listening style, from the provided options.
* **Conflict Approach** (`conflict_approach`, select_one, required: true): Select how you handle conflicts, from the provided options.
* **Feedback Style** (`feedback_style`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you prefer to give feedback, from the provided options.
* **Feedback Reception** (`feedback_reception`, select_one, required: true): Select how you prefer to receive feedback, from the provided options.
* **Communication Medium** (`communication_medium`, note, required: false): This section is not editable, as it is a description of the medium.
* **Preferred Medium** (`preferred_medium`, select_one, required: true): Choose your preferred communication medium, from the provided options.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, required: true): Select how quickly you respond, from the provided options.
* **Message Length** (`message_length`, select_one, required: true): Choose your typical message length, from the provided options.
* **Social Preferences** (`social_preferences`, note, required: false): This section is not editable, as it is a description of the preferences.
* **Group Communication Comfort** (`group_comfort`, select_one, required: true): Select how comfortable you are in group settings, from the provided options.
* **Small Talk** (`small_talk`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you feel about small talk, from the provided options.
* **Networking** (`networking`, select_one, required: true): Select how you approach networking, from the provided options.
* **Communication Strengths** (`communication_strengths`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the communication strengths that apply to you.
* **Communication Challenges** (`communication_challenges`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the communication challenges that apply to you.
* **Improvement Goals** (`improvement_goals`, text, required: false): Enter any specific improvement goals you have for your communication style.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or thoughts about your communication style.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date you completed this survey.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Select only one response for select_one questions.
* Choose all applicable answers for select_multiple questions.
* Fill out the form completely and honestly, as it will help you gain a more accurate understanding of your communication style.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification.
