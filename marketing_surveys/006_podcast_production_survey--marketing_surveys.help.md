# Podcast Production Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help our production team understand the needs and requirements of your podcast show in order to provide tailored services. This information will enable us to better assist you with content development, editing, distribution, and other related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide general information about your podcast show in the "Show Details" section.
2.  Specify whether your podcast targets a specific audience or demographic in the "Show Target Audience" section.
3.  Select the status of your podcast in the "Show Status" section.
4.  Choose how you would like to be contacted in the "Contact Info" section.
5.  Identify the services you are seeking from our team in the "Service Needs" section.
6.  Describe your podcast in 100 words or less in the "Show Description" section.
7.  Estimate the size of your target audience in the "Target Audience Size" section.
8.  Specify the frequency of your podcast episodes in the "Episodes Per Month" section.
9.  Assess your current engagement strategy in the "Engagement Strategy" section.
10. Choose the production level required for your podcast in the "Production Level" section.
11.  Specify the platform you use for recording in the "Recording Platform" section.
12. Specify the desired outcome or goal of your podcast in the "Desired Outcome" section.
13. Choose the budget for your podcast production in the "Show Budget" section.
14.  Specify your preferred method of contact in the "Contact Method" section.
15.  Choose your preferred method of communication in the "Preferred Communication" section.
16. Add any additional notes about your podcast in the "Additional Notes" section.
17.  Specify the genre of your podcast in the "Show Genre" section.
18.  Specify the style of your podcast in the "Show Style" section.
19.  Choose the niche or topic of your podcast in the "Show Niche" section.
20.  Specify the format of your podcast in the "Show Format" section.
21.  Re-specify the status of your podcast in the "Show Status" section (same as above).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Show Details**: Provide general information about your podcast show. This can include the title, format, and duration.
*   **Show Target Audience**: Specify whether your podcast targets a specific audience or demographic.
*   **Show Status**: Choose the status of your podcast: Active or Inactive.
*   **Contact Info**: Choose how you would like to be contacted: Yes, No, or specify a preferred method.
*   **Service Needs**: Identify the services you are seeking from our team: Yes, No, or specify the services required.
*   **Show Description**: Describe your podcast in 100 words or less.
*   **Target Audience Size**: Estimate the size of your target audience.
*   **Episodes Per Month**: Specify the frequency of your podcast episodes.
*   **Engagement Strategy**: Assess your current engagement strategy: Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Production Level**: Choose the production level required for your podcast: High, Low.
*   **Recording Platform**: Specify the platform you use for recording.
*   **Desired Outcome**: Specify the desired outcome or goal of your podcast: Yes, No, or specify the outcome.
*   **Show Budget**: Choose the budget for your podcast production.
*   **Contact Method**: Specify your preferred method of contact: Yes, No, or specify the method.
*   **Preferred Communication**: Choose how you prefer to be contacted: Yes, No, or specify the communication method.
*   **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information about your podcast.
*   **Show Genre**: Specify the genre of your podcast.
*   **Show Style**: Specify the style of your podcast.
*   **Show Niche**: Choose the niche or topic of your podcast: Yes, No, or specify the topic.
*   **Show Format**: Specify the format of your podcast.
*   **Show Status**: Re-specify the status of your podcast.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure that our team can best understand your needs.
*   If you are unsure about any of the options or questions, please feel free to reach out to our team for guidance.
*   The information collected in this form will only be used to better assist you with your podcast production needs.
