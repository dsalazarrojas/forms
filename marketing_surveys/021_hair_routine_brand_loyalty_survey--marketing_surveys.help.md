# Hair Routine Brand Loyalty Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Routine Brand Loyalty Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences regarding our hair care brand. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. For the select one question, choose only one of the provided options.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
5. Click submit to send your survey responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1 (Optional)** 
  (Page 1, Note, Not required): This page is a general introduction to the survey.
* **Page 2 (Questions, Select One, Required)** 
  (Select one of the options provided):
  - Choose the option that best reflects your current hair care routine.
* **Page 3 (Contact Information, Email, Not required)** 
  (Email, Not required):
  - Enter the email address you use most frequently for communication.
* **Page 4 (Phone, Text, Not required)** 
  (Enter your phone number): 
  - Provide your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question truthfully and based on your actual experiences.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the survey, please contact us.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your participation!
