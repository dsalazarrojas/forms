# customer_profile_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "customer_profile_survey" form is designed to collect customer profile information, which will help us tailor our marketing efforts to better serve your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.**
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, but remember that some fields are optional.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**:
  This field is used to collect your personal identifier.
  Please enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name (last_name)**:
  This field collects your surname or last name.
  Enter your full last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email (email)**:
  This is where you enter your primary email address.
  We use this to communicate with you about our marketing efforts and promotions.
* **Company (company)**:
  This field is for the name of your current or past workplace.
  Enter the name of your company if you have one.
* **Interests (interests)**:
  This is where you select the activities, hobbies, or interests that you enjoy.
  Choose all the options that apply to you.
  Select from:
  - Sports
  - Travel
  - Food
  - Music
  - Movies
  - Books
  - Video Games
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**:
  This field is for your contact phone number.
  Enter your phone number for us to reach you for further communication or promotions.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and honestly. This will help us better serve you with targeted marketing efforts. If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact our support team for further assistance.
