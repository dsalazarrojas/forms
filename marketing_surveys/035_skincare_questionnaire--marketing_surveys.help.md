# Skincare Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skincare habits and preferences to help us better understand your needs and tailor our products and services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you are answering the questions accurately and completely.
2. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. Select the options that best match your skin type, concerns, and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all skin types that apply to you, such as Dry Skin, Oily Skin, Combination Skin, Sensitive Skin, or Normal Skin.
* **Skincare Products** (`skincare_products`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of skincare product you use, such as Moisturizer, Serum, Toner, Exfoliator, or Cleanser.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all skin concerns you are experiencing, such as Acne, Fine Lines, Hyper-Pigmentation, Dark Circles, or Redness.
* **How Frequently Do You Use Skincare?** (`skin_frequency_use`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you use skincare products, such as Daily, Every Other Day, Twice A Week, or Less Than Once A Week.
* **What's Your Skincare Goal?** (`skincare_goal`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your skincare goal, such as Preventative Maintenance, Corrective Treatment, or Anti-Aging.
* **Which Skin Problems Do You Have?** (`skin_problems`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all skin problems you experience, such as Acne, Fine Lines, Hyper-Pigmentation, Dark Circles, or Redness.
* **What Skincare Products Are You Currently Using?** (`current_products_used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which skincare products you are currently using, such as Yes or No.
* **How Satisfied Are You With Current Products?** (`product_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your current skincare products, such as Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **How Frequently Do You Use Current Skincare Products?** (`skincare_frequency_of_use`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you use your current skincare products, such as Daily, Every Other Day, Twice A Week, or Less Than Once A Week.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us better serve you!
