# Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and satisfaction ratings regarding your experience with our service. Your input will help us improve and enhance our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name.
2. Provide your Email address (required field).
3. Share your feedback about your experience with us.
4. Rate your satisfaction level with us (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Enter the Date of your visit.
6. Enter the Time of your visit.
7. Enter the name of the product/service you used.
8. (Optional) Enter the specific version of the product/service you used.
9. Share how you found out about us (e.g., social media, search engine, word of mouth).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your email address that we may use to follow up on your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience with us. Be specific and detailed to help us improve.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with us. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product or service you used.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, text, optional): Enter the specific version of the product or service you used.
* **How did you find us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, text, optional): Share how you found out about us, for our reference.
