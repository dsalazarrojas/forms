# Marketing Analytics Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your marketing analytics communication. It will help our team understand your marketing efforts, campaign goals, and target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each question, select the most relevant answer from the provided options.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which marketing channel(s) are you using?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_2`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the marketing channel(s) you are using to promote your campaign, such as Email, Social Media, Influencer, Paid Advertising, Organic Search, or Word of Mouth.
* **What is the status of your marketing campaign?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_3`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the current status of your campaign, whether it is Active, In Progress, or Inactive.
* **What is the expected outcome of your campaign?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_4`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the desired result of your campaign, such as Increase Website Traffic, Boost Sales, Improve Brand Awareness, Engage with Customers, or Drive Conversions.
* **What is the primary objective of your campaign?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_5`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the main goal of your campaign, whether it is Generate Leads, Increase Sales, Boost Conversions, Improve Engagement, or Drive ROI.
* **What is the target audience of your campaign?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_6`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the group of people you are trying to reach with your campaign, such as Demographics, Behavioral, Location, or Interests.
* **What is the expected duration of your campaign?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_7`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the length of time you expect your campaign to run, whether it is Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term.
* **Is the campaign a part of a series?** (`marketing_analytics_communication_form_8`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about whether your campaign is part of a larger series of campaigns.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us understand your marketing efforts better.
