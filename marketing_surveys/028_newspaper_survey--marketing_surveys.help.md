# Newspaper Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with newspapers, focusing on specific aspects such as local papers, news sites, small businesses, listening to feedback, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant option for each field:
	* For "local_paper", choose the local paper you usually read.
	* For "news_sites", select all the news sites you regularly visit.
	* For "small_business", choose the small business you are most familiar with.
	* For "listen_feedback", indicate how often you listen to feedback.
2. If applicable, fill in your contact details:
	* "date" is a date field, where you can enter the date you last visited a local paper or news site.
	* "time" is a time field, where you can specify the time of your last visit.
	* "note" fields are text fields where you can provide additional information or comments.
	* "email" and "phone" fields are for entering your contact email and phone number, respectively.
3. Ensure you've selected or filled in all required fields, marked as such with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **local_paper** (`local_paper`, select_one, required): Select the local paper you usually read.
* **news_site** (`news_sites`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the news sites you regularly visit.
* **small_business** (`small_business`, select_one, required): Select the small business you are most familiar with.
* **listen_feedback** (`listen_to_feedback`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you listen to feedback, such as "yes, I listen" or "no, I don't listen".
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last visit to a local paper or news site.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Specify the time of your last visit.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **note2**, **phone2**, **note3**, **phone3**, **note4**, **phone4**, **note5**, **phone5**, **note6**, **phone6**, **note7**: These additional fields are duplicates of the previous ones, for your convenience in providing contact information and notes.
