# Marketing Reach Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Reach Evaluation Form is designed to assess the marketing reach of a team or organization. This form helps evaluate how well a team is performing in terms of its marketing efforts, identifying areas for improvement and opportunities for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the team or organization being evaluated.
2. Select the marketing channels currently being used (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Choose one performance indicator that best describes the team's performance (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
4. Enter a performance score for the team's marketing efforts (a numerical value).
5. Select the growth challenges the team is facing (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
6. Select the reach indicators that are currently being used (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7. Enter the target audience size (a numerical value).
8. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about channel performance.
9. (Optional) Select growth opportunities for the team (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
10. (Optional) Enter any ongoing analysis or comments about the team's marketing efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (Text): Enter the name of the team or organization being evaluated.
	+ This field is required.
* **marketing_channels** (Select Multiple): Select all marketing channels currently being used.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No".
	+ This field is required.
* **performance_indicators** (Select One): Choose one performance indicator that best describes the team's performance.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No".
	+ This field is required.
* **performance_score** (Number): Enter a performance score for the team's marketing efforts.
	+ This field is required.
* **growth_challenges** (Select Multiple): Select growth challenges the team is facing.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No".
	+ This field is optional.
* **reach_indicators** (Select Multiple): Select reach indicators currently being used.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No".
	+ This field is required.
* **target_audience** (Text): Enter the target audience size.
	+ This field is optional.
* **audience_size** (Number): Enter the target audience size.
	+ This field is optional.
* **channel_performance** (Note): Enter any notes about channel performance.
	+ This field is optional.
* **growth_opportunities** (Select Multiple): Select growth opportunities for the team.
	+ Options: "Yes", "No".
	+ This field is optional.
* **ongoing_analysis** (Text): Enter any ongoing analysis or comments about the team's marketing efforts.
	+ This field is optional.
