# Online Popularity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Popularity Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from users who have interacted with a particular product or service online.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the survey questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best apply to your experience with the product or service.
3. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Survey Comments" field to provide them.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): This field asks you to describe your experience with the product or service. Please write your answer in the space provided.
* **Demographic Questions** (`demographic_questions`, number, optional): This field asks you to provide your age range. Please select the number that best applies to you.
* **Survey Topics** (`survey_topics`, select_one, required): This field asks you if you think the product or service is useful. Please select the option that best answers this question.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is not currently used. Please do not enter any information here.
* **Survey Answers** (`survey_answers`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to answer yes or no questions about your experience with the product or service. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Date Taken** (`date_taken`, date, required): This field asks for the date you interacted with the product or service. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, time, optional): This field asks for the time you interacted with the product or service. Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
