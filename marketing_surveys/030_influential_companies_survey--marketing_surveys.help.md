# Influential Companies Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback and opinions from users about their experiences with different companies in various industries. Your input will help these companies improve their services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the company you are providing feedback about.
2. Select the scale that best describes your impression of this company.
3. Provide the main reason why you chose to use this company.
4. Share your expectation of their customer service.
5. If applicable, provide your email address and phone number (for future contact).
6. Answer the remaining questions based on your opinion of the company's reputation, products, customer support, and other aspects.
7. Finally, indicate if you would recommend this company to a friend and if you would use it again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company you are providing feedback about.

### Company Scale
* **Company Scale** (`company_scale`, `number`, optional): Select a scale from 1 to 10 to describe your impression of this company.

### Industry Sector
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Choose the industry this company operates in (Automotive, Banking, Technology).

### Question 1
* **What is your impression of this company?** (`question1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from Very Positive, Somewhat Positive, Neutral, Somewhat Negative, Very Negative.

### Question 2
* **What is the main reason why you chose to use this company?** (`question2`, `text`, required): Explain the reason behind your decision.

### Question 3
* **What is your expectation of their customer service?** (`question3`, `text`, optional): Share your expectation of the company's customer service.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).

### Question 4
* **What is your opinion on their products/services?** (`question4`, `date`, optional): Select from the date options.

### Question 5
* **What is your expectation of their future products/services?** (`question5`, `time`, optional): Select from the time options.

### Question 6
* **What is your opinion on their customer support?** (`question6`, `text`, optional): Share your opinion on the company's customer support.

### Question 7
* **How much do you value their company culture?** (`question7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how much you value their company culture.

### Question 8
* **What is your opinion on their company mission?** (`question8`, `text`, optional): Share your opinion on the company's mission.

### Question 9
* **Would you recommend this company to a friend?** (`question9`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes or No.

### Question 10
* **How likely are you to continue using this company?** (`question10`, `number`, optional): Select from 1 to 10.

### Question 11
* **What is your opinion on their company reputation?** (`question11`, `text`, optional): Share your opinion on the company's reputation.

### Question 12
* **How likely are you to recommend this company to others?** (`question12`, `number`, optional): Select from 1 to 10.

### Question 13
* **What is your opinion on their company website?** (`question13`, `text`, optional): Share your opinion on the company's website.

### Question 14
* **What is the main reason why you did not continue using this company?** (`question14`, `text`, optional): Explain the reason.

### Question 15
* **Would you use this company again?** (`question15`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes or No.

### Question 16
* **How satisfied are you with this company?** (`question16`, `number`, optional): Select from 1 to 10.
