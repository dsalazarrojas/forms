# Enablement Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enablement Insight Survey is a tool to gather information about enablement programs within your organization. It helps to understand the goals, targets, and frequency of enablement programs, as well as their impact on your team's productivity. This survey is designed to be completed by the person responsible for the enablement program.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please select the role within the company that best describes your position.
* Provide the name of the enablement program you are responsible for.
* Answer the question about the goal of the program in the text field.
* Choose the status of the program from the options.
* List any questions or topics included in the program.
* Select the delivery frequency of the program from the options.
* Type the name of the target audience of the program.
* Provide the size of the target audience of the program.
* Choose the impact of the program on your team's productivity.
* You may leave additional comments or information about the program in the fields at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the company?** (Select One: Sales, Marketing, Product, Support, Other): This question helps us understand who is responsible for the enablement program within the company.
* **Enablement Program Name**: This is the name of the enablement program you are responsible for. Please type the name of the program as it is known within your team.
* **What is the goal of this enablement program?**: Please provide a brief description of the goal of the enablement program. This will help us understand what the program is trying to achieve.
* **What is the current status of this enablement program?** (Select One: New, In Progress, Completed): This question helps us understand where the program is in terms of development and progress.
* **What questions or topics are included in this enablement program?**: Please provide a list of questions or topics included in the program, separated by commas.
* **How often is this enablement program delivered?** (Select One: Daily, Weekly, Monthly): This question helps us understand how frequently the program is delivered.
* **Who is the target audience of this enablement program?**: Please type the name of the target audience of the program.
* **How many people is the target audience of this enablement program?**: Please provide the size of the target audience of the program.
* **What is the impact of this enablement program on your team's productivity?** (Select One: High, Medium, Low): This question helps us understand the effectiveness of the program on your team's productivity.
* **Additional comments**: You may provide any additional comments or information about the program.
* **Additional information**: You may provide any additional information about the program.
