# DIY Beauty Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your at-home beauty practices. We want to understand your skincare routine, what motivates you, and how you purchase products. Your feedback will help us create better products for you!

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Answer all questions to the best of your ability**. You can choose multiple answers for questions that ask about multiple options.
2. **Be honest about your habits and preferences**. Your feedback will be used to improve our products and services.
3. **Double-check your answers before submitting**. You can review your form responses before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro**: This is a note field where you can briefly describe your relationship with DIY beauty practices. Please share your thoughts in a few words.
* **At-Home Routine**: Describe your typical at-home skincare routine.
* **Motivation**: What motivates you to engage in DIY beauty practices? 
* **Satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with your current skincare routine.
* **Challenges**: Select all challenges you face with your current skincare routine (e.g., Dull skin, Pores, Dry skin, Acne, Hyper-pigmentation, Fine lines).
* **Routine Frequency**: Describe how often you follow your current skincare routine.
* **Purchase Behaviors**: Select how often you make purchases related to DIY beauty products (e.g., Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Frequently).
* **Purchase Questions**: Select all questions you often ask when purchasing DIY beauty products (e.g., How often do you use skincare products?, What is your skincare routine like?, How often do you wear makeup?, Do you use sunscreen?).
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or updates.

Note: This form is for internal use only, and we will not sell or share your data with any third party. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.
