# Nail Polish Durability Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback about your experiences with nail polish, specifically your expectations and satisfaction with the durability of the polish. Your responses will help us understand how our products meet your expectations and what we can do to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Select the options that best describe your experiences with the polish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the color of the polish you're using?**
	+ This question helps us understand the color of the polish you're using, which can be helpful in understanding your expectations and experiences.
* **2. Which nail polish brand are you using?**
	+ Please select all brands that apply.
* **3. How long did it take for the polish to wear off?**
	+ Choose the option that best describes how long the polish lasted on your nails.
* **4. How would you rate the overall quality of the polish?**
	+ This question helps us understand your opinion on the polish's quality.
* **5. How many times have you had to reapply the polish due to chipping or fading?**
	+ Enter a whole number that reflects how many times you had to reapply the polish.
* **6. What causes the polish to chip?**
	+ Select all factors that apply to help us understand the reasons behind chipping.
* **7. Please specify other chip factors (if any).**
	+ Enter any additional factors that caused the polish to chip, aside from those listed.
* **8. How long did the polish last?**
	+ Choose the option that best describes how long the polish lasted.
* **9. How would you rate the finish quality of the polish?**
	+ This question helps us understand your opinion on the polish's finish quality.
* **10. How satisfied are you with the polish?**
	+ Choose an option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the polish.
* **11. Please specify any additional comments or feedback about the polish.**
