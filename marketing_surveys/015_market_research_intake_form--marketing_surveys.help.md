# Market Research Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Market Research Intake Form is a data collection tool used to gather demographic and contact information from potential participants in market research studies. This form helps us understand your background and preferences to better tailor our research to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Next, enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Then, provide your **E-mail Address**. Please ensure it is a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. Finally, enter your **Phone Number**, which will help us reach you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents or business cards.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents or business cards.
* **E-mail Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding market research studies or other relevant opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed for further discussion or study reminders.
