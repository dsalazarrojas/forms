# Influencer Marketing Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the effectiveness of influencer marketing campaigns. It is intended to gather data that can be used to evaluate and improve the success of these campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the campaign details provided and ensure you have all the necessary information to answer the questions accurately.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as required.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields to help provide a comprehensive picture of the campaign's effectiveness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Id** (`campaign_id`, `number`, required: false): This is the unique identifier for the campaign. Enter the ID number as provided by the system.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the campaign as it was known.
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, `number`, required: false): This score indicates the level of engagement achieved by the campaign. Please enter a number between 0 and 100 to represent the percentage of engagement.
* **Reach Score** (`reach_score`, `number`, required: false): This score indicates the level of reach achieved by the campaign. Please enter a number between 0 and 100 to represent the percentage of reach.
* **Conversion Score** (`conversion_score`, `number`, required: false): This score indicates the number of conversions achieved by the campaign. Please enter a number representing the total conversions.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total budget allocated for the campaign.
* **Campaign Goals** (`campaign_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe the campaign goals as provided by the client or marketing team.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required: false): Specify the target audience for the campaign.
* **Marketing Channel** (`marketing_channel`, `text`, required: false): Identify the marketing channel used for the campaign (e.g. social media, email).
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, `text`, required: false): Describe the overall strategy used for the campaign.
* **Marketing Tactics** (`marketing_tactics`, `text`, required: false): Specify the specific tactics used during the campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, `time`, required: false): Enter the duration of the campaign in the format `YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the campaign in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the campaign in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the person responsible for creating the campaign.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): Enter the person or team assigned to work on the campaign.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the tool used to manage this campaign from the options provided (ChatJimmie or other).
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, `number`, required: false): This is the form ID number. It is automatically generated and not editable.
