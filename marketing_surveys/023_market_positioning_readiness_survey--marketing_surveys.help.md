# Market Positioning Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to assess the market positioning readiness of a company or brand. The form will help to determine whether a company has a strong market position in their target segment.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and honestly, using data and information from your company's internal records and knowledge.
* Answer each question based on your current company data and strategy.
* If you're unsure or unclear about any question, please ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Target Segments**: Select all target segments that your company is currently marketing to or targeting. This field is optional, as some companies may not have multiple target segments.
  * Please select all applicable target segments that your company is currently marketing to or targeting.
* **Brand Positioning**: Choose the current brand positioning of your company. This field is optional and should be consistent with your company's current branding strategy.
  * Please select the current brand positioning that best describes your company.
* **Strategy Maturity**: This field measures the maturity of your company's marketing strategy. This field is optional and should be based on your company's current strategy implementation.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate the current level of strategy implementation (e.g., 1-10).
* **Target Segment**: Select the target segment that your company is currently targeting. This field is optional and should be consistent with your company's current target market.
  * Please select the target segment that your company is currently targeting.
* **Customer Pain Points**: Select all customer pain points that your company is addressing. This field is optional and should be based on customer feedback and data.
  * Please select all customer pain points that your company is currently addressing.
* **Brand Equity**: This field measures the current brand equity of your company. This field is optional and should be based on customer perception and market research.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate the current level of brand equity (e.g., 1-10).
* **Target Segment Awareness**: Select all target segments that your company has awareness in. This field is optional and should be based on market research and data.
  * Please select all target segments that your company has awareness in.
* **Target Segment Attractiveness**: Select all target segments that your company finds attractive. This field is optional and should be based on market research and data.
  * Please select all target segments that your company finds attractive.
* **Market Positioning Decision**: Choose whether your company has made a market positioning decision. This field is optional and should be consistent with your company's current strategy.
  * Please select 'Yes' if your company has made a market positioning decision, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Target Segment Attractiveness Score**: This field measures the attractiveness of the target segments. This field is optional and should be based on market research and data.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate the current level of attractiveness (e.g., 1-10).
* **Market Positioning Readiness Score**: This field measures the market positioning readiness of your company. This field is optional and should be based on the answers provided in the previous questions.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate the current level of market positioning readiness (e.g., 1-10).
* **Target Segments Score**: This field measures the score of the target segments. This field is optional and should be based on market research and data.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate the current level of target segment score (e.g., 1-10).
