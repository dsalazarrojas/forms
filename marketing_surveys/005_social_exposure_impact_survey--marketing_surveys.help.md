# Social Exposure Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant to capture data on the impact of social media on users' behavior, attitudes, and time spent on social media platforms. This data will help the marketing team understand the effectiveness of our social media campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form for each survey that has been completed.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the survey's terms and conditions before filling out this form.
3. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
4. If you have any additional comments or observations that are not captured in the form, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (1): Enter a brief title for the survey that summarizes its purpose and scope.
* **Category** (2): Select the category that best describes the topic of the survey (e.g., Marketing, Product, etc.).
* **Description** (3): Provide a brief description of the survey.
* **Rating 1** (4): Enter your rating for the impact of the social media channel on your behavior.
* **Rating 2** (5): Enter your rating for the impact of the social media channel on your attitude.
* **Rating 3** (6): Enter your rating for the importance of time spent on social media.
* **Social Channel** (7): Enter the name of the social media channel that was used in the survey.
* **Channel Impact** (8): Enter the impact of the social channel on your behavior (e.g., 1-5).
* **Survey Completed** (9): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Time Spent** (10): Enter the time spent on the social media channel.
* **Behavior Change** (11): Select the options that best describe your behavior change (e.g., True, False, Partial).
* **Behavior Change Reason** (12): Provide a brief explanation for your behavior change.
* **Attitude Change** (13): Select the options that best describe your attitude change (e.g., Positive, Negative, Neutral).
* **Attitude Change Reason** (14): Provide a brief explanation for your attitude change.
* **Behavior Change Likely** (15): Enter a rating for how likely you think you would be to make a behavior change.
* **Behavior Change Likely Reason** (16): Provide a brief explanation for your behavior change likelihood.
* **Behavior Change Possibility** (17): Enter a rating for how likely you think you would be to make a behavior change.
* **Attitude Change Possibility** (18): Enter a rating for how likely you think you would be to make an attitude change.
* **Behavior Change Importance** (19): Enter a rating for the importance of making a behavior change.
* **Behavior Change Importance Reason** (20): Provide a brief explanation for your behavior change importance.
* **Time Spent Important** (21): Select the option that best describes how important you think time spent on social media is.
* **Time Spent Important Reason** (22): Provide a brief explanation for your time spent importance.
* **Behavior Change Possibility Reason** (23): Provide a brief explanation for your behavior change possibility.
* **Attitude Change Possibility Reason** (24): Provide a brief explanation for your attitude change possibility.
* **Additional Comments** (25): Enter any additional comments or observations that are not captured in the form.

Note: This guide is intended to be used as a reference for filling out the form. It is not a substitute for the actual survey or its terms and conditions.
