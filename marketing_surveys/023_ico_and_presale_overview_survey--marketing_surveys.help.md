# ic_o_and_presale_overview_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an ICO and its presale. It is used to gather data on the token's overview, including presale information, token details, and marketing strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the presale information, including the token name, price, and description.
2.  Select the marketing strategy that best describes your ICO's approach.
3.  Choose the presale phase in which your token sale will occur.
4.  Enter the total number of tokens available for sale.
5.  Enter the price of the token during the presale.
6.  Enter the date of the presale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Presale Information (presale_information)**: Enter a brief description of your presale.
    *   This field is used to gather information about the presale, such as its details and description.
*   **Token Name (token_name)**: Enter the name of your token.
    *   This field is used to collect the name of the token being offered for sale.
*   **Token Price (token_price)**: Enter the price of your token.
    *   This field is used to collect the price of the token being offered for sale.
*   **Team Description (team_description)**: Enter a brief description of your team.
    *   This field is used to collect information about the team behind the token.
*   **Token Price ICO (token_price_ico)**: Enter the price of your token during the ICO.
    *   This field is used to collect the price of the token during the ICO.
*   **Marketing Strategy (marketing_strategy)**: Select the marketing strategy that best describes your approach.
    *   This field is used to collect information about the marketing strategy used to promote the token sale.
*   **Token Sale Date (token_sale_date)**: Enter the date of the token sale.
    *   This field is used to collect the date on which the token sale will occur.
*   **Total Tokens (total_tokens)**: Enter the total number of tokens available for sale.
    *   This field is used to collect the total number of tokens available for sale.
*   **Presale Phase (presale_phase)**: Choose the presale phase in which your token sale will occur.
    *   This field is used to collect the presale phase in which the token sale will occur.
*   **Presale Token Price (presale_token_price)**: Enter the price of the token during the presale phase.
    *   This field is used to collect the price of the token during the presale phase.
