# Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your thoughts and feelings about climate change, its impact, and your level of concern. Your responses will help us understand the attitudes and concerns of youth like you and inform our marketing efforts to address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please select the "Introduction" page to begin the survey. It will provide a brief overview of the purpose and instructions of the survey.
* Answer each question honestly and concisely. You can select multiple answers for question 2, and one answer for question 3.
* You can skip questions if you prefer, but keep in mind that your responses will help us better understand your views.
* When finished, submit the form to complete the survey.
