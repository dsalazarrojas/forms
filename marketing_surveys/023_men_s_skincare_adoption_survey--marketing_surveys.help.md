# Men's Skincare Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on men's skincare adoption and preferences, aiming to understand their routines, product usage, and discovery channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Use the provided options for select questions or enter a number for numerical questions.
3. Be aware that some questions are optional, but providing all the information will help gather more valuable insights.
4. If you need to skip a question or leave it blank, it's not recommended but you can do so if necessary.
5. Please ensure that you've completed the form entirely before submitting it.

    * Label: men_s_skin_care_routine
    * Type: select_one
    * Explanation: This question asks if you have a regular skincare routine, meaning you follow a consistent routine of skincare products and activities on a daily or several times a week basis.
