# Net Promoter Score Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score Form is designed to collect customer feedback and sentiment data to help us improve our services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Answer the question about your last interaction with our brand. This helps us understand your overall experience.
2. **Recommendation Score**: Choose a score between 1 and 5 to indicate how likely you are to recommend our brand.
3. **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share your thoughts on what we can do better.
4. **Return Intentions**: Answer questions about your intentions to return to our brand, suggest our brand to others, and other related aspects.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide your genuine thoughts and feelings.
* If you're not sure or haven't interacted with our brand recently, just let us know and we'll do our best to improve.
* You can skip any fields that you're not comfortable answering.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
