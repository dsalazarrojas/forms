# Cancellation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who are cancelling their subscription or service with us. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the reasons why you're cancelling, your level of satisfaction with our services, and any suggestions or areas for improvement. This data will help us to better understand your needs and make necessary changes to improve our services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. For text fields, type in your answer and click "Next" to proceed to the next field.
3. For dropdown menus and select boxes, choose the option that best describes your response.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (1): Type in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Customer Email Address** (2): Enter your email address associated with your account. This will help us follow up with you for further discussion.
* **Account Number** (3): If you have an account number or customer ID, enter it here.
* **Service Type Cancelled** (4): Select the type of service you are cancelling (e.g., Subscription plan, Premium features, etc.).
* **How Long Were You a Customer** (5): Choose how long you were a customer with us (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1 to 3 months, etc.).
* **Primary Reason for Cancellation** (6): Select the main reason you are cancelling our service (e.g., Too expensive, Found better alternative, etc.).
* **Tell Us More About Your Decision** (7): Please elaborate on your reason for cancellation.
* **How Satisfied Were You with the Price** (8): Rate your satisfaction with our pricing (e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, etc.).
* **How Satisfied Were You with the Features** (9): Rate your satisfaction with our features (e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, etc.).
* **How Satisfied Were You with Customer Service** (10): Rate your satisfaction with our customer service (e.g., Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, etc.).
* **What Features Were Missing** (11): Select all features that you feel were missing or didn't meet your needs.
* **Are You Switching to a Competitor** (12): Check if you've found an alternative service that you're switching to.
* **Which Competitor** (13): If you've found a competitor, enter its name.
* **How Likely Are You to Return** (14): Rate your likelihood of returning to our service in the future.
* **Would You Recommend Us to Others** (15): Rate your likelihood of recommending us to others (e.g., Definitely yes, Probably yes, etc.).
* **What Did You Like Most** (16): Describe the best aspects of our service that you enjoyed.
* **What Could We Improve** (17): Share any suggestions or areas for improvement.
* **Would a Discount Change Your Mind** (18): Check if a discount would make you reconsider cancelling our service.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-up** (19): Check if you're willing to be contacted for further discussion or feedback.
* **Any Additional Comments** (20): Share any other thoughts or comments you'd like to add.
* **Survey Completion Date** (21): Date and time you completed this survey.
 
Please note that all fields are required, as indicated by the "required: true" label in the YAML.
