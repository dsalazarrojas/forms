# High Heels Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer feedback and data for marketing purposes. It helps us understand the market sentiment about our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category that best suits your response from the options provided.
2. Optionally, provide a brief description of your experience with High Heels.
3. Enter your email address to receive any updates or follow-up questions.
4. If you wish, provide your phone number for us to contact you.
5. Share your feedback and thoughts about our products or services.
6. Rate the quality of our products or services on a scale of 1-5.
7. Select the price range of our products or services that you've experienced.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`marketing_surveys`, select_one): Select the category that best describes your response. This will help us filter and analyze your feedback.
* **Description (Optional)** (`description`, note): If you've had an experience with High Heels, please provide a brief description to help us understand your context.
* **Email (Optional)** (`email`, email): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up questions or updates. We'll respect your privacy and only contact you as needed.
* **Phone Number (Optional)** (`phone`, text): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number. We'll reach out only if needed.
* **Feedback (Optional)** (`form_comments`, note): Share your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about our products or services.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, select_multiple): Rate the quality of our products or services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Price Range** (`product_price_range`, number): Select the price range of our products or services that you've experienced.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* Only submit the form if you've had a genuine experience with High Heels.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
