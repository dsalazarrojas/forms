# Consumer Brand Loyalty Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your relationship with a brand. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand your experiences and perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your genuine feelings and experiences.
2. Choose one or multiple answers for each question, as applicable.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (if desired).
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the given options:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65+
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the given options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Prefer Not to Say
	+ Other
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, select_one, optional): Select your household income from the given options:
	+ Under $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $49,999
	+ $50,000 - $74,999
	+ $75,000 - $99,999
	+ $100,000 - $149,999
	+ $150,000+
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Favorite Brand in Category** (`favorite_brand`, text, required): Enter the brand that you consider your favorite in the category.
* **Product Category** (`brand_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your favorite brand.
* **How Long Have You Been Loyal** (`loyalty_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your brand loyalty.
	+ Less than 1 Year
	+ 1-3 Years
	+ 3-5 Years
	+ 5-10 Years
	+ 10+ Years
* **Why Are You Loyal** (`loyalty_reason`, select_multiple, required): Select all the reasons why you are loyal to the brand. You can choose one or multiple answers:
	+ Product Quality
	+ Price
	+ Brand Reputation
	+ Customer Service
	+ Convenience
	+ Habit
	+ Recommendations
	+ Values Alignment
	+ Other
* **Likelihood to Switch Brands** (`switch_likelihood`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to switch brands.
	+ Very Unlikely
	+ Unlikely
	+ Neutral
	+ Likely
	+ Very Likely
* **What Would Make You Switch** (`switch_trigger`, select_multiple, required): Select all the factors that would cause you to switch brands. You can choose one or multiple answers:
	+ Lower Price
	+ Better Quality
	+ Better Service
	+ Innovation
	+ Negative Experience
	+ Recommendations
	+ Availability Issues
	+ Better Values
	+ Nothing Would Make Me Switch
* **Trust in Brand** (`brand_trust`, select_one, required): Select how much you trust the brand.
	+ Completely Trust
	+ Mostly Trust
	+ Somewhat Trust
	+ Slightly Trust
	+ Do Not Trust
* **Would You Recommend This Brand** (`brand_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select if you would recommend the brand.
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Neutral
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Price Sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, select_one, required): Select how important price is to you when choosing a brand.
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Very Important
	+ Not Important
* **Quality vs Price Priority** (`quality_vs_price`, select_one, required): Select what matters more to you when choosing a brand.
	+ Quality Always
	+ Quality Usually
	+ Depends on Product
	+ Price Usually
	+ Price Always
* **Emotional Connection to Brand** (`emotional_connection`, select_one, required): Select how connected you feel to the brand.
	+ Very Strong Connection
	+ Strong Connection
	+ Moderate Connection
	+ Weak Connection
	+ No Connection
* **Brand Perception** (`brand_perception`, select_multiple, required): Select how you perceive the brand. You can choose one or multiple answers:
	+ Innovative
	+ Reliable
	+ Affordable
	+ Premium
	+ Trustworthy
	+ Fun
	+ Sustainable
	+ Traditional
	+ Trendy
	+ Other
* **Do You Follow Brand on Social Media** (`social_media_following`, select_one, optional): Select if you follow the brand on social media.
	+ Yes, Multiple Platforms
	+ Yes, One Platform
	+ 'False'
* **Subscribed to Brand Newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, optional): Select if you subscribe to the brand's newsletter.
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you purchase from the brand.
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Twice a Year
	+ Once a Year
	+ Rarely
* **Overall Brand Experience** (`brand_experience_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the brand.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts or feedback about the brand.

## Tips
* Answer truthfully and take your time to complete the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
