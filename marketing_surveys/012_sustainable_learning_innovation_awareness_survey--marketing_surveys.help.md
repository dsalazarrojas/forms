# Sustainable Learning Innovation Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on sustainable learning practices and innovations that can help inform the development of more efficient and effective learning solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Page 1" section and fill out the "What are some sustainable learning practices" field by choosing one or more options from the dropdown list.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you wish to be contacted for further feedback or follow-up questions.
3. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
4. Continue to subsequent pages (2-10) and fill out the same type of fields, repeating the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are some sustainable learning practices** (`sustainable_learning_practices`, select_multiple): This field asks you to select one or more sustainable learning practices that you are familiar with or have experience in.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further feedback or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Optionally, provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
