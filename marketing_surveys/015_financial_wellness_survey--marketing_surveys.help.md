# Financial Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Wellness Survey is a short form designed to assess your current financial situation and goals. It will help us understand your financial needs and provide you with personalized recommendations to improve your financial well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
- Take a few minutes to read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
- Select the correct answer for each question, taking into account your current financial situation.
- Submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **financial_wellness_survey_form** (`select_one`, required): Answer this question by selecting "Yes" if you currently consider yourself financially well off, and "No" if you're struggling or experiencing financial difficulties.
* **current_financial_situation** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your current financial situation. This can help us understand your overall financial health.
* **financial_security** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe your current financial security level, e.g., "secure," "somewhat secure," or "not secure."
* **financial_goals** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the options that best represents your financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or paying off debt.
* **financial_overview** (`time`, optional): Please provide a brief description of your current financial situation, e.g., "I've been living below my means for a while now" or "I've recently received a raise."
* **financial_awareness** (`number`, optional): Rate your financial awareness on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "not aware at all" and 10 being "completely aware."
* **financial_stress** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe your current level of financial stress, e.g., "high," "medium," or "low."
