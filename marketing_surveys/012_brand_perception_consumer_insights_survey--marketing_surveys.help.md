# Brand Perception and Consumer Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect data on consumer perception of a brand, focusing on awareness, associations, purchase likelihood, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your age range in the **Age Range** field.
2. Choose your gender from the options provided in the **Gender** field.
3. Select the location where you reside from the **Location** field.
4. If applicable, choose your annual household income range in the **Household Income** field.
5. Indicate your level of familiarity with the brand in the **Brand Awareness** field.
6. Choose up to three words that come to mind when you think of this brand in the **Brand Associations** field.
7. Select your likelihood of purchasing from this brand in the **Purchase Likelihood** field.
8. Indicate whether this brand is your first choice in the **Brand Preference** field.
9. Rate the perceived quality of the brand in the **Perceived Quality** field.
10. Rate the perceived value of the brand in the **Perceived Value** field.
11. Choose your level of trust for this brand in the **Brand Trust** field.
12. Indicate your likelihood to recommend this brand to others in the **Recommendation Likelihood** field.
13. Compare this brand with competitors in the **Compared to Competitors** field.
14. Select preferred channels for purchasing from this brand in the **Preferred Purchase Channels** field.
15. Indicate whether the marketing messages resonate with you in the **Marketing Message Resonance** field.
16. (Optional) Provide suggestions for improvement in the **What Could This Brand Improve** field.
17. Express your interest in participating in future studies in the **Interested in Follow-Up Research** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Range** (`respondent_age`, `select_one`, required): Select your age range to help us understand your demographic profile.
* **Gender** (`respondent_gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your gender to provide a better understanding of the target audience.
* **Location** (`respondent_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location where you reside to help us understand the brand's reach.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, choose your annual household income range to help us understand your financial situation.
* **Brand Awareness** (`brand_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of familiarity with the brand, from "Yes, Very Familiar" to "No, Never Heard of It".
* **Brand Associations** (`brand_associations`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose up to three words that come to mind when you think of this brand.
* **Purchase Likelihood** (`purchase_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Select your likelihood of purchasing from this brand, from "Very Likely" to "Very Unlikely".
* **Brand Preference** (`brand_preference`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether this brand is your first choice, from "Always My First Choice" to "Never Consider".
* **Perceived Quality** (`perceived_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the perceived quality of the brand, from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Perceived Value** (`perceived_value`, `select_one`, required): Rate the perceived value of the brand, from "Excellent Value" to "Very Poor Value".
* **Brand Trust** (`brand_trust`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of trust for this brand, from "Trust Completely" to "Distrust Completely".
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your likelihood to recommend this brand to others, from "Very Likely" to "Very Unlikely".
* **Compared to Competitors** (`competitor_comparison`, `select_one`, required): Compare this brand with competitors, from "Much Better" to "Much Worse".
* **Preferred Purchase Channels** (`purchase_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select preferred channels for purchasing from this brand, from "Brand Website" to "Marketplace".
* **Marketing Message Resonance** (`marketing_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the marketing messages resonate with you, from "Strongly Resonate" to "Strongly Disagree".
* **What Could This Brand Improve** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions for improvement, if any.
* **Interested in Follow-Up Research** (`follow_up_interest`, `select_one`, required): Express your interest in participating in future studies, from "Yes, Interested" to "No, Not Interested".
