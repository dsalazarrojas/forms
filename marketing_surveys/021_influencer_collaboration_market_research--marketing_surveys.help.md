# Influencer Collaboration Market Research - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about potential collaborators for market research purposes. The goal is to gather accurate and relevant data about potential collaborators on various platforms, minimize duplication of requests, and improve our collaboration strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself as a potential collaborator.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help us to better understand your needs and preferences.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as *required*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Information (Brand Information, text, required)**: This is the first step to identify yourself as a brand. Please enter your brand's name or a description of your brand.
* **Audience (Audience, number, required)**: This field is meant to measure the size of your audience on various platforms. Enter the number of followers you have on each platform (e.g. 100k for 100,000 followers).
* **Platforms (Platforms, select multiple, required)**: Select all platforms where your brand is present (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.).
* **Collaboration Preferences (Collaboration Preferences, select one, required)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for collaboration (e.g. Facebook, Email, Phone).
* **E-mail (E-mail, email, required)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Time Spent (Time Spent, number, required)**: Estimate the amount of time you spend on collaborations per week.
* **Notes (Notes, text, required)**: Add any additional information about yourself or your brand that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Additional Information (Additional Information, text, optional)**: If there's anything else you'd like to share about yourself or your brand, feel free to add it here.
* **Follow-up (Follow-up, text, optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up information, enter your preferred method of contact (e.g. Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Contact Method (Contact Method, select one, optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted, choose your preferred contact method (e.g. Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Additional Time Spent (Additional Time Spent, number, optional)**: If you have additional time spent on collaborations, estimate the amount.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes, text, optional)**: If you'd like to add more information about yourself or your brand, feel free to do so here.
