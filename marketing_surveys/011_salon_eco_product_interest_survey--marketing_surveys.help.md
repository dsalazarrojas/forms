# Salon Eco Product Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their interest in salon-related products, with a focus on eco-friendly options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your answers from the provided options. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is "Eco" to you?** (`question1`, text, required: false): 
  Please describe your understanding of what "eco" means to you.

- **Which eco-friendly products are you interested in?** (`question2`, select_one, options: "Option 1, Option 2, Option 3", required: false): 
  Choose one option that best describes your interest in eco-friendly products.

- **Which of the following eco-friendly products are you interested in?** (`question3`, select_multiple, options: "Option 1, Option 2, Option 3", required: false): 
  Select all options that apply to your interest in eco-friendly products.

- **Your Email** (`question4`, email, required: false): 
  Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you about our latest products and updates.

- **Your Phone Number** (`question5`, text, required: false): 
  Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your interest in our products.

- **Date of Interest** (`question6`, date, required: false): 
  Please enter the date you became interested in eco-friendly products.

- **Time of Interest** (`question7`, time, required: false): 
  Please enter the time of day you became interested in eco-friendly products (e.g., morning, afternoon).

- **Notes** (`question8`, note, required: false): 
  Please enter any additional information or details about your interest in our eco-friendly products. You can also provide the name of someone you are completing this form for.
