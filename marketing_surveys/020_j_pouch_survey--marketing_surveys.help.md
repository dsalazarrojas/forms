# J Pouch Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The J Pouch Survey is a form designed to gather information about your experiences and conditions related to pouchitis, a type of inflammatory bowel disease. This form is intended to collect data about your symptoms, medical history, and treatment plans to help us better understand the condition and provide more effective support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to your experiences and conditions.
3. Enter any additional comments or information as requested.
4. Ensure that you fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your medical condition?** (Pouchitis, Diarrhea, Abdominal Pain, Bloating, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand the condition you're experiencing.
* **How long have you experienced symptoms?** (Less than 1 week, 1 week to 1 year, More than 1 year, None):
  This question helps us understand the duration of your symptoms.
* **What kind of treatment plan is this?** (Medication, Diet, Surgery, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand the treatment you're undergoing or have considered.
* **Do you have a pre-existing medical condition?** (Pouchitis, IBS, Ulcerative Colitis, Crohn's Disease, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand your medical history.
* **How often do you experience symptoms?** (Every day, Several times a week, Occasionally, Rarely, None):
  This question helps us understand the frequency of your symptoms.
* **How do you rate the impact on your daily life?** (Severe, Moderate, Slight, None):
  This question helps us understand the effect of your symptoms on your daily life.
* **Other medical conditions**: This question allows you to provide additional medical conditions you may have.
* **Are you currently taking any medications?** (Medication A, Medication B, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand your current medical treatment.
* **How often do you take medication?** (Every day, Several times a week, Occasionally, Rarely, None):
  This question helps us understand your medication schedule.
* **How often do you visit the doctor?** (Every time, Occasionally, Rarely, Never):
  This question helps us understand your medical care routine.
* **Who is your medical team?** (Specialist, General Practitioner, Family Doctor, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand who provides your medical care.
* **Additional comments**: This question allows you to provide any additional information or comments.
* **How did you find this survey?** (Helpful, Unhelpful, Not applicable):
  This question helps us understand your experience with the survey.
* **Age**: Enter your age to help us better understand your demographics.
* **What is your gender?** (Male, Female, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand your gender identity.
* **Location**: Enter your location to help us better understand your demographics.
* **How would you like to contact us?** (Phone, Email, Other, None):
  This question helps us understand your preferred contact method.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Survey date**: Enter the date of the survey for metadata purposes.
* **Time of the survey**: Enter the time of the survey for metadata purposes.
* **Do you agree to the terms of the survey?** (True, False):
  This question ensures you agree to the survey terms.
* **Have you ever had IBS?** (True, False):
  This question helps us understand your IBS experience.
* **Have you ever had Crohns Disease?** (True, False):
  This question helps us understand your Crohns Disease experience.
* **Additional comments**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or information.

Note: Ensure that you answer all questions and provide all required fields to complete the survey accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
