# CB Apex Information Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CB Apex Information Sheet is a form designed to collect feedback, testimonials, and information about your experience with CB Apex. This form will help us understand your satisfaction level and areas where we can improve our services. Your feedback will be used to share your success story in our marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: 
   - Enter your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field (required).
   - Enter your **Business or Organization** name in the "Business or Organization" field (required).
   - Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field (required).
   - Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field (required).
2. Select services received: 
   - Choose the services you have received from CB Apex from the list in the "Services Received" field (required).
3. Describe your experience: 
   - Write a brief description of your experience and the results you achieved in the "Your Success Story" field (required).
4. Provide feedback on your satisfaction: 
   - Select your **Overall Satisfaction Rating** from the 5-star scale in the "Overall Satisfaction Rating" field (required).
   - Let us know if you would **refer CB Apex to a colleague** in the "Would you refer CB Apex to a colleague?" field (required).
5. Update your project status: 
   - If you are currently working with CB Apex, select the **Current Project Status** in the field (optional).
6. Additional support needs: 
   - If there are other areas where we can support you, enter them in the "Are there any other areas where we can support you?" field (optional).
7. Date of submission: 
   - Enter the date you are submitting this form in the "Date of Submission" field (required).
8. Verification: 
   - Confirm that the information you provided is up-to-date and accurate in the "I certify the information provided is current" field (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`<text>`, required): Your full name as per your identification.
* **Business or Organization** (`<text>`, required): Your business or organization name.
* **Email Address** (`<email>`, required): The email address associated with your business or organization.
* **Phone Number** (`<text>`, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Services Received** (`<select>`, required): Select all the services you have received from CB Apex from the options provided.
* **Length of Time Working with CB Apex** (`<select>`, required): Select the duration of your working relationship with CB Apex from the options provided.
* **Your Success Story** (`<text>`, required): Write a short description of your experience and achievements with CB Apex.
* **Permission to Publish** (`<select>`, required): Choose whether you consent to the use of your testimonial in our marketing materials, including your name and company name.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`<select>`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with CB Apex services from 1-5 stars.
* **Would you refer CB Apex to a colleague?** (`<select>`, required): Indicate if you would recommend CB Apex to a colleague.
* **Current Project Status** (`<select>`, optional): Select the current status of your project with CB Apex (if applicable).
* **Are there any other areas where we can support you?** (`<text>`, optional): If there are areas where we can further assist you, enter them here.
* **Date of Submission** (`<date>`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **I certify the information provided is current** (`<select>`, required): Confirm that the information you provided is up-to-date and accurate.
