# market_research_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The market_research_survey form is designed to gather information about your company for market research purposes. This data will help us better understand your market position and identify potential opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the intro section, which serves as an introduction to the form.
2. Proceed to the contact information section and fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
3. In the company size section, select the most appropriate answer from the provided options: Small, Medium, or Large.
4. In the industry section, select all the industries that apply to your company.
5. If you have a job title, enter it in the job_title field.
6. Enter the number of years your company has been in business.
7. Enter your company's revenue.
8. Enter your company's website.
9. Answer the questions about why you participate in market research, what you like about participating, and what you dislike about participating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_do_you_do** (text): This field is a brief introduction to your company, which can be up to 250 characters long.
* **name** (text): Enter your name or the name of the contact person responsible for filling out this form.
* **contact_info** (text): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details.
* **email** (email): Enter your business email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your business phone number.
* **company_size** (select one): Select the size of your company: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **industry** (select multiple): Select all the industries that apply to your company.
* **job_title** (text): Enter your job title.
* **years_in_business** (number): Enter the number of years your company has been in business.
* **revenue** (number): Enter your company's revenue.
* **company_website** (text): Enter your company website.
* **why_do_you_participate** (text): Answer why you participate in market research surveys.
* **what_do_you_like** (text): Answer what you like about participating in market research surveys.
* **what_do_you_dislike** (text): Answer what you dislike about participating in market research surveys.
