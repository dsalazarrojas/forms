# Gym Social Media Campaign Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants who have engaged with our latest social media campaign. We want to hear about your experience and gather feedback to improve our future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly, and make sure to select the correct options for each field.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback in the `Got any feedback for us?` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your impression of our latest gym social media campaign**
	+ This is a text field where you can describe your overall impression of our campaign. Please be as detailed as possible, and provide any thoughts or feelings you had about it.
* **Which social media platform did you visit first?**
	+ Select the platform where you first visited our social media post. This will help us understand which platform is most effective for our campaign.
* **What was the level of engagement with your post?**
	+ Choose all that apply to describe your level of engagement with the post. You can select 'Engaging', 'Somewhat Engaging', or 'Not Engaging' to indicate how much attention you paid to the post.
* **How did you feel about the ad?**
	+ Choose all that apply to describe your feelings towards the ad. You can select 'Positive', 'Negative', or 'Neutral' to indicate your overall opinion.
* **What's your Ad Impact Score?**
	+ This is a numerical field where you can rate the ad on a scale of 1-5. Please be honest, and rate it based on your actual experience.
* **What type of post was it?**
	+ Select the type of post you encountered. You can choose 'Photo', 'Video', 'Story', or 'Live' to indicate the type of content you saw.
* **How did you feel about the post?**
	+ Choose all that apply to describe your feelings towards the post. You can select 'Positive', 'Negative', or 'Neutral' to indicate your overall opinion.
* **What's your Post Feeling Score?**
	+ This is a numerical field where you can rate the post on a scale of 1-5. Please be honest, and rate it based on your actual experience.
* **Got any feedback for us?**
	+ This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about our campaign. Please be specific and provide any constructive feedback you have.
* **Will you be part of follow-up surveys?**
	+ Let us know if you're willing to participate in future surveys. You can select 'True', 'False', or 'Undecided' to indicate your availability.
