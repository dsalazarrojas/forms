# hair_salon_social_media_influence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help hair salons understand and measure the influence of social media on their marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best describes your social media usage or goals.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Info** (`salon_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering your salon's name, address, or any other identifying information.
* **Social Media Usage** (`social_media_usage`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you use social media for your salon's marketing:
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None
* **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the social media channels where you have a presence:
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ Instagram
	+ Pinterest
	+ None
* **Client Discovery** (`client_discovery`, select_one, optional): How do you discover new clients?
	+ Social media
	+ Online search
	+ Word of mouth
	+ None
* **Social Media Influence** (`social_media_influence`, select_one, required): How strong is the influence of social media on your salon's marketing?
	+ Very strong
	+ Somewhat strong
	+ Somewhat weak
	+ None
* **Online Search Engagement** (`online_search_engagement`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the online search types that are relevant to your salon:
	+ Search engine
	+ Online review
	+ Social media
	+ None
* **Social Media Advertising** (`social_media_advertising`, text, required): Briefly describe how you use social media advertising for your salon.
* **Social Media Goals** (`social_media_goals`, text, required): Describe the goals you have for your social media presence.
* **Client Choice Influence** (`client_choice_influence`, select_one, required): How do clients choose to use your salon?
	+ Social media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online search
	+ None
* **Client Choice Influence Other** (`other_client_choice_influence`, text, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Salon Website** (`salon_website`, text, optional): Enter your salon's website URL, if applicable.
* **Social Media Content** (`social_media_content`, text, optional): Briefly describe the types of content you post on social media.
* **Content Frequency** (`content_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you post on social media.
* **Content Types** (`content_types`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the types of content you post on social media:
	+ Facebook posts
	+ Instagram posts
	+ Twitter posts
	+ None
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, text, optional): Briefly describe the quality of your social media content.
* **Content Tone** (`content_tone`, text, optional): Describe the tone of your social media content.
* **Content Style** (`content_style`, text, optional): Describe the style of your social media content.
* **Client Interaction** (`client_interaction`, text, optional): Briefly describe how you interact with clients on social media.
* **Client Interaction Frequency** (`client_interaction_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how often you interact with clients on social media.
* **Client Interaction Type** (`client_interaction_type`, select_one, optional): How do you interact with clients on social media?
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ Instagram
	+ None
* **Content Localization** (`content_localization`, text, optional): Briefly describe how you localize content for different regions or markets.
* **Content Localization Other** (`other_content_localization`, text, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Marketing Materials** (`marketing_materials`, text, optional): Briefly describe the marketing materials you use.
* **Marketing Materials Other** (`other_marketing_materials`, text, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your social media marketing efforts.

Please note that you can always contact form@gic.mx for any clarification or questions about this form.
