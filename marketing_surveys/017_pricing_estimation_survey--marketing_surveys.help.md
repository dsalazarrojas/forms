# pricing_estimation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pricing_estimation_survey form is used to gather estimates for pricing-related surveys. This form is designed to collect information from end-users to help the business make informed decisions about pricing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title "pricing_estimation_survey" to access the form.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, and make sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pricing_estimation_form** (`number`, required: false): This field is used to enter a numerical value for the pricing estimation. Please enter a whole number without any decimal points.
* **pricing_estimation_survey_description** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the survey, including any relevant details about the pricing estimation.
* **pricing_estimation_survey_category** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more categories that best describe the survey, from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **pricing_estimation_survey_tags** (`note`, required: false): Add any relevant tags or keywords to help with search and filtering.
* **pricing_estimation_survey_email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address associated with the survey.
* **pricing_estimation_survey_phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter a phone number associated with the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field carefully, as the information provided will directly impact the accuracy of the pricing estimation.
* Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
* If you need help or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
