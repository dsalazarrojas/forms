# Art Sales Strategy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from art professionals about their sales strategies. It will help us understand how art sales professionals navigate the market, use different channels, and approach marketing and sales. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the form, one field at a time. 
2.  Each field has been labeled and explained below, so refer back to these explanations as you complete each one. 
3.  Make sure to fill in all required fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Survey Date**: This field is where you input the date you complete this form. 
- **Respondent Name**: This is your name and contact information. You can input your name, and if you'd like, you can also provide your organization name. 
- **Respondent Email**: Your work email address. If you don't have an email associated with your art work, you can leave this field blank. 
- **Organization Type**: This field asks you to select your type of organization. Select the one that best describes your work. 
    - **Individual Artist**: You are a solo artist. 
    - **Commercial Gallery**: You sell art through a commercial gallery. 
    - **Non-Profit Gallery**: You sell art through a non-profit gallery. 
    - **Art Fair Organizer**: You organize art fairs. 
    - **Art Consultant**: You help others with art sales. 
    - **Art Advisor**: You advise others on art purchases. 
    - **Auction House**: You sell art through an auction house. 
    - **Online Platform**: You sell art online. 
    - **Other**: If none of the above options apply, select "Other." 
- **Years in Business**: This field asks how long you've been selling art. 
    - **Less than 1 year**: You've been selling less than a year. 
    - **1-3 years**: You've been selling 1-3 years. 
    - **3-5 years**: You've been selling 3-5 years. 
    - **5-10 years**: You've been selling 5-10 years. 
    - **More than 10 years**: You've been selling over 10 years. 
- **Annual Sales Range**: This field asks for your approximate annual art sales. 
    - **Under $50,000**: You make less than $50,000 a year in sales. 
    - **$50,000-$100,000**: You make between $50,000 and $100,000 a year in sales. 
    - **$100,000-$500,000**: You make between $100,000 and $500,000 a year in sales. 
    - **$500,000-$1,000,000**: You make between $500,000 and $1,000,000 a year in sales. 
    - **$1,000,000-$5,000,000**: You make between $1,000,000 and $5,000,000 a year in sales. 
    - **Over $5,000,000**: You make over $5,000,000 a year in sales. 
- **Primary Sales Channel**: This field asks for your main sales channel. 
    - **Physical Gallery**: You sell through a physical gallery. 
    - **Online Website**: You sell through your website. 
    - **Art Fairs**: You sell through art fairs. 
    - **Social Media**: You sell through social media. 
    - **Auctions**: You sell through auctions. 
    - **Art Advisors**: You sell through art advisors. 
    - **Other**: If none of the above apply, select "Other." 
- **Sales Channels Used**: This field asks you to select all the sales channels you use.
