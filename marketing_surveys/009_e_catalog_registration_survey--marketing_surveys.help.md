# e_catalog_registration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_catalog_registration_survey form is designed to collect essential information from users for marketing purposes. This includes demographic details, contact preferences, and interests in products/services offered by the e-catalog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form fields with your information, ensuring you enter valid email addresses and phone numbers.
2. Select your interest(s) in the products/services offered by the e-catalog.
3. Choose your preferred method(s) of communication.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.

### 3. Street Address
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your street address.

### 4. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### 5. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.

### 6. Product Interests
* **What Are You Interested In** (`product_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the products/services you are interested in from the list. This will help us tailor our marketing efforts to you.

### 7. Email Frequency
* **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how frequently you would like to receive emails from us:
	+ **daily**: Daily emails
	+ **weekly**: Weekly emails
	+ **monthly**: Monthly emails

### 8. Communication Preferences
* **How Would You Like to Be Contacted** (`communication_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the methods you prefer to be contacted by us:
	+ **email**: Email
	+ **phone**: Phone
	+ **post**: Postal mail
	+ **mail**: Postal mail

### 9. Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration or interests.

### 10. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): This field is reserved for internal use and should not be filled out by users.

Note: The assigned_tool field is not meant to be filled out by users, as it is intended for internal use. However, if you're curious about what it might be used for, it could be a tool or system assigned to a user or a project.
