# newsletter_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your preferences for receiving newsletters from our organization. We want to ensure that you receive relevant and interesting content in a format that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us understand your preferences.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the *required* label).
3. Choose your preferred options for each question.
4. Click "Submit" to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you want to hear from us** (`newsletter_frequency`, number, not required):
    We want to know how often you'd like to receive emails from us. Select a frequency that works best for you: 1-2 times a week, once a month, or as needed.
* **What topics are you interested in learning about** (`newsletter_topic`, select_one, not required):
    Check the topics that interest you the most. We'll use this information to tailor our content to your needs.
* **What is your preferred method of receiving newsletters** (`newsletter_method`, select_multiple, not required):
    Choose how you'd like to receive our newsletters: Email, Phone, or Both.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
    Enter your email address to receive our newsletters. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
    If you'd like to receive our newsletters via Phone, enter your phone number. Please note that this will only work if you've given us your phone number previously.
* **What date is your birthday** (`birthday`, date, not required):
    Enter your birthday in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-01-01). This will help us personalize our content for you.
* **What time of day do you prefer to receive your newsletter** (`time_of_day`, time, not required):
    Choose a time of day that suits you best. We'll do our best to deliver our newsletters at that time.
* **Do you have any notes or comments about this form** (`notes`, note, not required):
    Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about this form or our newsletters.
