# Donor Demographic Research Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about donors and potential donors to help organizations understand their audience's demographics and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your name, job title, company, and contact information to provide context for your answers.
3. Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
4. Select your preferred frequency of donation (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, etc.).
5. Choose the platforms you use for social media (e.g., Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.).
6. Answer questions about your motivation for donating (e.g., to help others, support a cause, etc.).
7. Provide your income range (if applicable).
8. Enter your ZIP code and address (if applicable).
9. Select your job type (e.g., Non-Profit, Corporate, etc.).
