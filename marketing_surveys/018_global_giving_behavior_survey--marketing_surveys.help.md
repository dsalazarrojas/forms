# Global Giving Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Giving Behavior Survey is a form used to collect information about an individual's or organization's giving behavior, including contact information, donation frequency, and amount given. This form is designed to help research or marketing teams understand giving patterns and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by answering the questions on the Main Page.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Select your donor type and frequency of giving on the Donor Type and Frequency of Giving pages.
4. Enter any relevant contact information, such as your email, phone number, or address, on the Contact Method page.
5. Answer questions about your first and last gifts, including the amount and frequency on the First Given and Last Given pages.
6. Answer questions about your total given, including frequency and amount on the Total Given pages.
7. Review and confirm your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This is the starting point of the form, where you will answer general questions about yourself and your giving behavior.
	+ **Donor Type**: Select the type of donor you are (Donor, Fundraiser, Non-Donor, Supporter, Other, or Unknown).
	+ **Donor Frequency**: Select the frequency of your giving (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Once, Other, or Never).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, such as email, phone number, or address.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Donor Type**: Select the type of donor you are (Donor, Fundraiser, Non-Donor, Supporter, Other, or Unknown).
* **Donor Frequency**: Select the frequency of your giving (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Once, Other, or Never).
* **First Given**:
	+ **First Given Date**: Enter the date of your first gift.
	+ **First Given Amount**: Enter the amount of your first gift (in dollars or other unit of measurement).
	+ **First Given Frequency**: Select the frequency of your first gift (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Once, Other, or Never).
* **Last Given**:
	+ **Last Given Date**: Enter the date of your last gift.
	+ **Last Given Amount**: Enter the amount of your last gift (in dollars or other unit of measurement).
	+ **Last Given Frequency**: Select the frequency of your last gift (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Once, Other, or Never).
* **Total Given**:
	+ **Total Given Frequency**: Select the frequency of your total given (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Once, Other, or Never).
	+ **Total Given Amount**: Enter the total amount given (in dollars or other unit of measurement).
	+ **Total Donations**: Enter the total number of donations made.
* **Frequency Given**:
	+ **Average Gift Amount**: Select the unit of measurement for the average gift amount (e.g. dollars).
	+ **Average Gift Frequency**: Select the frequency for the average gift given (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Amount Given**:
	+ **Amount Given Frequency**: Select how often you give the amount (More often, Less often, About equally, No change).
	+ **Total Amount Given**: Enter the total amount given (in dollars or other unit of measurement).

## Tips

* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Other" or "Unknown" and provide a brief explanation.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
