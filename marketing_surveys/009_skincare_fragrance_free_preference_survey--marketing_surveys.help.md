# Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about customers' preferences regarding skincare products and their skin type and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introductory text on the first page.
2. Answer each question carefully, making sure to select the most accurate options that apply to you.
3. For select-one questions, choose only one option.
4. For select-multiple questions, choose as many options as apply to you.
5. Submit the form when you have finished.
