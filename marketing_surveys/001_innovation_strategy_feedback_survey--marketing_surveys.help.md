# Innovation Strategy Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Innovation Strategy Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from Sales, Marketing, and Product Development departments on their current innovation strategy. This survey will help the company understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant category for your department (Marketing, Product Development, or Sales).
2. Enter your name and department name in the provided text fields.
3. Provide your contact information (email and phone number) so we can reach out to you with follow-up questions.
4. Share your feedback and suggestions on the current innovation strategy.
5. Suggest any new ideas or improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Marketing, Product Development, or Sales)**: Select the department you belong to. This will help us understand which department's feedback we are collecting.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Department**: Select one or more departments that you are a part of. This will help us understand your perspective on multiple departments' innovation strategies.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address so we can contact you with follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number so we can contact you with follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback further.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on the current innovation strategy. Be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples.
* **Suggestion**: Share any new ideas or improvements you think will enhance the innovation strategy.

## Tips

* Please be as concise and clear as possible in your feedback and suggestions.
* Please do not provide confidential or sensitive information.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at [insert contact email or phone number].
* The survey responses will be reviewed and used to improve the company's innovation strategy, and we appreciate your input.
