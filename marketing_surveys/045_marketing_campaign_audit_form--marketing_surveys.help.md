**Marketing Campaign Audit Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Marketing Campaign Audit Form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness and performance of a marketing campaign. It helps to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, and provides valuable insights for improvement and future planning. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as "Required". 
2.  For fields that are not marked as "Required", provide any relevant information you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Campaign Strategy** (`campaign_strategy`, text, Required):
    This field is for a brief description of your campaign's overall strategy. 
- **Marketing Channels Used** (`marketing_channels_used`, select_multiple, Optional):
    Check all the marketing channels you used for your campaign, e.g., social media, email, print, etc. 
- **Marketing Channels Used Options** (`marketing_channels_used_options`, select_one, Required):
    Select one of the options you checked in "Marketing Channels Used". 
- **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, number, Required):
    Enter the length of your campaign in days. 
- **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, Required):
    Specify who your campaign was aimed at, e.g., customers, partners, or the general public. 
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional):
    Share any comments or suggestions about your campaign. 

## Tips
* Make sure to be as descriptive as possible in the "Campaign Strategy" field, as it will help in understanding your campaign's core goals and objectives. 
* For "Marketing Channels Used" and "Marketing Channels Used Options", be specific and accurate, as it will help in identifying what worked best and what didn't. 
* "Campaign Duration" should only include the number of days. 
* "Target Audience" should be as clear as possible to ensure the campaign's goals are well-aligned with the target audience. 
* "Feedback" is an open-ended field, but try to be constructive and suggest possible improvements.
