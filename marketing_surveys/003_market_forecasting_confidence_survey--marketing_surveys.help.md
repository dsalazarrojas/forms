# Market Forecasting Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your confidence and opinion on market forecasting, providing a snapshot of the market's trends, predictions, and overall score. It's intended for stakeholders to share their insights and sentiments on the market's current state and future prospects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as thoroughly and truthfully as possible.
2. Use the space provided for each question to type your response.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can leave it blank or add a note in the "Additional Comments" section.
4. Once you've completed all the questions, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market Forecasting Confidence Survey** (`market_forecasting_confidence_survey`, text, required): A brief statement about your confidence level in the current market trends.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Your name, email address, and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required): Your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional information you'd like to share about your confidence level or the market.
* **Stakeholder Comments** (`stakeholder_comments`, text, optional): Comments or feedback from stakeholders you've spoken with or observed.
* **Market Forecast** (`market_forecast`, text, required): A brief statement about your market prediction or forecast.
* **Stakeholder Preferences** (`stakeholder_preferences`, text, required): A statement about your preferred method of communication with stakeholders.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, text, required): A numerical score from 1-10 indicating your confidence level in the market.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
* If you're unsure or lack information, it's okay to leave it blank or add a note.
* Remember to submit the form once you've completed all the questions.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
