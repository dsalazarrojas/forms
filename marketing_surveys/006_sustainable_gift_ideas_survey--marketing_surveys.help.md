# Sustainable Gift Ideas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your preferences and behaviors related to sustainable gifts. Your input will help us identify trends and patterns in gift-giving habits and inform our marketing strategies and product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your preferred type of gift (Gift, Experience, Money, or Other).
2. If you selected "Other," please specify the type of gift you prefer.
3. Choose what motivates you to give sustainable gifts (Environmentally friendly, Personal connection, Social responsibility, or Other).
4. Select multiple ideas for sustainable gifts you would consider (Eco-friendly household products, Sustainable clothing, Renewable energy, Second-hand products, or Other).
5. If you selected "Other," please specify your gift ideas.
6. Indicate how often you give gifts (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
7. If you selected "Other," please specify your gift frequency.
8. Enter your age.
9. If you have any comments or questions, please use the text field at the end of the form.
10. Finally, select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail, or Other) and provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of gift do you prefer?** (`gift_preferences`, select_one): Choose the type of gift you prefer. Options include Gift, Experience, Money, and Other.
* **If other, please specify** (`gift_preferences_other`, text): If you selected Other, please describe the type of gift you prefer.
* **What motivates you to give sustainable gifts?** (`motivation`, select_multiple): Select the motivations that drive you to give sustainable gifts. Options include Environmentally friendly, Personal connection, Social responsibility, and Other.
* **What are some ideas for sustainable gifts?** (`ideas`, select_multiple): Choose multiple ideas for sustainable gifts you would consider. Options include Eco-friendly household products, Sustainable clothing, Renewable energy, Second-hand products, and Other.
* **Ideas Other** (`ideas_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify your gift ideas.
* **How often do you give gifts?** (`frequency`, select_one): Indicate how often you give gifts. Options include Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, and Never.
* **Frequency Other** (`frequency_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify your gift frequency.
* **What is your age?** (`demographics`, number): Enter your age.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **What method would you like us to contact you through?** (`contact_method`, select_one): Choose how you would like to be contacted. Options include Email, Phone, Mail, and Other.
* **Contact Method Other** (`contact_method_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify how you would like to be contacted.
* **Comments or questions?** (`comments`, text): If you have any comments or questions, please use this text field.
* **Submission** (`submission`, date): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time of your submission.
* **Time of submission** (`submission_time`, time): This field will be automatically populated with the time of your submission.
* **How did you submit this form?** (`submission_method`, select_one): Choose how you submitted this form. Options include Online and In-person.
* **Submission Method Other** (`submission_method_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify how you submitted this form.
* **What type of survey is this?** (`survey_type`, select_one): Choose the type of survey this is. Options include Marketing Survey and Research Survey.
* **Survey Type Other** (`survey_type_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify the type of survey this is.
* **What is the source of this survey?** (`survey_source`, select_one): Choose the source of this survey. Options include Social Media, Email, and Website.
* **Survey Source Other** (`survey_source_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify the source of this survey.
* **Which company would you like to contact?** (`company`, text): Enter the name of the company you would like to contact.
* **Company Other** (`company_other`, text): If you selected Other, please specify the company you would like to contact.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add?** (`submission_confirmation`, text): If you have any additional comments or questions, please use this text field.
