# Advertising Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from respondents about their experience with advertising, including their industry, channels used, campaign effectiveness, and budget. This data will help us understand the current state of advertising practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Respondent Name** if you'd like to remain anonymous or if you've been invited to participate with your name.
2. Enter your **Organization** name, if applicable.
3. Select your **Industry**, which will help us understand where you're coming from.
4. If applicable, enter your years of **Experience with Advertising**.
5. Choose all the **Channels Used** that apply to you in the past 12 months.
6. Rate the effectiveness of your typical **Campaign Effectiveness** by choosing one of the provided options.
7. Check any **Measurement Tools** that you use to track campaigns.
8. If applicable, enter your **Typical Monthly Advertising Budget** range.
9. If you'd like, describe the **Areas for Improvement** in your advertising results.
10. Check the box to **Agree to be contacted for follow up**, so we can get back to you with more questions or discuss further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name for identification purposes, if you'd like.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, please enter their name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Choose your area of expertise or the sector you work in.
  * Retail
  * Technology
  * Healthcare
  * Finance
  * Education
  * Other
* **Experience with Advertising** (`ad_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've worked with advertising.
* **Channels Used** (`channels_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all channels you've used in the past 12 months.
  * Social Media
  * Search
  * Display
  * Email
  * Offline
* **Campaign Effectiveness Rating** (`campaign_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the typical success of your campaigns.
  * Poor
  * Fair
  * Good
  * Very Good
  * Excellent
* **Measurement Tools** (`measurement_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Check which tools you use to track campaigns.
  * Google Analytics
  * Facebook Insights
  * Inhouse
  * Other
* **Typical Monthly Advertising Budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Enter the approximate range of your monthly advertising budget.
  * Under 1k
  * 1k to 5k
  * 5k to 20k
  * Over 20k
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Share any areas where you think we could improve our advertising practices.
* **Permission for Follow up** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, required): Please agree or disagree to be contacted for follow-up discussions.
