# Perfume Box - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Perfume Box" form is designed to collect information for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (form_title)** (`text`, `required`): Enter a brief title for the product or service you are interested in.
* **Description (form_description)** (`text`, `optional`): Provide a short description of the product or service.
* **Category (form_category)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select a relevant category for your product or service (e.g., Marketing, Other).
* **Subscription Plan (form_subscription_plan)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Choose one or more plans for your subscription (e.g., Monthly, One Time).
* **Payment Type (form_payment_type)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the type of payment you would like to make (e.g., Subscription, One Time, Payment Plan).
* **Payment Date (form_payment_date)** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date you would like to make the payment, if applicable.
* **Payment Amount (form_payment_amount)** (`number`, `required`): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Payment Method (form_payment_method)** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose one or more methods for payment (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **Email (form_email)** (`email`, `required`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone (form_phone)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number.
* **Note (form_note)** (`note`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
