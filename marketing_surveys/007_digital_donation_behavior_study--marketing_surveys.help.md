# Digital Donation Behavior Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Donation Behavior Study is a research project designed to gather information from donors about their online donation habits and preferences. By participating in this study, you will help us understand how donors interact with our online donation platform and provide valuable insights to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Gather Your Information
Please provide your Donor ID, email address, and other relevant details about your donations. Ensure that all the required fields (marked as "required") are completed before proceeding.

### Step 2: Answer Questions Accurately
For each question, provide honest and accurate information about your digital donation behavior. If you're unsure about any question, you can always ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Donor ID** (`donor_id`, number, required: false)
Enter your donor ID, if you have one.

### 2. **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address associated with your donations.

### 3. **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false)
Enter the amount of your last donation.

### 4. **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required: false)
Select how often you make donations:
*   Monthly
*   Quarterly
*   Bi-Annually
*   Never

### 5. **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the methods you use for making donations:
*   Online
*   Mobile
*   Mail
*   In-person

### 6. **Donation Reason** (`donation_reason`, note, required: false)
Enter your reason for making donations.

### 7. **Favorite Charity** (`favorite_charity`, select_one, required: false)
Select your favorite charity:
*   Charity 1
*   Charity 2
*   Charity 3
*   Charity 4

### 8. **Other (please specify)** (`favorite_charity_other`, text, required: false)
If your favorite charity is not listed, please specify it here.

### 9-22. **Survey Details** (`survey_**`, various, required: false)
The next several fields are for survey details and might be irrelevant for most users, so please only provide the required information:
*   **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required: false)
    *   The date you filled out this form.
*   **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, required: false)
    *   The time you filled out this form.
*   **Survey Location** (`survey_location`, text, required: false)
    *   The location where you're filling out this form.
*   **Survey Device** (`survey_device`, select_one, required: false)
    *   The device you're using to fill out this form:
        +   Computer
        +   Phone
        +   Tablet
        +   Other
*   **Survey Browser** (`survey_browser`, select_one, required: false)
    *   The browser you're using:
        +   Google Chrome
        +   Mozilla Firefox
        +   Microsoft Edge
        +   Safari
        +   Other
*   **Survey OS** (`survey_os`, select_one, required: false)
    *   The operating system you're using:
        +   Windows
        +   Linux
        +   MacOS
        +   Other
*   **Survey IP Address** (`survey_ip_address`, text, required: false)
    *   The IP address of the device you're using.
*   **Survey Country** (`survey_country`, text, required: false)
    *   The country where you're filling out this form.
*   **Survey State** (`survey_state`, text, required: false)
    *   The state where you're filling out this form.
*   **Survey ZIP** (`survey_zip`, text, required: false)
    *   The ZIP code where you're filling out this form.
*   **Survey IP Geolocation** (`survey_ip_geolocation`, text, required: false)

### Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed.
*   Answer questions accurately and honestly.
