Nail Salon Social Media Engagement Survey - Help Guide
==============================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the social media engagement of nail salons, allowing us to better understand their online presence and create more effective marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer accurately.
2.  Fill out all the required fields.
3.  If a field is not relevant to your business, please leave it blank.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: `client_name` - The name of the client or business owner.
* **Social Media Use**: `social_media_use` - A select option field asking the client to choose which social media platform they use (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Other).
* **Social Media Preferred**: `social_media_preferred` - A multi-select field allowing the client to choose which social media platforms they prefer.
* **Social Media Use Frequency**: `social_media_use_frequency` - A select option field asking the client to choose how often they use social media (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Social Media Engagement**: `social_media_engagement` - A select option field asking the client to rate their social media engagement (High, Medium, Low).
* **Social Media Goals**: `social_media_goals` - A multi-select field asking the client to choose their social media goals (Increase followers, Engagement, Awareness, Other).
* **Email**: `email` - A field asking for the client's email address.
* **Phone**: `phone` - A field asking for the client's phone number.
* **Note**: `note` - A free-text field for the client to add any additional comments.
* **Salon Name**: `salon_name` - The name of the salon.
* **Salon Address**: `salon_address` - The address of the salon.
* **Salon Phone**: `salon_phone` - The phone number of the salon.
* **Salon Email**: `salon_email` - The email address of the salon.
* **Social Media Handle**: `social_media_handle` - A field asking for the client's social media handle.
* **Social Media Platforms**: `social_media_platforms` - A multi-select field asking the client to choose which social media platforms they use (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Other).
* **Social Media Goals Objective**: `social_media_goals_objective` - A select option field asking the client to state their social media goals (To increase followers, To increase engagement, To create awareness).
*   **Social Media Platforms Other**: `social_media_platforms_other` - A field asking the client to describe their social media platforms if they choose "Other" from the previous field.
* **Social Media Preferred Preferred**: `social_media_preferred_preferred` - A field asking the client to describe their social media platform preferences.
* **Email Opt-in**: `email_opt_in` - A select option field asking the client if they want to receive emails from us.
* **Phone Opt-in**: `phone_opt_in` - A select option field asking the client if they want to receive phone calls from us.
* **Note Opt-in**: `note_opt_in` - A select option field asking the client if they want to receive notes from us.
* **Submission Date**: `submission_date` - A field asking for the date of submission.

Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to help us better understand your social media engagement.
