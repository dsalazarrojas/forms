# Novel Search Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a novel they have recently read. It aims to measure their satisfaction level with the novel and to gather insights on the challenges they faced while reading it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button when you have finished filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred genres from the list.
4. Describe your favorite character in the novel.
5. Provide any challenges you faced while reading the novel.
6. Rate your satisfaction level with the novel.
7. Explain how you discovered the novel.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (novel_search_survey_form): Enter the title of the novel you have recently read.
* **Preferred Genres** (preferred_genres): Select all the genres you enjoy reading, such as fiction, nonfiction, etc.
* (No explanation for this field as it's a multiple select)
* **Challenges Discovered Novel** (challenges_discovered_novel): Describe any challenges you discovered while reading the novel.
* **Challenges Discovering Novel** (challenges_discovery_process_novel): Describe any challenges you faced while discovering the novel.
* **Satisfaction Level Novel** (satisfaction_level_novel): Rate your level of satisfaction with the novel. Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Challenges Discovery Process Novel** (challenges_discovery_process_novel): Describe any challenges you faced in the discovery process of the novel.
* **How Discovered Novel** (how_discovered_novel): Explain how you discovered the novel.
* **Favorite Character Novel** (favorite_character_novel): Enter the name of your favorite character in the novel.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to mention them in the "Additional Comments" field.
