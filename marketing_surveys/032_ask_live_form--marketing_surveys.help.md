# Ask Live Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your preferences for participating in live events. Your input will help us tailor our content and make it more engaging for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Enter your name or pseudonym in the "Your Name or Pseudonym" field. This is how we will address you.
*   Select how you would describe yourself from the options provided in the "How would you describe yourself" dropdown list.
*   If you have a favorite way to connect with new people, you can enter it in the "What is your favorite way to connect with new people" field. This is optional.
*   If you have ever participated in a live Q&A session, select "Yes, as a participant" or "Yes, as a host" in the "Have you ever participated in a live Q&A session" dropdown list.
*   If you have a question you have always wanted to ask a stranger, type it in the "What is one question you have always wanted to ask a stranger" field.
*   Select your preferred mode of communication for live interactions in the "Preferred Communication Mode" dropdown list.
*   Decide if you would be willing to join a live stream by selecting "Yes, I would love to" or "Maybe, with more info" in the "Would you be willing to join a live stream" dropdown list.
*   Choose the topics that interest you most in the "Which topics do you find most engaging" multiselect field.
*   If you have any social anxieties or preferences we should be aware of, you can provide them in the "Any social anxieties or preferences we should be aware of" field. This is optional.
*   Finally, enter an email address that we can use to send event invitations if you select "Yes, I would love to" for the "Would you be willing to join a live stream" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name or Pseudonym (User Nickname Live)**: This is how we will address you. Please enter your name or a pseudonym you would like to be known by.
*   **How would you describe yourself (Personality Type Live)**: Select how you would describe yourself to help us understand your personality and preferences.
*   **What is your favorite way to connect with new people**: If you have a preferred way to connect with others, enter it here. This is optional and will help us tailor our content for you.
*   **Have you ever participated in a live Q and A session**: If you have, select "Yes, as a participant" or "Yes, as a host".
*   **What is one question you have always wanted to ask a stranger**: Type in the question that you have always wanted to ask someone new.
*   **Preferred Communication Mode (Communication Pref Live)**: Choose how you prefer to communicate with others.
*   **Would you be willing to join a live stream**: Select "Yes, I would love to" if you are willing, "Maybe, with more info" if you need more details, or "No, I prefer to watch" if you do not want to participate.
*   **Which topics do you find most engaging (Engaging Topics Live)**: Choose the topics that interest you most.
*   **Any social anxieties or preferences we should be aware of**: If you have any concerns, you can provide them here. This is optional.
*   **Email for Event Invitation (Invite Email Live)**: Enter an email address where you can receive event invitations if you select "Yes, I would love to" for the "Would you be willing to join a live stream" field.
