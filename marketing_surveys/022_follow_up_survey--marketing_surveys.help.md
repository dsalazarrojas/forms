# Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers after an interaction or service has been provided. It helps us understand their satisfaction and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Rate your experience by selecting an option from the `Rating` field.
3. Provide any comments about your experience in the `Comments` field.
4. If you have a website, enter it in the `Company Website` field.
5. If you have a company address, enter it in the `Company Address` field.
6. If you would like to leave a review, enter it in the `Review` field.
7. Enter your company's phone number in the `Company Phone` field.
8. Enter your company's email address in the `Company Email` field.
9. Select the person assigned to follow-up with you from the options provided in the `Assigned Person` field.
10. Enter the email address of the assigned person in the `Assigned Person Email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Rating** (select_one, required): Select the rating that best represents your experience.
* **Comments** (text, required): Share any comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Customer Email** (email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Company Website** (text, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL.
* **Company Address** (text, optional): If you have a company address, enter it here.
* **Review** (text, optional): Share a review of your experience.
* **Company Phone** (text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Email** (text, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Assigned Person** (select_one, optional): Select the person assigned to follow-up with you.
* **Assigned Person Email** (text, optional): Enter the email address of the assigned person.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
