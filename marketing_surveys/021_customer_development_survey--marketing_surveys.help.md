# Customer Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information about your interactions with our company, helping us understand your needs and preferences to better serve you.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, please fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Next, select the methods through which you discovered our company (social media, referral, or online search).
3. Enter your email address for us to contact you.
4. Provide your phone number if you would like us to have it on file.
5. If you have any additional information about your interactions with our company, please use the text area for notes.
6. Answer the questions regarding your purchase history and most recent contact with us.
7. Describe your experience with our product or service.
8. Let us know your current level of satisfaction with our product or service.
9. Finally, choose whether or not you would like us to follow up with you about your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you about your survey responses and any other company updates.
* **How did you discover our company** (`contact_method`): Select all the methods through which you found out about our company, including social media, referral, or online search.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): If you have a phone number you would like us to have on file, please enter it here.
* **Additional information** (`note`): Use this text area to provide any additional context or details about your interactions with our company.
* **How many times have you purchased from us in the past 6 months?** (`purchase_frequency`): Enter the number of times you have purchased from us in the past 6 months.
* **When was your most recent contact with us?** (`recent_contact_time`): Choose the date of your most recent contact with our company.
* **What time of day did your most recent contact with us occur?** (`recent_contact_time_2`): Select the time of day for your most recent contact with our company.
* **How many products/services have you purchased from us in the past 2 years?** (`product_purchase_count`): Enter the number of products or services you have purchased from us in the past 2 years.
* **Can you tell us about your experience with our service/product?** (`experience_with_product`): Describe your experience with our product or service.
* **What is your current level of satisfaction with our service/product?** (`satisfaction_level`): Select your current level of satisfaction, ranging from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Can we follow up with you about your experience?** (`follow_up`): Choose whether or not you would like us to follow up with you about your experience.
