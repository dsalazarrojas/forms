# Post Party Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests who attended a party. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the party and help us understand what worked well and what did not work well.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the event you attended.
2. Select the date and time of the event.
3. Enter the location of the event.
4. Choose the type of party you attended (e.g. Birthday party, wedding party, etc.).
5. Indicate whether you had a positive, negative, or neutral experience at the event.
6. Rate your overall experience at the event (if applicable).
7. If you attended the event with any guests, please list their names.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you attended, such as "John's Birthday Party".
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the event took place.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of day when the event took place.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the physical location of the event, such as "123 Main St".
* **Party Type** (`party_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of party you attended from the list of options (Birthday, Wedding, etc.).
* **Guest Feedback** (`guest_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you had a Positive, Negative, or Neutral experience at the event.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If you feel comfortable giving a rating, rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Event Attendants** (`event_attendants`, text, optional): If you attended the event with any guests, please list their names.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Guest Feedback" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us directly.
* This form is intended for guests, so please do not fill it out if you are the party host or organizer.
* If you have already provided feedback for this event, you do not need to fill out this form again.
