# Market Driver Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Driver Analysis Survey is used to collect data on the factors that drive market performance and inform strategic decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the survey for each market segment.
2. Enter the survey name in the "Survey Name" field.
3. Select the relevant market segment from the list provided.
4. Describe the impact of the market driver in the "Impact Description" field.
5. Rank the priority of the market driver in the "Priority Ranking" field.
6. Provide a description of the priority in the "Priority Description" field.
7. Answer the questions and provide the necessary information in each field, following the instructions provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Name** (survey_name, text, required: false): Enter a name for the survey to reference later.
* **Market Segment** (market_segment, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant market segment from the list provided.
* **Impact Description** (impact_description, text, required: false): Describe the impact of the market driver.
* **Impact Score** (impact_score, number, required: false): Enter a score to indicate the impact of the market driver.
* **Impact** (impact, select_one, required: false): Select the relevant impact from the list provided.
* **Drivers** (drivers, select_multiple, required: false): Select the drivers that have an impact on the market.
* **Impact Description 2** (impact_description2, text, required: false): Describe the impact of the market driver.
* **Priority Ranking** (priority_ranking, select_one, required: false): Rank the priority of the market driver.
* **Priority Description** (priority_description, text, required: false): Describe the priority of the market driver.

## Tips
* Please fill out the survey for each market segment to avoid duplication of effort.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* Please note that this survey is designed to collect data on the factors that drive market performance and inform strategic decisions.
