# World Cup 2026 Viewing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your viewing habits and preferences for the World Cup 2026. The survey aims to gather data on how you plan to watch the tournament, which platforms you will use, and how you will engage with the event. Your input will help us better understand the audience's behavior and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select your preferred television channel to watch the World Cup 2026 from the options provided.
2. Choose the viewing plan you have purchased for the tournament (if any).
3. Indicate on which platform(s) you will be watching the World Cup 2026.
4. Rate your excitement level for the upcoming World Cup 2026 on a scale of 1-10.
5. Answer whether you will be watching with friends and family.
6. Select your favorite team to win the tournament (if you have one).
7. Answer whether you will be watching with children.
8. Choose your favorite player(s) (if you have one).
9. Indicate if you plan to share your viewing experience on social media.
10. Answer whether you will participate in a discussion about the World Cup 2026.
11. Add any other comments or feedback you would like to share.
12. Enter your date of birth.
13. Provide your email address.
14. Enter your phone number.
