# Colorways Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is the Colorways Knowledge Survey, designed to collect information from users about their familiarity, frequency of use, and preferences related to colorways. The purpose of this survey is to gain insights into user behavior and preferences, which can help us improve our services and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer them accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your Respondent name, which is your name.
3. Rate your familiarity with colorways on a scale of 1 to 5.
4. Select the frequency of use of colorways for your projects.
5. Choose the tools you use for colorway creation.
6. Describe any top pain points or challenges you face when using colorways.
7. Indicate if you need training on colorways.
8. Select your preferred content format for training.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback in the Additional comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (Respondent name, text, optional): Your name as the respondent.
* **Role in design or marketing** (Role in design or marketing, text, optional): Your job title or role in design or marketing.
* **Familiarity with colorways** (Familiarity with colorways, select_one, required): Rate your familiarity with colorways on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Frequency of use of colorways** (Frequency of use of colorways, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of use of colorways for your projects.
* **Tools used for colorway creation** (Tools used for colorway creation, select_multiple, optional): Select all the tools you use for colorway creation.
* **Top pain points** (Top pain points, text, optional): Describe any main challenges or pain points you face when using colorways.
* **Need for training** (Need for training, select_one, optional): Indicate if you need training on colorways.
* **Preferred content format for training** (Preferred content format for training, select_one, optional): Select your preferred content format for training.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
