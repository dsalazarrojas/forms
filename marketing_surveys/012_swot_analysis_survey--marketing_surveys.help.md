# SWOT Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SWOT Analysis Survey is a tool used to identify and analyze a company's internal and external factors that may affect its performance. It helps businesses develop strategies to leverage their strengths, address their weaknesses, capitalize on opportunities, and mitigate threats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully and think about how it relates to your company's current situation.
3. Don't overthink the form; provide your initial thoughts and come back later to refine them if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (`Company Name`, text, optional)
* This is a field where you can input your company's name as it appears on your business cards, website, or other official documents.

### Company Address (`Company Address`, text, optional)
* This is a field to input your company's physical or mailing address where your business operates.

### Company Strengths (`Company Strength`, text, optional)
* Think about what your company does well. What are your company's key competencies, skills, and advantages? Be specific and provide examples.

### Company Weaknesses (`Company Weakness`, text, optional)
* Think about what your company struggles with. What are your company's limitations, areas where you need improvement, or vulnerabilities? Be specific and provide examples.

### Opportunities (`Opportunities`, text, optional)
* Think about areas where you can grow your business. What new markets, products, or services can you offer? What trends or innovations can you capitalize on?

### Threats (`Threats`, text, optional)
* Think about potential challenges or obstacles that might affect your business. What external factors, competitors, or changes in the market can impact your company's performance?

### SWOT Description (`SWOT Description`, text, optional)
* Provide a brief description of your company's SWOT analysis. This can be a summary of your findings or a plan for how you intend to address your strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

### Submit (`Submit`, text, optional)
* This is the button to submit your answers. Once you have completed the form, click this button to send your SWOT analysis to the relevant stakeholders.
