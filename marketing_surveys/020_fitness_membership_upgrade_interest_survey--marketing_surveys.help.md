# Fitness Membership Upgrade Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Fitness Membership Upgrade Interest Survey, a tool designed to help us understand your interests and preferences for upgrading your membership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: First Name, Email, and Category.
2. In the "What motivates you to upgrade your membership?" field, select the option that best describes your reason for upgrading. If none of the provided options match your reason, you can choose "Other (please specify)" and provide your own explanation.
3. In the "Which classes interest you the most?" field, select all that apply. You can choose from the provided options, or type in "Other (please specify)" if none of the options match your interests.
4. Fill out the "Additional Comments" field with any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
5. If you have a specific start date and time in mind, you can fill out the "Potential Start Date" and "Potential Start Time" fields. However, these fields are optional and only required if you plan to start your upgraded membership on a specific date and time.
6. Lastly, fill out the "Tool Used" field with the tool you currently use for your membership, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your membership account.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address associated with your membership account.
* **Category** (required): Enter a category that best describes your interests (e.g., Cardio, Strength Training, Yoga).
* **What motivates you to upgrade your membership?** (required): This field allows you to select one reason why you want to upgrade your membership. Choose from:
	+ To take advantage of additional features
	+ To save money on a monthly basis
	+ To have access to a wider range of classes
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Which classes interest you the most?** (required): Select all classes that interest you, choosing from:
	+ Cardio
	+ Strength Training
	+ Yoga
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If you have any other information you'd like to share with us, feel free to enter it in this field.
* **Potential Start Date** (optional): If you have a specific start date in mind for your upgraded membership, enter it here.
* **Potential Start Time** (optional): If you have a specific start time in mind for your upgraded membership, enter it here.
* **Tool Used** (optional): If you currently use a tool for your membership, enter the name of that tool.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or promotions.
