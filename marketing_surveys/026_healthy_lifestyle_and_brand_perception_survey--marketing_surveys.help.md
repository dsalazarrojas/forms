# Healthy Lifestyle And Brand Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your thoughts and opinions about your healthy lifestyle habits and your perception of our brand.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page and review the fields provided. Each field is clearly labeled with its name and type.
- Fill in your answers carefully, and if you have any additional comments, please use the text area at the end of the form.
