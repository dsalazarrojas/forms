# User Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and ratings on products or services that you have tested. Your input is valuable in helping us understand what works and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Complete each page of the form, starting from "What is this survey for".
* Answer each question or select an option according to your experience and opinion.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (*).
* When finished, select "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey for** (`1`, `text`, required): This question is an introductory question that explains the purpose of the survey. Please provide a brief answer explaining what you were testing or experiencing when you filled out this form.
* **User Input** (`2`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Product Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the product or service you have tested.
* **Rating** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the options that best describe your experience with the product or service.
*	* Option 1
*	* Option 2
*	* Option 3
* **Comments** (`5`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or observations about the product or service, please use this field to share them.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, optional): Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`8`, `date`, optional): Please provide the date you tested the product or service.
* **Time** (`9`, `time`, optional): Please provide the time you tested the product or service.
* **Submit** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your experience with the product or service.

**Tips**:
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: The fields marked as `required` must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
