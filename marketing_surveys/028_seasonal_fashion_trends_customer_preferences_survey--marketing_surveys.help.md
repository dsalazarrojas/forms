# Seasonal Fashion Trends Customer Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer opinions and preferences on seasonal fashion trends, customer preferences, and customer service experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the season you are interested in (Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter).
2. Choose one or more fashion styles that appeal to you (Boho-Chic, Eco-Friendly, Streetwear, etc.).
3. Select your preferred type of fashion item (Top, Bottom, Shoes, etc.).
4. Choose your preferred color (Black/White, Red/Pink, Blue, etc.).
5. If you wish, input a price range that you are willing to pay for fashion items.
6. Describe your fashion interests in the text input field.
7. Select your customer preferences (New Arrivals, Discounts, Quality, etc.).
8. Rate your customer service experience (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. Finally, select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone Number, Social Media, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seasonal Fashion Trends** (`seasonal_fashion_trends`): Select the season you are interested in.
	+ Select one of the following: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.
* **Fashion Style** (`fashion_style`): Choose one or more fashion styles that appeal to you.
	+ Select one or more of the following: Boho-Chic, Eco-Friendly, Streetwear, Luxury, Rustic, Sustainable, Vintage, Modern, Classic, Casual, Glamour.
* **Preferred Fashion Item** (`preferred_fashion_item`): Select your preferred type of fashion item.
	+ Select one of the following: Top, Bottom, Shoes, Accessory, Jacket-Coat, Dress, Other (please specify).
* **Preferred Color** (`preferred_color`): Choose your preferred color.
	+ Select one of the following: Black/White, Red/Pink, Blue, Green, Yellow, Brown, Grey, Purple.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`): Input a price range you are willing to pay for fashion items.
	+ This is an optional field.
* **Fashion Interest** (`fashion_interest`): Describe your fashion interests in the text input field.
	+ This field is a text input field where you can describe your fashion interests.
* **Customer Preferences** (`customer_preferences`): Select your customer preferences.
	+ Select one or more of the following: New Arrivals, Discounts, Quality, Style, Price, Service, Brand, Sustainability.
* **Customer Service Experience** (`customer_service_experience`): Rate your customer service experience.
	+ Select one of the following: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Select how you would like to be contacted.
	+ Select one of the following: Email, Phone Number, Social Media, In-Store.
