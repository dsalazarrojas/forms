# Customer Interest Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Interest Profile form is used to collect relevant information from customers about their demographic and contact details. This data helps us better understand our customers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information about the customer before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Interest Profile** (`customer_interest_profile`, text, required): This field is used to describe the customer's interest profile.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required): Enter the customer's client information, such as their company name or contact details.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, required): Enter the customer's surname or family name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the customer's home or office address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address (username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number. Use international format (e.g., +1234567890).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the customer's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 12/25/1999).

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, double-check the customer's information or ask for clarification.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact your supervisor or our support team.
