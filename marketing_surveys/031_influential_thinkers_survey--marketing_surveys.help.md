## Purpose
The Influential Thinkers Survey is designed to collect information about the influence of leaders and inspirers in the workplace. The survey consists of multiple-choice and text-based questions that help assess the impact of these individuals on employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Select the relevant options for each question or provide a detailed explanation in the text-based fields.
3. Ensure you have first-hand experience with the questions being asked before providing an answer.
4. Complete all the questions before submitting the survey.
