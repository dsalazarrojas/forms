# Retail Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Retail Market Research Survey is designed to gather information from end users about their shopping habits and preferences in the retail market. The data collected will be used to inform future marketing strategies and improve customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, as your participation is greatly appreciated.
3. For select_one fields (e.g., demographic_info, product_category, shopping_frequency, satisfaction, contact_method), choose the option that best matches your answer.
4. For numerical fields (e.g., age), enter a number.
5. For the email field, enter your valid email address.
6. For the feedback field, provide as much detail as you like about your shopping experience.
7. You can skip any field that you do not feel is relevant to you.
8. Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_info** (`Male`, `Female`, `Other`, optional): Please select your gender.
* **age** (`number`, optional): Enter your age.
* **location** (`Online`, `Offline`, `Both`, optional): Where do you usually shop?
* **product_category** (`Electronics`, `Food`, `Other`, optional): What type of products do you usually shop for?
* **shopping_frequency** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, optional): How often do you shop?
* **rating** (`number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with our store on a scale of 1-10.
* **satisfaction** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`, optional): How satisfied are you with your shopping experience at our store?
* **contact_method** (`Phone`, `Email`, `In-person`, optional): How do you prefer to contact us?
* **feedback** (`text`, optional): Please provide any feedback you have about our store or shopping experience.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **phone_number** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **intro** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief introduction or a special request.
