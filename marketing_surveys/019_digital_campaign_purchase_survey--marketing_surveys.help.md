# Digital Campaign Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the effectiveness of digital campaigns in driving purchases. It is essential for us to understand your experience with our digital campaigns to improve our marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your purchase experience from the first page.
2. Provide your honest feedback on the second page. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your satisfaction.
3. On the third page, select any relevant channels through which you learned about our digital campaigns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Purchase
* **Purchase Option** (`select_one`, Required): This is where you need to choose the option that best describes your purchase experience. Please select the one that accurately reflects your situation.
	+ Option 1: You purchased the product/service directly from our website.
	+ Option 2: You purchased the product/service through a social media ad.
	+ Option 3: You purchased the product/service through a recommendation.

### Page 2: Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction Feedback** (`text`, Optional): This is where you can provide your honest feedback about your satisfaction with the digital campaign that led to your purchase.

### Page 3: Buying Channels
* **Channels** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Please select any channels through which you learned about our digital campaigns.
	+ Option 1: Social media
	+ Option 2: Email marketing
	+ Option 3: Online video advertisements
