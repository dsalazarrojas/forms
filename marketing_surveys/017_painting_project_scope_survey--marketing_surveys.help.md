# Painting Project Scope Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and requirements for a painting project. Please provide your input on the project scope, details, and other necessary information to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and provide your answers accordingly.
2. For each question, choose the most suitable answer from the provided options.
3. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Please read through this section carefully to understand the scope of the project.
* **Project Details** (`2`, `number`, optional): Enter the details of the project, if necessary.
* **What type of project is this?** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of project you are undertaking.
	+ Option 1: House painting
	+ Option 2: Office space painting
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Project Description** (`4`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Customer Information** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the relevant options that apply to your project.
	+ Option 1: Residential
	+ Option 2: Commercial
* **Scope of Work** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the scope of work required for your project.
	+ Option 1: Painting
	+ Option 2: Wallpapering
* **What painting services are required?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the painting services required for your project.
	+ Option 1: Exterior
	+ Option 2: Interior
* **Where will the painting be done?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the locations where the painting will be done.
	+ Option 1: Home
	+ Option 2: Office building
* **Estimate Details** (`9`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimate details, if necessary.
* **Budget** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Contact Person** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you would like to be contacted for any further information.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Email** (`12`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you would like to receive updates on the project.
* **Phone** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Message** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional message you would like to provide.
* **Submission** (`15`, `note`, optional): A note to confirm your submission of the form.
