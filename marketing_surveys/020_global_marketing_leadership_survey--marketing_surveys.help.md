# Global Marketing Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Marketing Leadership Survey is designed to gather information from marketing professionals about their experiences, challenges, and strategies related to leadership and global marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name and other contact information in the "First Name," "Last Name," "Job Title," "Department," "Company," "Job Function," and "Years in Field" fields.
2. In the "Strategies and Tactics" field, please describe your current strategies and tactics for marketing and leadership.
3. Answer the multiple-choice questions about your marketing budget, marketing growth, global perspectives, and leadership.
4. Select the options that best apply to your current situation in the "Marketing Media," "Data Visualization," "Data Sourcing," "Reporting," and "Collaboration" fields.
5. Please provide brief descriptions in the "Marketing Challenge," "Best Practices," "Innovation," "Communication Channels," "Technology," and "Data Quality" fields.
6. Select the options that best apply to your current situation in the "Data Driven" field.
7. Finally, provide your answer to the "Marketing Growth" question, selecting "High," "Medium," or "Low" based on your assessment.
