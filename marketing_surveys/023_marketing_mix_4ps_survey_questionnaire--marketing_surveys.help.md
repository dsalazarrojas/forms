# Marketing Mix (4Ps) Survey Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Mix (4Ps) Survey Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about the key aspects of marketing strategies: product, price, place, and promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the first four fields with brief descriptions of your product, pricing strategy, distribution channels, and promotional activities.
2.  Provide more detailed descriptions or breakdowns of each marketing aspect in the next four fields.
3.  For the last field, select one of the provided 4Ps options that best describes your current marketing situation.
4.  Enter a score out of (likely) 5 for your 4Ps, indicating your assessment of your marketing strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product** (`product`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing your product or service.
*   **Price** (`price`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing your pricing strategy.
*   **Place** (`place`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing your distribution channels or where your product or service is sold.
*   **Promotion** (`promotion`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing your promotional activities.
*   **Customer Perception** (`customer_perception`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing how customers perceive your product or service.
*   **Marketing Mix** (`marketing_mix`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for describing your marketing mix strategies.
*   **Product Breakdown** (`product_breakdown`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for a detailed breakdown of your product or service.
*   **Price Breakdown** (`price_breakdown`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for a detailed breakdown of your pricing strategy.
*   **Place Breakdown** (`place_breakdown`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for a detailed breakdown of your distribution channels.
*   **Promotion Breakdown** (`promotion_breakdown`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for a detailed breakdown of your promotional activities.
*   **Customer Perception Breakdown** (`customer_perception_breakdown`, `text`, required: false):
    *   This field is for a detailed breakdown of how customers perceive your product or service.
*   **4Ps** (`marketing_mix_4p`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   This field is for selecting one of the provided 4Ps options that best describes your current marketing situation.
*   **4Ps Score** (`marketing_mix_4p_score`, `number`, required: false):
    *   This field is for entering a score out of 5 for your 4Ps, indicating your assessment of your marketing strategy.

## Tips
*   Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
*   Remember to save your answers for future reference.
