# Beauty Brand Mission Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather insights into your awareness and perception of our beauty brand. It will help us understand how well you know our brand mission, values, and values alignment with your own. Your input will aid us in making informed decisions to improve our brand communication and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the corresponding information. Make sure to select only one option for questions that ask for a single selection and multiple options for questions that allow for multiple selections. Take your time, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help.
