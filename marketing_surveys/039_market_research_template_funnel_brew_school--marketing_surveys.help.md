# Market Research Template Funnel Brew School - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect market research data on the "Market Research Template Funnel Brew School" project. The data collected will help us to understand your preferences, habits, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your favorite option from the "Select" dropdown.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown.
7. Enter a date in the "Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter the time of day in the "Time" field in the format HH:MM.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Enter a decimal value in the "Decimal" field.
11. Enter an integer value in the "Integer" field.
12. Select your preferred funnel option from the "Funnel" dropdown.
13. Select multiple funnel options from the "Funnel Multiple" dropdown.
