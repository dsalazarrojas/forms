# price_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The price_survey form is used to collect information about products, including their names, types, price ranges, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the product name in the "Product Name" field. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have the product name.
2. Select the product type from the options provided in the "Product Type" field.
3. Choose the price range for the product from the "Price Range" field.
4. Enter the date of the transaction or the date you'd like to record in the "Date" field. This field is not required.
5. If you'd like to record a specific time, enter it in the "Time" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the product in the "Note" field.
7. If you have a personal email address associated with the product, enter it in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number associated with the product in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the product you're surveying.
* **Product Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of product (e.g., "Product A", "Product B", or "Product C") that best describes the product.
* **Price Range** (select_multiple, optional): Select the price range for the product (e.g., "Low", "Medium", or "High").
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the transaction or the date you'd like to record.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the specific time you'd like to record.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the product.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your personal email address associated with the product.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the product.
