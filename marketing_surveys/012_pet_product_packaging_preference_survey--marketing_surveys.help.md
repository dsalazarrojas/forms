# Pet Product Packaging Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Product Packaging Preference Survey form is designed to gather data from customers on their preferences regarding pet product packaging. This form will help the company to better understand customer needs and make informed decisions about future product packaging.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of pet product packaging.
2. Please select the option that best answers each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Once you have completed the form, click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Product Packaging Preference** (`pet_product_packaging_preference_survey_form`, text, required: false): This field is the main question of the form. Please answer the question in the provided text box.
* **What type of material do you prefer for pet food packaging?** (`packaging_materials`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options: Cardboard, Paper, or Plastic.
	+ Cardboard: I prefer cardboard packaging for its durability and cost-effectiveness.
	+ Paper: I prefer paper packaging for its biodegradable nature.
	+ Plastic: I prefer plastic packaging for its convenience and water resistance.
* **Which of the following is most important to you when making decisions about pet food packaging?** (`eco_friendly_priorities`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply:
	+ Reducing waste: I prioritize reducing waste when making decisions about pet food packaging.
	+ Environmental concerns: I consider the environmental impact when making decisions about pet food packaging.
	+ Cost: I prioritize cost when making decisions about pet food packaging.
	+ Convenience: I prioritize convenience when making decisions about pet food packaging.
* **How satisfied are you with the current packaging?** (`satisfaction`, number, required: false): Answer on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 5 is "extremely satisfied".
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?** (`additional_info`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.
