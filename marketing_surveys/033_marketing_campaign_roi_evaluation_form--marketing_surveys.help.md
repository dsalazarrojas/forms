# Marketing Campaign ROI Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Campaign ROI Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the performance of a marketing campaign by collecting relevant information about its objectives, duration, budget, target audience, channels, and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer each question according to your campaign's specifics.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Campaign ROI Evaluation Form** (`form_intro`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the campaign you're evaluating.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, optional): Describe the objective of the campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the campaign.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget of the campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Describe the target audience of the campaign.
* **Campaign Channels** (`campaign_channels`, text, optional): List the channels used to reach the target audience.
* **Campaign Strategy** (`campaign_strategy`, text, optional): Describe the strategy used to execute the campaign.
* **Campaign Performance** (`campaign_performance`, text, optional): Describe the performance of the campaign.
* **ROI** (`roi`, number, optional): Enter the return on investment of the campaign.
* **ROI Calculation Method** (`roi_calculation_method`, text, optional): Describe the method used to calculate the ROI.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the marketing channels used (Online, Offline, Social Media).
* **Marketing Channels 2** (`marketing_channels_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the marketing channels used (Email, SMS, Print).
* **Target Audience 2** (`target_audience_2`, select_one, optional): Select the target audience type (Young Adults, Seniors, Adults).
* **Campaign Channels 2** (`campaign_channels_2`, select_one, optional): Select the main channel used (Online, Offline).
* **Campaign Strategy 2** (`campaign_strategy_2`, select_one, optional): Select the main strategy used (ROI, Cost, Revenue).

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all relevant fields accurately and completely to get an accurate evaluation of your campaign's performance.
