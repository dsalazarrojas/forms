# Gaming Income Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Income Opportunities Survey is designed to collect information about your income opportunities through gaming. This survey will help us tailor our content and services to better serve the community's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and accurately. You can select one or more options for each question. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any other comments or suggestions?" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current income?** (`income_level`, number, required: false): This question asks about your current income level, which will help us understand your financial situation.
* **How do you earn money through gaming?** (`income_source`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to describe how you earn money through gaming (e.g., esports, streaming, YouTube, other).
* **What is your current income level?** (`income_level_breakdown`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your current income level, which will help us understand your financial situation.
* **What motivates you to play games?** (`income_motivation`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select one or more options from the list to describe what motivates you to play games (e.g., fun, escapist, competition, social).
* **Why do you play games?** (`income_reason`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your reasons for playing games, which will help us understand your gaming habits.
* **How often do you play games?** (`income_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to describe how often you play games (e.g., every day, several times a week, rarely).
* **How long do you play games each session?** (`income_duration`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the length of your gaming sessions.
* **How many hours do you spend playing games per week?** (`income_gaming_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to describe how many hours you spend playing games per week.
* **How much do you earn from gaming?** (`income_earnings`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your earnings from gaming.
* **Do you earn money from streaming?** (`income_streaming`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to answer this question.
* **Do you earn money from YouTube?** (`income_youtube`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your earnings from YouTube.
* **Do you earn money from other sources?** (`income_other`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to answer this question.
* **Other (Please specify)** (`income_motivation_other`, text, required: false): If you earn money from a source not listed, please describe it in this field.
* **How often do you do this other income activity?** (`income_frequency_other`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of your other income activity.
* **How long do you do this other income activity per session?** (`income_duration_other`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the length of your other income activity sessions.
* **How many hours do you spend doing this other income activity per week?** (`income_gaming_frequency_other`, select_one, required: false): Please select one option from the list to describe how many hours you spend on your other income activity per week.
* **How much do you earn from this other income activity?** (`income_earnings_other`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your earnings from your other income activity.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`introduction2`, note, required: false): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use this field to share them.

Note: If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact us through our support channel. We'll be happy to help you with any questions or clarifications.
