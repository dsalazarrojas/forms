# Online Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your online shopping habits and preferences. Your input will help us understand your shopping behavior and provide valuable insights to improve our online services. Please note that this form is intended for customers or users who have online shopping experiences with us, and your participation is completely voluntary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your valid **Email** address in the second field.
3. If you have a contact number, you can optionally enter it in the third field.
4. Indicate the type of store you shop at most often from the list in the fourth field.
5. Choose how often you **shop online** from the options in the fifth field.
6. Select how often you **make purchases online** from the list in the sixth field.
7. Enter the **average amount you spend online** in the seventh field.
8. Select the category of items you usually purchase online from the multiple options in the eighth field.
9. If you have any feedback or suggestions, you can type it in the ninth field.
10. Optionally, provide any additional comments about your online shopping experience in the tenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to associate with your online shopping experience.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you have a contact number, you can enter it here. This will help us reach you in case we need to follow up on your experience.
* **What type of store do you shop at most often?** (`store_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of stores you visit most frequently, such as Book store, Magazine store, Clothing store, or Online store.
* **How often do you shop online?** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you shop online, from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **How often do you make purchases online?** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you make purchases online, from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never.
* **What is the average amount you spend online?** (`purchase_amount`, number, required): Enter the average amount you spend online, including cents.
* **What category do you usually purchase online?** (`purchase_category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category of items you usually purchase online, such as Books, Clothing, Furniture, or Online courses.
* **Any feedback?** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any feedback or suggestions, type them here. This will help us improve our services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your online shopping experience.
