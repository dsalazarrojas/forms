# Real Estate Marketing Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from potential customers or clients for real estate marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can leave any field blank if it's not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address. This will be used as a contact method for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for the "Email" field.
* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unable to provide any of the requested information, simply leave the field blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
