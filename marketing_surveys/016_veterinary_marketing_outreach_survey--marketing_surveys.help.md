# Veterinary Marketing Outreach Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the veterinary industry about their marketing preferences and outreach channels. It will help us understand how to best reach and communicate with the industry members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
3. For select_multiple fields, you can choose more than one option if necessary.
4. Leave any comments or suggestions in the "Comments" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`): This field is an optional text input where you can provide any additional information or comments about yourself.
* **What are your marketing channels?** (`marketing_channels`): This field asks you to choose one or multiple marketing channels that you use to promote your products or services. Please select at least one option.
	+ Animal Health Industry Trade Association
	+ Association of Animal Health
	+ Online Advertising
	+ Social Media
	+ Trade Show
	+ Veterinary Association
* **How often do you check your emails?** (`email_frequency`): This field asks you to indicate how often you check your emails. Please select one option.
	+ 1-2 times a day
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ Daily
* **Do you use social media for marketing?** (`social_media`): This field asks you to indicate whether you use social media for marketing purposes. Please select one option.
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your age range?** (`age_range`): This field asks you to select one age range category.
	+ 20-29
	+ 30-39
	+ 40-49
	+ 50-59
	+ 60 or older
* **Do you prefer online advertising?** (`online_advertising`): This field asks you to indicate whether you prefer online advertising. Please select one option.
	+ True
	+ False
* **How many years of experience do you have in the veterinary field?** (`experience`): This field asks you to select a number of years.
* **Do you prefer to receive information through email or print media?** (`media_preference`): This field asks you to select one option.
	+ Email
	+ Print Media
* **How often do you visit trade shows?** (`trade_shows`): This field asks you to select one or multiple options.
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your current role in the veterinary industry?** (`role`): This field asks you to select one option.
	+ Veterinarian
	+ Veterinarian Assistant
	+ Pet Owner
	+ Industry Representative
	+ Other
* **Do you prefer to receive information through social media or online advertising?** (`social_media_ads`): This field asks you to select one option.
	+ Social Media
	+ Online Advertising
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`comments`): This field is an optional text input where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
