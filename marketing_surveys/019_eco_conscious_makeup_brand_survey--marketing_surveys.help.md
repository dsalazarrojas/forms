# Eco Conscious Makeup Brand Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather user input on their attitudes and behaviors related to eco-conscious makeup brands. It is part of an ongoing effort to better understand consumer perspectives and preferences in the beauty industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best fit your responses for each question. For multiple-choice questions, choose only one option that best matches your opinion or behavior. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you like in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Yes/No): Please select "Yes" if you identify as someone who is concerned about the environmental impact of the beauty industry. 
* **Product Usage** (Yes/No, multiple selection allowed): Select all the options that apply to your current product usage habits. For example, you might select "Yes" for "Yes, for face products" and "No" for "Yes, for hair care."
* **Eco Concerns** (Yes/No, multiple selection allowed): Choose all the options that you are concerned about related to the beauty industry and the environment.
* **Favorite Eco-Friendly Brand** (Yes/No): Select "Yes" if you have a favorite eco-friendly brand.
* **Eco Practices** (Open-ended): Please provide any comments or suggestions you have on how to improve the eco-consciousness of the beauty industry. This field is optional and open-ended.
* **Email** (Email address): Enter your email address so we can contact you with future surveys or updates.
* **Phone** (Phone number): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Notes** (Open-ended): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about this survey or the beauty industry in general.
