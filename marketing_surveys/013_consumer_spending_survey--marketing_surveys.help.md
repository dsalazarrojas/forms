# Consumer Spending Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Consumer Spending Survey, a form designed to collect information about your demographic details, spending habits, and online shopping behavior. Your feedback and responses will help us understand consumer behavior and inform future product development.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form in sections, starting with demographic information.
- Answer questions honestly, and choose the best option from the list.
- For numerical fields, enter numbers accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**1. **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Select your age group from the provided options (Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+).

**2. **Annual Household Income** (`annual_household_income`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Choose the approximate annual income range for your household.

**3. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Select your current employment status (Employed Full-time, Employed Part-time, Self-employed, Student, Retired, Unemployed).

**4. **Average Monthly Discretionary Spending** (`monthly_discretionary_spending`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Choose the approximate average monthly spending on non-essential items (Under $100, $100-$250, $251-$500, $501-$1,000, More than $1,000).

**5. **Categories Where You Spend the Most** (`categories_with_most_spending`, select_multiple, **Required**)
    - Select up to three categories where you spend the most (e.g., Groceries, Housing/Rent, Transportation).

**6. **Spending Change Over the Last Year** (`spending_change_last_year`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Indicate if your spending has increased, decreased, or stayed the same.

**7. **Reason for Spending Change** (`reason_for_spending_change`, text, **Optional**)
    - If you answered that your spending has changed, provide a reason for the change.

**8. **Frequency of Online Shopping** (`frequency_of_online_shopping`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Select how often you shop online (Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Rarely).

**9. **Preferred Online Shopping Platform** (`preferred_shopping_platform`, text, **Optional**)
    - If you shop online, mention your preferred platform.

**10. **Impact of Social Media Advertising** (`impact_of_social_media_ads`, select_one, **Required**)
    - Indicate the influence of social media ads on your purchases (Strongly Influence, Moderate Influence, Little Influence, No Influence).

**11. **Budget for Next Major Purchase** (`budget_for_next_major_purchase`, number, **Optional**)
    - If you're planning a major purchase, provide an estimated budget for it.

**12. **Factors Influencing Major Purchases** (`factors_influencing_major_purchase`, select_multiple, **Required**)
    - Select up to three factors that influence your major purchases (Price, Quality, Brand Reputation, Peer Reviews, Sustainability/Ethics, Warranty/Service).

**13. **Date of Completion** (`survey_completion_date`, date, **Required**)
    - Ensure that today's date is selected accurately.

# Tips
- Answer questions carefully, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any doubts.
- Please fill out the form entirely to help us gather complete and accurate insights.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
