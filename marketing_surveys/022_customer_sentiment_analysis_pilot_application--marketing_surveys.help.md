# Customer Sentiment Analysis Pilot Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer contact information and sentiment data for the Customer Sentiment Analysis Pilot Application. This data will help us understand customer opinions and feelings about our products or services. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the contact information section with your contact details.
2. Rate your sentiment on the Sentiment Scale.
3. Enter your Sentiment Score and Date/Time of the sentiment.
4. Add any additional Notes about your experience.
5. Choose who the form should be assigned to from the Assigned To dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, not required): Please enter your name, address, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Phone Number** (Contact Number, number, not required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Email** (Contact Email, email, not required): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Job Title** (Contact Title, text, not required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization** (Contact Organization, text, not required): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Department** (Contact Department, text, not required): Enter your department name.
* **Sentiment Scale** (Sentiment Scale, number, not required): Choose a score between 1 and 100 to rate your sentiment (e.g., 1 = Strongly Negative, 50 = Neutral, 100 = Strongly Positive).
* **Sentiment Score** (Sentiment Score, number, not required): Enter your sentiment score corresponding to the Sentiment Scale.
* **Sentiment Date** (Sentiment Date, date, not required): Enter the date of your sentiment.
* **Sentiment Time** (Sentiment Time, time, not required): Enter the time of your sentiment.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about your experience.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, not required): Choose who the form should be assigned to for further action.
