# Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have canceled their subscription. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the explanations provided below.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- You can skip questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why are you cancelling your subscription?**: This is a text field where you can explain your reasons for canceling your subscription. Please be specific and provide details about your experience.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the service before cancelling?**: This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the service before you canceled. Choose a rating from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Would you recommend the service to friends or family?**: This question asks about your willingness to recommend the service to others. Choose 'True', 'False', or 'no preference'.
* **What was the main reason for cancelling your subscription?**: This is a select multiple field where you can choose one or more reasons that led to your cancellation. Choose from 'billing', 'content', 'service quality', or 'other' (if your reason is not listed).
* **What could we do to improve the service?**: This is a text field where you can suggest improvements or changes we could make to the service.
* **What was the level of customer service you received while a subscriber?**: This is a text field where you can describe your experience with our customer service.
* **Were there any billing issues that led to your cancellation?**: This is a text field where you can mention any billing issues you encountered.
* **Were there any issues with the content provided by the service?**: This is a text field where you can mention any content-related problems you faced.
* **What was the quality of the service?**: This question asks you to choose from 'excellent', 'good', 'average', or 'poor' to rate the service quality.
