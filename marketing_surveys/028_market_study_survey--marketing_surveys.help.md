# Market Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Study Survey is a form designed to collect data from customers for market study purposes. The goal is to gather information about customers' opinions, habits, and demographics to better understand their market preferences and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Customer Name.
2. Enter your Customer Phone number, if applicable.
3. Provide your Customer Email address, if applicable.
4. Select your opinions about our market study.
5. Choose your habits that apply to you.
6. Select your purchase habits, if applicable.
7. Enter any other purchase habits not listed above.
8. Provide your Date of Birth, if applicable.
9. Enter your Birth Date, if applicable.
10. Answer the Purchase Frequency question, if applicable.
11. Enter your Age, if applicable.
12. Select your Time of Day, if applicable.
13. Select your Time of Visit, if applicable.
14. Enter any customer notes or comments.
15. Enter your Customer Address, if applicable.
16. Enter your Customer City, if applicable.
17. Enter your Customer State, if applicable.
18. Enter your Customer Zip, if applicable.
19. Enter your Customer Country, if applicable.
20. Enter your Customer Region, if applicable.
21. Enter your Timezone, if applicable.
22. Choose whether or not you are timezone aware, if applicable.
23. Select your Customer Language, if applicable.
24. Enter your Other Language, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Opinions** (`customer_opinions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all opinions that apply to you.
* **Habits** (`customer_habits`, select_one, required: false): Select all habits that apply to you.
* **Purchase Habits** (`purchase_habits`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all purchase habits that apply to you.
* **Other Purchase Habits** (`purchase_habits_other`, text, required: false): Enter any purchase habits not listed above.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your Date of Birth, if applicable.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your Birth Date, if applicable.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, number, required: false): Answer the Purchase Frequency question, if applicable.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your Age, if applicable.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false): Select your Time of Day, if applicable.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Select your Time of Visit, if applicable.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, required: false): Enter any customer notes or comments.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any customer comments.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer Address, if applicable.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer City, if applicable.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer State, if applicable.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer Zip, if applicable.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer Country, if applicable.
* **Customer Region** (`customer_region`, text, required: false): Enter your Customer Region, if applicable.
* **Timezone** (`customer_timezone`, text, required: false): Enter your Timezone, if applicable.
* **Timezone Aware** (`customer_timezone_aware`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not you are timezone aware.
* **Customer Language** (`customer_language`, select_one, required: false): Select your Customer Language.
* **Other Language** (`customer_language_other`, text, required: false): Enter your Other Language, if applicable.
