# Customer Debrief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather feedback from customers about their experience with us. Your input will help us identify areas of strength and areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (`what_is_your_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **How was your experience?** (`how_was_your_experience`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our company.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the space provided.
- **What did we get right?** (`what_did_we_get_right`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following items we got right during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
- **What did we get wrong?** (`what_did_we_get_wrong`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following items we got wrong during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
- **What did we miss?** (`what_did_we_miss`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following items we missed during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
- **What did we not do?** (`what_did_we_didnt`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which of the following items we failed to do during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
- **What did we not provide?** (`what_did_we_didnt_2`, select_one, optional): Indicate which of the following items we failed to provide during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
- **What did we not include?** (`what_did_we_didnt_3`, select_one, optional): Indicate which of the following items we failed to include during your experience:
  - Yes
  - No
## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any additional comments, suggestions, or concerns, you can include them in the "Feedback" field.
* Your responses will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future customers.
