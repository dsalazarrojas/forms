# Internet Usage Survey Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internet Usage Survey Template is designed to collect data on how users use the internet. This form is meant to be filled out by individuals to provide insights into their online behavior, such as how long they use the internet, what they do on the internet, and their demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "start_here" page, which is a note field where you can briefly introduce yourself.
2. On the "question_1" page, answer the question "Internet Usage" in a text format.
3. On the "question_2" page, answer the question "how_long" by providing a numerical value (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
4. On the "question_3" page, select one of the three options that best answers the question "what_for" (e.g., work, entertainment, other).
5. On the "question_4" page, select all that apply from the options provided in "what_for".
6. On the "date_1" page, enter the date on which you last used the internet.
7. On the "time_1" page, enter the time at which you last used the internet.
8. On the "email_1" page, enter your email address.
9. On the "phone_1" page, enter your phone number (if applicable).
10. The "question_5" page is another note field where you can provide any additional comments or information not covered in the previous questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_here** (`start_here`, `note`, required: false): This is where you can briefly introduce yourself before answering the survey questions.
* **internet_usage** (`question_1`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your internet usage in a text format.
* **how_long** (`question_2`, `number`, required: false): How long do you usually use the internet? Enter a numerical value (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **what_for** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options that best answers why you use the internet.
* **what_for** (`question_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the options provided in the "what_for" question.
* **date_1** (`date_1`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date on which you last used the internet.
* **time_1** (`time_1`, `time`, required: false): Please enter the time at which you last used the internet.
* **email_1** (`email_1`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone_1** (`phone_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **question_5** (`question_5`, `note`, required: false): This is another note field where you can provide any additional comments or information not covered in the previous questions.
