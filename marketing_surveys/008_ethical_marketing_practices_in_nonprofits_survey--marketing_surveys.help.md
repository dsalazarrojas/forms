# Ethical Marketing Practices In Nonprofits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help nonprofit organizations assess their marketing practices and identify areas for improvement. It is designed to be a starting point for organizations to reflect on their current marketing efforts and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer each question to the best of your ability. You may refer to your marketing plan, previous surveys, or other relevant materials if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Intro** (`form_intro`, note, false): This section is an introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
- **What are the nonprofit's marketing goals** (`marketing_goals_awareness`, text, false): Describe the main goals of the nonprofit's marketing efforts. For example, what specific objectives you want to achieve through marketing.
- **What are the nonprofit's marketing objectives** (`marketing_objectives`, text, false): Describe what you want to achieve through marketing. This can include goals, outcomes, or results you want to obtain.
- **Who is the target audience for the nonprofit's marketing** (`target_audience`, text, false): Describe the people or groups you are trying to reach with your marketing efforts.
- **What are the biggest marketing challenges the nonprofit faces** (`marketing_challenges`, text, false): List specific challenges and opportunities you face in marketing.
- **What are the nonprofit's ethical marketing practices** (`marketing_ethics`, text, false): Describe the nonprofit's approach to ethics in marketing. For example, how you balance marketing goals with the organization's mission and values.
- **What regulations do nonprofit organizations need to follow** (`marketing_regulations`, text, false): Describe the federal and state regulations that apply to your nonprofit's marketing efforts.
- **How often are surveys conducted** (`survey_conducted`, select_one, false): Indicate how often you conduct surveys, including options for once a year, multiple times a year, or never.
- **What questions are asked when conducting a survey** (`survey_questions_important`, select_multiple, true): Select all questions that are important to you when conducting a survey, such as demographic information, marketing practices, marketing goals and objectives, and other.
- **Is training required for nonprofit marketing staff** (`training_required`, select_one, false): Indicate whether training is required for your nonprofit marketing staff.
- **Is there a policy for nonprofit marketing staff** (`policy_development`, select_one, false): Indicate whether there is a policy for your nonprofit marketing staff.
- **What guidelines do you follow when developing a policy** (`policy_guidelines`, text, false): Describe the guidelines you follow when developing a marketing policy.
- **Who reviews nonprofit marketing policy** (`policy_review`, select_one, false): Indicate who reviews the nonprofit marketing policy.
- **How often do you update nonprofit policy** (`policy_updates`, text, false): Describe how often you update your nonprofit policy.
- **How do you develop nonprofit marketing strategy** (`strategy_development`, text, false): Describe how you develop your nonprofit marketing strategy.
- **What makes a strategy measurable** (`strategy_measurable`, text, false): List the metrics you use to evaluate your strategy.
- **How do you evaluate nonprofit strategy** (`strategy_evaluation`, text, false): Describe how you evaluate your marketing strategy.
- **What is reported in nonprofit strategy** (`strategy_reporting`, text, false): Describe what is reported for strategy evaluation.
- **How adaptable is the nonprofit marketing strategy** (`strategy_adaptability`, text, false): Describe how adaptable is your marketing strategy.
- **What do you do with survey results** (`survey_results`, text, false): Describe what you do with survey results.
- **What is the nonprofit's marketing budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, false): Indicate how much is allocated for marketing, if applicable.
- **What data analytics tools do you use** (`data_analytics`, select_multiple, false): Select all data analytics tools you use, such as Google Analytics or social media analytics.
- **Do you collaborate with other nonprofits** (`marketing_collaboration`, select_one, false): Indicate whether you collaborate with other nonprofits.
- **Who are your survey partners** (`survey_partners`, select_multiple, false): Indicate who your survey partners are, such as other non-profits, for-profits, or both.
- **Other (please specify)** (`other`, text, false): If none of the above options apply, please provide additional information in this field.
