# Post Promotion Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from customers after a post-promotion activity. It is essential for us to understand your experience and gather information to improve future post-promotional activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by the asterisk \*).
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (Name, text, required)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
- **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you if needed.
- **Feedback (Feedback, text, optional)**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the promotion. This will help us understand your experience.
- **Promotion Type (Promotion Type, select_one, required)**: Choose the type of promotion you participated in (Promotion, Discount, or Gift).
- **Rating (Rating, select_multiple, required)**: Rate your experience as Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
- **Review (Review, date, optional)**: If you would like to share a specific date when you participated in the promotion, please enter it here.
- **Submitted (Submitted, time, required)**: Please indicate when you submitted this form.
- **Category (Category, select_one, optional)**: Choose the category related to your experience (Marketing, Sales, or Support).
- **Tags (Tags, text, optional)**: Add any relevant tags or keywords related to your experience.
- **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Share any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- **Post Promotion ID (Post Promotion ID, select_one, optional)**: If you know the ID of the promotion you participated in, please select it.
- **Customer Type (Customer Type, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose the type of customer you are (Customer, Employee, or Partner).
- **Follow-up (Follow-up, select_one, optional)**: Indicate if you would like to be followed-up on (True or False).
- **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

Remember, your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
