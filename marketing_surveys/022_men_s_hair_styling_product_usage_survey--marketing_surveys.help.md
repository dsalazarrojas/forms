# Men's Hair Styling Product Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about men's hair styling product usage and preferences. Your input will help us to better understand men's hair styling habits and inform our marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select one or multiple options for each field.
3. Make sure to provide your preferred language and contact channel.
4. Submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salons brands and researchers** (`salons_brand_researchers`): Enter the salons brands and researchers you are familiar with. This field is not required.
* **How often do you use hair styling products?** (`product_usage_frequency`): Select how often you use hair styling products from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Less than 1 time a week
	+ 1 time a week
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ Daily
* **What do you prefer when it comes to grooming?** (`grooming_preferences`): Select all that apply from the options:
	+ Clean shaven
	+ Light hold
	+ Medium hold
	+ Heavy hold
* **What type of hair do you have?** (`hair_type`): Select the type of hair you have from the options:
	+ Straight
	+ Curly
	+ Wavy
	+ Other
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email_address`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`): Select your preferred language from the options:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
* **Preferred contact channel** (`preferred_contact_channel`): Select your preferred contact channel from the options:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-app
* **Hair Texture** (`hair_texture`): Select the texture of your hair from the options:
	+ Thick
	+ Thin
	+ Other
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`): Select the color of your hair from the options:
	+ Black
	+ Blonde
	+ Brown
	+ Red
	+ Other
* **Face Shape** (`face_shape`): Select the shape of your face from the options:
	+ Oval
	+ Round
	+ Heart
	+ Square
	+ Other
* **Beard length** (`beard_length`): Enter the length of your beard.
* **Facial hair thickness** (`facial_hair_thickness`): Select the thickness of your facial hair from the options:
	+ None
	+ Little
	+ Medium
	+ Full
* **Preferred beard style** (`preferred_beard_style`): Select your preferred beard style from the options:
	+ None
	+ Short
	+ Medium
	+ Full
* **How often do you maintain your beard?** (`beard_frequency`): Select how often you maintain your beard from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Less than 1 time a week
	+ 1 time a week
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ Daily
* **How often do you wash your hair?** (`hair_maintenance_frequency`): Select how often you wash your hair from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Less than 1 time a week
	+ 1 time a week
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ Daily
* **How often do you wash your beard?** (`beard_maintenance_frequency`): Select how often you wash your beard from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Less than 1 time a week
	+ 1 time a week
	+ 2-3 times a week
	+ Daily
