# Market Sizing Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather market size and growth potential information from clients for businesses to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company's name under the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your email address under the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you for any additional information.
3. Enter your phone number under the "Phone" field for contact purposes.
4. Provide the address of your company under the "Address" field.
5. Describe your market or industry under the "Description" field.
6. Answer the question "What is the size of this market?" under the "What is the size of this market?" field.
7. Indicate to whom this market applies under the "To whom does this market apply?" field.
8. Explain why this market is attractive under the "Why is this market attractive?" field.
9. Identify key competitors under the "What are the key competitors?" field.
10. Provide growth potential estimates for the "Current Year", "Last Year", "Next Year", "5 Years", and "10 Years" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your company email address to enable us to contact you regarding any questions or additional information needed.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your company phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (4)**: Provide the physical address of your company.
* **Description (5)**: Briefly describe the market or industry you are targeting.
* **What is the size of this market? (6)**: Estimate the size of the market you are targeting.
* **To whom does this market apply? (7)**: Identify who the market applies to (e.g., customers, end-users, etc.).
* **Why is this market attractive? (8)**: Explain why this market is attractive to your company.
* **What are the key competitors? (9)**: List key competitors in the market.
* **Growth Potential (10)**: Provide a growth potential estimate for the current year.
* **Current Year (11)**: Estimate the size of the market for the current year.
* **Last Year (12)**: Estimate the size of the market for the last year.
* **Next Year (13)**: Estimate the size of the market for the next year.
* **5 Years (14)**: Estimate the size of the market for 5 years.
* **10 Years (15)**: Estimate the size of the market for 10 years. 

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when estimating the size of the market.
* Ensure the growth potential and market size estimates are consistent across all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
