# Online Marketing Strategies Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your experiences and opinions on online marketing strategies. Your input will help us improve our services and provide you with more effective online marketing solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each page, select the relevant option that best describes your role in the company.
2. For the questions that ask you to select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. If you have any challenges using online marketing strategies, type them in the "Challenges Using the Online Marketing Strategies Effectiveness Questionnaire" field.
4. For the questions that ask "What do you like to see improved?", select all the options that you think would be beneficial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the company?** (`what_is_your_role`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the option that best describes your role in the company, such as "Marketing Manager", "Sales Representative", or "Other".
* **What works best for your company?** (`what_works_best_for_your_company`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose all the options that you think are effective for your company.
* **What is your industry?** (`what_is_your_industry`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the option that best describes your industry.
* **Challenges using the Online Marketing Strategies Effectiveness Questionnaire** (`challenges_using_the_online_marketing_strategies_effectiveness_questionnaire`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Type any challenges you are experiencing using this questionnaire.
* **What do you like to see improved?** (`what_do_you_like_to_see_improved`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Choose all the options that you think would be beneficial to improve.
