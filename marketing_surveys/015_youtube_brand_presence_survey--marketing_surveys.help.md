# YouTube Brand Presence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The YouTube Brand Presence Survey is designed to gather information about your YouTube channel's current state, helping us better understand your channel's presence, performance, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and complete each field according to the instructions below.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions accurately.
3. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Familiarity** (`Brand Familiarity`, `number`, required: false): Please rate how familiar you are with your brand, with 1 being "not at all" and 5 being "very familiar".
* **Engagement** (`Engagement`, `text`, required: false): Describe how engaging you feel your content is with your target audience.
* **Content Preferences** (`Content Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of content you prefer, such as Video, Live, or Live and video.
* **Content Quality** (`Content Quality`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of your content, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".
* **Channel Strategy** (`Channel Strategy`, `text`, required: false): Describe your channel's current strategy, including your goals and target audience.
* **Social Media** (`Social Media`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the social media platforms you currently use for your content distribution.
* **YouTube Channel** (`YouTube Channel`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details about your YouTube channel, such as its purpose and target audience.
* **Frequency of Usage** (`Frequency of Usage`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how frequently you use each social media platform for content distribution.
* **Brand Image** (`Brand Image`, `text`, required: false): Describe your brand's image and tone.
* **Social Media Purpose** (`Social Media Purpose`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the primary purpose of your social media presence.
* **Content Distribution** (`Content Distribution`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the platforms where you distribute your content.
* **Content Topics** (`Content Topics`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the topics you cover in your content.
* **Content Format** (`Content Format`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the formats of your content, such as video, live, or live and video.
* **Content Length** (`Content Length`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the lengths of your content.
* **Language** (`Language`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the language of your content.
* **Content Style** (`Content Style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the styles of your content, such as formal, informal, or humorous.
* **Content Frequency** (`Content Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the frequencies of your content.
* **Brand Purpose** (`Brand Purpose`, `text`, required: false): Describe the purpose of your brand.
* **Brand Identity** (`Brand Identity`, `text`, required: false): Describe your brand's identity.
* **Social Media Goals** (`Social Media Goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the goals of your social media presence.
* **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the target audiences for your content.
* **Content Purpose** (`Content Purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the purposes of your content.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`Marketing Strategy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the marketing strategies you use.
* **Content Distribution Channels** (`Content Distribution Channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the platforms where you distribute your content.
* **Content Scheduling** (`Content Scheduling`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the scheduling strategies you use for your content.

Note: This is a comprehensive guide to help you understand each field, ensuring you can fill out the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
