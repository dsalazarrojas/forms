# Retail Promotional Campaign Opinion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and feedback from customers about a retail promotional campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the campaign and its purpose.
2.  Answer the questions on each page as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
3.  If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Questions" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, not required): This is a brief introduction to the campaign and its purpose.
*   **What did you know about this campaign** (`campaign_awareness`, text, not required): Please describe what you knew about the campaign before taking part in it.
*   **Which of the following attributes of this campaign were noticeable to you** (`campaign_performance`, select\_multiple, not required): Please select all the attributes that you found the campaign to have (Awareness, Engaging, Interesting, Informative, Reliable, Unnoticeable).
*   **How do you feel about this campaign** (`campaign_attitudes`, select\_one, not required): Please choose how you feel about the campaign (Negative, Positive, Neutral).
*   **How likely are you to remember this campaign** (`campaign_attitudes_2`, text, not required): Please describe your likelihood of remembering the campaign.
*   **What type of campaign do you think this campaign should be** (`campaign_preference`, select\_multiple, not required): Please select the type of campaign you think this campaign should be (Promotional, Educational, Informative, Awareness, Entertainment, Unsure).
*   **How can we reach you** (`contact_information`, email, not required): Enter your email address for follow-up.
*   **Phone number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
*   **Do you have any further comments on this campaign** (`additional_questions`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
