# Market Analysis Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect market analysis data for various market analysis templates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product(s) relevant to your market analysis from the options in the "market-analysis-template" field.
2. Answer the market analysis question in the "market-analysis-question" field.
3. Provide a market analysis answer in the "market-analysis-answer" field.
4. Enter the date and time for the market analysis in the "market-analysis-date" and "market-analysis-time" fields.
5. Enter your email address in the "market-analysis-email" field.
6. Select the website related to your market analysis from the options in the "market-analysis-website" field.
7. Enter the phone number for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-phone" field.
8. Add any comments about your market analysis in the "market-analysis-comments" field (optional).
9. Indicate whether you have any attachments related to your market analysis (yes or no) in the "market-analysis-attachments" field.
10. Select the tags (yes or no) that apply to your market analysis in the "market-analysis-tags" field.
11. Choose the category (Category A, B, or C) for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-category" field.
12. Indicate whether you have any embeds (yes or no) related to your market analysis in the "market-analysis-embed" field.
13. Select the share options (yes or no) for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-share" field.
14. Indicate whether you have any QR code (yes or no) related to your market analysis in the "market-analysis-qr-code" field.
15. Add any coding information related to your market analysis in the "market-analysis-coding" field (optional).
16. Select the coding language for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-coding-language" field (optional).
17. Indicate whether you have any coding platforms related to your market analysis (yes or no) in the "market-analysis-coding-platform" field.
18. Select the coding platform types (yes or no) related to your market analysis in the "market-analysis-coding-platform-type" field (optional).
19. Indicate whether you have any coding frameworks related to your market analysis (yes or no) in the "market-analysis-coding-frameworks" field.
20. Select the coding version for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-coding-version" field (optional).
21. Add any additional coding version information in the "market-analysis-coding-version-2", "market-analysis-coding-version-3", "market-analysis-coding-version-4", and "market-analysis-coding-version-5" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **market-analysis-template** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the product(s) related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-question** (`text`, required): Answer the market analysis question.
* **market-analysis-answer** (`text`, optional): Provide a market analysis answer.
* **market-analysis-date** (`date`, required): Enter the date for the market analysis.
* **market-analysis-time** (`time`, required): Enter the time for the market analysis.
* **market-analysis-email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **market-analysis-website** (`select_one`, required): Select the website related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-phone** (`text`, required): Enter the phone number for your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-comments** (`note`, optional): Add any comments about your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-attachments** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have any attachments related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-tags** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the tags (yes or no) that apply to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-category** (`select_one`, required): Choose the category (Category A, B, or C) for your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-embed** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have any embeds (yes or no) related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-share** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the share options (yes or no) for your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-qr-code** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have any QR code (yes or no) related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-coding** (`text`, optional): Add any coding information related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-coding-language** (`text`, optional): Select the coding language for your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-coding-platform** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have any coding platforms related to your market analysis (yes or no).
* **market-analysis-coding-platform-type** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the coding platform types (yes or no) related to your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-coding-frameworks** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have any coding frameworks related to your market analysis (yes or no).
* **market-analysis-coding-version** (`select_one`, optional): Select the coding version for your market analysis.
* **market-analysis-coding-version-2**, 3, 4, 5** (`text`, optional): Add any additional coding version information (optional).
* **market-analysis-coding-version** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have any coding version related to your market analysis (yes or no).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Use a relevant product name in the "market-analysis-template" field.
* Enter a valid email address in the "market-analysis-email" field.
* Select a valid website from the options in the "market-analysis-website" field.
* Enter a valid phone number in the "market-analysis-phone" field.
* Be specific when adding any comments or attachments related to your market analysis.
* Choose the correct category for your market analysis in the "market-analysis-category" field.
* Select the correct coding language and platform for your market analysis.
* Review and verify your answers before submitting.
* Be accurate and concise in your answers.
