# Corporate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from corporate employees or customers for marketing or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields:
	+ Name
	+ Company Name
	+ Department
	+ Email
	+ Question 5 (date)
* Answer the questions with the relevant options:
	+ Question 1 (select one)
	+ Question 2 (select one)
	+ Question 3 (select multiple)
	+ Question 4 (select one)
	+ Question 7 (time)
	+ Question 8 (time)
	+ Question 9 (select multiple)
	+ Question 10 (select multiple)
	+ Question 13 (select one)
	+ Question 14 (select one)
* Provide any additional comments or information in the Note fields:
	+ Question 11
	+ Question 12

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Company Name** (`company`, Select One, Required): Select the name of your company from the list.
* **Department** (`department`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all departments that apply.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Date of Last Survey** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the last survey you participated in.
* **Time of Last Survey** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the last survey you participated in.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Information** (`page_2`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the current survey.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the current survey.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the current survey.
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the current survey.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.
* **Question 10** (`question_10`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.
* **Question 11** (`question_11`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Question 12** (`question_12`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Question 13** (`question_13`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Question 14** (`question_14`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional): Click to submit the form.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
