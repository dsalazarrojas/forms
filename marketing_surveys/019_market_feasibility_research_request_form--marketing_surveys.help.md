# Market Feasibility Research Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request market research services for your project. It helps us understand your project's requirements and feasibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Market Feasibility Research Request Form**: Enter a brief title for your project.
2. **Category**: Select "Marketing Surveys" as the category for this form.
3. **Description**: Provide a short description of your project.
4. **Form URL**: Enter the URL of the form you want us to review.
5. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to your project (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other").
6. **Research Brief**: Enter a detailed description of your research brief.
7. **Research Brief Date**: Enter the date you expect the research brief to be completed.
8. **Research Brief Time**: Enter the expected time for completion.
9. **Research Brief Location**: Select where the research brief will be conducted (e.g., "New York", "Los Angeles", or "Other").
10. **Research Brief Status**: Choose the current status of your research brief (e.g., "Approved", "Rejected", or "In Progress").
11. **Research Brief Priority**: Choose the priority level of your research brief (e.g., "High", "Medium", or "Low").
12. **Research Brief Comments**: Add any comments or notes related to your research brief.
13. **Form Submission**: Enter the date and time when you submitted the form.
14. **Form Submission Location**: Select where the form was submitted (e.g., "New York", "Los Angeles", or "Other").
15. **Form Submission Status**: Choose the current status of your form submission (e.g., "Approved", "Rejected", or "In Progress").
16. **Form Submission Comments**: Add any comments or notes related to your form submission.
17. **Research Requester**: Choose who requested the research (e.g., "Marketing", "Sales", or "Other").
18. **Research Requester Department**: Select the department of the research requester (e.g., "Marketing", "Sales", or "Other").
19. **Research Requester Email**: Enter a valid email of the research requester.
20. **Research Requester Phone**: Enter a valid phone number of the research requester.
21. **Research Requester Extension**: Enter the research requester's extension (if applicable).
22. **Research Requester Comments**: Add any comments or notes related to the research requester.
23. **Research Requester Name**: Enter the name of the research requester.
24. **Form Submission Status**: This field is automatically populated based on your form submission. Please do not fill it out.

## Tips

* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure that all fields are up-to-date and relevant to your research brief.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your research assistant for clarification.
* Do not submit multiple forms for the same research brief.
* The form submission status will be updated automatically after review and approval.
* Do not enter any invalid or fake information.
