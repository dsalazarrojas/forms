# Price Sensitivity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Price Sensitivity Survey is designed to collect information about how sensitive a product's price can be for different target customers and price ranges. This survey helps us understand the pricing strategy and customer behavior related to price changes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. 

1. **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false) - This section is a brief description of the survey and its purpose.
2. **Product Description** (`Product Description`, `text`, required: false) - Please describe the product you are pricing in 1-2 sentences.
3. **Pricing Strategy** (`Pricing Strategy`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Select one or more pricing strategies that apply to your product. This includes "Cost-plus" and "Other".
	* "Cost-plus" pricing strategy involves calculating a price based on the cost of production and a markup.
	* "Other" pricing strategy involves other non-cost-based methods.
4. **Price Sensitivity** (`Price Sensitivity`, `number`, required: false) - If you have a specific price sensitivity, please enter it here as a percentage.
5. **Target Price** (`Target Price`, `number`, required: false) - Enter the target price for your product.
6. **Discount** (`Discount`, `number`, required: false) - Enter the discount amount if you are offering a discount.
7. **Price Increase** (`Price Increase`, `number`, required: false) - Enter the price increase if you are raising the price.
8. **Price Decrease** (`Price Decrease`, `number`, required: false) - Enter the price decrease if you are lowering the price.
9. **Sensitivity Level** (`Sensitivity Level`, `select_one`, required: false) - Select the sensitivity level of your price changes. This includes "Very Sensitive", "Somewhat Sensitive", and "Not Very Sensitive".
10. **Price Range** (`Price Range`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Select one or more price ranges that apply to your product. This includes "Low", "Medium", and "High".
11. **Target Customer** (`Target Customer`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Select one or more target customer segments. This includes "Young Adults", "Middle-Aged", and "Old-Aged".
12. **Customer Satisfaction** (`Customer Satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false) - Select the expected customer satisfaction level. This includes "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", and "Not Satisfied".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This field provides a brief description of the survey and its purpose.
* **Product Description**: Please provide a short description of the product being priced.
* **Pricing Strategy**: This field allows you to select one or more pricing strategies that apply to your product, including "Cost-plus" and "Other".
* **Price Sensitivity**: If you have a specific price sensitivity, please enter it as a percentage.
* **Target Price**: Enter the target price for your product.
* **Discount**: If you are offering a discount, enter the amount here.
* **Price Increase**: If you are raising the price, enter the amount here.
* **Price Decrease**: If you are lowering the price, enter the amount here.
* **Sensitivity Level**: Select the level of price sensitivity, which can be "Very Sensitive", "Somewhat Sensitive", or "Not Very Sensitive".
* **Price Range**: Select one or more price ranges that apply to your product, which can be "Low", "Medium", and "High".
* **Target Customer**: Select one or more target customer segments, which can be "Young Adults", "Middle-Aged", and "Old-Aged".
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Select the expected customer satisfaction level, which can be "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", or "Not Satisfied".

## Tips
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to select the correct pricing strategy and price ranges that apply to your product.
- Be as specific as possible when entering prices and sensitivity levels.
- Select the correct target customer segment and customer satisfaction level.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
