# Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey is designed to gather information from pet owners about their loyalty and purchasing habits towards pet brands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the most suitable answer from the available options.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers, as they will help us better understand pet owner loyalty and purchasing habits.
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to your experience or don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Owner's Name
**Pet Owner's Name** (`pet_owner`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name. It's not required, but it's helpful for us to know who is providing the answers.

*   **Very Frequently**: You usually buy from the same pet brand at least 3 times a month.

*   **Frequently**: You buy from the same pet brand at least once a month.

*   **Occasionally**: You buy from the same pet brand less than once a month.

*   **Rarely**: You buy from the same pet brand only a few times a year.

*   **Never**: You never buy from the same pet brand.

*   **Social**: The brand's social media presence or community plays a significant role in your purchasing decision.

*   **Price**: The brand's price or value proposition is a key factor in your decision.

*   **Quality**: The brand's product quality is the most important factor in your decision.

*   **Convenience**: The brand's convenience or ease of use is a key factor in your decision.

*   **Other**: Something else drives your loyalty to the pet brand, or you'd like to specify what that is.

*   **Very Satisfied**: You're very happy with the pet brand and would recommend it.

*   **Somewhat Satisfied**: You’re somewhat satisfied with the pet brand, but there’s room for improvement.

*   **Somewhat Dissatisfied**: You’re somewhat dissatisfied with the pet brand.

*   **Very Dissatisfied**: You’re very unhappy with the pet brand.

*   **Never**: You have never purchased from the pet brand.
