# Planter Collection Naming Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on the planter collection naming process. We are looking for users to provide their thoughts on the current names of planters and suggest new names that they like. This feedback will help us to better understand your preferences and make informed decisions about the planters' naming process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides some context about the purpose of this form.
2. Answer the question "Planters" to provide your thoughts on the current planters' names.
3. In the "Name Suggestions" section, select up to 3 options that you like from the provided list or type in your own suggestion.
4. If you have a favorite suggestion that is not listed, enter it in the "Your Favorite Suggestion" field.
5. In the "How would you like to be contacted?" section, select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or SMS).
6. For the "Contact Preference" question, select Yes if you would like to be contacted about this survey or No if not.
7. Finally, provide any other comments or suggestions you may have in the "Other Comments" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Submission Comments" field.
