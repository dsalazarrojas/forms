# Brand Loyalty Campaign Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experiences with brand loyalty campaigns. By answering the questions, you'll help us understand how effective our campaign was in promoting awareness, encouraging participation, and creating a positive impact on customer loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.**
2. **Select the most accurate option for each question.**
3. **Use a pen and paper to take note of any additional comments or feedback.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Awareness

* **Campaign Awareness** (`awareness_header`, **note**, required: false): This page serves as an introduction to the form.
* **Name of the Loyalty Campaign you participated in** (`target_campaign`, **text**, required: true): Please enter the name of the campaign you were part of.
* **How did you first hear about this campaign?** (`awareness_channel_select`, **select_one**, required: true): Choose one of the following options to indicate how you first learned about this campaign:
	+ Email
	+ Social Media
	+ In-store
	+ Website
	+ Word of Mouth

### Page 2: Participation and Engagement

* **How easy was it to participate in this campaign?** (`ease_of_part`, **select_one**, required: true): Select how easy or difficult it was to participate in the campaign:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **How appealing were the rewards / incentives offered?** (`reward_appeal`, **select_one**, required: true): Rate the appeal of the rewards or incentives:
	+ Highly Appealing
	+ Somewhat Appealing
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Very Appealing

### Page 3: Impact on Loyalty

* **Has this campaign increased your frequency of purchase?** (`frequency_change`, **select_one**, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Significantly Increased
	+ Somewhat Increased
	+ No Change
	+ Decreased

### Page 4: Feedback and Improvement

* **Do you feel more connected to our brand after this campaign?** (`connection_lvl`, **select_one**, required: true): Choose how connected you feel to the brand:
	+ Much more connected
	+ Somewhat more connected
	+ No change
	+ Less connected
* **How satisfied were you with the reward redemption process?** (`redemption_sat`, **select_one**, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with the reward redemption process:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ N/A
* **What did you like most about this campaign?** (`liked_feature`, **text**, required: true): Provide a brief description of what you liked most about the campaign.
* **What is one thing we could do to make our loyalty programs better?** (`improvement_ideas`, **text**, required: true): Share an idea on how we can improve our loyalty programs.
* **Would you participate in a similar campaign in the future?** (`future_intent`, **select_one**, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Maybe
	+ 'False'
* **Date** (`eval_dt`, **date**, required: true): Provide the date you're completing the form.

## Tips

* **Please keep your answers honest and as detailed as possible.**
* **Use clear and concise language in your answers.**
* **If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them with us.**
