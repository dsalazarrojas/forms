# Marketing Role Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Role Assessment form is a tool to collect information about an individual's marketing role, experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your role, such as Marketing Manager, Marketing Coordinator, or Marketing Specialist.
2. Enter your years of experience in marketing.
3. Select the marketing skills you possess, such as social media marketing, email marketing, content creation, data analysis, web development, or graphic design.
4. Choose your experience level, which is Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
5. Enter your date of joining the company (if applicable).
6. Select the last updated date (if applicable).
7. Add any comments about your experience and skills (optional).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Fill in your name, which will be displayed as "Submitter's Name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **The Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your current marketing role, such as Marketing Manager, Marketing Coordinator, or Marketing Specialist.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have spent in marketing.
* **Marketing Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Choose the marketing skills you possess, such as social media marketing, email marketing, content creation, data analysis, web development, or graphic design.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your experience level, which is Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, date, required): Enter the date you joined the company (if applicable).
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, time, required): Select the last updated date (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about your experience and skills (optional).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Submitter's Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Fill in your name as it will be displayed on the submission.
