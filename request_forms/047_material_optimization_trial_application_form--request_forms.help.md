# Material Optimization Trial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Material Optimization Trial Application Form" is designed to collect information from users about a material optimization trial. The form is used to gather details about the trial, including user information, trial objectives, material characteristics, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe the trial information, including its objectives and any relevant details.
3. Specify the material information, including its characteristics and properties.
4. Outline the trial requirements and any specific conditions.
5. Enter the trial location and duration.
6. Provide the trial start and end dates.
7. Identify the person responsible for the trial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Trial Info** (`trial_info`, text, optional): Describe the purpose and objectives of the trial.
* **Material Info** (`material_info`, text, optional): Specify the characteristics and properties of the material being tested.
* **Trial Objectives** (`trial_objectives`, text, optional): Outline the specific goals and objectives of the trial.
* **Material Characteristics** (`material_characteristics`, text, optional): Describe the material's properties and features.
* **Trial Requirements** (`trial_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific conditions or requirements for the trial.
* **Trial Location** (`trial_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the trial will take place.
* **Trial Start Date** (`trial_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the trial is scheduled to start.
* **Trial End Date** (`trial_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the trial is scheduled to end.
* **Trial Duration** (`trial_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the trial in days.
* **Trial Responsible** (`trial_responsible`, text, optional): Identify the person responsible for the trial.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure the successful execution of the trial.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the trial administrator for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.
