# Prayer Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting prayer requests to the organisation, ensuring that all necessary information is collected for correct processing and assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring you have filled in all required fields.
- Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
- Choose a prayer type from the given options.
- Select a category for your prayer request.
- Choose to assign the prayer request to either a user, a group, or an admin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **UserName** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **PrayerRequesterEmail** (`prayer_requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Prayer Request** (`prayer_request`, `text`, required): Describe your prayer request in a few words.
* **PrayerRequesterName** (`prayer_requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **PrayerRequesterNumber** (`prayer_requester_number`, `text`, required): Enter your number for contact purposes.
* **Candle Request** (`candle_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of candles you need for the prayer.
* **Prayer Type** (`prayer_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the given prayer types.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required): If applicable, enter the amount of donation you want to make.
* **Donation Currency** (`donation_currency`, `select_one`, required): If applicable, choose the currency of the donation.
* **Prayer Date** (`prayer_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the prayer to be done.
* **Prayer Time** (`prayer_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the prayer to be done.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or information about the prayer request.
* **Prayer Status** (`prayer_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the prayer request.
* **Assign To** (`assign_to`, `select_one`, required): Choose to assign the prayer request to either a user, a group, or an admin.
* **Prayer Category** (`prayer_category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more categories for the prayer request.
* **Assign To User** (`assign_to_user`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether to assign the prayer request to a user.
* **Prayer Requester Notes** (`prayer_requester_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or information about the prayer request.
* **Prayer Status Update** (`prayer_status_update`, `note`, required): Add any updates to the status of the prayer request.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date the prayer request was assigned.
* **Assigned By User** (`assigned_by_user`, `select_one`, required): Choose who assigned the prayer request.
* **Assigned By IP** (`assigned_by_ip`, `text`, required): Enter the IP address of the user who assigned the prayer request.
* **Assigned By Email** (`assigned_by_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of the user who assigned the prayer request.
* **Assigned By Organisation** (`assigned_by_organisation`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organisation that assigned the prayer request.
