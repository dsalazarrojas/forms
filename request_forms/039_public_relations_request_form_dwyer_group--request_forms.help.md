# public_relations_request_form_dwyer_group - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request public relations support for a press release. It helps us understand your needs and provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Press Release Request Form**: This is the main content area where you can provide a brief description of your press release request.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company related to the press release.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for this press release request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Press Release Details**: Describe the press release you are requesting, including any relevant details.
* **Why Press Release**: Provide a brief explanation of why this press release is important and worth our attention.
* **PR Request Date**: Choose the date you would like to request the press release.
* **PR Request Time**: Choose the time you would like to request the press release.
* **PR Request Location**: Enter the location where you would like to request the press release.
* **Press Release Topic**: Enter the topic of the press release you are requesting.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We'll do our best to review and process your request promptly.
