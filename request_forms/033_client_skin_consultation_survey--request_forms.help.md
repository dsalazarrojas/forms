# Client Skin Consultation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Skin Consultation Survey is designed to help us understand your skin concerns and concerns, allowing us to tailor our products and services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email address, phone number, and age range.
2. Select your skin type from the options provided.
3. Choose your primary skin concern from the list of options.
4. Indicate how long you've had your skin concern.
5. Describe any previous treatments or products you've tried for your skin concern.
6. If you have any product allergies or sensitivities, please list them.
7. Describe your current skincare routine.
8. Select your daily sun exposure level.
9. Choose your current stress level.
10. If you follow a specific diet, please describe it.
11. List any medications or supplements that might affect your skin.
12. Indicate your skin goals.
13. Select your preferred treatment type.
14. Finally, provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`): Please enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`): Select your age range from the options provided.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`): Choose your skin type from the options provided.
* **Primary Skin Concern** (`primary_concern`): Select your primary skin concern from the list of options.
* **How Long Have You Had This Concern?** (`concern_duration`): Indicate how long you've had your skin concern.
* **Previous Treatments or Products Tried** (`previous_treatments`): Describe any previous treatments or products you've tried for your skin concern.
* **Any Product Allergies or Sensitivities?** (`product_allergies`): If you have any product allergies or sensitivities, list them.
* **Current Skincare Routine** (`current_skincare_routine`): Describe your current skincare routine.
* **Daily Sun Exposure Level** (`sun_exposure`): Select your daily sun exposure level from the options provided.
* **Current Stress Level** (`stress_level`): Choose your current stress level from the options provided.
* **Dietary Habits** (`diet_habits`): If you follow a specific diet, please describe it.
* **Current Medications or Supplements** (`medications`): If you take any medications or supplements that might affect your skin, list them.
* **What Are Your Skin Goals?** (`skin_goals`): Indicate what you would like to achieve with your skin.
* **Preferred Treatment Type** (`preferred_treatment_type`): Select your preferred treatment type from the options provided.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your time!
