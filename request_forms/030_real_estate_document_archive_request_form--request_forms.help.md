# real_estate_document_archive_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the archiving of real estate documents. The form is designed to capture the necessary information from the person requesting the archive, including property details, contact information, and document specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and title in the "Form Submitter" field.
2. Select the type of property you are requesting the document archive for from the "property_type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the status of the property from the "property_status" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of the request in the "date_of_request" field.
5. Enter the date of the document transfer in the "date_of_transfer" field.
6. Enter the property address in the "property_address" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_person" field.
8. Enter the contact person's information in the "contact_info" field.
9. Describe the document details in the "document_details" field.
10. Select the type of document(s) being archived from the "document_type" multi-select field.
11. Enter the date of the document in the "document_date" field.
12. Enter the document number in the "document_number" field.
13. Enter any comments or remarks about the request in the "requestor_comments" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the realtor's name in the "realtor_name" field.
15. (Optional) Enter the realtor's license number in the "realtor_license_number" field.
16. (Optional) Enter the realtor's phone number in the "realtor_phone" field.
17. (Optional) Enter the realtor's signature in the "realtor_signature" field.
18. (Optional) Enter the realtor's address in the "realtor_address" field.
19. (Optional) Enter the realtor's fax number in the "realtor_fax" field.
20. (Optional) Enter the realtor's company in the "realtor_company" field.
21. Enter the requestor's name in the "requestor_name" field.
22. Enter the requestor's email address in the "requestor_email" field.
23. Enter the requestor's phone number in the "requestor_phone" field.
24. (Optional) Enter the requestor's address in the "requestor_address" field.
25. Enter your name in the "requestor_comments" field (for signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submitter** (1, text, required): Enter your name and title to begin the form.
* **property_type** (2, select_one, required): Choose the type of property you are requesting the document archive for.
* **property_status** (3, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the property (Active or Inactive).
* **date_of_request** (4, date, optional): Enter the date of the request.
* **date_of_transfer** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the document transfer.
* **property_address** (6, text, optional): Enter the property address (if applicable).
* **contact_person** (7, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **contact_info** (8, text, required): Enter the contact person's information.
* **document_details** (9, text, required): Describe the document details.
* **document_type** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of document(s) being archived (Deed, Lease, Contract, Other).
* **document_date** (11, date, required): Enter the date of the document.
* **document_number** (12, text, required): Enter the document number.
* **requestor_comments** (13, text, required): Enter any comments or remarks about the request.
* **realtor_name** (14, text, optional): Enter the realtor's name.
* **realtor_license_number** (15, text, optional): Enter the realtor's license number.
* **realtor_phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the realtor's phone number.
* **realtor_signature** (17, text, optional): Enter the realtor's signature.
* **realtor_address** (18, text, optional): Enter the realtor's address.
* **realtor_fax** (19, text, optional): Enter the realtor's fax number.
* **realtor_company** (20, text, optional): Enter the realtor's company name.
* **requestor_name** (21, text, optional): Enter the requestor's name.
* **requestor_email** (22, email, required): Enter the requestor's email address.
* **requestor_phone** (23, text, required): Enter the requestor's phone number.
* **requestor_address** (24, text, optional): Enter the requestor's address.
* **realtor_address** (25, text, optional): Enter the realtor's address.
* **realtor_fax** (26, text, optional): Enter the realtor's fax number.
* **realtor_company** (27, text, optional): Enter the realtor's company name.
* **requestor_comments** (28, text, required): Enter your name for signature.
