## Purpose
The Photo To Cartoon Me Form is designed to collect information from users who want to create or share cartoon versions of their photos. This form will help users understand their options and preferences related to photos and cartoons.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions about your photo preferences, including whether you own a drawing studio, how you gather and sell photos, and other related fields.
2.  Provide your contact information, including your email, phone number, and name.
3.  Add a description and category for your photo.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
