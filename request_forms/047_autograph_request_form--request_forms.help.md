# Autograph Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from fans and enthusiasts to request autographs from celebrities or public figures. The form helps us process and fulfill requests efficiently, ensuring that we cover all necessary details for shipping and other related processes. Please fill out the form accurately to help us provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Your Full Name** (`fan_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*  **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for status updates on your request.
*  **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for shipping questions and concerns.
*  **Celebrity or Public Figure Name** (`celebrity_name`, `text`, required): Type the name of the celebrity or public figure you are requesting an autograph from.
*  **Personal Message Request** (`personal_message`, `text`, optional): Write a message for the celebrity to sign, up to 15 words.
*  **Item to be Signed** (`item_to_sign`, `select_one`, required): Choose the item to be signed from the list provided (e.g., Photo, Poster, Book, Sports Equipment, Fan Provided Item).
*  **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
*  **City, State, and Zip** (`city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Provide the full location details, including city, state, and zip code.
*  **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred shipping method from the list (e.g., Standard, Express, International).
*  **Agree to Shipping Costs** (`agree_shipping_costs`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you will cover all postage and handling fees.
*  **I confirm this is for personal use and not for resale** (`personal_use_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Ensure that you are not planning to resell the signed item and that it's for your personal use.

## Tips

*  Make sure to double-check your shipping address for accuracy to avoid delays.
*  Ensure that you select the correct shipping method to suit your needs.
*  Review your request carefully before submission to avoid mistakes.
