# Urban Development Symposium Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Development Symposium Leave Of Absence Form is designed to be used by employees to request a leave of absence for the Urban Development Symposium. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is provided for the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Employee Name" field with your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
2. Enter your "Employee ID" number, which can be found on your ID badge.
3. Select your "Shift" for the date you are requesting leave, from Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Rotating shifts.
4. Enter the start and end times for your shift in the "Shift Start Time" and "Shift End Time" fields.
5. If you have breaks, enter the start and end times for your break in the "Break Start Time" and "Break End Time" fields.
6. Enter the total hours worked before taking leave in the "Total Hours Worked" field.
7. Select the date and time you plan to start and end your leave from the "Leave Start Time" and "Leave End Time" fields.
8. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the "Leave Reason" field.
9. If applicable, attach any supporting documents or attachments required for your leave request.
10. Finally, review and submit your form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge.
* **Employee ID** (number): Enter your employee ID number, which can be found on your ID badge.
* **Shift** (select_one): Select the shift you are requesting leave for, from Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Rotating shifts.
* **Shift Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (time): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Break Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of your break, if applicable.
* **Break End Time** (time): Enter the end time of your break, if applicable.
* **Total Hours Worked** (number): Enter the total hours worked before taking leave.
* **Leave Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (time): Enter the end time of your leave.
* **Leave Reason** (text): Provide a brief reason for your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (select_one): Choose whether your Team Lead has approved or declined your request, or if it is pending approval.
* **Manager Comment** (text): If your Team Lead has commented on your leave request, enter their comment here.
* **Approval Request** (select_one): Select "Yes" if you are requesting approval for your leave, or "No" if you are not.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or attachments required for your leave request.
