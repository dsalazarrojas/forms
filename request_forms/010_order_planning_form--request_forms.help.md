# Order Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Planning Form is used to collect information about upcoming orders. Fill in the required fields to ensure accurate order planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "department_id" fields with the correct department numbers as required.
2. Select the correct "priority" level for the order (High, Medium, Low).
3. Enter the expected "delivery_date".
4. Enter the expected "delivery_time".
5. Add any "request_details" about the order.
6. Add any "purchasing_notes" or "operations_notes" as needed.
7. Enter the email address of the "purchasing_manager_id".
8. Enter the email address of the "operations_manager_id".
9. Add any "delivery_details".
10. Enter the "delivery_address" and any "delivery_notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **department_id** (number, required): Enter the department number(s) associated with this order.
- **priority** (select_one, optional): Choose the priority level for the order: High, Medium, Low.
- **delivery_date** (date, optional): Enter the expected delivery date for the order.
- **delivery_time** (time, optional): Enter the expected delivery time for the order.
- **request_details** (text, optional): Add any additional details about the order.
- **purchasing_notes** (text, optional): Add any purchasing notes about the order.
- **operations_notes** (text, optional): Add any operations notes about the order.
- **purchasing_manager_id** (number, optional): Enter the purchasing manager's ID.
- **operations_manager_id** (number, optional): Enter the operations manager's ID.
- **department_id_1** to **department_id_10** (number, optional): Enter any additional department ID numbers.
- **delivery_details** (text, optional): Add any delivery details about the order.
- **purchasing_manager_email** (email, optional): Enter the purchasing manager's email address.
- **operations_manager_email** (email, optional): Enter the operations manager's email address.
- **delivery_address** (text, optional): Enter the delivery address.
- **delivery_notes** (text, optional): Add any delivery notes about the order.
- **submit_notes** (text, optional): Add any additional submit notes about the order.

**Tips**: Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the form's administrator.
