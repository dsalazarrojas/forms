# Tree Trimming Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Trimming Request Form is designed to collect information from clients who are requesting tree trimming services to maintain the health and safety of trees in urban areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required and optional fields with the relevant information.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Enter the name and any other relevant details of the client requesting the tree trimming service.
* **Tree Location (2)**: Specify the exact location of the tree that needs trimming.
* **Tree Size (3)**: Choose the size of the tree that requires trimming (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Service Type (4)**: Select the type of service required for the tree trimming (Tree Trimming, Tree Shaping, or Tree Pruning).
* **Service Date (5)**: Enter the date on which the service is required.
* **Service Time (6)**: Enter the time of day when the service is required.
* **Service Notes (7)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the service required.
* **Contact Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the client or contact person.
* **Contact Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the client or contact person.
* **Assigned to (10)**: Select the person or team responsible for providing the service (Chatjimmy or Other).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to ensure prompt and efficient service. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities or team members for guidance.
