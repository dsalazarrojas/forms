# drone_mission_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about drone missions. It is intended for use in various industries, such as agriculture, construction, and emergency services, to gather details about the mission, including its objectives, the individuals involved, and the specific equipment used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Enter your details in the "Mission Details" field, which should include the objectives and scope of the mission.
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details" field, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter details about the drone in the "Drone Details" field, including the drone's make, model, and serial number.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the mission in the "Mission Comments" field.
6. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Mission Details
• **Mission Details** (`mission_details`, text): This field is where you should enter the objectives and scope of the mission.
### Contact Details
• **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text): This field is where you should provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
### Drone Details
• **Drone Details** (`drone_details`, text): This field is where you should enter details about the drone, including its make, model, and serial number.
### Mission Notes
• **Mission Notes** (`mission_notes`, text): This field is optional and is where you can add any additional comments or notes about the mission.
### Mission Comments
• **Mission Comments** (`mission_comments`, text): This field is optional and is where you can add any additional comments or notes about the mission.
### Tips
* Ensure that you have permission to access and submit this form before proceeding.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
