## Transportation Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Transportation Reimbursement Request Form is used to request reimbursement for business-related or personal travel expenses. It is intended to provide a straightforward and organized process for submitting requests for reimbursement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide travel details in the "Travel Details" field.
2. Enter the reimbursement amount in the "Reimbursement Amount" field.
3. Enter your travel dates using the "Start Date" and "Return Date" fields.
4. Select the purpose of your travel by choosing from the options in the "Travel Purpose" field.
5. Attach any supporting travel receipts to the "Travel Receipts" field, providing as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your travel, including the destination, mode of transportation, and any other relevant information.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (`reimbursement_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount you are requesting for reimbursement. Include any supporting documentation or receipts as attachments where possible.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you started your journey. If you are requesting reimbursement for multiple trips, please enter the start date for each trip separately.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you returned. If you are requesting reimbursement for multiple trips, please enter the return date for each trip separately.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the primary purpose of your travel from the following options:
  • Business
  • Personal
  • Vacation
  • Other
* **Travel Receipts** (`travel_receipts`, `text`, required: false): Attach any supporting travel receipts, invoices, or documentation for your reimbursement request.
