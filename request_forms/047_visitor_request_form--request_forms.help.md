# Visitor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Request Form is a tool to gather information from visitors to the organization's premises for the purpose of tracking and managing their visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Date and Time of your visit from the respective fields.
5. Briefly describe the Purpose of your visit in the Comments field.
6. If applicable, indicate whether you will be Assighned a tool for your visit.
7. Indicate whether you will be submitting any comments or information about your visit.
8. Confirm whether you will be receiving a phone call from the organization before or after your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`request_form_1`, Text, Required: No): Enter your first name as it appears on your official ID or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter your last name as it appears on your official ID or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date_of_visit`, Date, Required: No): Select the date you plan to visit the organization's premises.
* **Time** (`time_of_visit`, Time, Required: No): Select the time you plan to visit the organization's premises.
* **Purpose** (`purpose_of_visit`, Text, Required: No): Describe the purpose of your visit, e.g. meeting, tour, event.
* **Assigned** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Required: No): Indicate whether you will be assigned a tool or equipment for your visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Required: No): Enter any comments or information about your visit.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate whether you will be submitting comments or information about your visit.
* **Confirm** (`confirm_email`, Select One, Required: No): Confirm whether an email will be sent to you before or after your visit.
* **Phone** (`confirm_phone`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Confirm whether a phone call will be made to you before or after your visit. 

Note: This form is for informational purposes only. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to ensure efficient processing and response from the organization.
