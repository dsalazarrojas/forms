# Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a sponsorship request for a specific project, event, or activity. Please fill out the form with the required information to provide a clear and concise request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all the required fields** (marked as "required") with the requested information. If you're unsure about the requirements, please check the "Help" section for each field's explanation.
2. **Submit the form** once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sponsorship Request Form** (1): This is the title of the form itself. You can leave this field blank if it doesn't apply to your specific request.
- **Name** (2): Your full name as the requestor. This field is optional, but it's recommended for official purposes.
- **Email** (3): Your email address. This is used to contact you for further questions or updates.
- **Phone Number** (4): Your phone number. This is optional but recommended for easier communication.
- **Date of Birth** (5): The date of birth of the individual or organization requesting sponsorship. This field is not required but might be necessary for certain types of requests.
- **Sponsorship Type** (6): Select the type of sponsorship you're requesting, which can be Sponsor, Donation, or Other (please specify in the notes).
- **Sponsorship Amount** (7): The amount of money you're requesting for sponsorship. This field is optional, but be as specific as possible for the best possible outcome.
- **Sponsorship Frequency** (8): If you're requesting recurring sponsorship, choose how often you'd like to receive the support (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
- **Notes** (9): A brief description of the project, event, or activity you're requesting sponsorship for. This is an optional field but can provide more context to your request.
- **Submitter** (10): This field is not clearly labeled, but it seems to be asking for the name of the person or organization submitting the request. 
- **Submitter Email** (11): A contact email for the person or organization submitting the request.
- **Submitter Phone** (12): A contact phone number for the person or organization submitting the request.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your request carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Please be patient with our response times, as we review each request thoroughly.
