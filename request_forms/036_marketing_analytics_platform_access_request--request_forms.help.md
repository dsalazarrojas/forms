# Marketing Analytics Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Marketing Analytics Platform. The platform is a tool used for data analysis and reporting for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select the dates you wish to request access from the "Request Date", "Start Date", and "End Date" fields.
5. Enter your reason for requesting access in the "Reason" field.
6. Select the approval status of your manager, team lead, and product manager in the "Manager Approval", "Team Lead Approval", and "Product Manager Approval" fields respectively.
7. Select the status of your request from the "Request Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select your department to which you belong.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are requesting access from.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your requested access period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your requested access period.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, required/optional): Enter your reason for requesting access.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether your manager has approved your request.
* **Team Lead Approval** (`team_lead_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether your team lead has approved your request.
* **Product Manager Approval** (`product_manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether your product manager has approved your request.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of your request.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
