# Contract Change Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about proposed changes to contracts. It's used to assess the nature of the change, its potential impact, and what actions should be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Requestor Name, Title, and Department.
2. Enter the Request Date for the contract change.
3. Enter the Contract Reference, such as contract name or number.
4. Enter the original Contract Date.
5. Select the Vendor or Contractor involved in the change.
6. Choose the Change Category (e.g., Scope of work, Pricing, Timeline, etc.).
7. Describe the Change in detail.
8. Explain the Reason for the Change.
9. Indicate who initiated the Change (e.g., our organization, vendor, mutual agreement, or external requirement).
10. Select the potential Cost Impact of the change (e.g., no cost impact, cost increase, cost decrease, or cost neutral).
11. If applicable, enter the dollar amount of the Cost Impact.
12. Select the Schedule Impact of the change (e.g., no delay, minor delay, moderate delay, or significant delay).
13. If applicable, enter the number of Schedule Impact Days.
14. Choose the Quality Impact of the change (e.g., no impact, positive impact, negative impact, or unknown).
15. Assess the Risk Level of the change (e.g., low risk, medium risk, high risk, or critical risk).
16. If applicable, describe any Alternative Options considered.
17. Identify any stakeholders affected by the change.
18. Select the Recommended Action for the change (e.g., approve as requested, approve with modifications, deny, or defer for more information).
19. Select the Approvals Needed for the change (e.g., project manager, department head, finance, legal, executive, or board).
20. Add any Additional Comments about the change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`): Your full name.
* **Requestor Title** (`requestor_title`): Your job title.
* **Department** (`department`): Your department within the organization.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Date of the request.
* **Contract Reference** (`contract_reference`): Contract name or number.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`): Original contract date.
* **Vendor or Contractor** (`vendor_or_contractor`): The name of the other party involved in the change.
* **Change Category** (`change_category`): Type of change (e.g., scope of work, pricing, timeline, etc.).
* **Change Description** (`change_description`): Describe the proposed change in detail.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`): Explain why the change is needed.
* **Change Initiated By** (`change_initiated_by`): Who initiated the change (e.g., our organization, vendor, mutual agreement, or external requirement).
* **Cost Impact** (`cost_impact`): Potential financial effect of the change (e.g., no cost impact, cost increase, cost decrease, or cost neutral).
* **Cost Impact Amount** (`cost_impact_amount`): If the Cost Impact is a cost increase or decrease, enter the dollar amount.
* **Schedule Impact** (`schedule_impact`): Potential timeline effect of the change (e.g., no delay, minor delay, moderate delay, or significant delay).
* **Schedule Impact Days** (`schedule_impact_days`): If the Schedule Impact is a delay, enter the number of days.
* **Quality Impact** (`quality_impact`): Effect on quality (e.g., no impact, positive impact, negative impact, or unknown).
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`): Risk level of the change (e.g., low risk, medium risk, high risk, or critical risk).
* **Alternatives Considered** (`alternatives_considered`): If applicable, describe any alternative options explored.
* **Stakeholder Impact** (`stakeholder_impact`): Identify any stakeholders affected by the change.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`): Suggest the recommended course of action (e.g., approve as requested, approve with modifications, deny, or defer for more information).
* **Approvals Needed** (`approvals_needed`): Select the approvals required for the change (e.g., project manager, department head, finance, legal, executive, or board).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional information about the change.
