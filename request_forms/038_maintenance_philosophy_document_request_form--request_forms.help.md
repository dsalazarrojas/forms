# Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Philosophy Document Request Form is a document that captures the essential practices, processes, and principles that govern the maintenance and upkeep of equipment, systems, or facilities. It is a crucial record that helps improve equipment reliability and reduce downtime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **What is the purpose of the request?**: Briefly describe the maintenance philosophy document you are requesting.
2. **Choose a category**: Select the category that best matches the type of maintenance philosophy document you are requesting. (Facilities, Operations, or Other)
3. **Choose the urgency**: Select the level of urgency for your request. (High, Medium, or Low)
4. **Preferred format**: Select the format you would like the document to be delivered in. (PDF, DOCX, or CSV)
5. **Contact Information**:
   * **Contact name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
   * **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information.
   * **Phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information.
6. **Message**: Provide a brief description of your request and any additional information that may be relevant to the maintenance philosophy document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the purpose of the request?** (`purpose`, text, required): Briefly describe the maintenance philosophy document you are requesting.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best matches the type of maintenance philosophy document you are requesting. (Facilities, Operations, or Other)
* **What is the urgency of the request?** (`urgency`, select_one, optional): Select the level of urgency for your request. (High, Medium, or Low)
* **What format do you prefer?** (`format_preferred`, select_multiple, optional): Select the format you would like the document to be delivered in. (PDF, DOCX, or CSV)
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required): Provide a brief description of your request and any additional information that may be relevant to the maintenance philosophy document.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in the form.
* Only submit a new request if it is not a duplicate of a previously submitted request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant team for assistance.
