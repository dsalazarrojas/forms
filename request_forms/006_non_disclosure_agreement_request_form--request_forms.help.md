# Non Disclosure Agreement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Disclosure Agreement Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking non-disclosure agreements, which are legal contracts that prevent the exchange of confidential information. This form helps us assess their request and ensure that we can provide the necessary guidance and documentation to support their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for your request in the Request Reason field.
3. If applicable, include any relevant details about the agreement you are requesting in the Agreement Details field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
5. Select your country of origin from the Country field.
6. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Disclosure Agreement Request Form** (`non_disclosure_agreement_request_form`, text, required: false): This is the main field for typing in your request.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a non-disclosure agreement.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, required: false): If you have specific requirements or constraints for the agreement, please describe them here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): If you are representing a company or organization, please enter its name here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical address for contact purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required: false): Enter your postal zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required: false): Select your country of origin.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and information about your request.

Please note that all fields marked as "required: false" are optional, and you can leave them blank if you do not have the necessary information. However, please make sure to provide as much information as possible to help us process your request efficiently.
