# fe_exam_study_plan_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students about their study plans, preferences, and schedule for a specific period or semester.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "studentName" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phoneNumber" field.
4. Select the subject of your study in the "subject" field.
5. Enter the date you want to start your study in the "studyStartDate" field.
6. Choose the time you want to start your study in the "studyStartTime" field.
7. Specify the duration of your study in the "studyDuration" field.
8. Select where you want to conduct your study in the "studyLocation" field (e.g., On Campus, Off Campus, or Online).
9. Choose how often you would like to study (e.g., Once, Twice, Three Times, Four Times, More Than Four Times) in the "studyFrequency" field.
10. Select a date range for your study start in the "studyStartDateRange" field.
11. Select a time range for your study start in the "studyStartTimeRange" field.
12. Specify the duration of your study in the "studyDurationRange" field.
13. Enter any additional notes about your study plan in the "notes" field.
14. Enter your name in the "submitterName" field.
15. Provide your email address in the "submitterEmail" field.
16. Enter your phone number in the "submitterPhone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Plan Request Form** (`Study Plan Request Form`, text, required): This is the main title of the form.
* **Student Name** (`studentName`, text, optional): Enter your student name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phoneNumber`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): Specify the subject of your study.
* **Study Start Date** (`studyStartDate`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to start your study.
* **Study Start Time** (`studyStartTime`, time, optional): Choose the time you want to start your study.
* **Study Duration** (`studyDuration`, number, optional): Specify the duration of your study.
* **Study Location** (`studyLocation`, text, optional): Select where you want to conduct your study (e.g., On Campus, Off Campus, or Online).
* **Study Method** (`studyMethod`, select_one, optional): Choose how you want to conduct your study (e.g., On Campus, Off Campus, or Online).
* **Study Frequency** (`studyFrequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you want to study (e.g., Once, Twice, Three Times, Four Times, More Than Four Times).
* **Study Start Date Range** (`studyStartDateRange`, date, optional): Select a date range for your study start.
* **Study Start Time Range** (`studyStartTimeRange`, time, optional): Select a time range for your study start.
* **Study Duration Range** (`studyDurationRange`, number, optional): Specify the duration of your study.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your study plan.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitterName`, text, optional): Enter your name for submission purposes.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitterEmail`, email, optional): Enter your email address for submission purposes.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitterPhone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for submission purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields (if any) correctly to avoid submission errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
* This form is used for planning purposes, and the information provided will be used to create a schedule for your studies.
