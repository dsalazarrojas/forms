# public_road_use_coordination_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information for public road use coordination.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form in the following pages: 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Text, Optional): Fill out your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Request Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the nature of your request, including the type of request (e.g., Public Utility Use, Emergency Repair, etc.).
* **Request Date** (Date, Optional): Fill out the date of your request.
* **Request Time** (Time, Optional): Fill out the time of your request.
* **Request Duration** (Number, Optional): Fill out the duration of your request.
* **Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Fill out your name.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Fill out your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Fill out your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Fill out your address.
* **Location Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional location details.
* **Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Provide any special instructions for the request.
* **Vehicle Information** (Text, Optional): Provide any information about the vehicle you are using.
* **Equipment Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any equipment details related to your request.
* **Attachments** (Text, Optional): Attach any relevant documents or images to your request.
* **Request Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of request (Public Utility Use, Emergency Repair, etc.).
* **Request Purpose** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the purpose of your request (Inspection, Repair, Maintenance, etc.).
* **Location** (Select One, Required): Choose the correct location (City, County, State).
* **Road Name** (Text, Optional): Fill out the name of the road.
* **Road Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the correct road type (Local, State, Federal).
* **Road Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the correct road status (Open, Closed, Restricted).
* **Road Condition** (Text, Optional): Fill out any remarks about the road condition.
* **Remarks** (Text, Optional): Fill out any additional remarks.
* **Road Condition** (Text, Optional): Fill out any remarks about the road condition.
* **Signature** (Note, Optional): Sign the form (note: not actually a signature field but a placeholder for your actual signature).
