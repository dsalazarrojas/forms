# Air Filtration Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Filtration Upgrade Request Form is designed to gather information from users requesting an air filtration upgrade. This form helps the requester provide essential details about their needs and preferences for the upgrade, enabling the upgrade process to be efficiently and effectively executed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Requester Name" field. This will help the team identify you as the person requesting the upgrade.
2. In the "Department or Unit" field, select the department or unit responsible for paying for the upgrade (if known).
3. In the "Location" field, enter the building and room number where the upgrade is needed.
4. Choose a reason for the upgrade in the "Reason for Upgrade" field. This can be due to Health / Safety Concern, Equipment End of Life, Compliance / Policy Change, New Room Usage, or Odor Control.
5. If known, select the "Current System Type" in the "Current System Type" field. This will help the team understand the existing system and choose the best upgrade option.
6. Select the "Desired Upgrade Level" from the available options to specify the target filtration level for the upgrade.
7. Choose the "Urgency" for the upgrade from the available options to provide a clear timeline for the installation.
8. If you have a budget code, enter it in the "Budget Code" field for internal billing purposes.
9. In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any specific requirements or additional information about the upgrade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name to help identify you as the person requesting the upgrade.
* **Department or Unit**: Select the department or unit responsible for paying for the upgrade, if known. If unknown, please leave this field blank.
* **Location**: Enter the building and room number where the upgrade is needed for the upgrade team to locate the area accurately.
* **Reason for Upgrade**: Choose a reason for the upgrade from the provided options (Health / Safety Concern, Equipment End of Life, Compliance / Policy Change, New Room Usage, or Odor Control) to help the upgrade team understand the priority and urgency of the request.
* **Current System Type**: If known, select the existing system type to help the team determine the best course of action for the upgrade.
* **Desired Upgrade Level**: Choose the target filtration level for the upgrade from the available options (MERV 13 Upgrade, HEPA Filtration, UV-C Sanitization, or Portable Air Scrubber).
* **Urgency**: Select the urgency of the upgrade from the provided options (Low, Medium, or High) to help the upgrade team prioritize and schedule the upgrade accordingly.
* **Budget Code**: Enter your internal budget code for billing purposes, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any specific requirements or additional information about the upgrade to help the upgrade team execute the process efficiently.
