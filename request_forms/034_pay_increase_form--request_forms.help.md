# Pay Increase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a pay increase for an employee. It is an essential part of the payroll process to ensure that employees receive fair compensation for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee for whom you are requesting a pay increase.
2. **Select the Increase Type**: Choose the type of pay increase from the dropdown menu. Options include Percentage, Dollar Amount, Bonus/Commutative, and Other.
3. **Enter the Amount of Increase**: Enter the amount of the pay increase in numerical format.
4. **Enter the Effective Date**: Enter the date on which the pay increase will take effect.
5. **Attach a Justification Letter**: If necessary, attach a letter explaining the reason for the pay increase.
6. **Choose the Approval Status**: Choose the status of the approval from the dropdown menu. Options include Approved, Denied, and Pending.
7. **Enter the Signature of Decision Maker**: Enter the signature of the person making the approval decision.
8. **Enter Comments**: If desired, add any comments regarding the pay increase.
9. **Enter the Approvers Name**: Enter the name of the person who approved the pay increase.
10. **Enter the Approvers Email**: Enter the email of the person who approved the pay increase.
11. **Enter the Approvers Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who approved the pay increase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: The name of the employee for whom you are requesting a pay increase. 
* **Increase Type (2)**: Select the type of pay increase from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	+ **Percentage**: A percentage-based pay increase.
	+ **Dollar Amount**: A dollar-based pay increase.
	+ **Bonus/Commutative**: A bonus or commutative pay increase.
	+ **Other**: Other type of pay increase.
* **Amount of Increase (3)**: Enter the amount of the pay increase in numerical format.
* **Effective Date (4)**: The date on which the pay increase will take effect.
* **Justification Letter (5)**: An optional field to attach a letter explaining the reason for the pay increase.
* **Approval Status (6)**: The status of the approval from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	+ **Approved**: The pay increase has been approved.
	+ **Denied**: The pay increase has been denied.
	+ **Pending**: The pay increase is pending approval.
* **Signature of Decision Maker (7)**: Enter the signature of the person making the approval decision.
* **Comments (8)**: An optional field to add comments regarding the pay increase.
* **Approvers Name (9)**: Enter the name of the person who approved the pay increase.
* **Approvers Email (10)**: Enter the email of the person who approved the pay increase.
* **Approvers Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of the person who approved the pay increase.
