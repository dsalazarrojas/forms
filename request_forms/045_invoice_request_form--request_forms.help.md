# Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Request Form is a tool used by users to submit their requests for invoices. It collects essential information that helps in processing and tracking these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your request in the "Request An Invoice" field.
2. Provide the due date for the invoice in the "Due Date" field (if applicable).
3. Enter the amount due for the invoice in the "Amount Due" field.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Fill out your contact details, including phone number and email, in the "Contact Details" and "Contact Email" fields respectively.
6. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Message" field.
7. Select the tool that will be used to process your request from the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Choose who submitted this request from the "Submitted By" field.
9. Enter the date and time you submitted your request in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields.
10. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request An Invoice**: A brief description of your request.
* **Due Date**: The date by which the invoice is due (if applicable). Format: "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Amount Due**: The total amount due for the invoice. This should be a numerical value.
* **Payment Method**: The preferred method of payment for the invoice. Options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal.
* **Contact Details**: Your contact details, including address or other relevant information.
* **Contact Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Your contact email.
* **Message**: Any additional comments or messages about your request.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool that will be used to process your request. Options: chatjimmy, chatjimmy2.
* **Submitted By**: The person or entity submitting the request. Options: John, Jane, Jill.
* **Submit Date**: The date on which you submitted your request.
* **Submit Time**: The time at which you submitted your request.
* **Note**: Any additional notes about your request.
* **Payment Method**: The preferred method of payment for the invoice.
* **Payment Method**: You can choose one or multiple payment methods.
