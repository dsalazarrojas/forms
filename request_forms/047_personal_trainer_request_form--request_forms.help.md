# personal_trainer_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a personal trainer at the gym. Please fill out the form with your information and preferences to help us tailor a training plan that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. **Request Info**: This is the main description of your request. Please provide as much detail as possible about your fitness goals and what you hope to achieve with a personal trainer.
2. **Name**: Please enter your full name.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
5. **Request Reason**: Describe why you're requesting a personal trainer. What are your fitness goals and what motivates you to work out?
6. **Best Contact Method**: How would you like to be contacted about your request? Please select one of the following options:
	* Contact: We will contact you in person or by phone.
	* Email: We will contact you via email.
	* Phone: We will contact you by phone.
	* Mail: We will contact you by mail.
7. **Availability**: When would you like to begin your personal training sessions? Choose a date from the calendar or type in a specific date.
8. **Preferred Contact Time**: When would you prefer to be contacted? Choose a time of day that works best for you.
9. **Note**: Any additional comments or questions you'd like to share with us?
10. **Trainer Info**: Is there anything specific you'd like your trainer to know about you? Please provide any relevant health information or personal anecdotes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Info**: 
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Request Reason**: 
* **Best Contact Method**: Select how you'd like to be contacted.
* **Availability**: Choose a date or type in a specific date.
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Choose a time of day for contact.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or questions.
* **Trainer Info**: Share any relevant health information or personal anecdotes with your trainer.
