# it-modernization-advisory-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect modernization initiative requests from various business units to be assessed and prioritized by the IT department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate IT Department that best matches the initiative or project being requested.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the modernization initiative or project in the "Modernization Initiative" field.
3. Add more details about the project in the "Project Details" field.
4. Specify the goals and objectives of the modernization initiative or project in the "Goals" field.
5. Add any additional comments or clarifications on the goals in the "Goals Notes" field.
6. Indicate the level of urgency for the request in the "Urgency" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the advisory request in the "Advisory Request" field.
8. Add any additional comments or clarifications on the advisory request in the "Advisory Request Notes" field.
9. Enter the contact information of the business lead who requested the modernization initiative or project in the "Business Lead Contact" fields.
10. Complete any other required fields as indicated in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Department** (required): Select the department that best matches the initiative or project being requested.
  • This will help the IT department to quickly route the request to the correct team.
* **Modernization Initiative** (text): Provide a clear and concise description of the modernization initiative or project.
  • This will help the IT department to understand the scope and nature of the request.
* **Project Details** (text): Add more details about the project.
  • This will help the IT department to better understand the project's requirements and complexity.
* **Goals** (text): Specify the goals and objectives of the modernization initiative or project.
  • This will help the IT department to align the request with the company's overall goals and objectives.
* **Goals Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or clarifications on the goals.
  • This is optional but highly recommended for better understanding.
* **Urgency** (text): Indicate the level of urgency for the request.
  • This will help the IT department to prioritize the requests based on urgency.
* **Advisory Request** (text): Provide a brief description of the advisory request.
  • This will help the IT department to understand the scope and nature of the request.
* **Advisory Request Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or clarifications on the advisory request.
  • This is optional but highly recommended for better understanding.
* **IT Department Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or clarifications for the IT department.
  • This is optional but highly recommended for better communication.
* **Business Lead Contact** (text): Enter the contact information of the business lead who requested the modernization initiative or project.
  • This will help the IT department to contact the business lead for more information or clarification.
* **Business Lead Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the business lead contact.
  • This will help the IT department to contact the business lead for more information or clarification.
* **Business Lead Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the business lead contact.
  • This will help the IT department to contact the business lead for more information or clarification.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible to ensure the IT department can properly assess and prioritize your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department for clarification.
* If you are submitting a duplicate request, please make sure to update the existing request instead of submitting a new one.
