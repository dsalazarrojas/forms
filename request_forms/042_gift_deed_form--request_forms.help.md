# gift_deed_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gift_deed_form is used to document and verify the transfer of gifts between individuals. It captures the grantor's and grantee's information, including their names, addresses, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Grantor Information**: 
   - Enter the grantor's name.
   - Provide the grantor's date of birth.
   - Enter the grantor's address.
   - Enter the grantor's city.
   - Select whether the grantor resides in the state (yes/no).
   - Enter the grantor's zip code.
   - Enter the grantor's signature.

2. **Grantee Information**: 
   - Enter the grantee's name.
   - Provide the grantee's date of birth.
   - Enter the grantee's address.
   - Enter the grantee's city.
   - Select whether the grantee resides in the state (yes/no).
   - Enter the grantee's zip code.

3. **Gift Information**: 
   - Enter the date of the gift.
   - Enter a brief description of the gift.
   - Enter the value of the gift.
   - Select the type of gift (yes/no).
   - Select whether a notary public is involved (yes/no).

4. **Notary Information**: 
   - Enter the notary public's name.
   - Select whether the notary public is involved (yes/no).
   - Select whether the notary's seal is attached (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grantor Name**: Enter the name of the individual giving the gift.
* **Grantor Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual giving the gift.
* **Grantor Address**: Enter the address of the individual giving the gift.
* **Grantor City**: Enter the city where the grantor resides.
* **Grantor State**: Check whether the grantor resides in the state (yes/no).
* **Grantor Zip**: Enter the zip code where the grantor resides.
* **Grantee Name**: Enter the name of the individual receiving the gift.
* **Grantee Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual receiving the gift.
* **Grantee Address**: Enter the address of the individual receiving the gift.
* **Grantee City**: Enter the city where the grantee resides.
* **Grantee State**: Check whether the grantee resides in the state (yes/no).
* **Grantee Zip**: Enter the zip code where the grantee resides.
* **Date of Gift**: Enter the date the gift was given.
* **Description of Gift**: Enter a brief description of the gift.
* **Value of Gift**: Enter the value of the gift.
* **Type of Gift**: Check whether the gift is of a certain type (yes/no).
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Grantor Signature**: Enter the grantor's signature.
* **Grantee Signature**: Enter the grantee's signature.
* **Acknowledgement**: Enter any acknowledgement related to the gift.
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was filled.
* **Notary Public**: Check whether a notary public is involved (yes/no).
* **Notary Name**: Enter the notary public's name.
* **Notary State**: Check whether the notary public resides in the state (yes/no).
* **Notary Seal**: Enter whether the notary public's seal is attached (yes/no).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check the information for accuracy before submission.
- Ensure that the grantor and grantee have signed the form properly.
- Attach the notary public's seal, if applicable.
