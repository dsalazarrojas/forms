# Sustainable Materials Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who are interested in receiving a quote for sustainable materials or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project name and location where the materials will be used.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Select the quote request date.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you require any sustainability certifications.
5. Provide any additional information about the project, including the project type and any other relevant details.
6. Agree or disagree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **quote_request**: Enter the project name and location where the materials will be used.
* **project_name**: Enter the name of the project you are interested in.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **quote_request_date**: Select the date you would like to receive the quote.
* **quote_requester_name**: Enter your name as the person requesting the quote.
* **project_location**: Enter the location of the project.
* **quote_requester_company**: Enter your company name.
* **requested_quote_amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting a quote for.
* **sustainability_certifications**: Check "Yes" if you require any sustainability certifications, or "No" if you do not.
* **quote_requester_title**: Enter your title.
* **quote_requester_role**: Enter your role in the company.
* **project_type**: Select the type of project (e.g. commercial, residential, etc.).
* **other_project_type**: If the project type is not listed, enter "other" and provide a brief description.
* **quote_requester_phone_extension**: Enter the extension of your phone number.
* **quote_requester_note**: Enter any additional information about the project.
* **quote_requester_address**: Enter your address.
* **quote_requester_city**: Enter your city.
* **quote_requester_state**: Enter your state or province.
* **quote_requester_zip**: Enter your zip or postal code.
* **quote_requester_country**: Enter your country.
* **quote_requester_phone**: Enter your phone number again.
* **quote_requester_email**: Enter your email address again.
* **agree_to_terms**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" or "I disagree to the terms and conditions".
