# Elderly Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your meal preferences and special dietary needs, so we can provide you with the best possible meal delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and preferences. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date** (Required): Enter the date you would like to receive your meal delivery.
* **Time** (Required): Enter the time you would like to receive your meal delivery.
* **Meal Type** (Required): Select the type of meal you would like to receive (Senior, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack).
* **Frequency** (Required): Select how often you would like to receive meal delivery (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely).
* **Meal Time** (Required): Select the specific time slot for your meal delivery (7:00 am - 9:00 am, 9:00 am - 12:00 pm, 12:00 pm - 1:00 pm, 1:00 pm - 3:00 pm, 3:00 pm - 5:00 pm, 5:00 pm - 7:00 pm, 7:00 pm - 9:00 pm, or 9:00 pm - 11:00 pm).
* **Frequency Details** (Optional): Enter any additional details about your meal frequency preferences.
* **Special Diet** (Required): Select any special dietary needs (None, Dairy, or Gluten).
* **Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments or special requests.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select the payment method you would like to use (Cash, Online Payment, PayPal, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Gateway** (Optional): Enter the payment gateway used (not required, but helpful for our staff to process payment).
* **Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Confirm** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agree to our Terms of Service.
* **Terms of Service** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agree to our Terms of Service.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address (not required, but helpful for us to contact you).
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number (not required, but helpful for us to contact you).
* **Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Meal Delivery Service** (Optional): Enter any specific details about your meal delivery service preferences.
* **Meal Delivery Address** (Optional): Enter your address for meal delivery.
* **Meal Delivery Contact** (Optional): Enter your contact information for meal delivery.
* **Meal Delivery Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about your meal delivery preferences.
* **Delivery Instructions** (Optional): Enter any specific instructions for your meal delivery.
* **Delivery Details** (Optional): Enter any additional details about your meal delivery preferences.

Please review each field carefully and ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting your request. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
