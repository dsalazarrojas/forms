# research_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The research_request_form is a tool used to collect information about research requests. This form is used to gather details about the research topic, study type, and other relevant information that will help in the research process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
3. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **research_request_form_page**: This is the title of the form, and you can leave it as is.
* **request_details**: This field is used to enter a brief description of the research request. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **research_study**: This field asks whether the research topic is a study or not. If it is, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **research_studies**: This field allows you to choose multiple research studies. Select all applicable studies that apply to your request.
* **research_type**: This field asks whether the research topic is a type or not. If it is, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **study_details**: This field is used to enter additional details about the research topic.
* **date**: This field is used to enter the date of the research request.
* **time**: This field is used to enter the time of the research request.
* **research_contact**: This field is used to enter the contact information for the research request.
* **research_contact_phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number for the research contact.
* **research_contact_email**: This field is used to enter the email address for the research contact.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and clarity in filling out the form.
* Use proper format for date and time fields.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research team.
