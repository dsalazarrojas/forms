# Monthly Coaching Call Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of the monthly coaching calls, including the date and duration of the call, and any notes taken during the call. Please fill out this form to ensure that all relevant information is captured and stored for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the coaching call from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the duration of the call in minutes.
3. Add any relevant notes from the call in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coaching Call Date** (`coaching_call_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to select the date of the coaching call. Please use the calendar picker to choose the correct date.
* **Call Duration** (`call_duration`, number, required: false): This field is used to enter the duration of the coaching call in minutes. Please enter a whole number value.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is used to capture any notes taken during the call. Please use this space to jot down any important details or action items from the call.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure that accurate information is captured.
* If you have any issues with the form, please reach out to [support contact] for assistance.
* This form is intended to be completed on a monthly basis, so please fill it out at the beginning of each month to keep your coaching call records up to date.
* If you have multiple calls in a single month, please create a separate entry for each call.
