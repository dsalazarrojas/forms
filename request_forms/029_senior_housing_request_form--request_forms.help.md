# senior_housing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "senior_housing_request_form" is a form designed to help individuals find senior living communities or housing options that meet their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Senior Living Community**: Type in the name of the senior living community or organization you are interested in.
2. **Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents or as you would like it to be represented.
3. **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
4. **Email** (Optional): If you have an email address, enter it here. This can be used for faster communication with senior living communities or organizations.
5. **Date of Visit**: Select the date you plan to visit the senior living community or organization.
6. **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by the senior living community or organization. Options include:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Phone/Text/Email
	* Mail

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the senior living community or organization directly.
* This form is not intended to be used for other purposes, such as requesting services or submitting applications.
* Fill out the form only once and wait for follow-up contact from senior living communities or organizations.
