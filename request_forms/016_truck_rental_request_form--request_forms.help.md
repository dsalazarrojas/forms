# truck_rental_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for truck rental purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the required fields.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if required).
5. If applicable, enter your company name and details in the "Company" field.
6. Enter the location where the rental will take place in the "Location" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the rental in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Select the type of rental from the options in the "Type" field.
9. Select the types of rentals from the options in the "Types" field.
10. Enter any additional notes about the rental in the "Note" field.
11. Confirm your email address in the "Email Confirmation" field.
12. Confirm your phone number in the "Phone Confirmation" field.
13. Confirm your company details in the "Company Confirmation" field.
14. Confirm your location in the "Location Confirmation" field.
15. Confirm the date and time of the rental in the "Date Confirmation" field and "Time Confirmation" fields.
16. Enter a unique form ID in the "Form ID" field.
17. Enter any additional user input in the "User Input" field.
18. Select another type of rental from the options in the "Another Type" field.
19. Select another type of rental from the options in the "Another Types" field.
20. Enter another note about the rental in the "Another Note" field.
21. Confirm another email address in the "Another Email" field.
22. Confirm another phone number in the "Another Phone" field.
23. Confirm another date and time of the rental in the "Another Date" field and "Another Time" fields.
