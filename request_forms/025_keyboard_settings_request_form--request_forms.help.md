# Keyboard Settings Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their keyboard settings preferences. It is designed to be completed by end users who need to request specific keyboard settings to be enabled or disabled on their devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your user account:
	* **User Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your keyboard preferences:
	* **Keyboard Preferences**: Choose the type of keyboard you are using (e.g. Laptop, Desktop, External keyboard).
3. Indicate your preferred keyboard settings:
	* **Preferred Keyboards**: Choose whether you prefer to have your keyboard settings set to "Yes" or "No".
4. Set your priority:
	* **Priority**: Enter the priority level for your request (optional).
5. Add any additional comments:
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional information about your request.
6. Enter your email address:
	* **Email**: Enter your email address (this will be used to contact you with updates on your request).
7. Enter your phone number:
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Set a date and time:
	* **Date**: Enter the date on which you would like your keyboard settings to be changed.
	* **Time**: Enter the time on which you would like your keyboard settings to be changed.
9. Add any additional notes:
	* **Note**: Enter any additional information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, required): This field is used to collect your name, email, and phone number.
* **Keyboard Preferences** (keyboard_preferences, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the type of keyboard you are using (e.g. Laptop, Desktop, External keyboard).
* **Preferred Keyboards** (preferred_keyboards, select_one, required): This field indicates whether you prefer to have your keyboard settings set to "Yes" or "No".
* **Priority** (priority, number, optional): This field allows you to set the priority level for your request (optional).
* **Comments** (comments, text, required): This field is used to enter any additional information about your request.
* **Email** (email, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address (this will be used to contact you with updates on your request).
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date** (date, date, required): This field is used to set the date on which you would like your keyboard settings to be changed.
* **Time** (time, time, required): This field is used to set the time on which you would like your keyboard settings to be changed.
* **Note** (note, note, required): This field is used to enter any additional information about your request.
