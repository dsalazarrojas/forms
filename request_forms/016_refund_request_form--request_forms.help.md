# Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Request Form is a standardized template for customers to submit refund requests. It collects essential information about the request, allowing staff to efficiently process and manage refunds.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your order details and a clear explanation of why you're requesting a refund.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email, so we can get back to you.
3. Select a reason for your refund request and choose a status (pending, approved, or denied).
4. If you're an authorized user, select who will be assigned to process your refund request.
5. Enter the date when you submitted the request and click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID** (`Order ID`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the order you'd like to request a refund for.
* **Reason for Refund** (`Reason for Refund`, text, required): Type a clear and concise explanation of why you're requesting a refund. Include any details that may help us process your request.
* **Refund Amount** (`Refund Amount`, number, optional): If you know the exact amount you'd like to refund, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank and our staff will assist you in determining the correct amount.
* **Customer Message** (`Customer Message`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you think may be helpful to support your refund request.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get back to you quickly.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the status of your refund request.
* **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, select_one, optional): Select the staff member you'd like to be assigned to process your refund request.
* **Assigned User Note** (`Assigned User Note`, note, optional): If you have any notes or comments for the assigned staff member, enter them here.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you submitted the request.
* **Status** (`Status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your refund request (pending, approved, or denied).
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the form was submitted by a staff member.

Note: Please ensure that the order has not already been refunded or cancelled before submitting this form.
