# toy_product_missing_parts_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report missing parts for a toy product. It's intended for users to provide the necessary information about the product and the missing parts, which will be reviewed and addressed by the relevant teams or departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the product name**: Provide the name of the toy product that is missing parts.
2. **Select the part type**: Choose the type of part that is missing from the product.
3. **Describe the part**: Briefly describe the part that is missing.
4. **Specify replacement request**: If you're requesting a replacement for the missing part, provide a detailed description of what you need.
5. **Choose the seller**: Select the seller responsible for the missing part.
6. **Select the customer service representative**: Choose the customer service representative who will handle your request.
7. **Enter contact email**: Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
8. **Enter contact phone number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: 
  * **Product Name** (text, required: false): The name of the toy product that is missing parts. Please enter the name of the product as it is printed on the packaging or as it is commonly known.
* **part_type**: 
  * **part_type** (select_one, required: true): The type of part that is missing from the product. Choose the correct option from the list to ensure that the issue is reported correctly.
* **part_description**: 
  * **part_description** (text, required: false): A brief description of the missing part. If it's a specific component or a feature of the product, please describe it in detail.
* **replacement_request**: 
  * **replacement_request** (text, required: false): If you're requesting a replacement for the missing part, please provide a detailed description of what you need.
* **seller_id**: 
  * **seller_id** (select_multiple, required: true): The seller responsible for the missing part. This is a critical field, please select the correct option to ensure that the issue is reported correctly.
* **customer_service_id**: 
  * **customer_service_id** (select_one, required: true): The customer service representative who will handle your request. This will ensure that your issue is addressed promptly and efficiently.
* **email**: 
  * **email** (email, required: false): Your email address for follow-up communication. Please enter a valid email address so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **phone**: 
  * **phone** (text, required: false): Your phone number for follow-up communication. Please enter a valid phone number so that we can contact you regarding your request.

Note: Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately, and follow up with the relevant team or customer service representative regarding your request.
