# propane_tank_return_and_refund_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who need to request a propane tank return and refund. If you have a propane tank that needs to be returned due to a defect or other valid reason, please use this form to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Enter the tank's serial number in the "Tank Serial Number" field.
3. Describe the reason for returning the tank in the "Reason for Return" field.
4. Select the date you received the tank from the "Date Received" field.
5. Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund in the "Return Amount" field.
6. Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. If applicable, specify the name of the delivery person who delivered the tank in the "Delivery Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Tank Serial Number** (`tank_serial_number`, text, Required): Enter the serial number of the propane tank you are requesting to return.
* **Tank Model** (`tank_model`, text, Optional): If you know the model of the tank, you can enter it here. (Not required but helps with processing.)
* **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return`, text, Optional): Describe why you are returning the tank. Examples: Defect, not the right tank for my needs, etc.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, Optional): Enter the date you received the tank, if known.
* **Return Amount** (`return_amount`, number, Optional): Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person`, text, Optional): If you know the name of the person who delivered the tank, you can enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
