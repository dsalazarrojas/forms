# Service Call Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Call Request Form is a tool used to capture essential information for requesting service calls to be performed by customer service agents. This form helps ensure that the necessary details are collected accurately and promptly, allowing for efficient handling of service calls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Service Call Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's information in the **Company Information** field.
2. Provide your company's **Name**, **Address**, and **Contact Person** details in the respective fields.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** and **Email** for communication purposes.
4. Describe the **Description of Issue** you are experiencing.
5. Select the **Priority** of the service call (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Specify the **Service Requested**, if applicable.
7. Choose a date and time for the **Service Call Date** and **Service Call Time**.
8. Indicate the expected **Service Call Duration** and **Service Call Frequency** (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
9. Select the **Assigned Tool** to be used for the service call (chatjimmy, chatbot, or webhook).
10. Enter any notes for the **Customer Service Agent** and **Customer Service Agent Note**.
11. Enter the **Service Call Request Date** and **Service Call Request Time**.
12. Specify the **Service Call Requester** and **Service Call Requester Note**, if applicable.
13. Enter the **Service Call Resolution** and **Service Call Resolution Note**, if applicable.
14. Select the **Follow-up Status** and enter any **Follow-up Note**, if applicable.
15. Finally, enter the **Follow-up Date**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information**: Enter your company's name, address, and contact person details.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it should be displayed on all service calls.

* **Phone Number**: Enter your company's phone number for communication purposes.

* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the service call (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Service Requested**: If applicable, specify the service requested for the service call.
* **Service Call Date**: Choose a date for the service call.

* **Service Call Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the service call.
* **Service Call Frequency**: Select the frequency of service calls (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned for the service call (chatjimmy, chatbot, or webhook).
* **Customer Service Agent**: Enter any notes for the customer service agent.
* **Customer Service Agent Note**: Enter notes for the customer service agent.
* **Service Call Request Date**: Enter the date of the service call request.
* **Service Call Request Time**: Enter the time of the service call request.
