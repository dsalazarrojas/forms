# Leave Request Form (Spain) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record employee leave requests that have already been agreed upon with their manager. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by <Required>.
2. Enter your employee information and leave details.
3. Get your manager's signature and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, Optional): If you want to specify your department, please select it from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, Optional): Enter your job position.
* **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, Optional): If you're taking leave, enter your start and end dates.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of leave you are taking.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, Optional): Please provide a brief explanation of your request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, Optional): Get your manager's signature to approve your leave.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, Optional): Get your own signature to confirm you have submitted this form.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, Optional): If your manager has approved your leave, enter the date they approved it.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, Optional): Your manager will select this to indicate their approval status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Add any comments or notes from your manager.

Note: Required fields are marked as <Required> above. The fields with dropdown menus will have the options listed, while text fields should be filled out with your personal information.
