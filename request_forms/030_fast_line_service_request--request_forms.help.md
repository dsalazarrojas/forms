# Fast Line Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fast Line Service Request form is used to submit a service request to the relevant department or team. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the service needed, including the date and time of submission, any additional details, and any specific instructions or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Request** field with a brief description of the service you are requesting.
2. In the **Request Details** field, provide any additional information about the service needed.
3. Select the **Service Type** that best describes the type of service you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" for a service request, "No" for a query or question).
4. If applicable, select **Assigned To** to indicate who should be responsible for handling the request (e.g., "Yes" for a specific person or team).
5. Set the **Priority** level of the request (e.g., "Yes" for high priority, "No" for low priority).
6. Enter any **Notes** about the request, if necessary.
7. Provide your **Email** address and **Phone Number** for follow-up communication.
8. Add any **Comments** or additional information about the request.
9. Click the **Submit Request** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting. This field is used to help the service team understand the nature of your request.
* **Request Details**: Provide any additional information about the service needed. This field is optional, but it helps to ensure that the service team has all the necessary details to fulfill your request.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time when you submitted the request.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" for a service request, "No" for a query or question).
* **Service Details**: Provide any additional information about the service needed.
* **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" if you want a specific person or team to be assigned to handle your request.
* **Priority**: Set the priority level of your request (e.g., "Yes" for high priority, "No" for low priority).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the request, if necessary.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments about the request.
* **Submit Request**: Click this button to submit your request.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date when you submitted the request.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time when you submitted the request.
* **Followup Date**: Enter the date when you expect to be contacted or followed up on.
* **Followup Time**: Enter the time when you expect to be contacted or followed up on.

Note: This guide is based on the assumption that the form is for internal use within an organization or company, and the requests might be related to maintenance, repairs, or other types of services. If the form is used for external requests, some fields might not be applicable or relevant.
