# on_set_health_monitoring_service_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The on_set_health_monitoring_service_request form is used to request health monitoring services from our team. This form is intended for customers or users who need to schedule a health monitoring service at a specific location. The form helps us understand their requirements and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the crew that will be performing the health monitoring service.
2. Choose the production status of the service (Pre-Production, In-Production, or Post-Production).
3. Enter the location where the service will be performed (if not required, you can skip this step).
4. Choose the type of monitoring to be performed (Camera, Audio, or Other).
5. Enter the frequency of the service (if not required, you can skip this step).
6. Enter the duration of the service (in minutes).
7. Select the start time for the service.
8. Add any additional notes about the service.
9. Provide your name and email address (required for contact purposes).
10. Enter your phone number (optional).
11. Add any additional message for our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew** (1, `number`, required): Select the crew that will be performing the health monitoring service.
* **Production Status** (2, `select_one`, required): Choose the production status of the service (Pre-Production, In-Production, or Post-Production).
* **Location** (3, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the service will be performed.
* **Monitoring Type** (4, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of monitoring to be performed (Camera, Audio, or Other).
* **Frequency** (5, `text`, optional): Enter the frequency of the service (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Duration** (6, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the service (in minutes).
* **Start Time** (7, `time`, optional): Select the start time for the service.
* **Notes** (8, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or information about the service.
* **Name** (9, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (10, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (11, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (12, `text`, optional): Add any additional message for our team.
