# resident_park_improvement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resident_park_improvement_request_form is designed to collect and process requests from residents for park improvements or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one category that best describes the type of improvement or maintenance you're requesting.
2. Choose the equipment or facilities you'd like to be installed or upgraded.
3. Indicate how frequently you'd like the improvement or maintenance to be performed.
4. Enter the name of the park where the improvement or maintenance is needed.
5. Provide the address of the park where the improvement or maintenance is needed.
6. Enter your contact number so that the park staff can reach out to you.
7. Optionally, provide your email address for further communication (though it's not required).
8. Finally, describe your message or request in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Park Maintenance, <id: 2>
- **select_one_category** (`select_one`, Park Maintenance, required): This field helps us understand the type of improvement or maintenance needed in the park. Select one option to describe the work you'd like to be done.

### Equipment to be installed/upgraded, <id: 3>
- **select_multiple_equipment** (`select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to choose the specific equipment or facilities you'd like to be installed or upgraded in the park. Select all that apply from the provided options.

### Frequency of Improvement/Maintenance, <id: 4>
- **select_one_frequency** (`select_one`, Frequently, required): This field helps us determine how often you'd like the improvement or maintenance to be performed. Select one option to specify the frequency.

### Park Name, <id: 5>
- **park_name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the park where the improvement or maintenance is needed. This will help us locate the park and process your request accurately.

### Address, <id: 6>
- **address** (`text`, required): Provide the address of the park where the improvement or maintenance is needed. This will help us identify the exact location of the request.

### Contact Number, <id: 7>
- **contact_number** (`text`, required): Enter your contact number so that the park staff can reach out to you for further information or updates.

### Email (Optional), <id: 8>
- **email** (`email`, Optional): If you have an email address, provide it for further communication. However, please note that it's not required.

### Message/Request, <id: 9>
- **message** (`note`, required): In this field, describe your request or improvement in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand your needs and process your request accurately.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
- Select only one category, equipment, and frequency option per field.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the improvement or maintenance needed.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the park staff.
