# Boundary Cancellation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boundary Cancellation Request Form is used to request the cancellation of a geographical boundary. This form requires accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please provide all necessary and required fields with accurate information.
- Ensure that the information provided is complete and clear.
- Review and verify the accuracy of the information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (requester_first_name, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (requester_last_name, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Title or Position** (requester_title, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization Name** (organization_name, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Address** (organization_address, text, required): Enter your organization's complete business address.
* **Phone Number** (requester_phone, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address** (requester_email, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Request Date** (request_date, date, required): Enter the date of this request.
* **Boundary Type** (boundary_type, select_one, required): Select the type of boundary being cancelled. Choose from:
	+ Property boundary
	+ Service area boundary
	+ Jurisdictional boundary
	+ Administrative boundary
	+ Voting district boundary
	+ School district boundary
	+ Utility service boundary
	+ Other
* **Boundary Identifier** (boundary_identifier, text, required): Enter the official boundary name or number.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (cancellation_reason, select_one, required): Select the reason for boundary cancellation. Choose from:
	+ Merger with adjacent area
	+ Administrative reorganization
	+ Legal requirement
	+ Property sale or transfer
	+ Error in original boundary
	+ No longer needed
	+ Other
* **Detailed Explanation** (cancellation_reason_detail, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation for the cancellation.
* **Affected Area** (affected_area, text, required): Describe the area affected by the cancellation.
* **Affected Area Size** (affected_area_size, text, optional): Enter the approximate size of the affected area in acres or square feet (if applicable).
* **Legal Description** (legal_description, text, optional): Provide the legal description of the property or area (if applicable).
* **Parcel Numbers** (parcel_numbers, text, optional): List any relevant parcel or lot numbers (if applicable).
* **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, select_multiple, required): Select any supporting documents that apply to your request. Choose from:
	+ Survey map
	+ Legal deed
	+ Property title
	+ Court order
	+ Government resolution
	+ Authorization letter
	+ Other documentation
* **Stakeholder Notification** (stakeholder_notification, select_one, required): Confirm if affected parties have been notified. Choose from:
	+ Yes, all notified
	+ Partially notified
	+ No notification yet
	+ Not applicable
* **Public Hearing Required** (public_hearing_required, select_one, required): Confirm if a public hearing is required. Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Public Hearing Date** (public_hearing_date, date, optional): If a public hearing is required, enter the scheduled date (if applicable).
* **Requested Effective Date** (requested_effective_date, date, required): Enter the date when the cancellation should take effect.
* **Replacement Boundary** (replacement_boundary, select_one, required): Confirm if a new boundary will replace the cancelled one. Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Replacement Boundary Details** (replacement_boundary_details, text, optional): Describe the new boundary if applicable.
* **Authorization Level Required** (authorization_level, select_one, required): Select the level of approval needed for the cancellation. Choose from:
	+ Department level
	+ Manager level
	+ Director level
	+ Executive level
	+ Board approval
	+ Government approval
* **Requester Signature** (requester_signature, text, required): Type your full legal name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (signature_date, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information.
