# Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form is a tool used to gather information about missing luggage from passengers. It helps our team to quickly identify and retrieve the missing baggage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant airline from the list of options for the "Airline Details" field.
2. Choose the corresponding flight number and travel date for your missing luggage.
3. Enter the contact information you prefer to be contacted (phone or email).
4. If you need to report missing baggage, enter the details about what was in the luggage.
5. Fill in the flight number if known.
6. Provide a brief description of what was in your missing baggage.
7. Select how you would like to be contacted in case we need more information.
8. Enter the airline's phone number (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Airline Details (Air Canada, British Airways, Qantas, United Air Lines)** (Select): This is a list of airlines that we will be contacting for your missing luggage. Please select your airline for faster processing.
* **Passenger Contact Information (Mr, Mrs, Other)** (Select): Choose how you would like to be contacted in case we need more information.
* **Baggage Details (text)**: If you are missing baggage, describe the contents in this field.
* **Flight Information (date)**: Enter the travel date for your missing flight.
* **Flight Number (text)**: If you know the flight number, enter it to help our team locate your missing luggage.
* **Baggage Contents (text)**: Briefly describe the items that were in your missing baggage.
* **Contact Information (Email, Phone)** (Select): Select your preferred contact method (email or phone) so we can get in touch with you.
* **Airline Phone Number (text)**: If the airline has a phone number, please enter it.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your missing luggage.
## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing information about your missing luggage.
* If you have any additional information or documents that may help us locate your missing baggage, please attach them to your form submission.
* Our team will be in touch with you within 24-48 hours to provide further assistance.
