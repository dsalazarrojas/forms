# Moodle Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moodle Training Request Form is a tool used to submit a request for training on a specific topic or subject. It is intended for users who wish to enhance their skills or knowledge in a particular area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise description of the training you are requesting in the **Form Title** field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Category** field to indicate if the training request is related to a specific category.
3. Provide a detailed description of the training request in the **Description** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field for contact information.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field for further contact.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Assigned Tool** field to indicate if the training request is related to a specific tool.
7. Enter a unique numeric ID of the form in the **Form ID** field (optional).
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Category 2** field to indicate if the training request is related to a second category (optional).
9. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Category 3** field to indicate if the training request is related to a third category (optional).
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Assigned Tool 2** field to indicate if the training request is related to a second tool (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a clear and concise title of your training request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the training request is related to a specific category.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your training request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the training request is related to a specific tool.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required: false): Enter a unique numeric ID of the form (optional).
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the training request is related to a second category.
* **Category 3** (`category_3`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the training request is related to a third category.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if the training request is related to a second tool.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are using this form to request training that aligns with your job requirements and responsibilities.
* Use a clear and concise description in the **Description** field.
* Use "Yes" or "No" consistently for all select fields.
