# Cultural Integration Advisory Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from requesters to facilitate cultural integration. It's a request form that helps us understand the cultural background and specific needs of the requester, which is then used for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last names
2. Provide a valid email address
3. Enter your phone number (if applicable)
4. Specify your language
5. Enter your address
6. Provide the country of origin
7. Explain the reason for your cultural integration request
8. Describe any support needs
9. Add any follow-up notes
10. Enter the name of the advisor or organization you're working with
11. Enter the organization's name and email address
12. Enter the organization's phone number (if applicable)
13. Specify follow-up details (email, phone, or mail) and schedule
14. Choose the frequency of follow-up (daily, weekly, or monthly)
15. Choose the method of follow-up (email, phone, or mail)
16. Select the start and end dates for follow-up
17. Specify the location for follow-up
18. Describe the next steps
19. Enter your name and email address to confirm your submission

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: A valid email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number (if you want to be contacted by phone).
* **Language**: The language you prefer to communicate in.
* **Address**: Your address for follow-up purposes.
* **Country of origin**: The country where you were born or raised.
* **Reason for Request**: A brief explanation of why you're requesting cultural integration.
* **Support Needs**: Any additional support needs you may have.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Any additional information that will help us better understand your needs.
* **Advisor Name**: The name of the advisor or organization you're working with.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization you're working with.
* **Organization Email**: A valid email address for the organization.
* **Organization Phone**: The phone number of the organization (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Email**: The email address to use for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Phone**: The phone number to use for follow-up (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Frequency**: The frequency of follow-up (daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **Follow-up Method**: The method of follow-up (email, phone, or mail).
* **Follow-up Start Date**: The date when you'd like follow-up to start.
* **Follow-up End Date**: The date when you'd like follow-up to end.
* **Follow-up Location**: The location where you'd like to meet up.
* **Next Steps**: A brief description of what you'd like to achieve with cultural integration.
* **Submitter Name**: Your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: A valid email address for you as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted by phone).

Note that fields with email address types require a valid email address, and fields with phone number types require a valid phone number.
