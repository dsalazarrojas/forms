# Ecommerce Domain Name Suggestion Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for store owners to provide information about their ecommerce business, including the type of business they operate, their location, and their domain name preferences. The goal is to provide store owners with suitable domain name suggestions that meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Start by selecting your Agency Name, if you have one.
3. Provide your Store Owner Email address so we can contact you with domain name suggestions.
4. Specify the Business Nature of your store (e.g., Business to Business, Business to Consumer, etc.).
5. Choose the types of stores you operate or are interested in (e.g., Apparel, Bookstore, etc.).
6. Enter your store's Location.
7. Provide your domain name preferences.
8. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): If you have an agency, please enter its name. This information will help us understand your affiliation with the store.
* **Store Owner Email** (`store_owners_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with domain name suggestions.
* **Business Nature** (`business_nature`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of business you operate (e.g., Business to Business, Business to Consumer, etc.).
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of stores you operate or are interested in (e.g., Apparel, Bookstore, etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your store's location.
* **Domain Suggestion** (`domain_suggestion`, text, optional): Enter your preferred domain name.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required): Click this button to send your request for domain name suggestions.
