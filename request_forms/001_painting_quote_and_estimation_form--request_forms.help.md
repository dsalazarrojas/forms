# Painting Quote And Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their painting project, enabling the service to provide an accurate quote and estimation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read and understand the form's purpose and the instructions provided.
- Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or indicated as required in the field-by-field explanations below.
- Review your submissions for accuracy before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional details about the customer, such as their name, contact information, or special instructions.
- **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred contact time from the options provided:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project, including the scope of work and any specific requirements.
- **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the project, including any materials or labor costs.
- **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the specific services required for the project, such as preparation, painting, and finishing.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool to be used for the project:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ another tool
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the project.
