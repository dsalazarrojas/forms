# Telework Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telework Request Form is a tool used to request approval for work-from-home arrangements. It is meant for employees who wish to work from home instead of coming into the office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your employee name, job title, and department.
2. Select your department from the options provided.
3. Enter the number of hours you plan to work from home.
4. Choose your job location (Home or Remote).
5. Select your start and end times for the telework period.
6. Enter the date you wish to work from home.
7. Provide a brief reason for your telework request.
8. Optionally, include any additional comments or notes.
9. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work From Home Request**: This is the title of the form and a reminder of its purpose.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears in our HR system.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you plan to work from home. Please use a standard 24-hour clock format (e.g., 8-10 hours).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose where you will be working from (Home or Remote).
* **Start Time** (`work_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your telework period in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **End Time** (`work_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your telework period in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to work from home.
* **Reason for Telework** (`reason_for_telework`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for your telework request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Include any additional comments or notes you would like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears in our system.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears in our system.

## Tips

* Please make sure to submit your request with accurate and complete information.
* If you are experiencing difficulties or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* Requests will be reviewed and approved based on company policies and guidelines.
