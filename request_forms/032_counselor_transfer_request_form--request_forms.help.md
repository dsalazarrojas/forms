# Counselor Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselor Transfer Request Form is used to request a transfer of a student to a new school or program. This form is typically completed by a student or their authorized representative, such as a parent or guardian. The form is reviewed and approved by authorized personnel, who will verify the requested transfer and update the student's records accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the current school your child is currently attending from the list of options.
2. Select the new school they will be transferring to from the list of options.
3. Choose the new program they will be transferring into from the list of options.
4. Enter a brief reason for the transfer, if applicable.
5. Enter the date the transfer is requested to take effect.
6. Enter the name of the counselor who is currently working with your child.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter the email address of the person approving the transfer.
9. Choose the person approving the transfer from the list of options.
10. Enter the date the transfer was reviewed.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes about the transfer.
12. Enter the action that should be taken regarding the transfer.
13. Enter the date the action should be taken.
14. Enter any notes regarding the transfer review.
15. Enter the title of the person approving the transfer.
16. Enter the ID number of the transfer request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Current School
*   **Current School** (Required): Select the name of the school your child is currently attending from the list of options.

### New School
*   **New School** (Required): Select the name of the new school your child will be transferring to from the list of options.

### New Program
*   **New Program** (Required): Select the name of the new program your child will be transferring into from the list of options.

### Reason for Transfer
*   **Reason for Transfer**: Enter a brief reason for the transfer, if applicable.

### Transfer Date
*   **Transfer Date**: Enter the date the transfer is requested to take effect.

### Counselor Name
*   **Counselor Name**: Enter the name of the counselor who is currently working with your child.

### Email
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Email of Approver
*   **Email of Approver**: Enter the email address of the person approving the transfer.

### Approver
*   **Approver** (Required): Choose the person approving the transfer from the list of options.

### Date Reviewed
*   **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the transfer was reviewed.

### Comments
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the transfer.

### Action
*   **Action**: Enter the action that should be taken regarding the transfer.

### Action Date
*   **Action Date**: Enter the date the action should be taken.

### Notes
*   **Notes**: Enter any notes regarding the transfer review.

### Title of Approver
*   **Title of Approver**: Enter the title of the person approving the transfer.

### Transfer Request ID
*   **Transfer Request ID**: Enter the ID number of the transfer request.
