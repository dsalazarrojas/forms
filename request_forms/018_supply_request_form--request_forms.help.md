A help guide for the Supply Request Form. This form is used to record and manage supply requests from suppliers. It's crucial to ensure that each request is accurately documented to prevent duplication and improve overall supply chain management. Before filling out this form, please review the following guide to understand its purpose and usage.

# Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Request Form is designed to collect information about requested supplies from suppliers. It's essential to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the supplier(s) you are interacting with.
2. Choose the product(s) you are requesting.
3. Enter the quantity of supplies needed.
4. Specify the quantity ordered from the supplier.
5. Enter the date the supply was ordered.
6. Enter the date the supply was delivered.
7. Select the status of the supply (pending, in stock, or delivered).
8. Add any notes about the supply request (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier**: The supplier name or ID you are interacting with. You can select multiple suppliers if applicable.
* **product**: The product name or ID you are requesting. You can select multiple products if applicable.
* **quantity**: The total quantity of supplies requested.
* **quantity_ordered**: The quantity of supplies ordered from the supplier.
* **date_ordered**: The date the supply was ordered from the supplier.
* **date_delivered**: The date the supply was delivered to the requester.
* **status**: The status of the supply (pending, in stock, or delivered).
* **notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the supply request (if necessary).
* **submit**: A text field for any additional information or comments (not a required field).

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately select the supplier and product(s) to avoid duplication of requests.
- Ensure that the quantity ordered is accurate to prevent oversupply or undersupply.
- Update the status of the supply as soon as possible to keep the supply chain management up-to-date.
- Use the notes field to add any important details that may not be captured in the other fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is captured.
