# creative_asset_reuse_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the reuse of creative assets such as images, videos, or other media. Users can fill out this form to submit their requests for reusing assets from a specific category or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Input** (`1`): Fill in your name and any other relevant contact information. This field is optional.
2. **Asset Details** (`2`): Provide a brief description of the creative asset you would like to reuse. Include as much information as possible.
3. **Asset Usage** (`3`): Describe how you plan to use the creative asset. This will help us understand the intended purpose of the request.
4. **Request Type** (`4`): Select the type of request you are submitting:
	* **Request**: I am requesting to reuse the asset for my own purposes.
	* **Reuse Request**: I am asking to reuse the asset for a client or third-party project.
	* **Reuse Inquiry**: I am inquiring about the possibility of reusing the asset.
5. **Request Status** (`5`): Select the current status of your request:
	* **In Progress**: The request is currently being processed.
	* **Reuse**: The asset has been successfully reused.
	* **Rejected**: The request was denied or not approved.
	* **Approved**: The request was approved, and the asset is ready for reuse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for your contact information and can be filled in to help us get back to you regarding your request.
* **Asset Details**: Describe the creative asset you would like to reuse, including any relevant details such as the asset's original purpose, creator, or any applicable licenses or agreements.
* **Asset Usage**: Describe how you plan to use the creative asset, including any specific use cases or projects where it will be applied.
* **Request Type**: Select the type of request you are submitting to help us understand your intentions and process your request accordingly.
* **Request Status**: Choose the status of your request to help us track and manage the reuse of the asset.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Asset Details" and "Asset Usage" fields to ensure that we can properly assess your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or need help, do not hesitate to contact us through the provided contact information.
* We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in helping us track and manage the reuse of creative assets responsibly.
