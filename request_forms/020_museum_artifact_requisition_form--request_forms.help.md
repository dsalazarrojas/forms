# museumArtifactRequisitionForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The museumArtifactRequisitionForm is used to document and manage artifacts in a museum's collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the artifact in the "Artifact Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the artifact in the "Artifact Description" field.
3. Specify the location of the artifact in the "Artifact Location" field.
4. Enter the name of the institution holding the artifact in the "Institution Name" field.
5. Identify the department or section where the artifact is housed in the "Department" field.
6. Name the curator or staff member responsible for the artifact in the "Curator" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artifact Title** (`artifacttitle`, text, required): Enter the title or name of the artifact.
* **Artifact Description** (`artifactdescription`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the artifact.
* **Artifact Location** (`artifactlocation`, text, required): Specify the location where the artifact is currently held or stored.
* **Institution Name** (`institutionname`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution that holds the artifact.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Identify the department or section where the artifact is housed.
* **Curator** (`curator`, text, required): Name the curator or staff member responsible for the artifact.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have permission to requisition the artifact before completing this form.
* Use the form to document changes in the artifact's status, such as loaning or selling.
* Complete separate forms for each unique artifact, even if the same form is completed multiple times for the same artifact.
