# Viewing Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for viewing purposes. It is intended to provide details about when and how you want to view something.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready to fill out the form.
2. Enter the viewing date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the viewing time in the format HH:MM:SS.
4. Add a note if necessary.
5. Enter your email address (if you have one).
6. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
7. Click the "viewing_information" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **viewing_information_form** (`viewing_information_form`, text, optional): This is the form title.
- **view_date** (`view_date`, date, optional): Enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2024).
- **view_time** (`view_time`, time, optional): Enter the time in HH:MM:SS format (e.g., 01:30:00).
- **view_note** (`view_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about your viewing information.
- **view_email** (`view_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
- **view_phone** (`view_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
- **viewing_information** (`viewing_information_form`, text, optional): This is a submit button to complete the form.
