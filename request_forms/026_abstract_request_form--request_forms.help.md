# Abstract Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for requesting abstracts for documents or materials. Please use it to provide details about the document you would like an abstract created for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the sections labeled "Full name" and "Email address".
2. Provide the name of your organization or institution in the section labeled "Organization or institution".
3. If applicable, enter your department or unit in the section labeled "Department or unit".
4. Enter the title of the document or material in the section labeled "Title of the document or material".
5. Select the type of document or material in the section labeled "Type of document".
6. Enter the name of the author or source of the document or material in the section labeled "Author or source of the document".
7. If known, enter the publication or creation date in the section labeled "Publication or creation date (if known)".
8. Select the purpose of the abstract request in the section labeled "Purpose of the abstract request".
9. If you have a preferred length for the abstract, select it in the section labeled "Preferred abstract length".
10. Enter any special instructions or formatting requirements in the section labeled "Any special instructions or formatting requirements".
11. Finally, enter the date when you would like the abstract to be delivered by in the section labeled "Date the abstract is needed by".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full name** (`requester_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email address** (`requester_email`, required): Enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
- **Organization** (`organization`, required): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
- **Department/Unit** (`department`, optional): If applicable, enter your department or unit name.
- **Title of the document or material** (`document_title`, required): Enter a clear and concise title of the document or material.
- **Type of document** (`document_type`, required): Choose the type of document or material from the options: "Research paper", "Presentation", "Report", "Thesis or dissertation", "Policy document", or "Other".
- **Author or source of the document** (`author_or_source`, required): Enter the name of the author or source of the document.
- **Publication or creation date (if known)** (`publication_date`, optional): If known, enter the date of publication or creation of the document.
- **Purpose of the abstract request** (`abstract_purpose`, required): Select the purpose of the abstract request from the options: "Academic research", "Internal review", "Conference preparation", "Grant application", "General reference", or "Other".
- **Preferred abstract length** (`preferred_length`, optional): Select your preferred abstract length from the options: "Brief (100-150 words)", "Standard (200-300 words)", "Extended (400-500 words)", or "No preference".
- **Key areas or topics the abstract should focus on** (`key_focus_areas`, optional): Provide a description of the key areas or topics the abstract should focus on.
- **Date the abstract is needed by** (`deadline`, required): Enter the date when you would like the abstract to be delivered by.
- **Any special instructions or formatting requirements** (`special_instructions`, optional): If necessary, provide any special instructions or formatting requirements for the abstract.
