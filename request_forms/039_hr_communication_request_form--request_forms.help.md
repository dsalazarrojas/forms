# HR Communication Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Communication Request Form is used to submit requests for HR-related communication, such as questions, concerns, or issues that require attention from the HR department. This form helps ensure that HR receives all necessary information to provide timely and effective support.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information about the employee, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter a clear and concise question or concern that requires attention.
3. Choose the priority level of the request (High, Medium, or Low).
4. Select one or more categories related to the request (Benefits, Payroll, or HR Services).
5. Provide a detailed description of the request.
6. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (1)**: This field is used to enter the employee's name, email, and phone number. Please provide accurate information to help us contact you efficiently.
* **Question/Concern (2)**: Enter a clear and concise question or concern that requires attention from HR. Try to be as specific as possible to ensure we understand the issue correctly.
* **Concern (3)**: Provide more context or details about the concern or question entered in the previous field.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Priority (6)**: Choose the priority level of the request (High, Medium, or Low) to help us determine the urgency of the issue.
* **Category (7)**: Select one or more categories related to the request (Benefits, Payroll, or HR Services) to help us direct the issue to the relevant team.
* **Description (8)**: Provide a detailed description of the request, including any relevant background information or context.
