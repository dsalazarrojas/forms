# Library Card Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew a library card. To renew your library card, please fill out this form with the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Enter your library card number in the "Library Card Number" field.
3.  Select your expiration date in the "Expiration Date" field.
4.  If you want to specify a specific time of day for your expiration date, use the "Expiration Date" field.
5.  If you need to add any additional comments or notes, you may use the "Expiration Date" field to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name as it appears on your library card.
*   **Library Card Number** (`library_card_number`, text, required): This field is where you enter your library card number, which can be found on your physical library card.
*   **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): This field is where you select the date on which you would like your library card to expire. You can select any date in the future.
*   **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, time, optional): This field is where you can specify a specific time of day for your library card expiration.
*   **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date_1`, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes related to your library card expiration.
