# select_extras_for_your_installation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request extras for an installation. It allows customers to add additional options or services to their installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Extra Requests" field with any additional options or services you wish to add to your installation.
2. Select the "Service Providers" field to indicate if you wish to have a service provider present during the installation.
3. Choose your "Visit Preferences" to select how you would like to be contacted during the installation.
4. Select your "Preferred Contact Method" to choose the best way for us to contact you.
5. Choose the "Installation Type" field to select the type of installation you are having.
6. Fill out the "Installation Date/Time" field with the date and time you would like the installation to take place.
7. Enter the "Installation Duration" field with the duration of the installation in minutes.
8. Fill out the "Contact Name" field with the person to contact in case of an emergency.
9. Enter the "Contact Phone" field with the phone number of the person to contact.
10. Enter the "Contact Email" field with the email of the person to contact.
11. Fill out the "Contact Notes" field with any additional notes for the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Installation Date** (1): Enter the date you would like to schedule your installation. This field accepts dates in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Extra Requests** (2): Enter any additional options or services you would like to add to your installation. This can be anything from additional equipment to special installation requests.
* **Service Providers** (3): Select "Yes" if you would like a service provider to be present during the installation and "No" otherwise. This will help us ensure that you have a service provider at your installation.
* **Visit Preferences** (4): Select your preferred method of contact during the installation. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Preferred Contact Method** (5): Select the best way for us to contact you during the installation. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Installation Type** (6): Select the type of installation you are having. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Installation Date/Time** (7): Enter the date and time you would like the installation to take place. This field accepts times in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Installation Duration** (8): Enter the duration of the installation in minutes.
* **Contact Name** (9): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (10): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (11): Enter the email of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Notes** (12): Enter any additional notes for the contact person.

## Tips

* Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* Please ensure you have selected the correct options and services you wish to add to your installation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
