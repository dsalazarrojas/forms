# request_a_free_demo_2_request_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users interested in requesting a free demo or trial of our product. It is intended to collect essential details such as first name, email, phone number, location, and any relevant comments about their request. This information will help our support team provide a more personalized and tailored response to their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide any additional comments you may have. Make sure to enter a valid email address for our team to contact you regarding your demo request. If you have any specific requests or requirements for the demo, please include them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required/optional**: False): Please enter your first name to help us better understand who you are and what you're looking for.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required/optional**: False): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your demo request. This will also be used to keep you informed about any updates or changes related to your request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required/optional**: False): If you have a preferred phone number where we can reach you, please enter it here. This will help our team contact you in case of any urgent matters related to your demo request.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, **required/optional**: False): Enter your location to help us better understand your preferences or requirements for the demo.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, **required/optional**: False): If you have any specific comments or requests about the demo, please enter them here. This will help our support team tailor the response to your needs.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your demo request.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us provide a more effective response.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to ask our support team.
