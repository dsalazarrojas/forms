# Shelf Life Extension Testing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Life Extension Testing Request Form is a crucial document that helps assess the possibility of testing and extending the shelf life of a product. By providing accurate and detailed information, you are helping the product owner to potentially improve the shelf life of their product, which can have significant commercial and quality implications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your product's name and ID details.
2.  Provide a valid reason for the shelf life extension request.
3.  Upload any supporting documents that can aid in the assessment process.
4.  Specify the name and address of the product's manufacturer.
5.  Detail the test methodology used for the product's shelf life assessment.
6.  Provide your contact information so that the product owner can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input the name of the product that is requesting shelf life extension. Please ensure that the product name is accurate and up-to-date.

*   **Product ID** (`product_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the product by the manufacturer or your company. If you don't have a product ID, please use a relevant identifier such as the product code.

*   **Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the current expiry, if applicable. This will help our team to understand the current state of the product and aid in the assessment process.

*   **Justification** (`justification`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief explanation for why shelf life extension is required. This can include details such as changes in storage conditions, new manufacturing process, or any other relevant information.

*   **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the product in as much detail as possible. This can include its use, ingredients, and intended application.

*   **Shelf Life Extension Reason** (`shelf_life_extension_reason`, `text`, required: false): Explain why you are requesting shelf life extension. Please be as detailed as possible, and include any relevant data or research that supports your request.

*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `note`, required: false): Upload any relevant documents that support your shelf life extension request, such as research papers, test results, or industry certifications.

*   **Manufacturer Name** (`manufacturer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product's manufacturer, if different from the product owner's name.

*   **Manufacturer Address** (`manufacturer_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manufacturer's address, if different from the product owner's address.

*   **Test Methodology** (`test_methodology`, `text`, required: false): Describe the test methodology that was used to assess the shelf life of the product.

*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and title, so that we can get back to you regarding your request.

*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, so that we can get back to you regarding your request.

*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.

*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the tool that was used for the shelf life assessment. This will aid in the review and assessment of your request.

*   **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all relevant categories for your product, which can aid in the review and assessment of your request.
