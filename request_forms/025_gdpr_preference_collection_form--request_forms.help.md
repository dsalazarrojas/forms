# GDPR Preference Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Preference Collection Form is a crucial document that helps us understand and manage your data sharing preferences. By filling out this form, you will be able to indicate your willingness to share your data with us or our partners, as well as how you would like to be contacted for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate options for each field according to your preferences. Please ensure that you have reviewed and accepted our User Agreement before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Accept Our User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to our terms and conditions, including the handling of your personal data. This is not required but is a necessary step to ensure you understand and accept our policies.
* **Confirm Your GDPR Declaration** (`gdpr_declaration`, `select_one`, not required): Choose 'Yes' to confirm that you have read and understood our GDPR declaration.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your valid email address here. This will be used for contacting you about your data sharing preferences and other important updates.
* **Type** (`email_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the type of email address you have provided (e.g., Email 1, Email 2, Email 3).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Provide your phone number here. This will be used for contacting you about your data sharing preferences and other important updates.
* **Type** (`phone_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the type of phone number you have provided (e.g., Home, Mobile).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your address here. This will be used for sending you important updates and notifications.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, not required): Enter your postal code here. This will be used for sending you important updates and notifications.
* **Data Sharing** (`data_sharing`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select how you would like to share your data (e.g., With our partners, With other services).
* **Marketing** (`marketing`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for marketing purposes (e.g., Newsletter, Social Media).
* **Unsubscribe** (`unsubscribe`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select how you would like to be unsubscribed (e.g., From all emails, From all newsletters).
* **Legal** (`legal`, `note`, not required): This section contains our company's legal information and policies. Please review it carefully before submitting the form.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, `note`, not required): This section outlines how we handle your personal data and our company's data protection policies. Please review it carefully before submitting the form.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, `note`, not required): This section outlines the rules and guidelines for using our services. Please review it carefully before submitting the form.
