# Pet Food Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Food Request Form is designed to provide pet owners with a convenient and streamlined way to request pet food when their pets have special dietary needs or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Pet Name field with the name of your pet.
2. Select the species of your pet from the Pet Species field.
3. Choose the type of food your pet needs from the Food Type field.
4. Select the subtype of food your pet requires from the Food Subtype field.
5. Enter the quantity of food you need.
6. Add any additional notes about your pet's food requirements.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your request.
8. Enter your phone number in case we need to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet, e.g., "Bella" or "Max".
* **Pet Species**: Choose the species of your pet from the list:
	+ Dog
	+ Cat
	+ Bird
* **Food Type**: Select the type of food your pet requires from the list:
	+ Dry
	+ Canned
	+ Raw
* **Food Subtype**: Choose the subtype of food your pet needs from the list:
	+ Beef
	+ Chicken
	+ Fish
	+ Turkey
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of food you need, e.g., "1 kg" or "5 lbs".
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your pet's food requirements or allergies.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you to confirm your request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number in case we need to reach out to you.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your request accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you to confirm your request and answer any questions you may have.
* You will receive an email with a copy of your submission for your records.
