# Laundry Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Laundry Refund Form is designed to collect information from users to process their laundry refund requests. It is used to gather details about the issue, the amount to be refunded, the service used, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
2. Be sure to select the correct options for "service" and "attached_files".
3. If you have attached files, make sure they are relevant to your refund request.
4. Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **issue_description**: Describe the issue you're experiencing with your laundry service. This field is required to ensure we understand the reason for your refund request.
* **amount**: Enter the amount you would like to be refunded. This field is required to process your request accurately.
* **service**: Select the service you used for your laundry. This field helps us identify the specific service associated with your request.
* **date_used**: If you have a date of service, please enter it here. This field is optional but helps us process your request more efficiently.
* **time**: If you have a time of service, please enter it here. This field is optional but helps us understand the timing of your request.
* **note**: If you have any additional comments or notes about your request, please add them here. This field is optional but helps us understand your concerns.
