# Bathroom Furniture Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bathroom Furniture Quote Request Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information about your bathroom furniture project. It helps our team understand your needs and preferences, ensuring we provide an accurate quote for your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the type of project you are undertaking (New Installation, Remodel, Replacement, Addition, or Commercial Project).
4. Choose the type of bathroom you are working on (Master Bathroom, Guest Bathroom, Powder Room, Kids Bathroom, En-Suite, or Multiple Bathrooms).
5. Select all the bathroom furniture items you need (e.g., Vanity Cabinet, Vanity Top, Mirror, etc.).
6. Choose your preferred design style (Modern, Traditional, Contemporary, Rustic, Farmhouse, Industrial, Coastal, or Transitional).
7. Specify your preferred color finish for the furniture (White, Black, Gray, Brown, Natural Wood, Blue, Green, or Custom).
8. If required, enter your specific size requirements for the space.
9. Select your estimated budget range for the project.
10. Specify when you need the project completed (Immediately, Within 2 Weeks, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, or Just Planning).
11. Indicate whether you require installation services for the project (Yes, No, or Maybe).
12. Enter your property address for delivery purposes.
13. If needed, provide any additional details about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, **email**, required): Provide your contact email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, **text**, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, **select_one**, required): Specify the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., New Installation, Remodel, etc.).
* **Bathroom Type** (`bathroom_type`, **select_one**, required): Choose the type of bathroom you are working on (e.g., Master Bathroom, Guest Bathroom, etc.).
* **Furniture Items Needed** (`furniture_items`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all the bathroom furniture items you need (e.g., Vanity Cabinet, Mirror, etc.).
* **Style Preference** (`style_preference`, **select_one**, required): Select your preferred design style for the furniture (e.g., Modern, Traditional, etc.).
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, **select_one**, optional): Specify your preferred color finish for the furniture (e.g., White, Black, Gray, etc.).
* **Size Requirements** (`size_requirements`, **text**, required): Enter your specific size requirements for the space if needed.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, **select_one**, required): Select your estimated budget range for the project.
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, **select_one**, required): Specify when you need the project completed (e.g., Immediately, Within 2 Weeks, etc.).
* **Installation Needed** (`installation_needed`, **select_one**, required): Indicate whether you require installation services for the project (Yes, No, or Maybe).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, **text**, required): Enter your property address for delivery purposes.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, **text**, optional): Provide any additional information about your project if needed.
