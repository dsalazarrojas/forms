# Seminar Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Details Form is designed to collect information for seminar events. This form is used by our team to organize and manage seminar events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to save your changes.
- Fill in the required fields (indicated by the red asterisk \*).
- Select options from the dropdown menus as needed.
- Review and confirm your input before submitting.

- **Seminar Details** (`seminar_details`, `text`, required: false):

- **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false):

    Enter the phone number of the attendee.
