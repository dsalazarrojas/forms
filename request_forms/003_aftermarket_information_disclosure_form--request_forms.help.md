# Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who request access to aftermarket data for a product. It ensures that the requester is aware of and agrees to the necessary steps for handling the data and helps the team provide accurate and relevant information in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact details, reason for request, and intended use of the information.
2. Select the specific data fields you are requesting, such as maintenance history, parts list, modification details, compatibility notes, and pricing history.
3. If required, select the confidentiality option to indicate if special handling is needed for your request.
4. Provide any additional information or attachments that support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Requester email**: Enter your best contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Product serial or model**: Enter the serial or model number of the product you are requesting information for.
* **Reason for disclosure request**: Briefly describe why you are requesting this information.
* **Data requested**: Select the specific data fields you are looking for, such as maintenance history, parts list, modification details, compatibility notes, and pricing history.
* **Intended use of disclosed information**: Describe how you plan to use the information.
* **Is confidentiality required**: Select 'True' if you need special handling for your request.
* **Preferred contact time**: If you have a preferred time to be contacted, enter it here.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any additional information or attachments to support your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a swift and efficient processing of your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or contact the support team for guidance.
* If you need to request confidentiality, please select the 'True' option and provide a clear explanation of your request.
