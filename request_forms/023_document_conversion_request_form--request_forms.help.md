# document_conversion_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The document conversion request form is used to request document conversion services. This form captures the essential information needed to understand the requirements and urgency of the conversion request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide additional information or special requests in the "additional_info" field.
3. Fill in the contact details if you want to be contacted for further information or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File Upload** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Upload the document to be converted.
  This field is used to upload the file that needs to be converted. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to upload files.
* **Document Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of document to be converted.
  This field allows you to select the type of document you need to be converted, such as PDF, DOCX, JPG, etc.
  Options: pdf, docx, jpg, png, mp3, mp4, mpg, tif, tiff, mov, mp2
* **File Format** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the format of the converted document.
  This field allows you to select the format of the converted document, such as PDF, DOCX, JPG, etc.
  Options: pdf, docx, jpg, png, mp3, mp4, mpg, tif, tiff, mov, mp2
* **Urgency Level** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the urgency level of the conversion request.
  This field is not used in the current form implementation, but you can still fill it in if you need to express the urgency of the conversion request.
* **Priority Level** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the priority level of the conversion request.
  This field is not used in the current form implementation, but you can still fill it in if you need to express the priority of the conversion request.
* **Required Fields** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the required fields for the conversion.
  This field allows you to select the fields that you consider crucial for the conversion. You can select multiple fields by holding the Ctrl key and clicking on them.
  Options: field1, field2, field3, field4, field5, field6, field7, field8
* **Additional Information or Special Requests** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional information or special requests.
  This field is a free-form text area where you can provide any additional information or special requests you have.
* **Contact Name** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
  This field is for you to enter your name so that we can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
  This field is for you to enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Company** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
  This field is for you to enter your company name.
* **Contact Number** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number.
  This field is for you to enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`12`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email.
  This field is for you to enter your contact email.
* **Contact Number** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Another contact number field that is likely a duplicate.
* **Department** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department name.
  This field is for you to enter your department name.
* **Contact Person** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact person's name.
  This field is for you to enter the name of the person you want to be contacted.
* **Project Name** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter your project name.
  This field is for you to enter your project name.
* **Project Number** (`17`, `number`, required: false): Enter your project number.
  This field is for you to enter your project number.
* **Project Status** (`18`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the project status.
  This field allows you to select the status of your project.
  Options: ongoing, complete, pending
* **Project Deadline** (`19`, `date`, required: false): Enter the project deadline.
  This field is for you to enter the project deadline.

  This field is for you to enter your email address as the requestor.

## Tips
* Always refer to your project's requirements and urgency level to help us understand your needs better.
* If you have any special requests or additional information, please provide it in the "Additional Information or Special Requests" field.
* If you need further information or updates, please fill in the contact details.
