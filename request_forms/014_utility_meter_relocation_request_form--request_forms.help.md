# Utility Meter Relocation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request relocation of a utility meter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the **User** field.
2. Identify the **Utility Service Provider** you are dealing with.
3. Provide the **Property Owner** details.
4. Enter the **Property Details** where the meter is currently located.
5. Enter the **New Address** where the meter will be relocated to.
6. Enter the **Current Address** where the meter is currently located.
7. Choose the **Service Type** you are requesting to be relocated (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, Sewage).
8. Enter the **Move Reason**, a brief description of why you are requesting to move the meter.
9. Select a date for the **Scheduled Date** you would like the meter to be relocated.
10. Optionally, enter any additional **Comments** about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User

* **User** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your name and contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
### Utility Service Provider

* **Utility Service Provider** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Identify the company or organization providing the utility service you are dealing with.
### Property Owner

* **Property Owner** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the property owner.
### Property Details

* **Property Details** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Describe the property where the meter is currently located.
### New Address

* **New Address** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the new address where you would like the meter to be relocated.
### Current Address

* **Current Address** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the current address where the meter is currently located.
### Service Type

* **Service Type** (`name`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose the type of utility service you are requesting to be relocated (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, Sewage).
### Move Reason

* **Move Reason** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting to move the meter.
### Scheduled Date

* **Scheduled Date** (`name`, `date`, `true`): Select a date for when you would like the meter to be relocated.
### Comments

* **Comments** (`name`, `text`, `false`): Optionally, enter any additional comments or details about your request.
