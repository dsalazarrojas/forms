# Manpower Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manpower Request Form is a tool for employees or team members to submit formal requests for additional staff or positions to support their work. It helps the management team make informed decisions about resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Manpower Request Form, follow these steps:

*   Read the purpose and guidance provided above
*   Ensure you have a legitimate business need for additional staff or positions
*   Select the "Yes" option for "requested_manpower_type" if you are requesting new staff or positions, or "No" if you are requesting to change or adjust existing staff or positions
*   Enter the number of staff or positions you are requesting
*   Choose the department that will be impacted or responsible for the new staff or positions
*   Select the person or team that will be impacted or responsible for the new staff or positions
*   Provide a brief description of your need for additional staff or positions
*   If applicable, provide the start date for the new staff or positions

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Manpower Request Form** (form_header): This field is not a field to fill, but rather the title of the form.
*   **Request Details** (man_power_request_details): Describe the specific need for additional staff or positions. This field should be completed if you have a legitimate business need.
*   **Requested Manpower Type** (requested_manpower_type): Select "Yes" if you are requesting new staff or positions, or "No" if you are requesting to change or adjust existing staff or positions.
*   **Requested Manpower Number** (requested_manpower_number): Enter the number of staff or positions you are requesting.
*   **Department** (department): Choose the department that will be impacted or responsible for the new staff or positions.
*   **Requested For** (requested_for): Select the person or team that will be impacted or responsible for the new staff or positions.
*   **Request Description** (request_description): Provide a brief description of your need for additional staff or positions.
*   **Requested Start Date** (requested_start_date): If applicable, provide the start date for the new staff or positions.

**Tips**

*   Be sure to provide clear and concise information in the "Request Description" field to help the management team understand your need.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   Complete the form only if you have a legitimate business need for additional staff or positions.
