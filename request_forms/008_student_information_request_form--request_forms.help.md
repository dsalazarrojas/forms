# Student Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Information Request Form is designed to collect information from students, which will be used to categorize and process their requests accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Enter your Student Number in the **Student Number** field.
5. Enter your Date Of Birth in the **Date Of Birth** field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Select your Student Type from the options provided in the **Student Type** field.
7. Enter your Email in the **Email** field in the correct format (e.g., [your email address]).
8. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
9. Select your Student Level from the options provided in the **Student Level** field.
10. Select your Request Type from the options provided in the **Request Type** field.
11. Enter any additional Comments in the **Comments** field.
12. Select who you would like to be assigned to process your request in the **Assigned To** field.
13. Enter the name of the person who created this request in the **Created By** field.
14. The current date and time will be automatically populated in the **Date Created** field.
15. Enter the name of the person who modified this request in the **Modified By** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your student identification number or ID number.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Student Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes you as a student.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [your email address]).
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Level** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the levels that apply to you as a student.
* **Request Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of request you have.
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Assigned To** (select_one, required/optional): Select who you would like to be assigned to process your request.
* **Created By** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who created this request.
* **Date Created** (time, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
* **Modified By** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who modified this request.
