# Homeowners Insurance Claim Premium Increase Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for homeowners to dispute premium increases on their insurance policies. If you believe your premium has increased unnecessarily, please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to review and resolve the issue.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your claim details, including the policy number, date of dispute, and premium increase amount.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter the policy effective date and any additional supporting documents or evidence you have to support your dispute.
4. Enter your signature and date to confirm your identity and agreement to the dispute.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your premium increase dispute, including the policy number and date of the premium increase.
* **Claimant Info** (`claimant_info`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your policy.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up.
* **Claimant Phone (again)** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your policy.
* **Claimant City** (`claimant_city`, text, required): Enter your city as it appears on your policy.
* **Claimant State** (`claimant_state`, text, required): Enter your state as it appears on your policy.
* **Claimant Zip** (`claimant_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code as it appears on your policy.
* **Claimant Date Of Birth** (`claimant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Claimant License** (`claimant_license`, text, optional): Enter your driver's license number, if applicable.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter the policy number associated with your insurance policy.
* **Premium Increase** (`premium_increase`, number, required): Enter the premium increase amount you are disputing.
* **Premium Increase Date** (`premium_increase_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the premium increase.
* **Premium Increase Amount** (`premium_increase_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the premium increase you are disputing.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, date, required): Enter the effective date of your policy.
* **Dispute Date** (`dispute_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this dispute.
* **Dispute Details** (`dispute_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your dispute, including any relevant evidence or supporting documents.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents or evidence you have to support your dispute.
* **Agency Comments** (`agency_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or feedback from your agent or broker.
* **Agency Signature** (`agency_signature`, text, required): Enter your agent's or broker's signature, if applicable.
* **Homeowner Signature** (`homeowner_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature to confirm your identity and agreement to the dispute.
* **Additional Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents_1`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents or evidence you have to support your dispute.
* **Agency Comments 1** (`agency_comments_1`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback from your agent or broker.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click submit to complete and submit your dispute form.
