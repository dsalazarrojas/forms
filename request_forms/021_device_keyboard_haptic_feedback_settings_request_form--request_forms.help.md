# Device Keyboard Haptic Feedback Settings Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request haptic feedback settings for device keyboards. Fill out this form to describe your issue or request adjustments to your device's keyboard settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email, phone number, and department.
2. Choose the type of request you're making.
3. Select the type of device you're using.
4. Choose the model and version of your keyboard.
5. Briefly describe the issue you're experiencing or the changes you're requesting.
6. Specify the expected and actual behavior of your keyboard.
7. Select the specific haptic feedback setting you're requesting.
8. Provide any additional comments or requests.
9. Enter your name, email, and phone number as the submitter.
10. Enter your department, position, location, and any comments you'd like to add as the submitter.
11. Enter your assignee's name, email, and department.
12. Enter your assignee's position and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): Please fill out your name, email, phone number, and department.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required): Select whether this is a "Haptic Feedback Setting Adjustment" or "Haptic Feedback Setting".
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose your device type (Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, or Smartphone).
* **Keyboard Model** (`keyboard_model`, select_one, required): Select the model of your keyboard (Model 1, Model 2, Model 3).
* **Keyboard Version** (`keyboard_version`, select_one, required): Select the version of your keyboard (V1, V2, V3).
* **Issue** (`issue`, text, required): Briefly describe the issue you're experiencing or the changes you're requesting.
* **Expected Behavior** (`expected_behavior`, text, required): Specify the expected behavior of your keyboard.
* **Actual Behavior** (`actual_behavior`, text, required): Specify the actual behavior of your keyboard.
* **Requested Setting** (`requested_setting`, select_one, required): Select the specific haptic feedback setting you're requesting.
* **Adjustment Request** (`adjustment_request`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or requests.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments you'd like to make about your request.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter`, email, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, required): Enter your position.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or requests.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, note, required): Add a signature as proof of your submission.
* **Assignee** (`assignee`, text, required): Enter your assignee's name.
* **Assignee Email** (`assignee_email`, email, required): Enter your assignee's email address.
* **Assignee Department** (`assignee_department`, text, required): Enter your assignee's department.
* **Assignee Position** (`assignee_position`, text, required): Enter your assignee's position.
* **Assignee Location** (`assignee_location`, text, required): Enter your assignee's location.
