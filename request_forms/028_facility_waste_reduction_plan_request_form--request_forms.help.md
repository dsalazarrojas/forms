# Facility Waste Reduction Plan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Facility Waste Reduction Plan Request Form" is used to collect information from facility management teams to identify and document opportunities to reduce waste within facilities. This form is used by facility management teams to request approval for new waste reduction projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you should submit a new request. If you have previously submitted a request for the same facility or project, please do not resubmit.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Do you have a new request?" on the first page to indicate that you have a new request.
3. Provide contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
4. Identify the project name and status.
5. Select the project type.
6. Enter the start and end dates (if applicable).
7. Provide the location and name of the facility.
8. Determine if the facility has a location within the country or not.
9. Enter the facility's address, city, state, and country.
10. Enter the contact person's name and address for the facility.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the facility and the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you have a new request?**: Indicate whether you have a new request or not. If you have a new request, answer "Yes". If you don't, answer "No". 
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting this form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Project Type**: Select whether the project is for waste reduction or not.
* **Project Start Date** and **Project End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the project (if applicable).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the project.
* **Is the facility located within the country?**: Determine if the facility is located within the country or not.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility.
* **Department**: Enter the department associated with the facility.
* **Facility Type**: Enter the type of facility.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility City**: Enter the city of the facility.
* **Facility State**: Select the state of the facility.
* **Facility Zip**: Enter the postal code of the facility.
* **Facility Country**: Enter the country of the facility.
* **Facility Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and address for the facility.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the facility and the project.
* **Facility Signature**: Enter your digital signature to confirm your submission. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you submit this form only for new projects and not for existing ones.
* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the facility management team for assistance.
