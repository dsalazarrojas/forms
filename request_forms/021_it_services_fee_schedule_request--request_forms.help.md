# it_services_fee_schedule_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a fee schedule for IT services. Fill in your required information to submit a request for IT services fee schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the required fields, which are:
	+ it_services (text field)
	+ pricing (number field)
	+ consultant_info (text field)
- You can also provide additional information in the fields:
	+ service_options (select multiple field)
	+ preferred_service_times (time field)
	+ contact_details (text field)
	+ preferred_service_dates (date field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **it_services**: This text field allows you to enter the type of IT service you are requesting. 
- **service_options**: This field allows you to choose one or multiple options for the service you are requesting. Choose 'Yes' if you want the service, 'No' otherwise.
- **pricing**: This number field allows you to specify the expected price or budget for the service.
- **preferred_service_times**: This field allows you to specify when you would like to have the service provided.
- **contact_details**: This text field allows you to provide additional contact information.
- **consultant_info**: This text field allows you to add any additional information about the consultant who will provide the service.
- **preferred_service_dates**: This field allows you to specify when you would like to have the service provided.
- **submit**: To finalize your request, click the 'submit' button to send your request to the relevant team.
 
## Tips
Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and relevant to your request. You can also refer to the service options for more information on what each option entails.
