# licence_renewal_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The licence_renewal_form is a request form used to renew a licence. To use this form, please ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to proceed with the licence renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form information page with your contact details.
2. Enter your licence number.
3. Select a valid licence expiration date.
4. Provide your customer name.
5. Enter the desired renewal date.
6. Choose a renewal deadline (if applicable).
7. Enter your customer email address.
8. Enter your customer phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information** (`form_information`, text, required/optional): This page provides space to enter any additional information about your request.
* **Licence Number** (`licence_number`, select_one, required/optional): Select the licence number you wish to renew. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Licence Expiration Date** (`licence_expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the licence expiration date. This date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the desired date for the licence renewal.
* **Renewal Deadline** (`renewal_deadline`, time, required/optional): If applicable, choose a deadline for the licence renewal.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and information before filling out this form. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
