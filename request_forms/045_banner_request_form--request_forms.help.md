# Banner Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting banners for various purposes, such as events, promotions, or directional signage. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth request process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your full name and email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your request.
2. Select the correct department and organization for your banner request.
3. Choose the type of banner you need, such as vinyl, fabric, or mesh.
4. Specify the size and dimensions of the banner, or if you prefer a custom size, provide the details.
5. Provide the text to be displayed on the banner.
6. Select the font style and color scheme for your banner.
7. If your banner requires a logo, upload or describe it.
8. Indicate if you need additional visuals or graphics.
9. State the purpose of the banner, such as an event promotion or grand opening.
10. If the banner is for an event, provide the name and date of the event.
11. Specify where the banner will be installed and if it will be used indoors or outdoors.
12. Choose the mounting hardware and installation service required.
13. Indicate if you need a rush delivery.
14. Finally, provide any additional comments or details regarding your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_full_name`, required/optional): Please provide your full name to ensure we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications regarding your request.
* **Department** (`department`, required/optional): Select the department you are associated with to ensure your request is routed to the correct team.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number to receive calls and messages regarding your request.
* **Department** (`department`, required/optional): Select the department you are associated with to ensure your request is routed to the correct team.
* **Organization** (`organization`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization or company you are representing.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, required/optional): Provide the date when you need the banner.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, required/optional): Enter the date by which you need the banner.
* **Banner Type** (`banner_type`, required/optional): Choose the type of banner you need, such as vinyl, fabric, or mesh.
* **Banner Size** (`banner_size`, required/optional): Select the size of the banner you need, or if you prefer a custom size, provide the details.
* **Custom Dimensions** (`custom_dimensions`, required/optional): If you selected custom size, provide the dimensions.
* **Banner Text** (`banner_text`, required/optional): Enter the text to be displayed on the banner.
* **Font Preference** (`font_preference`, required/optional): Select the font style you prefer.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, required/optional): Choose the color scheme you want to use.
* **Logo Required** (`logo_required`, required/optional): Indicate if you need a logo on your banner.
* **Logo File** (`logo_file`, required/optional): Upload or describe the logo you need.
* **Images or Graphics** (`images_graphics`, required/optional): Indicate if you need additional visuals or graphics on your banner.
* **Banner Purpose** (`banner_purpose`, required/optional): Select the purpose of the banner, such as event promotion or directional signage.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required/optional): If the banner is for an event, enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required/optional): If the banner is for an event, enter the date of the event.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, required/optional): Specify where the banner will be installed.
* **Indoor or Outdoor** (`indoor_or_outdoor`, required/optional): Choose where the banner will be used, indoors or outdoors.
* **Mounting Hardware** (`mounting_hardware`, required/optional): Select the mounting hardware you need, such as grommets or poles.
* **Installation Service** (`installation_service`, required/optional): Indicate if you need installation service.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of banners you need.
* **Budget** (`budget`, required/optional): Select your estimated budget for the banner.
* **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, required/optional): Indicate if you need a rush delivery.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or details regarding your request.

Note: The required fields are marked with `*`, indicating that they are required to be filled out to complete the form.
