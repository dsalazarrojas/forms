# Airport Pickup Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airport Pickup Reservation Form is used to collect information for airport pickup reservations, ensuring that pickup details are accurately captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** as it appears on your ID.
2. Provide your **Email Address** where you can receive confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your contact **Phone Number** for communication with our driver.
4. Specify the **Pickup Date** you require the service.
5. Select the **Pickup Time** you expect to arrive at the airport.
6. Choose the **Airport** where you will be picked up from the list provided.
7. Enter the name of your **Airline** and **Flight Number** for our reference.
8. Enter the **Terminal Number** at the airport (if known).
9. Specify the **Number of Passengers** needing pickup.
10. Enter the **Number of Luggage Pieces** you will be bringing along.
11. Select your preferred **Vehicle Type** from the options provided.
12. Enter your **Destination Address** where you need to be dropped off.
13. Add any **Special Requests or Comments** you may have (optional).
14. Choose whether to allow **Flight Tracking** for delays (optional).
15. Select your preferred **Payment Method** for the service.
16. If you have a **Promotional Code**, you can enter it here (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address**: We will send confirmation and updates to this email.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for driver communication.
* **Pickup Date**: Date you need to be picked up.
* **Pickup Time**: Expected arrival time at the airport.
* **Airport Name**: Select the airport for pickup.
* **Airline Name**: Which airline you are flying with.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number.
* **Terminal Number**: Arrival terminal at the airport (if known).
* **Number of Passengers**: Total number of people needing pickup.
* **Number of Luggage Pieces**: Total bags and suitcases.
* **Vehicle Type Preference**: Select your preferred vehicle type.
* **Destination Address**: Full address where you need to be dropped off.
* **Special Requests or Comments**: Add any comments or requests you may have (optional).
* **Allow Flight Tracking**: We can track your flight for delays (optional).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Promotional Code**: Enter code if you have one (optional).

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with your pickup service.
