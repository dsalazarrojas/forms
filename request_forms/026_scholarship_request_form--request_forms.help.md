# Scholarship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to request a scholarship, a financial award given to support their education or career development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Describe your career goals and how the scholarship will help you achieve them.
3. Provide details about the scholarship you are requesting, including any relevant information about the award.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is where you can enter your name and any other relevant student information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your current address or the address where you can be reached.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your career goals and how the scholarship will help you achieve them.
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide details about the scholarship you are requesting, including any relevant information about the award.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating a previous submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
