# Training Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request training funds for approved training courses that align with the company's training and development strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the approval of your manager for the training request.
2. Check with your department for any internal training requests that may be available.
3. Fill out the form with the required information.
4. Review and submit the form for approval by the management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Funds Request** (`training_funds_request_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main description of the training request, which should briefly summarize the reason for the request.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the training course you are requesting.
* **Date Needed** (`course_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you need to attend the training course.
* **Training Funds** (`training_funds`, number, required/optional): Enter the estimated training funds you are requesting.
* **Requested Amount** (`requested_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of training funds you are requesting.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's ID number.
* **Department ID** (`department_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your department ID number.
* **Request Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your approval (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the submitter.

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
