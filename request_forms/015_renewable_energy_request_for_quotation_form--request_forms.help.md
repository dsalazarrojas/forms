# Renewable Energy Request For Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or users who are interested in renewable energy solutions. Please fill out this form to help us understand your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information.
2. Choose the type of renewable energy you are interested in.
3. Enter your location and contact phone number.
4. If you have a specific message, please enter it below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Name
• **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Please enter your name and surname as they appear on your identification.
### 2. Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
### 3. Energy Type
• **Energy Type** (`energy_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of renewable energy you are interested in:
	+ Solar
	+ Wind
	+ Hydro
	+ Geothermal
	+ Biomass
### 4. Location
• **Location** (`location`, text, required): Please enter your city, town, or region.
### 5. Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
### 6. Message
• **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any specific questions or requirements, please enter them here.
### 7. Assigned Tool
• **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have a specific tool or equipment you would like to use, please enter it here.
### 8. Other Energy Type
• **Other Energy Type** (`energy_type_other`, text, optional): If none of the above energy types match your needs, please enter your own energy type here.
### 9. Other Location
• **Other Location** (`location_other`, text, optional): If your location is not listed above, please enter it here.
### 10. Other Message
• **Message** (`message_other`, text, optional): If you have any other questions or requirements not covered above, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask us for clarification.
* We will contact you to discuss your renewable energy needs and preferences.
* We will use the information provided to prepare a quotation for you.
