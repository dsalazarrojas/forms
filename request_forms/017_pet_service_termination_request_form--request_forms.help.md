# Pet Service Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and facilitate the termination of pet services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (ID 1)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Pet Name (ID 2)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the name of the pet service being terminated.
* **Effective Date (ID 3)** 
  (Date, Optional)
  Enter the date the pet service is to be terminated.
* **Reason for Cancellation (ID 4)** 
  (Select One, Optional)
  Choose a reason for the pet service cancellation from the provided options.
* **Customer Confirmation (ID 5)** 
  (Select Multiple, Optional)
  Confirm whether the customer has agreed to the cancellation request.
* **Notes (ID 6)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter any additional information that may be useful for future reference.
* **Follow-up (ID 7)** 
  (Select Multiple, Optional)
  Choose how you would like us to follow up with the customer (e.g., email, phone, in-app).
* **Email (ID 8)** 
  (Email, Optional)
  Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (ID 9)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool (ID 10)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the assigned tool or equipment associated with the pet service.
* **Customer Service Provider (ID 11)** 
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the name of the customer service provider.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have obtained the customer's consent before submitting this form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the customer for clarification.
