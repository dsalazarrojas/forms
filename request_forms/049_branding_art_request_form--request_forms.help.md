# Branding Art Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Branding Art Request Form is a tool to collect information from requestors for branding art requests. This form helps our team understand the requestor's needs and ensure that we deliver accurate and efficient branding art.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your information as a requestor.
2.  Choose the type of branding material you need.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the art request, including the purpose and use case examples.
4.  Specify the dimensions and technical requirements for the branding material.
5.  Choose the priority level and deadline for the request.
6.  Confirm that you have reviewed the current brand guidelines.
7.  Add any links to inspiration or moodboards if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (`req_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as the person making the request.
*   **Team / Department** (`team_dept_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the team or department requesting the branding art.
*   **Email Address** (`req_email_addr`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Type of Branding Material** (`merch_type_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the available options: Social Media Graphics, Presentation Template, Digital Banner, Print Flyer / Brochure, or Iconography.
*   **Detailed Description of the Art Request** (`request_desc`, `text`, required): Write a detailed description of the art request, including the purpose and use case examples.
*   **Purpose and Use Case Examples** (`use_case_desc`, `text`, required): Continue writing about the purpose and use case examples of the request.
*   **Number of Variations / Quantities** (`var_qty`, `number`, required): Specify the number of variations or quantities needed for the branding material.
*   **Dimensions and Technical Specifications** (`technical_specs_desc`, `text`, required): Enter the dimensions and technical specifications for the branding material, e.g. 1080x1080px.
*   **Priority Level** (`priority_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level from the available options: Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
*   **Deadline for Final Delivery** (`final_deadline_dt`, `date`, required): Enter the deadline for the final delivery of the branding material.
*   **Links to Inspiration or Moodboards** (`inspo_links`, `text`, optional): If applicable, add links to inspiration or moodboards.
*   **I have reviewed the current brand guidelines** (`guideline_ack`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the current brand guidelines for this request.
*   **Note**: This form is for new requests only, please do not use this form for updates. If you need to update an existing request, please use the [Update Request Form].

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
*   You will receive a confirmation email after submitting the form.
*   Our team will review your request and respond with further instructions.
