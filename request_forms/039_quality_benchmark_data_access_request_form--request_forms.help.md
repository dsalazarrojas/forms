# Quality Benchmark Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who need access to quality benchmark data. Fill out this form to submit your request and we will get back to you with more information on how to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information and submit. Please make sure to fill out all the fields marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Email (2)**: Please enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about your request.
* **Department (3)**: Select your department from the list. Please note that this field is required.
* **Job Title (4)**: Enter your job title. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Company (5)**: Enter your company name. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Date (6)**: Enter the date you need access to the quality benchmark data. This can be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (7)**: Enter the time you need access to the quality benchmark data. This can be in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00:30.
* **Department (Multiple) (8)**: Select all the departments you need access to. Please note that this field is required.
* **Job Title Note (9)**: Enter any notes about your job title.
* **Company Phone (10)**: Enter your company phone number. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Department Note (11)**: Enter any notes about your department.
* **Company Email (12)**: Enter your company email address. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Department Phone (13)**: Enter your department phone number. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Job Title Email (14)**: Enter your job title email address. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Department Phone Note (15)**: Enter any notes about your department phone.
* **Department Email (16)**: Enter your department email address. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Job Title Phone (17)**: Enter your job title phone number. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Department Phone Note (18)**: Enter any notes about your department phone.
* **Job Title Phone Note (19)**: Enter any notes about your job title phone.
* **Job Title Department Phone (20)**: Enter your job title department phone number. This can be left blank if you don't have one.
* **Department (22)**: Select your department from the list. Please note that this field is required.
* **Department Email Note (23)**: Enter any notes about your department email.
* **Department Phone Note (24)**: Enter any notes about your department phone.
* **Job Title (25)**: Enter your job title. This can be left blank if you don't have one.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your departmental contact for clarification.
* We will review your request and get back to you as soon as possible.
* Please note that your department head may need to approve your request before we can release the quality benchmark data.
