# Subscription Renewal Query Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers submit queries related to their subscription. By filling out this form, customers can describe their issue or question regarding their subscription and provide necessary information to facilitate a prompt and effective resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out the form with your contact information and describe your query in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields.
- Select your preferred method of contact in the "Best Contact Method" field (select all that apply).
- Describe your query in the "Query Description" field.
- Indicate the priority of your query in the "Query Priority" field.
- If you have a specific sales person associated with your account, provide their name in the "Sales Person" field.
- If you have a preferred sales team member, select them in the "Sales Team" field.
- If you have a query status, select it in the "Query Status" field.
- If you have follow-up status, select it in the "Follow-up Status" field.
- If you would like to schedule a follow-up, select the follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields, respectively.
- If you would like to specify a next follow-up date, select it in the "Next Follow-up Date" field.
- Provide any additional follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Note" field.
- Finally, provide any closed notes related to your query in the "Closed Note" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Query Description**: Briefly describe the issue or question you have regarding your subscription.
* **Query Priority**: Select the level of priority for your query. Options: Low, Medium, High.
* **Best Contact Method**: Select all contact methods that you prefer, such as Call, Email, or Chat.
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Sales Person**: If you have a specific sales person associated with your account, enter their name.
* **Sales Team**: If you have a preferred sales team member, select them.
* **Query Status**: Select the current status of your query. Options: New, In Progress, Closed.
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the current follow-up status. Options: Scheduled, In Progress, Completed.
* **Follow-up Action**: Select all follow-up actions that you prefer, such as Send Email, Call, or Chat.
* **Follow-up Date**: If you would like to schedule a follow-up, select the date.
* **Follow-up Time**: If you would like to schedule a follow-up, select the time.
* **Next Follow-up Date**: Select the next follow-up date, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Note**: Enter any additional follow-up notes.
* **Closed Note**: Enter any closed notes related to your query, if applicable.
