# Special Visitor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Visitor Request Form is designed to collect information from special visitors who may be visiting the organization for various reasons such as tours, information requests, or other purposes. This form helps the organization's staff prepare for the visit, ensure a smooth experience, and provide necessary support to the visitor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Be specific and clear in your responses, especially when providing the Description of Event field.
3. Select the correct Type of Visit from the options provided.
4. Check the Additional Comments field for any further details that may be necessary for the organization to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Special Visitor Request** (`special_visitor_request_form`, text): 
  Describe the purpose of the visit, providing as much detail as possible.
* **Name (name, text)**: 
  Enter the full name of the individual or group requesting the visit.
* **Organization (organization, text)**: 
  Mention the organization they represent or are visiting (if applicable).
* **Phone Number (phone_number, text)**: 
  Provide a contact phone number in case further information or clarification is needed.
* **Email (email, text)**: 
  Enter a contact email address for communication.
* **Type of Visit (type_of_visit, select_one)**: 
  Choose from the options: Visitor Request, Tour Request, or Information Request.
* **Description of Event (description_of_event, text)**: 
  Provide a detailed description of the event or the purpose of the visit.
* **Event Date (event_date, date)**: 
  Enter the date of the event or visit (optional).
* **Number of People Attending (number_of_people_attending, number)**: 
  Indicate the number of individuals attending the event or visit.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments, text)**: 
  Share any extra information that might be helpful for the organization to prepare for the visit.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* Be honest and clear in your responses.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the organization's contact person.
