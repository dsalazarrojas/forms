# Intervention Ticket Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intervention Ticket Submission Form is designed to collect necessary information from users who need assistance or have a specific issue that requires intervention. Please fill out this form to submit your request, and our team will review and address it accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **First Name**. This field is required and should be filled with your real name.
2. Next, enter your **Last Name**. This field is also required and should reflect your true identity.
3. Provide a valid email address in the **Email** field. This field is essential for communication and should be accurate.
4. In the **Description** field, type a clear and concise explanation of your request or issue. This field is optional but helps us understand the scope of your concern.
5. Enter your **Phone** number. This field is crucial for our team to contact you for further information or clarification, and it's required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your family name or the name you prefer to be known by.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Use a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your request.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Describe the reason for your intervention request in as much detail as necessary.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a working phone number where we can contact you for further clarification or to provide updates on your request.
