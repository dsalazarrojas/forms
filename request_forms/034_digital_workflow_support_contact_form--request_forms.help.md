# Digital Workflow Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Workflow Support Contact Form is designed to collect information from users who need support with their digital workflow processes. It's a crucial tool for us to understand your requests and provide timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Ensure that you've provided accurate and complete information about your request.
3. Select the relevant options from dropdown fields to provide more context.
4. Attach any necessary files or documents to support your request.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form**: This is the main section where you can describe your request in detail.
	+ **Issue Description**: A brief description of the issue or problem you're facing.
	+ **Issue Type**: The type of issue you're experiencing (Internal, External, IT, or Other).
* **Contact Information**: This section helps us to get in touch with you.
	+ **Submitter Name**: Your name, so we know who's submitting the request.
	+ **Submitter Email**: Your email address, so we can contact you directly.
	+ **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number, optional but recommended.
	+ **Submitter Department**: The department you're from, if applicable.
	+ **Submitter Location**: The location of your team or organization.
	+ **Submitter Title**: Your position or title.
* **Team Information**: This section is for team-related requests.
	+ **Team Leader**: The name of the team leader or manager.
	+ **Team Leader Email**: The email address of the team leader or manager.
	+ **Team Member**: The name of the team member responsible.
	+ **Team Member Email**: The email address of the team member.
* **Request Details**: This section provides more information about your request.
	+ **Request Type**: The type of request (Internal, External).
	+ **Request Urgency**: The level of urgency (Low, Medium, High).
	+ **Request Priority**: The priority level (Low, Medium, High).
	+ **Request Status**: The current status of your request (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed).
	+ **Request Category**: The category of your request (IT Request Forms, Request Forms).
* **Resolution**: This section is for resolution-related fields.
	+ **Request Resolution**: The outcome of your request (Approved, Rejected).
* **Additional Information**: This section is for any additional comments or attachments.
	+ **Submitter Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about your request.
	+ **Submitter Attachment**: Any file or document that supports your request.
	+ **Submitter Message**: A message to the submitter, if needed.
	+ **Submitter Agreement**: Any agreement or terms and conditions related to your request.
Please note that some fields might not be applicable to your specific request, but providing as much information as possible will help us better understand and address your needs.
