# School Counselling Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about student referrals to support school counselors in their work. Please use this form to gather relevant details about students who need additional support or attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the student's name.
2.  Provide the parent's name (if applicable).
3.  Specify the reason for the referral.
4.  Select the student's grade level.
5.  Describe the referral concerns.
6.  Share any parental concerns.
7.  Choose the student's behavior (if applicable).
8.  Enter the referral date.
9.  Indicate how the referral concerns will be handled in terms of privacy and confidentiality.
10.  Sign and select your role as the referrer.
