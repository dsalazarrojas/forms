# Request For Leave - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Leave form is designed to allow employees to submit a formal leave request to their manager for approval. This form includes details such as leave type, start and end dates, duration, and purpose of leave. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly ensures a smooth processing of your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Request For Leave form, follow these steps:

1. **Select your Leave Type**: Choose the category that best describes your leave purpose from the provided options (Work, Personal, or Leave Type).
2. **Enter Date of Leave**: Provide the date you wish to start and end your leave.
3. **Fill in Start Date of Leave and End Date of Leave**: Enter the start and end dates of your leave, ensuring they match your leave type and purpose.
4. **Enter Duration of Leave**: Provide the duration of your leave in numbers.
5. **Specify Purpose of Leave**: Describe the reason for your leave in the designated text field.
6. **Optional Fields**: If necessary, add any additional comments or remarks.
7. **Manager Comment**: Allow your manager to add any comments or feedback about your leave request.
8. **Approval**: Once your manager approves or rejects your leave, you'll receive an update on the status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`):
	+ Choose the leave type that best describes the reason for your leave. Select one of the three options: Work, Personal, or Leave Type.
* **Date of Leave** (`date`):
	+ Enter the date you want your leave to begin and end.
* **Start Date of Leave** (`date`):
	+ Enter the start date of your leave. 
* **End Date of Leave** (`date`):
	+ Enter the end date of your leave. Make sure it's consistent with the duration of leave and your leave type.
* **Duration of Leave** (`number`):
	+ Provide the number of days or hours you need for your leave.
* **Purpose of Leave** (`text`):
	+ Explain the reason for your leave in a few sentences or paragraphs.
* **Remarks** (`text`):
	+ Add any additional comments or remarks if needed.
* **Manager Comment** (`text`):
	+ Leave a comment for your manager to add their feedback or comments.
* **Approved by** (`text`):
	+ Displays the manager's approval or rejection of your leave request.
* **Status** (`select_one`):
	+ Displays the current status of your leave request: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

Note: If you're unsure about any field or have questions, refer to your HR policies or contact your manager for guidance.
