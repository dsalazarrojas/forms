# Business Recovery Planning Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Recovery Planning Application Form is designed to collect information about businesses affected by disruptions or crises. It helps our team assess the situation and provide targeted support to help your business recover.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your business and its current status.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and detailed information about your business.
3. Review each field carefully and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the form to our team for review and guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: The official name of your business as registered. Please enter the exact name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Registration Number**: The official registration or license number of your business. This helps us verify your business identity and ensure we are serving the correct entity.
* **Owner or Principal Contact Name**: The full name of the business owner or principal contact person. This is the person we will primarily interact with regarding your application.
* **Contact Email**: The primary email address where we can contact you. Please ensure it is up-to-date and working correctly.
* **Contact Phone Number**: The primary phone number where we can contact you. This should be a reliable number that is not subject to changes.
* **Business Address**: The physical location of your business. This helps us understand your business' location and potential challenges.
* **Type of Business**: The industry or sector of your business. This helps us understand your business' specific needs.
* **Years in Operation**: The number of years your business has been in operation. This helps us understand your business' experience and maturity.
* **Number of Employees**: The total number of employees in your business. This helps us understand your business' size and potential workforce challenges.
* **Annual Revenue**: Your business' approximate annual revenue. This helps us understand your business' financial capacity and potential resilience.
* **Type of Disruption or Impact**: Select all types of disruptions or impacts that affected your business. These include natural disasters, economic crises, supply chain disruptions, and more.
* **Detailed Impact Description**: Explain how your business was affected by the disruption or impact. Please provide a detailed description of the challenges you faced.
* **Estimated Financial Impact**: Your estimated loss or damage in currency. This helps us understand the financial implications of the disruption.
* **Operational Impact**: Describe how your business operations were affected by the disruption or impact. Please provide detailed explanations of any changes, challenges, or consequences.
* **Recovery Support Needs**: Select all types of support you need to recover your business. These include financial assistance, technical support, business consulting, and more.
* **Desired Recovery Timeline**: Choose the time frame when you need to be operational again. This helps us prioritize support and ensure we meet your needs.
* **Current Business Status**: Describe the current status of your business. Is it fully operational, partially operational, suspended, or ceased operations completely?
* **Existing Recovery Plan**: Do you have a recovery plan in place? If yes, please describe its details.
* **Insurance Coverage Status**: If you have business insurance, please indicate its status. This helps us understand any potential support or assistance you may need.
* **Application Submission Date**: The date you are submitting this application. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and complete.
* Review each field carefully to avoid missing essential details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for guidance.
* We will review your application and respond with support and guidance as soon as possible.
