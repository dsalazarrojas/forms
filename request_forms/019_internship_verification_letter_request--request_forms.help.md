# Internship Verification Letter Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request verification of student internship experience. It is used to confirm the details of the internship, including the employer, student name, dates, and duties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields accurately and truthfully.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
3. Use the "Request Note" field to provide any additional information or context that is not captured in the other fields.
4. Submit the form to request verification of your internship experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you will enter your basic information about the internship.
* **Employer** (`employer`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if the employer is verified, "No" otherwise.
* **School** (`school`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if the internship was conducted at a school, "No" otherwise.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Internship Start Date** (`internship_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your internship in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Internship End Date** (`internship_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your internship in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Internship Duties** (`internship_duties`, text, required): Describe your internship duties and responsibilities.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor or point of contact at the internship site.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the internship site.
* **Request Note** (`request_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or context that is not captured in the other fields.
