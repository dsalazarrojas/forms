# Pedagogical Innovation Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for consultation on pedagogical innovations, which are ideas or approaches to teaching and learning that aim to improve educational outcomes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields with your information:
   - First Name 
   - Last Name 
   - Title 
   - Email 
   - Phone Number 
2. Choose a date and time for your consultation appointment (if desired)
3. Add any additional information about your consultation request in the text field
4. Select one or more categories from the list to describe your pedagogical innovation (optional)
5. Provide a description of your pedagogical innovation in the text field (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required): Your first name is requested to identify you as the user who is submitting the request.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Your last name is requested to identify you as the user who is submitting the request.
- **Title** (3, text, required): Your title or position is requested to identify your role or affiliation.
- **Email** (4, email, required): Your email address is requested so that we can contact you to schedule the consultation.
- **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Your phone number is requested so that we can contact you to schedule the consultation.
- **Date** (6, date, required): A date is requested for your consultation appointment (if desired).
- **Time** (7, time, required): A time is requested for your consultation appointment (if desired).
- **Note** (8, note, required): Additional information about your consultation request can be provided in this field.
- **Category** (9, select multiple, optional): Select one or more categories to describe your pedagogical innovation (if desired).
- **Description** (10, text, required): A description of your pedagogical innovation is requested to help the expert better understand your idea (if desired).

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your request is processed accurately.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
