# Purchasing Department Material Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchasing Department Material Requisition Form is designed to assist departments in requesting materials or supplies for purchase. This form guides users through specifying the required details for the purchase, ensuring that materials are properly tracked and managed throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and efficient completion of the form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Material Name**: Enter the name of the material or supply being requisitioned.
2. **Specify Material Type**: Indicate whether the material is "Yes" or "No" in the material_type field. This helps in categorizing and managing the requisitioned materials.
3. **Enter Quantity**: Provide the number of units or pieces of the material needed.
4. **Set Unit Price**: Enter the cost or unit price of the material.
5. **Choose Department**: Select the department that will be using or requesting the material (or "No" if not applicable).
6. **Select Requested By**: Specify who requested the material, which could be a person or a team (or "No" if not applicable).
7. **Requisitioned By**: Identify who within the organization will be handling the material or the person making the request (or "No" if not applicable).
8. **Request Date**: Enter the date on which the material was requested.
9. **Delivery Timeframe**: Select the timeframe in which you would like to receive the material.
10. **Material Description**: Briefly describe the material or supply requested.
11. **Purchase Order ID**: If known, enter the purchase order ID associated with the requested material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **material_name**: The name of the material or supply being requisitioned. Enter a brief but descriptive name to ensure accurate identification and tracking.
* **material_type**: Indicate whether the material is "Yes" or "No". This helps in categorizing and managing requisitioned materials.
* **quantity**: Provide the exact number of units or pieces of the material needed for the purchase.
* **unit_price**: Enter the cost or unit price of the material. This helps in budgeting and financial planning.
* **department**: Select the department that will be using or requesting the material. This helps in ensuring the material is allocated to the correct department.
* **requested_by**: Specify who requested the material. This could be a person or a team. This helps in tracking who initiated the request.
* **requisitioned_by**: Identify who within the organization will be handling the material or the person making the request. This helps in ensuring the correct person receives the material.
* **date_requested**: Enter the date on which the material was requested.
* **delivery_timeframe**: Select the timeframe in which you would like to receive the material. This helps in planning and coordinating delivery schedules.
* **material_description**: Provide a brief description of the material or supply requested. This helps in ensuring the correct material is delivered.
* **purchase_order_id**: If known, enter the purchase order ID associated with the requested material. This helps in referencing the material within existing purchase orders.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid any delays in processing your requisition.
- If your material is already requisitioned or in the purchasing process, please contact the purchasing department for assistance.
