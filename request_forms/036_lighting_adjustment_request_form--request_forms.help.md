# Lighting Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lighting Adjustment Request Form is a tool used to submit requests for lighting adjustments to facilities. This form helps facilities staff to collect and manage requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate to it and select the "Submit" button. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the lighting issue occurred or is affecting.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the problem you are experiencing with the lighting.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, true): Choose the level of priority for your lighting request:
	+ Low: The issue is affecting only a small area and does not hinder the facility's overall functionality.
	+ Medium: The issue is affecting a moderate-sized area and slightly impacts the facility's functionality.
	+ High: The issue is widespread and heavily impacts the facility's overall functionality.
* **Adjustment Requested** (`adjustment_requested`, text, required/optional): If you have already made an adjustment or have a specific solution in mind, describe it in this field.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue to help facilities staff better understand your request.
* If you are unsure about your facility's location or the priority of the issue, feel free to ask for help.
