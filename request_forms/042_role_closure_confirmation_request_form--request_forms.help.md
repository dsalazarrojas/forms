# Role Closure Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Closure Confirmation Request Form is a crucial part of the role closure process. It helps to track and document the closure of roles in a structured manner, ensuring that all necessary steps are taken to complete the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your role type by selecting "Form Type" from the dropdown menu.
2.  Provide a brief reason for the role closure request.
3.  Enter the date and time of the request.
4.  Enter your justification for the request.
5.  Confirm that the role has been reviewed and approved by the relevant stakeholders (HR, IT, or Manager).
6.  Select the location where the confirmation took place.
7.  Enter the time and date of the confirmation.
8.  Provide any additional comments or feedback.
9.  Choose the current status of the role (Open or Closed).
10.  Select the individuals who confirmed the role closure (HR, IT, or Manager).
11.  Confirm that you have the necessary permissions and access to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Type** (`form_1_form_type`): This field requires you to select the type of form you are filing. Choose "Form Type" from the dropdown menu.
* **Reason** (`form_1_reason`): Enter a brief explanation for why you need to close the role. This will help your supervisor or manager understand the context of the request.
* **Request Date** (`form_1_request_date`): Enter the date when you submitted this role closure request.
* **Request Time** (`form_1_request_time`): Enter the time when you submitted this role closure request.
* **Justification** (`form_1_justification`): Provide a detailed explanation of why the role should be closed.
* **Confirmed By** (`form_1_confirmed_by`): Confirm that the role has been reviewed and approved by the relevant stakeholders (HR, IT, or Manager).
* **Confirmed Date** (`form_1_confirmed_date`): Enter the date when the role was confirmed.
* **Comments** (`form_1_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the role closure process.
* **Form Status** (`form_1_form_status`): Choose whether the role is currently Open or Closed.
* **Who Confirmed** (`form_1_who_confirmed`): Select the individual(s) who confirmed the role closure (HR, IT, or Manager).
* **Confirmed Location** (`form_1_confirmed_location`, `form_1_confirmed_location_2`, `form_1_confirmed_location_3`, etc.): Select the location(s) where the confirmation took place.
* **Confirmed Time** (`form_1_confirmed_time`, `form_1_confirmed_time_2`, `form_1_confirmed_time_3`, etc.): Enter the time when the role was confirmed.
* **Confirmed Date/Time** (`form_1_confirmed_date_time`, `form_1_confirmed_date_time_2`, `form_1_confirmed_date_time_3`, etc.): Enter the date and time when the role was confirmed.
