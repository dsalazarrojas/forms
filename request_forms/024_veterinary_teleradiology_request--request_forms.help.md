# Veterinary Teleradiology Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for veterinarians to request teleradiology services for their patients. Please ensure you are a veterinarian or authorized to make such requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required): Please provide the patient's name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Pet Details** (`Pet Details`, `text`, required): Provide the pet's species, breed, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Images** (`Medical Images`, `text`, optional): If you have medical images of the patient, please attach them here.
* **Medical Records** (`Medical Records`, `text`, optional): If you have medical records of the patient, please attach them here.
* **Review and Signature** (`Review and Signature`, `text`, required): Please read and sign the request carefully to confirm your identity and authorization.
* **Attachments** (`Attachments`, `text`, optional): You can attach any additional documents or images to support your request.
* **Review and Signature Date** (`Review and Signature Date`, `date`, required): Please provide the date and time you reviewed and signed the request.
* **Confirm Request** (`Confirm Request`, `radio`, required): Please select 'Yes' to confirm your request and 'No' if you need to make changes or cancel the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have attached all necessary medical images and records.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the relevant authority for guidance.
