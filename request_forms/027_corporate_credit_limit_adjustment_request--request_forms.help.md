# corporate_credit_limit_adjustment_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "corporate_credit_limit_adjustment_request" form is used to submit requests for adjustments to a company's corporate credit limit. This form should only be used when a company's existing credit limit needs to be modified, such as when the credit limit needs to be increased or decreased. Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a credit limit adjustment before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief company request in the "Company Request" field.
2. Provide a clear justification for the credit limit adjustment in the "Justification" field.
3. Attach any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
4. Enter the new credit limit amount in the "Credit Limit Amount" field.
5. Confirm the current credit limit in the "Current Credit Limit" field.
6. Enter the requested credit limit in the "Requested Credit Limit" field.
7. Provide the finance contact's name and email address in the "Finance Contact" field (optional).
8. Select the date of the request in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Request** (`Company Request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the credit limit adjustment.
* **Justification** (`Justification`, `text`, required): Clearly explain why the credit limit adjustment is necessary.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `note`, required): Attach any relevant documents that support your credit limit adjustment request, such as invoices, receipts, or other supporting materials.
* **Credit Limit Amount** (`Credit Limit Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the new credit limit amount you are requesting.
* **Current Credit Limit** (`Current Credit Limit`, `number`, required): Confirm the current credit limit.
* **Requested Credit Limit** (`Requested Credit Limit`, `number`, required): Enter the new credit limit amount you are requesting.
* **Finance Contact** (`Finance Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and email address of the finance contact who will review your request.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the request.
