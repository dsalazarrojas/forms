# material_storage_condition_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material storage condition review request form is used to report any issues or concerns regarding the storage of materials. This form should only be filled out by the person who is submitting the report, not on behalf of someone else.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information as the submitter.
2. Choose a priority level (High or Low) to indicate the urgency of the issue.
3. Describe the condition of the material in the Condition Description field.
4. Attach any relevant supporting documents, such as photos or videos.
5. Provide your contact information so that the reviewer can get in touch with you for more information.
6. Choose a date for the review to be completed by.
7. Optionally, fill out the Submitter Name, Department, Job Title, and other fields if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Storage Condition Review**: This is the title of the form.
* **Urgency Level**: Select 'High' if the issue is critical and needs immediate attention, or 'Low' if it's less urgent.
* **Priority Level**: This is the same as Urgency Level, used to indicate the level of importance.
* **Storage Condition**: Describe the condition of the material, such as 'Damaged', 'Missing', or 'Not accessible'.
* **Condition Description**: Provide a detailed description of the condition.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant files or photos that support your report.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number so that the reviewer can get in touch with you.
* **Submitter**: This field is not to be filled out by the submitter.
* **Team**: This field is not applicable.
* **Date**: Enter a date for the review to be completed by.
* **Time**: Enter the time for the review to be completed by.
* **Location**: This is not applicable.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the report.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so that the reviewer can get in touch with you.
* **Submitted By**: This field is not to be filled out by the submitter.
* **Assigned To**: This field is not applicable.
* **Status**: Select 'Active' if the review is active, or 'Inactive' if it's not.
* **Review Due By**: Enter the date for the review to be completed by.
* **Submitter Email**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter Phone**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about the report.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter Name**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter Department**: This field is not applicable.
* **Submitter Job Title**: This field is not applicable.
