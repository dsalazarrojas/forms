# Sole Source Purchase Justification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and justify sole source purchases, which are purchases where only one vendor is available to meet the agency's needs. A sole source purchase may occur when a product or service is unique or can only be provided by a single vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the request form field with a clear and concise description of the purchase request.
2. Provide information about the vendor in the vendor_info field, including their name and contact details.
3. Describe the purchase details in the purchase_details field, including the item or service being purchased and its expected use.
4. In the justification field, explain why this purchase is a sole source and why it cannot be met by any other vendor.
5. Enter the date and time of the purchase request in the date and time fields.
6. Enter your email address in the email field so that the vendor can contact you.
7. Optionally, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the purchase in the file_upload field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, required): This is the main description of the purchase request. Please fill this field with a clear and concise description of what is being purchased and why.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required): This field should contain information about the vendor, including their name and contact details (e.g., phone number, email address).
* **Purchase Details** (`purchase_details`, text, required): Here, describe the item or service being purchased and how it will be used by the agency.
* **Justification** (`justification`, text, required): Please provide a clear explanation of why this purchase is a sole source and why it cannot be met by any other vendor.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the purchase request.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the purchase request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the vendor can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is an optional field for providing your phone number. If you have any concerns or questions, it's best to be reached at.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): This field is for uploading any additional relevant information that may be required for the purchase.
