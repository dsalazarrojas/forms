# Logistics Optimization Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Logistics Optimization Grant Application Form is designed to collect information about logistics-related projects or initiatives that require financial support or resources optimization. It is intended to help companies, organizations, or individuals seeking funding or assistance to optimize their logistics operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "General Information" page with your project's basic details.
2. Provide an overview of the grant you are applying for in the "Grant Details" page.
3. Estimate the logistical costs associated with your project in the "Logistics Costs" page.
4. Enter your project's budget in the "Project Budget" page.
5. Share your details as the applicant in the "Applicant Details" page.
6. Update the "Application Status" page with your current application status.
7. Upload any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" page.
8. Review and submit your form using the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, optional): Enter the basic details of your project, such as its name, description, and location.
* **Grant Details** (`grant_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the grant you are applying for, including its purpose, duration, and expected outcome.
* **Logistics Costs** (`logistics_costs`, `text`, optional): Estimate the logistical costs associated with your project, including transportation, storage, and other expenses.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `text`, optional): Enter your project's budget, including the total amount and breakdown of expenses.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, optional): Share your details as the applicant, including your name, contact information, and affiliation.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `text`, optional): Update your application status, such as pending, approved, or denied.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your application, such as resumes, project proposals, or certificates of completion.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): Review and submit your form once you have completed all the required fields.
