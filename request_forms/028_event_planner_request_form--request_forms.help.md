# Event Planner Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Planner Request Form is designed to collect information about events and gatherings to help plan and organize them smoothly. Fill out this form to request specific services or resources for your event, and our team will take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields to help us understand your event planning needs.
3. Select the correct options for the "Event Type" and "Event Requirements" fields.
4. If you need help with specific event planning services, such as catering or audio-visuals, please specify in the "Event Details" field.
5. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information (1)** (Event Information, text, required): This is where you can provide a brief description of your event.
* **Setup Information (2)** (Setup Information, text, required): This is where you can provide more details about your event, such as its purpose and objectives.
* **Media Information (3)** (Media Information, text): This is where you can provide information about the visual and audio elements of your event, such as speakers, presentations, or decorations.
* **Catering Information (4)** (Catering Information, text): This is where you can specify your catering needs, such as food and beverages.
* **Fundraiser Information (5)** (Fundraiser Information, text): This is where you can provide information about fundraising activities or goals.
* **Event Type (6)** (Event Type, select one, optional): Choose the type of event you're planning, such as Event, Wedding, Party, Seminar, or Workshop.
* **Event Date (7)** (Event Date, date, optional): Provide the date of your event.
* **Event Time (8)** (Event Time, time, optional): Provide the time of your event.
* **Event Location (9)** (Event Location, text, optional): This is where you can specify the location of your event.
* **Contact Name (10)** (Contact Name, text, required): This is where you can provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact Number (11)** (Contact Number, text, required): This is where you can provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (12)** (Contact Email, email, required): This is where you can provide your contact email address.
* **Event Details (13)** (Event Details, note, optional): This is where you can provide any additional information about your event.
* **Event Requirements (14)** (Event Requirements, select multiple, optional): Choose the specific requirements for your event, such as Event, Audio-Visuals, Catering, Decorations, Photography, or Sound System.
* **Event Budget (15)** (Event Budget, number, optional): This is where you can specify your budget for the event.
* **Event Catering (16)** (Event Catering, text, optional): This is where you can specify your catering needs.
* **Event Decorations (17)** (Event Decorations, text, optional): This is where you can provide information about event decorations.
* **Event Photography (18)** (Event Photography, text, optional): This is where you can specify your photography needs.
* **Event Sound System (19)** (Event Sound System, text, optional): This is where you can provide information about the event sound system requirements.
* **Additional Requirements (20)** (Additional Requirements, text, optional): This is where you can provide any additional requirements or requests.
* **Terms of Service (21)** (Terms of Service, text, optional): This is where you can review our terms of service and agreement.
* **Privacy Policy (22)** (Privacy Policy, text, optional): This is where you can review our privacy policy.
* **Submit (23)** (Submit, text, required): Click this button to submit your request.
* **Cancel (24)** (Cancel, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your request.
* **Back (25)** (Back, text, optional): Click this button to return to the previous page.

Note: The form will automatically validate the required fields before submission. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it out accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
