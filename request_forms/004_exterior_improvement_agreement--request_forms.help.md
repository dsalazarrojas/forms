# Exterior Improvement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from homeowners who are requesting exterior improvements, such as renovations or repairs, for their properties. It is a mandatory form that helps homeowners confirm their understanding of the Homeowners Association (HOA) guidelines and rules before making any changes to their exterior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Home Owner Info"**: This question is used to determine if the homeowner is requesting exterior improvements for their property.
2. **Provide project details in "Project Details"**: This text field is used to collect a brief description of the project.
3. **Review and acknowledge the "HOA Guidelines"**: This field provides an overview of the HOA guidelines related to exterior improvements.
4. **Select "I have read and understand the guidelines" or "I have not read and do not understand the guidelines"**: This question is used to confirm the homeowner's understanding of the HOA guidelines.
5. **Enter your signature and name in "Signature" and "Submitter Name"**: These fields are used to confirm the homeowner's agreement and provide their signature.
6. **Upload a photo of the project in "Project Photo"**: This field is used to collect a photo of the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Owner Info**: Please answer "Yes" if you are the homeowner requesting exterior improvements, or "No" if you are not.
* **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of the project, including the type of improvement, location, and any other relevant details.
* **HOA Guidelines**: This is a note field that outlines the HOA guidelines related to exterior improvements.
* **Acknowledgement**: Please select "I have read and understand the guidelines" if you have read and understand the HOA guidelines, or "I have not read and do not understand the guidelines" if you have not.
* **Signature**: Please enter your signature to confirm your agreement to the guidelines.
* **Submitter Name**: Please enter your name as the submitter of the request.
* **Project Photo**: Upload a photo of the project to provide visual reference.
* **Submitter Signature**: Please enter your signature as the submitter of the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the HOA guidelines carefully before selecting "I have read and understand the guidelines".
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the guidelines, please ask your HOA representative for clarification.
* Make sure to upload a clear and legible photo of the project.
* Sign and date your agreement to confirm your understanding and commitment to the guidelines.
